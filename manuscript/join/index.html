The Magick Manuscript

20,000+ entries · 15 cross-linked grimoires · an interactive digital grimoire that never stops growing.

See Plans

Imagine having a witchcraft library that never stops growing. One that knows when you look up protection, you probably also want to know which crystals carry that energy, which plants strengthen it, which deities preside over it, and which rituals have been used across centuries to invoke it. One that lets you pull on any thread and follow it as deep as you want to go. That is what the Magick Manuscript is.

This is a 20,000+ page living grimoire built from years of research across hundreds of published witchcraft books. It holds 15 grimoires covering rituals and spells, deities and spirits, plants, minerals, oils, recipes, animals, correspondences, symbols, runes, tarot and cards, astrology, timing, dreams, and ritual materials. Every single entry is cross-linked to every related entry across every other grimoire. Nothing exists in isolation here.

It doesn’t just sit there. It works with you.

The Manuscript comes with tools that put all of that research to use in your actual practice, every single day.

  • The Calendar, in your own calendar. Add it to Google, Apple or Outlook in one tap: every festival of the year and every new, quarter and full moon, at the exact moment in your own time zone. Each event links straight to its page in the Manuscript, and it keeps itself up to date.
  • Today’s Correspondences. The moon phase, the planet that rules the day, the Sun’s sign and the season, with the herbs, crystals, colors, oils and spells that suit them. Worked out fresh every day.
  • Draw a Card. Pull your daily tarot card from a face-down fan. It lands upright or reversed and takes you straight to that card’s page, with all its meanings, keywords and interpretations.
  • 300+ tarot spreads, ready to read. Deal any spread digitally and turn the cards over one by one, or follow its layout with your own deck at your altar.
  • Dream Decoder. Write down your dream and the symbols in it light up, each with what it means.
  • Planetary Hours. Worked out from your own sunrise and sunset, so you always know which planet rules the hour you’re working in.
  • Pull a Rune. Draw from the Elder Futhark or the Witch’s Runes and go straight to the stone’s meaning.
  • Spell Builder. Choose what your spell is for and the Manuscript gathers the days, colors, herbs, stones, oils, deities and rituals tied to it. Save it as a working to come back to.
  • Correspondence Wheels. Choose a planet, a zodiac sign or a moon phase and see everything tied to it at once.
  • Your own notes. Write private notes on any page, save the pages you keep coming back to, and find it all in your account.
  • A 30-day beginner path. One page a day, from the basics to working with deities and spirits, emailed to you at 11:11 each morning or read at your own pace.
