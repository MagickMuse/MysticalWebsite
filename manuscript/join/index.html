It Works With You, Every Day

The Manuscript comes with tools that put all of that research to use in your actual practice, every single day.

  • The Calendar, in your own calendar

    Add it to Google, Apple or Outlook in one tap to get every festival of the year and every new, quarter and full moon at the exact moment in your own time zone. Each event links straight to its page, and it keeps itself up to date.

  • Today’s Correspondences

    The moon phase, the planet that rules the day, the Sun’s sign and the season, with the herbs, crystals, colors, oils and spells that suit them, worked out fresh every day.

  • Draw a Card

    Pull your daily tarot card from a face-down fan. It lands upright or reversed and takes you straight to that card’s meanings, keywords and interpretations.

  • 300+ Tarot Spreads

    Deal any spread digitally and turn the cards over one by one, or follow its layout with your own deck at your altar.

  • Dream Decoder

    Write down your dream and the symbols in it light up, each with what it means.

  • Planetary Hours

    Worked out from your own sunrise and sunset, so you always know which planet rules the hour you’re working in.

  • Pull a Rune

    Draw from the Elder Futhark or the Witch’s Runes and go straight to the stone’s meaning.

  • Spell Builder

    Choose what your spell is for and the Manuscript gathers the days, colors, herbs, stones, oils, deities and rituals tied to it. Save it as a working to come back to.

  • Correspondence Wheels

    Choose a planet, a zodiac sign or a moon phase and see everything tied to it at once.

  • Your Own Notes

    Write private notes on any page, save the pages you keep coming back to, and find it all in your account.

  • A 30-Day Beginner Path

    One page a day, from the basics to working with deities and spirits, emailed to you at 11:11 each morning or read at your own pace.
