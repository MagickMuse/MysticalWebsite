🧪Infused Oil of Henna Blossoms

◇Directions
Place henna blossoms in a clean jar and cover fully with your chosen carrier oil. Seal and set in a warm location, checking periodically over several weeks until the desired fragrance intensity is reached, adding fresh blossoms as needed. Strain before use. Ben oil is the traditional preferred carrier; safflower or sweet almond oil may be substituted if unavailable.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
Henna blossoms (fresh or dried) Ben oil as carrier (moringa oil), or safflower oil, or sweet almond oil
◇Description
A slowly infused oil using henna blossoms, prepared for deep, restorative sleep. Infusion may take several weeks to reach full fragrance intensity. Ben oil (also known as moringa oil) is the traditional preferred carrier; safflower or sweet almond oil may be substituted.
