🛡️Four Thieves Vinegar

◇Directions
Modern Version: Place all herbs into the jug of apple cider vinegar and place in a dark cabinet to age 6 to 8 weeks. Traditional Version: Combine all herbs, spices, and garlic in the distilled wine vinegar. Digest for seven to eight days with occasional agitation. Pour off the liquid, press out the remainder from the herbs, and filter the combined liquids.

◇Description
A legendary protective vinegar blend with origins in the plague era. Two versions are presented: a modern infusion using apple cider vinegar aged 6-8 weeks, and a traditional formula using distilled wine vinegar digested for 7-8 days. Widely used in Hoodoo and folk magick for banishing, protection, and crossing work.

Alternative Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • ½ cup lavender flowers
  • ¼ cup rosemary leaves
  • ½ cup sage leaves, coarsely chopped
  • Apple cider vinegar (enough to cover)

Directions

Put the garlic and herbs into a wide-mouth jar and pour in enough warmed apple cider vinegar to cover them. Let sit in a warm place for three to four weeks. Strain out the herbs. Pour the vinegar into a clean glass jar with a tight-fitting lid. Store in a cool, dark place; it will keep for a year.

Usage

Use as a household disinfectant by spraying countertops and doorknobs. To ward off illness such as a cold, take one to two tablespoons every three to four hours internally.
