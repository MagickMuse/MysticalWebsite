A banishing oil to drive away unwanted people; former lovers, false friends, or anyone who needs to stay gone.

Quantities

15 drops bergamot essential oil (Citrus bergamia)
15 drops black pepper essential oil (Piper nigrum)
5 drops clove essential oil (Syzygium aromaticum)
2 ml bottle carrier oil

Directions

Mix all essential oils and dilute one drop of synergy in a 2 ml bottle of the carrier of choice. Anoint a black candle for banishing on a Saturday (endings) to get someone to leave you alone for good. If the moon is waning or dark, all the better.
