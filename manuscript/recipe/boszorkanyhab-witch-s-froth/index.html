💕Boszorkányhab (Witch's Froth)

A Hungarian love spell recipe creating a light, fluffy apple dessert resembling clouds or snow. Traditionally kept secret by witches for their own use.

Quantities

4 medium apples
1 egg white
Confectioner's (Powdered) Sugar to taste
Optional: eau de vie, rum, Calvados, or almond extract

Directions

Preheat the oven to 350°F/180°C/Gas 4. Wash four apples and bake them in the oven for approximately 45 minutes, pricking the skins if you don't want them to burst. Remove the apples from the oven; when cool, remove the skins, core, and seeds. With a fork or wire whisk, beat the apple pulp until smooth. Stir in the egg white and sugar and beat for an additional 10 minutes until a light, fluffy, frothy texture is achieved. Add a touch of eau de vie, rum, Calvados, or almond extract if desired. Spoon into bowls and serve immediately or refrigerate until served. Choose apples with very white flesh for a snow-white result; for pink color, add tiny bits of red peel or juice from the baked apples. Four medium apples creates two generous servings.
