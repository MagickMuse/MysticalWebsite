Ten casual tea blends perfect for enjoying with a book, a friend, or family. Any black tea can be substituted with a preferred type such as Yorkshire, Irish, or Scottish Breakfast.

Quantities

  • 3 tsp China Rose; 1 tsp Chamomile
  • 3 tsp English Teatime; 1 tsp Chamomile; 2 tsp Rose hips
  • 3 tsp Earl Grey; 2 tsp Rose hips
  • 3 tsp Linden; 2 tsp Chamomile
  • 2 tsp English Teatime; 1/2 tsp Chamomile; 1 tsp Comfrey; 1/4 tsp Elder flower; 1 tsp Rose hips
  • 3 tsp English Breakfast; 1 tsp Hyssop; 2 tsp Rose hips
  • 3 tsp English Breakfast; 1 tsp Elder flower; 1 tsp Hops; 2 tsp Rose hips
  • Same as 7, adding 1 tsp Chamomile
  • 3 tsp black tea; 1 tsp Hops; 1 tsp Hyssop; 1 tsp Mullein; 2 tsp Rose hips
  • 1 tsp English Teatime; 2 tsp China Rose; 1 tsp Chamomile; 2 tsp Dandelion root; 1 tsp Elder flower; 1 tsp Hops; 1 tsp Rose hips

Directions

Choose a blend and brew using the standard method for a good pot of tea.
