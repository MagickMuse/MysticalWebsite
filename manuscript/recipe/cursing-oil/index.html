⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A potent cursing oil brewed from patchouli, graveyard dirt, Dragon's Blood, and sulfur steeped in virgin olive oil. The mixture requires a full day to set, followed by straining and three days of mulling in total darkness before it is ready for use in baneful workings.

Quantities

Cooking oil (preferably virgin olive oil)
Patchouli herb
Graveyard dirt
Dragon's Blood herb
Sulfur

Directions

Combine the patchouli, graveyard dirt, Dragon's Blood, and sulfur into the cooking oil and mix thoroughly. Allow the mixture to sit undisturbed for twenty-four hours. Strain the herbs from the oil and transfer the liquid to a container. Store it in a dark place for three days to mull. After three days in darkness the oil is ready for use.
