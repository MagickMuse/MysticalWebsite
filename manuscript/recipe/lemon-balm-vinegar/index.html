Lemon Balm

An infused vinegar blessed by the goddess Diana, carrying lemon balm's uplifting energy that soothes emotional wounds and instills love. Use for success, compassion, and prosperity magick.

Quantities

  • Fresh lemon balm leaves (enough to fill jar loosely)
  • Apple cider vinegar (to cover)

Directions

Harvest fresh lemon balm leaves in the morning after the dew has dried. Rinse gently and pat dry. Loosely fill a clean glass jar with the leaves. Pour apple cider vinegar over the lemon balm until completely covered, leaving about 1 inch of headspace. Use a non-metallic lid or place parchment paper under a metal lid to prevent corrosion. Store in a cool, dark place for 2 to 4 weeks, shaking occasionally. Strain through cheesecloth, squeezing to extract all liquid. Transfer to a clean bottle and label with the date. Use in salad dressings, marinades, or diluted as a tonic for success, compassion, and prosperity work. The vinegar will keep for up to a year stored in a cool, dark place.
