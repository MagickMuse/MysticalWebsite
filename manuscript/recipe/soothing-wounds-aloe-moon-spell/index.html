🌿Soothing Wounds Aloe Moon Spell

◇Directions
1. Under the moonlight, write your intention or what you seek to heal on the paper. For mending a rift with another person, write your name on one side and theirs on the other. Anoint the paper with healing Moon Oil if desired. 2. Cut a slice of aloe leaf large enough to hold the paper when folded. As you cut, focus on getting to the heart of the matter. Set aside any remaining aloe for other workings. 3. With the moon's illuminating energy in mind, cut a small hollow into the gel inside the cut leaf section. 4. Sprinkle dried rose petals onto the paper. Fold the paper toward yourself several times, drawing healing inward. Press the folded paper into the hollow in the leaf. 5. Step outside under the moonlight. Cradle the leaf in both hands and say aloud: "Aloe leaf, with inner gel to soothe, and a protective leaf with a shell that's toothed. Heal this wound and make things smooth, that nothing more can wake this wound." 6. Bury the aloe leaf in the earth to seal and release the intention.
◇Type
Talisman
◇Quantities
Pen and small piece of paper Moon Oil (optional) Fresh aloe leaf Knife Dried rose petals
◇Description
A gentle mending spell using aloe's dual nature — soothing inner gel and protective outer skin — to petition for healing of emotional wounds or rifts between people. An intention paper is tucked inside a fresh aloe leaf with dried rose petals and buried in the earth under moonlight. Especially suited to the Pisces moon for softening old wounds, the waning cycle for diminishing pain, the Cancer moon for reconciling rifts, and the Libra moon for restoring harmony.
