💨Exorcism Fumigations

◇Type
Incense
↗Correspondences1
🛡️Protection
↗Plants9

Five traditional incense formulas used to asperge and fumigate the four corners of a home in cases of bewitchment and haunting. Open all doors and windows before fumigating. The last three formulas contain toxic ingredients. Formulas 1 and 2 are recommended for practical use.

Quantities

  • Formula 1:
  • Calamint
  • Peony
  • Mint
  • Palma Christi (castor plant)
    Formula 2 (Simple):
  • Gum asafetida (burned alone)
    Formula 3:
  • Juniper
  • Rosemary
  • Hemlock plant (not the tree — very poisonous)
  • Sloe
  • Frankincense or pine resin
  • Dried garlic
    Formula 4:
  • Myrrh
  • Frankincense
  • Hellebore (poisonous)
  • St. John's Wort
  • Sulphur
    Formula 5:
  • Myrrh
  • Sulphur
  • Red sandalwood
  • Rotten apples
  • Wine galls (oak galls)
  • Vinegar
  • Red wine lees
  • Arsenic (extremely hazardous)

Directions

  1. Open all doors and windows in the home before beginning.
  2. Asperge and fumigate the four corners of each room using your chosen formula and the usual exorcism formula of fire and water.
  3. Formulas 1 and 2 are recommended for general use. Formulas 3, 4, and 5 are traditional; employ only with full awareness of their toxic components.
