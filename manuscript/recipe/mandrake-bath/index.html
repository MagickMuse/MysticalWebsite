✨Mandrake Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences2
✨Cleansing🧹Purification
Plants1
Mandrake

A heavy-duty spiritual cleansing for emergencies and severe cases of psychic contamination. Mandrake is soaked, not boiled or infused, in warm water so that its cleansing essence permeates the liquid without destroying the root. The root is preserved after use and can be employed again.

Quantities

  • 1 true mandrake root
  • Warm water, enough to soak the root; this liquid is then added to the bath

Directions

Soak a true mandrake root in warm water overnight or for up to three days. Do not boil or make an infusion. Remove the mandrake root from the water and carefully allow it to dry out completely to preserve it for future use. Add the soaking liquid to a tub filled with water. Bathe in the mixture.
