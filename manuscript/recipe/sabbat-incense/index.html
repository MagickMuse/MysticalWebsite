🔥Sabbat Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences2
🛡️Protection🧠Psychic Abilities
Plants8
FennelGerman ChamomilePennyroyalRoseRueSolomon’s SealWitch HazelWormwood

A general sabbat incense suitable for any of the eight Wiccan sabbats. The blend combines protective and purifying resins with traditional sabbat herbs. ⚠️ Caution: Contains rue and wormwood, which can be harmful. Burn in well-ventilated areas and do not inhale directly.

Quantities

3 parts Frankincense
2 parts Myrrh
1 part Benzoin
½ part Bay
½ part Fennel
½ part Thyme
½ part Pennyroyal
½ part Solomon's Seal
¼ part Rue*
¼ part Wormwood*
½ part Chamomile
½ part Rose petals

Directions

Grind the frankincense, myrrh, and benzoin. Add bay, fennel, thyme, pennyroyal, Solomon's seal, rue, wormwood, chamomile, and rose petals. Blend thoroughly. Burn in a well-ventilated area during any Wiccan sabbat. Do not inhale directly; contains rue and wormwood.
