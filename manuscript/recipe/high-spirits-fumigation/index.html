🪔High Spirits Fumigation

Type
Incense
Correspondences2
✨Cleansing🛡️Protection
Plants3
BenzoinFrankincenseMyrrh

A smoke cleansing blend that clears psychic debris, offers protection, and draws the attention of benevolent spirits. The three resins work together to create a highly effective purifying atmosphere.

Quantities

  • 3 parts frankincense
  • 2 parts benzoin
  • 1 part myrrh

Directions

Blend frankincense (3 parts), benzoin (2 parts), and myrrh (1 part). Burn the mixture and direct the smoke over the body from head to foot.
