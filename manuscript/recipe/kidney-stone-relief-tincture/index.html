💧Kidney Stone Relief Tincture

◇Type
Tincture
↗Correspondences2
🩺HealthKidneys
↗Plants5
DandelionDogwoodJuniperPoppyTurmeric

A blend of six pre-made tinctures — California poppy, Jamaican dogwood bark, juniper berry, marshmallow root, dandelion root, and turmeric root — combined in water and taken in 1-oz doses every 20 minutes until kidney stone pain subsides.

Quantities

½ tsp California poppy flower tincture
⅓ tsp Jamaican dogwood bark tincture
¼ tsp juniper berry tincture
½ tsp marshmallow root tincture
⅛ tsp dandelion root tincture
⅛ tsp turmeric root tincture

Directions

Add all tinctures to one ounce of water. Drink one ounce every 20 minutes until pain subsides.
