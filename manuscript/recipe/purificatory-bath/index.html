Recipe Grimoire

🛀Purificatory Bath

◇Directions
1. Combine all herbs and salt. Store in a sealed container. 2. When bathing, tie a handful of the mixture in a small muslin or cheesecloth bag. Cast it into the bathwater while chanting your usual water consecration words, or: So therefore he who would govern the works of the fire must first asperge with water of the loud, resounding sea.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
- Dried rosemary — 7 parts - Dried lavender — 7 parts - Dried basil — 4 and 1/2 parts - Dried vervain or lemon verbena — 2 parts - Dried hyssop — 1 part - Dried valerian — 1 part - Dried sage — 1/2 part - Dried fennel — 1/2 part - Cooking salt or table salt — half as much again as the total herb quantity Substitutes: - Hyssop and salt alone - Sweet balm (lemon balm) and salt alone
◇Description
Recommended before any complex operation such as demonic conjuration or necromancy, in all cases of bewitchment, before entering the grand circle, or before sleep during a magical attack.
