Antiseptic Bush

Aura cleansing accomplished during sleep using Antiseptic Bush flower essence remedy (Living/Australia). The initial bath dose is intensive and should only be repeated if the spell has not been performed in several months. Ongoing maintenance requires only a small amount rubbed into the hands or feet before bed.

Quantities

  • Antiseptic Bush flower essence (Living/Australia): 20 drops for the bath; a few drops for nightly maintenance

Directions

Add twenty drops of Antiseptic Bush flower essence remedy (Living/Australia) to a bath taken just before going to sleep. Bathe, then get into bed and rub a few additional drops into the thin skin between the thumb and index finger. For ongoing maintenance, only repeat the full bath dose if the spell has not been performed in several months. Otherwise, simply rub a few drops into the hand or onto the soles of the feet before bed each night.
