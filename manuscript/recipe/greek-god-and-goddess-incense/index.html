🏛️Greek God and Goddess Incense

A comprehensive incense honoring the Greek pantheon. Each ingredient corresponds to a specific deity: frankincense for Apollo, myrrh for Demeter, pine for Poseidon, rose for Aphrodite, sage for Zeus, white willow for Persephone, olive oil for Athena, and cypress oil for Artemis and Hecate.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

4 parts Frankincense (sacred to Apollo), 2 parts Myrrh (Demeter), 1 part Pine (Poseidon), 1 part Rose petals (Aphrodite), 1 part Sage (Zeus), 1 part White Willow bark (Persephone), a few drops Olive oil (Athena), a few drops Cypress oil (Artemis/Hecate)

Directions

Grind the resins (frankincense and myrrh) first. Add the pine needles or resin, crumbled rose petals, sage, and white willow bark. Mix thoroughly. Add the olive and cypress oils last, blending until absorbed. Burn to honor the Greek Gods and Goddesses.
