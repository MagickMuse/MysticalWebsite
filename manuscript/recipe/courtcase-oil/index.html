About Courtcase Oil

Courtcase Oil is one of the classic Hoodoo condition oils used for legal success, vindication, and justice workings. It is a multifaceted oil that can be used across a wide range of court case and justice spells — not only when literal court appearances are involved, but in any situation where you need the "verdict" to go your way.

Uses

  • Dress brown and/or purple candles for legal victory workings
  • Anoint charm bags and mojo hands before court appearances
  • Used as a cotton ball carry (dip a cotton ball in the oil and carry it in your pocket or bra during proceedings)
  • Combine with Fiery Wall of Protection Oil and/or San Cipriano Oil for compounded court case workings
  • Can be supplemented with Commanding Oil for added domination energy

Formula

Primary Ingredients:

  • Courtcase root (galangal), chopped then ground — the star botanical
  • Black mustard seeds, ground together with the root

Optional Enhancements:

  • Deer's tongue — for eloquence
  • Slippery elm — to shield against false testimony
  • Any other botanicals associated with legal victories

Oil Base:

  • Jojoba oil
  • Sunflower oil

(Blended together as the carrier)

Method:

  1. Chop the Courtcase root into small pieces.
  2. Blend the chopped root with black mustard seeds and grind them together into a fine mixture.
  3. Add any optional botanicals, also ground.
  4. Place the botanical mixture into a bottle.
  5. Cover completely with a blend of jojoba and sunflower oils.
  6. Allow to infuse for several weeks before use, or use immediately if needed.
