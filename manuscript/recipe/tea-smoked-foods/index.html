🍵Tea-Smoked Foods

Type
Food
Correspondences1
🧹Purification

A traditional Chinese cooking style that adds smoky flavor to chicken, fish, seafood, tofu, and mushrooms. The tea blend can be crafted with magical intent. Use Lapsang souchong for intense smokiness, jasmine green tea for subtle floral notes perfect with seafood, or add dried citrus rind for tangy smoke ideal with chicken.

Quantities

  • Blended tea leaves (amount as needed)
  • Dry white rice, uncooked (equal amount to tea)
  • Aluminum foil (several layers)
  • Food to smoke: chicken, fish, seafood, tofu, or mushrooms

Directions

Place the wok on the cooking surface and cover the cooking area with several layers of aluminum foil.

Take the amount of tea you have prepared and add the same amount of white rice for a 1:1 ratio of tea leaves to rice. This ensures the smoke is not too loud or harsh. Add the tea and rice mixture to the wok and turn on the heat to medium.

When the mixture begins to smoke, add the food to be cooked to the steamer basket and cover. Allow the heat and smoke to cook the food until ready.
