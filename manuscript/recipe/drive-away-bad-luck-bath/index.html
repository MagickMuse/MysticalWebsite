🛁Drive Away Bad Luck Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences1
🛡️Protection
Materials1
🌾Flour
Plants4
BasilLemonSesameSpearmint

A ritual cleansing bath using a hot basil and spearmint infusion combined with lemon juice, sesame seeds, flour, and Command and Compel Oil to drive away bad luck.

Quantities

Strong infusion of basil and spearmint
1 lemon (juice only)
Sesame seeds
Handful of flour
1 spoonful Command and Compel Oil

Directions

Make a strong infusion of basil and spearmint, straining the botanicals out or leaving them in for extra strength. Squeeze in the juice of one lemon, then sprinkle with sesame seeds and a handful of flour. Stir in a spoonful of Command and Compel Oil. Fill a bathtub with water, carry the bowl with the infusion to the tub, stand in the water, pour or ladle the infusion over your head, then sit down and soak.

About This Bath

A cleansing ritual bath that combines the purifying and luck-reversing power of basil, spearmint, lemon, and sesame with the commanding energy of Command and Compel Oil. Flour reinforces the encircling and protective aspect.
