🧪Law Keep Away Oil

◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Directions
Method 1 (Cold Infusion): Place botanicals in a jar and cover with oils. Steep overnight exposed to moonbeams if possible, then strain and bottle. Method 2 (Powder Method): Grind botanicals to a fine powder, place in bottle, and cover with the same oil blend. Use to dress vigil candles or rub on doorknobs.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities

◇Description
A protective Hoodoo condition oil blending High John the Conqueror, Courtcase root, hydrangea blossoms, and asafetida in an olive, castor, and jojoba oil base. Used to dress vigil candles and anoint doorknobs to repel legal authorities. ⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: Law Keep Away Oil is a Hoodoo condition oil. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

Law Keep Away Oil combines some of Hoodoo's most powerful legal and protective botanicals into a versatile condition oil designed to repel legal authorities, immigration officials, tax collectors, and any organization that holds power over you.

Method 1: Cold Infusion (Preferred)

  1. Place the following in a jar:
    • High John the Conqueror root
    • Courtcase root (galangal)
    • 1 tablespoon dried hydrangea blossoms
    • 1 teaspoon asafetida powder
  2. Cover with a blend of 2 parts olive oil + 1 part castor oil + 1 part jojoba oil.
  3. Allow to soak overnight, preferably leaving the jar exposed to moonbeams.
  4. Strain out the solids and bottle the infused oil.

Method 2: Powder Method

  1. Grind the same botanicals (High John, Courtcase root, hydrangea, asafetida) into a fine powder.
    • Note: High John is an extremely hard, solid root — grinding it is difficult. Use a strong mortar and pestle or reduce to small pieces first.
  2. Place the powder in a bottle.
  3. Cover with the same oil blend as above.

Uses

  • Vigil candles: Dress candles and burn them continuously until all danger has passed.
  • Doorknobs: Rub the oil onto doorknobs to encourage authority figures who touch them to turn away and leave.
