🍻Repairing Radler

A half beer, half soda drink with magical associations of love and friendship, perfect for bonding and smoothing over disagreements.

Quantities

  • 1 pint glassn- 12 oz beer (something with a light taste)n- 4 oz lemon-lime soda

Directions

Slowly pour the beer into the glass to avoid a head of foam.nnFill the glass with lemon-lime soda.nnShare with a friend while visualizing your bond strengthening and any disagreements dissolving.

History

This concoction was developed in Bavaria during the Roaring Twenties cycling craze. An ingenious bar owner created it when thirteen thousand cyclists showed up on a hot June day in 1922 and he was running out of beer.
