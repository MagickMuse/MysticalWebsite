🔥Anger into Action

◇Directions
Combine all essential oils. For Diffusion: Diffuse synergy during cleaning to help ground and calm intense emotions. For Massage: Combine all essential oils and add to 1 ounce sweet almond oil for self-massage after a good 'rage clean.'
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
• 1 drop cardamom essential oil • 1 drop ginger essential oil • 5 drops neroli essential oil • 2 drops petitgrain essential oil • 7 drops sandalwood essential oil • 1 ounce sweet almond oil (for massage version)
◇Description
An oil blend to transform fury into productive outlets, perfect for diffusing during a good rage clean.
