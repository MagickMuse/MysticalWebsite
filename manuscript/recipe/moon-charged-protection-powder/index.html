🌕Moon-Charged Protection Powder

◇Type
Powder
↗Astrology6
Cancer🌕Full MoonScorpio🌗Third Quarter Moon🌘Waning Crescent Moon🌖Waning Gibbous Moon
↗Correspondences3
✨Cleansing🌳Grounding🛡️Protection
↗Materials3
🥚Cascarilla Powder🥄Mortar & Pestle🧂Sea Salt
↗Plants1
Myrrh

A lunar-charged protective powder made from sea salt, myrrh resin, and eggshell, ground and consecrated under the full or waning moon. Use it to cast a protective barrier around your home, dress candles for protection spells, sprinkle on doorways and windowsills, or enchant lunar baths. For a simpler version, bless plain sea salt with the incantation alone and keep it on your altar or under your bed to ward off illness and ill will.

Quantities

Equal parts:
Sea salt (preferably)
Myrrh resin
Eggshell

Directions

Place equal parts sea salt, myrrh resin, and eggshell into the mortar. Grind counterclockwise with the pestle, charging the blend with cleansing energy while chanting:

"Charged by the moon as it wanes, to send unwanted energy away."

Pour the powder into a dish and carry it to where moonlight can reach it. Hold your hands over the dish and tune into the energy of its contents, visualizing the full moon shining down upon you and the powder. Speak aloud, filling in the blank with your specific intention:

"Lunar lady, shining bright, charge this powder for [intention] this full moon night."

Stir the powder clockwise with your finger, envisioning lunar energy infusing it. Leave it to charge in the moonlight overnight and collect it the following morning. Use as desired: cast clockwise around the perimeter of your property for a protective boundary, or sprinkle on doorways and windowsills.
