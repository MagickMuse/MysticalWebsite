💧Black Cat Oil

Closed Practice
Hoodoo
Type
Oil
Rituals8
🧲Basic Lodestone Luck Spell🕯️Black Cat Dual-Oil Luck Candle🕯️Black Cat Good Fortune and Hex-Breaking Candle✨Black Cat Oil Uncrossing🕯️Black Cat Uncrossing Candle⚖️End All Power Justice Candle🛡️Nine Pin Child Protection Mojo🕯️Seven-Eleven Black Cat Candle Ritual

A traditional Hoodoo luck and protection oil associated with the black cat's reputation for reversing bad luck and repelling hostile influences. Used to dress candles or anoint entrances against hexes and malign forces, often paired with Uncrossing Oil. ⚠️ Black Cat Oil is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

1/4 cup carrier oil (olive or sweet almond)
A few drops black cat's-eye or licorice-scented fragrance oil (traditional)
Optional: a pinch of black pepper or a small black feather for reinforcement

Directions

Combine the carrier oil with the fragrance oil in a small dark bottle, shaking to blend. If reinforcing with black pepper or a feather, add it to the bottle. Let the oil rest for at least 24 hours before use. Sprinkle at entrances or dress candles to reverse, repel, and remove malign influences.
