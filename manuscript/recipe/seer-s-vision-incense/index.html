🔮Seer's Vision Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences1
👤Spirits
Oils1
Clove Essential Oil

⚠️ Caution: Contains nutmeg, which can be toxic in large quantities. Use sparingly and do not ingest. Another formula to sharpen psychic powers.

Quantities

1 part Frankincense
1 part Sandalwood
1 part Cinnamon
1 part Nutmeg*
a few drops Orange oil
a few drops Clove oil

Directions

Grind the frankincense, sandalwood, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Add a few drops of orange oil and clove oil and blend thoroughly. Burn to sharpen psychic powers. Nutmeg can be toxic in large quantities; use sparingly and do not ingest.
