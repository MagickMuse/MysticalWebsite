Clary Sage Essential Oil

Use this blend to regain energy, focus, and insight. Raises vibrations while providing emotional support and wisdom.

Quantities

  • 5 drops vetiver essential oil for increased focus, success
  • 8 drops clary sage essential oil to combat depression and improve balance, wisdom
  • 10 drops ylang-ylang essential oil to raise vibrations and relax

Directions

Blend essential oils in 2 ml bottle. Dilute 1 drop of synergy in 2 ml bottle of carrier oil to anoint candles, charms, and talismans for emotional support and wisdom, and to raise vibrations of subject.
