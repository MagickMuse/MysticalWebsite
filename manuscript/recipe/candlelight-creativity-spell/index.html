🕯️Candlelight Creativity Spell

◇Directions
Light the incense and dim the lights to a soft, ambient setting. Anoint the candle with oil, pass it through the incense smoke, then set it in the candleholder behind the incense so the smoke rises between you and the flame. Close your eyes and visualize the moon rising above you, full and luminous, illuminating a dark landscape and lighting the way forward. Open your eyes and strike the match or lighter as you say aloud: "Bright inspiring moonlight, grant me creativity and insight." Light the candle. Breathing deeply, soften your gaze above the candlelight and smoke. Observe visions in their combined movements, or simply allow the light and incense to open your mind to the moon's guidance. Optionally, sprinkle a small pinch of ground cinnamon at a safe distance above the candle flame; it will produce brief sparks like stars, a magickal ignition of your own creative spark.
◇Quantities
Matches or lighter Jasmine or sandalwood incense Gold, orange, or yellow candle Moon Oil or magickal oil of choice Candleholder Ground cinnamon (optional)
◇Description
A simple candle and incense spell to invite creativity, inspiration, and new ideas under fire and air moon signs. Jasmine or sandalwood incense uplifts the mind while a dressed gold, orange, or yellow candle provides the spark. Best cast under Leo for creative inspiration, Gemini for new information, Aquarius for genius or visionary plans, or Sagittarius to rekindle hope and summon guiding philosophies. Also effective at the waxing crescent to plant ideas or the full moon to channel insight.
