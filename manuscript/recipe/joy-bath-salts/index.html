😊Joy Bath Salts

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences3
😊Happiness😊Joy💕Love
Entities2

Plants2
GeraniumOrange

Uplifting bath salts infused with citrus and floral oils to invoke happiness, joy, and emotional lightness. Associated with Hathor and the Charites.

Quantities

  • 2 cups Epsom salt
  • 1/2 cup sea salt
  • 10 drops bergamot essential oil
  • 8 drops geranium essential oil
  • 5 drops orange essential oil
  • Dried orange peel (optional)
  • Dried rose petals (optional)

Directions

Combine Epsom salt and sea salt in a large bowl. Add bergamot, geranium, and orange essential oils, mixing thoroughly to distribute the oils evenly. If desired, fold in dried orange peel and rose petals for visual appeal. Store in an airtight jar. To use, add 1/2 cup to warm bathwater and soak while focusing on feelings of joy and gratitude.
