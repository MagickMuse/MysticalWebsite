🌿Herb-Infused Dry Hair Treatment

Type
Oil
Correspondences2
💇Hair❤️‍🩹Healing
Materials1
Olive Oil
Plants4
German ChamomileRoman ChamomileRosemarySage

A warmed carrier or herb-infused oil applied to dry hair under a towel wrap before shampooing. Sage and rosemary suit dark hair; chamomile suits fair hair.

Quantities

Unsaturated carrier oil, warmed to just above body temperature
Sage and rosemary (for dark hair) OR chamomile (for fair hair)

Directions

Warm the oil gently. Apply the herb-infused or plain warm oil to the hair from roots to ends. Cover with plastic wrap and wrap a soft towel over that. Leave in for thirty minutes to two hours, then shampoo thoroughly.
