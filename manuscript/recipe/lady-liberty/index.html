Use this blend in spells for freedom, and to protect against ill health, hexes, and people.

Quantities

  • 2 drops Rose Geranium Essential Oil (Pelargonium graveolens) for healing, freedom, and mood
  • 5 drops Purification (Citrus reticulate) for inspiration, defense
  • 10 drops Ylang Ylang Essential Oil (Cananga odorata) for calm, harmony

Directions

Blend essential oils in a 2 ml bottle and diffuse 1 drop synergy per 100 ml water to dissolve threads of entanglement. Add 1 drop synergy to 2 ml carrier oil for anointing oil for candles, charms, charm bags, and more, for freedom, independence, inner strength, and the determination to be in control.
