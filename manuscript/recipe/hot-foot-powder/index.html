🧿Hot-Foot Powder

Type
Powder
Correspondences3
👻Exorcism🛡️Protection🧹Purification

An expelling powder used to drive unwanted people from a location, particularly effective when the target walks on it.

Quantities

  • Red pepper
  • Black pepper
  • Crushed hornets or red ants
  • Sulfur
  • Poppy seeds
  • Witch's salt (salt blackened with soot)
  • Angelica (optional, for blessing the target toward better circumstances)

Directions

Mix red and black pepper, crushed hornets or red ants, sulfur, poppy seeds, and witch's salt together thoroughly. If you wish to avoid causing harm and instead help the target move to better circumstances, add angelica to the mixture. Sprinkle the powder on the target's doorstep or office threshold where they will walk on it, sending it through the foot; then drop a small amount at each crossroads leading out of town, praying for the person to move each time you drop it. Alternatively, place the powder directly in the target's shoes. While applying, use this incantation:

By the Fire of Azazel!
I send thee to the Desert!
Barra! Edin Na Zu!
Barra! Edin Na Zu!
Barra! Edin Na Zu!

Some practitioners use the full Catholic Rite of Exorcism during application instead. This powder works best when the target physically walks on it or when it touches their feet; the combination of stinging ingredients creates the energetic push needed to drive someone away.
