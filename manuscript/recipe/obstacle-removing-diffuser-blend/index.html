🚧Obstacle-Removing Diffuser Blend

Type
Oil
Correspondences3
😊Happiness🛡️Protection🧘🏻‍♀️Spirituality
Oils2
Bay Laurel Essential OilFrankincense Essential Oil
Plants3
Bay LaurelFrankincenseMyrrh

A diffuser oil blend to dissolve lingering obstacles keeping you from your goal. Bay leaf removes barriers to success, frankincense lifts spirits, and myrrh calms fears about the future.

Quantities

  • 5 drops bay leaf essential oil
  • 10 drops frankincense essential oil
  • 4 drops myrrh essential oil

Directions

Blend the synergy in a dark bottle and top with an orifice reducer. Cap, shake, and let sit if possible. To diffuse, add 1 drop per 100 ml water for small spaces. Think about your current situation and meditate if you already do so. See your obstacles dissolving while enjoying the spicy fragrance of this blend.
