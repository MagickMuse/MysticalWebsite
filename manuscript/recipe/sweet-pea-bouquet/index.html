Sweet Pea Bouquet

◇Type
Oil
↗Oils3
Jasmine AbsoluteNeroli Essential OilYlang Ylang Essential Oil

A floral blend capturing the romantic, delicate scent of sweet pea flowers. Used in love and friendship recipes.

Quantities

  • Neroli Oil
  • Ylang-Ylang Oil
  • Jasmine Oil
  • Benzoin Oil
    Combine in roughly equal parts.

Directions

Combine all oils in a dark glass bottle. Cap and shake gently to blend. Allow to marry for at least 24 hours before use.

This bouquet recreates the sweet, romantic scent of sweet pea flowers. Use in any formula calling for sweet pea oil.

Store in a cool, dark place.

This floral blend approximates the delicate, romantic fragrance of sweet pea flowers, which cannot be captured as a genuine essential oil.

Magical Uses

Love: The gentle, romantic nature of this blend makes it ideal for love workings focused on tender affection rather than passionate encounters.

Friendship: Excellent for strengthening bonds of friendship and attracting new friends into your life.

Component Properties

Neroli provides floral brightness and calming energy.

Ylang-Ylang adds sweetness and love associations.

Jasmine contributes heady floral depth and attraction energy.

Benzoin grounds the blend and adds warm, vanilla-like sweetness.
