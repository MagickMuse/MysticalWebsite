✨Algiers Powder

◇Closed Practice

◇Type

📄Algiers Petition Spell

A classic New Orleans gamblers' formula powder named not for Algeria but for Algiers, Louisiana, the community across the Mississippi River where New Orleans Voodooists settled during 19th-century persecution. Provides gambling luck, increased money, and luck in love. Base is arrowroot, rice powder, or cornstarch (no talcum). ⚠️ This is a New Orleans Voodoo formula. Voodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Cinnamon sticks
Dried patchouli leaves
Vanilla beans
Optional base: arrowroot powder, rice powder, or cornstarch

Directions

Grind cinnamon sticks, dried patchouli leaves, and vanilla beans together with a mortar and pestle into a fine powder. Blend with arrowroot powder, rice powder, or cornstarch if a smoother base is desired. Carry the powder in a conjure bag and sprinkle or rub as needed.
