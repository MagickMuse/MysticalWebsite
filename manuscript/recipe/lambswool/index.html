🎃Lambswool

◇Directions
Roast apples until soft and fragrant, then crush them. Add the crushed apples to milk. Add hot spiced ale, cider, and/or wine according to preference. Stir in sugar to taste. If desired, add bits of toast to the drink and serve warm. Various recipes exist, so experiment with different proportions and combinations of liquids to find your preferred version.

Etymology

The name is believed to be a corruption of the Irish Gaelic "La Mas Nbhal" or "Feast of apple gathering." Pronounced "Lammas-ool," it eventually evolved into "lambs wool."

Variations

Various recipes exist, all based on roasted, crushed apples combined with warming liquids. Experiment with different proportions and combinations of milk, ale, cider, and wine to find your preferred version.
