🚫Spirits Depart Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences1
👻Exorcism
Oils1
Castor Oil
Plants3
CalamintPeonySpearmint

⚠️ CAUTION: Original recipe calls for castor beans, which are poisonous. Use castor oil as a substitute. Burn outdoors to drive away evil spirits and vain imaginings.

Quantities

  • 1 part Calamint
  • 1 part Peony
  • 1 part Spearmint
  • A few drops Castor oil

Directions

Grind the calamint, peony root, and spearmint together. Add a few drops of castor oil and blend. Burn outdoors to drive away evil spirits and dispel vain imaginings.

Caution

The original recipe calls for 1/4 part castor beans, which contain ricin and are extremely poisonous. Substitute a few drops of castor oil instead.
