🧈Herbal-Infused Blessed Butter

◇Type
Food
↗Materials1
Olive Oil
↗Plants2
ParsleySage

A kitchen witch butter infused with fresh sage and parsley in olive oil. Sage brings clearing and cleansing energy while parsley represents gratitude. Use on ritual breads, especially those you bake yourself.

Quantities

1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
1 16-ounce jar with lid
2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh sage
2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh parsley
1 1/3 cups heavy cream
Dash of salt
1 spatula
Cheesecloth (optional)

Directions

Begin the night before by pouring the extra-virgin olive oil into the jar. Add the fresh sage and parsley; breathe in as you form your intention, taking in the fragrance of the fresh herbs. Close the lid and allow the herbs to steep in the oil overnight. The next day, add the heavy cream and salt so the jar is about three-quarters full. Shake vigorously for 30 minutes and check the consistency; the cream will thicken and the fats will eventually separate. Strain off the buttermilk by-product, pressing the butter against the sides of the jar with a spatula or straining through cheesecloth to remove excess moisture. Transfer to another container or keep in the same jar. Keeps refrigerated for one to two weeks.
