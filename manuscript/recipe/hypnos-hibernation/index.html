😴Hypnos Hibernation

◇Directions
Blend all oils in a dark-colored glass container. Mix one drop of synergy per 100 ml water. Best if enjoyed with a caffeine-free tea and quiet reflection. Avoid using cell phones or tablets before bed, as the screen lights can disturb sleep cycles.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
10 drops bergamot essential oil (Citrus bergamia) 5 drops Roman chamomile essential oil (Chamaemelum nobile) 7 drops patchouli essential oil (Pogostemon cablin) Dark-colored glass container Water for diffusing (1 drop synergy per 100 ml water)
◇Description
Sleep and the realm of dreams await, with the help of the Greek god of rest. Diffuse this blend an hour before bed to have magickal sleep and calming dreams.
