✨Energy Cleansing Spritz

◇Type
Spray
↗Correspondences4
✨Cleansing⚡Energy🛡️Protection🧹Purification
↗Materials2
🧂Salts🔫Spray Bottle
↗Oils1
Sandalwood Essential Oil
↗Plants2
JuniperSandalwood

A refreshing hydrosol spray for cleansing and lifting energy in any space, combining juniper for protection, peppermint for purification, and sandalwood for calming.

Quantities

1 teaspoon table salt
3 drops sandalwood essential oil
1 ounce juniper hydrosol
1 ounce peppermint hydrosol
2-ounce spray bottle

Directions

Add 1 teaspoon table salt to a clean 2-ounce spray bottle. Add 3 drops of sandalwood essential oil and mix well with the salt. Add 1 ounce juniper hydrosol and 1 ounce peppermint hydrosol, leaving room for the spray pump. Cap the bottle and shake well to mix. Shake before each use and spray anywhere the energy needs a lift.
