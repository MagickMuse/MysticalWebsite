Pine

A magical infused sugar made with fresh pine tips, perfect for adding to teas, cocktails, or baked goods for vitality and friendship.

Quantities

  • 1 cup fresh pine tips
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • Pint jar with lid

Directions

Place 1 cup of fresh pine tips in a pint jar. Cover completely with granulated sugar, using about 2 cups. Seal the jar and let it sit in a cool, dark place for 2 weeks, shaking occasionally. The sugar will absorb the aromatic oils and flavor from the pine. Strain out the pine tips (or leave them in for stronger flavor) and store the infused sugar in a sealed container. Use in teas, cocktails, baking, or any recipe where you want to add pine's magickal properties of friendship, harmony, and vitality.
