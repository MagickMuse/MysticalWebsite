♊Gemini Incense

◇Directions
Burn as a personal altar incense or household incense to increase your powers.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
2 parts Gum Mastic, 1 part Citron (or 1 part mixed Orange and Lemon peel), 1/2 part Mace
◇Description
A personal altar or household incense for those born under Gemini or seeking to invoke Gemini energies. Use to increase your powers and mental agility.
