🍂Mabon and the Bounty of Harvest

Mabon

A simple harvest dish of roasted root vegetables perfect for Mabon celebrations. Root vegetables, olive oil, rosemary, garlic, and orange come together to honor the season's bounty. Ideal for sharing with ritual companions.

Quantities

  • Selected roots (sweet potatoes, yams, beets, carrots, parsnips, white potatoes), sliced and chopped
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Needles from a sprig of fresh rosemary
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • Orange slices with rind

Directions

In a medium bowl, toss the prepared roots with olive oil, rosemary needles, minced garlic, and sea salt until everything is evenly coated. Line a baking pan with parchment paper and spread the coated roots in a single layer. Top with a few thinly sliced orange rounds, rind and all. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes. Serve and share with your ritual companions.

About This Recipe

This roasted root dish draws on the deep earthen energies of Mabon, celebrating the second harvest through grounding vegetables pulled from the soil. The warmth of rosemary and garlic and the brightness of orange bring balance to the plate, echoing the equinox's equilibrium of light and dark. Share with those gathered for ritual to honor the communal spirit of the harvest season.
