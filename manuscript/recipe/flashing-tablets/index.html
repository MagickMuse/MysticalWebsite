Instructions for crafting elemental flashing tablets for meditation using complementary color pairs. Note: This is a craft project, not an incense.

Quantities

  • Square wooden boards or canvas (12 inches square recommended)
  • Acrylic paint in complementary colors:
  • Orange and Violet (Air)
  • Red and Green (Fire)
  • White and Black (Water)
  • Yellow and Blue (Earth)
  • Clear varnish (optional, for protection)
  • Paintbrushes

Directions

  1. Prepare your surface (wood or canvas board). Paint the background square in the appropriate color. Allow to dry completely. Paint the inner symbol (circle, triangle, crescent, or square) in the complementary color. Allow to dry completely. Optional: Apply clear varnish for protection and an attractive glossy surface.
    Elemental Tablet Designs:
  2. Air Tablet: Paint an orange circle inside a violet square
  3. Fire Tablet: Paint a red triangle inside a green square
  4. Water Tablet: Paint a white crescent moon inside a black square
  5. Earth Tablet: Paint a yellow square inside a larger blue square
    The outer square in each design represents the Earth element, containing and grounding the elemental energy on the physical plane.

Flashing tablets are meditation tools created from complementary color pairs painted on square boards. When gazed upon, they create optical illusions that induce meditative states.

Materials Needed

  • Square wooden boards or canvas (12 inches square recommended, though smaller sizes work)
  • Acrylic paint in complementary colors
  • Clear varnish (optional, for protection and glossy finish)
  • Paintbrushes

Elemental Tablet Designs

Create one tablet for each element:

Air Tablet: Paint an orange circle inside a violet square

Fire Tablet: Paint a red triangle inside a green square

Water Tablet: Paint a white crescent moon inside a black square

Earth Tablet: Paint a yellow square inside a larger blue square

The outer square in each design represents the Earth element, containing and grounding the elemental energy on the physical plane.

Construction Instructions

  1. Prepare your surface (wood or canvas board)
  2. Paint the background square in the appropriate color
  3. Allow to dry completely
  4. Paint the inner symbol (circle, triangle, crescent, or square) in the complementary color
  5. Allow to dry completely
  6. Optional: Apply clear varnish for protection and an attractive glossy surface

Color Pairs (Complementary Colors)

  • Orange and Violet (Air)
  • Red and Green (Fire)
  • White and Black (Water)
  • Yellow and Blue (Earth)

These complementary pairs create the optical flashing effect when gazed upon during meditation.

Usage

See the Flashing Tablet Meditation ritual for instructions on using your completed tablets.
