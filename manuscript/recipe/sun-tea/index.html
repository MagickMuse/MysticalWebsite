☀️Sun Tea

Type
Drink
Correspondences2
😊Happiness😊Joy
Plants2
DandelionRosehip

A refreshing tea brewed naturally in sunlight, served as iced tea. Can also be made with fruit flavors.

Quantities

  • 3 bags English Teatime
  • 1 tbs Rose hips
  • 1 tbs Dandelion root

Directions

Place tea bags and herbs in a clear glass jug of water. Set in the sunlight and allow the sun to brew it naturally. Serve as iced tea.
