🪔Evil Eye: Moroccan Incense (Extra Strength)

Type

A complex Moroccan protective incense blend built on benzoin as its base. Eight ingredients are combined, ground to a fine powder, and burned on charcoal to fumigate a person or space against the Evil Eye.

Quantities

  • Alum
  • Benzoin
  • Coriander seeds
  • Garlic
  • Gum ammoniac
  • Harmel (Syrian rue)
  • Juniper
  • Sulfur
  • Lit charcoal disc (for burning)

Directions

Gather all ingredients. Combine them in a mortar and pestle, mashing and grinding until reduced to a fine, uniform powder. Sprinkle the powder over lit charcoal and use the smoke to fumigate the affected person or area.
