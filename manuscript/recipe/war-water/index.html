A water of Mars that draws on the power of iron. Famous for potent hexes, its true character is aggressive power rather than malevolence: the caster decides its direction, and it also removes hexes and gives strong spiritual protection. ⚠️ War Water is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Water
Iron nails or other iron objects

Directions

Leave iron nails or other iron objects sitting in water until they rust, charging the water with the energy of Mars. Store the finished rusty water in a dark bottle.

About This Recipe

War Water is also called Mars Water or Iron Water. The rust left behind by iron soaking in water carries the energy of Mars into the formula.

Uses

War Water serves works of domination, protection, reversal, and cursing. Mars, ruler of iron, is the patron of war and warriors, which is why War Water also goes by the name Water of Mars. In its protective form, the formula keeps those serving in the military safe from harm.

Notes

Store War Water in a dark bottle.
