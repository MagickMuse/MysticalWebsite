Recipe Grimoire

🍷Vermouth

◇Directions
Allow 1 1/2 cups white wine to simmer with 1 cup sugar and 1 cup herbal mixture for 10 minutes. Do not boil. Put the lid on to preserve the volatile oils. After 10 minutes, remove from heat and allow to sit for an additional 22 minutes. Place the remaining cup of sugar into a small saucepan with 4 tbsp. water, just barely enough to partially dissolve it. Turn heat on high and gently swirl the mixture. Do not over stir; we want the sugar to caramelize without burning. Cook until thickened and ready to pour. Pour the caramel onto parchment paper on a cookie sheet and set aside to cool. Tip: place the baking sheet onto a cooling rack so the counter does not hold in the heat. After 20 minutes, cover a wire strainer with two layers of cheesecloth and strain the infused wine into a bowl. Wring the cheesecloth by hand to extract more liquid. Compost the herbs. Once the caramel is cool to the touch, break it into small pieces and place back in the saucepan. Pour infused liquid over the caramel, cover, and heat on low until caramel dissolves. Pour into a 1000 ml bottle and top with remaining white wine. Cap, shake, and label. Store in the refrigerator for up to three months. Enjoy with cucumber water, seltzer, added to vodka for a martini, or by itself on a hot summer night. Suggested botanicals: Angelica (acceptance, protection), Bay (harmony, hex breaking), Black peppercorns (protection, purification), Calamus (commanding, peace), Chamomile (luck, love), Cinnamon (purification, prosperity), Cloves (hex breaking, protection), Coriander (divination, secrets), Fennel (protection, warding), Ginger (power, money), Grains of paradise (wealth, luck), Juniper (protection, dreams), Marjoram (protection, love), Myrrh (meditation, spirituality), Orange peel (love, prosperity), Rosemary (courage, wisdom), Sage (cleansing), Star anise (love, fidelity), Vanilla (passion, relaxation), Wormwood (psychic stimulation).
◇Type
Infusion
◇Quantities
- 1 bottle dry white wine - 2 cups sugar - 1 cup dried herbal blend (your choice of botanicals) - 2/3 cup brandy - 4 tbsp. water - Small pot with lid - Small saucepan - Cookie sheet - Parchment paper - Cheesecloth - Wire strainer - Bowl - 1000 ml bottle
◇Description
A homemade fortified wine infused with botanicals and sweetened with caramel. Blend your own herbal mixture to create a personalized magical aperitif. Store in refrigerator for up to three months.
