🍾Counter Craft Witch Bottle

Type
Talisman

A counter-magick ward hidden inside the home or in the space of the one sending unwanted energy. Witch bottles have a long apotropaic history and can be tailored to the practitioner's specific need.

Quantities

  • Dark glass bottle (cobalt, amber, or opaque clay) with lid
  • Wax for sealing
  • Herbs and materials chosen by intention:
  • Scarcity breaking: coins, metallic objects (e.g. gold four-leaf clover), dried mint-family herbs
  • Psychic clarity: quartz crystal, salt, fennel seeds
  • Mending a love rift: ripped red felt stitched together, rose quartz, rosebuds, needle

Directions

Fill the bottle with your chosen herbs and materials. Seal the lid or cork permanently with wax. Carry the bottle to its secret location, speak the incantation, and hide it where it will remain undisturbed. Set your intention from a place of positivity.

About Counter Craft

Witches have long used counter craft to protect against unwanted energy. Apotropaic discoveries hidden inside old houses include mummified feline remains, single shoes, horseshoes, animal skulls, and ritualistic sigils. Witch bottles continue this tradition in a form that is both practical and adaptable.

Incantation

Speak as you place the sealed bottle in its hidden location:

Sacred magick,
Secret charm,
Undiscovered,
Do no harm.
Placed with power,
Subtle yet strong,
Upon my hand
Will come no wrong.
By all the power
Of three times three,
As I will,
So must it be.

Note

If you don't have an opaque bottle, wash and paint the outside of an empty spice bottle. The bottle may be hidden in your own home as a ward, or in the home of the person from whom the unwanted energy originates.
