🍷Sabbat Wine and Summer Cordial

◇Directions
Begin at the end of March. Bruise the hyacinth blooms and steep them in 1 gallon of alcohol. At the end of April, add the jonquil petals. Follow with the wallflower petals and finally the lily of the valley flowers as each comes into bloom. Add the orris powder, mace, and orange or lemon essence. Stir the macerating mixture daily for a week after all ingredients have been added. Distill the liquid slowly using a bain-marie (double boiler) method. Bottle and seal.
◇Type
Potion
◇Quantities
For the summer cordial: 6 oz hyacinth blooms; 1/4 lb violet petals; 1/4 lb wallflower petals; 1/4 lb jonquil petals; 1 oz orris root powder; 1/2 oz powdered mace; 2 oz orange or lemon essence; 1/4 lb lily of the valley flowers; 1 gallon grain alcohol.
◇Description
The sabbat cup may be filled with wine or a specially prepared cordial depending on the season. Red wine is traditional for most sabbats; it may be mulled in winter with herbs and spices, or replaced with hard cider or brandy warmed with cinnamon and clove-stuck oranges. For summer sabbats, a complex floral cordial may be distilled from the first blooms of spring.
