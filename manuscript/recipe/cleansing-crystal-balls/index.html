🔮Cleansing Crystal Balls

◇Type
Wash
↗Correspondences1
✨Cleansing
↗Plants2
AniseMugwort

A crystal ball or scrying glass must be kept in pristine condition, both physically and spiritually. Dust obscures the surface; accumulated negative spiritual residue can cloud or warp what is seen within.

Quantities

Choose one or more: spring water and vinegar; Holy Water, Marie Laveau Water, or Notre Dame Water; a water-based infusion of dried mugwort and/or anise seeds; mugwort hydrosol; flower essence remedies as desired (optional addition to any of the above)

Directions

Select one of the following cleansing solutions and apply to the crystal ball: spring water blended with vinegar; a charged water (Holy Water, Marie Laveau Water, or Notre Dame Water are especially recommended); a water-based herbal infusion made with dried mugwort and/or anise seeds; or mugwort hydrosol. Flower essence remedies may be added to any of the above. Mugwort flower essence amplifies the crystal ball's scrying power. Dr. Bach's Rescue Remedy or a comparable emergency formula helps process and release any disturbing or traumatic visions that have been absorbed.
