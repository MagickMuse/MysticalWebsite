🍋Summertime Cool-Down Switchel

◇Directions
Chop ginger into chunks and add to saucepan with water. Bring the water to a boil and boil for 5 minutes. This is called a decoction when you boil roots and barks that are too hard to make as a tisane. While heating, stir in honey until dissolved. Once it has boiled for 5 minutes, remove from heat and cool to room temperature. Scoop out the ginger. If you are making a Ginger Bug, toss the sugary ginger into the fermenting crock; if not, compost. Once this mixture is cooled, stir in the vinegar. If the water is too hot when you add the vinegar, it will kill off the beneficial bacteria. Add the lemon juice. Place in a pitcher and cool.
◇Type
Drink
◇Quantities
- 1 thumb ginger, chopped - 4 cups water - 1 cup local honey (if you commute to work, try to find local honey closer to the office) - 1 cup apple cider vinegar (with Mother if you want probiotic benefits) - Juice of 1 lemon (about 4 tablespoons)
◇Description
A refreshing ginger and lemon switchel perfect for calming tempers and soothing egos at summer gatherings. Serve at a picnic to sweeten temperaments in the home or office while keeping everyone healthy. The sweetener can be charmed with intent to calm tempers and soothe egos.
