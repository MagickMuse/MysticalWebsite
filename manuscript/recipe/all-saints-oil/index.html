🕯️All Saints Oil Candle Spell📿All Saints Oil Clover Charm📿All Saints Oil Cotton Ball Carry

A similar formula to Seven African Powers Oil, used in similar fashion to invoke the blessings of the Seven Powers. Seven botanical ingredients correspond to each of the Seven Powers.

Quantities

  • Cinnamon
  • Tonka beans
  • Patchouli
  • Vetiver
  • Lavender
  • Gardenia
  • Mugwort
  • Sweet almond oil
  • Jojoba oil

Directions

Grind and powder the seven botanicals: cinnamon, tonka beans, patchouli, vetiver, lavender, gardenia, and mugwort. Cover with sweet almond and jojoba oils. Bottle the blend. Use in the same fashion as Seven African Powers Oil to invoke the blessings and protection of the Seven Powers.
