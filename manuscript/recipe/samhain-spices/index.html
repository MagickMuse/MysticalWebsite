Recipe Grimoire

🍂Samhain Spices

◇Directions
Combine all spices in a mortar and pestle and blend thoroughly. Transfer to a small pot for storage. To use as an ancestor charm, place one teaspoon in the center of a black cloth and tie closed with a silver thread. Use as incense, as a coffee additive, in cooking, or as an altar offering at Samhain.
◇Type
Powder
◇Quantities
1/4 tsp allspice 1/4 tsp dried ginger 1/8 tsp dried and ground clove 1/2 tsp nutmeg 1/2 tsp cinnamon
◇Description
A warming autumnal spice blend carrying the energy of crone goddess power and the dark half of the year. Versatile enough to be burned as loose incense, stirred into coffee grounds before brewing, used as a culinary spice blend, or bundled into a black cloth charm as a Samhain ancestor offering.
