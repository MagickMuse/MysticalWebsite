🍵Purification Brew: Lemon & Chamomile

◇Directions
Brew as a standard infusion and drink before beginning ritual. Optionally add a splash of fresh lemon juice and a teaspoon of honey or sugar to taste. Sugar is traditionally used by Peruvian shamans in purification ceremonies.
◇Type
Drink
◇Quantities
- 1 part Lemon Verbena - 1 part dried Lemon peel - 1 part Chamomile
◇Description
A light, citrus-infused tea for internal purification before ritual work.
