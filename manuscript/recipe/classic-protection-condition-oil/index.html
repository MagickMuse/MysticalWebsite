A classic Hoodoo condition oil whose name states its promise outright: it surrounds the user with an aura of protection. ⚠️ Classic Protection Condition Oil is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Powdered Southern John root (galangal)
Magnetic sand
Oil

Directions

Blend the protection powder base of powdered Southern John root and magnetic sand into oil. A powder of dragon's blood, sandalwood, and salt added to oil also serves as Protection Oil.

Notes

Only the protection powder base of this formula is recorded here. Related formulas: Protection Powder.
