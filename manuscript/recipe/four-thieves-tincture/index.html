🧪Four Thieves Tincture

Closed Practice

A legendary herbal vinegar with deep roots in folk magick, hoodoo, and European witchcraft tradition. Four Thieves Vinegar is used for cleansing, protection, banishing, and reversals. It is named for a historical legend in which four thieves protected themselves from plague by soaking cloths in an herbal vinegar blend. Magickally, it is one of the most versatile and potent tools in the cleansing arsenal.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula has roots in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

Vinegar (apple cider or red wine): enough to fully submerge herbs
Rosemary, sage, thyme, rue, lavender: a generous handful each
Garlic: a few cloves
Steeping time: 4+ weeks

Directions

  1. Combine a generous handful of rosemary, sage, thyme, rue, lavender, and garlic in a clean jar.
  2. Cover completely with apple cider vinegar or red wine vinegar.
  3. Seal the jar and leave it to steep for at least four weeks, shaking daily.
  4. Strain out the herbs and store the liquid in a sealed bottle.
    Use as a floor wash, add to cleansing baths, sprinkle at thresholds for protection, or incorporate into reversal and banishing workings.
