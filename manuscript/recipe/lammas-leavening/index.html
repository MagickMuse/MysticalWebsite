A first harvest (Lammas/Lughnasadh) diffuser blend for increasing peace, luck, and harmony during observances or gratitude rituals.

Quantities

  • 15 drops neroli essential oil to sedate, release negative emotional attachments, shield, reduce impact of trauma, relieve stress
  • 15 drops benzoin essential oil for inspiration, harmony, love, memory, peace, purification
  • 1 drop cinnamon essential oil for good luck, protection, lifted spirits
  • 35 drops vanilla absolute for mental powers, relaxation, peace

Directions

Traditionally breads are prepared for first harvest and a portion is left out as thanks for a bountiful harvest. Diffuse this warm scent for increasing peace, luck, and harmony of home. Anoint a candle with this blend for rituals of gratitude any time of year. Look for vanilla absolute or vanilla CO2 for purity standards; the oil is expensive and often adulterated. True vanilla will not smell like sugar cookie air freshener; it smells more like an old library, as when paper ages it produces an aldehyde chemical called vanillin, which is named for the scent of natural vanilla. When people love the smell of old books, they are admiring the scent of vanilla. Add 2 drops of synergy to a 10 ml roller bottle and fill with carrier oil for anointing candles for magick involving family harmony, religious renewal, and worship.
