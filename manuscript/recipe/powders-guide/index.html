Powders Guide

Powders are ground herbs sprinkled to release their energies. Think of them as incense that is never burned, or a sachet that is never worn. Many ritual uses of powders are unique to this form.

Making Powders

Grind herbs as finely as possible using a mortar and pestle. While grinding, visualize your magickal goal. The physical process of mortaring creates an opportunity to connect deeply with the plant materials and program them with intent.

Once powdered, mix the herbs together and empower the blend before use.

Using Magickal Powders

Scattering

The simplest method. Sprinkle powders when and where their energies are needed.

Circle Casting

Sprinkle powder in a circle around yourself, beginning and ending in the East, moving clockwise. Sit within this circle to absorb the powder's energies.

Crystal Work

Sprinkle appropriate powder around crystals on the altar to amplify their power.

Candle Magick

Scatter powders around candles before lighting to boost their energies.

Altar Preparation

Sprinkle ritually appropriate powder over the altar before spellwork.

Symbolic Shapes

Sprinkle powder on the altar in specific shapes as focal points for visualization:

  • Protection powder in pentagrams
  • Love powder in hearts
  • Psychic powder in circles

The shape need not remain intact for long to be effective.

Powders for Capsules

Beyond scattering, powdered herbs can also be used as filler for capsules, making them easy to ingest in measured doses. This is a common method for taking healing herbs for a specific ailment. For example, powdered Valerian root is widely used as a sleep aid.

Caution

Some powders, particularly those containing dragon's blood, will stain carpets, bedsheets, clothing, and other materials. Be mindful when sprinkling.
