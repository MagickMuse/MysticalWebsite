🛡️None Shall Pass

Type

A preventive shield against bane work, hexing, jinxes, curses, and the evil eye.

Quantities

  • 4 drops frankincense essential oil (Boswellia carteri) for hex breaking
  • 2 drops bergamot essential oil (Citrus bergamia) for commanding strength
  • 3 drops lemon balm essential oil (Melissa officinalis) for success, happiness
  • 2 drops oakmoss absolute (Evernia furfuracea) for hex breaking, grounding

Directions

Mix all oils, and diffuse 1 drop synergy in 100 ml water to shield a home, sacred space, or office.

To translate this delectable-smelling oil into a perfume that can be used to keep away jinxes, curses, and the evil eye, place 5 drops of synergy in a 10 ml bottle and fill the rest of the way with carrier oil. Anoint candles, charm bags, and charms with diluted blend for increased magickal success.

About This Blend

Nothing is getting through this shield of allies.

The best way to keep from being the target of baneful magick is not to be a jerk. Barring that, people who attempt to change the world for the better collect grumpy people who want the world to stay the way it is.

Uses

  • Diffuser: Shield a home, sacred space, or office
  • Perfume: Place 5 drops synergy in 10 ml carrier oil to keep away jinxes, curses, and the evil eye
  • Anointing: Apply to candles, charm bags and other charms for increased magickal success
