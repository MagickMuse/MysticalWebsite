🛡️Hex-Removing Bath

Earthy bath salts to ground and return any misdeeds to the earth, designed to harm none.

Quantities

SYNERGY:
4 drops angelica essential oil
8 drops clove essential oil
8 drops ginger essential oil
10 drops lime essential oil
10 drops vetiver essential oil

BATH SALTS:
1/2 teaspoon synergy (about 50 drops)
2 cups Epsom salts
1 cup sea salt
1/4 cup cornstarch
Optional: 4 drops food coloring

Directions

Mix all of the essential oils in a glass vial to create the synergy: 4 drops angelica essential oil, 8 drops clove essential oil, 8 drops ginger essential oil, 10 drops lime essential oil, and 10 drops vetiver essential oil. Set the synergy aside. Then mix the bath salts: 2 cups Epsom salts, 1 cup sea salt, 1/4 cup cornstarch, and optional 4 drops food coloring. Pour the synergy (about 1/2 teaspoon or 50 drops) into the bath salts, and stir to combine. Blend well, and store in an airtight jar, preferably in dark glass or clear glass kept away from sunlight. Use in a full bath, and visualize any curses or maledictions dissolving in the light of your radiant bath.
