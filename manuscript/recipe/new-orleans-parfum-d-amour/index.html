🌹New Orleans Parfum d'Amour

◇Directions
Blend Florida Water, rose water or hydrosol, and cinnamon hydrosol together. Gently warm the blend. Dissolve honey into the warm liquid, stirring to distribute. Allow it to cool completely. Transfer to a bottle and wear as a fragrance.
◇Quantities
Florida Water Rose water or hydrosol Cinnamon hydrosol Honey (to taste)
◇Description
A New Orleans love perfume blending Florida Water, rose water or hydrosol, and cinnamon hydrosol, warmed together and finished with honey dissolved in. Worn as a fragrance.
