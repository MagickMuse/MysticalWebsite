🖐️Henna and Adorning the Skin

A traditional henna body paste adapted for witchcraft use, combining a tamarind, rose, coffee, and lime decoction with powdered red henna, eucalyptus oil, and okra gel. The finished paste is applied to the skin through a fine-tipped craft bottle to create temporary tattoos that last up to three weeks. Henna has documented antiseptic properties, making this preparation both magickal and medicinal. The design work begins on parchment before being transferred to skin.

Quantities

  • 4 slices tamarind fruit or 3–4 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 3/4 cup fresh or dried red rose petals
  • 1/3 cup coffee grounds
  • 1 dried lime (prepared 2–3 weeks in advance)
  • 1 quart spring water
  • 4 oz dried and powdered red henna
  • 3 drops eucalyptus essential oil
  • 2 fresh okra pods
    Equipment: medium saucepan, small glass jar, strainer lined with cheesecloth, parchment paper and pen, scissors, nylon stocking, small mixing bowl, small wooden spoon, Jacquard craft bottles with 2mm tip, straight pin, cotton and plastic wrap

Directions

Combine the tamarind, rose petals, coffee grounds, and dried lime in a saucepan. Cover with spring water and bring to a low simmer. Maintain a covered decoction for at least 30 minutes, then remove from heat and cool to room temperature. Strain through cheesecloth into a glass jar, squeezing the cloth to capture remaining liquid.

Stretch a cut nylon stocking between two chair legs to form a sieve above parchment paper. Sift the henna powder through the stocking, discarding any stems and coarse cellulose. Collect the sifted powder in a clean, dry mixing bowl.

Add the cooled decoction one tablespoon at a time, mixing until the paste is thick and lotion-like, moist but not runny. Stir in the eucalyptus oil. Slice the okra pods and press them through cheesecloth to extract the mucilaginous liquid, then fold it into the paste. Transfer to craft bottles by squeezing air out, inserting the bottle mouth into the paste, and releasing to draw it in.

Trace your sigils and symbols on parchment to practice. Apply to skin at the palms and soles for strongest color. Cover with cotton and plastic wrap to preserve the design while drying. Leave on as long as possible, overnight for the deepest result. Brush off dry paste; do not wash. The design will darken as it oxidizes and lasts approximately 3 weeks.
