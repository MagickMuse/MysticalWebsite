🦅Time to Leave the Nest

↗Plants4

Diffuse this blend to empower and encourage priests and priestesses with coven hiving, and bestow children leaving home with power and grace.

Quantities

8 drops bergamot essential oil
3 drops Texas cedarwood essential oil
1 drop vetiver essential oil
6 drops ylang-ylang essential oil
Dark-colored glass container
Water for diffusing (1 drop synergy per 100 ml water)

Directions

Blend all oils in a dark-colored glass container. Mix one drop of synergy per 100 ml water for diffusion.
