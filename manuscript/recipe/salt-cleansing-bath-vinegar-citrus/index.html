Salt Cleansing Bath: Vinegar & Citrus

◇Directions
Dissolve at least one cup of salt in a warm bath. Add at least one cup of vinegar. Slice a lemon and a lime in half, squeeze the juice into the water, and add the spent rinds as well. Rub the squeezed fruit halves over your body as you soak.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
- At least 1 cup of salt - At least 1 cup of vinegar - 1 lemon - 1 lime
◇Description
An enhanced salt cleansing bath that layers in vinegar and citrus for extra purifying action. The lemon and lime halves are used directly on the skin to reinforce the cleanse.
