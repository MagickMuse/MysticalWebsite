🌫️Magical Anti-Plague "Perfume"

◇Type
Incense
↗Correspondences2

A 16th-century European fumigation formula built on a Chypre base, blending aromatic botanicals and resins into small cakes that are burned to perfume, fumigate, and magically cleanse a room and its inhabitants. Note: the word 'perfume' derives from 'per fumum' — through fire — and this formula would today be described as incense.

Quantities

Powdered Chypre (as base)
Aloes wood
Ambrette seed
Cloves
Elder
Frankincense
Juniper
Mace
Mastic pearls
Myrrh
Nutmeg
Rue
Wormwood

Note: Adjust proportions until the blend holds together. If too dry, add more of the resins.

Directions

Begin with powdered Chypre, then add all remaining ingredients: aloes wood, ambrette seed, cloves, elder, frankincense, juniper, mace, mastic pearls, myrrh, nutmeg, rue, and wormwood. Blend all ingredients well using a mortar and pestle. The ingredients are a combination of dried botanicals and gum resins; once blended, a paste should form. Adjust proportions until the material holds together, and if too dry, add more of the resins. Form the blend into little cakes. Burn to perfume, fumigate, and magickally cleanse a room and its inhabitants.
