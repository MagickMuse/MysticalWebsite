🎃Prosperity Pumpkin Spell

◇Directions
Carve the pumpkin with a removable top, hollowing it out into a jack-o'-lantern shape. Set aside the seeds for roasting, future spellwork, or offerings if desired. Pour a generous layer of granulated sugar along the inside base of the pumpkin, enough to support a candle upright. Starting from the face or holes of the pumpkin and spiraling inward toward the center, trace a spiral of ground cinnamon, sandalwood powder, or Moon Dust Blessing Powder over the sugar; this spiral serves as a pathway guiding good fortune and benevolent spirits toward the central flame. Dress your candle with Moon Oil and cinnamon, sandalwood, or Moon Dust Blessing Powder, focusing your intention on drawing abundance and good fortune as you do. Nestle the dressed candle where the spiral ends, at the center of the sugar, using a candleholder if needed for stability. Take a handful of poppy seeds and sprinkle them counterclockwise along the outermost edge of the sugar and along the interior walls of the pumpkin; poppy seeds confuse unwanted energies and mischievous spirits while still carrying their own associations with luck, dreams, and prosperity. Lay bay leaves and/or cinnamon sticks in a star pattern atop the sugar around the candle, pointing outward from the center at a fire-safe distance from the flame. Say aloud: "With the blessing of pumpkin and this candle's guiding light, guide to me good luck and fortune, this Samhain/Blood/Hunter's Moon night. Let all misfortunate, ill-meaning eyes and spirits be deterred away, but those who bless and bring goodwill, to this home, find their way." Light the candle and, if fire-safe, replace the pumpkin top. Place it at your front door. Monitor it carefully, as an open flame should never be left unattended. Leave an offering of food or drink for visiting spirits if desired, and watch for ants attracted to the sugar.
◇Type
Talisman
◇Quantities
1 large pumpkin Knife and carving tools Granulated sugar, enough to form a base layer inside the pumpkin Ground cinnamon and/or sandalwood powder (or Moon Dust Blessing Powder) Green, gold, or white candle Moon Oil Handful of poppy seeds 5 bay leaves and/or cinnamon sticks Matches or lighter Candleholder (if needed) Offering of food or drink for spirits (optional)
◇Description
A Samhain season spell that recasts the jack-o'-lantern tradition as a working for abundance and good fortune. A carved pumpkin is filled with a sugar base, a spiral of cinnamon or sandalwood powder to guide good spirits inward, poppy seeds along the walls to confuse misfortune, and a dressed candle at the center. Placed at the front door, the lit pumpkin invites prosperity and welcoming spirits while deflecting ill-wishing energy.
