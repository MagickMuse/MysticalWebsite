Directions

Use to dress candles (white, black, or cross candles) for uncrossing work. Drill holes in seven-day candles to drip oil in. Anoint mojo bags, charms, and ritual objects. Combine with Van Van Oil for simultaneous uncrossing and fortune-drawing.

About Uncrossing Oil

Uncrossing Oil is a foundational Hoodoo condition oil used across a wide range of uncrossing, hex-breaking, and reversal rituals. It appears as a core ingredient in many candle dressing, mojo bag, and ritual bath workings.
