A magnetic balm to draw the right loving partner. This is a magnet spell that will not impede another's free will—it attracts without binding.

Quantities

  • 1/8 ounce beeswax granules
  • 1/8 ounce vanilla-infused jojoba oil
  • 4 drops ginger essential oil for energizing, love, passion, strength, protection
  • 3 drops lemongrass essential oil for passion, fidelity, honesty, uplifting
  • 6 drops patchouli essential oil for love, passion, protection, peace of mind, aphrodisiac
  • 5 drops turkish rose absolute for love, luck, peace

Directions

Gently heat beeswax in double boiler until granules are melted. Quickly remove from heat and incorporate jojoba. Allow to cool slightly, stirring consistently. Add ginger, lemongrass, patchouli, and Turkish rose oils gradually while continuing to stir. Carefully pour into small pots (usually sold for lip balm) and apply to pulse points to attract the right partner.
Synergy can also be used as anointing oil for candles, charms, and talismans.
