🌬️Smudge Stick Cleansing

Closed Practice
Indigenous (White Sage)
Type
Incense
Plants4
JuniperRosemarySageSagebrush

A smudge stick is a small wand of dried botanicals lit to produce directed cleansing smoke. Most potent cleansing botanicals for smudge sticks: white or garden sage, juniper, desert sage (Artemisia/sagebrush), and rosemary.

⚠️ White sage is a closed practice sacred to Indigenous peoples of North America. Use garden sage or another alternative instead.

Quantities

  • Dried cleansing herbs (white sage, juniper, rosemary, and/or desert sage recommended)
  • Thin cotton thread or twine
  • Optional: feather fan for directing smoke

Directions

Dry bunches of herbs completely. When fully dried, bind them tightly together with thin cotton thread. When needed, light the stick and direct the smoke toward and over whatever needs cleansing. Use a feather fan or bare hands to direct the smoke. Exercise fire safety as smudge sticks smolder long after flames appear out.
