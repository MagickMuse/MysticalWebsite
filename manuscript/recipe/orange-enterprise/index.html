🍊Orange Enterprise

Type
Drink
Correspondences6
🔍Clarity💪Confidence💪Empowerment💰Prosperity🛡️Protection🏆Success
Materials5
🥃Brandy Snifter🍸Cocktail Shaker🥄Hawthorne Strainer🥄Muddler✨Zester
Plants1
Orange

A cocktail spell for business owners and ambition, harnessing orange’s power for emotional clarity and focus.

Quantities

  • 1 zester
  • 1 heavy-bottomed glass
  • 1 muddler
  • 1 cocktail shaker
  • 1 large brandy snifter
  • 1 Hawthorne strainer
  • 1 orange (confidence, action, ability, focus)
  • 2 oz orange vodka (protection, business assistance, ambition, emotional clarity)
  • 1 tbsp superfine sugar (the sweetness of life)
  • 1 tsp orange peel zest
  • Ice

Directions

Embrace your power as you inhale the fragrance of the orange. Direct your will through the zester and into the orange to empower it.

Slice the orange in half and juice half. Picture any obstacles you encounter on your road to success being cut off. Slice the other half into wedges.

Place the wedges in the bottom of a heavy-bottomed glass or cocktail shaker. Sprinkle the superfine sugar over the oranges. Muddle while invoking the power of orange for creativity, fortune, and change.

Add orange vodka and ice. Shake the cocktail shaker vigorously while envisioning your new venture coming to fruition, such as opening day of your new store, first day at the new job, or something similar.

Strain into a large brandy snifter. Add a pinch of zest and ice.

Enjoy while picturing yourself at your most successful.

Sober Substitution

Juice your oranges and visualize your success. Buy orange juice, ground and center, then send the energy of success into the orange juice and drink.

Tool Substitutions

  • No jigger? A standard shot glass holds 1.25 oz of liquor. Measure carefully!
  • No cocktail shaker? Don't try two glasses (they could get stuck and cause injury). Pour ingredients into a thermos, cap, and shake!
  • No juicer or reamer? Don't try a fork. Squeeze the fruit by hand, or squeeze around a teaspoon for pulpy texture.
  • No bar spoon? Use the handle end of a serving spoon; it'll be longer than a fork and stir the drink more at the bottom.
  • No Hawthorne strainer? Use a tea ball to pour through. It'll pick up large pieces and hold back ice without robbing the drink of texture and foam.
