A powerful Hoodoo protection condition oil, made by adding Fiery Wall of Protection powder (salt, frankincense, myrrh and dragon's blood) to oil. Its name invokes Archangel Michael's flaming sword. Used in court case workings for its protective properties, shielding the practitioner from opposing magickal influence while supporting legal victory.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

Fiery Wall of Protection powder (salt, frankincense, myrrh, dragon's blood powder)
Carrier oil

Directions

  1. Add Fiery Wall of Protection powder to a carrier oil.
  2. If desired, consecrate the oil to Archangel Michael.
  3. Dress candles, mojo hands and charm bags with it for protection workings and court case spells.
  4. For comprehensive court case protection, combine with Courtcase Oil and/or San Cipriano Oil.
🔒
Closed Practice: Fiery Wall of Protection Oil is a Hoodoo condition oil. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Approach with respect and research before working with it.

About Fiery Wall of Protection Oil

Fiery Wall of Protection Oil is one of the most potent protection condition oils in Hoodoo. Its presence in court case spellwork reflects the understanding that legal battles can involve competing magickal workings, and a strong protective field keeps the practitioner shielded from whatever the other side might be doing.

Uses

  • Dress candles for protection during court proceedings
  • Anoint mojo hands and charm bags
  • Use as part of the cotton ball carry method (dip, carry in pocket)
  • One of the three primary court case condition oils: use alongside Courtcase Oil and San Cipriano Oil

Michael's Flaming Sword

Fiery Wall of Protection is among the most famous classic condition formulas. Its name calls on the protective flaming sword of Archangel Michael, and the formula may be consecrated to him. Its base combines powerful protective agents such as salt, frankincense and myrrh, while dragon's blood powder gives it its red color, the color of protection. The dried powder works as incense or magick powder; added to oil, it becomes Fiery Wall of Protection Oil. See Fiery Wall of Protection Incense.
