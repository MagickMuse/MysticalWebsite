🌙Dream Tea

Type
Drink
Correspondences1
💤Dreams
Plants5
CinnamonJasmineMugwortPeppermintRose

A tea to produce psychic dreams when drunk before sleep.

Quantities

2 parts Rose petals, 1 part Mugwort, 1 part Peppermint, 1 part Jasmine flowers, 1/2 part Cinnamon

Directions

Mix ingredients. Add one teaspoon to a cup. Pour boiling water over the herbs and let steep, covered, for a few minutes. Drink before going to bed to produce psychic dreams.
