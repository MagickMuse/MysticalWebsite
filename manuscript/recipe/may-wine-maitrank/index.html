🌸May Wine (Maitrank)

The traditional festive beverage of May Eve, Beltane, and Walpurgis celebrations, flavored with Sweet Woodruff.

Quantities

Young, fresh woodruff shoots (or dried Sweet Woodruff)
1 bottle white wine (Rhine or Alsatian)
Optional: a few tablespoons of brandy
Sugar to taste
Water
Optional: sliced fresh strawberries for serving

Directions

Place young, fresh woodruff shoots in a covered tureen. Add a bottle of white wine; some also add a few tablespoons of brandy. Cover the tureen and allow the wine to infuse for an hour. Dissolve sugar to taste in water and add to the infused wine. Add sliced fresh strawberries just before serving if desired. As fresh woodruff is not always available, dried herbs may also be used.

About This Recipe

This wine is flavored and scented with Sweet Woodruff (Asperula odorata), whose folk names include Forest Queen, Forest Mother Herb, and Master (or Mistress) of the Woods.

History

The earliest recorded mention occurred in 854 CE by Benedictine monk Wandelbertus. In German it is called Maitrank, traditionally made with Rhine or Alsatian white wine.

Note

As fresh woodruff is not always available, dried herbs may also be used.
