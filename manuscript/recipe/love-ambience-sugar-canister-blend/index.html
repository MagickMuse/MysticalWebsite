🍯Love Ambience Sugar Canister Blend

Type
Powder
Plants5
CardamomCinnamonHibiscusOrangeVanilla

A sealed canister of granulated sugar embedded with vanilla, cinnamon, cardamom, hibiscus, and orange blossoms, charged with visualized loving energy and used as a powder sprinkle or fed to partners during times of tension.

Quantities

Fine granulated sugar (white or brown)
Vanilla bean
Cinnamon stick
Cardamom pods
Dried hibiscus blossoms
Orange blossoms
Airtight canister

Directions

Fill an airtight canister with fine granulated sugar, white or brown. Embed a vanilla bean, a cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, dried hibiscus blossoms, and orange blossoms in the sugar. Visualize love flowing out of the canister and spreading through the room, then seal the canister. Use it as a powder sprinkle or feed it to your lover and yourself in times of tension.
