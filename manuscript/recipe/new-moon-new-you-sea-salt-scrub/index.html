🌊New Moon, New You Sea Salt Scrub

◇Directions
Pour the coconut oil, essential oils, and dried eucalyptus leaf into the jar. Stir counterclockwise, visualizing the blend's cleansing properties dissolving stagnant and unwanted energy. Add the sea salt and close the jar. Shake well, imagining the motion helping to shake off old energy and charge the mixture for purification. Use in the bath or shower to keep energy cleansed and focused. Scrub counterclockwise while visualizing what you wish to release; this works especially well as a hand scrub any time you wash your hands to dispel undesired energy as it arises. If adding cucumber for refreshing lunar energy, muddle 1 to 2 teaspoons and fold in, then add extra sea salt to balance the added moisture and refrigerate the mixture; use before the cucumber deteriorates.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
3–4 tsp coconut oil (olive or grapeseed work as substitutes) 6 drops lemon essential oil 3 drops rosemary essential oil 1 drop wintergreen essential oil 1 tsp cut and crushed dried eucalyptus leaf ½ cup sea salt Jar with lid Optional: 1–2 tsp muddled cucumber (refrigerate if added; use before cucumber spoils)
◇Description
A lunar cleansing salt scrub blended with hex-breaking wintergreen, rejuvenating lemon, healing eucalyptus, and purifying rosemary in a coconut oil base. Craft at the waning or new moon and use throughout the lunation cycle to scrub away stagnant energy, refresh the spirit, and step into new beginnings. Can double as a hand scrub during daily cleansing rituals.
