🧪Lunar Slime

◇Directions
Fill a jar or dish to the brim with pure spring water. Bring the container outside and position it so that the full moon is reflected within the water's surface. Maintain this alignment for as long as possible, allowing the lunar energy to fully charge the water. Once complete, the resulting water serves as a powerful ingredient for potions and spells. It can be substituted wherever a spell calls for water, including Holy Water.
◇Type
Spray
◇Quantities
- 1 jar or dish - Pure spring water (enough to fill the container)
◇Description
A technique for creating potent moon-charged water under the full moon. The resulting water can substitute for any water called for in spellwork, including Holy Water, and ranks among the most powerful ingredients in the materia magica.
