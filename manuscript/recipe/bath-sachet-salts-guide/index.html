Bath Sachet & Salts Guide

Herbal bath sachets transform an ordinary bath into a ritual experience. When herbs steep in warm water, they release their colors, scents, and magickal energies into the liquid. The bather essentially brews in a giant pot of herbal tea, absorbing the plants' power through the skin.

Bath sachets are among the simplest forms of herbal magick, yet they remain powerful tools for psychic development, love drawing, healing acceleration, and personal protection.

Making Bath Sachets

Ingredients

Use any of the bath sachet recipes in the Recipe Grimoire, or design your own blend based on your magickal intention.

Preparation

  1. Gather your ingredients and place them in a mixing bowl
  2. Blend the herbs with your fingers while visualizing your magickal goal
  3. Pour your personal energy into the mixture as you work
  4. Place about a handful of the blend in the center of a cheesecloth square (a washcloth works as an alternative)
  5. Gather the corners and tie securely

Storage

Prepare multiple sachets at once for convenience. Store in an airtight jar until needed. Properly stored, dry herb blends keep for several months.

Using Bath Sachets

Standard Bath Method

Fill a clean tub with warm water. Drop the sachet into the water and allow it to steep until the bath is colored and fragrant.

Shower Method

If you prefer showers or lack a bathtub, prepare a sachet in a washcloth. After your regular shower, scrub your body with the herb-filled cloth before toweling dry.

Infusion Method

For a stronger preparation:

  1. Boil two cups of water
  2. Pour over one or two sachets in a heat-proof container
  3. Cover and steep for ten to thirteen minutes
  4. Remove sachets and squeeze out remaining liquid
  5. Add the infusion to your bathwater

Direct Addition (Not Recommended)

Some practitioners add loose herbs directly to the bathwater without a sachet. This method often results in plant material clinging to your skin and clogging drains. The cleanup usually takes longer than any benefit gained.

Ritual Practice

As you enter the water, feel the herbal energies mingling with your own. Maintain your visualization throughout the bath. The herbs assist your magick, but you must actively participate by directing their energy toward your goal.

Duration: Repeat the bath for as many consecutive days as feels right. There are no fixed rules. Continue until you sense the working is complete.

Optional Enhancements:

  • Burn incense aligned with your magickal intention
  • Light candles in corresponding colors
  • Play soft music or maintain silence for deeper focus

Bath Salts

Bath salts offer an easy-to-prepare alternative to herbal sachets. Unlike many commercial products laden with harsh chemicals that can irritate skin, homemade ritual bath salts are gentle and fully customizable.

Creating Bath Salts

Base Recipe

Combine these ingredients in a large bowl:

  • 3 parts Epsom salts (magnesium sulfate)
  • 2 parts Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate)
  • 1 part Table salt (or Borax)

Mix thoroughly. This neutral base can be divided into portions for multiple recipes or prepared as one large batch of a single type.

Adding Color

Use plain food coloring to align your salts with color correspondences:

  1. Add drops one at a time onto the salt base
  2. For mixed hues (like purple), blend colors in a spoon first to avoid streaking
  3. Stir until color distributes evenly throughout
  4. More drops create deeper color; fewer drops yield lighter tints
  5. Recipes marked "Color: White" should remain untinted

Adding Fragrance

Incorporate essential oils drop by drop:

  1. Add one oil at a time, mixing between additions
  2. Continue until the scent feels balanced to your nose
  3. Stir until all salt particles are lightly moistened
  4. This process may take up to half an hour
  5. Visualize the oils' energies merging with each other and the salt
  6. Hold your magickal intention in mind throughout

General guideline: No more than ten total drops of essential oil per half-cup of bath salts. The finished product should be strongly scented, a more potent aroma means you'll use less per bath.

Using Bath Salts

Add two tablespoons to one-half cup of ritual bath salts to a full tub. Mix with your hands, feeling the energies dissolve into the water.

While soaking, open yourself to receive the salt's power, or alternatively, consciously release unwanted energies into the water.

Important Notes

  • Use genuine essential oils only: synthetic fragrances lack magickal potency
  • Clean your tub before and after ritual baths, using a commercial cleanser or baking soda on a damp cloth
  • Ritual baths in dirty tubs won't produce desired results
  • Recipe proportions are relative (parts, not exact measurements), trust your nose to determine final quantities
