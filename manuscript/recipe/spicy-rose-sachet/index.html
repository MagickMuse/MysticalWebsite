🌿Spicy Rose Sachet

Type
Powder
Correspondences3
✨Beauty💕Love🕯️Remembrance
Plants4
CloveHibiscusRoseRosemary
Symbols1
Pink

A thoughtful blend symbolizing love, remembrance, beauty, dignity, and resilience. Perfect as a gift for someone dear.

Quantities

  • 1 part Rose (for love)
  • 1 part Rosemary (for remembrance)
  • 1 part Hibiscus (for delicate beauty)
  • 1 part Clove (for dignity)
  • 1 part Chamomile (for energy in adversity)

Directions

Tie up in pink cloth and give to one you love.
