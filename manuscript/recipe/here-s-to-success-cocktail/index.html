🥂Here's to Success Cocktail

Type
Drink
Correspondences4
😊Happiness☮️Peace💰Prosperity🏆Success
Materials1
🍬Sugar
Plants2
LavenderLemon

An adult beverage to celebrate and ensure success in endeavors mundane and magickal.

Quantities

1 wedge Meyer lemon (or regular lemon if Meyer isn't available)
1 tablespoon brown sugar
1.5 ounces Earl Grey-infused gin
1.5 ounces fresh lemon juice
2 ounces lavender syrup (or simple syrup)

For Earl Grey-Infused Gin:
1/4 to 1/3 cup Earl Grey leaves
1 liter gin

For Lavender Syrup:
2 teaspoons lavender buds
2 cups sugar
2 cups water

Directions

Rim two martini glasses with the lemon wedge. Pour brown sugar onto a shallow plate and coat the rim of each glass. Set glasses aside. Add gin, lemon juice, and lavender syrup to an iced cocktail shaker (metal is preferred). Shake vigorously until frost appears on the outside of the shaker. Strain into rimmed martini glasses.

Earl Grey-Infused Gin: Pour gin over Earl Grey in a large pitcher and set aside for 2 to 3 hours. Strain the liquid back into the gin bottle with a coffee filter placed in a funnel. Resist the urge to wring the leaves into the gin, as it will make the gin bitter.

Lavender Syrup: Add lavender, sugar, and water to a saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring often. Boil two minutes and remove from heat. Allow to cool completely and strain flowers out before bottling. Store any remaining syrup in the refrigerator. Use lavender syrup in place of simple syrup for any beverage. Any sugar recipe can be made into a lavender recipe: simply add 1 teaspoon lavender buds per 1 cup sugar.
