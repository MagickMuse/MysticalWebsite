🍯Sore Throat Syrup

Type
Syrup
Correspondences4
🤧Common Cold😷Coughs❤️‍🩹Healing🗣️Sore Throat
Materials3
Double Boiler🍯HoneyStrainer
Plants4
AngelicaHyssopMallowRosehip

A honey-sweetened blend of mallow, hyssop, rose hips, and angelica simmered in water until soft and strained. Stored refrigerated to soothe sore throats.

Quantities

1⅓ cups water
½ teaspoon mallow
1 tablespoon hyssop
1 cup rose hips
½ teaspoon angelica
½ cup honey

Directions

Combine the herbs and water in the top of a double boiler. Heat over boiling water for about twenty minutes or until the rose hips are soft. Press the herbs through a strainer and stir in the honey. Store in a covered container in the refrigerator.
