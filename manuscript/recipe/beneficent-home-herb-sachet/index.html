🏡Beneficent Home Herb Sachet

◇Directions
1. Combine the dried rose petals and protective herbs of choice in a covered nonporous container. Add the salt, cover, and allow to sit for approximately three weeks to remove excess moisture. 2. After three weeks, sift out the salt and set it aside. 3. Grind together the powdered orris root, gum benzoin, and brown sugar. Add this mixture to the dried herbs and blend freely. 4. Mix the essential oil into the pure alcohol. Pour the alcohol blend onto the dry mixture and blend in thoroughly. 5. Leave overnight to steep. If the blend remains too liquid, add extra orris root or herb base. 6. Store in well-stoppered nonporous bottles away from bright light at a moderate temperature.
◇Type
Sachet
◇Quantities
- Rose petals (dried) — 2 quarts - Dried protective herbs of choice — a selection - Table salt or ground rock salt — 1 pound (for moisture removal) - Powdered orris root — 2 ounces - Gum benzoin (powdered) — 1 ounce - Brown sugar — 3 ounces - Pure alcohol (or brandy if unavailable) — 1 ounce - Essential oil of choice — 4 drops
◇Description
A fragrant, traditionally beneficent herb blend that may be placed around the home in sachets or suitable containers. Makes an acceptable Samhain or Halloween gift. Composed using the trituration method.
