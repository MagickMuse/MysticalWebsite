🌕Esbat

A Full Moon Esbat consecration of water through moonlight, rose, salt, and spoken invocations to the Great Lady of the Night. The water is blessed by reflecting the Full Moon through a compact mirror, sealed with the power of three and nine turned deosil, then bottled and stored for use in spell workings between Esbats.

Quantities

  • Water in a cup or pitcher (sufficient amount to store for use between Esbats)
  • 1 bowl
  • Rose petals, rose water, rose extract, or rose oil (note: oil is flammable)
  • Salt (3 pinches)
  • 1 small mirror (compact style)
  • 1 bottle with lid

Directions

Raise both arms beneath the moonlight and call out:

I call upon Thee, Great Lady of the Night, to bless this water I pour in Thy Light.

Pour water into the bowl and add a rose petal. Hold the bowl aloft in the moonlight and speak:

In this water doth shine Thy light, that it be consecrated in Thy sight. Cleansed and purified on this Esbat night, this water is blessed by Thy sacred rite.

Hold up the salt in the moonlight and speak:

Through the Lady hast all things their form, taking salt in the waters and lands of the Earth. Through Thy bright light, I consecrate this salt to aid in my work.

Drop three pinches of salt into the water and stir three times. Use the compact mirror, if needed, to catch and reflect the Full Moon onto the surface of the water. Speak:

By the light of the Moon, through the power in Thy Tides, a portion of Thy power now herein resides, that blessed be this water. By 3 times 3 this spell I bind, that it be cast with the power of nine. For as I will, So Mote It Be!

Set the mirror and bowl down, then turn the bowl nine times deosil in the moonlight and speak:

With power gathered and sent within, this spell is sealed 9 times again, that adds together 9 once more, that with these 3 is 9 the core. So Mote It Be!

Transfer the water into a bottle with a lid and store it away from light. Use as needed as blessed water between Esbats.
