💧New-Mown Hay Bouquet

Type
Oil
Oils3
Bergamot Essential OilLavender Essential OilOakmoss Absolute

A perfumer's blend capturing the honey-fresh scent of a just-mowed field of hay. Used for fresh perspectives, breaking negative habits, and new beginnings.

Quantities

  • Woodruff Oil
  • Tonka Oil
  • Lavender Oil
  • Bergamot Oil
  • Oakmoss Oil
    Combine in equal parts or adjust to preference.

Directions

Combine all oils in a dark glass bottle. Cap and shake gently to blend. Allow to sit for at least 24 hours before use.

This bouquet captures the fresh, sweet scent of newly mown hay. Use as a direct substitute in any recipe calling for new-mown hay oil.

Store in a cool, dark place.

This perfumer's blend recreates the evocative scent of freshly cut hay without requiring a genuine essential oil, which does not exist.

Magical Uses

Fresh Perspectives: Use when seeking new ways of looking at old problems. Diffuse while brainstorming or working through difficult decisions.

Breaking Habits: Particularly effective for breaking negative habits and addictions. The scent of new growth supports transformation and release of old patterns.

New Beginnings: Ideal for workings involving fresh starts, new chapters, and turning points in life.

Component Properties

Woodruff provides the characteristic sweet, hay-like base note.

Tonka adds vanilla-like warmth and depth.

Lavender contributes calming, balancing energy.

Bergamot brings brightness and success associations.

Oakmoss grounds the blend and extends its magical duration.
