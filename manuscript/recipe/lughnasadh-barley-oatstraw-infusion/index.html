🌾Lughnasadh Barley Oatstraw Infusion

◇Type
Infusion
↗Materials1
🍯Honey
↗Plants3
BarleyOatsPeppermint

A nourishing Lughnasadh tea that begins with barley water made from boiled whole barley, then uses that grain-rich liquid to steep oatstraw. Finished with fresh peppermint and sweetened with honey, it is a warm and grounding celebration of the sacred harvest grains.

Lughnasadh honors the first harvest, and oats and barley are among the most sacred of the harvest grains. This warming infusion draws on both, layering barley water with the nutritive depth of oatstraw and the brightening freshness of peppermint.

Quantities

  • 1 cup whole barley
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 ounce dried oatstraw
  • Fresh peppermint, a generous handful
  • Honey, to taste

Directions

  1. Bring the barley and water to a boil. Simmer until the barley pearls are fully tender, then strain, reserving the liquid. Set the cooked barley aside for eating or composting.
  2. Use the warm barley water to steep the oatstraw. Add the oatstraw to the liquid and allow it to infuse for 20 to 30 minutes.
  3. Add the fresh peppermint and steep for a further 5 minutes. Strain and sweeten with honey to taste.
  4. Enjoy warm as summer recedes into autumn.

Magickal Notes

Barley has ancient sacred connections to grain goddesses, mystery traditions, and the cycle of death and rebirth in the land. Oatstraw builds strength and nervous resilience, well suited to the reflective turning of the season. Peppermint adds clarity and vitality. This infusion is grounding and ceremonial, fitting for quiet Lughnasadh reflection, harvest altars, and giving thanks for the abundance of the year.
