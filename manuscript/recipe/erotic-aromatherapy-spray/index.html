🌶️Herbs to Boost Libido and Lust

A customizable essential oil spray using witch hazel and distilled water to ignite scent-based desire and consecrate a sacred sexual space.

Quantities

  • 2–3 drops essential oil (choose one or blend several)
  • ½ cup distilled water
  • 3 tablespoons witch hazel
  • 1 cobalt or other opaque glass spray bottle
    Essential Oil Options:
  • Jasmine
  • Ylang ylang
  • Rose
  • Sandalwood
  • Orange blossom (neroli)
  • Clary sage
  • Fennel

Directions

Explore each oil's fragrance individually first; what stimulates desire is personal, so trust your response over any list. Pour the distilled water and witch hazel into the glass spray bottle. Add 2 to 3 drops of your chosen essential oil or blend, seal, and shake gently. Spray the air directly in front of you and walk slowly through the mist before it settles. As you move through it, whisper:

"I walk in passion and desire. I am worthy of pleasure. May it be here now."

About This Recipe

The olfactory system is intimately connected to both memory and desire. Scent bypasses the analytical mind and speaks directly to instinct and can transport you to another state entirely and ignite arousal without a single word spoken. This spray creates a scented mist you walk through to shift your energetic state before intimate or ritual work.

Notes

⚠️ Essential oils should never be applied directly to skin undiluted. This spray is designed for the air and surrounding space only; do not apply it directly to the body. Shake before each use as oil and water will naturally separate. Store in a cool, dark place; use within 4–6 weeks for best potency.
