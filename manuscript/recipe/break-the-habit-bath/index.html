🔓Break the Habit Bath

Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences2
🚭Addiction🔄Habits
Plants5
LavenderLemongrassLemon VerbenaRosemarySage

A liberating bath ritual to release negative habits, addictions, and obsessive patterns along with their root causes.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 2 parts Rosemary
  • 1 part Lavender
  • 1 part Lemongrass
  • 1 part Lemon Verbena
  • 1 part Sage

Directions

Prepare as a bath sachet and add to warm water. Once the water is colored, step in and lie back. Visualize yourself living freely without the habit, whether it be smoking, drinking, substance use, depression, or obsessive patterns. See the water absorbing your desire for the negative condition. Imagine all the energy you have given to this habit flowing out of you and into the water. When the visualization feels complete, pull the plug and remain in the tub as the water drains, watching it carry away the unwanted energy. Splash fresh water over your body to wash away any remaining traces. Repeat daily until the habit releases its hold.
