Recipe Grimoire

🥃Old-Fashioned Witch

◇Directions
Place the sugar cube in the bottom of an old fashioned glass. Add 2 to 3 dashes of orange bitters directly onto the sugar cube. Muddle the sugar cube until it dissolves, envisioning what you want to attract or what you want to burn away. Add a large ice cube or several smaller cubes. Pour in the bourbon first; this is your fire foundation. Feel the solar energy entering the glass. Add the amaretto, watching it swirl and blend with the bourbon. This is the sweetness you're calling in. Stir gently 13 times clockwise to attract, or 13 times counterclockwise to banish. Express the orange peel over the drink by holding it over the glass and squeezing to release the oils. Drop it into the drink. Drink slowly and mindfully, feeling the warmth spread through you.
◇Type
Drink
◇Quantities
- 1 old fashioned glass - Ice - 1.5 oz bourbon (solar fire, protection, prosperity) - 0.5 oz amaretto (love, passion, creativity) - 1 sugar cube (sweetening, binding) - 2-3 dashes orange bitters (solar energy, purification) - Orange peel for garnish (joy, abundance)
◇Description
A classic cocktail spell combining bourbon's solar fire with amaretto's love energy for protection and attraction.
