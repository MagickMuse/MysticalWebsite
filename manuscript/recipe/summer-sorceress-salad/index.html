🥗Summer Sorceress Salad

◇Type
Food
↗Plants5
BorageMintMustardNasturtiumTarragon
↗Timing3
Lughnasadh☀️Midsummer📅Summer Solstice

A vibrant summer salad of baby spinach, arugula, and romaine tossed with herbed goat cheese and dressed with a honey-tarragon vinaigrette. Garnished with torn mint, jewel-blue borage flowers, and orange nasturtiums, this feast-worthy dish connects the kitchen witch to the life force of summer growth.

Quantities

1 quart baby spinach
1 quart arugula
1 quart shredded romaine lettuce
½ cup herbed goat cheese, crumbled
1 tablespoon minced mustard greens
1 tablespoon minced fresh tarragon leaves
2 teaspoons wildflower honey
1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
Dash salt and pepper to taste
⅓ cup olive oil
Torn mint leaves for garnish
1 cup borage flowers
½ cup orange nasturtiums

Directions

Wash the spinach, arugula, and romaine thoroughly and dry well by patting with a paper towel, spinning in a salad spinner, or placing in a lidded colander and shaking off the excess water. Combine the three greens in a large bowl and scatter the crumbled goat cheese over the top. In a small bowl, whisk together the mustard greens, tarragon, honey, and apple cider vinegar. Season with salt and pepper, then slowly drizzle in the olive oil, whisking continuously to emulsify. Pour over the greens and toss lightly. Offer a blessing over the salad before serving. Finish with torn mint leaves, borage flowers, and orange nasturtiums scattered over the top.
