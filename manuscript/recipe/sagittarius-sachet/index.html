An astrological sachet to amplify the positive traits of those born under Sagittarius.

Quantities

  • 3 parts Sassafras
  • 2 parts Cedar
  • 2 parts Clove
  • 1 part Star Anise
  • 1 part Dragon's Blood
  • 1 part Juniper

Directions

Tie up in purple cloth. Wear or carry to strengthen your sign's best qualities.
