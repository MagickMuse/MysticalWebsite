🌸Herbs for Radiating Love Outwardly

A 6-week herbal infusion in sweet almond oil to invoke Erato, muse of erotic poetry, and welcome sexual love into the practitioner's life.

Quantities

  • ¼ cup damiana
  • ¼ cup ginkgo
  • Kola nut
  • Lovage root
  • 1 to 1½ cups sweet almond oil (enough to completely cover all herbs)
  • 2 clean 16-oz glass jars with tight-fitting lids
  • Cheesecloth for straining

Directions

  1. Place all herbs into one of the glass jars. Pour sweet almond oil over them until fully submerged.
  2. Seal the jar, label it with today's date, and store in a cool, dark location.
  3. Allow the herbs to infuse for 6 weeks.
  4. After 6 weeks, strain the oil through cheesecloth into the second clean jar. Press the spent herbs to extract as much oil as possible, then discard the plant material.
  5. Seal the finished oil and store in a cool, dark place. Use within 9–12 months.

About This Recipe

Erato is the muse of erotic poetry; of the Camenae, she is the most sexually expressive, representing sensuality's sacred intersection with divinity. In witchcraft, sexuality is honored as a life-giving and sacred force. This anointing oil is crafted to invoke her presence, amplify erotic energy, and welcome sexual love into your life.

How to Use

Anoint a candle with the oil before lighting it during ritual. Apply the oil to pulse points, the neck and insides of the wrists, to carry its energy through the day or in spellwork.

Invocation of Erato

Speak the following aloud when ready to work:

Within me, you have ignited a flame.
With this spark of passion
I call upon the sacred name,
Erato! I invoke and invite you.
Fill my body with celestial fire,
With love and reverence,
Your power to inspire,
And allow me the means
To fulfill my desire.

Notes

This is a cold infusion: no heat is applied, which preserves the subtle energetic properties of the plants. Sweet almond oil is an excellent carrier for love and sensuality workings: nourishing, gentle, and magickally associated with unconditional regard and innocent erotic love.
