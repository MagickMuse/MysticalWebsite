📿Herbal Lip Scrub Charm

A hydrating and exfoliating lip scrub blending honey, sugar, coconut oil, vitamin E, vanilla, and a chosen herb-infused oil into a thick paste. The combination of granulated sugar and sweet carrier ingredients conditions the lips while removing dry or dead skin. No refrigeration required. Shelf-stable due to the antimicrobial properties of honey and sugar.

Quantities

  • 2 teaspoons granulated organic sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon wildflower honey
  • 1/8 teaspoon vitamin E oil
  • 1-2 drops diluted herbal oil of choice (e.g. mint infused in jojoba, or essential oil diluted in a carrier)
  • 1/8 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 2 drops vanilla extract
  • Small metal 1-ounce jar

Directions

Combine the sugar and honey in a small bowl, then add the vitamin E oil, herb-infused oil, and coconut oil. Stir until fully blended. Add the vanilla extract and continue mixing until the preparation forms a uniform, thick paste. Transfer any unused portion into the 1-ounce jar for storage. The natural antimicrobial quality of the honey and sugar keeps the scrub stable at room temperature.
