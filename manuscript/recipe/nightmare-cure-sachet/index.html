🌿Nightmare Cure Sachet

◇Directions
Tie up in light blue or white cloth. Hang on your bedpost near your head. Yew berries are highly toxic; handle with care and do not ingest.
◇Type
Powder
◇Quantities
- 1 part Lupine - 1 part Helenium (Heliotrope or Sunflower) - 1 part Marshmallow - 1 part Dock - 1 part Elder - 1 part Wormwood - 1 part Strawberry leaves - 1 part Yew berries*
◇Description
An ancient formula to ward off nightmares and malevolent spirits. According to old lore, this blend also cures "water elf disease" and mischief caused by goblins.
