🕎Between the Veils Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences1
👤Spirits
Plants2
PoppySandalwood

A 500-year-old formula to make spirits and strange shapes appear. Virtually impossible to compound due to unknown ingredients. Contains deadly poisons.

Quantities

  • Root of the weedy herb Sagapen (?)
  • Juice of Hemlock
  • Juice of Henbane
  • Tapsus barbatus (?)
  • Red Sandalwood
  • Black Poppy seed

Caution

This recipe contains hemlock and henbane, which are DEADLY POISONS. This is a historical formula included for reference only.

Historical Note

This 500-year-old formula is virtually impossible to compound. It is included here as an example of an authentic, ancient herbal incense. Most historical spirit incenses are as difficult to make as this one.

What is the "weedy herb Sagapen"? The identity of this ingredient has been lost to time. "Tapsus barbatus" is similarly uncertain, possibly referring to mullein (Verbascum thapsus).

Purpose

According to the original text, this incense was burned to make spirits and strange shapes appear. To make them flee, add parsley to this mixture, as this chases away all spirits and destroys all visions. This seems to contradict Spirit Incense #1, which uses parsley as an ingredient for summoning.

Identifiable Ingredients

  • Red Sandalwood: Fire element, used in spirit work and protection
  • Black Poppy seed: Associated with sleep, dreams, and the underworld
