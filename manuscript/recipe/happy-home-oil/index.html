🌸Happy Home Oil

A home-blessing infused oil combining gardenia petals, myrrh, and cinquefoil in a blended jojoba and coconut oil base, with a pinch of salt for purification.

Quantities

Gardenia petals
Myrrh resin
Cinquefoil (five-finger grass), pinch
Jojoba oil
Coconut oil
Salt, pinch

Directions

Blend jojoba and coconut oil as the base. Add gardenia petals, myrrh resin, a pinch of cinquefoil, and a pinch of salt. Allow to infuse. Strain if desired. Use to dress candles, anoint doorways, or apply in any home blessing working.
