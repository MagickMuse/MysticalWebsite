🪜Cinnamon Ladder for Success

A knot magick witch's ladder using cinnamon sticks to bind and climb toward your goals. Each cinnamon stick represents a step, theme, or aspect of your intention, knotted in place as you affirm your resolve. The finished ladder hangs as a living talisman, charged monthly at the full moon until your goal is reached. Ideal under earth sign moons for grounded ambition, or Sagittarius for far-reaching vision.

Quantities

Long piece of natural twine, string, or cord
Cinnamon sticks, one per goal aspect or intention
Charm such as a crescent moon, feather, stone, or bead (optional)
Bay leaf (optional)
Sharpie or permanent marker (optional)

Directions

Fold the string in half and tie a knot near the midpoint, leaving a small loop at the top for hanging and two long strands hanging down to use as the ladder's sides. Reflect on the key steps and aspects of your goal; each cinnamon stick will represent one part, whether a milestone, a theme like focus or discipline, or simply the growing energy of your success. Hold your first cinnamon stick with your intention in mind, tuck and center it between the two strands just below the top knot, visualize affirming your goal, then tie a secure knot around it. Continue adding cinnamon sticks horizontally as the rungs of the ladder, knotting the cord around the center of each one and spacing them as close or as far apart as feels right. As you work each knot, focus on what that rung represents and strengthen your resolve. Chant as you go:

"I climb the ladder of success, my determination knows no rest. I reach the summit of my goal, my pathway lit by my moonlit soul."

Once all sticks are in place, leave the strands loose at the bottom or attach a final charm. Optionally, write your intention on a bay leaf and tie or tuck it into the ladder. Hang the ladder somewhere it will catch your eye and reinforce your intention daily; recharge it under each full moon until your goal is achieved. To deepen the working, soften cinnamon sticks in warm Moon Water, tuck a written intention, bill, or symbolic paper inside each one, and roll them back into shape to dry before adding them to the ladder.
