💪Ginger Bug

Type
Drink
Correspondences4
🦁Courage🍀Luck💰Prosperity💪Strength
Plants1
Ginger

A wild fermented probiotic soda starter that harnesses ginger's magickal properties of strength, prosperity, courage, and luck.

Quantities

  • 2 cups filtered water
  • 2 tablespoons grated fresh ginger (with skin)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar

Directions

Combine 2 cups filtered water, 2 tablespoons grated fresh ginger (with skin), and 2 tablespoons sugar in a quart jar. Stir well to dissolve the sugar. Cover loosely with a cloth or coffee filter secured with a rubber band to allow airflow while keeping out debris. Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Each day for 5 to 7 days, feed the bug by adding 1 tablespoon grated ginger and 1 tablespoon sugar, stirring well. The mixture should become bubbly and slightly fizzy when ready, with an active, yeasty smell. Once established, feed every 1 to 2 days to maintain. Use 1/4 cup of active ginger bug to ferment 1 quart of sweetened juice or tea into probiotic soda. Store extra bug in the refrigerator, feeding weekly to keep it alive.
