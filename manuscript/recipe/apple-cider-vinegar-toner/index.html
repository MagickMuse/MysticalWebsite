🧴Apple Cider Vinegar Toner

◇Directions
Pour the vinegar into a clean bottle. Add the lavender essential oil. Cap and shake the bottle. Take the lid off and add the chamomile, witch hazel, and water. Replace the top and give another shake. Apply a small amount of toner to a clean cloth and gently wipe over the face. With a clean face and a winning smile, tell yourself things you love about you. Practice makes perfect, so do this every morning. (Discontinue if irritation occurs.)
◇Type
Wash
◇Quantities
- 8 oz. water (cleansing, connection) - 3 tbsp. apple cider vinegar (healing, beauty, sexual attraction) - 1 tsp. chamomile hydrosol or 1 tsp. dried chamomile flowers (purification, aura balance, soothing skin) - 1 tsp. witch hazel, for oily skin only (anti-anxiety, uncrossing, heart-healing) - 3 drops lavender essential oil (protection, balance, gentleness)
◇Description
A healing skin toner for acne, sunburn, and insect bites. Apple cider vinegar combined with witch hazel and chamomile creates a soothing topical treatment.

About This Recipe

This simple but effective toner combines the healing properties of apple cider vinegar with the purifying qualities of witch hazel and the soothing nature of chamomile. Use topically for skin irritations.

Magickal Properties

Apple brings healing and love energy. Witch hazel offers protection and purification. Chamomile adds calming, peace, and gentle healing.

Source

Blackthorn's Botanical Brews by Amy Blackthorn
