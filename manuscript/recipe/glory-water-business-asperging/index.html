💧Glory Water Business Asperging

Type
Spray
Correspondences4
💼Business💰Prosperity🏆Success💵Wealth
Plants1
Frankincense

Glory Water is made by infusing frankincense tears in orange blossom water or hydrosol with bergamot essential oil. It is asperged through business premises to increase business, attract blessings and customers, and overcome challenges.

Quantities

  • Frankincense tears (small pieces)
  • Orange blossom water or hydrosol
  • Bergamot essential oil

Directions

Place frankincense tears inside a bottle. Cover with orange blossom water or hydrosol. Add a few drops of essential oil of bergamot. Asperge Glory Water through the premises to increase business, attract blessings and customers, and overcome challenges.
