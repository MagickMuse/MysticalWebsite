⚔️Hex-Breaking Spray

Type
Spray
Correspondences3
⛓️Hex Breaking🛡️Protection🧹Purification
Materials1
🔫Spray Bottle
Oils1
Rosemary Essential Oil
Plants1
Rosemary

Busting hexes easy with mint family. Strong medicinal scent of rosemary reminds would-be hexes who is boss. Jinxes and curses do not stand chance.

Quantities

  • 1 teaspoon witch hazel
  • 3 drops rosemary essential oil for hex breaking
  • Cool water to fill

Directions

Add witch hazel to 2-ounce spray bottle of dark glass or number 1 plastic. Add rosemary essential oil to witch hazel, cap, and shake. Unscrew lid and fill rest of way with cool water. Cap and shake again.
Walk counterclockwise around outside of home, spraying exterior so no hexes can cross magickal threshold created. If you live in apartment, condo, or townhouse, spray interior of your space. If family, roommates, or spouse look concerned, this spray can also be used as natural fabric refresher. (Test small portion of fabric beforehand for colorfastness.)
Use remainder as hand wash to keep nasty magick from following you home. (Highly recommended practice to ghost hunters to ensure nothing nasty comes home with them.)
