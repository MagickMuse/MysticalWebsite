📖Moonlit Bibliomancy

A lunar divination practice using books to receive messages, insight, and guidance from the moon. Incense of jasmine, sandalwood, or poppy clears the mind and opens intuition, while the moon illuminates the chosen page. Best performed under the Sagittarius moon for prophecy and expanded belief, or the Gemini moon for information and research. The full moon sharpens sight; the dark or new moon reveals truth through the unseen.

Quantities

Incense — jasmine, sandalwood, or opium/poppy
Matches or lighter
A few books

Directions

Clear your mind with several slow, deep breaths before beginning. Light the incense and watch the smoke rise, visualizing it curling upward toward the moon. Place a book before you and soften your gaze. Ask your question aloud and visualize the moon's light falling across the pages. With your gaze still soft, slowly flip through the book and say aloud:

"The full moon allows me to see, knowledge now does come to me."

At the dark or new moon, replace that line with:

"In darkness, the light of truth can be seen."

As the pages pass, follow your instincts; you may feel the moon shining on a particular page, or simply sense the urge to stop. Stop and open to that page. Read it as it is, or refine further by pointing to a passage, sentence, or single word before reading. Interpret what you find, literally, symbolically, or metaphorically, and trust your intuition to find the meaning.
