🌼Spring Blossom Magick Meal

Type
Food
Plants3
DandelionMustardParsley
Timing2
Beltane🐰Ostara

Dandelion petal patties made with mustard greens and parsley, fried in Herbal-Infused Blessed Butter and topped with a spicy horseradish ketchup condiment. A perfect kitchen witch feast for Ostara and Beltane, celebrating the abundant blossoms of early spring. Use only wild-foraged dandelions free of pesticides and herbicides.

Quantities

40–50 fresh wild dandelion blossoms (enough to fill a 2-cup measure when packed, petals only)
1 cup breadcrumbs
1 small red onion, minced
4 fresh mustard green leaves, finely minced
2 sprigs fresh parsley, stems removed and finely minced
1 medium egg
2 tablespoons Herbal-Infused Blessed Butter
1 teaspoon horseradish
3 tablespoons tomato ketchup
Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

Trim the dandelion blossoms, removing all bitter greens so only the yellow petals remain. Combine the loose petals in a large mixing bowl with the breadcrumbs, minced onion, mustard greens, and parsley and blend thoroughly. Beat an egg in a small bowl, then pour it over the herb and breadcrumb mixture, tossing until everything is evenly coated. Scoop approximately one-quarter of the mixture, roll into a ball, and press into a patty; repeat with the remaining mixture. Melt the Herbal-Infused Blessed Butter in a medium skillet over medium heat and fry the patties for about 4 minutes, then flip once and cook until golden brown. Rinse and dry the bowl; combine the horseradish and tomato ketchup, season with salt and pepper, and stir thoroughly. Serve this spicy condiment spooned over the warm patties.
