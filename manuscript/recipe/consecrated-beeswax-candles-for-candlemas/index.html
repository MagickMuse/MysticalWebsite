🕯️Consecrated Beeswax Candles for Candlemas

Type
Tool
Plants2
BasilRose
Timing2
🕯️CandlemasImbolc

Handrolled honeycomb beeswax candles pressed with Imbolc herbs and anointed with rose oil. Created and lit at Candlemas as a ritual of light-bringing, with a spoken blessing charm to consecrate all ingredients before rolling.

Candlemas celebrates the first true light in the darkness of winter, and what better magick than to create and light your own candles as an act of devotion. Pressing herbs into the warm beeswax anchors seasonal intention directly into the flame.

Quantities

  • Honeycomb beeswax sheet
  • Cotton string (for wicks)
  • Essential oil of rose
  • Selection of Imbolc herbs: basil, coltsfoot, snowdrops, or tansy
  • Hair dryer

Blessing Charm

Before beginning, bless all ingredients with:

Blessed be the Bee
Blessed be the Cotton
Blessed be the Herbs
Burned but Not Forgotten
Blessed be the Light
And the Wheel as it Turns
Blessed be the Candles
And the Love with Which They Burn.

Directions

  1. Allow the beeswax sheet to come to room temperature until it is soft and pliable. If needed, use a hair dryer on the lowest setting to gently warm it, being careful not to melt the wax.
  2. Cut the cotton wick to length. Anoint the wick with rose oil and set it aside. Press your chosen herbs gently into the surface of the warmed wax sheet.
  3. Turn the sheet over. Lay the wicked string along one vertical edge, leaving half an inch extending beyond each end. Press the string into the wax and begin rolling carefully and evenly from that edge. The tighter and more even the roll, the better the candle will burn.
  4. Trim the bottom wick flush and leave a quarter inch at the top. Light your candle and let its light mark the return of the sun.

Magickal Notes

Beeswax is one of the most magickally potent candle materials, holding the energy of bees and the hive. The herbs pressed inside release their properties as the candle burns. These candles make powerful altar lights for Imbolc and Candlemas rites.
