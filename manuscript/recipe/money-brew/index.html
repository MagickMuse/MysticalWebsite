💰Money Brew

◇Directions
Half-fill a green glass bottle with fresh water. Add about a handful of the mixed, empowered herbs. Cap tightly and leave in full sunlight all day. At dusk, check the scent. If strong, strain and use. If not strong enough, chill overnight and return to the Sun the following day.
◇Type
Wash
◇Quantities
- 3 parts Sassafras - 2 parts Cedar - 1 part Allspice - 1 part Clove - 1 part Dill - 1 part Vetivert - 1 part Calamus
◇Description
A solar-charged money-drawing wash. Add to baths, wash hands before financial dealings, or anoint money charms and talismans.
