🍑Stone Fruit Whiskey

◇Type
Infusion
↗Correspondences3
🛋️Comfort💕Love🛡️Protection
↗Materials1
🥃Bourbon
↗Plants5
ApricotCherryPeachPlumVanilla

A patient 3-6 month whiskey infusion made from stone fruit pits, carrying protective and loving energy perfect for offerings to Demeter and Venus.

Quantities

  • 1 quart mason jar with lid
  • 750ml whiskey or bourbon
  • 1 cup stone fruit pits (cherry, peach, apricot, plum; any combination)
  • 1 vanilla bean, split (optional; adds love and comfort energy)

Directions

Collect stone fruit pits throughout the summer season. Wash them well and let them dry completely; the pits contain the seed of the fruit's potential, its concentrated magickal essence. Place the dried pits in your mason jar. As you add each one, speak an intention: protection, love, sweetness, or whatever you seek. If using vanilla, split the bean lengthwise and add it to the jar. Pour the whiskey over the pits until they're fully submerged. Seal the jar tightly and store in a cool, dark place. Shake the jar once weekly while repeating your intention. After 3 to 6 months, strain the whiskey through cheesecloth into a clean bottle. The resulting infusion carries the hidden heart energy of the stone fruits combined with the solar fire of bourbon. Use in rituals, as offerings, or sip mindfully. Do not eat the pits themselves; the infusion extracts the flavor and magickal properties safely into the alcohol.
