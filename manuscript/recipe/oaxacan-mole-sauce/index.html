🕯️Oaxacan Molé Sauce

💀Dias de los Muertos

A sacred Mexican sauce blending chocolate and chili peppers, traditionally placed on ofrendas during Día de los Muertos.

Quantities

One 30-ounce can red enchilada sauce
Two to four blocks Mexican hot chocolate mix (approximately 8 ounces or to taste)
Three cloves of garlic, finely minced
One-quarter teaspoon ground oregano
Ground cinnamon to taste
Two cups chicken broth or consommé
Dried red chili flakes to taste
Toasted sesame seeds for garnish

Directions

Add the enchilada sauce to a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the blocks of chocolate, garlic, oregano, and cinnamon. When the chocolate has dissolved, add the chicken broth. Stir and taste, then add the red chili flakes as desired. Simmer for an hour, adding more broth if the mixture becomes dry. Spoon the hot molé sauce over the main dish, traditionally turkey but also served over roasted chicken and Mexican dishes such as enchiladas and tamales, and sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds. The sauce is best served the next day so that the flavors can meld.

About This Recipe

This sauce is traditionally associated with Quetzalcoatl and the Days of the Dead in Oaxaca. Turkey molé with sesame seeds (representing happiness) is placed on ofrendas to delight visiting spirits.

The original recipe has over 30 ingredients and takes days to make; this is Angela Villalba's adapted version.

Serving Suggestions

Traditionally served over turkey, but also excellent over roasted chicken and Mexican dishes such as enchiladas and tamales. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds.

Note

The sauce is best served the next day so that the flavors can meld.
