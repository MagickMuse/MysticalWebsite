🫒Autumn Olive Joy

◇Directions
1. Drain all the olives and layer them together in a large glass jar with a lid. 2. Using a mortar and pestle, macerate the thyme, rosemary, sage, parsley, and garlic together to release their oils. Add the macerated herbs to the olive jar and toss to combine. Sprinkle over the crushed bay laurel and a dash of black pepper. Pour the olive oil over everything until the olives are fully submerged, then seal with the lid. 3. Steep in the refrigerator for 2 weeks. When ready, remove the olives with a slotted spoon and transfer to a serving bowl. The fragrant infused oil that remains can be used for dipping breads, making dressings, or as a ritual anointing oil.
◇Type
Food
◇Quantities
1 cup pitted black olives 1 cup green Spanish olives with pimento 1 cup pitted kalamata olives 4 sprigs fresh thyme 4 sprigs fresh rosemary 1 large sprig fresh sage 1 large sprig fresh parsley 3 dried bay laurel leaves, crushed 1–3 garlic cloves, to taste Dash black pepper 3 cups extra-virgin olive oil Large glass jar with lid
◇Description
A richly herbed olive blend steeped in extra-virgin olive oil, a symbolic and delicious addition to autumn ritual feasting. Rooted in the mythology of Athena and the Eleusinian Mysteries honoring Demeter, this recipe channels the harvest energy of Mabon and Samhain. The infused oil left over from the olives can be used for breads, dressings, or ritual anointing.
