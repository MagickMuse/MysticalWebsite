🍓Strawberry Moon Happiness Honey Syrup

Type
Syrup

A honey infusion jar spell crafted under the Strawberry Moon. Lavender-stuffed strawberries are engraved with intentions and submerged in honey, which draws out their sweetness and magickal energy over two weeks. The resulting syrup can be used in drinks, teas, fruit salads, or as a honey jar spell base for love, happiness, and harmony. Also known as the Honey Moon and Mead Moon — optionally pair with mead as an offering or shared toast under the moon.

Quantities

Fresh strawberries, enough to loosely fill desired jar
Dried lavender (approx. 1 tbsp per ½ cup honey)
Honey, enough to fill the jar

Directions

Hold the fresh strawberries in your hands and tune into their energy: happiness, love, and the sweetness of the Strawberry Moon. Eat one if inspired. Use a knife to cut off the tops and hollow out the center of each strawberry. Fill the cavity with dried lavender, as though inviting peace into your own center. Using a toothpick, lightly engrave each strawberry with your intention, your name, or a partner's name if working a commitment or love spell. Nestle the berries loosely into the jar, leaving room for honey. Sprinkle additional lavender over the berries generously, letting it rain down like the Strawberry Moon's joy and harmony. Pour honey slowly over everything, sealing the energy and drawing your desires toward you. Close the lid and store in the fridge. Shake every other day to encourage the strawberry juice to infuse throughout the honey. Allow to infuse for 2 weeks until the next new moon. Taste and adjust if needed. Use in drinks, teas, fruit salads, or lunar lemonades, or incorporate into a honey jar spell for love and happiness.
