A 6-week herb-infused olive oil made with basil, mint, catnip, bay laurel, and clove — started at the new moon and strained at the full moon for maximum prosperity potency.

About This Recipe

This infused oil uses herbs from the mint family, a lineage long associated with prosperity and financial abundance, anchored with bay laurel for achievement and clove for acceleration. The timing is lunar: begun at the new moon when intention is planted, and strained at the full moon approximately six weeks later when energy is at its peak. The oil can be used to anoint the twig in the Herbal Money Tree ritual, to dress candles in prosperity spellwork, or to apply to the hands and pulse points before financial negotiations or money-drawing work.

You Will Need

  • Fresh basil
  • Fresh mint
  • Fresh catnip
  • Fresh bay laurel leaves
  • 12 whole dried clove buds
  • Olive oil (enough to fully cover all herbs)
  • 1 clean glass jar with a tight-fitting lid
  • Cheesecloth for straining

Instructions

  1. Begin at the new moon. Combine the fresh basil, mint, catnip, and bay laurel in a clean glass jar.
  2. Add the 12 clove buds.
  3. Pour olive oil over the herbs until everything is fully submerged. Seal the jar tightly.
  4. Label with the date and store in a cool, dark location.
  5. Allow to infuse for approximately 6 weeks. As the moon waxes to full (roughly one and a half lunar cycles later), strain the oil through cheesecloth into a clean jar, pressing the plant matter to extract as much oil as possible.
  6. Discard the spent herbs and seal the finished oil. It is now ready to use.

How to Use

  • Anoint the twig in the Herbal Money Tree ritual before wrapping with herbs.
  • Dress green or gold candles before lighting in abundance spells.
  • Apply a small amount to the hands or wrists before financial work, interviews, or negotiations.
  • Add a few drops to a sachet with dried mint and bay laurel as a wallet or money-drawer charm.

Notes

The number twelve (one clove per month of the year) ties the oil to the full cycle of time and abundance. Olive oil is a traditional prosperity base; it is strongly associated with peace, plenty, and divine blessing. Fresh herbs are preferred over dried here; their living energy is part of what you are capturing in the infusion.
