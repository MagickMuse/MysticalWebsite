Mexican-Style Cleansing Bath

◇Directions
Boil stinging nettles in water. Allow the cooking liquid to cool. Make a separate infusion by pouring boiling water over rosemary and spearmint. Allow to steep and strain. Blend the nettle liquid and the rosemary and spearmint infusion together. Add the combined liquid to a drawn bath and bathe.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
- Stinging nettles, a generous handful - Rosemary and spearmint, a generous handful of each
◇Description
A three-herb cleansing bath drawing on the sharp purifying sting of nettles combined with the cleansing and uplifting properties of rosemary and spearmint. The nettle cooking liquid and the rosemary and spearmint infusion are blended before being added to the bath.
