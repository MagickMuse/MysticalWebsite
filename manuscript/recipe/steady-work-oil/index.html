🫙Steady Work Oil

Closed Practice
Hoodoo
Type
Oil
Plants4
BenzoinJoe-Pye WeedJojobaSunflower

The Steady Work Formula ingredients are ground and placed in a glass bottle, then covered with sunflower and jojoba oil to create Steady Work Oil. Rubbed on palms and soles when going to work or job hunting. ⚠️ Closed Practice: Steady Work is a Hoodoo condition formula rooted in African American folk magick. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

  • Benzoin resinoid
  • Gravel root
  • Sea salt
  • Sunflower oil
  • Jojoba oil
  • Small glass bottle or jar

Directions

Grind benzoin resinoid, gravel root, and sea salt together and place in a small glass bottle or jar. Cover with sunflower and jojoba oil to create Steady Work Oil. Rub on your palms and soles when going to work or looking for employment, or use to dress candles and mojo hands dedicated to the job search.
