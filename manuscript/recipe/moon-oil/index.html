🌕Moon Oil

◇Directions
1. On the night of the new moon, combine the essential oils, sandalwood powder, and carrier oil in the bottle. Allow the blend to infuse throughout the waxing phase, drawing in the growing lunar energy. 2. On the night of the full moon, leave the bottle out to charge under the moonlight. 3. Store away from sunlight. Use to anoint candles, crystals, and magickal tools for lunar workings. For psychic work: anoint the third eye and meditate while holding moonstone, labradorite, or lapis lazuli. Test on skin before use.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
6 drops lemon essential oil 12 drops jasmine essential oil 10 drops myrrh essential oil Pinch of sandalwood powder 2 tbsp fractionated coconut oil (olive or almond work as substitutes) 1-ounce bottle
◇Description
A magickally infused lunar oil blended to attune to the moon's energy for psychic awakening, manifestation, and deep lunar connection. Crafted during the waxing phase and charged under the full moon, this versatile oil can be used to dress candles, anoint crystals, bless spellcasting tools, and open psychic senses. Anoint the third eye and meditate with moonstone, labradorite, or lapis lazuli to deepen your lunar practice.
