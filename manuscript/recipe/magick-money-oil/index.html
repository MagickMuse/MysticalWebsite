💰Magick Money Oil

◇Directions
Wash the bottle and gemstone with mild soap and water, then dry thoroughly. Gather all ingredients and cast a circle around your workspace. Pour the carrier oil into the green bottle. Add several drops of peppermint essential oil and a pinch of glitter. Drop the tiger's eye or aventurine into the mixture. Seal the bottle and shake three times to charge the potion. Open the circle. Your Magick Money Oil is ready to use however you choose. Dressing Candles for Extra Power: Rub this oil on candles before burning them to amplify their effectiveness. Pour a small amount into your palms, then apply to the candle starting from the middle. To attract or increase something, rub upward toward the top (avoid the wick). To decrease or repel something like cutting expenses, rub downward toward the base. Oils associated with money (cedar, sandalwood, mint, clove, cinnamon) can also be used this way.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
- Green glass bottle with lid or stopper - Tiger's eye or aventurine (small piece) - 4 ounces carrier oil (olive, almond, or grapeseed) - Peppermint essential oil (a few drops) - Gold or silver glitter
◇Description
A versatile prosperity potion with many applications. Use it to dress candles for increased power, anoint talismans or gemstones, charge magickal tools, or apply to your body. This oil attracts all forms of abundance and can enhance other prosperity workings.
