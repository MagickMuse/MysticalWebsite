🌿Basil & Fumitory Prosperity Floorwash

Type
Wash
Correspondences5
💼Business✨Cleansing💵Fortune💰Prosperity💵Wealth
Plants2
BasilFumitory

A two-step prosperity working combining a basil floor wash with fumitory (Earth Smoke) incense burned afterward. Fumitory has ancient prosperity uses dating to Neolithic times and is mentioned in Chaucer's Canterbury Tales.

Quantities

  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Fumitory (Earth Smoke) incense

Directions

Pour boiling water over shredded fresh basil leaves. Allow to cool, then strain out the basil. Add the liquid to a bucket of floor wash rinse water and use it to wash the floors. Once the floor has dried, burn fumitory (Earth Smoke) incense throughout the space.
