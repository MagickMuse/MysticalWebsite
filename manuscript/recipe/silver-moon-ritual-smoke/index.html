🌕Silver Moon Ritual Smoke

◇Type
Incense
↗Plants3
FrankincenseRoseSandalwood

A more elaborate Full Moon incense featuring floral notes of gardenia and rose with the rich depth of ambergris oil.

Quantities

2 parts Sandalwood, 2 parts Frankincense, 1/2 part Gardenia petals, 1/4 part Rose petals, a few drops Ambergris oil

Directions

Burn during Full Moon rituals or to attune with the Moon. The floral additions create a more romantic, mystical atmosphere.
