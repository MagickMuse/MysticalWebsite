Van Van Oil

◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Directions
Dress candles, mojo hands, and charm bags. Rub on the soles of the feet to clear obstacles before walking into a difficult situation. Add a few drops to floor washes for whole-home cleansing and luck-drawing.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
Formula pending — see original Formulary source for the traditional lemongrass-based recipe.
◇Description
A classic Hoodoo condition oil with a sharp, lemony scent built on a base of lemongrass and other citrus-grass herbs. Used to clear away bad luck, draw good fortune, energize charms, and dress mojo hands. One of the most widely used and recognized formulas in the tradition. ⚠️ Closed Practice Note: Van Van Oil is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.
