🌾Mother Holies Cleansing Spell

◇Type
Incense
↗Plants13
AvensElderElecampaneGerman ChamomileMintMugwortMulleinPansyRoman ChamomileSlippery ElmSouthernwoodVervainYarrow

Overseen by Mother Holle (Hulde), Teutonic deity of love, sex, birth, and death. Twelve herbs gathered at Midsummer are dried, matured, and burned as a cleansing incense powder during the winter solstice season through January 6th.

Quantities

  • Equal parts:
  • Avens
  • Chamomile
  • Elder
  • Elecampane
  • Heartsease
  • Mint
  • Mugwort
  • Mullein
  • Slippery elm
  • Southernwood
  • Vervain
  • Yarrow

Directions

Traditionally, gather these twelve herbs during Midsummer's revels, then preserve and allow them to dry and mature. Use between the winter solstice and January 6th. Grind the botanicals into a powder and burn, wafting the smoke into all corners and crevices of the home.
