A Hoodoo condition oil carrying the essence of Saint Cyprian (San Cipriano), patron of occultists and magick workers. Used for protection, reconciliation, court case and anti-harassment workings, and calling on Saint Cyprian's intercession. ⚠️ San Cipriano Oil is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

  • Cinnamon powder
  • Orrisroot (pulverized)
  • Myrrh resin or myrrh essential oil
  • Cedarwood essential oil
  • Cypress essential oil
  • Bethroot (Trillium erectum)
  • Base oil: olive, safflower, sunflower, and/or jojoba oil

Directions

Pulverize all dry ingredients (cinnamon, orrisroot, myrrh resin if using, and bethroot) into a fine powder. Place the pulverized dry ingredients into a clean glass jar or bottle. Cover the dry ingredients completely with your choice of carrier oil; olive, safflower, sunflower, and/or jojoba oil work well. Add the essential oils (cedarwood, cypress, and myrrh if using essential oil instead of resin) drop by drop, testing the fragrance as you go. Continue adding essential oils until you achieve a favorable, balanced fragrance. Seal the jar and store in a cool, dark place, shaking gently before each use. This oil is traditionally used to dress candles, anoint objects, or add to other spell workings. It is especially potent for reconciliation spells, protection work, and any magickal endeavor.

Traditional Uses

  • Dressing candles for reconciliation spells
  • Anointing magickal tools and altar items
  • Protection workings
  • Love and relationship magick
  • Enhancing any magickal practice

Also Known As

Oil of Saint Cyprian.

About Saint Cyprian

Saint Cyprian (San Cipriano) was a third-century bishop and martyr who, according to legend, was a powerful sorcerer before his conversion. That history makes him a natural patron for occultists, magick workers, and anyone who needs a spiritual advocate in a difficult situation.

Court Case Uses

  • One of the three core Hoodoo court case condition oils
  • Dress brown and purple candles for legal victory
  • Anoint mojo hands and charm bags
  • Carry on a cotton ball during legal proceedings
  • Combines freely with Courtcase Oil and Fiery Wall of Protection Oil
