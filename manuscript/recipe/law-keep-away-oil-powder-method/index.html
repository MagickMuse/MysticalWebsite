🧪Law Keep Away Oil: Powder Method

An alternative preparation of Law Keep Away Oil: botanicals are ground into a fine powder, placed in a bottle, and covered with the olive/castor/jojoba oil blend without straining.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

  • High John the Conqueror root
  • Courtcase root (galangal)
  • Dried hydrangea blossoms
  • Asafetida powder
  • Oil blend: 2 parts olive oil + 1 part castor oil + 1 part jojoba oil

Directions

Grind High John the Conqueror root, Courtcase root, hydrangea blossoms, and asafetida into a fine powder. Note that High John is an exceptionally hard root and difficult to grind. Place the powder in a bottle. Cover with the oil blend (2 parts olive, 1 part castor, 1 part jojoba). The powder remains in the oil rather than being strained out. Use to dress vigil candles or rub onto doorknobs.

⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: Law Keep Away Oil is a Hoodoo condition oil. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

This is an alternative preparation method for Law Keep Away Oil. Instead of infusing and straining, the botanicals are powdered and left suspended in the oil.

  1. Grind the botanicals into a fine powder:
    • High John the Conqueror root (note: this is an extremely hard, dense root — grinding it is challenging. Break it into smaller pieces first, or use a dedicated spice grinder)
    • Courtcase root (galangal)
    • Dried hydrangea blossoms
    • Asafetida powder (already a powder; simply measure and add)
  2. Place the ground botanical mixture into a bottle.
  3. Cover with the oil blend: 2 parts olive oil + 1 part castor oil + 1 part jojoba oil.
  4. The powder is left in the oil rather than strained out.

Uses for the Oil

  • Vigil candles: Dress candles and keep them burning until all danger has passed.
  • Doorknobs: Rub the oil onto your doorknobs. Authority figures who touch them are said to turn away and leave.
