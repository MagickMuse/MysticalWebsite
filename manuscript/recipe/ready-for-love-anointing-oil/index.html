💘Ready for Love Anointing Oil

Use this oil to signal readiness for a new romantic partner.

Quantities

  • 1 drop basil essential oil to draw love
  • 2 drops tangerine essential oil for love and joy
  • 1 drop patchouli essential oil to arouse lust
  • 1 drop lavender essential oil for love
  • 2 tablespoons jojoba oil

Directions

Blend essential oils with carrier oil in a dark glass container. Anoint candles for love spells to signal readiness for love. This spell will not remove the free will of others. Alternatively, mix one drop of synergy per 100 ml water and omit the jojoba oil.
