A formula oil that pairs one botanical with each of the Seven African Powers in a palm oil base, used to call on their combined blessings and protection. ⚠️ Seven African Powers Oil draws on Hoodoo and Santería/Lucumí. Both are closed practices meant only for those within the originating cultures.

Quantities

Palm oil base (or substitute)
Real coconut extract
Real almond extract
Black pepper
Mugwort
Cinnamon
Seaweed
Mimosa, jasmine, or a pinch of tobacco (choose one)

Directions

Blend seven botanicals, one for each of the orishas, into a palm oil base; another carrier oil may stand in for palm oil if needed. Hold your goals and desires firmly in mind the whole time you prepare the oil.

Notes

Traditionally dressed onto a seven-knob candle, with one orisha or saint carved on each knob, beginning with Eshu-Elegbara. See Seven African Powers Wish Spell.
