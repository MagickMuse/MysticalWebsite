🎃Samhain Oil

◇Directions
Combine all the ingredients in a small bottle and leave in a sunny window until ready for use.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
1 tablespoon sunflower or olive oil 1 tablespoon calendula flowers 1 tablespoon marigold flowers 1 piece frankincense resin
◇Description
A solar-charged oil blend for Samhain using calendula, marigold, and frankincense. Leave in a sunny window until ready for use.
