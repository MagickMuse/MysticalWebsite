🌿Stress Tea

◇Type
Drink
↗Plants5
ElderGerman ChamomileHopsRosehipValerian

A soothing herbal tea blend to chase away pain, stress, and negativity while inviting positive energy.

Quantities

  • 1 tsp English Breakfast tea
  • 1 tsp Chamomile
  • 1 tsp Elder flower
  • 2 tsp Hops
  • 2 tsp Rose hips
  • 1 tsp Valerian root

Directions

Brew using the standard method for a good pot of tea. As each herb is added to the pot, speak its line of the incantation aloud. Once the tea is steeped, hold the cup and say the closing words before drinking.

Incantation

Speak each line as the corresponding herb is added to the pot:

English Breakfast tea: "Black is for strength, boosting the power herein,"

Chamomile: "Yellow is for health and calming the nervous within;"

Elder flower: "White is for purifying with healing and rest,"

Hops: "Beige is for gentleness and feeling my best;"

Rose hips: "Red is for energy to overcome stress,"

Valerian root: "Root is for steady protection made manifest."

Closing Words

Hold your cup and speak before drinking:

"Chase away pain and stress; chase away all duress; chase away the negative; open up the positive."
