Closed Practice Notice: This spell is rooted in Hoodoo court case magick. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

Foul-mouthed (Casearia hirsuta) earned its English nickname from its alleged power to make the foul-mouthed shut up. Its Spanish name, raspa lengua, tells the same story. In court case workings, this powder is not aimed at winning outright — it works by weakening your opponents, particularly those whose words can harm you.

  1. Pulverize foul-mouthed into a fine powder.
  2. Blend it thoroughly with Cascarilla Powder, white sugar, and cinnamon to create a smooth, fine mixture.
  3. Sprinkle the powder on or around the chair or bench where the prosecutor or opposing attorney will sit. This is said to make them quiet and less inclined to speak.

Should the opposing attorney step on the powder, its effects are said to intensify: they may lose their voice, or their speech may become slurred as though they had been drinking. In either case, foul-mouthed works to cause verbal errors and may even make the attorney unwilling to continue representing your adversary.
