🚫Begone! Banishing Powder

◇Type
Powder
↗Correspondences1
🚪Banishment
↗Rituals1
🌿Banishing Powders (Vermin)

A banishing powder of similar intensity to the basic formula, incorporating bay laurel and hydrangea alongside classic hot ingredients. Used in the same manner as the Basic Banishing Powder.

Quantities

  • Chopped bay laurel leaves
  • Black pepper
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Powdered hydrangea blossoms
  • Sea salt

Directions

Grind all ingredients together to form a powder. Sprinkle on the target's clothing (especially shoes), on the ground for the target to step over, or around and over candles to enhance other banishing workings.
