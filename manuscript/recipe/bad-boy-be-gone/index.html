🍸Bad Boy Be Gone

Type
Drink
Correspondences5
🔍Clarity👻Exorcism😊Happiness💕Love🛡️Protection
Materials3
🍸Cocktail Shaker🍸Gin🍸Martini Glass
Plants3
ApricotCherryLemon

A banishing cocktail to exorcise the ghosts of past relationships from your memory and libido.

Quantities

  • 1 cocktail shaker
  • 1 martini glass
  • Ice
  • 1½ oz gin (protection from ghosts)
  • 1½ oz apricot brandy (defense from evil, banishing distrust)
  • Juice from half a lemon (calming, clarity, love)
  • Cherry for garnish (happiness, love)

Directions

Place all ingredients minus the cherry into the cocktail shaker with ice.

As you shake, visualize the person or memory you're banishing. See their face dissolving, their hold on you weakening with each shake.

Empower the potion by shaking it as hard as you can, until frost forms on the metal of the cocktail shaker. The frost signals the energy has been raised and the working is ready.

Strain into a martini glass and garnish with a cherry.

As you drink, feel the past releasing its grip. You are free.

About This Working

We all have someone in our past that reminds us of how nice things used to be, but our memories lie to us. Things were never as rosy as our hormones want us to think they were.

Don't text that someone. Banish them from your bedroom and your libido with this cocktail.
