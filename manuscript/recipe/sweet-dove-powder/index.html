🕊️Sweet Dove Powder

◇Directions
Grind and powder bay laurel leaves, carnations, cardamom, cloves, marjoram, myrrh, and rose petals together until a fine, uniform powder is achieved. Sprinkle throughout the home to help create joy.
◇Type
Powder
◇Quantities
Bay laurel leaves Carnations Cardamom Cloves Marjoram Myrrh Rose petals
◇Description
A powdered incense blend of bay laurel, carnation, cardamom, cloves, marjoram, myrrh, and rose petals, sacred to spirits of primal female power including Aphrodite, Asherah, and Maria Padilha. Sprinkled throughout the home to create joy and harmony.
