💧Rid of Negatives Oil (Personal)

Type
Oil

A personal oil to clear and banish negative energy, harmful influences, and toxic patterns from the self and surroundings.

Quantities

  • 4 drops Patchouli
  • 1 drop Pine
  • 1 drop Rue

Directions

Blend into a carrier oil base. Apply to pulse points or dress black candles to banish negative energy, thoughts, or influences.

Ingredients

OilAmount
Patchouli4 drops
Pine1 drop
Rue1 drop
⚠️
Toxicity warning: Rue (Ruta graveolens) essential oil is a potent skin irritant and phototoxin. It is also contraindicated during pregnancy. Use with strong dilution and avoid sun exposure after topical application. Some practitioners prefer to use rue symbolically or as a dried herb rather than as an essential oil.

How to Make This Oil

Yield sizes:

  • Small batch (1–2 oz) — personal use, anointing candles
  • Large batch (6–8 oz) — massage

Steps

  1. Fill your bottle three-quarters full with a carrier oil of your choice (almond, jojoba, or similar).
  2. Using an eye dropper, add each essential oil one at a time, swirling gently after each addition.
  3. Once all oils are combined, hold the bottle between your hands and focus on banishment — visualize all negativity dissolving and being pushed out — charging the oil with that clearing force.
  4. Cap and label the bottle with the name and date.
  5. Swirl gently before each use to re-integrate the blend.

Dropper Care

Unless your bottle has a built-in dropper, flush with rubbing alcohol between each oil — draw in and push out several times — to prevent scent contamination.

Aromatherapy Use

To use in a diffuser, omit the carrier oil. Drop the essential oils directly into the diffuser water.

Preservation

Add 1 drop of benzoin to the bottle as a fixative to prevent rancidity (at least 2 drops in massage oils). A small chip of an appropriate crystal may also be added.

Candle Magick

To double-charge a candle spell, choose 2–3 appropriate herbs, grind finely, spread on a paper towel, and roll the dressed candle over the herbs before lighting.
