🥚Hertha House Protection: The Buried Egg🔢Square of Saturn House Protection Talisman🛡️The Grand Magical Circle of Defence

An earth incense for all Earth spells and protective workings, including the grand magical circle of defence and the Hertha egg burial. May also be made into incense cones.

Quantities

  • Base (choose one):
  • Dried powdered juniper wood — 3 parts
  • Or cypress raspings or leaves — 3 parts
    Plus:
  • Gum myrrh or tincture of myrrh — 2 parts
  • Powdered orris root — 1/2 part
  • Powdered patchouli leaves (or a few drops patchouli oil) — 1 part
  • Powdered sage — 1 part
    For incense cones, also add:
  • Saltpetre — 1/4 part
  • Patchouli or myrrh oil — enough to make a thick paste

Directions

Reduce all dry ingredients finely by trituration (grinding together progressively in pestle and mortar). For loose grain incense, the blend is complete.

For incense cones, add the saltpetre and enough oil to form a thick paste. Model into cones by hand and allow to stand until fully dry before use.
