The classic all-purpose spiritual cologne of Hoodoo and Latin American folk traditions: a citrus, spice, and floral blend in an alcohol base. Distinct from specific named variants like Florida Water Cologne or Better Business Floorwash, this is the plain traditional splash referenced as a scent addition in ancestor and blessing work.

Quantities

2 cups high-proof alcohol (vodka or rubbing alcohol); 1 teaspoon bergamot essential oil; 1 teaspoon lemon essential oil; 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon essential oil; 1/2 teaspoon clove essential oil; 1 teaspoon lavender essential oil; a few drops rose essential oil (optional)

Directions

Combine the alcohol in a clean bottle. Add the essential oils one at a time, shaking well after each addition. Let the blend rest for at least 24 hours, shaking occasionally, before use. Splash on the skin, add to bathwater, use as a floor wash, or spray around doorways and altars for a traditional all-purpose cleansing and blessing scent.
