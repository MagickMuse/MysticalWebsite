✨Wishing Powder

Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
Type

Correspondences1
🌟Wishes

SageSandalwoodTonka

A manifestation blend to help bring wishes into reality.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 2 parts Sage
  • 1 part Sandalwood
  • 1 part Tonka

Directions

In a secluded place, hold the powder in your dominant hand. Feel its energies and visualize your wish with perfect clarity. Raise power within yourself and channel it into the powder. When it pulses with energy, fling it as far as you can. As the powder touches the earth, it releases its energy and works to manifest your wish.
