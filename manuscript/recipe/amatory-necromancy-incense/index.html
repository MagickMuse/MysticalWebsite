💔Amatory Necromancy Incense

◇Directions
Blend all dry ingredients together, reducing each herb finely before combining. Moisten with a few drops each of olive oil, red wine, and honey, plus the operator's blood and a dash of chosen essential flower oil. Mix thoroughly. Blend on a Friday when the moon is waxing, then leave the mixture to steep overnight. Add additional dry herbs if the final consistency is too moist; the finished incense should hold together but remain burnable. Burn on a charcoal disc.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
- 1 part vervain herb, finely reduced - 1 part wormwood herb, finely reduced - 1 part powdered sandalwood, lignum aloes, or rose petals - 1 part dittany of Crete, finely reduced - 1 part gum benzoin - A few drops pure olive oil - A few drops red wine - A few drops honey - A few drops of the operator's blood - A dash of essential flower oil of choice (a favourite of the deceased if possible)
◇Description
A love incense designed for amatory necromantic workings, combining philter and spirit-summoning herbs. Blended on a Friday when the moon is waxing and left to steep overnight. The operator's own blood is a traditional personal link ingredient; a favourite scent of the deceased may be used as the essential flower oil where the working involves the dead.
