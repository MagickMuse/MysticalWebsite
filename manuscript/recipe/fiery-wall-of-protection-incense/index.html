🔥Fiery Wall of Protection Incense

◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Directions
1. Blend salt, frankincense and myrrh into a powder. 2. Add dragon's blood powder to give it its protective red color. 3. If desired, consecrate the powder to Archangel Michael. 4. Burn it as incense, or use it as a magick powder.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
Salt Frankincense Myrrh Dragon's blood powder
◇Description
Fiery Wall of Protection powder, one of the most famous classic condition formulas, named for the flaming sword of Archangel Michael. Its protective base of salt, frankincense and myrrh is colored red, the color of protection, with dragon's blood powder. The dried powder is burned as incense or used as a magick powder, and blended into oil it becomes Fiery Wall of Protection Oil. ⚠️ Fiery Wall of Protection is a Hoodoo condition formula. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Approach with respect.
