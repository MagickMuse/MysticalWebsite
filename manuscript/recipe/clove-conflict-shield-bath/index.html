🛁Clove Conflict Shield Bath

◇Directions
Pour the boiling spring water over the ground cloves and let it steep until cool. Add the infusion to the bathwater. Immerse completely, ideally three times, taking care to protect the eyes.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
½ teaspoon ground cloves (or whole cloves for sensitive skin) 2 cups boiling spring water
◇Description
A clove infusion bath that bolsters personal strength and the aura before an anticipated spiritual, verbal, emotional, or physical conflict.

About This Recipe

Cloves are a strong protective agent, especially for building personal strength and the aura ahead of conflict, and they carry a rich, warm fragrance.

Uses

The bath is the strongest way to apply cloves' protection. Gentler alternatives:

  • Burn cloves and fumigate hair and clothing with the smoke.
  • Blend clove bud essential oil into a carrier oil and massage it into the hair so its fragrance radiates outward.
  • Add a few drops of clove bud essential oil to laundry rinse water so clothing carries the scent.

Notes

Cloves are powerful skin irritants and can raise welts even on resilient skin. Anyone with very sensitive skin should skip the bath and choose one of the alternatives above. For mildly sensitive skin, leave the cloves whole rather than ground: less volatile oil is released, which may be slightly less irritating.
