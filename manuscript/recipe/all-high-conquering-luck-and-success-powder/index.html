🍀All High Conquering Luck and Success Powder

Correspondences4

A powdered blend of High John the Conqueror, bay, and frankincense, carried or sprinkled to clear obstacles and draw luck and success.

⚠️ This is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

High John the Conqueror chips
Bay leaves
Frankincense

Directions

Grind High John the Conqueror chips, crumbled bay leaves, and frankincense separately, then blend into a unified powder. Carry the finished powder in a conjure bag or sprinkle it across thresholds, workspaces, or paths to clear obstacles and draw luck and success.
