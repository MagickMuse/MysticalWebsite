🛡️Spirits Be Gone Spray

Type
Spray
Correspondences3
✨Cleansing🛡️Protection🧹Purification
Plants1
Pine

A powerful banishing spray to remove unwelcome spirits from your home, made with vodka-macerated pine tips.

Quantities

  • 4 oz fresh pine tips
  • Pint jar
  • Vodka (to fill jar)
  • Spray bottle
  • Water (to fill spray bottle)
  • Optional: 1 drop pine essential oil

Directions

Steep 4 ounces of fresh pine tips in a pint jar filled with vodka. Allow to macerate for two weeks, shaking occasionally. When ready to use, pour 2 ounces of the pine tincture into a spray bottle and fill the rest of the bottle with water. Add a drop of pine essential oil if desired. Spray the home liberally, paying special attention to corners, doorways, and anywhere that feels heavy or uncomfortable.
