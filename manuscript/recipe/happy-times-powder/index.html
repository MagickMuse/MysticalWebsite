✨Happy Times Powder

Type
Powder
Correspondences1
🍀Luck
Plants3
OrangeStrawberryVanilla

A luck-changing powder for improving fortune. Sprinkled around the perimeter of the home, behind doors, and in corners. The name expresses wishes and goals, not necessarily present reality — as with all spells, charms, and incantations, it is given in the present tense.

Quantities

Dried ground strawberry leaves
Dried powdered vanilla bean
Finely grated orange zest

Directions

Blend the dried ground strawberry leaves, dried powdered vanilla bean, and finely grated orange zest together, powdering further if necessary. Sprinkle the finished powder around the perimeter of your home, behind doors, and in all corners, for a change of luck and an improvement in fortune.

About This Powder

Magick spells, charms, and incantations are always given in the present tense. Happy Times Powder's name expresses wishes and goals, not necessarily present reality.
