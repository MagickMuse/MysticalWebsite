Quantities

Acorns, Barley, Honey, Ivy, Hellebore, Bay

Directions

Boil water in a cauldron over an open fire. Place all ingredients into the cauldron. Sit before it and entrance yourself by watching the flames. Smell its mystic scent and receive wisdom.

Caution

This brew is poisonous. Do not drink under any circumstances. The toxic ingredients (Ivy, Hellebore, Acorns) are for aromatic use only.
