🦴Do As I Say Oil

◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Directions
Grind sweet flag (calamus), myrrh, cedar chips, and peppermint together. Place the ground mixture into a bottle and cover with safflower, jojoba, and apricot kernel oil. Add a few drops of bergamot essential oil.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
• Sweet flag (calamus) • Myrrh • Cedar chips • Peppermint • Safflower oil (not yet in Oils database) • Jojoba oil • Apricot kernel oil • Bergamot essential oil
◇Description
A commanding condition oil that blends the commanding power of calamus with the aphrodisiac and seductive qualities of its botanicals. Also known as As You Please or As I Please Oil. Milder than most commanding oils, its intent is to inspire a genuine desire to please in others. Useful in both romantic and business contexts. ⚠️ Rooted in Hoodoo. Approach with respect for its African American folk magick origins.
