🕊️Born Again Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences1
💰Prosperity
Plants3
BonesetFrankincensePepper Tree

A comforting incense to burn when grieving the loss of a friend or loved one.

Quantities

  • 3 parts Frankincense
  • 1 part Mullein
  • 1 part Chrysanthemums

Directions

Grind the frankincense resin. Add crumbled dried mullein leaves and dried chrysanthemum petals. Blend thoroughly.

Burn when grieving the passing of a friend or loved one.
