Description

This is Sparta! An offensive or defensive magick oil for battling hexes. Use defensively to dissolve nasty hexes and ground hateful energy, or offensively as a warning: "Leave me alone, or I will hit back."

Quantities

  • 10 drops Salvia sclarea (Clary Sage Essential Oil) for centering, secrets, wisdom
  • 15 drops Foeniculum vulgare (Fennel Essential Oil) to battle hexes
  • 15 drops Cymbopogon citratus (Lemongrass Essential Oil) for physical energy, increased awareness, improved mood
  • 5 drops Vetiveria zizanioides (Vetiver Essential Oil) to balance and seal the aura
