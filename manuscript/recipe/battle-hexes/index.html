An offensive or defensive magick oil for battling hexes. Use defensively to dissolve nasty hexes and ground hateful energy, or offensively as a warning: "Leave me alone, or I will hit back."

Quantities

  • 10 drops clary sage essential oil for centering, secrets, wisdom
  • 15 drops fennel essential oil to battle hexes
  • 15 drops lemongrass essential oil for physical energy, increased awareness, improved mood
  • 5 drops vetiver essential oil to balance and seal the aura

Directions

Oil of action and warning. Add 1 drop synergy to 2 ml carrier oil to use as anointing oil. Add 1 drop synergy to 100 ml water diffuser to protect your home or space. Commendable for defensive magick but powerful for offense when needed.
