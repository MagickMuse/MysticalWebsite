🍭Magickal Lollipops

Type
Food
Correspondences1
✨Manifestation
Materials1
🍬Sugar

Homemade lollipops made with magickal tea instead of water. Lollipops disappear naturally as they are enjoyed, making them perfect for manifestation work. Stir clockwise to bring in desires, counterclockwise to banish.

Quantities

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 cup strong magickal tea
  • Nonflavored cooking spray
  • Optional: 1 1/2 tsp commercial flavorings
  • Optional: food coloring

Directions

Prepare your magickal space. Coat the inside of the mold with a small amount of cooking oil, then wipe away the excess; this should leave a light film of oil without making the candy oily. Place sticks in the molds if using them. Consider ringing a small bell to raise the vibration of your work space. Add corn syrup, sugar, and tea water to a medium-sized saucepan over medium heat. Stir consistently until the sugar is incorporated; stir clockwise to bring in a desire such as money, or counterclockwise to banish something such as fear. Once it is boiling, check the temperature with a candy thermometer; you want the mixture to reach the hard crack stage, or 300°F (around 150°C). Remove the saucepan from heat and place on a heatproof surface. Allow it to cool until the mixture is no longer bubbling, then stir in food coloring and flavor if desired. Gently pour the sugar mixture into molds, making sure to completely cover the top of each stick. If making candies instead, keep stirring for a few more moments, allowing the gel to cool further before pouring; it will be stickier but will hold shape better the colder it is when using a silicone baking mat. Allow the candies to cool completely before handling. Wrap and decorate if desired. Candy making moves fast, so make sure all tools are assembled before starting; avoid humid or rainy days as they can make it hard for the candy to set.
