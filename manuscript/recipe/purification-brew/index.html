✨Purification Brew

Type
Wash
Correspondences1
🧹Purification
Plants11

A traditional purification wash using nine sacred plants. Use for asperging the house, others, or yourself for ritual cleansing.

Quantities

  • Any nine sacred plants such as:
  • Vervain
  • Rue
  • Rosemary
  • Oak
  • Pine
  • Acacia
  • Rose
  • Carnation
  • Thyme
  • Basil
  • Jasmine

Directions

Collect any nine sacred plants. Place in a nonmetallic pot or bowl. Add rain water (or fresh water) and let the herbs soak, covered and away from light, for three days. Strain before use.
