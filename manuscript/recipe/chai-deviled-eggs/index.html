🥚Chai Deviled Eggs

◇Type
Food
↗Correspondences8
🦁Courage🌱Fertility⛓️Hex Breaking💕Love🍀Luck💰Prosperity🛡️Protection🧹Purification
↗Plants6
CardamomCinnamonCloveGingerPepperShallot
↗Timing1
🐰Ostara

Deviled eggs boiled in strong chai so the spices permeate the egg whites, perfect for Ostara when eggs are plentiful.

Quantities

  • Strong chai (enough to cover eggs by 1 inch)
  • 12 eggs (fertility) - hint: eggs a few days old peel easier
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard (memory, awareness, strength, courage)
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise (fertility, wishes granted, prosperity)
  • 1 tbsp minced shallot (protection, healing, strength)
  • 1 tsp hot sauce (banishing, purification, protection)
  • Salt, pepper, and smoked paprika to taste (purification, banishing, prosperity)
  • Cracked black pepper for garnish

Directions

Traditional chai uses spices like: cloves (hex breaking, prosperity, purification), cardamom (confidence, courage, purpose), ginger (love, passion, courage, hex breaking, luck), cinnamon (creativity, mood-lifting, protection).

Puncture the bottom of each eggshell with an egg piercer to make them easier to peel and allow the chai inside the shell.

Place the eggs into a saucepan and add enough cold, strong chai to cover the eggs by an inch. If you don't have enough tea, add water and a few peppercorns and cloves.

Place the lid on the saucepan and bring to a rolling boil over high heat. Boil for 8 minutes for a firm yolk.

Using a slotted spoon, transfer each egg to an ice bath to stop the cooking process and make peeling easier. Eggs can be stored for up to one week at this unpeeled stage.

Peel the eggs by swirling them around in cold water, bumping them into each other to loosen the shells. Cut each egg in half lengthwise with a sharp knife.

Place yolks in a bowl and egg white halves on a serving tray.

Mash each yolk into a crumbling powder with a fork. Add mayo, Dijon mustard, shallot, and hot sauce. Stir well before adding salt, pepper, and paprika.

Scoop the yolk mixture into a pastry bag (or plastic bag with tip cut off) and fill each egg half.

Top with cracked black pepper to complement the chai spices.
