🛁Orrisroot Protection Bath

◇Directions
Pour boiling water over sliced or ground orrisroot to make an infusion. Strain the liquid and add it to the bath.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities

◇Description
A protective bath made from an orrisroot infusion, drawn from Japanese magickal tradition.

About This Recipe

In European-derived magick, orrisroot serves almost entirely in love and seduction work. Japanese magickal traditions also value it as a source of spiritual protection.
