🌙Night Terror Banishing Syrup

Type

A soothing herbal syrup to banish nightmares and night terrors, promoting peaceful sleep and dream protection. Based on anise's traditional use for nightmare prevention.

Quantities

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 2 tablespoons anise seeds
  • 1 tablespoon dried lavender
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 bay leaf

Directions

Combine water, anise seeds, lavender, cinnamon stick, and bay leaf in a small pot. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let steep for another 15 minutes. Strain out all plant material. While the liquid is still warm (not hot), stir in honey until fully dissolved. Pour into a clean glass bottle and store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Take 1 teaspoon before bed, or add to warm milk or tea. Can also be used to anoint pillows or placed in a small dish by the bedside.
