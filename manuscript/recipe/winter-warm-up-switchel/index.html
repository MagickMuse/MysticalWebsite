🧣Winter Warm-Up Switchel

Type
Drink
Correspondences2
❤️‍🩹Healing🛡️Protection
Plants2
CloveGinger

A warming ginger and clove switchel designed to ward off colds and flu. Perfect for keeping the office healthy during cold season. Explain the drink as wanting to keep the office healthy while the sweetener has been charmed with intent to calm tempers and soothe egos.

Quantities

  • 4 cups water
  • 1 thumb ginger, chopped
  • 1 cup local honey (if you commute to work, try to find local honey closer to the office)
  • 1 cup apple cider vinegar (with Mother if you want probiotic benefits)
  • 1 teaspoon whole cloves

Directions

Chop ginger into chunks and add to saucepan with water. Bring the water to a boil and boil for 5 minutes. While heating, stir in honey until dissolved. Once it has boiled for 5 minutes, remove from heat and allow to cool to room temperature. Scoop out ginger. Once this mixture is cooled, stir in the vinegar. If the water is too hot when you add the vinegar, it will kill off the beneficial bacteria. Place in a pitcher and cool.
