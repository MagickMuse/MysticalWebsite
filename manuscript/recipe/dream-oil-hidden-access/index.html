💤Dream Oil: Hidden Access

◇Type
Oil
↗Correspondences1
💤Dreams
↗Plants3
HoneysuckleLilacMugwort

A honeysuckle and lilac infused oil for accessing buried memories from this lifetime and past ones. Commercially available lilac fragrance is virtually always synthetic; this oil must be made from fresh blossoms to carry any genuine power. Mugwort flower remedy is added after infusion for enhanced dream penetration.

Quantities

  • Fresh honeysuckle blossoms
  • Fresh lilac blossoms
  • Carrier oil (for infusion)
  • Mugwort flower essence remedy (a few drops, added after straining)

Directions

Create a flower petal-infused oil using fresh honeysuckle and lilac blossoms. Once the infusion is complete, strain out all plant material thoroughly. Add a few drops of mugwort flower essence remedy to the finished oil. Massage onto the body before bed or add to a bath to unlock buried memories from this or prior lifetimes.
