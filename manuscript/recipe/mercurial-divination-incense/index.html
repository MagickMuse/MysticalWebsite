A Mercurial incense base of gum mastic and cinnamon, mixed with any of seven traditional blend variants to support divination and spirit-communication workings.

Quantities

Gum mastic
Cinnamon (preferably in oil or essence form)

Blend 1: Wormwood, Flax, Cardamom, Anise, Camphor, Chicory
Blend 2: Orris root, Fennel, Pomegranate skin, Red sandalwood, Poppy seed
Blend 3: Valerian root, Saffron, Wormwood, St. John's Wort
Blend 4: Linseed, Psyllium
Blend 5: Violet roots, Parsley
Blend 6: Anise, Poppy seeds, Camphor
Blend 7: Cloves (powdered), Frankincense, Mastic, Cinquefoil

Optional additions (enhance any blend): Solomon's seal, Holy thistle
For materialization workings: add Dittany of Crete

Directions

Combine gum mastic and cinnamon (or cinnamon essence) as the base. Add any one or combination of the numbered blends. Fume on charcoal. To enhance the effect, incorporate Solomon's seal or holy thistle into any blend. When a materialization is required in the working, add a pinch of Dittany of Crete.
