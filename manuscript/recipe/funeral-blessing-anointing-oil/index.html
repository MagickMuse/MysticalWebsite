🕊️Funeral Blessing Anointing Oil

An anointing oil for blessing the deceased's remains or easing the grief of loved ones.

Quantities

1 drop angelica essential oil
1 drop clove essential oil
1 drop frankincense essential oil
1 drop myrrh essential oil
1 drop sandalwood essential oil
4 teaspoons (20 ml) carrier oil

Directions

Fill your container with 2 teaspoons carrier oil, and then add 1 drop angelica essential oil, 1 drop clove essential oil, 1 drop frankincense essential oil, 1 drop myrrh essential oil, and 1 drop sandalwood essential oil. Fill the container with remaining carrier oil, and put on the lid. Roll the container between your hands until the oils have been thoroughly mixed.
