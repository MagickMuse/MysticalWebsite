💕Love Ointment

A sweet, floral salve to open the heart and attract romantic connection.

Quantities

  • 4 drops Ylang-Ylang
  • 2 drops Lavender
  • 1 drop Cardamom
  • 1 drop Vanilla extract

Directions

Blend oils into a beeswax and carrier oil base. Apply to the body when seeking romantic love.
