💖Herbal Beauty Blessing of the Sex

A beeswax and jojoba anointing oil blended with personal essential oil selections for the Herbal Beauty Blessing of the Sex. Two suggested blends are provided as starting points: one built around oak moss, gardenia, and lilac, and another around patchouli, sandalwood, and lavender. The finished oil sets into a solid salve and yields two containers, one for personal use and one to gift or use for charging tools.

Quantities

  • Feminine Blend (optional):
  • 15 drops oak moss (base note)
  • 10 drops gardenia (middle note)
  • 3 drops lilac (top note)
    Masculine Blend (optional):
  • 10 drops patchouli (base note)
  • 10 drops sandalwood (middle note)
  • 3 drops lavender (top note)
    Base Recipe:
  • 1 ounce grated beeswax
  • 1 ounce jojoba oil
  • 3 drops essential oil blend of choice (per container)
  • 2 metal 1-ounce containers
  • Double boiler
  • Flat toothpicks

Directions

Melt the beeswax in the double boiler until fully liquid, then pour in the jojoba oil and stir the two together until smoothly combined. Divide the mixture evenly between the two metal containers. Add 3 drops of your chosen essential oil blend to each and work it in quickly with a toothpick before the wax begins to set. Allow both containers to cool and solidify fully before use.
