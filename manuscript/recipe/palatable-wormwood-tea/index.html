🧪Palatable Wormwood Tea

Type
Potion
Correspondences1
🧠Psychic Abilities

💧Water
Plants6

A pinch each of mugwort and wormwood masked with larger amounts of flavorful herbs, adjusted by trial until the famed bitterness disappears.

Quantities

1 pinch dried ground mugwort
1 pinch dried ground wormwood
Larger amounts of flavoring herbs (peppermint, lemon balm, licorice root, cinnamon, or any herbal flavor of choice)
Boiling water

Directions

Combine a pinch each of dried ground mugwort and wormwood with larger quantities of peppermint, lemon balm, licorice root, cinnamon, or any other herbal flavor you enjoy. Pour boiling water over the blend and infuse it moderately, for only five minutes, then strain and drink. Expect to discard the first few cups while adjusting the proportions until the blend hides the bitterness.

About This Recipe

Wormwood tastes even worse than mugwort, so this tea is best approached like a chemistry experiment, adjusting proportions until it becomes palatable.

Notes

Neither mugwort nor wormwood is safe for pregnant women or anyone actively trying to conceive. Never substitute either herb's essential oil for the dried herb.
