🫗Cast Off Evil Oil

Closed Practice
Hoodoo
Type
Oil
Correspondences4
⚔️Enemies⛓️Hex Breaking🛡️Protection😒Resentment
Materials1
🔘Cotton Ball
Plants6
AsafoetidaBay LaurelCastorHyssopRueYarrow

An oil of asafetida, bay, hyssop, rue, and yarrow in castor oil that casts off malevolent thoughts, intentions, and hexes sent by those who resent you. Mainly preventive, with a hex-breaking edge. ⚠️ Cast Off Evil Oil is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Asafetida
Crumbled bay leaves
Hyssop
Rue
Yarrow
Castor oil

Directions

Blend asafetida, crumbled bay leaves, hyssop, rue, and yarrow, then add the botanicals to castor oil. To use, soak cotton balls in the oil and leave them near the person who hates you, at the office or elsewhere. The spell cannot be hidden, since the smell can be quite unpleasant depending on how much asafetida you use, but it can still be cast anonymously if you are discreet.
