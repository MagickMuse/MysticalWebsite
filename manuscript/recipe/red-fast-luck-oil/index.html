🔴Red Fast Luck Oil

🎈Saint Expedite Spell

A New Orleans-style fast luck oil blended from cinnamon bark, wintergreen, and vanilla, colored red with Turkey red oil, alkanet, or red food coloring. Used to anoint cash, tools, stationery, machines, and business charms to quickly draw money and fortune.

Quantities

  • Essential oil of cinnamon bark
  • Essential oil of wintergreen
  • Vanilla absolute or pure vanilla extract
  • Carrier: Turkey red oil OR sunflower + jojoba oil
  • Colorant: alkanet or red food coloring (if not using Turkey red oil)

Directions

Blend cinnamon bark, wintergreen, and vanilla into your chosen carrier. The oil must be red: use Turkey red oil as the carrier, blend into sunflower and jojoba then dye with alkanet, or use red food coloring as a quick fix. Anoint all money in your cash drawer, tools, machines, stationery, and ledger books. If you don't work with cash, discreetly anoint a corner of all checks paid out. May be used to anoint any other better business charm.
