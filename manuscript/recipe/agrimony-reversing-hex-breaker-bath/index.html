🛁Agrimony Reversing Hex-Breaker Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Plants5
AgrimonyAngelicaCinquefoilCreeping CinquefoilLovage

An herbal infusion bath for reversing and repelling hexes. Agrimony is prized in magic circles for its ability to repel and return a hex.

Quantities

Angelica
Agrimony
Chamomile blossoms
Hydrangea flowers
Lovage
Tormentil or five-finger grass
Boiling water

Directions

Make an infusion by pouring boiling water over angelica, agrimony, chamomile blossoms, hydrangea flowers, lovage, and either tormentil or five-finger grass. When it cools, add everything to your bath.
