Rhodium Oil Spell to Lure Dogs Away

◇Directions
Blend the following essential oils with a carrier oil (such as jojoba) in proportions adjusted to achieve an effective fragrance: essential oil of cedarwood, essential oil of rose geranium, essential oil of palmarosa, essential oil of sandalwood. Use in one or more of the following ways: wear as a perfume; use the oil to mark a trail to lead dogs away.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
- Essential oil of cedarwood - Essential oil of rose geranium - Essential oil of palmarosa - Essential oil of sandalwood - Carrier oil (jojoba recommended)
◇Description
A folk perfume oil blend historically used by thieves to attract and lure dogs away. Rhodium oil mimics the scent of rhodium wood and is known to be appealing to dogs. Proportions are adjusted to personal preference.
