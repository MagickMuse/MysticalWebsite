Recipe Grimoire

🔮Scrying Secrets

◇Directions
Mix all essential oils and diffuse one drop synergy in 100 ml water while meditating, scrying, or divining. To scry with this oil, place 1 to 3 drops synergy in a dark-colored bowl half-filled with warm water. The warmth of the water helps release the scent of the oil. The shapes created by the oil on top of the water will help to trigger thoughts, words, images, and more within the mind's eye. The dark color of the bowl will help draw the eye into its depths, rather than merely reflecting the room. Scrying is most easily accomplished in a darkened room, with few distractions. Soft music can be helpful for tuning out distractions of the day.

Oil
◇Quantities
• 5 drops coriander essential oil for divination, secrets • 20 drops grapefruit essential oil for uplifting, purifying, mental clarity, scrying • 5 drops ylang-ylang essential oil to soothe, protect
◇Description
Use to divine the truth in obscured situations.
