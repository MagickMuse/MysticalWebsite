🧪Courtcase Root: Homemade Courtcase Oil

Closed Practice
Hoodoo

Courtcase root is the primary ingredient in Courtcase Oil. Grind galangal with black mustard seeds, add optional legal botanicals, and cover with a jojoba/sunflower oil blend.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

  • Courtcase root (galangal), primary amount
  • Black mustard seeds
  • Deer's tongue (optional)
  • Slippery elm (optional)
  • Jojoba and/or sunflower oil, enough to cover

Directions

Chop the Courtcase root into small pieces and blend with black mustard seeds, grinding together into a fine mixture. Add optional botanicals if desired. Place the blend in a bottle and cover completely with a jojoba/sunflower oil blend. Infuse for several weeks, or use immediately if needed.

Courtcase root (galangal) is the star botanical of Courtcase Oil, the foundational condition oil used across virtually all court case and legal victory workings.

See the full recipe on the Courtcase Oil page.

  1. Chop the Courtcase root into small pieces.
  2. Blend the chopped root with black mustard seeds and grind them together into a fine mixture.
  3. If desired, add other botanicals associated with legal victories — deer's tongue (for eloquence) and slippery elm (to guard against false testimony) are classic additions.
  4. Place the botanical mixture into a bottle.
  5. Cover completely with a blend of jojoba and sunflower oils.
  6. Allow to infuse for several weeks, or use immediately if needed.
