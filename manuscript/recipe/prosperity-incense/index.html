💰Prosperity Incense

◇Directions
Grind the frankincense. Add cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon balm, and citron. Blend thoroughly. Burn to attract wealth and prosperity.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
- Frankincense: 2 parts - Cinnamon: 1 part - Nutmeg: 1 part - Lemon Balm: 1 part - Citron: 1 part
◇Description
Burn to attract wealth and prosperity.
