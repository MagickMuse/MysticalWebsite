Use this devotional oil daily for spiritual connections.

Quantities

  • 10 drops Rose Geranium Essential Oil (Pelargonium graveolens) for spirituality
  • 20 drops Lemon Essential Oil (Citrus limon) for calming, clarity, love
  • 15 drops Sandalwood Essential Oil (Santalum album) for spirituality

Directions

Blend all essential oils in a 2 ml bottle and diffuse 1 drop synergy per 100 ml water during devotional work. Whether you are engaging in prayer, meditation, or spellwork, this oil can calm, center, and help you feel the loving support of deities in a practical setting.
