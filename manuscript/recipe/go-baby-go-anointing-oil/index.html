🚀Go, Baby, Go Anointing Oil

Type

Correspondences5

A motivating anointing oil to get unstuck and exorcise stagnation.

Quantities

1 drop angelica essential oil
2 drops basil essential oil
2 drops cedarwood essential oil
4 teaspoons (20 ml) carrier oil

Directions

Fill your container with 2 teaspoons carrier oil. Then add 1 drop angelica essential oil, 2 drops basil essential oil, and 2 drops cedarwood essential oil. Fill the container with remaining carrier oil, and put on the lid. Roll the container between your hands until the oils have been thoroughly mixed.
