🌍Grounding Anxiety

Simply being able to breathe can feel like magick. Use this blend when you need to take moment to breathe during overwhelming anxiety.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 4 drops patchouli essential oil for balance, centering, grounding, harmony
  • 3 drops clary sage essential oil for balance, harmony, wisdom, clarity
  • 5 drops tangerine essential oil for calming, antidepressant, protection

Directions

Place oils in 1-ounce glass or number 1 plastic bottle and gently stir. Fill bottle with carrier oil of choice, cap, and shake. Use this oil to anoint heart chakra when anxiety feels overwhelming.
Make second batch in 2 ml bottle without carrier oil to diffuse in spaces frequently attended, such as living room, bedroom (for great night's sleep), and office (if allowed).
