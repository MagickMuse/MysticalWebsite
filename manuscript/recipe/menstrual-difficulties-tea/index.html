🌿Menstrual Difficulties Tea

Type
Elixir
Correspondences3
🫀Dysmenorrhoea❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health
Plants2
CinnamonGinger

A warming two-herb tea of cinnamon bark and ginger root steeped thirty to forty minutes to ease menstrual difficulties. ⚠️ Both herbs are emmenagogues in therapeutic amounts; avoid during pregnancy.

Quantities

1 teaspoon chopped cinnamon bark
1 teaspoon chopped ginger root (or finely grated)
Honey to sweeten (optional)

Directions

Pour one cup of boiling water over the cinnamon and ginger. Cover and steep for thirty to forty minutes. Strain into a cup and sweeten with honey if desired.

Notes

An older traditional formula for the same purpose uses sassafras tea prepared by the same steeping method.
