🌲Nine Woods Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences2
Fire🧘🏻‍♀️Spirituality
Plants11
AppleCedarDogwoodElderHollyJuniperOakPinePoplarRowanSandalwood

Take sawdust of each, mix together, and burn indoors on charcoal when a ritual fire is necessary or desired but not practical. The incense emits the aroma of an open campfire.

Quantities

  • Rowan wood (or Sandalwood): 1 part
  • Apple wood: 1 part
  • Dogwood: 1 part
  • Poplar wood: 1 part
  • Juniper wood: 1 part
  • Cedar wood: 1 part
  • Pine wood: 1 part
  • Holly branches: 1 part
  • Elder (or Oak) wood: 1 part

Directions

Take sawdust of each wood type, mix together, and burn on charcoal indoors when a ritual fire is necessary or desired but not practical. The incense emits the aroma of an open campfire.
