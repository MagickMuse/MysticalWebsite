💧Oakmoss Bouquet

Type
Oil
Oils2
Cinnamon Bark Essential OilVetivert Essential Oil

A substitute blend that imitates oakmoss's warm, slightly spicy scent using more readily available oils.

Quantities

  • Vetivert Oil
  • Cinnamon Oil
    Combine in roughly equal parts, adjusting cinnamon to taste (it is strong).

Directions

Combine vetivert and cinnamon oils in a dark glass bottle. Cap and shake gently. The cinnamon should be used sparingly as it is potent.

This bouquet can substitute for oakmoss in money-drawing and grounding formulas.

Store in a cool, dark place.

This substitute blend captures the warm, slightly spicy character of oakmoss using more readily available essential oils.

When to Use

Use this bouquet when oakmoss absolute is unavailable or when its viscous nature makes it difficult to work with. The combination of vetivert and cinnamon provides similar grounding and prosperity associations.

Magical Uses

Money Drawing: Oakmoss is traditionally used in money-drawing mixtures. This substitute carries similar prosperous energy.

Grounding: Both vetivert and cinnamon have grounding properties that mirror oakmoss's earthy stability.

Application

Dilute the bouquet in a carrier oil and wear, or rub onto cash before spending to encourage its return multiplied.

Component Properties

Vetivert provides the deep, earthy base and grounding energy.

Cinnamon adds the warm, spicy top note and prosperity associations.
