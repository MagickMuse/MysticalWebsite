🌿Law Keep Away Powder Spell

Grind High John, Courtcase root, hydrangea, asafetida, and black poppy seeds into a powder. Sprinkle a boundary line across the path to your home or cast an unbroken protective circle around your property.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This formula draws directly from Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

  • High John the Conqueror root
  • Courtcase root (galangal)
  • Dried hydrangea blossoms
  • Asafetida
  • Black poppy seeds

Directions

Grind High John the Conqueror, Courtcase root, hydrangea blossoms, asafetida, and black poppy seeds together into a fine powder. Sprinkle the powder across the path that leads to your home as a boundary line, or cast an unbroken circle around the perimeter of your entire property to form a complete protective barrier.

⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: This powder working is rooted in Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

This powder creates a physical boundary charged with the combined legal-protection powers of five botanicals. It is used to establish a warding perimeter around your home or property.

  1. Grind the following together into a fine powder:
    • High John the Conqueror root
    • Courtcase root (galangal)
    • Dried hydrangea blossoms
    • Asafetida
    • Black poppy seeds
  2. Choose your method of application:
    • Boundary line: Sprinkle the powder across the path that leads to your home, creating a line that authority figures must cross to reach you.
    • Full circle: Cast an unbroken circle of powder around your entire property. The circle must be complete — any gap weakens the ward.
