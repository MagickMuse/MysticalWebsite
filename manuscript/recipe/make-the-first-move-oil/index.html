A love signal oil — the magickal equivalent of a "Do you like me? Check YES or NO" note.

Quantities

  • 1 drop vetiver essential oil for aphrodisiac and grounding
  • 3 drops black pepper essential oil for physical energy, courage
  • 4 drops sweet orange essential oil for attraction, beauty, luck

Directions

Diffuse this oil as signal to universe that you are ready for love. Anoint red or pink candle with 1 drop of synergy in 2 ml bottle filled with carrier oil of your choice. Burn candle on Friday (love, beauty) during waxing moon for best effect. This oil will not interfere with free will of any persons. This is flare, signaling interest. If person also interested, they are free to make first move.

About This Oil

This oil will not interfere with anyone's free will—it's simply a flare signaling interest in situations where walking up and stating interest could be met with awkwardness, fear, or violence.

If the person is also interested, they are free to make the first move.
