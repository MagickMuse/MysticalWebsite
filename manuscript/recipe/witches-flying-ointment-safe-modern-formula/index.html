🪨Witches' Flying Ointment: Safe Modern Formula

◇Directions
1. Chop fresh leaves finely. Fresh herbs are strongly preferred; growing them ensures availability. Dried herbs are a fallback. Do not use essential oils under any circumstances. 2. Melt the cocoa butter, shea butter, or chosen base fat in a double boiler. 3. Add the chopped leaves generously, pressing them down and layering in more as space allows. The aim is to thoroughly saturate the fat with plant material. 4. Allow to simmer gently, then remove from the heat. 5. Stir in approximately two teaspoons of lanolin until fully incorporated. 6. Pour through cheesecloth or a fine sieve into a clean jar. 7. Once cooled, check the consistency. If too firm, work in additional lanolin. Store and use as needed.
◇Type
Salve
◇Quantities
• Fresh cinquefoil, Dittany of Crete, and/or mugwort leaves (dried may be substituted) • Cocoa butter or shea butter as a base fat • Approx. 2 tsp lanolin, plus more as needed to adjust consistency • Additional herbs as desired
◇Description
A contemporary flying ointment built from non-toxic botanicals, formulated to evoke the sensation of witches' flight without the lethal hazards of historical preparations. ⚠️ Do not substitute essential oils for plant material. Mugwort essential oil is highly toxic. Use only whole fresh or dried herbs.
