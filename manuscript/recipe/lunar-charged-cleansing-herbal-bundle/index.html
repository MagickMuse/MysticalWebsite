🌿Lunar-Charged Cleansing Herbal Bundle

A handcrafted smoke-cleansing herb bundle made at the full or waning gibbous moon, when herbs are at peak energetic potency. Rosemary and thyme form the base, with rose petals or bay leaves woven in for spiritual attunement, healing, and release. The bundle dries through the waning phase and is ready to use by the new moon. Burn it before moon meditations or rituals to clear stagnant energy and prepare sacred space.

Quantities

Fresh rosemary sprigs
Fresh thyme sprigs
Rose petals or bay leaves
Twine, thread, or string (white for cleansing, black for protection, purple for harmony)

Directions

At the full or waning gibbous moon, gather your materials in a sacred space, ideally where moonlight is visible. Take a few grounding breaths; breathe in the fragrance of the herbs and feel gratitude for their energy, holding them briefly if you wish to tune into their essence. Bunch the rosemary and thyme sprigs together and tie once around the base to secure them. Wrap the twine upward toward the tip of the bundle, tucking rose petals or bay leaves in as you go; pull the string snug to hold everything in place, then wrap back down. As you bind the bundle, focus on the waning moon's cleansing energy empowering it. Chant if desired:

"As the moon wanes, these herbs are blessed, to cleanse away unwanted banes."

Knot the string at the base and leave a length of extra string for hanging. Visualize the waning moon blessing the bundle as it dries; it should be ready by the new moon. To use, reposition the twine slightly lower so it will not catch fire, then light the tip of the bundle. Blow out the flame, leaving smoldering embers, and rest it on a fireproof dish or a shell filled with sand to smudge your space.
