✨Maximum Power Cleansing: Six-Oil Healing Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences2
✨Cleansing🧹Purification
Plants7
CalendulaFrankincenseLavenderMyrrhRoseRosemarySandalwood

A deeply restorative bath for recovery after abuse, violence, sexual violation, or severe humiliation. Six essential oils work alongside sea salt and white rose hydrosol to cleanse and repair the aura. Rose and calendula blossoms floating in the water add an extra layer of healing energy.

Quantities

  • Sea salt, copious quantities
  • Essential oils of lavender, rose, rosemary, sandalwood, frankincense, myrrh: several drops each
  • White rose hydrosol, a generous splash
  • Rose and calendula blossoms, a handful (optional)

Directions

Fill the bath and dissolve a generous amount of sea salt in the water. Add several drops each of lavender, rose, rosemary, sandalwood, frankincense, and myrrh essential oils, plus a splash of white rose hydrosol. Float rose and calendula blossoms on the surface if available. Soak thoroughly, allowing all the ingredients to work through the aura.
