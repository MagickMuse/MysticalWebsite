🛁Fire-Breathing Dragon's Blood Bath

An intensified dragon's blood protection bath with essential oil and red coloring added for extra power.

Quantities

1 handful sea salt
1 handful dragon's blood powder
1 drop cinnamon leaf essential oil and/or 1 drop peppermint essential oil
Infusion of red hibiscus blossoms (or hibiscus tea bags), or red food coloring

Directions

Blend the sea salt and dragon's blood powder. Add one drop of cinnamon leaf essential oil, one drop of peppermint essential oil, or both. Turn the bath red with an infusion of red hibiscus blossoms (hibiscus tea bags also work) or, less ideally, red food coloring, and add everything to the bath.

About This Recipe

An intensified version of Sea Salt Dragon's Blood Bath, already powerful on its own.

Notes

Cinnamon leaf oil is a skin irritant. Use caution and never exceed the single drop.
