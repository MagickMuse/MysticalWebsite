💜Calming Massage Oil

Type
Oil
Correspondences2
❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health
Plants1
Lavender

A lavender-infused almond oil for calming massage. Dried lavender buds are steeped in almond oil for two to three weeks, then blended to scent with essential lavender oil. Can also be used as a relaxing bath oil.

Quantities

1½ ounces dried lavender buds
4 ounces almond oil
5–10 drops essential lavender oil

Directions

Put the dried lavender buds into a clean glass jar and pour the almond oil over them. Seal and set in a warm, sunny place for two to three weeks. Strain out the buds. Add the essential lavender oil drop by drop until the desired scent is reached.

To use for massage, pour a few drops into one hand, rub the hands together, and massage into the body. Always massage toward the heart: legs and arms from fingers and toes upward, shoulders and neck downward.

A few drops may also be added to a warm bath for relaxation. Take care getting in and out, as the oil will make the tub slippery.
