Elixir
Correspondences3

Cayenne Pepper

A steeped cayenne and salt paste dissolved in boiling water and combined with apple cider vinegar. Taken by the teaspoon to tablespoon every 30 minutes to one hour. ⚠️ Use gloves when handling cayenne — it is difficult to remove from skin.

Quantities

1½ teaspoons salt
2 teaspoons cayenne pepper
1 cup boiling water
1 cup apple cider vinegar

Directions

⚠️ Use gloves when handling cayenne pepper. Avoid touching eyes, face, or skin after contact. Grind the salt and cayenne together into a paste. Add the boiling water and steep until cool. Add the apple cider vinegar. Take 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon every 30 minutes to one hour.
