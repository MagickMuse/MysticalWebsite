🌿Chapped Skin Lotion

Type
Wash
Correspondences2
❤️‍🩹HealingSkin
Materials3
Borax🧴Glycerol🌹Rose Water
Plants2
QuinceRose

A two-solution shake lotion of borax in rose water combined with quince mucilage in glycerin. Blended together until smooth.

Quantities

1 tablespoon borax
3 ounces glycerin
12 ounces double rose water
5 tablespoons mucilage of quince

Directions

Shake the borax and rose water together until the borax is fully dissolved. Combine the mucilage of quince with the glycerin and shake thoroughly. Mix the two solutions together until well blended.
