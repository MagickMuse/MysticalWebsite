🫘Sabbat Oil (Traditional Formulas)

Type
Oil
Correspondences1
🧙Coven

Traditional herb-based Sabbat anointing oil formulas for use before attending Sabbat. Three formulas are given: a simple pre-Columbian formula, a more complex post-Columbian formula (contains dangerous herbs -- reference only), and a modern safe substitute formula. Anoint the soles of the feet, perineum, wrists, and temples while reciting the anointing chant.

Quantities

  • SIMPLE FORMULA (pre-Columbian, traditional):
  • Wild parsley root
  • Celery root (smallage)
  • Poplar leaves / Balm of Gilead
  • Cinquefoil
  • Saffron
    Steep crushed herbs in purified vegetable oil during the waxing moon; strain through muslin.
    COMPLEX FORMULA (post-Columbian, reference only -- contains poisonous herbs):
  • Smallage (wild celery)
  • Wolfsbane / Monkshood (TOXIC)
  • Cinquefoil
  • Henbane (TOXIC)
  • Hemlock (TOXIC)
  • Mandrake (TOXIC)
  • Moonwort (Botrychium lunaria)
  • Tobacco
  • Poppy
  • Poplar leaves / Balm of Gilead
  • Saffron
    MODERN SAFE FORMULA:
  • Balm of Gilead (poplar leaves)
  • Cinquefoil
  • Saffron
  • Lemon verbena or vervain
    Optional additions: musk, civet, a drop of ambergris, patchouli or cassia, a few drops of Cernunnos perfume.
    For a cream or ointment, mix with finely ground oatmeal or orris powder.

Directions

Herb formulas: Crush and steep herbs in purified vegetable oil during the waxing moon. Ideally, fresh herbs are cut with the athame under the waxing moon. Strain through thin muslin to remove all solids. Alternatively, steep crushed herbs in pure alcohol, then blend the infused alcohol with oil.

Store the oil in a small, well-stoppered phial. An ointment may be stored in a vessel made of animal horn.

To anoint before Sabbat: Place a drop on the soles of the feet, the perineum, the wrists, and the temples, chanting:

"Emen Hetan! Emen Hetan!
I am of thee and thou art mine,
I have nothing which is not thine.
In thy name [god's name if male; goddess' name if female],
Behold thy servant [witch name] anointing herself/himself;
I should some day be great like thee!
Thout a thout, thouht, throughout and about!"
