🍸Sazerac

One of the quintessential absinthe cocktails with wide magical associations—from protection and love to psychic visions and hex recovery.

Quantities

  • 2 glasses (one cocktail glass)
  • Optional: atomizer
  • Strainer
  • 2 oz rye whiskey (prosperity, fertility, love, beauty)
  • Ice
  • 1 tsp ice water
  • Absinthe rinse (dreams, hex breaking, psychic power, happiness, healing, initiation)
  • 2 dashes Peychaud's Bitters (desire, fertility, power, protection, inner growth, combatting chronic pain and illness, recovering from illness or travel)
  • 1 sugar cube (love, lust, mood-elevating, sex magick)
  • 1 strip lemon peel for garnish (calm, clarity, increase spell's power, divination, banish fatigue, fidelity, protection, abundance)

Directions

Rinse the cocktail glass with absinthe or spritz it from a cocktail atomizer; atomizers are excellent if you enjoy a whisper of vermouth in your martini, as they are less wasteful than rinsing. In the other glass, place sugar, bitters, and water. Stir to ensure the sugar is dissolved. Add ice and whiskey to the sugar glass and let sit for a moment to chill the whiskey without watering it down. Strain into the cocktail glass. Express lemon peel over the surface of the liquid and garnish. Enjoy while visualizing your set intention. Take conscious breaths between sips, in through the nose and out through the mouth, to help keep you centered.

Magical Uses

The wide variety of magical associations make this cocktail suitable for many intentions:

  • Protection from night terrors
  • Love potion (happiness plus love can draw the right lover)
  • Transformation of pain
  • Binding desire (for feelings you don't want)
  • Increasing psychic visions
  • Recovering after a magical attack

Ritual Note
