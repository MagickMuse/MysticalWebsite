Use this blend to soothe frayed nerves and treat depression. It's best over tea and conversation.

Quantities

  • 9 drops Roman Chamomile Essential Oil (Chamaemelum nobile) or Cape chamomile essential oil (Eriocephalus punctulatus) for stress relief
  • 3 drops Lemon Balm Essential Oil (Melissa officianalis) for comfort (associated with Diana and the moon)
  • 1 drop Rosemary Essential Oil (Rosemarinus officialis) to soothe nerves and gain protection, love

Directions

Mix all essential oils in a glass bottle, and diffuse the synergy when a frantic call comes from dear friends, confused clients, or frustrated family members. For an anointing oil that's helping during times of stress and helplessness, add 4 drops synergy to 1 tablespoon carrier oil. Your loved ones will remember time spent talking with you.
