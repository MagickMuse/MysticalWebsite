💕My Mistress's Eyes

Type
Oil
Correspondences7
🔮Divination😊Happiness😊Joy💕Love🍀Luck🧹Purification♻️Transformation
Oils2
Oakmoss AbsolutePetitgrain Essential Oil

A divination and love-drawing blend to see face of future lovers in dreams or draw new love. Combines oakmoss, amber, and petitgrain for manifestation and attraction.

Quantities

  • 4 drops oakmoss absolute for divination, luck, manifestation
  • 30 drops amber essential oil for attraction, happiness, love, soul mates
  • 20 drops petitgrain essential oil for joy, purification, transformation

Directions

Diffuse 1 drop synergy in 100 ml water half hour before bed to dream of next lover to grace your boudoir. Dilute 1 drop synergy in 2 ml carrier oil to anoint candle and ask universe to bring you next love on the way. Oakmoss absolute is thick and sticky; shake bottle hard to incorporate absolute before diffusing or diluting.
