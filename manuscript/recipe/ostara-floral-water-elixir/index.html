🌸Ostara Floral Water Elixir

◇Type
Elixir
◇Description
A bright, lightly sweetened floral water for Ostara, steeped with violet and elder blossoms, lemon zest and juice, and cream of tartar. Enjoyed as a springtime libation or ritual drink after a full week of resting. Based on a traditional recipe shared by herbalist Susun Weed.
↗Plants1
Lemon
↗Timing1
🐰Ostara

A floral water is a lightly infused, diluted herbal preparation suited to warm-weather ritual. This elixir is best made in small quantities and consumed quickly, as it is a living preparation without preservatives. It captures the freshness and floral abundance of Ostara beautifully.

This recipe is adapted from a preparation taught by Susun Weed.

Quantities

  • Zest of 4 lemons
  • Juice of 4 lemons
  • 1 rounded teaspoon cream of tartar
  • 1 3/4 cups sugar
  • 1 cup fresh violet blossoms
  • 1 cup elder blossoms
  • 1 gallon spring water
  • Large glass or plastic container

Directions

  1. Combine the lemon zest, lemon juice, cream of tartar, and sugar in the large container. Pour in the gallon of spring water and stir well. Add the fresh violet and elder blossoms, cover the container loosely, and allow everything to steep for at least 24 hours.
  2. After a full day, strain out the flowers. Allow the elixir to rest and develop for a full week before drinking.

Magickal Notes

Floral waters make lovely libations for spring altar work. Pour a small portion as an offering before drinking, or use it to sprinkle the altar and ritual space. The combination of violet, elder, and lemon connects to themes of renewal, sweetness, and the opening of the earth after winter.
