Diffuse this oil to banish hopelessness and anxiety and increase courage.

Quantities

  • 6 drops clove essential oil to encourage feelings of courage and protection
  • 6 drops black pepper essential oil to ward off fatigue and empower
  • 9 drops frankincense essential oil to combat depression
  • 16 drops lavender essential oil to banish nervous tension

Directions

Blend all essential oils into a glass or number 1 plastic bottle, and diffuse one drop synergy per 100 ml water.
