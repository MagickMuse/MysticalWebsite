🧼Glass Cleaner Oil

Type

Natural glass cleaner spray with myrrh, roman chamomile, or lavender essential oil for streak-free cleaning.

Quantities

1 cup/250 mL water
1 cup/250 mL white vinegar
1/4 tsp/2 mL liquid dishwashing soap
5 drops of Lemon Essential Oil or Tea Tree Essential Oil or Lavender Essential Oil

Directions

Add to a spray bottle. Shake well before using. Wipe off with a newspaper or lint-free cloth.
