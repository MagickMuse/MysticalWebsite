🧿Lucky Nine Oil

Closed Practice
Hoodoo
Type
Oil
Correspondences4

A nine-botanical lucky oil in a jojoba base for dressing mojo bags and candles or rubbing on hands and feet before important ventures.

⚠️ This formula references mojo bags, a Hoodoo practice. Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magic traditions. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.

Quantities

Allspice
Carnation
Cinnamon
Frankincense
Galangal
High John the Conqueror chips
Lemon zest or lemongrass
Myrrh
Vervain
Jojoba oil (carrier)

Directions

Grind allspice, carnation, cinnamon, frankincense, galangal, High John the Conqueror chips, lemon zest or lemongrass, myrrh, and vervain together into a fine powder. Transfer the powder to a bottle and cover fully with jojoba oil. Roll the bottle gently to distribute the botanicals evenly. Rub onto the hands or feet before important appointments or ventures, use to dress candles, or anoint mojo bags to enhance good fortune.
