🌿Basil & Anise Chest Rub

◇Type
Oil
↗Correspondences3
🤧Common Cold😷Coughs❤️‍🩹Healing
↗Materials3
🧀CheeseclothDouble BoilerOlive Oil
↗Plants3
AniseBasilHyssop

⚠️ Do not use during pregnancy. A hot herbal oil infusion of basil and anise (or hyssop) for massaging into the chest to ease congestion and chest cold symptoms.

Quantities

½ cup basil
¼ cup anise or hyssop
2 cups carrier oil (sunflower, almond, or light olive)

Directions

Combine the herbs and oil in the top of a double boiler. Heat over boiling water for two hours with the lid on. Do not allow the oil to boil. Strain through cheesecloth. Massage into the chest area.
