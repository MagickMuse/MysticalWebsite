🦴Cernunnos Incense

For use in any invocation of Cernunnos, or any spell designed to arouse lust. A strong incense that should be used sparingly. Prepared on a Thursday at the usual times (8AM, 3PM, or 10PM) during the waxing moon. Steep overnight and adjust dry ingredients to achieve the correct dry-but-burnable consistency.

Quantities

  • Dry:
  • A few drops patchouli essence, or patchouli leaves finely powdered
  • 1 part good-quality fine-grain church incense
  • 1 part bay leaves, finely reduced
  • 1 part cypress wood or pine needles, finely reduced
  • 1 part wormwood herb
  • 1 part vervain herb
    Moisten with a few drops of each:
  • Oil of cloves
  • Gum turpentine (pine oil)
  • Olive oil
  • Honey
  • The operator's blood
  • Red wine
    Optional: A drop of civet or musk essence if available.

Directions

  1. Finely reduce or powder all dry ingredients separately, then combine.
  2. Moisten with a few drops each of clove oil, pine oil/gum turpentine, olive oil, honey, the operator's blood, and red wine.
  3. Add optional civet or musk essence if available.
  4. Blend thoroughly. Steep the mixture overnight.
  5. If the blend is too moist after steeping, add more dry ingredients until a dry but burnable consistency is achieved.
  6. Prepare on a Thursday at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM during the waxing moon.
  7. Burn on a charcoal disc, using sparingly. This incense is very strong.
