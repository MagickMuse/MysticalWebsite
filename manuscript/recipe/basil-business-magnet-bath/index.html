🛁Basil Business Magnet Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences4
💼Business💰Prosperity🏆Success💵Wealth
Plants1
Basil

A personal magnetism bath spell rooted in a tradition believed to originate in Spain. Fresh basil infusions, leaves, and hydrosol are combined in the bath to attract clients and customers. Optional body powder after bathing further enhances the drawing effect. Beneficial for anyone whose prosperity depends on personal magnetism.

Quantities

  • Strong fresh basil infusion
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Basil hydrosol
  • Lodestone Body Powder or Drawing Powder Personal Dusting Powder (optional)

Directions

Add strong infusions of fresh basil, fresh basil leaves, and some basil hydrosol to your bath. Soak thoroughly. For extra enhancement, after bathing and air-drying, dust the body lightly with Lodestone Body Powder or Drawing Powder Personal Dusting Powder.
