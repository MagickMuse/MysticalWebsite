🕯️Amatory Dumb Supper⚰️Necromancy of Intelligence

A dense, resinous incense blended with wine, honey, and the operator's blood, used to fumigate a necromantic circle during spirit-summoning workings.

Quantities

3 parts wormwood herb (finely reduced)
2 parts good-quality, resinous church incense (frankincense)
2 parts gum mastic
3 parts dittany of Crete (finely reduced)
1/2 part pure olive oil
1/2 part wine
1/2 part honey
A few drops of the operator's blood

Directions

Blend all ingredients together and let stand overnight. The finished incense should be of a fairly solid, crumbly consistency. If it turns out too liquid, add extra dittany of Crete and wormwood until the correct, dryish texture is reached.
