🐍Eve Oil

◇Directions
Combine apple blossoms, dried pomegranate seeds, and snakeroot in a bottle. Fill with sweet almond and jojoba oil until the botanicals are fully covered. Use the finished oil to dress candles whenever you are performing visualization or working to deepen communication with animals.

◇Description
An oil blend for enhancing communication with animals, made from apple blossoms, pomegranate seeds, and snakeroot in a base of sweet almond and jojoba oil.

About This Recipe

The story of Eve and the apple is now most often read as a tale of sexuality, yet what Eve first desired was knowledge.

Variations

Wisdom and receptivity: Steep apple blossoms, dried pomegranate seeds, and snakeroot (aristolochia) in grapeseed oil. Anoint candles with the oil, or soak cotton balls in it, to heighten metaphysical wisdom and receptivity.
