A devotional oil for dressing candles dedicated to Martha the Dominator or for carrying as spiritual protection and courage.

Quantities

  • Spikenard root shavings
  • Licorice root
  • Calamus (sweet flag)
  • Myrrh resin
  • Olive oil
  • Jar or bottle
  • Cotton ball (for carrying)

Directions

Using a mortar and pestle, grind spikenard root shavings, licorice root, calamus (sweet flag), and myrrh resin together. Add this mixture to a jar or bottle and cover with olive oil. Use this oil to dress candles dedicated to Martha, or carry a cotton ball soaked with the oil for spiritual protection and courage.
