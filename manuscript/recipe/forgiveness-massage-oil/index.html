💙Forgiveness Massage Oil

Type

A massage oil blend to heal lovers’ quarrels or learn to forgive yourself.

Quantities

1 drop angelica essential oil
5 drops German chamomile essential oil
3 drops rose geranium essential oil
8 drops lavender essential oil
3 drops lime essential oil
1.3 ounces jojoba oil
1 drop or 1 gel cap vitamin E

Directions

Mix all oils in a dark-colored glass or PET bottle. Combine 1 drop angelica essential oil, 5 drops German chamomile essential oil, 3 drops rose geranium essential oil, 8 drops lavender essential oil, 3 drops lime essential oil, 1.3 ounces jojoba oil, and 1 drop or 1 gel cap vitamin E. For massage blends, a dilution of 2.5 percent is recommended (15 drops of synergy per ounce of carrier oil). Vitamin E is a good preservative for your massage oils, 10 drops per 8 fluid ounces of carrier oil.
