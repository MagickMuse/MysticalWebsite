🌿Medicinal Basil Pesto

Type
Food
Correspondences2
❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health
Plants4
BasilCilantroDandelionGarlic

A blended medicinal pesto of olive oil, garlic, fresh basil, dandelion, and cilantro, finished with walnuts and Parmesan. Frozen in ice cube trays for individual serving portions.

Quantities

½–1 cup olive oil
1–3 garlic cloves
½ cup fresh basil leaves
½ cup fresh dandelion leaves
1 cup fresh cilantro leaves and stems
½–1 cup walnuts
¼ cup grated hard Parmesan cheese

Directions

Combine the oil, garlic, and fresh greens in a blender or food processor until smooth. Add nuts and cheese, and pulse again until the desired consistency. Freeze in an ice cube tray for portion size. Empty frozen cubes into a freezer bag. Use on crackers, pasta, or in soup.
