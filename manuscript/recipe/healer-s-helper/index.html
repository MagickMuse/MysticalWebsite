Use this blend before talk therapy, massage, or reiki. Soft and comforting, this recipe is perfect for healing practitioners of all stripes.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 10 drops German Chamomile Essential Oil (Matricaria recutita) for healing (especially skin), antianxiety
  • 10 drops Clary Sage Essential Oil (Salvia sclarea) to soothe nervousness, restore balance
  • 5 drops Ylang Ylang Essential Oil (Cananga odorata) to soothe, protect health

Directions

Blend all essential oils, and diffuse 1 drop synergy per 100 ml water. For massage, mix 15 drops synergy per ounce of carrier oil (a good all-purpose oil is Jojoba Oil) plus a drop or two of vitamin E to preserve the mix.
