🌸North African Cleansing Incense

◇Directions
Mash mastic, dried myrtle leaves and berries, and dried rose petals together with a mortar and pestle. Burn on charcoal.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
- Mastic resin tears or powder - Dried myrtle leaves and berries - Dried rose petals
◇Description
A North African cleansing formula combining mastic — a component of ancient Egyptian embalming formulas and an excellent pre-ritual cleanser believed to enhance clairvoyance — with dried myrtle leaves and berries and dried rose petals.
