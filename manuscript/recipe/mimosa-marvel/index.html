Recipe Grimoire

🍊Mimosa Marvel

◇Directions
Hold the champagne flute and visualize golden light filling the glass. Pour 5 oz of champagne slowly, watching the bubbles rise. As they ascend, imagine your intention being carried upward to the universe. Add 2.5 oz of fresh orange juice, envisioning the warm energy of the sun infusing your drink with vitality and success. Finish with 0.5 oz of Cointreau for abundance and sweetness in your manifestation. Before drinking, hold the glass up to catch the light. Speak your intention aloud or silently, then drink and receive the magick.
◇Type
Drink
◇Quantities
- 1 champagne flute - 5 oz champagne - 2.5 oz fresh orange juice - 0.5 oz Cointreau (abundance, antidepressant, affection)
◇Description
A celebratory cocktail spell for broadcasting intentions to the universe, perfect for new beginnings and manifestation work.
↗Correspondences4
💰Abundance😊Joy✨Manifestation🏆Success
↗Materials1
🥂Champagne Flute

Sober Substitution

Replace champagne with sparkling apple cider. Replace Cointreau with a splash of orange juice and a drop of orange extract. The working remains effective; what matters is your intention and the solar energy you call in.
