🍋Medieval Lemon Kohl

Type

⚠️ NOT SAFE FOR THE EYES. A historic medieval kohl made by burning a lemon stuffed with plumbago and burnt copper down to ash. Formulas like this put magick ahead of health, so this one is for carrying as a protective talisman only, never for use near the eyes.

Quantities

Lemon (1)
Plumbago
Burnt copper
Charcoal brazier
Mortar and pestle
Rose water
Optional additions: pulverized coral, pearls, amber

Directions

  1. Scoop the flesh out of a lemon.
  2. Fill the empty peel with plumbago and burnt copper.
  3. Burn it on a charcoal brazier until it is reduced to carbonized ash.
  4. Pound the ash with a mortar and pestle.
  5. If desired, add pulverized coral, pearls and amber.
  6. Moisten with rose water and let it dry.
  7. Carry the powder for talismanic protection. Do not put it in or near the eyes.
