🌲Pine Bitters

Type
Drink
Correspondences2
⚡Energy♻️Transformation
Plants1
Pine

An aromatic bitter made with pine tips and high-proof alcohol, used in cocktails or elixirs for courage and vitality.

Quantities

  • Fresh pine tips (half a pint jar)
  • High-proof vodka or grain alcohol (to fill jar)
  • Pint jar with lid
  • Cheesecloth for straining
  • Dropper bottle for storage

Directions

Fill a pint jar halfway with fresh pine tips. Cover completely with high-proof vodka or grain alcohol (at least 80 proof, higher is better for extraction). Seal and store in a cool, dark place for 4 to 6 weeks, shaking every few days. The liquid will turn a deep amber-green color. Strain through cheesecloth, squeezing the pine tips to extract all liquid. Transfer to a dropper bottle for easy use. Add 3 drops to cocktails, water, or other beverages to impart pine's courage-building and invigorating properties. The bitters will keep indefinitely due to the high alcohol content.
