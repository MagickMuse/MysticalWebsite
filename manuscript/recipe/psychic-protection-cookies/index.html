🧿Psychic Protection Cookies

Type
Food
Correspondences3

🥚Cascarilla Powder🍬Sugar
Plants1
Anise

Edible cookies infused with protective herbs and spices to provide psychic protection and ward off negative energies. A delicious way to incorporate magic into everyday life.

Quantities

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon anise seeds
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder

Directions

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). In a bowl, cream together butter and sugar until fluffy. Beat in egg and vanilla extract. In a separate bowl, combine flour, anise seeds, cinnamon, salt, and baking powder. Gradually mix dry ingredients into wet ingredients until a dough forms. While mixing, focus on your intention for protection. Roll dough into small balls and place on a greased baking sheet. Flatten slightly with a fork. Bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden. Let cool on a wire rack. Eat one cookie when you need psychic protection or share with loved ones to extend protection to them.
