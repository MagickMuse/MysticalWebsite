🧹Purification Incense

◇Type
Incense
↗Minerals2
HaliteSulfur
↗Plants1
Witch Hazel

⚠️ Caution: Contains sulfur. Burn to purify the atmosphere of a disturbed home. Leave the windows open and do not inhale the sulfurous fumes!

Quantities

4 parts Frankincense
2 parts Bay
1 part Camphor
1 pinch finely powdered Salt
1 pinch Sulfur*

Directions

Grind the frankincense and bay. Add camphor, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of sulfur. Blend thoroughly. Leave windows open and burn to purify the atmosphere of a disturbed home. Do not inhale the sulfurous fumes.
