🌑Incubus Incense (Against The)

Type
Incense
Correspondences1
🛡️Protection
Plants12
AgarwoodBenzoinCalamusCardamomCarnationCinnamonCloveCubebGingerMaceNutmegSandalwood

An ancient protective mixture burned to ward off the incubus and other unwanted nocturnal spiritual intrusions. CAUTION: Contains clove and nutmeg, which can be harmful in large amounts. Burn in well-ventilated areas.

Quantities

  • 2 parts Sandalwood
  • 2 parts Benzoin
  • 2 parts Wood Aloe
  • 2 parts Cardamom
  • 1/2 part Calamus
  • 1/2 part Birthwort
  • 1/2 part Ginger
  • 1/2 part Pepper
  • 1/2 part Cinnamon
  • 1/2 part Clove
  • 1/2 part Carnation
  • 1/2 part Nutmeg
  • 1/2 part Mace
  • 1/2 part Cubeb seed
  • A few drops brandy

Directions

Grind all dry ingredients together thoroughly. Add the brandy drops last and mix well. Burn in the bedroom before sleep as a protective measure.

Caution

Contains clove and nutmeg, which can be harmful in large amounts. Burn in well-ventilated areas.
