🧂Basic Essential Oil Bath Salts

◇Directions
Add 30 drops of the synergy of your choice to 2 cups salt and stir thoroughly. Pour desired amount under warm running bathwater. If using a clear glass container for aesthetic purposes, mix small batches that will be used quickly. Store the excess salt mixture in an airtight container, preferably in a dark glass container or in a room without UV light exposure. Bath salts can have a drying effect on chapped or damaged skin, so consider using a bath oil in the winter.
◇Type
Bath Blend
◇Quantities
30 drops essential oil synergy of your choice 2 cups salt
◇Description
A simple bath salt blend using essential oils and salt for purification and relaxation. Can be customized with any essential oil synergy for different magickal intentions.
