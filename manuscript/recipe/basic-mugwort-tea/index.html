💤Basic Mugwort Tea

◇Directions
For a dream tea, place one teaspoon of dried mugwort in a cup, cover it with boiling water, and steep for ten minutes, then strain and drink one cup before bedtime. For psychic enhancement, use one teaspoon of fresh mugwort or half a teaspoon of dried, cover with boiling water, and steep for five minutes before straining and drinking. Add honey if needed to make it more palatable.
◇Type
Drink
◇Quantities
1 teaspoon dried mugwort (dream tea) Or 1 teaspoon fresh mugwort or 1/2 teaspoon dried mugwort (psychic enhancement) Boiling water Optional: honey
◇Description
A simple, potent mugwort tea that stimulates dreams and enhances psychic ability. The taste is very unpleasant, so honey may help.
