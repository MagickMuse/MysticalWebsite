🦃Turkey Breast Poached in Ginger Ale

Type
Food
Correspondences3
❤️‍🩹Healing🍀Luck🛡️Protection
Plants4
Bay LaurelCeleryGingerOnion

A comforting turkey breast poached in spicy ginger ale with aromatics, harnessing ginger's powerhouse energy for luck, healing, and prosperity.

Quantities

  • 2 lbs turkey breasts (comfort, nurturing, blessing)
  • 4 cups ginger ale (luck, healing, prosperity, lust)
  • 1 onion, quartered (banishing, hex breaking)
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped (concentration, lust, banishing enemies)
  • 1 ginger thumb, peeled and sliced into coins (luck, love, money, purification)
  • 2 tsp flour (binding, abundance, harvest, growth)
  • 1 tbsp butter (nurturing, protection, prosperity)
  • 1 bay leaf (attraction, exorcism, good luck, harmony)

Directions

Place Dutch oven on stovetop over medium heat. Add a tablespoon of oil.

Take 1/4 of the onion, separate into layers, and sprinkle in the bottom of the pan to caramelize, stirring frequently.

Once onion is caramelized, lay turkey breast on the bottom of the pan.

Add remaining onion, celery, ginger, and bay leaf. Pour ginger ale over everything. Cover and bring to a boil.

Lower temperature and simmer for 20 minutes or until meat thermometer reads 165°F.

While simmering, make a flour slurry: put flour in a small bowl and add cold water a tablespoon at a time, mixing with a fork.

Remove cooked turkey breasts and discard solids.

To make gravy: Add flour slurry to the stock and mix well. Keep on medium heat, stirring frequently. Once thickened, add butter and let it melt and incorporate.

Serve gravy over turkey with noodles, rice, or potatoes.
