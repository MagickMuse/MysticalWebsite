Petitgrain Essential OilTangerine Essential Oil

An energizing diffuser blend for hosts preparing for gatherings—keeps you (and your guests) uplifted and happy.

Quantities

  • 6 drops tangerine essential oil for hospitality, calm, clarity
  • 1 drop ginger essential oil for energy, beauty, power
  • 5 drops petitgrain essential oil for self-purification and energy

Directions

Diffuse this energizing blend while tending the pre-gathering to-do list. Feel free to keep it going once guests arrive; they will be energized and happy as well. Next time they smell delicate tangerine, they will remember fondly the great time they had. Just don't be surprised when asked to host again soon.
