🍶Four Thieves Legal Victory Path Crossing

◇Directions
Blend Four Thieves Vinegar with sea salt or black salt to form a damp paste. Sprinkle or lay a line of the paste across the path your adversary must travel. The person must step on or over the line for the spell to take effect.
◇Type
Powder
◇Quantities
- Four Thieves Vinegar - Sea salt or black salt - (Blend to a damp paste consistency)
◇Description
Blend Four Thieves Vinegar with sea or black salt into a paste and sprinkle it across your adversary's path. The person must step over the line for the working to take effect.
↗Correspondences1
⚖️Court Case

A direct and efficient path-crossing working using two of folk magick's most powerful adversarial ingredients.

Four Thieves Vinegar carries centuries of reputation for protection and offense against enemies. Salt, especially black salt, draws and fixes negative energy toward whoever crosses it. Together, they form a potent barrier designed to ensure your adversary's defeat.

  1. Blend Four Thieves Vinegar with sea salt or black salt to form a damp paste. The consistency should hold together without being too wet to sprinkle.
  2. Lay a line of the paste across the path your adversary must walk to reach the courthouse, their vehicle, or wherever they must pass.
  3. The person must step on or over this line for the working to take effect.
The spell's power is activated by the target's contact with the line. Placement is everything. Choose your location carefully.
