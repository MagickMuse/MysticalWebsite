🧪Hecate's Sweeter Sleep Bath

A sweetly fragrant ritual bath blend invoking Hekate for restful sleep. Warm milk and honey form the base, combined with vanilla extract and essential oils of lavender and chamomile. A sweeter-scented alternative to other Hekate sleep bath formulas.

Quantities

  • 1 cup milk
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 4 drops lavender essential oil
  • 4 drops chamomile essential oil

Directions

Warm the milk gently over low heat and stir in the honey until fully blended. Remove from heat, then add the vanilla extract and essential oils. Disperse the mixture into warm running bath water. Quantities may be scaled up as desired; maintain the proportions.
