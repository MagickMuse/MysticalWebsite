🌼Beltane Brandy

Type
Potion
Timing1
Beltane

A Beltane tincture made by macerating hawthorn berries in brandy over a full lunar cycle. Shaken daily under candlelight with an incantation, and left outside under the full moon to charge. The strained brandy retains the seasonal magick and flavor of Beltane.

Quantities

  • Brandy, enough to cover the berries
  • Hawthorn berries, enough to fill the jar halfway
  • 1 glass jar with tight-fitting lid

Directions

  1. Fill the jar halfway with hawthorn berries. Pour brandy over them until fully covered. Seal the jar tightly and store in a cool, dark place.
  2. Each day, shake the jar. While doing so, light a candle and recite: "Sacred Beltane fires burn / Light the way for spring's return / Winter's darkness now must end / The great wheel of life has turned again."
  3. At some point during the month, set the jar outside under the full moon overnight to charge.
  4. After a full lunar cycle, strain the brandy and compost the spent berries. The finished potion is ready to use.

Hawthorn is the herald of Beltane. Its blossoms are among the first flowers of spring, and its berries carry the fullness of the season's energy. This brandy captures that vitality through a month-long maceration, enlivened with daily intention and ritual.

Magickal Notes

This potion holds the peak energy of Beltane's fertility, fire, and renewal. A small measure can be poured as a libation, used to toast at ritual, or consumed as part of Beltane celebration. The daily practice of shaking and chanting builds cumulative intention into the preparation.
