⚖️Emotional Balance Oil

◇Directions
Pour jojoba oil into a small glass bottle. Add lavender, rose geranium, and basil essential oils. Drop the moss agate into the bottle to infuse the oil with balancing energy. Cap tightly and shake gently to blend. Let sit for 24 hours before use. Apply to pulse points, temples, or heart center when feeling emotionally overwhelmed or unbalanced.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
• 2 tablespoons jojoba oil (carrier) • 5 drops lavender essential oil • 4 drops rose geranium essential oil • 3 drops basil essential oil • 1 small piece moss agate
◇Description
A calming oil blend to restore emotional equilibrium and inner peace. Associated with Ma'at and Vishnu.
