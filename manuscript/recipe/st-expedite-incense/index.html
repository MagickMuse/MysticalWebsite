St. Expedite Incense

◇Directions
Combine equal parts of allspice, sandalwood resin, and honeysuckle. Pulverize them together into a fine blend.
◇Type
Incense
◇Quantities
Allspice (equal parts) Sandalwood resin (equal parts) Honeysuckle (equal parts)
◇Description
A simple pulverized incense blend sacred to St. Expedite, burned during petitions and altar work with the saint.
