😊Chill Out, Cheer Up!

◇Type
Spray
↗Correspondences9
🎯Centering⚖️Harmony❤️‍🩹Healing☮️Peace🛡️Protection🧹Purification🔄Rebirth🕊️Release💪Strength
↗Materials1
🔫Spray Bottle
↗Oils3
Grapefruit Essential OilLavender Essential OilPeppermint Essential Oil

A cooling spray blend to restore calm and emotional balance. This refreshing combination allows cooler heads to prevail, banishing melancholy and irritability while bringing peace and centering.

Quantities

  • 3 drops grapefruit essential oil to heal, protect, renew
  • 3 drops lavender essential oil for harmony, peace, strength
  • 3 drops peppermint essential oil to purify, release, lift
  • 2 tablespoons witch hazel
  • Cool water to fill

Directions

Place essential oils and witch hazel in a 2-ounce pump spray bottle. Cap and shake well. Unscrew the cap and fill the rest of the way with cool water. Spritz well and often to banish melancholy and irritability and bring peace and centering.
