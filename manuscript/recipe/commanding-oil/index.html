About Commanding Oil

Commanding Oil (also sometimes called Command and Compel or Compelling Oil) is used in Hoodoo to assert authority and direct the will of others. In a legal context, it is used to influence the behavior and decisions of those who hold power over the outcome — judges, attorneys, expert witnesses, and jury members.

Unlike the three primary court case oils (Courtcase, Fiery Wall of Protection, San Cipriano), Commanding Oil is an optional supplement — it adds domination energy on top of the legal success energy of the others.

Uses

  • Supplement to primary court case condition oils
  • Dress candles, mojo hands, and charm bags
  • Particularly useful when needing to sway a specific individual's decision
  • Can be combined with any court case working for added commanding energy
