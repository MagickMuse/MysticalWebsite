🔐Good Thief Water

◇Directions
For maximum power, create this potion on Good Friday during the daytime to coincide with the Crucifixion; the water can be made at other times but will not be at maximum strength. Pour boiling water over five-finger grass (cinquefoil) and allow it to steep for ten minutes. Strain the herbs, reserving the liquid. After the liquid cools, sprinkle it over possessions, windows, and thresholds to prevent theft and break-ins. Store in a clean glass container; the water keeps for extended periods if made on Good Friday.

◇Description
A magickal formula water associated with Saint Dismas (the Good Thief) used to prevent theft and break-ins. Made by steeping five-finger grass (cinquefoil) in boiling water.

Background

This protective water draws on the power of Saint Dismas, the Good Thief who was crucified alongside Jesus. Despite his associations with thievery, Dismas is now petitioned for protection against theft.

The Five-Finger Connection

Five-finger grass (cinquefoil) has long been associated with thieves in folk magick, both for committing theft and for protection against it. Its five-fingered leaves symbolize the hand, making it appropriate for work involving hands, including the hands of thieves.

Best Timing

  • Optimal: Good Friday during daylight hours (to coincide with the Crucifixion)
  • Acceptable: Any time, though potency will be reduced

Applications

  • Sprinkle on windowsills to prevent break-ins
  • Apply to thresholds to keep thieves out
  • Sprinkle over valuable possessions for protection
  • Add to floor wash for overall home protection
