💤Dream Oil: Happy Dreams

Type
Oil
Correspondences1
💤Dreams
Oils5

Plants1
Myrrh

A pre-sleep body oil for pleasant, abundant dreams. Blended from sweet almond and jojoba with frankincense, myrrh, and petitgrain essential oils. Massaged into the body before bedtime.

Quantities

  • Sweet almond oil
  • Jojoba oil
  • Frankincense essential oil
  • Myrrh essential oil
  • Petitgrain essential oil

Directions

Combine sweet almond oil and jojoba oil in a bottle as the carrier base. Add frankincense, myrrh, and petitgrain essential oils to the blend. Adjust quantities to suit your preference. Massage into the body before going to sleep.
