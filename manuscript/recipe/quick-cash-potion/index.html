🔮Tarot Spell to Increase Your Income
Tarot1
Ace of Pentacles

A magickal prosperity potion using ginger, mint, cinnamon, and the Ace of Pentacles to attract quick cash. Can be enjoyed warm as tea or cool as a refreshing drink. The magick begins working as soon as you consume it.

Quantities

  • A paring knife
  • Fresh gingerroot
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • A clear glass or cup (with no color or designs)
  • Spring water
  • Cinnamon
  • The Ace of Pentacles

Directions

Finely chop the ginger and mint leaves. Place them in a clear glass or cup and add spring water. Add a dash of cinnamon. The mixture may be heated to make tea, but do not let it boil. Place the Ace of Pentacles face-up on an altar or countertop and set the glass on the card. Leave for at least five minutes to allow the card's vibrations to imprint the mixture, then drink.
