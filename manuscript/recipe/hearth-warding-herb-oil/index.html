🏠Warding the Home with Protection Oil

Herbal protection oil infused with rosemary, basil, sage, and thyme for safeguarding home and personal protection.

Quantities

Catnip,Comfrey,Hyssop,Vetivert,Jojoba Oil,Grapeseed Oil

Directions

Add the herbs to the carrier and swirl, don’t stir, the oil in a Clockwise direction.
As the herbs blend together, say, “Herbs of protection, keep danger away. Watch over my home, night and day.”
Let the mixture steep a few hours. Then strain and discard the herbs using a piece of cloth.
Store oil in a dark-colored bottle to prevent spoilage.
Anoint pulse points and items in home.
