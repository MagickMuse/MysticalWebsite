🔥Arabka Soudagar Spell

Closed Practice
Hoodoo
Type
Incense
Correspondences4
💼Business💰Prosperity🏆Success💵Wealth
Plants5

The Hoodoo formulation Arabka Soudagar breaks through business blockages and repairs finances. Bay laurel leaves, cinnamon, frankincense, tonka beans, and vetiver roots are ground together and burned as incense, with the aroma allowed to permeate all areas of the business.

⚠️ Closed Practice Warning: This spell draws directly from Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. The Arabka Soudagar formula is rooted in this tradition. Please engage with respect, proper education, or seek guidance from a practitioner within the tradition.

Quantities

  • Bay laurel leaves
  • Cinnamon
  • Frankincense
  • Tonka beans
  • Vetiver roots

Directions

Grind bay laurel leaves, cinnamon, frankincense, tonka beans, and vetiver roots together into a powder. Burn the resulting powder as incense, allowing the aroma to permeate all areas of the business.
