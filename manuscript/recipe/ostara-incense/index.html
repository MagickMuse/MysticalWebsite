🐰Ostara

Burn during Wiccan rituals on Ostara (the Spring Equinox, which varies from March 20 to 24 each year), or to welcome the spring and refresh your life.

Quantities

  • Frankincense: 2 parts
  • Benzoin: 1 part
  • Dragon's Blood: 1 part
  • Nutmeg: 1/2 part
  • Violet flowers (or a few drops Violet oil): 1/2 part
  • Orange peel: 1/2 part
  • Rose petals: 1/2 part

Directions

Grind the frankincense, benzoin, and dragon's blood. Add nutmeg, violet flowers (or a few drops violet oil), orange peel, and rose petals. Blend thoroughly. Burn during Wiccan rituals on Ostara (the Spring Equinox), or to welcome the spring and refresh your life.
