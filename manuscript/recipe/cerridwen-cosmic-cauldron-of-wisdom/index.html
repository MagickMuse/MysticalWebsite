🔮Cerridwen Cosmic Cauldron of Wisdom

A smoke-burning ritual invoking Cerridwen's cauldron of infinite wisdom. Lemon balm, jasmine, and mugwort are stirred clockwise and set aflame in a cauldron or fireproof bowl, feeding the fire with focused breath to receive inspiration, insight, or transformation. The resulting smoke can also be used for smoke scrying, reading symbols in the embers for guidance.

Quantities

Dried lemon balm
Dried jasmine
Dried mugwort
Cauldron or fireproof bowl
Crystal, wand, spoon, pen, or stylus (for stirring)
Pen and paper or bay leaf (optional)
Matches or lighter

Directions

Add each herb to the cauldron or fireproof bowl one by one, holding each herb's magickal purpose in mind: lemon balm for healing and success, jasmine for prophecy and fortune, mugwort for psychic powers. Using a crystal, wand, spoon, pen, or stylus, stir the herbs clockwise. As you stir, say aloud:

"Like Cerridwen at her cauldron, I stir and stir. Wisdom and insight to me do occur. That I might transform the situation at hand. The answer I seek, shall come to land."

Optionally, write your question or intention on a piece of paper or a bay leaf and add it to the cauldron. Set the herbs aflame. Feed the fire with steady, intentional breath while focusing on your intention; relight as needed. Use the rising smoke to consecrate your body or a sacred item, or soften your gaze into the smoke to scry for symbols, shapes, or impressions that carry wisdom or inspiration.
