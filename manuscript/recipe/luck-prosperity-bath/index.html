🛀Luck/Prosperity Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences5
🩺Health🍀Luck💰Money💰Prosperity💆Self-Care
Plants2
CamphorCinnamon

To draw prosperity, sweetness, and success into your life through a magickal bath infusion.

Quantities

  • Cinnamon
  • Sassafras
  • Sugar
  • Water for brewing tea

Directions

Brew cinnamon, sassafras, and sugar into a tea and add it to your bathwater.

A restorative bath for drawing luck and prosperity back into your life after magical attack or crossed conditions. This simple herbal infusion combines three ingredients that work together to attract abundance, help you retain what comes to you, and generally sweeten your circumstances.

Purpose

After suffering a magical attack, your luck and prosperity may feel blocked or depleted. This bath helps restore the natural flow of good fortune and helps you hold onto the blessings that come your way.

Ingredients

The bath uses three key components:

  • Cinnamon - Draws money and luck while actively driving away misfortune. Its solar fire energy burns through blockages to prosperity.
  • Sassafras - Helps you hold onto what you come across. Once luck begins flowing again, sassafras ensures it sticks with you.
  • Sugar - Sweetens your condition in general, making life circumstances more pleasant and bringing gentle blessing energy.

Directions

Brew the tea: Combine cinnamon, sassafras, and sugar in hot water to create a strong infusion. Allow it to steep until the water is richly colored and fragrant.

Prepare the bath: Draw your bathwater at a comfortable temperature.

Add the infusion: Strain the tea and add it to your bathwater. (You can also add the tea directly without straining if you don't mind plant material in the tub.)

Bathe with intention: As you soak, visualize luck and prosperity flowing back into your life. Feel the sweetness of the bath restoring ease and abundance to your circumstances. Imagine any lingering bad luck or crossed conditions washing away down the drain.

Timing

This bath can be performed at any time, but is particularly effective during:

  • The waxing moon (to draw luck and prosperity)
  • The full moon (for maximum drawing power)
  • The new moon (for fresh starts after clearing away crossed conditions)

Context

This is a gentle, restorative working meant to help you recover financially and materially after defensive magic has cleared away the attack itself. Use it after you've dealt with the source of the magical assault and need to rebuild your prosperity and good fortune.
