A prophetic dream blend for far-seeing and divinatory dreams. Combines nutmeg, cedarwood, and lemon to understand how situations developed and see where choices will lead.

Quantities

  • 5 drops nutmeg essential oil for dreaming, good luck, strength, clairvoyance
  • 3 drops cedarwood essential oil for clairvoyance, consecration, divination, success
  • 15 drops lemon essential oil for calming clarity, blessings, ability

Directions

Need to understand how situation developed? Working to see where choices will lead a year from now? Diffuse 1 drop of synergy in 100 ml water up to thirty minutes before bed to encourage dreams of far-seeing or divinatory purpose.
For added benefit, dilute drop in 2 ml bottle of carrier before anointing yellow candle (success) on Sunday (success) before attempting journeywork through dreams.
