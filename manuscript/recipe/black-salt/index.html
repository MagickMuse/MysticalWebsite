A Hoodoo formula of iron scrapings and salt that prevents and repels evil. ⚠️ Black Salt is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Scrapings from an iron pan, pot, or cauldron
Sea salt or kosher salt

Directions

Scrape the inside of an iron pan, pot, or cauldron and collect the scrapings. Mix them into sea salt or kosher salt. Use in place of regular salt in any protection spell except salt baths to intensify it.

Uses

  • Quick repelling: Toss a handful of black salt toward the direction evil seems to be coming from. This should repel and prevent it, at least for a while.
  • Intensifier: Swap it in for regular salt in protection spells (not baths).
  • Works well in Salt Cross Doorstep.
