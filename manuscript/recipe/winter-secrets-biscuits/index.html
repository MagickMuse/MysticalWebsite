🍪Winter Secrets Biscuits

◇Type
Food
↗Astrology1
🌕Full Moon
↗Plants2
CloveGinger
↗Timing1
❄️Winter

Spicy round biscuits crafted for winter full moon feasting, drawing on the magick of the season's quiet dormancy. Winter invites kitchen witchery, and these biscuits carry the warming energy of ginger and the hidden power of whole cloves sealed between layers of golden dough.

Quantities

4 cups unbleached white flour
2 tablespoons sea salt
2 tablespoons baking powder
1 teaspoon ginger
1/2 cup butter
1 1/2 cups Mystic Moon Milk
2 teaspoons whole cloves

Directions

Preheat the oven to 450°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Combine all dry ingredients in a large bowl and mix thoroughly. Using a pastry cutter or two knives worked in opposite directions, cut in the butter until the mixture resembles small peas. Slowly pour in the Mystic Moon Milk and stir with a wooden spoon until the dough comes together and pulls away from the sides of the bowl.

Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and roll or pat to roughly 1/2-inch thickness. Cut out rounds using a biscuit cutter or the mouth of a clean jar. Gather the scraps, re-roll, and cut again. Lay one layer of rounds on the prepared sheet. Place a single whole clove bud at the center of each round, then press a second round on top, sealing the clove inside.

Refrigerate the assembled biscuits for 30 minutes, then bake for approximately 13 minutes, until the tops are golden.
