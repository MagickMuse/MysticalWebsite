A protective herb sachet for the home, tied in white cloth with red yarn or ribbon in Hertha's name.

Quantities

  • Powdered orris root — 8 parts
  • Pulverized sandalwood — 6 parts
  • Powdered lavender flowers or leaves — 4 parts
  • Powdered patchouli leaves — 2 parts
  • Powdered cloves — 1 part
  • Powdered pimento (allspice) — 1/2 part
  • Musk or civet essence — 1 drop

Directions

  1. Grind and blend all dry ingredients thoroughly by trituration.
  2. Add the musk or civet essence and blend in well.
  3. Tie up in white cloth with red yarn or ribbon, in Hertha's name.
