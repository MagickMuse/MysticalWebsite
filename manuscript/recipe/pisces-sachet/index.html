🌿Pisces Sachet

Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
Type
Powder
Astrology1
Pisces
Plants5
AniseEucalyptusLemonSageSandalwood

An astrological sachet to amplify the positive traits of those born under Pisces.
⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 3 parts Sandalwood
  • 2 parts Sage
  • 1 part Eucalyptus
  • 1 part Anise
  • 1 part Lemon

Directions

Tie up in purple cloth. Wear or carry to enhance your sign's best qualities.
