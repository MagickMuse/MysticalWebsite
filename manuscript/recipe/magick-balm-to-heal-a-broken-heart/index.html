💔Magick Balm to Heal a Broken Heart

◇Directions
Wash the rose quartz and jar with mild soap and water. Cast a circle around your workspace. Pour the carrier oil into the jar. Add the essential oil and inhale the fragrance, allowing it to relax your mind. Crush the chamomile leaves very fine and sprinkle them into the oil. Add the rose quartz. Cap the jar and shake three times to blend and charge the ingredients. Open the circle. Before bed each day, pour a small amount into your palm, dip your index finger in the balm, and rub it on your skin over your heart. Feel it gently soothing the pain. Take several slow, deep breaths, inhaling the pleasant scent and letting it calm your thoughts and emotions. Repeat each night and morning until your sadness diminishes. The flower essence Bleeding Heart can complement this working and is available online or at large health food stores.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
- Small piece of rose quartz - Glass jar or bottle (preferably green) with lid or stopper - 3 ounces carrier oil (olive, almond, or grapeseed) - 6 drops essential oil (rose, jasmine, or ylang-ylang) - 1/4 teaspoon dried chamomile leaves
◇Description
A soothing balm to ease the pain of heartbreak and begin the healing process. This gentle preparation combines the heart-healing properties of rose quartz with calming chamomile and nurturing essential oils.
