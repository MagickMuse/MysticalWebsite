🌾Sweetgrass Workplace Harmony Burn

Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sweetgrass)
Type
Incense
Plants1
Sweetgrass

Sweetgrass is burned in the workplace to encourage coworkers to operate harmoniously, without jealousy or backbiting. ⚠️ Closed Practice: Sweetgrass is sacred to many Indigenous peoples and is used in traditional ceremonies. Its use outside of Indigenous traditions is considered appropriation. Please source respectfully and consider using an alternative such as lavender or rosemary for harmony work.

Quantities

  • Sweetgrass (braid or loose)

Directions

Burn sweetgrass in or near the workplace to encourage coworkers to operate harmoniously, without jealousy and backbiting.
