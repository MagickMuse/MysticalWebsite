🪐Saturnian Incense of Wrath and Chastisement

A Saturnian incense for workings of wrath, chastisement, binding, and punishment. The historical formula is provided for reference only and contains dangerous or difficult-to-obtain ingredients; the modern formula is recommended for practical use.

Quantities

  • Modern formula:
  • Gum myrrh: 4 parts
  • Dried yew or juniper (powdered): 1 part
  • Dried elderberry leaves or twigs (powdered): 1 part
  • Dried cypress twigs (powdered): 1 part
  • Patchouli leaves (or a few drops patchouli oil): 1 part
  • Magnetized iron filings or lodestone dust: 1 pinch
    Historical formula (reference only — not recommended):
  • Black poppy seed
  • Henbane
  • Mandrake root
  • Powdered lodestone
  • Myrrh
  • Brain of cat
  • Blood of bat

Directions

Blend all dry ingredients thoroughly. Burn on charcoal in a thurible during Saturn hours (Saturday, or any day in the hour of Saturn). Use iron filings sparingly and with good ventilation.

Alternative: Pure gum myrrh alone may be substituted as a simplified Saturnian incense.
