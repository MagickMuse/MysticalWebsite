☕Business-Boosting Tea for Small-Business Owners

◇Directions
Place tea in infusion equipment (tea ball, filter, infuser, or bag). Add aromatics before pouring the water. Crush the star anise with the flat side of a chef's knife to release the full flavor. Make sure to use a tight-fitting lid after pouring the hot water. Otherwise, all of the volatile oils are escaping on the breeze, reducing the amount of gorgeous flavor this tea will have. As you grind your herbs and wait for the tea to steep (5 minutes), envision all of the bright new faces who will find your business. These are not just any customers, they are your dream customers. Remember, a rising tide lifts all ships. The people who are supposed to find your work, will.

Drink
◇Quantities
Amounts not given so you can scale to your needs. Blend a cup at a time, or make larger batches. - Black tea (sense of adventure, bravery) - Nutmeg: powdered is fine, but grinding your own is magical. Do not use a coffee grinder on nutmeg or you will kill the grinder (calming, dreaming, good luck, bringing awareness, creativity, hex breaking, health, justice, focus) - Cloves (dispelling negativity, hex breaking, mental powers, prosperity, protection, purification) - Star anise (protection, purification, growth)
◇Description
A magickal tea blend designed to attract dream customers to your small business. The combination of warming spices promotes prosperity, protection, and focused growth.
