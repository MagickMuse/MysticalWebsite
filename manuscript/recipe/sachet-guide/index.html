Sachet Guide

Magickal sachets, sometimes called herbal charms, amulets, or talismans, are small bundles of herbs and other materials wrapped in cloth. Some sachets repel unwanted energies or protect against illness, while others attract specific situations or powers into your life.

This section includes astrological sachets for those wishing to amplify the positive qualities of their Sun sign. Wear them daily as a personal power boost or place them on your altar to represent yourself in workings.

Sachets have a long history and cloth is only one of many traditional containers. Throughout time, people have carried herbs in horns, seashells, leather pouches, fur wrappings, and lockets. Magickal rings were sometimes designed as tiny sachets by placing an herb beneath the gemstone, allowing both materials to work together toward a goal. Herbs were also sewn directly into clothing for protection.

Creating Sachets

A handful of empowered herbs (or less) is usually sufficient. Household sachets tend to be larger than those meant to be carried on your person.

Method

  1. Mix your herbs according to the recipe and empower them with your magickal intention. As you work, know that the herbs are pulsing with specific, programmed energies directed toward your goal.
  2. Select your cloth. Choose a natural fiber such as felt, wool, or cotton in a color that corresponds to your intention. Synthetic materials like polyester can disrupt the herbs' frequencies.
  3. Cut the cloth into a square between four and nine inches across.
  4. Assemble the sachet. Place the empowered herbs in the center of the cloth, gather the corners together, and tie firmly with natural cord such as wool yarn or cotton twine.

Helpful Supplies

Keep a variety of cloth and yarn colors on hand. Felt is especially useful since it comes in many shades and does not fray when cut.

Using Sachets

Personal charms: Hold in your hand and squeeze gently to release the fragrance. Carry with you at all times.

Home or vehicle sachets: Squeeze to activate, then place in the most appropriate location.

Maintenance

Replace sachets every three months or so. Disassemble the old ones and bury the contents to return them to the earth.
