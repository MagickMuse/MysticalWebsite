🕯️Peaceful Home Oil Candle Spell

A condition oil blended from brown sugar, bee pollen, orrisroot, peppermint, and rose petals in a base of sweet almond and jojoba oils, formulated to end familial quarrels and encourage reconciliation.

Quantities

Brown sugar
Bee pollen
Orrisroot
Peppermint leaves
Pink rose petals
Sweet almond oil
Jojoba oil

Directions

Grind the brown sugar, bee pollen, orrisroot, and peppermint leaves together. Place the ground mixture into a bottle together with pink rose petals. Cover with sweet almond oil and jojoba oil. To use, soak cotton balls in the finished oil and tuck them strategically throughout the room.
