🌿Bay Rum Aftershave

Type

Bay leaves, allspice, cloves, and ginger macerated in rum for 3 to 4 weeks, strained, and finished with a drop or two of bay essential oil to strengthen the scent.

Quantities

Bay leaves (fresh preferred), to fill a wide-mouth jar
Ground or grated allspice, to taste
Whole cloves, to taste
Grated ginger, to taste
Rum, enough to cover the herbs
1–2 drops bay essential oil

Directions

Pack a wide-mouth jar with bay leaves, leaving space at the top. Add the allspice, cloves, and ginger. Pour in enough rum to cover the herbs. Seal tightly and leave in a warm place for 3 to 4 weeks. Strain out the herbs and rebottle the liquid. Add 1 to 2 drops of bay essential oil to strengthen the scent.
