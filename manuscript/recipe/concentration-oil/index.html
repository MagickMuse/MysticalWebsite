Focus-enhancing oil blend with basil, rosemary, and geranium for concentration and mental clarity.

Quantities

1 drop of Basil Essential Oil
2 drops of Rosemary Essential Oil
2 drops of Cypress Essential Oil
60 mL of carrier oil (Apricot Kernel Oil or Jojoba Oil)

Directions

Blend essential oils with carrier oil. Apply to pulse points or diffuse to enhance concentration and focus.
