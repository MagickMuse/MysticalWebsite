🏠Home Cleansing Anointing Oil

An anointing oil for blessing the home during cleansing rituals, best used on the full or new moon.

Quantities

1 drop angelica essential oil
1 drop clove essential oil
2 drops sweet orange essential oil
1 drop pine essential oil
4 teaspoons (20 ml) carrier oil

Directions

Fill your container with 2 teaspoons carrier oil, and then add 1 drop angelica essential oil, 1 drop clove essential oil, 2 drops sweet orange essential oil, and 1 drop pine essential oil. Fill the container with remaining carrier oil, and put on the lid. Roll the container between your hands until the oils have been thoroughly mixed. Use this oil as part of a regular cleansing schedule on the full or new moon. Sweep the home while chanting or singing. Carry incense or lit plant materials to each room in the home to chase away evil spirits. After sweeping and chanting, anoint the doors, windows, sinks (including garbage disposal), and backs of mirrors with the oil. Once the ritual is complete, ring a bell and announce that the home is whole and protected.
