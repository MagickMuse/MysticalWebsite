To be worn during any operation or adventure involving lust and seduction. Two formulas are given: a traditional rich perfume using luxury animalic bases, and a practical alternative using accessible essential oils. Patchouli oil alone, used sparingly, can serve as a substitute for either formula. A few drops may be mixed into Sabbat oil for extra impact.

For matters of lust rather than romantic love, favour the bitter and piney essences. Leave floral notes to Habondia and her workings of romance.

Quantities

  • Traditional Formula (heavy perfume):
  • 5 parts musk (synthetic essence acceptable)
  • 5 parts civet (synthetic acceptable)
  • 1 part ambergris (synthetic acceptable)
  • 1 part patchouli or cassia (synthetic acceptable)
    Alternative Formula (accessible, highly potent used sparingly):
  • 1/3 patchouli oil
  • 1/3 cedarwood oil
  • 1/3 pine oil (good-quality gum turpentine)
  • 1 drop vanilla
  • 1 drop jasmine oil or ylang-ylang
  • 1 drop oil of cloves
  • 1 drop essence of rose
  • 1 drop geranium oil

Directions

Traditional: Combine essences and allow to blend for at least 24 hours before use.

Alternative: Combine all oils in the given proportions. For a toilet water or cologne, dilute the blended perfume in pure alcohol. For a concentrated perfume, use undiluted.

Minimum version: Patchouli oil alone, used sparingly, is a fully valid substitute.
