🔥Fire Cider

A folk remedy for colds, flu, and winter illness. This blend of spicy vegetables and roots is steeped in apple cider vinegar for several weeks. Sip a few teaspoons three times daily when feeling a cold, add a teaspoon to juice every morning to ward off illness, use in soups and chilis, or make into a salad dressing. The strained vegetables taste amazing in a stir fry.

Quantities

  • 2-3 cups apple cider vinegar (with the Mother)
  • 1 orange, sliced
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1/2 cup horseradish, diced
  • 1/2 cup parsley, chopped
  • 1/2 cup garlic
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 1/4 cup raw honey (or more to taste)
  • 1/4 cup diced ginger
  • 1 habanero, split and deseeded
  • 2 tbsp rosemary
  • 2 tbsp thyme
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • Quart jar with lid

Directions

Chop all ingredients and place into a clean quart jar. Fill with apple cider vinegar. Store in a dark, cool cabinet for 4 to 5 weeks (up to 8 weeks). Strain out the material and add honey until palatable. Sip a few teaspoons three times a day when feeling a cold coming on, add a teaspoon to juice every morning to ward off illness, incorporate into soups and chilis, or use as a salad dressing base. The strained vegetables taste amazing in a stir fry.

Alternative Recipe

A simpler four-root formula without citrus or additional herbs. Can also be used as a salad dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 to 5 cloves of garlic, coarsely chopped
  • 3 to 4 tablespoons freshly grated ginger root
  • 3 to 4 tablespoons grated horseradish (bottled is acceptable)
  • Apple cider vinegar (enough to cover)
  • Honey and cayenne pepper to taste

Directions

Combine the onion, garlic, ginger, and horseradish in a wide-mouth quart jar. Pour over enough warmed apple cider vinegar to cover. Let sit in a warm place for three to four weeks. Strain out the herbs. Add honey and cayenne to taste. To use, take one to two tablespoons at the first sign of a cold and repeat every three to four hours until symptoms are gone.
