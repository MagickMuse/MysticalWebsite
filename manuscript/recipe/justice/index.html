A powerful synergy blend for justice work, court cases, and hex breaking. Creates justice, lifts spirits, and provides protection during legal matters and empowerment workings.

Quantities

  • 2 drops jasmine absolute for justice, ability, clarity
  • 3 drops benzoin essential oil for healing exorcism, hex breaking
  • 15 drops frankincense essential oil for justice, banishing, protection
  • 2 drops peppermint essential oil for justice, good luck
  • Pinch of coltsfoot to improve efficacy

Directions

Mix all oils together. Diffuse 1 drop synergy in 100 ml water during workings calling for justice. For an anointing oil, dilute 1 drop of synergy in 2 ml carrier oil. Add a pinch of coltsfoot to the anointing oil container before adding carrier to improve efficacy. Use the blend to anoint candles and charms for court cases, police involvement, justice work, hex breaking, and empowerment.
Court Magick: Court cases have a true name (docket number, file number, or case name like Jones v. Jones) and birthdate (filing date) that carry the essence of the case. These details appear on official correspondence. Copy court letters (keep originals for attorneys), anoint white candle for purity or black candle to banish the case, place in fireproof dish. Anoint the copy with Justice Oil. Burn banishing candle on Saturday or empowering candle on Sunday. Keep letter on altar or safe place until case settles.
