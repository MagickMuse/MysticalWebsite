💰Gold Galore

◇Directions
Mix all oils. Place 1 drop synergy in 2 ml bottle and fill with carrier oil for magickal use. Let your nose be your guide; the better it smells to you, the more likely you are to use it, and the likelier it is to work. Both myrrh and lemon balm are commonly sold in already diluted form, which is why their drop counts are higher than undiluted oils.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
• 10 drops lemon balm essential oil for good fortune, success, joy, money • 10 drops myrrh essential oil for good luck, prosperity, transformation, success • 1 drop peppermint essential oil for good luck, happiness • 5 drops petitgrain essential oil for purification, transformation, joy
◇Description
A prosperity blend for bestowing luck, fortune, success, and happiness, focusing on overall good fortune rather than fast cash.
