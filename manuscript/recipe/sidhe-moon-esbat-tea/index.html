🌙Sidhe Moon Esbat Tea

◇Type
Drink
↗Rituals1
🕯️Sidhe Moon Esbat Ritual
↗Timing1
🌕Esbat

A ritual tea blend for the Sidhe Moon Esbat, designed to draw the Fair Folk near and open the gateway between this world and Otherworld. Each ingredient is added with an incantation.

Quantities

  • 3 teaspoons black tea
  • ½ teaspoon chamomile
  • 1 teaspoon dandelion root
  • ½ teaspoon elder flower
  • 1½ teaspoons hops
  • ½ teaspoon mugwort
  • ½ teaspoon raspberry leaf
  • 1½ teaspoons rose hips
  • Boiling water
  • Sweetener and milk to taste

Directions

Warm the teapot before beginning. Add each ingredient one at a time, speaking the corresponding line of the incantation as you add it:

Black tea: "Black for power"
Chamomile: "Apple of night"
Dandelion root: "Root of the sun"
Elder flower: "Lady's Blessing"
Hops: "Lord's leap for joy"
Mugwort: "Then between the worlds"
Raspberry leaf: "To Fairy bramble"
Rose hips: "With token of love"

Pour boiling water over the herbs and cover. As you do, speak:

"Brewed to bring Fair Ones close to me."

Allow to steep while you complete your circle casting preparations. Serve with sweetener and milk as desired, alongside tea cookies for the Simple Feast.
