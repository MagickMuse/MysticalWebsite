🌱Imbolc Incense: Herbs of Rebirth

◇Type
Incense
◇Description
A ceremonial loose incense for Imbolc and Candlemas, blending white rose petals, bay laurel, myrrh, and copal. Burned on charcoal, it invokes the element of air and calls in the spirit of Brighid at the threshold of the year's first quickening.
↗Plants3
Bay LaurelMyrrhRose
↗Timing2
🕯️CandlemasImbolc

Imbolc is the first stirring of light returning after winter, and this incense honors that threshold with a blend sacred to Brighid. White roses are considered particularly holy to the Celtic goddess celebrated on this feast.

Quantities

  • Copal resin
  • Dried white rose petals
  • Bay laurel leaves
  • Myrrh resin
  • Small cauldron with heatproof trivet
  • Charcoal round

Directions

  1. Grind the copal resin, rose petals, bay laurel, and myrrh together until well combined. Set a small cauldron on a heatproof trivet and light a charcoal round. When the coal turns gray, sprinkle a measure of the incense blend onto the coal.
  2. Use the incense to invoke air and the east at the opening of an Imbolc ritual, or burn it as a solitary offering at any point of personal initiation or change.

While the incense burns, you may speak aloud:

Hail, Brighid!

Magickal Notes

This incense suits any working of initiation, creative rebirth, or healing. The myrrh and copal deepen the spiritual atmosphere, while the rose petals call in the goddess's tender and fierce love.
