🧹Better Business Floorwash: Cinnamon & Brick Dust

Type
Wash
Correspondences3
💼Business💰Prosperity💵Wealth
Plants1
Cinnamon

A traditional business-drawing floorwash combining cinnamon, brown sugar, and red brick dust in water with white vinegar. Applied from street to front door and from corners inward.

Quantities

  • Ground cinnamon
  • Brown sugar
  • Red brick dust
  • Water
  • White vinegar

Directions

Combine ground cinnamon, brown sugar, and red brick dust. Add this mixture to a bucket of water together with white vinegar. Scrub the walkway and doorway of your business beginning at the street and moving toward the front door. Scrub front steps. Cleanse interior floors beginning at the front door and corners, working toward the center of each room. Allow to dry without rinsing.
