🍸Werewolves of London Martini Tea Cocktail

◇Type
Drink
↗Correspondences3
🧲Attraction☮️Peace🛡️Protection
↗Materials2
🍸Gin🍸Martini Glass
↗Plants1
Lavender

A sophisticated tea martini combining the commanding and attraction properties of Earl Grey with the protective magic of gin and the centering, peaceful energy of lavender. Green Earl Grey produces a lighter, more delicate version.

Quantities

  • Ice
  • 2 1/2 tsp. Earl Grey tea (green Earl Grey is best)
  • 1 oz. dry gin (or Earl Grey Tea-Infused Gin for extra kick)
  • 4 oz. water (175°F for green tea, 195°F for black tea)
  • Lavender syrup to taste
  • Martini glass
  • Tea infuser
  • Cocktail shaker

Directions

Chill a martini glass with ice during preparation.

Boil water and cool to the desired temperature (175°F for green tea, 195°F for black tea).

Add tea to the infuser and pour the hot water over it. Steep 2 minutes for green tea, 4 minutes for black tea.

Remove tea leaves.

Pour over ice in a cocktail shaker. Add gin and lavender syrup. Shake.

Strain into chilled glass.
