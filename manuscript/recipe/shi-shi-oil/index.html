👝Shi Shi Oil Conjure Bag

A classic Hoodoo condition oil of powdered angelica root, bay laurel, and cloves in jojoba oil, worn on the skin or carried on a cotton ball to attract wealth fast.

Quantities

  • Angelica root
  • Bay laurel leaves
  • Cloves
  • Jojoba oil
  • A cotton ball

Directions

  1. This classic Hoodoo condition oil allegedly attracts wealth to its wearer fast! Try it and see.
  2. Chop, grind, and powder the following ingredients: angelica root, bay laurel leaves, and cloves.
  3. Add them to jojoba oil.
  4. Rub a drop between the palms of your hands.
  5. Dab a drop onto your hair or behind your ears.
  6. Soak a cotton ball in Shi Shi and carry it in your pocket or tucked into your bra.
