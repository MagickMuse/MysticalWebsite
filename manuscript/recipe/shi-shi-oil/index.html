🧴Shi Shi Oil

◇Directions
This classic Hoodoo condition oil allegedly attracts wealth to its wearer fast! Try it and see. 1. Chop, grind, and powder the following ingredients: angelica root, bay laurel leaves, and cloves. 2. Add them to jojoba oil. 3. Rub a drop between the palms of your hands. 4. Dab a drop onto your hair or behind your ears. 5. Soak a cotton ball in Shi Shi and carry it in your pocket or tucked into your bra.
◇Type
Oil
◇Quantities
- Angelica root - Bay laurel leaves - Cloves - Jojoba oil - A cotton ball
◇Description
A classic Hoodoo condition oil of powdered angelica root, bay laurel, and cloves in jojoba oil, worn on the skin or carried on a cotton ball to attract wealth fast.
