🚨"For Emergencies" Incense

Type
Incense
Correspondences2
🔄Habits🛡️Protection
Plants9
AsafoetidaCayenne PepperDragon's BloodFrankincenseGarlicGrains of ParadiseMyrrhRosemaryRue

A powerful banishing incense to be rid of foul demons, wrathful spirits, and other noisome creatures. The asafoetida, cayenne, rue, and garlic emit powerful smoke that is irritating to the eyes, nose, and lungs.

Quantities

3 parts Frankincense, 2 parts Dragon's Blood, 2 parts Myrrh, 1 part Rosemary, 1 part Asafoetida, 1 part Cayenne, 1 part Grains of Paradise, 1 part Rue, 1 part Garlic

Directions

Stand back and hold your nose, or better still, leave the room while this incense is smoldering.

Caution

Asafoetida, cayenne, rue, and garlic emit powerful smoke that is irritating to the eyes, nose, and lungs. Ensure good ventilation and leave the room while burning.
