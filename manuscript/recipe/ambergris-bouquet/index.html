🐋Ambergris Bouquet

◇Type
Oil
↗Oils1
Patchouli Essential Oil

A plant-based substitute for ambergris that replicates its warm, complex scent profile without using animal-derived ingredients. Ideal for ethical practitioners who wish to avoid whale products.

Quantities

  • Cypress Oil
  • Patchouli Oil
    Combine in equal parts, or adjust ratio to preference. More cypress creates a fresher scent; more patchouli creates a deeper, earthier blend.

Directions

Combine cypress oil and patchouli oil in a dark glass bottle. Cap and shake gently to blend. Allow to sit for at least 24 hours before use to let the oils marry.

Use this bouquet as a direct substitute in any recipe calling for ambergris. The cypress provides the fresh, slightly woody top notes while the patchouli contributes the deep, earthy base that mimics ambergris's complexity.

Store in a cool, dark place. This blend improves with age as the oils continue to harmonize.

This substitute blend provides the warm, complex scent of ambergris without relying on whale-derived ingredients. The combination of cypress and patchouli creates a similar magickal effect suitable for love workings, attraction magick, and enhancing other blends.

Why Use a Substitute?

Ambergris is produced by sperm whales, a species that has faced severe population decline. While ambergris washes ashore naturally rather than being harvested directly from whales, ethical practitioners may prefer plant-based alternatives.

Magickal Properties

Cypress brings purification, protection, and connection to the underworld and transformation. Its fresh, clean scent provides the top notes of this blend.

Patchouli contributes grounding, attraction, and prosperity energy. Its deep, earthy quality provides the base notes that give this bouquet its staying power.

Together, these oils create a blend suitable for:

  • Love and attraction workings
  • Enhancing other magickal blends
  • Grounding and centering
  • Transformation rituals

Usage Notes

This bouquet can be used anywhere ambergris is called for in traditional recipes. Adjust the ratio of cypress to patchouli based on your preference and the specific working at hand.
