🍊Orange and Clove Pomander

Type
Talisman
Plants5
CloveFrankincenseMistletoeMyrrhPine
Timing1
Yule

A fragrant Yule charm made by studding an orange with whole cloves in sigil patterns. Hung with ribbon, the pomander fills the home with warming solar scent and serves as a symbol of solar return. Dried in a paper bag, it lasts well beyond the season.

Quantities

  • 1 orange
  • Handfuls of dried whole clove buds
  • 1 toothpick
  • Floral wire and wire cutters
  • Ribbon
  • Pliers
  • Brown paper bag (optional)

Directions

Use a toothpick to perforate the orange's skin in sigils, star patterns, or elemental and planetary symbols. Press the stem of a clove bud into each perforation. Cut a length of floral wire and thread it through the orange, leaving half an inch exposed at the bottom; loop it at the top and run it back down through the fruit. Twist the wire ends together at the bottom and conceal them with a decorative ribbon or tuck them inward with pliers. The pomander is now ready to hang. To preserve it longer, set it inside a paper bag to dry out slowly.

A Yule pomander is one of the most satisfying seasonal charms to create. Before beginning, consider the magickal symbolism: the orange represents the sun returning after the longest night, while cloves bring protection, warmth, and attraction.

Evergreen Yule Crafts

Before making your pomander, consider also incorporating pine and fir into your Yule home. Pine needles and cones can be arranged as a potpourri and dusted with finely crushed frankincense and myrrh resins, filling the space with warmth and sacred scent. For a wreath, layer small evergreen boughs over a large embroidery hoop or shape 8-gauge wire into a circle and cover with greenery. Finish with a sprig of mistletoe and a brightly colored ribbon as a symbol of the unbroken wheel of the year.

Magickal Notes

The patterns you stud into the skin carry your intention. Planetary symbols draw planetary energies; elemental symbols align the charm with the four directions. The finished pomander makes a potent protective talisman for the home through the dark of winter.
