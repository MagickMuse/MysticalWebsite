🛁Cleansing Bath Scrub

◇Type
Bath Blend

A fragrant purifying salt scrub to use before ritual or magick work. Cleanses aura, calms mind, and prepares body for sacred work.

Quantities

  • 1/2 cup sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon zest
  • 1 cup fine Epsom salts
  • 1/2 cup sweet almond or jojoba oil
  • 3 drops lavender essential oil
  • 3 drops frankincense essential oil
  • 2 teaspoons lavender flowers (fresh or dry)
  • Lidded jar (at least 2 cup capacity)
  • Small glass bowl
  • Damp washcloth

Directions

Purify the ingredients first by visualizing white light around them. Grind the dried flowers finely while stating aloud that they will cleanse and purify body, mind, and spirit. In the covered jar, shake the two salts together. In the bowl, mix the oils. Open the jar and add the oil blend to the salt. Close the jar and shake to combine thoroughly, then open the jar and sprinkle the herbs over the oil and salt blend. Close the jar and shake to blend one final time. To use in the bath or shower, place about a tablespoon in the center of a clean, damp washcloth or in the palm of your hand. Gently rub the salts against the skin, imagining them loosening any negative energy clinging to you. Feel the purifying salt, lavender, frankincense, and lemon soaking into your body, cleansing your aura and calming your mind. When cleansed, rinse the salt scrub and the negative energy away with water and dry gently with a clean towel. Use warm, not hot, water. Do not use this scrub on the face. If you have sensitive skin, blend the herbs and essential oils into the oil only and omit the salt; rub the oil gently into your skin prior to bath or shower, then rinse off.
