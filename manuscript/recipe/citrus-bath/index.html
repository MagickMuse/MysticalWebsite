🔓Citrus Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences2
✨Cleansing🧹Purification
Plants5
LemonLimeOrangeTangerineYarrow

A bright, invigorating cleansing bath using as many varieties of citrus as available, combined with yarrow hydrosol. Direct application of the cut fruit to the body enhances the aura-cleansing effect.

Quantities

  • As many citrus fruits as possible (lemons, limes, oranges, tangerines, etc.)
  • Yarrow hydrosol, to taste

Directions

Draw a bath. Quarter each citrus fruit, squeeze the juice into the water, and toss the fruit halves in as well. Add yarrow hydrosol generously. Enter the bath and rub the cut fruit directly over your body. Allow yourself to air-dry afterward, letting the citrus residue remain on the skin for as long as possible.
