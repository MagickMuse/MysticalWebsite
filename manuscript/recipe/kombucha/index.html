🍵Kombucha

Type
Drink
Correspondences1
❤️‍🩹Healing
Plants1
Green Tea

A probiotic-rich fermented tea drink. The fermentation produces 0.5 to 1 percent alcohol, not enough to be considered alcoholic but those with alcohol sensitivity should be aware.

Quantities

  • 2 cups kombucha (starter)
  • 1 gallon water
  • 8 tea bags
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 SCOBY (handle gently)
  • Large fermenting jug
  • Tea towel and rubber band

Directions

Boil 4 cups of water. Add sugar and stir until dissolved. Pour into the large fermenting jug. Add the tea bags and allow them to steep 15 minutes. Add 2 1/2 quarts of water to the jug and remove the tea bags. Stir in kombucha (this makes the tea acidic enough to inhibit bad bacteria and yeast from forming). Gently transfer in a SCOBY. If you have more than one (they peel apart in layers), you can produce multiple batches simultaneously. Only one SCOBY per jar is needed. Cover the mouth of the jar with a tea towel and a rubber band to hold it in place. Ferment 7 to 10 days; the shorter the time, the less vinegary it will taste. The flavor will develop the longer it is in contact with the SCOBY. Give a little sip each day after 7 days and keep fermenting until it reaches the sweetness and tartness balance that tastes right to you. Set aside 2 cups of kombucha for the next batch. To infuse with fruits for extra flavor, put fruit, juice, or other flavorings in a separate jar without the SCOBY to infuse for 2 days before bottling. When bottling, leave 2 inches of headspace. Leave on a counter out of direct sunlight for 1 to 3 days to develop carbonation. Refrigerate after carbonated and drink within a lunar cycle.
