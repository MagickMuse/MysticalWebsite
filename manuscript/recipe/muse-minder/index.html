A creativity blend for all artistic pursuits, including vanquishing writer's block.

Quantities

  • 5 drops eucalyptus essential oil (Eucalyptus globulus) for awakening
  • 1 drop cinnamon leaf essential oil (Cinnamomum verum) for creativity, uplifting
  • 3 drops oakmoss absolute (Evernia furfuraceae) for manifestation
  • 10 drops tangerine essential oil (Citrus reticulata) for inspiration
    Total: 19 drops fills a 2 ml bottle

Directions

Mix all essential oils in a 2 ml glass bottle. To diffuse, use 1 drop synergy per 100 ml water in a diffuser near creative spaces and workshops to allow art to manifest. To use as anointing oil for candles, add 5 drops of synergy to a 10 ml roller bottle, then fill with carrier oil, shake well, and apply to candles. Do not use on skin if you are sensitive to cinnamon.

About This Blend

This fusion of high eucalyptus notes and dark oakmoss grounds the mind and the heart. The tangerine and cinnamon lift the burdens of the creative mind to allow the art to flow.
