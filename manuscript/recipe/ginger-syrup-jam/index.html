🍟Ginger Syrup/Jam

Type
Food
Correspondences5
🤧Common Cold😷Coughs😖Cramp🫀Dysmenorrhoea🤢Nausea
Plants1
Ginger

Fresh ginger root simmered in just enough honey to cover until soft and mushy, creating a dual-use honey syrup and jam. For colds, stomach cramps, motion sickness, coughs, and menstrual discomfort. Take 1 tablespoon as needed, or stir into hot water for a tea.

Quantities

1 large clump fresh ginger root, peeled and grated
Honey (just enough to barely cover)

Directions

Peel and grate a large clump of fresh ginger root and place in a pan. Add just enough honey to barely cover. Simmer over low heat for 10 to 15 minutes until the ginger is soft and mushy. Do not strain; the ginger will be soft and add texture. Pour into a glass jar and refrigerate. Take 1 tablespoon as needed, or stir 2 to 3 tablespoons into a cup of hot water for ginger tea.
