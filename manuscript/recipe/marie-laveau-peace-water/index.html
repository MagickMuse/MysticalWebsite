💧Marie Laveau Peace Water

Closed Practice
New Orleans Voodoo
Type
Wash
Correspondences3
✨Cleansing❤️‍🩹Healing☮️Peace

A New Orleans five-water wash invented by Voodoo Queen Marie Laveau for bringing peace and calm to the home.

⚠️ Closed Practice Warning: This formula draws from New Orleans Voodoo, a closed Afro-Caribbean and African American spiritual tradition. Please engage with respect, proper education, or seek guidance from a practitioner within the tradition.

Quantities

  • Rainwater
  • River water
  • Spring water
  • Ocean water
  • Holy water from a church
  • Optional: Florida Water (as substitute for any unavailable water)

Directions

This popular New Orleans method for bringing peace to the home was invented by the famous Voodoo Queen Marie Laveau. Collect equal parts of five types of water: rainwater, river water, spring water, ocean water, and holy water from a church. If you don't have access to one of these waters, it can be replaced by Florida Water. Mix all the waters together in a clean container. This water can be used as a floor wash by adding it to your mop bucket and washing floors from back to front, visualizing peace filling your home. Alternatively, simply sprinkle the peace water around the home in corners, doorways, and any areas that feel tense or troubled. You can also fill a spray bottle with peace water and mist it throughout your living space. As you apply the water, focus your intention on peace, calm, and healing settling into your home and relationships. This is particularly effective after magickal attack, household arguments, or any situation that has disturbed the peace of your space.

When to Use

Particularly effective after magickal attack, household arguments, or any situation that has disturbed the peace of your space.
