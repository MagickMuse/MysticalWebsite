🫙Tranquil Times Oil

Type

A condition oil blending white rose petals, cumin, and caraway seed in jojoba oil, used to dress blue and white candles to promote tranquility and peace.

Quantities

White rose petals (crumbled)
Cumin
Caraway seed
Jojoba oil

Directions

Add crumbled white rose petals, cumin, and caraway seed to jojoba oil. Allow to infuse. Use to dress blue and white candles and burn daily or as needed.
