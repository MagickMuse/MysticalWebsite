✨Mother Holle's Cleansing Bath

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences2
⚖️Balance✨Cleansing
Plants12
AvensElderElecampaneGerman ChamomileMintMugwortMulleinPansySaint John's WortSouthernwoodVervainYarrow

Mother Holle, the Germanic Queen of Witches and leader of the Wild Hunt, presides over this twelve-herb intensive cleansing bath. The botanicals are traditionally gathered on Midsummer's Eve and the bath is used between the winter solstice and January 6th for deep spiritual cleansing.

Quantities

  • All twelve botanicals, a generous handful of each
  • Boiling water, enough for a large strong infusion

Directions

Blend the following botanicals: avens, chamomile, elder, elecampane, heartsease, mint, mugwort, mullein, Saint John's Wort, southernwood, vervain, and yarrow. Pour boiling water over the herb blend and allow to steep. Strain the liquid and add to a drawn bath. For maximum spiritual cleansing and happiness, float fresh heartsease (wild pansy) blossoms in the bathwater.
