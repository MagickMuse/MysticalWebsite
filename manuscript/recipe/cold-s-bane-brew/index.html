🌼Cold's Bane Brew

Type
Drink
Correspondences3
❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health🤒Illness
Plants5
ElderGerman ChamomileHyssopLemon BalmRosehip

A second herbal tea blend to ease cold symptoms.

Quantities

  • 2 tsp black tea
  • 1 tsp Chamomile
  • 1 tsp Elder flower
  • 1/2 tsp Hyssop
  • 1/2 tsp Lemon balm
  • 1 1/2 tsp Rose hips

Directions

Brew using the standard method for a good pot of tea. Drink when experiencing cold symptoms.
