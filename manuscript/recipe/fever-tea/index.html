Recipe Grimoire

🌡️Fever Tea

◇Directions
Put the three herbs into a glass quart jar. Pour boiling hot water over them. Cap and let steep for thirty to forty-five minutes. Strain out the herbs. Pour out and heat the amount needed, adding honey to taste. Recap the remaining liquid to preserve the rest.
◇Type
Elixir
◇Quantities
1 part catnip leaf 1 part elderberry flowers 1 part spearmint leaf Honey to sweeten
◇Description
A three-herb infusion of catnip, elderberry flowers, and spearmint steeped in boiling water for thirty to forty-five minutes. Sweetened with honey. Useful for breaking fevers in children and adults. For a persistent or high fever, consult a doctor.
↗Correspondences2
❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health
↗Plants3
CatnipElderSpearmint

Dosage

  • Adults: ½ to 1 cup every two hours.
  • Children ages 3–6: ¼ cup every two hours.
  • Children under age 3: 1 teaspoon per year of age.

For a persistent or high fever, see a doctor.
