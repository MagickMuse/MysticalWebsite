🍞San Calogero Bread

A special Sicilian ritual bread made for Saint Calogero's feast day. Cut into anatomical shapes like ex-votos, blessed, and preserved to be eaten in times of need. Traditional bread is studded with fennel, sesame, and poppy seeds.

Quantities

  • Bread dough (your preferred recipe)
  • Fennel seeds
  • Sesame seeds
  • Poppy seeds
  • Wheat (for small pots grown in darkness, as traditional offering)

Directions

  1. Prepare bread dough using your preferred recipe. Traditional Italian bread dough works well.
  2. Add the sacred seeds. Knead fennel seeds, sesame seeds, and poppy seeds into the dough. These three seeds are traditional to San Calogero.
  3. Shape into anatomical forms. Cut or mold the dough into shapes representing body parts: arms, legs, hands, feet, heads, or any body part needing healing. These shapes function like ex-votos (votive offerings).
  4. Bake as usual until golden brown.
  5. Have the bread blessed on Saint Calogero's feast day (June 18) if possible, or bless it yourself with prayers to the saint.
  6. Preserve the bread. San Calogero bread is not eaten immediately but saved for times of illness or need. When you or a loved one requires healing, eat the blessed bread while petitioning the saint.
    Festival Tradition: During San Calogero's procession in Sicily, devotees pelt his passing statue with bread rolls. This is an act of joyful devotion, not disrespect.
    Additional Offering: Place small pots of wheat grown in darkness beside the saint's statue on his feast day.

Background

Saint Calogero is a beloved Sicilian saint associated with healing, particularly of joint disorders. His feast coincides with a traditional Sicilian bread festival, and the ritual bread made in his honor has powerful healing properties when blessed and consumed in times of need.

The Ex-Voto Tradition

Shaping the bread into anatomical forms connects to the ex-voto tradition: offerings made in gratitude for or in hope of healing. By shaping bread into the form of an afflicted body part, you create an edible prayer that can later be consumed when healing is needed.

The Three Sacred Seeds

  • Fennel - digestive healing, protection
  • Sesame - prosperity, opening doors
  • Poppy - sleep, peace, relief from pain

Best Timing

  • Prepare on or near June 18 (Saint Calogero's feast)
  • The festival in Agrigento runs from the first to second Sunday in July
