🃏Tarot Time Spritzer

Type
Drink
Correspondences3
💕Love🛡️Protection🧹Purification
Plants1
Strawberry

A strawberries-and-cream styled drink superb in summer for cooling the heat, but in winter a divine reminder of warmer times.

Quantities

  • 8 oz sparkling water
  • 1 oz Tarot Time Tea Vodka
  • 1 tsp simple syrup
  • ½ cup strawberries, diced
  • Ice

Directions

Add simple syrup to the glass.

Pour in diced strawberries and vodka.

Muddle.

Add a handful of ice cubes.

Top with sparkling water and stir gently.
