✨Astral Travel Powder

Type
Powder
Correspondences2
👻Astral Projection💤Dreams
Plants4
ArrowrootCinnamonMugwortSandalwood

A dreamy blend to encourage consciously directed astral projection during sleep.

Quantities

  • 2 parts Sandalwood
  • 1 part Mugwort
  • 1 part Cinnamon
  • Arrowroot powder (to blend)

Directions

Grind the botanicals (sandalwood, mugwort, cinnamon) to a fine powder. Blend with arrowroot powder. Sprinkle on your sheets before setting out on your astral journey.
