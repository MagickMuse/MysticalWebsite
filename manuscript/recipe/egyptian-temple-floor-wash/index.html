🏠Egyptian Temple Floor Wash

Type
Wash
Correspondences5
✨Cleansing🏺Egypt👁️Mediumship🧹Purification👤Spirits
Materials5
Baking Soda🪣Bucket🧂Sea Salt🧪Vinegar💧Water
Plants12

A kyphi-based floor wash that cleanses a room before ritual, spellcasting, or séances.

Quantities

Botanical ingredients of Kyphi
1 bucket of rinse water
1 handful sea salt
Baking soda
Vinegar

Directions

Blend and mash the botanical ingredients of Kyphi, then stir them into a bucket of rinse water along with a handful of sea salt, baking soda, and vinegar. Wash the floor with the mixture to cleanse the room before ritual, spellcasting, or séances.
