🌕Moon Water

A foundational lunar preparation made by charging water under the energy of the full moon or any significant lunar event. Moon Water can be used to empower ritual baths, craft cleansing sprays, support water scrying, bless hands or magickal tools, make lunar ice cubes, or simply be drunk to connect with lunar energy. Charge water on any favorable astrological occasion to preserve for future use.

Quantities

  • Glass jar with lid
  • Purified or spring water
  • Pen and paper (optional)

Directions

Choose a spot where moonlight can reach the water directly if possible, outdoors or on a moonlit windowsill. If neither is available, work on any windowsill and attune the water to the moon's energy through intention alone. Work with what you have.

If you are holding a specific intention for this lunation, bring it to mind now. If not, simply tune in to the energy of the current moon. If you feel called to, write your intention or a single word on the paper and set the jar on top of it, writing-side facing up.

Pour the water into the jar, visualizing the moon pouring its blessings into the vessel as you do. Cup your hands around the jar and hold the image of the moon above you in the night sky, its light flowing down and charging the water with your intention. Stay with the jar until you sense the water shift or respond energetically.

Using your fingertip, trace a moon symbol in the air above the opening of the jar, the crescent of astrology or the Triple Goddess symbol, then gently press the symbol downward toward the water with an open palm, sealing the energy in.

Leave the jar out overnight. Collect it in the morning, before sunrise if you can manage it. Refrigerate and use within a week. To preserve the water longer, add a small splash of vodka and label the jar with the date, time, and moon sign; this way you can draw on the energy of a specific lunation in future elixirs and tinctures.
