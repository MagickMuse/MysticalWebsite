🍸Mojito Magick

A drinkable influence spell combining peppermint and rum for clarity, healing, prosperity, or banishing. Think of it as a drinkable Bend Over Oil.

Quantities

  • 1 highball glass
  • 1 pub glass or pint glass
  • Ice to chill a glass
  • 1 oz light rum
  • 9 mint leaves
  • 1 tbsp superfine sugar
  • 1 lime, cut into pieces, plus extra for garnish
  • 3 oz club soda

Directions

Gather your tools: a highball glass, a pub glass or pint glass, and a muddler. Add half the ice you would normally use into the highball glass and let it chill. You will add more later. Close your eyes and connect with the earth. Envision a pale blue thread of peace leaving your heels and burying itself deep within the planet, allowing you to draw up the energies you need from therein. Muddle 9 mint leaves in the bottom of an empty pub glass. Envision the intention you are empowering (clarity, healing, prosperity, banishing) and direct the energy you draw up from the earth down through the muddler and into those mint leaves to release their highest potential. Add one tablespoon of superfine sugar, also called caster sugar or baker's sugar, to the muddled mint. This will dissolve quickly. Muddle mint and sugar to incorporate. Continue envisioning your outcome while sending energy into the muddler. Powdered sugar will remain lumpy and table sugar will not dissolve readily; if you do not have superfine sugar, pulse table sugar in a food processor or make simple syrup. Toss the cut lime pieces into the cup and muddle until the sugar is dissolved. Add 1 oz of light rum to the muddle and give it a few more swishes before pouring into the chilled highball glass. Top with 3 oz of club soda. Garnish with lime. Drink and receive the magick.

Legend & Origins

According to legend, enslaved workers on sugarcane plantations created this drink to elicit kinder behavior from their masters. The magickal origins may be contested, but the power of peppermint combined with rum creates a potent working.

Magickal Uses

  • Clarity – Clear the mind and see through deception
  • Healing – Physical and emotional recovery
  • Prosperity – Draw abundance and success
  • Banishing – Remove unwanted influences

Sober Substitution

Hold the mint as a bunch by the end and slap it on the table three times. This will release the flavor of the mint without turning your drink green. Place the mint into the glass. Add the superfine sugar to the glass and squeeze the lime wedges into the glass. Feel free to add some lime zest as well. Give the mix a good stir while sending the blue light into your drink, and top with club soda while imagining the intended outcome.
