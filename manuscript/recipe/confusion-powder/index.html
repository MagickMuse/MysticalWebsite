🧿Confusion Powder

◇Type
Powder
↗Correspondences3
😵‍💫Confusion🛡️Defense🛡️Protection
↗Rituals1
🔓Adverse Testimony Silencing Spell

A defensive powder to confuse and disorient enemies, creating mental fog and poor decision-making.

Quantities

  • Base Recipe:
  • Poppy seeds
  • Twitch grass (couch grass)
  • Black mustard seeds
  • Talcum powder or other powder base
  • Red food coloring (optional, for traditional red color)
    Optional Enhancement (for arguments and infighting):
  • Black pepper
  • Red pepper (cayenne)

Directions

Grind poppy seeds, twitch grass, and black mustard seeds into a fine powder. Mix thoroughly with talcum powder or another powder base. If desired, add red food coloring to tint the powder red, the traditional color for confusion work. For enhanced effect causing arguments and infighting among enemies, add ground black pepper and red pepper to the mixture. Spread the powder around the perimeter of the target's home, business, or property, ideally during the new moon; dust doormats or thresholds where the target will step; or dust lightly along paths the target regularly walks. While spreading, you may pray to deities of witchcraft and crossroads such as Hekate for assistance. Always perform divination before using confusion work, as confusion can lead to accidents or unintended serious harm; be prepared to accept responsibility for all consequences. This same recipe can be adapted as a Confusion Oil by infusing the herbs in carrier oil, or as Confusion Incense by burning the herbs on charcoal. Confusion spells are unpredictable and can cause dangerous situations; use only when other methods have failed and only after thorough divination confirms acceptable outcomes.

About This Powder

Creates mental fog, poor decision-making, and general disarray for the target. Can cause arguments and infighting when peppers are added. Particularly effective when spread around a target's property or business.

⚠️
Warning: Confusion spells are unpredictable and can cause vehicular accidents or other dangerous situations. Use only when other methods have failed and only after thorough divination confirms acceptable outcomes.

Alternative Forms

This same recipe can be used to make:

  • Confusion Oil (herbs infused in carrier oil)
  • Confusion Incense (herbs burned on charcoal)

The clever practitioner will employ all three forms together for maximum effect.
