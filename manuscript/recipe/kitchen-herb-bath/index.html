🛁Kitchen Herb Bath

◇Type
Bath Blend
↗Correspondences2
✨Cleansing🛡️Protection
↗Materials2
Bath💧Water
↗Plants3
ChervilDillFennel

Magickally powerful plants often masquerade as ordinary kitchen herbs. Fennel, dill, and chervil are all potent cleansing and protective botanicals hiding in plain sight on the spice rack. This bath draws out their magickal properties through a simple herbal infusion.

Quantities

  • Fennel, dill, chervil: a generous handful of each
  • Boiling water, enough for a strong infusion

Directions

Pour boiling water over fennel, dill, and chervil. Allow to steep until cooled and concentrated. Strain the liquid. Add the infusion to a drawn bath and bathe, then air-dry so the body radiates spiritual protection.

About This Recipe

Frankincense, benzoin, and dragon's blood are potent and exotic, but protective power rises from the mundane as well. Air-drying after this bath lets the body radiate spiritual protection.
