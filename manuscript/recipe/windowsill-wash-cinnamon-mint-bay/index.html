🪟Windowsill Wash: Cinnamon, Mint & Bay

◇Directions
If using fresh herbs: soak cinnamon, mint, and bay leaves in water. If using dried herbs: boil them in water for a few minutes. Allow to cool. Strain and combine with a pinch of baking soda. Use a cloth to wash windowsills thoroughly. Work from the top of the sill to the bottom. Repeat weekly or as needed, especially if facing a busy street.
◇Type
Wash
◇Quantities
• Cinnamon (stick or ground) • Fresh or dried mint • Bay leaves (fresh or dried) • Water • Pinch of baking soda
◇Description
A money-drawing, success-attracting windowsill wash developed by Asc Helvetius. Cinnamon, mint, and bay leaves cleanse the windowsill of accumulated ash and dust while drawing prosperity. The volatile oils in these herbs also repel insects.

About This Recipe

Developed by Asc Helvetius, this windowsill wash is especially practical for homes facing busy streets, where windowsills accumulate ash and dust within days. It combines physical cleaning with spiritual work by incorporating herbs associated with money-drawing and success.

The volatile oils in all three herbs also function as natural insect repellents.

Why Windowsills?

Windowsills are liminal architectural thresholds, the boundary between interior and exterior space. Like doors, they benefit from regular spiritual attention. Washing them regularly prevents the buildup of both material grime and miasmic spiritual residue.

Ingredients

  • Cinnamon (stick or ground), money-drawing, success, purification
  • Mint, prosperity, cleansing, insect repellent
  • Bay leaves, success, victory, wish-fulfillment
  • Water
  • Pinch of baking soda (neutralizes and brightens)

Method

If using fresh herbs: Soak in cold or room-temperature water for several hours.

If using dried herbs: Bring water to a boil, add herbs, simmer for a few minutes. Allow to cool completely.

Strain the liquid. Add a pinch of baking soda and stir. Apply to windowsills with a cloth, wiping thoroughly. Allow to air dry.

Notes

  • Can be used on door frames and thresholds as well
  • Frequency: weekly, or whenever you notice buildup
  • The herbs are all associated with money-drawing, consider setting an intention for abundance as you wash
