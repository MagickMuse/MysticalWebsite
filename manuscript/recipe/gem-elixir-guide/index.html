A reference guide for preparing gem elixirs and essences, including instructions for the mother elixir and dosage bottles, along with a full correspondence list of stones and their elixir properties.

Directions

  1. Preparing the Mother Elixir:
  2. Place a cleansed stone into a glass bowl filled with spring or filtered water. If the stone is friable or its dust is considered toxic, first place it inside a small glass jar, then set that jar into the bowl of water.
  3. Set the bowl in direct sunlight for twelve hours.
  4. Remove the stone and pour the elixir into a clean glass bottle with an airtight lid.
  5. To preserve for longer than one week, add 50% brandy or vodka.
  6. Store in a cool, dark place. Label clearly.
    Preparing a Dosage Bottle:
  7. Add 7 drops of the mother elixir to a glass dropper bottle.
  8. Fill with ⅓ brandy and ⅔ water for oral or topical use. If using near the eyes, omit the alcohol entirely.
  9. Take 7 drops, three times daily.
