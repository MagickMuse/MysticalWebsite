🍖Shrub Glaze for Meats and Veggies

Type
Food
Correspondences1
❤️‍🩹Healing

A versatile glaze made from shrub (drinking vinegar), stock, and aromatics. The fruit in the shrub provides sugar while the vinegar adds flavor and breaks down cell walls for easier digestion. Infinite combinations possible: watermelon Prosecco vinegar shrub for fish, cucumber cider vinegar shrub for light dishes, or plum balsamic shrub for grilled mushrooms.

Quantities

  • 1/2 cup shrub of choice
  • 1/3 cup stock or 1 stock concentrate packet
  • Additional aromatics for flavor: onion, shallot, scallions, rosemary, garlic, ginger, etc.
  • Oil for sautéing
  • Optional: honey or jam as thickener

Directions

Sauté chopped aromatics in oil to release flavor. Add shrub and stock to the hot pan. Reduce heat and stir until thickened. Consider adding honey or jam as a thickener. The glaze is ready when it sticks to the back of the spoon. Pour over desired foods.

Bonus: Try sautéed fruits and glaze made with tea or water for a zingy dessert. Peaches with ginger glaze and lavender garnish is a particularly lovely treat.
