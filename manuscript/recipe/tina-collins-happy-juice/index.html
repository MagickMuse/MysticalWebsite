🍹Tina Collins (Happy Juice)

Type
Drink
Correspondences2
😊Happiness🛡️Protection
Materials1
🫐Sloe Gin
Plants2
BlackthornLemon

A bright, effervescent cocktail for invoking happiness and protection. The perfect pick-me-up when you need a boost of joy.

Quantities

  • 1½ oz sloe gin (happiness, protection)
  • ¾ oz oleosaccharum (abundance)
  • ¾ oz lemon juice (purification)
  • 2 dashes orange bitters (happiness)
  • Sparkling water to top

Directions

Add the sloe gin, oleosaccharum, and lemon juice to a cocktail shaker with ice. Shake vigorously.

Strain into a Collins glass filled with ice.

Add the orange bitters and top with sparkling water.

Garnish with a lemon wheel and enjoy your happiness!
