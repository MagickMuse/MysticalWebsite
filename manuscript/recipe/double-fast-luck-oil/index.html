🟢Double Fast Luck Oil

◇Type
Oil
↗Correspondences4

↗Plants7

Red Fast Luck Oil enhanced with a green botanical water infusion to combine red (luck/life) and green (growth/prosperity) into one double-strength formula. Designed to make every day feel like Christmas Eve in terms of sales and fortune. The oil and water separate at rest; shake before use.

Quantities

  • Red Fast Luck Oil (prepared)
  • Green botanical infusion: basil, parsley, or mint (or green food coloring)

Directions

Create a green water infusion using cash-drawing botanicals such as basil, parsley, or mint. Use green food coloring for a brighter green. Gently pour the infusion over Red Fast Luck Oil. At rest, the oil and water form two distinct color layers. Shake to disperse when ready to use. Use exactly like Red Fast Luck Oil.
