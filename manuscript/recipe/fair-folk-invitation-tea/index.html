🧚Fair Folk Invitation Tea

Type
Drink
Correspondences3
🧚Fairy😊Joy💕Love
Plants7
DandelionElderGerman ChamomileHopsMulleinRaspberryRosehip

A magickal tea blend brewed with an incantation to invite the Fair Folk. Each herb is added with spoken words of power.

Quantities

  • 3 tsp black tea
  • 1 tsp Chamomile
  • 2 tsp Dandelion root
  • 1 tsp Elder flower
  • 1 tsp Hops
  • 2 tsp Mullein
  • 2 tsp Raspberry leaf
  • 2 tsp Rose hips

Directions

As each herb is added to the pot, speak its line of the incantation aloud. See page content for the full incantation.

Incantation

Speak each line as the corresponding herb is added to the pot:

Black tea: "Black for strength;"

Chamomile: "Then apple of night;"

Dandelion root: "Wild grown root;"

Elder flower: "And Lady's blessing."

Hops: "Hops for joy;"

Mullein: "Then between the worlds;"

Raspberry leaf: "Tangle of bramble and fairy knots,"

Rose hips: "Kissed with love, brewed to invite the Fair Folk to Tea."
