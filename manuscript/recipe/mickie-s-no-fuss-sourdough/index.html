🍞Mickie's No-Fuss Sourdough

◇Directions
Equipment: - Kitchen scale - 16-cup container for mixing (clear straight sides help gauge doubling) - Cast-iron Dutch oven with lid - Sharp blade for scoring (razor blade ideal) - Clean cotton tea towel - Banneton or 2-quart bowl for proofing Instructions: 1. In the mixing container, add sourdough starter to water and whisk. Add both bread flour and whole wheat flour and top with salt. Mix until a sticky, rough dough forms. 2. Mark the container to note the volume and set aside covered for 25 minutes. 3. With clean hands, do a stretch and fold by lifting the dough from one edge and folding it over the top. Turn the bowl a quarter turn and repeat 4 to 6 times. Cover and let rest for 25 minutes. 4. Do a total of 3 to 4 rounds of stretch and folds until the dough is less sticky and becomes elastic. 5. Leave on the counter for 2 to 3 hours, then refrigerate for 12 to 72 hours. If it hasn't doubled, bring to room temperature for a couple of hours until puffed up. 6. Turn dough out onto a clean, floured surface and form into a rough ball. Let rest under a tea towel for 10 minutes. 7. Use the towel to line a round bowl and coat with bread flour or rice flour. Shape the loaf by pulling the ball toward you, creating a tight surface with a rough seam at the bottom. 8. Place loaf seam-side up in the prepared bowl, cover with plastic wrap or a shower cap, and rest at room temperature for 2 to 3 hours until very puffed. 9. Preheat Dutch oven in oven at 450°F for at least 30 minutes while dough rests in the fridge (this makes scoring easier). 10. Flip dough onto parchment paper and score about ½ inch deep with a sharp blade. An X works well for beginners. 11. Transfer dough on parchment to the hot Dutch oven. Bake 25 minutes with lid on. 12. Remove lid and bake about 20 minutes more until golden brown. 13. Allow bread to cool completely for ease of cutting and best texture (at least 30 minutes).
◇Type
Food
◇Quantities
- 30g active starter (puffed up to double after feeding) - 355g water (about 85°F, non-chlorinated) - 400g unbleached white bread flour - 100g unbleached whole wheat flour - 10g sea salt - Rice flour for dusting (optional)
◇Description
A flexible sourdough bread recipe designed for home bakers. The dough can be refrigerated for 12 to 72 hours, allowing the bake to fit into any schedule.
