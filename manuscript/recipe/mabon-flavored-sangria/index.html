🍂Mabon Flavored Sangria

◇Type
Drink
↗Correspondences6
⚖️Balance😊Happiness🌟Hope💕Love💰Prosperity🛡️Protection
↗Plants1
Apple

A festive non-alcoholic harvest punch celebrating Mabon, infused with apples, pear, orange, and pomegranate.

Quantities

  • ½ gallon apple cider
  • 2-liter bottle of lemon seltzer water
  • 1 Jonagold, Gala, or Fuji apple (physical action, clear mental blocks)
  • 1 Granny Smith apple (strength, protection, consecration)
  • 1 pear (comfort, love, unions)
  • 1 orange (prosperity, strength, happiness)
  • 1 pomegranate (work/life balance, blessings, hope)

Directions

Wash the fruit thoroughly; grocery store apples are often waxed to preserve them.

Roughly chop apples and pear, and slice the orange.

To peel the pomegranate: on the top side of the fruit (the part with the flower petal top), carve a pentagon and lift off the lid of the rind. Along the white seams between the fruit, carefully slice down towards the bottom. Fan out the segments of the fruit and easily remove them.

Place all fruit into a large pitcher or punch bowl.

Pour seltzer water into the container, and top with apple cider. Since the seltzer is lighter than the cider, it will mix better this way.

Chill and enjoy!

Timing

Best prepared and served at Mabon (Autumn Equinox) or during harvest celebrations.

Source

Blackthorn's Botanical Brews by Amy Blackthorn
