Strength

A natural, caffeine-free and sugar-free energy booster using rutilated quartz, spring water, and citrus/mint essential oils charged with the Strength card. Perfect for overcoming afternoon fatigue.

Quantities

  • A clear glass jar with a lid
  • A small piece of rutilated quartz
  • Spring water
  • A few drops of essential oil of peppermint or spearmint
  • A few drops of essential oil of lemon
  • A few drops of essential oil of sweet orange
  • The Strength card

Directions

  1. Cleanse the jar and quartz crystal with mild soap and water, then dry.
  2. Fill the jar with spring water. Add the essential oils, then the crystal. Cap and shake three times to charge.
  3. Place the Strength card face-up on your altar or table. Set the jar on the card for at least five minutes, allowing the water to absorb the card's energy.
  4. Remove the crystal using fingers, spoon, or tongs. Sip this supercharged elixir whenever you need an energy boost.
