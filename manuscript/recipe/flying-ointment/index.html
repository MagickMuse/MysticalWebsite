Caution

This formula is extremely dangerous and potentially fatal. It contains multiple deadly poisons and is provided solely for historical reference. Do not attempt to make or use this ointment under any circumstances.

Historical Context

Flying ointments were reportedly used by practitioners in medieval and early modern Europe to induce trance states. The toxic alkaloids in these plants cause hallucinations, delirium, and sensations of flight, but also cardiac arrest, respiratory failure, and death.

Ingredients

  • Cinquefoil
  • Parsley
  • Aconite (Monkshood)
  • Belladonna (Deadly Nightshade)
  • Hemlock
  • Cowbane (Water Hemlock)
