💜Lavender Syrup

◇Directions
Bring water to a boil, turn off heat, and stir in the sugar. Continue to stir until clear. Add lavender flowers and allow it to cool to room temperature. Strain and bottle. Keep in the refrigerator for up to 1 month. Lavender London Fog: Add to Earl Grey tea with milk for a lavender London Fog tea latte. Bergamot is the energy of success, and lavender is calming, so it makes a perfect drink to take with you before a job interview!
◇Type
Syrup
◇Quantities
- 2 cups sugar - 2 cups water - 2 tsp dried lavender buds
◇Description
Calming lavender syrup perfect for London Fog tea lattes and pre-interview drinks - bringing success with a centered calm.
