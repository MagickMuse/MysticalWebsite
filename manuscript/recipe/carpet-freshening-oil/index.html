🏠Carpet Freshening Oil

Natural carpet freshening powder with peppermint, eucalyptus, lemongrass, and pine essential oils in baking soda.

Quantities

30 drops of Eucalyptus Essential Oil
30 drops of Cinnamon Bark Essential Oil
30 drops of Lemongrass Essential Oil
10 drops of Clove Essential Oil
1/2 cup of baking soda

Directions

Combine oils with baking soda in a jar. Let sit overnight or 1-2 days. Sprinkle over rug. Let sit for 15 minutes, then vacuum.
