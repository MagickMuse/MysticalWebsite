🔩Iron Nail Protection Salt Scrub

Type
Bath Blend
Correspondences1
🛡️Protection
Materials3
🔩Iron NailOlive Oil🧂Sea Salt
Plants6
CastorFrankincenseJojobaOliveSandalwoodVetivert

An extra-protective salt scrub of sea salt in castor, olive, and jojoba oils with frankincense, sandalwood, and vetiver, guarded by an iron nail set in its center.

Quantities

Sea salt
Castor oil
Olive oil
Jojoba oil
Frankincense essential oil
Sandalwood essential oil
Vetiver essential oil
1 iron nail

Directions

Make a salt scrub from sea salt blended with castor, olive, and jojoba oils, adjusting the proportions until the texture pleases you. Add essential oils of frankincense, sandalwood, and vetiver. Pack the scrub into its container and insert an iron nail into the center.

Notes

Apply following the method in Salt Scrub.
