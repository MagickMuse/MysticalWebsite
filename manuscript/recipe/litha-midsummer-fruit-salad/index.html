🍓Litha Midsummer Fruit Salad

Type
Food
Materials1
🍯Honey
Plants4
AppleBasilLemonMint

A festive midsummer fruit salad made with melon balls, grapes, pink lady apple, lemon, and a generous shredding of fresh basil and mint. Drizzled with honey or maple syrup and optionally topped with crumbled feta. A solar celebration feast worthy of the faerie folk.

The height of summer calls for something alive with color and herbs fresh from the garden. This salad brings the abundance of Litha to the table through solar fruits and the aromatic herbs of high summer.

Quantities

  • Fresh basil leaves, shredded
  • Fresh mint leaves, shredded
  • Assorted melon balls: watermelon, honeydew, cantaloupe
  • Sliced grapes
  • Minced Pink Lady apple
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Honey or maple syrup, to taste (optional)
  • Crumbled feta cheese (optional)

Directions

  1. Finely shred the basil and mint leaves and combine them thoroughly. Set aside.
  2. Assemble the fruit: melon balls of three varieties, sliced grapes, and minced apple. Spritz the fruit with fresh lemon juice and toss together with the herb mixture.
  3. If desired, drizzle with honey or maple syrup and finish with a light crumble of feta. Share with midsummer companions and enjoy.

Magickal Notes

Basil and mint are both herbs of vitality and prosperity, aligning well with the solar peak of the year. The combination of sweet melon and citrus echoes the abundance themes of Litha. Honey amplifies the solar and faerie associations of the feast. This is a dish well suited to outdoor ritual, picnic altars, and feasting with community.
