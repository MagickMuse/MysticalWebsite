Card Grimoire

Travel the World Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether this dream is right to fulfill now, or whether you should wait Card 2: Whether you'd be content with an exotic trip each year instead Card 3: Whether you have, or could raise, the resources to go sooner rather than later Card 4: How you can support yourself on the road if funds get short Card 5: Whether you need to leave anyone behind, or whether they'll come with you, meet you, or wait for you Card 6: Whether you should keep a blog, photo record, or documentary of your travels Card 7: Whether you need a definite itinerary, or should go wherever your fancy takes you Card 8: Whether you'll regret it if you don't go soon
◇Description
For the restless urge to break free and travel the world, weighing timing against waiting, whether a yearly exotic trip would satisfy you instead, raising the resources to go, and whether to bring someone with you. Best cast at a full moon.
