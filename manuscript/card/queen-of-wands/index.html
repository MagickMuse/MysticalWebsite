Upright GeneralUpright MoneyUpright EmploymentUpright LoveUpright Health
A fortunate card, the upright Queen gives you a "thumbs up." If this card signifies a real person, she's a figure who commands respect, whether she's in the boardroom or the home. A wise adviser, she is also a loyal friend, a loving partner, a trustworthy authority. She has a nurturing, colorful, and generous nature; she's confident in her abilities and encourages others to do their best. Like the King, she may play a prominent role in a business or her community.In a question about money, the Queen says, "Do what you love; the money will follow." You may receive money from a creative venture or prosper through a partnership or other alliance.If you asked about a job matter, the upright Queen shows a period of fulfillment, when a project bears fruit and you're happy with the outcome. This is also a good time to birth or expand a venture.In a reading about a relationship, she shows a passionate romance between loyal, loving partners.
Confidence, warmth, and inner strength define this queen, who stands her ground without needing to prove her power. Believing in one's own abilities, living authentically, and staying aligned with the vibrations one wants to manifest draw opportunities in with ease.Following passion tends to bring money along with it; profit through a creative venture or partnership is likely.A period of fulfillment is at hand, with a project bearing fruit and an opportunity to expand a venture.Passionate, loyal romance defines this energy, along with confidence in expressing desire and enjoying shared passions with others.Vitality and strength are strong here, sometimes showing up as a fertile window or a period of healing around sexuality and sexual health.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When the Queen of Wands is reversed, she can mean a situation won't come to fruition as fast as you'd hoped or your satisfaction will be limited. Your enthusiasm may flag or you just don't feel inspired. If she's a real person, she's a powerful, emotional woman who demands respect and absolute loyalty. She can be jealous and manipulative, both in love and in business, don't cross her. This is the "drama queen" card.In a reading about money, the reversed Queen warns you to be careful with investments or business spending, particularly in joint ventures.If you've asked about a job-related issue, she may indicate deception or power struggles. Watch your back; a coworker may not be trustworthy.In a relationship reading, she may suggest emotional upsets, infidelity, or a cooling of affection.
A driven nature has tipped into selfishness, self-importance, and a taste for power games; fake friendships, popularity contests, and group manipulation can surround this energy. Alternatively, this reversal can mean lost confidence and self-doubt after a mistake, calling for patience in rebuilding faith in oneself.Investments and joint financial ventures call for extra caution right now.Deception or power struggles may be at work; a coworker's trustworthiness is worth questioning.Emotional upsets, infidelity, or cooling affection may be part of the picture, along with a tendency to create or feed off drama.Pushing the body past safe limits through an intense new routine or diet risks injury or a long recovery.

Imagery and Symbolism

The Queen of Wands is often shown sitting on a throne decorated with lions and sunflowers, symbols of fire and strength. The sunflower itself is a solar symbol of life, joy, and satisfaction. A black cat at the queen's feet represents her power; depending on the situation, she can be gentle and graceful like a cat or ferocious like a lion. This queen sits with confidence, aware of the influence she wields.

Affirmation

I allow all the right things for myself to come to me with ease.

Astrology

This queen represents the cardinal fire sign of Aries, embodying its energy through confidence, assertiveness, and boldness. She radiates a warmth that attracts people, knowing charisma is a tool for getting what she wants. Aries brings its unwavering, fearless nature to this queen and is ruled by Mars, a motivating force and a sign of strength and action.
