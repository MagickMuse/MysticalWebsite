Twin Soul Recognition Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsSpiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether it feels as though you have known each other forever Card 2: Whether there was instant recognition or connection at the first meeting Card 3: Whether they fit naturally with your family, friends, and interests Card 4: Whether the relationship feels fast-moving yet natural Card 5: Whether a missing part of your life now feels complete Card 6: Whether you share constant deja vu or telepathic links
◇Description
Drawn from the full deck on the same day of the week and at the same time you met or reconnected, when deep, sudden love feels right, these six cards examine the sense of having known each other forever, instant recognition at first meeting, how naturally the connection fits with family and friends, the pace of the relationship, a feeling of completion, and shared deja vu or telepathic links.
