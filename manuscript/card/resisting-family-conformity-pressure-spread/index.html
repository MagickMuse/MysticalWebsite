Resisting Family Conformity Pressure Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision MakingShadow Work
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you've always felt different from the rest of your family Card 2: The main difference or problem that makes it hard to conform Card 3: Whether anyone in your family or community would speak up for you Card 4: Whether you can live within the family and keep your own beliefs Card 5: Where there's room for compromise Card 6: Where there's no room for compromise Card 7: Whether you need to temporarily or permanently move away from the family Card 8 (optional): How this can be resolved
◇Description
For holding beliefs, a lifestyle, or principles very different from your family's and feeling pressured to conform to something you know is wrong for you, weighing whether you've always felt different, where compromise is possible, and whether you need distance to stay true to yourself. Best cast on a significant family day.
