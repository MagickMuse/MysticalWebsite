New Family Member First Impression Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & Relationships
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you'll like this person instantly and the liking will grow Card 2 (if needed): How and when an uncertain first impression can improve
◇Description
For discovering whether you'll warm to a prospective new family member before meeting them, with an optional second card if the first suggests doubts, to ask how and when things can improve. Best cast the morning of the meeting.
