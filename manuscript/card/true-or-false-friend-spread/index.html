Card Grimoire

True or False Friend Spread

Spread Type
Love & Relationships
Question Style
Specific situation
Card Details
Card 1: What raises doubt about their loyalty Card 2: Whether the doubt stems from a fear of being unlovable Card 3: Whether they cause discomfort or exclusion around others Card 4: Whether they have betrayed a confidence Card 5: Whether you give more than you receive in the relationship Card 6: Whether they would help in a real crisis
Description
Drawn from the whole deck on a Wednesday, the day for uncovering deception, these six cards examine why someone's loyalty is in question, whether self-doubt is fueling the suspicion, whether that person causes discomfort or exclusion around others, any past betrayal of confidence, an imbalance of giving versus receiving, and whether they would actually help in a crisis.
