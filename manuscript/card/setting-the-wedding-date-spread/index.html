Setting the Wedding Date Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether this is a sticking point in the relationship Card 2: The real cause of your partner's reluctance to set a date Card 3: Whether finances or fear of the ceremony are causing problems Card 4: Whether your partner has unresolved past commitment issues Card 5: Whether marrying would affect the relationship for better or worse Card 6: Whether you are right for each other Card 7: Whether you can accept the relationship as it is
◇Description
Drawn from the full deck on a Thursday, the day of commitment, when time is ticking by after an engagement with no date set, these seven cards examine whether the delay is a sticking point, its real cause, whether money or ceremony fears are involved, unresolved past commitment issues, how marriage might change the relationship, overall compatibility, and whether you can accept things as they are.
