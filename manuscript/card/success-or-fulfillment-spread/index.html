Success or Fulfillment Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & MoneyDecision Making
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: The price you must pay for success Card 2: What you must give up or leave behind at home or socially if you go for the top Card 3: Whether you're prepared to be single-minded, or would prefer a good quality of life outside work Card 4: The long-term advantages of wholeheartedly embracing the opportunity Card 5: The long-term advantages of refusing the offer Card 6: Whether there's a middle way to have both Card 7: Your ultimate dream
◇Description
For weighing a career opportunity that promises success against the personal happiness it would cost, examining the price of saying yes, what's lost at home or socially, and whether a middle path exists between ambition and quality of life. Best cast on a Thursday, day for contemplating major upward steps.
