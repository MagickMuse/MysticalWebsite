Numerology

Number: 1. Keyword: power.

Aces are emphatic and powerful cards that strongly express their suit's elemental influence. They carry a more general meaning than other pip cards due to their forceful nature. An Ace's message may be further defined by surrounding cards, but its meaning is never overwhelmed or negated. Aces are almost always a positive omen, carrying creative and independent energy.

In Readings

The Ace of Spades is a card of conquest. The querent may face obstacles, even severe ones, but will ultimately succeed. This card predicts triumph over challenges. It may seem like a negative card because of the obstacles themselves, but the emphasis is on triumph. The obstacles would exist regardless; what the card is saying is that the querent will overcome them.

The conquest does not always lead to a joyful conclusion. Sometimes the triumph carries a sorrowful, bittersweet quality. For someone with an incurable illness, this card could even indicate death as a triumph over suffering. At other times, the Ace of Spades predicts a victory worthy of celebration. Whether joyful or solemn, the triumph will be powerful and unmistakable, even if the reader cannot yet see how it will manifest.

The Ace of Spades is almost always a positive card in its own way.
