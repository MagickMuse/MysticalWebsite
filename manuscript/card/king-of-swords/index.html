The upright King represents intellectual pursuits, communication, spiritual knowledge, and study. You may be mastering a mental task or using your communication skills successfully. Sometimes this card indicates a decision, judgment, or legal matter. If the King signifies an actual person, he may seem stern, analytical, or aloof, perhaps he's a lawyer or judge, a professor, military leader, scientist, or religious leader.In a reading about money, the King upright recommends taking charge of a financial matter. Do your homework, don't let your emotions influence a decision involving money or investments. This card may also mean profiting from an intellectual endeavor.If the reading is about your job, it may indicate you work in a field that requires keen intelligence, good communication, or analytical ability. The King advises you to show leadership regarding decisions or judgments, clarity and objectivity are important.In a reading about love, the King symbolizes a relationship based on shared interests and ideas rather than passion. You and/or your partner resist expressing deep emotions, preferring a friendly partnership. Perhaps you fear losing independence if you open your heart.
Keen intellect and commanding presence define this king, along with grace under pressure and a love of well-devised plans and thoughtful questions. Logic rules over emotion when facing problems, and multiple solutions are worth proposing after considering how each choice affects everyone involved.Taking charge of a financial matter and doing the homework first serves well here, keeping emotion out of decisions about money or investments; profit through an intellectual endeavor is also possible.Work that calls for sharp intelligence, strong communication, or analytical skill fits this energy, with leadership on decisions and judgments bringing clarity and objectivity to the table.A relationship built on shared interests and ideas rather than passion may be indicated, with emotional expression held back in favor of a friendly partnership; fear of losing independence may be part of the picture.Bringing in specialists rather than relying only on generalists leads to clearer answers, and gathering enough information to build a solid plan supports better outcomes.

When the King appears reversed, he can indicate mean-spiritedness, biting wit, arrogance, judgmental attitudes, or narrow-mindedness. If he represents an actual person, he may be someone who lacks compassion and can even behave cruelly, perhaps a harsh religious leader or an unscrupulous lawyer. Or, he may be fickle, indecisive, absent-minded, or gossipy. This is the "watch your back card."In a reading about money, the reversed King warns that losses may result due to unprincipled people, a business that's not on the up and up, or a legal matter. Poor judgment can cause problems too. Clarity and honesty are needed now, pay attention to details.If the reading is about your job, you may feel your boss is too harsh, your work too demanding, your colleagues too mean. Perhaps you think you've been treated unfairly. In some cases, this card may represent an organization or situation that's corrupt or at least shady.In a reading about love, the reversed King shows coldness, insensitivity, or dishonesty. You or your partner may be too critical, judgmental, or hurtful toward one another. Narrow-mindedness or arrogance may lead to nasty arguments.
Mean-spiritedness, biting wit, arrogance, and narrow-mindedness can surface here, sometimes alongside fickleness or gossip. Caution is warranted with someone who lacks compassion or behaves cruelly through their words.Losses may follow dealings with unprincipled people or a legal matter gone wrong; clarity, honesty, and attention to detail help avoid further poor judgment.A workplace that feels too harsh, demanding, or even corrupt may be at play, with unfair treatment adding to the tension.Coldness, insensitivity, or dishonesty shows up here, along with a critical or judgmental edge that can spark unnecessary arguments.Dismissing professional advice in favor of personal certainty can cause real friction; openness to the expertise and views of others helps avoid unnecessary conflict over care.

Imagery and Symbolism

Seated upon a carved stone throne, this king holds an upward-pointing blade. Crescent moons and butterflies carved into the high-backed throne symbolize elevated ideas and the ability to process a great deal of information at once. An angel appears in the throne's carving and on the crown, set at a higher level than the cherub found on the queen's throne. Birds and clouds in the background represent the air element. This king sits facing forward, ready to meet any challenge.

Affirmation

My mind is my most powerful asset.

Astrology

This king represents the fixed air sign of Aquarius, embodying intellectual authority, clarity, and visionary leadership. Aquarius is ruled by Uranus and Saturn, bringing a blend of innovation, humanitarianism, and disciplined thought to this card. This king sees the big picture and understands the rules well enough to create the change he seeks, making him unwavering in his commitments and principles.
