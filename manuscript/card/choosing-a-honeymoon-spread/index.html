Card Grimoire

Choosing a Honeymoon Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
Question Style
Specific situation
Card Details
Card 1: Whether you want a honeymoon or would rather save the money Card 2: Simple or exotic, at home or overseas Card 3: Whether an exotic honeymoon should take place at the wedding setting itself Card 4: Whether you want an action-adventure or romantic honeymoon Card 5: Whether to go right after the wedding or wait a while Card 6: How to compromise so you are both happy
Description
Drawn from the full deck at the beginning of a week or month, these six cards weigh having a honeymoon against saving the money, simple versus exotic options, whether to stay near the wedding setting, the style of trip wanted, timing, and how to compromise so both partners are happy.
