Help Me Grow Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Where I am now Card 2: Catalyst for change Card 3: The wall Card 4: Support system Card 5: Change manifested
◇Description
This five-card spread supports reflection on a current stretch of personal growth. After shuffling and cutting the deck, draw five cards and lay them left to right. The first card reflects where personal growth and development currently stand, the second reveals the catalyst behind the need for change, and the third names what stands in the way of making the necessary changes. The fourth card points to the support system available, whether resources, people, or skills, and the fifth shows what is most likely to happen if the needed changes are embraced.
