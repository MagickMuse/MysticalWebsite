Numerology

Number: 6. Keyword: depletion.

In standard numerology, six embodies balance, dependability, perfection, and satisfaction. In cartomancy, however, the Sixes are an exception to that pattern. They almost always carry a negative meaning, indicating a weakening or interruption of the suit's elemental influence. Sixes represent a loss of some kind.

The setback indicated by a Six is temporary rather than irreversible. The condition is already in progress and cannot be entirely avoided, but the loss will eventually correct itself or be forgotten with time.

In Readings

When the warmth and comfort of affection disappears, what remains is not just absence but a disturbed and disrupted emotional state. The Six of Hearts predicts emotional turbulence: anger, grief, arguments.

The conflict behind this card is not something that will appear out of nowhere. The conditions driving it are already in motion and may have existed for some time. A hidden indiscretion may soon come to light, or a growing resentment between the querent and a friend or family member may erupt into open confrontation. Whatever the trigger, it is already happening, so the best the querent can do is prepare for the impact.

This emotional disturbance will not last forever, but it could carry permanent consequences. When this card appears, it is wise to avoid making decisions or saying things that might be regretted later.
