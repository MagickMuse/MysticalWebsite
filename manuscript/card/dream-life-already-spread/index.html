Dream Life Already Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether there's anything you want to change about your present life Card 2: The hidden reasons others push you toward greater achievement Card 3: The benefits of your life as it is Card 4: Whether you need to convince others of the rightness of your life, or just ignore them Card 5: Your greatest source of happiness now and in the future
◇Description
For when you're genuinely content with your job, home, and relationships, but others keep insisting you're capable of greater achievement, weighing whether you actually want to change anything and what truly makes you happy. Best cast when an unwanted opportunity arises that you don't need to take.
