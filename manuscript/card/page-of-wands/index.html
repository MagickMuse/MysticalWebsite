The Page of Wands represents the early stages of a situation or undertaking. It shows an optimistic attitude, enthusiasm, and an eagerness to succeed. If the card represents a person in your life, it could be a younger relative or friend, an apprentice, student, or assistant. This is the "student card." Sometimes it indicates a person with a childlike (or childish) nature, regardless of age.In a reading about money, this Page shows a new investment has the potential to pay off. The card can also recommend taking a creative approach to making money.In a reading about work, you're probably starting a new job you enjoy, learning a new skill, or preparing yourself for a career of a creative nature.If you asked about love, this card indicates an innocent and playful approach to a relationship. It shows openness, honesty, and an optimistic attitude toward love.
This page carries an optimistic attitude, enthusiasm, and an eagerness to succeed while looking toward all that could be. Staying open to what's ahead, engaging in genuine conversations, and creatively working through the current situation matter here.A new investment shows real potential to pay off, and approaching money-making with a creative eye is worth exploring.A new job worth enjoying, a fresh skill worth learning, or a step toward creative work is on the horizon.An innocent, playful approach to a relationship shines through here, along with openness, honesty, and optimism toward love.Small daily changes, drinking more water, sleeping a little earlier, and eating more green foods, add up to real improvement even when each step feels simple.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
Reversed, the Page of Wands represents a delay, mix-up, or disappointment. Sometimes it says the time isn't right to start something or that you're on the wrong track. If the card signifies a real person, it may mean he or she can't be trusted or isn't up to the task.In a reading about money, the reversed Page advises caution. Seek advice from someone more knowledgeable and experienced. Wait a while and get more info before laying down your hard-earned cash.If you asked about work, this card suggests you may be naive about a situation related to your job. Or, it may show you need to learn more, develop your skills, or put in more effort to get ahead.In a reading about love, this Page can show childish behavior, hang-ups from childhood that interfere with the relationship, unrealistic expectations, or lack of commitment.
A tantrum or negative reaction rarely brings the desired result. Focusing only on the negative, avoiding accountability, or refusing to listen to reason are all signs of this energy; distance is the best option when someone else can't be reasoned with right now. Leaning on others well past the point of necessity, out of laziness rather than genuine need, is also part of this pattern.Spending beyond one's means and racking up debt that can't be repaid shows up here.Immature behavior at work deserves distance and a professional response rather than getting pulled down to that level.Childish behavior, keeping score, and rehashing the past will implode a relationship; growing up and taking responsibility for one's energy and actions is needed.Withdrawal from something hard to quit can bring out the worst in even the calmest person; treating the body with respect rather than as fair grounds for abuse matters most here.

Imagery and Symbolism

A well-dressed youth stands in the desert, holding up a staff with leaves. Their clothing is covered with salamanders, a mythical creature associated with transformative fire. They gaze up at the wand in awe, a symbol of opportunity waiting to be used along their journey. Pyramids in the background connect this card to the spiritual knowledge of ancient Egypt.

Affirmation

I greet each day with enthusiasm.

Astrology

There is no single astrological connection to this card. The Page of Wands connects to messages of fire, adventure, and energy, linking it to the fire signs of Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius. This connection may manifest in a reading as a person, timing, or influence.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • How can the energy of being a free spirit be embraced today?
  • What feels exciting right now?

Reversed

  • When has losing one's temper with someone felt tempting in the past? What would have been said and done, and why didn't it happen?
  • What are some warning signs of becoming unreasonable in a situation?
