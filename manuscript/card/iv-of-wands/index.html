Everything is working out well when this card comes up in a reading. It indicates pleasure and prosperity, financial stability, and a happy home life. This is the "comfort card." You've shown the world who you are and what you can achieve, and you've gained the recognition and rewards you deserve.In a reading about money, the Four upright suggests prosperity and financial security. You may reap benefits from investments or make money from the sale of property.In a reading about work, this card shows success, harmony, and stability in your business or career. You derive satisfaction from a job well done. The Four also represents happiness and fulfillment in a creative venture and perhaps recognition on a broader scale.In a reading about love, you find contentment, peace, and ease in a relationship. A happy and comfortable home life is yours.
This card brings celebration, socialization, and events tied to community life. Birthdays, weddings, graduations, coming of age celebrations, and any gathering that brings people together for an important occasion all connect here, offering a reminder that life includes plenty of moments meant for stopping to enjoy time with loved ones. Lasting memories and a genuine sense of belonging tend to build during these times. Stability and the safeguarding presence of the right people also come through with this card, marking a moment worth rewarding when everything is falling into place.This card may point toward buying a new home or completing renovations financially, or spending on a well deserved vacation.The rewards of hard work arrive with this card, as the fruits of labor finally ripen and room opens up to enjoy the benefits earned along the way.This is an extremely positive card for love and relationships. An engagement may be on the way for a well established partnership, or the relationship may be moving to its next stage, such as moving in together and celebrating with a housewarming. Something positive is on the horizon for those seeking love, making it a good time to get out and be seen.A time of rejuvenation and celebration arrives with this card in health and well being readings. It may show up as a clean bill of health and good news from medical professionals, or as success in physical competitions or sporting events.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
Even when the Four of Wands is reversed, it's still a positive card. You may celebrate your good fortune with less fanfare, or your success may be more modest than if the card were upright. Still you're content with what you've accomplished and feel good about yourself.In a reading about money, the reversed Four finds you financially secure but says you shouldn't be extravagant or frivolous with your money.In a reading about work, you're in a solid, secure position and content with your creative output, though you may not be able to rest on your laurels yet. For the most part, cooperation exists in your business or workplace and you feel things are moving along satisfactorily, although, perhaps, not as quickly as you'd expected.In a reading about love, this card says you can resolve problems now. With a little effort, a pleasant and harmonious relationship is possible. Sometimes the reversed Four means a celebration gets delayed temporarily.
The surrounding environment turns less positive under this reversal. Expressing thoughts and feelings to friends and family becomes harder, with a sense of walking on eggshells to avoid setting someone off. Plenty of tension and few resolutions tend to show up here, sometimes calling for relocating or finding temporary space to gain distance from the disruption and reflect on whether things can still be salvaged. Cancelled events, mistakes, or blunders during an important celebration can also appear, so backup plans are worth having ready to roll out when the unexpected strikes.Costs are running well above expectations financially, calling for a rethink of what is truly worth spending on.Failure and loss are simply part of life, and this reversal does not have to define a career going forward. A big bid at work may have been lost, or something important handed to someone else, but there is room to feel the disappointment before picking back up and carrying on.Home fails to feel like a happy place under this reversal, and relationships suffer as a result, with breakups sometimes on the horizon. Families fighting or struggling with new territory, such as teenagers wanting more freedom or growing pains with younger children, connect here too. Level heads and calming strategies matter most in these moments, rather than anything that adds more fuel to the situation.A sense of routine and capability feels shaken under this reversal. It may point toward an injury that halts a beloved sport or activity, or toward needing a significant break from the usual routine in order to heal.

Imagery and Symbolism

A welcoming celebration full of joy and happiness fills this card. Four upright wands adorned with garlands or flowers frame a festive gathering that symbolizes completion and community. Figures in the foreground appear to rejoice or dance beneath the canopy formed by the wands, their body language full of freedom and animation, while others celebrate alongside them in the background, lending the whole scene a shared sense of togetherness and happiness.

Affirmation

I am cherished and celebrated, and I belong.

Astrology

The Four of Wands carries the astrology of Venus in Aries. Venus holds the energy of love, beauty, and pleasure, and under the influence of Aries the two temper each other, striking a balance that joins their passions and calls for celebrating what each brings to the other.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What is worth celebrating in life right now?
  • Who are the people that bring a genuine sense of belonging?

Reversed

  • How might the negative environment at home or work be getting contributed to?
  • What past mistakes still need to be forgiven?
