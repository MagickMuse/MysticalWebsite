Card Grimoire

Takeover or Merger Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & Money
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether the rumors are exaggerated or unnecessarily negative Card 2: Whether what is known has positive aspects Card 3: What strengths or attributes assure you a place in the new organization Card 4: What new skills you need for a smooth transition Card 5: What your main fears are, and how to resolve them Card 6: What opportunities might lie outside the current organization Card 7: What changes in work practice you would welcome Card 8: What compromises you can't or won't make Card 9: Your situation nine months ahead
◇Description
For easing fears about surviving a takeover or merger when rumors are flying, weighing what's known, your strengths, and what opportunities might exist either inside or outside the organization. Best cast during a waxing moon.
