You've developed a project, business, creative endeavor, or other venture, and now you're ready to share it with others. By devoting yourself fully to your goal, you can succeed. Do what's necessary to bring your dreams to fruition.In a reading about money, the upright Three shows increase and financial support coming to you. You may have to spend money to make money, but this card promises success.In a reading about work, this card suggests an exciting time when your efforts start to bear fruit. You're giving it your all, inspiring others, and attracting people who are eager to help you. Your ideas begin to take form. Collaboration can be beneficial.If you asked about a relationship, the Three indicates a commitment, an engagement or marriage. You and a partner express passionate feelings for each other and celebrate your love.
Plans already set into motion are calling for an expanded reach, with work and creative energy focused toward what comes next. Steady progress and opportunities that broaden the horizon are the reward for staying the course. This is a good time to take in the big picture and step beyond a familiar comfort zone. Confidence tends to grow alongside experience under this card, encouraging bold thinking and consistent follow through.Attention turns to building savings or reviewing current deals that could reduce fees with financial institutions.Good tidings for business accompany this card, with growth that may call for new team members to keep up with customer demand. It also favors those seeking grants or a cash injection for work.A relationship going the distance is a strong possibility under this card. The connection is deepening, with compatibility showing up in many ways. Committed relationships are favored too, staying smooth as long as both partners continue to show up for each other.Health and well being are on the right track, with positive results on the way for continued effort. Current actions play an instrumental role in shaping future health, so patience and persistence remain key to making long term health goals a reality.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
You still have work to do before you go public with your ideas. Perhaps you could benefit from honing your skills. Some areas of an undertaking still need clarification, or you may need to win the support and blessing of others in order to advance.In a question about money, this card can mean the financial support you need isn't there at this time. You might have to work extra hard for a while to make ends meet, be prudent in spending.In a reading about work, the reversed Three suggests you're not ready yet to market your project/ideas. You feel passionately about what you're doing, but you still have to convince other people to get onboard. For artistic people, this card advises, "Don't give up your day job yet."In a reading about love, you may feel strongly about a partner but aren't ready yet to make a commitment. Or, the other person may not be as emotionally invested as you are.
This reversal advises keeping to the well worn path rather than reinventing anything or taking on too risky a venture that could prove more than can be handled. Limitations placed from a variety of sources may come into play here, along with a vision that has become too small and a fear of truly pursuing what is wanted. Other people's expectations can hold things back when allowed to take the lead, and plans previously in motion may stall for reasons outside personal control. Zooming out to take in the wider picture matters here, since staying hyper focused on small tasks can cause the bigger perspective to be lost.This is not the time to invest, spend, or sign loans or mortgages financially unless no other choice remains.A lack of cooperation, delays, bad luck, or obstacles may prevent progress for a company or team members under this reversal. Someone operating for personal gain could also introduce a risk of betrayal that deters progress toward larger goals.Long term goals that no longer align between partners tend to surface under this reversal, and the resulting misalignment causes issues that only grow harder to resolve with time. A relationship started now is unlikely to last, though a short term connection can still be enjoyed for what it is without expecting it to become something more.Frustration tends to arise from setbacks or obstacles that prevent forward movement under this reversal. A sense of being unprepared for current challenges may play a role, or external circumstances may be limiting the ability to expand into new health options. Reassessing strategies and realigning health goals with the present situation becomes the call here.

Imagery and Symbolism

A figure often stands atop a cliff overlooking the sea in this card, three sturdy wands firmly planted in the ground nearby. Eyes fixed on the distant horizon and the boats making their way across the water, the scene conveys a vision of what lies ahead and anticipation for what is still to come.

Affirmation

I take daily positive action toward my long-term goals.

Astrology

The Three of Wands carries the influence of the Sun in Aries, bringing consistency and independence to this card's meaning. Success rarely happens by accident under this pairing, arriving instead through showing up and doing what is needed each day.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What is the vision for the future?
  • What long term goals are worth pursuing?

Reversed

  • What specific setbacks or obstacles currently hinder progress, and how can they be proactively addressed?
  • Where might expectations need adjusting when faced with obstacles or challenges?
