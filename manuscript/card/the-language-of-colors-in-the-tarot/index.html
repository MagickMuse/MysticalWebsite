The Language of Colors in the Tarot

The colors used in tarot are often carefully considered during a deck's creation, true for the Rider-Waite-Smith tarot as well as most decks available on the market. Some decks select a color scheme for each suit that aligns with that element's core energies, while other creators favor specific palettes such as pastels or vibrant neon. Colors convey messages through vibration and through the meanings assigned to them by cultures worldwide.

Common interpretations of color vary depending on cultural background, making it worthwhile to add personal meaning to the ones offered here. Some colors or palettes may simply never resonate, no matter how much effort goes into connecting with them. The interpretations below serve as a starting point for developing personal meanings for the colors found in tarot.

Black

Black holds the absence of light, signifying emptiness, loss, mystery, and depression. It also proves essential for every other color to carry depth and variation of hue, making it a forceful feature that represents formality, dignity, force, convention, stability, and zero tolerance. Black connects as well to protection against negativity.

Blue

Blue stands as a dominant color found throughout nature, visible in the sky and the ocean alike. Its meanings include open spaces, freedom, imagination, expansiveness, inspiration, peace, and sensitivity, leaving behind a sense of calm and trust. Blue connects to the throat chakra, and its negative traits surface through challenging emotions, stagnant waters, and predictable behavior.

Brown

Brown carries the energy of groundedness and nature, the feeling familiar to anyone who has squished their toes into rich, moist earth. This earthy color reads as tangible, practical, stable, nurturing, and solid, without frills and strongly conservative in nature, offering exactly what it appears to be. Brown also represents decay and organic matter breaking down.

Gray

Gray brings uncertainty, its message clear whenever fog rolls in and its haze settles: distortion, neutrality, uniformity, intensity, and austerity. It signals a need for establishing middle ground, along with a reminder that things are sometimes different than they first appear. This color calls for a pause before acting.

Green

Green stands as a lucky color associated with all things that grow, its lush arrival each spring bringing attributes of youth, sentimentality, nature, adventure, growth, and health. Green connects to the heart chakra, and though usually harmonious, its negative traits surface through envy, materialism, and jealousy, fitting given money's own bright green color. This bright, happy color can also turn irresponsible and unstable.

Indigo

Indigo represents the unknown depths of the sea alongside emotion, strength, fluidity, persuasiveness, expressiveness, and pervasiveness. Connected to the third eye chakra, this energetic color carries less positive traits through theatrics and criticism, while holding the capacity to both trigger and inspire.

Orange

Orange forms a subdued blend of aggressive red and high pitched yellow, carrying warmth and glow without red's hot intensity. This color speaks to harmony, aspiration, sociability, courage, and contentment, and connects to the sacral chakra. Orange also reads as brash, driven, intelligent, and ambitious.

Red

Red exudes physical energy and intensity, implying a practical approach paired with movement. This color evokes strength, courage, passion, aggression, sexuality, and vibrancy, which explains why so many of the world's biggest brands choose a red logo. Red connects to the root chakra, and its burning intensity can quickly fan out of control, serving as a warning of impending danger and intense emotions such as anger.

Violet

Violet carries one of the highest energies in the color spectrum, speaking of deep spirituality, wisdom, and devotion, and often connecting to spiritual leaders and teachers. Found in the hue of flowers and sleepy sunsets, violet evokes communion, grandeur, high ideals, ambition, and peace. This color connects to the crown chakra and can read as both mysterious and arrogant.

White

White stands as the color of possibilities, representing peace, cleansing, illumination, purity, innocence, and the highest level of understanding. Some tarot decks use white to represent negative space within a card.

Yellow

Yellow carries a high vibration, just like the bright, warm rays of the sun. This color radiates creativity, protection, intellect, positivity, and clarity, bright and cheerful enough to serve as an instant mood lifter. Yellow connects to the solar plexus chakra.
