Card Grimoire

Controlling Partner Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsShadow Work
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: The underlying cause of your partner's negative behavior Card 2: What makes you accept this Card 3: Whether your partner has compensating good qualities Card 4: Whether you can or should accept or ignore the behavior, or whether it is eroding your confidence Card 5: How you can change the pattern Card 6: Whether you should walk away Card 7: Whether outside interference is making the problem worse Card 8: Whether you can change your own reactions to deal with the problem better Card 9: The ideal outcome
Description
Drawn from the full deck on a Saturday, the day of imposing boundaries, when a partner constantly undermines you, these nine cards explore the root of the behavior, why you accept it, any compensating good qualities, the toll on your confidence, how to change the pattern, whether to walk away, outside interference, your own reactions, and the ideal outcome.
