Card Grimoire

Summer Solstice Sun Spread

◇Spread Type
Seasonal
◇Question Style
General guidance
◇Card Details
Layout: six cards in a circle (ends pointing outward) turned up one by one, moving sunwise. - Card 1 (bottom): You or the querent - Card 2 (to the left): The situation or the question - Card 3 (continuing sunwise): Obstacles in your way - Card 4: Possible solutions to those obstacles - Card 5: Guidance or suggestions to give direction - Card 6 (to the right of Card 1): The future/outcome Ritual notes: You may begin with a short prayer for help and guidance and close with thanks. Performing outside at noon on Midsummer is especially appropriate.
◇Description
A simple six-card sun-shaped spread for Summer Solstice. Best used outdoors at noon on Midsummer for clear general guidance. Shuffle while holding your question, lay six cards face down in a circle and reveal them one by one.
