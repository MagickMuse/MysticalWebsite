If this Knight represents a real-life person, he's not a flashy or adventurous or even a particularly creative guy, but you can depend on him, and he's willing to do what's necessary to bring his goals to fruition. Money, possessions, and status mean a lot to him. He often succeeds through determination and effort, not inspiration or genius. Symbolically, this card speaks of perseverance, dedication, loyalty, and steadiness in the face of change or turmoil.In a reading about money, you may receive information about money matters or an investment. This card can also indicate profiting from a practical investment or endeavor or selling property. Be content with slow gains rather than sudden windfalls.If the reading is about your job, the upright Knight can mean changing jobs for a more financially lucrative position or traveling for business. Sometimes he represents a career in finance or one connected with farming, real estate, construction, or manufacturing.In a reading about love, this Knight indicates stability, practicality, and loyalty. He may not be the most romantic or inspired lover, but you can rely on him. This card can also point to a relationship based on security, financial or otherwise.
Reliability and responsibility define this knight, who values thoroughness, precision, and follow-through in everything they do. Loyalty to the people they care about matters deeply, and offers are carefully considered before a commitment is made.Steady, practical investments and endeavors bring slow but reliable gains rather than sudden windfalls.Work that pays the bills and allows for providing for others is indicated, even when it isn't a dream role; using downtime for a passion project or side hustle is worth considering.Loyalty, practicality, and a set routine define this connection; a little relationship tune-up, like making more time for each other, may be needed.Patience supports progress here, alongside a structured, practical routine; overdoing tracking or restriction should be avoided.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
Reversed, the Knight of Pentacles can mean stagnation, stubbornness, an inability to step outside the box, and traditional ways of doing things. Lack of imagination or courage can limit success. If the reversed Knight signifies an actual person, he's probably a young man without vision or curiosity, a "stick-in-the-mud." He may lack spiritual insight because his focus is on money and material possessions. Sometimes the reversed Knight shows a hedonistic nature or a willingness to take the low road if it leads to wealth or status.In a reading about money, this card suggests you're so caught up in making money you can't enjoy anything else. Conversely, it may say you lack the patience, persistence, and pragmatism to prosper.If the reading is about your job, the reversed Knight indicates boredom, but you aren't willing to do what's necessary to improve your lot. Laziness, lack of direction, indulgence, or feelings of unworthiness can inhibit your success.In a reading about love, you're bored but unwilling to put in the necessary effort to revive a romance. Perhaps a relationship is based on security, financial or otherwise. You long for a change, but you're afraid to go for it.
Extreme energies define this reversal, swinging between an obsessive, undelegating workaholic and a couch-potato lack of motivation; boredom and a mundane routine are calling for more excitement and balance.Draining resources on a fixation or hyperfocus, or lacking the patience and pragmatism needed to prosper, is indicated here.Cutting corners and disengagement from the work at hand are present, alongside a need for more assertiveness in asking for growth or responsibility.Unreliability and broken promises define this pattern, sometimes paired with unrealistic expectations used as leverage; being stood up or ghosted is also possible.Procrastination around positive health changes has gone on long enough; finding an enjoyable, less monotonous way to make those changes helps this pattern shift.

Imagery and Symbolism

This armored warrior sits upon a plow horse in a field, clasping a single golden pentacle between their gauntlets. Large fields ready for seeds to be sown stretch out behind them under a clear sky. Heavy plate armor represents strength and durability, and the draft horse is chosen for its ability to take on heavy loads and its sheer size. This knight carefully considers the landscape and the pentacle before deciding what action to take.

Affirmation

I act on the opportunities the universe provides.

Astrology

This knight represents the mutable earth sign of Virgo, with its dedication, meticulousness, and systematic approach to achieving goals. Virgo is ruled by Mercury, a planet of motion that infuses this card with practicality, attention to detail, and a strong work ethic. The Knight of Pentacles reflects Virgo's diligent and analytical nature, emphasizing commitment to steady progress and tangible results.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • Who are the dependable and loyal people in this life, and how can appreciation be shown to them today?
  • Is a daily routine in need of being established or reestablished?

Reversed

  • What tends to happen when boredom sets in?
  • How can more excitement be brought into life this week?
