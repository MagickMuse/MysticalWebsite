Numerology

Number: 9. Keyword: wisdom.

Nine is a number of wisdom and magick. The Great Ennead is a group of nine gods and goddesses worshipped in the Egyptian city of Heliopolis. In ancient Greece, the nine Muses governed the arts and sciences. As the final and greatest single-digit number, nine symbolizes the end of a cycle and spiritual awakening.

In card readings, Nines are indicative of wisdom. In the Spade and Club suits, a Nine indicates a lack of wisdom that can result in surprising and unpleasant outcomes. The situation may feel beyond the querent's control, but Nines speak of conditions that the querent has either created or can avert through their own actions. Unlike the Sevens (completion), Nines represent cycles and evolving situations. Whatever is happening is a process of change, not an end result. In this respect, the Nines share a similarity with the Threes.

In Readings

The Nine of Spades is a warning. When it appears, the querent needs to develop a more responsible attitude and carefully consider how present choices and actions will affect the future. The wisdom will come eventually, but the querent will be happier if it comes sooner rather than later.

If the querent does not examine the current situation wisely and meticulously, there will be an unpleasant outcome. The good news is that they have the potential to make good decisions now and avoid suffering a painful lesson later. This card indicates a process of change during which the querent should steer clear of foolish choices as much as possible.

This does not mean the querent should avoid the situation. When the Nine of Spades appears, the querent needs to take responsibility, but in a careful and thoughtful way.
