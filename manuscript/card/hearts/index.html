Hearts

Suit
Hearts
Keywords
Water, emotion, relationships, memories, social events, cold and moist
Correspondences1
Water
Symbols1
Heart

The suit of Hearts reflects the cool, moist element of Water. The keyword for this suit is emotion. Historically, the heart was perceived as the seat of emotions, possibly because it beats faster when a person is excited or afraid. People "give their hearts" to those they love, and those hearts are "broken" when that love goes unreciprocated. A deep, sincere emotion of any kind is described as "heartfelt."

The heart symbol is commonly associated with valentines and romantic love, but emotions come in many forms. Sorrow, fury, joy, and grief are all emotions. In astrology, the Water signs are the most emotional of the zodiac, and water frequently symbolizes emotions in dream interpretation. Heart cards reveal the querent's general emotional state but do not indicate how those emotions are manifesting. Surrounding cards provide that clarification.

The querent's age and lifestyle matter when interpreting Hearts. For younger, single querents, a Heart card is very likely to indicate romantic affection. For older querents or those in stable, long-term relationships, Hearts more often speak of familial or platonic emotions.

In Readings

Hearts address emotions and memories. Strong feelings toward a person or experience embed it deeply in memory, for better or worse. The desire to remember and be remembered by loved ones is the same force that sustains grief after loss.

Hearts almost always point to emotional relationships. Despite the valentine shape of the symbol, this suit covers platonic and familial bonds as readily as romantic ones. Romantic love is only one part of the emotional spectrum. The specific relationships indicated by Hearts depend entirely on the querent's circumstances.

Hearts can also indicate romance, from lasting partnerships to brief, passionate encounters. The interpretation depends on context and the querent's situation.

Beyond individual connections, Hearts can point to social situations: gatherings, parties, group hobbies, or shared activities. The key distinction is that these are communal experiences, not solitary ones.

Court Cards

Heart court cards are nearly the opposite of Diamond court cards. Where Diamond court cards suggest people to approach with caution, Heart court cards represent people the querent can feel comfortable around. These are most likely individuals already known to the querent. If the card indicates a stranger, it will be someone easy to connect with. Heart court cards tend to represent passionate, emotional people.

Tarot Equivalent

Hearts align with the suit of Cups in the tarot, which shares the same Water element.
