Numerology

Number: 8. Keyword: manifestation.

Eight takes the stability of four to the next level. It is a symbol of solidity and the material world. In numerology, eight is the highest number that can be divided into two equal parts, then each half divided again equally, and again, showing stable balance at every division. Due to this association, eight often represents material or financial success.

Eight is a number of manifestation. An Eight card indicates its suit's elemental influence manifesting in the querent's life, always involving the physical, material world. Eights are usually good cards, speaking of potential opportunities. They are forward-looking, always indicating a future event, typically one arising in the very near future.

In Readings

The Eight of Clubs brings money and material possessions into the arena of transformation. This card often addresses finances more directly than the Eight of Diamonds does. While the Eight of Diamonds can indicate fortune in any tangible or intangible form, the Eight of Clubs focuses on material wealth.

This card is neither good nor bad; it can go either way. The transformation could work in the querent's favor, but very often it serves as a warning. When the Eight of Clubs appears, it will be very easy to lose money through unwise investments or foolish spending. Look for potential opportunities, but avoid unnecessary risks and be careful not to spend on things that are not truly needed.

Similar to the Eight of Diamonds in regard to material resources, but with an important distinction: while the Eight of Diamonds suggests cautiously moving forward, the Eight of Clubs warns the querent to rein things in.
