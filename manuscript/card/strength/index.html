Archetypal Meaning

Strength represents the integration of higher and lower self. It can also be read as the learning process of recognizing oneself as capable of holding what is wanted.

The Fool's Journey: Resilience

Strength's stage brings challenges that call for courage, heart, and resilience. Here it becomes clear that patience and tolerance carry as much power as brute force.

Imagery and Symbolism

In many decks, the Strength card shows a serene figure, often a woman, with her hands resting on the jaws of a fierce lion, a precarious pose that captures how inner courage can triumph over brute force. Her calm demeanor against the lion's subdued posture speaks to harmony between the human spirit and raw animal instinct. The infinity symbol above her head reminds us that energy is never lost, and that we carry access to both our own inner power and something greater. Often set against bright skies and open surroundings, the scene reflects the quiet confidence and peaceful assertion this card offers.

Sometimes called Necessity, this card shows a fair-haired woman opening the jaws of a lion. She seems to use no undue force, for she does not wrench the lion's mouth open but parts it with her fingertips, and the lion does not struggle. Her approach is gentle. Though the lion is the more powerful creature in body, he is no match for the human figure, and the card stands for the power of moral and spiritual strength over pure brute force, the victory of spirit over flesh.

Like the magician's hat, the woman's hat has a brim shaped like a lemniscate, the figure of eight, a sign of magickal or superhuman power. Unlike the magician, though, she is not surrounded by elemental emblems and carries no wand, for all her power is internal and part of her being rather than drawn from outside forces. In the gentleness of her approach to the lion she wields a subtle power that is the premise of the feminine, a strength of compassion rather than of muscle. The lion itself is a potent symbol of the Sun and of divine power as well as of an untamed animal nature, one that must be met with gentle strength and understanding before it can be refined.

Affirmation

I acknowledge my strength; I acknowledge it is love.

Astrology

The Strength card is connected to the Fire sign Leo, ruled by the Sun. Leo's boldness, courage, and leadership infuse this card with vitality, charisma, and a strong sense of self, while the Sun brings a radiant, assertive warmth. Together they highlight the theme of mastering one's instincts and expressing yourself with confidence and authority.
