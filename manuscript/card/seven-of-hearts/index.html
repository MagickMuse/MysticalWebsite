Numerology

Number: 7. Keyword: completion.

Seven symbolizes completion and fulfillment. There are seven visible planets, seven notes in a musical scale, seven days of the week, and seven chakras. In the Nine Herbs Charm, an Anglo-Saxon healing spell, herbs are "sent into the Seven Worlds," meaning all realms of existence. Seven is widely regarded as a magickal number.

The Seven cards represent a perfect fulfillment of their suit's elemental association, neither excess nor lack. Like the Fours, Sevens tend to accentuate the positive qualities of Diamonds and Hearts and the negative qualities of Spades and Clubs. Sevens point to interpersonal situations, indicating the querent in relationship to at least one other person. The condition described may even affect the other people more than the querent. There is almost always a social aspect to the Seven cards.

In Readings

Sometimes called the marriage card, the Seven of Hearts predicts emotional completion and fulfillment. It can predict a coming marriage, but it often simply refers to one that already exists. The card may reference the querent's own marriage or someone else's. Surrounding cards and intuition help clarify.

This card speaks of true marriage: an ongoing, committed relationship between two people. The ceremony does not create the relationship, nor does a legal license define it. The Seven of Hearts describes the calm, serene bond between two souls who have grown comfortable with each other over time.

This card can sometimes indicate deep nonromantic, nonfamilial relationships as well, such as a profound friendship, though that is more the domain of the Eight of Hearts. The Seven of Hearts is a very social card and always describes some kind of fulfilling union with another person or persons.
