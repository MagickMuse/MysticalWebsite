Numerology

Number: 1. Keyword: power.

Aces are emphatic and powerful cards that strongly express their suit's elemental influence. They carry a more general meaning than other pip cards due to their forceful nature. An Ace's message may be further defined by surrounding cards, but its meaning is never overwhelmed or negated. Aces are almost always a positive omen, carrying creative and independent energy.

In Readings

The Ace of Hearts signals a surge of love. It may point to romance, but the emphasis falls on the emotional aspect rather than anything physical. The love may arise from within the querent or come from someone else, but either way it will be strong and prevailing.

This can be a very social card, involving not just one person but the querent's entire circle of friends. In that case, the affection is more dispersed, but the card still indicates a notable improvement in social life. This is a good time to pursue and nurture emotional relationships. Romance may be woven into these connections if the querent is open to it, but the card fundamentally speaks of positive emotional bonds, whether romantic or platonic. The Ace of Hearts is love in all its forms.
