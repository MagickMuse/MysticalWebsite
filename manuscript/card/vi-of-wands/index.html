The Six of Wands promises a time of happiness and fulfillment. You've marshaled your energies during times of struggle and overcome obstacles. Now you're on top and can expect recognition and rewards. You feel good about yourself and your accomplishments. Sometimes this card shows recovery from an illness or setback.You can expect financial success. A long-term investment may pay off now or you may receive a raise/bonus.This card can mean getting a promotion, receiving respect for your efforts, or seeing a creative project come to fruition. Enjoy your well-deserved success.The upright Six indicates a time of happiness, love, and cooperation in a relationship. If you're seeking a partner, this card offers encouragement for true love.
This card indicates that a goal has been achieved, along with recognition for that achievement. Public acclaim or praise from respected peers may follow, offering a real boost to confidence and encouragement to continue the effort already underway. Pride in what has been achieved and learned along the way feels well earned here. This card also serves as a welcome sign for anyone moving through a challenging time who wants reassurance that the outcome will be positive, though it is not yet the moment to stop putting in effort. A major milestone has been completed, but there is still further to go.A windfall or positive financial news is likely on the way financially.Positive reviews, endorsements, and customer praise all connect to this card in work and business, heralding a time of increased success and productivity. Entrepreneurs are encouraged to maintain steady focus and stay ambitious rather than resting on their laurels.Getting the relationship that is truly wanted becomes possible under this card, whether that means a crush admitting their feelings or more potential suitors making a connection. In established relationships, this card signals both partners working together toward long term success.Whatever is currently being done for health is working, making it worth continuing. Wins of all types connect to this card, marking a positive sign in health and wellness readings, with current confidence and strength carrying momentum from strength to strength.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When the Six of Wands is reversed, it can mean you still have work to do before you can enjoy the success and happiness you seek. You may feel frustrated, but if you continue fighting for what you want, you should win. Sometimes this card suggests reassessing your goals.This card can signify delays in getting money that's due to you or that an investment won't pay off as you'd expected.The Six reversed indicates some struggles may exist in your workplace. Perhaps egos are getting in the way of cooperation, or you don't feel you get the recognition you deserve.This card can mean you and your partner have some issues to work out before harmony can reign. If you're looking for love, you may have to wait a while longer.
A public fall or failure that hurts pride and confidence often accompanies this reversal. The choice of whether this setback defines the future or becomes something to rise above and try again from remains open. Real friends and true supporters tend to reveal themselves here, since anyone who relishes another's failures is not worth keeping close. Inner strength and the worth of continued goals remain intact even now. This reversal can also point toward spinning wheels and getting nowhere despite continued effort, suggesting it may be time to step back rather than push harder. A tendency to do kind things only for recognition rather than genuine goodwill can also surface here.Delays in receiving money that is due, or an investment that fails to pay off as expected, can show up here financially.Some struggles may exist in the workplace under this reversal, perhaps with egos getting in the way of cooperation or a sense of not receiving the recognition that is deserved.Some issues may need working out with a partner before harmony can return, and those seeking love may need to wait a while longer.Confidence in previously trusted wellness or treatment plans may waver under this reversal. Disheartening as the setback feels, it does not call for giving up altogether, and seeking more than one medical opinion alongside personal research tends to help.

Imagery and Symbolism

This card carries the energy of victory as a confident rider makes their way through a gathered crowd celebrating their triumphant return. A laurel wreath atop one wand echoes the rider's own symbol of success, while the cheering crowd offers welcome positive attention. Even the horse receives recognition here, finely adorned as an important partner in reaching this success.

Affirmation

I am victorious.

Astrology

The lucky planet Jupiter influences fiery Leo with this card, suggesting that anything approached wholeheartedly is likely to succeed. The stars and planets offer their approval here, marking the road ahead as one leading toward victory.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What does success look like right now?
  • What feelings come up when receiving attention and praise, and what might help build more comfort with it?

Reversed

  • How have past failures been handled, and what did they teach about the self?
  • Where is more acknowledgment wanted, and how can that be encouraged?
