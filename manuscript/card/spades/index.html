Card Grimoire

Spades

Suit
Spades
Keywords
Air, movement, disruption, chaos, challenges, warm and moist
Correspondences2
AirTornado
Symbols1
Spade

The suit of Spades reflects the element of Air, warm and moist. Nearly imperceptible when still and calm, air becomes a force to be reckoned with once set in motion. A gentle breeze has brought relief to humans and animals alike since the beginning of time. Eventually, humans learned to harness the wind, propelling ships across oceans. Today, wind turbines can supply much of the world's electrical power.

But moving air can also be destructive. Winds that propel a sailboat can strengthen at sea and build into a hurricane, demolishing buildings and threatening lives when they reach the coast. Inland, tornadoes rip across the plains. When air is set in motion, it frequently creates adversarial conditions. This dual nature is reflected in the suit of Spades: some cards indicate the wind is at the querent's back, while others predict difficulties.

In the oldest playing card decks, and still in some tarot decks today, this suit was represented as swords. Sword symbolism similarly cuts both ways: empowering or threatening, depending on which end of the blade the querent is facing.

The keyword for this suit is movement. The "cold" suits of Diamonds and Hearts (Earth and Water) tend to indicate stability, while the "warm" suits of Spades and Clubs (Air and Fire) speak of change. Movement is change, sometimes pleasant, often disruptive and unwelcome. Controlled movement pushes toward goals. Uncontrolled movement blows the querent off course.

In Readings

Wherever Spades appear, disruption follows. The challenges can often be overcome, but the work required may exceed expectations and the rewards may fall short. Spades interfere with plans, particularly when the querent does not confront the challenges directly.

A slight breeze scatters papers. A stronger wind moves heavier objects. A powerful gale destroys nearly everything in its path. Yet with effort and planning, wind can be harnessed to move ships or grind grain. Spades represent that same force. They do not always predict failure, but they signal chaos and obstacles that can lead to it if left unaddressed.

Court Cards

Spade court cards represent people who actively bring disruption into the querent's life. Unlike Diamond court cards, whose agendas merely happen to conflict with the querent's goals, Spade court cards indicate people targeting the querent personally. The antagonism may stem from a specific incident, creating a rift that is difficult or impossible to repair, or it may be entirely irrational. A court card from the suit of Spades is a warning.

Tarot Equivalent

Spades align with the suit of Swords in the tarot, which shares the same Air element.
