Upright GeneralUpright MoneyUpright EmploymentUpright LoveUpright Health
The King upright is a fortunate card, regardless of the situation. If he represents a real person, it's someone you can trust and rely on to do the right thing. He's a loyal friend, a fair-minded employer, a wise adviser, a competent and honest leader. This intelligent man has high ideals. He's an active, energetic, independent individual with a passion for life and an adventurous nature.In a reading about money, you can expect good things to come your way when the King appears. Sometimes the King means getting a raise, that a venture will pay off, or that you have financial support.If the reading is about your job, success is assured. The King may indicate a promotion or recognition for your efforts. It can also show you moving into a position of authority. Now is a good time to take a chance, for victory is yours.In a question about love, the King of Wands says the relationship is a strong one with plenty of affection and passion, perhaps between two lively, creative, outgoing people.
This king embodies natural leadership, entrepreneurship, and a clear vision for the way forward. Accepting the mantle of leadership and executing that vision matters here, leading by example even when starting out alone, since others will follow in time.Good financial news tends to follow this energy, whether through a raise, a venture paying off, or steady financial support.Success is likely here, possibly through a promotion, recognition, or a move into a position of authority; now is a good time to take a chance.A relationship full of affection, passion, and vitality is indicated, often between two lively, creative, outgoing people.Accepting help and support from others matters here, rather than letting ego get in the way of receiving the care that's deserved.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When the King of Wands is reversed, you may run into delays or your success isn't as great as you'd hoped for. If he represents a real person, it may be someone who's not available to you or who can't help you even if he wants to. Perhaps his loyalties lie elsewhere. He's probably not dishonest or irresponsible, but he may not be forthcoming.In a reading about money, the King reversed can mean you have to wait for money that's due to you or you won't receive as much as you would've liked.In a job-related matter, he can mean a project will take longer than you'd planned or you'll have to work harder than you'd expected. Perhaps some of the most creative aspects of an undertaking will get nixed by higher-ups.If you asked about a relationship, this King can indicate waning enthusiasm. Or, a romance may be slow getting started. Sometimes it shows a person who's committed to another.
A once-admirable belief in leadership has curdled into an ego out of control here, showing up as arrogance, coercion through fear, and little mercy or compassion; this pattern often masks deep insecurity rather than real strength.Waiting longer than expected for money that's owed, or receiving less than hoped, is likely.A project may take longer than planned, or require more effort than expected, with some of the more creative ideas getting rejected by those in charge.A controlling dynamic where one partner dictates what the other can do reflects this reversal; believing someone's true nature the first time they reveal it through anger is important.Dismissing medical advice in favor of personal certainty, or falling for miracle cures and faith healers, shows up here.

Imagery and Symbolism

In many depictions, this king sits on a throne adorned with lions to symbolize strength and salamanders to symbolize transformation. In one hand is a wand, and this king's focus rests on it rather than looking directly ahead, representing vision and an unwavering sense of purpose. A lizard at the bottom hints at this king's ability to thrive in challenging environments and situations.

Affirmation

I was born for this.

Astrology

This king represents the fixed fire sign of Leo, the charismatic, confident, and radiant leader of the zodiac. People follow this sign without being prompted or asked to do so. Leo is ruled by the Sun, a consistent, vibrant energy that this king embodies with ease. This astrological connection asks for boldness, confidence in trying new things, and a willingness to raise others up alongside oneself.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What vision exists for the year ahead?
  • What kind of leader feels true to who this energy wants to be?

Reversed

  • Who is the most arrogant person ever encountered, and what did that reveal about oneself through observing them?
  • What parts of the ego are worth developing in a more positive way?
