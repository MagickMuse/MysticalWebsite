Numerology

Number: 5. Keyword: dynamic.

Five is the keystone number, with four numbers before it and four after. Fives are adventurous and unpredictable, representing forceful change. Unlike the instability of the Deuces (which arises from contrast), the Fives carry a more balanced uncertainty. Red Fives (Hearts and Diamonds) generally indicate positive change, while black Fives (Clubs and Spades) more often signal negative change, though exceptions depend on the querent's situation.

Fives are the antithesis of routine. With the stable suits of Diamonds and Hearts, Fives are generally safe as well as exciting. Five is the number of the physical senses (touch, taste, sight, smell, hearing), and Fives represent both sensuality and personal experience. A Five speaks to the querent on a personal level, addressing things happening directly to them rather than to others around them.

In Readings

The Five of Hearts shakes things up. Where the Four of Hearts is the friendship card, the Five is the party card. It predicts fun, exciting, and interesting events in the querent's future.

For single querents, the Five of Hearts can be very romantic, hinting at flirtations and sensual connections. However, it is not a card for those seeking long-term commitment. A fun first date might eventually lead somewhere lasting, but this card promises only momentary pleasure.

For introverts, the Five of Hearts is not always welcome. It carries a strong social aspect that not everyone finds comfortable. Even for extroverts, there are times when parties and flirtations are more of a distraction than anything else.

This card can also serve as a caution if the querent is in a committed relationship where outside flirtations would be unwelcome. Usually, though, the Five of Hearts is positive, predicting entertaining connections that may be platonic but are no less exciting. However it manifests, it is always a deeply social card.
