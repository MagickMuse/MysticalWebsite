Card Grimoire

Seven Colors of the Rainbow Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual GrowthDecision Making
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1 (Red): How determined you are to succeed whatever the cost Card 2 (Orange): Whether fulfilling this dream is what you truly desire Card 3 (Yellow): Whether the facts and figures for success add up Card 4 (Green): Whether to let the dream grow slowly, or follow your heart and go all out Card 5 (Blue): Whether you have the confidence, knowledge, and experience to pursue it Card 6 (Indigo/Purple): What hidden resources or gifts will emerge if you go ahead Card 7 (Violet/White): Whether you'd still want to go ahead even without material success
◇Description
For discovering whether a dream or ambition is realistic, laying one card per color of the rainbow starting with red, each read in ascending order before the next is turned, covering determination, true desire, practicality, pace, confidence, hidden resources, and whether you'd proceed even without material success. Best cast when a rainbow appears in the sky or is reflected in the room.
