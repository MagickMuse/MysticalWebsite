Railroaded Into the Family Business Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & MoneyDecision Making
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: What you really want Card 2: The underlying cause of opposition to change Card 3: Whether you have support for change within or beyond the company or family Card 4: Whether you're ready to take up your new path now, or need more preparation Card 5: Whether you can negotiate or withdraw gradually Card 6: Whether it's all or nothing Card 7: How you can deal with the fallout Card 8: Whether you can stay within the family, or need to go away for a time to fulfill your dream Card 9: Your ideal longer-term outcome
◇Description
For being pushed to enter or stay in a family business or profession when you want something different, but you're scared of the reaction to taking a stand, weighing what you really want against support for change, how to negotiate or withdraw, and how to handle the fallout. Best cast at the end of a month.
