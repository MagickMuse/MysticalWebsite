Description
This large, comprehensive spread forecasts the year ahead, with each card representing the energy, challenges, and opportunities that a given month will bring. It is commonly completed in December for the upcoming calendar year, though it can be done during any month, in which case it covers the twelve months that follow. Reflect on how each monthly theme interacts with the card drawn for that position, noting energies worth manifesting, energies worth avoiding, and any dominant theme or missing element across the whole year.
