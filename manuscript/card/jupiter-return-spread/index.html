Jupiter Return Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual GrowthCareer & Money
◇Question Style
General guidance
◇Card Details
Card 1: Current blessings – what abundance already exists in your life Card 2: Area of expansion – where growth is most available to you now Card 3: Opportunities incoming – what doors are opening Card 4: Hidden potential – talents or resources you haven't fully tapped Card 5: Obstacles to abundance – what blocks your prosperity Card 6: Action to take – how to align with Jupiter's generous energy Card 7: What to release – beliefs or patterns limiting your growth Card 8: Outcome – the likely result if you follow Jupiter's guidance
◇Description
This spread is designed to explore themes of expansion, abundance, and opportunity during Jupiter's return to its natal position (approximately every 12 years). Use it to examine where growth is available, what blessings are incoming, and how to maximize Jupiter's beneficent influence in your life.
