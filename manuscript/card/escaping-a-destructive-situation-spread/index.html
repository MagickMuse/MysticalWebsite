Card Grimoire

Escaping a Destructive Situation Spread

Spread Type
Shadow WorkDecision Making
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: The self, and what is at stake Card 2: Fears tied to the destructive or abusive situation that hold you back Card 3: Action you can take Card 4: Who will rescue or help you Card 5: What makes you hesitate Card 6: The first step Card 7: The dangers of taking that first step Card 8: Who else you must consider or protect Card 9: The beginning of your new life
Description
Drawn from the full deck on a Saturday, the day of protection and endings, when someone feels powerless but knows they need to leave an abusive or destructive situation, these nine cards also suit any major life-changing event, a wider life review, or a moment that calls for a minor miracle. They explore what is at stake, the fears that hold the person back, possible action, who will help, what causes hesitation, the first step, its dangers, who else must be considered, and the start of a new life.
