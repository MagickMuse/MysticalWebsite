The upright Four represents a practical, conservative attitude toward money and material goods. You place great value on wealth and possessions and take pride in your worth. Because security is important to you, you hold on tight to what you own and may hoard money rather than enjoying it. You could think of this as the "miser card."In a reading about money, this card can indicate a cautious, prudent attitude toward financial matters. You play it safe and devote yourself to accumulating wealth. The upright Four may signify a sound investment or a stable financial position.If the reading is about your work, you've chosen a job that's secure and provides a good income, perhaps one in finance. Wealth gives you status and power, which you value. However, you may not feel inspired, challenged, or content in your position.In a reading about love, the upright Four represents a stable, secure, structured relationship. The partnership may be based on status and money rather than love and may lack passion.
Holding tightly to resources and security defines this card, sometimes tipping into an overly cautious, possessive grip on time, money, and material things. Left unchecked, this focus on "what's in it for me" can quietly push people away, leaving possessions to define a life that starts to feel hollow.A short term tightening of the budget or a dip into savings may be needed while money flow slows, though this slowdown will not last forever. Being intentional and prudent with existing resources matters more than taking new risks right now.This is not the moment to push for a promotion or a raise, since conditions favor patience over pursuit right now.A partner may be growing possessive, or love and resources may feel conditional, withheld until certain expectations are met. Either way, this card signals a relationship dynamic worth examining closely.Trying to control every aspect of a health situation tends to backfire, making it worth loosening the grip, trusting that the right support will arrive when needed, and reviewing any health or life insurance policies for needed changes.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When the Four of Pentacles appears reversed, it suggests your conservative attitude and limited vision are causes of stagnation. As a result, you encounter obstacles and delays that keep you from moving ahead. You need to make changes, to stop clinging to old ideas and tried-and-true methods, but you're afraid to take a risk.In a reading about money, this card recommends against an investment or financial venture at this time. Sometimes it indicates imprudent spending or bad decisions regarding money or property.If the reading is about your job, the reversed Four can mean you feel insecure in your position. Outsourcing, cutbacks, or competition may threaten your job. You may be passed over for promotion or fail because your skills are outdated or inadequate.In a reading about love, you may fear losing control in a relationship. Because you sense your power dwindling, you feel insecure. Perhaps you depend on your partner for financial support and feel trapped in the relationship.
Common sense around money has curdled into something closer to miserliness, with growing defensiveness over possessions and less willingness to help others financially. A deep fear of loss often sits underneath this pattern, making it harder to open up to other people no matter how much has been accumulated.Priorities need sorting out here, since possessions and money may remain intact while relationships with people who matter quietly erode if a limiting attitude does not shift.Anxiety about career and financial outlook can consume more energy than the work itself, so keeping the quality of that work high will pay off even before circumstances improve.Holding too tightly to an idealized version of a relationship can obscure what is actually happening. In more serious cases, this reversal can point to a controlling household situation that calls for an exit plan when it becomes safe to leave.A reluctance to invest time or money in health and well-being deserves a closer, non-judgmental look, especially examining where that belief originally came from and whether it still serves any real purpose.

Imagery and Symbolism

A figure sits so wrapped around their possessions that movement becomes impossible, with pentacles placed at the feet, over the heart, and near the head to show exactly what drives them. Whenever an action does not promise more money, this figure simply will not act. Cold stone surrounds the scene, a symbol of being stuck in place and unwilling to bend.

Affirmation

I need to hold on to what I have.

Astrology

The Sun in Capricorn grounds this card's energy, and can also tip it toward stubbornness. On the positive side of this pairing, real potential exists for production and regeneration.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • Is generosity easy to offer, or does the wallet tend to stay closed around others?
  • What truly motivates the pursuit of money?

Reversed

  • What views exist around the materialistic side of the self?
  • What fears surface around financial insecurity?
