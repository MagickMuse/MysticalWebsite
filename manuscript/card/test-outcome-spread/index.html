Card Details
Card 1: Likely outcome of the exam, test, or interview, and what could improve it if unfavorable
Description
Drawn in the days or weeks before an exam, driving test, or interview from the Minor Arcana's Ace-through-Ten cards, this single card points to the likely outcome and what could improve it if the result leans unfavorable; if its message is unclear, the card returns to the deck for a reshuffled replacement rather than adding a second card.
