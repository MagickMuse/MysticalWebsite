Card Grimoire

Contacting an Old Love Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & Relationships
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether dreams or unexpected reminders suggest a telepathic link Card 2: Whether you can or should try to find them to see if they're free Card 3: Whether you still want to make contact, even if they're not free Card 4: Whether you want to develop or revive the friendship either way Card 5: Whether you want a more intimate connection, by phone or in person Card 6: Whether pursuing it is still worthwhile even if it doesn't work out, just to put the relationship to rest
◇Description
For being unable to get an old love out of your mind, weighing whether dreams or coincidences suggest a lingering connection, whether to seek them out through social media or old contacts, and whether reviving the friendship or even closure is worth pursuing. Best cast just after a full moon.
