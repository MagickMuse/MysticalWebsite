Stop-Start-Continue Spread

◇Spread Type
Decision Making
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: What to release or stop Card 2: What to begin or start Card 3: What to keep or continue
◇Description
This spread is useful when evaluating habits, patterns, or a course of action. Shuffle and cut the deck, then lay three cards side by side. The first card indicates something to stop or release, the second suggests something new to start, and the third highlights something worth continuing as is.
