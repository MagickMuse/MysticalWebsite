Reuniting a Divided Family Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsShadow Work
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: The outward cause of the original quarrel Card 2: Whether this reflects existing underlying family tensions or resentments Card 3: Who is keeping the quarrel going and won't let it lie Card 4: How there could be a compromise Card 5: Who can best act as peacemaker, and how Card 6: How future conflict can be avoided
Description
For a family split by a pointless quarrel where members aren't speaking and no one will make the first move, tracing the original cause back to deeper tensions, identifying who's keeping it alive, and finding a path to compromise and a peacemaker. Best cast at sunrise.
