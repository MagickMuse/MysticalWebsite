Card Grimoire

Undeserved Financial Loss Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & Money
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: The current position to be remedied Card 2: What can be salvaged Card 3: What can be abandoned Card 4: The key to recovery Card 5: What potential financial help is available from friends and family Card 6: What official funding sources are available Card 7: Whether there's possible compensation from the person or organization that caused the loss Card 8: Whether you should rebuild or make a complete change Card 9: How swift recovery will be
◇Description
For recovering from a financial loss that wasn't your fault, laid out as a slanting ladder that moves from the current position through what can be salvaged or abandoned to the odds and speed of recovery. Best cast on a Sunday, for new beginnings.
