◇Description
This is the most involved of the standard cartomancy layouts, with each of its twelve positions corresponding to a different facet of life. As with a past-present-future reading, meaning comes primarily from position rather than a specific question, and the layout tends to surface what a querent needs to know rather than what they are hoping to hear.

Positional Pairings

Positions six apart form natural pairs when the layout is arranged like a clock face:

  • Positions 3 and 9: grounding and expansion
  • Positions 4 and 10: past and future
  • Positions 5 and 11: romantic encounters and lasting relationships

Checking a card's paired position can add nuance to its interpretation, though intuition may surface connections between any two positions regardless of formal pairing.

Reading the Cards

Each card in this spread tends to produce one of four reactions: a small number that immediately stand out and carry the reading's core message, one that feels significant but resists interpretation until a clarification card is drawn on top of it, one that feels intentionally blank or empty, and the majority that simply read as expected for their position. If more than a few cards feel unclear or blank, it may help to set the reading aside and return to it later.
