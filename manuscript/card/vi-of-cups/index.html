A card of emotional renewal, perhaps after a time of unhappiness, the Six of Cups represents a willingness to share joy with others. You eagerly give of yourself and receive the good things life offers. Through your actions, you express love and caring to people you know and to the things you value.This card suggests an upswing in your finances, perhaps after a loss or time of limitation. It can represent a joint venture in which you share expenses and profits with others. Sometimes it indicates a debt repaid.The Six upright shows renewed enthusiasm for your work. Perhaps a change offers you more creativity or an improved work environment. Developing new skills can benefit you now.This card shows you're overcoming problems in a relationship and renewing positive feelings for one another. Sometimes the Six of Cups can mean reconnecting with a past lover.
A gentle wave of nostalgia surfaces here, whether it shows up as longing for the glory days of the past or a chance to reminisce about childhood. Old friends, school reunions, and family gatherings all fall under this energy, alongside stories retold about earlier years. Keeping things simple and enjoying life's small pleasures is the invitation. This placement also speaks to the inner child, sometimes pointing toward healing an old wound or simply making more space for that part of the self to be expressed. The past cannot be changed, but the inner child can still be cherished today. Children present in life right now deserve quality time and the wisdom that comes from seeing the world through their eyes.Renewed enthusiasm surfaces here, sometimes through allowing space for creativity. Drawing, painting, writing, or returning to something enjoyed in childhood can bring a sense of refreshment that carries over into work.Someone from the past may reach out unexpectedly or return into the picture. This could arrive as a welcome gift or an unwanted disruption, and there is no obligation to re-engage with someone from the past just because of prior history.A recurring health issue is likely to flare up again, so avoiding known triggers where possible helps. This placement also signals that past trauma, especially from childhood, is ready to be healed.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
Because the Six of Cups is such a happy card, it signifies pleasant things even when it's reversed. It can show you strengthening your emotional muscles, building on past successes that will aid you in the future. However, it may mean you're stuck reminiscing about the "good old days" and can't seem to find happiness in the present.You may be clinging to a time in the past when your financial picture was brighter. By refusing to look at new options, you may be limiting yourself.You may be releasing old responsibilities in order to move forward. However, you may long for times when your career offered more excitement. Sometimes this card represents retirement.The reversed Six can mean it's time to grow up and leave childish romantic fantasies behind. In some cases, this card shows you deepening your feelings and taking a more mature approach to a relationship.
Old wounds from childhood may still be clung to or replayed, even when doing so causes harm. This can range from serious past trauma to simple disappointment that things didn't unfold as hoped in youth. Emotional baggage can weigh heavy, and progress through it may feel slower than desired, though outside perspective can help movement forward even in small steps. This placement can also point to emotional immaturity or gullibility, difficulty reasoning with someone who cannot manage their emotions, or a nudge toward self-reflection if that immaturity feels closer to home.Starting from an entry-level place is not a step down, everyone begins somewhere. Getting to know people at work slowly matters here too, since some carry more emotional messiness than first impressions reveal.Rose-colored glasses tend to make the past look better than it actually was, so remembering things clearly matters. This can also point to wishing a current relationship felt like it once did. Relationships change and evolve, and there's no benefit in staying stuck in what used to be.This can point to fertility concerns or complications tied to children's health. Some situations can't be changed through effort alone, and medical intervention around fertility carries real cost, worth weighing carefully against the investment and risk involved.

Imagery and Symbolism

Two children share what they have with each other on a pleasant day, cups between them filled with white flowers that symbolize innocence and childhood. An older figure moves away in the background, heading toward the safety of home. The children remain in a protected, safe environment throughout the scene.

Affirmation

Memories of love and joy fuel my heart and guide me toward a fulfilling future.

Astrology

The Six of Cups carries the light of the Sun in Scorpio, illuminating emotions tied deeply to the past and childhood. That past can be both a gift and a great teacher, just as it can be the place where old trauma first took root. It remains an important part of identity, and every sense feels magnified when this card appears in a reading.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What childhood interests could be revisited soon?
  • What is the most cherished memory from the past, and why does it hold that place?

Reversed

  • Who from childhood is a relief to no longer have present, and why?
  • In what ways has immaturity shown up in the past, and what did that behavior lead to?
