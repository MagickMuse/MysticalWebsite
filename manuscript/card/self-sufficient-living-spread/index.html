Card Grimoire

Self-Sufficient Living Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you're ready to take this step now, or should wait Card 2: Whether you can live more sustainably by adapting your present lifestyle without major change Card 3: Whether you can lease, rent, or buy suitable premises and land soon Card 4: Whether you should keep your present employment or work part-time while setting up Card 5: Whether you can eventually earn extra money from the land, through produce, courses, or guests Card 6: Whether this is a sufficiently ultimate lifestyle for you and your loved ones that you're willing to go all out for it
◇Description
For pursuing a dream of living close to and sustained by the land, weighing readiness, whether gradual lifestyle changes would suffice, finding suitable premises, and whether to keep working while setting things up. Best cast at a seasonal change point.
