Card Grimoire

Overcoming Intimidation Spread

Spread Type
Decision MakingShadow Work
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: Who or what seeks to crush you Card 2: The motive or real power behind the perpetrator Card 3: What evidence is on your side Card 4: Who will support you Card 5: Whether to walk away or fight on Card 6: The weakness or flaw of the person or organization intimidating you Card 7: What positive publicity or exposure could strengthen your cause Card 8: The ideal outcome
Description
Drawn from the Minor Arcana and Court cards on a Tuesday, the day of fierce power and protection, in an octagonal layout with each card placed along one of eight visualized sides, when the odds feel stacked against someone facing a powerful individual or organization, these eight cards examine who or what is seeking to crush them, the real motive and power behind it, what evidence is on their side, who will support them, whether to walk away or fight on, the opponent's weakness or flaw, what positive publicity could strengthen their cause, and the ideal outcome.
