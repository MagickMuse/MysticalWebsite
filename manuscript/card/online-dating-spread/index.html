Online Dating Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
Question Style
Specific situation
Card Details
Card 1: Whether online dating will open your life to love Card 2: Whether you would be happier building a relationship online instead of through face-to-face dating, agencies, or other activities Card 3: Whether you want to develop this into a phone, video, or face-to-face relationship Card 4: Whether you would prefer formal dating agencies or social media and interest groups Card 5: What scares you most about online dating Card 6: How you will know if a profile is genuine Card 7: Whether you will meet your twin soul online
Description
Drawn from the full deck on a Wednesday, the day of cyber contact, when location or circumstances make it hard to meet potential partners in person, these seven cards weigh whether online dating could open your life to love, the appeal of online connection versus other avenues, moving things toward phone or in-person contact, preferred platforms, your biggest fear about it, how to spot a genuine profile, and whether a twin soul could be found online.
