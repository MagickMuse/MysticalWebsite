True Love Later in Life Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & Relationships
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you're open to the right love when they come along, or need longer to heal Card 2: Whether this love will grow slowly from friendship, or be love at first sight Card 3: When and where you'll find this love Card 4: How you'll know they're the one Card 5: Whether this love will last forever Card 6: How you can avoid or ignore the disapproval or interference of those who won't welcome a new love in your life
◇Description
For longing to meet a lasting loving companion later in life or after a loss, weighing your openness to new love against healing still needed, whether it will grow slowly or strike at first sight, and how to handle disapproval from others. Best cast on Valentine's Day, or any full moon.
