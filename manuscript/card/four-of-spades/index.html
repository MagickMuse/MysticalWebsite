Numerology

Number: 4. Keyword: stability.

Fours are steady and reliable, sometimes to the point of stubbornness. They express their suit's elemental influence with more emphasis than Threes and in a way that is less mutable. Changing the influence of a Four can be difficult, though that same stubborn stability becomes a blessing when the card indicates something worth keeping.

Fours carry a low-key energy rather than anything fiery or erratic. Under the influence of four, the naturally chaotic suits (Spades and Clubs) are almost always negative, though not disastrously so. The situation indicated by a Four will remain the same unless the querent takes active and persistent steps to create change.

In Readings

The Four of Spades presents the contradiction of stasis and motion. Instead of flowing freely, energy here is blocked, circling around and around. The result is frustration and annoyance. Although it might be possible to turn the situation into something productive, this card suggests the querent may do better to simply ignore whatever is causing the irritation.

The motion of events in the querent's life is being resisted. The buildup of energy can make the situation seem larger and worse than it actually is, which is why ignoring it may be the wisest choice. The card indicates irritating circumstances, but nothing disastrous. Feeding more energy into the situation could actually make it worse.

Despite the negative tone, the Four of Spades can be reassuring in a reading. Knowing that aggravations are coming makes it easier to keep things in perspective and step away. The annoyance it describes is not something easily changed, but it is something the querent can choose to ignore.
