Card Grimoire

Celtic Cross Spread

Spread Type
Celtic Cross Style
Question Style
Open-ended
Card Details
Card 1: The context of the reading and underlying issues that led to the question Card 2: The here-and-now question or issue to be resolved Card 3: Potential obstacles or related circumstances shaping the question Card 4: Practical considerations, the root of the matter Card 5: Reality versus illusion, the head of the matter Card 6: The totally unexpected factor that can transform the situation Card 7: True feelings, both your own and the messages from those around you Card 8: Suggested action Card 9: Helpful influences that counterbalance Card 3's obstacles Card 10: Short-term outcome Card 11: Long-term outcome
Description
Using one or two full decks on a quiet evening, eleven cards resolve any major question, complex issue, opportunity, or in-depth life review, read in three sections. The Heart of the reading, Cards 1 to 3, sets the context, the core question, and potential obstacles or related circumstances. The Surrounding Square, Cards 4 to 7, weighs practical considerations, the line between realistic goals and illusion, a totally unexpected factor, and true feelings versus the messages of those nearby. The Pathway to Happiness, Cards 8 to 11, then moves through suggested action, helpful influences, and the short-term and long-term outcomes.
