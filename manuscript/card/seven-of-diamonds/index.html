Numerology

Number: 7. Keyword: completion.

Seven symbolizes completion and fulfillment. There are seven visible planets, seven notes in a musical scale, seven days of the week, and seven chakras. Seven is widely regarded as a magickal number.

The Seven cards represent a perfect fulfillment of their suit's elemental association, neither excess nor lack. Like the Fours, Sevens tend to accentuate the positive qualities of Diamonds and Hearts and the negative qualities of Spades and Clubs. Sevens point to interpersonal situations, indicating the querent in relationship to at least one other person. The condition described may even affect the other people more than the querent. There is almost always a social aspect to the Seven cards.

In Readings

While the Six of Diamonds indicates a loss, the Seven indicates a gain, but not for the querent. Seven cards always involve relationships, and the Seven of Diamonds points to a gain for someone close to the querent: a friend, relative, spouse, or business partner.

The gain is some form of fortune, which may or may not be financial. It could be improved reputation or a lucky break. If monetary, it will not be extravagant. Seven is a number of completion, not overwhelming abundance. Whatever form it takes, this gain will be a pleasant surprise for the recipient, something hoped for but not truly expected.

The querent may benefit indirectly, especially if the lucky recipient is a spouse or business partner. But any benefit to the querent will be a collateral effect. The primary beneficiary is someone else in their life.
