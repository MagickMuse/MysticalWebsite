Card Grimoire

Partner Wants Freedom Too Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
Question Style
Specific situation
Card Details
Card 1: Whether your partner is a dreamer who never grew up Card 2: Whether uncommitted friends are a major part of your partner's life Card 3: Whether you trust your partner when you are apart Card 4: How you can steer the relationship toward realistic future plans Card 5: Whether this is enough for you now and in the future, if your partner cannot or will not change
Description
Drawn from the twenty-two Major cards and sixteen Court cards on a significant anniversary in the relationship, when a partner is romantic and full of grandiose plans but also wants freedom, these five cards examine whether they are a dreamer who never grew up, the influence of uncommitted friends, trust while apart, how to steer things toward realistic plans, and whether the current situation is enough for you.
