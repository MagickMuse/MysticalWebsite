Card Grimoire

Life Interconnections Spread

Spread Type
Past-Present-Future
Question Style
General guidance
Card Details
Cards 1–8 (Row 1, the Past): Childhood on the left moving to more recent events on the right, including unresolved issues, revivable talents, and people who contributed to present success Cards 9–16 (Row 2, the Present): Current relationships, home and work opportunities and challenges, goals, achievements, and unfulfilled dreams, with lingering past issues often near the start of the row Cards 17–24 (Row 3, the Future): Potential paths from the immediate future on the left to up to ten years ahead on the right Card 25 (optional, placed above Row 3): The unexpected factor that could help future success
Description
Using the full deck once every six months, twenty-four cards are laid out in three rows of eight to reveal how the past unconsciously shapes the present and future. Row 1, nearest and read left to right, covers the past, moving from childhood on the left to more recent events on the right, including unresolved issues alongside talents that can be revived and people who contributed to present success. Row 2 covers present relationships, home and work opportunities and challenges, goals, achievements, and unfulfilled dreams, with lingering past issues often surfacing toward its start. Row 3, read from bottom to top, looks at potential future paths, from the immediate future on the left to as far as ten years ahead on the right, often shaped by the action and plan cards at the end of Row 2. An optional twenty-fifth card, dealt facedown above Row 3, reveals an unexpected factor that could help future success.
