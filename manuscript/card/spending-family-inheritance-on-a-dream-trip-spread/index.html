Spending Family Inheritance on a Dream Trip Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & Money
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you're entitled to spend your own money as you wish Card 2: Whether you should feel guilty for following your dream Card 3: Whether you'll regret it if you don't follow your dream
◇Description
For deciding whether to spend some of the family's future inheritance on a major trip or holiday for yourself, when adult children call it selfish, weighing your right to the money against guilt and future regret. Best cast when you're thinking about planning the adventure.
