Numerology

Number: 7. Keyword: completion.

Seven symbolizes completion and fulfillment. There are seven visible planets, seven notes in a musical scale, seven days of the week, and seven chakras. Seven is widely regarded as a magickal number.

The Seven cards represent a perfect fulfillment of their suit's elemental association, neither excess nor lack. Like the Fours, Sevens tend to accentuate the positive qualities of Diamonds and Hearts and the negative qualities of Spades and Clubs. Sevens point to interpersonal situations, indicating the querent in relationship to at least one other person. The condition described may even affect the other people more than the querent. There is almost always a social aspect to the Seven cards.

In Readings

The union of completion and transformation here is better expressed as a "complete mess." This is a card of conflict, similar to the Six of this suit, but with nothing subtle going on. Instead of a smoldering anger, the querent faces a full firestorm. The fire is complete; it is an inferno.

Expect arguments and conflicts when the Seven of Clubs appears. The conflicts may arrive unexpectedly, but they are the result of issues that have been building for some time. This is never a solo experience; other people are involved. Since the suit of Clubs draws attention to personal transformation, it is likely that some or all of the fighting is the querent's own fault to some degree.

It is possible to turn the situation around, especially if the conflict has not yet erupted, but it will not be easy. The Sevens are cards of completion, so the querent will also need to completely change their personal behaviors.
