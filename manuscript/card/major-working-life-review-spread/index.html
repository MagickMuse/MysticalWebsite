Major Working Life Review Spread

Spread Type
Career & Money
Question Style
General guidance
Card Details
Card 1: What is missing from your working life Card 2: What can easily be changed Card 3: What major changes you should make Card 4: What major changes would cause disruption Card 5: Whether you can progress within your present company Card 6: Whether you should change jobs Card 7: Whether you should relocate to advance your career Card 8: Short-term results, six to twelve months after change Card 9: Five-year prognosis of change
Description
For regaining direction when you've lost your sense of purpose at work, dealt clockwise in a circle to map what's missing, what can change, and the likely results over the next five years. Best cast at New Year's, on a birthday, or at the change of a season.
