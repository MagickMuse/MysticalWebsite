Family Takes You for Granted Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsShadow Work
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether your family is simply thoughtless, rather than deliberately selfish Card 2: Whether you grew up always expected to help others Card 3: What makes you say yes when you want to say no Card 4: Whether you get a bad reaction when you do say no Card 5: Whether you truly need to do so much for the family Card 6: Who will back you up if you start saying no Card 7: Whether you could become less available to others' needs generally Card 8: Which requests you can't or don't want to refuse Card 9: Whether you'd be happier fulfilling necessary requests on your own terms, with thanks Card 10: Who helps you, or could help you more Card 11: Whether you find it hard to ask for or accept help, and why Card 12: The ideal outcome for you
◇Description
For being treated as room service, an on-tap babysitter, or an ever-open wallet by your family, tracing whether it's thoughtlessness rather than deliberate selfishness, why you struggle to say no, and how to become less automatically available while still helping where it matters. Best cast when you never have time for yourself.
