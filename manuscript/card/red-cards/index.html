Red Cards

◇Keywords
stability, Diamonds and Hearts, Earth and Water, cold suits
↗Correspondences2
EarthWater
↗Symbols1
🔴Red

Red cards include the suits of Diamonds (Earth) and Hearts (Water). These are the "cold" suits, and they lean toward stability, both material and emotional.

Reading Color Balance

An abundance of red cards in a spread (roughly 75 percent or more) indicates a relatively stable situation. In a three-card reading, all three cards would need to be red to qualify. In a twelve-card spread, nine or more red cards would signal this pattern. The individual cards then define the specific nature of that stability.

Red card dominance suggests the querent's circumstances are grounded, whether in material security (Diamonds) or emotional well-being (Hearts). However, even a strong red presence can be disrupted by the specific cards in play. Context always determines the final interpretation.
