Numerology

Number: 8. Keyword: manifestation.

Eight takes the stability of four to the next level. It is a symbol of solidity and the material world. In numerology, eight is the highest number that can be divided into two equal parts, then each half divided again equally, and again, showing stable balance at every division. Due to this association, eight often represents material or financial success.

Eight is a number of manifestation. An Eight card indicates its suit's elemental influence manifesting in the querent's life, always involving the physical, material world. Eights are usually good cards, speaking of potential opportunities. They are forward-looking, always indicating a future event, typically one arising in the very near future.

In Readings

The Eight of Spades signals that motion and changes are about to come into full force. The querent is at a turning point where the current situation is more important than they may realize. This card is nearly the opposite of the Six of Spades: where the Six predicts depleted energy, the Eight predicts abundance.

There is nothing symbolic or philosophical about this card. The coming changes will be overt and significant. Even if the querent is anticipating the event, it will be more consequential than envisioned. The outcome could be good or bad, but the Eight of Spades usually implies a positive one. It is only unwelcome if the querent does not want change, because this card predicts changes arriving in a very big way. It marks an especially significant moment in the querent's life.
