When the Knight of Wands appears upright, he brings good news. Sometimes he represents a trip or relocation. Some sort of movement or activity is afoot, of a happy kind. If the Knight represents a real person, he's a good-natured and likable chap, generous of spirit, idealistic, a loyal friend.In a reading about money, he suggests a fortunate opportunity is on the horizon and/or a financial change is coming. You may receive money unexpectedly. Or, the card may advise you to take a financial risk.In a reading about work, the Knight upright may represent a promotion or a transfer to another locale/company. He can also point to a business trip in the near future.If you asked about love, this Knight indicates an enthusiastic and enjoyable affair, but don't expect it to last. Have fun, but realize your partner is a free spirit who doesn't like commitments.
Confidence and courage define this knight, who pursues dreams without hesitation and trusts their own instincts. Movement, travel, or a fresh opportunity for growth beyond the comfort zone is on the way, and adaptability serves well here.A fortunate opportunity or unexpected financial change is on the horizon; a calculated financial risk may also be worth taking.A promotion, transfer, or business trip may be coming, along with positive news after recent interviews.An enthusiastic, adventurous connection is likely, though commitment isn't this energy's strong suit; expect fun over permanence.Increased energy supports bold steps toward better health, especially through movement outdoors; travel for treatment or specialist care may also be part of the picture.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When the Knight of Wands is reversed, a message (or paperwork) may be delayed or an event postponed. If he represents a real person, this card cautions that the message, or the messenger, may not be reliable. That includes social media; check out those Facebook posts before you share them.In a reading about money, the reversed Knight advises you not to take a financial risk now. Or, it can mean you're spending more than you can afford, maybe on a car or trip. Tuck those credit cards away before you get in trouble.If you asked about a work-related matter, the Knight reversed can indicate restlessness or a job change. Sometimes it shows canceling or postponing a business trip.In a question about love, this card shows fickleness, flirtatiousness, and lack of trust. One person (or both) doesn't want to settle down. Sometimes a relationship ends or a romantic event (maybe a vacation) gets postponed.
Impulsiveness and restlessness have led to a mess that now needs cleaning up. Getting involved in situations that aren't one's concern, boredom, and a lack of discipline bring out this card's more destructive side; bringing control and taking responsibility for impatience is the path forward.Financial risks should be avoided right now, and overspending on things like travel or a vehicle deserves a second look.Restlessness or a looming job change may be at play, along with a postponed or canceled business trip.A pattern of love and leave shows up here, entertaining but not built for commitment; compromise beats a flat refusal when a partner wants to spice things up.Burnout calls for real downtime, not just a quick nap, and extra caution is warranted around injury or illness while traveling.

Imagery and Symbolism

This knight is headed somewhere important and isn't stopping for anyone or anything. Their horse jumps over an obstacle, rears up, and prepares to charge ahead. Pyramids in the distance mirror the Page of Wands's environment. Dressed more for adventure than war, this knight is a welcome sight to come across on the road. The fiery colors and salamanders on their clothing symbolize change and instinct.

Affirmation

Every day is an adventure, and I welcome the opportunity to explore and learn what it offers me.

Astrology

The Knight of Wands represents the mutable fire sign of Sagittarius, a dynamic, adventurous, and passionate sign. This knight values freedom and exploration, rarely staying in one place for long. Sagittarian values of spiritual growth, risk-taking, and the pursuit of knowledge all come through in this card. Sagittarius is ruled by Jupiter, lending its influence of optimism and change to the Knight of Wands.
