Court Cards

Court cards, also called face cards, depict human faces. Though influenced by elemental associations like the rest of the deck, court cards almost always represent people who can influence the querent directly through their actions and decisions. The elemental influence of the suit shapes the personality and energy of the person the card represents.

Queens often represent mature people fulfilling the nurturer, homemaker, or healer archetype, regardless of gender. A Queen might indicate the querent's mother or grandmother, but not a daughter, even if that daughter is older. Kings and Queens are people the querent looks up to or regards as peers.

As with Kings, the definition of maturity shifts according to the querent's own age. Queens can refer to someone of a specific gender, but they can also indicate any person fulfilling a traditionally nurturing or healing role in the querent's life.

Further distinctions are a matter of perspective and vary from one reading to another. It is up to the reader to determine how a court card should be interpreted based on who they are and who they are reading for.

In Readings

Like the other Heart court cards, the Queen of Hearts is defined more by function than by age. This Queen can be any age but is someone with experience, not a person the querent perceives as childish. The card may represent someone the querent has not yet met but will soon encounter. If it is someone already known, the relationship is about to change, moving to a deeper and more intimate level.

Depending on the querent's attractions, this card may speak of romance. The Queen of Hearts can be a very passionate card. If romance does not fit, it can still represent someone the querent has a meaningful connection with, such as a relative or friend.

If neither interpretation feels right, the card may simply indicate passion in general, regardless of anyone's gender. Intuition should always guide the interpretation of the Queen of Hearts.
