Upright GeneralUpright MoneyUpright EmploymentUpright LoveUpright Health
The upright Six heralds good times ahead, particularly in financial/material areas. You can profit from cooperative ventures, combining your talents and sharing resources for the greater good. This card can also represent philanthropic projects or providing work for other people. You could think of this as the "win-win card."The upright Six can signify an opportunity to participate in a group venture that will profit everyone. Sometimes it points to acquiring a loan or other financial backing.The Six represents teamwork. You may participate in a group effort that uses your talents; combining your abilities or resources with others' promises success.The upright Six suggests sharing resources, financial and otherwise, with a partner so you both benefit. This give-and-take card encourages you to open up to what a partner offers, and vice versa.
This card centers on the flow of wealth and resources moving between people, and it reads as positive no matter which side of the exchange someone stands on. Holding the scales means having more than enough to share, while needing help means that help is already on its way. Money is only one form this exchange can take, since time, energy, and knowledge count just as much, and everyone moves between both roles at different points in life.Access to a grant, a bonus, or a renegotiated contract can show up here, with money moving in a favorable direction and bigger opportunities opening up alongside it.Finding balance between personal needs and what an employer expects becomes important here, since resources and responsibilities are not always distributed fairly within an organization.Fairness in a relationship rarely means an even fifty-fifty split, since partners bring different things to the table. What matters is that both people are contributing toward each other's benefit, and anyone dating should watch for a partner who gives as generously as they do.Help arrives without strings attached here, often as a returned favor from someone previously supported in the past, easing the financial weight that health related spending has created.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
Reversed, the Six of Pentacles can show unfair financial dealings, unreliable colleagues, or unpaid debts. Perhaps a project or investment doesn't materialize as you'd hoped or the strings attached to it choke you. Sometimes the reversed Six suggests relying on others for financial support, even taking advantage of their goodwill.The reversed Six may mean getting involved in a questionable financial endeavor that compromises or obligates you in some way. The payoff may not be what you expected, or you feel unsatisfied with the way your money is handled.You may have difficulty balancing your needs and aspirations with those of your employer. You may become disillusioned at how a company handles resources and responsibilities.You may feel an inequity in the balance of power. Perhaps your needs aren't being met, or you aren't meeting your partner's needs. The reversed Six recommends focusing on give and take in a relationship, supporting and encouraging each other.
An imbalance of power tends to show up here, whether personal needs are going unmet or someone else's needs are being overlooked. Restoring genuine give and take, along with mutual support, matters more than anything else right now.A financial arrangement can end up compromising or obligating someone in ways that were not expected at the outset, leaving the payoff feeling far less satisfying than anticipated.Aid or support may come with hidden strings attached, sometimes through a caretaking role that goes financially unrewarded despite real effort and genuine care.Someone in the picture may value money over the relationship itself, staying involved mainly for what they can gain rather than genuine connection, which is worth recognizing before it goes further.A next financial decision could carry a bigger cost than it first appears to, making it worth slowing down and fully understanding the commitment before moving forward.

Imagery and Symbolism

A well dressed figure holds a balanced scale in one hand, a symbol of fairness, while distributing coins with the other to people clearly in need. Those receiving help kneel with hands outstretched, their bandages and patched cloaks a visible sign of hardship. The scene captures both sides of an exchange happening at once.

Affirmation

I have an open heart within my community and people.

Astrology

The Moon in Taurus brings the energy of material success to this card. Emotional and intuitive gifts pair here with a love of earthly pleasure, creating a positive, effortless flow of resources and experience into daily comfort.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What feelings surface when money arrives as a gift?
  • How can giving back to community happen this month?

Reversed

  • When has help been given to someone with conditions quietly attached, and what did that experience teach?
  • How can resources come back into balance?
