Dream Date Yes or No Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & Relationships
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether the person will agree to a date if you ask now Card 2 (if needed): Whether the person will say yes at a later time
◇Description
For discovering whether now is the right time to ask someone you've been dreaming about on a date, with a second card you can draw if the first answer is no, to ask whether they'll say yes in the future. Best cast the morning before you intend to ask.
