Numerology

Number: 9. Keyword: wisdom.

Nine is a number of wisdom and magick. The Great Ennead is a group of nine gods and goddesses worshipped in the Egyptian city of Heliopolis. In ancient Greece, the nine Muses governed the arts and sciences. As the final and greatest single-digit number, nine symbolizes the end of a cycle and spiritual awakening.

In card readings, Nines are indicative of wisdom. In the Diamond and Heart suits, wisdom protects that respective aspect of the querent's life. The situation may feel beyond the querent's control, but Nines speak of conditions that the querent has either created or can avert through their own actions. Unlike the Sevens (completion), Nines represent cycles and evolving situations. Whatever is happening is a process of change, not an end result. In this respect, the Nines share a similarity with the Threes.

In Readings

The Nine of Hearts is the querent's protection card. While the Nine of Diamonds protects reputation or financial interests, the Nine of Hearts offers a more general, all-encompassing protection. When this card appears, the querent is in control of the situation and should be able to make wise decisions leading to the desired outcome with little effort.

This can feel like a good luck card, but it is not truly luck, since the outcome results from the querent's own actions. The card indicates that the querent has the wisdom to choose the right path.

The protection here is deeply personal. The querent's heart is protected. They may even experience a profound sense of spiritual joy. This protection and joy are not so much a single event as an evolving process that will lead the querent in a new direction.
