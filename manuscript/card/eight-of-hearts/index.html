Numerology

Number: 8. Keyword: manifestation.

Eight takes the stability of four to the next level. It is a symbol of solidity and the material world. In numerology, eight is the highest number that can be divided into two equal parts, then each half divided again equally, and again, showing stable balance at every division. Due to this association, eight often represents material or financial success.

Eight is a number of manifestation. In Wicca, the year is celebrated with eight seasonal sabbats. Buddhism has its eightfold path to nirvana. An Eight card indicates its suit's elemental influence manifesting in the querent's life, always involving the physical, material world. Eights are usually good cards, speaking of potential opportunities. They are forward-looking, always indicating a future event, typically one arising in the very near future.

In Readings

The Eight of Hearts combines the material nature of the number eight with the emotions of this suit. It predicts that the querent will gain something of value through love or affection. This could be a raise earned not only through hard work but also because a supervisor genuinely likes the querent. It could be a gift given out of affection by a relative or friend. Something is coming, or an opportunity is opening, because of how others feel about the querent.

Expect friendly reactions from those nearby. The card does not specify exactly when the gift or opportunity will arrive, but it will come in the near future.

This card can also indicate improved business activity for anyone who sells or provides goods and services that inspire love and attraction between people. Usually, though, it predicts some kind of gift or gain rooted in the feelings others have for the querent. The gift is not always tangible; it could be something like an offer of assistance.
