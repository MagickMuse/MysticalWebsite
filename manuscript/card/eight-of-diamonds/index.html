Numerology

Number: 8. Keyword: manifestation.

Eight takes the stability of four to the next level. It is a symbol of solidity and the material world. In numerology, eight is the highest number that can be divided into two equal parts, then each half divided again equally, and again, showing stable balance at every division. Due to this association, eight often represents material or financial success.

Eight is a number of manifestation. An Eight card indicates its suit's elemental influence manifesting in the querent's life, always involving the physical, material world. Eights are usually good cards, speaking of potential opportunities. They are forward-looking, always indicating a future event, typically one arising in the very near future.

In Readings

The querent can expect positive, uplifting events in the near future. These events could be financial but more often involve something less tangible that still affects the physical world. An improved relationship, for instance, will produce very real benefits. Like the Four of Diamonds, the Eight can indicate improved health.

Think of this as the querent's good luck card, though not in the dramatic way of the Ace. The events are not life-changing, but they may present an opportunity for greater achievement. The querent should pursue that opportunity only if it is clearly within reach.

When the card speaks of finances, it points to something steady rather than overwhelming. This is a good time to begin or focus on saving money, not a time for speculative ventures. Investments should be solid and reliable. If referencing reputation, it is a good time to expand connections and influence.
