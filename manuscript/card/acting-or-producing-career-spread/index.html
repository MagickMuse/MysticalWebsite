Acting or Producing Career Spread

Spread Type
Career & Money
Question Style
Open-ended
Card Details
Card 1: Whether you can pursue every acting and producing opportunity available, however modest Card 2: Whether you should study at theater school or go directly into theater Card 3: Whether you're prepared to travel anywhere, any time, for auditions Card 4: Whether you'll sacrifice financial security for fulfillment Card 5: Whether you should relocate to famous film or theater centers, or try more obscure places Card 6: How far you'll succeed in the next five years
Description
For committing to a career as an actor or producer in theater or film when nothing else will satisfy you, weighing training, travel, financial sacrifice, and where to base yourself. Best cast at New Year's or the beginning of a month.
