Tarot-Astrology

Astrology and tarot have been deeply intertwined across many decks, and their connection enriches understanding of both systems. Each major arcana card carries its own astrological association, documented as a correspondence on that card's own page, and studying the two systems together can support timing readings, narrowing down the people represented in a spread, and mapping out a journey of self-discovery.

Every card in the tarot can be correlated with astrological principles. The Moon card, for instance, is often associated with the intuitive and emotional depths governed by the Moon in astrology, reflecting inner mysteries and subconscious undercurrents. The Hierophant, in turn, is connected to the zodiac sign Taurus, highlighting the shared energy of tradition and slow moving change.

Combined, tarot and astrology offer a more dynamic, layered interpretation, one that can support predicting potential future events and navigating present emotional and spiritual landscapes. This holistic approach allows for a more tailored guidance system that adapts to individual contexts, making the insights more relevant and actionable. Learning the tarot's meanings alone is already a substantial undertaking, so keeping this quick reference on hand makes it easy to revisit the astrological layer later.
