Overcoming Shyness Spread

◇Spread Type
Decision Making
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether shyness is a problem, or a comfortable private nature Card 2: The underlying fear that stops approaching or responding to others Card 3: Whether professional help is needed, or self-guided change is enough Card 4: Low-stress first steps toward being more sociable Card 5: Who you're most comfortable with Card 6: The environment or situation where you relax enough to practice socializing
◇Description
Drawn from the Major Arcana and Court cards on a Wednesday, the day of clear communication, when shyness stands between someone and the friendships they want, these six cards weigh whether shyness is truly a problem, the underlying fear, whether professional help is needed, low-stress first steps, who feels most comfortable, and the best setting to practice socializing.
