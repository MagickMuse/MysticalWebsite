Archetypal Meaning

The Wheel of Fortune represents a time of awakening to one's own destiny and soul purpose.

The Fool's Journey: Change

After that inward turn, the realization sets in that everything is connected and the only constant is change. The Wheel of Fortune's stage teaches that choices shape destiny, and that willingness to change is essential.

Imagery and Symbolism

A large wheel inscribed with alchemical symbols or the Tetragrammaton sits at the card's center, marking the eternal cycle of change. Four figures representing the fixed zodiac signs, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius, surround it, each holding a book of wisdom that speaks to stability found through learning and experience. A sphinx, a serpent, and Anubis appear as further symbols of mystery, transformation, and renewal. Together, the imagery reminds us that we rise and fall in turn, and that nothing stays fixed for long.

The tenth numbered card marks the closing of a circle, for the wheel is a sign of completion. Where the Hermit speaks of the solitary and unworldly life, the Wheel of Fortune is very much a card of the world and all its challenges and changes. Two strange creatures sit on either side of the wheel, which is crowned by a sphinx-like figure bearing a sword, an alarming presence wholly detached from the struggles of the creatures below. All the meaning of the wheel is gathered here: the alternation of good luck and bad, dharma, the passing of time, and the need for life to balance positive and negative experience. The wheel is a solar emblem, the turning of the Sun on its cartwheel course through the Heavens, a relentless life-force.

The two creatures can be read as opposing forces and as the very motion of the wheel. The one on the right seems to rise with its turning while the one on the left descends, at least for a time, and the A.E. Waite deck shows this descending creature as a serpent. These opposing forces also recall the yin yang symbol. Just as the figure in the Chariot need not steer his vehicle but moves as one with it and its horses, the Wheel of Fortune is in some sense a continuation of that journey. It is an impartial force of nature and a reminder that freedom of choice also means freedom to rise to challenges, all part of the rich breadth of experience that life offers.

Affirmation

I allow myself to change and evolve with the seasons of my life.

Astrology

The Wheel of Fortune is ruled by Jupiter, the planet of luck, expansion, and destiny, and carries the energy of the Fire element. Jupiter's optimistic, growth-oriented energy reinforces this card's message of cyclical transformation, reminding us that even amid uncertainty, growth and prosperity remain possible.

This card suggests that luck and timing play significant roles in your journey, encouraging you to trust the process.
