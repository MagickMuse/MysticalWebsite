Difficult In-Laws or Stepfamily Acceptance Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: The main problem you experience Card 2: Who is most rejecting or critical of you, and their motive Card 3: Whether there's a more amenable person within or close to the family to support you Card 4: Where there's room for compromise, or where injustices are being overlooked Card 5: What you shouldn't compromise on Card 6: Whether your partner is being emotionally manipulated by those causing the problem Card 7: How you can break the guilt factor used against you or your partner Card 8: Whether and how you can reduce contact or confrontational situations Card 9: Whether you can ignore the problem and hope it resolves Card 10: Whether you should confront the worst offender and hold your ground
Description
For still being treated as the outsider by in-laws or a stepfamily despite your best efforts, identifying the core problem, who's most critical of you and why, where compromise is possible, and how to break a guilt-driven hold over you or your partner. Best cast the night before a full moon.
