Upright GeneralUpright MoneyUpright EmploymentUpright LoveUpright Health
When the King of Pentacles appears upright, he signifies a practical matter, finances, property, career/work, common sense, and security. If he represents a real person, it's someone who's a pillar of the community, a business leader, a person of wealth and substance, perhaps your boss or father. You can depend on him for support or advice in practical areas.In a reading about money, this card shows you're in a secure position financially. An investment should pay off, or you're advised to go ahead with a money matter you're considering. Good organization and practicality will benefit you.If the reading is about your job, the King shows success coming to you. Your position is stable, and you're using your resources wisely. You can take the lead in a business venture.In a reading about love, this card indicates a mature, stable relationship in which you support one another. Sometimes it shows a relationship based on money, status, or security.
Wealth and experience combine into a steady, dependable security here. A traditional, well-considered path is preferred, with consistency and reliability defining this energy once a decision is made.A secure financial position is indicated, with investments likely paying off through good organization and practicality.Stability and success are indicated, with resources being used wisely and leadership in a business venture being possible.A mature, supportive, and committed relationship is indicated, sometimes tied to marriage or a long-term commitment ceremony.Balancing enjoyment of life's comforts with regular movement matters here; simple activity like walking or an active hobby supports overall strength and bone health.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
When reversed, the King of Pentacles indicates sluggishness, delays, and/or obstacles to material success. Sometimes it shows low energy or insecurity. You feel stuck but don't have the drive to get out of your rut. If the card represents an actual person, he may be mercenary, coarse, dull-witted, or even corrupt.In a reading about money, your finances are stagnant, you might even incur losses, perhaps because you're stuck in old patterns and have resisted making changes. You're worried about money and/or don't handle your resources well.If the reading is about your job, the King reversed may mean you're bored with your work but can't find something better. Perhaps you've sacrificed your passion for security. Or, your job may be in jeopardy and you feel insecure.In a reading about love, a relationship may offer stability but little excitement or romance. Routine or responsibility may drain your vitality. Perhaps you feel stuck in a relationship for financial reasons.
Rigid control and outdated expectations define this reversal, with a demand that others live by strict rules; refusing to adapt risks being left behind.Financial control within a family or relationship, such as withholding access to money or hiding resources, is indicated here.Feeling insecure or bored in a role while resisting change, or unwillingness to update outdated methods, may show up here.A relationship colored by controlling behavior or dominance without genuine consent is indicated, sometimes hidden behind excuses about being the one in charge.Resistance to changing long-standing habits, along with health concerns tied to diet and lack of movement, is present here.

Imagery and Symbolism

The King of Pentacles sits on a throne covered in symbols such as bulls and grapes. Rich-colored robes embroidered with grapevines, a symbol of abundance, spill out onto the ground around the figure. An armored left foot rests on top of a carved dragon's head, a symbol of power and prosperity in many cultures. This king's body language shows dominion, personal power, and abundance. A large castle sits in the background, and the figure looks downward at the pentacle resting on their knee with care, someone who takes material responsibilities seriously and ensures everyone is a priority.

Affirmation

I am grounded, and I am safe.

Astrology

This king represents the fixed earth sign Taurus, embodying stability, wealth, and material success. Taurus is ruled by Venus, which brings its energy of comfort, the finer things in life, and nurturing to this card. This king has a natural ability to create and maintain abundance and isn't shy about long-term commitments; the connection to Taurus highlights the role of protector and provider, valuing tradition, hard work, and the rewards that come from perseverance.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What is a long-term goal, and what is the plan to achieve it?
  • How can a better relationship with receiving abundance be developed?

Reversed

  • Has a controlling relationship been experienced in the past? If so, what would healing from it look like?
  • When has financial dishonesty affected life in the past, and what did that reveal about others and oneself?
