Archetypal Meaning

The Sun represents confidence and the emergence of one's true self, stepping out into visibility in full spiritual and physical embodiment.

The Fool's Journey: Joy

Hidden places are illuminated at the Sun's stage: joy. New beginnings, growth, and enlightenment follow, filling the traveler with energy and enthusiasm.

Imagery and Symbolism

Here the Sun beams directly over the heads of twin human figures of indeterminate sex, possibly children. Where the droplets on the Moon card were drawn back into the planet, on the Sun card they fall generously to Earth. Behind the twins stands a wall built from four courses of bricks. The twins recall the astrological sign of Gemini, and it has been said that one stands for the soul, the female and lunar element, and the other for the spirit, the Sun and male energy, so that once again a card points to the union of opposing forces. The hope of the Star that had to be held close to the heart through the tribulations of the lunar landscape in the eighteenth card now comes to fruition, for the Sun heralds the dawn of a new and better day, with nightmares dissolved in the heat of its rays.

The children play innocently in the full light of day, bounded by the solidity of the material world in the wall and bathed in heavenly blessings. They are symbols of the natural self, Adam and Eve in effect, in their state of innocence before they ate the fruit of wisdom, the blessed state into which all of us are born. The Sun card stands for harmony, happiness, a promise fulfilled, and the moment of completion that defines enlightenment.

In many decks, this card instead shows a single young child riding a white horse beneath a wide, radiant sun in tones of yellow and white. The child stands for innocence and growth, while the white horse carries themes of vitality and forward momentum. Sunflowers in the background turn to face the sun, drinking in its energy, and the overall openness of the scene signals that good things are already on their way.

Astrology

This card is ruled by the Sun itself and connected to the Fire sign Leo. The Sun is the center of our solar system, a symbol of vitality and life force without which life on Earth couldn't exist, and this connection brings themes of joy, success, and personal power to the card. Leo's charismatic, warm, and vibrant nature infuses the Sun with confidence and exuberance, suggesting a time of real achievement and clarity. Leo's energy promotes self-expression and creativity, encouraging bold individuality without shying away from the spotlight.
