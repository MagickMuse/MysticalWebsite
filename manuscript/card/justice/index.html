Archetypal Meaning

Justice represents a karmic rebalancing. What has fallen out of harmony within consciousness is brought back into proper relationship with divine love.

The Fool's Journey: Universal Laws

As change continues, understanding grows around universal law at Justice's stage. Responsibility for one's own actions comes into focus here, along with the lessons of cause and effect and a sense of divine order.

Imagery and Symbolism

Justice is often shown holding an upright sword in one hand and a set of balanced scales in the other. The sword represents the clarity needed to cut through illusion and reveal the truth, while the scales reflect the careful weighing of evidence behind every fair decision. Seated on a stone throne, with a stern, composed expression, the figure's firm and deliberate posture reflects the seriousness of law, ethics, and accountability.

Justice is the eighth card of the Major Arcana and the first of the second group of seven cards, which represent the soul and the idea of equilibrium. It shows an authoritative female figure, powerful and assured upon her throne, her seated posture reinforcing her authority. Its most significant emblems are the sword she holds firmly in her right hand, almost as a scepter and a link between Heaven and Earth that implies divine as well as earthly justice, and the scales she grasps in her left hand. Together the sword and scales have become universal symbols of justice.

As the eighth card it implies symmetry, recalling the balanced lemniscate of the infinity sign. The pillars of Jachin and Boaz appear once more in the upright parts of her throne, and at times the figure wears a blindfold to suggest impartiality. She also wears a crown or helmet bearing a solar emblem, a sign of the light of truth. A.E. Waite famously swapped the traditional eighth position of Justice with that of Strength, or force, which sits at number eleven.

Affirmation

I act with my karma in mind.

Astrology

Justice is connected to the Air sign Libra, the sign of the scales. Libra's pursuit of balance, harmony, and fairness mirrors this card's core message, that equilibrium in every area of life, tempered with compassion, leads to truth being received rather than resisted. Libra's diplomatic, even-handed approach reinforces the need to weigh every side of an issue before deciding.
