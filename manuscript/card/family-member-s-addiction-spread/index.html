Family Member's Addiction Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsHealth & HealingShadow Work
◇Question Style
Problem-solving
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether this is a new problem, an ongoing one, or one that's getting worse Card 2: Whether the family member is oblivious to or uncaring of the effects on the family Card 3: Whether you should press for treatment or outside intervention, even trying once more Card 4: Whether you should seek or persevere with outside help for the rest of the family to cope Card 5: Whether you must harden your heart and distance the troublesome relative to protect finances, safety, and peace of mind Card 6: Whether you should need proof of real change before letting down your guard Card 7: What support you can offer without risking yourself or the rest of the family Card 8: Whether you should keep offering access to treatment at intervals, or step back entirely Card 9: What unexpected factor will improve the situation within six to twelve months
◇Description
For a family member's addiction or destructive lifestyle dominating family life, weighing whether the problem is worsening, whether they're oblivious to the damage, pressing for outside treatment, and whether distancing yourself is needed to protect everyone's finances and peace of mind. Best cast when you're let down by false promises yet again.
