Numerology

Number: 6. Keyword: depletion.

In standard numerology, six embodies balance, dependability, perfection, and satisfaction. In cartomancy, however, the Sixes are an exception to that pattern. They almost always carry a negative meaning, indicating a weakening or interruption of the suit's elemental influence. Sixes represent a loss of some kind.

The setback indicated by a Six is temporary rather than irreversible. The condition is already in progress and cannot be entirely avoided. With the more fluid, mutable suits of Spades and Clubs, the situation can often be altered by the querent, though the influence will still be felt. The loss will eventually correct itself or be forgotten with time.

In Readings

The Six of Clubs is one of the more abstract cards. The transformative fires are depleted, and this can manifest in several ways.

First, it can indicate frustration. The Six of Clubs often points to a conflict the querent is angry about but cannot (or does not feel they can) attack directly. Instead, the anger is internalized. It is a slow, smoldering anger that can affect the querent deeply, even physically.

Second, this card can sometimes indicate an actual physical imbalance. Life itself is a process of constant transformation, so illness can be described as a depletion of that fire or energy. The person suffering may not be the querent; it could be a spouse, partner, or someone close. Regardless of who it indicates, the illness can often be averted if steps are taken immediately. Resting and rekindling that transformative fire is the prescription.
