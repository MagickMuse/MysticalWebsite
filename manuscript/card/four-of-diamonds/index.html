Numerology

Number: 4. Keyword: stability.

Fours are steady and reliable, sometimes to the point of stubbornness. They express their suit's elemental influence with more emphasis than Threes and in a way that is less mutable. Changing the influence of a Four can be difficult, though that same stubborn stability becomes a blessing when the card indicates something worth keeping.

Fours carry a low-key energy rather than anything fiery or erratic. Because of the endurance of the number four, the naturally stable suits (Diamonds and Hearts) are especially positive under this influence. The situation indicated by a Four will remain the same unless the querent takes active and persistent steps to create change.

In Readings

Combining fortune with stability produces a card of success. The potential hinted at in the Three of Diamonds has found solid ground here. There is no longer any need to wait; what the querent hopes for is going to happen.

Sometimes called the insurance card, the Four of Diamonds guarantees stability rather than dramatic change. The success it indicates is a manifestation of something already present, not a surprise from out of nowhere. Financially, it can point to a deserved raise or secure investments. If referencing reputation, others will recognize the querent's true character despite any gossip. It can also indicate good health.

The Four of Diamonds can signify understanding and the ability to manage one's circumstances. It speaks of personal stability and reliability in relation to the surrounding world.
