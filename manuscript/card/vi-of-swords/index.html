The upright Six suggests "smooth sailing" ahead, as you leave behind the trauma and sadness of the past. You're embarking on a new path, and the changes you make now are based in the wisdom you've gained through suffering. Sometimes this card symbolizes a journey, either of a physical or spiritual nature, perhaps a "vision quest."You've learned much as a result of past difficulties and now have more clarity that can help you benefit financially. Sometimes this card recommends letting go of an investment or possession. It can also represent relocation or travel for financial reasons.This card shows your work situation improving. Perhaps you've resolved past problems or you've chosen to move to a different position that suits you better. Sometimes the upright Six symbolizes a business trip.The upright Six indicates you and a partner have resolved major problems and arrived at a place of harmony and understanding. Let bygones be bygones. In some cases, this card means leaving an unworkable relationship for something better.
Life transitions can hold as much complexity as simplicity, and as much bittersweetness as joy. Moving forward sometimes requires leaving something important behind, and while that choice may come with hesitation, it is often the wisest path available, with an initial sense of loss eventually settling into acceptance. This card encourages making the hard choices when they arise and moving through whatever rite of passage stands ahead, since something better waits on the other side. Dwelling on what has already been left behind serves little purpose, and starting over, when it becomes the only real option, deserves to be embraced rather than resisted. Reason and logic help in weighing the road already traveled against where it leads next, and drawing on both prior knowledge and intuition sharpens the choices still to be made. On a literal level, this card can point toward travel over water, whether by boat, ferry, or international flight, carrying a touch of sadness over what is left behind alongside real opportunity in the new territory ahead.Financial pressure tends to ease under this card, offering real relief for anyone who has been living paycheck to paycheck. Paying down debt and setting aside a little for something relaxing, like a small trip, feels earned here.Travel and change tend to arrive through work under this card, whether through an out of town conference, a growing network, or a longer term assignment in a new setting. Staying open to it tends to reveal more benefit than expected at first glance.A shared change of scenery, more in the spirit of soul searching than ending things, tends to serve a relationship well here, whether pursued together or apart. Emotional baggage carried over from previous relationships deserves real attention now rather than continued avoidance. Searching for something new feels premature under this card, though a casual connection can still fit comfortably.This card often marks a shift away from illness or mental strain and toward steadier health, even when healing requires leaving familiar patterns behind. A more positive outlook paired with long term strategies, whether through therapy, a change of environment, or distance from ongoing stress, supports that continued improvement.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
The reversed Six can mean you're unwilling or unable to move away from a troubling situation. Perhaps you fear taking a leap of faith and leaving your old ways behind. Outdated attitudes may hold you back, however. Delays, obstacles, or other interferences, including input from other people, can also complicate your plans.The reversed Six can mean you're leaving behind losses or a bad investment. In some cases, however, it suggests you're stuck in an old pattern or regretting a financial decision that didn't turn out well. Don't keep throwing good money after bad.This card can indicate you're reluctant to move on after a painful experience. Perhaps you think you're at a dead end or doubt you can recover from a hardship, but this Six encourages you to marshal your energies and find a place where you can flourish.The Six reversed warns against sticking with old, outworn beliefs and behaviors out of fear. Unhappiness results when you're unwilling to make changes that awaken your heart, mind, and spirit to new possibilities.
Attempts to separate from a painful past and move forward can meet real resistance here, as though something keeps pulling backward. Avoiding old issues instead of facing them tends to bring them back around quickly, and this position points to unfinished business that genuinely needs closing out. Gathering the tools and support already available opens the door to real freedom once that avoidance ends. This reversal can also describe resisting a change that has already begun, one that may feel forced through a relocation, a partner's decision, or another outside influence, even though shifting perspective can reveal real value hidden inside that unwanted change.An idea that has been pushed for a while may simply not be working, and setting it aside in favor of something more productive serves better right now.Solutions at work may feel out of reach at the moment, making outside help worth seeking rather than trying to manage everything alone. Past work being reviewed or audited is also possible under this reversal.A tempting message from an ex is worth weighing carefully, since the past relationship it references is rarely as good as memory paints it. Reflecting honestly, or checking old journals or trusted friends for a reality check, helps here. This position can also mark real readiness to finally release a dramatic relationship, even after significant time and energy invested in it.Resisting a needed health change tends to keep things stuck rather than resolve them, making it worth reflecting on why the status quo still feels safer. This reversal can also point to delayed recovery or a relapse into old, unhelpful patterns. Self forgiveness, followed by small and steady positive steps, offers the better way forward.

Imagery and Symbolism

Three figures, often two adults and a child, appear moving across a body of water toward land not far ahead, facing the shoreline as a sign of leaving the past behind. Six swords stand upright within the boat, and while one stretch of water churns with turbulence, the water ahead grows calmer, suggesting gentler times to come as these travelers put distance between themselves and old turmoil.

Affirmation

I move into the future with a clean slate and open mind.

Astrology

The Six of Swords is tied to Mercury in Aquarius, with Mercury lending flexibility and movement to Aquarius and opening new options for a better future. That positive shift is not handed over freely; it comes earned through hard work and experiences that were not always pleasant. Knowledge gained from the past supports a wider view of what lies ahead, paired with sharpened wits and a real need for close observation.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • What does being a true friend to the self look like in practice?
  • What negative stories about the self or past experiences feel ready to be released?

Reversed

  • What negative outcomes followed past resistance to a needed change?
  • Which people nearby would genuinely offer help if asked?
