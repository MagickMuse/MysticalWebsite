Becoming Engaged Spread

◇Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsDecision Making
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether to wait for a proposal or initiate it yourself Card 2: Whether you want rings or an official announcement Card 3: Whether you want a big celebration or something more low-key Card 4: Whether to set a wedding date now or leave it open Card 5: Whether becoming engaged will improve or stabilize the relationship Card 6: How to avoid or deal with unexpected pressures
◇Description
Drawn from the full deck on Valentine's Day, New Year's Day, or any significant anniversary in the relationship, when you feel ready to tell the world you are a couple, these six cards weigh who should initiate the proposal, rings and announcements, the scale of celebration, whether to set a wedding date, whether engagement will strengthen the relationship, and how to handle unexpected pressure.
