Card Grimoire

Volunteer for Charity Overseas Spread

Spread Type
Career & MoneyJustice & Ethics
Question Style
Open-ended
Card Details
Card 1: Whether you're prepared to volunteer, even with responsibilities or people saying you shouldn't go Card 2: Whether you need extra training or voluntary vacation projects first Card 3: Whether you're committed to one country, age group, or community, or willing to go anywhere Card 4: Whether you have the resources to support yourself before expenses or fares are paid Card 5: Whether you're prepared to deal with bureaucracy and difficult officials as you get established Card 6: Whether this is all or nothing, regardless of tough conditions and dangers Card 7: Whether you'll go with a partner or friend, or want to go alone Card 8: Whether this is a permanent life change, or something you'll take project by project
Description
For knowing you could do more than fundraise from afar and wanting to volunteer overseas or in a community in need, weighing responsibilities that hold you back, the training required, and whether to commit to one place or go wherever needed. Best cast when something in the news reminds you of this desire.
