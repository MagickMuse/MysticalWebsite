Card Grimoire

First Holiday Without the Family Spread

Spread Type
Love & Relationships
Question Style
Open-ended
Card Details
Card 1: Whether you're fully confident everything is in order at home so you won't worry Card 2: Whether you've made a pact not to talk or think about family, bills, or work Card 3: Whether mobile devices are disabled except for emergencies, so you can enjoy peace and each other Card 4: Whether everyone, including your workplace, knows you're only to be contacted in a dire emergency Card 5: Whether you're staying somewhere adult-only, or planning activities the kids would hate Card 6: Whether you're prepared to get to know yourself and your partner again, free of parental labels
Description
For a first vacation without the kids or family, checking whether everything at home is genuinely covered, whether devices are off apart from emergencies, and whether you're ready to rediscover yourself and your partner away from parental roles. Best cast just prior to going on holiday.
