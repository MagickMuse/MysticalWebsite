Major Project Management Spread

Spread Type
Career & Money
Question Style
Open-ended
Card Details
Card 1: What you'll gain most from this challenge Card 2: What qualities you need to lead this project Card 3: What qualities of compromise you need to bring a team together so everyone feels valued Card 4: What difficulties or difficult people you anticipate standing in your way Card 5: The short-term advantages of succeeding Card 6: The next step after the project's successful completion
Description
For testing your readiness to successfully manage a major project when you've been given a big chance but fear falling short, weighing leadership qualities, team compromise, and the obstacles standing in your way. Best cast at the full moon, or on any Monday, for dreams fulfilled.
