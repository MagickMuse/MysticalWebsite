Numerology

Number: 6. Keyword: depletion.

In standard numerology, six embodies balance, dependability, perfection, and satisfaction. In cartomancy, however, the Sixes are an exception to that pattern. They almost always carry a negative meaning, indicating a weakening or interruption of the suit's elemental influence. Sixes represent a loss of some kind.

The setback indicated by a Six is temporary rather than irreversible. The condition is already in progress and cannot be entirely avoided, but the loss will eventually correct itself or be forgotten with time.

In Readings

The Six of Diamonds indicates a loss of money or damaged reputation. Unlike other Diamond cards, the Six almost always points specifically to finances or reputation rather than luck or health. The loss is typically neither chronic nor catastrophic, making it one of the less severe negative cards.

Context matters. Adjacent cards shape the severity. Next to the Ten of Spades, it could signal financial ruin. Near Diamond court cards, someone may be speaking ill of the querent behind their back.

The loss is often caused by a single, finite incident: misplaced cash, a lost receipt, or an embarrassing moment that temporarily tarnishes reputation but is eventually forgotten. This card rarely predicts something that can be prevented. Often the loss has already occurred or the events leading to it are already in motion.
