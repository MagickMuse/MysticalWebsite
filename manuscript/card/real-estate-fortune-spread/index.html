Real Estate Fortune Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & Money
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether you trust your intuition for good bargains at auctions or on repossessions Card 2: How you can access fast funds to seal deals Card 3: Whether you can renovate it yourself or find reasonably priced, efficient renovators Card 4: Whether you're prepared to make your own home part of the sales equation Card 5: Whether you can recognize bargains in up-and-coming areas Card 6: Whether you have, or can gain, access to self-building on land that will increase in value Card 7: Whether you should invest in commercial property or land even without immediate value Card 8: Whether you can hold your nerve in bargaining and know when to press for advantage
◇Description
For building a fortune in real estate, whether you're still seeking a foothold in the market or already own a first property, weighing intuition at auctions, access to fast funds, affordable renovation help, and when to invest in commercial land. Best cast when a property acquisition is within reach.
