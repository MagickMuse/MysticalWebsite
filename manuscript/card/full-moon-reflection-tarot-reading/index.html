Overview

At the full moon, the moon sits opposite the sun. Some feel the tension between these forces as conflict or drama; others feel empowered by the moon shining in her full radiance. This spread draws on that dynamic, using the reflective light of the cards the same way the moon reflects the sun — to illuminate what is hidden and reveal what is ready to shift. You can also cast it at the New Moon to divine what that lunation ahead may hold.

Best Timing

Full Moon, New Moon, or Lunar Eclipse

Lunar Energy

Reflection · Insight · Personal Growth

You Will Need

  • Tarot or oracle deck, or other divination tools of choice

Directions

  1. If possible, sit where you can experience the moonlight. You may also like to hold a lunar crystal and tune into its energy.
  2. Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths. Exhale tension and worry; breathe in peace and lunar clarity. With each breath, tune more deeply into your body and into any emotions or patterns that have surfaced with the lunation. Acknowledge them, and breathe them out.
  3. Savor — or visualize — the moonlight shining upon you, illuminating the dark and the answers to your questions. When you feel ready, draw your cards in the following positions:
