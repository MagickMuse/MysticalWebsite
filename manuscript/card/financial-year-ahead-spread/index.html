Financial Year Ahead Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & MoneySeasonal
◇Question Style
General guidance
◇Card Details
Card 1: Financial opportunities or challenges for the current month Card 2: Financial opportunities or challenges for month two Card 3: Financial opportunities or challenges for month three Card 4: Financial opportunities or challenges for month four Card 5: Financial opportunities or challenges for month five Card 6: Financial opportunities or challenges for month six Card 7: Financial opportunities or challenges for month seven Card 8: Financial opportunities or challenges for month eight Card 9: Financial opportunities or challenges for month nine Card 10: Financial opportunities or challenges for month ten Card 11: Financial opportunities or challenges for month eleven Card 12: Financial opportunities or challenges for month twelve Center Card (optional): A card summing up the financial year ahead
◇Description
For forecasting your financial position across the year ahead, laying cards in a clockwise circle with each one representing a month starting from the current one, then choosing a final card for the center to sum up the whole year. Best started at the current month, or the following one if the current month is nearly over.
