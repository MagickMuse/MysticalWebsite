When the King of Cups appears upright, he represents strong emotions and creative talent. If he represents a real person, it's someone who wants to be loved and admired, he may go to great lengths to gain your attention and affection. Although he's easily hurt, he may not show it and can hold a grudge. He can be rather indulgent and controlling, but his friends can count on his support and protection.In a reading about money, the King can mean receiving money through a creative venture. Or, it may suggest your emotions are influencing a financial matter. Sometimes it represents a financial backer.If the reading is about your job, the King can describe a creative occupation. However, he may indicate you're longing for something better. Perhaps you've grown complacent.In a question about love, this card represents a deeply emotional, committed, and romantic relationship. It can suggest jealousy and possessiveness, however, or a need for more trust and openness.
Strong emotional depth and creative talent define this figure; recognition and affection are wanted, hurt runs deep even when hidden, and loyalty and protection are offered generously to those closest to him.Financial gain often comes through creative ventures, and emotions may be quietly shaping a money decision; a financial backer could also be indicated.A creative occupation or role fits this energy well, though complacency may be masking a desire for something more fulfilling.A deeply emotional, committed, and romantic relationship is indicated, though jealousy and possessiveness can surface without enough trust and openness.Setting up an environment that supports wellness goals matters here; household members who fall short of support should not become an excuse for giving up on personal healing.
Reversed GeneralReversed MoneyReversed EmploymentReversed LoveReversed Health
The reversed King shows emotional turmoil, insecurity, and defensiveness. If he represents a real person, he can be manipulative, emotionally controlling, and vindictive. This card suggests dissatisfaction and frustration, perhaps because you aren't using your creativity.In a reading about money, the reversed King can show losses resulting from decisions made emotionally rather than rationally. Sometimes it indicates laziness, lack of direction, or an indulgent nature that lands you in debt.If the reading is about your job, this card suggests territorialism and power struggles. You may fear losing ground and try to control others. Secrecy, duplicity, and manipulation may exist in your workplace.In a question about love, the reversed King can mean fear of expressing love and caring. Jealousy, defensiveness, and/or emotional outbursts may cause problems. Perhaps you feel insecure in a partnership.
Emotional turmoil, insecurity, and defensiveness surface here; manipulation, emotional control, or vindictiveness may be present, often stemming from unused creativity and resulting frustration.Losses may follow decisions made from emotion rather than reason, and laziness, lack of direction, or overindulgence could be adding to debt.Territorial behavior and power struggles are at play; secrecy, duplicity, and manipulation may be circulating in the workplace.Fear of expressing love and care blocks connection here; jealousy, defensiveness, or emotional outbursts point to underlying insecurity in the relationship.Shame and unworthiness may be pulling toward risky situations or people who feed old patterns; facing this shadow work with proper support, such as therapy for addiction or trauma, is recommended.

Imagery and Symbolism

The king sits on a sturdy throne surrounded by water, undisturbed by being in the middle of the ocean. Waves, seaweed, and jumping fish in the background reflect his stillness and mastery over his element and his emotions. His upright, composed posture reflects inner strength and a thoughtful, caring nature; he takes his role seriously and understands his place in the world.

Affirmation

I fully understand the power of my emotions.

Astrology

This king represents the fixed water sign of Scorpio, carrying emotional depth, maturity, and mastery into an intense energy that manifests as strong leadership. Scorpio, ruled by transformative Pluto, brings intensity, passion, and resilience to this card. Its fixed nature emphasizes steadfastness and loyalty, reflecting a commitment to emotional integrity and authenticity.

Journal Prompts

  • What would being one's own protector look like right now?
