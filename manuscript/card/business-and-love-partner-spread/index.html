Business and Love Partner Spread

Spread Type
Career & MoneyLove & Relationships
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: Whether this is symptomatic of your emotional relationship Card 2: Whether it's the situation or structure of the business causing the issue Card 3: Whether your partner will compromise or listen to reason Card 4: What issues you cannot or will not compromise on Card 5: Whether there's an obvious intermediary Card 6: Whether there's an alternative role in the business you could fill Card 7: Whether you're essential to the business, or could find another job Card 8: Whether you could or should run your own business alongside it, or separately
Description
For working through feeling overridden at every turn when your business partner is also your love partner, weighing whether the issue is emotional or structural and whether an alternate role, separate business, or intermediary could help. Best cast on the last day of the month.
