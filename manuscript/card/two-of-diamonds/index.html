Numerology

Number: 2. Keyword: contrast.

Deuces represent opposition and extremes: hot and cold, good and evil, high and low. The duality is not necessarily antagonistic, but contrast is inherent. They function as wild cards in a reading, less connected to their suit's elemental association than other pip cards and more likely to influence adjacent cards. Deuces can also indicate balance, as opposites tend to cycle back toward equilibrium. They always point to unexpected developments rather than carefully planned outcomes.

In Readings

The Two of Diamonds is less influenced by its suit than other Diamond cards. It indicates a surprise, an unexpected event or outcome. Adjacent cards clarify the nature of the surprise. If other Diamond cards surround it, the surprise involves money or reputation. If no other fortune cards are nearby, the surprise could take almost any form. The Two of Diamonds breaks apart the old order to make way for the new.

The change may be pleasant or unpleasant but is never catastrophic. Without other Deuces nearby, it points to a small shift. When it falls beside one or more other Deuces, the change intensifies dramatically.
