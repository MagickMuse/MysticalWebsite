Card Grimoire

Aging Parent's Independence Spread

Spread Type
Love & RelationshipsHealth & Healing
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: Whether daily help, overseen by you, could let your relative stay in their own home Card 2: Whether a conversion or extension could give them independence with protection Card 3: Whether suggesting sheltered accommodation now, even if rejected, plants a useful seed Card 4: Whether there's someone the older person listens to who can discuss the future Card 5: Whether other relatives or social care should step up, if you've always done everything Card 6: Whether you should temporarily step back if the older person is very demanding Card 7: The best possible outcome for everyone concerned
Description
For an aging parent or grandparent who can't live alone much longer but refuses to give up independence and wouldn't thrive moving in with you, weighing daily home help, modifications for safety, sheltered accommodation, and whether other relatives need to step up. Best cast when the moon passes through Capricorn, or on a Saturday, day of older people.
