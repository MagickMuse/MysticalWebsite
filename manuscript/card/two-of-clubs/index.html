Numerology

Number: 2. Keyword: contrast.

Deuces represent opposition and extremes: hot and cold, good and evil, high and low. The duality is not necessarily antagonistic, but contrast is inherent. They function as wild cards in a reading, less connected to their suit's elemental association than other pip cards and more likely to influence adjacent cards. Deuces can also indicate balance, as opposites tend to cycle back toward equilibrium. They always point to unexpected developments rather than carefully planned outcomes.

In Readings

When the Two of Clubs appears, the querent can expect some emotional release. The Deuces always indicate surprising events, but with the Two of Clubs, those events are likely to elicit a deeply emotional response. This may seem more appropriate for the suit of Hearts, but the Two of Clubs does not generate emotion; it transforms emotion.

As a solitary card, the Two of Clubs simply indicates something unexpected is going to happen. In a multiple-card reading, however, it usually influences one or more adjacent cards. Next to the Six of Diamonds, the querent may lose money due to unexpected events. Next to the Four of Hearts, it suggests the querent will be surprised by one or more friends.
