Card Grimoire

Sabbatical Spread

◇Spread Type
Decision Making
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Whether the timing and circumstances are right Card 2: How the trip will be financed, and whether work is needed while away Card 3: Whether a career can be put on hold without damage Card 4: Whether to plan a definite itinerary, or stay open to opportunities Card 5: How to stay in touch with loved ones, and who might come along Card 6: The real purpose: seeing new places, or taking time out from the present life Card 7: Whether this marks a major turning point toward a different life path on return
◇Description
Drawn from the full deck on a Monday for overseas travel or a Thursday for interstate travel, when planning a major extended trip away but worried about leaving current life behind, these seven cards weigh timing and circumstance, financing, whether career can be safely put on hold, planning an itinerary versus staying open to opportunity, keeping in touch with loved ones, the real underlying purpose of the trip, and whether this marks a major turning point toward a different life path.
