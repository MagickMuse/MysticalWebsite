Wheel of the Year Spread

Spread Type
Seasonal
Question Style
General guidance
Card Details
Card 1: Month 1, the month following the reading: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 2: Month 2: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 3: Month 3: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 4: Month 4: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 5: Month 5: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 6: Month 6: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 7: Month 7: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 8: Month 8: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 9: Month 9: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 10: Month 10: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 11: Month 11: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 12: Month 12: opportunities and challenges ahead Card 13 (optional, placed in the center): Summary of the year as a whole
Description
Using one or two full decks, one card is chosen for each of the twelve months ahead and laid out clockwise in a circle starting with the month following the reading, with a final card placed in the center to sum up the year as a whole. Best begun during the current month, or the following one if the current month is nearly over, and especially suited to a birthday month, the new year, or any personal turning point such as an anniversary, engagement, wedding, birth, or breakup. Major Arcana cards in any month point to major events or outside circumstances, Minor cards to smaller but still significant happenings, and Court cards to dominant personalities, new love, or pregnancy. Two cards can be drawn for each month for more detail.
