Card 1: The answer or general impression
◇Description
This single-card draw offers a fast read on a simple, often yes-or-no question, such as whether to attend an event or how a day is likely to unfold. Rather than a detailed narrative, it delivers a general impression of the energy surrounding the question, and it also works well as a quick daily practice for building familiarity with a deck.
