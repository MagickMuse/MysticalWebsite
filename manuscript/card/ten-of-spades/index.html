Numerology

Number: 10 (reduces to 1). Keyword: force.

In numerology, multi-digit numbers are reduced to a single digit by adding their digits together. The number 10 (1 + 0) reduces to 1, creating similarities between the Tens and the Aces. Like the Aces, the Tens are aggressive cards that express their suit's elemental influence explicitly.

The difference is that Tens lack the triumphant quality of Aces. Aces hold the highest value in card games, while Tens are trumped by Aces and all three court cards. A Ten is always forceful but not necessarily triumphant for the querent. The Ten of Spades is particularly unwelcome in a reading. Surrounding cards help clarify how the situation will affect the querent.

In Readings

The Ten of Spades is a force as powerful as the Ace of this suit, but instead of bringing triumph, it brings sorrow and melancholy. Whether the predicted outcome is violent or subtle, it is always sad. This card never has a positive interpretation.

The sorrow may not be directed at the querent. If cards have been drawn to find out why someone close has been acting distant, this card indicates that person is dealing with personal issues. If the Ten of Spades falls next to the Jack of Hearts, somebody close to the querent will be struck with sorrow.

As a solitary card, the Ten of Spades often indicates events affecting only the querent, and this isolation can be accompanied by an intense feeling of loneliness. The loneliness may or may not be connected to other sorrowful events described by the same card. The Ten of Spades mirrors the Ace of this suit as its exact opposite: where the Ace represents conquest, the Ten represents defeat.
