Zodiac Wheel Tarot Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
General guidance
◇Card Details
Card 1: Initiative and Action (Aries, March 21–April 19) Card 2: Stability and Security (Taurus, April 20–May 20) Card 3: Communication and Adaptability (Gemini, May 21–June 20) Card 4: Emotions and Home Life (Cancer, June 21–July 22) Card 5: Creativity and Self-Expression (Leo, July 23–August 22) Card 6: Work and Health (Virgo, August 23–September 22) Card 7: Relationships and Balance (Libra, September 23–October 22) Card 8: Transformation and Depth (Scorpio, October 23–November 21) Card 9: Adventure and Philosophy (Sagittarius, November 22–December 21) Card 10: Ambition and Discipline (Capricorn, December 22–January 19) Card 11: Innovation and Community (Aquarius, January 20–February 18) Card 12: Intuition and Spirituality (Pisces, February 19–March 20)
◇Description
This twelve-card spread draws on the qualities, challenges, and energies of each zodiac sign to examine different aspects of life comprehensively, covering creativity, relationships, career, and spirituality. It works well for self-reflection, gaining insight into a specific situation, understanding how different energies interplay in life, or as a forecast reading for each sign's season using the timing given for each position. When interpreting the cards, reflect on how each one relates to the key energies of its sign and that particular aspect of life.
