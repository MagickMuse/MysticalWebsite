Shopaholic Spread

Spread Type
Career & Money
Question Style
Problem-solving
Card Details
Card 1: Whether the problem is increasing and causing real financial worries Card 2: The underlying cause of the problem Card 3: The short-term payoff of the shopaholic behavior Card 4: Whether the problem can be curbed informally Card 5: Whether professional help is necessary or advisable
Description
For addressing wasteful spending on items that are never used or opened, whether yours or a partner's, tracing the underlying cause and whether the habit can be curbed informally or needs professional help. Best cast during a waning moon.
