Remember the saying, "When the student is ready, the teacher appears"? When this card is upright, you may be looking for guidance from the invisible world. You want to gain perspective on your life, and you're open to the inner guidance that's available to you. The time has come to reunite with the Source, whether for guidance, to discover the truth, or to establish inner balance.In a reading about money, The Hermit offers guidance in a financial matter. He may encourage you to seek counsel from someone wiser or more experienced than yourself. This card can also mean leaving a job or company and going your own way, independently.In a reading about love, you may feel a need for some time alone to discover things about yourself and your path in life. The Hermit can also indicate a need for more independence or self-reliance in a relationship.
The Hermit invites you inward, asking you to trust the wisdom your own reflection can offer rather than seeking every answer from outside voices. A period of intentional withdrawal, quiet time, solitude, stepping back from the noise, helps you reach a more mature, grounded understanding of your path.Financially, this is a moment to seek guidance from someone with more experience, or to step back from material concerns and reconnect with a more values-driven approach to money.At work, you may feel the pull to step away from the daily grind in favor of independence, deeper focus, or simply going your own way rather than following the crowd.In relationships, you may need real time alone to understand yourself and what you want, or find that more independence within a relationship serves you well right now.Turn inward and prioritize rest and reflection over rushing your healing. The Hermit favors mindful, deliberate care over quick recoveries.

Archetypal Meaning

The Hermit marks a time when the soul must walk alone through darkness, guided by the divine and the inner light of spirit.

The Fool's Journey: Inner Truth

As maturity deepens, attention turns inward at the Hermit's stage: inner truth. Asking why leads to the Hermit, who guides the search for deeper answers and inner understanding.

Imagery and Symbolism

Many depictions of the Hermit show a robed figure carrying a lantern across a barren, snow-capped mountain landscape, its lack of greenery emphasizing how high and difficult the path is. As night swallows the last light, only the lantern remains visible, a symbol of illumination drawn from inner knowledge and spiritual growth rather than the world outside. The Hermit stands as a lone figure who understands that everyone needs to withdraw at times to integrate lessons and reflect, offering a living example of wisdom gained through experience and contemplation.

The ninth numbered card of the Major Arcana shows an old man whose beard marks his wisdom and experience, carrying a staff in his left hand and a lit lantern in the other. He holds the lamp aloft as if to light a path, a sign of surrounding darkness, yet the lantern of the hermit, usually a figure of spiritual leaning, may speak of inner illumination as much as outer. He is hooded and faces left, looking back toward the cards that come before him, almost as though he lights the way for all the figures who precede him. The lover of the sixth card returns here in the ninth position, turned about by a hundred and eighty degrees, no longer caught in a quandary over his choices but assured in himself and content to be alone, seeking the path of spiritual enlightenment. The staff serves him as a tool, a sign that the journey is not yet at an end despite his age and experience. When the God Woden takes human form he often appears as a shabby old man in a battered hat, and the hermit likewise stands for wisdom and a life dedicated to a higher authority.

Tarot-Numerology

Positive: Complete expression, deep understanding, charity, cycle ending

Negative: Emotionalism, dissipation, egocentricity, indiscretion, aimless dreaming

Affirmation

I allow peace and silence into my life daily.

Astrology

The Hermit is connected to the Earth sign Virgo, a sign known for its analytical mind, attention to detail, and sense of duty. Virgo's comfort with solitude and its drive toward meticulous, self-reliant planning mirror the Hermit's own need for withdrawal and careful reflection.

Journal Prompts

  • What is my inner truth?
  • Are my actions aligned with my values?

Reversed

  • What have I been avoiding for too long that requires addressing head-on?

Major Arcana Name Card

If The Hermit is your Major Arcana Name Card: You're introspective and wise, seeking inner truth and deeper meaning in life. You may feel drawn to moments of solitude for reflection and growth. This card suggests you're a guide for others even as you navigate your own path.
