Imagery and Symbolism

A figure faces seven cups arranged on a cloud, each holding something different to choose from. Traditional imagery includes a snake, a shrouded figure, a human head, laurels, a pile of wealth, a tower, and a dragon, some grounded in reality and others purely fantastical. All that remains is for the figure to make a choice.

Affirmation

I confidently make decisions that are right for me and my life.

Astrology

The Seven of Cups carries the energy of Venus in Scorpio, pulling desires and fantasies to the forefront. Things are not always what they appear to be, and intense feelings can drive choices made from emotional temptation. Opportunities may slip away unnoticed while the heart chases something larger than life.

Journal Prompts

Upright

  • How are decisions usually made when more than one option is on the table?
  • What dream is actively being worked toward, or deserves to be?

Reversed

  • When has delusion crept into a past situation, and what did that reveal?
  • What are some healthy ways to unwind and regulate emotions?
