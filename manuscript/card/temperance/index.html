Archetypal Meaning

Temperance is a card of integration, transformation, and alchemy. It carries the meaning of opposites blending into a new element entirely.

The Fool's Journey: Moderation

After that transformation, balance, stability, and harmony return at Temperance's stage: moderation. Equilibrium becomes the focus, along with tending to one's own well being.

Imagery and Symbolism

Temperance is often shown with one foot dipped into a pool of water and the other planted on dry land, balancing the subconscious realm of water with the grounded, practical world. She holds two chalices, pouring water between them in a flow that never stops, and alchemical symbols such as the square, triangle, and sun appear throughout the card. Spring daffodils and a new dawn in the background point toward renewal on the horizon.

Temperance is one of the cardinal virtues, speaking of self-restraint, care, and moderation, and of an easygoing nature wise enough to see the bigger picture rather than be caught up in petty concerns. The card shows a female figure, often winged as a messenger from God, who wears a five-petaled flower in her hair that frequently hides the meaning of the five-pointed star or pentacle. Her key feature, though, is the pair of jugs she holds, pouring liquid from one into the other. One jug is blue and the other red, and an alchemical process is at work, for blue, a feminine color, mixed with masculine red yields violet, the color of spirit. Seen this way the flower is far more than decoration, since five stands for marriage or the union of opposites, the female number two wedded to the male number three.

The card's imagery bears a striking resemblance to the astrological sign of Aquarius, which is linked to the element of air and to the circulation of the blood, the life-forces. Temperance may well be a reminder of rebirth and reincarnation, for it is not only liquid that the woman pours so carefully from one vessel to the other but, symbolically, the soul.

Tarot-Numerology

Number: 14 (reduces to 5)

As 5 – Positive: Change, versatility, freedom, progress, curiosity, life experience

As 5 – Negative: Irresponsibility, procrastination, carelessness, self-indulgence

Affirmation

I bring myself back into balance.

Astrology

Temperance is connected to the Fire sign Sagittarius, a sign known for optimism and a drive toward growth and exploration. Sagittarius encourages mixing diverse elements to find a new equilibrium, much like an archer aiming for higher ideals, pushing this card toward openness, adaptability, and deeper understanding.
