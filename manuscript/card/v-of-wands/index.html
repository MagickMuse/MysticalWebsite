Power struggles tend to be at play when this card appears, with everyone convinced they know best and should be the one in charge. Listening and reason often fall away in these moments, leaving actions taken with little regard for effectiveness. Agitating as these times can be, they remain largely unavoidable, testing both character and patience. Heated disagreements and situations without consensus show up here too, as every party holds firmly to a point of view and no one yields easily. These tensions will not last forever, though navigating them well takes time and diplomacy, and offers a real opportunity to reach the root cause of the conflict and find true resolution.Arguments over money are also likely to surface when this card appears.Difficulty setting aside personal wants tends to stall progress among colleagues under this card, calling for ego to be checked at the door in favor of what serves the team over the individual. Staying out of workplace drama and keeping a low profile serves well when tension is running high.Bickering and fighting for no clear reason often accompanies this card in romantic and relationship matters, with every small thing a partner or close friend does feeling irritating even when the reaction is known to be unreasonable. Some alone time tends to help here, whether through connecting with friends, carving out personal space, or finding another way to relieve stress rather than taking it out on loved ones. For those dating, this card serves as a red flag worth paying attention to, since instincts are likely correct.Stress is taking a toll on health and well being under this card, calling for a way to release built up tension in the body before it accumulates into later issues. Stress related concerns can include kidney stones, ulcers, stress headaches, and insomnia, among others.

Imagery and Symbolism

Five figures holding five wands appear caught in a scuffle beneath a cloudless blue sky, standing on solid ground. Each figure holds a different stance, lending a sense of continuous movement to the scene. Though the imagery can look negative at first glance, closer inspection reveals no injuries or harm, only clear opposition and tension between the group.

Affirmation

I enjoy healthy competition and face it head-on.

Astrology

The Five of Wands carries the influence of Saturn in Leo. Saturn holds the energy of rules and restriction, and Leo's bold presence resists yielding to those rules easily, creating a lively friction between the two. The ego finds itself challenged under this pairing, and the results can be less than graceful.

Journal Prompts

  • What are some ways stress could be released from the body?

  • What might encourage less avoidance going forward?
