Court Cards

Court cards, also called face cards, depict human faces. Though influenced by elemental associations like the rest of the deck, court cards almost always represent people who can influence the querent directly through their actions and decisions. The elemental influence of the suit shapes the personality and energy of the person the card represents.

Jacks represent younger, less mature people regardless of gender or archetypal role. These are individuals who influence the querent, so a Jack rarely indicates a child unless it is the querent's own adult child.

For querents over forty, Jacks usually point to younger people. Otherwise, a Jack might indicate someone the querent's own age but less experienced. For very young querents in their early teens, a Jack could even represent an actual child.

Further distinctions are a matter of perspective and vary from one reading to another. It is up to the reader to determine how a court card should be interpreted based on who they are and who they are reading for.

In Readings

With the suit of Clubs, court cards represent people at different stages of personal transformation. The Jack of Clubs is a person in the "before" stage, someone who very much wants to move forward. This person is ambitious and may even be perceived as aggressive and annoying, but they are not necessarily hostile toward the querent.

Because this is a less experienced person, the Jack of Clubs is often someone younger than the querent. This is almost always the case if the querent is forty or older. For younger querents, the Jack could be the same age or even older, but they will be subordinate to the querent in some way.

This person is someone who can affect or influence the querent, so it is wise to keep an eye out for an ambitious individual whose goals could either help or harm the querent.
