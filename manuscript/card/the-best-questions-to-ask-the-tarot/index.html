The Best Questions to Ask the Tarot

Introduction

The quality of a question shapes the quality of a reading. A clear, well-formed question makes it easier to draw the right information from the cards in any layout. Sometimes no specific question comes to mind, and those open readings can end up being deeply transformational. When a particular topic calls for exploration, clear, open-ended questions tend to work best.

The prompts below can be used in a personal tarot practice or with querents, adjusted freely to fit the situation at hand. They can also help reshape a querent's vague question into something a reading can actually answer.

Love and Romance

  • What is the romantic potential of this relationship?
  • What is the challenge or lesson in this current relationship?
  • What will be the likely outcome of pursuing a relationship with this person?
  • What actions can help improve this relationship?
  • What obstacles is this relationship likely to face in the future?
  • How romantically compatible is this pairing?
  • If an ex returns, would trying again be worth it for future happiness?
  • Will this relationship work out as desired if another chance is given?

Family and Friends

  • What is the current family or friend situation trying to teach?
  • How can a child, friend, or family member best be supported through their current situation?
  • What is the underlying cause of this family or child's situation?
  • How can the situation with children or family be improved?

Career and Work

  • Is this career the best fit for these talents and this sense of happiness?
  • What can be done to ensure success in this work or career?
  • What does the future hold if this position is kept?
  • What is the lesson being learned from this career or workplace?
  • What is blocking success in this career?

Money and Finances

  • What can be done to improve this financial situation?
  • What steps are needed to increase financial wealth?
  • What is blocking financial success right now?
  • How can a better perspective and relationship with money be created?

Health and Happiness

  • What habits are being held onto that are holding back progress in health?
  • What action can be taken to work toward better health?
  • What can be done to increase current happiness?
  • What is the best way to support this healing?
  • What part of the shadow requires healing and integration right now?

Spiritual and Personal Development

  • Will the current path lead to long-term happiness?
  • What needs to be resolved to move forward happily?
  • What do spirit guides want known about the current situation?
  • What spiritual support can be sought to aid this journey?
