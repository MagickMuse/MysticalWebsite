Numerology

Number: 1. Keyword: power.

Aces are emphatic and powerful cards that strongly express their suit's elemental influence. They carry a more general meaning than other pip cards due to their forceful nature. An Ace's message may be further defined by surrounding cards, but its meaning is never overwhelmed or negated. Aces are almost always a positive omen, carrying creative and independent energy.

In Readings

The Ace of Clubs is the ultimate power card, a card of achievement. It describes the querent as having a strong reputation and a secure place in their social unit. That unit can be almost anything: family, local government, neighborhood, or coven, as long as it is important to the querent.

With the force of this Ace, a transformation has already occurred within the querent. A part of their destiny has achieved, or will soon achieve, actualization. Picture the Club as a scepter held by a monarch.

In a multiple-card reading, the Ace of Clubs may describe someone else in the querent's life. It depends on where the card falls and what surrounds it. Even so, the person who has come or will come into this power is almost always an ally, so the card is still welcome. If this Ace bears any warning, it might be a gentle reminder of how easily one could fall from a high pedestal. Overall, though, the Ace of Clubs brings a positive message.
