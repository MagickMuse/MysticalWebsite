Card Grimoire

Tower of Freedom Spread

◇Spread Type
Decision MakingShadow Work
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: The freedom or independence you seek, and in what way Card 2: What or who restricts you from having it Card 3: Whether now is the right time, or whether to wait Card 4: The first step to freedom or independence Card 5: The immediate positive effects of attaining it, on yourself and others Card 6: Objections or obstacles that will arise in the early days Card 7: What must be sacrificed to attain freedom or independence Card 8: The longer-term positive effects within six to twelve months Card 9: Where you will be in five years if you follow this path
◇Description
Drawn from the full deck at the start of a new month or year, for breaking free of restrictions to live by one's own rules, when something no longer brings happiness but letting go feels frightening, these nine cards are laid out ascending diagonally like a leaning tower. They explore the freedom sought, what restricts it, whether the timing is right, the first step, the immediate positive effects, early obstacles, what must be sacrificed, the longer-term benefits within six to twelve months, and where the path leads within five years.
