Increase My Cash Flow Spread

◇Spread Type
Career & Money
◇Question Style
Specific situation
◇Card Details
Card 1: Current financial energy Card 2: Money mindset Card 3: Money opportunities Card 4: Money blocks Card 5: Money ally Card 6: Action plan Card 7: Money forecast
◇Description
This seven-card spread offers insight into growing financial abundance. After shuffling and cutting the deck, draw seven cards and lay them left to right. The first card reveals the present financial situation and the energy surrounding money matters, while the second uncovers attitudes, beliefs, and subconscious thoughts about money. The third card points to potential opportunities for increasing income or expanding wealth, and the fourth names the obstacles, fears, or limiting beliefs standing in the way of financial growth. The fifth card highlights resources, people, or skills that can help build financial abundance, the sixth offers steps to take toward manifesting more money, and the seventh shows the likely financial future if that guidance is followed.
