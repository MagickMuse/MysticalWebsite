Card Grimoire

Interview Your Tarot Deck Spread

◇Spread Type
Spiritual Growth
◇Question Style
Open-ended
◇Card Details
Card 1: What kind of readings will you excel at? Card 2: What are your strengths as a deck? Card 3: What limitations do you have? Card 4: What are you here to teach me? Card 5: How can I connect and work with you best? Card 6: What card best sums up our relationship?
◇Description
This spread opens a direct conversation with a deck rather than asking about an outside situation. Shuffle while holding the six questions in mind, cut the deck, and lay the cards out in order. It works especially well when getting to know a new deck or figuring out which decks in a growing collection are best suited to particular kinds of readings.
