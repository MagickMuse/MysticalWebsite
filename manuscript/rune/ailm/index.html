Ailm is the first few of the Fourth Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Associated with the silver fir (Abies alba) as well as pine, Ailm is a rune of new beginnings. It signifies growth, a fresh start, or a departure from old ways. Ailm can also represent clearing away old patterns and releasing the past. Magically, it pertains to seeing things clearly and taking charge. Fir trees represent forward motion and moving toward goals.

Keywords

Change, motion, and authenticity.

Traditional Uses

Ailm is written as one horizontal line crossing the vertical line, although sometimes the horizontal line is presented as a series of dots. The silver fir is often used as a Yuletide tree, connecting it to cycles of renewal and the return of light. Its evergreen nature symbolizes endurance through dark times and the promise of new growth.

Working with Ailm

Draw on Ailm when beginning new projects, seeking clarity and authentic self-expression, or when you need to take charge of a situation. This rune supports those who are ready for forward motion and genuine transformation. It favors those who approach change with authenticity and clear vision.
