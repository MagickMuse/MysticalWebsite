nGeadal is the third few of the Third Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

With connections to both reed and broom, nGeadal is a rune of hidden mysteries and clearing away. It calls upon you to be both flexible and resilient, able to release and let go. Call upon your strength and be ready to move and adapt to changes when you draw this rune. The association with broom connects it to sweeping away negativity and preparing sacred space.

Keywords

Courage, music, fertility.

Traditional Uses

Broom (Cytisus scoparius) was used to make besoms for sweeping away negative energy, as well as in other magical rites of protection and purification. Its flowers were employed in healing work. Weather magic traditions hold that throwing broom flowers into the air raises winds, while burning broom calms them. Some sources link nGeadal to reed (all reeds are members of the Poales order), and broom is considered a reed in certain traditions.

Working with nGeadal

Draw on nGeadal for purification, clearing negativity, and preparing for new undertakings. This rune supports those who must adapt to change while maintaining inner strength. Use it in workings requiring flexibility, cleansing, or the courage to release what no longer serves.
