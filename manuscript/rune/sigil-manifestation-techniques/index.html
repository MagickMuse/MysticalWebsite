Rune Grimoire

Sigil & Manifestation Techniques

Runes have magickal applications beyond divination. As symbols of power, they can protect, attract, or repel. One early use was labeling: carving "candle" in runes on a candle infuses it with mystery and importance.

Runes for Manifesting and Invoking

Using runes as mantras or spoken during spells sharpens focus and intention. You are creating a new reality according to your will.

Three Key Norse Runes:

  1. Mannaz (Self):
    • Represents the self, self-care, self-empowerment.
    • Belief in your own abilities.
    • Notice: Jēra and Kaunaz patterns are contained within Mannaz. Everything you need already dwells within you.
  2. Jēra (Harvest/Abundance):
    • Having enough. You define what "enough" means.
    • Sustenance, rewards of hard work.
    • Can signify time, material comfort, money, wealth, or all three.
  3. Kaunaz (Transformational Opening):
    • Not just fire, but an opening that can grow if properly tended.
    • Use to create openings in the field of dreams.

Inverted Triangle Formation

For manifesting (not divination), you choose runes consciously with clear intention. Manifest within one year or the current Wheel of the Year.

Layout:

  • Point (bottom): Mannaz
  • Upper right: Jēra
  • Upper left: Kaunaz

Practice:

  1. Enter trancelike state while gazing on the formation.
  2. You (Mannaz) are the foundation.
  3. Jēra makes your desire visible, tangible, observable.
  4. Kaunaz sets it in motion.

Why Inverted Triangle: Creates a visual vessel/funnel for focus. Alchemical symbol for water, which flows with great force, cannot be contained but can be channeled.

Action Required: Spoken charms and trance alone are insufficient. The confluence of intention AND action creates the opening.

Rune Stacking (Cairn Method)

Stacking differs from casting: you intentionally choose specific runes to activate outcomes. Stack tiles, wood slices, or balance sticks.

Cairn Symbolism:

  • Used by travelers to guide others
  • Directs unseen energies
  • Signals commitment to your path
  • Temporary; dismantle when blessing manifests

Stack Order (bottom to top):

  1. Mannaz (bottom): Core of your being
  2. Kaunaz (middle): The opening
  3. Jēra (top): Abundance flowing through

Mantra:

Jēra, Abundance!
Kaunaz, Opening!
Mannaz, All flows to me!

The cairn is a guidepost reminding you to act in accord.

Vertical Axis Stacking (Card Method)

Draw the stack on cardboard the size of a business/credit card. This follows the Golden Mean ratio, found in nature and architecture, inherently pleasing and carries divine patterns.

Placement Options:

  • Altar: For ongoing work
  • Wallet: For money/wealth manifestation
  • Pocket/shoe: For healing/health
  • Under pillow: For dream manifestation
  • Gift to others: For manifesting on their behalf

Stacking with Ogham Runes

Ogham runes share a common stave, making them ideal for stacking.

  1. Take credit-card-sized cardboard.
  2. Draw vertical line lengthwise through middle.
  3. One side: Esoteric stack (e.g., Duir for strength, Gort for flowering, Coll for wisdom).
  4. Other side: Transliterate your single-word intention.

Note: Ogham staves read bottom to top.

Creating Sigils with Runes

Sigils are deeply personal. Though runes are recognizable, a sigil's meaning is for the creator alone. Used on magick coins, squares, and amulets, sigils unite practitioners with celestial energy.

What is a Sigil: A monogram of overlapping letters within a circle or square, creating a new distinct character. The act of creation imbues it with magickal energy.

Core Components:

  1. Sound: Audible vibrational energy
  2. Character: Unique visual pattern
  3. Idea: The magickal mind's imagining
  4. Actual representation: The witch's ability to see beyond

Example Sigils (Combining Systems)

Each sigil contains a guide function: the pictographic element shows what you want; the transliteration shows what action to take.

1. Success/Harvest Sigil:

  • Center: Witch rune for Harvest (inscribed in circle)
  • Above: "REAP" transliterated right-to-left: Perthro, Ansuz, Ehwaz, Raidō

2. Truth/Seeing Sigil:

  • Center: Witch rune for Eye (inscribed in circle)
  • Above: "TRUTH" transliterated right-to-left
  • Use when someone is not forthright

3. Bonding Sigil:

  • Center: Witch rune of Rings (inscribed in circle)
  • Outer rings: First initials of both people
  • Center ring: Bindrune combining both initials

Materials: Cardstock, air-drying clay with scribe, or record in grimoire.

Protective Rune Sigils

Runes were frequently used in instruments of battle, emblazoned on shields, helmets, blades, and hilts. The awe they inspire aids the bearer with both power and protection.

The Psychology of Runic Protection

Fear arises when confronting the unfamiliar or unknown. This works in the rune bearer's favor. In accordance with Newton's third law: for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Any blow is absorbed by its target and changed by it.

The Aegishjalmr (Helm of Awe)

This spiked, rayed motif carries the power of invincibility from the Northern Tradition.

  • Aegis - Old Norse for "shield," synonymous with protection
  • Hjalmr - "Helm," meaning a position of responsibility, authority, and high visibility

Core Elements:

  • A central circle
  • Eight rays beginning at center and extending beyond the circle
  • Elhaz (Algiz) - for layered protection
  • Isa - to "freeze" enemies and render them incapable of attack

Modern Applications: Confrontations, negotiations, court proceedings, online interactions, relationships with power differentials.

Working with the Helm:

  1. Trace the symbol on your forehead
  2. Visualize radiating vanquishing power from the center
  3. See the power gain strength as it travels down the staves
  4. Push outward in any direction, as far and forcefully as needed

Apotropaic Runes

Like the Vegvísir, the Aegishjalmr provides both strength and protection through:

  • Repeating staves
  • Star motifs centered around a common intersecting point

Using Bindrunes: Combine runes for specific purposes. For victory, combine Nauthiz stave (need) + Teiwaz apex (victory) = "I am in need of victory."

Creating Personal Rune Staves

Determining Your Number:

  1. Add all numbers in your birth date
  2. Continue adding digits until you reach a single number under 10
  3. This determines the number of staves in your protection symbol

Construction Process:

  1. Use a circle template to locate the center point
  2. Mark vertical and horizontal half-circle points
  3. Divide equally based on your stave number
  4. Choose your rune system
  5. Repeat the pattern evenly along each ray

Ordeals and Obstacles

Odin discovered runes through ordeal. Hardship is inescapable; magick is used to confront, integrate, dispel, or transform constraints.

When Negative Runes Appear:

  • Do not fear. A challenge is at hand.
  • Check position in spread: past (generational wound) or future (actionable now).
  • To integrate an obstacle is to accept that shadows come with light.

Five Ways to Deal with Obstacles

  1. Dissipate: Lessen its power. Make it less important.
  2. Release: "I release that which no longer serves me."
  3. Integrate: Lessons that cannot dissolve become part of you, like a scar.
  4. Transform: Transmute the experience. Hail it as a teacher.
  5. Accept: Some events require pain. Rely on your moral code when intuition fails.

Creating a Stop

The opposite of manifesting. Magick can inhibit or delay things already in motion.

Uses:

  • Stop a person from taking action
  • Delay or interrupt a process
  • Deflect a misfire or quell a brewing storm
  • Break communication patterns

Construction:

  1. Double Isa: Draw vertically and horizontally (equal-armed cross). Commands standstill in all directions.
  2. Inverted Ehwaz: Blocks forward momentum and movement.
  3. Dagaz: At apex of Ehwaz. Transforms unwanted energy.
  4. Circle: Enclose to contain energy.

Using the Stop

Circle as Containment: Scribe the circle first. Write within it what you wish to stop (contact from someone, rumors, bad dreams, an infection, a chain of events). Then cover with the stop sigil.

Whisper no word. Full stop.
All goes unheard. Full stop.
Silence unbroken. Full stop.
My word has been spoken. Full stop.
And by my own will. Full stop.
This charm fulfill. Full stop.
