Tinne is the third few of the Second Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Tinne is associated with the holly tree and symbolizes immortality, regeneration, and the triumph of the eternal spirit. Tinne is a rune of rebirth, new beginnings, and starting over. It may also signify a family reconciliation. Tinne reminds us that endings contain the seeds of new beginnings, and that the eternal spirit triumphs through cycles of regeneration.

Keywords

Practicality, trustworthiness, tolerance.

Traditional Uses

Though often viewed as a bush, holly is actually a genus of small evergreen trees with red berries. Its presence evokes Yule and Midwinter. In England, it was custom to bring holly into the home in winter so the unseen ones had shelter from the cold weather. All trimmings had to be taken down by Twelfth Night; anything not removed had to stay up for the rest of the year, for it was bad luck to take them down after January 6. Holly is said to protect against evil spirits and bad weather, bring courage, promote dreams, and shelter spirits during the dark half of the year.

Working with Tinne

Draw on Tinne when seeking rebirth, fresh starts, or family reconciliation. This rune supports those navigating cycles of regeneration and those who need practicality and trustworthiness to guide them through transitions. It favors those who approach challenges with tolerance and an understanding of life's eternal cycles.
