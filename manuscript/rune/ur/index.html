Ur is the third few of the Fourth Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Ur is symbolized by heather (Calluna vulgaris). Shearing of the heather is a tradition in European and North American cultures. It represents the connection between this world and the next. Ur may be an indication that a spirit is trying to contact you. It is a rune of generosity, healing, and connection to the fae.

Keywords

Love, dignity, and restraint.

Traditional Uses

Heather is a low-growing shrub found along wasteland, where it is a valuable asset for grazing animals. The blooming of its flowers in shades of white to pink to purple indicates the end of summer. Magically, it is thought that heather pleases the faeries, bringing their blessings for good fortune and a favorable harvest. White heather is considered especially lucky.

Working with Ur

Draw on Ur when seeking connection with the spirit world, working with the fae, or when love and dignity are needed in a situation. This rune supports those who approach the otherworld with respect and restraint. It favors those who understand that generosity and gentle persistence often achieve more than force.
