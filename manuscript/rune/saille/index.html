Saille is the fourth few of the First Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Saille is the spirit of the osier willow (Salix viminalis), a white willow found along embankments and near water. Drawing Saille indicates death or significant endings—the closing of a life chapter that will not be reopened. This is a final ending of a path that will not be revisited in this lifetime. The willow's ability to bend without breaking reminds us that even final endings can be met with grace and adaptability.

Keywords

Resourcefulness, devotion, privacy.

Traditional Uses

Willow bark was historically used to treat pain and fevers, the natural precursor to modern aspirin. The osier willow's flexible branches are traditionally woven into baskets, demonstrating the resourcefulness this rune embodies. Mistletoe grows on willow (as well as apples and other trees), lending the willow sacred associations. Because of its mistletoe connection, willow is sometimes used in besoms to sweep away unwanted energies. Willow placed under the pillow will incubate dreams.

Working with Saille

Draw on Saille when navigating definitive endings, seeking resourcefulness in difficult circumstances, or when private matters require devoted attention. This rune supports those who must release what cannot be revisited and approach necessary endings with grace. It favors those who understand that some chapters must close completely before new ones can begin.
