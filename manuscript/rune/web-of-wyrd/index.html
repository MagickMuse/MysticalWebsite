Rune Grimoire

🕸️Web of Wyrd

Type
Bind Rune
Also called
Skuld's Net

The Web of Wyrd is a reminder of the laws of cause and effect, or karma. It teaches that every action, however small, affects all the others, and that everything is connected. The Web predates the arrival of Christianity in the West and belongs to an age when time was understood as cyclical rather than linear.

Despite a common misconception, "wyrd" does not mean the same as "weird" in the sense of strange. It shares its root with the word for "worth," or "to become." Modern scientific ideas about the interconnectedness of all things in the universe echo this Norse concept, which is so ancient as to be impossible to date, and show it to be as valid today as it was thousands of years ago.
