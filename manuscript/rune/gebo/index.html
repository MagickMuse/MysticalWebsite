Gebo represents a gift, whether a welcome surprise, a healthy relationship, or a balanced partnership. This rune of manifestation and acknowledgment points toward profound happiness.

Cosmological Lore

Gebo is the rune of "god" in the sense of the eminent unconscious magickal force present in the Ginnungagap before the formation of the Worlds. It is the holy mystery of the two (or many) in one.

This rune is simultaneously the giver, the giving, the given, and that which is given to. It describes a part of the mystery of sacrifice as the gift of power that is given to humankind by the gods in order to maintain the ecology of cosmic power.

By the power of this rune, persons are bound together through an act of will in order to bring about a result. This stave symbolizes the root force of runic orders and the retinue system.

Ecstasy and Numinous Knowledge

The G-rune has functional similarities to Ansuz, since it is also a rune of ecstasy. It is the mystery of ecstatic magickal power that is perceived and retained by the vitki as numinous knowledge.

Sex Magick and the Union of Polarities

Gebo contains the secrets of psychically joining two people, or several persons, in order to produce a creative power greater than their sum total. This is the rune of sex magick, which was practiced in ancient Germanic times especially for the acquisition of numinous knowledge and wisdom.

The great hero Sigurdhr was initiated into runic wisdom by the valkyrja Sigrdrifa (Brynhildr) in a scene rich with sexual symbolism (a child is born from their union). This was the usual mode of sex magick in which energy was exchanged along polarities. The eighteenth-rune stanza of the Havamal (stanza 164) alludes to this mystery as well, describing a secret that can only be shared with one who embraces the speaker.

Gebo is the rune of the brotherly or sisterly lover, and it is the psychosexual force exchanged between two poles of power: mortal to mortal, or divine to divine. In the latter case, Gebo describes the sexual life force used in fertility magick and in shamanistic practices.

Rune Character

A rune of sacrifice and giving. Something of personal value given freely, such as blood when consecrating the runes. This is a rune of initiation where personal sacrifices are made to obtain knowledge, power, and wisdom. No pain, no gain.

Magickally, Gipt is a bringer of gifts. Gipt relates to weddings and alliances. Also used in sex magick and binding spells. Can be used to bind another to an unwanted obligation and in casting love spells.

Constructive Applications

A bringer of gifts. Relates to weddings and alliances. Used in sex magick and initiation.

Baneful Applications

When used in baneful magick, it brings pain and sacrifice upon the victim with no reward. Can be used to bind another to an unwanted obligation. Used with Isa, the combination is powerful in binding enemies.
