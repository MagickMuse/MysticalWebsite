Rune Grimoire

Candle Magick with Runes

Runes provide illumination. They allow us to examine aspects of our lives from many perspectives, revealing information about the past and what is coming into being. Because runes are tools of illumination, they adapt naturally to candle magick.

Applications:

  • Focus intentions
  • Enhance meditations
  • Add power to spellwork
  • Augment seasonal rituals and lunar observances

A matrix is a specific altar arrangement incorporating runes, candles, and sacred objects (crystals, gemstones) to create a visual focal point for meditation and creative expression.

Pillar Candle Carving Basics

Pillar candles are a staple of witchcraft, used for spells, charms, rituals, and ambience. Their large surface area makes them ideal for rune work:

  • Creating sequences
  • Recording and illuminating a rune cast
  • Empowering magickal practice

Carving as Magickal Act:

Carving forces the practitioner to plan and focus. There is no erasing. Everything must be done with intention. This itself is a potent exercise in developing psychic abilities.

The Scribe

Before considering what to carve, consider your tool.

Bolline vs. Athame:

  • Bolline: The "practical" blade or white-handled knife, used for candle carving
  • Athame: Double-edged blade (often black-handled), used for carving sacred space

The choice of implement impacts the spell. Each auspicious element chosen with clear intention amplifies the outcome, either hastening it or adding effectiveness.

Understanding Power

Power in witchcraft refers to the effectiveness of the work, not dominion over others. A powerful witch channels intention into manifestation. The most successful spells are those with the most intentional work invested.

Choosing Your Scribe

Think about the nature of your work in broad terms first:

  • Invoking
  • Banishing
  • Changing

Then get more specific. You would choose a different tool for an uncrossing candle than for a hearth blessing.

Making a Scribe

A scribe can be a dedicated magickal tool or an ordinary household item:

  • Sharpened chopstick
  • Hefty toothpick or skewer
  • Pin or tack

DIY Method:

  1. Find a slender twig (diameter of your pinkie, length from palm base to fingertip)
  2. Sharpen with a pencil sharpener, or cut diagonally with scissors

Special Scribes

Breaking Point Scribe:

For radical change, find a fallen tree and examine where nature and gravity toppled it. If you need to tear down paradigms or break free of old patterns, the breaking point reveals your scribe.

Root Scribe:

For ancestral work or honoring the dead, trim a sturdy root from an uprooted tree. Roots symbolize ties to family and land. Consider using to honor indigenous spirits of the land you occupy.

The Template Method

For evenly spacing runes around a pillar candle.

Materials:

  • Paper, ruler, scissors
  • Calculator, pencil, pin
  • Your scribe

Steps

1. Calculate Circumference:

  • Measure candle diameter (edge to edge)
  • Multiply by 3.14 = circumference
  • Example: 3" diameter × 3.14 = 9.42" circumference

2. Create Template:

  • Cut paper strip ~1" wide × circumference length
  • Divide length by number of runes in your cast
  • Example: 9.42" ÷ 5 runes = 1.88" spacing
  • Draw vertical lines at each interval

3. Draw Runes:

  • Write runes from your cast right to left between the lines

4. Position for Timing:

PlacementManifestation Speed
TopFast, timing is of the essence
CenterModerate, balanced timing
BottomSlow, celebrations, things you want to last

5. Transfer Pattern:

  • Tape template around candle at chosen height
  • Use pin to prick through points along each rune
  • Remove paper
  • Connect the dots with your scribe

6. Scribe with Intention:

  • Focus on the prophecy revealed in your cast
  • Think about what you want to accelerate, prolong, celebrate, or change
  • Go over staves multiple times for consistency and depth

Creating a Rune Candle Matrix

A matrix is a specific altar arrangement incorporating runes, candles, and sacred objects (crystals, gemstones) to create a visual focal point for meditation and creative expression.

Building Your Matrix

1. Center Candle:

Start in the center of your altar and place one candle there.

2. Select Five Runes:

Arrange them around the outside perimeter of the candle so they are evenly spaced. Choose runes based on your intention. Consult the lists of meanings to make your choice. You may focus on a single influence or many. For example, select a combination of runes you would want to see in your ideal cast.

3. Outer Candles:

Place another candle at the other end of each rune stick or tile, creating a five-pointed arrangement radiating from the center.

4. Add Crystals:

Place tumbled stones or crystals in the negative spaces between the rune sticks and candles. When choosing stones, consider their metaphysical properties. You can consult resources on gem and mineral associations, or choose on instinct based on what you find visually or energetically appealing.

Lines, Crosses, and Curves

As you become more adept at matrix work, you may wish to create more complex patterns. Patterns occur in nature and are imbued with natural magick, the divine order of the universe follows numerous patterns observable in all life forms, from plants to sea creatures to celestial bodies. Developing skill at creating matrices aligns your practice with both terrestrial and celestial energies.

A matrix is a creative outlet, a place of focus, and something that beautifies your surroundings.

Lines

Beginning from a central point and adding lines that radiate outward is a symbolic way to manifest. You make it symbolically real on the earthly plane by infusing it with light, earth magick, and rune magick from the astral plane until it becomes actual reality.

  • A single line represents your present path
  • Repeating lines amplify intention so energy moves in all directions
  • Five runes around a center candle/crystal honor the sacred number five. Every fruit comes from a five-petaled flower; cut an apple and see five seeds arranged the same way

Five Aspects of Worldly Desire:

  1. Health
  2. Abundance
  3. Comfort
  4. Love
  5. Peace

Crosses

Incorporate intersecting lines, exploring the places where two lines meet:

  • Crossroads: Helps in decision-making
  • Celebration: Signifies points of change in the Wheel of the Year
  • Star motif: Adding vertical, diagonal, and horizontal crosses creates a star like those in the Witch runes
Etymology Note: The word "witch" may connect to "wicce," believed by many to have etymological ties to Wicca, meaning "to bend" or "to change."

Curves

Add curved lines to:

  • Accept the winds of change when they blow through your life
  • Imitate spirals found in nature
  • Remind yourself to smile

Realities in Magick

Whether your practice is secular or spiritual, learning variations in matrix work leads to deeper understanding of how magick works: how every color, pattern, shape, intention, and action shapes reality.

Accepted realities in magick include:

  • Nature and the observable world
  • Human-made creations and manipulation of the natural world
  • Entities and experiences unseen but deeply felt (including communication with oracles and spirits)
  • Personal gnosis: Spiritual knowledge gained by an individual practitioner without external validation

Subtle changes in the environment, both observable and intuitively felt, during a magickal working are accepted as affirmation that the work has power.

One theme that solidly recurs in all forms of magick is change.
