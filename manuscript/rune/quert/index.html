Quert is the fifth few of the Second Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Quert (also known as Ceirt) is associated with the apple tree—representing love, fulfillment, and wisdom. Apples encompass relationships and growth, particularly partnerships where there is a strong attraction. Quert reminds us that love and wisdom intertwine, and that the sweetest fruits come from tended relationships.

Keywords

Connection, wisdom, healing.

Traditional Uses

The magical properties of crab apples and cultivated apples are very similar, though some might disagree. Mistletoe grows just as easily on one as it does on the other! Crab apple fruits are edible, just different from cultivated varieties—essentially "wild" apple trees. The apple is featured in many fairy tales and mythology stories. Magically, it symbolizes abundance, fertility, longevity, and otherworld connections—Avalon, the Isle of Apples, takes its name from this sacred fruit.

Working with Quert

Draw on Quert when nurturing relationships, seeking wisdom to guide healing, or when abundance is ready to manifest. This rune supports those who understand that connection and partnership serve their goals. It favors those who tend their relationships with care and patience, knowing that the sweetest rewards come from sustained attention.
