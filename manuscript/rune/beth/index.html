Beith is the first few of the Ogham alphabet, belonging to the First Aicme. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Beith signifies new beginnings, rebirth, and transformation. Its white bark shines through the dark winter months when branches are bare, making it a beacon of light in darkness. As one of the earliest trees to leaf in springtime, birch embodies the promise of renewal and the emergence of new growth after dormancy.

Keywords

Commitment, preciseness, diligence.

Traditional Uses

Birch bark served as parchment for writing and record-keeping. Twigs were gathered to make besoms, the traditional witch's broom. Branches hung over doors at Midsummer ward away negative energy and provide protection for the home.

Working with Beith

Draw on Beith when beginning new projects, seeking clarity and precision in your work, or when transformation is needed. This rune supports those who approach their craft with dedication and attention to detail.
