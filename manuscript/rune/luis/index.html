Luis is the second few of the Ogham alphabet, belonging to the First Aicme. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Luis represents the rowan tree, also known as mountain ash. This rune is associated with revealing truth, uncovering deception, and protective magic. The rowan's red berries bear a five-pointed star or pentagram at their base, which feeds into its magical lore.

Keywords

Sympathy, curiosity, and independence.

Traditional Uses

Rowan has a long history of being protective. The wood makes sturdy walking staves, and the berries make dyes, preserves, and alcoholic beverages. Folk songs often feature someone yearning to be home among the rowan trees, reflecting its association with hearth and homeland.

Working with Luis

Draw on Luis when seeking truth, banishing deception, or calling for protection. This rune shines a beacon on the darker areas of the mind and supports those who value honesty, curiosity, and independence in their dealings.
