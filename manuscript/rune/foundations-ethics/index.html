Rune Grimoire

Foundations & Ethics

Runes combine archaeology, anthropology, linguistics, symbolism, and divination into a unified study. Rune casters share a secret yet common language that transcends cultures and locations.

Types of Rune Keepers

  1. Runologists (Scholars):
    • Academic keepers of archaeological, historical, and linguistic information.
    • Distinguish lexical runes (translatable) from non-lexical runes (decorative).
    • Study form, purpose, and meaning within archaeological context.
    • Example: Debunked the Mustang Mountain Cave runestone (Arizona, ~2012), revealing it as a 1990s creation using defunct Sudovian script.
  2. Runatics (Hobbyists):
    • Passionate rune hunters who delight in discovery.
    • Celebrate runes through secular festivities (e.g., Heavener, Oklahoma's annual runestone festival featuring feasting, ax throwing, fencing, blacksmithing).
    • Use runes as decor and seek validation from runologists.
  3. Runemal (Occultist Practice):
    • Magicko-religious rune casting taught orally by elders as sacred rite.
    • Associated with modern Heathenry.
    • Employs legomonism: direct channels of knowledge remain hidden.
  4. Rune-Masters (Occultist Teachers):
    • Highly skilled casters who train others.
    • Distinguished by their ability to teach, not just practice.

Key Terms

  • Heathenry: Modern revival of polytheistic pre-Christian religion focused on Germanic European traditions.
  • Ásatrú: American Heathenry drawing customs from Scandinavia.
  • Traditionalism: Adhering to established beliefs in opposition to modern/eclectic practices.

Misappropriation Warnings

Runes themselves are not problematic. What becomes problematic is when enthusiasts use them to deceive or pervert their meaning.

  • Political Co-option: Some groups incorporate racialist and nationalist ideas into rune practice. The Left-Hand Path refers to practices defined by the taboo absence of morals.
  • Sacred symbols can degrade into offensive emblems quickly. Engage with runes understanding how symbols can be corrupted through intention.
  • Commercial Use: Over 100 rune-bearing sites in North America are all relatively modern (18th century or later).

Ethics of Commercial Runes

  • Historically, runes were personal, often secret, and not commercial.
  • Modern commerce (selling rune sets, readings, games) is entirely contemporary.
  • No inherent wrong in buying runes or paying for readings, provided skill matches price.
  • Interact with runes and others honestly to avoid deviating into darkness.

Personal Boundaries

Runes Are Personal:

  • Do not lend your runes. They absorb vibrational energy of handlers.
  • Maintain this boundary as an exercise in autonomy, not isolation.

Avoid Self-Doubt:

  • Everyone has varying psychic ability that improves with practice.
  • Historical rune users were often barely literate.
  • As long as you use runes ethically without spreading misinformation, you are using them correctly.

What to Avoid:

  • Harassing the oracle with excessive consultations
  • Becoming overly dependent
  • Re-pulling due to dissatisfaction
  • The Temple of Delphi bore "Nothing in excess." Find the balance point.
