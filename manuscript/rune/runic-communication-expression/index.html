Rune Grimoire

Runic Communication & Expression

Because runes are alphabetic, it is entirely possible to transliterate using runes. Communication is key to any successful relationship, giving voice to one's own needs comes with a certain amount of risk. Some risks are worth taking, but it is often impossible to know from the outset how things are likely to progress.

This is where runes become invaluable. By creating a bind-rune, you signal your intention and inject energy into the field of perceptible energy.

Communicating a Need

Step 1: Dialogue with Yourself

Identify one specific need or requirement and focus only on that. Draw the characters from your preferred rune system.

Can you name what you need in the simplest terms? Can you simplify what you need into a single word?

Consider the dimensions of your life:

  • Family
  • Community ties
  • Love and relationships
  • Hobbies
  • Spirituality
  • Future goals
  • Past debts

Think about what drives you and what constrains you. Choose one, either a motivating factor or a constraint, and refine it into a single word.

Example words: Time, Health, Love, Travel, Work, Wealth, Study

These words are intentionally short because your next step will be to express them along a single stave.

Creating a Communication Stave

Draw a single vertical line in the middle of a blank grimoire page. If using Ogham runes, start from the bottom and work up, transliterating into your mother tongue.

Example Process:

ElementContent
ThoughtI need more time to accomplish my goals
IntentionGoal (be specific: clairvoyance, mediumship, good grades, new job)
ConstraintTime

External and Metaphysical Communication

After establishing dialogue with yourself, the next level is external and metaphysical, engaging with spirit, ancestor, deity, or the source of power connected to your modern practice.

Steps for Formal Request:

  1. Identify and name the metaphysical level of communication (specific deity, name of ancestor, elemental energy)
  2. Establish connection using a tool (mantra, galdr, pendulum, spirit board)
  3. Make a formal request for aid, insight, or prophecy

Once intention and tools are ready, silently focus on the stave while invoking metaphysical energy.

A Note on Time

Albert Einstein proved that time is relative, the rate at which time passes is connected to your frame of reference. Einstein argued that time is an illusion and that the separation of past, present, and future is not set.

Remember the truth about gravity, space, and the passage of time, and accept your own ability to exercise flexibility when manipulating time.

Runes as Body Adornment

Just as you can consecrate a candle using runes, you can apply the same technique to your physical person.

Invisible Intention Work

If you want to inspire sexy confidence, use your favorite essential oil blend to write your desires in runes on your own body. These intentions will not be visible to anyone, but you will know they are there.

Even a simple transliteral rune for "touch" or "kiss" traced with your fingertip up and down your arms and legs, or "hold" on your hand, will prepare your body and mind to welcome sensuality.

Visible Adornment

If outward expression feels right to you, liquid eyeliner can prove to be a useful medium for adorning the body with runes.

Invisible Application Methods

If visibility is not something you are ready for yet, or if you are less inclined toward formal anointing with essential oil blends, you can also use:

  • Moisturizer
  • Foundation

These invisible but powerful gestures become part of your physical makeup and add a layer of magick to your grooming.
