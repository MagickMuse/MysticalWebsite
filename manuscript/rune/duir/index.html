Duir is the second few of the Second Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Duir represents the oak tree—signifying great power and personal agency. It is a rune of strength. Boldness will be rewarded, and calculated risks are likely to manifest positive results. Duir encourages bold action grounded in wisdom, reminding you that true power comes with responsibility.

Keywords

Broad-mindedness, responsibility, charisma.

Traditional Uses

Oak trees are profoundly meaningful to the Druids, from whom they take their name. Some legends say it was an oak tree that imprisoned Merlin, while others speak of hollow oaks being doorways into the spiritual realms. The oak embodies Druidic wisdom, endurance, and lasting strength. Long-lived trees, oaks symbolize the power to contain great forces and provide doorways between worlds.

Working with Duir

Draw on Duir when you need to exercise greater personal agency, take calculated risks, or embrace responsibilities requiring strength and charisma. This rune supports those who approach challenges with broad-mindedness and are ready to act boldly. It favors those who understand that meaningful power must be wielded with wisdom.
