Modern Relevance

Wunjo symbolizes joy, happiness, and fulfillment. Linked to flowering fruit trees, it indicates that desires are within reach. It heralds beauty, possibility, and positive manifestations.

Tree, Element, and Deity

Tree: ash

Element: earth

Deity: Odin

Meaning: joy; harmony with the flow of events

Cosmological Lore

Wunjo is the root force of attraction that sympathetic beings (wights) have for one another, specifically wights descended from a common source. These beings are bound together in an organic whole, exemplified in the world of mortals by the clan and tribe. In the ancient Germanic world, society was clan-centered, for a strong traditional clan was the best protection against invaders from without or totalitarianism from within. By giving the honor and integrity of the clan first consideration, the individual was best able to maintain personal freedom.

Harmonious Existence

Wunjo is the mystery of the harmonious existence of varied affiliated forces. In the clan, this is the source of the highest human joy. When all members are harmoniously working together, integrated into their environment in a syncretic manner, a true state of holiness exists.

The power of the W-rune aids in the promotion of fellowship and goodwill between brothers and sisters, making it a powerful force in the maintenance of societies and guilds.

The Binding Force

Wunjo is the energy that binds different fields of force together and is therefore an invaluable concept in rune magick. With this energy, the vitki can bind several runes into a single harmonious force that can work toward a specific result.

Rune Character

A rune of honors and rewards. Our efforts are rewarded. Vend is bliss merging with light. Authority, respect, and strength. Excellent for banishing depression. Helps to unite family members and mend friendships. Breaks down barriers between oneself and others. Also a rune of healing, as it binds the healing of the mind with the healing of the physical self. Wards off diseases. Good for working with the heart chakra and for raising confidence and self-esteem.

Stadha

Stand upright with legs together. Place the fingertips of the left hand on the crown of the head. Keep the right arm close at the side.

Magickal Workings

  1. Strengthens links and bonds
  2. Invocation of fellowship and harmony
  3. Banishes alienation
  4. Happiness and well-being
  5. Realization of the links and multiplicity of relationships of all things
  6. Binding runes toward specific purposes (see bind runes)
  7. Banishing depression and warding off disease
  8. Healing of mind and body together

Constructive Applications

Banishes depression. Unites family members and mends friendships. Breaks down barriers between oneself and others. Binds the healing of mind and body. Wards off diseases. Good for working with the heart chakra. Raises confidence and self-esteem.

Baneful Applications

When directed for baneful magick, this rune can be used to instill overconfidence and trust of the wrong things in others, leading to their downfall.
