Rune Grimoire

Rune Work & Learning

Through a combination of the runes and the vitki's personal will and ability, all things are possible; but in order to gain this power the vitki must develop the skills basic to all ritual work: concentration, visualization, breath and posture control, and the art of incantation. The best course of action is one in which time is devoted to exercises designed to develop the basic skills necessary to the successful performance of runagaldr.

Runic power is often slow to develop in people (this may be due to centuries of widespread neglect), but when the force manifests itself in the vitki's life it is all-pervasive in its potency, unshakable in its strength, and overpowering in the stimulation it produces. A vitki of patience and perseverance will be well rewarded!

Preliminary Exercises

I. Find a rune that is particularly appealing to you. Fashion a meditation card from white poster board (about 3" x 5"). Paint the chosen stave in bright red. Sit comfortably with the card at eye level. Begin an even breath rhythm. Concentrate on the stave's form while silently chanting the name three times, pause, then three times again. Then close your eyes and imagine the shape in your hugauga ("mind's eye"). Practice until you can perform this smoothly for ten minutes.

II. Repeat Exercise I, except sing the name out loud while maintaining a breath pattern of ten seconds inhalation, two seconds hold in, ten seconds exhalation (while singing), and two seconds hold out. Experiment with other postures, sitting and standing. Once you can maintain this for ten minutes, progress further.

III. Perform in the I-rune stadha with hands overhead. Set up a ten-two-ten-two breathing rhythm while facing north. Visualize the F-rune in blazing red while intoning its name three times. Slowly turn with the sun in a circle, visualizing and vibrating each rune in turn. Once you can perform this instinctually with few breaks in concentration, rune work may be confidently undertaken.

The vitki should also design a course of intellectual and physical development. The serious study of Norse mythology and religion, the science of runology, and the Old Norse language will greatly improve understanding of runecraft. A healthy, strong body will reflect itself in more powerful magickal abilities.

Learning Runes

Commitment Required:

  • Study origin of your chosen system
  • Learn individual attributes
  • Master different spreads

Allow Acclimation:

  • Give readings time to settle into your psyche.
  • If a reading seems incorrect, wait before reconsulting.
  • Things may already be in motion that have not revealed themselves.

Stave, Symbol, and Sound

Learning Approach:

  • Focus on one rune per week.
  • Use physical runes, drawings in your grimoire, or index cards.
  • Prioritize: names, sounds, or meanings first.

Stave (Form):

  • A stave is simply a line bearing mystery, secret, or cipher.
  • Study linear vs. curved forms:
    • Cross: choice, decision, obstacle
    • Curve: transformation, the unexpected
    • Straight line: clear path, forward movement, or standstill

On Reversals:

  • Reversals are entirely modern additions.
  • Linguistically, they make no sense; an upside-down letter retains its phonetic value.
  • Some runes are symmetrical, making reversals impossible to detect.
  • Recommendation: Turn upside-down runes right side up to read them.

Tactile Learning:

  • Trace patterns with fingertips.
  • Write in runes.
  • This lessens dependence on others' interpretations.

Symbol (Meaning):

  • Symbols are doorways for intuitive information.
  • Example: Teiwaz resembles an arrow. Consider direction of movement and what that reveals.

Sound (Vibration):

  • Speaking runes aloud creates transformational vibration.
  • Begin by saying each rune's name as dialogue.
  • Progress to pronouncing phonetic sounds.
  • Create bindrunes through sound (start with your initials).
