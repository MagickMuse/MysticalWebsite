⚱️Pyramid Texts

Type
Magical Alphabet

Overview

The Ancient Egyptians were preoccupied with the Afterlife, and that concern shaped many of their rites and rituals. Above all they sought to protect the soul on its journey beyond death. The mummy was wrapped with an arsenal of charms, amulets, and other protective devices, and the pyramids in which the pharaohs were interred were encrusted with magical spells, all meant to guard the soul on its epic voyage and to appease the gods and other beings it might meet along the way. Many passages in the pyramid texts describe the glories awaiting the pharaoh in the Afterlife.

Among the spells is a curiosity known as the Cannibal Hymn, which seems designed to warn the gods themselves of the pharaoh's power: it tells of the pharaoh devouring the deities.

Though hard to date with certainty, the pyramid texts may reach back to 3000 BC, which would make them the oldest magical and sacred texts in the world. Inscribed in 🏺Hieroglyphs, they are the forerunners of the later ⚱️Coffin Texts, which carried such funerary spells beyond the pharaohs to a wider circle of the dead.
