Ruis is the fifth few of the Third Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Associated with healing, Ruis corresponds to the elder tree, which has a long association with witches. The healing may come in physical or emotional form, but it will be transformational and complete. Ruis may represent an encounter with a person who is a healer, or the healing may come from within. Elder's magical versatility encompasses healing, blessing, and cursing.

Keywords

Deflection, otherworldliness, abundance.

Traditional Uses

Elderberry (Sambucus nigra) holds deep magical significance. All parts of the elder are toxic when raw, but delicious jams, cordials, and wines can be made from both the flowers and the fruits. The tree grows prolifically in hedgerows and ditches. Magically, elder was used in many ways—from healing to blessing to cursing—making it one of the most versatile trees in folk practice. Elder is associated with the otherworld and the fae.

Working with Ruis

Draw on Ruis for healing work of all kinds, connection to the otherworld, or workings involving transformation and completion. This rune supports those seeking abundance, deflection of harm, or access to the mysteries between worlds. Approach elder with respect, as its power encompasses both beneficial and baneful magic.
