Nion is the fifth few of the First Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Nion is the spirit of the ash tree (Fraxinus excelsior), thought to be Odin's World Tree. Connected to the ash tree and associated with Poseidon, Nion is a rune of emotional depth and power. Drawing Nion can signify intense feelings that may also be tumultuous. The lesson of Nion is to safeguard against becoming emotionally overwhelmed. The ash tree's connection to all three realms of time reminds us that our present creativity shapes both our understanding of the past and our path into the future.

Keywords

Spontaneity, creativity, nebulousness.

Traditional Uses

The ash tree is thought to be Odin's World Tree—the axis connecting all realms. The three realities of past, present, and future were encompassed by the ash tree. This majestic tree has many marvelous and unusual features: ash sap is flammable even when green, making it invaluable for camping and fire-starting. The Yule log burned at Midwinter was often made of ash. Ash wood and seeds are used in prosperity magic, drawing on the tree's associations with cosmic abundance and the interconnection of all things.

Working with Nion

Draw on Nion when seeking spontaneity, expressing creative impulses, or when intense emotions need careful navigation. This rune supports those working with prosperity magic or seeking connection to the cosmic World Tree. It favors those who can channel emotional depth into creative expression while maintaining clarity.
