Gort is the second few of the Third Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Gort symbolizes the flowering ivy vine, representing resurrection and rebirth. It is the rune of beginning anew while keeping the lessons of the past—the emergence after difficulty, inspiration after a fallow period. Gort is a favorable omen that new endeavors will be successful. Because of ivy's stubbornness to grow in seemingly impossible places, it is used magically to bind things and add tenacity and determination to any work.

Keywords

Fidelity, enlightenment, stability.

Traditional Uses

English ivy (Hedera helix) climbs walls by getting into mortar between bricks, demonstrating remarkable determination to thrive in impossible places. Ivy is traditionally paired with holly in seasonal celebrations and magical workings. Its Latin name suggests a sacred spiral. Ivy was used in bindings, love charms, and workings requiring persistence and steady growth.

Working with Gort

Draw on Gort when you need determination, stability, or the tenacity to persist through difficult circumstances. This rune supports new beginnings built on past wisdom, binding workings, and any magic requiring unwavering commitment. It favors those who remain steadfast in their purpose.
