Eabhadh is the first few of the Fifth Aicme (Forfeda) of the Ogham alphabet. These are additional letters not always used in the Ogham divination system—latecomers to the fews as the language developed. They don't traditionally have trees associated with them in the same way as the main Ogham, but later practitioners have assigned correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Eabhadh is associated with the white poplar (Populus alba). All poplar trees are symbolic of change and evolution. Like aspens, poplars whisper when the winds blow, their leaves trembling with messages from the spirit world. Drawing Eabhadh may indicate a period of transformation, protection needed, or the endurance to weather coming changes.

Keywords

Strength, longevity, protection.

Traditional Uses

There are many different kinds of poplars, all sharing similar magical properties. Poplar is important in herbal medicine as an anti-inflammatory and an analgesic. Magically, poplar is used as a money draw and for workings involving change, evolution, and protection. The tree's ability to grow quickly and regenerate from cuttings symbolizes resilience and renewal.

Working with Eabhadh

Draw on Eabhadh when you need strength to endure transformation, protection during times of change, or support for prosperity workings. This rune favors those who can adapt and evolve while maintaining their core strength. It supports those seeking longevity in their endeavors and the resilience to weather life's storms.
