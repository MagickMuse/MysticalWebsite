Fearn is the third few of the Ogham alphabet, belonging to the First Aicme. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Fearn signifies the European alder (Alnus glutinosa), a tree associated with protection and longevity. As the rune of warriors and heroes, Fearn speaks to challenges met and obstacles overcome. It encourages inner strength, fortitude, confidence, and personal agency.

Keywords

Courage, adventure, and competition.

Traditional Uses

European alder thrives in damp places and is a sturdy wood, more resistant to rot than some of its softer companions. Alder has been used in the making of cigar boxes and flutes as well as the pilings of medieval buildings. Its resilience in wet conditions made it invaluable for foundations.

Working with Fearn

Draw on Fearn when facing challenges, seeking courage, or embarking on adventures. This rune is a harbinger of the appropriate time to speak up, take action, and move past difficulties. Fearn supports those who approach life with competitive spirit and warrior determination.
