Muin is the first few of the Third Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Muin signifies passion, joy, and exhilaration—but is mirrored by a darker side in which things can get out of hand. Associated with the fruited vine, Muin can signify great heights where the temptation is to throw caution to the wind. Poor judgment, rash decisions, or decisions made without due diligence are the lessons of Muin. This rune relates strongly to intuition, fate, and sometimes betrayal.

Keywords

Contradiction, communication, divination.

Traditional Uses

The vine (likely bramble—including blackberries, raspberries, and loganberries) belongs to the rose family, bearing small fruits and prickly thorns. Brambles grow prolifically along hedgerows throughout the British Isles. The vine's association with wine connects it to prophecy, altered states, and divine intoxication used in ritual practice.

Working with Muin

Draw on Muin for divinatory work, developing intuition, or when seeking communication with otherworldly realms. Be mindful of its cautionary aspect—it warns against excess and decisions made without proper consideration. This rune supports those navigating contradictory situations or seeking clarity through prophetic practices.
