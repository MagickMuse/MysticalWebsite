Onn is the second few of the Fourth Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Represented by the gorse shrub (Ulex europaeus), also known as furze, Onn is a rune of setting plans and allowing them time to manifest. Drawing Onn may represent that some type of short-term sacrifice may be needed in order to achieve a greater success. Magically, gorse draws things together; it encourages forward movement and a sense of community.

Keywords

Progress, understanding, and improvement.

Traditional Uses

Gorse is low-growing, more of a shrub than a tree, but it is renowned for clinging tightly and flourishing in poor conditions—rocky crags and sandy knolls where other plants struggle. Its bright yellow flowers are a welcome sight across the British Isles, blooming even in harsh conditions. This resilience makes it a powerful symbol of persistence and determination.

Working with Onn

Draw on Onn when you need to persist through difficult circumstances, draw community together, or make progress toward long-term goals. This rune supports those who understand that meaningful achievement often requires patience and small sacrifices. It favors those who can maintain forward movement even in challenging conditions.
