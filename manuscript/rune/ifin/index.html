Ifin is the fourth few of the Fifth Aicme (Forfeda) of the Ogham alphabet. These are additional letters not always used in the Ogham divination system—latecomers to the fews as the language developed. They don't traditionally have trees associated with them in the same way as the main Ogham, but later practitioners have assigned correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Ifin (also spelled Iphin) is associated with the gooseberry (Ribes uva-crispa). Drawing Ifin may indicate exposure of hidden matters, conflict that must be addressed, or closure that brings resolution. The gooseberry's thorny nature and tart fruit speak to the sometimes difficult process of seeing truth and finding completion.

Keywords

Exposure, conflict, closure.

Traditional Uses

Gooseberries create formidable hedges with their thorns, making excellent boundary markers and protective barriers. The berries are tart but make delicious pies and crumbles—sweetness that comes through transformation. Magically, the gooseberry is associated with nature spirits and seeing the unseen. It aids in perceiving what is hidden and in working with the fae.

Working with Ifin

Draw on Ifin when hidden matters need exposing, conflicts require resolution, or when seeking closure on difficult situations. This rune supports those who must see clearly even when the truth is uncomfortable. It favors those who can transform tart experiences into something nourishing and who maintain healthy boundaries while remaining open to the unseen world.
