Honeysuckle

Uilleann is the third few of the Fifth Aicme (Forfeda) of the Ogham alphabet. These are additional letters not always used in the Ogham divination system—latecomers to the fews as the language developed. They don't traditionally have trees associated with them in the same way as the main Ogham, but later practitioners have assigned correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Uilleann is associated with European honeysuckle (Lonicera xylosteum), sometimes called woodbine. Drawing Uilleann may indicate hidden abundance, secrets coming to light, or desires seeking expression. The honeysuckle's sweet scent and clinging habit speak to devotion, attraction, and the pursuit of heart's desires.

Keywords

Abundance, secrets, desire.

Traditional Uses

Honeysuckle is sometimes called woodbine, and if you've ever seen its winding habit around a slender tree, it's easy to understand why. Its scent is sweet and intoxicating. With its clinging habit, honeysuckle has earned the magical attributes of devotion, heady dreams, and psychic visions. It is used in workings for love, fidelity, and accessing hidden knowledge through dreams.

Working with Uilleann

Draw on Uilleann when seeking abundance, uncovering secrets, or pursuing desires with devotion. This rune supports dream work, psychic development, and matters of the heart. It favors those who approach their desires with persistence and who understand that some secrets reveal themselves only to the devoted seeker.
