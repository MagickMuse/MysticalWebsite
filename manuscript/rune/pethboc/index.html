Pethboc (also known as Straif) is the fourth few of the Third Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

A Note on Traditions

Different Ogham traditions assign different trees to this letter. Some sources associate Straif with blackthorn (Prunus spinosa), also known as sloe, while others connect it to dwarf elder (Sambucus ebulus). Both are valid traditional associations, and practitioners may work with either or both trees depending on their lineage and practice. This grimoire includes both plant correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Pethboc is a rune of sovereign power and completion. This can symbolize a promotion, graduation, or a gain in social status. It heralds a necessary ending, but one that will lead to greater stability and wholeness. When associated with blackthorn, it carries themes of protection, strength, and detachment; when associated with dwarf elder, it emphasizes healing properties and completion.

Keywords

Protection, strength, detachment (blackthorn tradition) or Authority, completion, longevity (dwarf elder tradition).

Traditional Uses

Blackthorn: The blackthorn tree bears the sloe berry and is known for its fierce thorns. It is associated with warriors, protection magic, and the dark half of the year. Blackthorn walking sticks (called "shillelaghs" in Ireland) were prized weapons and magical tools.

Dwarf Elder: Bears fruit with healing properties and has long been used in folk medicine. Associated with completion, sovereignty, and transformational endings.

Working with Pethboc

Draw on Pethboc when seeking protection, working toward completion of major life phases, or when transformation requires you to set firm boundaries. This rune supports those who must stand their ground with strength and detachment, guarding their sovereignty through necessary endings.
