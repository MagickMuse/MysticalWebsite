Uath is the first few of the Second Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Uath (also known as Huath) is symbolized by the hawthorn tree. It is a sign of caution that bad luck may be nearby. Ill fortune, disappointment, or an unrealized goal could be at hand. Prepare to release expectations and accept what will—or will not—come to pass. The tree carries paradoxical energy—protective yet dangerous to bring indoors, lucky yet a warning sign in divination. This duality reflects its role as a boundary marker between worlds.

Keywords

Charisma, originality, resourcefulness.

Traditional Uses

Hawthorn is a tree said to bring protection, as well as wise counsel and favorable outcomes. Even though hawthorn represents good luck, it was thought to be bad luck to bring any part of the tree into the home. A lone hawthorn is reputed to be a gateway to other realms. The may-blossom (hawthorn flower) blooms around Beltane and carries associations with fertility and the boundaries between seasons.

Working with Uath

Draw on Uath when you need to release expectations, seek wise counsel, or navigate uncertain outcomes. This rune supports those standing at thresholds or gateways, and those who must accept what they cannot control. It favors those who can use charisma and originality to navigate challenges with grace.
