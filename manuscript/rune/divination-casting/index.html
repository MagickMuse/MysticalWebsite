Rune Grimoire

Divination & Casting

Historical Roots of Runic Divination

Divination is among the oldest applications of the runes. Runic forms were carved into pieces of wood, cast onto a white cloth, and interpreted according to strict criteria. The Roman historian Tacitus documented this practice among the continental Germanic peoples in the first century CE, describing it as a fully developed divinatory rite. The runes used for divination were not separate from the magickal system; the same symbols that encoded sacred mysteries also served as oracular tools.

The Tacitus Method (Hlutfall)

Tacitus documented this ancient lot-casting technique in the Germania, and it remains one of the most accessible entry points for the modern vitki. The method requires twenty-four rune lots (hlutar) ritually constructed from oak or beech wood, each loaded with a different stave of the Elder Futhark. Flat, thin strips of veneer roughly one inch by two inches work well. A white linen cloth approximately three feet square is also needed.

At an auspicious time, preferably outdoors, spread the cloth on the ground with its flat edges aligned to the four quarters. Stand near the southern edge facing north and shuffle the lots while concentrating on the subject of inquiry. Hold the lots in both hands, assume the stadha, and fix the mind firmly on the question. Once a strong link has formed, sing the galdr:

Rúnar rádha rátt rádh!

(Runes rown right rede!)

Then cast the lots onto the cloth, calling the Nornic names before they land:

Urdhr, Verdhandi, Skuld!

Close the eyes and walk to the cloth. Randomly pick up three lots. The first reveals the past or root of the matter, the second illuminates the present condition, and the third indicates what should come about given the other two factors. Further insight into this technique may be drawn from the mysteries of Pertho.

Casting Methods

The method you choose depends on preference, spread type, and the energetic principles at play. The most appropriate cast aligns oracle, caster, and question.

Random Pull:

  • Draw from pouch or box array.
  • Liberating when confronting things beyond your control.
  • Relinquishing control diminishes ego, strengthens oracular connection.
  • Brings immediate peace to chaotic situations.

Deliberate Pull:

  • Use when a situation requires reigning in.
  • Controlled spread for focused inquiry.

Neither technique has value over the other. Match method to energetic context.

Developing Psychic Abilities

Divination ("seeing beyond") is both talent and skill.

  • Talent: Inherent aptitude that waxes and wanes based on nurturing.
  • Skill: Developed through intentional practice.

Key Principle: Repetition alone is insufficient. Repetition must be accompanied by clear intention to improve. Repeating mistakes creates neurological pathways for error.

Strengthening Techniques

1. Visualization

Learn to see beyond what eyes perceive or mind imagines.

Progression:

  1. Visualize familiar objects in excruciating detail (color, shape, texture, dimension, light reflection).
  2. Add sensory layers: temperature, scent.
  3. Increase complexity: count lotus petals, notice color gradations.
  4. Apply to known memories with full detail.
  5. Apply to emotions.
  6. Apply to unknowns you can verify (e.g., rooms in a friend's home).
  7. Visualize runes: trace staves mentally, listen for their sounds and messages.

2. Meditation

Quiet the mind. Create room for psychic awareness by emptying mundane thoughts.

  • Center consciousness on body and breath.
  • Focus on energy exchange with environment.
  • Start small. Racing thoughts are normal at first.

Important: Relaxation is a by-product, not the goal. Meditative awareness is heightened, not lessened. It should feel enlivening, not sleep-inducing.

3. Opening the Mind

An open mind is accepting, able to entertain possibilities, and able to receive. Requires confronting inherent biases and maintaining unattachment to outcomes.

When runes challenge you:

  • Examine if you hoped for something you did not see.
  • Sit with uncomfortable information.
  • Disturbing messages are not guarantees of disaster.
  • Warnings can lead you in the right direction.

Aura Exercise

Once each skill is developed in isolation, combine them:

  1. Meditate: Comfortable seated position. Breathe from diaphragm. Close eyes. Focus on breath.
  2. Mantra: Use a single rune name or bindrune pronunciation to center focus.
  3. Visualize aura: Picture it as a second skin of light. Notice color gradations and temperature.
  4. Extend aura: Project it outward to touch the unseen. Reach in all directions.
  5. Receive: Listen for temperature changes, visions, information coming through the projected field.
  6. Return: Call aura back. Return to present moment.

Preparing Your Space

Preparation is necessary. A casual preparation yields casual results.

Choose Comfort:

  • Physical discomfort draws focus from reading.
  • Grounding (floor, outdoors) is fine if comfortable.
  • Use a cleared table or altar with appealing chair or mat.

Remove Distractions:

  • Face away from clocks
  • Shut down computers
  • Silence phones
  • Create stillness

Consecrate the Space:

  • Make a declaration (silent or spoken) that you are entering sacred space.
  • Use a dedicated cloth, handled as valuable.
  • Light candles as beacons for insight.
  • Perform smoke clearing with burned herbs.
  • Keep grimoire nearby for recording.
  • A dish of salt, stick of incense, cup of water, and candle create a hearth for communion.

Preparing Yourself

  1. Hold runes (in pouch or box) at heart level, eyes closed.
  2. Feel heartbeat creating vibrational link to corporeal body.
  3. Breathe into the vibration. Focus on breath and heartbeat.
  4. Let everything else fall away.
  5. When mind is clear, touch the runes.

Asking the Question

Formulate clearly:

  • Begin with a single question (multiple questions obscure which part runes respond to).
  • Use open-ended words: "what" or "which."
  • Examples: "What is the likely result if I choose [scenario]?" or "Which course of action leads to [desired outcome]?"

Speak aloud:

  • Runes are a language. Query must travel vibrationally.
  • Silent queries can indicate distrust or "testing" the oracle.
  • Skepticism yields less accurate readings.
  • Words create change: speaking creates sound wave frequencies; writing creates enduring physical transformation.

Harnessing Energy

Three Methods:

  1. Pull/Draw: Deliberate. Best for clarity and choice.
  2. Toss/Throw: Chaotic. Best when stuck and needing direction.
  3. Swirl: Invokes change.

Swirl Technique:

  • Place runes facedown on smooth surface.
  • Cover with both hands.
  • Left hand clockwise, right hand counterclockwise.
  • Allow runes to "escape" between hands.
  • Stop when runes feel ready or spontaneously turn over.

Pull vs. Draw (Energetic Distinction)

  • Pull: Rune calling to you. Active. Engages the heart. Domain of experience.
  • Draw: You calling to rune. Subtle. Engages the mind. Domain of knowledge.

When to Pull vs. Throw

Practical Considerations:

  • Glass runes: Never throw (will chip).
  • Ogham sticks: Satisfying to toss.
  • Heavy stone runes: Too heavy to throw.
  • Ceramic: Can pull or toss.

Energetic Considerations:

  • Focused state but need to feel unbridled: Use random method.
  • Chaotic energy but need focus: Choose deliberately one by one.

The Six Steps of Rune Casting

  1. Preparation: Physical and emotional space. Variable: Surroundings and needs.
  2. Query: Open-ended question. Variable: Spoken, written, or both.
  3. Cast: Total number of runes. Variable: Pull, throw, or turn.
  4. Spread: Arrangement. Variable: Relates to depth of question.
  5. Interpretation: Reading. Variable: Modern or traditional meanings.
  6. Reflection: Integrating new information. Variable: Recorded or ephemeral.

Memorize these steps. As you become adept, you will learn which parts can be truncated.

Rune Sticks

Historical Discovery

In 1954 near Greenland's southernmost point, the Narsaq stick was discovered among textiles, bone fragments, and caribou antler tools, a pine stick carved with Norse runes never before seen in Greenland. This marked the first evidence of a Norse settlement there.

Four rune sticks total were found, bearing the Futhark in short twig form. Though carved by different individuals, their purpose appears consistent: magick and cipher. One interpretation suggests a prayer for safe sea passage; another appears to be a riddle or coded message.

Practical Applications

Talismans and Charms:

  • Carve or draw an intention on a stick
  • Carry it with you or wear on your person
  • Works with both Norse and Ogham runes

Spell Components:

  • Incorporate rune sticks into spell jars
  • Use as part of larger magickal workings

Casting with Rune Sticks

Rune sticks can be cast similarly to Witch runes:

  1. Enliven with friction: Roll sticks back and forth between your palms
  2. Release: Allow them to fall naturally
  3. Interpret position and relationship:
    • Which runes face you?
    • Which are close together?
    • Which are distant?
    • Which sticks are touching?

The spatial relationships guide your reading, adding a dimension beyond traditional spread positions.

Accepting Outcomes

Divination is an art, and practitioners sometimes reveal what they'd rather not know. Any predictive endeavor carries risk to the status quo.

Key Principles:

  • All participants retain free agency and personal will
  • A bad omen does not equate with a bad outcome
  • Warnings can be the impetus needed to ward off larger disaster
  • Short-term sacrifice is often necessary
  • Ordeals are native to rune work

Growth Through Acceptance:

Embracing the full range of human experience cultivates compassion, ethics, and openness.

Yes, openness carries danger, but runes are also linked to protective magick. Mastering multiple applications leaves practitioners feeling empowered rather than vulnerable.

Accepting an undesirable outcome is not the same as invoking it. You can always avoid sailing directly into the storm if you have the courage to take the helm and direct your journey via a safer passage.

Rune Spreads

A rune spread interprets selected runes in a specific order. Spreads range from simple check-ins to in-depth examinations. Each selection and placement carries distinct significance.

Getting Started

  1. Formulate a question: Make it specific but open-ended (avoid yes/no questions). Address the oracle directly as a source of power.
  2. Set the tone: Approach the runes with seriousness and respect, but maintain joy in your heart.
  3. Prepare: Clear your mind and eliminate distractions.

Single-Rune Spread

This is the simplest method for quick engagement.

  1. Stillness: Take a moment to center yourself.
  2. Focus: Speak your question aloud over the runes.
  3. Draw: Choose a single rune without looking.

Use for:

  • Daily focus
  • Checking the energy of a situation
  • Identifying immediate obstacles or influences

Three-Rune Spread

A spread for deeper insight, often referencing the Triple Goddess or the flow of time. Draw three runes and place them from right to left.

1. Right (The Present/You):

Represents your current state and what you need to hear right now.

2. Center (The Inner/Emotion):

Reveals your internal emotional landscape. Meditate on this rune for clarity on your feelings.

3. Left (The External/Environment):

Shows outside influences and how others see the situation. Guides your next steps.

Five-Rune Spread

Based on the pentagram and the connection of mind, body, and spirit.

1. Top (Head): You in the present situation.

2. Right (Left Hand): The obstacle or focus, what you must take in hand.

3. Bottom Left (Left Foot): The past events leading to this moment.

4. Bottom Right (Right Foot): The next step you must take.

5. Left (Right Hand): The near-term future or what is within reach.

(Note: Directional instructions are sunwise/clockwise).

Ten-Rune Spread

A pyramid-shaped spread for deep psychological dives and complex queries.

Layer 1 (Bottom, 4 Runes): Foundation & Future

Read right to left:

  1. Distant past influence
  2. Recent past influence
  3. Near future influence
  4. Far future implications

Layer 2 (Middle, 3 Runes): The Present

The standard three-rune spread (You, Inner Emotion, External Influence).

Layer 3 (Top, 2 Runes): Guidance

  1. The obstacle
  2. The solution or way to integrate the obstacle

Capstone (Topmost, 1 Rune): Outcome

The final culmination of the reading.

Keep a journal of your spreads. Runes are mysterious, and meanings often clarify over time.

Ogham-Specific Spreads

Since Ogham is a tree oracle and calendar system, casting requires attunement to nature and time.

Preparation & Timing

  • Connection: Connect with tree archetypes like Dryads (female tree spirits) or the Green Man. Best consulted outdoors.
  • Moon Phases:
    • Waxing/Full: For beginnings and new endeavors.
    • Waning/New: For closure and endings.

Ogham Three-Rune Spread (Seasonal)

Use this spread to determine the timing and duration of events.

  1. Rune 1 (The Season/Inception): Reveals the time of year the situation began.
  2. Rune 2 (Duration): Indicates how long the effects will last or the time required to prepare for the next phase.
  3. Rune 3 (Resolution): Forecasts the time of year when the energy will shift, loose ends will tie up, or completion will occur.

Ogham Five-Rune Spread (Vertical Line)

This spread uses a vertical alignment, mirroring the traditional orientation of Ogham script.

Layout: Place runes in a vertical line from bottom to top.

  1. Position 1 (Bottom): Distant past or past life influences.
  2. Position 2 (Below Center): Recent past influences just behind you.
  3. Position 3 (Center, First Drawn): Represents The Self and your present state of mind.
  4. Position 4 (Above Center): Near future influences (within the calendar year).
  5. Position 5 (Top): Distant future consequences (beyond the present year).

Ogham Ten-Rune Spread (Star/Circle)

Modeled after medieval star motifs, this complex spread examines the psyche and situation from all angles.

Layout:

  1. Center: The Self in the present moment.
  2. Crosses Center: The Obstacle or Challenge.
  3. Above Center: Highest hopes, desire, and best possible outcome.
  4. Below Center: Action to take or energy leading to the outcome.
  5. Right: External influences (Family, Community, Connections).
  6. Left: Inner emotions and internal state of balance.
  7. Northeast (between 3 & 5): Weakness to address.
  8. Southeast (between 4 & 5): Outward appearance and surrounding environmental energy.
  9. Southwest (between 4 & 6): Rune of Strength.
  10. Northwest (between 6 & 3): Future influences manifesting from the present course.
