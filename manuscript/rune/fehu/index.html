Modern Relevance

Fehu symbolizes livestock, which equated to wealth and social status in ancient times. Today, it represents ability, comfort, and success. Externally, it signifies respect and achievement; internally, it marks personal accomplishment.

Cosmological Lore

Fehu is the raw archetypal energy of motion and expansion in the multiverse. It is the force that flows from Muspellsheimr, the source of cosmic fire, from which Midgard was produced. This rune embodies the all-encompassing, omnipresent power symbolized by the charging bovine herd and by wildfire.

The cosmic fire of Muspellsheimr plays a dual role: it is instrumental in the creation of the world, but it is also the principal agent of its destruction at Ragnarok. The fire giant Surtr spreads the flames of destruction over everything, leaving only those gods and mortals who are destined to survive or be reborn on the Idha plain (the shining plain). Fehu is not the undifferentiated power of cosmic fire itself, but rather the mystery of its eternal working throughout the multiverse.

The Mystery of Becoming

Fehu contains the mystery of both creation and destruction, and the harmonious functioning of these two extremes. This harmony generates dynamic evolutionary force. It is the rune of eternal becoming, describing the holy cycle of arising, being, passing away, and new arising. Fehu is the archetypal force that gives impetus to this eternal process.

This rune rules the basic force of fertility.

Mobile Power and the Hamingja

The essence of Fehu is mobile power. The root word originally meant mobile wealth or property, which was later attached to livestock (the primary form of movable wealth among the earliest Germanic peoples) and eventually to money.

In the psycho-magickal realm, this concept of mobile force connects to the old Germanic idea of the hamingja. The hamingja is an aspect of the psychosomatic complex best described as a mobile and transferable magickal power. Often translated as "luck" or even "guardian spirit," the hamingja can be sent forth from an individual in a manner similar to the "astral body" in other traditions. Fehu is the directed expansive force that facilitates the projection of soul entities and magickal power from person to person, or from a person to an object.

In mythology, the power of the F-rune often manifests as an otherworldly glow around grave mounds and hills, or as a ring of fire.

Rune Character

Fehu begins the Futhark alphabet and is the first rune of the first ætt. It characterizes the beast of burden: slavish, slow, domesticated, mild, and cowardly. This is the sending rune used in magick, embodying the forces of growth, wandering, and destruction.

Stadha

Both arms slanted upward, the left being somewhat higher. Fingers pointed for directing power, palms pointed outward for drawing force. Face toward the sun.

Magickal Workings

  1. Strengthens psychic powers
  2. Channel for power transference or projection (the sending rune)
  3. Drawing the projected power of the sun, moon, and stars into the personal sphere
  4. Promotion of personal and social evolution
  5. Increase in personal monetary wealth

Constructive Applications

Wealth, possessions, honors, property, money, and expansion. Power over one's environment, increase in wealth, fertility, and mobility. These sigils can be applied directly to anything to imbue the magickal properties the rune represents, or used alongside spells to increase their rate of success and power.

Baneful Applications

Instills cowardliness and dullness. Breaks the spirit. Binds an enemy. Instills fear and dependence in an enemy.
