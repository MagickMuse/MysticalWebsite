Spindle Tree

Oir is the second few of the Fifth Aicme (Forfeda) of the Ogham alphabet. These are additional letters not always used in the Ogham divination system—latecomers to the fews as the language developed. They don't traditionally have trees associated with them in the same way as the main Ogham, but later practitioners have assigned correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Oir is associated with the spindle tree (Euonymus europaeus). Drawing Oir may indicate obligations that must be honored, questions that need answering, or an approaching epiphany. The spindle's association with spinning and weaving connects it to fate, destiny, and the threads we weave in our lives.

Keywords

Honor, fulfillment, questioning.

Traditional Uses

It is little surprise that spindle wood was used for spinning spindles, knitting needles, and weaving bobbins—tools that transform raw fiber into useful thread. Like yew, all parts of the spindle tree are toxic and should not be ingested. Magically, spindle is associated with obligations, epiphanies, and the fulfillment of duties. Its connection to spinning links it to fate-weaving and the Norns.

Working with Oir

Draw on Oir when facing obligations that require honor, seeking answers to important questions, or when you sense an epiphany approaching. This rune supports those who take their commitments seriously and weave their fate with intention. It favors those who question deeply and fulfill their duties with integrity.
