Coll is the fourth few of the Second Aicme of the Ogham alphabet. According to Old Irish myth, Ogham takes its name from Ogma, the god of speech, and each symbol represents a particular tree and its characteristics.

Divination Meaning

Coll represents the hazel tree—a symbol of wisdom. This rune is both pithy and succinct. Coll encourages direct communication with confidence. The thoughtfully written email or timely text will be well received, and negotiations look favorable. Coll supports those who must speak their truth with confidence, reminding us that wisdom expressed clearly carries power.

Keywords

Agility, stamina, sincerity.

Traditional Uses

Hazel has a long tradition in the making of magician's wands and therefore in spellcasting. This is another wood associated with weather magic and protection against storms. Hazel is also linked to meditation and inner journeys, supporting pathworking and wisdom-seeking. A forked hazel stick is favored by dowsers for water divining. Walking staves are often made from hazel, sometimes with wild honeysuckle spiraling around them.

Working with Coll

Draw on Coll when clear and direct communication is needed, when agility and stamina must serve your goals, or when negotiations are on the horizon. This rune supports those who value sincerity and wish to strengthen their position through honest expression. It favors those who can distill wisdom into clear, powerful words.
