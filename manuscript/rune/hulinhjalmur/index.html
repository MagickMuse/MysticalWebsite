Rune Grimoire

Hulinhjalmur

Type
Icelandic Stave
Notes/Warnings
The historical construction involves human and animal blood and remains. Recorded as folklore only, never as instructions. The old lore notes it has no counter stave to restore visibility.

Overview

The Hulinhjalmur is an Icelandic stave said to make its bearer invisible. The recipe recorded in the old grimoires is notoriously elaborate, calling for the sign to be engraved on lignite with blood hardened steel, using an ink blended from human and raven blood along with animal and human remains.

A Word of Caution

This entry preserves the stave as historical folklore, not as a working method. The early texts themselves carry a disclaimer, noting that Hulinhjalmur has no counter stave to undo the invisibility once it is granted.

⚠️
The traditional ingredients described in old grimoires include human blood and body parts. This is kept here only as historical lore and is not a practice to attempt or recreate.
