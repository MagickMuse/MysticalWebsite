Modern Relevance

Jera connects to the harvest and the span of a year. Success requires patience and will arrive only after the work is complete. This rune signifies sowing, tending, and gathering, with rewards based on prior investment.

Tree, Element, and Deity

Tree: oak

Element: earth

Deity: Freya

Meaning: the cycle of time, fruition

Phonetic value: j (pronounced y as in "yard"). In the later Old Norse period, after about 600 CE, the initial j was lost in the West Norse dialects and thereafter the rune stood for a.

Esoteric interpretation: life cycle, cycle of the sun

Ideographic interpretation: the holy marriage of heaven and earth, or the dynamic rotation of the summer-winter cycle

The Cyclical Pattern of the Universe

Jera embodies the cyclical pattern of the universe expressed in the formula: arising, being/becoming, passing-away to new arising. This is a basic pattern working throughout the rune row. The J-rune is one of the two "central runes" in the scheme of the Elder Futhark, and it defines the cyclical nature of the ever-becoming horizontal plane. It is the secret of the omnipresent circumference.

This is the mystery of the twelve-fold cycle of the yearly solar path. Raidho is the daily path and guiding force of the sun, Jera her yearly path, and Sowilo the archetypal sun herself.

Reward and Fruition

Jera is the reward for honorable, right, and lawful (natural) past action. This has no real moral implications; it is a natural law. If the sowing is done correctly, according to tradition, and hamingja (luck) is with you, then the reaping should be great. It is the fruition of efforts well spent toward a willed or instinctual goal. This is true and valid for the numinous as well as phenomenological realms.

Cosmic Fertility and the Eagle

The cosmic fertility aspect of this rune points to the Vanic god Freyr, who is invoked "til árs ok fridhar" (for good season and peace).

The Old Norse name ár provides the popular association of this rune with the eagle (Old Norse ari) as a symbol of the swift flight of the archetypal sun.

Rune Character

A rune of cycles, symbolic of the harvest where the efforts of planting and work in the fields are rewarded with crops. Ar represents the cycles of change: life cycles, lunar cycles, the cycles of the seasons. Ar is in contrast to Iss where everything stops. It signifies the return of the Sun and brings action. Ar symbolizes a vortex of cycling energy, the eightfold wheel of life, the point inside of the circle, which is the glyph for the Sun meaning regeneration.

When used in magickal operations, it can bring a reversal of personal fortunes. Like the Tarot card the Wheel of Fortune, Ar can reverse circumstances so misfortune is replaced with luck and vice versa. A rune of patience and awareness, moving in harmony with natural cycles. Excellent for working with nature and a rune of fruitfulness. Ingwaz is the seed planted, Berkano is the earth that receives it, and Jera is the growth and the harvest. A rune of long-term planning and persistence that ensures the success of plans. Also helpful as a guide in the correct timing of rituals, especially initiation.

Constructive Applications

Brings a reversal of personal fortunes, replacing misfortune with luck. A rune of patience, awareness, and fruitfulness. Excellent for working with nature and natural cycles. Ensures the success of long-term plans. Serves as a guide in the correct timing of rituals, especially initiation.

Baneful Applications

When used in baneful magick, this rune can bring the worst possible aspects of an individual's wyrd to manifest and develops the forces of self-destruction. Can also reverse circumstances so luck is replaced with misfortune.
