Witch Hazel

Amhangholl is the fifth few of the Fifth Aicme (Forfeda) of the Ogham alphabet. These are additional letters not always used in the Ogham divination system—latecomers to the fews as the language developed. They don't traditionally have trees associated with them in the same way as the main Ogham, but later practitioners have assigned correspondences.

Divination Meaning

Amhangholl (also spelled Eamhancholl) is associated with witch hazel (Hamamelis mollis). Drawing Amhangholl may indicate a need for careful scrutiny, revision of plans or beliefs, or restoration of something lost or damaged. The witch hazel's early blooming brings hope after winter's darkness.

Keywords

Scrutiny, revision, restoration.

Traditional Uses

Witch hazel's bright yellow flowers bloom at the end of winter, announcing the very welcome arrival of spring. Distilled witch hazel is found in pharmacies, where it is used for abrasions, mouthwashes, skin toner, and more—demonstrating its restorative and healing properties. Magically, witch hazel is said to bring hope and to help with finding lost items. It supports careful examination and the revision of what no longer serves.

Working with Amhangholl

Draw on Amhangholl when careful scrutiny is needed, when plans require revision, or when seeking to restore something to wholeness. This rune supports those who must examine situations closely before acting and those seeking lost objects or lost hope. It favors those who understand that endings contain the seeds of new beginnings, like the first flowers after winter.
