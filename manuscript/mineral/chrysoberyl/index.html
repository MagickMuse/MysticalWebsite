Magickal Properties

Chrysoberyl is an orthorhombic beryllium aluminate whose name is drawn from two Greek roots: chrysos, meaning golden, and beryllos, a reference to its high beryllium content. Despite this etymological connection, chrysoberyl is a distinct mineral species with its own structure and composition, unrelated to the beryl family. It has been prized across Asia for thousands of years as a powerful shield against the evil eye. In biblical tradition, it is attributed to Asher, described as the most prosperous and joyful of the twelve tribes of Israel, and is listed among the stones set in the breastplate of Aaron.

Chrysoberyl is fundamentally a stone of generous abundance. Its energetic signature is one of open-handed giving: it amplifies the frequency of generosity, forgiveness, and altruism, and then reflects those qualities back as prosperity and abundance flowing into the practitioner's own life. For the beginner, it softens the habitual tendency toward judgment or defensiveness, encouraging a genuine ability to see the good in others and understand perspectives that differ from one's own. It makes forgiveness feel natural rather than forced, and it orients the practitioner toward acts of service to people and the environment.

For the advanced practitioner, chrysoberyl is an activator of potential. It supports the recognition of one's own divine nature and inherent worth, clearing the internal resistance that blocks the graceful reception of prosperity. It aligns the practitioner with their highest self, opening a channel for wisdom that can guide major life decisions for the benefit of all involved. Used in healing grids, chrysoberyl is said to ensure that the healing achieved during a session reaches the root cause of the presenting issue, making the results lasting rather than symptomatic.

Chakra Work

Chrysoberyl bridges the Solar Plexus and Heart Chakras, linking personal power with the capacity for love and compassion. This connection is central to its abundance work: abundance that flows from chrysoberyl is not purely personal gain but is rooted in generosity and genuine service. Placed on the Solar Plexus, it reinforces a stable, grounded sense of self and supports the manifestation of heart-aligned goals. Placed on the Heart, it opens the practitioner to receiving as well as giving, dissolving the blocks that make prosperity feel unsafe or undeserved. Placed on the Third Eye, chrysoberyl is said to offer visionary access to future possibilities, with the understanding that all possible futures remain mutable and responsive to present choices.

Did You Know?

Arabic tradition holds that chrysoberyl has the power to render its bearer invisible during battle, a quality that speaks to the long-standing association between this stone and protection in high-stakes situations.

The True Cat's Eye

Chrysoberyl is the true Cat's Eye. Fine fibres running in a single direction within the stone reflect light in a moving band, the effect known as chatoyancy that gives the cat's eye its name.
