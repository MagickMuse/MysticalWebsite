Magnesite is a form of magnesium carbonate with a trigonal crystal structure, registering 3.5 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale. Often mistaken for howlite, the two are very different in both chemical composition and metaphysical properties, as howlite is a silico-calcite.

Appearance

Size and form varies widely, may be brain-like, chalky and marbled, or crystalline. Generally white in color, but depending on other minerals present, it can also come in shades of gray, brown, yellow, and even pink.

Origin

Named for its mineral makeup and its discovery site, Magnesia, Greece, where it was first identified in 1785. Also found in Austria, Brazil, Italy, and Poland.

Attributes

Magnesite brings a deep peace to meditation and relaxation. Placed on the third eye, this stone enhances visualization and imagery. It opens the heart chakra and stimulates heartfelt love, including love for the self, which is necessary before you can embrace love from other people.

Magnesite can be very helpful in the practice of unconditional love in situations where relationships with other people are difficult because of their behavior or addictions. It helps you to feel centered, standing by placidly and allowing the other person to be totally who they are without requiring them to change or being affected yourself in any way by their difficulties.

Psychologically, Magnesite brings to the surface all forms of self-deceit. It helps to recognize unconscious thoughts and feelings and to explore the reason for these, taking you back into the past if necessary. It induces a positive attitude to life.

The brain-like form of Magnesite has a powerful effect on the mind, bringing the hemispheres into harmony and stimulating ideas and their application.

Magnesite brings a calming effect to the emotions, promoting tolerance for emotional stress. It supports people who are nervous and fearful and helps them to overcome irritability and intolerance.

Magickal Properties

Magnesite has a soothing, loving energy. It opens your inner vision and encourages you to listen to your heart. It is a humble example of powerful energy, serving as a reminder that power has nothing to do with outward appearance.

For beginners, magnesite allows you to live your life in line with your heart's needs and soul's deepest desires by getting you into a state of flow. It helps you heal and relax so that you stop trying to manage and control all aspects of life. In doing this, you discover the space where the magick happens, whether you call it Zen, flow, universal energy, or the creative void.

For advanced practitioners, magnesite is a potent stone for those who seek the nuances hidden below the surface of any situation. It helps you understand the language of your heart, not in words, but in sensations and emotions. By connecting you with your true feelings, it also helps you recognize red flags in relationships. It reduces feelings of codependency and allows you to remain an individual while still in a romantic partnership.

Chakra Work

When placed on both the Third Eye and Heart chakras, magnesite stills the mind so that you can hear what your heart is saying. On the Third Eye and Crown chakras, it encourages you to be honest with yourself through positive and healthy levels of introspection, doing away with pretense so that you can live more authentically. On the Third Eye Chakra alone, it stimulates the development of psychic abilities. When added to manifestation grids, magnesite strengthens the power of your intentions and facilitates the materialization of your dreams.

Healing

Magnesite contains a high level of magnesium and aids its absorption in the body. It detoxifies and neutralizes body odor, acts as an antispasmodic and muscle relaxant, and treats menstrual, stomach, intestinal, and vascular cramps and the pain from gallbladder and kidney stones. Magnesite treats bone and teeth disorders and prevents epilepsy. It relieves headaches, especially migraines, and slows blood clotting. Magnesite speeds up fat metabolism and disperses cholesterol, preventing arteriosclerosis and angina. It is a useful preventative for heart disease. It balances body temperature, lessening fevers and chills.

Position

Place as appropriate in contact with the skin. Can be used as a gem essence for internal or external application. Place on the Third Eye and Heart chakras together to still the mind and hear your heart.

Lore

Magnesite was first identified in 1785 in Magnesia, Greece, and was named for both its mineral makeup and its place of discovery. Due to its high magnesium content, it is said to help with muscle spasms and tension when applied topically. The brain-like form of magnesite has a particularly powerful effect on the mind, bringing the hemispheres into harmony and stimulating ideas and their application.
