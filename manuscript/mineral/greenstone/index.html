Attributes

Greenstone is a generic term for green healing stones. In New Zealand, Pounamu (green Jade) is the sacred greenstone and has protective properties. Pounamu may not leave New Zealand unless it is given as a gift - and then it should return from whence it came when its work is complete. According to the Maoris, the stone chooses its owner. In China, Jade is the stone of heaven. In many cultures, Greenstone symbolizes the heart of the earth itself.

Psychologically, Greenstone helps you to find emotional balance and to recognize that you are exactly where you need to be for your soul growth. It assists in making decisions and facilitates looking at situations from different perspectives. Greenstone is a useful support during ritual or ceremony as it connects you to your ancestors and to the wisdom they held.

Mentally, Greenstone creates calm and assists memory retention. It is an excellent stone for meditation and dreamwork. Environmentally, it can be programmed to heal the earth.

Healing

Greenstone may assist the heart, blood, lungs and nervous system. It has been used to treat disorders of the hips and spleen and for water retention.

Position

Wear or carry over the heart or place as appropriate.
