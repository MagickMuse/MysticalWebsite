Marcasite is a polymorph of pyrite, meaning they share the same chemical makeup but differ in structure. It has an orthorhombic crystal structure and registers 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale. It is lighter and more easily fractured than pyrite, forming at lower temperatures and in more acidic environments.

Appearance

Opaque metallic crystal with brassy lustre.

Origin

Found in Myanmar. Marcasite has been utilized in jewelry by the ancient Greeks, Thracians, and Egyptians. The Incan culture of South America also used marcasite in jewelry and ritual items dedicated to the worship of the sun.

Attributes

Marcasite is an iron sulfide that enhances masculine energy and assertiveness. This stone is excellent for those who need to develop their yang qualities or who have difficulty standing up for themselves. Marcasite encourages courage, determination and the ability to take decisive action.

This crystal stimulates intellectual abilities and enhances mental clarity. It helps to organize thoughts and to see situations objectively without emotional interference. Marcasite is particularly useful for those in positions of leadership as it enhances authority and the ability to make difficult decisions.

Spiritually, Marcasite helps to ground spiritual energy into practical action. It assists those who tend to be overly dreamy or impractical by encouraging them to manifest their visions in the physical world.

Working with marcasite to gain insight into these patterns can give you deeper understanding of your choices, directing you toward your highest potential. Lore
