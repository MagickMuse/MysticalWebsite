Shiva Lingam is a sacred form of cryptocrystalline quartz in the Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, named after the Hindu god Shiva. It comes exclusively from India's sacred Narmada River and is gathered from the water only once a year during pilgrimage. Also known as Narmada River Stone.

Appearance

Shiva Lingam presents as smooth, opaque, egg-shaped or phallic formations in red, brown, beige, tan, and grey tones. The traditional variegated form features red and brown markings representing blood and feminine energy, while tan and neutral background tones represent masculine energy. A well-balanced piece with even distribution of both shades is considered the highest quality and purity. The lines are also said to mirror the markings on Shiva's forehead. Black Shiva lingams are especially rare and prized; historically, wherever one was found, a temple had to be erected. Natural specimens are uncommon, and many pieces available today may be artificially shaped and polished.

Magickal Properties

Shiva Lingam is a stone of intimacy, sexuality, and vitality that has been sacred for thousands of years. Said to have shattered and coagulated within Earth following a massive meteoric collision, it symbolizes the union of Shiva with his consort Kali, raising and controlling kundalini energy. It facilitates the union of opposites, bridging masculine and feminine, body and soul, and serves as a powerful tool for sexual healing and spiritual evolution through tantra and sexual magick.

For beginners, Shiva Lingam activates sexual energy and draws soul mates into the keeper's life. It reignites creative flow when the keeper feels stuck, inspiring action and kindling motivation. It renews passion and deepens intimacy within existing relationships. It imparts psychological insight, helping the keeper look within and release patterns that have been outgrown. It is particularly supportive for emotional pain rooted in early childhood, reinstating trust and drawing healing partners forward.

Advanced practitioners recognize that Shiva Lingam resonates with all four elements. "Lingam" is the sacred Sanskrit word for "phallus," and in Tantric practice, its shape represents the masculine energies of dynamic expression and knowledge alongside the cosmic egg, embodying the feminine powers of wisdom and intuition. When suitably programmed, it severs subtle etheric sexual connections after a relationship has ended, re-energizing the base chakras and clearing the way for new bonds. It transmits blessings of spiritual enlightenment, prosperity, and fertility, and serves as a powerful tool for creating self-loving rituals to reclaim feminine power.

Known as the sex stone, Shiva Lingam activates the Sacral Chakra, the energy center of vitality, pleasure, desire, and creativity. The Sacral Chakra is the origin of everything the keeper births into the world. When placed on this energy center, the stone holds the energies of universal consciousness, activating Kundalini energy and elevating feelings and experiences of love, sensuality, and sexuality. It also stimulates the electrical flow through the body's systems and subtle meridians.

Meditation

Meditating with Shiva Lingam at the Sacral Chakra opens a direct channel to both masculine and feminine divine energies. Hold the stone over the lower abdomen to awaken dormant creative and sexual power. The egg-shaped form naturally contains and circulates energy during meditative practice, making it ideal for Kundalini work and Tantric meditation.

Care

Shiva Lingam is durable at Mohs 7, safe for both water cleansing and sun exposure. Its connection to the Narmada River makes water cleansing especially resonant. Charge under moonlight to honor its feminine energies, or under sunlight to activate its masculine aspect. Its smooth, polished form makes it ideal for carrying as a pocket stone or placing directly on the body during healing sessions.
