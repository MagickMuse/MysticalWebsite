Picture Jasper is a trigonal mineral and one of the most sought-after varieties of jasper. Its intricate, landscape-like patterns are formed by petrified or silicate mud and dendritic inclusions, giving each piece a unique visual story. Sometimes called Desert Rose for its resemblance to a flower, Picture Jasper is found in South Dakota, United States.

Appearance

Opaque stone featuring elaborate, earth-toned patterns that often resemble landscapes, desert scenes, or natural imagery. Colors range from tans and browns to creams, with dark dendritic markings throughout.

Origin

United States (South Dakota)
