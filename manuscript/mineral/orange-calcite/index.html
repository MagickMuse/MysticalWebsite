Orange Calcite's Journey to You

Orange calcite is a calcium carbonate mineral and a stone of vitality, creativity, and sexual energy. Its warm orange color comes from hematite inclusions, and the depth of that color varies with the concentration of hematite present. It forms as microcrystalline masses rather than individual points, meaning its individual crystals are too small to see without magnification. Found across igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rock formations worldwide, it is one of the more widely distributed calcite varieties. Indigenous Mexicans, who called it the Burning Stone, believed it accumulated solar energy throughout the day and used that stored power to protect against negative and harmful forces at night.
