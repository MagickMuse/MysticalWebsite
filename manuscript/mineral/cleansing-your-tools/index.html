Cleansing Your Tools

Any object may be spiritually cleansed using various cleansing techniques. Always make sure the technique will not harm the object. (Sprinkling Indigo Water on a crystal is fine. If you soak fabric in Indigo Water, it will turn blue.)

General Methods

  • Pass objects through incense smoke
  • Fumigate with a smudge stick
  • Asperge with cleansing waters: Holy Water, Marie Laveau Water, and Rose of Jericho Water are particularly effective; choose what suits your purposes best
  • Salt water provides a strong cleansing, providing the object will not be damaged by salt
  • Singing bowls will cleanse objects as well as areas and individuals

Cleansing Crystals

Crystals and gemstones absorb and store energy. Depending upon how they are used, they may need to be cleansed periodically and re-charged.

Whether to cleanse is a personal decision. Some practitioners never cleanse their stones, believing that whatever energy a crystal carries is part of its intended journey. Others cleanse immediately upon acquiring a stone and again after use or after others have handled it. Neither approach is wrong. Intuition is a reliable guide here. If the source of a stone is known and trusted, cleansing may not feel necessary. Some practitioners intentionally avoid clearing stones whose origin or journey holds meaning for them.

Some stones do not require cleansing at all. Black kyanite, for example, does not hold or accumulate a charge and needs no clearing. Programmable stones like quartz, on the other hand, absorb energy and vibration readily and benefit most from regular cleansing.

  • Bury the stone in the ground overnight or longer. Most potent if done outside, but a flowerpot filled with dirt will work too. Mark the burial spot if you wish to retain the stone.
  • Expose the crystal to sunlight and/or moonlight
  • Pass the crystal through incense smoke
  • Many clear crystals may be cleansed with salt water: collect ocean water or add sea salt to spring water
  • Many Charged Waters may be used to cleanse crystals, particularly Florida Water or Notre Dame Water
  • Salt may leach color from a vivid crystal or damage a fragile one like moldavite: bathe these in spring water with a few drops of Dr. Bach's Rescue Remedy or a similar emergency flower remedy formula

The new moon is the phase most aligned with crystal cleansing. Its dark, receptive quality supports the release and purging of accumulated energy. Additional methods beyond those listed above:

  • Smoke: Sacred herbs and resins are potent cleansers. White sage (Salvia apiana), palo santo, and copal are among the most effective. Passing stones through the smoke resets them to their base energetic frequency, clearing anything accumulated in transit or use.
  • Water: Running water and collected natural water are powerful cleansing mediums. Placing stones in a bowl of spring water or recently gathered ocean water clears unwanted energy and reconnects the crystal to the energy of the earth. Always confirm a stone is water-safe before using this method.
  • Sound and frequency: The solfeggio frequency of 417 Hz, rooted in Gregorian chant traditions dating to the eleventh century, is used specifically for clearing unwanted energy. Play this frequency near your stones or use a tuning fork calibrated to 417 Hz. Drumming and chanting are equally valid sound-based approaches.
  • Intention and breath: Slow, deliberate exhalation directed across a stone with focused clearing intention is an accessible and effective method. Conscious breath connects personal life force with the crystal's energy, resetting its vibration to align with a specific purpose.
  • Clearing stones: Selenite and black kyanite neither accumulate nor hold a charge, so they never require cleansing themselves. Simply touching either stone to another crystal transmutes and clears accumulated energy. Both can also serve as passive cleansers when placed near other stones in a collection.

Charging and Programming

When first acquiring a stone, it is natural to wonder whether it is already aligned with a specific intention or whether it needs to be directed. Crystals can be charged and programmed for focused work, particularly quartz. Due to its inherent electric charge, quartz carries a stronger natural frequency and a broader electromagnetic field than most other stones, making it especially responsive to intentional programming.

Charging: Place the stone outside or on a windowsill under the full moon. As the lunar light infuses it, hold a clear intention for how the crystal will serve. The full moon is associated astrologically with Cancer, a sign connected to intuition, emotional depth, and receptive wisdom. This is the optimal time to charge a stone with a specific purpose, whether that is clarity, healing, abundance, or something else entirely.

Programming: Once charged, hold the crystal in the nondominant (receiving) hand and cover it with the dominant (projecting) hand. Visualize the dominant hand transmitting the specific intention directly into the stone. This process is sometimes called coding a crystal. The stone then carries that energetic instruction until it is deprogrammed.

Deprogramming: When a crystal is needed for a different purpose, it can be decoded and reset. The new moon, aligned with releasing and letting go, is the most supportive phase for this process.

Cleansing Crystal Balls

It is crucial that crystal balls and scrying glass are kept cleansed, both physically and spiritually. A dusty crystal ball reveals nothing. A crystal ball covered with negative spiritual debris may reveal distortions.

Cleanse with any of the following:

  • A blend of spring water and vinegar
  • A charged water: Holy Water, Marie Laveau Water, and Notre Dame Water are particularly recommended
  • A water-based herbal infusion made with dried mugwort and/or anise seeds
  • Mugwort hydrosol

Add flower essence remedies to any of the above as desired. Mugwort flower essence enhances the crystal ball's powers. Dr. Bach's Rescue Remedy or a similar emergency formula will remove and assimilate disturbing or traumatic visions seen within.
