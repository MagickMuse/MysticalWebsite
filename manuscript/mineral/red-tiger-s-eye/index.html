Red Tiger's Eye is a variety of tiger's eye in the Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7. Its distinctive rust and brick-red coloring results from oxidized iron within the stone. The ancient Egyptians associated tiger's eye with the sun god Ra and revered it as a stone of prosperity and abundance. In ancient Rome, tiger's eye was believed to bring fortitude and protection during battle. In parts of Africa, it is believed to ward off negative influences and harmful spirits. Red Tiger's Eye, in particular, is coveted as a stone of power, confidence, and higher consciousness.

Appearance

Red Tiger's Eye presents as red-banded specimens with a slightly shiny, chatoyant surface. It is often found small and tumbled, displaying shades of rust, brick red, and deep crimson interlaced with the characteristic silky luster of the tiger's eye family.

Colors

Rust, brick red, deep crimson, reddish-brown

Origin

Australia, Brazil, Canada, India, Namibia, South Africa, United States
