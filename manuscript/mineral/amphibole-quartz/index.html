💰Prosperity Stone

Amphibole quartz is a silicon dioxide mineral with hexagonal and trigonal crystal structures, registering 7 on the Mohs scale. Its name comes from the Greek "amphibolos" meaning ambiguous, a reference to the range of minerals that comprise the sprays of needle-shaped inclusions within this stone. Also known as angel wing phantom quartz due to its gossamer winglike inclusions, it is found in only one region of Brazil. The minerals within amphibole quartz can include actinolite, tremolite, hornblende, hematite, limonite, and lithium.
