Natrolite is a zeolite-group mineral crystallizing in the Orthorhombic system with a Mohs hardness of 5 to 5.5. Known as the great cleanser and activator of the Synergy Twelve group, Natrolite is energetically aligned with the human central nervous system, where it monitors and relays energetic messages. It facilitates contact with the higher self, the aspect of consciousness that knows best how to embody one's most profound truth.

Appearance

Long, lightly ribbed crystal or tumbled stone. Typically white to colorless. Orthorhombic crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 5 to 5.5.

Origin

Found globally. The most important localities are Australia, Austria, Britain, Czech Republic, Germany, Iceland, New Zealand, Russia, Sweden, and the United States (California, Illinois, New Jersey, Oregon).

Attributes

Natrolite prepares and fine-tunes the nervous system to an influx of new vibrations, harmonizing it with the lightbody.

Spiritually, pure white Natrolite can bring about a profound shift and stimulate metaphysical changes to channel high-level energy into the light and physical bodies, enabling living with full multi-dimensional awareness within a protective cloak for the awakened soul.

Mentally this stone assists in seeing the bigger picture and practising holistic thinking from an illumined mind.

Healing

Works beyond the physical level of being, but supports the nervous system.

Position

Hold in the left hand, grid, place over the third eye or soma chakra, or midway between soma and crown chakras.

Magickal Properties

Natrolite is one of the most sensitive stones for early energetic protection, alerting the practitioner before overwhelming energies can take hold. It is an essential tool for healers and empaths who are more affected by vibrational shifts. This stone creates a protective shell of energy around its bearer and helps empaths maintain strong auric boundaries during healing work. It promotes positive energy flow through both the physical and subtle bodies. Natrolite also brings significant pain relief, especially when discomfort originates from nervous system conditions. Place it wherever physical pain is present, or on the heart space for emotional pain. For advanced practitioners, Natrolite is an ally in the energy-gathering process required for spiritual ascension, strengthening spiritual reserves and extending awareness beyond the self toward planetary and universal energies. It is also a stone of deep compassion, revealing the true light and essence of people and experiences.

Chakra Work

Natrolite resonates with the Third Eye, Crown, and Soul Star Chakras. Its high-vibrational energy stimulates these upper centers, channeling powerful frequencies into the light and physical bodies. It supports living with full multi-dimensional awareness while maintaining a protective cloak for the awakened soul.

Correspondences

Partners Well With

Danburite, Fluorite, Larvikite, Moldavite, Seraphinite, and Tanzanite.

Colors

White to colorless, sometimes with a pearly luster.

Magickal Message

"Experience an amazing personal shift as you connect with my high-vibrational energies. If you are committed to elevating your spiritual growth to new levels, I will accelerate the process."

Lore

Rhodatrolite is a rare form of pink Natrolite recently discovered in Indonesia. It has a similar texture to Larimar and is said to bring tranquility, deep heart healing, and serenity.
