Spinel is one of the oldest gemstones on the planet, a magnesium aluminum oxide in the Octahedral crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 8. It is a record keeper of all the energy and wisdom of previous civilizations, associated with the sun and carrying a vibration of bright enthusiasm and courageous optimism.

Appearance

Spinel presents as small crystalline formations with terminations or tumbled pebbles, available in a spectrum of colors from black and red to blue and violet. Each color carries a slightly different mineral composition while sharing the same basic structure. Red spinel is an alternate birthstone for August and has historically been mistaken for ruby due to its deceptively similar appearance in the right quality and light. The name derives from the Italian "spinella," meaning "little thorn," referencing the sharp shape of its octahedral crystals. Readily available, with specific colors carrying additional properties.

Colors

Black, red, blue, violet, green, pink, orange, yellow, brown, colorless

Origin

Found globally, with the most important localities in Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Iceland, Madagascar, Mexico, Russia, United States, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Tanzania, Australia, Vietnam

Mistaken for Ruby

Because fine red spinel so closely resembles ruby, the two have long been confused, and spinels were at times called rubies outright. The Great Balas Ruby set in the British Imperial State Crown is in fact a spinel.

Magickal Properties

Spinel is a manifestation stone connected with energy renewal, encouragement in difficult circumstances, and rejuvenation. It opens the chakras and facilitates the movement of kundalini energy up the spine, allowing energies to manifest through the keeper rather than forcing any situation. It enhances the positive aspects of the personality and aids in achieving and accepting success with humility.

For beginners, Spinel brings confidence, which is why it is so closely tied to manifestation and creation. It helps the keeper believe in the ability to bring dreams to life and encourages utilizing available resources for the highest good. It promotes task completion, ensuring no detail is left unnoticed or incomplete, and brings stamina and a steady energy flow to support long-term projects and goals.

Advanced practitioners recognize Spinel as a stone of healers and leaders that brings vitality, passion, abundance, prosperity, and longevity. During difficult periods, it offers encouragement by helping the keeper appreciate life's beauty and supports movement through moments of crisis. Dark blue Spinel enhances intuition and facilitates astral travel. Green Spinel encourages compassion for all creatures of Earth. Violet Spinel guides the keeper along the path to enlightenment, enriching spiritual connection with the Divine. It can be gridded beneath a bed to support the physical body in recovery from chronic illness and injuries.

Chakra Work

Spinel activates all of the chakras, with each color resonating with a specific energy center. Red Spinel placed on the Root Chakra brings physical strength and stamina while stimulating passion and sexuality. Yellow Spinel on the Solar Plexus Chakra encourages rebuilding of the self-image and how the keeper projects into the world, supporting both physical and emotional transformation. Blue Spinel on the Third Eye Chakra enhances mental clarity and provides fresh perspective on problems. In general, Spinel brings revitalization to the entire energy system. Lay on the chakras or wear as appropriate.

Meditation

Meditating with Spinel opens all chakra points simultaneously, creating a unified flow of kundalini energy from base to crown. Select a color that aligns with the specific intention: red for grounding and passion, blue for clarity and astral work, violet for enlightenment, green for compassion. Hold the stone or place it on the corresponding energy center during meditation to amplify its color-specific properties.

Care

Spinel is exceptionally durable at Mohs 8, safe for both water cleansing and sun exposure. Cleanse under cool running water or place on a selenite plate. Charge under sunlight to honor its solar association, or under moonlight for a gentler recharge. Its hardness makes it ideal for daily wear in jewelry and for carrying as a pocket stone. Different color varieties can be charged with their corresponding elemental energy for enhanced effect.
