Appearance

Apophyllite is a tetragonal crystal from the Zeolite family of minerals, said to have ties to the ancient civilization of Atlantis. It appears as clear, white, green, blue, or violet, transparent to translucent with perfect pyramidal terminations, high vitreous luster. Its pyramid-shaped crystals are sometimes called "pyramids of light" and are said to be gateways to other dimensions, including the galactic library known as the Akashic Records.

Origin

Brazil, Canada, Germany, Greenland, Iceland, India, Italy, Britain, Australia, Czech Republic.

Attributes

Apophyllite has a high water content which makes it a conductor of energy and a carrier of the Akashic Record. This stone facilitates journeys out of the body and spiritual vision. It stimulates intuition and enables the future to be accessed.

Creating a conscious connection between the physical form and the spiritual realm, Apophyllite facilitates attunements and the transfer of energies from the higher realms.

Psychologically, Apophyllite promotes introspection into one's own behavior and the correction of imbalances or flaws that are perceived. This stone releases suppressed emotions. It overcomes anxiety, worries, and fears. It calms apprehensions and allows uncertainty to be tolerated.

Mentally, this crystal stimulates the intellect and promotes a desire for truth. It facilitates the solving of problems by seeing to the heart of the matter and then enables conscious decision-making about what action to take.

Spiritually, Apophyllite works on the astral body and is a powerful vibrational transmitter. It facilitates attunements and the transfer of energies from higher realms. This stone enhances mindfulness and takes you into deeper states of meditation and mystical vision.

Healing

Apophyllite works particularly well as a healing crystal for the respiratory system and can help with the symptoms of asthma. It is said to heal the eyes and aid problems associated with sight. This stone regulates bodily functions and encourages regeneration of the mucus membranes and the skin.

Position

Hold or place as appropriate. Spray with water regularly to increase the power.

Shapes

Pyramid-shaped Apophyllite naturally amplifies and then tightly focuses energy through the apex and is suitable for holding manifestation programs.

Apophyllite Clusters have many points bedded into a base and radiate energy out to the surrounding environment.

Chakra Work

Placed on the Crown Chakra during meditation, spiritual journeying, and astral projection, Apophyllite helps you consciously remember the wisdom gained during spiritual experiences once you return to the physical plane. On the Third Eye Chakra, it grants visions of the future based on your present circumstances, allowing you to see the consequences of your choices with clarity and either make necessary changes or recommit to your current path. Placed on the Heart Chakra, Apophyllite brings emotional healing and purification, helping you acknowledge and transform past pain and heartache into high-vibrational wisdom and enlightenment. For advanced practitioners, it activates the Soul Star Chakra, expanding awareness of how consciousness manifests in all beings and revealing how all things in creation are connected through Source energy.
