Appearance

Pale blue with white or darker lines, banded, often small and tumbled

Origin

United States, India, Morocco, Czech Republic, Brazil, Africa, South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Australia, Madagascar, Uruguay, Germany, Poland

Attributes

Blue Lace Agate is a wonderful healing stone. Its soft energy is cooling and calming, bringing peace of mind. It is particularly effective for activating and healing the throat chakra, allowing free expression of thoughts and feelings. It opens the way to experience of the higher energies. This is one of the great nurturing and supportive stones. It will neutralize anger, infection, inflammation, and fever.

Psychologically, Blue Lace Agate counteracts the repression and suppression of feelings that stem from fear of being judged and rejected. Judgment is often present in the parent-child relationship, both in childhood and adulthood. As a result, feelings are held back and the lack of self-expression blocks the throat chakra and may affect the chest, the feeling being of suffocation. Blue Lace Agate gently dissolves the old pattern of repression and encourages a new mode of expression. It is useful for helping men to release and accept their sensitivity and feeling natures.

Mentally, Blue Lace Agate assists with verbal expression of thoughts and feelings and counteracts mental stress. Emotionally, the peaceful energies exuded from this stone neutralize feelings of anger.

Spiritually, Blue Lace Agate clears the throat chakra so that the highest spiritual truths can be expressed. It is a stone that links thought to the spiritual vibration and brings in deep peace.

Healing

Blue Lace Agate is a powerful throat healer. Its property of counteracting blocked self-expression releases shoulder and neck problems, thyroid deficiencies, and throat and lymph infections. It lowers fevers and removes blockages of the nervous system, and treats arthritic and bone deformity, strengthening the skeletal system and healing fractures. It also aids capillaries and the pancreas. As an elixir, it treats brain fluid imbalances and hydrocephalus. Blue Lace Agate can also be used to enhance sound healing - it focuses and directs sound to the appropriate place.

Position

As appropriate, particularly at the throat

Spellworking

Blue lace agate is a trigonal variety of variegated chalcedony, first discovered in Namibia with smaller deposits found in Brazil, China, India, and South Africa. Its banding and color depth shift depending on the conditions under which it formed. Known as the Muse Stone, it carries the energy of creative inspiration and authentic voice.

Communication & Self-Expression

Blue lace agate is one of the most important stones for Throat Chakra work. Placed at the throat, it supports speaking truth from the heart without fear of judgment. It amplifies the power of words and deepens the effect of spoken affirmations, helping the bearer understand how language shapes reality. For anyone experiencing blocks around creative expression or public communication, including teachers, artists, lawyers, and lecturers, holding blue lace agate in the left hand opens a channel to inspired thought. It works gradually and steadily rather than instantly, encouraging patience and trust in the process.

Angelic Communication

For the advanced practitioner, blue lace agate strengthens the connection to guides and the angelic realm, particularly when seeking answers to specific questions. It sharpens discernment, helping distinguish between genuine intuitive messages and the noise of the imagination. Carrying it also develops the ability to sense the truth beneath another person's words. Pairs well with Aquamarine, Dumortierite, Larimar, Rose Quartz, or Vivianite to deepen communication work and heart-centered expression.

Crystal Grids

As a quartz-bearing stone, blue lace agate is well-suited as a grid centerpiece, especially in grids focused on communication, creative flow, or self-expression. Its calming vibration also makes it effective for easing anxiety, hyperactivity, and tension in the nervous system.

Magickal Message

"It is time now to speak and recognize the power of your words! If you feel uncertain about the right thing to say, I will gently guide you to the words that reflect your inner truth."

Colors

Blue lace agate is pale blue with white or darker banding. The depth and intensity of the blue varies by specimen, ranging from near-white to a soft mid-blue, always with the characteristic layered banding of chalcedony.
