About Purple Fluorite

Purple fluorite is a stone of soul work, growing in hues ranging from soft lilac to deep violet. It forms within the halide mineral family and shares the isometric crystal structure common to all fluorite varieties. Deposits occur across Australia, Colombia, France, Germany, Peru, Spain, the United Kingdom, and several regions of the United States.

Crystal Healing with Purple Fluorite

Purple fluorite activates the Crown Chakra, making it well suited for meditation, inner reflection, and deepening spiritual practice. Placed at the crown of the head or held in both hands during meditation, it sharpens psychic perception, expands awareness beyond ordinary consciousness, and strengthens connection to one's spiritual path. It carries a particular resonance with the concept of dharma, the understanding of one's true nature and the conscious alignment with higher purpose. Working with purple fluorite invites clarity about where one's gifts and life direction intersect, and it rekindles faith during periods of doubt or discouragement.
