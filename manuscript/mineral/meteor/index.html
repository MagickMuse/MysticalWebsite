Magical Lore

Meteorites have long been held in fascination by humans. They have been thought to be gifts from the gods and goddesses. Certain meteorites, such as the Kaaba stone in Mecca and a stone thought to represent the Great Mother Goddess of Phrygia, have been worshipped as symbols of divinity.

A four-ton stone has been revered in China as a holy object since the 1200s. The stone, shaped like a crouching ox, resides in a Buddhist shrine. Geologists determined it to be a meteorite that landed about 1,300 years ago.

In Babylon, the meteorite was a powerful magical protectant, thought to remove all evils due to its strange appearance and the "roar of its awful might."

Peridot is often found in meteorites—green crystals packed inside the extraterrestrial stone. Tiny diamonds have also been found inside meteorites—the first discovered that had formed off of our planet.

At various places on Earth, meteorites were used to explain the origin of life. If rocks fell to the earth from space, so too could plants, water, animals, and people.

Symbolically, meteorites can be viewed as the spiritual penetrating the physical, as astral power, divine order, or cosmic intention.

Protection

Meteorites are unearthly things, literally. They possess the powers of intergalactic flight, movement, speed, and energy unhindered by gravity. Use them in rituals of protection. Place one on the altar near white candles, or carry in the hand.

Astral Projection

Meteorites are called upon to promote astral projection. A small meteorite or a fragment of one is placed beneath the pillow during attempts at conscious astral projection.
