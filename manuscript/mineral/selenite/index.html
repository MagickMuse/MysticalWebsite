About Selenite

Selenite is a variety of gypsum, a mineral with roots stretching back to ancient Egypt, where gypsum served as a building material as far back as 7000 b.c.e. The name derives from the Greek "gypsos," meaning plaster. Gypsum as a mineral family promotes clear energy flow through the chakras and auric field, dissolving blockages and restoring harmony to the energetic body. Selenite specifically is a stone of clearing, moon magick, and spiritual connection.

Ancient Greeks believed the goddess of the moon, Selene, was held within its luminous form, and her qualities of intuition, psychic sight, and vivid dreaming are mirrored in the stone. King Minos of Crete famously built the walls of his palace from selenite so that under the full moon, the structure appeared to glow from within. It is a gem of divine connection, fragile in form yet formidable in spirit.
