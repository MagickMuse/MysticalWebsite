Origin

Myanmar, Czech Republic, Brazil, Kenya, India, Australia, Sri Lanka

Attributes

Yellow Sapphire attracts wealth to the home and can be placed in cash boxes to increase prosperity and earnings. If worn, it should touch the body.

Yellow Sapphire stimulates the intellect and improves overall focus so that the bigger picture is seen.

Healing

As an elixir, it removes toxins from the body.

Spellworking

Yellow Sapphire attracts wealth and prosperity—place in cash boxes to increase earnings. Use it for prosperity spells, intellectual focus, and seeing the bigger picture. Like all yellow stones, it stimulates the solar plexus and enhances mental clarity.

The Nine Gems

In a traditional Indian belief, each of the nine planets is paired with its own precious gem, the set known together as the navaratna. Yellow sapphire is one of the two gems assigned to Jupiter, paired with topaz. The full set assigns the pearl to the Moon, the ruby to the Sun, topaz or yellow sapphire to Jupiter, the diamond to Venus, the emerald to Mercury, red coral to Mars, blue sapphire to Saturn, hessonite garnet to Rahu, and cat's eye to Ketu. Rahu and Ketu are the northern and southern lunar points that come into play during eclipses, which is why they are also called the eclipse planets.
