Spellworking

Energy: Projective | Planet: Sun | Element: Fire | Associated Metal: Gold

Powers: Healing, money, protection

Brass has long been used as a magical substitute for gold. While it doesn't possess all of gold's attributes, brass is effective in money-attracting work.

Money and Prosperity

Place empowered olivine, aventurine, or other money-attracting stones on a piece of brass during prosperity rituals. Inscribe a pentagram on a small piece of brass and carry or wear it to draw wealth.

Healing

Brass has been used in ritual healing. Wearing a brass ring is said to halt stomach cramps. A brass key placed on the nape of the neck or dropped down the back is an old spell to stop nosebleeds.

Protection

Brass jewelry is worn to guard the person. It is used in defensive magic to send negativity back to its sender. Empowered brass objects placed in the home serve protective purposes.
