Pyrite is an iron sulfide mineral with a tetragonal crystal structure, widely recognized as a talisman of wealth and personal empowerment. Its name derives from the Greek word "pyr," meaning fire, a reference to the sparks it produces when struck against metal or other hard surfaces.

Magickal Powers

Prosperity

Origin

Pyrite occurs commonly across the globe in a wide range of geological formations. It begins as sedimentary deposits, progresses through hydrothermal veins, and appears in metamorphic rocks. As it develops, it takes on diverse forms: masses, stalactites, grains, globes, cubes, twelve-sided crystals, and flat radial disks known as "suns" or "dollars." Major deposits are found in Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Myanmar, New Zealand, Peru, Spain, and the United States (notably Connecticut, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, and Washington).

Appearance

Metallic gold luster with a brassy sheen. Forms in cubes, octahedrons, pyritohedrons, and flat disks. Opaque with a greenish-black streak.

Magickal Properties

Pyrite is the stone of confidence and inner fortitude. It embodies possibility, potential, and prosperity, encouraging you to step fully into your power. This mineral enlivens self-image and connects you to solar warmth, helping you become a source of inspiration for others. It brings positivity, vitality, and a sense of overall well-being to your daily life.

For advanced practitioners, Pyrite encourages radical self-acceptance, revealing the full depth of your potential just beneath the surface. It illuminates perceived flaws and shows how every facet of your inner world serves a purpose. When motivation falters and obstacles mount, Pyrite sustains enthusiasm and determination, helping you carry even the most demanding projects through to completion. The four faces of its cubic form represent a cosmic equilibrium of the four elements: earth, air, fire, and water.

Chakra Work

Pyrite activates the Solar Plexus Chakra to amplify confidence and inner strength. Placed on this energy center, it reinforces feelings of self-worth, particularly around feeling deserving of abundance and manifestation. Held or carried in a pocket, it elevates self-esteem and supports you in navigating conflict, especially with people who challenge your sense of self. It also sustains physical energy and infuses the body with vitality. Cubes of Pyrite make powerful additions to crystal grids focused on self-confidence and courage.

Correspondences

Pyrite partners well with Citrine, Clear Quartz, Green Aventurine, Jade, Malachite, Opalite, and Tiger’s Eye.

Colors

Brassy gold, pale gold, metallic yellow

Healing

Pyrite supports strong digestion and is recommended for healing of the intestines and stomach. Placing a cube on the navel during a crystal healing session is a common practice to restore the digestive system and promote balance in the energy field.

Magickal Message

"Meditate with me to tap into your potential, stimulate your flow of ideas, and be persistent in seeing your projects through to completion."

Care

Avoid water exposure entirely, as Pyrite's iron sulfide composition will oxidize and rust. Cleanse with smoke, sound, or selenite. Charge in sunlight for short periods or on a bed of quartz.
