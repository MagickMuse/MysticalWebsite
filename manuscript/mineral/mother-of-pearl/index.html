Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: Intuition, emotional balance, expression, overcoming fear, sea healing, soothing, protection.

Appearance

Iridescent inner layer of mollusk shells, ranging from white to cream with rainbow sheen.

Origin

Australia, Philippines, Japan, Italy, Egypt, United States, China, Indonesia.

Attributes

Mother-of-Pearl is the iridescent lining of mollusk shells, sharing many properties with Pearl. It carries the gentle, nurturing energy of the sea and moon. Mother-of-Pearl soothes and calms emotions while promoting intuition and imagination.

This material stimulates intuition, imagination, and sensitivity. It aids in expressing feelings and adapting to change. Mother-of-Pearl attracts prosperity and is protective.

Healing

Mother-of-Pearl benefits the digestive system and soft tissues.

Position

Wear as jewelry or place on the solar plexus.

Spellworking

Mother-of-pearl is the lustrous, opalescent interior of various sea mollusks. Though not a stone, it has been included here because of its long use in magic. Mother-of-pearl has been used in ritual jewelry throughout the ages. Seashells were the medium of exchange (money) in many parts of the world where metals were scarce or lacking, such as Polynesia.

Since this substance is the product of a living creature—the exterior skeleton, or shell—it is related to the fifth element, Akasha. Collect mother-of-pearl yourself in stream beds or the ocean. Since commercial mother-of-pearl is obtained by killing the animal that created it, it is therefore rather risky to use in magic.

Mystically, it relates to the ocean, to depth, and movement.

Protection

Mother-of-pearl is placed on newborn babies to protect them from the perils of their new existence.

Wealth Talisman Spell

Mother-of-pearl is a fine substance to use in wealth, money, and riches spells. Empower a bit of mother-of-pearl with your magical need for money. Anoint it with seawater (which contains gold) or a money-drawing oil, such as patchouly or cedar. Place a silver coin or any pieces of silver next to the shell. Wrap a dollar bill or green paper tightly around the mother-of-pearl and silver object, and secure with green string.

Place this talisman on your altar between two green candles. Let them flame for ten to fifteen minutes while visualizing. Then carry the charm with you.
