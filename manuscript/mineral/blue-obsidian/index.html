Appearance

Translucent blue, glassy volcanic glass. Often tumbled or polished.

Origin

Mexico, United States

Attributes

Blue Obsidian is a rare variety of obsidian with a soft blue color. Like all obsidian, it is volcanic glass formed from rapidly cooled lava. Blue Obsidian aids communication and helps speak your truth. It is particularly useful for opening and clearing the throat chakra.

Healing

Blue Obsidian is beneficial for the throat and communication issues. It aids speech impediments and helps with clear expression.

Position

Place on the throat chakra or hold during meditation for clarity of expression.

Spellworking

Sacred to Hecate, the patroness of witches, obsidian family stones are favorites for scrying mirrors and protection work. Blue Obsidian adds communication and truth-speaking properties to obsidian's traditional uses. It boosts strength and can help break down blockages related to self-expression.
