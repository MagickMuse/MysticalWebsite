Peach Moonstone is a member of the feldspar family with a monoclinic crystal structure, registering 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale. It displays the same adularescence as other moonstone varieties, with hematite inclusions resulting in its pink to orange inner glow and metallic sheen.

Appearance

Peach to cream colored moonstone with characteristic sheen and a warm pink to orange inner glow. Often tumbled or polished.

Origin

Found in Australia, India, Madagascar, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, and the United States.

Attributes

Peach Moonstone carries all the properties of Moonstone with added warmth and gentle nurturing energy. It supports the heart as it stimulates the mind, soothing worry or anxiety, and bringing out the best in people. Its loving energy promotes the Divine in all situations. Peach Moonstone is particularly supportive for sensitive or intuitive children.

Magickal Properties

Peach moonstone harnesses the creative powers of the moon phases. It is thought to represent the waxing gibbous moon phase, most often aligned with manifestation. It is the emotional healer of the moonstone family, helping you see beneath the surface of your challenges, feel your feelings, and go with the flow.

For beginners, peach moonstone supports you as you let go of what weighs you down and no longer serves you, encouraging you to learn from and forgive your past mistakes and permit the Universe to fill you with all the things you desire now. It builds excitement about what lies ahead in life and supports you as you tap into your true creative potential.

For advanced practitioners, peach moonstone helps you see transformation and the different stages of life as positive experiences, releasing the anxiety that is sometimes associated with change. In times of apathy or depression, it helps reignite your passion and reconnect you with the beauty of your authentic inner self. It encourages you to break stagnant patterns by trying something new or treating yourself with some indulgence and self-care. It shows you that by loving yourself, you can love others more fully, too.

Chakra Work

Peach moonstone is a powerful Sacral Chakra gem that boosts creativity and brings artistic inspiration. Placing it on this energy center allows you to dream about the many ways you express your creativity as well as the creative outlets you have not yet explored. It sparks your passion for innovation and can be gridded throughout your workspace to awaken and receive the wisdom of the Sacral Chakra for inspiration.

Partners Well With

Pair Peach Moonstone with Amethyst for spiritual clarity and emotional balance. Pink Aventurine deepens heart healing and self-love. Rhodochrosite amplifies emotional healing and compassion. Rhodonite strengthens the heart and encourages forgiveness. Sapphire brings wisdom and truth to your creative expression. Sunstone adds joyful solar energy and vitality. Unakite supports emotional release and spiritual growth.

Healing

Peach Moonstone supports the heart and stimulates the mind. It is particularly helpful for anxiety and emotional stress.

Position

Place on the Sacral Chakra, heart, or solar plexus. Wear as jewelry. Grid throughout your workspace for creative inspiration.

Lore

In Hinduism, peach moonstone is considered a stone of happiness and is given as a gift to celebrate life's milestones and new beginnings.

Magickal Message

There are no failures in life if you learn from everything you do. May you always know the depths of your own beauty, your own passion, and your own creativity.

Spellworking

Ruled by the moon, this peachy variety of moonstone has long been used to aid dream recall. It calms emotions, increases intuition, and benefits female health conditions. The warm peach color adds nurturing, heart-centered energy to moonstone's lunar properties. As a translucent stone, it excels in emotional situations.
