Appearance

Zircon is a tetragonal crystal and the oldest mineral on Earth, with origins dating back over 4.4 billion years. Its name comes from the Persian zargun, meaning "gold colored," though it appears in a wide range of colors from blue to red. Small transparent crystals or faceted gemstone in yellow, green, brown, red, and orange colors. Zircon has a deep affinity for magick, making it a favored tool for creating all things magickal, both in ancient times and today.

Origin

Australia, Cambodia, China, Madagascar, Myanmar, Russia, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, Thailand, Vietnam, United States, Brazil, India. Kyrgyzstan is also noted as a source.

Attributes

Zircon is a protective stone that releases toxicity at all levels and stimulates the liver. It helps you to let go of destructive patterns and opens the way to feeling less attached and more objective and spiritual. Spiritually, this stone helps you to detach from worldly concerns, yet nevertheless acts as a powerful earth healer. Zircon insists on being true to yourself and to others rather than following the crowd or trying to meet other people's expectations. Mentally, Zircon brings together the higher and lower minds, uniting spirituality with the everyday world. It synthesizes the spiritual and physical worlds and helps you find your place between them.

Yellow-green Zircon works on the solar plexus and heart chakras. Brown Zircon grounds and connects to the base and earth chakras. Red-orange Zircon is a manifestation stone useful for energizing the sacral chakra and grounding the lightbody in incarnation.

Healing

Zircon is an excellent detoxifier. It is said to be anti-spasmodic and to increase potency and fertility. Beneficial for jealousy, vertigo, insomnia, sciatica and cramps.

A magical substitute for diamond, clear zircon is worn for protection. Use it for clear thinking and to promote mental processes.

Chakra Work

Zircon corresponds primarily to the Root Chakra but is a high-intensity stone that activates all of the primary chakras, including the Earth Star and Soul Star. It has the power to transmute sluggish energies in the chakras and meridians, clearing stagnant energy from both the body and auric field when held in meditation. Zircon brings immense healing and balance to mind, body, and spirit simultaneously. For advanced practitioners, it connects you to Source, brings your goals into divine alignment, and solidifies thought forms into physical reality - facilitating the final step of manifestation by helping you take the thought and create the form.

Lore

Zircon is mentioned in the Book of Exodus in the Old Testament of the Bible as one of the stones of fire given to Moses and subsequently set in the breastplate of Aaron. In ancient Greece and Rome, Zircon was considered one of the most protective amulets and was often worn to safeguard against danger and negativity. During the Middle Ages, it was believed to drive away the evil spirits thought to cause nightmares, ensuring a good night's sleep. Zircon set in gold is said to be doubly powerful for all purposes.
