Appearance

Silvery-white to gray metalite with brilliant luster

Origin

China, Bolivia, Mexico, Russia, South Africa

Attributes

Antimony is a transformative metal that facilitates personal alchemy. It helps you shed old patterns and emerge renewed. Associated with the wild, untamed aspects of nature, it connects you to primal earth energies and assists in embracing your authentic self.

Healing

Antimony is said to support skin health and assist with detoxification processes. Use with caution as it is toxic in its raw form.

Position

Place near you during transformation work but do not handle directly.

Spellworking

Energy: Projective | Planet: Sun | Element: Fire

Powers: Protection

Protection

Wear or carry a small piece of antimony to guard against negative vibrations. Bits of antimony added to combinations of protective stones strengthens their powers.

Alchemical Symbolism

In alchemy, antimony is disguised as the Gray Wolf, also known as Lupus Metallorum. The Gray Wolf is described as the penultimate stage in making the Philosopher's Stone, and in spiritual or psychological terms it can mark the condition that brings a person close to the enlightenment they seek. Because the final stage of transforming lead into gold still lies ahead, the Gray Wolf can symbolize either success or failure.
