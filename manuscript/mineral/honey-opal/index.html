Attributes

Joyful Honey Opal helps make the most of opportunities. Assisting recovery from emotional heartache, it facilitates giving and receiving love. Holding this stone encourages speaking about what could not be spoken of previously. Honey Opal softens prickly, defensive people who have suffered mental, physical or emotional abuse, in any timeframe. Bringing the issues gently to the surface so that they are de-energized and released, it restores self-confidence and awareness of innate worth.

This gentle stone is excellent for sensitive people who find other crystals too strong. Program the stone to radiate peace and harmony out into your environment and dissolve dis-ease in whatever form it may take.

Honey Opal is said to be a natural antibiotic for fevers, colds and flu.

Hold, grid or position as appropriate. Disperse gem essence into the aura especially around the heart and solar plexus.
