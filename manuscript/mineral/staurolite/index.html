The Chariot

Staurolite is an iron aluminum silicate in the Monoclinic crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7 to 7.5. Its name derives from the Greek "stauros" meaning "cross," a reference to its distinctive cruciform twinned crystals. The most widespread legend tells that when the faeries heard news of Christ's crucifixion, they wept and their hardened tears crystallized in the shape of a cross. In North America, Cherokee tradition holds that Staurolite formed from tears shed along the Trail of Tears as the people were forced from their ancestral homes in the 1800s. It is also said that Pocahontas presented Captain John Smith with a Staurolite amulet as a talisman to keep him safe from harm. At least three United States presidents—Roosevelt, Wilson, and Harding—carried Staurolite as luck charms.

Appearance

Staurolite appears as brown to reddish-brown opaque crystals that resemble Chiastolite and often naturally form a cruciform or exhibit a cross pattern. When broken open or sliced, specimens display a symmetrical cross pattern of alternating light and dark colors. The brown crosses carry a certain shimmer and sparkle.

Colors

Australia, Austria, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Italy, Norway, Russia, Switzerland, United States (California, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Utah, Virginia)

Magickal Properties

Staurolite is a stone of earth wisdom, grounding, and protection. It is a powerful talisman for good luck and has been treasured across cultures as a protective charm. It enhances and strengthens rituals, particularly white-magick ceremonies, and connects the physical, etheric, and spiritual planes, promoting communication between them. Shamans often include a piece in their medicine or power bags, and it is a favorite item to exchange during giveaways.

For beginners, Staurolite deeply grounds and protects the heart and spirit. It guards against the harmful electromagnetic frequencies emitted by computers and cell phones. It is exceptionally useful for relieving stress, alleviating depression and addictions, and negating a tendency to overwork or overcommit energy. When placed in a child's room, it can bring sweet dreams and help prevent nightmares.

Advanced practitioners work with Staurolite to expand vision and increase awareness of other dimensions, particularly the Fae realms. It grounds the keeper and helps them feel safe and comfortable on Earth in their human body. It increases awareness of the magick held within the Earth and allows the keeper to identify places where Earth's energy is at its most potent, such as sacred sites and ley lines. It is an excellent stone for those wishing to stop smoking, assisting in understanding the hidden reasons behind nicotine addiction and providing grounding energy for airy people who have used nicotine to anchor themselves to the earth.

Healing

Staurolite treats cellular disorders and growths, increases assimilation of carbohydrates, and reduces depression. It was traditionally used to treat fever.

Chakra Work

Staurolite powerfully activates the Earth Star Chakra, connecting the keeper to the wisdom of the ancestors while feeling held and supported by Mother Gaia. Placed on this energy center, it allows the release of burdens into the earth, lightening the load. It is protective and anchoring, linking the keeper to the planet and its transformative healing power. Staurolite also resonates with the Heart Chakra, and when held against the heart, it helps release grief into the nurturing embrace of the earth. It connects the Root Chakra to the Crown, bridging earthly grounding with spiritual awareness.

Meditation

Hold Staurolite during meditation to ground the energy body and connect with ancestral wisdom. It helps the keeper feel anchored and supported, creating a safe space from which to explore the deeper realms. Place in any space intended for protection or where magick and wonder are desired.

Spellworking

Elemental Magick

Staurolite's natural cross shape makes it a powerful ally for elemental magick. Wear or carry it to balance the four elements within, or place on the altar during elemental rituals. To gain control over the elemental forces, wear a Staurolite set in electrum fashioned as a ring or pendant.

Fascination

Staurolite is also counted among the classic witch "fascination" gems set into rings and pendants, valued as a strong accumulator of witch power that wards off the evil eye.

Power

Carry, wear, or place on the altar for power during magickal rituals of all kinds.

Luck

As with all stones exhibiting unusual shapes or patterns, Staurolite is carried for luck. In Brittany, Staurolites were said to have fallen from the skies and were worn as charms.

Protection

Staurolite is worn or carried for protection against negativity, disease, and accidents. Empower one and place it in the car for this purpose.

Wealth and Sexual Energy

This stone is worn to draw wealth and to energize the sexual drive.

Care

Staurolite sits at Mohs 7 to 7.5, making it a durable stone suitable for regular handling and wear. It is both sun safe and water safe. Cleanse with running water, moonlight, smoke, or by burying in earth to recharge its deep grounding energy. Its natural cross shape makes it an ideal pocket stone or altar piece.
