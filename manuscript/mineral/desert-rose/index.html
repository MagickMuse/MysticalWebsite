Origin

United States, Mexico, Algeria, Morocco.

Attributes

Desert Rose dims your energetic light during travel, making it ideal for moving unnoticed or facilitating clandestine meetings. Psychologically, it helps break free from self-imposed negative patterns and limiting belief systems. When used in meditation, this stone transmutes ancient conflict into love.

Desert Rose teaches the balance of receiving love as well as giving it, and helps control emotional outbursts. It serves as a powerful healer for the earth itself and can restore balance to locations with disturbed earth energy. This stone connects the user to the protective embrace of Mother Earth.

Desert Rose supports the connective tissue and strengthens bones.

Carry while travelling for energetic concealment. Hold during meditation to release limiting beliefs and transform old conflicts. Place on disturbed land to heal earth energies.
