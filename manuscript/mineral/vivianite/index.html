Appearance

Transparent to translucent crystal, deep blue to green.

Origin

Bolivia, Russia, United States.

Attributes

Vivianite is a delicate iron phosphate mineral that enhances love, compassion and emotional healing. This gentle stone opens the heart chakra and helps you to give and receive love unconditionally. Vivianite is excellent for those who have closed their hearts due to past hurts or disappointments as it gently reopens the heart to love and connection.

This stone facilitates deep emotional healing and helps to release old wounds and traumas stored in the heart chakra. It encourages forgiveness of self and others and helps you to see the divine love that exists within all beings. Vivianite is particularly useful for healing relationships and for developing greater empathy and understanding.

Vivianite also enhances intuition and psychic abilities, particularly those related to emotional healing and counseling. It helps you to sense the emotional needs of others and to provide appropriate support and guidance.

Healing

Vivianite may assist with heart conditions.

Hold or place on appropriate body location.
