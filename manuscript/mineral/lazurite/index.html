Appearance

Opaque blue crystal, often with white calcite veining.

Origin

Afghanistan, Chile, Russia.

Attributes

Lazurite is the blue mineral that gives Lapis Lazuli its colour and metaphysical properties. As a stone of truth and communication, Lazurite encourages honesty and helps you to speak your truth with confidence. This crystal stimulates the throat and third eye chakras, enhancing both communication and intuition.

Lazurite is excellent for those who work in communication fields or who need to express themselves clearly and persuasively. It helps to organize thoughts and to present ideas in a logical and coherent manner. This stone also enhances listening skills and helps you to truly hear what others are saying.

Spiritually, Lazurite connects you to divine wisdom and helps you to access higher knowledge. It is particularly useful for meditation and for developing psychic abilities. Lazurite helps you to see through illusions and to perceive spiritual truths.

Healing

Lazurite may assist with disorders of the throat, thyroid and vocal cords. It is reported to help with eye problems and to support the nervous system.

Position

Wear at the throat or place on the third eye chakra. Use during meditation or communication work.
