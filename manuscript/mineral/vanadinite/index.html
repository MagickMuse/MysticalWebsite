Appearance

Vanadinite is a hexagonal crystal, dense but brittle, formed as a secondary mineral deposit when iron ore deposits in desert-like locations undergo oxidization. It appears as very small, bright, transparent crystals on a matrix. Handle with care due to its fragility, and note that Vanadinite is poisonous - prepare elixirs by the indirect method only.

Origin

Australia, France, Germany, Italy, Morocco, Spain, Britain, United States (Arizona, California, Nevada, New Mexico). Today, almost all Vanadinite in the world comes from Morocco.

Attributes

Vanadinite is an excellent stone for people who have problems accepting their physicality. It has a strong connection with the earth chakra in the earth body beneath the feet. Grounding the soul into the physical body and assisting it being comfortable in the earth environment, Vanadinite guards against squandering energy and teaches you how to conserve energy at the physical level.

Vanadinite is an aid to meditation. Shutting off mind chatter, it can facilitate a state of no mind or be used to direct awareness consciously for psychic vision and journeying. It has the power to open an internal channel within the body to receive an inflooding of universal energy. This energy aligns the chakras and brings the higher self into the physical body, facilitating a deep inner peace.

Mentally, Vanadinite fills the gap between thought and intellect. It assists in defining and pursuing goals and shuts off mind chatter, allowing insight and rational thought to combine in an inner voice of guidance.

This stone has the useful property of curbing overspending. Place in the wealth corner of the house or put a small piece in your purse to retain your money.

Vanadinite is poisonous and elixirs should be prepared by the indirect method.

Healing

Vanadinite is useful for breathing difficulties such as asthma and congested lungs. It facilitates the practice of circular breathing. Vanadinite treats chronic exhaustion and bladder problems. If used as an elixir, only one made by the indirect method can be taken internally.

Position

Place as appropriate or rub the elixir externally over the chest area. If using to aid acceptance of physicality, the indirect-method elixir should be taken for several weeks.

Chakra Work

Vanadinite is predominantly a Sacral Chakra stone. Held against this energy center, it brings increased productivity, creativity, and motivation across all areas of life. Placed on the Root Chakra during meditation, it builds stamina and strength. It also activates the Third Eye Chakra, boosting inspiration and clarity and helping you envision the ultimate outcome of a project so you can manifest it into reality. Vanadinite excels at linking the energies of the Root, Sacral, Solar Plexus, and Third Eye Chakras together, giving you that final surge of energy and enthusiasm to cross the finish line of a long-term project - especially those involving writing or other creative endeavors.

Lore

Vanadinite was initially discovered in Mexico in 1801 by the Spanish mineralogist Andres Manuel del Rio. Del Rio suspected the mineral contained an undiscovered chemical element, which he first called pancuronium and then erythronium. Years later, after the element vanadium was officially discovered, it was confirmed that del Rio's mineral contained high levels of it, and the crystal was subsequently named Vanadinite in its honor. Vanadinite is also a supportive stone to work with during menopause, as it is said to help counter the physical and emotional effects of diminished hormone production.
