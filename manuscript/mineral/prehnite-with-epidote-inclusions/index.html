Prehnite with Epidote's Journey to You

Prehnite with epidote is a combination mineral that forms when epidote crystals grow first within a surrounding rock matrix, then silica-rich waters flow over them, eventually encasing the epidote within the resulting prehnite. Prehnite has an orthorhombic crystal structure while epidote is monoclinic, and the combination registers 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale. Both minerals occur separately in various locations, but this particular pairing is rare and highly prized for its potent healing energy.
