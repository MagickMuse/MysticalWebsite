Tangerine Quartz is a variety of quartz in the Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, formed through a combination of hematite and water that gives it a vibrant red to orange coloration.

Appearance

Tangerine Quartz displays warm hues ranging from red to deep orange, with the color produced by hematite and water interacting during the crystal's formation. It carries the classic quartz structure with a vitreous luster and transparent to translucent clarity. Its vibrant coloring makes it one of the most visually striking quartz varieties.

Colors

Red, orange, tangerine

Origin

Brazil, Madagascar

Care

As a quartz variety, Tangerine Quartz is safe for water cleansing and sun exposure. Cleanse under cool running water or place on a selenite plate. Charge under sunlight to amplify its fiery, creative energy, or under moonlight for a softer recharge. Handle with care as the hematite coating can be delicate on some specimens.
