Barite is an Orthorhombic mineral with a Mohs hardness of 3 to 3.5, found in hydrothermal vents associated with silver and lead ore deposits. Its name comes from the Greek barys, meaning "heavy" — a nod to its unusually high density for its size. Healers often remark on how surprisingly weighty even a small piece feels in the hand.

Appearance

Appears in a wide range of colors including colorless, white, and gray, as well as blue, yellow, brown, and dusty pink. Crystals are typically bladed or tabular with a vitreous to pearly luster, and may also form fibrous masses.

Origin

Africa, Arkansas, Britain, Canada, China, Germany, India, Iran, Mexico, Morocco, Pakistan, Peru, Romania, United States

Magickal Properties

Barite is a powerful ally for breaking and healing ancestral cycles of power struggle that continue to echo into the present day. It encourages deep autonomy, liberating you from conforming to others' expectations and calling you back into alignment with your own inner authority. At the same time, it supports genuine loyalty to people and ideals that truly deserve it.

Known as the Flying Stone, barite is a traditional journeying stone that can carry you into other worlds and dimensions during meditation or spiritual practice, while keeping you grounded and safe. It opens access to celestial and angelic energies that restore faith in yourself and in humanity, and supports the clearing of karmic debts and past-life wounds that block growth in the upper chakras.

Barite facilitates communicating intuitive vision. A traditional journeying stone, Barite stimulates both dreaming and dream recall. If anonymity and stealth are required for ritual working, Barite confers this and guides you safely back.

Spiritually, this is a useful stone for cleansing and rebalancing the entire chakra system. Psychologically, Barite enhances autonomy. If you have conformed to others' ideals instead of your own, or have been at the beck and call of others, Barite sets you free. Conversely, it supports loyalty to an appropriate person or ideal. This stone overcomes shyness and assists with interpersonal communication and mental focus. It teaches you where your boundaries lie. A strongly motivating stone, Barite benefits people whose energies are scattered or exhausted.

Mentally, Barite is supportive for the memory and encourages efficient functioning of the brain, heightening your ability to organize and express thoughts.

Emotionally, Barite is beneficial for platonic friendships and encourages intimacy and insight into relationships of all kinds. A powerful transformer, it may bring about a catharsis in which old emotional patterns, obsessions and fears are released, and other crystals may be required to restore equilibrium. The emotional catharsis may release long-repressed emotions and restructure the emotional body to restore calm.

Healing

Assists vitality, over-sensitivity to cold or temperature changes, memory, chronic fatigue, detoxification, the brain, vision, addiction and sore throat. It balances brain chemistry and calms the stomach and nervous system.

Chakra Work

On the Crown Chakra, barite clears blockages that prevent you from fully receiving and integrating messages from your higher self, supporting spiritual evolution and expansion. On the Third Eye Chakra, it acts as a neurological healer, bringing balance to brain chemistry and the central nervous system and gently guiding brain waves into the theta state for deep, receptive meditation. It can also be placed on the Throat Chakra to ease apprehension around speaking up in group settings and to sharpen your intuition about how and when to share your ideas.

Position

Place on the Crown, Third Eye, or Throat Chakra as appropriate. Hold during meditation or journeying. Use in a grid for deep energetic clearing work.

Did You Know?

Barite takes its name from the Greek barys, meaning "heavy." Its specific density is notably high for its size, and even a small piece carries a satisfying, substantial weight in the hand.
