🪙Dreaming of Aluminum

Spellworking

Energy: Projective | Planet: Mercury | Element: Air

Powers: Mental abilities, travel, image magic

Aluminum is a "modern" metal with no history of usage among earlier peoples. It is sometimes recommended as an alternative to mercury, being less dangerous to use.

Mental Abilities

Carry small pieces of aluminum to stimulate mental abilities and enhance clear thinking.

Travel Magic

Due to its modern associations with aircraft and transportation, aluminum is utilized in spells involving trips to distant lands.

Image Magic

Aluminum foil can be used for image magic. Place a sheet of foil on your altar and light candles of the appropriate color for your magical need. With your intention in mind, form the foil into the proper shape. Let its shape fuel your visualization; send energy into and through it to bring your need into manifestation. Smooth out the foil and douse with water when finished. Dry and flatten, using the same foil daily until successful.

Transformation

Recycling aluminum is a modern form of transformation magic—turning discarded material into value. This practice is economically, ecologically, and magically sound.
