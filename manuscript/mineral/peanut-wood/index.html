Appearance

Peanut Wood appears as fossilized wood with distinctive peanut-like patterns in brown, red, white, and yellow colors.

Origin

Found in Australia. This is fossilized wood.

Attributes

Peanut Wood is an Australian fossilized wood. It assists those who have difficulty in walking on the earth, whether physically or energetically, and helps you to feel at home on the planet. As with all Petrified Wood, it is an excellent aid to grounding energy into the physical body and keeping that body anchored to the earth. A stable, calming stone it supports during trauma and healing or emotional challenges. A useful stone for past life healing, it facilitates previous life exploration and going to your soul's roots.

Healing

Peanut Wood reputedly helps weight loss and is useful in cases of incontinence or, placed behind the ears, tinnitus and poor hearing.

Position

Hold, place or grid as appropriate. Place behind ears for hearing issues.
