💰Prosperity Stone

Tangerose Quartz is a high vibration stone that combines the loving properties of rose with the joyful energy of tangerine, creating a powerful heart healing crystal.

Appearance

Translucent crystal with tangerine and rose coloring, often with natural formations and inclusions

Origin

Madagascar

Attributes

Tangerose Quartz works primarily with the heart chakra, facilitating deep emotional healing and opening the heart to greater love and compassion. This stone combines the gentle, nurturing energy of rose quartz with the enthusiastic, joyful vibration of tangerine, creating a balanced approach to heart healing.

The crystal assists in releasing emotional wounds and trauma stored in the heart chakra, while simultaneously filling the space with loving, positive energy. It helps to dissolve barriers to love and supports the development of healthy relationships with self and others.

Tangerose Quartz is particularly helpful for those who have experienced emotional betrayal or heartbreak, as it gently heals while maintaining optimism and faith in love. The stone supports emotional resilience and helps to transform pain into wisdom and compassion.

Healing

Tangerose Quartz supports the cardiovascular system and assists with emotional healing. It helps to release stored emotional trauma and supports the heart chakra.

Position

Place over the heart chakra during healing work. Hold during meditation to access heart healing energy. Wear as jewelry to maintain ongoing heart chakra support.

Spellworking

Tangerose Quartz combines rose quartz's love energy with tangerine's joyful vitality. Use it for heart healing after betrayal or heartbreak, dissolving barriers to love, and transforming pain into wisdom. Its blend of gentle nurturing and enthusiastic joy creates balanced heart healing.
