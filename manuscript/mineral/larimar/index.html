Appearance

Translucent, smooth, with whorls of color or white veins showing through the base color. Often small to medium, tumbled.

Origin

Dominican Republic, Bahamas.

Attributes

Recently discovered, ethereal Larimar is one of the spiritual stones that open to new dimensions, stimulating evolution of the earth. It radiates love and peace and promotes tranquility. Larimar effortlessly induces a deeply meditative state. It naturally raises consciousness and harmonizes body and soul to new vibrations.

Spiritually, it is empowering, dissolving spurious boundaries that constrain the spiritual self, and guiding the soul onto its true pathway in life. Larimar facilitates angelic contact and communication with other realms. It is an excellent stone for those seeking a soulmate, and it facilitates the healing of past-life relationships or heart trauma.

Psychologically, Larimar removes self-imposed blockages and constraints. It dissolves self-sabotaging behavior, especially a tendency toward martyrdom, and assists taking control of life. It is particularly useful for alleviating guilt and removing fear.

Mentally, Larimar brings serenity and clarity, and constructive thought. It stimulates creativity and encourages going with the flow.

Emotionally, Larimar brings calmness and equilibrium. It is an antidote to emotional extremes and ameliorates bipolar disorders. It heals trauma to the heart and reconnects to natural playfulness and joyful childlike energy.

Healing

Positioned over the heart, third eye, or solar plexus, or gently stroked over the body, Larimar removes attached entities. It stimulates third eye, heart, crown, and throat chakras, and promotes self-healing. It is particularly helpful for cartilage and throat conditions.

Position

Hold or wear for prolonged periods; use on feet.

Magickal Properties

Often called the "Atlantis Stone," larimar is said to carry the ancient wisdom of the lost city within its cool blue depths. First unearthed from the Filipinas Larimar Mine in the Dominican Republic, this light blue variety of pectolite possesses a calming, cooling frequency that opens and purifies the Throat Chakra. It guides you toward expressing your deepest truth with clarity, authenticity, and conviction. Larimar connects you to the Divine Feminine, the energy of the Goddess, the sea, and the sky, opening pathways to higher realms and spiritual insight. It dissolves barriers around the heart built from past pain and releases judgment, unhealthy attachments, and fear. This stone is especially valued by writers, public speakers, healers, and Reiki practitioners for its ability to promote clear and harmonious communication.

Chakra Work

Larimar resonates deeply with the Throat Chakra. When held against this energy center, it cleanses blockages that prevent you from connecting with and sharing your truth. It encourages honest, open communication with loved ones and creates a peaceful atmosphere wherever it is placed. Keep it on your desk to promote mental clarity, or in gathering spaces to foster harmonious dialogue.

Correspondences

Larimar partners well with Ajoite, Andean Blue Opal, Aqua Lemuria, Aquamarine, Chrysocolla, Moldavite, Ruby, Tibetan Tektite, Turquoise, and Zincite. It is associated with Leo and resonates with the Triclinic crystal system.

Colors

Larimar displays soft shades of light blue to blue-green, often with swirling white patterns and veins running through its translucent surface. The blue coloring comes from copper substitution within its pectolite structure. Deeper blue specimens are considered the most desirable and valuable.

Magickal Message

"Let the ocean wash over you, soothing and cold. Release what weighs heavy, and let the waves carry it away. Listen to the water, and you will hear your own truth."

Care

Larimar rates 4.5 to 5 on the Mohs scale, making it a soft stone that requires gentle handling. It is water safe but not sun safe, as prolonged exposure to sunlight can cause its blue color to fade over time. Cleanse with running water, moonlight, or sound. Store away from direct light to preserve its vibrant blue hue.
