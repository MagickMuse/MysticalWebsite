Appearance

Blue, green, yellow, white, or pink. Transparent to translucent, vitreous luster, hexagonal crystal system, prismatic crystals. Various colored varieties include aquamarine, emerald, morganite, and heliodor. Easily obtained.

Origin

United States, Russia, Australia, Brazil, Czech Republic, France, Norway

Attributes

Beryl teaches how to do only that which is necessary for your purpose. It encourages a positive view and reduces overanalysis and anxiety. This stone enhances courage, stress relief, and mental clarity.

Psychologically, Beryl helps you to realize your potential. It enhances courage and is useful for those who are overstressed. This stone filters out distractions and reduces overstimulation. It encourages a positive view.

Mentally, Beryl awakens love of learning and research. It makes you less critical and more discerning, and aids rational thinking. This stone brings wisdom and insight.

Emotionally, Beryl re-awakens love in marriage that has grown cold. It enhances the ability to deal with stressful situations.

Spiritually, this stone opens and activates the crown and solar plexus chakras. It assists scrying and spherical crystal gazing.

Healing

Beryl strengthens the circulatory and pulmonary systems, and increases resistance to toxins and pollutants. It treats the liver, heart, stomach, and spine, and heals concussion. Beryl supports the organs of elimination.

Position

Wear or place as appropriate.

Beryl is the stone par excellence for dealing with a stressful life and shedding unnecessary baggage. It aids in tuning into guidance as to what you should be doing. Representing purity of being, Beryl helps to actualize potential and is an excellent stone for scrying. It discourages overanalysis and anxiety, and reawakens love in those who are married but jaded. Psychologically, Beryl enhances courage, relieves stress, and calms the mind. It aids the organs of elimination, strengthens pulmonary and circulatory systems, and increases resistance to toxins and pollutants. As an elixir, it can be used to treat throat infections.

Shapes

Beryl spheres have been carefully shaped by hand for centuries as scrying tools. Seers gaze into their depths to ascertain the future.

Spellworking

In fifth-century Ireland, scryers who used beryl spheres were known as specularii. Dr. Dee's famous crystal, now housed in the British Museum, was of beryl, not clear quartz as often imagined. Ancient peoples utilized beryl in rituals designed to bring rain.

Protection at Sea

Like aquamarine, beryl is worn while on the water for protection against storms. This stone guards its wearer against drowning and seasickness.

Against Psychic Manipulation

Beryl is worn to prevent fascination—what today would be called deliberate psychic manipulation or persuasion, such as practiced by evangelists, salespersons, and politicians. It makes its bearer unconquerable, assuages fear, and increases optimism and happiness.

Debates and Understanding

In the sixteenth century, magicians prescribed beryl to win all debates and arguments, yet cause its bearer to be well-mannered and amiable, and gain understanding.

Scrying

Beryl has long been used to increase psychic awareness and was called "the stone of the seer." Spheres of beryl were considered superior to those of quartz crystal. It was also carved into flat, round mirrors for scrying. Beryl-scrying should be practiced only during the Waxing Moon for most potent results. Due to its lunar associations, beryl can be worn or placed on the altar during Full Moon rituals.

Finding Lost Objects

When you have lost something, hold a beryl in your hand and visualize the object. Still your mind and let psychic impressions reveal its whereabouts.

Love and Friendship

Beryl is exchanged between lovers to strengthen their relationship and is carried or worn to attract love. In the thirteenth century, an image of a frog was engraved on beryl and carried to reconcile enemies and attract friendship.

Study and Energy

During study, wear beryl to increase your conscious mind's retention of information. It is also used to send energy into the body. If you feel lazy, hold or wear a beryl and let its structured, low vibrations enter you.

Healing

Beryl was considered excellent for relieving liver ailments, swollen glands, and diseases of the eyes. It is also used to halt gossip.
