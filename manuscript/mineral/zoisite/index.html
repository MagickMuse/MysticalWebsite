Zoisite is a calcium aluminum hydroxy sorosilicate mineral crystallizing in the Orthorhombic system with a Mohs hardness of 6 to 7. Initially discovered in 1805 in the Sau Alps range in Austria, it was first named saualpite after this locale before being renamed after the Austrian scientist Sigmund Zois. Zoisite is generally found in schist and metamorphic rocks, growing alongside other minerals such as garnet, actinolite, and vesuvianite. It is well known for containing ruby inclusions, which amplifies its heart-healing properties. Though its name does not have Greek roots, "zoisite" does contain the Greek root word "zoe," meaning "life."

Appearance

Solid mass, pleochroic, all sizes. Orthorhombic crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 6 to 7.

Origin

Found globally. The most important localities are Australia, Austria, Cambodia, Canada, China, India, Italy, Kenya, Madagascar, Mexico, New Zealand, Pakistan, Russia, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, and the United States.

Attributes

Zoisite transmutes negative energies into positive ones and connects to the spiritual realms.

Psychologically, Zoisite assists in manifesting your own self rather than being influenced by others or trying to conform to the norm. It aids in realizing your own ideas and transforms destructive urges into constructive ones. This stone dispels lethargy and brings to the surface repressed feelings and emotions so that they can be expressed.

Mentally, Zoisite is a creative stone, bringing the mind back to its objectives after an interruption. Physically, Zoisite encourages recovery from severe illness or stress.

Healing

Zoisite is a detoxifier, neutralizing overacidification and reducing inflammation. It strengthens the immune system and regenerates cells and treats the heart, spleen, pancreas, and lungs. This stone stimulates fertility and heals diseases of the ovaries and testicles. When combined with Ruby, it increases potency.

Position

Wear or place on the body in contact with the skin as appropriate. Wear for long periods of time, as it is a slow-acting stone.

Magickal Properties

Zoisite helps the practitioner stay grounded, returning to center and to the self. It inspires creativity and focus while transmuting negative energy into positive. This stone magnifies happiness, appreciation, abundance, vitality, and growth, and helps reclaim a sense of wonder about the natural world, leading to deeper self-discovery. Zoisite channels, activates, and delivers a powerful surge of life-force energy, helping the bearer overcome depression, anxiety, and deep sadness. For advanced practitioners, it dispels lethargy and laziness by providing internal strength and motivation. It brings a sense of rebirth and regeneration to the energy flow and helps the practitioner understand the connection between inner and outer worlds, how personal energy affects the physical body and surroundings. Zoisite supports shedding societal norms in order to become one's true, authentic self.

Chakra Work

Zoisite activates the Heart Chakra. Held against the heart space during meditation, it opens the mind to receive the messages of the heart. It radiates an energy of growth and richness in all aspects of life, providing energy for the rebirth of the soul and encouraging spiritual development alongside an increased understanding of oneself and the world.
