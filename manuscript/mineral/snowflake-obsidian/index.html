Appearance

Mottled black-white., as though snowflakes were on the surface, often small and tumbled

Origin

Worldwide.

Attributes

Placed on the sacral chakra, Snowflake Obsidian calms and soothes, putting you in the right frame of mind to be receptive before bringing to your attention ingrained patterns of behavior. It teaches you to value mistakes as well as successes.

It is a stone of purity, providing balance for body, mind, and spirit. Snowflake Obsidian helps you to recognize and release "wrong thinking" and stressful mental patterns. It promotes dispassion and inner centering. With the aid of Snowflake Obsidian, isolation and loneliness become empowering, aiding surrender in meditation.

Healing

Snowflake Obsidian treats veins and the skeleton, and improves circulation. The elixir is good for the skin and eyes.

Position

Place as appropriate or use as elixir.

Spellworking

Use snowflake obsidian, a black stone with whitish spots, for protection. It works more gently than pure black obsidian while still providing grounding and shielding. This stone helps release "wrong thinking" and negative patterns. Use it for purification spells, breaking bad habits, and finding balance.

Magickal Properties

Snowflake obsidian is a natural volcanic glass with an Amorphous crystal structure, formed when felsic lava cools rapidly with minimal crystal growth. The distinctive snowflake patterns are actually radiating inclusions of cristobalite. Revered since ancient times as a stone of shadow integration and past-life recall, healers have long worked with it to draw subconscious thoughts to the surface and illuminate all that lies hidden and repressed. This crystal holds a deep connection with the natural world, calling in the presence of animal allies to offer wisdom, guidance, and gentle protection on your path. For beginners, it helps heal both present and past-life issues, opening communication with the spiritual realm to channel ancestral guidance. It brings cleansing energy to body, mind, and spirit, imparting hope, courage, and a sense of wholeness. It grounds and balances, fostering feelings of safety and belonging. During times of transformation, it reveals unhealthy thought patterns and supports releasing them in favor of energies that serve your highest good. For advanced practitioners, snowflake obsidian strengthens psychic sensitivity and aids past-life recall. It leads you on a powerful journey inward, bringing repressed material to the surface for deeper healing. It helps integrate lessons from past lives into the present, providing the wisdom to act in concert with your divine self. It boosts self-worth and helps move through patterns of self-victimization into greater power, confidence, and bravery. It also attunes you to messages from spirit guides, including lost loved ones who wish to make contact.

Chakra Work

Snowflake obsidian activates the Earth Star and Root Chakras, creating a strong foundation of grounding and stability. It is a potent companion for shadow work and past-life regression, useful for revealing old patterns and opening you to change. Keep it in your home or any space you wish to surround with stabilizing, safeguarding vibrations. Place it on tense areas of the body for energetic detoxification and healing. This gem opens channels between you and the natural world, making it an ideal companion for hikes or outdoor meditations. It also makes a thoughtful gift, bringing its gently protective magick to anyone who receives it.

Correspondences

Snowflake obsidian partners well with Black Obsidian, Black Tourmaline, Iolite, Magnesite, and Petrified Wood. It is associated with Virgo and has an Amorphous crystal structure.

Colors

Snowflake obsidian presents as a mottled black stone with distinctive white or grayish-white snowflake-like patterns scattered across its surface. The white inclusions of cristobalite create a striking contrast against the deep black volcanic glass, giving each piece a unique appearance reminiscent of falling snow.

Magickal Message

"Embrace your darkness, and let it come into the light. Remember that your shadow is one half of all that you are. Look to the darker parts of you with acceptance, and know that you are whole."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 5 to 5.5, snowflake obsidian is relatively soft and should be stored separately from harder stones. It is both sun safe and water safe. Cleanse with water, moonlight, smoke, or sound. As an earth-connected stone, burying it briefly in soil is also an effective way to recharge its energy.
