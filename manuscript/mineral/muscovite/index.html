Appearance

Pearl-like mica in layers, all sizes. Easily obtained.

Origin

Switzerland, Russia, Austria, Czech Republic, Brazil, New Mexico, United States.

Attributes

Muscovite is the commonest form of mica. It is a mystical stone with strong angelic contact, stimulating awareness of the higher self. Used in scrying, this visionary stone links to the highest spiritual realms. Muscovite stimulates the heart chakra, facilitates astral travel, and opens intuition and psychic vision.

Muscovite allows recognition of the flaws in humanity while simultaneously stimulating unconditional love and acceptance. It is a reflective stone, mirroring back and allowing you to recognize your projections, the parts of yourself you do not acknowledge and therefore see in others. It helps you understand that what you dislike in another may be characteristics you cannot accept in yourself. Muscovite then aids in the integration and transformation of these shadow qualities.

This stone can be gridded around earthquake areas as it gently and safely relieves tensions within the earth. It also releases tension within the physical body and aligns the subtle bodies and meridians with the physical, bringing about balance.

Psychologically, Muscovite disperses insecurity, self-doubt, and clumsiness. It is useful for those who suffer from dyspraxia and left-right confusion. It eliminates anger and nervous stress, bringing flexibility at all levels of being. It assists in looking joyfully toward the future and back to the past to appreciate lessons learned. By allowing you to see yourself as others see you, Muscovite aids in changing the image you present to the world. It supports exploration of painful feelings.

Mentally, Muscovite aids problem-solving and stimulates quick-wittedness. It facilitates clear expression of thoughts and feelings.

Physically, Muscovite improves your appearance, imparting sheen to the hair and sparkle to the eyes. It helps the body achieve its most appropriate weight.

Healing

Controls blood sugar, balances pancreatic secretions, alleviates dehydration, and prevents hunger while fasting. Regulates the kidneys. Relieves insomnia and allergies and heals conditions resulting from dis-ease or distress.

Position

Carry or hold. Stroke over the skin.

Additional Notes

Pink Muscovite is the most effective color for making angelic contact.

Violet Muscovite opens the higher crown chakras and facilitates raising consciousness to a very fine vibration.

Spellworking

Mica, a general term for minerals exhibiting paper-thin flexible sheets of crystals, is a common stone. Muscovite is the most common form of mica.

Moonlight Divination Ritual

Take a piece of mica at least an inch or so square. While visualizing yourself possessing complete control over your psychic powers, hold the stone in full moonlight. Capture the glow of the illuminary on the mica's shining surface. Gently moving the stone in your hands, let its shimmer drowse your conscious mind. Expand your psychic awareness and determine future events.

Protection

Mica is also carried for general protection.
