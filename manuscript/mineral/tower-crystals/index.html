📈Amplification🎯Directed Energy

Generators are six-sided terminated points with flat bases that build and direct energy upward in a focused, linear stream. Unlike spheres or clusters, which radiate outward in all directions, generators concentrate energy toward a single point of focus. Most are formed from quartz, which naturally grows in a hexagonal structure.

Generators work well as centerpieces for crystal grids because they gather energy from surrounding stones and draw it inward. The number six in sacred geometry represents the union of spiritual and physical worlds and the bridge between human energy and spiritual evolution, captured in the phrase "as above, so below."
