Rainbow moonstone is a feldspar mineral with a Monoclinic crystal structure, sometimes called white labradorite. Though not a "true" moonstone, it displays a beautiful shimmer known as labradorescence. The Sioux connected this gem with Hanwi, their moon goddess, while ancient Greeks referred to moonstone as "aphroselene," combining the names of Aphrodite and Selene. Often called the "prism stone" because it contains the full spectrum of rainbow light within it, rainbow moonstone holds the energy of all seven prismatic rays. Where Black Moonstone corresponds with the new moon, rainbow moonstone embodies the full moon phase, a time of celebration, gratitude, expansion, and completion. The flash and fire within this gem symbolize the phoenix and its spirit of rebirth and regeneration.

Magickal Properties

Rainbow moonstone brings joy, clears the emotional body, purifies and transmutes negative energy, and provides light during dark or challenging times. It harmonizes feminine energy and empowers connection with the Divine Feminine within. For beginners, it uplifts the spirit with an influx of light, positivity, and emotional well-being. It guides through darkness and helps rise above challenges, offering a higher perspective and deepening gratitude. It strengthens intuition, boosts fertility, enhances creative power, and attracts abundance of all kinds. Its soft, gentle vibration clears emotional blockages and soothes the emotional body. For advanced practitioners, rainbow moonstone is a gem of profound rebirth and transformation. It encourages working with the lunar phases to honor personal cycles of expansion and release. It unlocks psychic gifts, facilitates lucid dreaming, and supports channeling messages from beyond ordinary time and space. It grants access to the celestial realms, surrounding you in the light of angels and spirit guides. It supports the release of negative attachments, helping you focus on your own path. It promotes inner peace and embodies goddess energies.

Chakra Work

Rainbow moonstone opens the Soul Star Chakra, deepening your connection to the stars and other dimensions. It is an ideal companion for full moon work. Hold it while meditating under the full moon, or incorporate it into healing work during lunar rituals. Place it anywhere in your home to invite high vibrations of happiness, gratitude, emotional healing, and light. It is also a powerful tool for cleansing and brightening the aura. Hover it around the body to stimulate the positive flow of life-force energy, lift your personal frequency, and deflect negativity.

Correspondences

Rainbow moonstone partners well with Amethyst, Labradorite, Lemurian Quartz, Moldavite, Petalite, and Selenite. It is associated with Cancer, Libra, and Scorpio, and resonates with a Monoclinic crystal system.

Colors

Rainbow moonstone appears milky white to translucent, with a stunning play of iridescent color across its surface. Flashes of blue, violet, pink, gold, and green shimmer within the stone, giving it its prismatic reputation. The base color is typically white or colorless with a pearly, opalescent quality.

Magickal Message

"You are a rainbow, an arc of prismatic light. You bring joy to anyone who comes your way."

Lunar Manifestation

Rainbow moonstone draws lunar light and power directly to your visions and dreams, offering cosmic illumination and blessings upon all you seek to create. Working with this stone is like carrying the energy of the full moon with you all month long, rather than only during the few days of its peak. Include rainbow moonstone in a manifestation grid alongside solar stones like Yellow Apatite and Citrine to balance lunar receptivity with solar drive, or meditate with it to invite the full spectrum of prismatic light into your intentions.

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 6 to 6.5, rainbow moonstone is moderately durable. Cleanse and charge under full moonlight to align with its lunar energy. Avoid prolonged sun exposure to preserve its iridescent sheen. Handle with care as it can be sensitive to sharp impacts along its cleavage planes.
