Appearance

Phlogopite appears as light brown, greenish, yellow, colourless, or grey layered, plate-like stone.

Origin

Found in Greenland, Siberia, Russia, and Canada. This is a rare mineral.

Attributes

Phlogopite energetically filters electromagnetic pollution. It opens metaphysical abilities and activates all chakras. Use it to find the gifts hidden in the darker corners of your soul or to remove blocks to spiritual development. It connects to the four cardinal directions and is ideal for Medicine Wheel or Earth Mother ceremonies and communication with nature spirits.

Healing

Phlogopite keeps the subtle and physical bodies free of pollutant energy. Its high magnesium content energetically strengthens bones and tooth enamel, assists nutrient and vitamin assimilation, enzyme function, energy transfer, and correct working of the body.

Grid, place or hold with care as appropriate.
