The white or pale variety of jade, occurring as either nephrite or jadeite depending on origin. Shares the same tough interlocking fibrous structure as all jade and is highly regarded in Chinese tradition as a stone of purity, serenity, and spiritual clarity. An excellent protective talisman and heart healer.
