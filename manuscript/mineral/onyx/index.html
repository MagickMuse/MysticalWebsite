Onyx is a variety of chalcedony with a trigonal crystal structure, registering 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale. It features parallel bands and has been prized as a talisman of spiritual wisdom and strength since ancient times. The breastplate of the high priest Aaron in the Bible featured onyx prominently, representing the eleventh tribal name, Shoham.

Appearance

Banded, marble-like, often polished. Available in all sizes. Displays parallel banding in layers of black and white or other contrasting colors.

Origin

Found in Afghanistan, Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Czech Republic, France, Germany, India, Madagascar, Russia, the United Kingdom, and the United States (Colorado, Montana, Utah).

Attributes

Onyx is strength-giving. It provides support in difficult or confusing circumstances and during times of enormous mental or physical stress. Centering your energy and aligning it with a higher power, accessing higher guidance, is facilitated by Onyx as is connection with the whole. It can take you forward to view the future and, with its capacity to impart personal strength, facilitates being master of one's destiny. This stone promotes vigor, steadfastness, and stamina. It aids learning lessons, imparting self-confidence and helping you to be at ease in your surroundings.

Onyx is a secretive stone that assists in keeping your own counsel. However, Onyx is said to hold the memories of things that have happened to the wearer. It can be used for psychometry, telling the story to those who are sensitive to its vibrations.

This property of holding physical memories makes Onyx useful in past-life work for healing old injuries and physical trauma that are affecting the present life. Holding a piece of Onyx takes your attention to the site of the previous injury, which can then be released through body work, reframing, or crystal therapy. Onyx can also be used to heal old grief and sorrows.

Psychologically, Onyx recognizes and integrates dualities within the self. It anchors the flighty into a more stable way of life and generally imparts self-control. Onyx is a mental tonic that alleviates overwhelming fears and worries. Onyx conveys the invaluable gift of wise decisions.

Physically, Onyx assists in absorbing from the universe energies that are required for healing or other purposes. It balances the yin and yang energies within the body.

Magickal Properties

Onyx is believed to make the bearer physically strong and invincible and to attract good luck and a robust harvest. Its lengthy lineage attests to the power of onyx to ward off negative energy and deflect the evil eye.

For beginners, onyx helps empaths by absorbing and transmuting low vibrations within people or places and stabilizing your emotions. It brings self-discipline and focus, helping you realign your perceptions and actions to support strong decision-making and become a master of your thoughts, words, and actions.

For advanced practitioners, onyx activates your seat of inner power and enhances strength, stability, willpower, and courage. It eases fear and tension, strengthens and calms the central nervous system, and purifies your thoughts and inner monologue. It encourages you to think more positively, particularly in relation to beliefs about your own worth, breaking patterns of self-sabotage and self-harm.

Chakra Work

Onyx is a stone of physical strength, drawing energy up through the Earth Star and Root Chakras to promote recovery from chronic illness or injuries and support building stamina through physical fitness. It can be placed on these energy centers or held during meditation to bring you back down to earth when you are getting carried away with lofty ideas or unattainable goals. It provides endurance and discipline to see realistic projects through to their completion.

Partners Well With

Pair Onyx with Carnelian for vitality and creative fire. Emerald brings heart-centered wisdom and abundance. Hematite amplifies grounding and protective energy. Lapis Lazuli deepens spiritual insight and truth. Moonstone balances lunar intuition with onyx's earthy stability. Selenite cleanses and elevates the energy field.

Healing

Onyx is beneficial for teeth, bones, bone marrow, blood disorders, and the feet.

Position

Wear on left side of body. Place on the Earth Star or Root Chakra for grounding. Hold during meditation. Traditionally, onyx worn around the neck was said to cool lust and support chastity.

Lore

The name "onyx" comes from the Greek word for "fingernail," as it was believed that Cupid clipped Aphrodite's fingernails in her sleep, and they were transformed into stone as they fell on the ground. In classical ceremonial magick, the image of the head of Mars or a figure of Hercules was engraved on onyx and carried for courage.

Folklore

In the older telling of how onyx came to be, the Fates gathered the nail clippings that Cupid had scattered across the sand and, unwilling to let any part of the goddess go to waste, turned them into stone. Because onyx was used to summon spirits, tradition warned that it should never be worn at night, lest the wearer suffer nightmares. The Arabic name el jaza means sadness, and the stone carried a reputation for draining energy and stirring melancholy, which fed its wider name as an unlucky gem. In both India and ancient Egypt it was believed that wearing onyx could cool the passions and part lovers. Yet it was also valued in healing. Hildegard of Bingen advised steeping onyx in wine for up to thirty days and using the elixir to bathe the eyes and clear failing sight.

Magickal Message

Let my calming influence bring persistence, resilience, and a tranquil outlook. Allow me to help you put a stop to your negative self-talk. You are a high-value asset to this world.

Spellworking

In times past, onyx was thought to be the manifestation of a demon imprisoned in the stone. This demon woke up at night and spread terror and nightmares to any persons within its range of influence. This demon was also thought to provoke discord between lovers (though see below for the reason why "discord" might occur if this stone is misused).

Use this black stone to banish negative energy and ground magick spells. It encourages self-confidence and determination. Onyx also helps you break deeply ingrained habits, whether physical or emotional. Wear onyx when facing adversaries in figurative or literal battles. As an opaque stone, onyx is particularly effective for physical matters and provides a strong foundation for any protective or strengthening spell.

Protection

Onyx is a protective stone worn when facing adversaries in battles or conflicts of all kinds—or while hurrying down a dark street at midnight. (Isn't it nice that magic is practical?)

In classical ceremonial magic, the image of the head of the god Mars or a figure of the hero Hercules was engraved on onyx and carried for courage.

Onyx is a stone of many colors, from marbled green and brown to black as midnight. Each variety has its own strengths, but all of them lend magical fortitude needed to face challenges ahead. What makes onyx particularly valuable for protection is its ability to absorb and transmute negative energies—instead of merely negating them, onyx transforms harmful energy into something beneficial.

If used in a home grid, onyx acts as a powerful filter. It takes any negative thoughts or feelings aimed at residents of the home and transmutes them into energy that makes the grid stronger while keeping occupants happier and healthier. This transmutation ensures the grid functions with renewed vigor for years to come.

Mirror Defensive Spell

Onyx is used for defense against negativity consciously directed toward you. While such things as "psychic attack" or "hexing" are rare and often exist only in the mind of the "victim," performing defensive rituals can be psychologically cleansing.

Place a square mirror on your altar. Set a purple candle before it so that the flame is reflected in the mirror.

Empower nine onyx stones with reflective, or defensive, energy. Place one piece of onyx three inches from the candle's right side. Then place eight more stones in a semicircle around the candle, working from right to left, until the candle is half-ringed with onyx toward you, but is clear of it in front of the mirror.

Light the candle. Visualize the onyx as collecting the negativity and sending it into the candle's flame. Then see the flame acting as a lens, focusing the negativity and sending it into the mirror.

The mirror is a doorway onto the spiritual plane. The negative energy is sent through it back to the original sender. Protection is conferred.

Sexual Energy and Control

Onyx has been used to reduce sexual impulses. This should be approached with caution, for sexual release is a natural part of life. Natural sexual impulses exist for pleasure, for union with other humans and the divine, and for the continuance of human life.

However, onyx can be used to help curb uncontrollable sexual urges or to quell sexual desire when there is no chance of fulfillment with your partner—for example, during long physical separations or illness.

If uncontrollable desire is a problem, lie down fully clothed. Hold a piece of onyx about two inches above your groin. Let its soothing, spiritual vibrations bombard you. Visualize yourself desiring less sex, remembering that quality—not quantity—is all that matters. Do this for a few minutes once a day—but for no longer than a week. Wait a week before repeating this ritual.

Never continue using onyx to repress sexual desire for longer than a month or two, and reopen the sex center afterward with diamond or carnelian.

When empowered for protective purposes, onyx affects the sex center differently. Sex is linked with the survival of our species, so it "protects" life. Wearing onyx or utilizing it in protective rituals channels sexual energy into the stone and from there creates protection.

Diamond and Onyx

A safe alternative entails having a diamond—it doesn't matter how large or small—set in onyx. When the diamond (which is sexually arousing) is surrounded by the sexually inhibiting onyx, it symbolizes control over our sexual natures.
