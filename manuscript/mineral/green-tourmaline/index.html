Appearance

Green Tourmaline is a trigonal crystal, green in color, translucent to opaque, with striated prismatic form. Also known as Verdelite, it is the masculine counterpart to Pink Tourmaline's feminine vibration, strengthening and revitalizing where pink soothes and gentles. It corresponds with Venus and the element of Earth.

Origin

Afghanistan, Brazil, Madagascar, Mozambique, Namibia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Kenya, Tanzania, United States (California, Maine), Zambia.

Attributes

Green Tourmaline (Verdelite) opens the heart chakra and promotes compassion, tenderness, and patience. It brings a sense of belonging and transforms negative energy into positive. Green Tourmaline facilitates the study of herbalism and enhances the healing properties of plants.

This stone has a strong connection to nature spirits and the plant kingdom. It attracts luck, success, abundance, and prosperity. Green Tourmaline inspires creativity and attracts money and business success.

Green Tourmaline aids the heart, thymus, eyes, and immune system. It assists with weight loss, relieves exhaustion, and helps with claustrophobia.

Lore

Green Tourmaline was first discovered in the 1500s in Brazil, where it was initially mistaken for Emerald. It was not recognized as a separate mineral species until the 1800s. Interest surged after American deposits were found, particularly at the Pala mine in San Diego County, California, reported in the 1890s. The Indigenous peoples of the region had already been working with tourmaline for centuries, gifting it alongside other colors of the stone as funeral offerings. Green Tourmaline was also highly prized in China, where Empress Dowager Cixi was especially fond of the gem.
