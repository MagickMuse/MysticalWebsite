Attributes

Vera Cruz Amethyst is one of the highest vibration forms of Amethyst, carrying exceptionally clear spiritual energy.

Spiritually, this stone facilitates deep meditation, spiritual journeying, and connection to higher realms. It opens the crown and soul star chakras and assists in ascension work. The clarity of Vera Cruz Amethyst makes it particularly effective for channeling and receiving spiritual guidance.

Psychologically, Vera Cruz Amethyst assists in releasing attachments and achieving spiritual detachment from earthly concerns.

Mentally, it brings exceptional mental clarity and enhanced intuitive perception.

Emotionally, this crystal promotes emotional peace and spiritual serenity.

Physically, Vera Cruz Amethyst works primarily at the spiritual level, aligning the light body with the physical body.

Healing

Works primarily at spiritual and energetic levels; supports meditation practice and spiritual development.

Position

Hold during meditation; place on the crown or third eye chakra; create a meditation grid.

Additional Notes

Warning: Fades in direct sunlight. Keep away from prolonged sun exposure.
