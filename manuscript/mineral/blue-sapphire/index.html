Attributes

Blue Sapphire is a seeker after spiritual truth, traditionally associated with love and purity. It is extremely effective for earth and chakra healing.

This tranquil stone helps you to stay on the spiritual path and is used in shamanic ceremonies to transmute negative energies. It opens and heals the throat chakra and the thyroid, and facilitates self-expression and speaking your truth.

Additional Notes

In addition to these specific properties, Blue Sapphire carries all the generic attributes of Sapphire.

Spellworking

Linked with Neptune, the sea god, blue sapphire is the classic wisdom stone. Use it in spells for spiritual truth, self-expression, and speaking your truth. It deepens meditation and shamanic ceremonies. As a translucent blue stone, it excels in spiritual and communication workings.

The Nine Gems

In a traditional Indian belief, each of the nine planets is paired with its own precious gem, the set known together as the navaratna. Blue sapphire is the gem of Saturn. The full set assigns the pearl to the Moon, the ruby to the Sun, topaz or yellow sapphire to Jupiter, the diamond to Venus, the emerald to Mercury, red coral to Mars, blue sapphire to Saturn, hessonite garnet to Rahu, and cat's eye to Ketu. Rahu and Ketu are the northern and southern lunar points that come into play during eclipses, which is why they are also called the eclipse planets.
