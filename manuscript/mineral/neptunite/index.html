Attributes

Neptunite has a natural affinity with the moon and the sea, relieving underwater environmental pressure. Spiritually, it helps in understanding the delusions, illusions and deceptions you place on yourself and others, especially in spiritual matters. Focusing and centring, it teaches how to sweep aside the veil and see into true reality. Psychologically, this stone is a comfort in seemingly intolerable situations, dissolving anger and resentment, and it shows why your soul chose to undergo an experience, assisting in clearing false beliefs that block forward progress.
