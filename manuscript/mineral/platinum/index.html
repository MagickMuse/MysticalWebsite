Platinum is a precious metal on the periodic table with an isometric crystal structure, registering 4 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale. Although it was officially discovered and named in Colombia in 1736, it has been found worked into burial goods created by Indigenous Colombians dating back over 2,000 years. It was also found decorating the sarcophagus of the ancient Egyptian queen Shapenapit, who was laid to rest around 700 BCE.

Appearance

A dense, silvery-white precious metal with a lustrous sheen. Platinum is highly durable and resistant to tarnish, making it prized for fine jewelry.

Origin

Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, New Zealand, Russia, South Africa, United States (Alaska, California, Colorado, Idaho, Oregon, Utah).
