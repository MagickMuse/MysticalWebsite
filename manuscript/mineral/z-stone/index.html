Appearance

Blackish-brownish-grey knobbly, opaque stone in raw formation.

Origin

Sahara

Attributes

A concretion found only in the Sahara, Z-stone opens the third eye and links it via the soma chakra to the crown and higher crown enabling journeying and the expansion of consciousness into multi-dimensions. Acting as a bridge between one level and another, it links to elemental beings as well as extraterrestrials. Focused intent is required for working with this stone, which may be assisted by combining Z-stone with other crystals such as Tugtupite to open the heart seed chakra to provide an anchor for expanded awareness. It works well with Rhodozite, Brandenberg or a Trigonic Quartz.

Healing

Z-stone works beyond the physical but can assist with earth healing.

Position

Hold, grid or position as appropriate. NOTE: Make gem essence by indirect method.
