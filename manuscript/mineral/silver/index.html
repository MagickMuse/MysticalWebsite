Silver is a precious metal in the Isometric crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 2.5 to 3, formed from star explosions billions of years ago. The first silver mining can be traced back to 3000 B.C.E. in Turkey and Greece.

Appearance

Silver presents as a lustrous, soft, white metallic element with a brilliant reflective surface. Rich veins of silver and silver ore can be found throughout South America, and this precious metal is most commonly fashioned into jewelry, coins, tableware, and scientific equipment. In its pure form, it was one of the first metals used by humans, and its beauty and scarcity led to it being shaped into divine images and offering pieces across cultures.

Colors

Bright metallic white, grey, tarnished dark grey

Origin

Found globally, with rich deposits throughout South America, Turkey, and Greece

Magickal Properties

Silver holds feminine energy and is deeply associated with the moon, especially the new moon phase. Where gold draws down the energy of the sun, Silver calls on the wisdom of the moon, infusing every aspect of life and spiritual practice with nurturing and healing magick. It is known to deflect negativity and promote wealth and overall well-being. Associated with the water element, its energy is cooling and calming, providing relief to those with a strong presence of fire and air elements. It is associated with the High Priestess card of the tarot, symbolizing intuition and the illumination of the inner world. A good speaker is described as "silver-tongued," reflecting the metal's ancient connection to eloquence and wisdom.

For beginners, Silver guides through the darkness and encourages inner exploration, helping the keeper confront subconscious feelings and find emotional peace. It brings clarity to thought and communication, embodying grace, elegance, and humble confidence. It enhances attractiveness, strengthens patience, and deepens intuition. It acts as a spiritual mirror, reflecting unconscious energies into the conscious realm so the keeper can see what lies within their own soul. Those who feel called to wear and work with Silver may need to balance their yin energy and connect more deeply with the Divine Feminine. It makes a meaningful gift for mothers, grandmothers, and other matriarchal figures.

Advanced practitioners work with Silver to navigate the subconscious realm, bringing what lies in darkness into the light. It helps unravel life's deepest mysteries and explore the shadow aspects of the soul for greater self-awareness. It bridges the astral body with the physical body, leading the keeper to create a life on Earth from a place of spiritual oneness and understanding. Silver enhances intuition and psychic abilities, revealing truth beyond what physical eyes alone can perceive. It deepens sensitivity and empathy, grants access to alternate realms, and infuses life with otherworldly wisdom. Throughout the world, Silver is identified with the lunar manifestations of the Great Mother, the eternal goddess, and Wiccan high priestesses wear silver crescents in Her honor to this day.

Chakra Work

Silver resonates with all chakras, acting as a universal conductor of healing energy. It is especially potent at the Crown and Third Eye Chakras, where it deepens psychic abilities and makes the keeper a powerful ally during tarot readings or any other form of divination. Wear Silver paired with healing crystals to shield against negativity and infuse intentions with goddess energies. Place silver jewelry on any energy center to amplify the healing properties of stones set within it.

Meditation

Meditating with Silver opens a direct channel to lunar wisdom and the subconscious mind. Hold a piece of silver or wear silver jewelry during meditation to deepen psychic receptivity and invite goddess connection. On the night of a Full Moon, hold silver about two feet from the eyes, catch the reflection of the Moon on the surface, and gaze until psychic impressions become known. Donning silver jewelry before sleep produces psychic dreams, especially when paired with moonstone or other psychically attuned stones.

Folklore

Because the Moon itself appears silver, the metal has always been read as its earthly echo. The habit of turning a silver coin in the pocket at the sight of a new Moon is a survival of this old link. The Moon was seen as a cosmic mirror lit by the Sun, and an ordinary mirror is simply glass backed with a fine layer of silver, which only deepened the connection. Silver goblets were filled with water and used for scrying, for water too is bound to silver and the Moon.

As a precious metal, silver has been struck into coins since 700 BC, when it was used in the form of electrum. The word sterling, the currency of the United Kingdom, comes from sterling silver, since one Troy pound of it was the source of the original pound sterling. Because silver is tied to psychic power and intuition, the fortune teller or crystal ball reader has long asked the client to cross my palm with silver, not only as payment but to help the psychic current flow.

Magickal Lore

Silver is the Moon's metal. Because it is found in its pure form, it was one of the first metals to be used by humans. The metal's beauty and scarcity caused it to be fashioned into divine images and offering pieces.

Throughout the world silver is identified with the lunar manifestations of the Great Mother, the eternal goddess. To this day, Wiccan high priestesses and those who view the Moon as a sacred symbol of the Goddess wear silver crescents in her honor. Silver objects are also placed on the altar during Wiccan Full Moon rituals.

Worshippers of the Goddess may ring silver bells to invoke Her presence during rituals. Since the bell itself is a goddess symbol and since silver is dedicated to her, this is a most effective and magickally accurate ritual procedure.

Silver is the metal of emotions, of the psychic mind, and of love and healing.

Love

Silver jewelry or empowered stones—such as emeralds, pearls, jade, or lapis lazuli—mounted in silver rings, are worn to attract love. Alternatively, etch the symbol of Venus (♀) on a small, round, silver disc. Place a pink candle over the disc and burn while visualizing love coming into your life.

Because silver is linked with the emotions, some feel overwrought or emotionally overwhelmed if they wear it at the time of the Full Moon. If this occurs, wear some gold to balance yourself, or simply remove the silver.

Psychism

Silver is a psychic-influencing metal. When worn, it stimulates psychic awareness while lulling the conscious mind. Many psychics constantly wear silver in order to more easily tap into the subconscious.

Full Moon Scrying

On the night of the Full Moon, scry with silver. Take any piece out into full moonlight. Compose yourself and hold the silver about two feet from your eyes, resting your hand. Catch the reflection of the Moon on the silver and gaze at this until psychic impulses become known.

Dreams

Donning silver jewelry before sleep is one method of producing psychic dreams. If the piece is set with moonstones or any other psychic stone, its effects will be more powerful. Alternatively, place a piece of silver beneath your pillow. Still your mind as you lie above the metal. Visualize your need for a psychic dream and see yourself as remembering your important dreams in the morning.

Peace

If you are angry or nervous, wear some silver. There is an old belief that anyone touched with a silver ring, no matter what stone was set in it, would immediately become calm.

Protection

Silver is used for protective purposes. As the Moon reflects the light of the Sun, so too does its metal reflect negativity away from the wearer. Tiny silver globes (or any silver jewelry) are worn for magickal security. Silver crescents, whose "horns" turn back evil, are popular worldwide.

This metal is also fashioned into jewelry, empowered, and then worn to keep its wearer's thoughts and moods in line.

In China, small children are guarded by silver lockets worn around the neck. French couples about to be married are protected by a silver chain. The notion that silver bullets destroy vampires and werewolves has been spread by modern literature and the cinema.

Travel Protection

Silver is said to be particularly potent in guarding travelers from dangers, especially while at sea.

Money

Something like two-thirds of the world's population use silver (or silver-coated coins) as money. It is extensively utilized in money-drawing magick.

Empower a silver dime with money-attracting vibrations. If you don't have a silver dime, try a silver bead or some other small piece of silver. (Note: Only American dimes minted before 1965 are wholly silver.) Place this in or under a candleholder and then enchant a green candle. Burn the candle in the holder and visualize unexpected money flowing into your life.

Norse Runic Symbolism

In the Norse runic tradition, silver represents the disk of the moon and signifies change, transmutation, and the striving for higher knowledge. It is understood as a metallic expression of white.

The cool, reflective quality of silver connects directly to lunar mysteries and the subconscious mind. Silver-colored items amplify receptive, feminine, and psychic energies in ritual work.

Care

Silver sits at Mohs 2.5 to 3, making it a very soft metal that scratches easily. It is not water safe as moisture causes tarnishing; store in a dry place or wrap in anti-tarnish cloth. Sun exposure is generally safe but will not prevent tarnish. Cleanse energetically with smoke, moonlight (especially Full Moon light to honor its lunar connection), or by placing on a selenite plate. Polish with a soft cloth to maintain its reflective brilliance. Silver jewelry worn during ritual work should be cleansed regularly to clear absorbed energies.

The Seven Magickal Metals

Silver is the metal of the Moon, one of the seven magickal metals that the ancients paired with the first seven planets. The full set links silver to the Moon, mercury to Mercury, copper to Venus, gold to the Sun, iron to Mars, tin to Jupiter, and lead to Saturn. In the Dharmic religions, sacred objects such as singing bowls and temple statuary are cast from a blend of all seven metals.

Incorruptible Protection

Silver is regarded as a spiritually incorruptible metal that radiates an aura of protection. Only genuine silver carries this power; older coins such as dimes were once real silver, but modern ones are not, so a real silver bead or charm is the surer choice today.
