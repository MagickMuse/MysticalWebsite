Magical Lore

Steel is a relatively modern metal with no great history in magic. At one time, it was thought to offer protection against fairies, who could apparently be mischievous. Some traditional uses have been discovered and preserved.

Protection

Small pieces of steel are carried to guard against negativity. A steel ring worn as a protective amulet shields the wearer from harmful influences.

Anti-Nightmare

Hold any dull steel knife and visualize it piercing and driving away negativity. Block negative impulses from disturbing you. See yourself waking up in the morning refreshed and rejuvenated. Then place the knife beneath your bed and sleep over it. This practice prevents nightmares.

Healing

In American folk magic, a steel ring worn faithfully on the hand prevents rheumatism. This is one of many traditional remedies passed down through generations.
