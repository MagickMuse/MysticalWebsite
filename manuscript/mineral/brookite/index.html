Brookite's Journey to You

Brookite is a rare titanium dioxide polymorph, one of only five minerals in the world to share that composition. It was identified and named in 1825 after Henry James Brooke, the English mineralogist who first formally described it. Found forming within hydrothermal vents and in igneous and metamorphic rock, brookite is considered one of the Synergy Twelve stones, a group of high-vibration minerals recognized as catalysts for spiritual evolution and Source consciousness. It carries the energy of cosmic balance and unity, representing the harmonious interplay of forces operating throughout the universe. Brookite also expands the auric field outward to receive interdimensional wisdom, and when found as an inclusion within clear quartz, the surrounding crystal amplifies its properties considerably.

Placed on the Third Eye and Crown Chakras together, brookite opens elevated channels of interstellar and interdimensional communication, attuning you to frequencies beyond ordinary perception. Did You Know?

Brookite is one of only five naturally occurring minerals composed of titanium dioxide. The other four are akaogiite, anatase, ilmenite, and rutile. Minerals that share the same chemical composition but differ in crystal structure are called polymorphs.
