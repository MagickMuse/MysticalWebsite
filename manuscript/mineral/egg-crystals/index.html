Appearance

Oval, egg-shaped form. The more pointed end is useful as a tool.

Properties

Confines and shapes energy.

Uses

Can be used to detect and rebalance blockages in the body. The more pointed end is a useful reflexology or acupressure tool. Make excellent hand comforters to use in times of stress.

Magickal Properties

Gemstone eggs carry energies of rebirth, renewal, and regeneration. They are associated with Ostara, goddess of spring, whose symbols include the rabbit and the egg as expressions of new life and unfolding potential. These shapes hold space for what is not yet manifest, making them useful for any working centered on new beginnings or the early stages of creation rather than only in spring. When placed on altars or in sacred spaces, gemstone eggs anchor energies of desire, manifestation, and possibility.
