💛Ametrine Blended Family Harmony

Ametrine is a member of the quartz family forming in the Hexagonal (Trigonal) crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, sourced from the border zone where amethyst and citrine grow side by side within the same crystal.

Appearance

Bicolor quartz displaying both amethyst purple and citrine yellow zones within a single stone, transparent to translucent, vitreous luster.

Origin

Bolivia, Brazil, Uruguay

Ametrine carries the peaceful, balancing energy of amethyst alongside citrine's qualities of empowerment and abundance, while also holding its own distinct magick. It bridges divine guidance with grounded, practical action, helping you synthesize what you receive spiritually with what you do in the physical world. By encouraging you to see yourself through the eyes of the Divine, it dissolves fears around power, success, and decision-making.

Ametrine powerfully combines amethyst and citrine. This crystal is fast and effective in its action and is particularly useful for long-standing illness as it brings insight into causes of dis-ease.

Ametrine connects the physical realm with higher consciousness. It facilitates and protects during astral travel and relieves psychic attack. This stone stimulates creativity and supports taking control of one's own life.

Psychologically, Ametrine enhances compatibility and acceptance of others. It shows where everyone is linked, overcoming prejudice. An extremely energetic stone, it stimulates creativity and supports taking control of one's own life. It promotes optimism and well-being.

Mentally, Ametrine brings clarity, harmonizing perception and action. It strengthens concentration and aids thinking through complex problems. It removes negative programming from the mind.

Ametrine clears stress and tension from the head, calming the mind and bringing greater focus to meditation. It opens the third eye, promoting healing and divination. It unites masculine and feminine energies. An exceptional blood cleanser and energizer, it regenerates the physical body and strengthens the immune system, aids the autonomic nervous system and physical maturation, stabilizes DNA/RNA, and oxygenates the body.
