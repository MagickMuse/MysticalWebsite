Appearance: Transparent to translucent green crystal

Origin

Attributes

Siberian Green Quartz is a powerful stone for healing and connection to nature.

Spiritually, this stone opens the heart chakra and facilitates connection to earth energies and nature spirits.

Psychologically, Siberian Green Quartz encourages emotional healing and release of old wounds.

Mentally, it brings mental balance and clarity.

Emotionally, this crystal is deeply healing for emotional trauma and promotes compassion and forgiveness.

Physically, Siberian Green Quartz supports the heart and immune system.

Healing

Beneficial for the heart, immune system, cellular regeneration, and detoxification.

Position

Place on the heart chakra; hold during meditation; wear as appropriate.

Additional Notes

Note: Some Siberian Green Quartz may be heat-treated or artificially colored.
