Magickal Powers

Psychic shield

Appearance

Transparent to translucent cubic or octahedral crystals, often banded in various colors.

Origin

China, United States, Germany, Argentina, Mexico, Britain

Attributes

Fluorite is a highly protective stone, especially on a psychic level. It helps you to discern when outside influences are at work and shuts off psychic manipulation and undue mental influence. This stone cleanses and stabilizes the aura. It is extremely effective against computer and electromagnetic stress. Appropriately positioned, it blocks geopathic stress.

Used in healing, Fluorite draws off negative energies and stress of all kinds. It cleanses, purifies, dispels, and reorganizes anything within the body that is not in perfect order. This is the best crystal to use to overcome any form of disorganization.

Fluorite grounds and integrates spiritual energies. It promotes unbiased impartiality and heightens intuitive powers.

Fluorite makes you more aware of higher spiritual realities and can quicken spiritual awakening. It anchors intuitive insights into the physical plane. When working with the upper chakras, Fluorite increases psychic communication and inter-dimensional communication with other planes.

Fluorite is an excellent learning aid, it organizes and processes information, linking what is already known into what is being learned, and increases concentration. It helps you absorb new information, promoting quick thinking. Fluorite is extremely effective against computer and electromagnetic stress.

Psychologically, Fluorite dissolves fixed patterns of behavior and gently opens the door to the subconscious, bringing suppressed feelings to the surface for resolution. It increases self-confidence and dexterity.

Emotionally, Fluorite has a stabilizing effect on emotions. It helps you to understand the effect of the mind and emotions on the body. In relationships, Fluorite teaches the importance of balance.

Physically, Fluorite is known as the "Genius Stone" and represents the highest state of mental achievement, boosting aptitude and discernment, the absorption of new information, and helping one work through complex issues.

Healing

Fluorite is effective against computer and electromagnetic stress. It draws off negative energies of all kinds. It restructures DNA and regenerates the cells, particularly in the skin and respiratory tract, and heals ulcers and wounds. Fluorite strengthens bone tissue and alleviates rheumatism, arthritis, and spinal injuries. It improves the discomfort of shingles and other nerve-related pain. Fluorite rekindles sexual libido.

Position

Place or wear as appropriate. Position on a computer or between yourself and the source of electromagnetic smog. Wear at the throat or sweep all over the body to detoxify and relieve stress.

Shapes

Fluorite Wands are artificially shaped, most often from a mixture of Green and Purple Fluorite. They have a wonderfully soothing energy and can be stroked over the skin to relieve pain and inflammation. Even a small wand will absorb an enormous amount of stress and, if not cleansed, may crack under the strain. It is often suggested that the wand be immersed in water to cleanse it, the water then being returned to the earth for transmutation of the pain.

Square shaped Fluorite consolidates energy within its form and can draw off negative energy and transform it into positive.

Spellworking

Fluorite is one of the New Age stones. It is found in various colors and in masses of interlocking, interpenetrating cubes. Single crystals resembling two pyramids fused together at their bases are also available.

This stone has no long history of magical use; its influences are only now being discovered.

Mental Powers

Fluorite works with the conscious mind. It is useful for straightening your thoughts, for reducing emotional involvement in a situation in order to gain a more accurate perspective. It strengthens analytical abilities and is useful for theorizing and assimilating information.

Emotional Balance

Because it affects the conscious (intellectual) mind, fluorite quells strong emotions and smooths thought over the angry sea of desperation, depression, or anger.

Amplifying Other Stones

Some workers use fluorite to strengthen the effects of other stones.

Psychic Reception

Fluorite opens the gateway to psychic reception. Rest a stone over the Third Eye, then meditate, or drift off to sleep and dream.
