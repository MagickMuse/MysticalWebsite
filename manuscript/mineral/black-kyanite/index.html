Black Kyanite is an aluminum-rich silicate, a variety of kyanite (also known as disthene), that forms distinctive elongated, fanlike blades in metamorphic and sedimentary rocks. It has a triclinic crystal structure and registers 5.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale.

Appearance

Black, bladed, striated crystals often in fan-like formations. Some blades can reach over six inches in length. Sometimes referred to as the Witches' Broom crystal for its resemblance to a sweeping fan.

Origin

Found worldwide.

Magickal Properties

Black kyanite is one of only two stones (alongside selenite) that can clear the energy of anything it touches instantly, yet it does not hold or store energy itself, so it never needs to be cleansed or cleared. This makes it an extraordinarily efficient magickal ally. Place it around the home to ward off negative energies and invite positivity and white light into your space.

All varieties of kyanite complement one another, and working with them in any combination amplifies their properties. Combining them activates energies that balance your home, physical spaces, body, and emotions. These crystals may be among the hardest working healing allies in any collection, and their synergy creates a potent combination of healing magick. Black kyanite is unique among kyanites in that it alone never requires clearing.

For beginners, black kyanite opens the path to connecting with spirit guides and past lives through shamanic journeying. It filters out lower energies and disturbing memories before you continue on your journey, and gently reveals where your soul needs healing without causing overwhelm.

For more advanced practitioners, this stone brings powerful energetic purification to all chakras while enhancing flow through the central energy channel. It raises vibrations and deepens interdimensional awareness without leaving you feeling untethered, keeping you grounded and connected to the earth. Black kyanite helps you retrieve fresh insights from your subconscious about any situation. Its blades can also be used to cut or narrow energetic cords to toxic people, relationships, habits, and places.

Chakra Work

Black kyanite activates the Earth Star Chakra. Placed in this position, it helps you deeply connect with Mother Earth, the ancestors, crystal and mineral spirits, and the faerie realm. Its remarkable clearing properties ensure that any connections with these beings come through in total alignment with higher frequencies. It can also be placed on any area of the body or any object, including other crystals and even your phone, to clear dense or stuck energy.

Partners Well With

Pair Black Kyanite with Black Tourmaline for a double layer of grounding protection. Nuummite deepens shamanic journeying and past-life exploration. Combine with the other kyanite varieties, Kyanite, Green Kyanite, Blue Kyanite, Orange Kyanite, and Crystalline Kyanite, to amplify all properties and balance body, home, and emotions. Selenite shares black kyanite's self-clearing nature and together they create an unmatched purification duo. Shungite adds powerful energetic shielding and detoxification.

Healing

Black kyanite works primarily on the subtle bodies, repairing tears and holes in the aura. It may assist with muscle and skeletal issues and supports the adrenal glands.

Position

Sweep through the aura to clear and align chakras, or place on specific blockage points. Can be placed on any area of the body, on objects, or around the home for continuous clearing.

Lore

In some Christian and Muslim traditions, it is believed that the blade of the archangel Michael was forged from black kyanite, owing to its powerful protective and cleansing properties.

Magickal Message

I am a powerful siphoner and purifier of all negative energies. Let us work together to clear your life of all that does not serve your highest good and replace it with pure white light.
