Attributes

Nuummite is one of the oldest stones on earth, formed over 3 billion years ago. This ancient stone is a powerful protector against negative energies, psychic attack and black magic. It creates an impenetrable shield around the aura and helps to deflect harmful energies back to their source. Nuummite is excellent for those who work in healing or spiritual fields as it provides strong psychic protection.

This stone facilitates deep shamanic journeying and helps you to travel safely through the lower worlds. It connects you to your personal power and helps you to reclaim any power that has been given away or stolen. Nuummite assists in cutting energetic cords and releasing karmic patterns that no longer serve your highest good.

Spiritually, Nuummite helps you to see through illusions and to perceive the truth in any situation. It enhances discernment and helps you to recognize deception or manipulation.

Healing

Nuummite may assist with disorders of the brain and nervous system.

Position

Wear for protection, hold during shamanic work or place as appropriate. Grid around healing spaces.
