Mental Powers

Sphene (Titanite) is a greenish-yellow stone rarely found in transparent crystals and seldom used in jewelry due to its softness. The name "Sphene" comes from the Greek word for "wedge," describing the shape of its crystals. It is used to improve the mind and the processing of information, making it excellent for studying, theorizing, and debating.

Spirituality

Lore

Originally called titanite for its rich titanium content, this mineral was renamed sphene in 1801 after the Greek word sphenos, meaning "wedge," a reference to its characteristic wedge-shaped crystal habit. Sphene exhibits pleochroism, an optical phenomenon in which the stone appears to shift color depending on the angle of observation, revealing different hues as it catches the light. This quality has long fascinated gem collectors and metaphysical practitioners alike, as the stone seems to hold multiple realities within a single form. Sphene is sourced near the ancient city of Anatolia in Turkey, home to Gobekli Tepe, one of the oldest known temples on Earth, linking this stone to some of the deepest roots of human spiritual practice.
