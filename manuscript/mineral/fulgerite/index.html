Appearance

Sandy-beige, grey, or yellowish tiny grains cemented into a tubular formation.

Origin

United States, Sahara Desert, Gobi Desert, Australia, Germany, France.

Attributes

Fulgurite forms when lightning strikes sand, concentrating immense energy into a small space. Despite its fragility, it has long served as a powerful shamanic tool for soul work, retaining high-frequency energy from the strike.

In soul retrieval journeys, shamans place recovered soul fragments into fulgurite for safe transport back. The stone is then positioned over the heart chakra and the lost piece is gently blown home, with the lightning's energy opening the pathway. Fulgurite also assists in retrieving soul parts left behind during traumatic past-life events, deaths, or disappointments that anchor the soul to another lifetime. It can guide journeys into the between-life state to understand why a soul did not fully incarnate. As fragments pass through the tube, they are purified and prepared for reintegration.

Traditionally used to blow prayers to the universe, fulgurite is a potent manifestation tool. Speak your intention aloud and blow through the tube to release it toward your highest good. The hollow center acts as a conduit for divine energy to enter the physical realm, creating an energy vortex that moves from the chakras into the subtle bodies, purifying and realigning as it ascends through frequencies. A perfect shamanic anchor, it connects you deep into the earth's core.

Psychologically, fulgurite helps release whatever blocks progress, making space for behaviors aligned with your evolution. Mentally, it teaches how to hold only the highest intentions by bringing thought into form. Physically, gridding fulgurite around the body draws off negative energy and replenishes vitality lost to psychic vampirism or chronic illness.

Healing

Clears restrictions and constrictions blocking energy or blood flow. Raises overall energy levels and libido. Its deepest work occurs at the soul level.

Position

Hold or place as appropriate. Handle gently and avoid applying pressure.
