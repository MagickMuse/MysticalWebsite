Appearance

Four sides on a base, but the base itself may be squared off if the crystal is natural rather than artificially shaped.

Properties

Amplifies and then tightly focuses energy through the apex. Enhances and focuses the inherent properties of the crystal.

Uses

Suitable for holding manifestation programs. Can be used to draw off negative energies and blockages from the chakras, replenishing with vibrant energy. Naturally occurring pyramid-shaped crystals, such as Apophyllite, have particularly strong focusing abilities.

Additional Notes

Artificially shaped pyramids are available in an abundance of materials and enhance the base crystal's properties.

Magickal Properties

Obelisks mirror the sacred geometry of the Egyptian pyramid complexes at Giza. The four-sided base corresponds to foundational energy and the earth element. The three-sided pyramid faces correspond to trinity and the union of past, present, and future, a form sacred across spiritual traditions including the Maiden, Mother, and Crone in the Craft and Father, Son, and Holy Spirit in Christianity.
