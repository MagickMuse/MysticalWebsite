Appearance

Green stone with silvery feathers within the darker stone, often small and polished.

Origin

Siberia

Attributes

Placed on the third eye or meditated with, Seraphinite is a stone of spiritual enlightenment and is excellent for accessing self-healing. It is one of the crystals for making angelic connection and for opening the crown and higher crown chakras. This stone promotes living from the heart and has a gently cleansing effect on the heart chakra, opening to love.

Its feathery wings whisk you up to a high spiritual vibration and it is excellent for making journeys out of the body, protecting the physical body while you are gone.

Healing

Seraphinite works best at a subtle level. It activates the spinal cord and its links to the etheric body, especially behind the heart, and can release muscle tension up into the neck.

Position

Place on third eye or heart, or under pillow, or wear around the neck.

Chakra Work

Seraphinite is a Heart Chakra activator. Hold it against the heart space to encourage higher-realm communication and feel the loving support of angels and spirit guides. It assists in healing body, mind, and spirit by releasing what no longer serves the bearer. Held in meditation or gridded on the body during a healing session, Seraphinite aligns the nonphysical bodies, releases toxic emotions, and removes blockages in the energetic meridians. For beginners, it clears energetic attachments and guards against psychic attacks, while opening the portal to higher dimensions and the angelic realms.

Spellworking

Seraphinite is the perfect gemstone for opening energy channels and feeling the stability of the higher realms when you feel too anchored in the physical and need an extra dose of spiritual magick. It is considered a master emotional and physical healer because of its sweeping ability to encourage cellular regeneration. Meditate with it to facilitate angelic communication and deeper spiritual awareness, or carry it to keep the connection to your guides open wherever you roam. Sleeping with Seraphinite under your pillow is said to help you meet your guardian angels in your dreams. It partners beautifully with Charoite for spiritual transformation, Danburite for angelic attunement, Herkimer Diamond for amplified spiritual vision, Moldavite for accelerated evolution, Petalite for high-vibration peace, Scolecite for inner stillness and dream work, and Tanzanite for expanded psychic awareness.

Lore

Seraphinite is named after the seraphim, the highest order of angels in Judeo-Christian tradition, who are described as six-winged beings guarding the throne of God and guiding his chariot through the heavens. The stone's fibrous, feathery patterns are said to resemble the wings of these celestial guardians. Seraphinite was first described in 1888 by the Russian mineralogist Nikolay Koksharov, who discovered it in Siberia. It was initially named clinochlore, from the Greek "klino" (to lean) and "chloros" (green), referring to both the stone's color and the way its crystals arrange themselves at tilted angles. It was not until after World War II that jewelers began calling it Seraphinite, inspired by its angelic resemblance. Its fibrous patterns are created by inclusions of manganese, aluminum, and iron within a hydrothermally altered pyroxene, biotite, and amphibole matrix, producing a glowing, chatoyant appearance against the opaque green background.
