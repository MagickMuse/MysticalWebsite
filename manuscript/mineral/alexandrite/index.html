With its ability to show two colours at once, Alexandrite allows you to see both sides of the picture and links the heart and the mind so they come from a dispassionate, illumined perspective that encompasses and integrates the rational and the intuitive viewpoints.

Psychologically, Alexandrite, a regenerative stone, rebuilds self-respect and self-worth. Through realignment and centring you around your self, it reinforces your sense of who you really are. This stone strengthens willpower and dreaming, and assists in accurately assessing emotions – both your own and those of others.

Mentally, this stone inspires the imagination and attunes to your inner voice. A comforter that facilitates emotional maturity and encourages joyfulness in everyday life, Alexandrite teaches how to expend less effort. Physically, this stone harmonizes male and female energies and encourages tissue regeneration.

Spellworking

Alexandrite is an orthorhombic variety of chrysoberyl, first discovered in 1830 in the Ural Mountains of Russia on the birthday of Czar Alexander II, for whom it was named. It is among the rarest gemstones in the world; premium specimens exceed diamonds in value. Its most remarkable quality is pleochroism — called the alexandrite effect — in which the stone shifts from red to teal to violet depending on the light source. Rulers have prized it throughout history for enhancing personal will, strength, and clarity of insight.

Alexandrite works simultaneously across the Heart, Third Eye, and Crown chakras, making it one of the few stones capable of full upper-body alignment in a single placement. Worn as a necklace, it brings these three centers into harmony, easing grief and offering a felt sense of hope during times of loss. Placed over the Third Eye, it stimulates the pineal gland and balances left-right brain function, supporting the integration of wisdom from emotionally difficult experiences. At the Crown Chakra, it attunes all energy centers to the frequency of divine love and opens access to the Akashic records.

For the Beginner: Alexandrite invites joy, expansiveness, and emotional balance by aligning the heart, mind, and spirit. It supports the manifestation process by helping the bearer feel genuinely worthy of receiving what they desire.

For the Advanced Practitioner: Alexandrite opens the practitioner to the higher realms, collective consciousness, and the deepest layers of cosmic time. It is exceptionally useful in healing sessions for clients carrying grief or trauma that has become habitual those who hold their pain as a form of security. Its energy softens emotional barriers gently, replacing them with calm and openness. Pairs with Celestite, Clear Quartz, Labradorite, Ruby, or Sapphire for amplified higher-realm connection and heart healing.

Colors

Alexandrite displays a dramatic color shift depending on the light source, moving from teal and green in natural daylight to red and violet under incandescent light. This pleochroic quality, the alexandrite effect, is the stone's most celebrated feature and the reason it has been considered magical since its discovery.

Dark Magick Applications
