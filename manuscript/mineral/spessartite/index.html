About Spessartite

Spessartine garnet is a manganese-aluminum silicate variety of garnet found in shades of orange, red-orange, and red-brown. Known traditionally as the garnet of the sun, its name derives from the Spessart mountain range in Germany, one of its most notable source locations. Crystallizing in the isometric system, spessartine garnet is considered a stone of manifestation, passion, conception, and birth, with particular resonance for artists, creatives, and anyone engaged in bringing something new into existence.

Origin

Worldwide. Also found in Afghanistan, Brazil, China, Germany, Madagascar, Myanmar, Namibia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, United States.

Spessartite vibrates at a high rate. It imparts a willingness to help others and strengthens the heart. It enhances analytical processes and the rational mind. It is an antidepressant and it suppresses nightmares.
