Attributes

Kakortokite is a rare nepheline syenite that facilitates access to ancient wisdom and shamanic journeying. It connects you to the wisdom of indigenous peoples and helps you understand your place in the web of life. This stone is particularly useful for those who work with earth energies or who wish to develop a deeper connection to nature. Kakortokite stimulates the base chakra and helps to ground spiritual energy into physical reality.

This stone assists in past-life recall, especially lives connected to shamanic traditions or indigenous cultures. It helps you to integrate ancient wisdom into modern life and to find your spiritual path. Kakortokite is excellent for those who feel disconnected from their roots or who are searching for their spiritual heritage.

Healing

Kakortokite may assist with grounding and stabilizing energy in the body. It is reported to help with disorders of the base chakra and to support the skeletal system.

Position

Hold during meditation or place at the base chakra. Use during shamanic journeying or earth-healing work.
