Appearance

Opaque or clear, bubbly stone.

Origin

Norway, Russia, Canada, Tanzania, India, France, United States.

Attributes

Sunstone without yellowness, Moonstone without shimmer, Oligocrase facilitates the processing of emotions with the cycles of the moon, withdrawing to dream at dark moon and emerging to put ideas and insights into practice at full moon.

Psychologically, this stone breaks old patterns and habits, restoring trust and innocence, and works well at the north-west of the medicine wheel.

Healing

Balances fluids within the body, assisting lymphatic flow, and is beneficial for bones and fractures.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate.
