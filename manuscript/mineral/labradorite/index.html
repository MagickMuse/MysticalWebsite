Magickal Powers

Prevents energy leaks

Appearance

Grayish to black with blue or yellow flashes. All sizes, usually polished. Dark until catches light, then iridescent blue or gold flashes.

Origin

Italy, Greenland, Finland, Russia, Canada, Scandinavia.

Attributes

Iridescent Labradorite is a highly mystical and protective stone, a bringer of light. It raises consciousness and connects with universal energies. Labradorite deflects unwanted energies from the aura and prevents energy leakage. It forms a barrier to negative energies shed during therapy. It can take you into another world or into other lives.

Labradorite aligns the physical and etheric bodies and accesses spiritual purpose. It raises consciousness and grounds spiritual energies into the physical body. This stone stimulates intuition and psychic gifts, including the art of right timing, bringing messages from the unconscious mind to the surface and facilitating their understanding.

Psychologically, Labradorite banishes fears and insecurities and the psychic debris from previous disappointments, including those experienced in past lives. It strengthens faith in the self and trust in the universe. It removes other people's projections, including thought forms that have hooked into the aura.

Labradorite calms an overactive mind and energizes the imagination, bringing up new ideas. Analysis and rationality are balanced with the inner sight. Labradorite brings contemplation and introspection.

Labradorite is a useful companion through change, imparting strength and perseverance.

Healing

Labradorite treats disorders of the eyes and brain, relieves stress, and regulates metabolism. It treats colds, gout, and rheumatism, balances hormones and relieves menstrual tension, and lowers blood pressure. It may also assist with disorders of the nervous system and support the immune system.

Labradoradorite

Labradoradorite is a hybrid of Labradorite and Andesine that enhances magickal abilities and provides psychic protection. This stone is excellent for those who practice any form of magick or energy work as it amplifies intention and protects against psychic attack. Labradoradorite strengthens the aura and prevents energy leakage, particularly useful for healers and lightworkers. It stimulates the Third Eye and Crown Chakras, enhancing intuition and spiritual insight. It helps you to see through illusions and to perceive the truth in any situation. Labradoradorite is particularly useful during times of transformation as it helps you to navigate change with grace and wisdom. Psychologically, it builds confidence in your psychic abilities and helps you to trust your intuition. It dispels fears and insecurities, replacing them with inner knowing and strength.

Position

Wear over the higher heart chakra, hold or place as appropriate.

Additional Notes

Yellow Labradorite accesses the highest levels of consciousness, enhances visualization, trance, clairvoyance, and channeling. It is beneficial for the solar plexus chakra and expands the mental body, bringing in higher wisdom. It heals the stomach, spleen, liver, gallbladder, and adrenal glands.

Position: Place on third eye, solar plexus, or hold.

Spellworking

Labradorite is a powerful protector from the feldspar family, first discovered in Labrador, Canada. Its iridescent flashes of blue, green, and gold make it a striking stone for both aesthetic and magickal purposes. Known as the "teacher stone," it helps practitioners learn the mysteries of the universe while deflecting negative energies aimed at the wearer.

Protection

Labradorite excels at maintaining the integrity of your aura. It deflects unwanted energies while keeping your own energy contained within your energetic field. This makes it invaluable in emotionally charged environments, preventing your energy from leaking out or contributing to volatile feelings around you. Wear labradorite when you need to stay energetically neutral in high-conflict situations. This stone is especially helpful for paranormal investigators and anyone working in environments where psychic disturbances are common.

Aura Repair

Labradorite mends, cleanses, and fortifies the aura. It also nurtures perseverance and determination, two qualities every successful magickal career depends on. Sleep with a piece beneath the pillow. When the stone sees regular use, keep it on a steady cleansing schedule.

Magickal Properties

Labradorite is a stone of seers and mystics, deeply connected to the Third Eye Chakra. It sparks the imagination, rekindles passion, and fills you with hope and a sense of opportunity. According to Inuit lore, the northern lights once fell from the sky and became trapped within a stone. A warrior struck the rock with his spear, releasing most of the light, but some remained inside, giving labradorite its signature rainbow flashes. This stone embodies the energy of fresh starts, helping you navigate transitions with grace and confidence. It sharpens your focus, clarifies your deepest desires, and channels your intentions toward manifestation. Labradorite connects you to the energy of the dark moon, reminding you that even without visible light to guide the way, your intuition will always lead you forward.

Chakra Work

Labradorite resonates powerfully with the Third Eye Chakra. When placed on this energy center during meditation, it heightens your senses and expands your inner vision, allowing you to perceive the world with greater depth and clarity. Like the Hermit card in the tarot, it illuminates your path through challenges. It opens the psychic channels wide, supporting rapid energy transfers and accelerating the flow of information from higher dimensions.

Correspondences

Labradorite works beautifully alongside Amethyst, Celestite, Danburite, Fluorite, Lapis Lazuli, Moonstone, Opalite, Smoky Quartz, and Sunstone. It is associated with Leo and Sagittarius and resonates with the Triclinic crystal system.

Colors

Labradorite appears dark gray to black in its base form, but its hallmark labradorescence produces vivid flashes of blue, green, gold, orange, and sometimes violet as light refracts through its compacted internal layers. The intensity and range of color depend entirely on the angle of the light source and the depth of the stone's layers.

Magickal Message

"I am the stone of the dark moon, drawing the powers of the night and the mysteries of the universe into your soul. Call on me to support you as you undertake any new projects or intentions. Allow me to help you bring your creations to life as together we harness all the magick of the moon."

Care

Labradorite rates 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale, making it reasonably durable but worth treating with care to preserve its layered structure and brilliant flash. It is both sun safe and water safe. Cleanse with running water, moonlight, or sound. Charging under the dark or new moon is especially fitting given its deep lunar connection.
