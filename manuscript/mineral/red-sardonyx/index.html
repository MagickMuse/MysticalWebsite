Red colored, banded, opaque sardonyx. May be large or small, often tumbled.

Brazil, India, Russia, Asia Minor

Attributes

Red Sardonyx stimulates.

In addition to this specific property, Red Sardonyx carries all the generic attributes of Sardonyx.

Spellworking

Red Sardonyx stimulates while providing sardonyx's strength and protection. Use it for energizing spells, boosting vitality, and increasing motivation. Like all red stones, it stimulates passion and life force energy.
