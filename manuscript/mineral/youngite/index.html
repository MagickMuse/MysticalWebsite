Appearance

Striated or massive opaque stone in orange-brown and russet with white or grey inclusions.

Origin

United States.

Attributes

Youngite is a shamanic stone for journeying into other realms or reconnecting with your inner child and the gifts you brought into the world but may have forgotten or abandoned. This stone holds child energy, taking you back to the innocence and sense of wonder that you experienced before incarnating into a physical body, reminding you of soul imperatives and the purpose underlying your present life.

Psychologically, this stone releases everything that is not appropriate to who you truly are and brings out your inner playfulness, teaching that life is a game to be enjoyed rather than an ordeal to be suffered. If sexual, physical or emotional abuse has shut down your inner child or stolen your childhood from you, Youngite helps the inner child to heal and grow, teaching that the world is safe.

Physically, this stone assists journeying within the body and the meridians.

Healing

Beneficial for the adrenals, kidneys, meridians, reproductive system; assists recovery from abuse.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate.
