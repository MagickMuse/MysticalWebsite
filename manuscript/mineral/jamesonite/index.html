Appearance

Opaque metallic crystal with silky lustre.

Origin

Bolivia, Romania.

Attributes

Jamesonite is a useful stone for those who have difficulty making decisions or who get stuck in old patterns. It helps you to see situations clearly and to move forward with confidence. This stone assists in breaking free from limiting beliefs and behaviours that no longer serve your highest good. Jamesonite encourages flexibility and adaptability whilst maintaining your core values and principles.

Psychologically, Jamesonite helps with self-discipline and supports you in making necessary changes in your life. It is particularly useful for breaking addictive patterns or compulsive behaviours. The stone enhances mental clarity and helps you to think through problems logically.

Spiritually, Jamesonite facilitates transformation and rebirth. It helps you to release the past and embrace new beginnings with courage and determination.

Healing

Jamesonite may assist with disorders of the nervous system and support detoxification processes. It is reported to help with hair and nail growth.

Position

Hold, carry or place as appropriate. Use during meditation for decision-making.
