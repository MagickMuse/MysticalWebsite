Appearance

Black, opite carbon-based substance, often lustrous.

Origin

United States, China, Australia, Poland, Germany.

Attributes

Coal, the common substance used to heat millions of homes, has surprising magical properties related to prosperity and financial luck.

Position

Carry in the pocket or place with money.

Spellworking

Coal is considered by many to be an excellent money-attractant and hence is carried in the pocket and placed with money.

Speculators in the stock exchange in London often carry some coal with them for luck.
