A profoundly earthy and anti-geopathogenic stone, Shungite is found only in Karelia, northern Russia. What is known is that although the lake is highly polluted, the water that pours from it has been purified by the Shungite bed. The water has been used as a healing spa for hundreds of years.

Shungite contains virtually all the minerals in the periodic table. It has phenomenal shielding power that arises from its unique formation. Lore

Shungite is mainly composed of carbon in the form of amorphous graphite, with trace amounts of silicon, iron, magnesium, and phosphorus. In ancient Roman times, it was used to line aqueduct systems to remove impurities from drinking water. Centuries later, Peter the Great established Russia's first spa in the Karelia region, where Shungite is predominantly found, specifically to capitalize on its water-purifying properties. Scientific research as recently as 2017 has verified that Shungite exhibits both antibacterial and antioxidant properties, supporting anti-aging and cellular renewal practices. Noble, or elite, Shungite is the rarest form and is composed of up to 98 percent organic carbon.
