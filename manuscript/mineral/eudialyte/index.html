Eudialyte's Journey to You

Eudialyte was first identified in 1819 in Greenland, though the majority of specimens available today originate from northern Russia. Its name derives from the Greek "eudialytos," meaning capable of being dissolved, a reference to its solubility in acid. It forms as a primary mineral in alkaline igneous rocks and is one of the more complex silicate minerals in terms of chemical composition, often containing manganese, zirconium, and aegirine within its structure. Both the manganese and aegirine components are considered significant contributors to its magickal properties, the manganese lending emotional support and the aegirine providing auric and psychic protection.
