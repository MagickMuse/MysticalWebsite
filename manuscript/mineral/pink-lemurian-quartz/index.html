Pink Lemurian Quartz is a variety of quartz in the Hexagonal/Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, distinguished by hematite inclusions layered throughout the crystal during its growth process.

Appearance

Pink Lemurian Quartz displays soft pink hues produced by hematite deposits distributed throughout the crystal matrix. Like all Lemurian varieties, it features characteristic horizontal grooves along the shaft, known as the "ladder of the gods" or "staircase to heaven," believed to contain encoded messages from the ancient Lemurian civilization. The crystals exhibit the classic quartz form with vitreous luster and transparent to translucent clarity.

Colors

Pink, soft rose

Origin

Minas Gerais, Brazil
