Appearance

Remains or negative impressions of ancient creatures and plants transformed into stone over millions of years.

Origin

Found worldwide, including United States, Canada, Argentina, Australia, Germany, Morocco.

Attributes

Fossils are linked with Akasha, the fifth element, because they were once alive. In the mystic language of the psychic mind, fossils represent time, eternity, and evolution. They are a tangible example of how nothing in nature is wasted—energy cannot be destroyed, only manifestations of energy. Matter is transmutable.

The ritual use of fossils is ancient. Fossils have been found in Neolithic burial sites in Europe. Fossils are used as power tools by shamans throughout the world to amplify energy. Many contemporary Wiccans place them on their altars because of their mystic significance.

Position

Place on altar, carry, or wear as jewelry.

Spellworking

Fossils are bizarre, beautiful magical tools. Though not stones in the usual sense, the minerals that replace ancient creatures and plants create rock-like substances.

Protection

Fossils are used as protective objects. They are placed in the home, or fashioned into jewelry and worn to increase your natural defenses. In Morocco, stones embedded with fossils are carried for protective purposes.

Longevity

Due to their enormous age, fossils of all types are worn as amulets to increase the life span.

Ritual Power

They can be placed on the altar as symbols of the earth and the ambiguity of time, or to increase the power of magical rituals.

Specific Fossil Types

Ammonites (draconites): Fossilized, spiral-shaped sea animals. Due to their bizarre appearance, they were thought to be stones removed from dragon's heads, and were bound to the left arm for magical protection. In Britain, they were known as "snake stones."

Ancient Sponges (Witch stones): Round and pierced through with a natural hole. These are strung and worn like beads or hung in the house for protection.

Fossilized Sand Dollars: Show a natural five-pointed design and are often found on Wiccan altars. Linked with the pentagram and the elements. Because fossils are ruled by Akasha, sand dollars are carried or used in magic to gain awareness of the realms of earth, air, fire, and water.

Elemental Work

Fossilized sand dollars, with their natural pentagram shape, are used in elemental rituals to connect with all five elements simultaneously. They make excellent altar centerpieces for empowering spells.

Past-Life Work

Due to their immense age, fossils serve as powerful focal points for past-life regression work. Hold during meditation to access memories across lifetimes.

Dark Magick Applications

Fossilized Shark Teeth

Petrified remains of predatory creatures are frequently employed in aggressive magick. Shark teeth in particular can curse a target with insatiable hunger or heighten their vulnerability while in water.
