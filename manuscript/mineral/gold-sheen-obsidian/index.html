Appearance

Black obsidian with a golden metallic sheen. Often tumbled or polished spheres.

Origin

Mexico, United States.

Attributes

Gold Sheen Obsidian is a protective stone that shows you what is needed for healing. It takes you into the future and into the past to connect with the cause of dis-ease. Gold Sheen Obsidian eliminates ego conflicts and helps release deep conditioning. This stone is particularly effective for scrying.

Healing

Gold Sheen Obsidian is beneficial for balancing the ego and reclaiming personal power. It aids in healing the solar plexus chakra.

Position

Place on the solar plexus or use for scrying.

Spellworking

Sacred to Hecate, the patroness of witches, obsidian is a favorite stone for scrying mirrors. Gold Sheen Obsidian combines obsidian's protective qualities with solar energy for ego healing and personal empowerment. It boosts strength and can help break down blockages related to self-worth. As an opaque stone, it excels at grounding and shadow work.

Magickal Properties

Golden sheen obsidian is a naturally occurring volcanic glass with an Amorphous crystal structure, a rarer variety of Black Obsidian that embodies solar energy (in contrast to its lunar counterpart, silver sheen obsidian). Known since ancient times as the Wizard Stone, it has a long history in magickal practice. The ancient Maya reportedly worked with this stone to channel wisdom from other realms, gazing into it to bring subconscious thoughts to the surface and cleanse negative attachments. Today it remains a powerful auric cleanser and guide for shadow work. It transmutes low vibrations and encourages self-reflection, granting insight into the true nature of emotional challenges and helping you heal at deeper levels. For beginners, it empowers shadow integration and transmutation, helping you face darker aspects of the self with courage and compassion. It cuts through judgment, fear, and uncertainty, clearing the path to personal power. It purifies stagnant energy from the aura and provides clarity on higher purpose. For advanced practitioners, golden sheen obsidian illuminates the deepest aspects of the soul for lasting self-awareness. It helps acknowledge and release negative patterns that prevent alignment with your highest good, encouraging you to move past ego and act in concert with the wellbeing of all.

Chakra Work

Golden sheen obsidian activates the Root and Earth Star Chakras, creating a deep grounding connection with the earth and the ancestors. Keep it in the home to maintain a grounded and protected frequency in your space. Hold a piece while traveling or wear it as jewelry to clear the aura of negativity and keep your vibration pure and elevated. It provides strong support during times of release and transformation, making it an ideal companion during shadow work sessions. It is also a thoughtful gift for anyone undergoing periods of change or challenge.

Correspondences

Golden sheen obsidian partners well with Black Obsidian, Labradorite, Moldavite, Rainbow Obsidian, Rutilated Quartz, and Silver Sheen Obsidian. It is associated with Sagittarius and has an Amorphous crystal structure.

Colors

Golden sheen obsidian appears as a deep black stone with a lustrous golden metallic sheen across its surface. The golden effect comes from gas trapped in the magma during cooling. When polished, the contrast between the dark base and warm golden glow is striking, often appearing most vivid in spheres and tumbled forms.

Magickal Message

"Do not fear your shadow, it is part of who you are. So much wisdom and power are to be found in the darkness. I am here to help you mine the gold of your inner darkness, to see the gifts in your struggles."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 5 to 5.5, golden sheen obsidian is relatively soft and should be handled with care to avoid scratching. Cleanse with moonlight, smoke, or sound. As a solar stone, it can also be briefly charged in sunlight to amplify its energy. Store separately from harder stones to preserve its polished surface.
