Appearance

Clear Quartz with dark dendrites.

Origin

Brazil.

Attributes

Hollandite Quartz is excellent for past life work and for healing the ancestral line. The dendrites* within the crystal take you travelling back through time to discover the source of dis-ease or behavioral patterns that affects you in the present life and to heal or reprogramme them as appropriate. This is particularly useful for ingrained patterns that have been in your family for generations. If you have lived previous lives where you were deeply religious or were a monk or nun under a vow of celibacy, chastity or poverty, Hollandite heals the effect of the vows, allowing you to take an intimate partner and to prosper in the present life.

Hollandite Quartz is helpful if past life experiences of drowning or suffocation are affecting your present breathing or if you suffer panic attacks that appear to have no basis in present life experience.

*Dendrites are branching, treelike crystalline structures within a stone.

Healing

Hollandite Quartz is reputed to be helpful for lung and breathing disorders.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate.
