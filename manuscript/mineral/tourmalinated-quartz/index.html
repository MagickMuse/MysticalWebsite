💰Prosperity Stone

Appearance

Clear or smoky quartz. with black or dark green tourmaline needles running through it. The tourmaline inclusions appear as thin rods or strands trapped within the transparent crystal. Available in all sizes, often tumbled or polished.

Origin

Brazil, United States, Madagascar, India, Australia, China.

Attributes

Tourmalinated Quartz unites the amplifying properties of clear quartz with the protective qualities of black tourmaline, creating a powerfully effective combination stone. The tourmaline inclusions act as natural energy filters, deflecting and transmuting negativity while the quartz matrix amplifies this protective effect.

This crystal excels at transforming negative thought patterns and destructive habits. It brings together opposing forces into harmonious balance—light and shadow, yin and yang, conscious and unconscious—making it ideal for those seeking integration and wholeness. When worn or carried, it creates an energetic shield that guards against environmental pollutants, electromagnetic stress, and psychic attack.

Psychologically, Tourmalinated Quartz assists in seeing through illusions and getting to the root of problems. It encourages an objective, balanced perspective and helps dissolve crystallized patterns of thought that no longer serve you. This stone strengthens resolve against self-sabotage and supports breaking free from victim mentality.

Spiritually, it grounds high-frequency energies into the physical body while simultaneously releasing density and blockages from the aura. The stone facilitates shadow work by illuminating what has been hidden while providing protection during the process.

Healing

Tourmalinated Quartz is beneficial for the nervous system and may help with disorders related to tension and stress. It supports detoxification on all levels and is said to assist with digestive issues. The stone strengthens the immune system and helps restore balance to the physical body.

Position

Wear as a pendant or carry in a pocket for ongoing protection. Place on the solar plexus to release tension or on the third eye to enhance clarity. Grid around the home or workspace to create a protective barrier against negative energies.

Spellworking

Energy: Receptive | Planet: Pluto

Wear tourmalinated quartz or place it beneath the pillow to promote astral projection. Alternatively, obtain a sphere of tourmalinated quartz and, gazing at it, still your mind and project your astral body into the crystal.
