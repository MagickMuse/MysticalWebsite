Nephrite is a monoclinic mineral and one of the two recognized varieties of jade, distinguished from jadeite in 1863. Richer in magnesium than its jadeite counterpart, nephrite is a traditional stone of restoration, healing, and prosperity that has been revered across cultures for thousands of years.

Appearance

Nephrite comes in a variety of colors, with green being the most prized and valuable. It can range from creamy white to deep forest green, often with a smooth, waxy luster.

Origin

Afghanistan, Australia, Austria, Canada, China, Cuba, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Myanmar, New Zealand, Poland, Russia, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, Turkey, Ukraine, United States, Vietnam, Zimbabwe

Magickal Properties

Nephrite is a stone of prosperity, luck, and wellness. Keep a piece in your wallet or workspace to encourage success and good fortune in business endeavors, or place it in your garden to support the health and growth of your plants. It aids manifestation by encouraging mental clarity and endurance, and it attracts good luck to business ventures.

For emotional healing, nephrite holds great power over the Heart Chakra. It soothes the emotional body and surrounds you in comforting, loving energy, uplifting your frequency to help you attract soul connections aligned with your highest self. It encourages harmony and equality in relationships, promotes unconditional love, and surrounds partnerships with energies of peace, joy, and stability.

Nephrite encourages wellness by helping you identify the root cause of pain and suffering. It soothes the nervous system, relieves stress, provides gentle protection, and helps you release negativity, restoring you to a high-vibrational state.

For more advanced work, nephrite is a potent stone for balancing the Divine Masculine and Divine Feminine energies within, bringing internal opposing forces into harmony so you can feel that sense of balance and wholeness in your partnerships as well. It is a wonderful ally for those experiencing grief or loss, granting clarity in the realm of love and helping you see things for what they are. This gem supports emotional balance, encouraging you to feel your emotions without being overwhelmed by them. Work with nephrite to aid self-reflection and channel focus, tranquility, and wisdom as you manifest your desires.

Chakra Work

Nephrite resonates powerfully with the Heart Chakra. Bring it to your heart space in meditation or ritual to release negative feelings and open yourself to greater connection and compassion. Its gentle, nurturing energy helps dissolve emotional blockages and restores the heart to a state of openness and trust.

Partners Well With

Pair nephrite with Black Tourmaline for grounded protection, or with Carnelian to ignite creative energy alongside emotional healing. Copper amplifies nephrite's prosperity vibrations, while Garnet adds passion and vitality to its heart-centered work. Jet provides deep purification and grief support, Onyx strengthens resilience and willpower, and Red Jasper brings stabilizing earth energy and endurance.

Healing

Nephrite is traditionally associated with supporting the nervous system, relieving stress, and promoting overall wellness. It is believed to help identify the root causes of physical discomfort and restore the body to a balanced, high-vibrational state.

Lore

In ancient China, nephrite was known as the Yu Stone, or Stone of Heaven, and was often carved into weapons and used in funeral rites to ensure safe passage. In New Zealand, nephrite jade is known as pounamu and is sacred to the Maori people. It was traditionally carved into amulets of protection and fertility, named after ancestors who had passed away and said to be infused with their spirits and wisdom. Carved pounamu weapons were carried by chiefs as symbols of high status, used in ceremonies, and sometimes given as peace agreements between iwi, or tribes. Nephrite was also used to create Czar Alexander III of Russia's sarcophagus.
