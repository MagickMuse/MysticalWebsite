Silver sheen obsidian is a natural volcanic glass with an Amorphous crystal structure, a variety of Black Obsidian distinguished by its radiant silver-colored inclusions. It forms when volcanic lava meets water and cools rapidly. Where Gold Sheen Obsidian embodies solar energy, silver sheen obsidian is a stone of the moon, infused with lunar energies of healing, mystery, emotional depth, and Divine Feminine wisdom. Like the moon itself, it represents intuition, self-reflection, and the exploration of all things unseen. It is also said to balance the left and right sides of the brain.

Magickal Properties

Silver sheen obsidian brings calm, deepens spiritual awareness, and strengthens willpower. It helps release fear and the need for control, guiding you to move forward from the past with acceptance. For those dealing with conflict or darkness, it offers a glimmer of hope and leads toward the light with gentle healing and spiritual wisdom. It deflects negativity and promotes mental clarity, clearing mental clutter and opening channels between you, the earth, and the higher realms. For advanced practitioners, silver sheen obsidian supports astral travel, inner journeying, and subconscious healing. It brings innermost thoughts and feelings to the surface, helping release limiting beliefs carried from this life or past lives. It encourages union between the physical and astral bodies and aids in returning to the body after spiritual travels. It can be used in scrying and other forms of divination to reveal wisdom about past, present, and future. It fosters deep self-reflection, helping you see your true self with openness and acceptance, gaining clarity on your place in this world.

Chakra Work

Silver sheen obsidian heals the Root, Throat, and Third Eye Chakras, creating a fusion of grounding, truth, and psychic ability. Hold it to the Third Eye to propel subconscious exploration, awaken psychic vision, and open to messages from outside realms. Hover it over the Throat to clear energetic blockages that prevent speaking your truth with clarity and conviction. As a Root Chakra stone, it provides grounding and stability, centering scattered energies. It is a particularly powerful companion for lunar work. Incorporate it into rituals, seeking its support as you set intentions under the new moon or perform releasing work under the full moon. Keep it in your space to surround your home in gently protective vibrations.

Correspondences

Silver sheen obsidian partners well with Black Obsidian, Gold Sheen Obsidian, Labradorite, and Rainbow Moonstone. It is associated with Scorpio and Sagittarius and has an Amorphous crystal structure.

Colors

Silver sheen obsidian has a deep black base with a luminous silvery metallic sheen that catches the light across its surface. The silver reflections give it a mirror-like quality, particularly when polished. It can appear almost liquid in its reflectiveness, embodying the moonlit quality that defines its energy.

Magickal Message

"Let the moon shine a light on the deepest parts of your soul. Watch your inner truth be illuminated, glowing for all to see. You are radiant, imbued with the brilliance of the stars."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 5 to 6, silver sheen obsidian is relatively soft and should be stored separately from harder stones. Cleanse with moonlight, sound, or smoke. As a lunar stone, charging under the full moon is ideal. Avoid prolonged sun exposure and handle with care to prevent scratching its polished surface.
