Girasol is an opalized form of rose quartz with a hexagonal and trigonal crystal structure, registering 7 on the Mohs scale. Its distinctive adularescent sheen develops when microscopic droplets of silica-rich water become trapped during rose quartz formation. The name comes from the Italian "girasole," meaning "to turn toward the sun," which is also the Italian word for sunflower, reflecting how the stone's gentle iridescence always seems to follow the light.

Appearance

Opaque to translucent stone with blue glow.

Origin

Peru, Brazil

Attributes

Girasol (also known as Blue Opal) has an extremely soft and relaxing energy that is emotionally soothing. It enhances communication, especially of feelings that have previously been suppressed and clears emotional baggage from the past. Placing it over the higher heart chakra heals the etheric blueprint* around the heart, restoring it to perfection and bringing about emotional healing at a very deep level. It is a useful stone for improving communications within relationships and for those who fear intimacy or who have been emotionally or physically hurt, as it gently dissolves barriers around the heart. Girasol is beneficial for stabilizing mood swings and overcoming fatigue.

*The etheric blueprint is the subtle gridwork underlying the physical body.

Healing

Girasol may assist with diabetes, hypoglycemia and fluctuations in blood-sugar levels. It is reported to support the endocrine system and to be helpful for disorders involving body fluids.

Position

Wear continuously over the higher heart chakra, on the thymus, or place as appropriate.

Magickal Properties

Girasol is one of the most effective stones for opening honest, heartfelt communication in relationships. It brings peace, deep tranquility, and calm understanding to interpersonal dynamics. Whether in romantic partnerships, family bonds, or professional settings, girasol deepens your connections with the people who matter most.

For more experienced practitioners, girasol encourages you to pause and consider your options before reacting impulsively, helping you avoid situations that lead to regret. It strengthens your sense of autonomy and personal sovereignty over your own path. This stone inspires new creative ventures and the courage to pursue magickal adventures independently. Girasol also works to ease anxiety by calming the heart rate and quieting the rush of adrenaline, gently settling any emotions that feel overwhelming.

Chakra Work

Girasol resonates primarily with the Heart and Throat Chakras. Held against the heart space, it generates a gentle wave of hope and optimism, creating emotional distance from past hurts that continue to affect the present. Placed on the Throat Chakra, girasol supports healthy self-expression, inspiring more thoughtful and considerate speech that takes into account its effect on others. When gridded on or around the body, girasol opens channels of communication with the Divine and expands the aura, infusing it with clarity, calm, love, and compassion.

Partners Well With

Amber, Amethyst, Bloodstone, Citrine, Diopside, Emerald, Galena, Lepidolite, Petalite, Serpentine, Shattuckite

Lore

Girasol is found exclusively in Madagascar, sourced from the same mines that produce rose quartz. It is distinguished from its rose quartz cousins by a noticeably higher water content and a tendency to form in star-shaped crystal formations.
