Mineral Grimoire

The Science of Crystal Healing

Crystals have existed since the planet's formation. The oldest known crystal, a fragment of zircon discovered in what is now Australia, dates back 4.4 billion years. Since then, minerals have formed on every continent and are now more accessible than ever through global trade and commerce.

What Is Lithotherapy?

Lithotherapy draws its name from the Greek words lithos (stone) and therapeia (healing). It is a holistic practice centered on using minerals to support, balance, and realign the human energetic system. Each crystal holds a distinct chemical and molecular structure that shapes its unique properties. Stones can be used on their own or combined, and certain pairings intensify results, much like complementary ingredients that deepen a dish.

Crystal Structure and the Human Body

Crystals are solid matter built from molecules and atoms locked into precise, repeating patterns. Unlike the human body, which is made up of diverse organs and systems in a constant state of change, crystals are structurally fixed and energetically stable. This stability is what makes them effective partners in physical and emotional healing. Where the body seeks balance, crystals offer a consistent reference point.

Intuition plays a central role in selecting the right crystal. A strong draw to a particular stone often signals that some aspect of your system is seeking balance.

Dominant Oscillatory Rate (DOR)

Every crystal emits a specific frequency called its dominant oscillatory rate, or DOR. The human body, with its many organs and systems, holds multiple shifting DORs that change based on health and environmental factors. When the body falls out of balance, it undergoes a process called entropy, a degradation of its natural coherence.

Crystals counter entropy through a process called entrainment: the crystal's fixed DOR gradually draws the human energetic field into alignment with it, easing systemic degradation and reinforcing physical, mental, and emotional health. The end state is coherence, where the brain, heart, and internal organs settle into a shared vibrational frequency.

The number of facets a crystal has influences its DOR. More facets generally mean a higher DOR and a faster energetic spin within the human biofield. Higher-DOR crystals can strengthen, motivate, and help address low-vibrational states such as depression, grief, or discouragement. Lower-DOR crystals tend to calm and ground. Researcher Marcel Vogel advocated for carving healing crystals with multiple facets to intensify their effect on the biofield.

When vision, intention, and crystal energy come into full alignment, practitioners refer to reaching the zero point: the state where energy is at its most refined and all transformation becomes possible.

The Mohs Hardness Scale

Each mineral is assigned a hardness rating on the Mohs Scale, developed in 1822 by Friedrich Mohs. The scale runs from 1 to 10, with diamond at the top as the hardest known natural material. Rubies and sapphires follow at 9. A stone's hardness is not directly connected to its DOR, though some high-Mohs stones do carry a higher frequency.

Hardness is a practical consideration when wearing crystals as jewelry or placing them in grids. Softer stones are more prone to scratching and chipping under daily wear or contact with harder materials.

Crystals and the Human Biofield

The human energetic field is electromagnetic by nature. Quartz, in particular, is a piezoelectric material, meaning that under pressure its atoms generate an electrical charge. This makes quartz especially responsive to the human biofield, which extends roughly two feet in every direction from the physical body.

What enters this field has a measurable effect on health, emotion, and wellbeing. Crystals placed within the biofield, even without direct physical contact, interact with its electromagnetic properties and begin their work. The effects are present whether or not they are immediately perceived.
