Appearance

Has a five-pointed star-shaped formation in its depths. This rare stone can be various colors.

Origin

Myanmar, Czech Republic, Brazil, Kenya, India, Australia, Sri Lanka

Attributes

Star Sapphire draws you into its depths and opens intuition. It brings about centering of your thoughts and aids in anticipating the intentions of other people.

It is said to contact extraterrestrial beings.

Additional Notes

In addition to these specific properties, Star Sapphire carries all the generic attributes of Sapphire.

Spellworking

Star sapphires bring hope and clarity of purpose. The five-pointed star formation draws you into its depths and opens intuition. Use it for centering thoughts, anticipating others' intentions, and spiritual contact. Said to facilitate communication with extraterrestrial beings.

Folklore

When viewed at the right angle, a star appears trapped within the stone. Its rays were said to embody the three qualities of faith, hope, and destiny, held fast within the matrix of the gem. The star sapphire was thought to have been sent to watch over the actions of its owner and to grant good fortune to all their endeavors.
