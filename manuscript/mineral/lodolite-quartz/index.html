Appearance

Clear Quartz with inclusions of Chlorite, Feldspar, and other minerals creating landscape-like scenes.

Origin

Brazil, Madagascar, United States.

Attributes

Lodolite is a type of included Quartz crystal with Chlorite, Feldspar, and other mineral inclusions. It is also known as Lodelite, and Inclusion Quartz. The inclusions appear to be floating within the crystal like an underwater scene, landscape, or garden scene. Lodolite is said to be one of the most powerful stones on Earth for spiritual evolution.

This is an extremely powerful shamanic stone that takes you on inner journeys to retrieve wisdom for yourself and others, and to bring back healing energy. It connects to your wise eternal self. Lodolite facilitates moving between worlds and assists in soul retrieval work. Working with Lodolite changes the way you view your spiritual purpose and mission in life. A useful stone for meditation, it assists in sustaining and lengthening alpha states.

Lodolite supports during past life recall and facilitates cellular memory healing. It brings suppressed feelings gently to the surface for transmutation. The stone assists with earth healing and supports environmental protection work.

Chakra Work

Lodolite activates the Root and Soul Star chakras, creating an open channel from Earth to Spirit. This dual activation grounds the practitioner while simultaneously connecting them to higher dimensional frequencies. Holding or gazing into the stone intensifies shamanic journey states and produces heightened consciousness during meditation, bridging the physical and spiritual planes through the body's energy system.

Healing

Lodolite supports cellular memory healing and assists in bringing suppressed emotions to the surface for healing. It works to clear blockages in the energy system. Its high-frequency white light radiates through the physical and auric bodies, counteracting harmful or toxic energies and clearing the way for renewed vibrancy. For beginners, it attracts angelic energies and promotes forgiveness, peace, recovery, relaxation, and tranquility in the physical body.

Spellworking

Lodolite excels in any working that involves journeying, past life exploration, or interdimensional communication. It stimulates contact with spirit guides and guardians from higher realms and enhances dream vividness, even assisting with lucid dreaming. Use it as a focal point during shamanic sessions to deepen trance states and retrieve wisdom. It boosts self-belief and paves the way for manifestation of goals. Lodolite pairs especially well with Amphibole Quartz, Black Obsidian, Labradorite, Lapis Lazuli, Moldavite, and Rose Quartz.

Position

Hold during meditation or shamanic journeying. Place on the body during healing work. Disperse gem essence around the aura and environment for ongoing support.
