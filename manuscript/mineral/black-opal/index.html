Appearance

Dark body color with brilliant play of fire colors. Often polished.

Origin

Australia, Ethiopia, Mexico, Brazil

Attributes

Black Opal is the most valuable and powerful of the opals. Its dark body color makes the fire colors appear more vivid and intense. It carries all the properties of opal amplified, with added grounding and protection from its dark coloring.

Black Opal is highly protective and promotes a strong connection to the earth while enhancing psychic abilities and mystical visions.

Healing

Black Opal treats disorders of the eyes and aids in cellular regeneration. It purifies the blood and kidneys.

Position

Place as appropriate, especially on the heart and solar plexus.

Spellworking

Black Opal combines opal's psychic and visionary properties with the protection and grounding of black stones. Use it for invisibility magic, deep psychic work, and karmic understanding. In shamanic magick, it keeps potential enemies from seeing you while you access mystical visions.

Power Stone

Black opals are prized by magicians and Wiccans as power stones. They are often worn in ritual jewelry designed to increase the amount of power aroused and released from the body during magic.
