Appearance

Purple with blue undertones, similar to Mohave Turquoise in construction.

Origin

Laboratory made dyed American Turquoise.

Attributes

Navaho Purple Turquoise is made in a similar way to Mohave Turquoise and both are very similar. The underlying stone is a powerful blue enhanced with red dye and Navaho Purple Turquoise is reported to help you stay in a place of loving connection with others. This stone promotes unity consciousness and helps maintain heart-centered connections in relationships.

Healing

Navaho Purple Turquoise retains healing properties similar to natural Turquoise, though these may be somewhat altered by the enhancement process.

Position

Best worn as jewelry due to fragile nature, similar to other enhanced turquoise stones.

Spellworking

Navaho Purple Turquoise retains turquoise's protective properties while the purple-red enhancement adds heart-centered energy. Use it for maintaining loving connections with others and promoting unity consciousness. Best worn as jewelry set in silver.
