Appearance

Clear Quartz bonded with Platinum and Gold, creating a pink iridescent coating. Rose pink with iridescent sheen.

Origin

Artificially created (natural Quartz base from worldwide sources).

Attributes

Rose Aura Quartz is a powerful stone for heart healing and unconditional love.

Spiritually, this stone activates the heart and higher heart chakras, facilitating deep emotional healing and opening to divine love.

Psychologically, Rose Aura Quartz releases emotional wounds and trauma stored in the heart.

Mentally, it brings peaceful mental clarity and emotional intelligence.

Emotionally, this crystal is profoundly healing for heartbreak, grief, and emotional pain. It teaches self-love and acceptance.

Physically, Rose Aura Quartz supports the heart and emotional body.

Healing

Beneficial for the heart, emotional healing, self-love, and releasing grief.

Position

Place on the heart or higher heart chakra; hold during emotional healing work; wear for heart protection.

Additional Notes

Created through alchemical process of bonding natural Quartz with vaporized Platinum and Gold.
