Ultunda Stone

◇Notes/Warnings
⚠️ Belongs to a closed Aboriginal Australian tradition, sacred to those peoples and meant only for practitioners within that culture. A legendary stone rather than a true mineral, so it carries no physical care or safety considerations.

Overview

The Ultunda stone is a stone of legend rather than a true mineral. The name was given to the magickal stones said to be set within the bodies of Australian Aboriginal medicine men, where they were held to be the very source of a practitioner's magickal power.

⚠️ This belongs to a closed Aboriginal Australian tradition. It is sacred to those peoples and is meant only for those within the originating culture, and its secret aspects should never be reproduced.

Lore

A medicine man was not thought to be born holding an Ultunda stone. The stone could instead pass from one person to another, but only once the recipient had been properly initiated. The act of embedding was said to be painful, with the stone pressed into the skin or driven into the skull. Whether these stones truly exist is a mystery the medicine men guard closely. As an emblem, the Ultunda stone stands for occult knowledge and hidden information.
