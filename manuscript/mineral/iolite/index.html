Iolite is an orthorhombic silicate mineral with a Mohs hardness of 7 to 7.5, also known as cordierite after the French geologist Louis Cordier. Prized by the Vikings as a natural polarization filter, it was used to locate the sun on overcast days during their Atlantic voyages, earning it the name Viking Compass.

Magickal Powers

Handling unwanted situations

Appearance

Iolite displays a striking violet-blue color that shifts between blue, gray, and near-colorless depending on the angle of view, a property known as pleochroism. It is transparent to translucent with a vitreous luster and forms in the orthorhombic crystal system.

Colors

Violet, blue, gray, yellowish gray. The depth of blue varies by specimen and viewing angle due to strong pleochroism.

Origin

Australia, Austria, Brazil, Canada, China, Greenland, India, Madagascar, Kenya, Myanmar, Norway, Russia, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, United States (Alaska, New Hampshire)

Magickal Properties

Iolite is a stone of guidance, inner vision, and wisdom. It serves as a compass for navigating life's most challenging passages, helping its keeper chart a clear course when the way forward feels obscured. It bridges the gap between intuition and the rational mind, bringing understanding of what the heart truly desires alongside a lucid view of what lies ahead.

For deeper work, iolite sharpens the vividness and detail of psychic visions, illuminating knowledge from past lives. It draws the subconscious shadows to the surface, calling forward unhealed wounds and buried fears tied to older incarnations so they can be addressed and released. It also supports the recovery of lost parts of the self, those aspects buried under criticism or fear of being seen, and invites them back into the light.

Chakra Work

Iolite is one of the primary stones for activating the Third Eye Chakra. Placed against the third eye during meditation, it stimulates inner vision and helps integrate higher wisdom received in the meditative state. It heightens the vividness of guided meditations and astral travel.

Although not traditionally a Heart Chakra stone, iolite can also be placed on this center to bring hope during challenging circumstances. It provides the emotional distance and perspective needed to find solutions without becoming overwhelmed.

Healing

Iolite calms an overactive mind, helping its keeper reach deeper meditative states and gain healthy distance from reactive emotions. It supports those working through fear, grief, or emotional stagnation by encouraging openness to what truly needs addressing.

Partners Well With

Amethyst, Azurite, Citrine, Garnet, Lapis Lazuli, Peridot, Purpurite, Sugilite, Tanzanite

Varieties

Bloodshot Iolite is a variety found in Sri Lanka, distinguished by blood-red veins running through the stone caused by hematite and goethite inclusions.
