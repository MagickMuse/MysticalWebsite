Attributes

Siberian Blue Quartz is a powerful stone for communication and self-expression.

Spiritually, this stone opens the throat chakra and facilitates clear spiritual communication. It enhances channeling and clairaudient abilities.

Psychologically, Siberian Blue Quartz encourages speaking your truth and expressing your authentic self.

Mentally, it brings mental clarity and enhances communication skills.

Emotionally, this crystal helps in expressing emotions clearly and releasing emotional blockages held in the throat.

Physically, Siberian Blue Quartz supports the throat and respiratory system.

Healing

Beneficial for the throat, thyroid, vocal cords, and respiratory system.

Position

Place on the throat chakra; wear as a pendant; hold during communication.

Note: Some Siberian Blue Quartz may be heat-treated or artificially colored.
