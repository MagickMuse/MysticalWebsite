Bronze

↗Entities1
Brigid

Bronze is an alloy of copper and tin. The essential energy of bronze is healing. It combines the qualities of both its component metals: tin clarifies your intention, and copper directs that intention where it needs to go. Bronze is a good metal to wear and carry as an amulet for long-term healing of body or spirit, as its vibration is steady and enduring.

Visualization

Visualize a door at the end of a hallway. On a peg at the back hangs a deep green cloak, hand-woven of softest wool. You take the cloak and wrap it around you, feeling the comforting weight upon your shoulders. At the throat is a bronze penannular brooch, its ring and pin delicately carved with spirals. You thread the pin through the woolen fabric to close the cloak securely, and Brigid's healing magic is sealed.
