Attributes

Smoky Citrine combines the grounding properties of Smoky Quartz with the manifesting qualities of Citrine.

Spiritually, this stone grounds spiritual abundance into physical reality. It facilitates manifestation while providing protection.

Psychologically, Smoky Citrine builds confidence and self-worth while releasing negative thought patterns.

Mentally, it brings mental clarity and focus for manifestation work.

Emotionally, this crystal promotes joy and optimism while providing emotional grounding.

Physically, Smoky Citrine supports the solar plexus and base chakras.

Healing

Beneficial for the digestive system, metabolism, and grounding energy into the body.

Position

Place on the solar plexus or base chakra; hold during manifestation work; wear for protection and abundance.

Additional Notes

Warning: Fades in direct sunlight. Keep away from prolonged sun exposure.
