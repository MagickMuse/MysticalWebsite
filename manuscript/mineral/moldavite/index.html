🔮Moldavite Enhancement

Moldavite is not a crystal but a crystallized molten glass formed by the collision between a meteor and Earth more than 15 million years ago. It has an amorphous structure and registers 5.5 to 6 on the Mohs scale.

Appearance

Small, transparent, folded mass, often glassy. Deep green color with a distinctive textured surface.

Origin

The first pieces were found in the Moldau River Valley in the Czech Republic, which is how moldavite got its name. Also found in Austria, Germany, and Poland.

The heat of impact metamorphosed surrounding rocks, creating a strew field by flinging the resulting crystals over a vast area. Moldavite is therefore a fusion of extraterrestrial energies with mother earth. This is a rare stone. It is now found along the banks of the river Moldau and is unlikely to be discovered anywhere else in the world. The crystal will become extinct.
