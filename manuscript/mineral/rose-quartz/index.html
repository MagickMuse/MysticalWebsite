💍
Wearing Rose Quartz: Set in silver rather than gold — silver carries lunar, receptive energy that harmonizes with this stone's heart-opening qualities. Wear on the left hand or wrist to draw love and emotional healing inward. A pendant over the heart chakra works especially well for ongoing grief work or self-love practice.

Magickal Powers

Finding true love

Appearance

Usually translucent pink, ranging from pale to deeper rose. Often tumbled or carved. All sizes.

Origin

Madagascar, Brazil, South Africa, India, United States.

Attributes

Rose Quartz is the stone of unconditional love and infinite peace. It is the most important crystal for the heart and the heart chakra, teaching the true essence of love. It purifies and opens the heart at all levels, and brings deep inner healing and self-love. It is calming, reassuring, and excellent for use in trauma or crisis.

If you want to attract love, look no further than romantic Rose Quartz. Placed by your bed or in the relationship corner of your home, it is so effective in drawing love and relationships toward you that it often needs Amethyst to calm things down. In existing relationships, it will restore trust and harmony, and encourage unconditional love.

Rose Quartz gently draws off negative energy and replaces it with loving vibes. It strengthens empathy and sensitivity and aids the acceptance of necessary change. It is an excellent stone for mid-life crisis. Holding Rose Quartz enhances positive affirmations. The stone can then remind you of your intention. This beautiful stone promotes receptivity to beauty of all kinds.

Emotionally, Rose Quartz is the finest healer. Releasing unexpressed emotions and heartache and transmuting emotional conditioning that no longer serves, it soothes internalized pain and heals deprivation. If you have never received love, Rose Quartz opens your heart so that you become receptive. If you have loved and lost, it comforts your grief.

Rose Quartz teaches you how to love yourself and encourages self-forgiveness and acceptance, invoking self-trust and self-worth. It is excellent for those who have never believed themselves worthy of love.

Chakra Work

Rose Quartz is the premier Heart Chakra stone. Holding it close to the chest opens this energy center, reawakens trust, and dissolves negative energies that have accumulated around the heart space. It activates universal and unconditional love, known as agape in ancient Greek, and raises a vibration of love that reverberates across space and time. For those carrying emotional wounds or trauma, it gently breaks down the barriers built around the heart so the practitioner can become open to receiving the same frequency of love they emit.

Healing

Rose Quartz strengthens the physical heart and circulatory system and releases impurities from body fluids. Placed on the thymus, Rose Quartz aids chest and lung problems. It heals the kidneys and adrenals and alleviates vertigo. Rose Quartz is said to increase fertility. The stone or elixir soothes burns and blistering and smooths the complexion. It is helpful in Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and senile dementia.

Position

Wear, especially over the heart. Place on the heart, thymus, or in relationship corner of room.

Sleep

Rose quartz and its close ally Morganite are beautiful stones to keep near the bed or tucked under a pillow. Their gentle love frequency supports not only couples and romantic partners but also those who are single and seeking to reconnect with self-love and a sense of sacred sovereignty. Sleeping with rose quartz nearby wraps the keeper in a nurturing vibration that soothes the heart, eases emotional tension carried from the day, and invites restful, love-filled dreams.

Spellworking

Pink gems like Rose Quartz are perfect for love spells. This is the quintessential stone for attracting romance and deepening emotional bonds. It promotes self-love, heals heartache, and opens you to giving and receiving affection. Use Rose Quartz in any spell involving compassion, forgiveness, or emotional healing. As a translucent pink stone, it excels in emotional situations and matters of the heart. It pairs especially well with Emerald, Garnet, Kunzite, Morganite, Pink Calcite, Pink Tourmaline, Rhodochrosite, Rhodonite, and Ruby.

Lore

Rose Quartz has served as a love token since at least 600 B.C.E. In ancient Greece and Rome, the stone was considered a gift from Eros and his Roman counterpart Cupid, tying it directly to the mythology of romantic love. Romans believed it could prevent early aging and forge deep soul connections. Egyptian tradition holds that Cleopatra incorporated pieces of Rose Quartz into her milk-based facial and bathing elixirs for their beautifying properties. Throughout history, expectant mothers have worn the stone as a talisman to protect the developing child and strengthen the bond of love before birth.

Magickal Message

"I am the stone of universal love energy. If your heart space needs healing in order to give and receive love, I am your ally. Meditate with me to lower your walls, initiate self-love, and love others more deeply."
