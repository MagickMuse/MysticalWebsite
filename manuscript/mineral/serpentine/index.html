Appearance

Mottled, dual appearance., can be water-worn and often polished. All sizes.

Origin

Britain (Cornwall), Norway, Russia, Zimbabwe, Italy, United States.

Attributes

Serpentine is an earthing stone that aids meditation and spiritual exploration. Clearing the chakras and stimulating the crown chakra, it opens psychic abilities and helps you to understand the spiritual basis of life.

This stone opens new pathways for the kundalini energy to rise. It assists the retrieval of wisdom, and regains memory of past lives.

Psychologically, Serpentine helps you to feel more in control of your life. It corrects mental and emotional imbalances and assists the conscious direction of healing energy toward problem areas.

Physically, Serpentine is extremely cleansing and detoxifying for the body and blood. It is said to ensure longevity.

Healing

Serpentine eliminates parasites, aids calcium and magnesium absorption, and treats hypoglycemia and diabetes.

Position

Hold or place on appropriate spot.

Folklore

Serpentine is a clear instance of the Doctrine of Signatures, the idea that a thing's appearance hints at its use and meaning. It not only resembles the snakeskin that gives it its name, but was believed to cure snakebites should its use as a protective talisman fail to keep the creatures away in the first place. Because the snake is so closely tied to the Earth, serpentine was also held to carry earthing qualities. As a remedy for rheumatism, sufferers were told to hold pieces of the stone in their hands while sitting in the light of the Sun.

Spellworking

Energy and Protection

Serpentine is an earthing stone that aids meditation and spiritual exploration. Named for its resemblance to a serpent's skin, it has long been used to guard against venomous creatures—snakes, spiders, and bites of all kinds. Carry or wear serpentine when walking in wild places.

Money and Luck

Serpentine attracts money and good fortune. Place in a cash box or with financial documents to increase prosperity. Its green color aligns it with wealth-drawing energies.

Kundalini and Spiritual Work

This stone opens new pathways for kundalini energy to rise safely. Use in meditation to activate the serpent energy coiled at the base of the spine. Its connection to snake symbolism makes it powerful for transformation and shedding old patterns—just as a snake sheds its skin.

Past Life Work

Serpentine assists in retrieving wisdom from past lives. Hold during regression work or place on the third eye to access memories of previous incarnations. It helps you understand which lessons you are still working through.

Mental Clarity

Serpentine corrects mental and emotional imbalances. It helps direct healing energy consciously toward problem areas. Use it when you need to take control of scattered thoughts or conflicting emotions.

Longevity and Detoxification

Physically, serpentine is extremely cleansing and detoxifying. It was traditionally believed to ensure longevity. Use in healing rituals focused on purification of body and blood.
