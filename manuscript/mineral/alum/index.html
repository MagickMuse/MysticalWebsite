Appearance

Transparent to translucent crystalline stone

Origin

Italy, United States, Spain, France, Germany, China, Russia, Turkey, Greece, Chile, Australia, Morocco

Attributes

Alum is a powerful purifying and protective stone. It has strong astringent properties that draw out negativity and create a shield against psychic attack. This stone assists in clarifying thoughts and perceptions, allowing you to see situations with greater objectivity.

Psychologically, Alum helps to establish boundaries and maintain energetic integrity. It is useful for anyone who feels vulnerable to the influence of others or who absorbs negative energy from their environment.

Spiritually, Alum connects to the crown chakra and facilitates clear communication with higher realms. It assists in distinguishing between authentic spiritual guidance and illusory messages.

Healing

Alum may support the immune system and assist with infections and inflammation. It has traditionally been used for skin conditions and wounds.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate. Can be dissolved in water for external use only.

Spellworking

Alum has been worn in Egypt as a protective amulet against evil, and on the north coast of Africa it serves the same purpose. A piece of alum placed in the home guards it against negativity. Small amounts of this mineral can be sewn into or placed in children's hats to protect them.
