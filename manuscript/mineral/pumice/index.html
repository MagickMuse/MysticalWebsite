🪨Pumice Stone End Bitching

Appearance

Light gray to white, porous, rough-textured volcanic stone. Floats on water.

Origin

Iceland, Italy, Indonesia, Philippines, Japan, Chile, Mexico, United States, Greece.

Attributes

Pumice is a volcanic product with unique properties. Light and rough to the touch, it possesses the unique property of floating on water due to its porous nature filled with air pockets. This stone represents transformation, as it is created through the intense heat of volcanic eruption.

Pumice is associated with cleansing and purification on both physical and energetic levels. Its porous nature makes it excellent for absorbing negativity and unwanted energies.

Healing

Pumice has been used traditionally to ease childbirth and smooth rough skin. Its porous nature is symbolic of its ability to draw out toxins and impurities.

Position

Hold in hand during rituals, place on altar, or use in water-based banishing spells.

Spellworking

Energy: Projective

Powers: Easing childbirth, Banishment, Protection

Pumice, a volcanic product, is a curious substance. Light and rough to the touch (one soap boasts it contains pumice to help in cleaning dirty hands), it also possesses the unique property of floating on water.

Easing Childbirth

At one time, pumice was pressed into the hands of women during childbirth, or worn, to ease the passage of the new life into the outside world.

Banishing Spell

Take a piece of pumice and hold it in your projective hand. Visualize the problem you wish to be rid of—a damaging habit, negative emotion, physical ailment, or unrequited love.

While holding the stone, through your visualization send the energy that is behind the problem into the pumice. You might imagine it as streams of thick, black smoke, the consistency of molasses, flowing into the light, porous stone.

Then, throw the pumice into a lake, stream, the ocean, or any body of water.

As it hits the water, it releases the problem and its root causes into that element. Floating on the surface, the pumice strengthens your ability to "rise above" any and all negative conditions.

If you do not have access to bodies of water, fill a large basin or bucket with water and perform the ritual; then pour the water, stone and all, onto bare earth.

Protection

Pumice can also be placed on the altar during protective magic or in the home as an amuletic sponge. Empower it with the property of absorbing negativity.
