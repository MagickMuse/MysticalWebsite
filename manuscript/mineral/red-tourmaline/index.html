Red Tourmaline's Journey to You

Rubellite is a hexagonal crystal and a red variety of tourmaline, deriving its name from the Latin rubellus, meaning "reddish." Its rich color comes from the presence of manganese, and it has historically been mistaken for ruby. Rubellite simultaneously activates the Root and Heart Chakras and corresponds with the energies of love, boundaries, and emotional healing.

Lore

There was a time when all red gemstones were simply called "ruby," and rubellite was no exception. In 1777, King Gustav III of Sweden gifted Catherine the Great the famous Caesar's Ruby as a peace offering, a magnificent stone weighing 250 carats. It was not until 1922, when mineralogist Aleksandr Evgenevich Fersman examined the Russian crown jewels, that the stone was revealed to be not a ruby at all but a red rubellite tourmaline. Unlike many gemstones that shift color under different light sources, a true rubellite shines just as brightly under artificial indoor light as it does in natural sunlight, a quality that sets it apart from other red tourmalines.
