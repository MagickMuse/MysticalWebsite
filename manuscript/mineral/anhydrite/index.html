Appearance

White to colorless, blue-gray, or violet, transparent to translucent, vitreous to pearly luster, orthorhombic crystal system, often massive or granular

Origin

Italy, Germany, United States, Peru, Poland, France

Attributes

Anhydrite is the water-free form of gypsum. This stone facilitates acceptance and forgiveness, especially toward yourself. It encourages you to surrender to higher guidance.

Psychologically, Anhydrite alleviates hardness and encourages tolerance. It helps you to be more diplomatic in your approach and to speak kindly. This stone is useful for releasing suppressed anger in a constructive way.

Mentally, Anhydrite aids clear thinking and helps you to be more receptive to new ideas and different approaches. It encourages flexibility in attitude and helps you to let go of old grudges.

Spiritually, this stone facilitates angelic contact and communication with the spiritual realm. It can be used in spiritual healing and to access the Akashic Record. Anhydrite encourages you to live according to spiritual principles.

Healing

Anhydrite heals the throat and is helpful for the thyroid and parathyroid. It disperses excess mucus and can help with water retention. This stone is helpful in treating edema and conditions where fluid balance is disturbed.

Position

Place as appropriate or wear for extended periods. Keep away from water as it will transform back to gypsum.
