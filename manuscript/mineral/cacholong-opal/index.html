Cacholong Opal is a mix of white opal and fine-grained chalcedony with an amorphous crystal structure, registering 2.5 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale. The name "cacholong" comes from the word "cach," referring to a creek in Uzbekistan where the stone was originally found. Some claim it is simply a word coined to differentiate this stone from regular white opal. Cacholong opal has been used throughout history in fine jewelry and lapidary art, given as tokens of love, reflected in its modern metaphysical properties of heart opening and affection.

Appearance

Brilliant white, porcelain-like opal with a smooth, opaque surface. Its porous nature allows it to absorb essential oils and perfumes.

Origin

Found in Austria, Czech Republic, Russia, Tanzania, and Uzbekistan.

Magickal Properties

Cacholong opal is helpful in healing sessions with clients who find more powerful stones to be a bit too overwhelming. Using it alongside more intense stones mitigates their impact. Because cacholong opal is so calming, it is also beneficial for processing chronic trauma caused by long-term abuse or domestic violence, getting right to the heart of the matter without triggering symptoms of PTSD or anxiety.

For beginners, cacholong opal encourages a sense of optimism about life and helps you feel safe and supported. It is a calming and soothing stone that eases feelings of anger, bitterness, and depression. It instills a sense of faith in your own progress along your life path and encourages you to make decisions that keep you moving forward toward your goals. Thanks to its porous nature, you can place a drop of your favorite essential oil on this stone and carry it with you or place it anywhere you wish to enjoy the scent.

For advanced practitioners, cacholong opal is a powerful ally for deep emotional healing work. It has also been considered a love talisman, with energies that reunite lost lovers even after decades of separation.

Chakra Work

Cacholong opal is primarily a Crown Chakra stone. Placed on this chakra, it helps purify the entire energetic system by saturating the auric field with the full spectrum of light and color. This process increases your spiritual awareness and helps you receive information from higher realms. Placed on the Heart Chakra, it helps release pent-up emotions, encourages practicing self-care, and opens you to love from others and yourself. Carried or worn as jewelry against the heart, it also helps you feel welcome in groups and communities. Wearing cacholong opal is said to relieve the discomfort associated with late pregnancy and bring support during labor and birthing.

Position

Lore

Due to its brilliant white appearance, cacholong opal is increasingly being used as a more sustainable and ethical replacement for ivory.
