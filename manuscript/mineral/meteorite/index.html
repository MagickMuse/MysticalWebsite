Dark Magick Applications

Various space stones can serve a range of aggressive magickal purposes. Their energy tends to be highly magnetic, and they are frequently used to accelerate the speed and amplify the intensity of spellwork. Pallasite or iron meteorites in particular make effective boosters for war or defensive workings. When combined with other charged minerals, meteorites can push the effects of a spell well beyond their normal limits.
