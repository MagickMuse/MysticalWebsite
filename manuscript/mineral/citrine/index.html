Magickal Powers

Cleans and aligns the auras; prosperity

Spellworking

Citrine, a form of quartz, is worn at night to remove fear, to prevent nightmares, and to ensure a good night's sleep. It is also worn to facilitate psychic awareness.

A yellow variety of quartz, citrine is a great stone for banishing nightmares and improving psychic abilities. Because it's also known for its cleansing properties, you can use it to clean your other stones and magick tools, or to clear the air after an argument or disturbance.

Protection

Citrine's solar energy creates a protective shield that deflects negative influences and environmental stress. Its bright, sunny vibration naturally repels dark energies and psychic attack. Place citrine in your workspace or near entryways to ward off negativity. Carry it when entering challenging situations; it acts as an energetic bodyguard while simultaneously boosting your confidence and personal power. Unlike stones that absorb negativity, citrine transmutes it, making it self-cleansing and ideal for continuous protection work.

Magickal Properties

Citrine is a member of the quartz family, ranging in color from pale lemon yellow to deep amber, with color variation caused by the presence of aluminum and the degree of natural or artificial heat exposure. Its name derives from the Latin citrus, meaning the citron tree with its lemon-like fruit. Ancient Greeks worked citrine into cabochon rings as far back as 300 BCE, carrying it as a talisman against the malicious intentions of others and to attract financial prosperity.

The darker, orange-toned citrine widely available in shops is nearly always heat-treated amethyst; it carries the same metaphysical properties as its natural counterpart. Citrine that has naturally darkened has been irradiated within the earth, a process that occurs in geothermally warmer regions. Congolese citrine, for instance, naturally deepens in color due to higher subterranean temperatures compared to Brazilian deposits.

Citrine does not hold or accumulate negative energy. It transmutes whatever it encounters, making it one of the few stones that never requires energetic cleansing. This property also makes it an ideal centerpiece for crystal grids aimed at financial prosperity, as it continues to radiate clear, amplifying energy without becoming saturated.

For the beginner, citrine functions as a stone of activation and forward momentum. It increases the capacity to draw desires into physical reality, supports persistence through obstacles, and builds a genuine felt sense of worthiness around receiving. For the advanced practitioner, the work deepens: citrine assists in identifying and dismantling Solar Plexus blocks rooted in past experiences of powerlessness or abuse. It supports the healing of trauma stored in this center, restoring the practitioner's ability to hold clear boundaries and channel high-frequency intention into tangible outcomes on the physical plane.

Chakra Work

Citrine works primarily with the Solar Plexus Chakra, where it rebuilds self-esteem and confidence from the ground up. Placed on this center, it illuminates the unique strengths and attributes of the individual, cutting through self-doubt and comparison. It activates mental clarity, creative drive, motivation, and self-expression simultaneously, making it one of the most comprehensive Solar Plexus tools available. The confidence citrine cultivates is paired with assertiveness: it encourages the practitioner to move outward, engage more fully with the world, and hold firm emotional boundaries without apology.

Prosperous Manifestation

Citrine brings a crucial dimension to manifestation work: it ensures that what you create is prosperous and sustaining. This is especially important in spiritual pursuits, where the concept of sacred exchange can become complicated. Your dreams and goals should nourish you energetically and financially, and citrine holds that balance with ease. Place it at the center of a manifestation grid to anchor abundance into every intention, carry it during the day to magnetize opportunities, or meditate with it to align your Solar Plexus with the frequency of prosperity.

Did You Know?

Citrine's reputation as a prosperity attractor earned it the folk name "merchant's stone." Historically, shopkeepers would place a piece in their cash registers to encourage the steady flow of money through the business.

Stone of Success

The yellow of citrine ties it symbolically to the Sun, to warmth, and to vitality. That same yellow band of the spectrum is linked to the stomach in the system of energy centers known as chakras, and so citrine has been used to ease ailments of that part of the body.

In Chinese tradition citrine is said to bring wealth, and it is held to carry its power only when given by a generous person. Owning it is believed to draw abundance, prosperity, success, and wisdom, which earned it the name stone of success. Emperors wore it in the belief that it sharpened both intellect and wisdom. Through its link to wisdom and learning it became a lucky charm for students sitting examinations and for teachers and professors alike.
