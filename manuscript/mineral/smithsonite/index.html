Appearance

Pearly, lustrous., like layers of silky bubbles, all sizes.

Origin

United States, Australia, Greece, Italy, Mexico, Namibia.

Attributes

Smithsonite is a stone of tranquility, charm, kindness, and favorable outcomes. It has an extremely gentle presence and forms a buffer against life's problems. It is the perfect stone for releasing stress that is almost at breaking point and for alleviating mental breakdown.

This stone is ideal for anyone who has had a difficult childhood and who felt unloved or unwanted. Smithsonite heals the inner child and alleviates the effects of emotional abuse and misuse. It is gentle, dissolving emotional hurt subtly.

Its effects are noticed in feeling better rather than in traumatic emotional release. This is an excellent crystal for birth and rebirth and can treat infertility.

Smithsonite aligns the chakras and strengthens psychic abilities. Placed on the crown chakra, it connects to the angelic realm.

Psychologically, Smithsonite supports leadership qualities, especially where tact is required. Emotionally, Smithsonite aids difficult relationships.

Healing

Smithsonite heals a dysfunctional immune system, sinus and digestive disorders, osteoporosis, and alcoholism. It restores elasticity to veins and muscles.

Position

Place as appropriate or carry always. Place at crown to align chakras. Grid around the bed or body.

Easily obtained. Specific colors have additional properties. For example, the green variety opens the Heart Chakra, activating energies of love and compassion.

Chakra Work

Smithsonite corresponds with all chakras and can be placed on any part of the body in need of healing. During meditation, tune in to your energy and locate where you are holding stress and tension, then hold Smithsonite over those areas, allowing it to clear away negative or stagnant energies. Placed on the Crown Chakra, it connects to the angelic realm and opens higher-dimensional awareness. It resonates with the water element and brings a sense of overall energetic alignment and harmony when used across the full chakra system. Because it activates psychic abilities, it is also a potent gem to keep close while working with tarot cards, pendulums, or any other form of divination.

Spellworking

Smithsonite is a stone of relaxation, detoxification, and transformation. Keep it on your bedside table, in your meditation room, or anywhere you wish to invoke more calm, clarity, and peace. For beginners, it soothes tension, encourages harmony in relationships, and helps you gain perspective during challenges, moving through life phases with grace and ease. It lifts your vibration, restores physical energy, and helps you embody high frequencies of joy, peace, generosity, and connection. It also helps you embody angelic energy and share that magick with those around you.

For advanced practitioners, Smithsonite stimulates psychic abilities and opens you to wisdom from alternate realms. It grants access to higher dimensions, connects you with spirit guides, and helps channel their wisdom into your magickal work. It brings feelings of cosmic connection and oneness, deepening awareness of source consciousness, and embodies universal love. It partners well with Amethyst for spiritual expansion and calm, Aquamarine for emotional clarity and flow, Chrysoprase for heart healing and joy, Iolite for intuitive vision, Kunzite for divine love and emotional release, and Lepidolite for stress relief and emotional balance.

Lore

Smithsonite was named in 1832 after James Smithson, the English chemist and mineralogist who first identified it in 1802. Smithson is perhaps better known as the founding benefactor of the Smithsonian Institution in Washington, D.C. The stone is a member of the calcite group and is typically found in botryoidal, or grapelike, clusters. Also known as zinc spar, it resonates with the water element and comes in a wide variety of colors, each carrying its own unique magickal properties.
