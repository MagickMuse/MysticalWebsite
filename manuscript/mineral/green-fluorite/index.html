About Green Fluorite

Green fluorite belongs to the halide mineral family and shares its isometric crystal structure with its fluorite kin. Its coloration ranges from pale mint to deep forest green, depending on trace elements present during formation. Deposits are found across a wide range of regions including the United States, Australia, China, Mexico, Mongolia, Namibia, Russia, South Africa, and Tanzania.

Crystal Healing with Green Fluorite

Green fluorite bridges the Heart and Third Eye Chakras, bringing the heart and mind into alignment. It is a stone of deep emotional restoration, drawing out the roots of trauma held in the emotional body and replacing stagnant or painful energy with a sense of bliss and openness. Held against the Heart Chakra, it encourages feelings of warmth and receptivity. Placed on the Third Eye during meditation, it deepens connection to guides and supports a more heart-centered form of spiritual awareness. Green fluorite is also a useful companion when investigating the root causes of recurring physical illness, particularly those with an emotional or energetic origin.
