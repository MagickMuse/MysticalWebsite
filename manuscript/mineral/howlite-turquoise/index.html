Appearance

Opaque, porous stone with blue colour.

Origin

United States (Howlite base).

Attributes

Howlite Turquoise combines the properties of Howlite with the qualities of blue Turquoise. The blue colour encourages emotional expression and dissolves martyrdom and victim mentality whilst Howlite teaches patience and helps to eliminate rage and uncontrolled anger. Together, they are a useful combination when you need to voice your opinion clearly and calmly or be heard without losing your temper. The stone helps you to be true to yourself rather than follow the crowd. Howlite Turquoise stimulates the desire for knowledge and aids learning and memory. It is perfect for students or anyone acquiring new skills. It also helps to clarify muddled thoughts and processes.

Meditating with this crystal takes you to other dimensions and accesses both your personal and the collective unconscious. It can help you recall past lives, especially those in which you were a teacher or worked with the power of the word. If you are a natural teacher, Howlite Turquoise assists you to pass on your knowledge.

Healing

Howlite Turquoise is reported to assist disorders of the throat, eyes, chest, lungs, heart, immune system, thyroid and parathyroid. It may be beneficial for the brain, nervous system, circulation and detoxification.

Position

Wear over the throat chakra, place as appropriate or meditate with to access higher dimensions.

Spellworking

Howlite Turquoise combines Howlite's calming patience with Turquoise's protection and communication properties. Use it when you need to speak your truth calmly without losing your temper. Also excellent for learning, memory, and accessing past-life teacher knowledge.

Protection

Howlite Turquoise offers a unique form of protection by combining the defensive properties of both stones. The Howlite component absorbs anger, both your own and any directed toward you, making it excellent for keeping in your pocket during tense situations or around difficult people. It acts as a sponge for negativity, preventing it from affecting your emotional state. The Turquoise qualities add an additional layer of protection, particularly for communication: shielding you from saying things you'll regret while ensuring your words carry truth and clarity. This makes it especially protective in confrontational situations where you need to maintain composure and speak from a place of calm power rather than reactive emotion.
