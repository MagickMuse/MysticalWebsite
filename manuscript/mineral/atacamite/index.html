Appearance

Dark emerald green to blackish-green, transparent to translucent, vitreous to adamantine luster, orthorhombic crystal system, often small prismatic crystals

Origin

United States, Australia, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Namibia, Iran

Attributes

Atacamite is a powerful cleanser and purifier, especially of the kidneys and thymus. It acts as a detoxification agent for the whole body. This stone removes fear and promotes independence from outside influences.

Psychologically, Atacamite encourages independence and self-reliance. It helps you to rely on your own judgment rather than being swayed by others. This stone promotes clarity of thought and aids decision-making.

Emotionally, Atacamite is excellent for restoring lost trust, either in yourself or in other people. It helps to heal emotional trauma from the past. The stone teaches that the only security we have comes from within.

Spiritually, Atacamite opens the higher heart chakra and enhances the connection to spiritual realms. It assists in spiritual purification and removes energy blockages.

Healing

Atacamite is a powerful healer for the kidneys and thymus. It restores the thymus to its proper functioning after the spiritual heart center has been activated. This stone aids the nervous system and restores brain chemistry to its proper functioning. Atacamite heals the genitals and reproductive system.

Position

Wear or place as appropriate. Use with caution as it can be toxic - wash hands after handling.

Additional Notes

Despite its forcefulness, Atacamite is a very safe crystal to use to stimulate spiritual vision and aid visualization. It is a stone of great clarity that takes the soul safely to the highest possible levels during meditation. This newly discovered crystal restores lost spiritual trust and promotes connection to higher guidance. Atacamite works willingly to open the higher heart chakra, bringing more unconditional love into your life, and stimulates the thymus gland and immune system functioning. It is beneficial for the nervous system, overcoming stress and frayed nerves at a subtle level.
