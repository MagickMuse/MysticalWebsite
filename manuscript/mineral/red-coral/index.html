Red Coral is an organic material with a Mohs hardness of 3.5, formed by marine colonies that thrive in strong ocean currents at depths of 700 to 1,500 meters.

Appearance

Red Coral displays rich, deep red tones with a smooth, waxy surface when polished. As an organic material rather than a traditional mineral, it is composed of calcium carbonate secreted by marine organisms over decades of slow growth. Individual colonies can live for up to 100 years, building intricate branching structures beneath the ocean surface.

Colors

Red, deep crimson, dark orange-red

Origin

Adriatic Sea, Atlantic Ocean, Mediterranean Sea

Magickal Properties

Red Coral has been revered since ancient times as a source of healing, protection, and beauty. Ancient Egyptians adorned their dead with red coral jewelry, believing it would ensure safe passage into the afterlife. Celtic warriors decorated breastplates and helmets with it for protection during battle. Today, it remains a potent source of fertility, power, and life-force energy that calms and grounds the spirit.

For beginners, Red Coral stimulates life-force energy, boosts fertility, and strengthens courage. It clears spiritual blockages and encourages the flow of prana throughout the body. It anchors you firmly in the physical plane and surrounds you with a sense of safety and security. It builds confidence, promotes decisive action, increases stamina, and helps overcome procrastination and inertia. It supports the initiation of new ideas and projects, guiding the keeper toward goals aligned with the highest good and attracting good fortune.

Advanced practitioners recognize Red Coral as a link to the earth's deepest magick. This material embodies pure life-force energy and resonates with Mars, the planet of strength, vitality, and sexuality. It calms the emotional body and encourages balanced, healthy assertion of personal power. It also provides support during recovery from past-life trauma, helping release energetic baggage and embrace the future with renewed confidence.

Chakra Work

Red Coral activates the Root Chakra at the base of the tailbone, the center of stability and grounding. Hold it against this energy center to stabilize your field and root yourself in the physical plane. It is especially effective for returning to the body after meditation or spiritual work. Because of its oceanic origins, Red Coral also carries a connection to the Moon and its Divine Feminine wisdom, strengthening and revitalizing the auric field with healing, nurturing, and protective energy.

Care

Red Coral is a soft, porous organic material at Mohs 3.5. Avoid prolonged water exposure, as it can deteriorate over time despite its oceanic origins. Keep away from direct sunlight, which may cause fading. Cleanse gently with a soft cloth or by placing on a selenite plate. Smoke cleansing with sage or palo santo is another safe option. Store separately from harder stones to prevent scratching.
