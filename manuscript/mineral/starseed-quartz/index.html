Attributes

Starseed Quartz is believed to carry encoded information from other star systems and dimensions.

Spiritually, this stone facilitates connection to extraterrestrial consciousness and assists starseeds in remembering their cosmic origins. It activates dormant spiritual knowledge and DNA codes.

Psychologically, Starseed Quartz helps those who feel they don't belong on Earth to understand their purpose and mission.

Mentally, it downloads information and activates higher consciousness.

Emotionally, this crystal provides comfort and connection for those who feel alienated or different.

Physically, Starseed Quartz can activate cellular memory and DNA.

Supports DNA activation and cellular memory; works primarily at spiritual levels.
