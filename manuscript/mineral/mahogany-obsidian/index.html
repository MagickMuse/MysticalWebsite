Mahogany Obsidian is a variety of volcanic glass with an amorphous structure, registering 5 to 5.5 on the Mohs scale. It displays undulating banding or mottled spots of black and deep blood red together, caused by inclusions of oxidized hematite and magnetite. It is named after the mahogany tree due to its coloring.

Appearance

Black volcanic glass with undulating banding or mottled spots of deep blood red. The rich mahogany coloring makes it popular for carvings, where the red-brown hues accentuate the lines and formations of tools and statues.

Origin

Found in Afghanistan, Brazil, Japan, Mexico, New Zealand, and the United States (Arizona, California, Oregon).

Attributes

Mahogany Sheen Obsidian carries a lighter, more refined vibration that gently brings issues to the surface and transmutes them. It offers powerful protection to the aura and the lightbody and helps your soul to shine. Unlike regular obsidian which can be quite intense, Mahogany Sheen works more gently while still providing the grounding and protective qualities obsidian is known for.

Magickal Properties

Mahogany obsidian, like black obsidian, is deeply grounding, protective, and clearing. It also heals tears in the auric field, heals body, mind, and spirit, and can be placed in a room to clear your sacred space.

For beginners, it creates a clear channel of energy for manifestation and gives you space to move and flow through life without obstruction. It reveals limitations you may have placed on yourself out of fear, allowing you to consciously choose to move past them and into a place of abundance. It supports you in times of mourning and in letting go of heartache from the past.

For advanced practitioners, mahogany obsidian forms a shield of protective energy that absorbs negative frequencies and blocks psychic attacks. It is a stone for the warrior, for anyone undergoing challenges or doing serious spiritual work, such as releasing ancestral trauma and patterns of negativity. It will enable you to overcome life's obstacles with strength, courage, and ease, inspiring contemplation about your life path and excitement about all the possibilities that are open to you.

Chakra Work

Placed on the Earth Star or Root Chakra, mahogany obsidian brings powerful grounding, cleansing, and healing, particularly relating to feelings of unworthiness or scarcity. On the Sacral Chakra, it brings relief to old wounds and shame relating to the expression or repression of your sexuality.

Pyrite strengthens willpower and protective energy. Sugilite adds spiritual love and soul-level healing. Zincite ignites creative fire and personal power.

Healing

Mahogany obsidian works on both physical and energetic levels, providing gentle transformation and protection during healing processes. It is particularly helpful for addressing feelings of unworthiness and scarcity.

Position

Place on the Earth Star, Root, or Sacral Chakra. Hold or grid as appropriate for protection. Place in a room to clear sacred space.

Lore

Even in modern times, obsidian is used as material for scalpels in surgery, as it is sharper than steel and causes less tissue trauma while creating incisions. Mahogany obsidian is often used in carvings because its beautiful red-brown hues accentuate the lines and formations of tools and statues.

Magickal Message

You are worthy of all life has to offer you. Work with me to reclaim your personal strength, unlock opportunities, and overcome the challenges life throws at you.

Spellworking

Sacred to Hecate, obsidian family stones are favorites for scrying mirrors and protection. Mahogany obsidian works more gently than black obsidian while still providing powerful auric protection. Use it for gentle transformation and bringing hidden issues to light without overwhelming catharsis. As an opaque stone, it excels at grounding, physical matters, and shadow work.
