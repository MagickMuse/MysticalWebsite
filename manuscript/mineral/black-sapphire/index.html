Appearance

Black colored sapphire crystal, bright and transparent when polished.

Origin

Myanmar, Czech Republic, Brazil, Kenya, India, Australia, Sri Lanka

Attributes

Black Sapphire is protective and centering. It imparts confidence in one's own intuition.

This stone heightens employment prospects and helps in retaining a job.

Additional Notes

In addition to these specific properties, Black Sapphire carries all the generic attributes of Sapphire: known as the wisdom stone, focusing and calming the mind, releasing unwanted thoughts and mental tension, bringing peace of mind and serenity.

Spellworking

Linked with Neptune, sapphire increases spiritual knowledge and connection with the Divine. Black Sapphire adds grounding and protection while boosting intuition. Use it for career spells, job security, and trusting your inner guidance. As a dark stone, it provides centering and confidence.
