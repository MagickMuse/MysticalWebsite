Appearance

Metallic, needle-like fans. and blades that tarnish, often wand-like, silver color.

Origin

China, France, Japan, Mexico, Poland, Portugal, Romania, United States

Attributes

Uniting the base, sacral, soma and solar plexus chakras and forming a perfect holding space, Stibnite creates an energetic shield around the physical body and is a useful stone for journeying as it protects during travel and then brings the soul home to the physical body.

Spiritually, when used as a wand with focused intention, this stone separates out the pure from the dross and is an efficient tool for releasing entity possession or negative energy. Used wisely, it can attract all you desire, but ensure that this is what you really need for your highest good.

Psychologically, this stone shows you the gold in your centre, recognizing your gifts, and assists in finding the value in the most difficult experiences.

Shamanically, Stibnite carries the energy of Wolf, facilitating journeying with this perceptive power animal to explore the north of the medicine wheel.

Emotionally, Stibnite assists in eliminating tentacles from clingy relationships that penetrate the subtle or physical bodies, especially after physical separation. It assists in tie-cutting rituals and past-life releases and is particularly useful in situations where you find it difficult to say no to a former partner – although having cut a tie may stimulate a situation that tests if the cutting is complete and you can indeed stand your ground. If so, hold the crystal and focus your awareness to ensure success.

Healing

Helpful for cellular memory, the oesophagus and stomach and for dissolving rigidity, cold sores and infection.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate.

Additional Notes

As Stibnite is toxic, wash hands thoroughly after use and make the gem essence by the indirect method. Due to its fragile nature, store Stibnite carefully in a small container, ideally wrapped in cotton, to prevent it from bumping against hard surfaces and fracturing.

Chakra Work

Stibnite activates all chakras simultaneously, creating a unified energetic column from Earth Star to Soul Star. It is particularly powerful at grounding through the Root and Earth Star Chakras, anchoring the physical body to the earth while drawing up wisdom from the animal and plant realms. At the same time, it opens the Crown and Soul Star Chakras to integrate frequencies from higher dimensions, connecting you with elevated beings who can illuminate your universal purpose beyond the physical plane. This dual action of rooting below and reaching above makes Stibnite an exceptional stone for maintaining clarity and groundedness during deep spiritual work. It is associated with the planet Pluto, carrying potent energies of rebirth and transformation through every energy center it touches.

Lore

Stibnite is a fragile form of antimony that has been revered since ancient times. The Egyptians used powdered stibnite as eye shadow as early as 3100 B.C.E. The Greek scientist Dioscorides referenced it in his writings as platyophthalmon, meaning "that which enlarges the eye," alluding to its cosmetic use. Pliny the Elder also wrote about stibnite as a treatment for eye infections, though modern medicine has not confirmed this application. The mineral's orthorhombic crystal structure produces its distinctive metallic, needle-like formations that often grow in natural wand-like shapes, a feature that has made it prized among practitioners for directed energy work and ritual use.
