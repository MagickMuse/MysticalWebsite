Appearance

Opaque to translucent pink crystalline stone, may be found in various formations.

Origin

Mexico, United States, Brazil, Argentina, Iceland, India, China.

Attributes

Pink Calcite is a gentle heart stone that promotes self-love and compassion. It opens the heart chakra and helps to release emotional wounds and past trauma. This soothing stone brings calm and peace to the emotional body, making it easier to process and release difficult feelings.

Pink Calcite is excellent for those who struggle with self-acceptance or who are overly critical of themselves. It teaches that you are worthy of love exactly as you are. This stone encourages forgiveness of self and others, helping to dissolve resentment and bitterness.

Spiritually, Pink Calcite connects you to the energy of divine love and helps you to see yourself through the eyes of the divine. It assists in recognizing your own inner beauty and worth.

Healing

Pink Calcite may assist with heart conditions and emotional healing. It is traditionally used to support the circulatory system and to soothe anxiety and stress.

Position

Place over the heart chakra during meditation or healing work. Hold to promote self-love and emotional release.
