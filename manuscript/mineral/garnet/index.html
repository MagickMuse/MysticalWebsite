Passion and Fertility

Garnet is one of the most potent fertility stones in the mineral kingdom. Beyond its direct support for conception, garnet helps couples release the immense pressure that so often accompanies the fertility journey. By easing that emotional weight and allowing both partners to simply enjoy each other's presence, garnet creates the spaciousness that, paradoxically, often opens the door to successful conception. Place garnet beneath the mattress or on a nightstand to invite its grounding, passionate energy into the bedroom. Its fiery vibration also supports desire and physical vitality, making it a well-rounded ally for intimacy and connection.

Spellworking

In the thirteenth century, garnets were worn to repel insects.

Strength and Endurance

The garnet, a fiery red stone, is worn to enhance bodily strength, endurance, and vigor. It is worn or used in magic to tap extra energy for ritual purposes. Wear or carry one when exerting yourself - hiking a mountain, studying until dawn, heavy ritual workings, and so on.

Protection

Because it is a projective stone, garnet is worn for protective purposes. Five hundred years ago it was thought to drive off demons and night phantoms. Today, garnet strengthens the aura and creates a shield of highly charged positive vibrations that repels negative energies upon contact. Garnets are particularly prescribed as protection against thieves.

In the Middle Ages, a figure of a lion was carved onto a garnet and carried to guard the health, especially while traveling.

Healing

A healing stone, garnet is used to relieve skin conditions, especially inflammations. It also regulates the heart and blood.

Friendship

In the past, garnets were exchanged between parting friends to symbolize their affection and to magically ensure that they met again.

Colors

Garnet occurs in transparent, green, yellow, orange, ultraviolet, brown, and black, though never blue. The red variety is by far the best known.

Blood, Wounds, and War

Because red garnet so often carries a deep color close to that of venous blood, it has long been tied to that vital fluid and held effective against bleeding wounds, blood disease, and hemorrhages. It was even thought to staunch bleeding outright, so from the Crusades onward soldiers carried it as a protective talisman, setting garnets into sword hilts and shields.

Garnet was believed able to inflict mortal wounds as readily as heal them. In 1892, during fighting between Indian and British troops in Kashmir, Hunza tribesmen loaded spherical garnets as bullets among their ordinary ammunition, and these jeweled shots caused many serious injuries and deaths among the British forces.

Name and Fertility

The name garnet comes from the pomegranate, for the cut stone resembles the sparkling seed of that fruit. Through this likeness, garnet became associated with the fertility of the womb.

Light and Protection

Garnet is one of the twelve stones set into the breastplate of the High Priest of the Temple in Jerusalem. It is often shaped into a cabochon, its curved underside gathering and pouring out light, for the stone's hardness gives it very high refractivity. A garnet carved this way is called a carbuncle, and so much light was attributed to it that the Qu'ran says it lights the Fourth Heaven. This carbuncle-cut garnet is also counted among the classic witch "fascination" gems set into rings and pendants, valued as a strong accumulator of witch power. Legend tells that Noah's Ark carried a huge garnet set on a pedestal, shining so brightly it lit the ship both day and night. This brilliance is one reason garnet is held as a stone of protection, giving light in the darkness and causing enemies to flee.

Healing the Body and Spirit

As a healing stone, garnet is said to regenerate both body and spirit and to throw off melancholy, though its use carried strict cautions. It was prized as a powerful heart stimulant, yet if worn too long, or if the patient did not stay conscious and calm throughout, the fiery blood-red stone could overstimulate the passions. When used against melancholia, one side effect could be insomnia, a sign of how stimulating the stone was thought to be.

Dark Magick Applications

Garnet is sometimes used by psychic vampires and those with similar appetites to draw energy or attract victims. It is often seen in the form of a large garnet ring on the index finger or a prominent garnet pendant resting over the solar plexus.
