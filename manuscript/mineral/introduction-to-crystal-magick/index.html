Mineral Grimoire

Introduction to Crystal Magick

The art of working with the slow, concentrated energy of stones for spellcraft, healing, and transformation.

Ancient Origins

Long before people began prizing gems for their monetary value, they used them as magick charms. In early societies, only rulers, members of royal families, priests, and religious leaders wore gems.

The ancient Egyptians used stones for healing, protection, and other purposes, both physical and spiritual. Ancient peoples believed gemstones were "capable of human feelings and passions so that they could express jealousy and shock."

The reverence ran deeper than adornment. Egyptian burials wrapped the dead in gems and set carved stone amulets throughout the tomb, each placed for a deliberate protective purpose. Far to the east, the vibrational qualities of gems were recorded in Indian Ayurvedic medicine and in Chinese herbal tradition, where stones were understood to carry healing influence on body and spirit. Using crystals for healing is an ancient art whose roots reach back beyond any single civilization.

🏛️
Lost Knowledge: Many researchers believe crystals may have been sources of power and magick on the lost continents of Atlantis and Lemuria, and that crystals were used in building the pyramids and Stonehenge.

Born of the Stars

One enduring teaching holds that the stars and planets shaped gemstones from the slow decay of the earth's matter, and that each stone then came under the rulership of the planet that formed it. This is one reason so many crystals carry planetary correspondences, and why the heavens and the mineral kingdom are read as a single continuous web of influence.

Gems in Sacred Myth

Across traditions, the most awe-inspiring symbols were imagined in gemstone form. In Hindu myth the sacred mountain Meru, home of the Gods, turns a face of gold to the north, silver to the east, cat's eye to the south, and ruby to the west where the sun sets. The same tradition gives us the Kalpa tree, a world-tree built entirely of jewels, with fruits of ruby, leaves of green zircon and coral, shoots of emerald, a trunk of cat's eye, topaz, and diamond, and roots of sapphire. The Seven Heavens of Islamic faith are mapped in gems and metals, as are the twelve foundations of the New Jerusalem.

The breastplate worn by the High Priest of the Temple in Jerusalem held twelve gems credited with the power of prophecy. Those twelve stones seeded many of the associations that still link particular gems to the months and signs of the year.

The Doctrine of Signatures

A widely used method reads a gem's magickal purpose from its color. This color correspondence sits at the heart of how stones are matched to intention, since color carries a real and marked effect on mood. A stone's hue becomes a direct signpost to the work it supports.

Tapping the Magick of Stones

Today, precious and semiprecious gems are prized primarily for their beauty. Witches, however, recognize that crystals and gemstones serve many magickal purposes:

  • Spellworking - Amplifying and focusing intention
  • Divination - Seeking insight
  • Shamanic journeying - Accessing other realms
  • Meditation - Deepening practice and awareness
  • Dowsing - Finding what is hidden
  • Healing - Supporting physical and spiritual wellness

Living Entities

Like plants, crystals and gemstones are living entities, although they resonate at a slow rate that most people can't perceive.

💎
Crystal Energy: Their slow, concentrated energy enables them to keep working their magick for a long period of time, making them ideal for sustained spellwork and protection.

Ways to Use Crystals in Magick

You can include crystals or gemstones in virtually any spell to increase, focus, stabilize, or fine-tune your spell's potency:

  • Wear them to enhance your own personal energy field
  • Put them in amulets or talismans for portable magick
  • Set them near windows and doors to provide home protection
  • Meditate with them for deeper spiritual connection
  • Infuse magick potions with their energy
  • Dowse with a pendulum made of gemstone or crystal
  • Display them on your altar to attract positive energy
  • Offer them to deities or nature spirits in return for assistance
  • Gaze into them to see the past or future (scrying)

Birthstones and Zodiac Signs

Birthstones resonate with the qualities of the zodiac signs to which they correspond. Originally, people wore birthstones to enhance, balance, or moderate their own personal characteristics.

♈
Important: You should look at your birth sign, not the month of your birth, to discover your true birthstone. If you're an Aquarian, for example, your birthstone is garnet, regardless of whether you were born in January or February.

Clearing Stones Between Workings

Crystals and other stones can be used in more than one spell, but you must clear them after each working. Clearing is essential because using a crystal that retains vibrations from a previous spell can interfere with the outcome of a new spell.

Clearing Methods

Water, salt, incense, sunlight, and moonlight can all be used, alone or in combination, to clear stones. For example:

  • Wash a crystal in running water, place it in a dish of salt, then put the dish in sunlight
  • Smudge a stone with incense, then place it overnight in moonlight

The more clearing you feel the stone needs, the longer you leave it in the clearing medium. The important thing is that you feel the stone has been cleared of any magickal residue before you use it in another spell.

Building a Crystal Spellwork Tool Kit

Before purchasing crystals, take time to figure out which ones will help you the most. Grab your journal and answer these questions:

  1. What types of spellwork do you do the most? If you do a lot of abundance work, acquire crystals with this focus.
  2. What crystals immediately come to mind? Research these intuitively-chosen crystals for themes you had not considered. Add at least two to your tool kit.
  3. What crystals do you dislike? Why? Often, a crystal we do not like is one we ought to work with most. There may be a lesson that crystal wants you to learn. Choose one of these for your tool kit.
  4. Which element are you most drawn to? Include crystals representing each element, with your most-used element as a larger focus.
  5. How much space do you have for crystals? Resist the desire to buy large crystals when starting out. Tumbled stones and crystal chips work just as effectively.

Storing Your Tool Kit

Keep working crystals in a bowl on your altar, in a wooden box, or in a velvet drawstring bag near your sacred space. You can decorate a box with colors and sigils to create a special home for your magickal workmates.

Connecting with Your Crystals

Connecting with your crystals and learning how their energy feels is essential for effective spellwork. By connecting with each one, you build a solid working relationship with each crystal spirit and can effectively assign each crystal its special job during your spellwork.

Sometimes a crystal does not feel suited for a specific spell, and you must respect their wishes and choose another. You can tell if a crystal does not want to participate by holding it and gauging its energy. The crystal may feel colder than usual, or you sense resistance. After your working, check back with any crystal that declined. Sometimes they just need a good cleaning.

Ways to Connect

  • Carry your crystal in your pocket or bag throughout the day. Hold it often.
  • Talk to your crystal. This opens the pathway to receiving messages during spellwork.
  • Sleep with your crystal under your pillow or on your bedside table. Ask it to communicate with you in dreams.
  • Meditate with your crystal to open the lines of connection and sense what it wants to offer you.
  • Journal about your crystals. Track thoughts, feelings, and messages to help you decide which crystal to use in each working.

Cleansing and Caring for Crystals

Cleansing crystals before and after use is good practice for spellwork, healing, or meditation. Crystals are sensitive to energy and will pick up and hold onto energy when handled. Clearing ensures the focus is directly on what the crystals need to do during a particular spell.

🌙
Selenite is self-cleansing and can cleanse other crystals. Place crystals in a selenite bowl and ask for them to be cleansed. Say thank you when finished.

Cleansing Methods

  • Smoke: Pass crystals through the smoke of your favorite herbs or incense, asking the smoke to clear unneeded energy. Rosemary smoke works well.
  • Moonlight: Place crystals under a full moon, or on a windowsill. Direct moonlight is not required; intention is what matters.
  • Sound: Use a singing bowl, bells, or music. Clapping your hands also works.
  • Reiki: A quick method if you are attuned.
  • Rice: Place crystals in a bowl of uncooked rice for as long as you are able. Any type of rice works.

Care Tips

  • Keep crystals dry and out of the sun. Some crystals, especially amethyst, fade with prolonged sun exposure.
  • If you have small children or pets, store crystals on a high shelf or in a drawer.
  • Keep altar crystals in a safe place to prevent disruption of ongoing spells.

Clear Quartz as Universal Substitute

Because they can most easily be programmed and reprogrammed, clear quartz crystals can be substituted for almost any stone in any spell.

Programming Clear Quartz

To substitute clear quartz for another stone:

  1. Take the crystal and hold it in sunlight or moonlight
  2. Say: "Crystal, for this spell you are given the powers of [name the stone whose energies are required]."
  3. After the spell has been cast, clear the crystal to restore it to its original state
