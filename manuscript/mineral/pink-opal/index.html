Pink Opal is a combination of opal, chalcedony, and palygorskite with an amorphous crystal structure, registering 5.5 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale. This gem forms when water evaporates from silica and leaves a residual gel that solidifies into opal. The ancient Egyptians called it the Hope Stone, and pharaohs would adorn themselves with pink opal crowns. Political leaders in ancient Rome also wore this crystal, believing it to balance their emotions. Archaeological evidence suggests pink opal was used in Kenyan artifacts 6,000 years ago.

Appearance

Soft pink to rose-colored opal with a smooth, opaque to translucent surface. Pink opal from Mexico and Peru gets its color from quinones, organic compounds infused into the stone during formation. Specimens from America and Australia get their color from the presence of manganese.

Origin

Found in Mexico and Peru.

It resonates with the element of water and brings depth, renewal, perspective, and healing. It is a wonderful stone to give to a loved one and is an especially great choice for children. Keeping a piece in your pillowcase is said to deepen rest and facilitate past-life recall in dreams. Because pink opal is deeply healing to the emotional body, it is a powerful stone to keep close during talk therapy, journaling sessions, or any other forms of self-reflection and self-care.

For beginners, pink opal holds a gentle vibration, making it a good choice for beginning crystal healers or those who are sensitive to energy. It brings gentle grounding and relaxation to those feeling overwhelmed or stressed. It encourages peace, restores hope in love, and provides comfort and equilibrium during challenging times.

For advanced practitioners, pink opal deepens empathy and helps you attune yourself to the emotions of others. It opens your heart to receive spiritual messages from others regarding what they need to feel seen and cared for. Pink opal is also known as a stone of renewal, rejuvenating your connection with yourself and the Divine. It propels spiritual awakening, helping you work with the universe to cocreate the life you desire. For those recovering from trauma from this life or past lives, pink opal facilitates the release of negative energies and helps you return to a state of centeredness, calm, and serenity.

Lore

The ancient Egyptians called pink opal the Hope Stone, and pharaohs adorned themselves with pink opal crowns. Roman political leaders wore it to balance their emotions. The Inca tribes of South America held pink opal in high regard, believing it to connect them with spirits and deities, particularly Pachamama, the Earth Mother. Archaeological evidence suggests pink opal was used in Kenyan artifacts 6,000 years ago.
