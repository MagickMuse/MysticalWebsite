Attributes

Trigonic Quartz displays natural triangular markings called trigons on its crystal faces. These sacred geometric patterns are believed to hold encoded information and serve as gateways to higher dimensional consciousness. The triangular formations represent the divine trinity and the integration of mind, body, and spirit.

This crystal is a powerful tool for those working with sacred geometry, ancient wisdom traditions, and multidimensional healing. The trigonic markings act as portals that facilitate access to higher realms of consciousness and ancient knowledge banks. Trigonic Quartz assists in downloading information that is relevant to current spiritual evolution and planetary healing.

The stone supports those engaged in lightwork, energy healing, and consciousness expansion. It helps to integrate higher dimensional frequencies into the physical body and assists in maintaining spiritual connection during daily life. Trigonic Quartz is particularly helpful for accessing guidance from ascended masters and higher dimensional beings.

Working with Trigonic Quartz can accelerate spiritual development and assist in remembering one's soul mission and purpose. The crystal helps to align personal will with divine will and supports the manifestation of spiritual ideals in physical reality.

Healing

Trigonic Quartz works at the level of consciousness to facilitate healing and integration. It supports the nervous system and assists in integrating high-frequency energies.

Hold during meditation to access higher dimensional guidance. Place on the third eye or crown chakra during spiritual work. Use as a focal point during sacred geometry meditations and consciousness expansion work.
