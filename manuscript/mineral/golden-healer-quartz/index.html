Golden Healer Quartz is a master healer that works on all levels. The golden yellow iron oxide coating raises the vibrations of the Quartz and is said to access the gold ray of Christ Consciousness, the highest healing energy available to us on earth. This golden light is powerful, gentle and soft, accessing the crown chakra to create a divine connection. The iron in the coating accesses the base chakra, creating a perfect flow of energy to cleanse and balance all the chakras and aligning them with the lightbody. This stone facilitates healing on all levels and from all directions of time.

Psychologically, Golden Healer Quartz releases old hurts and traumas, putting the past into perspective and dissolving karmic patterns. Its golden energy fills the heart with love and confidence. Environmentally, Golden Healer Quartz can be gridded to send healing energy to the earth or to create a healing space.
