Appearance

Multicolored jasper with swirling patterns of earth tones, reds, greens, and creams.

Origin

Madagascar.

Attributes

Sourced near the now mined-out Ocean Jasper location, Polychrome Jasper is highly protective when journeying between the worlds. It helps you feel that you belong on earth as part of the clan of humanity, encouraging you to reach out to others from a place of strength rather than need. Resolving dualities, it assists in integrating into the whole. This is a useful stone for those seeking their power ally.

Healing

Polychrome Jasper supports grounding and emotional stability, helping to balance scattered energies.

Position

Carry during shamanic journeying or meditation. Hold when seeking connection with power animals or spirit allies.
