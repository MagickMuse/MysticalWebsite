Appearance

Gabbro with Moonstone appears as dark gabbro matrix with moonstone inclusions, creating a contrasting appearance.

Origin

Found in Madagascar and other locations where gabbro and moonstone occur together.

Attributes

The combination of Gabbro with Moonstone is energetically confusing to some people, the stone feels like it doesn't know whether to be deeply grounded or to float off the planet. In those who are attuned, it helps you to be 'here' and 'there' at the same time as you navigate the multi-dimensional layers of consciousness. In those that are not attuned, it may make it difficult to ground the information received in those spheres or, indeed, to return to the earth at all. If you only have a toehold in incarnation, this is not the stone for you.

Healing

Gabbro with Moonstone works on multi-dimensional levels but may be destabilizing for those not ready for such work.

Position

Use with caution. Only for experienced practitioners who are well-grounded in physical reality.

Spellworking

This unusual combination blends moonstone's lunar, intuitive properties with gabbro's grounding energy. Use it for navigating multi-dimensional consciousness while maintaining earthly connection. Best reserved for experienced practitioners who can handle its paradoxical energies.
