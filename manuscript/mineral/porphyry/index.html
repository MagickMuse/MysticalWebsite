Appearance

Porphyry appears as a type of marble with distinctive coloring and patterns.

Origin

Found in various locations as a type of marble.

Attributes

A type of marble, Porphyry draws divine energy down to the earth which is why it was used for church decoration. With a Porphyry floor, you literally stood on divine energy and were uplifted. It links to the ancient view that the divine was to be found in everything and facilitates connection to the divine within yourself. Meditating with Porphyry helps you to recognize soul lessons in the smallest details of life and teaches that the soul is always free to make a choice, no matter what the external circumstances might be. It strengthens your connection to your inner guidance so that you trust your insights. Opening your inner vision, it also helps you to trust others because you can see into their heart and check out their integrity for yourself. With the assistance of Porphyry you are able to inspire trust yourself by shining out your heart-light and displaying your spiritual integrity for the world to see.

Healing

Porphyry may assist the veins and structures of the body.

Position

Place, hold or meditate with as appropriate for spiritual connection.
