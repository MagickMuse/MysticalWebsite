Spellworking

Electrum is a general term describing a mixture or alloy of metals. Gold, silver, and platinum are often found in some combination in electrum used in magic.

Magical Lore

Naturally occurring electrum is rare and was once highly desired for magical use. Today, even though electrum is produced through artificial means, this doesn't lessen its energies. The process of mixing metals combines their powers, creating a "new" metal used in various magical operations requiring the combined forces of several different planets.

Ancient Egyptians made jewelry from naturally occurring electrum. Contemporary magical practitioners skilled in metalcraft make their own electrum for specific purposes.

Combined Planetary Forces

An electrum of gold and silver combines solar (projective) and lunar (receptive) energies. A Wiccan dedicated to the old goddess and god of nature might wear a ring or pendant of silver and gold electrum as a symbol of the unity of the two primal deities.

Historical Uses

Hundreds of years ago, an electrum of gold and silver was fashioned into a cup said to reveal the presence of poison by emitting half-round rainbows and sparks—an example of the powers attributed to this alloy.

Availability

Today, electrum is rarely commercially available and usually must be custom-made.
