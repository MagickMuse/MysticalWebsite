Stilbite's Journey to You

Stilbite is a zeolite-family mineral crystallizing in the monoclinic system with a Mohs hardness of 3.5 to 4. Discovered in 1797 by Jean Claude de la Metherie, it was named in 1801 by R. J. Hauy from the Greek "stilbein," meaning "to glitter or shine," a nod to its beautiful pearly peach sheen. It is often found growing alongside Apophyllite and Heulandite in volcanic environments. Two forms exist: Stilbite-Ca (calcium-rich) and Stilbite-Na (sodium-rich), though both carry the same metaphysical properties. It is a powerful stone that frees the keeper from self-limiting beliefs and steers them toward their bliss.

Stilbite is a highly creative stone that opens the intuition and carries a loving and supportive vibration in any endeavor. It is very helpful in metaphysical working at all levels. It grounds spiritual energy and helps to manifest intuitive thought into action on the physical plane.

Stilbite aids spiritual journeying, protecting and maintaining physical contact while traveling. The stone gives guidance and direction throughout the journey, no matter where the destination may lie. Used at its highest vibration, it aids traveling into the upper spiritual realms and bringing back the conscious memory of one's experiences there. Stilbite crystal clusters can be used as a scrying tool.

Care

Stilbite sits at Mohs 3.5 to 4, making it a soft and delicate stone that requires gentle handling. It is sun safe but not water safe; avoid water cleansing as moisture can damage its structure. Cleanse with smoke, moonlight, sound, or by placing on a selenite plate. Store separately from harder stones to prevent scratching.
