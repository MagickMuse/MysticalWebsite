Origin

Worldwide. Also found in South Africa, Myanmar, United States.

Attributes

Pyrope bestows vitality and charisma and promotes an excellent quality of life. It unites the creative forces within oneself. This stone protects the base and crown chakras, aligning them with the subtle bodies, and linking the groundedness of the base with the wisdom of the crown. Pyrope is a stabilizing stone.

Healing

It fortifies circulation and treats the digestive tract. It neutralizes heartburn and soothes a sore throat.
