Magickal Powers

Keeps the aura from absorbing negative energy

Appearance

Rounded, translucent nodules of black obsidian, often with a slightly smoky or grayish hue

Origin

United States (especially Arizona), Mexico, Peru, Armenia, Iceland

Attributes

Apache Tear is a form of black obsidian that has a gentler energy than its parent stone. Legend says these stones were formed from the tears of Apache women who mourned warriors who chose to leap to their deaths rather than be captured by the cavalry. This stone is a powerful grief healer, absorbing negative energy while comforting the mourner.

Psychologically, Apache Tear helps process grief and loss, gently releasing pain and trauma. It grounds spiritual energy into the physical body and protects against psychic attack. This stone promotes forgiveness and the release of long-held grievances.

Healing

Apache Tear supports the immune system, aids detoxification, and helps absorb vitamins and minerals. It eases muscle spasms and enhances vitality.

Position

Carry for comfort during grief or place over affected areas for physical healing.

Spellworking

Apache Tear, a globule of translucent obsidian, is carried as a good-luck charm. It is also used for protective purposes, sharing all the magical attributes of obsidian.
