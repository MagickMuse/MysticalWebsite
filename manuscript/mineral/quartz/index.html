It possesses a powerful energetic resonance capable of raising personal vibrations or amplifying cosmic energy on a planetary scale. The first recorded reference to quartz dates to 1530, when the German metallurgist Georgius Agricola, a pioneer of modern geology, described it in his writings. Ancient Greeks and Romans were among the first to recognize quartz as a potent crystal and began carving protective amulets from it.
