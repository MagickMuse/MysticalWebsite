Appearance

Purple Moonstone appears as gentle purple to lavender colored moonstone, often with characteristic moonstone sheen.

Origin

Found in various locations, though some specimens may be color-enhanced.

Attributes

A gentle heart-centred form of Moonstone that helps you to have compassion for yourself and others. This stone works with the heart chakra to promote self-acceptance and loving-kindness. Purple Moonstone may be colour infused in some cases, but still carries the gentle, nurturing energy characteristic of moonstone family minerals.

Healing

Purple Moonstone promotes emotional healing through self-compassion and helps develop empathy and understanding for others.

Position

Place over heart chakra or hold during meditation for emotional healing and compassion work.

Spellworking

Ruled by the moon, this purple variety of moonstone has long been used to aid dream recall. It calms emotions, increases intuition, and benefits female health conditions. The purple color adds spiritual depth and compassion to moonstone's lunar properties. As a translucent stone, it excels in emotional situations and heart-centered healing.
