Appearance

Brown, green, pink, yellow, or white colors, often showing different colors from different angles due to pleochroism

Origin

Spain, Brazil, and other locations worldwide

Attributes

Andalusite is known for its pleochroic properties, showing different colors when viewed from different angles. This optical phenomenon makes it an excellent stone for seeing situations from multiple perspectives and finding creative solutions to problems. It helps you remain centered and objective when dealing with complex issues, encouraging rational thought while still honoring intuitive insights. Andalusite is particularly helpful for those who need to make important decisions or solve difficult problems.

Healing

Andalusite helps bring clarity to mental processes and supports rational decision-making. It may assist with memory and concentration.

Position

Hold during decision-making or place on desk during problem-solving work. Carry when you need to see situations from multiple angles.
