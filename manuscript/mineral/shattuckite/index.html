Lore

Shattuckite was first discovered in the Shattuck mine in Arizona, United States, after which it was named. It is a rare copper-rich mineral that forms as a pseudomorph after malachite, meaning it replaces the original mineral while retaining its structure. It is often found growing in the same mines as mineralogically similar stones such as chrysocolla and malachite, and sometimes grows as inclusions within quartz, giving the host crystal a brilliant blue-green luster. Shattuckite is one of four powerhouse mineral inclusions in the gem known as Quantum Quattro, a combination considered to contain four of the most powerful regeneration and physical healing stones on the planet today.
