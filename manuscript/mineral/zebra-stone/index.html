Zebra Stone

Origin
Australia
Sun Safe?
Yes
Notes/Warnings
A sedimentary rock from the Kimberley region of Western Australia, characterised by distinctive alternating red and white banding created by iron-rich minerals. A stabilising and balancing stone that unites opposing forces and encourages a sense of wholeness. Strongly connected to earth and animal energies.

Appearance

Banded stone with contrasting colors, often tumbled. Black and white or pink and brown varieties.

Origin

Australia.

Attributes

Zebra Stone is a grounding stone that connects you to the earth's energy and stimulates physical energy. It enhances stamina and endurance, assisting you in reaching your goals no matter how difficult the path may seem. This stone protects against negativity and helps you face difficult situations with courage and determination.

Psychologically, Zebra Stone helps you see both sides of a situation and brings balance between opposing forces. It encourages you to walk your path with confidence and determination.

Physically, this is a stone of physical vitality and strength, helping to increase stamina during physical activities or recovery from illness.

Healing

Beneficial for teeth, bones and gums. Said to help with muscle spasms, heart palpitations and vitamin deficiency.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate.
