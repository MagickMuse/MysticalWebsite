Appearance

Crystalline to vitreous opaque pearly crystals on a matrix, easily obtained.

Origin

Brazil, South Africa, United States, Germany, Russia.

Attributes

An extraordinary, powerfully energizing stone, Herderite is the stone of the Higher Self and has several properties that support its high vibration, including evolution, enlightenment and communication with the highest realms. It is a stone of attunement and of manifestation and it facilitates change at every level of being.

Spiritually, this stone is attuned to, and assists in communicating with, higher spiritual beings and facilitates inter- and inner-dimensional travel, bringing back what is appropriate and necessary to assist the soul purpose in this life. It promotes enlightenment and draws like-minded souls together into a spiritual community so that knowledge and gifts are shared. It stimulates the mind, helping you to acquire knowledge and to recognize that all is not as it seems and yet is exactly as it is meant to be for your own personal evolution.

Psychologically, Herderite teaches you to take responsibility for yourself and your reality and to accept that you create your own life, either through your belief systems or by surrendering your power to someone else and then acceding to being 'done to' by them. It insists on empowerment of the self at the highest level and teaches that the answer to all your problems lies within your own soul. It is excellent for use in past life healing and releases mental imperatives that arose from past lives or from ancestral or cultural conditioning.

Emotionally, this stone teaches compassion and assists in handling your emotions without judgment or self-condemnation so that you see them for what they really are: a tool for growth and part of the human condition that is shared by all.

Healing

Works at the etheric level to heal the chakras and the energy bodies, bringing about alignment and integration. Said to be helpful for Alzheimer's and for disorders of the optic nerves, eyes, ears and sinuses.

Position

Hold, grid or place as appropriate. Affects people according to their energetic blueprint and readiness for evolution and will produce effects at whatever level is appropriate.
