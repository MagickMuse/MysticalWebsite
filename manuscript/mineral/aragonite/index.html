Appearance

White, gray, yellow, blue, green, brown, orange, or red, translucent to opaque, vitreous to resinous luster, orthorhombic crystal system, often fibrous or columnar

Origin

Namibia, Britain, Spain, Morocco, Turkey, Italy, United States, Czech Republic, France, Austria

Attributes

Aragonite is a reliable earth-healer and grounding stone. It transforms geopathic stress and clears blocked ley lines even at a distance. This crystal can be placed on maps for this purpose. It deepens connection with the earth and encourages conservation of earth's precious resources.

Aragonite acts as a stabilizing stone that centers and grounds physical energies, being useful in time of stress. It helps to prepare for meditation and stabilizes spiritual development. It provides insight into the causes of problems and situations.

Psychologically, Aragonite teaches patience and acceptance. It combats oversensitivity. This crystal facilitates delegating and is useful for people who push themselves too hard. It provides strength and support.

Mentally, Aragonite aids concentration on the matter at hand. It ensures that underlying problems are not overlooked and gives insight into the causes behind problems.

Emotionally, Aragonite combats anger and emotional stress. Aragonite provides strength and support during difficult periods.

Healing

Aragonite warms the extremities, bringing energy through the body. It treats Reynaud's Disease and chills. It heals bones, aids calcium absorption and restores elasticity to discs. Aragonite alleviates pain, especially nerve pain, and stops night twitches and muscle spasms. It strengthens the immune system and regulates processes that are proceeding too fast.

Position

Hold or place as appropriate. Position on maps to heal the earth.
