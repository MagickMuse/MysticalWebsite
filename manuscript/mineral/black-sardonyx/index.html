Appearance

Black colored, banded, opaque sardonyx. May be large or small, often tumbled.

Origin

Brazil, India, Russia, Asia Minor

Attributes

Black Sardonyx absorbs negativity.

Additional Notes

In addition to this specific property, Black Sardonyx carries all the generic attributes of Sardonyx: a stone of strength and protection that invokes the search for a meaningful existence and promotes integrity and virtuous conduct.

Spellworking

Black Sardonyx absorbs negativity while providing sardonyx's strength and protection. Use it for banishing negative energy, shielding from harm, and grounding. As an opaque black stone, it excels at physical protection and absorbing unwanted energies.
