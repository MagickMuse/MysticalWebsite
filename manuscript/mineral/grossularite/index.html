About Grossular Garnet

Grossular garnet is a calcium-and-aluminum-rich silicate mineral belonging to the garnet family, typically found within metamorphic limestone deposits. Its name traces to the French word for currant, in reference to the stone's resemblance to that small, light-green fruit. Tsavorite is a rarer, deeper emerald-green variety of grossular garnet, first identified in the Tsavo region of Kenya in 1960. While grossular garnet appears widely across the globe, significant deposits are concentrated in Australia, Austria, Italy, Kenya, Madagascar, New Zealand, Poland, Siberia, South Africa, Tanzania, and the United States.
