Appearance

Many crystals have points, some large and some so small they can hardly be seen with the naked eye. Points may be natural or artificially shaped. A single crystal point has a definite faceted, pointed end and the other end tends to look ragged where it has been separated from a cluster base.

Properties

Focuses energy into a beam. Directs energy in a specific direction.

Uses

Often used in healing. Pointed away from the body, it draws energy off. Pointed inward, it channels energy to the body.

Additional Notes

Naturally occurring long points focus energy in a straight line and are often mimicked in purpose-made crystal wands.

Magickal Properties

Single-terminated wands send energy outward through the point while drawing it in from the rough or rounded end. That secondary source can be a person, an energy field, or a physical location. Naturally formed points are considered more potent than carved ones, though carved pieces carry comparable energy. It is worth asking whether a wand is naturally formed or shaped before purchasing. Quartz is the most common material due to its high dominant oscillatory rate, though massage wands are also carved from bloodstone, shungite, and obsidian.
