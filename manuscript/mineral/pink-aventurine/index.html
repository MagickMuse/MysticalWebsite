Pink aventurine is a variety of quartz forming in the Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, deriving its pink-to-deep-red coloring from inclusions of iron and lithium.

Appearance

Ranges from soft blush pink to rich rose-red, translucent to opaque, with a subtle sparkle from reflective mineral inclusions and a waxy to vitreous luster.

History

In ancient times, pink aventurine was regarded as a talisman of protection, believed to bring restful sleep, health, longevity, and shelter from harmful spirits. During the Renaissance it was worked with to attract love and encourage fertility. Ancient Egyptian practitioners carried it as a shield against the evil eye. It is also associated with Venus, goddess of love and beauty, reflecting its deep connection to matters of the heart.

Magickal Properties

Pink aventurine heals and opens the Heart Chakra, welcoming all forms of love, connection, and abundance into your life. It brings good fortune to new business ventures and stimulates creative thinking, helping you discover inspired approaches that lead to meaningful success. As a stone of inner child work, it helps you acknowledge past wounds with compassion and offer yourself the unconditional love and care you always deserved. It deepens empathy, lifts heaviness from the spirit, and supports your return to wholeness after grief or emotional strain.

Keep pink aventurine in the home to fill your space with vibrations of compassion, stability, and balance. Worn as jewelry, its gentle protective energy keeps you feeling grounded and safe throughout the day.

Chakra Work

Pink aventurine works primarily with the Root and Heart Chakras, anchoring love in the physical body and creating a stable foundation from which the heart can fully open. Hold it to the heart space to clear blockages that stand between you and the love or abundance you deserve. It is also a wonderful stone for couples, supporting openness, harmony, commitment, and mutual understanding.

Healing

Pink aventurine supports emotional healing, easing stress, anxiety, and restlessness. It soothes extreme emotions and builds self-esteem. Its lepidolite content, a natural lithium mineral, brings additional support for mood balance, protection from negativity, and deep relaxation. It is also a grounding companion for re-entering the body after deep meditation or spiritual journeys.

Position

Hold to the heart space for Heart Chakra work, or to the Root Chakra for grounding and stability. Place in the home to create an atmosphere of warmth and abundance. Wear in jewelry for ongoing gentle protection and heart-centered energy.

Did You Know?

Pink aventurine contains lepidolite, which holds a natural form of lithium. This gives it an extra layer of mood-softening, balancing energy that sets it apart from other heart stones.
