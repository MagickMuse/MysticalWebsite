Purpurite is a manganese iron phosphate mineral with an orthorhombic crystal structure, registering 4 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale. Its name comes from the Latin "purpura" meaning purple, reflecting its vivid hue. First discovered in South Africa in 1902, purpurite gets its brilliant color from the presence of manganese and iron. Manganese is healing, strengthening, and enhances positive energy, while iron is stabilizing and grounding.
