Appearance

Opaque stone with mottled patterns in orange, brown and black coloring.

Origin

United States, Brazil, India, Egypt, Australia, Madagascar, Russia, China, Uruguay.

Attributes

Mottled Jasper shares the general stabilizing and nurturing properties of all Jaspers. This stone provides grounding and protection while maintaining connection to the earth's energies. The mottled patterns within the stone are said to represent the diverse experiences of life and help you to find balance amid chaos.

Mottled Jasper is particularly helpful for those who need support during times of stress or change. It provides steady, reliable energy that helps you to remain centered and calm. This stone encourages patience and persistence, helping you to work steadily toward your goals.

Healing

Mottled Jasper provides general support for the body's systems.

Position

Hold or place as appropriate for grounding and stability.

Spellworking

Wear mottled jasper for protection against drowning. It is said to be especially potent for this purpose when carved with the image of an equal-armed cross, representing the powers of the four elements, of foundation, and of control.
