Orange Kyanite is an aluminum-rich silicate, a variety of kyanite (also known as disthene), that forms fibrous, golden-orange flat blades in metamorphic rocks. It has a triclinic crystal structure and registers 4.5 on the Mohs scale. Its distinctive coloring comes from manganese inclusions, and it ranges from translucent to opaque.

Appearance

Fibrous, golden-orange striated blades, often flat. Ranges from translucent to opaque with a sparkling quality from mica flakes within its striations.

Origin

First discovered in 2008 in Tanzania's Arusha Region, an area also known for producing tanzanite and tsavorite. Tanzania remains its only known source, making it the rarest form of kyanite.
