Lore

Stromatolite is a fossilized form of algae, slowly encased in sediment and transformed into quartz, agate, or other minerals over thousands of years. Its name derives from a Greek word meaning "layered rock," describing its distinctive banded appearance. Stromatolite is among the oldest fossils ever discovered, sometimes containing algae dating back over 3.5 billion years, making it a living record of the planet's earliest chapter. Often called the "lungs of Earth," stromatolite-forming organisms are believed to have helped create and sustain the planet's oxygen supply during the earliest formations of life. In this way, working with Stromatolite connects you to the very breath of the ancient world. It is said that this stone will reveal your role on this planet and how best to serve the world around you as a healer.
