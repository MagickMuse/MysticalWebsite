Appearance

Color: Pink with smoky grey or brown

Appearance: Transparent to translucent crystal combining pink and smoky tones

Rarity: Available

Origin

Source: Worldwide

Attributes

Smoky Rose Quartz combines the grounding properties of Smoky Quartz with the heart-healing qualities of Rose Quartz.

Spiritually, this stone facilitates grounding heart energy and protecting the emotional body during spiritual work.

Psychologically, Smoky Rose Quartz assists in healing deep emotional wounds while providing grounding and protection.

Mentally, it brings mental clarity to emotional issues and helps in making heart-centered decisions.

Emotionally, this crystal is deeply healing for the heart, promoting self-love and compassion while releasing emotional trauma.

Physically, Smoky Rose Quartz supports the heart chakra and circulatory system.

Healing

Beneficial for the heart, emotional healing, stress relief, and grounding.

Position

Place on the heart chakra; hold during meditation; wear for emotional protection and healing.

Additional Notes

Warning: Fades in direct sunlight. Keep away from prolonged sun exposure.

Source: The Crystal Bible 3 by Judy Hall

Spellworking

Smoky Rose Quartz combines rose quartz's love energy with smoky quartz's grounding protection. Use it for grounded heart healing, protecting the emotional body, and releasing deep emotional trauma safely. This combination is excellent when you need to do heart work while staying anchored.
