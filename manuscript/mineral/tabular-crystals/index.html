Appearance

Has two wide sides., resulting in a flat crystal which may be double terminated. Many tabular crystals have notches in them that can be rubbed to activate the information contained within the crystal.

Properties

Energy flows freely through a tabular crystal, which offers little resistance. Removes confusion, misinterpretation, and misunderstanding.

Uses

Excellent aid to communication at all levels, both inner and outer. Said to be the finest tool for communication with other realms. In healing, links two points, bringing about perfect balance, and can be used to enhance telepathy. Activates other crystals.
