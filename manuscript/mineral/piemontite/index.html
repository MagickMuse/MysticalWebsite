Appearance

Opaque to translucent crystal with distinctive coloring.

Origin

Italy, United States.

Attributes

Piemontite is a stone of creativity and self-expression that helps you to manifest your unique gifts and talents in the world. This crystal encourages originality and helps you to break free from conventional thinking patterns. Piemontite is excellent for artists, writers and anyone involved in creative work as it stimulates inspiration and helps to overcome creative blocks.

This stone enhances self-confidence and helps you to express your authentic self without fear of judgment. It encourages you to take risks and to step outside your comfort zone in order to grow and develop. Piemontite is particularly useful for those who have been suppressed or who have difficulty asserting themselves.

Spiritually, Piemontite helps you to discover your soul's purpose and to align your life with your highest calling. It assists in developing your unique spiritual gifts and encourages you to share them with the world.

Healing

Piemontite may assist with disorders of the heart and circulation. It is reported to help with creative blocks and to enhance vitality and life force energy.

Position

Wear, hold or place as appropriate. Use during creative work and self-expression exercises.
