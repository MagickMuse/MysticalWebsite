Appearance

Colourless, white, grey, purple, yellow, green, pink, brown vitreous, pearly opaque stone.

Origin

Madagascar, Tanzania, United States.

Attributes

Marialite stimulates independence of thought and helps you to set achievable goals as you move confidently through life. It provides an impetus for assertive change and, if you feel that you have been scapegoated or martyred at any time, helps to de-energize old patterns and move forward in life. Placed over the eyes, it helps see your way clearly.

Healing

Marialite supports post-operative recovery and is said to repattern cellular memory.

Position

Hold, position, grid or disperse gem essence around the aura.
