Golden Labradorite is a rare member of the feldspar family, a masculine stone associated with the sun and the Golden Ray. It has a triclinic crystal structure and registers 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale.

Appearance

Golden labradorite does not display the same labradorescence seen in other forms of labradorite and feldspars. Instead, it shows a gorgeous, luminescent sheen when polished or faceted, reflective of its radiant metaphysical power. Its color ranges from warm golden-yellow to deep amber.

Origin

First discovered in 1855 in Bytown (now Ottawa), Canada, which gives it its alternate name, bytownite. Also found in Ethiopia, Mexico, Norway, and the United States.

Magickal Properties

Golden labradorite is considered a stone of the sun, carrying fiery, intense masculine energy. It is empowering, enhances intuition, and enables you to find the courage to do what you need to do. It brings wisdom, especially about your higher calling, and can be held in meditation or carried throughout the day to invoke deep confidence.

For beginners, golden labradorite helps you find your center and stay steady in times of change. It awakens and strengthens your sense of purpose and increases your ability to manifest your desires. It alleviates procrastination and enhances clarity of mind, vitality, and spiritual focus.

For advanced practitioners, golden labradorite illuminates when the will of the Divine is at work in your life, from small coincidences to more significant triggers of spiritual awakening. It teaches about the appropriate use of power and helps release issues or fears regarding the misuse of power from past lives. It can be used during dream work or while journeying to access higher planes of wisdom, helping you consciously and accurately recall the experiences and information gained while there.

Chakra Work

Golden labradorite activates the Solar Plexus Chakra. Placed on this energy center, it helps you move more confidently in the world, strengthening your sense of personal power and inner fire. Its masculine solar energy ignites willpower, courage, and a steady sense of self.

Healing

Golden labradorite is traditionally associated with supporting the digestive system and solar plexus organs. It is believed to enhance vitality and energize the body. It may also support mental clarity and help ease conditions related to stress and lack of motivation.

Position

Place on the Solar Plexus Chakra, hold in meditation, or carry throughout the day to maintain confidence and personal power.

Lore

Golden labradorite has occasionally been found as an inclusion in meteorites, lending an extraterrestrial quality to its already remarkable properties. It was first identified in Bytown, Canada in 1855, and its name, bytownite, honors that place of origin.
