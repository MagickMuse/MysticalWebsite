Appearance

Green colored sapphire crystal, bright and transparent when polished.

Origin

Myanmar, Czech Republic, Brazil, Kenya, India, Australia, Sri Lanka

Attributes

Green Sapphire improves vision, both inner and outer, and improves dream recall.

It stimulates the heart chakra, bringing loyalty, fidelity, and integrity. This stone enhances compassion and an understanding of the frailties and unique qualities of others. It honors trust and respect for other people's belief systems.

Additional Notes

In addition to these specific properties, Green Sapphire carries all the generic attributes of Sapphire.

Spellworking

Green Sapphire combines sapphire's wisdom with the heart-opening properties of green stones. Use it for dream recall, loyalty, fidelity, and understanding others. Like all green stones, it opens the heart chakra and promotes compassion. Also beneficial for improving inner and outer vision.
