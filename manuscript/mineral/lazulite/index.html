Lazulite is a magnesium-iron-aluminum phosphate with a monoclinic crystal structure, registering 5 to 6 on the Mohs scale. Its color ranges from rich azure to pale blue and sometimes green-blue.

Appearance

Ethereal blue stone ranging from rich azure to pale blue and occasionally green-blue. It displays a luminous quality reflective of its name, derived from the Arabic "lazaward," meaning sky or heaven.

Origin

Found in Austria, Brazil, Canada (Yukon), India, Madagascar, Sweden, and the United States (California, Georgia, Wisconsin).

Magickal Properties

Lazulite is a stone of psychic ability and focus. It encourages calm and discipline, deepening meditation and connecting you with the higher realms. As one of the purest gems of the Indigo Ray, it activates the Third Eye Chakra to strengthen intuition and aid divination.

For beginners, lazulite deepens meditation and channels tranquility when you are in need. It dissipates fear, doubt, and tension and promotes optimism and emotional well-being. It strengthens insight, helping you see the worlds around and within you clearly and from a higher perspective, and develops psychic abilities. It brings feelings of bliss, serenity, and divine connection, leading you to see the big picture of life and experience the peace that comes with that revelation.

For advanced practitioners, lazulite serves as a powerful guide when performing deep, transformative spiritual work. It induces lucid dreaming, aids astral travel, and grants access to past-life memories. It cleanses and opens the Third Eye, attuning you to wisdom from realms beyond our world of time and space. By helping you access alternate realities and past versions of yourself, lazulite leads you in clearing any trauma or emotional baggage you may be holding from past lifetimes. If you or a client has had experiences that were psychically overwhelming, lazulite can be used as a cosmic reset for the Third Eye Chakra.

Chakra Work

Lazulite is a stone of the Third Eye and Throat chakras. Place it upon the Third Eye during meditation or ritual to open and strengthen psychic vision. This gem is valuable for channeling spiritual visions, embarking on astral travel, and promoting lucid dreaming. Hold it to the Throat Chakra during healing work to cleanse energetic blockages, anything keeping you from sharing your truth with clarity, conviction, and honesty. Keep lazulite nearby as you perform tarot readings or any other form of divination, or by your bedside to receive divine messages in your sleep.

Healing

Lazulite is traditionally associated with supporting mental clarity, calming an overactive mind, and easing tension. It is believed to assist with conditions related to stress, worry, and emotional overwhelm.

Position

Place on the Third Eye or Throat Chakra during meditation or healing work. Keep nearby during divination practices or by the bedside for dream work.

Lore

The ancients worked with lazulite as a "worry stone," using it in meditation to gain insight into their troubles and unveil the best ways to work through them. Famed clairvoyant Edgar Cayce held lazulite in high regard, mentioning it as a potent healing stone in his readings and pairing it with azurite to promote higher awareness and open psychic channels. Its name comes from the Arabic "lazaward," meaning sky or heaven.
