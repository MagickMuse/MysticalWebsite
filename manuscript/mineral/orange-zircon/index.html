Spellworking

AKA: Jacinth, Hyacinth

Wear orange zircon to increase beauty and to still fears and jealousy. Carried during travel, it guards against injury. Worn or placed in the home, it safeguards against theft—keep an orange zircon with valuables. Set in gold, it is doubly powerful.
