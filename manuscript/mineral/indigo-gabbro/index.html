A trigonal crystal and naturally occurring fusion of quartz and psilomelane, indigo gabbro is a powerful stone of magick, psychic development, and spiritual wisdom. Sometimes called the Wizard's Stone, it is linked by crystal healers to Merlin of Arthurian legend, reflecting its reputation for shape-shifting energy and command over all forms of magickal work.

Origin

Indigo gabbro forms from the combination of silicon dioxide (quartz) and manganese oxide (psilomelane). It is found exclusively in New Mexico, United States, making it one of the rarest locality-specific stones in the mineral world.

Appearance

A striking stone displaying deep indigo, purple, and black tones interwoven with lighter flashes of grey and white. The surface may appear mottled or layered, reflecting its composite mineral structure.

Magickal Properties

Indigo gabbro opens psychic portals and strengthens all forms of spiritual practice. It aids past-life recall, mediumship, and shamanic journeying, attuning the practitioner to both the natural world and the spiritual realm. Visions of past incarnations arise in dreams and meditation, helping integrate ancestral wisdom into present life.

This stone attracts teachers from other dimensions, provides psychic protection, and lifts the veil between the seen and unseen worlds. It is a potent ally for contacting loved ones who have passed. Indigo gabbro also accelerates manifestation, encourages self-love, and fosters acceptance and gratitude toward past experiences.

It calls upon all four elements, making it a versatile companion for any working. It deepens intuition and supports the study of astrology, tarot, numerology, and all forms of divination, including scrying.

Chakra Work

Place indigo gabbro on the Third Eye during meditation to activate psychic vision and contact spirit guides. Hold it against the solar plexus to ignite confidence and willpower, or rest it at the crown of the head to open a channel to the higher realms. It works across the Solar Plexus, Third Eye, and Crown chakras simultaneously.

Correspondences

Indigo gabbro partners well with Amethyst, Moldavite, Obsidian, and Sugilite. It resonates with Gemini, Libra, Scorpio, and Pisces.

Colors

Indigo, purple, black, grey, white

Magickal Message

"You are magick, gifted with the ability to transcend time and space. The answers you seek are already within you, and as your ally, I will shine a light upon them. The gateway to great spiritual wisdom lies in your very own hands."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 6.5 to 7, indigo gabbro is reasonably durable for everyday handling and ritual use. Cleanse with smoke, sound, moonlight, or by placing on a bed of selenite. Avoid prolonged water submersion until safety is confirmed.
