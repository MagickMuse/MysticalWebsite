Amblygonite's Journey

A rare stone often found alongside other lithium-rich minerals such as lepidolite and kunzite. Its name derives from the Greek for "obtuse angled," a nod to its triclinic structure. Parent crystals are often covered in triangular formations called record keepers — thought to be interdimensional track marks and evidence of extraterrestrial connection to Earth dating back to the Atlantean civilization. Several decades ago, amblygonite was used in an informal experiment in Brazil, gridded in classrooms for children with special needs. The results were remarkable. It brings focus when many distractions are pulling your energy in too many directions.

Crystal Healing with Amblygonite

Place amblygonite on the Solar Plexus Chakra to enhance personal power and motivation, helping see creative projects through to completion. Placed on the Crown Chakra, it brings calm and clarity when overwhelmed by too many ideas, and connects to higher-realm wisdom that bridges imagination and reality. For on-the-go support, a small piece can even be sewn into the seam of a favorite garment.

For the Advanced Practitioner

It also addresses fears surrounding personal power, whether rooted in fear of being taken advantage of or in concern about misusing one's own power. Additionally, it eases the lingering effects of trauma caused by others wielding power harmfully.
