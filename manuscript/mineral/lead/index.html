Symbolism

As a weighty metal, lead came to stand for heaviness and the weight of an oppressive burden. It is the attribute of Saturn, and the planet and the god alike share a morose temperament, the god pictured as a grim, hunched old man carrying a scythe, and from this comes the word saturnine. In alchemy lead is the base metal that practitioners hoped to transmute into gold. The struggle to achieve that change mirrored the alchemist's effort to free the self from the limits of the material world that this heavy metal represents.

Magical Lore

Lead has long been used in magic. In ancient Greek times, tablets of this metal were ritually charged and inscribed with "words of power." These tablets were generally used in negative spells because the lead ensured the spell's long continuance.

In India during the eleventh century, charms and figures designed to cause conception or to increase the fertility of gardens and orchards were engraved on lead tablets.

Lead is a heavy metal that causes death when absorbed by the body. The ancient Romans discovered this by using lead dishes and cooking utensils.

Divination

A curious divination, recorded in the 1800s in Italy: Take three rose seeds (remove them from the "hip" that forms after a rose has lost its petals), three nettle leaves, two rue leaves, and three cumin seeds. Put these on a metal plate along with a small quantity of lead.

At midnight, while clearing your mind of needless mental clutter, burn two yellow candles and light a fire. Place the metal plate over the fire. Then fill a large basin with water. Once the lead has melted, pour it, along with the herb ashes, into the water.

When the lead nodule has cooled, remove it from the water and gaze at its shape. The ritual and the lead itself should allow access to your psychic mind. If nothing comes to you, place the nodule beneath your pillow and let your dreams guide you.

Protection and Defensive Magic

Lead is worn or used in protective spells and also plays its part in defensive magic. It can be placed near the entrance of the house to prevent negativity from gaining access.

Safety Warning

⚠️ TOXIC: Lead is poisonous. Do not handle with bare hands for extended periods. Never ingest or create elixirs. Wash hands thoroughly after handling. Keep away from children and pets.

The Seven Magickal Metals

Lead is the metal of Saturn, one of the seven magickal metals that the ancients paired with the first seven planets. The full set links silver to the Moon, mercury to Mercury, copper to Venus, gold to the Sun, iron to Mars, tin to Jupiter, and lead to Saturn. In the Dharmic religions, sacred artifacts such as singing bowls and temple statuary are made from a blend of all seven metals.
