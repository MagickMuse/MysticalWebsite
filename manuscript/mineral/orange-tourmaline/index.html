Appearance

Orange, translucent to opaque, striated prismatic crystal.

Origin

Brazil, United States, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Madagascar.

Attributes

Orange Tourmaline enhances creativity, sexuality, and the emotional body. It stimulates the sacral chakra and brings warmth and vitality. This stone encourages an optimistic outlook and helps overcome fears of intimacy.

Healing

Orange Tourmaline aids the reproductive system and digestive tract.

Position

Place on the sacral chakra or wear as appropriate.

Spellworking

Orange Tourmaline brings warmth, creativity, and vitality. Use it for spells involving creative endeavors, sexuality, and emotional warmth. Like all orange stones, it stimulates the sacral chakra and helps overcome blocks to intimacy and creative expression.
