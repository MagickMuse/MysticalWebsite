Attributes

Amazonite has a powerful filtering action. At a physical level, it blocks geopathic stress, absorbs microwaves and cell phone emanations, and protects against electromagnetic pollution. It should be placed between you and the source of any pollution or taped to a cell phone. At a mental level, it filters the information passing through the brain and combines it with intuition.

This is an extremely soothing stone. It calms the brain and nervous system and aligns the physical body with the etheric, maintaining optimum health. It balances the masculine and feminine energies and many aspects of the personality. It is a stone that helps you to see both sides of a problem or different points of view. At an emotional level, Amazonite soothes emotional trauma, alleviating worry and fear. It dispels negative energy and aggravation.

Spiritually, an elixir of Amazonite is extremely beneficial to all levels of consciousness. The stone itself assists in manifesting universal love.

Psychologically, Amazonite has an affinity with sensitive people. It has the power to invoke tolerance of others. It overcomes judgmentalism, gives support to anyone who is overwhelmed by responsibility, and encourages taking responsibility for oneself. It creates a personality that is upright, persistent, and dynamic. It can break old, self-defeating programs.

Mentally, Amazonite calms the mind, removing extraneous thought. It filters information reaching the brain and clarifies perception, sharpens the intellect and clears up confusion. With its ability to bring unfinished business to a conclusion, Amazonite is useful for closure on all levels.

Spiritually, Amazonite sharpens intuition and opens clairvoyance. A wonderful stone for meditation, it invokes high states of consciousness and spiritual awareness and encourages service to humanity. Amazonite shields the aura and aligns the chakras, clearing the throat chakra and bringing communication from a higher plane.

Healing

Amazonite heals and opens both the heart and throat chakras to enhance loving communication. It also opens the third eye and intuition. The stone dissipates negative energy and blockages within the nervous system. It is beneficial in osteoporosis, tooth decay, calcium deficiency, and calcium deposits, balancing the metabolic deficiencies that create these conditions. The elixir rectifies calcium problems. Amazonite also relieves muscle spasms. A major property is the protection it affords from the health hazards of microwaves and other sources of electromagnetic smog.

Position

Hold or place over the affected point, or wear to protect against microwaves. Place near computers or tape to your cell phone.

Spellworking

Amazonite is a triclinic feldspar mineral named in 1847 after the Amazon River, where significant deposits were discovered. It has been carved into jewelry and amulets for over 4,000 years and has also been called Amazon Jade and Amazon Stone. Its blue-green color comes from trace amounts of lead within the crystal structure, and like all feldspars it carries a characteristic shimmer in natural light. Amazonite is a stone of truth, flow, and authentic communication, helping the bearer speak clearly and with conviction.

Chakra Work

Amazonite is most effective placed on the Throat Chakra to clear communication patterns, especially for those who hold back out of fear of judgment or conflict. It supports honest expression while keeping the speaker grounded and open to other perspectives. Carry it for public speaking to sharpen articulation and calm nerves. Keep it near computers, phones, or any devices, as it filters harmful geomagnetic frequencies in the environment.

For the Beginner: Amazonite soothes the nervous system and helps you say what you mean without becoming overly emotional. It supports authentic communication, helps you recognize when others are being dishonest, and encourages actions that match words in all relationships.

For the Advanced Practitioner: Amazonite is a powerful ally for setting firm yet realistic interpersonal boundaries and for breaking self-defeating habits. It illuminates how negative self-talk limits manifestation, and helps redirect thought patterns toward language that supports the creation of a reality aligned with your highest good. Pairs well with Aquamarine, Turquoise, Chrysocolla, Moldavite, Larimar, Aqua Aura Quartz, or Nuummite for deepened throat chakra work, truth speaking, and boundary setting.

Notable Specimen

An amazonite scarab beetle ring was discovered among the treasures of Tutankhamen's tomb, confirming the stone's long history of ceremonial use in ancient Egypt.

Personal Power

Amazonite is said to grant the strength and courage of the legendary Amazon warriors. North African magick ranks it among the most magickally potent materials of all. Worn or carried, it heightens personal power, psychic ability, and boldness, and it shields the bearer as well.

Magickal Message

"If you seek to find and clearly express your innermost thoughts and feelings, I am your ally. I am the Stone of Truth, so you can trust the intuitive messages, dreams, and gut feelings that emerge when you work with me. Speak your intentions aloud while you hold me close, and I will show you how to manifest your dreams."

Colors

Amazonite ranges from pale aqua to deep teal green, often with white veining or streaking from feldspar inclusions. The intensity of color varies by origin, with Russian specimens tending toward blue-green and Colorado specimens often displaying a more vivid teal. The shimmer characteristic of feldspar minerals gives amazonite a soft, light-catching quality.
