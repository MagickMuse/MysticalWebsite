A composite rock composed of three distinct minerals, unakite combines clear quartz, epidote, and pink feldspar into a single powerful gem. The pink feldspar encourages self-love, the epidote brings protection and supports manifestation, and the clear quartz amplifies the properties of both. First discovered in 1874 in the Unaka Range along the border of western North Carolina and eastern Tennessee, this monoclinic stone takes its name from those mountains. Together, its components create a unique heart-centered gem that offers support and safety during deep emotional healing.

Origin

Unakite is found in Australia, Brazil, China, South Africa, and the United States, where it was originally discovered in the Appalachian regions of North Carolina, Tennessee, and Virginia. Its composite nature reflects the geological processes that fuse quartz, epidote, and feldspar into a single cohesive matrix.

Appearance

A mottled, opaque stone displaying a distinctive blend of pistachio green (from epidote) and salmon pink (from feldspar), often interspersed with patches of clear or milky quartz. The colors create an organic, earthy mosaic that varies from specimen to specimen. Commonly found as tumbled stones with a smooth, waxy luster.

Magickal Properties

Unakite is a stone of vision that balances emotions with spirituality. Placed on the Third Eye, it opens psychic sight and promotes visualization. It also provides grounding after meditation or psychic work and brings a calm, gentle energy to any environment.

This gem helps reveal where emotions have been repressed, supporting gentle exploration of what lies beneath the surface. It builds emotional resilience, particularly for those who have faced grief or sorrow, and brings strength and harmony to relationships. Unakite reminds the practitioner to engage in nourishing self-care practices for body, mind, and spirit.

As a master heart healer, unakite facilitates rebirth and renewal to balance the emotional body. It gently releases any condition that prevents spiritual and psychological growth, clears deep-seated emotions, and lifts the spirits. This stone is especially valuable during recovery from major illness, reminding you to slow down and take care of yourself when the body has been neglected.

Unakite can also be used as a casting crystal for scrying, signifying where compromise and integration are needed. It works well in sets of ten to twelve stones kept together in a bag, with individual stones drawn to answer questions or cast onto a scrying wheel. Placed in the environment as a large piece or bowl of tumbled stones, it brings calm energy and can counter the effects of electromagnetic pollution.

Unakite facilitates rebirthing and past-life healing, bringing insights from the past to light so that blockages can be understood and released. Whether the root of imbalance originates in the distant or recent past, this stone reaches it at every level and brings it to the surface for transformation.

Chakra Work

Unakite is a Heart Chakra stone that brings forth energies of compassion and kindness. Wearing it as a long necklace that rests against the heart space cultivates those qualities within, allowing them to radiate outward to others. Keeping unakite close supports healing on physical, emotional, and spiritual levels, fostering trust in the process and faith that everything will be okay. It can also be placed on the Third Eye to open psychic vision and support past-life exploration.

Correspondences

Unakite partners well with Datolite, Dioptase, Green Calcite, Rose Quartz, Serpentine, and Variscite. It resonates with Scorpio.

Colors

Pistachio green, salmon pink, with patches of clear or milky quartz

Healing

Unakite supports convalescence and recovery from major illness. It treats the reproductive system, stimulates weight gain where needed, and aids healthy pregnancy and the growth of skin tissue and hair.

Magickal Message

"I am a stone of self-love, reminding you to treat yourself with extra loving care. I bring a renewal of patience, perseverance, and healing to your heart space."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 6 to 7, unakite is durable and suitable for daily wear, pocket carry, and ritual use. Safe in both sun and water. Cleanse with running water, smoke, moonlight, earth burial, or sound. Can also be applied as an elixir.
