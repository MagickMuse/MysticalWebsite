Alectorius

◇Notes/Warnings
Alectorius is a legendary stone known only from folklore. It has no mineralogical basis and was said to be found inside a bird rather than mined.

Alectorius is a mythical stone said to form inside a bird, which earns it the less elegant name of cock stone.

Appearance

It is described as pink, about the size of a bean, transparent, and crystalline.

Origin

The finest examples were said to come from a bird that had been caponed at the age of three and slaughtered at the age of seven. At that age the bird was believed to refuse all food and drink, a sign that the stone within had ripened.

Magickal Properties

Because it was thought to absorb the bird's pugnacity, the alectorius was the favored charm of wrestlers. Held in the mouth, it quenched thirst and invigorated the fighter. It was also said to grant its owner eloquence, while making his wife quieter and more compliant. The stone was believed to make its owner courageous and invincible, alluring and seductive, and virile and fertile. Should it fail to confer these qualities, it was thought, most usefully, to render its owner invisible.
