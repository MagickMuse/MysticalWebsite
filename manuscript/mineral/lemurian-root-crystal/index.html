Lemurian Root Crystal is a rare variety of quartz in the Hexagonal/Trigonal crystal system with a Mohs hardness of 7, sourced primarily from Brazil.

Appearance

Lemurian Root Crystals are distinguished by their horizontal striations running along the shaft, referred to as the "stairway to heaven" in metaphysical circles. These markings are believed to contain encoded information from the ancient Lemurian civilization. The crystals display the classic quartz form with a vitreous luster and transparent to translucent clarity. Lemurian quartz occurs in multiple color variations: golden specimens carry high iron content, while pink varieties are rich in lithium deposits.

Origin

Brazil

Magickal Properties

Lemurian Root Crystals are considered master teachers within the mineral kingdom. Ancient Lemurian elders are said to have programmed these stones to hold, send, and receive information, encoding their collective wisdom into the crystal lattice. The intention was that when humanity reached a state of readiness, these root crystals would release stored knowledge to those prepared to receive and transmit it.

These crystals anchor the planet's universal light grid and serve as conduits for accessing ancient light codes. They activate a deeper knowing within the bearer, recalling paths previously walked and lessons previously learned to prevent old patterns from recycling into new behavior. They expand consciousness, accelerate personal learning, and support planetary ascension.

For beginners, Lemurian Root Crystals bring clarity through their high vibrations of spiritual awareness. They relieve stress, open the mind to hidden wisdom, support dream recall, and calm the central nervous system.

Chakra Work

Lemurian Root Crystals resonate most powerfully with the Crown Chakra and Soul Star Chakra, where they facilitate connection to higher dimensional wisdom, past life awareness, and universal consciousness. During meditation, focusing the Third Eye Chakra while tracing the crystal's striations activates new levels of encoded knowledge with each session.

As a quartz variety, Lemurian Root Crystal is safe for water cleansing and tolerates sun exposure. Cleanse by running under cool water or placing on a selenite plate. Charge under moonlight or beside Clear Quartz clusters. Handle with care when tracing the striations during meditation to preserve the encoded lines over time.
