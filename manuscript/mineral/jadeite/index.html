💎Gem Elixir Guide

A monoclinic mineral and one of two distinct species classified as jade, jadeite is a stone of abundance, centering, and heart healing. Revered in ancient China as the most beautiful natural material on Earth, it was carved into ritual objects as early as the Neolithic period around 3500 BCE. In Mayan culture it was honored as the Sovereign of Harmony, valued for its ability to bring peace within the individual and across communities.

Origin

Jadeite is a sodium-rich pyroxene mineral found across a wide geographic range including Afghanistan, Australia, Austria, Canada, China, Cuba, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Myanmar, New Zealand, Poland, Russia, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, Turkey, Ukraine, the United States, Vietnam, and Zimbabwe. Myanmar produces some of the finest specimens.

Appearance

A translucent to opaque stone most commonly seen in shades of green, though it occurs in white, lavender, yellow, orange, red, and black. Jadeite has a vitreous to greasy luster and a slightly waxy feel. It is denser and harder than nephrite, the other jade mineral.

Magickal Properties

Jadeite transmutes negativity and instills resourcefulness. It activates the Heart Chakra, promoting tranquil and harmonious relationships while dispelling negative energy and encouraging self-reflection. Worn against the heart space, it delivers a steady flow of nurturing earth energy that supports well-being and heart healing. It draws the practitioner into deeper connection with the natural world, cultivating gratitude for life's simple gifts.

This stone promotes all forms of abundance, from financial prosperity and fertility to emotional richness. It teaches mastery over money rather than allowing money to control you, and it can help preserve wealth received through gifts or inheritance. Jadeite encourages enjoyment of the body's physical senses and pulls awareness firmly into the present moment, filling it with joy and comfort.

For the advanced practitioner, jadeite sharpens attunement to a client's energetic needs during healing sessions, highlighting the most critical issues first. It connects to the wisdom of the spiritual plane and brings clarity of vision for strategic planning. It fosters self-sufficiency and confidence by aligning the practitioner with universal energies that are limitless and always supportive.

South American jadeite is said to open a channel to the elders of the ancient Mayan civilization, granting access to the energies of their traditional rituals and ceremonies.

Chakra Work

Jadeite works primarily through the Heart Chakra. Wear it as a long necklace so it rests against the heart space, or place it directly over the chest during meditation to activate its healing and nurturing qualities.

Correspondences

Jadeite partners well with Black Tourmaline, Carnelian, Copper, Garnet, Jet, Onyx, and Red Jasper. It resonates with Aries, Taurus, Gemini, and Libra.

Colors

Green (primary), white, lavender, yellow, orange, red, black

Magickal Message

"Allow me to charm your Heart Chakra to bring you boundless joy and abundance. The earth's abundance and vitality support you as you consciously create space to manifest all your desires."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 6.5 to 7, jadeite is durable and well suited for daily wear, jewelry, and ritual use. Cleanse with water, smoke, moonlight, or sound. Safe in sunlight for short periods.
