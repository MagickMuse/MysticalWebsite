An isometric metal and one of the rarest elements on Earth, iridium is a powerful stone of ascension, spiritual development, and expanded consciousness. Discovered in 1803 within the residue of platinum ore, it is believed to have been deposited across the planet by a massive asteroid impact, the same event some scientists link to the extinction of the dinosaurs. It is the most corrosion-resistant metal known to science and is now primarily recovered as a by-product of nickel refining.

Origin

Iridium occurs in trace amounts within the Earth's crust, distributed globally by ancient asteroid impact. Primary sources include Australia, Brazil, Myanmar, Russia, South Africa, and the United States (Alaska).

Appearance

A hard, silver-colored metal with a brilliant, reflective surface. Its name is the root of the word "iridescence," a quality it shares with the wings of dragonflies.

Magickal Properties

Iridium activates the Soul Star Chakra, opening a gateway to other realms and dimensions. It encourages ascension and deepens spiritual development while channeling universal energies to support manifestation. In meditation, iridium dissolves the boundaries of time and space, revealing that past, present, and future exist simultaneously. It teaches the practitioner to integrate this expanded consciousness in service of the highest good.

This mineral brings embodied peace and fosters both freedom and individuality, allowing you to simply be yourself. It sharpens the ability to process complex or seemingly illogical information, helping organize knowledge in ways that are easier to retain and apply.

For the advanced practitioner, iridium is deeply transformative. Dragonflies, whose iridescent wings take their name from this element, symbolize rebirth, transformation, imagination, light, and color magick. Both dragonfly and mineral remind us that we are light beings capable of bending, shifting, reflecting, and shining. Iridium reveals every face of the Divine present in all beings and energy streams, integrating that wisdom within the practitioner.

Chakra Work

Iridium works primarily through the Soul Star Chakra, the energy center located above the crown that serves as the gateway to higher dimensions and cosmic consciousness. Place or hold iridium above the head during meditation to activate this chakra and encourage spiritual ascension.

Correspondences

Iridium partners well with Amethyst, Copper, Libyan Gold Tektite, Platinum, and Zincite. It resonates with Cancer and Leo.

Colors

Magickal Message

"By acknowledging your own sacred nature, you open yourself to receiving wisdom from beyond the scope of time and space. The purpose of life is to learn continually, evolve, and progress so that you can reconnect with the collective of the cosmos. Allow me to be your ally and guide you on this path."

Care

With a Mohs hardness of 6 to 7, iridium is durable enough for regular handling and ritual use. Due to its extreme rarity and value (nearly $5,000 per ounce), specimens should be stored carefully. Cleanse with smoke, sound, or moonlight.
