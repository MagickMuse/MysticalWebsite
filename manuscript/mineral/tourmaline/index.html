Attributes

Natural Tourmaline wands are useful healing tools. They clear the aura, remove blockages, disperse negative energy, and point to solutions for specific problems. They are excellent for balancing and connecting the chakras. At a physical level, they rebalance the meridians.

Tourmaline has a strong affinity with the devic energies and is extremely beneficial for the garden and plants. It can act as a natural insecticide, keeping pests at bay, and, buried in the soil, encourages the growth and health of all crops.

Psychologically, Tourmaline aids in understanding oneself and others, taking you deep into yourself, promoting self-confidence and diminishing fear. It banishes any feeling of being a victim and attracts inspiration, compassion, tolerance, and prosperity.

Tourmaline is a powerful mental healer, balancing the right-left hemispheres of the brain and transmuting negative thought patterns into positive ones. This stone brings the mental processes, the chakras, and the biomagnetic sheath into alignment. It is helpful in treating paranoia, and for overcoming dyslexia, as it improves hand-to-eye coordination and the assimilation and translation of coded information.

Emotionally, Red, Yellow, and Brown Tourmalines are beneficial for sexuality and the emotional dysfunction that can lie behind loss of libido. Physically, Tourmaline releases tension, which makes it helpful in spinal adjustments. It balances male-female energy within the body.

The striations along the side of Tourmaline enhance energy flow, making it an excellent stone for healing, energy enhancement, and removal of blockages. Each of the different colors of Tourmaline has its own specific healing ability.

Spellworking

Tourmaline was unknown to ancient magicians and is today still little used in magic, though its popularity is increasing.

Tourmaline is a unique stone in many ways. It is transparent when viewed from the side of the crystal, opaque from either end. When heated or rubbed to create friction, it polarizes—one end becomes positive and attracts ashes or light straws, the other negative. This polarizing quality makes it useful for balancing energies.

The stone comes in a variety of colors, each with its own magical attributes. Some crystals possess two or three hues:

  • Pink tourmaline (receptive, Venus, Water): Draws love and friendship. Wear to promote sympathy toward others.
  • Red tourmaline/Rubellite (projective, Mars, Fire): Worn to lend energy to the body. Used in protective rituals. Promotes courage and strengthens the will.
  • Green tourmaline (receptive, Venus, Earth): Draws money and success in business. Place one in a piggy bank or coin purse. Stimulates creativity.
  • Blue tourmaline/Indicolite (receptive, Venus, Water): Wear to de-stress, for peace and restful sleep.
  • Black tourmaline/Schorl (receptive, Saturn, Earth): Used for grounding and to represent earth in elemental spells. Absorbs negativity when charged via visualization.
  • Watermelon tourmaline (projective/receptive, Mars/Venus, Fire/Water): Balances projective and receptive energies within the body. Love-attracting, works best when used by balanced persons.

Put one near your computer to dissolve electromagnetic fields.

Folklore

An Egyptian legend held that tourmaline gathered its many colors from a rainbow as it traveled up through the depths of the earth toward the surface, and for this it was held sacred to the Sun God Ra. Its wealth of hues also earned it a wide array of names. Pliny the Elder described a stone he called lychnis, meaning lamp, which when warmed by the Sun or by friction could draw straws and other light fibers toward it. The ancient Greeks used tourmaline to help kindle their lamps, and Dutch colonists in Africa, one of the places the stone is found, used tourmaline crystals to pull the ash from their meerschaum pipes.
