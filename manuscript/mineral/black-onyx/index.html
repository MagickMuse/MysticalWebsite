🖤Black Crystal Shield

Appearance

Solid black, banded chalcedony. Often polished or tumbled. Note that much commercial black onyx is dyed.

Origin

Brazil, India, United States, Germany, Uruguay

Attributes

Black Onyx is a variety of chalcedony that is traditionally banded, though solid black specimens (often dyed) are common in the market. It is a powerful protection stone that absorbs and transforms negative energy. Black Onyx provides strength and support during difficult circumstances.

This stone aids in developing emotional and physical strength and stamina, especially when under stress. It centers your energy and aligns it with higher guidance. Black Onyx helps you become master of your own destiny.

Healing

Black Onyx is beneficial for teeth, bones, bone marrow, blood disorders, and the feet. It aids in the absorption of nutrients.

Position

Wear on the left side of the body or place as appropriate.

Spellworking

Use this black stone to banish negative energy and ground magick spells. It encourages self-confidence and determination. Black Onyx helps you break deeply ingrained habits. Wear it when facing adversaries in figurative or literal battles. As an opaque stone, it's particularly effective for physical matters and protection.

Dark Magick Applications

Black onyx is used to inflict nightmares, mental torment, and to shatter relationships. If you are partnered or living with someone and discover stray pieces of black onyx appearing around your home, someone may be working to drive you apart or pull one of you away. Defensively, wearing black onyx can also shield against controlling "love" workings such as those involving hyraceum.
