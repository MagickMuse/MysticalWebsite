Attributes

Smoky Amethyst combines the grounding properties of Smoky Quartz with the spiritual qualities of Amethyst.

Spiritually, this stone facilitates grounding spiritual energy into the physical realm. It protects during spiritual work while maintaining connection to higher dimensions.

Psychologically, Smoky Amethyst assists in releasing addictive patterns and compulsive behaviors.

Mentally, it brings mental clarity while grounding scattered thoughts.

Emotionally, this crystal provides emotional protection and helps in processing difficult emotions.

Physically, Smoky Amethyst grounds high-frequency energy into the body.

Healing

Beneficial for stress, anxiety, addiction recovery, and grounding.

Position

Place as appropriate; hold during meditation; wear for protection.

Additional Notes

Warning: Fades in direct sunlight. Keep away from prolonged sun exposure.

Source: The Crystal Bible 3 by Judy Hall
