🔮Dreaming of Crystal Balls

Appearance

Usually shaped from a larger piece of crystal. May have planes or flaws within them. Perfectly round, polished smooth. Crystal balls can be clear (the traditional stereotype) or come in various colors. The crucial element is a smooth surface into which the user may scry.

Properties

Spheres emit energy equally in all directions into the environment. They radiate energy outward in a balanced, harmonious way. Crystal balls derive from the ancient tradition of lunar gazing—either gazing directly at the moon or into a basin of water reflecting the moon. Thus they are associated with the feminine, lunar element of water.

Uses

Used as a window to the past or future, they move energy through time and provide a glimpse of what is to come or what has been, a practice called scrying. One gazes into the crystal ball until images appear, whether in the ball itself or one's mind's eye. They are used for divination, spirit-summoning, and shamanic communication with other realms. Excellent for meditation and energy work. Can be placed in a room to emit continuous gentle energy in all directions.

Mastery and Practice

Scrying is an entirely intuitive, shamanic process. A beginner can read Tarot cards with an instructional guidebook in hand, but this is not the case with a crystal ball. One may gaze into a crystal ball for weeks, months, or even years before images dependably appear. This difficulty, combined with their cost as an investment piece, makes crystal balls less popular than Tarot cards or runes for divination.

Care and Maintenance

Traditionally, crystal balls are kept covered when not in use. They are cleansed using incense smoke or by careful cleansing with magical washes, usually herb-infused spring water or spring water to which flower essences have been added.

Additional Notes

For centuries Quartz, Obsidian, and Beryl have been carefully shaped by hand as scrying tools. Seers gaze into their depths to ascertain the future. Nineteenth- and early twentieth-century images of witches and fortune-tellers often portrayed them gazing into crystal balls. While the image has somewhat fallen out of fashion, crystal balls remain potent magical tools. Make excellent hand comforters to use in times of stress.

Magickal Properties

Because spheres broadcast rather than focus, their output is more diffuse than that of a generator or wand. This makes them less suited to targeted workings but ideal for filling an entire space with a consistent energetic quality over time. An amethyst or blue calcite sphere placed in a home will gently circulate harmonious energy throughout the environment. Practitioners who prefer a tighter energetic experience typically choose generators or wands instead.
