Celery (Apium graveolens) is a biennial vegetable with crisp stalks and compound leaves.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Moon

Element: Water

Powers: Protection, Love

Magickal Uses

For protection: Work with celery in protective sachets and workings, drawing on its lunar and water energies.

For love: Use in love workings and sachets.

For cramps: History records that some ate celery to prevent cramps.

As an aphrodisiac: Celery is considered by some to be an aphrodisiac, and was historically mixed with other herbs for that purpose.

Cultivated Varieties

Apium graveolens divides into several cultivated forms, each grown for a different part of the plant.

  • Celery seed is the form distilled for essential-oil work, drawn from the whole or crushed seeds. See Celery Seed Essential Oil.
  • Celeriac (A. graveolens var. rapaceum) is the swollen-rooted form grown for kitchen use.
  • Salad celery (A. graveolens var. dulce) is the familiar crisp-stalked vegetable.

The oil itself is principally produced in India, with secondary distillation in Holland, China, Hungary, and the United States.

Folk Medicine

The seed has long been used for bladder and kidney complaints, digestive upsets, and menstrual irregularity, while the leaves are turned to for skin ailments. Celery is known to increase the elimination of uric acid, which makes it useful in gout, neuralgia, and rheumatoid arthritis. It is also a quiet remedy for hepatobiliary disorders, with a documented regenerating effect on the liver. The British Herbal Pharmacopoeia lists celery seed specifically for rheumatoid arthritis paired with mental depression.
