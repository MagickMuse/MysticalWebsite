Aspen, a member of the poplar family, dislikes the shade of other trees and so often stands alone, favoring damper ground.

Characteristics

True to the "trembling" in its Latin name, the aspen is rarely still: its leaves stir at the slightest provocation, lending the tree a shimmering look and earning it the folk names "shaking" and "quivering" tree.

Folk Tradition

The aspen's ceaseless movement made it especially prized for phyllomancy, divination by the whispering of leaves in the wind. Legend holds that the tree trembles because its hearing is so acute that it reacts without end to sounds from far and wide, passing word to anyone with the skill to understand it. Because of these communicative gifts the aspen is linked to the god Mercury, and a leaf placed beneath the tongue is said to confer the gift of eloquence.

Magickal Uses

A tree of old age and resurrection, the aspen rules the Autumn Equinox. Its wood was used to fashion shields in antiquity, carrying its protective qualities into battle. Work with aspen for knowledge-seeking, past-life and reincarnation themes, and breaking or reversing curses.
