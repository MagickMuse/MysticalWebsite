Bladderwrack (Fucus vesiculosus) is a brown seaweed with distinctive air bladders.

Magical Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Protection, Sea Spells, Wind Spells, Money, Psychic Powers

Folk Names

Bladder Fucus, Cutweed, Kelp, Sea Spirit, Seawrack, Seetang, Meeriche, Sea Oak, Black Tang

Magical Uses

For protection: Seaweeds generally give protection to those at sea, or those who are flying over it. Therefore, intercontinental travellers should carry this plant.

For sea magic: It is used in sea spells to summon the spirits of the sea, by throwing it onto the waves and calling them. This will gain their favor and they will aid your magic.

For wind spells: Kelp is used to summon the winds: Stand on the shore and hold a long strand of the fresh seaweed in your hand, whip it around in the air above your head in a clockwise direction and whistle. The winds will come.

For money: Make an infusion of the plant and scrub the floors and doors of a place of business to attract customers and bring good vibrations into the store. Also use in all money spells. A particularly potent spell entails simply filling a small jar with whiskey, placing some kelp in it, capping tightly, and placing this in the kitchen window. This will ensure a steady flow of money into the household.

For psychic powers: Bladderwrack is also used in sachets to increase psychic powers and is carried to protect against mental derangement.

For hexing: Though primarily a protective botanical, bladderwrack placed hidden in a bathroom frequented by an enemy is said to cause urinary tract infections.

⚠️ Safety Note: Not recommended for internal use if pregnant, nursing, or if you have hyperthyroidism.

USDA Hardiness Zones do not apply to Fucus vesiculosus, a marine brown alga (seaweed). USDA zones are based on average annual minimum air temperatures for terrestrial plants. Bladderwrack is native to the cold and temperate intertidal zones of the North Atlantic and North Pacific.
