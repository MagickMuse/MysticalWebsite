Compass Plant (Silphium laciniatum) is a tall prairie plant whose leaves orient north-south.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Mercury
  • Element: Air
  • Powers: Direction, Finding, Truth

Folk Names

Pilot Weed, Rosinweed

Magickal Uses

For finding direction: The compass plant is used in spells to find one's way, both literally and metaphorically. Carry the leaves when you need guidance.

For truth-seeking: Use in divination to reveal hidden truths or find lost objects.

Storm Warding

For travelers: Carry compass plant to ensure you always find your way home safely.

The dried root burned during electrical storms traditionally averts lightning strikes. This weather-protective use reflects the plant's elemental connection to direction and elemental forces.

Meaning When Gifted

When compass plant is gifted it carries the meaning of faith.
