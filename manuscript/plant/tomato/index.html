Magickal Properties

Powers: Prosperity, Protection, Love

Ritual Uses

None specified in the source text.

Magickal Uses

  • Prosperity charm: Place a large red tomato on the mantle to bring prosperity to the home. Replace every three days.
  • Warding: Place a tomato on the windowsill or other household entrance to repel evil from entering.
  • Garden protection: Plants in the garden are protective; yellow flowers and bright red fruits scare off evil.
  • Love: When eaten, the tomato inspires love.

Aphrodisiac Properties

The tomato earned its folk name "Love Apple" from a chain of misinterpretation: the Italian Pomo Dor (golden apple) was confused with Pomo d'Moro (Moor's apple), which the French then romantically reinterpreted as Pomme d'Amour (apple of love).

The Suspicious Love Apple

When the tomato reached Europe from the Americas in the sixteenth century, its red color and succulent flesh made it an object of deep suspicion. A sensual fruit packed with countless seeds, it was named the love apple and held to be an aphrodisiac, and for that reason the Church frowned on it as a lewd and improper fruit.

A Sexual Symbol in Africa

The tomato carries the same sexual symbolism in parts of Africa. In Bambara lands couples eat tomatoes before making love, and women make offerings of them to the god Faro.

La Tomatina

The largest tomato fight in the world, La Tomatina, is held every August in the town of Bunol, near Valencia, in Spain.
