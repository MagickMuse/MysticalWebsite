🌱Squill Root Hex-Breaker💰Squill Root Money Spell

Squill is a powerful bulbous plant known as the "sea onion" with potent magical properties for protection, money drawing, and hex-breaking. This Mediterranean plant has been used in magic since classical times, particularly for home protection and breaking unwanted spells. The bulb is especially effective when used to ward off negative influences.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Money, Protection, Hex-Breaking

Magickal Uses

  • Home Protection: Hang a squill over the window to protect your home from negative influences and unwanted intrusions.
  • Money Drawing: Place one in a jar or box and add silver coins to attract wealth and financial prosperity.
  • Hex-Breaking: If you feel you have been hexed, carry a squill with you and it will break the spell and neutralize negative magic sent against you.
  • Classical Magic: The squill has been used in magickal practice since ancient times, making it one of the traditional protective herbs.

This humble bulb packs considerable magical power and has proven its effectiveness across centuries of use in folk magick traditions.
