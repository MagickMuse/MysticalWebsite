Celandine (Chelidonium majus) is a perennial herb with deeply lobed leaves and bright yellow flowers.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Planet: Sun
  • Element: Fire
  • Powers: Protection, Escape, Happiness, Legal Matters

Folk Names

Devil's Milk, Garden Celandine, Greater Celandine, Swallowwort

Magickal Uses

Gathering timing: According to old herbals, celandine should be gathered when the Sun is in Leo and the Moon is in Aries for maximum potency.

For protection and escape: Celandine aids in escaping unwarranted imprisonment and entrapments of every kind. Wear next to the skin and replace every three days.

For legal matters: The juice, when mixed with the milk of a black cow and placed on a gold ring, helps in legal matters when worn.

For joy and depression: Celandine imparts joy and good spirits when worn. It is said to cure depression.

Meaning When Gifted

When celandine is gifted it carries the meaning of future joy.
