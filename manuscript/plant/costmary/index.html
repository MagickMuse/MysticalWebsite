Costmary (Tanacetum balsamita) is a fragrant perennial herb with large, silvery-green leaves.

Botanical Information

Native to the Mediterranean region and western Asia. The plant has long, oval leaves with a distinctive balsamic, minty scent. Historically known as "Bible Leaf" because pressed leaves were used as fragrant bookmarks in Bibles and prayer books. Also called "Alecost" for its traditional use in flavoring ale before hops became standard.

Magickal Uses

For prosperity: Costmary's Jovian rulership makes it excellent for prosperity workings. The balsamic leaves are used in sachets to draw wealth and abundance.

For success: Include in success sachets and blends; the warm, balsamic odor of the leaves draws favorable outcomes.

For dreams: Add to dream pillows to induce vivid, memorable dreams. The balsamic scent promotes restful sleep while enhancing dream recall.

For love: Include in love sachets and charm bags. The sweet, herbaceous fragrance attracts romantic attention.
