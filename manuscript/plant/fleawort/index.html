Fleawort (Plantago psyllium) is an herb whose seeds have both magical and medicinal applications.

Botanical Information

Fleawort is native to the Mediterranean region and western Asia. The plant produces small seeds that are mucilaginous when wet. The seeds have been used medicinally as a gentle laxative and are the source of commercial psyllium fiber.

Magickal Properties

Fleawort seeds are traditionally used in divination and prophetic work. The seeds are burned as incense to enhance psychic abilities and promote prophetic visions. Their association with prophecy likely stems from ancient Mediterranean magical traditions.

Ritual Uses

Burn the seeds as part of divination incense. Add to prophetic dream blends. Use in rituals designed to enhance psychic perception and receive visions.
