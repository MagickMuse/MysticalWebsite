Asphodel (Asphodelus spp.) is a modest, very ancient flower long associated with the Underworld in Greek mythology.

Folk Tradition

A crown of asphodel blossoms was worn by Persephone, the Queen of the Dead. The Elysian Fields, the marshy meadows where the spirits of the dead wandered, were carpeted with asphodel flowers. The flower can be eaten, and its bulb was made into a kind of bread said to be enjoyed by both the living and the dead.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Protection

Magickal Uses

Asphodel is a symbol of protection. The blossoms are fashioned into crosses to strengthen this protective meaning.

Meaning When Gifted

When asphodel is gifted it carries the meaning of languor, regret, and death.
