Gardenia (Gardenia jasminoides, G. spp.) is a fragrant flowering shrub with glossy leaves and waxy white or yellow blooms.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Moon

Powers: Love, Peace, Healing, Spirituality

Magickal Uses

For healing: The fresh blossoms are placed in sickrooms or on healing altars to aid in the process. The dried petals are also added to healing incenses and mixtures.

For peace: Dried gardenia is scattered around a room to induce peaceful vibrations and is also added to Moon incenses.

For love and spirituality: Gardenias are used in love spells, and to attract good spirits during rituals. They are possessed of very high spiritual vibrations.

To attract love: Wear the fresh flower to attract love and new friends. Dry and crush the petals, mix with ground orris root, and lightly dust the body to attract the opposite sex.

As a Moon link: Use gardenia as a link with the Moon in lunar workings.

Botanical Description

Gardenia is a decorative bush, often grown for ornamental purposes, bearing fragrant white flowers.

Origin & Distribution

Native to the Far East, India, and China. Efforts to produce the essential oil commercially have been largely unsuccessful, and almost all gardenia oil on the market today is synthetically produced.

Related Species

There are several varieties of gardenia depending on location, including G. grandiflora, G. radicans, and G. florida, as well as G. citriodora and G. calyculata, the latter two found in Japan and Indonesia.

Folk Tradition

The flowers are used locally to flavour tea, much like jasmine.

Meaning When Gifted

When gardenia is gifted it carries the meaning of "you're lovely," secret love, purity, and refinement.
