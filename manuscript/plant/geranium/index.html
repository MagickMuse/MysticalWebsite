Geranium (Pelargonium spp.) is a popular flowering plant with colorful blooms and scented leaves, valued for its protective and fertility-enhancing properties.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold)

Planet: Mars (existing entry had Venus)

Powers: Love, Healing, Protection, Fertility

Magickal Uses

For potpourri: The red petals are added to potpourris for color.

For perfumery and sachets: Leaves of scented geraniums are used in perfumery and sachets. Alum Root (wild geranium) is used similarly.

For love: Wear the flowers or add them to love sachets.

By color: The white variety is worn to promote fertility; the red are good for protection and aid in healing. Plant pink and red varieties in the garden to protect the house and keep snakes away.

For protection: Geraniums of all types are protective when grown in the garden or brought into the home freshly cut and placed into water. The geranium protects against snakes, for: "Snakes will not go where geraniums grow."

For divination: A plot of red geraniums, planted near the Witch's cottage, told of coming visitors by their movements. The flowers were magically charged to point to the direction of the approaching strangers and thus warn the Witch of their impending arrival.

For health: Banks or pots of red geraniums are quite protective, and strengthen health.

For love spells: Pink-flowered geraniums are used in love spells, while the white varieties increase fertility.

For healing: Curanderos in contemporary Mexico cleanse and heal patients by brushing them with red geraniums, together with fresh rue and pepper tree branches.

For protection sachets: The rose geranium (Pelargonium graveolens) with its highly scented leaves, is used in protection sachets, or the fresh leaves are rubbed onto doorknobs and windows to protect them.

All of the scented geraniums have various magickal properties, most of which can be deduced from the scent (nutmeg, lemon, peppermint, etc.) Nutmeg-scented geraniums possess much the same powers as nutmeg, and so on.

Against evil spirits: Geranium is a favorite house and garden plant not only because it is bright, cheerful and hardy, but because it magickally repels evil spirits. Plant it to hold protective boundaries, or place individual plants where they are needed.

Folk Tradition (Medicinal)

The British plant herb robert (Geranium robertianum) and the American cranesbill (G. maculatum) are the most widely used true geraniums in herbal medicine, having been used since antiquity. They share many properties with rose geranium, being used for dysentery, haemorrhoids, inflammations, metrorrhagia, and menorrhagia. The root and herb of cranesbill is specifically indicated in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia for diarrhoea and peptic ulcer.

Related Species

There are over 700 cultivated varieties of geranium and pelargonium. Oil-producing species include Pelargonium graveolens (the primary commercial source), P. odorantissimumP. radens. The medicinal true geraniums of the Geranium genus, including herb robert (G. robertianum) and cranesbill (G. maculatum), are distinct from the Pelargonium species used in perfumery and aromatherapy.
