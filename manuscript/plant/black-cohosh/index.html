Magickal Uses

Black cohosh, popularly called black snake root, is among the botanicals believed to carry power similar to that of a serpent, or even to let the practitioner safely tap into true snake power. Its blackness adds to its protective nature, since black is an important color of protection, believed to absorb all dangers and render them harmless. The root guards against every kind of trouble, whether spiritual, magickal, or simply human, and offers particular magickal protection against threats of violence and lingering abusive situations.

Cultivation

After black cohosh became popular therapeutically, alongside echinacea and goldenseal, it was harvested nearly to extinction in the wild. Grow your own supply; the living plant is also full of magickal protection.
