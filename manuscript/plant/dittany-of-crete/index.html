Dittany of Crete (Origanum dictamnus) is a rare aromatic herb with fuzzy white leaves and pink flowers.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (Cold)

Planet: Mercury (source) / Venus (existing)

Element: Earth / Air (source) / Water (existing)

Powers: Divination, Manifestation, Astral Projection

Magickal Uses

As a fumigation herb: Dittany of Crete is a powerful magickal fumigation herb.

For evocation: It is an excellent base for evocation manifestations when burned as incense; spirits are said to be able to materialize in its smoke.

For manifestations: Dittany of Crete is burned to conjure spirits and to aid in astral projection. A pinch is added to other incenses to increase their power.

For spirit work: The fresh or dried herb, when burned, helps spirits to materialize so that they can be seen. For this reason it is used extensively in modern pagan ceremonies.

For disease demon dispelling: Burn Dittany of Crete as incense to drive away illness-provoking spirits.

For wish manifestation: Make a wish while inhaling the fragrance of Dittany of Crete. Allegedly this encourages your dreams to manifest into reality.
