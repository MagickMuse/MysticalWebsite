🌸Dreaming of Dahlias

Dahlia (Dahlia pinnata) is a tuberous perennial with showy, layered blooms in nearly every color but true blue. Native to the highlands of Mexico and Central America, the dahlia is the national flower of Mexico and was cultivated by the Aztecs both as an ornamental and as food.

Magickal Properties

Element: Water

Powers: Good Fortune, Dignity, Elegance, Commitment

Magickal Uses

For good fortune: Fresh, vibrant dahlias carry the energy of incoming good luck. Place a fresh-cut bloom on the altar or near the door to draw fortune into the home.

For dignity and elegance: The dahlia's layered, symmetrical bloom is a symbol of inner strength carried with grace. Carry a dried petal or wear the flower to embody composure under pressure.

For commitment: Long associated with lasting bonds, dahlias may be added to handfasting bouquets, anniversary workings, or any spell meant to deepen a promise already made.

For the kitchen witch: The tubers are edible with a flavor between potato and water chestnut. Cook them into grounding, prosperity-drawing dishes to anchor good fortune in the body.

Meaning When Gifted

When dahlia is gifted it carries the meaning of dignity and elegance and a promise of "forever thine," though it can also hint at instability.
