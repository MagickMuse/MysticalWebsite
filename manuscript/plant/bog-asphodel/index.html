Magickal Uses

Bog Asphodel is sacred to Persephone and the Underworld. It was said that this herb protected one from sorcery and the works of ill-intentioned practitioners.

Use in protective workings against curses and hostile magic, drawing on its deep connection to the chthonic realm and its sovereign, Persephone.

☠️
POISONOUS: Bog Asphodel is toxic. Toxic to livestock, causing photosensitization and organ damage. Not for internal use.
