Botanical Information

Citrus × paradisi (Rutaceae family). Masculine fruit tree originating from the West Indies and Central America. A modern hybrid of sweet orange (Citrus sinensis) and pomelo (Citrus maxima).

Herbal Lore

Grapefruit lacks the deep historical lore of ancient plants due to its recent hybrid origin. Its magickal properties inherit the combined legacy of both parent fruits, sweet orange and pomelo, creating a new solar dynasty with unique energetic signatures for modern magickal practice.

Botanical Source Detail

  • Scientific name: Citrus × paradisi
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Type: Cultivated tree, often over ten metres high, with glossy leaves and large yellow fruits
  • Origin story: Believed to have derived from the shaddock (C. grandis); recognised as a recent hybrid of C. maxima and C. sinensis.

Distribution

Native to tropical Asia and the West Indies. Cultivated in California, Florida, Brazil, and Israel. The essential oil is mainly produced in California.

Cultivars

Many different cultivars exist; the 'Duncan' cultivar is standard in Florida.

Folk Tradition

Grapefruit shares the nutritional qualities of other citrus species, being high in Vitamin C and serving as a valuable protection against infectious illness.

Therapeutic Actions

Antiseptic, antitoxic, astringent, bactericidal, diuretic, depurative, lymphatic stimulant, digestive stimulant, and tonic.
