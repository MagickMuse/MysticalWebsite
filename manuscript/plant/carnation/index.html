Carnation (Dianthus caryophyllus) is a perennial flowering plant with ruffled, fragrant flowers.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Sun

Powers: Protection, Strength, Energy, Luck, Healing

Magickal Uses

  • Protection: In past times, carnations were worn during coronations and other important ceremonies to give protection and strength. They were once worn by witches to prevent untimely death on the scaffold.
  • Energy: Carnations are placed on the altar to produce added energy and used in power incenses. For a tremendously powerful energy incense: dry nine red carnations in the Sun, crumble them free from the stems, pour one dram of carnation oil over them, mix well, and burn slowly on charcoal.
  • Healing: The flowers are used in healing spells, and when placed on a sick person they speed recovery. Carnations make an excellent gift for the sick to aid in their recovery.
  • Goddess offering: Carnations are traditionally used as offerings to honor the Goddess.

Folk Medicine (Culpeper)

According to Culpeper, the tincture of clove pink is used for headaches and other nervous disorders. It is also used to cleanse the blood and as a remedy for gout and all aches and pains of the joints. The herb promotes perspiration during fevers.

Meaning When Gifted

When carnation is gifted it carries the meaning of fascination and admiration.

  • Pink: I'll never forget you, a woman's love
  • Red: My heart aches for you, admiration
  • Purple: Capriciousness
  • White: Sweet and lovely, innocence, pure love, a woman's good-luck gift
  • Yellow: You have disappointed me, rejection
  • Solid color: Yes
  • Striped: No, I wish I could be with you
  • As Pink (the flower): Pure affection
  • As Mountain Pink: You are aspiring
