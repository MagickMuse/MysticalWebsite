Magickal Properties

Powers: Psychic Powers, Protection

Symbolism

Grass is so prolific and ordinary that its very mundanity becomes the source of its symbolic power. It serves as a living litmus of the land: dry, brown, and dying grass signals drought, while lush green grass speaks of healthy ground and fertility. The Roman army reserved its highest honor, the corona graminea or grass crown, for a leader whose command had saved the day, weaving it from the grasses, flowers, and weeds pulled from the battlefield itself. The whole army had to agree that their leader deserved it, and it was granted only after the most desperate of campaigns. Pliny the Elder recorded only nine men who ever received the honor.

Magickal Uses

House ward: Suspend a ball of green grass in the front window to drive out evil and protect the home. Tie knots in grass around the house for the same purpose.

Psychic aid: Blades of grass carried help strengthen psychic powers.

Wishing stone: Use green grass to mark a wish on a stone, or rub the stone until it stains green while visualizing your need. Bury the stone or throw it into running water to set the wish in motion.

Meaning When Gifted

When grass is gifted it carries the meaning of submission, usefulness, and gay love.
