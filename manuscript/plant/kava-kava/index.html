Kava‑Kava (Piper methysticum) is a Polynesian root of protection, luck, and true visions.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Cold)

Planet: Saturn

Element: Water (source: Earth)

Deities: Lono, Kāne, Kanaloa (Polynesian); Saturn, Kronos, Hecate, Demeter, Danu (from source)

Powers: Love, Visions, Protection, Luck, Hallucinogenic

Ritual Uses

Long used in rites in Hawai‘i and Polynesia.

Magickal Uses

  • Protection and luck: The infused root is traditionally drunk to drive away evil and invite good fortune.
  • Visions and psychic opening: Steep overnight (refrigerated) and drink prior to scrying or divination to enhance psychic powers and induce visions.

From the source book: The aromatic root is frequently used in love potions and sachets. Smells a little like lilac but is hallucinogenic.

Safety: Not recommended for internal use if pregnant or nursing, or if you have Parkinson's disease. Do not use in large amounts.
