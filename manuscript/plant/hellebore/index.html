Hellebore, Black (Helleborus niger) is a baneful herb of ancient exorcisms and invisibility lore.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold)

Element: Water (source: Earth)

Deities: Kronos, Nephthys, Hecate

Powers: Visions, Exorcism, Astral Projection, Invisibility

Magickal Uses

  • Invisibility powder: Scatter powdered hellebore before you as you walk to go unseen (traditional lore).
  • Exorcism and visions: Once used in exorcism and to induce astral projection, but too dangerous today.

From the source book: Both the black and green varieties were used in incenses to cause frenzy. Also used in exorcism and counter-magick to protect from evil spells. The author does not recommend using this herb at all.

Safety: Poison. Do not use internally or topically; avoid handling.

Meaning When Gifted

When hellebore, the Christmas rose, is gifted it carries the meaning of "relieve my anxiety."
