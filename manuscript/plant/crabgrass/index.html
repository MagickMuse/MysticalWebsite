🌾Crabgrass Law Keep Away Spell

Crabgrass (Digitaria sanguinalis) is a common, widespread annual grass considered a weed in most contexts. In folk magick, this very quality — its ability to spread, tangle, and disorient — is channeled into protective spells.

In Law Keep Away workings, crabgrass is hung in the corners of rooms to confuse, distract, and disorient authority figures — particularly law enforcement — who enter the space. Its sprawling, tangled growth pattern is believed to reflect back onto those who intend harm.

⚠️ This plant's use in Law Keep Away workings draws on Hoodoo-adjacent American folk magick tradition. Approach with respect for its origins.

Magickal Correspondences

  • Primary use: Confusion, disorientation, keeping authority away
  • Tradition: American folk magick / Hoodoo-adjacent
  • Form: Hung dried in the corners of rooms
