Pansy (Viola tricolor) draws love, calls rain, and opens the heart to omens.

The Flower of Loving Thoughts

The pansy is a flower of remembrance, owing in part to its name, which echoes the French pensées, meaning thoughts. Its petals are often read as a little face, and the flower invites a fond, almost human regard. Its old English names, love-in-idleness and heartsease, come from the belief that to carry a pansy was to be reassured of another's love, so that the flower stands above all for loving thoughts.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Saturn

Powers: Love, Divination, Communication, Happiness, Rain Magic

Magickal Uses

  • Draw love: Worn or carried to attract love.
  • Love divination: Plant pansies in a heart shape; if they prosper, so will your love.
  • Sailor‑love charm: A woman whose sailor goes to sea may bury sea sand in the pansy bed and water the flowers before sunrise to ensure they think of her.
  • Rain sign: Pansies picked when the dew still lies upon them will bring rain.

Folk Names

Heartsease, Love-Lies-Bleeding, Johnny-Jump-Up

Meaning When Gifted

When pansy is gifted it carries the meaning of "you occupy my thoughts."
