Camphor (Cinnamomum camphora) is a large evergreen tree that produces aromatic white crystalline camphor.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Chastity, Health, Divination

Magickal Uses

  • Repelling unwanted lovers: Use the odor of camphor in spells to turn away unwanted lovers. Simply smell camphor and your ardor will immediately cool. Camphor is also placed next to the bed for this same purpose.
  • Chastity: Camphor is used to lessen the sexual drive and promote chastity.
  • Health and purification: Camphor is also used in health and healing spells, and a bag of camphor hung around the neck wards off colds and flu.
  • Sleep: Add a drop or two of camphor to incenses to produce sleep. Burn in the bedroom prior to sleep to bring on restful slumber and prophetic dreams.
  • Divination: A small amount of camphor added to divinatory incenses will strengthen your psychic powers.

Other Names & Related Species

Camphor's name is shared with a botanically unrelated tree, and the two should not be confused.

  • Camphor (Cinnamomum camphora, Lauraceae): the Japanese and Formosan tree profiled here. Yields crystalline camphor and a steam-distilled oil rectified into white, brown, and yellow fractions. Considered relatively toxic, with brown and yellow fractions classified as carcinogenic.
  • Borneo Camphor (Dryobalanops aromatica, Dipterocarpaceae): a Southeast Asian giant whose trunk weeps a sacred crystalline resin called borneol. Botanically distinct, considerably gentler, and central to Persian, Indian, and Chinese ritual traditions. See Borneo Camphor and its oil Borneol Essential Oil.

Within C. camphora itself, the ho-sho variety yields ho leaf and ho wood oil, the Chinese variety yields apopin oil, and the Japanese/Taiwanese hon-sho type produces two chemotypes (camphor-safrol in Japan, camphor-linalol in Taiwan).
