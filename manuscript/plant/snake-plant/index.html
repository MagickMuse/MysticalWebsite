Snake Plant (Dracaena trifasciata), also called Mother-in-Law's Tongue, guards against gossip and negativity.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Protection, Blocking Gossip, Warding, Cutting Negativity

Botanical Connections

Another of the Dragon's Blood family, this plant grows from a bunch rather than a single stem, and has a sturdy growth habit and thick leaves.

Additional Notes

Think of snake plants as the broadsword of the plant world. Their upright, blade-like leaves cut through negative energy with decisive force.

Gossip Protection

They're called mother-in-law's tongue and can keep gossip from your door. Place near the entrance of your home or in rooms where difficult conversations tend to occur.

Care Tips

Snake plants are extremely low-maintenance, tolerating low light and infrequent watering. Their hardiness reflects their magickal strength-they're difficult to kill and even more difficult to overcome energetically.

Protective Applications

  • Place near front doors to block gossip and ill-wishing
  • Keep in the bedroom to guard sleep
  • Position in home offices to protect professional reputation
