Amazonian herb popular in South American magick. Believed to possess purifying and protective powers; protects against malevolent magick as well as attacks from animals and humans. Key ingredient in the limpia (cleansing bath), used to wash off saladera, the "salt" that some believe lingers on the subtle body and causes bad luck.

⚠️
Do not take mucura internally without expert medical supervision, as it is potentially very toxic.
