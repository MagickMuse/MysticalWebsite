Magickal Uses

The herb and root are most potent when harvested on March 25. Carry as an amulet to guard against wild animals and evil spirits. Burn during exorcism and cleansing rituals. Add to protective sachets, amulets, and incenses. Historically, the herb and root were used as a spring tonic and blood purifier.
