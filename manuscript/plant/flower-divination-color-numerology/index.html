Flower Divination: Color & Numerology

In flower divination, color carries the primary symbolic weight. Most people notice a flower's hue before any other attribute, making it a critical interpretive factor.

Color Symbolism

White

Represents innocence, purity, and virginity. Also signifies trust and honesty.

Red

Embodies love, passion, strength, and courage.

Yellow

Signals generosity and good fortune. Associated with abundance, prosperity, and fertility.

Green

Indicates hope, anticipation, and active growth.

Blue

Conveys knowledge, wisdom, and spirituality. Also suggests refinement.

Purple

Symbolizes ambition, power, attainment, and elevated position.

Numerology for Flowers

A flower's name carries interpretive weight. By converting letters to numbers using the standard numerological chart, you can calculate both the Expression number (total sum) and Soul Urge number (vowels only) to reveal the flower's energetic signature.

Example: Rose

Calculation:
R = 9, O = 6, S = 1, E = 5
Total = 21 → 2 + 1 = 3

Expression number: 3 (fun, laughter, expressing life's joys)
Soul Urge number: 11 from O + E (illumination, inspiration, intuition)

Rose brings both levity and access to higher consciousness. It opens pathways to intuition and profound insight while maintaining joyful energy.

Example: Rhododendron

Calculation:
R = 9, H = 8, O = 6, D = 4, O = 6, D = 4, E = 5, N = 5, D = 4, R = 9, O = 6, N = 5
Total = 71 → 7 + 1 = 8

Expression number: 8 (power, material manifestation, financial attraction)
Soul Urge number: 5 from three Os + E (freedom, variety, change)

Rhododendron supports financial progress and material goals. Its Soul Urge of 5 suggests affinity for independence and entrepreneurial ventures, making it particularly effective for self-employment and business growth work.

Interpreting a Full Reading

Most divinations produce mixed signals requiring synthesis. Here's a complete reading process:

Example Question: Reciprocated Love

Timing: Friday (correct day for romance questions), July 1, 2005 (reduces to 6, which favors close relationships)

Flower chosen: Marigold in full bloom, yellow

Numerological analysis:
M = 4, A = 1, R = 9, I = 9, G = 7, O = 6, L = 3, D = 4
Total = 43 → 4 + 3 = 7
Soul Urge (A, I, O) = also 7

Interpretation:

  • Positive indicators: Yellow signals generosity, fortune, and fertility. Day number 6 supports relationships.
  • Cautionary indicators: Double 7s suggest reserve, introspection, difficulty being known.

Synthesis: If the potential partner is naturally reserved, the 7s describe them accurately. If not, the 7s signal patience. Get to know them better before drawing conclusions.

When all factors align positively, interpretation is straightforward. Mixed readings require balancing contradictory elements and using judgment.

Reading Flowers for Others

This method requires developed intuition and acute observation. The client brings a single flower in water to maintain freshness.

Reading Sequence: Stem to Bloom

Bottom of stem (childhood):
Rough, torn cut = difficult early years
Clean, neat cut = stable foundation

Stalk (life to present):
Discoloration, thinning, twisting = problem periods
New branches = new beginnings
Leaves = friends and family

Bloom (future):
Bud = resolution still ahead
Full bloom = successful outcome approaching
Dying bloom = outcome already occurred (possibly unrecognized)

Color as Problem Indicator

  • White: Trust issues
  • Red: Love and passion concerns
  • Yellow/Orange: Financial matters
  • Green: Hope, anticipation, growth
  • Blue: Knowledge and spiritual questions
  • Purple: Ambition and power dynamics

This method develops clairvoyant skills through physical observation combined with intuitive interpretation. Most people possess latent psychic ability. Flower reading can activate and refine it.

Extended Personal Flowers

Any flower whose Expression or Soul Urge number matches your Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, or Day of Birth also qualifies as a personal flower. These create resonance with your numerological signature and can be used in personalized workings.
