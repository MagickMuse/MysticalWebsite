Pennyroyal (Mentha pulegium) braces the body, guards travelers, quells quarrels, and keeps the evil eye at bay. It belongs to the Lamiaceae family.

Characteristics

A perennial herb reaching up to 50 cm tall, with smooth roundish stalks, small pale purple flowers, and very aromatic grey-green oval leaves. Like other members of the mint family, it has a fibrous creeping root.

Origin & Distribution

Native to Europe and parts of Asia. Cultivated mainly in southern Spain, Morocco, Tunisia, Portugal, Italy, Yugoslavia, and Turkey.

Related Species

Several different varieties of pennyroyal exist according to location. In Britain the 'erecta' and 'decumbens' types are most common. The North American pennyroyal (Hedeoma pulegoides), which is also used to produce an essential oil, belongs to a slightly different species but shares similar properties with the European variety.

Folk Tradition

A herbal remedy of ancient repute, used for a wide variety of ailments. Pennyroyal was believed to purify the blood and to communicate its purifying qualities to water. Pennyroyal water was distilled from the leaves and given as an antidote to spasmodic, nervous, and hysterical affections, and was also used against cold and 'affections of the joints.' Pennyroyal is still current in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia, indicated for flatulent dyspepsia, intestinal colic, the common cold, delayed menstruation, cutaneous eruptions, and gout. Recorded actions include antiseptic, antispasmodic, diaphoretic, carminative, digestive, emmenagogue, insect repellent, refrigerant, and stimulant.

Safety

The essential oil is an oral toxin and an abortifacient because of its pulegone content; ingestion of large doses has resulted in death. The oil should not be used in aromatherapy, whether internally or externally. Pennyroyal is unsafe for pregnant people to handle under any circumstances.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Powers: Strength, Protection, Peace

Magickal Uses

  • Travel strength: Place in the shoe to prevent weariness during travel and strengthen the body.
  • Stomach charm: Stuff a green poppet with mint (including pennyroyal) and anoint with healing oils for stomach complaints.
  • Business and the evil eye: Worn, it acts against the evil eye and aids in making business deals.
  • House cleansing: To rid a place of evil, sprinkle salt water with a sprinkler made of fresh sprigs of mint, marjoram, and rosemary.
  • Peace: Given to quarreling couples it will cause them to cease fighting; an herb of peace.
  • Home serenity: Place pennyroyal in a bowl within the home to maintain serenity and a peaceful atmosphere.
  • Seasickness: Carried on board ships to prevent seasickness.

Safety: Essential oil is poisonous; avoid internal use of the oil.
