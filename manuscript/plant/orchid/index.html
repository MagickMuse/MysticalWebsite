Orchid (Orchis spp.; Orchidaceae) is a classic emblem and instrument of love and vision.

The Name and the Body

The word orchid comes from the Greek orkhis, meaning testicles, named for the paired shape of the plant's tubers. Greek mothers-to-be believed they could choose the sex of an unborn child by eating orchid roots, large ones for a boy and small ones for a girl. Between the shape of its tubers and a flower thought to resemble the female genitalia, the orchid ranks high as a symbol of sexuality and potency.

Barrenness and Spiritual Perfection

In China the orchid was carried as a charm against barrenness, though to cut one was to risk the death of one's children. The beauty of the flower made it a symbol of spiritual perfection, and the long life of its blooms made it a symbol of undying truth.

Source — Specific Uses

The root is said to be a powerful aphrodisiac and ingredient of numerous love charms and philters. The name satyrion comes from the ancient belief that the root resembles the male sexual organ, and thus by sympathetic magick governs love and lust.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Love

Magickal Uses

  • Love spells: Long used in love spells, especially the root, which is carried in a sachet to draw and secure love.
  • Vision lore: Some kinds are used to create visions and trance‑states and to induce psychic powers (traditional lore).

Meaning When Gifted

When orchid is gifted it carries the meaning of love, beauty, and refinement, serves as a Chinese symbol for many children, and expresses thoughtfulness.
