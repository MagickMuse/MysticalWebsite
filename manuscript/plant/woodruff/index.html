Source — Specific Uses

Wonderfully fragrant — acquires its scent only as it dries. Used in perfumery and bath herb mixtures. Use in incense in spring to clear away the closeness and drab atmosphere of winter. Carry it to be victorious and turn over a new leaf in your outlook on life.
