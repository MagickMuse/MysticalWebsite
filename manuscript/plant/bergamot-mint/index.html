Bergamot Mint (Mentha citrata) is an aromatic hybrid mint.

Characteristics

A perennial herb in the mint family with smooth dark green leaves and the squared stems characteristic of the Mentha genus. Crushed leaves release a sweet, lemony, eau-de-cologne fragrance.

Origin & Habitat

Native across Europe and the Mediterranean and naturalized widely. A close relative of water mint, often classified as Mentha aquatica var. citrata. Thrives in damp, partly shaded soil.

Other Names

Orange Mint, Eau de Cologne Mint, Lemon Mint, Lime Mint, Water Mint. Sometimes simply called "Bergamot," which is a frequent source of confusion. Bergamot mint is a true mint, while Bergamot Orange (Citrus bergamia) is a citrus tree and Wild Bergamot (Monarda) is a North American flowering herb. The three plants share a name and a soft citrus perfume but nothing else.
