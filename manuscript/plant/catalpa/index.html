Catalpa (Catalpa ovata) was counted among the sacred trees of ancient China, where it held the southerly position and was associated with the Sun. It stands as one of the four Trees of the Directions, set alongside the thuja, the acacia, and the chestnut, each tree guarding its own quarter of the world.

Magickal Properties

  • Powers: Solar Magick, Direction, Warding

Magickal Uses

For solar work: As the tree of the south and of the Sun, the catalpa lends itself to workings tied to warmth, light, and the full height of the day.

For directional magick: Set to the south as one of the Trees of the Directions, the catalpa can be called upon to anchor and ward that quarter of a ritual space.
