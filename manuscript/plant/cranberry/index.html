Cranberry (Vaccinium macrocarpon) is a low-growing evergreen shrub prized for its tart, dark red berries.

History

The link between cranberry consumption and reduced urinary tract infections was first identified by scientists in the early 20th century. Initially thought to be a result of the fruit's acidity, further research revealed that the effect stems from a combination of several compounds working together rather than any single mechanism.

Characteristics

Extensively cultivated in the bogs of the northeastern United States, cranberry is produced commercially in both Massachusetts and Washington in natural and constructed bogs alike. The plant bears ellipse-shaped evergreen leaves with a distinctly waxy surface. Dark pink flowers precede the arrival of the dark red berries, which reach around half an inch in diameter.

Magickal Properties

Cranberries ripen in late fall, placing them in alignment with the waning year and the energies of the crone. Dried cranberries can be worked into charms honoring the wisdom of elders or offered to the ancestors at seasonal observances.

Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: Action, abundance, protection, love.
