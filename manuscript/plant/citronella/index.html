Citronella (Cymbopogon nardus) is a tropical grass known for its distinctive lemony scent and powerful insect-repelling properties.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Mercury
  • Element: Air
  • Powers: Protection, Cleansing, Repelling Negativity

Folk Names

Lemon Grass (common misname), Citronella Grass

Magickal Uses

For love and attraction: Citronella is used in love workings and to attract others. It is used in perfumery, cosmetics, and soaps for this purpose.

For protection: Burn citronella to cleanse a space and create a protective barrier. The strong scent repels negative energies just as it repels insects.

For cleansing: Use citronella oil or dried herb in purification rituals to clear away stagnant or unwanted energies from a space or person.

For clarity: The fresh, clean scent helps clear the mind and promote mental alertness during magickal workings.

Botanical Description

A tall aromatic perennial grass derived from the wild-growing 'managrass' found in Sri Lanka. Belongs to the Poaceae (Gramineae) family.

Folk Tradition (Medicinal)

The leaves are used for their aromatic and medicinal value across many cultures, applied to fever, intestinal parasites, digestive and menstrual problems, as a stimulant, and as an insect repellent. In Chinese traditional medicine, the leaves are used for rheumatic pain.

Related Species

A closely related species, Java Citronella (Cymbopogon winterianus, also called Maha Pengiri citronella), is cultivated in the tropics worldwide, especially in Java, Vietnam, Africa, Argentina, and Central America. It yields roughly twice as much oil as the Sri Lanka type and is regarded as superior in perfumery work. See also Citronella Essential Oil and Java Citronella Essential Oil.
