Bromeliad (Bromeliaceae spp.) is a tropical plant family known for colorful rosettes and unique water-holding structures.

Magical Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Planet: Sun
  • Element: Air
  • Powers: Protection, Money

Folk Names

Chameleon Star, Earth Star

Magical Uses

For protection: Bromeliads are kept in the home for protection and to bring a sense of luxury.

For prosperity: Their exotic nature and association with tropical abundance makes them useful in money-drawing spells.
