Magickal Uses

Sacred to Jupiter: The chestnut is sacred to Jupiter. Its leaves and wood were burned as incense in his honor.

For healing: The fruit was used medicinally; work with chestnut in healing spells and health workings.

Sweet Chestnut (Castanea sativa) was counted among the four sacred trees of ancient China, where it stood for the westerly direction and the season of fall. Because its nuts were gathered and laid by for the winter to come, it came to be a symbol of foresight.

Folk Tradition

Elsewhere the sweet chestnut is held a symbol of honesty, and it should never be confused with the horse chestnut, which is no true chestnut at all. These are long-lived giants. The largest tree in Britain is a chestnut, the Tortworth Chestnut, which by 1766 already measured fifty feet around. An even greater specimen on Mount Etna in Sicily was said to be able to hold a whole flock of sheep within its hollow core, where a house had once been built, and was later found to be five trees grown together.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine

Magickal Uses

For foresight and provision: As the tree whose stored nuts carry life through the winter, the chestnut lends itself to workings of foresight, planning, and looking ahead.

For abundance: Its plentiful nuts make it fitting for spells of plenty and harvest.

For honesty: Carry or work chestnut where truth and plain dealing are sought.

Love Fix: Hand-feeding someone chestnuts is said to stimulate them to love you.
