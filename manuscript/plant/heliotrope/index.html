Heliotrope (Heliotropium europaeum; H. arborescens) is a potent but poisonous charm of dreams, wealth, and exorcism.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Exorcism, Prophetic Dreams, Healing, Wealth, Invisibility

Magickal Uses

  • Clairvoyance and prophetic dreams: The flowers are wonderfully fragrant and used in perfumery and potpourris. Placed beneath the pillow to induce prophetic dreams, especially to discover the thief if you have been robbed.
  • Exorcism and healing: Added to exorcism incenses and healing sachets.
  • Wealth: Carried in pocket or purse to attract money.
  • Invisibility charm: Fill a small horn with heliotrope and carry to avoid attracting attention.

Safety: Poisonous. External, symbolic use only.

Meaning When Gifted

When heliotrope is gifted it carries the meaning of devotion.
