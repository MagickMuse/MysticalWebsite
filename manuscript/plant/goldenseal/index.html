Goldenseal (Hydrastis canadensis) is a woodland perennial with thick yellow rhizomes and palmately lobed leaves, long valued for its protective and restorative virtues in folk magick.

History

Goldenseal was employed by Native Americans both as a dye and for its medicinal properties. European colonists adopted its use in the 1800s, incorporating it as an eyewash and general tonic, which helped establish its wider popularity. In folk medicine, goldenseal has historically been associated with healing conditions affecting the mouth and lips.

Characteristics

Goldenseal is a notably difficult plant to cultivate. Each seedling requires two to four years to develop a mature root, and the plant cannot be harvested until it is five years old. A woodland perennial, it prefers moist, shaded environments. Sustained overharvesting has made it increasingly rare; it is currently listed as endangered in six U.S. states.

Planet: Sun

Magickal Uses

For money: Goldenseal is used in money spells and prosperity workings. Add to sachets, sprinkle a small pinch on bank notes or coin jars, or keep a packet with business takings to encourage steady income.

For healing: Include in healing blends, bath sachets, or place near recovery altars to reinforce restoration and vitality symbolism.

For communication: Incorporate goldenseal into workings intended to open and improve communication, particularly where words have become a source of harm or conflict.

For mending disagreements: Use in spells aimed at healing rifts between people, repairing relationships damaged by harsh speech, or undoing the harm caused by gossip and rumor.

Safety notes: Not recommended for internal use if pregnant. Do not use long term.

Magickal Teas

Goldenseal can be brewed as a magickal tea to support healing, honor or invoke the God, inspire joy, attract prosperity, and encourage success. Drink 1 to 2 cups only during the spell event itself.
