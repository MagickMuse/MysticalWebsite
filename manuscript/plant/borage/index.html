Borage (Borago officinalis) is an annual herb with star-shaped blue flowers and hairy leaves.

History

Borage is native to Syria and was carried westward through the Mediterranean by Roman travelers and traders. The first-century C.E. writer Pliny the Elder praised it as a plant capable of driving away sadness and lifting the heart, and the Greek physician Dioscorides noted similar properties. Its long association with courage and cheerfulness made it a common addition to wine and herbal preparations across the ancient world.

Characteristics

A fast-growing annual, borage reaches roughly two feet in height and produces rough, hairy leaves with a fresh, salty flavor that recalls cucumber. Every part of the plant sees use in herbal practice: the flowers and leaves appear in teas, infusions, compresses, and poultices, while the seeds yield a beneficial oil. The star-shaped blue blossoms are edible and lend themselves to salads and sugared decorations.

Magickal Uses

Borage was said by the Romans and Greeks to give courage, and the flowers are carried or worn for the same purpose. The fresh blossoms are floated in a drink to give psychic powers, though caution is advised with the herb portion; mixed with mugwort and parsley, borage increases clairvoyance. Borage has been used for at least four hundred years for melancholy and minor depression, and it is identified with the famous Nepenthe of Homer, the herb that would cause forgetfulness of grief and sorrow. Borage carries a genuine reputation for elevating mood and dispelling sorrow, and its vivid pentacle-shaped blossoms are used to amplify uplifting energy, encourage happiness, and strengthen personal vitality and resilience.

Culinary Uses

The beautiful blue blossoms of borage have a light taste reminiscent of cucumber and can be described as slightly sweet. Their sacred star-shaped petal formation makes them a magickal ingredient for salads. Because they are slightly sweet, they can also be used to create sugared flowers.

Beauty & Skincare

Borage makes an excellent facial steam for dry skin, helping to open pores and brighten the complexion. A steam prepared from the leaves allows the skin to absorb moisture while releasing impurities. It is a gentle but effective treatment for dull or congested skin.
