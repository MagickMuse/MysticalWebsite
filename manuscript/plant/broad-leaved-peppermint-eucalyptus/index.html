Broad-Leaved Peppermint Eucalyptus Essential Oil

A robust, medium-sized eucalyptus tree of the Myrtaceae family, with a short trunk, spreading branches, and fibrous grey bark. Young leaves are blue and heart-shaped; mature leaves are very aromatic, thick, and tapering at both ends.

Origin & Distribution

Native to Tasmania and Australia, especially New South Wales and Victoria. The oil is also produced in South Africa.

Folk Tradition

Aboriginal Australian peoples used the burning leaves as a fumigation for the relief of fever, expressed in the saying that "heat went out of sick man and into fire."

⚠️ This is an Aboriginal Australian practice and is sacred to those traditions. It should only be practiced by those within the originating culture.

Related Species

Two chemotypes of broad-leaved peppermint look identical but yield different oils: one rich in cineol (Eucalyptus dives var. C) and one rich in piperitone (E. dives var. Type, the subject of this entry). It is also similar to the peppermint eucalyptus (E. piperita) and the grey or narrow-leaved peppermint (E. radiata var. phellandra). See also Eucalyptus and Lemon-Scented Eucalyptus.
