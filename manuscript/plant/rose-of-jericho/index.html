Magickal Properties

Rose of Jericho draws good fortune and opens the door to opportunity, and is especially fortuitous when relocating or seeking new paths.

Magickal Uses

  • Luck charm: Carry a dried Rose of Jericho blossom to draw good fortune, particularly during times of transition and opportunity-seeking.
  • Rose of Jericho Water: Use the water to bathe charms, charm bags, and any object to empower it as a luck talisman. Adding it to a bath allows the practitioner to radiate good fortune directly.
