Carrot (Daucus carota) is a biennial root vegetable with feathery leaves and umbrella-like white flowers.

Aphrodisiac Properties

The carrot is one of the "phallic vegetables," considered an aphrodisiac simply because of its shape.

Carrot Oils

Two oils trace back to this species. Carrot Seed Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the dried seeds (fruits). A separate solvent-extracted oil is drawn from the red fleshy root of the cultivated edible carrot and serves as a natural food colouring agent.
