May Chang Essential Oil

May Chang (Litsea cubeba) is a small tropical tree of the Lauraceae family whose pepper-shaped fruits yield an intensely lemony essential oil. The genus name cubeba derives from those small, pepper-like fruits.

Characteristics

A small tropical tree with fragrant, lemongrass-scented leaves and flowers, bearing small fruits shaped like peppers.

Origin & Distribution

Native to east Asia, especially China. Now also cultivated in Taiwan and Japan. China remains the main producer of the essential oil, much of which is used domestically.

Related Species

Despite its folk names exotic verbena and tropical verbena, May Chang is not related to lemon verbena (Aloysia triphylla). It belongs to the Lauraceae family alongside the laurel tree, rosewood, and cinnamon.

Folk Tradition

May Chang is planted as a wind breaker in China. Its essential oil carries antiseptic, deodorant, digestive, disinfectant, insecticidal, stimulant, and stomachic actions.
