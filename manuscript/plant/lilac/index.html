Lilac (Syringa vulgaris) clears places of evil and refreshes troubled rooms.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Venus

Powers: Protection, Banishing Negative Energy, Exorcism

Magickal Uses

  • House clearing: Drives away evil where planted or strewn; in New England, lilacs were planted to keep evil off the property.
  • Haunting remedy: Fresh blossoms placed in a haunted house help clear it.

For the Otherworld: Lilac is associated with the Otherworld and liminal spaces.

Meaning When Gifted

When lilac is gifted it carries the meaning of beauty and pride.

  • Purple Lilac: First emotions of love
  • White Lilac: Youthful innocence
