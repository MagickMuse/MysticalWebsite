Powers: Binding, Uncrossing, Protection

Magickal Uses

  • Use as incense only.
  • Luck carrying charm: A Western folk use of the sumbul root is to carry it for magically enhanced good fortune, though this differs from its traditional preparation as incense.

⚠️ Folk name overlap: "Jatamansi" is shared with Spikenard (Nardostachys jatamansi) and should not be used as a folk name here — Sumbul (Ferula sumbul) is a distinct plant. See also the Spikenard entry, which previously listed "Sumbul" in its By Other Names.
