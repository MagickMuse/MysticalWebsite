Marigold (Tagetes patula) protects, reveals, and draws respect.

⚠️
Tagetes vs. Calendula — Important Safety Distinction

Tagetes (French marigold, Aztec marigold, African marigold) is a different plant from Calendula (pot marigold, Calendula officinalis), which is the only completely safe marigold. Tagetes are potentially toxic: do not ingest or apply to the body. Inhaling the fresh flowers is considered sufficient for magickal use. A Tagetes essential oil exists but is not recommended; fresh flowers are the safer option.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Protection, Prophetic Dreams, Legal Matters, Psychic Powers, Marriage, Clairvoyant Dreams

Magickal Uses

  • Comfort the heart: Picked at noon when the Sun is hottest to strengthen and comfort the heart.
  • House ward and dreams: Garlands on doorposts stop evil from entering; scattered under the bed they protect and bring prophetic dreams (especially useful to discover a thief).
  • Respect and vision: Add to bath water to win respect and admiration; looking at the bright flowers strengthens the sight; carried in pocket favors justice in court.
  • Bird‑tongue charm: If a girl touches the petals with bare feet, she will understand the languages of birds (folklore).

Meaning When Gifted

When the common marigold is gifted it carries the meaning of cruelty, grief, and jealousy.
