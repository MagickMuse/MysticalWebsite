Plant Grimoire

Harvesting & Collecting Plants

If you gather your own plant materials, follow these guidelines to harvest responsibly and effectively.

Before You Collect

  • Collect only what you need. Do you really need five bags full of mugwort?
  • Attune with the plant before harvesting. Place your hands around it and feel its energy. You might chant a simple rhyme or speak words explaining why you are taking part of its life force.
  • Make an offering. Place something of value in the soil at the base of the plant. If you have nothing else, leave a coin. This represents your willingness to give in exchange for the plant's sacrifice.

The 25 Percent Rule

Never collect more than 25 percent of any plant's growth. If you must take roots, which requires harvesting the entire plant, leave nearby plants of the same species untouched.

Timing and Conditions

Do not collect after rain or heavy dew until the sun has dried the plants. Wet plant material may mold during the drying process.

Site Selection

Choose your collection site with care. Never harvest plants near:

  • Highways or busy roads
  • Stagnant or polluted waters
  • Factories or industrial sites
  • Military installations

Drying and Storage

To dry harvested herbs, strip off leaves or flowers and lay them on ceramic, wooden, or steel racks in a warm, dry place out of direct sunlight. Alternatively, place them in baskets and shake daily until dry. Store in airtight, labeled jars.
