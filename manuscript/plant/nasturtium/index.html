Nasturtium (Tropaeolum majus) is a vibrant flowering plant with round leaves and brilliantly colored blossoms ranging from deep red to orange and yellow.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Cold

Planet: Saturn

Element: Earth

Deities: Saturn, Kronos, Nephthys, Isis, Demeter/Ceres, Nut, Cerridwen, Danu, Hekate

Powers: Protection, Uncrossing, Reincarnation, Study, Knowledge, Psychic Sight, Beauty, Vitality

From the source book: Seeds used in incenses for psychic sight. Parts used: flowers, leaves, seeds.

History and Lore

In 1596, Spanish physician and botanical collector Nicolas Monardes observed that each nasturtium petal contained what he described as a drop of blood, so red and so firmly kindled in color that it could not be more vivid. Monet featured nasturtium flowers in his garden, and Thomas Jefferson planted them every year. The ancient Greeks likened the flowers and leaves to shields and helmets, lending nasturtium its associations with protection and strength.

Culinary Uses

Parts used: Flowers, leaves

Nasturtiums occur in such a wide range of colors that they add a dramatic bit of magick to savory dishes. Their peppery flavor makes them an attractive ingredient for tossed salads or as a garnish for soups.

Meaning When Gifted

When nasturtium is gifted it carries the meaning of resignation.
