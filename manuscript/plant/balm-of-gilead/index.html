Balm of Gilead is a healing tree resin with a sweet, balsamic fragrance used in both medicinal and magickal applications.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Venus

Powers: Love, Manifestations, Protection, Healing

Magickal Uses

Carry the buds of balm of Gilead to mend a broken heart or to attract new love. The buds can also be steeped in red wine to create a simple love potion.

For manifestation work, burn balm of Gilead as a material basis for spirits during evocation rituals. The sweet smoke helps create the proper atmosphere for spiritual communication.

The resin provides protection and healing when carried in a small pouch or charm bag. It's particularly effective for emotional healing and recovery from heartbreak.

For incense and perfumery: In North America, Balm of Gilead refers to poplar buds (Populus candicans, P. nigra, or P. balsamifera), widely used in incense blends and perfumery. In Southern folk tradition these buds are known as gilly or gilliam buds.

⚠️ Note: Many plants carry this name - be sure you know what you are buying or picking, as different species have varying properties and safety profiles.
