Blue Flag (Iris versicolor) is a wetland iris with distinctive blue-purple flowers.

Magical Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Venus
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Money

Folk Names

Flag Lily, Fleur-de-Lys, Iris, Liver Lily, Poison Lily, Poison Flag, Snake Lily, Water Flag, Water Iris

Magical Uses

For financial gain: Carry the root for financial gain. The root is also placed in cash registers to increase business.

⚠️ Danger: This plant is poisonous and should never be taken internally. External use only and avoid during pregnancy or if nursing.
