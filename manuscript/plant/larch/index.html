Larch (Larix europaea; Larix spp.) is a steadfast ward against flame and enchantment.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Hot)

Element: Fire/Water

Deities: Jupiter/Zeus, Amun, Dôn/Danu, the Dagda, Thor

Powers: Honor, Riches, Good Luck, Protection, Anti-Fire

Magickal Uses

  • Anti‑fire charm: Because larch wood is magically said to resist fire, it is used in sachets to prevent conflagrations. Pieces of larch wood kept openly in the home are said to prevent fires from occurring there.
  • Anti-bewitchment: Carried or worn to prevent enchantment and the evil eye.

Source Book Note

Source lists basic powers as honor, riches, and good luck. Cross-references Pine for additional specific uses.
