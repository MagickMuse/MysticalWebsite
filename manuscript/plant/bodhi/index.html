Bodhi (Ficus religiosa) is the sacred fig tree under which Buddha achieved enlightenment.

Magical Properties

  • Planet: Jupiter
  • Deities: Buddha, Vishnu
  • Powers: Fertility, Protection, Wisdom, Meditation

Folk Names

Bo-Tree, Peepul Tree, Pipul, Sacred Tree

Ritual Uses

The plant is sacred to Vishnu who, like Buddha, was said to have been born beneath it. In the East, sacred fires are fed with its wood. Since Buddha also sat beneath this tree in meditation for six years, it is sacred to Him, and the heart-shaped leaves still tremble remembering the divine vibrations.

Magical Uses

For protection: If you feel evil near, simply circle this tree several times and the evil shall flee in terror.

For fertility: Barren women walk naked beneath a bodhi tree to become fertile.

For wisdom and meditation: Use the leaves in meditation incenses and all mixtures designed to give wisdom.
