Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Protection

Magickal Uses

For home protection: Grow cactus in the garden and inside the house as a safeguard against burglary and unwanted intrusions.

For image magick: The spines are used like pins in image magick to mark or write symbols on images of wax or roots. While typically restricted to more negative workings, they can also be used constructively — for example, to mark spots of ill health in healing and tumor eradication workings.

Protective jar spell: Fill a small jar with cacti spines, rusty nails, old tacks, and pine needles. Add rue and rosemary leaves to fill the jar; seal tightly and bury under your doorstep as a powerful protective device.

Meaning When Gifted

When cactus is gifted it carries the meaning of endurance, and it declares that my heart burns with love.
