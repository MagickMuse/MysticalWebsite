Cowslip (Primula veris) is a flowering perennial with clusters of fragrant, drooping yellow flowers.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Feminine (Cold)
  • Element: Earth / Air
  • Powers: Visions, Love, Healing, Youth, Treasure Finding

Folk Names

Herb Peter, Paigle, Key Flower, Fairy Cup, Password

Magickal Uses

For visions: Cowslip wine is slightly narcotic, and this quality was said to enable the drinker to have visions.

For Freyja's realm: It was said that this herb would allow one entry into Freyja's pleasure palace.

Key symbolism: The pendant, drooping flowers closely resemble a bunch of keys, connecting cowslip to unlocking and opening.

For healing: Cowslip is used in healing sachets and spells to restore health and vitality.

For youth: The flowers are used in beauty spells and anti-aging formulas. Wash your face with cowslip water to preserve youthful appearance.

For treasure finding: In folk magick, cowslip is said to help find hidden treasures. Place the flowers under your pillow to dream of where treasure lies.

For the fae: Cowslip is associated with faeries and is planted to invite their presence into your garden.

Unlocking Locks

Cowslip holds the distinct virtue of unlocking every lock and even breaking iron chains. An old folk method from Transylvania holds that the harvester must go out at daybreak and creep through the meadow on hands and knees until the plant is found. The seeker then opens the left palm with a knife and allows the leaf to curl around the wound. This is said to grant the bearer the power to break locks.

Meaning When Gifted

When cowslip is gifted it carries the meaning of winning grace and rustic charm, along with healing, youth, and pensiveness.
