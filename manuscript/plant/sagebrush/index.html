An Artemisia, Not a Sage

Despite its English name, desert sage is a true artemisia rather than a salvia. It combines the best of both families, stimulating psychic ability while providing spiritual purification. Unlike its other psychic-inducing cousins such as Mugwort and Wormwood, it is native to North America. It is a popular ingredient in commercial psychic-inducing teas, with a smoky flavor that is less bitter than mugwort or wormwood.

Ritual Uses

Burned in American Indian ceremonies for purification; used to cleanse spaces and participants. ⚠️ This ceremonial use is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating Indigenous cultures.

Magickal Uses

  • Bath purification: Bathe with sagebrush to remove past evils and negative deeds.
  • Exorcism smoke: Burn to drive away malevolent forces and cleanse sickrooms.

Additional Notes

Use respectfully; observe safety cautions for internal use and dosage.
