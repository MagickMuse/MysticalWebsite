Castor (Ricinus communis) is a large annual plant with palmate leaves and spiky seed pods.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Planet: Mars
  • Element: Fire
  • Powers: Protection

Folk Names

Castor Bean, Castor Oil Plant, Palma Christi

Magickal Uses

For protection: The beans, when carried, protect the bearer, and when grown in the garden the plant protects it from theft and intrusion.

For breaking hexes: Castor beans are used to break hexes by throwing them one at a time at the walls of the home from the inside while visualizing the hex being broken.

⚠️ Danger: The seeds contain ricin and are extremely poisonous. Do not ingest. Handle with care and wash hands thoroughly after use.
