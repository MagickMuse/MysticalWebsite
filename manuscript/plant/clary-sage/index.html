Clary Sage (Salvia sclarea) is a biennial or perennial herb with large, aromatic leaves and tall spikes of lilac or white flowers.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Euphoria, Visions, Clarity

Folk Names

Clear Eye, See Bright, Muscatel Sage

Magickal Uses

As a perfume fixative: The oil of clary sage acts as a fixative in perfumes, resembling ambergris in its effect.

For love: Use clary sage in love workings and sachets; its magickal nature draws connection and affection.

For magick: Clary sage is a general enhancer of magickal workings.

For visions: Clary sage is burned as incense or added to dream pillows to induce vivid, prophetic dreams and visions. Its name "clear eye" reflects its association with spiritual sight.

For euphoria: The essential oil and dried herb are used in rituals to promote feelings of well-being and euphoria. It helps lift depression and bring clarity to confused situations.

For clarity: Used in spells and sachets to clear mental fog and bring understanding to difficult problems. Burn before divination work to enhance perception.
