Rye (Secale cereale) is a Venusian grain used for love and fidelity charms in Romani and European folkways. Serving rye bread to loved ones was a simple household rite to ensure steadfast affection and loyalty.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Venus

Element: Earth

Powers: Love, Fidelity

Magickal Uses

  • Love and fidelity: Serve or share rye bread to strengthen mutual devotion and keep bonds true.
  • Hearth magic: Include rye in kitchen‑witch prosperity loaves that bless the home with affection and well‑being.
