Eyebright (Euphrasia officinalis) is a small annual herb with tiny white flowers marked with purple and yellow.

Magickal Properties

  • Powers: Mental Powers, Psychic Power

Magickal Uses

For psychic powers: Eyebright is brewed into a tea and the resulting liquid is used to bathe the eyes to induce clairvoyance. Despite its name, this should not be dropped directly into the eyes.

For clairvoyant visions: Brew a mild potion with water to wash out the eyes when irritated. Anoint the eyelids daily for clairvoyant visions.

For mental clarity: When carried on the person, eyebright increases memory and mental powers.

Magickal Teas

Eyebright can be brewed as a magickal tea to sharpen mental clarity and heighten psychic awareness. Drink 1 to 2 cups only during the spell event itself.
