Magickal Properties

Powers: Love, Protection

Ritual Uses

Household threshold wards using plum branches.

Magickal Uses

  • Household protection: Place branches over doors and windows to bar malign influences. Boughs of flowering plum hung over doors, windows, and thresholds keep joy within the home while repelling malice and evil.
  • Love food: Serve the fruit to kindle love, renew tenderness, and maintain fidelity.
  • Peacekeeping: Use in home blessings and offerings to promote calm, affectionate relations.

Additional Notes

Protective brambles and sweet fruit make plum a classic kitchen‑witch ally for harmonious homes.

Spring, Purity, and Lao Tzu

An important tree in the Far East, the plum flowers at both ends of the year and so stands for spring as well as winter, a sign of hope and new beginnings. Because its blossom opens before the leaves appear, it is also an emblem of purity, the bare-branch flowering suggesting a kind of virgin birth. Like the peach, the plum is a symbol of immortality, and the great poet and philosopher Lao Tzu took it for his personal emblem, since he was said to have been born beneath one.
