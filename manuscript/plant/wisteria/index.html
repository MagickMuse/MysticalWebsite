Wisteria is a climbing woody vine in the legume family, native to East Asia and North America, prized for its cascading clusters of fragrant flowers. In magickal practice, wisteria is associated with past life recall and dream work. It is hung over the bed during sleep to encourage past-life memories to surface. Its connection to dream states and subconscious access makes it useful for any working that requires reaching beyond ordinary memory.

Magickal Properties

Wisteria supports access to buried or suppressed memories, including those from previous lifetimes. It is a passive working plant: its energy operates through proximity and sleep rather than active use. Hanging it above the sleeping space creates a sustained, gentle influence on the dreaming mind.

Meaning When Gifted

When wisteria is gifted it carries the meaning of welcome.
