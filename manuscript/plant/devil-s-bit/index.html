Magickal Properties

Devil's bit is carried as a luck botanical; the old adage holds that carrying it makes one a "lucky devil."

Why the Root Looks Bitten

Legend says the plant offered so much protection that Satan, in a rage, bit it off, which is why its root looks chewed and ragged. Worn around the neck, devil's bit is said to protect against every kind of evil.
