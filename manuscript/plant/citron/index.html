Citron (Citrus medica) is a large, fragrant citrus fruit with thick rind, one of the original citrus species.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Sun
  • Element: Fire
  • Powers: Healing, Psychic Powers

Magickal Uses

For healing: Use in healing spells and rituals.

For psychic powers: The peel is used to increase psychic powers.

The Buddha's Fruit

The citron gives one of the Three Blessed Fruits of Buddhist philosophy. The story tells that the Buddha, resting in the tree's shade, picked a fruit and found it bitter and unpleasant. He thought to destroy the tree, but instead chose to speak with it, urging it to make its fruits sweeter and more useful. The tree obliged, reshaping its fruit so that it matched the Buddha's outstretched hand. The tree proved useful indeed, for a fragrant oil is drawn from it and its crystallized peel is prized as a delicacy. Carrying many seeds within, the fruit is also a symbol of fertility, and in the Jewish faith the citron holds sacred status and was brought as an offering to the Temple.
