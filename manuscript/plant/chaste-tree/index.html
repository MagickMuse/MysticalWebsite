Chaste Tree

Notes/Warnings
Native to Croatia. Berries resemble peppercorns. Used for thousands of years to regulate menstruation. Has estrogenic effects that reduce sex drive. Smooth, white wood is easy to carve. Monks grew it in monastery courtyards and believed keeping the wood close maintained chastity.
