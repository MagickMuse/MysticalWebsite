Job’s Tears (Coix lacryma‑jobi) are powerful white seeds for healing, luck, and wishing.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine

Planet: —

Element: —

Powers: Healing, Luck, Wishes

Magickal Uses

  • Teething and sore throats: The seeds are strung as a necklace and placed around a child’s neck to aid teething, and worn by adults for sore throats and colds. The seeds absorb the pain or illness.
  • Luck: Carry three seeds for good luck.
  • Wishing rites: Hold seven seeds while making a wish, then throw them into running water. Or count out seven seeds while concentrating on your wish and carry them for a week.
