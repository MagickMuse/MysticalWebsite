Moroccan Chamomile Essential Oil

A handsome flowering plant of the Asteraceae (Compositae) family, distilled in Morocco for an essential oil that is often mistaken for true chamomile. Despite the shared common name, it is botanically and chemically distinct from German and Roman chamomile.

Characteristics

Reaches 90 to 125 cm in height, with very hairy leaves and tubular yellow flowers ringed by white ligulets.

Origin & Distribution

Native to north west Africa and southern Spain, having most likely evolved from the common Ormenis species that grows around the Mediterranean. It is also found growing on the plains of Israel. The flowering tops are steam distilled in Morocco for essential oil production.

Related Species

Distantly related botanically to German Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla) and Roman Chamomile (Chamaemelum nobile), but does not resemble either physically. The oil is often mistaken for true chamomile, though it should more correctly be called Ormenis oil. Chemically and olfactorily, it is distinctly different from German and Roman chamomile oils and is not a replacement for either.
