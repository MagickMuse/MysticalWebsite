Source — Specific Uses

The flowers are a powerful antidote to black magick. Wear as a protective amulet, or place vases of flowers in the home if you feel threatened. Carry to see through others' deceit. Add to incenses and oil mixtures to counteract charms and spells laid by others.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Powers: Protection, Anti‑Deception, Hex‑Return

Ritual Uses

Fresh blossoms placed on the altar during protective rites; seed worn as an amulet.

Magickal Uses

  • Anti‑deception: Wear any part of the snapdragon on your body to prevent people from deceiving you.
  • Bewitchment ward: Wear the seed around the neck to ensure you will never be bewitched.
  • Instant warding: If you feel evil nearby, step on a snapdragon or hold a blossom until the danger passes.
  • Return to sender: Place snapdragons on the altar with a mirror behind them to send back hexes and curses.

Plant white snapdragons around your home for ongoing protection against deception and illusion. The dragon-like appearance of the flower heads connects them to draconic protective energy.

Additional Notes

Also called Dragon Flower; handle respectfully and avoid internal use.

Meaning When Gifted

When snapdragon is gifted it carries the meaning of a gracious lady, and of strength.
