Cananga (Cananga odorata var. macrophylla) belongs to the Annonaceae family.

Description

A tall tropical tree about 30 metres high, with large, tender, fragrant flowers, pink, mauve, or yellow. The yellow are considered best for the extraction of essential oil. It bears year-round.

Distribution

Native to tropical Asia, especially Java, Malaysia, the Philippines, and the Moluccas.

Other Species

Closely related to ylang ylang (Cananga odorata var. genuina), which produces a similar but more refined oil. See Ylang Ylang.

Folk Tradition

Used locally for infectious illnesses such as malaria. The flowers are also used for decoration at festivals.
