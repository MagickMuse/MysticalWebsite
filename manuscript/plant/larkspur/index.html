Larkspur (Delphinium ajacis; D. consolida; Delphinium spp.) lifts, heals, and wards the threshold.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Venus

Element: Water

Powers: Health, Protection

Magickal Uses

  • Guard the home: Larkspur keeps away ghosts and evil influences.
  • Preserve sight: Looking through a bunch of larkspur at a Midsummer fire preserves the eyesight for the year.
  • Scorpion ward: The flowers were said to frighten off scorpions and other venomous creatures.

Meaning When Gifted

When larkspur is gifted it carries the meaning of an open heart, levity, and lightness, with a hint of fickleness.
