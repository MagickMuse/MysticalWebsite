Solomon’s Seal (Polygonatum officinale, P. multiflorum, P. biflorum) is a traditional warding and cleansing root used for household protection, boundary‑setting, and banishing. The rhizome is placed at the four quarters of a home to guard it; an infusion sprinkled about clears away malefic influences and stale conditions. In ritual practice, the root is employed in exorcism and protection spells and is also included in offertory incenses to sanctify place and purpose.

Magickal Uses

  • House ward: Bury or place pieces of the root at the four quarters to establish a protective perimeter.
  • Banishing and cleansing: Prepare an infusion of the root and asperge thresholds and corners to dismiss harmful forces and lingering negativity.
  • Ritual protection: Add chips of the root to protection and exorcism mixtures; burn in offertory incense to solemnize rites.
