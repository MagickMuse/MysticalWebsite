Magickal Properties

Powers: Luck, Honor

Magickal Uses

Briar has highly odorous leaves, especially just after a shower of rain. It symbolizes beauty and attainment in the midst of adversity and suffering. Its properties and uses are essentially those of roses in general — it can be worked into any working for love, luck, beauty, or protection.

Meaning When Gifted

When briar, the eglantine, is gifted it carries the meaning of poetry and "I wound to heal."
