Botanical Information

Citrus aurantium (Rutaceae family). Feminine flowering tree originating from Asia. Also known as Orange Blossom and Bitter Orange. Introduced to Mediterranean by Arab traders from China. Orange blossoms traditionally associated with Hera and Juno in wedding ceremonies.

Magical Correspondences

Element: Fire

Planet: Sun

Sign: Leo

Day: Sunday

Deity: Guan Yu (Taoist god of brotherhood and righteousness)

Crystal: Sunstone

Herbal Lore

Flowers placed in bridal bouquets to calm pre-wedding jitters. Brides anointed with orange blossom waters asking Hera or Juno to bless union with fertility and happiness. Due to extreme cost, flowers rather than oil used in wedding ceremonies historically.

Ritual Uses

Protection - Solar shielding stands up under heavy fire, works against psychic attacks. Use flowers in protective sachets and charm bags.

Sleep and Stress Relief - Calming sedative properties. Place dried flowers in pillowcases for restful sleep. Use in stress relief sachets.

Anxiety and Trauma Release - Encourages release of negative emotions. Supportive for trauma work alongside professional therapy.

Fertility and Matrimony - Traditional wedding flower for fertility blessings. Use in fertility charms, new family intentions, or business fertility work. Burn on Fridays or during waxing moon.

Psychic Awareness - Increases psychic senses and physical awareness. Add to hex-breaking blends and protective wards.

Grief Processing - Helps process grief from sudden change. Provides comfort during transitions and loss.

Self-Confidence - Uplifting nature provides emotional scaffolding. Carry dried flowers or use in confidence charms until strength restored.

Depression Banishing - Helps banish depressive states. Use in healing work, especially on Sundays with figural candles.

Joy and Happiness - Citrus and floral associations bring joy. Use flowers in happiness sachets and uplifting charms.

Meditation - Excellent for guided journeys and meditation. Create relaxation and peace in sacred spaces.

Tension Relief - Divine tension tamer. Add dried flowers to relaxation baths (use nonstick mat for safety).
