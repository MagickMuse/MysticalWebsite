Magickal Properties

Origin: Eastern United States and Eastern Canada

Planet: Moon

Powers: Healing

Magickal Uses

Soak some adder's tongue in cold water and apply it to a wound or bruise (wrap it in a piece of cloth) until the herb grows warm. Next, bury the wet herb in a muddy place. The wound will be cured.

This healing method demonstrates the sympathetic magick principle where the herb absorbs the injury and then transfers it to the earth when buried.
