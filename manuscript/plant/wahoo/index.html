Magickal Properties

Powers: Hex-Breaking, Courage, Success

Ritual Uses

  • Wahoo Bark Success Carry: Simply carry wahoo bark on the person to invite success in any undertaking and to fortify courage. The bark may be kept loose in a pocket or tucked into a charm bag.

Magickal Uses

  • Hex-breaking rite: Make an infusion of the bark, cool it, and rub on the forehead saying “Wahoo!” seven times to break hexes.
  • Success and courage: Carried to bring success in undertakings and to impart courage.
