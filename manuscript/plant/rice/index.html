Magickal Properties

Planet: Sun

Powers: Protection, Rain, Money, Fertility

Ritual Uses

Thrown after wedded couples for fertility; used in roof and threshold protections.

Magickal Uses

  • House protection: Place on the roof or keep a small jar by the entrance to guard the home.
  • Rain making: Throw into the air to call rain.
  • Money spells: Add to prosperity workings to attract wealth.

Additional Notes

Brahmins carried rice as an amulet against evil; do not eat raw.

Amulet Uses

Uncooked rice carried as an amulet promotes fertility and attracts money.

Food of the Gods

Rice is the staple food of Asia and of half the world, so essential that many Asian cultures hold it to be both a gift from the gods and the food of the gods themselves. Like bread in the West, rice carries spiritual weight, standing for life, light, good luck, abundance, and fertility, which is why it is thrown as confetti over couples at Western weddings. In China the everyday greeting, "Have you had your rice today?" simply means hello. In Japan hundreds of Inari shrines dot the land, dedicated to Inari, the deity known as the rice bearer. In feudal times rice served as currency, and so it became bound up with money itself.
