Source — Specific Uses

Sacred to Jupiter and considered a food of the gods.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Sun

Powers: Health, Mental Powers, Infertility, Wishes

Ritual Uses

  • Witches were said to dance beneath walnut trees in Italy.

Magickal Uses

  • Health: Carried to strengthen the heart and ward off rheumatism.
  • Caution: Attracts lightning; avoid carrying during electrical storms.
  • Wishes: Receiving a bag of walnuts brings the fulfillment of wishes.
  • Headaches: Leaves in a hat or around the head prevent headaches and sunstroke.
  • Family planning: On the wedding day, a woman may place roasted walnuts in the bodice equal to years to remain childless.

Beauty & Skincare

Walnut hulls are used in exfoliants for their gentle abrasive texture, buffing away dead skin cells to reveal a brighter complexion. Strong walnut infusions serve as a hair rinse for dark hair, contributing subtle color-deepening and shine. Internally, walnuts contain uridine, a compound that supports mood and contributes to a general sense of wellbeing.
