Ghost Magick

Hanging alyssum in every corner of a house is believed to exorcise a ghost that has taken up residence within it.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Protection, Moderating Anger

Magickal Uses

Dioscorides recommended the alyssum as an amulet, for it has the power to "expel charms." Hung up in the house it protects against fascination, that magickal process which is also known as "glamour."

Alyssum also has the power to cool down an angry person if placed in the hand or on the body, and was even said to cure hydrophobia (rabies).

The herb's calming influence makes it particularly valuable for peace-keeping magick and protecting against manipulation or enchantment by others.

USDA Hardiness Zone for Lobularia maritima sourced from Missouri Botanical Garden Plant Finder. Hardy in zones 9–11; commonly grown as an annual elsewhere.
