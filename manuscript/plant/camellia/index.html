Camellia (Camellia japonica) is an evergreen shrub with glossy leaves and large, showy flowers.

Magical Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Riches

Magical Uses

For prosperity: If you wish riches to come into your life, place some fresh camellia flowers in a clear bowl of water. Place this on your altar while visualizing yourself as being rich. The water should be changed every three days, and fresh flowers should be placed in the bowl.

Symbolism & Mythology

In Japan the camellia stands for friendship, harmony, and grace, though to the samurai class it carried a darker meaning, a symbol of death and the fleeting nature of life. In the nineteenth century the flower became an object of feverish speculation and great expense, a status symbol within reach only of the very wealthy.

Though it resembles the rose, the camellia holds a more sedate air for all its quiet sensuality, and it has long stood for pride and aloofness. Alexandre Dumas the younger drew on this in his novel La Dame aux Camellias, whose heroine carries white camellias for twenty-five days and then red ones, a coded signal of when she is sexually available.

Meaning When Gifted

When camellia is gifted it carries the meaning of admiration, perfection, and gratitude, and it makes a good-luck gift for a man.

  • Pink Camellia: Longing
  • Red Camellia: You're a flame in my heart
  • White Camellia: You're adorable
