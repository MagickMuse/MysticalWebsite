Briony (Bryonia dioica) is a climbing vine with heart-shaped leaves and red berries.

Magical Properties

  • Element: Fire
  • Powers: Image Magic, Money, Protection

Folk Names

Gout Root, Ladies' Seal, Mad Root, Snake Grape, Tamus, Tetterberry, Wild Hops, Wild Vine, Wood Vine, English Mandrake

Magical Uses

Author's note: The source author does not recommend using this herb at all.

For image magic: The briony root is often used as a substitute for the rare mandrake root in various image magic operations.

For money: A bryony root is set on a piece of money during the full Moon to cause one's riches to grow. Money placed near a briony root will increase, as long as it is left there.

For protection: The root is also hung in houses and gardens as a protection against the effects of bad weather.

⚠️ Danger: This plant is extremely poisonous and should never be taken internally.

Meaning When Gifted

When briony is gifted it carries the meaning of "be my support."
