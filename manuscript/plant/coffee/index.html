Coffee (Coffea arabica) is an evergreen shrub that produces the beans used to make coffee.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Mental Powers, Divination, Energy

Magickal Uses

For mental powers: Coffee beans are chewed to enhance mental powers and aid in concentration.

For energy: The drink is consumed to lend energy to any form of magic.

For divination: Coffee grounds left in a cup are used to read the future by their patterns and shapes.

For magickal enhancement: Add ground coffee to any incense to make it more powerful and effective.

Psychic Replenishment

Coffee does more than revive the body. Its aroma is recommended for psychic depletion, especially for those who have also been physically ill for an extended time. Brew strong coffee; drinking it is optional, as what matters here is inhaling the aroma. For a stronger working, see Coffee Power Blast.
