Lucky Hand (Orchis spp.) is a famed New Orleans root of luck, employment, protection, money, and travel.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Venus

Element: Water

Powers: Employment, Luck, Protection, Money, Travel

Magickal Uses

  • Conjure usage: Placed in sachets and conjure bags for general success; carried to obtain and keep employment; wards off ills.
  • Rose oil preparation: Soak several roots (hands) in a jar of rose oil. When you need something, wear one root in the appropriate place: near the heart for love, in the shoe for travel, in the purse or wallet for money, etc.

Amulet Uses

A lucky hand root carried as an amulet returns evil back to the sender.
