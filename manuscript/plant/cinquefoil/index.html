Cinquefoil (Potentilla canadensis, P. reptans) is a low-growing herb with five-leaflet compound leaves and yellow flowers.

Magickal Properties

  • Powers: Money, Protection, Prophetic Dreams, Sleep

Folk Names

Five Fingers, Five-Finger Blossom, Five-Finger Grass, Five-Leaf Grass, Goosegrass, Goose Tansy, Moor Grass, Pentaphyllon, Silver Cinquefoil, Synkefoyle

Magickal Uses

Frog lore: Frogs were said to enjoy sitting around this herb, a sign of its connection to earth and water energies.

Medieval magick: Cinquefoil was an ingredient in many medieval spells and love divinations.

For protection: Hang cinquefoil at the door as a protection. Hang from the bed or doors to guard the home while you sleep.

For money: Carry the herb in a green sachet to attract money, or wash your hands and forehead in an infusion of cinquefoil for nine consecutive mornings to ensure that money will flow to you.

For prophetic dreams: Place some cinquefoil under your pillow, and you will dream of your future mate.

For magickal enhancement: The five-pointed leaves make this a powerful herb for protection spells and for breaking hexes and curses. A cinquefoil hung from the front door guards the home against evil.

In court: Chew the leaves of cinquefoil before going to court, and the judge will look favorably upon you.

As an all-purpose herb: Cinquefoil represents love, money, health, power, and wisdom, making it one of the most versatile all-purpose magickal herbs.

Prosperity sachet recipe: Mix equal parts cinquefoil, cinnamon, and cloves with lemon balm, then add a whole vanilla or tonka bean. Place in a rich purple cloth bag and carry to increase riches.

The protection clover: Five finger grass, a very close relative of tormentil, is to protection what the four leaf clover is to luck. A perfect five leafed specimen, pressed and preserved, makes a prized protective amulet.

Meaning When Gifted

When cinquefoil is gifted it carries the meaning of a beloved child.

Tormentil (Related Use)

Tormentil, folk-described as the four-petal form of cinquefoil, is ruled by the Sun, tied to the element of Fire, and associated with the deity Thor.

Necromancy: An infusion of tormentil is said to protect practitioners journeying to the realms of the dead, helping ensure a safe return, and mediums have historically drunk it to guard against lasting possession by spirits.

Love and protection: The infusion is also drunk for personal protection, and the plant is hung in the home to drive away evil. It is served to a loved one to keep their affection, or carried to attract love.

Family harmony: Known by the folk name "Flesh and Blood," tormentil is hung over entrance doors and bedroom thresholds to promote family happiness and harmony.
