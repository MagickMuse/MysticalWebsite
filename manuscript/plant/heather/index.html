Heather (Calluna vulgaris; Erica vulgaris) carries luck, rain‑calling, and ghost‑conjuring virtues.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold)

Planet: Venus

Element: Water

Deities: Venus/Aphrodite, Isis, Cybele

Powers: Love, Protection, Rain-Making, Luck, Ghost Conjuring

Magickal Uses

  • Summer Solstice: Heather rules the Summer Solstice, symbolizing love and passion.
  • Sacred to Venus: Heather is sacred to Venus; red heather is sacred to Isis. Associated with bees and their deity Cybele.
  • White heath: White heath is a lucky charm against acts of unwanted passion.
  • Protection and luck: Carried to guard against rape and violent crime, or simply to bring good luck. White heather is best.
  • Rain magic: Burned with fern outdoors to attract rain.
  • Spirit work: Long used to conjure ghosts.

Color Correspondences

  • Red heather: Love
  • White heather: Protection (best for luck and protection work)
  • Purple heather: Spiritual development

Bridal Use

White heather carried in a bridal bouquet brings luck and protection to the bride.

Meaning When Gifted

When heather is gifted its meaning varies by color.

  • Lavender Heather: Admiration, solitude
  • White Heather: Protection, wishes will come true
