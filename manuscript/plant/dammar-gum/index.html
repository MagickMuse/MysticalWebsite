Dammar Gum is a Malayan resin from the genus Dammara, used in incense-making and historically for caulking ships.

Varieties

  • White Dammar comes from Vateria indica
  • Black Dammar (Black Dhup) comes from Canarium strictum

Usually found in powder form, dammar resin is used in making incense cones.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Mercury
  • Element: Earth / Air
  • Powers: Divination, Prediction

Magickal Uses

For divination: Burn dammar gum resin as incense to support divination workings and enhance prophetic insight.

For prediction: Work dammar into incenses designed to sharpen foresight and improve the accuracy of oracular work.

Historical use: Damars were once used for caulking ships, connecting this resin to journeys, navigation, and finding one's way.

Cat's Eye Incense

Dammar is an Asian tree whose resin is burned as incense. Known in Germany as "cat's eye incense," it is said to enhance clairvoyance, psychic perception, and the ability to see what could not be seen before. Burn dammar before psychic journeying and whenever extra clairvoyance is needed.
