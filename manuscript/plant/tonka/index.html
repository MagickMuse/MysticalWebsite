Source — Specific Uses

Carry the bean in love sachets to attract love. Seeds used in perfumery, sachets, and other mixtures for their scent and as a fixative. Also carried as love talismans. Said to bring good luck.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Powers: Love, Money, Courage, Wishes

Ritual Uses

None specified in the source text.

Magickal Uses

  • Love: Fragrant beans in love sachets and mixtures; carried to attract love.
  • Money and luck: Worn or carried to attract money and bring luck.
  • Courage and health: Worn to grant courage and to ward off illnesses.
  • Wishes: Hold a tonka bean, visualize the wish, then toss it into running water to make it come true.

Amulet Uses

A tonka bean carried as an amulet attracts good luck and wealth.
