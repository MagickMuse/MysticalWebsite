Grains of Paradise (Aframomum melegueta; Amomum melegueta) are pungent, peppery seeds used to spark luck, passion, and prosperity.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Lust, Luck, Love, Money, Wishes

Magickal Uses

For fumigation and exorcism: Burn mixed with other herbs to fumigate and exorcise a place. Do not do this with other people or pets present in the house.

For love, lust, luck, and money: Add to sachets and spells to inflame and draw these conditions.

Wishing charm: Hold some in your hands, focus on your wish, then throw a little of the herb to each direction, beginning in the North and ending in the West.
