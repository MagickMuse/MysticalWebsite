Lily of the Valley (Convallaria majalis) brightens mind and heart.

Silver, Witches, and the Return of Spring

Lily of the valley has long been said to attract silver, most likely because its small white bells gleam like coins in the darkness of its broad leaves. It was counted a flower of witches, growing where they lived in shaded and often forested places, and yet it was also a flower of spring and new life, blooming as the year turns back toward the light.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold; existing DB property also Feminine)

Planet: Mercury

Element: Air (source: Earth/Air)

Deities: Hermes, Thoth, Maat (from source); Apollo, Aesculapius

Powers: Knowledge, Peace, Mental Powers, Happiness, Memory

Magickal Uses

  • Clarity and memory: Used to improve the memory and mind.
  • Cheer: Placed in a room, the flowers cheer the heart and lift the spirits of those present.

From the source book: A water distilled from the flowers called aqua aurea ("golden water") was once thought to possess marvelous virtues to strengthen the memory and brain and to comfort the heart. Legend says it grows where the blood of a dragon had been spilled. Also used in perfumery.

Safety: Poison. ☠️

Meaning When Gifted

When lily of the valley is gifted it carries the meaning of sweetness, the tears of the Virgin Mary, happiness, and humility.
