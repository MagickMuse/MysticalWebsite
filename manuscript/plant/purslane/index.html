Purslane is a protective and love-attracting herb known for its ability to promote peaceful sleep and bring happiness to the home. This succulent plant with its fleshy leaves is particularly valued for its gentle protective qualities and its power to ward off nightmares and negative energies.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Moon

Element: Water

Powers: Sleep, Love, Luck, Protection, Happiness

Magickal Uses

  • Sleep Protection: If purslane is laid on the bed, no nightmares will disturb its occupants during the night, providing peaceful and restorative sleep.
  • Love and Luck: Carried, purslane draws love and luck while also keeping evil at bay, creating a shield of positive energy around the bearer.
  • Battle Protection: Soldiers carried purslane to protect themselves in battle, drawing on its protective properties during times of danger and conflict.
  • Home Blessing: Purslane sprinkled around the home spreads happiness throughout, creating an atmosphere of joy and contentment for all inhabitants.
  • Versatile Protection: This humble plant offers comprehensive protection - from physical harm in battle to spiritual attacks through nightmares.

Traditional Use: Purslane's gentle yet effective protective qualities make it especially suitable for family homes and those seeking peaceful, undisturbed rest.

Safety Note: Not recommended for internal use if pregnant or if you have kidney stones.
