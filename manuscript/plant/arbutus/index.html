Magickal Uses

The berries were historically consumed in Ireland, where the shrub grows in abundance, or fermented into a mildly narcotic wine used for vision work and prophecy. The plant is suited to divination rites, lunar workings, and any practice calling on the veil between realms.

Meaning When Gifted

When arbutus is gifted it carries the meaning of "thee only do I love."
