Gourd (Curcurbita spp.; Lagenaria siceraria) includes many hard‑shelled fruits long used as protective charms and ritual tools.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Moon

Powers: Protection

Symbolism

In Taoism the gourd is a symbol of good health. Li Tie Guai, one of the eight Taoist Immortals, carried the gourd as his personal emblem, and because he was able to leave his body at will, it came to signify release from the material world. As a vessel for carrying water, the gourd also stands for the body as the container of the soul.

Magickal Uses

Door ward: Gourds hung at the front door protect against fascination and evil influences.

Carry charm: Pieces of gourd carried in the pocket or purse ward off evil.

Rattles: Dried gourds filled with beans make powerful rattles to drive away evil spirits.

Scrying bowl: A dried gourd with the top cut off is filled with water and used as a scrying vessel.

Safety notes: Some gourds are poisonous; only eat culinary varieties sold as food.
