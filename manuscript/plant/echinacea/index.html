Mabon

Echinacea (Echinacea angustifolia, E. purpurea) is a purple coneflower with distinctive spiky flower heads.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Cold
  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Earth / Water
  • Powers: Healing, Strengthening Spells

Folk Names

Purple Coneflower, Black Sampson, Rudbeckia

Magickal Uses

Harvest timing: The flower may be harvested after the seeds mature on the cone, while the petals are still present. The root may also be used.

For healing and infection resistance: This herb increases the body's resistance to infection. Its action is antiseptic and it can be taken in capsule form for boils, cancer, and other blood impurities.

For strengthening spells: Echinacea is used as an offering to spirits to strengthen spells and rituals. It can be added to any mixture to give it extra power.

Medicinal Uses

Echinacea is one of the most widely recognized stimulating herbs in Western herbalism. It is used in short, targeted courses at the onset of illness to activate the immune system and help the body mount a faster response to infection. Because it is a stimulating herb rather than a tonic, it is most effective when used for brief periods rather than continuously; extended use can diminish its effectiveness. Echinacea is best reserved for acute situations rather than incorporated into a daily routine.

Magickal Teas

Echinacea can be brewed as a magickal tea to bolster the body's self-defense, promote empowerment, and invoke healing devas. Drink 1 to 2 cups only during the spell event itself.
