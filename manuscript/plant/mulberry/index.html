Mulberry (Morus nigra; M. rubra) protects and strengthens the will; its wood makes stout wands.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Cold)

Planet: Mercury (conflict: existing DB property also has Jupiter)

Element: Air (source: Earth/Air)

Deities: Minerva/Athena, Mercury/Hermes, Thoth, Maat (from source); San Ku Fu Jen, Diana (existing)

Powers: Protection, Strength, Knowledge

Magickal Uses

  • Garden ward: Protects the garden from lightning.
  • Will and wards: Aid to workings on the will; wood is a powerful protectant against evil.
  • Tools: Wands are made of mulberry.

From the source book: Use in incense or sachet bags; this herb is sacred to Minerva especially and is a symbol of wisdom.

The World Tree of China

In China the mulberry is held to be the World Tree, joining the Heavens, the Earth, and the regions below. A sacred grove of mulberries was planted outside the eastern gate of the early royal cities, and the tree was bound to that direction because, as the house of the Mother of the Suns, the Sun climbed the mulberry to rise each day. Its wood was made into bows that shot away any evil influence streaming from the points of the compass. There is evidence that the mulberry was revered in Islam as well, for the tree is often found close to Muslim sanctuaries.
