Sesame (Sesamum indicum) is famed for opening ways, revealing hidden paths, and drawing money, with deep ties to obstacle‑removing rites under Ganesha. Eaten, the seeds inflame lust. The legendary phrase “Open Sesame!” points to the seed’s power to discover hidden treasures and open locked ways.

Magickal Uses

Keeping an open jar of sesame seeds in the home attracts cash; refresh monthly. Eating the seeds quickens desire. Working with sesame in petitions to Ganesha removes obstacles and reveals hidden doors and opportunities.
