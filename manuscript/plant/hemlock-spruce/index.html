Hemlock Spruce Essential Oil

Hemlock Spruce is an evergreen conifer native to the west coast of North America and northeastern forests, with the essential oil mainly produced from trees in Vermont, New York, New Hampshire, Virginia, and Wisconsin. Related species include the spruces (Picea spp.), western hemlock (Tsuga heterophylla), and Douglas fir (Pseudotsuga taxifolia).

Not to be confused with Hemlock, an unrelated and deadly herb in the carrot family.
