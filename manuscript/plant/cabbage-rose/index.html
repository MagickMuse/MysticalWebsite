Cabbage Rose Absolute

Cabbage Rose (Rosa centifolia) is the source of rose maroc, French otto of rose, and the rose de mai concrete and absolute. It belongs to the Rosaceae family and is one of the great perfumery roses.

Characteristics

The rose generally used for oil production is strictly a hybrid between R. centifolia, a pink rose, and R. gallica, a dark red rose. The variety, known as rose de mai, grows to a height of 2.5 metres and produces an abundance of flowers with large pink or rosy-purple petals. There are two subspecies, one more spiny than the other.

Origin & Distribution

The birthplace of the cultivated rose is believed to be ancient Persia. Cabbage rose is now cultivated mainly in Morocco, Tunisia, Italy, France, Yugoslavia, and China. The concrete, absolute, and oil are mainly produced in Morocco; the absolute also in France, Italy, and China.

Related Species

There are over 10,000 cultivated rose varieties, and several subspecies of R. centifolia depending on country of origin. Other therapeutic rose species include the apothecary Rose (Rosa gallica) of traditional Western medicine, the oriental or tea rose (Rosa indica), the Chinese or Japanese rose (Rosa rugosa), and the Turkish or Bulgarian damask rose (Rosa damascena) also extensively cultivated for its oil. The French or Moroccan cabbage rose has narcotic properties and is reputed to be more aphrodisiac than the Bulgarian damask, possibly due to its higher phenyl ethanol content. Rosehip seed oil from Rosa rubiginosa is a very effective skin treatment that promotes tissue regeneration and is good for scars, burns, and wrinkles.

Folk Tradition

The healing virtues of the rose have been known since antiquity. Up to the Middle Ages, roses played an essential part in the materia medica and still fulfil an important role in Eastern medicine. They were used for digestive and menstrual problems, headaches and nervous tension, liver congestion, poor circulation, fever (plague), eye infections, and skin complaints. The symbolism connected with the rose is among the richest and most complex of any plant: traditionally associated with Venus, the Goddess of love and beauty. Recorded actions of cabbage rose oil include antidepressant, antiphlogistic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, anti-tubercular, antiviral, aphrodisiac, astringent, bactericidal, choleretic, cicatrisant, depurative, emmenagogue, haemostatic, hepatic, laxative, regulator of appetite, sedative, stomachic, and tonic of the heart, liver, stomach, and uterus.
