♾️Immortality

Acanthus (Acanthus mollis) is a hardy, thistle-like shrub of warm, dry climates, sending up long spikes of flowers above its foliage.

Characteristics

The plant is best known for its large leaves, which can reach as much as twelve inches in both length and width. The bold shape of that leaf was stylized in ancient times into a decorative motif, most famously carved at the tops of Corinthian columns.

Symbolism

Because the acanthus is so resilient a plant, it became a symbol of immortality. Where it appears carved on gravestones, this is its hidden meaning.

Meaning When Gifted

When acanthus leaves are gifted they carry the meaning of admiration for the arts.
