Kelp (Laminaria digitata; Laminaria spp.) is an ocean ally for wind, money, and business blessing.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine

Planet: Jupiter

Element: Water

Powers: Travel, Protection, Money, Winds

Magickal Uses

  • Wind spells: Stand on the shore, hold a long fresh strand, and whirl it clockwise above your head while whistling to call the winds.
  • Attract customers: Make an infusion and scrub the floors and doors of a place of business to bring in customers and good vibrations. Add to money spells.
  • Money jar: Fill a small jar with whiskey, add kelp, cap tightly, and place in the kitchen window to ensure a steady flow of money into the household.
