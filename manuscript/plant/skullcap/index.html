Skullcap

Notes/Warnings
Avoid during pregnancy and while breastfeeding. High doses may cause liver toxicity. Do not confuse with germander skullcap (Teucrium) which has caused serious liver damage.
Scientific Name
Scutellaria lateriflora
Gender
Feminine
Also called
Scullcap, Mad Dog Skullcap, Helmet Flower, Hoodwort, Quaker Bonnet, Virginian Skullcap, Blue Skullcap, Scutellaria lateriflora

Skullcap (Scutellaria lateriflora) is a North American perennial herb in the mint family (Lamiaceae), traditionally used in herbalism for its calming and nervine properties.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Saturn
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Peace, Love, Fidelity, Relaxation

Magickal Uses

Skullcap is used in peace and calming workings, fidelity sachets, and blends intended to promote restful sleep. It appears in protective herbal formulations alongside other nervine and calming botanicals.

Folk Herbalism

Traditionally used as a nervine tonic for anxiety, insomnia, nervous exhaustion, and tension. It has a long history of use in North American folk medicine for calming overactive nervous states.
