Lemon Verbena (Lippia citriodora) is a bright, fragrant ally for clear thinking and gentle love.

Source Entry Notes

⚠️ Gender conflict: Existing: Feminine. Source: Hot (Masculine). Both preserved.

Source astrology added: Jupiter.

Source deities added: Amun, Danu/Dôn, the Dagda, Thor.

Source folk names added: Verveine Citronella, Herb Louisa.

Source — Specific Uses

A mild sedative in tea with an overpowering lemon scent. The leaves grow in sets of three.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold)

Planet: Moon (source: Venus)

Element: Air

Deities: Isis, Osiris, Juno/Hera, Jupiter/Zeus, Odin (from source)

Powers: Protection, Love, Purification, Dreams (wish work)

Magickal Uses

  • Love: Used in love sachets and mixtures to draw affection.
  • Purification and clarity: Added to cleansing blends and baths to clear stagnant energy and brighten mood.
  • Wish divination (traditional): Place lemon verbena under the pillow while focused on a wish; dreams that relate are auspicious for manifestation.
  • Attractiveness: Wear to make oneself attractive to others.
  • Strengthening herb: Often added to charms to give them extra power.
  • Culinary: Leaves can be substituted for lemon or mint in poultry, fish, and stuffing recipes.
