Caraway (Carum carvi) is a biennial European herb whose curved, ridged seeds are widely used as a kitchen spice.

Characteristics

A biennial herb growing up to 0.75 metres tall with a much-branched stem, finely cut feathery leaves, and umbels of small white flowers above a thick, tapering root. The small curved seeds carry five distinct pale ridges.

Origin & Distribution

Native to Europe and western Asia, and naturalized across North America. Now widely cultivated, especially in Germany, the Netherlands, Scandinavia, and Russia. Plants grown in northerly latitudes produce more oil.

Other Species

Several regional varieties exist. The English, Dutch, and German types descend from a Prussian line and are chemically distinct from the Scandinavian variety.

Folk Medicine

A traditional remedy for dyspepsia, intestinal colic, menstrual cramps, poor appetite, laryngitis, and bronchitis. Caraway also promotes milk secretion in nursing mothers and is listed in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia as a specific remedy for flatulent colic in children.

In the kitchen, caraway is used extensively in bread, cakes, and cheeses across Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, and Scandinavia. The German brandy Kummel is distilled from caraway seeds.

Magickal Properties

  • Powers: Protection, Lust, Health, Anti-theft, Mental Powers

Magickal Uses

For passion: Add caraway seeds to love sachets and charms to attract love in the physical, passionate sense.

For fickleness: Caraway was used in spells to cure fickleness in lovers; a lover will never prove unfaithful if given food seasoned with the seeds.

For protection from evil spirits: Any object which holds caraway seeds is theft-proof. Caraway also protects against all manner of evil spirits, and is used in protection sachets and spells. The seeds are chewed to gain the love of anyone. It is used in lust formulas as well.

For mental clarity: Caraway strengthens the memory, and a lover will never prove unfaithful if given food seasoned with the seeds.

For anti-theft: Place some caraway seeds in any object you wish to protect against theft.

For health: The seeds, when carried, protect the bearer from illness. They are especially effective against colic in children if sewn into a small pillow and placed in the crib.

Infant Protection

Caraway seeds placed in a small charm bag and tucked under a child's mattress are used to protect the infant from harm and malevolent forces.

Amulet Uses

Caraway seed carried as an amulet protects a child from illness.

Culinary Uses

Parts used: Seeds

Caraway seeds bring protection and fidelity into the kitchen. They are used to season breads, cheeses, and savory dishes.
