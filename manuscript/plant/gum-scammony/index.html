Gum Scammony (Convolvulus scammonia) is a climbing plant native to the eastern Mediterranean and western Asia, producing a powerful resinous gum from its roots.

Botanical Information

Scammony is a climbing vine related to morning glory and bindweed. The resin is extracted from the root and was historically used as a strong purgative in medicine. The dried resin appears as dark, brittle pieces with a faint, unpleasant odor.

Historical Use

Scammony appears in ancient magical formulas, particularly those involving consecration of talismans and banishing work. Its inclusion in such recipes reflects the historical practice of using powerful (and often dangerous) substances in ceremonial magic.

Safety Warning

Gum Scammony is a violent purgative and is toxic. It should never be ingested or handled carelessly. This entry exists for historical documentation. Do not attempt to use this substance.
