Houseleek (Sempervivum tectorum) is the roof‑keeper of luck, love, and lightning‑ward.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Hot)

Planet: Mars (existing entry had Jupiter)

Element: Fire (existing entry had Air)

Deities: Thor, Horus, Tiw

Powers: Energy, Protection, Luck, Love, Prosperity

Magickal Uses

  • Roof ward: Grown on the roof it protects the building from lightning.
  • Sacred to Thor: Protects the home it grows on or near from fire, storms, and lightning, while ensuring prosperity.
  • Anti-sorcery: Guards against sorcery.
  • Rooftop plant: Grows on rooftops, especially in England and Europe.
  • Love charm: Worn fresh and renewed every few days to attract love.
