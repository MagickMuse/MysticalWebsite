History

Betony was once an immensely popular medicinal, used extensively in herbalism. Modern drugs have since replaced its therapeutic uses, but its magickal powers remain. An old Italian saying advises, "Sell your coat and buy betony."

Magickal Properties

Powers: Protection, Purification

Magickal Uses

Betony is a highly magickal herb in the Druidic tradition. It was said to have power against evil spirits, fearful visions, nightmares, and despair wherever it was carried, hung, or strewn in the house.

For protection: Betony's chief gift is protection: the power to repel and disperse negative forces, especially those that threaten to overwhelm. Sprinkle betony near all doors and windows to create a protective boundary. Carry the herb as an amulet against evil spirits, or wear it around the neck to repel malicious spirits. Burn betony on Midsummer's Eve to tap its peak power, or burn it whenever needed.

For sleep: Stuff a sleep pillow with betony to keep away bad dreams and nightmares.

For purification: Add to all incenses of purification and protection. Burn in outdoor fires at the Summer Solstice and leap through the cleansing smoke for ritual purification.
