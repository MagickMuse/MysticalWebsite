Clove (Syzygium aromaticum) is an aromatic evergreen tree whose dried flower buds are a popular spice with powerful magickal properties.

Magickal Uses

Cloves are worn or carried to drive away hostile and negative forces, and burned as incense to stop others from gossiping. Carrying cloves strengthens and enhances the memory. Add cloves to sachets for attracting romantic interest; they add warmth and passion to love magick. Applying a drop of clove oil to a sore tooth stops the pain. The undeveloped flower buds are used in incense, potpourris, pomanders, and perfumes. Cloves are burned to purify an area and drive out negativity and evil spirits, and are added to exorcism incenses. Carrying cloves in a charm bag attracts wealth and prosperity. Burning clove incense while studying enhances mental acuity and memory.

Amulet Uses

A clove carried as an amulet calls a lover.

Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: Comfort, exorcism, love and sex, money and riches, purification, protection, raising energetic vibration, spirituality, stopping gossip.

Culinary Uses

Cloves are widely used in baking, adding warm, aromatic depth to spiced cakes, cookies, and seasonal recipes. In kitchen witchery, cloves also lend their purifying and psychic-enhancing properties to any dish they flavor.

Seasonal Associations

Clove is a winter spice aligned with Yule and Imbolc, carrying the magickal principle of repose and rest.

Aphrodisiac Properties

Clove buds resemble small phalluses, a visual association that has reinforced their aphrodisiac reputation. In parts of Indonesia, a clove tree is traditionally planted at the birth of a baby boy.

Botanical Description

A slender evergreen tree with a smooth grey trunk reaching up to 12 metres in height. Large bright green leaves stand in pairs on short stalks. At the start of the rainy season, long buds appear with a rosy-pink corolla at the tip; as the corolla fades, the calyx slowly turns deep red. The buds are beaten from the tree and dried to produce the cloves of commerce. The tree belongs to the Myrtaceae family.

Origin & Distribution

Believed to be native to Indonesia and now cultivated worldwide, especially in the Philippines, the Molucca Islands, and Madagascar. The main oil-producing countries are Madagascar and Indonesia. The clove tree has been cultivated in plantations for over 2,000 years. Wild clove trees originally found in the Moluccas produce an essential oil that contains no eugenol at all.

Folk Tradition (Medicinal)

Tincture of cloves has been used for skin infections such as scabies and athlete's foot, for digestive upsets, to dress the umbilical cord, for intestinal parasites, and to ease the pain of childbirth when steeped in wine. It is most widely known for relieving toothache, and clove tea is used to relieve nausea. In Chinese medicine, clove oil is applied to diarrhoea, hernia, bad breath, and bronchitis. In Indonesia, the Kretak cigarette is a popular preparation made from two parts tobacco and one part cloves.

Meaning When Gifted

When clove is gifted it carries the meaning of "I love you secretly."
