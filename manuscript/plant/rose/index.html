In Love Workings

The rose is considered the most fitting flower for traditional love workings and the most germane to matters of romantic magick. The altar triangle in a philter or love working is encircled with a ring of fresh flowers; the rose is the preferred choice. It has always been the Lady's emblem in the annals of courtly love and among the symbols of alchemy. The rose's perfume and subtle magnetism are held to be especially in accord with works of love and enchantment. Kabbalistic legend associates the rose with Balkis, Queen of Sheba, reputedly a witch whose wisdom was magical in nature. The lily, by contrast, passed into Christian symbolism and carries connotations of cool chastity rather than passionate love.
