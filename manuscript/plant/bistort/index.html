Powers: Clairvoyance, Fertility, Divination

Magickal Uses

Bistort is a herb of psychics and diviners. Use it in divination incenses, especially blended with frankincense, to enhance clairvoyance and psychic sight.

For fertility: Carry bistort on your person if you wish to conceive.

Parts used: The herb, or the dried root gathered in March.
