Buckwheat (Fagopyrum spp.) is a grain crop with heart-shaped leaves and white flowers.

Magical Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Venus
  • Element: Earth
  • Powers: Money, Protection

Folk Names

Beechwheat, Brank, French Wheat, Saracen Corn

Magical Uses

For protection: Grind the seeds (make flour) and sprinkle around your house in a circle to keep evil from it. Or, use to form magic circles on the floor around you while performing magic.

For prosperity: Add a few grains of buckwheat to money incenses, and keep some in the kitchen to guard against poverty.

Symbolism & Folklore

Buckwheat was carried into Europe by the Crusaders, who knew it as Saracen wheat. It later took the name buckwheat because its three-cornered seed resembles the beechnut, from boc, the Anglo-Saxon word for beech.

In Japan its flour is made into soba noodles. Because goldsmiths once used buckwheat dough to gather up stray flecks of gold, soba became a charm for drawing wealth, and the noodles are eaten on New Year's Eve to invite good fortune in the year ahead, especially of the financial kind.
