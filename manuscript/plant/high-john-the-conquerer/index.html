High John the Conqueror (Ipomoea purga or I. jalapa) is the legendary conquering root of power, luck, and success.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Cold)

Planet: Mars (source: Saturn)

Element: Fire (source: Earth)

Deities: Saturn, Kronos, Zeus, Isis, Maat

Powers: Prosperity, Money, Love, Success, Happiness, Protection, Legal Matters, Hex-Breaking

Magickal Uses

  • Money draw: Anoint a root with mint oil and tie in a green sachet; carry to attract money.
  • All‑purpose power oil: Cut three roots, place in a bottle of vegetable, olive, or mineral oil; steep for weeks. Use to anoint candles, sachets, and tools.
  • Talisman: Carried as a talisman against danger and trouble.
  • Legal charm: Consecrate the root with the four elements, sleep with it under your pillow for seven nights, then carry it into court and wherever needed.
  • Wards and breaks: Carried to stop depression, bring love and success, protect from hexes, and break or destroy spells.

Safety: Poison. Do not ingest.
