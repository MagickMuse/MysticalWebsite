Bittersweet (Celastrus scandens - American; Solanum dulcamara - European) is a climbing vine with orange berries.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Planet: Mercury
  • Element: Air
  • Powers: Protection, Healing

Folk Names

For American Bittersweet: Wax Work, False Bittersweet

For European Bittersweet: Violet Bloom, Felonwort, Woody Nightshade, Scarlet Berry, Dulcamara, Bitter Nightshade

Magickal Uses

One of Circe's favourite herbs, bittersweet carries the energy of transformation and the darker arts of love magic.

Some bittersweet placed beneath the pillow will help you in forgetting a past love.

The bittersweet is also used to protect against and remove evil from both humans and animals, by tying a small piece of the herb somewhere on the body.

For healing: Tied to the neck, bittersweet cures vertigo or dizziness of the head, according to Culpeper.

⚠️ Safety Note: American Bittersweet is poisonous.

Meaning When Gifted

When bittersweet nightshade is gifted it carries the meaning of truth.
