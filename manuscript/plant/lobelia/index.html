Lobelia (Lobelia inflata) is a storm-halter, cautious love attractant, and powerful cleansing herb with a toxic nature that limits its use to external and symbolic applications.

History

Lobelia found a place in eclectic medicine during the 1800s and saw renewed interest in the 1970s. Sometimes called Indian tobacco, it acts as a stimulant with effects comparable to nicotine. Despite its use in some smoking cessation products, studies have confirmed that lobelia is unsafe for ingestion or inhalation.

Characteristics

Though widely cultivated, lobelia also grows wild in open woods and meadows across eastern North America. Its small blue blossoms make it a visually appealing herb. The plant produces branched stems, with the leaves and flowers being the parts used in practice.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Saturn

Powers: Halting Storms, Love

Magickal Uses

  • Stop storms: Throw some powdered lobelia at an oncoming storm to halt its approach.
  • Love: Used to attract love in spellwork.
  • Space Clearing: Lobelia is used to clear and purify sacred spaces. Burn as incense for offerings or as a cleansing step before a spell or rite, such as blessing a home after a move.
  • Well-Being: Lobelia promotes a general sense of well-being when used in ritual contexts.

Safety: Poison. External, symbolic use only.
