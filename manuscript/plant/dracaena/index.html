Dracaena, Red-Edged (Dracaena marginata) is a mini dragon with sharp, swordlike foliage.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Protection, Courage, Strength

Botanical Connections

The same genus that gives us the power and beauty of the Dragon's Blood tree (Dracaena draco, D. cinnabari) provides us this mini dragon. It still carries the protective Mars energy of its larger counterpart.

Additional Notes

Think of the red-edged dracaena as a sword plant—its sharp, blade-like leaves cut through negativity and protect the home with fiery Mars energy.

Care Tips

They don't flower as such, so they don't have a particular need for bright, direct light—feel free to supply diffuse light. They come from arid climes, so they like dry feet and aren't fond of overwatering.

Protective Qualities

Place near entrances to guard against negative energies entering the home. The sharp foliage acts as an energetic blade, severing unwanted attachments and influences.

Meaning When Gifted

When dracaena is gifted it carries the meaning of inner power.
