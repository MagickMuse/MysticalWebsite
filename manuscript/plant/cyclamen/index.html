Cyclamen (Cyclamen spp.) is a perennial plant with heart-shaped leaves and distinctive swept-back petals.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Venus
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Fertility, Protection, Happiness, Lust

Folk Names

Groundbread, Sowbread, Swine Bread

Magickal Uses

For protection while sleeping: Grow cyclamen in the bedroom as protection while sleeping.

For grief: Carry the blossoms to remove the grief of an ended love affair.

For garden and home protection: Grow cyclamen outside to protect the garden and the house.

For fertility: Carry the flowers to aid in fertility matters. When grown in the bedroom, cyclamen also increases fertility.

For happiness: The flowers are used to ease depression and bring happiness to the home when grown as a house plant.

For love: The cyclamen is also used in love spells to promote lust and to strengthen love.
