Source — Specific Uses

This is the Mideastern equivalent of the oak, as it also has very hard wood. It is also sacred to Jupiter and Bel.

Notes

Of the genus Pistacia, related to Pistachio (P. vera). The terebinth was one of the most important sacred trees of the ancient Near East.
