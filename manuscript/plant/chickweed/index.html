Chickweed (Stellaria media) is a low-growing perennial edible plant with small, white star-shaped flowers.

History

Historically valued as a demulcent -- soothing inflamed mucous membranes -- and as a diuretic, chickweed has largely been treated as a weed despite its practical merits. It carries genuine nutritional value and has long served as forage for chickens and other birds.

Characteristics

Chickweed spreads in dense mats formed by multiple plants growing from a shared taproot system. Its leaves and tender stems are edible and have been used in salads and cooked greens. A reliable herald of the growing season, it is typically among the first plants to emerge in early spring.

Magickal Properties

Chickweed is a healing herb whose bright white blossoms evoke purity and clarity of intention. Because it is edible, consuming the leaves offers a direct means of internalizing its energetic qualities and invoking transformation from within. It is also employed in protection workings and used as a ward against unwanted or intrusive energies.

Magickal Teas

Chickweed can be brewed as a magickal tea to tap into Crone wisdom and support divination work. Drink 1 to 2 cups only during the spell event itself.

Meaning When Gifted

When chickweed is gifted it carries the meaning of "I cling to thee."
