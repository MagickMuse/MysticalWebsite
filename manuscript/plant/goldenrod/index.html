Goldenrod (Solidago spp.) is a tall flowering plant with bright yellow blooms, valued for its divinatory properties and ability to attract good fortune.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Venus

Powers: Money, Divination

Folk Names

Aaron's Rod, Blue Mountain Tea, Goldruthe, Gonea Tea, Sweet Scented Goldenrod, Solidago, Verg d' Or, Wound Weed, Woundwort

Magickal Uses

For divination: To see your future love, wear a piece of goldenrod. He or she will appear on the morrow.

For dowsing: When held in the hand, the flower nods in the direction of hidden or lost objects, or where buried treasure lies.

For luck: If goldenrod springs up suddenly near the house door, unexpected good fortune will soon rain upon the family living there.

For money magick: Goldenrod is also used in money spells.

Meaning When Gifted

When goldenrod is gifted it carries the meaning of "be cautious" and encouragement.
