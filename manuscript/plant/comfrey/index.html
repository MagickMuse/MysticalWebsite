Comfrey (Symphytum officinale) is a healing plant known as "knitbone" for its ability to mend breaks and wounds.

History

Native to Asia and Europe, comfrey also grows widely throughout North America. It has long been associated with the healing of broken bones, gums, and teeth, as well as supporting immune function. Applied as a topical, it was known as "bruisewort" for its effectiveness on minor surface injuries, and as "knitbone" for its use as a poultice to reduce swelling around fractures.

Characteristics

Comfrey is drawn to water and grows wild near rivers and ponds, reaching up to six feet in height. It is recognized by its hairy stalk, purplish-pink flowers, and dark green leaves with deeply impressed veins. Its reputation carries some controversy -- studies linking it to liver damage in lab rats involved quantities far beyond normal use. It is not taken internally, but is widely used in topical preparations to address inflammation, and as an astringent and analgesic.

As a protective amulet during journeys: Pack comfrey in your luggage or carry in a charm bag to protect against travel dangers, delays, and negative experiences. The herb's Saturn energy provides grounding and protection far from home.

For healing: As its folk name "knitbone" suggests, comfrey is used in healing magick. Carry in sachets to speed recovery from injury or illness, or add to healing poppets.

For money drawing: Carry comfrey root in your wallet or purse to attract money and ensure financial stability during travels and in daily life.

For safe relocation: Tuck bits of comfrey root into possessions and furniture before loading them onto a moving van to ensure their safe arrival at the destination.

For long-term goals: Ruled by Saturn, comfrey is associated with slow, deliberate growth and the setting of boundaries. Incorporate it into workings when the intention is fixed on manifesting something over time rather than immediately -- comfrey supports patience, endurance, and the steady building of foundations.

Magickal Teas

Comfrey can be brewed as a magickal tea to support health, draw money, and promote safety. Drink 1 to 2 cups only during the spell event itself.

Beauty & Skincare

Comfrey is applied topically to reduce inflammation and soothe irritated skin. It functions as an astringent and analgesic when used externally, supporting the skin's recovery from minor injuries and swelling. For topical use only — not for internal use.
