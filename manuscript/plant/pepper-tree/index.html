Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Powers: Purification, Healing, Protection

Ritual Uses

Used by Mexican curanderos in healing rites; the sick are brushed with pepper tree branches to absorb illness, then the branches are buried to destroy the disease. Often paired with rue.

Magickal Uses

  • Purification baths: Add leaves to spiritual baths to cleanse negativity and restore vitality.
  • Protective berries: Carry the bright red berries for ongoing protection.
  • Household wards: Branches over or above the bed keep sickness away; a pine‑needle cross by the hearth blocks evil.

Additional Notes

Traditional in Mexican spiritualist and bruja practice; related cleansing work sometimes combines rue with pepper tree.
