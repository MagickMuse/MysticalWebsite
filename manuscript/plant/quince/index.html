Source Entry Notes

Source planet added: Venus (in addition to existing Saturn).

Source deities added: Aphrodite (Venus and Aphrodite are the same deity).

Source folk name added: Coyne.

Source — Specific Uses

Sacred to the Goddess in her Venus/Aphrodite aspect. This is the apple of the Greeks and Hebrews, as well as in other ancient myths. A symbol of love and happiness; also wards against the evil eye.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Protection, Love, Happiness

Ritual Uses

Associated with Venus and ancient matrimonial rites; featured in Roman wedding customs.

Magickal Uses

  • Protection: Carry seed to guard against evil, physical harm, and accidents.
  • Love and happiness: Share a quince between bridal partners to ensure future happiness.
  • Ingenuity charm: Folklore holds that pregnant people who eat quinces often beget “ingenious” children.

Additional Notes

Classic kitchen‑witch fruit for fidelity and joy.
