Prickly Ash is a North American plant traditionally used for its warming and stimulating properties, particularly known for its association with love magick. This spiny tree with its aromatic bark and fruits has been valued in folk medicine and magical practice for its ability to attract affection and enhance romantic connections.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Powers: Love

Magickal Uses

  • Love Attraction: Use the fruits of the prickly ash as a perfume to attract love and enhance romantic appeal.
  • Warming Magic: The plant's fiery nature makes it useful for heating up cold situations and bringing passion to relationships.
  • Stimulating Energy: Carry or use prickly ash to add energy and vitality to magical workings, especially those related to love and desire.
  • Traditional Folk Use: Native American and folk traditions have long recognized prickly ash for its ability to "wake up" dormant energies and bring life to stagnant situations.

Traditional Applications: The fruits can be dried and carried in sachets, or the aromatic oils can be extracted for use as a natural perfume in love magick.

Safety Note: Not recommended for internal use during pregnancy. External use preferred for magical applications.

Source: Cunningham's Encyclopedia of Magical Herbs
