Herb of the Centaurs

Centaury takes its name from the centaurs, since it is believed to be their legendary herb. Among its many gifts is magickal protection.

The Centaur's Herb

Centaury is named in honor of the centaurs, and Chiron the centaur served as spiritual teacher and mentor to many Greek heroes. A favorite of medieval witches' brews, centaury is said to act as a magickal teacher, inducing psychic power. Like mugwort and wormwood, plants of similar power, it tastes bitter (see Centaur's Tea).
