Botanical Information

Scientific Name: Beyeria leshnaultii

Family: Euphorbiaceae

Origin: Western Australia

Type: Native Australian bush

Antiseptic Bush is a small to medium native Australian shrub with fine, aromatic foliage. The plant has a distinctly medicinal, camphoraceous scent that hints at its traditional and magickal uses. It is perhaps best known in the context of Australian Bush Flower Essences, where it is produced by Living Essences of Australia as one of their signature remedies.

Magickal Correspondences

Antiseptic Bush is used primarily in aura cleansing and psychic purification, particularly during sleep. Its flower essence is considered one of the most effective tools for clearing accumulated psychic debris from the energy body. Unlike many cleansing botanicals that work through physical application or smoke, Antiseptic Bush works on the subtle body, making it well suited to dreamwork and overnight energetic maintenance.

Primary uses:

  • Aura cleansing
  • Psychic purification
  • Overnight and dream-state cleansing
  • Removal of energetic residue and spiritual debris

Flower Essence Use

The flower essence is produced by Living Essences of Australia and is used in small doses for regular energetic maintenance. A larger initial dose (twenty drops in a bath before sleep) is used for intensive cleansing, followed by small nightly doses rubbed into the hand or feet. Because the essence works during sleep, results accumulate gradually and are experienced as a general sense of renewal and lightness.

💧
Dosage note: The full bath dose should only be repeated if the remedy has not been used in several months. For ongoing maintenance, a few drops on the skin before bed is sufficient.
