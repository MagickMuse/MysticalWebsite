Button Snake Root (Liatris spicata) is a North American prairie wildflower used in folk magick for love, creativity, and friendship.

Magickal Properties

Element: Earth / Air

Powers: Love, Creativity, Friendship

Magickal Uses

For love: Carry or include in sachets and workings to attract love and deepen existing bonds.

For creativity: Work with button snake root when seeking inspiration or undertaking creative projects.

For friendship: Use in charms and sachets to draw loyal friends and strengthen friendship ties.

Notes

The root is the primary part used in magickal workings.
