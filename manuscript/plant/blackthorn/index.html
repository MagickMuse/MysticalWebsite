Blackthorn (Prunus spinosa) is a small, gnarled tree of the hedgerow, whose vicious thorns make it a dependable stock-proof barrier. Those same thorns carry a double meaning, for they can both attack and defend. Its small, bitter fruits, the sloes, are too sharp to eat but have long been used to flavor gin.

Folk Names

Sloe, Sloe Berry, the Wishing Thorn

Folk Tradition

One of the old names of the blackthorn is the wishing thorn. Its branches were once cut as divining rods, a tricky business given the spines, and shaped into wands for ritual use, where they served as wishing rods. The blackthorn and the hawthorn often grow side by side and are reckoned sisters, the blackthorn the dark counterpart to the light of the hawthorn. Its blossoms open very early, often in April before the leaves unfurl, making it a symbol of fertility.

Despite this, the tree keeps a dark reputation, for its spines can poison those they pierce. This made the blackthorn a dangerous weapon in the wrong hands, and folklore tells of malicious witches who used its spines to pierce the manikins of a cruel sympathetic magick. As with other thorn trees, it has been supposed that the blackthorn furnished the crown of thorns worn by Christ.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Powers: Protection, Defense, Fertility, Divination, Wishing

Magickal Uses

For protection and defense: The thorny branches form a warding barrier, and the wood is carried or worked for defense against harm.

For wishes and divination: Blackthorn wands, the wishing rods of old, are used to focus the will in wishing and divining work. The Blackthorn Wishing Wind is a traditional folk spell in this vein: craft a wand from blackthorn wood and use it to cast wishing spells.

For fertility: As one of the first trees to blossom, the blackthorn lends its early-blooming vigor to workings for fertility and new growth.

For blasting spells: The blackthorn is connected with black magick and blasting spells. The thorns are used for piercing wax images in workings of hostile magic against one's enemies.

Blackthorn wands are frequently fashioned into sturdy walking sticks, keeping ill will away from the bearer as they travel.

Safety Notes

⚠️ Seeds: Poison - Blackthorn seeds are poisonous in large doses. The thorns can also cause painful, slow-healing wounds.

Meaning When Gifted

When blackthorn, also called sloe, is gifted it carries the meaning of difficulty and austerity.
