Oats (Avena sativa) are a versatile Venus herb of calming, love, beauty, and prosperity — known as oatstraw when harvested green for magickal and medicinal use.

History

Native to the Mediterranean region, Avena sativa has been in use since the medieval era, when it was valued for supporting brain health and cognitive function. Today it is most widely cultivated as livestock feed, though its magickal and herbal applications have endured.

Characteristics

Oatstraw is an annual grass, hardy and adaptable to a range of soil conditions. It can reach up to five feet in height and thrives in cool, temperate climates. The plant is the same species that produces the familiar oat grain, but oatstraw is harvested before the seeds fully ripen, while they are still green, and includes the grassy green husks.

Magickal Uses

  • Prosperity work: Use in prosperity and money spells.
  • Calming and anxiety relief: Oatstraw carries strong nerve-calming properties and is used in workings to ease anxiety and restore a sense of ease.
  • Love and aphrodisiac magick: The relaxation and well-being oatstraw promotes is linked to the lowering of inhibitions, making it a natural ally for love spells and aphrodisiac workings. The phrase "sowing your wild oats" reflects its long association with uninhibited desire.
  • Beauty magick: Nourishing to the skin, oatstraw makes an excellent base for beauty workings and glamour spells.
