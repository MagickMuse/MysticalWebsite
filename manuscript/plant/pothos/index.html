🛡️Protection

Pothos (Epipremnum aureum), also known as Devil's Ivy, is a resilient vine with powerful protective properties.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Saturn

Element: Water

Powers: Protection, Resilience, Problem-Solving, Independence

Houseplant Magick

Ivy of all sorts is useful for protection magick. The vines can encase problem issues that arise, and their roots make it possible for them to cling to their support system and survive insurmountable odds.

Resilience Magick

If pothos are cut off from their support system, they have the resources to take care of themselves. This makes them powerful allies for:

  • Independence spells
  • Self-sufficiency work
  • Surviving difficult transitions
  • Bouncing back from setbacks

Care Tips

Pothos is famously hard to kill, thriving in low light and tolerating irregular watering. This resilience translates directly to its magickal properties-it teaches us to adapt and survive.

Protective Applications

Let the vines trail around windows and doorways to create a living protective barrier. As the plant grows, so does its protective reach.
