Ebony (Diospyros ebenum) is a dense, dark hardwood tree producing jet-black heartwood.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Saturn
  • Element: Earth
  • Powers: Protection, Power

Magickal Uses

For protection: Ebony wood repels negativity and is a protective substance.

For magical tools: Ebony wands give the magician pure, unadulterated power. The wood is used to fashion magickal images.

Color, Death, and the Underworld

Ebony wood is intensely dark, incredibly dense and hard, and polishes to a high shine; one of its best-known uses is for the black keys of a piano. Most of its symbolism flows from that color. Pluto, the King of the Underworld, was said to sit upon an ebony throne, and so the wood was bound to death and the Underworld.

Strength and the Cradle

Because its great hardness also made it a symbol of strength and protection, ebony was once a favored wood for the making of babies' cradles.
