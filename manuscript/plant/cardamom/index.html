Cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum) is a perennial herb producing aromatic seed pods used as a spice. Known to pastry chefs as French cinnamon and "grains of paradise," this member of the ginger family has been used for centuries in both culinary and magical applications.

Characteristics

A tall, reed-like perennial herb growing up to 4 metres high, with long, silky, blade-shaped leaves. Long sheathing stems bear small yellowish flowers tipped with purple, followed by oblong red-brown seed pods. The whole plant is aromatic.

Origin & Distribution

Native to tropical Asia, especially the rain forests of southern India. Cultivated extensively in India, Sri Lanka, Laos, Guatemala, and El Salvador, with the bulk of essential oil production coming from India, Europe, Sri Lanka, and Guatemala.

Other Species

A family with many cousins. Round or Siam cardamom (Amomum cardamomum), found in India and China, is used as a local spice and folk medicine. A second oil is produced from wild cardamom (E. cardamomum var. major). Both are kin to Grains of Paradise (Aframomum melegueta) within the wider Zingiberaceae family.

Folk Medicine

Cardamom has been used in traditional Chinese and Indian medicine for over 3000 years, especially for pulmonary disease, fever, digestive complaints, and urinary trouble. Hippocrates recommended it for sciatica, coughs, abdominal pains, spasms, nervous disorders, retention of urine, and even bites from venomous creatures. It remains current in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia as a specific remedy for flatulent dyspepsia.

Culinarily, it is one of the most-used spices of India, the Middle East, Latin America, and parts of Europe.

Historical Significance

The Ebers Papyrus (1550 BC), one of the oldest medical texts known to history, lists cardamom along with over 880 other medicinal herbs. In the realm of the magical, this spicy plant has been used in perfumes, incense, and fragrance for over three thousand years. It has been used to inflame passions and arouse lust, and has been added to wines as potent love potions.
