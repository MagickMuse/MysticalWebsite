Symbol of the Immortals

Like most conifers, the arborvitae stays green through every season, and for this it became an emblem of immortality and everlasting life. In Chinese tradition the thuja was held to belong to the East, the quarter of the rising Sun, where as an undying tree it welcomed the dawn each morning. Above all it was said to belong to the Immortals themselves, the deities who fed upon its resin and its seeds. The Latin name arbor vitae, the "tree of life," carries the same promise of unending vitality.
