Working with the Phases

Waxing Moon

The period from new to full moon is considered especially fertile for sowing seeds of above-ground crops and for repotting or transplanting. During this phase, plant energy and sap are believed to flow upward, supporting growth in plants that yield above the ground. The combined gravitational pull of the moon and sun at the new moon is also thought to draw underground water tables upward, helping plants absorb nutrients and moisture more readily.

Waning Moon

The full moon marks the peak of potency for harvesting plants intended for magickal use. As the cycle shifts into waning, sap and energy are believed to move downward, making this an ideal time for pruning (plants may heal more quickly with less stress), planting bulbs and root crops, and weeding. Harvesting root crops is best timed closer to the new moon, when energy has fully receded below the surface.

Fruitful Signs

The three water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces) and fixed earth sign Taurus are considered the most fertile. Capricorn and Libra are also fertile, though somewhat less so. When the moon moves through these signs, it is a favorable time to plant, bless seeds, and transplant.

  • Cancer: Plant leafy things, plant above ground fruit
  • Scorpio: Prune to encourage growth
  • Pisces: Plant root things
  • Taurus: Plant root crops
  • Capricorn: Prune to strengthen bulbs, roots, stalks growing
  • Libra: Plant flowers and vines

Barren Signs

All three fire signs (Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius) are considered barren, along with Gemini, Aquarius, and Virgo. These signs are less favorable for growth but well-suited to maintenance work: gathering, pruning, and removing weeds and pests.

  • Leo: Kill weeds and pests, cultivate and till
  • Gemini: Harvest herbs and roots
  • Virgo: Plow, till, weed
  • Sagittarius: Prune to stop growth
  • Aquarius: Reap crops, herbs, and roots, ground cultivation, kill weeds and pests
  • Aries : Gather and store herbs and roots, prune to reduce growth
