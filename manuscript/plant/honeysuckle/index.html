Honeysuckle (Lonicera caprifolium) is a sweet, clinging vine that draws money, awakens second sight, and guards the home.

History

Honeysuckle was brought to the United States in the early nineteenth century, where it became established as an invasive plant. Its original purpose was ornamental — it was planted to help control soil erosion.

Characteristics

Honeysuckle is a night-blooming climbing shrub with long flowers that appear in purplish-white or yellowish hues, and it produces black berries. Celebrated for its sweet nectar, honeysuckle is an evergreen plant native to Europe.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Jupiter

Element: Earth

Powers: Prosperity, Clairvoyance, Psychic Awareness, Harmony, Healing, Happiness, Protection

Magickal Uses

  • Money draw: Ring green candles with honeysuckle flowers to attract money, or place them in a vase in the home for the same purpose.
  • Psychic powers: Lightly crush fresh flowers and rub on the forehead to heighten psychic sensitivity and second sight.
  • Home luck and health: A honeysuckle plant growing near the home brings good luck; trained over the door it keeps fevers at bay for the household.
  • Prosperity sachets: Add to all prosperity sachets.
  • Love magick: Honeysuckle's sweet fragrance makes it synonymous with affection. The blossoms represent deep fondness and warmth, and are used in love workings to restore a waning relationship or to encourage the beginning of a new one.

Additional Notes

Honeysuckle is associated with liminal states—those threshold moments between one state and another. Its scent is strongest at evening, the liminal time between day and night, reinforcing these twilight associations.

Safety: Flowers are generally safe. Berries can cause digestive upset.

Meaning When Gifted

When honeysuckle is gifted it carries the meaning of the bond of love.

  • Coral Honeysuckle: I love you
  • Woodbine: Fraternal love
