Flax (Linum usitatissimum) is an annual plant grown for its fiber and oil-rich seeds (linseed).

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Masculine
  • Planet: Mercury
  • Deity: Hulda
  • Powers: Money, Protection, Beauty, Psychic Powers, Healing

Folk Names

Linseed

Ritual Uses

Offered in rites to Hulda (Holle), who taught mortals the arts of spinning and weaving. Flax was once among the plants most closely tied to women's primal power, and legend credits Hulda with first bringing flax to people. The spinning of flax runs through many fairy tales. Linen was made from this plant in ancient Egypt. Flax is a protection against sorcery; kept in the home it protects against disturbing outside influences and promotes peace.

Magickal Uses

For money: Keep seeds in pocket, wallet, or purse. Keep a jar on the altar with coins and flax seed added daily. Flax in the shoe wards off poverty.

For protection and sleep: Blue flowers worn against sorcery. Flax plus mustard by the bed with a pan of water on the other side guards sleep.

For household warding: Red pepper with flax in a box prevents evil from entering.

For beauty and health: Children dancing in growing flax at age seven promotes beauty. Sprinkle altar with seed in healing. Tie a hank around loins for lumbago.

For divination: Drink a cup of flaxseed tea before beginning divination to enhance the process.

For dizziness: Run naked three times through a flax field after sunset to cure dizziness (traditional lore).

Meaning When Gifted

When flax is gifted it carries the meaning of domestic life.
