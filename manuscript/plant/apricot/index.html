Gender: Feminine

Magickal Uses

Eat the fruit to obtain a sweet disposition, or use the juice in love spells and "potions." The leaves and flowers can be added to love sachets, and the pits are carried to attract love.

The sweet nature of the apricot makes it particularly suitable for gentle love magick focused on developing affection and kindness rather than passionate attraction.

Vulva Symbolism and Protection

A deeply cleft apricot is one of the traditional symbolic stand ins for the female genitals, the most protective of all images. Display apricot imagery in the home or wear it as jewelry to borrow that protective power. See Vulva.

Safety Notes

⚠️ Seeds: X - Apricot kernels contain compounds that can be toxic and should not be consumed.

Meaning When Gifted

When apricot blossom is gifted it carries the meaning of timid love.
