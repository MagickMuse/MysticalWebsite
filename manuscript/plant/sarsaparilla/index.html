Magickal Properties

Planet: Jupiter

Powers: Love, Money

Ritual Uses

Included in prosperity and love blends sprinkled around spaces or carried as sachets.

Magickal Uses

  • Money draw: Mix with cinnamon and sandalwood powder; sprinkle around premises to attract money.
  • Love work: Add to love spells to attract or steady affection.

Additional Notes

Most effective when combined with complementary herbs.

Meaning When Gifted

When sarsaparilla is gifted it carries the meaning of loveliness.
