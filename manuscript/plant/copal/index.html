Copal (Protium copal) is a tree resin that has been used as sacred incense in Mesoamerican cultures for thousands of years.

Magickal Properties

  • Element: Fire / Water
  • Powers: Purification, Protection, Spirituality, Prosperity

Folk Names

Copal Resin, Young Amber

Ritual Uses

Copal is one of the most important ritual incenses of Mexico and Central America. The Maya and Aztec civilizations burned copal as an offering to the gods and to create sacred space. It continues to be used in indigenous ceremonies today.

Magickal Uses

Origin and distribution: Copal is of Mexican origin — a fragrant, translucent white resin distilled for Copalquahuitl, the traditional Mexican copali incense. Originally found in Mexico, it has since been found in Zanzibar, West Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar, and India.

For prosperity: Work copal resin into prosperity incenses and offerings; its Jovian rulership makes it a natural ally for workings of abundance and expansion.

For purification: Burn copal to cleanse and purify a space, person, or ritual tools. It clears away negative energies and prepares an area for sacred work.

For protection: Copal smoke creates a protective barrier against negative influences and spirits. It is burned during rituals to maintain a safe, sacred space.

For spirituality: Copal opens the pathways to higher realms and facilitates communication with spirits, ancestors, and deities. It is excellent for meditation and prayer.

For divination and clairvoyance: Inhaling copal's aroma is said to enhance psychic perception and clairvoyant ability. Beyond its use as a general ritual incense, copal is also employed as a tool for systematic divination, providing structured oracles when used with intention.
