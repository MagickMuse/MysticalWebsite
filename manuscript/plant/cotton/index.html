Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum) is a flowering plant cultivated for its soft, white fibers that surround the seeds.

Magickal Properties

  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Earth
  • Powers: Luck, Healing, Protection, Rain, Fishing Magic

Magickal Uses

For luck: Cotton kept in the house brings good luck, as does throwing it over the right shoulder at sunrise.

For fishing: Cotton balls thrown on the water attract the fish.

For rain: To cause rain, throw cotton balls in the air and let them drift in the wind.

For protection: Use cotton in protection spells and healing magick.
