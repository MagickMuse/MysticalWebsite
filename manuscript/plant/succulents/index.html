Succulents (Various spp.) embody the magick of resilience and constancy in adversity.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Moon

Element: Water (stored)

Powers: Resilience, Endurance, Constancy, Adaptability

Additional Notes

These strong, drought-resistant plants are great for people who:

  • Work outside the home
  • Go away on business
  • Frequently forget to water other plant species

They are hearty (though not as hearty as plants in the Bromeliad family or even cacti-check those out if you're worried) and are associated with the magick of resilience and constancy in adversity.

Variety and Beauty

With the rising popularity of succulent gardens, there are cultivars with:

  • Dolphin-shaped leaves
  • People-shaped foliage
  • Every color in the rainbow
⚠️
Shopping Warning: Double-check succulents purchased at larger chain stores, as they likely use plant spray paint to make a plant look more appealing. Plants purchased at big box stores may not look that way once they have grown out a bit. Check near the soil line to be sure.

Magickal Applications

Succulents teach us to store our resources for times of need and to thrive in harsh conditions. Work with them for:

  • Building resilience during difficult periods
  • Maintaining constancy in long-term goals
  • Adapting to challenging circumstances
