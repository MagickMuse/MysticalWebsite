Meadowsweet (Filipendula ulmaria; Spiraea ulmaria) sweetens love, keeps peace, and reveals thieves.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine (source: Hot; existing DB property: Feminine)

Planet: Jupiter

Element: Air (source: Water)

Deities: Jupiter/Zeus, the Dagda, Thor, Diana, Selene (from source)

Powers: Love, Divination, Peace, Happiness

Magickal Uses

From the source book: One of the three most sacred herbs to the Druids: meadowsweet, vervain, and wild mint. Strewn around ritual circles for blessings. Add to love charms and sachets.

  • Love altar: Fresh on the altar for love spells; dried in love mixtures.
  • House peace and cheer: Strewn about the house to keep peace; its scent cheers the heart.
  • Bridal bouquet: Its folk name bridewort reflects its deep affinity with brides. Carried in the bridal bouquet, meadowsweet brings all the happiest marriage blessings.
  • Midsummer sign: If gathered on Midsummer, it reveals thieves—placed on water, if it sinks the thief is a man; if it floats, a woman.
