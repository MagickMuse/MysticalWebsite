Yellow dock is a purifying and prosperity-drawing herb used in both folk medicine and magickal practice.

History

Yellow dock root has been relied upon by herbalists for hundreds of years, valued both as a food source and as a medicinal remedy.

Characteristics

A perennial herb, yellow dock has a deep yellow root that can extend up to five feet into the ground. It has been used as a remedy for constipation. Its green leaves are edible but should only be consumed in small quantities, as they are known to block calcium absorption. The root is considered anti-inflammatory and has been used to soothe irritation of the nasal passages.

Source — Specific Uses

The powder stains things a powerful yellow color. To gain money or prosperity, sprinkle a little inside your mailbox or across the doorway into your place of business.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Purification, Prosperity, Business

Magickal Uses

  • Purification: Yellow dock is used as a cleansing and purifying herb to clear stagnant or negative energy from the body or environment.
  • Prosperity talisman: Carried as an herbal talisman, yellow dock draws abundance and good fortune to the bearer.
  • Attract customers: Ground yellow dock sprinkled around a place of business is a traditional working to draw customers and invite financial success.
