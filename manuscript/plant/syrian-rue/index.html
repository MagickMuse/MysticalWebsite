Uzarlik

Syrian rue is known as harmel in Arabic and uzarlik in Azerbaijani. Although it shares no botanical relationship with true rue (Ruta graveolens), both plants are bound together by a shared aura of sanctity and an exceptional reputation as weapons against the Evil Eye. Syrian rue ranks among the most widely employed magickal and shamanic herbs across North Africa and the Middle East.

The smoke produced by burning uzarlik is unusually powerful: it both removes the Eye from those already afflicted and prevents anyone in its vicinity from casting it. Where uzarlik smoke is present, the Eye is effectively immobilised. For this reason, amuletic use of the plant is widespread throughout the region, appearing routinely at weddings, births, and any occasion centred on something new and vulnerable — a new home, a new car, a newborn child.
