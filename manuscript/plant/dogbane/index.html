Dogbane (Apocynum cannabinum) is a perennial herb with small white or pink flowers and milky sap.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Venus

Element: Water

Powers: Love

Magickal Uses

For love: Dogbane is used in love sachets and spells to attract romantic love. The plant can be carried or worn to draw love to the bearer.

For binding love: Use in knot magic or binding spells to strengthen existing relationships or tie two lovers together.

⚠️ Safety Warning: Not recommended for internal use. The plant contains cardiac glycosides that can be toxic.
