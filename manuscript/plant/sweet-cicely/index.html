Sweet Cicely (Myrrhis odorata) is a tall, feathery-leaved perennial herb with a distinctive sweet, anise-like fragrance strongly reminiscent of myrrh.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Sun

Element: Water

Powers: Love, Aphrodisiac

Magickal Uses

For love: Work with sweet cicely in love sachets and workings. Its sweet myrrh-like scent carries the same loving, devotional energy as true myrrh.

As an aphrodisiac: The essence of sweet cicely is said to be an aphrodisiac.

Notes

Sweet Cicely smells remarkably like the herb myrrh, and can be used as a substitute in workings where myrrh is called for. All parts of the plant — the herb, seeds, and root — are used.

⚠️ Element note: Though masculine/solar in attribution, sweet cicely carries a Water element — a combination that lends it a gentle, receptive quality alongside its stimulating nature.
