Hickory (Carya spp.) yields strong wood and medicine‑bearing bark turned to justice wards and protection.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Powers: Legal Matters, Protection

Magickal Uses

  • Court charm: Burn a small piece of hickory root to ash. Mix with cinquefoil and place in a box. Hang over the door to avoid trouble with the law.
  • General ward: Hickory carries a protective, steadying virtue and may be added to defense sachets.
