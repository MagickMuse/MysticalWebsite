Cloth-of-Gold (Achillea filipendulina) is a tall perennial with flat-topped clusters of golden yellow flowers and feathery foliage.

Magickal Properties

  • Powers: Courage, Love, Divination

Folk Names

Fernleaf Yarrow, Golden Yarrow

Magickal Uses

For courage: Carry cloth-of-gold flowers to instill courage and confidence. The bright golden color is associated with solar power and strength.

For love: Like its cousin common yarrow, cloth-of-gold is used in love divination and spells to attract a partner.

For divination: The flowers and leaves are burned as incense to enhance psychic abilities and used in divination rituals, especially those seeking clarity about the future.
