Ritual Uses

Offered on temple altars and woven into garlands across Polynesian and South/Southeast Asian traditions.

Magickal Uses

  • Love workings: Add fresh or dried flowers to sachets, offerings, or bath rites to draw or sweeten love.
  • Devotion and fidelity: Wear or place blossoms on the altar during vows or reconciliation to renew tenderness and commitment.
  • Spirit offerings: Present fragrant blooms to invite benevolent attention and blessings on partnerships.

Javanese Bridal Preparations

In traditional Javanese wedding rituals, the bride is steeped in warm oils and flowers including frangipani, jasmine, and roses. These combined cleansing treatments, protective formulas, and love baths serve dual purposes: erotic enhancement and protection. Depending on the family's resources, the preparations may continue for several days.

Additional Notes

Known as frangipani; fragrant petals are emblematic of beauty and remembrance. Used in perfumery; burned as a love incense.
