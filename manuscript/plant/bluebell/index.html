Bluebell (Hyacinthoides non-scripta) is a spring flowering bulb with drooping blue flowers.

Magical Properties

  • Planet: Mercury
  • Powers: Luck, Truth

Folk Names

Harebell

Magical Uses

For luck: Wearing a bluebell compels the wearer to speak only truth.

For compelling truth: To compel someone to tell you the truth, wear a bluebell somewhere on your person.

Symbolism & Mythology

One of the old names for the bluebell genus was Endymion, after the beautiful youth of Greek myth. Selene, goddess of the Moon, cast Endymion into an endless sleep so that she could steal down each night to kiss him as he lay upon the hillside. Because his was so deep and dreamless a slumber, bluebells came to be seen as a charm against nightmares.

Meaning When Gifted

When bluebell is gifted it carries the meaning of humility, constancy, and gratitude.
