Magickal Uses

  • Love magick: Carried or worn to attract love and romantic attention.
  • Garden magic: The distinctive three-petaled blue-purple flowers open in the morning and close by afternoon, making spiderwort useful for timing-based spellwork.

Additional Notes

Also known as Dayflower and Widow's Tears. Can cause skin reactions like dermatitis in sensitive individuals—handle with care.
