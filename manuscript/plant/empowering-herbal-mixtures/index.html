Plant Grimoire

Empowering Herbal Mixtures

Plants contain energies useful for specific purposes. Lavender purifies, rosemary draws love, sandalwood heightens spirituality, yarrow increases psychic awareness. But plant power alone is not enough. You must add your own energy to the herbs and mixtures you create. Only by combining plant and human energies will herbal magick reach its full effectiveness.

Many herbs carry multiple traditional uses. Rosemary, for example, serves love, purification, protection, and healing. When you create a healing incense with rosemary as the main ingredient, you must program it specifically for healing. This redirects the herb's varied powers toward your single intention. You accomplish this by sending personal power, infused with your magickal goal, into the mixture.

This process is called empowering, charging, or enchanting.

Why Ritual Helps

No formal ritual is strictly necessary. If you visualize well, simply touch the herbs or hold the bottled mixture and send your energy into it. However, ritual is a remarkably effective tool because it helps you:

  • Focus completely on the magickal operation
  • Raise and direct personal power
  • Mark the transition from mundane preparation to charged material

A Simple Empowerment Method

Light a candle in a color appropriate to your intention: blue or purple for healing, green for prosperity, pink for love. Hold the mixture in your hands. Close your eyes and focus entirely on your goal.

Feel energy building in your body, then flowing down your arms and into your hands. Visualize this energy streaming into the mixture as shimmering strands of light that pour from your palms and merge with the herbs.

If visualization proves difficult, speak your intention aloud. For a healing bath mixture:

I CHARGE YOU BY THE SUN AND MOON
TO CONSUME DISEASE,
TO WASH AWAY ITS CAUSES AND TO HEAL.
SO MOTE IT BE!

For a protective incense:

I CHARGE YOU BY THE SUN AND MOON
TO DRIVE OFF NEGATIVITY AND EVIL
WHEREVER YOU ARE CONSUMED BY FIRE.
SO MOTE IT BE!

For an anointing oil, you might charge it to "destroy disease where you are rubbed" or to "spread peace and calm." Compose words that suit your mixture and need.

When you feel the energy has left your body and entered the mixture, set it down and shake your hands vigorously to cut off the flow. Pinch or snuff out the candle and save it for future empowerment rituals of the same type.

⚠️
Using mixtures that have not been empowered is the mark of a lazy herbalist. After all the work of creating incenses, oils, and brews, why omit the final step that energizes them for ritual use? This same technique can also charge commercially purchased herb products.
