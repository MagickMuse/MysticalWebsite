Joe‑Pye Weed (Eupatorium purpureum; Eutrochium purpureum) is a tall meadow herb that wins hearts and esteem.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Love, Respect

Magickal Uses

  • Love advances: Place a few leaves in the mouth when making love advances so you won’t fail.
  • Earn respect: Carry a few leaves so that you will be looked upon with respect and favor by everyone you meet.

Safety: Not recommended internally if pregnant or nursing; do not use on broken skin.

Gravel Root (Court Case Workings)

In Hoodoo court case workings, gravel root is carried as a charm to soften judicial proceedings and encourage a lenient sentence or favorable treatment. It is often paired with Cascarilla Powder to amplify its effects.

⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: Gravel root's use in court case workings is rooted in Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.
