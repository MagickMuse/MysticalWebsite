Cacao (Theobroma cacao) is the sacred "food of the gods"—the source of chocolate. The genus name Theobroma literally translates to "food of the gods" in Greek, reflecting its divine status in Mesoamerican cultures.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mars

Element: Fire

Powers: Love, Lust, Prosperity, Fertility, Offerings to the Gods

Herbal Lore

Cacao was sacred to the Aztecs and Maya, used as currency, medicine, and ritual offering. The goddess Chantico's sacred plant is cacao, and it remains a traditional offering to her. Cacao beans and Mexican-style hot chocolate are appropriate offerings for workings involving wealth, abundance, and protection of valuable items.

The bitter raw cacao carries transformative energy—it opens the heart and stimulates the senses. In modern magick, chocolate is associated with love, sensuality, and indulgence.

Magickal Uses

Love and Lust: Chocolate has long been considered an aphrodisiac. Add cacao to love spells, or share chocolate with a romantic partner to deepen connection.

Prosperity: Cacao beans were once used as currency throughout Mesoamerica. Incorporate into money workings or prosperity altars.

Offerings: An excellent offering to deities, particularly those of love, fertility, and abundance. Hot chocolate made from scratch carries particular potency.

Aphrodisiac Properties

Chocolate melts at blood temperature, making it uniquely sensual on the tongue. It also contains phenylethylamine, the same chemical the brain produces when falling in love. The Spanish conquistador Hernan Cortes was so taken with the aphrodisiac reputation of cacao that he planted two thousand trees in the hope of cultivating not gold but desire.

Transformation: The process of turning bitter cacao into sweet chocolate mirrors alchemical transformation. Use in workings involving personal change and metamorphosis.
