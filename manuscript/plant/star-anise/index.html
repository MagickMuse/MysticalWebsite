Star Anise is a powerful spice used to increase psychic abilities, attract luck, and enhance magickal power. The distinctive eight-pointed star-shaped fruits are burned as incense or worn as beads to develop psychic powers and attract good fortune. This aromatic spice from China has been prized in both culinary and magickal applications for centuries.

Botanical Description

Star anise grows as an evergreen tree reaching as tall as twelve metres, with a slender white trunk. It bears fruit made up of five to thirteen seed-bearing follicles attached to a central axis, arranged in the unmistakable shape of a star. Native to southeast China, the plant is also found in Vietnam, India, and Japan, with most commercial production rooted in China.

Related Species

Several related species share the genus, but only true star anise is used safely in cooking and herbalism. Japanese star anise, in particular, is highly poisonous and must never be substituted for the Chinese variety.

Folk Tradition

Chinese medicine has employed star anise for more than thirteen hundred years for its stimulating effect on the digestive system and for respiratory complaints such as bronchitis and unproductive coughs. Across the East it has long served as a remedy for colic and rheumatism, and the seeds are often chewed after meals to sweeten the breath and ease digestion. It remains a staple oriental domestic spice.

Magickal Uses

Seeds burned as incense increase psychic powers and enhance spiritual awareness, and they are also worn as beads for the same purpose. Star anise is sometimes placed on the altar to give it power, with one fruit set in each of the four directions to enhance magickal workings. Carried as a general luck-bringer, it attracts good fortune and positive opportunities. The seeds make excellent pendulums for divination work due to their perfect star shape and balanced energy. The eight-pointed star shape makes star anise particularly useful in spells involving cosmic alignment and celestial magick.

Aphrodisiac Properties

The star shape is sacred to the Goddess, making star anise a potent fertility symbol with aphrodisiac associations.

Star Anise and Anise

Star anise and true Anise have little in common botanically, yet magickally both induce and enhance psychic power. Ground together and burned, they make a double dose of psychic power (see Double Anise Psychic Incense).
