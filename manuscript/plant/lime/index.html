Lime (Citrus aurantiifolia; C. limetta) cuts crossings, heals, guards, and uplifts the spirits.

Botanical Information

Citrus aurantiifolia (Key Lime) and C. limetta (Sweet Lime) of the Rutaceae family. Fruit, juice, peel, zest, and essential oil are all used. The scent is bright, sharp, and citrusy.

Herbal Lore

Key limes originated in Southeast Asia and traveled to Southern Florida via the spice trade, hence "Key" for the Florida Keys. Key limes are smaller and more acidic than standard limes. Their seeds can be replanted, making them a gift that keeps on giving.

The lime tree is one of the citrus hybrids, descending from the mandarin orange and pomelo, alongside lemon, grapefruit, and other common citrus fruits.

Magickal Uses

Lime's stimulating properties make it an excellent choice for combating depression and low mood; adding lime juice to water or a lime-aid beverage when spirits need lifting lets the bright, sharp scent cut through mental fog and invigorate the senses. Lime carries strong energies of fidelity and commitment, and lime essential oil added to a carrier oil makes a fidelity oil for anointing candles carved with a partner's name. Lime attracts and strengthens friendships, and a pink candle dressed with lime oil burned on a Wednesday supports workings for new or stronger connections.

For luck, combine lime zest with sugar to draw out the aromatic oils and place the oleosaccharum in a jar with a written petition for good fortune. Lime's sharp, acidic nature makes it excellent for cutting through curses and hexes; piercing a fresh lime with old iron nails, spikes, pins, and needles, then throwing it into a deep hole, rids one of ills and hexes while the iron grounds and neutralizes the negativity. Lime enhances concentration and mental sharpness, and a focus pouch with dried lime peel and lime essential oil carried during study or demanding intellectual work is a traditional aid. For loyalty workings, carve the target's name into a yellow candle, anoint with lime oil, and burn on a Sunday.

Lime's solar energy helps heal from powerlessness and trauma; a poppet stuffed with dried lime peel, wrapped in black fabric, and anointed with lime oil while speaking healing affirmations supports this work. Twigs of the lime tree carried on the person protect against the evil eye and malicious gazes. A traditional remedy for sore throat is to wear a necklace of limes. Lime peel is used in love mixtures and incenses, and for toothache the old practice is to drive a nail into a lime tree, thanking the tree first for its sacrifice.

Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: Healing, love, hex-breaking, protection.

When lime is gifted it carries the meaning of conjugal love.
