Cayenne Pepper (Capsicum annuum) is a hot chili pepper with thin, pointed red pods.

History

Cultivated across many cultures for centuries, capsicum has served both as a culinary spice and as a vermifuge -- a preparation used to expel intestinal parasites.

Characteristics

Centuries of widespread cultivation have produced a remarkable range of varieties, each with its own potency, shape, and size. Colors span the spectrum from green to yellow, orange, and red, and fruits range from one to six inches in length. Pungency varies significantly depending on the specific variety.

  • Powers: Fidelity, Hex Breaking, Love

Folk Names

African Pepper, Bird Pepper, Red Pepper

Magickal Uses

For cleansing and purification: Cayenne is used in cleansing and purification workings to burn away negativity and stagnation.

For wound care: Cayenne can be used in a very light coating into wounds and cuts to help stop bleeding. It will sting only in areas of many exposed nerve endings, not in the average cut or gash.

Blood-building tonic: For the iron-poor: ¼ teaspoon of cayenne in ½ cup grape juice — you can build up to one teaspoon cayenne. Drink one to three times a day.

For sinus relief: A few grains of cayenne along with sea salt can be used in a neti pot for sinus relief.

For fidelity: Sprinkle around your home to break hexes and curses directed against you.

For love: Add to love mixtures to "spice them up". Scatter around the home to drive off evil.
