Magickal Properties

Gender: Masculine

Planet: Sun

Element: Fire

Powers: Health, Purification, Power, Luck

Magickal Uses

For power and purification: The bark is used in incenses of power and purification. Its solar energy lends strength and vitality to any working.

For health: Work with cascara sagrada in health and healing spells to restore vitality and ward off illness.

Harvesting Notes

The bark is collected in spring and early summer and must be stored for at least one year before use — fresh bark is a strong purgative and can cause griping. Properly aged bark is far milder.

Amulet Uses

Buckthorn carried as an amulet makes wishes come true.
