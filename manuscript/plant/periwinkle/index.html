Magickal Properties

Powers: Love, Lust, Mental Powers, Money, Protection

Ritual Uses

Traditionally gathered under strict lunar timings with spoken charm (per Pseudo‑Apuleius) for efficacy.

Magickal Uses

  • Broad protection: Carry for grace and prosperity; protect against snakes, poison, wild beasts, terror, evil eye, spirits; hang over the door to guard the home.
  • Love and lust: Utilized in love spells; carried or sprinkled under the bed to increase passions.
  • Money and memory: Carried to attract money; gazed upon to restore lost memories.

Additional Notes

Known as Lesser Periwinkle and Sorcerer’s Violet; observe traditional clean state and moon nights for harvesting if following classical rites.

From the source book (Greater Periwinkle, Vinca major): Gender: Cold | Planet: Venus | Element: Water | Deities: Aphrodite, Isis, Adonis, Luna, Astarte | Powers: Protection, Love. A favorite flower of the "wise folk" for making charms and love philters. Believed to protect from evil spirits, snakes, wild beasts, poison, possession, evil magick, hatred, and terror when hung in the doorway. Also brings prosperity. Must be picked when the Moon is nine, eleven, thirteen, thirty, or one night old. The Germans decorated tombs with it for immortality.

⚠️ Note: the source entry is for Vinca major (Greater Periwinkle); this entry was originally for Vinca minor (Lesser Periwinkle). Both identities are noted here.

Flower of Death

For all that it looks pretty, blue, and harmless, the periwinkle has long been entwined with death and witchcraft. It was called the flower of death, the fiore di morte, for it was woven into wreaths for dead infants and worn as a garland by the condemned on their way to execution. Yet in some places it stands instead for eternal life, so the same flower may have been laid upon the dying as a quiet sign of hope. It is held to be grave bad luck to pluck a periwinkle growing on a grave, an act said to call down haunting by the soul of the one buried there.

The plant's old name of sorcerer's violet hints at its other use. Its leaves carry a faint hallucinogenic quality and were likely turned to in shamanic practice.

Meaning When Gifted

When periwinkle is gifted it carries the meaning of early recollections, the pleasures of memory, and sweet memories.
