Source — Specific Uses

Has an invigorating scent that overrides cooking and tobacco odors. The finely divided leaves with a lemon smell are considered a sexual stimulant.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Mercury

Element: Air

Powers: Love, Lust, Protection

Ritual Uses

Burned as incense in fumigations; traditionally hung in wardrobes to repel moths.

Magickal Uses

  • Love and lust: Used in love spells and sachets to attract romantic partners and kindle desire.
  • Protection: Burned to ward off harmful influences; placed under the bed to arouse lust in those who sleep there.
  • Moth repellent: Hung in closets and wardrobes to protect clothing from moth damage—a practical magical use.

Additional Notes

Also known as Lad's Love and Old Man; a member of the Artemisia family alongside mugwort and wormwood. Not recommended for internal use if pregnant.

Antidote to Malicious Magick

In medieval Europe, southernwood ranked among the most potent spiritual antidotes to malicious magickal workings. As a member of the Artemisia family, it belongs to the plants sacred to Artemis. It is a close cousin of the more notorious Wormwood, and its name is a worn-down form of "southern wormwood."
