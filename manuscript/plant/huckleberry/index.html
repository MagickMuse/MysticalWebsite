Huckleberry (Vaccinium spp.; Gaylussacia spp.) carries luck, guards the traveler, and breaks crossing‑work.

Magickal Properties

Planet: Venus

Powers: Luck, Protection, Dream Magick, Hex‑Breaking

Magickal Uses

  • Luck and protection: Leaves placed in sachets and carried bring good luck and keep away evil; used to break hexes and curses.
  • Dream working: Burn the leaves in the bedroom directly before sleep so that your dreams come true within seven days.

Meaning When Gifted

When huckleberry is gifted it carries the meaning of faith and simple pleasures.
