Coconut (Cocos nucifera) is a tropical palm tree producing versatile fruit used in many cultures.

Magickal Properties

  • Gender: Feminine
  • Planet: Moon
  • Element: Water
  • Powers: Purification, Protection, Chastity

Folk Names

Coco, Niu, Tree of Life

Magickal Uses

For purification: Coconut is used in purification rituals. The water inside a fresh coconut is particularly cleansing.

For protection: Coconut shells are hung in the home for protection. The whole coconut is also used as a protective charm.

For chastity: In some traditions, coconut is used in spells to promote chastity and purity.
