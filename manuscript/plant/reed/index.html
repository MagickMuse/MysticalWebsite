Source — Specific Uses

Irish poets reckoned the reed a tree. Reeds were used for arrow shafts and royal scepters, including those of Egyptian pharaohs.

Magickal Uses

  • Use in workings for eloquence, sacred speech, and knowledge.
  • Associated with divine scribes and poets across multiple traditions (Thoth, Hermes, Ogma).
  • Irish Celtic tradition counted the reed among the sacred trees of the ogham alphabet.

Meaning When Gifted

When reed is gifted it carries the meaning of music.
