Mabon

Maple (Acer spp.) sweetens love, extends life, and draws money.

Magickal Properties

Powers: Love, Prosperity, Life, Health, Abundance

Magickal Uses

  • Love and money: Leaves used in love spells and money rituals; branches make excellent wands.
  • Long life: Passing a child through maple branches ensures a long life.

Magickal Lore

Maple is the source of sweet maple sugar and syrup, connecting it to sweetness in relationships and the sweetness of life itself.

Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: Love, grounding, longevity, money.
