Dulse (Palmaria palmata) is a red edible seaweed that grows on the northern coasts of the Atlantic and Pacific oceans.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine

Planet: Moon

Element: Water

Powers: Lust, Harmony

Magickal Uses

For lust: Add dulse to lust incenses and sachets. The sea's power is used to stir up passion and desire.

For harmony: Being from the sea, dulse is used in spells to calm turbulent emotions and bring harmony to relationships.

For sea magic: As with all seaweeds, dulse is used in sea spells and to gain protection while traveling over water.

For home sweetening: Sprinkle powdered dulse around the home to sweeten the atmosphere and encourage happiness.

⚠️ Safety Warning: Not recommended for internal use if you have hyperthyroidism. Do not use long term due to high iodine content.
