Life‑Everlasting (Anaphalis spp.; Gnaphalium uliginosum; Antennaria dioica) sustains life, health, and youthful vigor.

Magickal Properties

Gender: Feminine (source: Cold)

Element: Earth/Air

Deities: Kuan Yin, Venus/Aphrodite, Hathor, Freyja, Brigid

Powers: Love, Beauty, Creativity, Longevity, Health, Healing

Magickal Uses

  • Longevity and youth: Use in spells for long life and restoring youth; keep in the home or carry to prevent sickness.
  • Poultice: Use in a poultice for sprains and bruises.
  • Daily charm: Each morning before eating or drinking, drink an infusion and say:

“Chills and ills, pains and banes,

Do your fasting with life everlasting.”

Related Species

Distinct from Helichrysum (Helichrysum angustifolium / H. italicum), a Mediterranean herb of a different genus that shares the common name everlasting and is the source of the immortelle essential oil used in aromatherapy and perfumery.
