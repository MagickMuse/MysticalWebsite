River Otter: Sliding into Joy

Lontra canadensis

This fashionista of the waterways stays warm in her plush fur coat and by wriggling and squiggling, generating heat with her ridiculously high metabolism. River Otter's inner fire is fueled by constant snacking on crayfish and crabs. The cynical might say she's joyful because she gets to eat all day without gaining an ounce.

But River Otter knows that when you're lit from within, life provides endless amusement. Take that muddy riverbank for instance. I see a waterslide. Do you see it? River Otter reminds you to actively look for joy. She knows it's there, waiting to be found.

Energy Spectrum: River and Sea

Sea Otter is cousin to River Otter, but her energy differs. While River Otter stays in constant motion, Sea Otter's movements are more leisurely. She spends time floating on her back, grooming her pup in this position, cracking open mussels with a rock she keeps in a pouch under her arm, soaking up sunshine, even sleeping while holding paws with other Sea Otters so they don't get separated.

River Otter and Sea Otter are joyful critters. Their differences teach us that any given energy has a full spectrum of expression. Sea Otter expresses contented joy while River Otter has a more electric way of being. Sea Otter tends to conserve energy while River Otter spends it with abandon.

All emotions and actions exist on a spectrum. What spectrums are you working with in your life? Are you cruising from anger to elation? Are you dealing in shades of contentment or discontent? Do you swing from jealousy to generosity? Understanding the spectrum helps you see your daily actions and emotions in the larger context of your life and helps you gain perspective to resolve them.

Creature of the Moon

Like other animals that move through water and can vanish below the surface of the rivers and streams they inhabit, otters are symbols of the Moon and so of feminine magical powers. Because lunar creatures are held to have access to hidden knowledge and secret wisdom, the otter's skin is believed to carry a powerful magical symbolism.

Sacred Skin and Initiation

⚠️
This describes closed Indigenous practices and is offered as a respectful overview, not a practice to adopt.

Among some Native American peoples the otter's skin is used in rites of initiation and to make medicine bags, in which the most sacred stones and shells are kept. Because the otter is believed to possess a god-given immortality, its skin features in the ritual enactment of death and resurrection.

Psychopomp of the Waterways

For many peoples, including the Celts, the otter's watery home marks the place where the boundary between the worlds grows thin. The otter therefore acts as a psychopomp, guiding souls to the Afterlife, and its intimate knowledge of waterways and underground tunnels allows it to find the winding route to the next world.
