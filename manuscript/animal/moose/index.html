Sacred to Native Americans

⚠️
This reflects specific Indigenous traditions and is offered as a respectful overview, not a practice to adopt.

The moose is a sacred animal among many Native American peoples, closely linked with the raven and regarded as a gift from that god-like bird.

Male and Female Energy

The moose carries male energy through the qualities of a good and persistent hunter, yet it is also a female symbol, owing in part to its unpredictable nature. It is often content standing neck-high in water, grazing on aquatic plants such as water lilies, and this bond with water and earth deepens its feminine associations.

Detachment and Independence

The moose is a solitary creature that does not travel in herds but prefers to live alone. Its calf is weaned at six months, and the mother drives it away before the birth of the next, which makes the moose a symbol of detachment and independence.
