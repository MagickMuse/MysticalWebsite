Lunar Emblem and Rainbringer

As the snail withdraws into its shell and emerges again, it became a lunar emblem and a sign of seasonal renewal. Because snails appear when the ground turns damp, people reasoned that the creatures called forth the rain and counted them among its bringers.

The Self-Sufficient Spiral

Carrying its home wherever it goes, the snail stands for self-sufficiency. The spiral of its shell holds a deeper meaning still, for the spiral is itself a sacred form, the Golden Mean. The Maya looked to the coil of a snail shell when they devised a glyph for the concept of zero, so that a humble creature came to be bound up with a discovery that reshaped the world.

Fertility

The glistening trail a snail leaves behind was likened to the body's reproductive secretions, both female and male, and some African traditions held that the creature carried a store of seed within it. For these reasons the snail also became a symbol of fertility.

Christian Iconography

In Christian imagery the snail served as an emblem of the wicked and the slothful, and its slime was read as a mark of lewdness.
