Overview

Many people see the wasp as a picture of bad-tempered irritability, since it will sting without provocation. Unlike the bee, which is held in great affection and dies in the very act of stinging, the wasp can sting again and again as often as it pleases.

A Sacred Transmuter

In Africa, by contrast, the wasp is regarded as a sacred creature with the power to turn the profane into something holy. It is also counted among the beings said to have brought fire to humankind, and so is honored as a helpful and beneficial creature.
