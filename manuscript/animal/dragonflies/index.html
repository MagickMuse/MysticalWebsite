Blue Damselfly: Gateway to Small Magics

Enallagma cyathigerum

Damselfly is a creature of water and sunlight, her iridescent wings moving faster than the human eye can easily follow. She inhabits the interstitial spaces where earth elementals are said to dance, and draws attention to the rich complexity present in even the smallest patch of natural world.

Magickal experience is not limited to dramatic or transformative events. Life contains frequent serendipitous moments and small synchronicities that fade into background unless actively acknowledged. The tendency to seek large, lightning-bolt manifestations of magick may cause practitioners to overlook the quieter enchantments woven through ordinary daily existence. Damselfly's medicine is the cultivation of attention to these small magicks.

Dragonfly may receive more attention, but when it comes to iridescence it is cousin Damselfly who most fully embodies this quality. These two creatures are quite similar, differing primarily in the set of their wings, and are frequently thought of interchangeably.
