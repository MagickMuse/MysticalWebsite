Inventor of Fire

In many places the hedgehog, like the porcupine, was held to be the inventor of fire and so came to be associated with the Sun, the shape of its spines suggesting the sun's rays.

Wrath

Those same prickly spines, the creature's most distinctive feature, also make the hedgehog a symbol of bad-tempered wrath, since they are raised at once whenever the animal feels threatened.

Greed and Gluttony

In myth the hedgehog rolled about among lush fruits such as figs and plums, spearing them on its spines to carry home and eat in private. For this reason it became a symbol of greed and gluttony.
