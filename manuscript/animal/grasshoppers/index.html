Amulet Uses

A grasshopper carried as an amulet brings cheer, wealth, and plenty.

Symbol of Good Fortune

In China and across the Mediterranean the grasshopper, like the cricket, is counted a bringer of good luck, most of all when one appears inside the house. The Greeks held it sacred to Apollo.

Death and Resurrection

Because the grasshopper lays its eggs in the earth and the newly hatched young rise up out of the soil, it also reads as a symbol of death and resurrection.
