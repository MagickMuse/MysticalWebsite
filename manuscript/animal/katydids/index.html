Symbolism & Medicine

Microcentrum rhombifolium - Broad-winged Katydid

Katydid possesses five eyes like his Cricket cousin, two compound and three light-sensitive ocelli. This multi-lens vision captures life's intricate details that dual-eyed creatures overlook. Perched high in oak branches, he observes moonlight, shadows, starshine, synthesizing multiple images into comprehensive understanding of the world's slow evolution.

His medicine teaches pattern recognition: details accumulate into patterns, patterns reveal rhythm. Katydid predicts what's coming by deeply comprehending what has been. When he appears, attend to details, they'll show you the underlying rhythm and help you forecast the future through present-moment patterns.

Compound Vision

Imagine sitting before multiple video screens, each showing the same scene from different angles, that's how Katydid's compound eyes function. Each tiny lens captures one image; his brain weaves them into coherent narrative. The three ocelli add light-and-dark perception, enriching the total picture.

We miss much with our single perspective, both literally and metaphorically. We trust our eyes despite knowing they show incomplete views. When have your eyes failed to reveal a situation's entirety? When did you overlook crucial details?

What shifts if you change how you observe, seeking long-range patterns instead of isolated moments? Can you release personal beliefs and biases to genuinely see through another's eyes?
