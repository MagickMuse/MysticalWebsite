Symbol of Good Fortune

In China and around the Mediterranean the cricket, like the grasshopper, is regarded as a bringer of good luck, especially when one is seen or heard inside the house. The Greeks held the creature sacred to Apollo.

Death and Resurrection

Because it lays its eggs in the earth and the newly hatched young emerge from the soil, the cricket also carries the symbolism of death and resurrection.
