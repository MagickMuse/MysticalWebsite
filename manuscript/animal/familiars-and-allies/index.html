Animal Grimoire

Familiars and Allies

Animal Spells

Spellwork involving animals falls broadly into two purposes: protecting animals from physical and spiritual harm, and channeling animal power to benefit humans. The animals who actively participate in magickal workings, lending their own energy and gifts, generally belong to one of two categories: familiars and allies.

Familiars

Keeping animals as companions purely for affection is a relatively recent idea. In earlier centuries, those who formed unusually close bonds with animals often attracted suspicion and accusations of witchcraft, particularly if the creatures in question were cats, birds, rabbits, black dogs, reptiles, or amphibians.

The word "familiar" captures the essence of the relationship. A familiar is a real, living animal with whom a person shares a deep psychic and emotional connection. The dog that insists on sleeping beneath your bed, the cat that shadows you through every room, the bird content to ride your shoulder all day: each of these is a potential familiar.

Sometimes the familiar's mere presence is enough to kindle and amplify magickal work, even without deliberate participation. For other practitioners, consciously cultivating the psychic bond between human and animal unlocks a source of profound power and fulfillment.

Typical Familiars

Most familiars are animals capable of sharing a household: cats, dogs, rabbits, birds, toads, and snakes top the list. But anyone who lives or works in close quarters with other species may discover unexpected magickal connections as well.

Wild Familiars

A wild animal that stays wild can still become a familiar, though in these cases the animal invariably initiates the bond. A bird or a colony of bees might visit the same person daily. Scandinavian witches were traditionally known to favor flies. Wild dolphins have been observed singling out a particular human for repeated interaction. People living near forests or wilderness areas sometimes develop a sustained, personal relationship with an individual creature over time.

💛
A familiar cannot be forced. The bond is rooted in love, respect, and genuine chemistry, and it must form on its own terms.

Allies

Because a familiar must be a specific creature with whom you share a personal bond, the range of animals available is naturally limited. But what happens when a working calls for a rhinoceros, a crocodile, a dragon, or a unicorn?

All familiars are animal allies, but the category of ally reaches far beyond what a familiar can offer. An animal ally is fundamentally a spiritual relationship, and because it can unfold entirely in the spirit realm, there is no limit on which creatures may be approached. One may also petition the presiding spirit of an entire species rather than any single animal.

There is no cap on how many familiars or allies a person may have.

Discovering Allies

Alliances with animals cannot be forced, but they can be invited. A common belief holds that every person is born with the alliances they need, whether animal, botanical, or spiritual. The work lies in recognizing those connections and learning to draw on them effectively.

Methods for uncovering allies include card-based divination systems and dreamwork. Any animal or species that keeps appearing in your life, whether in dreams or waking encounters, may already be an ally or may be signaling its willingness to become one.

The Nagual

Across Mexico and Central America, certain practitioners are believed capable of shapeshifting into animal form. Such a person is called a nagual, and a coyote trotting down the road might be an ordinary animal or a transformed practitioner. In the state of Oaxaca, however, the concept takes a different shape. The nahual (the Oaxacan spelling) is understood as a guardian spirit received at birth, a sacred gift bestowed by a child's parents.

Animal Images

Prehistoric cave paintings rank among the oldest surviving human artifacts, testifying to a deep magickal connection between people and animals stretching back millennia. Portable figurines of specific animals, typically shaped from clay or stone, have been used since antiquity for healing, hunting, protection, and the development of psychic power.

Zuni Stone Fetishes

The Zuni people of New Mexico elevated this practice into a refined art. Their stone fetishes are small carvings of individual animals, originally made for ceremony and the hunt. Traditionally, carvers sought out stones that already suggested the form of a creature, requiring only minimal shaping to bring the animal into view. Because the charm is found rather than manufactured, the animal spirit has the chance to choose the person instead of the other way around.

Working with a Stone Fetish:

  1. Look for stones whose natural shape already hints at a particular creature. When you encounter the right one, you will know it. Like holed-stones, these are gifts from the Earth.
  2. Charge and consecrate the fetish by placing it inside a magick box alongside images, food, and botanical activators. Corn pollen was the traditional offering used to awaken the fetish; substitute whatever feels sacred and appropriate to you.
  3. Store the fetish in its box or container when it is not in active use. Feed it on a regular schedule to maintain its power and keep it activated.

Living Animal Allies

The Leni-Lenape Tradition

Some traditions favor a tangible companion over an abstract guardian. In Leni-Lenape practice, a newborn is given an actual animal, most often a puppy or kitten, to serve as a personal protector and lifelong partner. The creature is raised alongside the child, and no two children share the same animal. This is a bond meant to last a lifetime.

If the animal dies, it is believed to have shielded the child by absorbing some threat of harm, particularly illness or accident. A ceremonial burial closes the bond, and a new companion is given to the child without delay. If the child passes first, funeral rites release the animal from its obligation.

Dedicating Animals to Spirits

Living creatures can also be consecrated to a specific spirit rather than bonded to a human. This creates a three-way connection linking human, spirit, and animal. Spells for animal healing, protection, or recovery of lost animals often recommend dedicating the animal to a spirit as part of the working.

Spirit Protectors

The following spirits have earned a reputation as renowned protectors of specific animals. Incorporate them into spells for healing, protection, or locating lost animals as needed.

Cats: Artemis, Bastet, Freya, Hekate, Lilith

Big Cats (tigers, lions, leopards): Dionysus, Durga, Hathor, Kybele, Sekhmet

Dogs: Artemis, Hekate, Ogun, Saint Roch

Horses: Anat, Demeter, Epona, Poseidon, Rhiannon, Hayagriva

Toads: Agwe, Heket

Snakes: Athena, Ezili Freda Dahomey, Lilith, Mami Waters, Simbi, Asherah

Cows: Brigid, Hathor, Hermes, Isis, Lakshmi, Maeve, Shiva

Fish: Atargatis, La Baleine, La Sirene, Yemaya

Pigs: Demeter, Seth

Animals in general: Aphrodite, Artemis, Baba Yaga, Faunus, Hathor, Lilith, Saint Anthony (the spiritual detective: petition for assistance when anything or anyone is missing)
