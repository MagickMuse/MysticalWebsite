Amulet Uses

A crab claw carried as an amulet attracts love and promotes fertility.

Symbolism

When Isis gathered the scattered body of her murdered husband Osiris, one part was missing. The Nile crab known as the Oxyrhynchid had devoured his penis, and so in Egypt the crab became a cursed creature. Elsewhere the crab is closely tied to the Moon, since its growth is affected by the lunar cycles, and its appearance at the edge of the sea as the tides turned likely strengthened the link.

The crab appears as the zodiacal sign of Cancer, rising over the horizon at the summer solstice, another reminder of the turning seasons as the Sun begins its descending path. The crab also features in the Tarot on the eighteenth card, the Moon. The lobster appears on this card as well, for like the Moon both creatures sometimes move forwards and sometimes backwards.
