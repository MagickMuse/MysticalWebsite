Butterfly of the Night

A nocturnal creature, the moth is thought of as the butterfly of the night, though it carries a far more sinister symbolism than its frivolous sister.

The Flame

One of the moth's archetypal images is of it dancing around a flame. So greatly does this creature crave light that it will immolate itself in pursuit of it. The image holds a dichotomy: on one hand it speaks of the sublimation of the ego, as death dissolves the soul into the collective unconscious; on the other it is an emblem of stupidity, vanity, and hubris.

Markings and Omens

The markings on certain moths carry a symbolism of their own. The skull borne upon the back of the death's-head moth speaks for itself, and to find one is held to presage bad luck.

The Disembodied Soul and the Destroyer

Like the butterfly, the moth symbolizes the disembodied soul. Yet the moth is also seen as a destructive force, for unlike the butterfly it can do damage and is sometimes regarded as a pest. Though the clothes-eating moth is something of a rarity, moths in general have suffered a poor reputation, and old proverbs liken the way a person wastes away to a garment eaten by moths.
