Earthworm's Purposeful Work

Lumbricus terrestris

Earthworm's ecological function is the creation and maintenance of life-sustaining conditions in soil. She wriggles through earth creating channels for air and water, and minerals pass through her digestive system becoming more accessible to plant roots. She regenerates severed sections, making her biological purpose and her daily function identical. This alignment of daily activity with a larger regenerative purpose constitutes Earthworm's particular teaching.

Elemental Balance

Earthworm's presence signals healthy soil because she balances the classical four elements. By burrowing, she creates tubes allowing air and water entry, preventing compaction. By digesting large minerals, she facilitates composting and assimilation, processes associated with fire's transformative function. Earthworm's role is to supply whatever elements the soil lacks, making her an embodiment of elemental balance and completeness.

A Word for Many Creatures

The word worm has not always meant the common garden creature. It was once used as well for dragons, for legged lizards, and for snakes. It is the humble earthworm, however, that concerns this entry.

Life Out of Death

To call a person a worm is to name them weak, indecisive, and ineffectual. The worm itself, though, carries powerful associations, bound up with both life and death, and above all with new life arising from the corruption of death. A Chinese legend tells that humankind was made from the worms that fed upon the corpse of the first primordial being. However distasteful this may seem, the worm's labor in breaking down dead matter is essential work, and it seems unjust to scorn a creature for so useful a contribution.

The Lowly and the Illustrious

The worm is a humble creature, lowly in both the literal and the figurative sense. For this reason the worm motif has served in myth to mark the humble origins of illustrious figures. The great Celtic hero Cuchulain, for one, was said to have been born from a worm that his mother swallowed unawares.
