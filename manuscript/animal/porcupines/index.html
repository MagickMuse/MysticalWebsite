Emblem of Fighting Power

The fearsome, spiky appearance of the porcupine makes it a perfect symbol of fighting power, able to attack from both near and far, though it cannot, as people once believed, actually shoot its barbs. For this reason it was adopted as a device in heraldry, used most notably by the Duke of Orleans, who founded the Order of the Porcupine in 1397. The Knights of this Order recognized one another by the porcupine emblem engraved on their rings and pendants.

Bringer of Fire

For some Native American peoples the porcupine was a sacred creature, said to have brought fire to humankind and associated with solar power. This was likely owed to the spiky, flame-like appearance of its quills, which were treated as sacred items in their own right and often used to decorate clothing and headgear.

Messenger Between Worlds

In Nigeria the porcupine is believed to carry messages between humankind and God, and so it is called upon by shamans in their divinatory practices.
