Symbolism

To say that someone has chameleon-like tendencies means they change to suit their surroundings, hiding their true self and remaining mysterious and unknowable. Yet this very changeability is what defines the chameleon and makes it unique. For early Christians its power to change color linked the chameleon with the Devil, who likewise changes himself to deceive people and escape detection.

The chameleon carries more than one meaning, though.

⚠️ The following belief is sacred to the pygmy peoples of the Congo and is a closed practice meant only for those within that culture.

To the pygmy peoples of the Congo, the chameleon is the Creator of Mankind. Because it can climb to the very top of the tallest trees, it is able to speak directly with the Sun God, and in legend it was responsible for all the animals that fill the planet.

Because of its climbing habit the chameleon is also tied to the Sky Gods and the element of air. It is believed to draw thunder and rain to itself through its nearness to the sky. A spell to give a magician power over the weather called for burning a chameleon as a sacrifice on a fire of oak wood, a timber that itself attracted the wrath of the storm gods.
