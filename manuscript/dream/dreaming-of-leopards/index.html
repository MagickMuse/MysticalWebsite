Dream Grimoire

🐆Dreaming of Leopards

Animal Source1
Leopards
Correspondences7
💪Assertiveness🦁Courage💪Determination🕊️Freedom🏝️Isolation👑Power💪Strength
Interpretations
If you dream of seeing a leopard on city streets, the fierceness and the predatory nature of the leopard may be telling you to be more assertive. If you dream you are attacked by a leopard, you may be afraid of expressing strong views and challenging the status quo. If you are running with a leopard, you need freedom from someone who is trying to hold you back. If you see a solitary leopard climbing a tree, you should stand alone and express your unique gifts. Since leopards are associated with African storm deities and Fujin, the Shinto Japanese god of the wind, who carries the winds in a leopard-skin bag, a leaping leopard indicates the need for swift action. Staring down a leopard that backs away says that if you show courage and determination, you can overcome any obstacles. A leopard attacking you suggests that while the future looks good, many difficulties loom through misplaced confidence. Killing a leopard signals victory in your affairs. Seeing one caged implies that enemies will surround you but will fail to injure you. Seeing leopards in their native habitat trying to escape from you means you will be embarrassed in business or love, but by persistent efforts you can overcome the difficulties. Dreaming of leopard skin signals that your interests will be endangered by a dishonest person who will win your esteem. A leopard launching an attack on you suggests that although the future appears bright, many obstacles loom because of misplaced confidence. Killing a leopard points to victory in your affairs, while seeing one caged implies that enemies will surround you but fail to harm you. Watching leopards in their native habitat try to escape from you means you will be embarrassed in business or love, but persistent effort will overcome the difficulties. Dreaming of leopard skin signals that your interests will be endangered by a dishonest person who has won your esteem.
