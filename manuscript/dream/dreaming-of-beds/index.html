🛏️Dreaming of Beds

Correspondences13
🌱Beginnings⚠️Caution🔄Change🛋️Comfort💀Death💵Fortune👫Friendship🩺Health❤️‍🔥Passion☮️Peace👫Relationships😌Relaxation😴Rest
Interpretations
A shared bed symbolizes a comfortable relationship, passion, or dissent and toxicity, depending on the nature of the dream. An uncomfortable or broken bed signals natural peace, especially in a relationship, may have been shattered and needs fixing. An infested bed indicates toxicity has entered a relationship or lifestyle that may need starting over. Waking disoriented in an unfamiliar bed or bedroom suggests uncertainty about where you belong and want to be. Buying a new bed signals entering a new phase in life or relationship where relaxation, rest, or passion has lost its place. A clean, white bed is a sign of inner peace, and making a bed foretells a new friendship or professional connection. Being in bed in an unfamiliar room suggests unexpected visitors are on the way. A sick person dreaming of a bed may face further complications, and in some cases death. Sleeping in a bed outdoors promises wonderful experiences and chances to improve your fortune. A child wetting a bed brings unusual worry, and the ill may recover more slowly than expected; if you are the one wetting the bed, illness or misfortune may disrupt your daily life.
