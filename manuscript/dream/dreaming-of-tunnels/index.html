🚇Dreaming of Tunnels

◇Interpretations
Tunnels beneath buildings, which exist in many old cities, represent in a dream memories or fears subconsciously buried. Spirits lurking in a tunnel in a dream may link the dreamer to a haunted site. Dreaming of subway, rail, or road tunnels speaks of transitions and the need to face uncertainty in order to reach a desired objective. Stairs leading down to the tunnel signify the need to tackle hidden feelings head-on. A blocked-off tunnel says past relationships or opportunities should be released. If you dream you are crawling through a narrow tunnel, you are right now restricted but must continue to edge forward. Going through a tunnel in a dream is bad for those in business and in love. Seeing a train coming toward you while you're in a tunnel foretells ill health and change in occupation. Passing through a tunnel in a car denotes unsatisfactory business and much unpleasant and expensive travel. Seeing a tunnel caving in portends failure and enemies. Looking into a tunnel indicates that you will soon be compelled to face a desperate issue.
