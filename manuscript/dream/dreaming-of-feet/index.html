👣Dreaming of Feet

Correspondences8
💼Business🥊Conflict😰Despair🧭Direction👨‍👨‍👧‍👧Family🕊️Freedom🧍🏻Independence🚪Separation
Interpretations
Stamping your feet in a dream suggests you feel strongly about an issue and will not be deterred. If you dream of marching feet, especially if you are one of the marchers, it signifies moving in a direction shared by others, where you seem to have less choice than you imagined. If you are standing or walking alone, you are asserting independence and desire to walk your own path. If you dream you trip and miss your footing, you may be on uncertain ground and should check your facts before speaking or acting. If your feet are tied together, you may find it hard to continue with a project without first freeing yourself from others' obstacles and objections. If your feet are hurting, seemingly from walking back and forth between two people who are calling you, cut back on trying to be everything to everybody everywhere. Unseen footsteps in a dream can guide the dreamer in a set direction but may emanate from the past and may no longer have validity. Feet and footstep dreams often occur when the dreamer needs to follow their own direction. To dream of seeing your own feet forewarns of despair, with a sense that you may be overcome by the will and temper of another. To see the feet of others indicates that you will maintain your rights in a pleasant but determined way and win for yourself a place above the common walks of life. To dream of washing your feet means that you will let others take advantage of you, and a firmer boundary may be needed. To dream that your feet are hurting you portends troubles of a humiliating character, often arising from family quarrels. If you see that your feet are swollen and red, you will make a sudden change in your business by separating from your family in some way, physically, financially, or in shared work.
