🛗Dreaming of Elevators

◇Interpretations
Dreams of ascending elevators herald a rise in status and wealth career-wise; descending elevators, discouragement or reduction in opportunity. Being stuck in an elevator between floors indicates feeling trapped by a situation. The elevator rising through the roof and flying offers opportunities to soar, but with the risk of going farther or faster than wanted. If others press the button and take you in the reverse direction you wish to go, your life is being controlled. Being rescued from a stuck elevator, or the elevator suddenly starting, says help will come from a reliable source to resolve a dilemma. A free-falling or crashing elevator warns against impulsive decisions and overambition. Elevators mirror the way life is moving, upward, downward, or stuck going nowhere. If negative, elevator dreams refer to short-term restrictions; if positive, an approaching time of new opportunity. Ascending in an elevator means you will swiftly rise to position and wealth. Descending in one means your misfortunes will crush and discourage you. Seeing one descend without you means you will narrowly escape disappointment in some undertaking. A standing elevator foretells threatened danger.
