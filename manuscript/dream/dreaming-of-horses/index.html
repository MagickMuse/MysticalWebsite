🐴Dreaming of Horses

◇Interpretations
If you dream that you put a saddle or halter on a horse and it is cooperative, you will persuade someone significant to accept your point of view. If the horse is restless or shies away, it may be harder than you think to persuade others to compromise. Persist, for you will win. If you are riding a winged horse through the skies, opportunities are limitless, but do not become careless, as there is a long way to fall. If you are galloping on a horse, travel is favored, especially long-distance. If you dream that you win a horse show or race, you will gain recognition at work or business through combining your skills with a like-minded colleague or partner. The horse, an animal first domesticated in about 1750 BC, is a magical symbol of swiftness, loyalty, and power that depends on mutual trust between horse and rider, and trust between them or between two people is a central feature of horse dreams. Horse dreams can create or restore a love of horses, especially if the dreams link to old worlds. Dreaming of horses suggests accumulating wealth and enjoying life to the fullest. Riding a runaway horse warns that your interests will be harmed by the foolishness of a friend or employer. Watching a horse run away with others foretells news of an ill friend. Fine stallions indicate success and elevated living, though strong passions may overtake you. Broodmares signal harmony and freedom from jealousy for couples and sweethearts. Fording a stream on horseback foretells coming good fortune and rich pleasure. If the water is murky or unsettled, expected joys will fall short. Swimming on a horse's back through clear water means passionate desires will soon be fulfilled, and for those in business, great profit lies ahead. A wounded horse foretells trouble for friends, and a dead horse signals various disappointments. Riding a bucking horse means desires will be hard to attain. Being thrown indicates a strong rival and minor business losses through competition. Being kicked predicts rejection by a loved one and reduced fortune through poor health. Catching a horse to bridle, saddle, or harness predicts significant business improvement. Failing to catch the horse means fortune will betray you. Spotted horses signal that varied ventures will yield profit. Having a horse shod assures success. Shoeing the horse yourself implies effort to claim questionable property. Racehorses indicate overindulgence in fast living, but for the farmer they bring prosperity. Riding in a race brings prosperity and enjoyment. Killing a horse warns of harming friends through selfishness. Mounting bareback predicts wealth and ease earned through struggle. Currying a horse means business will not be neglected for frivolous pleasures. Trimming a mane or tail suggests strong financial skill. Horses pulling vehicles denote wealth tied to obligations, and love will encounter obstacles. Riding uphill where the horse falls but you reach the top brings fortune through struggle against enemies and jealousy. If both reach the top, the rise will be remarkable and lasting. Riding downhill predicts disappointment. A horse with a tender foot warns that some unexpected unpleasantness will intrude on otherwise favorable conditions. Mares in pastures in a dream denote success in business and congenial companions. A barren pasture foretells a period of poverty, but warm friendships.
