Dream Grimoire

✉️Dreaming of Letters

◇Interpretations
Letters indicate new opportunities or challenges. Seeing a registered letter suggests that money matters will disrupt long-established relations. An anonymous letter signals that you will be annoyed by an unsuspected source. Writing one suggests that you are jealous of a rival. Receiving letters bearing unpleasant news points to difficulties or illness. If the news is of a joyous character, you will have many things to be thankful for. If the letter is affectionate but written on colored paper, you will be slighted in love and business. Blue ink signals constancy, affection, and bright fortune. Red implies estrangements through suspicion and jealousy, which may be overcome by wisely maneuvering the suspected party. Failing to read the letter means you will lose something in either your business or social life. If your letter is intercepted, rival enemies are working to defame you. Trying to conceal a letter from a sweetheart or spouse signals that you are interested in unworthy occupations. A letter with a black border signifies distress and the death of a relative. Receiving a letter written on black paper with white ink signals that gloom and disappointment will assail you, and friendly intervention will render small relief. If such a letter passes between spouses, it suggests separation and scandal. To businesspeople, it points to envy and avarice. Writing a letter predicts that you will be hasty in condemning someone, and regrets will follow. A torn letter means that mistakes may ruin your reputation. Receiving a letter by hand indicates that you are acting ungenerously toward your companions or sweetheart, and that you also are not upright. Often dreaming of receiving a letter from a friend foretells their arrival, and you may also hear from this friend by mail. Letters point to new opportunities or challenges.
