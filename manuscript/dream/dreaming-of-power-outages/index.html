💡Dreaming of Power Outages

◇Interpretations
If the power goes out totally in your dream, you may be on overload and your mind may be telling you to step off the treadmill. If you dream you stop the electricity supply by deliberately cutting through wires, you are excluding some activity or person who is draining your energy. If you dream that a fuse has blown, a frustration at work, domestically, or in a relationship is building up and should be dealt with before full shutdown. If power lines have come down, you may have become entangled in others' conflicts or in quarrels you cannot win. If you dream that the power supply is intermittent, a person or situation is proving unreliable. Consider alternatives.
