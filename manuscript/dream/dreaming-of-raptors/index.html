🦅Dreaming of Raptors

◇Interpretations
Raptors such as hawks, eagles, and falcons in dreams symbolize vision, power, and spiritual awareness. They indicate rising above mundane concerns to see the bigger picture. A raptor soaring high suggests you need to gain perspective on a situation by stepping back from details. A raptor hunting successfully indicates you should trust your instincts and strike when opportunity presents. Being attacked by a raptor warns of aggressive competitors or those who prey on perceived weakness. A raptor landing on your arm or shoulder suggests a spiritual messenger or guide is trying to communicate with you. Feeding a raptor indicates you are nurturing your own power and should prepare to use it wisely.
