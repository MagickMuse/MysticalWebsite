🎭Dreaming of Actors

Interpretations
To see an actor in your dreams indicates that your present state will be one of unbroken pleasure and favor. If you dream of yourself as an actor, you will have to work for subsistence, but your labors will be pleasant. If you dream of being in love with one, your inclination and talent will be allied with pleasure and not toil. To dream of a dead actor warns that your good luck will be overwhelmed by misery. To dream of an unemployed actor foretells that your affairs will undergo a change from promise to failure.
