🐇Dreaming of Hares

Animal Source1
Hares
Correspondences3
🌱Fertility🧍🏻Independence🚫Sacrifice
Interpretations
To dream you see a hare running states you should shed an obligation or demand. A hare being chased by dogs suggests arguments among friends or family. Beware of attempting to make peace. Associated with Ostara, Norse goddess of spring, a dream hare sitting on its hind legs represents new beginnings and directions. Hares are linked with the moon in Indian, Buddhist, and Celtic traditions. Surrounded by moonlight, they indicate you should not make unreasonable sacrifices for the sake of pleasing others. Two hares fighting says there may be rival opinions or bids for your favor. Find an alternative. Dreaming of a nesting hare signifies fertility and is a good omen for busy working parents. In Celtic battles, a hare was released to gain the favor of Andraste, the Moon goddess. The hare was her sacred creature. The hare, unlike the more sociable rabbit, ultimately is a creature that chooses its path. A hare escaping from you in a dream means you will lose something valuable in a mysterious way. Capturing a hare means you will be the victor in a contest. Making pets of hares brings an orderly but unintelligent companion. A dead hare betokens death to some friend, and existence will be a commonplace affair. Hares chased by dogs suggest trouble and contentiousness among your friends, and you will attempt to bring about friendly relations. Shooting a hare means you will be forced to use violent measures to maintain your rightful possessions. A rabbit in a dream signifies luck, magickal power, and success. Rabbits also symbolize abundance, foretelling a favorable turn of events and pleasing progress. White rabbits represent faithfulness in love, while rabbits frolicking about imply that children will contribute to your joy.
