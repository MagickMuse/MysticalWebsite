👨‍👩‍👧Dreaming of Birth Parents

◇Interpretations
Suddenly dreaming of a birth parent who resembles you suggests a telepathic link has been awakened. If you are seeking a birth parent, a dream can offer clues about where the missing parent may be. If you do not wish to make contact, dreaming of the unknown parent reminds you that seemingly inexplicable interests may have roots in a genetic link. Dreaming of unknown relatives may signal it is the anniversary of a special time when you are remembered by the missing family. If dreams recur, you may need to make contact, if only once, to fill in a missing part of your life. Waking feeling angry after a birth parent dream link suggests subconscious abandonment issues.
