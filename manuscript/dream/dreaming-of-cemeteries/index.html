⚰️Dreaming of Cemeteries

◇Interpretations
Walking peacefully in a cemetery signals accepting mortality and honoring the past. Being frightened in a cemetery indicates unresolved grief or fear of death. Visiting a specific grave represents connecting with memories or seeking closure. Getting lost in a cemetery warns of dwelling too much on the past. A well-maintained cemetery indicates respect for history and ancestors. An abandoned cemetery suggests forgotten lessons or neglected memories. A beautiful, well-kept cemetery suggests unexpected news of the recovery of someone mourned as dead, or the return of property rightfully yours, while an overgrown, forgotten cemetery predicts living to see loved ones leave and being left in a stranger's care. A mother carrying fresh flowers to a cemetery can expect her family's continued good health, and children gathering flowers and chasing butterflies among the graves foretell prosperous changes with no graves to weep over.
