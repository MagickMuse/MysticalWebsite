Interpretations
Angels act as messengers in dreams to advise or warn when most needed. Seeing the same angel in subsequent dreams identifies that as your guardian angel. Seeing an angel in ordinary clothes except for a light shimmering around it signals you will receive practical help from a stranger in the days ahead. Even if you do not believe in angels, their appearance alerts you to information that your subconscious has picked up. Dreaming of a host of angels or visiting angelic realms indicates you are protected. Look for signs of angels the day after the dream. If drowning in daily demands, angel dreams remind you to get in touch with your spiritual side and listen to your intuition. Angels can also foretell blessings, good fortune, and spiritual wellbeing.

5 Principles for Reading This Entry
  1. Context shapes meaning — the same symbol in different settings carries different messages.
  2. Personal associations come first — your lived experience with a symbol outweighs any universal meaning.
  3. Symbols combine — read multiple symbols together like words in a sentence.
  4. Recurring symbols demand attention — repetition signals something your unconscious is emphasizing.
  5. Record before you look — write your immediate feeling about the symbol before consulting any reference.
