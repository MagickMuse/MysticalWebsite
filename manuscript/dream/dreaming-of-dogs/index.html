🐶Dreaming of Dogs

◇Interpretations
Dreaming about your dog, or a friendly dog if you do not have one, represents the need for a supportive loyal friend, especially if you lack human companionship. A dog running toward you in a dream indicates a reunion with an old friend, or a new one coming into your life. A ferocious dog represents feared hostility, whether your own suppressed anger or that of an unpredictable acquaintance. If you dream of a dog that has died, this may be direct contact or a reminder of the undying love between you. If you dream a dog slips the leash and runs away, you are unconsciously worried about losing a friendship or control of a situation. Dreaming of a constantly barking dog indicates you have something important to say. Choose the right time and do not overstate your case. If you are afraid of your dream dog, examine fears of being let down. A vicious dog in your dream points to enemies and misfortune that cannot be turned aside. A dog nuzzling you signals great gain and constant friends, and a dog of fine quality shows that solid wealth will be yours. If a bloodhound is tracking you, you are likely to fall into a temptation that threatens your downfall. Small dogs reveal that your thoughts and chief pleasures lean toward the frivolous. Dogs biting you in your dream warn of a quarrelsome companion in marriage or business. Lean, filthy dogs point to failure in business and sickness among your children. A dog show signals many and varied favors from fortune. Hearing dogs barking foretells depressing news, with difficulties likely to follow. Dogs chasing foxes or other large game suggest an unusual briskness in all your affairs. Fancy pet dogs reveal a love of show and an owner who is selfish and narrow; for a young woman, this dream foretells a fop for a sweetheart. Feeling great fright at the sight of a large mastiff means you will encounter inconvenience because of efforts to rise above mediocrity. Hearing dogs growling and snarling means you are at the mercy of scheming people and will be afflicted with unpleasant surroundings at home. The lonely baying of a dog foretells a death or a long separation from friends. Dogs growling and fighting portend that you will be overcome by your enemies and that your life will be filled with depression. If dogs and cats seemingly on friendly terms suddenly turn on each other, baring teeth and breaking into a fight, you will meet with disaster in love and worldly pursuits unless you can quell the row. A friendly white dog approaching you portends a victorious engagement, in business or in love; for a woman, this is an omen of an early marriage. A many-headed dog warns that you are trying to maintain too many branches of business at once; success comes only with concentration of energies. Anyone who wishes to succeed should take heed of this dream. A mad dog means your most strenuous efforts will not bring the results you want, and a fatal disease may even be clutching at your vitals. If a mad dog succeeds in biting you, you or some loved one is on the verge of insanity, and a tragedy may occur. Traveling alone with a dog following you foretells staunch friends and successful undertakings. Swimming dogs represent an easy stretch toward happiness and fortune. A dog killing a cat in your presence signals profitable dealings and some unexpected pleasure. A dog killing a snake in your presence is an omen of good luck. Puppies in a dream signal that you will entertain the innocent and hapless and thereby enjoy pleasure. Friendships will grow stronger and fortune will increase if the pups are healthy and well formed; the reverse if they are lean and dirty.
