🐆Dreaming of Panthers

◇Interpretations
Seeing a panther and experiencing fright denotes that contracts in love or business may be canceled unexpectedly due to adverse influences working against you. Killing or overpowering the animal means you will experience joy and be successful in your undertakings. If one menaces you by its presence, you will have disappointments in business; other people will likely renege on their promises to you. Hearing the growl of a panther and experiencing terror or fright means you should expect unfavorable news of reduced profit or gain, as well as social discord; if you're not frightened in the dream, the news won't be as bad. As with all cats, seeing a panther in a dream is a warning, unless you kill it.
