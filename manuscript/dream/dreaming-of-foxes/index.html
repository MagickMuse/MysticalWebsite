🦊Dreaming of Foxes

Animal Source1
Foxes
Correspondences6
🎨Creativity🦊Cunning👨‍👨‍👧‍👧Family✨Inspiration🔮Magick🏕️Survival
Interpretations
If a fox appears in your dreams, you need to question the status quo and discover viable alternatives if your thinking is becoming circular. If you are the fox, you will survive a present crisis but may need to be more single-minded and self-focused. If you dream of a vixen with her cubs, family matters will take center stage, and it is important to stay united. If you dream a fox is slaughtering chickens, beware of someone who is a ruthless influence in your life who gains access to your heart through flattery. Use subtlety and tact to persuade others to your point of view rather than direct confrontation. In some creation stories, Silver Fox made the universe and awakens creativity if life has become too predictable. Fox in Celtic mythology showed the way into the Otherworld of light and beauty, and fox dreams can likewise restore magick where comfort has taken away the edge of possibility. Chasing a fox in a dream implies you are engaging in doubtful speculations and risky love affairs. A fox slyly coming into your yard means to beware of envious friendships. Your reputation is being slyly assailed. Killing a fox points to winning in every engagement.
