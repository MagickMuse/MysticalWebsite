🐍Dreaming of Eels

Animal Source1
Eels
Correspondences7
💍Commitment💵Fortune🕊️Freedom🚀Opportunity❤️‍🔥Passion🤝Promises👫Relationships
Interpretations
A wriggling eel indicates someone who is trying to get out of a promise they have made. If you have caught an eel on a fishing hook, beware of a charismatic but unreliable person in your life who you may find hard to let go. Holding an eel that is trying to slip away suggests the need to hold on to advantage or sudden good fortune. If you are repelled by being given an eel, this may represent sexual passion without commitment, or fear of your own unrequited sexual feelings. If you catch a huge electric eel or sea snake, your certainties may be suddenly shaken, but this could lead to new energies and opportunities. If you see eels swimming through murky water, there are deep feelings you find hard to express or reveal that, if left unspoken, will prevent a relationship from going forward or end a relationship that seems to be going nowhere. Killing eels entangling you represents seeking freedom from emotional ties going nowhere. Eels, by their nature elusive, can represent immediate gratification but rarely offer lasting happiness, love, or good fortune. Maintaining a firm grip on an eel is a good omen; lose the grip and any fortune that comes will be fleeting. For a woman, seeing an eel in clear water denotes new but short-lived pleasures. A dead eel signifies that you will overcome your most maliciously inclined enemies. For lovers, the dream foretells the end of a long and hazardous courtship or marriage.
