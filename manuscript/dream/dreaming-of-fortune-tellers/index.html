🔮Dreaming of Fortune-Tellers

Correspondences8
💼Career🤔Decisions🔮Divination🔮Future🤷Indecision🔮Intuition🔮Prophecy🧠Wisdom
Interpretations
If the fortune-teller seems familiar in the dream, your own wise unconscious mind may be advising you in the guise of a fortune-teller. If the fortune-teller reveals images in a crystal ball, the messages will help you to see the best future path ahead if you note what they mean to you. If the fortune-teller in the dream picks only bad cards, this is not an omen of disaster, rather a reflection of your own buried fears. If the fortune-teller predicts major changes, these are options you are considering for which you are seeking permission. If you dream you are telling fortunes for someone you know, beware of making decisions for them and being blamed if they go wrong. Fortune-telling dreams often tell the dreamer what they want to hear, and it becomes a self-fulfilling prophecy, giving the dreamer permission to take the desired action. Even the best fortune-teller, in dreams or in actuality, ultimately offers an opt-out clause of matters only the dreamer should solve. Dreaming of consulting a fortune-teller signals you need help cutting through indecision in a career or personal matter. If you dream of being a fortune-teller yourself, you possess sound judgment worth trusting.
