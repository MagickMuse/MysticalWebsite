🛎️Dreaming of Servants

◇Interpretations
A servant in a dream is a sign that you will have good fortune, despite gloomy appearances. Anger is likely to push you into unnecessary worry and quarrels. Discharging a servant foretells regret and loss. Quarreling with one in a dream indicates that you will, upon waking, have real cause for censuring someone derelict in duty. Being robbed by one shows that you have someone near you who does not respect the laws of ownership.
