🐄Dreaming of Herds

◇Interpretations
If you dream of herding peaceful animals such as sheep or cows, beware that you do not assume others will automatically follow you. If you dream of wild herds, you may need to go along with the majority view. If you are riding on the back of a wild horse galloping with a herd, you crave freedom. If you are following a herd at a distance, you are not ready to take the lead or not even sure you want to. A herd of cows or goats being milked is a sign of growing prosperity. If you dream that a herd will not go in the direction you want, you may need to be more assertive or accept a nonleadership position. When surrounded or threatened by a herd, human or animal, beware of taking others for granted. Herd dreams are linked with issues of leading and following and say, 'There is a time to lead and a time to follow.' To dream of a herd of animals means that you will soon be following the good advice of friends and family for your financial gain.
