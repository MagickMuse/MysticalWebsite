🎓Dreaming of Good Manners and Politeness

Correspondences3
⚖️Ethics🙏Respect📝Values
Interpretations
If someone in a dream is excessively polite, they may simply be seeking admiration. If in the dream you are being considerate to others, do not let anyone influence you toward selfishness because of their poor standards. If you dream of being in a royal court or in the Victorian era, you may need to sometimes adapt gestures of old-fashioned courtesy without sacrificing consideration. If you are abused in a dream for being excessively courteous, selfish people in your life are seeking to divert attention from their own grasping ways. If you are behaving rudely in a dream, you may have wrongly been made to feel inferior or out of touch by others. Dreams about good manners reinforce the dreamer's ethics in a world where others may have different values. Politeness dreams can reflect any area, principle, or issue of life where the dreamer is pressured to abandon what they know to be right. Seeing people practicing poor manners in a dream implies a failure to carry out undertakings because of a colleague's actions. Meeting people with affable manners means you will be pleasantly surprised by affairs of moment, with your luck taking a favorable turn.
