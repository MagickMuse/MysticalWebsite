🧠Dreaming of Heads

◇Interpretations
Seeing a person's head in a dream that is well shaped and prominent means you will meet people of power and vast influence who will lend you aid in enterprises of importance. Dreaming of your own head means you are threatened with nerve or brain trouble. A head severed from its trunk and bloody means you will meet sickening disappointments and the overthrow of your dearest hopes and anticipations. Seeing yourself with two or more heads foretells phenomenal and rapid rise in life, but the rise will not be stable. An aching head indicates you will be oppressed with worry and that you may need to see a doctor. A swollen head suggests you will have more good than bad in your life. The head of a beast implies that the nature of your desires will run on a low plane, and only material pleasures will concern you. Washing your head means you will be sought after by prominent people for your judgment and good counsel.
