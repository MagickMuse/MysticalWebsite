Dreaming of Bones

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of bones after a good dinner indicates you should seize opportunities to enjoy life to the full. Crumbling bones speak of feeling helpless if change is imminent. Burying the bones of a dead bird or animal signals it is time to accept what cannot be mended. A family member or good friend revealed as being unduly thin beneath their clothes suggests they may have an eating disorder and need help. Fracturing a bone in the dream warns to be careful if engaging in extreme activities, but this also can indicate the divulging of a secret. Cutting spare meat off a bone and boiling the bones suggests you may need to find a new source of income. A bone protruding through the skin is a warning that treachery may trap or ensnare you, and a pile of bones points to financial loss.
