🐕Dreaming of Animals

◇Interpretations
Dreaming about an impending disaster shown by strange animal behavior indicates you are tapping into your psychic powers. Take precautions. Seeing fish leaping out of the water may be a forewarning of a disaster. Frequently having these dreams suggests considering working with animals or seismic monitoring. Sensing the dream is symbolic and not an actual warning of a disaster means your dream animals may be alerting you to an insecure situation to be avoided. Fish leaping from water or snakes emerging from holes without the immediacy of an actual prediction warns to beware of a huge emotional outburst by someone close. Sensing the animals are escaping to a particular area indicates that is generally a safe place. A dream about a friendly, calm animal is positive. However, to dream of an animal that you are afraid of, or you fear might attack you, is negative.

5 Principles for Reading This Entry
  1. Context shapes meaning — the same symbol in different settings carries different messages.
  2. Personal associations come first — your lived experience with a symbol outweighs any universal meaning.
  3. Symbols combine — read multiple symbols together like words in a sentence.
  4. Recurring symbols demand attention — repetition signals something your unconscious is emphasizing.
  5. Record before you look — write your immediate feeling about the symbol before consulting any reference.
