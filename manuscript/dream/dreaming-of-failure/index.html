❌Dreaming of Failure

Correspondences9
💔Betrayal💼Business💼Career💪Confidence🩺Health➖Loss❤️‍🔥Romance😰Stress🏆Success
Interpretations
Failure dreams can indicate areas where the dreamer is aware on a deep level that success might not bring happiness. Failure in a dream examination, test, or interview can symbolize fears of not living up to the unrealistic expectations of others. Failing-health dreams indicate stress, doing too much, or awareness of a health issue before it manifests in the body. Dreaming of a relationship failing may reflect a past betrayal, projected on the present situation, making the dreamer more likely to fail. If in a dream you experience a business or financial failure, check the reliability of others involved and proceed with caution, as relevant factors may yet be revealed. If you dream you fail to be included in a team, whether in sports or in the workplace, your lack of confidence may be causing you to make mistakes. If failure is unexpected, your dream radar may alert you to underhanded dealings or adverse factors causing your failure. If failure is inconsistent and inexplicable, the cause of the trend should be examined. For a lover, a dream of failure is sometimes of contrary significance, with fear in the dream rather than true injury. If a man dreams that he fails in his pursuit of marriage, this signifies that he only needs more mastery and energy, as he already has the love and esteem of his sweetheart. For a young woman to dream that her life is going to be a failure denotes that she is not applying her opportunities to good advantage. For a businessperson to dream of failure forebodes loss and bad management, which should be corrected, or failure threatens to materialize in earnest.
