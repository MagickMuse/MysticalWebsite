🥚Dreaming of Fertility

◇Interpretations
Dreams that are indications of potential conception include a pomegranate bursting open and its seeds scattering in the air; sharing a coconut with a partner; a hatching egg; and rabbits running in an open field. Apples falling from a tree suggest the baby will be a boy, and pears falling from a tree signify the baby will be a girl. Being given red roses by your partner in the dream predicts a boy, and white roses, a girl. Sharing a bag of hazelnuts with a partner suggests early conception and a second and even third or fourth birth, closely following the previous ones. Matching lotus flowers indicate twins; three lotus, triplets; and a whole bunch, a large family. Seeing a baby in a dream reassures you that you can have a child or children, especially if you see the birth in the dream or regularly dream of the same baby or child. This may be the soul of your yet-to-be-conceived child waiting to take birth. Fertility dreams usually express their meaning in symbols that have been used for thousands of years and relate to the natural world. Fertility dreams are reassuring where one or both members of a couple are fearful.
