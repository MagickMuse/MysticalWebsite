🐓Dreaming of Cock Crowing

Animal Source1
Roosters
Correspondences4
🥊Conflict😰Despair💏Infidelity💍Marriage
Interpretations
The crowing cock in dreams shifts its meaning entirely depending on the time of day in the dream. To dream of hearing a cock crowing in the morning is significant of good. If you are single, it denotes an early marriage and a luxurious home; the dawn call heralds new beginnings and domestic prosperity. To hear one at night means despair and cause for tears. The crow in darkness carries no hope with it, only sorrow and grief ahead. If you dream of seeing cocks fighting, you will leave your family because of quarrels and infidelity. This dream usually announces some unexpected and sorrowful events. The combat between roosters signals that conflict within the home will drive you from it.
