🐄Dreaming of Cattle

◇Interpretations
To dream of seeing good-looking and fat cattle contentedly grazing in green pastures denotes prosperity and happiness thanks to a congenial and pleasant companion. If the cattle are lean, shaggy, and poorly fed, you will be likely to toil all your life because of misspent energy and dislike of details in work. Correct your habits after this dream. To see cattle stampeding means you must exert power to keep your career profitable. A herd of cows at milking time indicates that you will achieve wealth as a result of the work of many people. If you dream of milking cows with full udders, good fortune is in store for you. See also: Calves. A well-fed ox signifies that you will become an influential person in your field. Fat oxen in green pastures represent fortune and your rise to positions beyond your expectations. If they are lean, your fortune will dwindle. Oxen well matched and yoked betoken a happy and wealthy marriage, or that you are already joined to your true mate. A dead ox is a sign of bereavement. Oxen drinking from a clear pond or stream mean you will end up with something or someone long desired.
