🦅Dreaming of Flying

◇Interpretations
If you fly out of trouble in a dream, this gives you power in daily life to overcome fears and attacks. Flying dreams are associated with out-of-body experiences, in which the spirit or mind is able to leave the body in the dream state and travel to a chosen destination. Flying dreams are associated with freeing oneself from restrictions, a major step forward in connecting with spiritual realms. Children's flying dreams occur because the child has not developed the concept of what is logically possible and so can fly anywhere in dreams and, when they awaken, can describe with accuracy what they saw. With adolescents, teens, and twentysomethings, flying experiences may be linked with emerging sexual powers. The emergence or reemergence of flying dreams in adults reflects growing psychic powers, especially for extraterrestrial experiences. Flying dreams are often linked with falling dreams, where the dreamer is able to save themself by floating or flying. Since many flying dreams are triggered by escaping from danger, astral dreams are no longer so necessary and therefore rarely occur once earlier fears are overcome. Flying high over the earth in a dream points to marital calamities. Flying low, almost to the ground, points to sickness and uneasy states from which you will recover. Flying over muddy water warns you to keep close with your private affairs, as enemies are watching to enthrall you. Flying over broken places signifies ill luck and gloomy surroundings. Noticing green trees and vegetation below you means you will suffer temporary embarrassment followed by a flood of prosperity. Seeing the sun while you fly suggests useless worries, for you will succeed despite any fears of evil. Flying through the firmament, passing the moon and other planets, foretells famine, wars, and troubles of all kinds. Falling while flying portends your downfall. Waking during your fall, however, means you will succeed in reinstating yourself.
