Dream Grimoire

⛸️Dreaming of Ice-Skating

Correspondences2
💔Betrayal➖Loss
Interpretations
Skating on ice in a dream signals that you are in danger of losing employment or something of value. Breaking through the ice means unworthy friends will counsel you. Watching others skate suggests that disagreeable people will connect your name in scandal with someone who admires you. Seeing ice skates points to discord among your associates.
