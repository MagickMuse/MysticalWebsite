Interpretations
A dream of a bright star overhead or one that moves close indicates future fame and fortune using your existing talents in the area where your gifts lie. A dream of a shooting star heralds a sudden flash of inspiration or that a loving deceased relative is supporting you. A single twinkling star foretells the conception and birth of a very gifted baby. Two stars joined or close together signify a twin soul either in your life or coming into your life soon. Stars that fade warn that an opportunity will be missed unless you actively pursue it. Following a moving star will guide you to success and maybe the chance of overseas travel. Dreams of traveling to or through the stars can be a form of out-of-body travel and indicate that you are a star soul. Looking at clear, shining stars in a dream foretells good health and prosperity. If they are dull, trouble and misfortune lie ahead. A shooting or falling star denotes sadness and grief. Stars appearing and vanishing mysteriously mean strange changes and events may occur in the near future. If a star falls on you, there will be a bereavement in your family. Stars rolling around on the earth are a sign of formidable danger and trying times.
