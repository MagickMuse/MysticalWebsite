🎤Dreaming of Microphones

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of being offered a roving or radio microphone says your influence will reach a wider audience. A fixed microphone indicates success in specific expertise. Broadcasting in a studio, singing, or acting on stage indicates success in the performing arts; the larger the stage or better known the network, the higher you can fly. If you dream your words are not clear through a microphone, express your ideas more assertively or in a different way. If the microphone is muted, you are wasting your time communicating with those who have their own agenda. Dreaming of karaoke indicates pleasurable, easy communication with friends, while sharing a mike with a lover promises harmony between you. A hidden microphone warns against speaking too freely with those you do not know or expressing views that may prove unpopular. Echoing microphones suggest that you express views of others with which you don't necessarily agree in order to keep the peace. Using a microphone yourself suggests that your advice and opinions will be heard loud and clear. Seeing someone else use one means you will have to be forceful with your advice and opinions if you hope to be heard.
