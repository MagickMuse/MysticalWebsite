🍑Dreaming of Apricot

Correspondences3
⚠️Caution😭Grief😢Sorrow
Plant Source1
Apricot
Interpretations
Growing apricots in a dream suggests that despite a promising and rosy outward appearance, bitterness and sorrow lie ahead. Eating them signals that troubling influences are drawing close. If others are the ones eating, expect your surroundings to become uncomfortable and difficult.
