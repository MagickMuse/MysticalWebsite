Dream Grimoire

✨Dreaming of Illusions

Correspondences6
🎭Deception🌙Escapism🦄Fantasy🎭Illusion❤️‍🔥Romance🌟Wishes
Interpretations
If you dream of a solution, such as walking toward an oasis, that suddenly disappears, this is telling you to strive for a more realistic relationship or opportunity. If the magick remains to the end of the dream, it may be attainable. If you dream that a stage magician doubles your money and then disappears in a puff of smoke, beware of a charismatic figure. If your dream is surrounded by mist or you are floating on clouds, this is an escapist dream to be enjoyed but not taken seriously. If an attractive partner comes close and then fades and you cannot touch them, you may have a wonderful romance rather than a permanent future. If you see yourself as a celebrity, you may have incredibly good luck, but more likely you will experience a gradual but lasting improvement in your circumstances. Illusion dreams are wish-fulfillment, but if the images fade before the end of the dream, they may not translate into reality. If we settle for illusion as seen in a dream, we may lose out on happiness waiting in the real world. An illusion in a dream, or awareness of one within the dream, suggests that valuable information entering your life will advance your career or personal goals.
