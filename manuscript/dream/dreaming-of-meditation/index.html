🧘Dreaming of Meditation

◇Interpretations
If you dream of ashrams or temples and meditating people wearing robes, this may be a past-life dream, using methods you have not consciously learned. If you dream of happily meditating with friends and family, you are entering a harmonious period in your personal life. If you are constantly facing interruptions when meditating, others' demands are intruding on your personal space. If you are also practicing yoga, tai chi, or other energy transference methods in your dreams, you are entering a period of being at home in yourself. If you cannot focus on meditation in your dream but others around you are focusing, you may be seeking the wrong kind of spiritual enlightenment. If you are meditating happily alone, you need more self-time, but if lonely you may need support from those who understand your life aims.
