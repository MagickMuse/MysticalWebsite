Dream Grimoire

⛽Dreaming of Gasoline

◇Interpretations
If you dream your vehicle is filled with gasoline and heading for the open road, life ahead will expand, in both work and pleasure. If your gas tank is empty and you reach the gas station just in time, you may have felt drained by life, but life will improve. If in your dream you run out of gas in the middle of nowhere, check your vehicle for roadworthiness. If the gas station is out of fuel, you should not rely on the usual resources or support networks. If by mistake you fill your car with diesel instead of gasoline, plans may have to be revised. If you are driving an electric car, you may have to or may need to move on from what is outworn or outmoded. If the dreamer ignores an opportunity to fill the vehicle with gasoline, they may subconsciously not want to reach their destination. Gasoline dreams can mark the end of the road in a life stage or relationship. To dream of gasoline can also indicate that, after a short struggle, there will be a successful completion of a task at hand.
