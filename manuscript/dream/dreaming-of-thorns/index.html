🌹Dreaming of Thorns

◇Interpretations
A thorn hedge is a traditional symbol of magick and psychic defense. It indicates growing occult knowledge or spiritual practices. Thorns provide a barrier against those who are critical or are draining your self-confidence, especially neighbors or relatives. A rose protected by thorns says you may feel pressured into sex or sexual practices with which you are uncomfortable. Trust your protective instincts. To be tangled in thorns indicates a difficult situation from which you need to extricate yourself carefully to avoid further pain. Thorn bushes in blossom promise better times ahead after stagnation or an impasse. If you dream that thorns are hidden in greenery, a seemingly supportive friend or family member will show secret spite or jealousy. Thorns in a dream are an omen of dissatisfaction, and evil will surround every effort toward advancement. Thorns hidden beneath green foliage mean secret enemies will interfere with your prosperity.
