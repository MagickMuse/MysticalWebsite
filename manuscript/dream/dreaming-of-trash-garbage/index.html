🗑️Dreaming of Trash, Garbage

◇Interpretations
If you dream you have cleared trash, you are entering a harmonious, orderly time in your domestic or work life. To dream of piles of trash in or around your home advises that you resolve old issues and long-standing arguments. Dreaming of disposing of other people's trash means you are accepting problems others have created. If in your dream every time you clear trash from your home or yard it reappears, protest, as others are adding to your workload. If you dream you are searching through trash for something precious, value what you have in a relationship or risk losing it. If you dream someone is taking your trash away, you have escaped the consequences of a mistake. To see heaps of garbage in your dream can also indicate looming thoughts of social scandal and unfavorable business dealings of all kinds drawing near.
