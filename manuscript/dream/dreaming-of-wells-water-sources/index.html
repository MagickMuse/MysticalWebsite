💞Dreaming of Wells (Water Sources)

Correspondences9
⛈️Adversity😰Despair⚔️Enemies🌱Fertility❤️‍🩹Healing💕Love⚡Vitality🧠Wisdom🌟Wishes
Interpretations
Traditionally an archetypal symbol of life, health, and fertility, wells were called the entrance to the womb of the Mother Goddess. Dreaming of taking water from a healing well suggests that you have within you the ability to heal a situation or to tap into a deep source of wisdom. A dried-up, abandoned, or polluted well expresses loss of vitality, optimism, and creativity. If a well is overflowing, you may be showing excessive generosity or nurturing to others and neglecting your own needs. If you dream you fall into a well, you may have become overemotionally involved in others' problems. Being stuck in a well suggests that you will succumb to adversity through your misapplied energies. You will let strange elements direct your course. Falling into a well signifies that overwhelming despair will possess you. A well caving in promises that enemies' schemes will overthrow your own. Seeing an empty well means you will be robbed of a fortune if you allow strangers to share your confidence. Seeing a well with a pump in it shows that you will have opportunities to advance your prospects. An artesian well means your splendid resources will gain you admittance into the realms of knowledge and pleasure. Drawing water from a well augurs the fulfillment of ardent desires, but if the water is impure, there will be unpleasantness.
