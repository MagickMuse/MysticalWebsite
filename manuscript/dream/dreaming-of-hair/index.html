💇Dreaming of Hair

Correspondences6
🧲Attraction✨Charisma⚡Energy🩺Health👑Power💪Strength
Material Source1
Hair
Interpretations
Shining long hair symbolizes strength, sexual attraction, and good health. Hair falling out in a dream indicates fears of loss of power and charisma. If you dream your hair is tangled, you may have tied yourself up with too many responsibilities at the expense of your own harmony. If your hair is blowing free in the wind and you are happy, break free of others' restrictions. If you are brushing your hair while gazing in a mirror, you may see your true love, present or future, behind you in the dream mirror. Washing your hair in a dream signifies clearing unresolved issues that are causing uncertainty in your actions and reactions. Having your hair cut badly in a dream warns that someone is draining your energy or dominating you. Hair represents the image we present to the world. Seeing your hair turning gray foretells death and contagion in the family of some relative or friend. Being covered with hair is an omen of indulgence in vices to an extent that will disbar you from the society of refined people. Well-kept and neatly combed hair means your fortune will improve. Cutting your hair close to the scalp indicates that you will be generous to the point of lavishness toward a friend. Hair growing out soft and luxuriant signifies happiness and luxury. Tangled and unkempt hair means life will be a burden, business will fall off, and the marital yoke will be troublesome to carry. Having your hair cut suggests serious disappointments. Hair falling out with baldness apparent means you will suffer financial loss. A lock of your hair turning gray and falling out is a sign of trouble and disappointment in your affairs, with sickness casting gloom over bright expectations. Washing your hair means your troubles will soon go down the drain.
