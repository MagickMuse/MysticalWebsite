💣Dreaming of Air Raids

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of wartime aircraft bombing tragedies or your home being bombed may indicate past-life trauma or free-floating memories acquired from a restless spirit by a psychic person. Escaping from an air raid in any land or war signals an attack on your reputation or threat to your job or finances will be avoided. Helping to free others from air-raid wreckage suggests you may be asked to help a friend or family member who has run into major difficulties. Hearing air-raid sirens warns against ignoring warning signs that you are taking unnecessary risks. Seeking refuge in an air-raid shelter advises waiting for a confrontation or action until matters have calmed down. Leading a bombing raid indicates matters to confront head-on at work or with hostile neighbors.
