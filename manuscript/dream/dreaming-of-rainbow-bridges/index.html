🌉Dreaming of Rainbow Bridges

Correspondences6
👻Afterlife⭐Destiny💕Love🐕Pets🎯Purpose🔄Transition
Symbol Source1
Rainbow
Interpretations
If you dream of a rainbow bridge at a point of major change, it's time to put travel or movement plans into action. If you are on the bridge with someone you have lost in death, you may be sharing their transition to the afterlife. If you dream of deceased pets on a rainbow bridge that has become associated with the place they waited for owners, you will know their love is still with you. If you are walking across a rainbow bridge and are told, whether by a deceased person or spirit guide, that you must go back to fulfill your destiny, this is equivalent to a near-death experience but experienced during a life crisis when you may not want to go on. If a rainbow bridge fades before you can cross it, you may not be ready yet for a new life.
