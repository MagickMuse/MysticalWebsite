🐱Dreaming of Cats

◇Interpretations
A cat in a dream generally signals misfortune unless you manage to kill or drive it away. A cat that attacks you warns that enemies will stop at nothing to ruin your reputation, but successfully driving it off means you will overcome serious obstacles and rise in fortune and renown. The sound of a cat screaming or meowing reveals that a false friend is actively working to do you harm. A cat that scratches you warns that a rival will succeed in stealing the rewards of your own hard work. A friendly cat represents independence, intuition, and feminine energy. An aggressive cat warns of hidden hostility or feminine power turned negative. A black cat can signal good fortune or, traditionally, warns to trust your intuition. Many cats indicate strong psychic sensitivity or feeling overwhelmed by demands. Feeding a cat suggests nurturing your independent, intuitive nature. A cat ignoring you reflects feelings of being dismissed or taken for granted.
