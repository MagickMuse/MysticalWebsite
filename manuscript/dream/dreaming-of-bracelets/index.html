📿Dreaming of Bracelets

Interpretations
Being gifted a bracelet signals you will have love or the increase of love by the donor. Wearing a charm bracelet indicates you will enjoy continuing good fortune. A charm that stands out signifies extra good fortune in the area of life symbolized by the charm. Losing a charm bracelet in your dream suggests you may fear that good fortune depends on fate and not on your own efforts. Wearing a silver bracelet represents new love, trust, and family affection. Gold represents lasting love. Copper represents health. A bracelet breaking or losing it indicates you may worry that a relationship is too fragile to last. Buying yourself a bracelet, especially a charm bracelet, signals to take control of your own destiny rather than relying on others. A bracelet encircling your arm assures you of an early and happy marriage. Losing a bracelet signifies loss and vexation, while finding one means good property will come into your possession.
