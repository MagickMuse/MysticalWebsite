🍊Dreaming of Oranges

◇Interpretations
Oranges are a symbol of the sun, health, fertility, prosperity, and travel. The orange blossom has been a good omen for betrothals, marriages, and children from Ancient China onward. A basket of oranges indicates many opportunities for prosperity in career and fertility in every way. Eating the orange is especially fortuitous. If you are wearing the color orange, you will gain popularity with others and your creativity will bring tangible rewards. Dreaming of squeezing oranges to make juice suggests that you may be asked to give too much of yourself. Seeing orange trees or eating oranges in a dream signifies health and prosperous surroundings.
