🍺Dreaming of Drunkenness

Correspondences8
🚭Addiction💼Business⚠️Caution🎮Control💼Employment💕Love🍎Temptation🥷Theft
Interpretations
To dream of being drunk, if you do not drink excessively, talks about fear of losing control. If in a dream you are drinking to excess, you may be tempted to abandon common sense and give way to temptation. If you are craving a drink in a dream and this is out of character, you may want to shed your inhibitions but fear the consequences. If in a dream someone you care for is very drunk, you may worry about their reliability in a crisis. If someone is deliberately trying to get you drunk in a dream, question their motives for controlling you in everyday life. If there is only alcohol to drink in a dream and you are very thirsty, are you prepared to take the consequences of satisfying a desire? Drunkenness in any form is unreliable as a good dream, a warning to everyone to shift their thoughts into more healthful channels. Being drunk on heavy liquors is unfavorable, signaling profligacy and loss of employment, with disgrace coming through forgery or theft. Seeing others in a drunken condition foretells unhappy states for you, and likely for them. However, being drunk on wine means fortune in business and in love, with exalted heights in literary pursuits, and always foretells aesthetic experiences.
