🏰Dreaming of Castles

Interpretations
Living in a castle represents aspirations for higher status, security, and achievement. A castle under siege warns of threats to your security or position. Exploring castle rooms indicates discovering hidden aspects of yourself. A ruined castle suggests abandoned dreams or past glory now faded. Being locked in a castle tower represents feeling trapped by your own or others' expectations. Defending a castle shows protecting what you've built against opposition. Being inside a castle in a dream reveals that you have the financial means to build the life you desire, with freedom to travel widely and form connections with people from many cultures. An old, vine-draped castle speaks to romantic sensibilities but warns against rushing into a poorly suited engagement or marriage. Leaving a castle foretells theft or the loss of a loved one to death.
