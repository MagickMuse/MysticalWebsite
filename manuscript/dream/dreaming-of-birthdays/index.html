Dream Grimoire

🎂Dreaming of Birthdays

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of a birthday celebration that materializes indicates a fortunate year ahead. Dreaming of a fabulous celebration that does not occur signals a year when you will have to work hard to fulfill your own expectations. Receiving a special birthday gift in a dream signals unexpected good luck in the form of an offer that will bring rewards. Your partner forgetting your birthday suggests they may be taking you for granted. No one remembering your birthday indicates your expectations of others are too high. Organizing a friend's, partner's, or relative's birthday celebration and they are pleased is a good sign for mutual give-and-take in the year ahead. For the young, dreaming of a birthday can signal poverty and falsehood, while for the old it indicates long trouble and desolation.
