Dream Grimoire

🎹Dreaming of Organs

Correspondences2
👫Friendship💰Good Fortune
Interpretations
Hearing the pealing of a pipe organ in a dream signifies lasting friendships and well-established fortune. Rendering harmonious music on an organ means you will be fortunate along your path to worldly comfort, with much social distinction given you. Hearing doleful singing with organ accompaniment forecasts that you are nearing a wearisome task and probable loss of friends or position.
