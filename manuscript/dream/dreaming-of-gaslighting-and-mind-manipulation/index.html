🔥Dreaming of Gaslighting and Mind Manipulation

Correspondences4
⚠️Abuse🎮Control🤥Manipulation💝Trust
Interpretations
If you dream of being unjustifiably blamed for mistakes at home or work and are starting to doubt yourself, a dream will reveal the culprit. If in your dream you are trying in vain to convince authorities of your competence, someone may be deliberately gaslighting you as a form of control. If you wake from a dream confused about what is a dream and what is reality, collect evidence if you feel you are being manipulated. If in your dream you are in the hospital when you are not ill, you are being stripped of your power, whether at work or home. Trust your own judgment. If a controlling partner is being unusually helpful in a dream, this may be a forewarning of an ulterior motive. If you are being recorded on a camera or audio device in your dream, someone may be trying to incriminate you into saying or doing something you will regret. Gaslighting or mind manipulation is a cruel form of psychological control, but dreams will often reveal the true situation. Gaslighting is a much-underrated problem in today's society, and dreams can offer the impetus for the dreamer to initiate defensive action.
