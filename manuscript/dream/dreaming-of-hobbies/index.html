Dream Grimoire

🎨Dreaming of Hobbies

Correspondences4
🔄Change🔗Connection🎨Hobbies🎨Self-Expression
Interpretations
If you dream you are relaxing by enjoying a solitary hobby, you may need more self-time away from others and work. If you dream you have a pile of discarded hobbies, you are seeking to find meaning in your life and may need to persevere with one, rather than giving up at the first obstacle. If you dream you are sharing a hobby with friends or family you have not seen for a while, contact the people in the dream to breathe new life into the hobby. If you are no longer enjoying a hobby in your dream, you may have outgrown a phase in your life. If you are practicing a hobby because you always have, be sure it is not holding you back from a more people-centered life. If you dream that your collection is broken or faded, there may be aspects of your life where you are holding on to what no longer exists. A hobby can be personal, but if it totally excludes others, it may represent your avoiding the world. Hobbies represent the part of the dreamer that expresses and enriches their life. A hobby appearing in a dream implies that annoying changes are coming up in your life, but they are inconsequential.
