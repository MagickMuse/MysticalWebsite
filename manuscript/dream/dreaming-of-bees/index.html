🐝Dreaming of Bees

Animal Source1
Bees
Correspondences9

Interpretations
Bees on flowers are a potent sign of imminent conception and a healthy child who will be lucky all their days. A single large queen bee buzzing loudly warns of a controlling older woman who can be spiteful if crossed. Bees flying in and out of hives indicate health, business or career success, and prosperity. A bee flying into a room or seen outside a window trying to enter heralds good news of friends and family, usually from afar. Dreaming you are a beekeeper signals promotion or an increase in status or recognition. An empty hive or all the bees flying away warns of empty promises, especially in love, or ventures that will not flourish. Bees in a dream are a sign of pleasant and profitable times ahead. Those in positions of authority can expect cooperative employees and a thriving atmosphere. A bee sting, however, warns of possible loss or physical harm.
