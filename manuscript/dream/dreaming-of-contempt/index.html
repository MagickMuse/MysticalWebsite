💔Dreaming of Contempt

◇Interpretations
Contempt in dreams carries distinct omens depending on its direction and whether it is deserved. If you dream of being in contempt of court, you have committed business or social indiscretions in waking life. The legal setting amplifies the weight of your misstep. If you dream of being held in contempt by others without just cause, you will succeed in winning their highest regard and will find yourself prosperous and happy. Unearned scorn inverts to esteem and abundance. If the contempt is merited, however, exile from business or social circles is indicated. The judgment reflects a genuine failing, and its consequences will follow.
