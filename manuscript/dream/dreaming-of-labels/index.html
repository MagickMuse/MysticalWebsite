🏷️Dreaming of Labels

Correspondences5
😱Fear🪪Identity📋Order📋Organization👑Self-Worth
Material Source1
🏷️Labels
Interpretations
If you dream you are labeling objects, clothes, or piles of paper, you are trying to bring order and organization to an area of disorganization in your life. If you dream the labels come off goods, you may need to assess for yourself the relative merits of an offer or situation. If items are wrongly labeled, you may doubt the true nature of a person or situation. If you dream you are wearing a label to identify yourself or your role at a meeting or conference, you may feel prejudged or undervalued. A designer label in a dream suggests that you may be being judged on superficial criteria. If an item has a REDUCED label, you may fear that your own welcome or usefulness is waning. Reading labels suggests that you have unknowingly let someone see your private affairs and will suffer from the negligence. Labeling things indicates that you are trying to make sense of something in your daily life. A mislabeled item means you are not looking at things the right way. Searching for a label shows that you are working to make sense of a situation.
