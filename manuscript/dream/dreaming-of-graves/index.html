⚰️Dreaming of Graves

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of graves is unfortunate. Ill luck in business transactions will follow, and sickness is threatened. Seeing a newly dug grave means you will have to suffer for the wrongdoings of others. Visiting a new grave reveals that danger of a serious nature is hanging over you. Walking on graves predicts an early death or unfortunate marriage. Looking into an empty grave denotes disappointment and loss of friends. Seeing a live person in a grave covered by earth except for the head means some distressing situation will take hold of that person, and you yourself may experience a loss of property. Seeing your own grave suggests that enemies are warily seeking to engulf you in disaster; if you are not watchful, they will succeed. Digging a grave speaks of uneasiness over some venture, as enemies will seek to thwart you, but if you finish the grave you will overcome opposition. If the sun is shining, good will come out of seeming embarrassments. Returning to bury a corpse that has disappeared means trouble will come to you from obscure quarters. A graveyard barren except for the tops of the graves signifies a season of much sorrow and despondency. Still, greater benefits and pleasure await you if you properly shoulder your burden. Seeing your own corpse in a grave foreshadows hopelessness and oppression.
