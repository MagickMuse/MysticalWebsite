🦽Dreaming of Immobility and Paralysis

Correspondences6
😟Anxiety🎮Control💬Expression🧍🏻Independence💕Love👑Power
Interpretations
If you are immobile in a dream, this reflects an inability to influence events in life. If you are abandoned in a wheelchair in a dream, you may feel life is passing you by and no one is listening to you. If you are being pushed in a wheelchair by a partner, relative, or workmate who is taking you where you do not want to go, you may need to assert yourself. If you dream your hands and arms are paralyzed, you may be finding it hard to reach out to others and express love. If you dream your legs are paralyzed, you may be stuck in a situation that right now you cannot leave. If you dream you are being pursued and attacked, you are suffering from buried anxieties that you should confront. Dreams of sleep paralysis, where the sleeper cannot move or speak immediately before going to sleep or waking, while not uncommon, can escalate into a full-blown nightmare if the dreamer is worried about being helpless. Immobility dreams in any context represent fears of being dependent on others, which need to be faced in order to maximize the dreamer's strengths in any situation. Paralysis in a dream can be indicative of your emotional state. Perhaps you are feeling paralyzed about what to do in a situation. It can also be an indication of the sleep state. REM sleep often renders the person unable to move, as if paralyzed.
