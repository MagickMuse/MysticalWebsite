Dream Grimoire

🏝️Dreaming of Islands

◇Interpretations
If in your dream you are happy on a desert island or with someone special, it may be time to take more leisure or private space for you and your partner. If you dream the island is overgrown and you are stranded, you may feel life is closing in on you. If you dream you are on vacation on a tropical or well-loved island, an unexpected vacation or adventure is soon coming your way. If the island is being engulfed by the sea, your privacy or personal space is being gradually encroached upon. If you are happy to be leaving an island, you are ready to widen your horizons. In dreams, islands can be sanctuaries, but they can become prisons if they isolate the dreamer from life and from other people. An island, however luxurious, must offer not only a refuge from hectic life but also suitable company to share the tranquility. Being on an island in a clear stream foretells pleasant journeys and fortunate enterprises. A barren island warns of forfeiture of happiness and money through intemperance. Simply seeing an island signals comfort and easy circumstances after much striving and worrying to meet obligations honorably. Seeing people on an island predicts a struggle to raise yourself higher in prominent circles.
