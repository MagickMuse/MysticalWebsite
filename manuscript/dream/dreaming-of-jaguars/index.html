🐆Dreaming of Jaguars

Animal Source1
Jaguars
Correspondences5
🦁Courage🔮Intuition❤️‍🔥Passion👑Power🧠Wisdom
Interpretations
If you dream you are flying through the sky with your jaguar, you need to take what seems a risky step, which will pay off. If you are running from a jaguar, you may be ignoring powerful emotions and sexual passion toward someone who has recently come into your life. If you see a jaguar alone in the forest, rely on your own wisdom and do not seek the approval of or emulate others. If you see your dream jaguar climbing a high rock, believe in your ability to rise high in your chosen field. If you dream that a jaguar attacks, you may be defeating yourself by not confronting fears and injustices. If you are riding a jaguar in a dream, you have a great deal more power than you realize or are told. Many jaguar dreams link with past-world memories of the Mayans and Aztecs, acting as a reminder that personal intuition in any spiritual art is more important than ego. Dreaming of this big cat indicates the agility and flexibility to handle a physical task you were not sure you could complete.
