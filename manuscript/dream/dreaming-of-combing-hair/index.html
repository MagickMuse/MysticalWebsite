💫Dreaming of Combing Hair

Notes
*See also: Dreaming of Hair
Correspondences4
💀Death👫Friendship🤒Illness➖Loss
Interpretations
Combing hair in a dream is an omen of illness or death touching a friend or relative. Damage to friendship and loss of property are also foretold.

To dream of combing hair suggests the illness or death of a friend or relative. The careful, intimate act of tending to the hair becomes in the dream world an omen of grief and loss. Damage to friendship and loss of property are also indicated alongside these heavier tidings.
