🕊️Dreaming of Pigeons

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of sending a pigeon with a message indicates you can trust a friend or family member to act as a messenger or arbitrator in a family dispute, especially with someone estranged. Dreaming of a pigeon flying toward you with a message strapped to its leg indicates unexpected communication from afar or the revival of an old friendship or love. Seeing a pigeon shot in a dream warns against acting as a go-between with warring factions or relatives. Pigeons flying in a flock and returning to their nest say you or a loved one will travel far but always return home. A carrier pigeon that gets lost or injured says there may be a misunderstanding via social media. If possible, communicate face to face. Pigeons nesting foretell happy family days and vacations and are an excellent omen if you are creating your first home or planning a family. Seeing pigeons and hearing them cooing above their cotes speaks of how proud you are of your children. Seeing them flying signals freedom from misunderstanding, and perhaps news from those far away.
