🩸Dreaming of Ulcers

◇Interpretations
Mouth ulcers in a dream represent angry words you are not speaking. Stomach ulcers signify a long-standing grievance or injustice, gnawing away at you, which needs resolving. Neck ulcers suggest you are maintaining a neutral position in a matter in which you rightly should intervene. Leg or foot ulcers suggest a fear of moving forward in a career or socially because envy by others is eating away your confidence. If in your dream you are sick with an internal ulcer, stress overload is eroding other parts of your life. If someone you know in the dream has an ulcer, but not in the real world, they may be jealous of you. Seeing an ulcer in a dream signifies loss of friends and distance from loved ones. Affairs will remain unsatisfactory. Having ulcers means you will become unpopular with your friends by giving yourself up to foolish pleasures.
