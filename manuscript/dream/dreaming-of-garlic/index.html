🧄Dreaming of Garlic

◇Interpretations
If you dream of garlic cloves hanging in your kitchen, you will become or remain financially secure, and good luck and good health will enter or reenter the home. If you dream you are eating garlic and you are worried that your breath smells, you may need to conceal future plans from someone disapproving. If you are wearing a garlic clove around your neck, you may be under attack but being blamed or disbelieved. Persist, for you will be vindicated. Wild flowering garlic represents protection, in the form of unexpected help or intervention against evil. If you see a clove of garlic on a window ledge, especially west-facing, one of your ancestors has a message for you that will appear as a sign in your daily life. If you are cooking with garlic, this is a powerful fertility symbol and also a sign of a good life together if you are preparing a meal for a lover. Garlic dreams may occur when a dreamer feels helpless and needs protection. To dream of passing through a garlic patch denotes a rise from poverty to prominence and wealth. To eat garlic in your dreams implies that you take a sensible view of life and leave its ideals to take care of themselves. To dream of a wreath of garlic placed at your own throat signals that protection is being arranged on your behalf by someone wiser than you, possibly without full explanation. Do not refuse it. To dream of rubbing garlic across the sashes of your bedroom windows or the jamb of your door foretells the sealing of a household against an intruder, supernatural or otherwise. Pay attention to who enters and leaves your life in the coming days. To dream of a stranger fixing a wreath of garlic around the neck of someone you love is a warning that the loved one is more vulnerable than they appear. Quiet vigilance and a willingness to be inconvenienced are called for. To dream of refusing or throwing down a bundle of garlic flowers means rejecting help that you genuinely need. Reconsider what you have been brushing off. To dream of wild garlic flowering on a moist meadow bank promises unexpected protection from an old source you had nearly forgotten.
