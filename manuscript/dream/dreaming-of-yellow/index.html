Symbol Source1
🟡Yellow
Interpretations
Yellow embodies cognition, intelligence, and eagerness to acquire knowledge. This energy requires careful direction, as there's an inclination to initiate numerous projects while completing only a handful. It frequently signals a dilemma requiring substantial contemplation to solve. A tarnished yellow reveals dishonesty and deception. If yellow is prominent in a dream, it signifies new beginnings, happiness, and confidence.
