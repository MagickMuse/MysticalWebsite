🍞Dreaming of Bread

Correspondences10
👨‍👨‍👧‍👧Family💵Fortune🤲Generosity🏠Home💰Money😵Overwhelm⏳Past Lives💰Prosperity🏆Reward🤝Support
Material Source1
🍞Bread
Plant Source1
Wheat
Interpretations
Baking bread speaks of creating prosperity by your own efforts, either alone or with your family's help. Buying bread from a bakery or supermarket indicates you will enjoy brief prosperity through the efforts of others. Sharing bread with others signals you will be rewarded for generosity, possibly by a stranger. Freshly baked bread promises unexpected financial gain. Stale bread warns not to allow others to take advantage of your easygoing nature or misuse your resources. Breaking bread with others represents reconciliation after a quarrel or a period of estrangement. Seeing bread in a dream generally signals that things are going well across both your personal and professional life. Sharing a meal of bread with others points to a sense of established security and capability in how you navigate your daily circumstances. See also: Dreaming of Baking, Dreaming of Crust. Loaves of bread in a dream symbolize frugality. Broken loaves bring discontent and bickering among lovers. Loaves that multiply phenomenally prognosticate great success, and lovers will be happy with their chosen partners.
