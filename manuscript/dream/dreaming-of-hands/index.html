👋Dreaming of Hands

◇Interpretations
If a dream hand is outstretched in friendship, it indicates a positive connection, perhaps from someone previously considered indifferent or hostile. A closed hand warns of meanness from a close friend or family member. Seeing praying hands covering yours is a spiritual dream revealing that your guardian angel or spirit guide is present. A dream of hands being bound or handcuffed indicates a sense of being restricted, unless it is a sexual fantasy dream. A fist, especially waved in the face, can reveal escalating anger that needs to be deflected. Holding hands with someone you desire as a lover or within a relationship following coldness or estrangement is a good omen of coming together physically and emotionally. Hands are very significant in their dream meaning, from the acknowledging wave of royalty to a subject, to a comforting hug. The dream content will reveal the nature and sincerity of the hand contact. Undisguised body language in dreams is clear, and hand movements in particular reveal true intentions when words deceive. Beautiful hands in a dream point to great distinction and rapid rise in your calling, while ugly or malformed hands warn of disappointments and poverty. Blood on the hands signals estrangement and unjust censure from members of your family. An injured hand means another person will succeed in what you are striving most to obtain. A detached hand indicates a solitary life, where people will fail to understand your views and feelings. Burning your hands warns that you will overreach the bounds of reason in your struggle for wealth and fame, and you will lose. Hands enlarged in the dream imply quick advancement, while hands appearing smaller predict the reverse. Soiled hands indicate that you will be envious and unjust, while washing your hands foretells participation in some joyous festivity. Hands tied together signal that you will be involved in difficulties. Loosening them shows that you will force others to submit to your dictates.
