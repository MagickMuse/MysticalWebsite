⚠️Dreaming of Risks

◇Interpretations
If you are a natural risk-taker and the dream highlights potential disasters, it's time to modify the risks. If in the dream you are taking risks to please someone else, consider whether the person has your interests and safety at heart. If your life is routine and you dream of dangerous activities, explore how your lifestyle can safely become more challenging. If you are deliberately risking a relationship in a dream because you are angry or bored with a lover, giving way to temptation may not be the best way to resolve suppressed resentment. If innocent people are involved in your risk-taking in a dream, are you seeking revenge against them or have you not thought through the consequences to others?
