🦷Dreaming of Teeth

◇Interpretations
If teeth fall out in a dream or are missing, they reflect fears of waning power and physical admiration, or their actual waning, often because of prejudice about aging, professionally, socially, or in a relationship where physical appearance is prized above all. False teeth represent someone lying. The more ill-fitting the teeth, the greater the falsehood. Dreaming you are unable to bite through food because of loose teeth suggests you are afraid to speak out. A dream of someone you know smiling slyly, hiding their teeth, or snarling means their apparently benign intentions may be hiding malice. If you are surrounded by smiling people and you are smiling back, your popularity and approval rating will increase for who you are and what you do and not for your physical appearance. A dream of a dentist hurting you or refusing anesthetic warns of someone in authority intruding on your personal space with inappropriate sexual or malicious remarks or behavior. Teeth falling out can forewarn of actual problems with teeth and gums, especially if checkups have been missed. An ordinary teeth dream augurs an unpleasant contact with sickness or disquieting people. Loose teeth predict failures and gloomy tidings. A doctor pulling your tooth means you will endure a desperate illness, and if not fatal, it will be lingering. Cavities filled forecast recovery of lost valuables after much uneasiness. Cleaning or brushing your teeth foretells that some great struggle will be demanded of you if you hope to preserve your fortune. Having a set of false teeth made predicts that severe burdens will fall upon you, and you will strive to throw them aside. Losing your teeth means burdens that crush your pride and demolish your affairs. Having your teeth knocked out denotes sudden misfortune, with business suffering or deaths or accidents coming close. Examining your teeth warns you to be careful of your affairs, as enemies are lurking near. Decayed teeth mean your business or health will suffer from intense strain. Spitting out teeth portends sickness, your own or in your immediate family. One tooth falling out foretells disagreeable news, two means unhappy states plunged into through no carelessness on your part. Admiring your teeth for their whiteness and beauty suggests that you will get your wishes and live happily ever after.
