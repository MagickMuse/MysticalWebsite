⚙️Dreaming of Coffee Grinders

◇Interpretations
The coffee grinder in dreams signals danger and hidden enemies depending on whether you see or hear it. To see a coffee grinder tells you that you are approaching serious danger; you need all your energy and alertness to avert possibly disastrous consequences. The grinding mechanism warns of a threat drawing close; full attention is required. To hear it grinding means it will be hard for you to recognize someone who doesn't have your best interests at heart. The sound without the sight signals that the danger is concealed; the enemy or the threat is harder to identify than you might expect.
