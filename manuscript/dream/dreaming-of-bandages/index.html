🩹Dreaming of Bandages

Correspondences6
🔗Connection❤️‍🩹Healing🍀Luck⌚Patience🤝Support🔬Surgical Healing
Material Source1
🩹Bandages
Interpretations
Applying a bandage to a romantic partner, family member, friend, or coworker suggests you will be asked to resolve a conflict or disagreement. Receiving a bandage indicates you may be settling for circumstances that don't truly satisfy you. Blood or pus leaking through the bandage signifies an issue that runs too deep for a simple solution and will require time and patience to resolve. Working as a medical professional bandaging an injury means you might be tasked with solving others' difficulties, but remember to seek assistance when you need it yourself. Lacking a bandage to treat your own injury or burn serves as a reminder to prioritize your own wellbeing alongside caring for others. A bandage is also a positive symbol of healing and good fortune on the way. Wearing one yourself suggests a current difficulty will find a straightforward resolution, while seeing a bandage on someone else indicates you will be the one helping another person work through their own challenge.
