🪥Dreaming of Brushes

Correspondences3
📞Communication⚠️Danger💰Prosperity
Material Source1
💇Hairbrush
Interpretations
Using a hairbrush in a dream warns that poor decision-making and mismanagement will lead to misfortune. Old hairbrushes specifically point to illness and declining health. A clothes brush signals a weighty task on the horizon, though actively brushing your clothes suggests payment or recognition for demanding work is forthcoming. Seeing an assortment of various brushes foretells a diverse and rewarding range of professional activities.
