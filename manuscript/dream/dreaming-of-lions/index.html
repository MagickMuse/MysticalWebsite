🦁Dreaming of Lions

Animal Source1
Lions
Correspondences8
👑Authority💪Confidence🦁Courage🎯Focus⚖️Justice👑Leadership👑Power🛡️Protection
Interpretations
Lions appear in dreams at times when we must focus single-mindedly on a goal. If you dream of a lion, you may be asked to take the lead in the workplace or community, in issues involving ethics or justice. Dreaming of being attacked by lions indicates powerful opposition. Rescuing a lion among them represents your own strength to triumph. A benign protective lion signifies a powerful ally entering your life and work. If a lion appears in your garden, old fears or former enemies may reemerge but, if tackled, will fade away. A lion is a sign of growing authority, confidence, and a successful bid for the top. A lion in a dream signals that a great force is driving you. Subduing the beast means you will be victorious in any engagement; being overpowered by it means you will be open to the successful attacks of enemies. Caged lions point to success that depends on your ability to cope with opposition. Watching someone control a lion in or out of its cage signals great mental power and success in business. Young lions hint at new enterprises that will bring success if properly attended. The roar of a lion signals unexpected advancement. A lion looming over you and snarling with bared teeth threatens defeat in your rise to power. A lion's skin signals a rise to fortune and happiness. Riding a lion speaks of courage and persistence in surmounting difficulties.
