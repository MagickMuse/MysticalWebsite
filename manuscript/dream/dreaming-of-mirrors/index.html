Dream Grimoire

🪞Dreaming of Mirrors

◇Interpretations
Being happy with your image in a dream mirror confirms your high self-esteem and confidence. If you are brushing your hair and gazing in a dream mirror, you may see your present or future love, known or unknown, standing behind you. If the mirror is dark, you will be aware of an ancestor or spirit guide looking over your shoulder in the dream, protecting you. To dream you are lost in a maze of mirrors indicates confusion about future plans and direction. Ignore conflicting messages. A broken mirror or breaking a mirror does not foretell bad luck; rather, it expresses fears that your real self has disappeared through the demands of others. Dreaming you are asked to lend your hand mirror warns that someone wants to steal your ideas or tempt your partner away. A looking glass in the dream of a woman means she will soon be confronted by shocking deceitfulness and discrepancies, which may result in tragic scenes or separations. Seeing yourself in a mirror in a dream indicates many discouraging issues ahead, with sickness causing distress and loss of fortune; it may also foretell unfaithfulness and neglect in marriage. Spotting another face beside your own in the mirror means you are leading a double life and will deceive your friends. Seeing others rather than yourself reflected suggests they will act unfairly to promote their own interests, while seeing animals reflected hints at disappointment and loss of fortune. Breaking a mirror portends an early and accidental death; seeing one already broken foretells the sudden or violent death of a relative.
