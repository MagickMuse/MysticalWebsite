🔮Dreaming of Snow Globes

Interpretations
Dreaming of seeing a scene within a snow globe indicates thoughts that have not yet emerged and the stored energy to fulfill desires in that area. If you drop a snow globe in the dream and it breaks, your illusions may be shattered. If you dream you are a child holding a snow globe, the globe reveals the area of your life where you seek to revive the past or a former connection. If you dream you are trapped in a snow globe, you are denying or not expressing your emotions and may feel restricted by pretending to be happy about a matter. If you dream you offer a snow globe as a gift, you are opening your vulnerable self to the person receiving it. If you dream you are given a snow globe, you can trust the donor with your heart and emotions.
