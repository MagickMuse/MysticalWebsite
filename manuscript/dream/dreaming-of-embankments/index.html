🛣️Dreaming of Embankments

◇Interpretations
Driving along an embankment in your dream suggests threats of trouble and unhappiness; if you continue the drive without incident, you will turn those forebodings to your advantage in social or employment matters. Riding on horseback along an embankment means you will fearlessly meet and overcome every obstacle on your way to wealth and happiness. Walking along one points to a weary struggle to elevate your status, with reward at the end.
