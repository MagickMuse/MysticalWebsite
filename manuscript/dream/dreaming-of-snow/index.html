❄️Dreaming of Snow

◇Interpretations
Dreaming of driving or walking through deep snow or being caught in a blizzard advises postponing a planned journey, life change, or decision until matters are clearer. Dreaming of being buried in a snowdrift reflects being snowed under with demands that have piled up and may need time to clear. A dream of playing in the snow or making a snowman is a sign to devote more time to leisure and pleasure and rediscover your spontaneous inner child. A dream of being hurt by snowballs suggests that there is petty childish spite against you or that you are being frozen out by envy and rivalry. A dream of melting snow brings the thawing of a relationship or personal isolation. Dreaming of watching snow fall from inside the home says, "Do not fight against restrictions but enjoy the breathing space."

❄️Snow

---

Snow in a dream signifies your inhibitions and repressed emotions, which need to be expressed. You also may be feeling neglected. A snowstorm denotes sorrow and disappointment at failing to enjoy some long-expected pleasure. Eating snow means you will fail to realize your ideals. Dirty snow foretells that your pride will be humbled. Seeing it melt means your fears will turn into joy. Large white snowflakes falling indicate a fresh start. Snowcapped mountains in the distance warn that your longings and ambitions will bring no worthy advancement. The sun shining through landscapes of snow foretells that you will conquer adverse fortune and be possessed of power. Snowballing implies that you will struggle with dishonorable issues, and if your judgment is not well grounded, you will suffer defeat. Being snowbound or lost means there will be a wave of ill luck breaking upon you.
