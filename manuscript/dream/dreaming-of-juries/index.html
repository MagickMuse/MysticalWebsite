⚖️Dreaming of Juries

◇Interpretations
If you dream you are cleared of a charge by a jury, your good reputation will be restored or vindicated. If a group of people is listening to a case against you, you may be seeking to gain recognition for your beliefs or point of view and win others over. If you are being misrepresented to the jury, you may be unfairly judged because your worldview is at odds with the status quo. If you are a member of a jury, you may feel pressured to go along with popular opinion. If a dream jury is made up of people who are biased against you, you are wasting your time arguing your cause. If you dream you are trying to avoid jury service, you may be unwilling to express an opinion on a subject but are being pressured to do so. Jury dreams are concerned with collective decisions and opinions where the dreamer is defending what they believe is right. Juries do not always mean justice, though they should. Where the majority view is weighted against the dreamer, the dream can warn of this and allow a new approach or support to get a fairer hearing. Serving on a jury signals dissatisfaction with your life and a wish to make material changes to your situation. It can also mean you are swayed by what others think of you rather than trusting your own sense of self. Being cleared of a charge by a jury promises that your business will succeed and your affairs will move your way; being condemned warns that bad influences will overpower and harass you.
