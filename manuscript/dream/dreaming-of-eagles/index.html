🦅Dreaming of Eagles

Animal Source1
Eagles
Correspondences11
🗺️Adventure🎯Ambition🦁Courage⭐Fame🕊️Freedom💰Inheritance🧠Knowledge🧬Legacy🏆Success🏆Victory💵Wealth
Interpretations
Dreaming of an eagle flying high says your ambitions will be realized and travel especially favored. Flying on the back of an eagle is a sign of emerging shamanic abilities, especially connected with the Americas, where the eagle represents the Great Spirit. The eagle represents the ability to reach out for major ventures and adventures. Being carried off against your will by an eagle signifies official or family attempts to restrict freedom. The appearance of an eagle indicates a legal matter against corrupt authority settled in your favor. A nesting eagle represents the need to withdraw from daily noise and chaos. An eagle soaring above you in your dream denotes lofty ambitions that you will struggle fiercely to realize; whatever the struggle, you will gain your desires. An eagle perched on distant heights suggests that you will possess fame, wealth, and the highest position attainable in your country. Young eagles in their aerie point to your association with people of high standing, whose wise counsel will benefit you, and a rich legacy will come to you in time. Killing an eagle in your dream means no obstacle will be allowed to stand between you and the utmost heights of your ambition; you will overcome your enemies and possess untold wealth. Eating the flesh of an eagle signals a powerful will that does not turn aside in ambitious struggles, even for death, with valuable possessions coming your way. A dead eagle killed by others signifies that high rank and fortune will be wrested from you ruthlessly. Riding on an eagle's back means you will make a long voyage into almost unexplored countries in your search for knowledge and wealth, both of which you will eventually gain.
