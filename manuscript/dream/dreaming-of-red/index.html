🔴Dreaming of Red

◇Interpretations
Red symbolizes strength. It conveys vitality, fervor, determination, aspiration, and honor. Typically, red carries positive meaning, though a tainted red reveals fury and hostility. Dreaming of the color red, or any article that stands out because it is red, indicates great passion and sensitivity in your emotional relationships.
