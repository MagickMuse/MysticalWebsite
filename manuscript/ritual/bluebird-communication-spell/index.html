🌕Moon Oil

A moon-blessed spell to open the pathways of communication and call messages, recognition, or word from someone close. A feather, echoing Mercury's winged sandals, is used to trace a communication device three times while a spoken charm draws messages forward; a bell seals the blessing. Works under the full or waxing moon and is especially potent when the moon is in an air sign. Can also be used to bless a device for clarity during Mercury retrograde.

Quantities

  • Tissue
  • Moon Oil (optional)
  • Communication device to bless
  • Feather, preferably blue or gray
  • Bell

Directions

  1. Stand before the device you are blessing.
  2. If using Moon Oil, place a small amount on the tissue.
  3. Using the tissue, lightly anoint the edges or protective case of the device in a clockwise direction, visualizing the moon growing toward full, luminous, magnetic, drawing messages to you.
  4. Set the tissue aside and pick up the feather.
  5. Hold it and bring to mind what you are calling toward you: a message, a reply, recognition, word from someone you love.
  6. Visualize the full moon glowing above you, its light streaming down and opening every channel of communication.
  7. Use the feather to slowly trace around the device three times.
  8. As you do, say aloud:
  9. "Birds of a feather, flock together.
    Like the bluebird sings, to me does ring."
  10. Fill in the blank with the name of the person or the message you are calling in. Repeat the chant once, three times, or until you feel the energy settle and ring through the air like a struck bell.
  11. Lay the feather down gently.
  12. Pick up the bell and ring it around the device in a slow circle.
  13. As the bell sounds, hold a clear image of the device lighting up with a message; see it, hear it, feel the spark of recognition it brings.
  14. Leave the feather near the device as an ongoing charm, or carry it with you as a talisman for clear communication.
