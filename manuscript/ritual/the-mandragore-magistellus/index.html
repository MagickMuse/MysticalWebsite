🌱The Mandragore Magistellus

◇Quantities
- 1 mandrake or white bryony root (freshly uprooted, carved to represent the sex opposite to the practitioner) - Distilled water mixed with blood or milk at a ratio of approximately 12:1, for watering - Vervain (Verbena officinalis) leaves or lemon-scented garden verbena for curing smoke - Athame - Thurible
◇Directions
1. On a waxing moon day, preferably just after the new moon and between the winter solstice and the vernal equinox, search a hedgerow or patch of wasteland for your mandrake. That night, unseen, draw a deosil circle around the plant with your Athame. Using the blade as a digging implement, loosen the earth around the root. Without injuring the main section, gently draw the plant from the soil. A traditional groaning may be heard as it leaves the earth. 2. Trim the foliage off the main stem, leaving a small piece of root adhering to it. Replant this foliage where the original plant was taken from. 3. Take the main root home and, using the Athame, carve the features of a doll of the sex opposite your own into the root. While carving, repeat continuously: Guard this home in Hertha's name. The carving accentuates the salient natural features of the root. 4. Replant the carved root in a churchyard or at a crossroads for maximum potency. Failing these, any convenient patch of soil will serve. Draw a deosil circle in the soil before replanting. 5. For the next full lunar month (28 days), water the root regularly with distilled water mixed with blood or milk in a ratio of approximately 12:1. Water with intention each time. 6. As midnight approaches on the 28th day, draw your circle around the burial spot and dig up the root again with your Athame. If the working has taken hold, the carving scars will have healed over with new bark and the root will appear to have naturally grown into the shape of a mannikin. 7. Clean the root thoroughly. Dry it in a heated oven with vervain smoke, or pass it daily through the thurible smoke of burning vervain for approximately three months until fully dried. Lemon-scented garden verbena may substitute for vervain. 8. When fully cured, enshrine the magistellus as close to the hearth as possible, or in the room used most. This completes the spell and initiates its protective activity.
◇Description
A plant-based elemental magistellus fashioned from a mandrake or white bryony root carved into a human figure representing the sex opposite to the practitioner. The completed root is watered over a full lunar month, then dried and enshrined near the hearth to serve as a protective guardian of the home.
