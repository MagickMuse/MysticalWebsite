🟢Green

A Bastet-dedicated luck spell using a green cat candle dressed with Horn of Plenty Oil and a scarab set beside it during the burn, then carried as a charged luck charm.

Quantities

Cat candle or green cat candle
Horn of Plenty Oil
Scarab

Directions

  1. Dress a cat-shaped candle, or a green cat candle as green is Bastet's sacred color, with Horn of Plenty Oil.
  2. Set a scarab beside the candle as it burns.
  3. Hold intentions, wishes, and petitions clearly in focus throughout the working.
  4. Once the candle is fully spent, carry the scarab as a charged luck charm.
