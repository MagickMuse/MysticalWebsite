🌊Melusine Weekly Bath Transformation

The most famous mermaid-women in folk-tale tradition is Melusine, who required a weekly private day to let her fish or water-snake tail emerge. Once a week, lock the door and forbid anyone from disturbing you. "Immersing oneself in water" can also be understood metaphorically to mean immersing oneself in magick, spirituality, or women's primal power.

Quantities

Fragrant bath ingredients of your choosing

Directions

  1. Once a week, lock the door and forbid anyone from disturbing you.
  2. This is your day to be whoever you wish to be, to shed the masks you must sometimes wear.
  3. Run a fragrant, magickal bath for yourself.
  4. Spend all day in it if you like.
