🌸Pink Moon Blush

A carved oak wood protection charm made during a moonlit walk through the trees, invoking Diana, ancient Italian goddess of the wild, witchcraft, and protection. Carve a crescent moon into the wood, speak the petition, leave a natural offering, and charge under the full moon. Carry the charm for ongoing protection and guidance. Retire it by burning as an offering to Diana when it's time to replace it.

Quantities

  • Small piece of wood from a local tree or oak
  • Knife
  • Natural offering such as Eggshell Blessing Powder, seeds, or other biodegradable materials
  • Optional: Pink Moon Blush powder mixed with water (to paint a sigil or moon on the wood)

Directions

  1. If possible, collect your piece of oak or local wood during a moonlit walk through the trees.
  2. Let the forest be your ritual space; the night, the full or crescent moon filtering through leaves, the scent of earth.
  3. Diana is most at home here.
  4. If this isn't possible, any piece of wood from a forest will do, and you can bring the woods to mind through visualization.
  5. Close your eyes.
  6. Breathe deeply and reach for Diana's energy, ancient, rousing, fiercely protective.
  7. She is the goddess of witches, of wild creatures, of those who move through darkness unafraid.
  8. When you feel connected, take your knife and carve a crescent moon into the wood.
  9. As you carve, pour your intention into every stroke.
  10. Then lay the wood on the ground and speak aloud:
  11. "Diana, goddess of the witches,
    Of the wild woodlands and the wolves,
    All that's aglow under the moon when full,
    Empower and strengthen me with your pull,
    As you guide and protect all under your rule."
  12. Place a natural offering before you; something that will return to the earth without harming wildlife.
  13. Eggshell Blessing Powder, seeds, dried flowers, or grain are all fitting.
  14. Decorate the wood charm as you wish.
  15. To paint a sigil or moon symbol directly onto the wood, mix a small amount of Pink Moon Blush powder with water to create a natural paint, then mark the wood with your chosen symbol.
  16. Leave the charm under the full moon overnight to charge.
  17. Carry it with you whenever you need protection or her guidance.
  18. Hold it in moments of uncertainty or danger and feel her presence.
  19. For a sachet, place oak shavings in a small sachet bag with a wolf charm, moon charm, or both, as a wearable or pocket talisman.
  20. When the charm has served its purpose and it's time to replace it, set it aflame in a fireproof container as a final offering and expression of gratitude to Diana.
