🧹Wild Sister Silver Broomstick Samhain Tea

A Samhain tea ritual for spirit communication and psychic opening. Mugwort leaves and a cinnamon stick are bundled together into a small bouquet, secured with twine, and steeped in boiling water poured over a tiny silver broom in a teacup. Mugwort and cinnamon carry maiden and crone aspects of the goddess; the practitioner acts as the mother, uniting both polarities to thin the veil and invite ancestral contact.

Quantities

Handful of fresh mugwort leaves
Kitchen scissors
Natural cotton cooking twine (1 foot per bouquet)
1 cinnamon stick per participant
1 small silver broom per teacup
Kettle, teacups, and saucers

Directions

  1. Trim mugwort leaves from the stalk with kitchen scissors, leaving a small stem. Lay silver-side up and select about eight leaves of similar length.
  2. Cut 1 foot of twine. Position a cinnamon stick in the center of the bundle with roughly 1/2 inch covered by the leaf stems. Find the midpoint of the twine and wrap it three times around the mugwort. Tie a tight half knot, flip the bouquet, and tie another on the opposite side. Continue alternating half knots up the stick until all leaf stems are covered.
  3. Tie a final tight knot and finish with a bow. Trim excess twine. Bring the kettle to a full boil. Place the silver broom in a teacup and pour boiling water over it. Steep until the water cools.
  4. Clear the mind and allow the third eye to open. Sip the tea and invite the spirit to reach beyond ordinary perception toward those who have passed. Receive any messages or insights as confirmation of the ritual's work.
  5. Remember that mugwort holds maiden energy and cinnamon holds crone energy. As the one who prepares and shares the tea, the practitioner embodies the mother aspect, completing the threefold goddess and drawing the veil thin.
