🌎Tellurian Incense

A vestige of sacrificial earth ritual in which an egg is sealed in a consecrated black box and buried in or near the home under Hertha's protection. The caster circumambulates the property three times at midnight on the new moon, visualizing a circle of witch power, then buries the egg permanently at the threshold, hearth, attic, or cellar.

Directions

  1. On the day of the new moon, purchase a fresh hen's egg. A fertile egg is best.
  2. At midnight, kindle the Tellurian Incense in the thurible and place salt and water in the chalice, clearly pronouncing these actions in Hertha's name.
  3. Take the athame in the right hand and the egg in the left. Walk three times deosil (clockwise) around the entire outer perimeter of the home, concentrating on Hertha's image. Invoke her by chanting her name or using the full invocation below. With each step, visualize blue glowing footprints of witch power left on the ground, forming a giant circle of protective light around the property.
  4. After completing the circumambulations, seal the egg into a prepared and exorcised black box cushioned within by dark fabric, preferably a piece of discarded clothing.
  5. Choose a burial location: as close to the threshold as possible, under the hearth, walled up in the attic, or buried in the cellar.
  6. Exorcise the chosen spot with earth incense and salt and water.
  7. Place the box into the space, calling upon Hertha three times to protect the house and bless all within it with harmony and good fortune.
  8. Spit thrice into the hole.
  9. Fill in or seal the space permanently.
    Invocation of Hertha:
    Hertha, Great One, Mother of all life, who gives birth to all and renews her Lord the Sun each day who bestows himself on all men equally; Guardian of sky and sea, all powers and potencies, through your might alone all nature falls silent then sinks into sleep. You bring back the light to dispel the darkness only once more to cover us most safely with your shadows. You in whose hand rests everlasting chaos, even wind, rain, and storm, at whose word oceans roar; who chases away the light and stirs up the tempest and at whose whim sends forth joyous day again; Ever faithful Sustainer of all life, when our souls depart, they fly to your keeping thence to return yet again. Rightly you are called Great Mother of All, for you conquer by your name alone. Source of strength for all men and gods without you naught is born nor perfected; I call upon you, ruler of creation; I call upon you as divine, I call upon your holy name, Hertha, be pleased to grant that which I ask, protect this house and home and all who live therein, so shall I always thank you with due faith.
