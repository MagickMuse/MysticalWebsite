💤Moon Potion

◇Form
Water/Spray
◇Method
Dreamwork
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• Spring water (a bottle) • Moonlight (Full Moon)
◇Directions
1. On a Full Moon night, place a sealed bottle of spring water outdoors in direct moonlight to charge overnight. 2. Refrigerate the water. 3. Before going to sleep, pour a small glass. 4. Take a few sips and leave the rest beside the bed. 5. Reserve a small amount to drink just before recording your dreams in the morning.
◇Description
A lunar-charged water preparation that aids not only dream recall but also understanding and clarification of dream content. The water is prepared at the Full Moon and kept refrigerated for use before sleep. One of the Dream Recall methods.
