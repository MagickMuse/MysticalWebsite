📿High John Root Charging Box

High John the Conqueror root is kept in a small box lined with bay leaves and filled with calendula blossoms, absorbing their legal victory power between court appearances. A Hoodoo carry talisman.

⚠️ Closed Practice Warning: This spell draws directly from Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please engage with respect, proper education, or seek guidance from a practitioner within the tradition.

Quantities

  • 1 small box
  • Bay leaves (enough to line the box)
  • Calendula blossoms (enough to fill the box)
  • 1 High John the Conqueror root

Directions

  1. Line a small box with bay leaves and fill it with calendula blossoms.
  2. Nestle one High John the Conqueror root inside.
  3. Remove the root and carry it with you as a court case talisman when needed.
  4. When not in use, return it to rest on its bed of calendula, absorbing the herb's powers of legal victory.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: High John the Conqueror root workings are central to Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

This working creates a living talisman — a High John root that continuously charges between uses, resting on a bed of botanicals associated with legal victory.

  1. Line a small box with bay leaves.
  2. Fill the box with calendula blossoms — these serve as both a fragrant bed and a source of continuous legal victory energy for the root.
  3. Nestle one High John the Conqueror root among the calendula.
  4. When you need to attend court or face a legal matter, remove the root and carry it with you as a talisman.
  5. When your court date has passed, return the root to its magickal box to rest and recharge, absorbing calendula's powers of legal victory until it is needed again.
High John works best when kept in motion — taken out for court, returned to rest between appearances. The box is not just storage; it is an ongoing working.
