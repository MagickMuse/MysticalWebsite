◇Directions
1. Soak cotton balls thoroughly in Follow Me Boy! 2. Oil. 3. Slip the oil-soaked cotton balls into your clothing, pockets, inner layers, or wherever they will stay concealed. 4. The oil works as a continuous wearable charm attracting goodwill and deflecting legal trouble.
◇Description
Soak cotton balls in Follow Me Boy! Oil and carry them in your clothing to attract favorable attention and avoid trouble with the law. A subtle, wearable Hoodoo protective working. ⚠️ This is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick traditions. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.

Closed Practice Notice: This spell draws on Hoodoo commanding oil traditions. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

This is a simple, wearable preventative working — best suited to those who want to stay off the law's radar before any legal matter arises, or who wish to move through the world with an air of attraction and protection.

Soak cotton balls generously in Follow Me Boy! Oil. Slip them discreetly into your clothing — tucked into a pocket, sewn into a lining, or nestled inside a layer close to the skin. The scent and energy of the oil work continuously throughout the day, drawing favorable attention and keeping legal entanglements at a distance.
