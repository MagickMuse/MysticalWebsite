⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A ten-step death spell using a poppet, graveyard dirt, and burial. The practitioner constructs an effigy filled with hatred and focused intent, buries it in a box with graveyard dirt, and inter it at a cemetery. Best performed during a waning moon in Scorpio or Capricorn. Once complete, the caster must release all thought of the target to avoid interference.

Quantities

1 poppet or effigy
Graveyard dirt
1 small box (cardboard is fine)
1 black candle
Personal article of the target (optional)

Directions

  1. Construct an effigy or poppet of the target.
  2. If a personal article of the target is available, incorporate it.
  3. During construction, pour all hatred and intent into the poppet while visualizing the target.
  4. This is best performed during a waning moon, especially when the moon is in Scorpio or Capricorn.
  5. Construct a small box out of cardboard or similar material.
  6. Light a black candle and concentrate intensely upon the death and destruction of the target.
  7. Do not allow yourself to be disturbed or lose focus.
  8. Place the effigy into the box and bury it in graveyard dirt inside the box.
  9. Reserve some of the dirt.
  10. Take the box outside or back to the graveyard and bury it, placing the reserved dirt on top.
  11. Know that the target will meet their end.
  12. After the spell is complete, do not think about the spell or the target, as this will interfere with the working.
