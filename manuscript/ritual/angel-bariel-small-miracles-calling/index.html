✨Angel Bariel Small Miracles Calling

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A supply of small white tea lights set in three circles around a central white larger candle
◇Directions
1. Light the central candle and say: "Bariel, angel of small miracles, bring the small miracle I do desire (name what you need and for whom) and I will do good in the world." From the candle, light clockwise the inner ring of candles, repeating the words for each one. 2. Blow out the central candle, saying the same words again, and let the candle ring burn. 3. Replace the inner candle ring. 4. On day 2, relight the central candle and from it relight clockwise the innermost and middle circles of tea lights, saying the same words for each one. 5. Blow out the central candle, repeating the words, and let the two tea light rings burn. 6. Replace the burned out candle rings. 7. On day 3, relight the central candle, saying the same words, and from it relight the innermost, middle, and outer tea light rings, saying the same words for each light. 8. Let all the candles burn through. 9. Perform when extra help is needed in an uncertain medical situation over three days.
◇Description
A three-day spell for when things look bad, calling angel Bariel for small miracles by lighting three circles of white candles around central candle to bring what is desired.
