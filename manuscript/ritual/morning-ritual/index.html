🕯️Morning Ritual

A daily morning ritual using the four elements to set intentions for the day. Uses a crystal, water in a chalice, incense, and essential oil on your altar. Helps visualize your perfect day, focus intentions, and call upon energies to support your goals.

Quantities

  • 1 small crystal
  • 1 cup or chalice of water
  • 1 stick or cone of incense
  • Essential oil of your choice

Directions

  1. Set a small crystal, a cup or chalice of water, a stick or cone of incense, and an essential oil that you find pleasing on your altar.
  2. Take a moment to think about the types of energies you would like to call upon to influence the unfolding day.
  3. Imagine your perfect day and try to form a very clear image of it in your mind.
  4. You may have a specific accomplishment in mind.
  5. See yourself achieving whatever your heart desires.
  6. Imagine yourself doing everything you do throughout the day in a state of joy and satisfaction.
  7. Put a small drop of oil on your fingertip and touch your fingertip to your third eye.
  8. See yourself bathed in the lovely morning light.
  9. Light the incense and let its scent waft over you.
  10. Say an affirmation or blessing and focus your intentions for the day.
  11. Hold the cup of water up to "toast" the day.
  12. Then bring it down to chest level, close to your heart.
  13. Dip your fingers in the water, close your eyes, and place a drop of water on each eyelid.
  14. Ask that your perception expand so you can see with clarity in all situations.
  15. Hold the crystal and project into it your intentions for the day.
  16. Ask it to help you stay focused on your purpose throughout the day and to give you strength and determination when you need them.
  17. You may choose to carry the crystal with you throughout the day.
