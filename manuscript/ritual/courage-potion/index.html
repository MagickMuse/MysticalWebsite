↗Plants1
Ginger

This warming tea potion combines the energizing properties of black tea with ginger's fiery courage to help you face challenges bravely.

Preparation

Brew black tea according to the Tea Potion Spell. Add either a piece of freshly peeled ginger root or several pieces of candied ginger to the cup.

For an extra boost, use a mug decorated with a lion or wolf, both animals associated with courage and strength.

The Working

As you drink, visualize yourself standing tall and confident, ready to face whatever challenges await. Feel the warmth of the tea spreading through you like liquid bravery. Complete the incantation from the Tea Potion Spell, filling in "courage" as your intention:

This cup of tea is now enchanted,
with seeds of courage,
firmly planted.
Aromatic magickal potion,
As I drink, set my spell in motion.
