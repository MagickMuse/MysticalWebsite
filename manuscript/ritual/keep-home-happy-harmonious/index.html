🏠Keep Home Happy Harmonious

A Sunday morning spell to keep your home happy and harmonious if you have busy life or people constantly rush in and out, tying six flowers to branch then wafting through home for peace every day of week.

Quantities

  • A small leafy branch
  • Six long-stemmed yellow or blue flowers
  • Twine or string
  • A small bowl of water

Directions

  1. Tie the flowers at regular intervals along the branch, saying: "Peace in my home is what I seek, every day of the week." Dip the pointed end into the water.
  2. Beginning at the front door, waft the branch an inch or two above the floor, moving from front of the house to the back, bottom to top and down again, saying the spell words softly and continuously.
  3. Take the branch out of the front/main door, standing it upright in the garden or a plant pot for the flowers to blow away.
  4. Tip the water outside the front door, saying: "Harmony is restored, tranquillity once more." This also works well if you have had sorrow or quarrels at home.
  5. Perform Sunday morning.
