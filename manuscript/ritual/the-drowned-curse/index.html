⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A sympathetic curse using a handmade poppet bound to a heavy stone and sunk into deep water. The pressure of the depths symbolizes the crushing weight of problems bearing down on the target.

Quantities

1 handmade poppet (driftwood, hay, grass, kelp, or seaweed)
1 large rock
Personal link to the target (hair, nail clippings, or inscribed seashell)
Binding material (hay, vines, seaweed, or similar)

Directions

  1. Craft a poppet from environmentally safe materials such as driftwood, hay, grass, kelp, or seaweed.
  2. Throughout the construction, keep the target firmly in mind and pour all of your negative feelings toward them into the figure.
  3. Attach a personal link to the target, such as hair, nail clippings, or a seashell inscribed with their name.
  4. Use hay, vines, seaweed, or similar materials to bind the poppet securely to a large rock.
  5. Travel to a body of deep, dark water, whether a wharf, shoreline, or by boat.
  6. Hold the poppet and rock close to your chest and stare into the darkness of the water.
  7. Speak aloud if you wish, or simply focus on your rage.
  8. Picture the target clearly in your mind, then drop the rock into the water and watch it drag the poppet down.
  9. The deeper the water, the better, as the pressure symbolizes the weight of the world crushing the target with problems.
  10. Remain for a time afterward to meditate on the curse and what you want to come of it.
  11. If the poppet separates from the rock and floats back to the surface, the working must be repeated from the beginning.
