🕯️Prayer to the Fates

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
PrayerInvocation
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
- No materials required - spoken invocation only
◇Directions
1. At Samhain, the world undecided, three ladies weave fine the filmy veil of night. Clotho's knee churns the spinning wheel by which all fortunes emerge, Lachesis assigns each thread spewed into her hands, assigns each vein its place in the great tapestry wrapping line by line alongside smaller and larger Fates. Atropos comes to the end, the middle, at bare starts cuts away, trims, or knots to what binds us here. We may seek the favor of these Moirae, a strong warp, a sweet weft, but once the knot is made we pray that we be woven of strong fiber.
◇Description
A prayer to the three Fates (Moirae) who weave destiny at Samhain. Honors Clotho who spins the wheel of fortune, Lachesis who assigns each thread its place, and Atropos who cuts the threads. Asks to be woven of strong fiber.
