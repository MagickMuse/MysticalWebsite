🔮Stone Purification Ritual

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
ElementalCleansingConsecrationIncense MagickCandle MagickWater MagickDirectional Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Basin or bowl of pure water - Red candle - Incense - Dish or flowerpot filled with freshly dug earth - Clean cloth for drying - The stone or crystal to be purified
◇Directions
1. Set up your altar or table with the four elements arranged by cardinal direction. 2. Place the basin of pure water to the West, the lit red candle to the South, burning incense to the East, and the dish or flowerpot of freshly dug earth to the North. 3. Set the stone to be purified in the center between all four elements. 4. This ritual is best performed at sunrise or during daylight hours. 5. Still your mind and enter a meditative state. 6. Pick up the stone in your projective hand and turn your attention toward the bowl of earth. 7. Place the stone on the earth and cover it completely with fresh soil. 8. Say "I purify you with Earth!" or similar words of intent. 9. Leave the stone covered for a few minutes while visualizing the earth absorbing all impurities from the stone. 10. Remove the stone from the earth and dust it clean. 11. Hold it in the incense smoke and pass it nine times through the smoke, moving from right to left. 12. Say "I purify you with Air!" See the smoke carrying away all disturbing energies that cling to the stone. 13. Quickly pass the stone through the candle's flame several times, being careful not to burn yourself or damage heat-sensitive stones. 14. Say "I purify you with Fire!" Visualize the fire burning away all negativity and unwanted vibrations. 15. Place the stone in the basin of water. 16. Say "I purify you with Water!" or speak your own words of cleansing. 17. Visualize the water washing the stone completely clean of all impurities. 18. Leave the stone in the water for a few minutes. 19. Remove the stone from the water and dry it thoroughly with a clean cloth. 20. Hold the stone in your receptive hand and sense the energy within it. 21. If the vibrations feel regular and healthy, the cleansing has been successful. 22. If the stone still feels unclear or heavy, repeat the entire ritual as many times as necessary until the work is complete. 23. Once purified, store the stone in a special place. 24. It is now ready for use in magick.
◇Description
A four-element indoor purification ritual to cleanse crystals and stones of unwanted energies using earth, air, fire, and water. The stone is passed through each element in turn while speaking words of intent, absorbing impurities and restoring the stone's natural vibrations. Can be repeated as needed until cleansing is complete.
