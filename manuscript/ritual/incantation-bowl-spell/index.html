🏺Incantation Bowl Spell

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
BanishingProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Shallow terracotta dish (purchased or handmade) - Permanent ink marker
◇Directions
1. Using permanent ink, write banishing incantations on the dish beginning at the outer rim and spiraling inward toward the center. 2. State your desire for a demon-free home and existence in your own words. 3. Include commanding phrases such as "Evil Spirit, Get Out!" or "Get out of the house belonging to [Name], child of [Name]. Do not disturb her." Incorporate traditional formulas such as "Bound and sealed are all demons and evil spirits." You may also frame the banishment as a divorce decree, permanently severing the spirit's connection to the home and ordering it to depart. 4. Fill the bowl completely with words. 5. Once complete, bury the bowl upside down beneath the doorstep or entry threshold.
◇Description
A demon-trapping and banishing device used historically by Jews, Mandaeans, and Pagans of pre-Islamic Mesopotamia. A shallow terracotta dish is covered in spiral incantations written from the outer rim toward the center, sometimes with a crude image of a demon drawn at the center. The bowl is buried upside down under the doorstep, trapping the spirit beneath it where the written charms disempower it.
