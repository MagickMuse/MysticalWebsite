A ritual of sacred witnessing, designed to reconnect you with the reality of your body and the depth of your emotional experience. This practice supports healing from body shame, disconnection, and the witch wound held in the flesh.

Quantities

  • A mirror you can stand or sit in front of
  • 1 or more candles (optional but recommended)
  • Optional: incense, calming music, or soft lighting to enhance the mood

Directions

  1. Prepare your space by turning off overhead lights and working by candlelight or lamplight.
    Set the mood with music, scent, or silence, according to your preference.
    Cleanse and bless your ritual space using incense, a bell, crystals, or visualization.
    Visualize a glowing white light expanding from your solar plexus and forming a protective sphere.
    State your intention to cleanse and protect the space, making it sacred and separate from the mundane.
    Close your eyes and take three deep, slow breaths to settle your body and quiet your mind.
    Stand or sit in front of the mirror and undress to your comfort level. Nudity is encouraged but not required.
    Open your eyes and meet your gaze in the mirror.
    Slowly let your eyes move across your reflection. Observe each part: your face, neck, shoulders, chest, arms, belly, hips, legs, and feet.
    Take in every detail: lines, dimples, curves, scars, skin tone, softness, and shape.
    Allow emotions to arise naturally. Witness them with gentleness and non-judgment.
    If tears come, let them. If laughter bubbles up, let it be free.
    If it feels right, engage in gentle self-touch. Let your hands glide over your arms, chest, stomach, and thighs.
    Explore movement slowly. Sway, stretch, or roll your shoulders. Move like water.
    Stay present with sensation and sight. Let your only goal be connection.
    When you feel complete, place your hands over your heart or belly and speak aloud:
    Body, I am sorry for all the times I have been unkind to you, and I am grateful for all the things you enable me to do. I appreciate you. I honor you. I respect you. And I love you.
    After the ritual, offer yourself care and comfort. Watch something warm, rest, or cuddle a pet.
    Honor your vulnerability. Let your healing integrate with kindness.
