🔮Rainbow Stone Divination

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Seven stones, one each of red, pink, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple - Soft cloth bag for storage Suggested stones by color: - Red: ruby, red jasper, red agate, rhodonite, red tourmaline, or garnet - Pink: pink tourmaline, rose quartz, pink calcite, rhodocrosite, or kunzite - Orange: carnelian, amber, citrine, or tiger's eye - Yellow: yellow tourmaline, topaz, or yellow fluorite - Green: jade, peridot, olivine, aventurine, emerald, or green tourmaline - Blue: celestite, aquamarine, sodalite, blue quartz, blue tourmaline, turquoise, or sapphire - Purple: sugilite, lepidolite, or amethyst
◇Directions
1. Gather seven stones of approximately the same size and shape, one of each color: red, pink, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. 2. Place them in a soft cloth bag. 3. When you need guidance, still your mind and formulate your question clearly. 4. Reach into the bag and select a stone at random. 5. The color and its symbolic meaning may answer your question. 6. If you need more information, draw a second stone and interpret the two together. 7. Color meanings: Red symbolizes anger or destructive emotions, birth, change, sex, passion, endings, energy, and confrontations. 8. Pink symbolizes love, friendship, peace, joy, relationships, and family. 9. Orange symbolizes illumination, personal power, energy, and growth. 10. Yellow symbolizes protection, communication, travel, and movement. 11. Green symbolizes growth, money, grounding, health, fertility, and business. 12. Blue symbolizes peace, sleep, healing, purification, emotions, and the subconscious. 13. Purple symbolizes spirituality, evolution, mysticism, and expansion. 14. Interpret the stones according to your personal associations with the colors and the context of your question. 15. Trust the first impressions or words that come to mind when you see each stone. 16. Analyze how the symbolic meanings relate to your situation. 17. If the reading suggests action, use magick to transform the insight into positive change. 18. Carry or wear the drawn stones to reinforce the working.
◇Description
A color-based divination method using seven stones, one of each color, drawn randomly from a cloth bag to provide guidance about the future. Stones can be drawn individually or in combination to answer questions through symbolic interpretation of color meanings.
