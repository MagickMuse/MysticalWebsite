💤Saint Agnes' Eve Silent Dream Spell

A Saint Agnes' Eve soul mate divination requiring a full day's fast, a salt-filled hard-boiled egg eaten in silence (including the shell), and walking backwards to bed while speaking an incantation. Silence is maintained until morning when the oracle is received in dreams.

Quantities

  • One hard-boiled egg
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Fast all day while holding your intention clearly in mind.
  2. Before beginning the ritual portion, prepare fully for bed: put on nightclothes and make the bed.
  3. Enter complete silence, no words or sounds until the incantation.
  4. Hard boil an egg.
  5. Cut it in half and remove the yolk.
  6. Fill the hollow with salt.
  7. Eat the entire egg with the salt, including the shell.
  8. Walk backwards to the bed and speak aloud:
  9. Sweet Saint Agnes, work your fast
    In my dreams, let this spell be cast
    If ever I am to marry a man, or a man to marry me
    I hope tonight him to see!
  10. Maintain silence from this point until morning.
  11. The soul mate oracle is revealed in dreams.
