Brigid

A guided sea-journey visualization across three enchanted islands, each offering a gift from Brigid's forge, cave, and sacred grove.

Quantities

Quiet space to sit or lie undisturbed for at least an hour
Soft scarf or blindfold (optional)
Recording of ocean sounds (optional)
Light food and a glass of cool water for afterward

Directions

  1. Allow at least an hour for the journey, since some immrama take many hours.
  2. Dress comfortably and warmly, since you will be sitting or lying still.
  3. Choose a silent space, or play a quiet recording of ocean sounds, avoiding music, which influences the emotions.
  4. Sit or lie down in a dark room, tying a soft scarf over your eyes if needed to block out light.
  5. Speak or think the invocation:
    Brigid of the green sea,
    Brigid of the swift wind,
    Brigid of the bright star,
    Guide my vessel.
  6. Visualize yourself seated in a small wooden boat on a misty shore, the sea just touching its bow.
  7. Orient yourself in the boat, notice anything you carry with you, and take in the mood of the ocean, sky, and weather.
  8. A wave sent from the depths of the ocean's magick lifts your boat afloat.
  9. Once on the open sea, unloose the ropes so the sail unfurls, note its color and any symbols upon it, and let the wind carry your boat outward on its journey.
  10. You approach a first island of grey stone.
  11. Tie your boat to a bronze ring, disembark, and climb a narrow path to the top, where you find a sheltered forge with a glowing anvil.
  12. Take the gift left for you there, give thanks, and return to your boat.
  13. The wind carries you to a second island, where stairs cut into a white cliff face lead up from the sea.
  14. Tie your boat to a silver ring and climb to a cave lined with mother-of-pearl, filled with an otherworldly musical resonance.
  15. Receive the gift placed in your open hands, give thanks, and return to your boat.
  16. The wind carries you to a third island covered in dense forest.
  17. Follow the forking path, noticing how each choice feels, until you reach a clearing where an ancient stone well sits at the center of a sacred grove.
  18. Lower the bucket into the well and draw forth a gift.
  19. Give thanks and return to your boat.
  20. With the three gifts beside you, the wind carries your boat back to shore, where three cloaked women await beside a stone altar.
  21. Place your gifts upon the altar.
  22. As each woman touches a gift it transforms into living flame; the flames merge into one, and the three women merge into the flame as well.
  23. Gaze into its depths, feel it warming your being, and receive any message meant for you.
  24. When ready, return to waking awareness, giving thanks for the journey and its gifts.
  25. Stay quiet to absorb the experience, then ground yourself by eating something light and drinking a glass of cool water.
