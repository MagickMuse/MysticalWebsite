🧘Journey to Spirit

A guided meditation journey inspired by Barn Owl's role as messenger between worlds. By entering the chamber behind your heart where your spirit dwells, you connect with inner guidance and listen for messages from your deepest self.

Quantities

  • Optional: Journal and pen for recording insights

Directions

  1. Barn Owl reminds you to connect with spirit's whisperings living within your own heart.
  2. This meditation journey helps you reconnect with your own spirit spark.
  3. Begin by getting comfortable sitting or lying down.
  4. Breathe in and out through your nose, allowing your body to relax.
  5. Now imagine your consciousness slipping outside your body, stepping out of your own skin.
  6. Glide around so you're standing behind yourself.
  7. Imagine a small door behind your heart.
  8. Open that door and step inside.
  9. What does it look like on the pathway to your heart?
  10. What does it smell and sound like?
  11. Walk in farther until you come to the chamber where your spirit lives.
  12. Notice your surroundings.
  13. Notice how your spirit looks.
  14. Ask Spirit if it has a message for you, then listen with your whole being.
  15. Call on Barn Owl to help you hear truly.
  16. When finished, walk out to the door, close it, and seal it behind you.
  17. Walk back around to your front and step back into your own skin.
  18. Journal what you noticed right away, before you lose the sense of your journey.
