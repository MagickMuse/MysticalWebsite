🕷️Witch's Mirror Ancestor Moon Meditation

◇Method
Mirror MagickAncestor WorkDivination
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
- Candle - Matches or lighter - Mirror - Dried rosemary sprig - An offering of food or drink - Veil or lace (optional)
◇Directions
1. Begin by breathing deeply and allowing your mind to soften and settle. 2. Cleanse your energy and the space around you, through smoke, breath, sound, or however you prefer to open a working. 3. Light the candle and place it before the mirror so its reflection doubles the flame. 4. If you feel called to, drape a veil or piece of lace over your head to signal your openness to the unseen. 5. Hold your rosemary sprig and touch its tip briefly to the candle flame until it catches and begins to smolder. 6. As the smoke rises, say: 7. "By the light of Samhain (or Hunter's or Blood) Moon, ancestors I call to commune. Guardian moon of the past, your wisdom and visions I seek at last." 8. Hold the smoking rosemary between yourself and the mirror. 9. Soften your gaze and look into the mirror without focusing hard on anything. 10. Let the candlelight and smoke move and shift. 11. Be patient, this is not a practice of force. 12. Simply wait and receive. 13. Notice any images, impressions, feelings, or thoughts that arise, however faint. 14. If nothing comes immediately, call upon the moon again and let yourself drop more fully into your intuition. 15. Trust what surfaces. 16. When you are ready to close, use the rosemary smoke to cleanse your energy, wafting it around your body, and give thanks aloud for whatever wisdom or presence came through. 17. Leave your offering, food, drink, or both, on your altar or near the working space for the ancestors or spirits that answered. 18. Discard it with gratitude after a day or a few days.
◇Description
A candlelit mirror scrying meditation for the dark half of the year, using burning rosemary smoke to open communication with ancestors and spirit guides. Gaze softly into the mirror through the interplay of candlelight and smoke and invite visions, wisdom, and messages from beyond the veil. Close with an offering of food or drink left out for any spirits that came through.
