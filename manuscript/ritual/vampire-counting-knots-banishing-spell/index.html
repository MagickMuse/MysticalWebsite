🛡️Vampire Counting Knots Banishing Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
BanishingKnot Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Fishing nets (to hang over entrance doors and windows) - Optional alternative: sieves
◇Directions
1. Hang fishing nets over all entrance doors and windows of the space you wish to protect. 2. The vampire will be compelled to count every knot before it can enter, buying enough time for sunrise or simply deterring it from entering at all. 3. If fishing nets are unavailable, sieves may be substituted or used alongside the nets.
◇Description
A vampire banishing working that exploits the traditional belief that vampires are compelled to count every knot they encounter. Fishing nets placed over entry points force the vampire to count all knots before entering, leaving it exposed to sunlight or causing it to give up. Sieves may be used as an alternative, as the vampire will be forced to count the holes instead.
