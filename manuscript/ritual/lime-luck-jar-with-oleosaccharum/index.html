🍀Lime Luck Jar with Oleosaccharum

A luck jar spell using lime oleosaccharum, combining the solar energy of lime with the sweetness of sugar to draw good fortune.

Quantities

  • Fresh lime zest (from 1-2 limes)
  • Sugar (enough to cover zest)
  • Small jar with lid
  • Paper
  • Pen

Directions

  1. Create lime oleosaccharum by combining fresh lime zest with sugar in a bowl.
  2. Allow the sugar to draw out the aromatic oils from the zest, which takes about 30 minutes to an hour.
  3. The mixture will become fragrant and slightly wet.
  4. Write a petition for good fortune on a small piece of paper.
  5. Place the oleosaccharum in a jar and add your written petition.
  6. Seal the jar.
  7. The sweetness attracts luck while the lime's solar energy empowers the working.
  8. Keep the jar on your altar or in a place associated with prosperity.
