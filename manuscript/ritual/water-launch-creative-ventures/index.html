🌊Water Launch Creative Ventures

A group water ritual launching small wooden rafts with candles, flowers, and fruit to carry wishes for new ventures and life changes.

Quantities

  • 1 small square piece of wood per person
  • 1 small beeswax or organic candle per person
  • Flowers in bright colors per person
  • Small fruits

Directions

  1. Go to a lake, river, a pool, or the sea, standing close to the waterline.
  2. Each person holds his or her wooden raft, in turn saying, "Carry for me," and silently naming his or her wishes, unless it is a joint venture, in which case devise the words in advance and speak them aloud in turn.
  3. Each person lights a candle, setting it on the raft, naming the wish again silently unless it is a joint wish.
  4. Add a flower and fruit to each boat and on a prearranged signal launch the boats gently at the same time, saying together softly, "Mother Sea, carry for me this wish swiftly and powerfully to return successfully. So shall it be." Perform any seasonal change point or close to the full moon.
