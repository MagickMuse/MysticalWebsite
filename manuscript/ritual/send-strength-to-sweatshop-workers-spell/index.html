⚒️Send Strength to Sweatshop Workers Spell

A Sunday spell for ritual to send strength to those working in sweatshops to produce cheap goods for Western market, rubbing crystal sphere with dark then white scarf seven times to take away pain and bring new hope.

Quantities

  • A small, clear, crystal quartz sphere or a small glass paperweight
  • A dark-colored scarf
  • A white or pale scarf
  • White candles (optional)

Directions

  1. Name the place and people being exploited, saying: "May tomorrow new hope bring, against those who exploit your suffering." Rub the sphere with the dark-colored scarf, and, as you do so, say seven times: "Take away the pain, take away the lust for gain, take away the profiteers who thrive on others' tears." Then rub the sphere with the white scarf, and, as you do, repeat the second set of spell words seven more times.
  2. Set the sphere where light will shine on it or, if it's dark or there is only hazy light, place a circle of white candles around it, saying the second set of spell words seven more times and letting the candles burn through.
  3. Perform Sunday.
