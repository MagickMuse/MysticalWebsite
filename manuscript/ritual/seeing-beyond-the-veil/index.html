🎭Seeing Beyond the Veil

A comprehensive solo movement ritual to see beyond the veil at Samhain. Uses mask work, dance, and trance to perceive ancestors, faeries, or new possibilities. Includes full circle casting, quarter calls, Hecate invocation, and can be adapted for limited mobility.

Quantities

  • Mask (decorated)
  • Altar cloth (black, orange, or red)
  • Black candle (skull candle ideal)
  • Matches
  • Wine, tea, olive oil, or water for libations
  • Bowl to catch libations
  • Calendula, marigold, or chrysanthemum petals
  • Rosemary, mugwort, or wormwood leaves
  • Towels
  • Vial of olive or sunflower oil
  • Optional: pomegranate seeds, myrrh, or frankincense for oil
  • Comfortable clothing
  • Scarf or head covering (white or black preferred)
  • Instrumental music
  • Athame or wand
  • Candle snuffers or fire safety tools
  • Optional: bookstand for ritual text

Directions

  1. PURPOSE:
    This ritual helps you see beyond the veil into the next world. This experience may be different for different people, you may see ancestors and faeries or you could simply see the world in a different way, coming away with a sense of new possibilities. While intended as a solo ritual, you may wish to invite a trusted friend to act as a spotter. This ritual is movement based but can be performed as meditation with minimal motion if needed.
  2. SETTING:
    A place you feel safe. Indoors or outdoors is fine as long as the space is clear, open, and free of furniture or things to trip over.
  3. PRE-RITUAL PREPARATIONS:
    Establish sacred space. Cast a circle (traditionally nine feet diameter, but can encompass entire room or floor). Prepare decoction of calendula, rosemary, and mugwort. Strain and add to bath. Bathe with intention to wash away daily worries and imbalances.
  4. Dry off and anoint yourself with olive oil at each point:
  5. Crown: "I connect to the divine."
  6. Eyelids: "That I see truth clearly."
  7. Lips: "Only truth may come from my lips."
  8. Heart: "I know the truth of my heart."
  9. Belly: "I celebrate the strength of my body in all worlds."
  10. Knees: "My body upholds me in my path."
  11. Feet: "I walk in truth and dance with the universe."
  12. Palms: "My hands are instruments to create the good."
  13. Stand with legs hip-width apart, arms spread wide.
  14. Visualize white light entering crown and traveling to each anointed point.
  15. Put on ritual clothing and head covering.
  16. THE RITUAL:
    Take athame or wand. Walk around designated area three times:
  17. First round:
    I take this place to hidden space
    between worlds, between veils,
    where life and death can interlace.
  18. Second round:
    All within this sacred round
    is protected, from sky to deep below ground.
  19. Third round:
    May all beings that truly see to my good
    come guard this circle, come,
    from city, sky, water, or wood!
    So mote it be!
  20. Quarter Calls (if fits your practice):
  21. East:
    Hail to the element of air,
    to the elementals that make the wind blow and the weather flow,
    hail to the guardian of the watchtower of the east, Raphael,
    watch over this circle that I may have cause for joy in learning,
    and protection from injury.
    So mote it be!
  22. South:
    Hail to the element of fire,
    to the elementals that make the fire dance and the heat warm,
    hail to the guardian of the watchtower of the south, Michael,
    watch over this circle, and protect me from all harm!
    So mote it be!
  23. West:
    Hail to the element of water,
    to the elementals that stir from within,
    hail to the guardian of the watchtower of the west, Gabriel,
    watch over this circle, and guide my awareness
    to what will benefit my consciousness!
    So mote it be!
  24. North:
    Hail to the element of earth,
    to the elementals that give us our quiet,
    hail to the guardian of the watchtower of the north, Uriel,
    watch over this circle, and guide me safely through this journey!
    So mote it be!
  25. Invocation to Hecate:
    Raise beverage above bowl. Speak:
  26. Hail to Hecate, queen of all witchery!
    You who keep all secrets,
    you who tell all truths.
    I accept that to come before you
    is to have secrets I keep from myself stripped away.
    Dread queen, be gentle with me, if you can,
    help me to see beyond my world,
    to open my avenue of thought,
    to let me see what I most need to see
    on this night.
  27. Pour libation into bowl.
  28. Light black candle.
  29. Gaze at bottom of flame, unfocusing vision, then snapping back.
  30. Repeat several times.
  31. Concentrate on music beat and movement it inspires.
  32. As you slip into profound consciousness, don the mask.
  33. Close eyes for ten seconds, then open.
  34. Dance or sway with mask on.
  35. Imagine mask is another person and you're seeing through their eyes.
  36. Observe: Does room seem familiar?
  37. What's different?
  38. How do your thoughts sound?
  39. How does your body feel as you move?
  40. When music ends, remove mask.
  41. Turn off music.
  42. Sit in quiet and meditate, stretching and looking around until all seems familiar.
  43. Turn on lights if needed.
  44. CLOSING:
    Pour libation for Hecate, sip yourself, pour rest in bowl:
  45. Blessed queen, Lady Hecate,
    you have gifted me with the privilege of seeing beyond myself.
    I have walked in your dread world,
    and you graciously returned me.
    For this, I give thanks.
    The rite is done, but the night goes on,
    so if you must go I wish you well on your way!
    Blessed Be!
  46. Release quarters (north, west, south, east).
  47. Open circle with athame.
  48. Drink water or juice, stretch muscles.
  49. Record your experience, physical sensations, emotions, shapes seen.
  50. Use during November to build personal symbols and omens.
