A written love talisman based on the nineteen-century-old Sator Square, carried by the person who wishes to become the object of desire. Unlike the transmission philters, this spell acts as a booster on the deep mind of the bearer rather than influencing an external target. It borrows from Christian letter symbolism but operates independently of religious belief through its accumulated magickal potency.

Quantities

Clean white paper
Pen and ink of art
Clean cloth or small box (for burial wrapping)

Directions

  1. Select a Friday with the moon waxing. Choose your working hour: 8am, 3pm, or 10pm.
  2. Dress the altar in the usual manner for love workings. Purify the chamber with fire and water. Perform the Habondia invocation with attendant images.
  3. Take a sheet of clean white paper (the same kind used for the Square of Mercury). Exorcise it with water and fire.
  4. Using pen and ink of art, divide the paper into twenty-five equal squares, five across and five down.
  5. Chanting a suitable rhyme expressing your intent with each letter as you inscribe it (such as: [Name] greatly desired and strong in love!), write the letters of the Sator Square into the grid in the following numerical order, charging each with full desire and yearning:
    S=9, A=1, T=4, O=13, R=19
    A=3, R=12, E=24, P=15, O=21
    T=23, E=18, N=20/7, E=5, T=10
    O=8, P=2, E=11, R=25, A=16
    R=6, O=26, T=17, A=14, S=22
    Begin with number 1 (A) and end with number 26 (O). The centre square (N) is written seventh and then written over again twentieth.
    The completed square reads:
    SATOR
    AREPO
    TENET
    OPERA
    ROTAS
  6. Seal the completed talisman with the triple cross and the words: So mote it be.
  7. Wrap it in a piece of clean cloth or place it in a box. Bury it in the earth for a day and a night, preferably at a crossroads or a place where two paths meet. A churchyard crossroads is traditional; a flowerbed or patch of ground where two garden paths cross is acceptable.
  8. After twenty-four hours, remove the talisman from the soil and give it into the keeping of the person it is intended to benefit.
  9. The recipient should carry it on their person and sleep with it under their pillow for as long as they wish to receive its aid. The talisman is effective for approximately twenty-eight days, after which a new one must be constructed.
