⚡Raising Power

◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Method
Energy Work
◇Category
Energetic Rite
↗Correspondences5
✨CleansingEarth✨Manifestation👑Power🧘🏻‍♀️Spirituality

Gathering energy from natural sources, solar, wind, water, bio-energy, storms, for spellwork. This technique involves visiting power sources, sensing their energy signatures, and learning to channel that power through the body until you no longer need to be physically present with the source.

Quantities

None required (energy-work technique)

Directions

  1. Visit the source: go physically to a place where your chosen energy is strong, such as a sunny hilltop, a rushing river, a windswept cliff, or a grove of old trees.
  2. Sense the signature: open your awareness and feel the unique quality of that energy; each source has its own signature.
  3. Learn to channel: allow the energy to flow into you, feeling it enter through your hands, feet, or crown, letting it fill your body without forcing it.
  4. Practice until portable: with enough practice, call upon that energy signature from memory, drawing it to you across distance without needing to be physically present.

Natural Power Sources

  • Solar energy: The sun's radiant force, especially potent at dawn and noon
  • Wind energy: Moving air currents, particularly strong during storms
  • Water energy: Rivers, oceans, rain, and running streams
  • Bio-energy: The living force of plants, trees, and earth
  • Storm energy: Lightning, thunder, and atmospheric electricity

Staying Focused

Only raise energy when the spell item is completely prepared and ready for empowerment or activation. Gathered energy needs to be released in one quick jolt once a peak is reached; delaying will dissipate the energy. The sequence is: raise energy, focus it on the intent of the spell, direct it into the spell materials, and send it on its way to accomplish the task.

Addressing the Elemental Winds

The completed spell materials may be raised to each Quarter to energize the spell. North wind lends itself to physical actions. East wind supports new beginnings and mental actions. South wind fuels power and vigorous actions. West wind aids cleansings and emotional actions.

Seeing the Goal as Accomplished

At the end of a spell, see the goal as already accomplished. Use a closing phrase such as "So Mote It Be!" or "It Is Done!" Visualize the raised energy as released to perform its task so it is no longer hovering about. Ground excess energy by touching palms to the floor or the Earth.

Feet

The feet raise energy during dance. The left foot draws up strength and power of Elemental Earth to be utilized in workings. The right foot gathers the spiritual, mental, and psychic energies of Elemental Air to be transmuted into physical manifestation through magickal work.

Breathing

Breathing is a method of raising energy. Chanting with a growing crescendo, using your own simple rhyme for rhythm or the Witch's Rune, builds power steadily. Altering the breathing pattern also works: sharp, short intakes of breath followed by one large intake, held and then released as focused and directed energy. Too much of this technique will cause fainting, so use caution.

A sweeping intake, moving right to left and back again, creates the loop of the cosmic lemniscate while gathering energy. A rapid exhale directed onto the spell object enlivens it. The breath of life energy sends gathered energies off with a gentle power that is not rushed or distracted.

Dancing

Dancing raises energy through physical movement and rhythm. Moving deosil (clockwise) around the Circle draws positive energy, increasing the energy drawn from the Earth. Moving widdershins (counterclockwise) around the Circle uses negative energy, gathering and compressing existing energy for directed release.

Notes

This is considered by many to be an advanced technique because it requires refined sensitivity to energy and the discipline to work without physical tools or props. It is closely related to psi-magick, magick worked solely with the mind.
