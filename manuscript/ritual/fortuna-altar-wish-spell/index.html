A petition ritual for Fortuna, Roman goddess of luck and fate, using a dedicated altar adorned with her sacred emblems. Fortuna is especially generous toward women and children.

Quantities

Gold candle
Wish list written on paper
Dice
Roses
Perfume
Lucky charms for altar decoration

Directions

  1. Devote an altar to Fortuna and decorate it with dice, lucky charms, roses, and perfume.
  2. Write a wish list and place the paper beneath a gold candle.
  3. Burn the candle while focusing on each wish, allowing the flame to carry the petitions to Fortuna.
