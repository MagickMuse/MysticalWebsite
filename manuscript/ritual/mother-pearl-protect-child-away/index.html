🦪Mother Pearl Protect Child Away

A Monday after full moon ritual empowering seven mother of pearl items in a jar to protect children during separation.

Quantities

  • 1 silver candle
  • 1 small lidded jar
  • 7 mother of pearl buttons or beads

Directions

  1. Light the candle, placing the jar where candlelight shines on it.
  2. Pass each button or bead around the flame once, saying for each one, "Be filled with my love, be filled with my blessings, be filled with my protection, that though apart, we are together in our hearts." After empowering it, place each one in the jar.
  3. Put on the lid, shake it seven times, saying for each shake, "Monday, Tuesday, every day, guard my precious child for me. So I ask and so I say, with these pearls protected be." Let the candle burn, then put the jar on a high shelf in the child's bedroom.
  4. Before she must be away, perhaps because you share joint custody or you are absent overnight, shake the jar seven times, repeating all the spell words seven times, once for each shake.
  5. If you are home, do the same every night during her absence.
  6. Perform the first Monday after a full moon, and whenever you apart.
