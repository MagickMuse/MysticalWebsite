An altar-based prosperity spell combining candle magick with carefully selected tarot imagery. The four cards work together: Ace of Pentacles draws new opportunities, Ten of Pentacles brings windfalls, Nine of Cups grants wishes, and The Star offers hope and guidance.

Quantities

  • A white and a green candle in candleholders
  • An object that represents your desire
  • The Ace of Pentacles
  • The Ten of Pentacles
  • The Nine of Cups
  • The Star card
  • Matches or a lighter

Directions

  1. Cast a circle around your ritual space.
  2. Position your candles at opposite ends of the altar. Between them, place an object representing your financial goals, a coin, currency, jewelry, or other symbol of wealth.
  3. Lay the Ace of Pentacles before the white candle to invite new financial opportunities. Position the Ten of Pentacles (associated with financial windfalls) in front of the green candle. Place the Nine of Cups (the wish card) and The Star (hope) in the center between both candles.
  4. Light the candles and recite:
    The money I spend
    and the money I lend
    comes back to me
    in multiples of three.
  5. Visualize money flowing to you from every direction. Make your mental images as vivid as possible. Continue as long as feels right, then extinguish the candles and offer thanks. Leave everything on your altar overnight.
