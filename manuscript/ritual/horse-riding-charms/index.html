✨Horse Riding Charms

◇Form
TalismanCharged Object
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Agate stone (worn, carried, or attached to tack) - Turquoise stone (worn, carried, or attached to tack)
◇Directions
1. Carry or wear one or both of the following talismans while riding, or attach to the horse's tack: 2. - Agate, protects the rider from falling and the horse from stumbling - Turquoise, offers general protection to both horse and rider
◇Description
A pair of mineral talismans that protect both horse and rider simultaneously. Agate guards against falls and stumbles; turquoise provides broad general protection for the pair.
