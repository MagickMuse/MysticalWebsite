🌍Three-Soil Fertility Spell

A sympathetic fertility working drawing on the power of three specific types of earth, each charged with potent generative and vital energy: soil from the threshold of a bordello, dirt dislodged by a bull's horns, and dirt dislodged by a boar's tusks. Combined in a red charm bag and carried on the body, they draw fertility power from the earth itself.

Quantities

Handful of dirt from a bordello doorway
Handful of dirt dislodged by a bull's horns
Handful of dirt dislodged by a boar's tusks
Red charm bag

Directions

  1. Gather three separate handfuls of earth: one from near the doorway of a bordello, one dislodged from the ground by a bull's horns, and one dislodged from the ground by a boar's tusks.
  2. Place all three into a red charm bag and carry it on your person.
