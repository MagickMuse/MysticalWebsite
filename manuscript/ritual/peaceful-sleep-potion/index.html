Tea potions to facilitate a good night's sleep. Two variations are provided. These potions are best drunk at bedtime.

Quantities

  • Chamomile tea
  • Linden flower tea
  • Five poppy seeds
  • Optional: a blue cup or mug
    OR
  • Valerian tea
  • Linden flower tea
  • Optional: a blue cup or mug

Directions

  1. Prepare the tea according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. For the first variation, combine chamomile tea with linden flower tea, adding five poppy seeds.
  3. For the second variation, combine valerian tea with linden flower tea.
  4. You may optionally use a blue cup or mug to amplify the calming, peaceful energy.
  5. As you stir, focus on your desire for peaceful sleep.
  6. Drink at bedtime.
