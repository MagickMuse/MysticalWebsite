⚰️Double Whammy Doll

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
BindingManifestationInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
Black fabric (enough to construct a doll) Devil's Shoestring Red Pepper Goofer Dust (mixed with powdered snake skin) 1 photograph or written name of the victim Red thread 1 small coffin War Water 1 small black candle Oregano Poke Root Rubbing Alcohol 1 cauldron or fire-safe pot
◇Directions
1. Construct a doll from black fabric. 2. Stuff it with Devil's Shoestring, red pepper, and Goofer Dust, preferably mixed with powdered snake skin. 3. Place a photograph of the intended victim inside the back of the doll, or write their true name along with identifying details such as parentage, address, and date of birth on a slip of paper and insert it. 4. Sew the doll shut with red thread. 5. Lay the doll in a small coffin that has been coated with War Water. 6. Burn a small black candle on top of the coffin, allowing the wax to drip and seal over the coffin itself. 7. Build a fire in a cauldron or fire-safe pot using oregano, poke root, and rubbing alcohol. 8. Meditate on the image of the target and cast the coffin into the flames while reciting the incantation. 9. Allow everything to burn to ashes and cool completely. 10. Grind the ashes to a fine powder, take them outside, and blow them into the air. 11. This working is best performed during the dark moon.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A ferocious Hoodoo-style poppet curse combining doll magick, coffin magick, and fire. A black fabric doll is stuffed with Devil's Shoestring, red pepper, and Goofer Dust mixed with powdered snake skin, then loaded with the target's identity and sewn shut with red thread. The doll is placed in a small coffin painted with War Water, sealed with melting black candle wax, and finally cast into a cauldron fire fueled by oregano, poke root, and rubbing alcohol. The powdered ashes are blown into the wind. Best performed during the dark moon.
