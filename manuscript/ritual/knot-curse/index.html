⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A new-moon knot curse that binds pain, darkness, and madness into a knotted black cord. The practitioner visualizes the target's suffering, chants an incantation three times, and seals the working with a single knot. The curse endures until the cord is severed on the following new moon through a blood reversal rite, after which the cord is burned and the ashes scattered into flowing water.

Quantities

1 six-inch length of black rope or yarn
1 athame, knife, or scissors
1 needle (for reversal)

Directions

  1. This working must be performed under the new moon.
  2. Hold the yarn in your left hand and close your fist around it.
  3. Visualize the pain you wish to inflict upon the target.
  4. Chant the incantation aloud three times, inserting the target's name and pronouns where indicated.
  5. When the chanting is complete, tie a single knot in the centre of the cord while focusing on the pain you wish the target to endure.
  6. The curse lasts until the next new moon, at which point it may be lifted through the reversal rite.

Incantations

Oh goddess, as last resort, hear my cry,
make (target) feel like they are going to die.
Bring them pain, darkness, rot their mind,
make them writhe in agony until the time
the cord be severed, yes, cut in two,
on the day the moon again be new.
With this blood I remove the spell,
but let (target) remember the hell.
With this cut, remove it and take it away,
but let the memory in their mind forever stay.
