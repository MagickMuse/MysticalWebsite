A hearth or cauldron fire working that evokes feelings of happiness and security through the sacred union of male and female primal energies (fire and vessel). Optional botanicals tossed into the flames intensify the blessings.

Quantities

A hearth fire or cauldron fire
Optional: any or all of the following botanicals to toss into the flames:
Angelica
Anise
Pine (needles, resin, or wood)
Rosemary
Wisteria

Directions

  1. Light a fire in your hearth or fireplace.
  2. If you do not have one, light a fire in a cauldron.
  3. Focus on evoking feelings of happiness, security, and domestic blessing.
  4. To intensify the working, toss any or all of the following botanicals directly into the flames: angelica, anise, pine, rosemary, and wisteria.
