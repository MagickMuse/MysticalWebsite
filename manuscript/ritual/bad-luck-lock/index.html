🔒Bad Luck Lock

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged ObjectWriting Spell
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraBinding
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 solid padlock (keyed, not combination) 1 sheet of paper 1 sharp pointed tool (screwdriver or similar) Black paint
◇Directions
1. Write the target's name on a sheet of paper and fold it in half. 2. Pierce the paper through the center using something sharp and pointed, such as a screwdriver. 3. Thread the hasp of a solid keyed padlock through the hole and lock the padlock. 4. Brush a thick layer of black paint over the keyhole and on both sides of the lock. 5. Hold the lock by the hasp while the paint dries and repeat the following incantation: 6. Blackest lock, blackest luck. By this spell, you now are stuck. 7. Bury the lock somewhere in your yard. 8. Bury the key as close to the target's home as possible. 9. If you do not know where they live, bury the key in your own yard but keep it far from the lock.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A simple bad luck spell using a padlock sealed over the target's name. Black paint blocks the keyhole, and the lock and key are buried separately to bind the target in misfortune.
