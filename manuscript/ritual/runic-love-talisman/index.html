Runic Love Bind Rune

A talismanic formula for love magick, utilizing a six-rune inscription with bind rune construction. Follows the standard loading ritual with special emphasis on emotional and sexual force during the formali.

Quantities

A wooden tine, for the bind rune
Carving/rune knife
Coloring pigment, for the bind rune
Optional: a piece of jewelry, for the alternate form

Directions

  1. Follow the standard twelve-step rune tine loading ritual, with two modifications: at Step 3 (Name Carving), etch the names of both persons to be brought together on the reverse side of the tine; at Step 10 (Formali), compose and load a poem of true love and burning lust with passionate sexual energy.
  2. Construct the tine as a bind rune fusing all six staves into a single galdrastafr (optionally using the alternate form of the E-rune), carving and loading each stave separately before binding them together during the coloring phase.
  3. Wear the completed tine yourself to attract the lover, or place it near the desired one, such as under their bed or a threshold they regularly pass through.
  4. Alternatively, etch the full formula onto a piece of jewelry given to the future lover. If secrecy is needed, encode the runes and names using the ættir cipher system (e.g., 3:1 designates the first rune of the third ætt) and perform the loading ritual as usual, strongly visualizing the rune into the numerical symbol.

Rituals to gain the love of another have been one of the principal subjects of runecraft since ancient times. The most successful attempts at runic love magick were performed with sincere intention. This does not stem from any moralistic accretion in the runic system but rather from the complex nature of sexual energies and relationships. Love magick works most effectively, and the variables are kept to a minimum, when the simpler emotion of true love is involved.

The Formula

Numerical Analysis

The rune count is 6 (multiple: 2 x 3). The runic total is 60 (multiple: 6 x 10). See the section on numerical symbolism in Foundations & Ethics for deeper interpretation of these values.

Construction Reference

Alternate Form: Enchanted Jewelry Requirements

This method requires certain conditions:

  • The vitki must know the desired one well enough for the gift to be proper and effective
  • It must be known that the recipient will wear or remain near the object for at least some period of time

The imagination and talent of the runester are the only limits to the ingenuity this type of talisman may reach, but strength in the basic magickal skills and deep familiarity with the runic system are essential.
