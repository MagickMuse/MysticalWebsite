A grounding threshold powder made from a rock taken from the property, charcoal, and benzoin, scattered across all entry points to anchor the practitioner firmly within their home and resist forced eviction. Caution: this spell may prevent departure even if desired, only cast if truly committed to remaining.

Quantities

Rock from the property (underground if possible)
Charcoal
Benzoin (powdered or essential oil)

Directions

  1. Grate a small amount of rock and charcoal into a mortar using a metal grater.
  2. Add the benzoin and work everything together into a consistent mixture.
  3. Scatter this mixture across all thresholds leading outside: every door, gate, window, and opening large enough to permit entry.
  4. Reserve the remaining materials.
  5. Repeat whenever the threat of eviction resurfaces, always using the same rock.
  6. Other ingredients may be replaced as needed, but once the rock is gone, consider it a sign to seek alternatives.
