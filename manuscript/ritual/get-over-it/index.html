🔓Get Over It

To cut energetic ties with painful experiences, close emotional chapters, and reclaim your power through symbolic action and ritual burial.

Quantities

  • 1 stick of incense (fumitory is ideal, but any kind will do)
  • 1 sheet of paper
  • 1 black pen
  • 1 piece of black thread or ribbon
  • 1 small shovel or trowel

Directions

  1. Preparation
  2. Use the black pen to write down your issue(s) succinctly on the paper. This could be phrases like "my marriage," "the accident," "the affair," "my childhood," etc.
  3. If you have more than one issue, list them all on the same sheet.
  4. Raising Power & Incantation
  5. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space.
  6. Light the incense and raise power.
  7. Spend a few minutes reading over the paper, focusing on how these words no longer define you.
  8. Roll the paper into a cylinder and waft it through the incense smoke.
  9. Say aloud:
    Smoke curl, paper furl,
    By the power of fumitory,
    I expel you from my thoughts.
    You have no power over me.
  10. Repeat as many times as needed until you believe it fully.
  11. Symbolic Destruction
  12. Crumple the paper into a ball.
  13. Place it in a doorway so it will be caught between the door and its frame.
  14. Slam the door on it, saying:
    Door jamb, door slam,
    By the power of Janus,
    I shut the door on [insert issue].
    This chapter of my life is closed!
  15. Slam the door multiple times if needed for emotional release.
  16. Bundle & Tie
  17. Remove the paper ball from the doorway.
  18. Tear or shred it into long strips.
  19. Gather and fold the strips, tying them repeatedly with the black thread or ribbon.
  20. This bundle symbolizes the closed chapter.
  21. Ground & Discard
  22. Toss the bundle near the trash or in a corner to affirm its irrelevance.
  23. You may complete this step immediately or at dawn (recommended for fresh starts).
  24. Final Burial
  25. Retrieve the bundle and use the trowel to bury it somewhere you walk often, like your yard or driveway.
  26. Bury it deeply to avoid it being unearthed.
    Alternatives:
  27. If burying isn't an option, flush it (if plumbing allows) or otherwise destroy it using your ingenuity.
    Afterward
  28. Every time you pass the burial spot, say:
    "I am over it." (stand on it)
    "It is behind me." (step past it)
    "I am fine. I am free." (next step)
    "I am moving on." (final step)
  29. Repeat this ritual often at first. Over time, you'll stop needing it. When you forget where you buried it, or even why, you'll know the spell is complete and your healing is real.

Whether you're carrying a failed relationship, a painful childhood, financial devastation, a crime committed against you, an unrealized dream, or any other burden, this spell helps you finally move past it.

Saturn rules endings that pave the way for new beginnings, making Saturday the ideal day to cast this working.

Why Fumitory?

If you can find fumitory incense, use it. This herb has ancient associations with purification and exorcism. Its smoke was believed to drive out unwanted influences and clear spiritual attachments. Any incense will work, but fumitory adds particular power to release work.

Why Janus?

The Roman god Janus guards doorways, transitions, and the passage between what was and what will be. He looks both backward and forward simultaneously. By invoking him while slamming a door on your past, you enlist his aid in sealing that chapter permanently.

The Power of Physical Action

This spell combines spoken word with dramatic physical gesture. The act of slamming a door on your written burden creates a visceral, memorable moment your subconscious cannot ignore. If you need to slam that door repeatedly to release pent-up emotions, do it. The physical release serves the magickal work.

Tearing the paper, binding it, folding it smaller and smaller, then tossing it aside like trash reinforces your new relationship to these old wounds. They are literally becoming smaller, more contained, less significant.

Burial and Completion

Burying the bundle somewhere you walk daily serves two purposes. First, it grounds the energy in the earth, which absorbs and neutralizes it. Second, it creates a physical location for the ongoing affirmation practice.

If burial is impossible (frozen ground, paved surfaces, apartment living), flushing works as an alternative. The key is permanent removal from your space.

The Walking Meditation

The true healing unfolds over time. Each time you cross the burial site, speak the four-step affirmation from the ritual directions above — aloud when alone, silently when others are present.

Signs of Completion

At first, you'll need this ritual every time you pass the spot. Then you'll start forgetting occasionally. Eventually you'll struggle to remember exactly where you buried the bundle, or even why. When the whole thing seems absurd to you, almost embarrassing in its intensity, you'll know the spell has fully manifested.

Forgetting is the goal. Healing means the wound no longer demands your attention.
