A New Moon ritual of fasting, space cleansing, and altar consecration using ancestor photographs, water, and a white candle to honor ancestors.

Quantities

  • Photographs of ancestors (as many as available)
  • 1 glass of water
  • 1 white candle
  • Cleansing water: spring water, Florida water, and/or holy water
  • Optional: sage or frankincense for burning
  • Small opening offering: coffee, incense, or a few coins

Directions

  1. Gather materials in the days before the new moon: photographs of ancestors, a glass of water, and a white candle.
  2. Begin fasting at sundown on the first night the new moon is visible.
  3. Clean the space where the altar will be consecrated using spring water, Florida water, holy water, or a combination. Optional: burn cleansing herbs such as sage or frankincense.
  4. Keep lighting soft and low throughout the night.
  5. Sit with photographs of the ancestors. Allow their presence to be felt. Speak to them directly, sharing memories (for known ancestors) or introducing yourself and expressing desire to build a relationship (for unknown ancestors).
  6. As the sky begins to lighten before dawn, set up the altar. Allow inspiration in this liminal moment to guide final decisions.
  7. At sunrise, greet the dawn and say a morning prayer if applicable.
  8. Make a small opening offering to the ancestors: coffee, incense, or a few coins.

This ritual establishes and consecrates a new ancestor altar. It is timed to the new moon and involves an overnight vigil, beginning at sundown and completing at sunrise.

Timing

Wait until the first night the new moon is visible in the sky. Consult a lunar calendar or watch for it throughout the month.

Communing With the Ancestors

During the overnight vigil, speak to the ancestors directly:

  • For ancestors known in life: Share stories and memories of time spent together.
  • For unknown ancestors: Offer an introduction and express the desire to build a relationship.

Setting the Altar

As the sky begins to lighten before dawn, allow the energy of the liminal moment to guide final decisions about arrangement. Inspiration in this time takes precedence over prior plans.
