🌱Lilies and Myrrh for Gabriel

A petition to Gabriel, Archangel of the Moon and divine messenger, who performs miracles of conception. He is called with myrrh as incense and lilies as offering. Gabriel presides over fertility, conception, pregnancy, and childbirth, and stands directly before God, well placed to carry your petition.

Quantities

Myrrh (resin or incense)
Lilies (fresh, or depicted)

Directions

  1. Burn myrrh as incense and place lilies on your altar or in your space.
  2. Light a white candle if desired.
  3. Address Gabriel directly by name.
  4. State your petition for conception clearly, without embellishment.
  5. Ask him to carry your need to God or to intervene directly in his own capacity as healer of infertility.
  6. In Ethiopian Christian tradition, if Gabriel helps bring a child, the child is named after him in gratitude.

Gabriel is the Angel of the Annunciation who brought news of miraculous conceptions to Mary and to Elizabeth, mother of John the Baptist. His association with conception is not symbolic but direct: he has announced pregnancies, presided over births, and is recorded across Jewish, Christian, Islamic, and Ethiopian traditions as a healer of infertility.

Myrrh is one of the oldest incense offerings in recorded religious history, used across the ancient Near East to call divine attention. Lilies are Gabriel's flower, sacred to him across multiple traditions and tied equally to purity, resurrection, and the announcement of new life.

✨
In Ethiopian Christian tradition, parents who believe Gabriel helped them conceive name the child after him as an act of gratitude. His feast is celebrated on the 19th of every month in the Ethiopian church.
