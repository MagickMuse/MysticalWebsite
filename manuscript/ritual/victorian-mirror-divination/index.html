🪞Victorian Mirror Divination

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
DivinationMirror MagickVisualizationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- One mirror large enough to see yourself clearly - One floral-scented candle - One hairbrush
◇Directions
1. Just before the stroke of midnight, light your floral candle and position it so its flame reflects in the mirror's surface. 2. When midnight arrives, gaze into the mirror and begin brushing your hair with slow, steady strokes. 3. Count silently in your mind to one hundred while maintaining this rhythm. 4. Upon reaching one hundred, continue the brushing motion while whispering these time-honored words repeatedly: 5. "Come my lover, come to me, over river, over sea, over mountains, far or near, in this looking glass appear." 6. When you feel yourself slipping into a dreamlike trance, set down the brush. 7. Press your lips gently to the mirror's surface, then close your eyes. 8. Open your eyes slowly and blink deliberately. 9. In the reflection behind you, you may briefly glimpse the face of the one destined to love you, whether someone you've already encountered or someone yet to meet. 10. If no image appears, close your eyes once more and count rapidly to one hundred. 11. Visualize the candlelit mirror in your mind's eye. 12. Extinguish the candle. 13. Your lover's face will come to you in your thoughts.
◇Description
A midnight mirror scrying ritual to glimpse the face of your future lover. Performed at the threshold hour with candlelight, rhythmic brushing, and an ancient incantation.
