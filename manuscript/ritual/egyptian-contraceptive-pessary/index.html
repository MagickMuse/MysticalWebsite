Egyptian contraceptive method combining physical barrier with magickal ingredients to prevent pregnancy.

Quantities

  • Sour milk
  • Honey
  • 'Crocodile dung' (Ethiopian soil)
    OR for herbal version:
  • Celery
  • Emmer grains
  • Beer
  • Vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Mix sour milk with honey and 'crocodile dung' (likely a code name for Ethiopian soil or similar substance).
  2. Form the mixture into a pessary and insert into the vagina before intercourse.
  3. The ancient Egyptians understood that male ejaculation led to pregnancy, so inserting something to block the sperm seemed worth attempting.
  4. The acidic properties of sour milk may have provided some spermicidal effect.
  5. Alternatively, a herbal contraceptive could be brewed: smoke celery with grains of emmer, then mix with beer and vegetable oil.
  6. Boil the mixture and take it for four mornings in a row.
