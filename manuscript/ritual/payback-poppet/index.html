👤Payback Poppet

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Thread/Sewing MagickHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
2 pieces of black fabric (cut into person shapes) Red thread or string 1 photo or drawing of the target Handful of graveyard dirt Dried patchouli
◇Directions
1. Cut two rough person-shaped pieces from black fabric. 2. Sew them together with red thread or string to form a poppet. 3. Use additional red thread to stitch two X shapes where the eyes would be. 4. Sew the pieces firmly so no contents can leak between the stitches. 5. If using a drawing to represent the target, make it accurate with distinguishing features such as proper hair color, and write their full name on the image. 6. Tear the photo or drawing into small pieces and stuff them all inside the poppet. 7. Add a generous amount of graveyard dirt, taken from anywhere within the cemetery boundaries, along with dried patchouli. 8. Give the poppet a shake to mix the contents, then sew it closed. 9. Bend the two arms together and stitch the hands to each other. 10. Bury the poppet upside down behind your home.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A revenge poppet stuffed with the target's torn image, graveyard dirt, and patchouli, then sewn shut with bound arms and buried upside down behind the practitioner's home.
