Lughnasadh

Craft a dough/clay/mud talisman charged under the August sun to attract prosperity. Incorporate solar herbs and wealth symbols, then program it with Lugh’s blessing.

Quantities

  • Base material: a small amount of clay, salt-dough, or mud (about 1/2 cup)
  • Optional herbs/oils to mix in: cinnamon, rosemary
  • Carving tool: toothpick, twig, or paring knife; protective gloves
  • Baking sheet and oven (for salt-dough or bakeable clay)
  • Optional wealth tokens for charging ring: coins, dollar bills, acorns, apples

Directions

  1. Make the base: If using salt-dough, mix roughly 2 parts flour to 1 part salt, add enough water for dough, and a little vegetable oil to reduce stickiness.
  2. Clay or high-clay mud also works.
  3. Add correspondences: Knead in a small pinch of cinnamon (solar, prosperity, luck) and rosemary (solar, luck) while focusing on wealth and opportunity flowing to you.
  4. Shape and engrave: Form a 2–3 inch disc.
  5. Engrave at center a lion (Leo, ruler of Lughnasadh).
  6. Above the lion add an acorn (wealth and luck).
  7. Around the lion, carve simple wealth marks (pentacles, stars, plus signs, apples, dollar signs).
  8. Add initials, a lucky number, or the word "wealth" if desired.
  9. Harden: Place on a nonstick baking sheet and bake at ~275°F until firm (about 20–45 minutes depending on thickness).
  10. Cool completely.
  11. Program and charge (outdoors under the sun): Create a ring of coins, bills, acorns, apples, or other wealth symbols. Stand within the circle holding the talisman. Feel solar rays and the wealth ring's current pour into the talisman. Say 3 times:
    "By the power of Lugh, by sun and by sky,
    money and riches, to me, will they fly!"
  12. Carry: Keep the talisman on your person through August to magnetize prosperity.
