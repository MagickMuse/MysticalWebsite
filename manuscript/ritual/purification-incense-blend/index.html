Burning incense is a great way to accentuate the magick of any ritual or spell. Blend this incense and light it as you're doing other workings, or just leave it burning in your space as a standalone magickal cleanse.

Quantities

  • All dried herbs: frankincense, lavender, pine, sage, rosemary
  • Fire-safe bowl
  • Charcoal disc

Directions

  1. Use the mortar and pestle to grind your herbs, one at a time, into a powder, releasing their fragrance and their magickal energy.
  2. Combine the ground herbs in a bowl, blending them together by stirring clockwise.
  3. Burn your incense in a fire safe bowl on a charcoal disc.
  4. To purify a room or house, walk in a clockwise direction, carrying the bowl of burning incense, saying, Sacred smoke, cleanse my space.
  5. Sacred smoke, purify this place.
  6. As the smoke rises, visualize it carrying negative energy with it, away from you and off to the universe.
