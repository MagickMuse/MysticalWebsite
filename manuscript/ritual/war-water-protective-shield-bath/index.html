🛁War Water Protective Shield Bath

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
BathWater/Spray
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ProtectionWater MagickCleansing
◇Category

◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
War Water (for the home and the bath) Optional: 7 or 9 bay leaves
◇Directions
1. Before the bath, cleanse the home with War Water, especially the room where you will bathe. 2. Add War Water to the bathwater, along with seven or nine bay leaves if desired. 3. Immerse completely three times. 4. Drain the water and air-dry.
◇Description
A bath that harnesses War Water's iron power as a protective shield against danger of every kind, preceded by a War Water cleansing of the home. ⚠️ War Water Protective Shield Bath is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Lore

War Water, also called the Water of Mars, draws on the power of iron. It is best known for sending notorious and powerful hexes, yet it also raises a strong protective shield that repels danger of all kinds.
