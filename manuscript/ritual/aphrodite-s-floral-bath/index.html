🌱Aphrodite's Floral Bath

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Bath
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Water MagickHerbal MagickDevotional Work
◇Category
Ritual

◇Quantities
Lemon balm (hydrosol, dried botanical, or essential oil with caution) Myrtle (hydrosol, dried botanical, or essential oil) Rose geranium (hydrosol, dried botanical, or essential oil) Roses (hydrosol, dried petals, essential oil, or fresh petals) Warm bathwater
◇Directions
1. Add lemon balm, myrtle, rose geranium, and roses to a warm bath using hydrosols, dried botanicals, or essential oils, or a combination. 2. Float fresh rose petals on top of the water for additional enhancement. 3. Enter the bath as a petition to Aphrodite, focusing on your intention to receive her blessing of fertility and vitality.
◇Description
A floral fertility and aphrodisiac bath petitioning Aphrodite to heal reproductive woes and restore generative vitality. Benefits both partners, though its flowery nature makes it especially suited to women.

Aphrodite governs far more than desire. She heals the reproductive system, restores fertility, and mends what grief or illness has broken. This bath works equally as an aphrodisiac and as a fertility remedy, making it well suited to share between partners.

⚠️
Lemon balm essential oil (also sold as melissa) can be a significant skin irritant. Use the dried herb or hydrosol form instead, especially in a bath. If using the oil, dilute carefully and patch test first.
