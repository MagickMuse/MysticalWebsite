🌙Crescent Moon Candle Wish Party

A when crescent moon can be seen spell for crescent moon candle wish party, each person lighting two silver candles and writing anonymous wishes to read aloud and burn for crescent moon to bring dream within three moons.

Quantities

  • Two silver candles for each person present
  • A large silver central candle
  • An empty pot
  • A large pot of soil in the center
  • Slips of paper and a blue pen for each person

Directions

  1. All the people sit in a circle, surrounding their candles with coins, jewelry, and glittery items.
  2. On the first candle each traces invisibly his or her zodiac sign (as a glyph if you know it) and lights the candle, saying: "Crescent moon, as you do grow, bring the dream I wish for so, within three moons [or another time frame]." Each then lights his or her second candle, repeats the words, and on a slip of paper writes the wish anonymously, putting it in the empty pot.
  3. Each person then chooses a wish from the pot by feel to read aloud and then singes it in the candle and drops it into the pot of soil.
  4. When this is done, all the people recite their own wishes in their minds nine times.
  5. On a prearranged signal, the candles are blown out, except for the central one, which is left burning for the rest of the evening.
  6. Perform when the crescent moon can be seen in the sky.
