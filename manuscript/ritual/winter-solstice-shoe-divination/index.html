🔮Winter Solstice Shoe Divination

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
One shoe
◇Directions
1. On the eve of the winter solstice, stand inside your home with your back to the front door. 2. Throw one shoe backwards over your shoulder. 3. Leave it where it falls without looking at it, ensuring no one disturbs it overnight. 4. The following morning, examine the shoe. 5. If it lands facing away from the door, a move is indicated during the next calendar year. 6. If the toe points inward, no change of residence is forthcoming.
◇Description
A winter solstice divination ritual using a thrown shoe to forecast whether you will change residences during the coming calendar year.
