A name paper hex based on the ancient belief that possessing a true name grants control. The target's name is written on paper, dressed with Commanding Oil, charged with intent, then subjected to a physical action that symbolically injures them, e.g., placed under drums being played to cause headaches, or taped inside cymbals.

Quantities

Paper
Pen
Commanding Oil
Drums, cymbals, or another instrument/physical agent of harm

Directions

  1. Write the name of your target clearly on a piece of paper along with any other identifying information.
  2. Dress it with a drop of Commanding Oil.
  3. Hold it in your hand, close your eyes, and focus your desire.
  4. Do something to the paper to demonstrate injury to the target: placing the name paper under drums while they're played allegedly causes the victim to suffer headaches, and taping it inside a pair of cymbals may produce even stronger effects.
