🎡Wheel of Fortune Property Wealth

A Wheel of Fortune card ritual for making money in real estate, using tarot cards with ten coins to manifest property wealth over ten years through the Wheel's turning.

Quantities

  • The Wheel of Fortune card in the center of a vertical row with the Ace of Pentacles at the bottom and the Ten of Pentacles at the top
  • 10 golden-colored coins

Directions

  1. No tarot knowledge is needed, but use a pack with illustrated minor cards Ace to Ten.
  2. Touch the Ace, saying: "From small beginnings do great things grow, I move toward wealth though it may be slow." Put the first coin down to start a circle around the Wheel of Fortune, saying: "Year by year will my properties increase, more acquisitions without cease." Touch the Ten of Pentacles, saying: "Within ten years I will attain, prosperity through property as more I gain." Repeat the spell cycle, touching the Ace again, saying the first set of spell words, then add another coin clockwise, repeating the second set of spell words.
  3. Touch the Ten and say the third set of spell words once more.
  4. Continue the spell until you have ten coins in the circle.
  5. Set the Ace and the Ten on either side of the Wheel.
  6. Finally, bury the coins in front of your first property acquisition.
