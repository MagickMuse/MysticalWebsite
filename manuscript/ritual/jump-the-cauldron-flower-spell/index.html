💖Jump the Cauldron Flower Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ManifestationEmpowerment
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
Cauldron Flowers
◇Directions
1. Fill the cauldron with flowers to represent fertility. 2. Hold hands and jump over the cauldron together, to be transformed from two into one.
◇Description
A marriage rite invoking the transformative power of the cauldron, as wielded by Cerridwen and Medea, to unite two people into one couple.
