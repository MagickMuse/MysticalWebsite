👻Night of Hekate Spirit Contact

◇Form
Altar WorkingOffering Rite
◇Method
Ancestor WorkDivinationDevotional WorkProtection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 3 black candles (arranged in a triangle)n- Offerings for Hekate: garlic, eggs, honey, or dark breadn- Offerings for ancestors: their favorite foods or drinksn- Divination tool (tarot, pendulum, or scrying mirror)n- Protection amulet or black tourmaline
◇Directions
1. Set up your altar with three candles forming a triangle, Hekate's sacred number. 2. Place offerings for her at the center.nnCast your circle, paying special attention to protection. 3. Call upon Hekate as guardian between the worlds.nnLight each candle, saying: 'Hekate Trivia, Queen of Ghosts, open the gates between worlds. 4. Protect me as I seek communion with those who have crossed.'nnPlace your protection amulet or stone on your person.nnSet out offerings for your ancestors beside Hekate's offerings.nnUsing your divination tool, open yourself to messages from the other side. 5. Ask specific questions or simply invite communication.nnRecord any messages received.nnWhen finished, thank Hekate and your ancestors. 6. Ask Hekate to close the gates and ensure any spirits return to their proper place.nnLeave the offerings at a crossroads after the ritual, or dispose of them respectfully.
◇Description
A ritual for the Night of Hekate to communicate with ancestors and spirits under the protection and guidance of Hekate, Queen of Ghosts.
