🕯️Harvest Moon

A reflective poem about gathering beneath the Harvest Moon, laughing with the Lady, and finding the spiral of the Wheeled Years in a snail shell.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. A contemplative poem for the Harvest Moon:
  2. Clean hair, waist long, blond and streaked in silvery shades;
    All blowzy blown in willow scramble, freeing from bindweed clutches,
    with twiggings plucked victoriously from strands in disarray.
    Ages spanned in looking back, seeing me in stained party best, making grass bird's nests for arriving guests.
    Naught's changed, there. Aye, that's me, now gathering feathers 'neath this Harvest Moon;
    Laughing with the Lady, trodding squishy lawn at pond's sodden edge,
    Oh look! A coiling snail shell gleaming back at me; its turnings as the Wheeled Years.
