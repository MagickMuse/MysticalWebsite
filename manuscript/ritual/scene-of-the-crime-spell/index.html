🕷️Scene of the Crime Spell

A spell invoking Hekate at a crime scene to bring the perpetrator to justice. Cast at night, best on a Saturday. Uses a spider web pattern and vinegar purification.

Quantities

Pencil
Three candles
Black yarn or ribbon
Optional: item touched by the criminal
Incense
Representational spider
Black pepper
Vinegar
Eggs and onions (for the offering)

Directions

  1. Use the pencil to carve or write the words "the criminal" into each of the candles; it is fine if the words are difficult to make out.
  2. Use the pieces of black yarn or ribbon to construct an image that represents a spider's web; the more serious the crime, or the farther away the criminal is suspected to be, the longer the pieces should be.
  3. Gather all of the spell ingredients except the pencil, and bring them to the crime scene at night, ideally on a Saturday.
  4. Get into a magickal mood and arrange the spider web on the ground, as close as possible to the spot where the crime is thought to have taken place.
  5. Position the candles upright in a triangle at the center of the web. If you have something the criminal touched, place it in the middle of the triangle, or near the web if it is large. Arrange the incense near the candles, and place the spider on the web.
  6. Raise power. Work widdershins to pour the black pepper into a circle around the web, visualizing the criminal being snared in the web and brought to justice.
  7. Light the candles and the incense, watch them burn for a bit, and meditate on what happened at the scene.
  8. Focus on justice catching up with the criminal and recite the incantation.
  9. While the candles and incense continue burning, pour or sprinkle vinegar in every part of the crime scene that feels as though it needs to be purified, visualizing the criminal being caught and held accountable. Repeat the spell if that feels necessary.
  10. Once the candles and incense have burned down, ground the power that you raised. If possible, leave everything at the crime scene overnight and clean up the area the next morning.

Hekate (pronounced "heh-ka-tay") is a powerful goddess whose dominion includes crime scenes, so she is present in places where crimes have been committed. When a crime is particularly unjust or gruesome, or remains unsolved, her presence may linger in that place for many years.

This spell can be cast at the scene of any crime whose perpetrator has not yet been caught. It is worded for a male criminal, but you can alter the wording if a female or a group of perpetrators is suspected.

A Note About Hekate

It is said that Hekate is likely to give you what you need, not necessarily what you ask for. You may never learn what outcome your spell had, but you can be sure that Hekate is working to help you. She is a very active goddess in the modern world, and invoking her may form a permanent connection between you. Be aware of these things before you decide to use this spell.

The Incantation

Great Mother Hekate,
Hear me, hear me, hear me,
In this place that was defiled
By the one who is reviled.
Seek him, find him,
Catch him, bind him!
Dark Mother Hekate,
Hear me, hear me, hear me.
Let the one sought
By justice be caught.
Hold him fast
In bonds that last.
Great Mother Hekate,
Seek him, find him,
Catch him, bind him!
So mote it be!

Afterward

The gods have their own sense of time, so be patient while you wait for this spell to manifest. Be mindful that justice can take other forms besides those that the criminal justice system metes out.

To thank Hekate for hearing your request, offer her the eggs and onions that were left overnight at the crime scene by taking them to a crossroads where three roads meet and leaving them beside the road for her on the night of a Full Moon. Do not take these offerings home or keep them in your car, as that may forge an unwanted link between you and the criminal.
