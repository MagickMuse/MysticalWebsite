📿Rainwater Mistletoe Spell

Solitary

◇Directions
1. Collect rainwater and chill in the refrigerator. 2. Place mistletoe in a white or blue bag. 3. Soak the bag in the chilled rainwater. 4. Squeeze out excess moisture and hang the bag in your home to dry. 5. It now serves as a protective amulet against fires.
◇Description
Mistletoe is soaked in chilled rainwater to create a protective amulet against fire. The charm harnesses the magickal properties of both rain and the sacred mistletoe plant.

Mistletoe

This spell attempts to harness the power of torrential downpours as a standing protective force in the home.

  1. Collect rainwater and let it chill in the refrigerator.
  2. Place mistletoe in a white or blue bag.
  3. Soak the bag in the rainwater.
  4. Squeeze out excess moisture and hang the bag in your home to dry. It will serve as a protective amulet against fires.

Other Fire-Prevention Amulets

Other objects said to prevent house fires include:

  • A resident happy, content tri-colored cat
  • An image or statue of Maneki Neko, the Beckoning Cat
  • Dried shed snakeskin hanging from the roof

Safety Note

Mistletoe berries and leaves are toxic. Use only the woody portions. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
