🌱Romany Threshold and Hearth Blessing

◇Quantities
Breadcrumbs A new baby (first homecoming)
◇Directions
1. At the moment the new baby enters the home for the first time, the mother carries the baby and lays it on the threshold. 2. She picks it up again, carries it to the hearth or the home's main heat source, and lays it down there. 3. She then lays the baby in every corner of every room in the house. 4. Each time the baby is placed down, beginning at the threshold, the father sprinkles breadcrumbs in a circle around it. 5. Throughout, both parents hold a vision of a home full of love and happy children. 6. Do not sweep up or vacuum the breadcrumbs for at least 24 hours.
◇Description
A Romani preventive rite performed the moment a new baby enters the home for the first time, sealing each threshold, corner, and hearth with breadcrumb circles to protect the family's ongoing fertility. ⚠️ This is a Romani working. Romani spiritual practices are closed and should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture.
