🕯️Nine-Day Candle Novena

🕯️Candle

A nine-consecutive-evening candle working for Evil Eye removal, drawing on the novena form that predates Roman Catholicism. A fresh candle is burned each evening over private recitation and focused intention. Each morning the hardened wax remnants are cast out as far from the home as possible. One of the Evil Eye Removal methods.

Quantities

A fresh candle, for each of nine evenings

Directions

  1. Burn a fresh candle each evening for nine evenings running.
  2. As it burns, speak quietly over it, recite sacred verses, voice your intentions, and charge the candle with your will.
  3. Each morning, gather whatever wax remains and throw it as far from your home as you can manage.
