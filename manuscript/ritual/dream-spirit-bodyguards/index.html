🛡️Dream Spirit Bodyguards

◇Solitary or Group

◇Quantities
Images of guardian spirits
◇Directions
1. Arrange images of the listed guardian spirits where they can oversee the sleeping space. 2. The images themselves carry protective power, but speaking aloud to each one, describing fears and intentions clearly, strengthens the bond and deepens the protection. 3. If nightmares appear connected to spiritual attack, pair this working with broader protective measures.
◇Description
Images of guardian spirits stationed in the bedroom provide a protective presence throughout the night. Speaking to them directly, naming specific fears and what help is needed, adds depth to the working beyond what the image alone provides.
