🧹Rosemary Anisette Cleansing

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
CleansingEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Rosemary stalks: several Anisette (liqueur): enough to soak the stalks
◇Directions
1. Soak several rosemary stalks in anisette. 2. Have the person to be cleansed lie down comfortably on a floor, bed, or massage table. 3. Begin with a gentle hand massage only, moving up from the palms and soles of the feet. 4. Use the fingers to trace sacred or protective symbols, such as crosses or pentacles, rhythmically over the body. 5. Remove the rosemary stalks from the liqueur, shake gently, and use them to lightly continue the massage. 6. While working, call on your chosen spirits for assistance and firmly direct any unwanted energies to leave.
◇Description
A two-person cleansing that works through gentle massage and the healing properties of rosemary and anisette. The person being cleansed focuses on their intentions, prayers, or petitions while the practitioner does the work. The massage is light and aura-focused rather than physical therapy.
