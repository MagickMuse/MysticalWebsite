An ancient Assyrian healing formula in which an anonymous clay or wax figurine is used to lure a disease spirit out of an ailing person and into the doll, which is then disposed of far from home. Requires a third party to communicate with the disease spirit on the patient's behalf. Both parties must undergo extensive cleansing afterward.

Quantities

Clay or wax (enough to form a figurine)
Disposal site far from home

Directions

  1. Create a clay or wax figurine.
  2. It must look anonymous and should not resemble the patient or anyone else.
  3. A person other than the patient is appointed to communicate with the Disease Spirit.
  4. The goal is to entice the illness to leave the human and enter the doll instead.
  5. Consider carefully how to accomplish this before beginning.
  6. Lesser disease spirits are not overly blessed with intelligence and can often be tricked.
  7. As soon as it becomes apparent that the disease demon has entered the doll, immediately remove the doll from the premises and dispose of it far from home.
  8. Return by a circuitous route.
  9. Both the patient and the communicator must afterward undergo extensive cleansing and protective rituals.
