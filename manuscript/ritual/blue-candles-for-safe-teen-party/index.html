An hour or two before party begins spell for blue background color harmonious and safe party for teenagers at your home, lighting six soft-blue candles saying bring harmony may party pass happily safely joyfully then blowing out and tying six pale-blue ribbons together as decoration.

Quantities

  • Six soft-blue candles, set around the main party room
  • Six pale-blue ribbons, one in front of each candle

Directions

  1. Light each candle in turn, saying: "Bring harmony, may this party pass happily, safely, and joyfully. May [name teenager(s)] and my home be enfolded in security." Let the candles burn until just before the party, then blow them out, saying the spell words for each.
  2. Tie the ribbons together, repeating the spell words five times, and hang them as a decoration in the party room.
  3. Perform an hour or two before the party begins.
