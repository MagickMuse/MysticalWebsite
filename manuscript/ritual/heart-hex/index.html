🫀Heart Hex

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
PoppetJar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickBindingEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Cow or sheep heart (from a butcher) Nine slips of brown paper 18 pins or needles (two per slit, crossed) Baker's string or cord Glass jar Equal parts pure grain alcohol and target's favorite beverage (whiskey or absinthe if unknown) Nine black candles
◇Directions
1. Obtain a cow or sheep heart from a butcher. 2. Make nine slits in it. 3. Write the target's name on nine slips of brown paper and place one in each slit. 4. Close each slit with a pair of crossed pins or needles, eighteen total. 5. Wrap the heart with baker's string or cord. 6. Blend equal parts pure grain alcohol with the target's favorite beverage, using whiskey or absinthe if unsure. 7. Place the heart in a jar and cover it with the alcohol. 8. Burn one black candle in its entirety on top of the jar every night for nine nights, nine candles total. 9. When the spell is complete, dispose of all spell articles and remnants far from your home and return via a circuitous route.
◇Description
A British hex using a cow or sheep heart from a butcher as a poppet substitute. Nine slits are made in the heart, the target's name is written on nine slips of brown paper placed in each slit, closed with crossed pins, wrapped in cord, submerged in alcohol in a jar, and a black candle is burned on top each night for nine nights. Allegedly causes 'heart trouble,' whether metaphorically or literally. Ancestor of New Orleans-style courtcase beef-tongue spells.
