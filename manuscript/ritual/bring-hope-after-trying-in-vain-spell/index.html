🌿Bring Hope After Trying in Vain Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
ManifestationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Ingredients to make pastry or dough
◇Directions
1. Sit on fresh green grass with your hands and feet firmly planted on the ground and say: "Mother Earth, I take in your fertility, I absorb your loving power that granted this infant will be." Return indoors and make a small pastry or dough roll, decorating the top with three figures, with the smallest in the middle. 2. As you make it, say the same words three times. 3. Eat the bread or pastry while sitting on the grass and say: "Mother Earth, I have taken within myself your fertility, I ask only that you grant me/us a healthy baby soon." Scatter the crumbs for the birds and if possible spend the day close to nature. 4. Perform a bright dry day.
◇Description
A bright dry day spell to bring hope after trying in vain, sitting on grass to absorb Mother Earth fertility then making pastry figures to take fertility within.
