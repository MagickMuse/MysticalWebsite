🌱Fertility

A Hungarian Romani fertility cure in which a woman and her partner eat gathered spider webs together. Spider webs are also called gossamer thread, reputedly spun by fairies, and consuming them transfers their magickal potency to the couple. ⚠️ This is a Romani working. Romani spiritual practices are closed and should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Spider webs (gathered fresh)

Directions

  1. The woman gathers spider webs.
  2. Both she and her male partner eat the webs together.
  3. The gossamer quality of the webs connects them to fairy-spun thread and its hidden fertility power.

Spider webs are called gossamer thread, a poetic name rooted in the belief that they are spun by fairies. Consuming them draws that fertility-charged substance into the body.

⚠️
Romani spiritual practices are closed. This working belongs to Romani tradition and should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture.
