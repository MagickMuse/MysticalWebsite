You may prefer magick that doesn't scream, look at me, I'm doing spells! at the family or next door neighbors. If that's the case, home decor crafts are your best friend! Make this pretty protective wreath to prevent danger and discomfort from entering your home. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Grapevine wreath
  • Fresh herbs
  • Ribbons
  • Bows
  • Flowers

Directions

  1. Use the grapevine wreath as a base and weave your fresh herbs into the stem securely.
  2. Start at the top of the wreath and work around in a clockwise direction.
  3. As you do say, say, Circle around, circle round, my home is safe from the roof to the ground.
  4. Decorate your wreath with ribbons, bows, flowers, and other goodies and hang it in your home near the front door.
  5. Once the herbs have dried, remove them and save them for use in other protective workings.
