A coffin box spell adapted from classic hexing traditions to bury addictions and bad habits rather than harm enemies. A personalized doll is sealed inside a hand-crafted coffin with banishing ingredients and Banishing Oil, then buried in a cemetery or left at a doorstep.

Quantities

  • Small box (coffin-shaped; Día de los Muertos handicrafts are a good source of inspiration)
  • Black paint
  • Purple glitter glue (optional)
  • Small doll
  • Pins
  • Candle stubs and leftover spell wax
  • Graveyard dirt
  • Asafoetida
  • Banishing powder (Lost and Away Powder especially recommended)
  • Banishing Oil

Directions

  1. A coffin box spell adapted from classic hexing traditions to bury addictions and bad habits rather than harm enemies.
  2. A personalized doll is sealed inside a hand-crafted coffin with banishing ingredients and Banishing Oil, then buried in a cemetery or left at a doorstep.

Directions

  1. Create a small coffin. Find or make a little box — the imagery must be unambiguous. Inspiration or a ready-made box may come from Día de los Muertos (Mexican Day of the Dead) handicrafts.
  2. Paint the outside of the box black. Decorate with purple glitter glue if desired.
  3. Place a small doll inside. The doll may be personalized to represent your addiction — consider what form that personification takes for you.
  4. Accompany and reinforce this spell with intensive candle burning.
  5. Pierce the doll with pins if desired. Add candle stubs, leftover spell wax, graveyard dirt, asafoetida, and banishing powder — Lost and Away Powder is especially recommended.
  6. Dress everything with Banishing Oil.
  7. Seal the box and either bury it in a cemetery or leave it at the doorstep of the person or place associated with the habit.

You may find the act of making the box therapeutic in itself. If so, reserve it for ritual use or destroy it once its purpose is served.
