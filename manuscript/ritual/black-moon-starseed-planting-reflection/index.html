🌑Black Moon Starseed Planting Reflection

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationLunar
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- No materials required
◇Directions
1. Close your eyes and take several slow, deep breaths. 2. Let your body settle and your mind go still. 3. Visualize yourself standing before a vast, open field of dark soil. 4. The sky above is deep and moonless, but alive with stars, a thousand points of light twinkling overhead, as if the sky itself is full of seeds waiting to be born. 5. Bend down and touch the earth. 6. Feel it: dark, moist, rich, and ready. 7. This is the most fertile ground you have ever known. 8. Breathe in the energy of this place, the alignment of sun and moon, the hush of the void before something new begins. 9. You have been given an extra lunation, a rare gift of more time and more power to bring something important into the world. 10. Sit with these questions, letting each one breathe before moving to the next: What energy is the black moon repeating for you? 11. How might you rework or refine an intention that hasn't fully taken hold? 12. What in your life is asking for a second chance? 13. Is there anything you want to rebirth in a new and different way? 14. What intention could use a second burst of supportive lunar energy? 15. If this black moon falls in a particular sign's season, what does that sign's energy have to teach you right now? 16. What does this season's moon offer you? 17. Once you have settled on what you are planting, cup your hands in front of you. 18. Visualize a soft light appearing there: a glowing gemstone, a tiny shooting star, a seed of pure intention radiating from your palms. 19. Slowly, reach down and press that light into the dark soil. 20. Cover it gently. 21. Know that as the moon builds toward fullness, so will your intention take root, grow, and be illuminated. 22. When you are ready, open your eyes and carry this energy into your spellwork.
◇Description
A grounding visualization for the black moon, the rare second new moon that offers an extra-fertile window to replant intentions that haven't yet taken root. Standing before a field of dark, star-lit soil, you reflect on what needs a second chance, then plant a seed of light that will grow as the moon does.
