🔮A Prayer before Divination

Your divination tool of choice
◇Directions
1. Speak this prayer aloud before beginning the reading. 2. If seeing multiple querents in one session, repeat it before each new person. 3. Do not repeat it if the same querent asks multiple questions in a row. 4. I call out to all of my ancestors, saints, and guides; all you beings of light who have chosen to walk with me in this life or in this moment. I give thanks for your clear guidance and continued presence in my life. 5. I pray that you will reach out right now to the ancestors, saints, and guides of [querent's name], and convey to me through this [divination tool] the guidance that they wish to provide to their child in this moment. 6. I pray that you will bless my sight, so I will see clearly; and bless my words, so that they will be the perfect medicine for [querent] in this moment. 7. I pray that this medicine will be received with an open heart and mind, that it will be processed over time and integrated fully. 8. And I pray that if any message conveyed tonight is not the perfect medicine for [querent] in this moment, that it will be forgotten, and I will be forgiven. Amen. 9. The word "child" (or "son" or "daughter") in the phrase "their child in this moment" carries particular weight and should not be omitted. 10. Specific ancestors, saints, or guides who serve as patrons of your divination practice may be named in the opening invocation.
◇Description
A prayer spoken before a divination session, invoking the practitioner's ancestors, saints, and spirit guides to assist in delivering clear and healing guidance to the person being read for. The prayer frames divination not as fortune-telling but as a healing act, with the reading understood as medicine for the querent. It includes a petition for the practitioner's own humility and a request for forgiveness if anything spoken falls short of what is truly needed. If reading multiple querents in one session, the prayer is repeated before each one.
