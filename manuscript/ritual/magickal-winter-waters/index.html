A collection of charged and energized waters created through exposure to specific celestial events, weather patterns, and winter phenomena. These magickally potent waters can be used throughout the year for anointing, consecration, ritual work, and spellcraft.

Quantities

  • Clean, clear glass jar(s)
  • Drinkable water (for charging)
  • Container(s) for collecting rainwater, snow, or dew
    Note: The quantity collected depends on your available containers and the winter weather in your area.

Directions

  1. Methods for Creating Magickal Winter Waters:
    Celestial Charging:
  2. Fill a clean, clear glass jar with drinkable water.
  3. Expose the water to:
  4. Winter Solstice sunrise or moonrise
  5. Other winter celestial occasions that are important to you
  6. Leave the jar out for several hours to charge.
  7. Waters charged under an eclipse are especially powerful.
    Natural Collection:
  8. Collect dew on the solstice or during the winter (if possible in your area).
  9. If it rains or snows during the Winter Solstice, collect the rainwater or snow for later use.
  10. Collect rain or snow that falls during a severe winter storm.
  11. Break icicles, melt at room temperature, and save.
    Uses Throughout the Year:
  12. Anoint tools, charms, poppets, or other magickal items
  13. Dress candles or altar items
  14. Sprinkle on plants or trees that need a magickal boost
  15. Use in ritual as an offering, for water sharing, or for the 'ale' in cakes and ale
  16. Brew charged waters into teas
    Variation:
    Save containers of snow or whole icicles in your freezer. When spring arrives, use them in a ritual designed to bid farewell to winter.
