🌳To Cause An Enemy To Lose A Limb

⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A sympathetic magick spell to cause an enemy to lose a specific limb. A dried tree branch is named for the target and a specific limb is named for the body part to be lost. The limb is cut and buried with a personal effect. Psalm 70 may be recited as an alternative or addition.

Quantities

1 dried tree branch with limbs
1 knife or cutting tool
1 personal effect from the target

Directions

  1. Locate a dried tree branch.
  2. Name the branch for the target and name one of its limbs for the exact body part you want the target to lose (right arm, left leg, etc.).
  3. Cut off the limb that represents the chosen body part.
  4. Bury the severed limb with a personal effect from the target.
  5. Alternatively, recite Psalm 70 over the severed limb, naming the target, so that they wither away just as the branch withers.

Psalm 70 (Alternate Incantation)

Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.
Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt.
Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.
Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.
But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.
