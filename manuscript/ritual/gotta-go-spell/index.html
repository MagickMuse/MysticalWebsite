🍊Gotta Go Spell

◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Method
BanishingVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 pen - 1 citrus stripper or vegetable peeler - 1 orange
◇Directions
1. A friend starts to change and is no longer welcome in the friends' group? 2. Poly partner picking fights? 3. Write their name on an orange with a pen. 4. Orange's magick includes authority, action, ambition, and focus. 5. Carve all traces of the name from the orange using the citrus stripper. 6. Start with the first letter of their name, going in order, while seeing their influence on your lives coming to an end. 7. Throw the peel outside somewhere off your property: in a planter, in the trash, or under a bush for the nutrients. 8. Peel the rest of the orange and eat it, thinking about good times ahead. 9. If others are impacted by this person's wrongdoing, feel free to get them involved. 10. If they aren't magickal practitioners, offer to share sections of the orange with them to support acceptance, emotional clarity on the issue, or attraction for a new person in the group, whether platonic or romantic.
◇Description
A citrus-based banishing spell for when someone is no longer welcome in your friend group or poly situation. Uses orange magick for authority, action, ambition, and focus to end their influence.
