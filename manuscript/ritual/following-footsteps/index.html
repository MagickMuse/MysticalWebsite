◇Directions

Create Sacred Space: Get into a magickal mindset. Light the candle and incense. Raise energy. 2. Focus & Incantation: Visualize yourself working successfully in your desired field. Charge the Tape: One by one, hold each tape roll in the incense smoke and mentally charge it with your intention. Drip a bit of wax from the orange candle onto each to seal them. 4. Why This Spell Works

Some careers are notoriously difficult to break into. Glamorous industries like fashion, broadcasting, and entertainment attract so many applicants that competition for every opening is fierce. Other fields like spaceflight, ballet, or surgery require years of highly specialized training. Still others, like politics, often require support or financial backing from established players. The barriers to entry can feel insurmountable even when you have genuine talent and determination.

This spell will not make you successful at something you are unsuited for or unqualified to do. What it will do is help you find your way into an industry where you belong but cannot seem to gain entry. It aligns your energy with those who have already succeeded in that field.

The Magick of Footprints

Footprint magick appears in traditions across the world and throughout history. Practitioners traditionally worked this magick by physically lifting dirt from someone's footprint. In our modern paved world, we adapt this ancient technique using adhesive tape to capture the energetic imprint of footsteps.

Choosing Your Collection Site

Think carefully about where to collect your footprints. You want the footsteps of people actually doing the work you aspire to do, not just anyone who happens to pass through.

For example:

  • If you want to be a stage actor, collect footprints near the stage door rather than the theater's main entrance
  • If you want to be a judge, collect footprints outside a judges' entrance or private elevator rather than the public courthouse steps
  • If you want to be a firefighter, collect footprints near the firehouse bay doors
  • If you want to be a surgeon, collect footprints near hospital staff entrances

The more specific your collection site, the more focused your spell will be.

Filtering the Energy

When you press tape to the ground, it picks up traces of everyone who has walked there. After lifting each strip, hold it to the wind (or blow on it yourself if the air is still) and visualize the footprints of passersby, delivery workers, and others being blown away. Only the footprints of those in your chosen profession remain energetically imprinted on the tape.

Roll each strip into a cylinder with the sticky side inward to seal in the energy. Repeat until you have several rolls.

Why Minerva and Apollo

The incantation invokes two deities associated with wisdom, skill, and achievement. Minerva (the Roman Athena) governs wisdom, strategic thinking, and crafts. Apollo governs the arts, healing, prophecy, and light. Together they represent both the intellectual and creative aspects of career success.

Working the Spell Afterward

Keep your sealed container somewhere safe. Take it out and handle it whenever you are actively pursuing your goal: applying for jobs, taking courses, attending networking events, or practicing your craft. The container becomes a touchstone for your intention.

Once you have established yourself in your chosen career, you may discard the container. The spell has done its work.
