🐾St. Francis Protecting Wildlife

◇Form
Circle RiteOffering Rite
◇Method
InvocationChant/MantraPetitionDirectional Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- Clearing surrounded by circle of trees - Natural food suitable for small animals and birds - 1 basket or pouch for food
◇Directions
1. Walk around the inside of the tree circle clockwise, making smaller and smaller circles as you approach the center, scattering food and saying rhythmically, "Good St. Francis, do your part, take all creatures to your heart. The young, the old, the sick, the lame, fierce, or gentle to you are the same." When you reach the center, scatter the rest of the food in all directions and stamp three times with each foot, saying, "Creatures of the ocean, creatures of the sea, creatures of the hills and plains by Francis protected be." Sit in the center, naming creatures or species who need help and habitats under threat. 2. Walk out in counterclockwise circles, whispering as you leave, "Good St. Francis, do your part. You hold all creatures in your heart, now and always." Perform early on a Saturday.
◇Description
A Saturday morning spiral walking ritual in tree circle scattering food to invoke St. Francis's protection for all wild creatures. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
