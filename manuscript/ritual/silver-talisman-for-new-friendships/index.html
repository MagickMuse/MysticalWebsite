🤍Silver Talisman for New Friendships

◇Form
Charged ObjectAltar Working
◇Method
Ancestor WorkPetitionDreamworkMeditation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
One piece of silver (jewelry or coin, new or already owned)
◇Directions
1. While the moon is waxing, take a piece of silver, jewelry or a coin, to your ancestor altar. 2. It can be an old piece you already own or something new you acquired recently. 3. Spend a moment with your ancestors and ask them how to be a good friend. 4. Depending on how much you have practiced mediumship, the answer may come right away. 5. If not, simply wait, clear your mind, and listen. 6. The answer may also come to you in a dream that night. 7. The next day, wear or keep the silver on your person and go out into the world. 8. When you meet a new friend, ask them how to be a good friend to them specifically. 9. As your friendship grows deeper, continue to ask how you can be a better friend to them. 10. Focus on doing right by people so you are ready when the right people show up in your life. 11. Short friendships can be beautiful too, and just because a friendship ends poorly does not mean the beginning and middle were not worthwhile.
◇Description
A waxing moon spell to attract genuine friendship. Charges a piece of silver at the ancestor altar while asking for guidance on how to be a good friend. The silver is worn the next day as a talisman while going out into the world.
