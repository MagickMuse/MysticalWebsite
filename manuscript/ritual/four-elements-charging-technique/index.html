🕯️Four Elements Charging Technique

A method for charging and consecrating magickal tools using the four elements: earth, air, fire, and water. This ritual transforms mundane objects into magickal implements through elemental blessing.

Quantities

  • Sea salt
  • Small bowl of water (or ocean water)
  • Incense (any purifying scent)
  • Matches or lighter
  • Cloth for drying metal tools

Directions

  1. Mix a small amount of sea salt into the water.
  2. Sprinkle the salt water on your tool as you say: "With earth and water I charge you to do my will." Light the incense and hold your tool in the smoke for a few moments while you say: "With fire and air I charge you to do my will." If your tool is metal, dry it thoroughly and rub it down after the salt water application to prevent tarnishing or corrosion.
  3. Your tool is now charged and consecrated for magickal use.
