Bloodstone, sacred to Isis, is recommended for menstrual problems by many healers including Hildegard of Bingen. Worn around the neck or most powerfully suspended around the hips. Other red stones (cornelian, red coral, rubies, garnets) are also beneficial, string them on red cord or silver chain long enough to wear as a belt.

Quantities

Bloodstone or red stones (cornelian, red coral, ruby, garnet)
Red cord or silver chain (belt length)

Directions

  1. Wear bloodstone around the neck or, most powerfully, suspended around the hips.
  2. Alternatively, string red stones or beads on red cord or a silver chain long enough to wear as a belt.
  3. Cornelian, red coral, rubies, and garnets are all suitable.
