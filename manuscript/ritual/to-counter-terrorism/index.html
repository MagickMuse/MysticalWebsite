🕊️To Counter Terrorism

A responsive ritual with world map, tree incense moved counterclockwise nine times, red candle for danger and white for peace.

Quantities

  • 1 map of the world (set flat on table)
  • 1 small tree fragrance incense stick in holder
  • 1 small red candle
  • 1 taller white candle

Directions

  1. Light the red candle and from it the incense, saying, "May there be peace and safety throughout the whole world and may the innocent of every land and belief be protected." Name the place where there is currently the most danger or where danger will affect you the most, then move the incense around the outside of the map, an inch or two above it, counterclockwise nine times, saying each time, "Take away harm, take away danger. Bring peace and safety to kin and to stranger." Return the incense to its holder to burn.
  2. Light the white candle from the red one, saying, "May the light of peace and safety shine throughout the world." Extinguish the red candle, but leave the white one burning.
  3. Perform whenever there is a terrorist threat or attack or you must travel to a dangerous region.
