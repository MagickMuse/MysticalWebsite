🐰Ostara

A cleansing shower or bath ritual using salt scrub to physically enact the shedding of what no longer serves. Inspired by Black Snake's periodic skin shedding, this practice ritualizes the mental shift required for growth, moving it from thought into physical embodiment.

Quantities

  • Sea salt or bath scrub (a few cups)

Directions

  1. In order for Black Snake to grow, she must shed her skin.
  2. Unlike human skin, snake skin isn't stretchy enough to allow her to expand.
  3. So, a few times a year, Snake will shed.
  4. During these times, a film protects her eyes leaving her temporarily blind and vulnerable.
  5. Growth and change often make us feel this way, too.
  6. Like Snake you may need to nest when you're shedding.
  7. You may feel metaphorically blind and unable to make smart decisions.
  8. Allow yourself space to feel into your new skin.
  9. We humans initiate change with a mental shift.
  10. Ritualizing this shift allows us to move from the realm of pure thought and into the realm of the physical.
  11. When you begin to feel like you're outgrowing your current skin, bring a few cups of sea salt or a yummy bath scrub with you into the shower.
  12. As you scrub your skin, sloughing off old cells, think about what you need to release.
  13. Picture it being loosened by the salt so it can slide down the drain.
  14. Imagine what no longer serves you being washed away.
  15. After Snake slides free of her constricting scales, she continues to expand until her outer casing chafes... and she begins the process again.
