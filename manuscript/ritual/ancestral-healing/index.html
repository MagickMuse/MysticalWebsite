Connect with your well and wise ancestors, release inherited trauma, and clear ancestral patterns at the soul and cellular level.

Quantities

  • 1 white candle
  • 1 black candle
  • 2 candle holders
  • 1 lighter or matches
  • 1 pen
  • 1 small scrap of paper
  • 1 pair of tongs or tweezers (for handling burning paper)
  • 1 fire-safe dish

Directions

  1. Prepare the space however you preferL dim lights, light candles, or play soft music.
    Cleanse the ritual area using incense, bells, crystals, or by visualizing white light expanding from your solar plexus into a protective sphere.
  2. Set your intention to bless and separate this sacred space from the mundane world.
    Sit comfortably, breathe deeply, and speak aloud:
    “Ancestors of blood and bone… Ancestors of heart… Ancestors of spirit… I greet you and welcome you into this sacred space.”
    State your full name aloud, followed by your known lineage using this format:
    “I am [Your Name], child of [Parent 1] and [Parent 2], grandchild of [Grandparents’ Names], great-grandchild of [Great-grandparents’ Names]…”
  3. Only include names you feel comfortable honoring, your well and wise ancestors will recognize you regardless.
  4. Speak aloud:
    “Well and wise ancestors near and far… I remember you, I honor you, I thank you…”
  5. Light the white candle in their honor and sit in their presence.
    Light the black candle from the white one, symbolizing the passing of ancestral wisdom.
    Reflect on a limiting belief or fear you carry, one that may be inherited through family or ancestral trauma.
    Write this belief on the scrap of paper.
    Acknowledge that it is not yours to carry. Say: “This belief is not mine, and I release it with ease.” Repeat as many times as needed.
  6. Hold the paper with tongs or tweezers and light it using the black candle.
    Place the burning paper into the fire-safe dish and watch it turn to ash as you visualize the belief dissolving from your body and spirit.
  7. Once cooled, dispose of the ashes.
    If needed, reframe the ritual with protective language: “I call upon my well and wise ancestors who have my best interests at heart,” and ensure only they may enter your ritual space.
