🌸T'ou Chen Niang Niang Smallpox Recovery Offering Ritual

◇Tradition
Chinese
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Offering RiteAltar Working
◇Setting

◇Method

◇Category

◇Quantities

◇Directions
1. Burn incense, offer cooling foods, and burn huge quantities of Spirit Money in T'ou Chen Niang Niang's honor. 2. Petition her very politely, as she is touchy and easily offended, to oversee the patient's recovery. 3. If the patient recovers, place T'ou Chen Niang Niang's image on a paper boat or chair, set these upon a paper phoenix, and burn everything on a pile of straw to give her a polite farewell and send her on her way. 4. If the patient dies, dispose of all offerings as you see fit; T'ou Chen Niang Niang is traditionally cursed off the premises under these circumstances.
◇Description
T'ou Chen Niang Niang, the touchy Chinese Lady of Smallpox and Thousand Flowers, must be petitioned with extreme politeness. Incense, cooling foods, and massive quantities of Spirit Money are burned in her honor. If the patient recovers, her image is ceremonially burned on a paper phoenix to send her on her way. If the patient dies, she is traditionally cursed off the premises.
