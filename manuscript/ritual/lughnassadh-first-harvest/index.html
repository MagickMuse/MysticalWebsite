🌾Lughnassadh First Harvest

A ritual burning grasses naming justice, making straw knots with red ribbon, sharing bread and wine, keeping until January 5.

Quantities

  • Long dried grasses or straw
  • Fire outdoors (or indoors orange candle and bucket of soil)
  • Red ribbon
  • Round loaf of bread decorated with ear of corn
  • Red wine or dark fruit juice in goblet

Directions

  1. Alone or with friends, each burn a single long grass/straw in the fire or singe the end in the candle before dropping it in the bucket.
  2. Name the justice or financial compensation/payment you or someone close to you needs, saying, "May justice be done and full restoration made." Cast a second grass/straw into the fire, saying, "I [or name] free(s) myself/himself/herself of this burden." Each person makes a straw knot or figure, binding it with red ribbon.
  3. Eat/share the bread by fire/candlelight, passing the wine/juice around, each person making a blessing for someone in need.
  4. Let the fire/candle burn down.
  5. Keep the straw knot/figure indoors until the twelfth night after Christmas, January 5 (the Eve of Epiphany, when the Wise Men arrived at the stable).
  6. Perform between July 31 and August 2 in the northern hemisphere, and between January 31 and February 2 in the southern hemisphere.
