Create a living healing garden with botanicals believed to radiate a protective aura that shields against disease spirits and transmits healing energy and vigor to the human body. Particularly beneficial for convalescence or chronic ailments.

Quantities

From the following, anemone, angelica, balm of Gilead, basil, chamomile, coriander/cilantro, fennel, garlic, heliotrope, henna, lavender, melissa/lemon balm, mint, mugwort, onion, peony, poppy, rose, rosemary, rue, Saint John's Wort, thyme, tobacco, wormwood
Trees (optional, ideally in a circle): apple, bay laurel, birch, cedar, olive, willow
Crystals, glass witch balls, votive imagery (optional)

Directions

  1. Fill a garden with all or any of the healing botanicals listed.
  2. Optionally surround with healing trees, ideally arranged in a circle so you may sit within their midst.
  3. Sit and relax within your garden to access its healing power, sitting on earth if comfortable or using a strategically placed bench.
  4. Arrange crystals, glass witch balls, and inspirational imagery as desired to enhance the magickal healing energy.
