Morning☀️Sunday

A bright morning spell to restore enthusiasm for life in an older person after illness or loss, rolling figure in sage and thyme then covering with crystals to renew strength and vitality.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • A play clay featureless figure
  • A small box of clear crystals, glass nuggets, beads, or pearl buttons
  • A bowl of mixed dried sage and thyme
  • A chopping board

Directions

  1. Roll the figure in the herbs on the chopping board.
  2. Say as you do so: "By thyme and sage, be renewed with the strength of youth in later age that you will smile again and feel joy in each new day once more." Shake excess herbs off the figure and cover it with shining crystals, saying: "Shine as the sun, glow as the stars and moon as vitality and hope flow back soon into your life." Set the figure where it will catch sun and moonlight indoors, and on dark evenings light a purple candle next to it.
  3. Perform any bright morning, especially Sunday.
