A waxing to Full Moon spell for strength during surgery. Two light blue votive candles are anointed with cypress essential oil, one inscribed with Berkanan and the other with Idho. Saint John's Wort and rosemary leaves are dropped into the flames to banish ill health.

Quantities

  • 2 light blue votive candles
  • Cypress essential oil
  • Saint John's Wort leaves
  • Rosemary leaves

Directions

  1. Raise open arms and speak:
  2. On this day of great energy, in this hour of self-preservation, I call upon the ruling Powers and the Elementals to aid me in this spell.
  3. Anoint two light blue votive candles with cypress essential oil.
  4. Inscribe Berkanan on one candle and Idho on the other.
  5. Set the candles on the altar and light them, then speak:
  6. I call upon the Lady and the Lord to stand by me.
  7. Let the Power of thy love and thy protection surround me, that this surgery be successful and I gain good health.
  8. Drop rosemary and Saint John's Wort leaves into the flames and speak:
  9. By the power of the Lady and the Lord, by herb and by light, let ill health take flight!
  10. So Mote It Be!
  11. Let the candles burn down.
  12. Bury the remains or dispose of them in the trash.
