Red, black, and blue cords braided with nine peacock feathers, smoked in incense, and hidden away as a protective witch's ladder. Intent alone separates a protective ladder from a malevolent one.

Quantities

1 red cord
1 black cord
1 cord in a color of your choice (blue recommended for protection)
9 peacock feathers
Incense

Directions

  1. Braid the three cords together, holding your intent and desires firmly and continuously in mind.
  2. Tie a feather into the bottom of the braid.
  3. Keep braiding, tying in nine feathers in total, spaced evenly from bottom to top.
  4. When complete, pass the braid through incense smoke.
  5. Hide it safely in a secret place.
  6. Variant (Extra Strength): for a loved one, especially a child, heading into dangerous territory, use your own hair in place of the third cord and feathers from your pillow in place of the peacock feathers.
