👕Van Van Personal Hex-Breaker

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Wash
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickWater MagickCleansing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Van Van Oil botanicals (or essential oils) Laundry rinse water
◇Directions
1. Do not use the oil itself. 2. Take the botanicals crafted to create Van Van Oil and make an infusion of it instead. 3. (See the Formulary.) If using essential oils, these may be applied directly to rinse water. 4. Strain out the botanical material and add the liquid to laundry rinse water.
◇Description
Uses an infusion of Van Van Oil botanicals (not the oil itself) added to laundry rinse water to remove a hex from an individual through their clothing.
