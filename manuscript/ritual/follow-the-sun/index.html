A solar observation practice inspired by Honeybee's navigation abilities. By tracking sunrise, sunset, and midday sun positions throughout the year, you align with your inner compass and understand seasonal cycles and rhythms.

Quantities

Optional: a calendar or date book

Directions

  1. Honeybee is a creature of sunlight and reminds us of the importance of following the sun, feeling into its rhythm, and remembering the cycle of the seasons.
  2. As we move between the solstices, the sun's angle changes.
  3. Honeybee knows this instinctively.
  4. Dedicate some time to following the sun to get yourself more deeply aligned with your inner compass.
  5. Note where on the horizon the sun rises and where it sets.
  6. This is a beautiful way to begin and end your days, reminding yourself that you too are a child of the sun and the earth.
  7. If you're able, add a midday moment with the sun.
  8. Without looking directly at it, notice how high it is in the sky.
  9. You can use a tree as a measuring rod, noting how high or low the sun sits at various times of year.
  10. If you track your observations in your calendar or date book, you'll begin to understand the cycles and rhythms of the sun's motions.
