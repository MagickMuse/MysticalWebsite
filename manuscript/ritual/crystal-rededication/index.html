🕯️Crystal Rededication

A ritual for changing the focus of an already-dedicated crystal. The crystal is cleansed through rebirth in the cauldron and rededicated to a new purpose. Note: If changing from Light to Dark (or vice versa), use the Moon matching the current alignment to cleanse, then realign with the desired Moon phase in a second ritual.

Quantities

  • For Full Moon:
  • White candles
  • White working votive
    For Dark Moon:
  • Black or purple candles
  • Purple working votive
    Both:
  • Cleansing incense (frankincense or lavender)
  • White, black, or purple cloth to wrap the crystal
  • Cauldron with libation
  • Blessed water
  • Pentacle
  • Athame
  • Crystal to rededicate
  • Salted water for Elemental passes

Directions

  1. Perform during Full Moon Esbat or Dark Moon.
  2. Begin with the crystal sitting on the pentacle.
  3. If changing focus from Light to Dark (or Dark to Light), use the Moon matching the crystal's current alignment to cleanse it first, then realign with the desired Moon phase in a second ritual.
  4. Pick up crystal and hold up in both hands over Altar:
  5. Behold this, the crystal I dedicated to [previous focus], but now I have another need.
  6. Harken unto the voice of your child, and see into my need.
  7. Like the seed that becomes new life in the womb of the Mother is this crystal placed into the cauldron of the Goddess.
  8. Let this crystal travel through the waters of life and into rebirth to be cleansed of my former need and to be brought forth to work with me in my Craft to a new focus.
  9. Place crystal inside the libation cauldron (it will get wet from the libation inside) and set the cauldron on top of the pentacle.
  10. Pour some blessed water over the crystal:
  11. You are cleansed and purified in the cauldron of rebirth.
  12. Remove crystal from cauldron and return cauldron to its former place on the Altar.
  13. Pass crystal through incense smoke:
  14. By fire and air are you brought into new life.
  15. Visualize the crystal as newly formed by geological fusion, birthed from the depths of the rocky earth, and pushed out of the ground through upheaval into the light.
  16. Wrap crystal in the cloth as if a comforting swaddling, drying it off.
  17. Unwrap crystal and see it as a shining new being.
  18. Set aside the cloth and place crystal on the pentacle.
  19. Hold athame upright in right hand while touching crystal with left hand:
  20. I call upon the power of the Elementals and the Divine to this place which is not a place, in this time which is not a time, as I stand between the worlds at the temple of my Circle to empower this crystal of Earth and Light [or Earth and Darkness].
  21. Remove hand from crystal.
  22. Hold athame with both hands and feel the power of the Lady and the Lord course into the blade.
  23. Touch crystal with tip of energized athame, seeing energy course into crystal:
  24. By the Divine Power of the Universe, is this crystal now focused as one of [state the new focus, e.g., meditation].
  25. Put athame on Altar, then pick up crystal and hold against third eye while concentrating on the new purpose.
  26. Feel that energy enter the crystal and unite with the matrix so it recognizes the personal energy it will work with:
  27. With me and through me do you work to [state focus], for we are connected one to the other through the Elementals, that we are kith and kin.
  28. So Mote It Be!
  29. Pass the crystal through the symbols of the Elementals:
  30. In the names of the Lady and Lord, I consecrate this crystal to be used in my practice of the Craft for [state the focus].
  31. I charge that this be so, and empower this crystal through Elemental Earth and Elemental Water [sprinkle with salted water]; through Elemental Fire and Elemental Air [pass through the candle flame and incense smoke].
  32. By the power of the Elementals is this crystal focused to aid me in my work.
  33. So Mote It Be!
  34. Set the crystal back on the pentacle and place hands palms down over it:
  35. Great Lady and Great Lord, together in the [light or darkness] of this Moon, You dwell within all.
  36. You are beginnings, fullness, and endings that lead into the cycle anew.
  37. You are the Energy of Life, Love Manifested and the Promise Fulfilled.
  38. Imbue this crystal with Your power and love to aid me in my Craft.
  39. Hold the crystal above the votive candle:
  40. Let this small light represent the power of the Moon and the Sun, illuminating and energizing this crystal to focus on [state the focus] for my use in my Craft.
  41. Proceed to Light Power or Dark Power Crystal Dedication (see Crystal Consecration & Dedication ritual), then continue with Closing.
