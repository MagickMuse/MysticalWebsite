🛡️Guardian Terrier Black and White Protection Bag

◇Quantities
Sea salt Black peppercorns Charm bag 5 black-eyed peas Images of a black Scottish terrier and a West Highland white terrier (photographs, drawings, or small figurines)
◇Directions
1. Grind sea salt and black peppercorns and blend them together. 2. Place the blend inside a charm bag together with five black-eyed peas. 3. Add your guardian spirits: images of a black Scottish terrier and a West Highland white terrier. Use photographs, drawings, or small figurines. 4. Hang or carry the bag for protection.
◇Description
A stronger version of Black and White Protection Powder. The salt and pepper blend goes into a charm bag with five black-eyed peas and images of a black Scottish terrier and a West Highland white terrier, small dogs that are fierce, protective, and persistent, to serve as guardian spirits.
