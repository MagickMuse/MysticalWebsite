🐶Whooping Cough Dog Bread Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
CharmFood/Edible
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BanishingEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Two slices of bread Butter One hair from the patient
◇Directions
1. Butter two slices of bread. 2. Place one of the patient's hairs between the slices like a sandwich. 3. Feed the sandwich to a dog. 4. If the dog coughs after eating, the spell is broken and the child will recover.
◇Description
A Devon folk remedy that treats whooping cough as a broken spell. A buttered bread sandwich containing one of the patient's hairs is fed to a dog. If the dog coughs after eating, the spell is broken and the child will recover.
