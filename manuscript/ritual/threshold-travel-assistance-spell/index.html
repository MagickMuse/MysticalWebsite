✈️Threshold Travel Assistance Spell

◇Form
Dressed Candle
◇Method
PetitionCandle MagickDevotional Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- The deceased's favorite drink (for libation) - A glass of the same drink for the practitioner - Sandalwood, frankincense, and/or benzoin (for burning) - White and/or silver candles
◇Directions
1. Pour a portion of whatever drink the deceased favored most as a libation. 2. Share a glass of the same drink yourself. 3. Burn sandalwood, frankincense, and/or benzoin. 4. Light white and/or silver candles while holding the departed in mind. 5. Speak the deceased's name frequently throughout, calmly and clearly, so the working is directed with precision. 6. Then say, aloud if possible: 7. I light this candle for [name]. I remember [name] and will always remember [name] even though [name] has departed. I wish [name] peace and love and safety. May [name] rest in peace. Our love journeys with you forever.
◇Description
A ritual to assist a lingering soul in making the journey to the next realm. A soul may remain because it needs reassurance, clear instruction, or simply help understanding it is time to go. The deceased's name should be repeated calmly and clearly throughout, so there is no confusion about who is being addressed. Incantation included.
