A spell to boost personal power and radiance so that a specific person will notice you. Uses sound vibration to break down energetic barriers and crystal programming to amplify and project your energy into the environment.

Quantities

  • 1 clear quartz crystal
  • 1 drum or gong

Directions

  1. Wash the quartz crystal with mild soap and water, then pat it dry.
  2. Cast a circle around your working area.
  3. Stand or sit in the center of the circle with the drum or gong, placing the crystal nearby.
  4. Begin playing the drum or gong.
  5. Feel the vibration breaking down the invisible wall that has prevented that certain person from seeing you clearly.
  6. Imagine drawing energy up from the earth into your body as brilliant silver light moving up your legs and spine until your entire body fills with silver glow.
  7. Continue playing while drawing energy down from the heavens.
  8. Visualize golden light flowing into the top of your head, down your spine, until your whole body is alive with golden glow.
  9. Pick up the crystal and hold it to your heart.
  10. Strike the drum or gong once as you imagine the mixture of silver and gold light radiating outward from your heart.
  11. As it flows through the crystal, the light magnifies tenfold and spreads out, filling the room.
  12. Strike again as you send the light further into the environment outside.
  13. Project this powerful light as far and wide as you choose.
  14. When you feel confident and secure in your newfound radiance, open the circle.
  15. Carry the crystal with you at all times.
  16. It will retain the energy of the spell and continue resonating with it, enhancing your personal power wherever you go.
