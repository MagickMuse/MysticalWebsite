🌒Waxing Crescent Moon

A body crystal layout and meditation designed for the waxing crescent moon and Moon in Aries, using three stones aligned with Aries energy: clear quartz to direct and magnify intention, carnelian for vitality and passion, and bloodstone for endurance and determination. Lying down, the practitioner places the stones on the body, breathes in moonlight, and uses the quartz point to consciously direct energy, first drawing it inward to the heart and solar plexus, then grounding it downward through the body, and finally pointing it outward into the world. Works well as a new moon intention-setting practice, a vitality boost during any part of the lunation cycle, or to charge a quartz point for ongoing ritual use.

Quantities

  • Bloodstone
  • Carnelian
  • Clear quartz point

Directions

  1. Choose a comfortable place to lie down, your bed works well, but outdoors in the moonlight or morning sun during the waxing crescent is especially potent.
  2. The waxing crescent rises with the sun, so this practice can be performed in the morning as well as at night.
  3. Set your three stones nearby, within arm's reach, and lie down.
  4. Close your eyes and take several slow, deep breaths.
  5. Inhale peace and focus; exhale scattered thoughts and unwanted energy.
  6. When you feel settled, place the bloodstone on your heart center and the carnelian roughly an inch below your navel.
  7. Hold the quartz point in your nondominant hand with the point directed up toward your arm.
  8. Feel the weight of the stones on your body.
  9. Visualize a column of light pouring down from the cosmos, through the crown of your head, through your entire body, and down into the earth's core, grounding you fully while keeping you open to the sky.
  10. Bring your intention to mind.
  11. See it clearly: what it looks like, what it feels like.
  12. Let it ignite something in your chest.
  13. With each inhale, breathe in moonlight and envision a lunar flame kindling inside you at the place of greatest excitement, growing brighter and more vibrant with every breath.
  14. Bring the quartz point to your heart center, point facing inward.
  15. Hold it there and envision the crystal drawing cosmic energy directly into your heart, feeding your passion and purpose.
  16. Slowly draw the quartz point down toward the carnelian resting below your navel.
  17. Pause here and continue to visualize the flame of energy and vitality building, the carnelian feeding warmth and drive into the working.
  18. When you feel the energy fully activated, continue moving the crystal point downward, visualizing the vitality spreading through your entire body and grounding itself into physical reality.
  19. Finally, move the quartz to your dominant hand and turn the point outward, away from the body.
  20. Hold this position and see the crystal focusing and magnifying your intention as it projects out into the world.
  21. Let yourself receive the complementary energies of the bloodstone and carnelian as they hold their positions on your body.
  22. Rest in this state for as long as feels right, at minimum a few more minutes.
  23. When complete, breathe deeply and return your awareness to the room.
  24. Keep the charged quartz point with you for ongoing lunar ritual work.
  25. Hold the carnelian whenever you need to reconnect with the vitality and drive you cultivated in this practice.
  26. Repeat the layout regularly to sustain your intention throughout the lunation cycle.
