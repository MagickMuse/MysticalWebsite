🥚EggPin

A paired fertility ritual in which a raw egg, standing in for the potential child, is passed mouth to mouth between partners, blown through and swallowed, and the emptied shell buried in earth to ground and seal the working. The physical difficulty is part of the spell's texture; the act of attempting it together is as meaningful as completing it.

⚠️ Raw egg consumption carries a risk of salmonella and other foodborne illness. Be aware of this risk before proceeding. One of the Egg Fertility methods.

Quantities

  • 1 raw egg
  • A pin

Directions

  1. Using a pin, carefully pierce a small hole in each end of a raw egg. (In some versions of this spell it is specified that the male partner must make the holes.)
  2. The partners kiss.
  3. Hold the egg between you, each taking one end in the mouth. No hands.
  4. The male partner blows the raw egg through the shell and into the female partner's mouth.
  5. She swallows the egg.
  6. Set the empty shell gently aside without breaking it.
  7. Have sex.
  8. When ready, there is no urgency, go outside together and bury the shell at least seven inches deep in earth. If outdoor burial isn't possible, bury it in a dedicated flowerpot and surround it with fertility plants.
