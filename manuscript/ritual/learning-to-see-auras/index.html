👁️Learning to See Auras

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
Materials: - Plain-colored wall (neutral tone preferred) - Dimmed lighting or adjustable lights - White paper (optional, for fingertip practice) - Practice partner (optional but recommended for later stages) - Comfortable clothing or practice naked/partially clothed
◇Directions
## Exercise 1: Fingertip Energy Threads 1. Place your forefingers together and gaze at them for about 10 seconds 2. Slowly move them apart while maintaining focus 3. Notice the fine, barely detectable thread of energy connecting your fingertips 4. At first, the thread will disappear once your fingers are about half an inch apart 5. With practice, you'll maintain the connection even as you move your fingers further apart Tip: If you have difficulty, try dimming the lights and holding your fingers over white paper. Some people prefer a darker background, experiment to find what works for you. Variation: Once you can see the thread between two fingers, try placing all four fingertips together and observe the threads joining each finger. Practice with a partner to see the energy between their fingertips. Friend Practice: Place your fingertips in contact with your friend's fingertips, then slowly separate. As friends, you'll see the thin lines of energy as you move apart. ## Exercise 2: Hand Aura You'll need a plain-colored wall and dimmed lighting. 1. Stand several feet away from the wall, facing it 2. Extend your right hand in front of you, fingers spread and facing the ceiling 3. Look at the wall through your fingers (focus on the wall, not your fingers) 4. After a few moments, notice the distinct aura around your hand, an almost invisible gray haze 5. When you become aware of it, focus on the aura (it may disappear when you do this) 6. If it disappears, gaze at the wall again until it returns 7. Repeat until you can look at the aura without having it disappear 8. Look at your fingertips, you may see fine streams of energy radiating outward 9. Notice that your entire aura is constantly moving Progression: Once you clearly see your right hand's aura, repeat with your left hand and other parts of your body. It's easier to see the aura around naked flesh than around clothing, so experiment naked or partially clothed if comfortable. Note on Color: Many people feel disappointed when first seeing their aura, expecting glorious colors. Most people start by seeing the aura as almost colorless. Your color awareness will develop gradually over time. ## Exercise 3: Partner's Aura Once you can see your own aura, begin practicing with a partner. 1. Have your partner stand a few feet in front of a plain-colored wall 2. Stand several steps away 3. Look in the direction of your friend but focus on the wall behind them 4. Notice your friend's aura in the same way you saw your hand's aura (don't try hard, simply allow it to reveal itself) 5. You may see a hazy aura entirely surrounding your friend, or only around their head and neck (clothing restricts the aura, making it harder to see) 6. Once you see the aura, focus on it (it may disappear again) 7. If it disappears, refocus on the wall until you see it again, then try once more 8. Keep practicing until you can examine the aura in detail 9. Notice how it shimmers and moves Touching the Aura: 1. Move forward and touch the aura 2. It will likely disappear when you do this 3. If it disappears, return to your previous position and wait for it to reappear 4. Try again until you can touch it while seeing it 5. When you touch it, it will feel the same as before you could see it 6. Notice the resistance when you press on it 7. Observe how increased pressure allows you to move inside the aura Full Body Exploration: - Feel the aura in different parts of your friend's body - Pay particular attention to the chakras, notice how they rapidly rotate (this is why they're called chakras, from the Sanskrit word meaning "wheel") ## Exercise 4: Emotional Experiments Now that you can see the aura, perform these experiments: 1. Ask your friend to think of something that makes them angry, notice changes in the aura (it might shrink or take on a dirty red hue) 2. Ask them to think of happy events, observe what occurs 3. Ask them to think of sad events, notice the difference ## Exercise 5: Public Practice Visit a busy environment such as a shopping mall: 1. Walk several paces behind your friend 2. Try to see their aura in this setting (you'll find this much more difficult) 3. Once you succeed, you'll start noticing auras around other people ## Developing Your Practice Practice Guidelines: - Practice as much as you can - Be patient if it takes longer than expected, everyone is different - Use a playful, lighthearted approach rather than grim determination - Experiment in different environments and lighting conditions - In time, you'll see auras whenever you wish Applications of Auric Sight: - Determine people's moods by looking at their auras - Often know when someone is lying - Observe health matters visible in the aura long before the person is aware of any problem This skill becomes an invaluable tool for understanding yourself and others at an energetic level.
◇Description
A progressive practice for developing auric sight. Through a series of exercises beginning with your fingertips and advancing to seeing full auras around yourself and others, this practice trains your ability to perceive the energetic field that surrounds all living beings.
