💤Protective Iron

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
One of the following: a knife, dagger or horseshoe; a sword or fireplace poker; or an iron bed
◇Directions
1. Place a knife, dagger or horseshoe under your pillow before going to sleep. 2. If you prefer nothing that hard in bed, keep a sword, fireplace poker or other iron implement beneath the bed. 3. Alternatively, sleep in a bed crafted from iron, which needs nothing in or under it to create an island of safety.
◇Description
Iron is among the most powerful protectors against nocturnal spiritual threats and guards you while you sleep. A knife or horseshoe goes under the pillow; if that feels too hard to keep in bed, a sword or fireplace poker can go under the bed instead. Sleeping in a bed made of iron needs nothing else at all: it creates an island of safety that also reinvigorates health, creativity and fertility through the night. One of the Dream Protection methods.
