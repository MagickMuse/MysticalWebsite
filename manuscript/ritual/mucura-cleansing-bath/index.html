Mucura

Mucura (Petiveria alliacea) is an Amazonian herb used throughout South American magick for purification, protection, and clearing saladera, the residual spiritual heaviness thought to accumulate on the subtle body and attract ongoing bad luck. Known as mucura in Peru and as guine or tipi in Brazil, it appears regularly in traditional limpia baths as a guard against malevolent magick and hostile energies.

⚠️ For external use only. Do not take mucura internally without expert medical supervision; it is potentially toxic.

Quantities

Dried mucura: approximately 2 grams
Water: 1 litre (for soaking overnight)

Directions

  1. Soak roughly two grams of dried mucura in one litre of water overnight.
  2. Add the soaking water to a drawn bath.
  3. Bathe with focused intention, visualising the removal of accumulated spiritual debris.
