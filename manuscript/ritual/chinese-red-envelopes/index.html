Adapt a Lunar New Year practice for Imbolc or the New Year by gifting red envelopes with handwritten blessings that acknowledge how someone positively affects your life.

Quantities

  • Red envelopes if available, any envelope works
  • Small cards, red index cards are nice, or any card or small note paper
  • Pen for handwritten notes
  • Optional: stamps and addresses if mailing

Directions

  1. Purpose:
  2. Share personal gratitude and goodwill, warming hearts and spreading luck for the year ahead.
  3. Prepare the envelopes:
  4. Choose recipients who regularly touch your life, family, friends, neighbors, teachers, clerks, delivery folks, anyone whose small acts matter to you.
  5. On a small card, handwrite one sincere sentence that tells the person how they make your life better. Be specific and personal, not generic.
  6. Place one card in each envelope. Hand‑label the envelope, and address if mailing. Red is traditional for good fortune, but any color works.
  7. Bless each envelope:
  8. Hold the sealed envelope in both hands. Invoke a fitting deity or guardian to bless it and its message. Choose based on the quality you are honoring, for protection you might call on Kwan Yin, for kindness you might call on Brigid, for prosperity you might call on Lakshmi.
  9. Say a short blessing in your own words. Visualize warm, bright luck surrounding the envelope and radiating outward when opened.
  10. Timing and delivery:
  11. Give them at Imbolc or on Chinese New Year for added surprise. You can also reward a child for a recent effort or accomplishment.
  12. Notes:
  13. Each note should be unique and handwritten. Aim for a heartfelt, specific appreciation rather than a task list acknowledgment.
