🔮Winter Augury of Nature's Signs

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Outdoor location with sky view - Journal and pen - Warm clothing and blanket - Thermos of hot beverage (optional) - Field guide to winter birds (optional) - Flashlight for night observations
◇Directions
Preparation: 1. Formulate a clear question or open yourself to general guidance. 2. Choose your time: dawn, dusk, or night for different energies. 3. Dress warmly and bring your journal. 4. Find an outdoor location with a clear view of sky and land. Cloud Reading (Nephomancy): - Winter clouds are stark against the sky; look for shapes and movements - Fast-moving clouds: quick changes coming - Slow clouds: patient approach needed - Dark clouds: hidden factors, caution - Bright clouds: clarity, positive omens - Animal shapes: consult animal symbolism - Direction of movement: where energy flows Bare Branch Patterns: - Observe the skeletal structure of winter trees against sky - Look for natural symbols formed by branch intersections - Runes, letters, numbers, crosses, Y-shapes (choices) - The tree species adds additional meaning - Note which direction you face when viewing Meteor Observations: - Winter nights offer clear viewing for shooting stars - Direction of streak indicates flow of fortune - Brightness shows intensity of coming events - Make a wish or note the timing for major workings - Multiple meteors: significant changes ahead Bird Augury: - Winter birds are easier to spot against bare branches - Flight direction: toward you (approaching), away (leaving) - Number of birds: count carries numerological meaning - Species: crows (magick), cardinals (vitality), jays (communication) - Calls and songs: messages to decode - Solitary vs. flock: individual or community focus Snowbank Formations: - Wind-sculpted drifts reveal shapes and symbols - Animal-like formations: consult animal meanings - Geometric patterns: structured approach needed - Chaotic drifts: embrace flexibility - Snow depth: how buried or visible your issue is Recording Your Observations: Note date, time, weather, moon phase, your question, and all signs observed. Patterns emerge over repeated practice throughout the winter season.
◇Description
Traditional winter augury practice reading omens in clouds, bare branches, meteors, bird flights, and snow formations. This comprehensive divination method uses multiple natural phenomena to answer questions or reveal hidden knowledge.
