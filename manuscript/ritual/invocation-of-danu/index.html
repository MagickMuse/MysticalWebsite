🌊Invocation of Danu

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
InvocationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Invocation
◇Quantities
No materials required
◇Directions
1. Dear Danu, great goddess! Mother of the Tuatha! Great Lady of the water! Great Ruler of the land! Dear Danu, great goddess, I invoke thee! Fill me with your strength! Fill me with your grace! Fill me with your power! May your eyes look through my face! Great Danu, Mother of the River! Mother of the Land! Mother of the People! Come into my hands! Danu, I invoke thee! Come to me now! Great mother, great Danu, come into me now!
◇Description
An invocation to Danu, Mother of land, water, and the Tuatha Dé Danann, asking to be filled with strength, grace, and power.
