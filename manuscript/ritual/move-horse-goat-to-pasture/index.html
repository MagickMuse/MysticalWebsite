🐴Move Horse Goat to Pasture

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 brown candle - 1 circle of copper wire - Hair or wool from one of the animals - Green string
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and pass the wire ring eight times over the flame, saying as you do so, "Within this ring protection bring, from all disruption and interruption. That this move will be easy for you and you will settle in pastures new." Wrap the wool or hair around and around the ring, securing it with string, repeating the spell words as you do so. 2. Blow out the candle, saying, "Protection bring within this ring." Hang the ring in the animal's current stable or enclosure and swing it twice, repeating both empowerments twice whenever you feed the animal(s). 3. When you have transported the animal(s), hang the ring in the new stable or enclosure, swinging it four times and saying both empowerments four times. 4. Repeat daily until the animals settle. 5. Perform a week before the move.
◇Description
A copper wire ring ritual with animal hair or wool for easy transitions to new stables, pastures, or paddocks.
