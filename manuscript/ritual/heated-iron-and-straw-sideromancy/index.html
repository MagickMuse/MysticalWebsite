🔮Heated Iron and Straw Sideromancy

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
• A cast iron pan • An odd number of broom straws or dried yarrow stalks
◇Directions
1. Heat a cast iron pan over fire to a temperature that is safe and manageable for you. 2. Remove it from the heat source. 3. Drop an odd number of straws onto the hot surface. 4. Broom straws are traditional, but dried yarrow stalks are a potent substitute due to their connection with the I-Ching. 5. Observe the movement the straws make as they land and react to the heat, the shapes they form, and the character of any smoke or flames that result. 6. Interpret all of these together as the oracle.
◇Description
Divination by heated iron in which an odd number of straws are dropped onto a hot cast iron pan and their movement, shapes, smoke, and flames are interpreted as the oracle. Historically reserved for ironworkers; now adapted as a household method. Exercise caution with heat.
