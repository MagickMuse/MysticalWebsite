💰Prosperity Spell Bag Financial Papers

A Thursday morning prosperity spell bag to keep with financial papers, mixing spices, herbs, coins or notes, and oil together while chanting to create ongoing abundance for a year and a day. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • A bowl and a wooden or ceramic spoon
  • Two spices: ginger, saffron, cinnamon, nutmeg, allspice, or turmeric
  • Two money-growing herbs or dried flowers: chamomile, lavender, patchouli, mint, sage, rosemary, or thyme, or use a herbal tea mix
  • Three gold-, silver-, or copper-color coins or green money notes of any denomination
  • A few drops of orange, patchouli, or frankincense essential oil or sunflower cooking oil
  • A fabric purse or drawstring bag

Directions

  1. Shake the spice and herbs in the bag or purse, mixing them together, saying faster as you mix faster: "Money grows, abundance flows, light grows, abundance knows no bounds." Add two or three drops of oil, repeating the words, continuing to chant and mix faster, until you plunge the spoon into the bowl, saying: "Abundance knows no bounds." Add money, repeating the words.
  2. Keep the bag with financial papers for a year and a day and then throw it into water and replace.
