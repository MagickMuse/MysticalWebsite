🕯️Candle

A full overnight flamekeeping vigil from sunset to sunrise, releasing all goals and following Brigid's guidance through the wee hours of the dark.

Quantities

A candle or lamp with enough fuel to last the night, or multiple candles to light in succession; food and drink for sustenance; comfortable seating

Directions

  1. Begin at sunset with all preparations made: a safe, comfortable space, food and water within reach, electronics silenced.
  2. Light your flame and release all expectations.
  3. Set no tasks for yourself.
  4. Follow your impulses as Brigid guides you through the night.
  5. Journal if moved to, divine if moved to, but do not feel you must accomplish anything.
  6. Tend the flame as it burns low, lighting a new candle or replenishing your lamp's oil as needed, feeling the intimacy of that moment.
  7. Let weariness and wakefulness and perhaps euphoria move through you naturally.
  8. At sunrise, bless and thank your flame.
  9. Extinguish it if you wish.
  10. Spend the day in peaceful contemplation and rest.
