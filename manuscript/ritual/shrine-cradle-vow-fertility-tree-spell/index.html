🌳Shrine Cradle Vow Fertility Tree Spell

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Devotional WorkManifestationPetition
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• A towel (to form the cradle) • A small stone (to represent the desired child)
◇Directions
1. Fold or tie a towel into the shape of a cradle. 2. Place a stone inside it to represent the child you are seeking. 3. Hang the filled cradle from a branch of the shrine tree. 4. Make a personal vow to the presiding saint or spirit: promise to fulfill it if and when your desire is granted.
◇Description
A shrine-based fertility working in which the practitioner absorbs the radiant generative energy present at a sacred site through proximity and petition. A symbolic cradle holding a stone child is hung in a tree at the shrine as a votive offering, accompanied by a personal vow to the presiding spirit. Simply being present at the shrine may be enough; the cradle vow amplifies and anchors the working.
