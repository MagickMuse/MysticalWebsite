🕯️Switch Lovers Double Action Red Black Candle Banish Draw Spell

A red and black double-action candle is used to simultaneously banish an unwanted lover (black wax) while drawing a new desired one (red wax), both ends burned at once.

Quantities

Red and black double-action candle; hat pin or spiked candlestick

Directions

  1. Carve the name of the lover you'd like to see vanish onto the black wax.
  2. Carve the name of the lover you'd now like to have onto the red wax.
  3. If necessary, carve the bottom of the candle, exposing the wick, so that both ends may be burnt at the same time.
  4. Stick the candle horizontally onto a spiked candlestick, or pierce the border between red and black with a long hat pin, then balance the candle over a bottle and burn both ends simultaneously.
