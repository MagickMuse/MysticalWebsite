🥛White Cow Milk Bath Healing Spell

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Bath
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickCleansing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Milk (ideally from a white or white-faced cow) Dried healing herbs of choice (For general vitality: chamomile, lavender, and rosemary)
◇Directions
1. Gently warm milk, ideally from a white cow or a white-faced one. 2. Pour the warm milk into a bowl over dried healing herbs and let them steep together. 3. Strain out the herb solids and add the herbal milk to your bath. 4. Any herbs suitable for your healing need may be used. 5. For general vitality, chamomile, lavender, and rosemary are recommended.
◇Description
A Celtic healing bath rooted in the ancient Irish and Scottish belief that cow's milk, ideally from a white or white-faced cow, transmits healing vitality. Dried herbs are steeped in warmed milk, strained, and the milk is added to the bath. Chamomile, lavender, and rosemary are recommended for general vitality.
