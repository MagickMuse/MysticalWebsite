🔓Severing the Ties Oaxaca Spell

◇Form
Bath
◇Method
Water MagickCleansing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Fresh, clean clothes - Access to a river or ritual bath
◇Directions
1. Immediately following the death, the widow retreats alone at home for nine days of grief. 2. She neither speaks to nor looks at anyone during this period. 3. Food is brought to her but all contact is otherwise avoided. 4. On the ninth day, fresh clean clothes are brought to her. 5. She takes the clothes and hurries directly to a river or ritual bath, pausing only three times to draw breath and speaking to no one along the way. 6. She submerges herself completely three times. 7. When she puts on the fresh clothes, her mourning is complete and her spiritual ties to the deceased are severed.
◇Description
An Oaxacan mourning ritual that formally severs the bond between a widow and her deceased husband. Nine days of isolated grief are followed by a river or ritual bath taken in strict silence, releasing the living woman from the spiritual influence of the dead.
