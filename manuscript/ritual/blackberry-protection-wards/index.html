🛡️Blackberry Protection Wards

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Herbal MagickProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- Blackberry juice (as needed for writing) - Four pieces of biodegradable paper - Writing implement (feather quill, chopstick, knitting needle, or blackberry itself)
◇Directions
1. Prepare your writing implement. Use it like a quill pen, dipping it in blackberry juice. 2. On four pieces of paper, write this spell (the same spell on all four): North, south, east, west Protect my home, protect my nest West, east, south, north Protect my rep, protect my worth 3. You've just created protective wards for your home and family. 4. Bury the papers at the corners of your property to create an invisible fence of protection around your home. 5. If you can't bury the papers outside, tuck them in corners of your home where they will not be disturbed. Do this spell at the solstices and equinoxes to renew the protection at each new season.
◇Description
Blackberry bushes can grow up to ten feet tall and have thick canes with thorny purple spines, creating a protective thicket. Those dark berries are full of antioxidants as well as vitamins C and K. Extend blackberry's protective aura beyond the body with this spell.
