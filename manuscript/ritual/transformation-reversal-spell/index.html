This spell takes the malevolent energy someone has deliberately aimed toward you and changes it into your chosen good fortune.

Quantities

A small black candle
A white candle
Brown paper, for a tray covering and for writing
Salt scrub or dry salt
A small flat sheet of copper

Directions

  1. Hold a small black candle in your hand and envision all the negative effects of the curse that has been laid upon you.
  2. When you are finished, place the candle on a tray covered with brown paper.
  3. Wash your hands by rubbing them with a salt scrub or dry salt, then rinsing.
  4. Hold a white candle in your hands and concentrate on a vision of your life as you would like it to be; see yourself happy, stable, and secure with all that negative energy transformed into positive.
  5. Consider what you need to make that happy vision a reality and write your requirements or a plan of action on a piece of brown paper.
  6. Place a small flat sheet of copper between the two candles so that one is at each end.
  7. Place the paper on top of the copper sheet.
  8. Light the black candle and say aloud:
  9. "Negative energy transform into good. Transform into [name your desire]."
  10. Light the white candle and say aloud:
  11. "My dreams and desires are accomplished. Nothing and no one obstructs me."
