🛡️Faery Theft & Nightmare Prevention

Folk Magick

◇Quantities
- Hag stone (stone with naturally worn hole through center) - String for hanging, or cardboard and glue for mounting - 2 long iron nails
◇Directions
1. Many legends forewarn that the faeries may try to steal children. 2. In addition, scary movies are often popular at this time of year, and the images a child or adult might take to bed can lead to some troubled sleep. 3. This spell covers both problems. 4. It should stop a faery from trying to steal your child (or you!) and it prevents nightmares after that ten-hour American Horror Story marathon. 5. The Spell: 6. All you need is a hag stone. 7. A hag stone is simply a stone with a naturally worn hole through the center. 8. These used to be difficult to obtain unless you lived near a beach in the United Kingdom; these days, however, you can order them online. 9. Hang it above the bed on a string or mount it on a piece of cardboard with glue. 10. Above it, attach two long iron nails that cross each other in the middle. 11. The nightmares will sweep through the hole and errant faeries won't come near you!
◇Description
A protective charm using a hag stone and crossed iron nails to prevent fairy theft and nightmares. The hag stone's hole allows nightmares to pass through while the iron keeps faeries away. Hung above the bed for protection during sleep.
