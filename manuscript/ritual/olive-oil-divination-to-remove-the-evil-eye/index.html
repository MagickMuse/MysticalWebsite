👁️Olive Oil Divination to Remove the Evil Eye

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Ancestor WorkPetitionPrayerWater MagickProtectionReversal
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Small amount of olive oil Dish of water Pin or scissors Optional preventive amulets (not required for the working itself): Turkish nazar (round blue and black eye amulet) Italian cornicello (horn-shaped charm, often red or gold)
◇Directions
1. Prevention (before the evil eye is placed) 2. Consider obtaining an amulet to ward against the evil eye. 3. Common options include a Turkish nazar (a round blue and black eye) or an Italian cornicello (a horn-shaped charm, often red or gold). 4. If you are caught without an amulet, you can discreetly form your hand into a fist and slip your thumb between your pointer and middle fingers. 5. This apotropaic gesture is called the fig. 6. Removal (once the evil eye may already be present) 7. Take a small amount of olive oil to your ancestor altar. 8. Pray that your ancestors will draw any evil eye placed on your loved one, intentional or not, into the oil. 9. Place a dish of water over the subject's head and add the oil drop by drop. 10. Observe the patterns the oil forms. 11. Eye shapes will stand out clearly if the evil eye is present. 12. Using a pin or scissors, prick or cut each eye shape you see in the oil. 13. When finished, dispose of the water and oil by dumping them down the toilet.
◇Description
A Mediterranean folk remedy for removing the evil eye from a loved one. Olive oil is prayed over at the ancestor altar and dripped into a dish of water held over the subject. Eye shapes that appear in the oil are pierced or cut to break the effect. Includes notes on preventive amulets.
