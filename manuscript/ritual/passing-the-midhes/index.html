🌀Passing the Midhes

A Full Moon or Sidhe Moon ritual performed on a Sunday or Monday during the hour of the Moon or Mercury, designed for journeying through the midhes (gateways) between the three worlds: Middleworld, Otherworld, and Underworld. A green altar cloth is set facing North with three pillar candles (black, red, white) and a black mirror at center. The Ogham fews of Coll (hazel) and Duir (oak) serve as keys to the midhes. Coll opens the passage to Otherworld, where calendula is offered and the four quarters are explored with a hazel wand. Duir opens the passage to Underworld, where mullein is offered and the four quarters are explored with an oak wand. Upon return to Middleworld, both wands are held together as Wisdom and Strength, and the quarters are explored a final time. Burdock is offered at the Middleworld transitions. The ritual concludes with creating the Midhe Charm by wrapping the folded paper in red, purple, and green threads, placing it in a black pouch, and knotting it thrice for the three worlds. The charm is charged on the pentacle with the Ogham fews before being stored in the Ogham bag.

Quantities

  • Green altar cloth
  • 3 pillar candles (black, red, white)
  • 1 athame
  • 1 wand
  • 1 candle snuffer
  • 1 small cauldron
  • 1 pentacle
  • 1 incense burner and charcoal disc
  • Acacia herb
  • Lavender flowers or incense stick
  • Spring water
  • Anointing oil
  • Matches
  • Food and beverage
  • Burdock root
  • Calendula
  • Mullein
  • Ogham fews
  • 1 piece of paper or parchment (3x5)
  • Pen
  • Dragon's blood ink
  • Three 12-inch lengths of thread (red, purple, green)
  • 1 black mirror
  • 1 small black pouch
  • 1 purple or lavender votive candle in container

Directions

  1. Take a bath scented with lavender or vervain before beginning.
  2. Cast a Circle as with the Sidhe Moon, calling upon the Elementals to aid and watch over your travels.
  3. Inscribe the purple candle with the Triple Moon for the Lady, the Horned God symbol for the Lord, Raidho for travel, and Dagaz for between the worlds.
  4. Place the black mirror at the center of the altar with the Ogham fews behind it and the three pillar candles across in front.
  5. Light the incense and the purple candle.
  6. If using a charcoal disc, light it and place some acacia on it to smolder.
  7. Focus on the intersecting points to Otherworld and Underworld, which are Coll and Duir, and how Coll the hazel connects the power of wisdom with the wand and the practitioner, and how Duir the oak links the power of courage with the seeking of truth.
  8. Take the red candle and light it from the purple one, then set it on top of the pentacle in front of the black mirror.
  9. Speak:
  10. Here I stand in the center of my world, passionate of life and physical of form.
  11. Drop some burdock into the red candle flame and speak:
  12. The Middleworld of challenge, knowledge, prosperity, and contentment bring me joy.
  13. Draw Coll on the upper right edge of the paper for traveling to Otherworld.
  14. Focus on the properties of the hazel as the Witches' tree, the tree of wicce, of wisdom, creativity, and perception.
  15. Speak:
  16. I call upon the spirit of the hazel to be my guide and open the path to me to Otherworld.
  17. Crease the paper.
  18. Pass it through the incense smoke, sprinkle lightly with blessed water, and pass quickly through the red candle flame.
  19. Speak:
  20. By Earth and by Air; by Water and by Fire, I call upon the power of Coll to guide me.
  21. I have the passage of hazel in my hand!
  22. Snap shut the section of paper with the few on it and fold the paper over tightly.
  23. Hold it with one hand and with the other, light the white candle from the red one.
  24. Speak:
  25. With Coll do I pass the midhe from Middleworld to Otherworld.
  26. Move the red candle to the right side and place the white candle in front of the black mirror.
  27. Gaze into the mirror past the flame and envision the path to Otherworld blocked by hazel branches.
  28. Lightly tap the mirror with the folded paper.
  29. Speak:
  30. Open the midhe, for I have Coll in my hand.
  31. See the branches part.
  32. See the path ahead and the paper transformed into a wand of hazel.
  33. Drop some calendula into the white candle flame and speak:
  34. I give the gift of calendula to receive the gift of sight in Otherworld.
  35. Let this herb open the way as an offering to the People of this fair world.
  36. Move along the path through a woodland and enter a broad plain where the edges of four realms converge.
  37. Speak:
  38. Here I stand in the center of Otherworld, filled with the wonder of eternity, and spiritual in form.
  39. Point the hazel wand to each quarter, one at a time:
  40. I move with Coll in the North.
  41. See what is shown of age and wisdom.
  42. I move with Coll in the West.
  43. See what is shown of light and gentleness.
  44. I move with Coll in the East.
  45. See what is shown of abundance.
  46. I move with Coll in the South.
  47. See what is shown of happiness.
  48. Return to the midhe and speak:
  49. I take the passage of Coll back to Middleworld.
  50. Set the white candle back at the right side and place the red candle before the mirror.
  51. Envision walking back along the path through the woods and back to the altar.
  52. With the paper still folded over the few of Coll, draw Duir in the upper left space to travel to Underworld.
  53. Focus on the properties of the oak as power, endurance, truth, and strength.
  54. Speak:
  55. I call upon the spirit of the oak to be my guide and open the path to me to Underworld.
  56. Crease the paper and pass it through the symbols of the Elementals.
  57. Speak:
  58. By Earth and by Air; by Water and by Fire, I call upon the power of Duir to guide me.
  59. I have the passage of oak in my hand!
  60. Snap shut the section of paper and fold tightly once more.
  61. Hold it with one hand and with the other, light the black candle from the red one.
  62. Speak:
  63. With Duir do I pass the midhe from Middleworld to Underworld.
  64. Move the red candle to the right side and place the black candle in front of the black mirror.
  65. Gaze into the mirror past the flame and envision the path to Underworld blocked by low-spreading branches of river oaks.
  66. Lightly tap the mirror with the folded paper.
  67. Speak:
  68. Open the midhe, for I have Duir in my hand.
  69. See the branches part.
  70. See the dark river ahead and the paper transformed into a wand of oak.
  71. Drop some mullein into the black candle flame and speak:
  72. I give the gift of mullein to receive the gift of sight in Underworld.
  73. Let this herb open the way as an offering to the people of this shadow world.
  74. Envision stepping into a small flatboat and crossing the placid river to land upon the shadowy shore.
  75. Enter the plain where four realms converge.
  76. Speak:
  77. Here I stand in the center of Underworld, serene with love and transformation, fleeting of form.
  78. Point the oak wand to each quarter, one at a time:
  79. I move with Duir in the North.
  80. See what is shown of death and transformation.
  81. I move with Duir in the West.
  82. See what is shown of cleansing and positive changes.
  83. I move with Duir in the East.
  84. See what is shown of introspection, growth, and fruition.
  85. I move with Duir in the South.
  86. See what is shown of energy, new beginnings, and hidden powers.
  87. Return to the midhe and speak:
  88. I take the passage of Duir back to Middleworld.
  89. Set the black candle to the left side and return the red candle before the mirror.
  90. Envision moving back to the river, boarding the boat, and crossing the dark river back to the altar.
  91. Look into the mirror past the flame of the red candle.
  92. Bend the folded paper so both ends point forward and see two wands in your hand, one of hazel and one of oak.
  93. Speak:
  94. The midhes stand open, for I have Coll and Duir, Wisdom and Strength, in my hand.
  95. Drop some burdock into the red candle flame and speak:
  96. I give the gift of burdock to receive the gift of sight in Middleworld.
  97. Let this herb open the way as an offering to the powers of this land.
  98. Enter onto the plain where the four realms of Middleworld converge.
  99. Speak:
  100. Here I stand once more, in the center of my world, passionate of life and physical of form.
  101. Point the double wand to each quarter, one at a time:
  102. I move with Wisdom and Strength in the North, to learn from challenges and overcome obstacles.
  103. See what is shown of gateways to success and self-expression.
  104. I move with Wisdom and Strength in the West, to gain in knowledge and learning.
  105. See what is shown of tests to come, balance, and decisions to be made.
  106. I move with Wisdom and Strength in the East, to achieve prosperity and harvest.
  107. See what is shown of inner transformations and development for harmony.
  108. I move with Wisdom and Strength in the South, to know contentment with the strength to enjoy life.
  109. See what is shown of inner strength, awareness, and confidence.
  110. Hold the doubled paper folded tightly together and speak:
  111. Here have I the paths opened from Middleworld to Otherworld and Underworld that I move between the worlds with ease of passage.
  112. Wrap the red thread around the paper and speak:
  113. Let the power of red enhance the strength of the oak.
  114. Wrap the purple thread around the paper and speak:
  115. Let the power of purple enhance the wisdom of the hazel.
  116. Wrap the green thread around the paper and speak:
  117. Let the power of green enhance this tool with the herbal blessing of Nature.
  118. Place the wrapped paper into the small black pouch, close it, and pass it through the symbols of the Elementals.
  119. Speak:
  120. My wand of Coll and Duir I keep close to me, passed through the Elementals, warded in black from negativity and knotted thrice: once for Otherworld, once for Underworld, and once for Middleworld.
  121. The passing of the Midhes lies in my hands.
  122. Tie each knot as spoken.
  123. Place the Ogham fews on top of the pentacle and lay the pouch on top of the fews.
  124. Snuff the white, black, and red candles.
  125. Leave the pouch on the altar for one hour, then place the charm in the Ogham bag.
  126. Proceed to the Simple Feast and opening of the Circle.
  127. Bury candle remains or dispose of them with trash.
