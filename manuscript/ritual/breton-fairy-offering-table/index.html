🧚Breton Fairy Offering Table

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PetitionDevotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• An actual table, set nicely (not a dresser top or counter) • Champagne, cordials, or whiskey • Wine • Milk • Berries and nuts • Apples • Pastry • Butter and bread
◇Directions
1. In the birthing room, spread a table for the fairies the Breton tradition calls Nos Bonnes Meres, "Our Good Mothers," who always attend births. 2. Set the table nicely, as though a genuine meal will be served. 3. Offer some or all of the following: champagne, cordials, or whiskey, wine, milk, berries, nuts, apples, pastry, and butter and bread. 4. Invoke the fairies only for a true birth; calling on them without real cause is said to provoke their anger and malice rather than their blessing.
◇Description
A Breton offering table set in the birthing room to welcome the childbirth fairies known as Nos Bonnes Meres, "Our Good Mothers"; inviting them without genuine cause is said to provoke their anger rather than their blessing.
