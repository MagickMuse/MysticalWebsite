🌱Night-Blooming Jasmine for Lailah

Jasmine

A petition to Lailah, Angel of Night and Conception from Jewish mystical tradition, who blesses and activates nocturnal lovemaking. He is called with night-blooming jasmine. Lailah oversees conception itself, lighting a candle for the child in the womb and teaching it all wisdom before birth.

Quantities

Night-blooming jasmine (fresh flowers, or jasmine incense)
A candle (white or silver, optional)

Directions

  1. Place night-blooming jasmine in your space, or burn jasmine incense.
  2. If desired, light a white candle.
  3. Invoke Lailah by name.
  4. Ask for his blessing on nocturnal lovemaking and for his presence at the moment of conception.
  5. Speak your petition openly.

Lailah is the Angel of Night and Conception in Jewish mystical tradition. His name derives from the Hebrew for night. He is not an angel of the moon but of darkness itself, the fertile dark that holds the seed of new life.

In Talmudic and midrashic sources, Lailah is the angel who carries the soul to the womb, lights a candle inside so the unborn child can see from one end of the world to the other, and teaches the child all wisdom before birth. At the moment of birth, he extinguishes the candle and seals the memory, leaving only the imprint.

Jasmine is his natural correspondence: night-blooming, releasing fragrance only after dark, it shares his domain completely. Its scent carries the quality of attraction, love, and fertility, and its nocturnal nature makes it the proper key for reaching him.
