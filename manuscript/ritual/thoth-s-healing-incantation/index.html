🪶Thoth's Healing Incantation

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
HealingInvocationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. An important principle of Egyptian medical magick was identification with the gods. 2. The magickian pronouncing a spell takes on the identity of a healing deity, while the afflicted person is identified with the ailing child Horus. 3. We are all vulnerable, like a child, when suffering and unwell. 4. By establishing a divine precedent for recovery, these healing formulas offer reassurance that the cure will work. 5. The magickian assumes the identity of Thoth, god of wisdom and magick. 6. The patient is identified with the ailing Horus. 7. In the myth, Isis halted Ra's sun-boat in the sky, refusing to let it move until her son was healed. 8. Ra sent Thoth down to cure the child, and the sun could resume its journey. 9. Speak the incantation aloud. 10. Egyptian magick required the spoken word (djed medu, 'the words to be said') to activate its power. 11. How something is said matters as much as what is said. 12. Recite: 'Awake, Horus. I am Thoth sent to cure you for your mother Isis, and I cure the suffering person likewise. May the poison die, may its fire be extinguished.' 13. The spell establishes a divine precedent: just as Thoth healed the poisoned Horus in the myth, so too will this patient recover.
◇Description
Egyptian healing spell for poison or illness. Based on the myth where Isis halts Ra's sun-boat and Ra sends Thoth to heal young Horus from a scorpion sting.
