Yule

A complete Wiccan Yule Sabbat ritual celebrating the Winter Solstice, the rebirth of the Sun, and the transition from Holly King to Oak King. Includes candle transitions symbolizing the departure of the Holly King and arrival of the Oak King, Yule log lighting, and optional Yuletide carols.

Quantities

  • Altar Candles
  • 2 bright red taper candles (outer positions)
  • 1 deep green taper candle (center position)
  • May be set in Yule Log holder
  • Candles may be scented with cinnamon, pine, or bayberry
    Extra Candles for Transition
  • 2 deep green candles
  • 1 bright red candle
    Incense
  • Bayberry, frankincense, or myrrh (choice or combination)
    Decorations
  • Holly (Goddess)
  • Mistletoe (God)
  • Ivy
  • Pine and pinecones
    Ritual Items
  • Ashwood twigs in cauldron
  • Athame
  • Wand
  • Bell (optional)
  • Libation bowl
    Cakes & Wine
  • Nuts, fruits, fruitcake or plum pudding
  • Poultry, game, or pork dishes
  • Wassail, wine, or other beverage

Directions

  1. Begin at the ritual portion of a Basic Circle Casting.
  2. Opening
  3. Clap or ring bell three times.
  4. Sing one or two Yule carols as desired.
  5. Raise open arms:
  6. Blessed are the Lord and the Lady who turn the mighty Wheel.
  7. Welcome the Yule for the turning point of Winter is here at last.
  8. The end of the Solar Year has come, and a New Solar Year begins.
  9. The Holly King departs as the Oak King is born; the Crone delivers Him of the Mother.
  10. Hail the Mystery of Sage and Babe, of Crone and Mother!
  11. Power and Fire
  12. Hold up wand:
  13. This day is a new beginning, and I send my power forth into union with the energies of the Sun God, that the Sun's rebirth rekindles my strength as the rays rekindle the warmth of the Earth.
  14. Set wand on Altar.
  15. Light kindling in cauldron with center Altar candle:
  16. This new fire is lit to bid farewell to the Dark Lord of the Solar Night and to greet the Lord of Light, seen in the rebirth of the Invincible Sun.
  17. May my power be added to the strength of the newborn Lord, and His to mine.
  18. Hailing the God
  19. Return candle to holder.
  20. Hold up athame in both hands in front of Altar:
  21. Hail to the God of Light and Joy!
  22. Hail to the movement of Time Eternal!
  23. The Holly King leaves with gifts for all as symbols of His love.
  24. His eight great stags carry His sleigh to the realm of Snow and Ice, where in His wisdom, He awaits the next turning of the Solstice Wheel.
  25. Hail the passage of the Sage and His rebirth at Yule!
  26. Hail the God Eternal!
  27. Put athame on Altar.
  28. Clap or ring the bell 3 times.
  29. Sing one or two Yule Carols.
  30. Candle Transition
  31. Let the Altar candles burn very low.
  32. Snuff the 2 red outer candles and replace them with the deep green candles.
  33. Light the new candles from the still-lit green center candle:
  34. The Holly King has left us; let us hold His wisdom in our hearts.
  35. Snuff the center green candle and replace with the extra red candle:
  36. The Oak King comes to us; let us hold His promise in our souls.
  37. Through the stages of life, death, and rebirth are we shown the Mystery, for there is no death, only passage, and the God holds the torch to light the way for us.
  38. God of Light and Joy, held within the Mother's arms, the radiance of Thy face lifts our spirits in the dark of Winter, and brings us comfort and peace.
  39. Yule Log Blessing
  40. Light the red center candle fresh (Yule log may remain as home decor through January 6th):
  41. May the Yule Log bring light, that all good enter here.
  42. Good Health, Good Cheer, Good Fortune in the coming year!
  43. That as I will, So Mote It Be!
  44. Pour a libation of the beverage into libation bowl and take a sip.
  45. Sing one or two Yule Carols.
  46. Proceed to Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
  47. Sing more carols as desired.
