⚔️Chango Resguardo

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group

◇Form

◇Setting

◇Method

◇Quantities
Arrowheads Thunderstones Brown sugar Dried aloe Mugwort Cedar chips 6 red apple seeds Red velvet Red silk thread 1 gold sword charm
◇Directions
1. Gather arrowheads, thunderstones, brown sugar, dried aloe, mugwort, cedar chips, and six red apple seeds. 2. Wrap everything in red velvet. 3. Stitch the packet closed with red silk thread. 4. Attach a gold sword charm to the outside. 5. If the sword ever breaks, bury the packet, make an offering to Chango, and create a new one.
◇Description
A protective packet dedicated to Chango, wrapped in red velvet and crowned with a gold sword charm. ⚠️ Chango Resguardo is a Santería/Lucumí working. Santería/Lucumí is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.
