📿Dedicating Jewelry to Sacred Practice

A ritual for dedicating a piece of jewelry to combine its essential energy with your own, creating a sacred object that strengthens your connection to Brigid or other sacred powers whenever it is worn.

Quantities

A piece of jewelry you wish to dedicate; optional altar space

Directions

  1. Hold the piece of jewelry in both hands.
  2. Feel its weight and warmth.
  3. Let your attention settle into its essential energy: the metal, the stones if any, the design, the intention behind its making.
  4. Speak your dedication aloud or silently.
  5. Name the quality or power you are invoking: Brigid's flame, the purity of gold, the intuitiveness of silver, the protective force of iron.
  6. State that you are combining this energy with your own and dedicating it to sacred purpose.
  7. Place it on your body with your full attention.
  8. Feel the moment of connection.
  9. When you remove it, thank it.
  10. When you put it on again, that act of wearing will instantly strengthen your connection to the sacred.
  11. The most humble iron ring holds as much power as the most elaborate gold torc: what matters is the attention and devotion you bring.
