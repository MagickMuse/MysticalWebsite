🪶Feather

An Indian hex to sow general discord within a home. A found crow's feather (never taken by force, that will backfire) is buried within the target's residence. Originally from a region with thatched roofs, so burying it in the roof was sufficient; any discreet spot in the residence will do, including under the front step.

Quantities

Found crow's feather (must be found, not taken by force)

Directions

  1. Bury a found crow's feather in the target's house.
  2. Any discreet place in the residence will do; under the front step, where the target is bound to step over it, should be equally effective.
  3. It must be a found feather, as taking one by force will cause the spell to backfire.
