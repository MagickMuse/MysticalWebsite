A Greek ritual that determines whether an illness derives from magickal or conventional sources. Dried cloves are burned one at a time over a candle flame with psalm recitation. If a clove snaps loudly enough to startle the petitioner, the spell is considered broken. If none snap, the illness is deemed organic.

Quantities

Dried cloves (traditionally 12; choose a personally significant number)
Needle or pin
Candle

Directions

  1. Select a set number of cloves before beginning; twelve is customary, but choose whatever number is most significant.
  2. This prevents the spell from being performed endlessly.
  3. Insert a needle or pin through a dried clove.
  4. Hold it over a candle flame.
  5. Murmur psalms; if you fear the illness is caused by malevolent magick or the Evil Eye, verbally send it back and reverse the hex.
  6. Once that first clove has burned down, repeat with another.
  7. Continue burning cloves one at a time, petitioning and chanting, until one clove snaps and makes a sound.
  8. If the sound makes the petitioner jump, flinch, or start, the spell is considered broken.
  9. If no cloves snap, the illness is deemed organic with no magickal influence; other causes and treatments must be sought.
