⚡Crystal Aura Repair

◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
HealingEnergy Work
◇Category
Energetic Rite
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
1 charged quartz crystal
◇Directions
1. Ask a helper, ideally someone with psychic gifts, to perform the working on you. 2. The helper slowly passes the charged quartz crystal around the entire body. 3. Wherever a cold spot, gap, or vulnerability is sensed, the helper stops and lets the crystal hover and rest over that area to rebuild and repair it. 4. Repeat the working several times if needed until the aura is fully restored.
◇Description
A helper passes a charged quartz crystal around the body to mend breaks and holes in the aura. The crystal rests over any cold or vulnerable spot to rebuild it, and the working may need several repetitions to take full effect.
