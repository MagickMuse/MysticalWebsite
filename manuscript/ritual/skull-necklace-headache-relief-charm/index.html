💀Skull Necklace Headache Relief Charm

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Knot MagickEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Skull-shaped beads and charms Red cord Small crystal skull (for storage/empowerment, optional)
◇Directions
1. Collect skull-shaped beads and charms. 2. String them onto red cord, knotting your desire after every bead. 3. When you feel a headache approaching, wear your necklace as a prophylactic charm, and place the skulls against your brow for extra power. 4. When not in use, store your skull necklace in a magick box, ideally with a small crystal skull for empowerment and revitalization.
◇Description
Fierce goddesses like Anat, Baba Yaga, and Kali wear skull necklaces that carry profound magickal healing properties, especially for headache relief. A knotted necklace of skull beads on red cord channels this power, no real skulls needed.
