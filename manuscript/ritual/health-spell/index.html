💗Health Spell

◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Method
Herbal MagickCandle MagickChant/MantraElementalDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 blue votive candle - 1 cauldron - Peppermint oil - 2 tsp lavender flower - 1 tsp thyme - 1 tsp allspice - 1 tsp coriander seed - 1 tsp willow leaf
◇Directions
1. Grind the herbs together and speak: 2. I charge you by the Sun and the Moon, on this day of high energy and hour of healing, to release your powers into my work! 3. Rub peppermint oil on a blue votive candle. 4. Inscribe with the Air, Water, and Earth triangular symbols, a word for the ailment, and then add the following symbols, speaking the name and purpose of each as it is inscribed: 5. Jupiter for health, Beorc for the Goddess, Water for fluids, Tyr for victory, Osa for the God, and Sigel to direct the healing energy. 6. Set the candle in the cauldron and light it, then speak: 7. I call upon thee Great Goddess to hasten my healing. 8. Through this candle dedicated to Health, inscribed with the symbols of the God and the Goddess, Healing Energy and Victory over the Watery confusion in my body, and with the herbs of healing whose energy may be released to my aid do I call upon Thee. 9. Cast aside my sickly imbalance I bid Thee, that as I will, So Mote It Be! 10. Add the herbal mixture and speak: 11. With the protective cleansing power of lavender; with the healing strength of thyme, allspice, and coriander seed, and with the protective and healing power of the willow do I infuse this spell with the power to work my will. 12. So Mote It Be! 13. Burn one hour, then snuff. 14. Scry the wax for images indicating the response time, then bury the remains or dispose of them in the trash.
◇Description
A waxing to Full Moon spell for healing. Herbs are ground and charged, then a blue votive is anointed with peppermint oil and inscribed with elemental triangles, a word for the ailment, and the runes Berkanan, Laguz, Tiwaz, Osa, and Sowilo, along with the Jupiter symbol. The candle is set in the cauldron, the herbal mixture is added to the flame, and the wax is scried for response time.
