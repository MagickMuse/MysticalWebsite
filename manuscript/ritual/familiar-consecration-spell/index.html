Purpose

A circle-casting rite for deepening and formalizing the psychic connection between you and your familiar.

Ingredients

  • Frankincense
  • Optional: dried catnip (for cat familiars)
  • Optional: diviner's sage (for cat familiars)

Directions

Create a circle spacious enough to contain you, your familiar, and any magickal tools you want to consecrate alongside the bond, such as leashes, collars, or ritual implements. Light frankincense along the edge of the circle. Settle inside with your familiar and remain until the moment feels complete. Return to the circle as often as needed to reinforce the connection.

Cat Familiar Variation

🐈
For a cat familiar, outline the circle with dried catnip instead. Replace the frankincense with diviner's sage to sharpen prophetic insight. Let the cat roam and play freely while you stay open to flashes of inspiration.
