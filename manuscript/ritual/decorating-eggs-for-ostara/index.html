🐰Ostara

Ukrainian folk art egg decoration with two traditional methods: Krashanka (single-color eggs for protection and healing) and Pysanka (wax-resist multi-symbol eggs as protective talismans). These magickal eggs can be eaten, displayed, carried as talismans, or given as gifts.

Quantities

  • For Krashanky (single-color eggs):
  • Hard-boiled eggs
  • Natural dye materials: red cabbage (red), beets (purple), yellow onion skins (yellow), carrots (orange), spinach (green)
  • 1 quart water per color
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar per color
  • Jar for each dye
    For Pysanky (wax-resist symbol eggs):
  • Raw eggs
  • Wax crayon or kistka (stylus)
  • Natural dyes as above
  • Oven access for wax removal
  • Paper towels

Directions

  1. Hard-boil your eggs for the single-color method.
  2. Make natural vegetable dyes by combining chopped dye material with a quart of water and boiling.
  3. Strain the liquid into a jar and add 2 tablespoons of white vinegar to set the dye.
  4. Soak the eggs in the color of your choice, making sure the liquid covers the eggs completely.
  5. The longer the eggs soak, the deeper the color will be.
  6. For the most intense color, allow the eggs to soak overnight in the refrigerator.
  7. Be careful when handling freshly dyed eggs, as some dye will rub off.
  8. Single-color eggs possess magickal powers.
  9. They can be eaten, and the shells placed under haystacks or in thatched roofs as protective charms against high winds.
  10. They can also be used for healing: a sick person might wear a whole egg on a string around the neck, or place the egg on the infected part of the body as a cure.
  11. After eating the eggs, burn the shells in a ritual fire or cast them into flowing water; it is bad luck to simply discard them in the trash.
  12. For the wax-resist symbol method, pierce both ends of a raw egg and carefully blow out the contents.
  13. With a wax crayon, draw magick symbols and images on the shell: pentagrams, spirals, runes, or other meaningful symbols.
  14. Immerse the egg in the dye; the wax will prevent the dye from adhering to the marked portions, and the wax designs will be lighter and stand out against the darker background.
  15. When satisfied with your work, allow the shell to dry completely.
  16. Remove the wax by warming the eggshell in an oven for a few minutes, then wiping the wax off with a paper towel.
  17. Wax-resist symbol eggs provide protection against fire and lightning.
  18. Display them in the home, carry them as talismans, or exchange them as gifts.
  19. According to legend, only these eggs can stem the flood of evil that threatens the earth; if people ever gave up the custom, a vicious monster would consume the world.
