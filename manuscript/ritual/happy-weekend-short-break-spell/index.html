🧳Happy Weekend Short Break Spell

A morning spell for happy weekend or short break at home or overseas, using four green candles and travel incense in circle with cord knotted for each day.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Four green candles
  • A long green cord or ribbon
  • Four travel incense sticks: fern, fennel, frankincense, sage, or lavender

Directions

  1. Make a circle with the four candles at the main direction points, with the incense sticks equidistant between them.
  2. Place the ribbon or cord in the center of the circle.
  3. Light each of the candles in turn, beginning with the one to the north, and say as you do so: "Light of laughter, travel with me, brief though this break may be, this I ask and it shall be." Light the incense, beginning with the one in the northeast, saying for each: "Breath of tranquillity, travel with me, brief though this break may be, this I ask and it shall be." Pass the ribbon or cord around each candle, repeating the spell words.
  4. Pass the ribbon or cord through the smoke of each incense stick in turn, again saying all the spell words.
  5. Tie a knot in the ribbon or cord for every day of the break, taking it in your luggage and undoing a knot for every day, making a wish.
  6. Perform the day before your minibreak, in the morning if possible.
