🕯️Lughnassadh Song of the God

◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Method
Chant/MantraDevotional Work
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. Sing at Lughnassadh (August 1) to honor the God's sacrifice and descent: 2. Dance, dance, wherever ye may be! When you dance with the Lord, He will dance with thee. Turn, turn, a Circle then ye form! And the Lord of the Dance is the Lord of the Corn! Down, down, into the Earth he'll go! Giving life to the grain that in Spring we'll sow. He rules the Shadowland 'til Yule, When His Sun is reborn and He joins us anew!
◇Description
A joyful song for Lughnassadh honoring the Lord of the Dance as Lord of the Corn, celebrating his descent into the Earth to give life to the grain until his rebirth at Yule.
