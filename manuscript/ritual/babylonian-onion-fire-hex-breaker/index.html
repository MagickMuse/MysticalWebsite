Onion

Based on an ancient Babylonian spell for those under psychic attack without knowing why. An onion is peeled and the peel thrown into a consecrated fire while chanting. The fire is extinguished along with the spiritual transgressions.

Quantities

1 onion
A fire

Directions

  1. Consecrate a fire.
  2. The subject of the spell peels an onion and tosses the peel into the fire.
  3. (Crying in this spell is good.) Chant incantations in which the undoing of one's "sins" are compared to this activity.
  4. Finally the fire is extinguished, as are the person's sins.
