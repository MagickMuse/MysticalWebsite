📿Duppie Repelling Charm

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
CharmPouch
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Herbal MagickProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Asafoetida Camphor Garlic Black cloth Black silk ribbon (2, one for tying the bag and one for wearing it)
◇Directions
1. Place asafoetida, camphor, and garlic inside a piece of black cloth. 2. Tie the cloth closed with a black silk ribbon. 3. Wear the bag around the neck or waist, secured by a second black silk ribbon.
◇Description
A Jamaican charm bag designed to repel duppies, the harmful and mean-spirited ghosts of Jamaican folk tradition. The bag is worn on the body as ongoing protection.
