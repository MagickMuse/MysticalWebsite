🏠Rebalancing Home Using Four Elements

A beginning of week or month spell for rebalancing home by using four elements, placing earth, air, fire, and water items in every room to blend and harmonize for empowerment by elemental powers.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • For earth: potpourri, salt, dried sage or thyme, fruit, flowers, or a pottery dish
  • For air: an oil burner or incense, dried lavender, a silver knife, a box of pins, feathers, or a wind chime
  • For fire: a mirror, clear crystals, or beads threaded on cord (or a sun catcher), golden candles, sun symbols, spices, or bright metal objects
  • For water: rose or perfumed water, a fish tank, a coffee machine, water bowls with colored stones or crystals, moon symbols, or a small water feature

Directions

  1. Place one of each elemental item in every room or area, saying for each: "Blend and harmonize, mix and synchronize, to make all right." In bedroom, add an extra water element, saying: "Peaceful rest and happy dreams are best." In living and eating areas, add extra water and earth, saying: "Joyous conversation, security, and relaxation." In working areas or hallways, add extra fire and air, saying: "Protection, achievement, and clear direction." Pass through all areas once more, saying: "Balanced be, calm and happy, empowered by elemental powers, every hour." Perform the beginning of a week or month.
