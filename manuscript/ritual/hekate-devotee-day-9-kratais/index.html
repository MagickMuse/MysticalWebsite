Kratais Sigil

Meaning: Strong One. An invocation of Hekate's immutable, oceanic strength, used to banish interfering spirits and ensure safe passage.

Quantities

  • Myrrh ink, for inscribing the sigil

Directions

  1. Sharply strike the back of one hand upon the palm of the other; this sound and gesture, accompanied by the cry of Kratais, immediately banishes unwelcome and interfering spirits from your sacred working space.
  2. When traveling over water, inscribe the Kratais sigil in myrrh ink on the instep of both feet to ensure a safe journey.
  3. Draw the sigil on a delicate object to make it less likely to break.
  4. Inscribe the sigil on your fist to give your blows threefold force.

Arcana

Kratais is the decisive blow, the act that engenders change, the stunning reversal that reveals our true natures, our destinies, our ultimate fate. It yields knowledge of what will be, what will never be, and what has always been. It humbles, it elevates, it reduces, it resolves.

Kratais was also the name of a sea goddess with whom Hekate was sometimes conflated, and who mothered the sea beast Scylla. Kratais therefore has a strong connection with Hekate's Einalian (or oceanic) aspect; and for good reason: like the sea, Hekate is ever-changing, yet always the same. Like a rock that withstands the crashing waves, Hekate is constant and immutable. Thus our changing perception of her nature reveals more about us than it does of her. With each passing age, the wave breaks - she does not.

Hekate is the daughter of Perses - of Destruction itself. She, the Endless One, is our ultimate end: the engine of change who is herself changeless; the axis around which creation rotates and revolves. She was, is, and ever will be the Dominating Force (Damnomenia), the Dominator of Mankind (Damasandra), the Dominator of Dominators (Damnodamia).
