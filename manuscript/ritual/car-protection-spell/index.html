A waxing to Full Moon spell to ward and protect a vehicle. A red candle is inscribed with the runes Thurisaz, Hagaiaz, Nauthiz, Algiz, Berkanan, and Dagaz, along with a triquetra and witch's knot. Herbs are bundled in light blue cloth tied with red string, consecrated through the four Elementals, then decorated with white feathers and silver and black beads. The charm is walked around the car and placed inside for continuous protection.

Quantities

  • 1 square of light blue cotton cloth
  • Pinch of betony
  • Pinch of mustard seed
  • Pinch of fennel seed
  • Pinch of St. John's Wort
  • Red string
  • White feathers
  • 9 silver and black beads
  • Dragon's blood, frankincense, or patchouli incense
  • 1 red candle
  • 1 cauldron
  • Blessed water
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Light the incense.
  2. Inscribe the red candle with the runes Thurisaz, Hagaiaz, Nauthiz, Algiz, Berkanan, and Dagaz, followed by a triquetra and a witch's knot.
  3. Set the candle in the cauldron and light it.
  4. Place betony and mustard seed on the cloth square and speak:
  5. Betony and Mustard Seed for their protective energies!
  6. Add fennel seed and St. John's Wort and speak:
  7. Fennel and St. Johnswort for their power and strength!
  8. Tie the ends of the cloth together with red string and speak:
  9. Red for might!
  10. Pass the bundle through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak:
  11. I call upon thee Elementals to charge this spell and bring forth the Power to work with me as I have cast!
  12. Be thou charged pouch of protection, by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt], by Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke], by Elemental Fire [pass through center candle flame], and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed or spring water].
  13. Decorate the charm with white feathers and speak:
  14. I call upon thee Wind for thy blessing of this charm!
  15. Decorate with silver and black beads and speak:
  16. I call upon thee Earth for thy blessings of this charm!
  17. Open the circle and take the charm to the car.
  18. Walk around the vehicle, touching the charm to the front, back, and sides and speak:
  19. Let the protective powers within this pouch bring safe travel, power, and strength to this vehicle!
  20. Place the charm in the glove compartment, under the front seat, or hang it from the rearview mirror.
  21. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
