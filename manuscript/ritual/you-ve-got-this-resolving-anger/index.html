🔥You've Got This Resolving Anger

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
ElementalBanishingShadow Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Paper and pen (red if available) - Cauldron or sturdy pot - 1 shot of absinthe (100 proof or higher) - Optional: dried wormwood or mugwort for sober version
◇Directions
1. Dig out a cauldron or sturdy pot for this working. 2. Wormwood is useful for resolving anger, transformation, and endurance. 3. It will lift the burden and weight of the rage while shoring up your defenses against any future onslaught. 4. Grab paper and a pen - make it red if you have one. 5. Let your anger burn hotly and write all your frustration out. 6. Start with the issue at hand and branch out to anything causing frustration, anger, or irritation, as those smaller issues can add up. 7. Write it out, draw a picture if helpful, pour it all onto the pages. 8. Once finished, lightly crumple the paper into a ball. 9. Make sure there is enough space in the ball for air to get in; otherwise, the paper won't burn well. 10. Whether you have one piece of paper or ten, stack them in the cauldron. 11. Walk outside, or go on the patio, fire escape, or balcony. 12. Don't set off the fire alarm inside. 13. Grab a shot of absinthe and douse the paper. 14. Make sure the proof is 100 or more - less than that and your paper won't burn. 15. Light! 16. See all of those frustrations, irritations, and issues go up in smoke. 17. Keep your cauldron or pot lid handy in case the contained fire gets out of hand. 18. Safety first, otherwise you have a different set of problems. 19. Sober Substitution: Make your list, crumple the papers into loose ball shapes, and sprinkle with dried wormwood or mugwort. 20. Ignite.
◇Description
A fire transformation spell using wormwood's power to resolve anger and rage. The working lifts the burden and weight of fury while shoring up defenses against future onslaughts.
