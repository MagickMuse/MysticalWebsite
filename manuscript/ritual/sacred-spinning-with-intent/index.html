🧵Sacred Spinning with Intent

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Devotional WorkThread/Sewing Magick
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Your spinning or weaving materials; a moment of pause before beginning
◇Directions
1. Before beginning any fiber work, pause. 2. Set sacred space and speak your honoring intent aloud or silently. 3. Ask Brigid's blessing for yourself, for the work, and for the person for whom you are making it. 4. Reach back mentally to notice the source of your materials: the animal, the land, the hands that sheared and prepared the fleece, the tradition that carries the technique. 5. Give thanks for every step along the way. 6. As you spin or weave, hold the sacred intent in your awareness. 7. You are acting within a continuing history of makers who used similar tools to create sacred and everyday items across millennia. 8. When you finish for the day, offer a brief thanks to Brigid and to all the hands, human and animal, that made your work possible.
◇Description
A practice of setting sacred intent before any spinning, weaving, or fiber work, asking Brigid's blessing for the work and the recipient and giving thanks for each step of the materials' journey from source to hands.
