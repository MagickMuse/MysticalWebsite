Square

A traditional magick square used to produce the experience of aerial flight. The completed square is worn against the body during the working, channeling crow and raven energy.

Quantities

  • Parchment paper
  • Raven's Feather Ink (preferred) or other writing instrument

Directions

  1. Write the following square onto parchment, preferably using Raven's Feather Ink:
  2. R O L O R
    O B U F O
    L U A U L
    O F U B O
    R O L O R
  3. Pin the parchment to your chest.
  4. Close your eyes, relax, and allow the working to carry you.
