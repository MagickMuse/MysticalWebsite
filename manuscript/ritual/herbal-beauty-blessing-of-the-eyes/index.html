👁️Eyebright Eye Compress
↗Symbols3
3🟣Purple⚪White

The first component of the Five-Fold Blessing for Beauty, adapted from the Five-Fold Kiss of Alexandrian and Gardnerian Wiccan initiatory tradition. This rite consecrates the eyes as instruments of perception, prophecy, and magickal presence, drawing on associations ranging from Hera's thousand-eyed peacock and the Eye of Horus to the concept of the third eye. It may be performed as a standalone self-blessing or woven into a broader initiatory rite. Its purpose is to build confidence, cultivate self-acceptance, and call forth recognition from others.

Quantities

  • 3-4 tablespoons dried eyebright
  • 2 cups distilled boiled water
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Medium bowl
  • White cloth
  • 2 cucumber slices
  • Small amethyst crystal
  • 3" x 4" muslin pouch
  • 1/4 cup dried lavender blossoms

Directions

  1. Prepare the eyebright infusion and milk blend, then pour it into a bowl and set it on your altar.
  2. Dip the white cloth into the warm liquid and wring it until damp but not dripping.
  3. Fold the compress and rest it across your closed eyes.
  4. As the warmth moves through you, whisper or speak the blessing aloud:
  5. THE BEAUTY THAT I FEEL WITHIN,
    MAY OTHERS SEE WITHOUT.
    MAY I ACHIEVE A PLACE OF PEACE,
    FAR FROM SHAME OR DOUBT.
    I SHOW FORTH BEAUTY IN MY WORK,
    GRACED BY MAGICK'S HAND.
    MAY THE BEAUTY OF THE WITCH ARISE
    A FLOWER FROM THE LAND.
    THE ASPECT OF BEAUTY, I INVOKE
    TO SEE AND TO BE SEEN
    LET ALL WHOSE GAZE MY EYES SHALL MEET
    REGARD ME AS A QUEEN.
  6. Lift the compress, wring it out again, and return it to your eyes.
  7. Repeat until you feel the full benefit of the herbs.
  8. When finished, do not discard the liquid by pouring it down the drain.
  9. Offer it to the earth or a garden instead, or store it in the refrigerator for up to seven days to repeat or share the blessing.
  10. Once the warmth of the compress has done its work, lay cool cucumber slices over your eyes and settle into a reclined position.
  11. Place the amethyst inside the muslin pouch and fill the rest of the pouch with the dried lavender.
  12. Rest the pouch over your third eye or hold it at your brow.
  13. Breathe in the lavender and enter a meditative state.
  14. Contemplate the particular quality of your vision, not only your eye color and the sensory world your eyes open to you, but the inner sight you carry.
  15. Those with visual impairments are reminded that it is the mind, not the eye, that ultimately perceives.
  16. Allow visions to arise, receive them openly, and let this meditation be an act of full acceptance of your beauty and an invitation to let it be witnessed by the world.
