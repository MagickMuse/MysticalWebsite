🧜Mama Jo Yaws Healing Spell

A beach ritual petitioning Mama Jo (from the French Maman de l'eau, 'Mother of the Water'), a mermaid spirit of the West Indies who cures diseases, especially yaws. The spell may be performed on any beach, not just in the Caribbean, and may be cast by one person on behalf of another.

Quantities

Silver candles (one per year of the ailing person's age; silver birthday candles work)
Small image of a black goat or black chicken (toy, or handmade from wax, wood, or wool)
Beach access

Directions

  1. Dig out a flat altar space within the sand.
  2. Place silver candles in the sand, one for every year of the ailing person's age, and light them.
  3. Place a small image of a black goat or black chicken near the shore; a child's farm toy or similar works well, and you may create your own from wax, wood, or wool if desired.
  4. Step into the water so that at least your feet are wet, call Mama Jo, tell her what you've brought and what you need, then leave whenever you're ready.
  5. Whatever happens to the image is up to Mama Jo, whether the waves pull it into the water or another person picks it up.
