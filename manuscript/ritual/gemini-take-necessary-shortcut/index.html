♊Gemini Take Necessary Shortcut

A between May 22 and June 21 or need ingenuity of Gemini spell for Gemini ritual if you need to take necessary shortcut and don't want to be detected, whispering secret to five dark stones with sleep mask to hide from disapproving eyes until crisis through.

Quantities

  • Five dark stones
  • A sleep mask, the kind worn on planes
  • A small bag

Directions

  1. Hold each stone in turn, saying: "Hide from the world what I must do, until this crisis is through." Bring each stone close to your mouth in turn, and whisper the shortcut you must take, then put it in the bag, saying: "Hold my secret safe." Set the bag in front of you and put on the sleep mask, saying: "Keep hidden from the eyes of those who'd shake their heads disapprovingly, for if I do this to survive, better times I'll see." Fold the sleep mask into a small bundle and place it in the bag with the stones, close the bag, and hide it where it cannot be found, saying: "Keep my secret safe." When the crisis is over, throw away the stones in water and recycle the mask.
  2. Perform between May 22 and June 21, or whenever you need to call on the ingenuity of Gemini the Twins.
