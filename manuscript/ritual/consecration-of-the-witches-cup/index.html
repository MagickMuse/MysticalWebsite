🍷Consecration of the Witches' Cup

A waxing moon working that steeps protective herbs in salt water, exorcises the vessel with charged fire and water, then seals it with painted runes and the practitioner's own witch name.

Quantities

A goblet of nonporous material, three to five inches across, not yet consecrated
A bowl of fresh water and a small handful of new salt
Powdered vervain, mint, basil, rosemary, hyssop, lavender, sage, valerian, and fennel
Quality incense and a charcoal block
Black or white enamel paint, or stove black, optionally mixed with a pinch of the powdered herbs
A new paintbrush

Directions

  1. During the waxing moon, steep the powdered herbs (vervain, mint, basil, rosemary, hyssop, lavender, sage, valerian, and fennel) in a bowl of salt water.
  2. Charge this water and a separate incense fire using the salt water and incense charging formula, holding your will, faith, and imagination toward the elements glowing with purifying light.
  3. Sprinkle the cup with the charged water, then pass it through the incense smoke, visualizing blue light flickering around the cup as you chant:
    By flame and water I call to you,
    let nothing hostile, nothing untrue,
    remain within your shape or form.
    Hear my will and heed my voice;
    as I have spoken, so shall it be!
  4. Paint the proper runes around the cup with the enamel paint, mixing in a pinch of the powdered herbs if you wish, and chant as each one is painted:
    Blessed be, vessel of the waters!
  5. Paint the runes of your witch name around the base of the cup, speaking each letter aloud as you go.
  6. Close the working with the words So mote it be!, then set the finished cup safely away for future use.
