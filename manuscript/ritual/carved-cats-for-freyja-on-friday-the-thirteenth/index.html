A candle rite performed on Friday the 13th, petitioning Freyja for fertility. Cat candles are carved, dressed, and burned in her honor on the day that combines Friday (her sacred day as a goddess of female power) with the number thirteen (the lunar number). Friday the 13th is one of the most potent days for invoking Freyja and goddesses of primal female power.

Quantities

Cat-shaped candles (one or more)
Dressing oil of your choice (associated with Freyja, love, or fertility)

Directions

  1. On Friday the 13th, obtain cat-shaped candles.
  2. Carve petitions, symbols, or Freyja's name into the wax.
  3. Dress the candles with an appropriate oil.
  4. Light them and speak your petition to Freyja clearly: name your desire for a child, the state of your heart, and what you are asking her to do.
  5. Tend the candles as they burn.

Friday the 13th has long been considered ominous precisely because it combines the day most associated with goddesses of primal female power with thirteen, the number of months in the lunar year. That combination of sacred feminine energy was a threat to patriarchal religious authority, and so the day was recast as evil.

For those who work with Freyja, Friday the 13th is among the most charged and effective days of the calendar. Freyja's power over love, sex, fertility, magick, and death makes her one of the most reliable intercessors for reproductive petitions.

The cat is her sacred animal. Cat candles combine her symbol with the traditional practice of candle petition magick.
