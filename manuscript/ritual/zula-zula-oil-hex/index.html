📌Zula-Zula Oil Hex

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAnointing
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 black figure candle or 7-knob candle 6 silver pins or needles Zula-Zula Oil
◇Directions
1. Anoint a black figure candle or a seven-knob candle with Zula-Zula Oil. 2. Push six silver pins or needles into the head or top of the candle, concentrating on the hatred felt toward the target with each pin. 3. Light the candle at midnight on the first day the moon wanes. 4. Allow the candle to burn for half an hour each day until it burns out entirely. 5. Each day the candle is lit, stare into the flames and think of nothing but hatred for the target. 6. On the final day, when the candle is about to burn out, voice your intent for the target three times aloud. 7. Close your eyes and let the flame extinguish on its own.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A multi-day candle hex fuelled by Zula-Zula Oil. A black figure or seven-knob candle is anointed with the oil and pierced with six silver pins while channelling hatred toward the target. The candle is lit at midnight on the first night of the waning moon and burned for half an hour daily. On the final day, the practitioner voices their intent three times before the flame dies.
