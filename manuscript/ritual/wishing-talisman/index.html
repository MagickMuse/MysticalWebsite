🪵Wishing Talisman

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
ManifestationConsecration
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
Wood (traditionally sraktya wood, or a suitable substitute)
◇Directions
1. Choose a wood that suits your purpose, consider woods used for magick wands. The Atharva Veda recommends sraktya wood, but a more accessible wood may be substituted. 2. Cut the wood to resemble the object of your desire. Possibilities include: protection, fertility, virility, prosperity, defense against malicious magick, or victory in conflict or battle. 3. Create, charge, and/or consecrate the charm. 4. Tie it to your right arm and wear it until your goal is achieved.
◇Description
A talisman-carving spell from the ancient Sanskrit magickal text the Atharva Veda. Wood is cut to resemble the object of one's desire, then charged and worn tied to the right arm until the goal is achieved. Wood used for magick wands is recommended.
