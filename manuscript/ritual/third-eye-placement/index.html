The active approach to crystal past life work. A crystal is placed directly on the Third Eye, the point just above where the brows meet, while the practitioner makes a deliberate, focused attempt to access past life memory. The source cautions that these memories respond to openness and intention, not force: they cannot be commanded to appear. One of the Crystal Past Life Spell methods.

Quantities

  • One past life crystal: cuprite, garnet, jasper, brown jasper, serpentine, or variscite

Directions

  1. Lie down in a comfortable position.
  2. Place one crystal on your Third Eye, the area just above the midpoint of your brow line.
  3. Close your eyes and gently, consciously attempt to remember.
  4. Maintain a receptive rather than commanding intention: past life memories surface in response to openness, not demand.
  5. Remain still and attentive to whatever impressions arise.
