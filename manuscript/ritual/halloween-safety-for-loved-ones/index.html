A year-end ritual sprinkling sage or rosemary in flame, naming sorrows, extinguishing and relighting for rebirth and new dreams.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 large red candle or hearth fire
  • Dried sage or rosemary

Directions

  1. Light the candle, saying, "This is the time of the year for the old fires to die and new ones be kindled that life may blaze anew." Either alone or with family members or close friends, sit or kneel close to the candle or hearth fire.
  2. Sprinkle just a few grains of herbs into the flame, saying, "There are no regrets for the death of the old, for the new will return and burn bright." Name a sorrow or regret you are consigning to the flame.
  3. Extinguish the fire or candle and then relight it, saying, "From the old comes new, from death rebirth and from decay growth." Pour any remaining herbs onto the fire, or outdoors if you're using a candle, saying, "At the year's turning, past triumphs and disasters are all the same, all to be consigned to yesterday and so I/we name our future dreams." Perform Halloween (the end of the Celtic year) or New Year's Eve.
