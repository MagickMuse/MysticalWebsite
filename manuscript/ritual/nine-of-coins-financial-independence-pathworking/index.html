🔮Nine of Coins Financial Independence Pathworking

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationManifestation
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• The Nine of Coins (Nine of Pentacles) tarot card • A green candle
◇Directions
1. Separate the Nine of Coins card from a Tarot deck. 2. Kindle a green candle. 3. Set the card upright before the flame and study its imagery closely. 4. Project your awareness into the scene depicted on the card and converse with it silently as the candle continues to burn.
◇Description
The Nine of Coins (Nine of Pentacles) represents the woman of independent means. This meditative pathworking uses candlelight and visualization to enter the card and commune with its energy of self-sufficient wealth.
