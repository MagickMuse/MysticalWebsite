💗Four Elementals Rune Healing Spell

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickCandle MagickChant/MantraElementalConsecrationInvocationDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Sandalwood incense - Goddess and God candles - 2 yellow votive candles - 2 containers for votives - Rue oil - Salt - Blessed water - Pinch each: ash bark, Saint John's Wort, tansy, woodruff - An herb related to the illness
◇Directions
1. Anoint two yellow votive candles with rue oil and speak: 2. These candles are dedicated to the healing of [Name], in the Names of the Lady and the Lord [Names]. 3. Pass each yellow votive through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak: 4. I consecrate thee through the power of Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt], through the power of Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke], through the power of Elemental Fire [pass through the flames of both Deity candles], and through the power of Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water]. 5. Inscribe both yellow candles with the runes Berkanan, Ansuz, Thurisaz, Dagaz, Osa, Kenaz, Uruz, and Ingwaz, along with a Witch's Knot. 6. Light one votive candle from the Goddess candle and one from the God candle. 7. Add the following herbs into the flames of each votive candle and speak: 8. Ash bark for health and protection; Saint John's Wort for health, protection, and strength; tansy for health and the love of the Goddess; woodruff for victory and the love of the God; [ailment herb] for the healing of [name or type of ailment]. 9. Let the votive candles burn until reduced nearly to the bottom of the container. 10. Snuff out and scry the wax. 11. Open the Circle. 12. Bury the remains of wax or dispose of them in the trash.
◇Description
A waxing to Full Moon healing spell dedicated to a named person. Two yellow votive candles are anointed with rue oil and consecrated through the four Elementals. Both are inscribed with the runes Berkanan, Ansuz, Thurisaz, Dagaz, Osa, Kenaz, Uruz, and Ingwaz, along with a Witch's Knot. Each votive is lit from a Deity candle, and healing herbs are added to the flames. The wax is scried after the candles burn down.
