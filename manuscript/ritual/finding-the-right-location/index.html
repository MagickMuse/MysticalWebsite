💬Wednesday

A Wednesday spell for finding right location by using magnet and pins, scattering pins on map with eyes closed to show where you will love the best and venture forth.

Quantities

  • A map covering the entire area that interests you
  • More detailed maps of the area, either a road map or one printed from the Internet
  • A box of pins
  • A magnet

Directions

  1. Spread out the area map on the floor and say: "North, south, east, west, show me where I will love the best. East, west, south, north, show me where to venture forth." Kneeling close to the map, close your eyes, repeat the words, and scatter the pins.
  2. Look where the largest concentration of pins has fallen, and if they are scattered, repeat until you get a clear result.
  3. Gather all the pins with the magnet and return them to the box.
  4. Take a detailed map of the area where there are most pins and repeat the words and actions until you have an even closer idea of the right location.
  5. Even if the location is unexpected, arrange to visit the area, and, as you explore, you will find the ideal home.
  6. Perform Wednesday.
