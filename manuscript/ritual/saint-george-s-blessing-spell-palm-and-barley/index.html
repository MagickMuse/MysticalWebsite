✨Saint George's Blessing Spell (Palm and Barley)

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
ProtectionConsecration
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 blessed palm frond (or wormwood/rosemary substitute) - 2 whole raw eggs - 1 pot of raw barley - Candles - 1 torch - Holy water or protective water formula
◇Directions
1. Begin with a palm frond blessed on Palm Sunday. 2. For a non-Christian substitution, use a sacred protective plant such as wormwood or rosemary. 3. Place the palm or plant before a sacred image along with two whole raw eggs and a pot filled with raw barley. 4. Light candles before the image. 5. Use a candle flame to light a torch. 6. Gather all the animals to be protected in a circle. 7. One person walks around the animals carrying the torch while a second person sprinkles holy water or another protective water formula over them, using the palm frond or plant as an aspergillum. 8. When the circling is complete, drive the animals to pasture by gently slapping them with the frond or branch. 9. Throw the palm or plant into running water. 10. Sow the barley in the ground. 11. Break the eggs on the ground well away from your property.
◇Description
An elaborate Saint George's Day blessing combining a blessed palm frond, raw eggs, barley, candles, and holy water to encircle and protect livestock before driving them to pasture.
