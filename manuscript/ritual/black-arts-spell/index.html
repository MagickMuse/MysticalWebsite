👤Black Arts Spell

◇Quantities
1 voodoo doll (made from black cloth) Black Arts Oil 1 black candle 1 small box Black cloth Twine 9 pennies 1 small bottle of rum
◇Directions
1. Make a voodoo doll out of black cloth to represent your enemy. 2. Anoint the doll with Black Arts Oil. 3. Each day for nine days, burn a black candle for seventeen minutes, holding the candle over the doll and allowing the wax to drip onto it. 4. While doing this, curse at the doll as if it were your enemy, saying every vile thing you have ever wanted to say, yelling and screaming freely. 5. After the ninth day, place the remainder of the candle along with the doll in a small box. 6. Wrap the box in black cloth and tie it closed with twine using nine knots. 7. Take the box to a cemetery and bury it. 8. Pay the spirits of the cemetery with nine pennies and a small bottle of rum. 9. Walk away without looking back.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING ⚠️ Hoodoo & Voodoo are closed practices sacred to African American and African diasporic spiritual traditions and should only be practiced by those within the originating cultures. A nine-day voodoo doll working. A doll made of black cloth is anointed with Black Arts Oil, then each day for nine days a black candle is burned for seventeen minutes while wax drips onto the doll and the practitioner curses aloud. After the ninth day, the doll and candle remnant are placed in a box, wrapped in black cloth, tied with nine knots of twine, and buried in a cemetery with an offering of nine pennies and rum to the spirits.
