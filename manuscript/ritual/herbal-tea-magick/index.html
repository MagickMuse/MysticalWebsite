🧪Herbal Tea Magick

◇Form
PotionElixir
◇Method
Herbal MagickDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Clear-seeing tea: - Cinnamon (dried) - Dried orange peel - Equal parts of each Mind magicks tea: - Cinnamon (dried) - 2 parts - Cardamom - 1 part Winter relaxer tea: - Spearmint (dried) - 2 parts - Chamomile (dried) - 1 part Tummy-settling tea: - Peppermint (dried) - Thyme (dried) - Equal parts of each For brewing: - Heaping spoonful of chosen tea mixture - Very hot water (not quite boiling) - Optional: Honey for sweetness
◇Directions
Preparing the Tea: 1. Place a heaping spoonful of your chosen herbal mixture into a tea infuser in a warmed mug or teacup, OR spoon the herbs directly into the cup. 2. Fill with water that is very hot but not quite boiling. 3. Allow to steep for 3 to 5 minutes. 4. Remove the infuser (or strain the mixture) and enjoy. 5. If desired, swirl in a spoonful of honey for sweetness. Magickal Use: Use these teas as part of any magickal working, before, during, or after, or simply for a delicious moment during your winter day. Variation, Tea Leaf Reading: For divination, spoon the herbs directly into a teacup (rather than a mug) and once the tea has steeped, drink without straining. Leave about a teaspoon of water in the cup, swirl it a few times, then read the tea leaves.
◇Description
Four customizable herbal tea blends designed for specific magickal intentions: clear-seeing for mental clarity and psychic powers, mind magicks for intuition, winter relaxer for rest and relaxation, and tummy-settling for digestive comfort. Can be used before, during, or after magickal workings.
