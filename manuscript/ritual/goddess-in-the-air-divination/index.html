Imbolc

An Imbolc divination that reads symbols in clouds or incense smoke, relying on personal intuition to interpret the signs for the coming light half of the year.

Quantities

  • If outdoors: paper and pen or a journal
  • If indoors: censer or two small censers, two sticks or cones of incense, or charcoal with loose incense or pellets, white tealight candle and holder, black cloth, paper and pen or a journal, window cracked for ventilation

Directions

  1. Timing and setting: Best on Imbolc with cooperative weather, but workable any time.
  2. Outdoors uses clouds.
  3. Indoors uses smoke.
  4. Outdoors, clouds version:
  5. Choose a time with some clouds, not clear sky and not solid overcast, and without active rain or snow.
  6. Sit or recline comfortably with paper and pen ready.
  7. Day invocation:
    Great God in the Sky, as your light reflects from the clouds I see, reveal to me the things I need to know about what is to be.
  8. Night invocation (full or near‑full Moon, partly cloudy):
    Great Goddess, in your guise as the Moon, daughter of the earth and sister to humankind, as your light reflects from the clouds I see, reveal to me the things I need to know about the future.
  9. Indoors, smoke version:
  10. Crack a window slightly for ventilation. Lay a black cloth on a table. Place the censer(s) at center and the candle 3 to 4 inches in front, or between two censers set about 4 inches apart.
  11. Light the candle and invoke:
    Goddess of the Flame, I call upon you to help reveal the Goddess in the Smoke, and illuminate the signs she sends so I will see them all.
  12. Light two sticks or cones from the candle and set them 4 inches apart, or sprinkle incense on pre‑lit charcoal. Offer:
    I offer this incense to the Goddess in the Smoke, that she might reveal to me the things I need to know about what is to be.
  13. Working method, both versions:
  14. Ask one clear question at a time, aloud if possible. Watch clouds or smoke for signs before asking the next.
  15. If you prefer, ask a sequence first, then observe, noting that it may blur associations.
  16. Position candle light to illuminate smoke from below for better contrast. Be especially observant indoors, where shapes change quickly.
  17. Recording and interpreting:
  18. Write each question and the shapes you see, animals, plants, objects, people, and underline or highlight when one shape transforms into another.
  19. End the indoor session when the first stick or cone burns out, or let charcoal run its course. Do not relight new incense during a reading. Extinguish the candle last.
  20. Review your notes after, and again at next Imbolc. Interpret through your own intuition, considering literal and symbolic meanings. The same sign can mean different things to different seers.
  21. Safety:
  22. Ventilate indoor rooms. Use heat‑safe censers and never leave incense or candles unattended.
