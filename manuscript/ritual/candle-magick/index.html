Candle Magick

Candle magick is one of the most accessible and versatile forms of spellwork. A single candle, chosen with intention, can serve as the entire foundation of a working. This guide covers the key decisions involved in planning a candle spell: selecting the right candle type and shape, dressing it with oil, inscribing planetary symbols, timing your burn to the correct planetary hour, and aligning with the appropriate moon phase.

Choosing a Candle Type

Different types of candles serve different purposes. The shape, size, and style of your candle should match the kind of work you are doing.

Spell Work

Votive Candle or Pillar Candle candles are the standard choice for most spellwork. They burn steadily and can be carved, dressed, and charged with intention.

Ritual

Taper Candle candles are traditional for formal ritual use. Their tall, elegant shape makes them ideal for altar work, invocations, and ceremonial settings.

Meditation and Divination

Votive Candle, Pillar Candle, or Taper Candle candles all work well for meditation and divination. Choose whichever feels most comfortable for the length of your session.

General Aura and Atmosphere

For setting the mood or creating ambient energy in a space, use a Votive Candle, Pillar Candle, or Glass Container Candle candle. Glass jars are especially practical for long, unattended burns.

Specified Spell Work

Some spells call for candles shaped to match the target or intention of the working. Options include Votive Candle, Pillar Candle, Figure Candle (man or woman), animal shapes (Cat Candle, Owl Candle, Dragon Candle, Snake Candle), and Skull Candle candles for workings focused on the mind.

Multi-Day Spells (5 to 7 Days)

For spells that unfold over several days, use a Knobby Candle candle, a tall Pillar Candle, or a Glass Container Candle candle. Burn one portion (one knob, or a measured section) each day until the spell is complete.

Dressing the Candle

Before burning, dress (anoint) your candle with a magickal oil that matches your intention. Refer to the Oil Grimoire for oils organized by correspondence. Apply the oil by rubbing it onto the candle from the center outward toward both ends, or from base to tip for drawing energy in, or tip to base for sending energy out.

Carve one or more planetary symbols into the candle wax to align your spell with planetary influence. You may use any combination that supports your goal.

Timing by Planetary Hour

Burn your candle for one hour during the planetary hour that matches your intention. Each day and night is divided into twelve planetary hours, and each hour is ruled by a different planet depending on the day of the week. Consult the Hours Correspondences by Day of the Week in the Timing Grimoire to find the right hour for your working.

Moon Phase Alignment

Align your candle spell with the correct moon phase to strengthen the working.

  • Waxing Crescent Moon/Waxing Gibbous Moon: For spells of increase, growth, attraction, and building. Use when drawing something toward you.
  • Full Moon: Peak power. Ideal for manifestation, divination, charging, and any working that needs maximum energy.
  • Waning Gibbous Moon/Waning Crescent Moon: For spells of decrease, release, banishing, and letting go. Use when sending something away.
  • Dark Moon or New Moon: For deep shadow work, binding, cursing, endings, and workings that require stillness or secrecy.

For specific moon phase dates and additional lunar timing, see the Timing Grimoire.

Candle Techniques

A symbol may be inscribed near the top of a candle so that as the symbol melts, the magick is released. A pin may be used to designate an accomplishment point: stick a pin into the candle near the top, stating that when the candle melts to that point the spell is sent into action, then chant, shake a rattle, meditate, or do whatever feels right while the candle burns down to the pin.

A goal may be written down and burned in the candle flame during spell casting, seeing the spell as sent on its way. Herbs may also be burned in the candle flame, stating the herb and its purpose while seeing the energy released to perform the task assigned.

Lychnomancy: Divination by Candle

Any candle lit for any purpose can simultaneously serve as a divination tool. Lychnomancy focuses on candle flames as the primary source of oracular information, but the wax, shadows, smoke, and ashes that a candle produces all carry interpretive value as well. Flame, wax, and smoke can each be read independently or coordinated together for a more layered reading. Those new to candle divination may find it easier to focus on one source at a time before working with all three.

Reading Candle Signs

Altar candles dedicated to a deity can serve as a direct oracular channel. The flame, wax, and smoke each carry interpretive meaning when working with an invoked spirit.

  • Flame shooting high and bright: The spirit you have invoked has arrived and is present.
  • Steady, calm flame: The working is proceeding without interference.
  • Flame guttering or going out: Resistance, interference, or the spirit's absence.
  • Smoke patterns: Observe the direction and shape of the smoke as it rises for additional signs.
  • Wax formations: As the wax melts and pools, shapes and patterns may form that carry oracular significance.

Working with Photos

A photo may be used in spellwork, but exercise great care so as not to violate the Rules of Conduct. It is wrong to try to force someone into an unwanted action. Photos may be used in healings and similar workings, and may be burned, torn, wrapped, or buried as the spell requires.

Pouches

Items may be combined into pouches to place somewhere or carry as ongoing spell vessels.

Putting It All Together

A complete candle magick working follows this sequence:

  1. Set your intention clearly.
  2. Choose a candle color that matches your goal (see the Material Grimoire for candle color entries).
  3. Select a candle shape that fits the type of working.
  4. Dress the candle with a corresponding oil.
  5. Inscribe planetary symbols that align with your intention.
  6. Burn for one hour during the appropriate planetary hour.
  7. Perform the working during the correct moon phase.

You may also use multiple candles of different colors, or candles with multiple colors, to layer intentions within a single working.

Colors in Dark Workings

Each candle color carries specific applications in baneful and destructive magick. Black should be present alongside any other color used in these workings, as it is the all-purpose foundation for dark spellcraft.
