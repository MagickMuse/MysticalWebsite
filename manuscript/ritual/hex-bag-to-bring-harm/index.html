⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice sacred to African American spiritual traditions and should only be practiced by those within the originating culture.
A highly customizable hex bag assembled from baneful materials including coffin nails, pins, a broken or pin-stuck doll wrapped in black cloth, personal effects from the target, graveyard dirt, and an array of baneful herbs. The practitioner selects whichever components suit their intent and may add items of their own choosing. Once assembled, the bag is anointed with crossing, cursing, or damnation oil.

Quantities

1 flannel bag
Small black candles
Coffin nails
Pins or needles
1 image of the target
1 doll
Black cloth
Nail clippings, clothing, or hair from the target
Graveyard dirt
Asafoetida
Red pepper
Dog grass
Black mustard seed
Tormentilla
Mugwort
Henbane
Belladonna
Crossing, cursing, or damnation oil

Directions

  1. Fill a flannel bag with your chosen combination of baneful materials.
  2. Options include small black candles, coffin nails, pins or needles, an image of the target torn in half or in pieces, a doll stuck with pins or broken in two and wrapped in black cloth, nail clippings, a piece of clothing, or hair from the target, graveyard dirt, and roots and herbs such as asafoetida, red pepper, dog grass, black mustard seed, tormentilla, mugwort, henbane, and belladonna.
  3. You do not need to use every listed material; select only what suits your needs and add different items as desired.
  4. When the bag is fully assembled, anoint it with crossing, cursing, or damnation oil.
