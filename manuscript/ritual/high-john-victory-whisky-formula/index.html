High John the Conquerer

Nine High John roots are steeped in whisky for 38 hours, then strained and blended with a lucky cologne to create a wearable victory formula. Based on Zora Neale Hurston's documentation. A Hoodoo closed practice.

Quantities

  • 9 small High John the Conqueror roots (or 1 large root, broken into 9 pieces)
  • Half a pint of whisky
  • ~1 oz lucky cologne (Jockey Club, Florida Water, or Chypre)

Directions

  1. Take nine small High John roots, or break one large root into nine pieces.
  2. Cover the roots with half a pint bottle of whisky; drink or offer the remaining half to whatever spirit you appeal to for protection.
  3. Steep the roots in the whisky for 38 hours (per Zora Neale Hurston's recommendation) or overnight if needed.
  4. Strain the liquid into a bottle.
  5. Add approximately one ounce of a lucky cologne, Jockey Club is the traditional choice, though Florida Water or Chypre (especially for financial proceedings) also work well.
  6. Wear as needed.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: High John the Conqueror root workings are central to Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

High John the Conqueror root is one of the most celebrated botanicals in Hoodoo — said to promote victory in any endeavor while conquering all opposition. This formula, documented by the great writer and folklorist Zora Neale Hurston, transforms the root into a wearable victory cologne.

  1. Take nine small High John roots, or break one large root into nine pieces.
  2. Cover the roots with half a pint of whisky. Drink the remaining half yourself — or offer it to the spirit you are appealing to for protection.
  3. Allow the roots to steep in the whisky for 38 hours (Zora Neale Hurston's recommendation), or overnight if circumstances require.
  4. Strain the liquid into a clean bottle, discarding or preserving the roots.
  5. Add approximately one ounce of a lucky cologne:
    • Jockey Club — the traditional choice
    • Florida Water — a widely available, effective alternative
    • Chypre — especially recommended if the proceedings involve financial matters
  6. Wear as needed before and during court appearances.
Note: The steeping time of 38 hours is Hurston's own specification. Others in the tradition consider overnight sufficient. Follow your instincts and the guidance of your tradition.
