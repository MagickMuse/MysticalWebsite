◇Directions
1. Meditation provides an effective way of gaining an increased understanding of your chakras. 2. This practice allows you to explore each energy center in turn, noticing what insights, images, and thoughts come to you. 3. Preparation: Relax and enter into the meditative state. 4. Root Chakra: When you are ready, focus your attention on your root chakra at the base of your spine. Notice how it grounds you to the earth, and provides you with a sense of security. Add red to your mental picture and see if your feelings change in any way. Observe the images and thoughts that come to you as you focus on your root chakra. You can stop at this point if you wish, or continue exploring the other chakras in turn. 5. Sacral Chakra: Focusing on your sacral chakra may give you insights into your sexuality, emotions, and how you experience pleasure. Visualize orange light at this energy center and notice what arises. 6. Solar Plexus Chakra: Your solar plexus chakra may tell you how much joy there is in your life. Add yellow to this center and observe what you discover. 7. Heart Chakra: Your heart chakra might give you insight into love and relationships. Visualize green light at your heart and notice what emerges. 8. Throat Chakra: Your throat chakra might offer suggestions on how you communicate with others and help you become more creative. Focus on blue light at your throat and observe what comes. 9. Brow Chakra: Your brow chakra may provide ideas to develop your imagination, vision, and clairvoyance. Visualize indigo light at your third eye and notice what insights appear. 10. Crown Chakra: Finally, your crown chakra might help you understand, know, and gain a closer connection with the universe. Focus on violet light at the crown of your head and observe what understanding comes to you. 11. Closing: When you have explored the chakras that call to you, take a few deep breaths and gently return to awareness of your physical body. Open your eyes when ready.
◇Description
An exploratory meditation for gaining understanding of your seven chakras. Focus on each energy center in turn, observe what insights arise, and enhance the experience by visualizing the corresponding color.
