A waxing to Full Moon spell performed during the hour of Mars on a Tuesday to protect a warrior and ensure safe return. A mandrake or fennel root is consecrated through the four Elementals, placed on a pentacle, and addressed as a rootman. The root is wrapped in red cotton yarn, charged with energy, then wrapped in red cloth and hidden until the warrior returns home safely. Upon return, the root is unbound and buried to release the spell.

Quantities

  • 1 mandrake root or fennel root
  • Incense
  • 1 red candle
  • Blessed water
  • Salt
  • Red cotton yarn
  • Red cloth

Directions

  1. Take the mandrake or fennel root and pass it through incense smoke and candle flame.
  2. Sprinkle with blessed water and salt.
  3. Speak:
  4. I consecrate this root by the powers of the Elementals that it may be cleansed and purified to aid in my work.
  5. Set the root on a pentacle and speak:
  6. Little man, listen to me.
  7. I will dress thee and keep thee, blessed in my company, that thou turn aside harm from my fighting man.
  8. That he return safely as soon as he can.
  9. Wrap the root in red cotton yarn.
  10. Raise energy and direct it into the rootman, speaking:
  11. In the eye of Mars, by the fires of fury, let my warrior be steadfast, safe-kept, and victorious.
  12. Wrap the root in a red cloth and hide it in a safe place.
  13. Keep it there until the warrior returns, then unbind the root and bury it.
  14. Speak:
  15. You have served me well, now I unbind this spell.
  16. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
