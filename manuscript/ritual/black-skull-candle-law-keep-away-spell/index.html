🧪Law Keep Away Oil

A Hoodoo candle spell especially effective for avoiding taxation and immigration authorities: a black skull candle dressed with Law Keep Away Oil is burned in timed increments over written goals, culminating in burning the paper completely. This is a Hoodoo closed practice.

Quantities

  • 1 black skull candle
  • Law Keep Away Oil (for dressing)
  • Paper and pen
  • 1 small saucer

Directions

  1. Dress a black skull candle with Law Keep Away Oil.
  2. Write your goals and desired protections on paper.
  3. Light the candle and scorch the paper in its flame.
  4. Place the paper beneath an upside-down saucer, set the candle on the saucer over it, and burn the candle in consistent timed increments, pinching it out each time.
  5. When the candle has burned fairly low, burn the paper completely in its flame before allowing the candle to finish burning naturally.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: This candle spell is rooted in Hoodoo magick tradition. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.

This spell is considered especially effective for those seeking protection specifically from taxation authorities and immigration officials.

  1. Dress a black skull candle thoroughly with Law Keep Away Oil.
  2. Write your goals, desires, and the specific protection you need on a piece of paper.
  3. Light the skull candle and scorch the paper in its flame — do not burn it completely yet, only char it.
  4. Turn a small saucer upside down. Place the scorched paper beneath it. Set the candle on top of the upside-down saucer, so it burns directly over the paper.
  5. Burn the candle in consistently timed increments appropriate to your situation (choose your interval and stick to it). When the time is up, pinch the candle out with your fingers — do not blow it out.
  6. Repeat the timed burning sessions. When the candle has burned down fairly low:
    • Burn the paper completely in the candle's flame.
    • Allow the candle to finish burning out naturally from that point.
The skull candle shape symbolizes power over opposition and the ability to turn enemies' intentions back on themselves.
