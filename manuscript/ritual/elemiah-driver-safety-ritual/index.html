🚗Elemiah Driver Safety Ritual

◇Tradition
Ceremonial
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary

◇Setting

◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
• 1 blue candle • 1 turquoise crystal or a green-and-black malachite, purple-striated kunzite, or black-and-purple sugilite (all Elemiah safe-driving crystals) • 1 blue bag or purse • Any two of the following: dried sage, celery seeds, eucalyptus, fennel, or mint • 1 bowl
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and hold the crystal in cupped hands toward the light, saying: "Elemiah, drive the hard roads with me in darkness and in light. Uphold me through long hours that sap my powers and deadlines I struggle to meet." Put the crystal in the bag. 2. Add the herbs to the bowl and run your fingers through them, repeating the spell words. 3. Pour the herbs into the bag and close it, shaking it three times and saying: "Elemiah, be my friend, from journey's start to journey's end. Wise angel, be I ask my guide, and stay always at my side." Keep the closed bag in the glove compartment of the vehicle or hidden under your seat. 4. Take it out and hold it before beginning a journey and whenever you are tired or hit bad weather.
◇Description
An Elemiah, angel of drivers, ritual for safety on the road and reasonable work hours and conditions, using a blue candle and safe-driving crystals with herbs in a bag for protection. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
