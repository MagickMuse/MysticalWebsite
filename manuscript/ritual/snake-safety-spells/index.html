A collection of folk methods for repelling snakes or ensuring safe passage around them, drawn from various traditions including Italian snake-charming practice.

Quantities

  • Galbanum resin or essential oil (to burn as incense, warm in aroma burner, or add to perfume)
  • Juniper wood and leaves (to burn)
  • Black cohosh root (for bath infusion)
  • Serpentine stone (to carry)
  • Agate stone, plain or engraved (to carry)

Directions

  1. Choose one or more methods.
  2. Burn galbanum as incense or warm it in an aroma burner to repel snakes from a space; it may also be added to perfume.
  3. Burn juniper wood and leaves to repel serpents.
  4. Prepare an infusion of black cohosh and add it to bathwater so the aroma discourages snakes; black cohosh is not safe during pregnancy.
  5. Carry serpentine as a charm for safety from snakes.
  6. From Italian snake-charming tradition, serpentine provides safety without repelling, making it suitable for those wishing to maintain a relationship with serpents.
  7. Carry an agate as a talisman against snakes and scorpions.
  8. The talisman is more potent when engraved with a snake image, or with the image of a person riding a snake.
