🔥Alcohol Flame Ceremony

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Offering RiteAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Devotional WorkInvocationElemental
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Fireproof metal bowl or chalice, all one piece (not soldered), no wider than 3 inches; Epsom salts; 91% isopropyl alcohol; wooden matches; heatproof surface (trivet or brick); oven mitt; damp towel; optional resin incense (copal or frankincense)
◇Directions
1. Set your fireproof metal bowl on a heatproof surface. 2. Add half an inch of Epsom salts to the bottom (optional but purifying). 3. Pour in one to two tablespoons of 91% isopropyl alcohol. 4. Light a wooden match and carefully drop it into the bowl. 5. Do not use a lighter. 6. The flame will rise up to eight inches and burn for several minutes before going out on its own; no extinguishing is needed. 7. As the fire burns, speak prayers and invocations, or gaze into its depths and listen for the voice of the Goddess. 8. If using resin incense, add it to the bowl now; it will release its fragrance as the flame dies. 9. Keep an oven mitt nearby: the bowl will remain hot after the flame is finished. 10. Always keep a damp towel within reach to smother the flame in an emergency.
◇Description
A dramatic ceremonial flame using isopropyl alcohol and Epsom salts in a fireproof metal bowl, raising a tall purifying fire for invoking Brigid's presence, blessing an altar space, and gazing into the depths for the voice of the Goddess.
