Yemaya, orisha with dominion over all women's issues, is petitioned for renewed and rejuvenated reproductive health. She is offered salt water, pound cake with dark molasses, and a dedicated blue seven-day candle.

Quantities

Glass of water with sea salt
Slice of pound cake
Dark molasses
Blue seven-day candle

Directions

  1. Offer Yemaya a glass of water with sea salt and a slice of pound cake covered with dark molasses.
  2. Dedicate a blue seven-day candle to Yemaya and tell her what you need.
