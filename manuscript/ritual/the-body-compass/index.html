🧭The Body Compass

☮️Peace

A no-materials bodywork spell for when you are feeling lost, confused, or aimless. Uses breathwork, neck movement, and somatic awareness to reconnect with both ancestors and descendants, orienting the body and spirit between the past and future.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Take three deep breaths in through the nose and out through the mouth.
  2. After the third deep breath, gently close your eyes while allowing your breathing to return to normal.
  3. Without lifting your shoulders, bow your head forward.
  4. Enjoy the stretch in the back of your neck.
  5. Think of your descendants: perhaps biological children, or the people who will inherit your art or join whatever groups you are a part of after you're gone.
  6. Remember that you don't get to take any of it with you, and everything that you do in this life is ultimately for them.
  7. Make sure you're still breathing.
  8. Bring your head back up to center.
  9. Now gently rest your head back.
  10. Let your mouth come open.
  11. Slide your jaw forward and back; yes, this will look ridiculous, but it will feel good.
  12. Think of your ancestors.
  13. You might imagine them standing behind you, cradling your head in their hands, or residing at the back of your neck in the place where your little hairs stand up when you sense danger.
  14. Make sure you're still breathing.
  15. Bring your head back up to center.
  16. Let your head fall to the right.
  17. Make sure your left shoulder doesn't pop up.
  18. If it does, coax it back down.
  19. If you are considering a particular course of action, imagine yourself taking that action.
  20. How does it feel in your body?
  21. Bring your head back up to center.
  22. Let your head fall to the left.
  23. Make sure your right shoulder doesn't pop up.
  24. If it does, coax it back down.
  25. If you are considering an alternative to that action, imagine yourself doing the alternative.
  26. How does it feel in your body?
  27. Bring your head back up to center.
  28. Gently and slowly bow your head forward, then roll it around in a circle three times.
  29. After the third circle is complete, roll it around in the opposite direction.
  30. Bring your head back up to center.
  31. Open your eyes.
