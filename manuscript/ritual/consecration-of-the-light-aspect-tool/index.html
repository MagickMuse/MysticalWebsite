A consecration ritual for tools used in Light aspect work, performed during the Full Moon Esbat. Includes inscribing with symbols, Elemental passes, and divine blessing.

Quantities

  • All ritual items for Full Moon Esbat
  • Bowl of fresh water
  • Red votive candle and container (to collect melted wax)
  • Tool to be consecrated

Directions

  1. Perform during Full Moon Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.
  2. Light red votive from the center Altar candle; set tool on the pentacle.
  3. Inscribe the tool with desired symbols, runes, ogham, and your Craft Name in runes, ogham, or other magickal alphabet.
  4. Hold tool in right hand over the pentacle:
  5. In the names of the Goddess and the God, [Names], I consecrate this [tool] to be used in my practice of the Craft.
  6. I charge this by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt] and Elemental Water [sprinkle with water]; by Elemental Fire [pass through votive candle flame] and Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke].
  7. This tool is now by Elemental Powers bound to aid me in my work.
  8. So Mote It Be!
  9. Set the tool on the pentacle; raise arms with open hands:
  10. Great Lady and Great Lord, together in the light of this Moon, yet also seen with my inner sight, You dwell within and without, for You are Endings and New Beginnings; the Promise and the Love Manifested.
  11. Let this tool be imbued with Thy Power to aid me in my Craft.
  12. Hold hands, palms down, over tool, feeling the energy pass through the hands:
  13. Lady and Lord, Mother and Father of All, Thy blessing is felt and thy Power is passed into this tool, that it be sanctified for use in my Craft.
  14. Kneel with both palms on the ground:
  15. As what is sent returns, so I return to Thee the Power graciously sent to me.
  16. This [tool] is sanctified through Thy Power and Grace, through my flesh and blood, and made ready for my use.
  17. So Mote It Be!
  18. Stand; remove tool from pentacle, keeping it on the Altar; snuff votive.
  19. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
