🌅Sunset

A twilight wishing spell cast upon the very first star visible in the evening sky. The window is narrow and requires close attention at dusk. An optional spoken charm strengthens the working.

Quantities

Clear sky at twilight; (optional) the traditional rhyme charm

Directions

  1. Pay close attention as twilight approaches; the opportunity is brief and easily missed.
  2. When the very first solo star becomes visible in the darkening sky, gaze at it and make your wish.
  3. The traditional charm may be spoken aloud:
  4. "Starlight, star bright
    First star I see tonight
    I wish I may, I wish I might
    Have this wish I wish tonight"
  5. The rhyme is optional but not required.
  6. If the first star cannot be identified with certainty, reserve the working for another night, luck is not with you on this occasion.
