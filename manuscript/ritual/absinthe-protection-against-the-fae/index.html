A property warding spell using absinthe, water, and iron to protect your home and land from Fae interference. The working combines absinthe's protective oils with iron's traditional power against the Good Neighbors.

Quantities

  • 1 part absinthe
  • 5 parts water
  • Rust or rusty nail or iron oxide
  • Bottle for mixing
  • Optional: 4 railroad spikes
  • Optional: torch or drum

Directions

  1. The Fae are popular in new age circles, but there are a good deal more stories where humans either don't live to the end or make out poorly.
  2. There's a reason people leave out butter and cream - they're paying protection money to the Faerie mafia so they don't try to come in the house.
  3. Gotta pay off the Mab.
  4. To protect your home and property from the Fae, mix 1 part absinthe, 5 parts water, and rust in a bottle.
  5. You can throw a rusty nail in there if that's all you have.
  6. Iron oxide is also sold online from scientific supply companies.
  7. Walk the boundaries of your property with your mixture, splashing the property lines with the combination.
  8. Feel free to sing a boundary song if you like.
  9. This spell works best on the Summer Solstice or a Sunday.
  10. Added oomph: After the first pass around the boundary, bless four railroad spikes with the absinthe mixture and pound all four spikes into the ground below ground level, so they don't stick up and hurt someone mowing the grass.
  11. Feel free to incorporate other beating-the-bounds magick, like carrying a lit torch or banging a drum at the borders of your property.
  12. If you suspect an infestation: Reclaim your property before the bounds beating by urinating in a bucket and letting it sit undisturbed for twenty-four hours where it can't get knocked over.
  13. Then dump it on your front porch, claiming the space as yours.
  14. If you're terribly squicked out by that thought, grab a bottle of ammonia from the store, spit in it, put the cap on, shake, and dump it on the porch.
