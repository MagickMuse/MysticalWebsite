🍷Sabbat Cakes and Wine Ceremony

The ceremonial sharing of cakes and wine that closes the Sabbat dances and opens the feast. The symbolic marriage of heaven and Earth, enacted through the union of wand or athame (masculine) and chalice (feminine). Each exchange is accompanied by the words 'Blessed be.'

Quantities

  • Chalice of Sabbat wine
  • Sabbat cakes (on the paten pentacle)
  • Wand, rod, or athame (for the magister)
  • Paten pentacle (for the cakes)

Directions

The high priestess holds the chalice raised high over the balefire. Sabbat wine is poured in.

The magister lowers his ceremonial rod, wand, or athame into the cup briefly, then removes it.

Both the magister and high priestess pass the chalice to the coven, who each drink from it.

The high priestess presents the Sabbat cake on the paten pentacle. The magister divides it with his athame and distributes it to the coven.

On handing the chalice or pentacle to one's neighbour, the words "Blessed be" are spoken.

This ceremony leads directly into the communal feast, to which all coven members have contributed. The feast need not be held within the circle. If the coven sits at table, the magister is seated at the head.
