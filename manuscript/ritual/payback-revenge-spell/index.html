⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A simple karmic revenge spell performed in darkness. The target's name is written on paper, propped against a black candle, and circled while chanting a phrase of return. The paper is burned, and the extinguished candle is placed where the target will encounter it.

Quantities

1 piece of paper
1 pen
1 black candle

Directions

  1. Sit in a darkened room.
  2. Write the offender's name on a piece of paper.
  3. Prop the paper against a black candle.
  4. Circle the candle while chanting the incantation repeatedly until the energy feels complete.
  5. Burn the paper in the candle's flame.
  6. Extinguish the candle and place it in the path of the target.
