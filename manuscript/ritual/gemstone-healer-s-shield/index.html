Protection Oil (Personal)

A protective working in which healers carry or wear specific crystals to create a shielding field during healing sessions, with optional anointing and post-session cleansing.

Quantities

Bloodstone
Clear quartz
Black tourmaline
Protection Oil (optional)

Directions

  1. Select one or more of the following stones to carry or wear throughout the healing session: bloodstone, clear quartz, or black tourmaline.
  2. If desired, anoint the stones or yourself with Protection Oil before beginning.
  3. Proceed with the healing work.
  4. When the session concludes, cleanse the stones to clear any energy absorbed during the working.

Particularly suited to spells and workings performed through body-based therapies such as massage, this method of protection creates a continuous energetic buffer for the healer without interrupting the flow of the work itself.
