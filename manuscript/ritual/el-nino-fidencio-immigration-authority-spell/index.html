A devotional working petitioning El Niño Fidencio, beloved Mexican folk healer and folk saint, for help with immigration matters, official papers, or keeping authorities at a distance.

Quantities

  • 1 image of El Niño Fidencio
  • Fresh flowers (bouquet)
  • 1 white candle (or El Niño Fidencio devotional candle)

Directions

  1. Post an image of El Niño Fidencio on your altar.
  2. Offer him a bouquet of fresh flowers.
  3. Light a white candle (or a devotional El Niño Fidencio candle if available).
  4. Speak openly and sincerely about what you need.
  5. Make a vow that when circumstances allow, you will help someone else facing the same situation.

El Niño Fidencio (1898–1938) was a beloved Mexican folk healer who continues to be petitioned for miracles after his death. He has become a specialist in immigration matters — speeding the arrival of official papers and keeping unwanted authorities at a distance.

El Niño loved flowers deeply and used their powers in many of his cures. He is an accessible, warm-hearted intercessor who accepts modest offerings made with sincere intent.

  1. Post or display an image of El Niño Fidencio in your working space or on your altar.
  2. Offer him a bouquet of fresh flowers as a gift and a gesture of respect.
  3. Light a white candle, or a commercially produced El Niño Fidencio devotional candle if you can obtain one.
  4. Speak from your heart. Tell him clearly and openly what you need.
  5. Make the customary vow: that when circumstances allow, you will offer help and assistance to someone else who faces the same situation you are in now.
For very large requests, a vow to make a pilgrimage to El Niño's shrine in Espinazo, Mexico may be appropriate.
