💰Money Drawing Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
ManifestationChant/MantraEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Runic prosperity sigil (in green or black) • Bank statement, paycheck, contract, or document connected to money inflow • Paper and metal currency (multiples of 3, 5, or 8 are auspicious) • 1 hagstone • 1 crystal • Effigy of power (deity statue, natural object, or personally significant item) • Bell
◇Directions
1. On your altar, place the prosperity sigil. 2. Layer on top: your financial document, then currency, then the hagstone, then the crystal. 3. Keep the effigy of power nearby. 4. As you place each object, recite the rune names (Dagaz, Berkanō, Jēra) to create a sacred vibrational field resonating with the currency. 5. Ring the bell. 6. All elements create their own frequency that intensifies through combination. 7. As the vibrational current grows, an opening in the field of reality is created, visible through the hagstone, symbolic of the metaphysical aperture you are forming. 8. Through this opening, manipulate the aperture to make room for all your needs to flow freely to you.
◇Description
An altar-based prosperity spell using layered symbolic objects to create a vibrational field that draws abundance. The practitioner stacks the runic prosperity sigil, financial document, currency, hagstone, and crystal while reciting the runes, then rings a bell to amplify the frequency. The hagstone serves as a symbolic aperture through which needs flow freely.
