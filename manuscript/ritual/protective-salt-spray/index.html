🛡️Protective Salt Spray

A salt-based spray for creating an interior protective barrier in the home. Available in water-only and oil-enhanced versions.

Quantities

Salt
Clean spray bottle
Water

Directions

  1. Charge the salt with intent to protect your home from entities that do not have your highest good in mind: negative spirits, curses, hexes.
  2. Add the charged salt to a clean spray bottle.
  3. Add water, visualizing its power empowering the solution.
  4. Circle the interior of your home, spraying the walls.
  5. Envision dangers to home and family being washed away while building a salt wall to deflect anything directed at your home.

Purpose

Salt is a tool of purification. This spray creates an interior salt circle to keep the home free of threats without the environmental damage of salt used outdoors.

Oil Version

Select an essential oil based on your protective intent:

  • Angelica: Banishing, binding evil, protecting children, dispelling negativity
  • Cloves: Hex breaking, dispelling negativity, protection
  • Frankincense: Banishing, protection, blessing, consecration
  • Peppermint: Consecration, good luck, uplifting, healing, purification, release

Instructions

  1. Pour three tablespoons of witch hazel or rubbing alcohol into the spray bottle.
  2. Add three drops of your chosen oil(s); the alcohol allows oil and water to combine.
  3. Charge the salt between your hands, sending energy into it and visualizing it driving away harmful forces.
  4. Add the salt to the bottle and allow the solution to absorb it.
  5. Fill with water, visualizing it washing away unhealthy energies.
  6. Circle the interior of the home, spraying the walls. Avoid fabrics, as oils may stain.
⚠️
Environmental Note: Salt damages soil and plant life. For outdoor work, substitute native plants, leaves, flowers, or crushed eggshell.

Magical Cleaning Variation

Combine wall washing with cleansing:

  • 1 cup vinegar (cuts oily dirt)
  • 1 cup fabric softener (repels future dirt)
  • 1 handful salt (purification)
  • Hot water to fill mop bucket
