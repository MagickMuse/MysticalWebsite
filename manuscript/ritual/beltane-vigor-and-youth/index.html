Beltane

A quick Beltane ritual that can be performed solo, with a partner, or with as many friends as you can gather. Takes only a few minutes from start to finish. Quickly puts you in touch with Beltane's fertile, creative energy flow, infusing you with fresh vigor and renewing youthful abilities of self-healing and regeneration. Purpose is to renew youth and restore vigor by attuning to Beltane's energetic current of fertility, growth, and creation.

Quantities

  • No supplies needed
    Setting: Daytime, preferably early morning when the ground is still wet with dew, in an outdoor location where the ground is soft and covered with vegetation.
    Pre-Ritual Preparations: Wear something you won't mind rolling around on the ground in!
    Can be performed solo, with a partner, or with as many friends as you can gather.

Directions

  1. Sit or lay on the ground, making as much contact between your skin and the earth as is comfortable.
  2. Stroke the vegetation with your hands, petting the leaves or blades of grass slowly and gently.
  3. Let the vegetation slide between your fingers, and sense the energies of the plants flowing into you as you do so.
  4. If there is dew on the ground, collect it on your palms and rub the dew into your face, anointing cheeks, lips, forehead, chin, and the area surrounding your eyes.
  5. Rub some dew into your hands, as well.
  6. If you're starting too late in the day for dew, substitute with a bit of vegetation, selecting an especially healthy-looking leaf or flower with which to stroke your face and hands.
  7. Whether it's dew or vegetation, envision a vigorous, thriving energy flowing into your body as you touch it to your skin.
  8. Say:
  9. "Like the plants of the earth, like the fresh morning dew,
    I am healthy and growing, youthful and new!"
  10. The ritual is now complete.
  11. Expect a feeling of renewal and energy to set in soon after.
  12. Effects should last throughout the growing season, helping to improve and sustain health, increase energy levels, and strengthen one's sense of youth and vigor.
