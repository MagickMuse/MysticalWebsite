⚖️Two-Stone Home Blessing

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
LunarSolarCleansingChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 round black stone - 1 round white stone
◇Directions
1. Hold the black stone in your open cupped hands, naming whatever you want to lose: quarreling, sickness, or lethargy. 2. Say over and over, "Be filled with negativity I seek to lose, grow heavy with the \[name\] I no longer choose; linger no longer in my home, become a stranger, far to roam." When the stone seems heavier, cast it into water or bury it in earth where nothing grows, saying, "You are gone, you are done, I have no more of you. Your power is through." Leave the white stone outdoors for a full sunlit day and a full moon. 3. Set it in a bright spot in your home, saying, "Attract positivity, happiness, and peace. Stone of hope, stone of light, may your power never cease." Every full moon, leave the stone for a full twenty-four hours outdoors. 4. Perform full moon, day and night.
◇Description
A full moon two-stone ritual absorbing negativity into a black stone and charging a white stone with positivity for ongoing home blessing.
