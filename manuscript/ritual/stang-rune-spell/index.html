A waxing to Full Moon consecration spell for a staff or stang, performed on a Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, or Friday during the hour of the Sun, Moon, Mercury, or Venus. An orange candle is anointed with vetivert or pine oil and inscribed with runes. The stang is passed through the four Elementals for consecration, then engraved or painted with a sequence of runes representing wisdom, success, joy, prosperity, creativity, and empowerment. Additional runes are added for partnership with the Goddess and God, claiming the tool as one's own, and connecting through Eoh. A Craft Name monogram and sigils of Moon and Sun complete the inscription, blessing and protecting the stang.

Quantities

  • 1 staff or stang
  • Rock salt
  • Lavender incense
  • 1 orange candle
  • Blessed water
  • Vetivert or pine oil
  • Wood burning tool or paint for inscribing runes

Directions

  1. Rub the orange candle with vetivert or pine oil.
  2. Inscribe the candle with Othala, flanked by Berkanan and Idho.
  3. Pass the stang or staff through the symbols of the Elementals while speaking:
  4. I consecrate thee through the power of Elemental Earth,
    Sprinkle the stang with salt.
    through the power of Elemental Air,
    Pass the stang through incense smoke.
    through the power of Elemental Fire,
    Pass the stang through the candle flame.
    and through the power of Elemental Water.
    Sprinkle the stang with blessed water.
  5. Engrave, burn, or paint the following runes down the stang or staff: Ansuz, Tiwaz, Ansuz, Fehu, Sowilo, and Hagaiaz.
  6. Speak:
  7. For ancient wisdom; success; joy; prosperity; creativity; and empowerment;
  8. Add Onn, Idho, and Thurisaz and speak:
  9. In partnership with the Goddess and the God, under Their protection;
  10. Add Othala and speak:
  11. I name this my possession.
  12. Beneath this, create a monogram of your Craft Name and speak:
  13. I, who am [name], name this my Craft tool.
  14. Add Eiwaz and speak:
  15. Through Eoh, are we connected, I to them and they to me, and this Stang to us.
  16. Add a sigil of Moon and Sun and speak:
  17. Through the Lady and the Lord, is this stang blessed and protected.
  18. So Mote It Be!
  19. Let the staff or stang stand with incense smoke and candle burning near the base for an hour.
  20. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
