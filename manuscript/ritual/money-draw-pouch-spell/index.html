💰Money Draw Pouch Spell

◇Form
PouchDressed Candle
◇Method
Herbal MagickCandle MagickChant/MantraThread/Sewing MagickInvocationElementalConsecration
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- 1 small green cotton bag - Green thread - 1 needle - 1 coin (silver or gold is best) - 1 three-leaf clover - 1 green candle - 1 silver candle - 1 gold candle - 2 lodestones - Clove oil - Pine or patchouli oil - Sweetgrass incense - Cinquefoil - Blessed water - Salt
◇Directions
1. Rub clove oil on the green candle and speak: 2. With clove are you enhanced to draw money by this spell. 3. Rub pine oil on the silver and gold candles and speak: 4. With pine are you enhanced with prosperity and energy for action. 5. Inscribe the green candle with Gera and speak: 6. Gera for rewards for my work and a fruitful harvest! 7. Inscribe the silver candle with Feoh and speak: 8. Feoh for fulfillment, material wealth, and ambition satisfied! 9. Inscribe the gold candle with Ethel and speak: 10. Ethel for acquisitions and possessions! 11. Light the sweetgrass incense. 12. Then light the silver candle and speak: 13. This candle draws to me the blessing of Bendidia, the Lady of the Moon, whose twig in hand points the way to the treasures of the Underworld. 14. Raise the candle and speak: 15. Bring prosperity and much money into my life through this spell! 16. Light the gold candle and speak: 17. This candle draws to me the blessing of Dis, the Lord of the Underworld, Guardian of the treasures of the Earth. 18. Raise the candle and speak: 19. Bring the manifestation of the Earth's wealth into my life through this spell! 20. Light the green candle and speak: 21. This candle shows the money coming to me through the blessings of She and He, to fulfill the will of Their child, [name]! 22. So Mote It Be! 23. Place the coin, clover, cinquefoil, and two lodestones into the pouch and sew it shut. 24. Speak: 25. Money drawn to me, bring me more than I might need, that with this wealth content shall I be. 26. With no harm to others wrung, be my wealth a blessed sum. 27. This spell is sealed and it is done, for as I will, So Mote It Be! 28. Consecrate by passing the pouch through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak: 29. Be thou empowered by Elemental Earth [sprinkle pouch with salt], by Elemental Air [pass it through incense smoke], by Elemental Fire [pass it through the flames of the three candles], and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water] to bring me wealth and put money in my hand! 30. For as I will, So Mote It Be! 31. Place in a purse, pocket, wallet, or other location associated with money. 32. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
◇Description
A waxing to Full Moon spell to draw wealth through a hand-sewn green pouch. Three candles are anointed with clove and pine oils and inscribed with the runes Gera, Feoh, and Ethel. The silver candle invokes Bendidia, Lady of the Moon, and the gold candle invokes Dis, Lord of the Underworld. A coin, clover, cinquefoil, and lodestones are sealed in the pouch, which is then consecrated through the four Elementals. Carried in a wallet, purse, or pocket. ⚠️ Sweetgrass is sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
