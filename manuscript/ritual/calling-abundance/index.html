🌟Calling Abundance

◇Solitary or Group

◇Setting

◇Quantities
- 1 green candle - 1 orange - 1 piece of bread (freshly ripped or commercial slice) - 1 small dish of salt - Your wallet (or wherever you store money)
◇Directions
1. Cut the orange into quarters. Hold the candle by its wick with your receptive hand. Use your projective hand to rub the orange on the candle until it glistens with juice. Set the candle upright and allow it to dry. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space. Place the candle on your altar or surface. Put the bread and salt in front of it. Place your wallet to the right of the bread and salt. Light the candle. Sprinkle a large pinch of salt over the bread. Sprinkle a small pinch of salt over your wallet. Hold your hands open, palms up, on either side of the candle. Gently move your hands to direct the candle smoke toward yourself. Say aloud: 2. By the bread of Isis And the salt of Sulis, By the fruit of Nikkal And the purse of Cernunnos, I welcome abundance into my life. Abundantia, Habondia, Andarta, Inanna, Rosmerta, Nehalennia, Nantosuelta, Damona, Epona, Concordia! I command abundance To descend upon my life. 3. Feel the energy in your hands. Name that feeling “abundance” and hold it in your memory. Eat the bread and the orange. Welcome the energy of the spell into your body. Allow the candle to burn all the way down. When finished, ground the power you raised. Clean up. Discard the candle remains.
◇Description
A prosperity spell to invite more than just money, friendship, love, health, and overall blessings, into your life. This spell invokes ancient deities of plenty and is especially effective for shifting your mindset toward material comfort and abundance.

What is Abundance?

Abundance is the state of plentitude, of having more than just your basic needs fulfilled. Bread may be the staff of life, but abundance spreads some jam on it. Most cultures have had gods and goddesses who rule abundance, and this spell invokes many of them.

Preparing the Candle

Cut the orange into quarters. Hold the candle by its wick with your receptive hand. Use your projective hand to slowly rub the orange on it until the whole candle is glistening with juice. Set the candle down in an upright position and allow it to dry.

Understanding Abundance

As you focus on abundance and raise power, concentrate on what abundance means to you, and what welcome changes it might bring to your life. Remember that abundance takes other forms besides money: an abundance of friends, love, health, or blessings is often more valuable than money.

The Incantation

By the bread of Isis
And the salt of Sulis,
By the fruit of Nikkal
And the purse of Cernunnos,
I welcome abundance into my life.
Abundantia, Habondia,
Andarta, Inanna, Rosmerta,
Nehalennia, Nantosuelta,
Damona, Epona, Concordia!
By Inanna's storehouse
And Nehalennia's dog,
By the Dagda's cauldron
And Copia's cornucopia,
Abundantia, Habondia,
Andarta, Inanna, Rosmerta,
Nehalennia, Nantosuelta,
Damona, Epona, Concordia!
I command abundance
To descend upon my life.

Do not worry about pronunciation. Instead, concentrate on the power and the beauty of these ancient names as you chant them aloud.

The Energy of Abundance

Feel the palms of your hands grow warm, and perhaps even perceive a sphere of energy in each hand. Whatever sensation you perceive, name that feeling "abundance," and hold it securely in your memory.

Eat the bread and the orange. Concentrate on welcoming abundance while the candle burns all the way down. Feel the energy of the spell entering you with the food and becoming a part of you.

What to Expect

Do not expect instant results, such as winning the lottery the week after you cast the spell. This is a spell that works through you and brings material improvement in your circumstances because it changes your attitude and your expectations. In the months that follow your casting this spell, you should begin to see improvements that gradually create more comfort in your life.

Keeping the Magick Flowing

To keep the spell's magick flowing, eat some bread and salt and oranges from time to time while focusing on making abundance welcome in your life. Keep bright the memory of the feeling that you named "abundance." Open your hands to the Universe, palms up, and accept blessing.
