⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A jar spell targeting someone who has abused their power. The written grievance is sealed with thorns, graveyard dirt, nettle, and red liquid, then crowned and burned to strip the target of their authority.

Quantities

1 sheet of white paper
1 jar with lid
Animal teeth or thorns
Graveyard dirt
Stinging nettle
Red liquid
1 non-flammable power symbol (e.g., aluminum foil crown)
1 red candle

Directions

  1. Write the name of the person who has abused their power on a sheet of white paper.
  2. On the same paper, write how their actions have made you feel, pouring all of your anger, betrayal, and disappointment into the words.
  3. Place the paper inside a jar.
  4. Add animal teeth or thorns, graveyard dirt, stinging nettle, and a red liquid of your choosing.
  5. As you place each ingredient into the jar, visualize it stripping away the target's power and cursing them.
  6. Seal the jar with its lid.
  7. Place a non-flammable representation of their power on top of the lid, such as a crown cut from aluminum foil.
  8. Set a red candle on top of the lid and light it while reciting the following incantation:
  9. I cried for mercy; you ignored my pleading.
    You had your crown, but now it's bleeding.
    You must learn that power is fleeting.
  10. Allow the candle to burn down completely.
  11. Dispose of the jar however you see fit, whether discarded or kept nearby to continue charging with your intent.
