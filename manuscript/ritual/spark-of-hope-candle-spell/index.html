A waxing crescent candle spell to reignite hope, positivity, and faith, in your dreams, yourself, or a specific working. Coconut, lemon, lavender, nutmeg, and sugar combine their uplifting, sweetening, and cleansing energies around a dressed candle anchored in a fresh lemon wheel, with sea salt to ground and dispel negativity.

Quantities

  • Fresh lemon wheel
  • Yellow or white chime candle
  • Coconut oil
  • Sugar
  • Tray or plate
  • Dried lavender
  • Dried chickweed pieces (optional)
  • A strand of your own hair
  • Matches or lighter
  • Sea salt
  • Ground nutmeg

Directions

  1. Pierce the center of the lemon wheel with the base of the candle to create a snug hole, then set it aside.
  2. Dress the candle with coconut oil, anointing it from wick to base and drawing the oil toward your heart, pulling hope, optimism, and positivity inward as you work.
  3. On your tray or plate, blend the sugar and lavender together.
  4. (If you have chickweed, add a pinch now.) Roll the dressed candle toward yourself through the mixture so that some clings to the oil-coated surface.
  5. Wrap a strand of your own hair around the candle to bind its energy to you.
  6. If the piece is short, tuck it near the candle's base or stir it into the herb mixture so the oil catches it.
  7. Thread the candle through the lemon wheel.
  8. Melt a small amount of the candle's base with a flame and press it firmly to the plate to anchor it, or seat it in a candleholder instead.
  9. Scatter sea salt in a ring around the lemon as a grounding barrier against any negative energy, then dust the remaining sugar and lavender blend over the top of the lemon wheel, layering their sweetening, uplifting, and soothing energies into the working.
  10. Cup your hands around the candle and close your eyes.
  11. Visualize a spark of hope kindling within you, your faith and sense of possibility growing steadily brighter.
  12. When you feel ready, light the candle and say aloud:
  13. "As this light grows, my hope renews.
    The possibility is, my dreams can come true.
    So mote it be, by the light of the waxing crescent moon."
  14. From about six inches above the flame, sprinkle ground nutmeg so it briefly sparks as it falls, a physical echo of the hope reigniting within you.
  15. Allow the candle to burn fully in a safe, attended space.
  16. Crystal companion (optional): While the candle burns, hold a piece of peach moonstone in your hand.
  17. Soften your gaze at the flame.
  18. Breathe its light inward with each inhale and exhale fear, doubt, and worry.
  19. Sense a light of power growing within you with every breath, fed by the flame.
  20. When the spell is complete, rest the stone at the candle's base to charge in its energy, then keep it with you in the days that follow.
