Blood🍞Bread

A folk spell to safeguard cattle from theft. Bread marked with three drops of blood from the youngest family member's smallest finger is crumbled and fed to the herd.

Quantities

  • 1 piece of bread
  • 3 drops of blood (from the smallest finger of the youngest child)

Directions

  1. Allow three drops of blood from the smallest finger of the youngest child in the family to fall onto a piece of bread.
  2. Crumble the bread and feed it to the cattle.
  3. This is said to bind the herd to the family and prevent the animals from being stolen.
