Lughnasadh

The First Harvest sabbat ceremony celebrating the Festival of Bread and the Marriage of the Sun and Earth. Features the Lord of the Dance chant, blessing of the grain, and sacred bread offering as the God of the Corn descends into the Shadowland.

Quantities

  • Yellow or wheat-colored altar candles
  • Frankincense or sandalwood incense
  • Summer flowers and grains for decoration
  • Loaf of multigrain or cracked whole-wheat bread
  • Cauldron
  • Wand
  • Bell (optional)
  • Cakes & Wine: blackberry wine or dark fruity beverage, multigrain bread, blackberry pie, corn on the cob, barbecue meats or fried chicken

Directions

  1. Cast a circle using your preferred method.
  2. Light the incense and carry it around the circle deosil.
  3. Clap or ring a bell three times and say:
  4. I celebrate this day the First Harvest, the Festival of Bread and the Marriage of the Sun and the Earth.
  5. Chant or sing while dancing around the circle:
  6. Dance, dance, wherever you may be;
    When you dance with the Lord, He will dance with thee.
    Turn, turn, a Circle then you form;
    And the Lord of the Dance is the Lord of the Corn!
  7. Raise open arms at the altar and sing or chant:
  8. Down, down, into the Earth He'll go;
    Giving life to the grain that in Spring we sow.
    He rules the Shadowland 'til Yule;
    When His Sun is reborn and He joins us anew!
  9. Clap or ring the bell seven times:
  10. Great is the power of the God of the Sun and the Goddess of the Earth from Whom spring all life!
  11. Hold your wand over the bread:
  12. The harvest of the corn that sustains us is brought through death and rebirth.
  13. The Lord of the Corn leaves the side of the Earth Mother that His power may be passed into the land for His children to live.
  14. Blessed Be the God of the Corn, Whose love for His children knows no bounds!
  15. In the Land of Shadows will He abide with the Lady as Crone, awaiting the time of His joyous rebirth.
  16. Touch the bread with the tip of your wand:
  17. May the God bless this bread that I eat in honor of the cycle of Life that created it and me.
  18. Tear a portion of bread as libation and place it in the cauldron.
  19. Eat a bite of bread and set aside the rest to share at your family meal or use in Cakes & Wine.
  20. Hold your arms open, palms up, level over the altar:
  21. My Lord and my Lady, I am blessed by Your gifts from the soil.
  22. These first grains are the promise of fruits to come.
  23. Let the power of the Goddess and the God be in me at this time and throughout the year, that I never forget that I am one with the Absolute, the All, the Two Who Are One.
  24. So Mote It Be!
  25. Read or recite the Charge of the God.
  26. Clap or ring the bell three times.
  27. Proceed to Cakes & Wine, then close the circle.
