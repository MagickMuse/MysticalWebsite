🌑New MoonSolar Eclipse

A crystal meditation for the new moon to attune to the combined energy of the sun and moon. At the new moon, both bodies align in the same astrological sign, creating a moment of cosmic harmony. Use crystals to connect with this energy for empowerment and inner alignment, or perform before casting spells or reading cards.

Quantities

  • Moonstone, or another stone to represent the moon
  • Moon sign crystal or mineral of choice, or a crystal related to your goal
  • Oracle or tarot card deck (optional)

Directions

  1. Settle into your sacred space and prepare for meditation in whatever way feels right.
  2. Ensure you are comfortable and relaxed, and take a moment to cleanse your energy and clear your mind.
  3. Hold the moonstone in your nondominant hand and your second crystal in your dominant hand.
  4. Close your eyes and breathe deeply three times, feeling the weight of the stones and slowly tuning into their energy.
  5. Visualize the sun and moon aligned in the cosmos above you, and feel their combined pull from where you stand on earth.
  6. Reflect on the current moon sign and sit with the following questions, allowing the moon to guide you, or drawing from a tarot or oracle deck if you feel called: What does the energy of this new moon feel like?
  7. What is something you need now, or an issue that has recently arisen?
  8. What are you called to pursue, and how can this energy best serve your path and goals?
  9. What is the next step forward in your journey?
  10. With those insights in mind, return to your visualization of the cosmos, seeing the moon aligned with the sun and earth.
  11. Turn inward and visualize your own inner sun, your light, warmth, and consciousness, and your inner moon, your intuition, feelings, and soul.
  12. See these two forces come into alignment within you.
  13. Breathe deeply three more times, feeling determination rise toward your new moon intention and envisioning it becoming real.
  14. Draw the celestial energy into your body with each breath, infusing your whole being with this alignment and your intentions.
  15. When the meditation is complete, carry the moonstone or moon sign stone with you as you work toward your goals.
