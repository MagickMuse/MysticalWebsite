🍞Bread Offering to Spirits

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Devotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- 1 slice of homemade bread - Outdoor offering space
◇Directions
1. Select a nice slice from your freshly baked bread. 2. Bring it outside and perform a brief devotional, offering it to your ancestors, spirits of the land, deities, or any spiritual beings that are part of your practice. 3. Speak words of offering such as: I break bread with you, honored spirits. As this loaf made by my own hand and heart nourishes me and mine, may this slice take the form most desired by you. 4. Leave the bread overnight or for a day so the spirits may take their fill of its essence. 5. Afterward, place any remnants near a bird feeder or scatter for wildlife to enjoy.
◇Description
A devotional offering of homemade bread to spirits, ancestors, land spirits, or deities. The offering honors the cycle of life represented in bread-making and shares nourishment with the spiritual realm.
