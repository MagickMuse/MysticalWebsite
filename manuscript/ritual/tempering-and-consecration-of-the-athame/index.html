🗡️Tempering and Consecration of the Athame

A waning moon working that tempers a new blade through repeated heating and quenching in an herbal brew, magnetizes it with a lodestone, then seals it with painted runes, the practitioner's witch name, and a three day burial.

Quantities

A new steel knife with a black handle, blade roughly five to six inches long (some traditions favor a shorter four inch blade)
Distilled water infused with a Martial herb of choice
A few drops of blood (your own, or a substitute from butcher's meat)
Charged water from the cup and incense burned in the thurible, mixed with the same herb
A lodestone or bar magnet
White paint, optionally mixed with the same powdered herb
A new paintbrush
A piece of exorcised cloth

Directions

  1. During the waning moon, infuse distilled water with a Martial herb of your choosing, then mix in a few drops of blood.
  2. Exorcise the blade with charged water from the cup and incense burned in the thurible, both mixed with the same herb.
  3. Heat the blade in the thurible's coals until very hot, then plunge it into the waiting brew, chanting with each immersion and visualizing the blade glowing with power:
    Blade of steel, I call to you,
    to turn away all I shall name.
    As I have spoken, so shall it be!
  4. Repeat the heating and quenching three times in all; this tempering, or forging, gives the blade its first charge.
  5. Magnetize the blade by holding the Athame in your left hand and drawing a lodestone or bar magnet from the handle to the point with your right hand, always in the same direction, for about five minutes, chanting at each stroke:
    Blade of steel, I call to you,
    to draw and hold all I shall name.
    As I have spoken, so shall it be!
  6. Paint protective runes on the handle with white paint, mixing in a pinch of the same herb if you wish, and chant as each one is painted:
    Blessed be, blade of the craft!
  7. Paint your witch name on the reverse of the handle, speaking each letter aloud as you go, then close with the words So mote it be!
  8. Bury the finished Athame point down in earth for three days and three nights, then dig it up and wrap it in a piece of exorcised cloth until ready for use.
