⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A Malaysian sympathetic charm to bring sickness and ill health. A wax poppet molded with the target's hair or clothing is pricked repeatedly with a coffin nail over three consecutive evenings.

Quantities

1/2 lb. melted wax
Hair or clothing from the target
1 coffin nail

Directions

  1. Melt half a pound of wax.
  2. If using clothing belonging to the target, cut or tear it into tiny pieces.
  3. Mold the wax together with the cloth or hair into an image of the target.
  4. Once the doll has set, take a coffin nail and prick it repeatedly while chanting:
  5. It is the heart that I prick, it is the liver that I prick, it is the spleen that I prick, it is the brain that I prick.
  6. Repeat the ritual each evening for three consecutive evenings.
  7. After the third night, wait nine days.
  8. If the target remains well after that time, the spell has been blocked by counter-magick or protective witchcraft, and a different working must be attempted.
