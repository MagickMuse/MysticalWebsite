Snakes and dragons, the traditional guardians of treasure, are placed around a computer to protect it and the valuables stored inside. A Chinese dragon tortoise adds good fortune and business inspiration, and a snake or dragon screen saver posts the guardian right at the threshold of entry.

Quantities

Snake or dragon images or statues
Chinese dragon tortoise amulet (optional)
Snake or dragon screen saver (optional)

Directions

  1. Place snake or dragon images above or beside the computer.
  2. Turn them to face the computer or to face outward, but never so they stare at you.
  3. For good fortune and business inspiration, add a Chinese dragon tortoise amulet facing the computer.
  4. Install a snake or dragon screen saver so the guardian stands on the threshold of entry.
