Egyptian spell to receive divine guidance through dreams by invoking Osiris, Isis, Nephthys, and all the gods. The benu-bird (phoenix) drawn on the hand serves as a conduit for the oracle.

Quantities

  • Myrrh water
  • Juice of any-wood
  • Black ink

Directions

  1. This spell, called a peh-netjer (oracle) of Osiris, grants the sleeping person the chance to consult Osiris directly through a magickally induced dream.
  2. Prepare the ink by mixing myrrh water with juice of any-wood and black ink.
  3. Using this mixture, draw a benu-bird (the sacred phoenix/heron) on your right hand.
  4. In the evening, extend your hand outward, palm facing the moon.
  5. Recite the following incantation while gazing at the drawing:
  6. 'O Isis, O Nephthys, O noble spirit of Osiris Wennefer, come to me, because I am your beloved son Horus.
  7. O gods who are in the sky, O gods who are on the earth, O gods who are in the primeval ocean, O gods who are in the south, O gods who are in the north, O gods who are in the west, O gods who are in the east, come to me in this night, instruct me about such-and-such a matter, about which I am enquiring.
  8. Quickly, quickly, hurry, hurry.'
  9. Replace 'such-and-such a matter' with your specific question.
  10. After reciting, go to sleep with your hand placed under your head.
  11. Say 'Good, good' four times as you drift off.
  12. The gods should visit you in your dreams with their answer.
