⚖️Restoring Life's Balance

A comprehensive spell to restore balance when life becomes hectic and overwhelming. Uses two amber stones/jewelry pieces, green candles, and custom incense blend of ten herbs and resins. Works in balanced pairs and sixes to mirror equinox energy. Charges amber for ongoing balance support.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp for future use) angelica
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) basil
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) sage
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) sweet woodruff
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) vervain
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) yarrow
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) comfrey
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) chamomile
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) frankincense
  • 1 tsp (or 1 tbsp) myrrh
  • 2 pieces of amber (stones or jewelry, preferably earrings, rings, or bracelets)
  • 2 green candles
  • Mortar and pestle (or electric grinder for magickal use only)
  • Fireproof container (cauldron)
  • 2 charcoal tablets
  • Lighter
  • Dish for incense
  • Optional: stream or burial location for ashes

Directions

  1. Often our lives get completely hectic and out of control.
  2. For many people this happens during the winter holidays and getting life back in balance again may seem impossible.
  3. We don't eat right.
  4. We don't sleep right.
  5. We might not be getting enough exercise.
  6. We may have let our spirituality slip to the back burner.
  7. While this spell can be done at any time, it's very well suited for the spring for its renewal connections.
  8. It's also extremely well suited for the equinox for its restoring balance aspect.
  9. What better day to restore balance to your own life than on the day that is naturally all about balance to begin with.
  10. Create a powdered incense mixture using mortar and pestle.
  11. Combine one teaspoon (or tablespoon for future use) of each: angelica, basil, sage, sweet woodruff, vervain, yarrow, comfrey, chamomile, frankincense, and myrrh.
  12. Resins are difficult to crush.
  13. Some use electric chopper or grinders, but keep them separate from food preparation for both physical and magickal reasons.
  14. You will need two pieces of amber, either stones or jewelry.
  15. Earrings are ideal since there are two and they go on each side of the body, creating balance.
  16. You could also use two rings (one for each hand) or two bracelets (one for each wrist), but try to avoid using a necklace if you can.
  17. If using jewelry, gold is preferred to silver.
  18. You will be able to recharge using the same amber pieces anytime needed.
  19. Set up altar area with fireproof container in center, dish containing incense next to it, and amber stone on opposite side.
  20. Place a green candle on each side of fireproof container, creating balance.
  21. Cast your circle if you didn't prior to preparing incense.
  22. Call in any deities you prefer to work with.
  23. Light two charcoal tablets in fireproof container (two for balance and better burning).
  24. Allow charcoal time to fully turn red and become coated in ash.
  25. Light the green candle on the left side and say:
  26. Sometimes life slips out of control.
    It is for me to set it right,
    to strike a balance on this day
    of equal day and night.
  27. Light the green candle on the right side and repeat:
  28. Sometimes life slips out of control.
    It is for me to set it right,
    to strike a balance on this day
    of equal day and night.
  29. Then add:
  30. As I light these candles,
    one on each side,
    I begin my journey back.
    Restoring balance
    step by step,
    putting my life back on track.
  31. Take a small pinch of incense and sprinkle it on top of lit charcoal tablets.
  32. As you do so say:
  33. Return balance to my life,
    and create a harmony
    that all parts of my life
    live together in symmetry.
    With these intentions
    charge this stone,
    so that my life
    takes a balanced tone.
  34. As the smoke rises into the air, take a piece of your amber and pass it through the smoke slowly in a clockwise circular motion.
  35. Sprinkle the incense with your nondominant hand and hold the amber with your dominant hand.
  36. Because this spell is about balance, we keep everything balanced.
  37. Instead of using three pinches of incense and saying the above three times, we do everything six times.
  38. This way we use a multiple of three, yet keep it balanced with an even number and not off balance with an odd number.
  39. After saying the above once and making your circle through smoke, use another pinch of incense and make another pass with amber.
  40. Continue until you have done it a total of six times.
  41. After finishing with first piece of amber, replace it on altar and repeat entire process with second piece.
  42. When you finish six rounds with second piece, place it back on altar.
  43. Pick up the candle on right-hand side and hold it close enough to your mouth to blow out.
  44. Say:
  45. The magick done here today
    sets in motion
    a brand new way.
    I pull together from all of my sides,
    to balance out
    as the chaos dies.
    I release this magick into the air
    so mote it be
    into the Goddess's care.
  46. Slowly blow out the flame and envision the magick being released and swirling in the air on its journey out into the universe.
  47. Pick up the candle on left side of altar and repeat exactly as with first candle, reciting the above, blowing out the flame, and envisioning the magick being released into the universe.
  48. Close your circle.
  49. Dispose of ashes from fireproof container.
  50. If you have a stream nearby this would be most ideal place to dump them, but you can also bury them.
  51. If there are still pieces of herbs or resins that did not burn, bury it in a location where you won't be planting anything.
  52. Some people keep a small section of yard (a couple square feet) or a special planter with just dirt in it for burying their magickal workings.
  53. Wear the jewelry to help bring your life into balance.
  54. If you used amber gems, keep one in your pockets on each side of your body to keep them balanced against one another.
  55. If you feel it is needed, you can repeat this spell at the Autumnal Equinox as well.
