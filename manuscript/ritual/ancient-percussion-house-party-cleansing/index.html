🥁Ancient Percussion House Party Cleansing

◇Quantities
Cymbals, sistra, tambourines, and/or frame drums with metal parts Optional: other drums, flutes, rattles, and percussion
◇Directions
1. Gather participants with instruments; cymbals, sistra, tambourines, and/or frame drums are preferred and required, and at least some must have metal parts. 2. Supplement with other drums, flutes, rattles, and percussion as desired. 3. No musical skill is required. 4. Have everyone grab an instrument and allow the spirits to guide them. 5. Make a joyful, raucous noise. 6. The reverberations remove negative debris and frighten away low-level malevolent entities. 7. The sounds also invite sacred revelers: Cybele, Dionysus, the Dactyls, Astarte, Hathor, Isis, Bes, and Taweret.
◇Description
An ancient cleansing method rooted in primal spiritual rites from Anatolia, Egypt, Nubia, North Africa, the Middle East, and the Mediterranean. Percussion cleansing banishes low-level malevolent entities through raucous noise while inviting protective and joyful deities. It also adds protective, aura-boosting, and spirit-summoning properties. Best followed by love, sexuality, protection, finances, justice, or healing spells, or a great party. Not suitable before serene or controlled ritual.
