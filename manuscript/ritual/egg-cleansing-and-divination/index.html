A cleansing ritual using a fresh egg rolled over the body to absorb negativity, then cracked into blessed water for divination. Interprets signs like blood, cloudy water, faces in yolk, evil eye symbol, or bubbles to identify hexes, ill will, or guardian protection. Can be done solo or with partner.

Quantities

  • Clear glass
  • Blessed water or moon water
  • Protection oil (a few drops)
  • Fresh, unbroken egg (one per person)
  • Piece of material or scarf (to keep egg from rolling)
  • Outdoor space for burial

Directions

  1. This cleansing is easier to do if you have the help of someone else, however, you can still do it on your own.
  2. Fill a clear glass with blessed water or moon water.
  3. Place it on your altar and add a few drops of your favorite protection oil.
  4. Place a fresh, unbroken egg on your altar as well.
  5. You may want to place it on a piece of material, such as a scarf, to keep it from rolling.
  6. Cast your circle and invite your deities in. Hold the glass of water in your hands and ask your deities for protection, for cleansing, and to assist you in seeing clearly.
  7. Place the glass back on your altar and then do the same with the egg.
  8. Also while holding the egg, ask your deities to allow it to absorb any and all negativity.
  9. Once you are done, take the egg and roll it around the crown of your head.
  10. Next roll it down your face, neck, each arm, your chest, torso, back, and the front and back of each leg.
  11. Finally, roll the egg over each palm and the soles of each foot.
  12. Always make sure you are rolling the egg downward toward the ground.
  13. This is where it comes in handy to have help.
  14. If working with a partner, lie down while they roll the egg all over your body, turn over and have them repeat the process.
  15. When it's their turn, do the same for them with a fresh egg.
  16. After rolling the egg all over your body, immediately crack the egg and pour the contents into the water.
  17. Take a moment to observe the egg.
  18. Pay attention to the smell, color, any shape it takes, and whether or not there is blood present.
  19. Bad odor, blood, or cloudy water is a sign of a problem, and more cleansing and protection work should be done.
  20. If a face appears in the yolk, it shows the face of the person who harbors ill will against you.
  21. Be careful of this person, forgive them, and work your cleansings and protections.
  22. If an eye appears in the yolk, this is the infamous evil eye.
  23. Work your cleansings and protections.
  24. You can perform the egg cleansing several times until the egg comes out normal looking.
  25. Small bubbles in the water mean your guardians have been at work for you and have already taken care of the problem.
  26. Send them thanks and reset your protections.
  27. Clear water, no blood, and no foul smell means no magickal workings against you.
  28. Reset your protections and thank your deities.
  29. No matter what you find in your egg, take it outside and bury it in the ground.
  30. This should not be done in a planter unless it is an extremely large one as not only will it possibly start to smell, animals such as opossums, raccoons, or even coyotes may be drawn to the smell and dig it up.
