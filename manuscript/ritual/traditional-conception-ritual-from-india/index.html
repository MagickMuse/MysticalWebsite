A full moon outdoor spell for traditional conception ritual from India, breaking coconut as symbol of fertility and planting five different seeds in mound to tend regularly.

Quantities

  • A whole coconut
  • A hammer or implement to break open the coconut
  • Five different kinds of seeds

Directions

  1. Facing the moon, hold the coconut in your outstretched hands and say: "The fruit is sweet with fecundity, the shell the womb, the milk new life, Mother Moon, by your bright light, I offer this symbol of fertility." Break open the coconut (or do this beforehand).
  2. Drink a little milk, eat some of the fruit, and dig a small mound in the earth or deep in a pot of soil.
  3. Repeat the words and bury the rest of the coconut in the soil, first tipping the milk into the hole.
  4. Plant five different kinds of seeds in the mound and tend them regularly.
  5. Perform when the moon is full, outdoors.
