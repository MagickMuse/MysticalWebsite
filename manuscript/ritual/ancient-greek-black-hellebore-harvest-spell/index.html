A Greek ritual for safely gathering black hellebore (the Christmas rose), a poisonous plant revered for its powerful magickal protective properties. The harvester casts a circle, petitions Apollo and Asklepios, and makes offerings before collecting the herb.

Quantities

  • 1 magick wand or ritual knife
  • Incense
  • Offerings for Apollo and Asklepios
  • Libation for the plant

Directions

  1. Using a magick wand or ritual knife, cast a circle around the plant you intend to harvest.
  2. If you do not work with either of those tools, find another respectful way to mark and consecrate the harvesting space.
  3. Once the circle is established, address Apollo and his son Asklepios, who are the presiding spirits of this plant, and formally request their permission to gather it.
  4. Burn incense and present offerings to the spirits as part of your petition.
  5. Finally, pour a libation directly onto the plant itself, and then harvest it.
