Ritual Grimoire

🏡Leave Beloved Lifetime Home

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
• 1 small item that is filled with good memories of your years in your home • 1 pot of growing lavender, rosemary, sage, or thyme • 1 small drawstring bag
◇Directions
1. Hold the item in your closed hands, saying: "Memories you fill my heart, sad it is that we must part. And yet I carry away with me, the essence of love that will always be." Pluck five leaves from the plant and put them in the bag, along with the memento, saying: "Herbs of fond remembrance, I leave with regretful backward glance. But for me you do remind that memories new I still can find." Leave the plant as a gift for the new owners/tenants. 2. Set the memento in the center of your new home as soon as you arrive and release the leaves from the bag outdoors.
◇Description
A ritual if you have to leave a beloved lifetime home, using a memento with herbs of remembrance to carry love forward while honoring the past and opening to new memories. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
