A ritual for summoning the guardian spirit Apoxias to perform binding, confusing, or expelling work against a person or spirit causing harm. Uses Apoxias' spirit house bottle, energy feeding, and the summoning mantra IO APOX-IAS IO HO along with a personal link to the target.

Quantities

  • Apoxias spirit house bottle (from Apoxias Spirit House ritual)
  • Altar space
  • Personal link to target (photo, object, or written name)
  • Paper for folding personal link
  • Writing implement to draw Apoxias sigil
  • Optional: energy source for feeding the bottle (visualization, breath work, etc.)

Directions

  1. This working assumes you have already created an Apoxias spirit house.
  2. If you have not, complete that ritual first.
  3. Set up an altar with Apoxias' spirit house bottle placed prominently in front of you.
  4. Begin by feeding the bottle with energy.
  5. You can do this through visualization, breath work, or any method that works for you to gather and project energy into the bottle.
  6. Once you have fed the bottle sufficiently, begin chanting the summoning mantra: IO APOX-IAS IO HO.
  7. Repeat this mantra until you feel that you have gotten Apoxias' attention.
  8. You may sense a shift in the energy, a presence, or simply an intuitive knowing that he is listening.
  9. When you feel his attention is with you, clearly state your request.
  10. Ask Apoxias to bind, confuse, or expel from your life the person or spirit who is harassing you.
  11. Be specific about what you need.
  12. If you want to make sure that no harm is brought to the target, explicitly state this intention.
  13. In cases where your life is in genuine danger, you may choose to let Apoxias act as he will, but understand that you must take full responsibility for whatever outcomes result from this choice.
  14. If you have a physical link to the target such as a photograph, personal item, or even just their name written on paper, use it to strengthen the connection.
  15. Take the personal link and fold it up in paper, taking care to fold the paper away from you as you do it.
  16. This symbolic action represents pushing the target away from you.
  17. Draw the sigil of Apoxias on the paper.
  18. Place the bottle directly on top of the folded paper.
  19. If the personal link is too bulky to fit comfortably under the bottle, place it in front of the bottle instead.
  20. Leave the working set up for at least three days if possible, or until you feel the work is complete.
  21. Thank Apoxias for his assistance when you dismantle the altar.
  22. As with all counter-magick, always perform divination beforehand to confirm that you are genuinely under spiritual attack from another human being and not experiencing crossed conditions stemming from your own missteps or retribution from spirits.
  23. Take responsibility for the outcome of your work.
