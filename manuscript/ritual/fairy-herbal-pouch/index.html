A Fairy charm crafted as a pillow or pouch to establish or maintain an energy connection with the Other People. Performed on a Friday, Midsummer's Eve, or November 11 during a Waxing to Full Moon. The practitioner selects herbs, cloth color, crystals, and decorations suited to the intended purpose, which may include attracting Fairies, seeking their aid in magick or divination, gaining companionship, receiving aid in Fairy Realm travel, dreaming of Faerie, or receiving Fairy protection. The item is sewn, consecrated through the Elementals with salt or burdock root, incense, candle flame, and blessed water, then charged on a pentacle with a wand. It is activated by placing in Full Moon light with an incantation inviting the Fair Ones.

Quantities

  • Cloth in chosen color
  • Thread in chosen color
  • Needle
  • Herbs suited to purpose
  • Crystals or stones suited to purpose
  • Decorative objects as desired
  • 1 pentacle
  • Blessed water
  • Sea salt or burdock root
  • Floral incense
  • Heather sprig (optional)
  • 1 wand
  • 1 lavender or light gray candle

Directions

  1. Select the color of cloth, herbs, and stones suitable to the intended purpose from the Lists of Correspondences.
  2. Sew the cloth in thread of the chosen color, leaving an opening for contents.
  3. Stuff the pillow with herbs, stones, and other chosen items, then sew shut.
  4. To consecrate, gather a pentacle, blessed water, sea salt or burdock root, floral incense, a heather sprig if desired, and a wand.
  5. Light a lavender or light gray candle.
  6. Pass the pillow through the Elementals and speak:
  7. I call upon the Powers of the Elementals to enliven the magick and stir the energies into this charm that it may call Fair Ones to me!
  8. By Earth, sprinkle with salt or root.
  9. By Air, pass through incense smoke.
  10. By Fire, pass through the candle flame.
  11. By Water, sprinkle with blessed water using the heather sprig.
  12. I consecrate this pillow to my use.
  13. So Mote It Be!
  14. Set the pillow on the pentacle and wave the wand or power hand overhead in a circle.
  15. Speak:
  16. I gather thy energies to work this spell and do my will.
  17. So Mote It Be!
  18. Touch the pillow with the wand or power hand and speak:
  19. Be thou charged, pouch of Fairy, [name the purpose of the pillow or pouch].
  20. It is done!
  21. So Mote It Be!
  22. To activate, store the pillow in a dark place until the Full Moon.
  23. Place it in moonlight and speak:
  24. Come to me Fair Ones,
    By the light of thy Faerie Moon!
    Bless this charm and grant my boon,
    With this pillow, [purpose] comes,
    May ye ever abide in friendship with me,
    That As I Will, So Mote It Be!
  25. Let the pillow stay in moonlight for one hour, then place where desired.
