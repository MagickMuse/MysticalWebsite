🕯️Nine-Night Honey Rose Candle Binding

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Dressed Candle
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickBindingHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Candle Honey Rosewater or rose hydrosol Rose petals Fabric (for wrapping on the ninth night) Red ribbon (Optional: menstrual blood, henna powder, or ground cinnamon)
◇Directions
1. Carve your name and your beloved's on a candle, adding any identifying information you wish. 2. Blend honey, rosewater or rose hydrosol, and rose petals into a sticky mixture, keep it on the drier side, adding as little floral water as possible. 3. Optional additions: menstrual blood, henna powder, or ground cinnamon. 4. Roll the candle in this mixture. 5. Light the candle at exactly 9 p.m. for exactly nine minutes on nine consecutive nights. 6. Incorporate the number nine wherever possible, ideally begin on the ninth day of the ninth month. 7. On the ninth night, take whatever remains of the candle and wrap it in fabric. 8. Tie with a red ribbon. 9. Bury this package at a crossroads, in a cemetery, at a crossroads within a cemetery, or under your front doorstep. 10. No one may witness any part of this spell or it will not work.
◇Description
A candle carved with both names, rolled in a honey and rose petal mixture, and burned for exactly nine minutes at 9 p.m. for nine consecutive nights, with the remnant wrapped in fabric and buried at a crossroads or cemetery. No witnesses are permitted at any stage or the spell fails.
