🔥Move-Your-Ass Fire Lighting

A move-your-ass ritual for a lazy boss or colleague whose work you have to do, using red candles with vigorous flame-blowing to light a fire under them daily.

Quantities

  • 2 large red candles

Directions

  1. Write the name of the lazy person invisibly on the first candle, top to bottom on one side.
  2. On the other side trace invisibly: "MOVE YOUR ASS," using your dominant hand's index finger.
  3. Light the candle, blow into the flame vigorously three times without putting it out and, between each blow, say: "Move your ass, this cannot pass; lazy bones always moans. No more shirking I do say, move your ass and I mean today!" Blow out the candle.
  4. Each morning before work, relight the candle and repeat the spell.
  5. When the candle is almost burned through, light it, light another red candle from it, and blow out the first candle, using the new candle for the spell until the situation drastically improves (maybe in an unexpected but good way).
