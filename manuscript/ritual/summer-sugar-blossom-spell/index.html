✨Summer Sugar Blossom Spell

◇Form
Food/EdibleCharged Object
◇Method
Herbal MagickCandle MagickManifestationVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Selection of fresh, edible blossoms of your choice 1 small oak twig, sharpened to a point to make a stylus Parchment paper or wax paper 1 nontoxic marker Confectioners' sugar 1 empty, clean salt shaker 1 medium egg 1 small pastry brush 1 votive candle in a glass holder
◇Directions
1. Gently rinse the flowers and remove any stems. Carefully blot them with a paper towel to remove excess moisture. Allow them to air-dry. 2. As the blossoms dry, scribe the Magick Square of the Sun on the parchment using the oak twig stylus. Meditate and focus on your intention or desire until you can distill it to its essence and express it in a single word. Once the word is clear in your mind, use the alphabet key (A=1, B=2, C=3... Z=26) to find the corresponding numbers. Trace the path of those numbers on the magick square to create a sigil. Set the sigil aside while you prepare the flowers. MAGICK SQUARE OF THE SUN: 6 32 3 34 35 1 7 11 27 28 8 30 19 14 16 15 23 24 18 20 22 21 17 13 25 29 10 9 26 12 36 5 33 4 2 31 3. For example, to create a sigil for "LOVE," lay the parchment over the grid and use a nontoxic marker. Begin at space 12 (L) and draw a connecting line to 15 (O), then continue to 22 (V), and finish at 5 (E). As you draw each connection, focus on your desired outcome. The resulting shape represents your journey from a starting point to the fulfillment of your wish. Embellish with flowers and leaves to make your sigil beautiful. Sigils can be as simple or as complex as you need them to be. 4. Put about 1 tablespoon of confectioners' sugar in the salt shaker. Separate the egg and discard the yolk. Beat the egg white until peaks begin to form. Dip the brush into the egg white and carefully paint the flower petals. Gently shake the sugar over the coated petals and allow any excess to fall off or gently blow it away. Repeat the sugar application. Allow the sugared flowers to dry completely on the parchment or wax paper. 5. As the flowers dry, envision the highest possible outcome of your intention transmuting from the paper into the flowers. The sigil informs upon the flowers as they dry. Allow the flowers to absorb your intention. 6. When the flowers are dry, fold the magick square and place it under the votive candle. Allow the candle to burn down as you enjoy your sugared flowers. Leave one flower outdoors as an offering to nature, and share one with a friend, especially if that person is in a position to impact the outcome of your intention. Take in the sweetness of nature and of life, made more potent by your intention and set into motion by your spell. Await the results with joyful anticipation.
◇Description
A kitchen witch spell that crystallizes edible flowers with sugar and a sun sigil to bring an intention to fruition. The sugared blossoms can be added to desserts, used to decorate a ritual feast, or shared to sweeten any outcome.
