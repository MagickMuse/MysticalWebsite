Hawthorn

A simple charm in which hawthorn leaves are placed beneath the mattress to magickally support contraception. Hawthorn is a plant with strong associations with both fertility and chastity, making it effective in either direction depending on intention.

Quantities

Hawthorn leaves

Directions

  1. Tuck hawthorn leaves under the mattress with the intention of enhancing contraception.
  2. Refresh the leaves periodically as they dry.

Hawthorn is one of the few plants in the European tradition explicitly associated with both fertility and chastity, depending on the context and intention. It is the sacred tree of Beltane - a festival of sexuality and union - and simultaneously a tree that appears in protective and restraining charms. The plant holds both possibilities.

Placing it beneath the mattress - the site of the working it is meant to influence - is a form of sympathetic proximity. The intention is embedded in the object and positioned at the exact location where it is meant to act.

Hawthorn's thorns are part of its character as a protective plant. The same thorns that make it a living barrier in hedgerows translate symbolically into a barrier around fertility when the intention is set that way.
