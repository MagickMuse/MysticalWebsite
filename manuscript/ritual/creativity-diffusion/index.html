✨Creativity Diffusion

◇Tradition

◇Solitary or Group

◇Category
Spell

Beginner
◇Quantities
- Pine essential oil - Diffuser - Optional: favorite color of paint or ink
◇Directions
1. To inspire a dormant muse, diffuse pine essential oil in creative spaces 10 minutes to a half hour before entering to begin work. 2. Walking into a clean studio (as clean as any art studio really gets) smelling of pine enlivens the spirit and invigorates the muse. 3. Consider adding a drop of pine oil to a favorite color of paint, or using pine-scented ink to write with as well. 4. The fresh, green scent clears stagnant energy and opens the mind to new possibilities.
◇Description
A practice to inspire a dormant muse by diffusing pine oil in creative spaces before beginning work.
