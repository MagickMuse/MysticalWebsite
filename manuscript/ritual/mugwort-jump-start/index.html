Mugwort

A single-use dream-stimulation bath using mugwort flower remedy to initiate or re-initiate an active dreaming practice. Not intended for regular use. Best suited for beginning dream work for the first time, starting a new course of dreaming, restarting after a long lull, or kicking off an intensive dream spell or oracle. ⚠️ Use the flower remedy only, never the essential oil. One of the Dream Bath methods.

Quantities

  • 20–30 drops of mugwort flower essence remedy (NOT essential oil)
  • Optional: follow manufacturer's dosage for internal use as well

Directions

  1. Draw a warm bath and adjust the temperature before adding anything.
  2. Once the water is ready, add 20 to 30 drops of mugwort flower essence remedy to the bath.
  3. Do not use the essential oil under any circumstances.
  4. Take this bath just before going to sleep.
  5. If you wish to continue using mugwort flower remedy in subsequent baths, reduce the dose to no more than five drops per bath after this initial use.
