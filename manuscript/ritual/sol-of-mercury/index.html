A simple prayer for blessing the intellect and understanding the nature of the logos.

Quantities

  • No materials required

Directions

  1. Perform this invocation on the day of Mercury at the hour of the Sun.
  2. Vibrate the vowels Epsilon followed by Iota (EH-EE), with the day vowel shorter in duration followed by a longer vibration of the hour vowel.
  3. Then speak the invocation:
  4. Hermes and Helios Honorable and Holy
    May my intelligence be illuminated
    by the Living Light of Life
    May my contemplations bring comprehension
    of the Cosmic center of all consciousness
    And initiate me into the inner adytum.
