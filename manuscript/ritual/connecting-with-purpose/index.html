A meditation practice inspired by Earthworm's clear sense of purpose. By visualizing your inner self as a still lake and dropping in the question of purpose, you receive soul answers through imagery and metaphor rather than ego-driven directives.

Quantities

  • Optional: Journal and pen for recording insights

Directions

  1. Sometimes believing your soul has greater purpose beyond paying bills and feeding yourself feels impossible.
  2. But what happens when you believe it for a few minutes daily?
  3. Find a comfortable, quiet place to sit or lie down.
  4. Breathe slow and steady, following your breath in and out.
  5. Imagine your inner self as a still, clear lake.
  6. You can see your reflection in the water, rippling as the lake breathes with you.
  7. Now imagine a pebble tossed into the lake.
  8. It's small and bears this question: What's my purpose?
  9. See what images arise as the pebble changes the ripples on the water, creating pictures.
  10. As you slowly return to yourself, record what you saw.
  11. Over days or weeks, piece together images answering your question.
  12. Discard directives as ego messages and focus on metaphor and imagery.
  13. Be Ready for Unexpected Answers: When you ask soul questions, you get soul answers, not always comfortable ones.
  14. The answer may not suit your ego or current lifestyle.
