A waxing to Full Moon spell to ward a home using bundled protective herbs tied with red thread. The bundles are consecrated on the altar with a ritual knife, then hung in each room for a year of continuous protection.

Quantities

  • A few stems of any combination: dill, fennel, marjoram, mint, mustard, mullein, rosemary, rue, white heather, woodruff, yarrow
  • Red thread

Directions

  1. Tie small bundles of herbs together with red thread and place them on the altar.
  2. Touch each bundle with the ritual knife and speak:
  3. I conjure thee, protective herbs, on this day of [the Moon] and in this hour of [Mars], to be a protection and safeguard against all adversity and evil.
  4. Protect well this house and all who dwell within.
  5. As I will, So Mote It Be!
  6. Hang one bundle in each room.
  7. Replace in one year, scattering outdoors in the wind or disposing of in the trash.
