🧘Calm and Centering

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Incense Blend
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
MeditationBreathworkHerbal Magick
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- 1 drop pine essential oil - Diffuser
◇Directions
1. Diffuse a drop of pine essential oil whenever you feel overwhelmed or out of control. 2. Settle into a comfortable position and breathe mindfully. 3. Inhale through the nose and exhale through the mouth. 4. Allow the clean, green scent of pine to anchor you to the present moment. 5. Feel your feet on the ground and your body in space. 6. With each breath, release tension and return to your center. 7. Continue for as long as needed until you feel grounded and calm.
◇Description
A calming practice using diffused pine oil to center yourself when feeling overwhelmed or out of control.
