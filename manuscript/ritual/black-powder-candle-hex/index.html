⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A reversed purple candle rolled in a blend of coal dust, Black Salt, and Goofer Dust, burned while channeling rage, with all spell remnants left at the enemy's doorstep.

Quantities

Charcoal or coal dust
Black Salt
Goofer Dust
1 purple candle
1 plate
100 pins

Directions

  1. Grate charcoal until you have a fine coal dust; true coal may be substituted.
  2. Blend the coal dust with Black Salt and Goofer Dust.
  3. Carve the purple candle as desired, then slice the top flat and expose the wick at the bottom so the candle burns reversed.
  4. Roll it in the powder blend.
  5. Set the candle on a plate and light it.
  6. Remove all clothing and jewelry, and loosen your hair.
  7. Channel your full rage into the working.
  8. Drive one hundred pins into the burning candle, screaming, shouting, and cursing as you see fit.
  9. When the working is complete, leave all spell remnants, including the plate, on the enemy's doorstep.
