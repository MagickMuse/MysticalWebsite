A Dumb Supper ritual performed by two living participants alongside invited deceased guests. The meal is eaten in complete silence. Afterward, both living participants may share and compare their experiences and perceptions.

Quantities

  • Food and drink for the meal
  • Table settings and chairs for all guests (living and deceased)

Directions

  1. Set the table for three or more, accounting for the two living participants and any deceased guests.
  2. Ensure there are enough chairs so the departed have a place to sit.
  3. Serve both the living and the departed.
  4. Maintain complete silence throughout the meal and until everyone has left the table.
  5. Afterward, both living participants are free to discuss what they each perceived, sensed, or experienced.
