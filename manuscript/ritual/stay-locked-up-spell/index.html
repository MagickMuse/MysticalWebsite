🏺Stay Locked Up Spell

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
Binding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Brown paper (for writing the target's name) - Black pepper - Cayenne pepper, ground chili pepper, or habanero powder - 1 nail (rusty preferred; coffin nail for life sentence intent) - Ammonia - 1 key - Clear bowl or glass vessel
◇Directions
1. Write the target's name on a piece of brown paper and place it inside a clear bowl or glass vessel so the contents remain visible from the outside. 2. Layer black pepper over the paper, then add a layer of cayenne pepper, ground hot chili, or habanero powder on top. 3. Add a nail to the bowl, using a rusty one for general purposes or a coffin nail if the intent is a life sentence. 4. Pour ammonia over everything until the contents are fully covered. 5. Place a key upright in the bowl, leaning it against the inside of the vessel. 6. The key will fall; vigilance is required to keep it standing. 7. Every day at noon, turn the key a quarter-turn to maintain the binding.
◇Description
A jar spell intended to keep a specific person incarcerated. Layers of hot peppers, a nail, and ammonia are built over the target's name inside a clear bowl, and a key is maintained upright in the vessel and turned daily at noon to keep the binding active.
