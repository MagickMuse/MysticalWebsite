Coconut OilJasmine AbsolutePeppermint Essential Oil

A shimmering coconut oil ointment blended with sparkly eyeshadow, peppermint, and jasmine essential oils under a Gemini or Libra full moon. Anoint your collarbones, throat, and under your ears to enchant your words and radiate social confidence. Can also be performed with perfume, lotion, or body glitter you already own, the incantation and intention are the working.

Quantities

  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil
  • Small container with lid
  • Toothpick
  • Sparkly eyeshadow or highlighter (blue or white preferred)
  • 2 drops peppermint essential oil
  • 5 drops jasmine essential oil

Directions

  1. Set your ingredients on your altar or in a spot where they can receive moonlight.
  2. Spoon the coconut oil into your container; coconut oil carries its own lunar association, making it a natural base for this working.
  3. Using a toothpick, lift a small amount of eyeshadow and stir it into the coconut oil.
  4. Start with less than you think you need; you can always add more.
  5. As you mix, visualize butterflies and lunar moths in flight, wings catching the moonlight, shimmering as they move through the night air.
  6. Add your essential oils: peppermint for communication and sharpness of thought, jasmine for lunar enchantment and irresistible charm.
  7. Stir everything together clockwise.
  8. Seal the container and place it outside or on a windowsill to charge under the moonlight.
  9. To use, stand before a mirror.
  10. Look at yourself and visualize your skin lit from within by moonlight.
  11. Say:
  12. "Blessed by the moon's light,
    My words as enchanting as a butterfly in flight."
  13. Anoint the base of your throat, under your ears, and along your collarbones with a small amount of the glitter oil.
  14. The throat is a powerful Gemini-ruled location, the seat of communication and expression.
  15. Go out and let yourself shine.
  16. If you'd prefer not to make the blend, you can perform the mirror incantation and anointment with any perfume, lotion, or body glitter you already own.
  17. The intention and the moon's blessing are the heart of the working.
