🦷Shark's Tooth Petition Charm

A targeted talisman for specific winnings or financial goals, a parchment petition written in Bat's Blood Ink spiked with pinesap, sewn into red fabric with a shark's tooth using cat hair, carried until the goal is achieved.

Quantities

Pinesap
Bat's Blood Ink
1 piece of parchment
1 shark's tooth
Red fabric (enough to wrap the parchment and tooth)
Cat hair (gathered from clothing, furniture, etc.)
Needle and thread

Directions

  1. Add pinesap to Bat's Blood Ink to strengthen it.
  2. Write the exact amount you need to win, or the specific goal to be achieved, on the parchment.
  3. Place the parchment inside a piece of red fabric together with the shark's tooth.
  4. Sew it shut using cat hair as thread; gather the hair from sweaters, sofas, or coats.
  5. There is no need to bother the cat, and doing so will bring the opposite of good luck.
  6. Carry the finished charm in your left pocket over your heart, or wear it inside your left shoe.
  7. Once the charm has worked and the goal is achieved, take it apart and burn the parchment.
  8. Reserve the shark's tooth for future workings.
