May Day is the holy day belonging to Flora, the Roman spirit of flowers and happy sex. She may also be petitioned for fertility, especially if infertility treatment and sex on demand has dampened the joys of the sexual experience.

✨
All rites for Flora are traditionally performed nude.
  1. Request healing, happiness, and access to life's pleasures.
  2. Beckon Flora with fresh flowers. She especially favors lupines. Her sacrament, the gift you can offer her, is blissful sex.
