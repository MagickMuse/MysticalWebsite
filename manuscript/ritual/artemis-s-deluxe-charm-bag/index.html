The Moon

A charm bag filled with artemisia herbs, moonstone or chalcedony, a silver charm, and a Moon tarot card, anointed and carried on psychic journeys for added magickal power and protection.

Quantities

1 charm bag
A pinch each of dried mugwort, wormwood, Sweet Annie, desert sage, southernwood, and/or tarragon
Moonstone and/or chalcedony
A piece of true silver (any small charm; a tiny silver dog or dragon is ideal)
Miniature Moon tarot card (optional)
Tuberose essential oil or night-blooming jasmine attar

Directions

  1. Add a pinch of all or some of the dried herbs to the charm bag: mugwort, wormwood, Sweet Annie, desert sage, southernwood, and tarragon.
  2. Add a moonstone and/or a chalcedony crystal.
  3. Add a piece of true silver; any small charm works, but a tiny silver dog or dragon is perfect.
  4. If you can obtain a miniature Moon tarot card, add it to the bag.
  5. Anoint the bag with a drop of tuberose essential oil or night-blooming jasmine attar.
  6. Carry it with you during psychic journeys for added magickal power and protection.
