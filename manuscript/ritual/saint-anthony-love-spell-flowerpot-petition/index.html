⛪Saint Anthony Love Spell Flowerpot Petition

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PetitionPrayerKnot MagickDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Two flowerpots containing basil One flowerpot containing rue Crimson silk ribbon (enough for three pots, each with three knots) Pins
◇Directions
1. Fill two flowerpots with basil and a third with rue. 2. Wrap each flowerpot with a crimson silk ribbon made with three knots and pierced with pins. 3. Petition and pray to Saint Anthony as you work the spell. 4. At midnight, place the pots on an open windowsill with the rue in the middle. 5. Listen for the first words or sounds you hear: this is the response to your petition. 6. Favorable signs include knocking at the door, barking dogs, people whistling, laughing, or singing. 7. Unfavorable signs include a hearse or a dark horse, indicating the saint will not work this petition. 8. A white horse is favorable but indicates considerable time and effort will be needed. 9. Additional signals may be pre-arranged with Saint Anthony.
◇Description
Two pots of basil and one of rue are wrapped in crimson silk ribbons with three knots and pierced with pins, then placed on a midnight windowsill (rue in the center). The first sounds heard afterward are interpreted as Saint Anthony's response: barking, knocking, or singing are favorable; a hearse or dark horse are not.
