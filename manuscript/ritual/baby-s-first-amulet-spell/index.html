Egyptian protective spell for newborn infants, creating a beaded amulet to ward off the demon Mereret and other hostile spirits.

Quantities

  • Gold beads
  • Carnelian beads
  • 1 sealing-bead with crocodile
  • 1 hand-pendant
  • 1 strip of fine linen

Directions

  1. Gather the following materials: beads of gold, beads of carnelian, a sealing-bead with a crocodile carved on it, and a hand-pendant.
  2. Recite the following incantation over the materials:
  3. 'Are you warm in the nest?
  4. Are you hot among the bushes?
  5. Is your mother not with you?
  6. Is there no sister to offer a breeze?
  7. Is there no nurse for protection?
  8. Let there be brought to me beads of gold, beads of carnelian, a sealing-bead with a crocodile on it, and a hand-pendant to slay and to dispel the female demon Mereret, to warm the body, to slay the male and female enemies in the West.
  9. You will break free!'
  10. After reciting, say: 'This is a protection.'
  11. String the beads and pendant on a strip of fine linen to make an amulet.
  12. Apply to the throat of the child.
  13. Each bead carries particular magickal meaning, giving the necklace the power to ward off demonic threats.
