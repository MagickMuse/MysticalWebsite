🪢Tanglefoot Charm

An old European binding spell using a red cord and nine knots to stop someone from doing a particular thing, whether harmful (like stalking or cursing) or mundane (like going to work). The spell tangles, binds, and chains the target's ability to perform the specified action through earth magick and crossroads burial.

Quantities

  • Red cord
  • Personal item or link to the target (letter, photo, hair, etc.)
  • Shovel for burial

Directions

  1. Preparation: Decide upon the specific action you wish to prevent the target from doing.
  2. Obtain a personal link to the target that can be tied into the cord (such as a letter they wrote, a photo, hair, etc.).
  3. The Binding: Take the red cord in your left hand.
  4. Concentrate intensely on the thing you wish your enemy to stop doing.
  5. Tie nine knots in the cord in this specific order: 1-3-5-7-9-8-6-4-2 (starting at the outer edges and moving inward).
  6. As you tie each knot, say:
  7. "(Name of target), I conjure thee
    By the power of Earth thou art tangled
    By the power of rock thou art bound
    By the power of clay thou art chained
    Held in place by the weight of the ground"
  8. Work the personal item of the target into the ninth (center) knot.
  9. Crossroads Burial: When you have completed all nine knots, go to a crossroads.
  10. Bury the cord there while saying:
  11. "(Name of target), I bury your power (to do harm to me, or whatever the binding is for)
    Twisted and tangled
    Chained and bound
    I lay thee to rest
    Committed to ground."
  12. Walk away from the site without looking back.
  13. Important Notes: Magick manifests in natural ways.
  14. The target may be physically prevented from the action through injury, circumstances, or other means.
  15. Be willing to accept responsibility for the consequences.
  16. Divine the outcome before performing this work.
