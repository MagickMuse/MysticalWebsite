A ritual to sever energetic attachments to people, places, objects, beliefs, or emotions using visualization or sympathetic magick.

Quantities

  • 1 piece of yarn or string
  • 1 pair of scissors
  • 2 symbolic objects (photos, candles, or small items, one to represent you, one to represent what you're releasing)

Directions

  1. Choose a quiet space where you won’t be disturbed.
    Tie one end of the string to the object that represents yourself.
    Tie the other end to the object symbolizing the person, place, or energy you are cutting ties with.
    As you do this, focus on the connection between you and what you’re releasing.
    When ready, take the scissors and cut the string between the two objects.
    Visualize the energetic cord between you being fully and cleanly severed.
    Take a moment to feel the shift, imagine your energy returning to you, and theirs returning to them.
    Gather all the materials and dispose of them outside your home, such as in a public dumpster or trash bin.
    Cleanse your space afterward, if desired, and ground your energy.
