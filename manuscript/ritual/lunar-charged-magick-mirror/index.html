A Chinese technique for drawing down the moon into a small round mirror. Over fifteen nights, the mirror absorbs lunar energy and merges with the owner's personal power, becoming a profound magickal tool for obtaining wishes and desires.

Quantities

  • 1 small round mirror (ideally crafted from gold or silver, ornamented with protective symbols)
  • Protective fabric pouch or wrapping cloth

Directions

  1. On the night of the Full Moon, hold the mirror so that it reflects the moon for a minimum of three hours.
  2. Once you feel sufficient lunar energy has been absorbed, wrap the mirror in a protective fabric pouch.
  3. From this point forward, the mirror belongs exclusively to one person.
  4. Although someone else may prepare the mirror as a gift, the remaining steps must be performed only by the owner.
  5. For the next fifteen consecutive nights, gaze into the mirror for substantial periods of time.
  6. This process is interactive: as you absorb the moon's gifts of radiance, beauty, psychic ability, and fertility, the mirror simultaneously absorbs your personal power and desires.
  7. After the fifteen-night period, the mirror is considered fully charged and in harmony with its owner.
  8. It now functions as a powerful magickal tool.
  9. To use the mirror, repeat the initial ritual of drawing down the Full Moon, then use it as a direct communication device to request gifts of love, marriage, psychic power, creative inspiration, personal fertility, or healing.
