A visualization practice for creating layered energetic barriers around a home using geometric shapes. Works alone or in tandem with crystal grids for anchoring.

Quantities

None required (visualization-based)
Optional: crystal grid for anchoring

Directions

  1. Visualize the three-dimensional shape encompassing the designated area.
  2. Ensure the shape extends underground to cover wells, water mains, electrical hookups, and cable lines.
  3. Add texture to the visualization for additional strength.
  4. Use the same techniques as raising power.
  5. Practice raising and lowering the wards until the process becomes fluid.

Definition

A ward is an energetic barrier blocking unwelcome energies from entering your space. Wards take many forms and shapes, are commonly layered for greater strength, and typically use geometric designs. More complex shapes provide stronger protection.

Assessment

Before setting wards, evaluate your living situation:

Shared Buildings

If you live in an apartment, townhouse, or row home:

  • Design wards that allow neighbors to come and go freely
  • Consider protecting the entire building from fire for your own safety
  • Layer smaller wards around your specific unit

Standalone Homes

Assess the surrounding area for hazards (trees that could fall in storms, etc.) and incorporate weather protection into outer ward layers.

Layered Ward Structure

Work from outermost to innermost, using increasingly complex shapes:

Example: House on Land

  1. Outermost (Cube): Covers entire property. Protects against weather and general harm directed at home or inhabitants.
  2. Middle (Octagon): A few feet inside the cube. Protects against theft and vandalism.
  3. Innermost (Icosahedron): Twenty-sided shape made of triangles, placed directly over the home exterior. Maximum protection.

Example: Apartment

  1. Outermost (Circle): Around entire building. Protects all residents from fire.
  2. Middle (Cube): Borders your apartment. Blocks anyone without your best interest.
  3. Innermost: Smaller shape protecting bedroom specifically.

Anchoring

Crystal grids can anchor wards and keep them energized. The two techniques work effectively in tandem.
