An emergency potion for when tragedy strikes, using hot chocolate with peppermint oil to jolt yourself out of shock. Invokes Isis. Created on 9/11/2001.

Quantities

Hot chocolate mix, not sugar-free unless diabetic, with real milk
Milk
1-2 drops essential oil of peppermint

Directions

  1. Get out a saucepan, a mug or cup, milk, a cocoa mix, a spoon, and oil of peppermint.
  2. Make the hot chocolate, keeping enough awareness to do this carefully.
  3. As you stir the cocoa, say repeatedly: "Isis, help me."
  4. Pour the hot chocolate into a cup. Add one or two drops of essential oil of peppermint, and stir the cup just once, so some oil floats on the surface.
  5. Take a big gulp as soon as the chocolate is cool enough to drink, then sip the rest slowly.
  6. Have a second cup later if needed.
