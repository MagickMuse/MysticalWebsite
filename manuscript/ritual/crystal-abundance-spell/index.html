🪨Crystal Abundance Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting

◇Method
Energy WorkVisualizationLunar
◇Category

◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- 1 quartz crystal (preferably one with greenish mineral inclusions, known as a "money crystal" or "abundance crystal") - An image that represents abundance to you (magazine picture or downloaded image)
◇Directions
1. Select a magazine picture or download an online image that symbolizes prosperity to you. 2. It might be an object or condition you desire, such as a new home, a sports car, or a vacation destination. 3. Lay the picture face up on a windowsill where the moon's light will shine on it. 4. Wash the crystal in warm water with mild soap to cleanse it of any unwanted energies. 5. Dry the crystal, then hold it to your third eye (between your eyebrows) while thinking about your intention. 6. Doing this sends the image into the crystal. 7. Set the crystal on the picture. 8. Make sure the crystal's point faces toward the inside of your home, to draw what you want to you. 9. Leave the crystal in place overnight. 10. In the morning, remove the crystal and picture and give thanks for the bounty you are about to receive.
◇Description
A crystal-charging spell using quartz and an abundance image. Some quartz crystals contain bits of greenish mineral matter, known as "money crystals" or "abundance crystals." If possible, use one of these. Otherwise, a clear quartz crystal will work fine.
