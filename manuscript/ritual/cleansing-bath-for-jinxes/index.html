A traditional cleansing bath using white oak bark, cinnamon, and pine needles to remove jinxes, curses, and accumulated negativity. Particularly effective for clearing evil you brought upon yourself through your own actions. Hyssop can be substituted for this specific purpose of self-caused harm. These ingredients can be added liberally to bathwater without the toxicity concerns of ammonia-based formulas.

Quantities

  • Required (choose one formula):
  • White oak bark: liberal amount
  • Cinnamon: liberal amount
  • Pine needles: liberal amount
    OR for self-caused evil:
  • Hyssop: liberal amount
    All ingredients can be added generously to bathwater without concern for overdose.

Directions

  1. Prepare bathwater.
  2. Add white oak bark, cinnamon, and pine needles liberally to the water.
  3. Alternatively, if cleansing evil you brought upon yourself through your own actions, use hyssop instead.
  4. The ingredients can be added directly to the bath or steeped first and strained if preferred.
  5. Enter the bath.
  6. Scrub downward from head to feet to remove the jinx and push it away from you.
  7. As you bathe, acknowledge any harm you may have caused yourself through ignorance or poor choices.
  8. Visualize the curse or jinx breaking apart and washing away.
  9. See yourself freed from all negative attachments and crossed conditions.
  10. Recite the fifty-first Psalm for purification, or speak words of your own acknowledging mistakes and claiming freedom.
  11. Soak as long as needed to feel the release.
  12. When complete, either drain the water or collect it to dump toward the rising sun in the east.
