🪔The Lamp of Reversal

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
ReversalHerbal MagickProtectionVisualizationCandle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Required: - Fresh coconut: 1 (with liquid inside) - Needles: 9 - Pins: 9 - Nails: 9 - Devil's shoestring: 9 pieces - Dill - Salt - Rue - Lamp oil or vegetable oil - Wick - Matches or lighter
◇Directions
1. Preparation: Carefully open the coconut by drilling or cutting a hole in the top, preserving the coconut water inside. The opening should be large enough to add ingredients but small enough to accommodate a wick. 2. Assembling the Lamp: Add the nine needles, nine pins, and nine nails to the coconut. These sharp objects will pierce and reverse the curse. 3. Add the nine pieces of devil's shoestring to bind and tangle up the malicious magick. 4. Add a generous pinch of dill for protection and hex-breaking. 5. Add a pinch of salt for purification and protection. 6. Add rue for its powerful reversing properties. 7. Filling and Lighting: Fill the coconut with lamp oil or vegetable oil until it nearly reaches the top, leaving room for the wick. 8. Insert a wick into the coconut, ensuring it reaches down into the oil. 9. Light the wick and let the lamp burn. 10. The Working: As the lamp burns, focus on the reversal of the curse. Visualize the harmful magick being gathered up by the needles, pins, and nails, bound by the devil's shoestring, purified by the salt and dill, and sent back to its sender by the rue. 11. Let the lamp burn until the oil is consumed or for as long as needed for your working. 12. Disposal: Once the working is complete, take the coconut and its contents to a crossroads and leave it there as an offering to Hekate, or bury it at a crossroads to seal the reversal.
◇Description
A coconut lamp reversal spell using nine each of needles, pins, and nails along with devil's shoestring, dill, salt, and rue to create a powerful reversal working that sends curses back to their sender while protecting the practitioner.
