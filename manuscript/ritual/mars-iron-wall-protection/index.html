🛡️Mars Iron Wall Protection

An 18-night Tuesday ritual with horseshoe-shaped paper clips, rosemary, salt, soil, and sour wine to invoke Mars's iron protection.

Quantities

  • Small quantity of soil
  • 1 small dish of salt
  • 1 ceramic lidded jar
  • 18 paper clips (straightened, shaped into small horseshoes)
  • Dried rosemary
  • Sour or inexpensive red wine or vinegar

Directions

  1. The first Tuesday, add a sprinkling of soil and salt to the jar, saying, "The protection of Mars makes iron walls, impenetrable to those who call, with hate or malice in their heart. Depart, I say, depart." Add a U-shaped paper clip to the jar, saying the same spell words.
  2. Place some dried rosemary in it, put on the lid, and shake it nine times, repeating the spell words nine times.
  3. Repeat this each night.
  4. When you have put in all 18 paper clips, add sour red wine; close the jar and shake it nine times, saying nine times, "Ferment, you are sent back by Mars's iron will. Barred from here, never again to appear." When it is dark, bury the jar outside or dispose of it.
  5. Perform beginning Tuesday and for eighteen nights, consecutive if possible.
