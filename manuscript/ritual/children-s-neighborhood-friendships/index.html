Circle🟢Green

A waxing moon ritual with green clay dolls in circle, rolled into ball, reformed joining hands to unite neighborhood children.

Quantities

  • Children's green modeling clay (formed into small featureless dolls, same number as children)

Directions

  1. Set the figures in a circle and touch each one, naming the person that doll represents, saying, "In and out of houses, sharing games and fun. Friendship grows in every way, uniting everyone." Now roll all the figures into a big ball, repeating the spell words.
  2. Re-create the figures, but this time make the arms longer so each one is joining hands in the circle, saying, "Play, stay friends through the passing years, share your triumphs and your secrets, your laughter and your tears." Roll all the figures into a ball again and keep it, wrapped, in a kitchen drawer until it crumbles.
  3. Perform any time during the waxing moon.
