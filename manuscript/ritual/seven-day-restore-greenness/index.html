🌲Seven-Day Restore Greenness

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/MantraElemental
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Seven small green plants and an empty tub of soil or a garden area - Water, if possible rainwater or water from a natural source - Seven moss or tree agates, the gardener's crystal, or any small green crystals or glass beads
◇Directions
1. On Friday, plant the first seedling and plant the crystal beneath it, saying: "Friday, bring back greenness, regenerate the fertile places anew, forests, fields, and wildernesses too. Restore the lungs of Mother Earth. For one tree planted, grow five, for five, ten, and then a hundred. Thus shall the earth be green again and breathe anew." On Saturday, say: "Friday, Saturday" and continue the words above as you plant the second seedling and crystal in a circle. 2. Continue until you have planted all seven seedlings and crystals, adding a day to the chant each time. 3. On day 7, name all seven days; you have planted all the seedlings and crystals. 4. Water weekly, naming all the days of the week in the chant. 5. If any plant dies, replace it on the appropriate day of the week. 6. Perform Friday for seven days.
◇Description
A seven-day Friday spell for restoring greenness and reforestation to the world, planting seven seedlings with crystals in circle to regenerate forests and make earth green again.
