🌍Garden of the Wider World

A candle garden for global issues, causes, and places you care about. Uses large candles for long-term solutions to world problems.

Quantities

Large candles, one for each global issue, place, or organization you care about
Optional: dragon's blood incense

Directions

  1. Choose global issues you care passionately about, such as the environment, world hunger, or endangered species; places you care about, such as a country, region, or your ancestral homeland; or organizations whose work you appreciate, and set aside a large candle for each.
  2. Add candles for groups of people you are concerned about, such as children, refugees, or disaster victims.
  3. Leave room to grow the garden; you'll likely add candles after watching the news or reading about new causes.
  4. When activating the garden, focus on sending positive energy to each problem or group as you light its candle. Dragon's blood incense is appropriate to burn during activation.

Whatever the spiritual path, if one follows it correctly, there comes a time when one is more concerned for others than for oneself. In this garden you include candles for global issues and causes you care passionately about.

It will be a time of great happiness whenever you discover that one of this garden's candles is no longer needed.
