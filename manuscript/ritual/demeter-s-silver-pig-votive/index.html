🌱Demeter's Silver Pig Votive

A petition to Demeter, goddess of fertility and the Eleusinian Mysteries, via a votive altar adorned with her sacred symbols: poppies, pomegranates, snakes, and horses. A small gold or silver pig is dedicated to her as a votive charm worn for fertility and luck. When a child is born, a larger pig is placed permanently on the altar in gratitude.

Quantities

A small gold or silver pig charm or figurine
Poppies (fresh, dried, or depicted)
Pomegranates (whole or depicted)
Images or figurines of snakes and horses

Directions

  1. Build an altar to Demeter.
  2. Decorate it with poppies and pomegranates and images of snakes and horses.
  3. Dedicate a small gold or silver pig figure to her as a votive offering.
  4. Address her directly, explaining your desire for a child.
  5. Wear the pig charm on your person for fertility and luck.
  6. If and when you bear a healthy child, place a larger, more valuable pig figure permanently on the altar as an offering of gratitude and fulfillment.

Demeter presided over the Eleusinian Mysteries, once the most prominent religious rituals in ancient Greece. Her mythology is woven through with the tension between fertility and barrenness, sacred sexuality, and the loss and reclamation of women's personal and political power.

The pig was Demeter's most sacred sacrificial animal, connected to the mysteries of earth, seed, and the chthonic world. Poppies are among her most recognized symbols, tied equally to the abundance of the harvest and the sleep of grief. The pomegranate, so central to the myth of Persephone, bridges death and fertility in Demeter's domain.

Snakes and horses both figure in her mythology: the horse through her union with Poseidon, the snake through her chthonic aspect as Black Demeter.
