⚔️St. Joan Courage Ritual

A Tuesday at sunset spell for St. Joan of Arc ritual for courage when faced with adversity or injustice, holding silver letter opener in cross positions from forehead to knee to left shoulder to right shoulder saying give me courage strength justice power victory for my cause is right be with me as day turns into night.

Quantities

  • A silver letter opener

Directions

  1. Facing the sunset, hold the letter opener about half an arm's length away from you, level with your forehead (carefully), saying: "St. Joan, give me courage for this fight, that I may show nobility as day turns into night." Bring the letter opener down to knee level at the same distance, saying: "St. Joan, give me strength for this fight, that I may show nobility as day turns into night." Bring the letter opener diagonally toward your left shoulder, holding it horizontally to the left of your shoulder, saying: "St. Joan, give me justice in this fight, that I may show nobility as day turns into night." Bring the letter opener horizontally to just beyond your right shoulder, saying: "St. Joan, give me power for this fight, that I may show nobility as day turns into night." Put the letter opener down, name your cause, and say: "St. Joan, give me victory, for my cause is right. Be with me now and always, as day turns into night." Wash the letter opener under running water.
  2. Perform Tuesday, at sunset.
