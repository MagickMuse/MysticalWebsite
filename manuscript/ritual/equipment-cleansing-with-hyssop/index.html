A spell for cleansing any equipment you inherit or buy to make it work for you, using hyssop or mint infusion sprinkled in counterclockwise circles to purify and bless.

Quantities

  • Dried hyssop or mint or green tea bags
  • A cup, jug, or teapot
  • A teaspoon

Directions

  1. Make a hyssop infusion with one teaspoon of dried herbs, pouring on almost a cupful of boiling water.
  2. For larger amounts, mix one ounce of the dried herb to a pint and a quarter of water.
  3. Or use tea bags.
  4. Stir the infusion, saying continuously: "Hyssop, hyssop [or name herb], cleanse and purify. Bless and sanctify that this [name equipment] will serve me well, giving birth to new resources and sources of income." Let the mix stand for five to ten minutes, stirring occasionally, then strain the infusion or the remove tea bag.
  5. Sprinkle three counterclockwise circles of the infusion around the item, groups of small items, or a photograph, repeating the chant.
  6. Pour any remaining mix outdoors on soil.
  7. Perform whenever you buy or hire equipment.
