Ritual Grimoire

🕯️Kel Maleh Rachamim

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
PrayerAncestor WorkChant/Mantra
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
Optional: a charitable donation made in the deceased's name
◇Directions
1. No ritual materials are required, though it is traditional to accompany this prayer with a charitable donation made in the name of the deceased. 2. Speak the appropriate version aloud, filling in the name of the person you are praying for and their parent's name where indicated. 3. For a woman: God, full of mercy, who dwells above, give rest on the wings of the Divine Presence, amongst the holy, pure and glorious who shine like the sky, to the soul of [name], daughter of [parent's name], for whom charity was offered in the memory of her soul. Therefore, the Merciful One will protect her soul forever, and will merge her soul with eternal life. The Everlasting is her heritage, and she shall rest peacefully at her lying place, and let us say: Amen. 4. For a man: God, full of mercy, who dwells in the heights, provide a sure rest upon the Divine Presence's wings, within the range of the holy, pure and glorious, whose shining resemble the sky's, to the soul of [name], son of [parent's name], for a charity was given to the memory of his soul. Therefore, the Master of Mercy will protect him forever, from behind the hiding of his wings, and will tie his soul with the rope of life. The Everlasting is his heritage, and he shall rest peacefully upon his lying place, and let us say: Amen.
◇Description
A traditional Jewish prayer for the dead used in both Ashkenazi and Sephardic liturgies, spoken during mourning rites and throughout the year as an ongoing act of remembrance. The prayer petitions for the soul of the deceased to be granted rest under the wings of the Divine Presence alongside the holy and pure. It is traditionally accompanied by a charitable donation made in the name of the deceased, with the act of giving tzedakah understood as a merit offered on behalf of the soul.
