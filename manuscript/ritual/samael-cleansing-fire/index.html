🔥Samael Cleansing Fire

◇Form
Candle RitePouchCharm
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickInvocationChant/MantraElemental
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• 9 small red candles (Samael's color), set in a circle • 9 different spices or fiery herbs (cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, curry, saffron, chili, basil, tarragon, or parsley - all Samael herbs) • 1 bowl • 1 small red bag or purse
◇Directions
1. Light the candles clockwise, saying: "Samael, archangel of cleansing fire, I do to greater things aspire [name them]. All I ask is you move this block, that/who bars every door on which I knock [name person or situation]." Add the herbs one by one to the bowl, mixing vigorously nine times clockwise and saying nine times: "Samael, archangel of cleansing fire, grant I ask, this one desire. For I am equal to the task, so please, mighty Samael, kick his/her/their/its ass." Put the herbs in the bag and pass it nine times, clockwise in both directions, around the outside of the candles, repeating the spell words nine times, faster and faster. 2. On the final ass, blow out the candles fast. 3. Carry the bag whenever encountering the problematic person or organization.
◇Description
A Samael, archangel of cleansing fire, ritual for kicking the ass of an immovable person or organization, using nine red candles and nine spices mixed and carried in a red bag.
