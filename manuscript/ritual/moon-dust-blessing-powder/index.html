🌙Moon Dust Blessing Powder

◇Form
Powder
◇Method
VisualizationLunar
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 tsp dried jasmine 1 tsp ground cinnamon 1 tsp sandalwood powder
◇Directions
1. Using your mortar and pestle, grind the dried jasmine into a fine texture. 2. Add the cinnamon and sandalwood powder, stirring clockwise to blend. 3. With your finger, draw a lunar symbol into the surface of the powder, such as the crescent moon or the Triple Goddess symbol. 4. Hold your hands above the powder and visualize the moon's light streaming down, illuminating the blend and opening new pathways and blessings before you. 5. On the morning after the new moon (or on a Monday or the first day of the month), place a pinch of the powder in your palm. 6. Open your front door, face it, and with your intention firmly in mind, blow the powder onto or toward the door, drawing blessings inward. 7. Store the remainder in a small vial or vessel for repeated use.
◇Description
A versatile lunar blessing powder blended from cinnamon, jasmine, and sandalwood, ground together with intention under the moon's influence. Traditionally blown through the front door at the new moon, on Mondays, or on the first of the month to draw in lunar blessings and abundance. The powder can also be used to dress candles, anoint written intentions and business cards, or be sprinkled in ritual shapes on the altar to amplify spellwork.
