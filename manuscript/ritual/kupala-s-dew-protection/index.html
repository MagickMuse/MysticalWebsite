📅Summer Solstice

Kupala, the Slavic spirit of water, botanicals, magick and fertility, is honored at the Summer Solstice, when she is at the height of her power and Earth's moisture is suffused with her protective blessings. Rolling in the morning dew lets your body and clothes absorb them.

Quantities

Morning dew on the Summer Solstice

Directions

  1. If possible, spend the night before the Summer Solstice reveling in Kupala's honor, which makes the spell most powerful.
  2. On Summer Solstice morning, roll in the dew so your clothes and body are suffused with its moisture.
  3. Receive Kupala's blessings of protection.

See also Saint John's Eve Meadow Dew Skin Healing Spells for a related Midsummer dew rite for healing.
