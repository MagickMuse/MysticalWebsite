♂️Mars Victory for Favorite Team

A Mars ritual to bring victory to a favorite team facing a hard fight, using dried legumes in a jar shaken nine times with stamping circuits to invoke Mars's strength and steel.

Quantities

  • Enough dried peas, beans, or lentils to half-fill a storage jar
  • 1 stainless-steel storage jar with tight-fitting lid
  • The club colors or a team picture
  • 1 small brush

Directions

  1. Place the dried legumes in the jar, securing the lid firmly.
  2. Shake it nine times over the picture/colors, saying nine times: "Mars bring steel, your strength reveal. Though odds are against winning, let victory be real and sure, right from the beginning." Keep shaking and begin stamping as you move clockwise around the picture/team colors, making nine clockwise stamping circuits and chanting the words nine more times.
  3. Take off the lid, scattering legumes over the picture/colors and shouting: "Win, win, win, it is no sin to want the best. Mars make us strong. The team's skill does the rest." Sweep up the legumes with a small brush and pour them back in the jar, collecting any spilled elsewhere.
  4. Go outdoors and scatter the jar's contents far and wide, saying: "We shall win. Mars begin."
