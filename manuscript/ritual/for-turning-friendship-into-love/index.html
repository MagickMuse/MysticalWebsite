💛For Turning Friendship into Love

A crescent to full moon ritual winding long yellow ribbon around photo with three knots, kept under pillow three days.

Quantities

  • 1 very long yellow ribbon
  • 1 picture of you and friend together

Directions

  1. Begin to wind the ribbon around the image, saying, "Look at me through the eyes of love. Let passion flare, may closeness grow. The deep bonds are already there, but true love I would know." Secure the ribbon with three knots, saying, "Three times the link between us grows. We are good friends, but true love may you show." Keep the picture and ribbon under your pillow for three days.
  2. On day 4, undo the ribbon, saying, "Three times the link between us free; let love between us grow willingly. Look at me through the eyes of love." Cut a small piece of the ribbon off and wear it in a knot next to your skin.
  3. Keep the rest in a drawer with the picture, wrapped in something soft belonging to you.
  4. Perform any day between the crescent moon and the full moon.
