🍋Lemon Curse

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAnointing
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 lemon 1 black candle 9 nails Cursing Oil 1 photo of the target 1 black bowl 1 ritual blade
◇Directions
1. Light the black candle and cut a slit into the lemon with the ritual blade. 2. Place the photo of the target into the slit. 3. Visualize anger toward the target and pierce the lemon with one nail. 4. One by one, insert all nine nails into the lemon while projecting energy into it. 5. Place the lemon in the black bowl and fill the bowl halfway with Cursing Oil. 6. Leave the bowl on the altar and allow the lemon to rot. 7. As the lemon rots, so does the luck and life of the target. 8. Once the lemon has rotted completely, bury it in a shallow grave.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A sympathetic decay curse. A lemon is slit open, stuffed with the target's photo, and pierced with nine nails while channeling anger. The lemon is submerged in Cursing Oil and left to rot on the altar, mirroring the decay of the target's fortune and vitality.
