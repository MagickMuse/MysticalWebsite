🌸Blessing of the Sex Anointing Oil
Symbols2
23

The fourth component of the Five-Fold Blessing for Beauty, this anointing rite consecrates sexuality as a sacred expression of the witch's path. Drawing on the Wiccan principle that all acts of love and pleasure are acts of devotion, this blessing honors the body and its desires as a reflection of nature's own life-giving duality. The anointing oil is prepared to please the practitioner's own senses first, with no aim toward attraction. The working yields two containers: one for personal use and one to offer a consort or to reserve for charging candles and ritual tools.

Quantities

  • Feminine Blend (optional):
  • 15 drops oak moss (base note)
  • 10 drops gardenia (middle note)
  • 3 drops lilac (top note)
    Masculine Blend (optional):
  • 10 drops patchouli (base note)
  • 10 drops sandalwood (middle note)
  • 3 drops lavender (top note)
    Base Recipe:
  • 1 ounce grated beeswax
  • 1 ounce jojoba oil
  • 3 drops essential oil blend of choice (per container)
  • 2 metal 1-ounce containers
  • Double boiler
  • Flat toothpicks

Directions

  1. Prepare the anointing oil using the linked recipe.
  2. When the oil has set and cooled, take one container for yourself.
  3. Apply the oil to your skin as an act of deliberate self-blessing.
  4. Choose a scent that you find genuinely pleasurable rather than one oriented toward attracting others.
  5. As you anoint, hold the intention of honoring your own sexuality and body as inherently sacred.
  6. The scent and the warmth of the oil are themselves the working.
  7. Offer the second container to a consort as a gesture of recognition and appreciation.
  8. If working alone, keep the second container for anointing candles, ritual tools, or future self-blessings.
