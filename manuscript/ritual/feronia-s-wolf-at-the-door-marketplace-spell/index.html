📅November 15

Feronia, ancient Italian deity of freedom and the marketplace, is invoked for a prosperous year by burning basil and rue in a brazier and posting an image of her sacred animal, the wolf, at the door. Most effective on her festival date of November 15th.

Quantities

  • Basil
  • Rue
  • Brazier or fire-safe vessel
  • Image of a wolf

Directions

  1. Set up a brazier and burn basil and rue.
  2. Post an image of a wolf at the door.
  3. Invoke Feronia's blessings for a good year.
  4. The most effective time coincides with her festival on November 15th.
