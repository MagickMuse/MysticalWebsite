🍾Seven Blessings Witch’s Bottle

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- a bottle with a tight-fitting cork or cap - a pentacle or pentagram drawn on a piece of paper - a white candle - white sugar - white salt - herbs for each of seven blessings (see list below; rosemary may be substituted for any or all) - a spoon or funnel
◇Directions
1. Gather all spell materials. Get into a magickal mood, and create sacred space. Focus on the blessings you want to attract to the home and its residents. Fill the bottle one-third full with salt. Use a spoon or funnel to avoid spills. Tap the bottle gently to settle the salt. Add at least one spoonful of thoroughly dried herbs for each of the following blessings: • Health • Happiness • Love • Prosperity • Peace • Protection • Good Fortune Add several spoonfuls of each herb if using a large bottle. Add herbs carefully to form distinct layers. Reduce herb size if needed to fit through the bottle neck. 2. Visualize the magickal power building with each herb you add. Use what herbs are available to you and build a bottle that's both beautiful and potent. Pour sugar over the herbs until the bottle is filled. Tap gently to help the sugar settle and add more if needed. Close the bottle tightly and seal with a cork or cap. Place the bottle in the center of your drawn pentacle. Position the candle at the top of the pentacle. 3. The Spell Raise power, and light the candle. Wrap your hands around the bottle and charge it with your intent. Clearly visualize the blessings being attracted. Pick up the candle and drip wax over the top of the bottle as you say: With health and happiness, enchanted be. With love and prosperity, enchanted be. With peace and protection, enchanted be. With good fortune, enchanted be. Whether placed in house or yard, Make life better, and less hard. Continue dripping wax until the candle is spent. Allow it to create a thick seal over the top and drip down the sides. Once hardened, bury the bottle in your yard or hide it inside the home, such as behind a closet door or in a potted plant near your entrance. While you bury or hide the bottle, repeat: Make life better, and less hard. Ground the power you've raised. Clean up your space. 4. Afterward To renew the bottle's magick, focus on it and say: "Blessed be this home with health, happiness, love, prosperity, peace, protection, and good fortune." Say aloud if alone or mentally if others are present. If you move: • A buried bottle can be left to bless the next inhabitants, or it may be taken with you. • A bottle not buried may be taken or emptied and discarded. • Mixing the layers won't ruin its power, but you should recast the spell when reinstalling it in a new home. 5. Variations You may make other bottles using this same technique to attract specific blessings. Use herbs or charms that correspond to the desired intention.
◇Description
This is a bottle spell that attracts positive energies to the home. You can make one for yourself or give others as gifts.
