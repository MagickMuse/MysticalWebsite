🌑Eclipsed Moon Reflection

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
BreathworkDivinationMeditationShadow WorkVisualization
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- Black moonstone (optional)
◇Directions
1. Prepare for ritual and meditation as desired. 2. Hold your black moonstone, a powerful stone for new beginnings and releasing toxic emotions, and let it anchor you as you rediscover your own power. 3. Breathe deeply into your body and close your eyes. 4. After a few calming breaths, visualize the earth, moon, and sun moving through the cosmic backdrop of the stars. 5. The moon orbits the earth as the earth orbits the sun, tracing the passage of time, all the past that has carried you to this present moment. 6. Sunlight strikes the earth and casts a shadow into the cosmos, much like your own shadow stretches out behind you when you walk in daylight. 7. Visualize the moon passing into the earth's shadow and turning red. 8. The moon represents your inner world of feeling, your intuition, emotions, and the residue of past experiences, and here it is colored by the shadow of the very planet you inhabit in this lifetime. 9. As it turns red, the color of life force, passion, and blood, it illuminates your own shadows: your primal self, your wants and needs, the patterns you carry, and the bonds that are ready to be released. 10. You may feel a flare of energy at your root chakra, at the base of your spine; this energy center is often activated by eclipse energy. 11. Tune in to what arises. 12. Notice how this eclipse makes you feel and what it reveals about what needs healing and attention right now. 13. Acknowledging these darker aspects of the self opens the door to uncovering old wounds and beginning to heal from them. 14. Reflect in your grimoire or journal, or explore with tarot or oracle cards, on what is being revealed at this time and why; what cycle or pattern is ending, or what it is finally time to end; what is ready to be released and let go of, and what has been holding you back; what healing might come from releasing this; and whether you sense any guidance from the spiritual world. 15. Invite insight from your guides, whether in this present moment or through symbols, messages, and synchronicities over the coming days. 16. With your reflections in mind and your illumination gathered, visualize the moon passing out of the earth's shadow and returning to its luminous, bright white presence in the cosmos. 17. You, too, will move through this time and emerge shining brighter, for it or despite it.
◇Description
A shadow work meditation that uses lunar eclipse energy to illuminate hidden emotions, past patterns, and shadow-self material. Guides the practitioner through breath, visualization, and reflective journaling or divination to uncover what needs healing and release.
