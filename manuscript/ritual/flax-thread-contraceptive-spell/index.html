🧵Flax Thread Contraceptive Spell

◇Directions
1. Soak a length of flax thread in menstrual blood. 2. Tie ten knots in the thread. 3. Wear it continuously for nine days and nine nights without removing it. 4. When the nine days are complete, bury the thread in a corner and say aloud: "I bury you for [state the length of time you wish to remain free of pregnancy]."
◇Description
A contraceptive working drawing on the sacred connection between flax, spinning, and the goddesses who hold dominion over the thread of life. Flax thread soaked in menstrual blood is knotted ten times and worn continuously for nine days and nights before being buried with a spoken duration. Originally the practitioner would have spun the thread themselves.

Flax

Flax is sacred to Frigg, to Hulda (Holle), and to Saint Paraskeva - the Russian spirit who wears the mask of a Christian saint while remaining one of the old Pagan spinning goddesses beneath. All three are associated with the distaff arts: spinning, weaving, and the governance of what is made from thread.

In older traditions, the practitioner would have spun the flax thread herself, placing her own physical labor and intention into the fiber from the very beginning. The spinning goddesses spin the thread of life; a woman who spins her own contraceptive thread enters into the same mythic gesture.

Ten knots lock the intention in multiple points along the thread's length. Nine days is a number of completion in European folk tradition - three times three, the deepest level of the pattern.

Burying the thread at the close of the working places the intention in the earth, held there until the stated time has passed. The spoken declaration is the working's seal: language fixes the duration.
