🌕Blue Moon Bay Leaf Wishing Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellIncense Blend
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickManifestationLunarDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
A bay leaf A marker Optional: oil Ground nutmeg, cinnamon, and ginger A fireproof dish
◇Directions
1. With a marker, write your intention clearly on the bay leaf. 2. If using oil, rub it over the bay leaf with your intention in mind, channeling lunar energy into it. 3. In a bowl or dish, mix the ground nutmeg, cinnamon, and ginger together in a clockwise motion, drawing your intentions toward you as you stir. 4. Press and massage the spice blend onto the bay leaf, sealing it with additional power. 5. Hold the bay leaf above a fireproof dish and light its tip. 6. The flame will likely sputter out quickly, leaving glowing embers and rising smoke; that smoke is your prayer ascending to the moon. 7. Relight as needed, as the blue moon's energy of second chances welcomes it. 8. Alternatively, keep the charged bay leaf in your wallet through a waxing phase for money and luck magick. 9. Before burning, you may also read the way the leaf crackles and curls as a divination of how your intention will unfold.
◇Description
A spiced bay leaf wishing spell designed to harness the rare, amplified energy of a blue or black moon. Write your intention on a bay leaf, anoint it with oil, coat it in a clockwise-mixed blend of nutmeg, cinnamon, and ginger, then burn it above a fireproof dish. The rising smoke carries your wish to the moon. The spell also offers a divination element, observe how the leaf burns and crackles, or skip the burning entirely and carry the infused leaf in your wallet as a money magick charm.
