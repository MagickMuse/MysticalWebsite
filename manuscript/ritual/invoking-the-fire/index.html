🔥Invoking the Fire

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PrayerDevotional Work
◇Category
Prayer
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
A candle or lamp
◇Directions
1. At the moment between unlit and lit, pause. 2. Speak an invocation as you strike your light. 3. The Celtic triadic form: 4. "Brigid of the spark, / Brigid of the flame, / Brigid of the embers, / I call you in. // Brigid of inspiration, / Brigid of shielding, / Brigid of comfort, / I call you in. // Be in my flame, O Brigid of courage. / Be in my flame, O Brigid of grace. / Be in my flame, O Brigid of beauty. / This night and every night, this day and every day." 5. Or more simply: "From the fire of Kildare to my altar fire, / the flame is eternal, the flame is one." 6. End with a closing word of your choosing: "Blessed be," the Irish áiméan (pronounced awe-mayn), or any phrase that signals the conclusion of your devotions with respect and warmth. 7. Develop your own invocation over time; Brigid will inspire the words.
◇Description
A spoken invocation to bless the lighting of a flame to Brigid, using rhythmic triadic prayer to acknowledge her presence in the fire and dedicate the moment to her.
