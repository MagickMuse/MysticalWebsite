🌙Maiden, Mother, Crone Lunar Blessing

A standing blessing ceremony performed with a cup of water (or moon drink) and a selenite wand to attune to all phases of the moon and the Triple Goddess. Raise the wand in an arc like the moon crossing the sky while reciting an invocation of the Maiden, Mother, and Crone. Circle the wand around the cup three times, then sip as a act of communion with the moon's power over water. Perform before or after spellwork to bless and empower any intention.

Quantities

  • Water or a corresponding moon drink
  • Cup
  • Selenite wand
    Note: Selenite is water soluble, do not dip the wand into the drink.

Directions

  1. Pour your chosen drink into the cup.
  2. Take a moment to watch it catch the light of the moon or a nearby candle, and let the sight anchor you in the moon's relationship to all liquid, tides, blood, rain, the body.
  3. Hold the cup in your left hand at chest height.
  4. Hold the selenite wand in your right.
  5. Bring your intention to mind.
  6. This may be something you are actively working to manifest in this lunar cycle, or simply a desire to connect with the energy of the lunar goddesses and the turning wheel of the moon.
  7. Slowly raise the selenite wand in a wide arc overhead, as if tracing the moon's path across the night sky.
  8. As you move it, speak aloud, pausing between each verse:
  9. "Maiden, Mother, Crone,
    At the new moon, may my potential be sown.
    Maiden, Mother, Crone,
    And with the waxing moon, its potential grow.
    Maiden, Mother, Crone,
    Full moon, grant me my boons, my power I shall know.
    Maiden, Mother, Crone,
    Waning moon, all matters I know, truth I be shown.
    Maiden, Mother, Crone,
    At the dark moon, unwanted matters disowned."
  10. Hold the wand over the cup and move it in a slow, deliberate circle around the outside of the cup three times.
  11. Keep the selenite clear of the liquid, selenite dissolves in water and would make the drink unsafe.
  12. Set the wand down.
  13. Lift the cup and sip slowly, feeling the moon's energy pass through you, its pull on the tides, on the body, on the unseen currents of your life.
  14. Let it bless your intention from the inside out.
