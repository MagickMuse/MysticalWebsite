Simple Vanishing Spell

◇Type
Ritual
◇Directions
1. write fears, insecurities, all negative reflection 2. fold, fold, fold (how small it is!) 3. tear into pieces 4. place into glass 5. pour water, feeling love overflowing 6. wait an hour 7. ink floats, paper remains, so wash it away (and let the current diffuse anything held in tense ways) (optional) script we write our woes, our scars to wear, we bleed ourselves, so others won't bear. we scratch this pen each line a tear letting fear reside somewhere but here. we flood us in our love drowning out anything but us no more scars to share only water to hold carrying every tear formed across infinite fractal shores

a Metaphorical bloodletting/release ritual
