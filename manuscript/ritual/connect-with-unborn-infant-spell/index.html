Imbolc

A spell to connect with unborn infant once baby is kicking, using special music and rose quartz crystal massage with soft blanket to send love as life begins.

Quantities

  • A special piece of music you love
  • A rose quartz, pink mangano calcite, or small crystal angel
  • A soft blanket

Directions

  1. Sit quietly, with your partner if you wish, and put on the music.
  2. Place your hands (and your partner's) over the womb and say softly: "Child of mine/ours, safe inside, we are as one safe within the womb, and so I/we send you love as life begins." Place the crystal over your womb and gently massage the womb (or ask your partner to), saying the words again.
  3. Wrap yourself in the blanket and repeat the words, adding any special blessings.
  4. When the music finishes, keep making contact with your hand(s) and talking softly to the baby within.
  5. When the baby is born, the music, crystal massage, and blanket will prove soothing to the little one.
  6. Perform once the baby is kicking.
