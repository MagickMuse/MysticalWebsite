🌙Night Blooming Living Altar

Moonflower

A nocturnal altar craft for witches who come alive after dark. This living altar uses a night-blooming vine trained around a pentacle armature to create a sacred, protective space that glows only by moonlight. Moonflower, evening primrose, night phlox, evening stock, and angel's trumpet all open at night, creating a garden that belongs entirely to the moon, this craft turns that garden into a living pentacle in your window: a growing, blooming altar that doubles as a protective charm.

Quantities

About 18 feet of jute twine

One small plastic hula hoop with a diameter of approximately 1 yard (3 feet)

One suction cup with hook

One window box or planter

Moonflower seeds or seedlings (Ipomoea alba)

Directions

  1. Build the pentacle armature: tie the twine to the topmost centered point on the hula hoop, run it diagonally down to between 7 and 8 o'clock, loop it around the hoop, and pull the long end through the loop, pulling tightly. Continue: run the twine across to the 3 o'clock position and repeat the loop-and-tighten process, then cross to 9 o'clock, then down to between 4 and 5 o'clock, finishing back at the top. The result is a giant pentacle inscribed within the hoop.
  2. Hang the armature: using a suction cup with hook, hang the pentagram in your window above the window box, positioned so the moonflower vine can reach it naturally as it grows upward from the planter.
  3. Plant and tend: guide the vine gently toward the armature as it grows. It will seek the structure on its own. By summer evenings, the ghostly white blooms will open before your eyes as darkness falls, remaining open through the night and closing again by morning.
  4. Activate the protective charm by reciting the warding verse, especially during times when you feel unsafe or wish to strengthen the protection of your home. The charm may be spoken at planting, at each new moon, or whenever you feel the need to reinforce its power.

The Protective Charm

May this pentagram protect
May this dwelling be blessed
That all who dwell and enter here
Shall do so in the absence of fear.

If you choose to invert the pentacle, its warding nature is amplified.

Magickal Notes

  • Moonflower (Ipomoea alba) blooms only at night; its white flowers correspond to lunar energy, the goddess in her nocturnal aspect, and liminal thresholds between worlds.
  • Evening primrose is an excellent companion planting for those who work at dusk, releasing fragrance during the golden hour between day and night.
  • This altar belongs to the dark half of the wheel: it is most potent during the waning and new moon, at Samhain and the winter months, and during any working connected to shadow, mystery, or the underworld.
  • The living nature of the altar means it grows and changes with you. Tend it with intention; what you cultivate in your window mirrors what you cultivate within.
