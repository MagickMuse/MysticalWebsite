A hex powder blend of six botanicals, asafetida, black pepper, patchouli, peppermint, valerian, and vetiver, ground together and combined with graveyard dirt. Sprinkled as desired.

Quantities

Asafetida
Black pepper
Patchouli
Peppermint
Valerian
Vetiver
Graveyard dirt

Directions

  1. Grind and powder the following: asafetida, black pepper, patchouli, peppermint, valerian, and vetiver.
  2. Blend all the botanicals together and add graveyard dirt.
  3. Sprinkle as desired.
