🎵Singing Bowl Cleansing Method

◇Form
Altar WorkingCircle Rite
◇Method
CleansingEnergy WorkMeditation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
A singing bowl A small cushion A mallet Optional: water
◇Directions
1. Place a small cushion beneath the bowl. 2. Walk with the bowl, directing the sound as needed. 3. For heavy-duty cleansing, place the bowl on its cushion on a table in the center of the room. 4. Strike it with the mallet (typically three times for maximum effect), letting the sound reverberate and radiate. 5. Experiment with tapping, rubbing, or striking with fingernails vs. mallet, and try adding varying amounts of water to alter the sound.
◇Description
Himalayan singing bowls produce harmonies used to wash and balance space with yin and yang energies. Particularly beneficial before ritual, they soothe participants and harmonize group energy. Traditionally crafted from seven metals (gold, silver, copper, iron, tin, lead, zinc), each corresponding to one of the seven visible planets. Singing bowls improve with age and develop a rapport with their owner. Store in soft heavy fabric like velvet when not in use.
