◇Directions
1. Activate the bag to coordinate with your menstrual period. 2. If you do not menstruate, coordinate with the Full Moon. 3. Add roots to represent the couple: a male root such as Adam and Eve root, High John the Conqueror root, or Sampson snake root, and a female root such as orrisroot or angelica root. 4. Light red candles. 5. Pass the bag through incense smoke. 6. Add assorted intimacies as they become available: bits of hair, nail clippings, and other personal items. 7. Add bits of silver and cowrie shell charms. 8. Continue keeping small magickal or personal items in the bag, which also serves to empower whatever it holds. 9. The target of any ligature inside must never look within the bag. 10. Doing so restores his sexual nature but ends the relationship.
◇Description
A women's conjure pouch from Memphis-area Hoodoo, worn at the waist to hold protective charms, control amulets, and cash money, and activated at the menstrual period or Full Moon. ⚠️ Consent note: This working influences another person's free will. Weigh that choice with care and take full responsibility for its effects. ⚠️ Nation Sack Women's Conjure Bag is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.
