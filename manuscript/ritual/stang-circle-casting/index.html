🕯️Stang Circle Casting

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
Directional MagickElementalInvocationVisualization
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 1 stang (forked staff) - Incense (for Air) - Candle (for Fire) - Bowl of water (for Water) - Bowl of salt or stones (for Earth)
◇Directions
1. Plant your stang at the center of your working space or carry it with you. 2. Ground and center, feeling your connection to the earth through the stang's base. 3. Begin in the East. 4. Hold the stang before you and walk clockwise around the perimeter, visualizing a circle of power forming where the stang's fork points. 5. Return to East and seal the circle. 6. Face East and raise the stang. 7. Visualize a great eagle soaring on currents of golden wind, bringing clarity and inspiration. 8. Say: 9. Hail, Guardians of the East, Powers of Air! Great Eagle who soars upon the winds of dawn, Bring us clarity, wisdom, and new beginnings. Hail and welcome! 10. Face South. 11. Visualize a magnificent lion wreathed in flames, radiating courage and transformation. 12. Say: 13. Hail, Guardians of the South, Powers of Fire! Great Lion who commands the sacred flames, Bring us passion, courage, and will. Hail and welcome! 14. Face West. 15. Visualize a shimmering dolphin leaping through deep blue waters, bringing intuition and emotional depth. 16. Say: 17. Hail, Guardians of the West, Powers of Water! Great Dolphin who dances in the twilight waves, Bring us intuition, healing, and love. Hail and welcome! 18. Face North. 19. Visualize a powerful bull standing upon rich dark earth, embodying strength and stability. 20. Say: 21. Hail, Guardians of the North, Powers of Earth! Great Bull who stands upon the midnight soil, Bring us strength, abundance, and grounding. Hail and welcome! 22. Release quarters in reverse (North, West, South, East), thanking each guardian and bidding farewell. 23. Walk counterclockwise with the stang, drawing the energy back. 24. Ground any excess energy through the stang into the earth.
◇Description
A full ceremonial circle casting using a forked staff (stang) with elaborate elemental visualizations: the bull (Earth/North), eagle (Air/East), lion (Fire/South), and dolphin (Water/West).
