Viburnum

A protective burial ritual using nine viburnum twigs buried in a walkway to bind evil forces and prevent harmful spirits from crossing the threshold.

Quantities

  • 9 viburnum twigs (devil's shoestring/hobble bush)
  • Small digging tool

Directions

  1. Gather nine twigs of viburnum (devil's shoestring or hobble bush).
  2. At your home's main walkway or entrance path, dig nine small holes in the ground where people walk.
  3. As you bury each twig, speak the charm: "Devil's shoestring, bind what's wrong, trip up evil, make protection strong. Nine by nine, the trap is set, no harmful spirit crosses yet." Cover each twig with soil.
  4. The buried twigs create an invisible barrier that trips up and binds malevolent forces attempting to approach your home.
