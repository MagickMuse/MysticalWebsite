🕯️Wedding Feast

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
InvocationChant/MantraElementalEnergy Work
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Dirt and/or fresh plants - Goblet - Red wine or water - Optional: soft pillows, blankets, music, two chairs
◇Directions
1. Purpose: Gain magickal protection for the earth, its crops, and wild vegetation, and touch the deeper mysteries of Lughnasadh. Setting: Day or night, outdoors if possible in a safe, private, comfortable space. 2. Pre‑ritual: Reflect on the interweaving of sun, earth, wind, and rain. 3. Center and relax with a walk, music, or ritual bath. 4. Roles: Land (earth/crops/vegetation) and Sky (sun/air/protector). 5. For groups, designate a lead for each. 6. Garb may be nude or ritual wear in earth/sky tones. 7. Create the space: Arrange pillows/blankets or find a soft natural spot. 8. Optional incense and music. 9. Cast a circle if desired. 10. Invocation of Land: Land lies down, palms to ground, feeling earth's current rise and bind to the body. 11. Invite the Great Mother of the Earth to indwell. 12. Others rub a little dirt or plants on Land to anchor the indwelling. 13. Invocation of Sky: Sky stands open, arms in a V, lifts root energy to crown and connects upward to sun and sky. 14. Invite the Great Father of the Sky. 15. Others visualize clouds and gentle winds enveloping Sky. 16. The joining: Land offers Sky a sip from the goblet ("Welcome, Sky."). 17. Sky returns ("Thank you, Land, and welcome."). 18. Exchange vows of mutual support. 19. Embrace and allow affectionate touching by consent, raising the current together. 20. Seal: "Land is joined to Sky, and Sky is joined to Land. We have forged a pact of love and power; our oath will not be broken!" 21. Protection chant (walk clockwise): Protected are the crops! Protected is the land! Protected are the trees, the roots, the leaves! Protected are the wild growing things! Protected are the tender shoots! Protected are the blossoming fruits! Protected are the ripening gourds, protected are the greens! Protected is the bountiful harvest! Protected is the Mother of Earth! Protected are the children of the sky and of the land! As we will it, then so will it be! A pact is forged between Land, Sky, and We! 22. Close: Thank and dismiss Land and Sky and any invited powers. 23. Ground with food/drink and rest. 24. Release any circle.
◇Description
A Lughnasadh “wedding feast” rite uniting Land and Sky to protect the earth, crops, and wild vegetation, and to touch the deeper seasonal mysteries.
