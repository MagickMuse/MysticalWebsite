📅New Year's Calendar Good Year

◇Form
Candle RiteWriting Spell
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraBanishingManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- A single calendar page from the current year with large squares marking the days - A red pen - A large, dark-colored candle - A ball of red yarn - A pot of soil or sand - A white candle
◇Directions
1. If you're alone, write in any square of the calendar page a disaster you would rather forget. 2. If family or friends are joining in, they can choose a square as well, and then share all the spell words and actions. 3. Light the dark candle and tie up the calendar page using nine knots. 4. Tear a corner off the calendar page, burning it in the dark candle flame. 5. Drop the burned paper into the pot, saying nine times: "Old year turn, old year burn, bad luck, do not return." Rip up the calendar page, throwing it unburned in the pot and put the pot outside the door before midnight. 6. At two minutes to midnight, light the white candle from the dark one. 7. On the first stroke of midnight, blow out the black candle and shout: "Come in New Year. You are welcome." Perform New Year's Eve, fifteen minutes before midnight.
◇Description
A New Year's Eve fifteen minutes before midnight spell for New Year's Eve calendar ritual to bring good year ahead, tying calendar page with nine knots and burning then lighting white candle at midnight for old year to burn and new to be welcome.
