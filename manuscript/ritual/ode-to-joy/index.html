This spell helps you reconnect with true joy. A full-body, uplifting sensation that transcends ordinary happiness. It uses music, candlelight, and incense to help you re-experience joy, capture its feeling, and keep it close for future use.

Quantities

  • an orange candle
  • amber or jasmine incense
  • a recording of Beethoven's Ninth Symphony
    (or substitute with any joyful music that resonates with you)

Directions

  1. • Get into a magickal mood. Burn the candle while listening to the music.
  2. Create sacred space. Raise power. Meditate on your past joys and sorrows.
  3. Recall your most joyful memories in vivid detail and how they felt in your body.
  4. Visualize that feeling as a physical image, crystal, ray of light, etc. Hold on to that image and feeling.
  5. When the "Ode to Joy" section begins, light the incense from the candle.
  6. Visualize the smoke blending with your feeling of joy. Let it surround and fuse with you.
  7. Cry, dance, sing, shout, whatever helps you fully express the joy.
  8. As the music ends, visualize putting the feeling of joy into a safe place in your mind (e.g., crystal cave, secret garden).
  9. Once it is safeguarded, extinguish the candle and ground the energy you raised.
  10. Eat or drink something if you need help returning to ordinary consciousness.
  11. Afterward
    Repeat the spell as needed to reinforce the association. Eventually, the music, scent, and candlelight will become powerful joy-triggers you can use to recall and relive that feeling anytime.

What is Joy?

Joy is the state beyond happiness, an exhilarating lightness of being, a total body and mind sensation that takes you beyond your ordinary self. It may be hard to recall it sometimes, but we have all experienced joy.

This spell helps you to remember what it felt like to be transported by joy, to recapture that feeling, and to keep it close to you.

Why Beethoven's Ninth?

The "Ode to Joy" section of Beethoven's Ninth Symphony is one of the most transcendent pieces of music ever composed. Its soaring melodies and triumphant crescendos are designed to lift the human spirit.

If you do not care for this piece of music, or cannot obtain it, substitute another for it. Choose a song or other piece of music that is joyful to you. The key is that the music moves you.

The Visualization

Recall the most joyous moments of your life and exactly how you felt while experiencing them. Visualize that feeling as a crystal, a ray of light, or whatever image makes sense to you. Hold on to it tightly. Keep it next to your heart, and in your mind.

When the "Ode to Joy" begins, light the incense from the candle. Allow yourself to be flooded with joy as you listen to the soaring music. Visualize the music and the incense smoke undulating around your feeling of joy, entwining with it, and fusing with it.

Express Your Joy

Be one with that feeling. If you are moved to cry, dance, sing, shout, or do something else, go ahead and do it. Joy is meant to be expressed.

Safeguarding the Feeling

Enjoy the music until it ends. Visualize taking the feeling of joy and putting it in a safe place, such as a crystal cave or a secret garden, from which you can retrieve it any time you like. When the joy has been safeguarded, extinguish the candle, and ground the power that you raised.

You should feel great, jazzed up, and energized after casting this spell. Have something to eat or drink to help ground yourself if you have trouble returning to ordinary consciousness.

Building the Trigger

You may need to repeat the spell many times to achieve this, but at some point your association of the sound of that piece of music, the smell of that incense, and the sight of that color candle burning will become one with your memory of joy.

When this happens, any one of them will become a trigger that enables you to feel your joy. The orange candle, the amber or jasmine scent, the opening notes of the symphony: these become keys that unlock the door to your safeguarded joy whenever you need it.
