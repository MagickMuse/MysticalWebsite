Beltane

Call upon the Fae for luck, mischief, and help breaking through stagnation. The Fae are most present at Samhain and Beltane.

Quantities

  • Optional offering: Cream, honey, or shiny objects
  • No other materials required

Directions

  1. Speak this prayer outdoors near trees, water, or in a garden.
  2. The Fae appreciate offerings of cream, honey, or shiny objects left nearby:
  3. "People of the hills, people of the mounds,
    Faeries of the land, spirits of the green,
    I call to you at this time when the veil is thin.
    Beltane brings you forth, and I seek your aid.
    Bring me luck and good fortune,
    Bring me your mischief and your magick.
    Help me break through what holds me back,
    Change my fate with your wild power.
    Fae folk, tricksters and helpers,
    I honor you and ask your blessing.
    May your presence bring transformation,
    May your magick shift my destiny.
    So mote it be."
  4. Be respectful but not fearful.
  5. The Fae appreciate sincerity and boldness.
  6. Leave an offering and don't look back as you walk away.
