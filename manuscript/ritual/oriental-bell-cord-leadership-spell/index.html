🔔Oriental Bell Cord Leadership Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Chant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A string of silver- or gold-colored bells on a cord (Tibetan ones are ideal) - Red ribbon in five equal pieces, each long enough to tie into a bow
◇Directions
1. Starting at the top and working down to the bottom of the cord, tie five red bows at regular intervals, saying: "Notice me positively and see my leadership potential powerfully as I increase my profile and prospects of promotion." Ring each bell, repeating the chant. 2. Hang the bells where natural light shines. 3. When you have hung the bells, ring each one separately and recite the words twice for each ring. 4. Then ring the bells together, repeating the words once more. 5. Leave the bells hanging on the wall. 6. Ring them and chant each day before work. 7. Perform the first day of the week, month, or year.
◇Description
An Oriental success cord spell to raise your profile so people see you as a natural leader, tying five red ribbon bows on silver or gold bells and ringing daily.
