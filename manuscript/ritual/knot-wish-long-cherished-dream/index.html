🪢Knot Wish Long-Cherished Dream

A nine-week Thursday knot magick ritual adding one knot per week to manifest long-cherished dreams, then untying all nine on Friday.

Quantities

  • 1 red candle
  • 1 long blue cord

Directions

  1. Light the candle, saying, "This is my long-cherished dream, [name dream], and week by week, what I seek, [dream], comes closer." Tie the first knot loosely and recite the whole chant each time you add a knot: "Knot one renew, my power in two, in courage be within knot three. The power is more; I make knot four. And so I strive within knot five. With fate not fixed, I tie knot six. My strength is leaven, I tie knot seven. I master fate, and so knot eight. The wish is mine within knot nine." Blow out the candle.
  2. Leave the candle and cord in place.
  3. Each Thursday, add a new knot, repeating the chant, and on week 9, let the candle burn.
  4. On Friday morning, undo the knots very fast, saying, "The wish is mine within knot nine. The power is free, so I decree and it shall be." Cast the untied cord into running water or leave it tied on a tree branch.
  5. Perform Thursday after dark for nine weeks.
