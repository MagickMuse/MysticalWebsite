🌹Red Witch Rose-Dressed Name Candle Love Spell

Scratch your beloved's name under the base of a red witch candle, dress with rose fragrance, and burn with a chant to draw them to you.

Quantities

A witch candle
Rose fragrance oil or perfume, for dressing

Directions

  1. Scratch your beloved's name beneath the witch's feet (the base of the candle).
  2. Dress the candle with the fragrance of roses.
  3. Burn the candle while chanting:
  4. "Red witch, red witch, bring my love to me!" or whatever best expresses your own situation.
