🕯️Winter House Cleansing and Blessing

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
CleansingSmoke MagickSalt MagickElementalProtection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
For cleansing: - Sage, sweetgrass, juniper, or cedar for smudging - OR salt water for aspersion - Bell or rattle (sleigh bells ideal) For protection (choose items from each element): - Earth: Crystals, small bowls of sea salt, ceramic or wooden trees, animal figurines (especially Bear) - Air: Incense, simmering spices, essential oils, white lights, paper snowflakes - Fire: Candles, fireplace wood, luminarias, warm beverages (tea, cocoa, or cider) - Water: Mirrors, water bowl or cauldron, smooth river rocks
◇Directions
1. Cleansing Process: 2. Smudge the entryways and corners using sage, sweetgrass, juniper, or cedar, OR asperge (sprinkle) with salt water. 3. Move deosil (counterclockwise) from one room to the next. 4. As you work, repeat this chant: May the winds inspire you, earth protect you, water heal you, and fire always warm the hearth. 5. As you move through the space, clap your hands to disperse old, stagnant energy, then use a bell or rattle to welcome in fresh, sacred energy. 6. Sleigh bells are perfect for this Midwinter cleansing. 7. Open windows to let in fresh air, even if for only a few minutes, this is great for cleansing, clarification, and health. 8. Protection by Element: 9. Once you've finished the cleansing, bring protective elements into your rooms or hang protective talismans over doors and windows: 10. Earth element (grounds and anchors): - Place crystals or small bowls of sea salt around the kitchen - Evoke crystalline ice and snow - Use small ceramic or wooden trees - Display small animal figurines, particularly Bear (who tunnels into earth during winter and emerges as light returns) 11. Air element (energizes and improves alertness and mental clarity): - Burn incense or simmer kettle of water and spices on kitchen stove - Diffuse essential oils - Hang small white lights everywhere - Cut paper snowflakes and let them dangle and spin from ceiling 12. Fire element (recalls the spark of life in the dead of winter): - Burn candles - Use fireplace if available - Set luminarias outside, lighting the path to your door - String lights inside and out, the more, the better! - Sip warm mugs of tea, cocoa, or hot cider to kindle internal fire during long evenings 13. Water element (calms, soothes, and heals): - Use mirrors to represent frozen pools and ice of winter - Set out small bowl or cauldron of water - Use smooth river rocks to adorn altars or tableaus 14. Variation: Use the above ideas to clean and prepare your home on New Year's Day, setting a fresh stage for the year to come.
◇Description
A winter holiday house cleansing ritual using smudging or salt water aspersion to clear stagnant energy and welcome fresh, sacred energy. Move deosil through each room, cleansing entryways and corners, then bring in protective elements representing all four elements. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
