The Wings of Isis Spell calls upon the great Egyptian goddess of magic to wrap you in her protective embrace. Isis is often depicted with wings outstretched, sheltering those she loves. This spell invokes that image, creating a shield of divine protection around you.

Who is Isis?

Isis is one of the most beloved goddesses of the ancient world, worshipped for thousands of years across multiple cultures. She is the goddess of magic, healing, protection, and motherhood. Her name means "throne," and she represents the power that supports and sustains. When Isis spreads her wings, nothing harmful can pass.

Choosing Your Candle

Select a pillar candle in colors sacred to Isis:

  • Blue: For her connection to the sky and the Nile
  • Gold: For her solar associations and divine royalty
  • White: For purity and magical power
  • Black: For her aspect as a goddess of mystery and the underworld

You may use a single color or a candle that combines several.

Preparing the Candle

Carve the ankh symbol into the candle. The ankh, the Egyptian key of life, is one of Isis's most sacred symbols. It represents eternal life and divine protection. Carve it large enough to be visible, drawing the loop at the top, the crossbar, and the vertical line below.

You may also carve Isis's name in hieroglyphics or simply write "ISIS" if you prefer.

Anointing with Flaxseed Oil

Flax was sacred in ancient Egypt, used for linen garments worn by priests and for wrapping the protected dead. Flaxseed oil carries this association with sacred preservation.

Pour a small amount of flaxseed oil into your palm. Anoint the candle from middle to ends, saying:

Oil of flax, ancient and blessed,

Prepare this candle for Isis's request.

Protection I seek beneath her wings,

Safety and shelter this magic brings.

The Working

Place the candle before you and light it. Gaze into the flame and call:

Isis, Great of Magic, hear my prayer.

Spread your wings and hold me there.

Beneath your feathers, safe from harm,

Wrapped in protection, held in your arm.

Close your eyes and visualize Isis standing before you, her great wings extending from her arms. See her step forward and wrap those wings around you completely. Feel the soft brush of feathers, the warmth of her presence, the absolute safety of her embrace.

Hold this visualization as long as you can. When you open your eyes, know that her wings remain around you, invisible but present.

Maintaining the Protection

Allow the candle to burn whenever you are home and can watch it safely. Each time you light it, repeat the incantation and renew the visualization. The protection strengthens with each lighting.

When the candle is fully burned, keep a small piece of the remaining wax as a token of Isis's continued blessing. Carry it with you or place it on your altar.
