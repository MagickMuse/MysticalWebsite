🧲Seven-Day Magnet Spell

A seven-day magnet spell to draw a lover closer, performed from Friday through Thursday. Uses magnets, red candle, oils, and a drawstring bag to create a powerful attraction charm.

Quantities

  • Two black magnets
  • One red candle
  • Patchouli oil or Dragon's Blood oil
  • One red drawstring bag

Directions

  1. Begin this spell on a Friday, the day ruled by Venus and sacred to love magick.
  2. Anoint your red candle with either patchouli or Dragon's Blood oil, focusing on your intention to draw love toward you.
  3. Light the candle and let it burn as you work.
  4. Hold the two black magnets in your hands, feeling their pull toward each other.
  5. Visualize this magnetic force representing the attraction between you and your desired lover.
  6. Anoint each magnet with the same oil you used on the candle.
  7. Place both magnets and any remaining oil into the red drawstring bag.
  8. As you do so, speak your intentions clearly, naming what you wish to attract.
  9. Each day for seven consecutive days, hold the bag in your hands and focus on your romantic desires.
  10. Feel the magnets inside pulling together, representing the drawing force of your spell.
  11. On the seventh day, the spell is complete.
  12. Carry the bag with you or keep it in a special place to maintain the attraction energy.
