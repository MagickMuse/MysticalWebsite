A popular spell-reversing body oil rubbed on under a Full Moon. Two versions: a powder-herb oil (lavender, lemon zest, peppermint, patchouli, vetiver, vervain in sea salt, mixed with almond/palm/jojoba oil) or an EO-based formula (lavender, lemongrass, patchouli, peppermint, vetiver EOs + salt in a carrier oil). Note: there is no essential oil of vervain, use dried powdered herb or flower essence remedy. The name's Haitian connection may relate to the origin of its signature vetiver.

Quantities

Powder version: Dried lavender, lemon zest, peppermint, patchouli, vetiver, vervain (all powdered) + sea salt + almond, palm, or jojoba oil

EO version: Lavender EO, lemongrass EO, patchouli EO, peppermint EO, vetiver EO + salt + carrier oil
Dried powdered vervain or vervain flower essence remedy (no EO of vervain exists)

Directions

  1. Powder version: Add sea salt to dried, powdered lavender, lemon zest, peppermint, patchouli, vetiver, and vervain.
  2. Mix the powder with almond, palm, or jojoba oil.
  3. EO version: Add essential oils of lavender, lemongrass, patchouli, peppermint, and vetiver to a carrier oil.
  4. Add some salt.
  5. Add dried powdered vervain or the flower essence remedy.
  6. Rub the final product onto your body while standing outside under a Full Moon.
