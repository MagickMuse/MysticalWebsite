🔥Sinistrari Incubus Banishing Incense Spell

A historical incense formula attributed to Ludovico Maria Sinistrari (1622–1701), a Franciscan friar and demonologist. The blend combines aphrodisiac and commanding ingredients to ward off an incubus. Note: Aristolochia species are toxic and not recommended for use.

Quantities

  • Sweet flag (Calamus)
  • Cubeb seeds
  • Roots from two different aristolochia species (toxic, not recommended)
  • Cardamom
  • Ginger
  • Long pepper
  • Pink cloves
  • Cinnamon
  • Cloves
  • Mace
  • Nutmeg
  • Frankincense resin
  • Benzoin
  • Aloeswood with its root
  • Sandalwood
  • Brandy (approx. 3.5 quarts / 4.5 liters mixed with water)
  • Water
  • A cauldron

Directions

  1. Place all botanical ingredients in a cauldron: calamus, cubeb seeds, aristolochia roots, cardamom, ginger, long pepper, pink cloves, cinnamon, cloves, mace, nutmeg, frankincense resin, benzoin, aloeswood with its root, and sandalwood.
  2. Add approximately three and a half quarts of brandy and water.
  3. Simmer the mixture over heat.
  4. The steam produced by the simmering brew repels the incubus.
