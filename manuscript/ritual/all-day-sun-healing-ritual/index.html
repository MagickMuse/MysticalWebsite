🌞All-Day Sun Healing Ritual

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Water MagickHealingBanishingSolar
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• 4 stone fruits (peaches or plums) in bowl, turning soft • 1 bowl of water left overnight outdoors
◇Directions
1. Sprinkle a little light-infused water over the first fruit, saying, "I reach to new life, the past is gone. Yet on this day sad memories come." Bury the first fruit, saying, "Rest in peace now." Repeat the spell words and actions at noon with the second fruit, and at sunset with the third. 2. At bedtime, as you bury the fourth fruit, say, "The fruit decays quite naturally, yet in spite of bad memories, good times remain. For the rest I let go what no more can be." Perform at sunrise (or when you wake up), noon, sunset, and midnight (or when you go to bed).
◇Description
A four-times-daily ritual at sunrise, noon, sunset, midnight sprinkling light-infused water on stone fruits and burying them.
