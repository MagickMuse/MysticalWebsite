🕯️Good Hunting Spell

◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/MantraIncense MagickInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 red candle - Patchouli incense - 1 star anise - Bergamot leaves - Artemisia leaves (mugwort) - Red carnation petals
◇Directions
1. Inscribe the red candle with the runes Raidho, Thurisaz, Algiz, and Othala, then light it. 2. Light the patchouli incense and speak: 3. Let this earthy scent attune me with Herne the Hunter, the bounty of the Earth, and the natural forces of life's cycles. 4. Drop the herbs into the candle one at a time. 5. First, star anise: 6. I call upon the spirits of hunter ancestors to aid me in my hunt tomorrow. 7. Bergamot leaves: 8. I call upon the power of bergamot to bring me success in my endeavors tomorrow. 9. Artemisia leaves: 10. I call upon the Lady of the Hunt to bring me success in my endeavors tomorrow. 11. Red carnation petals: 12. I honor the blood of the animal I seek; life unto life, I hunt what I eat. 13. The cycles we share flow ever eternal; Hunters and hunted, revered and fraternal.
◇Description
A spell performed during the hour of Mars on the day before a hunt. A red candle is inscribed with runes and lit alongside patchouli incense to attune with Herne the Hunter. Star anise, bergamot leaves, artemisia leaves, and red carnation petals are dropped one by one into the candle flame, each with a spoken invocation calling upon ancestral hunters, the power of bergamot, the Lady of the Hunt, and honoring the cycle of hunter and hunted.
