🤟Moroccan Crone Ceremony for Spirit Illness

A complex, elaborate Moroccan group healing ceremony for spirit-caused illnesses. Requires five women (one crone, three once-married women), flour bundles fumigated with benzoin, salt-free and salted breads, five varieties of fish, decorated offerings, dolls, and silver coins. The crone delivers an elaborate offering to a remote haunted place in silence.

Quantities

Flour (two handfuls donated by each of 3 married women)
White cotton handkerchief
Benzoin (white)
Barley (7 grains)
Five varieties of fish
Butter, onions, black peppercorns (no salt, for cooking)
Salt
Small flags: one black, one white, one red, one yellow
Three small dolls (man, woman, daughter)
Two small silver coins
Basket
Food offering container

Directions

  1. Each of the three married women donates two handfuls of flour.
  2. Blend all the flour together and place it in a white cotton handkerchief.
  3. Fumigate this medicine bundle by burning white-colored benzoin.
  4. Place it under the patient's head or pillow overnight.
  5. In the morning, the crone opens the handkerchief and removes three handfuls of flour to make a small salt-free loaf of bread.
  6. Use the remaining flour to make a salted, yeast-free loaf.
  7. Place seven grains of barley on the salt-free loaf.
  8. Bake both.
  9. Purchase five varieties of fish.
  10. Cook five pieces (one of each variety) in a brand new baking dish with butter, onions, and black peppercorns but no salt.
  11. Give a little of each fish and sauce to the patient to eat.
  12. Rub sauce on the patient's wrists, elbows, knees, and ankles.
  13. Place some under the bed and in each corner of the patient's room.
  14. Put the salt-free loaf in a basket with the remaining fish and sauce in a container.
  15. Decorate the container with four small flags (black, white, red, yellow) and add three small dolls (man, woman, daughter).
  16. Place a small silver coin inside and say aloud: "Forgive us if we have forgotten anything you like. Here's some money to buy whatever you want."
  17. The crone places a second silver coin in her mouth (kept there until she reaches home) and carries the basket to a remote, isolated, or 'haunted' place, with the seashore preferred.
  18. She must not speak or look behind her on the trip there or back.
  19. Leave everything inside the basket at the spot; the basket itself may be taken home.
  20. If animals eat the offering, this is an auspicious sign.
  21. On her return, the crone picks up seven stones from the road and throws them one at a time on the doorstep so those inside may let her in (she cannot knock or speak).
  22. She keeps the silver coin; it must not be given away.
  23. Meanwhile, prepare the remaining fish however you like, with salt.
  24. Serve this fish and the salted loaf to the household.
  25. If the patient is a child, only immediate family and other children may eat; if an adult, anyone may participate.
  26. Others who are unwell may also benefit from eating the salted meal.
