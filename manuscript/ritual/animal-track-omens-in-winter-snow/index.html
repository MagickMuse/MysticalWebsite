Winter divination practice interpreting animal tracks in fresh snow as messages and omens. Each species carries symbolic meaning that can guide decisions or reveal hidden truths about your path.

Quantities

  • Fresh snow
  • Quiet outdoor space (yard, park, woods)
  • Field guide for animal tracks (optional)
  • Journal and pen for recording
  • Warm clothing for outdoor observation

Directions

  1. Basic Practice:
  2. After fresh snowfall, venture outdoors to observe tracks.
  3. Identify the species (use field guides if needed).
  4. Note the direction of travel, gait pattern, and behavior indicated.
  5. Interpret based on traditional animal symbolism and your intuition.
  6. Record observations in your journal immediately while details are fresh.
    Common Track Interpretations:
    Rabbit: Quick thinking needed; fertility and abundance approaching; time to be fleet of foot in your endeavors.
    Deer: Gentleness and grace required; spiritual messages; new opportunities emerging softly.
    Fox: Cunning and cleverness called for; trust your instincts; something hidden will be revealed.
    Crow/Raven: Messages from the spirit world; transformation; magick at work in your life.
    Squirrel: Prepare and gather resources; focus on practical matters; busy times ahead.
    Cat (wild or domestic): Independence; mystery; something prowling at the edges of awareness.
    Dog/Wolf: Loyalty, protection, and community; pack dynamics; teacher approaching.
    Rodent (mouse, vole): Small details matter; don't overlook the mundane; hidden industry.
    Advanced Observations:
  7. Single track: Solo work or journey ahead
  8. Multiple tracks/crossing paths: Community, collaboration, or choice between paths
  9. Track leading to your door: Direct message or opportunity coming to you
  10. Track circling: Situation requires patience or reconsideration
  11. Tracks disappearing: Mystery, hidden elements, or incomplete information
    Best practiced regularly throughout winter to build your personal omen vocabulary.
