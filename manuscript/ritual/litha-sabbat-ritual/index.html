☀️Midsummer

A Midsummer sabbat ceremony honoring the Sun God at his peak and the transition of Oak King to Holly King. Features the nine sacred herbs of Litha, ritual water purification, and affirmation of love for the Lord and Lady.

Quantities

  • Dark blue altar candles
  • Incense (lavender, musk, or sandalwood)
  • Summer flowers and fruits for decoration
  • Cauldron with small amount of water
  • Water bowl (partially empty to receive cauldron water)
  • Red votive candle in flame-safe bowl
  • Bowl with pinch of nine herbs: wood betony (or basil), chamomile, fennel (or lavender), lemon balm (or dianthus/carnation), mullein, rue, thyme, St. Johnswort, vervain
  • Athame
  • Wand
  • Bell (optional)
  • Pentacle
  • Cakes & Wine: red wine or hearty beverage, herbed bread, pastries, cold meats or fried chicken, potato salad with hard-boiled eggs

Directions

  1. Cast a circle using your preferred method.
  2. Light the incense and carry it around the circle area deosil to purify the space.
  3. Clap or ring a bell three times and say:
  4. I celebrate Life on this Midsummer day!
  5. Sadness is cast aside and joy flows within as the High Summer now begins.
  6. Light the red votive candle from the center altar candle and hold it up in your right hand:
  7. The Light of the Sun, the God of Life, shines around me and in me for all the world to see.
  8. Set the candle in its bowl and place it on the pentacle.
  9. With your fingertips, sprinkle water from the cauldron upon the altar:
  10. The Lord and Lady of the Greenwood have made Their pact.
  11. The Oak King turns his face to that of Holly King so He may wed the Queen and pass into Her care.
  12. The Lord rises into the Lady and prepares to descend into the corn, in both ways to be born again of the Mother.
  13. Pour water from the cauldron into the blessed water bowl:
  14. The Life that enters the Lady's care is sanctified and purified in Her love.
  15. Clap or ring the bell nine times.
  16. Raise your open arms:
  17. As the Sun moves on His course, so the course of Life moves closer to Death that Life may come again.
  18. Soon will the Lord of the Corn move into His realm to become the Lord of Shadows, but now, in the fullness of Summer, He shares the joy of His life and His love with all of the Earth.
  19. Hold your athame over the votive candle:
  20. As the God and Goddess share Their light and life with me, so do I share with others and offer comfort as is meet.
  21. Mix the nine herbs in their bowl with your athame, then add the herbs to the votive candle:
  22. I call upon the powers of Fairie imbued in these herbs that the Midsummer Fire be empowered.
  23. Herbs of the Earth; symbols of Otherworld, the planets, of Life, and of Love, your scent fills the air and drives away care.
  24. Rest your palms on the altar:
  25. Lord and Lady, You fill my life with Your bountiful love and gifts!
  26. I call upon Thee Both for Your continued blessings and offer my petition to Thee Both that Your love and caring remain with me always.
  27. Bless me now and receive my blessing, my appreciation for life, for love, for joy, for that spark that brings me to you.
  28. May I pass this joy to others.
  29. So Mote It Be!
  30. Hold up your wand:
  31. At this time of Midsummer joy, I re-affirm my love for my Lady and my Lord.
  32. Optionally proceed to Re-affirmation of Oath.
  33. Proceed to Cakes & Wine, then close the circle.
