🥩Beef Tongue📄Paper📌Pins & Needles

A targeted variation of the beef tongue spell designed to silence hostile witnesses, suppress false testimony, or compel people to speak only the truth. Name papers are crossed with the caster's own name or commanding phrases before sealing inside the tongue. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Quantities

1 raw beef tongue
1 name paper per target
Cayenne pepper
Vinegar (Four Thieves recommended)
Pins and needles

Directions

  1. Write the name of each hostile witness, or anyone whose speech must be controlled or silenced, on individual slips of paper.
  2. Cross over each name with your own name written perpendicular so that your name literally covers theirs, or substitute a commanding phrase such as "Keep quiet!" or "Shut up!" While writing, focus intensely on your intention and speak it aloud forcefully.
  3. Cut slits into the beef tongue and insert each paper.
  4. Seal the slits with pins and needles and dress with cayenne pepper and vinegar.
  5. Either freeze the tongue (see Beef Tongue Spell 1) or proceed immediately to cook and eat it (see Beef Tongue Spell 3).
🔒
Closed Practice: Beef tongue spellwork originates in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo — closed practices with African American cultural and spiritual lineage. Approach with respect.

Purpose

Where the Basic Version targets all opponents broadly for legal defeat, this version zeroes in on the spoken word. It is appropriate when:

  • A specific witness is expected to give damaging false testimony
  • You need a witness to stay silent entirely
  • You want to ensure that only truthful testimony is spoken on the stand

The Crossing Technique

Crossing a name means writing your own name directly over theirs at a perpendicular angle, so that your name literally covers and dominates theirs on the paper. This is a foundational Hoodoo technique: your will crosses and overrides theirs.

For a more direct command, substitute a phrase like "Shut your mouth" or "Tell only the truth" written over their name instead.

Vocalization

The spoken command is considered an essential part of this working, not optional. Say out loud what you need each person to do. The combination of written command + spoken command + physical binding (pins and needles) is what makes this working effective.

After Assembly

Once assembled, either:

  • Freeze it — follow the Basic Version (Spell 1) directions for a long-term freeze
  • Cook and eat it — follow the Cooked Tongue (Spell 3) directions to internalize domination over the targets
