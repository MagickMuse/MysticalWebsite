🏡Downsize Property for Simpler Life

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RitePouch
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickManifestationHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Two golden-brown candles - A brown drawstring bag - Three dried leaves or petals from your present garden or a potted plant - Three brown crystals or pebbles from your garden or near your home - A small bowl of salt
◇Directions
1. Light the first candle. 2. Place three pinches of salt, leaves, and stones in the bag and close it, saying: "My home has been a special place for me, a haven and a sanctuary." Pass the bag three times around the candle flame, saying three times: "I will travel light to my new life of simplicity and harmony, to a fresh special place for me, a haven and a sanctuary." Let the candle burn. 3. Take the bag and the second candle with you. 4. In your new home, light the second candle and pass the bag around it three times clockwise, saying: "I make my new home a special place, a haven of simplicity." Keep the bag in the kitchen or near a hearth. 5. Perform the evening before you move.
◇Description
An evening-before-move spell to downsize property for simpler more natural life, using golden-brown candles with bag of salt, leaves, and stones to travel light to new sanctuary.
