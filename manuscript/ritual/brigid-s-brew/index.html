A hearth ritual using a red-hot iron poker plunged into stout to honor Brigid as goddess of the forge and the hearthfire. Iron + Fire + Beer = Brigid. Best performed at a working fireplace.

Quantities

1 pint stout (Guinness or a porter/seasonal stout); 1 fireproof mug; 1 clean iron poker; 1 plate or pan (in case of overflow)

Directions

  1. Pour the stout into the mug, leaving at least an inch of space at the top.
  2. Set the mug on a plate or pan to catch any overflow.
  3. Build or tend a good fire in your fireplace.
  4. Heat the tip of the iron poker in the heart of the fire until the tip glows red-hot.
  5. Plunge the poker directly into the stout.
  6. Iron + Fire + Beer = Brigid.
  7. Add an Irish drinking song to make the magick complete.
