Yule

After burning your Yule log, gather the cooled ashes and sew them into a small magickal bag or poppet to create a talisman against lightning damage and house fires. Keep the talisman on your altar or fasten it over an entrance door. On the next Yule, add the talisman to the kindling fire for that year's Yule log while sharing aloud your gratitude for the past year's well-being.

Quantities

  • Cooled ashes from your Yule log
  • Small piece of fabric (for bag or poppet)
  • Thread (for sewing)
  • Needle
  • Tack (optional, for hanging over door)

Directions

  1. Gathering the Ashes:
    The day after burning your Yule log, once the ashes have completely cooled, carefully gather them. These sacred ashes carry the protective energy of your Yule fire.
    Creating the Talisman:
  2. Sew the cooled ashes into a small magickal bag or poppet. The container should be securely sewn to prevent ash from escaping.
  3. As you sew, focus your intention on protection from lightning damage and house fires.
    Placement:
    Choose one of the following placements:
  4. Keep the talisman on your altar throughout the year
  5. Use a tack to fasten it over an entrance door for household protection
    Renewal Ritual (Next Yule):
    On the following Yule, when you prepare your new Yule log:
  6. Remove the talisman from its place of honor.
  7. Add the talisman to the kindling fire for that year's Yule log.
  8. As it burns, speak aloud your gratitude for the past year's well-being and the protection the talisman provided.
  9. After burning the new Yule log, create a fresh talisman from those ashes, continuing the cycle of protection and gratitude.
