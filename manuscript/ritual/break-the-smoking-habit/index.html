🚭Break the Smoking Habit

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BanishingChant/MantraHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A pack of playing cards, just the spades, ace to king, to represent the smoking, and the diamonds, ace to king, for giving up plus the money saved - A pot of lavender or any fragrant flower
◇Directions
1. Lay out the spades in ascending order, ace to king. 2. Surround them with a circle of the ace to king of diamonds in ascending order. 3. When you are tempted to smoke, crush the cigarette in the pot of lavender. 4. Cover the ace of spades with the ace of diamonds, saying: "From high to low does craving grow. Freedom grows with the sweet flowers, and I say no." Give yourself a small nonoral treat. 5. When the cards are all covered, start again, plant the flower, and use a new pot. 6. Perform ongoing.
◇Description
An ongoing spell to break the smoking habit, covering spades with diamonds while crushing cigarettes in lavender pot until craving diminishes and freedom grows.
