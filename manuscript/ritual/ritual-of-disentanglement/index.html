🔓Ritual of Disentanglement

◇Form
Circle RiteOffering Rite
◇Method
Ancestor WorkCord MagickBanishingVisualizationChant/MantraElementalInvocation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Thread or cord in multiple colors (at least 2-3, representing root causes identified in meditation) - Scissors or crochet hook - Small cauldron or firesafe container with lid - Long matches or long lighter - Quarter candles (optional)
◇Directions
1. Complete the Ancestral Meditation first to identify root causes. 2. Gather your items and stand before your altar, free of distractions. 3. Cast a circle of protection: Close your eyes and take a deep breath, drawing earth's energy into yourself. 4. Push that power into your dominant hand and see brilliant white light emanating from your index finger. 5. Draw the circle clockwise, saying: "This space is sacred and exists between the worlds. This rite deals with what exists inside and out; that which is above and below. As I will, so shall it be." 6. Call the Quarters, lighting candles if desired: North: "Guardians of the North, let energies of earth provide an anchor to this emotional work. Welcome!" East: "Guardians of the East, let your gentle breeze dry my tears and give me the knowledge to create lasting change. Welcome!" South: "Guardians of the South, let your fire cleanse the soul and allow for true transformation to take place. Welcome!" West: "Guardians of the West, let your waters wash away residual negativity and leave me ready for new growth. Welcome!" 7. Call to deity: "Lord and Lady, gather near to offer your guidance and strength to break these unwanted ties and allow me to become the best version of myself. Welcome!" 8. Sit in your sacred space with the colored threads representing your root causes. 9. Rub them between your palms, naming them aloud. 10. Tangle them together, visualizing how they have molded you and produced physical, emotional, and spiritual pain. 11. When ready, open your eyes. 12. Take your scissors or crochet hook and chant: "Lord, Lady, ancestors, and guides, transform that which no longer serves. Turn pain and uncertainty into what I most deserve." Continue chanting while detangling and removing thread pieces, placing them in the cauldron. 13. Light the threads with a long match and let them burn completely. 14. Allow ashes to cool (overnight if needed), then scatter them in the wind. 15. If burning is unsafe, bury the pieces whole. 16. Close the circle: "Lord and Lady, thank you for your infinite knowledge and your ability to transform my burden, making it lighter. Stay if you will; go if you must. Blessed be!" West: "Guardians of the West, thank you for using your healing waters to temper the depth of emotion I have felt. Farewell." South: "Guardians of the South, thank you for helping me transform my trauma through your cleansing fires. Farewell." East: "Guardians of the East, thank you for the breeze that comforts and allows for true understanding of the deepest issues. Farewell." North: "Guardians of the North, thank you for the strength to continue to work on my highest good. Farewell." 17. Close your eyes and see the circle. 18. Going counterclockwise, see the white light retract and return to you. 19. Take a cleansing breath and exhale as the energy returns. 20. Center yourself and return the energy to the earth. 21. Say: "The circle is open, but never broken. So mote it be."
◇Description
A ritual for releasing ancestral trauma by physically working with colored threads representing root causes identified during the Ancestral Meditation. Through circle casting, quarter calls, deity invocation, and the burning of disentangled threads, inherited energies are transformed and released.
