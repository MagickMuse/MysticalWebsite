🪆The Puppet Spell (L'Envoutement d'Amour)

A wax or clay poppet lust spell performed within a triangle of Cernunnos on a Thursday during the waxing moon. The puppet is modeled to resemble the target with exaggerated sexual organs, inscribed with their name and parentage, consecrated with oil and wine, then pierced through the heart with the athame. Wrapped in exorcised white linen and concealed near the target's home.

Quantities

  • Freshly exorcised modelling clay or beeswax
  • Target's hair, nail clippings, or a worn clothing item
  • Olive oil (for consecration)
  • Wine (for consecration and anointing the triangle)
  • Evergreens and horns (altar decoration)
  • Cernunnos incense (for thurible)
  • White linen cloth (exorcised, for wrapping)
  • Athame
  • Chalice
  • Thurible

Directions

  1. On a waxing-moon Thursday at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM, prepare the altar with horns and evergreens. Set the chalice of wine just outside the triangle. Light the lamps of art and kindle the Cernunnos incense in the thurible. Within the triangle, place freshly exorcised modelling clay or beeswax along with the target's hair, nail clippings, or a clothing item.
  2. Draw a large equilateral triangle on the floor using the athame point. Mark with white tape beforehand if desired.
  3. Chant the invocation to Cernunnos at each side of the altar (moving clockwise), anointing each side with a dab of wine. Visualize the god approaching through a deep forest.
  4. Model the puppet from clay or wax, making features resemble the target as closely as possible. Exaggerate the sexual organs clearly to show extreme arousal. Press hair into the head of the figure, or nail clippings into the extremities. If using a clothing item, set it aside for after inscribing.
  5. Using the athame tip, scratch on the front: [Name], son/daughter of [X] and [Y]. On the back: [Name] lusting for [M]. If using a clothing item, tie it around the puppet now.
  6. Consecrate with oil and wine, chanting three times while strongly projecting the target's mental image onto the figure: In the name of Cernunnos, the Horned One, Creature of earth [or wax], I name thee [N], son [daughter] of [X] and [Y]. Thou art [N], son of [X] and [Y]. Seal with the triple cross and So mote it be!
  7. Lay the puppet face up in the altar triangle with its head pointing east. Take the athame in the dominant hand and raise it above the puppet's heart. Visualize Cernunnos guiding the hand. Project the target's image firmly onto the puppet. Build emotional intensity while chanting the incantation, then plunge the athame into the puppet's heart: It is not my hand which does this deed but that of Cernunnos, the Horned One. As blade pierces heart, so shall the loins of [N] burn with lust for [M] [supplicant's own name]. As my will, so mote it be!
  8. Withdraw the knife. Wrap the puppet in purified white linen cloth.
  9. Conceal the wrapped puppet near the target's home: buried under eaves, hidden indoors, or buried where they will walk over it. Do not allow the target to find it.
