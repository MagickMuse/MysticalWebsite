💚Healing Spell

A candle ritual to mend relationship rifts or physical ailments by projecting healing intention into runes representing the parties involved.

Quantities

Paper
Green candle

Directions

  1. Draw two runes, one on each side of the paper (one for each party), and place the paper under the green candle. Light the candle.
  2. As the candle burns, contemplate what is necessary for healing, whether it is a physical change, restoration, apology, or gesture.
  3. For physical healing, choose the Sun rune plus a rune representing the person; visualize healing light entering the person and your intention traveling down the wick to charge the Sun rune.
  4. For relationship healing, choose the green candle, a rune for the person you wish to heal with, and a rune representing the relationship type (Rings for marriage, Romance for romantic, Star for friendship).
  5. Carry the rune paper as a healing talisman, give it to the afflicted person, or share it with the person with whom you are making amends.
