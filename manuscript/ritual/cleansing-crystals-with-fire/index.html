🔥Cleansing Crystals with Fire

Eclectic

◇Quantities
Your crystals or gemstones White or natural beeswax candle (avoid synthetic candles) Candleholder or fire-safe surface Matches or lighter Small bowl of water (safety precaution) Optional: running water for post-cleanse rinse
◇Directions
1. This method is best for experienced practitioners; keep a small bowl of water nearby for safety, ensure good ventilation, and work on a fire-safe surface. Avoid this method for heat-sensitive stones such as opals or selenite. 2. Light a white or natural beeswax candle and let it burn steadily for a few moments while you sit comfortably before the flame and take a few deep breaths to center yourself. 3. Hold the crystal in your cupped hands and gaze into the flame, then shift your gaze to the crystal and speak to it: "I honor your service and cleanse you now by sacred fire. All negativity that clings to you is burned away. All heaviness is transformed to light. You emerge from this flame renewed and pure, ready to serve your highest purpose." 4. Pass the stone directly through the flame itself, not just the smoke above it, moving swiftly and deliberately. Rotate a large stone to pass each side through the flame. 5. Examine the stone: if it is clean with no soot or residue, it is ready for use. If there is soot or darkening, rinse it briefly under running water and dry it completely. 6. As a gentler alternative, hold the stone just above the flame for about thirty seconds while visualizing fire energy purifying it, or pass it through the smoke of a candle or sacred herbs instead of the flame itself.
◇Description
A fire-based cleansing ritual that uses candle flame to burn away negative energy from crystals, purifying them through the transformative power of fire.

Fire cleanses through transformation, burning away negativity and purifying crystals with its sacred flame. This method is particularly convenient when performed before or after candle-based rituals.

Safety First

Important: This ritual involves passing crystals through open flame. Take care not to burn yourself. Have water nearby in case of accidents. Ensure good ventilation and work on a fire-safe surface.

Heat-Sensitive Stones: Some crystals can crack or discolor when exposed to high heat. Opals, selenite, and other delicate stones should not be cleansed with fire.

Timing

Fire cleansing is most powerful during the waxing or full moon, on Tuesday (Mars/fire energy), during fire sign moons (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), or at noon when the sun is at its peak.

After Cleansing

Allow the candle to burn for at least a few more minutes after cleansing, thanking the fire for its transformative work. You may extinguish it or let it burn down completely. Your crystal is now cleansed and may feel noticeably warmer or more active in your hand.
