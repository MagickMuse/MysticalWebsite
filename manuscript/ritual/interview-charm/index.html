💼Interview Charm

Form
Charm
Method
Manifestation
Category
Spell
Correspondences5
💼Career📞Communication✨Inspiration✨Manifestation💰Prosperity
Materials2
🔪Knife📏Tape

To improve your chances of getting hired by energetically planting your presence at a job site or interview location. Best used during job search periods, auditions, or when applying to highly competitive roles.

Quantities

  • Several strips of wide adhesive tape (masking or duct tape)
  • A pair of shoes you plan to wear to interviews or work

Directions

  1. Use the tape to take impressions of your own footsteps or the soles of your chosen shoes.
  2. Roll each strip into a tight cylinder, sticky side inward.
  3. Keep one cylinder with you whenever you attend an interview, audition, or job-related event.
  4. If you decide you want the job, discreetly leave the charm behind at the site, ideally in a hidden or overlooked spot like under a seat, beneath a table, or inside a planter.
  5. This creates a symbolic "foot in the door," anchoring your intent and energy at the location.
✏️
Adapting this spell? The core mechanism here is sympathetic magick via your energy signature — the footprint impression is what makes it work. You can substitute the tape for paper you've stepped on, a traced outline of your shoe sole, or even a photo of your footprint. What you leave behind matters more than what you use to capture it.

Why This Spell Works

Job hunting is rarely a one-and-done process. Most people go through multiple interviews before landing a position, which is why this spell is designed to be prepared in batches. Having several charms ready means you can use this magick consistently throughout your search.

The charm works on the principle of sympathetic magick. Your footprint carries your energy signature. By leaving a piece of that signature behind at a location, you create an energetic anchor that says "I belong here" to the space itself. The phrase "getting your foot in the door" is more than just an idiom; it describes exactly what this charm accomplishes on a magickal level.

Preparation Tips

Use shoes you genuinely plan to wear to interviews or to work in this field. The connection between the shoes and your professional self strengthens the charm. Press the tape firmly to capture a clear impression, either by stepping on it or pressing the sole of your shoe against it.

Roll each strip tightly with the sticky side inward. This seals in your energy and makes the charm compact enough to carry discreetly. Prepare several charms at once during a single focused session so they all carry the same intention.

Using the Charm

Carry one charm with you to each interview, audition, or job-related meeting. Keep it in your pocket, bag, or briefcase. Its presence reinforces your confidence and intention throughout the interaction.

The key decision comes afterward: if you want the position, leave the charm behind before you go. Find a discreet spot where it will not be disturbed or discovered for a long time. Good hiding places include:

  • Inside a planter or potted plant
  • Under a waiting room chair
  • Behind furniture
  • In a crack or corner

If you decide during or after the interview that you do not want this particular job, keep the charm with you. You can reuse it at your next opportunity or dispose of it however you choose.

What to Expect

The charm plants your energy at the location, creating a subtle pull that keeps you in the minds of those making hiring decisions. Combined with a strong interview performance and genuine qualifications, this magick gives you an energetic edge over other candidates.
