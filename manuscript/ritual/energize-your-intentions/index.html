◇Directions
1. Intentions exist within webs of energy, action, and relationships. 2. Spider, the ultimate web builder, guides you in the art of intending. 3. But heed fairy tale warnings: getting exactly what you ask for may not deliver what you truly want. 4. Before energizing intentions, examine goals and ripple effects through mindmapping (see Spider's reflection teaching). 5. Once you've explored your intention clearly, begin energizing it. 6. Imagine you are Spider sitting in your web: 7. Create an Intention Altar: Set up altar with your intention as focus (see Beaver's altar-building practice if needed). 8. Each time you work with your intention altar, you energize your desires. 9. Make Offerings: Light candles, sing songs, offer prayers. 10. Meditate or channel Reiki, giving energy to the intention held in your web. 11. Use Symbols: Employ symbolic acts to energize intentions. 12. Attracting love? 13. Burn rose petals. 14. Each symbol carries energetic resonance. 15. Create Rituals: Design ceremonies symbolizing your intention manifesting. 16. Many cultural rituals follow this pattern: weddings set intentions for couples' futures, birthday parties carry wishes for coming years. 17. Your ritual becomes the bridge between desire and reality.
◇Description
An intention-manifestation practice inspired by Spider's web wisdom. By creating an intention altar and mindfully energizing it through offerings, symbols, and rituals, you activate your desires while remaining aware of their interconnected effects on your wider web of relationships.
