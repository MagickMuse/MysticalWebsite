A variant shield with a swirling, oil-on-water surface that confuses and throws enemies off-kilter. Effective against psychic vampires, verbally abusive people, and those attempting to manipulate or drain you. The shield causes attackers to lose track of their intentions, stumble over words, and become frustrated until they abandon their efforts.

Quantities

  • Required:
    None (psychic technique only)
    Skills Needed:
  • Visualization
  • Energy work
  • Focus and will

Directions

  1. Call to mind the column of energy connecting you to the heavens and to the Earth.
  2. See the column descend from the heavens, passing through you down into the Earth, and feel the vital current arise from the Earth into you.
  3. As you inhale, feel the power from above and below flow into you.
  4. As you exhale, feel that energy move throughout your body, impregnating every cell of your body with power.
  5. Make a fist with your left hand and place it over your heart.
  6. Cover it with your right hand and apply about five pounds of pressure.
  7. As you inhale, feel the power gather at your heart, attracted there by the pressure and your focused will.
  8. See a small egg shape gather at your heart, but instead of gray, visualize its surface as swirling colors, like when sunlight hits oil on water.
  9. Release the pressure on your chest and feel the egg grow larger, passing through your skin and stopping just at the point where it is roughly one to two feet away from your physical body.
  10. See the swirling, iridescent surface creating confusion and disorientation for anyone attempting to attack or manipulate you.
  11. After the image has been strongly conjured in the mind and you know that the shield is there, simply turn your attention away from it and go about your business.
  12. The effects of the shield will wear off in a few hours unless continually fed with imagination and will.
  13. If you desire to dissolve the shield before it naturally fades, simply inhale deeply, then exhale and see the shield dissolve into space.
  14. Shields work against everyone, not just harmful forces.
  15. Using confusion shields can cause even stranger reactions than basic shields.
  16. Use them only when you feel that you need them.
