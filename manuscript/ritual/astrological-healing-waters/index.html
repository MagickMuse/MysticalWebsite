Twelve bottles of lunar-charged spring water are created over twelve Full Moons, each labeled with an astrological sign and used for healing the body part that sign governs. Rooted in the ancient medical tradition of astrological anatomy from Aries (head) to Pisces (feet).

Quantities

12 glass bottles
Pure spring water

Directions

  1. Each month on the night of the Full Moon, expose a glass bottle filled with pure spring water to the moonbeams overnight.
  2. In the morning, label the bottle with the appropriate astrological sign for that month (or divide into smaller labeled bottles).
  3. Repeat each month until all twelve signs are represented.
  4. Apply each water to the part of the body ruled by the corresponding astrological sign for healing purposes.
