📦Lucky Seven Beans and Roots Box

Fill a magick box with seven lucky botanicals: High John the Conqueror, Low John the Conqueror, Lucky Hand root, galangal root, tonka bean, vanilla bean, and black snake root. Dress with essential oils of vetiver and patchouli, and sprinkle with powdered nutmeg and mace.

Quantities

High John the Conqueror root
Low John the Conqueror root
Lucky Hand root
Galangal root
1 tonka bean
1 vanilla bean
Black snake root
Essential oil of vetiver
Essential oil of patchouli
Powdered nutmeg
Powdered mace
Box or container

Directions

  1. Arrange High John the Conqueror root, Low John the Conqueror root, Lucky Hand root, galangal root, a tonka bean, a vanilla bean, and black snake root in a box.
  2. Dress all items with essential oils of vetiver and patchouli and sprinkle them with powdered nutmeg and mace.
