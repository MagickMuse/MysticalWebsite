🥚Herod's Seventy-Seven Greedy Daughters Spell

A Russian folk fever spell based on the belief that there are seventy-seven fever-demon daughters of Herod. An egg boiled in water from three sources is cut into seventy-seven pieces and thrown into flowing water at sunset. The sisters fight over the egg pieces and forget the patient in the frenzy, allowing the fever to disperse. Steps 1–4 must be completed before sunset. The afflicted party typically performs this spell themselves.

Quantities

Water from three distinct sources
Iron pot
One egg
Cloth for wrapping

Directions

  1. Collect and blend water from three distinct sources.
  2. Place the water in an iron pot and hard boil an egg in it.
  3. Cut the egg into seventy-seven pieces and tie them into a cloth.
  4. Complete these steps before sunset.
  5. At sunset, take the packet to a river or stream and throw it over your left shoulder, without looking, into the water flowing away from you.
  6. Mutter aloud how you wish the fever would leave you.
  7. Go home without looking back.
  8. Catching the sisters in action will prevent the spell from working and may invite further danger.
