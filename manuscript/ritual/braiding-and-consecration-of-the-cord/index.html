🪢Braiding and Consecration of the Cord

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Consecrated Item
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ConsecrationElementalKnot Magick
◇Category
Consecration
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Red ribbon (or hand-spun flax or river rushes), cut into three six-foot lengths Charged water and incense from the salt water and incense charging formula
◇Directions
1. During the waxing moon, exorcise the three six-foot lengths of ribbon with the charged water and incense. 2. Knot the three ends together, then begin braiding, chanting with each twist to bind in the magickal light: Made to measure, wrought to bind, blessed be thou cord entwined. 3. Once the braid is finished, tie a large firm knot at the loose ends so they cannot unravel, then close with the words So mote it be! 4. Tie five more knots down the cord's length: at three feet six inches, four feet, four feet six inches, five feet, and five feet six inches from the first knot. These give the triple radii used to cast a magick circle, a larger set for coven work and a smaller set for solo or small group spells.
◇Description
A waxing moon working that exorcises three lengths of ribbon, braids and knots them into the working cord, then seals it with a binding chant.
