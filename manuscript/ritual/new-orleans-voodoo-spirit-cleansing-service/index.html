⚠️ Closed Practice Warning: This ritual originates from Haitian Vodou, a closed religious tradition. The lwa invoked, Granbois, Baron Carrefour, Baron Cimitière, and Damballah, are active, living spirits with identities and expectations. Invoking them without understanding or relationship carries spiritual risk. Research Vodou tradition and approach with deep respect.

⚠️ Fire Safety: Burning sulfur produces pungent, potentially irritating fumes. Ensure proper ventilation throughout.

An extremely powerful Vodou-derived cleansing and spirit-banishing ritual that emerged in New Orleans as an adaptation of Haitian ritual. Sulfur is burned at each corner of the room while four prominent lwa are invoked and commanded to expel all evil spirits, spells, and negative vibrations. Best performed at midnight during a waxing moon.

Quantities

  • Red paper (one square per corner)
  • Small metal or stone plate (one per corner)
  • Sulfur (pinch per corner)

Directions

  1. Place a square of red paper onto a small metal or stone plate.
  2. Prepare one plate per corner of each room to be cleansed, most rooms require four sets.
  3. Place a pinch of sulfur on top of each paper.
  4. Set each plate in a corner.
  5. Light the sulfur.
  6. Simultaneously address the four lwa, turning to face each new corner as each name is uttered:
  7. "Granbois! Baron Carrefour! Baron Cimitière! Damballah! I invoke you! With the power of your names, I command and compel all evil spirits, spells and vibrations, any negative entities, to leave me and my home and never return!"
  8. Withdraw from the room, maintaining a vigilant eye on fire safety.
