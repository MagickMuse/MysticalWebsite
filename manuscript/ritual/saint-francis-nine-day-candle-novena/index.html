A nine-day candle ritual to petition Saint Francis of Assisi for assistance with animals, household harmony, protection, or defeating negative forces.

Quantities

  • 1 brown candle (or 9 smaller brown candles, or 1 nine-day candle)

Directions

  1. Obtain a brown candle. Francis's color is brown. You may use a single candle burned over nine consecutive days, nine separate candles (one per day), or a special nine-day candle designed to burn continuously.
  2. Set up a simple altar. Decorate with images of animals Francis loved, but keep it simple. He disliked ostentation. A statue or image of Saint Francis is traditional.
  3. Light the candle on Day 1. Speak your petition to Saint Francis. Be specific about what you need.
  4. Continue for nine consecutive days. Each day, light the candle (or continue burning if using a continuous candle) and repeat your petition.
  5. On Day 9, conclude with thanks to Saint Francis.
    Best Day: Monday is Francis's sacred day.
    Best Date: Begin on or near October 4 (his feast day) for added power.

Background

The novena (nine-day prayer ritual) is a traditional Catholic devotional practice. Saint Francis responds particularly well to this format, especially when brown candles are used.

What to Petition For

  • Controlling animals peacefully
  • Protection from animal harm (bites, stings, venomous attacks)
  • Protecting beloved animals
  • Restoring household harmony
  • Peace in the family
  • Exorcising or banishing Beelzebub
  • Not dying alone

Tips

  • Keep the altar simple; Francis disliked ostentation
  • Consider making a donation to an animal charity or environmental cause as an offering
  • Monday is his sacred day, a good day to begin
