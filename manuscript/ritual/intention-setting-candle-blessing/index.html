🌕Moon Oil

A simple candle blessing spell for the new or waxing moon to set intentions and release them into the world for growth alongside the waxing lunar light. Carve your goal into a dressed candle, anoint it with oil and herbs drawn toward yourself, and light it to kindle the growing power of your desire.

Quantities

  • Pen and paper
  • Toothpick or needle
  • Small taper or chime candle
  • Jasmine or sandalwood incense (optional)
  • Moon Oil, coconut oil, or oil of choice
  • Sandalwood powder or ground nutmeg
  • Dried rose petals
  • Plate or surface to roll candle in herbs
  • Candleholder
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. On the night of the new moon, gather your materials on your lunar altar or in your sacred space and dim the lights.
  2. Take a moment to reflect on the current moon sign energy if you wish, allowing it to guide and color your working.
  3. Write your lunar wish or intention on the paper, giving it clear and honest form.
  4. With your aspiration held in mind, use the toothpick or needle to carve a single word into the candle that captures the heart of your intent, such as "success" or "clarity." Add your name if you feel called to draw that energy specifically to yourself.
  5. Pass the candle and paper through incense smoke to cleanse and awaken them, if desired.
  6. Anoint the candle with oil, working from the tip of the candle down toward the base, drawing the energy toward you.
  7. Dab a small amount of oil on each corner of the paper as well.
  8. Sprinkle sandalwood powder or ground nutmeg and dried rose petals on a plate or across the paper itself.
  9. Roll the candle toward yourself through the herbs, once more drawing your desires inward.
  10. If you use the paper as your rolling surface, the traces of oil and herbs it absorbs will add an extra layer of enchantment to your written intentions.
  11. Place the dressed candle in the candleholder and tuck the intention paper safely beneath it.
  12. Hold your hands around the candle and above the paper.
  13. Visualize the moon and sun aligning in the sky, their combined force pulling you toward your desired outcome.
  14. With that image alive in your mind, light the candle, a cardinal spark to ignite the growing light of your desire coming into reality.
  15. Gaze into the flame and let it illuminate the darkness around you.
  16. Allow the candle to burn all the way down.
  17. Afterward, fold the paper and carry it with you in a pouch, or place it somewhere you will see it daily.
  18. At the full or waning gibbous moon, you may choose to burn the paper and release the energy, reflecting on what the lunar cycle has brought into your awareness.
