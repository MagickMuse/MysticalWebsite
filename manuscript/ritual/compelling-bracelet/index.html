📿Compelling Bracelet

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Knot MagickCord Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• A small amount of hair from the target • Command and Compel Oil (or infusion of sweet flag and licorice) • 1 drop essential oil of bergamot • 1 drop essential oil of vetiver • A strand of your own hair • Red and/or black thread (for knotting or supplementing length)
◇Directions
1. Obtain a small amount of hair from the target. 2. Soak it in Command and Compel Oil. 3. Alternatively, make an infusion by pouring boiling water over sweet flag and licorice, and soak the hair in this instead. 4. Add one drop of bergamot essential oil and one drop of vetiver essential oil to the soaking liquid. 5. Let the hair dry completely. 6. Braid the target's dried hair together with a strand of your own hair. 7. Knot them together, ideally forming nine knots. 8. While knotting, murmur your desires into the hair with focused intention. 9. If the hair is too short to knot nine times, wrap red or black thread around the braid and tie nine knots in the thread instead. 10. If additional length is needed to make a bracelet, supplement with red and/or black thread. 11. Wear the completed bracelet on your dominant wrist. 12. Maintain a constant, conscious intention directed toward the target coming to you and fulfilling your desires.
◇Description
A charm bracelet made from braided hair that both beckons a target closer and encourages their cooperation. The target's hair is soaked in Command and Compel Oil (or a sweet flag and licorice infusion), treated with bergamot and vetiver essential oils, then braided with a strand of the caster's own hair and knotted nine times. The bracelet is worn on the dominant hand and continuously charged with conscious intent.
