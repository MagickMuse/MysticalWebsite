A spell for creating a rainbow of harmony in your space in a large impersonal organization, charging seven rainbow-colored crystals in water to make your workplace feel like home.

Quantities

  • Seven small round crystals in red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and white or clear glass nuggets, beads, or buttons
  • A glass bowl half filled with water

Directions

  1. Drop each crystal, in any order, into the bowl of water; as you do so, say: "Rainbow, rainbow, magick measure, sparkle and glisten with your treasure. Rainbow of joy make my workplace like home that safe I feel until day is done." When the sun fades, dry the crystals.
  2. Put them in a bag and bottle the rainbow water.
  3. Set the crystals in a circle in your workspace, touching them when you feel overwhelmed by company policy or the impersonal atmosphere.
  4. Sprinkle a little of the water on any plants in your area.
  5. Perform ideally during a rainbow in the sky or sunshine after rain.
