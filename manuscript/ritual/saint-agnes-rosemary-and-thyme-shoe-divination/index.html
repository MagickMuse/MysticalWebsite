🔮Saint Agnes Rosemary and Thyme Shoe Divination

A love divination ritual performed on Saint Agnes' Eve (January 20) to dream of your true love. Involves rosemary, thyme, and a chant to the saint.

Quantities

  • 1 sprig of rosemary
  • 1 sprig of thyme
  • Your own urine
  • Your shoes

Directions

  1. Sprinkle a bit of your own urine three times over a sprig of rosemary and a sprig of thyme.
  2. Place one sprig in each of your shoes.
  3. Place each shoe on either side of your bed, near the head.
  4. Get into bed and chant: "Sweet Saint Agnes, to lovers so kind, come and ease my troubled mind."
  5. Go to sleep. You will now dream of your true love or hear them whispering in your ear.
  6. Be utterly silent from the time you utter the rhyme until you wake up and record your dream.

Purpose

This ritual is performed on Saint Agnes' Eve (the night of January 20) to receive a dream vision of your true love.

Timing

This ritual can only be performed on Saint Agnes' Eve, the night before her feast day on January 21.

Important Notes

  • Complete silence must be maintained after the chant until you wake and record your dream.
  • Record your dream immediately upon waking.
