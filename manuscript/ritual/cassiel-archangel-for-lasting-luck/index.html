A Saturday Cassiel's day spell for archangel blessing to ensure luck enters and remains in your life if you are in down period, writing blessing by candle light asking Cassiel to turn wheel of fortune.

Quantities

  • An indigo or purple candle
  • A piece of white paper
  • A purple pen
  • Purple ribbon

Directions

  1. At bedtime, light the candle.
  2. By its light, copy the following blessing: WISE CASSIEL, ARCHANGEL WHO TURNS THE WHEEL OF FORTUNE, GRANT ME GOOD LUCK, [say how you need it most], AND A WINDFALL, HOWEVER MODEST, TO BOOST MY FORTUNES.
  3. I WAIT WITH TRUST THAT YOU WILL BRING A NEW BEGINNING WITH THE MORNING AND THAT THE TIDE OF GOOD FORTUNE WILL AT LAST TURN IN MY FAVOR AND REMAIN IN MY LIFE.
  4. Read this aloud very softly, facing the candle so that your words fade into silence.
  5. Roll the message into a scroll and tie it once with the ribbon.
  6. Keep it in a secret place.
  7. Let the candle burn.
  8. Every month on crescent moon, carry the scroll outdoors.
  9. Do not unroll it.
  10. Ask that Cassiel make your fortunes grow.
  11. Once a year, burn the old blessing and rewrite it.
  12. Perform Saturday, Cassiel's day.
