🐐Oracle of Hermekate/Hekahermes

◇Directions
1. This rite takes place during or following a gathering in which Hermekate/Hekahermes has been invoked. 2. Take the skull from its place of honor and hold it aloft. All present take some part of it in their hands so that its weight is equally distributed at shoulder height. 3. Breathe deeply together in silence. 4. Sing the Hermekate Drum Chant together. 5. Call the hybrid name of Hekahermes/Hermekate together, repeatedly. 6. The Hierophant poses the question aloud, then says: "If your answer is yes, let the head draw toward the heavens; if no, toward the earth." 7. Speak the question aloud and wait. Attend to whether the skull gently lifts or sinks. 8. Upon feeling it move, all present kiss the skull, saying: "Hail, Hekahermes; Hail Hermekate!" 9. When the rite has ended, cover the skull with a cloth. 10. Pour wine upon the earth with words of thanks, and blow out the candles.
◇Description
A divination rite using the consecrated skull of Hermekate/Hekahermes as a living oracle. All present hold the skull together at shoulder height, breathe deeply in silence, sing the Drum Chant, and call the hybrid name. The Hierophant poses a question and asks the skull to rise for yes or sink for no, then waits for its movement. All kiss the skull upon receiving the answer. The rite ends with wine poured on the earth and the candles extinguished.
