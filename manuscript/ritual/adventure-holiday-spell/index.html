A Wednesday spell for adventure or activity holiday, using feathers to spiral air over pictures with yellow candles and tree incense in triangle for excitement, thrills, and speed.

Quantities

  • Two large feathers or an old-fashioned fan
  • Three yellow candles alternating with three tree-fragrance incense sticks set in a triangle formation
  • Pictures of the sports or a brochure for the holiday
  • A picture of you or person for whom you are casting the spell side by side with the adventure pictures, inside the triangle

Directions

  1. Light each candle and incense in turn from its own candle to the left of it, saying: "Excitement, thrills, adventure, speed, this holiday is what I need [or name activity]." With a feather in each hand or the fan, spiral air over the pictures, saying the same words faster and faster as you move the feathers faster.
  2. End by saying: "Fire of excitement flare, danger be not there, and so I/we/[name] seize(s) the moment of adventure." Plunge the incense sticks, lighted end down, into a bowl of sand or soil.
  3. Let the candles burn, saying: "Fire flare high, excitement rise to the skies. Adventure awaits me." Perform any Wednesday before the trip.
