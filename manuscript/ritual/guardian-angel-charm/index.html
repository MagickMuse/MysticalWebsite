A charm invoking your personal guardian angel for guidance and protection during challenges that seem bigger than you can handle alone.

Quantities

  • 1 small silver or gold hanging charm in the shape of an angel
  • 1 white cord or ribbon 18" long
  • Amber essential oil

Directions

  1. Collect the listed ingredients.
  2. Cast a circle around the area where you will do your spell.
  3. Slide the charm on the cord or ribbon and tie a knot to make a pendant necklace.
  4. As you tie the knot, visualize yourself safe, happy, and healthy.
  5. Say the following incantation aloud:
  6. "Guardian angel, be with me.
    Keep me healthy, safe, and free.
    Guide my steps so I can see
    What I must do. Blessed be."
  7. Put a dot of amber essential oil on the angel charm.
  8. Inhale the scent and let it calm your nerves.
  9. You may sense your guardian angel nearby.
  10. Imagine yourself placing your concerns in the angel's hands, knowing that everything will be taken care of.
  11. Slip the necklace over your head and wear it for protection.
  12. Open the circle.
