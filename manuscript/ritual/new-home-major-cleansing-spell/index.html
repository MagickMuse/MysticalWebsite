🏡New Home Major Cleansing Spell

A powerful room or whole-house cleansing ideal before moving into a new home. A blue candle dressed with frankincense and sandalwood oils burns within a sea salt circle, absorbing all negativity. Everything is folded up and disposed of at a moving body of water.

Quantities

Essential oils of frankincense and sandalwood
Olive oil
A blue candle
A disposable saucer or plate
Newspaper or a disposable tablecloth
Sea salt

Directions

  1. Blend essential oils of frankincense and sandalwood into olive oil.
  2. Use this to dress a blue candle.
  3. Place the candle onto a disposable saucer or plate on top of spread-out newspaper or a disposable tablecloth.
  4. Cast a large circle with sea salt on the paper around the candle and saucer, large enough to maneuver comfortably within.
  5. Enter the circle, light the candle, and close the doors.
  6. Let the candle burn out completely.
  7. When finished, fold everything securely inside the newspaper, not spilling a single grain of salt.
  8. Take it to a moving body of water.
  9. Slide all biodegradable materials into the water; throw everything else in a trashcan at a distance from your home.
  10. Walk away without looking back.
  11. Repeat in every room you wish to cleanse.
