🕯️Twenty-Four Hour Healing Vigil

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickHealingPrayerMeditation
◇Category
Prayer
↗Correspondences5
🌅Awakening❤️‍🩹Healing🩺Health✨Manifestation🧘🏻‍♀️Spirituality
↗Materials1
📄Paper

A twenty-four-hour candle vigil if someone is very ill and you are awake, waiting for news, lighting one tea light per hour to send healing blessings.

Quantities

  • 24 long-burning tea lights in a circle (have a supply of spares for those that burn through)
  • A photo of the person or the person's name written in green ink on white paper

Directions

  1. Light the first tea light, name the person, and say: "I send you blessings at this hour, that your illness/affliction/danger may pass and you be restored to health or safety or know a peaceful passing." Call on God/the Goddess/your personal focus of worship or any angels.
  2. Let the light burn while you pray, meditate in the flame, or keep yourself busy.
  3. On the second hour, light the second tea light and say the same spell words, each hour lighting a new tea light.
  4. Replace any that are burned with new ones, so after twenty-four or twelve hours all are alight.
  5. Let them burn.
  6. When the last one goes out, say: "The light of hope burns still in my/our hearts, never to be dimmed. Blessings to you at this hour."
