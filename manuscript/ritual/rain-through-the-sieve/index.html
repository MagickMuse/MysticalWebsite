🧺Sieve

A fertility rite combining masculine rainwater with lunar feminine energy. Collected rain is charged under moonlight and poured through a sieve over a woman seeking to conceive.

Quantities

Collected rainwater (in a sealed container)
A sieve
A bright moonlit night

Directions

  1. Collect rainwater and keep it tightly sealed in the refrigerator.
  2. Wait for a bright moonlit night, then set the container outside to charge under the moonbeams.
  3. When ready, pour the water through a sieve over the woman who wishes to conceive; she should be naked if possible for greater potency.
  4. Direct the water over her head, genitals, or breasts, accompanied by petitions for a healthy child.

Rain is metaphysically understood as masculine generative power, the fertilizing force of thunder gods such as Zeus, Baal, Thor, and Chango, while the ocean and standing waters represent feminine fertility. This working combines both: masculine rainwater is charged with lunar energy before being directed through the sieve, a tool with its own deep fertility associations, uniting yin and yang forces in a single act.
