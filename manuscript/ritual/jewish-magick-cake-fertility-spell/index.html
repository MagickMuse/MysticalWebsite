🌱Jewish Magick Cake Fertility Spell

👼Akriel

A fertility charm from Jewish magickal tradition in which a specially decorated cake is prepared for a bride or woman seeking conception. The cake bears the woman's name, names of power, magick spells, and sacred texts, and is consumed entirely by the petitioner.

Quantities

  • Round cake, or a triangular slice of cake
  • The woman's name (inscribed on the cake)
  • Names of power and magick spells (inscribed on the cake)
  • Sacred texts, Deuteronomy 7:12 is traditional for counteracting sterility

Directions

  1. Decorate a round cake, or a triangular slice, with the petitioner's name, magick spells, names of power, and sacred texts written across the surface.
  2. A traditional text for counteracting sterility is Deuteronomy 7:12, chosen in part because its context invokes Akriel, the Angel of Barrenness, and contains the word berit, which is often understood as a reference to the genitals.
  3. Once the cake is prepared and inscribed, the woman eats it entirely.
