Cloves pierced and strung on red thread, then hung above a baby's bed as a protective charm.

A simple infant protection charm made by stringing cloves on red thread and hanging the finished strand above the baby's bed.

Materials

  • Whole cloves
  • Red thread or red string
  • A needle or pin (to pierce the cloves)

Instructions

  1. Using a needle or pin, carefully pierce each clove through its length to create a bead.
  2. Thread the pierced cloves onto red thread, knotting between each clove if desired.
  3. Hang the finished clove strand above the baby's bed or crib as a protective charm.
