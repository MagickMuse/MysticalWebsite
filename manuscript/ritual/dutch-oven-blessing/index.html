🍞Mickie's No-Fuss Sourdough

A consecration ritual for blessing a Dutch oven (bread cauldron) for sourdough baking. The vessel is rubbed with salt while speaking an incantation to ensure beautiful, well-risen loaves.

Quantities

  • Dutch oven (6-7 quart, cast iron or enameled)
  • Salt for rubbing

Directions

  1. Rub the inside of your Dutch oven with salt while speaking the incantation.
  2. This blessing transforms your baking vessel into a magickal tool of transformation, supporting oven spring and creating artisan loaves with complexity of texture, flavor, and presentation.
  3. With this cauldron, I bless each bake, and every loaf that I may make.
  4. Let dough spring up and crumb be light, each loaf beautiful in scent, taste, and sight.
