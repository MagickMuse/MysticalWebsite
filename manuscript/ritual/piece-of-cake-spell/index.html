🕯️Piece-of-Cake Spell

A kitchen witchery spell to sweeten frustrating situations. Uses baking as magickal practice, infusing intention into cake batter. Choose flavors to match your goal: chocolate or strawberry for love, cinnamon or mint for money, almond or vanilla for peace. Share with others involved in the challenging situation to make them cocreators in the spell's success.

Quantities

  • Cake mix or ingredients for your favorite cake recipe
  • Food coloring
  • Large bowl
  • Large spoon
  • Cake pan(s)
  • Candles in colors matching your intention
  • Matches or lighter
  • Frosting in a color matching your intention

Directions

  1. Cast a circle around your kitchen and preheat the oven.
  2. Follow the directions for making the cake according to the package or your favorite recipe.
  3. As you work, focus on your objective and imagine you are sending your intention into the batter.
  4. Add food coloring to tint the batter to match your intention: pink for love, green for money, and so on.
  5. Stir the batter clockwise if your goal is to attract something or stimulate an increase.
  6. Stir counterclockwise if you want to limit, decrease, or remove something.
  7. Pour the batter into the pan and bake.
  8. When the cake has finished cooking, let it cool.
  9. Ice it with frosting in a color that relates to your intention.
  10. Decorate it with symbols, pictures, and words that describe your objective.
  11. Add candles of an appropriate color.
  12. The number of candles should correspond to your goal: two for love, four for stability, five for change, and so on.
  13. Light the candles and concentrate on your wish.
  14. Blow out the candles.
  15. Share the cake with other people involved in the situation, or eat it yourself.
  16. Each person who eats some takes the intention into themselves and becomes a cocreator in the spell's success.
