🚿Oshun Five-Day Water Shrine Love Spell

Performed on five consecutive days beginning on a Friday. Most easily done in the bathroom, or taken to a river or stream. Always taste the honey before offering it.

Quantities

Candles, shells, mermaids, hand mirrors, and coconuts, for the shrine
Spring water
Honey
White and/or orange candles
Five cinnamon sticks
Five different types of perfume or perfume oils
Love drawing powder
Yellow and orange flowers
A bucket
Warm spring water
A photograph of your beloved

Directions

  1. Build a shrine to Oshun in the bathroom. Suggested decorations: candles, shells, mermaids, hand mirrors, and coconuts.
  2. Offer Oshun spring water and honey. Light white and/or orange candles.
  3. Run a bath.
  4. Place five cinnamon sticks, five different types of perfume or perfume oils, some love drawing powder, and yellow and orange flowers in a bucket, and cover with warm spring water.
  5. Write your beloved's name, birthday, and their mother's name on the back of a photograph. Place the photo on the altar or where you can see it from the bath.
  6. Coat your body with honey.
  7. Stand in the tub. Pour the ingredients in the bucket over you.
  8. Recline, relax and concentrate on your desires.
    The photograph and altar remain standing in the bathroom for the duration of the ritual. Lit candles may be maintained if safe.
