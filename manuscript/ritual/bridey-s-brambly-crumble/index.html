🫐Bridey's Brambly Crumble

A sacred summer dessert using three foods holy to Brigid: oats, butter, and blackberries. The blackberry symbolizes Brigid's tenacity and endurance, strong sweetness held intact through centuries of change. Measurements are intentionally flexible, in the spirit of the bean-a-tighe cooking from what is at hand.

Quantities

Blackberries; old-fashioned rolled oats; butter (softened); brown sugar; white sugar (optional); flour (optional; omit for gluten-free); cinnamon (optional); shallow baking dish; mixing bowl

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Pick over your blackberries for any stems or debris.
  3. Do not wash them, washing makes berries mushy.
  4. Fill the baking dish with a layer of berries about 2 inches deep.
  5. If desired, sprinkle a bit of white sugar and about a tablespoon of flour over the berries.
  6. Add cinnamon if you wish.
  7. Gently mix with your hands.
  8. In the mixing bowl, combine oats, brown sugar, and flour in roughly these proportions: 1 part oats, 1/2 part brown sugar, 1/4 part flour (e.g., 1 cup oats + 1/2 cup brown sugar + 1/4 cup flour).
  9. Measuring cups are not required, the recipe is very forgiving.
  10. Using your hands, gradually work in softened butter until the dry ingredients are well incorporated and hold together loosely.
  11. For 1 cup of oats, this will be about 1/2 cup (1 stick) of butter.
  12. More butter is always good.
  13. Distribute the crumble topping evenly over the berries.
  14. Bake for about 30 minutes, until the berries are bubbling and the topping is golden.
  15. Keep an eye on it, as baking time varies with dish size.
  16. Rest for at least 20 minutes before serving, the filling thickens as it cools.
  17. Serve with whipped cream.
