⚠️ New Orleans Voodoo is a closed practice sacred to its originating culture and should only be practiced by those within that tradition.
A Voodoo spell to strip a man of sexual virility as retribution for mistreatment. The cloth from intimate relations is knotted seven times and sunk into a river. An alternative method uses Black Devil Oil on the target's clothing.

Quantities

1 cloth, towel, or rag used after intimate relations
1 weight for sinking (stone or similar)
Black Devil Oil (alternative method)

Directions

  1. Keep the cloth used for cleansing after intimate relations with the target.
  2. Tie the cloth in seven knots.
  3. Weight the knotted cloth down and drop it into a river.
  4. The target will lose their sexual virility until amends are made or forgiveness is granted and the cloth is retrieved.
  5. Only the caster may break the spell by untying the seven knots.
  6. If the knotted cloth is lost, the effect becomes permanent.
  7. As an alternative method, obtain Black Devil Oil and sprinkle it on the unfaithful target's shoes or clothing.
  8. This will prevent them from becoming sexually aroused and may also draw them back to the caster.
