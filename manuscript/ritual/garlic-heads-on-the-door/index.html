🧄Garlic Heads on the Door

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ProtectionHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Garlic heads (1, 4, 5 or 12)
◇Directions
1. Hang garlic heads on your doors to repel evil. 2. For the most power, use one, four, five or twelve heads. 3. If the garlic begins to rot or break down, bury or burn it outside the house and hang fresh heads.
◇Description
Garlic heads hung on doors to repel evil, most powerful in counts of one, four, five or twelve, and replaced as soon as they begin to spoil.
