💍Handfasting Spell

A ritual for committing to a relationship for a year and a day, using candle colors and directions to reflect the bond's dynamic.

Quantities

Candles (one per partner, color per bond: red for passion, blue for loyalty, yellow for intellect, white for purity)
Compass

Directions

  1. Scribe the Rings rune onto the candles. Choose color based on the bond: red (passion), blue (loyalty), yellow (intellect), white (purity).
  2. Place candles on your altar using a compass to align with the elements/temperaments of the partners (North/Earth/Grounded, West/Water/Emotional, South/Fire/Passionate, East/Air/Intellect).
  3. Light the candles each night, moving them closer together until they meet in the middle (common ground).
  4. On the handfasting day, heat the bottoms of the candles and stick them together at a 10-degree angle so they lean into each other and "kiss."
  5. Allow them to burn down together, saving the wax drippings for one year and a day.
  6. After this period, decide to discard the wax to end the commitment, or melt it into a new candle with essential oil to renew the commitment.
