🕯️Elemental Cross Circle

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Directional MagickElementalInvocationVisualizationProtection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- 1 athame or wand - 4 candles (one for each quarter) - Salt - Water
◇Directions
1. This circle is designed for situations requiring maximum protection and power. 2. The unusual calling order (North first, then South, East, West) creates a cross pattern that anchors the circle more firmly between the worlds. 3. Purify the space by sprinkling salted water around the perimeter. 4. Take your athame or wand and cast the circle clockwise from the East, visualizing impenetrable blue-white energy forming walls and dome. 5. Face North. 6. Visualize a massive stone pillar rising from the earth, ancient and unshakeable. 7. Say: 8. Guardians of the North, Pillars of Earth! Rise as stone eternal, unmovable and true. Anchor this circle in the deep foundations. Hail and welcome! 9. Face South. 10. Visualize a pillar of living flame, crackling with power yet contained. 11. Say: 12. Guardians of the South, Pillars of Fire! Rise as sacred flame, transforming and fierce. Illuminate this circle with your power. Hail and welcome! 13. Face East. 14. Visualize a pillar of crystallized air, shimmering and translucent. 15. Say: 16. Guardians of the East, Pillars of Air! Rise as breath made visible, clear and keen. Carry our intentions on the winds. Hail and welcome! 17. Face West. 18. Visualize a pillar of deep water, impossibly standing yet fluid. 19. Say: 20. Guardians of the West, Pillars of Water! Rise as the depths made manifest, flowing yet strong. Wash clean all that would harm us. Hail and welcome! 21. Visualize all four pillars connected by lines of energy, forming a cross at the center where you stand. 22. The cross anchors the circle firmly between worlds. 23. Release in reverse order: West, East, South, North. 24. Thank each pillar and visualize it sinking back into its element. 25. Walk counterclockwise to release the circle boundary. 26. Ground thoroughly afterward, as this working draws significant energy.
◇Description
A high-power anchored circle for emergencies or intense workings. Elementals are visualized as stone pillars at each quarter, using an unusual N-S-E-W calling pattern for maximum stability.
