💗Health Pouch Spell

◇Form
PouchDressed Candle
◇Method
Herbal MagickCandle MagickChant/MantraThread/Sewing MagickElementalConsecration
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- 1 small yellow cotton bag - Red thread - 1 needle - 1 personal item - Rosemary leaves - Saint John's Wort - Thyme - Coriander seeds - 1 white candle - 1 red candle - Dragon's blood ink - 1 pen - Sage incense - Vetivert oil - Blessed water - Salt
◇Directions
1. Rub vetivert oil on both candles. 2. Inscribe the white candle with Thorn and speak: 3. Thorn for protection. 4. Inscribe the red candle with Ken and speak: 5. Ken for opening energy and transformation. 6. With pen and dragon's blood ink, draw Uruz on the outside of the yellow bag for physical health. 7. Light the sage incense. 8. Light the white candle and speak: 9. This flame doth burn to bring good health. 10. Light the red candle and speak: 11. And this flame doth burn to bring energy and strength. 12. Place the items into the yellow bag and speak: 13. Rosemary to hold health; thyme to ward illness; Saint John's Wort and coriander seeds keep me [add personal item] strong! 14. For as I will, So Mote It Be! 15. Sew the bag shut with red thread and speak: 16. Health is mine to have and hold! 17. Energy and strength are mine forevermore! 18. For as I will, So Mote It Be! 19. Consecrate by passing the pouch through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak: 20. Be thou empowered by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt], by Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke], by Elemental Fire [pass through candle flames], and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water] to protect my health and keep me well and strong! 21. This pouch keeps good health with me! 22. For as I will, So Mote It Be! 23. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash. 24. Carry the pouch in a pocket, purse, or similar.
◇Description
A waxing to Full Moon spell for sustained good health. Candles are anointed with vetivert oil: the white inscribed with Thorn for protection, the red with Ken for energy and transformation. The rune Uruz for physical health is drawn in dragon's blood ink on a yellow pouch, which is filled with rosemary, thyme, Saint John's Wort, coriander seeds, and a personal item, then sewn shut and consecrated through the four Elementals. Carried in a pocket or purse. ⚠️ Sage is sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
