🌼Marigold Ritual for Confidence

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• 1 strip of paper • 1 blue pen • 1 empty flowerpot • 1 pot of marigolds or another yellow flowering plant, just starting to bloom
◇Directions
1. On one side of the paper, write the name of yourself or the family member, and on the other: "I GROW IN CONFIDENCE DAY BY DAY, SHYNESS DISAPPEARS AWAY." Walk over a patch of earth or get the family member to do so (make an excuse), scoop up some of the soil, and put it in the bottom of an empty flowerpot. 2. Either dig a hole in the garden and drop in the hole the walked-over soil, followed by the piece of paper, or add the paper to the new plant pot and in both cases add more soil to cover the paper. 3. In either case, plant the marigolds over the paper and soil and fill in the hole, saying: "I [or name] grow in confidence every day, shyness disappears away." When you water the flowers, repeat the spell words.
◇Description
A ritual to gain confidence if you or a family member are very shy, using walked-over soil and marigolds planted with written affirmation to grow self-assurance as flowers bloom.
