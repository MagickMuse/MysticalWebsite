Crossroads

A protective freezing spell using the Crossroads rune to stop or prohibit a threatening situation or action.

Quantities

  • Pencil and paper
  • 1 foot of embroidery thread
  • Clean glass jar
  • Rock

Directions

  1. A protective freezing spell using the Crossroads rune to stop or prohibit a threatening situation or action.

Directions

  1. Draw the Crossroads rune on paper with a point in the center representing the person or action to thwart.
  2. Fold the paper (rune inside) until small, speaking the charm:
OUT OF SIGHT,
OUT OF MIND,
OUT OF TIME,
THIS I BIND.
WORD TO DEED,
WITH ALL DUE SPEED,
I HOLD THE KEYS,
AND THIS I FREEZE.
  1. Wrap embroidery thread tightly around the folded rune in various directions. Tie the ends together tightly when covered.
  2. Place the bound rune in the jar, weigh it down with the rock, and fill the jar halfway with water.
  3. Place the jar in the back of your freezer for as long as needed (usually at least a full lunar cycle).
  4. On the dark moon (new moon), remove the jar and bury it in a place where it will not be retrieved.
