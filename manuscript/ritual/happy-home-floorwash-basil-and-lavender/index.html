🧹Happy Home Floorwash (Basil and Lavender)

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Wash
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Cleansing
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
Holy Water or Marie Laveau Water (generous amount) Florida Water (generous amount) White vinegar (generous amount) Sweet basil leaves, crushed Lavender blossoms, crushed Cascarilla Powder Boiling salted water (to steep the botanicals) Floorwash rinse water (a full bucket)
◇Directions
1. Pour boiling salted water over the crushed lavender blossoms and sweet basil leaves. 2. Let the botanicals steep for an hour, then strain out the solids. 3. Add the strained liquid together with Holy Water or Marie Laveau Water, Florida Water, white vinegar, and Cascarilla Powder to a bucket filled with floorwash rinse water. 4. Use this water to cleanse all entrance areas as well as floors and windowsills.
◇Description
A Hoodoo-influenced floor wash using holy water or Marie Laveau Water, Florida Water, white vinegar, basil, lavender, and Cascarilla Powder to cleanse entrances, floors, and windowsills. Note: Marie Laveau Water and Florida Water are rooted in Hoodoo, a closed practice.
