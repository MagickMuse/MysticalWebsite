🍇Venus Mediterranean Love-Joining Rite

A noon sunlight ritual using grapes to bind two hearts together through a repeating cycle of eating and incantation. Can be performed alone or shared with a lover for stronger results.

Quantities

  • One bunch of ripe grapes

Directions

  1. Perform this ritual at noon in bright sunlight where the sun's power is at its peak.
  2. Begin the four-part cycle by picking a single grape and speaking the first verse: "The vine it binds and twines our love." Eat the grape.
  3. Pick another grape and speak the second verse: "Your heart to mine." Eat the grape.
  4. Pick the third grape and recite: "The vine it winds and finds us one." Eat the grape.
  5. Pick the fourth grape and speak: "Our hearts entwine and you are mine." Eat the grape.
  6. Continue repeating this four-verse cycle, eating one grape with each line, until you have consumed all the grapes or can eat no more.
  7. For enhanced results, share the ritual with your beloved.
  8. Offer them grapes as you speak each verse aloud, or feed them the grapes while speaking the words silently in your mind.
  9. The shared act of eating while hearing or feeling the incantation strengthens the binding between two hearts.
