✴️Four Archangels Cleansing Service

The four archangels are invoked at the cardinal points to cleanse and protect ritual space. Their presence burns like purifying fire while leaving holiness in its wake. Prepare the space first with basic cleansings (smudging or asperging). Fill the room with precious resin fragrance, angels feed off it and it enhances the cleansing.

Quantities

  • Sword, crystal-tipped wand, flaming torch, or other circle-casting tool
  • Precious resin incense (frankincense, myrrh, etc.), optional but enhancing

Directions

  1. Perform basic cleansing of the space first (smudging or asperging).
  2. Fill the room with precious resin fragrance to attract and honor the angels.
  3. Cast a circle using a sword, crystal-tipped wand, flaming torch, or other ritual tool.
  4. Pause at each cardinal point and invoke the appropriate archangel: Raphael to the East, Michael to the South, Gabriel to the West, and Uriel to the North.
  5. Make the invocation as simple or as elaborate as you choose.
  6. Different traditions assign different directions to each archangel; use what resonates for you.
