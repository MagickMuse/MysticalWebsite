🍇Wine Moon Sweet Tea

◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Method
LunarMeditation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 4½ oz water - 3 large red seedless grapes - 1 large blackberry - ½ tablespoon dried jasmine (food-grade) - ½ tablespoon honey - Moon Water ice cubes (optional) - Jasmine incense - Matches or lighter Use organic ingredients where possible.
◇Directions
1. Set your water to boil. 2. As it heats, wash the grapes, with Moon Water if you have it. 3. Hold in your mind the turning of the year toward darkness, the alchemy of the harvest: grapes to wine, intention to prosperity, effort to abundance. 4. In the bottom of a glass, muddle the grapes and blackberry together until juicy. 5. Add the dried jasmine. 6. Pour the boiling water over everything and allow to steep for 5 to 10 minutes. 7. Add the honey and stir clockwise. 8. As you stir, give thanks for the bees and their harvest work, and invite sweet abundance, wisdom, and psychic vision to flow toward you. 9. Drink the tea hot, or allow it to cool in the refrigerator or freezer. 10. For a more ceremonial serving, pour it chilled over Moon Water ice cubes and stir gently. 11. Light jasmine incense. 12. Sit with your tea, breathe in the smoke, and let your mind open. 13. Meditate on what you have harvested this year, what visions are rising, what prosperity is making its way to you. 14. Wine Moon Offering: Pour a glass of wine (if of legal age) or grape juice as an offering to Dionysus during the Wine Moon. 15. Leave the glass on your altar for a few days, then pour the offering into the earth as thanks for the harvest.
◇Description
A muddled grape, blackberry, and jasmine sweet tea brewed under the Wine Moon to summon abundance, prosperity, and psychic visions. Stir in honey clockwise as an offering to the bees and an invitation for sweet clarity. Drink hot or chilled over Moon Water ice cubes while burning jasmine incense. Includes a simple Wine Moon offering to Dionysus.
