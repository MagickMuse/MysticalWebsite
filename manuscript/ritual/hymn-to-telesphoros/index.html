🕯️Hymn to Telesphoros

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Devotional WorkChant/MantraInvocation
◇Category
Invocation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
A household altar with a lit lamp Offerings of the day
◇Directions
1. Stand at the household altar with the lamp lit and any offerings of the day arranged. 2. Take the standing posture of adoration, hands outstretched toward the heavens. 3. Speak or chant the hymn in a clear, measured voice, the invocation in the opening lines giving way to the body of praise. 4. We praise thee, O Makar, Blessed One, illuminator of all mortals, giver of every inner strength. You, who were created by Paian, glorious Telesphore, you are the one whom the Epidaurians honour with songs of protection, as they call enthusiastically upon King Akesin, because he heals people from brutal pain. 5. But we do not only sing your praise, Telesphoros, but also for long-haired Paian, who brings healing to life, Io Makar, Bakkhos Himself. 6. Drawn from the Athenian hymn For Telesphoros.
◇Description
A devotional hymn known to Athenians as 'For Telesphoros.' Telesphoros is a healing Daemon of the Asklepian sphere; his name marks him as the one who carries treatment through to its completion, and his charge is to watch over those recovering from illness. Athenian tradition credited him with lifting feverish disease from the land, and the song honours him as the one who brings joy and lets people set down their troubles. The hymn carries the typical hymn form of invocation joined to praise, without petition.
