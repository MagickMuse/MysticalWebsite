🔮Tasseomancy Tea Leaf Reading

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
Required: - 1 teaspoon loose tea or dried herbs - Plain white teacup (essential for reading) - Saucer to cover cup - Very hot (not boiling) water Optional: - Journal for recording readings - Camera for photographing patterns - Reference guide for symbol meanings Recommended teas: - Black tea (traditional) - Green tea - Chamomile - Mugwort - Peppermint - Rosemary - Any loose herbal tea
◇Directions
Preparation: 1. Use a teacup with a plain white interior for best visibility. 2. Place 1 teaspoon of dried loose tea or herbs into the cup. 3. Fill the cup with very hot (not boiling) water. 4. Top with a saucer and steep for 5 minutes. Drinking the Tea: 1. Remove the saucer. 2. Drink and enjoy the tea, leaving a small amount of liquid in the bottom of the cup. 3. Pause and clear your mind. Reading the Leaves: 1. Formulate a clear, silent question. 2. Cover the teacup with your hand. 3. Swirl the cup deosil (sunwise/clockwise) several times. 4. Remove your hand and allow the marc (wet leaf material) to settle. 5. Observe carefully: Are shapes, patterns, or images clearly apparent? Interpreting the Marc: Common Symbols: - Animals: Consult animal symbolism for meanings - Trees/Plants: Growth, natural cycles, grounding - Lines: Paths, journeys, connections - Circles: Completion, cycles, wholeness - Crosses: Choices, significant events, intersections - Letters or numbers: Initials, dates, or numerological significance - Stars: Hope, wishes, divine guidance - Hearts: Love, emotion, relationships - Keys: Solutions, unlocking, access - Clouds: Confusion, uncertainty, hidden factors Placement in Cup: - Rim/Top: Present or immediate future - Middle: Near future (weeks to months) - Bottom: Distant future or deep subconscious - Handle side: Matters close to home or self - Opposite handle: External influences, others Density and Clarity: - Clear, distinct shapes: Clarity, strong message - Scattered leaves: Multiple influences, complexity - Clumped leaves: Concentrated energy or blockage - Empty spaces: Open paths, lack of obstacles Recording Your Reading: Sketch or photograph the cup immediately. Note your question, the date, moon phase, and your interpretation. Review later to see how predictions manifested. Best Teas for Divination: - Black tea: Traditional, strong readings - Green tea: Lighter, gentler messages - Chamomile: Calming, dreams, intuition - Mugwort: Psychic visions, prophetic insight - Peppermint: Mental clarity, fresh perspective - Rosemary: Memory, protection, clear sight Note: Any loose tea or dried herbs will work. Avoid tea bags as they don't produce readable leaf patterns.
◇Description
Traditional tea leaf divination using loose tea or dried herbs in a white teacup. The marc (wet leaf material) forms patterns and symbols that answer questions and reveal hidden knowledge.
