🌿Three-Forked Stick Removal

A Romani removal spell worked over a boiling cauldron using a naturally three-forked stick as the ritual tool. The working is dedicated to a protective deity of the practitioner's choosing, Michael the Archangel is suggested when in doubt, as he is traditionally charged with the role of humanity's protector. The chant invokes seven ravens as agents of destruction against the Eye. Finding a three-forked stick spontaneously is considered auspicious; if none can be found, one may be carved. One of the Evil Eye methods.

Quantities

  • Stream water (drawn with the current)
  • 7 glowing coals
  • 7 handfuls of cornmeal
  • 7 cloves of garlic
  • Three-forked stick (found or carved)
  • Cauldron or pot

Directions

  1. Fill a cauldron or pot with water drawn from a stream, taken with the current rather than against it.
  2. Add seven glowing coals, seven handfuls of cornmeal, and seven cloves of garlic.
  3. Bring to a boil over fire.
  4. When the water begins to boil, stir it with a three-forked stick and speak the chant:
  5. Evil Eye, look here, your power is broken.
  6. Seven ravens come; seven ravens strike through every Eye, every last one.
  7. Evil Eye, look here, your strength is gone.
  8. Dust fills your sight.
  9. Five fingers pierce your gaze.
  10. You are blind.
  11. Evil Eye, look here, you are put out.
  12. Burn, burn, burn in the fire of [Deity's name].
  13. Continue stirring and repeating until the working feels complete.
