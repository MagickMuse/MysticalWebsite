🎴Bring Harm With A Playing Card

◇Directions
1. Select five playing cards: the Ace of Spades, Ace of Clubs, Eight of Spades, Eight of Clubs, and a hole card chosen to match the intended level of harm. 2. The Four of Spades brings illness, the Seven of Clubs causes overwork and chaos, and the Ten of Spades brings a final end. 3. Dress three black candles with Crossing Oil, Destruction Oil, or D.U.M.E. Oil depending on severity. 4. Arrange the candles in a triangle. 5. In the center, place the target's photo with their full name and birth date written upon it, along with any personal effects obtained. 6. On each card write the target's name followed by "this is your hand." Pin or nail the hole card to the top candle, the pair of aces to the left base candle, and the pair of eights to the right base candle. 7. Light the candles while praying for the curse to take hold. 8. The cards will catch fire and burn with the candles. 9. Gather all spell remains and dispose of them at a crossroads, in running water, or by burial in a graveyard depending on the severity desired.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. An advanced card-and-candle hex with tiered severity. Five specific playing cards are chosen, with a hole card determining the level of harm: illness, chaos, or death. Three black candles are dressed with cursing oils and arranged in a triangle around the target's photo. The cards are pinned to the candles and burned as a prayer is offered.

Items

5 playing cards (specific suits listed below)

3 black candles

Crossing Oil, Destruction Oil, or D.U.M.E. Oil

1 photo of the target with name and birth date

Straight pins or coffin nails

1 pen

Card Selection

The base hand is always the same:

  • Ace of Spades
  • Ace of Clubs
  • Eight of Spades
  • Eight of Clubs

The fifth card is the hole card, chosen to match the intended severity:

  • Four of Spades — illness
  • Seven of Clubs — overwork, chaos
  • Ten of Spades — a final end

Instructions

Dress three black candles with Crossing Oil, Destruction Oil, or D.U.M.E. Oil depending on the desired severity. Arrange them in a triangular position.

In the center of the triangle, place the target's photo with their full name and birth date written upon it, along with any personal effects.

On each card, write:

(Name), this is your hand

Pin or nail the hole card to the candle at the top of the triangle, the pair of aces to the left base candle, and the pair of eights to the right base candle.

Light the candles while praying for the curse to take hold. As the candles burn, the cards will catch fire. Once the work is done, gather all spell remains.

Disposal

Dispose based on severity:

  • Crossroads — standard harm
  • Running water — moderate severity
  • Graveyard burial — the most severe outcome
