🍎Apple Commitment Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraVisualizationManifestationLunar
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- One apple (or similar fruit if apple is disliked) - Silver-colored knife
◇Directions
1. Perform this ritual during the waning moon, an auspicious time for picking fruit and encouraging things to come to completion. 2. Begin by washing the fruit thoroughly under running water. 3. As the water flows over it, recite these words: 4. "Fruit sweet, as he/she does eat, move [name] to lasting state. Fruit sweet, as he/she does eat, let him/her not hesitate. Fruit sweet, as we do eat, no longer wait to say I do, Me and you, our lifetime through." 5. At an opportune moment when you and your lover are together, use the silver-colored knife to cut the fruit precisely in half. 6. Share the fruit with your beloved, eating your halves together. 7. As you both eat, silently recite the incantation in your mind three times. 8. After sharing the fruit, collect any leftovers, cores, or uneaten portions. 9. Bury these remains either in the ground or in a pot of soil, adding other fruit to the burial if available. 10. As you cover the fruit, speak the words of the incantation aloud one final time.
◇Description
A waning moon fruit-sharing spell designed to encourage a long-term lover to commit to permanence. Uses the symbolism of dividing and sharing a single fruit to represent two becoming one.
