🪙Pentacles Prosperity Protection Spell

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
ManifestationLunarConsecration
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Tarot card: Four of Pentacles - Jar with lid - Grains of choice (oats, rice, wheat, barley, or corn) - Sharpie or permanent marker - 1 bay leaf - 4 copper pennies or other coins
◇Directions
1. Place the Four of Pentacles face-up on your surface. 2. Spend a moment with the image: a king who holds tightly to each coin, reserving his resources for secured abundance. 3. Set your jar directly atop the card. 4. Fill the jar halfway with your chosen grain, a symbol of the harvest moon's abundance. 5. Using the Sharpie, write your intention clearly on the bay leaf. 6. Place the leaf and one coin inside the jar, then fill it the rest of the way with grain. 7. Seal the lid. 8. This jar is now your personal magickal grain reserve, for finances, energy, or both. 9. Sit comfortably. 10. Place one coin beneath each foot and one atop your head. 11. Hold the sealed jar to your chest. 12. Visualize what you are protecting and building, money and abundance to carry you through leaner times, or your own inner energy reserves after a season of expansion. 13. Let it become vivid and real. 14. When ready, speak aloud, filling in the name of the moon: 15. "___ moon, I hunger no more. Complete are my energy, money, and food stores. With some to keep and some to share, I have all I need and plenty to spare." 16. Breathe that sense of abundance fully into your body. 17. Feel safe, secure, and surrounded by plenty. 18. Store the jar on your altar. 19. Any time you feel your reserves running low, return to it: hold it, repeat the meditation, and light a candle atop the jar to recharge the working. 20. Note: The three remaining coins can be set aside for the Prosperous Property Jar Spell.
◇Description
A harvest-season grain jar spell drawing on the imagery of the Four of Pentacles, the king who guards his coins, to protect and build financial reserves, energy stores, or both. Grains symbolize the abundance of the harvest moons; the bay leaf carries your written intention; copper coins anchor material energy. The working calls on the waning gibbous moon's energy of turning inward, protecting what has been built, and preparing for the quieter months ahead.
