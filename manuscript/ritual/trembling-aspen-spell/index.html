📿Trembling Aspen Spell

A court case charm bag working that uses the trembling nature of the aspen tree as a sympathetic link to destabilize an opponent during legal proceedings. The aspen twig is charged with intention and placed in a charm bag alongside supporting botanicals.

Quantities

1 aspen twig
Calendula blossoms
Black poppy seeds
Slippery elm
Charm bag

Directions

  1. Hold an aspen twig in both hands and charge it with your desire, focusing on your intended outcome during courthouse proceedings.
  2. Murmur over the twig: "As this twig trembles, so trembles my opponent," naming the opponent aloud if possible.
  3. Place the charged twig into a charm bag together with calendula blossoms, black poppy seeds, and slippery elm.
