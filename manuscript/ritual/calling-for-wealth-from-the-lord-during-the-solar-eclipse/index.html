A prosperity invocation recited during a solar eclipse to call upon the Lord of Shadows and Lord of the Darkened Sun for wealth. The Lord of the Underworld is traditionally the holder of the riches of the Earth, who may be petitioned for a portion of His largess.

Quantities

A money or prosperity spell, to pair with this invocation

Directions

  1. Recite this invocation in conjunction with a money or prosperity spell during a solar eclipse.
  2. The spell calls upon the God in his dual aspect as Lord of Shadows (eclipse) and Golden Sun (emergence):
  3. Lord of Shadows, Lord of the Darkened Sun;
    I call upon Thee to complete the spell I have begun.
    Thou who holds power over wealth;
    Whose hand moves swiftly with knowing stealth,
    Take wealth into Thy hand for me,
    And pass it to Thyself as Golden Sun.
    Great Lord of Light, receive this treasure,
    Fill it with the Power of Thy pleasure.
    Open now Thy golden hand, and lay this wealth upon me and this land.
    Lord of the Sun with Thyself as Lord of the Night,
    Upon me cast Thy favored sight.
    Bring to me Thy gifts of wealth, health, and worldly delight.
    For as I am Thy child who loveth Thee, I am in You, and You are in me.
  4. Hear then my call and hearken unto me,
    That as I will, So Mote It Be!
    My blessings unto Thee, and Thine unto me,
    Let this Spell be bound to completion, So Mote It Be!
    With love t'wix Thee and me, the spell's conclusion is called by three.
    So Mote It Be!
