⚠️ Closed Practice: New Orleans Voodoo. New Orleans Holy Herbs, a protective formulation of seven botanicals, are brewed in a steaming cauldron so the steam fills an area, cleansing it while beckoning protective spirits.

Quantities

Black snake root
Catnip
Hops
Jasmine
Motherwort
Peppermint
Skullcap
1 cauldron of water

Directions

  1. Add black snake root, catnip, hops, jasmine, motherwort, peppermint and skullcap to a steaming cauldron.
  2. Brew the herbs.
  3. Let the steam permeate the area to cleanse it and call protective spirits.

See also Holy Herbs Child Protection Bath, which uses the same seven herbs.
