🫓Babalu-Ayé Quick Bread Skin Cleanse

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PetitionHealing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
Santería/Lucumí
◇Quantities
One slice of bread Palm oil or olive oil Altar of Babalu-Ayé
◇Directions
1. Dedicate a slice of bread to Babalu-Ayé by leaving it on his altar for a minimum of seventeen minutes. 2. Put palm or olive oil onto the soft bread. 3. Use this oiled bread as a sponge to gently cleanse the affected area.
◇Description
A simpler version of the Babalu-Ayé bread healing. A slice of bread is dedicated to Babalu-Ayé on his altar for at least seventeen minutes, then oiled and used as a sponge to gently cleanse the affected skin area.
