🫘Bean Ritual for Solo Fortune-teller

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- 1 handful of peas - 1 large pot of beans - Spoon for stirring and serving
◇Directions
1. Mix a handful of peas into a large pot of beans. 2. Avert your eyes and stir the pot without looking. 3. Lift up a spoonful: if there's a pea amongst the beans, expect a year of good fortune ahead. (Other interpretations include true love, marriage or a baby within the year.)
◇Description
A private divination ritual for solo practitioners. Mix peas into beans and stir without looking. If you draw a pea, expect good fortune, true love, marriage, or a baby within the year.
