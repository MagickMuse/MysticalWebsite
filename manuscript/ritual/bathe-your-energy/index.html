◇Directions
1. In Ancient Egypt, Cat was a representative of the Goddess Bastet come to dwell in your home, family royalty. 2. When a cat died, she was mummified, and her humans shaved their eyebrows and mourned her until their eyebrows grew back. 3. In ancient Celtic lands, Druids used Cat medicine to help them slip between worlds. 4. During the Spanish Inquisition, cats were seen as familiars, magickal avatars who assisted with sorcery. 5. Cat is associated with divinity and magick because she keeps her energy attuned. 6. You can keep your own energy field clear with regular cat baths: 7. Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths, letting yourself settle into your skin. 8. Using your hands, begin smoothing down your energy field, a few inches out from your physical body. 9. Not sure you believe you have an energy field? 10. That's okay! 11. Go through the motions and notice what you feel. 12. If you notice any spot that feels sticky or rough, pluck it out of your energy field and flick it away. 13. Trust your hands and don't think it. 14. Cat knows that keeping your energy field clean is an important step in cultivating a magickal glow.
◇Description
An energy field cleansing practice inspired by Cat's grooming habits. By smoothing down your energy field and removing sticky or rough spots, you keep your energetic body clear and cultivate a magickal glow.
