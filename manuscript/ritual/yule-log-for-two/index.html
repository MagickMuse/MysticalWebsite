🪵Yule Log for Two

◇Form
Altar WorkingCircle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/MantraDevotional WorkSolar
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Evergreen soap or smudge - Yule log (oak if possible, sized for desired burn time) - Fireplace or fire pit - Matches and fire-safety equipment - Pocketknife - Permanent markers - Colored paper - String - Red ribbons - Scissors - Evergreen sprigs or trimmings - Essential oils - Libation (ale/mead/whiskey) - Two acorns or paper representations - Two glasses - Cakes and ale
◇Directions
1. Purpose: Honor Midwinter and Yule log tradition (luck, protection, insights). 2. Timing: Traditionally solstice eve, but any time during Midwinter season. 3. Most effective at night. 4. Pre-Ritual: Wash with evergreen soap or smudge. 5. Dress casually with magickal jewelry (working with wood and knives). 6. Trim log to fit fireplace. 7. Assemble materials. 8. Memorize readings or write on index cards. 9. List things to let go of and wishes for coming year. 10. The Ritual: 11. Carry log together (each holding an end) and set before fireplace, saying: The Wheel turns and turns and then, we turn the Wheel once again. 12. Adorn the log together: carve Cailleach image or inscriptions, tie on wishes and ribbons, anoint with evergreen oils, tie on greenery sprigs (asking protection of evergreen trees). 13. Work together, talking, sharing ideas, recalling stories from past winters. 14. Finish with drizzle of ale or whiskey as offering to spirit within the log. 15. Place log and lay smaller fire around it to ignite the larger log. 16. Together, speak aloud the traditional story of the Yule log. 17. Extinguish all lights. 18. Sit quietly in darkness, each contemplating past year (challenges and good times) and imagining wishes for coming year. 19. When ready, light fire together. 20. As it burns, throw in dried evergreen sprigs and say farewell to old year (thanks, appreciations, banishment of old habits/pains). 21. Take turns offering farewells, feeding small fire with wood. 22. Once Yule log blazes, contemplate year ahead and power of possibilities. Watch carvings/offerings burn, imaging wishes carried skyward. Speak aloud: As you burn, our thoughts go free. As we will, so mote it be! 23. Throw acorns into fire to represent year ahead. 24. Speak aloud plans, wishes, resolutions, hopes. 25. Enjoy silent fire scrying, finding mysteries in flames. 26. Pour two glasses of libation and toast coming year and each other. 27. Sing song over burning log ("Deck the Halls" mentions solstice, solar year change, Yule log). 28. Enjoy cakes and ale. 29. Let log burn down to charred chunks and ashes (may end ritual before log burns down). 30. If sitting up through night, raise lights and tell stories or play parlor games. 31. Following ancient tradition, save fire remnants to start next year's Yule log fire. 32. Variation: Exchange gifts representing the coming year.
◇Description
A cozy Yule log burning ritual for two people (spouses, friends, or partners) to honor Midwinter tradition, bringing luck, protection, and insights through adorning and burning a log together.
