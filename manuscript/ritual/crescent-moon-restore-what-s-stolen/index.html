🌙Crescent Moon Restore What's Stolen

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
LunarChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• Small white stone or moonstone for each night from crescent to full moon • Small white flower for each night
◇Directions
1. Hold a stone/crystal between your open cupped hands. 2. Blow softly on it, saying, "Lady Moon, as you do grow, restore all to me. Let this be so [name what you have unfairly lost]." Begin a circle of stones/crystals with the first stone, scattering the petals of a single flower over it and repeating the spell words. 3. Continue the same spell words and actions each night until the full moon. 4. On full moon night, complete the stone circle, scattering any remaining flower petals over the last stone. 5. Walk around the outside of the circle counterclockwise seven times, saying seven times, "Lady Moon your night of power, restores power to me hour by hour. What was taken shall once more be mine, when next month's moon her fullness shines." Leave the circle in place. 6. Perform every evening outdoors from the crescent moon up to and including the full moon.
◇Description
A multi-night crescent-to-full moon ritual building stone circle with flowers each night, walking counterclockwise seven times on full moon.
