Ojo de Venado

An ojo de venado (deer's eye) is a bean that closely resembles an eye, used in Latin American magick to protect against the Evil Eye. It is strung onto red wool with amber, coral, jet, and glass blue eye beads, finished with a red pom-pom and hung as a protective charm. Charge and consecrate the beans before use for enhanced power. The bean may also be added to charm bags or glued to wall amulets or horseshoe charms. The bean's power may be enhanced by gluing spiritual images such as tiny votive cards onto it. One of the Evil Eye Spell methods.

Quantities

  • Ojo de venado bean(s)
  • Red wool
  • Amber beads
  • Coral beads
  • Jet beads
  • Glass blue eye beads
  • Religious medals or other protective symbols
  • Red pom-pom (to finish)

Directions

  1. String an ojo de venado onto red wool, together with amber, coral, and jet beads, glass blue eye beads, religious medals, and other lucky, protective symbols.
  2. Finish with a large red pom-pom at the bottom and hang the completed charm.
  3. Charge and consecrate the ojo de venado before use for enhanced power.
  4. Alternatively, add beans to protective charm bags, or glue one to wall amulets or wrapped horseshoe charms.
