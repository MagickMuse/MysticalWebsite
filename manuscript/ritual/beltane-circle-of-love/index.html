Beltane

A group ritual (minimum 4 participants, ideally 7+) designed to help all participants attract, express, and manifest more love in their lives while tuning in to the fertile, creative energies of Beltane. Helps participants improve their ability to attract, express, and manifest love, get into the romantic, carefree spirit of Beltane, heighten creative energy, and bring fresh inspiration.

Quantities

  • Candles: one for each participant plus one additional for center, and a candleholder for each (red or pink candles best)
  • Jasmine oil, rose oil, or patchouli oil (pure, natural essential oils, not synthetics)
  • Small pieces of notepaper and pens with blue ink, enough for everyone to have a pen and several sheets of paper
  • Two cups, filled halfway with water
  • Two wands
  • A bell
  • Optional: small campfire if location permits
  • Minimum 4 participants, ideally 7 or more
  • Setting: Nighttime, preferably outdoors, preferably in a wooded area or near body of water, preferably where small campfire is allowed

Directions

  1. Pre-Ritual: Dress in clothing that makes you feel sensual or good-looking.
  2. Shades of white, pink, red, or green are especially suitable.
  3. Light a small campfire if allowed, or place the additional center candle.
  4. In a circle surrounding it, place the candleholders.
  5. Beside the center, place the two cups, two wands, bell, notepaper, pens, and essential oil.
  6. Give one candle to each participant, then select a ritual leader and three additional volunteers.
  7. Ritual leader walks around the circle ringing the bell.
  8. Participants focus on the ringing sound, envisioning the space clearing of negativity.
  9. Second pass: infuse the area with positive vibration.
  10. Participants conjure happy, loving feeling and project it outward into the ritual space.
  11. Ritual leader and three volunteers come to center.
  12. Two with wands raise them high, drawing down active, assertive, masculine energy.
  13. Optional: call Cernunnos, Pan, the Horned God, or Belenos.
  14. Touch wands together forming an X, switch positions, form X again.
  15. Say: "Scepter to scepter, we welcome the energies of the Great God, the active principle, the seed, the Animus! Enter here now! Join us here now!"
  16. Two with cups raise them high, drawing down passive, nurturing, feminine energy.
  17. Optional: summon Anu, Brighid, Flora, Freya, or the Maiden Goddess.
  18. Pour water from one cup into the other, alternating.
  19. Say: "Cup to cup, we welcome the energies of the Great Goddess, the subtle principle, the nurturer, the womb, the Anima! Enter here now! Join us here now!"
  20. Those with wands plunge them into cups.
  21. One wand moves clockwise, other counterclockwise, swirling the water.
  22. All four say in unison: "Scepter to cup, we acknowledge the sacredness of all creation; we welcome love and light in all its multiple and many forms!" Volunteers return to circle.
  23. Ritual leader anoints each participant on palms, wrists, chest, and forehead with essential oil.
  24. Envision loving vibration of oil seeping into body, filling them with glowing beauty, causing them to radiate with love.
  25. Direct feeling of love from heart into participant's body through visualization, will, and intention.
  26. Say to each: "You are love. You are beautiful, and you are loved. You are love." Final person anoints the ritual leader.
  27. Ritual leader circles center candle/campfire three times, envisioning loving energy growing brighter, more powerful, magnified.
  28. Stop, hold hands safely above flame, say: "By Great Beal's Fire, we empower our desire with the power of creation, with the power to inspire! By the powers here that be, we manifest all that we see! Our light draws love and our love draws light! We'll get just what we will tonight!"
  29. Ritual leader taps one participant who goes to center, lights their candle from central fire, turns to face circle.
  30. Participant focuses on personal creative goals and/or wishes for love and romance.
  31. Other participants observe and shout out admirable, appealing, or attractive traits (e.g., "You have beautiful eyes," "You are worthy of love," "You are beautiful inside and out").
  32. Keep statements authentic and genuine.
  33. At ritual leader's signal, participants hold hands out, open palms toward center ritualist.
  34. Conjure feeling of love in hearts and minds, let it radiate throughout body then out through hands directly into center ritualist.
  35. Visualize energy as glowing, tinted light charged with emotion.
  36. Center ritualist places their candle in a holder at center and returns to circle.
  37. Next ritualist takes their place, lights candle from central flame, repeats process.
  38. Continue until every participant including ritual leader has had their turn.
  39. All participants in circle, hands joined.
  40. Send feeling of love around circle, projected from hand to hand, beginning and ending with ritual leader.
  41. Alternative: pass a kiss around the circle from cheek to cheek, or lips to lips.
  42. At ritual leader's signal, everyone holds hands toward central fire, palms open, projecting loving energy into the flames.
  43. Ritual leader says, sending final boost of loving energy and magickal intention: "By love, by fire, by you, by me, by God, by Goddess, what we will, it will be!"
  44. Socialize with ritual mates, talking openly about romantic feelings and wishes, sharing creative ideas and aspirations.
  45. Use notepaper and pens to write short notes for one another, offering compliments and/or invitations for romantic adventure.
  46. Candles/campfire can be extinguished or allowed to burn out.
  47. Expect love and creativity to noticeably flourish in lives of all participants immediately upon conclusion and continuing for several weeks or more.
