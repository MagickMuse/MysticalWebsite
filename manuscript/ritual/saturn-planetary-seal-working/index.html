🪐Saturn Planetary Seal Working

A ritual for working with Saturn's planetary seal to harness the powers of discipline, endings, binding, and time. Saturn's seal features a curved scythe and entwining ribbon representing the ties that bind. This working can be applied to five areas: illumination, financial matters, love endings, wrath, and healing.

Quantities

  • Drawing or image of Saturn's planetary seal
  • Black candle (optional)
  • Saturnian incense or offerings (optional)

Directions

  1. Create sacred space.
  2. Light a pink or white candle (or both) and arrange symbols of love, healing, and wholeness around you (rose quartz, roses, lavender, honey, images of healed hearts).
  3. Write a letter: On a piece of paper, write a letter to your ex-partner.
  4. This is for you, not them.
  5. Be honest.
  6. Express your pain, anger, disappointment, love, gratitude, whatever you feel.
  7. Don't hold back.
  8. Let it all pour out onto the page.
  9. When you've said everything you need to say, write: "I release you. I release us. I release this pain. I am free."
  10. Burn or bury the letter: Safely burn the letter in a fire-safe dish (cauldron, sink, outdoor fire pit), letting the smoke carry your words away.
  11. If burning isn't possible, bury the letter in the earth or tear it into tiny pieces and scatter it in moving water.
  12. As it burns, dissolves, or scatters, visualize the cord between you and your ex dissolving into light.
  13. Cleanse yourself: Take a ritual bath or shower.
  14. Wash your body with intention, imagining all residual hurt, bitterness, and attachment washing away down the drain.
  15. Use salt, lavender, or rose water if you have it.
  16. As you dry off, say: "I am cleansed. I am whole. I am ready to love again."
  17. Seal your heart with sweetness: Eat a spoonful of honey.
  18. As the sweetness fills your mouth, say: "I call in sweetness. I call in love. I open my heart to joy, to healing, to the love I deserve."
  19. Journal: Write down what you learned from this relationship.
  20. What red flags will you honor next time?
  21. What boundaries will you set?
  22. What kind of love do you truly want?
  23. Anchor these insights.
  24. Close: Thank yourself for doing this work.
  25. Blow out the candle and say: "This chapter is closed. I am free. I am open. I am ready."
  26. Aftercare: Be gentle with yourself.
  27. Grief may still surface, that's okay.
  28. Each time it does, breathe, feel it, and let it move through you.
  29. You're healing.
