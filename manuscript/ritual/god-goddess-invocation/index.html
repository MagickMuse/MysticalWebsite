☀️Midsummer📅Summer Solstice

A simple, general invocation to the Goddess and the God for Midsummer. Use at the start of a rite or as a standalone blessing.

Quantities

  • No materials required (optional: candle or incense if desired)

Directions

  1. Use this invocation at the start of a ritual or on its own.
  2. Read with clear line breaks:
  3. Great goddess, I greet you! You who are known by many names and many faces,
    shine your divine blessing down on me on this Midsummer Day, filling me with light
    and love.
  4. Great god, I greet you! You who are the lord of the beasts and the fields and the
    woods, shine your divine blessing down on me this Midsummer Day, filling me with
    strength and energy.
