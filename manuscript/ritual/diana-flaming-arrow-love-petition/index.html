Diana, the Roman lunar goddess and mistress of magick, has excellent archery skills. Don't try this spell unless your skills are worthy of the goddess. This is potentially a dangerous spell for more than one reason, Diana will not look favorably upon your petition if you burn down her forest.

Quantities

A written petition of love
An arrow

Directions

  1. Write out your petition of love.
  2. Affix it to an arrow.
  3. Set the arrow aflame.
  4. Quickly shoot it towards the moon.
