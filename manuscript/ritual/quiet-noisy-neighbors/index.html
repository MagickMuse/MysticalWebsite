🔇Quiet Noisy Neighbors

A pre-noise time ritual with beeswax or white candle, pressing jade or green beads into melting wax to bind noise with blessings.

Quantities

  • 1 natural beeswax or soft white candle
  • 1 heatproof tray or very wide holder
  • Tiny jade crystal, green glass nugget, or small green bead (one per offending person and animal)

Directions

  1. Light the candle and say, "You are too loud with your disruptive ways, you interrupt my days, keep me awake. For everyone's sake, I bind you now with blessings." As the wax melts, press the crystals around the base of the candle into the melted pool of wax, repeating the spell words.
  2. When there is enough melted wax to hold the crystals tight, extinguish the candle, repeating the spell words.
  3. Leave the candle and holder with the attached crystals on a high shelf once it has cooled.
  4. When the wax crumbles, retrieve and wash the crystals, disposing of the wax in a sealed paper bag.
  5. Perform before a typically noisy time of the day or evening.
