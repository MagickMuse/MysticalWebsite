🌕Esbat

A refresher version of the Passing the Midhes ritual, performed during a Full or Sidhe Moon Esbat. The purple candle is inscribed with Ur, Saille, and Beithe, then Coll and Duir are drawn on a paper marked with an X. Calendula is offered for Otherworld sight and mullein for Underworld sight. The paper is folded, wrapped with purple, red, and green threads, and placed in the Ogham bag.

Quantities

  • Lavender incense
  • 1 purple candle
  • 1 square of paper
  • 1 pen
  • Dragon's blood or red ink
  • Calendula
  • Mullein
  • Ogham fews in bag
  • 3 lengths of thread (purple, red, green)

Directions

  1. Light lavender incense.
  2. Inscribe the purple candle with Ur, Saille, and Beithe, then light it.
  3. Draw a large X on a square of paper.
  4. On the top edge, draw Coll and speak:
  5. I stand with Coll at the gateway to Otherworld.
  6. Drop some calendula into the candle flame and speak:
  7. I give the gift of calendula to receive that of Other sight.
  8. I travel through the realms of Otherworld and return unto my own.
  9. On the bottom edge of the paper, draw Duir and speak:
  10. I stand with Duir at the gateway to Underworld.
  11. Drop some mullein into the candle flame and speak:
  12. I give the gift of mullein to receive that of sight in Underworld.
  13. I travel through the realms of Underworld and return unto my own.
  14. Fold the paper, wrap with purple, red, and green thread, and place in the Ogham bag.
  15. Bury candle remains or dispose of them with trash.
