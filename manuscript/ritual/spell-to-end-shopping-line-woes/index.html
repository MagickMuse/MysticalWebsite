A quick intuition-based spell invoking Sheila the Shopping Goddess to guide you to the fastest checkout line. Requires no tools, only trust in your instincts.

Quantities

  • None

Directions

  1. As you approach the checkout area of the store, close your eyes, clear your mind, and take a deep breath.
  2. Think or quietly say this incantation:
  3. "Goddess Sheila so divine
    Guide me to the fastest line."
  4. Open your eyes and allow yourself to be drawn to a particular line.
  5. Do not analyze it or second-guess yourself.
  6. The shortest line may not be the fastest, and the shoppers with the fewest items in their carts might be the very people who will dawdle.
  7. Trust your instincts.
