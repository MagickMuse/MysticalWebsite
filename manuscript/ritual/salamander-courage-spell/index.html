🔥Salamander Courage Spell

A spell to draw upon the fire power of the universe with assistance from fire elementals (salamanders) to bolster vitality and confidence during setbacks, disappointments, or frustrating circumstances.

Quantities

  • 9 small red votive candles
  • Matches or lighter
  • 1 magick wand

Directions

  1. Arrange the candles in a circle around you, in a place where you can safely leave them to burn down completely.
  2. Beginning in the east, light the candles one at a time as you move in a clockwise direction around the circle (making sure that you'll be inside the circle when you're finished).
  3. When all the candles are burning, stand in the center of the circle and face south.
  4. Call out to the salamanders, the elementals who inhabit the element of fire.
  5. Tell them you have lit these nine candles in their honor.
  6. Explain your situation and request their assistance, by chanting the following incantation aloud:
  7. "Beings of fire
    Shining so bright
    Fuel my desire
    Increase my might.
    Help me be strong
    All the day long
    So I'll succeed
    In every deed."
  8. You may notice faint flickerings of light, other than the candles, in the room.
  9. Or, you might sense the energy around you quickening.
  10. It may even seem a bit warmer.
  11. That means the salamanders are present and willing to work with you.
  12. Take up your magick wand and point it toward the south, the region where the salamanders reside.
  13. Imagine you are drawing powerful energy in through the tip of your wand.
  14. You might see the wand glow or feel it tingle.
  15. Now turn the wand and aim it at yourself.
  16. Your movements should be strong and purposeful, not wimpy.
  17. Sense the energy you've attracted from the south flowing from the wand into your body.
  18. Feel yourself growing more powerful, more confident, more alive.
  19. Continue using your wand to pull energy and courage from the south in this manner for as long as you like.
  20. Remain in the center of the circle of candles until they have all burned down completely.
  21. Thank the salamanders for their assistance and release them.
  22. Open the circle and leave with renewed vitality and confidence.
