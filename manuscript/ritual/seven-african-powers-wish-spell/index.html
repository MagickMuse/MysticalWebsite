🧪Seven African Powers Oil

A seven-knob candle wish spell invoking the collective power of the Seven African Powers (orishas). Carve wishes into each of seven knobs, dress the candle with Seven African Powers Oil, and burn.

Quantities

1 seven-knob candle
Seven African Powers Oil

Directions

  1. Carve your wishes on each knob of a seven-knob candle.
  2. (The same wish may be repeated seven times, if desired.) Dress with Seven African Powers Oil and burn.

About This Spell

Among them, the Seven African Powers have mastery over all areas of life pertaining to human beings. They are like a celestial board of directors that can handle any problem, fix any dilemma, and fulfill every wish.

⚠️ Closed Practice Notice: This spell is rooted in Lucumí/Yoruba-derived African diaspora religion. Please research and respect the cultural context before working with the orishas.
