Chypre, a gambler's cologne named after Aphrodite's island of Cyprus, is worn to attract wealth and good fortune, especially when gambling. A heightened awareness of the scent mid-play is taken as a signal from the cologne.

Quantities

Chypre cologne (must contain rock rose / labdanum; oak moss is a frequent component)

Directions

  1. Wear Chypre cologne to attract wealth and general good fortune, especially when gambling.
  2. Close your eyes while applying it and make a quick wish or invocation.
  3. Pay attention to the fragrance during play.
  4. If the scent suddenly and unexpectedly intensifies, pay even closer attention: it may be offering players' tips.

Many colognes, like liqueurs, have their origins in magical potions and formulas. Chypre is the gambler's favorite. Named after Aphrodite's island of Cyprus, it allegedly also brings luck in business, the stock market, and anything having to do with money and finance.

Legend holds that the scent was brought to England by returning twelfth-century Crusaders in powder form.

To be true magical Chypre, the formula must contain rock rose (labdanum), strongly associated with Cyprus. Oak moss is also a frequent component for its power to draw cash to its wearer.

Instructions

Wear the cologne to attract wealth and general good fortune anytime, but especially when gambling. Close your eyes while applying it and make a quick wish or invocation.

Pay attention to the fragrance while playing. Normally you don't smell fragrance worn on your own body. If the fragrance suddenly and unexpectedly intensifies, pay even closer attention: it may be offering players' tips.

Note: The word "chypre" now designates a class of perfume broadly, and commercial chypres may lack any connection to this magical formula. For full potency, the formula must contain rock rose / labdanum.
