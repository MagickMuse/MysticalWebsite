A tenth-century English alternative to the Odin's Knot Binding Spell. Woden is Odin's Anglo-Saxon alter-ego; Baldur has vanished from this version. Most effective on Wednesday (Woden's Day). Seven knots are tied in black thread and wrapped around the injury while muttering the incantation.

Quantities

Black thread

Directions

  1. Make seven knots in a black thread.
  2. Wrap it around the injury or close to it, muttering:
  3. Then charmed Woden
    As well he knew how
    For bone sprain
    For blood sprain
    Bone to bone
    Blood to blood
    Limb to limb
    As though they were glued
  4. Best performed on Wednesday (Woden's Day) if possible.
