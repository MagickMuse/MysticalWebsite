🥥Coconut

Folk Magick

◇Directions
1. Carry the coconut to the front door, holding it in both hands. 2. Visualise every harmful force at work against you flowing outward through your hands and filling the coconut. 3. Picture the coconut's three dark spots as the Evil Eye itself, staring back from inside the shell. 4. When ready, open the front door and forcefully kick the coconut out into the street, commanding aloud that all evil depart, make the command emphatic. 5. Return inside immediately and carry out cleansing and protective workings for yourself.
◇Description
A coconut serves as a physical vessel to draw out and expel the Evil Eye and other harmful forces from the body, then remove them from the home entirely. The three dark spots on a coconut's surface echo a watching face, making it a natural container for removal work. This working may also be applied to reverse any other form of malevolent spell. One of the Evil Eye Reversal methods.
