🕯️Genealogy of Brigid

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PrayerDevotional WorkMeditation
◇Category
Prayer
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
No materials required
◇Directions
1. Speak aloud or silently: 2. "Every day and every night / That I say the genealogy of Brigid, / I shall not be killed, I shall not be harried, / I shall not be put in cell, I shall not be wounded, / No fire, no sun, no moon shall burn me, / No lake, no water, no sea shall drown me: / For I am child of Poetry; / Poetry, child of Reflection; / Reflection, child of Meditation; / Meditation, child of Lore; / Lore, child of Research; / Research, child of Great Knowledge; / Great Knowledge, child of Intelligence; / Intelligence, child of Comprehension; / Comprehension, child of Wisdom; / Wisdom, child of Brigid." 3. Use as a daily morning recitation, a grounding practice before entering ritual, or an anchor in moments of fear or overwhelm. 4. The effect is cumulative: each repetition deepens both the sense of Brigid's protection and one's own awareness of belonging within her lineage.
◇Description
An ancient Celtic incantation of protection that traces a lineage from Poetry back through Reflection, Meditation, Lore, Research, Great Knowledge, Intelligence, Comprehension, and Wisdom to Brigid herself. Recited daily or in moments of fear.
