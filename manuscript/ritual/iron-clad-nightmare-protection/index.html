🛡️Iron-Clad Nightmare Protection

Iron repels evil spirits, malevolent magick, and low-level spiritual entities during sleep. Silver works to a lesser degree. Multiple placement methods are described; use one or combine several.

Quantities

  • Iron, steel, or silver knife (or an amulet knife)
  • And/or: scissors
  • And/or: iron horseshoe
  • Optional: chain (for wearing an amulet knife around the neck)

Directions

  1. Choose one or more of the following methods.
  2. Place an iron, steel, or silver knife under your pillow; an amulet knife may be substituted and can also be worn around the neck on a chain, as can small scissor charms or any representation of a sharp metal implement.
  3. Alternatively, arrange a pair of scissors open and place them under the pillow.
  4. An iron horseshoe placed under the pillow wards off nightmares; if needed, place it across the chest instead.
