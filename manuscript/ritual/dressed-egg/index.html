MALEVOLENT WORKING. Documented for historical and protective knowledge only. A fertility hex in which a guinea hen egg is rolled in cayenne pepper and Goofer Dust, then boiled for an extended period in a pot of rainwater. Like the Dead Bee Bread hex, this working inverts a traditionally fertility-positive ingredient. Not intended for use.

Quantities

A guinea hen egg
Cayenne pepper
Goofer Dust
Rainwater

Directions

  1. Roll the guinea hen egg in cayenne pepper and Goofer Dust until well coated.
  2. Place it in a pot filled with rainwater and boil it for a long time.

Eggs are one of the most elemental symbols of fertility in existence. The guinea hen egg is specified rather than a common chicken egg, which suggests the working draws on some specific quality attributed to the guinea hen - a bird associated in various traditions with protection and the detection of malevolent presences.

Cayenne brings heat, aggression, and disruption to the coating. Goofer Dust is one of Hoodoo's most serious cursing agents, associated with illness, confusion, and harm. Rolling the egg in both substances dresses it (the Hoodoo term for coating an object with powders to embed a specific intention into its surface) with a compound specifically designed to harm.

Boiling the dressed egg in rainwater for an extended period extracts that compound into the water and destroys the egg's own fertile potential through heat and prolonged exposure. Rain water, naturally collected, is a neutral and powerful carrier in folk tradition. Here it becomes the medium in which the working develops.

⚠️
This is a historical hex documented for knowledge and self-protection only. It is presented here as context for understanding the full spectrum of fertility magic traditions, not as an instruction for use. Hexing another person's fertility without their knowledge or consent is considered among the most serious of malevolent workings.
