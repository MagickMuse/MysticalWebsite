📿Hathor's Mandrake and Poppy Necklace

◇Directions
1. Choose the form of the necklace: real plants strung together, a henna design painted on the body, or a beaded and bejeweled version crafted from artistic materials. 2. If working with real plants, string the mandrake and poppy together and weave knot magick into every knot of the cord during construction. 3. Wear the finished necklace during lovemaking for maximum effect. 4. If using real mandrake, keep it from resting against bare skin for extended periods.
◇Description
Reproduce and wear the ancient fertility necklace of Hathor, formed from mandrake and poppies, for maximum fertility and sexual power. Based on an ancient image of Hathor wearing this enchanted necklace.

An ancient image of Hathor, spirit of magick, pleasure, fertility, and women's power, depicts her wearing an enchanted necklace formed from mandrake and poppies. Poppy is also a fertility plant because of its abundant seeds. Both plants share narcotic properties and other similarities, though they are botanically distinct.

Method

Reproduce and wear Hathor's magick fertility power necklace. Three forms are possible:

  • Real plants: String actual mandrake and poppy, incorporating knot magick into the necklace's creation.
  • Henna design: Paint the necklace pattern onto the body in henna.
  • Beaded or bejeweled version: Craft an artistic version using beads or jewels to represent the botanicals.

For maximum effectiveness, wear the necklace while making love.

⚠️
If using real mandrake, do not allow it to contact skin for extended periods. Mandrake is toxic and should not be applied to the body directly.
