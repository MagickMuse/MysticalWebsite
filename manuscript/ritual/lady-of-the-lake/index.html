🌊Lady of the Lake

Every lake is presided over by a goddess or female nature spirit, often unnamed but always powerful. She may be called simply the Lady of the Lake. This spell honors her and sends your wish into her watery realm. Best cast at dawn on a misty morning, the spell bottle becomes a sacred offering tossed into her depths.

Quantities

  • A small glass bottle with a cork or cap
  • A red candle
  • A piece of paper with your wish written on it (optional: use colored ink to match intention, e.g., pink for romance, blue for justice, purple for wisdom)
  • Enough sunflower seeds to fill the bottle
    (alternatives: sunflower oil or petals if the bottle is too narrow)

Directions

  1. Preparation
  2. Enter a magickal mindset. Light the red candle and create sacred space.
  3. Write your wish on the paper. Roll it up and place it into the glass bottle.
  4. Fill the bottle with sunflower seeds (or oil/petals).
  5. Seal the bottle and drip candle wax over the cork to waterproof it.
  6. Set the bottle beside the candle. Concentrate on your wish as the candle burns down fully. The bottle is now charged and ready.
    The Spell
  7. Bring the spell bottle to a lake at dawn. Choose a serene and beautiful spot.
  8. Breathe deeply and connect with the lake's spirit. Raise power.
  9. Say aloud:
    Awake, awake, Lady of this Lake!
    I will give my wish for you to take.
    By the power of daybreak,
    Please grant it quickly, for my sake.
  10. Shake the bottle, and say:
    Lady of the Lake, awaken!
    Bottle shaken, bottle taken,
    Don't grant my wish if I'm mistaken.
    Lady of the Lake, awaken!
    Bottle shaken, bottle taken,
    Heart's wish unforsaken,
    No harm done when it's betaken!
  11. Throw the bottle into the lake as far as you can, and repeat:
    Awake, awake, Lady of this Lake!
    I give my wish for you to take.
    By the power of daybreak,
    Please grant it quickly, for my sake.
  12. Ground your energy. Sit quietly by the lake for a few moments. Then leave.
    Afterward
  13. Remember to thank the Lady of the Lake when your wish is granted.
  14. If the wish is not granted, understand that it may have caused harm if fulfilled.
  15. Reconsider the ethics or wording of your wish and ask again if necessary, focus on general desires (e.g., love, success) rather than specific outcomes.
  16. The bottle should never be retrieved. It is now a sacred offering.

Every lake has a presiding spirit, a goddess or female nature spirit who watches over its waters. You do not need to know her specific name to call upon her power. Simply addressing her as the Lady of the Lake opens the channel of communication between you and this ancient, watery presence.

Best Timing

This spell can be cast on any day when the lake is not frozen, but the most potent time is at dawn on a misty morning. The veil between worlds thins when mist hangs over the water, and the Lady of the Lake is most receptive to human wishes during this liminal hour.

Ink Correspondences

If you choose to write your wish in colored ink, select a shade that aligns with your intention:

  • Pink for romance
  • Dark blue for justice
  • Light blue for peace
  • Orange for action
  • Red for strength
  • Purple for wisdom
  • Green for healing or fertility

Preparing the Spell Bottle

Enter a magickal state of mind and gather your materials. Light the red candle and establish sacred space around your working area.

As you prepare the spell bottle, keep repeating your wish aloud. Roll up the paper containing your wish and slip it into the bottle. Fill the remaining space with sunflower seeds (or substitute sunflower oil or petals if the bottle's neck is too narrow). Seal the bottle and drip candle wax over the top until you have created a waterproof seal. Use as much wax as feels right to you, until the bottle looks magickal and feels enchanted in your hands.

Place the charged bottle beside the candle. Concentrate on your wish and monitor the candle until it burns itself out completely. Only then is the spell bottle ready to be offered.

Casting the Spell

Bring the bottle to a lake and choose a good spot from which to throw it into the water.

Breathe deeply. Listen to the sounds of the lake and any creatures who may be stirring. Notice the colors, scents, and beauty of your surroundings. Allow yourself to experience the magick of the place. Raise power and speak aloud:

Awake, awake, Lady of this Lake!
I will give my wish for you to take.
By the power of daybreak,
Please grant it quickly, for my sake.

Shake the spell bottle and say:

Lady of the Lake, awaken!
Bottle shaken, bottle taken,
Don't grant my wish if I'm mistaken.
Lady of this Lake, awaken!
Bottle shaken, bottle taken,
Heart's wish unforsaken,
No harm done when it's betaken!

Throw the bottle into the lake as far out as you can while repeating:

Awake, awake, Lady of this Lake!
I give my wish for you to take.
By the power of daybreak,
Please grant it quickly, for my sake.

Ground the power you raised. Contemplate the lake quietly for a few minutes, then leave.

Afterward

Remember to thank the Lady of the Lake when your wish is granted.

If your wish is not granted, understand that fulfilling it would have caused harm to you or to someone else. Meditate on your wish and examine it for ethical problems. Consider whether you can rephrase your desire in a way that causes no harm. For example, wish for the love of a good person rather than the love of a specific person, or wish for a better job rather than wishing for someone else's position. If you can formulate an alternate wish, ask the Lady of the Lake to grant that instead.

The bottle should never be retrieved. It is now a sacred offering.
