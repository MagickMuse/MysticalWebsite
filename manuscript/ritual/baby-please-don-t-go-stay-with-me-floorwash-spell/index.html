A version of the classic Stay With Me condition formula floorwash, made from a six-herb infusion and vinegar. Used to cleanse the home's thresholds and bedroom to forestall a partner's departure and revive love.

Quantities

Bloodroot; cardamom; coriander; cumin; forget-me-not; rosemary; vinegar; water

Directions

  1. Pour boiling water over: bloodroot, cardamom, coriander, cumin, forget-me-not, and rosemary to create a strong infusion.
  2. Strain out the botanical material, and add the liquid to a bucket of water together with some vinegar.
  3. Use it to cleanse your home, concentrating on thresholds to the outside and the bedroom.
  4. For maximum power, cast the spell while playing Lorraine Ellison's recording 'Stay With Me Baby.' Sing along or listen as inspired.
