A herbal protection bath to guard against psychic attacks and "bad vibes," using basil-infused water for purification and shielding.

Quantities

  • 1 large pot
  • 2 quarts of water
  • 1 large bunch of fresh basil
  • Epsom salts or sea salts (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat the water in the pot.
  2. Add the basil and let it simmer for several minutes.
  3. Allow the brew to cool.
  4. Strain the basil out and set it aside to dry; save it to use in other spells.
  5. Run a nice, hot bath.
  6. As the tub fills, slowly pour in the basil-infused water.
  7. If you like, you can add Epsom salts or sea salts; salt has protective and purifying properties, too.
  8. While you're doing this, say the following affirmation aloud:
  9. "I am now protected at all times,
    In all situations,
    Always and all ways."
  10. Soak in the bathwater for as long as you like.
  11. During this time, imagine yourself at peace, surrounded by pure white light, and sheltered from all harm.
