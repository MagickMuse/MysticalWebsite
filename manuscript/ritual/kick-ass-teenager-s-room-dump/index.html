🗑️Kick-Ass Teenager's Room Dump

A sixty-second kick-ass ritual if your teenager's room is a dump, using a timer and garbage bag threat to motivate immediate action through playful but firm boundary setting.

Quantities

  • 1 large garbage bag
  • Any device with a timer

Directions

  1. Stand outside your teen's room with your bag open and say: "Sixty seconds, just one minute, tidy up, or all goes in it." Set your timer, knock, enter the room, and start putting items and dirty clothes in the bag, saying the spell words.
  2. When the timer beeps or your child's protests act as the spell energy release, stop, empty the items on the floor, and say: "Just one more hour, sort it or I will and can deliver it to the garbage truck." Leave your teenager to sort out the mess and, after an hour, stand in the doorway with your bag as a reminder until action begins or continues.
