🌹Friday Passion Restoration Spell

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickIncense MagickChant/MantraVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- One red candle - Fresh or dried rose petals - Rose, strawberry, or musk incense with holder
◇Directions
1. Perform this spell in your bedroom on a Friday evening when you wish to restore passion to your committed relationship or maintain the existing flame of desire. 2. Light the incense and allow the fragrant smoke to fill the room, creating a sensual atmosphere. 3. Light the red candle and place it in a safe location in your bedroom. 4. Take a handful of rose petals in your hand. 5. Pass them through the candle flame three times, being careful not to ignite them. 6. As you do this, visualize passion and desire returning to or intensifying in your relationship. 7. Pass the petals through the rising incense smoke three times while speaking: 8. "Flame of passion, burn bright and true, Desire renewed between us two, Love's fire burning, strong and long, Our bond of passion ever strong." 9. Scatter the rose petals across your bed or around your bedroom. 10. Allow the candle and incense to burn completely if safe to do so, or extinguish them after your working. 11. Repeat this ritual on any Friday when you feel the need to tend to the passionate aspect of your relationship. 12. Regular practice keeps the energy flowing.
◇Description
A bedroom ritual to restore or maintain passion and desire in a committed relationship, performed on Fridays using red candles, rose petals, and incense to reignite the flame of love.
