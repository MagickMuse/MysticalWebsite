🏃Running for Special Opportunity Decision

◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A circle of gold candles - A picture of yourself smiling in the middle of the candles - A medium-size mirror to hold and later prop up on a flat surface
◇Directions
1. Light each candle clockwise, one from the other, saying for each: "Extra good luck with each candle glow, extra good luck that all may be so: [name urgent need or desire]. So will I shine when good fortune is mine." Prop the mirror so that you can see the candle circle within it and say: "Twice the luck with twice the light, this chance of mine will turn out right." Let the candles burn, with the mirror still reflecting the light. 2. In the morning, polish the mirror, repeating all the spell words. 3. Hang it where morning light is reflected in it. 4. Every time you pass the mirror, look into it, smile, and say: "So will I shine when good fortune is mine." Perform after dark.
◇Description
An after dark spell if you are in running for special opportunity or offer and need decision in your favor soon, lighting gold candles in circle around picture with mirror for twice the luck with twice the light.
