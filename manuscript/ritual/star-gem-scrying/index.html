A gemstone scrying method using star sapphires, star rubies, or staurolite (cross-stone or fairy-stone). Star sapphires and rubies are the most luminous and potent. Staurolite carries profound magickal power but its lesser luminosity makes the work more challenging.

Quantities

  • Star sapphire, star ruby, or staurolite (cross-stone / fairy-stone)

Directions

  1. Gaze at the center of the gemstone's cross or asterism, with eyes slightly out of focus.
  2. Allow your mind to wander freely and let the stone reveal whatever it will.
  3. Do not direct or force the experience.
  4. If using staurolite, be patient with the process, as its lower luminosity requires more time and receptivity than star sapphires or rubies.
