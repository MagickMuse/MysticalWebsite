Lodestone

A Spanish crossroads spell (with Romany, Jewish, and Christian influences) invoking Maria Padilla and Asmodeus, King of Demons. Powdered lodestone is added to aguardiente and drunk before sleep, followed by a ten-line chant offering three black cheeses to the Lodestone of Power, Maria Padilla, and Asmodeus in exchange for the beloved being brought to the caster.

Quantities

A tiny bit of lodestone or lodestone dust
Aguardiente

Directions

  1. This Spanish spell evokes Maria Padilla together with Asmodeus, King of Demons; it is a crossroads spell with Romany, Jewish, and Christian influences.
  2. Powder a tiny bit of lodestone or obtain a little lodestone dust.
  3. Add the powder to some aguardiente and drink it before going to sleep.
  4. Chant the following:
  5. To the Mount of Olives one day I did ride
    Three little black goats before me I spied
    Those three little goats on three carts I laid
    Three black cheeses from their milk I made
    One I bestow on the Lodestone of Power
    So that it will save me from all ills this very hour
    The second to Maria Padilla I give
    And to all of her ladies about her who live
    The third I fetch for Asmodeus lame
    That he fetch for me whomever I name!
  6. Name your lover out loud.
