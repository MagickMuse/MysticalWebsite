🗺️Get Lost and Far Away!

A thirteen-day banishing and boundary-setting spell using Lost and Away Powder and War Water. The target's name is written thirteen times, powdered, sealed in red wax, buried, watered daily, and finally burned on the fourteenth day.

Quantities

  • Square of paper
  • Pen
  • Lost and Away Powder
  • Red sealing wax
  • War Water (enough to water daily for 13 days)

Directions

  1. Lost and Away Powder has various uses in addition to banishment - it also establishes personal and psychic boundaries, and prevents someone from encroaching on those boundaries.
  2. Write your target's name thirteen times on a square of paper.
  3. Sprinkle Lost and Away Powder on this paper.
  4. Fold the paper up, always folding away from you.
  5. Seal it with sealing wax, preferably red.
  6. Bury this paper but mark the spot.
  7. Leave it buried for thirteen days, watering daily with War Water.
  8. On the fourteenth day, dig it up and burn it.
