🕯️Photograph Powder Debt Spell

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RitePowder
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickPowder Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
• A photograph of the debtor, or a paper bearing their written name • Powdered sweet flag (calamus) • Powdered licorice • Powdered bayberry • A green candle, or a green devil candle
◇Directions
1. Set a photograph of the debtor atop a plate, substituting a paper bearing their written name if no photo is available. 2. Dust the image with powdered sweet flag, licorice, and bayberry. 3. Stand a green candle (a green devil candle also works) on the powdered photograph and let it burn completely. 4. Focus on sending a mental message to the debtor for the duration of the burn.
◇Description
A photograph-and-powder debt-collection spell: a debtor's image is dusted with powdered sweet flag, licorice, and bayberry, then burned beneath a green candle while the caster mentally wills them to pay. ⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick. Approach with respect and awareness of its cultural origins.
