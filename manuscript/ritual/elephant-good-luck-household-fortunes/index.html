A feng shui ritual placing elephant figurines with gold coins at entrances and stairs to keep household fortune rising.

Quantities

  • 1 small model elephant for each external door
  • 1 elephant for bottom of stairs (if applicable)
  • 1 elephant for top of stairs (if applicable)
  • 1 gold-colored coin per elephant

Directions

  1. Set an elephant inside each entrance to the right as you enter.
  2. Hold a gold coin for each in turn, saying, "Good luck I call, elephant increase this gold for me that I may see a golden future every day in every way." Place the coin under the elephant, repeating the words.
  3. If you have stairs, set elephants at the top and bottom, putting a coin underneath each one, repeating the spell words.
  4. Touch the elephant at the bottom of the stairs, saying, "Lucky elephant, may my fortunes rise, so the only limit is the skies." Touch the elephant at the top, saying, "So does good fortune grow, higher and higher, as to new luck I daily inspire." If you do not have stairs, set the elephants in the main living area, one on the floor and one high up.
  5. Perform Thursday.
