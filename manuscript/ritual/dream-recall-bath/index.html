A pre-sleep bath using juniper, lavender, and mimosa essential oils to encourage dreams to linger longer upon waking, making them easier to remember and record.

Quantities

  • 4 drops juniper berry essential oil
  • 4 drops lavender essential oil
  • 4 drops mimosa essential oil

Directions

  1. Draw a full tub of warm water.
  2. Add the juniper, lavender, and mimosa essential oils to the bath.
  3. Soak before going to sleep.
