🧸Hexing An Enemy With A Doll

⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A seven-night poppet hex. A black doll is dressed with Dragon's Blood Ink, Crossing Powder or Oil, and bound with black cord while chanting. Black Arts incense is burned nightly at midnight. On the eighth day the doll is burned or buried.

Quantities

1 piece of parchment paper
Dragon's Blood Ink
1 black poppet (stuffed with dead flowers, grass, or leaves)
Glue, tape, or a pin
Crossing Powder or Crossing Oil
Black thread, ribbon, or cord
1 square of black cloth
Black Arts incense

Directions

  1. Write the target's name on parchment paper with Dragon's Blood Ink.
  2. Attach the paper securely to a black poppet stuffed with dead flowers, grass, or leaves using glue, tape, or a pin.
  3. Sprinkle the doll with Crossing Powder or anoint with Crossing Oil, rubbing it into whichever area you wish to affect: the head for headaches, the arms for numbness or injury, or the torso for digestive ailments.
  4. Tie black thread, ribbon, or cord around the doll's midsection, and as you draw it tight, repeat seven times as quickly as possible the binding chant.
  5. Place the doll on a square of black cloth and light Black Arts incense.
  6. While it burns, focus on the target and chant the hexing incantation.
  7. When done, wrap the doll in the cloth and pin it securely so that it is completely enclosed.
  8. Hide it in a secret place where no one will find it.
  9. Repeat the ritual for seven consecutive nights, preferably at midnight.
  10. On the eighth day, burn the doll and scatter the ashes, or bury it where it will never be found.
