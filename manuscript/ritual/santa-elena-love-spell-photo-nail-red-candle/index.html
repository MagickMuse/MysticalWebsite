🕯️Santa Elena Love Spell Photo Nail Red Candle

A red candle is dedicated to Santa Elena, placed before the beloved's photograph with a nail laid on top. Desires are stated explicitly. After the candle burns down, the wax remnants, photograph, and nail are wrapped together and hidden in a safe place such as under the mattress.

Quantities

Red candle
Photograph of the one you love
A nail

Directions

  1. Dedicate a red candle to Santa Elena.
  2. Place a photograph of the one you love before the candle.
  3. Place a nail on top of the photograph.
  4. Express your desires to Santa Elena explicitly and lucidly.
  5. When the candle has burned down, wrap up any wax remnants together with the photograph and nail and hide them in a safe place, such as under your mattress.
