⚠️ HISTORICAL SPELL ONLY. A traditional Guernsey recipe for seeing a sorcerer for who they truly are. The brew uses toxic plant material; daffodil bulbs are poisonous and the concoction must never be consumed in any form. Recorded here for historical and educational reference only.

Quantities

  • Roots of small sage
  • Roots of large sage
  • Pith of elder
  • Daffodil bulbs
  • Vinegar

Directions

  1. Combine the roots of both small and large sage, the pith of elder, and the daffodil bulbs in a vessel with vinegar.
  2. Leave the door of your home or working space open.
  3. Time the brew so that it comes to a boil at exactly a quarter of an hour before noon.
  4. As the first bubbles appear, the sorcerer you wish to identify will make an appearance.
  5. Watch closely.
  6. ⚠️ Daffodil bulbs are toxic.
  7. Do not ingest the brew or any portion of it.
