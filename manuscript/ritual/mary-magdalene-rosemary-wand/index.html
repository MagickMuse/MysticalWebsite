🌹Mary Magdalene Rosemary Wand

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
DivinationDreamwork
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
• Wine • Vinegar • Water • A rosemary wand (a thick sprig or bundle of rosemary) • A bowl
◇Directions
1. This ritual is performed only on the Eve of Mary Magdalene's feast day: the night of July 21st. 2. Blend wine, vinegar, and water together in a bowl beside your bed. 3. Soak the rosemary wand in the bowl until thoroughly saturated. 4. Gently shake off the excess liquid. 5. Place the damp wand between your breasts and go to bed. 6. Do not say another word until morning, to allow the prophetic dreams to arrive undisturbed.
◇Description
A British dream oracle performed only on the Eve of Mary Magdalene's feast day (the night of July 21st). A rosemary wand soaked in a blend of wine, vinegar, and water is placed between the breasts before sleep to produce prophetic dreams. No words may be spoken after the wand is placed.
