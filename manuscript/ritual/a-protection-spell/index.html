🛡️A Protection Spell

◇Quantities
- 1 blue candle - Salt (enough to form a circle around the candle and cards) - Incense (optional, as a deity offering) - Playing cards: Four of Diamonds, Nine of Hearts, Ace of Spades
◇Directions
1. Begin this spell between sunset on Saturday and sunset on Sunday, a period governed by the Sun, the most protective of the planetary bodies. 2. This working is well suited for times when you suspect someone has directed negative energy your way or when you have simply been experiencing persistent misfortune. 3. Light a blue candle. 4. If you wish, light incense as well, offered to a protective deity with whom you share a genuine connection. 5. Gather three playing cards: the Four of Diamonds, the Nine of Hearts, and the Ace of Spades. 6. Place the Nine of Hearts directly in front of the candle; it serves as the focal point of the spell and represents protection. 7. Set the Four of Diamonds to one side, symbolizing stability and a firm foundation. 8. On the opposite side, place the Ace of Spades, which stands for triumph over obstacles and adverse influences. 9. The arrangement mirrors a three-card reading, with the flanking cards clarifying and bolstering the central intention. 10. Together, the three cards form a clear statement: you are protected, you are grounded, and you will overcome any harm directed your way. 11. As you lay each card down, meditate carefully on what it contributes to the working: protection, stability, triumph. 12. Focusing your thoughts on the meaning of each card is essential. 13. The power of the spell comes from within you; the cards are tools that help channel and direct that inner strength. 14. Once the cards are in place, surround both the candle and the cards with a circle of salt. 15. As you pour the salt, use galdor to sharpen your intent. 16. If the moon is waxing, concentrate on building and strengthening your natural defenses. 17. If the moon is waning, focus instead on diminishing the obstacles and challenges before you. 18. While forming the salt circle, speak these words: 19. "Circle of safety, salt of the sea, cast out all harm. So let it be!" 20. The repeated s sounds echo the Sigil rune (Sowilo), the sun rune of guidance and protection from the Old English Rune Poem.
◇Description
A playing card protection spell combining the Four of Diamonds, Nine of Hearts, and Ace of Spades within a circle of salt, designed to ward off negative influences and break streaks of misfortune.
