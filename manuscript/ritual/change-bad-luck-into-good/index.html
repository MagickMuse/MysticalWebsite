🔄Change Bad Luck into Good

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickLunarChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A small purple candle - A larger white one, with the purple candle to the left - A small bowl of salt - A paper knife
◇Directions
1. Scratch the purple candle with the knife, writing vertically (from top to bottom), BAD LUCK AWAY, AND THEN WRITE ON THE WHITE CANDLE, GOOD LUCK STAY. 2. Light the purple candle, saying: "Take away sorrow with the dying of the moon." Light the white candle from the purple one, saying: "Good luck, bright luck, come to me, [areas of life where you need good luck most]." Add a pinch of salt to the white candle's flame, saying: "Burn bright and light the way to good fortune [name areas where you most need luck]." Extinguish the purple candle, saying: "Take bad luck away with the dying of the moon." Let the white candle burn a few minutes, then blow it out, saying: "I am lucky and will even luckier be." Leave the candles in place until the next spell. 3. You do not need to write the words again on the candle in subsequent spells. 4. Perform three times during the waning moon.
◇Description
A three times during waning moon spell to change bad luck into good by using waning moon, lighting purple and white candles with salt to take away sorrow and burn bright to good fortune.
