🎈Balloon Banishing

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Banishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• Balloon • Slip of paper and writing instrument
◇Directions
1. Write the target's name on a slip of paper. 2. Insert the paper into a balloon before or after blowing it up, whichever is simpler. 3. Take the balloon to an appropriate outdoor location and release it.
◇Description
A simple sympathetic banishing spell in which the target's name is written on paper, sealed inside a balloon, and released to carry them away.
