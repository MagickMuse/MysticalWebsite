⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A jar spell that captures a target's energy and puts their life on hold. A pentacle drawn in marker dissolves in ash-and-dirt water surrounding a garlic-filled jar nested inside a larger one. The dissolving ink powers the spell over time. The target cannot accomplish anything until the spell is broken or ended. Must be recharged monthly.

Quantities

2 glass jars with lids (1 small enough to fit inside the larger)
3 cloves of garlic
Wood ash (a few pinches)
Dirt (a few pinches)
Water
1 piece of white paper
1 heavy black marker

Directions

  1. Choose two jars so that the smaller one fits entirely inside the larger one, lids included. Use a heavy black marker to draw a pentacle on a piece of white paper, then cut it out to fit inside the bottom of the large jar. Place three cloves of garlic inside the smaller jar and tighten the lid. Speak the opening words of the spell.
    Set the small jar inside the large one, on top of the pentacle paper. Mix ash and dirt in water and pour the mixture into the large jar, enough to cover the smaller jar. Speak the second part of the incantation.
    As the ink washes off the paper into the water, the spell gains energy and begins to take effect within a few days. Leave the entire thing on your altar or hidden in a dark closet to keep working. Repeat the incantation over the jars after a month to recharge if needed.
