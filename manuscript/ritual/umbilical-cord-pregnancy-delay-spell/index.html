🪢Umbilical Cord Pregnancy Delay Spell

🌱Fertility

A postpartum pregnancy delay working performed immediately after birth, traditionally by a pre-arranged midwife or helper. Knots are tied into the umbilical cord (still attached to the placenta) equal to the number of years desired before the next pregnancy. The knotted cord and placenta are then buried together in the earth. Prior arrangements with whoever will perform the working must be made before the birth.

Quantities

The umbilical cord (still attached to the placenta)
A designated midwife or helper (pre-arranged)

Directions

  1. Before the birth, tell your midwife or designated helper how many years you wish to wait before the next pregnancy.
  2. Immediately after delivery, while the cord is still attached to the placenta, they tie that number of knots into the cord.
  3. The knotted cord and placenta are then buried together in the earth.

Like the Hills and Valleys spell, this working operates through a precise count: one knot, one year. The structure is the same: a decision is made in advance, and the working enacts that specific number. The clarity of intent is built into the mechanism.

The cord is the most intimate connection between two bodies that biology produces. Using it as the medium for a knot spell is extraordinarily direct. The cord that linked the child to the mother is now tied in a pattern that holds the space before the next child. The same substance that was the channel of one life becomes the vessel for measured time.

Pre-arrangement with the midwife is mandatory. This working cannot be improvised in the delivery room. The practitioner must plan it before labor, communicate it clearly, and trust the person designated to perform it. That prior communication is part of the working's integrity.

Burying cord and placenta together closes the working with the same earthen finality as the other placenta spells in this tradition. Earth holds what is placed in it.
