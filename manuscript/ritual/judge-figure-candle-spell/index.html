⚖️Court Case

A Hoodoo court case candle working in which a figure candle representing the judge is carved with identifying details, dressed with Friendly Judge Oil and optionally a commanding oil, then burned incrementally throughout the duration of proceedings. A devil candle in black or red may be substituted if the judge is known to be difficult. Hoodoo is a closed practice with African American cultural and spiritual roots, approach with respect.

Quantities

  • 1 figure candle (male or female, to represent the judge)
  • Friendly Judge Oil (for dressing)
  • Commanding Oil, optional

Directions

  1. Select a figure candle that best matches the judge's appearance.
  2. If the judge is known to be hostile or combative, substitute a black or red devil candle in place of the standard figure.
  3. Carve the judge's identifying information directly into the wax, their courtroom number, chamber number, or any other details available to you.
  4. Dress the carved candle with Friendly Judge Oil, working the oil into the surface.
  5. If desired, add a commanding oil on top.
  6. Begin burning the candle incrementally as soon as possible, continuing throughout the duration of proceedings.
  7. If the candle burns down entirely before the case concludes, begin with a fresh candle dressed in the same manner.
