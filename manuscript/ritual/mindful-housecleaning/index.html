Ritual Grimoire

🧹Mindful Housecleaning

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Meditation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Your usual cleaning tools
◇Directions
1. Before you begin, gather your cleaning tools with full attention. 2. Feel your intention flow from heart and hands through each tool and into your home. 3. Consider your cleansers, their scent, their environmental impact. 4. Consider the colors of your sponges and what they might mean: pink for happiness, green for healing, white for purification. 5. Dedicate your intention to make the work purposeful on a spiritual level as well as a practical one. 6. Begin at the front door. 7. Wash the woodwork, polish the doorknobs, sweep the threshold. 8. Refresh any blessing items at this first threshold. 9. Wash all the doors in the house in the same focused way, meditating as you go on the sacred nature of doors and Brigid's power there: opening, enclosing, welcoming, protecting. 10. Move to your windows. 11. As you clean them, give thanks for vision, clarity, perspective, and freedom. 12. Give thanks for your view, whatever it is. 13. Think of those who are imprisoned and have no view, and remember that Brigid comforts the oppressed. 14. Find something to love in each picture framed by each window. 15. Continue through your rooms, tidying, organizing, discarding, preserving, holding the intention that you are claiming every element of your home as sacred. 16. Not just the candles and icons, but the cutting board and the screen. 17. Charge the armchair to hold you with love; the plate to bless your food with healing; the rug to energize you as you walk upon it. 18. Feel the warm embrace of Brigid's cloak in sheets fresh from the dryer, folded with blessed intent. 19. What a blessing to have a home to clean. 20. What bliss to have a bed to make cozy, a kitchen that can get messy with the chaos of preparing a feast. 21. This is Brigid working through you to brighten every task with joy and gratitude.
◇Description
A practice for transforming everyday housecleaning into sacred work through focused attention and intention, claiming each part of the home as Brigid's domain and charging domestic objects with devotional energy. One of the Brigid's Broom practices.
