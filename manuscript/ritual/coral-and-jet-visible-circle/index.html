⭕Coral and Jet Visible Circle

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Coral pieces or beads Jet pieces or beads Amber (optional, in place of or alongside coral)
◇Directions
1. Lay out a circle of alternating coral and jet pieces. 2. If you prefer, swap the coral for amber, or alternate all three materials in the circle.
◇Description
A visible protective circle laid out in alternating coral and jet, two materials whose combined power is said to grow far beyond what either offers alone. Amber may replace the coral, or all three may be used together.
