🧸Pins and Needles Wax Doll Hex

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Romani/Romany
◇Quantities
Candle wax (melted) Bits of the target's clothing Intimate items (hair, nail clippings, etc.) if available Needle
◇Directions
1. Create a figure to represent your target from melted candle wax. 2. Add bits of the target's clothing and any intimate items you may possess: hair, nail clippings, and so forth. 3. Allow the doll to harden. 4. Prick it with a needle as the spirit moves you, but always in a series of three or nine.
◇Description
A Hungarian Romany version of the ancient and international tradition of pierced wax dolls (often misnamed 'voodoo dolls,' a term that maligns the Vodou religion). A wax figure representing the target is created, personalized with intimate items, and pricked with a needle in series of three or nine.
