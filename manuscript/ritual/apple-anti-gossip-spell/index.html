✨Apple Anti-Gossip Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- Apple juice (glass) - Three cloves - Paper napkin - Writing implement (feather quill, chopstick, knitting needle)
◇Directions
1. Grab a glass of apple juice. 2. Drop in three cloves to nail it. 3. Prepare your writing implement. Use it like a quill pen, dipping it in the apple juice. 4. On a paper napkin, write: Harmful talk, take a walk Mind your business and stop the squawk! Let words fade, rumors forget And truth and serenity reset 5. Wipe your lips with the napkin and say the spell aloud or to yourself before throwing the napkin away. This will help you even if you are the victim. One of the worst things you can do when gossip starts is engage with it.
◇Description
The gossip's apple is a longstanding tradition centered on this purpose: like the pomander that staved off illness, a clove-studded apple was a charm to still tongues. The apple's golden juice is a nod to the Golden Rule. Whether you're the victim or perpetrator of gossip, use this spell to stop it in its tracks.
