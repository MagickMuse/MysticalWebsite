🐍Snake Bag Fertility Spell

◇Form
PouchCharged Object
◇Method
Herbal MagickManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Any combination of: black cohosh, serpentaria, rattlesnake root, rattlesnake master, adder's tongue, bistort (snakeweed), snake head • Any additional roots or twigs that resemble a serpent in shape • Snake charms and/or carved stone fetishes (optional) • A piece of freely shed snakeskin (optional) • An iridescent beaded bag to hold everything
◇Directions
1. Gather as many of the listed botanicals as you can obtain. 2. Add any unnamed root or twig whose shape suggests a serpent. 3. Snake charms, carved fetishes, and a piece of shed snakeskin may all be included. 4. Pack everything into an iridescent beaded bag. 5. Carry the bag throughout the day and sleep with it beneath your pillow at night.
◇Description
A carried amulet bag assembled from botanicals whose very names invoke serpent power, a reflection of the ancient connection between snakes and women's fertility and reproductive health. The bag is carried by day and kept under the pillow by night. ⚠️ Carry these botanicals only. Do not consume them. Several are unsafe during pregnancy and should never be used as medicine without qualified expert supervision.
