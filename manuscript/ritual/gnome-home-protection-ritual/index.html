🏠Gnome Home Protection Ritual

💒Holy Water

A ritual to invite gnomes into your space to act as guardians of your property and home. By creating a dedicated outdoor space with wildflower seeds, blessed water, and clay portal doors, you welcome the earth elementals to lend their fierce protective energy to your boundaries.

Quantities

  • Blessed water (Moon water or freshly blessed water)
  • Wildflower seeds
  • Clay to create 2 small doors, or 2 premade "fairy" doors
  • Coloring page with gnomes and coloring medium of choice

Directions

  1. Gather the items you will need to create sacred space for your spellwork.
  2. See in your mind's eye the space surrounded by a soft white light that creates a bubble around you and your workspace.
  3. Say:
  4. In this space and in this hour, I welcome the guardians of earth, the gnomes.
  5. You are welcome in all my spaces.
  6. Sprinkle the seeds on the soil on the ground or in the pot.
  7. Blessing the Water (if not using pre-blessed water):
    Take a container of water and present the bowl to the quarters, saying:
  8. Lord and Lady, bless this water for this rite.
  9. Allow it to bless us with the power of connection.
  10. Creating the Portal Doors:
    If using clay, create 2 small doors. One door will be for the outside space and the other for inside your home. This creates a portal for the gnomes to move freely through. Once completed, place the "outdoors" door in the seeded area or your pot. After the spell, take the other one and place it somewhere in your home (the kitchen works well).
  11. Coloring Meditation:
    While sitting, take the coloring page and work to create a beautiful space for your gnomes. Find the five gnomes as you color. Five is a number associated with strength and protection. Give each gnome a unique look, and as you color each one, say:
  12. Gather the energies of the earth and guard our home.
  13. Closing:
    Close the circle by seeing the protective bubble dissipate and say:
  14. Welcome home.
  15. The gnomes will see the effort made to welcome them, and they will travel between their wild space and the home, lending their protection to all inside your boundaries.

About Gnomes

Gnomes are earth elementals deeply connected to the energies of the ground. They can tap into and channel that strength to act as guardians of property and home. Though they prefer wilder areas, they will take up residence in gardens, planters, and inside homes. They are typically shy and often silent, but when they speak, their warnings should be heeded.

Inviting gnomes into a space earns their protection in return.

Creating Sacred Space

To collaborate closely with the gnomes, show them they are welcome in your space. To do that, you will want to create a small space outside that is just theirs. One way to do this is to find a small corner of your yard to dedicate as their space. If you have a small apartment, you can create a mini space as part of a potted plant.

The Portal System

The two doors create a portal for the gnomes to move freely between their outdoor wild space and your home interior. This allows them to patrol and protect all areas within your boundaries.

The Power of Five

Five is a number associated with strength and protection. By finding and coloring five gnomes, each with a unique appearance, you're invoking the protective power of this number while creating a visual welcome for the earth elementals.
