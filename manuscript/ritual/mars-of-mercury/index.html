At times our intellect must be turned to overcoming obstacles and foes. This is a spell for cleverness at such times.

Quantities

  • No materials required

Directions

  1. Perform this invocation on the day of Mercury at the hour of Mars.
  2. Vibrate the vowels Epsilon followed by Omicron (EH-OH), with the day vowel shorter in duration followed by a longer vibration of the hour vowel.
  3. Then speak the invocation:
  4. Clever Mind and Conquering Might
    Combine and bring triumph in conflict
    May tactical thinking assist my offence
    May intellect and invention inform my defense
    Severity and Strength shall serve as my sword
    Strategy and Skill shall serve as my shield
