🍯Honey Pot Profitability Ritual

◇Form
JarCandle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- A jar of honey - A spoon - A beeswax candle or yellow wax candle
◇Directions
1. Light the candle, saying: "Lady Mother, honeybee, shine your sweetness down on me. As bee to nectar, butterfly to flower, will customers be drawn to me by my new selling power, and persuasively with honeyed words will orders on me shower." Put some honey on the spoon. 2. Hold it up to the light and say: "Lady Mother, honeybee, I offer you your own that your sweetness on me shower." Eat a little honey from the spoon, repeating both chants. 3. Return the lid to the honey and wash the spoon in hot water, as you do so saying both sets of words. 4. Let the candle burn down next to the honey pot. 5. Eat some honey before taking or offering goods or services for sale. 6. Perform in sunshine or extra candlelight.
◇Description
A honey pot ritual to increase profitability if customers are slow to commit, using honey and beeswax candle with honeyed words to draw customers like bees to nectar.
