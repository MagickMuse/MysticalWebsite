👼Pregnancy After Everything Angel Barbelo

A Sunday light-breaking spell for pregnancy when tried everything and almost given up hope, using crystal quartz in sun water to call Angel Barbelo for gift of child with fertilizing fire.

Quantities

  • An oval or round crystal quartz and a pointed one
  • A glass bowl half filled with water

Directions

  1. Drop the oval quartz into the water, stirring the water in all directions with the pointed quartz, saying: "Barbelo, wise angel who brings us gifts most needed and desired, fill my womb with your fertilizing fire, that we may be inspired with the gift of a child, for we will be the most loving parents." Leave the bowl in the light until noon.
  2. Touch the round crystal still in the water with the pointed one, saying: "So are we joined in love. Barbelo, bring forth our child; we ask, for we will be the most loving parents." Filter the sun water into clear glass bottles and every morning and evening add to bathwater or splash on your breasts and womb, repeating all the spell words.
  3. Tip any left on the following Sunday onto growing plants and, if you wish, repeat the spell, making new water.
  4. Perform Sunday, when light breaks through.
