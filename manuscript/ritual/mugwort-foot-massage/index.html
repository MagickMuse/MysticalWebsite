💤Mugwort Foot Massage

A bedtime foot massage using warmed walnut or hazelnut oil with mugwort and/or morning glory flower remedies, designed to aid dream recall and help clarify and interpret dreams upon waking. One of the Dream Recall methods.

Quantities

  • 1 tablespoon walnut oil or hazelnut oil
  • 2–3 drops mugwort flower remedy (FES) and/or morning glory flower remedy (FES)

Directions

  1. Gently and carefully warm a tablespoon of walnut or hazelnut oil.
  2. Bring it to your bedside.
  3. Add two or three drops of mugwort flower remedy and/or morning glory flower remedy to the warmed oil.
  4. Massage the blend into the soles of your feet before sleep.
