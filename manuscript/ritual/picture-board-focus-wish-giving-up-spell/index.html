🖼️Picture Board Focus Wish Giving Up Spell

An ongoing spell for picture board ritual to focus on wish if you feel like giving up, adding images of fulfilled dream while clapping and calling wish into being until board is full and wish moves toward actuality.

Quantities

  • A picture board
  • Images printed from the Internet and cut from magazines showing people fulfilling the dream or places and items connected with it

Directions

  1. Set the picture board flat.
  2. Write your wish and time frame over and over invisibly on the whole board in large letters with the index finger of the hand you write with.
  3. Add the first picture, saying: "This is my wish manifest in actuality, with courage I shall bring it to reality." Clap your hands after affixing each picture, saying: "I call my wish into being." Continue to add pictures, saying the words and clapping until you have covered half the board.
  4. Hang the board somewhere warm, such as the kitchen.
  5. Whenever you doubt your dream, add more pictures, repeating the words and actions until the board is full and your wish begins moving toward actuality.
  6. Perform ongoing.
