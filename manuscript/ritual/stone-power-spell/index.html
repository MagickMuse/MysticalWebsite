A Full Moon spell performed on a day of Mars during the hour of the Sun. Three stones of aventurine, carnelian, and smoky quartz are placed in a bowl of spring water and set outdoors so the moon reflects in the water. The caster dances around the bowl with wand raised, chanting to the powers of Luna, Sol, and Aries. At peak energy, the wand is pointed at the bowl to empower the stones. The charged water is stored as a success and power elixir, and the stones are wrapped in black cloths for later use as power boosters in spells and charms.

Quantities

  • 1 bowl
  • Spring water
  • 1 aventurine stone
  • 1 carnelian stone
  • 1 smoky quartz stone
  • 1 wand
  • 1 jar
  • Black cloths

Directions

  1. Place the stones of aventurine, carnelian, and smoky quartz in a bowl of spring water.
  2. Set the bowl outside so the moon is reflected in the water.
  3. Speak:
  4. Here on the day of Mars, in the hour of the Sun, I call down the Moon to charge and energize these stones!
  5. With wand in hand and arms raised, dance around the bowl chanting:
  6. Power of Luna, Power of Sol; Power of Aries, Power I call!
  7. When the energy level is at its highest, grasp the wand in both hands and point it at the bowl.
  8. Speak:
  9. Empowered Be!
  10. Drain the water into a jar and store for use as a success and power elixir.
  11. Remove the stones, wrap each in a black cloth, and put away until needed for an extra power boost in spells and charms.
