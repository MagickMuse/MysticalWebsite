🪢Secret Sortilege of the Seven Knots of Love

A historically popular Folk Christian binding spell combining knot magick, medieval high magick, and invocations to Saint Martha the Dominator and Saint Anthony. Seven knots are tied in a 12-inch red silk ribbon with specific incantations at each knot, silver charms attached, and the completed circle worn on the left arm for 14 nights while maintained on an altar dedicated to the saints.

Quantities

Red silk ribbon (~12 inches long)
Two silver charms (one representing the target, one representing yourself, silver head figures, male or female)
Leftover snipped ribbon (for attaching charms)
Altar materials for Saint Martha the Dominator and Saint Anthony
Final carry charm: silver heart pierced by a sword with three crosses, engraved with the word 'Blessed'

Directions

  1. Obtain a red silk ribbon approximately 12 inches long. Seven knots will be tied approximately one inch apart.
  2. Tie the first knot in the center. Say: "With this first knot, I tie you up [Name], child of [Name] in a magick circle, enclosed by the force of my love and will."
  3. Tie the second knot about one inch to the right of the first. Say: "This second knot binds your will [Name], child of [Name] with mine with the force of steel. You will not say or do anything that does not correspond with my desire."
  4. Tie the third knot about one inch to the left of the center knot. Say: "With this third knot I tie up your love and hold it firmly to mine. You will not be able to break this love, regardless of your intention. You will not break it. You will not loosen it. You will not shake it. I do not wish your energy to be weakened."
  5. Tie the fourth knot one inch past the second, to the right. Say: "Your thoughts are completely bound to my own. You will never be able to remove from your mind the image of me that follows you lovingly wherever you go and wherever you are. My love and my just desires are executed as I desire now. I demand this by the force and power of the Secret Sortilege. I demand this by the ardent petition of love that I dedicate to Saint Martha the Dominator and to Saint Anthony, so that they are my proponents in what I wish with all sincerity, with justice and without bad intentions."
  6. Tie the fifth knot one inch past the third, to the left. Say: "With this fifth knot, I imprison your heart. You will be unable to fall in love with any other person. You will not desire to fall in love with any other person. You will not attempt to fall in love with any other person. Your heart is entirely dedicated to my love and happiness."
  7. Tie the sixth knot one inch past the fourth, to the right. Say: "From this day forth, your words, your thoughts, your desires, your actions are on me because of this well-placed Secret Sortilege and the knot-work that I do."
  8. Tie the seventh (final) knot one inch past the fifth, to the left. Say: "Your love is entirely and completely mine. With this knot I close the circle. I enclose you in my magick circle, formed from this Magick Ribbon. With this circle, I surround your heart, I enclose your heart. With this circle I love you. With this circle you and I are bonded as one through these Seven Knots of Love. We are as one from this day forward. We will stand together for each other. Nothing will be able to break, interrupt, or destroy our happiness and unity."
  9. Tie the two ends of the ribbon together to form a circle. Snip off the leftover ribbon and use it to tie two silver charms to the circle: the charm representing the target is tied to the seventh knot; the charm representing yourself is tied to the center knot.
  10. Build an altar featuring Saint Martha and Saint Anthony.
  11. Every other night for 14 nights (seven times total), place the circle around your left arm above the elbow before going to sleep. Adjust placement if needed.
  12. Upon waking each morning, hide the charm behind or beneath the altar.
  13. Once the 14 days are complete, the charm remains on the altar permanently. From then on, carry the final silver charm: a heart pierced by a sword with three crosses, engraved with the word 'Blessed.'
