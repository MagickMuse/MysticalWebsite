⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A salt-and-sulfur revenge spell that targets the subconscious mind of the enemy. Salt is thrown upon a photograph while claiming dominion, then sulfur is burned through the forehead of the image to deliver the incantation directly into the target's psyche. A two-part chant invokes the forces of fire and hell to destroy the target's empire, inflict guilt, and seal their fate.

Quantities

1 photograph of the target
Salt
Sulfur (scraped from the red tip of an unburnt match)
Matches

Directions

  1. Obtain a photograph of the target, some salt, and sulfur scraped from the red tip of an unburnt match.
  2. Throw salt upon the photograph and speak the first incantation, claiming dominion over the target as one treads upon the earth.
  3. Next, sprinkle the sulfur over the photograph around the area of the forehead.
  4. Light the sulfur so that a hole burns through the forehead region of the photo.
  5. This carries the incantation into the subconscious mind of the target, where they will create their own downfall.
  6. As the sulfur burns, chant the second incantation with full emotion and conviction.

Incantations

As this salt is of the earth,
so art thee.
And as I trod upon the earth,
so do I trod upon thee.
Friends of fire, and friends of hell,
beckon to this horrid smell.
Destroy the empire that he's built,
let him suffer with his guilt.
From earth's own salt and sulfur's smell,
with your own evil you shall dwell.
With evil thought and vicious tongue,
I will undo what you have done.
No longer will you manipulate,
with your deeds you seal your fate.
The smell of vengeance is so sweet,
with these words my spell's complete.
