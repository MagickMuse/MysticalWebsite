Call upon Archangel Michael, humanity's divine defender and the embodiment of celestial justice, for protection in the courtroom or any legal situation. Post his image, burn gum resins, and speak his invocation.

Quantities

  • Image of Archangel Michael
  • Fragrant gum resins (frankincense, myrrh, copal, or similar)
  • Charcoal disc (for burning resins)

Directions

  1. Post an image of Archangel Michael in your working space.
  2. Burn fragrant gum resins as an offering.
  3. Speak the invocation clearly and with focused intent, calling Michael to surround you on all sides with his flaming sword of cobalt blue.

If there is one presence you would want beside you in a courtroom or any legal battle, it is Michael the Archangel. Michael's celestial mission is to be humanity's defender. He epitomizes justice, and he responds to those who call upon him sincerely.

Preparing the Space

  1. Post an image of Archangel Michael in your working space — on your altar, near the entrance to your home, or wherever feels right.
  2. Burn fragrant gum resins — frankincense and myrrh are traditional and deeply aligned with Michael's energy.

The Invocation

With the incense burning, speak the following invocation clearly and with focused intention:

Michael to the right of me,
Michael to the left of me
Michael above me
Michael below me
Michael within me
Michael all around me
Michael with your flaming sword of cobalt blue, protect me today!

Visualize Michael's presence surrounding you completely — to every side, above and below, and within. See his cobalt-blue flaming sword raised in your defense.
