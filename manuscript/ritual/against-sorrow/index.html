🛡️Against Sorrow

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickPrayerProtection
◇Category
Ritual

Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Hot glue gun - Cardboard or posterboard - Fern branch - Sheer scarf - Sewing pins - Medallion of Archangel Michael or image of protective deity
◇Directions
1. Gather materials: hot glue gun, cardboard/posterboard, fern branch, sheer scarf, sewing pins, and medallion of Archangel Michael (or image of protective deity from your pantheon). 2. Symbolism: • Fern: Keeps off old ghosts, inner and outer • Michael/Protector deity: Protects from all harm • Scarf: Represents veil of Isis, asking for painful things to stay behind the veil 3. Glue the archangel symbol (or deity image) to the cardboard. 4. Glue the fern branch so it frames the symbol. 5. Wrap the scarf over these and pin it to the cardboard. 6. Take the completed ward into your sacred space. 7. Speak sincerely, as you would to a trusted friend, about your recent troubles. 8. Explain why you need a break right now. 9. Hang the ward across from your front door so the image is behind you whenever you greet visitors.
◇Description
A protective ward crafted before Samhain divination to shield against sorrow and keep painful revelations behind the veil.
