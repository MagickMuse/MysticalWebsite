🍋Lemon and Clove Moon Blessing Charms

A winter moon craft that transforms the pomander tradition into a lunar blessing practice. Cloved lemon wheels are dehydrated and inscribed with intentions on bay leaves, then woven together with ribbon to create hanging charms, one for each lunar cycle of the year ahead, or gifted to loved ones as blessings.

Quantities

  • Baking sheet
  • Parchment paper
  • 1 large lemon
  • Knife
  • 8–13 whole cloves per charm
  • Ground nutmeg
  • Ground cinnamon
  • 1 bay leaf per charm
  • Toothpick or scissors
  • Ribbon

Directions

  1. Before you begin, decide on your intention for this working.
  2. You may create a single charm as a general home blessing, craft 8 charms for each phase of one lunar cycle, or make 12 or 13, one for each full moon of the year ahead.
  3. This choice will determine how many lemon slices and cloves you need.
  4. Preheat your oven to 200°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  5. Hold the lemon in both hands.
  6. Breathe and bring the moon into your mind, her cycles, her moods, the year of lunations stretching out before you.
  7. Lemon carries lunar associations: it purifies, it opens the heart to love and joy, and it calls in inspiration.
  8. Slice the lemon into wheels about ¼ inch thick.
  9. Thinner slices dry faster.
  10. As you cut, hold the image of blessings unfolding, one by one.
  11. Squeeze the cut ends over a drain, letting the cleansing juice carry away anything you are releasing.
  12. Rinse your hands and compost the ends.
  13. Count out cloves for each wheel.
  14. To represent a single lunar cycle, use 8 cloves, one for each phase.
  15. For a year-long blessing, use 12 or 13 cloves on one wheel, each representing a full moon.
  16. Clove carries the energy of Jupiter, luck, abundance, and spiritual expansion.
  17. Press each clove into the flesh of the lemon wheel with intention, spacing them evenly.
  18. The finished wheel echoes the turning cycle of the moon.
  19. Mix a small amount of ground nutmeg and cinnamon together and sprinkle a pinch over both sides of each lemon wheel, dusting it with additional blessings.
  20. Lay the wheels on the prepared baking sheet.
  21. Bake for 6 to 7 hours, turning the slices every 2 hours, until fully dehydrated.
  22. Allow to cool completely.
  23. On each bay leaf, write the name of the person you are gifting the charm to, a wish, or a specific intention.
  24. Use a toothpick or scissors to make a small hole through each lemon wheel and each bay leaf.
  25. Weave them together with ribbon.
  26. Hang your finished charms on your altar, above a doorway, or anywhere in the home that calls for blessing, or give them as gifts, carrying lunar energy and good fortune into the hands of those you love.
