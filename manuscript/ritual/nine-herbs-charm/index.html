🍃Nine Herbs Charm

An Anglo-Saxon healing charm from the Lacnunga manuscript (10th-11th century) that calls upon nine sacred herbs to ward off poison and illness through incantation and herbal salve.

Quantities

  • Mugwort
  • Plantain (waybread)
  • Lamb's cress
  • Attorlape (betony or cockspur grass)
  • Chamomile
  • Wergulu (possibly nettle)
  • Chervil
  • Fennel
  • Crab apple
  • Apple juice
  • Water
  • Ashes
  • Egg

Directions

  1. Each herb must be spoken to individually before any preparation begins.
  2. Grind each ingredient into a powder while chanting the following incantation three times per herb:
  3. "These nine stand in opposition against nine poisons.
    Recall {invoke the name of one specific herb from the list above}, what you declared,
    what you established at the great council.
    'Unique' you are called, most senior of herbs.
    You prevail against three and against thirty,
    you prevail against poison and against infection,
    you prevail against the harmful one that travels throughout the land."
  4. Once all nine herbs have been ground and enchanted, mix the powders with apple juice, water, and ashes.
  5. Boil with fennel and combine with egg to form a salve.
  6. Before applying the salve, sing the incantation into the patient's mouth, into both ears, and over the wound.
