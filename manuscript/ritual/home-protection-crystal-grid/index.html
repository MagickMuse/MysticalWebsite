A layered crystal grid for home protection using multiple stones in geometric placement. Creates redundant protective barriers throughout the dwelling.

Quantities

4 small black tourmaline stones
4 hematite stones
4 selenite bars
1 quartz point

Directions

  1. Place four small black tourmalines at the lowest level of the home, one in each corner, to protect against attacks and negative entities.
  2. Place four small hematite stones at the north, south, east, and west positions on the exterior of the home, if possible, to ground negativity directed at inhabitants or the structure.
  3. Place four selenite bars in the four corners of an upper floor, if one exists, to shield the home from outside influence.
  4. Position a single quartz point near the front door to anchor the wards and link all other stones together.
  5. For added potency, lay the grid during a full moon or other astronomical event, though the stones function effectively at any time.

Principle

Layered protection creates redundancy. If one layer fails, others remain active. Crystal grids coordinate multiple stones in geometric patterns to amplify protective intent. Use even numbers of stones for each function to promote harmonic resonance.

⚠️
Note: Selenite is a salt-based mineral and will dissolve if exposed to moisture. Keep indoors only.

Customization

Grids can be created for any intention: love, prosperity, protection. Select stones that resonate with your specific goals.
