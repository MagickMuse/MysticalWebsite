🛡️Shielding Yourself

Symbols2
3⚪White

An additional layer of protection for negative influences, without banishing them.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Visualize the Energy Column
  2. Call to mind a column of energy descending from the heavens, through you, and down into the earth.
  3. Feel vital energy rising from the earth into you.
    Breathing
  4. Inhale: Draw power from above and below into yourself.
  5. Exhale: Let the energy spread through every cell of your body.
    Hand Placement
  6. Make a fist with your left hand and place it over your heart.
  7. Cover it with your right hand, applying about 5 pounds of pressure.
    Focus Energy at the Heart
  8. As you inhale, feel the power gathering at your heart due to the pressure and your focused will.
  9. Visualize a small gray egg shape in place of your heart.
    Expanding the Shield
  10. Release the pressure.
  11. Imagine the egg growing larger until it extends 1–2 feet beyond your body.
  12. See the surface as impenetrable, blocking all malignant forces.
    Completion
  13. Once you clearly perceive the shield, turn your attention away and continue with your day.
    Duration and Dissolution
  14. The shield will last a few hours unless you keep sustaining it with imagination and will.
  15. To dissolve it sooner, inhale deeply, exhale, and see the shield dissolving into space.
