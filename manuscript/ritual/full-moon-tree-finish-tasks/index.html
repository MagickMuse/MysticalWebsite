🌕Full Moon Tree Finish Tasks

A full moon night ritual decorating branch with seven silver bells and baubles, tying seven ribbons, untying and shaking tree.

Quantities

  • 1 large silver candle
  • 1 leafy tree branch (small enough for large pot of soil)
  • 7 silver bells
  • 7 silver foil decorations or baubles
  • Silver thread
  • 7 silver ribbons

Directions

  1. Light the candle indoors, saying, "Moon power at this hour, this I ask, to finish these tasks that are grievously burdening me." Decorate the branch with baubles and bells, using silver thread to attach them and tie the ribbons in loose bows on the branch, saying, "Mother of the Moon, with tasks undone I bind your tree that I may be free." Pull the ribbons undone one after the other and shake the tree so the bells ring.
  2. Let the candle burn and leave the baubles and bells on the branch or plant throughout the week of the full moon.
  3. Perform after dark on a full moon night.
