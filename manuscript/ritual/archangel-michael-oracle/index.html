🪔Lamp

A dream incubation ritual calling upon Archangel Michael to bring clarity, protection, and divine guidance through dream visions. One of the Dream Incubation methods.

Quantities

  • An oil lamp
  • Oil for the lamp

Directions

  1. Cleanse and purify yourself thoroughly before beginning.
  2. Light an oil lamp.
  3. Speak to the lamplight throughout the vigil, observing and responding to the flame.
  4. Periodically throughout and at the very end, recite the following incantation:
  5. Lamp, light the way to Archangel Michael,
    If my petition is appropriate, show me water and a grave
    If not, show me water and a stone.
  6. The symbols of water-and-grave and water-and-stone are traditional; you may substitute your own clearly defined symbols if preferred.
  7. When the lamp burns out, fall silent and go to sleep.
  8. The dream that follows provides the oracle.
