This ethical spell is intended to restore a broken connection with someone from your past, be it a friend, lover, spouse, or family member. It focuses on healing, not control, and invokes Forseti, Norse god of reconciliation and justice.

Quantities

  • 1 red candle
  • 1 carving tool (to inscribe the candle)
  • 1 pentacle or pentagram (drawn on paper is fine)
  • Enough dried red or pink beans to outline the pentacle
  • Photos or symbolic items connecting you to the person (or their name written in red ink)
  • Optional: 1 rose quartz
  • Optional: music that reminds you of the person

Directions

  1. • Reflect deeply on the person and the benefits a reunion would bring to both of you. Avoid focusing on past conflict.
  2. Gather personal items (letters, gifts, clothes, etc.) that connect you to this person.
  3. Carve the person’s name or initials into the candle.
  4. Prepare sacred space and light music if using.
  5. Place the pentacle on your altar or working surface.
  6. Set the candle at the center of the pentacle.
  7. Place beans around the pentacle’s circle in a clockwise (deosil) direction.
  8. Arrange photos/items connecting you both around the beans; place large ones near the altar.
  9. Place the rose quartz (if using) near the candle.
  10. Raise power and light the candle. Focus on healing.
  11. With arms open wide, say:
    As these words aloud are spoken,Repair the ties that have been broken.If it will harm none, and is meant to be, Forseti, please bring ______ back to me.If it be in accord with the Universe,Restore ______ to me with this verse. Forseti, He Who Stills All Strife,Please return ______ to my life.As these words aloud are spoken,Heal the bond that has been broken.
  12. Continue to focus on reconciliation as the candle burns.
  13. Once it burns out, ground the energy you raised and clean up your altar.
  14. Package the personal items and beans, and store them safely for two full Moon cycles (two months).
  15. Keep the rose quartz with you as a charm during this time.
  16. If nothing changes in two months, the spell’s answer may be no.

A Different Kind of Love Spell

Is there someone you would like to have back in your life? This spell is different from the others in this chapter because it does focus on a particular person. No matter what the cause of an estrangement, it is not ethical to force someone's return. It is ethical, however, to ask the Universe to reunite you if you are meant to be together.

Whether it be a friend, lover, spouse, colleague, or family member, the best way to end an estrangement is usually non-magickal. Make an overture of some sort: apologize (or accept that person's apology), send a card, send flowers, invite the person out for coffee, have a heart-to-heart talk, and so on.

But if there is some reason for avoiding these obvious methods, or if you have tried them without success, you could cast this spell. If you are nervous about how your attempt at reconciliation might be received, you could cast it as a prelude to making a nonmagickal overture.

The Ethics of Reconciliation Magick

In order for this spell to be ethical, you must be willing to accept the will of the gods (the Universe) as to whether or not you are to be reconciled. You must also be willing to accept whatever type of reconciliation is offered. For example, the door to a romantic relationship may be closed, but the door to a warm friendship could open instead.

⚠️
If you use this spell to try to impose your will upon someone else, it is more likely to backfire horribly upon you than it is to work in the way you desire.

Why Forseti?

The spell invokes Forseti, a Norse god whose dominion includes reconciliation as well as mediation and arbitration. He is said to always listen carefully to both sides in any disagreement, and he is noted for the fairness of his decisions.

Timing

The spell can be cast at any time, but it is most powerful on the night of a Full Moon.

Preparation

Think about the person you want back. Concentrate on what you miss about them, and why you would like the two of you to be reconciled. Do not focus on what went wrong with your relationship, though, because you might be very much mistaken about what caused your estrangement. Instead, think about the benefits that a reunion would be likely to bring to each of you.

Identify and collect every object you possess that connects you and this person to each other. These might include such things as letters, photographs, gifts, books, souvenirs, items of clothing, emails, theater programs, and the like.

The Incantation

As these words aloud are spoken,
Repair the ties that have been broken.
If it will harm none, and is meant to be,
Forseti, please bring ______ back to me.
If it be in accord with the Universe,
Restore ______ to me with this verse.
Forseti, He Who Stills All Strife,
Please return ______ to my life.
As these words aloud are spoken,
Heal the bond that has been broken.

Setting Up the Altar

Place the pentacle on your altar or other working surface, and put the candle in the center of the pentacle. Working deosil (clockwise), place the beans around the circle of the pentacle. Arrange all the small items that connect the two of you around the pentacle; place any large items on or near the altar. If you are using a rose quartz, place it on the pentacle near the candle.

Why the Beans?

Dried red or pink beans can be found in any grocery store. The red color corresponds to passion and vital energy, while beans themselves are ancient symbols of life, rebirth, and connection. Outlining the pentacle with them creates a boundary of living intention.

Casting the Spell

Concentrate on welcoming this person back into your life as you monitor the candle until it burns itself out. When it stops burning, ground the power that you raised. You should feel calm, and willing to accept whatever outcome the spell will have.

Cleanup and Storage

Package all the personal items that you gathered for this spell. Put them away with the beans in a safe place for two full cycles of the Moon (two months), or until the spell has manifested. If you used a rose quartz, keep that with you as a charm during this period.

If the Spell Does Not Manifest

Should two cycles of the Moon pass without any movement of this person back to you, it must be accepted that reconciliation does not seem to be the will of the gods at this time. This is not a failure. It is an answer.
