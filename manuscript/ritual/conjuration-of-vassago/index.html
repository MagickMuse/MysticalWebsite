A ceremonial conjuration calling the grimoire spirit Vassago into a show stone or speculum to answer a question of great difficulty, closed with a mandatory license to depart.

Quantities

Altar table with triangle covering; two chairs; Athame, cord, thurible, cup, workbook, Square of Mercury, pen and ink of art; Mercurial incense and a Vassago attuned incense; a show stone or speculum on its stand with two candlesticks; a pentacle of protection for the operator and each assistant; two clean sheets of paper for the sigil and the recorded question; a trusted companion to act as scribe

Directions

  1. Cast and consecrate the circle, attune with the Square of Mercury, name and charge Vassago's sigil, invoke him three times while circumambulating the circle, gaze into the show stone or speculum to receive and record his answer, then close every working with the license to depart.
  2. Full steps are in the page content below.
⚠️
This is a greater working of conjuration, reserved for matters of real difficulty once simpler forms of divination have been exhausted. Never omit the license to depart at the end of the rite. Skipping it is said to have cost the diviner Chiancungi and his sister Napula their lives when the spirit Bokim, not yet fully constrained, seized them the moment they left their circle without dismissing him. Even a spirit described as good by nature deserves this courtesy and this caution.

Choosing Your Time and Company

Work during clear weather when the moon is two, four, six, eight, ten, twelve, or fourteen days old and waxing, though Vassago, a prince among the seventy two, is not strictly bound by this timing. Choose one trusted companion to act as scribe before you begin, along with any other assistants directly involved in the working. Lock yourselves into your place of practice.

Setting the Table

Cover your altar table with its triangle marking pointing east, with chairs to the west facing east across it. Gather your Athame, cord, thurible, cup, workbook, Square of Mercury, and pen and ink of art, along with a supply of Mercurial incense and a separate incense suited to Vassago's nature. Place your show stone or speculum within the triangle, flanked on either side by a candlestick holding an exorcised white candle, each about twelve inches behind the stone. The operator holds the wand in the right hand, and every person present wears their pentacle of protection.

Opening the Circle

Kindle your Mercurial incense and candles, then trace a triangle on the floor with your Athame before casting and exorcising your circle with fire and water as usual. Perform the Square of Mercury rite to attune yourself, then replenish your thurible with the Vassago incense.

Preparing the Sigil

On one clean sheet, draw Vassago's sigil at full size with your pen of art. On a second sheet, draw the same sigil small in the top right corner, then write your question beneath it; this second sheet will later hold your recorded vision.

Naming the Sigil

Holding the full size sigil in your right hand, asperge and fumigate it three times with the Vassago incense, stating each time: "Creature of paper, I name thee Vassago. Thou art Vassago." Move the paper to your left hand, take up your wand in your right, and trace the sigil in the air above it three times, visualizing a line of blue fire tracing a glowing sigil in the air, again repeating the naming words three times. Draw three crosses above the sigil with your wand and seal with "So mote it be."

Invoking Vassago

Carrying the sigil in your left hand and your wand in your right, circumambulate the circle clockwise, finishing each circuit at the east, facing east, wand raised over the sigil. Call on Vassago by name three times with each circuit, asking him to descend and make himself known within the stone or glass, to lend his presence and his knowledge, and to appear in truth and understanding. Three circuits and three invocations complete the calling. Return to the west side of the altar, facing east, and set the sigil in the triangle beneath the show stone or speculum.

Gazing and Recording

Sit comfortably before the table, hold your wand with both hands, and rest your gaze gently on the surface of the stone or glass. Visualize the same blue radiance around it while restating your question aloud. Your focus may drift in and out before the surface begins to haze with a blue grey mist, the sign that a vision is opening. Vassago may appear directly or simply present symbolic images. Keep your attention on the stone throughout; do not glance aside at movement forming at the edge of your vision, and let images build fully before describing them aloud for your scribe to record on the question paper. Visions fade quickly from memory, so record them as they happen rather than afterward.

Closing the Working

When the stream of images ends and the glass or stone clears, add more Vassago incense to the thurible and speak a formal license to depart, thanking Vassago and releasing him from the working. This step must never be skipped, regardless of whether any visible manifestation occurred.
