A leisurely bath in colored water provides a powerful way to absorb missing color energy into your body. This therapeutic practice combines the healing properties of water with the energetic qualities of specific colors to restore balance and vitality.

Quantities

  • Colored bath salts in your needed color, or food coloring
  • Bathtub with hot water
  • Good quality towels
  • Optional: Candles
  • Optional: Gentle music
  • Optional: Essential oils (a few drops)

Directions

  1. Allow plenty of time for this practice.
  2. Color bathing should never be rushed.
  3. Gather your materials and create a peaceful atmosphere.
  4. You may wish to play gentle music, burn candles, or add essential oils to enhance the experience.
  5. Fill the tub with water hot enough for comfort but not scalding.
  6. Add your colored bath salts or food coloring to create the hue you need.
  7. Step into the colored water and settle in comfortably.
  8. As you relax, visualize the color entering every cell of your body.
  9. See it filling you with energy and vitality specific to that color's qualities.
  10. Remain in the bath for as long as you wish, continuing to absorb the color through visualization and intention.
  11. When you feel complete, stand and dry yourself briskly with the towel.
  12. Stretch luxuriously before getting dressed.
  13. Relax for a few minutes before carrying on with your day.
