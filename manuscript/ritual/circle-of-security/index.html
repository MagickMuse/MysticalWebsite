A home protection spell using clear quartz crystals charged on a pentagram and buried around the home's perimeter to create a protective barrier against all harm.

Quantities

  • Small clear quartz crystals (enough to circle your home, or one per window and exterior door for apartments)
  • 1 black cloth
  • 1 garden trowel or shovel

Directions

  1. Collect the listed ingredients.
  2. If you live in a house and have a yard, you'll need enough crystals to completely circle your house.
  3. If you live in an apartment, you'll need one crystal for each window and each door to the outside.
  4. Wash the crystals with mild soap and water, then pat them dry.
  5. If you have a large area to cover and a lot of crystals, you might need to continue this spell over a period of days.
  6. Draw or find a picture of a pentagram and lay it on your altar, or another surface.
  7. Place the crystals on it and visualize them absorbing the protection represented by the pentagram.
  8. Lay the black cloth over the crystals, covering them completely.
  9. Allow the crystals to sit overnight.
  10. In the morning, remove the cloth.
  11. Pick up the crystals, put them in a bowl or other container, and take them outside.
  12. Beginning in the east, bury the crystals in your yard one at a time, making a protective circle that surrounds your home.
  13. Position them as close together or as far apart as feels right to you.
  14. If you live in an apartment, start at the east and place a crystal on each windowsill (inside) of your living space.
  15. Then set a crystal in a safe spot near or above each exterior door.
  16. As you work repeat this affirmation aloud:
  17. "Crystals wise, crystals strong
    Protect my home all day long.
    Crystals clear, crystals bright
    Keep it safe throughout the night."
  18. You may also want to hang a pentagram on each door that leads into your home.
