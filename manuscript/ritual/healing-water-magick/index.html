A solstice healing working that consecrates water with salt and summer herbs, infusing it with solar vitality for gentle cleansing and restoration.

Quantities

  • Deep cauldron or bowl filled nearly to the top with water (rain, river, lake, or spring preferred)
  • Small bowl of sea salt or salt
  • Healing herbs (e.g., elder flowers, lavender, lemon balm, yarrow, or vervain)
  • White or yellow cloth
  • Optional: a Midsummer gemstone in the bottom of the bowl

Directions

  1. Place the water where sunlight can reach it if possible, or visualize the sun upon it.
  2. Sit or kneel with salt, herbs, and cloth within reach.
  3. See rays of the sun dancing on the surface, lending their power to the water.
  4. Say:
    This water is pure and sacred.
  5. Sprinkle a few grains of salt, saying:
    Like the waves of the ocean and the tears of the Mother goddess,
    the salt and the water are one, and sacred.
  6. Crumble a small amount of herbs into the water, saying:
    These healing herbs, the gift of the blossoming earth,
    lend their healing powers to the water, and so too they are one,
    and sacred.
  7. Place hands on either side of the vessel, feel the energy. Envision an ancient holy well transforming into the bowl before you. Dip hands into the water, allowing healing energy to travel up your fingers and into your body. When complete, lift dripping hands and say:
    This water is sacred and blessed, and so am I.
  8. Dry hands on the cloth.
  9. Either leave the water on the altar for the day or pour it out with thanks.
  10. All-day progression: If you started at dawn with Greet the Dawn, perform this midmorning or midafternoon, then do Green and Growing Herbal Prosperity at noon, and finish at dusk with Fire Power Candle Magick.
