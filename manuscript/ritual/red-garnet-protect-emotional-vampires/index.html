💔Red Garnet Protect Emotional Vampires

◇Form
Candle RiteCharm
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/MantraProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• 1 dark-red candle • 1 red garnet on a chain long enough to be at heart level (it does not need to be gem quality) • Garlic powder (open the windows, as burning garlic smells foul)
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and, holding the garnet on the chain above the candle, swing the garnet all around the candle in nine counterclockwise spirals, saying: "No more do I give my energies away, no more shall vampires [name the current one] into my mind stray. No longer intrude through my sympathies, be gone with you and your miseries." Sprinkle just a grain or two of garlic in the candle and swing the garnet nine more times counterclockwise while repeating the spell words. 2. Extinguish the candle and hold the garnet over your heart, saying the spell words again. 3. Dispose of the candle and put on the garnet. 4. When you feel under attack, touch the garnet and say the spell words in your mind. 5. Spiral the garnet around a dark-red candle nine times counterclockwise monthly.
◇Description
A red garnet ritual to protect yourself from emotional vampires, spiraling garnet around candle with garlic to drive away energy drains.
