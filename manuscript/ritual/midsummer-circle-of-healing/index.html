🕯️Midsummer Circle of Healing

A group rite for healing and abundance using solar fire to burn away what holds participants back, then drawing in energy for growth and prosperity. Written for a single leader but easy to split among callers or adapt for a coven.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • God and Goddess candles (gold/silver or yellow/white) in fire‑safe holders
  • Four quarter candles: green (North/Earth), yellow (East/Air), red (South/Fire), blue (West/Water)
  • Bonfire or fire pit; if not possible, a cauldron or large bowl filled with sand or salt with candles inside
  • Small bowls for salt and water, plus one to mix
  • Sage smudge or incense in a fire‑safe holder; optional feather for wafting
  • Basket of lavender flowers; basket of daisies or other bright flowers (one per participant)
  • Matches and optional snuffer
  • Cakes and ale: round bread or sunny cookies; fruit juice, wine, or mead in a chalice or nice cup
  • Altar table and cloth; seasonal decorations (sun symbols, vase of flowers)

Directions

  1. Setting: Outdoors if possible, preferably around a bonfire.
  2. Best at dusk on Midsummer Day; begin early enough to finish before sunset.
  3. Pre‑ritual preparations:
  4. Place God and Goddess candles on the altar with baskets of lavender and flowers nearby. Set offerings/decorations.
  5. Put quarter candles at their directions (or altar edges): North/green (Earth), East/yellow (Air), South/red (Fire), West/blue (Water).
  6. Place cakes and ale to the side within reach. Light the bonfire before beginning.
  7. Cleanse and consecrate:
  8. Smudge the space clockwise with sage/incense, visualizing negativity cleared. Pass the smudge around; each person cleanses head to feet. Leave it safely smoldering or extinguish.
  9. Leader mixes a pinch of salt into water: “Salt into water, water into salt. Wash away all that is negative and impure, leaving only that which is positive and beneficial.”
  10. Pass the bowl; each person dabs forehead (thoughts), lips (speech), and heart (feelings).
  11. Cast the circle (leader walking the boundary, pointing with finger, athame, broom, or wand):
    “I cast the circle round and round, from earth to sky, from sky to ground. I conjure now this sacred place, outside of time, outside of space. The circle is cast. We are between the worlds.”
  12. Quarter calls (by leader or four callers, light each candle):
  13. East/Air (yellow): “I call the guardian of the east, the power of air, to come to this circle bringing refreshing summer breezes and clarity of thought.”
  14. South/Fire (red): “I call the guardian of the south, the power of fire, to come to this circle bringing warm sunlight and the spirit of love.”
  15. West/Water (blue): “I call the guardian of the west, the power of water, to come to this circle bringing life‑giving rains and healing energy.”
  16. North/Earth (green): “I call the guardian of the north, the power of earth, to come to this circle and ground us for the magickal work ahead.”
  17. Invocations (or use your pantheon):
    “I call thee, Brighid, triple goddess who rules over hearth and healing and inspiration. Share your gifts with us on this Summer Solstice day, and guide us with your gentle wisdom. Let your light warm our hearts and brighten our spirits. So mote it be.” [Light goddess candle.]
    “I call thee, Belenus, god of the sun, whose powerful rays bring healing and strength. Share your gifts with us on this Summer Solstice day, and empower us with your energy. Let your light warm our hearts and brighten our spirits. So mote it be.” [Light god candle.]
  18. Healing fire offering:
  19. Leader lifts the bowl of lavender: explain its Midsummer healing virtue. Pass the bowl; each participant tosses a handful into the bonfire (or around the central candles/onto a plate beneath them), casting what needs healing into the cleansing flames.
  20. Abundance receiving:
  21. “Now we are free to receive the solstice’s blessings of energy, abundance, and joy! Take a flower as a symbol of abundant Midsummer energy.” Pass the basket; each person takes a daisy or bright flower.
  22. Guided solar infilling (leader speaks, all stand, eyes closed or focused on the flower):
  23. Lead the solar body‑scan visualization from crown to toes, feeling the Sun’s power recharge each person’s core. Imagine the whole circle glowing, the light passing person to person, accumulating love and joy until all are filled to overflowing. Hold the flower to the heart, then raise it to the Sun and all shout: “Thank you and huzzah!”
  24. Cakes and ale:
  25. Leader holds cakes: “We give thanks for these cakes, the gift of the earth, and share them with each other with open hearts.” Share.
  26. Leader holds cup: “May our lives be as sweet as the fruit in this cup.” Share.
  27. Thanks and release:
  28. “Let us thank the god and goddess for their gifts of healing and energy!” All respond: “Thank you, Belenus! Thank you, Brighid!” (repeat as feels right). Snuff deity candles.
  29. Dismiss quarters counterclockwise starting North → West → South → East: “I thank you (earth, water, fire, air) for guarding our circle. Stay if you will, go if you must, in perfect love and perfect trust. So mote it be.” Snuff each candle after thanks.
  30. Open the circle (leader draws energy back counterclockwise, or all join hands and fling up to the sky): “The circle is open but never broken. Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again!”
