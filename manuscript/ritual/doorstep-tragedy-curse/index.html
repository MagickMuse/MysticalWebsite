⚡Doorstep Tragedy Curse

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellCharged Object
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Herbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
Clothing from the target (cut into 6-inch squares) Parchment paper Dragon's Blood Ink Black Arts Oil Valerian (a pinch per night) 1 coffin nail per night
◇Directions
1. Take a piece of the target's clothing and cut it into as many squares as possible, approximately six inches on each side. 2. Use one square each night. 3. On a piece of parchment, write the target's name backwards. 4. On the opposite side, write your curse, either in your own words or something simple such as: [target's name] is cursed, [target's name] is damned forevermore. 5. The writing must be done with Dragon's Blood Ink to which a drop of Black Arts Oil and a pinch of valerian have been added. 6. Fold the cloth square with the cursed parchment inside, turning each fold away from you, never toward yourself. 7. Take the folded bundle and nail it to the enemy's front door using a coffin nail. 8. Repeat each night until the desired result has been achieved.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING ⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice sacred to African American spiritual traditions and should only be practiced by those within the originating culture. A nightly curse using the target's own clothing cut into squares, each folded around a parchment curse written in Dragon's Blood Ink mixed with Black Arts Oil and valerian, then nailed to the enemy's door with a coffin nail.
