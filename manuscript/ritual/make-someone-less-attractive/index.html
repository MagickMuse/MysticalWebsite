💅Make Someone Less Attractive

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 small black candle Dirt Stick matches 1 photo of the target
◇Directions
1. Place the photo of the target face down on a table. 2. Light the black candle with a stick match and place it on top of the photo. 3. While the candle burns, sprinkle dirt on and around the photo and candle while repeating the chant. 4. The more times the chant is spoken, the stronger the effect.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A simple candle hex to diminish a target's physical attractiveness. A photograph is placed face down, a black candle lit atop it, and dirt sprinkled over everything while chanting. Repetition strengthens the effect.

Incantation

Picture of the beauty, learn,
behind their back this spell does burn.
This filth, this dirt, the picture made,
the beauty's attraction seems to fade.
