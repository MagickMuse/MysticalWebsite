III of CupsIX of PentaclesThe EmperorThe Empress

A comprehensive feng shui practice placing tarot cards in all nine guas (sectors) of your home. Each card represents what you desire in that area of life, creating a complete energetic blueprint for manifestation throughout your living space.

Quantities

  • 9 tarot cards from a deck you don't use for readings
  • One for each gua/life area

Directions

  1. Mentally superimpose the bagua grid over your home.
  2. To determine which gua corresponds to which area, stand at your main entry door facing inward.
  3. Select one card representing your desires for each of the nine life areas. For example:
  4. Three of Cups for the Friendship Gua
  5. Nine of Pentacles for the Wealth Gua
  6. The Empress or Emperor for the Fame/Future Gua
  7. Display each card in its corresponding gua throughout your home.
