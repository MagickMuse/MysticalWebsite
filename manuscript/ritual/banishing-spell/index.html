A strong dismissal ritual using the Scythe rune, fire, and black salt to block and banish unwanted influences.

Quantities

Paper
Black candle
Incense
Cauldron
Sea salt

Directions

  1. Draw the Scythe rune on the paper and place it under the black candle.
  2. Light the candle and burn the incense, envisioning a complete detachment.
  3. When the candle burns down, burn the rune paper in your cauldron.
  4. Once the paper is ash, mix in a pinch of sea salt.
  5. Take the black salt mixture to a moving body of water. Stand with the water behind you and throw the salt over your left shoulder with your right hand.
  6. Walk away immediately and do not look back.
