🔮Flour and Charcoal Sieve Divination

A sieve-based pendulum divination method for comparing two options or choices. Half the sieve is covered with white flour and the other half with charcoal powder. A pendulum swung within the sieve traces lines and patterns in the two contrasting substances. Works best with a flat-bottomed sieve, grain winnow, or finely perforated frame drum.

Quantities

  • A flat-bottomed sieve, grain winnow, or finely perforated frame drum
  • White flour
  • Charcoal powder
  • A pendulum (or create one from a stick and string; a mugwort or wormwood twig is ideal)

Directions

  1. Cover one half of the inside of the sieve with white flour and the other half with charcoal powder.
  2. Prepare or select a pendulum.
  3. A twig of mugwort or wormwood attached to a length of string is traditional and particularly potent.
  4. Allow the pendulum to swing freely within the sieve and observe the lines and patterns it traces through the flour and charcoal.
  5. Interpret the resulting marks as the oracle, particularly in relation to the two halves.
