🌾Demeter Spell for a Missing Child

An urgent spell invoking Demeter to help find a missing child. Because her daughter Persephone was abducted by Hades, Demeter knows the unimaginable pain of losing a child and the fierce joy of getting that beloved child back.

Quantities

White cloth
Pentacle
Picture of the missing child
Candle (black or white, matching your ritual attire)
Grain (such as barley)
Optional: mullein
Water (for a libation, if the child returns safely)

Directions

  1. Change your clothes if you are dressing for the spell in all black or all white, and gather the spell ingredients.
  2. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space around the area where you will be working.
  3. Put the white cloth on your altar or other working surface, place the pentacle on it, put the child's picture in the center of the pentacle, and place the candle upright on top of that.
  4. Working deosil, spread the grain around the pentacle's circle, doing the same with the mullein if you are using it.
  5. Raise power and light the candle. Focus on the missing child, concentrating on drawing that child back to safety or on leading searchers to the child. If it appears that someone has taken the child, try to send that person the thought that it would be better to leave the child in a safe place. Channel as much energy into this as you possibly can.
  6. Raise your hands over the candle, focus on the child and the distraught family, and recite the incantation.
  7. Remain with the candle, repeating the spell or speaking to Demeter in your own words, concentrating on the child and on drawing the child to safety.
  8. When the candle burns out, ground the power that you raised and clean up; take the grain and mullein if you used it, and scatter it outdoors.

Why Demeter?

Because her daughter Persephone was abducted by Hades, Demeter is a goddess who knows the unimaginable pain of losing a child and the fierce joy of getting that beloved child back. She is a powerful deity to turn to for help when a child disappears, no matter if the child is thought to be lost, to have run away, or to have been taken.

This is an urgent working, so the spell is best cast as soon as you learn that a child is missing. We do not generally use magick for people without a specific request for magickal assistance, but if tearful parents are pleading for help from anyone who can give it, we can take that request to include us as well.

The Incantation

Barley Mother, Giver of All,
Hear their pleas, answer my call.
By the power resident in grain,
Ease their sorrow, end their pain.
August Demeter, you are the key.
Rescue [name] as you did Persephone.

Afterward

If you hear the wonderful news that the child has come home, give thanks to Demeter by pouring out a libation of water to her, first to the East and then to the West.
