⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A Hoodoo conjure bag combining pins, needles, hair, snakeroot, and goofer dust in a red flannel pouch to bring bad luck and crossed conditions upon a target; the working persists as long as the bag remains hidden. ⚠️ Conjure Bag to Cross Someone is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

9 new needles
9 new pins
Hair from the target
1 piece of snakeroot
Goofer dust powder
1 red flannel bag

Directions

  1. Gather nine new needles and nine new pins, and tie them together using hair from the intended target.
  2. Add a piece of snakeroot and a measure of goofer dust powder.
  3. Place everything into a small red flannel bag.
  4. Hide the finished conjure bag inside the target's home or bury it in their yard.
  5. The working remains active for as long as the bag stays undiscovered.
