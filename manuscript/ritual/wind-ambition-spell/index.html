🌬️Wind Ambition Spell

A when wind speed picks up spell to harness powers of wind to make ambition or dream come true, lighting two white candles and letting wind blow them out then relighting to carry power and return as good fortune.

Quantities

  • Two tall white candles
  • A basket of green leaves

Directions

  1. Light the first candle, saying: "From South, East, West and North, the power of this wind I summon forth." State your ambition or dream.
  2. Light the second candle, repeating the spell words, and carry the two candles outdoors into the wind so their flames blow out, saying: "Carry my power at this hour, blow the changes I do desire. Carry this light higher and higher and return as the good fortune to which I aspire." Go back indoors with the candles.
  3. Carry the leaves outdoors, setting the basket down so the leaves will blow away.
  4. Go back inside and relight the candles, the second from the first, saying: "Light returns and what I yearn will materialize before my eyes. I will gain the prize." Perform when wind speed picks up.
