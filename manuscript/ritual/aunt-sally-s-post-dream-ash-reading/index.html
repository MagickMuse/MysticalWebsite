🌙Aunt Sally's Post-Dream Ash Reading

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Powder
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• Ashes from Aunt Sally's Dream Powder or other dream incense • Sieve (optional)
◇Directions
1. In the morning when you wake, go to the incense remains. 2. Using either your bare fingers or a sieve, carefully sift through the ashes of the dream incense. 3. Observe the patterns, shapes, and formations that emerge. 4. Interpret the results to receive your message or number.
◇Description
A companion divination technique to Aunt Sally's Dream Powder (or any dream incense). If no numbers appear in the dreams themselves, this method sifts the incense ashes the following morning to receive the oracle instead.
