Mistletoe

A hunter's luck and protection charm. Carry mistletoe on your person while hunting to bring luck and provide protection.

Quantities

Mistletoe (woody portions only; berries and leaves are toxic)

Directions

  1. Carry mistletoe for hunters' luck and protection.
  2. ⚠️ Safety: Use only the woody portions.
  3. Mistletoe berries and leaves are toxic.
  4. Keep away from children and animals.

About This Charm

Mistletoe has a long association with protection, luck, and the natural world. Carrying it while hunting provides both luck and protective energy.

⚠️ Safety: Mistletoe berries and leaves are toxic. Use only the woody portions. Keep out of reach of children and animals.
