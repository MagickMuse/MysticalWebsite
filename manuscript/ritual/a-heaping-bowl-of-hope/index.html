A vessel spell to revive and strengthen your hope muscles. Uses dandelion for tenacity, calendula and quartz for clearing, and personal symbols of hope to build the hopeful state needed for powerful magick.

Quantities

  • Vessel (bowl, jar, cup, vase, box, etc.)
  • Paper
  • Pen
  • Local dandelion
  • Calendula/pot marigold
  • Clear quartz or selenite
  • Personal symbols of hope (tarot cards, bell, bird feathers, match, dice with double sixes, etc.)
  • Personal taglock (hair, nails, full name)

Directions

  1. Clean and cleanse your vessel, then connect it to you.
  2. Add a piece of yourself (hair, nails, your full name) and visualize your energy and the vessel becoming one.
  3. Declare aloud that it is connected to you, and anything built up inside of it will build up inside you.
  4. Write your intentions on the paper.
  5. Fold the paper toward you three times and place it in the vessel.
  6. Place tools into the vessel one at a time, pausing to connect and ask for their assistance.
  7. Hold the dandelion and ask for its persistence and strength to fill your heart, and for its hope to embolden yours, then place it inside.
  8. Ask the calendula and quartz to clear away anything that clouds your heart and dims the shine of your hope, and ask them to bless you with hopeful confidence and the awareness to feel it, then add them to the vessel.
  9. Add your personal hope symbols last, feeling into the significance of each item.
  10. Conjure up the most hopeful feelings these items bring you and connect them to hopeful and happy moments from your life.
  11. Seal with the incantation:
  12. May this vessel awaken and stimulate the hope inside of me so that I may feel my hope growing with each day.
  13. As I will it, so shall it be.
  14. Let the vessel stay on your altar for at least three days, ideally a week.
  15. Visit daily and take three deep breaths, drinking in the energy it produces and filling up on hope.
  16. When finished, dispose of herbs and paper respectfully.
  17. Cleanse or clear items you intend to keep.
