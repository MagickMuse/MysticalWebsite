Hag Stone

A protective charm using holed stones, known variously as hag stones, witch stones, or mare stones, to prevent malicious spirits from riding horses at night. The Mara (source of the word 'nightmare') and similar spirits allegedly ride horses beyond their capacity under cover of darkness, leaving the animal exhausted, sweating, and frothing by morning. Holed stones interrupt this spirit activity.

Note: Charms and spells for horses may also be used for donkeys and mules.

Quantities

  • One or more holed stones (hag stones / mare stones)
  • Cord (for stringing and wrapping)

Directions

  1. Use one or both methods:
  2. - String a holed stone (mare stone) onto cord and wrap it around the horse's neck
  3. Braid holed stones into the horse's mane
  4. More spiritual protection is always preferable to less, both methods together are recommended.
