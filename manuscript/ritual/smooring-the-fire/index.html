🌙Smooring the Fire

An evening ritual of putting the hearth to bed, adapted for modern homes. Performed after cooking is done for the day, it invokes Brigid's protection over the home and household through the night, connecting the practitioner to all foremothers who have tended hearthfires before them.

Quantities

No materials required (performed at stove, hearth, or candle)

Directions

  1. Perform this at the close of the day, either as part of your bedtime devotions or after finishing cooking for the evening.
  2. Stand before your stove, hearth, or a candle flame.
  3. Turn off any cooking flames, or allow a candle to be the hearth-flame you smoor.
  4. Spread your arms wide to either side, as if to encircle your home, and speak the smooring prayer:
  5. "I will smoor the hearth
    As Brigid herself would smoor.
    In the name of Brigid:
    Be on the hearth,
    Be on the herd,
    Be on the household all."
  6. As you speak the words, feel for Brigid's presence and understand what it means to smoor as she would smoor: with love, with intention, with strength.
  7. With the words 'be on the hearth,' envision her blessing flowing around your home in a protective encircling.
  8. With 'be on the herd,' extend her blessing to any animals in your care and to all animals nearby.
  9. With 'the household all,' name in your heart all those who dwell with you or whom you wish to hold under her protection.
  10. Extinguish any candle or flame with intention.
  11. The inner hearth of devotion has been banked.
  12. The eternal fire endures even when tangible flames are extinguished.
