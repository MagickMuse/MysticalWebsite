⚔️Revocation of Archangel Michael (Nine Nights)

A nine-night protective reversal petitioning Archangel Michael. The enemy's name is placed in a glass with sea salt and charged waters (spring water, Carmelite water, angelica hydrosol), the glass is reversed onto a saucer without spilling a drop, and a slice of white candle is burned on top each night for nine nights.

Quantities

Small piece of paper and pen
Glass
Sea salt
Spring water, Carmelite water, and/or angelica hydrosol
Saucer
White candle (sliced into 9 pieces, wicks pulled from each)

Directions

  1. Write your enemy's name on a small piece of paper and place it inside a glass.
  2. Cover the paper with sea salt.
  3. Fill the glass with spring water, Carmelite water, angelica hydrosol, or any combination of these.
  4. Cover the glass with a saucer and turn it over quickly so that it rests atop the saucer, making sure no water spills or leaks out; if any water leaks, start completely from scratch.
  5. Slice a white candle into nine pieces, pulling out the wicks so that each becomes a distinct little candle.
  6. Every night for nine consecutive nights, place one candle slice on top of the reversed glass, burn it, and petition Michael for assistance.
