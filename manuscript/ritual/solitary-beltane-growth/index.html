Ritual Grimoire

🌱Solitary Beltane Growth

◇Form
Circle RiteAltar WorkingBathCharm
◇Method
Candle MagickWater MagickElementalSolarLunarEnergy WorkVisualizationMeditation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 2 tea light or taper candles with candleholders (preferably 1 gold or yellow, 1 silver or white) - Fresh plants, herbs, and flowers from your region (roots attached) - 1 large bowl - Water - 2 pieces of paper - 1 pen - 1 small square of green fabric - 1 piece of gold or yellow string/thread Optional for pre-ritual bath: - Sea salt, sugar, or oatmeal Optional accessories: - Gold or copper jewelry - Bell for space cleansing - Wand Optional clothing: - Skyclad (nude) or natural fabrics in green shades
◇Directions
1. Pre-Ritual Preparations: Cleanse with bath or shower, releasing negativity into water. Add sea salt to bath for solar attunement, or rub skin with sugar/oatmeal for purification. Dress skyclad or in comfortable natural green fabrics. Accessorize with gold or copper jewelry for solar power boost. 2. Create two lists: (1) accomplishments and personal growth over past year, (2) new goals for personal growth this season. 3. Set up ritual space: Place bowl of water on top of past accomplishments list. 4. Arrange candles on either side of bowl (gold/yellow candle on right, silver/white on left). 5. Place fresh plants in front of bowl, on top of new goals list. 6. The Ritual: Sit with palms flat on ground. Take deep breaths until calm and centered. Extend calm feeling outward to encompass entire ritual space. Stand and walk clockwise circle around space, projecting light, loving energy through chest, palms, eyes, or wand tip. Alternative: ring bell while circling to clear stale energy and invite fresh, positive energy. 7. Notice plants around you and their dependence on earth, sun, and rain. 8. Touch ground and living plants. 9. Feel earth's powerful energy, vibrating with life and creation, throbbing like a heartbeat. 10. Pull this energy into your body through contact point between hands and earth. 11. Use will and intention to direct energy. 12. Notice physical sensations (tingle, jolt, warmth, heaviness, etc.). 13. Let earth energy take over your body and emotion until ego is nil. 14. Rub dirt on skin or touch body with green plants/flowers to enhance feeling. 15. Earth element now invoked and flowing through you. 16. Turn attention to bowl of water. 17. Think of rivers, oceans, lakes, seas, rain. 18. Think of water beneath, above, and on earth. 19. Drink from bowl, envisioning water as flowing river or cool rain droplets. 20. Notice how water moves through you, quenching thirst and fueling transformation. 21. Invite this energy to stay within you. 22. Having invoked earth and water, envision yourself as damp soil, wet, dark, warm as a womb. 23. Pick up fresh plants placed on top of new goals list. Read list while cradling plants gently. Think of plants as the hopes and dreams outlined on list. Feel emotion within those desires. Let energy pour into plants, charging them with feeling of love and filling them with earth and water powers coursing through your body. Place plants in water and say: "These are the dreams of [insert your full name], a child of the earth, moon, sun, and sea." 24. Light silver/white candle (representative of moon). Hold candle above bowl. Think of moon's gravitational pull literally moving oceans, manifesting and directing tides covering 70% of planet's surface. Think how tides affect weather, and weather affects plants and animals. Consider that your body is 50%+ water, and like seas, moon sways your inner emotional tides. Let your dependence on moon sink in, then allow gratitude to flow into candle flame and water below. Think of past accomplishments list beneath bowl and new dreams represented by plants in bowl. With free hand, swirl water in bowl clockwise and say: "Great moon, as you move the sea, so too move me!" Watch plants swirl and envision goals manifesting, tides turning in your favor thanks to moon. Return candle to left side of bowl. 25. Light gold/yellow candle (representative of sun). 26. Hold candle up to sun. 27. Think about sunlight streaming into candle flame, adding solar energy to its bright, fiery power. 28. Walk clockwise around ritual space, noticing how living plants from ground pulsate with same solar charge now infused in candle flame. 29. Invite solar charge to flow into you. 30. Let candle flame draw sunlight, then direct energy down through wax into your body. 31. As you walk circle, envision plants growing, fueled by sunlight, earth, and water. 32. Stop in front of water bowl and hold candle high above it. 33. Envision yourself growing. Sense cellular and molecular processes in your body, just as dependent on sunlight, earth, and water as surrounding vegetation. Think about photosynthesis in green plant parts, pure sunlight transformed into nutrients fueling plant growth. Look at plants floating in bowl. Feel solar energy pulsating within your body, warm, bright, fiery, strong. Ask sun to help fuel your dreams. Direct all amplified solar power coursing through you and through candle to enter plants in bowl. Envision yourself growing ten feet tall, a thriving giant radiating health, vigor, and success, like a mighty tree king of the forest. Say: "Great sun, fuel and energy of Earth, charge me up now, give my future dreams birth!" 34. Place candle back to right of basin. 35. Let both candles burn out completely. 36. Leave basin of water with plants outside overnight. 37. Place new goals list in sunny spot in your home. 38. Keep or discard past accomplishments list, its magickal work is done. 39. Next morning: Remove plants from bowl and set in sun to dry. 40. Pour water on earth. 41. Once plants dried, tie them in small green cloth and secure bundle with gold/yellow thread (colored in tune with solar vibrations). 42. Place bundle on top of new goals list in sunny spot. 43. Rite complete, you should feel in tune with Beltane's energy flow, and your inner process of personal and spiritual growth will be highly accelerated, at least temporarily. 44. Make the most of it!
◇Description
A comprehensive personal growth ritual honoring both the growth of nature and the evolution of the practitioner's soul. Designed for solitary practice but adaptable for groups. Attunes with nature's tides, expresses gratitude for vegetation, contemplates personal growth, and manifests opportunities for greater development.
