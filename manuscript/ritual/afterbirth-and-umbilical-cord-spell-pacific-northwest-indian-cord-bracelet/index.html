⚠️ Closed Practice, a Pacific Northwest Indigenous tradition transforming the umbilical cord into a blessed, knotted bracelet worn by the baby.

Quantities

The dried umbilical cord

Directions

  1. Turn the umbilical cord into a bracelet for the baby's wrist or ankle.
  2. Bless the baby as you form each knot.
  3. Let the baby wear it for a few months or until it is naturally outgrown.
