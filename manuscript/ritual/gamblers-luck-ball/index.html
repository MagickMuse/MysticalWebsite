A Hoodoo jack ball, also called a luck ball in Spanish tradition: leftover wax from gambling luck candles is rolled into a ball, embedded with lucky charms, dusted in gambling powders or herbs, and carried as a mojo hand or inside one. ⚠️ Gamblers' Luck Ball is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Purple or green gambling luck candles (cat candles optional)
Optional: paper with monetary goal written on it
Lucky charms to embed: miniature dice, magnetic horseshoes, four-leaf clover charms, etc.
Powdered herbs for rolling: basil, vervain, or parsley (or a gamblers' powder)
Gamblers' Dressing Oil for anointing

Directions

  1. Burn gambling luck candles, plain purple or green, or cat candles.
  2. If you have a specific monetary or other goal, write it on a piece of paper and slip it under the primary candle before lighting.
  3. Do not dress the candles heavily with oil, as excess oil will affect the final texture of the wax.
  4. While the wax is still warm and soft, gather a small handful.
  5. Roll it into a ball sized to your preference and what you wish to insert inside.
  6. Press lucky charms into the wax: miniature dice, magnetic horseshoes, four-leaf clover charms, or whatever speaks to your intention.
  7. Roll the finished ball in powdered herbs (basil, vervain, or parsley) or in one of the gambling powders.
  8. Carry the luck ball as a mojo hand, or place it inside an existing mojo bag.
  9. Anoint with dressing oils as needed.
