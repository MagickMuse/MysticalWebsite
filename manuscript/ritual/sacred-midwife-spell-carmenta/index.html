Sacred Midwife Spell: Carmenta

◇Tradition

◇Solitary or Group

◇Directions
1. Make your request for childbirth protection during the festival of Carmentalia, January 11 through 15, the best time to petition her for children born that year. 2. Phrase the petition in song or rhyme, honoring her gift of prophecy and poetry.
◇Description
A petition to Carmenta, Roman spirit of prophecy and childbirth protection, offered during her festival of Carmentalia (January 11-15) in song or rhyme.
