For anyone that has trouble reconciling their intellect and their emotions. For those who let emotion override thinking, this is a spell for harnessing the power and energy of emotion and lust and letting the intellect channel it into useful purposes.

Quantities

  • No materials required

Directions

  1. Perform this invocation on the day of Mercury at the hour of Venus.
  2. Vibrate the vowels Epsilon followed by Eta (EH-AY), with the day vowel shorter in duration followed by a longer vibration of the hour vowel.
  3. Then speak the invocation:
  4. Lord of Learning and Lady of Love
    Bring to Balance the feeling and thinking minds
    May my instincts inform my ideas
    and imagine ingenious innovations
    May the heart and head and loins be aligned
    And may victory and glory be mine.
