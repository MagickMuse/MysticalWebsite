A blended essential oil of bay laurel, frankincense, myrrh, and sandalwood in a sweet almond and jojoba base, massaged into the feet before bed and before a loan meeting to draw a favorable outcome.

Quantities

  • Essential oil of bay laurel
  • Essential oil of frankincense
  • Essential oil of myrrh
  • Essential oil of sandalwood
  • Sweet almond oil
  • Jojoba oil

Directions

  1. Blend essential oils of bay laurel, frankincense, myrrh, and sandalwood into a base of sweet almond and jojoba oils.
  2. Massage this oil into the soles of your feet before bedtime and again before applying for the loan.
