🕯️Eight of Swords Incarceration Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Dressed Candle
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualizationMeditation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 Eight of Swords tarot card (or color photocopy) - Commanding Oil (or San Cipriano Oil) - 1 white candle or white cross candle - Several coins - Dried ground celandine - Dried vervain - 1 dish
◇Directions
1. Remove the Eight of Swords card from a Rider-Waite tarot deck, or use a color photocopy to preserve the original. 2. Spend time meditating on the image, focusing on the blindfolded figure who has broken free from confinement. 3. Place a single drop of Commanding Oil or San Cipriano Oil on the card. 4. Lay the card face up on a dish. 5. Carve and dress a plain white candle or white cross candle to express your intention, then place it on top of the card. 6. Sprinkle several coins around the candle along with dried ground celandine and vervain. 7. Burn the candle.
◇Description
A candle spell using the Eight of Swords tarot card as a focal point to work against imprisonment. The card depicts a bound figure breaking free, and its image is activated with condition oils, botanical herbs, and a dressed candle. A color photocopy of the card may be used to preserve the original.
