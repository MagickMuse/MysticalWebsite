🌀The Spiral Dance (Meeting Dance)

Form
Circle Rite
Discreet
No
Correspondences1
🧙Coven
Entities2
ArianrhodeCernunnos

The primary Sabbat dance of death and rebirth, also called the Maze, Meeting Dance, Wheel, or Round Dance. The coven spirals widdershins to a still centre representing death, lights tapers from the balefire, then spirals back clockwise. Symbolizes the interaction of dark and bright tides, the labyrinth, and the House of Death and Initiation. Associated with Caer Arrianrhod, the Glass Castle of the North, and the Corona Borealis.

Quantities

  • One lit taper per coven member
  • Balefire (already burning at centre)

Directions

The magister stands at the centre of the circle beside the balefire, facing north, arms crossed (the sign of death).

The coven begins at the northern periphery. Moving widdershins, each member also turns widdershins on their own axis, forming a winding spiral chain inward over three circuits. On reaching the magister at the centre, each member lights their taper from the balefire.

The coven then doubles back, spiraling clockwise and returning outward over a further three circuits.

During the dance, wild cries of "EEE-OOO AAH VOH AIEE" are given by the coven as they whirl. A chant may accompany; for goddess-oriented covens at Hallows or Yule:

"Queen of the Moon, Queen of the Sun,
Queen of the Heavens, Queen of the Stars,
Queen of the Waters, Queen of the Earth,
bring to us the Child of Promise!

It is the Great Mother who giveth birth to him;
It is the Lord of Life who is born again.
Darkness and tears are set aside
when the sun shall come up early!

Golden Sun of the mountains,
illuminate the land, light up the world.
Illuminate the seas and the rivers,
Sorrows be laid, joy to the world!

Blessed by the Great Goddess,
without beginning, without end,
everlasting in eternity!
IO EVOKE, blessed be!"

Note: This chant is used specifically at Hallows or Yule, as it refers to the coming of winter and the rebirth of the sun.

The Spiral Dance often leads directly into the Chain Dance.
