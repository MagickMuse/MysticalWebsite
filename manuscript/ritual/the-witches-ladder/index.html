◇Directions
1. Tie nine knots in a string, holding it in your left hand and starting from the left. 2. While tying each knot, chant the conjuration. 3. After all nine knots are tied, bury the string in the ground and chant the burial incantation.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A classic knot magick curse. Nine knots are tied in a string while chanting a binding conjuration that blinds the eyes, stops the ears, seals the mouth, and binds the limbs. The string is then buried in the ground with a final incantation to lay the target to rest.

Incantations

(Target's name), I conjure thee!
By night your eyes are blinded.
By earth your ears are stopped.
By clay your mouth is sealed.
By rocks your limbs are bound.
So be it.
Twist and tangle, (target's name), never to rise again.
Your eyes are dimmed.
Your limbs are bound.
Thus I lay you to rest, still and silent in the ground.
