A waxing to Full Moon consecration spell for a tarot deck, performed on a day of Mercury or the Moon during the hour of the Moon, Sun, or Mercury, following a Consecration of a Tool Ritual. Two white votive candles flank a pentacle where the cards are set. Frankincense or lavender incense is lit, and a black thread is wrapped around the deck while consecrating the cards through all four Elements: Air by incense smoke, Fire by candle flame, Water by sprinkling or breath, and Earth by placement on the pentacle. The thread is stored for rewrapping the deck as needed.

Quantities

  • 2 white votive candles
  • 1 pentacle
  • Frankincense or lavender incense
  • Black thread
  • Blessed water

Directions

  1. Set the tarot cards on a pentacle.
  2. Place a white votive candle on either side of the pentacle.
  3. Light frankincense or lavender incense and place it in front of the pentacle.
  4. Pick up the cards and wrap a black thread around the deck.
  5. Speak:
  6. This tarot is mine, consecrated to my use, bound by the thread that ties it to me.
  7. I charge that no unwanted influence hold sway over these cards by Air,
    Pass the cards through the incense smoke.
    by Fire,
    Pass the cards quickly through the candle flame.
    by Water,
    Sprinkle the cards quickly with blessed water and wipe dry, or give a quick breath with open mouth over the cards.
    and by Earth.
  8. Set the cards on the pentacle.
  9. Remove the thread from the cards and store it to wrap the deck whenever needed.
  10. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
