🔓Smoke and Cemetery Cross New Love Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Incense Blend
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickSmoke MagickDirectional Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Few strands of hair (from your own head) Thumbnail clippings Dried rose petals Grains of Paradise Dried vervain Lit charcoal
◇Directions
1. Add a few strands of hair from your head plus some thumbnail clippings to dried rose petals, Grains of Paradise, and vervain. 2. Burn them over lit charcoal like incense. 3. Stand over the rising smoke wearing outer garments but no underpants; inhale the fragrance as it wafts upward. 4. Still wearing no underpants, travel to a cemetery. 5. Walk through the cemetery in two directions, forming the shape of a cross, to destroy evil from all directions. 6. Return home by a different route than the one you used to arrive.
◇Description
Personal items and love herbs burned as incense, with the seeker standing in the smoke before walking a cross pattern through a cemetery to break through long periods of lovelessness and bad luck.
