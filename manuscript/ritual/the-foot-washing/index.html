🦶The Foot Washing

A cleansing ritual performed for another person to remove negative environmental influences absorbed through the feet. Rooted in the Christian tradition of Maundy Thursday foot washing and in ancestral practice, this working humbles the practitioner, petitions the subject's ancestors for healing, and optionally seals the cleanse with a condition oil or blessed olive oil on the soles. Cannot be performed on oneself.

Quantities

A metal basin
Warm water
Soap
A washcloth
A towel
Optional: holy water, spring water, or seawater
A chair
Optional: condition oil or olive oil, for the closing cross

Directions

  1. You will need a metal basin of warm water, soap, a washcloth, a towel, and optionally some holy water, spring water, or seawater to add to the basin.
  2. Do not add music, incense, or other sensual elements.
  3. Keep it simple.
  4. Invite your subject to sit in a chair.
  5. Remove their shoes and have them place their feet in the water.
  6. Let them relax and soak for a moment.
  7. This can be a good time to talk about what led you both to this moment, because your defenses are down.
  8. When you both feel ready, wash your subject's feet.
  9. As you do, pray softly to their ancestors.
  10. Tell them that their child is hurting and needs their help to remove whatever it is they stepped in. Ask them to cease the spread of any damage that has entered the body through the feet, and to heal whatever harm has already been done.
  11. When you are done, rinse the feet with clean water and dry them.
  12. If you wish, take a small amount of condition oil prepared for a particular goal, or pray over a small amount of olive oil for general blessing, and make a small cross on the soles.
