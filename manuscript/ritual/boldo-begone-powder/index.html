Boldo, an evergreen native to South America, allegedly helps repel stalkers when combined with traditional banishing powder ingredients. The finished powder is sprinkled across the stalker's path or across home thresholds and entry points.

Quantities

  • Boldo leaves
  • Asafetida
  • Chili powder
  • Sulfur
  • Arrowroot powder (base)

Directions

  1. Grind boldo leaves and blend with asafetida, chili powder, and sulfur.
  2. Blend this mixture into a base of arrowroot powder.
  3. Sprinkle the finished powder across the path of the person who must stop pursuing you, or across your thresholds and any vulnerable entry points into your home.
