🧲Draw Lasting Good Fortune Magnet

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
PouchCharm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A magnet and seven shiny nails - A silver ring and a gold-colored or brass ring - Dried allspice, ginger, or saffron in a little bag - A red bag
◇Directions
1. Put the nails around the magnet and turn the magnet until all are attached, saying: "Magnet, magnet, draw to me, good fortune soon and permanently, that I may attract wherever I go the means to live comfortably." Put the magnet and attached nails into the bag. 2. Add the rings and say: "Around and around, wheel's fortune turns, bringing me what I do yearn [name the areas of luck you most seek]." Add the spice, saying: "Gold and silver, now the fire, the strength of iron draws my desire [again name the areas where you seek luck most]." Carry your little bag with you whenever you need special luck. 3. On Midsummer Eve dispose of nails and spice, and on Midsummer Day renew the bag with new nails, new spice, and the same rings, bag, and magnet. 4. Perform Sunday around noon, if possible in bright sunlight.
◇Description
A Sunday around noon spell to draw lasting good fortune by using magnet, attaching seven nails to magnet with rings and spice in red bag to attract means to live comfortably wherever you go.
