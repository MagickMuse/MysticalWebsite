🌱Addiction Breaker

◇Quantities
1 black candle 1 red candle 1 drop of blood Oil of spirit or divine source helper 1 Tiger's Eye stone 1 living plant
◇Directions
1. Invoke your spiritual helper from all points of the ritual space. 2. On the right side of the altar, place the red candle with the name of the addiction carved into it, anointed with oil. 3. Set the living plant beside it. 4. On the left side, place the black candle and the Tiger's Eye stone. 5. Light both candles. 6. Place a drop of blood in the soil of the plant and declare it as the living addiction that will wither and die. 7. Focus all anger and hatred toward the addiction onto the plant. 8. Recall every loss and pain caused by the addiction. 9. Once all hatred is expended, disown the addiction with a clear statement of intent. 10. Make a request and burn it in the offering bowl. 11. The black candle absorbs the negativity. 12. Take the Tiger's Eye and fill it with visions of success and freedom. 13. Close the ritual and let the candles burn. 14. Let the plant die in a visible place. 15. Carry the Tiger's Eye during recovery, rubbing it and visualizing freedom when cravings arise. 16. Once the plant is fully dead, throw it on a compost pile.
◇Description
A self-directed curse ritual used to destroy an addiction or bad habit. A living plant is designated as the embodiment of the addiction and left to die. A red candle carved with the addiction's name absorbs the practitioner's hatred, while a black candle absorbs negativity. A Tiger's Eye stone serves as a talisman of hope and willpower during recovery.

⚠️
Tiger's Eye is an energy conduit. Those who are sensitive to it (who develop colds or symptoms after prolonged exposure) should clear the stone daily and leave it on the altar at least six hours each day to minimize symptoms.

Incantations
