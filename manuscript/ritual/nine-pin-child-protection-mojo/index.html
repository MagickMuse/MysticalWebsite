Nine Pin Child Protection Mojo

◇Tradition
Hoodoo
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ProtectionHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
1 mojo bag or small cloth pouch 9 straight pins Black Cat Oil (for dressing) Angelica root Frankincense resin Juniper berries Cumin seed Magnetic sand Coins Silver Black cat hair Queen of Spades, Seven of Spades, and Seven of Clubs playing cards
◇Directions
1. Lay out all materials and focus on the intention of protecting the child from harm. 2. Place the angelica, frankincense, juniper, and cumin into the bag. 3. Add the magnetic sand, coins, and silver. 4. Insert the three playing cards into or alongside the bag. 5. Push the nine pins through the outside of the bag in a deliberate pattern. 6. Dress the finished mojo hand with Black Cat Oil.
◇Description
A complex mojo hand for child protection assembled with nine pins, three playing cards, Black Cat Oil, and a range of protective botanicals and curios. ⚠️ Nine Pin Child Protection Mojo is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.
