🍯Herbal Lip Scrub
Symbols3
2CircleClockwise

The second component of the Five-Fold Blessing for Beauty, this charm consecrates the lips as instruments of magickal speech. Words spoken aloud carry vibrational energy that the universe receives, and the lips become sacred through their role in spellcraft. Applying this herbal scrub while holding a spoken charm magnifies both the beauty working and the power of the practitioner's spoken word.

Quantities

  • 2 teaspoons granulated organic sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon wildflower honey
  • 1/8 teaspoon vitamin E oil
  • 1-2 drops diluted herbal oil of choice (e.g. mint infused in jojoba, or essential oil diluted in a carrier)
  • 1/8 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 2 drops vanilla extract
  • Small metal 1-ounce jar

Directions

  1. Prepare the lip scrub paste ahead of time or refer to the linked recipe.
  2. Take a small amount onto your fingertips and begin working it across your lips in a circular motion.
  3. The paste will spread evenly as the sugar granules exfoliate the surface.
  4. Keep the motion going steadily, rolling the scrub around your lips as you enter a meditative state and hold or repeat the charm:
  5. LIPS THAT PART
    LIPS THAT SPEAK
    THE WORDS OF THE HEART
    THE BEAUTY I SEEK
    WORDS OF POWER
    WORDS OF PRAISE
    BLESSINGS OF FLOWERS
    ENERGY I RAISE
    MY WORD WILL BE TRUE
    THE MAGICK WILL COME
    IN ALL THAT I DO
    SO MUST IT BE DONE.
  6. Continue the motion until the charm feels complete and the oils have had time to absorb.
  7. Lick away any remaining paste and feel the result.
  8. Transfer the rest of the preparation into the storage jar.
  9. The scrub keeps at room temperature and can be used as often as desired.
