The Hermit

A winter moon meditation that uses the imagery of The Hermit tarot card to guide an inner journey by moonlight. Sitting in stillness with a moonstone or quartz point as a symbolic staff, the practitioner visualizes walking a snow-covered path illuminated by the moon, discovering what lies dormant in their life and following the lunar light toward insight.

Quantities

  • Cloak or blanket (optional)
  • The Hermit tarot card
  • Black, rainbow, or blue moonstone, or a quartz point
  • Candle
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Find a comfortable place to sit, ideally somewhere you can see or sense the moonlight.
  2. If the night is cold, draw a blanket or cloak around your shoulders.
  3. Take a moment to settle into the quiet and notice how moonlight falls differently than sunlight: softer, more interior, an invitation to turn inward.
  4. Place The Hermit card before you.
  5. Take in his image: a solitary figure moving through the snow, lantern raised, staff in hand.
  6. He is not lost.
  7. He has chosen to walk alone into the dark because that is where the light he is searching for lives.
  8. Pickup your moonstone or quartz point and hold it in both hands.
  9. This is your staff, your grounding anchor for the journey ahead.
  10. Lift your eyes or your awareness toward the moon, letting her light become the lantern guiding your inner path.
  11. Close your eyes.
  12. Visualize yourself walking through a still winter landscape blanketed in snow.
  13. Everything is quiet and at rest.
  14. Ask yourself gently: what in your life is currently frozen, covered over, or lying dormant beneath the surface?
  15. Continue walking.
  16. Look up and find the moon ahead of you, casting a soft ribbon of light through the snow.
  17. Follow it.
  18. Your cloak or blanket warms you as you walk.
  19. Do not force or reach; simply follow the light, allowing images, feelings, or knowings to arise naturally, until you sense what the moon has been guiding you toward.
  20. When insight arrives, open your eyes and light the candle.
  21. Let it represent the inner light you carry through the dark months ahead, the same lantern the Hermit holds aloft.
  22. Keep the crystal with you in the days that follow as a touchstone for whatever theme or truth emerged during the meditation.
