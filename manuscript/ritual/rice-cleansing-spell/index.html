Rice

A simple stationary cleanser that uses the natural absorbency of white rice to draw negative energy and spiritual debris from a space. White foods like eggs, onions, and rice share a quality of absorbing and trapping psychic toxins.

Quantities

  • 1 bowl of raw white rice

Directions

  1. Place an uncovered bowl of raw rice in the space requiring cleansing.
  2. The rice will absorb negative energy and spiritual debris over time.
  3. Replace with fresh rice every week.
  4. Discard the old rice outside the home or offer it to wild birds, whichever feels appropriate.
