Mabon📅September 29

A Michaelmas ritual performed on September 29 that invokes Archangel Michael to protect community protectors (firefighters, first responders, paramedics, police). Uses red candle anointed with basil oil, dirt from service stations, and images blessed daily until candle burns down.

Quantities

  • Pinch of dirt from nearest police station, fire station, or hospital
  • Red candle
  • Basil-infused olive oil
  • Printed pictures: city seal, police seal, fire department seal, hospital logo
  • Printed image of Archangel Michael
  • Heat-proof bowl
  • Spring water

Directions

  1. Place the image of Archangel Michael on a flat surface.
  2. Then set the police, fire department, city, and hospital images in the bowl.
  3. Dress the red candle from the middle out to both ends with the herb-infused olive oil while asking Archangel Michael to confer his blessing on your work.
  4. Set the candle on top of the images and light it while speaking either the traditional prayer of Saint Michael or a version you are comfortable with.
  5. A Pagan-friendly version might be:
  6. Archangel Michael,
    you who watch over our warriors,
    keep them from evil, lead them to good,
    watch over them in their daily battles
    for the good of all.
    So mote it be.
  7. Dust the pinch of dirt over the images, and then pour the spring water into the bowl so that the paper is just covered.
  8. Add a little fresh water and repeat the prayer every day, relighting the candle daily until the candle burns out upon reaching the water level.
  9. Once done, bury the remainder of the spell either on your property, at the edge of a public park, or near a highway easement.
