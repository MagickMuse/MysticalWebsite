A simple yet powerful meditation for generating and sending feelings of love and compassion to all humanity. Research shows this practice increases gamma wave activity and activates areas of the brain associated with positive emotions.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. The Practice
    You can perform a compassionate meditation at any time.
    Step One: Enter the Meditative State
    Close your eyes, take a few deep breaths, and relax.
    Step Two: Generate Compassion
    Generate feelings of love and compassion for all humanity. Allow these feelings to swirl around you like a cloud, and then filter into every cell of your body.
    Step Three: Receive a Color
    When you feel ready, ask the feelings to give you a color to symbolize love and compassion. Allow your cloud of compassion to be permeated with this color.
    Step Four: Expand and Send
    Let the colored cloud of compassion grow and expand as you mentally send your feelings out into the world.
    Remain in this state for as long as you wish.
    Closing
    To return to your everyday life, take a slow deep breath, smile as you exhale, and then open your eyes.

Compassionate meditation is designed to send thoughts of love and compassion to all humanity. Research published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (November 8, 2004) studied eight Buddhist monks and found that gamma wave activity in their brains increased markedly when performing compassionate meditation—reaching the highest levels the researchers had ever recorded.

Gamma wave activity is involved with mental processes such as learning, concentration, perception, and memory. Areas of the brain associated with positive emotions also showed increased activity. This indicates that meditating regularly will make you happier and better able to understand and learn. It also shows how beneficial a brief meditation can be before beginning a mentally involved task.
