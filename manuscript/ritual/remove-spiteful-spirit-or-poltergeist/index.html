Dusk⚔️Tuesday

A Tuesday sunset ritual with scented candle, incense, garlic powder, and six outward-pointing amethysts to banish malicious spirits.

Quantities

  • 1 lemon-, lavender-, or pine-scented candle
  • 1 frankincense or sandalwood incense stick
  • 1 bowl of garlic powder
  • 6 six-pointed amethysts (pointing outward)

Directions

  1. Light the candle in silence, and from the candle, the incense.
  2. Then write, around the garlic and around the amethysts in incense smoke letters, "YOU MUST DEPART IN PEACE. YOUR PRESENCE I RELEASE, AND TROUBLE NOW MUST CEASE." With the candle in your dominant hand and the garlic in your nondominant hand, go outdoors and blow the candle out over the garlic.
  3. Empty the garlic powder bowl outside and let the candle burn.
  4. When the incense has burned out, place each amethyst on an indoor window ledge inside your home.
  5. Wash them weekly.
  6. Perform Tuesday, around sunset.
