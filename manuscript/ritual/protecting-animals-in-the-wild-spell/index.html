🌍Protecting Animals in the Wild Spell

An afternoon especially if you hear of danger spell for protecting animals in the wild, planting greenery and releasing feathers and water in triangle to connect and protect all creatures of earth, sky, and waters.

Quantities

  • A green plant
  • Four feathers
  • A bowl of water

Directions

  1. Outdoors, set these three items in a triangle around you.
  2. Hold the plant between your hands and press your feet into the ground, saying: "I connect. Protect all creatures of the earth." Plant the greenery near where you are standing, repeating the spell words.
  3. Pick up the feathers and divide them between your hands.
  4. Extend your arms upward and say: "I connect. Protect all the creatures of the air." Release all the feathers except one or attach them with string to a nearby tree.
  5. Hold the water in your dominant hand, and, with the other, ripple it, saying: "I connect. Protect all the creatures of the waters." Empty the water around the new plant and spiral the remaining feather over and around it four times, saying: "I connect. Protect all the creatures of the earth, sky, and waters." Release the final feather or tie it to the tree, saying: "Fly free and send protection to all creatures of the earth, sky, and waters." Perform afternoon, especially if you hear of a danger or disaster to wildlife, locally or globally.
