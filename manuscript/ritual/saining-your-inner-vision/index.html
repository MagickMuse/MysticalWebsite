Brigid

A visualization and spoken chant that calls on Brigid to bless the eyes with clear sight, releasing blame, guilt, and shame after facing a hard truth.

Quantities

No materials required

Directions

  1. Close the eyes and envision Brigid standing close, her hands resting gently over them.
  2. Feel her blessing settle into your inner vision, sharpening your sight so the truth can be seen clearly and met without flinching.
  3. Speak the chant aloud or silently, then let the blessing linger for a few breaths before opening the eyes.

Confession, in the sense Saint Brigid practiced it, was never about punishment. When someone came to her troubled by their own mistakes, she "sained" (blessed) their eyes, so they could see the truth of their situation clearly and find resolution. This working carries that same blessing into modern soul-work. It pairs well with an honest conversation with a trusted counselor or friend, time spent journaling, or simply quiet, honest reflection alone.

Close the eyes and envision Brigid standing close, her hands resting gently over them. Feel her blessing settle into your inner vision, sharpening your sight so the truth can be seen clearly and met without flinching, whatever it may be. Then speak, aloud or silently:

In Brigid's name:
Let blame be forgotten,
Let cause be forgiven,
Let fault be abandoned.

Let the blessing linger for a few breaths before opening the eyes, carrying the sense of a clean slate back into the day.
