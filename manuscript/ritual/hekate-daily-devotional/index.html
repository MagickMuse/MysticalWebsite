🙏Hekate Daily Devotional

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Devotional WorkChant/MantraInvocationPrayer
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
Required: - Incense (optional but recommended) - Libation for offering (optional - water, wine, or other liquid) Optional: - Simple altar space - Representation of Hekate (statue, image, or symbol)
◇Directions
1. This devotional should be performed daily to build and maintain a strong relationship with Hekate. 2. It serves as an alternative to daily banishing rituals, which can create barriers to spirit communication. 3. Reserve banishings for times when you feel under attack or potentially faced with dangerous forces. 4. Opening Invocation: 5. Speak the following invocation with clarity and devotion: 6. Hail, many-named mother of the gods, whose children are fair Hail, mighty Hekate of the threshold, keyholder of the world Hail to thee, Enodia, keeper of the four- and three-way crossroads Nether, Nocturnal, and Infernal one I beckon to you Grant me the pleasure of your presence 7. Night Mother! Savior! Mistress of Solitude! Lady of Light, and the darkness that contains it You who walk disheveled and wild through tombs and cremation grounds Cloaked in saffron, crowned with oak leaves and coils of serpents You who are followed by hordes of ghosts, dogs, and restless spirits Yet are also the luminous Empress of the Empyrian realms. I beckon to you Grant me the pleasure of your presence 8. Kleidokhous, grant me the key to the mysteries Propylaia, throw open the gate Phosphoros, light my way with your twin torches of mercy and severity Propolos, steer me safely through the four rivers Brimo, shake the pillars of perception with your wrath Physis, bind all demons and set them into my service Kleidokhous, grant me the key to the mysteries Anassa Eneroi, Queen of the Dead, allow me contact with my ancestors. 9. Hekate Chthonia, Queen of Sorcery Teacher of howlings and bindings Instruct me in the mysteries 10. May the three Fates favor me May the three Graces bless me May the three Gorgons protect me May the three Furies avenge me May the three-faced goddess, Serpent-haired, serpent-girdled, with womb of serpent scales Be here now with my spirit! 11. Mantra Repetition: 12. Repeat the following mantra as many times as possible. 13. One hundred times minimum; one thousand times is preferable: 14. IO HEKA IO HO 15. Continue chanting until you feel that the presence of Hekate has been evoked and stabilized into your sphere. 16. You should sense a shift in the energy around you, a feeling of presence, or other signs that the goddess has answered your call. 17. Offerings: 18. When the presence feels established, move on to offerings. 19. This is a daily practice, so keep it simple: 20. - Rub your hands together vigorously and offer the heat from your hands, OR - Light incense and allow the smoke to rise as an offering, OR - Pour a libation into the ground or into an offering bowl 21. Speak the following words of thanks: 22. I thank you, Hekate, for your presence and blessing. And for the blessings you have given I give offering and praise to you in all your forms I give offering and thanks to the untimely dead who follow you I give offering and thanks to the spirits under your command May all be pleased. May all be at peace. 23. Closing: 24. Ego se etelesa Chairo! 25. Translation: "I have completed it to you. Rejoice!" 26. Notes: 27. Simplicity is key for daily practice. 28. The offering can be as minimal as the heat from your hands. 29. Consistency matters more than elaborate ritual. 30. This devotional opens channels of communication with Hekate and the spirits in her retinue, making it ideal for ongoing magickal work and spirit communication.
◇Description
A daily devotional practice for establishing and maintaining a relationship with Hekate, consisting of invocation, mantra repetition (IO HEKA IO HO), and simple offerings. This practice is recommended as an alternative to daily banishings for practitioners who wish to communicate readily with spirits.
