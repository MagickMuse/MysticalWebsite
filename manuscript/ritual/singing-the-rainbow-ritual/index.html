🌈Singing the Rainbow Ritual

3🔴Red⚪White

A responsive singing ritual chanting seven rainbow colors and powers three times up and down scale, ending in silence, pushing wish to rainbow.

Quantities

  • Nothing needed

Directions

  1. Sing the rainbow colors three times up and down the scale, then sing the seven powers listed below three times, using the same note formation up and down the scale.
  2. Finally, sing the colors up and down the scale three more times.
  3. Reverse the order for the descent, so upward would be red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet; and downward, violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red.
  4. The same applies with power names, red courage, orange joy, yellow power, green love, blue prosperity, indigo health, violet wisdom.
  5. Sing increasingly powerfully or hum the tune and sing the words in your mind with greater speed and intensity.
  6. On the last three sets, reduce the speed and intensity so the final descending color chant ends in silence.
  7. If there is a rainbow in the sky, cup your hands over your mouth, whisper a wish, and gently push it toward the rainbow.
  8. Perform any time you feel worried or sad, best when there's a rainbow in the sky.
