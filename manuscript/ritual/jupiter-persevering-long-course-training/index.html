Ritual Grimoire

Jupiter Persevering Long Course Training

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Knot MagickCord MagickChant/MantraPlanetary
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- A very long piece of blue cord, Jupiter's color - Scissors - A box
◇Directions
1. Tie knots along the cord to represent the stages of the course or training, including those you've completed. 2. Name for each knot the stage it represents and say: "Each milestone that is passed, from first until the last, brings me closer to the end. Jupiter, perseverance send." Moving left to right, cut each knot and the cord to the left of it to represent stages already passed, repeating the spell words. 3. Put the cut cords into the box, saying: "When I doubt and when I fear, by Jupiter I shall know. Success does daily nearer grow." Each time you reach a milestone in your training, repeat the spell, adding more cut cords to the box until you have only the knot at the end. 4. Keep this as a talisman for your future career. 5. Perform Thursday, Jupiter's day.
◇Description
A Thursday Jupiter's day spell for Jupiter ritual for persevering with long course of study or training if road seems endless, tying knots along blue cord for each stage and cutting completed ones for Jupiter to send perseverance and bring success daily nearer.
