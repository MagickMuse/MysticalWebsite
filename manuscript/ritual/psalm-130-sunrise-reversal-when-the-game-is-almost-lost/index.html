🌅Psalm 130 Sunrise Reversal (When the Game Is Almost Lost)

◇Form
Writing SpellCandle RiteAltar Working
◇Method
Ancestor WorkPrayerCandle MagickReversalPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Piece of paper (any sturdy paper works) Pen Candle
◇Directions
1. Rise before dawn. 2. Write the name of your opponent or oppressor on one side of a piece of paper. 3. On the reverse side, write out Psalm 130 carefully and with devotion, taking a moment to truly pray each line: 4. > Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord. 5. Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications. 6. If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? 7. But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared. 8. I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. 9. My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning. 10. Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. 11. And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. 12. Light a candle and place it on your ancestor altar so it burns over the paper. 13. The room should otherwise be dark. 14. Watch the sun rise.
◇Description
A pre-dawn working for desperate circumstances. The name of an opponent or oppressor is written on paper, Psalm 130 is handwritten on the reverse with full devotion, and a candle is burned over it on the ancestor altar while the sun rises. Combines written petition, psalm prayer, and elevation of the dead.
