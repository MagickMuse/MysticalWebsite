⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A dark witchcraft spell using the ace of spades, long associated with death and dark forces, to create illness for the target. The card, name paper, and earth-buried cloth are nailed together, buried, then burned.

Quantities

1 ace of spades (from a deck of playing cards)
1 rusted nail
1 piece of black cloth
1 piece of paper
1 black marker
1 sturdy base (piece of wood)

Directions

  1. Begin by burying a piece of black cloth in the ground and leave it for three days.
  2. Dig it up when ready.
  3. Lay out the ace of spades from a deck of playing cards.
  4. Place a piece of paper with the target's name written in black marker on top of the card.
  5. Cover both with the buried cloth.
  6. Drive a rusted nail through the center of all three layers, using a sturdy surface like a piece of wood underneath.
  7. Speak the following incantation aloud:
  8. By the power of my will,
    make this person get very ill.
    Magick power, ace of spades,
    many days until it fades.
  9. Bury the entire charm in the same spot where the cloth was originally buried.
  10. Leave it undisturbed for three more days, then dig it up and burn it to complete the spell.
