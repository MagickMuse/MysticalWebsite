Orange

Sunny orange embodies all the power of the Sun with its yellow-orange hue that screams positivity and creativity. When your spirits need a boost, use this spell.

Quantities

  • Orange juice (as needed for writing)
  • Biodegradable paper
  • Writing implement (feather quill, chopstick, knitting needle, or orange itself)

Directions

  1. Prepare your writing implement. Use it like a quill pen, dipping it in orange juice.
  2. On biodegradable paper, write:
    Sunny orange, light up my day
    Lead me from the blues, show me the way!
    Lif ting, lightening, happy heart
    Creative, inventive, a fresh, new start!
  3. Keep your paper close to you.
  4. Give it a sniff if you need extra motivation.
  5. Once the spell has done its job, burn the paper with a word of thanks to your orange ally.
