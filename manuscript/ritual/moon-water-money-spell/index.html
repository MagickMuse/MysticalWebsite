🌙Moon Water Money Spell

◇Form
PotionCharm
◇Method
LunarWater MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A deep glass bowl half filled with water - Seven silver coins - If it is a dark night, place silver candles around the bowl
◇Directions
1. Outdoors, position the bowl so that light shines into the water. 2. Drop all seven coins into the water and then plunge your hands three times into the silver water. 3. Say: "Fill my hands with magick gold. I gather all that I can hold. Come, lasting prosperity; give to me all I can see." Hold your hands above the bowl and allow the water to drip from your fingers. 4. Leave the moon water outside overnight and then fill three small bottles with it, placing a coin in each. 5. Sprinkle a few drops on your doorstep throughout the month to draw in money. 6. Perform on the night of the full moon or the nights immediately before or after one.
◇Description
A full moon water spell to call in money month after month, dropping seven silver coins into moonlit water and plunging hands three times to gather magick gold and lasting prosperity.
