⚠️ This entry draws on a New Orleans folk magick tradition. It is documented here for educational reference.

A seven-herb protective formulation called Holy Herbs, used in New Orleans folk tradition to protect children. The blend is ground to a powder, brewed as an infusion, and either added to the child's bathwater or sprinkled through their room.

Materials

Equal parts of each of the following seven dried herbs:

  • Catnip
  • Black cohosh root (black snake root)
  • Hops
  • Jasmine blossoms
  • Motherwort
  • Peppermint
  • Skullcap

Also needed:

  • A tightly sealed jar for storage
  • Boiling water
  • A strainer
  • An asperging tool (optional)

Instructions

  1. Grind equal parts of all seven herbs together into a powder.
  2. Store the powder in a tightly shut jar until needed.
  3. When spiritual protection is required, pour boiling water over the herbal blend to make an infusion.
  4. Allow the brew to cool completely, then strain out the solids.
  5. Add the liquid to the child's bathwater, or sprinkle it onto the child using an asperging tool.
  6. Sprinkle the remaining liquid into each corner of the child's room.
