🔴Exu Marabo Healing Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group

◇Form

◇Setting

◇Method

◇Category

◇Difficulty

◇Closed Practice
Afro-Brazilian Quimbanda
◇Quantities
Wormwood (twigs, leaves, and/or roots) Lit charcoal Red candles (red devil candles may be substituted) White candles Fine cigars Fine rum
◇Directions
1. Crush and grind wormwood twigs, leaves, and/or roots. 2. Sprinkle the ground wormwood onto lit charcoal so that it smokes. 3. Carve Exu Marabo's sigil into both red and white candles before lighting them. 4. Have fine cigars and rum at the ready as potential offerings. 5. Exu Marabo's acceptance of the invitation is signaled by the scent of sulfur (brimstone). 6. If and when you detect this scent, tell him clearly what you need.
◇Description
⚠️ DANGEROUS WORKING. This spell is from Afro-Brazilian Quimbanda tradition, a closed practice, approach only with deep knowledge of and respect for its originating culture. Exu Marabo is a volatile, potentially malevolent spirit; many equate petitioning him to calling on Satan. This is a last-resort working when all other healing options have failed. WARNING: Once invited, Exu Marabo cannot be asked to leave. He moves in permanently. You will be obligated to feed and appease him with gifts and prayers indefinitely. The exus tend to mirror the expectations of those who call them, the more fearful and ambivalent the petitioner, the more dangerous their behavior.
