A single-use purification broom is hand-crafted from cleansing botanicals (broom, cedar, fennel, hyssop, rosemary, sage, and/or vervain), tied to a branch with raffia using knot magick and visualization. After sweeping, the broom is disassembled outside and the components buried or cast into flowing water. One of the Broom Cleansing methods.

Quantities

  • One or more: broom, cedar, fennel, hyssop, rosemary, sage, vervain
  • Branch (ash preferred)
  • Raffia
  • Salted water or purification formula

Directions

  1. Use one or any combination of the botanicals: broom, cedar, fennel, hyssop, rosemary, sage, vervain.
  2. Arrange the botanicals and tie them to the bottom of a branch with raffia, visualizing, charging, and knotting.
  3. An ash branch is considered particularly powerful.
  4. Sprinkle with salted water or any preferred purification formula.
  5. Sweep the area.
  6. Disassemble the broom outside, away from the cleansed space.
  7. Bury the components in the ground or toss them into living waters flowing away from you.
