⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A short, forceful curse in which a piece of leather is pricked nine times and laid over the target's image. The bundle is either buried in barren ground or sealed in an earthenware pot.

Quantities

1 piece of leather
1 picture of the target
1 sharp pricking tool
1 earthenware pot (optional)

Directions

  1. Prick a piece of leather nine times.
  2. Lay the leather over a picture of your enemy and chant the following:
  3. Damn thee to hell, to the pits of suffering!
  4. Corruption of your soul until death!
  5. So be it.
  6. Either bury the leather and picture together in a shallow grave in a place where nothing will grow, or tie the leather around the picture and place it inside an earthenware pot to stow away somewhere hidden.
