🪙Ongoing Wealth Accumulation Wax Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Candle MagickLunarSolarManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A solid gold-colored wax candle - A large flat metal tray - Three silver coins - A paper knife
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and, looking into the flame, say: "Burn, flame, burn; turn Dame Fortune's smile to me, for I will use prosperity wisely and well." When the candle is melted, press the three silver coins into the pool of wax in a triangle shape on the tray. 2. With the paper knife draw a clockwise circle of dollar signs (or your own currency signs) in the wax. 3. Cut a circle of wax to include the coins and the currency signs and gently lift it out. 4. Leave the wax and coins where moonlight will fill them overnight and then until noon. 5. Wrap it in soft cloth and hide it where you do your finances. 6. Replace when it crumbles. 7. Perform on the full moon.
◇Description
A full moon ongoing wealth accumulation spell, pressing three silver coins into melted gold wax with currency signs drawn clockwise, charged by moonlight and sunlight for lasting prosperity.
