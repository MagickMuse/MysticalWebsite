A spell to attract the perfect romantic partner using your carefully compiled love list. Uses anointed candle magick and detailed visualization repeated over the waxing moon cycle to draw your ideal lover to you.

Quantities

  • Ylang-ylang, rose, or jasmine essential oil
  • 1 rose-colored (pink) candle
  • List of qualities you seek in a partner
  • Matches or lighter
  • Candlestick or fireproof container

Directions

  1. Cast a circle.
  2. Pour a little oil in your palm, then rub it over the entire candle except the wick.
  3. Set your list of desired qualities on your altar or working surface, and place the candle in a candlestick or fireproof container on top of the list.
  4. Light the candle.
  5. As the scent of the heated oil wafts into the air, vividly imagine your lover.
  6. Feel this person's presence right there in the room with you.
  7. How does this person look, act, speak, and dress?
  8. See as much detail as possible.
  9. What type of work do they do?
  10. What are their passions?
  11. Continue the visualization for as long as you like, making your mental images as rich as possible.
  12. When you have spent as much time as necessary, snuff out the candle flame and open the circle.
  13. Repeat this spell two more times before the full moon.
  14. On the night of the full moon, release your wish by allowing the candle to finish burning down completely.
  15. Express thanks for the love you are about to receive.
