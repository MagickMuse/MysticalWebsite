✨Curse Removal

A shamanic practice of transforming into animal form to access knowledge denied to humans. The caster chooses an animal based on desired goals, then regularly imitates and 'becomes' that creature in private. An animal ally altar may be built for assistance. Unexpected animal allies may reveal themselves; be prepared.

Quantities

  • Optional: altar materials for your chosen animal ally
    Choose your animal by goal:
  • Abundance, creativity, fertility: fish, frogs, cats, pigs, rabbits
  • After-life mysteries: bears, dogs, jackals, ravens, seagulls, swans, vultures, wolves
  • Occult secrets: snakes, crocodiles
  • Secrets of Earth: badgers, bears, snakes
  • Spiritual pursuits: birds, lizards

Directions

  1. Choose your animal based on your desired goals.
  2. Learn to shape-shift and prevent detection by imitating the creature.
  3. Access the childlike aspects of yourself and play.
  4. When you have some privacy, pretend to be the creature you choose.
  5. Practice becoming one or several animals regularly and observe what happens.
  6. For added enhancement, build an animal ally altar dedicated to your chosen creature and request personal assistance and tutoring in your quest.
  7. Do not be surprised if another creature shows up.
  8. Given the opportunity, animal allies may reveal themselves as orishas, lwas, and other spirits do, through ritual possession.
