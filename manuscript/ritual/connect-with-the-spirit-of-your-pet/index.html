🐾Connect with the Spirit of Your Pet

◇Quantities
- Coloring tools of your choice - Coloring page featuring your pet or animals - Journal or paper for writing - Quiet, comfortable space - Optional: soothing or joyful music
◇Directions
1. Gather your coloring tools. 2. Find a quiet space where you can be alone and get comfortable. 3. Set the lighting so it's pleasing. 4. Put on some joyful or soothing music if you like. 5. Take a couple of deep breaths. 6. Close your eyes and picture the pet you want to connect with, whether living in the physical or living in Spirit. 7. Pay attention to the details of how they appear in your mind. 8. Sit with this image for a bit and allow yourself to feel, hear, or sense any connection or message. 9. Once you've established your initial connection, open your eyes. 10. Take a moment to write your intention for your practice. 11. Do you want to hear how your beloved is doing? 12. Is there a concern you want insight on? 13. Is there a feeling or message you want to impart to them? 14. Whatever your goal, jot it down. 15. Continue by coloring in an image. 16. As you do, let your mind wander and see where it takes you. 17. Allow impressions, feelings, and messages to flow. 18. When you finish your practice, write down what you experienced. 19. Cut out your coloring page and place it somewhere you'll see it, on your bathroom mirror, nightstand, or altar. 20. Allow it to be a reminder of this meaningful and loving connection. 21. Pay attention to any unexpected or surprising thoughts that pop into your mind as you go about your day, as well as any dreams you have that stick with you when you wake. 22. Also watch for notable actions from your physically present animal companions. 23. These may all be reverberations of continued messages coming through from your beloved pet and the deep connection you've made with them.
◇Description
A meditation practice to connect with the spirit of your animal companion, whether living or in spirit. Through breathwork, visualization, intentional coloring, and journaling, you open a channel for subtle communication with your beloved pet.

About This Practice

This meditation works for animal companions living in the physical or in Spirit. The process for connecting with both is the same.

What to Expect

Connection with those who have passed may feel easier, likely because with living pets, we're so focused on the physical messages we're more blatantly sending one another. But subtle connection is very possible with both.

The messages may come as:

  • Unexpected thoughts or impressions
  • Visions in your mind's eye
  • An overpowering feeling of warmth and love spreading outward
  • A sense of presence during meditation or stressful times
  • Dreams that feel significant
  • Notable behaviors from living animal companions

The Coloring Component

Using coloring as a meditative tool allows your conscious mind to focus on a simple creative task while your subconscious remains open to receiving messages. The physical act of creating honors the connection and produces a tangible reminder of your practice.

After the Practice

Remain receptive in the days following. Our beloved pets often continue reaching out through:

  • Spontaneous memories that feel like visits
  • Dreams where they appear healthy and happy
  • Moments of comfort during difficult times
  • Living pets acting as messengers or comforters
