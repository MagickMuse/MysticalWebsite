A banishing hex using absinthe and simple syrup to remove a harmful person from your life. The working has the added benefit of bringing happiness and relieving anxiety for the caster.

Quantities

  • ½ shot of absinthe
  • Simple syrup (chilled if possible) to fill the rest of the shot glass

Directions

  1. The topic of hexing can be very polarizing.
  2. People may assume I'm advocating putting the time and energy into cursing everyone that ever looked crossways at me or may have overblown fears of scary ingredients like the eye of a newt and toe of frog.
  3. Banishing a harmful person from your life requires intent and a few tools.
  4. Laying a hex with wormwood has the added benefit of bringing happiness and relieving anxiety for the caster.
  5. Pour half a shot of absinthe, and fill the rest of the shot glass with simple syrup (chilled if possible).
  6. Hold the shot to your lips, whisper their name across the surface of the liquid, and state your intended outcome.
  7. Drink.
  8. Make sure you feel the energy, to empower the working.
