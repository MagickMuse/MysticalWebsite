Brown Candle

A brown justice candle is carved and dressed to accompany and fortify the beef tongue courtcase spells, serving as a plea for justice throughout the legal proceedings. This is a Hoodoo-rooted practice, please be mindful that this is a closed practice. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Quantities

A brown candle
Carving tool, for dressing the candle

Directions

  1. Carve and dress a brown candle so that it aligns thematically with your beef tongue spell.
  2. Choose when and where to burn the candle: while the tongue simmers on the stove, continuously from when the tongue enters the freezer until the case is resolved, on the stove beside the cooking tongue, on an altar dedicated to a deity or set up for your situation, or atop the toilet tank in the bathroom.
  3. Burn the candle as a simultaneous plea for justice, reinforcing and fortifying your beef tongue working.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: The beef tongue courtcase spells, including their candle companions, originate in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo traditions. These are closed practices. Please research and respect the cultural boundaries before working with these methods.

Brown justice candles are the natural complement to beef tongue spells. The variations are as plentiful as those within the tongue spell itself.
