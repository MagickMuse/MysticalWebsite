A two-part working for identifying and escaping a sukuyan, a Caribbean vampiric spirit. To identify her, scatter a large quantity of rice at a crossroads and observe whether anyone is compelled to pick it up grain by grain. To escape her hold once gained, repeat a specific verbal charm three times and protect all entry points with crosses and a mirror.

Quantities

  • 100 pounds (approx. 45 kg) of rice (for identification)
  • A mirror (to hang outside entrance doors)

Directions

  1. To expose a suspected sukuyan: empty a large quantity of rice at a crossroads.
  2. The sukuyan will be compelled to pick it up grain by grain, leaving her exposed to identification at sunrise.
  3. To escape the sukuyan's hold once she has gained access to you: murmur the phrase Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday three times.
  4. Mark a cross over every door and window in your home.
  5. Hang a mirror on the outside of your entrance doors.
  6. Leave the mirror in place until all danger has passed.
