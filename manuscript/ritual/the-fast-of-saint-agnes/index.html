📅January 21

A love divination ritual involving a day-long fast on January 20, followed by eating a salted egg and walking backward to bed while chanting.

Quantities

  • 1 egg
  • Salt
  • Bed and nightclothes

Directions

  1. Fast all day on 20 January, concentrating on your desires.
  2. At night, before proceeding, get ready for bed: bathe, put on night clothes, fix your bed.
  3. The following part demands complete silence. Do not utter a sound until it is time to chant.
  4. Hard-boil an egg. When ready, cut it in half and remove the yolk.
  5. Fill the egg's cavity with salt.
  6. Put the two halves back together and eat the egg and salt. (Traditionally, the shell is left on and also consumed, but this is optional.)
  7. Walk backward to bed chanting:
    "Sweet Saint Agnes, work your fast
    In my dreams, let this spell be cast.
    If ever I should marry a man or a man marry me
    I hope this night his face to see!"
  8. Resume silence until morning; the oracle should appear in your dreams.

Purpose

This ritual is performed on Saint Agnes' Eve (the night of January 20) to receive a dream vision of your future spouse. It involves a day-long fast followed by a ritual meal and incantation.

Timing

The fast begins on January 20 and the ritual is completed that night, before Saint Agnes' feast day on January 21.

The Ritual

Part 1: The Fast

  1. Fast all day on January 20, concentrating on your desires.
  2. Prepare for bed at night: bathe, put on night clothes, fix your bed.

Part 2: The Ritual (in complete silence)

  1. Begin silence: The following part demands complete silence. Do not utter a sound until it is time to chant.
  2. Prepare the egg: Hard-boil an egg. When ready, cut it in half and remove the yolk.
  3. Fill with salt: Fill the egg's cavity with salt.
  4. Eat the egg: Put the two halves back together and eat the egg and salt. (Traditionally, the shell is left on and also consumed, but this is optional.)

Part 3: The Incantation

  1. Walk backward to bed while chanting:

"Sweet Saint Agnes, work your fast

In my dreams, let this spell be cast.

If ever I should marry a man or a man marry me

I hope this night his face to see!"

  1. Resume silence until morning; the oracle should appear in your dreams.

Important Notes

  • The fast strengthens the ritual's power.
  • Silence is critical except during the chant.
  • Walking backward connects to liminal magic traditions.
