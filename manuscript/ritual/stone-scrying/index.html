A traditional divination method using reflective, polished stones to develop psychic awareness and contact the subconscious mind. Most commonly performed with quartz crystal spheres, but also effective with obsidian, beryl, or naturally reflective stones like cat's eye, moonstone, sunstone, tiger's eye, star ruby, and opal.

Quantities

  • Quartz crystal sphere or other reflective stone (obsidian, beryl, cat's eye, moonstone, sunstone, tiger's eye, star ruby, or opal)
  • Black, yellow, or white cloth for wrapping
  • Water for initial cleansing
  • Optional: white or yellow candles
  • Optional: stand or table for the sphere

Directions

  1. Wash the sphere in water, dry thoroughly, and wrap in black, yellow, or white cloth.
  2. Store wrapped when not in use.
  3. Traditionally, scrying spheres are kept from direct sunlight, though moonlight is used for purification and charging.
  4. To charge the sphere with Full Moon energy, take the wrapped stone outside during the Full Moon.
  5. Unwrap it and hold it up to the Moon with both hands.
  6. Visualize the moonlight flooding the crystal and attuning it with your energy.
  7. See yourself successfully scrying with the sphere.
  8. After a few moments, wrap it again.
  9. For scrying, work at night in a quiet spot where you will not be interrupted.
  10. Sit comfortably and place the crystal on a stand or hold it in your hands.
  11. Experiment with candlelight to find what works best for you.
  12. You might place white or yellow candles behind your back, to either side, or surrounding the crystal.
  13. Once situated, close your eyes and breathe deeply for a few moments.
  14. Release the worries and stresses of the day.
  15. Relax your body and mind completely.
  16. Open your eyes and hold the crystal in your hands until it warms.
  17. As your hands warm the stone, visualize your area of inquiry.
  18. Then replace the stone on the stand or continue holding it, whichever feels comfortable.
  19. Gaze into the crystal with a relaxed focus.
  20. Blink naturally and remain calm.
  21. Hold in mind that the crystal is a symbol of psychism, of Water, of your need to divine the future.
  22. If the operation is successful, you will contact your psychic mind and communication will take place.
  23. You may see wisps of smoke swirling within the sphere.
  24. Images may appear in your mind rather than within the stone itself.
  25. Unbidden thoughts, words, phrases, or complete sentences may arise from your psychic mind.
  26. Symbols seen during scrying are often symbolic rather than literal.
  27. Interpret them according to your personal associations and the context of your question.
  28. Alternate method for other reflective stones: Take the stone into sunlight, moonlight, or near a candle.
  29. Still your conscious mind and move the stone slowly in your hands while visualizing your area of inquiry.
  30. Continue for several minutes without forcing anything to happen.
  31. Allow the movements in the stone and the hypnotic motion of your hands to open your psychic awareness.
  32. Interpret any symbols that appear.
