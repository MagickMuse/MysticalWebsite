🤫You Need Me Professional Success Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Chant/MantraPetitionEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- An object from the target's desk or office
◇Directions
1. Discreetly pick up a needed object from the person's desk or office. 2. Hold it in your hands and focus your thoughts and desires. 3. Murmur over the object, chant silently if necessary, something to the effect of: '[Supervisor's name], you need me like you need this [name the object].' Replace the object exactly as you found it. 4. Reinforce by repeating as needed.
◇Description
A whispered charm for gaining favor with a specific person who holds power over your career. An object from that person's desk is held and murmured over with a petition tying their need for you to their need for the object, then replaced exactly as found. Ideal for personal assistants or secretaries with access to the supervisor's office.
