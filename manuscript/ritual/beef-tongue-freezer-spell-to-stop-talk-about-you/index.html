🧊Beef Tongue Freezer Spell (To Stop Talk About You)

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingInvocationPlanetary
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
Required: - Fresh beef tongue: 1 - Paper - Writing implement - Seal of Saturn (drawn or printed) - Thread and needle (for sewing) - Binding oils and powders (optional) - Material links to target (photo, name paper, etc.) - Freezer space
◇Directions
1. This spell literally freezes the tongue of gossip and binds your enemy from speaking ill of you. 2. Prepare your link to the target, this can be a photograph, a piece of paper with their name written on it, or other personal concerns. 3. On a piece of paper, draw or print the Seal of Saturn. 4. Write the target's name on the paper and place the seal directly over their name so it covers the name completely. 5. This symbolizes Saturn's binding force silencing their words. 6. If desired, dress the paper with oils and powders associated with binding, such as slippery elm powder, black pepper, or binding oil. 7. Take the beef tongue and carefully slice it open lengthwise, creating a pocket large enough to hold the prepared paper and any other links to your target. 8. Place the paper with the Seal of Saturn and the target's name inside the tongue, along with any additional material links. 9. Sew the tongue back together using strong thread, making sure it is securely closed so nothing can fall out. 10. As you place the sewn tongue in the back of your freezer (where it is coldest and most forgotten), speak this invocation: 11. "Ereshigal, Nocticula, Hekate Grow forth and witness my rite! Seize my enemy's tongue! May not even his words exist! Sigy! Sigy! Sigy! So shall it be!" 12. Leave the tongue in the freezer. 13. As long as it remains frozen, your enemy's ability to speak against you remains frozen as well. 14. The tongue should stay in the freezer indefinitely. 15. If you ever wish to release the binding, you can thaw and dispose of the tongue by burying it at a crossroads or in running water.
◇Description
A silencing spell that uses a beef tongue, the Seal of Saturn, and freezing to bind an enemy from speaking about you or spreading rumors. The target's name is covered with Saturn's seal, placed inside the tongue, sewn shut, and frozen while invoking deities of the underworld and silence.
