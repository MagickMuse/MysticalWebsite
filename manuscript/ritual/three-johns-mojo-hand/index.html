A mojo hand combining High John the Conqueror, Low John the Conqueror (bethroot/trillium), and Southern John (galangal) roots, dressed with Magnet Oil and Van Van, and carried in a red bag for protection and good fortune.

⚠️ Low John the Conqueror (bethroot/trillium) is derived from an extremely endangered plant family. Source sustainably and use with care.

⚠️ Three Johns Mojo Hand is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

High John the Conqueror root
Low John the Conqueror root (bethroot/trillium), source sustainably
Southern John (galangal) root
Magnet Oil
Van Van Oil
Red drawstring bag

Directions

  1. Place High John the Conqueror, Low John the Conqueror (bethroot or trillium), and Southern John (galangal) roots together in a red drawstring bag.
  2. Dress all three roots with Magnet Oil and Van Van.
  3. Carry the mojo hand on the body and re-anoint with oil weekly to keep the working active.
