🤍White Bath for Troubled Hearts

A soothing spiritual bath for anxiety, depression, or any time you feel off your baseline. Rooted in espiritismo practice, first shared by Katelan Foisy. Uses white, sweet ingredients to ease sorrow and invite calm. The main rule: include white, sweet things, ideally in an odd number so bad luck cannot divide them.

Quantities

  • Goat milk
  • Cow milk
  • Coconut milk
  • Holy water
  • Fresh white flowers (carnations or white roses)
  • Florida water
  • Cascarilla (white eggshell powder)

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.
  2. Take three deep, cleansing breaths.
  3. Close your eyes and take a moment to describe how you feel to your ancestors, taking as long as you need.
  4. Pray: "Ancestors, I pray that you will bless this bath so that it will soothe me. May my sorrows be eased so I can enjoy life and support the people I love." Either add the mixture to a soaking bath, or combine with warm water and pour over your head.

Overview

First shared by Katelan Foisy; since then, variants have been widely circulated among practitioners of espiritismo. This bath is appropriate any time you feel anxious, depressed, or otherwise off your baseline.

Prayer

Speak this before or during the bath:

Ancestors, I pray that you will bless this bath so that it will soothe me.
May my sorrows be eased so I can enjoy life and support the people I love.

Notes

The white and sweet rule: You can substitute ingredients based on what you have on hand. The guiding principle is to use white, sweet things.

The odd number rule: Some practitioners prefer an odd number of ingredients so that bad luck or a curse cannot "cut through" (divide) the ingredients.

Florida water substitutes: If Florida water is unavailable, substitute another sweet cologne, rose water, or a pinch of sugar. It can be found at botánicas, some drug stores, or online.

Cascarilla: A white powder made from crushed eggshells, used for cleansing and protection. Sold at botánicas as small chalk-like nubs in paper cups; also available online.
