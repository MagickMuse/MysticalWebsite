⚫Black⚪White

A simple yes/no divination using dark and light colored stones. A stone is drawn randomly with the receptive hand to answer questions: dark stones indicate yes or favorable prospects, light stones indicate no.

Quantities

  • Equal numbers of dark and light colored stones (enough for half dark, half light)

Directions

  1. Spend a few minutes gathering stones, ensuring half are dark and half are lighter in color.
  2. Place them on the ground before you.
  3. Ask your question or hold it clearly in mind.
  4. Close your eyes and jumble the stones around for a few seconds.
  5. With your left or receptive hand, remove one stone.
  6. If you picked a dark stone, the answer is yes or the prospects are favorable.
  7. If you picked a light stone, the answer is no.
