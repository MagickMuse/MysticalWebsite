Samhain

A three-day spell to rewrite your fate using runes and mugwort. Place troubling runes on a plate, overlay with desired outcome runes, surround with mugwort, and visualize transformation for three days. Can include offerings or petitions to the Fates.

Quantities

  • Set of runes
  • Plate
  • Mugwort
  • Rune dictionary (optional)
  • Offering or petition letter (optional)

Directions

  1. A few things in life are fated.
  2. Most, however, you can change.
  3. This spell is all about rewriting your fate, at least when there isn't some life lesson written in what's already coming.
  4. The Ritual:
  5. You will need a set of runes, a plate to rest them on, mugwort, and a dictionary that details rune meanings if you do not already have them memorized.
  6. You may also wish to make an offering or write a letter of petition to the Fates or the face of fate in your specific tradition.
  7. In your sacred space, take out three runes that represent the situation at hand or the three runes that troubled you when you gave yourself a reading.
  8. Set those out on the plate.
  9. Next, set the runes that represent your desired outcome on top of the original runes.
  10. Surround these with mugwort.
  11. Visualize the runes glowing, eventually making the energy of the runes beneath them dust, where only your desired fate remains.
  12. Leave the runes undisturbed in their herbal circle for three days.
  13. Samhain is a time when all things are possible.
  14. It is also a time to create new possibilities.
  15. Use this time as the ground grows colder to shape what might greet you in the spring.
  16. The air is rich with magick, so use this time to partake of that particular fruit!
