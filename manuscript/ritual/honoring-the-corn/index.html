🌽Honoring the Corn

◇Form
Circle RitePoppetOffering Rite
◇Method
InvocationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Large supply of corn husks, sticks, sheaves of wheat or other plant matter (for effigy) - Heavy string or twine - Heavy‑duty scissors - Offerings: fruit, bread, ale, mead, wine, water (your choice) - Simple snack or meal to share - Chair (and small pillow) - Optional: paint, cup of water (for harvest‑themed circle casting), hand drum, flowers or wreaths
◇Directions
1. Purpose: Honor the spirit of vegetation, express gratitude, and attune to the seasonal flow. Setting: Late afternoon into evening, outdoors (yard/garden, waterside, field, hilltop, or other peaceful spot). 2. Pre-ritual reflections: Consider the first harvest, literal and metaphorical. 3. Acknowledge the sacrifice behind the bounty and notice any gratitude, sorrow, or fear that arises. 4. Place a chair (with pillow if desired) within the space. 5. Make the effigy: - Gather plant matter. Choose a sturdy stick as the spine. Bundle corn husks/wheat/greens around it and bind with twine to form the torso. - Tie on sticks/plant matter for arms, legs, and head. Smooth and bulk with corn husks. Add hair with wheat. Paint a simple face on a corn husk and lash to the head. 6. Optional circle casting (harvest-themed): - Fill a cup with water (life and fertility). Hold a sprig of dried wheat (sacrifice and bounty). Dip wheat into water and, as a wand, project love and light to form an orb of protective power. All raise open palms to contribute loving energy. Seal the boundary so desired energies stay within and only invited powers enter. 7. Invocations: - Welcome any deities/powers you work with. Reserve the Spirit of Vegetation for last. Place the effigy at center or on the altar. 8. Calling in the spirit (all hands on the effigy): - Focus thoughts and emotion on the land, vegetation, and harvest. Chant in unison: Spirit of the harvest, spirit of the land, Come into your body; come in through our hands! - Continue until a shift is sensed (heat, pulsing, vibrancy, presence). Leader places one hand on head and one on chest. Others begin to walk clockwise to raise energy; steady heartbeat drumming helps. - Leader chants in a rising crescendo until complete: The spirit resides within this, and within this the spirit is sewn! 9. Honor and celebrate: - Seat the effigy on the chair, add pillow for comfort. Offer libations (ale, water, etc.) to its mouth. - Drum and dance; take turns dancing with the spirit. Speak thanks for the present harvest and for continued tending through the season. - As energy naturally settles, slow the drumming. Adorn the effigy with flowers or wreaths. Leave it in place or give it a prominent garden spot until later harvest rites. 10. Close: Share the simple meal together and ground. 11. Release any protective boundary in the manner you cast it.
◇Description
A joyful Lughnasadh rite that creates and invites the Spirit of Vegetation into a corn‑husk effigy, honors the first harvest, and deepens connection to the land.
