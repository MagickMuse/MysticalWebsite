🌙Fertility and Pregnancy Spell

A waxing Moon spell performed up to one day before the Full Moon, invoking Artemis as the High Fruitful Mother. A pale green votive is anointed with rose oil and inscribed with the runes Jera for fruitful harvest, Wunjo for joy, and Tiwaz for victory. Clover consecrates the space, offerings of milk and corn are raised to the Goddess and God, and the herbs cassia, vervain, carnation, woodruff, and cinquefoil are dropped into the candle flame. Damiana tea is drunk on the night of planned conception.

Quantities

  • 1 pale green votive candle
  • Clover
  • Cassia
  • Cinquefoil
  • Vervain
  • Carnation petal
  • Woodruff
  • Rose oil
  • Damiana tea
  • Offerings of milk and corn
  • Bowls for offerings

Directions

  1. Set up the altar, cast the Circle, and call the Elementals.
  2. Light the votive.
  3. Sprinkle clover on the altar and speak:
  4. This space is consecrated by Earth and Moon, that I may call upon Thee, Great Bountiful Mother of All!
  5. Rub the votive candle with rose oil.
  6. Inscribe the runes Jera, Wunjo, and Tiwaz, then light the candle and speak:
  7. Here is the sign of that which I seek: the bountiful harvest, brings joy and victory with the fruit of my womb!
  8. Hold up the bowls of corn and milk and speak:
  9. Accept my gifts, High Fruitful Mother and Harvest Father!
  10. Bring unto me Thy gifts of life and milk!
  11. Combine the herbs and drop them into the votive candle flame, speaking:
  12. Cassia for fertility; vervain for love; carnation petal, for the Goddess; woodruff for the God; cinquefoil for Motherhood: for as thou hast thine, grant that I have mine.
  13. Great Artemis, you have your child, now grant me mine, for I call upon you to bless me with thy bounty.
  14. Let the candle and herbs burn down, then snuff the candle and speak:
  15. Through thy Power, Great Lady, do I bind all power within this circle into this spell.
  16. So Mote It Be!
  17. Farewell the Elementals and open the Circle.
  18. Leave offerings overnight on the altar and discard in the morning.
  19. Bury the candle remains when cooled or dispose of them in the trash.
  20. Drink damiana tea on the night of planned conception.
