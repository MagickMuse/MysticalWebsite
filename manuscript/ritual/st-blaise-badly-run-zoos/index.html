🦁St Blaise Badly Run Zoos

A Friday morning ritual with eight toy animals, cutting pictures of exploited animals, invoking St. Blaise for animal liberation.

Quantities

  • 8 small ceramic or toy animals of ferocious or exotic species
  • 1 picture of wild animal in natural habitat
  • 1 picture of same species in cramped cage or performing
  • Scissors
  • 1 open pot
  • 1 handheld electric fan

Directions

  1. Place the toy animals in a circle, with the pictures side by side in the center, saying, "Good St. Blaise, who healed wild creatures in your cave, from cruelty and exploitation, I ask these creatures save." Take the picture of the exploited creature and cut it in pieces, saying, "Good St. Blaise, you turned no creature away, release from captivity these hurt ones, I pray." Name any causes dear to your heart.
  2. Throw the pieces into an open pot and dispose of them after the spell.
  3. Spiral the fan over the picture of the free creature and the model ones, repeating both sets of spell words.
  4. Put the picture of the free creature where you see it every day.
  5. Arrange the toy animals in your garden or among potted plants.
  6. Perform early Friday.
