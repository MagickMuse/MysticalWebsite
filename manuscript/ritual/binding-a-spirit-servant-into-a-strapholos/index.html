Hekate's Crossroads Sigil

A spell to bind a spirit-servant into a strapholos, so that when the Devotee calls upon Hekate, the Iynx compels her, or her servant, to come.

Quantities

  • Small glass bottle with a narrow mouth
  • White candle and matches
  • Salt and spring water (for salt water)
  • Pinch of graveyard dirt, ideally of one who died young
  • Myrrh, for smoke
  • Ash saved from burning the Crossroads Sigil (from the rites of The Call)
  • Feather and/or snakeskin
  • Cork and sealing wax
  • A simple two-holed strapholos and string
  • Something to inscribe the strapholos with

Directions

  1. Hold the mouth of the empty glass bottle over a lit candle flame until the flame is drawn inside and extinguished, trapping the flame within.
  2. Add to the bottle: your own saliva, a little salt water, a pinch of graveyard dirt (ideally of one who died young), smoke from burning a pinch of myrrh, and some of the ash saved from burning the Crossroads Sigil during The Call.
  3. Add a feather, a piece of snakeskin, or ideally both.
  4. Seal the bottle with the cork, then seal the cork with wax.
  5. Bury the sealed bottle in the earth for three days. As you bury it, say, in the name of Hekate-Persephone-Selene: "As Zagreus was buried in Persephone's womb, so let this earth bear you!"
  6. After three days, dig up the bottle and set it underwater for three days. As you submerge it, say: "As Dionysus was buried in Semele's womb, so let this water bear you!"
  7. After three days, retrieve the bottle and hang it from the branch of a tree so it is suspended in open air for three days. As you hang it, say: "As Sabazios was buried in Zeus' womb, so let this air bear you!"
  8. While the spirit gestates across these nine days, fashion or purchase a simple strapholos and inscribe upon it the Sigil of the Crossroads and the letters of Hekate's name. It will house the Iynx and, used correctly, will compel divine spirits to attend your workings.
  9. When the nine days of gestation are over, on a night of the waxing moon, take the bottle to a crossroads and kneel down.
  10. Break the bottle, making a long hissing sound as you do, and speak:
    Sky Snake!
    Shrike Serpent!
    Shadow Drake!
    Secret Servant!
    O subservient Iynx!
    O sacred slave who links heaven, earth,
    The Underworld, and all their rulers;
    [swifter] than the kestrel; keener, crueler;
    Hear me, Heaven's Harrier; Herald, Carrier,
    Whisperer in Hekate's ear;
    With grave-earth, incense, brine, blood, fire
    I call you into being. From nothing
    You came; to nothing you will return.
    You are a creature of great speed and strength;
    O quick and cunning Iynx,
    You exist solely to work my will.
    Spirit of mine; know that I have named you [state its name].
    When I recite your name thrice,
    You will appear to me in a form of [describe how it will appear].
    When called, you shall do me no offense,
    But will carry out my commands, and advise me wisely and truthfully.
    You must respond at the sound of your name
    When you hear the strapholos' song.
  11. Thread the string through the strapholos and begin spinning it, chanting:
    Hear the whirl of this strapholos, Iynx
    Hear it whip and hum
    Hear the hiss of this strapholos, Iynx
    And come, come, come
    And come, come, come
    And come, come, come.
  12. Repeat the chant until you are certain of the Iynx's attention, then say:
    Having come to me, Iynx,
    I now send you forth;
    Bear my prayers deeper, higher
    Through earth, air, ice, and fire
    To where my Hekate resides.
    And henceforth when I call the Black Dog,
    Make the Black Dog come.
  13. Tell the Iynx that when it hears you speak the words of irresistible compulsion, it must bring Hekate, or her servant, into your presence to attend your prayers or workings. Tell it that these are the words of compulsion:
    MASKELLI MASKELLO
    PHNOUNKENTEBAOTH
    OREOBAZAGRA REXICHTHON
    HIPPOCHTHON * PYREPEGANYX
  14. Tell the Iynx that you will reward it after it has successfully performed its function, and that such rewards might be regular songs, prayers, incense, and food offerings.
  15. Bid the Iynx to appear to you in a dream, in the form you have dictated, at 3:33 AM that very night, and set your alarm accordingly. The Iynx will appear.

Context

Having made the Call, the Reader must now begin forming a relationship with Hekate. The quickest way to do this is to enlist the aid of spirit allies: a spirit ally which is invoked, instructed, fed, and given a home will repay the Devotee with loyalty and service. Hekate is a titanic, multidimensional earth force of immense power and ungraspable size and strength - but she is a spirit just the same, and her attention can be caught with the help of a swift, clever ally the Greeks called an Iynx.

The ancient Greeks referred to otherworldly messengers as iynges, named for the wryneck bird, whose hissing, snake-like writhing made it seem both bird and serpent at once. The very first iynges were wryneck birds bound spread-eagle to small spinning wheels, understood to compel the attention of the gods. In time the living bird was replaced by a sorcerous tool called a strapholos: a small two-holed disk strung with cord which, when spun, whirls and hums with the same hissing note - the ancestor of the child's toy known today as the whirligig.
This working binds an Iynx into a consecrated strapholos, so that the Devotee has a helper spirit ensuring that when she calls Hekate, the Black Dog comes.

Closing the Call

This working completes Consecration of the Crossroads Sigil through The New Moon Dedication. Having celebrated Hekate's mysteries for nine nights and consecrated a strapholos to house an Iynx, the Reader becomes a Devotee, and turns next to a twenty-eight night study of Hekate's epithets - each treated as a separate limb of her many-faceted, anemone-like nature - culminating in the construction of Hekate's Ladder, a string of prayer beads which will house another ally.
