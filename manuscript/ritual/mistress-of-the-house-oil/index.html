✨Mistress of the House Oil

An oil formula designed to help women establish themselves as the ruling authority in their household. Combines the commanding properties of sweet flag with the empowering nature of rosemary, the aphrodisiac and love-drawing quality of orrisroot, and the protecting root of devil's shoestring, steeped in a blend of sweet almond and jojoba oil. Has a moderate aphrodisiac effect, especially for those sensitive to orrisroot. Applied to the partner's feet, shoes, or person.

Quantities

  • Powdered sweet flag (calamus)
  • Powdered rosemary
  • Powdered orrisroot
  • Devil's shoestring root (whole)
  • Jojoba oil
  • Sweet almond oil
    Carrier oil ratio: 2 parts sweet almond oil to 1 part jojoba oil.

Directions

  1. Add the powdered sweet flag, rosemary, and orrisroot to a glass bottle or jar.
  2. Add the devil's shoestring root.
  3. Blend two parts sweet almond oil with one part jojoba oil and fill the bottle with this mixture.
  4. Seal and let the oil steep for several days to reach full strength.
  5. If possible, expose the sealed bottle to full moonlight to empower the blend.
  6. To administer: massage the finished oil into the partner's feet, sprinkle it inside their shoes, or sprinkle it on them while they are sleeping.
