🧪Bathing in Brigid's Well

A ritual bath that combines fire-blessed lustral water with an oat, Irish moss, and hawthorn potion to invoke Brigid's threefold healing.

Quantities

Candles (for lustral water)
Clear quartz (optional)
3 cups dry oatmeal
1 cup dried or powdered Irish moss
1 cup dried hawthorn leaves
10 cotton muslin bath bags

Directions

  1. First make lustral water by burning floating candles, burning camphor in water, or standing a taper or pillar candle in a vessel of water and letting it burn down until naturally extinguished, giving the process your full attention to invoke Brigid's fire and imbue the water with her blessing.
  2. Add clear quartz for additional energy if desired, then save the lustral water in sealed jars.
  3. Mix the oatmeal, Irish moss, and dried hawthorn leaves together with your hands in a large bowl, sifting the mixture through your fingers and letting your intention radiate from your hands as you repeat:
  4. Threefold the healing of Brigid:
    Health of the body, restoring;
    Health of the mind, refreshing;
    Health of the spirit, renewing.
  5. Divide the mixture among the muslin bags and tie them shut, knotting the drawstrings three times, then store in a dry, cool place until ready to use.
  6. Run a very warm bath and light candles nearby so their light ripples on the water.
  7. Pour in some lustral water and drop in one of the potion bags.
  8. Get in the bathtub and rest in the quiet warmth, squeezing the bag to release its virtues into the water and caressing your skin with it.
  9. When you feel relaxed and open to receive the gifts of the healing bath, bless yourself with the following charm, cupping water in your hands and pouring it over your head and body with each line while visualizing Brigid's loving hands performing the blessing:
  10. A wavelet for thy form,
    A wavelet for thy voice,
    A wavelet for thy health,
    A wavelet for thy luck,
    A wavelet for thy good,
    A wavelet for thy vision,
    A wavelet for thy strength,
    A wavelet for thy pluck,
    A wavelet for thy faith:
    Nine waves for thy grace.
  11. Stay in the bath as long as it feels good, then dry off and go straight to bed to complete the healing with a good long sleep.
