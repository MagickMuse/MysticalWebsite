🌱Fertility

A Cherokee postpartum spell intended to delay the next pregnancy. Traditionally performed by the father, or a designated delegate, immediately after birth. The placenta is carried with reverence away from the home across a number of hills, each hill crossed representing one additional year before the next child is born. The placenta is then buried at the destination.

Quantities

The placenta (handled with reverence)
Alternate landmarks designated in advance if no hills are available

Directions

  1. Immediately following the birth, the father (or a trusted delegate) carries the placenta carefully and respectfully away from the home.
  2. Determine in advance how many years of spacing are desired.
  3. Each hill crossed represents one year.
  4. Cross that number of hills.
  5. If the landscape has no hills, identify and name other landmarks to serve as substitutes before beginning.
  6. When the intended number of landmarks has been passed, find a suitable place and bury the placenta in the earth.

This is a working of measured intention. The number of hills is not chosen arbitrarily. It is calculated in advance and carried through in action. The practitioner decides what they want and then enacts that specific number. The landscape becomes a counting tool, each hill a year held apart.

The tradition of delegating this task to the father (or to a trusted person if the father is absent) is significant. It acknowledges that in the immediate postpartum period, the mother's participation in a physically demanding outdoor working may not be possible. The spell is designed around that reality. Its power does not require the mother's direct presence.

The instruction to carry the placenta "with respect and reverence" is not incidental. The placenta has nourished a new life and is itself a biologically and spiritually significant substance. The care with which it is handled encodes the seriousness and intention of the working.

Landmarks can substitute for hills, as the tradition explicitly acknowledges. What matters is the act of traversal and the conscious assignment of meaning to each crossing.
