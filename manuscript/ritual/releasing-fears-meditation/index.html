🕯️Releasing Fears Meditation

A meditation to open communication with the Dark Aspects of the Divine by facing and releasing fears and anxieties. The practitioner enters a safe hermit's cave on a mountaintop, invites fears to appear, unmasks them, and banishes them. Once a fear is known and exposed, it no longer has power.

Quantities

  • Purple or black votive working candle
  • Incense: frankincense or sandalwood
    Optional bath preparation:
  • Muslin pouch with herbs (rosemary, basil, thyme, valerian)
  • Candles for bath

Directions

  1. You may first take a warm, candlelit bath scented with herbs (rosemary, basil, thyme, valerian) tied in a muslin pouch.
  2. Dress in comfortable clothes or whatever feels natural.
  3. Be in a place where you will not be disturbed, with the light off.
  4. Light the candle and the incense.
  5. Gaze into the flame, sitting and facing North.
  6. You may prerecord this meditation.
  7. Envision the votive candle as the Light that shines in the Land of Shadows, the Lamp that lights the path to wisdom.
  8. Enter a meditative state.
  9. You are in an atmosphere of safety, sitting within the comfort of a shallow, airy hermit's cave or other area to your liking.
  10. See yourself in a small, circular cave with an arched entry that gives you a view of the outside.
  11. The stony walls and ceiling form an alcove around you.
  12. The ground is covered with moss and leaves, and you are sitting on a soft bearskin rug.
  13. You are facing the opening to the cave and can see that you are in a secure place near the top of a mountain, at a vantage point that allows you to look out over the forest beneath the mountain.
  14. The Sun is setting, casting long shadows over the land.
  15. The cave darkens.
  16. The Sun disappears completely, taking the last of the colors with it, and the stars come out in the evening sky.
  17. There is a cool breeze bringing the sweet scents of the night to you.
  18. You are at peace.
  19. Now that the scene is mentally fixed, focus on a question: What do I fear?
  20. Images may begin to appear, which may include varieties of deaths, frightening faces, and so forth.
  21. Let the images come and go, but with each one that appears, ask: Why do I fear this?
  22. Listen for the answer, knowing that you are safe from any image that appears to you.
  23. Then ask: What are these fears?
  24. Listen to the answers, allowing the images to be unmasked.
  25. Realize that once a fear is known and exposed, it no longer has power over you.
  26. After you have faced your own fears and unmasked them, banish them with:
  27. Begone!
  28. The images fade away, often with a little laugh, for fear is only a game, after all, that you had allowed yourself to take too seriously.
  29. Stars shine again in the sky.
  30. You smile.
  31. Breathe in deeply the night air, and as you exhale, the tensions of fears dissipate.
  32. You take another breath and inhale the sense of all being right in the world.
  33. You are back in your meditation place, and may return to the cave whenever needed.
  34. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes and Wine of the Circle Casting.
