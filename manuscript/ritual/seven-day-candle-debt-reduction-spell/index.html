🕯️Seven-Day Candle Debt Reduction Spell

A seven-day Saturday night candle spell to reduce debt, lighting progressively fewer blue tea lights each night in a circle around debt paper until binding it with a red cross and freezing.

Quantities

  • Seven blue tea lights or small candles
  • A paper listing the debts or total amount you owe
  • A red pen

Directions

  1. On night 1, put all the tea lights in a circle, with the debt paper in the center.
  2. Light all seven tea lights, saying for each one: "Debt, melt away, no longer stay to blight my life. May solvency restore the light." Blow out the candles.
  3. On night 2, light only six candles and repeat the spell.
  4. Continue until on night 7 only one candle is lit.
  5. Repeat the spell but leave it burning.
  6. On the debt paper draw a red cross and write across it: DEBT, YOU ARE BOUND FROM TROUBLING ME OR INCREASING FURTHER.
  7. Fold the paper up small and put it in the freezer.
  8. On night 8, burn any unfinished candles, repeating the spell words just once.
  9. Perform Saturday after dark.
