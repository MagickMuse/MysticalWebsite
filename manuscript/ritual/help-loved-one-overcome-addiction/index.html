💪Help Loved One Overcome Addiction

A Sunday morning spell to help a loved one overcome alcohol or substance abuse, using four feathers in square around picture to restore freedom and blow away destruction.

Quantities

  • A picture of the person before the problem arose or became acute, however young
  • Four feathers
  • A white candle

Directions

  1. For the week before the first spell, put the feathers in a square around the picture and have them remain there between subsequent spells.
  2. Each night light the white candle and waft each feather in turn over the picture, saying: "May freedom be restored, more joyous than before. I blow away what destroys you that you will feel anew life and hope supported by those of us who love you." Extinguish the candle.
  3. On the actual spell morning, repeat the words and actions but let the candle burn.
  4. Carry the feathers to a windy place and release them, saying the same words for each one.
  5. Replace with new feathers and a new candle.
  6. Perform Sunday mornings.
