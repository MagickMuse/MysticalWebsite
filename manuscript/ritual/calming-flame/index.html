🕯️Calming Flame

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Candle MagickElementalVisualizationChant/MantraEnergy Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- One orange tealight candle (or small candle) - Glass of water (ideally placed on bare ground or directly on the floor/near a window) - Matches or lighter
◇Directions
1. Set the glass of water beside the candle. 2. Outdoors, put the glass directly on bare ground. 3. Indoors, place it on the floor or at a window so it's as close to Earth's energy as possible. 4. Place fingertips over the unlit candle and let the feelings rise: anger, angst, irritation, stress, unfulfilled desire. 5. Don't fight them, honor and release. 6. Envision yourself as an open tube the feelings can pour through. 7. Let the energies flow out through your fingertips into the wax until you feel hollow and indifferent. 8. If emotions still run strong, continue until you cannot release more. 9. Light the candle. Rest hands palms-up. Gaze at the flame and watch the wax melt. See the negative energy you poured into the wax transforming into pure power feeding a hungry sun. Visualize the sun above the flame and the energy flowing up through the flame into the sky and into the sun. Say: "I feed the sun the passion of my hunger! I feed the sun the power of my fears! I feed the sun the monster bred of injustice! I feed the sun the power of my tears!" 10. Place hands around the water. Invite Earth's energies into it by feeling forests, rich soil, ancient rock. See that power infuse the water. If you like, say: "Element of Earth, I call on you now! Please enter this glass of water, right here and now! Earth, come into this water right here and now! There is no other course, come into it now!" 11. Take thirteen slow sips, breathing deeply between each. 12. Feel the charged water calming and nurturing you, quenching body and spirit. 13. When finished, drip some of the remaining water onto the candle to extinguish the flame. 14. Exhale long and deep, and return to your day.
◇Description
A quick Lughnasadh spell to transmute agitation, anger, and overwhelming desire into solar fuel, then ground and calm with Earth‑charged water.
