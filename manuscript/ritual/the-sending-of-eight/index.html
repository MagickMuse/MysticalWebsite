📸The Sending of Eight

One of three simple spells for the jilted witch. A baneful circle rite using paired photographs of two lovers, fumigated over hot coals and conjured with a charge to eight spirits of destruction, then buried and retrieved to convey the working to the targets.

Quantities

  • 2 photographs (one of each target)
  • Pen and ink of art
  • Sulphur powder OR asafoetida gum
  • Hot coals in thurible
  • Athame

Directions

During a waning moon, cast the circle.

Obtain photographs of both targets. Write each person's name on their respective photograph with pen and ink of art.

Name each photograph with fire (pass through a flame) and water (sprinkle or pass over water), then exhibit each at the four quarters.

Glue the photographs back to back.

Move to the south of the altar and face north. Place sulphur powder or asafoetida gum on the hot coals in the thurible.

Hold the photographs in the rising smoke with the right hand; press the point of the athame with the left.

Chant the conjuration, naming the eight spirits of destruction: "Soignator! Usor! Dilapidator! / Dentor! Concisor! Devorator! / Seductor et Seminator!" followed by the full conjuration charging them with their mission.

Bury the photographs in earth for 24 hours.

The following night, dig them up while repeating the same chant and adding: "Go! Accomplish that which is commanded of you!"

Seal the working. Arrange for the photographs to pass into the targets' possession: concealed inside a gift, slipped beneath their threshold, or similar means.
