A full moon egg and seed planting ritual with entwined plants to ensure healthy, high-quality breeding outcomes.

Quantities

  • 1 egg
  • 1 packet of seeds
  • 2 identical green plants

Directions

  1. Dig a hole in your garden or an open space.
  2. Hold the egg in your nondominant hand, saying, "Be fertile, be healthy, may there be only the best to outshine all the rest." Break open the egg and place the raw egg and broken shell in the hole, repeating the spell words.
  3. Open the seed packet and, holding the seeds in your dominant hand, say the same spell words and drop the seeds into the hole.
  4. Entwine the roots of the two plants as you set them on top of the seeds and the egg, saying, "Be joined in strength and beauty, little ones grow healthily, that I may have the Best of Breed, so this venture will succeed." Whenever you tend to the plants, repeat the spell words.
  5. Perform at a full moon.
