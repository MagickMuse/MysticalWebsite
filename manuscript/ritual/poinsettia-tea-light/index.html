A crocheted poinsettia holder for LED tea lights, perfect for the Yule altar. The red and green hues symbolize renewal, while the radiant sunburst shape heralds the resurgence of light during the darkest time of year.

Quantities

  • Size H/8 5mm crochet hook
  • 52 yards (1 oz) yellow 4-ply yarn
  • 52 yards (1 oz) red 4-ply yarn
  • 52 yards (1 oz) green 4-ply yarn
  • Scissors
  • LED tea light (no real candles)

Directions

  1. Crochet Abbreviations:
    Ch = Chain | St = Stitch | Sc = Single crochet | Hdc = Half double crochet | Dc = Double crochet | Trc = Triple crochet | Picot = Ch 3, slip stitch into 3rd chain from hook | Sl St = Slip stitch | Rev Sc = Reverse single crochet | BLO = Back loops only | FO = Fasten off
  2. Using Yellow Yarn (Center):
    Round 1: Ch 3, 12 Dc in 3rd Ch from hook
    Round 2: In BLO, 1 Hdc in each stitch around
    Round 3: Rev Sc around, FO and weave in the end
  3. Using Red Yarn (Petals):
    Attach yarn with Sl St to any unused loop from Round 2.
    Round 4: {Ch 3, Dc in same St, picot, Dc in next St, Ch 2, Sl St in same St, Sl St in next St}
    Repeat 5 more times. FO and weave in the end.
  4. Using Green Yarn (Leaves):
    Attach yarn with Sl St behind center of any petal.
    {Ch 3, Dc, Trc in same St, picot, Trc, Dc, Ch 2 Sl St in next St, Sl St in next St}
    Repeat 5 more times. FO and weave end.
  5. Place LED tea light in center.
  6. Use on your Yule altar to symbolize hope for the return of light.
