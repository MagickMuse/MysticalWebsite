A spell to develop appreciation for yourself and your power. Self-awareness is linked with personal power, and most of us underestimate ourselves. Best performed outside under the full moon.

Quantities

  • 1 amber-colored candle
  • A candleholder
  • Frankincense essential oil
  • Myrrh essential oil
  • An object that represents personal power to you
  • 1 quartz crystal

Directions

  1. Thursday is Jupiter's day, and Jupiter encourages expansion.
  2. If you can't do this spell on a Thursday, do it during a full moon on any day except Saturday (Saturn's day).
  3. The object that represents your personal power should be something solid and three-dimensional: a stone or figurine, for instance, versus a photograph.
  4. Select the object with care.
  5. The amber-colored candle symbolizes the sun's golden light.
  6. The crystal you're using will amplify your desire.
  7. Pick a spot where you won't be disturbed.
  8. Dress the candle with both essential oils, fit it in the candleholder, and set it on your altar, at your right.
  9. The object that represents your personal power and the crystal should be positioned directly in front of you.
  10. Light the candle, and then open your arms to "embrace" the moon.
  11. Vividly imagine its light filling you as you say:
  12. "This light is presence,
    This light is power.
    It fills me
    Until I am presence,
    And I am power."
  13. Gaze at the crystal and your power object as the moon's light shines on them.
  14. Inhale the aroma of the oils for as long as you like, then snuff out the candle's flame.
  15. Carry your power object with you and touch it whenever you need a power boost.
