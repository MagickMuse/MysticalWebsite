A spell to protect beloved animal companions from harm, using a consecrated collar inscribed with protective words and the pentagram symbol.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 collar for your pet (leather or fabric)
  • Sage incense, or loose sage
  • 1 marker that will write on fabric or leather
  • Amber essential oil (4 drops)

Directions

  1. Purchase a new collar, made of leather or fabric, for your pet.
  2. Cast a circle around the space where you'll be working.
  3. Light the sage and hold the collar in the smoke to cleanse it of unwanted energies.
  4. On the inside of the collar, write: "[Pet's name] is safe and sound at all times." Draw a pentagram at the end of the statement.
  5. The pentagram, as you know, is a symbol of protection.
  6. On the outside of the collar, write your name and phone number; or, get a pet tag made that includes this information and attach it to the collar.
  7. Put four drops of essential oil on the collar, one at each end, on both sides of the collar.
  8. Open the circle.
  9. Place the collar in the sun for one day to charge it.
  10. Then fasten the collar on your pet to keep them safe.
  11. (To be extra safe, ask your vet about having a tiny computer chip permanently implanted in your pet's skin that will identify them to city pounds, veterinarian's offices, and other agencies with scanners that can read such chips.)
