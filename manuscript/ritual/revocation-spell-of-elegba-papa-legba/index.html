⛓️Revocation Spell of Elegba (Papa Legba)

A petition to Elegba/Papa Legba, owner of all roads in Vodou/Hoodoo, to revoke a curse and return it to its sender, simultaneously erecting a protective shield. A cup of Indigo Water, Florida Water, Flying Devil Oil, Protection Oil, Uncrossing Oil, and turpentine is ritually charged with rum, cigar smoke, nails, and a camphor square, then reversed onto a plate near the door with three knocks and a candle petition.

⚠️ This petitions a Vodou/Hoodoo spirit. Both Vodou and Hoodoo are closed practices. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.

Directions

  1. Fill a cup two-thirds full with Indigo Water.
  2. Top it off with Florida Water, Flying Devil Oil, Protection Oil, Uncrossing Oil, and turpentine, petitioning Elegba, owner of all roads, to send the evil afflicting you on its way quickly.
  3. Take a small mouthful of rum, hold it in your mouth, then blow it into the cup.
  4. Blow cigar smoke into the cup; if you do not smoke, simply waft the smoke over the cup.
  5. Add three nails and a camphor square to the cup.
  6. If you know your enemy's name, write it on a piece of brown paper and put the paper over the cup with the name facing in. Place a plate on top of it and quickly reverse the cup so that it is now upside down on the plate.
  7. Place this near a door.
  8. Knock three times on the floor, calling:
  9. "Open the door Papa Legba!"
  10. Place a white candle on top of the cup and burn it, explaining your predicament to Elegba.
