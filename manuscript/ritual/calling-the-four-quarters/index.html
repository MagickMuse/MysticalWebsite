🧭Calling the Four Quarters

◇Directions
1. Before casting your circle, erect four altars, one at each compass point, and decorate them with objects and symbols representing each direction's elemental energy. 2. Cleanse, purify, and consecrate each altar, having everything in place before casting the circle. 3. Once everyone who will participate is inside, light a candle at each altar and call in the quarters, visualizing beams of pure white light stretching into the sky and connecting you at each direction to the powers of the universe. 4. Facing outward with your back to the center, light a yellow candle and recite the East (Air) invocation. 5. Move clockwise, light a red candle, and recite the South (Fire) invocation. 6. Continue clockwise, light a blue candle, and recite the West (Water) invocation. 7. Continue clockwise, light a green candle, and recite the North (Earth) invocation. 8. Move into the center of the circle and recite the Center (Spirit) invocation. 9. Once you feel the presence of the beings you've invited, continue with your spell or ritual. 10. To close, begin in the north quarter and move counterclockwise, reciting each direction's closing invocation in turn (North, West, South, East) and extinguishing each candle as you go, visualizing the beams of light evaporating back into the Void. 11. Recite the Center closing, then optionally join hands with other participants and offer the closing chant. 12. At the end of your working, release the energies and thank them for assisting you.
◇Description
A complete ritual for invoking the elemental guardians at each compass point when casting a circle, including full invocations, closing, and a quick alternative.

Many magickal traditions call the quarters as part of casting a circle. Not everyone uses the same words or gestures; Wiccans probably won't perform this practice exactly the same way as ceremonial magicians. This ritual provides a functional, eclectic approach you can customize over time.

Some practitioners invoke angels when calling the quarters: Raphael (East), Michael (South), Gabriel (West), and Uriel (North). Deities are invoked in this order and released in the opposite order. You might also invite gods, goddesses, animal totems, or other spirits with links to your spell's purpose.

Invoking the Quarters

East (Air)

"Beings of Air, Guardians of the East, Breath of Transformation, Come! Be welcome in this sacred space. I/we ask that you stand firm to guard and protect, refresh and inspire me/us. Support the magick created here by conveying my/our wishes on every wind as it blows across the earth."

South (Fire)

"Beings of Fire, Guardians of the South, Spark of Creation that banishes the darkness, Come! Be welcome in this sacred space. I/we ask that you stand firm to guard and protect, activate and motivate me/us. Support the magick created here by conveying my/our wishes to the sun, the stars, and every beam of light that embraces the earth."

West (Water)

"Beings of Water, Guardians of the West, Rain of Imagination, Come! Be welcome in this sacred space. I/we ask that you stand firm to guard and protect, heal and nurture me/us. Support the magick created here by conveying my/our wishes to the dewdrops, rain, and the waves as they wash across the world."

North (Earth)

"Beings of Earth, Guardians of the North, Soil of Foundation, Come! Be welcome in this sacred space. I/we ask that you stand firm to guard and protect, support and provide for me/us. Support the magick created here by conveying my/our wishes to every grain of sand, every bit of loam that is our world."

Center (Spirit)

"Ancient One, the power that binds all the elements into oneness and source of my/our magick, Come! Be welcome in this sacred space. I/we ask that you stand firm to guard and protect, guide and enhance all the energy created here. May it be for the good of all. So mote it be."

Closing the Circle

Releasing the sacred space is as important as erecting it. Thank the powers who assisted you and ask them to keep guiding the energy you've raised.

North (Earth)

"Guardians, Guides, and Ancestors of the North and Earth, I/we thank you for your presence and protection. Keep me/us rooted in your rich soil so my/our spirit(s) grow steadily, until I/we return to your protection again. Hail and farewell!"

West (Water)

"Guardians, Guides, and Ancestors of the West and Water, I/we thank you for your presence and protection. Keep me/us flowing ever toward wholeness in body, mind, and spirit, until I/we return to your protection again. Hail and farewell!"

South (Fire)

"Guardians, Guides, and Ancestors of the South and Fire, I/we thank you for your presence and protection. Keep your fires ever burning within my/our soul(s) to light any darkness and drive it away, until I/we return to your protection again. Hail and farewell!"

East (Air)

"Guardians, Guides, and Ancestors of the East and Air, I/we thank you for your presence and protection. Keep your winds blowing fresh with ideas and hope, until I/we return to your protection again. Hail and farewell!"

Center

"Great Spirit, thank you for blessing this space. I/we know that a part of you is always with me/us, as a still small voice that guides, protects, and nurtures me/us. Help me/us to listen to that voice, to trust it, and trust in my/our magick."

Before leaving the circle, some witches join hands and offer a closing chant:

"Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again."

Quick Alternative

If you're short on time, stand in the center of the circle facing east and say:

"Before me Raphael, guardian of the east.
Behind me Gabriel, guardian of the west.
To my right Michael, guardian of the south.
To my left Uriel, guardian of the north.
Be here now."

Working with the Quarters

Working with the quarters and the spirits is like being in an evolving relationship with someone you respect and admire. If you treat these entities accordingly, you will rarely be disappointed.
