🛡️Peace Water Home Blessing

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Water/Spray
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Water MagickProtectionCleansing
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
New Orleans Voodoo
◇Quantities
Peace Water (commercially obtained or traditionally prepared) Small bottles (one per room)
◇Directions
1. Obtain Peace Water, a formula with two or three distinct layers of oil and blue liquid. 2. Keep a bottle prominently displayed in each room of the home to encourage peaceful, happy coexistence. 3. When tension, violence, or altercation arises, shake the bottle to disperse the layers and sprinkle it wherever peace is needed.
◇Description
⚠️ This is a New Orleans Voodoo formula. New Orleans Voodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture. A layered oil-and-water formula kept visibly displayed in each room to maintain peace, then shaken and sprinkled at sites of tension or altercation as needed.
