A petitionary bath to the lwa Maitresse Ezili, combining a basil, calamus, and allspice infusion with Florida Water, offered alongside candles and a gift of generosity. Part of the Spiritual Financial Aid Spell series (No. 1).

Quantities

  • Candles (offering)
  • A gift for after the bath (perfume, pastry, or jewelry)
  • Basil
  • Sweet flag (calamus)
  • Allspice berries
  • Boiling water
  • Florida Water

Directions

  1. This bath is considered a petition to the lwa Maitresse Ezili.
  2. Before taking the bath, offer her candles.
  3. Afterwards bestow a gift (perfume, pastry, jewelry).
  4. Demonstrate the generosity that you'd like her to express towards you.
  5. Create an infusion by pouring boiling water over basil, sweet flag (calamus), and allspice berries.
  6. Add this infusion to your bath together with Florida Water.
