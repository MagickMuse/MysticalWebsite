🫧Bursting Bubbles

◇Quantities
- 1 pen (preferably green or purple ink) - 1 piece of white paper - 1 green or purple candle - 1 fireproof container/cauldron - 1 stick of incense (cedar, copal, dragon's blood, frankincense, myrrh, patchouli, or sandalwood preferred) - 1 sheet of bubble wrap (1 bubble per issue; trim to fit) - 1 vessel of water (for fire safety)
◇Directions
1. Preparation ◦ Write down all the personal issues you want to release, taking your time. ◦ Phrase each issue like this: my bitterness, my envy, my anger, my pessimism, my tendency to blame others… ◦ Count your items and trim the bubble wrap so there is one bubble for each item. ◦ If needed, prepare a second sheet. 2. Set Up ◦ Place the fireproof container on your altar or work surface (protect against heat). ◦ Keep the vessel of water nearby. ◦ Open a window. 3. The Spell ◦ Light the candle. Use it to light the incense. ◦ Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space. ◦ Read your list aloud or silently, focusing on each item. ◦ Visualize each issue as a swirling dark bubble in your spirit. ◦ Raise power. Picture the bubbles calming and lining up to leave. ◦ Pick up the bubble wrap and, for each issue, say: I release [name the issue]. Go forth. Do no harm, and never return to me. ◦ As you speak, pop one bubble per item. ◦ Visualize the issue leaving you, carried away in the smoke. 4. Release the Smoke ◦ Fold your list into a fan. ◦ Waft the smoke toward the open window, visualizing everything you've released floating out. ◦ Once complete, touch the paper to the candle flame and drop it into the fireproof container to burn. 5. Closure ◦ Stay present until the paper finishes burning. ◦ Ground your power and clean up your space.
◇Description
A fiery smoke-cleansing ritual to release negative emotions, habits, and inner burdens. By symbolically popping each issue, you free yourself from its hold and send it away with intention.

This spell can be performed at any time. Exercise proper fire safety throughout.

Why Bubble Wrap?

The tactile satisfaction of popping bubbles taps into something primal. Children instinctively understand this release. By pairing that physical sensation with intentional visualization, you create a powerful mind-body connection that helps your subconscious truly let go of what you're releasing.

Preparing Your List

Take as long as you need with this step. Days or even weeks of reflection serve the spell well. Thoroughness matters more than speed.

Phrase each issue in the first person possessive:

  • "my bitterness"
  • "my envy"
  • "my anger"
  • "my pessimism"
  • "my unrealistic optimism"
  • "my guilt"
  • "my tendency to blame others"
  • "my tendency to blame myself for things beyond my control"

This phrasing acknowledges ownership while preparing for release. You cannot let go of something you refuse to claim.

Once your list is complete, count the items. You need exactly one bubble for each issue. Trim your bubble wrap accordingly. If your list exceeds a single sheet, prepare a second one.

Working the Spell

As you read through your list, visualize each issue as a dark bubble swirling chaotically within your spirit. These murky spheres represent the weight you carry.

When you raise power, picture those chaotic bubbles calming and forming a queue. They are ready to leave.

For each item, speak the release phrase and pop one bubble. Feel the dark energy burst harmlessly into the air above your incense or candle. Watch the smoke carry it away.

You should feel progressively lighter as you work through your list. Relief and renewed energy are signs the spell is working.

Sending It Away

The paper fan technique connects to childhood play while serving a magickal purpose. As you waft smoke toward the open window, visualize everything you've released floating outward, dispersing into the world where it can no longer affect you.

Burning the list completes the transformation. What was once a catalog of burdens becomes ash, then nothing.

Fire Safety

Always keep water nearby when working with flame. Place your fireproof container on a heat-safe surface. Never leave burning materials unattended. Open a window both for ventilation and to give your released energy somewhere to go.
