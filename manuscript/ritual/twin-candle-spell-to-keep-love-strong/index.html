🕯️Twin Candle Spell to Keep Love Strong

A twin candle spell using beeswax candles melted together to create a wax heart inscribed with initials, keeping love strong, desire burning, and fidelity lasting through the years.

Quantities

  • Two beeswax or real wax candles
  • A flat metal tray
  • A paper knife
  • Silk cloth for wrapping

Directions

  1. Light the first candle, melting a little wax onto the tray to secure the candles as they stand side by side.
  2. Light the second candle from the first and say: "As this wax melts, so melts his/her heart. As the candles burn, so burns his/her love for me, inflames and kindles desire and fidelity from year to year." When both candles have burned and melted, as the remaining pool of wax cools but before it is solid, use the knife to draw a large heart in the wax.
  3. Write both your and your love's initials within the heart and say: "May love grow stronger and endure from year to year." When the wax is almost solid, cut around the edges of the heart so that you can lift it.
  4. Keep the heart wrapped in silk in a drawer until it crumbles.
  5. Replace the heart and bury the crumbled one.
