💤Dream Message Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Dreamwork
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• One clear quartz crystal • A glass of spring water or Holy Water • Clean white bed sheets • Yarrow, wormwood, and thyme • A muslin bag or handkerchief
◇Directions
1. Decide precisely what dream you want to transmit, in as much detail as possible. 2. At the New Moon, place a clear quartz crystal in a glass of spring or Holy Water and leave it exposed to moonlight overnight to create lunar-charged water. 3. During the waxing moon, put clean white sheets on your bed and sprinkle the lunar water over them. 4. Place yarrow, wormwood, and thyme together in a muslin bag or tie them into a handkerchief. 5. Hold the herb packet in both hands and concentrate on the person for whom the dream is intended. 6. Place the packet inside your pillowcase. 7. As you fall asleep, visualize the dream you wish to send in as much detail as possible, as though watching it play out.
◇Description
A German spell for transmitting a specific dream to another person. Clear quartz in lunar-charged water is prepared at the New Moon; during the waxing moon the bed is dressed and an herb bundle held in concentration before being placed in the pillowcase.
