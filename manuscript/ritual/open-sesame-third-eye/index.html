Opening your third eye as a way as improving your psychic work.

Quantities

  • Sandalwood incense
  • Music suggestion: Om Kalsoum's "Alfy Layla wa Layla"

Directions

  1. Set your space
  2. Choose a moonlit night if possible (any time is okay).
  3. Place the items on your altar or a working surface.
  4. Prepare the area as sacred space; dim the lights.
  5. Dress the candle
  6. Rub sesame oil on the purple candle using the forefinger of your projective hand.
  7. Wash your hands afterward.
  8. Light incense and candle
  9. Burn the sandalwood incense.
  10. Light the purple candle.
  11. Apply Tiger Balm
  12. Use the forefinger of your projective hand to anoint your third eye (forehead) with Tiger Balm.
  13. Focus on how it tingles and activates your psychic center.
  14. Raise energy and concentrate
  15. Visualize your third eye opening like a blooming flower.
  16. Gaze at the candle until you feel ready.
  17. Recite the incantation (verbatim):
    Alfy layla wa layla,
    Alfy layla wa layla . . .
    By the Moon, on Arabian nights,
    By the silence of ancient sites,
    By the secrets of arcane rites,
    By the flicker of candle lights . . .
    Ifta, ya simsim!
    Open sesame!
    Open my third eye, open my mind’s eye,
    Release my sixth sense.
    Help me to see the unseen.
    Let me Be as I have never been,
    Let me See as I have never seen.
    Open my third eye, open my mind’s eye,
    Open my Self to understanding.
    Alfy layla wa layla
    Alfy layla wa layla . . .
    By the power of moonlight,
    By the power of clear sight,
    Ifta, ya simsim!
    Open sesame!
  18. Focus and visualize
  19. Envision stars or light pouring from your third eye.
  20. If energy builds or you feel physical sensations (e.g., vibrating, flame movement), that’s normal.
  21. Dance or move if needed to release excess energy.
  22. Gaze and ground
  23. Continue gazing into the flame and reflect on insights or sensations.
  24. Once finished, ground the raised energy.
  25. Monitor the candle until it burns down fully.
  26. Aftercare
  27. Eat and hydrate to return to normal consciousness.
  28. Clean your space and extinguish incense if needed.

This spell invites joy. Feel free to dance, chant, spin, or sing as you work. It's especially well-suited for those new to magick or psychic practice, or anyone looking to reawaken abilities that have grown dull over time.

Understanding the Third Eye

The third eye is your psychic center. As the sixth chakra, it governs all aspects of the sixth sense: intuition, insight, clairvoyance, precognition, telepathy, and extrasensory perception. Through it, you perceive what lies beyond ordinary sight.

Activating this chakra prepares you for deeper psychic work. Meditation, divination, visualization, scrying, fire-gazing, distance healing, past-life exploration, dream work, remote viewing, and astral projection all benefit from an open third eye.

While you can perform this working anytime, moonlit nights amplify its power.

Preparation

Finding Your Third Eye

Traditions differ on exact placement. Some say between the eyebrows, others the center of the forehead. Since each person is unique, only you can locate yours.

Try placing a small crystal between your eyebrows. Notice any subtle tingling or psychic sensation. If nothing resonates, move the crystal to the center of your forehead. With patience and repeated attempts, you'll discover the spot that responds. That's your third eye.

Substitutes for Tiger Balm

Tiger Balm creates a tingling sensation that heightens awareness of your third eye. If you don't have it, alternatives include:

  • Other commercial liniments or muscle balms
  • Diluted peppermint essential oil
  • Any skin-safe substance that creates a gentle tingle

Choose something that feels magickal to you and patch-test first to avoid allergic reactions.

Creating Atmosphere

Music enhances this working. While any evocative soundtrack works, recordings of the legendary Egyptian vocalist Om Kalsoum (particularly "Alfy Layla wa Layla," or A Thousand and One Nights) make an exceptionally potent accompaniment.

After the Working

This spell changes you. The effects vary from person to person.

Shifts in Psychic Perception

You may notice:

  • Heightened imagination and creativity
  • Sharper perception and keener awareness
  • Deeper, more focused meditations
  • Clearer intuitive insights
  • Easier visualization
  • Improved accuracy with divination tools like runes, pendulums, or tarot

Physical Changes

The body often responds as well:

  • Better sleep quality
  • Reduced anxiety, confusion, or fear
  • Fewer or milder headaches
  • Less neck tension
  • Resolution of lingering eye, hormonal, or skin issues

Maintaining the Opening

For some, one casting permanently opens the third eye. Others find periodic repetition helpful to keep the channel clear and receptive.
