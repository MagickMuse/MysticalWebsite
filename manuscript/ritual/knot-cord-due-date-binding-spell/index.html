A knot-magick working that ties a due date into a cord as a symbolic anchor against miscarriage, kept safe until it's time to untie it for delivery.

Quantities

  • A strong cord
  • A secure covered container or a baby blanket for wrapping

Directions

  1. At the first sign of possible miscarriage, tie a tight knot in a strong cord.
  2. Visualize the knot as your baby, and speak to it directly: as this knot holds firm, so may you hold firm in my womb, not loosening until your chosen due date.
  3. Place the knotted cord in a secure covered container, or wrap it in a baby blanket, and keep it somewhere safe.
  4. Untie the cord only when the appropriate time arrives.
