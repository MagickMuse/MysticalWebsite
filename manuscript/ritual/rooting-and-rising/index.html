Ritual Grimoire

⚡Rooting and Rising

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationBreathworkVisualizationEnergy WorkElementalSolar
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
- No materials required
◇Directions
1. Sit or stand on the ground if possible. 2. Close your eyes and take a slow deep breath, then another. 3. Feel the strength of the earth beneath you, solid and dependable, always available when you need it. 4. In your mind's eye, send roots down into the ground from your tailbone or the soles of your feet. 5. Let them reach deeper and deeper, toward the Earth's core. 6. Feel those roots connect with strength and the energy of growth. 7. Allow that energy to rise up through the roots into your core, filling you with strength, calm, and focused presence. 8. Now reach upward. 9. Sense the power and clarity from above, the vibrant energy of the Sun and the open sky. 10. Breathe that light into the deepest core of your being. 11. Feel the Sun's energy racing through your veins, recharging body, mind, and spirit. 12. Let the light wash away stress and care, leaving only strength and clarity, calm and focus. 13. Rest in this union: one with the Earth, one with the Sun. 14. At peace.
◇Description
A grounding-and-energizing meditation that roots into the Earth’s steady strength and draws clarity and vitality from the Sun. Designed as a simple practice you can do standing or seated on the ground.
