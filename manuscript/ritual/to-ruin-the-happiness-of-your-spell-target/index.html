A jar spell to destroy the target's luck and sour their fortune. The target's name is written nine times on paper, rubbed with Goofer Dust while cursing, placed in a jar with Black Salt and Red Wine Vinegar, shaken into a black sludge, and disposed of in a graveyard.

Quantities

1 bottle or jar
Paper
Goofer Dust
Black Salt
Red wine vinegar

Directions

  1. Write the enemy's name nine times on a piece of paper.
  2. Rub the name-paper thoroughly with Goofer Dust while cursing your enemy.
  3. Shake off the paper and place it into the jar.
  4. Fill the jar a portion of the way with Black Salt and say: "[name], this destroys your luck." Top it off with Red Wine Vinegar and say: "[name], this sours your fortune." Tightly cap the jar and give it a thorough shaking until the contents combine into a black sludge.
  5. Dispose of the jar in a graveyard.
