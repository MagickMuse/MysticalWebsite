A flexible fragrance-based insomnia remedy using essential oils. Choose from the listed oils and experiment to find the combination that works best.

Quantities

  • Bergamot essential oil
  • Chamomile essential oil
  • Frankincense essential oil
  • Lavender essential oil
  • Manuka essential oil
  • Myrrh oil
  • Neroli essential oil
  • Petitgrain essential oil
  • Pine essential oil
  • Rosalina essential oil
  • Sandalwood essential oil
  • Tuberose oil
  • Vetiver essential oil
  • Jojoba oil (carrier, optional)
  • Sweet almond oil (carrier, optional)

Directions

  1. Select one or more of the listed oils and use in any of the following ways: blend a few drops into a tablespoon of carrier oil such as jojoba or sweet almond and massage into the temples, soles of the feet, or other areas of the body; add several drops to a warm bath before bedtime; place several drops in an aroma burner or essential oil diffuser and inhale; or use the oils to dress a dream pillow or dream potpourri.
