🧵Thread Binding Against Straying

A ritual to deter a partner from straying to recapture lost youth rather than regaining it with you, braiding colored threads with both partners' hairs to bind love and prevent separation.

Quantities

  • Red, yellow, and blue strong, long threads or yarns
  • 9 hairs from your partner's hairbrush or comb and 9 from your own head
  • Thin red thread
  • 1 very small mirror

Directions

  1. Braid the three long threads, adding in the hairs, one from each person alternately, into the braid, using extra thread to secure the hair, if necessary.
  2. Say softly and continuously: "Thread and wind, secure and bind, the love between us willingly, as we are joined soul, mind, and heart. Let none try our love to part." When it is finished, knot each end and join the ends, saying: "Two as one, through good and bad, united we remain, 'til time is run. Let none break the knots of unity, the thread of love, long may it be." Hide the braid under the mattress.
