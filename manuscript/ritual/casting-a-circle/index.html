Circle

For protection or increase energy prior to spellwork.

Quantities

  • Feather, incense, or other air symbol
  • Candles or fire symbol
  • Glass of water
  • Cup of soil or earth symbol

Directions

  1. For a simple Circle, use the materials to make a circle.
  2. You could also invoke the 4 elements (Air Water Fire Earth) for a complex circle casting.
  3. Start facing East and place Feather, Incense, or other air symbol on the floor around your workspace.
  4. Use Candles or another symbol to represent fire and place it on the floor at the South point.
  5. Next, face West and use a glass of Water on the floor.
  6. Finally, turn to face North and place a cup of Soil or another item to represent Earth to complete the circle.
  7. Face north and visualize an energetic White light coming up from the ground into your feet and entering your Body.
  8. You can use these words at the end, “The elements of Air, Fire, Water and Earth come together.
  9. I cast this circle of Protection and Power above, below, and within.”

Circle Casting Notes

  1. Outdoor Adaptations — If outdoors, substitute "walls and floors" with "foliage, stones, and ground" or other appropriate description of the surroundings. Change "I" to "we" when others are participating. Use appropriate Altar and Circle decorations for the season: flowers, leaves, shells, stones, crystals, cord, or meaningful objects. Names for the Goddess and the God may be used with or instead of Lady and Lord.
  2. Anointing Sigils — The sigil used for anointing may be a Pentagram, Triple Moon, Solar Cross, Lunar Spiral, Solar Cross with Lunar Spiral over it, etc. as desired. Anointing may be added if desired when not part of the described Circle casting.
  3. Names in Ritual — Use Craft Name in rituals with others, or Secret/Working Name if alone.
  4. Directional Movement — Deosil (clockwise) is N-E-S-W-N and Widdershins (counterclockwise) is N-W-S-E-N unless otherwise noted, as when starting at the West or East, but the pattern follows the same from these starting points.
  5. Libation Disposal — If rinsing out libation bowl in sink, envision this as a journey to Earth waters.
  6. Circle Markers — The Circle area may be marked by a cord, by Elemental candles (votives in safe containers are easiest), by items relating to the ritual the Circle is being cast for, or by nothing at all save awareness of where it is from the energy sent to form it.
  7. Ritual Structure — Cast the Circle, insert rituals, spells, recitations, etc., where designated, then return to the Circle page for the Cakes & Wine and Circle Opening.
  8. Preparation — Decide in advance what will be done in Circle; place ribbons or paper slips in your Book of Shadows so each page used for the entire ritual can be easily turned to. A book holder or book stand makes it easier to turn to the desired pages. Do not feel rushed, but make the experience a meaningful one.
  9. Music — Music may accompany the Circle casting/opening and rituals; song, musical performance, and drumming may be added where it feels appropriate.

Another Method:
Preparation

Before casting your circle, gather everything you'll need for the ritual or spell you plan to perform. Once inside the circle, you won't want to break its boundary to retrieve forgotten items. Double-check your materials, tools, and any written words you might need.

Creating the Proper Ambiance

The atmosphere of your workspace affects the quality of your magick:

  • Lighting - Candles create ideal lighting, though a dimmer switch works if open flames aren't practical
  • Temperature - Keep the space comfortable, especially if skyclad (ritual nudity) is part of your practice
  • Sound - Silence electronics and minimize disruptions; soft instrumental music can enhance focus
  • Scent - Incense or essential oils can shift consciousness and support your intention
  • Privacy - Ensure you won't be interrupted; inform housemates or lock doors as needed

Your Magickal Workspace

You don't need a dedicated temple room. Any space can become sacred when properly prepared. Clear physical and energetic clutter, and arrange your altar or working surface to suit your needs. Some practitioners work indoors, others prefer the outdoors - what matters is your ability to focus.

The Four Elements Technique

Start facing East and place a feather, incense, or other air symbol on the floor around your workspace.

Use candles or another symbol to represent fire and place it on the floor at the South point.

Next, face West and use a glass of water on the floor.

Finally, turn to face North and place a cup of soil or another item to represent earth to complete the circle.

Face north and visualize an energetic white light coming up from the ground into your feet and entering your body.

You can use these words at the end: "The elements of Air, Fire, Water and Earth come together. I cast this circle of Protection and Power above, below, and within."

Basic Circle Casting & Opening

This is a complete step-by-step procedure for formal circle work.

Casting the Circle

1. Setup — Lay out Circle and at North set Altar items (may use fruit juice, water, or tea instead of wine). Ground and center.

2. Sweeping — Sweep the Circle area with a besom deosil (clockwise) NORTH, EAST, SOUTH, WEST, and return to Altar:

"As I sweep, may the Besom chase away all negativity from within this Circle that it may be cleared and ready for my work."

3. Lighting — Light incense and Altar candles (in selected colors); ring bell or clap hands 3 times:

"The Circle is about to be cast and I freely stand within to greet my Lady and my Lord."

4. Calling Light — Take center Altar candle deosil around the Circle, raising at each Quarter (and lighting Elemental candles if used), returning to Altar when done:

  • [N]: "I call upon Light and Earth at the North to illuminate and strengthen the Circle."
  • [E]: "I call upon Light and Air at the East to illuminate and enliven the Circle."
  • [S]: "I call upon Light and Fire at the South to illuminate and warm the Circle."
  • [W]: "I call upon Light and Water at the West to illuminate and cleanse the Circle."

5. Drawing the Circle — Raise athame in power hand in front of the Altar:

"I draw this Circle in the Presence of the Lady and the Lord that They may aid and bless me in my work."

6. Casting the Boundary — Lower athame at North; walk deosil around the Circle, envisioning a blue light shooting out from the point to form the Circle boundary:

"This is the boundary of the Circle, around me, through walls and floors, above me and below me as a sphere is the Circle cast and consecrated to the Lady and the Lord that They may work with and through Their child, [name]. This Circle is charged by the powers of the Ancient Ones! Only love shall enter and leave."

7. Blessing the Salt — Return to Altar; ring bell (or clap hands 3 times); touch point of athame in the salt:

"Salt is life and purifying. I bless this salt to be used in this sacred Circle by the Power of the Goddess and the God."

8. Creating Holy Water — Using tip of athame, drop 3 portions of salt into water; stir 3 times with blade:

"Let the blessed salt purify this water that it may be blessed to use in this sacred Circle. Through the Power of the Goddess and the God is this water cleansed."

9. Consecrating the Circle — Sprinkle blessed water deosil around the Circle:

"I consecrate this Circle by the Lady and the Lord. This Circle is conjured a Circle of Power that is purified and sealed. So Mote It Be!"

10. Censing — Put water bowl on Altar; take incense deosil around Circle to cense it:

"With the aroma of incense do I honor and make welcome my visitors and helpers, visible and invisible."

11. Self-Anointing — Dab anointing oil with fingertip on forehead in a sigil:

"I, [name], am consecrated in the Names of the Goddess and the God, in this Their Circle."

Admitting Others to the Circle

Cut doorway in circle for others to enter; close and seal with sigil. Challenge each as they approach the Circle, then anoint with oil and sigil of choice:

  • a. "What do you want?" — Answer: "To enter the Circle."
  • b. "What is the password?" — Answer: "In Perfect Love and Perfect Trust."
  • c. "Enter and Merry Meet! I bid you welcome."

12. Invoking the Elementals — With wand, invoke the Elementals at each Quarter starting at the North and moving deosil; raise and open arms to invoke, then close and lower arms, turning to the next Quarter until all are called:

  • [N]: "I call upon you, Elemental Earth, to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have body and strength, we are kith and kin!" [visualize a powerful bull arriving]
  • [E]: "I call upon you, Elemental Air, to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I breathe and think, we are kith and kin!" [visualize a soaring eagle]
  • [S]: "I call upon you, Elemental Fire, to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have energy and drive, and consume life to live, we are kith and kin!" [visualize a mighty lion]
  • [W]: "I call upon you, Elemental Water, to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have emotions and my heart beats, we are kith and kin!" [visualize a leaping dolphin]

13. Cosmic Lemniscate — Draw Cosmic Lemniscate (∞) with wand over Altar.

14. Hailing the Powers — Set wand on Altar; raise athame in both hands straight overhead:

"Hail to the Elementals at the Four Quarters! Welcome Lady and Lord to this Circle! I stand between the worlds with Love and Power all around!"

15. Libation — Set down athame; pick up cup; pour some beverage into libation bowl, cauldron, or the ground to honor the Lady and the Lord, then take a sip; ring bell or clap 3 times.

At this point, proceed with your planned ritual work. When complete, return to the Circle Opening section below.

The Cord and Athame Method

A traditional way to lay out the triple circle using the consecrated cord and Athame together.

Preparation

Clear a space large enough for a nine-foot-diameter circle (eleven feet for coven work). Pin one end of the cord to roughly the center of the room, using a heavy object such as a chair or table leg, or have someone hold it in place if others are present.

Tracing the Circles

Stretch the cord out tightly and wrap it around the Athame's handle at the knot four feet six inches from the pinned end. Using the chair or anchor point as a pivot, trace a nine-foot-diameter circle lightly on the floor with the knife's point, keeping the cord taut throughout.

Move deosil (clockwise), starting and ending in the east; a small pocket compass can help you keep your bearings. As you trace, visualize blue witch fire streaming from the Athame's blade like a torch, leaving a line of flickering violet flame in its wake.

Shorten the cord to the next knot (four feet from the pinned end) and repeat the tracing to draw an eight-foot circle, then shorten it once more to the knot at three feet six inches for a seven-foot inner circle.

Purifying the Circle

Exorcise the ground within the finished triple circle by sprinkling it with charged water and censing it with charged incense at each of the four quarters, east, south, west, and north, moving deosil throughout. Once this is done, the circle is ritually purified and ready to focus the power raised within it.

Leaving the Circle

Sometimes you must leave a cast circle - perhaps you forgot an item or need to use the bathroom. Rather than breaking the entire circle, you can cut a temporary doorway:

  1. Using your athame, wand, or finger, trace a doorway shape in the circle's boundary
  2. Visualize the energy parting like a curtain
  3. Step through and complete your task quickly
  4. When returning, step back through the same spot
  5. Reseal the doorway by retracing it in reverse, visualizing the energy knitting back together

Try to minimize exits, as each opening temporarily weakens the circle's protective barrier.

Dismantling the Circle

When your work is complete, release the circle with respect. Move counterclockwise (widdershins), reversing the order in which you called the elements:

  1. Begin in the North - thank the element of Earth and bid it farewell
  2. Move to the West - thank Water and release it
  3. Continue to the South - thank Fire
  4. End in the East - thank Air

Visualize the circle's energy dissolving back into the ground or returning to the cosmos. Some practitioners say something like: "The circle is open but never broken. Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again."

Ground yourself afterward by eating something, touching the earth, or simply sitting quietly for a moment.
