A spell to boost confidence before a major promotion or interview, creating a clay figure charged with four elements and gold nuggets to manifest worth and shine bright.

Quantities

  • Children's play clay
  • Salt
  • Any tree or fruit incense
  • A bowl of water
  • Clear and gold glass nuggets, glass beads, or buttons

Directions

  1. Shape the clay into a featureless image of yourself.
  2. Sprinkle it with salt for the earth, spiral the lighted incense stick over it for air, hold it to the light for fire, and sprinkle it with water.
  3. Say: "Within me, earth, air, water, fire, calls the job I most desire." Press the nuggets into the figure, saying: "May I shine bright as the sun, my gifts find expression without repression, and my true worth be manifest." Keep the figure where natural light shines.
  4. For your interview, take it in a little pouch or bag.
  5. Hold it before the interview, repeating both sets of words.
  6. As you go in, say in your mind four times: "May I shine bright as the sun." Perform outdoors, the brightest time of the day.
