Coffin Nail

A record of a traditional method of hexing horses by throwing coffin nails into the stable, which allegedly causes the animals to go lame. Included here as both a counter-magick reference and a stable protection guide. Keeping the stable immaculately clean is the primary defense.

Note: Charms and spells for horses may also be used for donkeys and mules.

Quantities

  • Coffin nails (hex ingredient, for reference only)
  • Vigilance in stable cleanliness (protection)

Directions

  1. To protect against this hex:
    Keep stalls and the stable immaculately clean at all times. Remove any strange nails or unrecognized objects found in the stable promptly to maintain safety and reverse any malevolent spells that may have been cast.
  2. The hex (for reference):
    Throw coffin nails into the stable. This allegedly causes horses to become lame.
