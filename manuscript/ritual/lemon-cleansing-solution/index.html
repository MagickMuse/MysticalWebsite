🍋Lemon Cleansing Solution

An all-natural lemon, vinegar, and rosemary cleaning solution charged under the full moon and infused through the waning week. Draw the waning crescent on the lemon peel before squeezing it in, stir with rosemary both counterclockwise and clockwise, then let it sit with a citrine crystal before transferring to a spray bottle. Use as an all-purpose home cleaner to clear stale energy and invite in freshness. Avoid on granite and similar surfaces.

Quantities

  • Jar with lid
  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup purified, spring, or Moon Water
  • 1 large strip of lemon peel
  • Knife or toothpick
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 5 rosemary or sage sprigs
  • 10 drops essential oil of choice (optional)
  • Citrine crystal
  • Spray bottle
    ⚠️ Avoid using on granite or other acid-sensitive surfaces.

Directions

  1. Begin at the full moon.
  2. Combine the white vinegar and water in your jar.
  3. Hold the strip of lemon peel in your hand, rind-side up.
  4. On the white inner side, use a knife or toothpick to carve a waning crescent moon symbol, invoking cleansing and banishment.
  5. Then turn the peel so the yellow outer side faces down over the jar.
  6. Squeeze it firmly to spritz the citrus oils into the mixture, then drop the whole strip in to infuse.
  7. Sprinkle in the sea salt for cleansing, grounding, and protection.
  8. Take one rosemary sprig and use it to stir the mixture counterclockwise, visualizing all stagnant and unwanted energy dissolving away.
  9. Then reverse direction and stir clockwise, seeing your home glowing with clean, renewed energy.
  10. Add all remaining sprigs (and any essential oils you've chosen) to the jar.
  11. Seal the lid.
  12. Place the jar outside or on a windowsill to charge under the full moon.
  13. If you'd like to charge your citrine at the same time, set it out beside the jar, but be sure to bring it in before sunrise, as prolonged sun exposure can gradually fade citrine's color.
  14. Collect everything the next morning.
  15. Allow the solution to infuse for one week as the moon wanes, letting the banishing energy of the waning phase work its way through the mixture.
  16. After a week, strain out the rosemary and lemon peel (or leave them in if you prefer).
  17. Transfer the solution to your spray bottle and drop the citrine inside to keep the energy positive and uplifted as you use it.
  18. Use throughout your home as an all-purpose cleaner, floor wash additive, or laundry boost, spraying with intention as you clean to clear the old and welcome in the new.
