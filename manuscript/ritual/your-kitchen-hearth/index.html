Brigid

A multi-step ceremony to consecrate the kitchen stove as a sacred hearth, invoke Brigid's blessing on all food prepared there, and enter a meditation traveling back through time to all ancestors who cooked before sacred fire.

Quantities

No materials needed beyond a clean stove

Directions

  1. Clean your stove until you feel genuinely satisfied with it. The energy you bring to this matters, if you're doubtful about the job you did, wait for a day when you can give it your full attention.
  2. When the stove is clean, place both hands on the stovetop and speak:
    "Brigid of the hearth flame, Brigid of brightness,
    Brigid of the hearth flame, Brigid of warmth:
    Be on this cook place, be on the food,
    Be on the spoon that stirs it and on the one who stirs,
    This day and every day, this night and every night."
  3. Remove your hands from the stove. Light each burner, starting with the lower left and moving sunwise (clockwise). Then turn on the oven. Keep everything on low.
  4. Stand before the stove, close your eyes, and feel the heat. Let it warm you as a hearthfire would. Envision Brigid's fiery presence encompassing you and the heart of your kitchen.
  5. Imagine a stove from one hundred years ago and the people who cooked there. Visualize the lighting of that stove, the work of making food, the heat, the flame, the pots, the labor, the food gratefully received at the end of a long day.
  6. Continue moving back in time in jumps, as far as Brigid inspires you to go. At each point in time, see the sacredness of preparing food and the holiness of receiving it.
  7. When you reach a natural stopping place, see that oldest cooking fire clearly and envision Brigid there. She has always been present. She is present still. With eyes still closed, raise your hands before you with palms toward the heat of the stove. Feel the connection across the ages with all who have stood before their hearths and felt Brigid's presence.
  8. Linger as long as you wish. Then turn off the oven and each burner in the same order you lit them, beginning with the lower left and moving sunwise. From now on, whenever you turn on a burner or the oven, envision Brigid as the source of the heat, the fire coming from her hands and heart to yours as you feed yourself and those you love.
