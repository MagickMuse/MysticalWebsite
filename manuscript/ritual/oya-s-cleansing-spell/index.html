🌪️Oya's Cleansing Spell

◇Form
BathCharmOffering Rite
◇Method
Knot MagickVisualizationInvocationSmoke MagickEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
New Orleans Voodoo
◇Quantities
- 18 ribbons (9 long in different colors including maroon, purple, brown; 9 additional in different colors) - 9 small purple eggplants - 1 bottle red wine - 1 bottle dark rum - 1 cigar - 9 coins (any denomination) - Wine-colored flowers - White candles - Sea salt
◇Directions
1. Soak the eggplants in the red wine. 2. Divide the nine main ribbons into three groups and braid each group. You will have three braids. 3. Braid these three together to form one large braided belt. 4. Tie the additional nine ribbons around the belt so they will fall around your hips when worn. Set aside. 5. Drain and dry the eggplants. 6. Pierce the top of each eggplant. 7. Run one ribbon through each eggplant and tie each securely to the braided belt, consciously knotting your desires. 8. Take a mouthful of rum and spray it onto the belt. 9. Light the cigar and blow smoke over the belt, petitioning Oya to grant your desire. 10. Tie the belt around your waist (this part is most powerful in the nude, but do whatever is comfortable). Whirl and twirl through your home, visualizing all negativity entering into the eggplants. 11. When cleansing feels complete, remove the belt and place it in a plain brown paper bag with nine coins. 12. Take the package to a cemetery and leave it at the gates, best done at night. Rattle and shake the gates if you can without attracting attention. Tell Oya why you've come and what you need. 13. Return home via a different route. 14. Light white candles in the bathroom. Fill a tub with warm water and sea salt. Bathe and relax. 15. Put on clean clothes and go to bed in clean, fresh sheets.
◇Description
⚠️ Closed Practice Note: Oya is a Yoruba orisha venerated in Yoruba religion and its diaspora traditions (Santería, Candomblé, etc.). Approach her with respect for her cultural and spiritual context. A complex full night's ritual calling upon Oya, Yoruba orisha of wild storm winds and the only orisha with dominion over the dead. She blows away all impurities, leaving a fresh cleansed atmosphere. Ideal for beginning completely anew or following any unsettling ghost contamination. Simultaneously provides space and personal cleansing.
