A candle and sympathetic magick spell invoking Artemis, Greek goddess of the hunt, whose silver bow mirrors the crescent moon. You construct a symbolic lunar bow and arrow from everyday materials, a candle as the arrowhead, a cinnamon stick as the shaft, a bay leaf inscribed with your intention as the fletching, and rose petals arranged in a crescent bow shape. Twisted silver and red thread form the bowstring. The spell harnesses Artemis's legendary aim to send your intention piercing forward toward success.

Quantities

  • A candle
  • Oil of your choice
  • A candleholder
  • A cinnamon stick (or a stick gathered on a moonlit walk)
  • A Sharpie or marker
  • A bay leaf
  • Dried or fresh rose petals
  • Silver thread, ribbon, or yarn
  • Red thread, ribbon, or yarn
  • Matches or a lighter

Directions

  1. Dress the candle in oil and set it in the candleholder.
  2. Hover your hand around the candle and hold your intention clearly in your mind.
  3. Visualize the candle's flame as a fiery moonlit arrow blazing through the dark, aimed directly at your goal.
  4. The candle is the tip of the arrowhead.
  5. Lay the cinnamon stick in line with the candle to form the shaft of the arrow.
  6. If you prefer, use a stick you gathered on a moonlit walk; the wood will carry that nocturnal energy with it.
  7. With the Sharpie, write your intention on the bay leaf.
  8. Place the leaf at the far end of the stick, where the fletching would be on a real arrow, the feathers that steady its flight through the air and keep it true to its mark.
  9. Arrange the rose petals in a crescent bow shape around the arrow layout, so the full scene looks like an arrow nocked and ready to fly.
  10. The petals echo the shape of the waxing crescent moon, Artemis's bow itself.
  11. Twist the silver and red thread together into a single strand.
  12. Silver is the color of the moon; red is the color of fate and life force.
  13. Lay the twisted cord across the crescent of rose petals to represent the bowstring, drawn taut and ready.
  14. Close your eyes and visualize Artemis standing in the night, silver bow raised, arrow gleaming.
  15. Feel yourself drawing your own arrow back; hold your intention in your body and your breath.
  16. When you are ready, say aloud:
  17. "Mistress of Hunt, little escapes your aim.
    Guided by the light of the moon, whether it waxes or wanes,
    Your sharp arrow guides the way.
    Grant me success in ___ this day."
  18. Release your imaginary arrow.
  19. Feel it fly from your hands, piercing through the dark night, guided by moonlight and Artemis's steady hand.
  20. Now light the candle.
  21. Allow the candle to burn down completely, keeping it in sight.
  22. If you wish, leave all the items arranged and carry the spell forward by burning them in a lunar ritual at the full moon.
