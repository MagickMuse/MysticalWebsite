Egyptian spell to ease childbirth pain, invoking Horus, Hathor, and a clay dwarf figure representing Bes or Aha.

Quantities

  • 1 clay dwarf figure
  • Plant-based poultice ingredients (acacia leaves)

Directions

  1. Prepare a plant-based poultice (acacia leaves if available) and a clay figure of a dwarf (representing Bes or Aha).
  2. The magickian identifies with Horus and recites the following spell several times while placing the poultice on the laboring woman's head:
  3. 'I am Horus.
  4. I was thirsty, and I came down from the mountain.
  5. I found someone calling out, weeping.
  6. His wife was nearing her time.
  7. I got him to stop weeping.
  8. His wife called out for an image of a dwarf, made of clay.
  9. Let someone hurry to Hathor, the lady of Dendera, to fetch her healing amulet, so that Hathor will make this woman give birth!'
  10. While the spell is recorded as used by male magickians, midwives may have also employed such spells, recipes, and images in their work assisting laboring women.
