A lunar wishing working that harnesses camphor's dominion under the moon. A camphor square is dissolved into lunar-charged spring water, and the resulting water is used to cleanse magickal tools so they may serve as conduits for wish fulfillment. Note: camphor can be toxic, handle with care.

Quantities

1 camphor square; lunar-charged spring water; appropriate magickal tools to cleanse

Directions

  1. Dissolve a camphor square into lunar-charged spring water.
  2. Caution: camphor can be toxic; do not ingest.
  3. Use the camphor-infused water to cleanse appropriate magickal tools so that they may serve as conduits to fulfill your wishes.
  4. The lunar and protective properties of camphor charge the tools with moon energy.
