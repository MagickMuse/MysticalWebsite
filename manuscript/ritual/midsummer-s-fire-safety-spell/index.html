The following seven botanicals are known as "the herbs of Saint John": hawkweed, mistletoe, mullein, orpine, vervain, wormwood, and Saint John's Wort.

Gather them at the moment of greatest potency: midnight on Saint John's Eve (Midsummer's Eve) or noon on Saint John's Day. Dry the herbs completely, then hang them within your home to provide magical protection against fire.

Note: Orpine does not yet have a dedicated plant page in the grimoire.

Safety Notes

  • Mistletoe berries and leaves are toxic. Handle only the woody portions. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Wormwood essential oil is extremely toxic. Use only the dried plant leaves here.
