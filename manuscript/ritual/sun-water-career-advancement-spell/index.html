☀️Sun Water Career Advancement Spell

◇Form
PotionAltar Working
◇Method
Water MagickSolarChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A clear glass bowl half filled with water - Five small yellow citrine crystals, golden glass nuggets, or yellow glass beads
◇Directions
1. At dawn, add the crystals to the bowl one at a time, outdoors if possible, saying for each: "Gold of sunlight, break through the dawn, that my golden future may soon be born. Glorious noon, shine, bring growing days, burn. Sun bright, open golden new ways." At noon, remove the crystals and leave them in a circle on the ground. 2. Tip half the water on the ground, repeating the words. 3. Let the rest filter into clear glass bottles to splash on pulse points at times when you have a chance to shine, again repeating the words. 4. Perform from dawn until noon on a Sunday. 5. Midsummer is best of all.
◇Description
A Sunday dawn to noon sun water spell to open the door to advancement, especially if there is a glass ceiling or prejudice, using citrine crystals charged in water from dawn to noon.
