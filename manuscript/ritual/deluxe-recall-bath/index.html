Honeysuckle

A luxurious pre-sleep bath using rare and costly botanical oils to stimulate vivid dreams and aid recall. Petitgrain may be substituted for neroli at lower cost; their fragrances are similar and petitgrain may carry greater sedative properties. One of the Dream Recall Bath methods.

Quantities

  • 3 drops honeysuckle absolute
  • 4 drops jasmine attar
  • 4 drops lavender essential oil
  • 3 drops neroli essential oil (or petitgrain essential oil as a substitute)
  • 1 drop tuberose absolute

Directions

  1. Draw a full tub of warm water.
  2. Add all the oils to the bath.
  3. Soak before going to sleep.
