A Hekatean spirit-trapping seal revealed during Hekatean workings, consecrated at a crossroads with offerings to trap Empusae, Lamia, Mormo, Vrykolakas, specters, phantoms, and kakodaemon.

Quantities

  • 1 Triangular Spirit Trap seal (burned into oak wood or drawn with dragon's blood ink)
  • Red mullet fish
  • Bread
  • Raw eggs
  • Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Cake
  • Honey
  • Aconite (deadly poison - handle with extreme care)
  • Dandelion root
  • Optional: Mullein stalk
  • Optional: Oil for mullein stalk

Directions

  1. Timing: Perform at the new moon, or when protection is urgently needed.
  2. Location: A crossroads, preferably three-way, though four-way is acceptable.
  3. Preparation: Create or obtain the Triangular Spirit Trap seal.
  4. The seal can be burned into oak wood or drawn with dragon's blood ink on parchment or wood.
  5. The Ritual:
  6. At the crossroads, lay out offerings to Hekate: red mullet fish, bread, raw eggs, cheese, garlic, cake, honey.
  7. Place aconite and dandelion root with extreme caution (aconite is deadly poison).
  8. Hold the seal and invoke Hekate using her secret names:
  9. "Aktiophis, Ereshkigal, Nebotosoualeth,
    Queen of the Crossroads, Keeper of Keys,
    I call upon your ancient power.
    Bind within this seal all spirits of ill intent:
    Empusae who walk with one brass leg,
    Lamia who preys upon the innocent,
    Mormo who brings terror in the night,
    Vrykolakas who cannot rest,
    Apotropaioi, specters, phantoms, kakodaemon,
    All spirits harmful and malign.
    Let this seal be their prison,
    Let this seal be my shield.
    By your power, Great Hekate, so be it sealed."
  10. Placement: Place the seal in doorways, windows, or under floorboards where protection is needed.
  11. The seal traps harmful spirits and prevents them from entering or remaining in the protected space.
  12. Optional: To summon and trap a specific troublesome spirit, take a mullein stalk, dip it in oil, light it in the center of the seal, and call the spirit's name.
  13. Once trapped, extinguish the flame.
  14. Warning: Never use aconite internally or handle without extreme caution.
  15. It is deadly poisonous.
