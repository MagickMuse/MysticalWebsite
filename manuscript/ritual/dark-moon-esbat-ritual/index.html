🌕Esbat

The specific Esbat ritual for the Dark Moon (when no light is visible), honoring the Goddess as Tomb and Womb, the Great Creatrix of transformation. Uses the Dark Power Circle casting. Follows the Esbat Opening and precedes Cakes & Wine.

Quantities

  • Altar setup from Esbat Opening with Dark Moon candles (black-purple-black or black-white-black)
  • Book of Shadows or recitation materials
  • Divination tools (optional)

Directions

  1. After completing the Esbat Opening (following the Dark Power Circle casting), cross arms over your chest and bow your head.
  2. Then open your arms downward toward the ground:
  3. Queen of the Night, Queen of Mystery; silent rests the tomb of passage into rebirth.
  4. All returns unto Thee, for of Thee is all born.
  5. Raise your opened arms and lift your head:
  6. As [Name] are you known to me, for this is the Moon of my Lady as Tomb and Womb; Great Creatrix who takes the remnants of life past and from these fashions life to come.
  7. Great Lady of Shadow and Wisdom, Thy face is turned from the Earth, hidden in secrecy and mystery.
  8. Here now is the time of Perfect Love and Perfect Trust, for your children stand seemingly without Thy presence, and yet they know that Thou art steadfast and true, and as the turning of the Moon, is the cycle of life continued.
  9. You, with Your Lord [Name] at Your side, abide in us all.
  10. So Mote It Be!
  11. Read from prepared recitations.
  12. Meditate on the Mysteries.
  13. Conduct divination if desired.
  14. When all Esbat work is complete, raise open arms:
  15. Dark Mother of All; Keeper of Wisdom and Revealer of Mysteries, Thou who dwells within, let me never forget Thy love.
  16. For Love is the Law and Love is the Bond!
  17. So Mote It Be!
  18. Proceed to Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
