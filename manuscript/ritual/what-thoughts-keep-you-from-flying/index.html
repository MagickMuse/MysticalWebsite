🔓What Thoughts Keep You from Flying?

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationShadow Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
Materials: Journal and pen
◇Directions
1. Sometimes we get trapped by our own desires or by other people's expectations. 2. It's time to uncage yourself. 3. Grab your journal and find a quiet place to sit. 4. Take a few deep breaths to center and calm yourself, then make a list of thoughts, expectations, or desires (yours or someone else's) that make you feel trapped. 5. Your sentences might start like this: 6. - A good daughter always... - I should... - It's important to... - My grandma (mother, father, boss) will be disappointed if... 7. Choose one and ask yourself: If this thought was located in my body, where would it live? 8. Don't overthink this! 9. Your first answer is the answer. 10. After you've located the thought in your body, imagine disengaging it. 11. You might envision cutting the cord between yourself and the thought, or digging it out and tossing it in the compost. 12. One by one, visualize disengaging from each entrapping thought. 13. You may need to do this a few times to make it stick in your psyche.
◇Description
A journaling and visualization practice inspired by House Finch's escape from captivity. By identifying entrapping thoughts and expectations, then visualizing their release from your body, you uncage yourself from limiting beliefs.
