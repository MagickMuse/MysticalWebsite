🤐Stop Snide Remarks and Spite

A ritual to stop snide remarks or someone being constantly spiteful to you in front of a group or in the workplace, using knotted string to bind the tongue of the perpetrator.

Quantities

  • 1 long piece of string or a bootlace

Directions

  1. Tie the string/lace in as many knots as possible, left to right, saying in your mind for each knot: "Tongue tie, bid malice good-bye. You [name the worst perpetrator], hence shall silenced be about me, and I shall be free." Touch each knot in turn, right to left, and repeat the spell words for each one, picturing the perpetrator red-faced or shunned by the others and her tongue in knots.
  2. Take the string to work and touch each knot left to right when the nasty person starts to make you the butt of jokes.
