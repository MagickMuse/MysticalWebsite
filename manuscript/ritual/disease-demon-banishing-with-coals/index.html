🔥Disease Demon Banishing with Coals

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Incense Blend
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BanishingSmoke Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
7 glowing coals from the hearth 1 bowl of water
◇Directions
1. Take seven glowing coals from the hearth. 2. One at a time, circle each coal around the head of the afflicted person seven times. 3. There will be forty-nine circles in total when all seven coals have been used. 4. After each coal has completed its seven circles, toss it into the bowl of water. 5. When all seven coals have been used, the working is complete at the sickbed. 6. Either a post-menopausal woman or a pre-menstrual girl must then take the bowl to a crossroads and dump out its contents. 7. She must not look at the water as it is poured out, and must not return home by the same route she took to the crossroads.
◇Description
A folk healing banishing spell using seven glowing coals circled around the afflicted person's head to draw out and dispel disease demons. Also effective against the Evil Eye. The spent coals are ritually disposed of at a crossroads.
