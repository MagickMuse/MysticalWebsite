A fertility-promoting oil named in honor of Astarte ("the filled womb"), a Semitic deity associated with erotic rites, independent women, and reproductive health. Many essential oils are NOT safe during pregnancy, especially in the first months, reserve this oil for magickal uses such as dressing candles and charms rather than direct body application during pregnancy.

Quantities

  • Essential oil of coriander
  • Essential oil of jasmine
  • Essential oil of myrrh
  • Essential oil of petitgrain OR neroli
  • Rose attar
  • Sweet almond oil (carrier, to fill bottle)
  • Jojoba oil (carrier, to fill bottle)
  • Optional: cowries or henna twigs and blossoms

Directions

  1. Blend the essential oils (coriander, jasmine, myrrh, petitgrain or neroli, rose attar) into a bottle filled with sweet almond and jojoba oils.
  2. If desired, add cowries or henna twigs and blossoms to the oil.
