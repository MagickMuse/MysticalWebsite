A guided shapeshifting meditation to attune to the element of Air through the body and perspective of a bird in flight.

Quantities

Comfortable, non-restrictive clothing
Optional: handheld fan (if outdoor air is unavailable)
Optional: cleansing incense
Optional: soft ambient sound

Directions

  1. Sit outside or near an open window to feel real moving air, or use a handheld fan as a substitute; optionally burn cleansing incense, play soft ambient sound, and wear comfortable clothing.
  2. Sit down, close your eyes, and breathe slowly, releasing tension from the feet upward with each exhale.
  3. Visualize a landscape that feels right and place yourself in it, exploring its temperature, sounds, smells, and the taste of the air.
  4. Become aware of your arms lifting and let yourself shift into the form of a bird.
  5. Feel the air through your feathers, spread your wings, take off, and play with soaring, gliding, banking, and diving.
  6. When ready, glide back to the ground, let the bird form dissolve, and feel yourself become human again while thanking the air and wind.
  7. Breathe slowly back into your body, open your eyes, and reorient by flexing your fingers and toes and moving gently.
  8. Journal about the sensations, sounds, and quality of the air experienced, noting differences between human and bird perspective.

A shapeshifting meditation for connecting with the element of Air through a bird's body and perspective. If the bird you work with is primarily tied to earth or water rather than air, adapt accordingly: substitute a water or earth setting, and imagine the bird in its natural context rather than in flight.

This meditation is not concerned with physical possibility. It is purely imaginative, and its purpose is felt experience, not accuracy.
