🪆Doll Hex Removal (Buried Poppet)

A poppet spell to remove a persistent, stubborn hex. A rag doll representing the caster is soaked overnight in Four Thieves Vinegar and charged water (ideally in full moonlight), dried in the sun, anointed with Uncrossing Oil, wrapped in white cloth, and buried while chanting. The hex is meant to leave the caster and settle into the ground with the doll. Protective plants and weekly feedings with oils or bodily fluids maintain the grave.

Quantities

Rag doll (representing the caster)
Name tags
Four Thieves Vinegar
Spring water or magickally charged water (equal parts with vinegar)
White cloth
Uncrossing Oil (or similar)
Optional maintenance: Black Cat Oil, menstrual blood, urine
Optional: protective plants (nettle, cactus) for the grave

Directions

  1. Make a doll to represent you; a rag doll is fine.
  2. Pin a name tag on the front and the back of the doll.
  3. Blend equal parts Four Thieves Vinegar and spring or magickally charged water.
  4. Put the doll in a dish and cover it with this solution.
  5. Let it soak overnight; if it can be exposed to full moonlight, this is extremely beneficial.
  6. In the morning, dispose of the liquid and let the doll dry out in the sun.
  7. When the doll is dry, place it on a white cloth.
  8. Pour Uncrossing Oil or similar over the doll.
  9. Wrap it up in the cloth, folding away from you.
  10. Bury the doll in the ground, chanting throughout the burial:
  11. "Here I lie! The curse on me dies!"
  12. The spell should leave you and settle into the ground with the doll.
  13. Go home and take purification and protection measures.
  14. Keep an eye on that grave; do not dig it up.
  15. Consider planting protective plants over it such as nettles or cactus, and feed weekly with Uncrossing Oil, Black Cat Oil, menstrual blood, and/or urine.
