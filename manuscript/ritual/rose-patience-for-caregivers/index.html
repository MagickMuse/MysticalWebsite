🌹Rose Patience for Caregivers

A rose-scented candle ritual for tolerance and patience if you have to care for a difficult, sick, or frail elderly relative, anointing with rose oil to maintain kindness.

Quantities

  • 1 rose-scented candle
  • 1 bottle of rose essential oil

Directions

  1. Hold the candle and say: "Rose of gentle love and kindness, release patience in me, that I may speak and act to [name] tolerantly." Light the candle and lift the oil so light shines through it, repeating the spell words.
  2. Let the candle burn and leave the oil where the light will reflect into it (but not too near the flame).
  3. When the candle is burned through, put a drop of oil on your brow, at the center of your throat, and on your two inner wrists points for your heart, repeating the spell words each time.
  4. When you get stressed or impatient with the difficult person, anoint yourself with the oil on your brow, throat, and inner wrist pulse points, saying the spell words.
  5. Once a week, repeat the spell to re-empower the oil.
