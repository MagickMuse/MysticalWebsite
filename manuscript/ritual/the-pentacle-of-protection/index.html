A three-part talisman comprising the Pentagram of Solomon, the Hexagram of Solomon, and the Secret Seal of Solomon, sewn together into a single packet and worn concealed around the neck on a red cord. It may also be displayed to the four quarters or to a manifesting entity during conjuration.

Quantities

  • Three 4-inch squares of talisman paper (exorcised with fire and water)
  • First square: pen and ink of art, for the Pentagram of Solomon
  • Second square: pen and ink of art, for the Hexagram of Solomon
  • Third square: blood ink (own blood or raw meat blood), for the Secret Seal of Solomon
  • Incense: pure dragon's blood resin, OR one part church incense, one part lignum aloes (agarwood), one part powdered alum, five drops cedarwood oil
  • Exorcised needle and red thread (for sewing)
  • Red chamois leather or silk wallet for housing the completed talisman
  • Red cord or ribbon for wearing

Directions

  1. Circle and Preparation:
  2. On a Tuesday or Saturday at midnight, with the moon waxing, cast and purify your regular circle using the incense described above.
  3. Cut three 4-inch squares from talisman paper. Exorcise each with fire and water.
    First Paper, Pentagram of Solomon:
  4. With pen and ink of art, draw a five-pointed star within a circle. Rule lightly in pencil first, using a compass to construct the circle. Fill in the magickal symbols and words beginning at the top point, working clockwise, finishing with the centre. As each word is written, chant it aloud combined with the following, substituting the relevant divine name:
  5. Be ye far from us, all hostility, deceit, and illusion, in the name of Abdia!
  6. Be ye far from us, all hostility, deceit, and illusion, in the name of Ballater!
    (Continue through all names at the points and centre)
  7. When complete, retrace the star and all symbols above the paper with the Athame, visualizing them burning in blue witch fire in the air.
    Second Paper, Hexagram of Solomon:
  8. Draw the six-pointed star (hexagram). Inscribe the T-shaped triple tau cross in the centre last. This cross, called the triple tau of the arch Mosms, is a variant of the triple sealing cross. Combined with the hexagram and its six minor taus, it forms an extraordinarily powerful talisman of protection.
  9. When the ink has dried, retrace the inverted triangle (point down) with salt water from the chalice, chanting: So therefore he who would govern the works of fire must first asperge with lustral water of the loud resounding sea.
  10. Then with the thurible, trace an upright triangle (point up) in the air above the star, chanting: And when after all the phantoms are banished, thou shalt perceive that holy formless fire, that fire which darts and flashes through the hidden depths of the universe: Hear thou the voice of fire!
  11. Retrace the whole star again with the Athame in silence, visualizing it glowing in blue light.
    Third Paper, Secret Seal of Solomon:
  12. Clean the pen of art thoroughly of all ink. Draw the Secret Seal using blood ink (own blood, or blood from raw meat). This seal is the symbol by which the seventy-two lords of the demons or Djinn were bound and imprisoned in a brazen vessel and sunk beneath the ocean, later released by Babylonian wizards. The legend is connected to the drowned Nephilim.
  13. When dry, retrace the seal with the Athame, charging it with light and conjuring with these words: Bound, bound, bound be all demons and powers of adversity from the north, south, east, and west! Bound, bound, bound be all ill-wishers and those that practise violence against the bearer of this! Bound, bound, bound shall be all; bound and sealed from N [witchname], son of M [parent] and L [parent]. Bound and held subject to his will.
    Assembly and Consecration:
  14. Sandwich the Secret Seal between the pentagram and hexagram, stars facing outwards. Using an exorcised needle and red thread, sew neatly around all four edges to create a permanent three-layer packet.
  15. Hold the packet in the incense smoke with the Athame tip pressed against it. Chant: O Pentacle of Power, be thou fortress and defence to [N] against all foes, visible and invisible, in every magickal work! Strongly visualize blue fire passing down the Athame blade to charge the pentacle.
  16. Draw the triple cross in the air with the Athame and seal with: So mote it be!
    Wearing:
  17. Place the pentacle in a small chamois leather or red silk wallet. Sew to a red cord or ribbon and wear concealed around the neck of the bearer.
