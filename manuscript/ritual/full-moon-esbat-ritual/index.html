🌕Esbat

The specific Esbat ritual for the Full Moon, featuring Drawing Down the Moon and communion with the Goddess in Her fullness. Follows the Esbat Opening and precedes Cakes & Wine.

Quantities

  • Wand
  • Altar setup from Esbat Opening
  • Book of Shadows or recitation materials
  • Materials for any planned spell work, consecrations, or water charging

Directions

  1. After completing the Esbat Opening, stand at the altar with arms open wide and feet apart:
  2. Behold the Great Lady, who travels the sky; the stars shine around Her and light up the night.
  3. Pick up the wand and open your arms toward the Full Moon (step outside if possible) for the Drawing Down:
  4. Lovely Lady known by so many names, but known to me as [Name], with the Lord [Name] at Your side; honor and reverence I give to You and invite You to join with me on this, Your special night.
  5. Descend, my Lady, and speak with Your child, [Name].
  6. Set the wand on the altar and listen in stillness for Her guidance.
  7. Read from prepared recitations, or allow spontaneous poetry, song, or expression to flow through you.
  8. Conduct any planned workings: Drawing Down the Moon, charging of water, consecrations, meditations, or other spellcraft prepared for this night.
  9. When all Esbat work is complete, raise open arms:
  10. You are the Mother of All.
  11. Maiden, Mother, and Crone; You are at life's beginning and at its end.
  12. You dwell within us all for You are Life and Love, and thus do You make me Life and Love.
  13. Love is the Law and Love is the Bond!
  14. So Mote It Be!
  15. Proceed to Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
