🌕Silver Bullet Spell

A serious spell invoking Diana, the Roman triple goddess of hunting and the Moon, to eliminate menacing problems. Best cast on the Full Moon. Uses a silver candle, bull's-eye target, and arrowroot powder to magickally aim at a clearly identified problem.

Quantities

Candle (carved with a bull's-eye/triple Moon symbol)
Bull's-eye image or drawing
Arrowroot powder
Optional: items connected to the problem

Directions

  1. Identify your problem clearly; sum it up in one or two words, meditating deeply or seeking a dream message if needed to ensure you have correctly identified it.
  2. Write the words in the center of the bull's-eye and carve them into the candle, along with the symbol for the triple Moon if the candle is large enough.
  3. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space around your working area, on the night of a Full Moon if possible.
  4. Put the bull's-eye on your altar and place the candle upright in its center. If using items connected to the problem, place flat items under the bull's-eye and other items around it.
  5. Working widdershins, spread the arrowroot powder around the candle.
  6. Raise power, raise your arms, and recite the first incantation calling on Diana.
  7. Touch the candle, visualizing it as a magickal silver bullet that will strike your problem and eliminate it. Raise power higher, then raise your arms again and recite the second incantation.
  8. Light the candle to set the spell in motion, reciting the third incantation as many times as feels necessary while concentrating on the bullet striking your problem.
  9. Monitor the candle while it burns, visualizing the problem shattering, fading, or otherwise resolving.
  10. Once the candle has burned out, gather the powder (it is fine if wax has mixed into it) and take it outside.
  11. Face each cardinal point in turn, starting with North and working deosil, tossing or blowing one-fourth of the powder in each direction while reciting the fourth incantation each time.
  12. Ground the power you raised, physically touching the earth or hugging a tree if necessary. Clean up, and eat or drink something if you are still feeling the effects.

Legend has it that a werewolf can only be killed by a silver bullet. This spell is for a problem so menacing, so fearsome, or so dangerous that it has become almost like a werewolf in your life, when all nonmagickal efforts to deal with it have failed.

The spell invokes Diana, a powerful Roman triple goddess whose dominion includes hunting and the Moon. Casting it in moonlight, or in view of the Moon, will increase its power.

⚠️
A Warning: This is a serious spell that should be reserved for serious problems. It is designed to eliminate problems, not to do harm to anyone or anything. To misuse this spell is to paint a bull's-eye on yourself and invite three silver bullets to strike you. Proceed with caution.

The Incantations

Hail Diana!
Queen of Witches,
I call upon thee.
Jana, Tana, Fana,
Please help me.
Maiden Huntress,
I call upon you
To steady my arm,
Let me do no harm,
And make my aim true.
Bullet speed, through the dark,
Straight on course, to the source,
Find your target, hit your mark!
By candle's light,
By bullet's flight,
Queen of the Night,
Make things right.
Diana Triformis, goddess trine,
Let a good solution be mine!

Afterward

Remember that the more difficult or complicated a problem is, the more complex and time-consuming its solution may be. If you have cast the spell properly, you should see some action toward a solution by the next Full Moon.

If nothing happens, or if the problem gets worse, consider that you may have been wrong in your assessment of the situation. Reconsider the problem, work toward a more accurate identification of it, and aim a new silver bullet.
