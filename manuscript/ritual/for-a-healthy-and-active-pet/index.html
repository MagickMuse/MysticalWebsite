A crescent moon jade water ritual dipping crystal seven times to fill pet with vitality and health.

Quantities

  • 1 small, round green jade crystal
  • Pet's water bowl, half-filled with water

Directions

  1. Go outdoors and face the crescent moon, putting the jade in the water and holding the bowl up to the moon.
  2. Say seven times, "Be filled with vitality, be filled with activity. Moon as you glow, so will health grow in my beloved pet , \[name\]." Take the bowl indoors and remove the jade, dipping it seven times into the bowl of water before giving the water to the pet and repeating the spell words seven times.
  3. Each time you fill the water bowl, dip the jade in the water seven times, repeating the spell words.
  4. Perform at the crescent moon.
