⚰️Assistance from Beyond the Grave Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Ancestor Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
Article of beloved's clothing Sea salt Graveyard dirt Small offering for the spirit Bag or packet
◇Directions
1. Bring an article of the beloved's clothing to the cemetery and find the grave of a spirit who is sympathetic toward you and holds influence over the beloved. 2. Bury the clothing at or directly beside the grave. 3. Speak openly with the spirit: state your desire clearly, explain your request, and give reasons why they should act on your behalf. 4. Leave the clothing undisturbed for three days. 5. On the third day, retrieve it without brushing away the earth clinging to it; take a small amount of additional graveyard dirt as well. 6. Leave an offering the spirit would welcome before departing. 7. Combine the clothing and graveyard dirt with sea salt and seal them inside a bag or packet. 8. Place this near or above a threshold the beloved is certain to cross. 9. The working is said to show results within nine days.
◇Description
A cemetery working to call a beloved back by enlisting a sympathetic spirit. The beloved's clothing is buried at a grave for three days, retrieved with graveyard dirt, combined with sea salt, and placed near a doorway the beloved will pass.
