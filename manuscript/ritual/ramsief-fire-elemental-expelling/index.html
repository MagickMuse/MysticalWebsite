🔥Ramsief Fire Elemental Expelling

A ritual for creating an artificial Fire elemental named Ramsief to expel an unwanted person from a location. Uses elemental pore breathing to gather Fire energy, projects it into a triangle to form a ram-headed humanoid with six flaming axes, then commands it to remove the target within three moons.

Quantities

  • Triangle marker for altar (drawn, painted, or formed from objects)
  • Wand or Fire tool (if using traditional elemental tools)
  • Materials for circle casting according to your tradition
  • Paper and writing implement for sigil and name
  • Three months timeframe from working date

Directions

  1. This example comes from a Philadelphia occult group that needed to expel a dangerous member.
  2. The group created an artificial Fire elemental combining the words Fire and Mars (the planet of aggressive action) to form the name RAMSIEF.
  3. A sigil was created from these letters.
  4. The form chosen was a ram-headed, very large, red humanoid with six arms, each holding a flaming axe.
  5. This form drew influence from Aries (Mars-ruled zodiac sign) to amplify the martial expelling energy.
  6. Open a circle and invoke the watchtowers or guardians according to your typical practice.
  7. Mark out a triangle on the altar or working surface.
  8. This triangle will serve as the manifestation space for the elemental.
  9. Begin pore breathing the element of Fire.
  10. Visualize Fire having the qualities of heat, dryness, and expansion.
  11. Draw this elemental force into your body through every pore of your skin, filling yourself completely with Fire energy.
  12. When you have gathered sufficient Fire element in your body (you may feel heat, energy, or a sense of expansion), project the energy out from your body into the triangle.
  13. See the Fire energy gather first as a cloud of flames within the triangle.
  14. Through force of will, command the cloud of flames to take the shape you have designed.
  15. In this case, see the flames coalesce into the form of Ramsief: a large ram-headed red humanoid with six arms, each wielding a flaming axe.
  16. Put as much detail as you can into the visualization.
  17. The more vividly you can see the form, the better the spirit will manifest.
  18. Once the figure is fully formed and can be clearly seen or sensed, draw your wand (the tool associated with Fire) or point your hand at Ramsief.
  19. Address the spirit with the following invocation, inserting the target's name where indicated: By the Lords of the Southern Gate, By the White ones of the Noontide Hour, By the Sovereign Spirits of Notus, And by All the Djinn of the Desert, I name thee Ramsief.
  20. Thou art Ramsief.
  21. Go forth and expel [name of target] from this city.
  22. Remove them from our midst.
  23. Accomplish this within the space of three moons.
  24. On the Third Full Moon, Whether you have accomplished your task or not, Dissipate back into the element of Fire, And seek the peace of oblivion.
  25. Heed my words and do my will.
  26. Fiat!
  27. Fiat!
  28. Fiat!
  29. After issuing these commands, see Ramsief depart from the triangle to carry out the assigned task.
  30. Close your circle according to your tradition.
  31. The elemental will work for three lunar months and then automatically dissipate back into Fire element whether or not the task is complete.
  32. This built-in dissolution prevents the elemental from becoming a persistent problem.
  33. As with all counter-magick, perform divination first to confirm you are genuinely under attack and not experiencing crossed conditions from your own actions.
  34. Be prepared to take responsibility for all outcomes.
