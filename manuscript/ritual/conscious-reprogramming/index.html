❤️‍🩹Healing🕊️Release

A curanderismo technique from Mexican healing traditions for identifying and releasing limiting beliefs energetically imprinted in your body. Extracts negative programming and replaces it with beliefs aligned with your true essence.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. REFLECTION
  2. Inhale through your nose and exhale through your mouth three times.
  3. Bring your hands to your heart.
  4. Ask yourself: What negative beliefs are stopping me from living to my fullest potential? What negative beliefs have been imprinted as truth but are simply lies? What voices do I carry inside that guide my path unconsciously that I need to release?
  5. If your heart reveals several limiting beliefs, ask: If released today, which belief will improve my life most significantly?
    LOCATION
  6. After your heart tells you what must be released, tune in to your body to find out where this energy has been implanted.
  7. Negative imprints may live in your chakras, auric field, soul, or mind. They may exist all over your skin but are likely concentrated in one or two places.
  8. They may feel like warm or cold spots, areas of energetic condensation, rough or bumpy areas, or unpleasant sensations.
    EXTRACTION
  9. Rub your hands for five to ten seconds, intending to activate their healing power. Ask them to help you extract the ideas that were not even yours to begin with.
  10. Gently tap the center of your palms five times while chanting: "I activate my innate ability to manipulate energy, a gift that resides within me. By remembering and claiming, I bring myself closer to my divine right of joy, love, and abundance."
  11. Ask the earth for permission to allow you to release negative imprints into her, to be transmitted into neutral energies or light. You can also work with the spirit of the ocean, the fire of a candle, deities or ancestors who may be showing up to support your process of liberation, the devas of plants, or a river.
  12. Imagine, feel, sense, and visualize that you are using your hands to pull out of your body all that you no longer need. Scoop it out with your fingers and keep releasing it to the earth or whoever is supporting you with this process.
  13. Repeat this release at least three times or until it feels like you have released as much as you are ready to release today.
    REPLACEMENT
  14. After you release those negative beliefs, substitute them for something completely opposite to what you have released. Examples: Releasing the idea that you cannot call upon a healthy relationship → Replace with the energy of feeling enough and feeling worthy of love. Releasing the idea of lack and never having overflow → Replace with the energies of prosperity.
  15. Begin by connecting with Spirit (Source, God, Goddess, creator, universe).
  16. Engage with the presence and ask it to give you the frequency of the energy you need to replenish all you have released.
  17. Visualize this energy descending from Spirit, through the planet's layers, down to the top of your head.
  18. Allow this energy to start integrating with you, from your crown chakra to your third eye, to your ears, your eyes, your mouth, and your throat, to your shoulders, arms, hands, upper and lower back, heart, solar plexus, belly button, sexual organs, sacral chakra, root chakra, hips, legs, knees, ankles, toes, and the bottom of your feet.
  19. Visualize this energy expanding and recalibrating your essence.
    INTEGRATION
  20. Bring your hands to your heart and recite the following decree three times: Grabado está en todas las dimensiones, tiempos, espacios, y frecuencias. (So be it in all the dimensions, times, spaces, and frequencies.)
  21. By reciting this decree, you are vocalizing and using your words as a spell, ensuring that what you have inserted and expanded integrates within yourself as well as yourself in other dimensions, times, spaces, and frequencies.
    TAKING CONSCIOUS ACTION
  22. Bring awareness to the way you have been living your life up to this point.
  23. Pay attention to the decisions and actions you may have taken due to having these negative beliefs.
  24. Consciously choose to repair what needs to be repaired.
  25. Embrace what needs to be embraced and let go of what no longer vibrates to the new frequency you have created.
    Journaling questions to support your process:
  26. What aspects of my life need repair due to the influence of negative beliefs?
  27. What no longer serves me or resonates with my new frequency?
  28. How can I ensure that my decisions align with the new beliefs I have integrated?
  29. What kind of person am I becoming now that I host a different set of beliefs in my energetic body?
  30. How can I continue to say yes to myself and my needs?
  31. How can I prioritize keeping my belief systems in check?
  32. How can I get into the discipline of practicing conscious programming at least once a year?
