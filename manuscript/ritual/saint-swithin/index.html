📅July 14

A dream oracle performed on Saint Swithin's Eve, July 14th. The four corners of a paper are burned, three questions are written in red ink on the remaining sheet, and the paper is wrapped with three hairs and knotted. It is placed under the pillow for three consecutive nights. One of the Dream Oracle methods.

Quantities

  • A square of fine paper
  • A new pen with red ink
  • Three hairs from your own head

Directions

  1. On Saint Swithin's Eve, July 14th, cut the four corners off a square of fine paper and burn the cut corners.
  2. Using a new pen with red ink, write the three things you most wish to know on the now corner-less paper.
  3. Fold the paper up.
  4. Take three hairs from your head and wrap them around the folded paper, tying a knot with each hair in turn.
  5. As you make each knot, state one of your three questions aloud.
  6. Place the folded paper under your pillow.
  7. Leave it there for three consecutive nights.
  8. The answers should be revealed across those three nights of dreaming.
