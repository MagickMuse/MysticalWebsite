⚠️ New Orleans Voodoo is a closed practice sacred to its originating culture and should only be practiced by those within that tradition.
A week-long invocation of the Loas of destruction. The target's image and hair are placed in a cup of vinegar, sealed with dripping candle wax, and the conjuration is repeated nightly for seven days.

Quantities

6 black candles
Musk incense
Hair strands from the target
1 picture of the target
1 black glass cup
Vinegar

Directions

  1. Light the musk incense and all six black candles.
  2. Speak aloud: I conjure and summon the Loas of destruction.
  3. Pour vinegar into the black glass cup.
  4. Look at the picture of the target and let your anger rise.
  5. Visualize their life being cursed and miserable.
  6. Place the picture into the cup and declare: Spirits of darkness, find [target's name].
  7. Destroy their life.
  8. Drip wax from each of the six black candles into the cup while saying: Find [target's name], spirits of destruction.
  9. Fill their mind with torture and pain.
  10. Place strands of the target's hair into the cup while saying: I conjure thee [target's name], may destruction and pain be your only life.
  11. May the spirits of darkness fill your body with their vengeance.
  12. Leave the altar setup undisturbed for one full week.
  13. Repeat the entire invocation each night throughout that week.
