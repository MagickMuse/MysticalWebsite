💚Malachite Merchant Spells

◇Form
Charged ObjectCharm
◇Method
Energy WorkEmpowerment
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Malachite stone(s) or malachite gem essence
◇Directions
1. Choose one or more applications: Place malachite strategically in your cash register. 2. Hang it in the corners of your business. 3. Wear it as jewelry while at work. 4. Carry it in a charm bag. 5. For gem essence: add to your bath, massage into the soles of your feet before bedtime, add to floorwash, or add to a spray bottle filled with spring water or magickal formula water and spray into the corners of your business and over its threshold.
◇Description
Malachite, the merchant's stone, is used in multiple ways to draw wealth and tip the scales of fortune. It can be placed in a cash register, hung in the corners of a business, worn as jewelry, or carried in a charm bag. Malachite gem essence can also be added to baths, massaged into the feet, added to floorwash, or sprayed into the corners of a business.
