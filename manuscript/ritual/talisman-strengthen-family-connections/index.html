A talisman made from shredded holiday cards and gift tags from loved ones, scented with essential oils and carried to strengthen family connections and keep distant loved ones close at heart during the winter season.

Quantities

  • Holiday cards, gift tags, or written messages from loved ones (as many as desired)
  • Cloth bag or poppet (small fabric figure)
  • 3-5 drops total of juniper, cedar, and/or cinnamon essential oil
  • Optional: small photograph of loved ones
  • Optional: needle and thread if using a poppet

Directions

  1. Preparation:
    Gather holiday cards, gift tags, or written messages from family members and loved ones. These personal items carry the energy and connection of those who sent them.
    Shred the cards and tags into small pieces, keeping the most meaningful sections with handwritten messages or signatures if possible.
    Assembly:
  2. Place the shredded papers into a cloth bag or inside a poppet (small fabric figure).
  3. Add a few drops of juniper, cedar, or cinnamon essential oil to the mixture to scent it. These oils carry associations with home, family, protection, and warmth.
  4. If desired, include a small photograph of loved ones or a family gathering to further strengthen the connection.
  5. Close the bag securely or sew the poppet shut.
    Activation and Use:
    Hold the talisman close to your heart and think of the people you wish to feel connected to. Visualize warm memories, loving conversations, and the bond you share.
    Carry the talisman in your purse or briefcase to keep your family close at heart, especially when physically separated during the holidays or throughout the year.
    Refresh the scent periodically by adding more drops of essential oil as needed.
