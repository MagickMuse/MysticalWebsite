🕯️Blessing of the Candles

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ConsecrationInvocationVisualizationCandle Magick
◇Category
Consecration
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- White altar cloth - One white candle in a safe holder for the central flame - All candles to bless, unboxed and unwrapped if possible, clear plastic is fine - A stable, fire safe table or altar against a wall is fine
◇Directions
1. Setup: - Drape the altar with a white cloth. Place one white candle in the center, this can be previously used, it is the only one you will light. Arrange all other candles so every wick is visible. 2. Ground and center: - Stand before the altar. Breathe deeply. Visualize roots from your feet into the earth, and branches from your raised arms into the sky, until you feel connected and steady. 3. Invoke the Goddess: Gracious Goddess, I come before you on this night or day to ask that your blessings be bestowed upon these tools of fire and light that I have gathered here. Brigid, Diana, Aphrodite, Danu, and Inanna, I call to you, Goddess, in all your forms to bestow your blessings on these candles so they always provide the pure and true energy of the universe, and never cause harm or ill to anyone. May these candles always please you, and call you back to their tiny flames. 4. Light the central white candle. 5. Invoke the God: Great God, I stand before this altar and ask that your blessings also be given to the candles gathered here tonight or today. Pan, Februus, Bragi, and Cupid, I call to you in all of your forms to bestow your blessings on these tools of fire and air so they may always provide energy that is steady, true, and positive. I ask that you guard these candles from outside energies that might taint them. May these candles always please you, and call you back to their tiny flames. 6. Transfer the blessing: - Focus on the central flame. Gather the raised power around it, then see it spread to every wick. Visualize sparks, tiny lightning, or threads of light touching each candle at the wick. Ensure every candle is reached. 7. Thanksgiving: Great Goddess, in all of your forms you bring blessings to me each day, thank you for blessing these candles. Great God, in all of your facets you protect, thank you for guarding the candles gathered here. - Thank any patrons you called by name. 8. Store with care: - Stow blessed candles where others will not handle them, in a cabinet, high shelf, or display that avoids touch.
◇Description
An Imbolc rite to bless and safeguard candles you will use through the year. Works for mixed purposes without changing their original alignments.
