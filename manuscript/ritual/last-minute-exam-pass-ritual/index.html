A ritual to pass a really important exam when you know you haven't studied as hard as you should have, using rosemary, sage, or thyme with four elements to invoke help and clarity.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 jar of one of the following herbs: dried rosemary, sage, or thyme
  • 1 small drawstring bag or purse

Directions

  1. On day 1, wash away a little of the herb under a running faucet, put a little on your tongue, bury a little, scatter some in the air, and put some in the bag or purse.
  2. As you do each action, say: "Earth, Water, and Sky, I do not know why I did not study more, but I will do my best. By winds North, South, East, and West, so powers that be, come help me, that I may see success, no less." Repeat the actions and the spell words on days 2 and 3, and if any herbs are left, scatter them on the ground.
  3. Take the bag to the exam and hold it before starting, repeating the spell words.
