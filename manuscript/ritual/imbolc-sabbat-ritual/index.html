Imbolc

The Mid-Winter Festival of Lights marking the quickening of the Earth, the flowing of ewe's milk, and the transfer of the Infant God from the Crone to the Mother. A purification sabbat focused on sweeping out the old, cleansing tools and space, and reaffirming one's oath to the Lady and the Lord.

Quantities

  • Altar Setup
  • 3 altar candles: white or pale blue
  • Incense: benzoin, vanilla, or similar
  • Besom (may be decorated with white or light blue ribbons)
  • White votive candle in cauldron
  • Dish of rosemary and bay to burn
  • Bell (optional)
    Decorations
  • White flowers for altar/circle decoration
  • Green wreath for altar (optional)
  • White taper candles (optional)
    Cakes & Wine
  • Dairy-based foods, bread puddings, creamy soups
  • Alliums: onions, leeks, shallots, garlic
  • Olives, foods with raisins
  • White wine (may be spiced), milk, or soy milk

Directions

  1. Cast circle as in the Basic Circle Casting.
  2. When the ritual work portion begins, clap or ring a bell three times:
  3. This is the Mid-Winter Festival of Lights.
  4. Spring lies within sight, the Earth quickens, the milk of ewes flows, and the seed is prepared for sowing.
  5. Now does Grandmother Crone place the Infant God of Light in the arms of the Mother, relinquishing Her hold on the Child of the New Year, that the seasons begin to turn once more.
  6. Her time of midwifery is past, and the Crone departs to reunite with the Holly King in the Land of Snow and Ice.
  7. Clap or ring bell five times.
  8. Pick up the besom and hold it in both hands before the altar:
  9. With my besom in my hand I will sweep out that which is no longer needed so as to purify my surroundings, preparing the way for new growth.
  10. Take the besom and sweep the circle deosil in outward motions:
  11. Clear out the old and let the new enter.
  12. Life starts anew at this time of cleansing.
  13. Place the besom on the altar.
  14. Light the cauldron candle and drop rosemary and bay into the flame:
  15. I call upon the power of these herbs that their scent now released in this cauldron's fire purify me, my surroundings, and the tools of my Craft.
  16. With this rite I am reaffirmed in my Craft and made ready for the renewal of life in the coming of Spring.
  17. Wave the brush end of the besom over the cauldron:
  18. May this besom be cleansed that nothing cast out of the Circle in the coming year return with it or cling to it.
  19. So Mote It Be!
  20. Place the besom next to the altar.
  21. Pass other tools of the Craft through the incense smoke.
  22. Raise opened arms:
  23. As I have purified all within this Circle, I am now ready to Renew my oath to my Lady and my Lord.
  24. Proceed to the Re-Affirmation of Oath, Initiation, or Dedication ritual if desired.
  25. Continue to the Cakes & Wine ceremony of the circle casting.
