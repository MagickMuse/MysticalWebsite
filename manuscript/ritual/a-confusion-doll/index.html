👤A Confusion Doll

A poppet working designed to confuse and disorient a perpetual enemy whom you cannot completely cut ties with, such as a difficult family member. The doll can be activated at will to send confusion when needed, making it ideal for ongoing situations where you need periodic relief from psychic attacks or harassment.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • 2 pieces of wood (for cross-shaped frame)
  • String or cord (to tie the wooden cross)
  • Red cloth OR target's clothing (for body)
  • Spanish moss (for stuffing)
  • Twitch grass/couch grass (for stuffing)
  • Clay OR existing doll's head
  • Poppy seeds (to work into clay head or stuff doll head)
  • Black mustard seeds (to work into clay head or stuff doll head)
  • Personal link to target (hair, photo, name paper, or item of clothing)
  • Confusion Incense (store-bought or homemade from poppy, twitch grass, black mustard)
  • Charcoal or incense burner

Directions

  1. Obtain the best personal link you can to the target; ideally a piece of their clothing, hair, photo, or handwriting.
  2. Tie two pieces of wood together in a cross shape to create the skeleton of the poppet.
  3. If you have the target's clothing, wrap it around the wooden cross frame.
  4. If using red cloth instead, wrap Spanish moss and twitch grass together around the wooden frame, then wrap the red cloth around the outside to contain it.
  5. Secure the wrapping with thread or cord.
  6. For the head, if using clay, work poppy seeds and black mustard seeds into the clay while forming the head and attach it to the top of the cross.
  7. If using an existing doll's head, stuff it with poppy seeds and black mustard seeds and attach it to the top of the cross.
  8. Hold the completed doll in your right hand.
  9. Trace a cross in the air over the doll with your right hand.
  10. As you draw the vertical line, say: "I name thee [target's full name]." As you draw the horizontal line, say: "Thou art [target's full name]."
  11. Light Confusion Incense on charcoal.
  12. Transfer the doll to your left hand and hold it over the rising smoke.
  13. Bring to mind all the harm the target has caused you.
  14. Feel righteous anger and the desire for justice.
  15. Allow these feelings to flow from your body into the poppet.
  16. Speak the incantation:
  17. Inimicus Carpo!
    Fazed and flustered
    Vexed and addled
    Lost in the smoke of delusion
    I hold thee firm
    Bewildered and bound
    Cast into confusion
    By word and will addressed to thee
    Confusion to [target's name] be!
  18. Store the poppet in a safe place wrapped in red cloth.
  19. When you need to activate the confusion again, repeat the incense fumigation and incantation.
  20. The doll can be used repeatedly as long as the problematic relationship continues.
