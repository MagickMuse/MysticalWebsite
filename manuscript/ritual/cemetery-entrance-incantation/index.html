The Incantation

Oh, Oya, I call on You! Please lift Your head
And grant me swift entrance, Oh Queen of the Dead,
Into Your kingdom. I've brought You a present,
Nine pennies in red wine, which I think You'll find pleasant.

I beg of You earnestly, grant me admission;
Let me enter Your realm without terms or condition!

Entering the Cemetery

Once Oya grants permission, enter the cemetery and uncap the bottle. Protocol matters here: the pennies must jingle together and make noise. You are not only presenting a gift to Oya; you are also alerting the dead that they have company. You want them to hear you. Drop the wine-soaked pennies into the nearest urn or vase, along a plot curb, or onto the pathway.

Exiting the Cemetery

When you have finished your work, thank Oya and Her subjects for their time and for sharing their home with you. You may exit by backing out of the cemetery, by following the departure protocol described in the Graveyard Dirt collection guide (leaving by a different route, turning three times at the gate), or both. Doing both is recommended.

Timing

For benevolent workings, graveyard dirt should be collected within the hour before midnight. For malevolent workings, collect within the hour after midnight. If your cemetery locks its gates at a set time, go when they are open.
