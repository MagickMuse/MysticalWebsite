⚠️ Closed Practice: New Orleans Voodoo. Michael, the angel with the flaming, revolving sword who guards the gates of Eden, is posted at the doors of the home with his sword at the ready for ever vigilant protection.

Quantities

2 images of Archangel Michael holding his sword (1 if the home has only one entrance)
2 small bags of Grains of Paradise (1 if the home has only one entrance)

Directions

  1. Fill small bags with Grains of Paradise.
  2. Attach a bag to the back of each image of Michael. Be sure every image shows him with his sword.
  3. Post one image at the front door and the other at the back door so the warrior angel stands guard.
  4. If the home has only one entrance, use a single image and a single bag.

See also Sacred Image Law Keep Away Spell, a related door working aimed at keeping the law away.
