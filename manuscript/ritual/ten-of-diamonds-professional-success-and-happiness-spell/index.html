A playing card spell using the ten of diamonds (a card of professional success) as a focal point. The card is anointed with Crown of Success and Magnet Oil, surrounded by money, and used as a base for a dressed green candle. Once burned, all remnants are gathered into a red mojo hand to be carried or kept safe.

⚠️ Ten of Diamonds Professional Success and Happiness Spell is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Ten of diamonds (from a new deck)
Crown of Success Oil
Magnet Oil
Coins, paper money, and/or spirit money (handful)
Green candle
Dish
Red mojo hand (conjure bag)

Directions

  1. Remove the ten of diamonds from a new deck of playing cards and lay it on a dish.
  2. Sprinkle the card with Crown of Success Oil and Magnet Oil.
  3. Add a handful of coins and/or paper money and/or spirit money.
  4. Carve a green candle with your identifying information and dress it with Crown of Success Oil and Magnet Oil.
  5. Place the candle atop the card and burn it.
  6. Once the candle has completely burned, gather up the ashes, coins, whatever remains of the playing card, and any fortuitous-looking wax remnants.
  7. Place them all in a red mojo hand.
  8. Carry with you or keep in a safe place.
