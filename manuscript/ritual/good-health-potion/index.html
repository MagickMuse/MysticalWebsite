🍵Good Health Potion

To help your body maintain its good health. Five variations are provided, all stirred with the ankh symbol.

Quantities

Yerba mate tea and a slice of dried apple (Version 1)
Ginger tea, rose hip tea, and a slice of dried apple (Version 2)
Green tea and peppermint tea (Version 3)
Green tea and ginseng tea (Version 4)
Green tea and rose hip tea (Version 5)
Optional: green cup or mug

Directions

  1. Choose one of the five versions below and brew the tea(s).
  2. Add that version's extra ingredient, if any.
  3. Stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the shape of the ankh, the ancient Egyptian symbol of life and health, into the surface of the tea.
  4. As you drink, visualize your body strong, vital, and healthy: your immune system working perfectly, your energy levels balanced, and your physical form thriving.
  5. Eat the dried apple after the tea is finished, if using.

These tea potions support your body in maintaining good health. Five variations are provided. Choose the one that appeals to your taste or rotate through them.

Version 1: Yerba Maté with Dried Apple

Brew yerba maté tea. Add a slice of dried apple.

Version 2: Ginger and Rose Hip with Dried Apple

Brew ginger tea and rose hip tea together. Add a slice of dried apple.

Version 3: Green Tea and Peppermint

Brew green tea and peppermint tea together. The combination provides cleansing and energizing health benefits.

Version 4: Green Tea and Ginseng

Brew green tea and ginseng tea together. Ginseng is renowned for its health-promoting and longevity properties.

Version 5: Green Tea and Rose Hip

Brew green tea and rose hip tea together. Rose hips are rich in vitamin C and protective energies.

Optional Enhancement

For any of these potions, using a green cup or mug strengthens the health magic, as green is the color of healing and vitality.

Stirring Symbol

Trace the shape of the Ankh into the surface of the tea as you stir.
