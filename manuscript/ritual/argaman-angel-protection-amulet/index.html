📿ARGAMAN Angel Protection Amulet

◇Tradition
Kabbalistic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
CharmTalisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ProtectionInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Paper (2 sheets or strips) Pen or ink A locket or charm bag (optional, for the carried version)
◇Directions
1. Write the acronym ARGAMAN, standing for the angels Ariel (Uriel), Raphael, Gabriel, Michael, and Nuriel, carefully and with full attention on paper, choosing ink and paper that feel appropriate to the working. 2. Make two copies: keep one on your person during the day, and post the second on the wall near your bed so it radiates protection while you sleep. 3. Alternatively, write the acronym on a small strip of paper and carry it inside a locket or charm bag close to your body.
◇Description
An amuletic device invoking five protective angels grouped under the acronym ARGAMAN, written and carried or displayed to shield expectant mothers and newborns.
