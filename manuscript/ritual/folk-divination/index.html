🍀Folk Divination

◇Quantities
- Fresh daisies (for petal and bouquet counts) - Flowers gathered from beneath an oak tree (for dream divination) - Pillow (to tuck oak-blessed flowers under) - Access to a well or basin of water at midnight (for the Welsh washing charm)
◇Directions
1. Choose one or more of the following on or around Midsummer. 2. Daisy Petal Oracle: Pluck petals one by one while reciting: He loves me, he loves me not, he loves me, he loves me not. The final line answers the question. 3. Daisy Count for Years to Marriage: In a field with daisies, close your eyes, grasp a random handful of grass and flowers, then count how many daisies you pulled. The number equals years until marriage. 4. Oak-Flower Dreaming (Midsummer Eve): Gather flowers from beneath an oak tree. Tuck them under your pillow for the night to dream of the person you will marry. 5. Welsh Midnight Well Charm: At midnight, at a well or basin of water, wash clothes while chanting: He who would be my partner, let him come and wash with me. This invites your future lover to appear and help.
◇Description
A collection of traditional Midsummer folk divinations centered on love and future partners, using daisies, oak-blessed flowers, and a midnight well charm.
