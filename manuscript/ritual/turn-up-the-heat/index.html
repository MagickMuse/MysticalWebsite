💖Turn Up the Heat

◇Form
Writing SpellCircle Rite
◇Method
ManifestationSmoke MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Fireplace, balefire pit, barbecue grill, hibachi, or other safe fire location - Matches or lighter - 1 piece of paper (hot pink or purple preferably) - 1 pen with red ink - Cayenne pepper - Mustard seeds or dry mustard - Ginger (freshly grated or powdered)
◇Directions
1. Cast a circle around your working area. 2. Build a small fire in a safe place. 3. On the paper, write what you find enticing about your partner and what you desire from them. 4. Be as descriptive and explicit as you like, as no one but you will read what you have written. 5. When finished, draw the rune Gebo (which looks like an X) and the rune Teiwaz (which looks like an arrow pointing up) around the edges of the paper. 6. Gebo represents love and Teiwaz represents passion. 7. Place the spices on the piece of paper and fold it to make a packet that contains them. 8. Visualize you and your lover in a passionate embrace. 9. As you hold this image in your mind, toss the packet of spices into the fire. 10. As it burns, your intention is released into the universe. 11. When you feel ready, open the circle.
◇Description
A passion spell to rekindle the spark in an existing relationship. Uses fire and spices to heat up things between you and your partner, combined with rune symbols for love and passion.
