Why This Works

The cotton ball acts as a vessel and slow-release diffuser for the condition oil's magical properties. Carried on the body, it keeps the oil's energy in constant contact with you throughout the most critical moments of a legal proceeding.

This method is deliberately inconspicuous — a cotton ball in a pocket is invisible. It is also flexible: you can prepare it in the car on the way to court if nothing else was possible.

Oil Selection

  • Courtcase Oil — core legal success energy
  • Fiery Wall of Protection Oil — shields you from opposing influences
  • San Cipriano Oil — petitions Saint Cyprian's intercession

All three can be combined on a single cotton ball for a comprehensive effect.

Note

This same technique — carrying an oiled cotton ball on the body — works for any condition oil. The court case oils simply target the specific energy needed in legal situations.
