📋Reverse Unfair Tax Lien Decision

◇Form
Candle RiteWriting Spell
◇Method
Candle MagickIncense MagickSmoke MagickChant/MantraBindingKnot Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A golden-brown candle - Three frankincense or sandalwood incense sticks - A brown cord
◇Directions
1. When you wake up, light the candle and then light the first incense stick from the candle, saying three times: "I call upon the power of flame to turn my losses into gain. Let reason prevail and wisdom renew, that I recoup what I am due." Write the spell words over the cord in incense smoke, using the incense stick like a smoke pen and say the spell words as you do so. 2. Repeat the spell words and actions after lighting the next two incense sticks. 3. Tie three knots in the center of the cord, saying: "As I wind, so I bind the taxman [or name] from unfair action. So I know it must be so, the taxman [or name] will discretion show and I recoup what I'm entitled to." Blow out the candle, letting the incense burn. 4. Hang the knotted cord where air will circulate around it. 5. Perform when you receive the unfair decision or demand.
◇Description
A when you receive unfair decision or demand spell to get unfair tax lien or unjust official decision reversed, writing spell words in incense smoke over brown cord then tying three knots to bind and recoup what you are due.
