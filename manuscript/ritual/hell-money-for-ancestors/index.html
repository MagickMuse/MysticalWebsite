Chinese tradition of burning paper money for ancestors and spirits, or using it in attraction magick with red bag and magnet.

Quantities

  • Hell money (paper money for the dead)
    For attraction variant:
  • Red drawstring bag
  • Magnet
  • Small note

Directions

  1. Ancestral Offering Variant:
    Burn hell money in big bundles (inflation is ridiculous in the land of the dead). The money goes to the afterlife so departed relatives can participate in the underworld economy.
  2. Perform as reciprocation to grandparents or dead relatives who made sacrifices or enacted kindnesses while alive.
  3. Hell money can also pay for support from elemental spirits as a tip after they complete errands.
  4. Attraction Variant:
    Get a red drawstring bag, a magnet (refrigerator magnet works), and a small note stating what you want to attract.
  5. Carry the bag with you until what you want is part of your life.
  6. Once you obtain your desire, the spell is complete.
  7. Keep the bag and magnet.
  8. Burn the hell money and the note.
  9. When a new need comes, add new hell money and a note, and carry the bag again.
