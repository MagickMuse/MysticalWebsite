Ritual Grimoire

🔮Ravens and Swans Together

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
3 candles 12 or more white stones 12 or more black stones
◇Directions
1. Sit at a table in a darkened, quiet room and light the three candles, invoking earth, sea, and sky. 2. Set the white stones to one side and gather the black stones in front of you. 3. Hold your hands over the black stones, feeling the energetic connection between you, and bring to mind your worries, confusion, and mental cacophony. 4. Begin moving the black stones around the table while reciting: 5. There, through the broken branches, go The ravens of unresting thought... 6. Continue reciting as you stir and swirl the black stones, letting yourself fall into a trance if it happens; the stones are the ravens trying to get their messages through. 7. When ready, lift your hands from the black stones and hold them over the white stones, feeling the same energetic connection. 8. The white stones are the swans, bringing strength and quiet calm so you can hear the ravens' messages. 9. One by one, bring the white stones in among the black stones while reciting: 10. They drift upon the still water, mysterious, beautiful... 11. Continue reciting as you move the stones in any arrangement that pleases you, letting your hands move instinctively while your mind receives what has been trying to come through. 12. When you feel complete, take several deep breaths and give thanks to Brigid and her allies. 13. Extinguish the candles or let them burn out on their own, then cleanse the stones and store them respectfully until needed again.
◇Description
A tactile grounding meditation with black and white stones that quiets unresting thoughts and invites Brigid's clarity.
