Lughnasadh

A Lughnasadh reflection to honor the season’s bounty and acknowledge the people, events, and blessings that enrich your life.

Quantities

  • Quiet outdoor spot if possible
  • Optional: journal or sketchbook

Directions

  1. Settle in a quiet place, ideally outdoors.
  2. Close your eyes and let thoughts and emotions move through you without grasping.
  3. When your mind feels as clear as it’s likely to get, begin:
  4. - Envision yourself surrounded by a field of ripened fruits, vegetables, or grain. Notice how it feels to be amidst such abundance.
  5. Look toward the field’s edges. Invite images of people, moments, and circumstances you’re grateful for to appear.
  6. If you have messages to share, project gratitude into the scene. You may imagine offering symbolic gifts to each person or situation that arises.
  7. Gently open your eyes, breathe slowly, and return.
  8. Journal or paint what you experienced and any insights that surfaced.
