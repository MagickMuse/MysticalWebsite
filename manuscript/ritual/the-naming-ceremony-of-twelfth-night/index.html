A coming-of-age ceremony for a child moving into adulthood, traditionally performed on January 6 (Twelfth Night). The child is blindfolded and led through experiences of the four Elementals, learns the cycle of the Wheel of the Year, receives the Fivefold Blessing, and chooses their Craft Name. May be followed by the Initiation Ritual if desired.

Quantities

  • 1 adult male and 1 adult female as officiators (usually the parents)
  • Blindfold for child
  • Gifts for the child (hidden under the Altar)
  • Fresh bread
  • Cooked meat
  • Feather fan
  • 1 white candle and matches
  • Anointing oil
    For outdoor ceremony: leafy tree branch, natural water source
    For indoor ceremony: leafy tree branch, sink or tub of running water

Directions

  1. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting, preferably on January 6 (Twelfth Night). Child may be twelve to thirteen years old, or have received their first menses.
    Note: This is usually a family ritual with parents as officiators. If doing a public rite, at least one parent stands with the child outside the Circle during casting. May be followed with the Initiation Ritual if desired.
    ---
    ### Entering the Circle
  2. Child stands outside of Circle during casting. One parent (acting as Officiator) brings the child to the doorway:
    This is my child, [N], who is moving into adulthood and seeks entrance into the Circle to stand before the Lady and the Lord.
  3. Officiator within the Circle:
    Do you come of your own free will to the Circle?
    Child: I do.
  4. Officiator inside the Circle:
    Are you willing to follow the Path of Initiation?
    Child: I do.
  5. Officiator inside Circle:
    Let this child enter before the Lady and the Lord.
  6. Child is brought through the doorway into the Circle and the doorway is closed.
  7. Child is anointed with oil in the Lunar Spiral/Solar Cross sigil:
    I consecrate you in the names of the Goddess and the God. With Perfect Love and Perfect Trust, you are welcomed. Merry Meet.
  8. Circle Casting (and Esbat Ritual, if using) continues to the designated ritual work point.
    ---
    ### The Wheel of the Year
    A man and woman (may be the parents) review the life cycle of the Lord and Lady:
    Woman: The Lord is born at Yuletide with the returning of the Sun. The Lady and all the Earth rejoices at His return.
    Man: The Lady rests at Imbolc and prepares for the coming of the Spring. Her son grows strong and plays the games of childhood.
    Woman: Together They walk the fields and woods at Ostara, and all Nature awakens as They pass.
    Man: With Beltane They sport and play, and all the land and creatures of Nature rejoice with the life and vigor of fertility.
    Woman: At Litha They wed and are One and the promise of the harvests gives hope of sustenance to Their children of the Earth.
    Man: The Lord enriches the grain and gives His life for the creatures of the Earth at Lughnassadh, yet the Promise of Rebirth already lies within the Lady.
    Woman: With Mabon, the Lord and Lady give us of Their bounty and the spirit of the Lord fills the fruit of the vine and the barleycorn. The Lord moves into His Dark Realm where He offers peace and rest to those whose life's cycle is ended.
    Man: In Samhain the veil between the worlds is thinnest, and the Lady stands by the Lord in the Land of Shadows. Through His passage does He change the Tomb of the Crone to the Womb of the Mother, making Hallows all the worlds and realms at the moment of transformation. The Lady holds the Promise within Her and we bide the turning of the Wheel with Her.
    Woman: At Yule the cycle is renewed and the Lord returns with the Sun. The Wheel of the Seasons is the Wheel of Life, and as the one turns, so turns the other. You are in the Turning of the Wheel.
    ---
    ### The Fivefold Blessing
    Both parents/adults (one recites, the other anoints with the Solar Cross sigil at each place):
    Blessed Be thy feet, that take you on your path.
    Blessed Be thy knees, that support you before the Divine.
    Blessed Be thy abdomen, that gives you inner strength.
    Blessed Be thy breast, that holds your heart true to the Lady and the Lord.
    Blessed Be thy lips, that speak the Sacred Names.
    Blessed Be thy eyes, that see the beauty of Their love.
    Blessed Be thy mind, that seeks Their knowledge and Their wisdom. [Anoint at Third Eye.]
    Officiator to child: Are you willing to continue with the Ceremony in Perfect Love and Perfect Trust?
    Child: I am.
    Officiator to parent: Then blindfold your child that they may see anew.
    ---
    ### Journey Through the Elementals
  9. Child is blindfolded and a doorway is cut in the Circle for all to exit, then the door is closed and sealed with a Pentagram.
  10. Child is led through surroundings that demonstrate the four Elementals, the Goddess, and the God. One person leads while another recites, then swap roles as needed.
    Woman (Earth): I am the Earth Mother from whose body you were formed. Know me in the feel of the ground beneath your feet, in the touch of the trees against your hand. [Guide the child's hand to touch a tree, or brush with leafy twig.]
    Man (Earth): I am the Lord of the Wildwood and the Grain whose life fills the wilderness and also gives you bread. Know me in the animals I protect and in the grain you eat, for all life consumes life, but with respect to the spirit. [Give the child a taste of bread and bite of cooked meat, or bread and an animal to pet.]
    Man (Air): I am the God of the Sky whose breath gives you life. Know me when you inhale my essence. [Fan the child's face.]
    Man (Fire): I am the Lord of the Dance, know me in the fire of the Sun and in the energy that moves you through your life. [Hold a candle flame close to the child, or take by both hands in a merry, energetic circular romp, or move the child into direct sunlight.]
    Woman (Water): I am the Goddess whose water of life flows through your body. Listen to my voice in the moving currents. [Move child to where there is water to hear: garden hose, sprinkler, natural water, etc.]
    Woman (Spirit): I am the Goddess who is from the beginning of time and will be to the end of time. My power and my love brings forth the fruitfulness of the Earth and all that are born thereof. [Blow gently on the child's face.]
    Man asks child: What name have you chosen for the Circle?
    ---
    ### Naming & Closing
  11. Child states chosen Craft Name. Blindfold is removed. All return to Circle, opening, closing, and sealing the doorway.
  12. Officiator in Circle:
    We are blessed by the Goddess and the God, known to us as [Names], by Their attendance at this Naming Ceremony. Know, Lady and Lord, that [Given Name] is your child [Craft Name], and receive them into Your guidance!
  13. Child is kissed/embraced by all in the Circle and given gifts of tools (athame, wand, etc.) and/or tokens of the Craft (shell, antlers, statues, etc.).
  14. Child may now take part in ritual readings, or be guided in or perform the Initiation Ritual.
    Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
