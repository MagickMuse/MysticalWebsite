🛄Happy Home Charm Bag

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
1 bay laurel leaf per family member 1 garlic clove per family member Salt, 1 pinch Cinquefoil (five-finger grass), 1 pinch 1 iron nail Van Van Oil, Protection Oil, and/or Saint Martha the Dominator Oil (for dressing)
◇Directions
1. Gather one bay laurel leaf and one garlic clove for each member of the family. 2. Place them in a charm bag and add a pinch of salt, a pinch of cinquefoil, and an iron nail. 3. Dress the bag with Van Van Oil, Protection Oil, and/or Saint Martha the Dominator Oil. 4. Hang in a discreet place within the home. 5. Anoint with fresh oil once a week to maintain the working.
◇Description
A Hoodoo-influenced household charm bag made with bay laurel and garlic scaled to the family's size, plus salt, cinquefoil, and an iron nail. Dressed weekly with Van Van, Protection, and/or Saint Martha the Dominator Oil and hung discreetly in the home. ⚠️ This is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick traditions. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.
