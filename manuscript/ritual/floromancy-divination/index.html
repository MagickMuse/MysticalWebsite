Using flowers in divination. This version involves finding the first spring flower and noting the day of week for prediction: Sunday = very good luck for weeks, Monday = good luck all spring, Tuesday = success in all attempts, Wednesday = marriage coming, Thursday = financial troubles warning, Friday = wealth, Saturday = bad luck. Must look daily to be accurate.

Quantities

  • Daily observation of natural surroundings
  • Awareness of day of week

Directions

  1. Using flowers in divination is called floromancy.
  2. While there are several different types of floromancy, not all of them have specific names, such as this version below.
  3. Not everyone will be able to do this divination, it depends on where you live and what the local vegetation and flora is like.
  4. It's easy enough to do, however, and may be more likely to happen if you are a person who takes a lot of walks.
  5. All you have to do is look for the first spring flower and know the day of the week.
  6. If you find it on Sunday, you will have very good luck for the next several weeks.
  7. If you find it on Monday, you will have good luck throughout the spring season.
  8. If you find it on Tuesday, whatever you attempt, you will succeed.
  9. If you find it on Wednesday, a marriage is coming (doesn't necessarily mean yours).
  10. If you find it on Thursday, this is a warning of financial troubles.
  11. If you find it on Friday, this predicts wealth.
  12. If you find it on Saturday, bad luck.
  13. In order to do this divination correctly you really must look for flowers every day.
  14. You can't just go out on Fridays looking for flowers and then say you're going to be wealthy.
  15. It doesn't work like that.
