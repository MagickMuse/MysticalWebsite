🥎Gourd Milk

◇Form
PotionBath
◇Method
ManifestationElemental
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• 1 gourd • Milk
◇Directions
1. Pierce or carve a hole in the gourd large enough to pour through. 2. Fill the gourd with milk. 3. Bring the milk to a boil inside the gourd. Use a double boiler arrangement if needed to prevent scorching. 4. Allow the milk to cool. 5. Drink the cooled milk, or use it to bathe the body, abdomen, or vulva.
◇Description
Here the magick is in the vessel as much as the contents: milk boiled inside a hollowed gourd absorbs the gourd's fertility energy before being drunk or applied to the body. The gourd itself, a fertility symbol across many traditions, transforms the milk into a charged potion. One of the Fertility Potion methods.
