Ritual Grimoire

🌿Tend the Magick

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Prayer
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Materials vary: milk, honey, whiskey, cornmeal, or tobacco for offerings - Stones for cairns - Garden supplies - Bird bath - Wind chimes - Prisms
◇Directions
1. Nature exudes its own magick. 2. When we tend to it, we begin to recognize magick within ourselves. 3. Set the intention to tend to the natural world and feed the forces you cannot see: 4. - Put out offerings for the elementals. 5. These ephemeral energies live alongside us and play in the natural places (some people call them fairies). 6. In Ireland, traditional offerings were milk, honey, and whiskey. 7. In the Americas, native peoples offered cornmeal and tobacco. 8. Other offerings can include a song, a prayer, or Reiki. 9. - Build a cairn in your yard. 10. These stacks of stones are a way to pause and honor the natural world. 11. They're a prayer made physical. 12. - Plant a garden. 13. - Bring home a potted plant. 14. - Hang wind chimes. 15. - Keep a bird bath in your garden, yard, or on your deck. 16. - Put prisms and mirrors in the garden. 17. - Sing to the birds. 18. - Build a bee hotel for the carpenter bees. 19. Whatever you choose to do, do it in the spirit of reverence and intentional connection.
◇Description
A nature-tending practice inspired by Damselfly's connection to interstitial spaces where magick lives. By offering reverence to the natural world through various acts, offerings, cairns, gardens, bird baths, song, you recognize magick within yourself and feed the unseen forces.
