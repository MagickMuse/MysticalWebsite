A holed stone empowered with rosemary and recharged at each New Moon. Rosemary's purifying and strengthening properties concentrate in the stone's natural perforation, amplifying the charm's fertility power over time.

Quantities

  • A holed stone
  • Rosemary (dried or fresh)
  • A medicine bag or cord for wearing

Directions

  1. Sprinkle rosemary over the holed stone, working some into the hole itself.
  2. Carry the stone in a medicine bag or wear it around your neck.
  3. Repeat the sprinkling ritual at each New Moon to renew the charge.
