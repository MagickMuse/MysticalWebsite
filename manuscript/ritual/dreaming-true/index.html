🔮Dreaming True

◇Quantities
Lavender oil Rosemary oil Peppermint oil Thyme oil Powdered poppy seed Camphor (equal parts) Powdered aloeswood (equal parts) Jasmine (equal parts) Pulverized cucumber seeds (equal parts) Powdered white sandalwood (equal parts) Wormwood herb 1 piece plain white paper Pen and ink of art
◇Directions
1. Wait until the moon is waxing and approaching full. 2. Combine the oils of lavender, rosemary, peppermint, and thyme with the powdered poppy seed to form the bath mixture. 3. Draw a warm bath and add a few drops of the mixture to the water. 4. While bathing, fumigate the bedroom with an incense blend of camphor, aloeswood, jasmine, cucumber seeds, and white sandalwood in equal parts. 5. Before lying down to sleep, write your question on a piece of plain white paper using the pen and ink of art. 6. Place the paper and a small sachet of wormwood herb under the pillow. 7. Sleep. The answer to the question should present itself by morning.
◇Description
A waxing moon bath and fumigation ritual to incubate a prophetic dream and receive an answer to a specific question by morning.
