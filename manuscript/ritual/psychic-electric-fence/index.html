⚡Psychic Electric Fence

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Energy WorkVisualizationProtection
◇Category
Energetic Rite
◇Quantities
- A gold candle
◇Directions
1. Vigorously shake your fingertips on both hands until you can feel energy flowing. 2. Hold your hands about six inches (15cm) apart, fingers spread wide open, and palms facing each other. 3. Keep bringing your hands together so that they nearly touch, move them apart, then move them back together, continuing this for five or six cycles, faster and faster. 4. As you move your hands, picture the light becoming brighter and clearer, and creating sparks or rays. 5. Shake these sparks from your left hand so that they move clockwise upward, until they join with your right hand, and continue traveling downward around your body to join with those rising from your left hand in a sealed sphere. 6. Picture the sparks as a golden zigzag in yellows and orange, not in any way harmful, but firmly repelling any negativity. 7. When in a hazardous situation or with a nasty person, shake your hands subtly to activate the shield. 8. Perform Sunday morning.
◇Description
A Sunday morning spell to create psychic electric fence to stop potential attacker physical or psychological in his tracks, shaking fingertips and moving hands together and apart creating sparks that travel clockwise upward from left hand to right hand downward in sealed golden zigzag sphere firmly repelling any negativity.
