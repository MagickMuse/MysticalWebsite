🍈Oshun's Sweet Melon Altar

◇Directions
1. Acquire a cantaloupe or honeydew melon solely for this purpose, not one already in your kitchen. 2. Set it on Oshun's altar and state your petition to her aloud. 3. Write your request on a slip of brown paper. 4. Use a knife to cut a slit in the melon and slide the paper inside. 5. Wrap the entire melon in yellow cloth. 6. For five days, keep it on the altar flanked by continuously burning candles in gold, yellow, orange, or white, and return each day to pray and reiterate your petition. 7. On the fifth day, carry the melon to a river and offer it to Oshun's waters.
◇Description
A five-day fertility petition to Oshun using a cantaloupe or honeydew melon as a ritual vessel. A written request is slipped inside the melon, which is then wrapped in yellow cloth and placed on Oshun's altar, surrounded by burning candles in her colors, for five consecutive days before being offered to a river.

Oshun's domain extends from sweet rivers to honey jars, and the melon — sweet, golden-fleshed, heavy with seed — sits squarely in her symbolic vocabulary. Like the pumpkin, the melon's interior is a womb-like chamber, and inserting a petition into it transforms the fruit into a sealed vessel carrying your desire directly into her hands.

Brown paper has a long tradition in folk petition work as a neutral, unbleached surface — raw rather than manufactured, closer to the earth. Writing on it and sliding it into the melon seals the request within Oshun's fruit itself.

The yellow wrapping mirrors her solar colors: gold, amber, warmth. The five days correspond to her sacred number, and each day of prayer reinforces and deepens the working. The river disposal closes the working by returning the offering to her element, allowing her to receive and act upon it.
