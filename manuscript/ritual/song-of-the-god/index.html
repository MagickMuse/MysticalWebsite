🕯️Song of the God

◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Method
InvocationDevotional Work
◇Category
Invocation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. Speak this invocation to honor the God, particularly at Solstices or Sabbats celebrating his cycle. 2. The text is written in the God's own voice: 3. I am the radiant king of the skies, flooding the earth with my warmth; awakening the seeds of life within the High Fruitful Mother that all her creation be manifest in new birth. 4. I send forth my shining rays to bring light to all beings without distinction, and daily turn my golden face upon my beloved Earth, rousing those who have slumbered and sending others to their rest. 5. I bring nourishment to nature and the soul. 6. I am the Oak King upon the Earth; the Greenman, wild and free; I run with the stag, swim with the salmon, soar with the hawk, and dance with the crane. 7. The ancient woods and wild places are my familiar haunts, filled with my power, hallowed sanctuaries of my fertile life essence. 8. I am the sacrifice; my body the grain harvested that my children may eat and live through me; my spirit the fermented beverage drunk in cleansing revelry and solemn remembrance of me. 9. Behold, the mystery I bring! 10. For new seed is born of the harvest, as Spring is born of the Winter. 11. As life feeds on life, all life feeds on me, for I am the nourishment of all. 12. I am the Father, and the Son of the Mother, the Begetter and Begotten who dances creation. 13. By many names am I known, yet all are the same. 14. My emblem is the antlered stag in the wild; the sheaf of harvested grain; the filled cup distilled with my essence. 15. And through all, my golden rays light the cycles of life that are part of my path and my holy rite. 16. Born at the Solstice of Winter, I turn my face closer to Earth until the Solstice of Summer. 17. As Holly King my light fades, and the knowledge I share is of the swiftly shortening days and the cycle of passage. 18. I am the Lord who rules from the Shadow; my emblem the Dark Sun, my realm that of repose. 19. In me ye will find life, peace, and joy in thy passing, for my blessings are poured out to all, withheld from none. 20. I give thee peace and rest in my realm until ye choose to return to my sunlit Earth.
◇Description
A devotional invocation spoken in the voice of the God in his solar and earthly aspects. Celebrates the radiant Sun King, the Oak King and Greenman of wild places, the sacrificed grain God, and the Holly King who rules the waning year. Honors the cycle from Winter Solstice birth through Summer Solstice peak to the Dark Sun of shadow and repose.
