🌗Waxing Half Moon Hymn to Hekahermes

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Black Candle: 1 White Candle: 1 Bowl: 1, filled with water mixed with olive oil Olive Oil: mixed into the bowl of water Water: mixed with olive oil in a bowl Barley: a cup, mixed with lentils Lentil: mixed with barley in a cup Garlic: 1 clove, pierced by a nail Nail: 1, used to pierce the garlic clove
◇Directions
1. Purify yourself before beginning. 2. Light one black candle and one white candle. 3. Between the candles, set: a bowl of water mixed with olive oil, a cup of barley mixed with lentils, and a garlic clove pierced by a nail. 4. At dawn or dusk, in sight of the waxing half-full moon, recite the Hymn to Hekahermes three times in full. 5. Hymn: "O husbandless, wifeless, strifeless Hekate! / O luster of the bronze sun and silver moon. / O childless womb that yet engenders and sustains. / O seedless phallus that penetrates our knowledge / Of nothing; that reigns in absence. / AZOSTOS! AZOSTOS! O unconstrained / By the bonds of flesh; everchanging / God that cannot be bent into a shape / By chains of gender and of sex. / O Hekahermes, O Hermekate, / O Fire unchecked! O / Unreckoned / Ambiguity. / Unruly Salamander / Born of flame, encumbered / By no mortal mate: having no equal, unsequeled; / Who yet perpetuates. O Parthenogenic one who woos / And preys upon her mates. O unabated Mistress/Master / Who evades the tongue's attempts to capture your / Uncapturable self. It cannot be helped: from your core / Primeval rays reach in every direction equally, always / And ever: and never can one blade of flame be bent / Beyond its eternal trajectory. So how could you / Concede the need to rut, with the concessions / That adhere? O God/Goddess of Force / And Voice and Fear; O Slayer/Savior; / O Blesser/Breaker; Raiser/Wrecker; / Murderer/Maker; Releaser/Netter; / Parasite/Host; Male/Female; / Flesh/Ghost." 6. Thank Hekate in your own words for the gift of herself. Speak of how her liminality and amorphousness have allowed you to apprehend and celebrate the liminal aspects of your own outer and subtle selves. 7. Speak Hermes' name three times. In your own words, ask him to enter into you, to be with you henceforth, and to hereafter grant you luck, joy, wisdom, and success. 8. If there is a personal impasse, especially concerning identity, gender, sexuality, or relationships, ask Hekahermes and Hermekate to fortify you with insight, endurance, and companionship for the journey.
◇Description
A solemn hymn invoking Hekate's liminal male/female nature as Hekahermes, performed at the waxing half moon to aid transition from one stage of practice to the next. The Hierophant purifies herself, sets candles and offerings, and recites the hymn thrice at dawn or dusk in sight of the half-full moon. She then speaks gratitude to Hekate and invites Hermes to enter and accompany her.
