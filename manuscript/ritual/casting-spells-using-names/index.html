Casting Spells Using Names

Most magickal systems view a person's name as part of their intrinsic identity, a source of great power and vulnerability. Just as the subtle body (aura) extends beyond the physical form, a name carries an energetic signature that can be targeted in spellwork.

True Names

Knowing someone's true name grants power over them. Historically, people were given multiple names: one for public use, one for ritual use, and a secret name known only to the individual. Mothers would whisper a hidden name into their baby's ear, never to be repeated, ensuring one name always remained protected. In ancient Egypt, possessing such exceptional magick power that even one's own mother didn't know one's true name was the highest praise.

The Mother's Name

The connection between name and mother holds deep magickal significance. While patriarchal cultures identified people through the father's name, magick spells and magickal documents have always used the mother's name instead. The reasoning is practical: a mother's identity is never in question, making the mother's name the only reliable way to guarantee correct identification in a working.

The standard magickal formula for writing or calling someone's name is: "[Name], child of [their mother's name]."

Names of Power

Angels, demons, and spirits of all kinds are summoned by calling their true name. They supposedly cannot resist the call, and much summoning magick depends upon this premise.

Correct pronunciation is essential. This knowledge was originally transmitted orally, and significant disagreement exists among historians, magickal practitioners, and scholars over how even well-known Greek and Egyptian deity names should be pronounced.

Some spell-casting traditions incorporate long series of magickal names. The Magickal Papyri contain examples of these spells, though the names lack surviving mythological context and may not have been recognized even by practitioners of that era.

When working with Names of Power, exercise caution and discernment. Rather than reciting unfamiliar names, consider using Names of Power you have a personal connection with: trusted Spirits, ancestral spirits, or living people whose assistance you value and trust.
