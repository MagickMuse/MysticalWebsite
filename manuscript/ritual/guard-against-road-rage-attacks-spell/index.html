🛡️Guard Against Road Rage Attacks Spell

A monthly spell to guard against road rage, muggings, and attacks on trains and buses, using incense smoke squares over toy vehicle covered with cup for invisibility and invulnerability.

Quantities

  • A small toy vehicle to represent your most common mode of travel in which there are potential regular hazards
  • A cup or vase large enough when inverted to cover the toy
  • A mint, lavender, frankincense, dragon's blood, or other spicy incense stick

Directions

  1. Light the incense and draw protective squares in smoke in the air around and over the vehicle, saying: "In times of danger, give me invisibility, shield me with invulnerability, that I may be unnoticed and unnoticeable until I reach safety." Cover the vehicle with the cup or vase and repeat the words, passing the incense smoke, again in squares, around the inverted vase or cup.
  2. Let the incense burn and leave the toy inside the inverted vase or cup.
  3. Say the spell words whenever you feel afraid.
  4. Perform monthly or before a trip alone that worries you.
