🪢Thumbelina Walnut Shell Fidelity Charm

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingKnot Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Adam and Eve Root (or wax miniature figures as substitute) Come to Me Lover! Oil or Amor Oil Walnut (two halves, hollowed) Spider web Red candle wax (Optional: used sock)
◇Directions
1. Anoint an Adam and Eve Root with a drop of Come to Me Lover! or Amor Oil. 2. Place it inside one hollowed walnut shell half. 3. Lay some spider web over it as a gossamer blanket. 4. Place the other walnut half on top and seal both halves together with red candle wax. 5. Secrete the charm into your lover's clothing inside a closet or drawer, it must not be discovered. 6. Alternatively, place the sealed walnut inside a used sock, tie a knot to seal your intentions, and place the sock under your mattress. 7. If Adam and Eve Root is unavailable, miniature figures crafted from wax may be substituted.
◇Description
An Adam and Eve Root anointed with love oil, sealed inside a hollowed walnut shell with spider web and red candle wax, then hidden in a lover's clothing or knotted inside a sock under the mattress to bind sexual magnetism and fidelity. Note: this charm is concealed from the target without their knowledge.
