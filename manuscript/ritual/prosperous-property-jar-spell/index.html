A coin-and-candle jar spell that lays energetic boundaries around your property while calling in wealth, abundance, and prosperity. Rooted in the folk tradition of burying pennies at the four corners of one's land, this spell builds a living prosperity talisman that grows as you add to it over time. Best performed during a waxing or Supermoon for maximum drawing power.

Quantities

  • Green or gold candle
  • Moon Oil or a prosperity oil
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Ground nutmeg
  • A jar with lid (large enough to hold the candle upright)
  • 5 pennies
  • 1 nickel, 1 dime, 1 quarter
  • 1 dollar bill
  • Matches or a lighter
  • Optional: cinnamon sticks, bay leaves, pyrite, or green aventurine

Directions

  1. Dress the candle by anointing it with Moon Oil or prosperity oil.
  2. On a plate, mix a small amount of ground cinnamon and nutmeg together, then roll the candle toward yourself through the blend to coat it lightly with the herbs.
  3. Secure the candle upright in the center of the jar's base.
  4. Anoint all coins with the same oil.
  5. Place four of the pennies around the outside of the jar in a diamond or square shape, marking the four corners of your property in miniature.
  6. Sprinkle cinnamon and nutmeg around the setup if you feel called to.
  7. Hover your hands over the arrangement without touching it, tuning into the resonance of the coins, the candle, and your intention.
  8. When you feel ready, speak aloud:
  9. "As the moon ebbs and flows, my wealth, evergreen, nonstop grows."
  10. Repeat until you feel a buzz of energy building in your hands and body.
  11. Visualize abundance spreading outward from the jar: money growing around your home, opportunities ripening, blessings flowing in steadily from every direction.
  12. Holding this vision, drop the fifth penny, the nickel, the dime, and the quarter one by one into the jar around the candle.
  13. Slide the dollar bill beneath the jar.
  14. Light the candle and allow it to burn all the way down safely.
  15. Once it has finished, carry the four pennies that surrounded the jar outside and bury one at each corner of your home or property, anchoring the spell into the land.
  16. Retrieve the dollar bill and place it inside the jar.
  17. Add optional cinnamon sticks, bay leaves, or stones such as pyrite or green aventurine for additional drawing power.
  18. Seal the jar and place it somewhere you will see it regularly.
  19. As you come across spare change or small bills, add them to the jar to feed the spell.
  20. Recharge it periodically with incense or a fresh candle to keep the prosperity current flowing.
