A heart-clearing smoke and feather petition to Thoth, Egyptian god of writing, scribes, and magick, invoking his lunar inspiration before creative work or ritual. Just as Thoth weighs the heart against a feather in the afterlife, this spell uses that same imagery to cleanse what weighs on you, so your heart is light enough to receive divine inspiration and channel your art.

Quantities

  • Incense (myrrh is recommended)
  • Matches or lighter
  • A feather

Directions

  1. Light the incense.
  2. Breathe onto it gently and deliberately, feeding its embers with intention as it begins to smoke.
  3. Pass the feather through the incense smoke, then use it to fan and sweep the energy around your heart center.
  4. Use the feather like a fan, wafting away anything stagnant or heavy sitting in your chest.
  5. If you don't have a feather, use your hands; pluck at the air around your heart, pulling energy away from your body and releasing it.
  6. As you do this, say aloud:
  7. "I cleanse my heart, that I may be free and clear, to channel my art."
  8. Repeat this three times, continuing to fan or pluck as you speak.
  9. When you feel a sense of release or lightness, turn your gaze to the full moon, or if it isn't visible, close your eyes and picture it clearly in your mind.
  10. Reflect on Thoth: the self-created god, the one who spoke himself into existence, the master of words and wisdom.
  11. Breathe in the light of the moon as if it were entering your body through your chest, lifting your vibration.
  12. Place the feather gently against your forehead.
  13. Close your eyes and say aloud:
  14. "As you weigh hearts against the feather,
    To me writing inspiration do weather."
  15. Sit in stillness for a moment, receiving.
  16. When you feel ready, return to your work, keeping the feather nearby on your desk, altar, or in your hand.
  17. You may also lightly brush your pen, keyboard, or other creative tools with the feather to anoint them with the energy of the working.
