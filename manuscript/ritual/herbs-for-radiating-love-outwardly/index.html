An altar herbal matrix ritual for projecting an aura of love outwardly and making space for love to enter your life.

Quantities

1 red hibiscus blossom
8 pieces dried cinnamon bark
8 sprigs rosemary
Yarrow (clusters)
Rosebuds or rose petals

Directions

  1. Place the red hibiscus blossom at the center of your altar as the focal point of the matrix.
  2. Arrange 8 sticks of dried cinnamon bark in an equidistant, radiating pattern around the hibiscus; these form the primary rays of the matrix.
  3. In between each cinnamon ray, place a slender sprig of rosemary, filling in the surface area and expanding the matrix outward.
  4. Around the outer perimeter defined by the rosemary, position clusters of yarrow, leaving deliberate gaps between clusters so love can flow inward.
  5. In the negative spaces between the cinnamon and rosemary rays, place rosebuds or rose petals, taking care not to block any channels.
  6. Sit with your completed matrix. Meditate on the beauty you have already brought into the world, and on the space you have made for love to find you.

About This Ritual

An herbal matrix is a sacred arrangement similar to a flower mandala, constructed with intention to align the energetic signatures of specific herbs with a desired invocation. This matrix is designed to radiate love outward. The rays of the arrangement send love out to the universe, while the negative spaces between them form channels through which that energy returns to you, the creator.

Before laying the matrix, clear physical space on your altar. This act mirrors the inner work of clearing emotional space in your own life to make room for love to enter.

Notes

The matrix functions as both a spell and a contemplative practice. It does not compel love from a specific person; it opens you as a vessel for love in all its forms. Use fresh flowers and herbs when possible for the strongest energetic resonance.
