🌊Nine Flowers Bath

This bath uses nine white flowers as the cleansing instrument itself, working them across the body until each one falls apart. The number nine and white flowers both carry strong purifying associations. After the bath, leave the tub uncleaned for several hours, or let someone else handle it, so you do not reabsorb what was released.

Quantities

3 white roses
3 white carnations
3 white lilies
Hot water: enough to cover the flowers in a dish

Directions

  1. Place all nine flowers in a dish and cover them with hot water.
  2. Strain the liquid into the bath, then bring both the water and the flowers to the tub.
  3. Starting from the head, use one flower at a time to scrub the body downward, continuing until the flower disintegrates.
  4. Work through all nine flowers this way.
  5. Step out of the water and let yourself air-dry.
  6. Put on clean clothes. Leave the bathtub for several hours before cleaning it, or have someone else clean it for you.
