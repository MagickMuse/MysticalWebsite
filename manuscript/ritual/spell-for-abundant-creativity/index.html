✨Spell for Abundant Creativity

◇Form
Altar WorkingDressed Candle
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualizationEnergy WorkManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Smoke, sound, or other space-cleansing method - Large firesafe dish - Small piece of paper and pen - Blue candle - Prosperity oil (or olive oil mixed with cinnamon powder) - Dried rosemary - Crystal for creativity (suggested: rutilated quartz) - Crystal for opening senses (suggested: moonstone) - Crystal for self-acceptance (suggested: rhodonite) - Crystal for protection (suggested: hematite) - Matches - Journal
◇Directions
1. Gather your items and take them to your spellwork altar or sacred space. 2. Take a few moments to ground and center. 3. Decide on your intention for this spell; consider how it feels and whether it needs adjustment, then speak it aloud to ensure it is worded correctly. 4. Place your firesafe dish on your altar space and cleanse your space with smoke, sound, or whichever method feels best. 5. Write your intention on a small piece of paper, read it aloud three times, then fold the paper toward yourself three times, saying 'And so it is' each time. 6. Place the paper under your dish. 7. Pick up the blue candle, prosperity oil, and rosemary and hold them to your third eye, saying: 'My ideas flow abundantly, and I can easily pursue each one.' Rub a small amount of prosperity oil on the candle, avoiding the wick, then sprinkle rosemary on the candle's body over the dish and place the dressed candle in the middle of the plate. 8. Hold your creativity crystal to your solar plexus chakra and say: 'I step into my creativity with confidence and positivity.' Place it behind the candle. 9. Hold the opening-of-senses crystal above your head at the crown chakra and say: 'I am open to new ideas, messages, and assistance from those who guide me from above.' Place it to the left of the candle. 10. Hold the self-acceptance crystal near your root chakra and say: 'I accept and love who I am. 11. All thoughts and feelings I have are valid and worthy.' Place it to the right of the candle. 12. Hold your protection crystal to your heart chakra and say: 'I am grounded and protected against any energy that will not serve my higher purpose, and so it is.' Place it in front of the candle. 13. Sit quietly and note any feelings, thoughts, or messages you receive. 14. Take deep cleansing breaths and repeat your intention aloud once. 15. Hold your hands over your spell setup and close your eyes, visualizing the energy from all crystals as light infusing the blue candle and then moving into your hands. 16. Light the candle and say: 'This spell has been activated. 17. I will take any actions necessary to help the energy of this spell be successful and be open to all that is sent to me. 18. And so it is, and so it is, and so it is, thank you.' Let the candle burn down. 19. Journal your experience and record any messages for your spellwork action steps.
◇Description
A crystal-focused spell to invite abundant creativity into your life. Four crystals are placed around a dressed blue candle, each assigned a specific job: creativity, opening senses, self-acceptance, and protection. The spell combines candle magick with crystal energy work to boost creative flow and confidence.
