🕯️Obeah Threshold of Time Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Candle MagickWater Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Dish of plain spring water - Candles (sufficient quantity for nine days of burning)
◇Directions
1. In the room where the death occurred or where the body was prepared for burial, set a dish of plain spring water and keep candles burning for nine consecutive days. 2. Nothing else in the room should be changed during this period. 3. Candles may be replaced as they burn down and the water must be refreshed daily, but no other alterations are made. 4. When nine days have passed, remove the water, the dish, and the candles from the home entirely. 5. Then redecorate the room: rearrange furniture, change pictures and ornaments, or bring in new pieces to fully transform the space.
◇Description
An Obeah ritual that creates a nine-day healing threshold following a death in the home. The working supports both the lingering soul and the living family, maintaining a sacred liminal space until the period is complete, after which the room is fully transformed.
