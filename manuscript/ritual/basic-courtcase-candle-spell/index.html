The foundational Hoodoo court case candle working. Brown candles are burned for justice; purple for power. Dressed with court case condition oils and burned with intention, this is the most accessible active working for legal victory.

⚠️ This is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick traditions. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.

Quantities

1–2 candles (brown and/or purple)
Condition oil(s) of choice

Directions

  1. Choose a brown candle for justice, a purple candle for power, or use both together.
  2. Hold the candle in both hands, breathe your intention into it, and press your will into the wax.
  3. Carve any relevant words, symbols, or names into the candle.
  4. Dress the candle with Courtcase Oil, Fiery Wall of Protection Oil, San Cipriano Oil, Commanding Oil, or any combination you feel called to use.
  5. Burn the candle.
🔒
Closed Practice: Court case candle spellwork is rooted in Hoodoo. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magic tradition. Approach with respect.

Candle Color Symbolism

Brown candles carry the energy of justice, stability, and the earth — appropriate when you are seeking a fair and grounded outcome.

Purple candles carry the energy of power, authority, and command — appropriate when you need to assert dominance over the situation or the people involved.

Using both together combines justice and power, which is generally the most effective approach.

Dressing the Candle

Any of the three core court case condition oils can be used alone or in combination:

  • Courtcase Oil — the primary working oil for legal success and vindication
  • Fiery Wall of Protection Oil — adds a protective layer against opposing magical influence
  • San Cipriano Oil — invokes the intercession of Saint Cyprian, patron of occult workers
  • Commanding Oil — optional supplement to add domination energy

See Courtcase Oil, Fiery Wall of Protection Oil, San Cipriano Oil, and Commanding Oil for details on each.

Enhancement

This is the base working. For a more powerful version, roll the dressed candle in powdered court case botanicals before burning — see the Basic Courtcase Spell Botanical Candle for that variation.
