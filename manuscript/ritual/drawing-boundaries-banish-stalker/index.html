A sunset spell for drawing boundaries to banish stalker, nasty neighbors, unscrupulous debt collector, or ex who harasses you, mixing salt earth and water three by three then scattering three lines to banish henceforward.

Quantities

  • A small bowl of salt
  • A small container of soil
  • A small jug of water

Directions

  1. Sprinkle nine pinches of salt on top of the soil and add nine drops of water so the soil is still crumbly, then say: "By salt, earth, and water, three by three, you [name person], can no longer reach me. By salt, earth, and water, three by three, banished henceforward shall you be." Outside your home or business, facing the sunset, scatter a straight line of the soil, salt, and water mixture in front of you.
  2. Take a step backwards in the direction of your home, making a second line, and step back again, making a third line.
  3. Stand behind the three lines, facing the fading sun, and say: "By salt, earth, and water, you can no longer reach me." Go indoors and close the door, leaving the container outside to be scattered in the morning.
  4. Perform sunset.
