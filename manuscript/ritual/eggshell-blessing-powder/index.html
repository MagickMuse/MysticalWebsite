🥚Eggshell Blessing Powder

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Powder
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickProtectionManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Eggshells - Mortar and pestle - Dried blue cornflower - Dried lavender - Dried rose petals - Dried violets (optional)
◇Directions
1. Begin by grinding your eggshells with the mortar and pestle. 2. Work slowly and with intention. 3. As you grind, reflect on the dual nature of the egg and the moon, both protective shells, both nurturing containers for new life. 4. Sprinkle in your dried flowers one by one and continue grinding until everything is blended into a fine powder. 5. As you add each flower, contemplate its energy: cornflower for peace, lavender for blessing and calm, rose for beauty and love, violet (if using) for protection and wishes. 6. At the new or full moon, take your powder outside. 7. Beginning at one point and moving clockwise, sprinkle it in a ring around the perimeter of your home, or across the soil of a nearby park or garden. 8. As you scatter it, say: 9. "Flowers, eggs, and the moons of spring, Protect this home/seedling and only blessings bring." 10. As you complete the circle, visualize the powder rising into a soft luminous shell around your home or space, an eggshell of moon energy that allows only warmth and positive intention to pass through, while holding your growing desires safely within. 11. The powder can also be used to dress a candle, line an abundance jar, or scatter in soil around seedlings or new plantings as a blessing for their growth.
◇Description
A ground blessing powder made from eggshells and dried flowers, lavender, rose, cornflower, and optional violet, mixed under the Taurus moon to nurture growth and protect space. Sprinkle clockwise around your home or in nature to create a lunar protective boundary, bless new seedling intentions, or give thanks to the earth's cycles of abundance.
