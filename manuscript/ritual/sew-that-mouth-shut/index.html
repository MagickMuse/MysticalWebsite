A conjure bag charm using ginger root and High John the Conqueror root, with a felt face sewn onto the bag and its mouth stitched shut with black thread to silence gossip. One of the Banish Gossip spells.

⚠️ Sew That Mouth Shut! is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Ginger root
High John the Conqueror root
Conjure bag
Small piece of felt
Black thread and needle

Directions

  1. Place ginger root and High John the Conqueror root in a conjure bag.
  2. Shape a face from a small piece of felt and sew it onto the bag.
  3. Stitch the mouth of the felt face shut with black thread to silence gossip.

Directions

  1. Place a ginger root and a High John the Conqueror root inside a conjure bag.
  2. Create a small face from felt and attach it to the outside of the bag. Embellish as you wish.
  3. Using black thread, sew X's across the mouth of the felt face, sealing it shut. Focus on your desire with each stitch.
  4. Carry the bag with you.
