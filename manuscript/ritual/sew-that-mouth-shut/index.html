◇Description
A conjure bag charm using ginger root and High John the Conqueror root, with a felt face sewn onto the bag and its mouth stitched shut with black thread to silence gossip. One of the Banish Gossip spells. ⚠️ Sew That Mouth Shut! is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Directions

  1. Place a ginger root and a High John the Conqueror root inside a conjure bag.
  2. Create a small face from felt and attach it to the outside of the bag. Embellish as you wish.
  3. Using black thread, sew X's across the mouth of the felt face, sealing it shut. Focus on your desire with each stitch.
  4. Carry the bag with you.
