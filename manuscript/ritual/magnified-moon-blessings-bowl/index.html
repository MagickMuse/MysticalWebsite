A supermoon attraction spell using a blessing bowl filled with sugar, a written bay leaf, lodestones, and symbolic objects to magnify your desires and draw them toward you. Cinnamon smoke seals the working, and the bowl can be refreshed over multiple days or weeks to keep the attraction energy building.

Quantities

  • Cinnamon stick
  • Bowl (silver-colored or white preferred) or cauldron
  • Sugar
  • Bay leaf
  • Sharpie or permanent marker
  • 2 pieces of lodestone
  • Object(s) representing your intention (coins for money, rose petals for love, dice for luck, etc.)
  • Matches or lighter
  • Optional: Moon Water to drink beforehand

Directions

  1. Before you begin, perform the Celestial Gravity Meditation to attune yourself to the moon's energy.
  2. If you have moon-charged water ready, drink some to deepen your connection.
  3. Cleanse your tools and arrange them before you.
  4. Take a moment to study your bowl, its roundness, its reflective quality, its kinship with the moon.
  5. Recognize it as a vessel, like an offering cup, designed to hold and bless your efforts.
  6. Hold the cinnamon stick and trace it around the outer rim of the bowl three times in a clockwise direction.
  7. As you do, say aloud:
  8. "Like the moon grows, good luck do I know.
    When I roll the dice, the moon blesses me thrice."
  9. Fill the bowl with a generous layer of sugar, enough to nestle your objects into.
  10. Sugar is a powerful attractor, drawing desire toward you.
  11. On your bay leaf, write what you wish to draw into your life.
  12. Place it in the center of the sugar with the word facing you.
  13. Now nestle your intention objects around the bowl, coins for abundance, rose petals for love, dice for luck, or whatever speaks to your working.
  14. As you press each one into the sugar, visualize your desires growing and multiplying, fed by the supermoon's magnified energy.
  15. Take both lodestones separately in your hands.
  16. Feel the natural pull between them.
  17. Think clearly on what you wish to attract.
  18. Slowly bring the two stones together as you whisper your intention, then set them together atop the bay leaf at the center of the bowl.
  19. Hold your palm over the bowl and breathe your will into it.
  20. Trace the cinnamon stick around the bowl three more times, repeating the chant.
  21. Then light one end of the cinnamon stick and blow out the flame so it smolders like incense.
  22. As the fragrant smoke rises, say:
  23. "As the supermoon magnifies my lunar call,
    With these magnetized stones, my desires to me are drawn."
  24. Allow the ember to burn out naturally, or press it into the sugar to snuff it.
  25. Keep the bowl somewhere visible.
  26. Relight the cinnamon stick daily as you focus on your intention.
  27. Refresh the bowl each week by adding new objects, if working on abundance, add a coin or bill on a Friday to symbolically multiply what you're calling in.
