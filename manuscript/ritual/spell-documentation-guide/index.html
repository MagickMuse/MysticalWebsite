Spell Documentation Guide

Keeping careful records of your spellwork serves multiple purposes. It helps you track what works best for you, identify patterns in your practice, and refine your techniques over time. A well-maintained spell journal becomes an invaluable reference as you grow in your craft.

What to Record

For each spell or ritual you perform, consider documenting the following:

Basic Information

  • Spell name or type - What kind of working is this?
  • Date and time - When did you perform it?
  • Moon phase - What was the moon doing?
  • Weather conditions - What was happening outside?
  • Location - Where did you cast?

Personal State

  • Your physical health - How were you feeling physically?
  • Your emotional state - What was your mood?
  • Your energy level - Were you well-rested or depleted?

Spell Details

  • Purpose and intention - What were you trying to accomplish?
  • Tools and ingredients used - What materials did you work with?
  • Deities or spirits invoked - Did you call upon any entities?
  • Full text of incantations - What words did you speak?
  • Duration - How long did the working take?

Results

  • Immediate reactions - What did you feel during or right after?
  • Signs and synchronicities - What meaningful coincidences appeared?
  • Outcome - Did the spell manifest? How? When?
  • Lessons learned - What would you do differently?

Why Documentation Matters

Over time, your records reveal which correspondences resonate most strongly for you, which moon phases align with your energy, and which techniques consistently produce results. This personal data is far more valuable than any book's recommendations because it reflects your unique magickal signature.

Your spell journal also helps you avoid repeating mistakes and builds confidence as you see your successes accumulate. When a spell works beautifully, you can recreate those conditions. When something falls flat, you can analyze why and adjust your approach.
