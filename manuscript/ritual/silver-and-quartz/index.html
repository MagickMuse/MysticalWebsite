🧿Silver and Quartz

◇Form
Bath
◇Method
CleansingWater Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Borrowed silver (antique coin, jewelry, or silverware) • Pure spring water (tub) • Quartz crystals (optional, to intensify)
◇Directions
1. Borrow silver from a neighbor; an antique coin, a piece of jewelry, or silverware. 2. Modern coins are not true silver. 3. There is no need to explain why you need it. 4. Place the silver inside a tub filled with pure spring water. 5. Optionally add quartz crystals to intensify the action. 6. Bathe the child in the tub. 7. Return the silver to the neighbor afterward, with a small gift.
◇Description
A child's Evil Eye removal bath using borrowed silver placed in spring water, optionally intensified with quartz crystals. The silver must be returned to the neighbor afterward, with a small gift. One of the Evil Eye Bath methods.
