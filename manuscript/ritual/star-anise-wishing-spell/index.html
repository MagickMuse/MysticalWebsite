◇Quantities

◇Directions
1. Choose a candle to represent your wish. Carve and dress it to suit your personal situation. 2. Arrange four star anise seeds around the candle, one at each of the four cardinal points. 3. Fill in the rest of the magick circle with clove buds. 4. Burn the candle.
◇Description
A candle wish spell using star anise placed at the four cardinal points and clove buds completing the magick circle. Choose and dress a candle to represent your wish, set it within the botanical circle, and burn.

About This Spell

A candle wish spell that uses star anise and clove buds to create a magic circle of botanical power around the central candle.

Items Needed

  • 1 candle chosen to represent your wish
  • Dressing oil of choice
  • Carving tool
  • 4 star anise seeds (one per cardinal point)
  • Clove buds (to fill the rest of the magic circle)
