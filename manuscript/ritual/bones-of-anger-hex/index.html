A hex invoking Kali to bring torment, anguish, and destruction upon an enemy using crushed bone dust scattered on their property.

Quantities

Chicken bones
A hammer or heavy stomping footwear
A bag for the crushed remains
Optional: a bell

Directions

  1. Dry chicken bones in sun for several days.
  2. Work into a frenzy of anger.
  3. Ring bell 3 times if available.
  4. Invoke Kali and wait for presence.
  5. Recite incantation while crushing bones with hammer or feet.
  6. Sweep up remains into bag.
  7. Scatter dust and fragments around enemy's property.

Preparation

Gather chicken bones and dry them in the sun for several days before performing the hex.

Instructions

When you are ready, work yourself into a frenzy of anger and hatred. Hold onto every wrong the target has committed. This emotional intensity is critical to the potency of the hex.

If you have a bell, ring it three times, then speak the invocation:

I call upon the Ancient Ones from the great abyss to do my bidding. I invoke Kali, Goddess of destruction, and all the creatures of the underworld, hear me now...

Wait until you feel Kali's presence, then recite the incantation. When you reach the words "With these bones I now do crush," take a hammer or use your feet to stomp and crush the bones as though they were your enemy before you.

Bones of anger, turn to dust,
Full of fury, revenge is just.
I scatter these bones, these bones of rage,
Take my enemy, bring them pain.
I see my enemy before me now,
I bind them, crush them, bring them down.
With these bones that I do crush,
Make my enemy turn to dust.
Torment, anguish, out of control,
With this hex I curse their soul.
So mote it be.

Disposal

Sweep up the crushed bone dust and remains and place them in a bag. Sprinkle the dust and fragments around your enemy's property, circling their home.
