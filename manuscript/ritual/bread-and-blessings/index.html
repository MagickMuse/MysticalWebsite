Lughnasadh

A fast Lughnasadh reset to express gratitude, invite good fortune, and re‑center. Offer bread to the four directions and pour a ring of water with a simple chant.

Quantities

  • A piece of bread
  • A glass of water
  • Optional: a compass to find the cardinal directions

Directions

  1. Pre-ritual: Shake out the body vigorously, arms, legs, head, and neck, to release stress.
  2. Locate north, east, south, and west (use a compass if needed).
  3. Rite:
  4. Carry the bread and water outdoors. Stand tall holding both. Recall hunger and thirst, and how simple food and water sustain life. Call to mind your blessings: food, water, friends, family, sun, and earth.
  5. Break the bread into four pieces. Moving clockwise, place one piece at each direction:
  6. North (Earth): Give thanks for material support, stability, harvest, soil, crops, wild vegetation, manifestation.
  7. East (Air): Give thanks for breath, change, circulation, thought, intelligence, clarity, movement.
  8. South (Fire): Give thanks for transformation, warmth, sunlight, energy, illumination, cleansing fire.
  9. West (Water): Give thanks for love, emotion, life, creativity, and the waters.
  10. Walk clockwise once more, slowly pouring a ring of water as you turn, saying:
    "I give my thanks to the powers that be!
    To these powers, connect
    the powers of me!
    This, we will it, and so it will be!"
    Repeat as you complete the circle. Reserve a final sip for yourself and drink to close.
