A famous Hoodoo dream incense formula designed to induce all kinds of dreams, with particular reputation for producing lottery numbers in sleep. Originally produced in conjunction with Aunt Sally's Policy Player's Dream Book and Wheel of Fortune (first published in the 1890s). Cardamom, cinnamon, coriander, and licorice are ground and burned on charcoal before bed. Dreams are then analyzed for symbolic meanings and embedded numbers using a dream book.

⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Use with awareness and respect for its cultural origins.

Quantities

  • Cardamom
  • Cinnamon
  • Coriander
  • Licorice
    Grind all in equal or intuitive proportions.

Directions

  1. Grind all ingredients together into a fine powder.
  2. Sprinkle on a lit charcoal disc and burn completely in the bedroom before bedtime, following the standard dream incense method.
  3. When you awake, record your dreams immediately.
  4. Pay close attention to any numbers in your dreams; count objects if necessary, or use a dream book to interpret symbols and derive number correspondences.
