10⚫Black⚪White

A evening before confrontation spell for black and white ritual to get your point across once and for all to stubborn colleague, boss, family member or friend, tossing ten black round stones and ten white pointed stones in sieve saying black and white clear as day you cannot have your way then removing black stones leaving on way to work carrying white ones in bag touching before making statement.

Quantities

  • Ten small round black stones
  • Ten small pointed white stones
  • A sieve or a colander
  • A drawstring bag or purse

Directions

  1. Toss all the stones in the sieve, saying: "Black and white, clear as day, you simply cannot have your way." Remove the black stones from the sieve, saying: "Black and white, I am right. Just you, [name], listen to what I say. I'll get through to you, come what may." Leave the black stones on the way to work or outside the house and carry the white ones in a bag or purse, touching them before making your statement.
  2. Perform the evening before the confrontation.
