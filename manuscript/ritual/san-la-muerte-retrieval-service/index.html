🔮San La Muerte Retrieval Service

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
DivinationDevotional Work
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- 1 San La Muerte amulet - Cord or string - 1 red or black candle (for thanks)
◇Directions
1. Hang a San La Muerte amulet upside down from a cord like a pendulum. 2. Twist the cord tightly and then release it. 3. Once he stops spinning, San La Muerte will allegedly face the direction where the missing object is located. 4. When the item is recovered, burn an upside-down red or black candle in thanks and respect. 5. It is believed that if the item isn't returned, the thief will die.
◇Description
A divination technique using a San La Muerte amulet as a pendulum to locate lost or stolen objects.
