A ritual to reshape and repair the aura after magickal attack or healing work that leaves energetic holes. By invoking the six directions and lying in their magnetic field, the practitioner's microcosm is brought into alignment with the macrocosm. This creates an egg-shaped aura extending properly in all directions from the body.

Quantities

  • Space large enough to lie down without touching circle edges
  • Ability to create sacred space according to your practice
  • No physical materials required beyond what you use for circle creation

Directions

  1. Set up a circle large enough for you to lie down in without touching the edges.
  2. This circle is oriented to the North instead of the usual East because you are working with magnetic fields rather than the movement of light and darkness.
  3. Begin in the northern quarter.
  4. Face outward and invoke the powers of the quarter:
  5. I call upon the black bull of the North and the gods of night.
    Rulers of the mountains and gnomish spirits,
    Lords of Boreas, the northern wind,
    And all ye princes of the powers of Earth,
    I stir, summon, and call you.
    I throw open the gate and call you to the circle
    Grow forth and witness!
  6. Move to the East and invoke:
  7. I call upon the eagle of the East and the gods of the breaking day.
    Rulers of wind and whirling sylphs,
    Lords of Eurus, the eastern wind,
    And all ye princes of the powers of air,
    I stir, summon, and call you.
    I throw open the gate and call you to the circle
    Grow forth and witness!
  8. Move to the South and invoke:
  9. I call upon the lion of the Sun and the gods of noon.
    Rulers of the desert and darting Djinn,
    Powers of Notus, the southern wind,
    I stir, summon, and call you.
    I throw open the gate and call you to the circle
    Grow forth and witness!
  10. Move to the West and invoke:
  11. I call upon the water-bearers and gods of twilight.
    Rulers of the deep and flowing undines,
    Lords of Zephyrus the western wind,
    And all ye princes of the powers of water,
    I stir, summon, and call you.
    I throw open the gate and call you to the circle
    Grow forth and witness!
  12. Having invoked the four quarters, move to the center of the circle and face North.
  13. Invoke the powers of above and below:
  14. I call upon the dove of the great heights and the serpent of the depths.
    I throw open the powers of the sky!
    I throw open the powers of the Earth!
    I throw open the pylons of the heavens!
    I throw open the pylons of the underworld!
    I call the powers of the zenith and nadir to the circle
    Grow forth and witness!
  15. Lie down in the circle with your head pointing North and speak:
  16. All ye powers of the heights
    All ye powers of the depths
    All ye powers of the horizon
    I align myself with you.
    May my own presence be in harmony with yours
    As above so below
    So mote it be.
  17. Lie there for as long as feels right.
  18. Allow the powers of the directions to magnetically adjust your energetic field.
  19. You may feel tingling, heat, coolness, pressure, or simply deep peace.
  20. Some practitioners report seeing actual beings moving in from the six directions to work on the aura.
  21. The power of this rite rests in the ancient principle that the human being is a microcosm, a mirror image of the macrocosm.
  22. By invoking the macrocosm, your microcosm is brought into alignment with it.
  23. The six directions represent the totality of space, and your aura naturally reforms into its proper egg-shape when exposed to these forces.
  24. When you are finished, leave the circle in silence.
  25. There is no need to formally close or banish.
  26. The powers you have invoked are cosmic forces, not summoned entities that need dismissal.
  27. This ritual need not only be used after magickal attack.
  28. It can be employed any time you feel out of balance, energetically depleted, or physically sick: after magickal attack or psychic assault, healing work that removed something (like the egg transference method), serious illness or injury, emotional trauma, extended periods of stress or exhaustion, or any situation where your aura feels damaged, depleted, or misshapen.
