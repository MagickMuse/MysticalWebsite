🧘Stress-Buster Ritual

A relaxing ritual using crystals, candle magick, and essential oils to release stress and stay calm. Features amethyst and rose quartz for neutralizing anxiety.

Quantities

  • Soothing music (new age or classical, instrumental or chanting)
  • A tumbled chunk of amethyst
  • A tumbled chunk of rose quartz
  • A blue candle
  • A candleholder
  • A ballpoint pen
  • Essential oil of lavender, vanilla, sweet orange, or ylang-ylang
  • Matches or a lighter

Directions

  1. Collect the ingredients needed for this spell.
  2. Wash the stones with mild soap and water, and then pat them dry.
  3. Start the music you've chosen.
  4. Cast a circle around the area where you will do your spell.
  5. With the ballpoint pen write the word "Peace" on the candle.
  6. Dress the candle by rubbing it with essential oil (not on the wick).
  7. Fit the candle in its holder and light it.
  8. Hold one gemstone in each hand.
  9. Sit in a comfortable place and close your eyes.
  10. Begin breathing slowly and deeply.
  11. Inhale the soothing scent and allow it to calm your mind.
  12. Rub the smooth stones with your fingers.
  13. Feel the stones neutralize stress, irritability, and anxiety.
  14. Focus on your breathing.
  15. If your mind starts to wander, gently bring it back and say or think the word "peace."
  16. Spend at least ten minutes this way, longer if you wish.
  17. When you feel ready, open your eyes and extinguish the candle.
  18. Open the circle.
  19. Let the music continue playing or shut it off.
  20. Carry the stones with you and rub them whenever stress starts to mount.
