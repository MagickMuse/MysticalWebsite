🤝Cooperative Contraceptive Spell

A Moroccan contraceptive working that requires active cooperation between partners. A dried fig or bead is charged with a few drops of menstrual blood and concealed by the man in a location unknown to the woman. The period of sterility is understood to last as long as the object remains hidden. The working is broken when the man retrieves and displays the object to the woman.

Quantities

A few drops of menstrual blood
One dried fig (cut lengthwise) OR one bead with a hole

Directions

  1. Cut a dried fig lengthwise and sprinkle a few drops of menstrual blood into the slit, or place the blood inside the hole of a bead.
  2. The man then hides the fig or bead in a location unknown to the woman.
  3. Sterility is understood to hold for as long as the object stays hidden.
  4. To break the spell and restore fertility, the man retrieves the object and displays it to the woman.

What distinguishes this working from most contraceptive spells is that it requires mutual participation and cannot be dissolved unilaterally. The woman provides the blood - the biological material tied directly to her reproductive cycle. The man controls the hiding place and therefore controls the reversibility of the working. Neither can complete or undo it alone.

The fig is an ancient symbol of female sexuality and fertility. The act of slitting it open and secreting blood inside creates a charged object whose concealment mirrors the intention of keeping fertility closed off and hidden.

The theatrical reversal - the man producing the object 'with a flourish' - is part of the spell's mechanics, not merely decorative. The display and the revelation are the countersign. The working knows it is being undone because it is shown to the light.
