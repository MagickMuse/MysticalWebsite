🌙Selene Sleep Sachet

◇Form
Pouch
◇Method
DreamworkLunarInvocationHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 cucumber (seeds only) - Bowl - Short selenite wand - Dried jasmine - Dried mugwort pieces (optional) - Poppy seeds - Dried rose petals (optional) - 1 star anise - Sachet bag in silver, white, purple, or blue
◇Directions
1. Night one, preparation: 2. Slice the cucumber and scoop out the seeds into a small dish. 3. Set the rest of the cucumber aside for eating, for Beltane Beauty Facial Ice Cubes, or for an infusion. 4. Place the seeds where they can dry overnight under the moonlight. 5. Night two, the spell: 6. Sit quietly and bring your focus to Selene, moon goddess, matron of all who love and work by her light, the one who visited her sleeping beloved Endymion at the end of each lunar cycle. 7. Let her presence settle around you. 8. Look at the bowl in your hands, its circular shape like the full moon. 9. Hold the selenite wand and trace it around the outside of the bowl slowly, encircling it with lunar energy. 10. Then lightly tap the side of the bowl three times. 11. With the moon's light in your mind, begin adding each ingredient with intention: 12. - Jasmine for psychic dreams and lunar enchantment - Mugwort for prophetic vision and psychic sleep - Poppy seeds for deep, peaceful, and receptive sleep - Rose petals for love, spirituality, and the heart's openings 13. Using your finger, make a small hollow in the center of the herb blend. 14. Nestle the dried cucumber seeds there. 15. Say aloud: 16. "Under the power of the moon goddess Selene, here I plant the seeds, That in my dreams, the information that I seek, I shall receive." 17. Place the star anise atop the cucumber seeds as a symbol of celestial blessing and five-pointed alignment. 18. Transfer the full mixture into the sachet bag, then tuck the selenite wand inside. 19. Leave the sachet outside or on a windowsill to charge under the full moonlight overnight. 20. To use: 21. Whenever you wish to receive a prophetic dream, remove the selenite wand from the sachet. 22. Touch its tip gently to your forehead while holding the closed sachet in your other hand. 23. Speak your request or question aloud. 24. Hold the selenite there for a moment, letting the intention settle into your body. 25. Return the wand to the bag, seal it, and place the sachet under your pillow. 26. Hold the selenite wand as you drift off to sleep, and let Selene carry your question into the dreaming.
◇Description
A sleep sachet assembled over two nights under the full moon, invoking Selene to bring prophetic dreams, ancestor messages, and psychic visions. Dry cucumber seeds in moonlight the first night, then blend them with jasmine, mugwort, poppy seeds, rose petals, and star anise in a bowl encircled by a selenite wand. Transfer everything to a sachet and charge under the full moon. Hold the selenite to your forehead to activate before tucking it under your pillow.
