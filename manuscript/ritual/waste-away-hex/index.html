A destructive hex from the New Orleans tradition designed to cause the target to gradually lose vitality, becoming passive and listless. Graveyard dirt is combined with a collection of condition formulas and an intimate personal item belonging to the target, then buried or hidden near their home. ⚠️ This spell derives from New Orleans/Hoodoo tradition, a closed African American folk magick practice. Research and respect the cultural context before engaging. Most of the listed ingredients (Babel Powder, Black Cat Oil, Damnation Water, Four Thieves Vinegar, Jezebel Root Powder, Lost and Away Powder, Mummy Oil) are not confirmed as tagged pages.

Quantities

  • Graveyard dirt
  • Asafoetida powder
  • Babel Powder (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Black Cat Oil (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Damnation Water (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Four Thieves Vinegar (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Goofer Dust
  • Jezebel Root Powder (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Lost and Away Powder (not confirmed as tagged)
  • Mummy Oil (not confirmed as tagged)
  • A personal item belonging to the target (as intimate as possible)
  • A small bag

Directions

  1. Combine the graveyard dirt with all listed condition formulas.
  2. Mix the blend with a personal item belonging to the target, choosing something as intimate as possible.
  3. Place everything in a small bag.
  4. Bury the bag or conceal it somewhere near the target's home.
