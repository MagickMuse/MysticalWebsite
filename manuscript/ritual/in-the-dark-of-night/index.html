🌑In the Dark of Night

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Smoke MagickVisualizationCandle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 black candle Mugwort (a pinch or two) Dragon's Blood resin (a chunk) 1 charcoal tablet 1 heat-proof dish 1 large piece of white paper or cloth Black ink or marker
◇Directions
1. Draw three concentric circles on the paper or cloth with an X in the centre. 2. Place the dish and charcoal tablet on the X, and the black candle outside the circles. 3. Light the charcoal and allow it to smoulder. 4. Place a pinch or two of mugwort on it along with a chunk of Dragon's Blood resin. 5. When both are smoking, light the candle. 6. Watch the smoke rising from the dish and visualize it floating through space to the intended target. 7. Picture the smoke carrying nightmares and fear into their sleep. 8. Hold this visualization for several minutes while focusing on the target. 9. After a few minutes, extinguish the charcoal. 10. Switch the positions of the dish and the candle so the candle now sits on the X. Leave the candle to burn down completely. 11. The target will begin having nightmares the following night, lasting for three days.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A black magick nightmare spell cast between midnight and dawn. Mugwort and Dragon's Blood resin are burned on charcoal within three concentric circles while the practitioner visualizes the smoke delivering nightmares and fear to the target. The candle is then moved to the centre and left to burn out. The hex lasts three days. Warning: produces smoke.
