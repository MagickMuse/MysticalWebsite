⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A devastating hex that uses sympathetic magick to shatter a marriage through psychological torment. Nails driven into a photograph on wood target the heart and mind, while black candle wax dripping over the image seals the working in darkness, negativity, and chaos. Designed for a target the practitioner despises with genuine intensity, the intended result is total marital destruction: hatred, violence, separation, and divorce.

Quantities

4 black candles
1 photograph of the target
Anointing oil
1 red marker
1 hammer
2 nails
1' x 1' block of wood

Directions

  1. Anoint the four black candles while concentrating on the intended chaos.
  2. Visualize the target's psychological torment and ultimate separation.
  3. Place the photograph on the block of wood and draw red hearts on the forehead and chest with the red marker.
  4. Drive one nail into the chest heart, saying: 'With this nail I stab your heart, soon your love so near will part.' Drive the second nail into the forehead heart, saying: 'With this nail I stab your mind, insanity you now shall find.' Facing west, arrange the four black candles at the cardinal points around the photograph.
  5. Light the candles and meditate on the hex, allowing black wax to drip onto the photograph.
  6. Recite the main incantation.
  7. Extinguish the candles but leave everything in place.
  8. Part two may be repeated for three consecutive nights.

Incantations

With this nail I stab your heart,
Soon your love so near will part.
With this nail I stab your mind,
Insanity you now shall find.
Lords of Darkness, Lords of Night,
Bring this hex now to flight.
Take this man/woman I see before me now,
Speed up his/her karma, bring him/her down.
Take his/her marriage and break it apart,
Bring it chaos and hate, now to start.
Tear their love to shreds so fine,
What is gone, now is the time.
No longer shall they be together,
His/her close-knit family shall now be severed.
When together they start to smother,
Each does want to kill the other.
No longer shall they be as one,
Your marriage is over,
What's done is done!
Lords of Darkness, Lords of Night,
Bring this hex now to flight.
So mote it be.
