🎭Leo Ritual for Banishing Stage Fright

A Leo zodiac ritual for a child or adult performing publicly to banish stage fright, drawing a golden lion on paper with glitter and stars to invoke confidence and shine.

Quantities

  • 1 gold pen
  • 1 sheet white paper
  • 1 glue stick (preferably gold)
  • 1 bowl of glitter
  • 1 bowl of gold or glittery stars
  • 1 gold purse or drawstring bag

Directions

  1. In the center of the paper, draw a golden lion, saying, "Power of the lion, fill me with power, that when the hour is mine I [or name person] will shine." Spread glue around the paper, but not over the lion, and sprinkle glitter over the glue, saying, "Ever brighter, Jungle Lord, that when I [or name] appear, all will applaud and all will cheer." Edge the paper with gold stars and, when dry, set the paper where light will shine on it.
  2. Put any remaining glitter and stars in the bag and carry the bag to the event.
