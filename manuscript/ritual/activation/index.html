Ritual Grimoire

🔥Activation

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Verbal WorkingCharged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
Myrrh: burned as incense, fumes inhaled and exhaled onto the open book Blood: 1 drop, placed on the page after the final word Flames: 3 consecrated candles or fires, over which the book is passed
◇Directions
1. Perform the usual preparations and consecrate the space. 2. At a liminal hour, stand before three consecrated flames with burning myrrh. 3. Hold the Hekattion in your hands. Call out the Hymn of Activation with great conviction: "IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / HEKATE -- HEKATE -- HEKATE! / CALLEO SEH! CLOUTHI MEU! KLEIDZO SEH! / Crownless Queen, / Bless this book; Be this book; Come yourself into this book! / Bless this book; Be this book; Come yourself into this book! / Bless this book; Be this book; Come yourself into this book!" 4. Inhale the myrrh fumes deeply, then exhale them onto the open pages of the book. 5. Continue: "Book, you are no book, / But the pulse of Brimo's heart. / Breathe, breathe, breathe, / And as a spirit start. / Book, become alive / As I conclude my part. / O Fire-Breather, breathe your fire / Into this Work of Art!" 6. Again inhale the myrrh fumes deeply, and exhale them onto the open pages; then cry out: "IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / IO PYRIPNOOS * IO PYRIPHOITOS * IO PYRIBOULOS! / KHAIRA, KHAIRA, KHAIRA! / HEKATE!" 7. As you proclaim the fiery epithets, riffle the book's pages and pass the book over three consecrated flames. 8. Upon speaking the final word, place a drop of your blood on the following page. 9. Slap the book shut. 10. Press it to your heart. 11. Speak to it: introduce yourself, confirm that you are its master, and that it exists to serve you, empower your charms, give you insight and inspiration, true dreams, and sacred visions. 12. Thereafter: put it under your pillow when you sleep; take it with you when you travel. Feed it each time you use it. Offer another drop of blood as reward. It is your companion for the journey.
◇Description
The final rite of the Hekattion, in which the book itself is awakened as a living spirit companion. At a liminal hour before three consecrated flames and burning myrrh, the Hierophant holds the book, riffles its pages, inhales and exhales myrrh fumes onto it, recites the Hymn of Activation three times, and seals the rite with a drop of blood before slapping the book shut and pressing it to her heart. The awakened book feeds each time it is used, grows stronger with each drop of blood offered, and becomes a many-eyed protector and source of true dreams, sacred visions, and inspiration.
