🔥Egg and Oil Burning

Five raw eggs are drawn down over the body from head to toe, absorbing the Evil Eye's force as they go. Each egg is transferred into a dish of olive oil once used. When all five are collected, cayenne and cinnamon are added and cotton wicks are set into the oil and lit, burning the extracted force out of existence. One of the Evil Eye Removal methods.

Quantities

  • 5 whole raw eggs
  • Olive oil, enough to fill a dish
  • Ground cayenne pepper
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Cotton wicks

Directions

  1. Fill a dish with olive oil and set it nearby.
  2. Working with one egg at a time, rub the body from head to toe using smooth downward, outward strokes.
  3. After each egg has been worked over the body, place it in the oil dish. Repeat with each of the five eggs in turn.
  4. When all five eggs are resting in the oil, sprinkle cayenne pepper and ground cinnamon over them.
  5. Push cotton wicks into the oil and light them, burning the contents of the dish.
