🕯️Simple Love Spell with Runes

◇Form
Dressed CandleSigil
◇Method
Candle MagickManifestationInvocationVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Railroad spike • Red taper candle approximately 6" long • Match • Copal resin • Mortar and pestle • Attraction oil or sensuous essential oil blend
◇Directions
1. Decide on a rune system to use, either Elder Futhark or Ogham. 2. Use the railroad spike to press transliterations of your desire (love, lust, union) vertically into one side of the candle. 3. On the other side, create a transliteration of a deity associated with love such as Freya, Venus, Eros, or Hera for stable relationships. 4. If deity work does not align with your practice, focus on what you want. 5. Make staves with care but without hesitation, studying the forms beforehand. 6. For Ogham, start from the bottom and work up. 7. Use the match to remove any stray bits of wax so the staves are clear. 8. Grind copal resin in your mortar and pestle. 9. As the larger pieces break, envision communication barriers breaking and obstacles falling away, transmuted to dust by your magick and will. 10. Hold the candle over the mortar and work pinches of the fine copal powder into the carvings, making them more visible against the red. 11. Think about how you would like to be touched and mirror that energy, playful or gentle, as you work. 12. When carvings are visible, take a drop of oil on your fingertip and use it to remove excess copal dust from the candle surface, avoiding getting oil in the carvings as it will darken the copal. 13. Enjoy the scent and your craftwork, then light the candle and enjoy its seductive glow.
◇Description
A candle spell using runic transliterations carved into a red taper candle with a railroad spike. One side displays the transliteration of a desire (love, lust, union), the other side a deity name associated with love (Freya, Venus, Eros, or Hera for stability). The carvings are filled with ground copal resin and dressed with attraction oil.
