🧊Banishing Freezer Jar Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
JarWriting SpellDressed Candle
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
LunarBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Freezer-safe jar with lid - Water - Ground black pepper - Sea salt - Pen and paper - Item or image representing what you are suspending (e.g. candy for a sugar addiction) - Ground red pepper - Black candle - 1 lime - Matches or lighter
◇Directions
1. Under the waning moon (ideally third quarter or waning crescent), fill the jar three-quarters full with water, leaving room for expansion when frozen. 2. Stir in black pepper and sea salt counterclockwise, directing the motion toward banishment and dissolution. 3. Leave the jar outside or on a windowsill overnight to charge under the waning moon's diminishing, clearing energy. 4. On the following night, ideally the first night of the dark moon, write the name, habit, or situation you are freezing on a piece of paper. 5. Be specific and direct. 6. Fold the paper away from you, physically enacting the energy moving out of your life. 7. Tuck the folded paper into the jar. 8. Add any object or image that represents what you are suspending. 9. Seal the lid. 10. Squeeze lime juice over the black candle to dress it, then roll the candle away from you through a mixture of black pepper, red pepper, and salt. 11. Affix the candle securely to the top of the sealed jar. 12. Light it and let it burn down completely. 13. Once the candle has finished burning, place the jar in the freezer. 14. Leave it there until you are certain the situation has fully resolved. 15. Defrosting too soon can allow the banished energy to resurface, so wait until you feel clear. 16. A home cleansing or cord-cutting ritual afterward can reinforce the working. 17. When you are ready to release the spell entirely, discard the jar somewhere far from your home, or thaw it and pour the water out at a crossroads as a final disposal.
◇Description
A two-night waning moon freezer spell to suspend and banish harmful people, bad habits, or unwanted situations. Charge salted pepper-water under the waning moon on night one, then on the dark moon seal a written intention inside the jar, dress a black candle with lime and spices, burn it atop the jar, and freeze. Leave frozen until the situation has fully cleared. Follow with cleansing or cord cutting to affirm the banishment.
