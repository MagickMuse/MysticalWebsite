🛡️Bitchy Coworker Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteWriting Spell
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualizationProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 blue candle - 1 lavender essential oil (diluted) - Paper - Pen - Small mirror - Watermelon tourmaline or rose quartz
◇Directions
1. Perform this working on a Saturday during a waning moon for banishing negativity, or during a waxing moon to draw peace. 2. Write the coworker's name on the paper. 3. Do not write curses or ill wishes. 4. Simply write their name and beneath it write: "Your negativity does not reach me. Your words slide past me. I am protected." Anoint the blue candle with diluted lavender oil for peace and calm. 5. Place the mirror face-up with the paper on top of it, name side down, so their energy reflects back to them. 6. Set the crystal on top. 7. Light the blue candle and say: "I release all hooks this person has placed in me. Their energy returns to them. I am surrounded by peace." Let the candle burn for at least twenty minutes. 8. Dispose of the paper by burying it away from your home or burning it safely. 9. Keep the crystal at your workspace for continued protection.
◇Description
A spell to neutralize workplace negativity and protect yourself from a difficult coworker's harmful energy without causing them harm.
