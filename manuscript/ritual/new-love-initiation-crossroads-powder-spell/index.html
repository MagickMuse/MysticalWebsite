💨New Love Initiation Crossroads Powder Spell

◇Form
Powder
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
ManifestationInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Acacia splinters; aloes wood splinters; 3 hairs from your head
◇Directions
1. Pulverize splinters of acacia and aloes wood together with three hairs from your head. 2. Take the resulting blend to a crossroads on a breezy day. 3. Let the powder slip from between your fingers as you revolve in a circle, murmuring an invocation of love.
◇Description
A spell to magickally signal readiness for love to the universe. Powdered acacia and aloes wood blended with three personal hairs are released at a crossroads on a breezy day.
