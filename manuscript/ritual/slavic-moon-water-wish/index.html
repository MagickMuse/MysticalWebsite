💖Slavic Moon Water Wish

A Slavic water-blessing ritual using moonstones and moon-charged water to bring the right love when you've been alone for a while.

Quantities

  • One large silver-colored bucket or glass bowl, half filled with water
  • Seven small moonstones or white stones

Directions

  1. On a bright full moon night, take your vessel of water outside and position it so the moon's image appears reflected on the water's surface.
  2. Gaze into that silver reflection and speak:
  3. "Father Moon, I would not hold you captive a moment longer than I need. Send to me my lasting gentle love and go on your way with blessings."
  4. Drop the first moonstone into the water while repeating your words.
  5. Continue with each remaining stone, one at a time, speaking the invocation with each drop.
  6. Thank Father Moon for his blessing and leave the water outside all night to absorb the moonlight.
  7. When morning comes, pour the moon-blessed water into small glass bottles.
  8. Use this water in your baths and showers before socializing throughout the month ahead.
  9. Keep the seven moonstones in a silver-colored bag as a love token to carry with you.
