Adder's Tongue

For wounds and bruises that refuse to heal, adder's tongue herb is soaked in warm water and applied to the skin, covered with a white handkerchief. When the herb grows warm from drawing in the injury, it is buried in mud or a swamp to transfer the pain into the earth.

Quantities

Adder's tongue (botanical)
Warm water
Clean white handkerchief

Directions

  1. Soak adder's tongue in warm water.
  2. Place it on the bruised or wounded skin.
  3. Cover with a clean white handkerchief.
  4. When the herb grows warm, remove it.
  5. Bury the herbs and handkerchief together in mud or a swamp.
  6. The wound or bruise will allegedly now heal.
