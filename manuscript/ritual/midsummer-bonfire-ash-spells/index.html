☀️Midsummer

Two workings using Midsummer bonfire ashes for fertility: filling charm bags to carry, and sprinkling ashes on the sheets or using them to cast a circle around the bed before lovemaking.

Quantities

Midsummer bonfire ashes
Small charm bag or pouch (for charm working)

Directions

  1. Collect ashes from the Midsummer bonfire once fully extinguished.
  2. For the charm bag working, fill a small bag with the ashes and carry it for good fortune, healing, and renewed fertility.
  3. For the bed rite, bring the ashes home and either sprinkle them across the sheets before lovemaking, or cast a circle around the bed using the ashes and make love within it.

Midsummer bonfire ashes retain magickal power. They can be used in two ways:

Spell 5: Bonfire Ash Charm Bag

Fill charm bags with the ashes and carry them for good fortune, healing, and renewed fertility.

Spell 6: Bonfire Ash Bed Rite

Gather some ashes and bring them home. Choose one of the following:

  • Sprinkle the ashes on the sheets and make love.
  • Cast a circle around the bed with the ashes and make love within it.
