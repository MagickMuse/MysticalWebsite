A mojo bag for money drawing using smartweed, lodestones, magnetic horseshoes, and Magnet Oil, fed with magnetic sand every three days until the needed sum is received. One of the Conjure Bag methods.

⚠️ Smart Ass Mojo is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Brown paper
Bergamot essential oil and/or Magnet Oil
Magnetic horseshoes
Lodestones
Smartweed (also known as water pepper or smart ass)
Magnetic sand (gold and/or green if possible)
Conjure bag

Directions

  1. Write the amount of money you need on a piece of paper.
  2. Anoint it with essential oil of bergamot and/or Magnet Oil and place it in a conjure bag.
  3. Add magnetic horseshoes plus lodestones plus some smartweed, also known as 'water pepper' or 'smart ass.' Feed the bag by sprinkling it with magnetic sand every third day until the money needed is received.
  4. Magnetic sand is sold dyed in colors, use gold and/or green to enhance the spell.
