🔄Tangled in the Devil's Shoestrings

A reversal spell that binds a curse and returns it to the sender by using nine pieces of devil's shoestring to gather the malicious energy, then delivering it back to the culprit's property mixed with hot peppers and poppy seeds.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Devil's shoestring root: 9 pieces
  • Dirt from offender's home
  • Jar: 1
  • Red pepper
  • Black pepper
  • Poppy seeds

Directions

  1. After deciding whether to reverse the spell and divining the identity of the culprit if possible, you are ready to perform the reversal.
  2. This spell is particularly effective when the curse centers on your home.
  3. Begin by bringing to mind the jinx that has been placed upon you.
  4. Try as best you can to tap into that force set against you, to get a psychic feel for its frequency.
  5. Take up a piece of devil's shoestring in your right hand and trace a cross in the air in front of you, feeling the root gather up the energy of the spell.
  6. As you make the vertical arm of the cross, say: "I am not in power of thine."
  7. When making the horizontal arm of the cross, say: "Thou art in power of mine."
  8. Do this nine times, once with each piece of devil's shoestring.
  9. Take all nine pieces of devil's shoestring and place them in a jar with the dirt from your enemy's house, some red and black pepper, and some poppy seeds, intermingling them all together.
  10. Go back to the culprit's house and, as you sprinkle the mix in their yard or on their porch, say:
  11. "What malice you have aimed at me
    shall now be returned unto thee
    By word and will, so shall it be."
  12. Walk away from the home without looking back.
  13. This ritual quite literally binds the curse up, removing it from your sphere, and delivers it back upon the sender in one neat package.
  14. When you return home you should banish well, cleanse your house, and fortify whatever protections you have in place.
