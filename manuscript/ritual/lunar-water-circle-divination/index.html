🔮Lunar Water Circle Divination

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
• A dish, bucket, or pail of water • 4 lit torches (or candles)
◇Directions
1. Place a dish, bucket, or pail of water in a position where it will directly capture the light of the full moon. 2. Surround the vessel with four lit torches, arranging them to form a circle or sacred space around it. 3. Gaze into the water and observe the oracle it reveals. 4. Also pay attention to the interplay of the torch flames, as they provide additional information. 5. The water used in this working is simultaneously charged with lunar energy and may be set aside for use in future spellcasting.
◇Description
A Full Moon water scrying method in which a dish of water is placed to capture moonlight and surrounded by four lit torches. The water surface and the interplay of the flames together provide the oracle. The water is simultaneously charged with lunar energy and may be reserved for future spellcasting.
