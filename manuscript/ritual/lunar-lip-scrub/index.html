💋Lunar Lip Scrub

A food-grade sugar and coconut oil lip scrub blended with lemon zest, nutmeg, peppermint, and jasmine under the full moon to enchant speech and beautify. Stir clockwise with the luminous moon in mind and leave to charge in the moonlight. Use one to three times a week while visualizing harmonious, enchanting words. Vary the moon sign to tune the intention: Libra for diplomacy, Cancer for nurturing warmth, Gemini for wit and youthful charm.

Quantities

  • Generous pinch of lemon zest
  • Small jar with lid
  • ⅛ cup granulated sugar
  • 1½ tablespoons coconut oil
  • ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 2 drops peppermint essential oil
  • 2 drops jasmine essential oil
    Use food-grade, organic ingredients where possible.

Directions

  1. Begin under your chosen moon sign, Libra, Cancer, or Gemini, ideally at the full moon, waxing crescent, or waxing gibbous.
  2. Add the lemon zest to the jar first.
  3. As you do, visualize your words landing well: heard, welcomed, received with warmth.
  4. Add the sugar to sweeten your speech, then spoon in the coconut oil (a moon-associated ingredient), followed by the nutmeg and both essential oils.
  5. Stir everything together clockwise, thinking of the moon above, luminous, beautifying, drawing out what shines.
  6. Let that image fill the scrub as you blend.
  7. Seal the jar and leave it to charge under the moonlight overnight.
  8. To use, apply a small amount to your lips and scrub gently in circular motions.
  9. As you do, visualize your lips forming words that are harmonious, enchanting, and magnetic.
  10. Rinse away and follow with balm or moisturizer.
  11. Use one to three times a week to maintain the enchantment.
  12. ---
  13. Moon sign variations:
  14. Libra moon, diplomatic, balanced, and harmonious communication
  15. Cancer moon, nurturing words, self-care focus, emotionally attuned conversations
  16. Gemini moon, clever, witty, youthful expression
