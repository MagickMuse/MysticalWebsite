💖Seven Daughters of Hathor Love-Knot

◇Form
Candle RiteCharm
◇Method
Candle MagickKnot MagickInvocationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Seven red ribbons of equal length - One red candle
◇Directions
1. Light the red candle and arrange the seven ribbons in a line beside it, one behind the other. 2. Take the first two ribbons and tie them together with a single knot while speaking: 3. "Daughters of Hathor, I ask that my true love may find me and stay with me lovingly. Mother Hathor, guide the way." 4. Join the third ribbon to the second with another knot, repeating your invocation. 5. Continue this pattern, tying each subsequent ribbon to the one before it, fourth to third, fifth to fourth, sixth to fifth, and finally seventh to sixth. 6. Speak the words with each knot you make. 7. Allow the candle to burn completely. 8. When it has finished, take your chain of ribbons and lay it across your bedroom windowsill to rest overnight. 9. In the morning, carry the ribbon chain outside and secure it to a tree. 10. Repeat your invocation seven times as you tie it in place.
◇Description
An Egyptian ribbon-binding ritual calling upon Hathor's seven daughters to attract and secure lasting love through knotwork and invocation.
