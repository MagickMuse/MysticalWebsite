Ritual Grimoire

🌅For Successful Early Retirement

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
VisualizationManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Straight path along tree-covered avenue or pedestrian underpass, leading to light - 1 piece of paper - 1 red pen
◇Directions
1. Walk along the pathway toward the light, saying aloud if possible, "The way from the past closes without sorrow, for joy will come in my tomorrow." When you reach the light at the end of the trees/underpass, stop and look back, saying, "I need not go back there any more. My future is an open door." Push away with your hands toward the direction from which you have come. 2. Your fingers should be pointing upward and your palms facing outward from your body when doing this. 3. Then face the sun again. 4. Do not return down the path. 5. Go home another way and write down your ten-point plan for the future, making time for leisure. 6. Perform on a sunny day.
◇Description
A walking ritual through a tree-covered path toward light, symbolically closing the past and opening to future possibilities.
