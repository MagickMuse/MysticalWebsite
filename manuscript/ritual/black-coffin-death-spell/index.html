⚰️Black Coffin Death Spell

◇Quantities
1 miniature coffin (2-3 inches, made from wood, wax, or clay) Black paint 1 small doll Pins 2 burnt stumps of black candle XX Doublecross oil or Black Arts oil (optional)
◇Directions
1. Construct a miniature coffin roughly two to three inches in length from wood, wax, clay, or any material you can shape with ease. 2. Paint the coffin black. 3. Fashion a small doll to represent the target and pierce it with pins. 4. Place the doll inside the coffin. 5. Under cover of night, set the coffin on the enemy's doorstep and position a burnt stump of black candle at each end. 6. Some practitioners sprinkle XX Doublecross oil or Black Arts oil over the coffin before placing it.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING ⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice sacred to African American spiritual traditions and should only be practiced by those within the originating culture. A folk magick death spell from the southern United States using a miniature coffin, poppet, and black candles to direct harm toward an enemy. Some practitioners dress the coffin with crossing or baneful oils before placement.
