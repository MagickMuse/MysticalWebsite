📚Education🧠Knowledge🔮Magick📚StudyTeachingVisualization

A visualization journey to study with one of the world's great teachers of magick. Any teacher who holds the needed knowledge may be chosen.

Quantities

An offering suited to the chosen teacher
Music (optional)
Fragrance (optional)

Directions

  1. Choose a teacher with care, and learn as much about them as possible before the lesson.
  2. Save the lesson for a time with quiet and privacy.
  3. Make the teacher an offering, much like the traditional apple for a schoolteacher.
  4. Set the stage with music and fragrance if that feels inspiring.
  5. Journey inward to the teacher in visualization, memorizing the path, since it leads back home.
  6. Return to the practice if the first attempts fail; it may take several tries, especially for those new to visualization.
