A Paganized version of St. Patrick's Lorica (word armor) that serves as a protective petition to be recited upon Winter Solstice morning or as a daily winter mantra. Can optionally be crafted into a physical breastplate charm.

Quantities

  • For spoken invocation:
  • The Lorica prayer (provided below)
    For physical charm (optional):
  • Small piece of paper
  • Black ink or pen
  • A few rosemary needles
  • Red ribbon
  • Optional: Small magickal bag for enclosure

Directions

  1. Spoken Invocation:
    Recite these words upon arising on Winter Solstice morning or upon greeting the solstice sunrise. You can also use this as a daily morning mantra throughout the winter:
    I arise today
    through the strength of the heavens;
    light of the sun,
    splendor of fire,
    clarity of ice,
    speed of the wind,
    depth of the snow,
    stability of the earth,
    firmness of the rock.
    The light has returned!
    Creating a Physical Breastplate Charm:
    To convert this Lorica into a real protective breastplate:
  2. Write the words of the Lorica on a small piece of paper using black ink (for grounding and permanence).
  3. Add a few rosemary needles to the paper.
  4. Fold the paper into a secure packet.
  5. Tie the packet with a red ribbon (for vitality).
  6. Place the packet next to your bed or on your altar where you can see it daily. Alternatively, tuck it into your purse, briefcase, or pocket to keep its protection with you throughout the day.
  7. Optional: For extra protection and beauty, enclose the packet in a small magickal bag.
