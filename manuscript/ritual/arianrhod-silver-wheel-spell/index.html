🕸️Arianrhod Silver Wheel Spell

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Thread/Sewing MagickChant/MantraLunarManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- A candle - Incense - Matches or a lighter - Silver thread or yarn - Colored thread or yarn in colors representing your goals or intentions - 4 sticks (size according to how large you want your wheel)
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and incense. 2. Hold the colored threads that represent your intentions. 3. Let your goals settle clearly in your mind, and pass the threads slowly through the incense smoke to cleanse and charge them. 4. Take the silver thread and use it to bind two sticks together at their centers, forming a cross. 5. This shape symbolizes polarity, the meeting of opposites, and the four quarters of the moon. 6. Repeat for the remaining two sticks, binding them into a second cross. 7. Now bind the two crosses together at right angles to create an eight-armed frame. 8. This second binding represents the crescent and gibbous phases, the transitions between quarters, and defines the spaces between them. 9. The center, where all four sticks intersect, will be dense with thread. 10. This is the inception point of your magick, the heart of the working. 11. Anchor the silver thread at the center and begin weaving slowly, passing over and under each stick in turn, pushing the thread inward to build a tight, layered pattern. 12. As you weave, contemplate the balance between the light moon and the dark moon, between waking consciousness and the unseen, the unformed, the world of spirit. 13. Speak aloud as you work: 14. "I weave the silver wheel, the tapestry of life. I weave into this wheel, my desires this full/new moon night." 15. Repeat the chant as you continue. 16. When you are ready, introduce the colored threads, weaving them into the wheel as living symbols of your goals. 17. Visualize each intention taking root in the weave, becoming real, becoming matter, just as Arianrhod spins fate from the silver wheel of the moon. 18. You can layer in as many threads as you like: one for finances, one for love, one for travel, one for health, whatever threads make up the life you are weaving into being. 19. When the wheel feels complete, tie off the threads. 20. If you wish to hang the wheel, tie a loop at the top. 21. Leave it to charge in the moonlight, surrounded by lunar crystals if you have them. 22. Afterward, hang it in your home, place it on your altar to charge other workings beneath it, or carry it as a protection and manifestation charm for the lunar year ahead.
◇Description
A weaving spell inspired by Arianrhod, the Welsh goddess whose name may mean "silver wheel," a reference to the moon and her role as a weaver of fate. You construct a god's-eye-style wheel from sticks and thread, weaving your intentions into its pattern as you work by candlelight. The finished wheel becomes a charged manifestation talisman, protection charm, or altar piece that continues to hold your intentions throughout the lunar year.
