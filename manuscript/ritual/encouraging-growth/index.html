Pine

A pocket charm using pine oil on cotton to support personal growth during difficult situations.

Quantities

  • 1 drop pine essential oil
  • Cotton round
  • Pocket in dominant-handed side of clothing

Directions

  1. Growing as a person is hard work.
  2. We must always be growing and evolving, or we die.
  3. When encountering a difficult situation that can help you grow as a person, place a drop of pine essential oil on a cotton round and keep it in your dominant-handed pocket.
  4. Bring it out to smell occasionally to calm your mind, relax your body, and remind yourself that things are great.
  5. That being said, if you feel like something is wrong, and not just low-grade anxiety, examine it.
  6. We know anxiety lies to us, but sometimes there is something going on.
  7. Be as safe as you can out there.
