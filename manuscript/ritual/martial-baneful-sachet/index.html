A Martial hex sachet for workings of wrath, harm, and chastisement. Bound with red ribbon or yarn during the waning moon, in the day and hour of Mars. Poisonous ingredient alternatives are listed for historical reference only.

Quantities

  • Safe/non-poisonous ingredients:
  • Blackthorn leaves
  • Black pepper
  • White pepper
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Ground ginger root
  • Dried chilis
  • Dragon's blood resin
  • Powdered sulphur (use sparingly)
  • Holly chips
  • Ash chips
  • Hickory chips
  • Dried stinging nettles
    Historical/poisonous alternatives (reference only, do not use):
  • Euphorbium (Euphorbia corollata / Snow on the Mountain)
  • Gum bdellium
  • Wolfsbane / Monkshood (Aconitum anglicum)
  • Gum ammoniac
  • Death cap mushrooms (Amanita phalloides)

Directions

Combine chosen safe ingredients. Bind the sachet closed with red ribbon or red yarn. Prepare during the waning moon, in the day and hour of Mars (approximately 8 AM, 3 PM, or 10 PM on a Tuesday).
