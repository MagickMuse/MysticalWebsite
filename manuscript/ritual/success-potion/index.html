To encourage success in your life, career, or any specific endeavor. Three variations provided, each stirred with three runes.

Quantities

Lemon balm tea and 1 dried allspice berry (Version 1)
Cinnamon tea and a drop of pure vanilla extract (Version 2)
Ginger tea, a cinnamon stick, and a strip of orange peel (Version 3)
Optional: golden spoon, orange cup or mug

Directions

  1. Choose one of the three versions below and brew the tea according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. Add that version's extra ingredient.
  3. Stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the three success runes shown below into the surface of the tea.
  4. As you drink, visualize yourself succeeding at your goals: your projects flourishing, your career advancing, and your endeavors reaching successful completion.
  5. Discard or retain the solid ingredients as noted for your version. Do not eat the allspice berry unless you choose to save it as a charm.

These tea potions encourage success in your life, your career, or any specific endeavor. Three variations are provided.

Version 1: Lemon Balm with Allspice

Brew lemon balm tea. Add one dried allspice berry. DO NOT EAT THE ALLSPICE BERRY (a type of pepper found where spices are sold). Discard it after you finish the potion, or wash it, dry it thoroughly, and retain it as a success charm.

Version 2: Cinnamon with Vanilla

Brew cinnamon tea. Add a scant drop of pure vanilla extract. The warming, prosperous energies of cinnamon combine with vanilla's sweetening influence.

Version 3: Ginger with Cinnamon and Orange

Brew ginger tea. Stir with a cinnamon stick and add a strip of orange peel. This is the most potent version, combining three success-drawing ingredients.

Optional Enhancements

Use a golden spoon to stir the potion, symbolizing success and achievement. An orange cup or mug strengthens the success energy.

Stirring Symbols

Trace the shapes of these three runes into the surface of the tea as you stir: Tiwaz Wunjo Sowilo
