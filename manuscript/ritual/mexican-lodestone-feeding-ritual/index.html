A Mexican folk tradition for sustaining a lodestone's vitality through a weekly cycle of soaking, feeding, and rest, treating the stone much like a living worker who needs regular downtime to stay sharp.

Quantities

1 lodestone
1 glass of wine, water, or aguardiente (strong liquor)
Magnetic sand (iron filings)

Directions

  1. On Friday evening, submerge the lodestone in a glass of wine, plain water, or aguardiente and let it soak overnight.
  2. On Saturday morning, lift the stone out and dust it generously with magnetic sand.
  3. Set the lodestone aside in a quiet spot and let it rest undisturbed through the rest of Saturday and all of Sunday.
  4. By Sunday night or Monday morning, the stone is refreshed and ready to return to its usual role in a charm bag, pocket charm, or other working.
