A deeply restorative and luxurious dream bath using monoi oil, designed to promote vivid dreaming and spiritual restoration during sleep. One of the Dream Bath methods.

Quantities

  • Monoi (gardenia-infused coconut oil)
  • A few drops of lavender essential oil

Directions

  1. Draw a warm bath.
  2. Before getting in, generously massage monoi into your body.
  3. Add a few drops of lavender essential oil to the bath water.
  4. Step into the tub and allow the water to gradually disperse the monoi from your skin.
  5. Relax fully.
  6. When finished, air dry if possible; if not, pat dry rather than rubbing with a towel.
  7. Go directly to bed.
