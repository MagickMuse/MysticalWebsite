🪞Mirror Beauty Blessing

A lunar beauty spell using rose and cardamom water charged under the moon to bless the mirrors in your home, enchanting all who see their reflection to recognize the beauty within themselves. Enhances self-love and draws Libra's eye for inner beauty into the heart of the home.

Quantities

  • Dried or fresh rose petals
  • Ground cardamom (a generous pinch)
  • Distilled or spring water
  • A bowl
  • A cloth

Directions

  1. Set a bowl outside where the moonlight can reach it.
  2. Add rose petals and sprinkle in a generous pinch of ground cardamom.
  3. Hover your hands above the ingredients, breathing in their scent and tuning into the soft buzz of their energy amplified by the moonlight.
  4. Let your mind settle on the moon's connection with beauty, deepened when the moon moves through Libra, and with the soul itself.
  5. Visualize the moonlight charging the roses and cardamom to draw out inner beauty in all who see their reflection.
  6. Pour the water into the bowl and speak aloud:
  7. "Like the moon reflects the sun's radiance, and water reflects the light,
    Bless this potion, so that all see beauty within their sight."
  8. Leave the bowl to charge in the moonlight overnight.
  9. The following day, dampen the cloth with the infused water and wash the edges and corners of each mirror in your home, repeating the chant at each one.
  10. As you move through the house, hold the intention that every person who looks into these mirrors will be met with a glimpse of their own inner light.
