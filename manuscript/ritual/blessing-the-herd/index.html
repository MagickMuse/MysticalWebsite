A Brigid blessing for domestic animals and pets, using candleflame and prayer to sanctify their sleeping space and invoke her protection. Includes a traditional protective charm for animal companions.

Quantities

1 candle

Directions

  1. Light a candle.
  2. Move sunwise (clockwise) around your animal's sleeping area or favorite resting spot three times while holding the flame.
  3. If you cannot walk all the way around, move the flame over the spot three times instead.
  4. As you encircle the space, speak:
  5. "May the encompassing of Brigid protect you,
    May the encircling of Brigid keep you,
    May the soft hand of Brigid soothe you,
    May the eye of Brigid be ever on you,
    To guard you and to cherish you,
    For today, forever, for eternity."
  6. To invoke Brigid's broader protection over animal companions, speak this traditional charm:
  7. "The charm placed of Brigid
    About her cows, about her kine,
    About her horses, about her goats,
    About her sheep, about her lambs:
    To keep them from eye, to keep them from omen,
    To keep them from spell, south and north,
    To keep them from venom, east and west,
    To keep them from envy and from wiles of the wicked,
    To keep them from hound and from each other's horns,
    From the birds of the high moors and from the beasts of the hills,
    To keep them from wolf, from ravaging dog,
    To keep them from fox, from the swiftest of pursuers."
  8. Extinguish the candle or allow it to burn down.
  9. A small protective symbol (a triskele, Brigid's cross charm, or Saint Brigid medal) may be placed near food dishes or sleeping areas to reinforce the blessing.
