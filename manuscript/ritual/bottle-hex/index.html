🍾Bottle Hex

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
BindingBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 glass bottle 1 photograph of the target 1 piece of paper with the target's name Holly Ivy Black ink War Water
◇Directions
1. Place the target's photograph inside a glass bottle. 2. Write the target's name on a piece of paper and add it to the bottle as well. 3. Stuff holly and ivy into the bottle. 4. Pour in some black ink and War Water. 5. Seal the bottle shut and bury it upside down.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A straightforward bottle hex. The target's photograph and written name are sealed inside a glass bottle along with holly, ivy, black ink, and War Water. The bottle is buried upside down to invert the target's fortune.
