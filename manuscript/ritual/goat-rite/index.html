🐐Goat Rite

A solo ceremony to deepen the Hierophant's bond with the Underworld and its scion, Dionysus-Zagreus-Sabazios. In private darkness, the Hierophant undresses and kneels before the consecrated goat skull xoanon suspended from a pole or hook, with a lit electric votive candle secured within it. She invokes Dionysus by his chthonic epithets, presses her palms to her eyes in inner vision, then feeds the spirit from her own body by pressing the skull's mouth to her left breast. As the goat feeds, she traces the contours of her own body, affirming its holiness and beauty.

Quantities

Goat skull (consecrated xoanon): 1, suspended from a pole or hook
Electric votive candle: 1, secured inside the skull

Directions

  1. Find a private, safe place, preferably indoors.
  2. Suspend the consecrated goat skull from an upright pole or hook. Secure a lit electric votive candle within the skull.
  3. Completely undress.
  4. Turn off all other lights.
  5. Kneel before the xoanon, raising both hands above your head, and say: "BROMIOS! CHTHONIOS! MYSTES! / Hail, Child who died at twilight! / Hail, Babe born at midnight! / Hail, Youth who shuns the daylight! / Hail, God who roams the dark night!"
  6. Press your palms against your eyes, and say: "Behold, behold, behold! / I see a god, beautiful, bearded, young / Dressed in the skin of a panther; / I see him lift the fennel-wand / To raise the heels of his dancers. / I see a god crowned with vines / Whose hands are red (but not with wine) / Whose black, radiant eyes shine / With the eternal question's eternal answers."
  7. Open your eyes and say: "Hail BROMIOS! Hail CHTHONIOS! Hail MYSTES!"
  8. Take the goat skull and press the point of its mouth against the flesh of your left nipple. Hold it there, pressing rhythmically, while declaring: "Behold, I am a black goat's bride; / Behold, I am a wife! / Behold, I bare a breast to feed / The one whose tongue's a knife. / Take, Lord, from this breast that bleeds, / The sacred stuff of life: / Drink me, Dionysus! Make me / The black goat wife!"
  9. As the goat feeds, let your free hand trace the lineaments of your body, its contours, planes, absences, and excesses, knowing that the act affirms your body's beauty and holiness.
