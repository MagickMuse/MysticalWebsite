👜Protective Bag Angry Ex Person

◇Form
Pouch
◇Method
Herbal MagickSalt MagickChant/MantraProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A dried angelica root or rosemary - Salt and pepper mixed in a twist of silver foil - A small animal or bird bone, or seven black beads - A purple fabric drawstring bag - A few drops of brandy or barley wine
◇Directions
1. Place the angelica or rosemary, salt and pepper, bone or beads, one by one, into the bag. 2. As you add each one, say: "Remain in secrecy. Protectors shall you be. Keep from me all fears and harm, though outside may rage, within me is calm." In silence, sprinkle two or three drops of the alcohol over the contents of the bag, close the bag, and hide it at home. 3. Replace the bag every two months, burying the old one under a dead tree or bush after dark. 4. Perform Saturday, late evening.
◇Description
A Saturday late evening spell for making protective bag if you have to see angry ex because of children, or someone seriously unpleasant who unsettles you, placing angelica, salt, pepper, and bone in purple bag with brandy to remain in secrecy as protectors.
