1. Fumigate the affected space with camphor and mint. 2. If a single application does not resolve the haunting, repeat the working in mystically significant intervals: three, seven, nine, eighteen, twenty-one, or twenty-seven days, as needed.
◇Description
Fumigating a home with camphor and mint drives unwanted ghosts out in search of new housing. A single application may not be sufficient for persistent hauntings. Repetitions in magickally significant numbers are used for reinforcement.
