A flash paper spell to give yourself a second chance after business mistakes, using pine oil to cleanse and renew.

Quantities

  • Flash paper (1 or more pieces)
  • Pine essential oil (undiluted, 1 drop per paper)
  • Fireproof cauldron or pot
  • Lighter or long-necked candle lighter

Directions

  1. Everyone makes mistakes.
  2. We are all human.
  3. To give yourself a second chance, write your mistake on a piece of flash paper.
  4. Be as detailed as you need to be.
  5. You may use multiple pieces if the situation requires it.
  6. Anoint each paper with one drop of undiluted pine oil.
  7. Place the papers into a fireproof cauldron or pot.
  8. With a lighter or long-necked candle lighter, carefully light the flash paper.
  9. There will be a flash of light, but no risk of fire or burns, and no ash to clean up.
  10. The mistake is released to the universe, and you are free to begin again with lessons learned.
