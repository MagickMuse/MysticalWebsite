Galangal

A simple reversal ritual using galangal root to spit back a jinx to its sender, performed by chewing the root while focusing on the sender and then spitting the chewed root in their direction or at a crossroads.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Galangal root: 1 piece

Directions

  1. Take a piece of galangal root and hold it while bringing to mind the person who has jinxed you and the nature of the jinx itself.
  2. Put the galangal root in your mouth and chew it thoroughly while continuing to focus on the sender of the jinx and the harmful magick they have sent your way.
  3. When the root is well chewed, spit it out forcefully in the direction of the person who jinxed you (if you know where they are) or at a crossroads.
  4. As you spit, visualize the jinx traveling back to its sender with the force of your intention.
  5. Walk away without looking back.
