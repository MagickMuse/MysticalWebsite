🔑Hekate Crossroads Petition

An outdoor candlelit petition to Hekate, goddess of the dark moon, crossroads, and keys, performed at a three-way intersection or doorway. Leave an offering at the crossroads, speak the petition aloud, then whistle out the candle flame and walk away without looking back. Watch for signs over the next 24 hours, especially dogs barking. Includes a Hekate's Key Pendulum variation for ongoing divination guidance.

Quantities

  • Candle
  • Matches or lighter
  • Offering for Hekate: 3 garlic cloves, a pomegranate, onion, lavender, or another appropriate offering

Directions

  1. Perform after dark, outdoors if possible.
  2. A three-way crossroads is ideal, a diverging garden path, a T-junction, or even a doorway, all of which fall under Hekate's domain.
  3. If you must work indoors, close your eyes and visualize yourself standing at a crossroads.
  4. Light the candle.
  5. Hold it in your hand the way Hekate holds her torch: a light in the darkness, illuminating the paths that branch before you.
  6. Sit with the image of the crossroads, not as a place of confusion, but as a place of possibility.
  7. Every direction is available to you.
  8. Place your offering in a safe location at the crossroads, garlic, pomegranate, onion, or lavender are all sacred to her.
  9. As you set it down, speak aloud:
  10. "Maiden, mother, and wise crone,
    Hekate of the key and crossroads,
    Who in the dark of night, a torch does hold,
    Reveal to me which way I should go,
    [or: A solution to ___ (name your issue) she does sow.}"
  11. Close your eyes and breathe in slowly, drawing Hekate's power and clarity into yourself.
  12. Then exhale through pursed lips, making a soft whistle as you blow out the candle flame.
  13. Turn and walk away without looking back.
  14. Over the next 24 hours, stay open and watchful.
  15. Hekate rules the dark hours when the moon is not visible, answers may arrive in dreams, in unexpected encounters, or in subtle signs.
  16. Pay particular attention to dogs barking, as dogs are one of Hekate's sacred animals.
  17. ---
  18. Hekate's Key Pendulum: Thread a key onto a length of red string or cord to create a pendulum under her guidance.
  19. Use it for divination when you need clarity at your own crossroads, inviting Hekate to direct your questions and interpret the answers.
