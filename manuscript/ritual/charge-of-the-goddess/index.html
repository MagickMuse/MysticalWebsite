🕯️Charge of the Goddess

◇Directions
1. Speak this traditional Charge during Esbats, particularly at Full Moon, or during Drawing Down the Moon. 2. The High Priestess traditionally speaks as the Goddess: 3. Listen to the words of the Great Mother; She who of old was also called among the people Artemis, Astarte, Cerridwen, Hecate, Demeter, Danu, Ishtar, and many other names: 4. Whenever ye have need of any thing, once in the month, and better it be when the Moon is full, then shall ye assemble in some quiet place and adore the Spirit of me, who am Queen of all Witcheries. 5. And thou who thinkest to seek for me, know thy seeking and yearning shall avail thee not unless thou knowest the mystery: 6. That if that which thou seekest thou findest not within thee, thou wilt never find it without thee. 7. For behold! 8. I have been with thee from the beginning, and I am that which is attained at the end of desire.
◇Description
The traditional Charge of the Goddess by Charles Leland, Gerald Gardner, and Doreen Valiente. Invokes the Great Mother by her many names: Artemis, Astarte, Cerridwen, Hecate, Demeter, Danu, and Ishtar. Instructs practitioners to assemble when the Moon is full and contains the central mystery that what is sought must be found within.
