🛡️Apostles Courtcase Decision Sage Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Christian
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Visualization
◇Category
Spell

Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- 12 sage leaves
◇Directions
1. Write the name of one of the eleven apostles, excluding Judas Iscariot, on each of eleven individual sage leaves. 2. Place all eleven inside your right shoe. 3. On a twelfth sage leaf, write the name of a biblical or literary figure who most closely reflects your own circumstances. 4. For example, choose Tamar for a disenfranchised former spouse, Judith if acting in defense against an abuser, Gideon for justifiable homicide, or Jezebel if slandered and defamed. 5. If no figure suits your situation, leave the twelfth leaf blank. 6. Place this final leaf inside your left shoe, kept separate from the eleven.
◇Description
A Pow-Wow sage-based court case spell that allows the caster to direct the outcome toward either full justice or a divided verdict, depending on their situation. Eleven apostles are written on individual sage leaves for the right shoe, while a twelfth leaf in the left shoe carries the name of a figure who mirrors the caster's own circumstances. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
