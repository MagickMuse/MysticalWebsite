💖Attracting New Love

◇Quantities
- Sheet of paper - Pen (preferably pink or red ink, black also acceptable) - Red pillar candle - Matches - Firesafe candleholder (small plate, ramekin, or ceramic coaster) - Optional: Carving tool (tweezers head, pin, pocketknife tip, or small point) - Optional: Carrier oil (olive oil, coconut oil, or avocado oil, about a quarter-sized dollop) - Optional: Essential oils (rose, sweet orange, lavender, or premade love blend) - Optional: Dried herbs and spices (rosehips, ground cinnamon, vanilla, cardamom, sugar) - Optional: Crystals (rose quartz, clear quartz, larimar, moonstone, carnelian, or others) - Optional: Selenite or Florida Water for cleansing crystals - Optional: Tarot card
◇Directions
1. Begin by writing a detailed list of your ideal partner's qualities on paper. 2. Use complete sentences and be as descriptive as possible, including physical descriptions, personality traits, and any other desires. 3. Paint a vivid picture of your ideal partner, inside and out. 4. Store this paper somewhere safe. 5. Once your list is complete, decide when to light your candle for the first time. 6. Ideally, choose a Friday during the New Moon or waxing Moon. 7. Regardless of moon phase, the first lighting should be on a Friday, the day associated with Venus, which rules love and pleasure. 8. Avoid Fridays during Mercury retrograde or eclipses. 9. On the chosen Friday, prepare the space where you will light your candle. 10. This may be your altar or another flat surface such as a kitchen counter. 11. Ensure the surface is clean and clear of clutter. 12. The space should not be disturbed by pets or family members, as the candle should not be moved once lit. 13. If using magickal extras, prepare them now. 14. For carving, do so before anything else while the wax is dry and unblemished. 15. Carve words that represent your love spell, such as a specific quality you seek, a reminder like "my person," or simply the word "love." Use the head of tweezers, a pin, the tip of a pocketknife, or another small point. 16. For carrier oil, place a dollop about the size of a quarter in your dominant hand's palm. 17. Wrap your hand around the top of the candle and slide down toward the base, smearing oil as you go. 18. Twist your hand as you pull to coat the candle evenly. 19. For essential oils, add a few drops to your carrier oil before dressing the candle. 20. If you've already dressed the candle or are not using carrier oil, sprinkle a few drops of essential oil directly on the wax. 21. For herbs and spices, sprinkle them on a plate and roll the oil-dressed candle across the plate once or twice. 22. The oil will hold the dried ingredients. 23. For crystals, cleanse them first using Full Moon light, Florida Water, or selenite pieces, then arrange them around the base of your candle. 24. For tarot cards, select a card that represents love or the kind of person you are calling in. Place the card near your candle. 25. Avoid cards not conducive to healthy relationships. 26. Place the prepared candle and any magickal extras on your altar or chosen flat surface. 27. Locate your list of ideal partner qualities and matches. 28. Take these to your candle, then kneel or stand in front of it. 29. Set the matches aside and read your list out loud from start to finish. 30. Take your time and speak clearly. 31. Once finished, fold the paper and place it underneath the candleholder. 32. Pick up the matches. 33. As you strike the match, say: 34. > My ideal partner is on their way to me. 35. I am ready, I am waiting, for us to finally meet. 36. Light your candle's wick. 37. Let the candle burn for as long as possible, with a minimum of one hour. 38. Stay near the candle to ensure it burns safely. 39. Never leave the house or fall asleep while a candle is lit. 40. When you need to leave or head to bed, extinguish the flame using a snuffer, your breath, or the pads of your fingers. 41. As the wick's smoke wafts, say aloud: 42. > And so it is. 43. Leave the candle and all magickal extras exactly as they are. 44. If the candle is in a heavily trafficked area, gently move the items to another flat surface for storage. 45. On subsequent Fridays, relight your candle following the same steps: read through the list of ideal qualities, strike a match and say the spell, let the flame burn as long as possible, and end with "And so it is." Continue this practice until you burn through the candle or meet someone special. 46. When the candle has done its job, give it a proper burial. 47. Keep the candle somewhere safe until you can bury it. 48. Dig a small hole in the ground on your property, or bury it in a large potted plant or a place sacred to you. 49. Thank the candle for its efforts. 50. If you decide to light another candle for your love life, start fresh with a new list from scratch. 51. Use the same ingredients if it feels right, or switch things up with different magickal extras. 52. As the kind of partner you seek may change over time, let your magick change too.
◇Description
A multi-week candle spell to attract a romantic partner with specific desired qualities. Uses a red pillar candle lit on Fridays during favorable moon phases, with optional enhancements like carved words, carrier oils, essential oils, herbs, crystals, and tarot cards. The spell emphasizes respecting free will by not targeting a specific person.

This candle spell respects free will by not targeting a specific person. Each person was born with the power of free will, and magickally ensnaring someone takes that free will away. If the person you desire is the right one for you, a general love spell will still invite them into your life. The universe works on its own timeline. This spell may bring results quickly or may take time. If it takes a while for someone special to materialize, do not be discouraged. Keep working your magick and putting yourself out there to give the universe ample opportunity to ensure the two of you cross paths.

Magickal Extras

There are many optional ingredients that can boost your candle magick. Each one adds another layer of power to the working.

Carving gives the candle a clear directive. Carve while the wax is dry and unblemished, before doing anything else to the candle. Use words that represent your love spell, such as a specific quality you seek, a reminder like "my person," or simply the word "love." Use the head of tweezers, a pin, the tip of a pocketknife, or another small point.

Carrier oil dresses the candle. Use olive oil, coconut oil, or avocado oil. Place a dollop about the size of a quarter in your dominant hand's palm, wrap your hand around the top of the candle, and slide down toward the base, smearing oil as you go. Twist your hand as you pull to coat the candle evenly.

Essential oils scent the candle and deepen the working. Add a few drops to your carrier oil before dressing the candle, or sprinkle directly on the wax if no carrier oil is used. Rose is wonderful for love, though sweet orange, lavender, or a premade love blend also work well.

Herbs and spices adhere to an oil-dressed candle. Sprinkle dried rosehips, ground cinnamon, vanilla, or cardamom on a plate and roll the dressed candle across it once or twice. Add a little sugar since you're looking for someone sweet.

Crystals arranged around the base of your candle strengthen the magick. Rose quartz, clear quartz, larimar, and moonstone are excellent for love spells. For something more specific, carnelian draws a brave partner. Cleanse crystals before use with Full Moon light, Florida Water, or selenite.

Tarot cards placed near the candle invite in that energy. Select a card related to love, such as the Lovers or the Two of Cups, or one that represents the kind of person you are calling in. Use cards that point toward a healthy, happy relationship.

Timing and Repetition

Light the candle for the first time on a Friday during the New Moon or waxing Moon. Friday is the day of the week associated with Venus, which rules love and pleasure. Avoid Fridays during Mercury retrograde or eclipses. On subsequent weeks, relight your candle on Fridays until the candle burns through or you meet someone special.

Disposal

When the candle has done its job, give it a proper burial. Keep the candle somewhere safe until you can bury it. Dig a small hole in the ground on your property, or bury it in a large potted plant or a place sacred to you. Thank the candle for its efforts. If you light another candle for your love life later, start fresh with a new list and choose whether to use the same or different magickal extras.
