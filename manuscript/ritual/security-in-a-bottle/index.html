🫙Security in a Bottle

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
ProtectionBlood Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 glass jar with a tight-fitting lid Sharp objects (glass, nails, razors, pins, needles, splinters, etc.) Personal taglock (hair, nail clippings, or blood) Urine (semen and menstrual blood optional) Tape or wax to seal the jar
◇Directions
1. Fill the jar halfway with sharp objects such as glass shards, nails, razors, pins, needles, and splinters. 2. Add your personal taglocks: hair, nail clippings, or blood. 3. If desired, add menstrual blood or semen. 4. Fill the remainder of the jar with your urine. 5. Screw the lid on tightly and seal it with tape or wax. 6. Bury the bottle approximately one foot deep as close to your front door as possible. 7. If burial is not an option, hide the jar out of sight in the back of a closet or cabinet. 8. The bottle will protect you from anyone who wishes ill upon you.
◇Description
A classic witch's bottle for personal protection. The jar is filled with sharp objects to impale hostile intentions, personal taglocks to link the bottle to the caster, and bodily fluids to power the working. Sealed and buried near the front door or hidden in a closet, the bottle defends against all enemies, known and unknown.
