🔓Cutting the Cord with a Curser

An Ozark curse-breaking ritual that severs the sympathetic cord connecting a cursed person back to their curser. Based on the law of contagion, this working invokes the spirit Scissorbill, a giant heron with a sharp beak who cuts magickal cords. This ritual can be performed for yourself or on behalf of another, and does not require knowing the identity of the curser. In Ozark healing theory, severing this cord is essential before any other cleansing can take effect.

Quantities

  • 7 black strings, organic material, 2 feet long each
  • 2-inch square of black paper
  • Black pen or marker
  • Identifying materials from target (hair, nail clippings, clothing, etc.; optional)
  • 2-inch square of white paper
  • Black taper candle, 6 inches or more, with holder
  • White taper candle, 6 inches or more, with holder
  • Lighter or matches
  • Knife
  • Red taper candle, any length (optional)
  • White cloth for wrapping

Directions

  1. Perform this ritual over a heat-safe surface.
  2. Begin by creating your magickal cord that will represent the connection between yourself and your curser, or between the person you are working for and the one who cursed them.
  3. Group all 7 of the black strings together.
  4. The left end will be for the curser, and the right for the one to be healed.
  5. On the small black paper square, write the curser's full name (FIRST MIDDLE LAST) as well as their birthdate and the zodiac symbols for their Sun, Moon, and rising signs, if you know that information.
  6. You can also attach any identifying materials from them to the paper using wax from the black candle.
  7. Roll this into a small tube, then on the left end of the 7 strings, make 3 tight knots around the paper tube.
  8. It should be sealed tight in the knots and should not fall out easily.
  9. Repeat this on the right side with the white paper square that represents you or the one you are healing.
  10. Make sure to include their full name, birthdate, and zodiac signs, as well as any identifying materials that will be stuck to the paper using wax from the white candle.
  11. You can also use a small photo instead of the paper for both the curser and the one to be healed.
  12. Repeat the first part of the verbal charm:
  13. Sealed, stuck, tied down
    What can we do?
    Scissorbill, fly down
    And cut them in two!
    Fire, fire, leap up too
    Make [NAME or ME] whole again
    Make me something new!
    Something new that's free from pain
    Free from curses and cords too!
  14. Next, tie the two ends of the strings to the middle of the two taper candles in their holders.
  15. The left side with the curser should be tied to the black candle and the one to be healed to the white candle.
  16. Place the candles apart enough to pull the cord relatively tight.
  17. Light the candles.
  18. Take your knife and heat the tip of the blade on the white candle.
  19. Recite part two of the verbal charm while it is heating:
  20. Scissorbill, fire, knife
    Cuts cords like nothing else!
    What I cut is cut forever
    Take back your curse
    Take back your trouble
    Take back the cord you wove!
    Knots for love
    No knots for cursing
    Go to the ground, curser
    Go and be healed too
  21. When the knife is hot enough, quickly touch it to the middle of the cord and let the knife burn through.
  22. It might take a couple of tries if it was not hot enough.
  23. Once it is cut through, blow out any strings that might have ignited.
  24. You can also use a red taper candle instead of a knife.
  25. In this case, light the red candle on the white candle, then use it to burn apart the string.
  26. Finish by taking the black candle with half of the cord still attached and burying it outside, preferably in a graveyard, but a four-way crossroads or in the roots of a dead tree will also work.
  27. Wrap the white candle and other half of the string in white cloth and hide it away.
  28. As long as you still have it wrapped up, the curser will not be able to curse you again.
