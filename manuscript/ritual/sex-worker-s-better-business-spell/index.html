A traditional charm bag from sex worker folk magick, adapted for anyone whose business relies on personal contact. A lodestone dressed with basil, bergamot, and lavender oils is enhanced with Come To Me, Follow Me Boy, and/or Attraction Oils, then sprinkled with magnetic sand and cinnamon and carried or worn.

Quantities

  • Lodestone
  • Essential oil of basil
  • Essential oil of bergamot
  • Essential oil of lavender
  • Come To Me Oil and/or Follow Me Boy Oil and/or Attraction Oil
  • Magnetic sand
  • Ground cinnamon

Directions

  1. Dress a lodestone with essential oils of basil, bergamot, and lavender.
  2. Add Come To Me, Follow Me Boy, and/or Attraction Oils.
  3. Sprinkle with magnetic sand and a pinch of ground cinnamon.
  4. Carry or wear to draw business.
