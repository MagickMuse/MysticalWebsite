🕯️Night Shielding

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PrayerCandle Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 candle
◇Directions
1. Perform this in the evening before bed. 2. Turn off the lights in your home. 3. Light a single candle. 4. Carry the candle from window to window throughout your home. 5. At each window, see the flame reflected in the dark glass: this is Brigid's protection shining outward into the night. 6. As you walk, speak the shielding prayer: 7. "Be the encompassing of Brigid around me From every spectre, from every evil, From every shade that is coming harmfully In darkness, in power to hurt. Be the encompassing of the strength of Brigid Shielding me from every harm, Be keeping me from everything ruinous Coming destructively toward me this night." 8. If you wish to be thorough in the Celtic tradition (which names specific beings to close every loophole), add: 9. "Be the encompassing of Brigid around me From every firbolg and Formorian, From all ballybogs and buachailleens, From howlers and the Hag of the Dribble, From púca and from Fin Bheara, King of the Faery Dead." 10. When you have moved through all the windows, what belongs inside is in and what belongs outside is out. 11. Extinguish the candle and go to bed.
◇Description
A candle-carrying ritual for evening protection, moving from window to window to establish Brigid's shielding around the home. Includes two traditional prayers, one general and one naming specific Celtic otherworldly beings by name.
