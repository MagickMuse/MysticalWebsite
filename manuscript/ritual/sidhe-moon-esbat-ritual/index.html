🌕Esbat

A complete standalone Esbat ritual for the Sidhe Moon (the second Dark Moon in a solar month), including its own widdershins circle casting, Otherworld-oriented invocations, Simple Feast of Fairy Tea and Cookies, and circle opening. Requires advance preparation and dietary adjustments the day of the ritual.

Quantities

  • Altar Setup
  • White, purple, or gray altar cloth
  • 3 altar candles: gray or pale lavender outer, lighter grayish-white center
  • Gray or lavender pillar working candle
  • Wand of elder, hazel, or hawthorn
  • Bowl of spring water
  • Bowl of burdock root
  • Incense (earthy, green, or misty scent)
  • Libation bowl with flower petals
  • Bowl of milk
  • Bowl of crumbled mugwort
  • Athame
  • Candle snuffer
  • Tiny bell (optional)
    Quarter Markers
  • North and South: pale green crystals or candles
  • East and West: pale gray crystals or candles
    Simple Feast
  • Teapot and kettle
  • Teacup and saucer
  • Sweetener and milk
  • Plate of tea cookies
  • Two place settings if doing Companion Quest
    Fairy Tea Recipe
  • 3 teaspoons black tea
  • 1/2 teaspoon chamomile
  • 1 teaspoon dandelion root
  • 1/2 teaspoon elder flower
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons hops
  • 1/2 teaspoon mugwort
  • 1/2 teaspoon raspberry leaf
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons rose hips
    Ritual Bath Herbs
  • Lavender, linden, marigold, rosemary in an herb bag
  • Patchouli incense for the room
  • 1 to 3 gray candles for lighting

Directions

  1. This Esbat requires more advance preparation than others.
  2. Perform on the second Dark Moon in a solar month.
  3. The entire ritual is Otherworld-oriented with Fairy references throughout.
  4. Day-Of Preparation
  5. During the day, avoid eating meat, poultry, or seafood.
  6. Instead, eat roots, tubers, vegetables, soy, or dairy products.
  7. Preparation Before Circle
  8. Arrange altar or table so you face West.
  9. Cover with white, purple, or gray cloth.
  10. Set out all materials as listed in Quantities.
  11. Ground and center.
  12. Bathe with an herb bag of lavender, linden, marigold, and rosemary in a room scented with patchouli incense and lit by 1 to 3 gray candles.
  13. Dry off and robe lightly or remain skyclad.
