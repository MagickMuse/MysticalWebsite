🛡️Tongue Control Crossroads Powder Spell

◇Form
Powder
◇Method
ProtectionPetitionPowder Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 pinch white sugar - 1 pinch flour - 1 pinch fine ground salt
◇Directions
1. Blend a pinch each of white sugar, flour, and fine ground salt together in your palm. 2. Hold the powder and visualize your desired outcome clearly. 3. If you wish to call upon spiritual assistance, petition a deity of your choosing. 4. Michael and Ogun, both warriors who despise liars, are recommended, as is Ma'at, Egyptian dispenser of ultimate justice. 5. Chant: "I am innocent of these accusations! [Deity's name] Protect me!" Release the powder and allow it to scatter in the wind. 6. For maximum potency, perform this working standing at the center of a crossroads. 7. A window is a suitable alternative. 8. A sudden gust of wind during the casting is a highly auspicious omen.
◇Description
A powder spell cast at a crossroads or window to counter gossip, slander, and false testimony. The three-ingredient powder is held, charged with intention, and released into the wind. Michael, Ogun, and Ma'at are each recommended as patron spirits for this working, as warriors and dispensers of justice who despise lies.
