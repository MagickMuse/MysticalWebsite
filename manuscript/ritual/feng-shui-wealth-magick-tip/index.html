🏠Home✨Manifestation💰Money💰Prosperity💵Wealth

A simple feng shui spell using tarot to attract prosperity. By positioning a wealth-related card in your home's Wealth Gua, you create a visual anchor for financial abundance.

Quantities

  • 1 tarot card representing prosperity (from a deck you don't use for readings)

Directions

  1. Select a tarot card that symbolizes prosperity to you (use one from a deck reserved for spellwork).
  2. Display this card in the Wealth Gua of your home.
  3. Finding your Wealth Gua: Enter through your main door and face inward.
  4. The Wealth Gua occupies the far left sector of your living space.
