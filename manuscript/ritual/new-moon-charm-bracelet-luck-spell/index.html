Pink Candle

A New Moon candle ritual to charge a chosen charm with focused intention, then attach it to a bracelet worn as an accumulating luck talisman. Goals may be swapped freely by removing and adding charms.

Quantities

Charm representing your goal
Small pink candle
Bracelet

Directions

  1. Choose a charm that symbolizes the goal sought.
  2. At the New Moon, set the charm beside a small pink candle.
  3. Charge the candle with the desired intention, carving and dressing it as preferred, and burn it completely.
  4. Once the candle is spent, attach the charm to a bracelet and wear it, or store it safely.
  5. When the goal is achieved, choose a new intention, select a fresh charm, and begin again at the next New Moon.
  6. Any charm whose purpose has changed may simply be removed.
