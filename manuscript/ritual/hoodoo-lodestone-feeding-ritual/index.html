⚡Hoodoo Lodestone Feeding Ritual

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
ConsecrationEnergy Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
- 1 lodestone - Condition oil of choice (e.g., love-drawing oil for romance, money oil for prosperity), or substitute with essential oil, true oil, or a few drops of whiskey - Magnetic sand (iron filings)
◇Directions
1. Select a condition oil that aligns with the outcome you want the lodestone to attract. 2. If you are working toward a new romance, choose a love-drawing blend. 3. For financial goals, use a prosperity or money oil. 4. Dress the lodestone thoroughly with the oil, coating all surfaces. 5. Once the stone is anointed, sprinkle magnetic sand over it and allow the filings to cling to the oiled surface. 6. If condition oils are unavailable, essential oils, true botanical oils, or a splash of whiskey serve as acceptable alternatives.
◇Description
A Hoodoo method for nourishing a lodestone by dressing it with condition oils chosen to match the practitioner's intention, then finishing with a dusting of magnetic sand. The oil selection tailors the lodestone's drawing power toward a specific goal. ⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This ritual is explicitly rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Hoodoo is open but not open for appropriation. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.
