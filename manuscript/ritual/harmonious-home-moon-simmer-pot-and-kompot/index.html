🍎Harmonious Home Moon Simmer Pot and Kompot

◇Form
Potion
◇Method
LunarDivinationHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 apple - Peeler - Pot - 3 cups Moon Water or purified/spring water - Knife - 1/8 tsp ground nutmeg - 1 pear, sliced - 1 small lemon, sliced - 3 bay leaves - 2 cinnamon sticks - 1/3 cup granulated sugar (optional, for kompot)
◇Directions
1. Hold your apple and bring your intention or question to mind. 2. Begin peeling it in one long, continuous strip, try to keep it unbroken, but if it snaps, that is fine. 3. As you peel, let the question or intention settle in your hands and the fruit. 4. With your eyes closed or your gaze turned over your opposite shoulder, toss the peel into the pot. 5. Open your eyes and observe where and how it lands: look for shapes, letters, or symbols in the curl and curl of the peel, and receive whatever meaning comes. 6. Thank the peel for its message. 7. Fill the pot with Moon Water. 8. Slice and add the apple, then add the nutmeg, pear slices, lemon slices, and bay leaves. 9. Stir clockwise using one of the cinnamon sticks, visualizing warmth, harmony, and your moon-blessed intention radiating outward from the pot and filling every corner of your home. 10. Place the pot on the stove and bring to a gentle simmer over low heat. 11. Let the fragrance build and spread through your space. 12. Breathe it in consciously, this is the blessing entering your home. 13. Once the aroma has fully saturated the space and you feel the spell is complete, turn off the heat. 14. You may strain out the solids and keep the fragrant water as a ritual offering, pour it at the base of a tree or onto the earth with gratitude. 15. Or, let the pot cool slightly and look into the leftover ingredients for further divination: any shapes, patterns, or imagery that arise. 16. To make kompot: once roughly 1 cup of liquid has evaporated during simmering, stir in the sugar. 17. Return to a gentle boil and let cook for 30 more minutes, until the pear and apple are fully soft. 18. Strain, cool, and serve as a warming, spell-infused drink to share with your household.
◇Description
A lunar kitchen spell that fills the home with a warm, spiced aroma enchanted for harmony, blessing, and luck. Apple peel divination opens the working; the ingredients, apple, pear, lemon, bay, cinnamon, and nutmeg, are stirred clockwise with intention into Moon Water and brought to a gentle simmer. The finished liquid can be saved as an offering or used to divine from the remaining solids. With the optional addition of sugar, the pot transforms into a traditional Eastern European kompot drink, making this a rare spell that nourishes both the home's energy and its inhabitants.
