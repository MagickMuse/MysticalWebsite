These tea potions attract love and make you receptive to romantic possibilities. Five variations are provided.

Ethical Note: Remember that it is ethical to invite love into your life, but not ethical to use magick to try to force someone to love you. The Goddess of Love often has a cruel sense of humor, so don't tempt her by misusing love spells or potions.

Version 1: Peppermint and Rose Hip

Brew peppermint tea and rose hip tea together according to the Tea Potion Spell.

Version 2: Lemon Balm and Rose

Brew lemon balm tea and rose tea together. Rose is the classic flower of love, enhanced by lemon balm's gentle heart-opening properties.

Version 3: Strawberry Leaf and Lemon Balm

Brew strawberry leaf tea and lemon balm tea together. Strawberry's sweet energy attracts romance.

Version 4: Jasmine with Vanilla

Brew jasmine tea according to the Tea Potion Spell. Add one scant drop of pure vanilla extract. Jasmine's seductive lunar energy combines with vanilla's sweetness.

Version 5: Black Tea with Cinnamon and Rose Quartz

Brew black tea according to the Tea Potion Spell. Stir with a cinnamon stick and place a small rose quartz in the cup. DO NOT INGEST THE CRYSTAL. After drinking, wash the stone and carry it with you as a love charm.

Optional Enhancement

Using a pink or orange cup or mug strengthens the love-drawing energy.

Stirring the Rune

When you stir any of these potions, first stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the shape of Gebo into the surface of the tea.

The Working

As you drink, visualize yourself open and receptive to love. See yourself radiating warmth and attraction, drawing compatible partners to you through natural magnetism.
