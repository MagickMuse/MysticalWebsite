Brigid

A five-step toning and silence practice that clears mind-noise and opens space to hear Brigid's guidance.

Quantities

No materials required

Directions

  1. Sit comfortably somewhere you feel free to make some noise. 2. Close your eyes and envision a flame glowing at the center of your chest. 3. Intone a low single-syllable sound, starting softly and gradually increasing in volume, until you feel submerged in the sound. 4. Stop. 5. Keep your eyes closed and let the silence that follows the sound wash over you; open your eyes when you feel complete.

Modern life is loud, not only with the sound of machines, traffic, and other invasive noise, but with a constant barrage of mind-noise that makes it hard to find the silence needed to hear Brigid's guidance and the wisdom of the soul. This practice offers a simple way to feel the power of that silence.

  1. Sit comfortably in a place and at a time when you feel free to make some noise.
  2. Close your eyes. Focus your awareness in the center of your chest. Envision a flame glowing there.
  3. Begin to intone a low single-syllable sound, such as Aum (Om), Ma, or Ah. Start softly and gradually increase the volume. Take deep breaths so you can hold the note as long as possible. Keep intoning until you feel surrounded and submerged in the sound, to the point where you aren't consciously making the sound, it's just emerging from you, louder and louder.
  4. And then stop.
  5. Keep your eyes closed and feel the shimmering energy of the silence that follows sound. Don't direct your thoughts; just let the silence wash over you. When you feel complete, open your eyes.
