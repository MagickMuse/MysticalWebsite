An ancient Monday sea spell for the return of a lover who has left, using water, a gold offering, and a glass bottle to call upon Lady Ocean to bring your love back home.

Quantities

  • The sea, a tidal river, or any flowing water
  • A gold earring, thin gold ring, or gold-colored coin
  • An empty glass bottle with a screw-top lid or cork

Directions

  1. Visit the water source before twilight.
  2. Cast the gold into the water as an offering to the benign guardian essences of the water.
  3. Scoop some water in the bottle, and as you do so, say: "Lady Ocean, Mother Sea, I take of yours not willingly, but as a token of what I lack. I ask your help to bring him/her back." (Use sea even if you're using a stream, for ultimately all water ends in the sea.) Hold it and call your true love's name nine times and say: "Lady Ocean, Mother Sea, carry him/her home in love to me." Tip the water back into the water source, saying: "Lady Ocean, Mother Sea, I return what is yours; return mine to me." Go home and make contact.
