💍Ring

A ring of invisibility attributed to Abbot Trithemius of Spandau (1462–1516). Must be crafted from amber-colored electrum (a natural silver-gold alloy) at the exact birth moment of the intended wearer, and inscribed with the Hebrew Tetragrammaton. Worn on the left thumb for invisibility; on any other finger it allegedly antidotes poisons and changes color in the presence of enemies.

Quantities

Amber-colored electrum (natural silver-gold alloy)
Knowledge of wearer's exact birth time
Hebrew Tetragrammaton inscription

Directions

  1. Craft the ring from amber-colored electrum (a natural alloy of silver and gold favored by the ancient Egyptians).
  2. Requirements: The ring must be cast at the precise time the person wishing to wear it was born.
  3. It must be inscribed with the Hebrew letters of the Tetragrammaton (the four letters of the Ineffable Name).
  4. Wear the ring on the left thumb to provide invisibility.
  5. When not seeking invisibility, wear it on any other finger: it allegedly antidotes poisons and changes color in the presence of enemies.
