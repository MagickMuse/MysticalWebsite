A Russian folk spell that appeases Herod's twelve fever-demon daughters by paying them off with twelve individually wrapped pies left at a crossroads. May be repeated as the situation demands.

Quantities

Twelve pies (size your choice)
Fabric for wrapping (twelve pieces)

Directions

  1. Bake twelve pies in a size of your choosing and wrap each one individually in fabric.
  2. Take them to a crossroads and leave them there, saying: "Here you go, twelve daughters! This is for you. Now leave me alone." Depart without attracting the attention of anyone.
  3. Repeat as the situation demands.
