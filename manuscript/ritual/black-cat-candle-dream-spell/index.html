🐈‍⬛Black Cat Candle Dream Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteWriting SpellBath
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickDreamworkHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• Black cat candle • Small slip of paper and pen • Acacia gum or gum resin (to attach paper if needed) • Dream-inducing oils (for bath) • Dream-stimulating tea
◇Directions
1. Fully prepare your bedroom before beginning so that you can go immediately to sleep once the candle is extinguished. 2. Hold the black cat candle in your hands and clearly consider the specific dream you need. 3. Write down your dream request succinctly and clearly on a small slip of paper. 4. Slip the paper into the cat's mouth. 5. If the mouth is sealed with wax, use a knife to cut a slit, or attach the paper with a little oil and acacia gum. 6. Charge the cat candle with its mission and light it. 7. While the candle burns, take a relaxing bath using dream-inducing oils. 8. Drink a dream-stimulating tea. 9. Keep an eye on the candle. 10. Once the paper has been completely reduced to ash, pinch the flame out and go immediately to bed. 11. To revisit the same dream or dream issue: use the same candle. 12. If the candle's surface has flattened, rest a new paper slip atop the wax. 13. If not, attach it with gum resin or cut a fresh slit. 14. For a completely new dream or a new situation: begin with a new black cat candle.
◇Description
A dream incubation spell for when you know exactly what kind of dream you need. A black cat candle is charged with the desired dream, a paper request is inserted into the cat's mouth, and the candle burns while the caster takes a dream-inducing bath and tea. When the paper has turned to ash, the candle is pinched out and the caster goes directly to sleep. The same candle may be reused for the same dream issue; a new candle begins a new working.
