🌍Mother Earth Protection for Child

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Chant/MantraInvocationProtectionElemental
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Soil from garden or planter near front door
◇Directions
1. As the child walks down the path/the steps/through the front gate, throw a handful of earth after him (subtly), saying, "May Mother Earth protect you until safely you return. My shield of care guards you everywhere. Blessed may you be." Brush the soil off your hands over the ground or planter, repeating the words and say, "Mother Earth, protect my child who is your own." Perform when the child of any age leaves the house for a challenging day.
◇Description
A daily earth blessing ritual throwing soil after a child leaving for challenging days or trips, invoking Mother Earth's protection.
