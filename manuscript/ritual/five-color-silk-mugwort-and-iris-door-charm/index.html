A door charm of mugwort and iris bound together with silk thread in five colors. Hung over door hinges, it turns away malicious spirits and harmful magick.

Quantities

Mugwort
Iris
Silk thread in five different colors

Directions

  1. Gather mugwort and iris.
  2. Bind them together, wrapping the bundle with silk thread in five different colors.
  3. Hang the bundle above the door hinges to keep malicious spirits and harmful magick away.

Lore

The fifth day of the fifth Chinese month marks the Dragon Boat Festival, a date that coincides with the Summer Solstice and Midsummer's Eve. The doubled five is no accident, since five ranks among the chief numbers of protection.
