💚Heart to Heart Absent Healing Spell

A Friday heart-to-heart absent healing spell for a person or animal with close emotional connection, using healing crystal held to heart to send cord of healing from heart to heart.

Quantities

  • Your healing crystal
  • A green candle (optional)

Directions

  1. Polish the crystal with a soft white cloth, saying: "My love for you as healing I send my care for you. And so I send from my heart to yours the cord of healing willing, true." Hold the crystal to your heart with both hands.
  2. Say the same words.
  3. Call the person's name three times softly.
  4. When you feel the energy flowing freely between your heart and the crystal in a two-way direction, hold the crystal outstretched to a natural source of light or green candle, repeating the words softly and continuously.
  5. When you sense healing is complete, allow the connection to fade and put the crystal down on the table to break the connection between you, but very gently.
  6. Healing is complete.
  7. Polish the crystal gently.
  8. Perform on Fridays.
