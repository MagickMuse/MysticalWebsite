Tonka

A pair of tonka beans dressed with love-drawing oil on a New Moon Friday, placed in a charm bag in a secure location and re-anointed every Friday to keep the bond unbreakable.

Quantities

Pair of tonka beans
Love-drawing oil
Charm bag

Directions

  1. On a New Moon Friday, dress a pair of tonka beans with a love-drawing oil.
  2. Place them together in a charm bag and keep it in a safe, secure place.
  3. Re-anoint the beans with oil every Friday, ideally together with your partner.
  4. Nothing should break you apart.
