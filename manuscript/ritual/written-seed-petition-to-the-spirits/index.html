🔥Written Seed Petition to the Spirits

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickPetition
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
Cumin seeds Coriander seeds Paper and envelope
◇Directions
1. Chew coriander and cumin seeds while composing a written letter to the Spirits. 2. Use the same format as in the verbal petition: invoke their protection, name the patient and their parent, and ask for the release of any harm caused. 3. Do not stop chewing; if the seeds are fully chewed, keep them in your mouth. 4. Place the letter in an envelope. 5. Spit the seeds out and lick the envelope to seal it with them. 6. Burn the sealed letter together with fresh coriander and cumin seeds.
◇Description
A distance variant of the cumin and coriander djinn petition (Spell 3), performed without the patient's presence. The healer chews seeds while composing a written petition to the spirits using the same incantation format, seals the envelope by licking it with seeds in the mouth, then burns the letter with fresh seeds.
