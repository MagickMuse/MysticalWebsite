⚔️Overcoming An Enemy

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAnointing
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 black 7-knob candle (or a black candle marked into 7 equal segments) Cursing Oil
◇Directions
1. Anoint the candle with oil starting at the center and working toward the bottom or tip, always rubbing the oil away from yourself as if to cast the enemy away. 2. Imagine the enemy and overcoming them. 3. Speak aloud the curses and what you wish to happen to them. 4. Light the candle. 5. Burn it for seven days, allowing only one segment to burn at a time. 6. Once the candle has burned down completely, dispose of the remnants by burying them in a cemetery, on the enemy's property, or tossing them in a public trash can. 7. Never your own.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A seven-day candle curse using a black 7-knob candle anointed with oil. The caster speaks curses aloud while visualizing the enemy's downfall. One knob is burned per day for seven days. Remnants are disposed of at a cemetery, on the target's property, or in a public trash can.
