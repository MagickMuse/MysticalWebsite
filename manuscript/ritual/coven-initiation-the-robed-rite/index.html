🕯️Coven Initiation: The Robed Rite

A ceremonial coven initiation performed robed, aligned with the knowledge-and-power aspect of the Craft. Uses the four elemental watchtowers for purification and consecration of the candidate. Ideally performed at one of the grand Sabbats (Beltane or Hallows), with the full coven present.

Quantities

  • Cingulum (witches' cord) for binding the candidate's hands and neck
  • Short red cord for binding the ankles
  • Consecrated salt
  • Coin (symbolic earth pentacle, placed between the candidate's lips)
  • Chalice of pure water
  • Athame or coven sword (for the challengers and oath)
  • Censer with Cernunnos Incense (for the south/fire quarter)
  • Blindfold (hoodwink)
  • Coven workbook / Book of Shadows (for signing the register)
  • Sterilized needle or lock of the candidate's hair (for the bond token)
  • Coven witch jewel for investiture (pendant, garter, bracelet, necklace, or ring)
  • Sabbat Oil and wine (optional, for closing consecration)
  • Chafing dish or balefire (already burning as part of the Sabbat circle)

Directions

PREPARATION: The Sabbat circle is already cast and consecrated with four Watchtowers. The grand master wears his horned helmet or animal mask with the lit candle or torch at its crown. All coven members stand within the circle. The candidate is led by the summoner to the northern perimeter, already robed, fully blindfolded, and divested of all metal artifacts.

NORTH -- EARTH: A coven member touches the candidate on the breast with an athame or sword and issues the challenge:

Challenger: Whence come you?
Candidate: From the north, the place of greatest darkness.
Challenger: Whither goest thou?
Candidate: I travel east in search of light.
Challenger: What passwords dost thou bring?
Candidate: Perfect love and perfect trust.
Challenger: I, the guardian of the watchtower of the north, forbid thee entrance. Thou canst not enter this holy place from the north, save thou first be purified and consecrated. Who vouches for you?
Summoner: I, Guide of Souls, so do.
Challenger: Child of Darkness, approach thou the watchtower of the north and receive of me the bonds of death and blessing of earth!

The candidate's hands are tied behind the back with the cingulum, which is drawn up and tied around the neck with the free end hanging as a cable-tow. A short red cord is bound loosely around both ankles, leaving the feet neither bond nor free. A few grains of consecrated salt are sprinkled on the forehead, and a coin is placed between the lips.

WEST -- WATER: The summoner leads the candidate deosil around the full outer perimeter of the circle to the west. The coin is removed from the lips. The challenge is repeated; the candidate answers the first query with "From the north, the gate of death." The guardian of the west administers the cup of memory (a draught of pure water from the chalice) and the purification of water (a few drops upon the forehead).

SOUTH -- FIRE: The candidate is led clockwise to the south. The representative of fire lays the flat of the sword or athame on the candidate's right shoulder and censes the candidate three times with Cernunnos Incense, bestowing the sword of power and the consecration of fire.

EAST -- AIR: The candidate is led to the east. The representative of air breathes on the candidate's head three times, bestowing the breath of life. The blindfold is then removed. The first sight to greet the candidate's eyes is the grand master in his blazing mask: Lucifer, the Sun at Midnight.

ENTRY: Having been purified and consecrated by the four elements, the candidate is conducted into the circle at the north. The hands are unbound. The grand master extends the blade of his sword or athame to the candidate, who kneels and places the right hand upon the blade to take the oath:

"I [witch name], in the presence of all there assembled, man or god, living or dead, do of my own free will most solemnly swear that I shall ever keep secret those things entrusted to my ears alone by this coven, except it be to a proper person, properly prepared, within such a circle as I am now in; and that I shall never deny the secrets to such a person should he be properly vouched for by a fellow member of this coven. All this I swear upon my life, now and hereafter, and may those powers I possess, now or hereafter, turn against me should I break this most solemn oath! So mote it be!"

REGISTER: The coven workbook is produced. The candidate signs the witch name in the register and dates it. Some covens also record the candidate's height (taking the measure). A drop of blood (taken with a sterilized needle) or a few hairs from the head are placed in the register or filed separately as a bond token.

INVESTITURE: The grand master lays hands on the kneeling candidate's head and calls down the blessing and acceptance of the coven. The candidate is invested with the coven witch jewel. Some covens include a consecration by Sabbat Oil and wine at this point.

PRESENTATION: The new initiate rises and is presented by witch name to each coven member in turn. The coven's working tools (sword, chalice, lamps, etc.) are presented to the candidate.

CLOSE: The Sabbat festivities and feast of cakes and wine continue as usual.
