🪞Broken Mirror Spikenard Fidelity Binding

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Mirror MagickBindingInvocationDevotional Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Small hand mirror (last looked into by your lover, do not look into it yourself) Spikenard essential oil, or spikenard root infusion Love-drawing flower (for planting over the burial spot) Flowerpot with dirt (if no yard available)
◇Directions
1. Without looking into the mirror yourself, smash it into tiny pieces. 2. Bury the shards in your yard, or in a flowerpot filled with dirt kept indoors if no yard is available. 3. Plant a love-drawing flower over the spot to mark it. 4. Every Friday, anoint the ground above the buried shards with spikenard essential oil or a spikenard root infusion, or shave a piece of spikenard root directly onto the spot, while repeating an invocation that includes your lover's name.
◇Description
A mirror last looked into by a lover is smashed and buried, with weekly Friday anointings of spikenard oil or root shaved over the shards to bind the lover home and ensure fidelity. The caster must not look into the mirror before breaking it.
