This enchanted oil summons the watchful protectiveness of your ancestors.

Quantities

  • 3 tablespoons carrier oil
  • 1 tablespoon dried mugwort
  • 4 drops of sandalwood oil
  • 4 drops of frankincense oil
  • 3 drops of myrrh oil
  • 1 small glass bottle

Directions

  1. Before you get started, create your own Sigil on Paper or a Bay Laurel leaf, something along the effect of “my ancestors protect me”.
  2. Fill a cleansed glass jar with a carrier oil and then add your items, including your sigil, one by one.
    fasten the lid and briefly shake.
  3. Hold the filled jar to your Heart Chakra while visualizing your ancestors gathered around you in a protective circle, holding hands.
  4. Allow the vision of the comforting, protective energy and divine guidance of your ancestors to swirl up through you and into the bottle.
  5. Mindfully carve your sigil into the white candle until it is fully covered. Anoint the candle with the sacred potion of your ancestors, and place it in a firesafe vessel on your altar. Once lit, say quietly or aloud,
    I call upon my ancestors, holy and wise, and offer my gratitude to my providential guides. Show me the way, keep my path clear, and offer me insight and protection so dear.
