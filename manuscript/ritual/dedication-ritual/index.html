A major rite of passage performed not earlier than a year and a day after Initiation. Creates permanent commitment to the Divine, includes death/rebirth visualization, receiving a secret Working Name, and sealing the dedication with offerings to all four Elementals. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.

Quantities

  • Parchment paper
  • Writing instrument and ink
  • White votive candle in safe container (to collect melted wax)
  • Needle or pin (or athame tip)
  • Bowl of soil

Directions

  1. Perform not earlier than a year and a day after the Initiation Ritual.
  2. Perform a vigil the night before: question your motives, address hopes and fears, seek answers to any doubts.
  3. This ritual creates permanent commitment to the Goddess and God and opens lines of communication with the Divine.
  4. Review the ritual beforehand so it flows smoothly; do not rush the stages, but feel each one.
  5. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.
  6. Statement of intent to the Goddess and the God (may use this or create another):
  7. Great Lady and Great Lord, I am Thy child known to you as [Craft Name].
  8. I have studied the Craft of the Wise, and I have communed with Thee for at least a year and a day.
  9. You have given me of Thy blessings, aided me in my Craft, and showered me with Thy love, asking nothing in return.
  10. Now I seek to offer something back to Thee, making my commitment to Thee and opening the communication between Thee and me, that we are ever united in the bonds of love.
  11. Contemplate the role of the Four Elementals in the ritual.
  12. Open raised arms:
  13. With Elemental Air, do I make my promise to the Divine to try and live up to my potential, to open my mind to the voices of the Ancient Ones, and to develop my magickal skills.
  14. Light white candle:
  15. With Elemental Fire, do I light the way to my new life.
  16. Set candle safely on the ground in front of the Altar.
  17. Envision the ground as a deep hole:
  18. With Elemental Earth, do I lay myself down into the grave signifying the end of this life.
  19. Lie down in front of the Altar safely next to the candle, seeing this as a burial (on the back, feet together, arms folded over waist or crossed over chest).
  20. With eyes closed, visualize sinking down into the Earth, being still, feel that the life has ceased, and there is silence within the enfolding Earth.
  21. When the ground feels like it is in motion, let all negative feelings drain out into the Earth below, leaving an empty feeling that may cause crying.
  22. See that the body and cares of this life are dying, and the Spirit is moving into the Land of Shadows.
  23. When ready, think of Elemental Water, but do not give voice to these thoughts:
  24. With Elemental Water am I cleansed.
  25. Visualize the sacred waters of the Earth, the seas, springs, snowfall, rainfall, and rivers, washing over and cleansing the Spirit.
  26. Feel as though floating in the Waters of Life, and feel at peace.
  27. When the sensation of relaxation sweeps over, feel the blessing of the water.
  28. The Lady may speak a new name at this point, calling to you.
  29. If not, continue with the ritual.
  30. Think of the Spirit, and slowly move into a fetal position, preparing to be reborn into the Light.
  31. With eyes open, look at the Candle, thinking of the God as Fire, Sun, Hunter, and Protector, the One who yearly travels this path, leading the way for spirits to understand the passage through the Crone to find new life in the Light.
  32. Sit up gently, with knees drawn up and arms around them, and relax.
  33. Visualize being born into the Light while looking at the candle.
  34. Rise now, taking the candle and setting it in the center of the Altar.
  35. If no name was received earlier in the ritual prior to rebirth, close the eyes and call upon the Divine:
  36. Great Lady and Great Lord, I call upon You to receive my Dedication unto You.
  37. I, whom You have guided under the name of [Craft Name], do ask You to give me a new name, one that is secret between Thee and me.
  38. As parents name their children, so do I, as Your Child, seek Thy naming of me.
  39. Tell me, my Lady and my Lord, what is my name.
  40. They will come and pronounce the name.
  41. If need be, ask for the name to be spelled out.
  42. Ask for Their Secret Names, and do not share these names with anyone, for this is a Sacred Trust, giving a private access line.
  43. To acknowledge the receipt of Their gift, seal the Dedication in the Elementals.
  44. With Air: write on the parchment paper the promise spoken with Elemental Air, worded now with the new Secret (Working) Name, then kiss the paper.
  45. With Fire: burn the paper in the white votive flame.
  46. With Earth: pull out a few hairs and burn these in the flame.
  47. With Water: with the needle, pin, or athame tip (sterilized in the flame), prick the tip of the little finger of the left hand and squeeze out a few drops of blood onto the Earth in the bowl (or on the ground if outside).
  48. If using the bowl of soil, when the Circle is Opened, take it outside and empty onto the ground, or into the soil of a potted plant.
  49. Henceforth, the Working Name may be used in private to call upon the Lady and the Lord as "Your child, whom You have named..." while the Craft Name may be used in public as desired.
  50. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
