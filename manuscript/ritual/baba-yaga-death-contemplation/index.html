A contemplative altar working focused on Baba Yaga, the Russian death spirit who governs the intersection of magick and harsh reality, limits and possibility. Like Kali and La Santisima Muerte, she encompasses all the mysteries of life and death. This working is not an invocation and is not meant to make direct contact. The Baba has no patience for casual callers. Instead, this is a meditation-through-object practice: sitting with her altar, gazing at it, and allowing comprehension to arise. Food and drink are essential offerings, as Baba Yaga is voraciously hungry.

Quantities

  • Birch wood and leaves
  • Animal imagery
  • Mortar and pestle
  • Broom
  • Real food and drink, or cut photo images of food (Baba Yaga is especially fond of rich Russian dishes like coulibiac)
  • Samovar or tea set with Russian caravan tea
  • Optional: a water pipe

Directions

  1. Build an altar to Baba Yaga using birch wood and leaves as a foundation.
  2. Add animal imagery and her traditional tools: a mortar, pestle, and broom.
  3. Prioritize food and drink above all else, as she is always hungry.
  4. Offer either real food or cut-out photo images: rich Russian dishes are particularly fitting.
  5. Include a samovar with fine Russian caravan tea, and a water pipe if desired.
  6. Sit before the altar and let your gaze move across the objects from different angles.
  7. Handle them if it feels right.
  8. Observe whatever thoughts, impressions, or understandings arise.
