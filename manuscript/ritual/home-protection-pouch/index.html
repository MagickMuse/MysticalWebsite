A waxing to Full Moon spell to create a protective ward for the home. Two candles are inscribed with the runes Fearn, Thorn, Ethel, and Osa, then rubbed with rosemary oil. Herbs, iron, and frankincense oil are placed into a black cotton bag while speaking their purpose, then the bag is sewn shut and consecrated through the four Elementals with an invocation to the Lord and Lady. Placed near the entry to the home.

Quantities

  • 1 athame
  • 1 black cotton bag
  • 1 black candle
  • 1 white candle
  • Bay leaves
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Fennel seed
  • Dandelion root
  • Fennel root or mandrake
  • Rosemary oil
  • Frankincense oil
  • Frankincense incense
  • 1 cotton ball
  • 1 small piece of iron
  • Blessed water
  • Salt
  • Needle and thread

Directions

  1. Rub rosemary oil on both candles.
  2. Inscribe the white candle with Fearn for strength and a solid foundation, and Thorn for protection and defense.
  3. Inscribe the black candle with Ethel for home and possessions, and Osa for good fortune and a favorable outcome.
  4. Light the frankincense incense, then light the white candle and speak:
  5. This flame burns for a solid, well-protected home!
  6. Light the black candle and speak:
  7. This flame burns for good fortune to smile upon my home and my possessions!
  8. Place each item into the bag, speaking as they are added:
  9. Bay for my desires; garlic for protection power; fennel seed for protection and deflecting negative energies; dandelion root for grounding; fennel root, inscribed with Fearn or Osa, to be a protective watcher; iron for strength in defense of this home; and frankincense oil, dabbed onto a cotton ball, for protection and power, that as I will, So Mote It Be!
  10. Sew the bag shut and speak:
  11. Herbs and oil and iron strong; protect this house; that no harm come by day or night!
  12. In a quiet place is where you'll lay, keeping negativity far away, for as I will, So Mote It Be!
  13. Consecrate the pouch by passing it through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak:
  14. Lord and Lady, bless this charm of protection made for your child, [Name].
  15. Be thou empowered charm of mine, to protect my home, by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt]; by Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke]; by Elemental Fire [pass through candle flames]; and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water] are you charged, that as I will, So Mote It Be!
  16. Place the pouch near the entry to the home.
  17. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
