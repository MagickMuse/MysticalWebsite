A living spell-in-progress: a mojo bag of fertility charms such as acorns, mistletoe twigs, cowrie shells, pearls, and silver rabbit or moon charms, which gains power as new items are added and is dressed with Astarte Oil and Van Van Oil. One of the Fertility Mojo methods. ⚠️ Lunar Fertility Mojo Bag is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

A mojo bag
Any combination of: acorns, mistletoe twigs, cowrie shells, pearls, silver rabbit charms, silver moon charms
Astarte Oil
Van Van Oil

Directions

  1. Fill a mojo bag with fertility charms: acorns, mistletoe twigs, cowrie shells, pearls, and/or silver charms shaped like rabbits or moons.
  2. Treat this as an ongoing working and add charms as they come to you.
  3. Dress the bag with Astarte Oil for fertility and Van Van Oil to draw your desires into reality.
