🪶Feather

A freely found (never taken by force) raven feather is charged with the caster's desire and held in the hand to promote virtual invisibility, the inability of others to notice the holder. Stillness and silence are prerequisites. Considered an extremely effective technique, but requires practice. The spell is essentially a blessing from the ravens.

Quantities

One raven feather (must be freely found or given, never taken by force or trapping)

Directions

  1. Prerequisite: Practice stillness. Try to increase the intervals of time you can sit completely motionless and silent.
  2. Find a raven feather. It must be freely given, you can spontaneously find it, but you cannot trap the raven or take it by force.
  3. Charge the feather with your desire.
  4. When you wish to be rendered virtually invisible (unnoticeable), hold the feather in your hand.
    Note: This doesn't make you literally invisible but promotes the inability of others to see you. It is extremely effective but requires practice.
