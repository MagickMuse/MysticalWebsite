An altar is created and consecrated to Aphrodite with offerings, and a personal love charm (belt, waist beads, jewelry, or similar) is placed upon it overnight to be empowered. The charged charm is then given to the beloved or used to draw new love.

Quantities

Offerings for Aphrodite (flowers, fruit, perfume, jewelry)
Something to be empowered as a love charm (belt, waist beads, small charm, or piece of jewelry)

Directions

  1. Create an altar for Aphrodite and consecrate it with offerings to her such as flowers, fruit, perfume, or jewelry.
  2. Place upon the altar something that you'd like her to empower as a love charm; a belt or waist beads are traditional, but any small charm or piece of jewelry is effective.
  3. Be very clear and explicit as to what belongs to Aphrodite and will remain hers forever versus what she's empowering for you as a charm.
  4. Let the charm rest on the altar overnight.
  5. Give it to your beloved as a gift or use it to draw new love.
