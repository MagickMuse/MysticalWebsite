🌬️Monthly Clear Home Stagnation

A monthly bowl-swirling ritual mixing cleansing ingredients to banish stagnation and welcome fresh energies at month's end.

Quantities

  • Individual bowls of: dried mint, salt, pepper, juniper berries, dried cloves, dried cranberries, and coriander, cumin, or sunflower seeds
  • 1 larger empty bowl
  • Flower petals (for next morning)

Directions

  1. Add salt to the larger bowl, saying, "Never let the sun go down in anger, nor slumber while stagnation lingers; the month is nearly gone, and so new life must come." Add pepper, berries, and seeds, repeating the same words for each.
  2. Swirl the bowl nine times clockwise, nine counterclockwise, and nine clockwise, speaking the words faster as you swirl the bowl faster.
  3. Put the bowl down with a bang, saying, "The month is nearly done, new energies will come. Staleness begone." Go outside early the next morning, scattering the mix.
  4. Fill the larger bowl with flower petals and the dried mint, saying, "New month with every hour, continuing goodness on me shower." Perform the last day of the month, before sunset.
