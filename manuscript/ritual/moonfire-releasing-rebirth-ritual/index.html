A Samhain-season burning ritual that uses the Blood Moon or Hunter's Moon energy to release what no longer serves and call in what you wish to grow. Paper is divided into what to banish and what to manifest, the banishing half is burned in a cauldron, and the ashes are blended with pomegranate juice, a symbol of sacrifice, blood, and transmutation. The positive half is anointed with the mixture as a pledge of commitment, then kept as a sacred reminder. The remaining ash-and-juice offering is poured into the earth. Best performed under a waning or dark moon, during a lunar eclipse, or when the moon is in Scorpio.

Quantities

  • Pen and paper
  • Cauldron or fireproof bowl
  • Matches or lighter
  • Pomegranate juice

Directions

  1. Fold a sheet of paper in half.
  2. On one half, write everything you wish to banish, change, or release, old habits, limiting patterns, memories that hold you back, ties that have run their course.
  3. On the other half, write what you want to call in to replace those things: intentions, qualities, or new beginnings you wish to grow.
  4. Tear the paper in two along the fold.
  5. The ripping is itself an act of severance, symbolically cutting away what you are releasing.
  6. In your cauldron or fireproof bowl, set the banishing half alight.
  7. Watch the flames and say aloud:
  8. "As the full moon waxes and wanes, life is constantly remade.
    Moonfire, burn what no longer serves, ashes to new things give birth."
  9. Let the fire do its work.
  10. As it burns, hold the understanding that, just as wildfire clears the way for new growth, this flame is making room for what comes next.
  11. Once the ash has cooled slightly, pour pomegranate juice over it, this seasonal fruit carries the symbolism of sacrifice, the blood of transformation, and the myth of Persephone descending and returning.
  12. The mixture also becomes a nourishing offering to the earth.
  13. Dip your finger into the pomegranate-ash mixture and anoint the positive half of the paper.
  14. This mark is your sign of commitment: you have made the sacrifice, and you are ready for the rebirth.
  15. Fold or roll the anointed paper and place it somewhere sacred, your altar, a special box, or wherever your intentions are held and honored.
  16. It serves as a reminder of what you are calling in through the Wheel of the Year.
  17. Take the remainder of the pomegranate and ash mixture outside and pour it into the earth as an offering, completing the cycle of sacrifice and return.
  18. Note: For a deeper working, pair this ritual with a Mabon Moon Tarot Meditation beforehand.
