A Scorpio zodiac spell to shield yourself from someone who is obsessed with you and your life, painting gray stick figures to obscure their vision and cool their unwanted attention.

Quantities

  • Strong paper
  • Thin gray watercolor paint
  • 1 brush or a child's gray paint stick
  • Scissors
  • 1 container

Directions

  1. Paint two featureless stick figures, one representing you and one representing the stalker, on either side of the paper, and say, "Follow me no more, no longer seek me, your interest in my life does freak me." Cover the whole paper, including the figures in gray paint, saying when you have finished, "Your vision of me now is obscure, your unwanted attention I will not endure. You must find a new obsession, and now shall learn this needed lesson." Cut the paper jaggedly in half.
  2. When it is dry, fold the half representing yourself very small and put it in a container in the refrigerator, not in the freezer, for nine days, saying, "Your overconcern in my life I cool. No longer seek me, that's the rule." Throw the other half in a garbage can.
