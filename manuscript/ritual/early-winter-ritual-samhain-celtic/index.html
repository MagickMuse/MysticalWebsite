A between October 31 and November 2 or April 30 and May 2 spell for early winter ritual called Samhain in Celtic calendar, forerunner of Halloween, lighting purple candle with garlic and photos of deceased relatives for ancestors to protect and advise.

Quantities

  • A purple candle
  • A clove of garlic next to the candle
  • Photos of any deceased relatives you feel are close

Directions

  1. Light the candle, saying: "May only goodness and light enter here on this night of the beloved ancestors." Blow softly into the candle, saying: "I call my beloved ancestors at this your special time to protect and guide me/us as winter approaches, especially [name those in the photos]." Hold each photo in turn, speaking words of love or regret, then saying: "As winter comes near, I carry this fear [name your problem], asking you to advise me or send signs to me." Blow out the candle, saying: "Go in peace and blessings." You may hear in your mind your deceased relative's comforting words, sense her presence, or smell her fragrance.
  2. The answer to your dilemma will come in a dream or an unmistakable sign in your daily life.
  3. Bury the garlic.
  4. Perform between October 31 and November 2 in the northern hemisphere, and between April 30 and May 2 in the southern hemisphere.
