👃Train Your Nose

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Meditation
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
- 3 to 5 essential oils
◇Directions
1. We constantly inhale scents throughout daily life, but only the strongest or most unusual smells capture our attention. 2. Spotted Skunk understands scent as powerful self-defense. 3. To harness scent's power, first train your nose to notice, then to recognize. 4. Select 3 to 5 essential oils to work with, you needn't enjoy their aromas. 5. Remove all bottle caps and shuffle them so you can't identify which belongs where. 6. Using only your nose, match each cap to its corresponding bottle. 7. Check the oil names only after you've completed all matches. 8. Practice this exercise several consecutive days until you know the oils by scent as well as by name. 9. Notice similarities between scents. 10. If two smell related, research them to identify the shared component you're detecting. 11. For example, both eucalyptus and lavender contain camphor. 12. Once you recognize the similarity, you'll identify that component elsewhere too. 13. Like acquiring a new language, scent work may feel completely foreign initially. 14. Allow yourself to play and experiment as you develop this sense.
◇Description
A scent-training practice inspired by Spotted Skunk's mastery of olfactory defense. By learning to identify and distinguish essential oils through repeated practice, you develop heightened sensory awareness and deepen your relationship with scent as a tool.
