✝️Black CrossPentagram

A two-part photograph curse using either the Black Cross (for binding) or the Reversed Pentagram (for cursing). Part one consecrates and charges a photograph of the victim in a widdershins circle rite. Part two plants a psychic trap at the victim's threshold. The photograph is then slowly destroyed to sustain the working.

Quantities

  • Photograph of the victim (reasonably recent)
  • Athame
  • Wand
  • Pen and ink of art
  • Incense of Wrath (Martial or Saturn, depending on intent)
  • Thurible
  • Lamps of art (candles or oil lamps for the circle and altar)
  • Fire and water (for the altar)
  • Sabbat Oil or gum Arabic (for the threshold symbol)
  • Optional: dilute nitric or hydrochloric acid in a developing pan (for photo destruction)

Directions

  1. Part One: The Circle Rite
  2. Choose the symbol to use. Draw the Black Cross (circle-cross of death, skull-and-crossbones motif) for binding. Draw the Reversed Pentagram (two points aloft) for cursing.
  3. On a night when the moon is waning, preferably in its darkest quarter, cast a circle with an Incense of Wrath burning in the thurible.
  4. On the altar, place the athame, fire, water, lamps of art, pen and ink of art, and wand. Place the photograph in the altar triangle.
  5. Approach the altar facing north. State the purpose of the work as concisely and forcefully as possible. Visualize and call upon Cernunnos (for cursing) or Nocticula-Hecate (for binding) as witnesses to the use of your own witch power, not as presences to summon into a triangle.
  6. Name the photograph: asperge and fumigate it three times, saying each time: "Creature of paper, I name thee [N]! Thou art [N]!"
  7. With the wand, write the victim's name above the photograph in glowing blue witch fire three times, chanting the name aloud each time.
  8. Carry the photograph clockwise around the circle with the wand in the right hand. Display the image at east, south, west, and north, chanting the victim's name at each quarter. Return to the north.
  9. Move to the west of the circle. Face east across the altar. Take the pen of art in the left hand. Draw the chosen symbol (Black Cross or Reversed Pentagram) on the photograph very slowly and deliberately, chanting: "I [witch name] inflict upon thee [victim's name] [state the specific misfortune or intent]."
  10. Replace the pen. Take the athame in the left hand. Repeat the words and actions with the blade, pouring all concentrated wrath or loathing into the working. Visualize blue witch fire crackling from the blade across the photograph.
  11. Hold the charged photograph in the left hand. Display it to north, west, south, and east three consecutive times (twelve displays total), announcing at each quarter: "As the symbol dwindles, so shall my words prove effective!"
  12. This widdershins reverse circumambulation (northways, against the sun) completes the first half.
    Part Two: The Threshold Trap
  13. The next day (or the same night), visit the victim's home secretly. At the threshold of the door or apartment, lightly draw the same symbol used in the rite using Sabbat Oil or gum Arabic so it remains invisible to the eye.
  14. Trace over the symbol with the athame, charging it with witch power. The symbol is programmed solely to react to the victim's presence and poses no danger to any other passer-by.
  15. If access to the threshold is impossible, draw the symbol at any point the victim must cross and place their foot.
    Part Three: Destroying the Photograph
  16. The photograph must be destroyed slowly to sustain the working. Two methods: (a) dissolve it face-up in a pan of dilute acid, watching the image fade; or (b) tear off a small piece each night at approximately the same time as the original rite.
