💰Money Does Grow on Trees

◇Form
Altar WorkingOffering Rite
◇Method
Chant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 6 tarot cards that represent abundance to you - Hole punch tool - Scissors - 72 inches of green ribbon (biodegradable material, not plastic) - Chalice - Wine or another libation such as apple cider - Large, healthy tree
◇Directions
1. The druids believed trees were sacred, and in many spiritual traditions, trees stand as central figures in the path to enlightenment. 2. Because trees represent growth, and many trees live longer than people do, they can add beneficial energy to your spells. 3. Before you begin this spell, select a large, healthy tree and ask it to assist you in this spellwork. 4. From a deck you don't use for readings, choose six cards that represent abundance to you. 5. Punch a hole in each card, preferably in a spot that doesn't obliterate important symbolism. 6. Cut six pieces of ribbon in pieces that are long enough to tie around branches or twigs of the tree you've chosen as your helper. 7. Thread a piece of ribbon through the hole of each card. 8. Into your chalice, pour wine or another libation. 9. Swirl it around three times, in a clockwise direction, while you project positive thoughts into the beverage. 10. Carry the cards and your chalice to the tree, and loosely tie the cards on small branches or twigs. 11. Tie three knots in each ribbon, and as you tie the knots, envision abundance coming to you. 12. When you've finished, toast the tree and thank it for its assistance. 13. If you like, say an incantation that expresses your hopes and gratitude. 14. Take a sip of the wine or other beverage, then pour the rest at the base of the tree as an offering.
◇Description
The druids believed trees were sacred, and in many spiritual traditions, trees stand as central figures in the path to enlightenment. Because trees represent growth, and many trees live longer than people do, they can add beneficial energy to your spells. Before you begin this spell, select a large, healthy tree and ask it to assist you in this spellwork.
