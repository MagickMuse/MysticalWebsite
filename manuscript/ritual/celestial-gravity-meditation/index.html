🌊Celestial Gravity Meditation

◇Directions
1. Set your cup before you. 2. Arrange shells or ocean objects around its base as a reminder of the moon's dominion over the tides. 3. Close your eyes and breathe deeply. 4. Visualize the moon in its orbit, drawing closer to Earth at its nearest point. 5. Imagine yourself on a moonlit beach, feeling the salt wind, hearing crashing waves, sensing cool sand beneath your feet. 6. The full moon hangs above, and the tides surge wild and high before you. 7. Rest in this space. 8. Open your eyes and pour water into the cup, envisioning the moon's energy flowing into it. 9. Take a sip. 10. Feel it move through your mouth, down your throat, into your body; roughly 60% of your body is water. 11. You are the tide, lit up and pulled by the moon. 12. Reflect on the moon's deep bond with Earth: how in the planet's early days, lunar gravity shaped the oceans, drawing minerals from the land that seeded the conditions for life. 13. To this day, creatures in the wild live in rhythm with the moon; sea turtles hatch and follow moonlight to the water. 14. The moon calls and nurtures all. 15. Return to your visualization. 16. Feel the water moving through you, your emotions, your sensations, the liquid coursing through every part of your body. 17. Breathe in the moon's light and power with each inhale, growing more luminous and charged. 18. You may stop here if you feel complete, or continue to charge the water with a specific intention. 19. Decide on your intention: whether you want to direct a particular energy into your body, or simply invite pure lunar power. 20. Hold your palms hovering above the cup. 21. Visualize moonlight pouring through your hands. 22. Focus your intention and direct it down into the water. 23. Hold the visualization until you feel the water vibrating back up toward your palms in response. 24. Take the quartz point and stir the water clockwise; as the supermoon amplifies the tides, quartz amplifies your intention. 25. Drink the water and visualize every cell of your body absorbing it, saturated with your intention. 26. Repeat whenever needed, especially as a preparatory step before ritual or spellwork.
◇Description
A supermoon meditation that attunes you to the moon's gravitational pull on the tides, and by extension, on your own body and energy. Through guided visualization of a moonlit beach and the sensation of the moon's draw, you charge purified water with intention using your palms and a quartz point, then drink it to absorb that energy into your body. Can be used as a preparatory ritual before any spellwork.
