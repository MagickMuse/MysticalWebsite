🌿Henna Body Decoration Funeral Protection Spell

◇Form
Consecrated Item
◇Method
ProtectionConsecrationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Henna paste
◇Directions
1. Using henna, decorate the hands and feet of the deceased. 2. Incorporate designs meaningful to the deceased's tradition, or use Egyptian funerary amulet imagery such as the Eye of Horus or Buckle of Isis. 3. As you draw, chant sacred texts or prayers and direct focused intent into each design, charging it as you would a physical amulet.
◇Description
The deceased's hands and feet are decorated with henna after death to provide good fortune and protection in the next realm. Different traditions use different designs; the source encourages choosing what resonates. In the context of Egyptian funerary tradition, drawing the image of an amulet while chanting sacred texts and charging through magickal focus was considered equal in power to a physical amulet, meaning the henna itself, when properly charged, becomes the protective object.
