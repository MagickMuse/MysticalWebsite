💍Wedding Ring Blessing Bath

Silver

A Swedish newborn bath using the mother's wedding ring and silver to bless the baby with wealth, security, and true love.

Quantities

The mother's wedding ring
Real silver
A bowl of water

Directions

  1. Place the mother's wedding ring and some real silver in a bowl of water, so the baby will be blessed with wealth, security, and true love, presuming the parents' marriage is happy.
  2. Sprinkle this water over the baby.
