Reading omens and messages in winter weather patterns including snow types, wind directions, temperature shifts, and storm behavior. Weather has spoken to humans since ancient times, and winter's dramatic conditions make messages especially clear.

Quantities

  • Daily weather observation practice
  • Journal and pen
  • Dark fabric for catching snowflakes (optional)
  • Thermometer (optional)
  • Weather app or barometer (optional)
  • Warm outdoor clothing

Directions

  1. Snow Divination:
  2. First Snow of Season:
  3. Day it falls reveals timing of major events
  4. Depth indicates intensity of coming year
  5. Wet vs. dry snow: emotional vs. practical focus needed
  6. If it melts quickly: temporary challenges
  7. If it stays: persistence required
  8. Snowflake Observation:
  9. Catch flakes on dark fabric to observe structure
  10. Perfect symmetry: balance achieved or needed
  11. Broken crystals: something incomplete
  12. Clustered flakes: community and cooperation
  13. Individual flakes: solo work ahead
  14. Snow Quality:
  15. Powdery snow: lightness, ease, gentle approach
  16. Wet heavy snow: burden, weight, endurance needed
  17. Ice pellets: hardship, protection required
  18. Mixed precipitation: complexity, multiple factors
  19. Wind Augury:
  20. Direction (face the wind to determine):
  21. North wind: challenges, endurance, ancestors
  22. South wind: warmth, growth, expansion
  23. East wind: new beginnings, dawn, fresh start
  24. West wind: endings, completion, wisdom
  25. Wind Behavior:
  26. Steady wind: consistent energy, stay the course
  27. Gusting wind: unpredictable changes, flexibility needed
  28. Howling wind: important message, pay attention
  29. Calm after wind: peace after struggle
  30. Wind chimes sounding: spirits communicating
  31. Temperature Omens:
  32. Sudden Drops:
  33. Rapid freeze: situation crystallizing quickly
  34. Prepare and take action before things set
  35. Thaws:
  36. Midwinter thaw: relief, release, letting go
  37. False spring: don't be fooled by appearances
  38. Extreme Cold:
  39. Test of endurance
  40. Conservation and protection needed
  41. Inner work favored over outer action
  42. Storm Interpretation:
  43. Before Storm:
  44. Barometric pressure drop: intuition heightened
  45. Animals seeking shelter: heed warnings
  46. Eerie calm: pause before transformation
  47. During Storm:
  48. Power outages: forced stillness, meditation
  49. Thundersnow (rare): powerful magick, significant omen
  50. Whiteout conditions: loss of direction, need for grounding
  51. After Storm:
  52. Damage assessment: what must be rebuilt
  53. Beauty of fresh snow: clean slate
  54. Return of sun: hope, clarity returning
  55. Folkloric Weather Omens:
  56. - Smoke rising straight up: fair weather, clear path
  57. Smoke descending: storm coming, obstacles
  58. Halo around moon: storm within 24 hours, hidden things revealed
  59. Red sky at night: sailor's delight, positive omen
  60. Red sky at morning: sailors take warning, caution needed
  61. Crystal clear stars: cold coming, sharp clarity
  62. Journaling Practice:
  63. Record weather observations daily throughout winter.
  64. Note correlations between weather patterns and life events.
  65. Your personal weather oracle will develop over time.
