🌬️Weather Augury for Winter Divination

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Daily weather observation practice - Journal and pen - Dark fabric for catching snowflakes (optional) - Thermometer (optional) - Weather app or barometer (optional) - Warm outdoor clothing
◇Directions
1. Snow Divination: 2. First Snow of Season: - Day it falls reveals timing of major events - Depth indicates intensity of coming year - Wet vs. dry snow: emotional vs. practical focus needed - If it melts quickly: temporary challenges - If it stays: persistence required 3. Snowflake Observation: - Catch flakes on dark fabric to observe structure - Perfect symmetry: balance achieved or needed - Broken crystals: something incomplete - Clustered flakes: community and cooperation - Individual flakes: solo work ahead 4. Snow Quality: - Powdery snow: lightness, ease, gentle approach - Wet heavy snow: burden, weight, endurance needed - Ice pellets: hardship, protection required - Mixed precipitation: complexity, multiple factors 5. Wind Augury: 6. Direction (face the wind to determine): - North wind: challenges, endurance, ancestors - South wind: warmth, growth, expansion - East wind: new beginnings, dawn, fresh start - West wind: endings, completion, wisdom 7. Wind Behavior: - Steady wind: consistent energy, stay the course - Gusting wind: unpredictable changes, flexibility needed - Howling wind: important message, pay attention - Calm after wind: peace after struggle - Wind chimes sounding: spirits communicating 8. Temperature Omens: 9. Sudden Drops: - Rapid freeze: situation crystallizing quickly - Prepare and take action before things set 10. Thaws: - Midwinter thaw: relief, release, letting go - False spring: don't be fooled by appearances 11. Extreme Cold: - Test of endurance - Conservation and protection needed - Inner work favored over outer action 12. Storm Interpretation: 13. Before Storm: - Barometric pressure drop: intuition heightened - Animals seeking shelter: heed warnings - Eerie calm: pause before transformation 14. During Storm: - Power outages: forced stillness, meditation - Thundersnow (rare): powerful magick, significant omen - Whiteout conditions: loss of direction, need for grounding 15. After Storm: - Damage assessment: what must be rebuilt - Beauty of fresh snow: clean slate - Return of sun: hope, clarity returning 16. Folkloric Weather Omens: 17. - Smoke rising straight up: fair weather, clear path - Smoke descending: storm coming, obstacles - Halo around moon: storm within 24 hours, hidden things revealed - Red sky at night: sailor's delight, positive omen - Red sky at morning: sailors take warning, caution needed - Crystal clear stars: cold coming, sharp clarity 18. Journaling Practice: 19. Record weather observations daily throughout winter. 20. Note correlations between weather patterns and life events. 21. Your personal weather oracle will develop over time.
◇Description
Reading omens and messages in winter weather patterns including snow types, wind directions, temperature shifts, and storm behavior. Weather has spoken to humans since ancient times, and winter's dramatic conditions make messages especially clear.
