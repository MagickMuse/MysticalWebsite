A waxing to Full Moon spell to create a portable ward against accidents and danger. A white candle is inscribed with Thorn for protection and a black candle with Eh for safe journey. Herbs and lodestones are placed into a small black pouch with frankincense oil, sewn shut, and consecrated through the four Elementals. Carried on the person for continuous protection.

Quantities

  • 1 personal item
  • Anise seeds
  • Comfrey
  • Mullein
  • Heather
  • Rosemary oil
  • Frankincense oil
  • 1 cotton ball
  • 1 small black cotton pouch
  • Red thread
  • 1 needle
  • 1 white candle
  • 1 black candle
  • 2 lodestones
  • Dragon's blood or frankincense incense
  • Blessed water
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Rub rosemary oil on both candles.
  2. Inscribe the white candle with Thorn for protection.
  3. Inscribe the black candle with Eh for safe journey.
  4. Light the incense, then light the white candle and speak:
  5. This flame burns for protection from accident or danger!
  6. Light the black candle and speak:
  7. This flame burns for safety, warding accident or danger!
  8. Place each item into the pouch, speaking as they are added:
  9. Anise for protection; comfrey for safety; mullein for protection; heather for protection; two lodestones of balance to draw favorable energies and repel unfavorable energies; and frankincense, dabbed onto a cotton ball, to bind them all, that as I will, So Mote It Be!
  10. Sew the pouch shut and speak:
  11. Herbs and lodestones work with me; protect me with thy energy!
  12. Keep me safe from any harm or accident; for as I will, So Mote It Be!
  13. Pass the pouch through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak:
  14. Be thou empowered by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt], by Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke], by Elemental Fire [pass quickly through both candle flames], and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water] to protect me from accident and danger.
  15. So Mote It Be!
  16. Keep in a purse, briefcase, car glove compartment, pocket, or wherever desired.
  17. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
