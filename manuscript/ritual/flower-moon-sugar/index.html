🌸Flower Moon Sugar

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickLunarManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 8-oz or pint jar with lid - ½–⅓ cup fresh or dried edible flowers (violet, lilac, rose, elderflower, gardenia, chrysanthemum, chamomile, lavender, or other edible blooms, use less of strongly scented flowers like lavender, more of subtler ones like rose) - About 1 cup granulated sugar - 1 large lemon (optional) - Peeler (optional)
◇Directions
1. Begin at the new moon. 2. Gather your flowers, sugar, and lemon. 3. If using fresh flowers, ensure they are fully dry before layering, any moisture will cause the sugar to clump or mold. 4. (Save the lemon flesh for lunar lemon bars or spring cocktails.) 5. Take a moment to hold your intentions. 6. Think about what you are inviting in during this season, growth, beauty, sweetness, renewal. 7. Let the spring moon's energy orient your focus. 8. Peel two strips of lemon rind. 9. Lay them in a single layer at the bottom of the jar, they cleanse away stale energy and open the working to joy. 10. Cut the strips in half if needed so they lie flat without stacking too much. 11. Cover with a half-inch layer of sugar. 12. Add a layer of flowers over the sugar, pressing them in lightly. 13. As you do, reflect on the attracting, beautifying energy of blooms, how they draw pollinators, how they signal abundance. 14. Cover the flowers with another layer of sugar. 15. Continue layering: lemon, sugar, flowers, sugar. 16. To make a blend, alternate your flower varieties, one layer of rose, one of lavender, one of violet, and so on. 17. With each layer, hold the intention you are sweetening into existence. 18. Seal the jar and leave it to infuse and charge from new moon to new moon, or full moon to full moon, approximately four weeks. 19. Set it somewhere it can catch moonlight when possible. 20. After four weeks, blend briefly to break up any clumps and transfer to an airtight container. 21. The sugar keeps for about three months, though the floral essence may gradually fade. 22. Store in the freezer to preserve it longer. 23. Use to sweeten teas, cocktails, and baked goods with lunar intention; dust onto dressed candles; stir into ritual baths or sugar scrubs; or jar and give as a spring blessing gift.
◇Description
A lunar-infused flower sugar made by layering edible blossoms and lemon peel with granulated sugar from new moon to new moon. Use it to sweeten spells, dress candles, enchant recipes and drinks, make a rejuvenating sugar scrub, or gift it to someone to sweeten your connection with them. A spring moon staple for love, positivity, and prosperity workings.
