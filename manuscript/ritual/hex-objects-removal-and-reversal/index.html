🔄Hex Objects Removal and Reversal

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Outdoor

ReversalElemental
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Iron pan Oil Metal tongs Fire source
◇Directions
1. Heat up an iron pan filled with hot oil. 2. Pick any hex objects up with metal tongs; do not touch with bare hands. 3. Toss them into the hot oil, frying them briefly. 4. Then throw them into a fire to be destroyed. 5. This negates the hex's effect and automatically reverses it to whoever sent it; the hexer's identity need not be known.
◇Description
A Chinese magickal tradition for removing, repelling, and automatically reversing a hex back to its sender. Hex objects are picked up with metal tongs (never bare hands), fried in hot oil, then thrown into fire. No knowledge of the hexer's identity is needed for the reversal to work. One of the Antidote remedies.
