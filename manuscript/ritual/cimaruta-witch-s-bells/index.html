🔔Cimaruta Witch's Bells

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
TalismanCharged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ProtectionCleansing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- Size H/8 5mm crochet hook - 52 yards (1 oz) green 4-ply yarn - 2-inch ring - 1/2 inch jingle bells - Charms (see traditional options below) - Yarn for hanging loop
◇Directions
1. Crochet abbreviations used in this pattern: Ch stands for chain; Sc for single crochet; Sl St for slip stitch; Picot for ch 3, slip stitch into 3rd chain from hook; and FO for fasten off. 2. For the rue sprig, chain 20, then work down one side of the chain and back up the other. 3. Single crochet in the 2nd chain from the hook, sc in the next 4 chains. 4. Ch 9, sc in the 2nd ch from hook, sc in the next 3. 5. Ch 4, sc in the 2nd ch from hook, sc in the next 12 chains. 6. Work the following sequence twice: ch 6, sc in the 2nd ch from hook, sc in the next 8 chains. 7. Slip stitch 12 up the other side. 8. Ch 9, sc in the 2nd ch from hook, sc in the next 3. 9. Ch 4, sc in the 2nd ch from hook, sc in the next 6 chains. 10. Slip stitch 7 to end, picot, slip stitch into the 1st sc made. 11. Fasten off, leaving a long tail to sew onto the ring. 12. Traditional cimaruta charms and their meanings: the hand (mano fico) for strength to fight evil; the moon for Diana as goddess of the Moon; the key for Hekate, goddess of Witchcraft and knowledge; the flower for protection, as rue and vervain have five-petaled flowers; the horn for power and virility; the rooster for sunrise and the end of night's realm; the serpent for wisdom and healing; the heart for love and devotion; and the dagger for Diana's arrow. 13. Sew charms to the branches of your sprig of rue, then sew it to the bottom of the ring. 14. String bells and hang them on either side of the rue sprig. 15. Cut another length of yarn for the hanger. 16. Place a charm or bell in the middle and tie it to the top of the ring with a Hercules knot, also known as a reef or square knot: tie right over left, then left over right. 17. This knot represents healing. 18. Tie a knot at the ends to create a hanging loop. 19. Hang from a doorknob or hook after cleansing your space.
◇Description
Crocheted witch's bells in the shape of a sprig of rue (cimaruta). Witches use rue to heal and protect, and charms in its likeness ward off the evil eye. Combined with bells, which cause negative energy and spirits to flee, these make powerful protective wards. Hang on a doorknob after spring cleaning to keep your space clear of unwanted energies.
