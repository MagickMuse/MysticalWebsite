💖Workplace Love Enchantment

◇Form
Elixir
◇Method
Chant/MantraBinding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- One mug - Tea, herbal tea, or coffee - One long metal spoon
◇Directions
1. Prepare a mug of tea or coffee for the person whose affection you seek. 2. You may need to make beverages for everyone to avoid suspicion, but enchant only the one intended. 3. Add your chosen tea or coffee and hot water to the mug. 4. Hold the spoon in your dominant hand and begin stirring clockwise. 5. Simultaneously, move your other hand in a counterclockwise motion above the mug (maintaining a safe distance from the heat). 6. As you stir, speak these words softly or in your mind: 7. "Look on me with eyes of love, let lasting bonds between us grow. I bind and wind connection in, that lasting love we may soon know." 8. Gradually let your voice become softer and your stirring motion slower until both words and movement fade into complete silence and stillness. 9. Offer the enchanted drink to your would-be beloved. 10. As you hand it to them, speak silently in your mind: "With growing love from me to you."
◇Description
A subtle coffee or tea enchantment performed during a natural break to awaken romantic interest in someone who hasn't noticed you, using counterposed hand movements and binding incantations.
