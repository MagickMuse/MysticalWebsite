⚔️Ogun Iron Nail Surgery Protection Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar WorkingOffering Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
InvocationProtection
◇Category

◇Closed Practice

◇Quantities
Seven iron nails Dish of salt water Red and black double action candle Fine cigars and over-proof rum (offerings to Ogun)
◇Directions
1. Make offerings to Ogun before surgery with a promise of additional tribute should things proceed as desired. 2. Ogun's day is Wednesday; his numbers are three and seven; his colors are red and black. 3. He accepts fine cigars and over-proof rum. 4. Soak seven iron nails in a dish filled with salt water. 5. Burn a red and black double action candle next to the dish. 6. Following surgery, bury the nails in earth and spill the salt water on the ground.
◇Description
Ogun, spirit of metal and patron of surgeons, is petitioned before surgery with promises of tribute. Seven iron nails soaked in salt water with a red-and-black double action candle burning alongside form the working. After successful surgery, nails are buried in earth and water spilled on the ground.
