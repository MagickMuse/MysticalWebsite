🌱Money-Drawing Oil

An altar craft spell using herb-wrapped twigs and prosperity-drawing herbs to focus intention on abundance and create a living symbol of manifestation.

Quantities

1 small branch or twig resembling a tree
Money-Drawing Oil (for anointing)
Fresh herbs: basil, mint, catnip, parsley, and bay laurel
Wire cutters
Florist wire
Small cauldron or flower pot
Putty or florist foam (to secure the twig upright)
A selection of tumbled crystals, agates, or semiprecious stones

Directions

  1. Find your branch: walk outdoors and look for a twig that naturally resembles a small tree. It should come to you as a gift; do not force it from a living tree. Anoint the twig with Money-Drawing Oil.
  2. Prepare the herbs: gently separate the leaves, leaving as much stem intact as possible, and mix the herbs together.
  3. Wrap the tree: cut the florist wire into 1½-inch lengths, bundle no more than three leaves together, and secure each bundle with a wire. Wrap the bundles onto the twig's branches, leaving one bare section as the trunk. As you work each bundle, hold a specific vision of the prosperity you are calling in and speak your intentions aloud.
  4. Plant the tree: once the tree is dense and green, set it upright in the cauldron or pot, secured with putty or florist foam. Surround it with the crystals and semiprecious stones.
  5. Place the finished tree on your altar, desk, or another spot where it will be seen regularly.

About This Ritual

Money does not literally grow on trees, but intention does. The herbal money tree is an altar craft and active meditation: a physical symbol you build and then maintain on your altar or workspace. Its power is twofold. First, in its creation: the act of wrapping each bundle of herbs while holding a precise vision of what abundance looks like for you. Second, in its existence: the tree becomes a focal point that keeps your attention anchored to prosperity rather than scarcity. The herbs from the mint family carry particular resonance: the word "mint" has long been a direct synonym for money, and the color green links these plants to growth and wealth across many traditions.

Notes

The spell's ongoing power comes from keeping the tree in a visible, auspicious location: the herbs continue working as they dry, their energy released slowly over time. As the herbs fade and dry, you may renew the tree with fresh bundles during each new moon.
