🧲Horseshoe

A folk protection spell using a horseshoe or mule shoe kept in the oven or hung inside the fireplace to guard chickens from predators.

Quantities

  • Horseshoe or mule shoe (must have been worn by a horse or mule)

Directions

  1. Keep a horseshoe in the oven to protect your chickens from predators.
  2. A mule shoe is considered preferable.
  3. The shoe must have actually been worn by a horse or mule to hold its power.
  4. An alternative method hangs the shoe inside the fireplace.
