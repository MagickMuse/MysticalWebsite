🌿Botanical Psychic Enhancement

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Garden
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
MeditationHerbal MagickDivination
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty

◇Quantities
Living plants, any or all of: mugwort and other artemisias, rowan, juniper, vervain, diviner's sage, Syrian rue (harmel), yarrow, mistletoe, oleander, thorn apple, Saint John's Wort, hazel
◇Directions
1. Grow some of the listed plants in the garden or indoors in pots. 2. Sit next to them with a clear, meditative mind. 3. Absorb their energy and let inspiration appear unbidden, particularly while engaged in divination.
◇Description
Sitting with living plants of strong psychic and magickal association to absorb their energy and let inspiration arrive unbidden, especially helpful during divination. Proximity to the living plant is the optimum method.

Safety

None of these plants need to be ingested, and doing so is not necessarily advisable. Some, such as oleander and thorn apple, are poisonous. Being in their physical presence is enough.
