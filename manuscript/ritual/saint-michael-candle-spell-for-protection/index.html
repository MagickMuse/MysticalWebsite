🛡️Saint Michael Candle Spell for Protection

A candle spell to protect a person, animal, or thing. A Saint Michael glass candle is dressed with oil and protective herbs, placed over a photo of the subject, and burned until the danger has passed. Warrior ancestors or Saint Michael are petitioned for defense.

Quantities

Photo of whoever or whatever is to be protected
Saint Michael glass candle (or a red glass-encased candle + printed image of Saint Michael + glue stick)
Protective oil (for dressing)
Small amount of ground protective herbs or resin such as frankincense or basil (use sparingly, fire hazard risk with too much)
Liquor, tobacco, or both (gratitude offering when danger has passed)

Directions

  1. Get a photo of whoever or whatever you would like to protect, and a Saint Michael glass candle.
  2. If a Saint Michael votive is not available, use a red glass-encased candle, print out an image of Saint Michael, and paste it onto the glass with a glue stick.
  3. Bore small holes in the top of the candle and add a few drops of protective oil, or a very small amount of ground protective herbs or resin such as frankincense or basil.
  4. Less is more: too much herb material in a candle is a fire hazard.
  5. Say a prayer to Saint Michael, or invoke your ancestors who served as warriors in whatever form that took: military, police, fire department, or organized crime.
  6. Place the candle on top of the photo and light it.
  7. If the danger has not passed by the time the candle burns down, light another candle and continue.
  8. When the danger is over and your subject is safe, offer the ancestors you invoked some liquor, tobacco, or both in gratitude.
