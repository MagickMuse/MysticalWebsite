🧵Gold Thread Child's Transitions

A Sunday noon sewing ritual using gold thread and silver needle to empower clothing for major life transitions.

Quantities

  • Gold metallic thread (for power of sun)
  • 1 silver-colored needle (for protection of moon)
  • Garment child or teenager will wear at transition

Directions

  1. Sit in sunlight or bright daylight.
  2. Thread the needle and make nine stitches hidden inside the lining of the garment, on a tag, or hem.
  3. As you sew the nine stitches, say softly nine times, "I sew for you stability, I make for you security, that this change will bring you harmony. May you be protected by gold, sewn in love as life unfolds." Leave the garment outside until the light fades.
  4. Say the spell words in your mind nine times as your child leaves for kindergarten or school for the first time, changes schools, goes to college, or enters his first home, and give him, or pack, the empowered garment.
  5. Perform noon, on Sunday.
