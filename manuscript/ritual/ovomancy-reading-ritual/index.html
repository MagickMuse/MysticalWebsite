A lunar divination ritual using an egg cracked into Moon Water to scry the state of your energy, spirit, and hidden truths. Drawing on the egg's associations with psychic perception, cleansing, and rebirth, and Scorpio's connection to the phoenix, this practice illuminates what lies beneath the surface and offers guidance for further spellwork.

Quantities

  • 3 eggs, room temperature
  • A drinking glass
  • Lukewarm Moon Water
  • Sea salt

Directions

  1. Set three room-temperature eggs before you and hold your question in mind.
  2. Without overthinking it, select the egg that draws your attention most strongly.
  3. If the other two have not been out long, return them to the fridge.
  4. Fill the glass nearly to the brim with lukewarm Moon Water.
  5. As you pour, visualize the moon working through the water, offering healing, reflection, and clarity to all that flows through it.
  6. Hold the chosen egg in both hands.
  7. Close your eyes and breathe slowly and deeply, keeping your question at the forefront of your mind.
  8. Let any grief, doubt, or fear rise to the surface as sensation rather than thought.
  9. Gently pass the egg over your skin, pausing wherever you feel those emotions most strongly in your body.
  10. When you are ready, exhale any lingering energy into the egg, releasing it from yourself and sealing it within the shell.
  11. Hold the egg over the glass and crack it cleanly, letting the contents fall into the Moon Water.
  12. As the egg breaks, visualize the unwanted energy shattering and the truth of the matter being revealed.
  13. Observe how the egg settles in the water: the position of the yolk, the direction and shape of the white's wisps, whether anything feels significant or catches your eye.
  14. Set the glass aside undisturbed for 10 to 15 minutes, allowing the egg to shift and settle further into its reading.
  15. Return to the glass and interpret what you see.
  16. The yolk is sometimes read as the physical body or the core situation; the white as the spiritual or energetic body.
  17. Notice whether the yolk sits centered or to one side, whether it appears whole or broken.
  18. Look at the wisps of white: are they reaching in many directions, forming recognizable shapes, clumping together, or drifting upward?
  19. Trust your personal symbolism above all else.
  20. Your guides communicate in the language most legible to you, and your fluency will deepen with each reading.
  21. When you have finished, sprinkle sea salt into the water to neutralize and cleanse the energy, then discard the egg.
  22. Let the reading guide your next steps; if the egg suggested scattered or tangled energy, consider following this ritual with a cleansing lunar spell.
