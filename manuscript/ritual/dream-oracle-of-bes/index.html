Egyptian spell from the Roman period to request a dream oracle from the dwarf-god Bes at his famous oracle site at Abydos. Used for temple incubation or private practice.

Quantities

  • Ink for drawing
  • Dark cloth (cloth of Isis)
  • Reed mat
  • 1 unbaked brick
  • Writing materials

Directions

  1. Draw an image of Bes on your left hand.
  2. The papyrus containing this spell includes a sketch of Bes to copy, showing him as a dwarf with leonine features.
  3. Wrap your hand in a dark cloth of Isis (dark linen or dark fabric).
  4. Wrap the rest of the cloth around your neck.
  5. Prepare a small table near your sleeping place so you can write down what Bes says immediately upon waking, lest you forget it after sleep.
  6. Sleep on a reed mat with an unbaked brick placed near your head.
  7. Go to sleep without speaking to anyone.
  8. Bes should appear in your dreams and deliver his message.
  9. Record everything you remember immediately upon waking.
  10. This spell was used at the famous Bes oracle at Abydos, where pilgrims from across the Mediterranean came to seek guidance.
  11. The oracle was so popular that Roman Emperor Constantius II ordered it closed in 359 CE, though archaeological evidence suggests it continued operating for another century.
