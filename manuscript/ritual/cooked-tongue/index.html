🍲Cooked Tongue

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method

◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty

◇Quantities
1 raw beef tongue 1 name paper per opponent Cayenne pepper (light) Vinegar (Four Thieves recommended) Pins and needles Garlic Salt Optional: brown beans
◇Directions
1. Write all opponents' names on individual slips of paper. 2. Cut slits into the tongue and insert each name paper, then seal each slit shut with pins and needles. 3. Sprinkle with cayenne and vinegar, using restraint with the cayenne since you will be eating this tongue. 4. Boil the tongue with garlic and salt, seasoning to taste; some versions include brown beans. 5. Cook everything together including the papers and pins. 6. When eating, remove all pins and needles carefully and place them in a brown paper bag; the papers may be eaten. 7. Dispose of the pins and needles away from your home, going and returning via different circuitous routes. 8. Do not reuse them and do not discard them inside your home.
◇Description
A cooked version of the beef tongue court case spell intended for civil suit threats. The tongue is prepared with name papers and sealed with pins, then boiled with garlic and salt and eaten, physically internalizing dominion over all named opponents. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Closed Practice: Beef tongue spellwork originates in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo — closed practices with African American cultural and spiritual lineage. Approach with respect.

Purpose

The cooked tongue version is specifically recommended for civil suits — situations where the stakes are financial or material rather than criminal. By cooking and eating the tongue, you are literally consuming dominion over your opponents. Their names go into your body; their power over you is digested and neutralized.

The Critical Cayenne Note

The source is explicit about this: do not apply so much cayenne that the tongue becomes inedible. The spell requires you to eat it. Use enough cayenne to carry the magical intention, not enough to make the meal unpleasant or impossible.

Cooking the Papers and Pins

Yes, you cook everything together — paper and metal included. This is intentional. The cooking process fuses all the magical intent into the meat. The pins are removed before eating; the papers may be consumed along with the tongue.

Disposal Protocol

The pins and needles used in this working carry residual binding energy. They must be:

  • Placed in a brown paper bag immediately after removal
  • Carried away from your home for disposal
  • Disposed of via circuitous routes, going and returning by different paths
  • Never reused
  • Never discarded inside your home
