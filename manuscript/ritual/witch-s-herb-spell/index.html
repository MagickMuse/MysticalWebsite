🪴Yerba Bruja (Kalanchoe pinnata)

Yerba bruja (Kalanchoe pinnata), a plant under the joint rulership of Yemaya and Oshun, is planted atop a buried coin and lodestone so its roots draw on the coin's financial energy and pull money toward the grower. May be dedicated to either or both orishas. ⚠️ Santería/Lucumí is a closed practice meant only for those initiated within the tradition.

Quantities

  • A coin, ideally a genuine silver coin or lucky Chinese coin for extra enhancement
  • A lodestone
  • A pot filled with soil
  • A cutting or small specimen of yerba bruja (Kalanchoe pinnata)

Directions

  1. Bury a coin within a pot of soil.
  2. Nestle a lodestone beside the buried coin, then plant a cutting or small specimen of yerba bruja above them. The roots grow toward the coin and pull its financial energy outward, drawing money to the caster.
  3. Enhance the working further by using a genuine silver coin or a lucky Chinese coin in place of an ordinary one.
