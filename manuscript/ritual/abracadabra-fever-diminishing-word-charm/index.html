✨Abracadabra Fever Diminishing Word Charm

◇Tradition
Kabbalistic
◇Solitary or Group

Writing Spell

Spell

Beginner
◇Quantities
Parchment or metal lamella Pen or stylus (Hebrew letters are most powerful) Flax cord or string long enough to wear around the neck
◇Directions
1. Inscribe the word ABRACADABRA on parchment or metal lamella in a diminishing triangle form, removing one letter per line from the right side until only 'A' remains. 2. For fever use the inverted triangle; for asthma, the upright triangle; for enhanced general healing, the parallelogram. 3. Write neatly with focused intention, ensuring letters do not touch each other. 4. Hebrew letters are considered most effective. 5. Attach the charm to a piece of flax long enough to wear around the patient's neck and wear it for nine days. 6. After nine days, throw the charm over the left shoulder into water flowing eastward.
◇Description
The most famous spell in the world, a written diminishing word charm from 2nd century CE (first recorded by Quintus Serenus Sammonicus, physician to Emperor Septimius Severus). As the written word diminishes line by line, so does the fever. Worn for nine days then cast into eastward-flowing water. The inverted triangle form is for fever; upward triangle for asthma; parallelogram for enhanced healing.
