A week-long candle ritual culminating on the full moon to increase visibility and earn respect. As you expand the circle of candles each day, you symbolically extend your personal influence and radiance into the world.

Quantities

  • A tarot card that represents you
  • 7 purple candles in candleholders
  • Matches or a lighter

Directions

  1. Begin seven days before the full moon. Choose a tarot card that represents you or resonates with your energy. Place it face-up on your altar or a safe surface where the setup can remain for a week.
  2. Arrange all seven candles in a tight circle around the card. Starting at the top and moving clockwise, light each candle in turn.
  3. Gaze at the arrangement, imagining yourself brilliantly illuminated as if standing in a spotlight. Visualize others watching and admiring you.
  4. When ready, extinguish the candles in counterclockwise order.
  5. Repeat the ritual daily. Each day, widen the circle by moving each candle outward an inch or two.
  6. Continue for seven days, expanding the circle gradually. As the ring of candles grows, so does your personal power and the light you shine into the world. On the full moon night, allow the candles to burn down completely, releasing your light into the universe. (Never leave burning candles unattended.)
