⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A freezer curse designed to inflict mental sickness and misery upon the target. The practitioner layers bones, graveyard dirt, dead flowers, bees, cat hair, vinegar, a rusty nail, and a photograph of the victim into a sealed bag, speaking a distinct incantation with each addition. The bag is frozen for one month, then buried in a cemetery to complete the working.

Quantities

1 small freezer bag
Chicken bones (dry)
Dead flowers
Graveyard dirt (fresh)
3 dead bees (or live)
Cat hair
Vinegar
1 small rusty nail
1 photograph of the target

Directions

  1. Place the bones, graveyard dirt, and dead flowers into the freezer bag.
  2. As you add them, speak the first incantation, calling upon the dead for aid.
  3. Next, add the bees to the bag while speaking the second incantation, directing the sting of the bee into the target's mind.
  4. Add the cat hair while speaking the third incantation, invoking the screech of the cat to drive the target to flee.
  5. Finally, add the vinegar, photograph of the target, and rusty nail while speaking the closing incantation, sealing the target in misery and sickness with no escape.
  6. Seal the bag and place it deep in the back of your freezer where no one will find it.
  7. After one month, take the bag to a cemetery and bury it.

Incantations

The dead I beg, please help me. Bring my sorrows down upon my enemy.
Grasp this plea; may their minds feel the sting of the bee!
When all is said and done, the screech of the cat will make them run.
They are now stewing in misery, they're now sick with dismay. No escaping, they cannot flee. So mote it be!
