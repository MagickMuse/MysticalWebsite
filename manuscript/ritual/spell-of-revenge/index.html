⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A waning-moon revenge hex targeting a faithless lover. A red candle anointed with Black Devil Oil is pricked three times with a needle at midnight while reciting an incantation invoking the goddess Venus. The hex inflicts impotence and heartbreak upon the target. The candle and needle are discarded at a crossroads or into flowing water.

Quantities

1 red candle
Black Devil Oil
1 needle

Directions

  1. This working is performed on the waning moon at midnight.
  2. Anoint a red candle with Black Devil Oil.
  3. Light the candle and prick it three times with a needle while reciting the incantation, inserting the target's name where indicated.
  4. The incantation invokes the goddess Venus to cast a hex of impotence and heartbreak upon a faithless lover.
  5. When finished, discard the candle and needle at a crossroads or cast them into flowing water.

Instructions

Perform this working on the waning moon at midnight. Anoint a red candle with Black Devil Oil. Light the candle and prick it three times with a needle while reciting:

Thrice the candle is broke by me,
thrice thy heart shall be as well.
You dirty cheat, burn in hell.
Impotence and heartache you will know.
In the name of the goddess Venus,
this hex I cast, lose all functioning of your penis.
May your life fall apart, and you die of a broken heart.

Discard the candle and the needle at a crossroads, or cast them into flowing water.
