🌿Cumin & Coriander Shamanic Healing Petition

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/MantraInvocation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Cumin seeds Coriander seeds
◇Directions
1. Pass handfuls of cumin and coriander seeds around the patient's head. 2. Holding the seeds in both hands, address the Spirits aloud, though traditionally one person performs this ritual on another's behalf: 3. "I place myself under your protection, under the protection of all the great ones and the small ones among you. I place myself under the protection of [invoke the names of spirits whose patronage and healing skills you seek]. If [patient's name], child of [parent's name], has wronged you, offended you, angered you, behaved badly, was angry, resentful, selfish, or rude [continue], please seek out the one who has done harm to her. Implore him to release her." [You may also ask the Spirit to intercede with God.] 4. Try to get the patient to chew the seeds. 5. If unable or unwilling, you chew them, spit them out, and immediately lick the patient's hands.
◇Description
A North African shamanic healing petition in which cumin and coriander seeds are passed around the patient's head while a spoken invocation requests djinn protection and the release of any harm caused. The patient or healer then chews the seeds, and the healer licks the patient's hands.
