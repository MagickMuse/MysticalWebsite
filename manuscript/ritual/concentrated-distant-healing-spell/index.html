💫Concentrated Distant Healing Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickIncense MagickWater MagickSalt MagickChant/MantraHealingElemental
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Four white candles in a square - A photograph of the person to be healed in the middle of the square - A floral incense stick - A bowl of salt - A bowl of water
◇Directions
1. Light the candles and say: "I send to you [name] the power of fire, your healing to quicken and inspire." Light the incense, passing it nine times around the candles and photograph, saying nine times: "I send to you [name] the power of air, your healing to restore and repair." Add six pinches of salt to the water, swirl six times, and sprinkle a circle of drops around the candles, saying: "Here in healing four powers unite, healing powerful, golden, bright, water, earth, air, and fire, bring the healing I desire." Let the candles and incense burn through and tip the water away into earth. 2. Leave the photo where natural light will shine on it. 3. Perform sunset nightly until things improve.
◇Description
A sunset spell for sending concentrated healing to a family member or friend many miles away, using four white candles in square with four elements to unite healing powers.
