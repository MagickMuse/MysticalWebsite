🕯️Pagan Memorial Candle Hex

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraReversal
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Black candle
◇Directions
1. Carve and dress a black candle as desired. 2. Reverse the candle by slicing the top flat and exposing the wick at the bottom. 3. Burn it while chanting aloud what you envision happening to your target.
◇Description
A secular alternative to the Church Memorial Candle Hex: a reversed black candle is burned while chanting what the caster envisions happening to the target, rather than reciting religious memorial prayers.
