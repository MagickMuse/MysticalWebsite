🌿Seven Blessings Bouquet Garni

◇Quantities
A square of cheesecloth Rosemary or thyme, for Health Marjoram or lemon balm, for Happiness Basil or rose petals, for Love Bay leaf or mint, for Prosperity Lavender or chamomile, for Peace Sage or dill, for Protection Parsley or cinnamon, for Good Fortune Kitchen twine
◇Directions
1. Cut a square of cheesecloth large enough to hold your chosen herbs. 2. Select an herb for each of the seven blessings: health, happiness, love, prosperity, peace, protection, and good fortune. 3. As you place each herb in the cheesecloth, name its blessing aloud. 4. Gather the corners of the cloth and tie them securely with kitchen twine, making seven knots, repeating with each knot: "Health, happiness, love, prosperity, peace, protection, good fortune." 5. Drop the bundle into your simmering pot. As the liquid bubbles, visualize golden light spreading through the food, and stir clockwise seven times, repeating the blessings once more. 6. Remove the bouquet garni before serving. As you ladle the food into bowls, hold the intention that each person receiving a portion also receives the seven blessings.
◇Description
A cooking charm made from herbs bundled in cheesecloth, stirred into soups or stews to infuse food with blessings of health, happiness, love, prosperity, peace, protection, and good fortune. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

The Seven Blessings Bouquet Garni transforms an ordinary meal into a spell of nourishment for body and spirit. While the classic witch's bottle stores blessings in a sealed container, the bouquet garni releases them slowly through heat and intention, allowing the magic to be literally absorbed by those who eat.

What is a Bouquet Garni?

In culinary terms, a bouquet garni is simply a bundle of herbs tied together or wrapped in cheesecloth, used to flavor soups, stews, and broths without leaving loose herbs floating in the dish. In magical practice, this humble kitchen tool becomes a vehicle for blessing.

The spell requires no announcement. The magic works whether or not your guests know it has been cast.

Variations

This spell works beautifully for family dinners, holiday gatherings, or meals prepared for someone who needs extra care. You may also prepare the bouquet garni in advance, storing it dried until needed. Simply recharge it with the seven blessings before use.
