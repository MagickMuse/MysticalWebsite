🌟Talisman to Make a Good Impression

◇Form
Talisman
◇Method
ConsecrationKnot MagickIncense MagickVisualizationManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• Sandalwood incense • Incense burner • Matches or lighter • Red nail polish or red paint • Small brush • 1 small stone • 1 piece of paper • 1 pen • 1 orange cloth pouch (preferably silk) • Cedar chips • Cinnamon • Dried parsley • 1 yellow ribbon, 6" long • Salt water
◇Directions
1. Cast a circle around the area where you will do your spell. 2. Fit the incense in its burner and light it. 3. Use the nail polish or paint to draw the rune Inguz (ᛜ), which looks like two ×s stacked one on top of the other, on the stone. 4. This rune represents new beginnings, fertility, and power. 5. While the nail polish or paint is drying, write on the paper what you intend to accomplish. 6. Whom do you wish to impress? 7. What results do you desire from this meeting? 8. As you write your list of objectives, see yourself already achieving them. 9. When you've finished, fold the paper so it's small enough to fit into the pouch and say aloud: 10. "This is now accomplished in harmony with Divine Will, my own true will, and for the good of all." 11. Put the stone, paper, cedar, cinnamon, and parsley into the pouch. 12. Tie the pouch closed with the ribbon, making three knots. 13. Hold the image of your success in your mind as you tie the knots. 14. Sprinkle the talisman with salt water, then hold it in the incense smoke for a few moments to charge the charm. 15. Open the circle. 16. To bring good luck, carry this talisman in your pocket or purse when you go to your meeting. 17. Just knowing it's there will increase your self-confidence and help you make a good impression.
◇Description
A lucky charm to boost self-confidence and help you shine during job interviews, presentations, or important meetings. Uses the Inguz rune for new beginnings and power.
