📿Your Badge of Courage

Making your mark in the world, climbing the corporate ladder, finishing school, dealing with competition, even finding a way to do what you love, all take courage. This spell can help you connect with your personal power and muster the courage you need to succeed.

Quantities

  • A tarot card that symbolizes courage to you
  • A pen or marker
  • A hole-punch tool
  • A piece of red ribbon about 24" long

Directions

  1. On the front of the card you’ve chosen, write your name.
  2. On the back of the card, write an affirmation to bolster your courage.
  3. Punch a hole through the card near the top, being careful not to destroy important imagery, and thread the ribbon through the hole.
  4. Tie the two ends of the ribbon together, making eight knots, and repeat your affirmation each time you tie a knot.
  5. Slip the ribbon over your head and let the “badge” hang near your heart when you feel a need for some extra courage.
