A profound visualization cleansing spell drawing on the sacred imagery of vultures as holy, protective beings. The caster gazes into an image of a vulture or Nekhebet (the Egyptian vulture deity) with outstretched wings, requests cleansing of all spiritual debris, and visualizes the vulture enfolding them with her wings. When she departs, the cleansing is complete. Not for the faint-hearted.

Quantities

  • Image of a vulture with outstretched wings and direct gaze (realistic, or an image of Nekhebet)

Directions

  1. Use either a realistic image of a vulture or an image of the Egyptian vulture deity, Nekhebet.
  2. What is crucial is that the image has outstretched wings and a direct gaze.
  3. Look the image in the eyes.
  4. Request that she come and cleanse you of all spiritual debris.
  5. Visualize the vulture enfolding you with her wings.
  6. When she releases you and departs, you are cleansed.
