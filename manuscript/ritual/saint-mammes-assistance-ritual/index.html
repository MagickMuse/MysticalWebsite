🌙Saint Mammes Assistance Ritual

◇Form
Offering RiteAltar Working
◇Method
PrayerPetitionDevotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Bread - Cheese - Candles
◇Directions
Traditionally performed at a church, but may also be accomplished at a home altar. 1. Place bread and cheese on the altar 2. Pray, petition, light candles, and communicate your desire to Saint Mammes 3. Bring your bread and cheese outside and offer them to the first person you see 4. If the person turns you down, keep offering until someone takes them
◇Description
A traditional ritual to petition Saint Mammes for assistance, especially with milk production for nursing mothers or tax relief.
