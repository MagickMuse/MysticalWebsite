💘Return a Wandering Lover

◇Form
Candle RiteDressed CandleWriting Spell
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraBlood Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 small piece of white paper - 1 needle (not a pin) or black/dragon's blood ink - 1 red votive candle - 1 cauldron to hold candle and melted wax
◇Directions
1. Inscribe the red candle with the runes Uruz, Tiwaz, Berkanan, Sowilo, Wunjo, Osa, and Gebo, along with the zodiac signs of both the intended and the seeker. 2. Light the candle. 3. If writing in your own blood, heat the tip of a needle in the flame and prick the thumb of the power hand (the one used for writing). 4. Using ink or blood, write your own name and the name of your lover on the white paper so the names form a circle. 5. Draw two circles around the names, then fold the paper toward yourself and touch it to the candle flame. 6. Speak: 7. The Blood speaks clearly! 8. What does it say? 9. Bring back my lover within one day! 10. For as I will, So Mote It Be! 11. Drop the entire paper into the candle flame, then let burn for one hour. 12. Bury the remains or dispose of them in the trash.
◇Description
A waxing Moon spell to bring back a lost lover, performed on a day of Venus during the hour of Venus or Moon. A red votive is inscribed with the runes Uruz, Tiwaz, Berkanan, Sowilo, Wunjo, Osa, and Gebo, along with the zodiac signs of both the seeker and the intended. The seeker writes both names in a circle on white paper using black ink, dragon's blood ink, or their own blood. The paper is folded toward the self and burned in the candle flame.
