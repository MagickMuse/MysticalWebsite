Flower essence remedies address nightmares at the emotional and spiritual level, building the capacity to understand and integrate disturbing dreams rather than suppress them. Once a nightmare is genuinely understood it tends to lose its hold. Note that this work can produce a temporary deepening of difficulty before clarity arrives; choose a stable time and setting to begin.

Quantities

  • Amaranth (Pegasus): calms and soothes disruption caused by disturbing dreams
  • Grey Spider Flower (Australian Bush): relieves terror and panic; assists with integrating mysterious or confusing nightmares
  • Saint John's Wort (FES, Green Hope): eases fear of dreams, darkness, or sleep; provides spiritual protection
  • Black-eyed Susan (FES, Green Hope, Australian Bush): supports understanding of nightmare content
  • Scarlet Pimpernel (FES): supports understanding of nightmare content
  • Snap Pea (Green Hope, Perelandra): supports understanding of nightmare content

Directions

  1. Choose one or more essences suited to the situation and take them according to the producer's instructions.
  2. Some initial intensification is a normal part of the process as buried material rises.
  3. Begin during a period of relative stability and with emotional support available.
