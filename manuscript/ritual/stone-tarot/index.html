A comprehensive divination system using 22 stones corresponding to the Major Arcana, based on Wiccan symbolism rather than Christian influences. Each stone carries specific divinatory meanings. The reader interprets the stones based on their symbolic associations, proximity to other stones, and relative positions to unlock psychic awareness and determine future trends.

Quantities

  • 22 polished or tumbled stones of approximately the same size:
  • 0. The Fool: Agate
  • 1. The Shaman: Quartz crystal
  • 2. The High Priestess: Emerald or pearl
  • 3. The Goddess: Peridot, olivine, or turquoise
  • 4. The God: Ruby
  • 5. The Chief: Topaz
  • 6. The Lovers: Rose quartz
  • 7. The Elements: Staurolite, cross stone, or twinned crystals
  • 8. Strength: Diamond, Herkimer diamond, or garnet
  • 9. The Wise Elder: Sapphire or blue tourmaline
  • 10. The Spiral: Sardonyx or black opal
  • 11. Justice: Carnelian
  • 12. Initiation: Beryl or aquamarine
  • 13. Change: Amber
  • 14. Temperance: Amethyst
  • 15. Folly: Black diamond, black tourmaline, or any square black stone
  • 16. Force: Lodestone or lava
  • 17. The Star: Meteorite or any star stone
  • 18. The Moon: Moonstone or chalcedony
  • 19. The Sun: Tiger's eye or sunstone
  • 20. Rebirth: Fossil
  • 21. The Universe: Opal or kunzite
  • Yellow cloth bag for storage
  • Optional: Yellow candles
  • Optional: Sandalwood incense
  • Optional: Psychic oils (tuberose, nutmeg, lemongrass, or sandalwood)
  • Optional: Psychic stones to wear (lapis lazuli, moonstone, azurite)

Directions

  1. Obtain all 22 stones and cleanse each thoroughly.
  2. Work with any unfamiliar stones until you know their symbolism and magickal uses.
  3. Study the divinatory information for each stone and attune with one stone at a time, relating the meanings to the stone itself.
  4. Continue this practice until you can recall each stone's meaning instantly when you see it.
  5. When not in use, store the stones in a yellow cloth bag.
  6. Expose them periodically to moonlight for purification.
  7. To perform a reading, wear psychic-influencing stones such as lapis lazuli, moonstone, or azurite if desired.
  8. Burn yellow candles and sandalwood incense.
  9. Anoint yourself with tuberose, nutmeg, lemongrass, or sandalwood oil.
  10. Cast the stones and interpret based on their divinatory meanings, proximity to other stones, and relative positions.
  11. Allow the overall picture to flash in your mind rather than relying on written meanings.
