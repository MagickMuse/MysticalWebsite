🍋Waning Crescent Cleansing Candle Spell

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
LunarBanishingCleansing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 lemon - Knife - White candle - Ground rosemary - Sea salt - Matches or lighter
◇Directions
1. Cut the lemon in half. 2. As the knife passes through, visualize it severing any unwanted energy, connections, or limiting ties you are releasing. 3. Take one half and trim a small, flat slice off the bottom so it can stand upright on its own. 4. Set the standing half aside, this is your candle holder. 5. Keep the other half in hand for the cleansing walk. 6. Anoint the white candle by rolling it through lemon juice, then roll it away from you through a mixture of ground rosemary and sea salt, coating it with banishing and cleansing energy. 7. Insert the base of the dressed candle into the wider, cut side of the standing lemon half, pressing it in firmly so the candle is stable and supported. 8. Set the lemon candle holder on your altar or a safe surface. 9. Do not light it yet. 10. Take the remaining lemon half and a generous handful of sea salt (or a bowl of saltwater) and walk counterclockwise through your home. 11. At every drain, sinks, tubs, showers, squeeze a little lemon juice down it, sending unwanted energy away. 12. Sprinkle sea salt or saltwater along thresholds, corners, and anywhere energy feels heavy, visualizing it dissolving and dispersing. 13. Return to the candle. 14. Light it. 15. Watch the flame as it warms and brightens the space, severing any remaining ties and renewing the energy around you. 16. Sprinkle a clockwise circle of sea salt around the lemon candle holder, visualizing a ring of protection forming around your home and your inner light. 17. Let the candle burn while you rest nearby, a moon bath or salt scrub makes a perfect accompaniment. 18. As the flame burns down into the lemon, the juice will naturally extinguish it, just as the spell extinguishes what you have released. 19. If the candle does not self-extinguish, snuff it rather than blowing it out. 20. Moon sign variations: - Aquarius moon, sever limiting beliefs, outdated identities, and restrictive connections; invoke inspiration and vision - Pisces moon, focus on healing old emotional wounds and releasing grief - Cancer moon, orient the walk as a thorough home cleansing and protection working
◇Description
A lemon-and-rosemary candle spell for cleansing home and spirit under the waning moon. Walk the home counterclockwise with a lemon half and sea salt to banish stale energy down every drain, then seat a white candle in the remaining lemon half and burn it as a circle of salt is laid around it for protection. The lemon's juice extinguishes the candle as it burns down, sealing the banishment. Adaptable to Aquarius (sever limiting beliefs), Pisces (heal old wounds), or Cancer (cleanse home) moon energies.
