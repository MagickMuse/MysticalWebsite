🌈Simple Chakra Balancing Spell

◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. Sit in a quiet, comfortable place and breathe slowly, deeply. 2. Focus your attention on the first (root) chakra and send beautiful, clear red light there. 3. Do this for a minute or two, until you feel warmth at this energy center. 4. Then shift your attention to the second (sacral) chakra and focus orange light there. 5. Repeat, moving up through your body, sending the appropriate-colored light to each chakra: yellow to the solar plexus, green to the heart, blue to the throat, indigo to the third eye, and purple to the crown. 6. By the time you reach the top of your head, you will feel more relaxed and balanced in body, mind, and spirit.
◇Description
A visualization spell using only imagination and willpower to balance and harmonize all seven chakras. Practice daily to maintain personal harmony and well-being.
