🛡️Four-Poster Nightmare Magick Square

◇Form
CharmTalisman
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 4 amulets (one per bedpost) - 1 additional amulet (to wear around the neck) - And/or: a piece of wormwood (to place under the mattress) - And/or: a metal implement such as a sword (to place under the bed)
◇Directions
1. Hang a different amulet from each corner of a four-poster bed to create a magickal protective square. 2. Wear a fifth amulet around your neck as the final reinforcing point. 3. If preferred, place a piece of wormwood under the mattress or a metal implement such as a sword under the bed in place of the neck amulet.
◇Description
A protective working using a four-poster bed as the frame of a magickal square. Amulets are placed at each corner, with a fifth worn at the throat. Wormwood or a metal implement may be added beneath the mattress or bed as an additional layer of reinforcement.
