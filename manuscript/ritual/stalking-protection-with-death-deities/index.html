🛡️Stalking Protection with Death Deities

◇Form
Dressed CandleWriting Spell
◇Method
BanishingCandle MagickPetitionProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 black candle - Fireproof candleholder - Banishing herb of choice (asafoetida recommended) - Carrier oil (jojoba recommended) - Paper and pen for petition - Carving tool (optional)
◇Directions
1. Being stalked creates a unique kind of terror. 2. Someone has fixed their attention on you in ways that feel inescapable. 3. This working petitions death deities to grant a specific boon: the death of the stalker's obsession with you. 4. Apollo serves this purpose well. 5. His arrows bring both healing and death, and ancient Greeks made offerings to him to ward off plague and pestilence. 6. However, any death deity you have a relationship with may be petitioned. 7. Choose based on your practice and existing connections. 8. Write your petition to the deity, stating clearly what you ask: that the stalker's fixation end, that their obsession with you die. 9. Fold the paper. 10. Select a banishing herb. 11. Asafoetida works powerfully here due to its strong associations with driving away evil. 12. Be warned: it earns its folk name "Devil's Dung" through an intensely unpleasant smell. 13. If the scent is unbearable, choose another banishing herb that aligns with your intention. 14. Choose an oil for anointing. 15. Jojoba carries magick for overcoming obstacles and refusing to give up, making it well suited for this work where you need the stalker's hold on you to break completely. 16. Place a pinch of your chosen herb on top of the folded petition. 17. Set your fireproof candleholder on top of both, anchoring the spell components. 18. Anoint your black candle with the oil, focusing on your intention. 19. If you know the stalker's name, carve it into the candle surface. 20. Place the dressed candle in the holder and light it. 21. Allow the candle to burn completely if safe to do so. 22. Dispose of remains at a crossroads or bury off your property.
◇Description
A petition spell invoking death deities to end a stalker's obsession. Uses black candle magick with banishing herbs and obstacle-breaking oil to sever the harmful fixation at its root.
