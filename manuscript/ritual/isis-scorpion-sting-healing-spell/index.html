🦂Isis Scorpion Sting Healing Spell

Charm

Spell

None required
◇Directions
1. An important principle of Egyptian medical magick was identification with the gods. 2. The magickian pronouncing a spell takes on the identity of a healing deity, while the afflicted person is identified with the ailing child Horus. 3. We are all vulnerable, like a child, when suffering and unwell. 4. By establishing a divine precedent for recovery, these healing formulas offer reassurance that the cure will work. 5. The magickian assumes the identity of Isis, the Great Lady of Magick (Weret Hekau). 6. The patient is identified with the ailing child from the myth. 7. Speak the incantation aloud to call the poison out of the body. 8. Egyptian magick required the spoken word (djed medu, 'the words to be said') to activate its power. 9. How something is said matters as much as what is said: tone shifts, emphasis, and vocalization were part of proper incantation. 10. Recite: 'May the child live and the poison die, and those who suffer will be cured likewise.' 11. The spell establishes a divine precedent: just as Isis healed the poisoned child in the myth, so too will this patient recover. 12. Recovery is proof that the magick has worked.
◇Description
Egyptian healing spell for scorpion stings and poisoning. Based on the myth where Isis's tamed seven scorpions bit another woman's son, and Isis used her power to call the poison out.
