A fidelity powder combining pulverized bedsheet fabric with ground allspice, cloves, Deer's Tongue, mullein, sage, and vetiver in a rice powder base. Dusted on the body before bed to keep a lover from wandering.

Quantities

Strip of bedsheet (ideally soiled with both partners' sexual fluids); allspice berries; cloves; Deer's Tongue herb; mullein; sage; vetiver; rice powder base

Directions

  1. Cut a strip from a bedsheet, preferably soiled with both partners' sexual fluids. Pulverize it completely into a powder by any means necessary.
  2. Grind allspice berries, cloves, Deer's Tongue, mullein, sage, and vetiver into a fine powder.
  3. Blend the two powders together.
  4. Add the combined powder into a rice powder base.
  5. Dust yourself with this powder before going to bed with your partner.
