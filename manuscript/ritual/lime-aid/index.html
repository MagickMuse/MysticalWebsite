Lime

A magickal lime beverage for healing, purification, and drawing solar energy. Perfect for hot weather refreshment with intention.

Quantities

  • Fresh lime juice (from 4-6 limes)
  • Sugar or honey to taste
  • Water (4 cups)
  • Ice

Directions

  1. Juice fresh limes, straining out seeds and pulp if desired.
  2. Combine lime juice with water and sweeten to taste with sugar or honey.
  3. Honey adds additional magickal properties of healing and sweetness.
  4. As you stir the mixture, do so clockwise to draw in positive energy, or counterclockwise for purification and release.
  5. Set your intention over the drink before consuming.
  6. This beverage draws on lime's solar correspondence and is excellent for healing work, cleansing the body, or simply inviting more sunshine into your day.
