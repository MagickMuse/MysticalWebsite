A waning moon spell to purify a room using fire, incense, and blessed water. A white candle and frankincense are carried around the space to drive out chaos, then the room is asperged with a sprig of white heather dipped in blessed water. All materials are taken outdoors afterward and returned to the earth.

Quantities

  • 1 tray
  • 1 sprig white heather
  • 1 white candle in a holder
  • Frankincense incense in a holder
  • 1 bowl of blessed water

Directions

  1. Arrange the sprig of white heather, the lit white candle in its holder, the lit frankincense in its holder, and a bowl of blessed water on the tray.
  2. Take the candle and the incense and walk around the interior of the room.
  3. Speak:
  4. By fire and flame, by smoke and scent;
    I drive from this room all chaos and dissent.
  5. Asperge the room using the heather sprig dipped in the blessed water, sprinkling the baseboards, walls, cornices, and floor.
  6. Speak:
  7. By water and salt, by herb and flower;
    Peace and contentment return in this hour.
  8. Take the tray out of doors and speak:
  9. Follow the light, follow the scent.
    Into the Earth and into the Air,
    Energies gathered here I now vent.
  10. Blow out the candle, bury the incense, lay the sprig over it, and pour out the water on top.
  11. Bury the candle or dispose of it in the trash.
