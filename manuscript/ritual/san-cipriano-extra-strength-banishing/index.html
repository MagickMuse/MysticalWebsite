For confronting full-strength major evil entities, this advanced working empowers San Cipriano Oil with frankincense and benzoin, then uses alternating brown and white candles arranged in a large protective circle. The practitioner stands within, invokes San Cipriano's presence, and follows with the Bell, Book, and Candle banishing. Extensive cleansing is performed once the candles have burned down.

Quantities

  • San Cipriano Oil
  • Frankincense
  • Benzoin
  • Brown candles (sufficient to form a standing circle, alternating with white)
  • White candles (sufficient to form a standing circle, alternating with brown)

Directions

  1. Empower San Cipriano Oil by adding frankincense and benzoin.
  2. Arrange sufficient alternating brown and white candles in a circle large enough to stand within comfortably.
  3. From inside the circle, light the candles.
  4. Request San Cipriano's presence and call the spirits' bluff, telling them he is on the way.
  5. Accompany this with the Bell, Book, and Candle banishing spell.
  6. When the candles have burned down, continue with extensive cleansing.
