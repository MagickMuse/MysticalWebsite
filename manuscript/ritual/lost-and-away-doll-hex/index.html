🛎️Lost and Away Doll Hex

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
BanishingBindingPowder Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Rag doll Enemy's name label and intimate items if possible Lost and Away Powder (made from crossroads dirt and exorcism agents) Black wax Paper bag or shoebox
◇Directions
1. Personalize a rag doll: label it with your enemy's name and add intimate items if possible. 2. Put the doll inside a paper bag or a shoebox. 3. Sprinkle it with Lost and Away Powder. 4. Melt black wax gently in a double boiler or bain-marie on the stove. 5. Pour the wax over the doll and sprinkle it with more Lost and Away Powder before the wax hardens. 6. Bury the doll in the ground away from your home. 7. Visit the doll's grave once a week and sprinkle the spot with Lost and Away Powder.
◇Description
Technically a banishing spell delivered with malice. A personalized rag doll is coated in black wax and Lost and Away Powder (made from crossroads dirt and exorcism agents) then buried away from the home, with weekly visits to maintain the hex.
