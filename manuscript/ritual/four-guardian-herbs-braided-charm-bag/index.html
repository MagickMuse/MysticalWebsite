Hyssop, lavender, patchouli and rue are said to make evil and malevolent forces unable to exist in their presence. Their combined power multiplies when they are kept together, either growing side by side or braided into a red charm bag.

Quantities

Hyssop (1 sprig, or a living plant)
Lavender (1 sprig, or a living plant)
Patchouli (1 sprig, or a living plant)
Rue (1 sprig, or a living plant)
Red fabric bag

Directions

  1. For the strongest protection, grow hyssop, lavender, patchouli and rue together as living plants, since their power multiplies when they are kept side by side.
  2. For protection you can take with you, gather one sprig of each plant.
  3. Braid or weave the four sprigs together while concentrating on what you want.
  4. Tuck the braid into a red fabric bag and keep it on you.
