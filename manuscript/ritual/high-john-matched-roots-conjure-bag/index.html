Matched male and female High John roots are dressed separately, the female with damiana-and-catnip-infused sweet almond oil, the male with Magnet Oil and High John Oil, placed together in a red flannel bag and sprinkled with love-drawing powder. Lodestones with magnetic sand may be substituted. ⚠️ High John Matched Roots Conjure Bag is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Matched male and female High John the Conqueror roots
Dried damiana
Dried catnip
Sweet almond oil
Magnet Oil
High John the Conqueror Oil
Red flannel drawstring bag
Love-drawing powder

Alternative: matched lodestones + magnetic sand instead of roots

Directions

  1. Obtain matched male and female High John the Conqueror roots.
  2. Steep dried damiana and catnip in sweet almond oil overnight, then strain out the botanicals.
  3. Dress the female root with this infused sweet almond oil.
  4. Dress the male root with Magnet Oil and High John the Conqueror Oil.
  5. Place them both inside a red flannel drawstring bag.
  6. Sprinkle with a love-drawing powder until your desire is fulfilled.
  7. As an alternative, lodestones may be substituted for High John the Conqueror; sprinkle with magnetic sand instead.
