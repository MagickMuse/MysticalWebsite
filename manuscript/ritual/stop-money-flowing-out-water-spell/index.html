💧Stop Money Flowing Out Water Spell

A Saturday after dusk water spell to stop money from flowing out too quickly, using a sink to symbolically control the flow of money with plugging and slowing the water.

Quantities

  • A sink or washbasin

Directions

  1. Turn on the cold tap, leaving the plug out.
  2. As the water flows away, say: "Flowing and going, going and flowing, stop the outgoing tide." Slow the flow of water and put in the plug; as you do so, say: "Slowing the flowing, slowing the going, slowing outgoing tide. Stay money, stay, flow inward and not flow away." Pull out the plug, letting the water flow away.
  3. Turn the tap on again even more slowly and leave the plug in, saying: "Money flow slowly, inward and stay. Money flow slowly; in, not away." When the sink is half full, turn off the tap, leaving the water with the plug in for at least ten minutes before letting out the water.
  4. Repeat weekly, each time leaving the final water an extra five minutes before emptying.
