🕯️Nine Candle Spiral Ritual

◇Form
Altar WorkingCandle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickMeditationVisualization
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• 1 cauldron or fireproof container • Soil or kitty litter (to fill cauldron) • 9 tapered candles • Matches or lighter
◇Directions
1. Fill your cauldron with soil or kitty litter; the cauldron symbolizes the womb of the Goddess. 2. Take nine tapered candles and plant them into the earth in a spiral pattern, beginning in the center and continuing in a clockwise direction. 3. With each candle, contemplate a different aspect of Imbolc. 4. Light the candle in the middle first, picturing light penetrating winter's darkness. 5. As you light the second, welcome the approaching spring. 6. Think of the possibilities contained in new life and new beginnings as you light the third. 7. With the fourth, imagine your own rebirth and your relationship to the Divine Mother. 8. As you light the fifth, consider the lessons your spiritual path has taught you; give thanks for challenges met and knowledge gained. 9. The sixth candle represents the unknown, the lessons that lie before you and all the things you have yet to learn. 10. Light the seventh and meditate on things you wish to change, whether mental, physical, emotional, or spiritual, and think of how you can use your magick to make positive changes in your life. 11. The eighth represents the things you wish to heal, whether physical ailments, the suffering of the planet, rifts in relationships, and so on; make room for the healing to begin within you, release old wounds and past hurts, take responsibility for your health, and focus on the best possible outcomes for situations that are beyond your control. 12. As you light the ninth and final candle, welcome inspiration into your life. 13. Sing or play music. 14. Write a poem in Brigid's honor. 15. Paint a picture. 16. Even if you don't think you are particularly artistic, use the energy of the season and enjoy the creative process. 17. Fashion a special charm or a new blend of incense. 18. Whatever your chosen method of expression, ask the Goddess to inspire you and lend her beauty to your work.
◇Description
A fire ritual honoring Brigid at Imbolc. Nine tapered candles are planted in a spiral pattern in a cauldron filled with soil, each representing a different aspect of the sabbat: light penetrating darkness, welcoming spring, new beginnings, rebirth, lessons learned, the unknown, transformation, healing, and inspiration.
