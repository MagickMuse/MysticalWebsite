🔥Someone Special Name Paper Orange Blossom Ash Vial Spell

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingManifestationKnot Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Paper or parchment (for names) Cauldron, fireproof bowl, or pan Orange blossoms, leaves, or zest Small box or perfume vial Wax or silk thread (to seal)
◇Directions
1. Write the desired one's name on a strip of paper or parchment. 2. Write your desired goal on another strip. 3. Cover the other person's name with your own. 4. Place both in a cauldron, fireproof bowl, or pan together with orange blossoms and/or orange leaves and/or orange zest. 5. Burn everything. 6. Gather the ashes. Slip them into a small box or perfume vial. If a bottle, seal shut with wax; if a box, use knot magick (silk threads) to close. If you someday no longer desire this relationship, break the seal and scatter the contents to the winds.
◇Description
Name papers for the target and the spell-caster's goal are layered, burned with orange blossoms in a cauldron, and the ashes sealed in a vial or knotted box to bind the desire.
