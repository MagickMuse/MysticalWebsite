🪢Stop An Enemy

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
VisualizationCord Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 small box (wooden or cardboard) 1 photograph of the target 1-2 yards of black ribbon 1 pair of chicken feet
◇Directions
1. Write the name and birthdate of the target on the back of their photograph. 2. Hold the photograph against both chicken feet, face outward, and wrap them together with a third of the black ribbon. 3. Place the wrapped bundle into a small box and tie the box closed with the remaining ribbon. 4. As you wrap the ribbon around the bundle, visualize as clearly as possible your enemy becoming completely immobilized, unable to attack you any longer. 5. When you tie the box closed and when you bury it, visualize again as clearly and forcefully as possible that it is not the bundle you have tied into the box, but your victim. 6. Bury the box three feet into the ground at a crossroads or in a cemetery. 7. As you leave the area, do not look back at the burial site.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A binding curse to completely immobilize an enemy. The target's photograph is inscribed with their name and birthdate, held against a pair of chicken feet, and wrapped with black ribbon. The bundle is placed in a box, tied closed, and buried three feet deep at a crossroads or cemetery. The practitioner must not look back after burial. The spirits invoked will inflict unbearable pain and prevent the target from finding any escape.
