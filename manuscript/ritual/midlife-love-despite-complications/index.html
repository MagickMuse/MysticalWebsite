💚Midlife Love Despite Complications

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraKnot MagickBinding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A green candle - Two small boxes, filled with old buttons and beads and lots of knots of black thread - A large empty box with a lid - Two spools of green thread
◇Directions
1. Light the candle, saying: "Love's not easy, commitments piled high, baggage reaching to the sky. But we will win through, me and you, our hearts are willing, our love is true." Start emptying the small boxes alternately into the bigger one, saying: "Loose ends, tangled threads, we both our luggage haul, need a carriage for a marriage, but better encumbered, sooner lumbered, than not together at all." When everything is in the large box, put or cram on the lid and start wrapping it, first with the one spool of thread going around the big box in one direction, then the second spool going around the box in the other direction, securing them together with big knots. 2. Then say: "Tied together, just about, we'll muddle through, not a doubt. If things spill out, we'll laugh, not shout." Blow out the candle, saying: "Together forever." Find somewhere safe to keep your togetherness box. 3. Perform Thursday evening.
◇Description
A Thursday evening spell for love in midlife when you've both got kids, interfering exes, and alimony claims, emptying two boxes of buttons beads and black thread knots into large box then wrapping with two spools of green thread saying love's not easy baggage reaching sky but we will win through tied together forever better encumbered than not together at all.
