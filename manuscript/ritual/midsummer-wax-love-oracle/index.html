🔮Midsummer Wax Love Oracle

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
• Piece of wax • Unwashed sock • Dish of cold water
◇Directions
1. On Midsummer's Eve, place a piece of wax inside an unwashed sock and sleep with it under your pillow. 2. In the morning, remove the wax and melt it. 3. Pour the melted wax into a dish of cold water and observe the shape it assumes as it hardens. 4. Interpret the resulting form as a symbol or image relevant to your future love life.
◇Description
A Midsummer's Eve ceromancy in which wax placed inside an unwashed sock is slept upon overnight, then melted and poured into cold water the following morning. The shape the hardening wax assumes reveals something of the practitioner's future love life.
