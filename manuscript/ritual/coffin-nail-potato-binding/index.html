🪢Coffin Nail Potato Binding

Two matched potato halves carved with each person's name and identifying information, spread with honey, nailed together with a new or coffin nail, bound with red silk cord, and buried by moonlight. The unused halves are cooked with basil and parsley and shared with the lover.

Quantities

Two potatoes of similar shape and size
Honey
New nail or coffin nail
Red silk cord
Basil (for the shared meal)
Parsley (for the shared meal)

Directions

  1. Take two potatoes of similar shape and size and slice each in half vertically.
  2. Set one half of each aside for the charm; cook the remaining halves garnished with basil and parsley and eat them together with your lover.
  3. Carve your name, initials, birthdate, and identifying details into the cut side of your potato half, and your partner's information into the other.
  4. Spread honey over both cut sides.
  5. Press the two halves together so they appear as one whole potato.
  6. Drive a new nail or coffin nail horizontally through both halves, going through your partner's half first so the nail head is on their side and the point comes out through yours.
  7. Bind the potatoes together further with red silk cord.
  8. By moonlight, bury the bound potato in earth.
  9. If you have no access to land, bury it in a flowerpot and grow love herbs or a cactus.
