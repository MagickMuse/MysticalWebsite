🕯️Consecration of a Statue

An elaborate ritual for enlivening a deity statue with herbs selected by correspondence, Sacred Cow materials (butter, cheese, milk), and inviting the Divine to inhabit the image. Creates a focal point for practice.

Quantities

  • Altar arranged as for Full Moon Esbat
  • Statue to be consecrated
  • 1 herb from Goddess, God, or Dual Deity listing
  • 1 herb each from the matching Elementals
  • Muslin or cotton pouch with drawstring (or string to tie)
  • Small amount of butter
  • Soft white cheese (brie, cottage cheese, yogurt, or sour cream)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup spring water
  • Black cloth to cover statue
  • Offering dish
  • Flowers
  • Food item for offering
  • White candle
  • Incense (frankincense)

Directions

  1. Perform during Full Moon Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.
  2. Select one herb from each deity category according to the statue image.
  3. The Goddess herbs are marjoram, moonwort, and elder flower; her Earth elementals are cypress, honeysuckle, and jasmine; her Air elementals are anise seed, comfrey, elder wood, eyebright, hazel, lavender, and mugwort; her Fire elementals are angelica, celandine, coriander, heliotrope, hyssop, nettle, primrose, and rowan; her Water elementals are chamomile, camphor, catnip, geranium, hawthorn, hyacinth, ivy, rose, and willow.
  4. The God herbs are woodruff, yarrow, and bergamot; his Earth elementals are cedar, fern, High John the Conqueror, horehound, and pine; his Air elementals are acacia, benzoin, mistletoe, nutmeg, thyme, and wormwood; his Fire elementals are alder, basil, betony, cinnamon, clove, holly, oak, peppercorn, and thistle; his Water elementals are ash, burdock, hops, orris root, and yarrow.
  5. For a Dual Deity statue, the herbs are mullein, dianthus, and heather; their Earth elementals are cinquefoil, mandrake, patchouli, sage, and slippery elm; their Air elementals are eucalyptus, lemon verbena, mugwort, peppermint, sandalwood, and spearmint; their Fire elementals are bay, juniper, marigold, rosemary, rue, saffron, St. Johnswort, and vervain; their Water elementals are apple, elecampane, heather, meadowsweet, poppy, star anise, and shamrock.
  6. Place the cauldron on pentacle; hold each herb over the cauldron before dropping in:
  7. I call upon thee [herb] to lend unto me thy power for the consecration and enlivening of this image of [deity name].
  8. Stir all with athame:
  9. Through the power of the Goddess and the God are these herbs blessed to enliven this image of [deity name].
  10. Place the herbs in a small pouch.
  11. If it has drawstrings, wrap the ends around the stuffed bag; otherwise fold the pouch shut.
  12. Set the cauldron aside.
  13. Pass the herbal pouch through each symbol of the Elementals:
  14. By Earth you have form [sprinkle with salt],
    By Air you have breath [pass through incense smoke],
    By Fire you have energy [pass through candle flame],
    By Water you have the fluids of life [sprinkle with water].
  15. Place the pouch on the pentacle.
  16. With athame in power hand, raise open arms overhead to gather in energy:
  17. I call upon the Power of the Lady and the Lord to bless and empower these herbs with their divine essence.
  18. Bring both hands together to hold the knife then touch the tip to the pouch, seeing energy flow through the blade into the pouch, then set down the athame.
  19. Place both hands with palms down above the pouch:
  20. By Spirit are you charged and enlivened.
  21. The image is now given spiritual birth through the materials of the Sacred Cow (as found in India and in Hathor, the ancient goddess of Egypt portrayed as the Sacred Cow with solar disk between her horns):
  22. With the essences of the Sacred Cow, who gives of herself in many ways for the lives of others, is this image now blessed.
  23. Rub the statue all over with soft butter:
  24. With butter are you anointed.
  25. Rub it all over with soft cheese (brie, cottage cheese, yogurt, or sour cream):
  26. With cheese are you fed.
  27. Wash the statue with about half of the cup of milk:
  28. With milk are you given the substance of life.
  29. Rinse the statue off with spring water or running water:
  30. With water are you cleansed and purified.
  31. Dry the statue and anoint it with consecration oil:
  32. In the names of the Goddess and the God, I consecrate this statue.
  33. Let this image of [Name] draw [His/Her] divine power into my home and into my Craft.
  34. Let this image remind me that [Name] is always close to me, as I am always close to [Name].
  35. Pick up the herb bundle and tuck it securely inside the statue, closing off the bottom with a piece of felt cut to fit and glued along the edges if needed.
  36. Usually the curves of the figure are sufficient to hold the pouch in place.
  37. Set the image on the pentacle and cover it completely with a black cloth.
  38. With athame (for images of the God and Dual Deity) or with wand (for images of the Goddess), call the Divine into the statue.
  39. Hold the athame or wand upright, with arms raised and open before the covered statue:
  40. I call upon thee Elemental Earth, Elemental Air, Elemental Fire, and Elemental Water to bring thy energy and thy power that this image of [Deity Name] be made ready for enlivening.
  41. Bring hands together to hold the tool and slowly point it towards the statue:
  42. Come Great Lady [or Great Lord, or Great Lady and Great Lord] and inhabit this image I have prepared for You.
  43. Let Your presence in my life and in my home be a comfort and joy to me.
  44. Touch the statue with the tip of the tool and feel the power of the Divine entering into the image.
  45. Leave the statue on the pentacle for grounding and set the tool back on the altar.
  46. Carefully unveil the statue and set aside the cloth.
  47. Verbally welcome the deity by name and place an offering before it (cornbread muffin, fruit, or flowers, or use a dish with food and a small cup with the rest of the milk or other beverage).
  48. Light an incense such as frankincense on the right side of the statue and a white candle on the left side:
  49. Welcome to my home and my heart, Great Lady/Lord [Name].
  50. I am blessed by thy presence, and my blessings I give unto Thee.
  51. Let the light of Thy love be with me always.
  52. After an hour, put the statue in the intended place (Altar, Shrine, etc.).
  53. Offerings, incense and candles will change in accordance with the need, the season, the Sabbat, and so forth because the statue is now a focal point for the practice.
  54. Talk to and meditate on the image, for this ancient practice strengthens the connection to the Divine.
  55. For statues with no hollow space for an herb bundle, touch the herb pouch to the statue.
  56. Lightly anoint the image with consecration oil, then set the statue on the pentacle and cover it with a black cloth with the herb bundle under the cloth as well (under the statue, behind it, or draped against it).
  57. Follow the rest of the procedure as above, keeping the herb bundle close to the statue, perhaps in a small covered jar behind the image.
  58. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
