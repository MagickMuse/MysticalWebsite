✈️Sagittarius Fun and Adventure Ritual

◇Form
Writing SpellAltar Working
◇Method
VisualizationManifestationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• 1 brown crayon or marker • 1 picture of your partner • Adhesive tape • 1 sheet of white paper • Pictures of places you would like to go (printed from Internet) • 1 photo of yourself • 1 piece of cardboard or a bulletin board
◇Directions
1. With the brown crayon or marker, draw mud all around your partner's photo (loosely attached with adhesive tape to the center of a sheet of white paper), taking care not to mark the picture, and say, "Stick-in-the-mud, though dearly I love you, you're driving me nuts by what you won't do." Carefully lift up your partner's photo from the mud picture, saying, "Dear Stick-in-the-mud, come out to play. We really could share such wonderful days, adventures, and outings, brimming with laughter. Don't stay in your mud, waiting for happy hereafter." Stick your partner's photo next to yours on the cardboard or bulletin board with pictures of places where you would like to go, and put the cardboard where it can be seen by your partner. 2. Dispose of the mud picture.
◇Description
A Sagittarius zodiac ritual for bringing fun and adventure back into your life if you have a stick-in-the-mud partner, drawing mud around their photo then moving them to adventure vision board.
