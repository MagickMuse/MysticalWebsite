A spell to welcome late unexpected pregnancy to existing family or to start second family, threading colored beads of different sizes to represent unity and new harmony.

Quantities

  • Colored beads of different sizes and a string on which to thread them

Directions

  1. In the center of the string, thread a large bead for the parents, a medium-size one for existing children (even if adult), and a smaller one for the anticipated or desired arrival.
  2. Say as you thread: "We are one family, we are a unity, but now we welcome new life and new vitality, always together, but adding new harmony." Add an even number of tiny beads on each side of the others, naming for each one the benefits and blessings an increasing family will bring.
  3. Wear the necklace or bracelet or hang it behind the front door.
  4. Touch each bead weekly and repeat the spell words until the birth.
  5. Perform when the pregnancy occurs or is planned.
