A long-working maple spell jar for drawing money, love, and abundance. Maple sap moves slowly, and this jar works the same way, results are gradual but lasting. Maple syrup, jasmine, and cinnamon are layered with an intention paper and lucky coins or bills, then sealed and fed regularly with a green candle to keep the magick active.

Quantities

  • Small jar with lid
  • Lucky bills or coins
  • Maple syrup (enough to fill the jar; honey or sugar syrup may substitute)
  • Pen and paper, or Seed Planting Paper
  • Dried jasmine
  • 3 cinnamon sticks
  • Green candle
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Place your lucky bills or coins into the jar, these act as the seed of what you wish to grow.
  2. Fill the jar halfway with maple syrup.
  3. On a piece of paper (or Seed Planting Paper), write your intention clearly.
  4. Focus on it fully, then blow your breath across the paper to carry your will into the words.
  5. Fold the paper toward yourself, drawing the intention inward, and lay it into the syrup.
  6. Sprinkle in most of the dried jasmine, set aside a small pinch for the candle, then nestle in the cinnamon sticks.
  7. Fill the remainder of the jar with syrup until it reaches the top, then close the lid.
  8. Anoint the green candle with a small amount of maple syrup.
  9. Roll the candle toward yourself through the reserved jasmine and the pinch of ground cinnamon, pulling abundance and money in your direction as you work.
  10. Affix the dressed candle to the top of the jar lid and light it.
  11. As it burns, visualize abundance flowing steadily toward you.
  12. To feed and maintain the working, affix and burn a new candle atop the jar regularly.
  13. Choose a rhythm that resonates: every new and full moon, every Monday (Moon's day), Thursday (Jupiter's day), or Friday (Venus's day).
  14. Burning once a week is ideal for as long as you wish to keep the spell active.
