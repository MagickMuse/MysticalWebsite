🌒Waxing Crescent Moon

A ritual for seeking an Otherworld Companion, performed during Sidhe Moon Esbat. Using a black mirror as a portal, the practitioner invites an Other from the misty Otherworld forest to share tea and discuss companionship. If a Companion is found, a crystal is sought as a gateway for passage.

Quantities

  • Black mirror
  • Gray candle (from Sidhe Moon Esbat)
  • Table with 2 chairs (East side facing West for practitioner, West side facing East for Otherworld guest)
  • Tea service for two
  • Sugar and milk
  • Biscuits or cookies
  • Bowl of flowers
  • Bowl of milk (as offering)

Directions

  1. Perform as part of Sidhe Moon Esbat at the ritual work point.
  2. Go to West side of table and pull chair aside to offer a seat, then return to East side.
  3. Sit and look into black mirror beyond candle flame:
  4. Here lies the doorway into Otherworld; I welcome my guest through this portal.
  5. Gaze into mirror.
  6. Envision a misty, fog-shrouded Otherworld forest.
  7. Feel the cool, refreshing air.
  8. Smell the damp leaves on the forest floor, the moss on the ancient large trees.
  9. Hear the sound of water gurgling along a narrow brook.
  10. Hear the soft, delicate, hesitant footfalls of a browsing deer.
  11. Listen for the quiet steps of an Other approaching through the dark forest, coming toward you.
  12. The forest will remain in view behind the Guest so that the table will appear to be partly in woodland.
  13. The sounds of forest animals and the brook will continue in the background during the ritual.
  14. When the Other is gazing back, lift bowls of flowers and milk in each hand:
  15. Hail to thee and blessed be thy feet that brought you on this path; blessed be thy heart that beats steadfast; blessed be thy eyes that see between the worlds; blessed be thy hands offered in friendship and clasped in mine.
  16. Set bowls down to flank the Guest's tea setting.
  17. Offer both hands together, palms up, fingers bent toward palms:
  18. I am honored by your presence, and you are welcome at this table set between the worlds.
  19. Release handclasp and introduce yourself by Craft Name:
  20. I greet you most heartily, and I am called [Craft Name].
  21. Please sit with me here, that I may serve you tea.
  22. Serve tea for both places, adding sugar and milk, and biscuit or cookie on saucer edges.
  23. Open arms, palms up, and bless the meal:
  24. I know of my needs and offer my appreciation to that which sustains me.
  25. May I ever remember the blessings of my Lady and my Lord.
  26. The Lord brings spiritual life through the bounty of the Lady of Otherworld, that all is created in undying beauty.
  27. I honor the inner beauty of the spirit.
  28. Eat and drink with Guest, visiting and discussing the desire for a Companion.
  29. The food and drink of the Other is not consumed in a material manner, but the flavor or essence will be removed; do not eat or drink of it.
  30. If this is to be your Companion, ask to find a crystal suitable as passage that may be carried as the gateway for the Companion.
  31. When finished, stand:
  32. I am blessed by your having shared this tea with me.
  33. My blessings I give to thee.
  34. We came in friendship and depart in friendship.
  35. Merry we meet, merry we part, and merry we will meet again.
  36. Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again.
  37. See the Guest stand.
  38. Extend your hands palm up and feel the cool touch of the Other's hand in farewell.
  39. Sit before the candle and mirror.
  40. Envision the misty forest becoming darker.
  41. The quiet footfalls of the departing Guest disappear into the Wild Wood.
  42. The splashing water of the brook becomes fainter.
  43. The deer bounds off into the depths of the wood.
  44. The forest disappears, and the door closes.
  45. You are looking into the black mirror and see yourself looking back.
  46. Proceed to Sidhe Moon Esbat Conclusion and Fairy Tea and Cookies.
