A reversed black pillar candle is carved, dressed with Commanding Oil, and burned atop the toilet tank as an angry plea for protection and vindication. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

⚠️ Retribution Candle is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Black pillar candle
Commanding Oil
Powdered sweet flag (calamus)
Powdered licorice root
Dish
Optional: brown paper square for a name paper
Optional: Goofer Dust

Directions

  1. While the beef tongue is cooking or once it has been placed in the freezer, prepare a black pillar candle.
  2. Slice off the top of the candle so that it is completely flat.
  3. Carve the bottom of the candle, exposing the wick so it can be lit; the candle has now been reversed.
  4. Carve and dress this candle as you see fit, including Commanding Oil.
  5. Pour powdered sweet flag (calamus) and powdered licorice root onto a dish.
  6. Set the upside-down candle onto the dish and burn it atop the toilet tank.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: This spell belongs to the beef tongue courtcase lineage rooted in Hoodoo tradition. These are closed practices. Please research and respect the cultural boundaries before working with these methods.

This fierce candle ritual accompanies beef tongue spells, functioning as a furious plea for protection and vindication.

Optional enhancements:

Name papers may be placed beneath the candle on the dish so that the melting wax drips over them. If a single individual is the focus of the working, write their name on a square of brown paper and place it under the candle.

If you are truly furious, or truly frightened, and feel compelled to destroy your adversary's very ability to testify, replace the herb bed with Goofer Dust, or use it in addition to the powdered herbs.

It is imperative that these candles be burned in the bathroom. They are descendants of outhouse spells; older versions called for reversed black candles to be left burning all night in a dish of feces.
