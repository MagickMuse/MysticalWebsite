🪴India Moss Crystal

A crystal elixir working: an India moss crystal is left in water overnight, allowing the stone's fertility properties to infuse the water. The woman drinks this charged water in the morning. Tradition holds that a woman who drinks it will never be sterile. One of the Fertility Potion methods.

Quantities

  • 1 India moss crystal
  • A glass of water

Directions

  1. Place the India moss crystal in a glass of water.
  2. Leave it overnight.
  3. In the morning, remove the crystal and drink the water.
