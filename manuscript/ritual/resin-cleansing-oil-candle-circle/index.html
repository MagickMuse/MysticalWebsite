A two-part working combining a purifying oil blend with a candle circle ritual. The oil is a blend of cleansing resins and woods in a jojoba base. Candles anointed with it define a sacred circle for seated purification.

Quantities

  • Jojoba oil (base)
  • Frankincense (EO or dried botanical)
  • Benzoin (EO or dried botanical)
  • Sandalwood (EO or dried botanical)
  • Myrrh (EO or dried botanical)
  • Liquidambar/sweetgum (EO or dried botanical)
  • 4+ white candles

Directions

  1. Blend frankincense, benzoin, sandalwood, myrrh, and liquidambar into jojoba oil using either essential oils or dried botanicals.
  2. Anoint at least four white candles with the oil.
  3. Arrange the candles in a circle large enough to sit within comfortably.
  4. If using exactly four, place one at each cardinal point.
  5. Light the candles and sit within the circle, remaining until you feel cleansed and purified.
