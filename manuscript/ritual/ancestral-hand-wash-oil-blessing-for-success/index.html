A hands-on blessing performed for another person. The subject's hands are washed with scented water while visualizing their ancestors lending support, then anointed with prayed-over oil and massaged to soften blockages and charge the hands with power. Cannot be done for yourself.

Quantities

Bowl of water
Scented addition for the water: Florida water, rose water, or fresh flower petals
Towel
Bottle of oil: condition oil, or essential oils in a carrier (almond or jojoba oil)

Oil suggestions by intention:
Mint: success in money matters
Rosemary: memory, studying, exams
Lavender: purification of spirit
Orange: happiness
Olive oil: always appropriate for ancestor work

Directions

  1. This spell is performed for another person; it cannot be done for yourself.
  2. Prepare a bowl of water with something scented: Florida water, rose water, or fresh flower petals.
  3. Have the subject hold their hands over the bowl while you rinse them with the water.
  4. As you do so, visualize the hands of their ancestors reaching out through the water to meet them, lending a helping hand, shaking hands in congratulation, or holding on in encouragement and support.
  5. Dry the subject's hands.
  6. Hold the oil in your own hands, warming it to body temperature.
  7. While holding it, pray to the subject's ancestors.
  8. Ask them to remove any blockages to their child's success, to guide and support them on their path, and to give them strength to face whatever obstacles they may encounter.
  9. Choose the oil according to the nature of the endeavor; a condition oil from a spiritual supplier works well, or blend your own essential oils into a carrier such as almond or jojoba.
  10. Olive oil is always appropriate for magick that calls on the ancestors.
  11. Apply the oil to the subject's hands and arms using gentle, circular strokes (effleurage) to warm the body to receive the working.
  12. With the hands receptive, massage the palms using any technique you know.
  13. Throughout, visualize the lines of the palm becoming softer and sweeter under the oil.
  14. Work each finger: gently squeeze the space between each joint, then squeeze and slide from base to tip as though removing a ring.
  15. As you do this, hold the image of the hands becoming strong, effective, and radiating power out into the world.
