A tea potion to encourage clear thinking and help you read people and situations correctly. Three recipe variations are provided.

Quantities

Black tea and fresh lemon juice or lemon peel (Version 1)
Spearmint tea and a strip of tangerine peel (Version 2)
Peppermint tea and a clear quartz crystal (Version 3)
Optional: clear glass cup or mug

Directions

  1. Choose one of the three versions below and brew the tea.
  2. Add that version's extra ingredient. Do not ingest the crystal if using Version 3.
  3. As you drink, visualize your mind becoming clear and sharp, confusion dissolving like mist in sunlight.
  4. Complete the incantation, filling in "clarity" as your intention: "This cup of tea is now enchanted, with seeds of clarity, firmly planted. Aromatic magickal potion, as I drink, set my spell in motion."
  5. After drinking Version 3, wash the quartz and reserve it for future use.

This tea potion clears mental fog and sharpens perception, helping you think clearly and read people and situations correctly. Three variations are provided. Choose the one that appeals to your taste or try all three to see which works best for you.

Version 1: Black Tea with Lemon

Brew black tea. Add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a strip of lemon peel. Lemon's cutting, clarifying energy dispels confusion and reveals truth. Do not add milk, as it will curdle with the citrus.

Version 2: Spearmint with Tangerine

Brew spearmint tea. Add a strip of tangerine peel (the zest). Spearmint refreshes the mind while tangerine's bright solar energy illuminates understanding.

Version 3: Peppermint with Clear Quartz

Brew peppermint tea. Place a clear quartz crystal in the cup with the tea. The crystal amplifies the clarifying energies of the peppermint.

For this version, using a clear glass cup or mug enhances the spell by allowing you to see through the liquid, symbolizing clarity and transparency.
