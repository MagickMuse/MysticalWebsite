💧Water

The world's simplest, most secret, and maybe most effective health and healing spell, empowering a glass of water between your hands for any health purpose anywhere.

Quantities

  • A glass of water
  • Alternatively, a plastic bottle of mineral water. If necessary, empower any available drink

Directions

  1. Hold a glass of water between your hands.
  2. Because water is pure, you can use it as a magickal space and imprint it with whatever you need.
  3. For a specific health or healing purpose, say what you most need.
  4. For example, say: "My headache is lifting and I am full of energy." Empower the water for health, vitality, and strength for the days or hours ahead.
  5. Keep your hands cupped around the glass for about two minutes and then slowly sip the water, thinking the empowerment.
  6. If you always feel tired in the morning, fill a glass of water before sleep, endowing it with energy and enthusiasm for the day ahead.
  7. Drink the water as soon as you wake, saying: "I fill myself with the enthusiasm and joy for the day ahead." Perform for any health or healing purpose, any place.
