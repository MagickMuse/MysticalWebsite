🌑Dark Sun Midnight Worry Release

A midnight dark sun ritual writing fears repeatedly, singeing corner, freezing paper in container, disposing at dawn.

Quantities

  • 1 dark-blue candle
  • 1 small bowl of salt
  • Paper and pen
  • 1 bowl of soil
  • 1 small lidded plastic container
  • 1 blue pen

Directions

  1. Light the candle and, looking into the flame, say, "The dark sun across the world does shine, but let all be peaceful in this place of mine." Sprinkle a few grains of salt into the flame, saying, "The sun will rise again at dawn, 'til then I consign all fear 'til morn." On the paper, write over and over again the fears and the worries that are troubling you.
  2. Tear off just a corner and singe it in the candle flame.
  3. (Have a bowl of soil ready in case it catches on fire.) Extinguish the candle, and put the folded paper and the singed corner, burned or not, into a small plastic container in the freezer.
  4. Dispose of the plastic container and its contents the next morning.
  5. Perform midnight, called the time of the dark sun, indoors or in the garden.
