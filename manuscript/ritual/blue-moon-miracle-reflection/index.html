A preparatory meditation for blue moon magick that reconnects you to wonder, luck, and the feeling that anything is possible. By revisiting a personal memory of rare, unexpected good fortune, you raise your vibrational energy into alignment with possibility, then carry that feeling into your lunar spellwork.

Quantities

  • Optional: a journal or grimoire for writing down memories of wonder and luck

Directions

  1. Begin by breathing deeply.
  2. Cleanse your space however feels right, through smoke, sound, salt, or a moment of stillness.
  3. Settle into your body.
  4. Ask yourself these questions slowly and honestly: What themes are arising in my life that could use the magickal support of the blue moon?
  5. What in my life could use a second chance?
  6. What are some elements of life that evoke wonder in me?
  7. Close your eyes.
  8. Let your mind drift to what fills you with amazement: the sunset bleeding color across the sky, the full moon hanging luminous and huge, the simple fact that this earth exists and life is here.
  9. Breathe into that awe.
  10. Now scan your life, past and present, for moments of chance, rarity, and unexpected good fortune.
  11. The time you met someone who changed everything.
  12. The unexpected windfall.
  13. The door that opened when none should have.
  14. Let these memories surface without effort.
  15. If you feel inspired, write them down in your grimoire as a record of proof that good things do find you.
  16. From all of those memories, choose the one that moves you most, the moment that still makes your heart lift when you think of it.
  17. Place yourself back there.
  18. Reconstruct every detail you can recall: what you saw, heard, smelled, felt.
  19. For five full breaths, inhale that experience into your body and let it bloom in your chest.
  20. Now draw that energy forward into the present moment.
  21. Feel it as a live current, a tingle of possibility running through you, bright and ready.
  22. This is the energy you carry into your blue moon workings.
  23. The magick you cast from here is cast from a place of genuine, embodied hope.
