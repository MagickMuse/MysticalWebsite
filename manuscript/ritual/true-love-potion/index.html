🍵True Love Potion

◇Form
Potion
◇Method
Herbal MagickVisualizationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Jasmine tea (per the Tea Potion Spell) One needle of fresh or dried rosemary
◇Directions
1. Brew jasmine tea according to the Tea Potion Spell. 2. Add one needle of fresh or dried rosemary to the cup; the single needle represents the singular nature of true love. 3. As you drink, visualize yourself meeting your true love, focusing not on a specific face but on the feeling of recognition and the deep knowing that this is the one.
◇Description
To help you recognize and be receptive to your true love when they enter your life.
