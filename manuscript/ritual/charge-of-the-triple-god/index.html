A devotional invocation to the Triple God in his aspects throughout the Wheel of the Year: the Bright Child at Winter Solstice, the Greenman of Spring, the Holly King at Summer Solstice, the Stag-Antlered Hunter of the Wild Hunt, and the Goat-Horned Lord of Shadows. Honors his many names including Herne, Cernunnos, Pan, Dionysus, Shiva, and Osiris.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Speak this invocation to honor the Triple God at Sabbats or devotional workings.
  2. The text is written in the God's own voice:
  3. Listen to the words of the Triple God, Stag-crowned King, Greenman, Earth God, Horned One, Lord of the Animals and the Wildwood, known among the people as Herne, Cernunnos, Pan, Dionysus, Shiva, Osiris, and by many other names:
  4. I am the Cycle of the Wheel of the Year that matches the cycle of life on Earth.
  5. I am the Bright Child, the Oak Leaf Crowned King of Winter Solstice; I am the Greenman of Spring, the Holly Leaf Crowned King at the marriage feast of Summer Solstice; I am the Stag-Antlered Hunter, leading the Rade that sweeps through the storm-tossed skies; I am the Goat-Horned Lord of Shadows, offering rest and renewal to spirits in passage through my Lady as Crone; I am the Youth who dances with the Maiden, the Father who embraces the Mother, and I am the Son of the Mother.
  6. Honor me through the seasons of the year, as I show the way, for I am the Spirit of Nature, free and untamed, and by my hand are ye led through the Great Mystery unto thy rebirth.
