A devotional petition to Kwan Yin, who watches over pregnancy from conception through delivery, offered here to request her protection and comfort during an ongoing pregnancy.

Quantities

  • A votive image of Kwan Yin, ideally shown holding an infant
  • Fresh oranges
  • Pomegranates
  • Baby shoes or baby shoe charms

Directions

  1. Display a votive image of Kwan Yin, favoring one that shows her holding an infant for greatest effect in this working.
  2. Offer oranges, pomegranates, and baby shoes or baby shoe charms before the image.
  3. Speak openly of any fears or concerns about the pregnancy and ask for her assistance and protection through delivery.
