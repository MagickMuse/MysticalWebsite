Mabon

A nine-night Mabon candle working that rekindles passionate community engagement. Anoints a red candle with salt and herbs to awaken the activist spark within and call true companions to collective action.

Quantities

  • Red candle
  • Olive oil (two tablespoons)
  • Salt (two pinches)
  • Basil
  • Cinnamon
  • Spearmint

Directions

  1. First, mix a pinch of salt in a tablespoon of olive oil.
  2. Rub the solution on the candle from top to bottom, with the wick pointing away from you.
  3. This symbolically cleans the candle.
  4. Then, in another tablespoon of olive oil, mix a pinch each of basil, cinnamon, and spearmint.
  5. Rub this oil/herb mixture on the candle from the middle outward and then from the middle toward yourself.
  6. Stand at your front door if it leads to the outside.
  7. If it is an interior apartment, either step outside on a balcony or go to a door that opens to the outside world.
  8. Light the candle and say:
  9. Great God/ess, you have awakened me.
    I see the changes needed,
    the work that must be done to make them.
    Gently, gently awaken the true companions in this task,
    that we may share and live
    and make our good together.
    So mote it be!
  10. Blow the candle out, and then repeat this every night for nine nights.
  11. On the last night, bring the candle inside and allow the flame to burn for nine minutes.
  12. Proceed to burn the candle for nine minutes a day until it has melted completely.

A star symbolizes guidance, guardianship, high ideals, insight, and aspiration.
