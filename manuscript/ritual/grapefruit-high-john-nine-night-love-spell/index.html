🍊Grapefruit High John Nine-Night Love Spell

A grapefruit half seasoned with salt, sugar, pepper, and Epsom salts holds a rolled name-paper tied with red thread. Nine candles are arranged around it and burned nightly for nine nights while the caster circumambulates sunwise rolling a High John root. Traditionally performed by men; women seeking a woman substitute angelica root.

Quantities

1 grapefruit (halved)
Salt, sugar, pepper, and Epsom salts
A strip of brown paper and pen
Red thread
9 candles (red, pink, yellow, and/or gold)
High John the Conqueror root (or angelica root for women seeking women)

Directions

  1. Because of the use of High John the Conqueror root, this spell is traditionally performed by men. A woman wishing to seduce another woman should substitute an angelica root.
  2. Cut a grapefruit in half, horizontally.
  3. Place one half in a pan.
  4. Sprinkle it with salt, sugar, pepper, and Epsom salts.
  5. Write your beloved's name on a strip of brown paper.
  6. Roll up the paper and tie it with red thread.
  7. Insert it into the center of the grapefruit.
  8. Arrange nine candles (red, pink, yellow, and/or gold) around the grapefruit half.
  9. Light them.
  10. While the candles burn, circumambulate around them sunwise while rolling a High John root in your hand, praying and petitioning.
  11. Pinch each candle out.
  12. Repeat for a total of nine nights (nine minutes a night for the first eight nights). On the last night, let the candles burn out completely.
