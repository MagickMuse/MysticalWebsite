🌿Basic Botanic Fumigation Spell

Eclectic

Indigenous (White Sage)
◇Quantities
Choose any combination of: aloes wood, benzoin resin, bloodroot, cajeput, cinnamon, cloves, dragon's blood resin, eucalyptus, frankincense, garlic, harmel (Syrian rue), juniper, mastic, mugwort, myrrh, onions, rosemary, sage (especially white sage), Saint John's Wort, sandalwood, thyme, wormwood, yarrow - Charcoal disc (for resins and wood chips)
◇Directions
1. Burn any of the listed botanicals alone or in combination. 2. Direct the smoke toward what needs to be cleansed. 3. For resins and wood chips that cannot be bound into a wand, burn on a charcoal disc placed on a fire-safe dish, iron pan, or censer.
◇Description
Burning specific botanicals provides magickal and spiritual antiseptic effects. These plants can be burned alone or in any combination and always radiate a cleansing, purifying aura regardless of method. Many also radiate a protective aura. Potent cleansing resins and wood chips are usually burned on charcoal placed on a dish, iron pan, or incense censer so the smoke can be directed as needed. ⚠️ White sage is a closed practice sacred to Indigenous peoples of North America. Use garden sage or another alternative instead.
