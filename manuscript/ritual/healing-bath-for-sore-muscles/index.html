❤️‍🩹Healing Bath for Sore Muscles

Credit to:
Fusspilz4
Type
Ritual

Materials Needed2

Plants Needed6

Symbols1
Clockwise

Quantities

Dried Chamomile 3 tbs.
Cinnamon 3 sticks (or no more than 1/2 tbs. of powder)
Dried Rosemary 3 tbs.
Dried Bay leaves 3 tbs.
Dried Olive leaves 3 tbs.

Directions

Boil 1-1.5 liters of water in a pot.

Crush cinnamon sticks, bay leaves and olive leaves while waiting for the water to boil. You can charge the herbs now or later in the process.

Remove boiling water from heat, immediately add herbs to water and gently stir clockwise. This is a good time to add your intention to the pot and speak any words you wish to speak.

Cover pot with lid or towel and let the infusion steep for 15-20 minutes.

Once the infusion is finished, strain out the herbs using a fine mesh strainer, coffee filter or a towel.

Add infusion to bathwater and stir clockwise. This is another great time for adding intention and/or chanting.

Enjoy your bath.

Cinnamon,

Cinnamon is not only associated with healing, but also with warmth, which has a stimulating effect that helps with circulation and muscle relief.

Warning:

Cinnamon in high concentration can irritate the skin. Make sure to dilute it with enough water.

Chamomile,

Chamomile is associated with healing, anti-inflammatory and soothing effects.

Rosemary,

Rosemary is associated with healing and said to relieve muscle tension.

Bay Leaves,

Bay leaves are also associated with healing and are traditionally used for boosting circulation.

Olive Leaves,

Olive leaves, like many ingredients in this recipe, are associated with healing and said to have anti-inflammatory properties.

Cooling variation,

If you'd rather have a cooling than warming bath, you can replace the cinnamon with mint.
