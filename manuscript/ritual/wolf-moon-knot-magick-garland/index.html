🪢Wolf Moon Knot Magick Garland

A knot magick garland crafted under the Wolf Moon or Capricorn moon, harnessing winter themes of strength in numbers, community bonds, and abundance. Bay leaves are threaded and knotted onto green cord with spoken intention, then consecrated through incense smoke and charged under moonlight. The finished garland can be displayed on an altar, above a doorway, or over the bed as a lasting blessing, or eventually burned as an offering.

Quantities

Prosperity incense
A needle
Bay leaves
A marker
Cord
Juniper, hawthorn, cranberries, or other decorations

Directions

  1. Light the prosperity incense. Use a needle to poke a hole through each bay leaf so the cord can pass through easily.
  2. If desired, write intentions, names, or wishes on the bay leaves with a marker. In group or family workings, invite each person to inscribe their own.
  3. Hold the cord in your hands. Settle into your intention, are you strengthening bonds with loved ones, a coven, or spirit allies? Building personal resilience? Drawing abundance closer?
  4. Tie a knot at one end of the cord to anchor your working. Speak aloud:
    "With each knot, a bond I form.
    I tie abundance, connection, and strength with this cord."
  5. Thread the unknotted end through the needle. String a bay leaf onto the cord and tie a knot after it. Continue knotting and chanting, weaving bay leaves between each knot. Add juniper, hawthorn, cranberries, or other decorations as desired.
  6. When the garland reaches your desired length, pass it slowly through the incense smoke to consecrate it.
  7. Lay or hang the garland under the full moon's light overnight to charge it with lunar blessing.
  8. Display it where it will hold and attract blessings, altar, doorway, hearth, bed, or solstice tree. When you are ready to release it, burn it as an offering.
