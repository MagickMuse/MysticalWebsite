In Asian magickal tradition the tiger is the sworn enemy of evil spirits, tied to warrior spirits and witchcraft, with magickal knowledge to match its courage and strength. An image of a tiger posted at the entrance, looking outward, guards the home.

Quantities

Paper image or statue of a tiger

Directions

  1. Do not keep tiger images inside the home, since their wild nature tends to stir up chaos there.
  2. Instead, attach a paper tiger image to the entrance door, or set a tiger statue beside it.
  3. Make sure the tiger faces away from the home, watching for danger.

⚠️ Chinese magickal tradition advises against keeping tiger images inside the home, because their fierce, uncontrollable nature can stir up havoc.
