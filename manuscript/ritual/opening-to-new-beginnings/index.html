Ritual Grimoire

⚡Opening to New Beginnings

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationVisualization
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
- No materials required (seated meditation) - Optional: audio recording or reader
◇Directions
1. In this meditation, you will focus your energies toward a successful new beginning. 2. Whether it is a life change, a new job, a new project, or something else, this meditation will focus your energies on that new beginning and help you visualize it through to fruition. 3. You will want to record this meditation or have someone read it to you. 4. Get into a comfortable position, close your eyes, and take several deep breaths, exhaling and inhaling for at least a count of five each time. 5. Relax and begin your recording of the meditation here: Ahead of you lies a new path, a new beginning. 6. The path is cloudy and foggy and you cannot see very far down it, but you feel compelled to walk forward into the fog. 7. With each step you take you feel you are moving closer to the fog, yet you never seem to be catching up with it. 8. Each step you take is in clean, crisp, fog‑free air. 9. The fog remains ahead of you. 10. As you walk forward, you notice new things appearing on the path next to you. 11. Images from your past show themselves to you, a childhood friend, a favorite teacher, your first girlfriend or boyfriend. 12. As you walk past each one, they turn their gaze to follow you as you continue down the path. 13. You are passing by your past as you move forward, but you do so with gladness. 14. These memories bring you joy and happiness. 15. You realize that with each new person, there was a first time you met. 16. (Allow a pause to recall images of the past.) You see yourself ride a bike for the first time, learn to swim, go to an amusement park, or go on a favorite family vacation. 17. You see yourself getting a driver’s license, graduating high school, maybe even graduating college. 18. All are happy events from your past that you enjoy remembering. 19. Each one of these events had a first time. 20. Each one of these events was at one time a new beginning. 21. Each one of these events brought you joy and happiness even though right before it happened, and maybe while it was happening, you were nervous and unsure about the situation or event. 22. You made it through and you did so amazingly well. 23. With each step you take, the images at your sides become visible and clear, but the fog in front of you still remains. 24. You continue stepping forward and the images at your sides are more recent. 25. You are in your present now. 26. You see people you have recently met, events that recently happened, and situations you recently found yourself in. (Allow a brief pause.) You are happy to be reminded of these people and events and remember that they too were once a part of a new beginning. 27. These parts of your present make you feel happy, content, and secure. 28. You no longer worry about these things as you are now comfortable with them. 29. You have no fear and enjoy having these aspects in your life. 30. (Allow a brief pause.) As you continue walking down the road, you notice a bright light ahead of you, as if the sun is suddenly beating down upon the path. 31. The fog begins to burn away, disappearing before your eyes. 32. Briefly you are able to catch a glimpse of your future, of the new beginning that you are starting out in life. 33. You can feel it pulling at you through the sun’s rays. 34. You feel comfortable in the sun’s light, warm and safe, as you are able to get glimpses of your life in the future. 35. Nothing is too clear though, as the sun quickly grows brighter and brighter until all you can see is the brightest of the sun’s rays. 36. Your future is ahead of you and it awaits in a bright, sunny, happy, and content place. 37. You only have to keep walking forward in order to grasp it. 38. Take some time to enjoy this feeling of anticipation. 39. (Allow a silent pause for reflection.) When ready, give silent thanks for this brief glimpse and any inspiration you have gathered from it. 40. Take a couple deep breaths, remind yourself of where you physically are right now, then gently open your eyes.
◇Description
A guided visualization to align energy with a successful new beginning. It reframes past, present, and future experiences to build confidence and joyful anticipation while moving forward on a clear path.
