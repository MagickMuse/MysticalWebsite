✨Magickal Sign Animal Spell (Birch Bark)

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
PetitionInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 3 pieces of birch bark - Pen or writing instrument - 1 small stone
◇Directions
1. Write three messages on pieces of birch bark, one addressed to each of the nature tsars: the Forest Tsar and Tsarina, the Earth Tsar and Tsarina, and the Water Tsar and Tsarina. 2. Each message should include a description of the lost animal with enough detail to distinguish it from others, a demand for its immediate return, a warning that you will petition against them if they fail to comply, and a specific reward you pledge in exchange for their help. 3. Fasten one message to a tree in a forest. 4. Bury the second in the earth. 5. Attach a small stone to the third and cast it into a body of running water. 6. The animal is expected to find its way home shortly. 7. If the spell succeeds, honor your pledge without fail.
◇Description
A Russian spell that petitions the ruling spirits of forest, earth, and water to locate and return a lost animal. Written messages on birch bark are delivered to each domain as formal notices, complete with a pledged reward.
