🔥Bring Fire to Your Heart

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationElemental
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Materials: Fire or candle
◇Directions
1. Eagle flies close to the sun, fire's birthplace. 2. Fire represents creativity and kinetic energy, bringing passion and warmth. 3. Call on Fire and bring it into your heart to spark renewal and regeneration. 4. This ritual is beautiful performed before a fire, but a candle works in a pinch. 5. Begin by closing your eyes. 6. Let the light dance on your lids, warming your sight. 7. Breathe in the warm air, feeling it in your mouth and lungs. 8. Hold up your hands, letting heat seep into your palms. 9. Then carefully imagine gathering the light in your hands and pulling it toward you. 10. First, gather the light and pull it toward your eyes that your vision may be clear. 11. Next, gather the light and pull it toward your navel, the center of creativity. 12. Finally, pull the light to your chest, letting it warm your heart, softening, sparking, and clarifying. 13. Sit with the fire until you feel complete.
◇Description
A fire meditation practice inspired by Sea Eagle's flight toward the sun. By gathering firelight into your eyes, navel, and heart, you invite renewal, regeneration, passion, and clarity through fire's transformative energy.
