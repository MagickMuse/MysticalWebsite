🌅Morning Garden

◇Form
Altar WorkingCandle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickInvocationSolarVisualization
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
One candle, yellow or rose-pink, for each thing you want to help grow or that is beginning Bells, gongs, or conch shells, as an optional charm Optional: an image of a dawn deity
◇Directions
1. Set up the garden facing East, where it greets the rising Sun each day. 2. Add a yellow or rose-pink candle for each plan, project, child, garden, or business you want to help grow. 3. Add candles for things that are beginning, such as healing processes, artistic endeavors, enlightenment, new friendships, or new love, to encourage their growth and success. 4. Add a bell, gong, or conch shell to sound the beginning of each activation, and, if desired, an image of a dawn deity. 5. Activate the garden in the morning, when its energy aligns with the rising sun.
◇Description
A candle garden facing East to greet the rising Sun, dedicated to growth, new beginnings, and things you want to help flourish.

A Morning Garden harnesses the energy of dawn and new beginnings.

Appropriate dawn deities include Aurora (Roman goddess of dawn), Aya (Mesopotamian goddess of dawn), Bast (Egyptian goddess associated with the rising sun), Eos (Greek goddess of dawn), Isis (Egyptian goddess of magic and new beginnings), Janus (Roman god of beginnings), Ushas (Hindu goddess of dawn), and the Zorya (Slavic dawn goddesses).
