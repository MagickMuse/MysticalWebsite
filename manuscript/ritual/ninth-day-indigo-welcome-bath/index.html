🛡️Ninth Day Indigo Welcome Bath

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group

◇Form

◇Setting

◇Method

◇Quantities
Bathwater Rum (a small splash) Silver (a small piece from each family member) Indigo (enough to tint the water blue)
◇Directions
1. Pour a small amount of rum into the baby's bathwater. 2. Have each family member drop a small piece of silver into the water. 3. Stir in real indigo until the water turns blue. 4. Draw a blue cross on the baby's forehead, in the hollow between the eyebrows, or on the back of the neck. 5. Speak blessings and prayers for the baby's safety and happiness while lifting the child from the bath.
◇Description
A family bath given on the ninth day after birth. Rum, silver, and indigo go into the water, and the baby is marked with a protective blue cross.

Lore

Some babies arrive with a faint blue cross over the bridge of the nose, between the eyebrows, which fades as the skin matures and thickens. Many traditions read this mark as a highly fortunate sign of spiritual protection. This bath deliberately bestows the same blessing on a child not born with it.
