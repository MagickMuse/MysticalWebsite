🍀Luck

A daring Russian hunter's folk ritual performed in church on Easter Sunday. When the words 'Christ is risen' are pronounced during the Easter service, the hunter shouts aloud their hunting desire. Allegedly, the stated desire will be granted for the following year.

Quantities

None

Directions

  1. In church, on Easter Sunday, when the words 'Christ is risen' are pronounced, immediately shout out your desire aloud (such as 'My bullet hits the mark!') or name whatever your specific hunting wish is.
  2. If you dare, it will allegedly be yours for a year.

About This Spell

A Russian hunter's ritual requiring daring outside the wilderness. This spell uses the charged spiritual moment of the Easter announcement to lock in a hunter's wish for the coming year.

Note: A similar mechanism is used in the Easter Sunday Gambler's Wish Spell, which targets gambling success rather than hunting.
