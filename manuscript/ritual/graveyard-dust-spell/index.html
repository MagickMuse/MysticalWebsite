⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A Hoodoo-rooted hexing spell using Graveyard Dust, which may be cemetery dirt, mullein powder, patchouli powder, or a blend of all three. The dust is combined with hexing agents such as asafoetida, Goofer Dust, and various cursing oils, then mixed with a personal belonging or written name of the target. The mixture is bagged and buried near the target's home, sprinkled on their doorstep, or smeared on their doorknobs. The hex causes the target to grow sluggish, sleepy, and eventually waste away.

Quantities

Graveyard Dust (cemetery dirt, mullein powder, patchouli powder, or a combination)
Hexing agents: asafoetida, Goofer Dust, or similar powders and oils
1 personal belonging of the target (or parchment paper with their name)
1 small bag

Directions

  1. Graveyard Dust may be cemetery dirt, mullein powder, patchouli powder, or any combination of the three.
  2. Combine the Graveyard Dust with hexing agents such as asafoetida, Goofer Dust, and any available hexing powders or oils.
  3. Mix in something belonging to the target.
  4. If no personal belonging can be obtained, write the target's name on a small piece of parchment paper.
  5. Place the mixture and the taglock in a small bag.
  6. Bury or hide the bag near the target's home.
  7. If the mixture is composed solely of powders, also sprinkle it on the target's doorstep.
  8. If the mixture includes oils, smear it on the target's doorknobs.
  9. Both actions are performed in addition to hiding the bag.
  10. The working causes the target to become sluggish and sleepy, eventually wasting away.

Background

Graveyard Dust is one of three things: dirt taken from a cemetery, mullein powder, or patchouli powder. It can also be a combination of all three. It is commonly used alongside asafoetida, Jezebel Root, Lost and Away powder, Black Cat Oil, Four Thieves Vinegar Oil, Goofer Dust, Mummy Oil, Damnation Water Oil, and other hexing powders and oils.
