◇Directions
1. Clearly and specifically identify your goal. Consider paths to your goal and come up with at least one action plan, give yourself a realistic time period for reaching it. 2. Clear your mind, raise power, create sacred space. Light the incense. Concentrate on your goal, visualize it as already achieved. Take a mental snapshot and keep your focus on that as you continue. Hold the pen in the incense smoke and concentrate on charging the ink with magick. Do this until the incense burns itself out. Use the pen to write your goal at the top of the paper. Count the days that you have allotted for reaching your goal and write that number on the paper. Hang this paper somewhere you will see it every day. Everyday, scratch out the number of days and write the next lower number beneath it, as a countdown. Say this affirmation (or your own) every day when you write the new number. "I am strong. I am determined. Nothing with distract me, divert me, or prevent me from reaching my goal."

Why This Spell Works

Setting a goal, having a hope, or making a wish is like placing a marker on the roadmap of your life. The question becomes: how do you get from here to there? This spell provides a daily ritual structure that keeps your goal visible and your intention charged throughout the entire journey.

Casting a spell alone is rarely enough to reach most goals. You must also make practical efforts: working out, saving money, attending classes, learning new skills, or whatever your specific goal requires. The spell amplifies and focuses your efforts rather than replacing them.

The Power of the Countdown

The countdown creates psychological momentum. Each morning when you cross off a number and write the next one, you are actively engaging with your goal. The shrinking numbers create a sense of urgency and progress. The daily affirmation reinforces your determination and reminds your subconscious mind of what you are working toward.

If your goal has a long timeframe, you may need to tape several sheets of paper together. This is perfectly fine. What matters is that you see your countdown every single day.

Charging Your Writing Implement

The overnight moonlight charge adds lunar energy to your pen or pencil. After the incense charging, leave the implement on a windowsill or somewhere it can absorb moonlight. The next morning, it carries both the fire energy of the incense and the intuitive power of the moon.

The Confidentiality Option

If your goal is private and you worry about others seeing it, use a pencil. Write your goal at the top of the paper, then erase it completely. The paper retains the energetic imprint of what you wrote even after the words are gone. The universe does not need to read your goal to understand your intention.

Optional Crystal Enhancement

If you own a sapphire, garnet, or turquoise ring, wear it on your projective hand (your dominant hand) while casting the spell or during any work toward your goal afterward. These stones support ambition, determination, and achievement.

Daily Practice

Every morning, use your charged writing implement to:

  1. Cross off the current number
  2. Write the next lower number beneath it
  3. Speak your affirmation aloud

The standard affirmation is: "I am strong. I am determined. Nothing will distract me, divert me, or prevent me from reaching my goal."

Feel free to create your own affirmation if different words resonate more strongly with you. What matters is that you speak it with conviction every single day.

What to Expect

Take at least one small step toward your goal every day. The combination of daily magickal practice and consistent practical action creates powerful momentum. Most people find they achieve their goals before the countdown reaches zero.
