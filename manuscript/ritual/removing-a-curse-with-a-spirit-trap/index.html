A 10 p.m. bottle trap ritual with blackberry leaves or thorn twig in vinegar, salt, and cayenne pepper to trap curses.

Quantities

  • 9 blackberry leaves or 1 thorn twig
  • 1 wide-necked, sealable bottle or jar
  • 3 pinches sea salt
  • 6 pinches red (cayenne) pepper
  • Small jug of vinegar
  • Red thread
  • Packing tape

Directions

  1. Add the leaves or twig to the jar.
  2. Pour in enough of the vinegar mix to cover the twig or leaves, saying, "Evil curse enter here, where you are trapped with barbs so fierce I need no longer fear." Put on the lid and tie the jar all around with the red thread, making knots and saying, "Now the evil threat is bound, the curse is trapped and sealed around." Seal the jar all around with packing tape, repeating the second set of spell words.
  3. The next morning, take the jar in a sealed bag to a garbage dump, or hide it in a dark place until the refuse is collected.
  4. Perform any night, at 10 p.m.
