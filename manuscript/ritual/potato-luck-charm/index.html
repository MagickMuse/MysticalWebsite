Lughnasadh

A Lughnasadh potato‑stamp charm to attract good luck and increase good fortune by carving a simple symbol, stamping it, and returning the potato to the earth.

Quantities

  • 1 potato (washed)
  • Small paring knife or carving tool and protective gloves
  • Non‑toxic, biodegradable paint/ink or natural “inks” (grape juice, blackberry juice, mud)
  • Small saucer or ink pad
  • Small piece of paper (for the stamped charm)

Directions

  1. 1) Halve the potato horizontally. Choose a simple symbol for your aim (examples):
  2. Prosperity: money sign, pentacle
  3. Love: heart
  4. General luck/fortune: star, simple four‑armed cross or solar mark
  5. 2) On the cut face of one half, scratch the outline of your chosen symbol, then carefully trim away excess around it so the symbol stands proud as a stamp.
  6. Wear protective gloves and carve slowly.
  7. 3) Place a little paint/ink on a saucer or use an ink pad.
  8. Favor non‑toxic, biodegradable color; natural juices work well.
  9. 4) Dip/press the potato stamp to coat the raised symbol.
  10. Focus on the good luck and fortune you want to draw.
  11. Press the stamp onto the paper, placing the symbol at the center.
  12. 5) Cut off the stamped symbol end and discard.
  13. Bury the remaining potato in the soil.
  14. If using biodegradable color, you may rinse and bury the symbol piece as well (optional).
  15. Hang the stamped paper in your home or carry it with you for as long as you wish.
