🥩Beef Tongue📄Paper📌Pins & Needles

A beef tongue variant designed to produce a hung jury, a split jury unable to reach a verdict. The tongue is suspended tip-down over a hot fire during the proceedings and requires an assistant to maintain the working while the caster is present in the courtroom. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Quantities

1 raw beef tongue
1 name paper per opponent
Pins (optional, for the tip hole)
Fire source
Trusted assistant

Directions

  1. Write each opponent's name on individual slips of paper.
  2. Cut a single hole into the tip of the tongue; do not cut multiple slits, and stuff all name papers into that hole, pinning it loosely shut if needed.
  3. During the actual proceedings, have a trusted assistant suspend the tongue tip-down over a hot fire, close to but not touching the flames.
  4. The assistant maintains this position for as long as necessary, until a verdict is reached or fails to be reached.
🔒
Closed Practice: Beef tongue spellwork originates in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo — closed practices with African American cultural and spiritual lineage. Approach with respect.

Purpose

This version is not aimed at victory or defeat — it targets the process itself. A hung jury creates a mistrial, which can be strategically valuable when a loss seems likely and a retrial gives more time to prepare.

The Assistant

This spell cannot be self-cast if you are present in the courtroom, which you almost certainly will be. An assistant — someone trusted who is not required in court — maintains the tongue-over-fire working at home or at another location simultaneously with proceedings.

Coordination on timing matters: the assistant should begin when proceedings start and hold the position until the jury is dismissed or a verdict announced.

The Fire

The tip of the tongue is kept close to the flames but not touching — suspended, hot, uncomfortable, undecided. This reflects exactly the state desired for the jury: heated, unable to settle, unable to reach consensus.
