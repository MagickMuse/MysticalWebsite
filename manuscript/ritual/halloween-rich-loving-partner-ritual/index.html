A Halloween afternoon divination game with blindfold and three plates (clean water, dirty water, empty) to divine partner qualities.

Quantities

  • 1 blindfold
  • 3 plates or bowls (one empty, one with clean water, one with dirty water)

Directions

  1. Put on the blindfold, getting a friend to place the three plates in a row.
  2. Turn around three times, saying, "Fortune, fortune, tell me. Am I pretty or plain? Or am I downright ugly, and ugly to remain? Shall I marry a gentleman? Shall I marry a clown? Or shall I marry old pots and pans, shouting through the town?" Adapt the words if you seeking a female partner.
  3. Reach out with your left hand.
  4. If you touch the clean water, your future spouse will be rich, attractive, successful, loving, and unmarried.
  5. If you touch the dirty water, your future spouse will have a few complications, maybe children, a difficult ex, or debts.
  6. If you touch the empty plate, traditionally it means you will remain unmarried until next Halloween.
  7. The quicker you reach for the right plate, the easier love will be.
  8. Have your friend remove the empty plate and repeat the spell with two plates and finally you will achieve the desired result.
  9. Perform Halloween afternoon.
