⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
An ancient-style curse in which personal effects from the target, including nail parings, hair, eyebrows, and spittle, are molded into a wax likeness using beeswax from a deserted comb. The figure is scorched slowly over a lamp each night for seven nights while reciting an incantation targeting the target's vital organs. After the seventh night, the image is burned completely.

Quantities

Beeswax (from a deserted bees' comb)
Nail parings from the target
Hair from the target
Eyebrow clippings from the target
Spittle from the target
1 lamp

Directions

  1. Gather parings of nails, hair, eyebrows, spittle, and other personal effects of the target, enough to represent every part of the person.
  2. Mold them into the target's likeness using wax from a deserted bees' comb.
  3. Each night for seven nights, scorch the figure slowly by holding it over a lamp while saying:
  4. It is not wax that I am scorching, it is the liver, heart, and spleen of [name] that I scorch.
  5. After the seventh night, burn the waxen image entirely.
