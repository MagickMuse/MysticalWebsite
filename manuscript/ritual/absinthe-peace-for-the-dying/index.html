🕊️Absinthe Peace for the Dying

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Anointing
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
HealingHerbal MagickEnergy Work
◇Category
Energetic Rite
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- Absinthe in a roller bottle for anointing - Or wormwood/mugwort salve - Or cotton balls soaked in strong wormwood infusion - Optional: 1-2 drops lavender essential oil
◇Directions
1. One of the hardest, and most rewarding, things we as humans can do for one another is to sit at the bedside of someone who is dying. 2. Wormwood and mugwort are boundary plants. 3. They can reinforce boundaries, or leave them a smoldering ruin. 4. They can help you cross the hedge, or hide you from view. 5. For a loved one who is dying, you can get permission in the manner that suits the situation, and anoint their hands and feet with a small amount of absinthe (in a roller bottle is just fine). 6. The person may be lucid enough to welcome wormwood or mugwort salve. 7. It is a soothing sedative that encourages restful sleep and the happiest dreams of their loved ones. 8. It gently opens the door to the other side. 9. As they near death, they may start to see loved ones who have crossed over. 10. There is nothing to fear there. 11. Anoint yourself after caretaking missions as well. 12. You may have days where you decide to anoint your insides as well. 13. (Always in moderation, of course.) No judgment here. 14. Aromatherapy Option: Aromatherapy is being more understood and accepted in hospitals. 15. Make a strong infusion of wormwood in water and soak cotton balls in it with a drop or two of lavender. 16. Most nurses know about lavender oil being calming for patients. 17. The loved one can smell the cotton balls, as it helps calm, open that door to loved ones, and aids their crossing the veil.
◇Description
A compassionate end-of-life working using absinthe and wormwood to ease the transition for dying loved ones. The boundary-crossing properties of wormwood gently open the door to the other side.
