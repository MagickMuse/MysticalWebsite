A Latin American remedy for susto, soul loss caused by intense fright. An egg is passed over the body to draw out malignancies. If blood appears when broken, stronger shamanic intervention is needed. Otherwise, a spearmint and chamomile infusion is drunk and used as a head compress.

Quantities

One whole raw egg
Spearmint (strong infusion)
Chamomile (strong infusion)
Clean cloth

Directions

  1. Pass an egg gently over the patient's entire body, concentrating on drawing malignancies into the egg.
  2. Break the egg into a bowl.
  3. If blood is observed, this confirms a serious situation and you should seek stronger shamanic intervention.
  4. If the egg appears normal, prepare a strong infusion of spearmint and chamomile.
  5. Give the patient some of the infusion to sip.
  6. Dip a clean cloth into the infusion, squeeze out excess liquid, and use it as a compress on the patient's head.
