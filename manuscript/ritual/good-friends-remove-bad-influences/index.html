Ritual Grimoire

👥Good Friends Remove Bad Influences

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
ProtectionCandle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Two bright-red and two white candles, set alternately in a square around the photograph - A box of pins - A magnet - A photograph of your child having a good time
◇Directions
1. This reading works best when you already have a specific person in mind (a crush, new date, current partner, or potential love interest). 2. Ground and shuffle: Clear your mind, focus on the question "Are [Name] and I a good match?" Shuffle until it feels right. 3. Draw two cards and place them side by side: 4. Card 1 (You): This card represents your energy, intentions, needs, and what you bring to the connection. 5. Card 2 (Them): This card represents their energy, intentions, needs, and what they bring. 6. Observe: Do the cards complement each other or clash? 7. Are they both moving in the same direction (partnership, growth, openness) or in opposite directions (avoidance, conflict, different timelines)? 8. Draw a third card and place it between the first two: 9. Card 3 (The Relationship Dynamic): This card shows what happens when your energies combine. 10. It reveals the nature of the connection (passionate, stable, challenging, transformative, short-lived, karmic, etc.). 11. Interpret the full picture. Pay attention to: - Suit harmony (do Cups and Cups meet? Or Swords and Wands?) - Major Arcana presence (big soul lessons or fated connection) - Court cards (maturity levels, communication styles) - Imagery (are figures facing each other or turning away?) 12. Journal your impressions. 13. If you need more clarity, pull an additional card asking "What do I need to know right now about this connection?" 14. Close by thanking the cards and your intuition.
◇Description
A Wednesday after dark spell to help child or teenager make good friends and remove or ignore bad influences who are causing problems, lighting red and white candles and using magnet with pins to draw good influences and repel negative ones.
