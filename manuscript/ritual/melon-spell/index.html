A Chinese fertility spell in which sympathetic friends secretly steal a melon, dress it in baby clothes, and give it to an infertile couple. Most powerful when performed on the birthday of Lady Chang'O, the fifteenth day of the eighth month of the Chinese lunar calendar.

This Chinese fertility spell requires the assistance of sympathetic friends.

The Working

  1. Friends of an infertile couple steal a melon. It must be a genuine theft, not a purchase.
  2. The friends dress the melon in baby clothes.
  3. They give the dressed melon to the couple. This must be secret and not prearranged.
  4. In exchange, the couple is expected to provide a reasonably lavish dinner for the helpful thieves.

Optimal Timing

This spell is most powerful when performed on the fifteenth day of the eighth month of the Chinese lunar calendar, the birthday of Lady Chang'O, the resident woman on the moon.
