🕯️Thunder Gods Invocation

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
InvocationPrayer
◇Category
Invocation
◇Quantities
- No materials required (if raining, protect text in a plastic sleeve and speak outdoors only if safe)
◇Directions
1. Gods of storm and gods of thunder! Gods of lightning, powerful and bright! Thank you for your life-bringing rains that fertilize the crops and the land. Agni and Baal! Jupiter and Perun! I invoke you! I call your healing waters and your storms, which wash clean. Taranis and Thor! Thunar and Zeus! I invoke you! I ask that your gifts be measured and steady. That the rains come gently and the storms pass quickly, harming none, and aiding all. Bring your precious waters to the lands that have none and ease your presence in the lands that have much. Agni and Baal! Jupiter and Perun! I thank you! Taranai and Thor! Thunar and Zeus! I thank you! On this blessed Midsummer, I thank thee, and I praise thy names.
◇Description
A storm-time Midsummer invocation to thunder and lightning deities, asking for measured rains and cleansing storms.
