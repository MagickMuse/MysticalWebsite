A traditional banishment ritual that collects dirt from a target's footprint, mixes it with hot-foot ingredients, bottles it, and casts it into running water while invoking the five rivers of the Greek underworld. Used when protection and reversal spells prove insufficient and complete separation is needed.

Quantities

  • Dirt from target's footprint
  • Red pepper
  • Black pepper
  • Sulfur
  • Crushed hornets
  • Jar or bottle
  • Access to running water (river, stream, etc.)
  • Angelica (optional, for blessing)

Directions

  1. This working is performed when protection and reversal magick have proven insufficient and complete separation from the target is required.
  2. Step One: Collect the Footprint
  3. Observe where your target walks, ideally as they leave their workplace or home.
  4. Quietly collect some dirt from one of their footprints.
  5. This creates a powerful magickal link to the person.
  6. Step Two: Prepare the Mixture
  7. Mix the footprint dirt with red pepper, black pepper, sulfur, and crushed hornets.
  8. If you wish to help the target move to better circumstances rather than cause harm, add angelica to bless their transition.
  9. Step Three: Bottle and Cast Away
  10. Place the entire mixture into a jar or bottle and seal it.
  11. Take it to running water (a river or stream).
  12. Cast the bottle into the water while speaking this invocation:
  13. By Acheron, by Cocytus
    By Phlegethon, Styx, and Lethe
    I cast thee out!
    May not even the memory
    Of your name remain!
    By the Acheron,
    By the Cocytus
    By the Phlegethon, Styx, and Lethe
    Begone! Begone! Begone!
  14. The five rivers of the Greek underworld (Acheron, Cocytus, Phlegethon, Styx, and Lethe) are invoked to carry the target away completely, with Lethe specifically invoked to erase even the memory of their presence.
  15. Alternative Method (Shotgun Shell Variant):
  16. An alternative method involves placing the footprint dirt and hot-foot materials into a hollowed-out shotgun shell and shooting it off into the distance while praying to Jesus for the person to be removed from your life.
  17. This dramatic method has been recorded in traditional American folk magick practices.
  18. Expected Results:
  19. The target should find themselves drawn to leave the situation, often through new opportunities like better employment elsewhere.
  20. This working aims for complete separation rather than harm.
