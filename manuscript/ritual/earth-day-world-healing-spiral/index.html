An Earth Day or World Healing Day spell for solitary or group ritual, walking in ever-increasing spirals chanting rain, wind, sun, moon, stars, waters three times each then scattering seeds herbs petals to heal and bless Earth Mother of all.

Quantities

  • Seeds, dried herbs, or petals

Directions

  1. Walk around an open area in ever-increasing spirals, tracing the same path inward.
  2. As you walk, chant: "I am the rain, and the rain falls and sanctifies me." If you're in a group, let individual spirals overlap as each person says the rain chant three times until all have said the spell words three times.
  3. Continue spiraling, and, if you're alone, when you're ready, chant: "I am the wind, and the wind blows and sanctifies me." In a group, all recite the second chant three times in turn, continuing to spiral.
  4. If you're alone, when you're ready, change the chant to: "I am the sun, and the sun warms and sanctifies me." In a group, say the new chant three times each.
  5. Keep walking and chanting, adding natural forces to the chant, such as the moon, the stars, and the waters.
  6. You or all end by scattering seeds, herbs, or petals, saying: "Heal and bless the Earth, Mother of all." Perform Earth Day or World Earth Healing Day.
