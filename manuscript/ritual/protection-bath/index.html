🛁Protection Bath

◇Form
Bath
◇Method
Water MagickSalt MagickCleansingProtectionPrayer
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
Required: - Salt: 1/2 cup - Vinegar: 1/2 cup - Ammonia: 1 teaspoon diluted in at least 4 gallons of water Safety Warning: Ammonia is toxic and can be harmful if inhaled. Use only the specified amount in proper dilution. More is not better - excess ammonia will remove positive and neutral influences as well as negative ones. Be careful with sensitive skin areas.
◇Directions
1. Prepare bathwater (at least 4 gallons). 2. Add 1/2 cup salt and 1/2 cup vinegar to the water. 3. Dilute 1 teaspoon ammonia in the bathwater, ensuring proper dilution ratio. 4. Enter the bath. 5. Scrub downward from head to feet to push negative energy away from you. 6. As you bathe, visualize all negative influences, crossed conditions, and harmful energies being washed away and neutralized. 7. See yourself surrounded by an impenetrable protective barrier. 8. Recite prayers, psalms, or protection incantations appropriate to your practice. 9. The twenty-third Psalm is traditional for protection. 10. Soak as needed. 11. When complete, either drain the water (if bathing indoors) or collect it in a basin to dump toward the rising sun in the east, casting out all negativity drawn from you during the bath.
◇Description
A potent spiritual bath for protection using salt, ammonia, and vinegar. This cleansing removes negative influences and creates a protective barrier. The ammonia is a powerful cleanser that removes not only negative influences but positive and neutral ones as well if overused, making proper dilution essential. Take just before dawn for maximum effectiveness or before confronting hostile situations.
