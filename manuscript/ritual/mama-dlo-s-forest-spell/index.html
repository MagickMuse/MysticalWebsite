◇Description
A survival protocol for encountering the Trinidadian spirit Mama Dlo (corruption of 'Maman de l'Eau'), a half-woman, half-anaconda nature spirit who punishes indiscriminate hunters, polluters, and those who damage trees and botanicals. If she is angered by you, the only escape is to remove your left shoe while never taking your eyes off her, then hop out of the woods backwards. This display of humiliation and absurdity amuses her enough to grant your safety, but only works once.

About Mama Dlo

Mama Dlo's name is a corruption of "Maman de L'eau" (Mother of Water). She is a Trinidadian spirit — half-woman, half-anaconda — who punishes those who hunt indiscriminately, pollute land and water, and damage trees and other botanicals.

Questions surround her origins: Does she derive from Africa, like the water-snake spirit Maitresse Ezili? Or is she an Amazonian guardian spirit? Her relationship to the mermaid Mama Jo — who shares a similar name — is also debated.

Lesson

Mama Dlo's existence is as much a reminder as a ritual: respect the forest, hunt only what you need, do not pollute, and do not damage trees or plants.
