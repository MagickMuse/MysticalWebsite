💰Coins

A simple protective craft suitable for adults and children alike. Paint Athena's symbols onto a nickel-content coin, consecrate it through the elements, and carry it as a talisman for courage and clear thinking in uncertain situations.

Quantities

  • 1 coin with high nickel content (US quarter recommended for size)
  • Paint and brush
  • Optional: representations of four elements for blessing

Directions

  1. Select a coin with high nickel content.
  2. In the United States, quarters work well due to their size and grip.
  3. Nickel carries associations with problem-solving, making it ideal for this working.
  4. Athena governs more than courage alone.
  5. As goddess of crafts, strategy, and reason, she offers clarity when threat assessment becomes difficult.
  6. Modern life presents many situations where someone's behavior feels wrong but hasn't crossed obvious lines.
  7. Athena's blessing helps distinguish genuine danger from discomfort and empowers action when action is needed.
  8. Decorate your coin with symbols sacred to Athena.
  9. Those comfortable with detailed work might paint a helm on one side and an owl on the other.
  10. For simpler designs, a spear and serpent serve equally well.
  11. When the paint dries, consecrate the coin by passing it through representations of the four elements, or simply hold it and petition for courage to fill it.
  12. Keep the medallion with you.
  13. Practitioners with existing relationships to other courage-aligned deities should work with those beings instead.
