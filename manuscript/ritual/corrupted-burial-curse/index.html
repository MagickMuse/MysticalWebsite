⚰️Corrupted Burial Curse

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraInvocationBlood Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
1 piece of paper 1 photograph or picture of the target Ashes from the burned paper Urine Feces Spit Blood
◇Directions
1. Write down on a piece of paper everything you want to happen to your enemy. 2. Burn the paper and collect the ashes. 3. Take a photograph or picture of the enemy and drop it into a hole dug in the ground. 4. Pour a mixture of the ashes, urine, feces, spit, and blood over the image. 5. Fill the hole and bury it. 6. Stand over the burial site and speak the following: 7. You are my enemy! 8. Your putrid stench sours many lives. 9. I call upon the dead to torment you. 10. I corrupt your life as you have corrupted the lives of others. 11. I call upon the gods of death to deliver this justice! 12. As it is done, so be it.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A burial curse invoking the dead and the gods of death. A written list of desired misfortunes is burned, and the ashes are mixed with bodily fluids and poured over the target's image before burial.
