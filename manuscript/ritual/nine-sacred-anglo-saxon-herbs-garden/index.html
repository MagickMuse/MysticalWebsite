A garden of the nine sacred Anglo-Saxon herbs surrounds the gardener with spiritual and magickal protection against every kind of evil. Only eight are known, so the ninth place is left empty or filled with another plant honored in Anglo-Saxon magick.

Optional ninth plant: cowslip, flax, woodruff or wormwood

Directions

  1. Plant chamomile, chervil, crabapple, fennel, mugwort, nettle, plantain and watercress together in a garden.
  2. Decide what to do with the ninth place: leave it empty to honor the plant species lost to extinction, or fill it with another plant celebrated in Anglo-Saxon magick, such as cowslip, flax, woodruff or wormwood.
  3. Spend time in the garden to surround yourself with its protection.

The Missing Ninth Herb

Only eight of the nine sacred Anglo-Saxon herbs can be named. The ninth, called atterlothe, has never been identified.

Substitutes and Their Deities

  • Cowslip: sacred to Freya
  • Flax: sacred to Frigg and Hulda
  • Woodruff
  • Wormwood
