👛Empty Wallet Hex

◇Tradition

◇Solitary or Group

◇Category

◇Quantities
New wallet (purchased from a store, in its original box) No money inside
◇Directions
1. Give a new wallet as a gift, straight from the store, in a nice box, with nothing in it. 2. The target will likely be happy to receive it, unless they're sophisticated enough to realize they've accepted a wish for a perpetually empty purse.
◇Description
A subtle Romany hex intended to create a negative financial impact. A brand-new wallet is given as a gift, straight from the store, in a nice box, with nothing in it. The recipient unknowingly accepts a wish for a perpetually empty purse. Moral: always put at least a penny in a wallet before gifting it, and be wary of gifts received.
