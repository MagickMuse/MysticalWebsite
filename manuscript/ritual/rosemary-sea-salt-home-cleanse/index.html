🌕Moon Water

A lunar water blessing to cleanse the home of stagnant energy and invite protection and positive vibration. Best worked under the waning or new moon, this ritual uses salt, lemon, and rosemary in moon-charged water to refresh each room with intention. The same blend can be used under a waxing moon to amplify good energy rather than release what has grown heavy.

Quantities

  • Bowl
  • Moon Water (or plain water left out to charge under the moon beforehand)
  • Sea salt
  • Lemon peel
  • Fresh rosemary sprig

Directions

  1. Pour your moon water into the bowl.
  2. If you're preparing the water specifically for this ritual, leave it out under the moonlight first and return to it once it has charged.
  3. Sprinkle sea salt into the water counterclockwise, focusing on releasing and dispersing any unwanted energy from your space.
  4. Hold the lemon peel with the yellow outer side facing the water and squeeze gently to release its aromatic oils into the bowl.
  5. Let yourself feel the brightness and uplift of the lemon's energy, envisioning joy spreading through your home.
  6. Drop the peel into the water or set it aside.
  7. Dip the rosemary sprig into the mixture and stir it clockwise, calling to mind your home as you want it to feel, clean, protected, and full of warmth.
  8. Carry the bowl through your home room by room, using the rosemary sprig to flick drops of the water into each space as a blessing.
  9. Move with intention, spending a moment in each area before moving on.
  10. On clockwise and counterclockwise movement: Many practitioners work counterclockwise to release and cleanse, and clockwise to invoke and draw in. For a more layered practice, move counterclockwise through your home first, starting at the front door, with a cleansing bundle to clear and release.
  11. Then walk the circuit once more clockwise, carrying the rosemary water to seal in the blessings and restore harmony.
