A dawn ritual invoking the four Celtic elements using a triskele symbol to restore home harmony and banish disharmony.

Quantities

  • 1 herbal incense cone (for sky)
  • 1 red candle in deep heatproof holder (for fire)
  • 1 dish of potpourri or dried sage (for earth)
  • 1 dish of lavender or rose water (for sea)
  • 1 square slate, stone, or chalkboard
  • White chalk (to draw triskele)

Directions

  1. Indoors, light the candle in the center of the triskele, the incense cone on the upper left coil, the fragrance on the upper right, and the rose or lavender water on the lower coil.
  2. Light the incense from the candle.
  3. Say, "By the power of the earth," scattering potpourri around the outside of the slate, "By the power of sky," passing incense around it, and "By the power of the sea," scattering rose water around it, all clockwise.
  4. Lift the candle above the center of the triskele, saying, "By the power of fire so do I call, earth, sea, and sky, elements all, banish disharmony that I may know unity." Rub out the triskele.
  5. Perform at first light.
