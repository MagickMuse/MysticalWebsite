A seven-day spell using intentionally chosen playing cards laid out in a spread to shape the future rather than read it. Cards are selected for their meanings and placed with purpose, supported by candlelight and daily visualization.

Quantities

1 candle (color matched to intention), salt for circle, optional: incense, personal link item for proxy work

Directions

  1. Choose a layout (three-card for focused goals, twelve-card for sweeping life changes).
  2. Select cards that represent the desired outcome. Place the central card first, then supporting cards. Consider using Deuces as energetic batteries in modifier positions.
  3. Place a candle behind the cards on a stable surface that can remain undisturbed for seven days.
  4. Surround everything with a complete circle of salt (or herbs). If casting for another person, place a personal link item (photo, jewelry, hair) between the candle and cards.
  5. Begin ideally between Tuesday sunset and Wednesday sunset (Mercury rules magick and communication). Work at the same time each day for seven consecutive days.
  6. Light the candle. Sit with the cards for at least seven minutes, visualizing the outcome as if it has already manifested. Focus on each card and why it was chosen.
  7. After seven minutes, extinguish the candle and speak a closing phrase. Repeat daily for seven days (seven is the number of completion).

A seven-day working that reverses the flow of cartomancy. Instead of drawing cards at random to see what the future may hold, the caster intentionally selects cards that represent the desired outcome and lays them out with purpose. The magick comes from within the caster, channeled through the familiar language of the deck.

Choosing Cards

Select cards whose meanings align with the intended outcome. Any layout can be used, but the three-card layout works well for focused goals (central card for the core desire, flanking cards to clarify intention), while the twelve-card layout is suited to broader, life-changing workings.

The Deuces as Batteries

All four Twos are wild cards that create change and movement. They serve as energetic batteries in a card spell:

  • Two of Hearts: Romance or friendship spells
  • Two of Diamonds: Financial goals, career advancement
  • Two of Clubs: Internal transformation, personal growth
  • Two of Spades: Launching new projects or ventures

In a three-card layout, use a Deuce as one of the modifier cards, not the central card. In a twelve-card layout, place a Deuce in any position where no specific intention is needed.

Candle Colors

A blue candle works for any card spell, but choosing a color matched to the intention adds focus:

  • Red: Healing, lust, romance
  • Orange: Courage, resolve
  • Yellow: Friendship, knowledge, intellect
  • Green: Prosperity, fertility
  • Blue: Protection, all-purpose
  • Purple: Power, influence

Setting Up the Working Space

A surface that can remain undisturbed for a full week is required. An altar or dedicated workspace is ideal, but any level surface with some privacy will serve.

  1. Place the candle on the surface
  2. Lay out the chosen cards in front of the candle
  3. If working on behalf of a loved one (with their consent), place a personal link between the candle and cards: a photograph, piece of jewelry, hair, nail clippings, or anything the person has had intimate contact with
  4. Surround everything with a complete circle of salt to focus the energy

The Seven-Day Working

The best time to begin is between Tuesday sunset and Wednesday sunset, the period ruled by Mercury, which governs communication and magick. If Wednesday is not convenient, any day will work. The critical requirement is performing the working at the same time each day for all seven days.

Each session:

  1. Light the candle (and optional incense as a deity offering)
  2. Sit with the cards for a minimum of seven minutes
  3. Visualize the outcome not as something that will happen, but as if it has already come to pass
  4. Focus on each card individually, remembering why it was chosen and what it represents
  5. After at least seven minutes, extinguish the candle
  6. Speak a closing phrase: "So mote it be," "Amen," or whatever statement provides a sense of closure

Repeat for seven consecutive days. Seven is the number of completion.

Enhancements

Planetary Timing

Beginning the spell on a day ruled by a planet that governs the goal adds potency. Each natural day begins at sunset the evening before (the same tradition behind Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve, and All Hallows' Eve).

  • Sunday (Sun): Growth, knowledge, health, mental strength
  • Monday (Moon): Protection, secrets, psychic ability
  • Tuesday (Mars): Courage, health, physical strength, victory
  • Wednesday (Mercury): Magick, communication, commerce
  • Thursday (Jupiter): Prosperity, success, growth, health
  • Friday (Venus): Love, lust, emotions, beauty
  • Saturday (Saturn): Stability, security, binding

Moon Phases

To bring something into life or empower something, begin the spell between the first visible crescent and the half-moon so the entire seven days fall within the waxing phase.

To banish or diminish something, begin after the full moon but before it shrinks past half so the entire spell is worked during the waning phase.

Ideally, begin on the night of the half-moon: this allows the spell to finish at the full moon (for attracting workings) or at the dark moon (for banishing). Timing the best day given planetary and lunar variables is a skill that develops with practice. Trust instinct.

Herbal Circles

The salt circle can be replaced or blended with dried herbs matched to the goal:

  • Courage: Mullein, black tea, thyme, yarrow
  • Happiness: Hyacinth blossoms, lavender, St. John's wort
  • Healing: Lemon balm, bay leaves, mint, cinnamon, thyme
  • Love: Basil, cloves, dill, rose petals, rosemary
  • Luck: Daffodils, holly, violets
  • Lust: Cinnamon, daisies, dill, parsley, southernwood
  • Mental Strength: Lily of the valley, rosemary, rue
  • Harmony: Gardenia blossoms, lavender, pennyroyal, violets
  • Protection: Basil, bay leaves, dill, garlic
  • Success: Lemon balm, cinnamon, clover

The herbs should completely enclose the cards and candle, though a large pile is not necessary. Salt and herbs can also be combined.

Galdor (Verbal Magick)

The spoken word has its own power. The Old English called this art galdor, and the modern word "spell" itself evolved from spellian, meaning to speak or proclaim. Stating intention aloud while lighting the candle strengthens the working, but crafting the words with care amplifies the effect.

Alliteration repeats consonant sounds at the beginning of words, creating rhythmic energy. For a healing spell using lemon balm and bay leaves: "By balm and bay, I beckon a boon, bind blood to blood and bone to bone."

Rhyme makes words cling to the mind. For the same healing spell: "By lemon balm and sweet bay leaf, let (name) know relief." Or with salt: "Mind and body, spirit and soul, (name) shall now be whole."

Both techniques can be combined. Single couplets work well, but longer incantations of four or more lines are equally valid. Some casters develop an all-purpose closing couplet that can follow any working, such as: "By all the power of land and sea, as my words proclaim, so let it be."

Sacred Space

Practitioners of ceremonial magick, Wicca, or any tradition with its own method of creating sacred space may cast a ritual circle around the working area before beginning each session. Unlike the salt circle, this larger ritual circle can be dismantled after each daily session and recast the following day.
