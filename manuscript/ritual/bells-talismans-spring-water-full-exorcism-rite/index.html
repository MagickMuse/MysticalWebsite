A multi-step exorcism for cases of spirit possession or haunting that combines intensive space cleansing, sound work with bells, cymbals, and sistra, invocation of protective spirits, talisman creation and destruction, and a closing purification drink. A ritual assistant is generally needed to perform this work effectively.

Quantities

  • Bells, cymbals, and sistra
  • Materials for drawing talismans of protection
  • Spring water (boiled and cooled)

Directions

  1. Purify the area using intensive cleansing methods.
  2. Ring bells, cymbals, and sistra loudly throughout the space in addition to any other methods employed.
  3. Invoke benevolent protective spirits such as Archangel Michael, Kwan Yin, and Isis.
  4. Draw up talismans of protection.
  5. Summon the demons, demanding they come forward and depart.
  6. Observe for signs of departure, dramatic spirits will make their exit clear.
  7. If departure is uncertain, use divination to determine whether the methods have been effective or what further action is needed.
  8. Once certain the spirits have gone, burn the talismans.
  9. Mix a teaspoon of the cooled ashes into boiled, cooled spring water and have the afflicted person drink it.
  10. Close the ceremony.
