❄️Cooling Anger

A freezer spell adaptation to cool a bad temper. Uses a poppet inscribed with the Mars symbol and sweetened mint tea to freeze anger and regain self-control.

Quantities

  • 1 poppet of yourself (sewn doll, chess piece, or anything representing you)
  • Pen or marker that writes on the poppet
  • 1 cup sweetened mint tea
  • Sealable plastic bag big enough to hold the poppet
  • Your freezer

Directions

  1. A freezer spell adaptation to cool a bad temper.
  2. Uses a poppet inscribed with the Mars symbol and sweetened mint tea to freeze anger and regain self-control.

Do you have a terrible temper? Does your hotheadedness create problems for you? Has anyone suggested that you get anger management training? Do you smash things, have a vicious tongue, or cause harm in some other way? Do you need to learn to control your temper for your own good as well as for the good of others? If so, this spell can help you.

Why Sweetened Mint Tea?

Mint has cooling properties, both physically and energetically. The sweetener is added because you are not trying to become cold or bitter, but rather to temper your fire with sweetness and calm. The combination helps transform destructive anger into measured response.

Preparation

Get into a magickal mood, and create sacred space around the area where you will be working.

The planet Mars rules anger, so inscribe the poppet with its symbol: ♂

The poppet can be anything that represents you in your mind: a sewn "voodoo doll," a chess piece, or anything else.

Make a cup of mint tea, and add sugar, honey, or another sweetener to it. Drink half of the tea, then put the rest of it into the refrigerator. Leave it there until it has grown very cold.

Focus on your anger while you wait for the tea to cool. Think about all of the problems that it has caused for you and for others. Own that anger, and accept responsibility for it. Know that you are the only one who can do anything about it. Visualize all the positive changes that will likely be made in your life, and the lives of others, once you get your temper under control.

The Spell

Take the tea out of the refrigerator once it is cold. Raise power. Hold the poppet in your projective hand, and command it to cool off. Tell it that this is the end of the road for your bad temper. Put the poppet into the bag, and pour the cold tea over it. Seal the bag, and put it into your freezer. Stick it out of the way in a place where it will not be seen every time the freezer is opened.

Visualize your anger cooling as the tea freezes. Tell yourself that you will now rule yourself, instead of allowing your temper to rule you. Ground the power that you raised.

Afterward

Summon the feeling of ice water into your veins whenever you feel yourself getting angry. If you do have a tantrum, or indulge yourself in any other display of anger, get the poppet out of the freezer and hold it until you cool down.

An Important Note

If you are so out of control that your anger has caused you to commit crimes, such as arson or domestic violence, you need to get professional psychological help as well as casting this spell. If you care about your karma, you also need to accept justice or make restitution for these crimes.
