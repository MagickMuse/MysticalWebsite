A home-based alternative to the beach petition to Yemaya: a heavily salted bath recreates the sea, seven blue candles are lit, and an offering of salted water with seashells is presented while petitioning her for protection from domestic violence.

Quantities

Sea salt (large quantity)
7 blue candles
1 wine glass
Seashells
White roses (optional, for maintaining on altar)

Directions

  1. Add large quantities of sea salt to your bath water.
  2. Light seven blue candles for Yemaya and offer her a wine glass containing salted water and seashells.
  3. Soak in the water, gaze at the candles, and tell her what you need.
  4. If possible, maintain white roses on an altar beside the goblet of salt water.
