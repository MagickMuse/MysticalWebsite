🪞Sacred Midwife Spell: Hathor

An offering-table ritual requesting Hathor's assistance in the birth chamber and a favorable destiny for the new baby, honoring both Hathor and the Seven Hathors who greet each birth.

Quantities

Copper handmirror
Sistrum
1 glass pure spring water
Beer
Myrrh
1 piece turquoise
1 piece malachite
Kohl powder
7 red ribbons

Directions

  1. Create an offering table for Hathor and place a copper handmirror and a sistrum upon it.
  2. Shake the sistrum rhythmically to call her.
  3. Offer her a glass of pure spring water, some beer, myrrh, a piece of turquoise, a piece of malachite, and some kohl powder.
  4. Place seven red ribbons on the table, one for each of the Seven Hathors.
  5. Soon after the birth is complete, if satisfied with Hathor's performance on your behalf (and if you think you'd like to appeal to her again), weave the ribbons together and toss them into a spring or river.
