A dual-herb protection pillow calling upon both Artemis (mugwort, night) and Apollo (Saint John's Wort, sun) to watch over the sleeper around the clock. Silver charms in the shape of dolphins or moons may be added for enhancement. One of the Dream Pillow methods.

Quantities

  • Equal parts dried mugwort and dried Saint John's Wort
  • Optional: small smooth silver charms (dolphin or moon shapes)
  • A pillow or bag to fill

Directions

  1. Combine equal parts of dried mugwort and dried Saint John's Wort.
  2. Use this blend to fill the dream pillow.
  3. If desired, tuck small smooth silver charms in the shape of dolphins or moons into the pillow alongside the herbs.
