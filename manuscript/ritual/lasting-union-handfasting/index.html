💕Lasting Union Handfasting

◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Method
Candle MagickWater MagickElementalInvocationBinding
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 1 red candle (to symbolize love and fire element) - 1 glass of water with ice (preferably in clear, blue, or silver cup) Optional ritual attire: - For fire person: Red, orange, gold, yellow, or white robes/clothing - For water person: Translucent fabrics, blue, silver, or black clothing Optional space preparation: - Broom for clearing - Incense - Extra candles - Fresh flowers - Altar for placing objects
◇Directions
1. Pre-Ritual Preparations: Decide which role each participant will play: fire or water. Let personal preferences guide selection, or consider having the person with more masculine, dominant, or active personality play fire, while the person with more feminine, softer, gentler personality plays water. 2. Ritual attire options: Go skyclad or don special clothing. 3. Fire person might wear red or orange robes (symbolizing flame), gold or yellow (honoring sun), or solid white (representing pure light). 4. Water person might wear translucent fabrics or blue shades (symbolizing water), silver (paying homage to moon), or solid black (representing darkness of deep water, darkness beneath ground where seeds grow, and darkness of cosmic womb from which we all emerge). 5. Space preparation: If outdoors in peaceful natural setting, ensure area is free of debris and feels safe and comfortable. 6. If indoors, choose clean, uncluttered place. 7. For spaces with negative or stale feeling, perform space clearing: Sweep with broom starting from center and moving toward edges in outward counterclockwise spiral, visualizing stale/negative energies dissipating. 8. You may also perform basic circle casting, light incense and extra candles for magickal ambiance, or enhance space with fresh flowers. 9. The Ritual: Begin facing one another, fire holding candle, water holding glass. Fire lights candle, envisioning sunlight and flame radiating from body's core, dissolving all traces of ego. Fire says: "I am Fire. I am Sun. I am the warmth, the light, the seed, and the will. I am [insert your full name]." 10. Water takes sip from glass, envisioning rivers, oceans, and rains flowing down throat and integrating into being as they swallow, letting energetic vibration of water overpower personality. Water says: "I am Water. I am Moon. I am the blood, the food, the womb, and the intention. I am [insert your full name]." 11. Water becomes cosmic womb while fire becomes fertilizing seed, combination from which springs new creation: the new cooperative partnership between anima and animus. 12. This is sacred union, magick of intermingling polarities giving rise to creation and manifestation. 13. Be whatever you are representing, say it, open heart and body to it, invite those energies to flow through you. 14. Be vessel for what you represent. 15. Forget who you are in everyday life. 16. You are fire. 17. You are water. 18. You are womb. 19. You are seed. 20. You are yin. 21. You are yang. 22. Don't just think it, be it to greatest extent you can manage. 23. By emptying yourself of identity and ego, you make yourself open vessel for any energies you wish to temporarily "host" within your body. 24. This is invocation: actually invoking or taking within your own body the spirit, entity, or energy you've invited. 25. Once both partners are fully "in the zone," in character with respective roles and unhampered by mundane thoughts and feelings, look into each other's eyes and raise the energy. 26. Think of specific outcome you hope to achieve with ritual, main aim and purpose you're hoping to manifest. 27. Feel emotions of that reality, what it would be like and feel like to have successful, lasting partnership. 28. As you look into partner's eyes, let emotions flow back and forth between you, magnifying with each "pass" until you sense energy is at its highest vibration. 29. At this point, Water opens completely, envisioning fertile field, womb, and primordial darkness ready to embrace spark of life. 30. Fire continues focusing on ritual goal (creating permanent, loving bond), and when vision and feeling of love and unity is clear in both mind and heart, send every bit of energy you have into body and spirit of Water. 31. Envision magickal power you've raised through emotion and visualization flowing out of your eyes into partner's eyes. 32. Water should feel this action, it should feel like powerful blast of emotionally charged energy. 33. Once energy has been received by Water, exchange objects you are holding, passing glass of water to Fire and giving candle to Water. Both ritualists say: "By our powers combined, by moon and by sun, by water and fire, what we will, will be won!" 34. Fire takes sip of water. Cup and candle are placed side by side on altar. Partners clasp hands together over altar, gazing steadily into each other's eyes as they repeat in unison nine times: "We are bound together, together forever!" 35. As this is said, ritualists envision ropes of golden light, made of pure love, circling around wrists and entwining hands together. 36. Partners share loving, passionate kiss. 37. Water then takes ice cube out of glass and holds it over candle flame, melting ice and putting out candle. 38. Clasping hands once again, partners say in unison: "Our love remains through dark and light! May love transform us, day and night!" 39. Partners share another kiss. 40. Handfasting ritual is complete.
◇Description
A Beltane handfasting ritual designed to create a lasting union and forge a cooperative, happy, and loving marital partnership between two people. Based on the concept of sacred union, this ritual is moving and powerful, offering a meaningful Pagan alternative to mainstream marriage ceremonies. This is a type of binding magick that ties together the spiritual energies of two individuals, creating a new union with established and fortified boundaries and limitations.
