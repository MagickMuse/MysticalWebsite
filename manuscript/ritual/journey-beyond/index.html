A shamanic journey practice inspired by Horse's ability to transport souls beyond physical reality. Using drumming to induce theta brain waves, you travel through inner landscapes to connect with guides, spirit animals, and subconscious realms.

Quantities

  • Shamanic drumming track (online or recorded)

Directions

  1. Asiatic shamans call their drum a "horse" because vibrations help them travel to realities beyond the physical.
  2. You can travel beyond your physical self too.
  3. Search online for "shamanic journey drumming track." Drumming helps the brain slip into theta wave patterns, letting your mind journey beyond everyday consciousness.
  4. Lie down or sit comfortably.
  5. Set a guiding intention for your journey, such as "I want to connect with my spirit animal."
  6. Play the drumming track and think about a place in nature that's comfortable and familiar.
  7. In your mind, create a door from that place into the Earth.
  8. Open the door and step through, imagining yourself traveling downward into the world's heart.
  9. Use all your senses to become aware of this dream space.
  10. Time may pass quickly, and you'll be surprised when you hear the "call back" sequence, when the drumming rhythm speeds up signaling it's time to return.
  11. Retrace your steps, coming back the same way you went in. Be sure to close the door behind you to separate your waking life from the subconscious realms of the journey.
  12. Spend a few minutes noting your experience while fresh in your mind, capturing details like color, symbol, and archetypes.
