🕯️Paian to Hygeia

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Devotional WorkChant/MantraInvocation
◇Category
Invocation
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
A household altar with a lit lamp Offerings of the day
◇Directions
1. Stand at the household altar with the lamp lit and the offerings arranged. 2. Take the standing posture of adoration, hands outstretched toward the heavens. 3. Speak or chant the paian in a clear, measured voice. 4. O Hygeia of the Blissful Ones, you are most revered by mortals, with you I traverse the life remaining in me. For what is the joy of wealth or of children, or what brings people closer to the Gods? The power of a king, or the secret lust brought by Aphrodite, or that obvious relief from pain which is the god-sent delight? With you, blissful divine Hygeia, the company of the Kharites shines and blooms. Without you, no one has well-being. 5. Drawn from Ariphron, Paian to Hygeia.
◇Description
A paian composed by Ariphron in honour of Hygeia, addressed as the most revered of the Blissful Ones among mortals. The poem sets the joys of wealth, children, kingship, and the secret lust brought by Aphrodite alongside the simpler relief from pain that comes as a god-sent delight, and concludes that without Hygeia, and without the bright company of the Kharites, no one carries any well-being at all. Suited to household devotion focused on health, recovery, and the steady ground of a sound life.
