Dill, Saint John's wort and vervain bundled in white handkerchiefs and tied with blue are hung around the home for spiritual and magickal protection.

Quantities

Dillweed
Saint John's wort
Vervain
White handkerchiefs
Blue ribbon or blue thread

Directions

  1. Gather dillweed, Saint John's wort and vervain into white handkerchiefs.
  2. Tie each packet closed with blue ribbon, or sew them into sachets with blue thread.
  3. Hang them strategically throughout the home.
