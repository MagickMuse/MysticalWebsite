🌑Basic Banishing Powder🛡️Four Thieves Vinegar

An extra-strength nine-night banishing spell ideally timed to the waning moon. Each night a name paper with banishing powder and a personal link is burned and added to a bottle of Four Thieves Vinegar. After the ninth night, the bottle is wrapped in black cloth, knotted nine times, and thrown into running water or a cemetery.

Quantities

  • Brown paper (9 pieces)
  • Pen
  • Banishing powder (any formula)
  • Target's personal link: 1 hair, nail clipping, or thread from their clothing (per night)
  • 1 bottle or jar
  • Four Thieves Vinegar (enough to fill the jar)
  • Black fabric (enough to wrap the bottle)
  • Cord or string

Directions

  1. An extra-strength banishing spell:
  2. Each night for nine consecutive nights (ideally timed with the waning moon, but cast the spell as necessary), write the target's name on brown paper together with a brief, explicit message, such as "Go home!"
  3. Sprinkle one of the banishing powders over the paper.
  4. Add one of the target's hairs, a nail clipping, or a thread from their clothing.
  5. Burn everything; place the ashes in a bottle of Four Thieves Vinegar.
  6. Following the ninth night, wrap the bottle tightly in black fabric, securing it with cord.
  7. Make nine knots in the cord, reiterating your desire for banishment with each one.
  8. Throw this bottle into running water or a cemetery and return home via a circuitous route.
