Samhain

An elemental blessing for the new year using water, beer, fire, and air. Asks that brightness pass to all present so efforts prove fertile in living good cheer.

Quantities

  • Water
  • Beer (or libation)
  • Fire (candle or bonfire)
  • Smudge or incense for air

Directions

  1. Hail to the New Year!
    We cleanse you with water,
    we gift you with beer,
    we brighten you with fire,
    we smudge your air.
    Pass this brightness to all here
    that our efforts prove fertile
    in living good cheer.
    Buoy our spirits,
    make the world bright.
    Gre tings, new annum, on this Samhain night!
