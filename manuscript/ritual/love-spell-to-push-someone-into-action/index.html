💘Love Spell to Push Someone into Action

◇Form
Candle RiteDressed CandleSigil
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/MantraDivinationIncense Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 red votive candle - Red paper - Pen and ink (dove's blood or black) - Small piece of parchment or red paper - 1 cauldron or heat-safe container - 1/2 tsp marjoram - 1/2 tsp yarrow - Pinch of pine needles - 1 marigold flower - Stick or cone incense (jasmine, rose, or musk) - Something belonging to the intended, a photo, or parchment with their name and birthdate
◇Directions
1. On red paper, draw a mandala: a circle with a double ring around the edge. 2. Write within the ring, separated by dots: Habondia at the top, Phul at the bottom, Adonis at the left, and Hagith at the right. 3. Alternatively, fill the ring with "Love Conquers All So Mote It Be" written in runic symbols. 4. Draw a pentagram inside the circle so the tips touch the inner rim of the double ring. 5. Fill the mandala as follows: Hagith sigil in the center, the Venus symbol in the head of the star, Coll in the left leg, the Sun symbol in the right leg, and the arms left empty. 6. Place the Och sigil under the left arm and the Phul sigil under the right arm. 7. The zodiac sign of the desired person goes above the left arm, and the planetary ruler of that sign above the right arm. 8. The seeker's own zodiac sign and planetary ruler go in the space between the legs of the star. 9. Inscribe the red votive candle with the runes Idho, Tiwaz, Berkanan, Sowilo, Wunjo, and Gebo. 10. Place the cauldron on top of the paper pentacle, set the red candle inside, and light it. 11. Speak: 12. Red candle burn bright as [name] is possessed by burning love for [me]. 13. Let [their] desire burn for me as nothing has been so desired before. 14. Let [them] burn with love for me. 15. Drop something belonging to the intended into the flame, or use parchment paper with the intended's name and birthdate. 16. Speak: 17. This IS [name] burning with love for me! 18. Add a pinch of pine needles and a marigold flower to the candle flame and speak: 19. Bring [name] to [me], that [name] love only me, and may the essence of these herbs incite [name] to love only me, and to come to me! 20. So Mote It Be! 21. Let the candle burn down for one hour while scrying for signs. 22. Snuff, cool, remove the wax, and bury in the ground. 23. Speak: 24. Here goes the seed into the fertile soil. 25. Let the spell grow with naught to foil. 26. So Mote It Be!
◇Description
A waxing to Full Moon love spell performed on a day and hour of Venus. A mandala is drawn on red paper with a double ring inscribed with the names Habondia, Phul, Adonis, and Hagith. A pentagram is drawn inside the circle and filled with sigils of Hagith, Och, Phul, the Venus symbol, Coll, and the Sun symbol, along with the zodiac signs of both the seeker and the intended. The red votive is inscribed with Idho, Tiwaz, Berkanan, Sowilo, Wunjo, and Gebo, then set in the cauldron atop the paper pentacle. Pine needles and marigold are added to the flame, and the wax is scried for signs before burial.
