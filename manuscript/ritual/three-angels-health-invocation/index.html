🕊️Three Angels Health Invocation

◇Tradition
Folk Christian
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraPrayerInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
No materials required (optional: sacred texts for recitation)
◇Directions
1. Chant: 2. "Three angels stand on the far side of Jordan One binds, one resolves, One cries out, Kadosh, Kadosh, Kadosh!" 3. Continue with further chants and recitations of sacred texts. 4. Tell the three angels what you need and state your petition clearly.
◇Description
A healing invocation chanting to three angels standing at the far side of the Jordan River, one who binds, one who resolves, and one who cries 'Kadosh, Kadosh, Kadosh!' (Holy, Holy, Holy). Followed by further sacred recitations and a personal petition.
