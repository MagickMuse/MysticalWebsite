A ritual for creating and activating a Magick Square of Mars talisman for cursing, wrath, or martial protection. The practitioner inscribes the 5x5 Mars square on paper or iron and consecrates it with Martial Incense, activating it through a personal charm or mantra repeated while tracing the numbers in sequence.

Quantities

  • Sheet of paper or iron plate for inscribing
  • Pen, dip pen, or iron stylus
  • Martial Incense of Wrath and Chastisement (burned in thurible)
  • Optional: red cord or cloth for wrapping the finished talisman

Directions

  1. Work on a Tuesday during the waning or dark moon.
  2. Exorcise the paper or iron with salt water and Martial Incense smoke before inscribing.
  3. Draw the 5x5 Magick Square of Mars on the surface. The square holds numbers 1 through 25 arranged so that every row, column, and diagonal sums to 65. The rows read: 11 24 7 20 3 / 4 12 25 8 16 / 17 5 13 21 9 / 10 18 1 14 22 / 23 6 19 2 15.
  4. Light the Martial Incense in the thurible and pass the inscribed square through the smoke to charge it.
  5. Place the finished square on your working surface. Lay both hands upon it.
  6. Fix the target in your mind. Compose or select a short personal charm or mantra for the working (such as "Mars, strike true" or a chain of martial names: "Mars, Ares, Tyr").
  7. Trace the numbers 1 through 25 in sequence with your fingertip, repeating the charm or mantra at each number.
  8. When the sequence is complete, the talisman is activated.
  9. Wrap the talisman in red cloth or cord and carry it, bury it near the target's home, or place it in a location connected to the target.
  10. Divine the outcome before and after this working.
