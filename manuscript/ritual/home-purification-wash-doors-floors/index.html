Have you recently had someone toxic move out of your home? The first step to eliminating their bad energy is to get rid of all their stuff, which is really gratifying. Then do a thorough cleansing using this home purification. For extra magickal energy, create this on the night of the full moon. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • All of the herbs needed for the spell can be fresh or dried and you need about one tablespoon of each
  • The water should be hot, about 1 gallon

Directions

  1. Place the herbs in the draw string bag and add it to the hot water.
  2. Stir It In a Clockwise direction to blend it.
  3. Use the water to wash your hard floors and clean your windows.
  4. As you clean, say, My home is sacred, my home is safe, my home is a sanctuary, and with this water, I cleanse it of ill intent and negative energy.
  5. When you finished, take the water outside to dispose of it.
