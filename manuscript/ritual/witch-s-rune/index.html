🕯️Witch's Rune

A Green Witchcraft adaptation of the classic energy-raising chant based on Gerald Gardner and Doreen Valiente's work. Invokes Hecate and Cernunnos alongside mysterious entities Azarel and Shadiel, calls the four directions and elements, awakens ritual tools, and raises power for spellwork. Used to build energy before releasing it toward magickal intention.

Quantities

  • Wand
  • Cauldron
  • Candle(s)
  • Censer with incense
  • Pentacle
  • Cord
  • Bell

Directions

  1. This chant raises energy for spellwork.
  2. Begin with your ritual tools assembled on the altar.
  3. Speak or chant the opening invocations to the mysterious powers and deities:
  4. Eko, Eko Azarel!
    Eko, Eko Shadiel!
    Eko, Eko Hecate!
    Eko, Eko Cernunnos!
  5. Continue with the main body of the rune, calling the directions and awakening your tools:
  6. Darksome night and shining Moon
    Hearken to this Witch's Rune.
    North, then East, then South and West;
    Attend me here at my behest!
    By power of Earth, Air, Fire, and Sea
    Be thou all in accord with me.
    Wand and Cauldron by Candle's light;
    Awaken all ye into life!
    Censer, Pentacle, and my Cord;
    Hearken ye to ward discord!
    By Power and by Spoken Word,
    I charge my spell be cast and heard!
    Queen of Witches, by ringing bell,
    Send your aid unto this spell!
    Horned Hunter of the Night,
    Work my will by magick rite!
    By the Powers of Land and Sea,
    As I Will, So Mote It Be!
    By the Powers of Moon and Sun,
    As I Will, this Spell is Done!
  7. Close with the Ekos again:
  8. Eko, Eko Azarel!
    Eko, Eko Shadiel!
    Eko, Eko Hecate!
    Eko, Eko Cernunnos!
  9. Repeat the Ekos to raise energy to its peak, then release toward your magickal intention.
