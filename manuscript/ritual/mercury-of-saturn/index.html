Mercury of Saturn

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
InvocationPlanetaryChant/MantraBinding
◇Category
Invocation
◇Quantities
- No materials required
◇Directions
1. Perform this invocation on the day of Saturn at the hour of Mercury. 2. Vibrate the vowels Omega followed by Epsilon (OHW-EH), with the day vowel shorter in duration followed by a longer vibration of the hour vowel. 3. Then speak the invocation: 4. Masters of Malifica and Motion Restrict and Restrain my Rivals Bind their intelligence with inertia End their expressiveness with entropy Cut their connections and communications Until they no longer pose a threat to our plans.
◇Description
A curse for confounding communications and for binding movement.
