🌙Draught of Wisdom

◇Directions
1. Load a rune tine according to the standard ritual form, with two modifications: do not color the runes with pigment, instead wet them with mead, ale, or beer; and do not carve deeply, etch the runes lightly into the surface of the tine. 2. After loading, take the rune knife and scrape the runes off into the cup of mead while singing the scraping chant. 3. Mix the contents well with the rune knife while repeating the mixing chant. 4. Hold the cup or horn aloft and speak the invocation: "Odhroerir roar into the draught!" 5. Drink the contents, rune might and all.
◇Description
A ritual in which the vitki loads a rune tine, scrapes the staves into mead or ale, and drinks the draught to absorb rune wisdom directly into the personal system. Rooted in the Sigrdrifumal initiatory tradition.

With the Draught of Wisdom, the vitki loads a rune tine, scrapes the runes into a drink of mead, ale, or beer, and drinks it down, rune might and all. In the sixth stanza of the Sigrdrifumal, the valkyrja Sigrdifa offers a fine example of this practice in the context of an initiatory ritual, speaking to the hero Sigurdhr:

I bring thee beer,
thou warrior of battle,
blended with might
and mighty renown;
it is full of songs
and soothing staves,
good magick
and mighty runes.

The valkyrja then teaches the hero rune lore.

Chants

Scraping chant:

I shave the shining runes,
and their shaping-might,
from the wood of wisdom;
into the draught they drop!

Mixing chant:

Rune-might be mixed
with this mead of wisdom,
blended together in a bond of strength.

See Loading a Rune Tine (LAUKAZ) for the standard rune tine loading ritual referenced in the Directions.
