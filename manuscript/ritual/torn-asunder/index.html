🦴Torn Asunder

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraCord Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 dry chicken bone (drumstick or larger, dried 2 weeks) Do As I Say oil or Bend Over oil 1 piece of red yarn 1 piece of black yarn
◇Directions
1. Allow a chicken bone, preferably a larger one such as a drumstick, to dry out completely for at least two weeks. 2. Rub a small amount of Do As I Say oil or Bend Over oil onto each end of the bone. 3. Tie a piece of red yarn to one end and a piece of black yarn to the other. 4. Do not tie the two pieces of yarn to each other, only to the bone. 5. Grip one end of the bone in each hand and repeat the following: 6. Break is broken, spell is spoken. 7. Speak the names of both people in the relationship aloud three times, then snap the bone in half. 8. The bone must break cleanly for the spell to work. 9. If it bends rather than snapping, do not force it. 10. Bury the bone and wait until you have a new, fully dried bone before attempting again. 11. If the bone snaps as intended, keep the two halves far apart from each other.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A dark love spell intended to break apart an existing relationship. A dried chicken bone is anointed with oil, tied with red and black yarn, and snapped in half while the couple's names are spoken.
