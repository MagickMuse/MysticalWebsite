🔮Cup Scrying

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• Large wine glass • Spring water, Holy Water, or Marie Laveau Water (made with lavender water, not essential oil) • Few drops of liquefied palm oil • White candle
◇Directions
1. Fill a large wine glass with spring water, Holy Water, or Marie Laveau Water. 2. If using Marie Laveau Water, ensure it is made with lavender water and not lavender essential oil. 3. Add a few drops of liquefied palm oil. 4. Place a lit white candle behind the glass so its light illuminates the water from behind. 5. Calmly gaze into the glass until visions appear.
◇Description
A traditional method in which a wine glass filled with spring water or Holy Water, with a few drops of palm oil, is used as a scrying vessel in the manner of a crystal ball. A white candle placed behind the glass provides illumination.
