🌈Rainbow Power for Good Fortune

An early morning spell for absorbing power of rainbow for increased good fortune in your life and home, eating foods from six color groups to fill heart and soul with iridescent light and radiance.

Quantities

  • Fresh raw or lightly cooked foods from each of the six color groups (combining indigo and purple):
    Red: apples, red currants, strawberries, red peppers
    Orange: carrots, oranges, melons, pumpkins
    Yellow: bananas, sweet corn, grapefruit, yellow peppers
    Green: cucumber, pears, lettuce, spinach, grapes
    Blue: plums, blueberries, damsons
    Purple: black currants, grapes, eggplant

Directions

  1. Going out into the open air if possible, eat a little of the food from each color group in turn.
  2. Say before you eat each one: "Rainbow, rainbow, shining bright, fill my heart and soul with light. As your iridescence grows, rainbow within me, so your radiance glows." Use any remaining rainbow food in cooking.
  3. Perform early morning.
