A petition and spell work to Saint Rita to remove an ex-husband who continues abusing after the marriage has ended; roses are offered and the marriage document is burned to sever the bond.

Quantities

A bouquet of roses
Vase for the roses
Baking soda
Marriage license or similar document (original or copy)

Directions

  1. Place a bouquet of roses in a vase and offer them to Saint Rita.
  2. Sprinkle baking soda around the base of the vase.
  3. Obtain either a copy or the original of your marriage license or a similar document of your marriage, and burn it.
