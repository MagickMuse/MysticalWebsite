💤Romantic Mugwort

A sensual, romantic variation of the mugwort dream bath that retains full dream-stimulating potency while adding erotic and love-dream qualities. Particularly suited for love-dream oracles and erotic dream work. One of the Dream Bath methods.

Quantities

  • One handful of dried hibiscus flowers
  • One tablespoon of orange blossoms
  • One tablespoon of dried red rose petals
  • One teaspoon of dried mugwort
  • Three dried bay leaves

Directions

  1. Combine all the botanicals in a bowl and cover with boiling water.
  2. Allow the infusion to cool completely.
  3. Strain out the plant material if desired, then pour the liquid into a warm bath.
  4. Take the bath just before going to sleep.
