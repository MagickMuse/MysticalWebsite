⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A multi-tiered fear curse performed within a pentagram of five candles. The practitioner chooses from four escalating levels of severity: a basic fear incantation, a quartered-image amplification, a bonfire invocation of Satan, or a mutilated-image chant that strips the target of light and fills them with darkness. Each tier produces increasingly intense dread in the target upon seeing the caster.

Quantities

1 photograph or written name of the target
5 candles (arranged in pentagram formation)
1 bonfire (optional, for Tier 3)
1 red marker (optional, for a fifth variation)
Scissors or blade (for Tiers 2 and 4)

Directions

  1. Arrange five candles in a pentagram formation and place the target's name or photograph in the centre.
  2. This working offers four escalating tiers of fear, each with its own method and incantation.
  3. Tier 1 requires only the name or photo placed in the pentagram with a spoken incantation.
  4. Tier 2 requires slicing the name or photo into four pieces and placing them in the centre.
  5. Tier 3 requires a bonfire; the name or photo is cast into the flames while invoking Satan.
  6. Tier 4 requires cutting the first letter from the target's name, or the face from their photograph, and reciting a chant that strips the target of light and fills them with darkness.
  7. A fifth variation using a red marker is referenced but left to the practitioner's design.

Setup

Arrange five candles in a pentagram formation. Place the target's name or photograph in the centre of the pentagram. Choose one of the four tiers below based on the level of fear you wish to inflict.

Tier 1: Average Fear

Place the name or photograph in the centre of the pentagram and speak:

Dark forces, corrupt thy foe and feast on their fear. All their nightmares will be about me. All of the frightening tales they hear will be about me in their ears.

The following day, the target will regard you with visible unease wherever they encounter you.

Tier 2: Fetal Position Fear

Slice the name or photograph into four pieces and place all four in the centre of the pentagram. No spoken incantation is required; the act itself carries the intent. Upon seeing you, the target will be unable to resist curling into a fetal position.

Tier 3: I Wish I Was Never Born Fear

This tier requires a bonfire. Cast the name or photograph into the flames and chant:

Satan, make their soul suffer. Make their heart jump from chest to throat. Make them be very afraid.

From this point forward, the mere sight of you will fill the target with abject terror.

Tier 4: Scared Shitless

Slice the first letter from the target's written name, or cut the face from their photograph. Chant:

You are no more. Light be removed from this sorry excuse of a human being, and darkness be placed inside of this shrivelling shell.

The next day, the target will be paralysed with fear at the sight of you.
