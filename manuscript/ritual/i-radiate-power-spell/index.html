A persuasion-enhancing spell in which commanding botanicals are steeped in laundry bluing at the New Moon, and the treated bluing is then used to wash clothing worn when making requests of others.

Quantities

  • Coriander
  • Licorice root
  • Sweet flag (calamus)
  • Laundry bluing

Directions

  1. At the New Moon, place coriander, licorice root, and sweet flag into a bottle of laundry bluing.
  2. Allow the botanicals to steep in the bluing for seven days, then strain them out.
  3. Add the treated bluing to your laundry when washing appropriate clothing.
  4. Wear these garments when in the company of others and making a request or seeking to persuade.
