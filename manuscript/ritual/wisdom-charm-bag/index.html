Mabon

A Mabon working that creates a charm bag to guide the carrier toward wisdom and balanced decision-making, using acorns, rose thorns, sunflower seeds, and an owl charm anointed weekly with sage or rue oil. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

Small black velvet drawstring bag
5 acorns
5 rose thorns (optional)
Image of a person who represents wisdom to you
Small owl charm
Handful of sunflower seeds
3-inch square of aluminum foil
Sage essential oil diluted in olive oil, or rue-steeped sunflower oil

Directions

  1. Place the rose thorns (if using), sunflower seeds, and acorns on the aluminum foil and say:
    "Herbs, I bless you and awaken the wisdom
    you have to share with me."
  2. Fold the foil into a packet, place it inside the drawstring bag, and say:
    "Guide me to the wisest choices."
  3. Place the owl charm and the image of personal wisdom in the bag and say:
    "Help me see what I might miss,
    to question until I truly understand."
  4. Anoint the outside of the bag with a touch of the sage or rue oil.
  5. Tuck the bag into a clothing pocket, a hidden coat pocket, or the back of a purse.
  6. Take it out once a week and reanoint it with the oil.
