A chamber spell to enchant the master bedroom for married or deeply committed couples. Invokes love and marriage deities by inscribing their names on walls to foster perfect love, perfect trust, and an enduring bond.

Quantities

Incense
Optional: potted plant (ivy, roses, rosemary, violets, or cyclamen)

Directions

  1. Empty the bedroom completely, open the window(s) to air it out, and clean the room thoroughly, including any closets, bathrooms, hallways, fireplaces, or attached areas such as a deck or balcony.
  2. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space in the bedroom.
  3. Bring in the incense and light it.
  4. Visualize the bedroom as a place that fosters perfect love and perfect trust, focusing on a stable, loving, enduring relationship.
  5. Raise power and pour all your love and hopes for the relationship into the room, envisioning it as a sanctuary where love will be renewed each time the couple sleeps there. Fill the room with the strength of this conjugal love, visualizing it being absorbed into the walls, floor, and ceiling.
  6. While the incense burns, inscribe the names of the following deities upon the walls, saying each sentence aloud as you write the name:
  7. On the door or above the doorway: "BES, to protect this chamber." "OSHUN, to safeguard our relationship." "SELKET, to preserve our union." "VARA, to protect our marriage."
  8. Above the bed: "PARVATI, to watch over us." "FREYA, for a happy marriage." "HATHOR, for pleasure in marriage." "FRIGG, for great sex in marriage." "APHRODITE, for the sanctity of our marriage." "ISIS, to protect our love."
  9. On any wall: "BENZAITEN, for good fortune." "VESTA, for stability." "JUNO, for a prosperous marriage." "THOR, for a fruitful union." "HOLDA, for a proper home." "SHEKINAH, for blessings."
  10. Bring in a potted plant, if using one, and place it near a window.
  11. Remain in the room until the incense finishes burning, lighting another stick or adding more to the censer if needed.
  12. Once your visualization is complete and the incense has burned out, ground the power that you raised. You can now paint or wallpaper the bedroom, then furnish and decorate it.

What Are Chamber Spells?

Chamber spells are spirit-of-place workings that improve the energy signatures of rooms in specific ways. The names of the deities invoked will be written on walls and doors, so it will be necessary to paint or wallpaper after casting the spells. That will conceal the Words of Power but not deactivate them.

This spell is best cast by both partners together but can also be cast by just one of them.

⚠️
Important: The room must be completely emptied and thoroughly cleaned before the spell is cast.

Optional Plants

Any of these potted plants can enhance the spell's energy:

  • Ivy: For fidelity and eternal love
  • Roses: For passion and romance
  • Rosemary: For remembrance and loyalty
  • Violets: For faithfulness
  • Cyclamen: For love and fertility
