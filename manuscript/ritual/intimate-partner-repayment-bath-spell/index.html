A discreet persuasion working for collecting a debt from someone you are intimately involved with, using a dressed purple candle, a hidden petition paper, and Command and Compel Incense to soften them into repaying you calmly and willingly. ⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick. Approach with respect and awareness of its cultural origins.

Quantities

  • Bath water
  • Command and Compel Oil or Essence of Bend Over Oil
  • A piece of paper
  • A purple candle
  • Command and Compel Incense

Directions

  1. Draw a bath for the person who owes you, discreetly adding Command and Compel Oil or Essence of Bend Over Oil to the water.
  2. While they bathe, go into another room.
  3. Write your desire on a piece of paper, writing both your names with your name covering theirs.
  4. Fold the paper and place it discreetly beneath a purple candle dressed with Command and Compel Oil. Burn Command and Compel Incense alongside it.
  5. When the person emerges from the bath, calmly and firmly ask for what you are owed.
