◇Directions
1. Lay out the four Aces, Cups, Pentacles, Swords, and Wands, together with The Sun card. 2. Spend a few quiet moments with the spread, using it to welcome the new soul growing within you and to ease the path toward its emergence for both of you.

A simple tarot spread using the four Aces and The Sun card to welcome an unborn child and ease the transition toward birth.
