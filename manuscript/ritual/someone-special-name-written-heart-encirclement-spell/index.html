📜Someone Special Name Written Heart Encirclement Spell

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingManifestationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Parchment paper
◇Directions
1. Write the name of the person you desire on parchment paper seven times. 2. Draw a heart around these names by writing your own name in continuous script, without picking up the pen. Lengthen each letter or repeat your name; the key is not to lift the pen until all seven names are completely enclosed. 3. Chant: 'You are in my heart' or 'My heart encompasses you.' 4. Preserve this talisman in a safe place. Should you change your mind, rip up the paper, breaking the heart, and destroy it.
◇Description
The target's name is written seven times on parchment and enclosed inside a heart drawn by writing your own name in unbroken script around it. The talisman is preserved to hold the spell.
