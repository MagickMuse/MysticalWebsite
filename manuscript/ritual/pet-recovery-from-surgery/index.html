🏥Pet Recovery from Surgery

A candle progression ritual moving from black through gray to white with nine hematite crystals for post-surgery recovery.

Quantities

  • 1 black candle (behind circle)
  • 1 gray candle (left of circle)
  • 1 white candle (right of circle)
  • Picture of pet in good health (inside circle)
  • 9 small hematite crystals or round gray stones (in circle)

Directions

  1. Light the black candle, saying, "Take away pain, protected may you be, that each day is a step to recovery." Light the gray candle from the black one, saying, "Slow and steady, 'til you are ready. Step by step to recovery." Extinguish the black candle.
  2. Light the white candle from the gray one and say, "So health is restored and you will have vitality once more." Extinguish the gray candle and leave the white one burning.
  3. Use a new white candle each time you do the spell.
  4. Even when the pet is better, leave the crystals enclosing the picture.
  5. Perform the day before the surgery/treatment and as many days after as you wish.
