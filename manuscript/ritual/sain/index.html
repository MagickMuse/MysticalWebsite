Ritual Grimoire

💧Sain

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
CleansingWater MagickDirectional Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Holy Water collected from a local spring or living water source, between midnight and dawn on a waxing moon
◇Directions
1. Collect Holy Water from a local spring or nearby living water source between midnight and dawn, coinciding with a waxing moon. 2. Gather all participants in a circle. 3. Pass the water sunwise for each person to sip and sprinkle over themselves (or sprinkle their neighbor). 4. Sprinkle the water in each corner of each room.
◇Description
A Scottish method of ritually cleansing and blessing that simultaneously purifies an area and all participants. Holy Water must be collected from a local living water source between midnight and dawn on a waxing moon.
