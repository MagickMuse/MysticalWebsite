A healing practice from Dravidian India and Romany tradition in which illness-impregnated clothing or linens are brought to a sacred place (tree, shrine, or holy site) to be purified, or offered to Sarah Kali at her shrine to receive her healing power in return. The same principle applies to a personal shrine with a votive image.

Quantities

Sick person's clothing or bed linens (Dravidian method)
OR clothing to offer (Sarah Kali method)

Directions

  1. Dravidian method: The sick person's clothes and bed linens are believed to be magickally impregnated by illness beyond what laundering can cure.
  2. Bring them to a place of extreme baraka (benevolent sacred power), and hang them on a sacred tree, in a holy place, or at a shrine, to regenerate, revitalize, and make them safe.
  3. This also creates highly charged talismanic fabrics for new linens or ritual clothing.
  4. Sarah Kali method (Romany): At her annual May festival in Les Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, or at a personal shrine, offer clothing to Sarah Kali on behalf of someone who is ill.
  5. Place the clothing on her image or statue; as the clothing absorbs her divine power, the healing is transmitted to the petitioner, whether present or far away.
  6. This principle applies equally to any votive image close to your heart.
