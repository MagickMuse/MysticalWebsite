🏆Win at Sports Spell

A waxing to Full Moon spell performed during the hour of the Sun. A bright yellow candle is inscribed with the runes Raidho, Kenaz, Tiwaz, and Ingwaz, then lit alongside dragon's blood incense. Orris root, woodruff leaves, and bergamot leaves are dropped one by one into the candle flame, each with a spoken invocation calling upon ancestral power, competitive edge, and success. Energy is raised with a chant and directed through a wand into the flame. The candle burns for one hour, then the wax is scried and the remains buried or disposed.

Quantities

  • 1 bright yellow candle
  • Dragon's blood incense and burner
  • 1 wand
  • Orris root
  • Woodruff leaves
  • Bergamot leaves

Directions

  1. Inscribe the yellow candle with the runes Raidho, Kenaz, Tiwaz, and Ingwaz, then light it.
  2. Light the dragon's blood incense and speak:
  3. I call upon the power and energy of dragon's blood that I may be victorious in [name of sport or contest] on [when it will occur]!
  4. Drop the orris root into the candle flame:
  5. Let my competition not exceed me!
  6. Drop the woodruff leaves into the candle flame:
  7. I call upon the energies of woodruff that I may overcome all obstacles in [sport or contest]!
  8. Drop the bergamot leaves into the candle flame:
  9. May the power of bergamot enhance my success!
  10. Raise energy chanting with the wand in hand:
  11. By root and by herb, by resin and light; the victory I seek shall be mine by right!
  12. This spell is bond and the rhyme complete;
  13. Direct the energy with the wand into the candle flame:
  14. That as this I will, So Mote It Be!
  15. Let the candle burn for one hour, then snuff it.
  16. Scry the wax for signs.
  17. When cooled, bury the remains or dispose of them in the trash.
