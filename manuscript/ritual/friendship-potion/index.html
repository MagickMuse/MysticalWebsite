To encourage new friends to enter your life, help friendships blossom, improve relationships with friends, and encourage a friendlier personality in yourself.

Quantities

Strawberry leaf tea (per the Tea Potion Spell)
Small rose quartz crystal

Directions

  1. Brew strawberry leaf tea according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. Place a small rose quartz crystal in the cup with the tea. Do not ingest the crystal.
  3. When stirring, first stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the shape of <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/1f516dcf7164810bbfc9c25f3d772e44"/>, the rune of joy and fellowship, into the surface of the tea.
  4. As you drink, visualize new friends entering your life, existing friendships deepening, and yourself becoming more open and welcoming. Complete the incantation from the Tea Potion Spell, filling in "friendship" as your intention.
  5. After drinking, wash the stone and reserve it for future use.

This gentle tea potion uses strawberry leaf's sweet energy and rose quartz's loving vibration to draw friendship into your life.
