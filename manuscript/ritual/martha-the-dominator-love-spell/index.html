A traditional love spell invoking Martha the Dominator to keep a desired person's attention focused on the spell caster.

Quantities

  • 1 green or purple candle
  • Martha the Dominator Oil
  • Sharp tool for inscribing

Directions

  1. Dedicate a green or purple candle to Martha the Dominator.
  2. Using a sharp tool, scratch the name of the person whose love you seek into the wax.
  3. Rub the candle with Martha the Dominator Oil, maintaining focus on your desire throughout.
  4. Hold the candle in both hands and chant:
  5. Saint Martha, conquer [desired person's name]
    Keep me in his/her mind.
  6. Repeat the chant a minimum of three times, then light the candle.
