🔥Joss Paper Wishing Spell

◇Tradition
Chinese
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellOffering Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
PetitionSmoke MagickManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Gold-foil joss paper (square sheets featuring gold foil at the center); pen or brush; fire source; (optional) sandalwood essential oil or sandalwood incense
◇Directions
1. Write your wish clearly upon the gold-foil joss paper. 2. If desired, anoint the paper with a few drops of sandalwood essential oil, or burn sandalwood incense alongside it for added power. 3. Burn the paper, if the flame goes out, relight it immediately. 4. Ensure every last letter is reduced to smoke and ash so that the wish fully enters the atmosphere. 5. Take the ashes outside and scatter them to the winds.
◇Description
A Chinese magickal paper wishing spell in which a wish is written on gold-foil joss paper and burned completely to carry the wish into the atmosphere for fulfillment. Sandalwood oil or incense may be added for extra power. The ashes are scattered outdoors to the winds.
