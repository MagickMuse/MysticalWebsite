🌿Five-Herb Abundance Bag

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
PouchCandle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickSalt MagickChant/MantraManifestationLunar
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- Five small dishes, each containing one of dried basil, bay leaves, coriander seeds, cloves, juniper berries, mint, rosemary, sage, or thyme - A drawstring bag - A golden brown candle - A small dish of soil - A small dish of salt
◇Directions
1. Place the seven dishes in a circle with the candle in the center. 2. Light the candle, saying: "I offer light to Mother Earth that she will bring abundance to birth to this home and all who live herein." Add a small quantity of each herb to the bag, naming for each a blessing that your home life brings and one you seek. 3. Add three pinches of salt, saying: "I offer salt to Mother Earth that there will be enough for my/our needs and tranquillity." Sprinkle soil into the bag, saying: "I offer her own to Mother Earth for a safe, peaceful, and happy home." Close the bag, hiding it on the floor behind furniture that is rarely moved. 4. Perform the evening of the new moon.
◇Description
An evening of new moon spell for five-herb abundance bag, mixing five herbs with salt and soil in drawstring bag to bring abundance, tranquillity, and peaceful happy home from Mother Earth. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
