People in Spell-casting

Every spell begins with a person. Without a practitioner's intent and direction, magickal energy simply radiates outward without focus or purpose. Yet some workings draw on more than the energy, desire, and creative force of a single individual.

Working with Partners

Not every spell is performed alone. Healing and cleansing rites, in particular, often need to be carried out on behalf of the person who stands to benefit. Choosing spell partners wisely is essential. Each participant's personal energy flows through the working just as powerfully as any botanical, mineral, or elemental force. It is equally important to exclude anyone whose presence might weaken the outcome, whether through deliberate interference or simple misalignment of intention. Select collaborators whose goals mirror your own and who genuinely want to see your work succeed.

The Power of the Body

A complete, living person is not always necessary for a working. Different parts of the human body are understood to carry their own distinct magickal charge, and just as each serves a unique biological function, each performs a different role in spellwork. In most cases, the body part's power is represented symbolically through an image rather than through the part itself. This is the principle behind the milagro, a small metal charm shaped like a specific piece of anatomy. Eyes, genitals, hands, and hearts are the most frequently depicted, and they appear most often in healing and protective workings.

Personal Concerns

Occasionally a spell calls for something more tangible than a symbolic image. "Part" is not quite the right word, since no pain is involved in obtaining these materials. The body naturally sheds certain substances that retain the essence of the person they came from and maintain an energetic link back to that individual. Manipulating these shed materials gives a practitioner a direct channel to the person who produced them. In Hoodoo tradition, they go by the name personal concerns.

Because personal concerns expose the source individual to magickal influence, they could, in theory, serve hostile aims. In reality, though, harmful use represents only a fraction of how they are actually put to work.
