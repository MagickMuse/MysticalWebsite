🕯️Cutting Ties Cord Burning

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickCord MagickChant/MantraBanishing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A dark blue pillar candle - A length of thin red curtain cord or strong thread - A container of soil
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and say: "By the power of fire, I am set free." Tie a knot in the center of the cord and say: "Tie and wind and seek to find freedom from [name the destructive person or situation]." Hold the knot over the flame with the cord taut in both hands. 2. Say: "The cord between us was long. The cord between us was strong. But now it is decaying, fraying, burning, breaking." Drop both burned halves into the pot of soil, letting the flame burn out, saying: "The link is broken, and I am free." Bury the charred cords where nothing grows, still in the soil from the spell.
◇Description
A Saturday after dark ritual for cutting the ties of a destructive relationship or one going nowhere, using a blue candle and red cord burned over flame to symbolically sever the connection.
