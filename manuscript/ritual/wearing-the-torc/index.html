🔵Wearing the Torc

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Consecrated ItemAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Devotional WorkEmpowermentInvocation
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
A torc
◇Directions
1. Hold your torc in both hands. 2. Feel its weight and the twist of the metal. 3. Acknowledge Brigid as goddess of metalcraft, in whose sacred tradition this metal was made and shaped. 4. Speak your intention: what challenge you are moving into, what project you are committing to, what quality of sovereignty you are claiming today. 5. Name it clearly. 6. Slightly untwist the torc, fit it closely around your neck with the opening at the front, and twist it back into shape. 7. Feel the finials rest on your collarbones at the base of your throat. 8. The fifth chakra, governing communication and expression, opens before you. 9. Your head, the seat of sovereignty, is supported. 10. Know that you are wearing what was worn by kings and queens, warriors and poets, devotees of the gods across millennia. 11. Brigid was invoked in its making. 12. Wear it for as long as your commitment requires; when you remove it, do so consciously, with thanks.
◇Description
A ceremonial putting-on of a Celtic torc as an act of sovereignty and commitment, invoking Brigid as goddess of metalcraft and connecting to the long lineage of those who wore this ancient symbol of strength and nobility.
