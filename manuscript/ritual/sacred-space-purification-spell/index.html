🕯️Blessed Water Recipe
Timing2
🌙Monday☀️Sunday

A waxing to Full Moon purification spell using heated blessed water infused with star anise, bay laurel, and sage in a consecrated vessel. The container is first passed through frankincense smoke and sprinkled with blessed water, then the herbal steam is released to cleanse and protect the space. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 small cook pot or aromatherapy bowl
  • Blessed water
  • 1 star anise
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp sage
  • 1 burner or tealight heat source
  • Frankincense incense

Directions

  1. Consecrate an aromatherapy bowl or small cooking pot by passing it through the smoke of frankincense and sprinkling it with blessed water.
  2. Speak:
  3. I consecrate this container to aid in my Craft, that the spell I now weave will take hold and last.
  4. Heat spring water in the bowl over a tealight or in the pot on a burner.
  5. When the water is hot, add the star anise and speak:
  6. Let all negativity be deflected from this place.
  7. Add the bay leaf and speak:
  8. As the scent of this leaf moves through this place, may purification enter into this space.
  9. Add the sage and speak:
  10. Through sage I now make this spell manifest, that with peace and protection this space will be blessed.
  11. For as I will, So Mote It Be!
  12. Allow the scent to move through the area for an hour, then dispose of the contents and wash out the container.
