🕯️Santa Elena Binding Candle Bowl Love Spell

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Dressed Candle
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Small bowl Spring water Florida Water Honey Sugar 5 cloves 5 cinnamon sticks 5 sprigs of mint A white candle A white ribbon
◇Directions
1. Build an altar or shrine of love. 2. Fill a small bowl halfway with spring water. 3. Add a splash of Florida Water, a tablespoon of honey, sugar, five cloves, five sticks of cinnamon, and five sprigs of mint. 4. Dress a white candle. 5. Tie a white ribbon around this candle and stand it in the center of the bowl. 6. Request that just as the ribbon is tied around the candle, so two people will be tied together; that you be given the opportunity to wrap your arms around your lover like the ribbon wraps around the candle. 7. Ask for the opportunity to burn with passionate happiness. 8. Burn the candle. 9. When it has burned down, add the liquid and contents of the bowl to a bath. 10. Repeat in its entirety for seven consecutive nights, by which time you should see results.
◇Description
A Cuban binding spell invoking Santa Elena. A white candle tied with a white ribbon is stood in a bowl of spring water mixed with Florida Water, honey, sugar, cloves, cinnamon, and mint. The ribbon-around-candle is petitioned to bind two people together in passionate love. The candle is burned and the liquid added to a bath; repeated seven consecutive nights.
