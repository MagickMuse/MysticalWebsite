Smudging, or the act of releasing smoke into the air, is a great way to purify a space. If you've moved into a new property - or want to revitalize the place you've already been in - make these smudge sticks, light them, and send negativity away. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Fresh plants 6 to 10 inches long (sage, rosemary, lavender, or cedar)
  • Cotton string, 5 ft long

Directions

  1. Hold the bundle of your herb so that the cut ends are aligned, and the leafy ends are all together.
  2. Starting at the stems, line the string tightly around the bundle, working your way to the bottom and back up.
  3. As you wrap them, say, Protection and safety, these I call, to protect each room and every Hall.
  4. Protection and safety, always known, keep ill will from the sacred home.
  5. Be sure to leave a bit of extra string so you can hang your smudge sticks up to dry.
  6. When they are completely dried out, late the end and use the smoke to clear a space of negativity.
