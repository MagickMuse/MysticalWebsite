🧿Guard Against Evil Eye Spell

An as sun is setting spell to guard against evil eye, drawing eye on label and sticking to mirror back then sprinkling with salt and olive oil water to reflect back eye of envy and glance of jealousy.

Quantities

  • A blue permanent marker
  • A small pressure-sensitive label
  • A double-sided small reflective mirror or an object with a shiny surface on both sides
  • An orange candle
  • A small dish of water containing a few grains of salt and a drop of olive oil

Directions

  1. Draw on the label an eye shape and stick the label onto the back of the mirror or shiny object.
  2. Light the candle and hold the mirror so you can see the candle in it, then flip it over so the candlelight illuminates the eye.
  3. Say: "[Name], your eye of envy, your glance of jealousy, reflected back shall be." Sprinkle the unmarked side of the mirror or object with a little salt-and-oil water, and as you do, repeat the chant.
  4. Rub the mirror clean and keep the mirror positioned so when the person causing you problems approaches your home or workspace, the eye is facing you and the shiny surface is facing outward.
  5. Repeat the spell words silently when you meet the jealous person and avoid eye contact.
  6. Perform as the sun is setting.
