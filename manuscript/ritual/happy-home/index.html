Motherwort

A simple sympathetic working that places dried motherwort in a jar surrounded by family photographs, creating a focal point for household happiness and family harmony.

Quantities

Dried motherwort (enough to fill a jar)
1 jar
Family photographs (as many as desired)

Directions

  1. Place dried motherwort in a jar.
  2. Surround the jar with family photographs to link its harmonizing energy to the household's specific members.
