A Romany fertility potion meant to be shared by both prospective parents in the same glass, in bed, immediately before lovemaking. Timed to align with the New or Full Moon according to which phase best suits the couple's reproductive cycles. Repeated for three consecutive nights for full effect.

⚠️ Comfrey is not recommended for internal use by pregnant women. Use this potion only while actively trying to conceive. One of the Fertility Potion methods.

Quantities

  • Spring water
  • Comfrey (for the infusion)
  • Brandy (equal parts to the infusion)
  • A single shared glass

Directions

  1. Prepare a comfrey infusion using spring water.
  2. Combine with an equal volume of brandy.
  3. Pour into one glass and drink it together in bed.
  4. Make love immediately after.
  5. Repeat for three consecutive nights.
    If no result occurs, wait until the same moon phase in the following month before trying again.
