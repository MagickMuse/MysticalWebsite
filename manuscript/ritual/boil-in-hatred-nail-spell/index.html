⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A boiling curse using a nail and water gathered from a road, combined with an article of the target's clothing. The ingredients are boiled in a pot while reciting an incantation to brew discord, malice, misfortune, and pain into the target. The mixture is bottled and buried at night on the target's property, at a crossroads, or far from where it was made.

Quantities

1 nail (found on a road)
Road water
1 article of clothing from the target
1 large pot
1 bottle

Directions

  1. Gather a nail from a road and collect a puddle of water from a road as well.
  2. Take an article of clothing from the target and place all three into a large pot.
  3. Bring the contents to a boil and while stirring, recite:
  4. It is not the nail I brew
    Nor the clothing in the water too
    But discord and malice,
    misfortune and pain
    That as they boil they will
    boil in you [name]!
  5. Pour everything into a bottle and bury it at night on the property of the target, at a crossroads, or far away from the house where it was made.
