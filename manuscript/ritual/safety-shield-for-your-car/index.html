🛡️Safety Shield for Your Car

◇Form
SigilTalisman
◇Method
VisualizationProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 white paper square (4 inches) - Colored markers - Amber essential oil - Tape, glue, or other adhesive
◇Directions
1. Cast a circle around the area where you will be working. 2. Cut a 4-inch square of paper. 3. Within this square, draw a circle. 4. Write the word "safe" or an affirmation of your choice in the center of the circle. 5. If you wish, add other symbols that represent safety (such as a pentagram) and travel to you. 6. The pattern you have created is your safety shield. 7. Dot the four corners of the paper with amber essential oil. 8. Open the circle. 9. Attach the shield to the dashboard or window of your car. 10. Each time you get into your car, look at the shield and touch it to activate its protective energies. 11. Visualize yourself and your car surrounded by pure white light, and know that you are protected wherever you go.
◇Description
A protection spell for your car using a paper shield inscribed with symbols and dotted with amber essential oil. Creates a talisman attached to dashboard or window that activates through touch and visualization of white light. Protects during daily driving or long-distance travel.
