A devotional petition to Mary Magdalene for a happy pregnancy and safe delivery, made with offerings of myrrh, roses, and spikenard.

Quantities

  • A votive image of Mary Magdalene, ideally shown with the moon or a closed box
  • Myrrh
  • Roses
  • Spikenard

Directions

  1. Display a votive image of Mary Magdalene, favoring one that shows her with the moon or a closed box for extra potency in this working.
  2. Offer myrrh, roses, and spikenard before the image, and request her blessing for an easy pregnancy and smooth delivery.
  3. Requests are welcome anytime but are considered especially favored on her feast day, July 22nd.
