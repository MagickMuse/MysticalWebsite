A candlelit mirror meditation to illuminate your inner light and reflect it outward with intention. Anoint the mirror with Moon Water, breathe in the moon's power, and visualize your inner flame expanding through your body and aura until you feel fully charged. Close with a spritz of rosewater or Moon Water. Adaptable to Leo, Scorpio, Libra, or Aries moon energies for confidence, truth, beauty, or courage respectively.

Quantities

  • Mirror
  • Moon Water
  • Candle
  • Matches or lighter
  • Rosewater or Moon Water spray (optional)

Directions

  1. Sit comfortably before the mirror.
  2. Dip your fingers in Moon Water and anoint the mirror's perimeter, tracing it like a frame of intention.
  3. Close your eyes.
  4. Take three slow, deep breaths.
  5. As you breathe, feel the moon above you, pulling gently at your heart.
  6. Somewhere inside your chest, visualize a small steady flame, your own inner light, quietly burning.
  7. The moon sees it.
  8. The moon feeds it.
  9. Open your eyes.
  10. Place a candle between yourself and the mirror and light it.
  11. Soften your gaze.
  12. Watch how the mirror catches the candlelight and throws it back into the room, doubling and magnifying it. Let that observation settle into you: the mirror doesn't dim the light, it amplifies it. Sit with these questions:
  13. What parts of yourself are already shining outward?
  14. What parts are still hidden?
  15. What do you want the world to see when it looks at you?
  16. Now look at yourself in the mirror.
  17. Visualize your inner flame growing stronger with each breath, breathing in the moon's ethereal light as fuel, breathing out anything dim or contracted.
  18. The light expands from your chest, through your body, past your skin, into the space around you.
  19. If you feel called, use your hands to draw the energy outward from your center, shaping it into your aura.
  20. When you feel fully aglow, pause and let the mirror reflect your radiance back at you.
  21. Receive it.
  22. When complete, spritz rosewater or Moon Water around your body and the space to seal the energy.
  23. Extinguish the candle.
  24. Repeat as often as feels right, daily during a Leo moon is especially potent.
  25. ---
  26. Moon variations:
  27. Leo moon, focus on inner warmth, confidence, and radiance
  28. Scorpio moon, replace the mirror with a bowl of Moon Water and scry into your ego and deeper truth
  29. Libra moon, orient the meditation toward inner beauty and what you find loveable in yourself
  30. Aries moon, reflect courage back to yourself; use the visualization to build the will to take action
