🩹Lime Trauma Poppet Working

A healing poppet working using lime peel and black fabric to address and release trauma stored in the body and spirit.

Quantities

  • Black fabric (enough for a small poppet)
  • Stuffing material
  • Dried lime peel
  • Thread and needle
  • Personal concerns (optional)

Directions

  1. Create a poppet from black fabric to represent yourself or the person needing healing.
  2. Black absorbs and transmutes negative energy, making it ideal for shadow work and trauma release.
  3. Stuff the poppet with filling and dried lime peel.
  4. Lime's cleansing and healing properties work to purify the trauma held within.
  5. If desired, add personal concerns to strengthen the link.
  6. As you sew the poppet closed, speak healing intentions over it.
  7. Work with the poppet over time, holding it during meditation and visualizing the lime drawing out and neutralizing stored trauma.
  8. When the working is complete, you may bury the poppet with gratitude or keep it as a healing talisman.

Source

Lime, The Magick Muse
