A large-scale healing and cleansing ritual to Omolu (Babalu-Ayé) that directs prevention or healing toward an entire area, all those within it and all who approach. Vast quantities of doboru (sacred popcorn) are popped and used to fill or cover the space, left overnight, then disposed of on naked Earth. For an entire building, the roof is covered and birds and nature handle disposal.

Quantities

Vast quantities of doboru (sacred popcorn, see Doboru Preparation ritual)

Directions

  1. Pop vast quantities of doboru.
  2. Cover whatever needs to be cleansed with Omolu's flowers.
  3. For a room, fill the room, let it rest overnight, then dispose of the doboru on naked Earth.
  4. For an entire building, cover the roof with doboru and let birds and Mother Nature take care of disposal.
