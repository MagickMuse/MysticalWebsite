A waxing to Full Moon spell for drawing wealth and success. Herbs are mixed in a bowl with the athame and consecrated through the four Elementals, then a green votive is anointed with bergamot oil and inscribed with the runes Berkanan, Ansuz, Tiwaz, Fehu, Othala, and Dagaz. The candle is set in the cauldron on the pentacle, the herbs are dropped into the flame or a simmering pot pourri, and the wand is moved widdershins three times to release the spell.

Quantities

  • Patchouli incense
  • 1 green votive candle
  • 1 cauldron
  • 1 blue candle
  • 1 white candle
  • 1 orange candle
  • 1 wand
  • 1 athame
  • 1 bowl
  • Pinch each: allspice, bergamot, comfrey, chamomile, cinquefoil, whole cloves, mint, marjoram
  • 1 nutmeg (crushed)
  • Blessed water
  • Salt
  • Optional: simmering pot pourri with water

Directions

  1. Empower the herbs by mixing them in a bowl with the athame, then pass the bowl through the symbols of the four Elementals and speak:
  2. I call upon thee Elementals to charge these herbs and bring forth their powers to work with me in the spell I cast!
  3. Be thou charged by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt], by Elemental Air [pass through incense smoke], by Elemental Fire [pass through center candle flame], and by Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed or spring water].
  4. Set the cauldron or pot pourri on the pentacle.
  5. Rub the green candle with bergamot oil and inscribe with the runes Berkanan, Ansuz, Tiwaz, Fehu, Othala, and Dagaz.
  6. Drop the herbs into the candle flame or simmering pot pourri and speak:
  7. With the power of Air is the spell carried; with the power of Fire is the magick released; with the power of Water is the will spread [sprinkle water]; and with the power of Earth is the goal brought into being [add athame tip of salt].
  8. Move the wand widdershins three times over the votive or pot and speak:
  9. As this spell spreads through the air; nothing may my work impair!
  10. Bring success and wealth to me; for as this I spell, So Mote It Be!
  11. Let burn one hour, then snuff.
  12. Scry for signs.
  13. Bury the candle wax or dispose of it in the trash.
  14. If a pot pourri was used, wash out the container.
