🌊Before Sunrise at the Shore

A Moroccan rite performed exactly forty days after moving into a new home. Arriving at the ocean before sunrise and unseen, the practitioner offers a loaf of bread to the sea, receives seven waves, and petitions the presiding sea spirits for children and freedom from spirit obstruction.

Quantities

1 loaf of bread
Access to the ocean
40 days elapsed since moving into a new home

Directions

  1. Forty days after moving into a new home, set out for the ocean before sunrise.
  2. Travel and arrive without being noticed or recognized by anyone; draw no attention to yourself.
  3. Reaching the shore, throw a loaf of bread into the sea.
  4. Allow seven waves to pass over your body.
  5. Then address the presiding spirits of the sea:
  6. Oh my uncle, the sea.
  7. I'm troubled with spirits.
  8. Give me children and health.
  9. When ready, turn and walk home without looking back.

Moving into a new home is considered a spiritually vulnerable transition. Old obstructions from previous occupants or unresolved spirit entanglements can linger. This Moroccan rite uses the move itself as a ritual reset point, with the forty-day mark serving as the threshold between old life and new.

The spell appeals to the legendary King of the Sea. Any marine spirit meaningful to the practitioner may be petitioned in the incantation instead.
