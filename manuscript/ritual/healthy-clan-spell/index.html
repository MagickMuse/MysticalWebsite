The Healthy Clan Spell uses the steady, enduring burn of a pillar candle to send healing energy to your entire circle of loved ones. Unlike spells that target one person's ailment, this working creates a protective field of wellness around your whole "clan," however you define it.

Why a Pillar Candle?

Pillar candles burn slowly over many hours or even days, making them ideal for sustained magical work. The gradual release of energy mirrors the way health is maintained: not in one dramatic burst, but through consistent, ongoing support.

Choosing Your Candle

Select a green pillar candle. Green corresponds to healing, growth, and vitality. The size of the candle determines how long the spell will work. A small pillar might burn for a few hours; a large one can last for days, extending the blessing.

Preparing the Candle

Using a pin, needle, or small carving tool, inscribe the names of everyone you wish to include in the spell around the candle. Write from bottom to top, symbolizing rising health and energy. You may use full names, nicknames, or even symbols that represent each person.

If your clan is large, you may write "my family," "my coven," "my household," or another collective term, holding each individual in your mind as you carve.

Anointing with Olive Oil

Olive oil has been used for blessing and healing since ancient times. Pour a small amount into your palm. Rub the oil onto the candle, stroking from the middle outward toward both ends. As you anoint, say:

With oil of olive, ancient and pure,

I bless this candle, healing secure.

For [name each person or say "my clan"],

Health and wholeness, this is my plan.

The Working

Place the candle in a safe holder where it can burn undisturbed. Light it during daylight hours to draw on solar energy, preferably when the sun is strong.

As the flame catches, visualize golden sunlight pouring down into the candle, filling it with warmth. See that light radiating outward to touch each person whose name is carved there. Say:

Sun above, lend your might.

Fill this flame with healing light.

To my clan, both near and far,

Health and strength, beneath your star.

Allow the candle to burn as long as safely possible. If you must extinguish it, snuff rather than blow, and relight when you can, repeating the final incantation each time.

Completion

When the candle has fully burned, gather any remaining wax and bury it in your yard or a potted plant, returning the energy to the earth with thanks.
