🔮Baneful Magick🔮Negative Magick

A technique note explaining how any curse or hex can be adapted to target a situation or bad habit rather than a person. The target's name is simply replaced with the name of the habit or situation.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Any curse or hex may be adapted to target a situation or bad habit instead of a person.
  2. Simply replace the target's name with the name of the habit or situation.
  3. For example, rather than cursing a person named John Doe, curse "Smoking" or "Marital Problems."

Technique

Any curse or hex may be adapted to target a situation or bad habit rather than a person. Simply replace the target's name with the name of the habit or situation.

Examples

  • Instead of cursing "John Doe," curse Smoking
  • Instead of cursing a person, curse Marital Problems, Debt, Insomnia, etc.

This technique can be applied to any of the curses in this grimoire.
