A comprehensive framework for weather working and storm raising. Draws on the doctrine of the four elements to produce specific atmospheric effects: wind/gale (Mercury, Wednesday), destructive storms (Thursday, waning moon), electrical storms (Cernunnos, fire, Tuesday, waning moon), and rain/mist/cloud (Habondia, Tiamat, Moon, Monday). Cloud-splitting may also be performed impromptu using pyramid powers of will and visualization alone.

Quantities

  • WIND WORKING:
  • Wand (emblem of airy power)
  • Mercurial incense
  • Square of Mercury (optional attunement)
    ELECTRICAL STORM:
  • Athame or sword (emblem of fiery power)
  • Incense of Wrath
    RAIN / MIST / CLOUD:
  • Chalice of salt water (emblem of watery power)
  • Lunar incense

Directions

CLOUD SPLITTING (impromptu, no circle required): Focus the pyramid powers of will and imagination on the cloud mass. Visualize it splitting and parting; harry it with concentrated will and faith until it dissolves. This is a binding applied to a natural phenomenon.

FOR A BENEVOLENT WIND (zephyr): Cast a properly prepared circle with Mercurial incense. Invoke Herne and the Kerubic Eagle from the Watchtower of the East. Wield the wand as the emblem of airy power. Call up stormy, wind-swept memories and augment them with a rhyming spell of your own composition. Timing: Wednesday, waxing moon, at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM.

FOR A DESTRUCTIVE STORM: Same working as above but with destructive intent. Timing: Thursday, waning moon, same hours.

FOR AN ELECTRICAL STORM: Cast the circle. Invoke Cernunnos and all powers of fire. Call the Great Lion from the Watchtower of the South. Brandish the athame or sword as the emblem of fiery power. Burn Incense of Wrath. Invoke Thunor and all storm gods. Timing: Tuesday, waning moon.

FOR RAIN, MIST, OR CLOUD: Cast the circle. Invoke Lady Habondia in her aspect as Ruler of the Great Waters and Tiamat from the Watchtower of the West. Use the chalice of salt water as the magickal weapon of invocation. Burn lunar incense. Timing: Monday, at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM.

NOTE: The operator must be possessed by a wave of godhead throughout, using activated archetypal elemental images as the triggering device. Begin experimenting at seasons when the desired elemental activity would not be unusual. Work toward the improbable before attempting the impossible.
