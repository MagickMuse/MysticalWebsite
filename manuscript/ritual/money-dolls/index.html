🪆Money Dolls

Despite the stereotypical "voodoo doll," dolls are most frequently used in spells as magickal helpers. Money dolls magickally generate wealth and financial inspiration.

Quantities

Green flannel (enough to cut a doll shape)
Dillweed
Irish moss
Needle and thread

Directions

  1. Cut out the shape you desire from green flannel, a person, elephant, leprechaun, mermaid, or whatever you envision.
  2. Stuff it with dillweed and Irish moss, then sew it up and embellish as desired.
  3. Before you go to sleep, talk to the doll, tell it your troubles and request financial advice.
  4. Pay attention to your dreams.
