🍾Broken Mirror Vengeance Bottle Hex

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
BindingBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
1 bottle Shards of broken mirror Pins Needles Coffin nails Your own urine Bat's Blood Ink 1 slip of brown paper Wax from a purple or black candle
◇Directions
1. Fill a bottle with shards of broken mirror, pins, needles, coffin nails, and your own urine. 2. Using Bat's Blood Ink, write the target's name on a slip of brown paper and put it in the bottle. 3. Seal the bottle with wax from a purple or black candle. 4. Bury it as close to your target's front door as you dare.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A vengeance witch bottle filled with broken mirror shards, sharp implements, coffin nails, and urine, with the target's name written in Bat's Blood Ink on brown paper, sealed with candle wax and buried near the target's front door.
