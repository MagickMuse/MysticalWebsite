A New Orleans-style protection spell: fennel seeds (or a fennel and Grains of Paradise blend) are sewn into small bags and attached to the back of Archangel Michael images posted at the front and back doors of the home. An image of El Niño Fidencio may be used instead for immigration-specific protection.

Quantities

  • Fennel seeds
  • Grains of Paradise (optional, for a blended version)
  • 2 small cloth or paper bags/pouches
  • 2 images of Archangel Michael (or El Niño Fidencio for immigration protection)

Directions

  1. Fill two small bags with fennel seeds alone, or a blend of fennel and Grains of Paradise.
  2. Attach one bag to the back of an image of Archangel Michael; post this image at the front door.
  3. Attach the second bag to a second image of Archangel Michael; post this at the back door.
  4. For immigration-specific protection, substitute images of El Niño Fidencio for Archangel Michael.

A classic New Orleans-style protection working that combines sacred imagery with botanical power in a single, continuous ward posted at both entrances to the home.

  1. Prepare two small bags. Use cloth pouches or paper bags. Fill each with:
    • Fennel seeds alone, or
    • A blend of fennel seeds and Grains of Paradise
  2. Attach one filled bag to the back of an image of Archangel Michael. Post this image at your front door.
  3. Attach the second bag to the back of a second image of Archangel Michael. Post it at your back door.

The image provides the spiritual protection; the botanical packet amplifies and directs that energy toward keeping authorities at bay.

Substitutions

  • If your concern is specifically immigration authorities, substitute images of El Niño Fidencio for Archangel Michael at either or both doors. El Niño Fidencio has become a specialist in immigration matters and is particularly responsive to those needs.
  • Plastic bags are technically functional but have the practical disadvantage of making the contents visible. Cloth or paper is preferred.
