Samhain

A farewell prayer to the old year as the balefire flickers out in the between-hour. Bids goodbye to trouble and joy alike, both ending in mystery and dying flames as the new year awaits.

Quantities

  • No materials required - spoken invocation only

Directions

  1. This holiday represents the new year running into the old one.
  2. It is a time of divination in part because the magick of the Samhain season makes it an excellent time to do a few workings to sweeten the future ahead.
  3. Bless the land, bless the family, bless yourself, and bless the year ahead.
  4. The balefire flickers out
    as the time on the old year ends;
    it's the between-hour, without a doubt,
    perilous moments 'til we light it again.
    Farewell to the trouble,
    farewell to the joy,
    it all ends just the same:
    with mystery shrouded in stories forgot
    in the dying of the flames.
