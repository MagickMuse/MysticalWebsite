🧪Pleasant Dreams Potion

Tea potions to encourage restful sleep and pleasant dreams. Three variations are provided. These potions are best drunk at bedtime.

Quantities

  • Chamomile tea
  • Spearmint tea
  • Optional: a pale blue cup
    OR
  • Chamomile tea
  • Valerian tea
  • Optional: a pale blue cup
    OR
  • Spearmint tea
  • Valerian tea
  • Optional: a pale blue cup

Directions

  1. Prepare the tea according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. For the first variation, combine chamomile tea with spearmint tea.
  3. For the second variation, combine chamomile tea with valerian tea.
  4. For the third variation, combine spearmint tea with valerian tea.
  5. You may optionally use a pale blue cup to enhance the dreaming energy.
  6. As you stir, fill in the word or phrase that sums up your desire, such as "peace" or "good health." Drink at bedtime.
