A devotional legal appeal to the Holy Child of Atocha (or Elegba) for the prevention and delay of lawsuits. Image placed facing the front door, offerings of a golden candle, candy, and rum. Best performed on Monday.

Quantities

  • 1 image of the Holy Child of Atocha (or Elegba's concrete head with cowrie shell eyes)
  • 1 golden (yellow) candle
  • Candy (generous amount)
  • Rum
  • Fine cigar (for Elegba variant, in place of candy)

Directions

  1. Place an image of the Holy Child of Atocha facing your front door, or positioned just behind it, to strengthen his vigilance over your home and legal situation.
  2. Offer him a golden candle, generous amounts of candy, and rum (acknowledging that he may not be a child).
  3. Monday is the most effective day to make this appeal.
  4. If you prefer to work directly with Elegba rather than the syncretic Catholic image, use a concrete head with cowrie shell eyes and ears; offer rum and a fine cigar in place of the candy.
⚠️
Syncretism Notice: This spell sits at the crossroads of Catholic folk saint veneration and Yoruba/African diaspora spiritual traditions. Approach with research and respect for both lineages.

The Holy Child of Atocha is a beloved folk saint whose specialty is the prevention and delay of lawsuits. His origins lie in a legend from Atocha, Spain: during the wars between Christians and Moors, imprisoned Christians were kept alive by the mysterious daily appearance of a beautiful small boy bearing food and water. Many believed him to be the Christ Child himself.

This devotion spread from Spain through Latin America and into African diaspora spiritual traditions. Whether the figure being petitioned is the Christ Child or Elegba — the West African trickster master of crossroads — wearing a Catholic mask is a question the tradition itself leaves open. In either form, the appeal is heard.

The Working

  1. Place an image of the Holy Child of Atocha facing your front door — or just behind it — to heighten his powers of vigilance over your legal situation.
  2. Offer him a golden candle, generous amounts of candy, and rum (offered with the understanding that he may not be a child).
  3. The most effective day to make this appeal is Monday.

Working Directly with Elegba

If the syncretic Catholic imagery does not feel right for your practice, spiritual and magickal supply stores carry images of Elegba in the form of a concrete head with cowrie shell eyes and ears. Arrange this head as described above.

  • Offer rum
  • Offer a fine cigar in place of candy
  • The request and intention remain the same
