🔮Brigid Candle Divination

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
DivinationCandle MagickInvocation
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Small white taper candle (avoid dripless; birthday candle works) - White card stock or index card - Permanent marker or pen - Heat‑safe, fireproof surface (tile, brick, concrete, or old ceramic plate) - A specific, well‑framed question and up to four possible answers - Lighter or matches
◇Directions
1. Prep the question and answers: - Frame a clear, specific question (e.g., “Will it rain tomorrow?” not “What will the weather be?”). - Limit answers to ≤4 for easy reading; include “Other” if outcomes may fall outside choices. - On the card, draw equal connected sections from a common center (two = a single dividing line; four = a cross). Write one answer in each section. 2. Mount the candle: - Drip a drop or two of wax at the exact card center. Press the candle base into the warm wax to glue it upright. Let set. 3. Set space and invoke Brigid: - Place the card on a fireproof surface in a draft‑free spot. Center yourself. - Say: Goddess Brigid, Goddess of Flame, Healer, and Keeper of the Sacred Well, I ask that you honor me with your presence today and show me the answer to my question. - Speak your question aloud. Light the candle with matches or a lighter (do not use another candle). 4. Observe and allow to burn out: - Gaze at the flame, holding the question in mind. Then drop your eyes to the candle and watch wax paths. - Let the candle burn to completion for accurate results. Never leave unattended. If scorching begins, extinguish immediately. - If you must pause, leave everything in place and relight as soon as possible. 5. Read the wax: - After cooling, remove any remaining nub without disturbing pools. - The section with the most wax indicates the answer. If wax pooled across multiple sections, choose the one with the greatest amount. - If two are very close, score along the divider and peel each pool to compare (or weigh). Near‑ties can indicate shared truth. 6. Notes: - Keep the room still to prevent drafts from biasing results. - You can reuse the answer card with the same set of choices for future questions.
◇Description
A simple Imbolc divination using a small white candle fixed to a marked card to let wax decide among clearly defined answers. Ideal as an approachable, multiple‑choice style scry for beginners.
