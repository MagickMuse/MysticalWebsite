🌰Jupiter's Walnut Offering

A passive fertility spell using walnuts and chestnuts, both sacred to Jupiter, displayed in bowls to radiate and transfer fertility energy. Particularly beneficial for men's fertility. The Latin name for walnut, juglans, references Jupiter's testicles.

Quantities

Walnuts
Chestnuts
One or more bowls for display

Directions

  1. Place bowls of walnuts and chestnuts in plain sight throughout the living space so they continually radiate fertility energy into the environment.
  2. Eat from the bowls as desired.
  3. Replenish with fresh nuts as they are depleted or age.

The Latin name for walnut, juglans, references Jupiter's testicles. Walnuts and chestnuts are particularly potent for men's fertility workings.

Nuts and seeds broadly represent the force of germination. Oak galls, known as serpent eggs, may also be transformed into fertility charms and amulets.

Acorns gathered at night carry the strongest fertility power. The Full Moon amplifies this. Look for an oak with particularly abundant acorns when gathering.
