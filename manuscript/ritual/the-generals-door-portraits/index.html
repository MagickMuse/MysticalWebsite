Portraits of the generals Tu-ch'ih and Ch'in Ch'iung in full military dress, posted side by side on the main entrance to guard the home from all danger, especially malevolent ghosts and spirits.

Quantities

Pictures of the generals Tu-ch'ih and Ch'in Ch'iung
Red paper slips
Pen

Directions

  1. Fix the generals' pictures side by side on the main entrance.
  2. Write your wishes, desires and instructions for the generals on slips of red paper.
  3. Fix the slips to the door as well.

The Legend of the Generals

Depending on the telling, the generals Tu-ch'ih and Ch'in Ch'iung protected the second Tang emperor either from a plague of ghosts or from a seductive and deadly fox spirit. They stood guard all night so the emperor could sleep, and they succeeded. After several days, though, the generals badly needed sleep themselves, so portraits of them in full armor were tried in their place. The portraits worked just as well, and they have served as door guardians ever since.
