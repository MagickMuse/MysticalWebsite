A general incense working using equal parts frankincense, myrrh, and copal, the three resins of the Magi, burned on charcoal throughout travel preparation while the moon is in Sagittarius. Ancestors who were pilgrims, nomads, and immigrants are petitioned for road protection.

Quantities

Equal parts frankincense, myrrh, and copal
Charcoal
Optional: small travel objects, such as a passport, tickets, and maps

Directions

  1. Grind together equal parts frankincense, myrrh, and copal, ideally while the moon is in Sagittarius.
  2. Whenever you are preparing for an upcoming trip, looking at flight prices, planning your itinerary, packing your bags, burn this incense blend on a piece of charcoal.
  3. As the smoke rises, pray to the three kings or any of your ancestors who were pilgrims, nomads, and immigrants to protect you on the road.
  4. You may pass small objects such as your passport, tickets, and maps through the smoke to bless them.
