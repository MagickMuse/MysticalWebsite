🧖Beltane Beauty Facial Ice Cubes

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Water/Spray
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickLunar
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 cup Moon Water (made at the spring full moon) - 2 tablespoons fresh or dried rose petals - 1 tablespoon dried lemon balm (optional) - Cucumber slices - Aloe leaf (optional) - Ice cube tray For Makeup-Setting Spray variation: - Spray bottle - Glycerin (optional, as preservative)
◇Directions
1. Bring your Moon Water to a boil. 2. While it heats, place the rose petals and lemon balm (if using) into a heat-safe container with a pouring lip, such as a Pyrex measuring cup. 3. Pour the boiling water over the botanicals and allow them to steep and cool to room temperature. 4. Once cooled, add cucumber slices and scoop in the gel from an aloe leaf if using. 5. Transfer to the refrigerator and let the blend infuse overnight. 6. The next day, strain out and discard the cucumber slices. 7. Pour the fragrant, moon-blessed water into an ice cube tray and place in the freezer. 8. Once fully frozen, the cubes are ready. 9. To use, massage a cube gently across clean skin; the chill is bracing but quickly gives way to a cool, refreshing sensation. 10. Follow immediately with your moisturizer to seal in the hydration. 11. For a makeup-setting spray, skip the freezing step entirely. 12. After the overnight infusion, strain the water and pour it into a spray bottle. 13. Mist over your face as a setting spray or light facial refresher. 14. Keeps in the refrigerator for about one week; add a small amount of glycerin to extend its shelf life.
◇Description
Moon Water ice cubes infused with rose petals, lemon balm, cucumber, and aloe for a cooling, beautifying skin ritual timed to the spring full moon. Massage across skin to rejuvenate and enchant after a moon ritual or during Beltane season. Can also be left unfrozen as a lunar facial mist or makeup-setting spray. Use organic ingredients throughout.
