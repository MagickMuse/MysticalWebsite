😌Keep from Worrying So Much

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualizationBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A purple and a white candle, set side by side - A dark stone in front of the purple candle and a white stone in front of the white candle
◇Directions
1. Light the purple candle, saying: "Worry, worry, worry, you tangle inside me, fret, fret, fret, yet I shall be free." Hold the dark stone in front of the purple candle, saying: "Worry, I have no more of you, you and I, fear, now are through." Extinguish the purple candle and put the dark stone just outside the door. 2. Light the white candle and hold the white stone in front of it, saying: "Here is peace and harmony, happiness, and tranquillity." Let the white candle burn through, afterwards putting the white stone just outside the door. 3. When you return home, hold the dark stone, putting worries in it, then pick up the white stone and receive harmony. 4. Perform Saturday evening.
◇Description
A Saturday evening spell for second ritual to keep from worrying so much, lighting purple candle with dark stone then extinguishing and lighting white candle with white stone for peace, harmony, happiness, and tranquillity.
