🌟Polaris Lonely Far from Roots

◇Form
Altar WorkingCharm
◇Method
Incense MagickSmoke MagickChant/MantraPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A lavender, lilac, or any floral incense stick - A small star charm or pendant - A photo of the people you miss
◇Directions
1. Identify the Pole Star (using an online star map, if necessary) and light the incense, saying: "Star, I call you far from home, this night I feel so alone. Carry my wishes across land or sea, that I may feel my loved ones, [name], close to me." Holding the star symbol in your nondominant hand, write in incense smoke in the air over it: STAR, GUIDE ME HOME WHEN RIGHT TO BE, OR LET MY LOVED ONES COME TO ME. 2. Place the star symbol on top of the photo. 3. In incense smoke, draw a star in smoke around them, an inch above, sending silent messages of love. 4. Let the incense burn and take the photo and the symbol indoors. 5. Make contact with your loved ones. 6. Whenever you miss them, wear the star pendant or carry the charm. 7. Perform after dark.
◇Description
An after dark spell for Pole Star/Polaris ritual for those in northern hemisphere when you are lonely and far from your roots, writing in incense smoke over star charm with photo to guide home or let loved ones come to you.
