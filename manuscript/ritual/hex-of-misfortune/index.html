🩸Hex of Misfortune

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellCandle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraBlood Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 piece of parchment paper Black ink 1 black candle
◇Directions
1. Write the target's name nine times on a piece of parchment paper using black ink. 2. Prick your finger and place a single drop of blood onto the paper. 3. Light a black candle and recite the incantation aloud, inserting the target's name where indicated. 4. When the incantation is complete, place the paper into the candle's flame and let it burn entirely.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A blood-sealed hex of misfortune. The target's name is written nine times in black ink on parchment, anointed with a drop of the caster's blood, and burned in a black candle's flame while reciting an incantation that steals the target's joy and life force.
