🍞Babalu-Ayé Skin Healing Bread Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar WorkingOffering Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
InvocationPetitionHealing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Closed Practice
Santería/Lucumí
◇Quantities
Glass of dry white wine Raffia cloth Bowl of milk Two slices of bread (soaked in milk) One additional slice of white bread White candle Palm oil
◇Directions
1. Place a glass of dry white wine on a raffia cloth together with a bowl of milk in which you've soaked two slices of bread. 2. Place an additional piece of white bread on this altar. 3. Light a white candle and petition Babalu-Ayé to remove your affliction. 4. Leave everything overnight. 5. The next morning, dispose of everything but that additional slice of bread. 6. Soak that bread in palm oil and use it to cleanse the afflicted skin, then dispose of the bread outside the home.
◇Description
Babalu-Ayé, Father of Smallpox, is petitioned to heal any skin disorder. Bread soaked in milk is offered alongside dry white wine on a raffia cloth with a white candle. The following morning, the remaining slice of bread is soaked in palm oil and used to cleanse the afflicted skin.
