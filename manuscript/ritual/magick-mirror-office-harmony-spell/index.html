🪞Magick Mirror Office Harmony Spell

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Mirror MagickSolarCleansingProtectionBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Small round mirror (palm-sized) - Dark fabric (to wrap mirror)
◇Directions
1. On a bright, sunny day, bring a small round mirror to the workplace. 2. Slowly and discreetly circulate throughout the room, reflecting everything, including individuals if necessary, for at least several seconds each. 3. The mirror absorbs the negative energy. 4. When finished, face the mirror downwards or wrap it in dark fabric. 5. When you can, take the mirror outside and hold it up to the sun for no longer than nine seconds. 6. The negativity is burned away and replaced by positive solar energy.
◇Description
A small hand mirror is carried through the workplace to collect and vacuum away malevolent energy, jealousy, and backstabbing. The mirror stores the negative reflections; then outdoors it is held up to the sun for no longer than nine seconds, burning away the negativity and replacing it with positive solar energy. A Chinese-derived spell understood through the lens of Sekhmet, who both heals and destroys with solar rays. Perform only on a bright, sunny day. Hold mirror to sun for no longer than nine seconds.
