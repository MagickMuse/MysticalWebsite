🥣Bowl🥚Egg

A two-person egg cleansing in which the egg absorbs negative energy from the subject's body. The broken egg is then examined for diagnostic information: blood or abnormality signals the need for further, stronger cleansing work. Fertilised eggs are preferred so that the possibility of blood can be meaningful as a sign. One of the Egg Cleansing methods.

Quantities

1 whole raw egg (fertilised preferred)
1 dish for breaking

Directions

  1. One person passes an egg entirely over the body of the person being cleansed.
  2. The person being cleansed exhales onto the egg.
  3. Break the egg into a dish and examine it carefully; if blood or any abnormality is present, further and stronger cleansing action is needed, while a normal yolk free from blood indicates the cleansing is considered complete.
  4. Fertilised eggs are theoretically preferred so that the presence of blood carries diagnostic weight.
  5. If blood appears in a regular supermarket egg, this is an extremely powerful indication that further cleansing is required.
