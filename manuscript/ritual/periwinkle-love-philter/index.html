💜Periwinkle Love Philter

◇Tradition
Folk MagickCeremonial
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
ElixirAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickInvocationChant/MantraWater Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Dried periwinkle leaves (Vinca major or minor) A Mercury-ruled herb (any herb under Mercury's rulership) Cinquefoil Dried vervain Dried rose petals Pure distilled water (in the chalice)
◇Directions
1. Observe the correct time: Friday at 8am, 3pm, or 10pm during a waxing moon. Prepare the place of working exactly as for the Coriander Love Philter. Invoke Habondia. 2. Place the dried periwinkle leaves, Mercury herb, cinquefoil, vervain, and rose petals in the mortar. Grind to a fine powder. 3. As you grind, repeat continuously a rhyme stating your intent, such as: By this act I draw [name] and [name] into a bond of love and desire. 4. Take two very small pinches of the resultant powder and mix into the distilled water in the chalice. Charge it with the full force of your crystallized desire. 5. Seal with the words: So mote it be. Draw the seal of the triple cross in the air above the chalice with the right forefinger. 6. Leave to steep for twelve hours. Strain through fine muslin or cheesecloth. 7. Introduce a few drops secretly into the food or drink of those named in the charm.
◇Description
A powdered love philter ground from five botanicals, charged with intent while chanting a personalized rhyme, steeped in distilled water and introduced secretly into the food or drink of those named in the charm.
