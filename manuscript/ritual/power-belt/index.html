A spell to create a magickal belt that enhances personal power, vitality, and confidence. By tying knots into a purple cord while channeling earth and celestial energies, you capture power that can be released when needed by untying knots.

Quantities

  • Music that energizes you and signifies power to you
  • 1 purple cord long enough to circle your waist three times (drapery cord works well)

Directions

  1. Cast a circle around the area where you will do your spell.
  2. Begin playing the music you've selected.
  3. Close your eyes and allow the music to stimulate you, making you feel stronger and more energetic.
  4. Let your breathing become deeper.
  5. Grasp the purple cord in your hands as you see yourself drawing up silvery light from the earth.
  6. Feel the light flow up your legs, into your torso, arms, and head.
  7. Next, visualize yourself drawing golden light down from the heavens, into the crown of your head.
  8. Feel this light flow into your torso, arms, and legs until your whole body resonates with it.
  9. Imagine these two forces blending harmoniously in and around you, increasing your vitality, confidence, and personal power.
  10. Holding on to this sensation, open your eyes and begin tying knots in the cord.
  11. See yourself capturing some of the energy you've raised into each knot you tie.
  12. Tie as many knots as you like, just make sure the cord will still fit around your waist.
  13. When you're finished tying the knots, wrap the belt around your waist and secure it.
  14. Open the circle.
  15. Wear this power belt to help you address whatever challenges you face.
  16. If at any time you need a quick rush of vitality or courage, untie one of the knots and release the energy it holds.
