☀️Solar Citrine Charging

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
SolarSalt MagickEnergy Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Salt - 1 small citrine crystal - Sunny, outdoor place to work - Optional: Wand
◇Directions
1. Kneel down and place both palms flat on the ground. 2. Let yourself feel tired, sluggish, bored, wan, whatever is ailing you. 3. Allow these energies to flow freely through your body, then direct the flow out through your hands into the bare ground. 4. Empty your body of as much stale, sluggish energy as possible, letting it drain into earth until you experience an empty, hollow feeling. 5. Take salt and sprinkle it over the dirt into which you've just released your body's stale energy. 6. Salt has purifying quality that will help clear away patterns going on behind scenes of your current rut. 7. Turn your attention to the sun. 8. Feel warm sunlight as it shines on your skin. 9. Focus on warmth and brightness, tell this energy to enter your body. 10. Envision light flowing into you, feel heat and strength filling you from top to bottom. 11. You may want to raise your hands or wand up toward sun to help facilitate energy flow. 12. Hold citrine stone up to sun. 13. Citrine has many energetic attributes in common with sunlight, strong, very positive vibration, great at neutralizing or purifying negativity and other forms of "darkness." As you hold citrine skyward, use visualization and willpower to "pull" sunlight into stone. 14. Feel stone "charging up", increase in temperature or higher rate of energetic vibration. 15. Once stone feels "full," place it on top of scattered salt. 16. Rub citrine over surface of area, moving salt crystals around. 17. Think about how you're ready for something new, boost of fresh energy to inspire you with fresh purpose and motivate you toward new adventure. 18. Imagine as vividly as you can so that you actually feel and experience emotionally what it will be like to have greater energy, to feel awake and powerful, to feel excited and renewed. 19. Pay attention to your body posture and adjust accordingly so you're physically aligned with your goal. 20. (For instance, if you want to feel happy, stand like you're happy, smile like you're happy, etc.) 21. Hold citrine in your palm. 22. Sprinkle a bit more fresh salt over it. 23. Rub it around in salt on your palm as you let more sunlight pour into mix. 24. Feel the energy, sense enormous power in citrine increasing and vibrating wildly. 25. Affirm out loud or mentally: "Like the sun and this stone, I am supercharged and energized!" 26. Spell is complete. 27. Carry citrine with you or leave it in open area of your home for best results. 28. You should experience immediate boost of energy that should continue for at least several days, possibly much longer, depending on strength of your magick compared to strength of energy-zapping factors in daily life.
◇Description
To clear away stagnation and give body and mind a boost of energy. Utilizes sun power and natural power of salt and stones to banish sluggishness and tiredness.
