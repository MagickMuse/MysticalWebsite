Lucky Hand

Lucky Hand root, from a highly endangered orchid resembling a tiny hand with extra fingers, is anointed with dressing oil and carried in a mojo hand as a powerful employment charm for finding new work and keeping existing employment; the more fingers on the root, the stronger it is, and ethical sourcing requires growing your own. ⚠️ Lucky Hand Employment Spell is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Lucky Hand root
Dressing oil
Mojo bag

Directions

  1. Anoint Lucky Hand root with a drop of dressing oil and carry it in a mojo hand.
