A rattle ritual to banish misfortune and welcome new beginnings in all areas of life using directional work, rhythmic movement, and spoken incantations.

Quantities

  • Two rattles or lidded jars
  • Dried peas or lentils (enough to half-fill the rattles or jars)

Directions

  1. Gather your materials and create sacred space.
  2. Holding a rattle in each hand, stand in the center of the room and face each of the directions in turn (approximately north, east, south, west).
  3. Shake the rattles softly and say in each direction:
  4. Misfortune, misfortune, go away, do not return any day.
  5. Turn clockwise faster, nine times through all the directions, rattling more and more rapidly, and say faster:
  6. Life starts anew, past sorrows through.
  7. Bad luck cannot stay, misfortune away.
  8. When you can rattle and speak no faster, stand in the center facing north and slow the words and rattling until they fade into silence.
  9. Go outdoors, shake the rattles just once softly, and say:
  10. I welcome my new beginnings.
