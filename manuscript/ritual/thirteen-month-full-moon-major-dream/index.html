🌕Thirteen-Month Full Moon Major Dream

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
LunarManifestationChant/MantraVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Silver bells on string - 1 twig or clear crystal point - Optional: 7 silver candles in ring (if indoors)
◇Directions
1. Face the moon, standing within the reflected moonlight with the twig in the hand you write with and the bells in the other hand, saying, "Moon light, moon bright, as you do reach your fullness, in thirteen moons or even less, may this my dream be manifest." Ring the bells seven times slowly, repeating the words after each ring, and then make your wish. 2. Walk in a counterclockwise circle seven times within the pool of light, circling the twig in clockwise circles, repeating the words seven times. 3. Then, facing the moon, repeat the wish. 4. Ring the bells again once, repeating the words. 5. Bury the twig or crystal point in the center of the light. 6. Do the spell every full moon. 7. Between spells, hang the bells just inside your front door. 8. Perform outdoors on full moon night or any night around then when the moon is bright. 9. If necessary, do the spell indoors with seven silver candles in a ring and stand in the middle.
◇Description
A monthly full moon ritual ringing silver bells seven times and circling with a twig to manifest major dreams within thirteen moons.
