🌹Scarlet Ribbon Spell

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• A length of red ribbon exactly measuring the full height of the target's body • A candle (size appropriate for burning upside down) • Carving tool • Optional: true indigo ribbon or cloth (stronger effect; use with care, as indigo is a permanent dye)
◇Directions
1. Measure a single length of red ribbon from head to toe along the target's body, or as close to their full height as you can manage. 2. Set it aside. 3. Take a candle and slice off the top (the end with the original wick). 4. Carve the bottom so that the buried end of the wick is exposed. 5. This inverts the candle's polarity, it will now burn from what was the base. 6. Dress, carve further if desired, and charge the candle with your intention. 7. Tie the red ribbon around the center of the candle. 8. Place the candle upside down (burning from the carved bottom) and light it. 9. Note: This working can also be performed using true indigo in place of the red ribbon, which is said to intensify the effect. 10. Use with caution, indigo is a profound and permanent dye. 11. Ensure you are familiar with its properties before applying to clothing or skin.
◇Description
A Mexican folk spell for pacifying abusive men. A red ribbon the exact length of the target's body is tied around a candle that has been prepared so the wick is exposed at the bottom rather than the top, then burned upside down. May optionally be performed with true indigo ribbon or cloth for a stronger effect, with caution due to indigo's permanence as a dye.
