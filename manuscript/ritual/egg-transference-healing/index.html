🥚Egg Transference Healing

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
HealingVisualizationEnergy WorkPrayer
◇Category
Energetic Rite
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Closed Practice
Haitian Vodou
◇Quantities
- Fresh egg: 1 - Prayers to your deities - Visualization of white purifying light - Place to bury the egg with respect (not on your property)
◇Directions
1. Preparation: 2. Before beginning, prepare yourself spiritually. 3. You must pray fervently to your gods and call down the light of spirit into your body. 4. You can use the Conjuration of the Column (Descendat Columba, Ascendat Serpens) to accomplish this, or you can work with simpler visualization. 5. Calling Down the Light: 6. Imagine that a white purifying light descends from infinite space above you. 7. See it enter through the crown of your head. 8. Feel this light filling your body completely, pushing physical and emotional disease out of your body as it fills you. 9. The light displaces sickness, forcing it to the surface where it can be absorbed by the egg. 10. The Egg Transfer: 11. Take the egg in your hands. 12. Make a prayer to your deities, asking them to witness this healing and bless the egg's sacrifice. 13. Rub the egg over yourself, starting from the head and moving down the body. 14. Work systematically, over the crown, face, neck, shoulders, arms, torso, hips, legs, and feet. 15. As you rub the egg over each area, feel the sickness being pulled from your body into the egg. 16. The egg gives the disease a place to go other than resettling in the body. 17. It acts as a container for what you are releasing. 18. Pay special attention to any areas where you feel pain, illness, or energetic blockage. 19. Spend extra time there, allowing the egg to draw out the concentrated sickness. 20. Respectful Disposal: 21. When you are finished, you must take the egg someplace away from your home and bury it with respect. 22. Even though it is just an egg, what was once a potential life has done you a service by taking your disease into itself, sacrificing itself in the process. 23. Make an offering to the spirit of this potential life. 24. This could be a few drops of water, a pinch of tobacco, a spoken prayer of gratitude, or another appropriate offering according to your tradition. 25. Commit the egg to the ground with respect, thanking it for its service. 26. The disease can now be absorbed into Mother Earth, who processes and transforms what would harm humans. 27. Important Considerations: 28. Do not bury the egg on your own property. 29. Burying disease-laden magickal items on your land keeps that energy too close to you. 30. Take it to a neutral location away from your home. 31. Do not break the egg or crack it open. 32. The egg must remain whole to contain what you have transferred into it. 33. Wash your hands thoroughly after handling the egg and before burial. 34. You don't want to reabsorb what you've just released. 35. Note on Auric Damage: This method removes illness effectively, but like any healing technique that pulls something out, it can leave a hole in the aura. 36. See the Auric Realignment Circle ritual if you need to repair your energetic field after this working.
◇Description
A self-healing method that transfers physical and emotional disease from the body into an egg, which then acts as a sacrifice. This working draws on vodou and Nepalese shamanic traditions adapted for solo practice. The egg absorbs illness while divine light purifies and pushes disease out of the body.
