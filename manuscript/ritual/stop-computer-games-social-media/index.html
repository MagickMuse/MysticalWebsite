Ritual Grimoire

📱Stop Computer Games Social Media

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Chant/MantraEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Access to computer, tablet, or smartphone - 4 green crystals (green and black malachite are best), one set at each corner - 1 small bag with drawstring or purse
◇Directions
1. Hold your hands so that your fingers are pointing upward and your palms inward toward the screen. 2. Move them from about an extended arm's length away to an inch or two from the screen, in and out in a slow, regular motion, saying, "May there be limitations, sensible reservations, lose your power, hour after hour, to fascinate and dominate." Clap your hands three times, saying three times, "Wait, please dedicate your time to better use. The power of the machine is now defused." Shake your fingers in front of the screen, repeating the second chant. 3. Hide the crystals in a small closed bag or purse high in the room where the young person mainly uses the device. 4. Perform when the young owner is not present.
◇Description
A hand movement ritual with green crystals to break the power of technology addiction in children and teenagers.
