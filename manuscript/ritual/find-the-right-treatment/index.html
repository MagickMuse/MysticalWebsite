💬Wednesday

A Wednesday 10pm healing hour spell to find the right treatment for chronic or debilitating condition, using orange candle and stones with incense triangles to guide you to those who know the answer.

Quantities

  • An orange candle
  • Three orange or red stones or crystals in a triangle around the candle
  • Three sticks of any tree or fruit incense in separate holders in a triangle around the stones

Directions

  1. Light the candle and say: "Light your way to me, you who know the answer. I will find those who know the answer to open up the way." Light the first incense and write in incense smoke over the stones and candle: GUIDE ME TO YOU, YOU WHO KNOW THE ANSWER.
  2. Repeat the words and actions as you light the second and then the third incense.
  3. Blow out the candle, repeating both sets of words, and let the incense burn outdoors.
  4. Take the stones outdoors, bury one in the ground, and say: "Over land and over seas, through the skies, there comes to me, the one who knows the answer." Take the second stone into an open space and throw it as far as you can, repeating the spell words.
  5. Cast the final stone into water and say the words once more.
  6. Check the Internet and support groups for updates, repeating all the words before you use the web browser.
  7. Perform Wednesday, if possible at 10 p.m., the healing hour.
