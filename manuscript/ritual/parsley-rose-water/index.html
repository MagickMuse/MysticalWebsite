💧Parsley & Rose Water

Parsley

A thorough personal cleansing designed for moments when you feel spiritually shaken, uneasy, or tainted following a difficult experience or encounter. The spell combines the absorptive power of the egg with the cleansing strike of fresh parsley and the purifying anointment of rose water. Having another person perform the egg and parsley portions is potentially more effective than doing it alone. One of the Egg Cleansing methods.

Quantities

1 whole raw egg
1 bunch of fresh parsley
Rose water: enough to anoint or fill a shallow bath

Directions

  1. Remove all clothing.
  2. Rub an egg over the entire body, working outward: from the shoulders to the fingertips, from the thighs down to the toes.
  3. Slap yourself all over with a bunch of parsley.
  4. Anoint yourself with rose water. For a deeper cleansing, pour a shallow bath of rose water and lie in it, turning over so that both sides of the body are fully covered.
  5. Put on clean clothes.
  6. Place the egg and parsley in a brown paper bag and dispose of them far from your home.
