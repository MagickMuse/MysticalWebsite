👊Stamping Stop Giving In Demands

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Chant/MantraEmpowerment
◇Category
Spell
↗Correspondences5
🚧Boundaries⚡Energy🕊️Freedom✨Manifestation🛡️Protection
↗Materials1
🖊️Pen

A stamping spell if you're tired of giving in to others' demands, using rhythmic stamping and clapping with verbal affirmations to reclaim your power and assert boundaries.

Quantities

  • Nothing if you do the spell at home
  • In the office, you can press your feet into the floor rhythmically and circle your pen slowly in the air clockwise while you say the spell words in your mind

Directions

  1. Stamp hard nine times, saying nine times, once after each stamp: "I insist." Name the change you insist on and stamp nine times more.
  2. Now stamp hard nine more times and clap nine times at the same time, saying: "I want" nine times, once after each stamp.
  3. Then name what you want and stamp nine more times.
  4. Stamp nine more times, saying nine times, once after each stamp: "I will have." Name what you will have and stamp and clap nine more times.
  5. End by naming what you insist, what you want, and what you will have, and make a last stamp and clap, saying: "Now!"
