A protective poppet in gingerbread man shape, stuffed with herbs and crystals to guard Yule gifts from harm or prying eyes. Includes variations for love and home protection poppets.

Quantities

  • For gingerbread man guard poppet:
  • Gingerbread man cookie cutter
  • Gold felt or fabric (enough for 2 identical shapes)
  • Thread
  • Needle
  • Sage (dried)
  • Cinnamon (dried)
  • Shredded gift wrap or ribbons
  • Small bells (a few)
  • Snowflake obsidian (small bits)
    Optional decorations:
  • Gold star embellishment
  • Fabric scraps for soldier's outfit details
    For heart-shaped love poppet (variation):
  • Heart-shaped cookie cutter or template
  • Fabric
  • Rose petals (dried)
  • Saffron
  • Sprig of mistletoe
    For tree-shaped protection poppet (variation):
  • Tree-shaped cookie cutter or template
  • Fabric
  • Evergreen needles (pine, fir, or spruce)
  • Juniper berries

Directions

  1. This reading is best done on the Autumn Equinox (Mabon) or during the days surrounding it, when the veil between light and dark is balanced.
  2. Prepare: Set up your space with symbols of harvest and balance (fallen leaves, apples, grains, equal-height candles, seasonal colors).
  3. Ground yourself and light a candle.
  4. Shuffle while focusing on the question: "What do I need to release, what do I need to harvest, and how do I prepare for the dark season ahead?"
  5. Lay out five cards in the shape of a balance scale:

    2 3

    7.
    Card 1 (The Fulcrum – Balance Point): What is in balance right now?
  6. This card shows where you are centered, what's working, or what needs to be your anchor as the season shifts.
  7. Card 2 (Light Side – Gratitude & Harvest): What blessings, lessons, or abundance have you gathered this year?
  8. This card reveals what to celebrate and give thanks for.
  9. Card 3 (Dark Side – Release & Let Go): What must you release before the dark season begins?
  10. This card shows what no longer serves you (habits, relationships, beliefs, projects, grief).
  11. Card 4 (The Descent): What will you face or explore as you move into autumn and winter?
  12. This card reveals themes or inner work ahead (shadow work, rest, grief, introspection, transformation).
  13. Card 5 (The Foundation): What grounds you through the dark?
  14. This card shows your source of strength, comfort, and stability during the inward season.
  15. Interpret the spread as a whole.
  16. Notice whether the cards feel balanced or if one side (light or dark) is pulling more strongly.
  17. Both are necessary.
  18. Journal your insights.
  19. If any card is unclear, pull a clarifier.
  20. Close by saying:
    "I honor the harvest. I release what I no longer need. I prepare for the dark with trust and gratitude. Blessed Mabon."
  21. Leave the cards on your altar through the equinox, then photograph or journal them for reflection throughout the season.
