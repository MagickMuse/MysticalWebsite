☠️To Cause Death

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Binding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
1 jar or bottle with a lid 9 needles 9 pins Water used to wash a corpse
◇Directions
1. Poke holes in the lid of the jar. 2. Fill the jar with water that has been used to wash a corpse. 3. The corpse may be any deceased creature: a beloved pet, an insect found dead, or otherwise. 4. Add nine needles and nine pins to the water. 5. Secure the lid on the jar. 6. Take the jar to the target's property. 7. Bury it or conceal it upside down so that the water slowly leaks out through the holes. 8. When all the water has emptied, the target will die.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A death hex using corpse water, nine needles, and nine pins sealed in a jar with a perforated lid. The jar is buried or concealed upside down on the target's property. As the corpse water gradually leaks out, the target's life drains with it.
