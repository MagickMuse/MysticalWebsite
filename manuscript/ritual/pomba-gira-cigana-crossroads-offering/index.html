🌹Pomba Gira Cigana Crossroads Offering

A full crossroads offering to Pomba Gira Cigana for legal assistance: two diamond playing cards, seven red roses, cigarettes, anisette, and two red taper candles at a T-shaped crossroads. State your need explicitly and name your payment.

Quantities

  • 1 brand new deck of playing cards (remove ace + 7 of diamonds)
  • Red cloth
  • Black cloth
  • 7 red roses
  • Fine cigarettes
  • 1 box of matches
  • 1 small bottle of anisette
  • 2 red taper candles

Directions

  1. Remove the ace and seven of diamonds from a brand new deck of playing cards.
  2. Go to a T-shaped crossroads at the appropriate time.
  3. Lay a red cloth over a black cloth.
  4. Place the two diamond cards on the cloths along with seven red roses, fine cigarettes, a box of matches, and a small bottle of anisette.
  5. Light two red taper candles.
  6. Tell Pomba Gira Cigana clearly why you have called her, what you need her to do, and what you will pay her when the work is accomplished.

Pomba Gira Cigana is a specific manifestation of Pomba Gira who specializes in legal matters: lawsuits, estate distribution, immigration matters, separations, divorces, and custody issues. Cigana is Portuguese for Gypsy, and Pomba Gira Cigana embodies the figure of the Gypsy fortuneteller. She knows many secrets, reads playing cards, and moves between worlds with complete authority.

If you can read playing cards yourself, an ordinary deck may serve as an oracle for communication with her.

The Working

  1. Get a brand new deck of cards. Remove the ace of diamonds and the seven of diamonds. These are her cards.
  2. Go to a T-shaped crossroads.
  3. Lay a red cloth on top of a black cloth.
  4. On the cloths, place:
    • The two diamond cards
    • Seven red roses
    • Fine cigarettes
    • A box of matches
    • A small bottle of anisette
  5. Light two red taper candles.
  6. Speak directly to Pomba Gira Cigana:
    • Tell her why you have called her
    • Tell her explicitly what you need her to do
    • Tell her what you will pay her when the work is accomplished
Pomba Gira Cigana is a businesswoman of the spirit world. She expects a clear and binding agreement. Vague requests receive vague results.
