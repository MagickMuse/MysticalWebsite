🐄Nanna Guided Meditation for Fertility

A guided visualization journey invoking Nanna, the ancient Sumerian moon deity associated with cows and lapis lazuli, to connect with the fertile, abundant energy of the crescent moon. Follow a glowing-horned cow across a moonlit pasture to a circle of blooming flowers, receive messages, and hold your intention in the lunar light. Close by pouring milk as liquid moonlight and toasting the moon. Combine with the Milk Moon Flower Bath for a full lunar milk ritual.

Quantities

  • Bell
  • Cup
  • Milk of your choice
  • Lapis lazuli

Directions

  1. Prepare your space for meditation as you normally would.
  2. Set the bell, cup, and milk before you.
  3. Take the lapis lazuli into your hand, feel its cool weight, its deep blue.
  4. Close your eyes.
  5. Take several slow, deep breaths and let your body settle completely.
  6. The Meditation:
  7. Visualize yourself standing in a wide-open pasture.
  8. The night spreads vast around you.
  9. Stars fill the sky in every direction, and their light touches everything, the grasses, the flowers, the breathing dark.
  10. You feel held by the stillness of it.
  11. In the distance, a cow moves slowly forward.
  12. Something in you feels called to follow.
  13. You begin to walk.
  14. As you move through the pasture, notice the world around you, the warm whisper of the night wind against your skin, the shimmer of dew on the grass, the soft sounds of life settling in for the night.
  15. Everything is alive and glistening.
  16. The cow leads you deeper into the landscape until it comes to rest at the center of a wide circle of blooming flowers.
  17. It stops, turns, and faces you.
  18. Look at its horns.
  19. They are luminous, curved and glowing like the crescent moon against the starlit sky, the ancient symbol of fertility and new life.
  20. Meet its eyes.
  21. They glow like stars, ancient and knowing.
  22. Stay here for a moment.
  23. Let images, feelings, or words rise without forcing them.
  24. Ask inwardly: What do I wish to come to fullness?
  25. Sit down inside the circle of flowers.
  26. Feel the lunar light from the cow's horns falling on you, warm and silver.
  27. Hold your intention clearly in your mind, what you wish to grow, to birth, to manifest.
  28. Visualize the light growing brighter, pouring into your intention, blessing it, sealing it in living energy.
  29. When you feel ready, gently return your awareness to your body and open your eyes.
  30. Closing:
  31. With the moon's blessing still warm inside you, pour the milk slowly into the cup, as if pouring liquid moonlight.
  32. Lift the cup toward the moon (or toward the sky, or a candle flame) as a toast.
  33. Sip, and feel the sustenance of the moon move through you.
  34. If you wish, pour any remaining milk outside as an offering to Nanna and the fertile earth.
  35. Ring the bell once to close the ritual.
  36. Keep the lapis lazuli on your person until your intention manifests, or place it outside to charge under the full moon to renew its energy.
