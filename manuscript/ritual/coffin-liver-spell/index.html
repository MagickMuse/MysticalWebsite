⚰️Coffin Liver Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Energy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Miniature coffin Raw chicken liver Asafetida powder Cayenne pepper Black candle stubs (optional)
◇Directions
1. Craft a miniature coffin. 2. Place a raw chicken liver within the casket. 3. Sprinkle asafetida powder and cayenne pepper over the liver and add candle stubs if desired. 4. Leave the casket on your target's doorstep.
◇Description
A public coffin hex using a raw chicken liver in place of a doll. The liver is dusted with asafetida and cayenne pepper and placed inside a miniature coffin left on the target's doorstep.
