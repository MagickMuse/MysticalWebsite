A simple candle charging spell intended as a pure petition for justice, with no aggressive or hexing energy. The caster focuses on the situation at hand and the reasons justice should be served, then surrenders the outcome entirely to universal forces. The path to justice is not visualized, only the appeal.

Quantities

  • 1 brown candle

Directions

  1. Hold a brown candle between both hands.
  2. Close your eyes and concentrate on your circumstances, the ways justice has not been served, and why it should be.
  3. Do not try to envision how justice will come about.
  4. Allow that to be determined by forces greater than yourself.
  5. When the candle feels fully charged and complete in your hands, set it in a holder and burn it.
