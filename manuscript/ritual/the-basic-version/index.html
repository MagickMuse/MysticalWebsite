🥩Beef Tongue📄Paper📌Pins & Needles

The foundational beef tongue court case spell from Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo tradition. Name papers for every opponent, judge, and hostile witness are sealed inside a raw beef tongue dressed with cayenne and vinegar, then frozen until all legal danger has passed.

⚠️ Closed Practice Warning: This spell draws from Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo, both closed African American and Afro-Caribbean spiritual traditions. Please engage with respect, proper education, or seek guidance from a practitioner within the tradition. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Quantities

1 raw beef tongue
1 slip of paper per opponent (fortune cookie size)
Cayenne pepper
Vinegar (Four Thieves Vinegar recommended)
Pins and needles (1+ per slit)

Directions

  1. Write the name of each opponent, judge, hostile attorney, or adverse witness on individual fortune-cookie-sized slips of paper, one name per paper.
  2. Using a sharp knife, cut one slit into the tongue for each name paper.
  3. Insert one name paper into each slit.
  4. Sprinkle generously with cayenne pepper and vinegar; Four Thieves Vinegar is recommended, but any vinegar is acceptable.
  5. Seal each slit shut with pins and needles.
  6. Wrap the tongue well and place it in the freezer.
  7. Leave it for at least a year, or until you are fully certain the legal threat has passed.
🔒
Closed Practice: Beef tongue court case spellwork originates in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo — closed practices with African American cultural and spiritual lineage. Approach with respect and research before working with these traditions.

Background

Beef tongue spells are perhaps the most well-known of all legal success workings. Their roots reach back to Britain and were carried to the Western hemisphere where they were developed and refined in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo. The tongue is the obvious symbolic vehicle — the organ of speech, testimony, and persuasion.

This is not animal sacrifice. Beef tongue is a food product obtained from a butcher, and no harm comes to any animal for magical purposes. It can still be purchased from specialty butchers, Latin American grocery stores, and farmers markets.

How It Works

The name papers bind each named person's influence inside the tongue. The cayenne and vinegar sour and heat their intentions. The pins and needles seal their power shut. The freezer holds everything still and powerless for as long as the tongue remains frozen.

Name Papers

Cast the net wide. Include:

  • The opposing party or parties
  • Opposing counsel
  • Hostile expert witnesses
  • Any judge who may rule against you

More name papers = more tongues kept in check.

Timing

Assemble this working well before any court date. The sooner the tongue goes into the freezer, the longer the suppression has to take hold.
