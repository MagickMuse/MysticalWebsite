🎃Faery Godmother Turn Loss into Gain

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A pointed stick or crystal as your wand - A pumpkin - A sharp knife - A dish and a bowl
◇Directions
1. Tap the pumpkin seven times with the crystal or stick, saying: "Faery godmother, bring to me, no golden coach with horses three, but turn my loss into gain, that I may know success again." Cut the top off the pumpkin, scooping out the flesh and seeds as though making a jack-o'-lantern. 2. Keep the flesh for cooking and put the seeds in a dish. 3. Cut seven large holes around the pumpkin and light a candle inside. 4. Gently revolve the pumpkin clockwise, reciting the days of the week, starting with the present one, then tap the pumpkin seven times again, saying: "Faery godmother, turn my fate, week by week, to what I seek, not a prince or fancy ball, but I ask you, restore all." Blow out the candle, bury the pumpkin seeds, and cook the pumpkin flesh. 5. Each night, tap the pumpkin, turning it seven times and repeating all the words. 6. Continue until the pumpkin loses its freshness. 7. Perform between 11 p.m. and midnight.
◇Description
A late-night pumpkin spell invoking a faery godmother to turn loss into gain and restore success, tapping a carved pumpkin seven times nightly, revolving it clockwise through the days of the week, and burying the seeds until the pumpkin fades.
