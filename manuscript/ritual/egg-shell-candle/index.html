🪨Egg-Shell Candle

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Manifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• Raw eggs (buy a dozen; practice may be needed) • Wax • Candle wick • Optional: fertility herbs, tiny seed beads, or small charms • An egg carton • An ice pick or similar thin sharp tool
◇Directions
1. Gently punch a small hole in the large end of a raw egg. 2. Use a cuticle scissors to carefully cut a small circle of shell around the hole. 3. Empty the egg into a bowl for other use. 4. Rinse the interior of the shell thoroughly with water, removing all membrane and residue. Allow it to dry completely. 5. Prepare the wax for pouring. Optional additions (herbs, tiny charms) may be set aside for mid-pour. 6. Set the shell hole-end up in an egg carton to hold it stable. 7. Pour in the liquid wax, reserving a small amount for the next day. When the shell is half full, add any optional herbs, seed beads, or charms if desired. Leave to harden overnight. 8. The following day, gently reheat the reserved wax until liquid. 9. Carefully chip away the eggshell to expose the candle. 10. Heat an ice pick or similar thin tool and push it through the center of the candle, then thread a wick through quickly before it cools. 11. Seal the wick hole with the hot reserved wax. 12. Decorate as desired. Keep the candle as a fertility amulet or burn it in a spell.
◇Description
An egg-shell used as a candle mold, then chipped away to reveal the finished candle within. The result carries the egg's generative symbolism in every layer: the shell as cosmos, the wax as the substance of creation poured in and set. Herbs, seed beads, or small charms may be added during the pour. The finished candle can be kept as a fertility amulet or burned in a working. One of the Egg Fertility methods.
