🐰Ostara

The Spring Equinox celebration of balance, rebirth, and manifestation. A seed-planting ritual where a written intention is burned into the soil, empowered with raised energy, and planted with a physical seed to grow alongside the practitioner's desire.

Quantities

  • Altar Candles
  • 3 altar candles: pastels such as green, peach, lavender, yellow, or pink
    Incense
  • Jasmine, light-scented florals, Secret Garden, or Rain
    Decorations
  • Spring flowers and wildflowers for altar/circle
  • Garden motif items
  • Loose bundle of wildflowers on altar
    Ritual Items
  • Earthenware or wooden bowl with soil
  • Large seed for planting (placed on pentacle)
  • Sheet or piece of parchment
  • Ink (Dove's Blood, Black, or Purple) and writing tool
  • Wand
  • Athame
  • Bell (optional)
    Cakes & Wine
  • Seed-type foods: sprouts, soy beans
  • Sweet wine or beverage (honey may be added)
  • Roasted ham
  • Pineapple rings
  • Decorated hard-boiled eggs (made into deviled eggs)
  • Yellow cake with poppy seeds (optional: 1 cup sherry)
  • Banana nut bread

Directions

  1. Cast circle as in the Basic Circle Casting.
  2. When the ritual work portion begins, clap or ring a bell three times:
  3. I call upon me the Blessings of the Ancient Ones as merry do we meet at this Springtime Rite.
  4. Lady and Lord, hear your child, [Name], for I am here to celebrate with You and for You as we greet the Spring together!
  5. Sprinkle wildflowers deosil around the inside perimeter of the circle:
  6. Lady and Lord frolic and play; stir up the creatures along your way.
  7. Clap or ring bell three times:
  8. Springtime is when we sow the seed; it is the time for me to plant what I want to grow.
  9. This season brings hope and joy; expectations for desires realized; and inspiration for new ideas.
  10. My life is brought into balance and I am reborn, resurrected, as is the Earth with Her renewal.
  11. I welcome thee, Ostara, beautiful Spring!
  12. Place the bowl of soil on the pentacle.
  13. Focus on seeing the seed as an idea to be manifested.
  14. Clap or ring bell once.
  15. Write your seed idea on the parchment.
  16. Light the parchment from the center candle and drop the burning ashes into the soil:
  17. Lady and Lord, let this soil be prepared and fertile to receive this seed of my desire, that here it may grow and prosper, to ripen and bear fruit, as it grows and prospers in my mind and heart to reach maturity and fruition.
  18. Mix the ashes into the soil with the athame.
  19. Raise the wand with arms open.
  20. Dance or skip deosil three times around the circle, playing music if desired, to raise energy.
  21. Stop at the altar with wand and arms open and raised:
  22. By the power instilled in this raised wand will the seed be planted in the ready soil.
  23. Blessed Be the Wand of Spring and Blessed Be the Earth that receives it!
  24. Kiss the tip of the wand, envisioning the energy raised entering into it.
  25. Make an indentation in the center of the soil with the wand, seeing the energy move from wand into soil.
  26. Set down the wand.
  27. Hold up the seed and focus energy into it.
  28. Know that the idea will be manifested as the plant grows.
  29. Place the seed into the furrow and close the soil over it:
  30. This seed is planted in the Mother's womb to be part of the Earth, of Life, and of me.
  31. Let this seed and what it represents grow to manifestation, as I will it.
  32. So Mote It Be!
  33. Clap or ring bell three times.
  34. After the circle is opened, transplant the seed and soil into a garden, flower pot, or other suitable place.
  35. Proceed to the Cakes & Wine ceremony of the circle casting.
