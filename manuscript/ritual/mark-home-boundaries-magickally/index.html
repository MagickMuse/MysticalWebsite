🏡Mark Home Boundaries Magickally

◇Form
Charged ObjectCharm
◇Method
ProtectionChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- 1 small silver bell, Tibetan bells, wind chimes, or recording of bell music with headphones - 8 protective plants, bushes, or trees (basil, bamboo tied with red string, bay tree, cactus, eucalyptus, hawthorn, juniper, mint, myrtle, palm, pine, rosemary, roses, rowan, or sage)
◇Directions
1. Begin inside the home, walking the four external walls on every floor and ringing the bell; repeat for the boundaries of your garden and front path or balcony. 2. Place a protective flower, shrub, or herb pot at each of the farthest four corners of home and garden (eight in total), saying for each, "Be as protection for me against all negativity, be guardian and shed positivity within my boundaries." (If you do not have a garden, put two plants at each corner inside the property.) Visit each of the corners again inside and outside, saying at each, "One from theft, two from storm, three from sickness and from harm, four from fire and careless stranger, five from foe and unseen danger, six from malice, seven from pain, eight from flood, nine may joy remain. May this spell last through moon and sun." Perform just before nightfall.
◇Description
A comprehensive boundary protection ritual using bells and protective plants at eight corners to safeguard home and land from all harm. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
