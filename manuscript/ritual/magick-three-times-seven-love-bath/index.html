🛁Magick Three Times Seven Love Bath

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Bath
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ManifestationHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
7 pink or red rosebuds 7 lovage leaves 7 Grains of Paradise Boiling water
◇Directions
1. This bath is recommended before going someplace where you hope to meet someone new. 2. Blend seven pink or red rosebuds with seven lovage leaves and seven Grains of Paradise. 3. Pour boiling water over the botanicals and allow them to steep. 4. Do not strain out the botanical material; add everything to your bath. 5. Let yourself air-dry afterwards to radiate your desire for a new love.
◇Description
Seven rosebuds, seven lovage leaves, and seven Grains of Paradise are steeped in boiling water and added unstrained to the bath. The bather air-dries afterwards. Recommended before going somewhere you hope to meet someone new.
