💤Lover Dream of Me

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Dreamwork
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Allspice berries • Orrisroot • Dried honeysuckle blossoms • Dried red rose petals • Dried hibiscus flowers • A lock of your hair • Pine essential oil (1 drop) • Sandalwood essential oil (1 drop) • Optional: 1 drop of menstrual blood • Red silk or satin to cover the pillow
◇Directions
1. Grind allspice berries and orrisroot together to a fine powder. 2. Place the powder in a bowl along with dried honeysuckle blossoms, red rose petals, and hibiscus flowers. 3. Add a lock of your hair to the blend. 4. Add one drop of pine essential oil and one drop of sandalwood essential oil. 5. If you choose, add one drop of menstrual blood as well. 6. Stir gently until everything is thoroughly combined. 7. Allow the mixture to dry completely. 8. Fill a small dream pillow with the dried blend. 9. Cover the pillow in red silk or satin for maximum potency, and present it as a gift to the intended person.
◇Description
A dream pillow gifted to someone whose dreams you wish to enter, designed to place your presence in their dreams. A pillow covered in red silk or satin is considered most potent. One of the Dream Pillow methods.
