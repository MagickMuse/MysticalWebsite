🧱The Vegetarian Version

◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method

◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Name papers (1 per opponent) 2 red bricks 1 metal bucket of ice (during proceedings) Optional: menstrual blood or urine for name papers Optional: candles (brown for justice, purple for power)
◇Directions
1. Create a name paper for each opponent. 2. Optionally, cross over each name with your own and speak aloud: "I cross you and I cover you. I command you and I compel you to [state your intention]." Place the name papers between two red bricks. 3. The assembly may be prepared at any time before the legal proceedings. 4. During the actual trial, set a metal bucket of ice on top of the stacked bricks. 5. For optional enhancements, sprinkle name papers with menstrual blood or urine before placing between the bricks, or ring the bricks with appropriately colored burning candles.
◇Description
A vegetarian alternative to the beef tongue court case spell requiring no meat, no refrigeration, and no advance preparation. Name papers are placed between two red bricks; during proceedings, a metal bucket of ice is set on top to add a freezing element. One of the Beef Tongue Courtcase Spell methods.

Closed Practice: This spell is rooted in Hoodoo court case tradition — a closed African American folk magic practice. Approach with respect.

Purpose

This version achieves the same freezing-and-suppressing effect as the beef tongue spells without requiring meat, a butcher trip, or a freezer. It is the most accessible variant and the most versatile — it can be assembled at any time, adjusted easily, and broken down and reassembled if needed.

Why Bricks?

Red bricks carry the energy of earth — weight, solidity, immovability. Placing name papers between them literally buries the targets under the force of that earthen pressure. The red color adds heat and intensity to the domination.

Brick magic in this form is specifically a Hoodoo working. Red brick dust (polvo de ladrillo rojo) is a related but separate tradition used to mark protective thresholds.

The Ice Bucket

The metal bucket of ice placed on top during proceedings adds the freezing element that completes the working. The bricks press down; the ice cools and stills. The combination replicates the effect of a beef tongue in a freezer: heavy, cold, immobile.

Strengthening the Working

Personal fluids (menstrual blood or urine) on the name papers add your biological signature to the working, connecting it directly to you and creating a stronger energetic bond.

Candles ringed around the bricks during assembly or proceedings bring additional color magic into the working:

  • Brown candles — justice
  • Purple candles — power and domination

Adjustments

Unlike the buried tongue version, this working can be revisited. Papers can be added or removed, and the ice bucket can be renewed for each day of proceedings.
