A ritual to call a love telepathically if you are both involved in other relationships and can't meet, using water to ripple a psychic connection across distance when meeting is impossible.

Quantities

  • A body of water, whether inland, still, or flowing, or the ocean

Directions

  1. Touch the water with your hands or, if it's shallow, stand in it and move your feet, saying your love's name in your mind, softly but persistently.
  2. Keep saying the name and rippling your hands or feet until you feel a warm connection, as if you were being hugged by your love.
  3. Extend your hands outward, as if reaching out and holding hands across the water.
  4. Say in your mind: "Winds from the waters, winds from the sea, across the land, as we join hands, [name], hard though it be, reach out, my love, now to me." Now speak the words in your heart you cannot say aloud and lower your hands.
  5. Cast a stone, shell, flower, or crystal in the water in thanks.
  6. Try to make actual contact in the next twenty-four hours and you may find normal obstacles no longer stand in the way.
