✂️Peaceful Parting Spell

A cord-cutting spell for ending partnerships, businesses, friendships, or romantic entanglements. Uses a 12-knot incantation to reclaim your power and sever ties. Best started on the full moon or a Friday.

Quantities

  • 2 figural candles or chime candles (colors reflecting you and the other person)
  • Cotton cord (poultry-tying cord works best)
  • Paring knife
  • Fireproof surface (old cookie sheet works)
  • Paper bag for disposal

Directions

  1. Name one candle with your name and one with the other person's name.
  2. The candles' color should reflect you and the other person.
  3. If using figure candles, write each name on the bottom under the feet - they'll burn last and keep the impression longest.
  4. Take cotton cord and knot one end around your candle and the other end around theirs.
  5. Wind until they meet at the center of the string.
  6. Allow the candles to sit together, tied up, for three minutes, three hours, or three days.
  7. Threes are terrifically magickal, and the moon changes signs every two and a half days.
  8. The longer they stay tied together, the better, but sometimes we don't have the luxury of waiting.
  9. When it's time to proceed, start winding the string around their candle, taking up the slack from your candle.
  10. When you reach your feet, take up the paring knife and cut the cord away.
  11. Set your candle on the fireproof surface.
  12. Take the end of the string you've just cut from your candle's feet.
  13. Start to tie knots in it, working your way to their candle.
  14. See the road between you closed.
  15. That person has no power over you.
  16. You are taking your power back with each knot.
  17. By knot of one, this spell has begun.
    By knot of two, you pay your dues.
    By knot of three, I am free.
    By knot of four, no more tears I pour.
    By knot of five, I feel alive.
    By knot of six, no one falls for your tricks.
    By knot of seven, myself I've forgiven.
    By knot of eight, you won't be late.
    By knot of nine, I'm feeling fine.
    By knot of ten, there's no "remember when?"
    By knot of eleven, no hell or heaven.
    By knot of twelve, this spell I salve.
  18. Cut the twine with the paring knife from the second figure's feet and light both candles, back to back.
  19. As they burn (if safe to do so), move the candles further apart on the tray until they're as far away as they can get and still be fire-safe.
  20. Dispose of the candle remains in a paper bag at a busy 24-hour big box store's trash can.
  21. You can be sure it won't be there the next time you come back.
