🧸Soft Doll Romantic Binding Spell

◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Fabric, for a pair of dolls Dried love-herbs, for stuffing Aromatic romantic oils Beads, for ornamentation Fabric, for wrapping
◇Directions
1. Create a pair of dolls from fabric, one to represent each partner. 2. Stuff them with dried love-herbs. 3. Rub them with aromatic romantic oils. 4. Dress and ornament them, adding beads if possible and forming a knot with every bead. 5. Tie the dolls together, face to face, to protect and preserve romance. 6. Wrap them in fabric and keep them in a safe place for as long as you wish this romance to last.
◇Description
The advantage of these doll spells over some other binding spells is that these spells aren't permanent. Should you change your mind about this romance, just separate the dolls to terminate the binding effect.
