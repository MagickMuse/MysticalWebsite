🪬Hand of Power Eye Talisman

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
TalismanWriting SpellSigil
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
1 sheet of parchment or copper A bit of yellow sulfur (optional, for the eye) Protective runes or sacred verses to inscribe
◇Directions
1. Create the talisman on parchment or a sheet of copper. 2. Form it in the shape of a hand with an eye at the center. A bit of yellow sulfur may serve as the eye. 3. Inscribe it with protective runes or sacred verses. 4. Carry it, post it or give it to someone in need.
◇Description
A hand shaped talisman with an eye at its center, made on parchment or copper and inscribed with protective runes or sacred verses, then carried, posted or given to someone in need.
