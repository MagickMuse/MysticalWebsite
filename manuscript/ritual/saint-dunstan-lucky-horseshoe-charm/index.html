🐴Saint Dunstan Lucky Horseshoe Charm

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Salt MagickProtection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 1 horseshoe (real or ornamental) - Hammer and nails - Salt - 1 red candle (for annual renewal)
◇Directions
1. Sprinkle salt around the horseshoe, saying, "By the power of Saint Dunstan, who nailed the Devil to the wall and said none might enter where the horseshoe stood tall, bring immense good fortune to my home and good luck to me/my family wherever I/we roam." Hammer the horseshoe to the wall or over the door (points up to keep luck in or points down to draw luck from earth), saying over and over, "Power of the horseshoe charm, protect me/us from all harm, bring us/me fortune through the year, secure in life and with no fear." Each May 19, Dunstan the blacksmith bishop's day, light a red candle near the center of your home, repeating all the spell words. 2. Perform the first of the month.
◇Description
A protective horseshoe charm invoking Saint Dunstan, installed over doorways to bring fortune and ward against harm.
