🔮Love Match Tarot Divination

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Complete tarot deck - Flat surface for laying out cards - Quiet space for concentration 25 archetypal cards total: - 16 court cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King of each suit) - Optional 9 major arcana: The Magician, High Priestess, Empress, Emperor, Hierophant, Hermit, Hanged Man, Devil, Fool
◇Directions
1. Separate the Archetypal Cards: Remove all 16 court cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King of Cups, Wands, Pentacles, Swords). Optionally add 9 major arcana: Magickian, High Priestess, Empress, Emperor, Hierophant, Hermit, Hanged Man, Devil, Fool. Set these 25 archetypal cards aside. 2. Select Relationship Quality Cards: Look through the remaining deck one by one. Select any cards that represent elements you desire in a future relationship. Don't use book meanings, follow your intuition and emotional response to each image. Choose as many cards as you like. 3. Arrange the Spread: Place your selected quality cards face up in front of you. Beneath them, place the 25 archetypal cards face down (in a pile, single row, or 5x5 grid). 4. Perform the Divination: Look at your face-up quality cards. Think about the aspects you hope to find in your next romance. Concentrate on the question: "Who will give it to me?" Without peeking, select one card from the face-down archetypal cards. 5. Interpret Your Result: The card you selected indicates the type of person ideally suited as your romantic partner. Consult the interpretations below for court cards, or use tarot guides for major arcana. 6. Court Card Interpretations: 7. Page of Cups: Youthful, playful, fun-loving, artistic, live-for-the-moment spirit. 8. Lacking in practicality but full of enthusiasm and spontaneity. 9. Knight of Cups: Very romantic, dramatic in expressing affection. 10. Declares love in elaborate ways and expresses admiration freely. 11. Queen of Cups: Kind, loving, romantic heart but keeps inner self hidden. 12. Appears light and bubbly but has secretive side where deepest passions are concealed. 13. King of Cups: Joyful, compassionate, caring, loving. 14. Self-assured and confident but soft-spoken. 15. Heart rules head. 16. Sentimental with strong love of laughter, friends, and family. 17. Page of Wands: Youthful, loyal, excellent communicator. 18. May work in communications or deliveries. 19. Knight of Wands: Unconventional and different. 20. Loves to impress. 21. Far-fetched or idealistic ideas. 22. May be egotistical but decidedly nonconformist, refusing convention and tradition. 23. Queen of Wands: Independent, confident, bolder than they appear. 24. Strong love for animals and nature. 25. Very social, enjoys parties. 26. Changeable affections but easily lets go of negativity. 27. Often self-made, rising through own talent and effort. 28. King of Wands: Successful, established, respected, unconventional. 29. Lives in own world, trouble seeing other perspectives. 30. May seem domineering due to insecurity. 31. Page of Pentacles: Serious-minded, youthful, excellent focus. 32. Hard worker, dedicated student. 33. May be student or recently employed. 34. Sensible, practical, intelligent, responsible but can be dull. 35. Knight of Pentacles: Practical and sensible. 36. Enjoys simple things, doesn't mind hard work. 37. Dedicated and determined, sometimes too single-minded. 38. Loves animals and nature. 39. Queen of Pentacles: Strongly independent, passionate, earthy. 40. Introverted, loves deeply. 41. Can be clingy and has trouble letting go, but very loyal and practical. 42. Will be there when needed. 43. King of Pentacles: Mature, established, wealthy. 44. Success in business and agriculture. 45. Good math skills and green thumb. 46. Loves luxury and material wealth but earthy in spirit with strong nature connection. 47. Enjoys providing for others' comfort. 48. Page of Swords: Bold, rash, rebellious. 49. Attitude of having something to prove. 50. Can be irresponsible and unscrupulous, pursuing goals with little regard for consequences. 51. Can be dishonest but lives for the moment, loves chances and action. 52. Brave with heavy dose of cockiness. 53. Knight of Swords: Heroic type. 54. Brave, bold, champion of noble causes. 55. Strives to do right at all costs. 56. Can appear single-minded, putting causes before people. 57. Extremely charming, energetic, strong. 58. "Knight in shining armor" type. 59. Queen of Swords: Strong, determined, graceful, tenacious. 60. Commanding personality, fits leadership roles. 61. Appears to have everything together, maintains austere countenance. 62. Touch of inner sadness they conceal. 63. Often misunderstood as cold but loves deeply and forever once trust is earned. 64. Will stand up for cherished people regardless of others' opinions. 65. King of Swords: Forceful personality, commanding presence. 66. Often in power and leadership positions. 67. Strong tempers, can be bossy. 68. Feels comfortable in dominant role. 69. Great determination, unlikely to change paths once dedicated to a pursuit. 70. Notes on Interpretation: Court cards represent archetypes and personality traits, not specific genders, skin tones, or hair colors. Cards indicate maturity level: Kings/Queens/Major Arcana tend toward mature and serious; Pages are less experienced and playful; Knights are adult but still energetic and youthful. Examine occupation, station in life, and other attributes revealed in the card for additional clues.
◇Description
Beltane divination method to identify a potential love match using tarot court cards and selected major arcana. Separate archetypal cards, choose cards representing desired relationship qualities, then draw one archetypal card to reveal the personality type of your ideal romantic partner.
