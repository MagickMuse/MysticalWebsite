🛡️Amulet to Block Unwanted Attention

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
CharmPouchIncense BlendCircle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Knot MagickVisualizationProtectionSalt MagickSmoke Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 piece of amber - 1 piece of onyx - Pine incense - Incense burner - Matches or lighter - 1 piece of paper - Pen with black ink - 1 black pouch (silk or leather preferred) - Dried basil leaves - Anise or fennel seeds - 1 ash leaf - 1 white ribbon, 8 inches long - Salt water
◇Directions
1. Collect all ingredients and wash the amber and onyx with mild soap and water. 2. Cast a circle around your working area. 3. Fit the incense in its burner and light it. 4. Envision yourself safe and sound, completely surrounded by a sphere of pure white light that no one can penetrate without your permission. 5. On the paper, write the word "Protection." Draw a circle around the word and fold the paper small enough to fit into the pouch. 6. Add the botanicals (basil, anise or fennel seeds, ash leaf) and stones (amber, onyx) to the pouch along with the folded paper. 7. Tie the pouch closed with the white ribbon, making eight knots. 8. Each time you tie a knot, repeat this incantation aloud: 9. "From energies I don't invite, This charm protects me day and night." 10. Sprinkle the amulet with salt water, then hold it in the incense smoke for a few moments to charge it. 11. Open the circle. 12. Wear or carry the amulet with you at all times until the unwanted attention stops.
◇Description
A protective amulet to repel unwanted attention and establish clear boundaries when someone refuses to respect your space. Combines protective stones with boundary-setting botanicals.
