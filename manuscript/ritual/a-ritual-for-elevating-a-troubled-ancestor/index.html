A nine-day candle ritual to help elevate a troubled ancestor whose unresolved trauma is affecting the practitioner's life.

Quantities

White cloth
Glass of water
Nine white candles (chime candles, votive candles, or tea lights)
Eight sturdy books of similar size
Picture of the ancestor, or a significator (a dream drawing or divination card)
Optional: something sweet or soothing, such as honey or aloe

Directions

  1. On day one, place the white cloth on the floor, set the glass of water on it with the picture or significator behind or beneath it, then light the first candle and speak the invocation naming the ancestor and their impact.
  2. On days two through nine, at the same time each day, slide one more book beneath the cloth to raise the setup slightly, light the next candle, and repeat the invocation.
  3. On the ninth day, if improvements have been observed, optionally offer something sweet or soothing to the ancestor.

This ritual is performed when a specific ancestor with unresolved trauma is causing problems to manifest in the practitioner's life, particularly problems related to self-esteem and mental health. It is most commonly needed within the first few years after a death, when the soul is still in transition and the living family is restructuring. It can also be relevant for ancestors who lived long ago, including those whose names are not fully known but whose disruptive presence has been identified through divination or dream.

Notes

The soul continues to evolve after death. An ancestor who has been a source of difficulty may become a powerful and elevated presence over time. This ritual holds space for that possibility.
