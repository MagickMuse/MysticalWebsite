💖A Love Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteCircle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraVisualizationManifestationPlanetary
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 red candle - Incense (optional, as a deity offering) - Red rose petals (enough to form a circle around the candle and cards) - Playing cards: Five of Hearts, Ten of Hearts, Seven of Hearts
◇Directions
1. Cast this spell between sunset on Thursday and sunset on Friday, a window ruled by Venus, the planet most closely associated with love. 2. Regardless of which day you begin, the spell must always be performed during the waxing moon, when lunar energy nurtures growth and new beginnings. 3. This working is designed to draw romance into your life rather than targeting a specific person. 4. It opens the way for genuine connection and emotional fulfillment. 5. Light a red candle. 6. If you have a relationship with a love deity, you may also light incense as an offering. 7. Suitable choices include Hathor, Bastet, Branwen, or Frigg, though the most important thing is that you already share a genuine bond with whichever deity you call upon. 8. The incense is entirely optional; this spell works perfectly well as a purely secular working without prayers or offerings of any kind. 9. Lay out three playing cards in a row in front of the candle, from left to right: the Five of Hearts, the Ten of Hearts, and the Seven of Hearts. 10. This arrangement follows a modified from-through-to format, with all three cards representing stages of future development. 11. As you place the Five of Hearts, meditate on social interaction, chance encounters, and new connections. 12. This card reminds you that love begins with meeting people and putting yourself out into the world. 13. On a magickal level, it draws you toward social gatherings and opportunities to connect. 14. Next, place the Ten of Hearts, which represents the rush of joy and elation that comes with falling in love. 15. It speaks to the spark and excitement of a new romantic connection, the thrill before deeper bonds take root. 16. Finally, set down the Seven of Hearts, the card of complete emotional fulfillment and lasting partnership. 17. This is the ultimate destination of the spell: a deep, enduring love. 18. Once all three cards are in place, surround the candle and cards with a circle of red rose petals. 19. As you form the circle, speak these words: 20. "Wreathed around with roses red, I summon now the love I'll wed." 21. After completing the spell, take practical steps to support the magick. 22. Seek out social gatherings, pursue shared interests, and let others know you are open to a romantic relationship. 23. The spell helps draw the right person to you, but you must meet the universe halfway.
◇Description
A playing card love spell using the Five, Ten, and Seven of Hearts within a circle of red rose petals, cast during the waxing moon to invite romance and deep emotional fulfillment into your life.
