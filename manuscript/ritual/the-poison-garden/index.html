☠️The Poison Garden

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Garden
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
Some or all of: aconite (Aconitum napellus), deadly nightshade (Atropa belladonna), thorn apple (Datura stramonium), hellebore (Veratum album), henbane (Hyoscyamus niger), mandrake, oleander (Nerium oleander) A bench
◇Directions
1. Plant some or all of the listed plants in an enclosed garden. 2. Place a bench in a shady spot. 3. Sit and absorb their protection, taking care not to absorb their poison.
◇Description
⚠️ These plants are dangerous and toxic; if children or animals can reach your garden, this spell is neither safe nor appropriate. Perhaps the ultimate protective garden, it is filled with plants that can genuinely harm a careless trespasser and that, on a metaphysical level alone, produce a powerful aura of protection. Witches, sorceresses, herbalists, pharmacists and botanists once kept small, enclosed, gated poison gardens.
