🐰Ostara🌸Spring📅Spring Equinox

A short equinox-themed self-assessment meditation to take inventory of life areas and restore balance by consciously noticing opposites such as work and play, stress and peace, and refocusing attention on blessings.

Quantities

No materials required, seated meditation with optional audio recording or reader

Directions

  1. Begin by getting comfortable in your chosen spot, then start the meditation.
  2. If you are recording the meditation to play back aloud, or having someone read it to you, start here: Turn off the world around you and tune in to yourself.
  3. Turn off any noises coming from anywhere else.
  4. Turn off your thoughts about the day.
  5. Turn off any distractions you may have.
  6. Feel yourself relax and unwind.
  7. Find your center and focus on being in the here and now.
  8. Nothing else matters right now.
  9. Everything else can wait.
  10. Right now is about you and your spirituality.
  11. Right now is for you.
  12. With the equinox comes a time of balance, a time to step back and examine your life.
  13. How in balance is your life?
  14. Do you ever feel like things are out of your control?
  15. Do you feel overwhelmed, frustrated, or just plain anxious?
  16. These are all signs that something is off‑kilter in your life, something is out of whack and needs to be realigned, rebalanced.
  17. Everything has its opposite, just as the day has the night.
  18. Everything in your life has its opposite too.
  19. The work you do has to be counteracted with play.
  20. The taking with giving.
  21. The bad times with good.
  22. If you have too much of one thing and not enough of its counterpart, you are not in balance.
  23. Even having all good times in your life throws you off.
  24. Having bad times mixed in with the good teaches you empathy and compassion.
  25. Constant good times without any bad leaves you missing out on parts of life.
  26. They may not be fun parts of life, but they are necessary.
  27. Eventually everyone will have something bad come into their life.
  28. When you are not used to it, it can make those hard times extremely difficult to deal with.
  29. People who are used to the hard times are better equipped to deal with them simply from the practice.
  30. Spend some time thinking about all of the things you have going on in your life.
  31. Are there equal amounts of good and bad, work and play, stress and peace, or are you off balance with something?
  32. What do you do for fun?
  33. What do you do when something upsets you?
  34. How often do things upset you?
  35. Is your life pretty calm and serene, or do you find yourself jumping from crisis to crisis on an almost daily basis?
  36. (Allow silent pause to reflect.) Perhaps you need help in dealing with stress.
  37. Perhaps you find yourself worrying about things you really have no control over.
  38. Maybe you take on more than you can handle and then stress out when things do not go the way you had hoped they would.
  39. Perhaps you are out of work, injured, or suffering from some sort of illness.
  40. Whatever the “bad” things are in your life, you know they are there.
  41. You need to learn to correctly identify the problem, then find workable solutions for them.
  42. Generally, if you are too stressed out, you do not have enough fun and play in your life.
  43. This goes both ways.
  44. When you do not take the time to have fun and play, you do not get the opportunity to blow off some steam, and so you are more stressed.
  45. Always remember to fit in some good times to balance out the ones that make your life difficult.
  46. It is important to remember that life has its ups and downs.
  47. People do not want to be sad or miserable or hurt, but it is important to remember there are lessons to be learned from the events and situations that lead to these feelings.
  48. What are some of the good things going on in your life?
  49. Do you have a supportive spouse or significant other, great friends, a good job?
  50. Do you do volunteer work to help those who are less fortunate than you?
  51. (Allow silent pause for reflection.) Reminding yourself of your blessings is an excellent way to bring balance into a stressful life that often feels filled with negativity.
  52. We often forget the good and focus on the bad.
  53. When you count your blessings, you may find you do have balance in your life, the bad really does not outweigh the good.
  54. You may find it is simply where your focus lies that needs to be adjusted.
  55. (Allow brief pause for reflection.) When ready, give silent thanks for the blessings in your life.
  56. Remember that when things seem bad, you can always return to focusing on your blessings in this way to achieve greater balance.
  57. Take a couple deep breaths, remind yourself of where you physically are right now, then gently open your eyes.
