To encourage contentment and help you focus on your blessings rather than problems. Three variations provided. Note: This potion will not treat depression, which requires medical treatment.

Quantities

Earl Grey tea and fresh lemon or lemon peel (Version 1)
Black tea and a strip of tangerine peel (Version 2)
Lemon balm tea and a slice of dried apple (Version 3)

Directions

  1. Choose one of the three versions below and brew the tea.
  2. Add that version's extra ingredient.
  3. Stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the shape of Wunjo (ᚹ), the rune of joy, into the surface of the tea.
  4. As you drink, visualize contentment filling your heart: appreciating all the good things in your life, finding reasons to smile, and feeling genuinely happy.
  5. Eat the dried apple after drinking, if using Version 3.

These tea potions encourage contentment and help you focus on your blessings rather than your problems. Three variations are provided.

Important Note: These potions will not treat depression. Depression is a medical condition that requires medical treatment. These potions are for encouraging a positive outlook, not for treating mental illness.

Version 1: Earl Grey with Lemon

Brew Earl Grey tea. Add a squeeze of fresh lemon or a strip of lemon peel. Do not add milk, as it will curdle with the citrus.

Version 2: Black Tea with Tangerine

Brew black tea. Add a strip of tangerine peel (the colorful outer zest).

Version 3: Lemon Balm with Dried Apple

Brew lemon balm tea. Add a slice of dried apple.
