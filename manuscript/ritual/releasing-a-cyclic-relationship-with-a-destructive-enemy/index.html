🔓Releasing a Cyclic Relationship with a Destructive Enemy

An Ozark retribution ritual to break the magickal back-and-forth cycle with a longstanding enemy. This spell assists in finally severing whatever ties hold you to your enemy so you can both move on and heal. Fire consumes and dissolves the connection completely. Dispose of ashes at a four-way crossroads or atop a high place.

Quantities

  • Photo of target and/or identifying materials (hair, nail clippings, clothing, etc.)
  • Black or red pen or marker (optional)
  • Newspaper
  • Firepit or grill (or heat-safe metal container, cauldron, or flowerpot)
  • Charcoal briquets or wood for fire
  • Lighter or matches
  • Juniper berries, dried and crushed, or juniper essential oil
  • Camphor essential oil or resin
  • Black taper candle, any size (optional)

Directions

  1. Begin with the photo of your enemy and any other identifying materials you have from them.
  2. If you do not have a photo, write their full name (FIRST MIDDLE LAST), date of birth, and the symbols of their Sun, Moon, and rising zodiac signs on a piece of paper.
  3. Include as much information as you know.
  4. Fold these materials into three layers of newspaper to form a parcel.
  5. Light a fire in a firepit or grill outside.
  6. You can use wood or heat up charcoal briquets.
  7. If you cannot have a fire, burn your materials outside in a heat-safe metal stockpot, cauldron, or flowerpot instead.
  8. To purify the fire, throw on your juniper berries.
  9. If using a container, anoint the inside with seven drops of juniper essential oil.
  10. Take your newspaper parcel and stand so that the fire or container is in front of you as you face the west.
  11. Anoint the parcel with camphor essential oil.
  12. If you prefer to use resin, include it inside the parcel itself.
  13. Repeat the verbal charm, then toss the parcel onto the fire or coals.
  14. You, treacherous one
    Go into the fire!
    You, deceitful one
    Go into the wind!
    As fire burns everything to ash
    Be burned up and forget me!
    May all memories and thoughts of me
    Be burned up, be blown away!
    Blown away into the ten winds
    Blown away into the west
    Where all my enemies forever live!
  15. If you are not using a fire, light the parcel using the black candle, then drop it into your heat-safe container.
  16. As the parcel burns, visualize your enemy standing in front of you.
  17. Piece by piece, their entire body disappears into the west, blown away by the magickal winds.
  18. Let everything burn up completely to ash.
  19. Once cool, collect some of the ashes from the fire, or all of them if you used a different container.
  20. Take them off your property and dispose of them at a four-way crossroads or on top of a high place like a cliffside, mountain, bluff, or even the roof of a tall building.
