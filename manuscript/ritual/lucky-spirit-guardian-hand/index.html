🧜Lucky Spirit Guardian Hand

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Herbal MagickDevotional Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
1 galangal root 1 matched pair of lodestones 1 four-leaf clover (real or replica charm) 7 tonka beans 1 Saint Anthony medal (optional) 1 fork-tailed mermaid charm (optional) 1 conjure bag
◇Directions
1. Place the galangal root, matched pair of lodestones, four-leaf clover, and seven tonka beans into a conjure bag. 2. Add a medal of Saint Anthony and/or a charm depicting a fork-tailed mermaid. 3. Carry the sealed hand when gambling.
◇Description
A conjure hand invoking divine protection for gamblers, assembled from galangal root and lodestones paired with a four-leaf clover, tonka beans, a Saint Anthony medal, and a fork-tailed mermaid charm. ⚠️ This working draws on Hoodoo tradition. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.
