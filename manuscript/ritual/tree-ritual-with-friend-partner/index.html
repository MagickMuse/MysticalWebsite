🌳Tree Ritual with Friend Partner

An any bright morning spell for tree ritual with friend, partner, or family member, winding ropes clockwise around tree then unlooping counterclockwise to release power for wish to be won.

Quantities

  • Two thick environmentally friendly cords or ropes long enough to wrap around a tree

Directions

  1. Stand on either side of the tree and begin winding the ropes clockwise around the tree so that they overlap, looping them together in a loose knot on the trunk.
  2. Leave sufficient length so that you can each move away from the tree and hold your rope taut.
  3. As you wind, say: "Empowering tree, rise into me, knot binding, knot finding, knot winding. Powerful we'll be, success we'll see." Very quickly, slip the knot and unloop the cords counterclockwise, yelling: "The power is free, the power's in me, and so you see, my/our wish [your wish at the same time whether a joint wish such as healing an absent person or preserving a threatened area of land or wildlife habitat or separate personal wishes] shall be." Let go of the ropes so that they fall on the ground, shouting: "As we count three, two, one, the wish is won." Knot the wish cords side by side on the tree.
  4. Perform any bright morning.
