🌪️Uranus Persuade Radical Necessary Change

A Saturday Uranus's day spell for Uranus ritual to persuade family or colleagues to make radical but necessary change, cutting paper figures with bright blue pen and blowing with electric fan for winds of change to embrace new things in pastures new.

Quantities

  • White paper and a bright blue pen, the color of Uranus
  • Scissors
  • An electric table fan

Directions

  1. Partly open the windows in the room so a gentle breeze enters, saying: "Change winds do blow; inertia, you must go. Life shall alter, yet [name those resistant to change] still falter, always saying no." Close the windows and sit near one, drawing and cutting out paper figures, and naming aloud a figure for each resistant person.
  2. Place the figures next to the fan, saying: "Winds of change shall blow, and forward must we go. So life can flourish in pastures new, embracing new things for me and you [again name the resistant folk(s)]." Switch on the fan, repeating the spell words, and let the figures blow where they will.
  3. Turn off the fan, pick up the figures, and partly open the window again so the breeze enters but the figures are not blown away.
  4. Hide the figures in a room where the people sit.
  5. Perform Saturday, Uranus's day (you can do this at home to change a work situation).
