A fidelity charm made from dried herbs mixed with salt and placed in a blue bag, hung over the marital bed to keep love focused and faithful.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Three or four of the following lasting fidelity herbs, dried: yarrow, rosemary, sage, basil, caraway seeds, or thyme
  • A small bowl of salt
  • A blue drawstring bag
  • A bowl and spoon

Directions

  1. Slowly mix a small quantity of your chosen herbs in the bowl with three pinches of salt, saying softly and continuously: "Earth, air, water, fire, for me alone my love's desire. Fire, water, earth, and air, may he only for me care." Continue mixing and chanting faster.
  2. When you feel the power in the herbs pulsating, mix more slowly and chant more softly until your words fall into silence and the mixing is still.
  3. Put the mixture into the bag, close it, and hold it, repeating the words nine times.
  4. Wash away any spare herbs under running water.
  5. Hang the bag over the marital bed until it loses its fragrance and then renew it.
  6. Tip the old herbs to the four winds.
