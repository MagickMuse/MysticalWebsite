🕯️Past Lives Meditation

A meditation to explore past lives using a black mirror, gaining insight into past issues and events affecting the present. Since time is a spiral rather than linear, future lives or lives in other places may also appear. Best performed at Samhain when the veil between the worlds is thinnest.

Quantities

  • Black mirror (propped on table or Altar)
  • Black cloth to cover the mirror
  • Chair
  • 1 black candle (pillar, votive, or taper) as working candle
  • Drum or rattle (optional)
  • Incense: frankincense, sandalwood, or copal
    Optional bath preparation:
  • Muslin pouch with herbs (rosemary, basil, thyme, valerian)
  • Candles for bath

Directions

  1. Preparation:
  2. May first take a warm, candlelit bath scented with herbs (rosemary, basil, thyme, valerian) tied in a muslin pouch. Dress in comfortable clothes or whatever feels natural.
  3. Be in a place where you will not be disturbed, and have the light off.
  4. Light candle in front of the mirror and the incense.
    ---
    ### Entering Meditation
  5. Begin meditation by gazing into the mirror past the flame, while sitting and facing North. (Meditation may be prerecorded as desired.) Enter meditative state.
  6. Envision the votive candle as a beacon to wisdom:
    This candle glows to light the path into Darkness.
    ---
    ### The Mirror Working
  7. Gaze into the mirror, past your own eyes. Drum or shake rattle if desired:
    I call upon the Dark and the Past; the Ancient of Days in the Realm of Repast. Unleash to my sight the paths I have roamed; and show me the forms of lives I have known.
  8. Continue to chant the rhyme, shaking rattle or drumming without listening to it or paying attention to the motion. Focus on the mirror and watch as the face changes into those of past lives.
    ---
    ### Closing
  9. When done, if still drumming or shaking a rattle, stop:
    The memories of past and future are shown; the faces of me in Time are now known. Blessings I give to the Veiled One of Night; for through this mask am I given the Sight.
  10. Cover the mirror with a black cloth and snuff the candle.
    Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
