🔮Child Thumbnail Scrying Oracle

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• A small amount of oil
◇Directions
1. Place a small amount of oil on a child's thumb. 2. Polish the nail until it is smooth and shiny, which the child may do themselves. 3. Have the child gaze at the nail. 4. Either pose your question aloud or allow the child to freely describe what they see without being told the specific question. 5. It is not necessary for the child to understand what is being asked.
◇Description
A thumbnail oil scrying method using a child as the oracular medium. The child gazes at the polished nail and describes what they see. It is not necessary for the child to understand the question. ⚠️ Historical method involving a child as oracle. Must be entirely voluntary, framed as play, and stopped immediately at the child's request. See the Children correspondence page for full ethical context.
