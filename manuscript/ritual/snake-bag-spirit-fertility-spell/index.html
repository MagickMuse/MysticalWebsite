🐍Snake Bag Spirit Fertility Spell

◇Form
Altar WorkingCharged Object
◇Method

◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Haitian Vodou
◇Quantities
A snake bag A snake altar A central serpentine image Coiled snake candles Dishes of milk and small treasures Doll babies Charms
◇Directions
1. As you assemble the snake bag, build a snake altar at the same time. 2. Choose a central serpentine image: the Minoan snake goddess, Mami Waters, a naga, Maitresse Ezili in her water-snake form, another of the serpentine lwas, or an image of an actual snake such as a photograph, candle, or statue. 3. Use whatever speaks to you with genuine resonance. 4. Light coiled snake candles on the altar. 5. Offer the snakes dishes of milk and small treasures. 6. Place doll babies on the altar to anchor your desire in physical form. 7. Leave your charms and assembled snake bag on the altar overnight to absorb the spirits' attention. 8. If the snakes move you to dance, follow that impulse.
◇Description
The snake bag working taken further through direct spirit petition. A snake altar is built alongside the bag and used both as a focal point for the working and as a charging station for the bag and its contents. The spirits invoked span multiple traditions; choose images that resonate genuinely. ⚠️ Haitian Vodou is a closed initiatory tradition. The lwas referenced here, including Mami Waters and Maitresse Ezili, belong to living spiritual communities. Approach with respect and do not treat these figures as interchangeable tools.
