✍️Instant Tapping Balance Spell

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Chant/MantraEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A pen to be used only for tapping
◇Directions
1. Tap the pen on a surface gently, building up a slow regular rhythm, one and two and three, one, two, three, four or whatever rhythm feels right. 2. When you have established your rhythm, chant softly aloud in time: "May peace embrace this entire place, tranquillity too, flowing through, calmness now enfolds all as I do call this tapping into being." Keep the pen in your workspace, and whenever tensions rise, start tapping the rhythm with the pen softly. 3. If tensions run high, chant in your head the spell words along with the tapping. 4. Perform Friday evening initially at home and then whenever needed at work.
◇Description
A Friday evening instant tapping spell for restoring balance when workplace tempers run high or impossible deadlines cause tension, establishing a rhythm with a pen to call peace and tranquility.
