🌿Restore Life Urban Wasteland Spell

A morning spell to restore life to littered or abandoned urban wasteland, circling picture with incense smoke and planting herbs to make abandoned lands grow lovely with flowers.

Quantities

  • A pot of your favorite cooking herb
  • Any floral incense stick
  • A photo of the area with the pot in the center

Directions

  1. Light the incense stick and circle the picture nine times, making clockwise smoke spirals, saying softly: "May beauty be restored, what is abandoned cared for, and the waste-littered lands grow lovely with flowers once more." Write the same words while saying them slowly in incense smoke in the air over the pot of herbs and the picture.
  2. Let the incense burn through.
  3. When you have time, plant the pot of herbs in or near the unloved place, and using gloves and a stick, pick up some litter, repeating the words.
  4. Perform if possible, after morning rain.
