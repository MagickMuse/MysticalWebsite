🌍Multicultural Community Harmony

A new week/month ritual mixing six types of nuts or seeds, shaking six times, rotating both directions, scattering freely for unity.

Quantities

  • 6 small tubs of different edible nuts or seeds (almonds, coriander, cumin, hazelnuts, pine nuts, poppy, sesame, sunflower, walnuts, or other)
  • 1 fine-mesh sieve or strainer with handle (or clear container)
  • 1 big ceramic or wooden bowl

Directions

  1. Add some seeds or nuts from each tub to the sieve in turn, saying six times, "Though we have different views, all are one family, alike in wanting harmony. And so I offer friendship and seek your friendship too." Shake the sieve six times saying six times, "United in one community, our needs are not the same. But wanting to live peacefully, that can be our aim." Pour the nuts and seeds into the bowl and gently rotate it six times in both directions, saying just once, "We all have much to learn as we live side by side, for we are one community and so can share it peacefully." Scatter the nuts and seeds freely outdoors until the mix is gone, saying, "One family, one community, in unity and harmony." Perform the beginning of a new week or month.
