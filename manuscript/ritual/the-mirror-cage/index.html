🪞The Mirror Cage

◇Form
Poppet
◇Method
Mirror MagickReversalBindingInvocationProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Required: - Small box with lid (coffin-shaped recommended) - Mirror shards (enough to line interior of box) - Wax figure or fabric doll for poppet - Spanish moss (for fabric doll stuffing) - Appropriate herbs (for fabric doll stuffing) - Personal link or name paper - Black or purple candle (optional, for altar work) Optional materials if available: - Article used in original bewitchment (powder, conjure hand) - Personal link to target (hair, cloth)
◇Directions
1. Prepare the box by lining the entire interior with mirror shards, covering as much surface as possible. 2. Create a poppet to represent your enemy using either a wax figure candle of appropriate gender or a fabric doll stuffed with Spanish moss and appropriate protective herbs. 3. If you have an article used in the original bewitchment (such as powder or a conjure hand) or a personal link to the target (hair, cloth), work it into the doll. 4. If you lack these, include a name paper inside the doll or carve the target's name onto it. 5. If the offending sorcerer's identity is unknown, write "The one who works against me" on the paper. 6. Hold the doll in your left hand. 7. With your right hand, trace a cross over the doll. 8. As you trace the vertical arm of the cross, say: "I baptize thee [name of target]." If you don't know the name, substitute the word "shadow." As you make the horizontal arm of the cross, say: "Thou art in my power." Place the doll into the mirror-lined box and speak this invocation: "[Name of target], for your own sake I pray you no more trouble make. For to torment me for your own gain will only bring you greater pain. For I am a child of Hekate and she is mightier than thee. From this hour forth all ill intent shall back upon thy head be sent. In the name of Neboutosaoaleth, Ereshkigal, and Aktiophis, and by my own word and will, thou shall be thine own victim." Close the box and place it in a safe location on your altar or bury it at a crossroads. 9. The spell creates a mirror effect that reflects anything the sender directs at you, whether good or ill, back upon themselves. 10. The box should remain undisturbed once sealed and placed.
◇Description
A reversal spell using a mirror-lined box and poppet to reflect all harmful magick back to the sender, invoking Hekate and underworld deities to bind the effect.
