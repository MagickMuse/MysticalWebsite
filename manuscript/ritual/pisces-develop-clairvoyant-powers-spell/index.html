🐟Pisces Develop Clairvoyant Powers Spell

A February 19 through March 20 or need Pisces psychic powers boost spell for Pisces ritual if you want to develop clairvoyant powers, dripping red and purple candle wax onto water to see visions and symbols for future messages and past worlds.

Quantities

  • A red candle, the kind that drips wax
  • A heatproof bowl, half-filled with water
  • A purple candle, the kind that drips wax

Directions

  1. Set the red candle to the left of the bowl and light it, saying: "Lead me to the unknown ways, that as I in this water gaze, visions will see that will show me, what most I need to know." Light the second candle from the first and set it to the right of the bowl, saying: "Let me view wondrous scenes, past worlds where once my life has been, or future dreams." Holding a candle in each hand, begin to drip wax from both slowly onto the water surface.
  2. As swirls of liquid wax mix on the water surface, you will see a picture or symbol that afterwards will become a message in the form of words or images in your mind.
  3. When the wax sets, empty the water, drying the solidified wax.
  4. Place it next to any cards you read or crystals you use.
  5. Perform February 19 through March 20, or whenever you need Pisces the Fish to give your psychic powers a boost.
