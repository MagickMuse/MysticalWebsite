❄️Snowflake Tea Light

◇Form
Charged ObjectAltar Working
◇Method
ManifestationDevotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Size H/8 5mm crochet hook - 104 yards (2 oz) white sparkle 4-ply yarn - Scissors - LED tea light (no real candles)
◇Directions
1. Crochet Abbreviations: Ch = Chain | St = Stitch | Hdc = Half double crochet | Dc = Double crochet | Trc = Triple crochet | Picot = Ch 3, slip stitch into 3rd chain from hook | Sl St = Slip stitch | Rev Sc = Reverse single crochet | BLO = Back loops only | FO = Fasten off 2. Instructions: Round 1: Ch 3, 12 Dc in 3rd Ch from hook Round 2: In BLO, 1 Hdc in each stitch around Round 3: Rev Sc around, FO and weave in the end 3. Attach yarn with Sl St to any unused loop from Round 2. {Ch 3, Dc, Trc in same St, picot, Trc, Dc, Ch 2 Sl St in next St, Sl St in next St} Repeat 5 more times. FO and weave ends. 4. Place LED tea light in center. 5. Use on your Yule altar or in a window to let your light shine for others to see.
◇Description
A crocheted white sparkle snowflake holder for LED tea lights. Festivals of light have long been how folks pass the time and keep hope alive during the darkest season. This snowflake tea light stands for the hope of the return of life to the earth.
