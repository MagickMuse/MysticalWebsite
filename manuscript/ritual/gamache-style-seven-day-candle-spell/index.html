🕯️Gamache-Style Seven-Day Candle Spell

A complex seven-day uncrossing candle ritual inspired by Henri Gamache (author of The Master Book of Candle Burning), combining fire and water. Uses 8 candles minimum (3 white, 1 black, 2 cross candles, 1 black seven-day, 1 white seven-day) and at least 3 dressing oils (Blessing, Confusion, and Commanding). Daily hex-breaking baths with rotating uncrossing ingredients and daily reading from the Book of Job.

Quantities

3 white candles
1 black candle
2 cross candles
1 black seven-day candle
1 white seven-day candle
Blessing Oil
Confusion Oil
Commanding Oil
Uncrossing Oil, Black Cat Oil, Four Thieves Vinegar (for rotating baths)

Directions

  1. Place four candles on an altar, three white and one black, lighting the black candle last.
  2. Place two cross candles dressed with Blessing Oil on the back corners.
  3. Dress a black seven-day candle with Confusion Oil and a white seven-day candle with a Commanding Oil.
  4. While the candles burn, take a hex-breaking bath.
  5. The seven-day candles may burn around the clock; the other candles burn only while the person is bathing, then are extinguished.
  6. Use a different uncrossing oil or ingredient in the bath each day, including Black Cat Oil, Four Thieves Vinegar, and Uncrossing Oil.
  7. Read verses from the Book of Job daily.
  8. The crossed condition should be removed by the end of seven days.
