Mabon

The Second Harvest sabbat ceremony celebrating the fruits, nuts, and vines of the season. Features the Wheel of the Year meditation, elemental quarter honors, and blessing of charitable offerings for those in need.

⚠️ Sweetgrass is a sacred material in many Indigenous traditions. Consider myrrh or pine alone as alternatives if you are outside those cultures.

Quantities

  • Wine, russet, brown, or orange-brown altar candles
  • Myrrh, pine, sage, or sweetgrass incense
  • Fall leaves, flowers, grapes, berries, vines, acorns, and corn for decoration
  • Canned goods for donation
  • Loaf of nutbread on a plate
  • Cup of wine
  • Wand
  • Bell (optional)
  • Cakes & Wine: currant or dark grape wine, nutbread, fruit pies, smoked or roasted poultry, hearty bean soup

Directions

  1. Cast a circle using your preferred method.
  2. Light the incense and carry it around the circle deosil.
  3. Clap or ring a bell three times and say:
  4. This day I celebrate the Second Harvest, that of fruits, nuts, and the vines, and I remember those who struggle without.
  5. As I accept the gifts of the Lord and the Lady, I remember that what was sown is now reaped, and this is the time of offerings, payment of dues, and enjoyment of just rewards.
  6. So, too, do I pass along what I may to those who have need.
  7. Clap or ring the bell three times:
  8. The Wheel of the Year is ever turning, through Sun Tides and Moon Tides, through seasons and harvests, for plants, for animals, and for people; for all life moves within the Wheel of the Year from life to death to life again.
  9. The balance and the harmony of the dance of our life is the spiral dance of Energy Eternal, lead by the God guiding us.
  10. He yearly travels the path of Nature on our behalf that we know and not fear the cycles of our being, for balance and harmony in the motion of life are His truths.
  11. Hold up the plate of nut bread and cup of wine:
  12. I ask the blessing of the Lady and the Lord, upon this food that the harvest be bountiful.
  13. Set the plate down and clap or ring the bell three times:
  14. The Lord of Shadows rules in His Shadowland, yet His love holds true, and with Him my own dance will one day move the Other Way.
  15. As this harvest season moves onward to the last harvest, I call upon the Lady and the Lord to bless this beautiful season and bless my life within it, that my life be the harvest of the Goddess and the God.
  16. Take your wand and honor the Elementals deosil, pausing at each quarter:
  17. [N]: Hail to thee, Elemental Earth!
  18. Your steadfastness helps me to maintain the things of my home, my health, my security, and my comfort!
  19. We are kith and kin, and I honor you!
  20. [E]: Hail to thee, Elemental Air!
  21. Your inspiration helps me to learn and understand!
  22. We are kith and kin, and I honor you!
  23. [S]: Hail to thee, Elemental Fire!
  24. Your energy helps me with the drive I need to accomplish my goals!
  25. We are kith and kin, and I honor you!
  26. [W]: Hail to thee, Elemental Water!
  27. Your gentle flow helps me maintain calm and balance in my relationships with others!
  28. We are kith and kin, and I honor you!
  29. Touch the canned goods with your wand at the altar:
  30. In the names of the Lord and the Lady, and with the aid of the Elementals, I bless these fruits of the harvest for those who are in need.
  31. I offer aid and comfort to those whose needs arise throughout the turning of the Wheel.
  32. May the Goddess and the God add Their blessing to these offerings, to the one who gives, and to the one who receives.
  33. So Mote It Be!
  34. Clap or ring the bell three times.
  35. Proceed to Cakes & Wine, then close the circle.
