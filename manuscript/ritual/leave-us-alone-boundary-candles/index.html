🕯️Leave-Us-Alone Boundary Candles

Silver Candle

A leave-us-alone spell if your partner's best friend or yours always tags along, using entwined candles to set boundaries while maintaining friendship.

Quantities

  • 1 red or white entwined lovers' candle (if possible) or two matching red candles in matching holders, side by side
  • 1 small brown or gray candle, placed either close to the right of the entwined lovers' candle or squeezed between the matching candles

Directions

  1. Light the entwined or twin candles, saying: "Two's company, three's a crowd. Please give us space, [name], because that's allowed." Light the friend's candle, name the friend, and say: "Welcome you are, but not 24/7. Give us a break, that would be heaven." Now move the friend's candle as far as possible away from yours, blow it out, and relight it, saying: "Your light is too bright, it dazzles our sight. So sometimes keep your distance, can't you feel our resistance? See you again, but not too soon. That way good friends we can remain, no more strain." Let the candles burn through.
