◇Directions
1. Consecrate a black candle and light it. 2. Burn an image of the target in the candle's flame. 3. The image may be a wax sigil, photograph, drawing, or personal item. 4. As the image burns, recite the incantation.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A general-purpose curse invoking the four elements and the four watchtowers. The practitioner summons fear, guilt, and suffering upon the target by burning an image of the subject in the flame of a consecrated black candle while reciting an incantation that calls threefold retribution. The image may be a wax sigil, photograph, drawing, or personal item.

Incantation

There has been unfairness done to me.
I summon the elements,
I invoke them,
I conjure them to do my bidding.
The four watchtowers shall lay their eyes and mind.
There shall be fear and guilt and bad blood,
there shall be submission and no pity.
I point the threefold law against thee,
against thee it shall be pointed.
Threefold, a hundredfold is the cost of my anger.
Thee shall be blinded by the fear,
blinded by the pain,
blinded by me,
blinded by me,
cursed by me.
So mote it be.
