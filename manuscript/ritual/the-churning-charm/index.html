🫙The Churning Charm

🫙Jar

A traditional butter-making ritual invoking Brigid's blessing on the churn. The churning charm is chanted throughout the process, combining domestic craft with sacred invocation. The finished butter is offered in part to the house faeries.

Quantities

1 cup heavy cream (preferably organic); salt (optional); 1 lidded jar (2-cup capacity or larger); 1 fine strainer

Directions

  1. Let the cream come to room temperature before you begin, this can take 2 to 4 hours.
  2. Do not rush.
  3. When you are ready, invoke Brigid aloud or in your heart, asking her to bless your task and be present with you.
  4. Begin shaking the jar.
  5. As you shake, chant the churning charm:
  6. "Come, thou Brigid, goddess calm,
    Hasten the butter on the cream,
    Thou who bless my hearth and home,
    From humble floor to high roof beam:
    Come, ye rich lumps, come!
    Thou who put light in moon and sun,
    Thou who put fish in stream and sea,
    Thou who put food in flock and herd,
    Send sweet butter now to me!"
  7. Continue shaking and chanting.
  8. The cream will thicken, then begin to separate into butter solids and buttermilk.
  9. When you see a thinner liquid pulling away from the solids, carefully pour it off through the strainer.
  10. Shake again until the butter has fully come and no further change is happening.
  11. Pour off all remaining buttermilk.
  12. Transfer the butter to a bowl.
  13. Add ice-cold water to wash the butter and strain again.
  14. Repeat two or three times until the water runs clear.
  15. (Skip this step if you plan to eat the butter immediately.)
  16. Salt if desired.
  17. Set aside a small portion for the house faeries, leave it by the hearth or at the back door.
  18. Give thanks to Brigid for all her delicious gifts.
