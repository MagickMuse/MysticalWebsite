🌳Hand-Parting Tree Cord Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Cord MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A dark red cord about three to six and a half yards long - A tree
◇Directions
1. Wind the cord around a tree nine times, securing it with a very loose knot, looping the other end over your hands. 2. Slowly unwind the cord from the tree, moving in circles farther and farther from the tether, chanting: "Hand part, my heart; hand fast, not last; let go, unwind, unbind, untie, release, too tight bonds now must cease." With a final tug, pull the knot free, unloop your hand, and as you do so, say: "Unbound, unwound, hand parted, and so life begins anew; the ties are through." Loop the cord over the tree so that it will decay naturally.
◇Description
A hand-parting spell performed the hour before sunset, unwinding a red cord from a tree nine times to symbolically release bonds and begin life anew.
