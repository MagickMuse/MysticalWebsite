A comprehensive garden protection spell using nine bamboo with red knotted strings and four corner sentinels to guard property boundaries.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 9 bamboo canes or plants
  • 9 red strings
  • 4 protective plants: cactus, orange or lemon trees, hawthorn, sage, rosemary bushes, lilac, or honeysuckle
  • Optional window box herbs: basil, catnip, chamomile, cumin, dill, parsley, rosemary, sage, thyme, vetiver, or wild garlic

Directions

  1. Plant a cactus or cool weather bush at each corner of the house outdoors (as near as possible if you are attached to another house), saying, "Stand sentinel for me against all danger, from malevolent foe or malicious stranger." Inside put cacti in the indoor corners, including the balcony and just outside your apartment's entrance.
  2. Insert nine bamboo cane or bamboo plants along your perimeter fence.
  3. Tie scarlet string from each of the bamboo, knotted nine times, saying the same words.
  4. If you do not have a garden, plant a protective window box outside your apartment with basil, catnip (very lucky), chamomile, cumin, dill, parsley, rosemary, sage, thyme, vetiver, or wild garlic.
  5. Use the same spell words.
  6. Perform Saturday afternoon.
