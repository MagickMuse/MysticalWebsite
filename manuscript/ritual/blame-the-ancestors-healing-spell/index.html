🏺Blame the Ancestors Healing Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Ancestor WorkHealing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
Paper offerings (Bank of Hell cash, checkbooks, or credit cards) Offering food (something substantial; feed them well) Ancestral altar materials as needed Divination tools (optional)
◇Directions
1. According to traditional Chinese philosophy, some illnesses and misfortunes, especially those of a chronic or resistant nature, may be caused by Ancestral Spirits who are aggrieved or depleted. 2. The remedy is to restore their energy and honor your obligations to them. 3. Begin by feeding your ancestors well. 4. Prepare a meaningful food offering and present it with care and sincerity. 5. Next, consider whether any ancestral grave needs tending. 6. If you are able to visit and improve the site, do so. 7. If the grave is beyond reach, unknown, or does not exist, explain this directly to the Ancestors in full detail. 8. Tell them what you intend to do as compensation for this inability. 9. Divination may also be useful at this stage, to receive the Ancestors' own perspective on what is needed. 10. Build or reinforce an ancestral altar. 11. This creates a dedicated space for ongoing communication, offerings, and relationship with those who came before you. 12. Burn paper offerings on their behalf. 13. Traditionally this is Bank of Hell cash, though Bank of Hell checkbooks and credit cards are now also available and appropriate. 14. Burning these transfers the energy of the gifts to the spirit realm. 15. If the illness or misfortune resists all efforts and conventional diagnosis has failed, consider seeking a shamanic diagnosis to determine the specific nature of the ancestral disruption and what further steps may be required.
◇Description
A traditional Chinese-influenced ancestral healing working for chronic illness or persistent misfortune attributed to neglected or under-nourished Ancestral Spirits. The ancestors are not malevolent; they simply lack sufficient energy to protect you from harm.
