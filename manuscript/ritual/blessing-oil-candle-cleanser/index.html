🕯️Blessing Oil Candle Cleanser

◇Solitary or Group

◇Quantities
- Frankincense (ground resin or essential oil) - Benzoin (ground resin or absolute) - Olive oil - Jojoba oil - White candle(s) - Blue candle(s)
◇Directions
1. Create Blessing Oil by adding frankincense and benzoin to a blend of olive and jojoba oils. 2. Use either the ground resin or essential oils. 3. Dress white and blue candles with the oil and burn to purify the atmosphere.
◇Description
Blessing Oil is made by adding frankincense and benzoin to a blend of olive and jojoba oils. White and blue candles are dressed with the oil and burned to purify the atmosphere, delivering the cleansing effects of fire without smoke or heavy fragrance.
