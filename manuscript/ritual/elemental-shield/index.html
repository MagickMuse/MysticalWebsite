A versatile shield technique using any of the four elements (Fire, Water, Air, Earth). Each elemental shield provides defense against its opposing element and enhances specific qualities: Fire boosts energy and will, Water enhances understanding and wisdom, Air increases intelligence and cunning, Earth improves grounding and centering. Choose the element based on your needs or the type of attack you face.

Quantities

  • Required:
    None (psychic technique only)
    Skills Needed:
  • Visualization
  • Energy work
  • Elemental awareness
  • Knowledge of elemental correspondences

Directions

  1. Choose which element you wish to work with based on the elemental correspondences:
  2. Fire: Red, hot, dry, expansive. Defends against Water attacks, boosts energy and firm will.
    Water: Blue, cool, wet, fluid. Defends against Fire attacks, enhances understanding and wisdom.
    Air: Yellow, warm, wet, rapid movement. Defends against Earth attacks, boosts intelligence and cunning.
    Earth: Brown, cool, dry, dense, heavy. Defends against Air attacks, enhances grounding and centering.
  3. Concentrate on the color and qualities of your chosen element.
  4. As you inhale, will yourself to breathe in that element from the space around you.
  5. As you exhale, see your body filling up with the element's color and energy.
  6. Make a fist with your left hand and place it over your heart.
  7. Cover it with your right hand and apply about five pounds of pressure.
  8. See the elemental energy gather at your heart in the appropriate color, concentrated and focused.
  9. Release the pressure and project the colored sphere out into an egg shape around you, stopping at roughly one to two feet from your physical body.
  10. See that elemental energy forming a shell around you, glowing in its color and carrying its specific qualities.
  11. Duration: The effects of the shield will wear off in a few hours unless continually fed with imagination and will.
  12. To Dissolve: Inhale deeply, then exhale and see the shield dissolve into space.
  13. Important Note: Elemental shields can cause unusual reactions in others.
  14. Use them only when you feel that you need them.
