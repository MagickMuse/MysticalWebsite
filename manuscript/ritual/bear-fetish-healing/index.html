A Zuni-influenced shamanic healing practice using a bear figurine or stone fetish as a healing companion and advisor. The fetish is chosen intuitively, carried constantly, slept with, and fed regular offerings. White bears and turquoise-carved bears are considered especially powerful. Pay special attention to bear dreams during illness.

Quantities

Bear fetish or figurine (stone preferred; turquoise or white bear most powerful)
Offerings (corn pollen traditional; follow your bear's preferences)
Charm bag (optional, for carrying)

Directions

  1. Select a bear fetish that calls to you, the one that stimulates a craving for possession.
  2. The fetish that chooses you will always work most powerfully for you.
  3. White bears and bears carved from turquoise are considered especially powerful.
  4. Treat the fetish like a fairy-tale bear companion.
  5. Keep it with you all day, tucked into your pocket or carried in a charm bag.
  6. Sleep with it under your pillow or by your bedside.
  7. Feed it on schedule; actual substances are offered, though the bear eats the essence.
  8. A traditional Native American offering is corn pollen, but consider what your particular bear needs.
  9. Most importantly: talk to the bear and listen.
  10. Once activated, it will eventually offer healing advice and recommendations for maintaining health.
  11. Pay close attention to dreams featuring bears while using this practice.
