👼Calling Household Angel Mother Derdekea

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
InvocationCandle MagickPrayer
◇Category
Invocation
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- An orange candle - A small ceramic or crystal angel statue in front of the candle near the center or heart of your home
◇Directions
1. Each evening, light the household angel candle, saying: "I call on Mother Derdekea to care for my home and family/pets and enfold me/us in her soft green cloak as I/we sleep." Hold the angel statue and repeat the words, asking Derdekea for any special blessings you need for your home life. 2. Carry the candle through every room, transferring it to the meal table, saying: "Derdekea, I thank you for the abundance you bring. May I always have enough for my needs and a little more." After eating, blow out the candle. 3. Relight it for a few minutes in your bedroom, asking Derdekea for quiet sleep and safety until the morning. 4. Perform when you return home in the evening or are ending your day.
◇Description
A when you return home evening spell calling household angel Mother Derdekea to bring happiness and abundance to your home, lighting orange candle and carrying through rooms for her soft green cloak to enfold you.
