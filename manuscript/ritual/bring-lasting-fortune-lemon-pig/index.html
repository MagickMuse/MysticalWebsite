A Chilean tradition creating a lemon pig to banish misfortune and release good luck through fire purification.

Quantities

  • 1 lemon
  • 4 silver-colored long pins (or 3, your choice)
  • 1 dish of salt
  • 1 metal bucket half filled with sand
  • Cloth for wrapping pins

Directions

  1. Using the lemon as the body of the pig, roll it in salt, sticking the pins in as the legs.
  2. If you wish, draw the features and tail.
  3. Say five times, "Lemon pig, take misfortune away, release good luck in my life to stay." Burn the pig in a metal bucket half filled with sand outside the front door or on a bonfire, removing the pins beforehand.
  4. Wrap the pins securely in cloth.
  5. Make lemon pigs monthly if you have a lot of misfortune to replace.
  6. Perform Saturday evening, the waning moon.
