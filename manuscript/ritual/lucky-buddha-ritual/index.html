☯️Lucky Buddha Ritual

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Devotional WorkManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 1 laughing wooden or metal Buddha - 1 small dish for offerings - Daily offerings (crystal, coin, or fresh flower)
◇Directions
1. Rub the tummy of the Buddha three times clockwise and ask for any good fortune you need in any area of your life, especially luck in financial affairs. 2. If you need good news, name where you need the good news and rub the Buddha's right ear clockwise. 3. It is said you will receive good news before the week is over. 4. Place a crystal, coin, or flower in the dish as an offering (keep the flowers fresh). 5. Add something small every day, rubbing the Buddha's tummy just once, again asking for any special good luck you need. 6. When the dish is full, put the nonperishable items in a big bowl near the Buddha to attract abundance to your home. 7. Perform whenever you need good luck and/or good news.
◇Description
An ongoing devotional practice rubbing Buddha's tummy and ear with daily offerings for good fortune and good news.
