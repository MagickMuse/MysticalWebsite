A personal initiation and dedication rite in which the practitioner immerses in cold natural water to leave the old self behind, dresses in new clothing as a symbol of rebirth, and speaks a personal oath of commitment to Brigid's service as a flamekeeper.

Quantities

Access to a natural water source (stream, lake, river, or sea)
New or specially prepared clothing to change into
A personal oath, written in advance
A candle to light afterward (optional but traditional)

Directions

  1. Choose a meaningful time for this rite.
  2. Imbolc is traditional, but any threshold moment serves.
  3. Prepare a personal oath expressing your commitment to Brigid's service in your own words.
  4. The terms are between you and the goddess.
  5. Travel to a natural body of water.
  6. Undress fully and wade or plunge beneath the surface, understanding this as a crossing, a river of death from which a new self will emerge.
  7. Remain as long as feels right.
  8. Emerge from the water.
  9. Dry off and dress in new or specially prepared clothing as a symbol of your changed self.
  10. Speak your oath aloud, addressing Brigid directly.
  11. If desired, include the traditional Celtic pledge: "If I should break faith with you, may the skies fall upon me, may the seas drown me, may the earth rise up and swallow me."
  12. Light a candle and sit with the flame you have committed to tend.
  13. You are now a keeper of Brigid's fire.
