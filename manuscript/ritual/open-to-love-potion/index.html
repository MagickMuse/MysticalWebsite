🍵Open to Love Potion

◇Form
Potion
◇Method
Herbal MagickVisualizationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Lemon balm tea, rose tea, and a small rose quartz (Version 1) Linden flower tea, a cinnamon stick, and a freshwater pearl (Version 2) Optional: clear glass cup or mug
◇Directions
1. Choose one of the two versions below and brew the tea according to the Tea Potion Spell. 2. Place the corresponding crystal or pearl in the cup with the tea. Do not ingest it. 3. As you drink, visualize your heart opening like a flower: accepting compliments, kindness, and affection, and becoming more receptive to the love others wish to give you. 4. After drinking, wash the stone or pearl and keep it as a charm for remaining open to love.
◇Description
To encourage you to learn to accept the love that is all around you. Two variations provided.
↗Correspondences5

↗Symbols1

This tea potion helps you learn to accept the love that is all around you. Sometimes we resist or deflect love without realizing it. This potion gently opens your heart to receiving.

Version 1: Lemon Balm and Rose with Rose Quartz

Brew lemon balm tea and rose tea together. Place a small rose quartz in the cup.

Version 2: Linden Flower with Cinnamon and Pearl

Brew linden flower tea. Stir with a cinnamon stick and place a freshwater pearl in the cup.

Optional Enhancement

Using a clear glass cup or mug symbolizes transparency and openness, enhancing the receptive energy of this potion.
