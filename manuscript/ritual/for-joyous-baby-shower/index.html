Ritual Grimoire

🍼For Joyous Baby Shower

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Chant/MantraKnot Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• String of bells or Tibetan bells on cord • Gift for baby (or small item for your shower) • 9 yellow ribbons (or pink/blue if sex known) • 9 paper flowers (if your baby shower)
◇Directions
1. Ring the bells above the baby item nine times, saying, "One calls joy, two health, three wisdom, four wealth, five talents yet untold, six the heart and mind of gold, seven for courage, eight gentle power, nine blessings on this baby shower." Tie the nine ribbons, each with a knot, around the gift (wrapped if you're taking it along), repeating a line of the chant for each one. 2. If it is your baby shower, put your beribboned item in a basket decorated with nine paper flowers to hold the other gifts, saying, "Nine months to grow in your unique way, greetings baby on your shower day." Alternatively, say the spell words in your mind as you hand over the gift. 3. Perform the morning of the baby shower.
◇Description
A morning-of ritual ringing bells nine times over baby gift, tying nine ribbons with knots, optionally decorating basket with nine paper flowers.
