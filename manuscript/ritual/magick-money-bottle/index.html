🫙Magick Money Bottle

◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Manifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Jar or bottle Money-drawing incense Magnetic sand or money-drawing powder Coins (minimum 1 daily)
◇Directions
1. Empower a jar or bottle by passing it through your choice of money-drawing incense. 2. Sprinkle magnetic sand or money-drawing powder into the bottle. 3. Add a minimum of one coin daily, as little as a single penny, so that your finances will prosper. 4. Do not remove coins from the bottle or the spell will be broken. 5. Once the jar is so full that no more can be added, remove exactly one coin. 6. Begin a new bottle from Step 1, incorporating the single removed coin as a magickal starter, like a sourdough starter. 7. Either spend the entire contents of the previous bottle on something that gives pleasure, or bury the bottle with its coins.
◇Description
A jar spell that accumulates coins daily to grow personal finances. The bottle is fed continuously until full, then a sourdough-starter coin begins the next bottle.
