🏡Shutting Out World After Hard Day Spell

A when you get home after bad day spell for shutting out world especially if you live alone, lighting scented candle with salt and eating candies or fruits to create sanctuary and haven of tranquillity.

Quantities

  • Candies or small fruits
  • A large scented candle
  • A small bowl of salt

Directions

  1. Switch your cell phone to silent and play soft music.
  2. Light the candle, saying: "This is my sanctuary, my haven of tranquillity, safe cocooned within my peace, all beyond this place shall cease." Put grains of salt in the candle flame, naming any cares or irritations from the day.
  3. Repeat the spell words.
  4. Sit comfortably, choosing a candy or fruit.
  5. Before you eat it, say: "I take within myself all pleasure, my own company will I treasure." As you eat it, say: "Safe cocooned within my peace. All beyond this place has ceased. This then is my sanctuary, my haven of tranquillity." Eat as many or few candies or fruits as you want.
  6. Forget all ideas of reconnecting with the world.
  7. Life will still be there in the morning.
  8. Perform when you get home after a bad day or if you find it hard to let go of work issues.
