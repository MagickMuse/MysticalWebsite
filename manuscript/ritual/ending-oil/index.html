Van Van Oil

A variant of Evil Eye Oil that specifically repels deliberately cast spells and ends a crossed condition. Three blue crosses are drawn on a white china plate with indigo or blue ink, then dissolved into Van Van Oil. The resulting Ending Oil is massaged onto the body (especially soles of feet), used in the bath, or applied to uncrossing candles.

Quantities

White china plate
Indigo, food coloring, or blue water-soluble ink
Water
Van Van Oil (in a container to catch the rinse water)
Bottle for storage

Directions

  1. Draw three blue crosses onto a white china plate with indigo, food coloring, or blue water-soluble ink.
  2. Rinse it off completely with water into a container filled with Van Van Oil; the crosses must dissolve completely if you anticipate success.
  3. Funnel the solution into a bottle.
  4. Use the Ending Oil to massage your body, especially the soles of your feet.
  5. It may also be used in the bath or on uncrossing candles.
