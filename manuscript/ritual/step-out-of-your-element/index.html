Ritual Grimoire

🧘Step Out of Your Element

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. Spring Peepers (like all frogs) begin life as tadpoles. 2. In his first few weeks, Frog swims the shallows, propelled by a single flagellating tail, breathing through gills like a fish. 3. As weeks progress, he grows two rudimentary back legs powerful enough to jump great distances in a single bound. 4. Then he grows front legs, each little froggy toe equipped with an adhesive pad so Frog can climb and cling. 5. After about 12 weeks, Frog leaves the water to live on land. 6. His transition complete, he can now breathe through his nose and absorb oxygen through his skin. 7. Spring Peeper teaches you to slip out of your comfort zone and swim away from your home pond to take in the view from the trees. 8. Think about where you are comfortable, where you feel at home, where you can breathe easily. 9. Now challenge yourself to do one thing that feels out of your element. 10. If you're a homebody, try a dance class. 11. If you're an extrovert, learn to knit. 12. You don't have to make a lifelong commitment, simply create an opportunity to try something new. 13. See how it alters the way you think, feel, and breathe!
◇Description
A practice inspired by Spring Peeper's metamorphosis from water-breathing tadpole to land-dwelling frog. Challenge yourself to leave your comfort zone and try something completely outside your element, observing how it shifts your perspective and way of being.
