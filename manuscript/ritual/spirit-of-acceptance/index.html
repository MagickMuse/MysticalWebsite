A charm bag working to foster self-acceptance, personal responsibility, openness to change, and emotional healing.

Quantities

1 charm bag with drawstrings
Mustard seeds, enough to fill one third of the bag
1 small crystal
Paper
Pen
1 sprig of clover (optional)
2 sticks of incense

Directions

  1. Settle into a magickal frame of mind and set up the workspace.
  2. Light one stick of incense and create sacred space.
  3. Put the mustard seeds and the crystal into the charm bag.
  4. Turn the bag in the incense smoke until it is fully smoked.
  5. Tear the paper into thin strips.
  6. Write each of these affirmations on its own strip: "I accept myself." "I accept responsibility for myself and my actions." "I accept responsibility for my life." "I accept that I deserve the blessings of this Universe."
  7. Write further affirmations suited to personal needs, such as "I accept reality," "I accept change," "I accept healing," "I accept love into my life," "I accept transformation," or "I accept everything that I need to accept."
  8. Fold or roll each strip and place it in the bag.
  9. If using clover, place the sprig in the bag after the last strip.
  10. Light the second stick of incense.
  11. Close the bag and knot the drawstrings, with three knots if possible.
  12. Raise power and hold the bag in the smoke while reciting:
    Crafted so it does no harm,
    With magick I enchant this charm.
    Winds of change, blow through me.
    Spirit of acceptance, flow to me.
    This I command, so mote it be!
  13. Keep reciting while holding the bag in the smoke until the incense has burned down fully.
  14. Ground the power, clean up the workspace, and discard used spell materials.
  15. Keep the bag close, whether under a pillow, worn, or in a pocket. Results may be gradual but build over time.
  16. Renew the spell by repeating the process and smoking the bag again.
  17. Once the issue is resolved, dispose of the bag by burying, burning, or storing it, and cleanse the crystal if it will be reused.

We have a remarkable talent for sabotaging ourselves. We excel at creating our own problems, building roadblocks and detours along our paths, and stubbornly refusing to act in our own best interests. Often these struggles relate to our failure or refusal to accept things: the truth about ourselves, the truth about people and situations, changes that need to happen, the reality of our circumstances, or the blessings the Universe offers us.

This spell is for anyone struggling with acceptance. Cast it anytime, though waxing moon phases (between New Moon and Full Moon) amplify its power.

Why Mustard Seeds?

Mustard seeds carry potent symbolism across spiritual traditions. Their small size belies immense potential for growth, representing faith that even tiny steps toward acceptance can blossom into transformation. In folk magick, mustard seeds protect against negativity and aid in mental clarity, both essential when confronting what we resist accepting.

Writing Your Affirmations

The core affirmations address universal struggles:

  • "I accept myself."
  • "I accept responsibility for myself and my actions."
  • "I accept responsibility for my life."
  • "I accept that I deserve the blessings of this Universe."

Beyond these, write affirmations specific to your situation. Consider statements like:

  • "I accept reality."
  • "I accept change."
  • "I accept my body."
  • "I accept my illness."
  • "I accept compromise."
  • "I accept my family."
  • "I accept healing."
  • "I accept my past."
  • "I accept recovery."
  • "I accept my success."
  • "I accept my losses."
  • "I accept adulthood."
  • "I accept love into my life."
  • "I accept that death is part of life's spiral."
  • "I accept that I need to make positive changes."
  • "I accept transformation."

If you worry about forgetting something important, add: "I accept everything that I need to accept."

Take your time with this. Be honest and thorough. You know your own life better than anyone else. If space runs short, write on both sides of the paper strips.

Working the Spell

Give careful thought to each affirmation as you write it. Fold or roll the strips tightly so they fit in the mojo bag. After adding all your written intentions (and the clover, if using), seal the bag with three knots if possible. The act of knotting binds the magick.

Hold the bag in the incense smoke while reciting the incantation. Repeat as many times as feels right. Let the smoke carry your words into the bag, infusing each affirmation with power.

What to Expect

Results from this spell build gradually, then snowball once momentum begins. You may start feeling better about yourself and your life in subtle ways at first. Remember that transformation is a process, not a sudden shift, even when a dramatic event sparked your need for change.

Keep the mojo bag close: under your pillow at night, in your pocket or worn during the day. Its presence reinforces your intention.

Renewal and Completion

Renew this spell anytime by holding the mojo bag in incense smoke and speaking the incantation again.

When the issues that brought you to this spell no longer trouble you, consider the work complete. Dispose of the mojo bag however feels right: bury it, burn it, store it safely, or simply discard it. The crystal can be cleansed and kept for future workings.
