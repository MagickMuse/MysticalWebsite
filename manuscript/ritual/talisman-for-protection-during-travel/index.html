✈️Talisman for Protection During Travel

◇Form
PouchCharm
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Small magickal bag or pouch - Small pieces of turquoise, tanzanite, and/or zircon crystals (December birthstones) - A few needles from an evergreen tree (pine, fir, spruce, cedar, etc.) - A few dried hawthorn berries (if available) - OR a few dried juniper berries (if available) - Small amount of soil or a few pebbles from your front door threshold - Strong cord or string for tying
◇Directions
Gathering Materials: Collect small pieces of turquoise, tanzanite, and/or zircon, December birthstones with correspondences of power, protection, and calming. Gather a few needles from an evergreen tree. If available, collect a few dried hawthorn or juniper berries. Most importantly, gather a bit of soil or a few small pebbles from the threshold outside your front door. This low magick element keeps you connected to your home ground no matter how far you travel. Creating the Talisman: 1. Fill a small magickal bag with the crystals, evergreen needles, and berries. 2. Add the soil or pebbles from your threshold. 3. Tie the bag shut with a strong cord, securing the contents and sealing your intention for safe travels. Using the Talisman: Tuck the talisman into your car's glove box for safe travels during the winter holidays. Alternatively, keep it with you in your pocket, purse, or luggage during your travels. The talisman serves as both protection and a connection to home, ensuring safe passage and a safe return.
◇Description
A travel talisman using December birthstones (turquoise, tanzanite, zircon), evergreen needles, hawthorn or juniper berries, and soil from your home threshold. This charm keeps you connected to your home ground while providing protection during winter holiday travels. Tuck it into your car's glove box or keep it with you during journeys.
