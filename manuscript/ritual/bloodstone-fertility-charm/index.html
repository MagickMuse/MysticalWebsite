🟢Bloodstone Fertility Charm

◇Quantities
- Bloodstone (raw, polished, or carved; ideally into a Tjet/Buckle of Isis form)
◇Directions
1. Obtain a piece of bloodstone, raw, polished, or carved. 2. Wear it as a pendant, ring, or bracelet; carry it in your pocket; or tuck it into a charm bag you keep on your person. 3. No further activation is required, though setting your intention when first handling it deepens the working.
◇Description
Bloodstone is among the gemstones most strongly associated with fertility. Even uncut, it radiates fertility energy continuously. Keeping bloodstone close to the body, worn as jewelry, carried in a pocket, or held in a charm bag, allows one to absorb this energy passively throughout the day. Its power is amplified when carved into a Tjet (Buckle of Isis) amulet form, which is the traditional talisman shape of Isis.
