A Sunday ritual to restore fun, passion, and laughter to a relationship that has hit a dull patch, using mint, cinnamon, ginger, and lavender swirled in a bowl and kept in a bag for spontaneous joy.

Quantities

  • A ceramic bowl
  • Dried mint
  • Powdered cinnamon
  • Dried ginger
  • Lavender
  • A red bag

Directions

  1. Add all the ingredients to the bowl in the order given above, saying: "Mint for excitement and surprises, ginger for passion for life and for love. Cinnamon to make our hearts glad and our eyes sparkle. Lavender for laughter and childlike fun and all that is spontaneous." Swirl the bowl faster and faster, reciting the words louder and faster until they jumble.
  2. With a final swirl and fun, scoop some of the mix into the bag, close it, and toss it nine times higher and faster, repeating the words and then slowing them down to silence and stillness.
  3. Plan a trip or weekend away and keep the bag with a picture of the two of you in natural sunlight.
