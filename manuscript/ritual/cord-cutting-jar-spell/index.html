Scythe

A jar spell using the Scythe rune and a severed cord to make a clean break from a person or situation that no longer serves.

Quantities

  • Pen with black ink
  • 3" × 5" piece of cardstock
  • 1 yard of red cord or ribbon
  • Scissors
  • Clean, small glass jar
  • 1 black tumbled gemstone (e.g., onyx or obsidian)
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 sprig of rosemary
  • Sealing wax

Directions

  1. A jar spell using the Scythe rune and a severed cord to make a clean break from a person or situation that no longer serves.
✏️
Adapting this spell? Before substituting, check: Does the swap share this spell's core correspondences (endings, severance, release)? Does every step still feel intentional to you? The cord-cutting gesture and disposal step are the heart of this working — keep those even if you swap materials.

Directions

  1. Draw the Scythe rune on the cardstock in black ink and place it on your altar.
  2. Focus on the Scythe while running the cord through your hands.
  3. Hold the cord in your left hand and pull it through your right thumb and forefinger, infusing it with the intention of the person or environment you wish to separate from.
  4. Pull the cord as needed while speaking the charm:
I USE THE SCYTHE
FOR ALL THINGS DIE.
PROTECTED BY
MY PSYCHIC EYE,
THE PLAN I MADE
NOW MUST FADE.
RETURN TO EARTH,
AWAIT REBIRTH.
THE SACRED RUNE,
THE BLESSED MOON.
ALL THINGS I SEE
AND BLESSED BE.
THE MIGHTY SCYTHE
BEFORE MY EYES.
ALL THINGS DIE
BEFORE THEY RISE.
  1. Once you have projected all due energy (honesty, truth, responsibility, readiness) into the cord, wind it around the cardstock Scythe.
  2. Use scissors to cut through the cord into many irreparable pieces. Acknowledge that this change is desirable and creates room for new manifestations.
  3. Place the cord pieces in the glass jar.
  4. Add the black stone (to absorb energy), cinnamon (offering), and rosemary (memory).
  5. Fold or roll the Scythe rune cardstock facedown to fit in the jar, covering the contents. Seal the jar tightly with sealing wax.
  6. Disposal: Either leave the jar in the vicinity of the broken bond and never return, or bury it and plant a magickal herb (basil, mint, catnip) over it to complete the transformation.
