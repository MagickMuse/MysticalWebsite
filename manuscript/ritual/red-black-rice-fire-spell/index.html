🍚Red & Black Rice Fire Spell

A complex, multi-stage removal working for children. Three straws from a new broom are burned and passed around the child's head; their ashes mark the child's forehead. Dyed rice is then mixed with camphor, salt, chili pepper, the child's hair and nail parings, and earth, either from beneath the front door or graveyard dirt from a protective ancestor. The mixture is passed around the child and thrown into fire. A thick, pungent, irritating aroma from the burning confirms the working has taken effect; a weak or absent smell signals a severe case requiring further action. One of the Evil Eye Removal methods.

Quantities

  • White rice (to be dyed red and black)
  • 3 straws from a new, unused broom
  • White camphor
  • Salt
  • Chili pepper
  • Child's hair and nail clippings
  • Optional: personal articles of the Eye-caster
  • Soil (from beneath/near front door) OR graveyard dirt from a protective ancestor

Directions

  1. Begin by dyeing white rice in two batches, one red, one black.
  2. Pluck three straws from a new, unused broom.
  3. Light them and pass the smoking straws in a circle around the child's head, then allow them to burn out fully.
  4. Rub your fingers in the cooled ashes and use them to mark the child's forehead.
  5. In a separate vessel, combine the dyed rice with white camphor, salt, and chili pepper.
  6. Add some of the child's hair and nail clippings.
  7. If the Eye-caster is known, their personal articles may be added as well.
  8. Finally add earth: soil gathered from directly beneath or near the front door, or graveyard dirt from the grave of a spirit who was devoted to and protective of the child in life.
  9. Pass the entire mixture around the child, then cast it all into fire.
  10. A thick, pungent, irritating smoke confirms success.
  11. A faint or absent aroma means the case is severe and further workings will be needed.
