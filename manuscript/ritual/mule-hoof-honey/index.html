🍯Honey

MALEVOLENT WORKING. Documented for historical and protective knowledge only. A variant of the Mule Hoof Bread hex in which charred mule hoof parings are mixed into honey and covertly fed to a woman. Honey, a substance strongly associated with fertility and abundance, is inverted by the sterility power of the mule. Not intended for use.

Quantities

Charred mule hoof parings
Honey

Directions

  1. Char mule hoof parings and mix them into honey.
  2. Feed the mixture to the target without their knowledge.

This is a companion to the Mule Hoof Bread hex, using the same primary ingredient (charred mule hoof parings) in a different vehicle. Where the bread hex buries the mule's sterility inside nourishing food, this one uses honey, a substance with its own deep fertility associations.

Honey is the product of bees, which are among the most potent fertility symbols in the traditions that produced these workings. It is sweet, golden, naturally preservative, and associated with abundance, sexuality, and the wealth of the hive. The mule's charred hoof mixed into honey creates an extreme inversion: the most fertile medium carries the most definitively sterile substance.

The contrast between vehicle and payload is the point. Honey should amplify positive forces; when it carries the mule's essence, it delivers the opposite of what honey normally promises.

⚠️
This is a historical hex documented for knowledge and self-protection only. It is presented here as context for understanding the full spectrum of fertility magic traditions, not as an instruction for use. Hexing another person's fertility without their knowledge or consent is considered among the most serious of malevolent workings.
