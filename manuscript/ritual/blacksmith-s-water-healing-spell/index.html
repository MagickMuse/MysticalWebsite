💧Blacksmith's Water Healing Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Water/Spray
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Energy WorkWater Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Small vessel of pure water Red-hot iron (applied by blacksmith)
◇Directions
1. Fill a small vessel with pure water and bring it to a blacksmith, who dips a piece of red-hot iron into it. 2. Give this water to the child to drink.
◇Description
A children's healing remedy in which pure water is brought to a blacksmith, who dips red-hot iron into it. The charged water is then given to the ill child to drink, regardless of the source of the illness.
