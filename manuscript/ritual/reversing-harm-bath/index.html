🔄Reversing Harm Bath

A powerful bath specifically for reversing harm back upon its sender. Uses eucalyptus leaves, red pepper, and rue to break attacks and return them to their source. This is not merely protective - it actively reverses malicious magick. Use in conjunction with reversal spells when you are under active magickal attack and need to send the energy back to whoever sent it.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Eucalyptus leaves: approximately 1/2 cup
  • Rue: approximately 1/2 cup
  • Red pepper (cayenne): a pinch
    Safety Warning:
    Red pepper can irritate sensitive skin. Use sparingly and avoid contact with eyes, mucous membranes, and sensitive areas.

Directions

  1. Prepare bathwater.
  2. Add approximately 1/2 cup each of eucalyptus leaves and rue to the water.
  3. Add a pinch of red pepper - a little goes a long way and too much can cause skin irritation.
  4. Stir to combine.
  5. Enter the bath.
  6. As you bathe, scrub upward from feet to head to draw the reversal energy to you and charge yourself with the power to send harm back to its source.
  7. Visualize the attack that has been sent against you gathering in the bathwater.
  8. See it transforming, becoming a mirror that reflects all harm back to the sender threefold.
  9. Speak your intention clearly: that all harm sent to you returns to its source, that all who wish you ill receive exactly what they intended for you.
  10. Recite reversal prayers or incantations appropriate to your practice.
  11. Soak and let the herbs do their work.
  12. When you feel the reversal is complete, drain or collect the water to dump toward the rising sun.
  13. The work is done.
