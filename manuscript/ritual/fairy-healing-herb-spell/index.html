🧚Fairy Healing Herb Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Offering RiteCharm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Small gifts for the fairies: milk, berries, crystals, or similar Seven different fairy herb species, gathered at noontime during a summer full moon (Choose from: althea, eyebright, foxglove, ragweed, Saint John's Wort, self-heal, speedwell, thyme, toadflax, yarrow, select what suits you)
◇Directions
1. Begin by leaving small gifts for the fairies: milk, berries, crystals, or similar offerings that appeal to them. 2. Accompany the gifts with your sincere petition for healing or illness prevention. 3. At noontime during a summer full moon, gather seven different fairy herb species from the list. 4. This spell calls for magickal use of the herbs, not medicinal; do not prepare them as remedies. 5. Instead, arrange the gathered herbs in bunches and hang them throughout the home to protect against illness and speed the healing of anyone within.
◇Description
A petitioning spell asking the Fairies, botanical masters with deep knowledge of healing herbs, for miracle cures and illness prevention. Seven fairy herbs are gathered at a sacred time and hung throughout the home to protect against illness and speed healing.
