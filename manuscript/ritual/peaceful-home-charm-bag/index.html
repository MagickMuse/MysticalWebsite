A botanical pouch assembled with a braided hair cord, angelica root anointed with chamomile oil, and a blend of herbs and flowers to bring peace, harmony, and unity to the family home.

Quantities

One strand of hair from each family member (pet hair optional)
Blue thread
Angelica root
German chamomile essential oil (one drop per family member)
Balm of Gilead buds
Flax seeds
Lavender blossoms
Pink and white rosebuds

Directions

  1. Braid the hair strands together with blue thread.
  2. Use the braid to tie a bow around the angelica root.
  3. Anoint the root with one drop of chamomile oil for each member of the family.
  4. Place the anointed root together with the balm of Gilead buds, flax seeds, lavender blossoms, and pink and white rosebuds into a sachet or charm bag.
