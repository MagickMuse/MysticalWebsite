🌙Lunar Water Bedroom Fertility Charging

A fertility working timed to the New Moon that draws on the combined power of moonlight and living water. A bowl of natural water is charged outdoors beneath moonbeams and placed under the bed on the woman's side, where it works continuously from New Moon to Full Moon. The cycle repeats with fresh water each month.

Quantities

  • Bowl (large enough to hold half-full of water)
  • Natural living water, sea, river, or rain water preferred
  • Spring water (if natural living water unavailable)

Directions

  1. Beginning at the New Moon, fill a bowl halfway with natural living water; sea, river, or rain water is ideal.
  2. Spring water may be substituted when those are unavailable.
  3. Carry the bowl outside and set it where it can absorb the moonbeams directly.
  4. Leave it out for three hours to charge.
  5. Bring the bowl inside and place it under the bed, positioned approximately beneath where the woman sleeps.
  6. Make love over this lunar-charged water.
  7. Continue nightly until the Full Moon.
  8. At the next New Moon, discard the old water and begin the cycle again with a fresh bowl.
