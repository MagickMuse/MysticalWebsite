⚠️ Hoodoo & Voodoo are closed practices sacred to African American and African diasporic spiritual traditions and should only be practiced by those within the originating cultures.
A complex conjure doll stuffed with Spanish moss and black pepper, adorned with crow feathers, and anointed with both Black Arts Oil and Obeah Perfume Oil. The target's name is written in Dragon's Blood Ink on parchment with the curse inscribed below, attached to the doll with a honey locust thorn. The doll is buried alongside a fresh catfish in the enemy's backyard without their knowledge.

Quantities

1 voodoo doll (made from black fabric)
Spanish moss (stuffing)
Black pepper (1 whole can)
Crow feathers
1 piece of parchment paper
Dragon's Blood Ink
1 honey locust thorn
Black Arts Oil
Obeah Perfume Oil
1 fresh catfish

Directions

  1. Make a voodoo doll out of black fabric and stuff it with Spanish moss and a generous amount of black pepper.
  2. Attach crow feathers to the head.
  3. On a piece of parchment paper, write the name of the one to be tormented using Dragon's Blood Ink.
  4. Write your curse below the name.
  5. Attach the name paper to the doll with a honey locust thorn.
  6. Anoint the feathers with Black Arts Oil and anoint the body with Obeah Perfume Oil.
  7. Bury the doll along with a fresh catfish in the backyard of your enemy without their knowledge.
