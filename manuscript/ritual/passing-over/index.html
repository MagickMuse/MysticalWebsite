RosemaryRue

A funeral or memorial ritual for the deceased. Rue or rosemary is burned to release negative memories, while positive ones are treasured. The departed is blessed on their journey through the Cauldron of Life toward Summerland and eventual rebirth. Followed by a feast of remembrance.

Quantities

  • Rue or rosemary herb
  • White votive candle in container
  • Food and beverage for farewell feast
  • Things of the deceased for remembrance (photo, favorite possessions, etc.)
    Traditional foods: pomegranates, apples, raisin bread, pork dishes, acorn squash, dark wine or other dark beverage

Directions

  1. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting.
    ---
  2. Clap or ring the bell 3 times:
    Today we bid farewell to [Name of deceased]. We remember the good times and the less happy times, for all life has both pleasant and unpleasant experiences. We release the negative and hold onto the positive.
  3. Light the white candle:
    Like the flame of this candle will the memory of [N] light up our hearts and our minds.
  4. Take a bit of the rue or rosemary and drop slowly into the candle flame:
    As the rue [or rosemary] burns, it takes away the power of any negative memories about [N], that we will treasure the good and put aside the ill.
  5. Raise open arms:
    Great Lady and Great Lord, give rest and refreshment to [N] that they may be brought into your love and journey again through the Cauldron of Life. Death is a transition, moving from Underworld for repose into Summerland for refreshment until the next incarnation. This time is not sad, for the spirit is renewed and communicates with those who have gone before and those who remain in the physical realms of Middleworld. We remember our loved ones, but they remember us as well, visiting when needed or called upon, aiding us in this life and standing by us in our transition to the next. So Mote It Be!
  6. Lower arms; slowly ring the bell nine times:
    Farewell, dear [N] as you take your journey of passing. Our love and blessings take with you to your rest. May you soon rejoin the Dance of Life.
  7. Sharing of memories among those present commences, followed by a feast of remembrance.
    Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
