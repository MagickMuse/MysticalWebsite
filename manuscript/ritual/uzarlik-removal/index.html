🧿Uzarlik Removal

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
ProtectionChant/MantraSmoke Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• A handful of uzarlik (Syrian rue, harmel) • A handful of sea salt • A fire
◇Directions
1. Prepare a fire. 2. Gather a handful of uzarlik (Syrian rue, harmel) and sea salt together in one hand. 3. Circle this handful around the target's head three times, chanting throughout each rotation: 4. Uzarlik, uzarlik You are air You are against every grief Pluck out the Evil Eye and place it in my hand. 5. When the three rotations are complete, cast the full handful of herbs and salt into the fire.
◇Description
A handful of uzarlik (Syrian rue/harmel) and sea salt are circled three times around the target's head while a traditional chant is spoken, then cast into fire. Loud popping and sputtering from the fire as the herbs catch is a strong positive omen. One of the Evil Eye Removal methods.
