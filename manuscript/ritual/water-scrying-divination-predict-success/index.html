🔮Water Scrying Divination Predict Success

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
DivinationMeditationWater MagickBreathworkVisualization
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Wide, preferably black ceramic bowl (paint black if needed) - Water (ocean, river, lake, rain, or clean tap) - One candle and matches - Optional: notebook and pen, audio recorder, or a note‑taker
◇Directions
1. Find a comfortable seated or lying position in a quiet, softly lit space. 2. Close your eyes and begin breathing deeply and slowly. 3. With each exhale, feel your body becoming heavier and more relaxed. 4. Body scan: Starting at the crown of your head, bring your awareness slowly down through your body (forehead, eyes, jaw, neck, shoulders, arms, chest, belly, hips, legs, feet). 5. Notice any areas of tension or discomfort without judgment. 6. Breathe into those places and imagine them softening. 7. Breath awareness: Now shift your attention fully to your breath. 8. Don't change it, just observe. 9. Notice the cool air entering your nostrils and the warm air leaving. 10. Notice the rise and fall of your chest and belly. 11. If your mind wanders, gently guide it back to your breath without criticism. 12. This is the practice. 13. Counting breaths (optional anchor): If your mind is very active, try counting your breaths. 14. Inhale (one), exhale (two), inhale (three), exhale (four), up to ten. 15. Then start over at one. 16. If you lose count, simply start again at one. 17. Expanding awareness: After 5–10 minutes of breath focus, let go of counting and simply rest in open awareness. 18. Notice sounds, sensations, thoughts passing by like clouds. 19. You are not your thoughts, you are the sky behind them. 20. Return gently: When you feel ready, bring small movements back into your body (wiggle fingers, toes, stretch gently). 21. Take a deep breath and slowly open your eyes. 22. Reflect: Sit quietly for a moment. 23. Notice how you feel. 24. Calmer? 25. More centered? 26. That's the gift of meditation. 27. Tips for Beginners: - Start with just 5 minutes a day, consistency matters more than length - It's normal for your mind to wander, the practice is noticing and returning to your breath - Meditation is not about stopping thoughts, it's about changing your relationship with them - Use a timer so you don't worry about how long you've been sitting - Try meditating at the same time each day to build a habit 28. Next Steps: - Once comfortable with breath awareness, try guided meditations, body scans, loving-kindness practices, or visualization meditations - Explore different postures (sitting, lying, walking meditation) - Consider joining a meditation group or using an app for structure and support 29. Meditation is a practice, not a performance. 30. Be patient and kind with yourself.
◇Description
A Lughnasadh scrying rite to check on the progress of your goals and foresee what you’re likely to harvest. Uses a dark water surface to enter trance, receive images, and evaluate outcomes.
