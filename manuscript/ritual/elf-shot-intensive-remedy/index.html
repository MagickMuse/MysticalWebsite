🌿Elf-Shot Intensive Remedy

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Herbal MagickSmoke MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Betony Chervil Dill Fennel Garlic shoots Mugwort Plantain Rue Wormwood
◇Directions
1. Gather the nine herbs listed. 2. Bind them together while murmuring sacred verses or protective charms. 3. Bless both the herb bundle and the patient nine times. 4. Wave the herb bundle over the patient's body, or gently rub it along the body. 5. Burn the bundle, wafting the resulting smoke over the patient as a fumigation. 6. Dispose of the ashes in living, flowing water; the water must flow away from you as you release the ashes.
◇Description
An elaborate Anglo-Saxon nine-herb remedy for elf-shot. Nine is the sacred number of Anglo-Saxon magick. This intensive working must be performed by another person on behalf of the afflicted. If any of the nine herbs are unavailable, substitute others to maintain the sacred number.
