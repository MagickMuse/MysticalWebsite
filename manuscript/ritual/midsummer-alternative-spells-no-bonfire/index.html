☀️Midsummer

Two alternative Midsummer fertility workings for those without access to bonfires: walking naked in the garden on Saint John's Eve, and burning gathered flowers on Midsummer's Eve so their ashes substitute for bonfire ashes.

Quantities

Garden (vegetable or flower)
Flowers gathered on Midsummer's Eve (for flower ash working)

Directions

  1. Two traditional alternatives carry the fertility power of the season without a bonfire.
  2. For the first, walk naked through a vegetable or flower garden on Saint John's Eve to absorb the earth's fertility energy directly.
  3. For the second, gather flowers on Midsummer's Eve and burn them that night.
  4. Their ashes carry the same magickal potency as those of the traditional bonfires.

For those for whom bonfires are not realistic, there are traditional alternatives.

Spell 7: Naked Garden Walk

Fertility power is traditionally gained by walking around naked in your vegetable or flower garden on Saint John's Eve (Midsummer's Eve).

Spell 8: Flower Ash Substitute

A Balkan tradition: gather flowers on Midsummer's Eve and burn them that night. Their ashes will substitute for the ashes of Midsummer bonfires.
