🤱Cesarean Birth Gentle Entry

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraKnot MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A red cord - A red soft purse or drawstring bag - A red crystal
◇Directions
1. Hold the crystal between your cupped hands and say: "May your entry into the world be gentle, may your passage be smooth and uneventful; soon you will be securely in my arms, assisted by those who will help me move you safely into life and me to speedy recovery. So shall it be." Tie just one loose knot in the center of the cord, repeat the words, and put the cord and crystal safely into the bag. 2. Before the cesarean, open the bag, untie the cord, and say: "Soon you will be securely in my arms, assisted by those who will help me move you safely into life and me to speedy recovery. So shall it be." Hold the crystal and say the same words. 3. Put both back in the bag, and just before you go to meet your baby, touch the bag and say: "Baby, we are one." Afterward, keep the crystal near the baby's cradle and throw the cord into water when you are recovered. 4. Perform any Wednesday before the birth date.
◇Description
A Wednesday before birth date spell if you know you are having a cesarean birth, using red crystal and cord with one knot for gentle entry, smooth passage, and speedy recovery.
