🕯️Candle of Bewitchment: Saturnian

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Method
Candle MagickInvocationVisualizationBinding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 black candle - Sabbat Oil (for anointing) - Optional: Saturn Incense of Wrath and Chastisement (burned in thurible)
◇Directions
1. Work on a Saturday during the waning or dark moon. 2. Set up your working space with black or very dark surroundings if possible. 3. Anoint the black candle with Sabbat Oil. 4. If using incense, light the Saturn Incense of Wrath and Chastisement in the thurible. 5. Invoke Nocticula, the dark aspect of Habondia, as Lady of Night and Death, asking her to lay her cold Saturnian hand upon the target and bind their power, freeze their actions, and seal off their ability to harm. 6. Visualize the target wrapped in chains or bound in cold darkness, immobilized and powerless. 7. Light the candle. Let it burn as a slow, inexorable force pressing down upon the target. 8. Allow the candle to burn fully or snuff and relight on subsequent Saturdays. 9. Dispose of spent remains away from home.
◇Description
A Saturnian binding and cursing ritual using a black candle, performed under the waning or dark moon on a Saturday. Nocticula, the dark aspect of Habondia, is invoked to bind, freeze, and incarcerate the target's power.
