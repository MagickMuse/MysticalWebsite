🏺Witch Bottle

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
ProtectionHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Ingredients: - Glass or ceramic bottle - 9 needles - 9 pins - 9 nails - Additional sharp objects (fishhooks, razors) as desired - Lethal herbs (nightshade, hemlock) - Your own urine
◇Directions
1. Take a glass or ceramic bottle and add nine needles, nine pins, and nine nails. 2. Add other sharp objects as you see fit, such as fishhooks and razors. 3. Include lethal herbs such as nightshade and hemlock. 4. Add your own urine to the bottle. 5. The urine attracts the spirit and spell looking for you, and the sharp objects entangle and destroy it. 6. Some practitioners place hair and fingernail clippings into the bottle as well, though this is not recommended as they can be removed and used as magickal links against you if the bottle is found. 7. Urine is difficult to remove when it dries up and undesirable to work with if not yet dried. 8. Seal the bottle tightly. 9. Bury the bottle somewhere on your property, usually under the walkway or doorstep. 10. According to old belief, if a witch who means you harm walks over the bottle, they will experience great pain on the spot and may even die. 11. The bottle serves as a protective decoy, drawing attacks away from you and neutralizing them.
◇Description
A traditional protective decoy amulet buried on property to attract and destroy malevolent spirits and spells. Contains sharp objects and urine in a sealed bottle. Commonly used in England and Germany from the 1600s-1700s.
