🍀To Stop a Run of Bad Luck

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Chant/MantraLunar
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• 1 round white shell or stone • 1 permanent black marker
◇Directions
1. Write GOOD LUCK all over the shell or stone. 2. Toss it in your hands seven times, the number of good luck, saying: "Black cats, horseshoes, four-leaf clover, bring me good luck when I turn the stone over." Keep the stone in your bedroom and every morning turn it over and say the spell words. 3. When you next see the moon shining, turn your stone over three times and make a wish. 4. But remember, every time you have good luck, do something nice for someone else.
◇Description
A ritual to stop a run of bad luck, writing 'GOOD LUCK' on a white shell or stone and turning it over every morning to change fortune from bad to good.
