Mabon

An equinox potion working that charges the atmosphere with healing and peace energy. Uses herbal steam to gently influence community conditions and bring troubled neighbors toward harmony.

Quantities

  • Resealable jars or bottles
  • Funnel
  • Coffee filters or cheesecloth
  • Pan
  • Wooden spoon
  • Tap water (2 cups)
  • One part (about a teaspoon) each: St. John's wort, juniper berry, angelica, cloves, cedar chips

Directions

  1. Distribute herbs evenly on the bottom of the pan.
  2. Pour in two cups of water.
  3. Stir constantly until the mixture reaches a rolling boil.
  4. As you stir, perform this chant while visualizing the mixture glowing a healthy pink:
  5. Cleanse all poison,
    close all wounds.
    All who breathe this
    come, commune.
    Be well!
    Be well!
    Where we all dwell
    on this shared land
    all may excel!
  6. Once boiling, turn off the heat and allow the mixture to cool completely.
  7. Set the bottle or jar in your sink.
  8. Line a funnel with a coffee filter or cheese cloth.
  9. Pour the potion a little bit at a time through the filter into the jar.
  10. Find a quiet time of night to pour the potion outside your home or near busy intersections and meeting places in your neighborhood.
  11. As the liquid evaporates, it will carry the magickal charge into the atmosphere, bringing subtle influence toward the more harmonious community you seek.
  12. You may wish to say something as you pour, linking it to the equinox: "Take the troubled with the dark, move them to where light can heal them."
