🔮Saint Thomas Eve True Love Divination

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
DreamworkDivinationHerbal Magick
◇Dream Interpretation
The person who appears in your dreams during this night is your true love or future spouse.
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- 1 onion - 1 handkerchief or cloth
◇Directions
1. Peel an onion on Saint Thomas' Eve while invoking Thomas' aid and blessings. 2. Wrap it in your handkerchief or in a specially designated cloth. 3. Sleep with it under your pillow. 4. Your true love or your spouse should appear in your dreams.
◇Description
A traditional love divination performed on Saint Thomas Eve (December 20th, the night before December 21st) to reveal one's true love or spouse in a dream.
