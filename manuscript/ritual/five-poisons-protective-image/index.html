A Chinese talisman depicting the Five Poisonous Creatures, centipede, lizard, scorpion, snake and toad, the creatures Chinese magick considers most venomous. Their combined fierce image protects rather than harms, guarding against disease, poison, venomous creatures, animal attack and spiritual danger. Especially valued for protecting children and infants.

Quantities

Image or collage of the Five Poisonous Creatures (handmade is most powerful; store bought images also work)

Directions

  1. Create an image or collage of the five poisonous creatures: centipede, lizard, scorpion, snake and toad. The spider may replace the toad; use whichever frightens you more.
  2. A handmade image is always the most powerful, but one bought from a Chinese market or feng shui supplier also works.
  3. Wear the image, or post it in your space, for protection against spiritual danger, poison, venomous creatures and animal attack.
  4. Use it especially to protect children and infants.
