🌱Seed Charm Keep Malevolent Away

A Friday traditionally Good Friday spell for traditional seed charm to keep malevolent or spiteful people from entering your home, filling net with birdseed nine times saying count seeds never same to leave in shame.

Quantities

  • A long seed net or a fine-mesh bird feeder, the kind you hang on trees
  • Birdseed

Directions

  1. Fill the net or feeder with seeds, and, as you do so, say very fast over and over again: "Count the seeds, never the same, count the seeds or leave in shame." When the net or feeder is full, seal it.
  2. Hold it between your hands, opening and closing your hands around it nine times, repeating the spell words nine times fast.
  3. Suspend it where wild birds can feed from it, and when it is empty, repeat the spell and refill it.
  4. Empty any leftover birdseed on the ground, repeating the spell words nine times.
  5. Perform Friday, traditionally Good Friday.
