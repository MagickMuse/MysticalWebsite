Kohl protects simply by being carried, but to guard the eyes themselves it has to be worn. Unlike modern eyeshadow, it goes inside the eye along the lash line, so only kohl made from completely safe ingredients may be used.

Quantities

Dry kohl powder (eye safe formula only, such as Castor Oil Lamp Kohl)
Wooden applicator stick
Small dish

Directions

  1. Place a pinch of dry kohl powder on a dish or in your palm.
  2. Think through exactly what you want the kohl to do for you.
  3. Once your intention is clear, moisten the kohl by spitting on it lightly, or lick the tip of the wooden applicator.
  4. With your eyes gently closed, set the applicator between the lids at the inner corner, next to the nose.
  5. Draw it carefully across to the outer corner. Your tears will carry the powder where it needs to go, though it may take some practice.

⚠️ Kohl is placed inside the eye, not on the lid. Never use lead based kohl, the Medieval Lemon Kohl, or any formula with unknown ingredients. Some traditional market kohls put magick ahead of health.
