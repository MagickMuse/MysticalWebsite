Kuan Ti, the Chinese Lord of War, is a deified general whose own victories taught him that war is hell. He is invoked to prevent wars and to protect soldiers and civilians alike from its horrors.

Quantities

Offering table
A stiff drink
Spirit money

Directions

  1. On his birthday, the 13th day of the 5th month of the Chinese calendar, set up an offering table for the Lord of War.
  2. Serve him a stiff drink.
  3. Burn spirit money and tell him what you need.
