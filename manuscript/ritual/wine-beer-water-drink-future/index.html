Lughnasadh

A playful Polish divination tradition to predict future spouse prospects using the three harvest liquids: wine, beer, and water. Performed with a partner (preferably non-romantic) who chooses a beverage while you sit with your back turned.

Quantities

  • One glass of wine
  • One pilsner of beer
  • One cup of water
  • A table
  • Optional: a mirror

Directions

  1. Set a glass of wine, a pilsner of beer, and a cup of water on a table in a room.
  2. Sit with your back to the room so your expression cannot influence your partner's choice.
  3. If desired, position yourself so you can watch through a mirror.
  4. Have your partner enter the room after you are seated and invite them to drink from any of the three beverages.
  5. Interpret the result:
  6. Wine: You will marry rich
  7. Beer: You will always have work
  8. Water: You will stay single
  9. This charm works best with a partner who is not a romantic interest, preserving the playful and unbiased nature of the divination.
