A Full Moon spell performed on a day of Venus, Mercury, or the Moon during the hour of the Moon or Venus. A grotto or shrine is created and decorated to attract the Fair Ones. Lavender incense and a lavender candle are lit, and the grotto is sprinkled with spring water. An offering of muffin and wine is presented with an invocation calling the Fairies to dwell nearby and bless the home. The grotto is tended regularly with tokens and offerings of milk and grain, with wine or whiskey on special occasions.

Quantities

  • Decorations for grotto or shrine
  • Spring water
  • Lavender incense
  • 1 pale lavender votive or small pillar candle
  • 1 muffin (whole grain or cornmeal)
  • Offering dish
  • Wine in a shallow bowl

Directions

  1. Set up a grotto or shrine area to attract the Fair Ones, decorating as desired with flagstone, potted herbs, shamrocks, and delicate wind chimes.
  2. Light the lavender incense and lavender candle.
  3. Sprinkle the grotto with spring water.
  4. Hold up the offering of muffin and wine in front of the grotto and speak:
  5. I call upon thee, Fair Ones, with gifts of cake and wine, I offer blessings unto thee, and bid thee dance and dine.
  6. Come dwell at hand and share this land, that by thee my home shall blessed be.
  7. Set the offerings in the grotto or shrine.
  8. When the incense has burned out, extinguish the candle and leave the grotto.
  9. Light the candle occasionally as desired, and when it is used up, dispose of the remaining wax in the trash and replace with a new candle.
  10. Burn incense occasionally as desired.
  11. Tend the grotto regularly, leaving tokens and offerings of milk and grain or muffin.
  12. On special occasions, leave wine or whiskey instead of milk.
