A Hekate-centered exorcism script for freeing individuals or places from obsession or possession by troublesome spirits. Invokes Hekate's mythological victories, the four guardian spirits from the Sphere of Hekas, Greek female triads, and alliterative sets of Hekate's epithets to bind and expel malevolent entities. Requires trained witnesses and mental health professionals present.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Multiple trained witnesses (for safety and accountability)
  • Mental health professionals trained in treating possession/obsession cases
  • Legal standards of care personnel
    Optional:
  • Holy water or purifying water
  • Protective circle materials
  • Incense for purification

Directions

  1. CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS:
    This exorcism is an example script that must be repeated multiple times or expanded upon by the exorcist. It should NEVER be performed alone.
    Required Precautions:
  2. Multiple witnesses must be present in the room
  3. Mental health professionals trained in treating possession/obsession must be present
  4. Personnel upholding legal standards of care must be present
  5. Possessed or obsessed patients sometimes become physically violent during exorcisms
  6. Would-be exorcists have sometimes committed abuse under the umbrella of exorcism
  7. These precautions protect both the exorcist and the afflicted
    Three Key Features of This Exorcism:
  8. Links to historical or mythological events - This script recalls Hekate's role in overthrowing the Titans, leading Demeter through the underworld, slaying the gigantes Clytius, and her role in the Chaldean Oracles, relating these victories to the current exorcism
  9. Invocation of powerful associated spirits - The guardians from the Sphere of Hekas (Abaek, Pyrhum, Ermiti, Dimgali) and sets of female triads from Greek mythology (Sirens, Fates, Gorgons, Harpies, Furies) create a surrounding effect on the troublesome spirit
  10. Invocation of different epithets - Certain names or powers are more effective at thwarting certain spirits. This exorcism calls upon epithets of Hekate in alliterative groups of three. Longer exorcisms dedicated to her contain one hundred names
    ---
    The Exorcism Script
    Opening Invocation:
    Oh, spirit who is oppressing [name of afflicted person or place]
    I exorcise and bind you in the name of Hekate,
    Who holds sway over the old gods and the new
    As Zeus called on Hekate
    To gain dominion over the Titans,
    I claim dominion over you, [insert name of spirit if known]
    Through her power.
    As Demeter called on Hekate to reveal Persephone's fate,
    And overcome the trials of the underworld,
    I call on her to name you, [spirit name],
    And overcome all your power.
    As Hekate slew the gigantes Clytius with her twin fire brands,
    She casts you out, [spirit name],
    As she moves effortlessly between the formless and the formed worlds of Had and Hadad,
    There is no refuge from her light
    By the Secret Names:
    By the secret names Aktiphis, Ereshkigal, Nebotosoaleth
    You are chased from the realms of the living, the dead,
    And the dreaming and you are bound here before me.
    Elemental Banishment with Guardian Spirits:
    By Hekate's divine breath and by Abaek's flashing swords
    You are chased from the Air
    By Hekate's fiery spirit and by Pyrhum's flaming breath
    You are chased from the Fire
    By Hekate's boiling blood and by Ermiti's poison chalice
    You are driven from the Water
    By Hekate's incorruptible flesh and by Dimgali's shackles
    You are driven from the Earth
    Universal Constraint:
    There is no sphere that is beyond her reach
    There is no place that can grant you refuge from her command
    I exorcise you, [spirit name], that you withdraw from [person/place]
    Never to return.
    If you do not listen and obey this instance
    You will be chased ceaselessly by Hekate's hoard
    Binding by Triads:
    As all triads belong to her you are thrice exorcised and bound
    By a pentagram of triads you are exorcised and bound!
    The Sirens bewitch you with their song
    The Fates bind you with their thread
    The Gorgons petrify you with their gaze
    The Harpies steal sustenance with their claws
    The Furies rip you from [person/place] and carry you off to Tartarus in chains
    Epithets of Power:
    In the name of Hekate Trimorphos
    I cast you out!
    In the name of Hekate Hexacheira
    I cast you out!
    In the name of Hekate Soteria
    I cast you out!
    By Trimorphos! Trionymos! Triphthoggos!
    You are bound!
    By Propolos! Propylia! Prothyraea!
    You are constrained!
    By Aoroiboros! Aoroidamnia! Aoroianaxsia!
    You are subdued!
    By Damnamene! Damnaseneia! Damnodania!
    You will obey!
    By Nykteria! Nyktiphania! Nyktiboos!
    You are exorcised!
    By Pasiphaessa! Pasimedousa! Pasimedeonsa!
    You are cast from [person/place] and shall go to [destination]
    Closing:
    It is done!
    ---
    Notes on the Epithets:
  11. Trimorphos: Three-formed (referring to her triple nature)
  12. Hexacheira: Six-handed
  13. Soteria: Savior
  14. Trionymos: Three-named
  15. Triphthoggos: Three-voiced
  16. Propolos: Guide
  17. Propylia: Before the gate
  18. Prothyraea: Before the door
  19. Aoroiboros: Untimely-devouring
  20. Aoroidamnia: Untimely-subduing
  21. Aoroianaxsia: Untimely-ruling
  22. Damnamene: Taming
  23. Damnaseneia: Subduing
  24. Damnodania: Controlling
  25. Nykteria: Nocturnal
  26. Nyktiphania: Night-shining
  27. Nyktiboos: Night-howling
  28. Pasiphaessa: All-shining
  29. Pasimedousa: All-ruling
  30. Pasimedeonsa: All-protecting
    Repetition and Expansion:
    This script is a short example. True exorcisms require:
  31. Multiple repetitions of the entire script
  32. Expansion with additional prayers and invocations
  33. Persistence over multiple sessions if necessary
  34. Constant monitoring by mental health professionals
    The exorcist should be prepared for the work to take considerable time and should never assume a single recitation will be sufficient.
