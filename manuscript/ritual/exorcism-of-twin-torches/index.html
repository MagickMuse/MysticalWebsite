🔥Exorcism of Twin Torches

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
InvocationBanishingBinding
◇Category

◇Quantities
Required: - Multiple trained witnesses (for safety and accountability) - Mental health professionals trained in treating possession/obsession cases - Legal standards of care personnel Optional: - Holy water or purifying water - Protective circle materials - Incense for purification
◇Directions
CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS: This exorcism is an example script that must be repeated multiple times or expanded upon by the exorcist. It should NEVER be performed alone. Required Precautions: - Multiple witnesses must be present in the room - Mental health professionals trained in treating possession/obsession must be present - Personnel upholding legal standards of care must be present - Possessed or obsessed patients sometimes become physically violent during exorcisms - Would-be exorcists have sometimes committed abuse under the umbrella of exorcism - These precautions protect both the exorcist and the afflicted Three Key Features of This Exorcism: 1. Links to historical or mythological events - This script recalls Hekate's role in overthrowing the Titans, leading Demeter through the underworld, slaying the gigantes Clytius, and her role in the Chaldean Oracles, relating these victories to the current exorcism 2. Invocation of powerful associated spirits - The guardians from the Sphere of Hekas (Abaek, Pyrhum, Ermiti, Dimgali) and sets of female triads from Greek mythology (Sirens, Fates, Gorgons, Harpies, Furies) create a surrounding effect on the troublesome spirit 3. Invocation of different epithets - Certain names or powers are more effective at thwarting certain spirits. This exorcism calls upon epithets of Hekate in alliterative groups of three. Longer exorcisms dedicated to her contain one hundred names --- The Exorcism Script Opening Invocation: Oh, spirit who is oppressing [name of afflicted person or place] I exorcise and bind you in the name of Hekate, Who holds sway over the old gods and the new As Zeus called on Hekate To gain dominion over the Titans, I claim dominion over you, [insert name of spirit if known] Through her power. As Demeter called on Hekate to reveal Persephone's fate, And overcome the trials of the underworld, I call on her to name you, [spirit name], And overcome all your power. As Hekate slew the gigantes Clytius with her twin fire brands, She casts you out, [spirit name], As she moves effortlessly between the formless and the formed worlds of Had and Hadad, There is no refuge from her light By the Secret Names: By the secret names Aktiphis, Ereshkigal, Nebotosoaleth You are chased from the realms of the living, the dead, And the dreaming and you are bound here before me. Elemental Banishment with Guardian Spirits: By Hekate's divine breath and by Abaek's flashing swords You are chased from the Air By Hekate's fiery spirit and by Pyrhum's flaming breath You are chased from the Fire By Hekate's boiling blood and by Ermiti's poison chalice You are driven from the Water By Hekate's incorruptible flesh and by Dimgali's shackles You are driven from the Earth Universal Constraint: There is no sphere that is beyond her reach There is no place that can grant you refuge from her command I exorcise you, [spirit name], that you withdraw from [person/place] Never to return. If you do not listen and obey this instance You will be chased ceaselessly by Hekate's hoard Binding by Triads: As all triads belong to her you are thrice exorcised and bound By a pentagram of triads you are exorcised and bound! The Sirens bewitch you with their song The Fates bind you with their thread The Gorgons petrify you with their gaze The Harpies steal sustenance with their claws The Furies rip you from [person/place] and carry you off to Tartarus in chains Epithets of Power: In the name of Hekate Trimorphos I cast you out! In the name of Hekate Hexacheira I cast you out! In the name of Hekate Soteria I cast you out! By Trimorphos! Trionymos! Triphthoggos! You are bound! By Propolos! Propylia! Prothyraea! You are constrained! By Aoroiboros! Aoroidamnia! Aoroianaxsia! You are subdued! By Damnamene! Damnaseneia! Damnodania! You will obey! By Nykteria! Nyktiphania! Nyktiboos! You are exorcised! By Pasiphaessa! Pasimedousa! Pasimedeonsa! You are cast from [person/place] and shall go to [destination] Closing: It is done! --- Notes on the Epithets: - Trimorphos: Three-formed (referring to her triple nature) - Hexacheira: Six-handed - Soteria: Savior - Trionymos: Three-named - Triphthoggos: Three-voiced - Propolos: Guide - Propylia: Before the gate - Prothyraea: Before the door - Aoroiboros: Untimely-devouring - Aoroidamnia: Untimely-subduing - Aoroianaxsia: Untimely-ruling - Damnamene: Taming - Damnaseneia: Subduing - Damnodania: Controlling - Nykteria: Nocturnal - Nyktiphania: Night-shining - Nyktiboos: Night-howling - Pasiphaessa: All-shining - Pasimedousa: All-ruling - Pasimedeonsa: All-protecting Repetition and Expansion: This script is a short example. True exorcisms require: - Multiple repetitions of the entire script - Expansion with additional prayers and invocations - Persistence over multiple sessions if necessary - Constant monitoring by mental health professionals The exorcist should be prepared for the work to take considerable time and should never assume a single recitation will be sufficient.
◇Description
A Hekate-centered exorcism script for freeing individuals or places from obsession or possession by troublesome spirits. Invokes Hekate's mythological victories, the four guardian spirits from the Sphere of Hekas, Greek female triads, and alliterative sets of Hekate's epithets to bind and expel malevolent entities. Requires trained witnesses and mental health professionals present.
