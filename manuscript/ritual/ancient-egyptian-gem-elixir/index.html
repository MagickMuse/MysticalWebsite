🌅Dawn

An ancient Egyptian technique for creating gem elixirs by harnessing the morning dew that collects around crystals left on the grass overnight. The dew absorbs the stone's energy through prolonged ground contact under the open sky.

Quantities

  • Crystals of choice (cleansed)
  • Access to a grassy outdoor area

Directions

  1. On a clear evening, lay your cleansed crystals directly on the grass in an outdoor space where they will remain undisturbed through the night.
  2. Leave them exposed to the open sky until morning.
  3. At dawn, carefully collect the dew that has gathered on and around the stones.
  4. This moisture, having spent the night in intimate contact with the crystals and the earth beneath them, now carries the energetic imprint of both.
  5. Bottle the dew and use it as you would any gem elixir.
