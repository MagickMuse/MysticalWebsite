◇Directions
1. A Winter Retreat 2. During winter's dark quiet, many Pagans feel an urge to reflect, make plans, and put their lives in order, and it's an urge underpinned by strong biological and magickal roots. 3. For these reasons, winter is a powerful time for personal retreat. 4. What is retreat? 5. Humans have long sought ways to separate themselves from their daily routines by turning inward. 6. The monastic cloister, the vision quest, and even the vacation are all kinds of retreat. 7. You can combine place, purpose, magickal practices, activities, and ritual to create your own winter retreat, focusing on settings and activities that correspond to the season. 8. Ideas for a winter retreat: 9. • Your retreat can occupy anything from a couple hours to a long weekend or even to several days. 10. Choose a time and place in which you can be quiet, undisturbed, and need attend to no one but yourself. 11. • Honor the nature and value of silence and meditation as part of your retreat, both as a nod to winter's silence and also to benefit your own inward journey. 12. Turn off electronic devices. 13. • Prepare for the retreat by gathering everything you'll need and purifying yourself in some way: shower, smudge, or whatever works for you. 14. • Open with a ritual or a simple statement of your purpose. 15. • Engage in activities that focus on planning, reflection, and personal growth. 16. Journaling and divination are fabulous winter retreat activities, as is planning a course of research or study. 17. Some folks use winter retreat time to sew garb or create magickal tools. 18. • Work with magickal correspondences that link you to winter. 19. • If you wish, carry out meditation, spellwork, or ritual to support your aims. 20. • If weather allows, consider a long, reflective walk outdoors, watching and listening for sights, sounds, and messages. 21. Walks at the liminal times of sunrise and sunset will be especially wonderful. 22. • Bring light into your retreat with light strings, candles, or the magick of a hearth fire. 23. Sit quietly with the light, enjoying its presence. 24. • Enjoy nutritious, healing foods to nurture your body and inspire your soul. 25. • Celebrate your retreat with a closing ritual, and if possible, create a small charm or talisman to keep with you as a reminder of the event. 26. And now, settle back and embrace the season. 27. Turn off your cell phone, computer, and television and sit quietly with the lights or by the fire, contemplating the quiet depths of winter. 28. Take walks. 29. Feed the birds. 30. Read a new book. 31. Meditate. 32. Play board games with your friends or family. 33. Be grateful for the lessons of winter and joyous as you anticipate the return of the light.
◇Description
A comprehensive guide for creating a personal winter retreat, from a few hours to several days, focusing on silence, reflection, planning, and spiritual wholeness through various contemplative activities.
