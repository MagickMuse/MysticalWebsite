🧘Meditation 101

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationBreathworkVisualization
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
A quiet place Loose-fitting, comfortable clothes A straight-backed chair, or a bed or floor to lie on Optional: a blanket
◇Directions
1. Preparation: You will need a quiet place where you will not be disturbed. Wear loose-fitting, comfortable clothes. 2. Positioning: Many people like to meditate while sitting in a straight-backed chair with the backs of their hands resting on their thighs or lap. 3. However, if you find it hard to relax in that position, it is perfectly acceptable to sit in a more comfortable chair or lie down on a bed or the floor. 4. Make sure the room is warm enough, as you are likely to lose a degree or two of body heat during meditation. 5. You might like to cover yourself with a blanket. 6. Stage One: Creating Your Safe Space When you are ready, close your eyes and take a few slow, deep breaths until your breathing slows down. The first stage of meditation is to temporarily release all your everyday worries and concerns. 7. Finding Your Secret Place: It is helpful to imagine a beautiful, secret room. 8. Whenever you go into this room you automatically release all tensions and concerns. 9. This room is an imaginary place. 10. You might create an imaginary scene, or go to a place you have been in the past where you felt totally relaxed, serene, and happy. 11. Designing Your Space: If you choose to create an imaginary place, you can place anything you wish inside it. 12. A typical secret room might have lush carpet, comfortable furniture, and beautiful paintings on the walls. 13. It is always pleasantly warm. 14. Working with Persistent Worries: If you have a particular worry you find hard to discard, focus on the concern. 15. Think about it for as long as you wish. 16. Once you have done this, give the concern a color and a shape. 17. In your mind's eye, allow the shape to grow larger and smaller. 18. Make the shape as small as you can, and then release it from your secret place. 19. You might attach it to a helium balloon so it floats away, place it in a sink and turn on the tap, or place it outside your door. 20. Stage Two: Breath Focus Now that your worries have been temporarily put aside, focus on your breathing. Take steady, rhythmic breaths. As you inhale, say to yourself: "relaxation coming in." As you exhale, say silently: "tension and stress going out." Continue doing this for as long as you can. 21. For Beginners: It is a good idea to stop at this point if you have not meditated before. 22. Open your eyes and sit quietly for a minute or two. 23. Repeat the meditation up to this stage several times over the next week or two. 24. Stage Three: Finding the Stillness Within In your mind's eye focus on a spot just above your navel. Inhale deeply and exhale slowly. As you exhale, silently say the word "ten." Continue to focus on the spot just above your navel as you take nine more deep breaths and count down to one. Forget about your breathing and allow yourself to sink deeply into the area above your navel. Enjoy the pleasant, comfortable, tranquil sensation for as long as you wish. 25. When you have had enough, allow your concentration to return to your head and focus on your breathing again. 26. Picture yourself in your secret place, and then mentally return to the present. 27. Visualize your surroundings for a few moments before opening your eyes. 28. When you feel ready, stand up and stretch. 29. After Your First Meditation: You may feel excited and full of energy, or slightly tired. Everyone is different. If you feel disappointed, it simply means you need to practice. Reaching the stillness within is something everyone can do, some find it easily, while others have to work at it. 30. Troubleshooting: The most common reason people fail is rushing through the breathing and relaxation stages. Take each step more slowly next time. The other possibility is overanxiety. If this applies, experiment again with an attitude that you don't care if you are successful, just enjoy the experiment. 31. Building a Practice: Practice meditation on a regular basis. Twice a day would be ideal. A brief meditation in the middle of the day will make you more productive. The key to successful meditation is breathing and relaxation.
◇Description
A foundational meditation practice for beginners that guides you through creating a safe mental space, releasing worries, focusing on breath, and finding the stillness within. Regular practice develops the ability to reach deep meditative states.
