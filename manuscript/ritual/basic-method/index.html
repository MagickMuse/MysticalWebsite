◇Directions
1. Formulate your question clearly. 2. Close your eyes, flip the book open to a random page, and place your finger somewhere on the open page without looking. 3. Open your eyes and read the word or passage your finger has landed on. 4. Interpret what you find as the oracle's response to your question.
◇Description
The foundational method of bibliomancy: divination through the random selection of a passage in any large book. Sacred texts are traditional choices, though dictionaries, encyclopedias, and other substantial works serve equally well. One of the Bibliomancy methods.
