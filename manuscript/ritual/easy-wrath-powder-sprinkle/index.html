🌹Easy Wrath Powder Sprinkle

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
PowderWriting Spell
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
ProtectionPowder MagickHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
Paper and pen Rose petals Sandalwood powder Sweet Hungarian paprika 6 drops jasmine attar
◇Directions
1. Write your wishes, goals, and desires on paper. 2. Burn the page. 3. Mix the ashes with rose petals, sandalwood powder, and sweet Hungarian paprika, and grind to a fine powder. 4. Add six drops of jasmine attar. 5. Let the powder dry. 6. Traditionally, toss the powder over your target so they love you again, or at least stop being angry. 7. If you would rather not get near them, sprinkle it discreetly where they will step over it or sit on it.
◇Description
Ashes of written wishes ground with rose, sandalwood, sweet paprika, and jasmine attar into a powder that eases someone's anger toward you.
