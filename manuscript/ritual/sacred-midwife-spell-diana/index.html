Sacred Midwife Spell: Diana

◇Tradition
Roman
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PetitionCandle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
White candles An image of the crescent moon A bow and arrow (or image of them)
◇Directions
1. Petition Diana for an easy childbirth. 2. Light white candles. 3. Place an image of the crescent moon beside them, along with a bow and arrow. 4. Diana may be understood as an aspect of Artemis, primal deity of childbirth, wild nature, and the moon, who dwells in the Forest of Nemi with her consort Virbius.
◇Description
A candlelit petition to Diana for easy childbirth, with white candles set beside a crescent moon image and a bow and arrow.
