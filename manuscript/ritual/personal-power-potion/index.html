To empower you, increase awareness of your powers, help develop and strengthen personal powers, and encourage using power wisely. Teaches that 'power with' is better than 'power over.'

Quantities

Ginseng tea (per the Tea Potion Spell)
Small quartz crystal
Cinnamon stick
Optional: orange or magenta cup, or a mug decorated with your power animal or astrological sign

Directions

  1. Brew ginseng tea according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. Place a small quartz crystal in the cup. Do not ingest the crystal.
  3. Use a cinnamon stick to stir the potion exactly eight times.
  4. After stirring eight times, trace <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/1f516dcf7164817d9b22cb7885ee15ea"/>, the rune of strength and personal power, into the surface of the tea.
  5. As you drink, visualize your personal power growing and expanding, seeing yourself becoming more confident, more capable, and more aware of your innate gifts.
  6. After drinking, wash the stone and reserve it for future use. Discard the cinnamon stick.

This tea potion empowers you and helps you develop, strengthen, and wisely use your personal powers. It also teaches an important lesson: that "power with" is better than "power over." Remember that true power comes from working with others, not dominating them.
