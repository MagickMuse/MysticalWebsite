👑Commanding Oil

A simple commanding charm in which ginger root anointed with Commanding Oil is carried in a conjure bag to project and sustain a dominant presence. ⚠️ Commanding Oil Ginger Charm is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

  • A piece of ginger root
  • Commanding Oil
  • A conjure bag

Directions

  1. Anoint a piece of ginger root with a drop of Commanding Oil.
  2. Place it in a conjure bag and carry it on your person to maintain a commanding and dominant presence.
