👤Eight-Night Image Candle Debt Spell

An eight-night image-candle working to compel debt repayment and loosen a tight-fisted target's generosity: two figure candles, dressed with bergamot-enhanced Command and Compel Oil and Jezebel Oil, are pinned through their giving and receiving hands and burned nightly until the debt is repaid or, on the ninth night, a Jezebel-root wax effigy is buried on the target's property or at a crossroads. ⚠️ Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick. Approach with respect and awareness of its cultural origins.

Quantities

  • Two human figure candles (green for the target; red, white, or purple for yourself)
  • Command and Compel Oil
  • Essential oil of bergamot
  • Jezebel Oil
  • Brown paper
  • Command and Control Incense
  • Pins
  • Jezebel root

Directions

  1. Obtain two human figure candles: a green one to represent the other person, and a red, white, or purple one to represent yourself.
  2. Carve identifying marks or names into both candles as needed.
  3. Blend five drops of essential oil of bergamot into Command and Compel Oil, then dress both candles with the mixture. Separately, rub the green candle with Jezebel Oil.
  4. Record your goal on a piece of brown paper and pass it through the smoke of burning Command and Control Incense.
  5. Slide the smoked paper beneath the green candle.
  6. Pierce the candle representing yourself with a pin through whichever hand you consider the receiving hand.
  7. Pierce the other candle with a pin through its giving hand, typically the right hand unless personalized otherwise.
  8. Light both candles.
  9. Speak the other person's name aloud three times and state your need firmly.
  10. Allow the candles to burn briefly, then extinguish them by pinching out the flames.
  11. Repeat the working for eight consecutive nights, stopping early if your desire is granted.
  12. If the debt remains unpaid by the ninth night, remove a portion of wax from the green candle.
  13. Shape the wax into a ball and press a Jezebel root into its center.
  14. Let both candles burn down completely, burning the paper note beforehand.
  15. Mix the note's ashes into the wax ball.
  16. Bury the finished wax ball on the target's property or somewhere in their regular path. Use a crossroads or a cemetery as an alternate burial site if neither option is possible.
