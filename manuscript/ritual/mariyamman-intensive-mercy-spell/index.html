An intensified petition to Mariyamman for protection and mercy against smallpox. A brass pot filled with water and neem leaves is placed on an altar, surrounded by yellow candles, and pumpkins are offered.

Quantities

Brass pot
Water
Neem leaves
Yellow candles
Pumpkins

Directions

  1. Place a brass pot filled with water and ornamented with neem leaves on an altar.
  2. Burn yellow candles around it.
  3. Offer Mariyamman pumpkins and request her protection and mercy.
