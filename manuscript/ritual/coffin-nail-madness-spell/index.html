A modern spell using a voodoo doll and a coffin nail soaked in Black Arts Oil for nine nights. On the tenth night the nail is driven into the doll's head, cursing the target with headaches, memory loss, and depression before the next full moon.

Quantities

1 doll (labeled with target's name)
1 coffin nail
Black Arts Oil
1 bottle or small container

Directions

  1. Label a doll carefully with the target's name.
  2. On the first evening of a full moon, place a coffin nail into a bottle of Black Arts Oil, ensuring the nail is fully submerged.
  3. Leave the nail to soak for nine nights.
  4. On the tenth night, remove the nail and pound it into the head of the doll.
  5. Before the next full moon arrives, the target is said to be cursed with severe headaches, faulty memory, and deep depression.
