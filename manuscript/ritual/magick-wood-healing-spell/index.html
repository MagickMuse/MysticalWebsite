A seven-night fire magick ritual using magickally powerful woods such as oak, thorn, or rowan. A charred fragment of wood is dropped into cold water each night while envisioning the illness breaking apart and disappearing.

Quantities

Magickally powerful wood (oak, thorn, or rowan recommended)
Pot of cold water
Metal tongs

Directions

  1. Create a fire with magickally powerful wood.
  2. Concentrate on your desired healing goals while gazing into the fire.
  3. When the fire has almost died out and the wood has been reduced to charred fragments, pick one piece up using metal tongs.
  4. Drop the wood into a pot of cold water while simultaneously envisioning the illness breaking up, dispersing, and disappearing.
  5. Repeat for seven consecutive nights.
