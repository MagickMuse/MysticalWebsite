Mabon

A 24-card tarot spread designed for the autumn equinox to examine what you have sown and what you will reap. Three groups of eight cards reveal patterns from distant past, immediate past/present, and distant future, with special interpretive weight given to key cards at the equinox.

Quantities

  • One tarot deck
  • Space to spread 24 cards
  • Optional: camera to photograph the reading
  • Optional: notebook to record gut responses

Directions

  1. Prepare your space with room to lay out 24 cards in three separate groups.
  2. First Group (Distant Past):
    Shuffle the deck thoroughly. Lay down four cards in a row, these represent what you have sown in the distant past. Directly beneath each of these four cards, lay down four more cards, these represent what you will reap from those past actions. This creates an eight-card group showing cause and effect from your distant past.
  3. Second Group (Immediate Past and Present):
    Gather all cards, shuffle again, and repeat the process. Lay down four cards representing what you have sown in your immediate past, then four cards beneath showing what you are currently reaping. This eight-card group reflects recent patterns.
  4. Third Group (Distant Future):
    Gather all cards one final time, shuffle, and repeat the process a third time. The top four cards show what you are sowing now, and the bottom four reveal what you will reap in your distant future.
  5. Interpretation Guidelines:
    At the equinox, certain cards carry additional weight beyond their usual meanings:
  6. The Empress: Supreme fertility signal.
  7. Upright means all systems go.
  8. Inverted prompts examination of relationships with women around you, asking whether you see mutual respect and shared power.
  9. The Chariot: Signals visitors or journey.
  10. Upright suggests you may travel, inverted suggests you may receive visitors.
  11. Both can strain resources, so examine surrounding cards for timeline hints.
  12. Justice and Judgment: Justice restores what was lost, Judgment calls for responsible action.
  13. These often appear together.
  14. If Temperance appears near them, what has happened is a direct consequence of your choices.
  15. If The Magickian appears, you have power to change it.
  16. If The Priestess appears, seek higher wisdom.
  17. The Tower: Drastic change (standard interpretation holds true).
  18. Death: Permanent, deep psychological transformation (standard interpretation holds true).
  19. Optional: Take photos of each spread and write notes about your gut responses.
  20. Review these readings from one harvest to the next, noting when you change course or seek additional wisdom, observing how these shifts affect your life over time.
