AngelicaCypressJuniper

A named incense blend combining cypress, angelica, and juniper with healing, strengthening, and empowering properties. It also opens the road to the next realm when that transition is inevitable. Use dried powdered botanicals, whole herbs, or essential oils.

Quantities

  • Cypress
  • Angelica
  • Juniper

Directions

  1. Blend cypress, angelica, and juniper in dried powdered form, as whole botanicals, or as essential oils.
  2. Burn the blend as incense in the presence of a person who is approaching or has recently crossed the threshold of death.
