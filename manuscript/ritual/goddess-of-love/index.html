The Ethics of Love Magick

What brings you to needing a love spell? Is there an empty place in your heart, your bed, or your life? Would you like companionship? Do you yearn for love?

Magick can help with these things, but love spells are tricky. You have to be careful with them. To cast a selfish or mean-spirited love spell is a magickal mistake. Even if the spell seems to work, it will ultimately backfire on you in some way.

This spell is meant for single people who are free to enter new relationships with other single people. It invites love rather than compelling it. If you use it as directed, there will be no magickal backlash.

Why the Goddess of Love?

When you need love, you can turn to the Love Goddess for help. Venus, Atargatis, Aphrodite, Ishhara... the Goddess of Love has been known by different names in various cultures throughout human history. This spell invokes her by many of those names, alerting her that you are available to give and receive love.

Recognize that love works best when it is mutual and reciprocal. You will have the most success with this spell if you focus on what you want to give, as well as on what you hope to get, while you cast it.

Timing

The spell takes five days to complete. It is best begun five days before the Full Moon (use a calendar to time it). If you seek passionate love, cast it at night rather than during the day.

The Importance of Specificity

As you prepare the tray, concentrate on the kind of love you wish to invite into your life. Love takes many forms, so it is important to be specific in love spells. If you fail to make yourself clear, you might wind up with a new puppy instead of the lover you had in mind. Visualize what you want, and focus on it.

The Incantation

By Hera, by Rhea, by Olwen and Maia,
By Sarah, by Vara, by Oshun and Gaia,
By Goda, by Holda, by Allat and Inanna,
By Lada, by Milda, by Blathnat and Diana,
By Anahita, Esmerelda, Dictynna, and Ishhara,
By Cytherea, Syria Dea, Shekinah, and Ostara,
I declare myself ready to receive love!

Alter the final line to reflect the type of love you seek. This might be "romantic love," "passionate love," "the true love of a good partner," or whatever is appropriate.

Understanding the Visualization

During the spell, concentrate on the love that you need. Do not focus on a specific person or on physical or character traits. Think about the way that the person your spell attracts will feel about you, on how that will make you feel, and on how you will make the other person feel. Paint a picture in your mind of how you would like things to be.

Open your hands. Hold them slightly to your sides with the palms facing upward as you chant. Repeat the incantation three times.

Working Over Five Days

Repeat the spell every day for five days. If candle wax or incense ashes accumulate on the tray, consider them as adding to the magick and leave them there. Keep the tray in a place where you will often see it during the five days. Each time you notice it, concentrate on the love you wish to manifest in your life.

Disposal

Disassemble the tray after you cast the final spell on the fifth day. Items that you wish to retain, such as the tray, should be washed in running water. Everything else should be buried near your home. If that is not practical, bury the charms in a potted plant inside your home.

What to Expect

Be mindful of the spell ingredients you buried, and remember that their magickal vibrations are working. Know in your bones that you have cast the spell effectively, and wait patiently but expectantly for the spell to manifest.
