🍯Sweeten Up Household Harmony Spell

◇Form
Jar
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
↗Correspondences1
🔥Hearth Magick
↗Materials1
🍯Honey

A honey jar working to restore harmony and sweeten relations among household members. Uses honey's binding and sweetening properties to smooth tensions and encourage cooperation.

Quantities

  • 1 small jar with lid
  • Honey (enough to fill jar)
  • Paper and pen
  • Optional: cinnamon stick, rose petals, or lavender

Directions

  1. Write the names of all household members on a small piece of paper.
  2. If tensions exist between specific people, write their names facing each other.
  3. Fold the paper toward you three times, turning it clockwise between each fold.
  4. As you fold, speak:
  5. From the fields and the trees,
    Sweetness flows with greatest ease.
    Harsh words soften, tempers cool,
    Harmony becomes the rule.
  6. Place the folded paper in a small jar.
  7. Pour honey over it until covered, speaking your intention for household peace as you pour.
  8. Seal the jar.
  9. Keep it in the kitchen, preferably near the stove or in a cabinet.
  10. Once weekly, shake the jar gently while repeating the charm.
  11. When harmony is restored, bury the jar in your yard or dispose of contents at a crossroads with thanks.
