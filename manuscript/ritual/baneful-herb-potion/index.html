⚗️Baneful Herb Potion

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
PotionPoppet
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- Baneful herbs (aconite, belladonna, foxglove, moonflower, or henbane), harvested by night under the waning moon - Oil (enough to fill a cauldron) - Wax figure in the likeness of the target - Paper bearing the target's full name - Personal links to the target (hair, nail clippings, dirt from their footprint, etc.) - Iynx - The Infernal Knife (consecrated blade housing BAINKHOOKH)
◇Directions
1. When the moon wanes, purify yourself and consecrate a private space in the usual manner. 2. Light a fire and place a cauldron of oil over it to heat. 3. While the oil heats, chant the vowels of Apollon's Lyre, then summon Hekate using your iynx and the MASKELLI MASKELLO formula (see Consecration of the Dagger). 4. Once the oil simmers, drop in the baneful herbs without allowing skin contact. All are mortal poison. 5. Let down your hair. Drop to hands and knees. Bark loudly three times, merging the last bark into the following, repeated thrice: EUPHORBA PHORBOREU PHORBA PHORBOR BORPHA BORBORPHA PANPHORBA 6. Rise before the cauldron. Point to its contents with the Infernal Knife in which BAINKHOOKH resides, and say: Spit of the black dog Sweat of a corpse Tears of a black god Serpent's source Seed of a hanged man Menses flood Orion's urine Gorgon's blood 7. Dip the knife into the oil, reciting the following while making each stated action: O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Sink beneath the waves of Styx; (stir the blade clockwise in the oil) O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Drink beneath the waves of Styx; (dip the blade deeply into the oil) O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Sleep beneath the waves of Styx; (hold the blade still within the oil) O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Wake beneath the waves of Styx; (tap the blade against the cauldron) O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Rise above the waves of Styx; (draw the blade slowly out) O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness Walk across the River Styx. (draw an X shape on the oil's surface) Flush, furious, full of force, envenomed With five rivers' blood. And with you, bring the Spirit of aconite/ Belladonna/foxglove/moonflower/henbane. 8. Lift the blade and address the gathered plant spirits as the oil slides off: Spirits of aconite/belladonna/foxglove/moonflower/henbane attend! Before you stands the one who wields BAINKHOOKH; Before you stands a servant of the Black Queen. Before you stands a serpent with a bull's head. Before you stands a bull with a serpent's tail. Before you stands the great serpent-bull, Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness, BAXYXSYXYX; Who calls you by your great and secret name, which is AGONEXA; In the name of Persephone-Artemis-Selene, Rise up before me. Spirits, I bid you seek the soul of [Name of your target], Who defies the great and harms the good. Hear me, hear me, hear me! (Hereafter name the target:) [Name] has kicked a pregnant bitch and laughed as it miscarried. [Name] spat into a glass and gave the drink to Kore. [Name] shat at the crossroads path, and left that as a levy; [Name] swears that Artemis' shafts have never struck their quarry. [Name] says that Rhea's face is twisted and misformed; [Name] brags that Hekate's foes have never come to harm. And besides this, she/he has (now list their actual crimes). 9. Strike the cauldron thrice with the blade's edge, then say: O Son-of-Darkness, Soul-of-Darkness, As the sickle reaps the wheat; As the sieve removes the chaff; Let this blade cleave [Name], As it splits this form in half. 10. Pierce the wax figure with the heated blade and slowly cut it in half, barking out the following thrice: EUPHORBA PHORBOREU PHORBA PHORBOR BORPHA BORBORPHA PANPHORBA 11. Hold the wax remains on the blade tip over the simmering oil until they melt. Fling them into the cauldron, shouting: NEBOUTOSAOULETH! IA! 12. Draw a sigil on the earth with the blade's oil-soaked tip. The sigil corresponds to the epithet of Hekate most fitting the aspect invoked (such as BRIMO). 13. Safely pour the oil into a sealed container for future baneful workings. Deconsecrate the space and extinguish the fire. Wash the Infernal Knife and cauldron with care, allowing no flesh contact with the oil or anything it has touched. Thank BAINKHOOKH as you cleanse the blade and reward it later. A cauldron used in this rite must not be reused for any purpose other than baneful work.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A Greco-Egyptian vengeance rite in which poisonous herbs are brewed in oil over fire, baneful plant spirits are summoned through the Infernal Knife, and a wax figure of the target is destroyed via a slander spell addressed to the chthonic goddesses.
