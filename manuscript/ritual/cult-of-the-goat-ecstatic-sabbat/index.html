🐐Cult of the Goat: Ecstatic Sabbat

A group ecstatic sabbatic rite bridging archaic Bacchic and medieval sabbatic traditions, performed with at least two people. One person plays the Darkness (cloaked, masked, bearing the consecrated goat skull on a stang); the rest are Maenads. The rite has three sections: the Revel, in which the Maenads dance counterclockwise around a witch-fire and chant until the Darkness emerges to join them; the Feast, in which the Maenads kneel and hymn Dionysus before each feeding the skull from her own bared breast; and the Congress, in which the Darkness becomes the Goat God and carries each Maenad in turn while she holds the xoanon aloft and envisions riding to the Moon.

Quantities

Goat skull (consecrated xoanon): 1, lit from within by a votive candle
Votive Candle: 1, inside the skull
Stang: 1, to display the skull
Mask: 1 black mask (such as the Mask of Dionysus), worn by the Darkness
Cloak: 1, worn by the Darkness
Bowl: 1 fire-bowl, central to the Revel
Drum: 1 or more
Wine: an offering, brought by the Maenads

Directions

  1. ROLES: One person plays the Darkness (cloaked, masked). All others are Maenads.
    THE REVEL:
  2. The Darkness precedes the Maenads to the sacred, secure space. He sets the consecrated skull upon the stang, then conceals himself nearby and waits.
  3. The Maenads approach bearing a fire-bowl, drum, and an offering of wine.
  4. Upon reaching the space, the Maenads light a witch-fire in the central clearing and consecrate the space in the usual way.
  5. They gather around the fire-bowl and begin dancing counterclockwise, spinning clockwise as they go. At least one drums a wild rhythm. All chant the Hermekate Drum Chant repeatedly.
  6. As ecstasy builds, the leader begins the call-and-response chant, with the rest replying as indicated: "O Black Radiance! / O Divine! O Everliving! O Roarer! Rager! Render of flesh! / O Dancer! Disaster! Deathless Death! / O Masker! Master! Withheld breath / That finds relief in a shout! / Come out! (Come out!) Come out! (Come out!) / Come out! (Come out!)"
  7. When the time is right, the Darkness emerges and seamlessly joins the dance, moving with ecstatic joy.
    THE FEAST:
  8. Eventually the Darkness ceases dancing; the Maenads cease as well. They face him and kneel.
  9. The lead Maenad speaks the Feast Hymn (others repeat the words in parentheses): "O child (O child!) conceived in a grave; / O twice-born (twice-born!) bull who raves; / O goat (O goat!) whose coat is flayed / To hold the blood of the vine! / IA! / O cat (O cat!) whose maddened scream / Begets (begets!) the waking dream / Of women (women!) who careen / In syncopated time! / IA! / O snake-tailed (snake-tailed!) bull who's bound / To a pillar (pillar!) under ground, / Who's scourged (who's scourged!) until the ground / Runs red with sacred wine! / IA!"
  10. At the final cry, the Darkness takes the skull from the stang, approaches the lead Maenad, and holds it out. She takes it and presses its mouth against her bared breast, saying: "Behold, I am a black goat's bride; / Behold, I am a wife! / Behold, I bare a breast to feed / The one whose tongue's a knife. / Take, Lord, from this breast that bleeds, / The sacred stuff of life: / Drink me, Dionysus! Make me / The black goat wife!"
  11. The rest of the Maenads call out: "Khaira, Hekahermes! Khaira, Hermekate! / Khaira, Twice-Born Bull of the Earth! / EUOI SABOI, DIONYSUS!"
  12. The Darkness touches the middle finger of his left hand to the Maenad's brow and inscribes it with his personal sigil. He takes the skull back and presents it to each Maenad in turn, who feeds it and receives the sigil, until all have done so.
    THE CONGRESS:
  13. The Darkness sinks to his knees and hands the skull to the lead Maenad. He removes his cloak and goes down on all fours.
  14. The lead Maenad lightly scourges him, then climbs onto his bare back, straddling him and facing away from his head toward the other Maenads. She holds the xoanon high above her.
  15. As the Darkness arches his back upward, she closes her eyes and envisions herself riding to the Moon. (Optional: his back may be coated with a cream of baneful herbs, mingling with her skin to heighten trance.)
  16. As she rocks on his arched back, holding the skull high, she proclaims in ecstasy: "EUOI SABOI!" The rest echo the cry.
  17. The Darkness lowers her to the ground. She kisses the skull's brow and whispers to it any blessing or favor she would call down, then climbs off. Each willing Maenad takes her turn.
  18. Once all Maenads have ridden, the Darkness rises and holds the skull high above his head. Seeing it, the Maenads raise their hands and cry: "EUOI SABOI, DIONYSUS!"
  19. When they look up again, the Darkness is gone, and so is the skull. The Maenads take the fire-bowl, disenchant the space, and leave together in silence.
