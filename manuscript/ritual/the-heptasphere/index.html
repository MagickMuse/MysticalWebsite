CircleGrey

A ritual for the management and regulation of planetary energies based on PGM XIII 824. The Heptasphere acts as a barrier and filter between macrocosmic planetary forces and the microcosmic individual, rebalancing and regulating energies before they reach the practitioner. Uses the seven planetary seals positioned around a sphere with corresponding Greek vowels.

Quantities

  • Visualization of the seven planetary seals
  • Knowledge of Greek vowel pronunciations

Directions

  1. Begin with the Gesture of Eviction.
  2. Make a fist with your left hand and place it over your heart.
  3. Cover it with your right hand and apply about five pounds of pressure.
  4. Imagine that power concentrates at the heart, taking the shape of a gray sphere about the size of a baseball.
  5. Visualize this clearly.
  6. Release the pressure and in one motion step forward, throwing your arms out as the energy in the sphere explodes forth.
  7. As the sphere grows and spreads, it pushes back all malefic forces and harmful spirits, growing past your body and continuing for miles.
  8. Accompany this with the statement:
  9. HEKAS HEKAS ESTE BEBELOI!
  10. After the force has cleared your sphere, retract the energies and form them into a sphere around you.
  11. Face East.
  12. Hold your hand out and visualize the symbol of the Moon in shimmering silver branded onto the sphere.
  13. Vibrate the syllable Alpha (Ah).
  14. Move your hand and see the symbol moving with it along the surface of the sphere.
  15. Feel for changes in the energies being transmitted.
  16. Eventually you will feel a click that indicates the optimum placement for the seal.
  17. This is called tuning.
  18. Let intuition and spirit be your guide.
  19. Turn North.
  20. Hold out your hand and visualize the symbol of Mercury branded onto the sphere.
  21. Vibrate the syllable Epsilon (eh).
  22. Tune as above.
  23. Turn West.
  24. Hold out your hand and visualize the symbol for Venus branded onto the sphere.
  25. Vibrate the syllable Eta (ay, like in fate).
  26. Tune as above.
  27. Turn South.
  28. Hold out your hand and visualize the symbol for the Sun.
  29. Vibrate the syllable Iota (ee, like in tree).
  30. Tune as above.
  31. Touch the ground and visualize the symbol for Mars at the nadir of the sphere, which may be under the floor upon which you stand.
  32. Vibrate the syllable Omicron (oh, as in old).
  33. Touch your own heart and visualize the symbol of Jupiter blazed upon it.
  34. Vibrate the sound Upsilon (eu, the ee sound with lips rounded).
  35. You can move this to different points in the body such as the head or feet.
  36. This represents your own sovereignty over the sphere.
  37. Finally, reach upwards and visualize the symbol for Saturn over your head.
  38. Vibrate the sound Omega (oh, as in home).
  39. After accomplishing this, make the following prayer:
  40. I call upon the eternal and unbegotten Aeon
    who holds together the creation of all things
    whom none understand
    and whom the gods worship.
    Empower the sphere that surrounds me
    that the seven rays be a balanced and beautiful blessing
    that dignify me and my work.
    Amen.
