👻Absinthe Seeing Spirits Summoning

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Ancestor WorkCandle MagickMeditationVisualization
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- 1 black or white candle - Absinthe for anointing - Candle scribe, ballpoint pen, or nail - Optional: photograph of the deceased
◇Directions
1. Anoint a black or white candle with absinthe and allow it to dry completely. 2. Do not light while still wet; the alcohol content is highly flammable. 3. Carve the name of the deceased loved one into the candle with a candle scribe, ballpoint pen, or nail. 4. Include their birthdate if known. 5. Consider adding a photograph if you have one. 6. If using a framed photo, position it so you can see the flame of the candle reflected in the photo's glass. 7. Make sure the photo is far enough away to safely stand and not present a fire hazard. 8. If the ancestor is unknown, write "ancestor" or some similar significator. 9. Burn on a Friday if the ancestor is beloved; otherwise, burn on a Saturday, the day associated with the dead. 10. While the candle burns, meditate on their face, their name, or their connection to you while you await your message.
◇Description
A candle ritual for connecting to the afterlife and communicating with deceased loved ones, using absinthe-anointed candles to thin the veil between worlds.
