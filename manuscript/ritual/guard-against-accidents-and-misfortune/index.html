🛡️Guard Against Accidents and Misfortune

◇Form
PouchCharm
◇Method
Salt MagickProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A bowl of salt - A feather - A bowl of water - Nine, in total, of any of the following blunt or rusty items: a knife, scissors, a razor blade, metal nails, pins, needles, or screws - A large piece of plastic protective wrapping - A large padded bag - A red pen
◇Directions
1. Sprinkle salt around each item, except the plastic protective wrapping and the bag, moving counterclockwise, saying for each one: "Danger be blunted and lose your edge." Spiral the feather nine times over each item, moving counterclockwise and repeating the same words over each one. 2. Spread out the plastic wrapping, adding each item separately. 3. Close the wrapping, placing the parcel in the padded bag, repeating the spell words nine times as you do so. 4. On the front of the bag write in red pen: BLADE TURN YOUR SHARPNESS FROM ME/MY FAMILY. MAY THIS PROTECTION LAST FOR YEARS, THREE BY THREE. 5. Put the parcel in an inaccessible place. 6. Repeat the spell every nine years. 7. Perform at a full moon.
◇Description
A full moon spell to guard you or family members against accidents, misfortune, or attack, sprinkling salt and spiraling feather counterclockwise over nine blunt items then wrapping in bag for danger be blunted and lose your edge.
