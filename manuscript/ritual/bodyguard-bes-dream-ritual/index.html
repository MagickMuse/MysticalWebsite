An ancient Egyptian ritual calling upon Bes, the beloved protective spirit who decorated Egyptian beds as a night watchman, to send a specific dream answer. Bes normally serves as Isis's only male spiritual companion. This working draws his protective attention toward the dreamer by drawing his image on papyrus, reciting a Names of Power incantation, and sleeping with consecrated black cloth wrapped around the hand and neck.

Quantities

  • Dove's Blood Ink
  • One additional drop of myrrh (to add to the ink)
  • Raspberry juice, pomegranate juice, or grenadine syrup (a little, to add to the ink)
  • A piece of papyrus
  • A black strip of cloth or black scarf (to be consecrated to Isis)

Directions

  1. Prepare the ink: take Dove's Blood Ink and add an extra drop of myrrh and a small amount of raspberry or pomegranate juice, or grenadine syrup.
  2. Stir together.
  3. Consecrate the black strip of cloth or scarf to Isis.
  4. Using the prepared ink, draw an image of the protective spirit Bes on the papyrus.
  5. Use your non-dominant hand; left hand if right-handed, right hand if left-handed.
  6. While drawing and then writing, begin reciting a Names of Power incantation.
  7. Open with the words: "Send the truthful seer out of the holy shrine." Invoke those you believe will protect you and send you the needed dream.
  8. You may use ancient names or create your own invocation of Names of Power.
  9. Ancient practitioners favored long repetitive chains of sacred names.
  10. Write out the specific question requiring a dream answer on the papyrus while continuing the incantation.
  11. Chant your Names of Power and conclude with the words: "Come this very night!"
  12. Take the black strip of cloth.
  13. Wrap one end around your left hand and the other around your neck.
  14. Go to sleep.
  15. Bes may appear in person with your answer, or the answer may arrive as a dream.
