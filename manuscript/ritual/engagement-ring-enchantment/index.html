A fifteen-day love enchantment that transforms any ring into a talisman of love through incense fumigation, silk wrapping, and the binding of the couple's hair.

Quantities

Frankincense resin
Myrrh resin
Patchouli
Vetivert
Lit charcoal
Red silk thread
Silk cloth
3 hairs from the practitioner
3 hairs from the beloved

Directions

  1. Tie the ring to a length of red silk thread.
  2. Burn frankincense, myrrh, patchouli, and vetivert together on lit charcoal and suspend the ring in the smoke until thoroughly fumigated.
  3. Remove the thread and wrap the ring securely in silk cloth.
  4. Carry this packet against your skin for nine days and nights, ideally against the heart or tucked under the left armpit.
  5. After nine days, pass the ring through the incense smoke a second time.
  6. Braid three of your own hairs with three hairs taken from your beloved.
  7. Wind this braid around the ring.
  8. Wrap the ring and braid once more in silk and carry it against your skin for six more days.
  9. On the seventh day of this second wearing, remove the lover's braid and present the ring to your beloved as an enchanted ring of love.
