💐Maypole Garland Toss to Attract a Lover

Beltane

A Beltane ritual where a woman seeking a partner tosses a circular garland over the top of the Maypole, signifying the sacred union and asking the Goddess to send her a lover.

Quantities

  • 1 circular garland or wreath (made of flowers, vines, or ribbons)
  • A Maypole or tall pole
  • Flowers for decoration (optional)

Directions

  1. Create or obtain a circular garland, traditionally made from flowers, vines, or woven ribbons.
  2. The circular shape represents the feminine, the receptive, the womb.
  3. Approach the Maypole, which represents the masculine, the active, the phallic.
  4. Hold your garland and focus on your desire for a partner.
  5. Visualize the qualities you seek in a lover; not a specific person, but the type of relationship you want.
  6. Toss the circular garland over the top of the pole.
  7. The garland encircling the pole signifies the sacred union of God and Goddess, the Great Rite.
  8. As the garland lands, say aloud or silently: "Goddess, send to me a lover true, one whose heart beats as mine does too." Leave the garland on the pole as an offering, or keep it as a charm.
