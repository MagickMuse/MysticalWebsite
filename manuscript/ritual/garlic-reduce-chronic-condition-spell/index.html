🧄Garlic Reduce Chronic Condition Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Salt MagickHealingChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A clove of garlic or garlic powder - A dish of salt - An area of earth
◇Directions
1. Crush the garlic clove into the salt or sprinkle the powder, saying: "Cleansed from me, buried be, that new growth I shall see [or name person]. May new health blossom, flourish, the old obliterated, decimated, expurgated, deep in Mother Earth so new growth will give birth to healthy me." Bury the mix in a deep hole in the earth, repeating: "May new health blossom. Flourish, the old obliterated decimated, expurgated, deep in Mother Earth so new growth will give birth to healthy me." Perform Saturday late.
◇Description
A Saturday late garlic spell to reduce a chronic condition that frequently flares up, crushing garlic into salt and burying deep in earth for new growth and health to blossom.
