A petition ritual directed to Juan Soldado, an unofficial folk saint executed in 1938 for a crime he did not commit. He is particularly beneficial for those facing false accusations, slander, or situations where others believe untruths about them. He has also become a patron for those seeking safe passage when emigrating across borders.

Quantities

  • Candles
  • Liquor
  • Cigarettes

Directions

  1. Offer Juan Soldado candles, liquor, and cigarettes as customary gifts.
  2. Make your petition clearly, particularly if you face false accusations or situations where others believe the worst about you.
  3. If requesting a significant favor, make a sincere vow to visit his tomb in Panteon I in Tijuana, Mexico, should he fulfill your request.
