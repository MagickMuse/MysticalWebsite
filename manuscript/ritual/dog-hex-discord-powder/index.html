🐕Dog Hex Discord Powder

A hex that destroys the target's peace and harmony, sowing enmity and discord. Dirt collected from the site of a dog fight is combined with cat hair, salt, black pepper, and cayenne or habanero pepper to create a powder that is sprinkled wherever one wishes to sow enmity.

Quantities

Handful of dirt from where dogs were fighting (collected after the fight ends)
Cat hairs (picked from a cat-lover's sofa or clothing)
Salt
Black pepper
Cayenne or habanero pepper

Directions

  1. Go to a dog park and wait for dogs to fight, ideally a mass free-for-all with gnashing teeth and wild barking.
  2. Do not intervene in the fight.
  3. When the battle ends, gather a handful of dirt from where the dogs were fighting.
  4. Pick some cat hairs off a cat-lover's sofa or clothing.
  5. Grind the dirt and hair together with salt, black pepper, and cayenne or habanero pepper until a fine powder is created.
  6. Sprinkle this powder wherever you wish to sow enmity.
