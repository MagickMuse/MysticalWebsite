🏆Competing Horse Animal Shows

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellCharm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method

◇Category

◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- A flyer or printout of a promo for the event from the Internet - A green pen - Scissors - Glue or tape
◇Directions
1. On the flyer or printout, write your name in large letters and SUCCESS, FIRST PLACE, FIRST PRIZE, THE WINNER, BEST OF BREED, and as many winning phrases and words as you can think of. 2. Cut the paper in four, saying four times: "To the four corners of the world, my victory will be unfurled." Glue or tape the four pieces back together, saying: "It all comes together, victory will come to me." Fold the paper and take it with you to the event. 3. Look at it before you start, saying or thinking both sets of spell words fast. 4. Perform the day before the event.
◇Description
A day before event spell for competing in horse or animal shows, events, and races, writing success phrases on event flyer then cutting and gluing back together for victory to be unfurled to four corners of world.
