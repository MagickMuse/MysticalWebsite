🕯️Ostara for Two

◇Form
Circle RiteAltar Working
◇Method
InvocationElementalDirectional MagickMeditationDevotional Work
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Altar with representations of Earth, Air, Water, and Fire - Deity representations: gold item or candle for the God, silver item or candle for the Goddess - Element tools: water in a clear container, earth (dirt, salt, or sand), burning incense or a feather, one candle for fire - Offerings for the Lord and Lady (your choice, prepared in advance) - Optional: participant-made altar decorations
◇Directions
1. Prepare a single altar as you would for a solitaire rite, including close-to-true representations of all four elements and clear representations of the God and the Goddess. 2. Add any handmade decorations and set out your offerings. 3. Cast your circle together. 4. Call the elements in turn, keeping the spoken lines with line breaks preserved: 5. Sacred air, blessed by the Goddess and watchtowers of the east, join with us tonight on this sacred day of equal day and equal night. 6. Sacred fire, blessed by the Goddess and watchtowers of the south, join with us tonight on this sacred day of equal day and equal night. 7. Sacred water, blessed by the Goddess and watchtowers of the west, join with us tonight on this sacred day of equal day and equal night. 8. Sacred earth, blessed by the Goddess and watchtowers of the north, join with us tonight on this sacred day of equal day and equal night. 9. Invite the deities: Maiden Goddess, soon to be Mother, known by many names, we invoke your presence today, on this day of balance, of equal day and equal night, on this day of beginnings, to join with us in celebration. Goddess of the earth, Goddess of all living things, Goddess of the spring, join with us now. 10. Robust Lord, soon to be Father, known by many names, we invoke your presence today, on this day of balance, of equal day and equal night, on this day of beginnings, to join with us in celebration. God of the earth, God of all living things, God of spring, join with me now. 11. Alternate lines to sustain balance: Today is the day of the Vernal Equinox. It is the day the earth awakens, with streams flowing once again. The sap courses through trees, bearing the announcement of new life. Snow melts away, the grass begins its greening, and soon flowers shall be fragrant on the breeze. The earth has come alive once more. Today is a day of balance and a day of new beginnings. Today brings the dawning of a new spring. The light and the dark are in balance. All existence comes together in balance. 12. Face one another and hold both hands. 13. Use a balance-pairs exercise, taking turns naming a concept and answering with its opposite. 14. Continue with your own pairs until complete. 15. Optionally perform a balance meditation (for example, Seeking Balance ), then take turns asking and answering reflective questions about restoring balance and planting new growth this spring. 16. Make petitions together: We have brought forth our desires, our needs, to bring balance and new growth into our lives. Whether that growth be spiritual, emotional, mental, or physical, we ask for the blessings of our lord and lady. We ask for strength and courage when needed. We ask for empathy and compassion when needed. Help us to see the path set before us. Help to light our way. 17. Present offerings: To the Lord and the Lady we present these offerings... These gifts we give to honor the love and devotion we wish to express and to thank the Lord and the Lady for their guidance, love, and support. We honor thee with these gifts. 18. Close with thanks and release the quarters in reverse order, preserving the spoken lines. End together: The circle shall be open but remain unbroken.
◇Description
A two-person Ostara working focused on cooperative balance and shared preparation. Partners co-lead casting, quarter calls, invocations, balance reflections, and offerings while honoring the God and the Goddess.
