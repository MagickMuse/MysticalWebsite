🌱The Thorn Apple Homecoming

An alternative Romani homecoming rite to prevent secondary infertility, using thorn-apple seeds, goose fat, breadcrumbs, and brandy. ⚠️ This is a Romani working. Romani spiritual practices are closed and should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Datura (thorn apple) seeds
Goose fat
Breadcrumbs
Brandy
A new baby (first homecoming)

Directions

  1. Before the baby is brought home, scatter thorn-apple seeds in all the spots where the baby will be laid.
  2. When the baby arrives, place it first on the threshold, then in all four corners of each room.
  3. Next, lay the baby on the hearth, which has been smeared with goose fat beforehand.
  4. Finally, place the baby on the family's customary eating table.
  5. Make a circle around the baby with breadcrumbs and drops of brandy to complete the rite.

An alternative to the breadcrumb-only rite, this version uses datura seeds, goose fat, and brandy as additional activating agents. The hearth placement is given special weight through the anointing with goose fat.

⚠️
Romani spiritual practices are closed. This working belongs to Romani tradition and should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture.
⚠️
Thorn apple (datura) is highly toxic. Ensure the baby cannot put seeds into their mouth at any point during or after the rite.
