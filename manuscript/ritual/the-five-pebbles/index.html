🛡️The Five Pebbles

A protective charm using five pebbles collected from a moving stream. The pebbles are carried at all times to strengthen the wearer's protective shield through their ancient, weathered energy.

Quantities

  • Five small pebbles from a moving stream
  • Optional: small pouch, cloth, or jewelry setting to hold the pebbles

Directions

  1. Go to a moving brook or stream.
  2. Stand in the water facing downstream.
  3. Remove five small pebbles from the streambed while visualizing your need for protection.
  4. As your hand closes over the pebbles, see them beaming out protective energies.
  5. Recognize that just as the pebbles are hard and have weathered for eons, they will strengthen your protective shield.
  6. Carry the pebbles at all times for protection.
  7. If desired, place them in a small pouch, wrap them in cloth, or incorporate them into jewelry.
