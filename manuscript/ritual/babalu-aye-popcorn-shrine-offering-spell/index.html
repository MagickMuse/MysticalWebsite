🍿Babalu-Ayé Popcorn Shrine Offering Spell

Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group

◇Quantities
Raffia cloth Popcorn Palm oil Dry white wine, and/or milk with bread soaking in it (alternative offerings)
◇Directions
1. Create a simple shrine by laying down raffia cloth. 2. Pour palm oil over a bowl of popcorn and place it on the cloth as an offering, allowing it to stand for seventeen days. 3. The offering may be removed earlier as you deem appropriate, as it is not wise to maintain Babalu-Ayé's altar longer than necessary. 4. Use the shrine as the focal point for prayer and petition. 5. After seventeen days, take the popcorn outside to a park or similar location and feed it to the birds. 6. If the popcorn disappears quickly, this is a very favorable sign.
◇Description
A simple shrine to Babalu-Ayé, orisha of pestilential illness and healing, built on raffia cloth with palm-oil-drenched popcorn as offering. Maintained for seventeen days, then dispersed outdoors to the birds. Overly lavish offerings may offend him; keep it simple.
