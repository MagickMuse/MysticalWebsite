🔓Fire Nightmare Banishing

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Banishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Paper - Pen - White, silver, black, or red candle - Matches or lighter - Saucer or tray - Optional: a second plate to cover ashes - Brown paper bag (for ash disposal if needed)
◇Directions
1. Immediately after waking, write out every detail of the nightmare on a fresh sheet of paper. 2. Choose a candle in white, silver, black, or red and light it. 3. Touch a corner of the paper to the flame and let it catch. 4. Allow the paper to burn down entirely in a fireproof bowl or cauldron; an inverted plate may be placed over it to contain the ash. 5. Relight the paper if the flame dies before it is fully consumed. 6. Once nothing remains but ash, carry it outside and scatter or release it. 7. If that is not immediately possible, seal the ash in a paper bag and remove it from the home at the first opportunity.
◇Description
A fire-based banishing spell to burn away nightmare energy. Most effective immediately after waking from a nightmare, but can also be used to clear lingering effects. Open a window during the spell if possible to encourage the nightmare's energy to disperse.
