🌊Healing Water Ritual

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickVisualizationLunarHerbal Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Candle - Toothpick or ritual knife (optional, for engraving) - Rose oil, coconut oil, Moon Oil, or an anointing oil of choice - Rose petals - Cauldron or fireproof bowl - Sand or small rocks - Water, ideally Moon Water; chilled jasmine tea or cucumber-infused water also work - Matches or lighter - Pen and paper (optional) - Lemon wedge (optional) - 1 teaspoon sea salt (optional)
◇Directions
1. If you choose to engrave the candle, use a toothpick or ritual knife to carve a word or symbol representing what you are releasing. 2. Anoint the candle with your oil of choice and press rose petals into the surface for healing. 3. Melt the base of the candle slightly and press it firmly into the center of your cauldron or fireproof bowl, holding it steady until the wax sets. 4. Pour sand or small rocks around the base to keep the candle grounded and upright. 5. With the candle secure, hover both hands around it and let your focus settle on what you are releasing. 6. Breathe into it. 7. Visualize that weight, that pattern, that wound, and feel yourself beginning to pour it into the candle's form. 8. Slowly pour in your moon water (or jasmine tea, or infused water), filling the bowl to roughly halfway up the candle or less. 9. As you pour, see the water as luminous and lunar, charged with the moon's healing energy, flowing in to meet what you are letting go. 10. Light the candle. 11. Hold the image of its flame in your mind: bright now, as this thing once was in your life, but soon to be swallowed by the healing waters, just as the moon dims and disappears in eclipse. 12. On your paper, write what you are releasing, old patterns, fears, wounds, habits, feelings. 13. You may write one thing or many, as feels right. 14. When you are ready, tear the paper so each word or phrase is on its own separate slip. 15. With each tear, feel yourself pulling free. 16. Hold one slip above the bowl. 17. Looking toward the eclipsed moon, or simply holding it in your awareness, speak aloud: 18. "As the moon is eclipsed, I burn all that's on this list. Fire to transform that which I have torn, With salt to cleanse, and water to mend, At this lunar eclipse, to my soul do I tend." 19. Touch the slip to the candle flame, and as it begins to burn, drop it into the water before the fire reaches your fingers. 20. Repeat for each remaining slip, one by one. 21. Now wait. 22. Watch the candle burn lower and lower. 23. Know that the moon is working alongside you, that with each inch of wax that melts, the thing you named loses its hold. 24. Allow the flame to be snuffed naturally by the rising water. 25. Once the flame is out, you may squeeze a lemon wedge into the bowl and swirl the water counterclockwise, symbolically unwinding the energy from your life. 26. Add the salt counterclockwise as well, grounding and cleansing what remains. 27. Carry the bowl outside and pour the water onto the earth, returning its energy to the ground to be neutralized. 28. As you pour, say: 29. "With the waning moon, I cast this energy away. It no longer plagues me, from this day." 30. Close the ritual with healing or creative work, a new intention, a candle for what you wish to invite in, or simply rest. 31. You have made space. 32. Let something better fill it.
◇Description
A lunar eclipse ritual using the dual forces of fire and water to burn away old wounds, patterns, and fears, then wash them free. As the candle flame slowly drowns in moon-charged water, the light of what you are releasing dims and extinguishes. What remains is cleansed, grounded, and ready to begin again.
