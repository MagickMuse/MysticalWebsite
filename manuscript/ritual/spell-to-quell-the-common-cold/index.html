🤧Spell to Quell the Common Cold

◇Form
Bath
◇Method
Herbal MagickVisualizationHealing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Spring water - Hyssop leaves and/or flowers - An aqua beeswax candle with a cotton wick - A candleholder - Matches or a lighter
◇Directions
1. Brew a strong tea from the hyssop leaves and/or flowers. 2. Strain the herb residue from the water. 3. Fill a bathtub with comfortably hot water and add the tea to it. 4. Fit the candle in its holder and light it. 5. Get into the tub and soak for as long as you like, inhaling the soothing scent of hyssop. 6. Focus your mind on loving thoughts and feelings. 7. Envision yourself surrounded by love. 8. As you inhale, imagine you are bringing love into your body. 9. See and sense love circulating through your entire body, from head to foot. 10. Spend several minutes doing this. 11. Let the loving energy gently nourish you, strengthening your system so it can throw off the cold. 12. Remain in the tub for as long as you like. 13. After you get out of the bathwater, extinguish the candle. 14. Repeat this pleasant ritual whenever you like.
◇Description
A healing bath spell using hyssop tea and candlelight to relieve cold and flu symptoms through herbal magick and loving visualization.
