An ancient Egyptian protective working using the papyrus hieroglyph as a talisman against crocodiles. Papyrus was the hieroglyphic emblem of Lower Egypt and the Nile Delta swamplands, places of crocodile territory and sacred refuge. The protective symbolism extends to a candle invocation of Isis, Sobek, and Mami Waters, all spirits who both protect crocodilians and shield people from them.

Quantities

  • Parchment and drawing implement (for hieroglyph talisman)
  • Henna or tattoo ink (optional, for body application)
  • Photograph of living papyrus plants (optional)
  • Beta Israel lidded Moses basket talisman (optional)
  • Green candle
  • Altar with deity image (crocodile for Sobek or Mami Waters; serpent for Isis)

Directions

  1. Draw the papyrus hieroglyph on parchment and carry it as a talisman.
  2. For stronger protection, draw the hieroglyph onto the body using henna; for constant danger, consider a permanent tattoo.
  3. Alternatively, carry or prominently display a photograph of living papyrus plants.
  4. A traditional Beta Israel pottery piece depicting a lidded basket revealing baby Moses inside may also serve as a talisman.
  5. To invoke spiritual protection through deity, charge a green candle with your fears and desires, carving and dressing it as needed.
  6. Place it on an altar dedicated to your chosen spirit.
  7. Sobek and Mami Waters may be represented by a crocodile image; Isis by a serpent.
  8. State your needs aloud.
