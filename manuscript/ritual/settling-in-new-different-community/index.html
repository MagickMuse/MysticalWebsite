🏘️Settling in New Different Community

◇Form
Food/EdibleAltar Working
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- A selection of local produce that can be cooked - A cooking pan - Dried bay leaves or sage, if produce is savory; allspice or cinnamon, if produce is sweet - A wooden spoon
◇Directions
1. Chop up the produce and place it in the pan, saying: "So am I absorbed into this community, so take in its different ways. That at home here I will be and welcome feel always." Add the herbs or spices and suitable liquid and stir the pot, saying: "Though I will not lose my roots, yet can I cultivate new shoots, to mix and blend and soon fit in and from my new neighbors respect win." Cook the produce and invite your new neighbors to share the meal. 2. Perform Thursday morning.
◇Description
A Thursday morning spell for settling in new community very different from your old one, cooking local produce with bay sage allspice or cinnamon and stirring to absorb into community and cultivate new shoots. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
