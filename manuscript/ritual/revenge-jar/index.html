🫙Revenge Jar

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary

◇Directions

Items

  • 1 jar with a lid
  • Apple cider vinegar
  • Dried wormwood
  • Black peppercorns
  • Chili flakes
  • Salt
  • 1 grape
  • Pins
  • A piece of paper with the target's name, or their photo
  • A rusty nail (optional)

Instructions

  1. Fill the jar with apple cider vinegar. Sprinkle in dried wormwood, black peppercorns, chili flakes, and salt.
  2. Take a grape and pierce it with pins like a pincushion.
  3. Drop the grape and any extra pins into the vinegar mixture. A rusty nail can also be added for extra potency.
  4. Place a piece of paper bearing the target's name or their photo into the jar.
  5. Seal the jar tightly and shake it well.

Burial

Bury the jar outside and allow it to rot. The target will experience an awful run of bad luck that worsens as the fruit decays.

Reversal

To remove the curse, the jar must be smashed.
