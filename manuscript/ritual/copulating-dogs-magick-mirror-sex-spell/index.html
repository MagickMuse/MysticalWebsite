◇Directions
1. Purchase a small hand mirror with some sort of back to it. 2. Pay the asking price without haggling. 3. Remove at least some of the backing and write your beloved's name three times in that space. 4. Find a pair of copulating dogs and, without disturbing them, hold the mirror so their image is reflected in it. 5. Do not look into the mirror yourself. 6. Somehow induce the object of your desire to gaze into the mirror. 7. Then hide the mirror for nine days in a location your beloved is guaranteed to pass frequently. 8. After nine days, carry the mirror on your person. 9. The target will allegedly become sexually aroused whenever in your presence.
◇Description
A hand mirror with a solid back is inscribed with the beloved's name, used to reflect copulating dogs, then induced to be viewed by the target. Hidden for nine days near their path, then carried on the caster's person to cause sexual arousal in the target's presence.

This source's variant (warlock's charm): Performed on a Thursday at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM. The mirror is gifted to the target; on seeing their reflection they are overcome with lust. The spoken charm, recited while catching the copulating dogs' reflection in the mirror and bending an intense basilisk gaze upon the scene:

I the dog and she the bitch,
I the helve and she the axe,
I the cock, and she the hen.
As my will, so mote it be!

The loaded mirror is then presented as a gift. One look and the effect takes hold.
