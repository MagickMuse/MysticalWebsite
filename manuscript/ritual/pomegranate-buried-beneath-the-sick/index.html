Pomegranate

A Mesopotamian healing rite in which a whole pomegranate is buried to draw illness out of a sick person's body and into the earth.

Quantities

One whole, ripe pomegranate, unbroken
Small trowel or spoon
One white candle
Clean white cloth
Optional: written petition to slip under the fruit before burial

Directions

  1. Light the white candle where it can be seen from the place you will dig.
  2. Hold the pomegranate in both hands at chest height and name the sick person and the illness they carry aloud.
  3. Speak plainly to the fruit, telling it what you are asking it to take.
  4. If using a written petition, slip it under the fruit now.
  5. Dig a hole at the threshold of the home (beneath a floorboard, in a houseplant pot, or in the garden bed nearest the front door), deep enough to fully cover the pomegranate.
  6. Lay the pomegranate in whole and uncut, and say aloud that the illness goes with it.
  7. Fill the hole, press the soil down, and snuff the candle.
  8. Wash your hands with the white cloth.
  9. Leave the fruit undisturbed for a full lunar cycle; when the moon returns to the same phase, the rite is complete.

This rite is attributed to the unmarried priestesses dedicated to Inanna-Ishtar and to the Sumerian goddess of death and healing in her Gula path. The pomegranate's correspondence with both resurrection and immortality is used to draw illness away from the living body and into the seeds, which carry it down into the earth. The rite can be adapted to a modern home with a houseplant pot, a garden bed at the threshold, or a buried jar.

Notes

If the sick person recovers, leave the buried fruit in place as thanks and plant a new pomegranate seed nearby if the climate allows. If the illness deepens, the rite is best supported by medical care and additional spiritual work, not repeated alone. This is a folk healing practice and not a substitute for medicine. This rite belongs to a specific Mesopotamian priestess lineage and should be approached with respect for that tradition. Do not perform it on someone else's home without their knowledge and consent.
