🛡️Safe Travel Arrival Unrest Area Spell

A spell for safe travel and arrival especially if there has been previous unrest or plane crash, using Saint Christopher medallion or turquoise/moonstone/pearl with blessed water and cross for protection.

Quantities

  • A Saint Christopher medallion or a turquoise, moonstone, or a pearl, either on its own or as part of a necklace or bracelet, or a lucky pendant
  • A paper knife
  • A bowl of water
  • A bowl of salt

Directions

  1. Add three pinches of salt to the water and draw a cross on the surface with the paper knife, saying: "Blessings be on this water, preserve me/us from all danger, accidents, crashes, harmful situations, and ill-intentioned strangers." Scatter water drops over the medallion/gems (empower one for each family member; you can even attach them to pet collars), repeating the words.
  2. With the paper knife, draw a cross in the air above the medallion(s) and then add a circle around that, saying: "With gates of steel, so am I/are we bound, that safety around me/us will be found." Wear the medallion(s) while traveling and on vacation.
  3. Perform two or three times in the week before you travel.
