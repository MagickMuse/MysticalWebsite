🌧️Rainy Day End Destructive Relationship

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
Water MagickBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A large circle of green paper - A black pen - Pens of various colors - Scissors
◇Directions
1. On one half of the paper circle, draw an image to represent yourself in black pen, and on the other half, the two of you together. 2. Draw a jagged line down the middle and cut the paper circle in half. 3. Place the half representing the relationship from which you wish to be severed in the rain, saying: "Not to harm you, but to calm; you must let go, it shall be so. New love you'll find when you release me from your heart and mind." Leave the half representing the relationship in the rain until it has dissolved. 4. Color the image of yourself in bright patterns and write across it: I AM FREE. 5. Pin it on a wall until you find new love and forge a new life without your ex. 6. Perform when the rain is steadily falling and looks set to continue for much of the day.
◇Description
A when rain is steadily falling spell for second rainy day ritual to end destructive relationship when lover or ex won't accept that it's over, cutting green paper circle in half and leaving relationship half in rain to dissolve for freedom and new love.
