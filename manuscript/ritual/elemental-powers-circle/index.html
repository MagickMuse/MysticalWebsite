A wand-based circle casting with explicit Light and Element quarter calls, invoking each direction with specific elemental powers and colors.

Quantities

  • 1 wand
  • Yellow candle (East/Air)
  • Red candle (South/Fire)
  • Blue candle (West/Water)
  • Green candle (North/Earth)

Directions

  1. Take your wand and point it outward at the eastern edge of where the circle will be.
  2. Walk clockwise around the perimeter, visualizing energy flowing from the wand and creating a sphere of protection.
  3. Return to the East and seal the circle.
  4. Face East and raise your wand.
  5. Visualize golden yellow light filling the eastern quarter.
  6. Say:
  7. Powers of Air, I call upon thee!
    Bring clarity of thought and freedom of spirit.
    Hail and welcome!
  8. Light the yellow candle.
  9. Turn to face South.
  10. Visualize brilliant red light filling the southern quarter.
  11. Say:
  12. Powers of Fire, I call upon thee!
    Bring passion, courage, and transformation.
    Hail and welcome!
  13. Light the red candle.
  14. Turn to face West.
  15. Visualize deep blue light filling the western quarter.
  16. Say:
  17. Powers of Water, I call upon thee!
    Bring intuition, emotion, and healing.
    Hail and welcome!
  18. Light the blue candle.
  19. Turn to face North.
  20. Visualize rich green light filling the northern quarter.
  21. Say:
  22. Powers of Earth, I call upon thee!
    Bring stability, abundance, and grounding.
    Hail and welcome!
  23. Light the green candle.
  24. When ready to close, release the quarters in reverse order (North, West, South, East), extinguishing each candle and thanking the elemental powers:
  25. Powers of [Element], I thank thee for thy presence.
    Go if you must, stay if you will.
    Hail and farewell!
  26. Walk counterclockwise with your wand, drawing the circle's energy back.
  27. Say:
  28. The circle is open but never broken.
    Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again.
