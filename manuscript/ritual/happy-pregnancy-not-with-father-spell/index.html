🌹Happy Pregnancy Not With Father Spell

A spell for happy pregnancy if you are not with the father of the child, placing rose petals on pictures to promise beloved, security, carefree childhood, and happy family even if just you two.

Quantities

  • A picture of yourself with the baby's father if he is in the child's life, otherwise a family member or friend who has been supporting you
  • A scan picture
  • Four pink roses in a vase

Directions

  1. Place the pictures with the scan on top, next to the vase.
  2. Take the first rose, putting a single petal on top of the pictures, saying: "You will always be beloved, your life through, even if just me and you." Add a petal from the second rose, saying: "You will always know security with me, even if just me and you." Add one from the third rose, saying: "You will have a carefree childhood, your life through, even if just me and you." Add one from the fourth rose, saying: "You will always have a happy family, your life through, even if just me and you." Release four rose petals outdoors, saying all four sets of words.
  3. When the roses fade, add the petals to a potpourri or dried lavender in a bowl in the infant's future room.
  4. Perform if you are feeling emotionally low.
