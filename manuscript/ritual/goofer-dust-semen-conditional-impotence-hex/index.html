⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING A conditional hex combining reserved semen and Goofer Dust, buried where the target will step, allegedly causing insanity and impotence if he ever attempts infidelity. ⚠️ This is a Hoodoo formula. Hoodoo is a closed practice rooted in African American folk magick traditions. Engagement outside the originating culture is spiritually inadvisable.

Quantities

Reserved semen (from a condom or similar, with no intermingled sexual fluids)
Goofer Dust
Paper or fabric for wrapping

Directions

  1. Obtain reserved semen from a source such as a used condom, ensuring no other sexual fluids are present.
  2. Add Goofer Dust.
  3. Wrap the mixture in paper or fabric.
  4. Bury it in earth at a location the target will step over.
  5. Nothing occurs while he remains faithful.
  6. If he ever attempts sex with another, he allegedly suffers both insanity and impotence as a result.
