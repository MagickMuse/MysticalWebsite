💦Florida Water

A strengthened doorstep wash of red brick dust, cascarilla, salted boiling water, Indigo Water, Florida Water and urine, used to scrub the front steps and threshold.

Quantities

Red brick dust
Cascarilla Powder
Boiling salted water
Indigo Water
Florida Water
Your own urine
Bucket

Directions

  1. Put red brick dust and Cascarilla Powder into a bucket.
  2. Pour boiling salted water over them.
  3. Once the water cools, add Indigo Water, Florida Water and some of your own urine.
  4. Scrub the steps and the threshold area of your front entrance.

⚠️ Some versions add lye to this wash. Lye is dangerous to handle, especially around children and animals, so it has been left out here.
