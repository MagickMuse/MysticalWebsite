◇Directions
1. Write an explicit description of your situation on brown paper. 2. Wrap the paper around some knotgrass. 3. Charge the packet with your desires, and then burn it. 4. Pay attention to your dreams and random words overheard; solutions will appear and you don't want to miss them.
◇Description
A spell to untangle seemingly insoluble problems. Write your situation explicitly on brown paper, wrap it around knotgrass, charge the packet with your desires, and burn it. Solutions will then appear through dreams and overheard words.
