🔥Sabbat Rite: Opening the Balefire

The traditional opening sequence of the witches' Sabbat. Performed at the exact moment of midnight. Covers circle casting, removal of all metals, laying of the balefire from nine woods, the solar invocation, the lighting of need fire, and the Hertha earth consecration. Ends with the saluting of the magister as the Sun at Midnight and the high priestess as Our Lady of the Moon.

Quantities

  • Nine varieties of wood for the balefire (choose species that burn well; poplar and cornel/dogwood are most traditional; other good choices: oak, birch, hawthorn, holly, beech, yew, pear, apple, ash, pine, cherry, sandalwood, cedar, juniper)
  • Spirit or alcohol (to aid lighting, optional)
  • Quick-lighting charcoal (optional bed under the balefire)
  • Need fire taper (lit by friction or by concentrated sunlight; matches are acceptable)
  • Coven cingulum (as compass cord for the 9-foot circle)
  • Four lamps of art (unlit, placed outside at the four quarters for indoor use)
  • Thurible with Sabbat Incense
  • Sword or athame (for tracing the outdoor circle trench, dug deosil from outside the perimeter)
  • Sabbat cake (crumbled and cast at the west after the Hertha chant)
  • Small candle or taper for the magister's headdress (lit from the balefire)

Directions

CIRCLE: Cast the nine-foot circle using the cingulum as a compass cord. The triple tracings and fire-and-water exorcisms are performed from outside the circle. Fumigate with Sabbat Incense. For outdoor workings, trace a narrow shallow trench deosil with the sword, standing outside the perimeter. The northern segment serves as the gateway: the way between the worlds.

METALS: All coven members enter through the northern gateway, but first remove every metal object -- witch jewels, headpieces, sword, athame, chalice, everything metallic -- and leave them outside. This is essential. Metals interfere with the delicate vibrations about to be initiated. They also carry associations with worldly currency and are removed as a gesture of ritual poverty and purity before the gods.

BALEFIRE: Lay the balefire at the centre of the circle from nine varieties of wood, set crossways on one another. Sprinkle with alcohol or lay quick-lighting charcoal beneath if needed.

MIDNIGHT: Extinguish all lamps and lights. If indoors, darken the whole house. The coven stands silently in a ring facing inward, hands clasped.

SOLAR INVOCATION: The magister moves to the east of the circle and bows nine times eastward. The summoner places an unlit taper in the magister's right hand. The magister chants:

"Eastward stand I!
For favours I pray!
I pray thee, Mighty Prince of Light!
I pray thee, Holy Keeper of the Sky!
Up to Heaven I pray
and down to Earth I call thee!"

The coven responds in unison:

"Eastward he stands,
For favours we pray.
We pray thee, Great One!
We pray thee, Mighty Prince of Light!
We pray thee, Holy Keeper of the Sky!
Up to heaven we pray
and down to Earth we call thee!"

All visualize elemental power descending as a great flake of fire or triple tongue of light upon the taper as it is lit.

BALEFIRE LIGHTING: The magister applies the taper flame to the balefire. As it blazes up, the coven chants, visualizing the coven symbol as a living, coruscating form burning in the fire's centre:

"IO EVOHE! [EEE-OOO, AAH VOH AIEE] Blessed be!"

The magister circles the balefire three times southways and halts again in the east.

HERTHA CHANT: The high priestess prostrates herself to the west, pounding the earth three times flat-handed for each utterance of the goddess name:

"Hertha! (three blows)
Hertha! (three blows)
Hertha! (three blows)
Mother of Mankind,
hail to thee, Great Mother!
Be fruitful in god's embrace!
Be filled with food for the use of men!"

The coven repeats this chant, feeling the answering earth beneath them. The high priestess crumbles a Sabbat cake and casts it in the circle trench at the west (or on the boundary line indoors).

RE-ENTRY OF METALS: With the balefire blazing, all metal implements and witch jewels are brought in from the north. The leaders assume their headdresses. The taper on the magister's headdress is lit from the balefire. He stands in the east facing west, arms crossed on the breast.

SALUTATION: The coven salutes the magister with the horned hand (right hand raised, fingers clenched, thumb and little finger extended) as the Sun at Midnight. The high priestess stands in the west, feet apart, arms raised in a wide crescent. She is likewise saluted with the horned hand as Our Lady of the Moon.

At this point the magister and high priestess seek to set their personalities aside and embody the god and goddess. Magickal operations required by the coven are then performed, followed by any initiations, so that new initiates may join the ceremonial dances.
