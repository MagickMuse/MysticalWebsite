🎉Harmonious Office Party Spell

A night before party spell for harmonious office party, threading brightly colored beads on string while naming troublemakers to tie up dissension and bind in peace for amicable and profitable connections.

Quantities

  • A tub of brightly colored beads with holes
  • A long string on which to thread them

Directions

  1. Tie a knot in the left-hand end of the cord, saying: "I tie up all dissension, I bind in peace these I mention." List any potential troublemakers or those who will drink too much and speak or act unwisely.
  2. Begin threading the beads one by one, holding each and naming anyone significant or whom you wish to impress or connect with romantically as you add it, saying: "May this connection be amicable/profitable, the occasion joyful and protect me from those who have had a skinful." Tie the two ends of the cord together, saying: "Joined in harmony may we be," and if you do hope to make advantageous connections add: "And may this benefit me/my career considerably." Hide the beads in the room before the party.
  3. Perform the night before the party.
