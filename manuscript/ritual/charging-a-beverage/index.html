🍵Charging a Beverage

A method for transforming any beverage into a magickal potion by raising energy and directing it into the drink. Any potable liquid can be charged with intention.

Quantities

  • 1 beverage of your choice

Directions

  1. Begin with a clear picture of your desired goal.
  2. You must know the end result before you start.
  3. Prepare your drink of choice.
  4. This can be water, tea, coffee, juice, or any beverage.
  5. The liquid itself carries potential.
  6. Water is hypoallergenic and demonstrates that any potable beverage can be magickal.
  7. Caffeinated drinks add stimulant energy that can make your magick go.
  8. Raise energy through whatever method works for you: tell a joke, sing a song, dance, clap, or drum.
  9. Picture yourself at a concert where the crowd is one living organism, everyone stomping their feet and clapping their hands.
  10. That stadium energy is what gets spells going.
  11. Once energy has been sufficiently raised, direct all the power into your cup.
  12. For a more elaborate working, visualize yourself in a moonlit grotto.
  13. You carry a chalice as you step from trees into a clearing.
  14. The moonlight shines onto a lake of cool, clear, spring-fed water.
  15. Take some water into your chalice.
  16. Raise it to the moon and allow the rays to reach into the depths of the water.
  17. Feel the heartbeat of the earth.
  18. Feel the energy of the earth rise through your feet, calves, legs, hips, belly, up through your arms, and into the glass.
  19. Feel where the earth and the moon meet in the palms of your hands.
  20. See the energy pour out the top of the glass.
  21. Drink.
  22. You can drink fast or slow depending on your intention.
  23. On a hot day, drink quickly.
  24. To relax and meditate on your intention, sip slowly.
  25. The energy is there, waiting.
  26. Once you master this with liquid, try the visualization with no water, reminding yourself that the magick is within you and not the water.
  27. Then try with no chalice and no water, simply seeing them in your mind's eye.
  28. This trains you to charge your water daily before drinking, drawing anything you desire.
