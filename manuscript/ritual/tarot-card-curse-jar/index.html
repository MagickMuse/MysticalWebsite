🏺Tarot Card Curse Jar

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Jar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Printed copies of 6 tarot cards (The Chariot, The Wheel of Fortune, Ten of Pentacles, Three of Cups, Knight of Pentacles, The Tower) 1 mason jar with lid 1 taglock of the target (photo preferred) Tape Curse powder or red pepper flakes (optional) Additional items as desired (rusted nails, war water, etc.)
◇Directions
1. Print copies of the following tarot cards and cut them out: The Chariot, The Wheel of Fortune, the Ten of Pentacles, the Three of Cups, the Knight of Pentacles, and The Tower. 2. Take a mason jar and tape a taglock of the target, preferably a picture, to the inside of the lid. 3. Lay all the printed cards out before you. 4. Pick up The Chariot first, hold it above the taglock, and declare its upright meaning of determination and willpower. 5. Reverse the card and speak your curse, that the target may lose focus and motivation. 6. Tear the card and place the pieces in the jar. 7. Repeat the process with The Wheel of Fortune, declaring good luck and then reversing it to curse the target's fortune. 8. Third, take the Ten of Pentacles for finance, reverse it to invoke financial failure, and tear it into the jar. 9. Next, the Three of Cups for friendship and joy is reversed to burn bridges and fill the heart with sadness. 10. Finally, the Knight of Pentacles for efficiency and hard work is reversed so the target's efforts prove fruitless. 11. Tear each card and add it to the jar after speaking. 12. Once all shredded cards are inside, add curse powder or red pepper flakes for extra potency. 13. Close the lid and shake the jar forcefully, letting your anger build while envisioning the target suffering each misfortune. 14. Open the jar and place The Tower card upright inside. 15. Close the lid and declare: may The Tower bring the destruction you deserve. 16. Hide the jar in a dark place and shake it whenever the curse needs reinforcement.
◇Description
⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING An elaborate jar curse using six reversed tarot cards to strip the target of willpower, luck, finances, friendship, and productivity. Each card is declared, reversed, cursed, and torn into the jar. The Tower is placed upright last to deliver destruction.
