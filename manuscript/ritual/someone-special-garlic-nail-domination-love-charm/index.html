🧄Someone Special Garlic Nail Domination Love Charm

◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BindingManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
2 garlic cloves; 1 steel nail; red thread (optional)
◇Directions
1. Puncture two garlic cloves, one on top of the other, with a steel nail so they are held together. Optionally wrap a red thread around them for reinforcement. The clove on top represents the person making the charm, ensure it stays on top. 2. Hide this charm in a dark, private place until the other person demonstrates signs of affection (or until you no longer want them). If the bottom garlic sprouts, this is a very auspicious sign.
◇Description
Two garlic cloves are pierced together with a steel nail (one on top representing the spell-caster) and hidden in a dark place to inflame passion and dominate the target's affections. A sprouting bottom clove is an auspicious sign.
