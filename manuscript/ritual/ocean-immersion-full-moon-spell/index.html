🌊Ocean Immersion Full Moon Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Bath
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
LunarWater MagickElemental
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Access to the ocean (or large body of open water) Full Moon night
◇Directions
1. On the night of the Full Moon, go to the ocean. 2. Remove clothing if possible. 3. Enter the water fully. 4. Submerge at least once completely beneath the surface under the moonlight. 5. The combination of the Full Moon's light and the ocean's waters creates the most potent fertility force available.
◇Description
A Full Moon fertility rite in which the practitioner enters the ocean naked and fully immerses at least once under the moonlight. The Full Moon shining over the ocean is considered the most potent convergence of fertility forces on Earth.
↗Correspondences1
🌱Fertility

The Full Moon shining over the ocean is considered the most potent convergence of fertility forces on Earth. The ocean's pull responds to the moon; together they create a field of generative power that can be absorbed through full physical immersion.

The Working

On the night of the Full Moon, enter the ocean naked if possible. Completely immerse yourself beneath the surface at least once.

⚠️
Only enter the water if it is safe to do so. Never swim alone in open water without someone aware of your location. Strong currents, tides, and cold water are real hazards. Safety first, always.
