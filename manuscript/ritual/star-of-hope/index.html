The Star

To communicate a deeply held, secret hope to the Universe and petition Pandora, Giver of All Gifts, to help bring it to fruition. Only perform this spell when you are absolutely certain you want your hope realized.

Quantities

  • The Star card from a tarot deck (preferably one that depicts a woman pouring water from a jar)
  • A tin box with a lid (no substitutes; large enough to hold the tarot card)
  • A white feather
  • A small piece of high-quality paper
  • A pencil with an eraser
  • Two sticks of incense (violet if available)
  • A pentacle or pentagram (any kind, even hand-drawn)
  • (Optional) An opal ring, worn on your projective hand during the spell

Directions

  1. Assemble all materials and enter a magickal mindset.
  2. Set the pentacle on your altar or sacred space with its top point facing East.
  3. Place the tin box in the center of the pentacle.
  4. On the new moon, light one stick of incense and raise energy.
  5. Place the Star tarot card inside the tin box and close it.
  6. Hover your open hands over the box, palms down, and say:
  7. Goddess Pandora, Giver of All Gifts,
    I thank you for giving us
    The powerful gift of hope.
    I name this box Pandora's Box.
    If it will do no harm,
    I ask you to fulfill the hope
    That you will find within it.
  8. Write your secret hope on the paper using pencil.
  9. Be specific and honest.
  10. Erase the writing; the message remains energetically.
  11. Open the box and place the paper under the tarot card, folding it up to three times if needed.
  12. Lay the white feather on top of the card and close the box.
  13. Ground the power you raised.
  14. Keep the box atop the pentacle and store it somewhere safe and undisturbed until the Full Moon.
  15. On the full moon, set the pentacle and box back on your altar, again oriented East.
  16. Remove the feather from the box and place the incense atop the lid.
  17. Light the second stick of incense and raise power.
  18. Use the feather to fan the incense smoke upward and say:
  19. Goddess Pandora, Giver of All Gifts,
    Anesidora, She Who Sends Up Gifts,
    Receive my hope!
  20. Repeat the phrase as needed while visualizing your hope ascending and reverberating across the Universe.
  21. When incense is half-burned, begin speaking to Pandora sincerely.
  22. Explain your hope and the outcome you desire; be specific.
  23. Once the incense burns out, thank Pandora, end the spell, and ground the energy.
  24. Return the tarot card to the tin box.
  25. Bury the paper outdoors in the earth.
  26. You may keep the box and feather as a charm or reuse them for future spells.

Why This Spell Works

Everyone carries secret hopes, things we have never spoken aloud to anyone. These hopes often remain unrealized precisely because we keep them locked inside. This spell provides a way to communicate your deepest hope to the Universe while still maintaining its secrecy from other people.

The two-week structure between New Moon and Full Moon mirrors the natural cycle of manifestation. The New Moon is the time of planting seeds and setting intentions. The Full Moon is the time of culmination and sending forth. Your hope gestates in Pandora's Box during the waxing period, gathering energy before being released.

Important Warnings

Do not cast this spell unless you are absolutely certain you want your hope to manifest. The Universe has a way of granting wishes literally, which is why the second half of the spell requires you to speak directly to Pandora about the outcome you desire. Without specifying what "fulfilled" means to you, the spell could manifest in unexpected ways.

For example, if your secret hope is to get a certain job but you do not specify that you want to be happy and successful in that job, you might get the position only to find it makes you miserable. Always explain what the ideal outcome looks like.

You should also feel genuinely hopeful when casting. If you are sad, angry, pessimistic, or negative, wait until your emotional state improves. The spell amplifies whatever energy you bring to it.

Why Tin?

The tin box is essential and cannot be substituted. Tin has a long history in folk magick as a metal associated with Jupiter, the planet of expansion, luck, and blessing. Cookie and biscuit tins work well for this purpose. You may decorate the box if you feel this enhances your connection to the working.

Why Pandora?

Modern retellings often portray Pandora negatively, but her original name means "Giver of All Gifts." She is also called Anesidora, "She Who Sends Up Gifts." Pandora is an Earth goddess who brought humanity countless blessings, including the gift of hope itself. When all the troubles escaped her famous box, hope remained inside as the greatest gift of all.

By naming your tin "Pandora's Box," you invoke this mythology and ask her to fulfill the hope you place within it.

The Erasing Technique

Writing your hope and then erasing it serves two purposes. First, it keeps your hope secret from anyone who might see the paper. Second, the act of writing imprints your intention onto the paper energetically even after the visible words are gone. The paper remembers what you wrote.

Completing the Spell

The conversation with Pandora during the second half is crucial. Speak to her naturally, as you would to a trusted friend. Explain why this hope matters to you, what outcome you desire, and why fulfilling this hope would be good for you. Take your time. The incense burning down gives you a natural timeframe for this conversation.

After the spell, bury the paper because Pandora is an Earth goddess and the burial returns your hope to her domain. The feather can be kept as a charm, and the tin box can be reused for future workings.
