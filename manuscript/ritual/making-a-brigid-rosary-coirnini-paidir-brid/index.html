📿Making a Brigid Rosary (Coirníní Paidir Bríd)

📿Beads

A handcrafted devotional prayer rosary strung and consecrated in Brigid's honor.

Quantities

Round-nose jewelry pliers
Jewelry nippers or cutters (or rosary pliers, which combine both)
About 70 21-gauge eyepins
40 8mm heart beads (glass, stone, or a mix)
5 breath beads (different shape, size, or texture)
5 liminal beads (10mm or 12mm)

Directions

  1. Bless the workspace and invite Brigid to be present while making the rosary.
  2. Prepare all beads by placing each on an eyepin, bending the pin 90 degrees flush against the bead, and clipping the tail to about 1/4 inch.
  3. Grasp the tail of the first heart bead with round nose pliers and roll the pin back toward the bead to form a slightly open loop.
  4. Attach the eye of the second heart bead to the open loop of the first and close it, then repeat for the third and fourth beads.
  5. Add one breath bead, then four more heart beads to complete one strand of nine.
  6. Repeat until five strands of nine beads each are complete.
  7. Connect the five strands with the five liminal beads to close the circle.

Overview

This ritual guides you in crafting a handmade prayer rosary dedicated to Brigid, known in Irish as Coirníní Paidir Bríd. The act of making is itself the devotion: combining metal and glass (both fire-born materials), linking bead to bead with intention, and consecrating the finished circle to Brigid's presence.

The Rosary's Structure

This is an unbroken circle of 5 strands × 9 beads, with a breath bead at the center of each strand and a liminal bead connecting each strand to the next.

The Prayer

I come into thy presence,
Goddess of the hearthfire,
I come into thy presence,
Goddess of the threshold.
I come into thy presence,
Brigid of brightness,
I come into thy presence,
Brigid of grace.
[Pattern begins again.]
I come into thy presence,
Goddess of healing,
I come into thy presence,
Goddess of light...

At each heart bead, say one line of the prayer. At each breath bead, the pattern shifts from "Goddess" lines to "Brigid" lines. At each liminal bead, the pattern begins again. Improvise the ends of the lines freely.

Using Your Rosary

Move from bead to bead with your fingers. For each heart bead, say a line of your prayer. At each breath bead, note the shift. At each liminal bead, begin again. Let the rosary carry you into spontaneous devotion.

Variations

  • A rosary for the three Celtic elements: earth, sea, and sky
  • A rosary for the three realms: underworld, middle world, otherworld
  • A rosary for the triple aspects of Brigid: healer, poet, smith
  • A memorial rosary incorporating beads from a beloved ancestor's jewelry
