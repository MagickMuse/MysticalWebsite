🌅A Morning Prayer for the Ancestors

A personal morning prayer invoking the ancestors as active participants in the day ahead. Structured in two movements: a brief opening praise of the day itself, cultivating wonder, followed by a series of direct invocations asking the ancestors to breathe, move, attract, and strengthen the one who prays. The invocation section was inspired by a prayer St. Augustine composed to the Holy Spirit. It closes with a personal petition for the day's specific joys. The prayer is meant to be spoken aloud immediately after rising.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. After rising, take a few minutes to stretch.
  2. Find a window and look out at the world beginning to wake.
  3. Speak the prayer aloud:
  4. I love this day, which is like
    no other that has ever been,
    and no other that will
    ever come again.
  5. Breathe into me, my ancestors,
    and inspire me today.
  6. Move in me, my ancestors, so that my
    work today may be true to who we are.
  7. Attract my heart, my ancestors, that I
    may love with the power of our line.
  8. Strengthen me, my ancestors, that I
    may defend life in its diverse forms.
  9. I pray that today will be full of good food,
    good music, and good dancing.
  10. The final line can be adapted to reflect what matters most to you personally: art, creative work, connection, beauty, or any other priority.
