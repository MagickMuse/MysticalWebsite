💍Ivy Fidelity Ritual for Marriage

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Herbal MagickWater MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- A pot of trailing ivy (or honeysuckle or vine) - A bowl of water - Your engagement or wedding ring or any ring to symbolize unending love
◇Directions
1. Pick off nine ivy leaves, starting at the top of the frond, and as you pick each one, say: "Ivy, ivy, I love you, keep my lover true, though I would not bind him/her, I now our vows renew." Surround the ring with the nine leaves, one at a time, and say for each one: "Our hands and hearts with one consent, have tied this knot until time does end." Add the leaves to the water one at a time, saying for each: "Ivy, ivy, I love you, keep my lover true. Though I would not bind him/her, I now our vows renew." Swirl the water nine times clockwise and bind the two leaves that move closest together with some of the ivy frond. 2. Cast them into flowing water, repeating all the spell words. 3. Bury the other seven leaves. 4. If you cannot obtain ivy, substitute honeysuckle or vine.
◇Description
A fidelity ritual for marriages in later life or those facing ongoing difficulties, using nine ivy leaves, water, and a ring to renew vows and keep love true without binding.
