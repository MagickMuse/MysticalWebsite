A devotional poem honoring Auma, the Divine Androgyne, the unified Lord and Lady as Shiva/Shakti, Aum/Uma. Celebrates the Two who are One, the spiral dance of souls through the Rites of Passage.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. A devotional poem to Auma, the unified Divine:
  2. I am He and I am She;
    Lord and Lady, live or dead,
    We are One who dwell in thee;
    Shiva/Shakti, bound and wed.
    Rites of Passage mark the way;
    Birth through life, and then beyond.
    Human souls in urns of clay;
    Swimming 'round the spiral pond.
  3. Sun and Moon shine down on thee;
    Watchers of Our children's play.
    Full of love and joy are We;
    Aum and Uma share your day.
    Life to life and on it flows;
    Spiral dance of Time moves on.
    Energy that learns and grows;
    Hastens forth, to Mother drawn.
  4. Grace and Power are Our Names;
    Come to us to join and blend.
    We are Two, and yet the Same;
    Bide in Us, Our garden tend.
    Call Us Earth or call Us Sky;
    Call Us Moon or call Us Sun.
    Male or Female, live and die;
    Symbols of the season's run.
  5. Primal font of energy;
    Aum and Uma; Auma, then.
    Flow into the galaxy;
    Dance apart and then reblend.
    Matter comes and goes, but still;
    Souls with need may be reborn.
    Wait for all, that is Our Will;
    Time is timeless in Our Form.
  6. Blessings given, blessings got;
    What you send, We send to thee.
    Tap into Our Force or not;
    Love is given happily.
