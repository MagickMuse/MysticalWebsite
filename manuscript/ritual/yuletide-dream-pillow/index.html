A sewn herbal dream pillow with customizable herb blends for sleep, inspired dreams, psychic inspiration and astral travel, or attracting holiday magick. Tuck inside your pillowcase for sweet dreams.

Quantities

  • Basic materials:
  • Small piece of fabric (for pillow)
  • Thread
  • Needle
    Herb blend (choose one based on intention):
    For sleep:
  • Lavender (dried)
  • Cedar (dried)
    For inspired dreams:
  • Mugwort (dried)
  • Rosemary (dried)
  • Cloves (dried)
    For psychic inspiration and astral travel:
  • Cinnamon (dried)
  • Cardamom (dried)
    For Santa's visit (cookies theme):
  • Cinnamon (dried)
  • Vanilla (dried)
  • A dash of sprinkles

Directions

  1. Preparation:
    Follow the instructions for creating a magickal bag: sew three sides of your fabric together and turn the bag right side out.
    Choose Your Herbal Blend:
    Select one of the following dried herbal mixtures based on your intention:
  2. For sleep: lavender and cedar
  3. For inspired dreams: mugwort, rosemary, and cloves
  4. For psychic inspiration and astral travel: cinnamon and cardamom
  5. To make sure Santa will come (cookies theme): cinnamon, vanilla, and a dash of sprinkles
    Assembly:
  6. Fill the sewn bag with your chosen dried herbal mixture.
  7. Sew the fourth side closed to seal the pillow.
  8. Tuck the dream pillow inside your pillowcase.
  9. Enjoy sweet dreams throughout the Yuletide season.
