🏡An Elemental Homecoming

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PrayerElementalDevotional Work
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Optional: a stone or soil (earth), a cup of water (sea), a candle or open window (sky)
◇Directions
1. Move through your home with intention, attuning to each element in turn. 2. Speak each prayer aloud. 3. Begin with earth: walk through your home and assess where you feel safe and where you do not. 4. Address any practical vulnerabilities that make you feel exposed or unsettled. 5. From a place of grounded awareness, speak: 6. "May Brigid's blessings of earth be on this place: Stability of mountain, Rootedness of tree, Abundance of grain, Be with us this day and every day." 7. Turn next to sea: attend to the emotional climate of the home. 8. Notice any objects that carry unresolved grief or conflict and consider setting them aside with care. 9. Tend to what your spirit thirsts for, then speak: 10. "May Brigid's blessings of sea be on this place: Connection of currents, Patience of tides, Wisdom of depths, Be with us this day and every day." 11. Finally, sky: quiet the noise of the home as much as possible. 12. Seek a moment of genuine silence. 13. From that stillness, speak: 14. "May Brigid's blessings of sky be on this place, Inspiration of wind, Beauty of cloud, Vision of stars, Be with us this day and every day."
◇Description
A three-part home blessing invoking Brigid through the Celtic triad of earth, sea, and sky. Each element is attuned to within the home before a prayer is spoken.
