Brigid

A bedtime visualization that shapeshifts the practitioner into one of Brigid's creature allies to receive their teachings.

Quantities

Quiet, undisturbed space to fall asleep

Directions

  1. Practice this visualization at bedtime, alone if you usually share a bed, or after your bedmate has fallen asleep.
  2. In the dark, begin breathing slowly and deeply with your eyes closed, opening your inner vision to receive what will be given.
  3. Sense Brigid's hand passing over your closed eyes as she places in your mind an image of one of her creatures.
  4. Observe the creature, then begin to feel yourself shapeshifting into it: notice the weight or lightness of your new body, the senses and abilities you gain and lose, and how the world looks through your new eyes.
  5. Join the creature-guide Brigid has sent and go where the vision takes you, without trying to consciously direct the scenario.
  6. Rather than returning to full awareness, move directly into sleep and let yourself float into the dreamworld.
  7. In the morning, note any dreams you remember, but do not worry if none surface.
  8. The essence of the creature you became stays with you and will reveal its gifts in time.
  9. Give thanks to the ally you came to know, and to Brigid.
