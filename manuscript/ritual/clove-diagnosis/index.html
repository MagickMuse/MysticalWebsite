Clove

A traditional Italian working used to diagnose the presence of the Evil Eye and break it if confirmed. Nine cloves are passed over the afflicted person's head and burned one by one over a candle flame. A clove that bursts signals Evil Eye influence; if the person flinches at the snap, the Eye has been broken in that moment. The process continues until no cloves snap. The working closes with the curer shaking charged water over the afflicted person and marking crosses on the forehead and back of the neck. A chant invoking a triple deity is spoken throughout, the original uses the Christian trinity, but Selene, Artemis, and Hekate or The Morrígan are well-suited pagan alternatives. One of the Evil Eye methods.

Quantities

  • 9 cloves (plus additional sets of 3 if needed)
  • 1 candle (church candle traditional)
  • 1 needle
  • 1 glass of cold water

Directions

  1. The curer takes nine cloves in the right hand and passes them over and around the afflicted person's head.
  2. Light a candle, a church candle is traditional, but adapt as needed.
  3. Pierce a clove through its head with a needle and hold it to the candle flame to ignite it.
  4. While it burns, make the sign of the cross over the afflicted person and chant:
  5. "Three looked upon you. Three cast the Eye.
    From your mother you entered this world.
    In the name of [your chosen trinity],
    Let all harm depart."
  6. Drop the spent clove into a glass of cold water.
  7. Repeat with a second clove.
  8. If a clove bursts, the Evil Eye is present.
  9. If the person flinches at the snap, the Eye has broken, the involuntary movement is the departing evil.
  10. If no cloves snap by the end, no Evil Eye is present and the ritual is complete.
  11. If the ninth clove snaps, burn three more.
  12. If the third of that series snaps, repeat with another three.
  13. Continue until the final clove does not snap.
  14. Once finished, have the afflicted person take three small sips of water from the glass, each from a different point on the rim.
  15. Facing the person, the curer dips their fingers into the water and shakes droplets in all directions, repeat this twice more for a total of three rounds.
  16. Finally, dip fingers again and trace three crosses on the person's forehead and three on the back of the neck.
