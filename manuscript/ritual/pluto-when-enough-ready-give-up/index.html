Ritual Grimoire

Pluto When Enough Ready Give Up

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
Chant/MantraEmpowerment
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A large piece of paper and a black pen - Scissors - A computer screen
◇Directions
1. When you have had enough of a situation or a person, don't shout, don't yell, but steal a moment unobserved and write on the paper or onscreen over and over again in varying sized letters: THAT'S IT. 2. I QUIT. 3. Either shred the paper or cut it in tiny pieces, or delete by backspacing the words on the computer, saying: "Bide my time, so victory's mine." Each time you get frustrated, repeat the ritual, making the writing larger and larger and plan your strategy for the most advantageous exit and time. 4. When you finally leave, shout: "That's it. I quit!" Perform Tuesday, Pluto's special day (when you are ready to quit your job or get out of a bad situation).
◇Description
A Tuesday Pluto's special day when ready to quit job or get out of bad situation spell for Pluto ritual for when enough is enough and you are ready to give up, writing THAT'S IT I QUIT over and over in black then shredding to bide time for most advantageous exit.
