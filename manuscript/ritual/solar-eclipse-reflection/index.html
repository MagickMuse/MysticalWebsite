A guided meditation for the solar eclipse, the supercharged new moon when both sun and moon vanish from the sky and a powerful portal opens to a new life path. Holding sunstone and moonstone to embody both celestial forces, you reflect on what the eclipse season has illuminated, then open yourself to the new pathway that emerges as the light returns.

Quantities

  • Sunstone (optional)
  • Moonstone (optional)
  • Tarot or oracle cards (optional)

Directions

  1. Find a comfortable place to settle.
  2. Breathe deeply, letting your body relax with each exhale.
  3. If you have them, hold the sunstone in one hand and the moonstone in the other.
  4. Close your eyes.
  5. Visualize the moon and sun moving through the sky, slowly drawing closer together.
  6. Hold both in your awareness, the moon, which governs your emotional and intuitive world, your inner compass; and the sun, which shapes your identity, confidence, radiance, and force in the world.
  7. Watch as the moon edges into alignment with the sun.
  8. At the precise moment of eclipse, it steps fully into the light, and the sky goes dark.
  9. In this fleeting darkness, the light within you becomes visible instead.
  10. The sun and moon are perfectly aligned, merged, still, and quiet.
  11. A true blank slate.
  12. Rest in that stillness.
  13. With sunstone and moonstone warm in your hands, turn your awareness inward.
  14. What has this eclipse season brought to light in your life?
  15. What wound, truth, calling, or crossroads has surfaced?
  16. What path or restart is your soul quietly pointing you toward?
  17. When you feel ready, visualize the moon beginning to move past the sun.
  18. Light returns, first a sliver, then growing, then flooding back.
  19. As it does, see it illuminating a new pathway stretching out before you.
  20. Notice what it reveals.
  21. If you have tarot or oracle cards, draw one or more now to receive guidance on this new direction.
  22. Let the images speak to the path your soul is ready to walk.
  23. Breathe deeply, and when you are ready, open your eyes.
