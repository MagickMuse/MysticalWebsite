The second of the Three Beasts rites of the Red Blade, in which the Adept animates a two-headed clay hound infused with grave-earth to relentlessly pursue and harry a chosen target.

Quantities

  • Clay
  • Graveyard dirt from the grave of one who died violently
  • Dog hair
  • Your own hair, spittle, and blood
  • A leaf of aconite or hellebore
  • Thorns of hawthorn, blackthorn, or honey locust (for teeth)
  • A living scarab beetle (or other beetle)
  • A small piece of paper, plus a personal item of the target
  • Myrrh incense
  • Dark flowers
  • Fresh fruit
  • Nephalia (spring water mixed with honey)
  • Black and red paint
  • A strapholos
  • The completed Moon Girdle

Directions

  1. On a night of the waning gibbous moon, put on the Moon Girdle.
  2. Mix clay with the grave-earth of one who died violently. Fashion a two-headed dog at least four inches long, with four legs, no tail, and an opposing head at either end, mouths open with thorn teeth.
  3. Form a hollow in the center of its back. Place inside it dog hair mixed with your own hair, spittle, and blood, a leaf of aconite or hellebore, a living scarab beetle, and a paper naming your target (with a personal item of theirs) surrounded by the letters of Persephone's name. Seal the hollow.
  4. Inscribe the growing word Orthos on the dog's flesh, letter by letter.
  5. Bake the dog in an oven for an hour until the clay hardens, then paint it black and red.
  6. Set it upon a table with lit myrrh incense, dark flowers, fresh fruit, and nephalia. Purify yourself and consecrate the space.
  7. Chant the Gibbous Moon Hymn to Hekate-Persephone three times while spinning your strapholos. Pass the dog through the incense, touch both faces to the flowers, fruit, and nephalia in turn, and exhale slowly into its open mouths.
  8. Recite the animating words, then in your own words charge the dog to seek out, pursue, overtake, and harry the named target unceasingly until their health and peace of mind vanish like the waning moon, while never able to harm you or those close to you, and that it endures until its clay form is destroyed.
  9. Pull out one of its thorn teeth and mail it to the target, or place it on the target's property.

Arcana

Being a rite to create a vengeful servant that will pursue and harry its quarry.

The Rite

On a night of the waning gibbous moon, put on the Moon Girdle, and then take clay and mix it with the grave-earth of one who has died violently. Fashion the clay into the shape of a two-headed dog, at least four inches in length. The dog should have four legs but no tail; there should be a head on either end, facing in opposite directions. The mouths of the heads should be open, showing teeth - which should be made of cuttings from thorns of hawthorn, blackthorn, honey locust, or some such sacred plant. A small hollow should be formed in the center of its back, and into that hollow should be put dog hair mixed with your own hair, spittle, blood, as well as a leaf of aconite or hellebore. Also place into the hollow a living thing such as a scarab beetle, as well as a piece of paper containing the name of the one you want it to hunt (with any material of theirs) surrounded by the letters in Persephone's name. Seal the hollow off.

Into the flesh of the dog inscribe the following words:

O

Or

Ort

Orth

Ortho

Orthos

Bake the dog in an oven for an hour until the clay hardens. When you have taken it out, paint it black and red. Set it upon a table containing lit incense of myrrh, dark flowers, fresh fruit, and nephalia. Do the usual acts to purify yourself and consecrate your space. Then, after chanting the Gibbous Moon Hymn to Hekate-Persephone three times while spinning your strapholos, pass the dog through the incense, touching its two faces to the flowers, fruit, and nephalia in turn. Exhale slowly into its open mouth, and then say the following:

Spirit of the grave;

[Name of Departed] son/daughter of [Name];

From nothing I called you,

And to nothing you will return.

Know that you are a two-faced hound;

Know that you are forever bound;

Know that you are consecrated to the service

Of one who serves Hekate-Persephone: for I

Serve Hekate-Persephone. Know that I am your mistress.

See these sacred foods I set before you, upon which you now feed.

Be alive. Be loyal to me. In the name of Hekate-Persephone, obey my command.

And my command is this:

Recite the dog's charge in your own words; namely, that it seek out, pursue, overtake, and harry a certain person, whom you now name. Tell the dog that it does not sleep, nor need to sleep; it is alert at all times and everywhere, and that it will seek its prey until it finds it, and harry it unceasingly so that the target can neither eat, drink, rest, sleep, or be at ease in any way, so that his health and peace of mind vanish like the waning moon. Tell the dog that it can never hurt you or those close to you, and that its existence may be ended only when its clay form is destroyed. Otherwise it will perform its function perpetually.

Tell the dog to follow its tooth, and pull out one of its thorn teeth and either mail it to your target or put it on the target's property.
