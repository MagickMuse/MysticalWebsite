A protective household charm made by braiding thirteen onions or garlic bulbs onto a doubled length of twine. Performed during the Full Moon of August or a Waning Moon on a Saturday during the hour of Saturn. The braid absorbs negative energies and protects the home. Once completed, it is hung in the house immediately or set aside until Hogmanay (New Year's Eve) to replace the previous year's braid.

Quantities

  • 1 yard of twine
  • 13 onions with stems or garlic bulbs with stems

Directions

  1. Double the yard of twine and tie a knot to create a loop for hanging the finished braid.
  2. Lay the twine on a tabletop with the loose ends toward you.
  3. Place an onion or garlic bulb with the bulb up and stem down, crisscross the twine twice, and add the next one.
  4. Continue until you reach the end of the twine, then knot.
  5. Hang the braid in the house or wrap it and set aside until New Year's Eve.
  6. Speak:
  7. Charm of Onions [or garlics], charm of string;
    Evil to thy own self bring!
    Charm of Onions [or garlics] and of twine;
    Protector of this house and mine!
    I give my blessing unto you;
    For this selfless work you do!
