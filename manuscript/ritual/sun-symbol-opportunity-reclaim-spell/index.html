☀️Sun Symbol Opportunity Reclaim Spell

◇Form
Candle RiteSigil
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraSolar
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A circle of yellow or orange paper - A black pen - An orange, red, or gold candle
◇Directions
1. Draw a large black dot in the center of the paper circle to represent the astrological glyph for the sun. 2. Light the candle and say: "Light shine on me, I deserve it, you see, my sun has been dimmed, unfairly you know unjustly and so, I reclaim my place in the sun, now, anyhow, anywhere, sun get me there." Write all around the sun circle until it is filled precisely with what you deserve and when and the obstacles or people in the way. 3. Set the sun in front of the candle and repeat the spell words. 4. Let the candle burn through. 5. Every Sunday, light another candle and repeat the spell words as you hold the sun circle. 6. Perform at midday.
◇Description
A midday sun symbol spell for when you have had an opportunity taken from you, drawing sun glyph on orange paper and repeating weekly to reclaim your place in the sun.
