☮️Peace Between Warring Factions

A Friday ritual with green candle, lilac incense, two clay figures rolled into ball with herbs in green bag with three smoke knots. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 green candle
  • 1 lilac or lavender incense stick
  • Green children's modeling clay
  • 1 green drawstring bag or purse
  • Lavender, dried rose petals, or lavender potpourri
  • Dried sage and dried thyme
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Light the candle, saying, "The time has come for conflict to end, differences heal and hatred mend." Light the incense from the candle and, with the clay, shape two featureless figures to represent the warring sides, then roll them together into a ball.
  2. Say, "May [name factions] be bound in new understanding, that they may learn tolerance and accept their differences." Place the clay ball in the bag, together with the other ingredients in any order, one by one, repeating the first set of spell words for each one.
  3. Close the bag or purse, draw three knots in the air over it in incense smoke, and repeat the second set of spell words.
  4. Extinguish the candle, and let the incense burn.
  5. Keep the bag near a picture of the troubled area, printed out from the Internet.
  6. Perform Friday.
