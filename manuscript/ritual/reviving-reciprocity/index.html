Ritual Grimoire

🐟Reviving Reciprocity

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Devotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Daily offerings (eggshells, seeds, mulch, etc.) - Duration: One week
◇Directions
1. Salmon travels thousands of miles, first across oceans and then upstream, steering by starlight and the subtle pull of magnetic fields to reach the hatching grounds where she was born. 2. Fraught with danger, first in the ocean's depths where Salmon is food for sharks, seals, and humans, and then on the river where she dodges eagles and bears, Salmon makes her way home. 3. This journey is heroic; the final stages more so. 4. Salmon, now accustomed to a seafood diet from years of ocean living, finds no food as she moves up the freshwater river. 5. Her skin begins to dissolve. 6. But determined Salmon will complete her journey, spawning where she was hatched. 7. Most likely, Salmon will die after laying her eggs. 8. Her body will then leach phosphorus into the water, a necessary nutrient for young salmon, beginning the cycle again. 9. Salmon teaches that you must give back to the river and the land which gave you life. 10. For the next week, make daily gifts to the land or a river if you have one nearby. 11. Maybe it's egg shells (full of calcium) or seeds sprinkled for the birds. 12. Maybe you'll offer mulch to your garden. 13. Give back and in giving back remember what it's like to be part of the cycle of exchange.
◇Description
A week-long practice of daily offerings to the land or water, honoring Salmon's lesson that you must give back to the earth which gave you life. Through this cycle of exchange, remember what it means to be part of the living web of reciprocity.
