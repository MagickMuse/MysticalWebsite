🌿The Wand Switching Spell

One of three simple spells for the jilted witch. A baneful wand-cutting rite performed in two parts: the cutting of a fresh hazel switch on a Saturn or Mars day before sunrise, followed that night by a circle rite in which the operator circles widdershins, beats the altar with the switch, and releases the working to the east.

Quantities

  • 1 hazel branch (one year's growth; any tree may substitute)
  • Athame
  • Incense of Wrath (recipe forthcoming)

Directions

PREPARATION (before sunrise on a Saturday or Tuesday, during the waning moon): Go to a hazel tree and cut a branch of one year's growth with the athame while chanting: "I sever thee, thou virgin branch, in the name of [victim's name] for the purposes of his downfall!"

THAT NIGHT: Cast a circle with Incense of Wrath burning in the thurible.

Visualize the victim's face in the altar triangle.

Begin moving widdershins around the circle, simultaneously rotating on your own axis, chanting with each revolution: "Droch! Mirroch! Esenaroth! / Betu! Baroch! Maaroth!"

Each time you face the altar, strike it with the switch held in the left hand.

Complete three full revolutions of the circle in this manner.

Drop to the floor at the east, letting the working go, and chant: "So mote it be!"
