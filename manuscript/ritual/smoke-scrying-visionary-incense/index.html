A smoke scrying ritual using equal parts dried peppermint and lavender, stirred with a rosemary sprig and set alight in a cauldron. As the smoke rises, soften your gaze and allow your mind to expand beyond its usual limits, watching for shapes in the smoke for divination, or wafting it around the body for cleansing and liberation. Best under an air sign or Sagittarius moon. In a pinch, a single rosemary sprig or any favorite incense will do.

Quantities

  • Dried peppermint
  • Dried lavender
  • Cauldron or fireproof bowl
  • Dried rosemary sprig
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Place equal parts dried peppermint and lavender into your cauldron or fireproof bowl.
  2. Breathe in their combined scent: crisp, floral, clarifying.
  3. Let it begin to open your mind before the smoke even rises.
  4. Settle into your intention.
  5. What are you seeking insight about?
  6. What are you cleansing away, or freeing yourself from?
  7. Or simply turn your attention to the Aquarius moon above, its electric, forward-looking, limitless energy.
  8. Take the rosemary sprig in hand.
  9. Rosemary is a plant of the mind and clarity, and here it also stands in for the lightning of Aquarius: sudden illumination, genius, the unexpected spark.
  10. Use the sprig to stir the herbs clockwise three times, focusing your intention with each slow turn.
  11. Leave the rosemary resting in the bowl and carefully set the herbs alight.
  12. If the flame dies down quickly, that is fine; use your breath to keep the embers smoldering.
  13. Air is Aquarius's element.
  14. Let your breath be part of the ritual: each exhale intentional, feeding the smoke.
  15. As the smoke begins to rise, soften your gaze and let your mind rise with it.
  16. Release fixed expectations.
  17. Let your sense of what is possible expand upward, like the smoke, to a wider plane of vision and freedom than you usually allow yourself.
  18. For smoke scrying, watch the movement of the smoke: its direction, speed, shapes, and patterns.
  19. Note whatever arises without forcing interpretation.
  20. Trust what you see.
  21. To use it for cleansing, gently waft the smoke around your body with your hands, directing it through your energy field to dissolve limiting thoughts, restrictive patterns, and anything that has been keeping your vision small.
