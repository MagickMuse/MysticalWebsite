🔥Jail Fix Incense Spell

◇Form
Incense Blend
◇Method
VisualizationPrayerPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
- 1 High John the Conqueror root - 1 Courtcase root (galangal) - Dried cloves - Dried rosemary - Dried sage - 1 pinch salt - Tobacco from 1 cigarette
◇Directions
1. Finely chop one High John the Conqueror root and one Courtcase root. 2. Using a mortar and pestle, powder both roots together, then grind them thoroughly with dried cloves, rosemary, and sage. 3. Add a pinch of salt and the tobacco from one cigarette. 4. Blend all ingredients together until well combined. 5. Burn the resulting Jail Fix Powder as incense, accompanied by visualization, prayer, and petition.
◇Description
A Hoodoo powder blend burned as incense to prevent imprisonment or work toward release. The formula combines High John the Conqueror, courtcase root, cloves, rosemary, sage, salt, and tobacco. Hoodoo is a closed practice with African American cultural and spiritual roots, approach with respect. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
