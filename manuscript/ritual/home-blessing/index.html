This spell blesses your home and everyone within it, inviting positive energy (like friendship, prosperity, and protection) while banishing negativity. It invokes the Goddess in her triple form, Maiden, Mother, and Crone.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Water (spring, rain, or tap)
  • Salt (sea salt or table salt)
  • A bowl or chalice
  • 3 sprigs of fresh herbs (rosemary, sage, or any available herb)
  • Blue or white ribbon (or substitute, like a rubber band)

Directions

  1. • Tie the herb sprigs together securely with ribbon, leaving leafy ends free.
  2. Get into a magickal mood. Assemble ingredients and create sacred space.
  3. Fill the vessel two-thirds full with water and raise power.
  4. Hold the vessel with both hands and visualize it glowing with energy.
  5. Set it down and add a large pinch of salt.
  6. Stir the water deosil (clockwise) with your herb bundle while focusing on drawing blessings to your home and each member inside.
  7. Once charged, carry the vessel and herb bundle to your front door.
  8. Hold the vessel in your receptive hand and herbs in your projective hand.
  9. Dip herbs into the water and draw a pentagram on the back of the door. Say:
    Goddess powerful, Goddess trine,Grant blessings to this home of mine.
  10. Shake herbs to sprinkle water by the door. Repeat:
    Goddess powerful, Goddess trine,Grant blessings to this home of mine.
  11. Walk deosil through your entire home. Repeat the dipping, sprinkling, and incantation in every room, including closets, staircases, basements, attics, etc.
  12. Return to the front door, step outside, and draw another pentagram on the exterior of the door. Repeat the spell one final time.
  13. Go back inside and ground the power.
  14. If water remains, pour it down a drain to return it to the Goddess.
  15. Wash the vessel. Discard the ribbon. Rinse the herbs and use them in cooking (fresh or dried).
  16. Variations
    To focus on specific needs, change the wording “grant blessings to” to:
    “make happy,” “bless with love,” “make secure,” “bless with health,” “make prosperous,” “bless with peace,” “make harmonious,” etc.

Why Bless Your Home?

Blessing your home helps to protect it and everyone who lives there. It attracts positive energy, such as friends and prosperity, and averts negative energy, such as unwanted visitors and negative entities and elementals. Blessing the home draws blessings to its inhabitants.

We can all use all the blessings that we can get, so cast this spell as often as you like. Use it for a house, an apartment, or even a temporary home such as a dorm room.

The Triple Goddess

The spell invokes the Goddess in her triple aspect as Maiden, Mother, and Crone. This reflects the lunar phases of Waxing Moon, Full Moon, and Waning Moon, so this spell can be cast at any time.

The Incantation

Goddess powerful, Goddess trine,
Grant blessings to this home of mine.

Understanding the Visualization

Hold the vessel up with both hands. Regard the water, which will be vibrating slightly, and visualize it as a medium for the transference of magickal power. Do this in any way you like, such as by imagining the water suffused with light from within.

As you stir the water deosil with the herb bundle, think about the blessings you wish to attract. Visualize them reaching every member of the household, including pets.

Working Through Your Home

Proceed deosil through your entire home, carrying the vessel and the herbs. Repeat the dipping, the sprinkling, and the spell in every room. You may wish to do this once in each room, or to asperge every corner of every room. This is your home and your spell, so that is entirely up to you.

If you wish to asperge places such as closets, hallways, and staircases, or things such as a bed, desk, or fireplace, do it. Do not forget the basement, attic, sun room, or attached garage, if your home has any of these.

Work your way deosil through your home until you are back at your front door. Open it, step outside, and repeat the spell a final time as you draw another pentagram on the exterior of the door.

After the Spell

Go back inside and ground the power that you raised. You should feel good, and be in a very positive frame of mind.

If any water remains in the vessel, return it to the Goddess by pouring it down a drain. Wash the vessel, and discard the ribbon. Rinse the herbs in clear water, and use them fresh or dried in cooking.

Customizing the Spell

When there is a specific need or problem, customize this spell as required by altering the words "grant blessings to." Variant wordings might include:

  • "make happy"
  • "bless with love"
  • "make secure"
  • "bless with health"
  • "make prosperous"
  • "bless with peace"
  • "make harmonious"
