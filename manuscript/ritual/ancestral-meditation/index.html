A guided meditation to collaborate with ancestors and spirit guides to identify root causes of ancestral trauma. The visualization takes you to your soul's home where you commune with your ancestors to discover inherited traumas manifesting as colored threads entangled within you.

Quantities

  • Quiet, safe space
  • Comfortable position (lying down preferred)

Directions

  1. Find a quiet area where you feel safe and connected to your ancestors or spirit guides.
  2. Lying down is preferred for this meditation as it requires extra time.
  3. Begin by visualizing a bright white sphere of energy wrapping around you, repelling negativity from your meditative space.
  4. See that white light pulse and change to a soft warm golden glow, the light that will draw your ancestors to you.
  5. Take three deep cleansing breaths.
  6. Feel your body relax, muscles loosen, breath deepen.
  7. Feel your connection to earth, stabilizing your resolve.
  8. Feel your connection to air, opening yourself to knowledge.
  9. Feel your connection to water, connecting to emotions.
  10. Feel your connection to fire, working through transformation.
  11. Feel yourself connected to Spirit and invite in the ancestors or guides who will help you determine the causes of ancestral trauma.
  12. A pathway appears through the woods.
  13. Walk until you find your soul's home, a dwelling that will accommodate you comfortably.
  14. As you approach, hear the chatter of familiar voices beyond the door.
  15. Open the door and step inside to commune with your ancestors and spirit guides.
  16. Find a space among those who have chosen to assist you.
  17. Open dialogue with them about the trauma that has not been fully addressed.
  18. Ask where in your family line or collective consciousness the unresolved issues are located.
  19. As you acknowledge and accept their guidance, see the root causes as threads of assorted colors wound around and through you, tying up your ability to cope.
  20. With your mind, move these threads into a tangled ball held in your hands.
  21. Sit and relax with your ancestors.
  22. When ready, thank them and leave the house.
  23. Walk back down the path, feeling reconnected with your body.
  24. You may return to this place for additional work.
  25. When completely ready, open your eyes.
