⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A freezer spell targeting a faithless lover's potency. A cucumber carved with the target's name and birth date is frozen, rendering the target impotent until the cucumber is removed and buried under a full moon.

Quantities

1 cucumber
1 knife
1 plastic bag (optional)

Directions

  1. Find a cucumber that most closely resembles the size and shape of the target's anatomy.
  2. Carve the target's first and last name and birth date into the cucumber while concentrating on them staying soft.
  3. Place the cucumber into the freezer; a plastic bag may be used.
  4. As the cucumber freezes and softens, the target will be unable to perform.
  5. To lift the spell, remove the cucumber and bury it under a full moon.
