💼Spell to Get a Better Job

A vision board style manifestation spell using the Part of Fortune symbol to attract your ideal career. Stand on your creation to embody and attract your dream job.

Quantities

  • Pictures from magazines, the Internet, or other sources depicting your dream job
  • Paper or cardboard large enough to stand on (tape sheets together if needed)
  • Orange marker
  • Glue, paste, or tape

Directions

  1. Spend time thinking about the job you'd really like.
  2. What images come to mind?
  3. Is travel a factor?
  4. If so, a plane might be a good symbol.
  5. Perhaps a big walnut desk represents authority to you, or a TV screen suggests fame.
  6. Coins and paper currency signify money.
  7. Cut out magazine pictures that depict various aspects of your dream job or download images from the Internet.
  8. Gather up all your images along with the other ingredients needed for this spell.
  9. Cast a circle around the area where you will do your spell.
  10. On the paper or cardboard, use the marker to draw a symbol that astrologers call the Part of Fortune.
  11. This lucky design looks like an X with a circle around it.
  12. Make your drawing large enough so that you can paste all your pictures inside it.
  13. Begin attaching the pictures you've collected to the Part of Fortune.
  14. As you work, imagine yourself happy and successful in your new position.
  15. When you've finished, lay the paper on the floor.
  16. Remove your shoes and stand in the middle of it.
  17. Close your eyes and imagine yourself becoming one with your new job.
  18. Make the visualization as real as possible.
  19. Stand there until your mind starts to wander, then step off the paper and open the circle.
  20. Repeat as necessary, until you land your ideal job.
