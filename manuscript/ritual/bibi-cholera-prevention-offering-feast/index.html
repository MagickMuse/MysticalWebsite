A Romany propitiatory feast honoring the disease spirit Bibi to prevent cholera and draw her blessings of prosperity. The preserved white cake from the feast carries emergency healing powers, especially for children.

Quantities

White cloth (for table)
White cake
Candles
Incense
Food and drink for a feast
Offerings for Bibi (placed on or beneath an altar)

Directions

  1. Set a table with a white cloth and serve white cake.
  2. Give each participant a slice of cake in exchange for an offering to Bibi, but do not eat the cake yourself.
  3. Preserve it for emergencies, as it carries magickal healing powers especially beneficial for children.
  4. Light candles and incense and place all of Bibi's offerings on an altar or underneath it.
  5. Hold a party in Bibi's honor: eat, drink, toast her, and request her blessings.
