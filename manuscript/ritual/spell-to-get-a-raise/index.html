A prosperity spell using bay laurel and orange candle to manifest a salary increase by amplifying your worth and drawing recognition of your value.

Quantities

  • 1 orange candle
  • 3 bay leaves
  • Cinnamon essential oil (diluted)
  • Pen with green or gold ink
  • Clear quartz crystal

Directions

  1. Perform this spell on a Thursday during a waxing moon.
  2. On each bay leaf, write one word: your name on the first leaf, the word RAISE on the second leaf, and the specific amount or percentage you seek on the third leaf.
  3. Anoint the orange candle with diluted cinnamon oil, drawing from base to wick to attract prosperity.
  4. Arrange the three bay leaves in a triangle around the candle with the clear quartz at the center.
  5. Light the candle and speak: "I have earned this increase. My work speaks for itself. Abundance flows to me as recognition of my value. The raise I seek finds its way to me." Visualize yourself receiving the news, seeing the new amount on your paycheck, feeling the satisfaction of being valued.
  6. Let the candle burn for at least thirty minutes.
  7. When finished, place the bay leaves in your wallet or work bag.
  8. Burn them after your raise is granted as an offering of thanks.
