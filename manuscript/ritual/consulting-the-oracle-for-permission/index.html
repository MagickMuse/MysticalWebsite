A meditation on one's readiness for dedication, followed by an adjuration compelling the oracle stones to reveal whether Hekate grants permission to proceed. Rite 8 of Book I.

Quantities

1 pen, 1 sheet of paper, 1 bowl for salt water, matches, 3 white candles, myrrh incense with a censer or heat-safe holder, cinnamon for dusting the hands, the consecrated pouch of six stones.

Directions

  1. As usual, purify yourself and set forth the sigil, three white candles, incense, the bowl of salt water, and the pouch of stones.
  2. Sing the consecration hymn, imagining a helix of blue fire encompassing you and your space; let your breathing grow deep and steady.
  3. Visualize a sandy path with the sigil beneath your feet, standing at a crossroads: to the right, a narrow rocky trail plunging into a dark wood; to the left, a wide sandy path curving back along a ridge above a starlit sea.
  4. See yourself in your mind's eye, your physical features, flaws and strengths alike, and find yourself worthy to proceed regardless.
  5. Look past your physical form to your subtle self, transparent as gauze, which may resemble you or not. Consider honestly whether it is ready for the demanding path of dedication, devotion, and magickal practice (the narrow, rocky trail), or whether it needs, for now, to heal, rest, and strengthen instead (the wide, sandy path). Neither choice is wrong.
  6. Look past both your physical and subtle forms to the small, diamond-bright spark at their core, perfect, unchanging, eternal, and present in all living things. Know that whichever path you choose, this spark remains unaltered.
  7. Make your choice. If you choose the sandy path, see yourself walk peacefully back the way you came, and end the rite here. If you choose the narrow, rocky path, open your eyes and prepare to learn whether permission is granted.
  8. Take the pouch of stones in your right hand and pass it slowly through the rising incense and candle flame; with your left hand, sprinkle it with the salt water.
  9. Speak the adjuration below aloud, chanting its last three lines three times:
    "O Earth! / O you Daughters of Earth! / O you Sons of Earth! / O you Servants of Earth! / O you Serpents of Earth! / O you Powers of Earth! / O you Prisoners of Earth! / O you who dwell in darkness, / In caverns unseen by the sun; / In mile-deep lakes, in corridors / That vein the vaults that run / Beneath, beneath, beneath! / Speak, now, with truthful tongues, / Willful ones: You rulers, you rebels, / You dwellers of the deep; O dark deceivers, / Deceive not me! For by the ineffable name / Of Hekate, Hekate, Hekate / Do I adjure you: by the name / Of TARTAROUKHOS / Are you called, are you / Compelled to come: / Make these stones / Tell truth! / As the wind yields rain, / As the waves yield foam, / As the well yields water, / As the quarry yields stone, / So let these tongues speak / And reveal the will of Hekate.
    KYNA KORE KYNA BASILEA KYNA HEKATE
    TARTAROUKHOS TARTAROUKHOS * TARTAROUKHOS
    AH EH AY EE OH EW AU"
  10. Breathe deeply, concentrate on your desire, and ask aloud: "Hekate, great goddess, greatest titan, do you grant me permission to dedicate myself to you tomorrow, and pursue the path of devotion?" Then reach into the pouch and draw a single stone.
  11. A dark stone means YES: thank Hekate sincerely in your own words, then meditate on the great step you take tomorrow. A light stone means NO, not a final refusal, only a sign that there is more to learn about yourself and your needs first. Rest a few days, then begin the nine-day sequence again from Day 1; you may consult the oracle again at Day 8. If you draw three light stones across three attempts, it is likely not in your best interest to proceed at this time, and another devotional path may serve you better.

Context

The eighth of nine nights leading to the new moon dedication, one day before it, in which the reader meditates honestly on her own readiness and then adjures the titanic spirits within the oracle stones to reveal whether Hekate grants permission to proceed with tomorrow's dedication.
