🔮Finding What Is Lost

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged ObjectAltar Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
DivinationVisualizationInvocationMeditation
◇Category
Divination
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- Pendulum (any object on a chain or string) - Comfortable spot with sunlight for visualization
◇Directions
1. This spell works better without a circle cast. 2. Tune the pendulum by holding it out in front of you and establishing a yes/no system. 3. State your desired communication pattern aloud, such as "Left for yes, right for no," or "Clockwise yes, counterclockwise no." 4. Stand in the room where you most likely lost the object. 5. Hold the pendulum in front of you and ask, "Is the lost object in this room?" If the pendulum indicates no, try a different room until you receive a yes answer. 6. When you receive a yes, pick a corner of the room and ask, "Is my lost object close to this corner?" If no, try each corner until you get a yes. 7. Once you identify a quadrant, hold the pendulum over each object in that area and ask for a report to see if it gives a yes or no answer. 8. Look under objects, move couch pillows, or shift furniture as needed, depending on the strength of the pendulum's swing. 9. If you go through your house and only receive no answers, ask if the item is still in your house. 10. If the answer is no, repeat this process in your car. 11. If you have not turned up anything, resort to a vision process. 12. Settle yourself in a comfortable spot, preferably in the middle of a patch of sunlight. 13. Visualize yourself surrounded by blue or golden light. 14. In your mind's eye, see a blackbird. 15. If the bird leads you to the stag, the object is lost outside. 16. Ask the stag the same question and see where it leads you. 17. If the stag leads you to the owl, then someone has picked up what you've lost. 18. Ask the owl. 19. If the owl leads you to the eagle, then the item has traveled from its intended location. 20. Ask the eagle. 21. If the eagle leads you to the salmon, the thing you lost may no longer be in its original form. 22. The salmon will show you what form your lost object now takes. 23. Whether you can replace it from there is up to you.
◇Description
A Mabon divination working that uses pendulum dowsing and guided visualization through sacred animals of Welsh legend (blackbird, stag, owl, eagle, salmon) to locate missing items. Invokes the wisdom spirits that guided Mabon's rescuers.
