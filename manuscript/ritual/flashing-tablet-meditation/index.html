A flashing tablet matching your element (Air, Fire, Water, or Earth); see the Flashing Tablets recipe in the Recipe Grimoire
◇Directions
1. Select the tablet whose element most relates to your magickal purpose. 2. Position the tablet about three feet from your face, at a height where you can gaze at its center without raising or lowering your head or eyes. 3. Relax comfortably and gaze at the center of the tablet for a few minutes. 4. If your eyes feel heavy: close them and observe the afterimage, allowing yourself to sense you are inside the element itself. Pause for any intuitive insights, then visualize your goal and mentally send it to the universe. 5. If the colors begin flashing: continue staring for another sixty seconds, then close your eyes and enjoy the afterimage. Rest for a minute or two, remaining open to any information that comes, then focus on your goal and consciously send it to the universe with confidence.
◇Description
A color meditation technique using complementary color pairs to enter a meditative state and send intentions to the universe through elemental focus.

Flashing tablets are meditation tools that use complementary color pairs to create optical illusions. When you gaze at them, the design appears to flash between colors, inducing a hypnotic state that helps you contact your subconscious mind and clearly visualize your goals.

How Flashing Tablets Work

When you stare at a colored area for an extended time, your eye muscles relax slightly and change focus. This causes you to suddenly "see" the complementary color flash before your eyes. The alternating flashes create a meditative state ideal for visualization work.

Elemental Designs

Each element has a specific design using complementary colors:

Air: Orange circle inside a violet square

Fire: Red triangle inside a green square

Water: White crescent moon inside a black square

Earth: Yellow square inside a larger blue square

Each symbol is placed inside a square, which represents the Earth element containing and grounding the work on the physical plane.

This technique creates a bridge between your conscious intention and subconscious mind through the language of color and light.
