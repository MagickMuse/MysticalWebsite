Beltane

A spell to deepen feelings and commitment between partners, whether you are beginning a relationship or want to strengthen an existing bond. The gemstones represent both partners and carry love (rose quartz) and passion (carnelian) energies.

Quantities

  • 1 oval or circular piece of rose quartz
  • 1 obelisk-shaped piece of carnelian
  • 1 piece of dark blue ribbon (long enough to tie around both stones)
  • 1 piece of white silk (large enough to wrap the stones)
  • 1 metal box with lid (copper, brass, or lead preferred)
  • Shovel for burial

Directions

  1. In the morning, wash the gemstones with warm, soapy water.
  2. Stand them side by side on your altar so they are touching.
  3. Choose which stone represents you and which represents your partner.
  4. Rose quartz carries feminine and yin energy while carnelian carries masculine and yang energy, though either can represent either person.
  5. Tie the stones together with the dark blue ribbon, making two knots.
  6. As you tie each knot, visualize you and your partner connected by a strong bond of love and devotion.
  7. The dark blue color signifies strength, sincerity, and permanence.
  8. Cover the bound gems with the white silk cloth for protection and leave them on your altar until evening.
  9. Once the moon has risen, wrap the gemstones in the white silk cloth and place the package in the metal box.
  10. Take the box outside and bury it in the ground, preferably beneath an oak or apple tree.
