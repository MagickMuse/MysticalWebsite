🔮Italian Candle Method

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
3 rose hips 3 nettle leaves A small amount of cumin seeds A few sprigs of rue A bowl or pan of icy water 2 tallow candles (or candles of your choice) Scarlet ribbon (enough to bind both candles) A red charm bag (for carrying lucky shapes)
◇Directions
1. Place three rose hips, three nettle leaves, some cumin seeds, and a few sprigs of rue into a bowl or pan of icy water. 2. Bind two candles together with scarlet ribbon and set them upright within the pan of water. 3. Light both candles and observe the wax as it drips down into the cold water below, noting the shapes that form and harden. 4. Interpret the shapes as the oracle. 5. Any shapes that appear particularly auspicious may be removed, dried, and carried within a red charm bag.
◇Description
An Italian ceromancy method in which two candles bound with scarlet ribbon are placed in a bowl of icy herb-infused water and lit. The shapes formed by the dripping wax are interpreted as the oracle. Lucky shapes may be carried in a red charm bag. One of the Ceromancy methods.
