🕯️Hecate's Supper Ghost Good Behavior Spell

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
InvocationLunarPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A three-headed image of Hecate - Fish - Honey - A round cake - Candles (for the cake)
◇Directions
1. Each month at the full moon, bring a three-headed image of Hecate to a three-way crossroads. 2. Position the image so that each face looks out toward one of the roads. 3. Place food offerings on the ground before the image, including fish, honey, and a round cake set with candles. 4. Make a spoken invocation to Hecate, clearly requesting that she compel the ghosts to behave and refrain from causing harm or mischief. 5. Leave the entire meal at the crossroads and walk away without looking back. 6. The food is for whoever comes.
◇Description
Hekate guards the boundary between the living and the dead and can compel ghosts to behave, even if she does not always banish them entirely. This monthly full moon ritual petitions her intervention on behalf of the living.
