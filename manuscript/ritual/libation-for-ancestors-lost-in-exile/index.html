An ancestor candle and water working for those with a pattern of travel-related difficulties rooted in ancestral loss. A white candle and glass of water are offered to ancestors who died on the road or in exile while the moon is in Sagittarius, praying their journey to the next world be easier than their travels in this one.

Quantities

A white candle
A glass of water

Directions

  1. While the moon is in Sagittarius, light a white candle and put out a glass of water for ancestors who lost their lives on the road or who died in exile.
  2. Pray that their travel to the next world goes easier than their travels within this world did.
  3. If travel-related issues continue, try the elevation ritual for ancestral trauma.
