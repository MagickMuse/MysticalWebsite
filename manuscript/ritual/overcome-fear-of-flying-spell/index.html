A nightly week-before spell to overcome fear of flying, using sodalite/malachite/turquoise/lapis or crystal angel to call angel Damabiah to carry on wings of sky.

Quantities

  • A sodalite, malachite, turquoise, or lapis crystal; a small crystal angel; or a favorite piece of jewelry

Directions

  1. Hold the crystal or jewelry in the open hand you write with and pass the other hand, palms down and fingers together, above the crystals.
  2. Moving your hand slowly and rhythmically, say: "On the wings of angel Damabiah, angel of the sky, carry me high. I fear nothing, safe in your care, I can fly anywhere knowing I will be unharmed. I am calm with no anxiety. So I ask and it shall be." Take the crystal in your cabin luggage and before takeoff do the spell subtly, holding the crystal or jewelry in the same hand and again during turbulence and before landing.
  3. If you panic, keep saying the words, picturing Damabiah supporting the plane with his blue wings.
  4. Perform nightly, the week before flying.
