✨Algiers Powder

A paper-burning petition spell using Algiers Powder: write the amount needed, dust with the powder, burn the paper, and sprinkle the ashes on your shoes before going out to play. ⚠️ This working draws on New Orleans Voodoo tradition. Voodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

1 piece of brown paper
Algiers Powder (prepared)
Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Write the exact amount of money you need to win on a piece of brown paper.
  2. Sprinkle Algiers Powder generously over the paper.
  3. Fold the paper up.
  4. Burn it completely.
  5. Collect the ashes and sprinkle them on the soles of your shoes before going out to gamble.
