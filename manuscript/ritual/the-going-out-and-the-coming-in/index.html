Ritual Grimoire

🚪The Going Out and the Coming In

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
PrayerVisualizationMeditation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
No materials required
◇Directions
1. Open your front door and stand just inside it. 2. Place your hands on either side of the door frame. 3. Close your eyes. 4. Feel the liminal power of the threshold: the between-place where you are neither fully inside nor outside. 5. Feel the solidity of your home at your back and the openness of the world before you. 6. Envision a golden light rising from the center of your chest and spreading outward until the open doorway glows with it. 7. Hold awareness of the flame of Brigid burning within you. 8. Speak the following prayer: 9. "The light of Brigid be about me On my going out and on my coming in. May she walk before me on every road. May she stand behind me at every challenge. May she hold the map of my journey And bring me safely home at journey's end." 10. When you are ready, open your eyes and close the door with gratitude.
◇Description
A threshold blessing performed at the front door, using visualization and prayer to sanctify the liminal space between home and world.
