A two-part ritual for cleansing crystals with sea salt burial (2-7 days) and programming them with intention through visualization and energy work. Includes salt disposal guidelines.

Quantities

  • For Cleansing:
  • Container to set crystal inside
  • Blessed water
  • Sea salt
  • Wooden pentacle
    For Programming:
  • Herbs and sea salt (for purification bath)
  • Crystal to be programmed

Directions

  1. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.
  2. For cleansing: rinse out your container with blessed water, then wash it in cool running water.
  3. Dry and set on top of a wooden pentacle.
  4. Pour in sea salt to coat the bottom of the container.
  5. Place the crystal on top of the salt so it does not touch metal, then add more salt over the crystal until it is buried, giving it a chance to clear and rest.
  6. The crystal stays covered for two to seven days; use numerological significance as desired (see Lists of Correspondences).
  7. Rinse crystal in cool running water and proceed to programming.
  8. For programming: bathe to purify yourself with tub water enhanced with herbs and sea salt, by swimming in the ocean, or with a sponge bath with water mixed with sea salt.
  9. Hold the crystal at your third eye psychic point.
  10. Envision the crystal as cleared.
  11. Envision your own white aura expand outward as a cascading waterfall from the center of the body, transforming any darkness into light.
  12. Use this cascading white light to cleanse the crystal.
  13. Project an image that relates to what the crystal is meant to help with, while thinking of the words that match the image.
  14. Once programmed, the crystal only needs a reminder of the image to help in that task.
  15. For disposal of used salt: sea salt from the container may have residue of chaotic energies and can be tossed out away from the dwelling.
  16. It may also be recycled by pouring from one container to another outdoors where the wind can pass through it and dissipate or carry away any chaotic energies.
  17. Store the recycled salt in a dark container with a tight lid.
  18. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
