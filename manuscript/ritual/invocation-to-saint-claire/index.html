🥚EggWhite Candle

A widely practised Cuban Santeria-derived cleansing spell, most effective when performed on a Monday. An egg held in water absorbs negative energy over seven days while a candle dedicated to Saint Claire burns continuously alongside prayer and focused visualisation. This spell has passed broadly into the general magickal community; whether you work with Saint Claire as a spiritual ally or simply honour the tradition in name, perform it in the way that resonates most authentically with you. One of the Egg Cleansing methods.

Quantities

1 whole raw egg
1 glass of water
1 seven-day candle dedicated to Saint Claire (or white seven-day candle)

Directions

  1. Place a whole raw egg carefully into a glass filled with water, taking care not to break it.
  2. Light a seven-day candle dedicated to Saint Claire. A commercial Saint Claire candle is ideal, or substitute a white seven-day candle. If unavailable, burn individual white candles across seven consecutive days.
  3. Over the seven days, accompany the burning candle with prayer, petition, and visualisation of your desired outcomes. If the water evaporates significantly, replace it.
  4. At the end of seven days, carefully remove the egg. Throw the water out the front door onto the street.
  5. Carry the egg to a park (never your backyard) and smash it on the ground. As the egg breaks, your troubles disperse.
  6. Return home via a different, circuitous route.
