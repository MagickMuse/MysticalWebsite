🌾Harvest Moon Grain Gratitude Offering

A cornmeal-and-grain blend mixed under the Lammas or Harvest Moon as an offering of gratitude for past abundance and a request for continued protection. Scatter it around your property as a boundary blessing and land offering, use it to dress a candle, fill an abundance jar, or pack a charm bag or corn husk poppet.

Quantities

  • Cornmeal
  • Dried blue cornflowers
  • Onion powder
  • Dried basil
  • Grains such as barley or oats

Directions

  1. Under the Lammas or Harvest Moon, gather your ingredients.
  2. As you measure each one into a bowl, hold its meaning in mind:
  3. - Cornmeal for abundance and prosperity
  4. Blue cornflowers for peace
  5. Onion powder for protection and money
  6. Basil for protection and wealth
  7. Grains to honor the harvests already given, and to plant the seeds of those still to come
  8. Begin mixing the ingredients together slowly.
  9. As you blend them, say aloud:
  10. "Harvest moons come, the grain fields glow.
    Around me, prosperity and abundance grow."
  11. Repeat the chant as feels right.
  12. While you stir, visualize the earth around you glowing with golden, fertile light, your land blessed and bountiful, all that you have cultivated coming fully to fruition.
  13. Leave the finished mixture in a bowl or jar overnight to charge beneath the moon.
  14. When ready, use it however calls to you:
  15. Scatter a ring of the mixture around the perimeter of your property as a protection boundary and an offering to the land and ancestor spirits
  16. Spoon it into an abundance jar
  17. Press it as a dressing onto a dressed candle
  18. Pack it into a charm bag or corn husk poppet for ongoing prosperity
