🛡️Triple Elemental Circle Shield Gangs

◇Form
Circle RiteCandle Rite
◇Method
ElementalProtectionCandle MagickIncense MagickSalt MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A photo of you/the family, taken outside your home - Four candles in a circle around the photo (green for North/Earth, yellow for East/Air, red for South/Fire, and blue for West/Water) - A bowl of water in which nine pinches of salt have been mixed, to the left of the picture - A frankincense incense stick, to the right of the picture
◇Directions
1. Light each candle clockwise, green first, saying: "Earth, Air, Fire, and Water, protect my home and family, that we will no more harassed be, within or outside our own front door. I ask no more." Make a clockwise circle of saltwater drops around the outside of the candles, repeating the spell words. 2. Light the incense from the green candle, making a smoke circle outside the saltwater circle, saying the spell words again. 3. Pass the red candle around the outside the smoke circle, saying: "Three by three, the power I raise, for tranquil nights and secure days. Protect us." Leave the candles burning.
◇Description
A Tuesday triple elemental circle ritual to shield yourself, home and loved ones if local gangs are threatening you using four element candles and saltwater circles for protection.
