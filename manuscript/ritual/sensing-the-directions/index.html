🧭Sensing the Directions

◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Method
MeditationDirectional MagickElementalEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
## Purpose 1. This exercise builds your sensitivity to directional energies before using them in spellwork. 2. Practice both indoors and outdoors when possible, many witches prefer working surrounded by nature. ## The Practice ### East: Dawn and Beginnings 3. Stand facing east and close your eyes. 4. Take several deep breaths to quiet your thoughts. 5. With an open mind, sense the energy at this compass point, the energy of dawn, birth, and new beginnings. 6. Give yourself time to receive the universe's vibrations. 7. You might feel tingling, warmth or coolness, a subtle emotional shift, or something else entirely. ### South: Fullness and Maturity 8. Turn to face south and sense the energy flowing toward you. 9. This is the energy of fullness and maturity. 10. Notice whether it feels different from what you experienced facing east. ### West: Winding Down 11. Turn to face west and sense the energy flowing toward you. 12. This is the energy of winding down and letting go. 13. Observe how it appears to you. ### North: Silence and Endings 14. Turn to face north and sense the energy flowing toward you. 15. This is the energy of turning inward, silence, and endings. 16. Notice what you feel. ## Notes 17. If you do not sense these energies immediately, continue practicing. 18. With time, you will learn to pick up on the different resonances and attune yourself to them. 19. Record your experiences in your grimoire. 20. As you strengthen your magickal abilities, you will develop a keen awareness of your interconnection with everything in the cosmos. 21. You will realize that you can shape your own reality by aligning with the dynamic, magickal forces surrounding you, and possibilities you never imagined will begin to open.
◇Description
A foundational meditation exercise for sensing the energies of the four cardinal directions before incorporating them into spells and rituals.
