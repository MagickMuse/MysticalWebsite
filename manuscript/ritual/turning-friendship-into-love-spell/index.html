💛Turning Friendship into Love Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Knot MagickLunarManifestationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A very long yellow ribbon - A picture of you and your friend together (if necessary, crop a group photo or use an image-editing program to create a picture of the two of you together); if you don't "do" technology, cut out the two images and paste them on paper
◇Directions
1. Begin to wind the ribbon around the image, saying: "Look at me through the eyes of love. Let passion flare, may closeness grow. The deep bonds are already there, but true love I would know." Secure the ribbon with three knots, saying: "Three times the link between us grows. We are good friends, but true love may you show." Keep the picture and ribbon under your pillow for three days. 2. On day 4, undo the ribbon, saying: "Three times the link between us free; let love between us grow willingly. Look at me through the eyes of love." Cut a small piece of the ribbon off and wear it in a knot next to your skin. 3. Keep the rest in a drawer with the picture, wrapped in something soft belonging to you. 4. Perform any day between the crescent moon and the full moon.
◇Description
An any day between crescent and full moon spell for turning friendship into love, winding long yellow ribbon around photo for three days then undoing with three knots to let passion flare and closeness grow.
