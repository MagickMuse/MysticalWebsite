Beltane

Spell for manifestation and abundance reminiscent of Hopi Powanu Bean-Planting Ceremony. Plant beans indoors representing goals, tend them daily with love. When beans sprout, wishes begin to manifest. Incorporates aspects of Hopi tradition into contemporary magickal working.

Quantities

  • Handful of dried beans (any variety)
  • Dirt/Soil (avoid chemical fertilizers, use quality soil)
  • 1 flowerpot, shallow pan, or other container
  • A few pieces of quartz crystal
  • Several candles (white, gold, or yellow)
  • Water

Directions

  1. Fill container with soil.
  2. Avoid potted plant mixtures with chemical fertilizers; find nice quality soil for optimal conditions.
  3. Think of earth and growing things: plants, trees, animals, living creatures inhabiting planet.
  4. Feel how you are one of these living creatures, just another growing thing living on Earth.
  5. Think about how our lives are linked to and dependent on earth; earth provides both foundation and fuel on which we grow.
  6. Although you only have small container of earth, it is energetically one in same as greater earth outside your door.
  7. Place your hands over container.
  8. Affirm out loud or to yourself: "This is the earth. This is the place where my wishes become manifest. This is the land in which I will grow." Envision entire planet, seeing your place on globe as you stand right where you are.
  9. Think of flourishing plants all over world, imagining their appearance and smells as clearly as possible.
  10. Hold beans in your hand, think about how those particular seeds will also flourish.
  11. For each bean, envision one of your goals manifesting.
  12. Imagine you've already got what you want, project the emotion and feeling that gives you into each respective "wish bean" you prepare.
  13. Plant beans in container of dirt, pour some water over it.
  14. Poke bottom ends of quartz crystals into soil so points stick out a bit.
  15. Quartz is natural amplifier of energy; its presence will help magnify magickal power you've already poured into wish beans.
  16. Surround container with candles, thinking as you do so of sun and its intense power that fuels earth.
  17. Light candles, inviting sunlight and any other energies or entities you like to enter flames, lending their strength to help wish beans thrive.
  18. Think of power of candle flames, power of sun, flowing into beans and into your wishes, charging them up to grow.
  19. Extinguish candles to close this part of working.
  20. Spell is not yet complete.
  21. Continue to water and care for your wish beans until they sprout.
  22. Sit beside container for a few minutes each day and send feeling of love and joy down into soil and into beans.
  23. Envision all your wishes as manifest, imagining total success in all endeavors.
  24. Repeat each day until beans sprout, then keep them or transfer to outdoor location if desired.
  25. Sprouting of beans should coincide with time when each wish begins to manifest in earnest.
  26. If many wish beans fail to sprout, reexamine those particular corresponding wishes, or else blame soil quality and simply try again!
