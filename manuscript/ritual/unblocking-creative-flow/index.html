✨Unblocking Creative Flow

◇Form
Potion
◇Method
Salt MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Salt rock crystal - Milk (animal or vegetable) - Cup or glass
◇Directions
1. Gather a salt rock crystal, milk (animal or vegetable), and a cup or glass. 2. Drop the rock crystal into the milk, saying: 3. "Clear the mind, free the flow, mind aligned in creative glow." 4. The milk will eat away the salt. 5. As the salt melts, so does your creative block. 6. Leave the crystal in the milk until fully dissolved.
◇Description
A dissolving salt ritual to break through creative blocks and maintain artistic flow during winter's darkness.
