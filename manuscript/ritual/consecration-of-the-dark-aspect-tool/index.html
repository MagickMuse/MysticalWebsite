A consecration ritual for tools used in Dark aspect and shadow work. Uses dark altar setup with black/purple candles and dark beverages. Draws dark energy up from the Earth through the body into the tool.

Quantities

  • 3 Altar candles (black or purple)
  • Optional: 4 Quarter candles (purple, white, or black)
  • Incense: mugwort, lilac, Night Lady, or other rich scent
  • Matches
  • Bowl of water
  • Bowl of rock or sea salt
  • Pentacle
  • Cup and dark beverage (blackberry, black currant, elderberry, or Opal Nera)
  • Cauldron
  • Votive (black or purple) with container
  • Plate with dark bread/cake
  • Anointing oil
  • Candle snuffer
  • Athame
  • Wand
  • Tool to be consecrated

Directions

  1. Perform during Esbat or Circle Casting at the designated ritual work point.
  2. Best suited for Dark Moon work.
  3. Inscribe the tool with desired symbols, runes, ogham, and your Craft Name in runes, ogham, or other magickal alphabet.
  4. Hold tool in right hand over the pentacle:
  5. In the names of the Dark Lady and Dark Lord, [Names], I consecrate this [tool] to be used in my practice of the Craft.
  6. I charge this by Elemental Earth [sprinkle with salt] and Elemental Water [sprinkle with blessed water]; by Elemental Fire [pass through center candle flame] and Elemental Air [pass through the incense smoke].
  7. This tool is now by Elemental Powers bound to aid me in my work.
  8. So Mote It Be!
  9. Set the tool on the pentacle:
  10. Great Lady and Great Lord, together in the darkness of this Moon, veiled from my sight yet seen with my inner sight, You dwell within for You are Endings and New Beginnings, the death that leads to new life; You are the Promise and the Love Manifested.
  11. Let this tool be imbued with Your Power to aid me in my dark journeys.
  12. Light votive candle from the center candle:
  13. Let this small light illuminate the dark path to the Realm of Shadows.
  14. Hold up tool in right hand; hold left hand palm parallel to ground:
  15. Lord of Shadows hidden within the Lady of Darkness, together They hold the darkness in Balance.
  16. He is the Passing, She is the Passage, together They move from life into life.
  17. With my hand in Theirs, I call upon Them to share Their presence with me and pass dark power into this [tool] that it may be sanctified to serve me in my dark journeys.
  18. See the Dark Energy shoot up from the ground, into the left palm, pass through the left arm, across the shoulders into the right arm, up the right arm into the right hand, and into the tool.
  19. Set energized tool on pentacle; kneel with both palms on the ground:
  20. As what is sent returns, so I return to You the Power graciously sent to me.
  21. My [tool] is sanctified through Power and Grace, through flesh and blood, and made ready for my use.
  22. So Mote It Be!
  23. Stand; remove tool from pentacle, keeping it on the Altar.
  24. Snuff the votive:
  25. The path is closed; darkness returns to the Shadow Realm.
  26. Proceed to Esbat Conclusion or Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
