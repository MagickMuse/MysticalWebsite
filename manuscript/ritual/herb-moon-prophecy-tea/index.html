A prophetic herbal tea for the summer Herb Moon (also called the Buck Moon, Wort Moon, or Thunder Moon), brewed with sage, lavender, and rosemary to attune to psychic downloads and herbal wisdom at their seasonal peak. The rosemary sprig doubles as a stirring wand, its lightning-bolt shape channeling flashes of insight. Best sipped while divining, meditating, or watching summer storms. Use only food-grade, ideally organic, herbs and flowers.

Quantities

  • 5 oz water
  • 5 fresh sage leaves
  • ½ tsp dried lavender
  • ½ tbsp honey, or to taste
  • 1 fresh rosemary sprig
  • Tarot cards (optional)

Directions

  1. Bring the water to a boil.
  2. Place the sage leaves and dried lavender into a teacup.
  3. Pause before the herbs, breathe in their aroma, hover your hands above them, and take a moment to acknowledge these herbal allies and tune into their wisdom.
  4. Pour the hot water over the herbs.
  5. Stir in honey to taste, a fitting touch for a summer harvest moon tea.
  6. Allow the herbs to steep for 5 minutes, then strain.
  7. Take the rosemary sprig and stir the tea clockwise.
  8. As you stir, notice the sprig's resemblance to a lightning bolt, its length, its branching form.
  9. Let this imagery guide you: invite flashes of insight and clarity into the liquid with each turn, infusing the tea with rosemary's clarifying energy.
  10. Sip slowly while meditating or reading tarot cards.
  11. Open yourself to blessings, psychic prophecy, and the wisdom available under the Herb and Thunder Moon of summer.
