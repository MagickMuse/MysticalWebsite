Jade carved into a circle with a hole in its center, essentially a double concentric circle, provides spiritual protection. Examples include bangle bracelets and circular charms strung on red cord.

Quantities

1 circular jade piece with a center hole (bangle bracelet or charm)
Red cord (for a charm)
Sandalwood for burning

Directions

  1. Hold the jade in your hand to charge it with your purpose.
  2. Empower it by exposing it to moonlight.
  3. Wear the bangle, or string the charm on red cord.
  4. Cleanse it periodically by passing it through sandalwood smoke.
