A pregnancy confirmation spell for safe and healthy pregnancy and delivery, lighting seven pink candles in circle with seven colored crystals to guide baby's safe birth.

Quantities

  • Seven crystals of different colors, choosing ones you like. If you cannot obtain crystals, substitute pearls
  • A white drawstring bag for the crystals
  • Seven small pink candles in a circle

Directions

  1. Set the candles in a circle and the crystals on top of the bag in the center.
  2. As you light each candle clockwise, say: "Seven lights, you baby of love, I bring that safe you grow and strong within; seven lights to your safe birth will guide, dear baby of love, rest warm inside." Hold each of the crystals in turn, and for each crystal, make a wish for the baby's future life.
  3. Set each crystal in front of a candle to make an inner circle.
  4. Repeat the same words, adding: "You, baby of love, I promise true, through all my days to cherish you." Blow out the candles and send the light to the little one silently asking for a safe pregnancy and delivery.
  5. Give the crystals in the bag to the mother.
  6. Perform once your own pregnancy or that of someone close to you is confirmed.
