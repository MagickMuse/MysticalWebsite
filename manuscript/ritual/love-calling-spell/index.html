A simple spell to attract love using a green candle, jewelry, and incense to call your love to find you and stay faithfully.

Quantities

  • One green candle
  • Your favorite piece of jewelry
  • One rose or lavender incense stick in a holder

Directions

  1. Place your jewelry directly in front of the green candle.
  2. Light the candle and gaze into its flame while speaking the invocation:
  3. "I call my love to find me and stay with me faithfully and lovingly as long as the sun shines and the waters of the earth flow."
  4. If you have a specific person in mind, use their name when calling them throughout the working.
  5. Use the candle's flame to light the incense stick, then trace the same words in the air above both the candle and jewelry, letting the smoke write your intention.
  6. Circle the jewelry over the candle flame three times in a clockwise motion, repeating your invocation with each pass.
  7. Extinguish the candle while speaking the words one final time.
  8. Wear this jewelry when going out to meet new people or on dates with someone you're interested in. You may perform this spell again on any Friday to renew and strengthen the energies.
