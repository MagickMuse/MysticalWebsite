🍀Luck🏆Success

A Chinese folk magick spell for scholastic and general success, drawing on the legendary image of magickal carp swimming upstream against all obstacles to transform into dragons, a symbol of perseverance and achievement. Post carp-transforms-into-dragon imagery for general success, or for maximum power, stock a koi pond with golden carp. The pond's location should be determined by a feng shui practitioner.

Quantities

Carp-transforms-into-dragon images (posters, prints, artwork)
OR: A koi pond stocked with golden carp

Directions

  1. Option 1 (Basic): Post carp-transforms-into-dragon images in your home or study space.
  2. Option 2 (Maximum Power): Stock a koi pond with golden carp.
  3. Build the pond outside; for best effect, consult a feng shui practitioner for the most favorable location.
  4. This pond may be used to magickally affect your own scholastic success or that of your children.

About This Spell

According to Chinese legend, certain magical carp swim upstream, fording all sorts of obstacles, to finally be rewarded by transformation into dragons. The folk motif “carp transforms into a dragon” has become a symbol of perseverance and success in various fields, including scholastic achievement.

Two Methods

Option 1 — Basic

Post carp-transforms-into-dragon images in your home or study space to tap into the energy of perseverance and transformation.

Option 2 — Maximum Power

Stock a koi pond with golden carp. Build the pond outside; for best effect, consult a feng shui practitioner for the most favorable location.

This pond may be used to magically affect your own success or that of your children.
