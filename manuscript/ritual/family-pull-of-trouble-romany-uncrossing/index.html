A Romany ritual from Hungary to lift a dark cloud of misfortune from an entire family. Performed at a sacred 'lucky mountain' or powerful natural site. A ritual leader is designated; the whole family participates. Involves a milk libation, burying meat for each family member, fire-spitting, and distributing ash amulets.

Quantities

Donkey's milk
Sow's milk (libation)
One piece of meat per family member
Small fire
Small bags (for ash amulets)

Directions

  1. Identify a sacred power spot, a place where Earth's spiritual and magickal powers are magnified, such as a lucky mountain or its equivalent for you.
  2. Designate one person as the ritual leader.
  3. Approach the power spot together as a family.
  4. The leader offers a libation of donkey's and sow's milk.
  5. Bury one piece of meat for each member of the family.
  6. Pray, petition, and visualize the desired outcome.
  7. Kindle a small fire.
  8. Each person who is to benefit from the uncrossing spits three times into the fire.
  9. Let the fire die out.
  10. Collect the ashes and distribute them in small bags to each family member as protective amulets.
