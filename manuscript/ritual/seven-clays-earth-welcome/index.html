A ceremony held at about seven months, or once the baby is eager to crawl. It formally introduces the child to Mother Earth and reads the child's destiny from the first object the child touches.

Quantities

Clay (seven soft, moist balls, each a different color)
Bathwater
Large bamboo or wicker basket or cage
Rice grains
Coins
Gold chains
Feathers (or a live hen)

Directions

  1. Shape seven soft, damp balls of clay, each in its own color.
  2. Roll each ball of clay over the baby's body.
  3. Give the baby a ritual bath.
  4. Set the baby inside a large bamboo or wicker basket with rice grains, coins, gold chains, and either feathers or a live hen.
  5. Watch which object the baby touches first, since it reveals something of the child's character and destiny.
  6. Slowly lower the baby until the basket rests on the ground.

Lore

A baby's hold on life is considered fragile until about seven months of age. During this time the child is believed to drift between the spiritual and physical worlds, settling a little more firmly into the body each day. For that reason the baby is never set on the ground and instead rides in a sling against the mother. This ceremony marks the moment the child is ready to meet the earth.
