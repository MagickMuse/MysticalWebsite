🎭The Operation of Grand Bewitchment

◇Form
Circle RitePoppetCandle Rite
◇Quantities
ALTAR TRIANGLE (inside): - Paten pentacle - Clay or wax (modeling material) for the poppet - Cernunnos paraphernalia (horns, evergreens, etc.) ALTAR TRIANGLE (outside): - Thurible with Incense of Wrath (recipe forthcoming) - Chalice of bitter wine (rue or myrrh) - Wand - Cord - Athame - 1 red candle of bewitchment (unlit) - Lamps of art - Sabbat Oil - Square of Mars talisman - Object links from target (hair, nails, blood, or saliva) - Baneful herbs + graveyard dust (recipe forthcoming) - Dagydes (exorcised needles of art) -- one or more
◇Directions
TIME AND SETUP: Tuesday, midnight, just before the new moon. Ideally near the winter solstice. Arrange a triangle around the altar pointing north; align the table of practice also to the north. ATTUNEMENT: Work through a numerical progression on the Square of Mars to attune to the Martial current. CONSECRATE THE TRIANGLE: Asperge the floor triangle with bitter wine, accompanied by an invocation to Cernunnos ("Eko, Eko, Azarak"). PREPARE THE MATERIAL: Grind together the baneful herbs and graveyard dust. While grinding, continuously chant: "I work to the destruction of [victim's name]!" Mix the ground powder into the clay or wax and knead it thoroughly, sustaining the chant throughout. MODEL THE POPPET: Shape the clay or wax into a figure 5 to 8 inches tall, chanting the target's name without ceasing. Insert the object links (hair, nails, etc.) into the appropriate body parts. Engrave the target's name and the words "Cabye. Aaaze. Hit. Pel. Meltat." into the figure. CONSECRATE THE POPPET: Asperge with bitter wine and declare: "In the name of Cernunnos, the Horned One / Creature of wax (or earth) I name thee [victim's name]! / Thou art [victim's name]!" Infuse life by touching the poppet with the wand and tracing the target's name in witch fire. Exhibit the poppet at the north, west, south, and east, chanting the name at each quarter. Place the figure in the triangle, head pointing north. ANOINT THE CANDLE: Anoint the red candle with Sabbat Oil no fewer than 81 times, chanting a litany of hate and destruction that specifies the desired torment in detail. Place the candle at the northernmost tip of the triangle, beyond the poppet's head. Light it. INVOCATION POSTURE: Move to the north, face south. Cross both arms on the breast in the gesture of the God of death. Chant: "It is not my hand which does this deed, but that of Cernunnos, the Horned One!" THE STABBING: Accelerate pulse by swift breathing to enter an altered state. Allow Cernunnos to guide the left hand to the Dagyde (needle). Chant the conjuration: "Arator, Lapidator, Omtator! / Somniator, Subaerfor, Iquator! / Signator, Sudator, Combustor! / Pugnator, Ductor, Seductor! / Comostor, Oneator! / Vos omnes ministri odey et destructiones..." [Continue with the full Latin conjuration as written.] Drive the Dagyde into the designated body part of the poppet and declare: "So mote it be!" For general wasting: use a single Dagyde through the heart, then dissolve the poppet in fire (if wax) or water (if clay). CLOSE: Allow the candle to burn out entirely. This rite may be repeated on multiple occasions to build a pyramid or vortex effect. COVEN VERSION: The circle is cast around the triangle with all members joining in the words and visualization. A single operator performs the physical actions within the triangle.
◇Description
The most complex baneful operation in this chapter. A full ceremonial poppet-working performed at Tuesday midnight near the new moon, ideally near the winter solstice. Involves constructing a clay or wax figure kneaded with baneful herbs and graveyard dust, consecrating it as the target under Cernunnos, anointing and lighting a red candle of bewitchment, and working Dagydes (exorcised needles of art) into the figure with a Latin conjuration. May be performed by a solitary operator or with a coven circle surrounding the altar triangle.
