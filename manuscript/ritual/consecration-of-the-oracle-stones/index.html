A rite to consecrate an oracle of six stones, three light, three dark, that will be consulted at key moments to learn Hekate's will. Rite 7 of Book I.

Quantities

1 pen, 1 sheet of paper, 1 bowl for salt water, matches, 3 white candles, myrrh incense with a censer or heat-safe holder, cinnamon for dusting the hands, a small cloth pouch, six stones of similar size and shape (three light-colored, three dark), salt, spring water.

Directions

  1. Find a private place at a liminal hour and do the usual: wash your hands thrice, dust them with cinnamon, draw the sigil, and light three white candles and myrrh incense. Then set a bowl of salt water to the left of the sigil, and the pouch of six stones, three light-colored, three dark, all of similar size and shape, to the right.
  2. Breathe deeply until centered. Tip a lit candle so a little hot wax drips into the salt water, then asperge the four corners of the room with it.
  3. Sing the consecration hymn you learned for the previous rite, visualizing a helix of blue fire forming around you as you do.
  4. Take the six stones from the pouch and line them up before you. Speak aloud your intention: "In the name of Hekate, I now consecrate these stones to serve me, so that by consulting them I shall know the will of Hekate; once questioned, the dark stones coming to my hand will indicate YES, and the light ones coming to my hand will indicate NO."
  5. Take one dark stone and one light stone, these will be the Tongues of the Sea. While speaking the first stanza below, pass them through the rising myrrh fumes, then through the flame of the center candle, then dip them into the bowl of salt water. Call out the voces magicae at the stanza's end, then set the two stones down:
    "Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Sea; / See the one who stands before you, for she / Would be a black dog, / Who serves the Black Queen. / Hear my words, / Hear my words, / For this is what they mean: / As Hekate led Kore from the earth in spring, / So you must bring knowledge to me; / When asked, answer truthfully; / As you honor her, so serve me: / Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Sea. / AKHA AKHAKHA KHAKH / KHARKHARA KHAKH"
  6. Take the next dark and light stone, the Tongues of the Air, and consecrate them the same way while speaking the second stanza:
    "Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Air; / See the one who stands before you, and prepare / To give voice to voiceless spirits / That attend the Night Mare; / Hear my words, / Hear my words, / For this is what they share: / As Hekate questioned the Sun in autumn, / So I will question you; / When asked, answer me, to give the Queen her due; / As you honor her, so serve me too. Now, prepare: / Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Air. / AKHA AKHAKHA KHAKH / KHARKHARA KHAKH"
  7. Take the final dark and light stone, the Tongues of the Underground, and consecrate them the same way while speaking the third stanza:
    "Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Underground; / See the one who stands before you, and go down / To the caves within the earth, / And retrieve all that you've found; / Hear my words, / Hear my words, / For this is how they sound: / As Hekate made Hades in winter unlock his iron gate, / So my call will draw forth Fate / In the form of six stones which give tongue to what awaits; / As you honor her, stones, so to me be bound: / Stones, you are no stones, but two Tongues of the Underground. / AKHA AKHAKHA KHAKH / KHARKHARA KHAKH"
  8. Once all six stones are consecrated, return them to the pouch. Close your eyes and extend your senses first to the candles before you, then gradually outward to every corner of the room. Abide with any presence you encounter for as long as it stays; if none appears, rest content, tomorrow the oracle will speak.
  9. End the rite by singing the first six lines of the consecration hymn, then blow out the candles, and kiss and burn the sigil.

Context

The seventh of nine nights leading to the new moon dedication, two days before it, in which the reader consecrates a six-stone oracle that will later confirm her readiness to advance.

Recommended Reading

The Orphic Hymn to Artemis, which sets forth the many names and attributions of Hekate's titanic first cousin, with whom she was often conflated.
