A psychic awareness exercise that extends proprioception beyond the physical body. Enhances personal protection by connecting spatial awareness to extrasensory perception.

Quantities

None required (visualization-based)

Directions

  1. Find a comfortable position and relax your mind, taking a few deep breaths until you feel ready.
  2. Picture yourself from about ten feet away, rotating your vision of yourself 360 degrees to confirm the image is firmly in place.
  3. Imagine whiskers growing from your face, like a cat's, extending outward until they reach past your shoulders by a comfortable margin.
  4. Adjust the length as needed: expand them for greater awareness of your surroundings, or trim them when crowded spaces provide more feedback than you need.
  5. Consider adding a few longer hairs down the length of your spine for additional rear awareness.

Understanding Proprioception

Proprioception is the sense of where your body exists in space. It's what allows you to know where your foot is even when you can't see it. It's also the sense that alerts you when someone stands behind you before they speak, or when you're being watched.

This isn't a psychic sense in itself, but it connects to the unconscious. This exercise links proprioception to extrasensory perception (clairaudience, clairsentience, clairvoyance) for enhanced personal protection.

Calibration Notes

Evaluate your whisker length occasionally. If pulled too close to the body, you may find yourself walking too close to corners and doorways, bumping into things. This physical clumsiness indicates your spatial awareness needs expansion.

This exercise works with or without external shielding and can be maintained throughout daily activities.
