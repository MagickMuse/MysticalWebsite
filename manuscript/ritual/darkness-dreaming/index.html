⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING
A beginner baneful spell that causes nightmares and troubled sleep for the target. Black ink is dripped into water before a candle flame while a chant is repeated three times.

Quantities

1 large clear glass
Pure water (rainwater preferred)
Black ink (or black/dark purple food coloring)
1 black candle

Directions

  1. Fill a large clear glass with pure water, preferably rainwater.
  2. Set the glass before a lit black candle.
  3. Look through the water and watch the flame for a moment.
  4. While watching the candle, drip three drops of black ink into the water.
  5. Watch intently as the ink floats down through the water and begins to dissipate.
  6. Recite the following chant:
  7. Darkness falls and darkness flows,
    wonder what a dreamer knows?
    Fear and pain and things that scare,
    magick brings [target's name] a nightmare.
  8. Drip one drop of ink onto the tip of your left index finger.
  9. Repeat the chant a second time.
  10. Touch the inked finger to the surface of the water and recite the chant a third and final time.
  11. Leave the water sitting overnight.
  12. The ink can be washed from your finger immediately if desired.
  13. If liquid ink is unavailable, black or dark purple food coloring or dye may be substituted.
