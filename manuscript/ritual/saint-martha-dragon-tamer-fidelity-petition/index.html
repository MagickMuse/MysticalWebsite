🕯️Martha the Dominator Oil

A candle petition to Saint Martha the Dominator to tame a straying spouse and restore fidelity to the home.

Quantities

Depiction of Saint Martha with dragon
1 green or white candle
Martha the Dominator Oil
Personal item belonging to mate (representing infidelity)

Directions

  1. Set up an altar for Martha with a depiction of the saint and her dragon.
  2. Carve a green or white candle with your name and identifying information, charging it with your desire.
  3. Dress the candle with Martha the Dominator Oil.
  4. Place an item belonging to your mate beside the candle.
  5. Make your petition to Martha on a Tuesday, her most favorable day.
