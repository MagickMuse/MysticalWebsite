A tiny bit of pubic hair (right side) A little armpit hair (left side) Fresh coffee beans Optional: chocolate, sugar, cream, aphrodisiac liqueurs

1. Snip off a tiny bit of pubic hair from the right side. 2. Snip a little hair from under your left arm. 3. Grind these together with fresh coffee beans and brew as coffee. 4.
