Imbolc

A daily practice of recommitting to love, inspired by Swan's lifelong partnerships and annual migrations. By placing hands on heart and consciously choosing your devotion each day, whether to a person, project, pet, or yourself, you cultivate the endurance that sustains lasting bonds.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Swans mate for life and travel great distances each year, migrating across continents yet always returning to the same partner.
  2. Though your relationship stretches across time and hardship, you recommit each dawn.
  3. This is the practice of choosing love again, day after day, year after year.
  4. Like Swan's long flights home, love requires intention renewed daily.
  5. Place both hands over your heart.
  6. Close your eyes and breathe.
  7. Feel your heartbeat beneath your palms.
  8. Ask yourself: What do I choose to devote myself to today?
  9. This may be a person, a project, a pet, or yourself.
  10. Speak aloud: "Today I choose [name what you choose]. I commit my heart, my time, my presence. I show up. I return. I choose this bond again."
  11. Breathe into this commitment.
  12. Feel it settling into your body.
  13. When you're ready, open your eyes and carry this renewed devotion into your day.
  14. Repeat this practice daily, or whenever your commitment wavers.
  15. Like Swan returning across vast distances, you are practicing the endurance that sustains lasting love.
