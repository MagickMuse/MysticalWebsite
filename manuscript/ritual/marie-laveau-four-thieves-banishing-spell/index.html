🛡️Four Thieves Vinegar

Traditionally attributed to Marie Laveau, considered one of the most effective banishing spells. The target's name is written nine times and crossed with your own name and command, sealed in Four Thieves Vinegar, and disposed of in running water.

Quantities

  • Square of paper
  • Pen
  • 1 small glass jar or bottle with tight lid
  • Four Thieves Vinegar (enough to fill the jar)

Directions

  1. Write your target's name nine times on a square of paper.
  2. Cover and cross each name with your own, saying: "I cross you, I cover you, I command you, I compel you to [fill in your desire]."
  3. Place this paper inside a small glass jar or bottle.
  4. Fill the bottle with Four Thieves Vinegar and seal it tightly shut.
  5. Dispose of the bottle. The traditional mode is to toss it over your left shoulder into running water without looking; adapt this to your needs and situation.
