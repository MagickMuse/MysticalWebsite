🔮New Year's Divination Party

A group ritual for New Year's Eve where magickal friends gather to share divination techniques, exchange readings, and perform a collective midnight divination to open magick eyes for the coming year.

Quantities

  • For the Host:
  • Single large candle for the center
  • Enough libations for all guests
  • Cups or glasses for toasting
  • Snacks and treats
  • Extra divination tools (optional)
  • Small notebooks or cards for recording
  • Cushions and blankets for comfort
  • Symbol interpretation guides or books
    For Each Guest (to bring):
  • Their favorite divination tools
  • A treat or beverage to share
  • Personal journal for recording
    For the Space:
  • Lighting control (ability to turn off all lights)
  • Central gathering area
  • Comfortable seating for all
  • Tables or surfaces for divination work

Directions

  1. Planning the Party:
    Invitations:
    Ask magickal friends to join you on New Year's Eve. Request that each person bring:
  2. Their favorite divination tools (tarot, runes, pendulum, scrying mirror, etc.)
  3. A favorite treat or beverage to share
  4. A journal for recording their midnight divination
    Timing:
    Plan to gather 3-4 hours before midnight to allow time for sharing, readings, and the ritual.
    Party Structure:
    First Hour or Two: Sharing Techniques
  5. Gather in a comfortable space with good lighting.
  6. Each person takes 10-15 minutes to demonstrate their divination method.
  7. Share tips, favorite spreads, or unique approaches.
  8. This is a teaching and learning circle, ask questions!
    Next Hour: Giving and Getting Readings
  9. Pair up or work in small groups.
  10. Exchange readings with each other.
  11. Focus on questions about the coming year.
  12. Take notes on what you learn.
  13. Rotate partners to get multiple perspectives.
    Leading Up to Midnight: Preparation
  14. About 15 minutes before midnight, prepare libations for everyone.
  15. Each person should have their primary divination tool at hand.
  16. Clear a central space for gathering.
  17. Place a single candle in the center (unlit for now).
    The Midnight Ritual:
    Just Before Midnight:
  18. Pour a libation for each person.
  19. Turn off every light in the house.
  20. Gather in a circle around the central candle.
  21. Light the single candle.
  22. Together, holding hands or with hands on hearts, repeat:
    Gather we as old year ends
    together here with knowing friends
    as the New Year passes by
    open up our magick eyes.
    At the Stroke of Midnight:
  23. Turn on all the lights.
  24. Everyone sits with their divination tool.
  25. Remain silent.
  26. Each person performs their own New Year's divination asking: "What does the new year hold for me?"
  27. Continue in silence until everyone is finished (15-30 minutes).
    After Individual Divinations:
  28. Toast the New Year together.
  29. Share highlights from your readings (if desired).
  30. Celebrate with food, drink, and conversation.
  31. Exchange contact information to check in throughout the year.
    Recording the Night:
    Encourage everyone to journal their midnight divination immediately. Suggest checking in with each other quarterly to see how predictions are manifesting.
    Hosting Tips:
  32. Have extra divination tools available for guests who want to try something new
  33. Provide small notebooks or cards for recording readings
  34. Set up divination stations around your space
  35. Offer both alcoholic and non-alcoholic libations
  36. Create a cozy, magickal atmosphere with cushions, blankets, and low lighting
  37. Have symbol interpretation guides or books available for reference
  38. Consider recording the group chant for those who want to repeat it at home
    Variation for Virtual Gatherings:
    Host via video call. Everyone lights their own candle at home. Mute all participants for the midnight divination, then unmute to share and celebrate together.
