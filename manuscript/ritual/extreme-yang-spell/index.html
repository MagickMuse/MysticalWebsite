Firecracker🪞Mirror⚔️Sword

In traditional Chinese belief, what is commonly called demonic activity may be a manifestation of excessive yin energy. It can be neutralized by flooding the space with potent yang energy using one or more of the following: firecrackers, mirrors, the crowing of a rooster, or swords. Reading sacred texts or philosophy books is also effective, as they generate order, an environment in which demons, who crave chaos, cannot thrive.

Quantities

  • One or more of: firecrackers, mirror, sword
  • OR: a rooster (its crowing)
  • OR: sacred texts or philosophy books

Directions

  1. Select one or more yang-dominant tools or methods: set off firecrackers, place a mirror in the space, allow a rooster to crow, or display a sword.
  2. Alternatively, read sacred texts or philosophy books aloud.
  3. Apply whichever method suits the situation to flood the area with yang energy and drive out the excessive yin manifesting as demonic presence.
