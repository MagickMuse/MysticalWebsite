🕯️Dedicating a Well in the Name of Brigid

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form

◇Setting

◇Quantities

◇Directions
1. Perform the dedication on Imbolc. 2. Anoint a white candle with rosemary and lavender oil, surround it with rowanberries, and set it in a sheltered lantern to protect it from winter weather. 3. Place a Brigid statue facing the well to help focus intention. 4. At sunset, carry the lit candle to the well along with a written blessing, then speak the blessing aloud: 5. I bless this well in the name of the goddess Brigid on her feast day of Imbolc. May these waters be filled with her power, light, and grace. As with her holy well of Kildare, so shall it be in the waters of this land. May all who touch these waters be filled with healing for mind, body, and spirit. May the waters of this well be filled with the blessings of the goddess Brigid on her feast day of Imbolc. By the power of earth, air, fire, and water, I name this well Brigid's Well. May her loving energy bless and preserve us with her healing, protection, and grace. As I will it, so shall it be! 6. Afterward, the well's water carries sacred energy into showers, dishwashing, and drinking water throughout the home. 7. Rededicate the well each year on Imbolc to renew the blessing. 8. For city water rather than a well, tie a white ribbon around the main incoming pipe, knotting it three times while saying "In the name of the goddess Brigid" with each knot. 9. Place a white candle in a jar candleholder, tie a second white ribbon from the same spool around the jar, knot it three times with the same words, then perform the blessing above at the kitchen sink. 10. The candle and the incoming pipe become energetically connected, turning household water into an ongoing blessing.
◇Description
A candle and water blessing that dedicates a well, or a home's incoming water, to the goddess Brigid on Imbolc.
