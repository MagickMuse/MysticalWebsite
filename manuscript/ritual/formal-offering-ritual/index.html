🍲Formal Offering Ritual

A complete offering ritual that rewards protectors and familiars while pacifying hostile spirits. This working speaks to elemental and spirit forces generally, using incense and optional libations as physical support for mentally multiplied offerings that fill all space. Safe for regular practice and suitable for beginners.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Incense (juniper, sandalwood, or your preferred blend)
  • Matches or lighter
  • Altar or table
    Optional:
  • Libation (water, tea, or whiskey) to pour on ground if working outdoors
    Avoid:
  • Spirit manifestation herbs (dittany of Crete, mullein) when offering to potentially hostile forces

Directions

  1. Part 1: Purification of the Offerings
  2. Arrange your incense and libation (if using) on an altar or table.
  3. Do not light the incense yet.
  4. Hold your hands over the offerings, forming the triangle of manifestation between your hands.
  5. Say:
  6. By Earth, the body of the gods
    By Water, their flowing blood
    By Air, the breath of the gods
    By Fire, their burning soul
    May these offerings be made blessed and made pure.
  7. As you speak, visualize impurities being washed, blown, and burned away from the offerings.
  8. Part 2: Inviting the Guests
  9. IO EVOHE! IO DAEMONES! DAIMONES EVOHE!
    Spirits of the firmament of Earth and of ether
    Spirits of the dry land and of the flowing water
    Spirits of the whirling air and of rushing fire
    Come! Come!
    Phantoms of the dead, the quick, and in-between
    To those to whom I owe debt and who owe debt unto me
    Famulus and guardians who are bound unto me!
    Come! Come!
    Every dryad, sylph, and satyr who dwells within this place
    Every undine and salamander, every fey and gnomish spirit!
    Every succubi and incubi, every spectre of ill will
    Come! Come!
    All spirits who cause help or harm in response to human action!
    Come here according to your desires, be seated on the thrones
    Io Evohe! Come! Come!
  10. Part 3: Making the Offering
  11. Light the incense.
  12. Give the sign of offering by rubbing your palms together several times until they become hot.
  13. Turn your palms upward, and as the heat leaves your hands, imagine clouds of offerings flowing from them, mingling with the incense smoke and filling all space.
  14. Clouds of offerings, I give to you
    Food and drink and fumigation
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    Let the offerings arise and pervade all space
    Let it take the form that is most desired
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    Friends and family from former lives
    I am grateful for your past kindness
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    You who form obstacles as retribution to my action
    Forgive any offense made by mistake or delusion
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    Spirits of the dead and trapped in-between spaces
    Wardens of this ground and keepers of the winds
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    Guardians and familiars, be thou fulfilled
    Quickly realize my hopes and desires
    Enjoy! Enjoy!
    To each of you I offer inexhaustible treasures and
    Delightful substances and enjoyments.
    You who would harm me
    Partake of this feast and be at peace
    You who would help me
    Be fulfilled and accomplish that with which you are charged
  15. After making this charge, you may either proceed to the closing or sit and commune with the forces invoked.
  16. Part 4: License to Depart
  17. Honored guests of this temple, the window of our communion is closing,
    Take your last taste of these enjoyments and go in peace.
    Vacate the thrones of the feast and go forth unto your abodes and habitations as you desire,
    Forever act as friends and helpers.
    So Mote It Be
