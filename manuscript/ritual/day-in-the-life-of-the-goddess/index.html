Lughnasadh

A day‑long Lughnasadh practice honoring Tailtiu’s legacy and the divine feminine. Carry a consecrated apple as the goddess companion while embodying Beauty, Compassion, Inspiration, and Tenacity. Close at sunset with apple‑star divination and thanksgiving.

Quantities

  • One small apple (to carry all day)
  • Athame or small blade for the closing apple cut
  • Optional: bath supplies for a purifying shower, clothing or jewelry that helps you attune to goddess energy

Directions

  1. Purpose: Honor goddess energies and attune to the Lughnasadh current.
    Timing and setting: Begin at morning and continue until sunset. Indoors or outdoors.
  2. Pre‑ritual purification:
  3. Bathe or shower, envisioning negativity rinsing away. Dress as you feel the goddess, colors like white, red, black, or silver; moon or pear motifs; anything that evokes beauty and strength.
  4. Consecrate the companion apple:
  5. Hold a small apple and invite the goddess as you understand her (or name a deity) to indwell it and walk with you through the day. Keep the apple on your person (pocket, bag, etc.).
  6. Live the qualities through the day (choose your own acts; examples below):
  7. Beauty: Tend appearance with care. Walk as radiant. Or stroll in nature and consciously take in beauty.
  8. Compassion: Do a heartfelt act of kindness. Give quietly. Listen deeply. Make it sincere and from the heart.
  9. Inspiration: Go where your spirit lights up (park, museum, beach, concert, studio). Then be a muse, spark others' creativity with words, chalk art, music, or encouragement.
  10. Tenacity: Do something requiring effort and follow‑through. Examples: volunteer cleanup, help a neighbor, practice or train, refrain from a habit for the day. Keep health and safety in view.
  11. Sunset closing rite:
  12. Sit comfortably with the apple. Reflect on the day you and the goddess shared. When ready, set a simple space (cast a circle if you wish). Invite any further presences.
  13. Address the goddess with gratitude in your own words or:
    Great Lady, Great Goddess, Great Mother of the Earth!
    Great Lady of Beauty!
    Great Goddess of Compassion!
    Tenacious Mother and Source of Inspiration!
    Today I walked with you, and today you walked with me.
    Thank you, Gracious Goddess, for all the things you helped me see!
  14. Apple‑star message:
  15. With an athame or small blade, cut the apple horizontally to reveal the five‑pointed star of seeds. Gaze softly at the star. Let inner images arise. Speak impressions aloud or record notes. When finished, consider what to continue beyond today.
  16. Closing:
  17. Thank the goddess and any spirits present. Release the space. Eat the apple altar‑side to complete the rite and leave any scraps outdoors for wildlife.
