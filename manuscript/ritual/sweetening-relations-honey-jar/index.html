A symptom of magickal attacks are relationship problems. You can use this honey jar to sweeten things up.

Quantities

  • Honey is the main ingredient, but you can add some sweets like molasses or sugar to add a little extra pizzazz

Directions

  1. Place all the items in the jar and burn a white candle on top of the jar while saying,
    ”By honey, sugar, and all things sweet,
    Establish understanding and peace.
    May good will reign between me and thee,
    As I will, so shall it be.”
  2. Keep the jar in a space where you and the person(s) frequent.
  3. Variant (Sweeten Your Heart): write your target's name on a slip of white paper, place it in a small lidded jar, cover it with honey, and close the jar tightly. Set a small white candle on the lid and burn it to sweeten that person's heart toward you.

A honey jar working to repair and sweeten relationships damaged by magical attack or conflict. This spell uses the binding and preserving qualities of honey combined with personal links to restore harmony between people.

Basic Honey Jar

The foundation of this working requires:

  • A jar (size depends on how many people are being linked)
  • Honey (the primary ingredient)
  • Additional sweets like molasses and sugar for extra potency
  • A personal item from each person who needs relationship healing (hair, nail clippings, handwriting, photos, or small personal objects)

Consent: It is best if everyone who will be linked to the jar knows about the spell and willingly gives their personal link. However, this is not strictly necessary if you are working to heal your own relationships.

Directions

Place all the personal items into the jar. Fill the jar with honey, adding molasses and sugar as desired. Ensure all items are fully submerged and coated.

Burn a white candle on top of the sealed jar while speaking this incantation:

"By honey, sugar, and all things sweet,

Establish understanding and peace.

May good will reign between me and thee,

As I will, so shall it be."

Placement

The location where you keep the jar matters:

  • Bedroom: If the jar is intended to heal relations between you and your spouse or romantic partner
  • Near the hearth/kitchen: If it is to work for your whole family or household

Keep the jar in place for as long as the relationship needs sweetening. You can periodically burn another white candle on top to refresh the working.

Sexual Healing Variation

If sexual problems were caused by the attack, you can create a specialized jar for you and your partner that includes:

  • Sex fluids from both partners
  • Pubic hairs from both partners
  • Love-drawing roots and botanicals:
    • Adam and Eve Roots (carried as a pair to ensure continued happiness)
    • Pairs of Bloodroot (for love and protection)
    • Orris (Orris root for love drawing)
    • Trillium (for love and wealth in the relationship)
    • Raccoon penis bone (traditional love charm, also called "coon dick" in hoodoo)

This variation focuses specifically on restoring sexual intimacy and passion that may have been disrupted by magical interference.

Context

Relationship problems are one of the most common symptoms of magical attack. If during the attack you experienced difficulties with your spouse, children, or others in your life, this honey jar provides a gentle way to repair bonds and restore peace. The sweetness literally "sweetens" attitudes and emotions, making people more favorably disposed toward one another.
