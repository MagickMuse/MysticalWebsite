Ritual Grimoire

🌈Family Acceptance for LGBTQ+

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraHealing
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
• 1 jug of water • 1 green plant for each family member, in a circle • 1 single flowering plant for you, in the center of the circle • 1 small pink rose quartz or amethyst
◇Directions
1. Water each of the plants in the circle clockwise, yours last, saying for each: "We all are nourished from one source, through the years we've been together. I ask you now accept my course, I am just the same as ever." Hold the rose quartz/amethyst over each plant in turn, in the same order, saying for each one: "The peace of love and the family, I am as I was and always will be. Welcome the choices I have made, respect and value me this day." Go outdoors and plant the greenery in a circle around the flowering plant. 2. Push the rose quartz/amethyst into the soil beneath the flowering plant, saying: "All one family, can we still be. Please be happy and loving to me."
◇Description
A ritual for overcoming prejudice within your family if you are in a same-sex relationship or are transgender and your family members just don't get it, using circle of plants with crystal to request acceptance.
