Imbolc

Brigid's cloak talisman, a length of cloth left outside on Imbolc Eve to be blessed by the goddess.

Quantities

Length of Irish linen or wool cloth
Green silk ribbon (variation)

Directions

  1. Leave a length of cloth, traditionally Irish linen or wool, outside overnight on Imbolc Eve for Brigid to bless as she passes by.
  2. Bring the cloth in the next morning.
  3. It can then be worn, used for healing, or placed on the altar, and brought out again each year to be blessed anew.
  4. As a variation, tie a length of green silk ribbon to a tree and leave it there all year, to blow in the wind and disperse Brigid's blessings as the seasons turn.
