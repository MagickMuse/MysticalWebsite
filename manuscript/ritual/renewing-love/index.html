Ritual Grimoire

❤️‍🔥Renewing Love

◇Form
Candle RiteAnointing
◇Method
Candle MagickIncense Magick
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Red or pink taper candle with holder, or pillar candle - 2 white taper candles and holders - Essential oils or two small incense sticks, one representing each participant - Incense heater if using incense - Chalice or wine glass - Two small glasses with pouring spouts - Drinking liquids, one representing each person - Altar cloth in white, pink, or red - Music, optional - Small items that remind each person of the other - Coffee stirrer, wooden craft stick, or mixing spoon, optional - Small cloth for dressing candles, optional
◇Directions
1. Purpose: - Affirm or reaffirm love between two people during Imbolc, the Milk Moon, or Valentine's Day. 2. Preparation: - Choose a time without interruptions. Dim lights, turn off phones, play gentle music if desired. - Spread altar cloth on a safe, sturdy surface. Place white candles in holders on left and right sides of altar. - Place red or pink candle in center. Place empty chalice a few inches in front of center candle. - If using oils, place small amounts in dishes next to each person's white candle. If using incense, place sticks next to white candles and position censer in front of chalice. - Place two small glasses on altar, one on each side. Fill partially with chosen liquids that will blend well when mixed. - Light both white candles before beginning. 3. Ritual: - Sit facing each other in front of altar. Clear minds and make eye contact when ready. - Person on right speaks first, expressing love and pointing to their white candle as representing the light brought into their life. Use personal, meaningful words. - Person on left responds similarly, pointing to their white candle. - Person on left pours liquid from their glass into chalice. - Person on right responds with their statement and pours their liquid into chalice. - Both stir liquids together with index fingers or stirrer, overlapping dominant hands. - Both say together: "Two become one and the one empowers two." - Both hold chalice at eye level. Person on right drinks while person on left says: "Tonight, my spirit is yours and yours is mine. As the liquids meld, so do our souls." - Person on left drinks while person on right repeats the words. - If using oils, both dress the center candle with their oils. If using incense, both light their incense sticks from their white candles and place in censer side by side. - Both say together: "Two souls become one just as two scents become one. Two souls become one just as two flames become one." - Join hands over chalice. Each lift their white candle with free hand and together light center red or pink candle. - Return candles to altar. Celebrate the energy created together. 4. Closing: - Extinguish candles safely. Allow incense to burn out naturally if remaining in room. 5. Notes: - Ritual can be performed skyclad if both are comfortable. - Can be done within a magick circle or without, as both prefer. - Personal deities can be invited if desired. - All spoken words should be personalized to the relationship. - Performing under moonlight adds energy, any moon phase works. - Best performed indoors unless weather permits outdoor skyclad ritual.
◇Description
A ritual to affirm or reaffirm the love between two people. Written in a gender-neutral way for any couple, including same-sex relationships. Can also be adapted for platonic love between two people with modifications.
