📿Mugwort Fertility Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
CharmPouch
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Herbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Mugwort gathered from nine different fields
◇Directions
1. Visit at least nine different fields and pick mugwort from each. 2. Gather with intention. 3. Bind all the gathered plants together into a bundle. 4. Post the bound bundle as a fixed amulet in the home, or carry it in a charm bag on the person. 5. For greatest potency, gather the mugwort under a Full Moon or during the Midsummer's Eve festivities.
◇Description
A mugwort fertility charm in which the plant is gathered from nine separate fields, bound together, and posted as an amulet or carried in a charm bag. Most powerful when gathered under the Full Moon or during Midsummer's Eve festivities.
