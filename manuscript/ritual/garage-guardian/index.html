🛡️Garage Guardian

◇Form
CharmConsecrated Item
◇Method
ProtectionWater MagickConsecration
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Silver coin (silver dollar ideal) - Quartz crystal - Running water (natural source, fountain, or tap) - Glue or tape - Optional: Second coin and crystal - Optional: Jar filled with source water (for renewal)
◇Directions
1. In historic rural Ireland and Scotland, scores of spells revolved around protecting barns and livestock. 2. Pagan farm owners may well still use those spells. 3. Since the advent of the automobile, magickal people may also in turn apply barn magick to their garage, as it protects one of our primary and most costly forms of transportation. 4. The Spell: 5. You will need to visit a source of running water. 6. While a natural source is preferable, if you live somewhere landlocked you can use a public fountain or simply run your tap. 7. Take a silver coin (a silver dollar is ideal) and a quartz crystal. 8. Dip the coin into running water, envisioning the molecules of the water and the coin comingling and becoming part of one another. 9. Take the coin and the crystal and glue or tape it to the inside of your garage door or to the ceiling of your garage. 10. You may want to keep a second coin and crystal in a jar of your source water. 11. When it comes time to renew house blessings and defenses, anoint the mounted crystal and coin with the water to refresh its energy.
◇Description
A protection charm for garages and vehicles, adapted from historic Irish and Scottish barn protection spells. Uses a silver coin and quartz crystal blessed in running water, then mounted in the garage for ongoing protection of transportation.
