🥞Dumb Cakes True Love Dream Spell

A traditional sleepover dream spell performed in total silence. Flat cakes made of flour, salt, and fireplace ashes are cooked on a griddle, marked with each participant's name or initials, and then placed under pillows (not eaten!) to incubate dreams of true love. Complete silence is maintained from the moment of casting through the next morning, when all dreams are recorded.

Can be performed alone or as a sleepover party with sisters or close friends.

Quantities

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup salt
  • A sprinkle of fireplace ashes (ashes from love incense or a romance divination fire are ideal)
  • Water (enough to form a batter)
  • A greased griddle
  • Additional salt for flipping

Directions

  1. Maintain complete silence from the start of this ritual through the next morning after all dreams have been recorded.
  2. Not a single word.
  3. Combine one cup of flour, one cup of salt, and a sprinkle of fireplace ashes.
  4. Ashes from love incense or from a fireplace romance divination are most potent, but any fireplace ash works.
  5. Add just enough water to form a batter.
  6. Ladle the batter onto a heated, greased griddle, making one dumb cake per participant.
  7. Flip the cakes as you would pancakes with a spatula.
  8. Before each cake is completely done, scratch each participant's name, initials, or personal identifying mark into the surface so everyone knows which cake belongs to whom.
  9. Each time a cake is flipped, add an additional sprinkle of salt.
  10. When all cakes are done, do not eat them.
  11. Each person places her dumb cake under her pillow.
  12. Dream of your own true love.
  13. The next morning, each person records their dreams before breaking the silence.
