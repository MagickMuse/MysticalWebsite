Choosing a Witch Name

Why Take a Witch Name

A magical name supplements your mundane one and becomes an important part of your emerging witch identity — the name your fellow practitioners will come to know you by.

Method 1: Names of Legend

Many practitioners borrow directly from legendary sorcerers and witches — for example Merlin, Zyto, or Cyprian; or Morgana, Nimue, or Vivienne. Choose one that gives you a genuine thrill and feels appropriately potent and evocative.

Method 2: Mythological & Astrological Names

Look to gods and demigods from any pantheon — Greek, Roman, Norse, Celtic, or otherwise. Your astrological birth sign and ruling planet can point toward a fitting legendary figure whose story resonates with your own nature or craft.

Method 3: Numerology

Convert your everyday name to numbers (A–I = 1–9, J–R = 1–9, S–Z = 1–8), sum the digits, and reduce the total to a single digit. Match that digit to its ruling planet — 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Mars, 4 Mercury, 5 Jupiter, 6 Venus, 7 Saturn, 8 Uranus, 9 Neptune — then look to the myths surrounding that planet for name inspiration.

Method 4: Intuition

If none of the above appeals, let a name arise on its own through reverie or plain instinct. This is ultimately the most important test: the name should feel like a true reflection of who you are beneath the mundane self.

Keeping It Secret

Once chosen, guard your witch name closely — it becomes a key to your deep mind, spoken silently to "switch on" your focus before a working. Share it only with close, trusted practitioners, ideally within a coven, and inscribe it on your tools and jewelry using a witch alphabet such as the Theban Script.
