🧧Chinese New Year🌙MondayMorning

An ongoing kitchen shrine honoring Chinese kitchen deities with incense and offerings to prevent accidents and spread harmony through the home.

Quantities

  • 1 picture of Chinese kitchen god Tsao-Wang and wife Tsao-Wang Nai-Nai (or small jade or wooden Buddha)
  • 1 small kitchen shelf
  • 1 small offerings dish
  • 3 incense sticks
  • 1 small green fern or feathery plant
  • 1 red candle (for New Year renewal)
  • Tiny fruits, crystals, seeds, or flowers for offerings

Directions

  1. Light the incense sticks one from the other, saying for the first, "I ask the wise guardian(s) to keep safe this kitchen and my home from accidents and disasters." For the second incense say, "I ask the wise guardian(s) to bring blessings and harmony for all who live or enter here." For the third say, "I ask the wise guardian(s) that there will always be sufficient food, fuel, clothing, and money within this home." Fill the offerings dish with tiny fruits, crystals, seeds, or flowers.
  2. Light three incense sticks whenever you have time, repeating the words.
  3. On New Year's Eve and the beginning of the Chinese New Year, burn a red candle.
  4. Perform Monday morning and ongoing.
