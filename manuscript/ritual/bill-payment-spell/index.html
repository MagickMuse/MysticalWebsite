💰Bill Payment Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Writing SpellIncense Blend
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ManifestationSmoke MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Green paper or paper resembling currency - Pen with gold or silver ink - Black ribbon - Peppermint incense stick - Incense burner - Your bills, checkbook, and regular pen
◇Directions
1. Gather your bills, checkbook, and spell materials. 2. Cast a circle around your workspace. 3. Cut the green paper into a rectangle shaped like currency and write $1,000,000 on it. 4. Using the gold or silver ink pen, inscribe this affirmation: "I always have more than enough money to pay all my bills. My prosperity increases every day in every way." 5. Write your checks to cover each bill. 6. When finished, stack the payment receipts and place your green paper currency on top. 7. Bind everything together with the black ribbon. 8. Light the peppermint incense and hold your payment envelopes in the rising smoke for several moments. 9. Release any financial fears and visualize having everything you need exactly when you need it. 10. Open the circle. 11. Repeat this spell every time you pay bills to strengthen your prosperity consciousness.
◇Description
Transform bill-paying from a dreaded chore into a prosperity ritual. This spell shifts your mindset around finances by acknowledging that creditors extended you trust, and now you extend that same confidence to yourself. Repeat each time you pay bills to reinforce abundance thinking.
