🍉Yemaya's Watermelon Candle at Sea

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteOffering Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method

◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Santería/Lucumí
◇Quantities
1 watermelon 1 biodegradable or environmentally safe candle
◇Directions
1. Take a watermelon to the ocean. 2. Carve a well into its surface wide and deep enough to hold a candle securely. 3. Place the candle inside. 4. Speak your petition to Yemaya. 5. Light the candle. 6. Send the watermelon out to sea while the candle burns. 7. Use only a biodegradable or otherwise environmentally responsible candle; the offering goes into Yemaya's home, and she notices what you send her.
◇Description
A fertility petition to Yemaya combining a watermelon vessel with a lit candle at the ocean's edge. A well is carved into the watermelon to hold a candle. The petition is spoken and the candle lit before the watermelon is sent out to sea. The candle must be biodegradable or otherwise environmentally safe.

This working adds fire to the offering — the candle flame riding atop the watermelon as it goes out with the tide. The combination of water and fire within a single vessel is a signal: the petition is illuminated, lit and moving, not static.

The environmental caveat embedded in this working is itself a piece of the tradition. Yemaya is the sea. Sending plastic or synthetic materials into her element is not an offering but an insult. The practitioner who respects her domain demonstrates the right relationship before asking anything from it.

A beeswax candle, a taper made from natural materials, or any candle certified as biodegradable will serve. The material matters less than ensuring what you release can safely return to the earth.
