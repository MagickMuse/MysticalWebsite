🕯️Medieval Pierced Wax Doll Love Spell

The original version of this fifteenth-century spell to evoke a woman's love was written backwards in a blend of Hebrew and Yiddish.

Quantities

Virgin wax, for the female figure
Spring water

Directions

  1. Craft a female figure from virgin wax.
  2. Emphasize the female parts of the anatomy and try to make it resemble the object of your intentions.
  3. Carve her name over the doll's breast and onto her back, between the shoulders.
  4. Chant:
  5. May it be your will, Oh God
    that [Name] daughter of [Name]
    burns with passion for me and for me alone!
  6. Bury this figure in Earth for 24 hours, being careful not to damage it.
  7. Dig it up and hide it under the eaves of a house, covered with a stone for 24 hours.
  8. Disinter her yet again.
  9. Dip her in spring water three times, ritually invoking a different archangel each time: Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael.
  10. Finally, dip the doll into your urine.
  11. Dry her gently.
  12. The doll is complete.
  13. When you wish the woman she represents to feel the pangs of passion for you, pierce appropriately.
