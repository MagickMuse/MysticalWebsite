🕓Dreams of the Dead Nightmare Banishment

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Ancestor WorkChant/MantraPetition
◇Category

◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
• No materials required
◇Directions
1. Travel to the grave of the deceased. 2. Remove your shoes, lie down beside the grave, and speak the following refusal three times in a clear, strong voice: 3. By God's will and by my own will, I'm not going with you or with any other dead person. Don't come after me! Leave me alone! Don't come after anyone I love either, you, your friends, allies or messengers. I live in this world and not in any other. 4. Rise, put your shoes back on, and walk away without turning to look back.
◇Description
A waking ritual delivered at the grave of a deceased person who repeatedly visits the dreamer with an invitation to join them in death. The refusal is spoken aloud three times. If the actual grave cannot be reached, the working may be carried out entirely in visualization.
