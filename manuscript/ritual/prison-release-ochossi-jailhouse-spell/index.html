🔓Prison Release Ochossi Jailhouse Spell

A petition-based release working cast by someone on behalf of a person they wish to free from prison, not by the prisoner themselves. Calls upon the orisha Ochossi, Hunter and lord of the woods, to hunt down and secure the target's freedom. Uses prison dirt from four facilities arranged at the corners of a cloth, forest dirt in the center, an arrow or crossbow image, a white candle for the initial petition, and then seven days of red candle burning over a photo of the target. Remnants are disposed of as close to the target's prison as possible. ⚠️ Dirt, cloth, plate, and arrow/crossbow image not confirmed as tagged grimoire pages.

Quantities

  • A handful of dirt from a forest
  • Handfuls of dirt from four different prisons (kept separate until step 6)
  • A cloth, spread on an altar
  • An image of an arrow or crossbow
  • 1 small white candle or white cross candle
  • A bowl (for blending dirt)
  • A photo of the spell's target
  • 1 white or terracotta plate
  • 1 red seven-day candle OR a single red candle (repeated for 7 days)

Directions

  1. Collect a handful of dirt from a forest and separate handfuls of dirt from four different prisons, keeping each source distinct.
  2. Spread a cloth on an altar.
  3. Place each handful of prison dirt at one corner of the cloth to form a square.
  4. Place the forest dirt in the center.
  5. Set an image of an arrow or crossbow on top of the forest dirt.
  6. Light a small white candle or white cross candle beside it.
  7. Pray and petition Ochossi for the release of the person.
  8. Leave it overnight.
  9. In the morning, blend all the dirt together in a bowl, leaving it on the cloth.
  10. Place a photo of the target face-up on a white or terracotta plate.
  11. Sprinkle the blended dirt over the photo.
  12. Light either a red seven-day candle on top of the dirt, or a single red candle to be burned each day for seven days.
  13. Sprinkle a small amount of dirt on the candle daily.
  14. On the eighth day, or when the candle has burned completely, wrap all remnants, including all the dirt, inside a cloth.
  15. Dispose of the bundle as close to the prison where the target is incarcerated as possible.
