💖White Flower and Moon Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
LunarWater MagickChant/MantraKnot Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Nine white flowers - One bowl - Water - One crystal or stick for stirring - One pink ribbon
◇Directions
1. Begin on the night of the waxing crescent moon. 2. Fill your bowl with water and float the nine white flowers on its surface. 3. Take your crystal or stick and gently stir the water in a clockwise direction while focusing your thoughts on the love you wish to attract. 4. Speak your intentions aloud or in your mind as you stir. 5. Continue this practice each night as the moon grows fuller. 6. Each evening, stir the water clockwise and renew your romantic intentions. 7. On the ninth night, when the moon is nearly full, remove the flowers from the water. 8. Bind them together with the pink ribbon, tying nine knots as you do so. 9. With each knot, state your intention for the love you seek. 10. Keep the bound flowers in a special place as a charm to draw your desired love to you.
◇Description
A nine-night moon ritual using white flowers floated in water to attract romantic love. Performed during the waxing moon, each night focuses intention through stirring and binding with pink ribbon.
