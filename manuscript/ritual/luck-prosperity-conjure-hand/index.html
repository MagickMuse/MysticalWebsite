A mojo hand for drawing luck, seizing opportunities, and claiming personal power, combining lucky hand root, High John the Conqueror root, and cinnamon bark, and fed regularly with condition oil to keep it active. ⚠️ Luck/Prosperity Conjure Hand is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

1 lucky hand root
1 High John the Conqueror root
Cinnamon bark
Red flannel bag or leather pouch
Condition oil for feeding, such as New Orleans Style Fast Luck Oil (cinnamon oil, vanilla oil, and wintergreen)

Directions

  1. Select a red flannel bag or small leather pouch to serve as the hand.
  2. Red is traditional for drawing power and luck.
  3. Gather your three main ingredients: one lucky hand root (for grabbing opportunities as they pass), one High John the Conqueror root (for cleverness, personal power, and overcoming obstacles), and cinnamon bark (for drawing money and prosperity).
  4. As you place each ingredient into the bag, speak its purpose aloud.
  5. For the lucky hand root: "I seize every opportunity that comes my way." For the High John root: "I claim my power and overcome all obstacles." For the cinnamon: "Money flows to me easily and abundantly."
  6. Tie or sew the bag closed, creating a permanent seal for your conjure hand.
  7. Feeding the Hand:
  8. Mojo hands must be fed regularly to keep them active and working on your behalf.
  9. Feed your Luck/Prosperity Hand at least once per week, or more often if you're actively working toward a specific goal.
  10. To feed the hand, apply a few drops of condition oil to the outside of the bag.
  11. New Orleans Style Fast Luck Oil (made from cinnamon essential oil, vanilla oil or extract, and wintergreen) is traditional for this hand, but you can use any prosperity or luck oil that resonates with you.
  12. As you anoint the bag, hold it in your hands and speak to it.
  13. Tell it what you need.
  14. Be specific about opportunities you want to attract, money you need to draw, or obstacles you need to overcome.
  15. The hand works best when it knows exactly what you want.
  16. Carrying the Hand:
  17. Keep your conjure hand with you, in your pocket, purse, or bag.
  18. The more you carry it and the more you handle it, the stronger your connection to it becomes.
  19. Some practitioners sleep with their mojo hands under their pillow to strengthen the bond through dreams.
  20. Others keep them on their altar between uses.
  21. Never let anyone else touch your conjure hand.
  22. This is your personal power object, and another person's energy can interfere with its work.
  23. Renewing the Hand:
  24. If your hand stops working or if you feel its power waning despite regular feeding, you may need to refresh it.
  25. Open the bag, add fresh cinnamon, and speak your intentions over it again.
  26. Some practitioners add a small amount of whiskey or Florida Water to "wake up" a sluggish hand.
  27. If the hand has accomplished its purpose or you no longer need it, thank it for its service and bury it at a crossroads or dispose of it respectfully in running water.
