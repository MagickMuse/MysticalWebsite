💖Fortunetelling with Eggs and Bannocks

◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Method
Divination
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
- Hard-boiled eggs (as many as goals/wishes/questions) - Bannocks or biscuits (as many as goals/wishes/questions) - Pen or marker to write on eggs and draw symbols - Grassy hill Traditional: Bannocks are thick, round oatmeal cakes with one side marked with cross Modern variation: Use biscuits with solar symbol drawn on top or bottom
◇Directions
1. Boil eggs. 2. If you don't want to search for traditional bannock recipe, simply bake batch of biscuits. 3. On each egg, write a goal, wish, or question for which you would like to know the prospects. 4. On each biscuit, draw a solar symbol on either top or bottom surface. 5. Take biscuits and eggs to grassy hill. 6. Roll them down, thinking of the goal, wish, or question with each one as you release it down hill. 7. Interpretation: 8. If eggshell cracks or biscuit lands solar-symbol up: Answer is no, unlikely, or not at this time. 9. If egg travels downhill unscathed or biscuit lands solar-symbol downward: Answer is yes, sign of good fortune, affirmation that goals and wishes will likely come true. 10. Traditional interpretation: Intact egg and bannock landing blank-side up = good fortune. 11. Cracked egg or bannock landing cross-side up = poor luck expected. 12. Try with friends for maximum fun!
◇Description
Traditional Beltane fortunetelling method from Scotland and England. Roll hard-boiled eggs and bannocks (or biscuits) down hills to divine fate. Intact eggs and blank-side-up bannocks signify good fortune; cracked eggs or cross-side-up bannocks indicate poor luck. Modern variation for divining prospects of goals, wishes, questions.
