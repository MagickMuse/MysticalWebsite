A stone-charged elixir technique that steeps one of Brigid's allied stones in water or whiskey to create a potent potion.

Quantities

One or more stones (garnet, citrine, amber, moss agate, or rainbow obsidian)
Pure spring or well water, whiskey, or a mix of both
Glass or pottery vessel with a seal

Directions

  1. Choose a stone allied with Brigid, such as garnet for healing, citrine for courage, amber for self-esteem, moss agate for peace, or rainbow obsidian for transformation.
  2. Place the stone and your chosen liquid together in a glass or pottery vessel and seal it.
  3. Let it sit for a period that feels right, a moon cycle, from holy day to holy day, or over a nineteen-day flame vigil.
  4. Even twenty-four hours will make a potent potion, especially if empowered by sunlight, moonlight, and prayer.
