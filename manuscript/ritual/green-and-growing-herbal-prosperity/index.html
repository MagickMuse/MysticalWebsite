A noon prosperity working that harnesses Midsummer abundance with a green candle and fresh herbs to invite growth and flow of resources.

Quantities

  • Three or four fresh herbs or green plants (e.g., thyme, parsley, basil)
  • Green candle and a fire‑safe surface
  • Small bowls of salt and water
  • Sage or incense
  • Athame or small pointed tool to carve symbols (a toothpick works)
  • Empty bowl or cauldron

Directions

  1. At noon if possible, outdoors in the bright sun, carve Fehu and any Midsummer symbols you like into the candle.
  2. Set the candle safely and arrange herbs around it.
  3. Light the candle and say:
    I call down the power of the Midsummer sun to energize
    my magickal work with the element of fire.
  4. Sprinkle herbs with a little salt and say:
    I call upon the element of earth to energize my magickal work
    and bless these plants that sprang from its fertile soil.
  5. Sprinkle herbs with a little water and say:
    I call upon the element of water to energize my magickal work
    as the rains empower the growing things in the fields and meadows.
  6. Light sage or incense and waft over the herbs, saying:
    As the winds blow the pollen and fertilize the world,
    let them blow prosperity and abundance to me.
  7. Hold some of each herb, feel their living energy, crush slightly to release aroma, and say:
    Herbs and plants, sun and light,
    empowered by Midsummer’s glow.
    Green to green, gold so bright,
    let prosperity flow.
    So mote it be.
  8. Place the herbs into the empty bowl.
  9. Leave on the altar or in sunlight, or cook with them if edible.
  10. All-day sequence: After Greet the Dawn at sunrise and Healing Water Magick midmorning or midafternoon, perform this at noon, then close the day at dusk with Fire Power Candle Magick.
