⚠️Warning Hex Gris-Gris Bag

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Powder MagickBinding
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Salt Black pepper Gunpowder Dried, pulverized manure Bag or cloth
◇Directions
1. Combine salt, pepper, gunpowder, and dried, pulverized manure in a bag. 2. Place the gris-gris on the target's doorstep, within their home, or in their clothing, somewhere they are guaranteed to discover it. 3. The discovery itself is part of the spell's power, signaling to the wrongdoer that the victims are not helpless or passive and that continued harmful behavior may bring further consequences.
◇Description
A hex that functions simultaneously as a warning and as an act of power where other protest is denied. Rooted in the tradition of enslaved people in the American South, who placed gris-gris on the doorsteps or in the clothing of abusive slaveholders. The hex communicates that a boundary of intolerable behavior has been crossed and may persuade the wrongdoer to amend their behavior. The traditional Southern slave's warning hex-bag contains salt, pepper, gunpowder, and dried pulverized manure.
