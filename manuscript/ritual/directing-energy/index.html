⚡Energy❤️‍🩹Healing✨Manifestation🧘Meditation🧘🏻‍♀️Spirituality

A visualization practice to shape and direct personal energy through your palms for manifestation and magickal work. Once mastered, this technique allows you to charge candles, create thoughtforms, and send intention into the universe.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Begin by grounding and centering.
  2. Connect with the earth and bring energy into your center.
  3. Once you feel that connection established, send the energy outward from your center to your palms.
  4. Your palms will begin to tingle, similar to the sensation of goosebumps on your arms.
  5. Hold your hands facing each other, several inches apart.
  6. You can shape the energy between your palms into a ball through your will alone.
  7. Visualize it taking form.
  8. You can choose the size of the ball as well as the shape, changing these through intention.
  9. When you can easily create a ball of energy between your palms, practice changing it.
  10. Try picturing your favorite fruit.
  11. The image should appear clearly on the edge of your mind along with its scent, color, and size.
  12. This trains your visualization skills.
  13. The energy ball can be various colors to accomplish different goals.
  14. Visualize the color that matches your intention.
  15. This energy can be sent out into the universe, directed back into yourself for healing or empowerment, or pushed into a candle or object to help manifest your desires.
  16. Once you gain practice moving energy in this way, there is little you cannot do with it.
  17. The key is consistent practice until the sensation becomes familiar and controllable.
    ## Methods for Raising Energy
  18. There are many ways to raise energy before directing it.
  19. Picture yourself at a concert or sporting event where the stands are packed and the crowd is one living organism, everyone stomping their feet and clapping their hands.
  20. That stadium energy is what gets spells going.
  21. Common methods include singing, dancing, clapping, drumming, laughing, chanting, and breathwork.
  22. The most important part is what to do with the energy once it has been raised.
  23. In a coven setting, the entire group raises the energy, and the high priestess directs the symphony of all the coven members and sends the energy where it needs to go.
  24. When you are a solitary practitioner, all of that falls to you.
