🧿Bride's Anti-Evil Eye Coin Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Protection
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
1 coin
◇Directions
1. Before the ceremony begins, the bride places a coin beneath her left armpit and holds it there throughout the entire wedding rite. 2. Upon leaving the ceremony, she discreetly allows the coin to fall to the ground, releasing it as payment to the spirits who will guard against the Evil Eye, or as a bribe to those who would otherwise cast it. 3. Whoever finds and picks up the coin unknowingly receives a year's good fortune.
◇Description
A discreet wedding day protective ritual in which the bride carries a coin against her body and releases it as a spirit payment upon leaving the ceremony, warding off Evil Eye attracted by the celebration.
