Beltane

To attract a partner for love and romance. This spell joins two candles representing yourself and your desired lover into one, manifesting union.

Quantities

  • 2 candles
  • 2 candleholders
  • 1 toothpick or needle (to scratch wax)
  • 1 fireproof dish
  • Your own saliva
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Find a special, quiet place.
  2. Clear your head, letting worries and mundane thoughts slip away.
  3. Think of yourself as a magickal being beyond ego and identity.
  4. Clear the space by walking in a circle, using your palm or wand to mentally push negative or stale energy out.
  5. The space should feel clear and empty.
  6. Fill the space with powerful energies.
  7. Call on the elements (air, fire, water, earth), inviting them into the sacred space.
  8. Invite powers of Goddess and God.
  9. You may call on Beltane-associated entities such as Beal or the Horned God, or the Goddess in her Maiden aspect.
  10. Feel the power of each energetic being as it enters.
  11. Remember: the mind designs the plan, emotions enact it.
  12. Hold one candle and think about the kind of lover you want.
  13. Imagine the feeling when this lover holds you, touches you, tells you how loved and attractive you are.
  14. Focus on that feeling rather than need or desire.
  15. Say with determination: "This is not a candle, but my new lover that I hold." Light the candle and place it in a candleholder.
  16. Hold the other candle and think about all your wonderful characteristics you offer your romantic mate.
  17. Conjure feeling of love and passion.
  18. Imagine expressing love and admiration for your new lover to come, pour this energy into the candle.
  19. Use toothpick to scratch your full name into the wax.
  20. Kiss the candle and rub a bit of your saliva down its length.
  21. Say with passion: "This is not a candle, but myself as a wonderful lover."
  22. Light the second candle.
  23. Hold one in each hand, one representing yourself, one representing your new lover.
  24. Use flames to slightly melt wax down one side of each candle, then press them together to form single candle with two wicks.
  25. Melt bottom slightly with lighter so it sticks, place in fireproof dish.
  26. Watch flames burn.
  27. Imagine being with your new lover, send feelings of love and passion into the double candle as it melts.
  28. You can watch it burn completely down, or put it out and relight the next night (possibly repeating 2-3 nights), each time pouring love and passion into it.
  29. Once completely melted, take leftover wax lump and place it in the sun on top of a large rock, preferably near body of water.
  30. You should find your next lover within three lunar cycles.
  31. While spell is in effect, have faith but don't dwell on it or doubt it, this inhibits magick.
  32. Pay attention to people you interact with.
  33. Look for new faces and personalities inexplicably drawn to you or in tune with you.
  34. Keep mind, heart, and eyes open.
  35. Look and act your best.
  36. If not satisfied after a few months, repeat or try different spell from another angle.
