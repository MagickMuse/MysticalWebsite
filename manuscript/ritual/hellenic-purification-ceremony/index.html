🕯️Hellenic Purification Ceremony

◇Tradition
Greek
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Altar WorkingOffering Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
CleansingInvocationPrayerDevotional WorkIncense MagickSmoke MagickSalt MagickWater MagickPetitionChant/Mantra
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- A live flame kept on the hearth or a vigil candle - A bowl of clean water for the Khernips - A bronze implement that can be struck for sound - A small piece of copper - Sulphur - Salt - Frankincense incense, charcoal disc, and a burner or tripod - Red wine for libation - A fresh sprig of rosemary
◇Directions
1. Bathe beforehand and observe a light cleansing diet so the body arrives at the rite already prepared. 2. Settle the altar with the Khernips, the salt, the sulphur, the copper, the bronze implement, the wine vessel, and the offering dishes. 3. Light the hearth fire so the flame can hold steady through the entire ceremony, and ready the burner with charcoal for the frankincense. 4. Stand at the altar in stillness for a moment, then speak the opening: 5. I call upon the bronze-sounding Kouretes, the guardian Koryvanta, and the purifying Apollon, to stand by me, in this ritual of cleansing. 6. Kindle the frankincense. 7. While striking the bronze implement to produce a clear ringing tone, raise the hymn to the Kouretes: 8. Bronze-sounding Kouretes, heavenly, and earthly, and of the waters, much blessed, life giving spirits, glorious saviours of the world, You who remove the dangers from peoples' lives, immortal Kouretes, possessors of exalted arms, when coming to Earth thundering you fill the wild beasts with fear! Immortal Gods, who nurture the mortal species. O' Kouretes, noble and powerful, youths of Zeus, eternal spirits, soul nurturers, gracious saviours. I call upon you to come with good intentions, shield bearing guardians of [name the place], bring harmony, perfect order, and peace. Heracles, Paionai, Epimedes, Iasios, and Ida! 9. Renew the frankincense as an offering on the burner, then deliver the hymn to the Koryvantes: 10. I call on the great eternal Khthonian king Koryvant', the blessed; Arean and terrifying youth of the night, who dispels evil fears, helper of the imagination, wandering Koryvanta, multi-formed king, dual natured god, many-formed, bloodstained, blood-drenched brother, You who, with the consent of Zeous, changed your pure body, assuming beast-like and serpentine form: Come, Blessed, hear this call and dispel all grievances, chase away unhealthy imaginings and the undesirable needs of the soul. 11. Pour a libation of red wine in honour of the Koryvantes. 12. Hold a moment of inward stillness, then speak the hymn to Apollon: 13. I think and sing of Apollon Hecatios. From years past, it is a sacred custom to call upon you. Evil-averting Paian, far-seeing ruler of the World. Great Archer, who established purifications, with yourself as example; after the slaughter of Python, you fled to Karmanora, where you were of service to yourself and atoned. Shoot, with your invincible arrows, the Pythons and the Typhoës, O' Lykêos, with the broad quiver. Infallible giver of oracles, who reigns over the immortal Gods and the mortals of life-giving Earth. I come to you in purity. Grant me fortitude and enlighten my mind so that the dark êrebus which inflicts my soul may be dissolved. Assist me, O' golden-lyred, far-shooting Apollon, that I may resist the onslaught of deceitful and deceptive spectres, release me from infectious ignorance and darkness. Ie Paiean, Ie Paiean, Ie Paiean, Ie Katharsios. 14. Close the rite by repeating the banishing formula seven times in a rising voice: 15. Êkas, Êkas, ostis alitros. Be gone, be gone all that is wicked. 16. Prepare the holy water in the ancient manner by drawing fire from the hearth into the bowl of water until the flame is extinguished within it. 17. On the seventh repetition of the closing formula, pass the sprig of rosemary through the flames of Hestia, dip it into the holy water, and sprinkle the area and every participant. 18. After the ceremony, gather every by-product of the rite, the spent incense and ash, any wine that was caught after libation, and the used rosemary, and place them in clean ground so they may return to the earth. 19. See Khernips (Greek Holy Water) for fuller guidance on preparing the cleansing water.
◇Description
Hellenic household rite that cleanses body, soul, and ritual space before approach to the Gods. Performed on its own whenever miasma is felt, or as the opening movement of any larger household ceremony. The act should leave the practitioner with the calm of catharsis and a state fit for sacred contact.
