🔥The Moorish Charm (Spanish Return Spell)

A Spanish spell for calling back a lost love. Caraway, coriander, cumin, mastic, white lime, verdigris, myrrh resin, and dragon's blood are burned one by one in a fire at a crossroads or cemetery, with a specific incantation chanted over each ingredient. Broom straw is burned last: 'Oh magick broom, fly him to me!'

Quantities

Caraway seeds
Coriander seeds
Cumin seeds
Mastic resin
White lime (burned limestone; substitute: oyster shell calcium tablets)
Verdigris (green patina from brass or copper; obtainable from an art supply store or pharmacist)
Myrrh resin
Dragon's blood
Broom straw

Directions

  1. Gather all materials and take them to a crossroads or cemetery.
  2. Make a fire.
  3. Toss in the ingredients one by one, caraway, coriander, cumin, mastic, white lime, verdigris, myrrh resin, and dragon's blood, chanting over each in turn: 'Oh magick caraway: bring him to me!' / 'Oh magick coriander: blind him with love for me!' Leave broom straw for last, chanting: 'Oh magick broom, fly him to me!'
