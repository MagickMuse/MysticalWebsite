🐟Medieval Man's Fish Donkey Milk Love-Binding Spell

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary

◇Quantities
1 whole fish Donkey's milk (enough to cook the fish in)
◇Directions
1. Cook a whole fish in donkey's milk. 2. Strain the liquid, reserving it, this is what the spell requires. 3. Discard or eat the fish as desired. 4. Let the liquid cool. 5. While the woman is sleeping, gently bathe her vaginal area with some of the cooled cooking liquid.
◇Description
A medieval fish spell for men. A whole fish cooked in donkey's milk; the strained cooking liquid is applied to a sleeping woman's body to bind her to the relationship. ⚠️ Non-consensual.
