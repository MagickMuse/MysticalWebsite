Ritual Grimoire

🌹Loving Being Here

◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Method
Ancestor WorkMeditationEmpowerment
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
No required materials. Optional: candle, herbs, bath ingredients, whatever calls to you in the moment.
◇Directions
1. There is no such thing as a self-love spell that comes in a bottle, a crystal, or a candle. 2. Dozens of spiritual products sell the promise of self-love. 3. None of them are necessary. 4. And hearing someone else tell you to love yourself, or seeking an external solution for how to do it, tends to make the task feel harder than it really is. 5. You are the product of generations of love. 6. Your ancestors did not necessarily love each other when they conceived their children. 7. But they loved being here in this world enough to stick around, to have sex, to give birth, to nurse babies, and to raise children. 8. They decided it was worth bringing someone new into the world. 9. Or, more accurately, to bring someone old back. 10. Fall in love with the world around you, and you will fall in love with yourself too. 11. Love being here, and everything else will fall into place. 12. Nothing and no one is forcing you to be here. 13. You choose it every day. 14. > "Love doesn't just sit there, like a stone, it has to be made, like bread; remade all the time, made new." (Ursula K. Le Guin) 15. Do whatever you would love to do right now. 16. Light a candle or burn some sage. 17. Take a bath in fresh rose petals or a strong tea of lemon balm, or whatever other herbs feel right. 18. Don't worry about whether they are associated with any particular magickal property unless you love that sort of thing. 19. The spell is simply to feel what you would love to do right now in your body, and to do that.
◇Description
A no-materials spell rooted in the truth that self-love cannot be purchased or prescribed. You are the product of generations of ancestors who loved being alive enough to stay, to build, and to bring new life into the world. The spell is simply to feel what you would love to do right now in your body, and to do it.
