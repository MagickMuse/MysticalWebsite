👤Marie Laveaux Banishing Doll Ritual

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Poppet
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Water MagickVisualization
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
New Orleans Voodoo
◇Quantities
1 bottle Four Thieves vinegar 1 piece of parchment paper 1 small doll (made from black cloth or dressed in black)
◇Directions
1. On a piece of parchment paper, write the name of the person you want to leave. 2. Attach the parchment to a small doll made from black cloth or a store-bought doll dressed in black. 3. Stuff the doll into a bottle. 4. Fill the bottle with Four Thieves vinegar and seal it tightly. 5. Throw the bottle into any moving body of water. 6. As the bottle is carried away by the current, so shall this person be removed from your location.
◇Description
⚠️ New Orleans Voodoo is a closed practice sacred to the African diasporic spiritual traditions of Louisiana and should only be practiced by those within the originating culture. A banishing spell attributed to Marie Laveaux, the Voodoo Queen of New Orleans. The target's name is written on parchment, attached to a doll made from or dressed in black cloth, stuffed into a bottle filled with Four Thieves vinegar, sealed, and thrown into a moving body of water. As the bottle is carried away by the current, so shall the target be removed from the practitioner's location.
