A dressed candle ritual combining cinnamon, cinquefoil, and Irish moss to attract luck, happiness, and a prosperous life.

Quantities

Bright gold or orange candle
Ground cinnamon
Powdered cinquefoil (five-finger grass)
Powdered Irish moss

Directions

  1. Spread ground cinnamon, powdered cinquefoil, and powdered Irish moss across a flat surface.
  2. Roll the gold or orange candle through the blend, pressing gently so the botanicals adhere to the wax.
  3. Burn the dressed candle to draw luck and a happy, prosperous life.
