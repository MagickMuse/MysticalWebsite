A justice spell using pine oil on candles, with different variations for dismissing lawsuits versus seeking court justice.

Quantities

For dismissal: 1 blue candle, pine essential oil (diluted), carrier oil, pen or carving tool

For court justice: 1 yellow candle, 1 purple candle, patchouli oil, pen or carving tool

Directions

  1. Pine works for justice because it seeks balance and restores calm.
  2. Unjust situations get upsetting really quickly.
  3. To dismiss a lawsuit before it can get to court: Anoint a blue candle with "justice" written on it.
  4. Dress with diluted pine oil.
  5. Burn it on a Saturday (endings).
  6. If the matter needs to go to court to get justice: Anoint a yellow candle (thought) with patchouli oil for a Just Judge, and a purple candle (divine justice).
  7. Burn them together on a Sunday.
  8. Focus on fair and balanced resolution as the candles burn.
