A mojo bag assembled for wish fulfillment. A written wish, shed snakeskin, Rattlesnake root, Low John the Conqueror root, and five Job's tears are placed together in a red drawstring bag, then dressed with Lucky Lodestone Oil, Van Van Oil, and/or Magnet Oil.

⚠️ Wishing Bag is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Red drawstring bag
Paper and pen (to write the wish)
1 piece of shed snakeskin
1 Rattlesnake root
1 Low John the Conqueror root
5 Job's tears
Lucky Lodestone Oil, Van Van Oil, and/or Magnet Oil

Directions

  1. Write out your wish explicitly on paper.
  2. Place it in a red drawstring bag together with a piece of shed snakeskin, a Rattlesnake root, Low John the Conqueror, and five Job's tears.
  3. Dress it with Lucky Lodestone Oil, Van Van Oil, and/or Magnet Oil.

About This Charm

A Hoodoo mojo bag assembled for wish fulfillment, combining botanical roots, shed snakeskin, and condition oils.

⚠️ Closed Practice Notice: This working is rooted in Hoodoo, a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.
