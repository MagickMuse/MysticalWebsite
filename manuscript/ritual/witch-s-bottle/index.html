A protective jar spell filled with sharp objects and personal fluids, designed to absorb and neutralize any negative or malevolent energy directed at you.

Quantities

Small glass jar with screw-on lid
Assorted sharp objects (broken glass, razor blades, rusty nails, screws, pins, needles)
Personal taglocks
Urine
Tape or wax for sealing
Optional: menstrual blood or semen

Directions

  1. Fill jar halfway with sharp objects.
  2. Place taglocks on top.
  3. Optionally add menstrual blood or semen.
  4. Fill remainder with urine.
  5. Screw lid on tightly.
  6. Seal with tape or wax.
  7. Bury at least 1 foot deep near front door, or hide in dark closet or pantry.

Items

  • Small glass jar with a tight-fitting, screw-on lid
  • An assortment of sharp objects: broken glass, razor blades, rusty nails and screws, pins and needles, or similar
  • Personal taglocks
  • Your urine
  • Menstrual blood or semen (optional)

Instructions

Fill the jar at least halfway with the sharp objects. Place your taglocks on top. If you choose, add the blood or semen. Finish filling the jar with your urine. Screw the lid on tightly, then seal it well with tape or wax.

Placement

Traditionally, the jar should be buried at least a foot deep on your property, as close to the front door as possible. If burial is not an option due to your living situation, place the jar somewhere out of sight: the back of a dark closet, a pantry, or any undisturbed location. Leave it alone and let it do its work.
