🌱Seed Moon Blessing

A simple lunar blessing for seeds, literal or metaphorical, performed during the growing light of late winter and early spring. Seeds are cradled in a half eggshell and charged with candlelight and spoken intention, infusing them with the energy of the spring moons ahead before being planted and grounded in the earth.

Quantities

  • Seeds you intend to plant, or Seed Planting Paper
  • 1 half eggshell, cleaned and dried
  • Candle
  • Matches or lighter

Directions

  1. Begin by placing your seeds or seed paper inside a clean, dry half eggshell.
  2. The eggshell is not merely a vessel, it is a symbol of rebirth and protection in its own right, and its mineral content will eventually nourish the soil once planted.
  3. Light the candle and set it nearby.
  4. Let yourself notice its glow, feel its warmth.
  5. This is the growing light of spring, the same light that has been slowly strengthening since midwinter, coaxing the world back toward life.
  6. You may use a Candlemas candle if you made one, continuing that working into this one.
  7. Cup your hands above the seeds in the eggshell.
  8. Close your eyes and visualize a soft stirring of energy within them, a warmth, a readiness, a potential beginning to quicken.
  9. Let the candle's light feed that image.
  10. When you feel the energy building, speak aloud:
  11. "Warmth of the growing light of spring,
    Blessings to these seedlings bring,
    That as each grow on their own time,
    Abundance and plenty bless their vines."
  12. Hold the intention until it feels complete.
  13. Then step back and allow the candle to burn all the way down on its own, do not extinguish it.
  14. The spell is sealed when the flame goes out naturally.
  15. Once the candle has finished, take your seeds outside.
  16. Plant them in soil, then grind the eggshell and sprinkle it over the planting site as a final layer of blessing and protection.
  17. Tend what you have planted with the same care you would give to your intentions: the right timing, the right conditions, and patience for each seed to grow in its own season.
