Coconut

Homemade coconut candles release genuine coconut energy into the air for long-term spiritual purification in a relaxed, fragrant manner. Commercial coconut candles are almost always synthetic and hold no power. White or brown are the preferred colors. A hollowed coconut shell makes an ideal container. Fragrance can be supplemented with ginger, frankincense, or lemon essential oils.

Quantities

  • Pour-and-melt wax (white or brown preferred)
  • Pure coconut extract
  • Container (coconut shell half ideal)
  • Wick
  • Optional: ginger, frankincense, or lemon essential oil

Directions

  1. Prepare the candle container before melting the wax; a hollowed half-coconut shell is ideal but any container works.
  2. Melt the wax until liquid.
  3. Stir in pure coconut extract to your desired fragrance intensity.
  4. Optionally add a few drops of ginger, frankincense, or lemon essential oil.
  5. Secure the wick to the container (hold in place or drape over a pencil/stick).
  6. Pour the scented wax into the container.
  7. Allow to harden fully before use.
