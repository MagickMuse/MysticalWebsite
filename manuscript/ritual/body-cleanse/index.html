A week-long gentle detox practice inspired by Oyster's filtration abilities. By supporting your body's natural cleansing systems, bowel, kidneys, bladder, lymphatic system, blood vessels, and lungs, you emulate Oyster's continuous filtering and transforming.

Quantities

  • Flax, chia, or psyllium seed
  • Lemons or limes
  • Dry brush

Directions

  1. Call in Oyster energy by supporting your body's multi-tiered filtration system with this lightweight, full body cleanse.
  2. Try this for one week:
  3. Morning: Drink 8 ounces of water mixed with one teaspoon of flax, chia, or psyllium seed to support the bowel and aid in clearing toxins from the colon.
  4. Throughout the Day: Drink at least 64 ounces of water with a squeeze of lemon or lime.
  5. Water helps the kidneys and bladder excrete urine, which removes waste from the blood.
  6. Citric acid from the lemon or lime can give your digestive system a boost.
  7. Before Shower: Dry brush your skin to help stimulate the lymphatic system.
  8. During your shower, use hot water to bring on a sweat, then switch to cold water, alternating back and forth twice.
  9. This hot-cold action is thought to tone the blood vessels so they can continue the work of carrying nutrients and moving toxins out.
  10. Evening: For 5 minutes, practice deep and conscious breathing to support healthy lung function.
  11. If you occasionally do a big detox, see how it feels to make like Oyster and do a light detox on a more regular basis.
