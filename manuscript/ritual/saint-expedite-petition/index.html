🕯️Saint Expedite Petition

◇Quantities
1 red, white, or yellow altar cloth 1 image or statue of St. Expedite 1 red, white, or yellow candle (or 7-day St. Expedite candle) Parchment paper or plain white paper St. Expedite Oil St. Expedite Incense 1 glass of water 1 pound cake (for offering) 3 coins (for offering) 1 red, white, or yellow candle (for offering) 1 large plate (for offering)
◇Directions
1. Dress your altar with a red, white, or yellow cloth. 2. Place an image or statue of St. Expedite upon the altar along with a glass of water. 3. On parchment or plain white paper, write out your very specific request to St. Expedite in the form of a letter. 4. Anoint a red, white, or yellow candle with St. Expedite Oil. 5. If using a seven-day candle, poke a hole in the top with a knife or pointed object and pour several drops of the oil into it. 6. Burn St. Expedite Incense on the altar as you recite your prayer and petition aloud: 7. O St. Expedite, I conjure your presence and power with complete trust and confidence that you will do your duty for me in my time of need. 8. [State your petition.] If you grant my request, I promise that I will make an offering to you in the form of [describe your offering]. 9. O St. Expedite, whose speed is unsurpassed, grant my petition fast, fast, fast! 10. Anoint the edges of the petition paper with a few drops of St. Expedite Oil. 11. Fold the paper toward you several times and place it beneath the image of St. Expedite. 12. Light the candle and allow it to burn down completely. 13. Do not make your final offering until St. Expedite has completed the task, as premature payment signals fulfillment. 14. Once the request is granted, place a pound cake on a large plate and arrange three coins in a triangle upon it. 15. Set a red, white, or yellow candle in the center of the cake, light it, and let it burn down. 16. You must then tell at least one other person of St. Expedite's power. 17. He also accepts offerings of candy, flowers, liquor, and cinnamon. 18. After the working is complete, your bargain fulfilled, take the refuse and the pound cake to a crossroads.
◇Description
⚠️ New Orleans Voodoo is a closed practice sacred to the African diasporic spiritual traditions of Louisiana and should only be practiced by those within the originating culture. A formal petition ritual to St. Expedite (St. Expeditus), an unofficial Catholic saint widely venerated in New Orleans Voodoo and folk Catholic tradition. Believed to have been a Roman centurion martyred in 303 A.D. in Melitene, Turkey, St. Expedite is invoked to speed the action of any working and may be petitioned for virtually any purpose, including the destruction of enemies. He does not judge the nature of a request, but demands extreme specificity in wording, as he is easily confused. All promises of payment must be honored swiftly after the task is granted, or he will withdraw the favor with severe consequences. His preferred day is Thursday, though Tuesday and Wednesday are also effective. Offerings are made in multiples of three. His colors are red, yellow, and white.
