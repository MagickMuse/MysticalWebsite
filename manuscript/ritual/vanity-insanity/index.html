⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A devastating curse for the vain. Using the target's hair, nail clippings, and a photograph marked with the words "Beauty and Charm," the practitioner burns everything in a black cauldron while reciting an incantation that strips the target of their physical beauty, shatters their ego, and drives them to insanity. The curse invokes the powers of Lucifer, Beelzebub, and Astarot. The resulting ashes are later sprinkled around or inside the target's home to deliver the working.

Quantities

Hair from the target
Nail clippings from the target
1 photograph of the target
1 black marker
1 black cauldron
1 small bag

Directions

  1. Obtain hair and nail clippings from the person you wish to punish, along with a photograph of them.
  2. With a black marker, write the words "Beauty and Charm" on the photograph.
  3. Place all items in a black cauldron and set them alight.
  4. As the fire burns, recite the incantation, inserting the target's name where indicated.
  5. At the conclusion, call upon Lucifer, Beelzebub, and Astarot to carry out the working.
  6. Once the fire has burned out, collect the ashes and place them in a small bag.
  7. When the opportunity arises, sprinkle the ashes around or inside the target's home.
