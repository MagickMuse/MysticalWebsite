Lime

A focus pouch combining dried lime peel, lime oil, and yellow calcite to enhance mental clarity and concentration.

Quantities

  • Small pouch (yellow fabric preferred)
  • Dried lime peel
  • Lime essential oil
  • Yellow calcite chip or tumble

Directions

  1. Assemble this pouch on a Sunday to draw on solar energy for mental clarity.
  2. Place dried lime peel in a small pouch.
  3. Add a chip or small tumble of yellow calcite, which stimulates the higher mind and enhances focus.
  4. Anoint the pouch with a drop of lime essential oil.
  5. As you assemble each component, speak your intention for clear thinking and enhanced concentration.
  6. Carry the pouch when you need mental sharpness, or keep it on your desk during work or study.
✏️
Adapting this spell? Yellow calcite can be swapped for citrine or clear quartz (same solar/mental clarity correspondence). Any citrus peel works in place of lime. If you can't source yellow fabric, any natural-fiber pouch charged with focus intention will do — the material amplifies, but intention is the engine.
