✈️Angel Protection for a Journey

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RitePouch
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Candle MagickKnot MagickPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Three silver candles arranged in a triangle - A silver ribbon - A drawstring bag
◇Directions
1. Set the ribbon on top of the bag. 2. Light the candles, beginning with the single one at the top, saying for each: "Guardian angels three, Raphael, Ambriel, and Damabiah, let no harm befall me [or name]." Tie three knots in the ribbon, naming Raphael for the first, Ambriel for the second, and Damabiah for the third, adding for each: "Let no harm befall me [or name]," and put it in the bag. 3. Let the candles burn down around the bag and then take the bag with you or give it to a loved one. 4. Perform the night before travel, after dusk.
◇Description
A night-before-travel spell for angel protection on any journey or time away, using three silver candles in triangle with ribbon knotted for Raphael, Ambriel, and Damabiah.
