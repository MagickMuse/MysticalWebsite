Camphor

An aarti-inspired flame offering in which camphor pellets are burned atop a bowl of water, their purifying flames skittering across the surface in honor of Brigid's union of fire and water. The remaining water becomes lustral water for blessing and purification.

Quantities

Camphor pellets; metal tongs or tweezers; a shallow bowl of water; optional small plate

Directions

  1. Fill a shallow bowl with water and set it on a safe surface.
  2. Using metal tongs, hold a camphor pellet and light it with a match, then gently place it on the surface of the water.
  3. Watch the flame skitter and dance across the surface in a purifying display.
  4. Light additional pellets and wave them through the air in blessing gestures before setting them on the water, if desired.
  5. Speak prayers to Brigid or simply be present as the flames dance and fade.
  6. When the flames have gone out, the remaining water is lustral water: sacred through the union of fire and water.
  7. Use it to bless and purify yourself, your altar, or your space.
