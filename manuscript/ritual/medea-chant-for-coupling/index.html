A conjugal chant spoken before coupling with a lover to invoke Dragon-bodied chthonic Zeus into the beloved and embody Serpent-formed Persephone in the practitioner, spiritually conceiving Dionysus-Zagreus and securing authority over BAINKHOOKH.

Quantities

  • A lit red candle (held in the left hand)
  • A blade or lancet (held in the right hand)

Directions

  1. On the first night after the new moon, in a private place, let down your hair, disrobe, and straddle your lover.
  2. Hold a lit red candle in the left hand and a blade or lancet in the right. Call out the following, letting wax fall from the candle onto the lover's chest as indicated:
    O you of the lashing tail! Great Old
    Serpent of a thousand scales;
    Many-toothed dweller of the deep; you
    Who thrash in shallow sleep,
    And dream as aeons slowly slide
    Across the fabric of the void;
    God of the soon-to-be-destroyed.
    Awake! Awake! Awake!
    (Let fall a few drops of red wax upon the lover's chest.)
    O you of the flashing eye! Adrift
    In the currents of the night;
    Too-bright devourer of worlds,
    Hear the echo of my words
    And let the full flag of your wrath unfurl.
    Awake! Awake! Awake!
    (Let fall a few more drops of red wax.)
    Dragon Father, distant stranger,
    I call to you with sacred anger, and with blood.
    O Hear the cry of your own brood; the kings
    Of earth are heaven's food. So feed: with each pricked
    Finger you grow stronger; let your hunger sleep
    No longer: Awake! Awake! Awake!
    (Prick the ring finger of the left hand.)
    O Ender of Suffering! Satisfier
    Of desire. Fire of Heaven, Fist
    Of Fate: the kingdoms of the earth retreat too late
    As you uncoil: let all systems blaze
    With your burning breath, O Deathless
    Harbinger of Death: See! The cities
    Melt like glass; the world tilts; it
    Cannot last. Your scales grow bright
    As new-mint coins! Descend, Descend,
    As we conjoin. Descend, Descend, as
    We conjoin. Descend, Descend as
    We conjoin.
  3. Touch the pricked finger to the lover's brow five times with drops of blood, saying:
    With this hand I crown your brow
    With five red stars; and consecrate you
    To the cause of the coming wars.
  4. Touch the pricked finger to your own lips, then the lover's, saying:
    The stars are all connected; the stars become
    A crown. By the Dragon we are joined,
    And by the Dragon bound.
  5. The two merge in coupling, spiritually conceiving Dionysus-Zagreus within the practitioner.
