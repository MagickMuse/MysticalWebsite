A thirteen-day ceremonial working to summon the shade of a named deceased person within a protective circle at the new moon, conducted as a last resort when all other divinatory methods have failed to yield the needed answer.

Quantities

Athame
Cord
Cup (ritual)
Wand
Thurible
Necromancy incense
Mercurial incense (for circle purification)
Altar table
Equilateral triangle of white tape (large, laid on the floor to the west)
Small triangle cloth (for altar)
Lamps of art (2)
Skull (actual, replica, or hand-drawn)
Photograph or portrait of the deceased
Hourglass or egg timer
Pentacle of protection (one per participant)
Paper
Pen and ink of art

Directions

  1. For 13 nights before the ritual, spend a brief midnight meditation focused on a photograph or portrait of the deceased whose shade you intend to summon.
  2. On the 14th night, which must fall on the new moon, secure the room entirely against intrusion.
  3. On the floor to the west of the circle, lay out a large equilateral triangle of white tape. Place the skull within it.
  4. On the altar, arrange a small triangle. Inside it, place the photograph of the deceased flanked by the lamps of art. Set the hourglass nearby.
  5. Ensure all participants wear a pentacle of protection at the breast.
  6. Just before midnight, cast and purify the circle with Mercurial incense.
  7. At the first stroke of midnight, approach the west side of the altar table facing east. Consecrate the photograph with salt, water, and incense. With the wand, trace an equal-armed cross surrounded by a circle in the air above the photograph, visualizing it blazing with blue radiance. With each consecration, speak: Colpriziana Offina Alta Nestera Fuaro Menut — I name thee [name]. Thou art [name].
  8. Carrying the wand in the right hand and the photograph in the left, walk clockwise moving backwards to the eastern rim of the circle. Hold the wand upright over the photograph and speak the conjuration to the east: Spirit of [name] deceased, thou mayest now approach the gates of the east to answer truly my liege demands. Berald, Beroald, Balbin! Gab, Gabor, Agaba! Arise, arise, I charge and command thee.
  9. Repeat step 8 at the south, west, and north in turn, changing the gate name each time. Any companions present walk the perimeter with you.
  10. Still walking backwards, return alone to the east of the altar. Facing west, replace the photograph in the altar triangle. All present maintain strict silence for one to two minutes.
  11. Place the necromancy incense in the thurible. Once fuming well, speak the grand citation, striking the photograph gently with the wand at the end of each line: By the Mysteries of the deep [strike], by the flames of Banal [strike], by the power of the east [strike], and by the silence of the night [strike], by the holy rites of Hekate [strike], I conjure and exorcise thee, spirit [name] [strike], to present thyself here [strike] and answer truly our demands [strike]. So mote it be! [strike].
  12. Replenish the thurible with necromancy incense. Extinguish all altar lights, plunging the room into darkness. Walking backwards, move to the west rim of the circle. Kneel facing the floor triangle. Slowly cross both arms upon the breast — the gesture of the skull and crossbones — and inwardly repeat the charge: Allay Fortission, Fortissio, Allynsen Roa!
  13. Close the eyes and remain in silence for a few minutes. With eyes still shut, greet the spirit quietly as though present before you.
  14. Summon courage and pronounce the deceased's full name clearly and loudly three times. Slowly extend both arms wide in the form of a cross and open the eyes. If the operation succeeds, a dim form will gradually appear in the floor triangle, seemingly composed of incense smoke and occasionally lit from within by a faint bluish phosphorescence. Present your question. If an immediate answer is not given, it will come later through a chain of inexplicable coincidences.
  15. When the form fades, the raised power has ebbed. Walking backwards, return to the east of the altar facing west. Rekindle the lamps and replenish the thurible if needed.
  16. Speak the licence to depart: Go, go, departed shade [name], by Omgroma Epin Sayoc, Satony, Degony, Eparigon, Galiganon, Zogogen, Ferstigon. We licence thee depart into thy proper place, and be there peace between us evermore. So mote it be!
  17. Record all results and observations in the magickal workbook.
