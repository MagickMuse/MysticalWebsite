🕯️Drawing Down the Sun

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Circle RiteAltar WorkingConsecrated Item
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
InvocationCandle MagickSolarElementalEmpowermentDirectional Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- God and Goddess candles (gold/silver or yellow/white) - Four quarter candles: yellow (East/Air), red (South/Fire), blue (West/Water), green (North/Earth) - Sun pillar candle (yellow or orange or red) in a fire-safe holder - Wide ribbon matching the sun candle color (to tie at the waist) - Cast-iron cauldron or fire-safe container or candle holder - Small bowls: salt, water, and one to mix - Incense or sage smudge, holder - Matches and optional snuffer - Optional: altar cloth, altar table, broom or wand or athame, sunflowers or daisies, blanket to sit on - Cakes and ale: sun-shaped cookies and juice, wine, mead, or ale in a chalice
◇Directions
1. Setting: Ideally outdoors at noon on Midsummer. 2. Otherwise any time during daylight within a couple of days of Midsummer, where sunlight can fall directly on you. 3. Pre‑ritual setup: - Place God and Goddess candles on the altar. Put the Sun pillar between them toward the front so you can sit before it. Tie the ribbon loosely around the candle's base. - Place quarter candles at their directions (or on altar corners): East/yellow (Air), South/red (Fire), West/blue (Water), North/green (Earth). - Set offerings and decorations. Place cakes and ale within reach. 4. Cleanse and consecrate: - Smudge the space clockwise with sage/incense, visualizing negativity clearing. Smudge yourself from head to feet. - Mix a pinch of salt into water: "Salt into water, water into salt. Wash away all that is negative and impure, leaving only that which is positive and beneficial." - Touch brow (thoughts), lips (speech), and heart (feelings) with the water. 5. Cast the circle: - Walk the perimeter, pointing with finger, athame, broom, or wand: "Earth to sky, sky to ground; let sacred space be all around." Visualize white light enclosing the space. 6. Call the quarters (turning East → South → West → North): East (Air): "I call the guardian of the east, the power of air, to come to this circle bringing refreshing summer breezes and lightness of spirit." Light the yellow candle. South (Fire): "I call the guardian of the south, the power of fire, to come to this circle bringing warm sunlight and a passion for summer joy." Light the red candle. West (Water): "I call the guardian of the west, the power of water, to come to this circle bringing life-giving rains and a healing flow." Light the blue candle. North (Earth): "I call the guardian of the north, the power of earth, to come to this circle bringing energy for growth and the strength of the ground below." Light the green candle. 7. Invoke the Goddess and the God (or your preferred forms) and light their candles: "Great goddess, I greet you! You who are known by many names and many faces, shine your divine blessing down on me on this Midsummer Day, filling me with light and love." "Great god, I greet you! You who are the lord of the beasts and the fields and the woods, shine your divine blessing down on me this Midsummer Day, filling me with strength and energy." 8. Drawing Down the Sun (charging): - Stand or sit before the Sun candle and light it. Raise arms, palms up. Feel the Sun on your face. Draw its energy in through your crown, filling your body with light and power. - Open your eyes and gaze into the candle flame. See solar power flow into the candle and into the ribbon beneath it. - Say: "Today is Midsummer and the sun is at its zenith. Its power and energy are all around me. Its power and energy are part of me. I am the fire of the sun." - Lift the ribbon to the Sun, then tie it around your waist: "Within this token, I store the power and energy of the sun, that they may carry me through the darker days ahead." 9. Cakes and ale (optional): - Hold the cake: "I thank the earth for its harvest and for the bounty it brings to my life." Eat. - Hold the cup: "I thank the sun for its life‑giving rays that help the harvest to grow and bring sweetness to this cup and to my life." Drink. 10. Thanks and close: - "Blessed lady, blessed lord, I thank you for your presence in my circle here today and in my life always." Snuff deity candles. - Dismiss quarters counterclockwise, starting North → West → South → East: "I thank you (earth, water, fire, air) for watching over my circle and my magickal work." Snuff each candle after thanks. - Open the circle counterclockwise, seeing the wall of light drop away. - Wear the ribbon all day or store it safely for future boosts. Re‑wear or carry it when you need the Sun's energy.
◇Description
A noon rite to draw the Sun's power into the self, charging the spirit for the darker months ahead. Designed for a solitary practitioner with optional full circle, quarter calls, and cakes-and-ale. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
