🌕Moroccan Magick Moon Lost Love Revival Spell

Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group

◇Quantities
Pot of spring water Cloth steeped in partner's sexual fluids (or strip cut from unlaundered bedsheet) Seven pieces of harmel (Syrian rue) Seven myrtle twigs Four lumps of alum One piece of rock salt One piece of sulfur Ingredients to bake a homemade loaf of bread
◇Directions
1. On a moonlit night, prepare yourself as for a seduction: groom, apply oils and make-up, clean your teeth, freshen your breath. Go to the courtyard (or private outdoor space) with a pot of spring water and the cloth. Remove your clothes and dishevel your hair as if you've just risen from bed. Sit beside the pot of water and speak to the moon: "Oh, Moon, if you're in the mood for love and if I look good, Come on down, come to me!" The intent is to invite the Moon to enter the water and impart its magickal power. Sprinkle some of the lunar-charged water onto the cloth soaked with your partner's sexual fluids. Place seven pieces of harmel (Syrian rue), seven myrtle twigs, four lumps of alum, one piece of rock salt, and one piece of sulfur onto the cloth. Wrap everything up and hide this packet under your partner's pillow for three nights. After three nights, remove the packet and grind up all of its contents. Make a homemade loaf of bread, incorporating the ground packet contents into the dough. Break the baked loaf in half. Slip one half under where the partner's head lies in the bed and the other half under where their feet rest. Leave both pieces there for three nights. After three nights, collect the bread and grind it into a powder. Use the powder to make a porridge-type cereal and feed it to your partner, by hand if at all possible.
◇Description
A 10-step Moroccan spell to revive the withdrawn love and affection of a partner who is still physically present but emotionally distant. Uses lunar-charged spring water, Syrian rue (harmel), myrtle, alum, rock salt, and sulfur to create a pillow charm, then a charged bread that is ground into porridge and fed to the partner by hand. Adapt the traditional courtyard setting to any private outdoor space.
