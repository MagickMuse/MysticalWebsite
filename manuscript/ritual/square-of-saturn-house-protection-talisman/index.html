A Cabalistic magick square talisman using the Square of Saturn, a 3x3 grid in which every row, column, and diagonal sums to 15. The reverse bears a sketch of the home surrounded by three concentric circles, and both sides are charged in Hertha's name for protection and home blessing before being concealed within the structure of the home.

Directions

  1. On a Saturday on or just after the new moon, at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM, kindle Tellurian Incense in the thurible and place salt water in the chalice.
  2. Exorcise a piece of talisman paper by passing it through fire and water.
  3. Using pen and ink of art, divide the paper into nine compartments (a 3x3 grid).
  4. Slowly and deliberately fill in the numbers 1 through 9 in the Saturn Square arrangement, concentrating on Hertha's image with each number and chanting an invocation phrase evoking Earth power, such as names of goddesses: Rhea, Ceres, Hulda, Vesta, or Hertha, bless this house and all who live in it, or any verse evoking safety, protection, or prosperity.
  5. When number 9 is complete, turn the paper over. Draw a simple plan or sketch of the house on the back, surrounding it with three concentric circles drawn clockwise beginning with the outermost. Repeat the chant with each circle.
  6. Cense the completed talisman and seal with the triple cross and So mote it be.
  7. Fold into a packet and tie firmly with red ribbon, knotted three times.
  8. Conceal immediately in a consecrated place in Hertha's name, making it part of the structure of the home.
