📔NotebookTea Light Candle

A nineteen-day flamekeeping cycle honoring the ancient tradition of Brigid's priestesses. Either a punctuated cycle with flame lit on days one and nineteen, or nineteen consecutive daily candles each dedicated to a quality of Brigid.

Quantities

19 tealights (or one candle for the punctuated version); optional journal

Directions

  1. Punctuated cycle: Choose a start date.
  2. On day one, light a devotional flame and speak with Brigid about your hopes, concerns, and intentions for the nineteen days ahead.
  3. Extinguish the flame and leave it unlit until day nineteen.
  4. On day nineteen, relight the flame.
  5. Review the days past with Brigid: give thanks, ask for clarity, reflect on the lessons learned.
  6. Extinguish with the awareness that Brigid herself tends the flame on the twentieth day.
  7. Repeat throughout the year.
  8. Consecutive cycle: Light a new tealight each evening for nineteen nights.
  9. Dedicate each candle to a particular quality of Brigid or a focus for her blessing.
  10. Each evening's devotion can be a single lit moment or hours of contemplation, journeying, journaling, or art-making.
  11. If desired, lay out all nineteen tealights in the triangular pattern and light them in order (see image below).
  12. At the end of night nineteen, offer the flame to Brigid and envision her taking it from you, to keep it burning bright forever.
