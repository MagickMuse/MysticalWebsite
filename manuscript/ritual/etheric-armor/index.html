Directions

  1. A psychic projection technique using sense memory of a cherished comfort object to create an etheric layer of protection.
  2. Works with jackets, blankets, jewelry, or any item with strong emotional connection.

The Concept

Controlled breath, visualization, and grounding form the foundation for psychic projections. Beyond the egg shield, you can project etheric copies of real objects that carry emotional significance.

The ideal comfort object has these qualities:

  • Strong emotional connection
  • History of favorable sense memories
  • Familiar sight, sound, feel, and smell
  • Makes you feel safe when using it

This could be a leather jacket, a cozy blanket, a cherished necklace, a favorite belt, or well-worn shoes.

Creating the Etheric Copy

Step 1: Sensory Exploration

Sit with the physical object in your lap. Spend a few moments remembering it with all your senses:

  • Examine the texture of its surface
  • Notice the temperature of any metal components
  • Feel its weight
  • Acknowledge the feeling of safety it gives you
  • Inhale any scents that linger in it

Step 2: Memory Activation

Close your eyes and pull up each sense in turn. Explore your memories of this object:

  • Times you wore or used it
  • Places you've been with it
  • The weight of it against your body
  • Emotional moments it accompanied

The stronger your emotional connection, the stronger your energetic link will be.

Step 3: Projection

Stand up and set the physical object aside. Now visualize putting it on or holding it in your mind's eye:

  • Recall the feelings of safety and security it produces
  • Project the visual of the object
  • Feel the surety and protection it represents

Your etheric armor is now active. Practice until you can summon it without the physical object present.
