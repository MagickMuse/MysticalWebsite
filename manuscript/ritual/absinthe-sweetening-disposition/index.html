A bonding ritual and honey jar working to soften someone's hostile disposition toward you. Absinthe's antianxiety and mood-lifting properties create space for reconciliation.

Quantities

  • Social Method:
  • Bottle of quality absinthe
  • Sugar and water for preparation
  • Absinthe spoon (optional)
    Honey Jar Method:
  • Paper and pen
  • Scissors
  • Jar
  • Honey or sweetener (simple syrup, waffle syrup, molasses)
  • Candle (optional, for empowering)

Directions

  1. Absinthe can be a bonding experience and a friendly gesture.
  2. If there is someone in your life who seems to sour at your presence, consider sharing absinthe with them to talk out any past issues.
  3. It is antianxiety, antidepressant, lifts mood, and counters misfortune.
  4. It could be that you are projecting anxiety onto the situation.
  5. Remember, fear is not intuition.
  6. If there is an issue you are unaware of, this is an excellent chance to bond and work out any missteps.
  7. If you're too shy to approach them for a night out, you have options.
  8. You can buy a bottle and invite other coworkers to your place, where there is safety and comfort, and make it a group wellness exercise.
  9. Honey Jar Option: You can also make a paper poppet of the person in question, place it in a jar, and "feed" it honey to get the person to act with less hatred towards you.
  10. Cut out a gingerbread man shape and name it with the person's full name and (if you have it) any biographical data like date, time, and place of birth.
  11. If some of your data is missing, try to be descriptive: "Bob from accounting," "the jerk in 3B."
  12. Jars are a long-term working, but work rapidly.
  13. Place Paper Bob in the jar and pour honey over the doll.
  14. (Really any sweetener will work: simple syrup, waffle syrup, molasses...) Talk to it and say the things you wish you were empowered to say to Bob directly.
  15. Occasionally add more sugar or honey and keep talking to the jar.
  16. Feel free to put the lid on and burn candles over the jar between chats to empower it.
