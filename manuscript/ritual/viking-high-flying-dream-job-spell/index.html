A traditional Tuesday Viking high-flying spell to facilitate major career ambitions or get the job of your dreams, offering three gold coins to the ocean with fire and herbs.

Quantities

  • Three gold-colored coins
  • Ideally a seashore or tidal river before high tide; if not, any water source
  • Dried thyme and rosemary

Directions

  1. Build a small fire close to the water's edge.
  2. Sprinkle thyme and rosemary into the flames, saying: "Burn bright that the height of my ambition I achieve. Leap high, do not deny, my speedy rise. Flame that I may swiftly attain what must be mine sooner to gain." Cast the gold coins into the water, naming your urgent ambition, saying: "Lady of the Ocean, Mother of the Sea, I bring you gold, give power to me." Wait until high tide meets the fire, carrying the fire away.
  3. Repeat your wish, casting a second tiny piece of gold into the sea, repeating the second chant.
  4. If you are working with nontidal water, scoop a bucket of the water to douse the fire, using the same words.
  5. Make the final offering before leaving the shore, repeating the words.
  6. Perform on Tuesday.
