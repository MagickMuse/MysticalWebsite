Amethyst's anti-intoxication powers are strongest if one drinks from an amethyst cup; an amethyst-colored cup may be sufficient.

Notes

Amethysts reinforce your personal determination and provide a measure of protection. They also have a cleansing effect: metaphysically they will help remove and assimilate toxins, including alcohol.

Amethyst will not stop you if you are truly determined to drink; rather, it bolsters your own attempts not to drink, whether that means abstaining entirely or simply moderating excess.

Mythology

The amethyst is sacred to Dionysus, Spirit of Ecstasy and Intoxication. According to myth, on a difficult morning-after, Dionysus, suffering from a hangover, declared he would terminate the very next person to cross his path. That person was the young nymph Amethyst, on her way to present offerings at Artemis' shrine. Before Dionysus could act, Artemis saved her by transforming her into a luminous clear crystal. Abashed, Dionysus poured his wine over the crystallized nymph, turning her the characteristic purple color, and swore that whenever he saw this gemstone, he would become an ally against the ill effects of alcohol.
