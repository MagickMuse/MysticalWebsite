🌿Parsley Sage Rosemary Thyme Love

A midnight candle spell to call a lost love back when all else has failed, using the four sacred herbs (parsley, sage, rosemary, thyme) shaped into a heart with wax and wrapped in white silk.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • A fast-burning deep red candle
  • Old photos and mementos from happier times with your love, including music you both enjoy
  • A deep-lipped or flat metal tray to collect candle wax
  • Mix of dried rosemary, parsley, sage, and thyme
  • A piece of white silk

Directions

  1. Light the red candle.
  2. As it burns, look at the old mementos, listening to the music.
  3. After the candle burns out, sprinkle the dried herbs in the shape of a heart into the pool of melted wax, repeating until you have finished.
  4. Say: "By parsley, sage, rosemary, thyme, call back, I ask, this love of mine." Gently wrap the heart in silk with a picture of you and your love, adding a few more loose herbs, tying it with white ribbon, saying: "By parsley, sage, rosemary, and thyme, I hold your heart entwined in mine." Each week unwrap the picture, add a few more herbs, and repeat the spell words until your love returns.
