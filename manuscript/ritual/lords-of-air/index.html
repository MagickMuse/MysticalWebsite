To send a wish into the wind and call upon the Lords of Air to help manifest it. Best cast on a windy night, this spell draws on ancient traditions of air deities from multiple cultures, including Aeolus, Skan, Aquilo, Feng Po, Stribog, and Njord, to empower your intention and carry it out into the world.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • A piece of white or pale blue paper with your wish written on it (optional: use ink color aligned to your wish, pink/red for love, green for money, orange for success, black for banishing, etc.)
  • Your favorite perfume, cologne, or aftershave
  • A flat, shallow container (tray or dish)
  • A stick of incense (ginseng, sage, lavender, sandalwood, or violet recommended)
  • (Optional): Finely chopped, powdered, or crumbled herbs related to your wish

Directions

  1. Preparation:
  2. Light the incense and create sacred space.
  3. Spray or anoint the paper with perfume. Don't worry if the ink smears, it will still carry your wish.
  4. Focus deeply on your wish as you waft the paper through the air to dry it, then repeatedly pass it through the incense smoke to scent and charge it.
  5. Cut or tear the paper into confetti-sized pieces and sprinkle them onto the tray.
  6. (Optional): Add a large spoonful of matching herbs and mix them with the paper.
    The Spell:
  7. Take the tray outside at night on a windy evening.
  8. Raise power. Face East. Hold the tray up and say:
    Gods of Winds, Lords of Air,
    Grant my wish if it be fair.
  9. Place the tray somewhere the wind can reach it, like a balcony, windowsill, or lawn chair.
  10. Focus on your wish going forth into the world. Repeat:
    Gods of Winds, Lords of Air,
    Grant my wish if it be fair.
  11. Visualize your wish becoming manifest. Ground the power you raised.
    Afterward:
    Leave the tray outdoors overnight. Check it in the morning.
  12. If the contents are gone, this is a positive sign.
  13. If anything remains, it is considered a negative omen and the spell may need to be recast at another time.

Why This Spell Works

When we say something is "in the air" or "blowing in the wind," we acknowledge an ancient understanding: the element of Air carries information, possibility, and change. This spell takes that understanding literally by putting your wish into the wind and asking the Lords of Air to carry it forth into manifestation.

Nearly every human culture has recognized deities who rule the winds. This spell invokes them collectively, drawing on the power of Aeolus (Greek), Skan (Native American), Aquilo (Roman), Feng Po (Chinese), Stribog (Slavic), and Njord (Norse). By calling upon all the Lords of Air, you cast your net wide across traditions and tap into a universal understanding of wind as messenger and mover.

The Power of Scent

Anointing your written wish with perfume serves multiple purposes. Scent is one of the most evocative of our senses, directly connected to memory and emotion. Your personal fragrance carries your energy signature. When the perfume merges with your written wish, it binds your essence to your intention.

Do not worry if the perfume causes the ink to run or smear. The paper absorbs and holds your wish regardless of whether the words remain legible.

Reading the Results

The morning check provides divination. If the wind has carried away all the confetti and herbs, the Lords of Air have accepted your wish and are working to manifest it. This is a positive omen.

If anything remains in the container, this suggests resistance or obstruction. The wish may need refinement, the timing may not be right, or other factors may be interfering. You can recast the spell on another windy night, perhaps rewording your wish or adding different herbs.

Timing

This spell is best cast on a windy night when the Lords of Air are most active. However, any night will work if you place the container where wind can reach it by morning.
