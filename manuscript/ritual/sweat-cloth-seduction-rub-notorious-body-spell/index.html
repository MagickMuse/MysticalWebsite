💦Sweat Cloth Seduction Rub Notorious Body Spell

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ManifestationChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Cloth
◇Directions
1. Work up a sweat. 2. Wipe the sweat with a cloth. 3. Reserve the cloth; don't wash it. 4. Use this sweat-soaked cloth to rub the person you'd like to seduce, while thinking: 5. 'My sweat on your skin / Your sweat on mine / My love is yours / Your love is mine'
◇Description
Sweat is collected on a cloth and used to rub the target of desire while intoning a love chant, binding their love through bodily essence without requiring ingestion.
