A crocheted Yule log to display tea lights. The Yule log symbolizes hope, just imagine how bleak it would have been without a log for the hearth during the shortest night. This version won't burn away and makes an excellent display stand for poinsettia or snowflake tea lights. Traditional Yule logs use oak for strength, pine for prosperity, or birch for fertility.

Quantities

  • Size H/8 5mm crochet hook
  • 312 yards (6 oz) dark brown 4-ply yarn
  • 104 yards (2 oz) light brown 4-ply yarn
  • Stitch marker
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle
  • Fiberfill stuffing

Directions

  1. Crochet Abbreviations:
    Ch = Chain | St = Stitch | Sc = Single crochet | Sc inc = Single crochet increase | Hdc = Half double crochet | Popcorn = Make 5 Dc in same stitch, remove hook and put it in the 1st Dc made, then back into active loop, draw loop through Dc, and Ch one | Sl St = Slip stitch | BLO = Back loops only | FO = Fasten off
  2. Log Ends (Make 2) - Light Brown:
    Ch 2
    Round 1: 8 Sc in 2nd Ch from hook
    Working in BLO from here on:
    Round 2: 2 Sc in each St around (16). Move stitch marker as you finish each round.
    Round 3: {Sc, Sc inc} around (24)
    Round 4: {Sc twice, Sc inc} around (32)
    Round 5: {Sc 3 times, Sc inc} around (40)
    Round 6: {Sc 4 times, Sc inc} around (48)
    Sl St into next 2 stitches, Ch 1
    Top stitch with Sl Sts on unused loops to make a spiral design back to center. Use a different shade of brown if desired.
  3. Log Body - Dark Brown (hold 2 strands together):
    Ch 41
    Row 1: Hdc in 2nd St from hook, Hdc across (40)
    Rows 2–6: Hdc in each St across (40)
    Row 7: 8 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc (40)
    Rows 8–12: Hdc in each St across (40)
    Row 13: 15 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc, popcorn, 8 Hdc (40)
    Rows 14–18: Hdc in each St across (40)
    Row 19: 8 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc (40)
    Rows 20–24: Hdc in each St across (40)
    Row 25: 15 Hdc, popcorn, 15 Hdc, popcorn, 8 Hdc (40)
  4. With right sides together, use Sl Sts to join long ends, forming a tube.
  5. Sew on one log end, stuff with fiberfill, and sew on second log end.
  6. Place three tea lights across the top.
  7. Display in a window or on your altar.
