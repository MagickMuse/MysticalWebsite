Briar

A British folk magick healing ritual for children. The child's mother must find, entirely on her own, a wild briar rose that has bent to the ground and rooted, forming an arch. The child is then passed through the arch on nine consecutive mornings.

Quantities

Wild briar rose arch (naturally formed, found solely by the child's mother)

Directions

  1. The child's mother must search for and locate, entirely on her own with no assistance, wild roses, specifically briar roses that have bent to the ground and become rooted, forming a natural arch.
  2. Once found, take the child to the plant on nine consecutive mornings and pass the child through the arch each time, for a total of nine distinct passes.
