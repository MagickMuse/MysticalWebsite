🍵Surrounded by Love Potion

To encourage you to recognize and appreciate the love that is already in your life. Stir the shape of a heart into the potion.

Quantities

At least two of: hibiscus, strawberry leaf, raspberry leaf, or rose hip tea (per the Tea Potion Spell)
Slice of dried apple
Optional: rose-pink cup or mug

Directions

  1. Choose at least two of the following teas: hibiscus, strawberry leaf, raspberry leaf, or rose hip. Brew them together according to the Tea Potion Spell.
  2. Put the slice of dried apple into the cup before you add the boiling water, so the apple infuses with the tea and softens.
  3. When stirring, first stir deosil (clockwise), then trace the shape of a heart into the surface of the tea.
  4. As you drink, think about all the people who love you, visualizing their faces, remembering their kindnesses, and feeling gratitude for their presence in your life. Complete the incantation from the Tea Potion Spell, filling in "love's recognition" as your intention.
  5. Eat the apple after the potion is finished.

This tea potion helps you recognize and appreciate the love that already surrounds you. Sometimes we are so focused on seeking new love that we overlook the abundance of love already present in our lives.

Using a rose-pink cup or mug strengthens the love energy of this potion.
