A fish stuffed with enemies' names, sealed with silver pins and molasses, kept on Yemaya's altar for seven days, then cast into the sea so she will shield you from them. Not a hex. ⚠️ Yemaya Fish Shield Against Enemies draws on Santería/Lucumí, a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

1 whole fish, bought for this spell
Slips of paper, one per enemy
Silver pins
Dark molasses
Blue and white candles
Strongly fragrant incense (optional)
7 silver coins

Directions

  1. Buy a fish specifically for this spell.
  2. Write each enemy's name on its own slip of paper.
  3. Place the slips in the fish's mouth; if the mouth is too small or the list too long, cut slits in the belly and insert them there.
  4. Close the mouth or slits with silver pins.
  5. Cover the fish with dark molasses.
  6. Surround it with blue and white candles.
  7. Let the fish stand on Yemaya's altar for seven days, keeping the candles burning throughout; strongly fragrant incense is advisable.
  8. After seven days, place seven silver coins inside the fish and throw it, with everything in it, into the sea.
