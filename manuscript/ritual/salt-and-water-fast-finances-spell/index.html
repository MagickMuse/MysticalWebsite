🧂Salt and Water Fast Finances Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Salt MagickWater MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A bowl of salt - A piece of paper or card on which to collect the salt - A bowl of water
◇Directions
1. Run your fingers through the salt in the bowl and say: "This is instant prosperity. May my prosperity likewise grow instantly and keep flowing." Pour a pile of salt onto the paper or card, heaping it into a cone shape, saying the words again. 2. Tip the salt into the bowl of water and say the words: "This is instant prosperity. May my prosperity likewise flow and flow." Swirl the bowl of water nine times clockwise, repeating these words. 3. Pour the bowl of salt water into flowing water, saying: "Prosperity now, will flow to me, now and continually, so I ask and it shall be." Perform before sunset.
◇Description
A salt and water spell for fast and short-term finances, creating a cone of salt dissolved in water and swirled clockwise nine times before releasing to flowing water.
