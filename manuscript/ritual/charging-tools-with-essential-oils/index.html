A method for charging magickal tools by anointing them with appropriate essential oils. Different oils correspond to different tools, enhancing their magickal properties through scent and intention.

Quantities

  • Essential oil appropriate to your tool (see Directions)
  • Clean cloth
  • Your magickal tool

Directions

  1. Select the appropriate essential oil or blend for your tool.
  2. For wands, use cinnamon, sandalwood, clove, musk, patchouli, or cedar.
  3. For pentagrams, use mint, pine, amber, basil, fennel, or anise.
  4. For athames, use carnation, lavender, ginger, or honeysuckle.
  5. For chalices, use rose, ylang-ylang, jasmine, or lily of the valley.
  6. Rub a small amount of the essential oil onto the tool while saying: "With this oil I charge you to do my will." Focus your intention on the tool's purpose as you anoint it.
  7. These suggestions are starting points.
  8. You may design a more elaborate or personal ritual for charging your magick tools.
  9. The more personal the ritual, the better.
