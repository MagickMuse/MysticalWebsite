A New Year's midnight Scottish tradition ritual sending First Footer out with bag of gifts, returning upstairs and down, dropping crystals in water for wishes.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • 1 copper, silver, and gold-colored coin
  • Wrapped sweets, dried fruits, and nuts
  • Dried basil, juniper berries, sage, or thyme
  • Coal or wood
  • 1 drawstring bag
  • Small crystals or glass nuggets
  • 1 white candle (optional)
  • 1 large bowl of water

Directions

  1. Five minutes before midnight, send the person chosen as First Footer (the person to first set foot in the house in the New Year in the old Scottish tradition) outside the front door with all the items, except the crystals, in the bag.
  2. At midnight all shout, "Come in, New Year. New Year, you are welcome," rattling pans and making welcome greetings.
  3. The First Footer enters, shuts the door, and goes out the back door (if there is one) or out of the front door again, saying: "Out you go, Old Year. Your time is past." The First Footer comes back in, slams the door, walks upstairs to the top of the house and down again, shouting, "Happy New Year." The First Footer deposits the bag on the hearth or in front of a burning white candle.
  4. All toast the New Year and drop crystals into the bowl of water, making New Year's wishes.
  5. Perform New Year's Eve, before midnight.
