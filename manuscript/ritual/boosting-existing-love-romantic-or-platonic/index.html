💕Boosting Existing Love, Romantic or Platonic

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteDressed CandleWriting Spell
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickManifestationPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- Pad of sticky notes (preferably pink or yellow paper) - Black pen - Yellow pillar candle for platonic love, or red pillar candle for romantic love (pink pillar candle works for either purpose) - Matches - Firesafe candleholder (small plate, ramekin, ceramic coaster, or similar) - Optional: Carving tool (tweezers head, pin, pocketknife tip, or small point) - Optional: Carrier oil (olive oil, coconut oil, or avocado oil, about a quarter-sized dollop) - Optional: Essential oils (lemon or sweet orange for friendship; rose or lavender for romantic love) - Optional: Dried herbs and spices (ground cumin, ginger, cocoa, or lavender) - Optional: Crystals for platonic love (moss agate, aventurine, turquoise) - Optional: Crystals for romantic love (rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst, moonstone) - Optional: Selenite or Florida Water for cleansing crystals - Optional: Tarot card
◇Directions
1. Decide when you will light your candle for the first time. 2. If boosting platonic love, light the candle on a Sunday. 3. If boosting romantic love, light the candle on a Friday. 4. Ideally, the day you light your candle for the first time would also be a New Moon or waxing Moon. 5. Regardless of moon phase, do not light your candle for the first time during Mercury retrograde or an eclipse. 6. On the day you have chosen, locate a pad of sticky notes and a pen. 7. Think about the relationships in your life that would benefit from a boost of love, then choose one individual to focus on. 8. Write their full name on a sticky note, then set it aside. 9. Prepare the space where you will light your candle. 10. This may be your altar or another flat surface such as a kitchen counter. 11. Ensure the surface is clean and clear of clutter. 12. The space should not be disturbed by pets or family members, as the candle should not be moved once lit. 13. If using magickal extras, prepare them now. 14. For carving, do so before anything else while the wax is dry and unblemished. 15. Carve words that represent your spell, such as the name of the person you have in mind or the word love or friendship. 16. Use the head of tweezers, a pin, the tip of a pocketknife, or another small point. 17. For carrier oil, place a dollop about the size of a quarter in your dominant hand's palm. 18. Wrap your hand around the top of the candle and slide down toward the base, smearing oil as you go. 19. Twist your hand as you pull to coat the candle evenly. 20. For essential oils, add a few drops to your carrier oil before dressing the candle. 21. If you've already dressed the candle or are not using carrier oil, sprinkle a few drops of essential oil directly on the wax. 22. Lemon and sweet orange essential oils are wonderful for friendship. 23. To boost romantic love, use rose or lavender essential oil. 24. For herbs and spices, sprinkle them on a plate and roll the oil-dressed candle across the plate once or twice. 25. The oil will hold the dried ingredients. 26. For crystals, cleanse them first using Full Moon light, Florida Water, or selenite pieces, then arrange them around the base of your candle. 27. For platonic love, use moss agate, aventurine, or turquoise. 28. For romantic love, use rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst, or moonstone. 29. For tarot cards, if boosting platonic love, select a card depicting friendship such as the Three of Cups. 30. If boosting romantic love, use a card that signifies partnership such as the Two of Cups, or a card you relate with your partner, also called a significator. 31. Place the card near your candle. 32. Place the prepared candle and any magickal extras on your altar or chosen flat surface. 33. Locate the sticky note and a box of matches. 34. Take these to your candle, then kneel or stand in front of it. 35. Set the matches aside and say the individual's full name out loud. 36. Then, adhere the sticky note underneath the candleholder. 37. Pick up the box of matches. 38. As you strike the match, say: 39. > Boost this love that exists in my life. 40. Bonded, harmonious, and free from strife. 41. Light your candle's wick. 42. Let the candle burn for as long as possible, with a minimum of one hour. 43. Stay near the candle to ensure it burns safely. 44. Never leave the house or fall asleep while a candle is lit. 45. When you need to leave or head to bed, extinguish the flame using a snuffer, your breath, or the pads of your fingers. 46. As the wick's smoke wafts, say aloud: 47. > And so it is. 48. Leave the candle and all magickal extras exactly as they are. 49. If the candle is in a heavily trafficked area, gently move the items to another flat surface for storage. 50. On the following Sunday or Friday (depending on whether your candle's intent is platonic or romantic), relight your candle following the same steps: state the person's full name, strike a match and say the spell, let the flame burn as long as possible, and end with "And so it is." Continue this practice until you burn through the candle or no longer feel the need for it. 51. When the candle has done its job, give it a proper burial. 52. Keep the candle somewhere safe until you can bury it. 53. Dig a small hole in the ground on your property, or bury it in a large potted plant or a place sacred to you. 54. Thank the candle for its efforts. 55. You can do this spell as many times as you like, though use a new candle and new sticky note each time. 56. If it feels right to use the same magickal extras, do so. 57. Otherwise, switch things up with different ingredients. 58. If one candle spell is not enough, such as if you have more than one partner or several friendships that could benefit from a boost, you can do this spell for multiple people as long as you use one candle per person per sticky note.
◇Description
A multi-week candle spell to enhance an existing loving relationship, whether romantic or platonic. Uses a yellow candle for platonic love or red candle for romantic love (pink works for either), lit on Sundays for platonic or Fridays for romantic relationships during favorable moon phases. The spell strengthens bonds that already exist without influencing free will.

This spell enhances a loving relationship in your life, whether that love is romantic or platonic in nature. Typically, before doing spellwork to influence a person in your life, you should ask for permission. However, you are not influencing anyone's free will with this spell, and for that reason, you do not need to get permission. This spell is a bit like sprinkling fertilizer on soil: if there's already a seed planted, fertilizer will help it grow, but if there is nothing in the soil, fertilizer has no effect. You are magickally strengthening the bond that already exists between you, and the success or failure of your spell may reveal something about your relationship.

Magickal Extras

These optional ingredients can boost your candle magick. Feel free to use as many as you like, or none at all.

Carving gives the candle a clear directive. Carve while the wax is dry and unblemished, before doing anything else to the candle. Use words that represent your spell, such as the name of the person you have in mind or the word love or friendship. Use the head of tweezers, a pin, the tip of a pocketknife, or another small point.

Carrier oil dresses the candle. Use olive oil, coconut oil, or avocado oil. Place a dollop about the size of a quarter in your dominant hand's palm, wrap your hand around the top of the candle, and slide down toward the base, smearing oil as you go. Twist your hand as you pull to coat the candle evenly.

Essential oils scent the candle. Add a few drops to your carrier oil before dressing the candle, or sprinkle directly on the wax if no carrier oil is used. Lemon and sweet orange essential oils are wonderful for friendship. To boost romantic love, use rose or lavender essential oil.

Herbs and spices adhere to an oil-dressed candle. Sprinkle ground cumin, ginger, cocoa, or lavender on a plate and roll the dressed candle across it once or twice.

Crystals arranged around the base strengthen the magick. For boosting platonic love, use moss agate, aventurine, or turquoise. For romantic love, use rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst, or moonstone. Cleanse crystals before use with Full Moon light, Florida Water, or selenite.

Tarot cards placed near the candle invite in that energy. If boosting platonic love, select a card depicting friendship, such as the Three of Cups. If boosting romantic love, use a card that signifies partnership, such as the Two of Cups, or a significator card you associate with your partner.

Timing and Repetition

Light the candle for the first time on a Sunday if boosting platonic love, or on a Friday if boosting romantic love. Ideally, the day would also be a New Moon or waxing Moon. Avoid lighting during Mercury retrograde or eclipses. On subsequent weeks, relight your candle on Sundays for platonic love or Fridays for romantic love until the candle burns through or you no longer feel the need for it.

Disposal

When the candle has done its job, give it a proper burial. Keep the candle somewhere safe until you can bury it. Dig a small hole in the ground on your property, or bury it in a large potted plant or a place sacred to you. Thank the candle for its efforts. You can repeat this spell as many times as you like using a new candle and new sticky note each time. If one candle spell is not enough, you can do this spell for multiple people as long as you use one candle per person per sticky note.
