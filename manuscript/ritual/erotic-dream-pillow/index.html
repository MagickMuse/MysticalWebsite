A dream pillow filled with one or more botanicals known to stimulate sensual and erotic dreams. Any combination of the listed plants may be used. Note: tagetes should not be included in a bath infusion variant.

Quantities

  • One or more of: agarwood, anise, cardamom, catnip, cloves, coriander seed, damiana, gardenia, henna, hibiscus, jasmine, myrrh, orchid, rose, marigold (tagetes), tuberose, vanilla, vervain
  • A dream pillow or bag

Directions

  1. Select one or any combination of the listed botanicals and use them to fill a small dream pillow or bag.
  2. Place it under or beside your pillow before sleep.
  3. Alternatively, place the loose dried herbs in a covered box kept by the bed and inhale the fragrance before bedtime.
  4. For a bath variation, make an infusion from any of these plants except tagetes and add it to a bath taken before sleep.
  5. Fresh cut flowers or living potted plants placed on the nightstand also release their erotic dream-stimulating fragrance while you sleep.
