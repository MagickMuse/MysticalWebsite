💤Saint Agnes' Garter Dream Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Knot MagickDreamwork
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• A left garter • A right stocking
◇Directions
1. Get into bed. 2. Twist your left garter around your right stocking, tying a knot. 3. As you knot it, petition Saint Agnes in a murmur: 4. This knot I knot Helps me see The face of who my true love will be. 5. Place the knotted bundle beneath your pillow and go to sleep.
◇Description
A charm worked on Saint Agnes' Eve in which a garter is knotted around a stocking while petitioning Saint Agnes to reveal the face of one's true love in dreams. The knotted bundle is placed under the pillow before sleep.
