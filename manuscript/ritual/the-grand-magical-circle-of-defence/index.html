🛡️The Grand Magical Circle of Defence

◇Directions
1. Assemble everything needed for the vigil inside the circle before beginning. Exorcise all items with fire and water. 2. Mark the circle with masking tape. Draw the triple circle of light with the Athame. 3. Exorcise the ground inside the circle with water and fire, using Tellurian Incense in the thurible. East, Air: 4. Place a lamp of art just outside the circle boundary to the east and light it. Take the wand in both hands, raise it above the head, close the eyes, and slowly chant: All-wise Eagle, Great Ruler of Tempest, Storm, and Whirlwind, Master of the Heavenly Vault, Great Prince of the Powers of the Air, be present we pray thee and guard this circle from all perils approaching from the East! Visualize a wind rising from the east, steadily increasing; become aware of light shining from the east; hear the creaking of the branches of the world tree, the beating of mighty wings. All coven members present must hold the same visualization. South, Fire: 5. Move clockwise to the south and light the lamp of art there. Take the Athame and raise it high. Visualize the midsummer sun blazing overhead; see a fiery sword, a bolt of lightning, cleave toward the south side of the circle. See a pillar of incandescent fire spring up at the circle rim; hear the thunderclap crash. Cry: O thou Lion, Lord of Lightnings, Master of the Solar Orb, Great Prince of the powers of Fire! Be present, we pray thee, and guard this circle from all perils approaching from the south! West, Water: 6. Move clockwise to the west and light the lamp. Sprinkle a few drops of salt water from the chalice to the west. Visualize a mighty glass-green sea opening beneath the feet; the full moon hanging low over the waters; cold mist rising; the sound of waves. Cry: O thou Serpent of Old, Ruler of the Deeps, Guardian of the Bitter Sea, Prince of the Powers of Water, be present we pray thee and guard this circle from all perils approaching from the west! North, Earth: 7. Move clockwise to the north and light the lamp. Take the pentacle-paten (not the Pentacle of Protection being worn), close the eyes, and sprinkle a few grains of salt from the disc outside the circle. Visualize the earth trembling and rocking, splitting asunder, a yawning chasm opening. Cry: Black Bull of the North, Horned One, Dark Ruler of Mountains and all that lies beneath them, Prince of the Powers of Earth, be present we pray thee, and guard this circle from all perils approaching from the north! Sealing, Centre: 8. Return to the centre, face east, stand with legs apart and arms extended wide on either side. Close the eyes and chant: So mote it be! Visualize a brilliant rotating wheel of fire within the breast, throwing spears of scintillating light outward in all six directions: north, south, east, west, above, and below. This stance enacts the five-pointed star, symbolizing the reconciliation of the four elemental powers by the quintessence, the alchemical holy centre or stillpoint of the wheel. The Hebrew word Ararita may also be chanted at this point, signifying: The One; principle of His unity; the principle of His oneness; His changing form being One. 9. Place the mandragore or alraun just within the circle perimeter to the north, with its head pointing north toward the place of greatest darkness. 10. Settle in for the vigil. The circle is adequate defence against the worst likely attack. Sleep in peace within it. Daily Protective Circle (when out and about): Trace an imaginary circle of blue light around yourself deosil with the Athame in the air and chant: I [N] hereby surround myself with this circle of protection across which no hostile power dare set its foot. Renew every twelve hours.
◇Description
A defensive circle cast to exclude hostile currents during an active magickal attack, sealed by invoking the four Watchtower guardians at the cardinal quarters. Once cast it must not be broken for any reason, and the practitioner may spend the entire night within it in safety.
