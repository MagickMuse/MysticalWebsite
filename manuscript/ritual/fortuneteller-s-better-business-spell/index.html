A spell to improve business for fortunetellers, seers, and prophets. Best cast on one of Saint Agabus' feast days but can be performed at any time.

Quantities

  • 1 candle (any color, though purple or white are associated with Saint Agabus)
  • Divination tools representing your trade (cards, runes, astrological charts, pendulum, etc.)

Directions

  1. Dedicate a candle to Saint Agabus.
  2. Hold the candle in both hands, focus on your needs and desires, and make your plea to the saint.
  3. Light the candle.
  4. Place something representing your trade next to or before the candle: cards, runes, or astrological charts, for instance.

Purpose

This spell improves business for fortunetellers, seers, prophets, and anyone who works in the divinatory arts. It invokes the blessing of Saint Agabus, the prophet who accurately foretold the famine of 45 CE and warned Saint Paul of his capture.

Best Timing

This spell is best cast on one of Agabus' feast days:

  • February 13 (Roman Catholic)
  • March 8 (Orthodox)
  • April 8 (Orthodox)

However, it can be performed at any time and whenever needed.

The Spell

  1. Dedicate the candle: Hold a candle and dedicate it to Saint Agabus. Purple or white candles align with his colors, but any candle will work.
  2. Focus and petition: Hold the candle in both hands, focus on your needs and desires, and make your plea to the saint. Be specific about what kind of business improvement you seek.
  3. Light the candle: As you light it, visualize your divination practice flourishing.
  4. Display your tools: Place something representing your trade next to or before the candle: tarot cards, runes, astrological charts, a pendulum, a crystal ball, or whatever tools you use in your practice.

Offerings to Agabus

In addition to candles, Saint Agabus appreciates:

  • Offerings on behalf of the poor or needy
  • Providing a free reading to someone in need in his honor
