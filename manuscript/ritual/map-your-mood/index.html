A reflective practice to honor the aspects of Deer energy you are currently expressing, acknowledging the triad of fawn (innocence), doe (mothering), and buck (virility) within yourself.

Quantities

Images, for a mood board
A board, or an online collage or mood board application

Directions

  1. Reflect on which face of Deer you are currently wearing.
  2. Ask yourself: Are you feeling innocent and new?
  3. Protective and nurturing?
  4. Robust and virile?
  5. What is the energy of this season of your life?
  6. Create a mood board to honor the aspects of Deer you are currently expressing.
  7. You can do this the old-fashioned way by collecting images and tacking them on a board, or with an online collage or mood board application.
  8. This practice acknowledges where you are at and reveals how your creative energy is flowing.
