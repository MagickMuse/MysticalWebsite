🐰Ostara🌸Spring📅Spring Equinox

Combines pyromancy with fresh bay laurel leaves to predict the future. Throw leaves into fire while thinking of question. Crackling fire = favorable timing, smoldering/silent = unfavorable. One flame = single-minded, two flames = need friend's help, three flames = amicable conclusion. Fun group activity around bonfire.

Quantities

  • Fresh bay laurel leaves (not dried)
  • Fireproof container (cauldron) OR open fire (bonfire or fireplace)
  • Optional: 2 charcoal tablets if using cauldron

Directions

  1. Daphnomancy combines pyromancy with bay laurel leaves to predict the future.
  2. You will need fresh (not dried) leaves for this type of divination and either a fireproof container or an open fire of some sort (bonfire or fireplace).
  3. If you use a fireproof container such as a cauldron, you can use a couple of charcoal tablets as your fire source.
  4. Get the tablets lit and brightly glowing before adding a few leaves, or go ahead and throw them into an open fire while thinking of your question.
  5. If the leaves catch fire and crackle as they burn, timing is favorable.
  6. If flames smolder, suffocate, and die out, or if leaves burn silently without crackling, timing is not favorable.
  7. Flames rising together are a good sign.
  8. Only one flame point means you are being single-minded, or you may need to be single-minded.
  9. Two flame points visible means you are going to need the help of a friend.
  10. Three flame points burning means your outcome will have an amicable conclusion.
  11. Sitting around a bonfire taking turns reading the burning leaves for yourself, family, and/or friends can be a fun way to spend your equinox evening.
  12. Not to mention, the burning of the leaves smells pretty good.
