A mojo bag of Fiery Wall of Protection Powder dressed with two protection oils and holding an image of Archangel Michael or a tiny sword, renewed weekly. ⚠️ Fiery Wall of Protection Extra Strength Mojo is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Fiery Wall of Protection Powder (a handful)
Fiery Wall of Protection Oil
Protection Oil
Medallion of Archangel Michael and/or a tiny doll sized sword (a fancy toothpick works)
Charm bag

Directions

  1. Put a handful of Fiery Wall of Protection Powder in a charm bag.
  2. Drizzle it with Fiery Wall of Protection Oil and Protection Oil.
  3. Add a medallion of Archangel Michael, a tiny sword, or both.
  4. Carry the bag in your pocket.
  5. Every week, replace the powder and dress it with fresh oil.
  6. Cleanse, charge and consecrate the charms whenever needed.
