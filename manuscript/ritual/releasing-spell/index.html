🍃Releasing Spell

◇Form
Writing SpellCharm
◇Method
BanishingElementalChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Piece of paper Small rock or stone Small cauldron
◇Directions
1. Draw the Flight rune on a piece of paper and whisper to it what you most need to release. 2. Wrap the paper around the small rock or stone and place it on your altar. 3. Hold your hands over the bundle and chant your desire: > MOON ABOVE, > EARTH BENEATH, > BRING ME BALANCE. > BRING ME PEACE. > WHAT NO LONGER SERVES, > THIS I RELEASE. > TO THE WINDS, > THE ASHES BLOW. > AND I TAKE FLIGHT > TO SOAR, TO GROW. 4. After reciting the chant, burn the charm (rock wrapped in paper) in the small cauldron. 5. Allow the cauldron to cool completely, then retrieve the rock, washing away any carbon and scorch marks under running water. 6. Take the cauldron and rock outside. Stand with the wind behind you, pour the ashes into your hand, and blow them into the air in the direction of the wind. 7. Dust off your hands and prepare for new things to enter your life. Leave the rock behind.
◇Description
A ritual to purge things that no longer serve you, creating space for new manifestations, using the Flight rune and the element of air.
