🧴Bottle🧽ClothWax

A contraceptive working disguised in the form of a witch bottle. Clothing stained with menstrual blood is washed and the rinse water is collected and sealed into a bottle with red wax, then buried in a secure location where it will not be disturbed.

Quantities

Clothing stained with menstrual blood
A bottle
Red wax

Directions

  1. Wash clothing that has been stained with menstrual blood.
  2. Collect the bloody rinse water and pour it into a bottle.
  3. Seal the bottle closed with red wax.
  4. Bury it in a safe place where it will remain undisturbed.

The witch bottle is one of the most enduring formats in European folk magic. Traditionally used as a protective device (filled with sharp objects and personal matter to deflect harm back to its source), the form is adapted here toward a different purpose. The contraceptive intention replaces the protective one, but the structure and logic remain: something personal is sealed inside, the seal holds the working, and burial keeps it stable.

Menstrual blood is already a substance of extraordinary resonance in fertility magic, used across dozens of traditions as a carrier of reproductive power. Here, the rinse water from blood-stained clothing, the diluted and washed-away form of that substance, is collected and enclosed. The washing takes something that was active and makes it residual. The sealed bottle preserves that residual form.

Red wax is the most traditional sealing material for bottles that carry blood-related workings. Red is the color of the substance being worked with. The seal locks the intention inside in both a literal and symbolic sense.

Burial is the holding mechanism. As long as the bottle remains buried and undisturbed, the working persists. Disturbance, whether accidental or intentional, disrupts the spell.
