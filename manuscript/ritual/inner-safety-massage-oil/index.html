Sometimes danger comes from within. When anger and rage threaten to overwhelm, this calming oil massaged onto the body, especially the back of the neck, offers a sense of inner safety.

Quantities

Chamomile essential oil
Spearmint essential oil
Lavender essential oil
Sweet almond oil (base)
A few drops of cherry plum flower essence

Directions

  1. Add chamomile, spearmint and lavender essential oils to a base of sweet almond oil.
  2. Add a few drops of cherry plum flower essence.
  3. Massage onto the body as the need arises, especially the back of the neck.
