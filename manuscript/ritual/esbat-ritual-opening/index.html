🕯️Esbat Ritual Opening

◇Tradition
Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Directional MagickElementalInvocationLunar
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Advanced
◇Quantities
- 3 altar candles arranged left-center-right: - Full Moon: blue-white-orange - New Moon: green-white-red - Dark Moon: black-purple-black or black-white-black - Blue Moon: blue-white-orange - Sidhe Moon: pale lavender or gray outer candles, lighter grayish-white center - Blood Moon: russet orange-red-russet orange (for Power workings) - Incense (sandalwood, frankincense, or moon-appropriate blend) - Blessed water from circle casting - Pentacle - Athame - Censer with incense - Beverage cup - Cauldron for libation - Working candle appropriate to the moon phase - Spell materials as needed
◇Directions
1. Begin this opening at the designated point in your circle casting: after the Basic Circle for Full and New Moon Esbats, or after the Dark Power Circle for Dark Moon Esbats. 2. Raise wand with arms open in greeting: 3. I, [Name], who am Your child, stand between the worlds and call upon my Lady and my Lord to hold communion with me. 4. Clap or ring bell three times: 5. "An it harm none, do what ye will." Thus runs the Witch's Rede. 6. Once more I affirm my joy of life and my love for the Lady and the Lord. 7. I honor the Goddess and the God, [Names], for the favors and love They have bestowed upon me, and I ask Their blessings upon me. 8. Place the blessed water from circle casting on the pentacle and point the athame toward it: 9. Great Mother, bless this creature of Water and of Earth to Your service. 10. May I always remember the cauldron waters of rebirth and the many forms and beings of the blessed Earth. 11. Of Water and Earth am I. 12. Set down the athame and raise the water bowl: 13. Great Mother, I honor You! 14. Return the water bowl to the altar. 15. Place the censer on the pentacle and point the athame toward it: 16. Great Father, bless this creature of Fire and of Air to Your service. 17. May I always remember the sacred fire that dances within every creation and may I always hear the voices of the Divine. 18. Of Fire and Air am I. 19. Set down the athame and raise the censer: 20. Great Father, I honor You! 21. Return the censer to the altar. 22. Raise the beverage cup: 23. Power and Grace; Beauty and Strength are in the Lady and the Lord both. 24. Patience and Love; Wisdom and Knowledge. 25. I honor You Both! 26. Pour a small libation into the cauldron and take a sip from the cup. 27. Proceed to the appropriate Lunar Phase Esbat Ritual for your working.
◇Description
The common opening sequence for all Esbat rituals, performed after casting the appropriate circle (Basic for Full/New Moon, Dark Power for Dark Moon). Includes elemental blessings, deity invocations, and libation before proceeding to the specific lunar phase ritual.
