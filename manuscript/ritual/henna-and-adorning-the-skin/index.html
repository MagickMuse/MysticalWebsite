🌿Mehndi Paste

A mehndi working that adapts the sacred South Asian and North African wedding tradition of henna body art for witchcraft practice. The paste is prepared from a decoction of tamarind, rose, coffee, and lime combined with powdered red henna, eucalyptus oil, and okra gel. Sigils and personal symbols are written first on parchment to charge them with intent, then applied to the skin in paste. The palms and soles of the feet absorb the dye most deeply, making them the primary working surfaces. The spell is spoken during the parchment-writing stage. Designs oxidize and last up to three weeks.

Quantities

  • 4 slices tamarind fruit or 3–4 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 3/4 cup fresh or dried red rose petals
  • 1/3 cup coffee grounds
  • 1 dried lime (prepared 2–3 weeks in advance)
  • 1 quart spring water
  • 4 oz dried and powdered red henna
  • 3 drops eucalyptus essential oil
  • 2 fresh okra pods
  • Parchment paper and pen, nylon stocking, cheesecloth, craft bottle, cotton and plastic wrap

Directions

  1. Prepare the Mehndi Paste using the linked recipe.
  2. While the decoction simmers, take parchment paper and a pen and write out the symbols, sigils, and intentions you wish to carry on your skin: elemental, zodiacal, planetary, or symbols of your own making.
  3. As you write, speak the incantation:
  4. PASSED DOWN FROM WOMAN TO WOMAN
    OUR CONNECTION IS ONE OF SKIN TO SKIN
    FROM MANY LANDS, FROM MANY DREAMS
    AND MANY HANDS WASHED IN MANY STREAMS
  5. THE SPELL MUST NOW BEGIN
    FROM HEART TO HAND AND HERB TO LAND
    I BLESS THE SKIN IN WHICH I LOVE
    AS IT IS BELOW, SO MUST IT BE ABOVE.
  6. Set the inscribed parchment on the floor beneath two chair legs and use the stretched nylon stocking to sift the henna over your written work, charging the powder with your intentions before mixing the paste.
  7. Once the paste is complete and transferred to the craft bottle, trace over your parchment designs to practice the lines.
  8. Then apply to skin; the palms and soles of the feet receive the deepest, darkest result.
  9. Cover the design with cotton and plastic wrap to protect it while it sets.
  10. Leave it as long as possible, overnight if you can.
  11. Brush the dry paste away without water and allow the design to darken over the following 24 hours.
  12. As you wear the design, it continues to work.
  13. Each time you see the sigils on your skin, you reinforce the intention.
