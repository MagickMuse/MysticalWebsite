🏠Sell Your House Quickly

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Incense MagickSmoke MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A small ceramic bowl - Six small pieces of blue howlite - One piece of white howlite - A rose or frankincense incense stick
◇Directions
1. Shake the bowl seven times fast, saying seven times: "Fast will this sale be, folks will flock to this property. Right price, right location, it will sell, no hesitation." Add the crystals to the bowl, hold it high, and repeat the spell words seven times even faster. 2. Light the incense stick and over the bowl write in incense smoke as fast as you can the address of the property and "SOON IT WILL BE SOLD WITH NO HESITATION." Put the white howlite over the lintel of the door to the main living area or, if it won't fit, on the highest object in the room. 3. Put the blue howlites on downstairs window ledges (if you have a really small apartment, arrange them along the inside of the largest window ledge). 4. For each, as you set it, say the words: "Soon it will be sold with no hesitation fast." Let the incense burn through.
◇Description
A before open house ritual to sell a house fast in a buyer's market using blue and white howlite crystals arranged in a bowl with incense smoke writing to draw buyers quickly and overcome hesitation.
