🚪Banishment🛡️Protection

The single most effective method for banishing vermin requires no physical ingredients, only visualization. The practitioner travels in a meditative state to the queen of the pest species and formally issues an ultimatum: vacate by a stated deadline or face extermination. The queen must be perceived as equal to or larger than the practitioner, and the interaction must be conducted with respectful firmness.

Quantities

  • No physical materials required; visualization only

Directions

  1. Enter a meditative or trance state and visualize traveling to the palace of the queen of your pest species, which corresponds to whatever home that species naturally uses (a hive, a nest, etc.).
  2. You will be met by guards and courtiers; insist you have an urgent message for the queen and refuse to deliver it to anyone else.
  3. Once admitted to her presence, speak respectfully but firmly.
  4. State clearly that if her creatures have not vacated your specified premises by your stated deadline, you are not responsible for what befalls them.
  5. You may request a gift from the queen before departing if you prefer not to call an exterminator.
  6. Leave via the same route you arrived and wait until the deadline before taking further action.
