A breathwork practice to gather scattered energy throughout your body and consciously bring it into your center, somewhere between your belly button and diaphragm. Centering calms an anxious mind and prepares you for magickal work.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Stop what you are doing and find a comfortable position.
  2. Understand it will feel strange to pause for a few minutes, and your mind may tell you there are better uses of your time.
  3. There are not.
  4. The tasks will still be there when you finish, and this practice reduces stress while making it easier for your brain to access creativity and stored information.
  5. Breathe in slowly through your nose and out through your mouth for at least four breaths.
  6. Try to breathe out longer than your slow inhale.
  7. You might yawn, which is normal.
  8. Your body is not used to receiving this much oxygen, so it needs to confirm this is not a trick.
  9. These are belly breaths that push your abdomen out rather than raising your chest.
  10. During daily life, most people breathe only in the top of their lungs, using perhaps ten percent of their lung capacity.
  11. While breathing mindfully, do not worry about groceries, work, or dishes.
  12. Instead, mentally gather all of your scattered energies throughout your body and consciously bring them into your center, the space between your belly button and your diaphragm.
  13. Feel your awareness consolidate into this single point.
  14. When thoughts arise, acknowledge them and let them pass, returning your focus to the breath and the center.
  15. When you feel gathered and present, you are centered and ready to proceed with your work or your day.
