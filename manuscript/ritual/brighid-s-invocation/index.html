🕯️Brighid's Invocation

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
InvocationChant/MantraSolar

Beginner
◇Quantities

◇Directions
1. I call thee, Brighid, triple goddess who rules over hearth and healing and inspiration. Share your gifts with me on this Summer Solstice day, and guide me with your gentle wisdom. Let your light warm my soul and brighten my spirit. So mote it be. 2. I call thee, Belinos, god of the sun, whose powerful rays bring healing and strength. Share your gifts with me on this Summer Solstice day, and empower me with your energy. Let your light warm my soul and brighten my spirit. So mote it be.
◇Description
A Celtic Midsummer invocation for healing, inspiration, and solar strength, addressed to Brighid and Belenus.
