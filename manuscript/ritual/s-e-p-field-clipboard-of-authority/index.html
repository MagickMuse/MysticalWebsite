📋S.E.P. Field & Clipboard of Authority

Two quick projection techniques for being left alone in public. The S.E.P. Field projects 'Somebody Else's Problem' energy; the Clipboard of Authority projects an 'on a mission' aura that deters interruption.

Quantities

None required (or an actual clipboard, optional for the Clipboard of Authority technique)

Directions

  1. To cast the S.E.P. Field: cast a small bubble around yourself and steadily project the energy of "someone else will deal with whatever is going on here." You will not be invisible, but you will move through spaces unbothered by people without authority over you.
  2. To use the Clipboard of Authority: either carry an actual clipboard or project the etheric image of one, projecting the aura of someone "on a mission." This works at work, in parks, on busy streets, or anywhere you need to move undisturbed.
  3. Strengthen either projection with a furrowed brow or focused expression.

The S.E.P. Field (Lesser Invisibility)

This technique exploits the natural human tendency to ignore things that don't personally affect them. The concept originates from Douglas Adams' The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, where a generator projects "Somebody Else's Problem" energy around an object.

The Clipboard of Authority

Similar to the S.E.P. Field, this technique projects the aura of someone "on a mission." People recognize the clipboard-bearer as someone with purpose and steer clear. The assumption of official business or sales deters approach.

Both techniques require no materials and can be deployed instantly in any public situation.
