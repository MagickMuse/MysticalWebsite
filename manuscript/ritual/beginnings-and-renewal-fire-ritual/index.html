A fire ritual for new beginnings or renewal, burning a written intention anointed with pine oil in a pine fire or with flash paper.

Quantities

  • Pine wood or pine incense
  • Paper
  • Pen
  • Pine essential oil (diluted) or pine needles
  • Optional: flash paper for fire-free alternative

Directions

  1. Build a fire of pine wood or burn pine incense to create sacred space.
  2. On a piece of paper, write out the new beginning you desire or describe the manner of renewal you seek.
  3. Be specific and write in the present tense as if it has already come to pass.
  4. Anoint the paper with diluted pine essential oil or place a few pine needles on top of the paper.
  5. Burn the paper in the fire while visualizing your intention being carried to the universe with the rising smoke.
  6. If fire is difficult in your current situation, use flash paper instead.
  7. There will be no heat, smoke, or ashes left behind, making this a discreet option for any living situation.
