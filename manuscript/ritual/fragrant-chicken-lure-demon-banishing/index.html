Creativity and flexibility are the keys to successful demon banishing. Low-level demons are both greedy and unintelligent, making them easy to manipulate. A traditional Chinese method from a ship in Singapore illustrates this: the cook prepared the most fragrant, delicious chickens possible and threw them overboard, luring the malicious spirits out after the food. Fireworks were then detonated to ensure the demons did not return.

Quantities

  • Chickens (prepared as fragrantly and deliciously as possible)
  • Fireworks

Directions

  1. Identify and exploit the demons' weakness, whether greed, stupidity, or susceptibility to specific repellents.
  2. In the Chinese method: prepare the most fragrant, delicious chickens possible.
  3. When the aroma has fully tempted the spirits, throw the chickens out of the infested space.
  4. The demons will follow the food.
  5. Immediately detonate fireworks to drive them away and discourage return.
