An Animal Ally altar spell for wealth. Surrounding yourself with images of frog, toad, snake, dragon, cat, rat, rabbit, and fish generates cashflow and financial inspiration.

Quantities

Images or statues of wealth animal allies (frog, toad, snake, dragon, cat, rat, rabbit, fish)

Directions

  1. Surround yourself with images of the Animal Allies of wealth and financial fortune: frog, toad, snake, dragon, cat, rat, rabbit, and fish.
  2. Set up an Animal Ally Altar following the directions in Elements of Magick Spells (page 107) to generate cashflow and financial inspiration.
