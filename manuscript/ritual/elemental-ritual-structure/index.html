A comprehensive framework for creating and performing color and elemental magick rituals, including circle casting, altar setup, and working with the four directions.

Quantities

Candles (red, yellow, blue, green, plus one in your elemental color)
Incense
Optional: feathers or fresh fragrant flowers (for Air)
Container of water
Crystals or stones (for Earth)
Objects relating to your request (e.g., dollar bills for money, photos for your goal)
Small object in your elemental color (to keep after the ritual)
Altar cloth in a color matching your working
Optional: bath salts (for the bathing ritual)

Directions

  1. Choose a quiet, undisturbed location; if desired, take a cleansing bath first, then dress in clean, loose clothing (or work skyclad, by choice).
  2. Set up your altar (cloth matching your working, facing east or north) with candles, incense, crystals, a container of water, request-related objects, and a small object in your elemental color.
  3. Cast your magickal circle, either physically (drawn, a rug, or elemental materials) or visualized, moving clockwise starting from the East.
  4. Step into the circle, face east, and optionally call upon the four archangels (Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, Uriel).
  5. Face east: visualize yellow filling a quarter of the circle and say, "Element of Air, I salute you and thank you for your help in my request."
  6. Face south: visualize red filling another quarter and say, "Element of Fire, I salute you and thank you for your help in my request."
  7. Face west: visualize blue filling the third quarter and say, "Element of Water, I salute you and thank you for your help in my request."
  8. Face north: visualize green completing the circle and say, "Element of Earth, I salute you and thank you for your help in my request."
  9. Face east again, sensing the complete four-colored circle of protection.
  10. Focus on your elemental object and make your request aloud: light a candle for Fire; hold incense, feathers, or flowers for Air; hold a crystal for Earth; or bless and drink a glass of water for Water.
  11. Give thanks to the universe for making your request a reality, as if it has already been accomplished.
  12. Close the circle by thanking each direction in turn, moving counterclockwise from the east, sensing the circle dissolve, then extinguish any candles or incense.
  13. Ground yourself by eating or drinking something, then reflect briefly before continuing your day.
  14. Repeat the ritual at least once a week until your request manifests.

This ritual provides a complete framework for performing elemental and color magick workings.

Moon Phase Timing

Waxing Moon (growing): Use for rituals of increase, attraction, growth, or bringing something into your life.

Waning Moon (decreasing): Use for rituals of decrease, banishing, releasing, or removing something from your life.

Day of the Week Correspondences

You can align your ritual with specific days that correspond to color energies:

  • Monday: White
  • Tuesday: Red
  • Wednesday: All colors
  • Thursday: Blue
  • Friday: Green
  • Saturday: Black
  • Sunday: Yellow

Elemental Tools and Materials

Gather objects representing the element most connected to your intention:

Fire: Candles and small red objects

Air: Incense, feathers, fresh fragrant flowers, and small yellow objects

Water: Water and small blue objects

Earth: Crystals, stones, pottery, and small green objects

The Four Archangels

If you have worked with the four archangels before, you may call upon them before invoking the directions: Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, and Auriel.

The Power of the Small Object

The small colored object you placed on your altar becomes imbued with the circle's energy during your ritual. Each time you see or hold it afterward, its color reminds you of the ritual and adds additional power to your request.
