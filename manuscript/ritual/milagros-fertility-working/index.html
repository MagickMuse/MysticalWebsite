👜Charm BagMilagro

A fertility working using milagros, small magickal charms traditionally offered at shrines, shaped to represent the womb, ovaries, heart, baby, or hedgehog. The charms are charged with desire and presented at a shrine as both petition and promised payment, following the ancient magickal law of reciprocity.

Quantities

Milagro charm(s) in a fertility shape (uterus, ovaries, heart, baby, or hedgehog)
Optional: charm bag
Optional: rags (if no shrine is accessible, to tie to a personal tree or well)

Directions

  1. Select a milagro in a shape that represents your desire. Fertility milagros include the uterus, ovaries, the heart (synonymous with the vulva and the love offered to a child), a baby, or a hedgehog (from the old European belief that the womb resembles one).
  2. Charge the milagro by one or more of these methods: holding it in both hands while concentrating fully on the desire; sleeping with it under the pillow; weeping onto it until it is wet with tears; or carrying it in a charm bag for seven days and nights until it is saturated with personal energy.
  3. Bring the charged milagro to a shrine as a petition, stating clearly what blessing is desired and precisely what will be offered in return when the miracle is received.
  4. If no shrine is accessible, tie the milagro or a rag to a personal tree or well as the point of petition.
  5. When the miracle is received, render the promised payment without delay.

Milagros (Spanish: "miracles") are tiny magickal charms, most commonly formed from metal, depicting a specific part of the body or a symbolic shape. They are the modern continuation of the ancient ex-voto tradition, with roots in Greek, Celtic, and Iberian healing shrines. Today the practice is most vigorous in Latin America.

The Magickal Principle

The working operates on the law of magickal reciprocity:

  • A petition is made and a specific gift is promised in exchange for the miracle
  • Once the miracle is received, the promised payment is rendered

Be precise on both counts. State the desired blessing explicitly. State exactly what will be offered in return, and when.

Fertility Milagro Shapes

  • Uterus (literal)
  • Ovaries (literal)
  • Heart (the Valentine's Day shape, synonymous with the vulva; also represents the love offered to a child)
  • Baby (literal)
  • Hedgehog (based on the old European belief that the womb resembles a hedgehog)

Charging the Milagro

Charge the charm by any or all of the following:

  1. Hold it in both hands and concentrate on the desire with complete focus
  2. Sleep with it under the pillow
  3. Weep onto it until wet with tears
  4. Carry it in a mojo bag for seven consecutive days and nights

Presenting the Petition

Bring the charged milagro to a shrine. Shrines typically have a velvet board or the votive image itself for pinning ex-votos. If no shrine is accessible, tie the milagro or a rag to a personal tree or well.

ℹ️
Milagros are commonly offered in two stages: a modest charm at the time of petition, with a promise of a finer or more valuable one upon receipt of the miracle. Render payment promptly.
