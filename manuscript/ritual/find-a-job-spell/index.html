🏢Find a Job Spell

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
ManifestationCandle Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 figure candle - Open-topped box (decorated as ideal workplace) - Carving tool
◇Directions
1. Designate and carve a figure candle to represent you, include your name, identifying info, a badge on the chest, or the image of a briefcase. 2. Build a model of your ideal workplace from an open-topped box; decorate as desired with a door large enough for the candle to enter. 3. Face the candle toward the workplace. 4. Burn in 30-minute increments for six consecutive waxing moon days, moving the candle ever closer to the workplace. 5. On the seventh day, bring the candle within the workplace and let it burn completely.
◇Description
An arts-and-crafts candle spell using a carved figure candle to represent the job-seeker and a model workplace built from an open-topped box. Burned in 30-minute increments over six consecutive waxing moon days, the candle moves ever closer to the workplace model. On the seventh day it is brought inside and burned completely.
