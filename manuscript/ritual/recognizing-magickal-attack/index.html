Recognizing Magickal Attack

Understanding the sources of magickal attack and crossed conditions enables proper diagnosis and response. Attack rarely appears from nowhere. Recognizing its origin allows you to select appropriate protective measures and remedies.

Before You Assume You're Cursed

True curses are rare. They require time, attention, effort, and often specialized supplies and a knowledgeable practitioner. Most people who fear they've been cursed are simply experiencing a run of bad luck, which can be addressed through refreshing protections.

Own your magick. Your magick belongs to you alone. Don't give it away by telling yourself you've been cursed. Stand in your power and recognize your strength. Laugh (yes, really) in the face of the mere idea of a curse. Laughter carries sound waves of joy and mirth. By directing laughter at a potential curse, you've already defeated nine out of ten magickal threats. Jinxes especially, being small things devoid of true intention, crumble against genuine mirth.

Ask yourself: Who would want to curse me? Hopefully the answer is simple. Most practitioners have one or two people they've argued with, but these are usually minor threats. If you have a long list of people who hate you enough to hex you, it's time to re-evaluate. What do these people have in common? If they're all social media conflicts, consider whether that's where your time is best spent. If they're people you've wronged, honesty is the only path forward. If they're people whose boundaries you've maintained, good for you. The only people who hate healthy boundaries are those who benefit from your lack of them.

Curse Threat Assessment

Check all that apply:

  • ☐ A run of bad luck
  • ☐ Unexplained pain
  • ☐ Perception of soul loss or soul theft
  • ☐ A perception of people talking about you, especially disembodied voices
  • ☐ Sudden clumsiness
  • ☐ Feelings of someone standing on your chest or arms
  • ☐ Being run-down without cause
  • ☐ Nightmares out of the ordinary
  • ☐ Feelings of being watched or followed

Grading Your Assessment

1-3 checks: Bad luck or anxiety. Refresh your home's warding and protections. Vary your cleansing methods on a regular schedule: asperge with full moon water and a pine branch one month, bang pots and pans together the next, burn mugwort the month after. Varying practice prevents the magickal equivalent of a resistant infection, keeps your methods unpredictable, and accounts for the fact that friends don't always stay in your life forever.

4-6 checks: Possible jinx. A salt bath or shower blessing wouldn't hurt. Sound is excellent for cleansing: bells, tuning forks, or loud music all help. Layer your home protections with three, six, or nine different types of magickal barriers. No single method is proof against all threats.

7-9 checks: Possible curse or hex. Cleanse yourself first with herbal washes or waters, then cleanse your home several times using different methods in logical order (smoke before floor wash, so you can clean any ash). Leave offerings for your house spirit: coffee, bread, porridge, or wine. They are your first line of defense, but they need relationship. A cup of coffee weekly can save you a lot in repair bills.

10 checks: Seek professional help. This is too much for one person. Therapeutic and magickal interventions go hand in hand. Seek recommendations for an ethical practitioner from a trusted friend or local metaphysical shop.

Four Primary Sources

Occult attack and crossed conditions generally stem from one of four sources, each requiring different approaches to resolution.

1. Offended Spirits

Humans share this planet with numerous spiritual beings and intelligences. All space carries awareness and energy. Through thoughtless actions that disturb the natural environment, practitioners risk upsetting these spirits and earning their wrath.

Common offenses include burning and dumping waste, damming rivers and lakes, building in sacred spaces, and other disruptions to natural environments. These spirits exist as energy and awareness, affecting people on subtle layers that filter into physical symptoms through the immune and nervous systems. They also influence emotional disposition and thought processes.

Traditional medicine and shamanism across cultures address maladies caused by these spirits. Resolution typically requires:

  • Treating physical symptoms
  • Exorcising the offending spirits
  • Making appropriate offerings
  • Asking forgiveness from the spirits

2. Places of Power

Locations carrying concentrated spiritual energy or housing powerful entities can harm the unprepared. Modern life has dulled most people's psychic sensitivity, allowing them to pass through such places unaffected. However, those with even slight sensitivity may experience sudden, overwhelming amplification of awareness when entering these spaces, leaving them vulnerable to forces they never knew existed.

Naturally powerful sites include ley-line nexuses and fairy mounds where geo-psychic energy gathers.

Human-empowered sites include ancient shrines, graveyards, or locations where psychically potent events occurred: murder, assault, or even séances performed long ago.

Even benign powers can overwhelm someone unprepared for the shift in perception. These places can ignite latent psychic potential without warning, creating shock and vulnerability.

Risk for practitioners: Occultists who regularly work with unseen forces encounter these dangers more frequently than ordinary people. Even common practices like casting circles, scrying, divining, and raising power illuminate the astral plane and attract attention from subtle realm inhabitants. Advanced experimental work increases risk, but even basic practices can cause problems for some people, particularly those with existing emotional or mental health challenges.

3. Missteps in Practice

Broken vows, improper ritual closure, neglected obligations to spirits, or other errors in occult practice create openings for negative consequences. This category includes:

  • Broken promises to deities or spirits
  • Abandoned workings left incomplete
  • Violated taboos within specific traditions
  • Improper handling of spiritual contracts
  • Failed protective measures during dangerous work

These attacks stem not from external malice but from the practitioner's own errors creating vulnerability or invoking natural consequences.

Broken vows deserve special attention. Promises made during initiations, self-empowerment ceremonies, or solitary vows to gods and spirits will rebound if broken. When vows invoke powerful guardians of a coven or magickal order, breaking them can escalate from simple crossed conditions to full attacks from the very guardians that once protected you. Symptoms include spells ceasing to work and noticeable loss of vitality.

Resolution requires creating a rite of apology and offering, potentially renouncing the vow if it cannot be kept. Be discerning about what you agree to before making spiritual commitments.

4. Attacks from Other Practitioners

Deliberate magickal assault from another person represents the most commonly feared but least common source. Despite claims that "real" witches never harm others or that karma prevents magicians from cursing, history and practice prove otherwise. Curses and bindings have always been part of witchcraft, from Greek defixiones tablets to traditional rootwork to modern practitioners worldwide.

Reality of harmful magick: Most practitioners apply the same ethical standards to magick as to other actions. If they would take mundane revenge or influence, they may use magick for the same ends. Even self-identified "white lighters" sometimes curse when they believe their cause is just. Items for harmful and coercive magick remain popular in occult shops.

Professional curse work: Practitioners can be hired to send curses, though many workers refuse such jobs without good cause. In traditional cultures, approaching a witch for both cursing and uncrossing work was common practice.

Unintentional attacks: Not all harmful magick is deliberate. Gifted individuals can manifest powerful hexes without training or intent through sheer emotional charge. The Italian Maloccio (evil eye) operates through envy, hatred, or ill-wishing. People with weak constitution can become psychic parasites, unintentionally draining vitality from those around them. These accidental attacks still require attention and remedy.

Signs include sudden and inexplicable misfortune coinciding with conflict with another practitioner, patterns of bad luck that feel targeted rather than random, or direct threats made by someone with magickal knowledge.

Diagnosis

Proper diagnosis requires honest assessment:

Consider timing: When did symptoms begin? What preceded them?

Examine your actions: Have you disturbed natural spaces, broken spiritual commitments, or come into conflict with practitioners?

Assess the pattern: Does misfortune feel random or targeted? Does it affect specific life areas or manifest as general malaise?

Rule out mundane causes: Physical illness, mental health conditions, and ordinary bad luck account for most perceived attacks. Consult appropriate medical and mental health professionals first.

Response Framework

Once you identify the source, select remedies that address the specific type of attack:

For offended spirits: Offerings, apologies, exorcism, and behavior correction

For places of power: Cleansing, grounding, protective boundaries, and avoiding the location

For practice missteps: Fulfilling obligations, closing workings properly, making amends to spirits, and correcting technique

For practitioner attacks: Protective workings, reversals, binding, and direct confrontation when necessary

Many protective rituals in this grimoire specify which sources they address. Select workings appropriate to your situation rather than applying general protection indiscriminately.

Identifying Attack

Regular banishing rituals and protections prevent most problems. Vigilant practice strengthens mind and spirit while shielding against unwanted influences. However, no protection is foolproof. Attacks, accidental or intentional, sometimes breach defenses and affect health, luck, and wellbeing. Recognizing attack when it occurs is the first step to combating it.

The Denial Problem

People resist admitting attack is happening. Non-practitioners may fear they are losing their minds rather than acknowledge occult causes. Even suspecting magickal reasons, they avoid speaking up to prevent being labeled mentally unbalanced.

Even magicians struggle to admit vulnerability. Many overestimate their abilities or believe their protections are infallible. Ego makes it difficult to admit something got through defenses. This proves especially challenging for teachers and leaders who fear losing credibility by appearing vulnerable.

Two key principles:

  1. No one is impervious to psychic attack. Basic protections defend against most accidental attacks and general malefic energy, even most intentional attacks, but no technique is foolproof regardless of initiation level or perceived power.
  2. No harm comes from defending against an attack that may not be happening, but great harm comes from ignoring a real attack. Play it safe.

Psychic Hypochondriacs

Some people convince themselves they are constantly under attack with no basis in reality. These individuals create drama through "psychic soap operas" and seek help repeatedly.

An inverse relationship exists between insistence someone is being attacked and the probability they actually are. These folks claim persecution from unknown "black lodges" and "dark magicians" with no motive beyond pure evil.

When encountering these patterns, offer simple cleansing or shielding instructions and perhaps an amulet. When they return repeatedly, gently decline by performing divination and stating you cannot detect the source of attack. This avoids contradicting their beliefs while establishing you cannot help.

Symptoms of Attack

Spiritual attack manifests in three categories: external conditions, mental conditions, and physical conditions.

External Conditions: Crossed Conditions

These attacks affect probability and luck, creating "crossed conditions." Early signs include:

  • Feeling out of step with time
  • Chronic lateness despite efforts
  • Patterns of bad luck
  • Everything touched going wrong
  • Multiple mishaps in short timeframes (several car incidents within days)
  • Objects breaking or falling when handled
  • People losing patience with you
  • Unexpected bills piling up
  • Inability to hold onto money

Left untreated, symptoms escalate: job loss, relationship dissolution, car accidents, false accusations, potential jail time. Severity depends on the attacker's power and how long conditions persist.

Each incident has rational material explanation. Taken alone, they mean nothing. Together, a string of unfortunate coincidences indicates something is wrong, especially when accompanied by mental and physical symptoms.

Mental Conditions

Most magickal and psychic attacks produce mental symptoms in targets. While suggestion is powerful and can manifest symptoms, genuine magickal attacks typically remain secret. True attackers do not tip off their targets.

Common mental symptoms:

  • Despair, oppression, anxiety, and fear without identifiable cause
  • Inexplicable confusion or inability to focus
  • Troubled dreams
  • Feeling constantly followed
  • Hearing voices when alone
  • Seeing shadows and outlines of things that are not there
  • Smelling scents without source (sulfur or rot can indicate attack)

Coercive attacks: Telepathic attacks, hypnotic attacks, and coercive magick aimed at influencing actions rather than causing harm produce primarily mental effects. These manifest as:

  • Uncharacteristic compulsions
  • New affinities or aversions
  • Acting out of character

The mind justifies these feelings as natural, making self-recognition difficult. If friends and loved ones report you are acting strangely, consider their observations seriously. Normal influence operates like ringing a doorbell from outside, attack operates like lifting floorboards and pulling the bell wires directly.

Important medical note: Many mental attack symptoms overlap with depression, schizophrenia, anxiety, ADHD, and other psychological conditions. Magickal defenses should NOT substitute for therapy and psychological treatment. These methods may be used alongside conventional treatment but must never replace medical care.

Physical Conditions

Physical symptoms can accompany attacks. Always seek medical attention for physical symptoms. Treat the symptom medically while occult defense addresses the cause.

Common physical symptoms:

Headaches: Early warning sign, especially when the scalp feels stretched too thin over the skull. When lying down, aches may gather to one side indicating the direction of attack.

Fatigue: Second most common indicator, particularly in parasitic and vampiric attacks.

  • Parasitic attacks: Someone with weak constitution unintentionally drains higher vitality from others, common between family members or close friends, especially in caregiver relationships ("the caregiver goes first")
  • Living vampires: Intentional drawing of vitality from others to add to their own power
  • Undead vampires: Spirits that have fought off astral decay and sustain themselves by feeding on the living, leaving microscopic wounds
  • Expedition Mort: Haitian Vodou curse where the target is handed to the dead. Something personal is placed in a willing spirit's grave, and grave dirt is planted on target or home. Creates terrible fatigue and troubled, unrestful sleep that can lead to death if untreated.

Weight on chest while sleeping: Called "Hag Riding," the most common complaint when spirits attack or haunt. Sometimes accompanied by feelings of sexual aggression. Unexplained bruises can develop from nighttime attacks.

Sudden persistent illness: From simple flu to cancer to undiagnosable conditions, curses affect the physical body directly. In rare cases of powerful attacks, heart attacks and aneurysms can result.

Sexual dysfunction: Loss of interest and impotency from jinxes by jealous or jilted lovers. Folk magick worldwide contains spells for removing and restoring "sexual nature."

Digestive issues: Stopped bowels are a traditional attack form found in grimoires like Albertus Magnus's Egyptian Secrets and the Icelandic Galdrabok.

Omens and Early Warnings

Beyond direct symptoms, watch for warning signs:

Dreams

The deep mind communicates through dreams. Rather than relying on dream dictionaries (each person has their own symbol set), assess overall content and character:

  • Were you being persecuted or pursued?
  • Were loved ones turning away?
  • Did you feel trapped?

Watch your dreams if you suspect attack. Talented oneiric practitioners can sometimes divine the attacker's name from dreams.

Animal Behavior and Omens

  • How do animals react to you?
  • Are you encountering more chthonic creatures (spiders, snakes)?
  • Have animals died in your yard?
  • Strange animal occurrences signal potential attack

Early Warning Systems

Plants: Keep live plants in each room. Under magickal attack, plants suffer first and will be affected before you are.

Silver: Keep silver in your shoe and around your neck (silver dimes with holes are traditional in the southern United States). Silver turns black under attack due to sulfur in curse powders like Goofer Dust.

Fresh egg on altar: Indicates and absorbs attack. Eggs go bad quickly or break under negative energy.

Amulets: When protective amulets break or get lost, their protection has been breached. Broken amulet cords signal attack.

Cooper's Color Code for Awareness

Col. Jeff Cooper developed a code of awareness that helps explain mental readiness in response to a threat, and how each scenario should affect it. This framework applies equally well to magickal threats as physical ones.

Code White - Completely unaware of your surroundings, unable to respond to a threat. This includes being asleep in your bed at home, texting, walking with head down, being lost in thought, and others.

Code Yellow - Aware of your surroundings, but not worried about any specific threat to your person. Engaged with your surroundings.

Code Orange - You've been alerted to a specific threat: this could be a car coming toward you, an angry dog coming your way, or a person invading your space in a threatening manner. Formulate your response now, before it's a threat to you.

Code Red - Immediate danger, requires active response. This could be running away, yelling, fighting, or more. Implement the plan you came up with in the previous step.

Code Black - This is a freeze response, and it is totally normal to freeze when faced with a potential threat. There is no shame in this. However, it is a learning experience. The way to train out of a freeze response is through practice. If you can go over scenarios in your mind while out and about, it can help you plan your moves ahead of time. Remember, action is always faster than reaction.

Candle Magick for Each Awareness Level

Use Cooper's color code to do some candle magick for your protection.

White - Burn a white candle anointed with diluted anise oil to light the way for pleasant dreams, the best time to be unaware of your surroundings.

Yellow - Empower a yellow candle on a Sunday with sunflower oil to alert you to magickal threats; think an early warning system. Repeat anytime you suspect something afoot.

Orange - In color magick, orange is a combination of yellow (air/thought) and red (fire/action). Use an orange candle on a Wednesday (to speed action) to spur you into action to protect yourself, family, or home.

Red - Anoint on a Tuesday with jojoba (removes obstacles) to give you the strength needed to fight back. Add a pinch of cumin for some Mars fire.

Black - Burn a black candle on a Saturday (endings) to ward off a freeze response in stressful situations. Journal responses to threats so your brain knows what to do instead.

Important Perspective

Taken individually, every symptom has a logical mundane explanation. When many symptoms and events occur within a short time, this indicates genuine attack. Err on the side of caution. Do not ignore symptoms when they occur.

Reversals and Counter-Magick

If you are vigilant in your banishings, use protective amulets, and have strong guardians, even if someone were to lay a curse directly on you, it is likely that you would not even notice. The entire attack will roll off you or be sent back to the sender automatically. There can be times, however, when normal defenses do not hold, and a more active position must be assumed to ensure the wellbeing of you and your loved ones. Thus far this page has dealt only with strictly pacifying, protective, and preventative measures. Under the majority of circumstances, these will be all that is needed. Unfortunately, in some cases, an obsessive enemy will show no sign of stopping their harassment, and you will need to resort to more heavy-handed methods to ensure successful defense. For this reason, it is important to master the techniques of reversal and counter-magick.

The Law of Three

There is a well-known axiom that has entered Wicca known as the Law of Three. This law is typically interpreted as a sort of amplified karma and states that any harm done by the witch will come back upon them three times over. According to some contacts in traditional craft, the original meaning of the Law of Three is different. The idea was that if a witch is harmed or cursed that they should send it back to the offender three times over. This ensures that the enemy, should they survive the triple reversal, will not attempt the same foolishness again. As this grimoire focuses on defense, the emphasis here is not on sending things back "three times over." It is, however, sometimes a good idea to reverse an attack upon the attacker, forcing them to fall into a trap of their own making.

Identifying Your Attacker

Though it's not always necessary, it is beneficial if you know who it is that you are reversing a curse back upon. Unfortunately, this is not always possible. While some less-adept sorcerers rely on the psychological power of someone knowing they have been cursed as part of the curse itself, a truly powerful witch will not reveal their identity, allowing the curse to work in secret. If your enemy has not shown their hand by pointing dramatically and pronouncing a curse verbally, they may have left evidence in the form of physical objects used to deliver the curse to you.

Physical Evidence:

  • Powders such as Goofer Dust and Graveyard Dust are common, as are condition powders such as Bend Over, Crossing, Commanding, and Black Arts
  • These powders are usually laid in places where you will come into contact with them or step on them, such as on the doorstep, in the car, or even on paper that is mailed to you
  • Items such as gris-gris bags, jack balls, and paper talismans can be placed in or around your home or office to deliver power to you
  • If any of these or other suspect items are found, they can be gathered and used to hone your reversal spells on the correct sender

Personal Links:

If there are no items you can find, perhaps the offender has collected something of yours to use as a link. Think about:

  • People who have been guests and used your bathroom recently where they could have gotten hair from your brush or nail clippings from the wastebasket
  • Anyone who had access to your office or living space
  • People who have offered you food or gifts (many types of curses can be delivered through food, and gifts can be dressed and consecrated to convey curses)

Ask yourself if any of these people would have reason to harm you. Assess whether they would have the ability and knowledge to place a curse themselves and, if not, whether they would pay someone to do it on their behalf. There shouldn't be a lot of candidates who fit the bill. If there are, you have bigger problems than just this one curse.

The Critical Importance of Divination

Whether you can come up with suspects using these methods, or if you just think you know who did it, you should ALWAYS do a divination before attempting a reversal geared at sending a curse back to a specific person. This cannot be stressed enough.

Why divination is essential:

  • If you pick the wrong person, they likely won't feel the effect of the reversal, but they could pick up some bad energy sent their way in the process
  • What you think is a curse might actually be crossed conditions brought on by magickal missteps or inadvertently broken oaths
  • If you attempt to reverse this back upon the sender, you will be sending it back upon yourself
  • Like two mirrors placed face to face, the reflection will go on forever, making the problem worse and worse

Divination Methods:

Write the names of suspects on paper, adding one additional name of "unknown" in case the real culprit is not on your list. Place a pendulum (a lodestone pendulum suspended by your own clipped hair works well, but a store-bought pendulum will work fine) over the first name and ask if that person is the one who placed the curse on you. Clear your mind, connect with the divine, and ask if that name is the name of the one who cursed you. If it is, the pendulum should swing wildly.

Other methods such as drawing cards or runes for each name are equally valid. If you find that you are too scattered to do an effective divination (you are potentially under a hex, after all) or if you are too emotionally invested to get an objective answer, a third party should be brought in to do the divining on your behalf.

Whatever the preferred method, you should rely on divination to confirm your suspicions and to see what outcome will be had. It may be that your reversal will do more harm than good.

A Cautionary Tale

Early in magickal practice, being the target of a nasty jinx can cause minor headaches and broken knick-knacks due to fumbled hands. After searching for curse material, finding a sigil on paper under one of the floor mats in a car can lead to reversing the jinx back upon the sender by burning a candle upside down.

A week or so later, it might come to attention that a girlfriend parted on not-so-good terms with got into a car accident and was in the hospital with serious injuries. She wasn't a serious practitioner, but after confronting her with the sigil, she might admit that she had picked up a book and decided to lay a jinx to show that the target wasn't such a good magickian.

As it happens, most of her jinx would be counteracted by daily rituals and protection measures, measures that she was not taking because she was only a casual practitioner. She received the full force of the jinx she meant to place on the target, and because she had no magickal protection, it was quite destructive.

This is a case where, if the outcome had been known through divination, the jinx would not have been reversed because causing that kind of harm to someone you know, even if they did something against you out of anger, may not align with your ethics or intent.

The lesson: Always divine before reversing. Know what you're sending and where it's going. Understand that an unprepared target will receive the full force of what you send back, and you must be willing to accept responsibility for that outcome.
