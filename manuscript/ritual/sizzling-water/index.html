🧿Sizzling Water

Form
Altar Working
Method
DivinationWater Magick
Category
Spell
Correspondences2
🧿Evil Eye🛡️Protection
Materials1
Charcoal

A fire-and-water Evil Eye removal in which water first passed over the afflicted person is poured onto a heated surface. Any hissing or sizzling that follows is understood as the water communicating about the state of the working; the sounds reward careful attention and interpretation. One of the Evil Eye Removal methods.

Quantities

  • Hot coals or a metal plate
  • An overturned jar
  • A bowl of water
  • A metal implement

Directions

  1. Build a hot surface by laying hot coals on a plate or heating a metal plate directly.
  2. Set an overturned jar on top.
  3. Fill a bowl with water, pass it over the afflicted person, then pour the water over the jar so it runs down onto the hot surface beneath.
  4. Note any sounds: hissing, sizzling, or other noise carries information about the removal's progress.
  5. Once the bowl is empty, lay a metal implement across it and leave it there until you are ready to perform a full spiritual cleansing on it.
