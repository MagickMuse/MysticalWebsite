🌙Moon Card Working

◇Form
Meditation/Visualization
◇Method
MeditationVisualizationInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- The Moon card (Major Arcana XVIII) from a Rider-Waite or thematically similar tarot deck
◇Directions
1. Remove The Moon card from a Rider-Waite tarot deck or a deck with similar imagery. 2. Place it where you can sit and meditate on the image comfortably. 3. Study the scene: the two canines at the threshold are the awaiting psychopomps; the crayfish or crab emerging from the water is the soul itself, beginning its next long journey. 4. Practice entering the card, visualize yourself stepping inside the scene. 5. Practice exiting back out again. 6. Repeat until you feel fully confident you can enter and return safely. 7. This confidence is essential to the success of the working. 8. When ready, enter the card with intention and observe what assistance presents itself to be brought back.
◇Description
A meditative working using The Moon card (Major Arcana XVIII) from a Rider-Waite deck or thematically similar deck to request psychopomp escort service, either for oneself during the dying process, or for a loved one immediately following death. The Moon card's imagery is treated as a living threshold: the two canines depicted are the awaiting psychopomps; the crayfish or crab emerging from the water represents the soul beginning its next long journey. The practitioner trains themselves to enter and exit the card's imagery safely before using it in earnest. The source acknowledges that death is a mystery, and so to some extent is this spell. One of the Escort Service to the Beyond methods.
