🕯️Requiem

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
PrayerAncestor WorkChant/Mantra
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. No preparation or materials are required. 2. Settle into a quiet state. 3. You may be standing, seated, or kneeling. 4. If praying the rosary, speak this prayer at the end of each decade in place of the Glory Be. 5. Speak the prayer aloud, once for an individual or using the plural for a group of ancestors: 6. In Latin: Requiem aeternam dona ei (eis), Domine, et lux perpetua luceat ei (eis). Requiescat (Requiescant) in pace. Amen. 7. In English: Eternal rest grant unto him/her (them), O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon him/her (them). May he/she (they) rest in peace. Amen. 8. Repeat as many times as feels right. 9. This prayer carries no prerequisites and requires no tools. 10. It can be spoken under any conditions, at any time.
◇Description
A brief, traditional Catholic prayer for the dead, petitioning for eternal rest and perpetual light for the deceased. One of the most widely used prayers for the ancestors in the Catholic tradition, it can be prayed daily or substituted for the Glory Be at the close of each rosary decade to direct the merit of that prayer toward the dead. It is effective in any language and can be adapted for singular or plural recipients.
