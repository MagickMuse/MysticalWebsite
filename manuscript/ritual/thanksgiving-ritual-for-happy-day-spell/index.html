🦃Thanksgiving Ritual for Happy Day Spell

A fourth Thursday in November early morning spell for Thanksgiving ritual for happy day, sprinkling twelve herbs and berries around bayberry candle then filling bag to scatter outdoors for luck, love, health, and joy.

⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.

Quantities

  • Small dishes of allspice, dried bay leaves, bayberries, cinnamon, cloves, dried cranberries, ginger, powdered nutmeg, dried parsley, rosemary, sage, and thyme
  • A long-burning bayberry candle in a deep candle holder
  • A small red and white festive bag

Directions

  1. Sprinkle a little of the contents of each dish around the base of the candle, saying for each one: "Herbs of luck, herbs of love, herbs of health and joy, bring to me what I do wish, that Thanksgiving I/we may enjoy." Light the candle and set it in the middle of the kitchen, saying: "Candle of luck, candle of love, candle of health and joy, bring to me what I most do wish, that Thanksgiving I/we may enjoy." Take sufficient berries and herbs to fill the bag, repeating the herb chant.
  2. Close the bag and leave it in front of the candle while you get everything ready.
  3. At mealtime, put the candle on the main table.
  4. Scatter the contents of the bag outdoors before the meal begins.
  5. You may need a new candle.
  6. Perform the fourth Thursday in November, early morning.
