🪡Lodestone Needle Spell

◇Form
Charged Object
◇Method
Energy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
• Lodestone • Command and Compel Oil • Needles (quantity as needed)
◇Directions
1. Dress a lodestone with Command and Compel Oil. 2. Place the needles upon the dressed lodestone. 3. Alternatively, dip the needles directly into the Command and Compel Oil before laying them on the lodestone. 4. Allow them to charge. 5. Whenever you need a specific person's cooperation, remove one needle from the lodestone and discreetly attach it to their clothing. 6. Hems, cuffs, and the creases in the back of garments are traditional locations for concealed attachment.
◇Description
A compelling working in which a lodestone dressed with Command and Compel Oil charges a set of needles with dominating energy. Whenever cooperation from a specific target is required, one needle is removed from the lodestone and discreetly pinned to the target's clothing.
