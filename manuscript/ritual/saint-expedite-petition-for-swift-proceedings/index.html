Sometimes legal delays are just as harmful as legal defeats. If attorneys are dragging their feet, bureaucracy is gumming up the works, or you simply need matters to move faster. Saint Expedite is your man.

Saint Expedite is the patron of urgent causes and swift deliveries. He specializes in cutting through red tape and making things happen now.

  1. Offer Saint Expedite a glass of rum and a slice of pound cake.
  2. Inform him clearly and directly that when matters proceed as you have requested, you will bring him a whole bottle of rum and a whole cake in gratitude.
  3. Light a white candle in his honor, or use a commercially manufactured Saint Expedite devotional candle if you can obtain one.
Some practitioners turn Saint Expedite's image upside down, or even suspend it upside down in the air, believing that the discomfort motivates the saint to work even faster. This is an optional but traditional approach.
Always make good on your payment when matters resolve in your favor. Saints who are not paid remember.
