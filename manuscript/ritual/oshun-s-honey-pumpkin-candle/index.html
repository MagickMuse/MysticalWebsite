🕯️Oshun's Honey Pumpkin Candle

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteJar
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickPetition
◇Category
Ritual
◇Closed Practice
Santería/Lucumí
◇Quantities
1 small pumpkin Honey A piece of paper and something to write with A yellow votive candle
◇Directions
1. Hollow out a small pumpkin. 2. Write your name and your message to Oshun on a piece of paper. 3. Smear the paper with honey, tasting the honey yourself before placing it. 4. Lay the honey-smeared paper flat inside the pumpkin. 5. Hold a yellow votive candle between both hands and charge it with your desire for fertility. 6. Place the candle inside the pumpkin on top of the paper and light it. 7. Repeat for five consecutive days using fresh materials each time. 8. Those petitioning Oshun for fertility traditionally abstain from eating pumpkin and any form of yellow squash throughout the working.
◇Description
A fertility petition to Oshun, the Yoruba orisha of sweet water, love, and the female reproductive organs, using her sacred plant the pumpkin. A personal petition is written, smeared with honey (the petitioner must taste the honey), placed inside a hollowed pumpkin, and topped with a charged yellow votive candle. Repeated for five consecutive days with fresh materials. Note: Oshun is a Yoruba orisha venerated in Santería and related African Diaspora traditions. This working draws from that tradition. Those petitioning her for fertility traditionally abstain from eating pumpkin and yellow squash for the duration.

Oshun presides over sweet water, love, and the female reproductive organs. Her domains are not separate: they are all expressions of the same principle, that which flows, nourishes, and gives life. Reproductive disorders fall under her power as directly as floods or dry rivers do.

Honey is her embodiment and her primary offering. The tasting is not optional. A petitioner who presents honey without tasting it is, in Oshun's lore, presenting something that might be poisoned, and she will not accept it. The act of tasting is simultaneously an act of trust and participation.

The pumpkin is her sacred plant and her vehicle. Its seeds are understood as her children. Hollowing the pumpkin creates a womb-like vessel, and placing the petition inside transforms it into a container for the working itself. Five is Oshun's number, which is why five consecutive days gives this spell its maximum force.

⚠️
Oshun is a Yoruba orisha venerated within Santería, Candomblé, and related African Diaspora traditions. Approach with respect for the tradition's origins.
