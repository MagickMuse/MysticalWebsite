Mabon

A Mabon honey jar working that sweetens the coming year and blesses physical and metaphorical crops. Household members write hopes for the year, seal them in honey with herbs, then burn colored candles for specific blessings before burying the jars.

Quantities

  • Glass jar(s) with flat metal lid(s)
  • Honey or maple syrup
  • Pen (any color)
  • Paper (any color)
  • Sugar
  • Sweetening herbs: rose petals, cinnamon, nutmeg, lavender, or basil
  • Votive candles: red, green, yellow, brown, or orange
  • Aluminum pan with high edge/lip

Directions

  1. Have each member of your household write down what they know is coming in the next year and what they hope to see happen, asking the God/ess that it be as good as or better than they ask.
  2. When completed, put the letters in the glass jar.
  3. If letters become especially long, you may need to establish one jar per person.
  4. Cover the letters with honey and add your mix of herbs.
  5. Speak a prayer over the jar(s) and say:
  6. May the year be sweet and gentle,
    honeyed and bright!
    Bring abundance to all the things that sustain life!
    So mote it be!
  7. Seal the jar(s) as tightly as possible.
  8. Set the jar(s) inside an aluminum pan with a high edge/lip.
  9. On top of the jar, burn a candle: red for energy, green for growth and fertility, yellow for prosperity, brown for a peaceful home, or orange for happy surprises.
  10. When the candles have burned down completely, bury the jars on your property.
  11. If you do not own property, find a favorite nature spot and set them in the ground before it freezes.
