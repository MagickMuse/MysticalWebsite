A candle garden focused on personal and professional goals, using separate candles for each objective. Invokes Ogun, the god who helps with achieving goals.

Quantities

One candle for each goal, in purple, silver, dark green, red, or orange
Eight bay leaves, as a charm
Optional offerings to Ogun: hot peppers, small bags of salt, palm fronds
Optional: an iron cauldron, small metal objects such as chains, wires, nails, or miniature tools

Directions

  1. Set aside a separate candle for each of your goals, whether work-focused, such as a raise, promotion, recognition, or better assignments, or personal, such as organization, fitness, or budgeting.
  2. Choose candle colors: purple, silver, or dark green for ambition, red for advancement, orange for success. If working with Ogun, his colors of black, dark blue, light green, and red are also appropriate.
  3. Add eight bay leaves as a charm, and if working with Ogun, traditional offerings such as hot peppers, small bags of salt, and palm fronds, plus his customary iron cauldron and small metal objects.
  4. When activating this garden, focus on moving forward and on your action plan for reaching each goal, visualizing each objective as already achieved.
  5. Take a concrete step, however small, toward each goal every day; the garden amplifies your efforts but does not replace them.

Ogun is a god who helps with achieving goals.
