💖Twin Soul Summoning

A circle-of-six ritual using pink candles and rose petals to summon your twin soul, whether known or unknown, calling them to find you and remain faithful.

Quantities

  • Six pink candles
  • One pink or red rose
  • One heatproof dish half filled with soil

Directions

  1. Begin by inscribing the following words with your dominant hand's index finger onto each of the six pink candles: "Come to me, my twin soul, come swiftly and stay with me everlastingly and faithfully."
  2. Arrange the six candles in a circle.
  3. Light the first candle you inscribed while speaking the words aloud.
  4. Continue lighting each remaining candle in sequence, repeating the invocation with each flame.
  5. Pluck a single petal from the rose and pass it briefly through the flame of the first candle, allowing it to singe.
  6. As it burns, speak the words again.
  7. Place the singed petal into the dish of soil.
  8. Repeat this process with the remaining candles, taking one petal at a time, singeing it in each flame while repeating your invocation, then dropping each into the dish.
  9. Once all petals have been burned and placed in the soil, hold the dish above the center of your candle circle.
  10. Speak the invocation one final time.
  11. Extinguish all six candles as quickly as possible by blowing them out.
  12. Take the dish outdoors and bury the singed petals in the earth.
  13. Scatter any remaining rose petals from the stem over the burial spot.
