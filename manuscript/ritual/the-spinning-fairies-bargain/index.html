🌱The Spinning Fairies' Bargain

◇Directions
1. Write the phrase 'Keshalyi lisperesn' (meaning 'Fairies spin') with glue on cardboard. 2. Sprinkle glitter or powder over the wet glue so the phrase becomes visible. 3. Let it dry, then post it somewhere you can see it regularly. 4. Make an offering of milk, fruit brandy, and cakes. 5. Begin to chant 'Keshalyi lisperesn' aloud until you have a sense of the Keshalyi's presence. 6. Speak with them, explain your need, and bargain for their assistance.
◇Description
A Romany working calling on the Keshalyi, fairy spirits traditionally invoked to prevent and heal infertility. Their name is written in glue on cardboard, sprinkled with powder or glitter until visible, then posted where it can be seen. Offerings of milk, fruit brandy, and cakes are made, the phrase is chanted aloud until their presence is felt, and a bargain is struck. ⚠️ The Keshalyi are a spirit tradition from Romani culture, a closed practice. This working should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural guidance.

The Keshalyi are fairy spirits in Romani tradition, specifically invoked to prevent and heal infertility. Unlike many fairy beings, they are understood to be approachable through the correct phrase and proper offerings, and willing to bargain for their services.

The phrase 'Keshalyi lisperesn,' meaning 'Fairies spin,' is itself the key of approach. Writing it in a visible, lasting form creates a site of ongoing invitation. The chanting brings the practitioner into the right state of awareness to perceive their presence.

The offerings reflect what these spirits are said to value: the nourishment of milk, the warmth of fruit spirits, and the sweetness of cakes, a triad of domestic abundance offered in good faith.

⚠️
The Keshalyi belong to Romani spirit tradition, a closed practice. This working should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural guidance from a knowledgeable community member.
