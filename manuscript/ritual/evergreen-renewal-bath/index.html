🌲Evergreen Renewal Bath

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
BathCandle Rite
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Water MagickEnergy WorkCandle MagickHerbal Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
5 drops silver fir essential oil 5 drops myrtle essential oil 2 drops pine essential oil 1 shot of vodka 5 drops larch flower essence Pine-scented candles
◇Directions
1. Add the silver fir, myrtle, and pine essential oils to a shot of vodka. 2. Shake the vodka to disperse the oils. 3. Pour the scented vodka into a tub of warm water. A separate drink without essential oils, or another potion, may be enjoyed during the bath. 4. Add five drops of larch flower essence to the bath. 5. Light pine-scented candles. 6. Soak in the bath and feel energy returning.
◇Description
Evergreen essential oils dispersed in vodka, larch flower essence, and pine-scented candlelight combine in a bath that restores depleted psychic and magickal power. The scent of evergreen trees reinvigorates and replenishes that power.
