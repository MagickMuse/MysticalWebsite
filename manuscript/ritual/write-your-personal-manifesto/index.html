📝Write Your Personal Manifesto

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
MeditationShadow Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
Time: 10 minutes Final output: 3-5 core sentences
◇Directions
1. Butterfly enters her chrysalis with only the barest idea of who she will become. 2. This idea is called an imaginal disc, a group of cells holding the pattern of the parts she will need to create in order to take flight. 3. You too have metaphoric imaginal discs, patterns embedded deep in your psyche that help you know who you are. 4. You can follow these patterns to rebuild yourself during transitions. 5. Let's pull these patterns to the surface so you can see them and begin to understand the essence of your own being. 6. Set a timer for 10 minutes and use the time to write a personal manifesto. 7. Start with the phrase "I am" or "I honor" or "This is what is most important to me." When you're done writing, underline 3 to 5 of the most important sentences, crafting them into a personal manifesto that you can refer to when you feel like cellular soup with no clue how to re-emerge.
◇Description
A timed writing practice to identify your personal imaginal discs, the core patterns embedded in your psyche that define who you are. Like Butterfly's cellular blueprint, this manifesto guides you through transformation when you feel dissolved into soup with no clear path forward.
