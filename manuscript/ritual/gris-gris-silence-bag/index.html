📿Gris-Gris Silence Bag

◇Form
Pouch
◇Method
Herbal MagickBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Required: - Licorice root - Slippery elm bark - Adder's-tongue (fern) - Black fabric bag or pouch: 1
◇Directions
1. This is a straightforward silencing charm that works through proximity and the natural magickal properties of three herbs known for stopping unwanted speech. 2. Gather equal parts of licorice root (which sweetens and soothes but also binds), slippery elm (which makes words slide away and gossip stick in the throat), and adder's-tongue fern (which stops harmful speech through sympathetic magick). 3. Place all three herbs into a small black fabric bag. 4. Black is the color of binding, banishing, and silence. 5. As you tie the bag closed, focus your intention on silencing the person. 6. Visualize their words about you fading to nothing, their gossip falling on deaf ears, their ability to speak your name becoming difficult or unpleasant for them. 7. Plant the bag somewhere on the target's property where it will not be discovered: - Under their doorstep (most traditional and powerful placement) - Buried shallowly in their yard near the entrance to their home - Hidden inside their house if you have access (in a closet, under furniture, behind something) 8. The closer to their threshold or entrance, the more effective the charm will be, as it catches their words as they leave their space. 9. Leave the bag in place. 10. The magick will work as long as the bag remains undiscovered on their property.
◇Description
A simple three-herb charm bag designed to silence an enemy or noisy neighbor. The bag combines licorice root, slippery elm, and adder's-tongue, all herbs associated with stopping gossip and binding speech, and is planted secretly on the target's property.
