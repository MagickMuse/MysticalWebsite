🕯️Song of the Goddess

◇Form
Verbal Working
◇Method
InvocationPrayerDevotional Work
◇Category
Invocation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. Speak this invocation to honor the Goddess in her fullness, or use during Drawing Down the Moon or Esbat rituals. 2. The text is written in the Goddess's own voice, allowing you to embody her presence as you speak: 3. I am the Great Mother, adored by all creation which I have brought forth from my fertile womb. 4. I am the Primal Mother, life-bringing force of the Divine Female, boundless and eternal. 5. My faces are many, for I am Transformation and I bring change to all. 6. I am the Goddess of the Moon, Lady of all Magick, passing through phases of Maiden, Mother, and Crone. 7. I am the Maiden whose name is carried upon the tides and the winds. 8. I wear the Moon upon my brow as Crescent, Full, and Horned, the stars rest beneath my feet, and the Serpent of Regeneration gazes up at me in adoration. 9. I am Mysteries, yet I reveal these to any who seek such of me. 10. I open the New Path for the spiritual questor, comfort the weary traveler upon the old, and receive into my arms the soul in passage. 11. I am the Blessed Mother, the Bountiful Lady of the Harvest. 12. I am clothed in the cool depths of the waters and draped in the gold of fields laden with grain. 13. My tabard is the myriad forms of life in woodland, field, valley, river, sky, and sea. 14. My hair cascades across my shoulders as soft shadows stirring in the forests. 15. By me are all seasons of the earth ruled that all things come to fruition through me, for lo, I am the Life-Giving Mother, fertile and joyous in my abundance. 16. I am the Crone, Grandmother, and Death Mother, wise and tender. 17. Through me pass all in the spiral dance of life, death, and rebirth. 18. I am the Wheel, the shadowed Moon, giving release and renewal to weary souls. 19. The God ushers the Spirits unto me, for I am the Tomb through whom all must pass to be born of my Womb. 20. I am the Eternal Maiden, Mother of All, and Crone of Transformation. 21. I stir the cauldron of Wisdom, Abundance, and Renewal, and I pour forth my Limitless Love upon all my peoples of the Earth.
◇Description
A devotional invocation honoring the Triple Goddess in her aspects of Maiden, Mother, and Crone. Speaks in the voice of the Great Mother herself, celebrating her roles as Moon Lady, Life-Giver, Harvest Bringer, Death Mother, and Cauldron Keeper. Suitable for Esbat rituals, goddess devotionals, or personal meditation.
