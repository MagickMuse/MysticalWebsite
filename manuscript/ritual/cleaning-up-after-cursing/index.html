🧹Cleaning Up After Cursing

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Candle RiteIncense Blend
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
BanishingCleansingCandle MagickSmoke MagickPrayer
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
dragon's blood incense (enough for each room) 1 black chime candle
◇Directions
1. Close all windows and doors throughout your home. 2. Light dragon's blood incense in every room, moving through the space with authority. 3. Demand firmly that all negative energies leave, raising your voice to command them out. 4. Light a black chime candle for banishing. 5. You may speak a spell of your own design or use the following: 6. I call upon all that is pure and just, Remove the evils within this place, Take it away, burn it to dust Empty it outside of time and space 7. Alternatively, you may recite Psalm 51 in place of the spell. 8. Key verses include: 9. Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy loving kindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions. Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me. Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit. 10. Allow the black chime candle to burn all the way down. 11. Once extinguished, dispose of the wax remnants in the trash and remove the trash from your home immediately. 12. Open all windows to release any lingering negativity.
◇Description
A space-cleansing ritual to purify the home or ritual area after performing baneful workings, using dragon's blood incense and banishing candle magick.
