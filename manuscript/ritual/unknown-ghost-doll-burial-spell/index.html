👤Unknown Ghost Doll Burial Spell

◇Form
Poppet
◇Method
Banishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- One distinctly male doll (wood or true earthen clay only, not synthetic materials) - One distinctly female doll (same requirements) - Real food and drink for the feast
◇Directions
1. Craft one clearly male and one clearly female doll from wood or true earthen clay. 2. Synthetic materials will not work for this spell; the dolls must be able to decompose completely. 3. Host a real feast in the dolls' honor with genuine food, drink, and celebration. 4. When the party is over, speak to the dolls and explain that their wandering is finished. 5. Bury the dolls in uncultivated land where they are unlikely to be disturbed for a long time.
◇Description
An ancient Greek method for laying a ghost to rest when neither the identity nor the gender of the ghost is known. Two dolls are crafted from biodegradable materials, feasted, and buried so they can fully decompose and carry the wandering spirit with them.
