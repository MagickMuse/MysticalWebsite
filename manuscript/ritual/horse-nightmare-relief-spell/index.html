A herbal infusion spell intended to relieve a horse's nightmares. The blend of anise, garlic, and licorice root is administered by drinking, mixed into food, or used as a bath, depending on the animal's willingness.

Note: Charms and spells for horses may also be used for donkeys and mules.

Quantities

  • Anise seeds
  • Fresh garlic
  • Licorice root (not candy)
  • Boiling water

Directions

  1. Pour boiling water over anise seeds, fresh garlic, and licorice root to make an infusion.
  2. Allow to cool, then strain.
  3. Feed the infusion to the horse to drink.
  4. If the horse will not drink it, add the infusion to its food or use it to bathe the horse.
