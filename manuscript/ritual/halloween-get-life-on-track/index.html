Samhain

An October 31 divination ritual with pumpkin carved with three holes, circling counterclockwise and clockwise three times, gazing into past/present/future.

Quantities

  • 1 sharp knife
  • 1 pumpkin
  • 1 small white candle

Directions

  1. With the sharp knife, take the top off the pumpkin and scoop out the flesh.
  2. In the middle of the pumpkin in a horizontal line, make three parallel holes around the sides.
  3. When it gets dark, place the candle inside the pumpkin and light it.
  4. Circle the pumpkin three times counterclockwise, saying, "Time of no time, show to me, worlds long past but linked to me." Gaze into the first hole, close your eyes, blink; look again to see a picture in the pumpkin or your mind from the past, your childhood, or past worlds, relevant to the present.
  5. Circle the pumpkin three times clockwise, saying, "Time of no time, show to me, the present world, but hidden from me" to see what you need to know to understand about what is going on now.
  6. Gaze into the second hole.
  7. Circle the pumpkin three times counterclockwise, saying, "Time of no time show to me, a future time but linked to me" to see the best options ahead for you.
  8. Gaze into the third hole.
  9. Let the candle burn.
  10. Perform October 31, before it gets dark.
