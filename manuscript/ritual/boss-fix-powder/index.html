🌶️Boss Fix Powder

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Powder
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal MagickPowder Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
Hoodoo
◇Quantities
- Tobacco from one cigarette (preferably boss's brand) - Shredded newspaper (preferably one the boss reads) - Chili powder
◇Directions
1. Empty tobacco from one cigarette. 2. Combine with shredded newspaper. 3. Add chili powder and grind all ingredients together into a fine powder. 4. When opportunity arises, sprinkle a tiny bit on or around your boss's chair, or drop a little over the office threshold.
◇Description
A Hoodoo powder blend made from tobacco, shredded newspaper, and chili powder, ground fine and discreetly sprinkled on or around a difficult boss's chair or office threshold. Allegedly causes supervisors to regard you with love and favor, or at minimum leaves you alone to work in peace. Tailored to one target. Make fresh powder for each person. ⚠️ Closed Practice: Hoodoo is an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.
