A guide to creating personal altar arrangements of multiple candles, each charged for a specific person, condition, or goal. The garden grows stronger with repeated use and serves as an organized way to work on multiple objectives simultaneously.

Quantities

  • Candles for each magickal goal (sizes matching duration: large for long-term, small for short-term)
  • Fire-safe tray, mirror, dish, bowl, or tile
  • 1 white taper as altar candle
  • Optional: herbs, charms, crystals, images
  • Optional: dressing oil
  • Optional: incense

Directions

  1. A guide to creating personal altar arrangements of multiple candles, each charged for a specific person, condition, or goal.
  2. The garden grows stronger with repeated use and serves as an organized way to work on multiple objectives simultaneously.

A candle garden is a collection of attractively arranged candles burned together for magickal purposes. Unlike decorative gardens where candles match in color and scent, a magickal garden contains unique candles carefully selected so each one's size, shape, color, or scent resonates with its specific intent.

Designing Your Garden

Each candle should represent a person, condition, problem, idea, or issue. Use large candles for long-term goals and smaller candles for short-term objectives. Tea lights work well for single occasions like job interviews or exams.

Though each candle is unique, all parts must work harmoniously together. The arrangement should please the eye, and different scents should mingle pleasantly rather than clash.

Charging the Candles

Consider whether your garden encourages or halts. Word each charge accordingly. For example, charge a candle to "support efforts to find a job" rather than "end financial problems," or to "help end loneliness" rather than "help find love."

Assemble all components. Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space. Place and light the altar candle, then raise power.

Take each garden candle in both hands. Focus on its purpose and name it aloud. Concentrate on transferring magickal energy from your hands to the candle. If using oil, rub the candle while doing this. Place it upright in the garden and say:

This candle will glow, and its magick will grow.
This candle will glow, and its magick will flow.

Continue until all candles are charged and arranged. Add any charms, stones, or statues. Hold your hands over the candles, concentrate on the garden's purpose, and say:

This garden is dedicated to the Lord and the Lady.
It will create change, but never cause harm.

Extinguish the altar candle when sufficiently charged.

Activating the Garden

Get into a magickal mood and create sacred space. Raise power. Light the altar candle, then use it to light each garden candle while naming its purpose aloud.

Stand back and regard your garden. Visualize magickal energy shooting from each candle toward its goal. Focus on the changes as already accomplished. Raise your arms and say:

The magick is unpent and sent.

Allow candles to burn as long as feels necessary, then extinguish them. Many witches use snuffers rather than blowing out candles, and some extinguish in reverse order of lighting.

Maintaining the Garden

Tend your garden regularly by trimming candles, scraping wax, and adjusting as needed. Remove candles when goals are reached. Replace spent candles whose objectives have not yet manifested. Add new candles for additional objectives, charging them before placement.

Consider maintenance a sacred task, and your garden's magick will flourish.
