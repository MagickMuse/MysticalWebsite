✈️Smooth Travel Spell

◇Form
Dressed CandleTalisman
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraLunarPlanetary
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 ballpoint pen - 1 white candle - Candleholder - Rosemary essential oil - Matches or lighter - 1 piece of tumbled rose quartz (add one for each travel companion)
◇Directions
1. Perform this spell at night, within twenty-four hours prior to your departure, ideally where you can see the moon. 2. With the ballpoint pen, draw the astrological symbol for Jupiter in the candle's wax. 3. It looks like the number 4. 4. Jupiter is the planet that rules travel. 5. Dress the candle with rosemary oil, fit it in the candleholder, and light it. 6. If you wish, burn a little dried rosemary in a fireproof dish. 7. Set the rose quartz in front of the candle. 8. Repeat this incantation while you gaze into the candle's flame and inhale the scent of rosemary: 9. "By the light of Lady Moon, I reach my destination soon. The trip shall safe and happy be, For all concerned, as well as me." 10. Extinguish the candle and pick up the rose quartz. 11. Rub a little rosemary oil on it and carry the stone with you while traveling.
◇Description
A pre-travel spell for safe and smooth journeys regardless of destination or transportation mode. Uses the symbol of Jupiter, rosemary for protection, and rose quartz as a travel talisman. Best performed at night where you can see the moon.
