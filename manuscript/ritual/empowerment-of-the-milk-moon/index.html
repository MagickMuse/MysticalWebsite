Imbolc

An Imbolc self‑empowerment rite under the Milk Moon to replenish energy, heal hidden wounds, and gain wisdom to set healthy boundaries so you can keep helping others from a full well.

Quantities

  • One white candle dedicated to this work, in a safe holder
  • A safe, stable surface for burning
  • A clear view of the Moon if possible (a window is fine)
  • Optional: a darkened room if the Moon is visible during daylight

Directions

  1. Timing and setting: Best under the Full Milk Moon, but any time in the Milk Moon cycle works.
  2. Indoors or outdoors with a view of the Moon if possible.
  3. Prepare:
  4. Arrange the candle where you can safely see both the flame and the Moon together, or easily glance between them. Settle and quiet the mind by gazing at the Moon’s surface and letting its calm seep in.
  5. Invocation:
  6. Raise your arms to the Moon and say:
    Beautiful Sister Moon, during this cycle of the Milk Moon, I ask you to nourish me, and help me replenish my own energy. Help me have the things I need in order to help others, and show me when it would be better to not help. Give me the wisdom to know when to say “no,” and the courage to stand by that decision. As I light this candle, I ask that you send me your nourishment so even my invisible wounds will heal. Bring your energy to this candle, lit in your honor.
  7. Light and align:
  8. Light the candle. If safe, lift it so the flame appears within the Moon’s disk, then bow and set it back down.
  9. Contemplation and receiving:
  10. Watch the flame. Recall the many ways you’ve given to others, large and small. Visualize fine drops of lunar water flowing from the flame and into you, healing and re‑energizing from the inside out. Stay private and humble; listen for insights about situations or people where “no” would be wise.
  11. Close and continue:
  12. Extinguish the flame safely. Any time you feel depleted, relight this candle and reflect on your needs. When you recognize a need, act on it so you remain strong enough to help others.
