🙏Prayer Healing Mind Heart Soul

◇Form
Candle RiteAltar Working
◇Method
PrayerInvocationHealing
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
- One candle in a safe holder
◇Directions
1. Light the candle and pray to Brigid: 2. Goddess Brigid, Keeper of the Flame, Tender of the Well, I come to you to ask for the healing of an injured soul. Please look into deepest places within [person’s name] and find the injuries that lie beneath the surface. I ask that you ease the pain within my or that soul. Just as this candle shines, I ask that you shine a light on a path towards healing. Shine a light so bright and clear that the path to healing is unmistakable. Please send whatever assistance will be needed to make this soul whole once again. 3. Sit in reflection as the candle burns. 4. Journal any insights and follow through on supportive actions.
◇Description
An Imbolc prayer to Brigid to heal inner wounds, illuminate the path to emotional and spiritual recovery, and send needed support.
