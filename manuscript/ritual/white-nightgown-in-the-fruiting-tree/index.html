🌱White Nightgown in the Fruiting Tree

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting

◇Method
Divination
◇Category

◇Quantities
1 new white nightgown A fruitful tree
◇Directions
1. On Saint George's Eve, hang a new white nightgown from the branches of a fruitful tree. 2. Leave it overnight. 3. In the morning, inspect the garment carefully. 4. If any living creature is found within it, the woman can expect to conceive before the next feast of Saint George. 5. Put the nightgown on immediately to activate the blessing. 6. Wearing it during lovemaking is additionally beneficial.
◇Description
A Saint George's Eve fertility divination in which a new white nightgown is left overnight in a fruitful tree. Any living creature found inside the garment in the morning is a promise of conception before the following year's feast.

🐉Feast of Saint George

Saint George's Eve is one of the most powerful magickal nights in the calendar. The Syrian shrine dedicated to Saint George was once famed across the Mediterranean for miraculous conceptions. Some traditions hold that beneath Saint George's saintly exterior lies Baal, the Semitic rain and thunder deity whose rains fertilize the earth.

The fruitful tree amplifies the working's fertility current. Any living creature found in the garment, however small, carries the blessing and the promise.
