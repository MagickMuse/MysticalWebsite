For those that need to shut up and listen every now and then instead of just talking and doing.

Quantities

  • No materials required

Directions

  1. Perform this invocation on the day of Mercury at the hour of Saturn.
  2. Vibrate the vowels Epsilon followed by Omega (EH-OHW), with the day vowel shorter in duration followed by a longer vibration of the hour vowel.
  3. Then speak the invocation:
  4. I call the powers of motion and inertia.
    Counterbalance my speech with silence.
    Discipline my thought and defend me from distraction.
    Restrict my movements that I may keep to the task
    And regulate my actions rightly
    That I may work harmoniously with time itself.
