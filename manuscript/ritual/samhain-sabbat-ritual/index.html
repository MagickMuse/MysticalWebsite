🕯️Samhain Sabbat Ritual

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
Directional MagickInvocationBanishingHerbal MagickCandle MagickAncestor Work
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Quantities
- Orange and/or black altar candles - Patchouli or myrrh incense - Small pumpkins, Indian corn, gourds, fall flowers and leaves for decoration - Cauldron with black votive candle inside - Parchment with weaknesses or bad habits written down - White votive candle in bowl - Sprig of heather - Apple on plate - Patchouli oil (optional) - Wand - Bell (optional) - Pentacle - Cakes & Wine: dark wine, pumpkin pie, stuffed acorn squash, stuffed game birds, apple cider, taffy apples - Dumb Supper (optional): bread, salt, and cider or beer at an extra place for family spirits
◇Directions
1. Cast a circle using your preferred method. 2. Light the incense and carry it around the circle deosil. 3. Clap or ring a bell three times and say: 4. I celebrate the dance of life to death to new life and the balance of the cosmos in my life! 5. The last harvest is gathered and stored for the dark months ahead, and the Wheel has turned to the time of the Hunter. 6. Clap or ring the bell nine times: 7. At this time is the veil between the worlds thin, and the gates are thrown open, so do I welcome thee spirits who have gone before and thee Others, who pass between two worlds. 8. This is the Crone's time and with the Lord of the Shadows, She is the passage from life to life that all must take. 9. They give a refreshing rest in the continuous turning of the spiral dance that goes and returns, yet ever moves on. 10. With the Ancient Ones [Names], I move with the dance unperturbed. 11. Love gives strength; give to gain. 12. Hold up your wand with open arms: 13. Great Lady, Fruitful Mother, You have showered me with Your bounty, and in this turning of the seasons, I bid You farewell as You walk now as Crone with the Lord of the Hunt. 14. I know that within You is yet another fruit waiting to be born, and I will bide patient until the Mother returns. 15. Set the cauldron on the pentacle and light the black votive from the center altar candle: 16. Here is the cauldron of endings and new beginnings. 17. Into this burning flame do I cast my weaknesses and the habits that keep me from attaining my potential. 18. By the death of these things will I live a better life. 19. So Mote It Be! 20. Burn the parchment in the votive. 21. When reduced to ash, clap or ring the bell nine times. 22. Pass the white candle through the patchouli incense or anoint with patchouli oil: 23. With this candle and by its light I welcome you spirits this Samhain night. 24. Set aside this white candle for use in your jack-o'-lantern. 25. Hold the heather over the altar: 26. I call upon the power of this herb to bless this house and the spirits that come to visit. 27. Drop the heather into the cauldron: 28. The air is purified and made pleasant for the spirits and Others who may call upon me. 29. Blessed Be! 30. Hold the apple above the altar: 31. I call upon Thee Lord and Lady, to bless this fruit to be the food for the dead. 32. Let any who visits find sustenance in this apple whose center reveals the pentagram and reminds us of the promise of the Lady of passage from one life to the next. 33. May the spirits who pass here move on refreshed. 34. So Mote It Be! 35. Clap or ring the bell three times. 36. Proceed to Cakes & Wine, then close the circle. 37. After the sabbat concludes, bury the apple outside as food for the spirits.
◇Description
The Third Harvest and Celtic New Year sabbat ceremony celebrating the dance of life to death to new life. Features the cauldron of endings and new beginnings for banishing weaknesses, spirit welcoming with the white candle, heather blessing, and apple offering for the dead.
