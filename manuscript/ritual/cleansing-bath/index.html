A purifying herbal bath ritual to cleanse the body, spirit, and soul of negative energy after baneful workings, using cleansing herbs, salts, and visualization.

Quantities

4 white candles
uncrossing oil
cleansing herbs (sage, chamomile, cinnamon, lemon balm, lavender, rosemary, hyssop, peppermint)
1 muslin cloth or bag
epsom salts or sea salts (optional)

Directions

  1. Anoint four white candles with uncrossing oil and arrange them around the bathtub.
  2. Run a warm bath, incorporating cleansing herbs by tying them into a muslin cloth or bag and hanging it from the faucet so the water runs through the herbal bundle into the tub.
  3. Alternatively, float the bags in the water or add loose herbs directly, though be mindful that loose herbs may clog the drain.
  4. Suitable cleansing herbs include sage, chamomile, cinnamon, lemon balm, lavender, rosemary, hyssop, and peppermint.
  5. Dry or fresh herbs both work.
  6. Epsom salts or sea salts may be added as well, and oils may be incorporated for additional potency.
  7. Once the bath is drawn, immerse yourself completely.
  8. Allow yourself to relax fully, which may take several minutes.
  9. Focus on the warmth enveloping your body.
  10. Breathe deeply, inhaling the herbal scents rising from the water.
  11. Close your eyes and attune yourself to the rhythms of your own body.
  12. Visualize all negative energy within you and imagine it being drawn out through your skin, bit by bit, dissolving and diluting into the water.
  13. While the negativity leaves your body, meditate on how rejuvenating the bath is.
  14. See your body, spirit, and soul being cleansed and purified by the herbs and the water.
  15. When you feel ready, stand and step out of the tub.
  16. Release the drain so that all the negativity absorbed by the water flows away.
    ### Ongoing Protection
  17. Keep pots of rosemary beside the front and back doors to ward off evil.
  18. Grow garlic in the garden to protect against those who would take your prosperity.
