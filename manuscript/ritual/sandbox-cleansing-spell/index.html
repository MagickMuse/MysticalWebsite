🌿Sandbox Cleansing Spell

◇Form
Wash
◇Method
CleansingHerbal Magick
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Euphorbia (sandbox tree) leaves - Boiling water
◇Directions
1. Make an infusion by steeping euphorbia leaves in boiling water. 2. Use the cooled liquid to wash and cleanse the interior of the home from which the body has been removed, giving special attention to the areas where the deceased spent the most time and where the body lay.
◇Description
An intensive spiritual cleansing of a home from which a corpse has been removed, using an infusion of sandbox tree (euphorbia) leaves. Despite the name, sandbox tree has nothing to do with children's sandboxes, it is a common name for euphorbia, a member of the spurge family. ⚠️ Euphorbia sap is caustic and irritating to skin and eyes; handle with care.
