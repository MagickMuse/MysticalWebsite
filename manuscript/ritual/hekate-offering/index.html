A petition to Hekate, Mistress of the Crossroads and Queen of Witches, appealing for justice against a rapist. Hekate is described as the sole spirit who came to Demeter's aid following Persephone's abduction and as one who witnesses all crimes. She is said to have particular hatred for rape and violence against women and children. This spell may be performed in place of, or in addition to, the candle working. Petitions are accepted during the Dark of the Moon, on the last day of each month, or on October 31, which is considered especially potent. A dog appearing after the offering, particularly a solitary or unexpected one, is a traditional sign that the petition has been accepted. One of the Bring a Rapist to Justice Spell methods.

Quantities

  • 3 cloves of garlic, peeled
  • Honey (enough to cover the garlic)
  • Dried lavender
  • A paper plate or a plate you are willing to permanently leave behind

Directions

  1. Peel three cloves of garlic and place them on a paper plate or a plate you are prepared to leave permanently at the crossroads.
  2. Cover the garlic completely with honey, then sprinkle dried lavender over the top.
  3. Carry the offering to a three-way crossroads.
  4. Leave the offering there.
  5. Speak plainly to Hekate, telling her exactly what you need.
  6. Do not return for the plate.
