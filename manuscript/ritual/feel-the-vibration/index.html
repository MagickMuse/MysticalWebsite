An underwater sound experimentation practice inspired by Orca's sonic navigation and communication. By submerging your head and making various sounds, you explore how vibration and acoustics behave differently in water versus air, deepening sensory awareness.

Quantities

  • Bathtub, pond, or swimming pool

Directions

  1. Orca communicates and navigates through sound.
  2. Whistles serve private communication underwater while pulsed calls reach the entire pod.
  3. Pods share common dialects for group communication.
  4. Through echolocation (analyzing echoes from sound series) Orca determines distances and object locations for navigation.
  5. We humans understand how sound travels through air.
  6. Water presents completely different acoustics.
  7. Next time you bathe or visit a pond or swimming pool, submerge your head underwater and experiment making various noises.
  8. Feel the vibration in your head and jaw.
  9. Notice how sound bounces (or doesn't) in water.
