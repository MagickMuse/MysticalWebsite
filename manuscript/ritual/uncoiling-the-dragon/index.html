Imbolc

A group ritual for 5 or more people (13 is ideal) that awakens the earth's dragon at Imbolc. Participants form a spiral representing the coiled dragon, then uncoil clockwise as the dragon awakens and flies, lighting each person's candle to bless their goals for the coming spring.

Quantities

  • Altar table in center of ritual area, optionally adjacent to balefire
  • Yellow altar cloth
  • White pillar candle
  • White taper candle
  • Candleholder for taper, easy to pass while burning, candle lantern with glass or metal mesh preferred
  • Dragon's blood incense and censer, sticks or cones most convenient, or charcoal with pure dragon's blood resin, tarragon on charcoal works as substitute
  • Personal candle and holder for each participant, any color or design, lead should use taper if using lantern

Directions

  1. Purpose:
  2. Awaken the earth's dragon at Imbolc, symbolizing the awakening of spring and new life. Give energy to ideas taking form on the physical plane.
  3. Preparation:
  4. Cover altar with yellow cloth. Place white pillar candle in center with incense and censer.
  5. Light pillar candle before beginning. Light charcoal in advance if using.
  6. Place white taper candle on table next to holder or lantern.
  7. Participants place their personal candles in holders on altar as they arrive.
  8. Optional: Can perform within magick circle if desired, but ensure circle is large enough for full group.
  9. Forming the Dragon:
  10. Everyone stands in circle in order they will stand for ritual. Lead and anchor stand next to each other to close loop.
  11. Face altar and hold hands, extending into largest comfortable circle. Release hands.
  12. Anchor retrieves her candle from altar, returns to her spot in circle, places candle there.
  13. Anchor stands next to altar in front of unlit taper, facing white pillar candle.
  14. Person to left of anchor's empty spot retrieves candle, places in circle spot, stands to right of anchor.
  15. Continue one by one: each person gets candle, places in circle spot, joins spiral around altar.
  16. Form single counterclockwise spiral, not series of circles. Make space for people to reach altar.
  17. Lead takes final position, becoming dragon's head. Anchor is dragon's tail.
  18. Awakening the Dragon:
  19. Anchor lights incense from pillar candle (or adds incense to charcoal).
  20. Anchor says: "Great old dragon, guardian of earth's warmth, bringer of blessings, we call you in to the spiral we have made in your honor."
  21. Anchor lights taper candle from pillar candle, inserts in lantern if using.
  22. Anchor proclaims: "Great dragon, we offer you this flame from the heart of our altar to rekindle your sleeping fires."
  23. Group responds: "Great dragon, awaken!"
  24. Anchor passes candle to person on right saying "Blessings of the flame." Continue passing right with blessing each time.
  25. When candle reaches lead, lead says: "We call upon you, great old dragon, to take the flame we offer, that you may awaken from the slumber of the dark days and bless us as we prepare for the fertile days that are fast approaching."
  26. Group chants: "Awake! Awake! AWAKE!"
  27. Lead holds candleholder high and calls: "The dragon has awakened and is ready to fly!"
  28. The Dragon Flies:
  29. Lead turns 180 degrees with candle at front of line, moves clockwise behind rest of dragon.
  30. As lead moves behind each participant, that person turns and joins flying dragon.
  31. Continue uncoiling until anchor joins flight.
  32. Lead continues around until reaching their candle at circle's edge.
  33. Slow entire dragon as flight ends. All should be standing near their candles.
  34. Sharing the Dragon's Fire:
  35. Lead removes taper from lantern if used.
  36. Lead proclaims: "The dragon is awake and shall share the good fortune of his warming fire with us all."
  37. Lead kneels and lights personal candle with taper.
  38. Pass burning taper clockwise. Everyone lights their candles.
  39. When anchor's candle is burning, taper returns to lead.
  40. Lead says: "We celebrate the awakening of the dragon who heralds the approaching spring. The dragon has given flame to our plans for the spring and has given a blessing to each of us. We thank you Great Old Dragon and hope that you will grant us the strength of your flame until the dark days return again. We thank you for joining us and sharing your warmth. From a tiny flame, great fires can grow."
  41. Closing:
  42. Participants bid farewell and give thanks to dragon of earth.
  43. Enjoy energy as candles burn. Share feast if part of group practice.
  44. Personal candles can be extinguished at any time.
  45. If working within magick circle, properly banish circle before extinguishing candles.
  46. Notes:
  47. Ritual intended for 5+ people, 13 is perfect number.
  48. Can be adapted for larger groups.
  49. Terms "lead" and "anchor" used instead of High Priestess/High Priest for inclusivity.
  50. Can be performed outdoors with balefire as center point.
  51. Space needed for entire group to hold hands in large circle.
