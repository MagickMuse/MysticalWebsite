⚗️Egyptian Medicine Spell (Heka Invocation)

◇Tradition
Egyptian
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Potion
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
HealingChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Medicine (as prescribed)
◇Directions
1. The spell equates heka (magick) with the medicine the patient needs to swallow, and vice versa. 2. Consuming the medicine means consuming its heka, which in turn will put an end to the patient's suffering. 3. This principle was central to Egyptian healing: magick and medicine were not separate categories but intertwined forces. 4. The spell invokes the myth of Horus and Seth's tribunal at Heliopolis to establish divine precedent for recovery and wholeness. 5. The myths were collected on a papyrus written around 1100 BCE known as 'The Contendings of Horus and Seth.' A tribunal of gods at Heliopolis called Horus and Seth to appear before them to end their conflict over the throne of Osiris. 6. After lengthy proceedings, the gods' final verdict vindicated Horus, restoring him to wholeness and health. 7. He could do anything he wanted, just like the gods. 8. By invoking this precedent, the patient receives the same divine restoration. 9. Recite the incantation aloud while administering medicine to the patient. 10. Egyptian magick required the spoken word (djed medu, 'the words to be said') to activate its power. 11. Recite: 'Here is the medicine to dispel bad substances from my heart and limbs. Heka is strong because of this medicine, and this medicine is strong because of heka. Do you remember when Horus and Seth were taken to the Great Palace of Heliopolis for an inquiry into Seth's testicles? After that, Horus flourished like a living person; he could do anything he wanted, just like the gods.'
◇Description
Egyptian spell recited to convince a patient to swallow medicine. Notes for the magickian praise this formula as 'a true method, proved an infinite number of times.'
