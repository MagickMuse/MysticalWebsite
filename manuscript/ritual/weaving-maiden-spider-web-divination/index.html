This spell lets you know whether you're in the Weaving Lady's favor. Spells requesting assistance from spirits are to some extent dependent on whether you've found favor with the deity, if they like you, the spell is most likely to work.

Quantities

A spider (found, not purchased)
Box

Directions

  1. On the morning of the seventh day of the seventh month of the Chinese calendar, find a spider.
  2. Place this spider gently into a box and leave it there for 24 hours.
  3. The following morning, open the box, free the spider to go where it will, and check the spider's progress: if the spider has woven a web, you have the Weaving Lady's favor. The larger and more intricate the web, the more she likes you.
Note: Spider does not yet have a dedicated entry in this grimoire.
