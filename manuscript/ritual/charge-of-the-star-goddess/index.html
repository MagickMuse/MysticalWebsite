🕯️Charge of the Star Goddess

Wiccan
◇Solitary or Group

◇Setting

◇Directions
1. Speak this invocation to honor the Star Goddess during Esbats, Drawing Down the Moon, or personal devotion. 2. The text is written in the Goddess's own voice: 3. Hear ye the words of the Star Goddess; she in the dust of whose feet are the hosts of heaven, and whose body encircles the universe. 4. I who am the beauty of the green earth, the white moon among the stars, and the mystery of the waters call unto thy soul; arise and come unto me. 5. I am the soul of nature who gives life to the universe. 6. From me all things proceed, and unto me all things must return. 7. Before my face, beloved of gods and of men, let thine innermost divine self be enfolded in the rapture of the Infinite. 8. Let my worship be within the heart that rejoices, for behold! 9. All acts of love and pleasure are my rituals. 10. Therefore, let there be beauty and strength, power and compassion, honor and humility, mirth and reverence within you. 11. To thou who thinkest to seek me, know that thy seeking and yearning shall avail thee not unless thou knowest the mystery. 12. If that which thou seekest thou findest not within thee, thou wilt never find it without. 13. For behold! 14. I have been with thee from the beginning; and I am that which is attained at the end of desire.
◇Description
A traditional Charge of the Goddess based partly on works by Charles Leland, Gerald Gardner, and Doreen Valiente. Spoken in the voice of the Star Goddess whose body encircles the universe, calling souls to arise and return to her. Contains the famous lines about acts of love and pleasure being her rituals, and the mystery that must be found within.
