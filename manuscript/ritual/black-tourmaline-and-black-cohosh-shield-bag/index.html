Black tourmaline, black cohosh root, and real silver carried together in a black velvet bag. Both the stone and the root repel and shield rather than absorb, and pairing them multiplies the protective effect.

Quantities

Black tourmaline
Black cohosh root
Real silver (a small piece)
Black velvet bag

Directions

  1. Place a black tourmaline, a black cohosh root, and a piece of real silver inside a black velvet bag.
  2. Carry or wear the bag for protection.

Lore

Black is one of the traditional colors of protective magick because, like a black hole in space, it can absorb endless quantities of negative spiritual power and so minimize the presence of danger. The drawback is that most black crystals store these psychic toxins and could, in theory, release them again, so they must be cleansed thoroughly and on schedule. Black tourmaline and black cohosh work differently: rather than absorbing, both repel and shield.
