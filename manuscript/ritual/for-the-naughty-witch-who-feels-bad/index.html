A cleansing ritual for the witch whose conscience weighs heavy after baneful workings, using fire, sacred resins, and the warmth of the rising Sun to purify past actions.

Quantities

1 piece of parchment
1 black pen or ink
frankincense resin
myrrh resin
1 charcoal disc
1 brass dish
1 gold candle
1 pin
soap and water

Directions

  1. Perform this ritual on a Sunday morning as the sun rises.
  2. On the parchment, write a list of all the wrongdoings weighing on your conscience.
  3. Prick your thumb with the pin and press a thumbprint of your blood onto the parchment.
  4. Light the gold candle, then burn the frankincense and myrrh on the charcoal disc in the brass dish.
  5. Read your list aloud and allow yourself to feel genuine remorse for each act.
  6. When you feel truly ready, light the parchment in the candle flame and drop it into the dish to burn completely.
  7. Once only ashes remain, rub your hands thoroughly in the ashes, then wash them with soap and water while reciting:
  8. My past is cleansed
    my future blessed
    no more my sleep
    shall bring unrest
  9. Dry your hands in the warmth of the candle flame and the rising sun.
  10. Resolve to be more careful going forward.
