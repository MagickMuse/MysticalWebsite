Mule Hoof Bread

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form

◇Category

Charred mule hoof parings Flour and bread ingredients
◇Directions
1. Char mule hoof parings and blend them into flour. 2. Bake bread from this flour. 3. Feed it to the target without their knowledge.
◇Description
MALEVOLENT WORKING. Documented for historical and protective knowledge only. A fertility hex drawing on the mule's deep association with sterility. Charred mule hoof parings are ground into flour and used to bake bread, which is then covertly fed to a woman with the intent to render her barren. Not intended for use.

The mule is sterile by nature - a hybrid whose very existence terminates the genetic line it carries. This isn't merely a symbolic association but a biological fact that folk tradition recognized and worked with directly. The mule became the most potent symbol of infertility available precisely because its sterility is inherent and absolute.

Charring the hoof parings transforms them from a discarded material into a powdered substance that can be incorporated invisibly into food. The charring process is also a purification and concentration step - burning away the organic material to leave only the most fundamental residue.

Blended into flour and baked into bread, the substance is consumed by the target along with nourishment. The bread that should sustain carries the property of the mule. Bread made from infertile flour, carrying the essence of the sterile animal, becomes the vehicle for transmitting that sterility.

Exposure to the mule's sterility power through ingestion is the mechanic. The same principle governs the honey variant of this working.

⚠️
This is a historical hex documented for knowledge and self-protection only. It is presented here as context for understanding the full spectrum of fertility magic traditions, not as an instruction for use. Hexing another person's fertility without their knowledge or consent is considered among the most serious of malevolent workings.
