A yin-yang balance charm. By displaying images of perfect creative equilibrium, particularly joyful heterosexual intercourse or its symbolic equivalents, the spell neutralizes fire's destructive yang power with an equal force of creative yin.

Quantities

Illustration or photograph of a man and woman happily and equally engaged in sexual intercourse (or symbolic alternatives: yin-yang symbol, Star of David, collection of mortars and pestles)

Directions

  1. Obtain an illustration or photograph of a man and woman extremely happily engaged in sexual intercourse.
  2. Both must appear equally happy.
  3. Any hint of bondage, domination, or sadomasochism negates the spell.
  4. Hang the image on the wall, traditionally in the kitchen or wherever fire is a particular concern.
  5. For a more discreet alternative, post a symbolic depiction of yin-yang balance instead: the yin-yang symbol, the six-pointed Star of David, or a collection of mortars and pestles.

According to general occult theory, Chinese yin-yang philosophy, and mathematics, providing something's perfect balance neutralizes it and cancels its effects. Water puts out fire. Fire is maximum yang. Water is maximum yin.

Fire is a destructive, consuming force that leaves waste and void. What better neutralizer than an act of pure creation?

Obtain an illustration or photograph of a man and woman extremely happily engaged in sexual intercourse. Hang it on the wall, traditionally in the kitchen or wherever fire is feared most.

Rules for the Image

  • Both figures must appear equally happy.
  • Any hint of bondage, domination, or sadomasochism negates the spell.
  • The image must depict a man and a woman specifically, for the purpose of representing the perfect balance of yin and yang.

Discreet Alternatives

For situations requiring more discretion, post a symbolic representation of perfect yin-yang balance instead:

  • The yin-yang symbol
  • The six-pointed Star of David
  • A collection of mortars and pestles

These substitutions trade some potency for discretion.
