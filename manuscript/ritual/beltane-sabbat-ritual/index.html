Beltane

The May Day celebration honoring the union of the May Queen and the Green Man. A fire ritual featuring the burning of nine sacred woods (birch, oak, rowan, willow, hawthorn, hazel, apple, vine, and fir), each carrying specific blessings and correspondences.

Quantities

  • Altar Candles
  • 3 altar candles: dark green
    Incense
  • Floral or woodsy scent: lilac, Secret Garden, or forest
    Decorations
  • Seasonal flowers for altar/circle
    Ritual Items
  • Cauldron with dark green votive candle
  • Bowl of woodruff herb
  • Nine wood chips in separate labeled containers:
  • Birch (honors the Goddess)
  • Oak (honors the God)
  • Rowan (magickal life)
  • Willow (celebrates death)
  • Hawthorn (Fairies)
  • Hazel (wisdom)
  • Apple (love)
  • Vine (joy)
  • Fir (rebirth, immortality)
  • Libation bowl
  • Candle snuffer or lid
  • Goblet
  • Bell (optional)
    Cakes & Wine
  • Breads, cakes, or custards flavored with flowers/extracts
  • Oatmeal cakes
  • Vanilla ice cream
  • Floral or herbal wine

Directions

  1. Cast circle as in the Basic Circle Casting.
  2. When the ritual work portion begins, clap or ring a bell three times:
  3. The Goddess of Spring walks through the land with the God of the Forest, and the dark time of Winter is behind me.
  4. Clap or ring bell seven times:
  5. The animals breed and the plants pollinate, as the May Queen and the Green Man bestow Their blessings upon the Earth and Her creatures.
  6. I, who am Their child [Name] rejoice with Them and ask that Their happy union become the example for all humanity to live in love and harmony.
  7. Light the dark green votive candle in the cauldron:
  8. The Dark Days are cleared away that the Mayday can now begin!
  9. Drop some woodruff into the cauldron flame:
  10. May the Light of the May Fire bring happiness and peace, and may the Victory of the King of the Wood come into my life, that I may always dwell in the joy of the Lady and the Lord.
  11. So Mote It Be!
  12. Drop a pinch of each type of the nine wood chips into the cauldron flame while saying the chant:
  13. I burn thee birch to honor the Goddess;
    And now add thee oak to honor the God.
    Thou rowan I add for a magickal life;
    And add thee willow to celebrate death.
    Thou hawthorn I burn for Fairies near me;
    Thou hazel I burn for wisdom you bring.
    I add thee good apple to bring to me love;
    And thou sweet vine whose fruit brings me joy.
    Fir you are added to remember rebirth;
    Your sweet savor sings of immortality.
    My blessings I give to all of thee,
    And thine I call from thee upon me,
    That as I will, So Mote It Be!
  14. As the wood chips burn, raise the goblet in both hands:
  15. I greet the time of unions and give honor to the Lord and the Lady for Their fruitfulness!
  16. Pour a libation into the libation bowl.
  17. Take a sip from the goblet and set it back on the altar.
  18. Extinguish the flame in the cauldron with a lid or other covering and leave covered until cooled.
  19. Clap or ring bell three times.
  20. Proceed to the Cakes & Wine ceremony of the circle casting.
