📿Komboloi Color Practice

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
MeditationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
- 16-23 beads in your chosen color (any size, though larger/heavier beads are easier for some) - Length of cord or string - Tassel or lucky charm for decoration Alternatively: Purchase ready-made komboloi from Greek import stores or online.
◇Directions
Construction: 1. Tie a knot at one end of the cord (larger than the bead holes) 2. Thread the first bead and let it drop to the knot 3. Thread the cord through this first bead one more time to create a fixed bead 4. Thread remaining beads loosely onto the cord 5. Tie the two ends together, creating a circle 6. Attach a tassel or charm to the knotted area Traditional Use: 1. Hang the komboloi from your middle finger (non-dominant hand), palm up 2. The knot, fixed bead, and tassel rest on your fingers 3. Flip the beads up and around your first finger to land in your palm 4. Push each bead off your palm with your thumb, one at a time 5. Each bead creates a rhythmic clicking sound as it falls 6. Repeat, entering a meditative state Alternative Method: Hold the komboloi between your hands and gently hit the beads together to create the clicking sound. Practice: When you need a specific color's energy, use a komboloi of that color for several minutes. The repetitive motion and sound induce deep relaxation. At the end of this time you'll feel relaxed, rested, revitalized, and full of the energy of the color you needed. Advanced Applications: Komboloi work also enhances clairvoyance and precognition by creating the relaxed, mystical state that allows psychic perceptions to emerge. Collection: Consider making or collecting komboloi in all seven rainbow colors plus a multi-colored one for when you need energy from multiple colors simultaneously.
◇Description
A Greek prayer bead practice for absorbing color energy through repetitive motion and meditation. Komboloi (worry beads) create a rhythmic, hypnotic clicking sound that induces deep relaxation while infusing you with the energy of your chosen color.
