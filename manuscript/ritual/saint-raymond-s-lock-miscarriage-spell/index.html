🛡️Saint Raymond's Lock Miscarriage Spell

A spell requesting Saint Raymond Nonnatus's aid in preventing miscarriage, using the symbolism of a locked lock to keep the baby safe until birth.

Quantities

  • 1 red candle
  • 1 lock
  • 1 key

Directions

  1. Dedicate the candle to Saint Raymond and light it.
  2. Lock the lock and, without removing the key, place it before the candle.
  3. Leave the locked lock before the candle until the candle completely burns out, then preserve it in a safe and secret place.
  4. Do not unlock and remove the key until you are ready to give birth.
