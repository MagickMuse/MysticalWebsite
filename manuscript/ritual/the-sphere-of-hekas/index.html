🛡️The Sphere of Hekas

A complete ceremonial banishing ritual for grounding, centering, purification, and empowerment. Connects the practitioner to chthonic and ouranic forces, creates a protective psychic barrier, and invokes guardian spirits at the four directions. The ritual takes about five minutes once memorized and should be performed daily, preferably twice a day.

Quantities

Optional: a wand or athame

Directions

  1. Part 1: Conjuring the Column
  2. Stand facing east at the center of the universe.
  3. Take a deep inhalation and visualize a column of pure white light descending from the heavens, entering your crown and passing through you into the ground.
  4. This white light purifies and centers.
  5. Exhale and intone:
  6. DECENDAT COLUMBA!
  7. (The descent of the dove!)
  8. Take another deep inhalation and visualize reddish light rising from below through the column, passing through you upward.
  9. This red light vitalizes.
  10. Exhale and intone:
  11. ASCENDAT SERPENS!
  12. (The ascent of the serpent!)
  13. Inhale and feel both energies entering from above and below.
  14. Exhale and feel them flow throughout your body, impregnating every cell with power.
  15. Feel your connection between Earth and sky, underworld and heavens.
  16. With your right hand, point at your third eye and intone:
  17. I (ee)
  18. Move your hand over your heart, palm facing chest, and intone:
  19. A (ah)
  20. Move your hand lower over your genitals, turn palm upward, connect thumb and forefinger, and intone:
  21. O (oh)
  22. This grounds, centers, purifies, and empowers you to exercise authority over forces you wish to banish.
  23. The Dove and Serpent are universal symbols of chthonic (underworld) and ouranic (heavenly) forces.
  24. The IAO formula represents the whole spectrum of spheres (Moon through Saturn) ruled by Helios, the Sun.
  25. Part 2: Delineating the Boundaries
  26. Still within the column, pronounce:
  27. HEKAS HEKAS ESTE BEBELOI!
  28. (Away, away, all ye profane!)
  29. Make a fist with your left hand and place it against your heart.
  30. Cover it with your right hand and apply about five pounds of pressure.
  31. Visualize the force from the column concentrating at your heart, taking the shape of a gray sphere the size of a baseball.
  32. Release the pressure.
  33. In one motion, step forward with your left foot and throw your arms outward in the Sign of the Enterer.
  34. See the sphere grow and push back all malefic forces and harmful spirits until it stops where you wish to make the boundary, forming a wall of grayish astral force.
  35. Intone:
  36. GYRUM CARPO!
  37. (I seize the circle!)
  38. Take your wand or athame (or use your finger if working without tools) and extend your arm straight out.
  39. The tip should touch the edge of the circle.
  40. If you've extended the circle past the walls of the room, simply point toward the edge.
  41. Pivot or walk the edge and intone:
  42. CONSECRO ET BENEDICO ISTUM CIRCULUM
    UT SIT MIHI ET OMNIBUS SCUTUM AT
    PROTECTIE DEI FORTISSIMI HEKATE INVICIBILE
  43. (I consecrate and bless this circle
    That it may be to me and all a shield
    And protection in the name of the most powerful invincible goddess Hekate)
  44. Consider this sphere an invisible, impenetrable fortress surrounding you, keeping out all harmful forces and spirits of ill intent.
  45. Part 3: Invoking the Guardians
  46. The guardians in this ritual are spirits revealed by Hekate directly and bound by her as protective entities: Abaek, Pyrhum, Ermiti, and Dimgali.
  47. They can be replaced with quarter guardians of your choice, such as the four archangels (Raphael, Michael, Gabriel, and Uriel) or the spirits of the four winds (Notus, Zephyrus, Boreas, and Eurus).
  48. East: Abaek
  49. Face east and visualize Abaek at the eastern edge facing center.
  50. He has a man's body and bull's head, snorting and breathing wildly, holding two scimitars that clash together.
  51. Make a conjuring gesture and invoke:
  52. ORKIZO ABAEK!
    Bull-headed guardian of the East
    Remember your vow and fill the throne
    which has been set for you!
  53. See Abaek fill the throne and turn to face outward.
  54. South: Pyrhum
  55. Face south and visualize Pyrhum at the southern edge facing center.
  56. He has a man's body and horse's head breathing fire, holding a large ebony trident.
  57. Make a conjuring gesture and invoke:
  58. ORKIZO PYRHUM!
    Horse-headed guardian of the South
    Remember your vow and fill the throne
    which has been set for you!
  59. See Pyrhum fill the throne and turn to face outward.
  60. West: Ermiti
  61. Face west and visualize Ermiti at the western edge facing center.
  62. She has a woman's torso with serpent head and lower body, holding a net and skullcup overflowing with boiling blood.
  63. Make a conjuring gesture and invoke:
  64. ORKIZO ERMITI!
    Serpentine guardian of the West
    Remember your vow and fill the throne
    which has been set for you!
  65. See Ermiti fill the throne and turn to face outward.
  66. North: Dimgali
  67. Face north and visualize Dimgali at the northern edge facing center.
  68. She has a woman's torso with black dog's head, holding a whip in her left hand and adamantine shackles in her right.
  69. Make a conjuring gesture and invoke:
  70. ORKIZO DIMGALI!
    Bitch-headed guardian of the North
    Remember your vow and fill the throne
    which has been set for you!
  71. See Dimgali fill the throne and turn to face outward.
  72. Part 4: Closing
  73. You have grounded and centered yourself, connected with Earth and heavens, swept away obstructing energies, created a psychic barrier, and invoked guardian spirits in the four directions.
  74. Take a single deep breath and bring your hands together in front of your heart as if praying.
  75. PROCUL HINC PROCUL ITE PROFANI
    PER NOMINA DEI ATISSIMI HEKATE
  76. (Away, away, all ye profane
    In the name of the most powerful HEKATE)
  77. The wording is not critical.
  78. Latin is used as a magickal language, but you can use English translations or other appropriate words of similar meaning.
  79. Once memorized, this ritual takes about five minutes.
  80. Perform it daily, preferably twice daily, as its effectiveness tends to last through either sunset or sunrise.
