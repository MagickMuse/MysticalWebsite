Remember to charge and consecrate all crystals prior to their first use, and cleanse them regularly as needed.

Several crystals carry longstanding reputations for supporting legal matters. Incorporate one or several into your courtcase work:

  • Chalcedony — Pierce the stone and string it on a donkey's tail hair to render the wearer fortunate in legal matters.
  • Hematite — Provides protection and assistance in all legal situations. Wear or carry directly on the body.
  • Malachite — A bead necklace of malachite protects against litigation altogether. If legal proceedings have already begun, malachite still helps — it supports courtroom success as well as prevention.
  • Moonstone & Topaz — If you or someone you know tends toward excessive litigiousness, wearing both moonstones and topaz reportedly relieves one of that inclination. These can also be gifted with intention to redirect that energy in another person.
