📿Indian Gemstone Cleansing and Activation Rite

◇Form
Consecrated ItemOffering Rite
◇Method
ConsecrationEnergy WorkPrayer
◇Category
Consecration
◇Quantities
- 1 gemstone to be cleansed and activated - 1 cup of fresh milk - Quartz crystals - Sacred objects for the altar
◇Directions
1. On the evening before you intend to begin working with the stone, place it in a cup and cover it entirely with fresh milk. 2. After soaking, lift the stone out and position it on your altar while it is still damp with milk. 3. Arrange quartz crystals and any personally meaningful sacred items around it. 4. Allow the stone to rest in this charged space through the night. 5. At first light, retrieve the stone and wash away the milk residue. 6. Face east and lift the stone toward the sun as it rises above the horizon. 7. Speak your prayers, asking the stone to channel its energy toward your chosen purpose. 8. When the activation feels settled, the stone is ready to be worn, carried, or used in whatever capacity your practice requires.
◇Description
A consecration rite originating in India's rich tradition of gemstone therapy. The practitioner purifies a stone by soaking it in milk, resting it overnight on an altar flanked by quartz and sacred items, then greeting the dawn with the stone raised to the sun and spoken prayers directing its energy toward a stated purpose.
