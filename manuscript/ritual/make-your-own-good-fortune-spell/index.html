👣Make Your Own Good Fortune Spell

◇Form
Candle Rite
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Five candles of ascending size - Five paper footprints of your right foot in a row from hall to front door
◇Directions
1. Set the candles in ascending size next to each footprint in the hallway, leading toward the front door and the largest candle. 2. Light the smallest candle, candle 1, and step on the first footprint, saying: "Step 1, good luck I make anew, within five years, I will win through." Light candle 2 and step on the second footprint, saying: "Step 2, a natural expression of what will be my due progression to lasting good fortune." Light candle 3 and step on the third footprint, saying: "Step 3, Why should I hesitate? The future now is looking great." Light candle 4 and step on the fourth footprint, saying: "Each step I take, my fortune makes." Light candle 5 and step on the fifth footprint, saying: "Step 5, I acknowledge my potentiality, grasping the infinite possibility of making my own fortune." Open the front door and step outside, saying: "The world is mine. I make my own good fortune." Come in and close the door, blowing out each candle in reverse order, making a wish for each. 3. Perform Thursday.
◇Description
A Thursday spell to make your own good fortune, lighting five candles of ascending size while stepping on paper footprints from hall to front door to acknowledge infinite possibility of making own fortune.
