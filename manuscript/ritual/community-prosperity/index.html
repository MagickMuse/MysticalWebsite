A Mabon herbal working that kindles imagination and balance in a neighborhood facing hard times. Uses goldenseal, lovage root, and dried apricots to feed optimism into the collective spirit and open new pathways for prosperity.

Quantities

  • One part goldenseal root
  • One part lovage root
  • Three dried apricot ears
  • Water for brewing
  • Strainer

Directions

  1. Mix one part each of goldenseal and lovage root, and add three dried apricot ears.
  2. Brew the herbal formula.
  3. After you strain this herbal formula, you may eat the rehydrated apricot ears.
  4. Take in a little prosperity for yourself!
  5. Then chant:
  6. Goodness, come to us all!
    Good luck befall us all!
    New ways open, good things show
    within, without more treasures
    than we ever before did know.
