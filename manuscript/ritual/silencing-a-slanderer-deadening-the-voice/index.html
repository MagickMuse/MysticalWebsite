🕯️Silencing a Slanderer (Deadening the Voice)

An Ozark ritual also known as "deadening the voice." The goal is to silence someone who is slandering you by magickally "killing" their tongue. This does not mean physical harm but rather that they will no longer be interested enough to talk about you at all. Your name will be dead in their mouth.

Quantities

  • Red paper
  • Scissors
  • Black pen or marker
  • Black taper candle, any size, with holder
  • Heat-safe tray or plate
  • Lighter or matches
  • Identifying materials from target (hair, nail clippings, clothing, etc.; optional)
  • Black string
  • Tongs, metal chopsticks, or a metal knitting needle

Directions

  1. Begin by making the symbolic tongue of your enemy.
  2. Cut out a rectangle from the red paper big enough to fit in the palm of your hand from wrist to fingertips.
  3. It should only be a couple of inches wide.
  4. Round off one end of this rectangle to form a tongue shape.
  5. Using black ink, write the full name (FIRST MIDDLE LAST), birthdate, and the zodiac symbols for their Sun, Moon, and rising sign across the length of the tongue.
  6. Include as much of this information as you know.
  7. Light the black taper candle and place the candleholder on a heat-safe tray or plate to catch the materials you will be burning.
  8. Place your paper tongue in your left palm so that it is parallel to your fingers.
  9. Recite the verbal charm.
  10. Where indicated with an X, clap your hands together with the tongue in between.
  11. Here little bird
    Your voice so sweet
    Into the trap I set!
    Snap! Pop! Crack! X
    Pop! Crack! Snap! X
    Crack! Snap! Pop! X
  12. Fold the tongue in half.
  13. You can now add any identifying materials you might have in between the folded halves.
  14. While you finish the rest of the charm, wrap the folded tongue and identifying materials with your black string.
  15. Into the trap I set!
    Here little bird
    I have your voice!
    Return never
    Never return
    Till the Moon is the Sun
    And the Sun is the Moon!
  16. At this point, hold the tongue in the candle flame using metal tongs or metal chopsticks.
  17. Alternatively, pierce through it using a thin knitting needle or small knife and hold it in the flames until it is completely burned away.
