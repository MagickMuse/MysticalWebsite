🚗Safe Commuting Angel Elemiah Spell

◇Form
CharmPouch
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickProtection
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A green candle - Small home-related items such as miniature pictures of family and pets, a favorite lucky charm, a Saint Christopher medallion, or a tiny crystal angel - Dried lavender or sandalwood or rose potpourri you have used at home - A green drawstring bag
◇Directions
1. Light the green candle, saying: "Angel Elemiah, who shields all commuters from harm, bring protection on my daily/weekly travels." Fill the bag by its light, saying the same words softly over and over. 2. Blow three times into the candle and three times into the bag, saying after each breath: "Elemiah, bring protection on my daily weekly/travels." Leave the bag in front of the candle until it burns through. 3. Keep the bag in the glove compartment of the vehicle. 4. Each morning hold it and repeat: "Elemiah, bring protection on my daily weekly/travels." Re-empower monthly by burning a green candle, holding the bag, and saying all the spell words, again, leaving the candles to burn. 5. Perform the night before your weekly or regular commute.
◇Description
A night-before-commute spell for safe commuting, using green candle with home items, potpourri, and bag to call Angel Elemiah for daily or weekly travel protection.
