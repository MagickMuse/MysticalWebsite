💬Wednesday

A Wednesday rite that prepares the triangle of manifestation and either a show stone or a homemade mirror speculum, along with its wormwood blackened pedestal, for use in scrying and greater divination.

Quantities

A black cloth or board for the table of practice; white paint or tape for the triangle of manifestation; a crystal or glass show stone, or a concave clock or watch glass for a speculum; a slab of wood for the pedestal; black paint or enamel mixed with powdered wormwood

Directions

  1. On a Wednesday, exorcise and consecrate your black cloth or board, your triangle marking, and your pedestal wood with fire and water and a Mercurial incense inside a cast circle, using a charm such as "Blessed be thou, focus of power."
  2. Paint or tape the triangle, about twelve inches to a side, onto the black cloth or board.
  3. Hollow a shallow rounded dip into a wood slab about six inches by one inch, then stain or paint it black with paint mixed with powdered wormwood, continuing your charm as you work.
  4. For a show stone, wash the stone itself in a wormwood infusion, set it on the dried pedestal, then cense and seal both.
  5. For a speculum, coat the convex side of a concave clock or watch glass with two coats of black enamel and wormwood, glue it curved side inward into a similarly blackened pedestal, then cense and seal.
  6. Keep the finished show stone or speculum wrapped in black cloth, ideally silk, when not in use.

See Triangle for more on the triangle of manifestation this pedestal and covering are built around.
