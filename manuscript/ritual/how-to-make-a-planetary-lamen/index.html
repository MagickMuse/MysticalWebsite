A comprehensive guide to constructing a double-sided lamen for planetary magick. The front invokes the chosen spirit, while the back provides protection through geometric figures, Hebrew letters, and divine names. Includes planetary entity correspondences and symbolism tables.

Quantities

  • Paper, card stock, parchment, poster board, or foam-core board
  • Paints in planetary colors (violet and yellow for Jupiter example)
  • Chain, cord, or collar for wearing
  • Laminating materials (optional, for thin materials)
  • Tools for drawing sigils and symbols

Directions

  1. Begin by choosing the planetary force you want to invoke.
  2. Determine which spirit or angel will best suit your needs, as well as which angel or divine force can oversee the spirit and add protection.
  3. In planetary magick, the spirit is the entity most closely aligned with our plane of existence and is therefore most directly in charge of enacting magickal change.
  4. Yet the spirit is in some ways a blind, potent force that must always be governed and guided by angelic, archangelic, and Higher Divine Forces.
  5. Select your spiritual entities from the planetary correspondences.
  6. Each planet has associated godnames, angels, intelligences, and spirits.
  7. Choose imagery that corresponds to the energies you have selected using the Hebrew names and symbolism table.
  8. The Qabalistic number of the planet determines its geometric figure.
  9. This figure, along with the Hebrew letter assigned to the planet, can be used as a powerful seal of protection for the back of the lamen.
  10. However, these are not the only figures of defense you can choose.
  11. For any planetary lamen, you could use the figure of a hexagram or a heptagram.
  12. Beyond geometric figures, any number of protective symbols could be applied, such as the Rose Cross, the Circled Cross, the Calvary Cross, the Equal-Armed Cross, the Eye of Horus, the Golden Dawn's Banner of the East, the All-Seeing Eye, or the Hamsa Hand.
  13. Construct the basic lamen out of your chosen material.
  14. It can be square or circular depending on the planetary correspondence.
  15. Paint it in the primary color ornamented with the secondary color from the planetary symbolism table.
  16. A lamen made out of thin material such as paper or parchment can be laminated after it is finished to protect it.
  17. Leave extra material at the top of the lamen to form a loop with a hole in it, so you can attach the lamen to a chain, cord, or collar.
  18. You could also have two such loops on either end of the top of the lamen, which will prevent it from flipping around when it is worn.
  19. On the front of the lamen, add the name and sigil of your chosen spirit to the center of the geometric figure, along with the Hebrew letters of the name.
  20. The names of the planetary intelligence, angel, and godname can be inscribed around the sides of the figure.
  21. The back of the lamen should be marked with the protective figure (hexagram, the planet's geometric figure, or other protective symbol), the symbol and seal of the planet, and the Hebrew letter, along with the godname and other forces that govern the actions of the spirit.
  22. After you have finished creating the lamen, attach a cord or chain to it that is big enough to slip over your head so you can wear the lamen in ritual.
  23. The lamen is worn during ritual to express your authority and protect you during spirit work, and should only be used in its intended ritual.

A lamen is a consecrated double-sided magickal tool worn during ritual to express the practitioner's authority and provide protection during spirit work. The front side is used for evocation and invocation, while the back side is used for protection. In planetary magick, lamens connect the practitioner with planetary spirits, intelligences, angels, and divine forces.

Alternative Protective Symbols

While the planetary geometric figures and Hebrew letters provide traditional protection, other symbols can be used on the back of any lamen:

Geometric Figures: Pentagram (for elemental or zodiacal lamens), Hexagram (for any planetary lamen), Heptagram (for planetary work), Triangle (for alchemical lamens)

Traditional Symbols: Rose Cross, Circled Cross, Calvary Cross, Equal-Armed Cross, Eye of Horus, Golden Dawn's Banner of the East, All-Seeing Eye, Hamsa Hand

Example: Lamen of Hismael

For a ritual to obtain good fortune, joy, and increase in finances, you might create a lamen for Hismael, the Spirit of Jupiter. The geometric figure associated with Jupiter is the square, so create either a square lamen or a circular lamen with a square figure on it. Paint the lamen violet ornamented with yellow. On the front, place the name and sigil of Hismael in the center of the square, along with the Hebrew letters of the name. Inscribe the names of the planetary intelligence (Iophiel), angel (Sachiel), and godname (El) around the sides of the square. On the back, mark the protective figure of the hexagram, the symbol and Seal of Jupiter, the Hebrew letter Kaph (כ), and the godname along with other forces that govern the actions of the spirit.

Consecration Ritual

After constructing your lamen, it must be consecrated to activate its magickal properties. This example uses Jupiter correspondences, but the structure can be adapted for any planetary lamen.

Altar Setup: Cover your altar with a violet cloth (or blue, another Jupiter color). Use the Golden Dawn system of flashing colors by placing a circle of yellow ribbon, twine, or cord on the center of the violet cloth (or orange on blue). These complementary colors create an optical vibration that aids magick. Place a violet or blue candle just outside the circle on the far side of the altar. Choose appropriate incense for Jupiter such as saffron, anise, clove, cedar, sage, pine, juniper, copal, or a mixture. Have violet or blue linen cloth ready for wrapping the lamen when not in use. You will also need a chalice of water. Place the lamen in the middle of the circle on your altar.

Fourfold Breath: Begin either seated or standing. Bring awareness to your breath using the Golden Dawn's Fourfold Breath exercise, which emphasizes the number four of Jupiter. Empty the lungs and remain empty while counting to four. Inhale, counting to four, filling to the throat. Hold this breath while counting to four. Exhale, counting to four till the lungs are empty. Continue for two or three minutes until relaxed.

The Consecration: If seated, stand in front of the altar. Light the candle and incense. Vibrate the Qabalistic name of power YHVH (pronounced Yod-Heh-Vav-Heh in slow, long syllables), the Tetragrammaton or four-lettered name of God. As you vibrate the name, visualize a square directly over the lamen. Trace the lines of this square clockwise starting from the upper left corner. While tracing the first line, inhale deeply then slowly vibrate YOD. When tracing the second line, intone HEH. Trace the third line and vibrate VAV. Trace the fourth line and vibrate HEH.

Take up the chalice of water. Dip your index and middle fingers into the water, then trace an equal-armed cross over the lamen while saying:

I purify with water.

Put the chalice down and take up the incense. Wave the incense in the form of an equal-armed cross directly over the lamen and say:

I consecrate with fire.

Replace the incense. Trace another clockwise square around the lamen, this time vibrating the name EL four times as you trace the lines.

With your index and middle fingers, trace the symbol of Jupiter over the lamen, then say:

The Celestial is above and the Terrestrial is beneath. And between the Above and the Below, the forces of magick awaken. I call upon the Powers and Forces governing the nature, place, and authority of the planet Jupiter under the esteemed name of Tzedek, by the majesty of the divine name EL, with which in Earth life and language, I ascribe the letter Kaph, to which is assigned the Angel SACHIEL, the Intelligence IOPHIEL, and the Spirit HISMAEL. May the Divine One under the holy name of EL confirm mystic and potent influence upon this lamen of the Spirit Hismael, which I dedicate to purity and the work of Magick! Mighty EL, direct your Angel Sachiel and your intelligence Iophiel to govern and to guide your Spirit Hismael in the workings of Jupiter for which this lamen has been created. May the wearing of this lamen strengthen me in the work of the Magick of Light. May it aid and protect me in all endeavors which appertain to the sphere of Jupiter, including the pursuit of fortune, happiness, prosperity, mercy, joy, expansion, leadership, and benevolent power. So mote it!

Imagine a halo of light surrounding the lamen, infusing it with divine energy. Wrap your lamen in cloth until you are ready to use it. Extinguish the candle and close the rite.

Usage and Care

Wear the completed and consecrated lamen during ritual work with the chosen planetary force. The structure of the ritual may vary: invocation, evocation, scrying, talisman consecration, or any other formal magickal ceremony that concerns the planetary sphere. The lamen expresses your authority as a practitioner and provides protection from any possible negative effects. It should only be used in its intended ritual and stored carefully wrapped in appropriate cloth when not in use. You can make lamens for any spiritual force needed and pinpoint the exact spirits and angels you want to work with. A consecrated lamen makes any magickal work with the planetary force more powerful and focused.
