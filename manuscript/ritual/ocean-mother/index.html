🐚Ocean Mother

◇Quantities
- Seven blue, white, or silver candles - One stick of sandalwood incense - A piece of blue, white, or silver cloth - A piece of blue, white, or silver ribbon - A piece of blue, white, or silver paper, with your wish written in silver ink - A small amount of fish food (from a pet store) - Seven small offerings (tiny shells, miniature perfumes, sea glass, soap, perfume, yams, etc.)
◇Directions
Preparation 1. Gather your materials and take time selecting your 7 offerings. Choose items that hold emotional or symbolic meaning for you and are appropriate to Yemaya (flowers, grains, jewelry, crystals, etc.). 2. Lay the cloth flat on your altar or sacred space. 3. Place the wish paper in the center. 4. Add your offerings on top of the paper. 5. Sprinkle the fish food over everything. 6. Wrap the cloth tightly around the contents and secure it with the ribbon, tying seven knots. 7. You should now have a neat bundle that feels magickal and beautiful. The Spell 1. Go to the beach while the tide is coming in, ideally under moonlight. 2. Arrange the 7 candles in the sand however you like, then light them. 3. Light the incense, raise power, and focus. 4. Take the bundle and pass it through the incense smoke 7 times. 5. Bury or plant one candle in the sand near your chosen offrenda spot. 6. With deep sincerity, recite: Ocean Mother, Queen of the Sea, Hear my plea and answer me. Mother of Secrets, Mother of Fish, Accept my gift, and grant my wish. By the power of moonlight, Grant my wish if it be right. 7. Leave the bundle at the shore where the waves can pull it out to sea. You can also toss it from a jetty or rock if needed. 8. Remain until the candles burn out, then ground your energy. Afterward • If the tide immediately pulls the bundle out, it is a positive sign. • If the bundle is returned or stays behind, it may mean your wish will not be granted or may not be in your best interest. • Give thanks to Yemaya regardless, and reflect on the ethical clarity of your request.
◇Description
To petition Yemaya (or any Ocean Mother aspect) for help, blessings, or wish fulfillment, especially related to emotional healing, family, fertility, or protection. This ritual draws power from moonlight, the ocean's tide, and heartfelt offerings.

Yemaya, the Ocean Mother, is a benevolent goddess known for helping human beings in countless ways. Originally an African river goddess, her worship traveled to the New World with Africans who had been kidnapped into slavery. Her veneration spread throughout North America, South America, and the Caribbean. Though she is known by different names in different lands, Yemaya is universally recognized for her willingness to answer human calls for help.

Best Timing

Cast this spell at a beach on a moonlit night while the tide is coming in. The spell is most powerful beneath a Full Moon or a Crescent Moon, so consult a calendar to time your working accordingly.

About the Offerings

Appropriate offerings to Yemaya include:

  • Fans
  • Flowers
  • Jewelry
  • Sea shells
  • Crystals
  • Coral
  • Soap
  • Perfume
  • Grain
  • Yams

Use small items for this spell, such as tiny shells, pieces of yam, or miniature bottles of perfume.

Preparing the Offering Bundle

The spell begins the moment you first consider which offerings to use. It grows in power as you gather them and assemble the offering bundle. The spell reaches its peak when you present the bundle to Yemaya.

Study the tidal patterns at your chosen beach for several days before casting. Choose an offering site that the tide will certainly reach. If you need to toss the offering from a jetty or down onto rocks, that works perfectly well.

Assemble all ingredients except the candles and incense. Enter a magickal state of mind and create sacred space around your working area.

Lay the cloth flat upon your altar or working surface. Place the paper with your wish in the center, then arrange your seven offerings on top. Sprinkle fish food over everything. Wrap the cloth around the contents to create a small bundle, then secure it tightly with the ribbon, tying exactly seven knots. When finished, you should have a small, attractively wrapped package.

Casting the Spell

Go to the beach at your chosen time. Arrange the seven candles in the sand in whatever pattern pleases you, then light them. Enter a magickal state of mind and raise power.

Light the incense from one of the candles and plant it in the sand. Pass your offering bundle through the incense smoke seven times, then place it reverently at your chosen offering site.

Speak aloud:

Ocean Mother, Queen of the Sea,
Hear my plea and answer me.
Mother of Secrets, Mother of Fish,
Accept my gift, and grant my wish.
By the power of moonlight,
Grant my wish if it be right.

Monitor the candles until they burn themselves out completely, then ground the power you raised.

Reading the Signs

It is best if you can remain on the beach until the tide comes all the way in and then recedes again. The ocean's response to your offering carries meaning:

  • If the waves immediately snatch your offering and drag it out to sea: This is a favorable omen. Your wish has been received.
  • If the sea does not accept your offering, or if it is returned with an incoming tide: Your wish is not likely to be granted in its current form.

Afterward

Give thanks to Yemaya regardless of the outcome. If your offering was returned, reflect on the ethical clarity of your request. Consider whether your wish might cause harm or conflict with a greater good. You may rephrase your wish and try again at a future time.
