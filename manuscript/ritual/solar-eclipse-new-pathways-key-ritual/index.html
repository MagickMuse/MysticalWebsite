A door-opening candle spell that harnesses the supercharged new moon energy of a solar eclipse to step into new paths aligned with the soul. Using a charged key, tarot trio, sun and moon candles, and a red ribbon, this working blesses the way forward and creates a lasting charm for ongoing road-opening work.

Quantities

  • Tarot cards: The Star, the Two of Wands, The World
  • Matches or lighter
  • Sandalwood or cinnamon incense
  • Yellow, orange, or red candle (sun candle)
  • White or silver candle (moon candle)
  • Two candleholders
  • A key (blank, skeleton, or charm key)
  • Red ribbon
  • Moonstone and sunstone (optional)

Directions

  1. Lay the three tarot cards in a row from left to right: The Star, the Two of Wands, and The World.
  2. The Star speaks to destiny, spiritual alignment, and the faith required to move through turbulent eclipse energy.
  3. The Two of Wands places you at the threshold, holding the future in your hands, aware of every possibility stretching before you.
  4. The World represents the limitless realm of potential ahead, the close of one cycle and the dawn of another.
  5. Light the incense and spend time with the cards.
  6. Reflect on what this solar eclipse means for you personally: what you are moving toward, what you are leaving behind, and what new path your soul is ready to walk.
  7. Place the sun candle (yellow, orange, or red) in its holder to the right of the cards, and the moon candle (white or silver) to the left, both positioned behind the spread.
  8. Set moonstone beside the moon candle and sunstone beside the sun candle if using them, or hold one in each hand during meditation.
  9. Cup the key in both hands and close your eyes.
  10. Visualize yourself standing before a door on the threshold of your new path.
  11. Feel anticipation and excitement move through your body.
  12. When ready, light the candles and speak aloud:
  13. "As the new moon and sun align
    I take heed of the signs.
    As the moon eclipses the sun
    A new pathway is begun."
  14. Turn the key in the air between the two candles as though unlocking the door.
  15. With your new path in mind, thread the red ribbon through the key's hole and tie it in a knot, binding the thread of fate to your intention.
  16. Pass the key and ribbon slowly through the incense smoke, then lay them on the altar between the two candles to charge in the candlelight.
  17. Allow the candles to burn down fully.
  18. Keep the charged key as a personal charm or altar piece.
  19. Use it in future spells tied to this intention, pair it with road-opening workings, or string beads and supportive stones onto the ribbon to carry it as a blessing charm for the path ahead.
