⚠️ This working is rooted in Yoruba and African diaspora spiritual traditions. Approach with study and respect for the originating cultures. A petitioning ritual to Yemaya, orisha of the sea, using seven white roses and seven waves to ask for luck and security.

Quantities

Seven long-stemmed white roses
Blue silk ribbon
Seven small white or blue candles
Matches
Access to the sea

Directions

  1. Very gently rub each white rose against your face and body while petitioning Yemaya for luck and security.
  2. Wrap the roses into a bouquet with blue silk ribbon.
  3. Take the bouquet and candles to the beach.
  4. Hollow a space in the sand, set the candles inside it, and light them.
  5. Wade into the sea carrying the bouquet.
  6. Cradle it as seven waves wash over you, then respectfully release the bouquet into the sea as an offering to Yemaya.
