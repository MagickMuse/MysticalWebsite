🍳Pan

A wedding threshold ritual in which the bride momentarily embodies Lakshmi, walking good fortune into the home through her first entrance as a new resident.

Quantities

Pan of paint
Access to the home's front door

Directions

  1. Before the bride enters the home for the first time following the marriage ceremony, place a pan filled with paint at the front door.
  2. The bride removes her shoes, steps into the wet paint, and walks through the threshold into the home, imprinting good fortune with each step.
