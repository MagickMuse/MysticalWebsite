📿Bracelet Guard from Ill Intentions

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickSalt MagickProtectionConsecration
◇Category
Consecration
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Red coral or jet bracelet (for adults) - Jade or pink coral bracelet (for children) - 1 candle per bracelet (in circle) - 1 bowl of mixed salt and pepper
◇Directions
1. Hold each bracelet in turn, naming the recipient, saying, "May I/she/he be enclosed in this circle of love from all harm and ill intentions." Light each candle in turn and hold its bracelet to the light, saying, "May I/she/he be enclosed in this circle of light from all harm and ill intentions." Enclose the bracelets and candles in three counterclockwise circles of sprinkled salt and pepper, saying, "May I/she/he be enclosed in this circle of fierce protection from all harm and ill intentions." Leave everything in place until the candles are burned. 2. Dispose of any remaining salt and pepper under a running faucet, saying, "Wash away all harm and ill intentions." Give the bracelets to the intended recipients. 3. Perform Monday morning.
◇Description
A Monday morning bracelet empowerment ritual using coral or jade with candles and salt/pepper circles for protection from envy.
