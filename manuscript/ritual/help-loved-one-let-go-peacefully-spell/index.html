🕊️Help Loved One Let Go Peacefully Spell

A spell to help friend or family member let go of life peacefully when they are afraid, burning three flower petals in descending candles to pass from our arms to those waiting in love.

Quantities

  • Three brown or dark golden flowers in a vase
  • Three purple candles in descending order of size, left to right
  • A candlesnuffer or cup
  • A bowl of soil

Directions

  1. Light the tallest candle and burn a single petal from one of the flowers in it, saying: "Dear [name], do not be afraid to let go. When you are ready to move on, we let you go willingly from our love to the love hereafter. You will pass from our arms to those who are waiting in love to welcome you." Burn the petal in the candle and drop it into the soil.
  2. Light the second candle from the first and burn a petal from the second flower in it, extinguishing the first candle and dropping the burned petal to the earth, repeating the words.
  3. Light the third candle from the second.
  4. Take a petal from the third flower and again burn it, saying the words once more.
  5. Extinguish the second candle.
  6. Let the final candle burn.
  7. Release the remaining petals on the flowers one by one to the winds.
  8. Perform when it feels right.
