These tea potions awaken your spirituality and encourage spiritual development. Two variations are provided.

Version 1: Jasmine with Passionflower

Brew jasmine tea. Add a few drops of passionflower juice. Jasmine's lunar energy combined with passionflower's transcendent qualities creates a deeply spiritual brew.

Version 2: Green Tea with Juniper

Brew green tea. Add five juniper berries to the cup. DO NOT EAT THE JUNIPER BERRIES. Juniper berries can be found where gourmet spices are sold.

Optional Enhancement

Using a white, violet, or light blue cup enhances the spiritual energy of these potions.

Stirring Symbol

Trace the shape of Berkanan into the surface of the tea as you stir.
