A healing visualization and dreamwork journey in which the practitioner rides a dream bear to receive traditional medical wisdom and remedies for a known but untreated illness.

Quantities

Bear image or object (3D preferred: stone fetish or toy bear; photograph or flat image acceptable)

Directions

  1. Take a bear image or object to bed.
  2. Three-dimensional images are ideal; a Zuni stone fetish or a sufficiently bear-like stuffed animal allows you to hold it while journeying.
  3. A photograph works if that is all available.
  4. Prepare yourself for sleep, then turn out the light while keeping your awareness active.
  5. Visualize your bear, noting that the dream bear need not resemble the physical image.
  6. See the bear clearly, then visualize the moment of meeting: approach, mount the bear, and tell it what you need to find.
  7. Then release control and let the bear guide the journey.
  8. At some point you will likely fall asleep, though waking visualization is also possible.
  9. The journey should continue in your dreams.
  10. When the journey concludes, ensure the bear returns you to where you began.
  11. If this does not happen in the dream, complete the return consciously while awake.
  12. Repeat as needed until the information you require has been received.
