💉Raise Pain Threshold Invasive Treatment

A waning moon or Saturday spell for raising pain threshold or coping with invasive long-term treatment, breathing into blue candle then burning twig and paper to resist pain powerfully.

Quantities

  • A deep blue candle
  • A piece of paper and a blue pen
  • A small leafy twig

Directions

  1. Light the candle and say: "I take this light to fill me with the power to resist pain/to respond favorably and powerfully to my treatment." Write the words on the paper and say them again.
  2. Breathe softly three times into the candle and repeat the words three times, once after each breath.
  3. Let the candle burn in a safe place.
  4. Wrap the paper firmly around the twig, if necessary securing it with string, and burn the twig and paper outdoors in a metal container such as a bucket and scatter the ashes.
  5. As you do so, repeat the words five times.
  6. Cast the spell again during each waning moon or on Saturdays until the treatment is over.
  7. Perform any waning moon day (or Saturday).
