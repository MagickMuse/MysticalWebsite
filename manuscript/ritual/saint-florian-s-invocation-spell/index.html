An invocation to Saint Florian, patron of firefighters and spiritual firefighter himself. A red candle is burned before an image of a burning building as a petition for protection.

Quantities

Image of a burning house or building, red candle

Directions

  1. Find an image of a burning house or building.
  2. Burn a red candle in front of it and speak to Saint Florian, expressing your fears and desires for protection.
  3. His ritual may be performed at any time, but Sundays are preferred.
  4. May 4 is his feast day and an excellent time for petition.
  5. Saint Florian may also be invoked in any crisis for emergency aid.

Saint Florian is the patron of firefighters. It is his job to guard them, protect them, and bring them home safely. He is also invoked to protect homes and property from fire.

His annual feast falls on May 4th, an excellent time for petition. His ritual may be initiated, repeated, and reinforced at any time during the year. Given the choice, the best day to petition Saint Florian is a Sunday.

  1. Find an image of a burning house or building.
  2. Burn a red candle in front of it.
  3. Speak to Saint Florian, expressing your fears and what you are asking him to protect.

Saint Florian may also be invoked during any crisis for emergency aid.
