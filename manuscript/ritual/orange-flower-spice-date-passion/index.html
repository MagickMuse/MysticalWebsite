🟠Orange Flower Spice Date Passion

An hour before your date arrives spell for orange flower and spice ritual to spice up date at home if would-be partner lacks passion, plucking four orange flower petals adding orange spice and chopped orange pepper swirling four times then dividing into four bags hiding in four corners saying liven up loosen up spice is nice passion's food.

Quantities

  • Four small orange flowers
  • A bowl
  • An orange-colored spice, such as saffron, cinnamon, turmeric, or curry powder
  • An orange pepper, chopped small and deseeded
  • Four small orange paper or cloth bags

Directions

  1. Pluck the petals one by one and put them in the bowl, saying: "Lover, do not be so slow, passion's fun, don't you know. Warmer be, filled with spice, then this date will be twice as nice." Add some spice and chopped pepper to the bowl, repeating the spell words.
  2. Swirl the bowl four times, twice in each direction, saying: "Liven up, loosen up, don't be so sedate. Spice is nice, let's heat the mood, a spicy date is passion's food." Divide the mix among the four bags, tie, and shake each one, then hide them in the four corners of the room where you will eat or relax.
  3. Add the remaining spice and pepper to the meal.
  4. Perform an hour before your date arrives.
