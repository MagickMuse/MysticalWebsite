Ritual Gestures

Gestures are physical expressions of intent used in Sabbat rituals, invocations, and energy work. Each position of the body communicates something specific to the forces being called upon.

Indicating the Goddess

Hold the palms up with arms open and raised. This posture receives and honors the feminine Divine.

Indicating the God

Cross the arms against the chest with palms turned inward. This gesture is used during Solar phases of passage: Lughnassadh, Mabon, and Samhain.

Expressing Unity

Begin with palms facing outward, gathering energy from the air. Turn the hands with palms inward and draw them to the chest, blending external and internal energies at the heart. Then thrust the hands back outward in front of the body with the heel of the palms down, fingertips up, pushing outward to return excess energy.

Use this gesture once for unity, twice for balance, and three times for completion. It may be used when calling the Quarters as well as during invocation of the Lady and the Lord.

Drawing Divine Blessings

Place the palms inward against the chest, then extend the arms with hands motioned away, palms down. Move the hands outward to release excessive energies. This gesture draws Divine blessings and energy inward before returning the surplus.

Accessing Universal Energy

Raise the arms and hands into a teepee position overhead, with the heel of the palms nearly touching, the center of the palms angled back, and the fingers bent inward like a blossom or bowl. Bring the hands down on either side of the head, palms up, until level with the shoulders. This draws Divine energy into balance with the personal energy of the third eye and crown, augmenting receptivity.

Hold this position through the invocation. Then move to the hands against the chest and motion outward to complete the cycle.
