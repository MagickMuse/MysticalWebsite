💀Consecration of the Skull of Hermekate/Hekahermes

A consecration rite to awaken a ram or goat skull as a xoanon embodying Hermekate/Hekahermes, performed at the waxing or waning half moon. The Hierophant sets the skull above rising frankincense and myrrh smoke, stands ungirt with hair unbound, and with candles, flowers, wines, and breads arrayed. She and all present chant both hymns, then touch the skull and recite the Consecration Hymn, each exposing themselves and feeding the spirit from their own body. The skull is then bedecked with flowers and offered the wines and breads before all share a meal in its company.

Quantities

Skull: 1 ram or goat skull, set upon a stone or staff
Frankincense: burned beneath the skull
Myrrh: burned beneath the skull
Black Candle: 1
White Candle: 1
Red flowers: placed between the candles
White flowers: placed between the candles
Red wine: 1 cup, placed between the candles
White wine: 1 cup, placed between the candles
Black bread: 1 loaf, placed between the candles
White bread: 1 loaf, placed between the candles

Directions

  1. Perform at the waxing or waning half moon.
  2. Set a ram or goat skull upon a stone or staff above rising clouds of frankincense and myrrh. Light the incense.
  3. Stand before the skull ungirt, with hair unbound, having purified yourself and the space.
  4. Light one black candle and one white candle. Between them place: red and white flowers, cups of red and white wine, and two loaves of white and black bread.
  5. Chant the Hymn to Hekahermes in full, then chant the Hermekate Drum Chant in full.
  6. All present then touch their hands to the skull and call aloud the Consecration Hymn: "Within, Without / Above, Below / Right, Left / Quick, Slow / East, West / North, South / First, Last / Flood, Drouth / Young, Old / Weak, Strong / Good, Bad / Right, Wrong / Before, After / True, False / Male, Female / Virtue, Fault / Quickening, Withering / Hungry, Fed / Feral, Tame / Alive, Dead / O Hermekate! O Hekahermes! / O ANDROGYNE in whom all opposites resolve, / Behold the one who calls you forth / Reciting your holy and exquisite names, / Invoking all extremes that you contain. / Behold! Ungirt, I bare myself to you in this place / To see the invisible, touch the intangible, / Hear silence, taste void, know the unknown, / And claim that which is neither others', nor my own. / Feel my hands upon your horns, and reap / The seeds you've sewn. Hear my voice, see my shape / Smell my scent! Behold, I expose to you / My right self; my left self; taste me, and take / Essential essence, holy fire, the sacred stuff of life. / Pull it from me as a suckling kid, and in return / Grant me wisdom, cunning, power, strength, and inspiration / And be my friend hereafter."
  7. At the words "Behold, I expose to you my right self, my left self," expose your chest to Hekahermes/Hermekate. At the word "taste me," touch the skull's mouth to your breast and hold it there, allowing the newly-consecrated being to draw force from your body.
  8. All present repeat this welcome and feeding in like manner.
  9. Once all have finished, bedeck the skull with flowers and touch its mouth to the two wines and the two breads in offering.
  10. Set the skull back in its place of honor. The Hierophant and companions then share a meal in the presence of the xoanon.
