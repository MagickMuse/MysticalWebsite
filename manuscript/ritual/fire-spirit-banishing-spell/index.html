A botanical amulet spell for banishing Fire Spirits and preventing their return. Bundles made from specific plants are posted near doors and windows as protective barriers. Note: some of the listed plants are poisonous and must be handled with care.

Quantities

  • One or a combination of: water lilies, liverwort, spurge, mandrake, leeks, watercress, water mint, plantain, henbane (note: some are poisonous)
  • Cord or twine for bundling

Directions

  1. Form bundles from any one or combination of the listed botanicals, exercising caution as some are poisonous.
  2. Post these bundles as amulets near doors and windows to banish Fire Spirits and prevent them from returning.
