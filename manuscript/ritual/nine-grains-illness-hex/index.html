🗯️Curses🪄HexSkin

Nine types of grain are brewed together and the brew is poured out at a crossroads or outside the target's home. If the target steps over it, they fall ill, typically with an irritating skin disorder. Notably, this can also be used as an antidote if the caster believes they have been bewitched.

Quantities

Nine types of grain (any variety)

Directions

  1. Brew nine types of grain together, then pour the brew out at a crossroads or outside the home of the one you wish to bewitch.
  2. If the target steps over the potion, they will fall ill, typically with an irritating skin disorder.
  3. The same brew may also be used as an antidote if you feel you have been bewitched yourself.
