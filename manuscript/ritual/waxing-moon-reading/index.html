🔮Waxing Moon Reading

◇Method
DivinationVisualizationLunar
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Tarot or oracle deck (crystals, runes, or meditation may be substituted)
◇Directions
1. Settle into a calm, meditative space in whatever way feels natural to you. 2. Once centered, shuffle your cards with intention. 3. Call upon your spirit guides and speak aloud: 4. "With the growth of the moonlight, grant me foresight. Illuminate for me, the path forward this night." 5. Draw one card for each of the following questions, each anchored in the energy of a specific waxing phase: for the Waxing Crescent, ask what action you can take to support the manifestation of your intention; for the First Quarter, ask what challenges or obstacles may arise along the way; for the Waxing Gibbous, ask what wisdom you need to embrace in order to succeed. 6. You may draw more than one card per question if called to do so. 7. Sit with the imagery and symbolism of each card. 8. Notice the suit, the figures, and the feeling evoked; is the card urging action, or offering a caution? 9. A Swords card may point to mindset or communication; a Cups card may speak to emotional undercurrents or intuition. 10. Use the guidance received to map a path forward through the waxing cycle. 11. Let it inform the rituals, meditations, or reflective practices you choose in the days ahead, especially when you sense roadblocks emerging. 12. If you completed the Intention-Setting Candle Blessing, keep your intention paper nearby to hold your goals clearly in view as you read.
◇Description
A tarot or oracle card reading to divine guidance across the waxing lunar phases, supporting the manifestation work begun at the new moon. Use the insight gleaned to build an action plan and navigate any challenges that arise.
