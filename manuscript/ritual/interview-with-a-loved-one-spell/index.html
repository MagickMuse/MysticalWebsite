A complex fourteen-day necromantic ritual attributed to Eliphas Levi, designed to contact a deceased person with whom the practitioner shared a close loving relationship. Fourteen days of preparation culminate in a final ceremony involving fire, frankincense, and sustained silence.

Quantities

  • Portrait of the deceased
  • White fabric (to veil the portrait)
  • Fresh flowers daily (for 14 days)
  • 1 candle or lamp
  • Fragrant incense meaningful to the deceased (benzoin or mimosa if uncertain)
  • Bread, fruit, and wine
  • Cypress wood
  • Frankincense

Directions

  1. Select a meaningful date for the final communication.
  2. Begin preparation fourteen days beforehand: observe daily cleansing rituals, abstain from sex, alcohol, and meat, and eat only simple minimal food throughout.
  3. Set up the room belonging to the deceased, or recreate it as faithfully as possible.
  4. Place a portrait of the deceased prominently and veil it in white fabric.
  5. Surround the portrait with flowers, replacing them with fresh ones every day.
  6. Each night during the fourteen days, enter the room alone in total silence at the same time each evening.
  7. Sit with the portrait for at least one hour by candlelight or lamplight placed behind you.
  8. Before leaving, fumigate the room with a fragrance meaningful to the deceased.
  9. Use benzoin or mimosa if no other choice is clear.
  10. Leave the room walking backward, never turning your back on the portrait.
  11. On the final day, dress in celebratory attire.
  12. Avoid all contact with others.
  13. Earlier that day, set a table for two in the room and eat a meal of bread, fruit, and wine while facing the portrait.
  14. Place half the food and a glass of wine before the portrait without unveiling it.
  15. Eat your portion and leave.
  16. Return at the appointed time in silence.
  17. Build a fire of cypress wood and cast frankincense onto the fire seven times.
  18. Call the person's name.
  19. Extinguish the candle or lamp and allow the cypress fire to burn down.
  20. Once the fire is reduced to embers, add more frankincense.
  21. Call upon the Creator of the Universe in a manner aligned with the spiritual beliefs of the deceased, speaking from the perspective of the departed.
  22. Rest in silence for fifteen minutes.
  23. Then speak aloud as if the person is present.
  24. Pray aloud for them to appear and then silently cover your face.
  25. From a kneeling position, call the person's name three times in a strong voice with your eyes closed.
  26. Call once more very gently.
  27. Open your eyes.
