🌿Ambriel Juggling Priorities

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Potion
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Herbal MagickChant/MantraInvocationManifestation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Two or more jars of your favorite culinary dried herbs, one for each major demand on your time, such as career, family, and social or sporting activities - A large sealable container
◇Directions
1. Take the first jar, name one priority, and shake the jar five times, saying: "Ambriel, angel of versatility, lend dexterity to me, that I can juggle my priorities." Do the same for each jar. 2. Then, opening the first jar, pour its contents into the sealable container, swirling the container five times clockwise and saying: "Ambriel, angel of versatility, blend all for me in harmony." Do this until you have used all the jars. 3. Swirl the container five more times counterclockwise, saying: "Mix, combine, Ambriel, make versatility mine." Seal the container and use the herbs in cooking. 4. If things start to overwhelm you, take the container outdoors and scatter some of the herbs, repeating the second and third set of spell words. 5. Perform any time in May, or whenever it's urgently needed.
◇Description
An any time in May or whenever urgently needed spell for Ambriel angel of May ritual for successfully doing two or more things at once when you are under pressure, mixing jars of dried herbs with five shakes and five swirls clockwise then counterclockwise for versatility and harmony to juggle priorities.
