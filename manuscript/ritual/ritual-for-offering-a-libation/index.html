🍷Ritual for Offering a Libation

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Ancestor WorkDevotional Work
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
New, unopened bottle of wine, milk, honey, oil, or spring water Optional: gardening trowel A stick (if not digging a hole)
◇Directions
1. Go outside, ideally on private property, or a secluded spot in a nearby park. 2. Prepare the ground: dig a small hole with a trowel, or trace an equal-armed cross on the ground with a stick. 3. Open the bottle, hold it up, and speak the prayer: "I offer this libation wholly, without reservation, to my ancestors. And should I return home and discover that this was the last bottle of [wine/milk/honey/oil/water] left on earth, I still would not regret it. Such is the depth of my love." 4. Pour the entire bottle into the hole or at the center of the cross, averting your gaze as you pour. 5. When the bottle is empty, refill the hole or cover the cross with fresh dirt from nearby.
◇Description
A libation poured directly into the earth, offered wholly to the ancestors, rooted in ancient Greek practice.

Rooted in ancient Greek practice, which held that contact with the realm of the dead through the earth is not always safe for the living to witness directly; the gaze is averted while pouring for this reason.

After the Ritual

Libations tend to inspire. This is a good time to play music, draw, write, make a meaningful phone call, or have a heartfelt conversation with someone you love.
