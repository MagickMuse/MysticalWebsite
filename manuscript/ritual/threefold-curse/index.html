⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A variant of the elemental curse that invokes the four watchtowers and the threefold law to deliver hundredfold retribution upon the target. The practitioner burns an image of the target in the flame of a consecrated black or red candle while chanting an incantation that summons fear, guilt, submission, and binding. Nearly identical to the standard Curse but permits a red candle and emphasises the threefold cost of the caster's anger and pain.

Quantities

1 consecrated black or red candle
1 image of the target (wax sigil, photograph, drawing, or personal item)

Directions

  1. Consecrate a black or red candle and light it.
  2. Burn an image of the target in the candle's flame.
  3. The image may be a wax sigil, photograph, drawing, or any personal item.
  4. As the image burns, recite the incantation, summoning the elements and the four watchtowers to direct threefold retribution upon the target.

Incantation

There has been unfairness done to me.
I summon the elements,
I invoke them,
I conjure them to do my bidding.
The four watchtowers shall lay their eyes and minds.
There shall be fear and guilt and bad blood,
there shall be submission and no pity.
I point the threefold law against thee,
against thee it shall be pointed.
Threefold, a hundredfold is the cost for my anger and pain.
Thee shall be blinded by the fear,
blinded by the pain,
blinded by me,
bound by me,
cursed by me.
So mote it be!
