🌺Afterbirth and Umbilical Cord Spell: Aymara

◇Method

Ritual

↗Correspondences2
🫄Placenta🤰🏻Pregnancy

An Aymara (Bolivia and Peru) afterbirth burial rite that pairs the placenta with symbolic tools shaping the child's future path. Closed Practice: belongs to Aymara Indigenous tradition of the Titicaca Basin; approach as reference only unless you hold that lineage.

Quantities

Flowers
Earth for burial
Appropriate tools, real or miniature (traditionally cooking tools for girls, farm tools for boys, or other meaningful objects)

Directions

  1. Cover the afterbirth with flowers.
  2. Bury it in earth together with tools, real or miniature, reflecting the parents' desires for the child's happy future. Traditional tool choices are cooking implements for girls and farm implements for boys, though objects may be changed to suit any parent's own visions and desires.
