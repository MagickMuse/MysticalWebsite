💖Spring Love Attraction

A joyful outdoor love attraction spell for spring. Uses red and pink roses with glitter thrown into the air while dancing to send intention out to the universe. Calls on Aphrodite/Venus and Eros/Cupid to guide the right person your way. Ethical approach asks for divine guidance rather than targeting specific person.

Quantities

  • Red glitter (at least 1/2 cup, more is better)
  • Pink glitter (at least 1/2 cup, more is better)
  • Fresh red roses (a couple)
  • Fresh pink roses (a couple)
  • Large bowl
  • Water (optional, for spritzing petals)
  • Outdoor space

Directions

  1. With spring and love buzzing all around the air, this spell is literally like sticking a butterfly net right into the middle of the swirl to see what you can catch.
  2. It isn't about seeing one particular butterfly and chasing it down, it's about putting the net out there and seeing which one is curious enough to come check it out.
  3. This spell is best done outside, as it will make a little bit of a mess and because you want to send your intent out as far and wide as you can.
  4. PREPARATION:
    Carefully peel each petal off of the roses and put them into a large bowl. Next add the glitter and gently mix the two up a bit with your hands. You don't want to damage or bruise the rose petals and the glitter will stay pretty much separated, but if you can get at least a few of the rose petals to have some glitter stick to them that is good. If you want, before adding the glitter, spritz a little water on the rose petals to help the glitter stick.
  5. THE SPELL:
    Once you are prepared, cast a circle calling in either the Greek gods Aphrodite and Eros or their Roman equivalents Venus and Cupid.
  6. Hold the bowl out in front of you and say:
  7. (Name your chosen deities),
    bless these petals and infuse them with your power.
    Help them to help me in my search for love this hour.
    Find the one that's meant for me and send them on their way,
    to come into my life and to become what may.
  8. You will notice that in this spell we are not asking for a specific person nor a specific trait.
  9. We are asking the god and goddess we are entrusting to find who they deem appropriate to fit the bill and to give that person a shove in the right direction.
  10. Love spells should never be done asking for a specific person to fall in love with you.
  11. This goes against the person's free will and is simply unethical.
  12. Cupid and Eros know what they are doing, and they will direct their arrows to where they need to go.
  13. This is the part of the spell that gets fun even if you feel a little silly doing it.
  14. It is spring, love is in the air and that means being a little silly is perfectly fine!
  15. You will continue to repeat the last two lines of this spell:
  16. Find the one that's meant for me and send them on their way,
    to come into my life and to become what may.
  17. As you recite these two lines, dance around, scoop up handfuls of petals and glitter, and throw them as high as you can into the air.
  18. You will most likely get covered with glitter, but hey, it is glitter, so it's all good.
  19. Throw the petals and glitter in all directions: up, north, south, east, west.
  20. Spin and throw it, just toss it really good all over the place.
  21. When you are finished say:
  22. (Name your chosen deities),
    in your names, I ask for this love to be brought to me.
    If it pleases you as well, so mote it be.
  23. Close your circle.
  24. You will probably also want to wash your clothes and take a shower!
