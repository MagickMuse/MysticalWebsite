Sator Square

The SATOR square is the most ubiquitous of magic squares, found wherever the Roman legions traveled. Its exact meaning is unknown. One theory links it to Mithraic rites popular with Roman soldiers; another holds it to be a Christian charm, the words being an anagram of Pater Noster. Its most famous use is fire safety.

For Fire Prevention

  1. Write the magic square carefully on parchment. Letters must line up neatly and no two letters may touch.
  2. Place the parchment at the highest point within the home.

To Extinguish Fire Without Water

  1. Etch the magic square onto a wooden plate.
  2. Throw the plate onto the fire.

In 1742, the state of Saxony formally ordered that plates bearing this formula be kept on hand for firefighting. Some versions of the tradition suggest engraving the square on silver tablets instead.
