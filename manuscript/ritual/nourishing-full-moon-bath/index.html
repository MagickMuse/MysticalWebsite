Some full moons call for grand ceremony, but others fall during quieter seasons, times when rest and release are the medicine. This lunar bath uses the cleansing power of salts and eucalyptus to clear unwanted energy, while lemon oil and lemon balm nourish the body and uplift the spirit. Both skin and energy emerge feeling soft and restored. Use it before or after a moon ritual, or as a stand-alone practice. Substitute any skin-safe lunar essential oils as preferred.

Quantities

¾ cup sea salt
1 cup Epsom salt
¼ cup baking soda
1 tablespoon dried lemon balm, rose, or other skin-safe moon-associated herb
12 drops lemon essential oil
6 drops eucalyptus essential oil
Seashell (optional)
Cucumber slices (optional)
Bowl or container

Directions

  1. Sit somewhere the moonlight is visible. Gather and arrange all materials before you.
  2. Under the moon's light, add each ingredient to the bowl in turn: sea salt (honoring the ocean tides the moon governs), Epsom salt, baking soda, and the healing lemon balm.
  3. Add the essential oils. Stir counterclockwise, visualizing the blend drawing out and dissolving unwanted energy.
  4. Charge the mixture overnight under the moonlight in a sealed container, or use it immediately. When ready, stir ½ cup of the mixture into a warm bath.
  5. In the bath, use a seashell to scoop water over your shoulders and body. Visualize the healing waves of the ocean under the moonlight washing through you, cleansing and nurturing.
  6. For a touch of rejuvenation, rest with cucumber slices over your eyes. Visualize the bath glowing softly with the moon's light, its youthful, restorative energy soaking into your skin.

Magickal Notes

🌊
Sea salt invokes the ocean tides, governed by the moon — a direct channel to lunar cleansing power. Eucalyptus sweeps away stagnant energy. Lemon oil and lemon balm are Cancer-aligned plants, deeply nourishing to both the aura and the physical body. The seashell acts as a ritual tool, connecting the working to the nurturing energy of the ocean. Cucumber carries the moon's cool, youthful, rejuvenating essence.
⚠️
Skin safety: Always patch-test essential oils diluted in a carrier before a full bath. Eucalyptus essential oil can be sensitizing — use within the drop counts given and do not exceed them. Avoid if pregnant without consulting a practitioner.
