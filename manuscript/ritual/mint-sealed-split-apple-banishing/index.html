🍎Mint-Sealed Split Apple Banishing

Form

Method

Category
Spell

A banishing spell in which a split apple absorbs an unwanted problem or situation, then rots it away underground. Optionally, the problem may be named on a mint-oil-soaked paper tucked between the halves, though naming it on paper carries a risk of further manifestation.

Quantities

Apple
Mint leaf
Skewer
Black silk or satin ribbon
Optional: slip of paper and essential oil of mint

Directions

  1. Cut an apple in half horizontally so that the star in the center is exposed.
  2. Rub one half of the apple with a mint leaf while visualizing what needs to be banished.
  3. Put the two halves of the apple back together again.
  4. Stick a skewer through the pieces so that they remain joined.
  5. Tie the pieces securely together with black silk or satin ribbon.
  6. Bury the apple; your problem should dissipate as the apple rots.

Optional: Write your problem on a slip of paper, dip it in essential oil of mint, and place it between the apple halves before rejoining them. Note that naming the problem on paper carries a risk of further manifestation; cast whichever version resonates.
