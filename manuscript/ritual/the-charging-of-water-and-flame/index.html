The base charging formula for salt water and incense smoke, spoken to charge the raw water and fire later used to exorcise and purify the materials for any new working tool.

Quantities

A small handful of new salt
A bowl of fresh water
A pinch of quality incense
A glowing charcoal block
An ashtray or other fireproof dish

Directions

  1. Cast a small handful of new salt into a bowl of fresh water.
  2. Breathe the following words onto the surface of the water, holding all your will, faith, and imagination toward a faint blue light gathering above it:
    Element of water joined with earth,
    let nothing here be given birth
    that stands against or crosses me.
    As I have spoken, so shall it be!
  3. This becomes your charged salt water of exorcism, kept ready for purifying the raw materials of any tool you make afterward.
  4. Scatter a few grains of good incense onto a glowing charcoal block set in a bowl or ashtray, then hold your hand out over the rising smoke and speak:
    Spirit of the flame, receive this charge,
    let no restless shade come near or linger.
    My will alone speaks to you now;
    as I have spoken, so shall it be!
  5. Picture the coals glowing with that same faint blue light as you speak. This charged fire, together with the charged water, completes the exorcism of your raw materials.
