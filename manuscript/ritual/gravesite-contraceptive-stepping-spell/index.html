🌱Fertility

A contraceptive working performed at a burial site after a funeral. The practitioner remains after others have departed and steps three times over a grave in a single consistent direction, holding the intention to avert pregnancy. The same technique, with a different intention, can be used to restore fertility.

Quantities

None

Directions

  1. After attending a funeral, remain at the burial site after others have left.
  2. Approach the grave and step over it three times, always moving in the same direction; do not go back and forth.
  3. Hold your intention to remain free from pregnancy clearly in mind throughout.
  4. The same stepping pattern with the reversed intention can also be used to reinvigorate fertility.

Graves and burial sites carry concentrated liminal power. They are thresholds between the living world and what lies beyond it, and in folk magic traditions across cultures, that threshold quality makes them sites where intentions cross from one state to another.

Stepping over a grave three times in one consistent direction enacts a one-way crossing: the intention moves through the threshold and does not come back. The prohibition against going back and forth is not arbitrary - reversal of direction reverses the spell.

Three is a number of completion in most European folk traditions. Three steps closes the working.

The dual application of this spell is worth noting. The same technique, the same location, the same number of steps - only the intention differs. The gravesite does not distinguish between the two requests. The practitioner does.
