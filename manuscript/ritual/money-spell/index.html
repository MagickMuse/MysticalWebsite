💰Money Spell

◇Form
Candle RiteDressed Candle
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraInvocation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 dollar bill - 1 silver candle (Goddess) - 1 gold candle (God) - 1 green working candle - Patchouli oil - 1 cauldron (to hold melted wax and burn dollar) - Lid or covering to snuff flame
◇Directions
1. Light the silver and gold candles. 2. Rub patchouli oil on the green candle and inscribe with the appropriate symbol. 3. Set the green candle in the cauldron and light it. 4. Burn a dollar bill in the candle flame while rotating the cauldron nine times and speaking: 5. With Saturn, Jupiter, and the Sun; As Lunar cycles spin endlessly; God of gold, laughing and free; Lady of silver, dancing with me; Bring me thy power, fill up my bower, Bring me this Quarter, abundance of money! 6. Bury the candle remains or dispose of them in the trash.
◇Description
A quarterly money spell performed during the waxing to Full Moon of each solstice and equinox month. Silver and gold candles are lit for the Goddess and God, then a green working candle is anointed with patchouli oil and set in the cauldron. A dollar bill is burned in the candle flame while rotating the cauldron nine times and invoking the blessings of abundance.
