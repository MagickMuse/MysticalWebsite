🕯️New Moon Esbat Ritual

◇Form
Circle Rite
◇Method
InvocationMeditationLunar
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Altar setup from Esbat Opening - Book of Shadows or recitation materials - Materials for any planned Dark Power spell work, consecrations, or water charging
◇Directions
1. After completing the Esbat Opening, cross arms over your chest and bow your head. 2. Chant the name of the Goddess as Crone. 3. Raise your opened arms: 4. As [Name] are you known to me, for this is the Moon of my Lady as Crone. 5. Lady of Darkness, of Wisdom, of Mysteries revealed. 6. The Wheel turns through birth, death, and rebirth, and every end is a new beginning. 7. You are the Passage from life to life; You are She who is at the beginning and the end of all time. 8. You, with Your Lord [Name] at Your side, abide in us all. 9. So Mote It Be! 10. Read from prepared recitations, or allow spontaneous expression to arise. 11. Meditate in silence on the Mysteries and your personal connection to the Dark Goddess. 12. Conduct any planned Dark Power workings: charging of water, consecrations, meditations, or spellcraft prepared for this night. 13. When all Esbat work is complete, raise open arms: 14. You are the Dark Mother of All; the Tomb that is also the Womb. 15. You are at the beginning and end of life. 16. You dwell within us all for You are Life, the Passage to New Life, and Love; and thus do You make me immortal through the cycle of Life, the Passage to New Life, and Love. 17. Love is the Law and Love is the Bond! 18. So Mote It Be! 19. Proceed to Cakes & Wine of the Circle Casting.
◇Description
The specific Esbat ritual for the New Moon, honoring the Goddess as Crone and focusing on mysteries, wisdom, and the threshold between endings and beginnings. Follows the Esbat Opening and precedes Cakes & Wine.
