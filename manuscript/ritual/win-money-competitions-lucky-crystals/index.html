💎Win Money Competitions Lucky Crystals

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Incense MagickSmoke MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- 1 crystal (green aventurine, amazonite, chrysoprase, or snowflake obsidian) - 1 incense stick (pine, patchouli, or rose) - 1 green scarf
◇Directions
1. Put the crystal in the center of the scarf. 2. Light the incense stick. 3. Using the stick like a pen, write in smoke in the air seven times over the scarf, "WIN," with no spaces between the words. 4. Pick up the crystal in the hand you do not write with, drawing seven times over it in incense smoke, "WIN." Write in the air in front of yourself at brow level, still holding the crystal, seven times, "WIN." Let the incense burn. 5. Place the crystal on top of competition entries or by the phone or computer. 6. Between uses, keep the crystal knotted in the scarf tied with seven knots. 7. After seven uses, re-empower the scarf and crystal with a new incense stick, repeating the spell. 8. Perform Wednesday after dawn.
◇Description
A charm spell using incense smoke and lucky crystals to win money and competitions by writing WIN in the air seven times.
