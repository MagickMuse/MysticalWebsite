🕯️Dark Power Circle Casting

A widdershins circle casting invoking the Dark Goddess (Crone) and Dark God (Lord of Shadows), featuring Shadow Light quarter calls, Shadow Elemental guardians, and a complete Dark-themed Cakes & Wine ceremony with Underworld symbolism. Can be adapted for Sidhe (Fairy) Powers by starting from the West.

Quantities

  • 3 altar candles (black, dark gray, deep purple, blue, burgundy, wine, white, or deep brown)
  • Elemental candles for quarters (optional)
  • Incense (copal, dragon's blood, frankincense, lilac, mullein, myrrh, or patchouli)
  • Athame
  • Wand
  • Bell or hands for clapping
  • Besom (ritual broom)
  • Bowl of water
  • Ritual salt
  • Anointing oil
  • Cauldron or libation bowl
  • Blackberry wine or dark fruit juice
  • Dark bread, cake, fruitcake, or multi-grain bread

Directions

  1. Set up Circle and Altar items with three altar candles.
  2. Ground and center, then light candles and incense.
  3. Sweep the site deosil starting at the North for Dark Powers or the West for Sidhe Powers, saying:
  4. As I sweep this Circle, may it be cleared of negative and chaotic energies that it be prepared for my work.
  5. Clap or ring bell three times:
  6. The Circle is about to be cast and I freely stand within to greet my Dark Lady and my Lord of Shadows.
  7. Take the center candle widdershins, raising it or lighting an Elemental candle at each Quarter starting North (or West for Sidhe), invoking the Shadow Light with appropriate visualizations:
  8. [N]: I call upon the Shadow Light within Earth to illuminate and strengthen this Circle.
    (Visualize magma core or phosphorus in cave)
    [W]: I call upon the Shadow Light within Water to illuminate and cleanse this Circle.
    (Visualize bio-luminescence or glowing water hyacinth)
    [S]: I call upon the Shadow Light within Fire to illuminate and warm this Circle.
    (Visualize blue/white flame or corpse candle)
    [E]: I call upon the Shadow Light within Air to illuminate and refresh this Circle.
    (Visualize cloud lightning or aurora borealis)
  9. With athame or wand, go widdershins around the Circle starting at North (or West):
  10. I cast this Circle above me, below me, around me; through walls, floors, furniture, and ground, in the presence of the Dark Goddess and the Dark God to be a sacred space where they may manifest and bless their child [name].
    (Visualize a clear blue light-encasing sphere)
  11. Touch athame to the ritual salt:
  12. Salt is purification, preservation, and necessary to life.
  13. I bless this salt to be used in the Circle in the name of the Crone and of the Dark Lord.
  14. Add 3 athame tips of salt to the water bowl and stir 3 times:
  15. Let the blessed salt purify this water for use in this sacred space.
  16. I consecrate and cleanse this water in the name of the Crone and of the Dark Lord.
  17. Sprinkle Circle widdershins with the blessed water:
  18. I consecrate this Circle in the name of the Crone and of the Dark Lord.
  19. This Circle is conjured a Circle of Power that is purified and sealed.
  20. Take incense around the Circle widdershins, then set it at the South.
  21. At Altar, anoint forehead with oil in a chosen sigil:
  22. I [Craft name], am consecrated in the name of the Crone and of the Dark Lord.
  23. With wand, invoke the Shadow Elementals widdershins starting at North (or West).
  24. Use Dark Power visualizations (wolf/cat, moray eel/shark, phoenix/gila monster, owl/moth) or Sidhe visualizations (cobalt, selkie, will-o'-the-wisp, sylph):
  25. [N]: I call upon you Elemental Earth to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have body and strength, we are kith and kin!
    [W]: I call upon you Elemental Water to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have blood and feelings, we are kith and kin!
    [S]: I call upon you Elemental Fire to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have consumed life to live, we are kith and kin!
    [E]: I call upon you Elemental Air to attend this rite and guard this Circle, for as I have breath and thought, we are kith and kin!
  26. Draw the Cosmic Lemniscate (Infinity sign) with the wand over the Altar.
  27. Set down wand and raise athame in both hands overhead:
  28. Hail to the Elementals!
  29. Welcome Lady and Lord to this rite.
  30. I stand between the worlds with love and power all around!
  31. Pour some beverage into cauldron/libation bowl and take a sip:
  32. I who am thy child, [name], call upon my Lady as Crone and my Lord as Shadow to hold communion with me.
  33. I affirm my joy of union with the Divine and acknowledge Your blessings unto me.
  34. What I send returns to me, and I conduct my Craft accordingly.
  35. Perform ritual or Craft work.
  36. Raise arms at altar:
  37. I know my needs and offer my gratitude to that which sustains me.
  38. May I ever remember the blessings of my Lady and my Lord.
  39. Pick up cup in left hand, athame in right, slowly lower point into cup:
  40. The Lord enters into the fruitful darkness of the Lady.
  41. In Shadow is He and Shadow is She.
  42. The womb is the tomb, but the tomb is her womb, therefore let me never forget that the light is reached through the dark, and She is the passage I must follow as He follows for my sake.
  43. Take a sip from the cup (make no libation yet).
  44. Touch athame to cake/bread:
  45. The food of the dead and of Underworld is the food of the soul.
  46. Let this cake be a symbol of the feeding of my spirit that this be nourished as well as my body, that I may be whole in both.
  47. Take a bite from the food, then eat and drink, saving a portion for a final libation.
  48. Hold athame over Altar:
  49. Lady and Lord, I am blessed by your sharing this time with me and aiding me in my Craft.
  50. You watch over me and protect me, guiding me here and in all things.
  51. I came in love, and I depart in love.
  52. Add the remainder of beverage and cake to the libation bowl and hold it up over the Altar:
  53. The remains of this life are passed into the Cauldron of Rebirth, for this is the Promise: into death doth all life flow, to be refreshed and to move into life anew.
  54. The Cauldron of Rebirth brings life out of death, and I honor the Lady and the Lord who bring light and life through darkness and death.
  55. The dance is evermoving and neverending.
  56. So Mote It Be!
  57. Set down the libation and raise the athame in salute:
  58. Love is the law, and love is the bond.
  59. Merry did we meet, merry do we part, and merry will we meet again.
  60. Merry meet, merry part, and merry meet again!
  61. The Circle is cleared!
  62. So Mote It Be!
  63. Kiss the flat of the blade and set on Altar.
  64. With wand, farewell the Elementals widdershins starting at North (or West), snuffing candles if used:
  65. [N]: We have met in kinship, thee and me. Depart in peace, Elemental Earth, my blessings take with thee!
    [W]: We have met in kinship, thee and me. Depart in peace, Elemental Water, my blessings take with thee!
    [S]: We have met in kinship, thee and me. Depart in peace, Elemental Fire, my blessings take with thee!
    [E]: We have met in kinship, thee and me. Depart in peace, Elemental Air, my blessings take with thee!
  66. Return to Altar with raised arms:
  67. Beings and Powers of the visible and invisible, you aid in my work, whisper in my mind, bless me from realms of Shadow and Otherworld.
  68. Depart in peace and let there always be harmony between us.
  69. My blessings take with you.
  70. The Circle is cleared.
  71. Take athame and proceed deosil starting at North (or West) around Circle, visualizing the light drawn into it:
  72. The Circle is open, yet the Circle remains as its magickal power is drawn back into me.
  73. At Altar, touch flat of blade to forehead, visualizing the blue light swirling inwardly, then raise athame in salute:
  74. The ritual is ended!
  75. Set down athame, open arms outward with palms up:
  76. Blessings have been given,
    Bring arms crossed over chest and bow:
    and blessings have been received.
  77. Bring arms forward with hands low together in front, palms upright and outward:
  78. May the peace of the Goddess and the God remain in my heart.
  79. So Mote It Be!
  80. Pick up the incense at the South and carry it completely around the circle:
  81. This space is cleared and fumed, that no dark energies may linger, but hasten away in peace with my blessings.
  82. Set the incense on the Altar.
  83. Touch palms to floor to ground excess energy.
  84. Put away tools and clear the Altar.
  85. Empty libation on earth outside or rinse out in sink, but not in an indoor plant.
