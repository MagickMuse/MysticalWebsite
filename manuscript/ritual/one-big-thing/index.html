◇Quantities
- 1 white balloon with a string (helium-filled preferred, but air-filled is okay) - 1 felt-tip pen - 4 sticks of incense (same scent; recommended: cedar, copal, dragon's blood, frankincense, myrrh, patchouli, or sandalwood)
◇Directions
Best done on a bright day in pleasant weather. Must be done outside. 1. Preparation ◦ Reflect deeply on the burden you wish to release. ◦ Reduce it to a few words and write them directly on the balloon. ◦ If privacy is needed, write on the inside of the balloon before inflating it. ◦ Tie the balloon with the string. ◦ Choose an open, quiet location free of overhead obstructions. A helium balloon works without wind; if using an air-filled balloon, choose a breezy day. 2. The Spell ◦ Take your balloon and incense to the selected site. Secure the balloon. ◦ Get into a magickal mood, raise power, and light all four incense sticks. ◦ Face each cardinal direction, saying the following incantations: North Guardians of the North, Powers of Earth, powers of clarity, Make this spell a reality. (Push one stick of incense into the ground.) East Guardians of the East, Powers of Air, powers of thought, Make this spell work as sought. (Push the second incense stick into the ground.) South Guardians of the South, Powers of Fire, powers of energy, Make this spell work immediately. (Push the third stick into the ground.) West Guardians of the West, Powers of Water, powers of emotion, Put this spell into motion. (Push the last stick into the ground.) ◦ Stand in the center of the incense-marked space, holding the balloon. ◦ Focus on your burden and your desire to be free of it. ◦ Let go of the balloon and visualize your burden lifting away with it. ◦ Watch the balloon rise and feel yourself becoming lighter. It's okay if the balloon pops. 3. Closure ◦ Remain in the space until all incense has burned down. ◦ Ground the energy you raised and clean up the area. Afterward • Whenever you are reminded of your burden, recall the image of the balloon soaring away. • Remind yourself: "That thing has nothing to do with me now." • Reclaim your freedom from it and rejoice in your release.

Why This Spell Works

Sometimes life hands us something so significant that it lodges in our psyche and refuses to leave. It might be an injustice that still burns, a victimization that left scars, a loss that never healed, a failure that haunts you, an illness that changed everything, a missed opportunity you replay endlessly, a disaster you witnessed, a mistake you cannot forgive yourself for, a historic event that shattered your worldview, or a person you simply cannot move past.

This spell works by giving that burden a physical form and then releasing it to the sky. The balloon becomes a vessel for your pain, and watching it rise and disappear creates a powerful psychological and magickal break between you and your attachment to the issue.

Understanding the Elements

The spell calls upon all four cardinal directions and their elemental guardians. Each element contributes something essential to the release:

  • Earth (North) brings clarity and grounding, helping you see the situation as it truly is
  • Air (East) engages the power of thought, allowing you to mentally process and release
  • Fire (South) provides the energy to break free and act immediately
  • Water (West) addresses the emotional component, putting the release into motion

The incense sticks mark the four quarters and create sacred space around you. As they burn down, they carry your intention into the element of Air while grounding through Earth.

Choosing Your Location

The outdoor setting matters. You need open sky above you with no obstructions. Trees and power lines can catch or redirect the balloon, which interferes with the visualization and the magick. Privacy is also important because the working requires emotional vulnerability. You need to be able to fully focus on your burden and your release without worrying about observers.

Weather plays a role as well. Helium balloons will rise on any day, but if you are using an air-filled balloon, wait for a day with enough breeze to carry it away. The movement matters more than the height.

The Privacy Trick

If you are concerned about someone finding your balloon and reading what you wrote, the spell offers an elegant solution: turn the balloon inside out before writing on it, then turn it right side out and inflate. Your words remain hidden while still serving their magickal purpose. The act of writing is what matters. The universe does not need to read your words to hear your intention.

After the Spell

The most powerful part of this working comes afterward. Every time something reminds you of your one big thing, you have a new tool. Instead of spiraling back into the old pattern of rumination, recall the image of that balloon rising into the sky. Remember watching it grow smaller and smaller until it vanished or popped.

That thing left you. It soared away. It has nothing to do with you now. This visualization becomes a mental reset button you can press whenever the old thoughts try to creep back in.
