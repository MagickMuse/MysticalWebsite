A powerful stationary cleanser combining Florida Water and Holy or Marie Laveau Water with camphor, left open in a pan to passively absorb negativity.

Quantities

  • Equal parts Florida Water and Marie Laveau Water or Holy Water
  • 1 camphor square

Directions

  1. Fill a pan with equal parts Florida Water and Marie Laveau or Holy Water.
  2. Add one camphor square to the liquid.
  3. Keep the pan under the bed or in another strategic location.
  4. Change the water once a week or if it starts to smell off.
