🌸Lunar Loose-Leaf Flower Tea

◇Form
Food/Edible
◇Method
LunarDivinationMeditation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Purified, spring, or Moon Water - 1 teaspoon dried rose petals - 1 tablespoon dried butterfly pea flower - ½ teaspoon dried lavender - 1 teaspoon dried jasmine (food-grade) - ⅛ teaspoon ground clove - Wide-mouthed teacup (preferred for leaf reading) - 1 lemon wedge - 1–2½ tablespoons honey - Cinnamon stick Use food-grade, organic ingredients where possible.
◇Directions
1. Set your water to boil. 2. While it heats, combine the rose petals, butterfly pea flower, lavender, jasmine, and clove. 3. If you intend to read the leaves afterward, leave them loose directly in the cup. 4. If not, place them in a tea strainer. 5. As the water heats, hold your hands near the botanicals and tune into their energy. 6. Imagine the moon reaching down and awakening the essence in each flower and herb. 7. Hold your question or intention gently in mind. 8. Pour the hot water over the blend and watch the deep blue of the butterfly pea flower bloom into the water. 9. Let steep for 5 to 7 minutes. 10. Squeeze the lemon wedge into the cup; the citric acid will shift the color from blue to violet or pink, a small piece of lunar alchemy in your hands. 11. Use the cinnamon stick to stir in honey clockwise to taste, keeping your intention present with each turn. 12. Take your cup outside or to a moonlit window. 13. Sip slowly. 14. With each breath of steam and each sip, feel your awareness rising, softening, opening, reaching toward the cosmic current and the spirits of the natural world. 15. When the cup is nearly empty, hold a little liquid in the bottom. 16. Think of your question or intention, then swirl the cup three times. 17. Flip it upside down onto a paper towel and let it rest for a minute. 18. Turn the cup right-side up and look at what remains. 19. Most leaves will have fallen, but some will cling to the sides and bottom in patterns. 20. Read these intuitively: shapes, symbols, directions, clusters. 21. Tasseography is a subjective and meditative art; trust what arises and look up a tasseography guide to deepen your practice over time. 22. This tea works beautifully at any moon. 23. During a Pisces moon, emphasize psychic receptivity and going with the flow. 24. During a Taurus moon, savor the sensory pleasure and focus on peace and earthly abundance. 25. During a Libra moon, share a cup with someone and focus on harmony and connection. 26. During a Blue Moon, set a rare or long-held intention into the cup.
◇Description
A color-changing floral loose-leaf tea blended from rose, butterfly pea flower, lavender, jasmine, and clove, brewed under the moon for psychic clarity, peace, and communion with nature spirits. A squeeze of lemon turns the deep blue tea violet. Leave leaves loose in the cup for tasseography reading afterward. Sip alongside tarot or oracle cards. At minimum, rose, butterfly pea flower, and clove will suffice.
