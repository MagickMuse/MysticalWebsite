Pine

A binding spell using a flexible young pine branch woven with white thread to create a spider web wreath, burned on Saturday to end harmful behaviors.

Quantities

  • 1 young, thin pine shoot (flexible)
  • White thread
  • 1 candle

Directions

  1. Consider binding carefully.
  2. Binding someone binds them to you, even if you are attempting to prevent harm.
  3. This working is best reserved for loved ones or when banishing is not appropriate.
  4. Obtain a young, thin pine shoot that is flexible enough to bend into a small circle.
  5. Take white thread and tie the end around the top section of the branch.
  6. Bend the branch into a circle and loop the thread around to bind the ends together.
  7. Then take the thread across the circle, loop it around the branch, and come back a few inches away on either side of where you started.
  8. Continue back and forth, making an intricate spider web of thread and intent.
  9. As you weave, picture the binding needed and how it will help the intended target.
  10. Once the small spider wreath is complete, set a candle nearby and burn on a Saturday (to end bad habits).
  11. Allow the candle to burn completely while the wreath remains nearby.
