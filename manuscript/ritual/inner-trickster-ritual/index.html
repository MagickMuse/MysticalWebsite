🌙Inner Trickster Ritual

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Shadow WorkMirror MagickVisualization
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- Any mirror - A white candle in a fireproof holder - Matches or a lighter - A blanket or shawl - A length of red string or yarn
◇Directions
### Naming the Trickster 1. First, name your trickster shadow self. 2. Anything goes, because no one but you will know about it. 3. Go to the mirror and light the candle for illumination. 4. Drape the blanket (or shawl) over your head and hold it closed under your chin so that only your face shows. 5. Stand with your back to the mirror and name your trickster self by saying: 6. I've been waiting for you, (your trickster name)! ### The Invocation 7. Turn around to look in the mirror and recite this three times: 8. You tricked me before, and you tricked me then. Now I become you, you can't trick me again! ### Releasing the Trickster 9. Throw the blanket off and tie the string around your waist (under your clothes is okay). 10. Blow out the candle and go out into the world as a trickster. 11. However you decide to manifest this energy is up to you, but do something outrageous or out of character or something that forces you to face a fear. 12. Give yourself permission, because it is not you, it is your trickster self! 13. Ideas: - Wear a disguise when going out - Play "opposite night" with your family - Give your children cake and ice cream for breakfast - Go on a spontaneous side quest while running errands - Watch a scary movie or documentary - Try a food you've been afraid of - Do something "immature" like building a blanket fort - Play a practical joke on someone 14. As long as you are not causing any problems, the possibilities are endless! ### Returning the Trickster 15. When you are satisfied that your trickster self has had enough time to play, simply cut the red string and discard it (in the trash is fine) while declaring: 16. (Your trickster name), you've had your fun, now go back to your hiding place. We are as one, you and I, but I choose when to wear your face! 17. Repeat this ritual as often as you want. 18. Just as you have different moods, you can use a different name for your trickster at any time.
◇Description
A harmless way to let your shadow self out as a trickster for a little while, because it will be more manageable if you give it some love. Perform alone at any time.
