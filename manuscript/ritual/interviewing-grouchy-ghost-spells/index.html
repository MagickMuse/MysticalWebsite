A set of incense formulas for summoning difficult, reluctant, or emotionally compromised ghosts. Three approaches are available depending on the ghost's condition. ⚠️ Solomon's Seal not confirmed as a tagged Plant page.

Quantities

  • Formula 1 (depressed ghost or unaware of death): 3 parts wormwood, 1 part vervain
  • Formula 2 (grumpy or unwilling ghost): 3 parts wormwood, 1 part Solomon's Seal
  • Peace offering (if ghost is reluctant): Honey

Directions

  1. Select the appropriate formula based on the ghost's likely condition.
  2. For a ghost who was suffering from depression at the time of death, or who may not yet know they have died, burn three parts wormwood to one part vervain.
  3. For a ghost who is grumpy or unwilling to communicate, burn three parts wormwood to one part Solomon's Seal.
  4. If there is any possibility the ghost would have preferred not to be disturbed, offer honey immediately upon contact as a peace offering.
