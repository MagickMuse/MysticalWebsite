🧠Beeswax Memory and Memorization

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickChant/Mantra
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Closed Practice
Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
• 1 natural beeswax candle • Dried sage and thyme • 1 flat-based candleholder or small tray • 1 knife • 1 spatula • Parchment paper • 1 small bag or purse • The material you need to memorize
◇Directions
1. Roll the candle to and fro nine times on the table, saying softly nine times: "Sage and thyme, make memory mine, as this candle flows, so my blocks do go. I will recall what I need, achievement indeed." Light the candle and scatter sage and thyme around the base of the candle on the candleholder, saying the spell words. 2. When the wax has melted, enclosing the herbs, say: "Easy will learning be. Knowledge flows into me." As the candle is cooling, cut out a small herb and wax disk with the knife, lifting it with the spatula onto parchment paper. 3. Dispose of the remaining wax. 4. When cooled, put the disk in a small bag and keep it near the material you need to memorize. 5. Carry it with you on the day you need to use the knowledge.
◇Description
A beeswax candle spell if you can't memorize essential material or data and a test or your job depends on it, using sage and thyme melted in wax to unlock memory and recall. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
