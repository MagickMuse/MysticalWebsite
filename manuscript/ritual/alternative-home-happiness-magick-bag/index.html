🎒Alternative Home Happiness Magick Bag

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Pouch
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Candle MagickHerbal MagickSalt MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 red bag - Dried bay leaves or juniper berries - Oats or grains in tiny sealed bag - 3 silver coins - 1 small twig - Knotted sewing silk - Salt wrapped in foil - 1 orange candle
◇Directions
1. Add the items one by one to the bag by the light of an orange candle, saying for each, "Enough for my needs, food, fuel, clothes, money, health, and good fortune, these I ask and I will share my abundance." Close the bag and shake it seven times, saying each time, "Abundance in all ways comes to me, a year of fortune will I see in my beloved home." Blow out the candle. 2. In the morning, hold the bag, repeat all the spell words, and hide it in a high, dark place at home. 3. Make a new bag on the nearest transition date to a year and bury the old one. 4. Perform any transition evening just before the beginning of a week, month, or year.
◇Description
An annual transition ritual creating a magick bag with seven symbolic items to ensure household abundance and wellbeing.
