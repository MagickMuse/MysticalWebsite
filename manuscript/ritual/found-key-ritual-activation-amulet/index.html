A multi-step ritual transforming a found metal key into a permanently activated luck and opportunity amulet through incense cleansing, heat charging, nine nights of sleep contact, and sustained daily carrying.

Quantities

Found metal key (older or antique preferred)
Incense
Cast iron pan or cauldron

Directions

  1. Pick up the found key discreetly.
  2. Bring it home and pass it through incense smoke to cleanse it thoroughly.
  3. Place the key in a cast iron pan or cauldron, heat until burning hot, then turn off the heat and leave the key inside to cool entirely at its own pace.
  4. While it cools, hold a clear intention for what door this key should unlock: love, money, freedom, or another chosen purpose.
  5. Sleep with the key under the pillow for nine consecutive nights and carry it throughout each day.
  6. After nine days, the key is a fully activated amulet.
  7. Keep it in a magick box or charm bag, or hang it on the wall.
