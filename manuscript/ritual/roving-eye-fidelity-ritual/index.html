🕯️Roving Eye Fidelity Ritual

◇Form
Candle RiteSigil
◇Method
Candle MagickChant/MantraProtection
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- A rich blue candle - A red candle - A silver paper knife or engraving tool - A sealed glass jar
◇Directions
1. Etch on the blue fidelity candle "love forever," and on the red roving eye candle "temptation be gone." Light the blue candle, saying: "May love burn eternal between me and you. May none come between us, blaze faithful flame true. None ever have, nor ever will do." Light the red candle from the blue one and say: "Be as a shield against all false fire, burn only for me, make me your desire." Extinguish the red candle with a snuffer or cup and throw it away. 2. Let the blue candle burn out. 3. Collect any wax and engrave your own and your partner's entwined initials on it, placing it in a sealed glass jar in the kitchen.
◇Description
A midnight candle ritual to keep your partner faithful under temptation, using blue and red candles to shield against false fire and strengthen fidelity.
