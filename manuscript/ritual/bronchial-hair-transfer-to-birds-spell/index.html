🐦Bronchial Hair Transfer to Birds Spell

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Either
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
ElementalEnergy Work
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Patient's hair (shaved) Access to trees and bushes outdoors
◇Directions
1. Shave the patient's hair. 2. Hang this hair on trees and bushes so that birds will carry it away and use it to feather their nests. 3. The illness is allegedly transferred with the hair to the birds, who know how to process it in a positive manner.
◇Description
A European folk healing spell for bronchial complaints. The patient's shaved hair is hung on trees and bushes so birds carry it away to feather their nests, transferring the illness with the hair. Note: an African-derived magickal tradition specifically warns against birds using your hair in nests, as it may cause headaches or madness. Choose the tradition that resonates for you.
