◇Directions
1. Create an altar for Bossu using red and black as his colors. 2. Place his image or a horn as his representative symbol. 3. Choose your offerings based on the intensity of working you intend: grain and beer keep the bull calm and cooperative; spicy foods and rare beef invoke his full primal force. 4. Present your petition for fertility directly and honestly.
◇Description
A fertility altar petition to Bossu, the three-horned bull lwa of Haitian Vodou, who responds to women's pleas for fertility despite his volatile, primal nature. Colors are red and black. A horn may substitute for a standard image. Offerings are debated: grain and beer for a gentler working; spicy foods and rare beef for the full force of the bull. ⚠️ Bossu is a lwa of Haitian Vodou, a closed initiatory practice. This working should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural guidance.

Bossu, depicted as a three-horned bull, is one of the more volatile lwa: primal, testosterone-charged, and not easily managed. He occupies a position similar to Baal's in Semitic tradition, a great masculine power of generation who can be dangerous when not approached with care.

He is, however, known to respond to women's fertility petitions. The choice of offerings is not trivial: grain and beer are the traditional way to keep the bull gentle, while spicy foods and rare beef tap into the unmediated force of the spirit.

⚠️
Bossu is a lwa venerated within Haitian Vodou, a closed initiatory practice. This working should only be undertaken by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural guidance from an initiated practitioner.
