An invocation to Cerridwen, mother of wheat and wisdom, honoring her as the white sow and keeper of the cauldron. Asks the Wild Crone for mercy and guidance through the cold dark ahead.

Quantities

  • No materials required - spoken invocation only

Directions

  1. Hail to Cerridwen,
    mother of wheat!
    As the white sow,
    you wander the fields;
    as the mother of wisdom,
    you confer knowledge
    unto the worthy.
    Be merciful to us
    who approach you
    in the wavering year,
    time itself may be your cauldron,
    its ingredients the universe itself.
    We are stirred by you,
    Wild Crone.
    Summon us to wisdom
    through the cold dark that lies ahead.
