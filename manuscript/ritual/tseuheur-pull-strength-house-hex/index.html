A more elaborate North African tseuheur intended to bewitch all those who live in a house. Seven pebbles, seven mirror shards, seven large seed pods, antimony powder, pubic hairs, and menstrual blood clots are packed into a hidden packet. WARNING: because the packet contains the caster's own personal items, if discovered it leaves the caster extremely vulnerable to spell-reversal.

Quantities

Seven pebbles
Seven sharp shards from a broken mirror
Seven large seed pods
Antimony powder
Hex-caster's pubic hairs
Menstrual blood clots

Directions

  1. Fill a packet with seven pebbles, seven sharp shards from a broken mirror, seven large seed pods, antimony powder, and the hex-caster's pubic hairs together with menstrual blood clots.
  2. Find a way to secrete it in your target's home; a houseplant is an extremely popular hiding place.
  3. Because the packet contains the caster's own personal items, if discovered the caster is extremely vulnerable to a spell-reversal.
