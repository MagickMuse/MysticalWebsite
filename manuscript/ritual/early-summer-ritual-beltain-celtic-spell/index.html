🌳Early Summer Ritual Beltain Celtic Spell

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Group
◇Form
Food/EdibleOffering Rite
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
Chant/MantraDivination
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- Thirteen oat cakes or biscuits; if more than thirteen people are present, add more cakes or biscuits - A bag to hold the cakes or biscuits - A sharp knife - If indoors, a large green plant; if outdoors, a tree - Colored ribbons - Different-colored flowers - Twine - Silver bells on strings
◇Directions
1. Mark each of the cakes/biscuits except one with a cross and the last one with a star and put them in the bag. 2. Say: "Welcome summer, enter now, for we honor the green tree bough." Decorate the plant/tree with ribbons, flowers secured with twine, and bells, circling it thirteen times both ways, saying: "Who shall be the Summer King or Queen, who the year shall rule supreme?" Have each person take an oat cake from the bag. 3. To whomever gets an oat cake with the star, supreme good luck is promised; whoever gets an oat cake with a cross makes a secret wish. 4. If you're indoors, crumble the remaining biscuits on the fire or in a dish to feed the birds later, or take them outdoors to scatter the crumbs on the ground. 5. Perform between April 30 and May 2 in the northern hemisphere, and between October 31 and November 2 in the southern hemisphere.
◇Description
A between April 30 and May 2 or October 31 and November 2 spell for early summer ritual called Beltain in Celtic calendar, marking thirteen oat cakes and decorating tree with ribbons flowers bells to choose Summer King or Queen with star cake for supreme luck.
