🔮New Year's Cauldron Charm Divination

A Russian group divination ritual based on New Year's fortune-telling traditions in which each participant's personal charm is drawn from a cauldron after they chant their secret wish, revealing the oracle for that wish. One person serves as supervisor throughout.

Quantities

  • One personal ring or small metal charm per participant (each distinctive and identifiable)
  • One portion of spring water or magickal formula water per participant
  • One cauldron with a lid
  • One sacred metal object for the cauldron (pentacle, horseshoe, or similar)
  • Mugwort
  • Oats
  • One lit charcoal disc per participant

Directions

  1. Each participant brings a distinctive personal ring or small metal charm and a portion of spring or magickal formula water.
  2. One by one, each person places their charm into the cauldron.
  3. A sacred metal object such as a pentacle or horseshoe is also added.
  4. Each participant then pours their water into the cauldron while making a secret wish.
  5. Once all water has been combined, the supervisor sprinkles in mugwort and oats.
  6. One lit charcoal is added to the cauldron for each person present.
  7. The supervisor stirs the cauldron using only the left little finger.
  8. The cauldron is then covered.
  9. Each participant takes a turn chanting a rhyme that alludes to their secret wish without naming it.
  10. After each rhyme, the supervisor reaches into the covered cauldron and draws out one charm.
  11. The charm that emerges provides the oracle for that person's desire.
  12. When the ritual is complete, charms may be exchanged or returned as the group sees fit.
  13. The water must not remain in the house: it is emptied immediately at a crossroads.
