📡Call Pet Telepathically from Distance

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
VisualizationEnergy Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- 1 small pet treat
◇Directions
1. When the pet is in another part of the house, or outdoors, where she cannot see you, hold the pet treat in front of you. 2. Say the animal's name softly in your head, followed by however you usually call the animal to you; for example, using a whistle, still all in your mind. 3. Turn up the volume of the signal gradually in your head, so you are calling louder and louder in your mind, then say aloud in a whisper, "Come to me, find me, I have a treat for you." When the animal arrives, praise her and offer her the treat. 4. If your pet is still not tuned in after a few attempts, start to call her aloud, but softly, increasing the volume of your voice until she arrives. 5. Persevere, reducing the volume gradually until the call is within your head. 6. You will eventually make the psychic link. 7. Gradually increase the distance between you and your pet when you psychically call her. 8. Perform when you and the pet are alone (works best for dogs, cats, and horses).
◇Description
A mental calling ritual gradually developing telepathic connection with pets using treats and visualization.
