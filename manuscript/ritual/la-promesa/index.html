🙏La Promesa

◇Form
Offering Rite
◇Method
Devotional WorkPetition
◇Category
Prayer
◇Quantities
- Candles (optional, for shrine or home altar) - Offering appropriate to the magnitude of your request
◇Directions
1. Begin by asking the saint for what you need. 2. This can happen at their shrine or anywhere else. 3. Then make your promise: state exactly what you will give and when. 4. Avoid vagueness. 5. The timeframe might be immediately upon receiving your miracle or after a set period. 6. For pregnancy petitions, specify that your offering comes after a healthy birth, not merely conception. 7. Naming the child after the saint is a traditional offering for long-awaited children. 8. Ensure the timeline fits your circumstances. 9. Offerings range from lavish to simple. 10. Saint Jude asks only for public testimonial, explaining all those newspaper ads and online posts thanking him. 11. The offering should match the magnitude of your request. 12. Small offerings include candles lit at shrines or home, purchasing a statue of the saint, good works performed in the saint's name, or pilgrimages to associated sites. 13. Ensure your promise is within your capability. 14. Never promise what you cannot deliver. 15. Most saints show mercy, but some insist all vows be honored regardless of circumstance. 16. When your request is granted, fulfill your promise promptly. 17. Most saints accept prayers, good deeds, charitable donations, testimonials, candles, and pilgrimages. 18. What matters most is giving lovingly and respectfully, never grudgingly. 19. Heartfelt gifts outweigh careless lavish donations.
◇Description
The most traditional and effective method for requesting miracles from saints. La Promesa ("the promise") is an ancient approach that exists across virtually all spiritual traditions with variations. Saints have been venerated this way for thousands of years. You make a specific request and promise a specific offering in return when your miracle is granted.
