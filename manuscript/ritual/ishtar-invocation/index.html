Mabon

A harvest invocation to Ishtar, Queen of Heaven, honoring her as Great Mother and priestess of the skies. The invocation recalls her tears for Tammuz and asks for gentle guidance through the harvest season and fallow times.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. Speak this invocation aloud during Mabon rituals or harvest celebrations:
  2. Queen of Heaven,
    we look toward the eagle
    to send our messages of love to you.
    Great Mother, priestess of the skies,
    be gentle with us,
    pour your rain upon our fields
    as once you wept for Tammuz.
    Cradle us, that though we reach skyward
    we do not hurtle to the earth
    but come to it gently
    when our fallow times come.
