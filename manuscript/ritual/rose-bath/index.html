💤Rose Bath

◇Form
Bath
◇Method
Dreamwork
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
• 1 cup rose hydrosol • 5 drops rose attar • 5 drops neroli essential oil or petitgrain essential oil • 5 drops honeysuckle flower essence remedy (Bach flower) • Optional: fresh rose petals and/or orange blossoms
◇Directions
1. Fill a tub with warm water. 2. Add the rose hydrosol, rose attar, neroli or petitgrain essential oil, and honeysuckle flower remedy to the bath. 3. For maximum effect, float fresh rose petals and orange blossoms on the water as well. 4. Luxuriate in the bath before going to sleep.
◇Description
A luxurious pre-sleep bath combining rose hydrosol, rose attar, neroli or petitgrain, and honeysuckle flower remedy to stimulate erotic dreams. Fresh rose petals and orange blossoms may be floated in the bath for maximum effect. One of the Erotic Dreams methods.
