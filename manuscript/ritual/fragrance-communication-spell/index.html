👻Ghosts

A working to establish a structured, mutually agreed communication system with a ghost. It is used when the practitioner is on compatible terms with the ghost and wishes to move from passive, ghost-initiated contact to an equal, consensual exchange initiated by either party.

Quantities

A chosen personal fragrance

Directions

  1. Select a specific fragrance as your personal signal for wanting to communicate with the ghost.
  2. The next time the ghost's own identifying fragrance appears, speak aloud to them.
  3. Explain that you would like to establish a formal communication arrangement based on equality rather than the ghost arriving and departing on its own schedule.
  4. Demonstrate your chosen scent and make clear it will only be used when you are actively seeking contact, never at other times.
  5. Ask the ghost for a sign that it is in agreement with this arrangement.
