Saint John's Wort

Saint John's Wort is burned in an iron cauldron and carried through the home, allowing the smoke to permeate every corner and magickally fireproof the space.

Quantities

Saint John's Wort, iron cauldron

Directions

  1. Burn Saint John's Wort in an iron cauldron.
  2. Carry the cauldron through the home so that the smoke permeates all corners.
  3. This acts as magickal fireproofing for the space.

A smoke-based protective working using the most solar and fire-aligned of the Saint John herbs.

Burn Saint John's Wort in an iron cauldron. Carry the cauldron slowly through every room of the home, allowing the smoke to permeate all corners. This is said to serve as magical fireproofing for the entire space.
