🌕Lunar Tool Consecration Ritual

A full moon ritual for consecrating and empowering moon-magick tools with lunar energy. Items are cleansed, anointed with oil, passed through lunar incense, and arranged within a cord circle before being charged overnight under the full moon.

Quantities

  • Altar or ritual cloth
  • Tools to be consecrated
  • Lunar incense (jasmine, eucalyptus, or sandalwood)
  • Silver or white cord, ribbon, or yarn
  • Moon Oil, Moon Water, coconut oil, or oil of choice

Directions

  1. Cleanse yourself and your items energetically using smoke, sound, salt, or your preferred method.
  2. Lay out your ritual cloth and arrange your items on it, or place them on your altar.
  3. Light the lunar incense and let the smoke rise upward toward the moon's energy.
  4. Visualize the moon's silver light pouring down through the smoke, connecting with you. Feel her presence move through you like liquid silver, filling your skin with moonlight.
  5. Use your cord or ribbon to form a large circle, representing the full moon, large enough to hold all your tools.
  6. Consecrate each item one at a time: hold it, anoint it lightly with oil, pass it through the incense smoke, then place it inside the circle. Repeat for each tool.
  7. When all items are within the circle, hover your hands above them. Visualize lunar light entering through the crown of your head, traveling down your arms, and flowing into the tools. Optional chant: "The moon increases your power with every passing hour."
  8. Leave the items to charge overnight. If moonlight can fall directly on them, the effect is especially potent.
