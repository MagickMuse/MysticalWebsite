💎For a Bad Hair Day

A ritual for a bad hair day when you've got to look your best, using sparkling jewelry and mirror work to outshine any appearance issues with confidence and radiance.

Quantities

  • 1 long mirror so you can see your full figure in it
  • All your most sparkling jewelry

Directions

  1. Look at yourself in the mirror and smile, even if you feel like screaming.
  2. Twirl in the light so you are reflected in the mirror, saying over and over again: "So shall I shine, make this day mine. Beauty untold the world shall behold. Brighter than bling, I outsparkle everything." Put on all your sparkly jewelry and, twirling faster and faster, say the spell words faster and faster.
  3. When you start to feel dizzy, steady yourself, raise your hands above your head, and clap six times.
  4. Leave on at least one bit of bling, hidden if necessary under your clothes, and as you walk out the front door, clap and repeat the spell words (in your mind, if people are around).
