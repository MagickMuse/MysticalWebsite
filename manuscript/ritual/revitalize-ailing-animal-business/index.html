🐾Revitalize Ailing Animal Business

An early Sunday morning spell to revitalize ailing animal-based career or business, wafting nine feathers over work item in pairs then setting feathers free to bring life back to world you prize.

Quantities

  • A small item you use daily in your career
  • Nine feathers

Directions

  1. Place the item on a table with the nine feathers in a circle around it.
  2. Take feathers 1 and 2, one in each hand, and waft them over and around the item nine times, saying nine times: "Go free, revitalize, bring life back to the world I prize." Repeat the words and actions with feathers 3 and 4, then 5 and 6, 7 and 8, and finally 9 and 1, then set the feathers free outdoors, one at a time, saying the same spell words for each one.
  3. Each time you use the item at work, repeat the spell words in your mind, and when it's not in use, keep the item where light will shine on it.
  4. Perform early Sunday morning.
