🗼Tower of Freedom Release

A Tower of Freedom card ritual if you can't let go of a love who has deserted or betrayed you, using the Tower with four Aces to break free from pain and open to new beginnings.

Quantities

  • The Tower of Freedom card (representing freedom from restrictions but not without pain)
  • 1 dark candle behind the Tower
  • The four Aces, two on either side of the Tower: Pentacles (for a new practical start), Cups (for a new love), Wands (for new ventures), and Swords (to cut free)
  • 4 white candles behind the four Aces

Directions

  1. Light the dark candle, saying: "No more of you, lover untrue." Blow out the candle, saying: "Gone from my heart, old love depart." Light the Pentacles candle, saying: "Just one step at a time, to a life that's truly mine." Light the Cups candle, saying: "New love when my heart is whole, who will cherish me, mind, body, soul." Light the Wands candle, saying: "New adventures, exciting ventures." Light the Swords candle, saying: "Hardest of all, but I walk tall. I cut the ties to you, good-bye." Clap your hands fast over each of the four candles and let them burn.
