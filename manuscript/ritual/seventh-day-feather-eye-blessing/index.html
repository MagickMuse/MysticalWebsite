🪶Seventh Day Feather Eye Blessing

A newborn blessing performed on the seventh day after birth. A feather dipped in a paste of olive oil, salt, and onion juice is passed over the baby's open eyes to make them beautiful, sharp, and healthy.

Quantities

Olive oil
Salt
Onion juice
Henna (optional)
Kohl powder (optional)
A feather

Directions

  1. On the seventh day after birth, blend olive oil, salt, and onion juice into a paste, adding henna and kohl powder if desired.
  2. Dip a feather into the paste.
  3. Pass the feather over the baby's open eyes without letting it touch them, so the eyes grow beautiful, sharp, and healthy.
