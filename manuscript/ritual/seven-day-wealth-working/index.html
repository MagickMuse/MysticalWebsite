A seven-day wealth creation program building the foundation for prosperity magick. Creates energetic shifts toward abundance through daily practices (richuals) including visualization, space cleansing, belief reprogramming, money blessing, charged water, and gratitude work. Approach as a game with fun energy rather than lack or desperation.

  • Pen

  • Rosewater spray

  • Heat-safe dish or incense burner
  • Lighter or matches
    Day 4:
  • Paper
  • Pen

  • Wallet
  • Money (any amount)
  • Optional: cinnamon or mint, small pouch

  • Drinking water

Directions

  1. Before You Begin
    Approach this magick as a game, with fun energy rather than being in lack or desperation. Whatever you want to manifest is just "extra" to your already beautiful and amazing life. After each daily practice, go out and live your life. Do not obsess over whether you did it right, if it is working, or how results will come. That is the fastest way to stop manifestation.
  2. Stay aware of synchronicities or coincidences during your wealth journey.
  3. These are signs the magick is working: feathers on the ground, cosmic numbers like 11:11.
  4. Perform the magick knowing it is real and has already worked, then forget about the details.
  5. ---
  6. Day 1: Define Your Wealthy Life
  7. To activate your Wealthy Witch life, you need to know exactly what that means.
  8. Wealth is not just about having a specific amount in your bank account.
  9. It is about the experience you will have, the freedom to do whatever you desire.
  10. If you were making consistent abundant income no matter what, what kind of life would you be living?
  11. Be specific and honest about what you would be doing with your wealth.
  12. Break down all areas of your life and dream it out: career, work, business, money, investments, relationships, family, hobbies, vacation, beauty, spirituality.
  13. Imagine the ways you will spend money, save money, and receive money.
  14. Practice: Write down what you want specifically, as if you have already received these things and how it makes you feel.
  15. Avoid writing what you do not want.
  16. After writing, visualize yourself having it all and living your Wealthy Witch life, like a daydream.
  17. ---
  18. Day 2: The Observer Practice
  19. This practice can be done in as little as ten seconds or take ten minutes.
  20. It can be done anywhere.
  21. Step One: Notice how you are feeling.
  22. Tired, alert, frustrated, angry, sad, happy, or hungry?
  23. Be the observer without creating a story about why you feel this way.
  24. Just notice what is happening within and around you.
  25. Notice how your physical body feels.
  26. This step takes just a few seconds.
  27. Step Two: Gaze at something in front of you and ponder how magickal and beautiful that thing is.
  28. Notice how extraordinary it is that it exists in this physical world.
  29. Step Three: When you feel a sense of beauty and peace, ask yourself these questions:
  30. How did I become so wealthy?
  31. How did I end up living such a wealthy life?
  32. These questions are not meant to be answered.
  33. They are not statements or affirmations.
  34. Ask yourself these questions from your heart with openness and curiosity.
  35. Continue this practice for the remaining five days.
  36. ---
  37. Day 3: Space Cleansing and Rearranging
  38. The energy of our space affects the flow of abundance and wealth.
  39. Today we cleanse and rearrange our space to the ultimate wealth frequency.
  40. Step One: Cleanse your space with herbal smoke such as lavender or rosemary.
  41. Spritz the space with rosewater spray.
  42. Seal in prosperity by burning a cinnamon stick.
  43. Step Two: Step into your visualization of wealth.
  44. If you were already wealthy, what would your space look like?
  45. Step Three: Start moving things around, add things such as new flowers and plants, or move your crystals.
  46. As you move each object, affirm: "This is where this item is supposed to be to call in great wealth in my life."
  47. You can move everything or just one or two things.
  48. The main point is that you are intentionally moving objects and, with each placement, affirming wealth and abundance.
  49. ---
  50. Day 4: Belief Reprogramming
  51. Our reality is created by our beliefs.
  52. Beliefs are statements we have thought many times that we believe are true.
  53. These are programmed into our subconscious and determine how things show up for us.
  54. If you want a different life, you need different beliefs.
  55. Step One: Take out a sheet of paper and make two columns.
  56. On the right side, write down all of your negative beliefs that are holding you back from your dreams.
  57. Step Two: On the left side, write the positive opposite of each negative belief.
  58. Now you have new beliefs that will take you to your goals.
  59. Powerful beliefs to borrow:
  60. My life gets better and better every day.
  61. Everything always works out for me no matter what.
  62. Everything I need, want, and require is always provided for me.
  63. Step Three: Choose one to three affirmations that resonate with you and repeat them every day until they become your truth.
  64. Best done in the morning when your mind is fresh.
  65. Repetition is the key.
  66. ---
  67. Day 5: Blessing Your Wallet and Money
  68. We are blessing our wallet and money.
  69. We want to keep money happy so it stays with us and creates big wealth.
  70. Step One: Open your wallet and add more money to it (any amount).
  71. Step Two: As you add the money, tell it how much you love it and what you will be doing with all the wealth you will acquire.
  72. Share how you will use it: spending on yourself or loved ones, making donations, putting it in savings.
  73. Money is energy that is alive.
  74. The more we love money, the more money will love us and keep coming to us.
  75. Magickal Bonus: Sprinkle cinnamon or mint in your wallet for wealth attraction.
  76. If you prefer, put the cinnamon or mint in a small package before placing it in your wallet.
  77. ---
  78. Day 6: Wealth Water Infusion
  79. Today you will infuse your water with wealth codes and reaffirm everything from the past five days.
  80. Step One: Pour drinking water in a glass.
  81. Step Two: Write one or more of your beliefs from Day 4 on a sticky note and place it on the glass.
  82. Step Three: Hold the glass in both hands and visualize your ideal Wealthy Witch life (like Day 1).
  83. Step Four: When you feel complete, wrap up your vision in a golden bubble and imagine sending it down your arms, into your hands, and into the glass of water.
  84. Step Five: Drink the water.
  85. As you drink, feel your physical body and energy change to match your Wealthy Witch self.
  86. Feel that it is your new reality.
  87. ---
  88. Day 7: Gratitude Transmission
  89. Gratitude is the fuel for the vehicle that is your intention.
  90. Today's gratitude magick is different from just writing down what you are grateful for.
  91. Practice: Tell three people why you are grateful for them.
  92. These can be loved ones or strangers.
  93. Examples:
  94. Tell your parents how much you truly appreciate them and why
  95. Tell your kids how much you love them and why
  96. Tell the person who makes your coffee how grateful you are for them
  97. Tell a stranger that their presence and smile brightened your day
  98. Tell your animals how happy they make you feel
  99. Whatever you tell them, be sincere and truly believe it.
  100. There is no faking gratitude.
  101. The seed of your gratitude power will pass to those people and spread, fueling your wealth intentions.
  102. ---
  103. After the Seven Days
  104. Wealth is not created by doing magick for one week.
  105. We dedicate ourselves to our craft and show up regularly.
  106. You can continue this seven-day practice every week, change it however you like, or do all practices together in one day.
  107. Choose both the practices you love and those you are resistant to.
  108. Resistance often signals the biggest catalyst for alchemy.
  109. It is what you choose to do today that will determine your future.
  110. Whatever you desire is already yours.
  111. Dream big and get there step by step.
