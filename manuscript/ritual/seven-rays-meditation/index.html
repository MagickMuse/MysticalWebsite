Ritual Grimoire

🌈Seven Rays Meditation

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationVisualization
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
None required
◇Directions
1. This meditation is a form of creative visualization that uses all the colors of the rainbow. 2. It gives you the benefits of walking through the rainbow in a slightly different way, by journeying through a magickal painted landscape. 3. Entering the Painted World: Relax and enter the meditative state. Visualize yourself in your secret place, and see a beautiful painting of a landscape hanging on a wall. If your secret place is outdoors, the painting might be leaning against a tree or table. The painting is so beautiful that you get closer and closer to it, until you are suddenly inside it. 4. Walking Through Green Countryside: Now you are walking through lush green grass and gazing at the beautiful trees as you walk through the countryside. You are enjoying the walk, and the slight breeze makes the leaves of the trees murmur as you walk under them. You walk up a rise and sit down at the summit, looking around at the countryside that spreads out in all directions. 5. The Indigo Sea: In the distance you can see the sea. 6. It's a deep indigo color, and you can see a few white flecks on the waves. 7. The Clear Blue Sky: You lie down on the grass and gaze up at the clear blue sky. A few fluffy clouds appear to frolic in the sunlight, but your overall sensation is of the wonderful, beautiful, clear blue of the sky. 8. The Garden of Colors: After a while you decide to start walking again. You get up and look around you. To your left is a large floral garden that you decide to explore. 9. Yellow Daffodils: Someone has planted daffodils in the grass and you pause to admire them. 10. They are the most beautiful yellow that you have ever seen. 11. Orange Sunflowers: You continue on to the formal garden and see that the border is full of sunflowers. 12. You gaze at their orange centers and think of the healing rays of the sun. 13. Red Roses: Inside the garden, most of the flowers are roses. 14. You particularly enjoy the differing shades of the red roses. 15. You marvel at their perfection. 16. The Violet Temple: You see now that this garden is planted in front of a large building with several marble pillars in the front. You walk up the marble steps to the main entrance. It is obviously a temple of some sort. There is a soft glow coming from the entrance. You see why when you walk inside. The entire lobby area is a magnificent violet color, and you feel a sense of peace and tranquility. You are alone, but you suddenly feel the presence of the divine. 17. The Seven Rainbow Rooms: You look outside, but decide to carry on exploring the interior of the building. Ahead of you are seven doors, each painted a different color of the rainbow. You open the red door and discover that the room behind it is completely red. There is red carpet, wallpaper, furniture, and lighting. Each of the other rooms also reflects the color shown on the door. You spend a bit of time visiting the rooms that appeal to you, and then go outside again. You sit on the top step and gaze around at the glorious landscape. 18. Returning: All too soon, it is time to leave. You walk through the garden again, up to the top of the rise and down the other side to where you started. You see the back of the picture frame and climb through it, returning to your secret place. You look back at the picture. You are sorry that you have had to leave, but you know that you can return any time you wish to be bathed by all the colors of the rainbow. 19. Closing: Take a few slow deep breaths, stretch, and open your eyes.
◇Description
A creative visualization journey through a magickal landscape where you experience all the colors of the rainbow. Enter a painted scene and explore countryside, gardens, and a violet temple with seven rainbow-colored rooms.
