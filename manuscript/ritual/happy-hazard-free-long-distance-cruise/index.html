💖Friday

A Friday afternoon spell for happy and hazard-free long-distance cruise, binding pearl in shell with seven knots to sail cocooned in palace of sea to magickal lands.

Quantities

  • A double-lidded shell such as a mussel or oyster shell
  • A pearl, moonstone, silver button, or bead or any small silver item such as a thin ring
  • Twine or string in silver or white

Directions

  1. Set the pearl inside the shell and say: "So will I see the world, so will new sights unfurl while I am safe in luxury, cocooned in my palace of the sea." Close the shell and bind it tightly with seven knots, repeating the words and adding: "Sevenfold, sevenfold, new joys to behold." Cast the shell into water and say: "Sail far, to places uncharted, magickal lands beyond measure, each moment of my voyage will bring a fresh treasure. And so I venture on the adventure of a lifetime." Perform Friday afternoon.
