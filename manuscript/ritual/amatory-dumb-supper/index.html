🕯️Amatory Dumb Supper

◇Solitary or Group

◇Method

◇Quantities
Two place settings using household crockery and cutlery passed through fire and water. Food and beverage favoured by the deceased, served in one-quarter portions. Bouquet of sweet-smelling flowers. Two lamps before the portrait. Thurible with amatory necromancy incense. New white exorcised altar cloth.
◇Directions
PREPARATORY PERIOD (13 nights, primary variant only): Each midnight, seal the working space. Light a lamp of art in the east. Unveil the photograph or portrait of the deceased, flanked with flowers. Kindle amatory necromancy incense. Sit facing the portrait; inwardly call the person by name. Meditate on shared memories of love for 10 to 60 minutes. Drape the portrait and extinguish the lamp to close. Maintain seclusion from company throughout these 13 days. NIGHT OF EVOCATION: Fast from sunset. Just before midnight, cover the altar table with a new white exorcised cloth. Place two chairs: yours at east facing west, the deceased's at west facing east. Set a bouquet of sweet-smelling flowers on the table. Light two lamps before the portrait. Kindle the thurible with amatory necromancy incense. Consecrate the space by walking backwards from east through south, west, and north, asperging and censing, calling upon Habondia to aid the work. Every action from this point must be performed silently and backwards. Lay two place settings, yours in the east and the deceased's in the west, using household crockery and cutlery previously passed through fire and water. Include food the deceased favoured and their preferred beverage. Serve very small portions (one quarter of a normal serving), walking backwards to and from the kitchen in complete silence. As midnight strikes, approach the portrait backwards. Slowly unveil it, mentally repeating the grand citation: "By the mysteries of the deep, by the flames of banal, by the power of the east, by the silence of the night, and by the holy rites of Hecate, I call thee by the ties of love, Spirit of [name] to break thy eternal fast with me. So mote it be!" Replenish the incense. Walk backwards clockwise to your seat. Begin eating your portion. Do not look directly at the chair opposite; this is critical. If this rule is broken, the spell is nullified. When finished eating, leave dishes in place. Walk backwards to replenish incense and extinguish the portrait lamps, still not staring at the western chair. Return to your seat. Close your eyes. Call the deceased's name three times. Mentally repeat: Allay Fortission Fortissio Allynsen Roa! Greet the loved one mentally with eyes still shut. Slowly open your eyes. Allow the communion, which may be a visible form, a mental blending, or simply a felt presence, to unfold. Do not rush. When the manifestation fades or as midnight's hour passes and the current slackens, speak the licence to depart silently: "Go, go, departed shade [name] by Omgroma Epin Sayoc, Satony, Degony, Eparigon, Galiganon, Zogogen, Ferstigon, we licence thee depart into the proper place and be there love between us evermore. So mote it be!" Cast a few grains of incense on the coals. Silently bid farewell. Complete the magickal record. EIDOLON VARIANT: Omit the 13-night preparation entirely. Perform on Midsummer's Eve (the night before the summer solstice). This variant summons the wraith of a future beloved rather than a deceased one. It is less reliable than the necromantic variant; no object link is possible.
◇Description
A necromantic love rite in two variants. The primary working involves 13 nights of preparatory vigil at midnight, meditating before a portrait of a deceased beloved with flowers and amatory necromancy incense, building a bond of love across the veil. On the 14th night, a silent supper is laid backwards for two — the practitioner in the east, the deceased in the west — and conducted wholly in silence and backwards movement, with no protective circle, bound by love alone. The shade is summoned through a grand citation to Hecate and Habondia, communion is allowed to unfold without staring at the western chair, and the spirit is dismissed with a formal licence to depart. A separate eidolon variant (no preparatory period required) is performed on Midsummer's Eve to summon the wraith of a future beloved rather than a deceased one; this variant is less reliable and no object link is possible.
