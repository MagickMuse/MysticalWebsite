◇Directions
1. Select one or more flower essence remedies suited to the dying person's emotional or spiritual state. 2. Administer them either internally as drops, or by gently massaging the essence into the soles of the person's feet. 3. Choose based on the specific need: spiritual surrender, fear, abandonment, anger, isolation, or connection with guides.
◇Description
Flower essence remedies administered to the dying represent one of the most powerful applications of flower essence work. They are given either internally or massaged gently into the soles of the dying person's feet. Recommended essences and their specific purposes: Angels' Trumpet (eases the process and promotes spiritual surrender), Angelica (connects with angels and psychopomps, beseeches their protection), Blackberry (eases depression and fear), Chrysanthemum (builds courage to face the journey ahead), Grape (eases the sense of abandonment during dying and after death), Purple Monkeyflower (resolves terror of dying), Scarlet Monkeyflower (resolves rage and anger about death), Sweet Chestnut (relieves the bitter isolation of the soul), Tobacco (promotes spiritual surrender). ⚠️ Angels' Trumpet, Purple Monkeyflower, and Scarlet Monkeyflower not confirmed as tagged Plant pages.
