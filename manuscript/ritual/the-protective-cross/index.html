Cross

A powerful charging ritual for creating a protective cross from two bound beams. The cross can be made from sacred wood (rowan, oak, thorn), iron nails, bones, or other meaningful materials. The binding moment itself becomes the focal point for charging this ancient protective symbol.

Quantities

  • Materials (choose one):
  • Two beams of sacred wood (rowan, oak, or thorn)
  • Two iron nails (coffin nails if available)
  • Two bones (chicken, animal, or human)
  • Black string or leather for binding
    Note: If using bones, make offerings to the attached spirit and perform divination first.

Directions

  1. Stand with arms fully extended, holding the two beams of the cross out in front of you in a pose similar to repelling a vampire.
  2. Visualize the arms of the cross extending outward into infinity.
  3. Focus your mind on the point where they intersect.
  4. As you hold the cross, make a proclamation such as:
  5. By Boreas, Zephyrus, Eurus, and Notus
    By Phlegethon, Cocytus, Styx, and Acheron
    By all the princes and powers of the four directions
    I bind and consecrate this cross
    That it may forever be a shield and protection
    Against all manner of malevolent powers
    Hateful spirits and baleful spells
    By Will and Word
    So Shall It Be
  6. After charging the cross, set it down upon the altar or ground without separating the beams.
  7. Bind them together by wrapping in black string or leather.
  8. If working with iron and possessing the skill, weld the cross together.
  9. Wear or carry the finished cross as a protective amulet.
