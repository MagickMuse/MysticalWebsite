✨Clear Space for an Altar

◇Tradition
Folk Magick
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting

◇Method

Indigenous (Sage & Sweetgrass)
◇Quantities
- Cleansing supplies (sage, cedar, mugwort, or incense) - Elemental symbols (candle, water bowl, plant, feather) - Personal sacred objects
◇Directions
1. Clear your space and ground yourself. 2. Shuffle your tarot deck while focusing on the question: "What do I need to understand about this betrayal, and how do I open my heart again?" 3. Lay out three cards in a row: 4. Card 1 (The Wound): What hurt me? 5. What wound does this betrayal touch or reopen? 6. This card shows the emotional injury, the pattern, or the fear that the betrayal triggered. 7. Card 2 (The Lesson): What is this experience teaching me? 8. This card reveals the growth opportunity hidden in the pain (boundaries, self-worth, discernment, forgiveness, etc.). 9. Card 3 (The Path Forward): How do I heal and love again? 10. This card offers guidance on how to move through the pain, rebuild trust (in yourself or others), and open your heart without hardening it. 11. Interpret each card slowly. 12. Journal your insights. 13. If a card feels unclear, pull a clarifying card beneath it. 14. Close by placing your hand over your heart and saying: "I honor my pain. I choose to heal. I open to love again, wiser and whole." 15. Keep the cards on your altar or photograph them as a reminder of your healing journey.
◇Description
An altar-building practice inspired by Beaver's intentional creation. By designating and clearing sacred space, then thoughtfully adding meaningful elements, you create a workspace for spiritual practice, honoring, meditation, reflection, and dreaming. ⚠️ Sage and sweetgrass are sacred to Indigenous peoples and should only be used by those within Indigenous traditions.
