🧪Moon Foam

◇Form
PotionWater/Spray
◇Method
LunarWater MagickChant/MantraInvocation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
- 1 bowl or pitcher - Pure spring water - 1 oleander branch - Strainer or skimming tool
◇Directions
1. Approach the gates of a cemetery at the New Moon and remove all clothing. 2. Fill a bowl or pitcher with pure spring water and place it in the moonlight. 3. Mount an oleander branch and ride it around the perimeter of the cemetery counter-clockwise seven times, chanting a personal incantation as you circle. 4. This action is meant to attract the attention of the moon's guardian spirits. 5. After the seventh pass, a lunar spirit should manifest and ask you to reveal your desire. 6. Tell her you want Moon Foam. 7. She will resist, so you must negotiate and make various offers until she agrees. 8. If the negotiation succeeds, the moon will descend into the water. 9. Eventually, visible silver foam will appear on the surface. 10. Carefully skim it off, as you would oil from water. 11. Take the foam home and reserve it for future use.
◇Description
A Moroccan ritual for capturing the essence of the New Moon as a silver foam. Traditionally used to enforce fidelity, restore fertility, cure impotence, and ignite passionate desire in the practitioner.
