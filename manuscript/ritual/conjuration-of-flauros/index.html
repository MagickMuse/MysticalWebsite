Sigil of FlaurosWitches' Drum Symbol

An advanced demonic evocation calling Flauros, a great duke of the Goetia who appears primarily as a terrible leopard. This operation requires a fully fortified grand circle of protection with four Watchtowers, and a triangle of manifestation at the north bearing the sigil of Flauros. Presented in outline only, as it is intended for experienced practitioners who can fill in details from personal practice and the source grimoires.

Quantities

  • Exorcised paper and pen and ink of art (for drawing the sigil of Flauros)
  • Thurible with Martial incense OR pure asafoetida
  • Cingulum (cord) for binding the sigil
  • Black cloth for hooding the sigil
  • Athame or sword
  • Exorcism incense (for purification after the rite)
  • Sigil of Flauros worn on the breast in a chamois or silk sachet (with Pentacle of Protection)

Directions

TIMING: When the moon is 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, or 14 days old; between sunrise and noon; clear weather preferred.

SETUP: Cast a grand circle of protection fully fortified with the four Watchtowers. Place a triangle of manifestation at the north of the circle. Draw the sigil of Flauros on exorcised paper with pen and ink of art and place it in the triangle. Light Martial incense or pure asafoetida in the thurible. Wear the sigil of Flauros together with the Pentacle of Protection on the breast in a chamois or silk sachet.

WORDS OF POWER: "Themesho - Masanin!"

IDENTIFICATION: Perform the initial identification ritual using fire, water, and wand (the operator's standard opening). If desired, incorporate the initiation ritual from the preceding chapter.

SIGIL PRESENTATION: Remove the sigil from the breast. Bind it with the cingulum. Hood it with the black cloth. Carry it to each of the four quarters, challenging at each with the athame. Return to the altar.

FURTHER CONSECRATION: Perform further consecration of fire and water over the sigil. Unveil it and place it in the altar triangle.

CHARGE: Charge Flauros with the specific mission. Issue the license to depart.

CLOSE: Thoroughly purify the room with fire and water. Burn exorcism incense.

NOTE: This conjuration is presented in outline only. It is intended for advanced practitioners familiar with the full Solomonic system and capable of supplying the missing details from experience and from the source grimoires.
