🪞Preserved Mirror Photo Binding (Romany)

Two photographs, one of the caster face-up, one of the target face-down, are arranged gazing at each other through a small mirror, wrapped in red cord and red silk, and hidden or buried near the target. A traditional Romany binding. The source notes ethical complexities around hiding it in a gifted plant versus keeping it under your own control.

Quantities

Photo of yourself
Photo of your heart's desire
Small mirror
Red cord or thread
Red silk cloth

Directions

  1. Place a photo of yourself face-up beneath a small mirror.
  2. Place a photo of your heart's desire face-down in front of the mirror so the faces in both photographs are looking at each other with the mirror between them.
  3. Wrap all three objects together with red cord or thread, focusing on each knot.
  4. Wrap the bound mirror in red silk cloth.
  5. Wrap the package in thread once more so it is very tight and secure.
  6. Hide the packet: the traditional Romany recommendation is to bury it somewhere in the target's home, this can be done by concealing it inside a potted plant given as a gift.
  7. Alternatively, bury it in earth beside apple trees, roses, or other love plants, or in a flowerpot kept under your own control at home.
  8. Consider: if hidden somewhere inaccessible, you may not be able to retrieve and undo it later.
