🔮Michaelmas Daisy Divination

A traditional British flower petal divination originally performed with Michaelmas daisies in late September. The familiar game of plucking petals while reciting alternating answers provides yes/no responses to any question.

Quantities

  • One flower with petals (traditionally a Michaelmas daisy, a small white flower that blossoms in central Great Britain around the end of September)
  • Any flower with petals can substitute

Directions

  1. Select a flower with petals.
  2. Traditionally this divination used the Michaelmas daisy, but any flower with multiple petals will work.
  3. Make sure not to take the flower from someone else's yard without permission.
  4. Formulate a yes or no question clearly in your mind.
  5. Do not count the petals beforehand, as this defeats the purpose of the divination.
  6. Begin plucking petals one at a time, alternating between two answers.
  7. The traditional phrases are "loves me" and "loves me not" for romantic questions, but you can adapt the alternating phrases to suit any yes/no question.
  8. Continue plucking petals, reciting the alternating answers, until you reach the final petal.
  9. The answer spoken on the final petal is your divination result.
