A double-headed coin talisman made by gluing two quarters together with luck-drawing spices, nutmeg, clove, and cinnamon, sealed between them so the coin always shows heads. Charged under the Sagittarius or Blue Moon. Carry it when you need fortune on your side, or keep it on your altar for luck spells and meditation. Recharge each full moon by passing through lunar incense.

Quantities

  • 2 clean, shiny quarters
  • Strong glue (such as superglue)
  • Ground nutmeg
  • Ground clove
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Jasmine incense (optional)

Directions

  1. Begin by washing both coins thoroughly with soap and water to clear any energy they've picked up.
  2. Hold the coins in your hands and look up toward the moon, or simply hold her image in your mind.
  3. Visualize the moonlight pouring down and illuminating the coins, filling them with luck, blessing, and the rare, golden quality of a blue moon.
  4. Apply a thin layer of strong glue to the tails side of each coin.
  5. Sprinkle a small amount of nutmeg (for luck and attraction), clove (for luck and protection), and cinnamon (for manifestation and drawing in what you desire) across the glued surface of each coin.
  6. Press the two coins together, tails facing inward and heads facing out on both sides, so no matter which way the coin falls, a heads side faces up.
  7. Hold them firmly together in your palms as the glue sets.
  8. As you hold them, close your eyes and say:
  9. "No matter which way the coin falls, no matter the labor;
    Like the luck of the blue moon, it always lands in my favor."
  10. Visualize the moon's blessing sealing itself into the bond between the coins.
  11. If using jasmine incense, pass the finished talisman through the smoke for an additional layer of lunar enchantment and attraction.
  12. Leave the talisman outside or on a windowsill to charge overnight in the moonlight.
  13. To maintain the spell over time, pass the coin through lunar incense at each full moon as a renewal of its charge.
