🌍Redress Poverty Corrupt Regime Aid

A toward end of waning moon outdoors spell to redress poverty in lands where corrupt regime diverts overseas aid so it does not reach those in need, pouring water between pots for resources to be wisely spent not squandered.

Quantities

  • A large jug of water
  • Two small pots or jars of equal size
  • A bottle or jar with a lid

Directions

  1. Start pouring water from the jug into one of the pots until it overflows, saying as you do so: "Resources, money pouring in, squandered, wasted, it is a sin. That those who need it, never see it, and those in power, money for themselves do shower." Start to slowly pour water from the overflowing pot into the other pot until the overfull pot has no more excess water, saying: "Carefully used and wisely spent, that is how the aid was meant. Corruption, dishonesty, they must stop, no more on the rich shall resources drop." Put any spare water from the jug in a bottle or jar with a lid and close the lid, keeping the water to drink or pour on flowers.
  2. Perform toward the end of the waning moon, outdoors on grass or soil.
