Circle

Nine herbs said to stand against every spiritual and magickal danger, planted or potted in a circle around the space to be protected. In times of spiritual danger, sit inside the circle.

Directions

  1. Plant the nine herbs around the edge of the area you want protected, in a circle if possible.
  2. If you need the circle to be movable, keep the herbs as living potted plants that can be set out in a ring whenever needed.
  3. During times of spiritual danger, sit inside the circle.
