🍀Ceremony for Inviting Luck and the Goodwill of the Gods

Hellenic household ceremony that petitions the Gods for goodwill and good fortune at the start of any new endeavour or before a major decision. Adapts the Orphic hymns to Demeter and Hera with a hymn to Tyche-Artemis, calling on three Goddesses whose spheres each bring prosperity in their own measure. The work assumes that even where outcomes do not depend on personal skill alone, the favour of the Gods can shape what unfolds. The attitude of the suppliant matters as much as the offerings, so the rite is approached with a virtuous and steady mind. Best performed in the early days of the lunar month and on the auspicious days identified within Hellenic household practice.

Quantities

  • Red wine for libations
  • Water for libation
  • Fresh fruits as offering to Demeter
  • Honey as offering to Tyche
  • Olybanum (frankincense) incense, charcoal disc, and a burner or tripod
  • Optional perfumes for the offering to Hera

Directions

  1. Bathe and settle the mind beforehand so the suppliant arrives composed, virtuous in attention, and clear of distraction.
  2. Arrange the altar with the wine vessel, the water vessel, the dish of fruits, the dish of honey, the burner with charcoal, and the olybanum (oh-LIB-ah-num).
  3. If perfumes are kept on hand, set them near Hera's place at the altar.
  4. Stand at the altar in stillness, then speak the opening invocation:
  5. I call on Heavenly Demetra (deh-MEH-trah),
    Flourishing Hera (HEH-rah),
    and Artemis Tyche (AR-teh-mees TEE-keh),
    to enter our lives with goodwill.
  6. Libate red wine.
  7. Kindle the olybanum on the charcoal as the offering to Demeter, and set out the fruits before Her place.
  8. Raise the hymn:
  9. O' Dio (DEE-oh), mother of all,
    respected Goddess,
    Demetra (deh-MEH-trah), giver of Happiness.
    O' Goddess, bringer of wealth,
    who provides everything and rejoices in peace,
    nurturing Goddess of many children,
    much respected, multi-formed, prolific.
    Come, blessed and pure, bringing peace, and desirable prosperity,
    blessed wealth and the Noble Hygeia (hee-YEH-ah, Health).
  10. Libate with water.
  11. Renew the olybanum, or substitute perfume, as the offering to Hera.
  12. Speak the hymn:
  13. Hera (HEH-rah), Queen of all, blissful wife of Zeus (ZOOS),
    Air-formed and seated in dark blue bays,
    granting to mortals the gentle soul-nurturing breeze.
    All begetting, Mother of rain, tender of the winds.
    No living thing knows the complete nature of life without you,
    because you partake in everything merged with the sacred air.
    You alone dominate all and rule over everything.
    Blissful Goddess, renowned queen of all, may you come with goodwill to our solemn ceremony.
  14. Libate red wine.
  15. Set out honey before Tyche's place and renew the frankincense.
  16. Speak the hymn:
  17. Come Tyche (TEE-keh), I invite you, benevolent ruler, hear my prayer;
    kind traveller of all roads, in wealthy estates,
    the reigning Artemis (AR-teh-mees), the renowned, born of Euvouleos' (eff-voo-LEH-os) blood, possessing undefeatable glory,
    sepulchral, meandering, famed among men;
    From You flows the diversity of mortal life,
    for some, you provide a wealthy multitude of possessions,
    and to others dread poverty, welling up as angry outrage in the soul.
    Goddess, I plead for you to bring your goodwill,
    filling our lives with well-being and wealthy gifts.
    And I plead with You, O' Goddess, come with benevolence into our lives, bringing happiness and wealth.
  18. Then repeat the petition seven times in a steady, gathering voice:
  19. O' Benevolent Goddess Tyche, foster good fortune and bring about the desired results.
  20. At the seventh repetition, libate red wine.
  21. Close the rite by speaking the thanksgiving:
  22. Heavenly Demetra,
    Flourishing Hera and Artemis Tyche
    we thank You and your attendant company
    for being present at our solemn ceremony
    and hearing our supplications.
    We are grateful for the virtues
    and the goods which you have offered us.
    May friendship and harmony touch our hearts.
    May the Gods and the Goddesses, the Heroes and Heroines,
    May the Blessed Ancestors shower virtue
    and healthy understanding in our minds.
    May health forever dwell in our bodies.
    May happiness and good fortune not ever leave this home.
    May father Zeus always provide all that is good.
  23. Libate red wine.
  24. Seal the working with the closing word:
  25. ΓΕΝΟΙΤΟ (yeh-NEE-toh, it is done).
  26. After the ceremony, return any spent incense, ash, and the libated portion to clean ground or running water so the offerings may pass back to the Earth.
  27. Share the remaining fruits and honey at the household table, since shared food carries the blessing forward into the days that follow.
