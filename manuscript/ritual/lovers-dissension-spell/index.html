🍊Lovers' Dissension Spell

One of three simple spells for the jilted witch. A baneful philter compounded in a chalice during a waning moon circle rite, charged with a spoken curse, and subsequently introduced into the targets' food or drink to sow discord between them.

Quantities

  • Grated orange peel
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Cumin seed
  • Powdered rue
  • 1/4 cup boiling distilled water
  • Chalice (set in altar triangle, wreathed as for Hekate-Nocticula spells)

Directions

Cast the circle during a waning moon. Set the chalice in the altar triangle, wreathed as for Hekate-Nocticula spells.

Mix into the chalice: grated orange peel, salt, pepper, cumin seed, and powdered rue.

Pour approximately a quarter cup of boiling distilled water over the mixture while chanting:

"I [witch's name] make this philter. / May it bear / malediction, deep affliction, / here upon this married pair, / [name] and [name]. / May they never be united, / may they quarrel every day, / may their marriage-bond be blighted / ere a year has passed away! / This shall be the life between them, / let that life be as it may!"

Seal with "So mote it be!" and a triple left-hand cross.

Introduce the philter via food or drink (chocolates, baked goods, wine, etc.) shared by both targets.
