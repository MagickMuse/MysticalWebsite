A milk bath ritual for the Full Moon, Supermoon, or Blue Moon, drawing on the ancient tradition of goddess milk baths, most famously associated with Cleopatra. Powdered milk, cornstarch, and baking soda are blended with lavender oil, rose petals, and violet into a storable bath blend. The working infuses the aura with spring lunar beauty, peace, and rejuvenation, and can also be used as a love bath shared with a partner.

Quantities

  • 1 cup powdered milk
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • ½ cup baking soda
  • 10 drops lavender essential oil
  • ¼ cup dried rose petals
  • ⅛ cup dried violet or other safe flower of choice
  • Jar with lid (for mixing and storage)

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in the jar: powdered milk, cornstarch, baking soda, lavender essential oil, dried rose petals, and your chosen flowers.
  2. Seal the lid and shake thoroughly to blend.
  3. As you shake, use the motion to raise energy and visualize fresh, youthful vitality blooming like a flower opening inside the mixture.
  4. Set the jar aside and fill your bathtub with warm water.
  5. Pour half a cup of the mixture into the bath, or more as desired.
  6. Using your hand, stir the water clockwise while visualizing the moon's light pouring down and illuminating the bath around you.
  7. When you feel ready, speak aloud:
  8. "Ethereal light of the milk moon,
    Enchant my energy in this nurturing cocoon."
  9. Disrobe and ease into the bath.
  10. Rest in the nurturing lunar energy.
  11. You may sip Lunar Loose-Leaf Flower Tea for added flower moon magick and let your mind rest gently on your intentions for the moon.
  12. Store the remaining mixture in the sealed jar and use within one month.
