A spell invoking Zemlya, the Slavic mother goddess, to define and defend your personal boundaries against intrusions on your time, space, and privacy. Most powerful on a Saturday.

Quantities

Paper and pen (for the Grievance List and Action Plan)
Small pile of earth
Black candle
Fireproof container
Water
Digging tool

Directions

  1. Identify the groups and individuals who regularly overstep your bounds and write your grievances down as a Grievance List; set it aside for at least twenty-four hours, then reread it, meditate on it, and revise it as needed, taking any nonmagickal action first.
  2. Define your boundaries and the actions you will take to enforce them on a second sheet of paper as your Action Plan; set it aside for at least twenty-four hours, then reread it, meditate on it, and revise it as needed.
  3. Assemble the spell materials and get into a magickal mood, creating sacred space around your working area.
  4. Put your Action Plan on your altar or working surface, place a small pile of earth in its middle, and spread the soil out so it lines the four sides of the paper. Place the black candle upright in the empty center of the paper.
  5. Position the fireproof container nearby on a heatproof surface, put the Grievance List beside it, and place the water nearby.
  6. Raise power, light the candle, and recite the incantation, pushing or sprinkling some of the soil from the sides of the paper near the candle each time you say Zemlya's name, until all the soil surrounds the base of the candle.
  7. Carefully move the candle aside, pick up the Action Plan by either end, and tip all the soil into the fireproof container. Replace the candle in the center of the Action Plan.
  8. Take the Grievance List, touch one end to the candle flame, set it ablaze, and drop it into the fireproof container, letting it burn completely while you visualize ending all violations of your boundaries.
  9. Once the paper is consumed and the candle burns itself out, pour the water into the container with the ashes and soil.
  10. Take the container, your Action Plan, and a digging tool outside (or to a potted plant if working indoors), dig a hole, bury the Action Plan, pour the mud and ashes over it, cover the hole, and ground the power that you raised.

Coexisting with other human beings can be stressful, especially in crowded conditions such as cities and large households. When we have trouble getting along with people, it is often because they overstep the boundaries that we have unconsciously set, then failed to communicate.

The best approach is to deal directly and honestly with others, to tell them frankly but kindly what we expect or will not tolerate. But if you have already tried that and it has not worked, this spell can help you to define and defend your boundaries.

The spell invokes Zemlya, a Slavic mother goddess also known as Mati Syra Zemlya, meaning "Moist Mother Earth." The spell can be cast at any time, but it is most powerful on a Saturday or when the Sun or Moon is in Saturn.

The Incantation

Beloved Zemlya, Mother Earth,
Protect me from annoyance, interference, and incursion,
From harassment, intrusion, and invasion.
Beloved Zemlya, Mother Earth,
Protect me from all that besieges, poaches, or encroaches,
From all that seeps or creeps or prevents sleep.
Mati Syra Zemlya, Moist Mother Earth,
Protect me from all that presses, stresses, depresses, or trespasses,
From all that disrupts, interrupts, pushes, assails, or infringes.
Beloved Zemlya, Mother Earth,
Protect me from all that is rude, crude, or intrudes,
From all that crowds or is too loud.
Mati Syra Zemlya, Moist Mother Earth,
Protect me from everyone and everything
That would overstep my boundaries!

Afterward

Be vigilant in patrolling the borders you have defined for yourself. The best way to keep this spell working is to carry out all the items on your Action Plan. If you need some magickal help with this, water the place where you buried the spell debris whenever you have problems with boundary issues, take a tiny pinch of dirt from that spot, sprinkle it on your head, and ask Mati Syra Zemlya for her help.
