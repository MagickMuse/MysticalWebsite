🦊Energetic Camouflage

◇Directions
1. Fox is at home slinking down a suburban street or running in cornfields. 2. She easily shape-shifts her exterior to blend into her environment. 3. You can too. 4. Energy follows intention. 5. Begin reworking your energy signature by thinking about what's making you stand out from the background energetic pattern. 6. Are you moving too fast or too slow? 7. Are you being too loud or too quiet? 8. Are you projecting meekness or confidence? 9. To energetically camouflage yourself, you need to vibrate at the same frequency as the world around you, aligning energetically with your surroundings. 10. Shape-shifting begins with changing your energy. 11. Then you'll change the way you move your body to align with that new energy. 12. Focus on the energy you're projecting and shift it with intention. 13. If you're moving too fast, consciously decide to slow down. 14. If you're being too meek, consciously decide to project confidence. 15. This simple shift begins your energetic camouflage. 16. Deepen the illusion by feeling into the energy you've donned and letting your body move in sync with it. 17. Play with energetic camouflage when you don't need it, so you've mastered this ritual for the times when you do.
◇Description
An energy-shifting practice inspired by Fox's shape-shifting abilities. By identifying what makes you stand out from the background energetic pattern, then consciously adjusting your energy and movement to match your surroundings, you master the art of blending in or standing out as needed.
