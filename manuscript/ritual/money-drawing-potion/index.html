These tea potions attract monetary gain and encourage financial stability. Five variations are provided.

Important Note: Money potions are not substitutes for steady effort or gainful employment. You have to work in order for these potions to work.

Version 1: Chamomile and Blackberry Leaf

Brew chamomile tea and blackberry leaf tea together.

Version 2: Clover and Mint with Sunflower Seeds

Brew clover tea and mint tea together. Add three sunflower seeds. If the seeds have shells, discard them after drinking; if unshelled, eat them afterward.

Version 3: Ginger with Cloves and Orange

Brew ginger tea. Add four cloves and a strip of orange peel. Discard the cloves and orange peel after drinking.

Version 4: Mint with Poppy Seeds and Aventurine

Brew mint tea. Add three poppy seeds and a small green stone such as aventurine. DO NOT INGEST THE STONE. Discard the poppy seeds, but wash and retain the aventurine as a money charm.

Version 5: Clover and Chamomile with Almond

Brew clover tea and chamomile tea together. Add one almond. Eat the almond after drinking the potion.

Optional Enhancements

A green cup or mug strengthens money magic. A golden spoon symbolizes wealth. Honey sweetens your financial situation.

Stirring Symbol

Trace the shape of Fehu, the rune of wealth and prosperity, into the surface of the tea as you stir.
