🍀Horseshoe Good Fortune Relocation Charm

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Talisman
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraPetition
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
Horseshoe Red thread Square of cardboard Sequins Glitter glue Lucky seeds and beans Votive images of San Martín Caballero
◇Directions
1. Wrap a horseshoe in red thread until only the tips remain exposed. 2. Attach the wrapped horseshoe with its tips facing downward to a square of cardboard. 3. Decorate with sequins, glitter glue, lucky seeds and beans, and votive images of San Martín Caballero. 4. Once the physical construction is complete, activate its blessings by repeating the phrase "Citron nueve" (nine fruits) twenty-one times.
◇Description
A Latin American folk talisman wrapping a horseshoe in red thread and decorating it with lucky seeds, sequins, and images of San Martín Caballero, activated by repeating an incantation twenty-one times to ease relocation and bring good fortune.
