🪢Braiding the Crios Bríde

◇Tradition
Celtic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charm
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Cord MagickKnot MagickHealingInvocation
◇Category
Ritual
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
- Three lengths of satin cord (rattail), each at least twice your waist measurement, in three different colors - A candle - Beads (optional, for the trimmed ends)
◇Directions
1. Bless your workspace and make sure you won't be disturbed. Light the candle to invoke Brigid's presence, and put on deepening instrumental music if you like. 2. Gather the three strands in your hands and set your clear intention that this will become a ritual object, imbued with the power of Brigid's healing, saying: The girdle of Brigid is my girdle, The girdle of three strands. Brigid who is three in one, Guide this work of my hands. 3. Knot the three strands together about 8 inches from one end, and secure that end to something stationary (a clipboard, chair back, or doorknob works well) to keep the braid from slipping as you work. 4. Braid the strands in a simple, unhurried, repetitive rhythm. Each time you cross and tighten the strands, envision the scattered parts of your spirit coming back together in a stronger pattern of empowerment. 5. Speak a line of an original triad with each movement of the braid, if desired. 6. When the braid is long enough to tie comfortably around your waist, tie a knot at the end as you did at the beginning, and trim the dangling ends. Add beads to the ends if you wish, unless you plan to wear the girdle under your clothes regularly. 7. Wear the finished crios Bríde for at least one moon cycle to keep your intention strong and Brigid's blessings close; you don't need to leave it on at all times. After that, let Brigid's guidance tell you when to wear it.
◇Description
Braiding three cords into a single strand channels Brigid's healing power to mend a scattered or overburdened spirit; the finished cord becomes a wearable talisman of her ongoing blessing.
