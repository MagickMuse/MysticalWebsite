🪢Nine Knot Underwear Candle Women's Fidelity Ligature

A fidelity ligature combining nine knots tied in a woman's unwashed underwear with a dressed red candle; the knotted garment is later hidden wrapped around a heavy metal tool.

Quantities

Woman's unwashed underwear
Red figure candle, red seven-day candle, red devil candle, or red phallus candle
Carving tool and dressing oil
A few of the spell-caster's pubic and underarm hairs
Hammer or heavy metal tool (for final concealment)

Directions

  1. Choose a red candle to represent yourself, a figure candle, seven-day candle, red devil, or red phallus.
  2. Carve and dress it so it is clearly identified with you.
  3. Tie nine knots in the woman's unwashed underwear, announcing your intent aloud with each knot, calling her name at the same time, for a total of nine proclamations and nine names spoken.
  4. Arrange the knotted garment around the candle; if using a glass-sheathed seven-day candle, tie the garment to it.
  5. Spit on the candle and sprinkle a few of your pubic and underarm hairs over it.
  6. Announce the target's full name and declare she belongs to you.
  7. Light the candle.
  8. When the candle finishes burning, take the knotted underwear and tie it to a hammer or other heavy metal tool.
  9. Hide it in a secret place.
