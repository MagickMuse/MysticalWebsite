A protective garden maintains an aura of safety around the perimeter of the home. Include as many of the listed protective plants as possible.

Quantities

As many as possible of: angelica, cactus, clover, dill, elder, flax, gardenia, garlic, holly, juniper, mugwort, nettles, oregano, roses, rosemary, rowan, rue, Saint John's wort, southernwood, thyme, vervain, wormwood, yarrow

Directions

  1. Choose as many of the listed protective plants as possible.
  2. Plant them in the garden around your home.
  3. Tend the garden to maintain its protective aura.

Living Boundaries

In fairy tales, witches' cottages are surrounded by magickal gardens. A garden is a living altar, cultivated to achieve your desired goals, and a protective garden creates an aura of safety around the perimeter of your home.
