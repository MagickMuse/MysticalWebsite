🌙Coven Initiation: The Skyclad Rite

A coven initiation performed skyclad (naked), suited to goddess-oriented covens concentrating on love and healing aspects of the Craft. The ceremony is performed by the high priest if the candidate is a woman, the high priestess if a man. Includes a first degree and an optional second degree. The rite enacts the myth of the goddess's descent into the realm of the dead, receiving the fivefold kiss and scourge of the Horned God before the conferment of power.

Quantities

  • Athame or sword (for the challenge and circle repair)
  • Cingulum for binding wrists and neck (cable-tow)
  • Ankle cord
  • Small bell
  • Scourge of art (whip of cords, 40 strokes for first degree; 120 for the second degree initiator)
  • Sabbat Oil (for the triangle or pentacle consecration)
  • Consecrated wine
  • Coven workbook / Book of Shadows (for the oath and register)
  • Blindfold (first degree only)
  • Altar ring (for tying the cable-tow)
  • Coven witch jewel for investiture

Directions

  1. PREPARATION: Circle cast by watchtower. Candidate blindfolded at the perimeter and challenged at sword or athame point. Passwords given: "perfect love and perfect trust." The initiator pulls the candidate backwards into the circle, left arm around the waist, right arm around the neck, and gives the third password: a kiss. The breach in the circle is repaired with the athame. The candidate is bound with cingulum and ankle cord as in the robed rite.
    PROCLAMATION: The candidate is led around the circle and proclaimed to each of the four watchtowers (east, south, west, north) as a prospective candidate for admission.
    CHARGE OF THE GODDESS: The high priestess delivers the Charge of the Goddess to the candidate:
    "Listen to the words of the Great Mother, who was of old called among men Artemis, Astarte, Dione, Melusine, Aphrodite, and many other names. At mine altars the youth of Lacedaemon made due sacrifice. Once a month and better it be when the moon is full, meet in some secret place and adore me, who am the queen of all magics.
    There assemble, and to those who would learn sorcery, I will teach things yet unknown. And you shall be free, and as a sign that you be really so, be naked in your rites, dance, sing, feast, make music and love. All in my praise, for I am a gracious goddess, who gives joy upon earth, certainty, not faith, while in life; and upon death peace unutterable, rest and the ecstasy of the goddess. Nor do I demand aught in sacrifice, for behold, I am the mother of all living, and my love is poured out upon the earth!"
    SKIPPING CIRCUIT: The candidate, clasped at the waist by the initiator, is led around the circle with a skipping step.
    FIVEFOLD KISS: South of the altar, after eleven knells on a small bell, the fivefold kiss is bestowed:
  2. Feet: "Blessed be thy feet that have brought thee in these ways."
  3. Knees: "Blessed be thy knees that shall kneel at the holy altar."
  4. Genitals: "Blessed be thy organ of generation without which we would not be."
  5. Breasts: "Blessed be thy breasts, formed in beauty and in strength."
  6. Lips: "Blessed be thy lips that shall utter the sacred names."
    SCOURGING: The candidate kneels at the altar; the cable-tow is tied to the altar ring, forcing a crouching position. The feet are also bound. The candidate is asked whether they will always be true to the art. On affirmative answer, a knell of 3, 7, 9, and 21 strokes is rung. The candidate is purified with 40 strokes of the scourge of art. After the candidate promises to help and defend all brothers and sisters of the art, the oath is administered (substantially as in the robed rite).
    CONSECRATION: The feet and eyes are unbound. Triangle consecration: anoint the genitals, right breast, left breast, and genitals again -- first with Sabbat Oil, then with consecrated wine, then with a kiss -- naming the candidate priest/priestess and witch.
    CLOSE: Hands are unbound. Working tools are presented in turn, with a kiss for each. The initiate is presented to the four quarters, saluted at each as newly made priest/priestess and witch.
    SECOND DEGREE (optional): Differs from the first in that the candidate remains unhoodwinked, gives no passwords, and participates in the opening ceremonies and chants. The oath is sworn "upon the mother's womb." Consecration uses the pentacle rather than the triangle: anoint genitals, right breast, left hip, right hip, left breast, and genitals again (an inverted pentacle sequence). The initiator lays hands on the new initiate and instructs them in the use of the magickal implements and in the mysteries of the threefold law, whether for blessing or cursing. The ceremony concludes with the new initiate scourging the initiator with three times as many strokes as received in the first degree (120 total). The initiate is then presented to the four quarters as a duly consecrated high priest and wizard, or high priestess and queen of the Sabbat.
