A cord magick spell invoking Anath, weaving nine cords in gold, green, and orange with charms to draw prosperity and abundance.

Quantities

Nine cords or ribbons, about a foot long: three gold, three green, three orange
Three small charms representing prosperity, such as a coin, key, horseshoe, clover charm, or crystal

Directions

  1. A cord magick spell invoking Anath, weaving nine cords in gold, green, and orange with charms to draw prosperity and abundance.

The Weaving Prosperity Spell calls upon Anath, the fierce Canaanite goddess of war and fertility, to braid abundance into your life. Though often remembered for her warrior aspect, Anath is also a goddess of the harvest, and her energy ensures that what is sown will be reaped in plenty.

Why Anath?

Anath fiercely protects what she loves. When you invoke her for prosperity, you are asking not just for wealth to come, but for the strength to claim it and the power to keep it. She does not grant passive abundance. She empowers you to seize opportunity.

The Nine Cords

Gather nine cords or ribbons, each about a foot long:

  • Three gold cords: For wealth, success, and solar energy
  • Three green cords: For growth, fertility, and money
  • Three orange cords: For ambition, attraction, and opportunity

You will also need small charms to weave into the braid. Choose three that represent what prosperity means to you. These might be a coin, a key, a tiny horseshoe, a four-leaf clover charm, a small crystal, or any symbol that speaks of abundance in your life.

The Working

Hold all nine cords together and tie them in a knot at one end. Secure this end so you can braid freely.

Begin braiding the cords together. As you work, chant:

Anath, fierce one, hear my call,

Bring abundance, great and small.

Gold for wealth and green for gain,

Orange to draw what I would claim.

When you reach the point where you want to add your first charm, weave it into the braid and say:

First blessing woven, prosperity grows.

Continue braiding. Add the second charm:

Second blessing woven, abundance flows.

Continue to the third:

Third blessing woven, fortune I know.

Finish the braid and tie off the end with a secure knot.

Activating the Talisman

Hold the completed braid between your palms. Visualize golden light filling the woven cords. Say:

By Anath's might, this spell is bound.

Prosperity circles, round and round.

What I have woven shall come to be.

As I will, so mote it be.

Using the Charm

Hang the braided talisman near your front door to draw prosperity into your home, keep it in your workspace to attract professional success, or carry it with you when pursuing financial opportunities. Recharge it monthly by holding it during the waxing moon and repeating the final incantation.
