🔥Welsh Ivy Leaf Fire Divination

A Welsh Halloween fire divination for reading the fate of a romantic connection.

Quantities

2 ivy leaves (one pointed, one rounded); 1 small fire

Directions

  1. On Halloween night, build a small fire outdoors or in a safe hearth.
  2. Select two ivy leaves: one with a sharply pointed shape and one with a softer rounded shape.
  3. Assign one leaf to yourself and one to the person whose feelings you wish to read (the shape carries the symbolism; the leaves are not gendered).
  4. Toss both leaves into the flames at the same moment and watch carefully.
  5. If the leaves curl toward one another in the heat, love and union are favored.
  6. If they curl away from one another, the connection is doomed and will not last.

Materials

  • 2 ivy leaves (one pointed in shape, one rounded in shape)
  • A small fire (outdoor bonfire, fire pit, or safe hearth)
  • If one leaf burns far faster than the other, the slower-burning love is the more lasting one.

Notes

This is a Welsh Halloween divination, traditionally worked on the night when the veil thins and the future shows itself in flame and shadow. Perform it with sincere intent; ivy is a plant of binding and fidelity, and it does not answer idle curiosity kindly.
