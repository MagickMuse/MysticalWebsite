⚠️ MALEVOLENT WORKING. A simple but potent binding spell to banish a specific person from your property and prevent them from returning. A photograph is wrapped completely in string, binding the target's image, then buried where it will not be discovered. The binding can be reversed by unearthing the photograph, untying the string, and burying each component separately.

Quantities

1 photograph of the person
Twine or string (enough to fully cover the photograph)

Directions

  1. Take a photograph of the person you wish to banish from your property.
  2. Wrap the photograph with the string until it is completely covered, focusing your intent on preventing the target from setting foot on your land.
  3. Bury the wrapped photograph in the ground in a location where it will not be found.
  4. If you wish to remove the binding at a later time, dig up the photograph and untie the string.
  5. Then bury the photograph in one hole and the string in a separate hole.
