🦋Dandelion Butterfly Faery Energy Wishes

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar Working
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Chant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A dandelion clock with spores or butterflies
◇Directions
1. Pick a dandelion and blow softly three times so that you do not dislodge any spores. 2. Make your wish after each breath. 3. Turn three times clockwise, three times counterclockwise, and three times clockwise again, saying nine times: "Dandelion faery, flying high, carry my wishes to the sky." Blow harder as the spores fly away, repeating the words after each breath. 4. When all are flown, whisper: "Dandelion faery, I give you my wishes in hope and in trust and ask you return with my dreams fulfilled." Count the number of times you blow the spores as an indication of days, weeks, or months, depending on the nature of the wishes, before fulfillment. 5. If there are no dandelion spores nearby, visit a butterfly farm, choosing a settled butterfly each time you make a wish. 6. Blow softly three times toward each butterfly, whispering the wish three times for each butterfly as they fly away. 7. When all have flown, say: "Butterfly faeries, carry my wishes and return with my dreams fulfilled." Perform Wednesday.
◇Description
A Wednesday spell for bringing wishes into actuality by using dandelion or butterfly faery energy, blowing spores or whispering to butterflies three times while turning clockwise and counterclockwise for faery to carry wishes to sky.
