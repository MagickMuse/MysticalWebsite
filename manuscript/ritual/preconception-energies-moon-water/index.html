🌙Preconception Energies Moon Water

◇Form
Potion
◇Method
Water MagickLunarHerbal MagickChant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A silver-colored bowl, half filled with water - Seven small white flowers - Silver candles (if indoors)
◇Directions
1. Go outdoors and face the moon, or if it is dark, light silver candles indoors near an uncurtained window. 2. Set the flowers around the bowl where the moon or candlelight shines in the water, saying: "Lady Moon, I call your fertility within this water that you will grant me/us a healthy son or daughter. Bright as your light, gentle as your glow, that I may know when right it is to be, and joys of conception and pregnancy." Leave the water overnight, mesh-covered if necessary, and in the morning filter it into small glass bottles and refrigerate. 3. Each morning and night, massage a few drops of moon water over your womb, repeating the words. 4. Continue the ritual monthly until you start trying for a baby. 5. If you wish, you can continue until conception. 6. Perform when the moon is bright, full, or nearly full, a few months before you start trying for a baby.
◇Description
A full moon spell to increase preconception energies especially after long contraception use, creating moon water with seven white flowers to massage over womb for fertility.
