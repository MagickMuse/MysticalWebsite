A monthly house cleansing ritual performed on the new moon, inspired by the ancient Greek Hekate's Deipnon. Combines physical tidying, sweeping, altar tending, a crossroads offering, and closing incense to transition cleanly from one lunar cycle to the next.

Quantities

  • Dried rue or protective herb (optional; for difficult months)
  • 1 crossroads offering: eggs, leeks, onions, garlic, or a garden vegetable
  • Incense (any; reflecting your intentions for the coming lunar cycle)

Directions

  1. Tend to all altars and shrines: remove old offerings, refresh water, dispose of spell remains from the previous lunar month.
  2. Optionally sprinkle dried rue or another protective herb on floors before sweeping.
  3. Sweep through every room of the home.
  4. Take out trash from every room; clear spoiled food from the refrigerator.
  5. Take a small offering to a three-way crossroads near your home.
  6. Leave the offering and speak the ancestor prayer.
  7. Return home and light incense of your choice to close the ritual.

Overview

This ritual is inspired by Hekate's Deipnon, a ceremony the ancient Greeks celebrated on the night of the new moon, when the moon is completely dark. The Deipnon involved purifying the home and making offerings to Hekate and the dead.

Hekate was regarded as a Titan, a member of the race that preceded the Olympian gods, and as queen of heaven, earth, and the underworld. Because of her authority in all three realms, she was associated with witchcraft and the dead.

This modern adaptation uses the Deipnon structure to close out the old lunar cycle and open a clean, protected space for the new one.

Timing

Perform on the night of the new moon, when the moon is completely dark.

Crossroads Prayer

Speak this prayer when leaving the offering at the crossroads:

Ancestors, I give thanks for
the lunar cycle that has been,
and I praise the lunar cycle
that is to come.
I pray that no ghosts, no jealousy,
and no bad intentions follow me
from one moon to the next.
I have made space in my life
for the blessings that you
will bestow upon me.

Notes

  • This ritual can be as simple or elaborate as needed
  • The crossroads offering and the sweeping are the most essential elements
  • The incense chosen for closing should reflect what you are calling in, not just what you are clearing out
