Mabon

A Mabon blessing ritual that honors teachers at the start of the school year. Assembles a basket of supplies decorated with apple motifs, charging each item with good humor and wisdom to support educators in their service to the community.

Quantities

  • Basket
  • Apple-themed decorations
  • School supplies: printer paper, glue, construction paper, scissors (including left-handed), facial tissue, hand sanitizer, baking soda, Lysol, crayons, pens, erasers, gel ice packs, dry erase markers, sticky notes
  • Optional: cold prevention remedy, gift cards for educational apps
  • Thank you note

Directions

  1. Assemble a basket of school supplies at the beginning of the year.
  2. Decorate in an apple motif if you can.
  3. You need not include actual apples.
  4. Include common classroom needs such as reams of printer paper, glue, construction paper, scissors (all the better if you can get a few left-handed scissors in), facial tissue, hand sanitizer, baking soda, Lysol, crayons, pens, erasers, gel ice packs, dry erase markers, sticky notes, and perhaps a small bottle of one of those cold prevention remedies.
  5. If you are feeling especially generous, gift cards for phone and tablet apps can go a long way toward helping your child's teacher give the best education possible to their class.
  6. As you assemble the basket, imagine the items glowing or taking on the personality inanimate objects do in an animated movie.
  7. Imagine each item spreading good humor, good cheer, and a sense of equanimity and passing that wisdom along to the teacher who runs the classroom.
  8. Package the items up as well as you can and send a note along, thanking the teacher for their service to your children and to your community.
