🫧Bubble Wishes for Urgent Needs

A late afternoon bubble-blowing ritual repeating wishes ten times and releasing them to the wind for urgent manifestation.

Quantities

  • 1 bubble blower and bubbles (purchased or homemade)
  • Wildflower or edible seeds

Directions

  1. Shake the bubble mixture ten times, reciting your wish ten times; take off the lid and stir clockwise ten times, saying ten times, "Bubbles, work your magick for me, that my wish(es) are granted urgently." Blow the first bubble, and as it floats away, say: "Wish(es) fly free, carrying my hopes from thought to actuality." Continue blowing and wishing (more than one wish if you like), for each wish repeating the second wish chant as you release each bubble, until the bubble jar is empty.
  2. Scatter seeds in thanks.
  3. Repeat the spell weekly for four weeks in total for a longer-term wish, if possible on a Wednesday or Thursday afternoon in the same location.
  4. Perform late Wednesday or Thursday afternoon.
