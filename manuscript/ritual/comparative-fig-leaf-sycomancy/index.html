Fig

A sycomancy method for comparing multiple options or choices. One fig leaf per option is labeled and observed under identical conditions. The freshest leaf longest determines the oracle's recommended path. If all leaves age at the same rate, a better option not yet considered is indicated.

Quantities

  • One fresh fig leaf per option or choice
  • A pen or writing instrument

Directions

  1. Write a distinct plan, proposal, or option on each fig leaf, one leaf per choice.
  2. Place all leaves together in the same location under identical conditions, ensuring they do not touch each other.
  3. Observe them over time.
  4. The leaf that retains a fresh, green appearance the longest represents the oracle's favored choice.
  5. If all leaves age at exactly the same pace, the oracle indicates that a better option exists that has not yet been considered.
  6. Revisit the question before committing to a decision.
