🕯️Double-Action Reversal Candle

A candle spell to reverse a jinx using a special dual-colored candle (white on top for clearing the curse, black on bottom for sending it back) dressed with reversing oil and burned with a prayer invoking Hekate to return the curse to its sender.

Quantities

  • Required:
  • Double-action reversal candle: 1 (white on top, black on bottom)
  • Reversing oil (dill, rue, devil's shoestring in carrier oil)
  • Photo or object link to target (if known), or paper addressed to "those who do me harm"
  • Matches or lighter
    For Reversing Oil:
  • Dill
  • Rue
  • Devil's shoestring
  • Carrier oil (almond oil or virgin olive oil)

Directions

  1. Preparation:
    If making your own reversing oil, soak dill, rue, and devil's shoestring in a carrier oil such as almond oil or virgin olive oil.
  2. Dressing the Candle:
    Hold the candle horizontally with the bottom facing you. Rub the reversing oil from the bottom to the top, away from your body. As you do this, concentrate on reversing the curse or crossed condition back from where it came.
  3. Setting Up:
    If you know the identity of the target, get a photo or some other object link and place it underneath the candle. If you don't know the identity of the target, place a paper under the candle addressed to "those who do me harm." Place a drop of reversing oil on each of the four corners and in the center of the paper or photo.
  4. The Working:
    Light the candle and pray:
  5. Shadow, hear my call to thee
    Pitiful hate-filled enemy
    Be malignant if you will
    For I am more malignant still.
  6. I turn you over to Hekate
    To send the curse back to thee
    Damning you as the wax burns down
    Committing your power to the ground.
  7. Let the candle burn all the way down.
  8. Disposal:
    Collect the wax and the personal items and bury them near a three-way crossroads. If you can't do this at a three-way crossroads, you can do it at a graveyard (but not on a grave, which would be overkill). The idea is that you are sealing the spell by delivering it to Hekate Chthonos, who dwells in the underworld.
