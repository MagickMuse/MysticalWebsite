🪬Blessing the Altar and Shrine

Annual cleansing and re-blessing of a Brigid altar or shrine. Each sacred object is removed, the surface is cleansed, and items are restored one by one with full intention, asking Brigid whether each still belongs.

Quantities

Cleaning materials for the altar surface; 1 candle

Directions

  1. Perform this at least once a year. Imbolc (February 1) is the traditional time.
  2. Remove everything from the altar or shrine. Set each item aside with care.
  3. Thoroughly clean the altar surface. As you clean, envision clearing away any stagnant or accumulated energy and making the space fresh and open.
  4. Light a candle and set it on the empty surface. Invite Brigid to be present as you restore the space.
  5. Take up each item one at a time. Hold it. Look at it as if for the first time. Remember where it came from and what it means to you. Feel its associations and symbolism.
  6. Before returning it to the altar, ask, silently or aloud, whether it still belongs there, and if so, why. Listen. Trust what comes.
  7. Return only those items that feel right. Do not add anything automatically or simply because it has always been there. If something no longer belongs, set it aside with gratitude for its service.
  8. If the space has felt dull or stagnant, consider introducing something new, a different symbol, a simpler arrangement, or a single object you have not used before. A bare surface and a single candle can be among the most powerful altar configurations of all.
  9. When the altar feels complete, speak a word of thanks to Brigid and allow the candle to burn for a time before extinguishing it.
