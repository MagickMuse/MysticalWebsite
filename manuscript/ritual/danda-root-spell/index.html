Danda Root

A Brazilian folk magick working focused on influencing communication and speech, particularly what another person says.

Danda root is chewed like Courtcase root, but with an important difference: spitting is not the point. Simply keep a small piece of the root tucked into your cheek.

While the root is concealed in your cheek, concentrate intensely on what you need the other person to say. Hold the vision clearly and direct your will toward shaping their words accordingly.

This root's power is said to be in its subtle influence on speech. Keep it quiet, keep it focused.
