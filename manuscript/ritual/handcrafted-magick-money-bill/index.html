🎨Handcrafted Magick Money Bill

◇Directions
1. Create your own currency, do not just put a high number on a green rectangle; design magick money that will radiate its own power. 2. Choose your colors and symbols. If you cannot draw, trace symbols or create a collage. Focus on your desires, goals, and needs as you create this bill. 3. Your bill is your spell; if you have put energy into it, it will radiate power all its own. 4. Hang it on the wall in plain sight to draw wealth and prosperity toward you.
◇Description
A prosperity bill spell using hand-designed currency. Rather than a real bill, create your own magick money charged with intention and hang it on the wall to draw wealth.
