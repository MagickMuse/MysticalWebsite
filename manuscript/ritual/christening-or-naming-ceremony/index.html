💧Christening or Naming Ceremony

◇Tradition
Eclectic
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Altar WorkingConsecrated Item
◇Setting
Outdoor
◇Method
Water MagickChant/MantraConsecrationElemental
◇Category
Ceremony
◇Difficulty
Intermediate
◇Quantities
- A small round polished crystal - A jug of water
◇Directions
1. Anyone connected with the child can do this. 2. Take the crystal and jug of water into the garden or to anywhere you have a large green plant. 3. Hold the crystal over the earth or the plant and pour a little water on it. 4. Name a gift or blessing you wish for the baby. 5. As the water falls on the earth, say: "Mother Earth bless and protect my/this child that he/she may walk safely and happily the pathways of beauty all through life." Continue to pour and ask for blessings. 6. Ask for the things you consider important as well as for worldly success, and each time you pour, repeat the blessing of Mother Earth. 7. When the jug is empty, go indoors, dry the crystal, and use it to begin a collection of crystals you will give to the child on a milestone birthday. 8. Each birthday or anniversary of the christening/naming ceremony, buy a new crystal and repeat the spell. 9. Perform the morning of the christening or naming ceremony.
◇Description
A morning of christening or naming ceremony spell for anyone connected with child, pouring water over crystal on earth while naming blessings to walk safely pathways of beauty through life.
