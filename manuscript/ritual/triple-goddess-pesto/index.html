A potent fertility pesto made from three herbs each associated with a powerful fertility goddess. The herbs should ideally be ground with a mortar and pestle, whose parts and process mimic the procreative act.

• Basil, associated with Ezili Freda Dahomey and Lakshmi
• Lemon Balm, associated with Aphrodite
• Parsley, associated with Persephone

Traditionally served over pasta; may also dress fish or meat. One of the Basil Fertility Spell methods.

Quantities

  • Fresh basil
  • Fresh lemon balm
  • Fresh parsley
  • Pine nuts or walnuts
  • Garlic (to taste)
  • Olive oil
  • Optional: Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Grind basil, lemon balm, and parsley together using a mortar and pestle (preferred for symbolic value) or a food processor.
  2. Add pine nuts or walnuts and as much garlic as desired.
  3. Once the dry ingredients are processed, add olive oil a little at a time, mixing until the desired consistency is reached.
  4. Traditionally served over pasta, but may also dress fish or meat.
  5. Store covered in the refrigerator.
  6. Mix in Parmesan cheese just prior to serving, if desired.
