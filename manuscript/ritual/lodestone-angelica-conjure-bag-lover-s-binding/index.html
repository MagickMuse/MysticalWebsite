A red conjure bag packed with a named paper heart anointed with three love oils, a photograph, a lodestone, a magnetic horseshoe, and angelica and High John roots knotted together with red thread, kept under the mattress as a binding charm. High John the Conqueror is toxic; handle with care and do not ingest. ⚠️ Consent note: This working influences another person's free will. Weigh that choice with care and take full responsibility for its effects. ⚠️ Lodestone Angelica Conjure Bag Lover's Binding is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

Paper (for cutting a heart shape)
Come to Me Lover! Oil
Cleopatra Oil
Command and Compel Oil
Red conjure bag
Photograph of your love (or both of you)
Lodestone
Small magnetic horseshoe
Angelica root
High John the Conqueror root
Red thread

Directions

  1. Draw or cut out a paper heart.
  2. Write both your names inside it.
  3. Anoint the paper with Come to Me Lover!, Cleopatra Oil, and Command and Compel Oil.
  4. Place it inside a red conjure bag.
  5. Add a photograph of your love or of the two of you together and happy.
  6. Also add a lodestone and a small magnetic horseshoe.
  7. Add two roots: angelica to represent women and High John the Conqueror to represent men.
  8. Tie them together with red thread, knotting securely.
  9. Keep the bag beneath your mattress and anoint with more oil as needed.
