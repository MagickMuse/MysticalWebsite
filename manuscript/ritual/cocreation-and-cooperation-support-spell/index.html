A waxing gibbous spell to combine forces with another person, spirit ally, habit, or concept and sweeten the energy toward a shared goal. Two chime candles are engraved, dressed with oil and herbs, bound together with thread, and burned in a sugar base to magnify cooperation and propel intentions toward success. Can also be adapted to bind a new habit or theme to your purpose.

Quantities

Two chime candles
Oil, for dressing
Ginger, lemon balm, and jasmine
Thread
A bowl of sugar

Directions

  1. Engrave each chime candle with a name, title, keyword, or trait; one representing yourself or your goal, the other representing your collaborator, spirit ally, habit, or concept.
  2. Dress both candles with oil.
  3. Mix the ginger, lemon balm, and jasmine on a flat surface.
  4. Roll each candle through the mixture, drawing it toward yourself as you coat it.
  5. Hold the candles together and wrap them with thread.
  6. As you bind them, chant.
  7. For a habit or success working: "With this thread, I bind to me, the ___ that I need." For harmony, cooperation, or love: "With this thread, I bind to me, that we might work in harmony."
  8. Stand the bound candles upright in the bowl of sugar; the sugar holds them in place and sweetens the energy.
  9. Sprinkle remaining herb and spice mixture clockwise into the sugar around the candles if desired.
  10. Light both candles and visualize the two forces joining to create something greater than either alone.
  11. Let the sugar melt slightly as the candles burn.
  12. Allow both candles to burn completely in a safe, supervised space.
