A crystal-based purification technique for cleansing sacred space by walking counterclockwise spirals while using a charged stone to repel negative energy.

Quantities

A citrine or clear quartz crystal

Directions

  1. ## Choosing Your Stone
  2. Citrine, a yellowish-colored quartz crystal, is a good choice for space purification, although clear quartz works as well.
  3. Before using your stone to purify a space, wash it in warm water and pat it dry.
  4. Remember to cleanse your stones often to remove any negative energy that may cling to them.
    ## Charging the Stone
  5. Program or charge the crystal with the purpose of purification by speaking your intention to the stone.
  6. Rather than visualizing negative energy being absorbed into the stone, imagine the stone pushing away unwanted vibrations.
    ## The Purification Process
  7. Beginning at the perimeter of your area, slowly walk counterclockwise in circles that grow decreasingly smaller, spiraling in toward the center.
  8. As you do this, hold the crystal out and visualize it repelling the unwanted energy away from your space.
  9. If you notice a place that feels as if it has collected lots of negative energy, pause there and move the crystal around and up and down slowly, until you feel that the energy has been dispersed.
    ## Completing the Working
  10. Finish in the center of the space.
  11. As an added precaution, you can leave the crystal lying on the floor to allow it to continue chasing away unwanted energy.
