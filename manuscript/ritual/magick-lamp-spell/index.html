💤Magick Lamp Spell

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
Dreamwork
◇Category
Divination
◇Quantities
• An oil lamp (consecrated before use) • A natural mat or blanket to sleep on • Ink scented with myrrh • A strip of white cloth or linen • An olive branch • Kyphi, or frankincense and myrrh (for fumigation)
◇Directions
1. Set up the magick oil lamp. 2. If it is terracotta, decorate the body with appropriate symbols or sigils before the working. 3. Consider precisely what you need from the dream. 4. Compose an incantation that addresses your need and requests assistance from the deity of your choice. 5. Prepare a natural mat or blanket on the floor to sleep on. 6. Using ink scented with myrrh, write your dream requirements onto a strip of white linen. 7. Wrap the linen strip around an olive branch with the written side facing inward. 8. Place it on the mat so that it will rest at the left side of your head when you lie down. 9. Fumigate the room and yourself thoroughly with kyphi or with frankincense and myrrh. 10. Kneel beside the lamp and repeat your incantation seven times while gazing into the flame. 11. Then lie down and go to sleep.
◇Description
A dream incubation spell using a magick lamp as a focal point, with kyphi incense burned to open the dreaming mind to messages and oracles. One of the Dream Incubation methods.
