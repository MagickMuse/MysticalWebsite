The Reality of Invisibility

This isn't the movies. You won't truly vanish. However, you can become imperceptible, blending into crowds and avoiding the attention of those who might cause trouble.

The Practice

Preparation

  1. Find a comfortable position and relax your mind. Take a few deep breaths until ready.
  2. Picture yourself from about ten feet away. Rotate your vision 360° to confirm the image is firmly in place.

Size Shifting

  1. Picture yourself shrinking very small, until your etheric body is the size of an ant. Everything else seems enormous. At this size, things pass you by easily: people spoiling for a fight, hexes, general trouble.
  2. Once you have that feeling, reverse it. Picture your etheric body growing until you tower over everything. Anyone wishing you harm is now as small as you were a moment ago. You're too large to feel the effects of whatever magic might have been headed your way.
✨
Note: When your spirit is taller, you may notice you stand straighter and feel more grounded. Our energetic bodies have significant effect on our physical bodies.
  1. Return to your regular size. Use the allover visualization to make sure everything is back where it belongs.

The Fade

  1. Watch yourself become enveloped in fog or mist. You start to fade from view until only your outline remains, then even that disappears.

Unwanted attention, negative energy, and hostile intentions pass by without finding you.

Important Limitations

You remain imperceptible only while not attracting attention to yourself:

  • Move about as commonly as you can
  • Don't do anything that draws eyes to you
  • Use this to blend into crowds or avoid nosy neighbors

Regular practice increases speed and effectiveness.
