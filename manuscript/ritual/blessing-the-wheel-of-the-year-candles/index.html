Imbolc

A beeswax candle-making and blessing practice that charges eight pairs of candles for the turning of the Wheel of the Year.

Quantities

Beeswax sheets
Cotton wick
Fine glitter (optional)

Directions

  1. Roll a beeswax sheet around a length of cotton wick to form a simple candle.
  2. For a two-color spiral candle, cut the rectangular sheets diagonally, stack two different-colored triangles, and roll from the wide side down to the point.
  3. For extra sparkling energy, roll finished candles in very fine glitter, taking care while burning since it can pop.
  4. At Imbolc, make or bless eight pairs of candles for the coming turn of the Wheel, one pair each for spring equinox, Beltane, summer solstice, Lughnasadh, autumn equinox, Samhain, winter solstice, and Imbolc again.
  5. Envision the energy of the growing light while working and ask Brigid to bless and charge each candle.
  6. Wrap and label the candles for their intended use and store them in a quiet place.
