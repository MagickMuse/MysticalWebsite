✨Grape Juice Truth Revealing Spell

◇Form
Writing Spell
◇Method
Herbal MagickDivination
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- Red wine or grape juice (as needed for writing) - Biodegradable paper - Writing implement (feather quill, chopstick, knitting needle) - Black ink pen
◇Directions
1. Prepare your writing implement. Use it like a quill pen, dipping it in red wine or grape juice. 2. On biodegradable paper, write: From vine to bottle, purple-red I must know, although I dread Reveal what is hidden, pull back the curtain So that I may see and know for certain 3. After writing the spell, write out a sentence of that which you would like to know. 4. Let the paper dry. 5. Fold it three times horizontally, then three times vertically. 6. Tuck it in your wallet or between your mattress and box spring. 7. Watch for signs and slips of the tongue. 8. Be completely informed before you act on any new knowledge. 9. Once the truth has been told, write "Thank you" across the paper in black ink and bury it.
◇Description
Dionysus's favorite fruit can be put to work as a kind of truth-telling serum. See the deep purple color as the veil behind which secrets are kept. Warning: Make sure that the information you're after is something you can live with knowing.
