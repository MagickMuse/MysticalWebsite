A Norse invocatory opening formula that incorporates the Hammer Rite into a larger ritual framework. The vitki invokes Odin by multiple epithets, traces a protective circle with the wand, performs the rune ring and hammer signing, and enkindles a sacred brazier with salt and brewer's yeast while singing galdrar. This rite prepares a charged ritual atmosphere for the magickal working that follows.

Quantities

  • Rune wand
  • Brazier (fire-pot)
  • Salt (a pinch)
  • Brewer's yeast (a pinch)
  • Herbs, leaves, or thin wood strips (optional, matched to ritual intention)

Directions

  1. Stand in the center of the vé, face north or east in the stadha, and intone the invocatory galdr:
  2. Fare now forth
    mighty Fimbultýr
    from heavenly homes all eight
    Sleipnir be saddled,
    hither swiftly to ride:
    Galdrsfadhir,
    might to give and gain.
  3. Holy rune-might flow
    from the hooves of Hangatýr's steed;
    in streams of steadfast strength,
    through staves of stalwart standing!
  4. Move to the northern or eastern rim of the vé and trace the circle with the wand in the direction of the sun, from left to right.
  5. While drawing the circle, sing:
  6. The rune-might is drawn
    'round the holy-stead,
    unwanted wights wend way!
  7. When the circle is complete, return to the center.
  8. Facing the original direction, perform the rune-ring portion of the Hammer Rite.
  9. When the rune ring is in place, speak:
  10. The worrisome wights now wend their way
    eastward toward etin-home;
    hallowed be the hall of Hroptatýr,
    with the help of Hrungnir's slayer!
  11. Perform the remainder of the Hammer Rite.
  12. If the working calls for a brazier, enkindle the fire-pot.
  13. The need-fire friction method may be used if the vitki knows it and the rite requires it; otherwise, light the brazier with a previously prepared flame.
  14. Have containers of salt and brewer's yeast at hand.
  15. While lighting the fire, sing the following galdr, adding a pinch of salt and yeast to the flame at the point indicated:
  16. Endless light of life
    give thy living gift
    fill the night of need:
    to the hearth of this hall
    bring thy boon so bright
    to quicken this salt
    and yeast all so cold
    together live long and well
    in the hearts of Hár's sib.
  17. Once the fire-pot is burning, leaves, thin strips of wood, or herbs corresponding to the intention of the rite may be added to the flame.
  18. The body of the magickal working may now begin in a charged atmosphere.
