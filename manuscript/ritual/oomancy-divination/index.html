The art of divining with eggs from Greek oon (egg) and manteia (divination). Also known as ooscopy, oomancia, oomantia, ooscopia, ovamancy, or ovomancy. Most common method separates egg white from yolk, pours white into very hot water, and interprets shapes that form. Can predict pregnancy multiples by cracking egg over pregnant belly.

Quantities

  • Fresh eggs
  • Pot for boiling water
  • Bowl or container for hot water
  • Heat source
    For pregnancy prediction:
  • Fresh egg
  • Bowl
  • Pregnant mother

Directions

  1. This practice is the art of divining with eggs and comes from the Greek words oon for egg and manteia for divination.
  2. It is also known as ooscopy, oomancia, oomantia, ooscopia, ovamancy, or ovomancy.
  3. The most common type of egg divination begins by separating the whites from the yolk and then quickly pouring the white into very hot water and interpreting the shapes that form.
  4. The best way to do this is to bring the water to a rolling boil and then remove it from the heat, allowing the bubbling to cease before quickly adding the whites.
  5. How you interpret the shapes you see is entirely up to you, but remember to keep an open mind and don't just see what you want to see.
  6. This is also a good divination to do with a partner so you can bounce ideas and interpretations off of each other.
  7. What one may see as a dollar sign, another may see as a snake.
  8. Another form of divination with eggs is to tell whether a mother is having a single baby or multiples.
  9. Take a fresh egg and rub it on the mother's pregnant belly for a few minutes, then crack the egg into a bowl.
  10. The number of yolks predicts how many babies the mother will have.
  11. This can be a scary divination for some people, particularly if there is blood in the egg or if the yolk is broken.
  12. These are signs of a miscarriage or some other problem with the birth.
