This spell calls on Janus, Roman god of beginnings, endings, thresholds, and transitions, to place a psychic shield of protection around your home, entrances, or property. It's best cast at dawn or dusk on the first day of the month, but can be done anytime. Janus is invoked to guard portals, perimeters, and people, not to replace practical safety.

Quantities

  • four rocks of the same approximate size (smooth, flat stones that show both sides)
  • a permanent felt-tip pen
  • Optional: grains of wheat (or bulgur, wheat germ, flour) mixed with salt (kosher, sea, or table salt)

Directions

  1. Draw Janus' face in profile on both sides of each rock, left-facing on one side, right-facing on the other. As you draw each image, say aloud:
    "This is Janus."
    Believe it and it will be Janus.
    Place the rocks on your altar or somewhere honored. If using wheat and salt, mix them and sprinkle deosil around the rocks as an offering.
    Choose four locations that form the corners of your house, room, or property, ideally aligned with cardinal directions. These can be indoor or outdoor. Visualize them forming a shield.
    Get into a magickal mood. Collect the rocks and create sacred space. Raise power and focus on Janus. Speak to him in your mind and ask him to charge the rocks with protective power. Concentrate until you feel the charge. Hold your hands over the rocks and say:
    Powers of Janus, powers of rock,
    Through the year, around the clock,
    Protect us better than a lock.
    Powers of Janus, powers of stone,
    Across, about, between, together, alone,
    Protect all that is our own.
    Place the rocks in the chosen positions. Repeat the incantation as you place them. When placing the second rock, visualize a force field connecting it to the first. Keep building that shield with each placement.
    If using wheat and salt outdoors, sprinkle them over the rocks as a final offering. Ground the power you raised.
  2. Afterward:
    To maintain the spell, revisit the visualization regularly. Keep the rocks clean and visible, dust them, clear debris, and renew the image of Janus as needed.

Who Is Janus?

Janus (pronounced Jayn-us or Yayn-us) is the double-faced Roman god of beginnings and endings. His dominion includes entrances, exits, thresholds, the first day of each month, and the first day of the year. January, the first month of the year, is named for him. He can be invoked to protect portals, passages, perimeters, people, and property.

Be reasonable in your expectations of what this spell can do. Placing a psychic shield of protection around your home does not mean that you can then safely leave doors unlocked, ignore needed repairs, or do anything foolish.

Timing

This protection spell can be cast at any time, but it is most powerfully cast at dawn or dusk on the first day of a month.

Depicting Janus

Janus is usually depicted as a mature man with short curly hair and a curly beard. Your artistic ability or accuracy in depicting Janus is not important; the power of your intent is. As you draw each image, say aloud: "This is Janus." Mean it when you say it. Believe it, and it will be Janus.

Choosing Locations

Select four spots to position the rocks. The locations should represent the cardinal points as closely as possible. Options include:

  • Indoors in the four corners of your house or apartment
  • Outdoors at the four corners of your house, yard, or property

You can determine positions mentally or by physically walking to each location before the ritual.

The Incantation

Hold your hands over the charged rocks and speak:

Powers of Janus, powers of rock,
Through the year, around the clock,
Protect us better than a lock.
Powers of Janus, powers of stone,
Across, about, between, together, alone,
Protect all that is our own.

Placing the Rocks

When the rocks are sufficiently charged, begin placing them in the positions you have chosen. Repeat the words of the spell continuously while you do this.

As soon as you place the second rock, visualize a force field or laser light (or whatever image works for you) shooting between the first and second rocks. Build this image as you place the rest of the rocks until the force field covers the entire perimeter, exterior or interior, of your home or property.

If the rocks have been placed outdoors, gather up the wheat and salt and sprinkle them over the stones as an offering to Janus.

Maintaining the Shield

To keep this spell activated:

  • Periodically renew your visualization of the force field
  • Tend the rocks by dusting them, clearing leaves or snow from them
  • Do whatever is needed to keep the images of Janus visible
  • Redraw the faces if they fade over time
