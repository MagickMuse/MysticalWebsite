This ceremony honors Brigid's three domains, healing, poetry, and craft, by having a circle of participants divide into three groups and create gifts for one another.

Begin by inviting Brigid's presence with a burst of flame, such as a candle lit with intention or a small ceremonial fire, calling her name aloud as the flame rises.

Once Brigid has been welcomed, divide the circle into three groups by field of work:

  • Healers: those who do any kind of healing, peacemaking, parenting, or other caregiving work.
  • Poets: those who work with words in any way, such as writing, editing, music, or teaching.
  • Crafters: those who create a product in their work, laborers, artists, and anyone who works with tools.

Each group then creates a small gift for the other two groups: healers might make simple herbal charms, poets might compose a short song or verse, and crafters might fashion small symbolic amulets from paper, ribbon, or other simple materials.

When the gifts are ready, each group offers its gifts to the others in turn. As each gift is received, the receiving groups thank the givers for their contribution to the circle and to the world, and invoke Brigid to bless their work in the days to come.

This ceremony works well at any gathering where people bring different skills together, a circle, a retreat, or a community celebration. It can also be adapted for smaller groups or done gathering by gathering over time, with new participants and new gifts. Beyond the ceremony itself, seeking out others who work alongside you in your own field carries Brigid's blessing too: spiritual circles, political affinity groups, storytelling gatherings, writers' groups, book clubs, art collaborations, and needlework circles all qualify. Claim your tribe; claim your clan.
