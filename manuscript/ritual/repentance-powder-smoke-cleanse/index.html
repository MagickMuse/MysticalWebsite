🔓Repentance Powder Smoke Cleanse

A fumigation variation of the Repentance Powder formula. Rather than sprinkling the powder on sheets, the blend is cast over burning frankincense and the smoke is used to cleanse the body. Prepare the powder from Repentance Powder Dreambed Cleanse first.

⚠️ Repentance Powder Smoke Cleanse is a Hoodoo working. Hoodoo is a closed practice meant only for those within the originating culture.

Quantities

  • Repentance Powder blend (ashes of Psalm 23, hyssop, lavender, sea salt), see Repentance Powder: Psalm 23 Sheets
  • Burning frankincense (sufficient to produce aromatic smoke)

Directions

  1. Prepare the Repentance Powder blend as directed in Repentance Powder: Psalm 23 Sheets.
  2. Place burning frankincense on lit charcoal in a fireproof vessel.
  3. Sprinkle the mingled ashes, herbs, and salt over the burning frankincense.
  4. Cleanse the body with the aromatic smoke that rises.
