Samhain

A peaceful offering to the faery folk when the veil thins at Samhain. Offers whiskey, herbs, and milk to convey peaceable intentions and avoid harm from unpredictable faeries.

Quantities

  • Whiskey
  • Herbs
  • Milk

Directions

  1. Samhain has a long history of faery lore alongside its legends of love and death.
  2. Just as the veil thins between the living and dead, it also thins between the world of mortals and faeries.
  3. Some of these beings are mischievous, with no real intention of harm.
  4. Some really, really do not like mortals and will eat you if you take a wrong step.
  5. Faeries do not think like humans, nor do they share human morality.
  6. They can, however, communicate with us.
  7. Some modern Pagans attempt to propitiate faeries.
  8. Others prefer to avoid them.
  9. Since you can't know what type of faery you will get, the following incantation might protect you from damage and convey peaceable intentions.
  10. Drink this whiskey,
    eat these herbs,
    have this milk
    then go gently on your way.
    Good Folk, we have no quarrels here,
    keep well, far from fear.
