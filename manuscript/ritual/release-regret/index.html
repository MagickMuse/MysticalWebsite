Ritual Grimoire

🦎Release Regret

◇Form
Altar Working
◇Method
MeditationShadow Work
◇Category
Meditation
◇Quantities
Materials: Journal, calendar or timeline
◇Directions
1. Twenty years from now you will probably be more disappointed by the things you didn't do than by the things you did. 2. Looking back on your life, what have you left undone? 3. What regrets do you have for missed opportunities? 4. Whether it's a trip you didn't take, a job you turned down, or a friend you didn't make time for, understanding your regrets helps you to make different choices moving forward. 5. List your regrets. 6. Go back year by year. 7. Use the calendar so you can be precise. 8. Once you have a list, take it one step further: Sit with each regret and ask if you still have sadness or grief around this moment. 9. If you don't, if you feel complete, let it go. 10. But if there's still an emotional pull, take time to understand why. 11. Was it a missed opportunity for personal growth? 12. Was it a crossroads in your life where you feel like you chose the wrong road? 13. Understanding your regrets helps you understand your own choices and allows you to make different ones in the future.
◇Description
A reflective journaling practice inspired by Skink's willingness to risk his tail. By cataloging past regrets year by year, then examining which still carry emotional weight, you understand your choices and create space to make different ones moving forward.
