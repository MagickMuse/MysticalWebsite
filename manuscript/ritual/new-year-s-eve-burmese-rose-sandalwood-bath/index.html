🛁New Year's Eve Burmese Rose Sandalwood Bath

◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Bath
◇Setting
Indoor
◇Method
Herbal Magick
◇Category
Ritual
◇Quantities
Rose attar (a few drops) Rose hydrosol or rose water (1 cup) Sandalwood essential oil (a few drops) Sandalwood hydrosol (1 cup) Fresh flowers (optional)
◇Directions
1. Add a few drops of rose attar to a cup of rose hydrosol or rose water. 2. In a separate cup, combine a few drops of sandalwood essential oil with sandalwood hydrosol. 3. Pour both blends into a tub filled with warm water. 4. Float fresh flowers on the surface for additional enhancement. 5. Bathe on New Year's Eve as a ritual of renewal and cleansing in preparation for the year ahead.
◇Description
A traditional Burmese New Year's Eve renewal bath combining rose attar, rose hydrosol, sandalwood essential oil, and sandalwood hydrosol, with fresh flowers floated on the surface for extra enhancement.
