A three-nights-before-full-moon spell to conceive a child, using pink rose quartz and pin moving closer together over four nights until touching on full moon to become three.

Quantities

  • A pink rose quartz crystal, a pearl, or a pink glass bead
  • A long silver pin, long needle, or thin paper knife
  • A pink scarf

Directions

  1. On night 1, hold the crystal in the hand you do not write with and the pin in the other hand, saying as you move them close together, not touching: "So shall two become three, baby, we ask you to join our loving family." Leave them on an indoor window ledge in your bedroom and repeat on days 2 and 3.
  2. On the full moon night, touch the crystal with the sharp point of the pin and say the words again.
  3. If possible, make love.
  4. The next morning, wrap the crystal and pin in the scarf and keep in a bedroom drawer until conception is confirmed or for the next month.
  5. Perform the three nights before the full moon and the full moon itself.
