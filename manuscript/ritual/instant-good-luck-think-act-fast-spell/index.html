🎄Instant Good Luck Think Act Fast Spell

◇Form
Charm
◇Method
Chant/MantraManifestation
◇Category
Spell
◇Quantities
- A tree or bush outdoors or an ornamental potted one indoors - Ten shiny baubles (the Christmas kind) or glittery decorations
◇Directions
1. Hold the first bauble saying: "Lucky me that I can get from A to Z instantly and not a trip or slip, but slide and glide effortlessly to fortune." Tie the bauble to the tree, saying the words again, and repeat for the next nine baubles in the same way. 2. Spin each bauble on the tree in turn, saying for each: "Turn and spin, spin and turn, for I am in good luck mode, think fast, act faster, and so I win the day." Say all the spell words fast three times, spin around ten times clockwise, and use the momentum to think on your feet and quickly resolve the problem. 3. Perform Friday.
◇Description
A Friday spell for instant good luck if you need to think and act fast in changing situation, tying ten shiny baubles to tree then spinning them to win the day.
