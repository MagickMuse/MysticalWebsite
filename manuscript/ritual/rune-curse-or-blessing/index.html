Runes, magickal alphabets of Northern Europe dating to c. 8000 BCE, can be used to deliver either a blessing or a curse depending entirely on the practitioner's intent. A chosen rune is written on paper and maneuvered so the target unknowingly picks it up and accepts it, effectively accepting the curse or blessing.

Quantities

Slip of paper
Pen

Directions

  1. Choose the rune that best represents and transmits your desire.
  2. Write that rune onto a slip of paper.
  3. Maneuver it so that the target thinks they dropped it, or place it within other papers the target will pick up; the target must accept the rune willingly, if unknowingly.
  4. When the target takes the rune-paper and places it in their pocket, they have effectively accepted the rune, curse or blessing as the case may be.
