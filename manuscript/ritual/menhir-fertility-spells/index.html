💎Stones

Two fertility workings drawing on the earth power of French standing stones (menhirs). In the first, the practitioner sits directly on the stone to absorb its emanating fertility energy. In the second, making love among the menhirs on Midsummer's Eve is said to draw tremendous fertility power and Earth's blessing on the union.

Quantities

A menhir (standing stone)
Optional: loose skirt worn without undergarments (Spell 1 alternative)

Directions

  1. For Spell 1: Sit directly on a menhir to absorb the fertility power emanating from that point in the earth.
  2. Traditionally this was done naked, though a loose skirt worn without undergarments serves as a practical modern alternative that preserves direct contact with the stone's energy.
  3. For Spell 2: On Midsummer's Eve, make love among the menhirs.
  4. This is said to bring tremendous fertility power and invite Earth's blessing on the union.

The menhirs of France are standing stones believed to have been placed with tremendous effort over specific points in the earth where special power emanates from the ground. Unlike holed stones, which are passed through, menhir power is accessed by direct contact: sitting on the stone itself.

Spell 1: Sitting the Stone

Sit directly on the menhir to absorb the fertility energy radiating from that point in the earth. Traditionally performed naked. A loose skirt worn without undergarments provides a practical modern adaptation that preserves as much direct contact as possible.

Spell 2: Midsummer Revels Among the Stones

Midsummer's Eve revels were once held among the menhirs and sometimes still are. Making love among the stones on that night is said to bring tremendous fertility power and draw Earth's blessing on the union.
