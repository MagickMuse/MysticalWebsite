🏠Home Defence Against Magickal Attack

◇Tradition
Folk MagickCeremonial
◇Solitary or Group
Solitary
◇Form
Charged Object
◇Setting
Either
◇Method
ProtectionBanishing
◇Category
Spell
◇Difficulty
Beginner
◇Quantities
Paint or chalk Athame Doormat Horseshoes or horns Old knife or sword Bay leaf wreath Iron nails (3 per door) Hammer Witch bottle (a bottle packed with bent nails and pins) Very dilute nitric acid or vinegar Small saucers Fresh onions
◇Directions
1. Pick one or more of the protective measures below and apply them to every entrance of the home. 2. Draw or paint outward pointing pentagrams on each doorstep, charge each one with your athame, and hide it under the doormat. 3. Nail horseshoes or horns, ends pointing up, above each entrance. 4. Hang an old knife or sword on the front door and the back door, framed by a wreath of bay leaves. 5. Hammer three iron nails into each door in a triangle, two at the bottom and one at the top. 6. Bury a witch bottle under each doorstep. 7. If you expect the attacker to materialize in the home, set a small saucer of very dilute nitric acid or vinegar in every room at the expected time of attack. 8. As an alternative to step 7, cut fresh onions in half and place a newly cut half in every room. 9. Throw away all of the onions as soon as the danger has passed, and never eat them.
◇Description
A collection of traditional measures to protect the physical home against a magickal attacker who may visit in person. Iron is emphasized as particularly effective, as it appears to scramble magickal signals when deployed with intention.
