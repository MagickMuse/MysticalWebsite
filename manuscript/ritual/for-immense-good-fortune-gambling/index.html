🎲For Immense Good Fortune Gambling

A charm spell to bring luck in games of chance by embedding crystals in candle wax while chanting and spiraling.

Quantities

  • 1 large green candle of natural wax (or beeswax candle)
  • 3 green crystals (amazonite, jade, chrysoprase, or aventurine - mixed if desired)
  • 1 green bag or purse

Directions

  1. Light the candle.
  2. As the wax melts, push each crystal into the wax near the base of the candle, saying, "Much haste, no waste, no time, the win is mine. As I chance my arm, keep me calm to speculate and calculate the hand of fate." Clap as you move around the table clockwise, moving faster and faster, saying, "Much haste, more speed, win I shall, win indeed. Burn bright, success is in sight." Keep chanting and spiraling with a final clap over your head, saying, "Success will shine, the win is mine." Let the candle burn.
  3. Put the crystals and the attached wax into the bag.
  4. Take the bag when you enter a game of chance and put it on top of tickets or betting slips.
  5. Perform Saturday around noon.
