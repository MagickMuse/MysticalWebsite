🪵The Alraun Magistellus

◇Quantities
- 1 rowan tree (living, to be visited over a full lunar month) - Distilled water mixed with blood or milk at a ratio of approximately 12:1, for watering - Vervain for curing smoke - Athame - Thurible - Selected rowan branch in a reasonably thick section, for carving into a 5 to 12 inch female figure
◇Directions
1. Seek out your rowan tree. On the night of the waxing moon, draw a deosil circle around it with your Athame. Declare your intention to compose an alraun in Hertha's name. 2. Water the tree for a full lunar month with the same distilled water mixture used for the mandragore (approximately 12 parts distilled water to 1 part blood or milk). Each time you water, mentally select a reasonably thick branch for carving and declare your intent aloud. 3. At the end of the month, sever the selected branch with your Athame. 4. Carve the branch into a small female image, between 5 and 12 inches in length. Use a spoken charm throughout the carving, similar to the mandragore: Guard this home in Hertha's name, or similar words. 5. Cure the completed image by passing it through thurible smoke of burning vervain until thoroughly dried. 6. At the full moon, sleep with the alraun in your bed, keeping it that night, as the old books say delicately, as your wife. 7. Enshrine the alraun near the hearth to complete the operation and initiate its protective activity.
◇Description
A plant-based elemental magistellus carved from a rowan branch into a small female figure, serving the same protective purpose as the mandragore and traditionally used by warlocks. Rowan is considered among the most powerful countermagick woods in European lore; once carved and cured with vervain smoke, the alraun is enshrined near the hearth.
