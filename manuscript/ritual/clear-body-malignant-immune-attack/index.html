A spell to help clear the body of malignant condition or immune system attack, using six candles around mirror to fade pain with light then contain it in darkened glass.

Quantities

  • Six small pink or blue candles
  • A small round or oval mirror propped up so that you can see into it

Directions

  1. Arrange and light the candles in a semicircle around the mirror, sitting behind them facing the mirror so that your face is illuminated.
  2. Recite six times in a whisper: "Fade with the light from here. Pain be contained, absorbed, diminished within the glass, a pale reflection, soon to cease." Blow out all the candles in rapid succession, saying as you do so: "Pain fade, rest in the darkness, flow from the body into the darkened glass and let pain pass." Perform any time you (the sick person) are despairing.
