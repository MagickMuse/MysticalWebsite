🌍Healing the World Meditation

🔵BluePink⚪White🟡Yellow

A color-based meditation that channels healing energy through pink, yellow, and blue light, first filling yourself and then radiating that healing out to the world.

Quantities

None required

Directions

  1. This meditation works with three primary healing colors (pink, yellow, and blue) to create a flow of compassionate energy that extends from your own being outward to encompass the entire world.
  2. Each color carries specific healing qualities that work at different levels of body and spirit.
  3. Preparation:
    Relax in your usual way and enter into a meditative state. Take your time settling in. There is no rush.
  4. Stage One: White Light Protection
    Visualize yourself surrounded by a pure white light of protection. This light creates a sacred boundary for your practice and ensures that only positive energy flows through you.
  5. Stage Two: Rose Pink Healing
    Allow the white light to slowly transform into a beautiful rose pink. Feel and sense the delicate healing energies of this magnificent color.
  6. Absorbing Pink Energy: With each inhalation, you are absorbing rose pink energy that travels to every cell of your body.
  7. Feel it filling you completely, your organs, your bones, your blood, your skin.
  8. Let yourself become saturated with this gentle, loving vibration.
  9. Sending Pink Energy: When you feel yourself filled to overflowing with this color, send it out into the universe to comfort and heal others.
  10. Visualize it spreading out from you in waves of rose pink energy that ultimately encircle the entire world.
  11. See it touching everyone who needs comfort, compassion, and healing.
  12. Picture the rose pink energy for as long as you can.
  13. Stage Three: Pure Yellow Vitality
    Allow the pink to gradually transform into pure yellow. Feel the warmth, energy, and vitality of the yellow light. This is the color of the sun, of life force, of renewal.
  14. Absorbing Yellow Energy: Allow the yellow to penetrate and revitalize every cell of your body.
  15. Visualize it as a circle of energy inside your solar plexus, radiating outward from your center.
  16. Sending Yellow Energy: Feel a sense of love for the world and everything within it.
  17. When this feeling fills you completely, send that love out to everyone who needs it as waves of perfect yellow energy.
  18. See this vibrant light reaching across the planet, bringing warmth and vitality wherever it touches.
  19. Stage Four: Gorgeous Blue Purification
    Allow the yellow to gradually transform into blue until you are completely surrounded by swirling mists of the most gorgeous blue you can imagine.
  20. Absorbing Blue Energy: Feel the blue light purifying and healing you with every breath you take.
  21. It cleanses, soothes, and brings peace to your entire being.
  22. Sending Blue Energy: When you feel ready, send this perfect blue energy out into the world for everyone who needs it.
  23. Watch as waves of cool, peaceful blue light spread across the earth, bringing calm and healing wherever there is pain or distress.
  24. Stage Five: Return to White Light
    Allow the blue to slowly transform back into pure white light. Feel comfortable and at peace inside it. Give thanks to the universal spirit for all the blessings in your life, and for enabling you to spread healing energy to people who need it.
  25. Closing:
    When you feel ready, take a few deep breaths, stretch, and open your eyes. Return to the present moment with the knowledge that you have sent healing energy into the world.
