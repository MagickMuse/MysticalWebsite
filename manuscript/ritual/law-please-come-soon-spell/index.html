⚖️Law Please Come Soon Spell

A Hoodoo working to draw legal authority toward a specific target, used when someone close to you is engaged in dangerous activities that need official attention. Combines dirt from seven locations with a black candle dressed in Commanding and San Cipriano Oil.

⚠️ Closed Practice Note: This working is rooted in Hoodoo, an African American folk magick tradition. Approach with cultural respect and awareness of its origins.

Quantities

  • Dirt from: target's residence or business, a graveyard, a crossroads, a prison, a courthouse, 4 different police stations
  • 1 black seven-day candle (or 7 individual black candles)
  • Commanding Oil (for dressing)
  • San Cipriano Oil (for dressing)

Directions

  1. Collect a handful of dirt from: the target's home or place of business, a graveyard, a crossroads, a prison, a courthouse, and four different police stations.
  2. If using commercial Graveyard Dust that doesn't contain actual dirt, also collect dirt from a cemetery.
  3. Blend all dirt in a bowl.
  4. Carve and dress a black seven-day candle (or seven individual black candles) with Commanding Oil and San Cipriano Oil.
  5. Stand the candle in the bowl of blended dirt and burn it.
  6. On the eighth day, once the candles have burned completely, sprinkle the wax remnants and the dirt onto the targeted individual's property.
⚠️
Closed Practice Notice: This spell draws on Hoodoo candle and dirt magick traditions. Hoodoo is a closed African American folk magick tradition. Please research and respect the cultural context.
📌
Intended Use: This is not a court case spell for your own legal situation. It is used when someone else's dangerous activities need to be brought to the attention of legal authorities — when calling the police directly is not simple or safe.

Some legal situations call for the law's presence, not its absence. This working is designed for circumstances where you need to draw official attention toward a specific individual or location, without drawing it toward yourself in the process.

  1. Collect a handful of dirt from each of the following locations:
    • The target's home or place of business
    • A graveyard
    • A crossroads
    • A prison
    • A courthouse
    • Four different police stations
    • If your Graveyard Dust does not contain actual dirt, also collect a handful of dirt from a cemetery.
  2. Blend all of the collected dirt together thoroughly in a bowl.
  3. Use a black seven-day candle, or burn seven individual black candles on consecutive days.
  4. Carve the candle with your intentions and dress it with both Commanding Oil and San Cipriano Oil.
  5. Stand the dressed candle within the bowl of blended dirt and burn it.
  6. On the eighth day, once the candle(s) have burned completely, gather the wax remnants together with the dirt and sprinkle everything onto the targeted individual's property.
