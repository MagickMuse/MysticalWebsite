🕯️Navigating Difficult Changes

A candle spell using black and blue candles to release negative emotions around difficult life changes and embrace positive forward movement. Black candle burns away stress, confusion, and hurt while blue candle absorbs and disperses positive intentions. Can be repeated as needed for ongoing support.

Quantities

  • Black candle (place on left)
  • Blue candle (place on right)
  • Matches or lighter
  • Safe candle burning location
  • Preferably outdoors if possible

Directions

  1. You will need two candles for this spell, one black and one blue, and something to light them with.
  2. You will also need a place where you can be alone, cast a circle, and be at about eye level with the flame of your candles.
  3. This should be a safe location to burn candles, where you won't be in danger of falling asleep.
  4. If at all possible, you should perform this spell outside.
  5. Set the two candles next to each other, black on the left and blue on the right.
  6. Cast your circle as you normally would and then either sit or stand in front of the two candles.
  7. Take a moment to take several deep breaths and to empty your mind of all other thoughts, particularly if you are already stressed about the change you are here to work on.
  8. Relax as much as you can while still focusing on what the problem is, but feel yourself getting distance from it.
  9. Light the black candle.
  10. As you do so, say either aloud or to yourself:
  11. The confusion, hurt, and stress I feel
    burn away inside this flame.
    The change I face is made for me;
    no other can take my pain.
  12. Spend some time staring into the flame of the candle.
  13. Project your hurt, sadness, confusion, stress, anger, and any negative emotions you have regarding this change into the flame of that candle.
  14. Imagine those feelings burning up in the flame and turning into nothing but smoke that disappears into the air.
  15. Spend as much time as you need doing this.
  16. When you think you are done, take a few moments to just relax.
  17. See if any more of those feelings come to the surface, and if they do, go back to projecting them back into the candle.
  18. Give yourself a couple of breaks and do a "stress check" each time you do.
  19. Once you can tell you really do feel a bit better, go ahead and move on to the next part of the spell.
  20. Light the blue candle and say:
  21. Moving forward, moving on,
    changes must be made.
    The past is gone, it slips away
    from my mind to fade.
    The future is what matters now.
    I need my strength so bold,
    to make the changes I must make
    and let them take ahold.
  22. Now stare into the flame of the blue candle.
  23. Imagine positive reactions and results from whatever these changes are.
  24. Keep anything negative out.
  25. If a negative thought creeps in, turn back to the black candle and send it into that flame instead.
  26. Keep all of your positive thoughts going into the flame of the blue candle, where they are absorbed by the flame and then dispersed into the universe.
  27. Keep the negative thoughts going into the black candle, where they are burned up and destroyed.
  28. Go back and forth between both candles as much as you feel necessary.
  29. When all the negative emotions are gone and you feel ready to take on this new challenge, move back to the black candle and say:
  30. Negative feelings, I don't need you.
    I cleanse you and release you into the air.
  31. Blow the black candle out, allowing the feelings to just float off and away.
  32. Next move back to the blue candle and say:
  33. These changes I make are for the best.
    I accept you and release you into my surroundings.
  34. Blow the blue candle out gently, allowing the positive energy to stay in the room with you and permeate the air.
  35. You may need to perform this spell several times over to fully rid yourself of the negative feelings associated with the change you have to make.
  36. Small changes won't take as long, but harder, more difficult changes may need you to perform this spell nightly for a couple of weeks until you feel you have things more under control.
  37. Then you can cut down to once a week, once every other week, finally to once a month, and then only if you feel those feeling trying to creep back in again.
  38. If you have more than one change going on at a time, you can work on those at the same time, just be careful not to overwhelm yourself, particularly in the beginning.
  39. If it starts to feel like it's too much to deal with at once, then break those changes down into separate sessions until you are strong enough to tackle them all at once.
