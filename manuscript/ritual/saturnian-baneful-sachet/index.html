A Saturnian hex sachet for workings of wrath, binding, and chastisement. Bound with black or dark ribbon during the waning moon, in the day and hour of Saturn. Poisonous ingredient alternatives are listed for historical reference only.

Quantities

  • Safe/non-poisonous ingredients:
  • Graveyard dust (churchyard earth, dried powdered mullein, or bone ash)
  • Pulverized myrrh wood
  • Black poppy seeds
  • Elderberry leaves, flowers, or berries (Holda berries)
  • Rue
  • Bitter aloes
  • Sourgrass / sorrel
  • Hawthorn blossoms
  • Periwinkle blossoms
  • Juniper wood and berries
  • Yew wood and berries (external use only; yew berries are toxic if ingested)
    Historical/poisonous alternatives (reference only, do not use):
  • Bittersweet / woody nightshade (Solanum dulcamara)
  • Deadly nightshade / belladonna (Atropa belladonna)
  • Enchanter's nightshade (Circaea lutetiana)
  • Henbane (Hyoscyamus niger)
  • Jimson weed / Datura (Datura stramonium)
  • Hemlock (Conium maculatum)

Directions

Combine chosen safe ingredients. Bind the sachet closed with black or dark ribbon. Prepare during the waning moon, in the day and hour of Saturn (approximately 8 AM, 3 PM, or 10 PM on a Saturday).

Note on graveyard dust: This term refers to one of three things: earth or dust taken from a churchyard; dried, powdered mullein herb (Verbascum thapsus); or, historically, bone ash and asphaltum (mummy dust).
