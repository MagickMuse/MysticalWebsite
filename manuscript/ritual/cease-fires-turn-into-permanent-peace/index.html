Imbolc

A when you hear of cease-fire in troubled area spell so cease-fires and truces may turn into permanent peace, tying nine yellow ribbons around paper then cutting free after week for conflict to flow away and harmony to stay.

Quantities

  • A piece of paper and a blue pen
  • Nine long yellow ribbons

Directions

  1. Write on the paper: MAY THERE BE LASTING PEACE IN [name a specific place or say everywhere there is conflict].
  2. Tie the first ribbon in a loose knot around the paper, so there is an end trailing from either side of the paper, and say: "May each day cease-fires grow, and increased understanding show. So peace remains permanently, and lasting settlement there can be." Knot two more ribbons and join one at each side of the first ribbon, repeating the spell words.
  3. Continue until you have knotted four ribbons to each side.
  4. Tie the two long loose ends of the two sides around and around the paper and secure them with a knot, saying: "Be bound in peace as truces grow and conflict flows away. Cease-fires increase to lasting peace, and harmony will stay." After a week, cut all the knots and ties free, and dispose of them and the paper, saying: "So is conflict put away." Perform when you hear of a cease-fire in a troubled area.
