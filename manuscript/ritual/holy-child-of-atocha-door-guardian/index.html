A figure of the Holy Child of Atocha, with whom Elegba was syncretized, kept behind the door and fed on Mondays. Beneath the sweet face of a richly dressed child on a throne hides the trickster Elegba.

⚠️ This working comes from Lucumi (Santeria) and related African diasporic traditions, which are closed practices. Approach with respect and consider working with an initiated practitioner.

Quantities

Figure of the Holy Child of Atocha
Rum (every Monday)
Candy and small toys

Directions

  1. Place the figure of the Holy Child of Atocha behind the door.
  2. Feed him every Monday. Rum is still appropriate.
  3. Rather than tobacco, give this watchful little boy extra candy and small toys.

When enslaved Africans were brought to the Western hemisphere, their own spiritual traditions were brutally suppressed. To survive, African spirits took on the masks of acceptable Roman Catholic saints, and Elegba became identified with the Holy Child of Atocha. See also Elegba Head Behind the Door.
