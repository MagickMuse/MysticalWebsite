A Thai ritual in which an older couple prepares the bridal bedroom during the ceremony to bless the newlyweds with happiness and fertility.

Quantities

Bags of rice
Sesame seeds
Coins
Tomcat (not neutered)

Directions

  1. An older couple (whose spouse and children are still living) spiritually cleanses the bedroom.
  2. Protective talismans are set up and left to radiate good fortune.
  3. Talismans include: bags of rice, sesame seeds, coins, and a tomcat (not neutered).
  4. The cat need not be a permanent gift, it may be a pet that simply spends time in the room, emanating fertility energy.
Note: The cat must not be neutered, as this defeats the purpose of the spell. This is a good opportunity to foster a shelter cat prior to neutering.
