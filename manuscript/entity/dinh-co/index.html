Magickal Properties

Dinh Cô began her incarnation as a fishing goddess of the southern Vietnamese coast who preferred death to returning to her parental home. She drowned at sea but emerged as a powerful goddess. She protects people at sea and became the spiritual guardian of the mass exodus of Vietnamese known as the "boat people." She is credited with protecting and sponsoring those who reached land, enabling them to start anew and find success.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Dinh Cô for protection during sea travel and for success in starting over in new locations. Since the late 1990s, she has emerged as the matron goddess of women who work in Ho Chi Minh City's markets. Petition her for business success and entrepreneurial ventures.

Offerings

Lavish gifts in gratitude, often in fulfillment of vows. These offerings testify to her prowess and attract new devotees. Former refugees returned to her shrine to offer lavish gifts in gratitude.

Favored People

Dinh Cô is the matron of female urban entrepreneurs, boat people, market workers, and those seeking to start anew in foreign lands.

Petition For

Safety at sea, business success, protection during travel and relocation, assistance in starting new ventures, success in markets and trade.

Warnings

She has a volatile nature similar to her sister goddess Ba Chua Xu. It is crucial to fulfill all vows made to Dinh Cô, as breaking promises will incur her wrath.

Additional Notes

Her popularity has soared since the late 1980s as former refugees returned to offer thanks. Hundreds of thousands visit her beachside shrine in Long Hai, east of Ho Chi Minh City, annually. Her annual festival is held on the twelfth day of the second month of the lunar calendar.
