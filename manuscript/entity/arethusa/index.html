Magickal Properties

Guardian spring‑deity of Ortygia whose waters merge with the river Alpheios; heals, protects, and prospers people and land.

Ritual Uses

Petition for healing, protection, prosperity, and safe travel by water; ask for clear sight into underworld matters from the spring's depths.

Offerings

Fresh water libations, simple flowers, coins to wells or springs, and dolphin imagery.

Favored People

Islanders, spring‑keepers, clean‑water advocates, and those seeking protection.

Petition For

Healing, protection, prosperity, and safe water travel.

Warnings

Respect sacred fish; never pollute springs; avoid molesting or taking fish from sacred waters.

Additional Notes

Linked with Alpheios and Syracuse; dolphins are emblematic.
