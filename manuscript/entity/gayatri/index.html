Gayatri is a Hindu goddess of the Vedic and Puranic traditions, honored as the second consort of the creator god Brahma alongside his primary consort Sarasvati. She is remembered in the tradition as a milkmaid whom Brahma took as wife, and she is also identified with the personified form of the Gayatri mantra, one of the most sacred verses of the Rig Veda.

Domains

The embodiment of sacred speech in its most concentrated form, the personified Gayatri verse of the Rig Veda, the second consort of Brahma, and the pastoral origin of one of the great mother-figures of the tradition.

Iconography

Gayatri is depicted seated on a lotus, often shown with five heads and ten arms, holding the sacred instruments of Vedic ritual. In her aspect as the personified mantra she is understood as the mother of the Vedas and the ground of Brahmanical initiation, and her mantra is chanted at dawn and dusk by initiated practitioners as a foundation of daily devotion.
