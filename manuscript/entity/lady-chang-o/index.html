Magickal Properties

Lady Chang'O is the beautiful Moon Lady who inhabits the lunar palace made of cinnamon wood, accompanied by a three-legged toad and the moon rabbit who pounds the elixir of immortality. She gained her immortality by secretly consuming the Elixir of Immortality intended for her husband Yi the Archer, which caused her to float up to the moon. She was originally an attendant of Hsi Wang Mu, Queen Mother of the West, before her marriage. Chang'O grants secret wishes during the Moon Festival and provides oracles through moonbeam divination.

Ritual Uses

Petition Chang'O during the Moon Festival by gazing at the full moon and articulating secret wishes that must remain unheard by others. Create outdoor altars in moonlight featuring moon cakes and fruit offerings. Light incense at her altar and whisper questions, then listen silently for her oracular responses from passersby. Work divination by dipping bay laurel leaves into spring water under moonlight. Honor the power of Yin and divine feminine lunar energy through ritual celebration.

Lore

Chang'O lives in exile on the moon in a cinnamon wood palace with her sole companions: a three-legged toad and the moon rabbit who pounds the elixir of immortality. China's lunar exploration program is named in her honor. She appears in Amy Tan's children's book "The Moon Lady." Her Moon Festival occurs on the 15th day of the 8th Chinese lunar month, celebrating the power of Yin and divine feminine force.

Warnings

Wishes must be truly secret, telling anyone what you wished for breaks the spell. Don't approach casually, she expects genuine reverence for lunar power. She grants secret desires, not just any wishes.

Additional Notes

Celebrated during annual Moon Festival (Autumn Harvest Festival). Altars placed outside in moonlight with 13 moon cakes (one per lunar month) and five fruits. China's lunar exploration program named in her honor. Invariably described as very beautiful.
