⚠️ Anaisa Pye is venerated within Dominican Vodou (21 Divisions) and Haitian Vodou traditions. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or under proper cultural guidance.

Magickal Properties

Joyous, flirtatious lwa of love and sensuality, generous in favors but demands constancy and exclusivity. Closely associated with perfume, fragrance, and glamorous presentation.

Ritual Uses

Petition for romance, attraction, sensuality, healing of female reproductive issues, and domestic or marital matters.

Offerings

Champagne or beer served elegantly, sweets, delicate fruits, marzipan fruits, chocolate-dipped fruits, cigarettes, jewelry, perfume, scented soaps and cosmetics. Keep her altar beautiful with many flowers.

Favored People

Devotees willing to provide constant attention and upkeep, lovers seeking beauty and desirability, those working domestic and marital concerns.

Petition For

Love, attraction, heightened beauty and desirability, romantic success, sensual confidence, assistance with domestic and marital issues.

Warnings

A jealous, possessive Metresa who expects total, continual devotion. Do not start with her unless ready for an ongoing relationship. Avoid crowding her near female spirits, rivalry with Metresili is noted. Communicate via perfume, she signals presence by scent.
