Magickal Properties

  • North American spirit guide, warrior for justice who intercedes in all legal matters especially prison release
  • Primary spirit guide of Mother Leafy Anderson, founder of Spiritual Churches of New Orleans
  • "Saint for the south" while White Hawk served the north
  • Identity debated, usually associated with Sauk and Fox war leader (1767-1838) who led 1832 uprising

Ritual Uses

  • Keep his image positioned near front door looking out to serve as sentry and watchman
  • Conduct rituals at night illuminated by red candles, not electricity
  • Invoke for legal difficulties and court cases
  • Place in vulnerable areas for protection and surveillance

Offerings

Red candles, quiet vigil, heartfelt petitions for justice

Favored People

Those with legal difficulties, prisoners, the wrongly accused, those seeking justice

Petition For

Legal matters, release from prison, justice, protection of home and family, overcoming legal obstacles

Warnings

Keep requests just and honorable. Follow through on vows made to him. Maintain respectful decorum.

Additional Notes

  • Historic figure: Sauk and Fox leader who died after Bad Axe Massacre, skeleton once displayed in Iowa governor's office
  • Iconography: Mother Anderson wore mantle bearing his image
  • Attributes: Tomahawk (cuts through obstacles), spear/lance (reaches distant goals)
  • Color: Red
  • Alternative theory: May be Oglala Lakota chief who performed with Buffalo Bill's Wild West Show
