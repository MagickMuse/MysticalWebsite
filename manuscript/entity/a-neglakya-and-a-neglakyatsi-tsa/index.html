The Siblings of the Underworld

A'neglakya and A'neglakyatsi'tsa are a brother and sister who once lived deep in the Underworld in Zuni cosmology. They often climbed up into the outer world to roam, each wearing a garland of Datura flowers around their heads, watching and listening to everything around them. Each time they returned home they told their mother all that they had seen and heard.

The Encounter With the Divine Ones

One day in the outer world they met the Divine Ones, who asked how they were. The siblings answered that they were happy, for the knowledge they carried let them see ghosts, put people to sleep, and spot thieves. The Divine Ones decided the brother and sister knew too much and banished them back to the Underworld. Datura flowers grew up in the place where they had stood, and the Divine Ones named the flowers a'ntglakyn after the boy.

The Four Cardinal Points

The flowers of A'neglakya grow in white but are also tinged with yellow, blue, and red, the four colors of the cardinal points. To work with datura within the Zuni tradition is to remember the siblings whose gifts of sight, sleep, and revelation pass through every bloom.
