Magickal Properties

Immortal, oracular, miraculous stallion born of Demeter and Poseidon; a speaking horse who grants information and guidance.

Ritual Uses

Petition for oracles, swift rescue, and sure‑footed travel. Ask for answers in dreams or through inspired speech and song.

Offerings

Clean water, oats or apples left at horse shrines, songs in his honor.

Favored People

Horse‑folk, diviners, travelers, and those seeking clear answers.

Petition For

Reliable messages, safe journeys, and speed.

Warnings

Do not force him into service; honor his sovereignty. Never treat Arion as a mere mount or attempt to force him into service.

Additional Notes

Child of sea and grain deities; oracular speech is his hallmark.
