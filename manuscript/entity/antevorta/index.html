Magickal Properties

Antevorta embodies the prophetic power of future sight and the divine wisdom that comes from understanding what lies ahead. As the spirit of the future who travels in Carmenta's entourage alongside her sister Postvorta (spirit of the past), she carries the archetypal energy of foresight, preparation, and the ability to navigate challenges by seeing them before they arrive.

Ritual Uses

Future Divination: Invoke her energy for prophetic dreams, accurate divination readings, and clear vision of upcoming opportunities or challenges.

Childbirth Assistance: Call upon her when babies are not in correct position for birth, for protection during pregnancy and labor.

Strategic Planning: Use her energy when making long-term decisions that require understanding of future consequences.

Weather Prediction: Petition her for advance warning of storms, seasonal changes, or other meteorological events.

Offerings

Moon-shaped items, water offerings, paired items with Postvorta when working with both sisters, and offerings made on the 11th and 15th of January.

Favored People

Diviners and fortune-tellers, meteorologists and weather forecasters, midwives and birthing professionals, and pregnant women seeking safe delivery.

Petition For

Clear visions of the future, successful childbirth with babies in proper position, healing of reproductive disorders, accurate weather predictions, and wisdom in long-term planning.

Warnings

Do not attempt to lock someone's fate in place when she is invoked; she governs the unfolding future and punishes manipulative attempts to rewrite destiny.

Additional Notes

Her name meaning "before change" emphasizes her role in helping people prepare for upcoming transitions. Her iconography with Postvorta resembles Janus, with each sister facing a different direction.
