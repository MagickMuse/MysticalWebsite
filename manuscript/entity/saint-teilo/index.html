Saint Teilo is renowned for healing nervous disorders and mental illness.

Roman Catholic and Anglican saint, among the leaders of the Celtic Christian Church in sixth-century Wales.

Life and Legend

Saint Teilo (c. 480-560 CE) was the grandson of King Ceredig of Ceredigion, for whom the Welsh county of Cardiganshire is named. He is one of the Welsh saints associated with King Arthur.

Teilo was a close companion of Saint David. Together they made pilgrimages to Jerusalem and Rome. Teilo founded a monastic community in Wales, which he eventually transferred to Brittany after a devastating plague struck the region in 549. While in Brittany, Teilo reputedly subdued and tamed a winged dragon.

Domains

Long a comparatively obscure saint outside Wales, Teilo is genuinely associated with healing, particularly of nervous disorders and mental illness. Historically, institutions dedicated to healing have been named in his honor. He is also patron saint of horses and fruit trees.

Iconography

Teilo is portrayed wearing bishop's robes and riding a stag.

Sacred Site

Llandaff Cathedral in Cardiff, which Teilo may have founded and where his body is allegedly contained. Many other churches also claim to possess his corpse.

Feast Day

9 February.
