Magickal Properties

Alpheios, son of Oceanus and Tethys, is Lord of the Alpheios River, the longest river in the Peloponnese. As befitting the spirit of such an impressive river, Alpheios was once an important, significant deity. He was renowned as an amorous, virile, sexually aggressive spirit, pursuing Artemis and other spirits, particularly the Nymph Arethusa.

Ritual Uses

Work with Alpheios for virility and fertility rituals, particularly for men seeking to father large families. The Alpheios comes second in Hesiod's catalog of great rivers, just after the Nile, demonstrating his importance in Greek spiritual traditions.

Offerings

Sacrifices of bulls, as befitting such an impressive river spirit. Traditional offerings acknowledging his power over the river and fertility, presented near rivers or flowing water.

Favored People

Those seeking virility, male fertility, and large families. People who need assistance with protecting and feeding their children, and those seeking abundant resources for households.

Petition For

Virility, male fertility, lots of children, and assistance with protecting and feeding families. Help with ensuring abundant resources for large households.

Warnings

Son of Oceanus and Tethys, Lord of Alpheios River. Renowned as amorous, sexually aggressive spirit. His energy can be overwhelming and should be approached with respect for his powerful nature.

Additional Notes

His image was carved on the west pediment of the temple of Zeus at Olympia, demonstrating his former importance in Greek religion. Alpheios was renowned for his persistent pursuit of various spirits, demonstrating the aggressive, life-giving force of flowing water. His association with major religious sites shows his integration into the classical Greek pantheon.
