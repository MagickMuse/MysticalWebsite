Magickal Properties

Elvis Presley (January 8, 1935 to August 16, 1977), the amazingly popular American singer known simply as Elvis or the King, has evolved from beloved entertainer into an unofficial saint in the years since his death. Some fans have become devotees. Home shrines dedicated to him range from small to massive. Visits to his grave at Graceland have taken on the aura of pilgrimage. Offerings are left for him. Elvis himself was a man with deep spiritual interests.

Ritual Uses

In life, Elvis was perceived as caring about people, including strangers. Stories circulated about extravagant gifts including Cadillacs and charitable donations. A generous, caring, empathic nature is often considered a prerequisite of sainthood. In the truest sign of a saint, Elvis has begun to be credited as a healer. Healing has occurred in response to his image as well as his voice. Elvis is frequently compared to an angel and depicted as one, with many intending this quite literally. He appears in Mexican Day of the Dead altars and art, often labeled simply El Rey (the King) with no name needed. He is frequently depicted as a winged angel with pompadour and cigarette.

Offerings

Flowers and teddy bears are particularly popular. If you would like to cook for him, several cookbooks feature Elvis' favorite recipes.

Favored People

Those with deep spiritual interests, people experiencing illness or hardship, fans who have evolved into devotees

Petition For

Healing, serving as mediator or intercessor with God, comfort during difficult times, inspiration

Warnings

Some consider comparisons to angels humorous or poignant while others intend them quite literally. The concept of a beloved deceased entertainer evolving into a folk saint may be unusual in the United States but not elsewhere.

Additional Notes

Frequent post-death sightings imply that he has somehow cheated, escaped, defeated, or transcended death. Pilgrimage sites include Graceland (especially the Meditation Gardens where Elvis, his parents, and paternal grandmother are buried), Sun Studios in Memphis, and sites associated with his childhood in Tupelo, Mississippi. There is an annual Elvis Week every August in Memphis. Elvis is feted at his birthday (January 8) and memorialized on his death day (August 16).
