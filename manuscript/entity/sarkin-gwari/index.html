Head of the Tenth House

Sarkin Gwari is the head of the tenth house of Bori Spirits, the Gwari Spirits. The Gwari are a Pagan Nigerian people. His sister Bagwariya is married to the important Bori spirit, Malam Alhaji. Gwari spirits reject peppers. When making food offerings, make sure not to include peppers.
