Overview

Manannan Mac Lir, son of the ancient sea deity Lir, is a powerful sea spirit and trickster god. His name means "son of Lir." Ocean waves are his horses, and the sea is his cloak. He is a master of true magick, illusion, and sleight of hand, and controls the weather. Manannan presides over Mag Mell ("Plain of Honey"), an Otherworld realm that is a joyous paradise populated by dead souls and members of the Tuatha Dé Danaan. He serves as a psychopomp, ferrying souls to his realm.

Ancient Status

Although Manannan is classified among the Tuatha Dé Danaan, it is widely acknowledged that he is older than that family of spirits. He may be among the primordial spirits of Earth.

Magickal Treasures

Manannan possesses a magickal crane-bag filled with treasures:

  • A boat that requires no sails or oars but obeys the thoughts of its captain
  • A horse that rides equally well on land or water
  • A magic sword named Fragarach ("The Answerer") that penetrates any armor
  • Pigs that are slaughtered and consumed but are reborn fully grown the next day, providing endless food

Manifestation

His cloak of mist enables him to be invisible when he chooses. He wears a flaming helmet. Often envisioned as an older man with long, flowing white hair and beard, he is a master shape-shifter and full of surprises.

Attributes

  • Sword: Fragarach ("The Answerer")
  • Cloak: Mist cloak (grants invisibility)
  • Helmet: Flaming helmet
  • Crane-bag: Contains magickal treasures
  • Powers: Weather control, shapeshifting, illusion, true magick
  • Emblem: Some scholars believe the Triskelion (the three-legged wheel which is the emblem of the Isle of Man) represents Manannan
  • Element: Water

Sacred Sites

  • Isle of Man: Named in his honor; he was its first king and still serves as the island's spirit guardian
  • Mag Mell: The Otherworld realm beneath the waves that he rules

Sacred Day

Midsummer's Eve or the summer solstice.

Altar

Decorate his altar with marine imagery.

Offerings

Flowers, bundles of reeds, and/or alcoholic beverages including Okell's Mac Lir wheat beer (which features his image on the label).

Role as Psychopomp

Manannan ferries souls to Mag Mell, serving as a guide between the world of the living and the realm of the dead.
