Magickal Properties

Anjani is the mother of Hanuman, the great monkey god who embodies devotion, strength, and service. As the celestial apsara who took earthly form, she carries the archetypal energy of the divine mother whose child is destined for greatness. Her story demonstrates the principle that spiritual power often comes through maternal lineage.

Ritual Uses

Work with Anjani for maternal protection, intercession with Hanuman, and understanding the sacred relationship between mother and divine child. She can be petitioned for blessings on children and for accessing Hanuman's protection through maternal channels.

Offerings

Modest, maternal offerings that honor her role as Hanuman's mother. Traditional offerings appropriate to Hindu maternal deities.

Favored People

Mothers seeking protection for their children, devotees of Hanuman seeking maternal intercession, and those honoring the divine feminine in its maternal aspect.

Petition For

Protection for children, intercession with Hanuman, gentle maternal aid, blessings on motherhood, and connection to divine lineages.

Warnings

Approach with maternal respect; avoid coercive bargains, steady devotion is preferred.

Additional Notes

Anjani's transformation from celestial apsara to earthly mother of Hanuman shows how divine power manifests through humble, devoted service and maternal love.
