Classification

Unofficial saints (Sicilian folk tradition).

Origins

The Decollati rest in the graveyard behind Palermo's Church of the Beheaded Ones. These executed criminals died without family or friends to claim their bodies. Before their executions, however, each renounced violence and begged forgiveness from God and their victims.

Why They're Venerated

The Gospel of Luke declares that Heaven rejoices more over one repentant sinner than ninety-nine who never strayed. Because these criminals brought such joy to Heaven through their final repentance, the faithful began venerating them as saints and intercessors. The Decollati seem eager to atone for their earthly crimes by performing good works from beyond the grave.

Powers and Domains

These former criminals now guard against crime, thieves, and wrongdoers. But they're versatile spirits: petition them for luck, love, health, work, reversals of fortune, or lottery numbers. Their souls reportedly dwell beneath a large stone inside the chapel, but they'll travel anywhere and have long been invoked at home altars throughout Sicily and beyond.

Traditional Love Spell

The Decollati aren't always gentle. This Sicilian spell asks them to harass a romantic target until they yield:

Souls of the beheaded,
Three hanged, three slain, three drowned,
All nine join forces,
Go to [name] my sweetheart,
Pester [name] with [desired torments],
Don't kill [name]
But make them come to me with love and desire.

Make an initial offering and promise a larger one (specify what) if the spell succeeds within your stated timeframe.

Communication

When invoked, the Beheaded Ones allegedly always respond, even if only with sounds. Listen carefully for words or noises.

Sacred Site

Chiesa dei Decollati once stood by the River Oreto, originally dedicated to the Madonna of the Bridge. Little remains except the side chapel most closely associated with the Decollati, though images of their miracles once decorated the sanctuary.

Petitioning

Shape your desires from wax and present these images to them.
