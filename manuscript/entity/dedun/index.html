Magickal Properties

Dedun ('Lord of Nubia') is the Lord of the Nubian Desert, spirit of incense, wealth, and trade who epitomizes the great wealth and treasure of ancient Nubia. He protects and sponsors traders in incense and traditional Nubian arts, representing the prosperity and cultural richness of the Nubian civilization.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Dedun for success in trade and business, especially those dealing in luxury goods, incense, or traditional crafts. Work with him to protect valuable goods during transport or storage. He can guide you to profitable trade opportunities and partnerships.

Offerings

Myrrh (among Nubia's greatest treasures) and other fine incenses, trade goods especially those from traditional Nubian commerce, gold and precious metals, items representing craftsmanship and artistic skill.

Favored People

Traders and merchants, incense dealers and aromatherapists, craftspeople and artisans, those working with traditional Nubian arts and culture, anyone seeking to build wealth through trade.

Petition For

Wealth and prosperity through trade, successful business ventures, protection of valuable goods and investments, skill in traditional crafts, recognition of artistic work, profitable partnerships.

Warnings

Approach with quality offerings that show understanding of true value and craftsmanship. Don't insult him with cheap imitations when seeking prosperity, he knows genuine worth.

Additional Notes

Usually depicted in human form but sometimes appears as a lion, emphasizing his royal power and strength. Associated with the wealth of ancient Nubia including gold, incense, and skilled craftsmanship. Represents the prosperity that comes from valuing quality and tradition.
