Kama is the Hindu god of love, desire, and attraction, the equivalent of Eros or Cupid. He shoots flower arrows that inspire passion and longing. Beautiful and eternally young, Kama rides a parrot and carries a bow made of sugarcane with a string of bees. His arrows are tipped with flowers corresponding to the five senses.

Kama was famously reduced to ashes by Shiva's third eye when he tried to interrupt Shiva's meditation, but was later resurrected by the prayers of his devoted wife Rati. This myth represents desire's power to disturb even the greatest ascetic, and its eventual restoration to the cosmos.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kama for attracting love, increasing passion in existing relationships, and celebrating the beauty of desire. His energy supports romantic pursuits, marriage, and the joyful expression of sensuality. Work with him during spring, especially during Holi festival, when his energy is strongest.

Warnings

Desire is a powerful force that can disturb even deep meditation. Invoke Kama's energy responsibly and with awareness of the consequences of awakened passion.
