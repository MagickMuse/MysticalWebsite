Magickal Properties

Brazilian witch spirit, deified Moorish sorceress who is font of mystic, esoteric, and botanical secrets. Speaks fluent Portuguese, Arabic, and Latin, but may know other languages. Matron of those who practice magickal arts with power to oversee spell processes.

Ritual Uses

Petition to oversee spell processes, seek magickal instruction and information, request botanical and esoteric secrets. Invoke for guidance in magickal practices and witchcraft traditions.

Offerings

Red witch candles burned in her honor, rum, cigarettes, magickal tools.

Favored People

Practitioners of magickal arts, witches, those seeking magickal instruction and esoteric knowledge.

Petition For

Magickal instruction and information, oversight of spell processes, botanical secrets, esoteric knowledge, guidance in witchcraft practices.

Warnings

Her actual name is secret, "Evora" refers to Portuguese city variously ruled by Romans, Moors, Christians, and Visigoths. May be survivor from Roman Temple of Diana era.

Additional Notes

"Bruxa Evora" means "Witch Evora" but should be "Witch of Evora." Portugal suffered mild witch hunts, but woman was burned as witch in Evora in 1626. Can manifest in any form she chooses but usually portrayed as stereotypical crone with broomstick. Has big black cat familiar named Luzbel. Sacred color: Red.
