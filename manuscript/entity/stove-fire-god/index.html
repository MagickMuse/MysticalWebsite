Overview

The Stove, or Fire God, is one of the two primary house spirits described by the author. Found in every kitchen, the stove is understood as a living spiritual entity providing access to one of the most primordial elements of life: hearth fire.

Our ancestors spent tremendous energy tending hearth fires. The stove delivers controlled fire in exchange for virtually no effort, freeing us to reclaim that energy for art, love, and rest.

Dominion

All fire and heat sources within the home are "ruled" by the Stove/Fire God:

  • Electric lights
  • Radiators
  • Pocket lighters
  • Any other heat or fire source

This mirrors the way planets are said to rule plants in traditional herbalism.

Relationship with the Water God

The Stove and Faucet are a dynamic duo — often found in close proximity to one another in the kitchen. Together they govern the two primordial elements (water and fire) that sustain life in the home, and together they are ascribed the powers of creation, procreation, and recreation.

How to Honor

Unlike most spirits, the Fire God does not take offerings — it is powerful enough not to need them. Instead, it is honored through:

  • Contemplation — reflecting on the ancestral significance of hearth fire
  • Prayer — spoken gratitude and acknowledgment
  • Song — singing in the kitchen
  • Keeping the area clean — clearing kitchen clutter from around the stove
