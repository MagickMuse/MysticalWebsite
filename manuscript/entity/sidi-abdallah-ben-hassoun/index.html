Sidi Abdallah ben Hassoun lived in Salé, Morocco, in the 16th century and is now considered that city's patron saint. He plays a role similar to that of Saint Christopher for Muslim travelers and is also the patron saint of boatmen.

Domains

Safe travel, protection of boatmen, patron of sailors and those who journey by water.

Feast Day

His moussem (feast, commemoration of death) takes place annually on the eve of the Mouloud, the festival honoring the birth of the Prophet. What was once a simple religious processional has evolved into Salé's Festival of Candles. Boatmen place elaborate flaming candelabra on his tomb. A processional featuring huge intricately carved wax lanterns mounted on giant poles travels to his shrine accompanied by the sounds of flutes and drums.

Sacred Site

His shrine is at the end of the rue de la Grande Mosquée in Salé, Morocco.
