Asklepios is renowned for healing all illnesses through dreams, herbs, and sacred springs. As the Greek god of medicine, he offers diagnosis and cures through dream incubation at his healing sanctuaries.

Magickal Properties

Asklepios (Aesculapius to Romans) is the Greek and Roman deity of healing and medicine, patron of physicians and protector of the ill. He represents the divine aspect of medical knowledge and the power to restore health through proper treatment and care. Master healer who cures via dreams, herbs, and sacred springs. Prototype of hospital sanctuaries; chthonic physician who mediates between body and soul.

Ritual Uses

Create healing rituals that honor both divine intervention and medical knowledge. Work with him to enhance medical treatments and support healing processes. His energy combines spiritual healing with practical medical care. Sleep in sacred space for incubatory dreams and remedies. Petition for diagnosis through dreams, herbal guidance, and post-illness recovery.

Offerings

Clay votives (especially anatomical models or dough body-part ex-votos representing parts needing healing), eggs (symbols of new life and renewal), laurel wreaths, clear water, simple pillows, miniature healing tools, incense offered with sincere healing intentions, and a rooster after recovery.

Favored People

Physicians, healers, the ill and their families, medical students, anyone working in healthcare or healing arts, sleep and hydrotherapy practitioners, dream diviners.

Petition For

Healing from illness, specific cures, successful treatments, protection during medical procedures, enhanced ability to help others heal, medical insight, restorative sleep, guidance to competent care.

Warnings

Approach Asklepios as a healer and physician, offering clear intentions, clean water, incense, or votive anatomical models. Never promise or claim miraculous cures you cannot back with care. Do not substitute ritual for medical treatment and always seek professional medical help when needed. Cures not hospice.

Additional Notes

His association with snakes connects him to the ancient symbol of healing and renewal through shedding skin. Snakes and dogs are sacred to him. Modern medical symbols like the Rod of Asclepius (staff with a single serpent) derive from his imagery, not to be confused with Hermes' caduceus. He teaches the importance of combining spiritual healing practices with practical medical knowledge and care. Son of Apollo, father of Hygieia and Panacea. His sanctuaries were ancient healing centers where patients would sleep to receive diagnostic dreams.

Encyclopedia Source Notes (Aesculapius)

  • Developed from the Greek deity Asklepios and introduced into Rome in 293 BC as a plague god
  • Source frames the caduceus as among his attributes; the existing notes correctly distinguish his actual emblem (the Rod of Asclepius) from the caduceus of Hermes

Greek Encyclopedia Source Notes (Asklepios)

  • Period of worship. Circa 800 BC or earlier through Christianization circa 400 AD.
  • Centers of cult. Epidauros; Kos; the Asklepeion in Pergamon. The Epidauros sanctuary became an influential place of pilgrimage by the sick and infirm in classical times.
  • Literary sources. Iliad of Homer; Catalogues of Hesiod.
  • Parentage and family. Son of Apollo and a mortal consort, Coronis. Reared by the centaur Charon. Father of two sons, Podaleirios and Machaon, who were also physicians, and of the goddess Hygieia (health).
  • Mortal aspect. Asklepios lived effectively as a mortal and died as such, yet was nonetheless regarded as a deity.
  • Symbolism. Symbolized by a rod with twin snakes coiled around it, and represented in his sanctuaries by a captive snake.
  • Death. Met his death at the hand of Zeus for presuming to bring a mortal being back from death.
  • Asklepiadai. Physicians on Kos formed into a guild called the Asklepiadai, the sons of Asklepios.

Role in Hellenic Household Worship

For household devotion, offerings to Asklepios include frankincense (manna) incense and bread. His emblem is the medical staff. The colour linked to Him is red. His sacred animals are the serpent, the dog, the goose, and the rooster.
