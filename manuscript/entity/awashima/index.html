Magickal Properties

Awashima is a Japanese kami who governs women’s reproductive health and healing. She bestows fertility, protects pregnant people, oversees childbirth, and remedies ailments of the female reproductive system, including sexually transmitted disease. She also receives and safely houses dolls that may carry soul or spirit.

Ritual Uses

Petition for conception, safe pregnancy and delivery, post‑partum healing, and cure of reproductive disorders. Annual rites include Girl’s Day observances and doll‑floating ceremonies.

Offerings

Dolls, sewing needles, hair combs and ornaments, and personal items. Women traditionally offer undergarments when requesting healing for venereal disease.

Favored People

Women and those seeking fertility, protection in pregnancy, or healing of reproductive ailments.

Petition For

Conception, safe childbirth, recovery from reproductive illness, and gentle protection of girls and mothers.

Warnings

Observe purity customs and approach with respect at shrines. Encounters associated with funerary or doll rites should be handled solemnly.

Additional Notes

Legends link Empress Jingu’s storm rescue and safe landfall to Awashima’s island shrine. Awashima is venerated alongside Sukunahikona‑no‑Mikoto and Empress Jingu. Festival: 3 March (Hina Matsuri).
