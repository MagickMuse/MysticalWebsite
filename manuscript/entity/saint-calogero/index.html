Saint Calogero is renowned for healing joint disorders, arthritis, and rheumatism, and for helping the lame and disabled walk again.

Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Almost nothing is known of Calogero before his arrival in Sciacca, Sicily. Legend holds he fled persecution from a distant land. A Black man, he is thought to have originated from Ethiopia. He lived as a hermit on the mountain now bearing his name, dying there in 486 CE.

The Thermal Caves

Mount Calogero looms over Sciacca, one of Europe's oldest spa resorts. The mountain contains thermal vents called "stoves" that have been used therapeutically since at least the fifth century BCE. According to Greek legend, Daedalus himself first channeled the hot vapors seeping through rock fissures into healing baths. The vapors treat allergies, arthritis, rheumatism, and skin and gynecological conditions.

These steaming vents were long considered sacred territory, home to pagan spirits. Calogero banished those spirits, claiming the site and using the cave vapors to heal the sick. The largest cave, la Grotta del Santo (the Saint's Grotto), honors him.

Powers and Domains

Calogero is beloved wherever Sicilians have settled. He heals all ailments but specializes in joint disorders, and is renowned for helping the lame and disabled walk again. He also guards the summer harvest.

The Bread Festival

His feast coincides with a Sicilian bread festival:

  • San Calogero bread is shaped into anatomical forms (arms, legs, etc.) like ex-votos, blessed on his feast day, and saved for times of need
  • During processions, devotees pelt his passing statue with bread rolls
  • Traditional feast bread is studded with fennel, sesame, and poppy seeds

Iconography

Often depicted as a white man, despite being commonly called "the black saint" with African ancestry central to his legend.

Attribute

Book.

Sacred Sites

The Church of San Calogero stands over his cave dwelling. His votive statue in Agrigento holds a silver basket containing his leg bone, surrounded by ex-votos proclaiming his miracles.

Feast Day

18 June

Festival

His fiesta in Agrigento runs from the first to the second Sunday in July, commemorating when he prevented a massive earthquake.
