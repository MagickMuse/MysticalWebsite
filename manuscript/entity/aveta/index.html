Magickal Properties

Aveta is a Celtic goddess of healing, abundance, and successful childbirth. She protects mothers, infants, and households, and promotes prosperity in the home and field.

Ritual Uses

Petition for fertility, healthy pregnancy, safe delivery, and recovery. Seek her aid for steady provision and the wellbeing of infants.

Offerings

Simple household foods and clean water. Votive images reflecting her attributes are appropriate when available.

Favored People

Mothers, midwives, and families seeking protection and prosperity.

Petition For

Fertility, uncomplicated childbirth, infant health, and household abundance.

Warnings

Approach at springs and domestic altars with quiet reverence.

Additional Notes

Her pilgrimage shrine stood at Trier by a healing spring; inscriptions and images show her with a swaddled child, a lapdog, and baskets of fruit, signaling care, healing, and plenty.

Encyclopedia Source Notes

In the Romano-Celtic Gallic tradition, Aveta is known mainly from clay figurines found at Toulon-sur-Allier, France. The models show the goddess with infants at the breast and indicate she is especially concerned with nursing mothers. The figure is often accompanied by a small lapdog.
