Magickal Properties

E Bukura e Dheut is Queen of Albania's Fairies, ruling over a host of other beautiful spirits. She embodies the epitome of beauty and happiness. Her name is sometimes used to describe an ideal woman. Before she was an Albanian Fairy, she was an Illyrian goddess of beauty and love.

The spirits under her command are volatile, capricious, and utterly unpredictable. When encountered they may be generous and kind or maliciously destructive. E Bukura e Dheut herself may be petitioned for help if any of her subordinates are misbehaving.

Ritual Uses

E Bukura e Dheut is invoked to manage and placate misbehaving fairy spirits. She can be called upon to bring order when Albanian fairies are causing trouble.

Offerings

Offerings to placate misbehaving subordinate spirits.

Help with misbehaving fairy spirits, beauty, happiness, love.

Warnings

The spirits under her command are extremely volatile, capricious, and utterly unpredictable. Their behavior can shift from generous and kind to maliciously destructive without warning.

Additional Notes

E Bukura e Dheut lives in a fairy-tale castle atop a mountain guarded by fabulous beasts and creatures. She may also possess an underworld realm. Her lover is Tomor, supreme deity of the Illyrians, an Indo-European people and ancestors of many Albanians.
