⚠️ Amaru is venerated within Aymara, Quechua, and Andean Indigenous traditions. These are closed practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

The Amarus (plural) are ancient Andean snake spirits, guardian deities of the Incas. Amaru is a Quechua word literally translated as "snake," but they may be dragons. Spirits of wisdom, the Amarus served as advisors to the Incas and their predecessors, continuing to protect the Incas even after Spanish conquest.

Ritual Uses

Work with Amarus for protection of sacred knowledge and ancestral wisdom. Legend has it that following the Spanish conquest, a party of Incas fled from Cuzco carrying sacred artifacts, building enchanted bridges as they went. These Incas ventured deep into the rain forest, building a secret hidden fortress-city called Paititi guarded by Amarus.

Offerings

Respect for Inca sacred sites and ancestral protections. Honor for traditional knowledge and indigenous wisdom, offerings that acknowledge their role as guardians of ancient ways.

Favored People

Those seeking wisdom and protection of ancient knowledge, indigenous peoples maintaining traditional ways, and those who respect ancestral wisdom and traditional practices.

Petition For

Wisdom, protection of sacred knowledge, guidance from ancestors, preservation of traditional ways of life, and protection of indigenous cultural heritage.

Warnings

Guardian deities of Incas. Legend says they continue protecting Incas in hidden fortress-city Paititi, guarded by Amarus and giant felines. Their protective power includes devastating natural phenomena.

Additional Notes

Someday, it is prophesied, the Incas will return, retracing their route back to Cuzco. Their return will be accompanied by devastating natural phenomena, such as earthquakes and hailstorms. The Amarus will roar, frightening away those who have usurped the Incas' power, and once again the Incas will rule. Enchantments were placed upon their bridges so that attempts to follow produce enchanted eternal sleep.
