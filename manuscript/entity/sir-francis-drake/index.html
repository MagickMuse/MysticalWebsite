Magical Properties

Sir Francis Drake was a navigator, pirate, politician, and reputedly a wizard who was the first Englishman to circumnavigate the globe. According to legend, Drake had a magic mirror that enabled him to see ships all over Earth. Another legend suggests he was among the coven of Devon witches who cast spells and raised storms that sank the Spanish Armada. On his deathbed, he ordered his drum sent back to Buckland Abbey with instructions that if England was ever endangered, someone should beat it to summon him.

Ritual Uses

Drake's drum should be beaten when England is threatened or in danger. The sound will summon him to appear and lead the country to victory. His spirit leads the Wild Hunt accompanied by his Wish Hounds, driving an old-fashioned carriage-hearse pulled by headless horses.

Offerings

The beating of his drum serves as both offering and summoning. No other offerings are traditionally recorded.

Favored People

English people, sailors, those defending England from invasion or threat, naval officers and maritime defenders.

Petition For

Protection of England from foreign threats, victory in naval battles, safe sea travel, leadership during national crises, defense against invasion.

Warnings

Drake leads the Wild Hunt with headless horses and possibly headless Wish Hounds. His appearance signals serious national danger.

Additional Notes

His drum allegedly beats spontaneously whenever England is threatened - it was heard at the start of both World Wars. He is described as driving an old-fashioned carriage-hearse pulled by headless horses, accompanied by barking Wish Hounds that may also be headless. His ship was named the Golden Hind.
