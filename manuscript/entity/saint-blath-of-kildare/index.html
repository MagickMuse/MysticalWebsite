Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Saint Blath served as cook at Saint Brigid's famous convent in Kildare. While Brigid may have miraculously multiplied the ingredients, Blath transformed them into actual meals. According to tradition, her bread and bacon surpassed the finest feasts served anywhere else.

Her Gaelic name translates to "flower," leading some to call her Saint Flora. She died around 523 CE of natural causes.

Spiritual Ally

Blath worked alongside Saint Brigid and the two may be honored together.
