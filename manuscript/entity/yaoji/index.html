Classification

Twenty-third and youngest Fairy daughter of Hsi Wang Mu, Queen Mother of the West; among the primary Taoist goddesses.

Magickal Properties

Yaoji has dominion over birth, sex, lust, life, death, healing, and dreams. A shaman and master herbalist, she is responsible for the presence of many medicinal herbs on Earth. She sponsors healers, heroes, and adepts, and is lover of kings. As a protective weather goddess, she raises and soothes storms, and commands dragons.

Origin Story

Once Yaoji lived in the Celestial World, where she mastered all the requisite Fairy crafts but grew bored. She wished to see the world, so she assembled a flock of Fairy attendants and embarked on a grand tour. Flying through the air, appreciating the beauty of Earth, she grew frustrated when she reached China's Wushan Mountains and found her vision blocked by thick clouds and rain stirred up by destructive dragons. (An alternative version of the myth holds that her vision wasn't blocked at all; rather, she saw only too well the dangers facing travelers in the perilous Wushan Gorge.)

She descended to Earth with her entourage and tamed and dredged the gorge, forming mountains in the process. By the time the work was complete, she discovered she loved the Wushan Peaks and Gorge, and decided to move in permanently with her Fairy entourage. Eventually she transformed into one of the peaks, the Goddess Mountain (the other peaks may be transformed Fairies or dragons).

Ritual Uses

Yaoji protects ships, sailors, and people in general, and prevents natural disasters. She reveals the secrets of healing, instructs Taoist adepts, and teaches the art of seduction and sacred sexuality. She is the subject of operas, films, folktales, and literature.

The controversial Three Gorges Dam effectively cut off access to the land below her peak, leaving her stranded, or rather, she may not be stranded at all, since she can simply fly away; however, those who seek her have had their access routes destroyed.

Manifestation

She most commonly appears as an irresistibly beautiful woman or as a beautiful blue stone, but she can take virtually any form, appearing as an apparition, a corporeal presence, or in dreams.

Attributes

Mount: tiger. Creatures: cranes, phoenixes, dragons.

Sacred Sites

Goddess Peak (Shennu Peak), one of twelve Wushan mountain peaks, which from a distance resembles a beautiful feminine silhouette. A temple stands at its base.

Correspondences

Plant: lingzhi, the mushroom of immortality.
