The Ocean Goddess of Chiloé

Pincoya is the ocean goddess of Chiloé, the "Enchanted Island," located five hundred miles south of Santiago off Chile's Pacific Coast, literally the end of the Earth.

The Dance of Abundance

Pincoya rises from the sea to dance on the beach or atop waves.

  • If she faces the water while dancing, an abundant harvest of seafood is promised
  • If she faces the land, local people anticipate hunger instead

Pincoya can be appeased and persuaded to change her prognostication by rituals conducted by local witches. Sometimes Pincoya helps the shipwrecked.

Manifestation

A beautiful mermaid or woman dressed in kelp. When she's not dancing, she's usually sitting on ocean rocks, combing her long hair.
