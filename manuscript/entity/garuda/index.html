Overview

Garuda is a mythological bird of the Hindu pantheon who bears the god Vishnu upon his back as his vehicle or steed. To call a creature a god's vehicle usually means more than that the god rides it: the god is somehow contained within it, sharing part of its soul or able to take its shape. Garuda is described as a golden-winged sun bird, and in appearance and meaning he shares much with the eagle, the simurgh, and the phoenix. His form varies, shown sometimes as a bird, sometimes as a winged man, and sometimes as a hybrid of man and bird.

Enemy of the Serpent

Garuda is an ally of humankind and the enemy of Naga the serpent, and he is often depicted with a snake gripped in his talons, wrestling it. The image is shared with the eagle and stands for the struggle between heaven and earth, spirit and matter, and between fire and water, the hot sun of Garuda drying up the streams and rivers.
