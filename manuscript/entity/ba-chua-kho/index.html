Magickal Properties

  • Vietnamese Lady of the Storehouse and Granary who governs fair exchange, provisioning, and symbolic loans of prosperity
  • Guardian of ledgers, marketplaces, and the ethics of repayment

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for capital to start or stabilize a venture, for honest partners, and for repayment of debts
  • Make vows clearly and record them; repay spiritual “loans” promptly

Offerings

  • Spirit money burned honestly in stated amounts, with thanks upon repayment

Favored People

Merchants, business owners, and any who seek sustainable prosperity and protection of goods

Petition For

Marketplace success, protection of inventory, fair pricing, trustworthy partners, and timely repayments

Warnings

She enforces bargains. Failure to repay or fulfill vows may result in withdrawal of favor or reclamation of gifts. Keep ledgers, and promises, clean.

Additional Notes

  • Titles: Lady of the Storehouse, Lady of the Granary, Goddess of the Market
  • Timing: Major festival and market rites observed locally
