Magickal Properties

Buddhist Worthies who achieved freedom from ignorance and suffering; miracle workers who protect, heal, and teach. Inner circle of Immortal Arhats preserve the Dharma in difficult times.

Ritual Uses

Petition for healing, protection from illness and anguish, wise teachers, and perseverance on the path. Make vows and offer service to others.

Offerings

Incense, lamps, clean water, good deeds performed for the benefit of beings; tie children's bibs or tokens at images of Pindola Bharadvaja for protection.

Favored People

Students of Dharma, healers, children (especially under Arhat Pindola's care), and those seeking relief from suffering.

Petition For

Recovery from illness, protection, right teachers, and steadfast practice.

Warnings

Approach with sincerity and humility; do not ask for harm.

Additional Notes

Traditions venerate 16, 17, or 18 Immortal Arhats; each leads a large retinue. Angaja protects from illness; Pindola heals; Rahula instructs in the Heaven of the Thirty‑Three.
