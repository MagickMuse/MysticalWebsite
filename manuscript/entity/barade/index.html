⚠️ Barade is venerated within Hausa Bori traditions, an African traditional religion. This is a closed practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Hausa warrior spirit who governs battle, pride, and the healing of internal wounds
  • Patron of martial strength and those wrestling with defeat or sulking
  • Can afflict with bleeding if disrespected

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for victory in battle, protective strength, and healing of spiritual wounds
  • Invoke to break patterns of sulking, stagnation, or wounded pride
  • Offer tokens before conflict or struggle

Favored People

Warnings

Avoid angering Barade; disrespect can result in bleeding afflictions. Keep offerings modest and timely; follow local Bori protocols.
