Buddha

Binzuru is renowned for healing physical pain, psychic pain, and heartache. Rub his statue on the corresponding body part to receive his healing blessing.

Magickal Properties

Binzuru is the Japanese name and manifestation of Pindola, first of the sixteen Arhats. He broke a vow of chastity or used magick thoughtlessly, so Buddha forbade him from attaining Nirvana. He is a compassionate, proactive spirit of healing and pain relief who lives in pain himself.

Ritual Uses

Rub your hand against the part of Binzuru's statue that corresponds with the part of your body that needs healing or relief while invoking his assistance.

Offerings

Warnings

Do not substitute ritual healing for proper medical care; seek appropriate treatment alongside devotion.

Additional Notes

Legend says he broke a vow of chastity, or alternatively, magick got him into trouble, he flew through the air causing pregnant women to miscarry. Buddha punished him by ordering him to stay on Earth until Maitreya, Buddha of the Future, arrives. Because he himself lives in pain, he has vowed to alleviate that of others. Although most associated with physical pain, he heals psychic pain and heartache too. He manifests as an elderly man with long, unkempt eyebrows and a bushy white beard who wanders performing rescues.
