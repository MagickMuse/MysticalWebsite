Magickal Properties

The Dactyls are mysterious metal-working spirits born when mother goddess Rhea fell to the ground during labor pains for Zeus, plunging her fingers into Earth. Ten iron boys (or five boys and five girls) sprang up where she penetrated Earth, and their first act was to successfully help Rhea with the difficult childbirth.

Ritual Uses

The Dactyls invented iron smelting and are spirits of secret metalworking guilds. The sons of the right hand become ironworkers; the sons of the left hand become shamans and magickal practitioners. They serve as healers and obstetricians, and educated Athena in her youth.

Offerings

Tools and materials related to metalworking and smithcraft, offerings appropriate for healing work and shamanic practices, items honoring their role as obstetricians and healers.

Favored People

Metalworkers and smiths, shamans and magickal practitioners, healers and obstetricians, those working in secret guilds or traditional crafts requiring specialized knowledge.

Petition For

Skills in metalworking and smithcraft, shamanic knowledge and magickal abilities, healing powers and medical knowledge, assistance during childbirth and difficult births, protection of traditional crafts.

Warnings

They are spirits of secret knowledge and may require proper initiation or understanding of traditional craft mysteries. Associated with both creation and the potential dangers of metalworking.

Additional Notes

Three named Dactyls are Akmon ('Anvil'), Damnameneus ('the Compeller,' a hammer metaphor), and Kelmis ('Knife'). They are primordial, phallic spirits concerned with fertility, well-being, healing, and shamanism. Another version says left-hand Dactyls cast spells while right-hand Dactyls break them. They are of small stature, sometimes described as Dwarves.
