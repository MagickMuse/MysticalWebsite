Magickal Properties

  • Irish sovereignty goddess, one of the land‑mother triad with Ériu and Fódla
  • Patron of poets, guardians of place, and national protection

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for land blessing, protection of territory, and clarity of duty to the land
  • Speak a poem to the land and pour spring water upon the soil

Offerings

  • Milk, blackberries, symbols of Ireland, poems or prayers affirming her sovereignty

Favored People

Poets, nationalists, land defenders, women reclaiming power, and all who honor the land as sacred

Petition For

Sovereignty, connection to land spirits, protection of territory, ancestral veneration, sacred femininity, resistance to colonization

Warnings

Petition with respect rather than sentimentality. She may withdraw support from those who dishonor or exploit the land.

Additional Notes

  • Member of Ireland's sovereignty triad with Ériu and Fódla; allied in lore with protective war goddesses

Additional Attributes

Banba is described as one of the aspects of the Morrigan. She is also a war goddess, capable of changing shape from girl to hag, and into birds and animals. She is closely associated with Badb, Eriu, and Maeve.

Sacred Ceremony

Banba is a name of the "Sovereignty of Ireland," to whom the king was married in a symbolic ceremony.
