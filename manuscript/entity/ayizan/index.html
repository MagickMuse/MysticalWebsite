⚠️ Ayizan is venerated within Haitian Vodou. This is a closed initiatory practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Marketplace Matron and Initiation Keeper of Haitian Vodou
  • Guardian of sacred order, ritual purity, and priestly lineages

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for protection and right order in initiations and community rites
  • Safeguard marketplaces, vendors, and fair exchange

Offerings

  • Simple, dignified offerings appropriate to market rites (left under priestly guidance)

Favored People

Initiates, market‑tenders, vendors, and communities maintaining ritual order.

Petition For

Protection of initiatory steps, cleansing of ritual space, order in markets and communities.

Warnings

Respect initiation protocols and marketplace rites. Do not attempt priestly functions without training. Do not commercialize or trivialize her role.

Additional Notes

  • Works closely with Legba at thresholds; embodies the law and order of communal life
