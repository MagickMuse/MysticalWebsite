Magickal Properties

Anahita, goddess of fertility and war, has dominion over all liquids including seminal, vaginal, and amniotic fluids. She purifies and strengthens sperm, empowers the womb, and bestows ample milk supply. She is unusual as she is specifically invoked to heal male reproductive disorders, a condition the ancient world typically didn't acknowledge. She was once very widely venerated throughout ancient Persia, Babylon, and Armenia.

Ritual Uses

Anahita is the sole significant Zoroastrian goddess, with an entire chapter (Yasht V) of the sacred text the Avesta devoted to her. It is Anahita who teaches Zarathusra to fight for justice and how to perform sacrifices. Young noble women engaged in sacred sexual rites prior to their marriages at Anahita's shrine at Erez in Akilisene.

Offerings

White or gold roses and tulips. She is a particularly watery spirit, concerned with cleansing and purifying sources of water. Your efforts on behalf of protection and preservation of fresh water sources should be appreciated.

Favored People

Those with reproductive disorders (especially men), warriors seeking victory in battle, and those needing fertility assistance.

Petition For

Fertility, healing male reproductive disorders, victory in battle, successful breastfeeding, and healing reproductive disorders in general.

Syncretism

The ancient Greeks identified Anahita with both Aphrodite (on account of her dominion over romance and reproduction) and Athena (on account of her martial aspect, driving chariots and leading warriors into battle). References to Persian Artemis or Persian Diana almost invariably indicate Anahita as well. She manifests as a tall, powerful, beautiful maiden wrapped in a gold embroidered cloak, adorned with jewelry and a gold crown. Her chariot is pulled by four white horses named Wind, Rain, Cloud, and Hail. Athor Anahta (Anahita's Throne), a mountain in ancient Sophene, is among her homes.
