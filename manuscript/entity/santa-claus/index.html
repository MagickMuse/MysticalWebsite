Magickal Properties

Santa Claus flies through the air during Yuletide in a sleigh pulled by antlered reindeer, accompanied by elves. He travels up and down chimneys, behavior traditionally associated with witches and shamans. The Yuletide night sky is crowded: Santa Claus shares it with witches and the Wild Hunt, often led by white-bearded Odin.

Once upon a time, night-riding witch-goddesses like Perchta visited homes during Yuletide expecting food offerings like pancakes or dumplings. During the witch-craze, households leaving offerings to Perchta were charged with witchcraft. Yet to this day, households ritually leave cookies and milk for Santa Claus, a saint lauded for behavior forbidden to witches.

The Pagan Origins

Santa Claus was unknown to early Christians. His incorporation into Christmas was considered disreputable and semi-Pagan, actively discouraged until the twentieth century. Fundamentalist Christians still reject him, recognizing Santa Claus as clearly Pagan. Modern Christmas celebrations incorporate many Pagan traditions: gift-giving, Christmas trees, Yule logs, mistletoe, and Santa Claus.

Ostensibly Santa Claus is an affectionate nickname for St Nicholas, a beatified third-century bishop from Myra, Turkey (now considered apocryphal). It's generally believed that beneath St Nicholas lies the bearded Greek sea god Poseidon. But who is Santa Claus?

Theories About Santa's Identity

Hermes: The horned god Hermes carries a sack indicating he provides for devotees and brings gifts. Horned spirits survived within Christianity under Santa Claus's guise. Santa no longer wears horns on his head but remains closely identified with his herd of horned reindeer.

Saturn: Christmas corresponds with the Roman Saturnalia, characterized by feasting, celebrating, and giving gifts to children. Saturn is a jolly old man with long white beard who distributes gifts and presides over merriment.

Odin: Odin flies through air at Yuletide. He's a world traveler whose pet ravens once covered the globe daily, just like Santa allegedly does at Christmas. Santa's reindeer associations may recall Odin's sojourn with Saami shamans.

Freyr: Santa's role as leader of elves indicates Freyr, Lord of Fertility and Elven King—the elves work for him. Among Freyr's sacred attributes is the pine tree.

Saami Shamans: Reindeer are closely identified with the Saami people living near the North Pole. The Saami were traditionally nomadic reindeer herders renowned for powerful shamanic traditions. Saami shamans soul-journey, often utilizing chimneys.

Black Peter Connection: The original Black Peter (Zwarte Piet) was a shaman dressed in rough ragged clothes with fur hat topped with horns, carrying a huge sack with pine tree sticking out (similar to phallic pine logs in Dionysian processions). The sack contained small human figures, not dolls but babies to be born in the New Year, gifts of fertility. "Black" referred to charcoal and soot (color of fertility), not diabolical nature. If original Black Peter was cleaned up, with soot removed, horned hat traded for clean red hat but big bag of gifts retained, he looks remarkably like Santa Claus.

Other Possibilities: Old Pagan frost gods like Russian Morozko (Father Frost) or Nordic Holler, consort of witch-goddess Hulda.

Historical Evolution

Modern American Santa derives from eighteenth and nineteenth-century German immigrant traditions. His costume wasn't standardized. Instead of red and white suit, he frequently dressed in animal skins or tattered rags. He carried bag of treats but also whip or stick and broom. He was soot-faced and carried jingle bells, a shamanic figure resembling those on Dutch Speculaas Poppen cookie molds.

Modern red-suited Santa Claus is a creation of the Coca-Cola Company, spreading worldwide in 1932 as part of advertising campaign. Some perceive his bright red and white suit references Amanita muscaria mushrooms. The original Coca-Cola formula incorporated coca and kola, two botanicals associated with shamanic traditions. Amanita muscaria spirits are reputedly red and white, as are the mushrooms.

European Variations

In parts of Europe, St Nicholas appears as dignified Christian bishop accompanied by clearly Pagan, frequently horned assistant. St Nicholas represents the Church; his partner stands in for Satan, having charge of "bad" children (literally beating them, giving coal, or taking them away to Hell).

Ritual Uses

Work with Santa Claus energy to connect with multiple Pagan deities hidden beneath modern mask. Honor Yuletide traditions of gift-giving, chimney magick, reindeer symbolism, and elf-working. Recognize the shamanic roots in his night-flight, soot-covered appearance, and passage through chimneys.

Older Roots and the Modern Image

Before the familiar image took hold, Father Christmas was a taller, slimmer figure dressed in slubby brown or green, in keeping with his origins as a pagan spirit of nature. One of his guises is Old Winter, honoured in a Norse custom in which an old man went from door to door and was fed and watered wherever he called, so as to propitiate the spirit of winter, just as food and drink are still left out for Santa today.

The red-suited image we know was popularised, though not wholly invented, by the Coca-Cola corporation; it drew on an earlier cartoon of 1863 by Thomas Nast in the American Harper's Weekly, an etching of a smiling old man with holly tucked into his hat brim, a pipe in one hand and a bundle of toys under his arm.

The gift-giving custom has its own old roots: children once stuffed their shoes with food for Odin's eight-legged horse Sleipnir, and in return Sleipnir filled the boots with gifts. Santa's reindeer carry much the same meaning as the horse, able to conduct spirits between the worlds of the living and the dead. The Lapps catch these wild deer by scattering the red and white fly agaric mushrooms they crave, whose flesh brings on sensations of flight, then lead the intoxicated animals away. In parts of India the season is heralded not by reindeer but by a chubby genie in red and white, borne on a flying carpet to open a great winter shopping spree.
