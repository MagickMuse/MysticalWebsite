Heiress of Jangare

The Spiritual Mother

Magajiyar Jangare is the spiritual mother of all devotees of Bori spirits. She holds a unique position in the Bori pantheon as the matriarchal figure who watches over all those who work with these spirits. Her title "Heiress of Jangare" refers to Jangare, the invisible city where the Bori spirits live.

Her Nature and Temperament

She is not a spirit to be trifled with: the afflictions she potentially causes include:

  • Impotence
  • Severe headaches
  • Pneumonia

However, she is generally a good-natured spirit who is not easily angered. If you like Bori spirits and show them proper respect, she probably likes you. She embodies the protective maternal aspect of the Bori tradition: fierce when necessary, but nurturing to those who approach with genuine respect.

Sacred Marriage

Magajiyar Jangare is married to Sarkin Aljan Biddarene, Chief of the Second House of Bori Spirits. This sacred marriage connects her to the leadership structure of the Bori spiritual hierarchy.

Color

White

Magickal Properties

Magajiyar Jangare represents the maternal, protective aspect of Bori practice. She serves as spiritual mother to all Bori devotees, offering guidance, protection, and connection to the Bori traditions. Her presence reminds practitioners that working with spirits requires respect, commitment, and proper initiation into the tradition.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Magajiyar Jangare for:

  • Spiritual guidance in Bori practice
  • Protection while working with Bori spirits
  • Connection to traditional Hausa spiritual practices
  • Healing from her specific afflictions (if caused by her)
  • Blessing new initiates into Bori traditions

Lore

Magajiyar Jangare's role as spiritual mother to all Bori devotees establishes her as a central figure in the tradition. Her marriage to the Chief of the Second House of Bori Spirits demonstrates the complex hierarchical and familial structures within the Bori pantheon. Like a good mother, she is protective of those who honor the traditions properly but will discipline those who show disrespect.
