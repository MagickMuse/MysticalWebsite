Sidi is a North African honorific that literally means "Sir" or "Lord." The word is used in secular situations, but it is also the traditional North African term of respect for male saints, Jewish as well as Muslim, and also for high-ranking djinn. The honorific is always used and essentially becomes part of the saint's name.

Usage

As an honorific, "Sidi" functions similarly to "Saint" in Christian traditions. It precedes the name and conveys reverence and spiritual authority. Both humans who have achieved sainthood and powerful djinn may bear this title.

Related Term

For information regarding saints of the South Asian Siddi community, see Siddi Saints. The female equivalent of Sidi is Lalla, used as an honorific for female saints and powerful female djinn in North African traditions.
