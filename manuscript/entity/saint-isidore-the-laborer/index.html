Saint Isidore the Laborer is the patron saint of farmers, farmworkers, and agricultural laborers. He is renowned for miraculous aid in farming and bringing rain.

Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Isidore (c. 1070–15 May 1130) was a poor farm laborer who worked the fields outside Madrid his entire life. Despite his poverty and lack of education, he was renowned for his deep piety, rising early each day to attend Mass before work.

His fellow workers complained to their employer, Juan de Vargas, that Isidore spent too much time in prayer and arrived late to the fields. When Vargas went to investigate, he witnessed angels plowing alongside Isidore—or, in some versions, a second plow being guided by an angel while Isidore prayed. The same amount of work was accomplished as if three men had labored all day.

Miracles

Isidore performed numerous miracles involving agriculture and animals:

  • Angels plowed his fields while he attended Mass
  • He struck his staff into the ground to produce a spring of fresh water for his thirsty master (this spring, the Fuente de San Isidro, still flows in Madrid)
  • He shared his meager lunch with the poor and hungry, yet his food was never diminished
  • In winter, he shared his grain with starving birds, yet his sack remained full
  • His prayers brought rain during drought

Marriage

Isidore married María Torribia, later venerated as Saint María de la Cabeza. They had one son who died young. After this loss, they lived in continence for the rest of their lives. María is also invoked for rain and good harvests.

Incorruption

Isidore's body was exhumed forty years after his death and found incorrupt. It remained so for centuries and was credited with numerous healing miracles. He was canonized in 1622 alongside Ignatius Loyola, Francis Xavier, Teresa of Ávila, and Philip Neri. He is also venerated as a patron of Madrid.

Iconography

A peasant farmer in simple work clothes, often shown with:

  • A plow or farming implements
  • Angels plowing beside him
  • Sheaves of wheat
  • A sickle

Attributes

Plow, sickle, sheaves of wheat, staff producing water.

Sacred Sites

  • Cathedral of Saint Isidore in Madrid (his relics)
  • Ermita de San Isidro, Madrid (site of the miraculous spring)
  • Meadow of San Isidro, Madrid (annual pilgrimage site)

Feast Days

15 May (celebrated with the Fiestas de San Isidro in Madrid)

Syncretism

In Haitian Vodou tradition, the spirit Papa Azaka is syncretized to Saint Isidore the Laborer.

Related Entities

Saint María de la Cabeza, Saint George
