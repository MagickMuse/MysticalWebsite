Aparajita (Hindu Goddess)

Bhutadamara, whose name means tumult of demons, is a god of the Mahayana Buddhist tradition. He is a wrathful figure whose office is the subjugation of demonic forces, and his very name announces the din of that struggle.

Iconography

Bhutadamara may be depicted reclining upon the Hindu goddess Aparajita, in a posture that ritually establishes the Buddhist deity's dominion over the earlier tradition and its powers. He is three-eyed and wears snakes in his hair.

Attributes

A staff, and the snakes coiled in his hair.

Petition For

Subjugation of hostile spirits, the quieting of demonic tumult, and the wrathful authority that clears the ground for practice.
