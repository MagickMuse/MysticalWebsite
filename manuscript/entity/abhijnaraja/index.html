Abhijnaraja is a physician god of Tibetan Buddhism, counted among the sMan-bla, the series of medicine buddhas honored in Tibetan and Lamaist traditions. He carries the healing currents of that lineage and the iconography of an awakened being.

Iconography

Abhijnaraja is typically depicted with stretched earlobes, a traditional mark of an enlightened being in Buddhist art. The elongated ears signal his attainment and his attentive listening to the suffering of the world.

Attributes

As one of the medicine buddhas, Abhijnaraja embodies the principle of healing through spiritual realization. His domain spans physical remedy, the diagnosis of difficult conditions, and the transmission of healing knowledge to those who study the medical arts.

Colors

Red.

Lore

Abhijnaraja belongs to the sMan-bla group, a lineage of medicine buddhas in Tibetan Buddhism whose collective work supports the healing of body and mind. Each member of the series carries a distinct aspect of the broader healing principle, and Abhijnaraja takes his place among them as a physician god whose presence is invoked when sickness must be addressed at both the physical and spiritual levels.
