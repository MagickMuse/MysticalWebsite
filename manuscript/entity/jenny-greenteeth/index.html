Entity Grimoire

Jenny Greenteeth

Favored People
None. She is a danger to be avoided, especially by children and the elderly.
Culture
EnglishBritish
Petition for
Do not petition. She is a drowning spirit, not a working ally.
Gender
Female
Type
Spirit
Also called
Ginny Greenteeth

Jenny Greenteeth is a water spirit who dwells in rivers, pulls passersby into the water, and drowns them. She is recognised by the long reach of her arms.

Where She Hides

Jenny Greenteeth tends to dwell in still waters, where large accumulations of duckweed (Lemnoideae) conceal her from passersby.

Who She Takes

Her arms are long and she can reach out from quite a distance. She is especially fond of children and old people.
