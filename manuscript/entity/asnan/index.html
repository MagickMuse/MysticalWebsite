Vegetation goddess of the Mesopotamian Sumerian and Babylonian-Akkadian traditions, a minor deity probably known to the Sumerians from circa 3500 BC or earlier. She is concerned with the abundance of grain in the fields and was sent as its protectress by the gods Enlil and Enki.

Cosmogony

In the Sumerian creation accounts, Asnan and the cattle god Lahar were first intended to serve the needs of the Anunnaki, the celestial children of An, but the heavenly creatures were unable to make use of their products. Humankind was therefore created to provide an outlet for their services.

Iconography

Her attributes are ears of grain sprouting from her shoulders.
