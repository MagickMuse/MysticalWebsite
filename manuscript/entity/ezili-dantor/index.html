Magickal Properties

Ezili Dantor is the most militant, powerful, and fiercely protective of the Ezili spirits. She is a revolutionary warrior goddess and the patron of single mothers, particularly those who must struggle to support their children. Ezili Dantor is the protector of abused women and children. She is a dark-skinned spirit, in contrast to her lighter-skinned sister Ezili Freda Dahomey.

Ezili Dantor cannot or will not speak. Her tongue was cut out so she could never betray the plans of the slave rebellion. She makes sounds but cannot form words. When she possesses devotees, she may make sounds or gestures but does not speak.

Ezili Dantor is mother to Anaïs, her beloved daughter. Everything Ezili Dantor does is for Anaïs. She lives, breathes, and works for her child. This fierce maternal love extends to all children under her protection. Ezili Dantor is intensely jealous and protective. She is not a spirit of romantic love but of fierce, protective maternal love. She may be petitioned by lesbians for protection and support.

Ritual Uses

Petition Ezili Dantor for fierce protection of women and children, especially in cases of abuse or violence. Invoke her for strength as a single mother, for vengeance against those who harm women and children, and for defense against those who would exploit or attack the vulnerable.

Offerings

Dark rum, unfiltered cigarettes, black pigs, fried pork, black rice and beans, daggers, knives, perfume. Her colors are red, black, and navy blue.

Favored People

Single mothers, abused women, survivors of violence, women who work hard to support their families, children, lesbians.

Petition For

Protection for women and children, vengeance against abusers, strength for single mothers, fierce maternal protection, defense against violence, support for lesbians.

Warnings

Honor Ezili Dantor with fierce protective offerings and keep her shrine private and well provisioned. Do not neglect vows or try to control her because she is a jealous protector of women and children. She is not to be trifled with. If you make promises to her, keep them. Do not invoke her for petty matters.

Additional Notes

Ezili Dantor is syncretized with the Black Madonna, particularly Our Lady of Czestochowa, the Polish Black Madonna whose icon shows scars on her face. These scars echo Ezili Dantor's own scarred face and her muteness. Her sacred day is Tuesday. Her sacred number is 7 or 9. Her sacred animals include the black pig. She may manifest with visible scars on her cheek, representing the wounds she received during the revolution.

Corn Mother Aspect

Although not an ancient grain goddess, Ezili Dantor is often identified as a Corn Mother. She epitomizes the effects of the traditional Corn Mother who, in the act of destruction (slashing at the corn with her daggers), simultaneously transforms death into nourishment (grain is ground into meal). Corn is also a conduit to the sacred: in Vodou tradition, vèvès (the sigils created to celebrate the lwa and communicate with them) are often drawn with corn meal. Her traditional offerings include fried pork, corn sprinkled with gunpowder, and omelets filled with corn and peppers.
