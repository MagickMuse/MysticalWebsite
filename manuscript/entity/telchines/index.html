Overview

The Telchines are mysterious magical spirits who rule the island of Rhodes. They are sea spirits, the children of Thalassa. They are magicians, shamans, and smiths, and befitting these occupations, they are discreet and protective of secrets, including their own identity. It is not even clear how many there are; reports vary from four to nine.

Magical Properties

The Telchines invented metalworking. They can raise storms, control weather, and produce miraculous animated statues. According to one myth, they forged Poseidon's trident (and may have crafted the sickle used to castrate Uranus). They are credited with producing the first images of deities.

Mythology

According to Greek myth, Poseidon was not swallowed by Kronos. Instead, Rhea rescued him and brought him to Rhodes, where he was raised and guarded by the Telchines, with the assistance of Kapheira, who may be the same spirit as Cabeiro. The Telchines eventually left Rhodes. One myth suggests that, being prophetic, they foresaw the Great Flood and abandoned the island for higher ground. An alternative version holds that Rhea brought Zeus to Crete to be guarded a third time; Zeus, fearing their power and magic, had them thrown into Tartarus and gave Rhodes to Apollo instead.

Manifestation

The Telchines are sometimes described as dwarfs and are sometimes depicted with canine heads and fish flippers instead of hands.
