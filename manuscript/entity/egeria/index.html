🤰🏻Sacred Midwife Spell: Egeria

Magickal Properties

Egeria is the wise Nymph who resides with Diana in the Forest of Nemi. She is Diana's servant and assistant midwife and may be venerated independently or together with Diana.

Ritual Uses

Egeria is a spirit of wisdom and healing. Invoke her when seeking prophetic vision, fertility assistance, or healing through spring waters. Her waters allegedly conferred wisdom, prophetic vision and fertility.

Offerings

Libations of spring water or milk. Terra-cotta ex-votos similar to modern milagros have been found in her spring at Nemi.

Favored People

Midwives, those seeking prophetic vision, those petitioning for fertility or healing.

Petition For

Wisdom and prophetic insight, assistance in childbirth and midwifery, fertility, and healing from spring waters.

Warnings

Egeria is venerated in wells, springs, and grottoes within groves. Keep offerings private and respectful rather than ostentatious.

Additional Notes

Plutarch reported that Egeria's spring gushed from a giant oak. She was the lover and advisor to Numa Pompilius, second king of Rome, and is credited with helping him establish official Roman state religion. She may manifest as a beautiful woman or a mermaid.
