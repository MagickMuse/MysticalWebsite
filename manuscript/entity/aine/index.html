Spirit of the Sun

Aine is a powerful, dynamic, and versatile Irish solar spirit. She presides over love, romance, fertility, healing, and protection. As a deity of sunlight and warmth, she embodies vitality, abundance, and the creative force of summer.

Demotion to Fairy Queen

With the rise of Christianity in Ireland, Aine was among several powerful Celtic spirits demoted to fairy status. Once venerated as a goddess, she came to be classified as one of several Queens of Fairy. This diminished title allowed old devotees to maintain some form of contact with her, even as her original role was suppressed by the new religious authority. Despite the reclassification, Aine retained her power and influence.

Midsummer Associations

Aine is closely connected to Midsummer celebrations. Her sacred hill, Knockainey (Cnoc Aine) in County Limerick, was traditionally the site of Midsummer Eve rituals involving torchlit processions through fields and cattle herds to invoke her blessings of fertility and protection.
