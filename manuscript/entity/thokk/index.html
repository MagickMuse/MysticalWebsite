The Hag Who Doomed Balder

Troll-hag whose name means "Coal." When Balder died, Hel agreed to release him if every being wept. Frigga secured tears from all (gods, humans, animals, plants, stones, elements) except Thokk. Sitting in dark cave, she coldly refused: "Let Hella keep what she has." Baldur remained in death until Ragnarok.

Identity Debate

Loki in Disguise: Most believe Thokk was Loki (who caused Baldur's death) sabotaging resurrection.

Angrboða's Manifestation: Others see Thokk as Loki's first wife Angrboða (Hel's mother), refusing to weep out of loyalty to daughter, ensuring Hel kept this powerful resident.

Symbolic Power

Single dissenting voice undoing unanimous agreement. Triumph of bitterness over light. Inevitability of death. Permanent consequences of murder. The intractable force refusing all pleas, one "no" more powerful than all the "yeses" combined.
