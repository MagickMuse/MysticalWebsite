🔓Sidi Messaoud Marriage Curse Breaking Ritual

Moroccan folk saint who breaks curses that prevent women from finding husbands.

Domains

Sidi Messaoud specializes in removing the spiritual obstacles that prevent marriage. According to Moroccan folk belief, women who remain unmarried despite their efforts may be suffering under a curse or spell of bad fortune. Sidi Messaoud can lift these afflictions at his shrine.

Ritual to Break the Marriage Curse

Women seeking Sidi Messaoud's aid visit his shrine in the Riad Larouss district of Marrakesh and perform the following ritual:

Bring a comb and henna to the shrine. Comb your hair thoroughly and apply henna to it. At the precise moment when the muezzin calls the faithful to prayer, remove all clothing and bathe with water drawn from the shrine's holy well. When departing, leave the comb at the shrine along with any hair that was combed out and additional offerings to the saint. This ritual banishes the bad fortune that has blocked marriage prospects.

Sacred Site

His shrine in the Riad Larouss district of Marrakesh, which includes a holy well used in the curse-breaking ritual.
