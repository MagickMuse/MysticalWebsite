Magickal Properties

Chantico is the Aztec goddess of fire and hearth who was venerated both in homes and temples as a guardian spirit. She protects whatever is valuable and precious, making her a powerful ally for safeguarding wealth and treasured possessions. Her association with the hearth connects her to the heart of domestic life and family prosperity.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Chantico for protection of homes and valuable possessions. Light hearth fires or candles in her honor to maintain her protective presence in the household. She can be petitioned for matters involving health, fertility, wealth, and general abundance.

Offerings

Ideally light a hearth fire, or if not possible, light candles or maintain a fire in an iron cauldron. Offer cacao beans, molé sauce, and Mexican-style hot chocolate. Starbucks named a deluxe chocolate drink after this goddess, so serve her something comparable.

Favored People

Families seeking protection and abundance, those with valuable possessions requiring safeguarding, people working to build domestic prosperity and security.

Petition For

Health and physical well-being, fertility and family growth, wealth and financial security, abundance in all forms, protection of valuable and precious items, household harmony and security.

Warnings

Her primary votive image was secretly buried in 1519 after the arrival of the Conquistadores and missionaries to keep it safe. This suggests the importance of protecting sacred objects and maintaining reverent approach to her worship.

Additional Notes

Chantico manifests both as a woman and as fire itself. Her sacred plant is Theobroma cacao (chocolate), and her animal is the red snake. She carries cactus spines as an attribute, reflecting her connection to the harsh but life-sustaining desert environment.
