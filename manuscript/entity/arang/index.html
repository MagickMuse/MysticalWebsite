Aryong Jong

Magickal Properties

Wronged ghost who protects vulnerable women and punishes rapists; accepts propitiation and confession.

Ritual Uses

Cleansing rites and formal apologies at wells, tombs, and shrines; petitions for justice and safety.

Offerings

Fruit, wine, incense, candles; pilgrimages to her pavilion.

Favored People

Young or vulnerable women; seekers of justice.

Petition For

Exposure of crimes, compelled confession, and protection in travel at night.

Warnings

Do not disturb isolated wells, tombs, or shrines; avoid traveling alone at night near her sites.

Additional Notes

Annual rites on the 16th day of the fourth lunar month at Miryang's Arang Pavilion.
