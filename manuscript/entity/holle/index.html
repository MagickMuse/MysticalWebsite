In Witchcraft

Hulda is named in traditional witchcraft as one of the forms or titles of the Lady of Delight, the governing feminine power of romantic love and enchantment. She stands alongside Habondia, Herodias, Aradia, Diana, Rhiannon, and Hecate as a name by which the witch-goddess may be addressed before workings of love and philter-craft.

Hulda and the Elder

The elder tree is sacred to Hulda, the Germanic witch spirit of love and magick, affectionately called Mother Holle. She is among the leaders of the Wild Hunt, the reveling procession of spirits and witches. Christian propaganda claimed elder repelled witches, yet Hulda and her witches use it in abundance. See Elder.
