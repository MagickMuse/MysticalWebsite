Magickal Properties

Cliodna is a goddess of love, beauty, healing, and death who presides over an afterlife paradise where age, death, pain, illness, and decay don't exist, only beauty. She was swept into the sea by Manannan's magical wave and now rules as Queen of Fairies. She's a shape-shifter allegedly incarnate in every ninth wave breaking on Ireland's shores.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Cliodna for love magick and beauty work. She excels at healing illnesses and bringing romance into life. Work with her near water, especially coastal areas where waves break in sets of nine. She can provide access to fairy realms and otherworld healing.

Offerings

Apples from the otherworld (or the best apples you can find), songs and beautiful music, lovely and beautiful objects, offerings made at coastal waters, items representing love and beauty.

Favored People

Those seeking love and romance, people needing healing from illness or heartbreak, lovers and romantics, anyone wanting to access fairy realms and otherworld knowledge.

Petition For

Love and romance, healing from physical and emotional ailments, beauty and grace, access to fairy realm and otherworld wisdom, protection during otherworld journeys.

Warnings

She may lure human lovers into Fairy Land from which they never emerge, possibly because they're living happily ever after. Time works differently in her realm. Approach with awareness of fairy realm dangers.

Additional Notes

Accompanied by trio of magickal songbirds whose voices heal - one blue with red head, one red with green head, one speckled with golden head. They eat otherworld apples and their song lulls sick people to healing sleep. Associated with numbers 3 and 9. She is said to dwell in a barrow near Mallow, County Cork.
