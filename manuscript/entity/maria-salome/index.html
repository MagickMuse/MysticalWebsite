María Salomé is renowned for miraculous healing, having been healed of breast cancer herself and then becoming a powerful curandera.

María Salomé Loredo Otaola (11 October 1854 to 2 October 1929) was an unofficial saint born in Spain of Basque ancestry who moved to Argentina with her family in 1869. Though privileged and wealthy, by 1881 she was desperately ill with breast cancer. When physicians could no longer help her, she visited the famed healer Pancho Sierra "without faith and without hope." He healed her and advised that although she would have no more biological children, she would have thousands of spiritual children.

Becoming a disciple of Pancho Sierra, her talent as a curandera emerged. She was arrested for practicing medicine without a license in 1914. María Salomé dedicated herself to charity, donating her wealth to the poor and founding her own church, now officially recognized in Argentina, which blends belief in reincarnation with Christianity.

Domains

A popular saint, Madre María is invoked for virtually everything, but especially for miracles of healing.

Iconography

Madre María is depicted on religious medals together with Pancho Sierra.

Sacred Sites

Her grave in the Chacarita Cemetery in Buenos Aires.
