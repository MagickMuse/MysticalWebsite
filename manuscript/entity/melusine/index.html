In Witchcraft

Melusine appears among the enchantresses and fays of medieval legend whose symbolism informs the witchcraft chalice tradition. Alongside Viviane, Brisen, Nimue, the Lady of the Lake, Morgan le Fay, and the May queens and corn maidens of European folklore, she belongs to the current of the Lady of Delight: the governing feminine power of love, enchantment, and the faery realm. Greek Persephone and Euridice are her classical counterparts within the same symbolism. This current is invoked through the chalice in traditional love workings, and stands in contrast to the athame, which is never used in witchcraft of the romantic type.
