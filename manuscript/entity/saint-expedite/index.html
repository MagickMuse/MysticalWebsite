Saint Expedite is the patron saint of computer programmers, merchants, navigators, and those needing speedy solutions.

Classification

Roman Catholic and Orthodox saint.

Overview

Saint Expedite is the spirit of speedy deliveries and quick solutions. He despises delay. He is the lord of fast action. If things are going too slowly, mired in red tape, bureaucracy, or other delay, ask Saint Expedite to slice through the obstacles and deliver a solution now. Among his specialties is speeding up legal proceedings. He is the saint of emergencies.

History

While some believe him unofficial, Saint Expedite is very official, albeit from before documentation was required. Legend has it he was an Armenian martyred in the fourth century. He has consistently been among the most magical saints and some believe his image conceals hidden veneration of a banished Pagan god.

Expedite may really be Mercury in disguise, with Mercury's rooster exchanged for Expedite's crow and Mercury's caduceus for a cross. The image of Saint Expedite is used in modern Vodou traditions to represent spirits of the cemetery like Baron Samedi and Baron LaCroix.

"Expedite" refers to a type of Roman foot soldier who carried no baggage and hence were fast or expeditious. Saint Expedite is quick to act. If you invoke him, you should see results shortly or not at all. Make sure to pay debts and fulfill vows quickly. He does not like to wait.

Warnings

Saint Expedite is controversial. He has a reputation for unscrupulousness. He is rumored to assist sorcerers with curses and revenge spells, although some devotees vociferously deny this.

Rituals

Nine-Day Candle Ritual

To request a favor, burn nine green candles in front of his image for nine consecutive days (81 small candles total). Or use nine novena candles, repeating prayers daily.

House Guardian Ritual

  1. Balance a coin on his cross
  2. Request he watch over your home, occupation, or finances
  3. Keep the coin there while things go smoothly
  4. If fortunes decline, remove the coin until the situation improves
  5. When returning the coin, give him something extra: rum or pound cake
Should Saint Expedite grant your request, place fresh flowers beside his statue. Some believe flowers must be included or the saint will react with anger. He is especially fond of red carnations.

Iconography

A handsome young Roman soldier holds a cross aloft while stepping on a crow, which cries "Cras." That is a pun: cras mimics the sound a crow makes, but it also means "tomorrow." Saint Expedite stamps out tomorrow in favor of today. In some German images, Expedite may point to a clock.

Attributes

Cross and crow, palm branch (martyrdom), clock.

Colors

Red, green.

Feast Days

  • April 19 (Catholic)
  • August 27 (Orthodox)

Sacred Sites

Generally the subject of private veneration and home shrines, but chapels exist worldwide. His statue in the Church of Our Lady of Guadalupe, International Shrine of Saint Jude in New Orleans, is a pilgrimage destination. Extremely popular in Reunion, where he is the subject of many roadside shrines. A chapel exists in San Juan, Argentina, near the shrine of Difunta Correa.
