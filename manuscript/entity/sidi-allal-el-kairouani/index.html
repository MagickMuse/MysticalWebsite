Sidi Allal El Kairouani is a Moroccan saint and the father of Lalla Beida, the presiding saint of Casablanca. The two may be venerated together.

Domains

Protection and safety at sea, patron of fishermen.

Life and History

In approximately 1340, Sidi Allal left Kairouan, Tunisia, by boat en route to Senegal but was shipwrecked near what is now Casablanca. Fishermen rescued him and brought him ashore, where he remained stranded.

When his wife died back in Kairouan, Sidi Allal sent for his daughter, Lalla Beida. Her boat was wrecked in the very same spot as her father's, but unlike him, Lalla Beida drowned. Sidi Allal buried her near the sea and was later buried beside her.

Sacred Site

His grave near his daughter's shrine in Casablanca. La Maison de la Blanche (House of the White Woman) by rue de Tnaker contains both their tombs.
