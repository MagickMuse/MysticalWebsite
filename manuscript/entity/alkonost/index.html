Magickal Properties

Alkonost is one of several magickal Russian spirit birds, the bird of paradise and a wind spirit who raises storms. Originally, she lived in the Pagan Russian paradise on the Otherworld island of Buyan. Post-Christianity, she was transferred to the Garden of Eden where she serves as God's personal messenger, traveling to Earth to sing marvelous songs to saints.

Ritual Uses

Work with Alkonost for protection during daylight hours and spiritual guidance. She has a sister spirit, Sirin, her alter ego whom she resembles very closely. They were once considered sacred birds of fortune, venerated side by side as protective spirits. Alkonost guards good fortune by day while Sirin protects it at night.

Offerings

Songs, drumming, dancing, and joyful celebrations. Carved wooden images of Alkonost and Sirin traditionally graced entrances to Russian peasants' cottages.

Favored People

Those seeking good fortune, protection, and spiritual guidance. Saints and spiritual practitioners seeking connection to divine realms, people needing bolstering of faith during difficult times.

Petition For

Good fortune by day, protection from evil, spiritual messages and guidance, connection to paradise realms, bolstering of faith during difficult times.

Warnings

Bird of paradise and wind spirit who raises storms. Originally lived in Pagan Russian paradise, later transferred to Garden of Eden as God's messenger. Her storm-raising abilities should be considered when working with her.

Additional Notes

Alkonost manifests as a bird with a woman's face or head, sometimes as a winged woman, often wearing a crown. Over time, Alkonost was classified as entirely "good" while Sirin became associated with more ambiguous, seductive qualities. She promises saints eternal life in Paradise and foretells their happy future, endeavoring to bolster their spirits when they waver.
