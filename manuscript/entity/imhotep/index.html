🏺Egypt❤️‍🩹Healing

Imhotep is renowned for healing all ailments. As the original divine physician, he offers diagnosis and remedies through sacred healing dreams at his sanctuaries.

The Divine Physician

Imhotep was a historical figure who became worshipped as a god, or at the very least a demi-god who could intercede between mortal humans and the divine realm. He lived in the reign of the 3rd Dynasty King Djoser (c. 2600 BCE) and has been credited as an architect, a physician, and a polymath.

Historical Life

Originally based at Saqqara, Imhotep served King Djoser and is credited with designing the Step Pyramid, the first monumental stone building in Egypt. His reputation for wisdom and healing grew over the centuries until he was elevated to divine status.

Worship

The worship of Imhotep became widespread throughout Egypt. Thousands of bronze statuettes representing him survive in museum collections. These show him as a seated scribe, embodying wisdom and learning.

Appearance in Art

In scenes carved on temple walls, Imhotep was depicted as a respectable priest with a skull cap or closely shaved head. The temples honoured him as priest, magician, and medical man, summing up the role of a priest-magician in ancient Egypt.

Healing Pair with Amenhotep

In the Ptolemaic Period, Imhotep and Amenhotep, son of Hapu, formed a healing pair in several temples in and around Thebes. They appear together in the temple of Hathor at Deir el-Medina, carved into columns inside the entrance. They also appear in a temple of Thoth at nearby Qasr el-Aguz.

Sanctuary at Deir el-Bahri

Imhotep and Amenhotep had their own dedicated sanctuary at Deir el-Bahri, where graffiti from the Roman period give thanks for healing and protection. The sanctuary may have offered sleeping spaces where the faithful could spend the night, hoping for a healing message from the gods in their dreams. Any diagnosis and remedy received this way would be considered especially potent, coming straight from these deified doctor-priests.
