Overview

Maria Bagnesi was born on 15 August 1514 in Florence. A small, beautiful, happy child, she was raised mainly by her sister, a Dominican nun, in her sister's convent. Four of her sisters entered the religious life, but Maria's father arranged a marriage for her. She was so horrified at the thought of marriage that she became sick, bedridden, and unable to walk. The wedding was canceled.

Religious Life

After Maria became a Dominican tertiary in 1544, she was able to leave her bed for brief periods. However, she suffered from asthma, kidney disease, and pleurisy, and was often immobilized. Her father hired doctor after doctor in attempts to heal her, but many were quacks who worsened her condition rather than improving it.

Visions and Mysticism

Maria began to have visions. She engaged in conversation with angels, demons, and saints. Her neighbors feared she was possessed. However, a local priest became her spiritual advisor and assured neighbors that Maria was not in league with Satan. She developed a reputation as a holy woman. Pilgrims began to visit her. She became deeply mystical and was seen to levitate.

Love of Cats

Maria had a deep love for animals, especially cats. Many slept on her bed and allegedly guarded her songbirds. When she was hungry, cats fetched cheese for her.

Iconography

Maria is often portrayed with at least one cat.

Spiritual Ally

Maria was deeply devoted to Saint Bartholomew, appending his name to her own. They may be venerated together.

Feast

27 May.

She remains an unofficial saint, with canonization pending.
