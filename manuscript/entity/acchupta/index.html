Acchupta is a goddess of learning in the Jain tradition of India. Her name carries the meaning untouched, marking her as a presence whose knowledge remains pure and undefiled by the disturbances of the world.

The Sixteen Vidyadevi

Acchupta is one of the sixteen vidyadevi, the goddesses of knowledge venerated within Jain practice. The vidyadevi are headed by Sarasvati, who presides over the entire group as the lead goddess of learning. Each member of the sixteen embodies a particular face of knowledge, and Acchupta takes her place among them as the untouched, the one whose wisdom cannot be soiled.

Domains

As a vidyadevi, Acchupta governs the steady, protected gathering of knowledge. She supports the disciple in retaining what has been studied, in keeping the mind clear of distortions, and in approaching learning with the discipline that the Jain path requires.
