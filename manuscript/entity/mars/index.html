Mars is the Roman god of war, agriculture, and the guardianship of the state. He is the father of Romulus and Remus, protector of Rome, and one of the most prominent deities of the Roman pantheon.

Domains

War, martial prowess, agriculture, and the defense of the state.

Syncretisms

Roman syncretism often identified Mars with local war deities across the empire, including Belatucadros in Celtic Britain.
