Enki

Abu is a minor vegetation god of the Mesopotamian Sumerian tradition. He embodies the steady emergence of plant life from the soil and the quiet, generative work that turns seed into shoot and shoot into harvest.

Origin

Abu is said to have sprung from the head of the god Enki. The myth treats his birth as a living image of plants emerging from the earth's soil, with the head of Enki standing in for the dark ground from which green growth pushes upward.

Domains

As a vegetation god, Abu governs the green strata of life, gardens, herbs, crops, and the fertile threshold where buried seed becomes visible plant. His presence is felt in the small, ordinary miracle of sprouting and in the slow, reliable work of cultivation.
