Magickal Properties

Aglauros was Athena's first Athenian priestess and eldest of the Aglaurides sisters. She was venerated as guardian of those willing to give their lives for the good of others, particularly young soldiers. She engaged in a secret love affair with Ares, producing daughter Alkippe who was an exact replica of her mother.

Ritual Uses

Work with Aglauros for protection of young soldiers and those willing to sacrifice for others. Athenian youths swore their oath of arms in her sanctuary. She was subject of a Mystery tradition and connected to sacred oath-taking.

Offerings

Scatach says that Aglauros loves her horse and chariot above all else, so gifts evoking horses and chariots should meet with favor.

Favored People

Young soldiers willing to sacrifice for others, those needing protection in battle, people associated with sacred mysteries, and those taking sacred oaths or vows.

Petition For

Protection for those willing to sacrifice for others, guardianship of young soldiers, assistance with sacred mysteries, and safe passage for children away from home.

Warnings

First Athenian priestess of Athena, subject of Mystery tradition. Was venerated before Athena took residence at Acropolis. Her story involves sacrifice and death, so approach with appropriate respect for her sacrificial nature.

Additional Notes

Having jumped to her death, she was widely venerated not only in Athens but also in Salamis, Cyprus, and the island of Salamis in the Saronic Gulf. She may have been venerated at the Acropolis before Athena took up residence. She is a friendly spirit who shares shrines with various other deities. Her sacred site is a cave on the eastern side of the Acropolis.
