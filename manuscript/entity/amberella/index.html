Magickal Properties

The beautiful princess Amberella fell in love with the Prince of the Sea and joined him in his underwater palace, abandoning human life for immortality. Sometimes she misses her old life and has regrets, sending gifts of amber to those she left behind. Although amber is the product of trees, because it was often found tossed up by waves, it was associated with the sea.

Ritual Uses

Amber is the vehicle with which to communicate with Amberella. Place amber jewelry on an altar dedicated to Amberella, requesting her blessings before wearing it. She is a completely benevolent spirit who may be requested to assist with various needs.

Offerings

Amber jewelry and offerings to sea spirits. Decorate her altar with sea treasures such as shells, sea glass, and small stones, as well as images of mermaids and sea creatures. Add amber to personalize it for her.

Favored People

Those in forbidden love, pregnant women, and those seeking relief from poverty. She particularly aids women and families in need.

Petition For

Love (especially forbidden love), fertility, pregnancy, and relief from poverty. She assists with matters of the heart and abundance.

Warnings

A Baltic sea princess linked to amber gifts, decorate altars with amber or sea treasures and do not take washed amber without making an offering first. Petition her for love or pregnancy blessings and keep coastal offerings tidy to avoid offending her.

Additional Notes

Alternative versions suggest she was the daughter of Perkunas, the Thunder Lord, who disapproved of her eloping with a sea spirit. Amber is considered magickally beneficial during pregnancy. She may appear with legs or a mermaid's tail, wearing an amber crown and bedecked with amber jewelry.
