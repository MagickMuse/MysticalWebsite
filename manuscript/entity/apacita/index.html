Apacita

Precautions

Spirit

Apacita is a guardian spirit of the Inca tradition of pre-Columbian South America, recorded in Peru and the surrounding Andean region. The name belongs to the apotheosis of a pile of stones marking the top of a pass or some other critical point on a route.

Veneration

Travelers invoke Apacita with small offerings to strengthen them on their journey. The cairn itself is the body of the spirit, growing as each passerby adds a stone, and the offering left on it draws the guardian's protection over the next stretch of the road.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Apacita belongs to the Inca and Andean Indigenous traditions, which remain living among the Quechua and Aymara peoples of the Andes today. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform these rites outside of the cultural and initiatory context in which they belong.
