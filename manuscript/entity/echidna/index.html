Magickal Properties

Echidna, the snake woman, is the daughter of Gaia the Earth. She is a great goddess, mother of a pantheon of spirits, an alternative to the Olympians. Echidna is a guardian of Earth's treasures and a repository of ancient wisdom.

Ritual Uses

She can be a protective guardian. Earthquakes are the result of Echidna, the great snake, uncoiling and moving about. She is invoked to prevent earthquakes or to provide safety and minimize the damage should one occur.

Offerings

An altar in a quiet cave or grotto is best. Altars within homes should be placed in quiet corners. She may be venerated alongside Typhon, her children, Keto, or Gaia. Decorate altars with serpentine imagery or images of her children.

Favored People

Those seeking protection for Earth's treasures or access to ancient, serpent-woven wisdom.

Petition For

Prevention of earthquakes, safety and minimized damage should one occur, protection of Earth's treasures, and access to ancient wisdom.

Warnings

Echidna is a very private and primordial spirit. Keep rites quiet and intimate. Do not stage ostentatious public displays. She was worshipped as an earthquake goddess in Phrygia, now in modern Turkey.

Additional Notes

Depending on myth, Echidna is the mother or grandmother of many spirits including Cerberus, Orthrus, the Chimera, Hydra, the Nemean lion, Ladon, and the sphinx. Typhon is her brother, lover, and father of her children. Hesiod described her as ageless and immortal. She manifests as a beautiful woman from the waist up; below the waist, she is a snake. She may have wings or a double-tail. She possesses a snake's venom.
