Anatolian Great Mother embodying wild nature, mountains, fertility, ecstatic spiritual experience. Worship involved frenzied drumming, dancing, altered consciousness. Represents raw nature power and ego dissolution. Rides lion-drawn chariot, wears crown shaped like city walls.

Domains & Sacred Objects

  • Drums

Kybele and Attis: Goddess-Consort Pattern

Exemplify ancient hierarchy where powerful goddesses had younger male consorts whom they initiated, tutored, and sometimes killed. Goddess eternal; consorts replaceable. Attis dies/resurrects in her mysteries under goddess's dominion. Christianity inverted this, making male devil dominant over female witches.

Origins and Roman Transformation

Turkish: Kubaba (Hittite) → Kybele (Phrygian) → Cybele (Roman). Means "Place of Caves." Unwanted child raised by leopards/lions, became witch so powerful she evolved into immortal goddess.

Roman Evolution: Originally served by women in caves/forests. Roman law required male officiators, Kybele's response: transgendered clergy (galli) who underwent ritual castration, dressed as women, crafted replica vaginas for sacred sex. Festivals scandalous, spontaneous ecstatic castrations, drums, processions.

Christian Suppression: Church despised women prominence, LGBTQ+ clergy, lower-class popularity. St. John Chrysostom led death squads (397 CE). Emperor Justinian ordered temple destruction, murder of priestesses/galli, burning texts. Her meteorite may be buried under St. Peter's Basilica.

Warning: Approach ecstatic rites with community guidance. Never perform self-harming initiations alone. Seek experienced facilitators.
