Magickal Properties

Daimones are wild, rustic spirits of countryside, forests, mountains, and uncultivated fields. They form entourages for Artemis, Dionysus, Hermes, Pan, and Kybele. They are spirits of ecstasy, intoxication, and sex, gregarious spirits who travel in fluid packs or gangs, happy to expand their circle if you're fun and don't try to exploit them.

Ritual Uses

Seek out Daimones for prophecy and revealing secrets of past and future. They can locate who or what is missing. Relationships with them need to be maintained on their turf, they won't live happily indoors. They drink, dance, and play flutes and percussion instruments, offering companionship for those who appreciate natural pleasures.

Offerings

Wine and other intoxicants, music and dancing, natural pleasures and outdoor celebrations. They appreciate those who can match their energy for fun and ecstasy without trying to control or exploit them.

Favored People

Those seeking ecstasy, intoxication, and natural pleasures, people who appreciate wild nature over civilization, practitioners needing prophetic guidance and divination, anyone wanting genuine companionship and fun without exploitation.

Petition For

Prophetic abilities and divination, revelation of secrets from past and future, location of missing persons or things, companionship and genuine fun, guidance in natural pleasures and ecstasy, resistance to or indulgence in temptation.

Warnings

Not malicious or evil but can be raucous and may find it fun to scare mortals just to see them panic and run. They're sharp-witted with good eye for true inner character. Post-Christianity, this unruly bunch was reclassified as 'demons.' They are not tame and require outdoor relationships.

Additional Notes

Name corrupted from Theoi Nomioi meaning 'Spirits of the Countryside.' They don't want your soul, just company and fun. Many can be benevolent and generous if so inclined. They are spirits of prophecy but also temptation, offering pleasures of sex, intoxication, and nature.
