The Snow Woman

Travelers caught in snowstorms in Japan, especially in the mountains, describe encountering a ghostly woman, tall and beautiful, with very pale skin and breath like ice. She looks serene and does not appear to be freezing, even when appearing completely naked in sub-zero temperatures. Yuki-Onna is the spirit of the killing power of snow.

Encounter and Danger

Few survive encounters with Yuki-Onna, although some do, encounters are not invariably fatal. Her methods vary:

  • She may breathe on someone, fatally freezing them
  • She may deliver the final blow with a kiss
  • She leads travelers astray on snowy nights so they become hopelessly lost and die in the snow
  • She sometimes has sex with men before they die
  • She kills people in their sleep, they fall asleep and never wake
  • She may carry a baby and ask someone to hold it; those who do instantly freeze to death

Nothing indicates that refusing to hold the child will save you. If caught in a snowstorm alone with Yuki-Onna, the only thing that may save you is her mercy.

Goddess of Mercy

Yuki-Onna is frequently classified as a vampiric spirit who fatally sucks the breath from victims, albeit gently, even sweetly. As an erotic spirit, she is sometimes classified as a succubus. However, she may also be understood as a goddess of mercy, she appears to those already doomed or dying and blesses them with a peaceful, quiet, even happy death rather than a prolonged, fearful, agonized one. As angels of death go, there are far worse.

In the Home

Most typically Yuki-Onna reveals herself to those stuck in snowstorms, but she can invade a home in the form of a gust of wind, transforming into a frosty white mist or vapor that enters through cracks or keyholes. Some legends say snowflakes are her teardrops. More malevolent legends suggest she doesn't just appear when it snows, she actually creates blizzards to actively entrap people.

Cultural Legacy

Yuki-Onna makes frequent appearances in literature, movies, manga, and anime. Her myth is most famously retold in Lafcadio Hearn's 1904 Kwaidan: Ghost Stories, filmed in Masaki Kobayashi's 1965 classic Kwaidan, and was the inspiration for the CLAMP manga Shirahime Syo (Tales of Snow Princess).

Manifestation

Tall, serene, and cold, Yuki-Onna has long black hair and may lack feet. She can appear as vapor, a cold wind, or snow itself. Alternatively, she is snow that sometimes takes the form of a woman.
