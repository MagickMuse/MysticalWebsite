Kishijoten is the Japanese Buddhist goddess of beauty, luck, prosperity, and fertility. She is one of the Shichi Fukujin (Seven Gods of Fortune in some versions) and is particularly associated with bringing good fortune and abundance. Kishijoten embodies feminine beauty and grace and bestows these qualities on her devotees.

She is often depicted as a beautiful woman in elegant robes holding wish-granting jewels or other symbols of prosperity. Kishijoten brings both material wealth and the spiritual wealth of beauty and happiness.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kishijoten for luck, beauty, prosperity, and fertility. She is particularly helpful for artists and performers seeking success and recognition. Petition her when you need good fortune in endeavors or wish to cultivate beauty in your life.

Warnings

Offer beauty, luck, and respectful tokens. Do not trivialize her cult or commercialize her image for cheap fortune-telling or superficial gain. Approach her with genuine respect and appreciation for beauty and abundance.
