Magickal Properties

Daphne was an oracular goddess who presided over the famous Oracle of Daphne near Tarsus and Antioch, where bay laurel originated. More than a priestess, she was the presiding spirit whose oracle was obtained by dipping bay laurel leaves into a sacred spring. She shares characteristics of her sacred tree as goddess of divination, prophesy, intoxication, spiritual protection and sensual pleasures.

Ritual Uses

Use bay laurel leaves for divination (daphnomancy), simplest method involves placing leaves beneath your pillow for psychic dreams. Her shrine featured lush groves merging secular pleasure with spirituality. Bay laurel leaves have spiritually protective properties. Place at least one in each corner of room for safety.

Offerings

Fresh water, bay leaves, divination devices and intoxicants. Her shrine was renowned for licentious rites and carnal pleasures before being destroyed by Christianity.

Favored People

Oracles, diviners, and prophetic practitioners, those seeking spiritual protection from harm, people needing guidance through intoxication and sensual experience, women fleeing unwanted advances.

Petition For

Divination abilities and prophetic insight, spiritual protection especially for women, guidance through altered states and sensual experience, escape routes from dangerous situations, connection to ancient oracular traditions.

Warnings

Not a virginal spirit, her objection to Apollo wasn't about chastity. Her shrine and groves were early casualties of Christianity. The tree offered her escape from attempted rape, symbolizing protective properties.

Additional Notes

Daughter of Gaia and Ladon the river spirit. When Apollo pursued her, Earth swallowed her up and bay laurel sprang up to mark the spot. Laurel leaves became identified with Apollo and remain symbolic of victory. Her springs were main water source for Antioch.
