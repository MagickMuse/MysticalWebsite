Overview

Telesforos is Hygeia's brother, son of Asklepios, a spirit of convalescence, recuperation, and recovery from illness or injury. This divine child possesses profound healing powers and has dominion over sleep and dreams, an important component of Asklepian healing.

Magical Properties

Telesforos may appear in dreams to devotees or to perform healing, and he also provides fertility. He is a guardian of infants.

Manifestation

Telesforos appears as a young, barefoot boy wearing a wide cloak with a hood drawn low over his face, or a Phrygian cap similarly shadowing his face, resembling the Genius Cucullatus. Some theorize that he is the original Genius Cucullatus, or vice versa; his image is found on many coins.

Additional Notes

Hygeia and Telesforos frequently work and travel together.
