Badb is a Celtic Irish war goddess, one of the aspects of the Morrígan.

Domains

War, battle, prophecy of death, and shapeshifting.

Attributes

She is capable of changing shape at will.

Iconography

In her aspect as Badb Catha she takes the form of a crow, the harbinger of death.

Lore

She confronts the Irish hero Cú Chulainn before battle and terrifies him by turning into Badb Catha, the crow of death.
