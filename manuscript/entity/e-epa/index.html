Magickal Properties

E'epa is a Hawaiian term indicating a person born with something marking their close affiliation with spirits. This something, whatever it is, will manifest early; if not at birth, then in infancy or early childhood. The mark of the E'epa may be a gift such as special ability, particularly psychic talents. It may be a physical manifestation such as a baby born with a caul or teeth. It may also be what conventional society might now consider a physical disability.

The E'epa maintains relationships with spirits during life, serving as a mediator for other people. After death, the E'epa will transform into a spirit. Special funeral rites may be required. E'epas transform into all kinds of spirits, but many have a propensity for becoming tricksters. E'epas generally become family or ancestral spirits, protecting the family into which they were born.

Ritual Uses

Because their future spiritual destiny is recognized, bonds and relationships between the E'epa and future devotees can be developed and negotiated while the E'epa is still in human form. Work with E'epa spirits to establish familial protection and spiritual mediation.

Offerings

Respect and recognition of spiritual gifts, proper funeral rites.

Favored People

Those born with spiritual marks, psychics, mediums, families honoring ancestral spirits.

Petition For

Understanding spiritual gifts, mediation with spirits, family protection.

Warnings

Special funeral rites may be required for E'epa individuals to ensure proper transformation into spirit form.

Additional Notes

E'epa individuals serve as mediators between the spirit world and the living during their lifetime. Their transformation into family spirits after death provides ongoing protection for their descendants.
