Magickal Properties

  • Egyptian female counterpart of Bes, often described as lioness-demon
  • Relationship to Bes unclear, may be his sister, consort, daughter, or feminine path
  • Associated with spellwork and rituals involving snakes, possibly snake charming
  • Found in company of Taweret, Bes' official consort, they all get along and may be venerated together

Ritual Uses

  • Invoke for spellwork and magickal rituals involving serpents
  • Use in shamanic rituals (well-worn masks found suggest ritual use)
  • Petition alongside Bes and Taweret for combined protective power

Offerings

Artifacts with her image found among papyri containing magick spells, suggesting incense and magickal materials

Favored People

Those working with spellwork, snake charming practitioners, shamanic ritualists

Petition For

Spellwork assistance, snake charming skills, shamanic ritual power, magickal protection

Warnings

Often described as lioness-demon, approach with respect and proper protective measures during magickal work.

Additional Notes

  • Iconography: Resembles feminine version of Bes, usually naked with clear gender distinction
  • Artifacts: Image appears on amulets, wooden figurine with movable arms found holding metal snakes/wands
  • Found at Ramesseum in Thebes beneath tomb, suggests funerary/magickal context
  • All three spirits (Beset, Bes, Taweret) seem compatible for joint veneration
