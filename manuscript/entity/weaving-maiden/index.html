Magickal Properties

Depending on the version of her myth, the Weaving Maiden is either the eldest or youngest of the Jade Emperor's seven daughters, collectively known as the Seven Sisters or Seven Fairies. They are star maidens associated with the Pleiades. She is no trivial spirit nor an ordinary weaver: her weaving helps maintain universal order.

The Weaving Maiden blesses people with happy romance and marriages. She gives the gift for which she herself longs. She unites lovers and families and may be petitioned to help discover your soul mate. She is a particularly beloved spirit also invoked for assistance with fertility and healing. Although at the peak of her power and generosity on her feast day, she may be invoked anytime.

Origin Story

One day, the Seven Sisters descended to Earth to frolic in hot springs. They were observed by a young cowherd called Ox Boy, who stole an item of clothing left on the banks of the spring. When all her sisters flew away home, the Weaving Maiden discovered that without her garment she was stuck and could not fly.

The Weaving Maiden fell madly in love with Ox Boy, and they had two children and lived happily for seven years. After seven years, celestial guards were sent to forcibly escort her back to Heaven, separating her from her husband and children.

She wept so bitterly that finally it was decided that the lovers could reunite for one night each year. Once a year, on the Feast of the Double Seventh, magpies build a bridge across the Milky Way and the lovers are permitted to reunite.

Ritual Uses

On the day of the Double Seventh, the Weaving Maiden is particularly sympathetic to lovers. In Chinese folk magick, it is considered the ideal day for love spells and requests for the Weaving Maiden's blessings of true love.

Spirit Ally Test

The Weaving Maiden is a straightforward spirit who will let you know where you stand with her. This ritual can only be performed on the Feast of the Double Seventh:

  • On the morning of the Feast of the Double Seventh, find a spider.
  • Using extreme care and caution, very gently place the spider in a box and leave it there for twenty-four hours.
  • After twenty-four hours, liberate the spider to go where she will. Examine the box:
    • If the spider started a web, the Weaving Maiden likes you.
    • The bigger the web, the more she likes you.
    • If there is no web, if the spider escaped without starting a web, or if you were never able to find a spider and so could not perform the ritual, then perhaps another spirit should be considered.

Manifestation

In human form, the Weaving Maiden is an exceptionally beautiful, sweet, gentle Fairy. You can also see her in the skies as one of the Seven Sisters (the Pleiades) and as Vega, the brightest star in the constellation Lyra across the Milky Way from her lover, now manifest as the star Altair.

Additional Notes

Another version of the myth suggests that her marriage to Ox Boy was arranged by her father. There was no objection to the marriage until the two lovers became so passionately engrossed in each other that they neglected all other responsibilities. Hsi Wang Mu, Queen Mother of the West, forcibly separated the couple and ordered them back to work.

Depending on the version of their legend, the Seven Sisters' father may be a solar deity or the Kitchen God instead of the Jade Emperor.
