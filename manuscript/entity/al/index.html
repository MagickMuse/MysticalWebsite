Magickal Properties

The Al are generally malevolent, anti‑fertility spirits held responsible for miscarriage and the deaths of very young children. Often invisible, when they manifest they are described with brass fingernails, iron teeth, boar‑like tusks, and snake‑like hair. Their presence is associated with suffocation of infants and sudden infant death.

Ritual Uses

Do not work with the Al. They are warded against, not petitioned. Some traditions claim they can be constrained by pinning their garments, but attempting command is extremely dangerous and not recommended.

Offerings

None. Focus on protective measures rather than propitiation.

Favored People

None. The Al are inimical to pregnant people and infants.

Petition For

Protection from the Al, banishment and warding, safeguarding pregnancies and newborns.

Warnings

Extremely dangerous anti‑fertility spirits targeting pregnancy through the first seven months of a child's life. Iron amulets are traditional protections. Avoid isolated, damp places where they are said to lurk.

Additional Notes

Some accounts suggest these figures may mask suppressed pre‑Christian deities turned wrathful in later folklore. Their preference for stables and remote roadsides is often cited in regional traditions.
