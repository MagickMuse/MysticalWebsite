Magickal Properties

Al Khidr, whose name means "The Green One," is the immortal wandering saint who achieved immortality by drinking from the Fountain of Life. As an eternal wanderer, he appears wherever help is needed most, rescuing the lost, feeding the hungry, and providing divine intervention in moments of crisis. His interventions are often subtle but always perfectly timed.

Ritual Uses

Work with Al Khidr for safe passage and spiritual guidance. Travelers traditionally invoke Al Khidr before dangerous journeys, and fishermen and sailors seek his protection on the waters. He is petitioned for guidance when lost (literally or metaphorically) and appears as a mysterious stranger who offers help at crucial moments.

Offerings

Vows fulfilled at Saint George's shrines if Khidr's are inaccessible, offerings at springs and wells, and pilgrimages to sacred sites associated with him. Present offerings that honor his role as guide and protector.

Favored People

Travelers, spiritual seekers, initiates into mystical paths, those lost or in distress, fishermen, sailors, and people seeking divine intervention.

Petition For

Safe passage, spiritual guidance, healing springs, mystic initiation, divine intervention during crises, and assistance when lost or in need.

Warnings

Honor vows made at Al Khidr's springs and shrines and approach him with truthfulness and ritual cleanliness because failing to observe vows or etiquette may withdraw his protection and blessings.

Additional Notes

He has been syncretized with Saint George, Elijah the Prophet, Dionysus, Osiris, and various local saints throughout the Islamic world. He is associated with springs and wells, green islands and oases, crossroads and mountain passes. His color green connects him to eternal life, spiritual enlightenment, and the life-giving power of water.
