Sinivali is a minor goddess of the Hindu Vedic tradition, associated specifically with progeny and the granting of offspring. Like the other minor Vedic goddesses, her temperament is benign, though her sphere is more narrowly defined than the broader figures of abundance with whom she is sometimes grouped.

In Vedic literature

Sinivali is described as the mistress of the family, "broad-hipped," and prolific. When she is named in the Rg-veda it is typically in the context of petition for children, and her invocation belongs to the rites and prayers offered by those hoping for sons or descendants. Her sphere of action is fertility and the continuation of the family line rather than wealth or harvest, and she stands somewhat apart from the abundance group in this respect.

Cited hymns

Key Rg-veda references occur at 2.32 and 10.184.
