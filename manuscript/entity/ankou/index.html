Magickal Properties

Ankou embodies the inexorable power of death as harvester and the ancient understanding that when death comes calling, no one returns empty-handed. As a Breton Angel of Death who serves as psychopomp, he carries the archetypal energy of the soul harvester who ensures that the natural cycles of death and renewal continue without fail.

Ritual Uses

Death Omens Recognition: Understanding his signs helps recognize when death approaches, allowing for appropriate preparation and farewell rituals.

Soul Transition Work: Call upon his assistance when helping souls of the recently deceased find their way to appropriate afterlife destinations.

Ancestor Communication: Use knowledge of his role to facilitate communication with deceased family members who may travel in his entourage.

Ending and Release Rituals: Invoke his energy when situations, relationships, or phases of life need to be definitively ended and released.

Offerings

Approach with extreme caution. Traditional offerings to death spirits, acknowledgment of his necessary role in cosmic balance, and prayers for gentle treatment of deceased loved ones.

Favored People

Ankou does not "favor" the living, his attention typically means death is approaching. He may be drawn to those whose time is near or people who work closely with death.

Petition For

Petition with great care. Gentle treatment of deceased loved ones, peaceful transitions for the dying, and protection from premature death.

Warnings

Work with Ankou only for funerary purposes; avoid leaving food to rot; avoid mockery and noise near tombs. Hearing his creaky cart wheels means death is near. Hide immediately if you hear his approach, and never look him directly in the face.

Additional Notes

His traditional cart pulled by black and grey horses has reportedly been upgraded to a modern hearse in recent sightings, showing how ancient spirits adapt to contemporary times.
