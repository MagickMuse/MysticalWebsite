⚕️A Ceremony to Assist with Health

Chiron is renowned for healing knowledge and herbal wisdom. As the archetypal wounded healer, he can heal everyone but himself, teaching others to transform their wounds into wisdom.

Magickal Properties

Chiron ('The Wounded Healer') is the exception to raucous centaurs, a brilliant, civilized culture hero and immortal son of Kronos and Philyra. He's renowned as tutor and foster-father of heroes including Achilles, Jason, and Heracles. Accidentally wounded by Heracles' poisoned arrow, he suffers eternal agony but cannot die, becoming the archetypal wounded healer.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Chiron for healing knowledge and teaching abilities. He excels at training others in healing, herbalism, music, and prophecy. Work with him when you need to turn your own wounds into wisdom that helps others. He can teach advanced healing techniques and herbal medicine.

Offerings

Healing herbs and medicines, educational materials and teaching tools, music (he taught Heracles), items representing the healing arts, respectful study and practice of healing knowledge.

Favored People

Healers and medical practitioners, teachers and mentors, wounded healers who help others through their own pain, herbalists and musicians, those seeking deep wisdom through suffering.

Petition For

Healing knowledge and herbal wisdom, teaching and mentoring abilities, transformation of wounds into wisdom, musical and prophetic abilities, guidance in healing others.

Warnings

He embodies the wounded healer paradox, he can heal everyone but himself. Approach with respect for the pain that creates wisdom. His knowledge comes through suffering transformed.

Additional Notes

Lives in cave on Mount Pelion with family including mother Philyra and wife Chariklo. Appears as old man with white beard and pony hindquarters. Musician, healer, herbalist, prophet, and sorcerer who never lifts hand in anger. Zeus placed him in heavens as Sagittarius constellation.
