Sidi

Venerated as a Sufi saint, Sidi Ali ben Harazem was a 12th-century mystic scholar so wise and eloquent that djinn attended his lectures.

Life and Legend

Sidi Ali ben Harazem's grave-shrine (koubba) in Fez was established in the 17th century. It is located in an area that was once a leper colony, in a quarter traditionally populated by saints, sorcerers, thieves, and lunatics.

Magical Properties

Sidi Ali ben Harazem is the protector of those tormented by evil spirits, mental illness, and especially those unable to distinguish between the two.

Sacred Sites

A therapeutic spring associated with him near his shrine is now a modern spa resort. The mineral springs, rich in calcium, sodium, and magnesia, reputedly heal arthritis, kidney conditions, and high cholesterol. Sidi Harazem is also a brand of Moroccan mineral water.

Feast

His moussem (commemoration of his death) occurs in spring, usually around March or April, and is among the largest in Fez.
