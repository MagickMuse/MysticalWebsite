Nak

Culture
Cambodian
Type
Deity
Animals1
Crocodiles

In Cambodian belief, Nak is the deity who governs the earth and the waters, sharing a name with the crocodile that moves so easily between both realms.

Domains

Nak presides over the earth and the waters, the two realms the crocodile inhabits. The shared name reflects how closely the deity and the creature are bound together in Cambodian thought.

Crocodile and Lightning

Because the crocodile can lie still for a long time before striking with sudden speed, the Cambodians made it a symbol of lightning. Nak carries the same association through the creature that bears the deity's name.
