Almita Visitación Sivila is an Argentinean Almita.

Life and Death

On June 1, 1908, Visitación Sivila set off for the market in Jujuy, Argentina. A man made sexual advances toward her, which she rejected. (He may or may not have stalked her previously.) Looping a rope around her neck, he pulled her into the bushes where he raped, murdered, and mutilated her.

When Visitación failed to return home, her husband contacted authorities, who searched for her. They discovered her murderer in the process of literally consuming Visitación (one version suggests he barbecued her thigh).

Powers and Miracles

Since her death, thousands of miracles have been attributed to her.

Sacred Sites

She has two sacred shrines:

  • Her tomb at San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina
  • The roadside site of her murder
