Magickal Properties

Amida Buddha is the Japanese name for Amitabha, who vowed to save, without exception, even the lowest, most perverse, wicked person. Amida rescues souls and brings them to Pure Land, the Western Paradise over which he presides. Calling the name of Amida Buddha in trust and devotion, especially at the moment of death, allegedly guarantees salvation and passage to the Pure Land.

Ritual Uses

The belief that repetitive recitations of his name enables humans to reach the Pure Land after death evolved into Nembutsu, a system by which one could not only save one's own soul but could also send malevolent spirits and angry ghosts to Pure Land, purifying them and effectively banishing them in a kindly manner.

Offerings

Repetitive recitations of his name and chanting "Namu Amida Butsu" ("Hail Amida Buddha"). Post the words ideally in Sanskrit or Japanese to protect against ghosts and evil spirits.

Favored People

Theoretically everyone, but Amida is also the special guardian of those born in the Chinese Years of the Dog and Pig. Those seeking salvation regardless of their past sins.

Petition For

Salvation, passage to Pure Land after death, protection against ghosts and evil spirits, and spiritual purification for oneself and others.

Warnings

Rescues souls and brings them to Pure Land. Calling his name in trust guarantees salvation. Traditional guardian of those born in Chinese Years of Dog and Pig.

Additional Notes

Amida heads an entourage of angels, spirits, and Bodhisattvas to welcome believers to the Western Paradise. Through magick and prayer, dangerous spirits and ghosts were not only banished and exorcised but saved and sent to a better place. His direction is Northwest and his color is red.
