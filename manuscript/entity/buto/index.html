Magickal Properties

Buto (also called Wadjet) is the ancient Egyptian cobra goddess and fierce protector of Lower Egypt. She is among the oldest Egyptian deities, a primordial mother goddess who became the protective serpent on the pharaoh's crown. Buto embodies fierce protective power, sovereignty, the burning eye of Ra, and the dangerous protective aspect of the divine feminine. She spits fire at enemies and her venom protects the righteous while destroying the wicked.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Buto for fierce protection, defense against enemies, sovereignty work, protection of children and the vulnerable, and when needing the power of righteous wrath. She is particularly powerful for mothers protecting their children. Work with her when establishing or defending boundaries, when needing to manifest the dangerous protective aspect of the sacred feminine.

Offerings

Gold (her sacred metal), green offerings (her sacred color), snake imagery, bread and beer, milk, honey, representations of the uraeus (rearing cobra), protective amulets, incense.

Favored People

Rulers and those in positions of authority, mothers and protectors, those defending the vulnerable, guardians of sacred spaces, practitioners of protective magick.

Petition For

Fierce protection from enemies, defense of the innocent and vulnerable, sovereignty and rightful authority, protection of children, victory over enemies, establishing and maintaining boundaries, protective magick, wrathful justice.

Warnings

Buto is a powerful protective goddess but also dangerous when roused. Her aspect as the fiery eye of Ra means she can unleash destruction. Approach with respect and clear intention. Do not invoke her for petty grievances, her power is for true threats and protection of the vulnerable. She is a mother goddess in her protective aspect, fierce as a cobra defending her young.

Additional Notes

Buto manifests as a cobra, often the rearing cobra (uraeus) on the pharaoh's crown, or as a woman with a cobra head or wearing the red crown of Lower Egypt. She is paired with Nekhbet (vulture goddess of Upper Egypt) as the "Two Ladies" protecting all of Egypt. Her oracle at Pe (Buto) was one of Egypt's most important. She is sometimes identified with or as an aspect of the Eye of Ra. Sacred city: Pe (Buto) in the Nile Delta.
