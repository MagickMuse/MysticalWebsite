Magickal Properties

  • Rainbow spirit and benevolent messenger between islands and deities
  • Protection, especially sudden rescue and safe landings
  • Healing through color and energy transmission

Ritual Uses

  • Color‑work and energy‑healing rites calling the rainbow's harmonizing spectrum
  • Petitions to safely bridge distances, reconcile communities, or carry messages

Offerings

Fresh water libations, lei or fresh flowers, small prisms or reflective glass, child-safe sweets left with thanks

Favored People

Healers who work with energy and color, those seeking safe passage or reconciliation, children and their guardians.

Petition For

Protection in travel and falls, healing and vitality via color energy, successful transmission of messages between places and peoples.

Warnings

Approach with kindness and clarity of intent; as a messenger, unclear requests may not reach their destination.

Additional Notes

  • Manifestations: The rainbow itself, or a beautiful woman wrapped in rainbow colors
  • Sacred area: Manoa Valley, Oʻahu
  • Dominion: Liaison among Hawaiian and Tahitian deities; guidance for the Menehune is noted in tradition
