Amoghasiddhi, whose name means "unfailing power," is the fifth dhyanibuddha or meditation buddha in Buddhist tradition, with a known period of worship from circa 500 BCE to the present.

Cult Center

His cult is pan-Asiatic.

Literary Sources

He appears in the Sadhanamala and Tantric ritual texts.

Cosmology

He is one of five mystic spiritual counterparts of a human buddha in Vajrayana Buddhism, a product of the Adibuddha who represents the branch of the cosmos concerned with consciousness. He originates from the green mantra HUM and lives in the northern paradise.

Consort and Companions

His Sakti is Arya-Tara, and he is normally accompanied by two Garudas or dwarfs.

Iconography

  • Color: green
  • Attributes: staff and sometimes a seven-headed snake

Tibetan Lamaism

He is also taken as a tutelary deity in Lamaism, in which case his attributes include a bell, three monkish robes, and a prayer wheel.

Emanations

His emanations include Visvapani and many other minor names.

Related Dhyanibuddhas

See also Aksobhya, Amitabha (Amida Buddha), Ratnasambhava, and Vairocana (Dainichi Nyorai).
