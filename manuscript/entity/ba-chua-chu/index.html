Magickal Properties

Vietnamese Lady of the Realm and storehouse patron who steadies household prosperity, fair trade, and right order. Guardian of travelers, merchants, spirit‑workers, and those displaced seeking refuge.

Ritual Uses

Petition for honest prosperity, safe travel and return, protection of goods, and resolution of debts and contracts. Make vows clearly, keep ledgers clean, and pay back what you promise.

Offerings

Incense, roast pork, salt, fresh water, fruit and flowers, candles, paper clothing, gold leaf; dress her image respectfully.

Favored People

Vietnamese devotees, merchants and business owners, travelers, spirit mediums, and refugees seeking safe passage.

Petition For

Sustainable income, fair dealings and timely payment, protection of stores and ledgers, debt resolution, and safe journeys.

Warnings

Prosperity is reciprocal: keep vows, avoid exploitative or dishonest trade, and return in thanks.
