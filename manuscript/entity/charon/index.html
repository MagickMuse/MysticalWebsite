Magickal Properties

Charon is the ferryman who controls passage across the river threshold between realms of life and death. He's a grim, cranky, merciless spirit who was likely an old Pelasgian wolf-spirit of death, possibly once ruler of the death realm before being demoted to ferryman with the rise of Olympian spirits. Those lacking payment or proper funeral rites are left behind.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Charon when you need to journey to underworld realms or communicate with the dead. Shamans seeking round-trip travel on his ferry need to cultivate relationship with him. He can facilitate proper passage for souls and ensure deceased receive appropriate funeral honors.

Offerings

Coins (traditionally an obolos placed in corpse's mouth), proper funeral ritual preparations, respectful honors for the dead, items acknowledging his authority over death passages.

Favored People

Shamans and psychopomps seeking underworld travel, those ensuring proper funeral rites for the dead, people working with death magick and ancestral communication.

Petition For

Safe passage to underworld realms, communication with deceased spirits, proper funeral rites and honors for dead, protection during death-related magickal work, psychopomp abilities.

Warnings

He's genuinely grim and merciless, approach with proper respect and payment. He only accepts passengers who have received proper funeral rituals. Don't expect kindness, he values duty over compassion.

Additional Notes

Appears in various forms, decrepit elderly man, dark-winged angel, or may have wolf ears. Associated with tawny owl ('Charon's bird'). Prototype for ferryman to afterlife and possibly Grim Reaper. His carved image appears on Athenian tombs. Attributes include oar and double-hammer.
