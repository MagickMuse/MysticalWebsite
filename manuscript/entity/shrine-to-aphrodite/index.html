Aphrodite · Cypris · Cytherea · Venus

Semitic-rooted goddess of love and the sea. Queen of Beasts. Power over desire, beauty, fertility, winds, and destiny. Born of sea foam, she charms gods and mortals alike.

Aphrodite! Blessed goddess of love, I invoke you and ask that you send me love in all its positive and life-affirming forms. Let this day be filled with love!
