Magickal Properties

Aetna is the volcano goddess of Sicily's Mount Etna, the spirit for whom the mountain is named. Etna is the highest mountain in Italy south of the Alps and Europe's most active volcano. She has ultimate control over the volcano and perhaps over all of Sicily, mediating between powerful forces.

Ritual Uses

Aetna brokered the truce between Demeter and Hephaestus when they argued about property rights on Sicily. Work with her for mediation in disputes involving powerful forces or competing claims over territory or resources.

Offerings

Efforts on behalf of Sicily and its people, volcanic stones (obsidian, pumice, lava pieces), and offerings that honor her volcanic nature and connection to the island's well-being.

Favored People

Those connected to Sicily, volcano researchers, geologists, those seeking mediation between powerful forces, and people working with volcanic or earth energies.

Petition For

Control over volcanic forces, mediation in disputes, protection from natural disasters, successful management of powerful natural forces, and harmony between competing interests.

Warnings

Aetna is fiery, temperamental, passionate, and generous. She has ultimate control over the volcano and perhaps over Sicily. Various deities claim marriage to her, showing her desirability and power.

Additional Notes

In ancient days, Mount Etna was highly coveted real estate, making it difficult to unravel her personal mythology as various deities claimed marriage to her. She may hide beneath the mask of Saint Agatha in Christian syncretism. Her role as mediator shows the importance of divine intervention in managing powerful natural forces.
