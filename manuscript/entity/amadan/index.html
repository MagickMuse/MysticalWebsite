Magickal Properties

Amadan may be the most dreaded of all Sidhe. To be touched by Amadan is to be felled by a stroke. Amadan's stroke is no mild stroke; inevitably it is severe, devastatingly debilitating and resists all manner of healing. Even Biddy Early, among the greatest of Ireland's Fairy doctors, was unable to heal Amadan's victims.

Ritual Uses

These are dangerous spirits to be avoided rather than worked with. The best course of action is to avoid him if you see him coming, although as he is a shape-shifter, this is a challenge.

Offerings

None, this is a dangerous spirit that should not be propitiated or encouraged.

Favored People

None, causes harm rather than providing assistance.

Petition For

Protective prayers and avoidance. Constant repetitions of the prayer, "The Lord be between us and harm," counteract his power until you can escape.

Warnings

DANGEROUS: Most dreaded of all Sidhe. Touch causes devastating stroke. Cannot be reasoned with or healed. Active in June. His attack doesn't derive from a casual brush but from deliberately reaching out to touch someone, who is then immediately seized with sudden stroke.

Additional Notes

Unlike most disease spirits, Amadan does not heal the affliction he transmits. Victims may simultaneously suffer lost wits. He is visible at least some of the time and can be a shape-shifter. Because he may be a crazy fool, there is no reasoning with him or successful method of appeal. He is most active and at the peak of his powers in June.
