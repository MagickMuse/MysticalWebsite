Magickal Properties

Deva rivals known as Ashura/Asura: a warlike, powerful class of spirits associated with iron, storms, and cosmic battles.

Ritual Uses

Work with caution for protection in conflict, understanding of adversaries, and balancing combative forces.

Offerings

Incense and disciplined vows; avoid frivolous petitions.

Favored People

Those seeking to master conflict.

Petition For

Protection in strife, clarity about adversaries, restraint of destructive force.

Warnings

Opponents of the Devas; do not provoke; handle with respect. Traditionally demonized; do not romanticize.

Additional Notes

Linked to ancient metalworking castes and myths of the Elixir of Immortality's betrayal.

Encyclopedia Source Notes (Asuras)

The Asuras are sky gods of the Hindu Vedic tradition, identified in the opening of the Rg Veda. In later Hinduism they become demonic, the antagonists of the deva gods.
