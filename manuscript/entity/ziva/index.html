Magical Properties

Siva (also spelled Siwa or Żywie) is a Slavic goddess of life, fertility, and vital energy. She represents the life-giving force that causes seeds to sprout, wombs to quicken, and all living things to flourish. She is the counterpart to Marzanna, the goddess of death and winter.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Siva for fertility magic, vitality, and life force. She is particularly powerful during spring when life returns to the land. Work with her for conception, healthy pregnancy, and agricultural abundance.

Offerings

Grain, flowers, honey, milk, and offerings of living things such as plants, seeds, or sprouts. Offerings should celebrate life and growth.

Favored People

Farmers, those seeking fertility, women trying to conceive, and those honoring the cycles of life and growth.

Petition For

Fertility, life force, vitality, agricultural abundance, healthy pregnancy, spring blessings, renewal of life.

Warnings

Honor her alongside the natural cycles. She is strongest in spring and summer; respect the balance between life and death in Slavic tradition.

Additional Notes

As an embodiment of the Divine Feminine, Siva represents the fertile, creative, life-giving aspect of goddess energy. In the Slavic tradition, she and Marzanna represent the eternal cycle of life and death, growth and decay.
