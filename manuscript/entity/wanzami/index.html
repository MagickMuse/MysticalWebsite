Overview

Wanzami is the official butcher, barber, circumciser, and scarifier (practitioner of cicatrisation) of the Bori. Though these roles may sound mundane, they are traditionally all considered sacred, charged with profound magick and spirituality. As befits a spirit of her importance, Wanzami serves as a judge among the Bori and lives in the House of Sarkin Aljan, Suleimanu, whom she serves as personal adviser.

Family

Her father is Mai Dawa, Owner of the Bush, Chief of the Bori House of Hunters. Her mother, Barhaza, is the youngest sister of Inna, making Suleimanu her uncle. She was raised by her mother's brother, Sarkin Fulani.

Spirit Allies

Wanzami is a close personal friend of Dan Galadima, who exerts considerable influence over her. They appear frequently together, enjoy each other's company, and are venerated together.

Magickal Properties

Afflictions Wanzami causes include baldness, shaving rashes, sensitivity to shaving, painful ingrown hairs prone to infection, and slow healing or failure to heal after surgery; operations gone wrong may be laid at her door.
