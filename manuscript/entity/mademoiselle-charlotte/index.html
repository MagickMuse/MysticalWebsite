Magickal Properties

Mademoiselle Charlotte is a lwa of European origin who speaks only French, not Kreyol. Those she ritually possesses may discover themselves suddenly fluent in stylish French even if they normally cannot speak the language. She epitomizes the stereotypical rich, upscale white woman with emphasis on her more difficult traits.

Ritual Uses

Petition Mademoiselle Charlotte when seeking to develop style, sophistication, or French language abilities. She can be invoked for matters requiring refined European sensibilities or when needing to navigate high-society situations. Her assistance must be carefully coaxed rather than demanded.

Serving Mademoiselle Charlotte

Serve all beverages in champagne flutes and offer food on fine china. She is served in similar fashion to Ezili Freda Dahomey. She is fussy, impatient, and a stickler for etiquette and appropriate behavior. She is capricious and somewhat lazy, not overly hardworking even for those she favors. Her attention span is limited, and she will only help those who appeal to her and only for as long as they continue to appeal to her. She rarely manifests during ceremonies, perhaps due to snobbery or awareness she is not entirely welcome.

If you are very chic or stylish, she may desire you as a pet (the equivalent of a human poodle). If you are persuasive and persistent, she may even do large favors, but her assistance is temporary and conditional on maintaining her interest.
