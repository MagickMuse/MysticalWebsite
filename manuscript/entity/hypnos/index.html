Domains & Sacred Objects

  • Sleep

Hypnos, Lord of Sleep, is renowned for healing insomnia and granting peaceful, restful sleep. He is among the children of Nyx, Goddess of Night, and causes sleep by touching someone with his magick wand or fanning them with his wings. Hypnos lives an insomniac's dream: a dark cave where the sun doesn't shine and the light can't waken him. Beautiful opium poppies grow by the cave's entrance. He sleeps in an exceedingly comfortable feather bed surrounded by black curtains to further block the light.

Ritual Uses

Insomniacs, those who wish to sleep but cannot, can invoke Hypnos' aid. His son/brother/servant Morpheus, Lord of Dreams, makes sure that no one and no noise disturbs Hypnos' slumber. Keep his altar in a dark, quiet, restful place.

Warnings

Anything that disturbs sleep disturbs Hypnos: bright light, noise, and so forth. Keep his altar in a dark, quiet, restful place, a closet if need be. Be cautious with sleep and dream work because Hypnos controls slumber. Do not use sleep magick to coerce or immobilize others because ethical and safety boundaries apply.

Hypnos lives with his twin brother and close companion Thanatos, Lord of Death. The two often work together. Hypnos is perceived as the more tender-hearted, sympathetic of the brothers. One may appeal to Thanatos through him. He manifests as a dark-winged man or a night bird. Images of Hypnos were once popular cemetery monuments and gravestone motifs.
