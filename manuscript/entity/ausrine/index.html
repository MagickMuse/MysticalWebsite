Magickal Properties

Ausriné is the Lithuanian goddess of the Morning Star and radiant dawn. She brings love, beauty, joy, and renewal, rising before the sun to ready the sky.

Ritual Uses

Petition at daybreak for new beginnings, inspiration, and tender happiness.

Offerings

Roses, songs at first light, and pure water.

Favored People

Those seeking renewal, artistry, and gentle joy.

Petition For

Fresh starts, beauty, and inspiration.

Warnings

Maintain light, honest petitions; avoid coercive love workings.

Additional Notes

Daughter of Perkunas and Saulė; closely linked with Venus and seasonal rites at the Vernal Equinox and Summer Solstice.
