Nakada is a Bori spirit. The son of Malam Alhaji, he renounced Islam and was banished from his father's home, and now lives in the Ninth House of Bori Spirits, the House of Pagans.

Last Spirit Summoned

Nakada is an extremely important spirit because he is the last one summoned to Bori ceremonials. Upon arriving, Nakada closes the door. No other Bori follows him.

Mental Illness

The affliction Nakada causes and heals is mental illness.
