Shrine to Thoth

🪶
Thoth · Tehuti · Djehuti · Djehuty · Lord of Divine Words · Lord of Books

Ibis-headed scribe of the gods. Master magician. Inventor of writing, the calendar, alchemy, and the magickal arts. Author of the first book.

🟣
Moon, measure, ibis, crystal. The ink pot, the palette, the scepter, the ankh. Patron of scribes, master of the spoken and written word.
He created himself by speaking his own name. He invented writing, gambling, star-gazing, engineering, geometry, botany, medicine, mathematics, and magick spells.

Offerings

Hymns, prayers, written invocations, ink, palette. Knowledge-work and magickal writings. Original research and clean records.

Correspondences

Manifestations: Ibis-headed man for scribes. Baboon for the master magician. A master shape-shifter, he can be anything.

Attributes: Ink pot, palette, ankh, scepter.

Celestial: The moon and its phases.

Sacred Animals: Ibis, baboon.

Petition For

Knowledge, wisdom, writing, memory, justice, magick, protection, safe passage in the underworld, learning, and creativity. Resolution of impassioned situations through the cool mind.

Favored People

Scribes, secretaries, authors, magicians, shamans, sorcerers, witches, alchemists, occultists, and librarians.

Sacred Days

Thoth's rites move with the moon. He records the result at the weighing of the heart. His primary shrine stood in Khemennu, renamed Hermopolis by the Greeks.

Words of Power

Thoth · Tehuti · Djehuti · Djehuty · Lord of Divine Words · Lord of Books · Hermes Trismegistus

Cosmic Origins

In one account of creation, Thoth self-manifested as an ibis and laid the cosmic egg from which all of existence in Egyptian myth was born. He recorded for the gods, established new laws, and taught the goddess Isis the art of magick.

⚠️
Notes and Cautions

Thoth is kind, patient, and generous, the cool deity who calms impassioned situations. He values precise language and original work. Bring him truth, never plagiarism.
