The Future Buddha

Maitreya is the Buddha of the Future, the bodhisattva who will appear on Earth to teach the dharma when the teachings of Gautama Buddha have been completely forgotten. He is the essence of love and compassion and will be the successor to our Buddha Shakyamuni (Maitreya will be Buddha number five). His name means "loving-kindness" or "friendliness" (metta/maitri), and he embodies the essence of love, compassion, and universal friendship. He currently resides in Tushita Heaven, awaiting the appropriate time to descend to Earth.

As with other messianic figures, over the centuries many have claimed to be Maitreya, but his estimated arrival on Earth is circa 100,000 CE.

Manifestation

Maitreya is typically depicted seated in the Western fashion (with legs down) rather than in lotus position, symbolizing his readiness to descend to Earth. He is often shown with a golden complexion, wearing the ornaments of a bodhisattva, and sometimes holding a water vessel or dharma wheel.

Attributes

  • Primary quality: Metta (loving-kindness)
  • Current residence: Tushita Heaven (the fourth heaven)
  • Future role: The fifth and final Buddha of this world age
  • Color: Often depicted in gold
  • Gesture: Hands in teaching mudra or holding sacred objects

Symbolism

  • The water vessel (representing purification and spiritual nourishment)
  • The dharma wheel (teaching)
  • The stupa or pagoda (enlightenment)
  • White lotus flowers

Magickal and Spiritual Uses

  • Cultivating loving-kindness (metta meditation)
  • Developing compassion
  • Working toward enlightenment
  • Promoting peace and harmony
  • Future-oriented spiritual work
  • Preparing for spiritual transformation

Cultural Variations

  • Chinese (Mi-lo Fo): Often depicted as the "Laughing Buddha" with a large belly, though this is technically a different figure (Budai) who became associated with Maitreya
  • Japanese (Miroku): Highly venerated in Japanese Buddhism
  • Tibetan: Important in Tibetan Buddhist practice and prophecy

Prophecy

According to Buddhist tradition, Maitreya will appear thousands of years in the future when human lifespan has increased greatly, and he will achieve enlightenment and teach the dharma anew.

Working with Maitreya

Rather than traditional petition and offering, connection with Maitreya comes through:

  • Cultivating loving-kindness meditation (metta bhavana)
  • Practicing compassion toward all beings
  • Preparing oneself and the world for future enlightenment through ethical conduct
  • Recognizing the Buddha nature in all beings

Maitreya meditation focuses on radiating unconditional love to all beings.

Iconography

Maitreya is portrayed as a Bodhisattva; he holds a vessel containing the elixir of immortality.

Attribute: Golden stupa (a mound-shaped reliquary, symbolic of enlightenment)
