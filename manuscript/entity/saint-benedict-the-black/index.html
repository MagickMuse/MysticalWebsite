Saint Benedict the Black

❤️‍🩹Healing

Saint Benedict the Black is renowned for healing smallpox, fever, and infectious diseases.

Classification

Roman Catholic saint. Franciscan friar.

Life and History

Benedict was born around 1526 near Messina, Sicily, to parents who had been brought from Africa as slaves. His parents' owner freed Benedict at birth as a reward for their faithful service. Though free, Benedict grew up in poverty and never learned to read or write.

Early Life and Persecution

As a young man, Benedict worked as a day laborer in the fields around San Fratello. He endured constant mockery and abuse because of his race, bearing it all with extraordinary patience and humility. His gentle nature and deep piety attracted notice.

Religious Life

Around age twenty-one, Benedict joined a group of Franciscan hermits led by Jerome Lanza. When Jerome died, Benedict was elected leader despite his youth and illiteracy. When Pope Pius IV ordered the hermit communities to either dissolve or join established orders, Benedict joined the Franciscan Friars Minor at the friary of Santa Maria di Gesù near Palermo.

The Illiterate Superior

Though assigned to work in the kitchen as a cook, Benedict's holiness was so evident that his brothers elected him Guardian (Superior) of the friary—an almost unprecedented honor for an illiterate former slave. He served with such wisdom that nobles and scholars sought his counsel. After completing his term as Superior, he happily returned to the kitchen.

Miracles and Healing

Benedict was credited with numerous miracles during his lifetime and after death:

  • Healing the sick, particularly those with smallpox and fevers
  • Multiplying food to feed the poor
  • Reading the hearts of those who came to him
  • Levitation during prayer

Death and Legacy

Benedict died on April 4, 1589, at age sixty-three. His funeral drew massive crowds. He was beatified in 1743 and canonized in 1807. He is the patron saint of African Americans, Black Catholics, and Palermo.

Iconography

Depicted as a Black Franciscan friar in brown habit, often holding the Christ Child, a crucifix, or a heart.

Attributes

Franciscan habit, crucifix, heart, the Christ Child.

Sacred Sites

  • Church of Santa Maria di Gesù, Palermo, Sicily (where his relics rest)
  • San Fratello, Sicily (his birthplace)

Feast Day

April 4

Related Entities

Saint Francis of Assisi, Saint Martin de Porres
