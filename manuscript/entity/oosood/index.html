Serbian Birth-Spirit Fairies

Oosood are Serbian birth-spirit fairies, a sub-species of the Vila. They arrive on the seventh day after a birth to pronounce the newborn's destiny.

Sacred Timing

Oosood appear on the seventh day after birth, a different timing than other birth-spirit fairies, reflecting Serbian folk tradition's distinct sacred calendar.

Unique Visibility

Only the mother can see the Oosood when they arrive. This exclusive visibility creates a private spiritual encounter during the vulnerable postpartum period, linking the mother's perception to the child's fate.

Connection to Vila

As a sub-species of Vila (Slavic female nature spirits), Oosood maintain connections to broader Serbian and Slavic spiritual traditions while specializing in birth-fate determination.

Etymology

Fairy comes from Old French fée, derived from Latin fatua (female seer) and fatum (fate or destiny). Oosood represent the Serbian expression of the pan-European tradition of birth-spirit fairies who determine destiny.

Sleeping Beauty Connection

The fairy tale of Sleeping Beauty describes actual spiritual rituals: offering tables laid for fairies who bestow a baby's fate at birth celebrations. Oosood are the Serbian manifestation of this tradition.

Petition

Prepare offerings on the seventh day, focusing especially on flowers, which Oosood particularly appreciate in addition to traditional food offerings. Create a clean, beautiful space that honors both the mother's recovery and the spirits' arrival. Only the mother will see them, so her testimony should be respected.
