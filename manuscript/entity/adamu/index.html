Magickal Properties

Adamu is the primordial female principle in Chaldean tradition, the source of life and female womb representing the metaphoric "red earth" from which new life is formed. Her name is etymologically the same as the biblical Adam. As Adam is the primal father, Adamu is the primal mother. She is an ancient blood goddess who oversees menstruation and childbirth.

Ritual Uses

Create private, sacred rituals honoring the feminine life-force and menstrual cycle. Work with her for fertility rites and childbirth blessings. Honor her through earth-based ceremonies connecting to the primal feminine creative force.

Offerings

Libations of menstrual blood poured onto Earth, burial of afterbirth as thanks. These offerings connect directly to her domain over feminine creative power and life-giving blood.

Favored People

Women and menstruating individuals seeking to regulate cycles, conceive, or delay menopause. Midwives and those involved in childbirth. People working with sacred feminine mysteries and blood magic.

Petition For

Regulation of menstrual cycles, stimulation of desired menstruation, fertility support, delaying menopause as needed, assistance with childbirth, and connection to primal feminine creative power.

Warnings

Offer menstrual libations or bury afterbirth with ritual care and avoid shaming or publicizing these rites because Adamu is a primordial blood-fertility mother whose offerings are sacred and private.

Additional Notes

Her red color symbolizes both menstrual blood and the red earth of creation. She represents the most ancient understanding of feminine creative power and the sacred nature of women's biological cycles. Her worship honors the life-giving aspects of feminine blood mysteries.
