The Balam, whose name means "jaguar," are Mayan Yucatec guardian deities from the classical Mesoamerican tradition of Mexico.

Domains

Protection of individuals in daily life, guardianship of villages and dwellings, and defense against dangerous animals and thieves.

Attributes

Four Balam stand at the cardinal points around a village to guard against dangerous animals. They also protect the four sides of a milpa, or smallholding, against thieves.

Lore

They are poorly defined spirits whose presence is felt in the everyday protection of home and land.
