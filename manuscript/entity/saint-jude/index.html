Classification

Venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity.

Saint Jude is renowned for healing addiction and helping those struggling to break free from substance dependencies.

The Miraculous Saint

Are you desperate? Does your cause seem utterly lost? Does whatever you require seem impossible? If you don't know where to turn and doubt anyone can help, it may be time to invoke Saint Jude, the patron of hopeless causes and desperate people.

The Two Judases

Jude is an abbreviated form of Judas. Jesus had two Apostles named Judas: Judas Iscariot and Judas Thaddeus. There was so much confusion that their identities began to merge and intertwine. The Church began referring to Judas Thaddeus as Jude to minimize confusion.

Devotees avoided Saint Jude because he reminded them of Judas the betrayer. During a time when witches consorting with Satan were burned at the stake, it was considered wisest to stay far from anyone with even tenuous associations with Satan.

Jude's Vow

Poor Saint Jude was reputedly extremely despondent over this lack of veneration. Eager to take his place among the miracle-producing saints, he vowed to fulfill any sincere request directed to him, provided it wasn't harmful or illicit.

Perhaps over the centuries, Saint Jude has bent his rule about illicit requests. The drug traffickers of Medellin, Colombia, have historically demonstrated deep devotion to Saint Jude. Thieves and drug users also call on him, reputedly with success.

That Saint Jude was able to rehabilitate himself and emerge as one of the most popular of all saints is testament to his ability to work miracles.

Relationship to Jesus

Judas Thaddeus was the brother of James the Just, so depending on interpretation, Jude may be the cousin, full brother, stepbrother, or half-brother of Jesus. According to legend and iconography, Saint Jude bore a strong physical resemblance to Jesus. Jude was reputedly a powerful healer and exorcist while alive.

Ritual to Request Assistance

  1. Place a fresh bunch of basil in water before his image every day for nine days (nine bunches total)
  2. Light a small green candle each day for nine days, or one large nine-day novena candle
  3. Pray, petition, and invoke Saint Jude's aid

Traditional Vow

The traditional vow in exchange for favors granted is to testify publicly of miracles received. Standard fulfillment involves placing an ad in a newspaper's classified section. It must be public - you need not sign your full name, but you must testify on behalf of Saint Jude. It is sufficient to write: "Thank you, Saint Jude, for the favors given."

Manifestation

Saint Jude is robed in green and bears a sacred medal on his chest.

Iconography

The flame of enlightenment shoots from Saint Jude's brow.

Correspondences

Sacred day: Sunday, also the 28th of each month. Number: 28.

Attributes

Anchor, oar, boat hook (he was a fisherman, but also signs of hope and help), club (he was bludgeoned to death), axe, sword.

Gemstone

Chryosprase.

Botanicals

Basil, mint, patchouli, scattered starwort (Aster passiflora).

Feast Days

  • June 19 (Eastern Church)
  • October 28 (Roman Catholic)

Sacred Sites

  • Crypt of Saint Peter's Basilica in the Vatican (his body reputedly lies here)
  • Church of San Hipolito, Mexico City (statue renowned for producing miracles)
  • Saint Jude Thaddeus Shrine in Chicago
  • International Shrine of Saint Jude in Our Lady of Guadalupe Chapel, the oldest church building in New Orleans
