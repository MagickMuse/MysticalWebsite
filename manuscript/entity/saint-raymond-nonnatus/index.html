Saint Raymond Nonnatus is renowned for healing difficult childbirths, preventing miscarriage, and silencing slander and harmful speech.

Saint Raymond Nonnatus (1204-1246) was a Catalan saint whose name means "not born," referring to his Caesarian birth after his mother died in childbirth. He joined the Mercedarian Order and traveled to Algiers to ransom Christian slaves.

Domains

Raymond assists with difficult childbirths and prevents miscarriage. He also defends the falsely accused and terminates slander, gossip, and dangerous speech. His padlocked lips make him particularly effective in silencing harmful tongues.

Iconography

Raymond is depicted with his attributes: a padlock and keys, symbolizing his torture and his power to lock and unlock mouths.

Lore

When Raymond ran out of ransom funds in Algiers, he exchanged himself for a slave. During his imprisonment, he converted several Muslim jailers to Christianity. Condemned to death, his sentence was commuted to running a gauntlet. A hot iron poker bored a hole through his lips, and a padlock was inserted to silence him. After eight months, he was freed and returned to Spain. He died at age thirty-six while traveling to Rome to receive a cardinal's hat.
