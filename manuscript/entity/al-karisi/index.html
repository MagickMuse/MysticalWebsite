Magickal Properties

Al Karisi, known as "The Red Wife" or "The Scarlet Woman," is a deadly vampiric spirit from pre-Islamic Turkic shamanism who craves the inner organs of horses and humans. This malevolent entity is particularly dangerous to newborns and their mothers, though she also targets children. She is a stealth killer who prefers magickal attack over brute force, with powers including hypnosis, paralysis, and shape-shifting.

Ritual Uses

Al Karisi cannot be worked with safely. She is approached only for banishment and protection. Her attacks manifest as sudden, unexplained death (similar to SIDS) or chronic wasting illness that resists medical treatment. She targets sleeping victims, making vigilance crucial for protection.

Offerings

Protective iron amulets, sacred texts. Do not attempt to appease Al Karisi - focus on protective measures instead of offerings to her.

Favored People

None - Al Karisi is universally malevolent and dangerous to all, especially newborns, nursing mothers, and children.

Petition For

Protection from Al Karisi's attacks, banishment of her influence, safeguarding of vulnerable family members, and healing from her effects.

Warnings

Protect newborns and nursing mothers by keeping them alert and watched because Al Karisi is a stealthy, vampiric killer of infants and prefers sleeping victims. Use amulets, talismans, and vigilant guardianship rather than solitary rituals. She can render victims conscious but unable to speak, move, or scream for help.

Additional Notes

Al Karisi typically appears as a large, terrifying woman dressed in red, but can also take the forms of birds, cats, dogs, or foxes. Most dangerously, she can alter her appearance to resemble someone the victim knows and trusts. Can theoretically be stopped by staking through the heart with wooden stake or silver needle. She may cause paralysis, sudden sensory loss, children's illnesses, and fertility problems.
