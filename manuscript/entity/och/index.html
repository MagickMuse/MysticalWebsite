Och is the Olympic Spirit of the Sun, one of seven planetary spirits from the Medieval grimoire tradition. These beings represent the pagan energies of celestial bodies and predate the angelic and demonic classifications of later Ceremonial Magick.

Attributes

Planet: Sun ☉

Metal: Gold

Day: Sunday

Powers: Wisdom, healing, longevity, dignity, transmutation of base metals to gold

Provinces: Rules 28 Olympic provinces

Legions: Commands 36,536 legions

Working with Och

Och may be invoked to lend solar power to spellwork. The spirit's sigil can be inscribed on candles, drawn on paper pentacles with spell materials placed atop, or written onto paper with charm words and burned or added to pouches.

Associated Names

Names of Planetary Spirits (Sun): Shadiel ("Horned One")

Names of Power: Nakiel, Sorath

Source

Arbatel de Magia Veterum (1575)
