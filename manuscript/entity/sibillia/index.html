Magickal Properties

According to Italian legend, the Cumaean Sibyl took refuge in a cavern in the Apennines. Her underground paradise is reached through a grotto filled with snakes in the mountains of Norcia, a region renowned for its witches and mushrooms. In this cavern, Sibilla teaches the magickal arts to those who wish to learn them.

A Christian rendition of Sibilla's legend acknowledges her powers of prophecy but claims that the Sybil didn't enter her cave voluntarily but was instead locked in as punishment for her arrogance in believing that she, not the Virgin Mary, was destined to bear the Messiah. It's possible that this refers to now-suppressed claims that the Sybil would bear a holy child who would be a savior. By the Middle Ages, the ancient prophetess had emerged as a goddess of witches. In Ferrara, Italy, Sibilla as La Signora del Corso presided over the witches' flight and witches' ball. At feast's end, she touched all bottles and platters with her golden wand and they immediately replenished. Witches gathered bones from completed meals, placed them within animal skins, and Sibilla touched the rolled-up skins with her wand, and the animals returned to life.

Ritual Uses

Seek Sibilla for magickal instruction and witchcraft training. Work with her for abundance magick using the golden wand that replenishes all it touches. Invoke her for transformation and resurrection magick, as demonstrated in the bone-to-life ritual. Her shrine at the Ridge of the Sibillini below Mount Vettore was visited by Europeans throughout the fifteenth century, some bringing grimoires to consecrate at Sibilla's lake.

Offerings

Pilgrimages to her shrine (though Church excommunicated those who did so). Grimoires brought to consecrate at her lake. Participation in the witches' feast and ball.

Favored People

Witches, those seeking magickal instruction, pilgrims defiant of Church authority, those seeking abundance and transformation.

Petition For

Magickal instruction and witchcraft training, prophecy and divination, abundance and replenishment, transformation and resurrection magick, entry to the witches' paradise.

Warnings

Stay in Sibilla's cavern for over a year and you remain ageless and alive amidst abundance and revelry, but you can never leave, trapped forever in a witches' paradise. Sibilla sprouts a snake's tail every Saturday. At night, all inhabitants of her paradise may turn into snakes. The Wandering Jew allegedly transformed into a snake and serves as her door guardian. Some legends claim one must have sex with snakes to gain admittance to Sibilla's Cave.

Additional Notes

Sibilla is a hospitable spirit. Diana and Fata Morgana frequently live with her. Those who defied Church decree and visited her shrine were allegedly blessed by Sibilla with a lifetime of joy. The real-life shrine existed during the fifteenth century. By the end of that century, the Church had a standing order to excommunicate anyone who made the pilgrimage to Norcia.
