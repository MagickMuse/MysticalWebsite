Magickal Properties

The Erotes are the many-winged spirits of love’s play: a host of youthful cupids who stir affection, delight, mischief, and the soul’s remembering. They attend Aphrodite and move between mortals with laughter, fragrance, and song, turning hearts toward joy and tender connection.

Ritual Uses

Invite the Erotes to brighten courtship, renew sweetness in established bonds, and inspire art, music, and poetry of love. Their presence suits festivals, dances, and gentle rites that honor beauty, fragrance, and play.

Offerings

Flowers and garlands, perfumes, fruit and honeyed sweets, music and dance offered in a spirit of consent and joy.

Favored People

Lovers, celebrants, dancers, artists, and those seeking lighthearted affection and mutual charm.

Petition For

Soul-guided attraction, playful romance, reconciliation through kindness, and creative inspiration in love arts.

Warnings

Do not attempt binding or coercive love works. The Erotes delight in free hearts and willful consent; compulsion provokes chaotic consequences.

Additional Notes

Also known as the Cupids. Often appear in joyous groups, garlanded and winged, accompanying Aphrodite.
