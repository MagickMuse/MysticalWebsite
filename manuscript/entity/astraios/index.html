Magickal Properties

Astraios, Lord of Stars and Planets, governs the night sky and the arts of astrology and astronomy. He illuminates new celestial knowledge and guides seekers by starlight.

Ritual Uses

Petition for guidance, discovery, and understanding of stellar movements; for wise timing and navigation.

Offerings

Star‑gazing vigils, recorded observations, and orderly sky‑craft.

Favored People

Astrologers, astronomers, navigators, and sky‑watchers.

Petition For

Guidance, insight, discovery, and right timing.

Warnings

Approach with disciplined study and humility before the cosmos.

Additional Notes

Father of winds and stars in some traditions; consort to Eos.
