Judge of Witchcraft Accusations

Orisha Oko is the Judge who settles disputes, especially witchcraft accusations among women. He serves as a wise arbiter when communities are torn apart by suspicions of malevolent magic.

Wise Sorcerer of Earth Magick

Orisha Oko is a wise sorcerer of Earth magick who protects against witchcraft and heals its effects. His power comes from the earth itself: the soil, the crops, the land that sustains life.

Center of Justice at Irawo

His main cult center was at Irawo in northwest Yorubaland, an important shrine for settling witchcraft accusations. People traveled from far and wide to seek his judgment when no other authority could resolve accusations of harmful magick.

Female Devotees and Hereditary Links

His devotees are largely female, and devotion to Orisha Oko is hereditary. Witchcraft accusations forge links to this orisha that continue through generations. Those accused of witchcraft, whether guilty or innocent, become bound to him through the act of accusation itself.

Gender and Forms

Orisha Oko is variously described as female or male. Most commonly represented as a hunter who decided to farm, embodying the transition from wild game to cultivated crops.

Agricultural Magic

He is closely associated with agricultural magick rituals. His power over the land determines whether the earth yields bounty or remains barren, whether women bear children or remain childless.

Partnership with Ogun

Orisha Oko works closely with Ogun, who forges his tools. The hoe blade that turns the soil comes from Ogun's forge, representing the partnership between earth magick and metal craft.

Divine Anger

Orisha Oko manifests his anger through unyielding, infertile Earth and barren, infertile women. When he withdraws his blessing, nothing grows and no children are born.

Sacred Attributes

Attribute: Iron stave made from hoe blade

Colors: Red and white

Offerings: Yams
