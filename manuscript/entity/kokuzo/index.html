Kokuzo is a bodhisattva of memory and wisdom.

Akasagarbha: The Mahayana and Lamaist Origin

In the Buddhist (Mahayana) and Lamaist Tibetan traditions, this deity is known as Akasagarbha, meaning "essence of the sky." An alternate Sanskrit form of the name is Khagarbha. In Japanese Buddhism, Akasagarbha becomes the god Kokuzo.

Astral Domain

Akasagarbha is classified as an astral god and counted among the bodhisattvas, the spiritual meditation buddhas. He is said to live in the "womb of the sky."
