Hekate · Trivia · Bendidia

Dark Moon Goddess. Queen of the Night. Ruler of Heaven, Earth, and the Underworld. Queen of Witches. Keyholder of the world. Guardian of thresholds and three-way crossroads.

Hail, many-named mother of the gods, whose children are fair
Hail, mighty Hekate of the threshold, keyholder of the world
Hail to thee, Enodia, keeper of the four- and three-way crossroads
Nether, Nocturnal, and Infernal one
I beckon to you
Grant me the pleasure of your presence

Sacred Dates

Night of Hekate · 16 November

Recalling that Hekate was once a great goddess complete with temples and shrines, venerated by many, not only her spiritual daughters, witches.

Dark Moon · monthly

The lunar phase sacred to her dream work, divination, and shadow practice.

Samhain · 31 October

Optimal timing alongside the Dark Moon for inaugurating Hekate's Pillow and other dream charms.
