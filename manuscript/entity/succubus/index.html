The Demon Lover

Beneath a beautiful and alluring appearance the succubus may hide bat-like wings, horns, cloven hoofs, and a forked tail, features that are easily overlooked. She is a demon who takes the form of a lovely woman and delights in seducing men in their sleep, drawing them into intercourse. She is said to take particular pleasure in tormenting monks and other men of the cloth. The victim's sexual energy nourishes her, though the act may drain a man so utterly that he dies of it. Lilith, named in legend as the first wife of Adam, was demonized for refusing to obey her husband and has likewise been called a succubus.

The Sign Over the Door

In the British Isles a number of inns dating to the sixteenth century still bear the effigy of a succubus set discreetly above the door, once a secret sign that the house was also a brothel.

The Desert Punisher

In the Middle East the succubus is understood somewhat differently. Still a beautiful woman to look upon, she wanders the desert, and her purpose is to punish adulterers by seducing the guilty man. At the moment of union she severs his penis with razor blades hidden within her, and then devours him alive.
