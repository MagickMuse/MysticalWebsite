Magickal Properties

Abeona is the spirit of literal and metaphoric departures, governing all forms of leaving and transitions. She has dominion over first departures from home, educational journeys, marriage transitions, military service, and even death as the ultimate departure. She works in conjunction with her sister Adeona to teach babies to walk.

Ritual Uses

Create sacred space incorporating gate and road imagery to honor her domain over thresholds and departures. Present petitions before journeys begin so she oversees the very first steps. Work with Adeona for children having difficulty learning to walk.

Offerings

Traveler's talismans and protective amulets for journeys. Present offerings at thresholds and doorways before departure. Create or consecrate items that will accompany travelers on their journeys.

Favored People

Parents with children facing first departures, travelers embarking on journeys, those facing major life transitions, military families, and students leaving for education.

Petition For

Safety for children during first departures from home, protection during travel and journeys, assistance with all types of departures (marriage, military, education, death), help for children learning to walk, protection for children in comas or distant situations.

Warnings

Present petitions and offerings before departure begins, not retroactively. Abeona governs departures and is most effective when invoked at the threshold moment rather than after the journey has already commenced.

Additional Notes

Her name derives from "to depart" or "go forth" but also references death, making her appropriate for protection beyond the realm of living. She safeguards children whose souls may have temporarily departed, such as in comas or emotional distance situations.
