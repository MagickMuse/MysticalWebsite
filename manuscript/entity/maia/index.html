Overview

Maia is the eldest and most beautiful of the seven Pleiades, the daughters of Atlas and Pleione. Her name means "mother" or "nurse," and she embodies the nurturing aspects of motherhood and the renewal of spring. She is the mother of Hermes by Zeus and is honored as the Queen of May, with the month of May named in her honor.

Manifestation

Maia appears as a beautiful, mature woman often associated with springtime, flowers, and new growth. She radiates maternal warmth and nurturing energy.

Attributes

  • Sacred month: May (named after her)
  • Sacred day: May 1st (Beltane/May Day)
  • Celestial form: One of the seven stars of the Pleiades constellation
  • Title: Queen of May
  • Element: Earth

Family

  • Parents: Atlas (father) and Pleione (mother)
  • Sisters: The other six Pleiades
  • Son: Hermes/Mercury (by Zeus/Jupiter)

Magickal Uses

  • Fertility magick and conception
  • Motherhood and nurturing
  • Spring planting and agricultural magick
  • Growth and abundance spells
  • Renewal and fresh starts

Sacred Sites

Mount Cyllene in Arcadia, Greece (where she gave birth to Hermes).

Roman Aspect

In Roman tradition, Maia was also associated with an ancient Italian goddess of spring and growth. On May 1st, the flamen of Vulcan made sacrifices to her, and May 15th was sacred to her as well.

Symbolism

Spring flowers, the month of May, the Pleiades star cluster, motherhood, growth, and abundance.
