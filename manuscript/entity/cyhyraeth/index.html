Magickal Properties

Cyhyraeth ('The Weeper' or 'The Griever') is a Banshee-like spirit from Glamorganshire, Wales. She is rarely seen but frequently heard crying and moaning as a death omen. Some are associated with specific families and wail when members die far from home, while others weep for multiple deaths affecting entire communities, foretelling epidemics or disasters.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Cyhyraeth for warnings of impending danger or disaster. She doesn't cause harm but provides advance notice if you can interpret her signs. Work with her at crossroads and near water sources where she's most active. She can guide souls of those who died in shipwrecks to proper rest.

Offerings

Candles placed at crossroads where she's been heard, offerings left near water sources (especially coastal areas), memorial tokens for those who died far from home, recognition of her guidance for lost souls.

Favored People

Families with whom she has hereditary connections, communities facing potential disasters, coastal peoples vulnerable to shipwrecks, those who honor the restless dead.

Petition For

Warnings of impending danger or death, protection from epidemics and disasters, guidance for souls who died far from home, interpretation of omens and signs, comfort for the grieving.

Warnings

She is a harbinger, not a killer, don't blame her for the deaths she foretells. Her weeping may predict epidemics or disasters affecting whole communities. Learn to interpret her warnings rather than fear them.

Additional Notes

Frequents crossroads and lingers near water, especially along Glamorganshire coast before shipwrecks. Wanders accompanied by corpse-lights (will-o'-the-wisps). In Christian interpretation, guides souls of shipwreck victims to nearest church cemetery.
