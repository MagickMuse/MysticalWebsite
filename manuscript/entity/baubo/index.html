Magickal Properties

  • Greek goddess who made Demeter laugh during her grief over Persephone's abduction
  • Spirit of bawdy humor, female sexuality, and the power of laughter to heal sorrow
  • Embodies the sacred power of anasuromai (ritual exposure/lifting of skirts)
  • Breaks through grief and depression with shocking, liberating humor

Ritual Uses

  • Invoke for relief from depression, grief, and paralyzing sorrow
  • Use bawdy humor and uninhibited expression to shake loose stagnant energy
  • Petition for empowerment through reclaiming sexuality and bodily autonomy
  • Work with her for breaking taboos around female bodies and desires

Offerings

Humorous offerings, representations of female genitalia, ribald poetry or songs, figs

Favored People

Those struggling with grief or depression, women reclaiming bodily autonomy, comedians, performers

Petition For

Relief from sorrow, healing through laughter, empowerment, breaking free from shame, reconciliation with joy

Warnings

Her methods involve shocking, transgressive humor that challenges propriety. Not for those uncomfortable with explicit sexuality or bawdy expression.

Additional Notes

  • Part of the Eleusinian Mysteries; her gesture to Demeter was considered sacred revelation
  • Associated with Bona Dea (Roman "Good Goddess"), Uzume (Japanese), and Hathor (Egyptian)
  • Represents the healing power of transgressive female sexuality and humor
  • Her name may relate to female genitalia
