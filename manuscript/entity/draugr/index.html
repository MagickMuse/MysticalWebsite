Magickal Properties

The Draugr are the living dead, animated corpses who emerge from the grave to roam about, causing havoc, trouble, and mayhem. They are not mindless zombies but develop magickal powers, becoming oracular and possessing information from past, present, and future. Some Draugr can shape-shift, making them even more dangerous as they gain access to people and places not normally given to walking, decaying corpses.

Ritual Uses

There are no beneficial ritual uses for Draugr, they are uniformly hostile. Rituals exist to prevent their rising or to stop them once they have risen. Precautions include binding the corpse to the grave with magickal incantations and bindrunes, tying the corpse's big toes together to hobble it, or removing the corpse via special corpse doors.

Offerings

No offerings are appropriate for Draugr, they are hostile undead spirits that cannot be placated.

Favored People

None. Draugr are hostile toward the living in general, although they tend to target their own relatives first.

Petition For

Protection from the undead, proper burial rites to prevent rising, assistance in stopping active Draugr, safe passage during nighttime when they are active.

Warnings

The Draugr is simply unable to rest in peace and is angry, resentful, and violent. They are insatiably, eternally hungry and cannot be sated. At daybreak they return to their graves only to rise again at night. If one does rise, it must be staked through the heart or beheaded with its own sword, then cremated with ashes dispersed at sea.

Additional Notes

Draugr chase livestock until the animals drop, sometimes riding them. They torment and torture people, especially those walking alone at night. They look like decaying cadavers that become huge, bloated, and very heavy, emanating the stench of death. Many are shape-shifters. Their activity tends to increase in winter.
