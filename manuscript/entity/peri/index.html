Magickal Properties

Peri are Iranian fairies, tiny, sweet, gossamer beings nourished solely by the aromas of fragrant flowers and trees. They embody delicacy and beauty, representing the ethereal and refined aspects of the fairy realm.

Iranian Dualism

Iran is the home of dualism; the philosophy permeates their fairy tales, too. The Peri exist in constant opposition to malevolent spirits, representing the eternal struggle between light and darkness, good and evil.

The Threat of Deevs

Malicious spirits called deevs (possibly the root origin of "devil") constantly attempt to capture peris by hanging iron cage-traps from treetops. When peris accidentally fly into these iron cages, they become trapped and helpless.

Sisterhood and Mutual Aid

Their sister spirits, however, sustain these trapped peris by feeding them perfume, which in turn repulses the deevs so that they are unable to complete their nefarious plans. This demonstrates the power of sisterhood and mutual aid among fairy spirits.

Ritual Uses

Work with Peri for beauty magic, delicate and refined workings, and understanding the power of sisterhood and mutual protection. Their vulnerability and the care they show each other provides a model for protective magic and community support.

Offerings

Perfume, fragrant flowers, aromatic essences, incense, protection of delicate spirits from those who would trap or harm them.

Warnings

Peri are extremely delicate and vulnerable to iron. They require protection and cannot be worked with through force or coercion. Their nourishment comes only from fragrances, not from physical food.
