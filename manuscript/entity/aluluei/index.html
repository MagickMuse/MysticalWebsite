Magickal Properties

Aluluei is Lord of Knowledge and Navigation who taught his people how to make outrigger canoes, which traditionally carry a wooden image of Aluluei. He possesses all knowledge of the sea and sailing, and is known for sharing rather than hoarding information. He has two faces: one facing forward to see where he's going and one facing backward, watchful of danger approaching from behind.

Ritual Uses

Work with Aluluei for navigation and knowledge-sharing activities. One day, his daughter discovered three deities who gave her a navigational chart marking all birds, fish, and islands. Rather than hoarding this knowledge, Aluluei shared it with people, teaching them sea lore and navigation skills.

Offerings

Navigational charts, offerings to sea spirits, and items that honor his tradition of knowledge sharing. Present offerings that acknowledge the importance of sharing wisdom rather than hoarding it.

Favored People

Navigators, sailors, sea captains, those seeking knowledge, and people committed to sharing wisdom with others rather than hoarding information.

Petition For

Navigation skills, sea lore, knowledge sharing, safe sea travel, wisdom in maritime endeavors, and guidance in teaching others.

Warnings

Lord of Knowledge and Navigation. Taught people to make outrigger canoes. Has two faces, one forward and one backward. His lower body is that of a stingray.

Additional Notes

His upper body is that of a man, but his lower body is that of a stingray. His colors are red, black, and white. He represents the principle that knowledge becomes more valuable when shared rather than hoarded, and demonstrates the importance of both forward vision and vigilant awareness of potential dangers.
