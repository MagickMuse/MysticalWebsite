Magickal Properties

Alseids are Nymphs whose name derives from Alsos, literally meaning "grove." They are technically Nymphs of groves but are also sometimes associated with grassy meadows, mountain glens, and lightly forested areas. As spirits of groupings of trees, they tend to appear in groups themselves, unlike more solitary Nymphs.

Ritual Uses

Work with Alseids in their natural habitats of groves and meadows for group activities and celebrations. They enjoy group activities and have a good time together, making them suitable for group rituals and celebrations in natural settings.

Offerings

Offerings in groves, meadows, and forested areas. They appreciate group celebrations and communal activities in their natural habitats.

Favored People

Those who enjoy nature, groups working together, and travelers seeking guidance. People who appreciate the playful aspects of nature spirits and those who work well in group settings.

Petition For

Group harmony, connection to nature, protection in wild places, guidance on forest paths, and assistance with maintaining positive group dynamics.

Warnings

Nymphs of groves who appear in groups. Have reputation as pranksters who play tricks on travelers. While generally harmless, their pranks should be expected when traveling through their territories.

Additional Notes

Eurydice, Orpheus' love, may be an Alseid. These spirits represent the playful, social aspects of forest magick and the importance of community among nature spirits. They embody the group consciousness of forest groves and the collaborative spirit of trees growing together.
