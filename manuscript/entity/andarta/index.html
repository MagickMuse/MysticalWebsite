Andarta is a probable fertility goddess of the Celtic Gaulish tradition. She is the patron goddess of the Vocontii tribe.

Name

Her name seems to have derived either from artos, meaning bear, or from ar, meaning ploughed land. The two roots point to the two faces by which the Vocontii likely knew her, the wild strength of the bear and the cultivated fertility of the worked field.

See Also

The name and figure of Andarta may be linked to Andraste, the Romano-Celtic British war goddess, although the two are venerated in distinct regions and contexts.
