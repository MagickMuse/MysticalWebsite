Adibuddha, the primeval buddha, is the original buddha in the Buddhist cosmos. He is the primordial force from whom the five dhyanibuddhas arose, and the embodiment of the concept of emptiness. Some authorities consider him identical with Vaharaja and Vajrasattva. His image, sitting on a lotus leaf, is often carried by other Buddhist deities.

Additional Notes

  • Epithets include Svabhava (self-creating) and Svayambhu (self-enlightened)
