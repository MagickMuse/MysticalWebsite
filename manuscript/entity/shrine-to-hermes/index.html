⚕️Shrine to Hermes

Hermes · Mercury · Lord of Animal Husbandry · Patron of Euthanasia

Trickster of the crossroads. Shaman, psychopomp, herald. Lord of language, communication, trade, travel, and divination.

🟠
The four-way crossroads. The herm of stones. The traveler's broad-brimmed hat and winged sandals. The caduceus entwined by two serpents.
Born in a cave to Maia, in Mekone the Poppytown. He invented the alphabet, the musical scale, weights and measures. He travels between realms and brings dreams.

Drawn from Hermes

🕯️
Leave Your Devotion

Erect a small cairn of stones at a crossroads, threshold, or doorway. Speak your bargain plainly. Leave honey, a coin, or cheese.

Offerings

At a four-way crossroads: cakes, honey, olives, goat or sheep cheese, wine, water, incense.

Correspondences

Animals: Dog, tortoise, snake. Horned livestock: cows, goats, sheep.

Tree: Palm.

Number: 4.

Attribute: Caduceus, a staff entwined by two snakes. He uses it to induce sleep.

Spirit Allies: Maia, Pan (his son), the Nymphs.

Petition For

The gift of gab, business success, true omens, mother-wit and cunning. Safe travel, escort of the dead, prophetic dreams or relief from nightmares. Help with troublesome ghosts.

Favored People

Merchants, gamblers, travelers. Clever non-violent thieves, those who live by their wits, boxers. Shamans and magicians who walk between realms.

Sacred Days

Sacrifices to Hermes were made on the final day of Greek festivals of the dead so he would escort souls back to Hades. Approach him at dusk, at thresholds, and at four-way crossroads.

Words of Power

Hermes · Mercury · Hermes Trismegistus · Killer of Argus · Lord of the Crossroads · The Magician

Notes and Cautions

Be explicit in bargains and leave clear offerings at roads and thresholds. He punishes ambiguous bargains. Never use him to conceal theft or harm. He enjoys practical jokes and is troublesome to those without humor.
