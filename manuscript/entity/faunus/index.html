Magickal Properties

Faunus is among the most ancient indigenous Italian spirits and is known as "The Benefactor." Faunus epitomizes Earth's irrepressible male generative force. He is a spirit of the forest and wild nature, representing the innate fertility of land and people, a surging force that cannot be contained.

Faunus is a primordial spirit. He is so ancient that he cannot communicate as a human does but speaks through forest noises and nature sounds. His presence is often made manifest by nightmares even when he is bringing good news. Faunus is by nature a benevolent rather than a harmful spirit, however he is wild and uncontrollable. Once upon a time, his priests served as mediums, interpreting Faunus' oracles and communiqués.

Faunus manifests as a horned human male cloaked in a wolf-skin or with a human's upper torso and the lower body of a goat, similar to Pan and the satyrs.

Ritual Uses

Work with Faunus for fertility of land and people, healing of infertility, psychic abilities, and protection of livestock from wolves and vice versa. His devotees once slept in his sacred precincts, dressed in the skins of freshly sacrificed lambs, in hopes of receiving dreams from the god.

Invoke Faunus for oracular dreams and visions, though be prepared for these to manifest as nightmares or disturbing imagery. This is how he communicates. His priests traditionally served as mediums to interpret his messages.

Offerings

Spring water, offerings on behalf of wolves (especially Italian wolves), preservation of wild nature. Alcohol is discouraged, as Faunus is already uncontrollable. His attributes include a goblet and a wreath.

Favored People

Women petitioning for fertility, shepherds, hunters, those who seek visions in dreams, priests who can interpret forest sounds and oracles.

Petition For

Fertility of people and land, healing of infertility, psychic abilities and oracular dreams, protection of livestock from wolves and vice versa, balancing the relationship between wolves and livestock, connecting with wild nature.

Warnings

Faunus is primordial, violent, and not gentle. His responses often manifest as nightmares or violent dreams, which are considered auspicious. He may appear sexually aggressive toward women. He communicates not with words but through sounds of the forest. Petitioners should approach with caution and may wish to invoke him alongside Juno or Fauna for mediation.

Additional Notes

Faunus uses different names, each reflecting different aspects of his nature:

As Lupercus, the wolf-god, Faunus arbitrates the balance between wolves and livestock. He is the protector of both predator and prey, maintaining the natural order.

As Fatuus, Faunus gives oracles and bestows psychic ability. This is his aspect as oracle-giver and dream-sender.

As Innus, meaning "He Who Makes Fruitful," Faunus increases herds and ensures fertility of livestock and land.

His sacred creatures are goats and wolves. His sacred day is February 14th. Faunus had a shrine on the Tiber Island. Rome's Church of St Stefano Rotondo sits on the site of a temple once dedicated to Faunus.

The Lupercalia festival celebrated on February 14th is the origin of Valentine's Day traditions, though the modern holiday bears little resemblance to the ancient wild rites honoring Faunus.

Faunus is closely related to the fauns, spirits in his retinue who also manifest with goats' legs and horns. He is sometimes identified with Pan, though they are distinct spirits with similar domains.
