Overview

Mani is the Norse personification of the moon, sibling to Sol, the spirit of the sun. His name means simply "Moon." He is responsible for steering the moon's course across the sky and determining the phases of the lunar cycle, making him the celestial keeper of time.

Myth

In Norse mythology, Mani and Sol are endlessly pursued across the heavens by two great wolves. The wolves are always chasing but never quite catching, until the end of times, Ragnarok, when they finally devour the sun and moon. Lunar eclipses occur in this framework when the wolves draw close enough to shadow their prey.

Magickal Properties

Mani is an appropriate deity to call upon for any work tied to the phases of the moon, the tracking of time, or navigating cycles of change. He embodies the steady, unfailing movement of the moon and can be invoked for clarity, rhythm, and guidance through the turning of the months.
