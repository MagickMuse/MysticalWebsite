Magickal Properties

Eos, Goddess of Dawn, is the sister of Helios and Selene. Every morning, at dawn, she opens the gates of the sky with her rosy fingers so that Helios can ride. She is the dawn itself. She is a golden goddess who wears robes dyed gold, orange, and crimson with saffron.

Ritual Uses

Eos is a warrior goddess who emerges at dawn to drive off recalcitrant nocturnal demons. They flee at her presence. She is a goddess of wisdom and a defender against demons. Ask her to banish malevolent nocturnal spirits. She is an erotic goddess of fertility and may be petitioned for assistance with romance and conception.

Offerings

Roses, crocuses, and foods scented with rosewater or saffron offered at dawn are appropriate.

Favored People

Those seeking protection from nocturnal spirits, lovers and petitioners hoping for fertility or romantic aid, and those who venerate the morning light.

Petition For

Protection against nocturnal and demonic forces, assistance with romance and conception, blessings of fertility and a favorable new day.

Warnings

Eos is a lusty goddess, a free spirit who takes lovers as she will. She is a spirit of fertility. Her son Memnon, an Ethiopian king, fought on the side of the Trojans and was killed by Achilles. Eos' tears moved Zeus to resurrect Memnon and grant him immortality.

Additional Notes

She and Astraios are the parents of the winds, many stars, and the planet Venus. Eos is portrayed flying across the sky of her own volition powered by her wings, or driving a golden chariot pulled by winged horses. The image of Eos cradling the corpse of her naked bearded son was immensely popular and may have served as a model for the Christian Pietà.
