⚠️ Ajysit is venerated within Yakut (Sakha) Indigenous Siberian traditions. This is a closed practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

Ajysit is a goddess of laughter, fate, fertility, and childbirth who lives atop a seven-story mountain. She is involved in the whole birth process, beginning at conception, and may be petitioned specifically for a male child. Ajysit assists during labor, her presence helps ensure safety, eases the pain of labor, and brings the soul to the child. She records every new birth in a golden book, together with the child's future fate.

Ritual Uses

Create a special offering table in the delivery room for Ajysit. She expects various post-birth rituals to be performed involving the midwife, the new mother, and other women, particularly those also seeking fertility. A feast is prepared in her honor. After three days, the mother's bedding is tied into the top of a tall tree, and Ajysit departs until the next birth.

Offerings

Butter, feast preparations involving the midwife and new mother. Post-birth rituals and feasts are prepared in her honor, with butter being her preferred offering.

Favored People

Pregnant women, new mothers, midwives, those seeking fertility, and women participating in birth rituals and celebrations.

Petition For

Safe childbirth, male children, fertility, protection during labor, successful delivery, and blessings for mother and child.

Warnings

If delivery is unsuccessful, no rituals or feasts need to be performed for Ajysit, who departs quietly and discreetly. She is very receptive to fertility petitions during the post-birth period when rituals are being performed.

Additional Notes

Ajysit has dominion over domestic animals, with the cow being her sacred creature. For three days following the birth, the new mother is sequestered while her female friends come to visit and share butter with her. She determines the fate of children and records this information in her golden book.
