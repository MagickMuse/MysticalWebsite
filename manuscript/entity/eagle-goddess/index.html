Magickal Properties

The Eagle Goddess is a powerful Manchurian deity sent by Abka Hehe (the primordial Manchu sky goddess) to Earth to raise the first shamaness. She represents the divine link between heaven and earth, carrying shamanic power from the celestial realms to humanity. As an eagle, she embodies the qualities of keen vision, soaring spirit, and the ability to move between worlds. The Eagle Goddess serves as a guardian and teacher of shamanic traditions, ensuring the transmission of sacred knowledge and healing powers to worthy women.

Ritual Uses

The Eagle Goddess is invoked when the world seems to be falling apart or during times of disaster and crisis. She is petitioned to pass shamanic power to worthy women and to provide divine protection during catastrophic events. Work with her when seeking shamanic instruction or initiation, particularly for women called to the shamanic path. She can be called upon for cosmic perspective and spiritual elevation during overwhelming times.

Offerings

Traditional Manchu offerings include wine, beans, grains, cooked fish and meats (especially pork). Offerings should be made with reverence for the shamanic tradition and the sacred connection between heaven and earth.

Favored People

Shamans and those seeking cosmic protection, women called to shamanic practice, those needing aid during disasters, people seeking divine perspective during overwhelming circumstances, practitioners honoring Manchu spiritual traditions.

Petition For

Help with disasters and catastrophic events, relief when the world seems to be falling apart, protection from cosmic or earthly dangers, the passing of shamanic power to worthy women, initiation into shamanic practice, divine guidance and instruction, elevation of consciousness to see beyond immediate chaos.

Warnings

The Eagle Goddess was sent specifically to raise the first shamaness, indicating her role in shamanic initiation is serious and transformative. Do not petition her casually for shamanic power. This is a sacred calling that requires readiness and commitment. Her intervention during disasters suggests she responds to genuine need, not trivial concerns.

Additional Notes

The Eagle Goddess is an offspring of Abka Hehe, connecting her to the primordial creative powers of the Manchu cosmos. As an eagle deity, she shares the eagle's attributes of far-seeing vision, connection to the sky realm, and ability to soar above earthly concerns. Her role in raising the first shamaness establishes the lineage of Manchurian shamanic practice. She represents the transmission of sacred knowledge from the divine realm to human practitioners. The willow tree is associated with Manchu shamanic practice and may be connected to her worship.
