Raka is a minor goddess of the Hindu Vedic tradition, gathered with Purandhi, Parendi, and Dhisana among the deities of bounty and riches.

In Vedic literature

Raka is named no more than about a dozen times across the Rg-veda, and the texts preserve very little about her distinctive nature. The references that survive describe a benign goddess whose touch is felt through prosperity and material well-being, in keeping with the broader theme that links her small grouping. She is not the focus of a sustained hymn, and her temperament is reconstructed almost entirely from the contexts of her invocation.
