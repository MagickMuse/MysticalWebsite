Magickal Properties

Chemharouch is King of Morocco's Djinn and holds tremendous power over all djinn in the region. Although he is a djinn himself, he is also venerated as a saint in Marrakesh and elsewhere in Morocco. He has authority to resolve conflicts with troublesome djinn and possesses capabilities that extend far beyond typical djinn abilities.

Ritual Uses

Address complaints about troublesome djinn to Sidi Chemharouch for resolution. He can be petitioned for powerful magickal assistance in matters that seem impossible to resolve through ordinary means. His intervention is sought when supernatural forces are causing significant problems.

Offerings

Pilgrimage to his shrine at the foot of Mount Toubkal is traditional. Present respectful petitions appropriate for addressing a djinn king. Honor his status with formal titles and reverent approach.

Favored People

Those experiencing trouble with other djinn who need royal intervention, pilgrims seeking powerful spiritual assistance, people facing supernatural challenges requiring a djinn king's authority.

Petition For

Resolution of conflicts with troublesome djinn, powerful magickal assistance beyond ordinary capabilities, protection from supernatural harm and interference, intervention in seemingly impossible situations.

Warnings

Always address him as Sidi or Lord in recognition of his royal status. His grave at the end of a deep gorge beside a waterfall is believed to be a survival of a pre-Islamic shrine. As of recent times, non-Muslims are forbidden to cross the bridge leading to his shrine, though pilgrims visit year-round.

Additional Notes

His shrine is located in the village of Sidi Chemharouch at the foot of Mount Toubkal, North Africa's highest peak. Thursday and Friday are his sacred days. There is little that Sidi Chemharouch cannot accomplish, making him one of the most powerful supernatural entities in Moroccan spiritual tradition.
