Weret Hekau, Great Lady of Magick. She who is rich in spells. Speaker of spells. The Great Witch. Corn Mother and resurrector of Osiris.

Water, the moon, and the constellation Virgo. The Knot of Isis, the tyet, blood-red. Vervain and myrrh. Bloodstone. The Corn Mother's winnowing sieve.

Offerings

Milk, honey, flowers, herbs, and incense. No blood sacrifice exists in her rites. Stalks of barley and wheat carried in procession.

Correspondences

Sacred Animals: Snakes, crocodiles, cows, scorpions, and kites.

Sacred Mineral: Bloodstone.

Botanicals: Vervain and myrrh.

Celestial: Water, the moon, the constellation Virgo.

Emblem: The tyet, the Knot of Isis, the Blood of Isis.

Petition For

Fertility, protection, resurrection, healing, sea travel, magick. Protection of the vulnerable, especially mothers and children. Magickal instruction. Mastery of multiple disciplines.

Favored People

Everyone, but especially women, single mothers, orphans, occultists, and mariners. Her cult in Rome was open to free people, women, and the enslaved alike.

Sacred Days

The spring rising of Sirius marks the flooding of the Nile and her renewal. Honor her at the harvest, when grain is cut and Osiris remembered. The 1st of March is the Roman Festival of Isis.

Words of Power

Au Set. Queen of the Throne. Mistress of Magick. She Who is Rich in Spells. The Great Witch.
By the Tyet, by the Knot, by the Blood of Isis.

Weret Hekau, the Great Lady of Magick

Isis collected saliva from the aging Ra, fashioned a serpent from earth, and animated it. When the serpent struck Ra, only she could heal him. The price she demanded was his true name. From that moment, no being possessed greater magickal authority.

⚠️
Notes and Cautions

Supreme Mistress of Magick. Maintain ritual purity and respect temple protocols. Never exploit magick for coercion or harm to the vulnerable.
