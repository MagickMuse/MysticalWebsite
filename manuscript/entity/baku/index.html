Magickal Properties

  • Nightmare-eater who devours bad dreams and malign sleep influences
  • Guardian of children and the sensitive; restores calm, protective sleep

Ritual Uses

  • Petition at bedside after a nightmare: speak clearly, “Baku, come eat my dream,” then describe the dream briefly so he takes only the nightmare
  • Bedtime protections for children, light sleepers, and trauma survivors

Offerings

  • Spoken invocations, Baku talismans or images, the name inscribed on a pillow tag or headboard, a small plush for children

Favored People

Children, light sleepers, trauma survivors, and the energetically sensitive

Petition For

Removal of nightmares, protection from intrusive or prophetic dreams, restoration of peaceful sleep, sealing of porous dream boundaries

Warnings

Invite Baku explicitly, he does not act unbidden. Do not over-use: excessive summoning may consume all dreams and leave emotional flatness.

Additional Notes

  • Traditional phrase: “Baku, come eat my dream”
