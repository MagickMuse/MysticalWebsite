Ah Kumix Uinicob

⚠️ Ah Patnar Uinicob are venerated within Mayan Yucatec and Indigenous Mesoamerican traditions. These are closed practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Ah Patnar Uinicob, "owners of the jars men," are Yucatec Mayan attendant water gods. They are four huge deities who pour water onto the earth from jars. The end of the dry season is marked on May 3, completing an eight-day rain ceremony.
