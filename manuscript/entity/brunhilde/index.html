Magickal Properties

Brunhilde is a powerful Valkyrie and shield-maiden from Norse mythology, possessing extraordinary warrior skills and magickal abilities. She has the power to determine victory in battle, choose who lives and dies in warfare, and bestow or withdraw divine protection from warriors. As a Valkyrie, she can travel between realms and has access to Odin's wisdom and battle-magick. Brunhilde represents the fierce feminine warrior spirit and the magickal power of oath-binding and curse-breaking.

Ritual Uses

Brunhilde is invoked by warriors seeking victory in battle, protection during combat, and strength to face overwhelming odds. She can be petitioned for courage in the face of adversity, particularly when standing up for justice or defending the innocent. Her energy assists in breaking binding oaths or curses, especially those that trap individuals in unjust situations.

Offerings

Weapons (particularly swords and spears), armor pieces, ale, mead, golden jewelry, items representing courage and strength, offerings made before battle or challenging endeavors.

Favored People

Warriors, soldiers, those fighting for justice, women seeking to develop their warrior spirit, people trapped by unjust oaths or agreements, anyone facing seemingly impossible battles.

Petition For

Victory in battle, courage in adversity, breaking of binding curses or oaths, protection for warriors, strength to face challenges, justice for wrongdoing, warrior skills and battle prowess.

Warnings

Do not invoke her lightly for petty conflicts. Her power is meant for significant battles and just causes. Respect the warrior code and honor in combat. Be prepared for the consequences of calling upon such powerful battle-magick.

Additional Notes

In the Volsunga Saga and Ring Cycle, Brunhilde was punished by Odin and surrounded by a ring of fire for defying his will. She could only be awakened by a hero brave enough to cross the flames. Her tragic love affair with Siegfried/Sigurd demonstrates both the power and danger of passionate love combined with warrior might. She represents both the protective and destructive aspects of the divine feminine warrior.
