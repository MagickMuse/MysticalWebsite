Magickal Properties

Agrat bat Mahalat is a fierce demon queen commanding 180,000 malicious spirits. She rides through the air in her big chariot leading bands of angels of destruction. She trains witches, procures succubi, and reveals magickal secrets. According to legend, she coupled with King David while he dreamed and conceived Ashmodai.

Ritual Uses

She trains witches and communicates magickal secrets to those brave enough to approach her, though this is extremely dangerous. A fifteenth-century spell exists for receiving visits from succubi through her power. She was once free to roam wherever and whenever she wanted, but a compromise with rabbi Hanina ben Dosa restricted her to regular hours.

Offerings

Not traditionally offered to directly; approached only via complex magickal operations with strict timing and strong protections. Any approach must involve specific timing and protective measures.

Favored People

Witches and sorcerers seeking forbidden knowledge, though approaching her is extremely dangerous even for experienced practitioners.

Petition For

Magickal secrets, succubi encounters, esoteric wisdom, all very dangerous pursuits that should not be undertaken lightly.

Warnings

Fierce demon queen commanding 180,000 spirits. Peak power Wednesday and Friday nights. Men strongly cautioned against being alone or entering water during these times. She is allegedly more interested in human men than women and can completely overwhelm those unprepared for her power.

Additional Notes

Agrat bat Mahalat means "Agrat daughter of Mahalat." She was once free to roam wherever and whenever she wanted, but rabbi Hanina ben Dosa negotiated restrictions on her activities. She is a spirit of wisdom who trains witches and reveals magickal secrets, but approaching her is extremely dangerous.
