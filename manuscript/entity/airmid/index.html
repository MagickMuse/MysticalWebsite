Miach

Airmid is renowned for healing through secret herbal knowledge. As keeper of all plant medicine wisdom, she can communicate arcane remedies to those who petition with genuine intent to heal.

Magickal Properties

Airmid is the daughter of Dian Cecht and sister of Miach, keeper of all plant medicine wisdom that was lost when her father scattered the healing herbs. Three-hundred sixty-five healing plants grew from Miach's grave, allegedly one for every illness. She carefully picked, dried, and arranged these plants on her cloak, ready to heal all, but her father overturned the cloth and the herbs were scattered, some lost forever.

Ritual Uses

Work with Airmid as the keeper of secret, arcane healing knowledge. If petitioned with proper respect and reverence, she may communicate secrets of healing and plant medicine wisdom. Create rituals honoring the lost healing knowledge and the sacred relationship between healers and plants.

Offerings

Petitions for healing knowledge, herbs arranged with reverence on a cloak or cloth in her honor, and offerings that show respect for plant medicine wisdom and healing traditions.

Favored People

Healers, herbalists, those seeking plant medicine knowledge, people working with traditional healing methods, and practitioners dedicated to preserving healing wisdom.

Petition For

Healing secrets, herbal knowledge, arcane remedies, communication of healing wisdom, understanding of plant medicine, and guidance in healing practices.

Warnings

Daughter of Dian Cecht, sister of Miach. The healing knowledge she holds was scattered by her father, making these remedies secret and arcane. Approach with genuine intent to heal and help others.

Additional Notes

Had her father not scattered the healing herbs, all remedies would now be known and all illness could be cured. She represents both the lost potential of complete healing knowledge and the hope that this wisdom can still be recovered through proper petition and relationship with plant spirits. She is associated with the Tuatha Dé Danann and the ancient Irish healing traditions.
