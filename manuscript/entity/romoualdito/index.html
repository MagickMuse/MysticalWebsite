Romoualdito Ibáñez

Unofficial Chilean folk saint venerated for miraculous intercession.

Life and Death

In 1930, Romoualdito Ibáñez was attacked and left to die near the central train station in Santiago, Chile. A shrine was erected at the site of his death. Miracles were soon attributed to him, and the shrine expanded until it now runs the entire length of the train station wall, forming a series of interconnected shrines.

Veneration

The name "Romoualdito" refers to both the saint himself and his extensive shrine. The wall is covered with plaques testifying to miracles received and blackened with soot from countless candles lit by devotees seeking his assistance. Romoualdito is invoked for any kind of help or miracle needed.

Sacred Site

His shrine near the central train station in Santiago, Chile.
