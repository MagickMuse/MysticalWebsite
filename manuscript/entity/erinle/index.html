⚠️ Erinle is venerated within Yoruba Indigenous religion and its diasporic expressions including Lucumí/Santería and Candomblé. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

Erinle is the underwater king and bush hunter, an orisha of rivers, healing, and concealed wealth. As divine physician and provider, he bridges forest and water, guiding prosperity where fresh and salt waters meet and restoring vitality to body and spirit.

Ritual Uses

Work at river mouths, mangroves, and brackish inlets for prosperity, healing, and protection. Pair river pebbles with clean water to “open” petitions. Venerate alongside Ogun, Ochossi, and Osain for coordinated hunts, healing, and provision.

Offerings

Small metal fish charms, sparkly fish images, Swedish fish candy. Place smooth river pebbles on the altar to activate petitions.

Favored People

Queer and trans devotees, hunters, healers, river people, and those seeking dignified prosperity and restoration.

Petition For

Wealth, fertility, healing, and protection where rivers meet the sea.

Warnings

Honor boundaries and tides. Keep petitions clean and sincere; avoid waste or pollution at natural waters.

Additional Notes

Allied with Ogun, Ochossi, and Osain. Often syncretized with Archangel Raphael. Colors include coral, indigo, and turquoise; number 7.
