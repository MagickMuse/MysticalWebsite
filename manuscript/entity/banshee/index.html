Magickal Properties

  • Death‑wail messenger who heralds transition and guards families in vigil
  • Bringer of omens and clarity around ancestral passages

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for gentle crossings, recognition of death omens, and protection for the grieving
  • Keep a quiet watch, sing a soft lament, and tend water or milk at windows or doors

Offerings

  • Bowl of water or milk; a white cloth at a window; traditional mourning song

Favored People

Old Irish families and descendants with second sight or strong ancestral ties; caretakers of the dying

Petition For

Peaceful passage, clear signs, ancestral protection, and comfort for mourners

Warnings

Never mock, bind, or summon lightly. Her keening is bound to specific families and signals an imminent death within their line.

Additional Notes

  • Names: Bean Sidhe; found in Irish, Scottish, Welsh, and Breton lore
