Vampires

Overview

The Vampiri are a Macedonian variant of the vampire: a dead soul unable to transition to the next realm because of unfulfilled obligations. Unfortunately, having transformed, many forget their original goal, which keeps them tied to Earth. They are doomed to wander the Earth until they either fulfill their vowed obligations or are sent to the next realm via magical or shamanic rituals.

Magickal Properties

The Vampiri rise after dark, congregating in the cemetery, where they dance and socialize with other Vampiri. They tend to return home to their families, whom they usually do not harm, unless that was their original unfulfilled goal. They will drink blood from their family's livestock or other people's livestock.

Although a dead male Vampiri can father children, they tend to return to women they married in life; an unfulfilled obligation may be fathering a child to continue the family name or lineage, but a Vampiri may also start a fresh new relationship. When such a child matures, they may manifest extraordinary shamanic skills, including profound exorcism talents; many are extremely gifted at recognizing demons in disguise.

Manifestation

The Vampiri take corporeal form and are exceptional shape-shifters, transforming instantly from human to animal forms. Their favored manifestation includes a four-eyed dog.
