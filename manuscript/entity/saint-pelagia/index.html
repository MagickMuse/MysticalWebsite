Saint Pelagia is renowned for healing transformation—helping those who wish to shed old identities and begin anew.

Classification

Venerated as a saint in the Roman Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Churches. Feast day October 8.

Overview

Pelagia of Antioch was a celebrated actress and dancer in fourth-century Syria, renowned for her beauty and provocative performances. After encountering Bishop Nonnus of Edessa, whose sermon moved her to repentance, she gave away her wealth, received baptism, and withdrew to live as a hermit on the Mount of Olives. She disguised herself as a man named Pelagius, and her true identity was only discovered after her death.

Life and Legend

Pelagia was the most famous actress-dancer in Antioch, draped in gold and pearls, attended by admirers wherever she went. When she happened to pass a gathering where Bishop Nonnus was preaching, his words pierced her heart. She sought him out, confessed her sins, and begged for baptism.

After her conversion, Pelagia distributed her fortune to the poor and traveled to Jerusalem. There she donned male clothing and lived as a beardless hermit in a small cell on the Mount of Olives, known to all as "Brother Pelagius." She was so respected for her holiness that pilgrims came seeking her counsel. Only when she died did those who prepared her body discover that the revered monk had been a woman.

Magical Properties

Saint Pelagia is invoked for transformation and leaving behind a former life. She assists those who wish to shed an old identity—whether escaping a troubled past, changing careers, or undergoing personal metamorphosis. Her energy supports dramatic life changes undertaken for spiritual growth.

Ritual Uses

Salt Water Healing Ritual

This ritual invokes Saint Pelagia's powers of transformation and purification:

  1. Obtain seven white candles and a basin of salt water
  2. Light the candles while calling upon Saint Pelagia
  3. Immerse yourself in or wash with the salt water seven times
  4. With each immersion, release one aspect of your former self you wish to transform
  5. After the seventh immersion, emerge renewed, as Pelagia emerged from baptism

Favored People

Actresses, entertainers, dancers, penitents, those leaving sex work, people seeking to escape their past, those undergoing gender transition or presentation changes.

Petition For

Transformation, repentance, new beginnings, healing from shame, courage to change, protection while starting over, shedding old identities.

Margaret of Antioch, Margaret of Cortona, Marina the Monk
