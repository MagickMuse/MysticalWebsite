Encyclopedia Source Notes (Athirat)

Fertility goddess of the Western Semitic Canaanite tradition. In Old Babylonian texts of Hammurabi she is identified as the daughter-in-law of the king of heaven. She is also known from pre-Islamic southern Arabia as a consort of the moon god Amm.
