Magickal Properties

The Lady of the Night is a title shared by powerful nocturnal goddess figures, most notably Hekate as Dark Moon Goddess and Queen of the Night. This archetype embodies the feminine spirit of darkness, the one who rules the hours between dusk and dawn when the veil between worlds is thinnest.

She walks with hounds before her, torch in hand, illuminating what daylight conceals. Where the Lady of the Day nurtures growth in sunlight, the Lady of the Night presides over mystery, transformation, and the hidden knowledge that only darkness reveals.

Ritual Uses

Work with the Lady of the Night for all forms of nocturnal magick, dark moon rituals, shadow work, and divination performed after sunset. She governs the crossroads between waking and dreaming, the living and the dead.

Invoke her during the dark moon or the deep hours of the night when the mundane world sleeps and the spirit world stirs.

Lore

Related figures include Hekate (Queen of the Night, Dark Moon Goddess), Holle (who rides through the night with her hounds), and Diana in her nocturnal huntress aspect. The Lady of the Night is an archetype woven through many cultures, always representing the sacred feminine power of darkness.
