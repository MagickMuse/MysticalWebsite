Lord of the Forest

Virbius is Diana's chief companion at her shrine in the Forest of Nemi. He may be her first priest now deified, or identified with Hippolytus brought back to life. He manifests as a man or a stag and is invoked for virility. He may always have been a spirit of the woods. The Greeks identified him with Hippolytus, brought back to life by Asklepios and spirited away to the oak groves of Nemi; since Hippolytus died trampled by horses, horses are not permitted in Virbius' grove at Nemi. Statues of Diana depicting her beside a stag may indicate either that the stag is her literal sacred animal, or that it is Virbius himself in stag guise.
