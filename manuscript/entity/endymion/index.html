Magickal Properties

Endymion is the beloved of Selene, kept in enchanted sleep in a cave so the Moon may visit and love without end. He embodies liminality between sleep and waking, desire and stillness, and the quiet blessing of nightly renewal.

Ritual Uses

Work at dusk or under the waxing Moon for gentle romance, erotic tenderness, prophetic dreaming, and rest after turmoil. Invite peaceful sleep and moon‑kissed inspiration.

Offerings

Quiet vigil by moonlight, a silver coin, milk and honey, white flowers placed at a window.

Favored People

Dreamers, lovers, poets, and those who are moon‑touched or seeking soft reawakening.

Petition For

Fertility, romance, prophetic dreams, psychic vision, and gentle awakenings.

Warnings

Approach in silence and sincerity. Keep petitions soft and non‑coercive; avoid manipulation in love.

Additional Notes

Linked to Selene, Zeus, and the Lorialets (moon‑kissed children) in later lore.
