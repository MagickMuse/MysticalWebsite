Magickal Properties

Antaura embodies the destructive power of disease and the ancient understanding of illness as spiritual invasion. As a sea-dwelling disease demon who specializes in causing lingering, protracted migraines, she carries the archetypal energy of affliction that comes from the depths and resists easy cure.

Ritual Uses

Disease Banishing: Understanding her nature helps create more effective protection against headache-causing entities.

Migraine Prevention: Use knowledge of her methods to develop protective rituals against chronic headaches.

Entity Identification: Study her characteristics to recognize when headaches may have spiritual rather than purely physical causes.

Protective Warding: Create barriers against sea-based or water-associated disease spirits.

Offerings

Do not make offerings to Antaura. Instead, make protective offerings to Artemis of Ephesus, who specializes in counteracting Antaura's influence.

Favored People

Antaura does not "favor" anyone, she targets humans for affliction.

Petition For

Do not petition Antaura directly. Instead, petition Artemis of Ephesus for protection from persistent headaches and migraines.

Warnings

Invoke Artemis to counteract; base charms on historical precedents. Antaura represents dangerous spiritual forces that cause genuine physical harm through persistent headaches.

Additional Notes

Third-century CE amulets found in Roman Carnuntum specifically address the confrontation between Artemis and Antaura, showing that this spiritual battle was recognized by ancient practitioners.
