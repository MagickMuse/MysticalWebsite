Shrine to Persephone

🌸
Persephone · Kore · Queen of the Underworld

The abducted bride who became sovereign. Possibly more ancient than Demeter or Hades, the original ruler of the dead. She returns each May with a procession of nine dancing spirits.

🟣
The violets in the meadow where Hades took her. The pomegranate seeds. The threshold between life and death.
Without descent, there is no return. Without the season of death, there is no season of growth. The queen who was taken became one of the most sovereign figures in the Greek pantheon.

Drawn from Persephone

🕯️
Leave Your Devotion

Set a bowl of spring flowers or a single pomegranate on your altar. Speak the names of those you love among the dead. Sit with the threshold.

Offerings

Spring flowers, roses, black crystals. Pomegranate.

Correspondences

Attribute: Pomegranate. Horse harness.

Constellation: Virgo, the solitary woman with a stalk of wheat or the divine child.

Spirit Allies: Demeter, Dionysus, Orpheus. The Sirens are her servants. Hekate is her handmaiden and messenger. Artemis and Athena are her friends.

Altar: Decorated with images of Medusa, her protective guardian device.

Petition For

Finding true new or lasting love. Enhanced beauty and charisma. Banishing ghosts. Communication with the dead. A painless, peaceful death.

Favored People

Widows. Those seeking love. Those working with the Eleusinian Mysteries.

Sacred Days

May, when grain emerges from Earth and she returns to her mother. The growing seasons belong to her ascent. Winter is Demeter's grief and Persephone's reign below.

Words of Power

Persephone · Kore · Destroying Face · Queen of the Underworld

Notes and Cautions

The queen of the dead is sovereign, not a pawn. Approach her with respect for the threshold she keeps. Her image wards off ghosts. Do not summon her for trivial work.
