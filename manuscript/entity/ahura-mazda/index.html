Magickal Properties

Ahura Mazda is the supreme deity of Zoroastrianism, the "Wise Lord" who represents absolute goodness, truth, and light. As the creator of the universe and all good things within it, Ahura Mazda stands in eternal opposition to Angra Mainyu (Ahriman), the destructive spirit of darkness and evil. He is not merely a distant creator but an active force maintaining cosmic order (asha) against chaos and lies (druj).

Ritual Uses

Work with Ahura Mazda through sacred fire ceremonies, as he is the source of all fire including the sacred flames maintained in Zoroastrian fire temples. Approach through the principle of "Good Thoughts, Good Words, Good Deeds" (Humata, Hukhta, Hvarshta) and actively choose righteousness over evil.

Offerings

Sacred fire, frankincense, pure water, white flowers, and offerings that maintain ritual purity and honor the principle of cosmic order.

Favored People

Zoroastrians, followers of ethical monotheism, those seeking truth and righteousness, people working to maintain cosmic order, and practitioners committed to good thoughts, words, and deeds.

Petition For

Truth, righteousness, protection from evil, divine wisdom, cosmic order, guidance in ethical decisions, and strength to choose good over evil.

Warnings

Approach with truthfulness and ritual purity, petition only for righteous, benevolent aims, and never invoke him alongside or in the company of Daeva spirits because Ahura Mazda upholds cosmic order and opposes demonic forces.

Additional Notes

The prophet Zoroaster received direct revelation from Ahura Mazda around 628-551 BCE, establishing ethical monotheism that influenced Judaism, Christianity, and Islam. He is served by six Amesha Spentas (Holy Immortals): Vohu Manah (Good Mind), Asha Vahishta (Best Truth), Khshathra Vairya (Desirable Dominion), Spenta Armaiti (Holy Devotion), Haurvatat (Wholeness), and Ameretat (Immortality). His sacred symbol is the Faravahar, a winged disk representing the divine spark in humanity.
