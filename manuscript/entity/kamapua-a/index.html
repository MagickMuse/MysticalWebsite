Kamapua'a is the Hawaiian pig-god, a powerful shapeshifter who can transform between human, pig, and fish forms. He is a deity of fertility, agriculture, rain, and wild masculine energy. Kamapua'a is famous for his passionate and tumultuous relationship with the volcano goddess Pele; they are eternal lovers and rivals whose conflicts create rain and volcanic activity.

He represents the untamed, fertile power of nature and embodies transformation, passion, and the integration of civilized and wild aspects.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kamapua'a for fertility magick (land and body), calling rain, agricultural blessings, passionate love, and transformation work. His energy supports shapeshifting practices, integration of shadow and light, and the celebration of wild masculine power balanced with agricultural nurturing.

Warnings

Kamapua'a's energy is passionate and can be overwhelming. His relationship with Pele is volatile; do not invoke both simultaneously unless you're prepared for intense, conflicting energies. Respect his wild nature. Respect pigs and agricultural boundaries and avoid hunting or harming sacred pigs. Do not provoke shapeshifting or boar spirits near freshwater springs.
