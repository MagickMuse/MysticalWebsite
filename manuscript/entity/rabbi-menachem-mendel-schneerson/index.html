Venerated as a Jewish saint, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (18 April 1902-12 June 1994) was the seventh and potentially last Lubavitcher Rebbe.

Life and Legend

Born in what is now Ukraine, Rabbi Schneerson demonstrated spiritual gifts early but was also a Sorbonne-trained electrical engineer and mathematician. A devoutly traditional religious man, he was also a strong proponent of solar energy. He and his wife escaped Nazi-occupied France in 1941, joining his father-in-law in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. He reluctantly assumed leadership of the Chabad-Lubavitcher movement after his father-in-law's death in 1950.

Under his leadership, Chabad-Lubavitch expanded into a massive worldwide organization. Hundreds of thousands of people stood in line for hours to receive his blessings. By the end of his life, many were convinced he was the Messiah. After his death, all suggested successors declined his mantle. Rabbi Schneerson continues to guide followers from beyond, and many believe he is not truly dead but will reemerge.

Magickal Properties

Rabbi Schneerson still offers blessings and assistance. His blessing is invoked for every conceivable circumstance, but especially healing, fertility, marital issues, and success. He also offers spiritual guidance through bibliomancy, the art of divination using books.

Bibliomancy Ritual

To receive guidance from Rabbi Schneerson, use a volume of his own writings:

  1. Formulate your question while holding the book in both hands
  2. Invoke his blessings and intercession
  3. When ready, randomly flip the book open and, with eyes closed, point to a passage
  4. This passage is your answer, whether straightforward or requiring meditation and interpretation

Sacred Sites

Jews and non-Jews alike journey to the Rebbe's grave, known as the Ohel, in Montefiore Cemetery in Queens, New York, to invoke his blessings. It is traditional to leave little notes explaining your situation and needs. Those unable to visit may make requests via fax, email, or postal service through the Chabad organization.
