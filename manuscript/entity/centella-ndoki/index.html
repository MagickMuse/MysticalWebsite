⚠️ Centella Ndoki is an mpungu venerated within Palo Mayombe and related Congolese-derived Afro-Caribbean traditions. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Owner of the cemetery and gatekeeper of the portal between life and death
  • Queen of the World, among the most powerful forces in the universe
  • Exceptionally significant spirit in Palo tradition due to work with dead souls
  • May or may not be the same spirit as Oya
  • Associations with marketplace, storms, and graveyard

Ritual Uses

  • Essential for Palo rituals involving work with dead souls
  • Cemetery and crossroads work
  • Storm magick and weather work
  • Protection and power work

Offerings

Present petitions and offerings in the graveyard where she resides

Favored People

Practitioners of Palo tradition, those working with ancestor spirits and cemetery magic

Petition For

Access to work with dead souls, power from the cemetery, crossroads guidance, storm magick, marketplace protection and success

Additional Notes

In traditional Congolese culture, ndoki refers to someone possessing great reserves of magick power. The power is neutral and may be used for good or ill depending on desires and intent of the individual. Like Oya, Centella Ndoki has associations with the marketplace and storms as well as the graveyard.
