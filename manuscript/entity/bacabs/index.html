The Bacabs are four Mayan attendant deities identified with the cardinal points of the compass and their associated colors.

Members

Hobnil resides in the east and is associated with the color red. He also serves as a patron of beekeepers. Can Tzicnal resides in the north and is associated with the color white. Zac Cimi resides in the west and is associated with the color black. Hozanek resides in the south and is associated with the color yellow.

Lore

The Bacabs are also identified as the Toliloch, or opossum actors, where each carries the image of the ruling god for the incoming year on his back.
