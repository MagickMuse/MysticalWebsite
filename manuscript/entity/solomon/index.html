Master of Magick

Biblical King Solomon (died circa 925 BCE) was more than a political king. He was Master of Magick, the world's wisest man, and builder of Jerusalem Temple. Master of Djinn, he commanded an army of spirits to complete his building projects. He possessed a magickal ring engraved with the Ineffable Name that gave him power over spirits.

Folk Magician

Solomon was expert folk magician, root doctor, shaman, high priest, and ceremonial magician. He spoke to spirits and birds. With Djinn help, he constructed a magickal ritual bath that healed all who submerged.

Seal of Solomon

The hexagram is known as the Seal of Solomon. Grimoires attributed to him include the Testament, Greater Key, and Lesser Key of Solomon.
