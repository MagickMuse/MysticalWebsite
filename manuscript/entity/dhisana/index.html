Dhisana is a minor goddess of the Hindu Vedic tradition, named alongside Purandhi, Parendi, and Raka among the deities tied to bounty and material plenty.

In Vedic literature

Dhisana is mentioned no more than about a dozen times across the Rg-veda, and the texts give no extended portrait of her distinctive nature. The little that survives points to a benign figure whose influence is registered through prosperity and the steady provision of riches, in keeping with the shared theme of her small grouping. She is not the subject of a sustained hymn, and her character is reconstructed from the company she keeps when invoked rather than from any direct description.
