Magickal Properties

Charos is the evolved form of Charon who abandoned his boat and resumed what may have been his original function as Angel of Death. In modern Greek folklore, Charos is no longer just the ferryman but has become Lord of Death and Ruler of the Realm of Death. Charos is death itself, having turned the tables and banished Lord Hades in the same manner that he himself was once dethroned.

Ritual Uses

Charos occupies Hades with his entire family, his mother, wife, and children all assist in the family business of death. He personally visits the land of the living to kill, collect, and transport the dead. He cuts victims' throats, releasing their souls, which he then transports to his realm. He cooperates with angels and archangels to escort souls.

Offerings

Charos cannot and will not be bribed with material offerings. However, his mother is kind and softhearted, she is the one to appeal to. Present pleas and arguments to her asking her to intercede on your behalf.

Favored People

Those fearing imminent death, families caring for the dying, people seeking protection from sudden death, mothers of young children who might be spared by his mother's intervention.

Petition For

Protection from sudden death, mercy for loved ones facing death, intercession from his compassionate mother, gentler passage to the afterlife, protection for newlyweds and young mothers.

Warnings

Charos is a coldhearted, merciless spirit who turns a deaf ear to pleas for mercy. He brutally overpowers those who struggle and cannot be bribed. His mother is the only one who sometimes spontaneously urges him to spare mothers of young children or true lovers.

Additional Notes

Appears as either a sharp-featured, white-haired old man with severe expression, or as a vigorous warrior dressed in black riding a black stallion with either raven black or shining gold hair. He carries sword, double-edged knife, and keys - souls in his custody are kept under constant lock and key. His wife Charontissa can fill in for him if needed. They have a three-headed watchdog as their family pet.
