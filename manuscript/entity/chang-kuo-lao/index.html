Eight Immortals

Magickal Properties

Chang Kuo Lao is one of the Eight Immortals and a brilliant occultist with vast magickal knowledge. He was a seventh or eighth-century Taoist hermit who developed a reputation as an alchemist. He is a master of resurrection and longevity magick, able to bestow longevity to others.

Ritual Uses

Consult Chang Kuo Lao for healing, magickal advice, and instruction in occult arts. He may be petitioned when seeking to understand alchemical processes or when needing guidance from an elder teacher.

Offerings

Incense

Favored People

Chang Kuo Lao is traditionally the patron of blind fortune-tellers, alchemists, and occultists seeking advanced magickal knowledge.

Petition For

Healing, advice, instruction in occult arts, alchemical knowledge, and longevity magick.

Warnings

Respect alchemical tools and elixirs and do not steal or shortcut hermetic craft. Offer thanks to elder teachers and avoid claiming mastery without apprenticeship.

Additional Notes

Chang Kuo Lao rides backward on his magickal white mule, which can be folded up like paper and carried in his wallet when not needed. His attribute is the yuku or fish drum, a musical instrument formed from bamboo with one end covered by snake skin. He ignored imperial summons to serve at court, preferring his wandering hermit life.
