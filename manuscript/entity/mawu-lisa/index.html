Mawu-Lisa is the primary deity of the Dahomey tradition, an androgynous figure embodying the union of opposites.

The Four Days of Creation

Mawu-Lisa is the creator of the world. On the first day the world was made; on the second, all plant and animal life were brought into being; on the third, humans were given intellect and languages; and on the fourth, the far-sighted God created technology.

Religious Associations

  • Mawu-Lisa's four-day creation account has drawn comparison to the Abrahamic creation narrative, in which the world is made in six days.
