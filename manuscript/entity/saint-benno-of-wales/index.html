Saint Benno of Wales is renowned for healing children and animals, especially cattle.

Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Saint Benno (died c. 640) was the grandson of a Welsh prince. King Arthur was allegedly his great-uncle. Benno was the uncle of Saint Winifride, as well as her teacher, guardian, and spiritual advisor.

The Miracle of Winifride

The most famous legend of Saint Benno involves his successful reattachment of Winifride's severed head. He became a visionary in his old age and died of natural causes.

Healing Miracles

After his death, many healing miracles were reported at his tomb. It is still traditional to sit or place ailing or frail children on the stone slab of his tomb in order to receive his gift of healing.

Powers and Domains

Saint Benno is invoked to heal children and animals, especially cattle.

Iconography

Benno is usually depicted in the act of putting Winifride's head back where it belongs.
