⚠️ Yakut (Sakha) Siberian spiritual traditions are closed practices sacred to Indigenous peoples of eastern Siberia. This entry is offered for reference only and should not be appropriated by those outside the originating culture.

Overview

Arsan Duolai, whose name means terrible dweller of the underground world, is the chief spirit of the underworld in the Yakut tradition of eastern Siberia. His known period of worship extends from prehistoric times until circa 1900 CE.

Cult and Sources

No synonyms or centers of cult are recorded. No art references are positively identified, though he may possibly be the subject of certain wooden icons. The principal literary source is The Yakut by Jochelson.

Domain

Little is known of this animistic god. He was considered to live in the lower world and rule over a nebulous group of spirits, the Abasy. Horned cattle were sacrificed to these subterranean deities. The Abasy also lived in the upper world, in which capacity they were the recipients of horse sacrifice.
