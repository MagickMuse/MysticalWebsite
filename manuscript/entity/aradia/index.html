In Witchcraft

Aradia, along with her alternate name-forms Ariadne and Arianne, is named in traditional witchcraft as one of the titles of the Lady of Delight. She stands alongside Habondia, Hulda, Herodias, Diana, Rhiannon, and Hecate as names by which the presiding goddess of romantic love and enchantment may be invoked. She is Mistress of the Moon as well as the realm of Venus. The Lady's traditional visualization before a love working: robed in silver, mantled in starred darkness, long-haired, crowned with flowers and corn, with the lunar disk above her brow flanked by two rearing serpents, and the white dove perched on her right hand.
