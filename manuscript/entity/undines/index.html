Water Elementals

Undines are closely akin to the Nymphs. The alchemist Paracelsus named the undine the elemental spirit of water, one of the four elementals alongside the salamander of fire, the gnome of earth, and the sylph of air. They are beautiful females, mischievous at the least and cruel at the worst.

The Treacherous Guide

Undines are known for offering to lead lost travelers through dangerous swamps and marshy ground, when their true aim is to send them astray and drown them. Like mermaids they are lovely and coy, and folktales tell how they tempt handsome men to follow them into the deep waters of the lakes they inhabit. There the hours slip past like minutes, and death comes swiftly to the young man whose judgment has been clouded by desire.

A Warning Against Seduction

The undine stands as a reminder of what may happen when a person surrenders to the powers of seduction.
