San Bartolo is renowned for healing nervous conditions, neurological diseases, and hangover relief.

Classification

Folk saint (Mexican tradition).

Identity

Though nominally the Apostle Bartholomew, this Guanajuato saint bears no resemblance to his official counterpart. Where Portugal's maritime Bartholomew echoes Poseidon, San Bartolo channels the spirit of Bacchus.

Unique Requirements

San Bartolo patronizes those who drink. Local tradition holds that prayers offered in his church receive answers only if the petitioner is genuinely intoxicated or convincingly faking it. Many believe he isn't actually the Apostle but rather a beloved local holy man who gained unofficial sainthood after death and merged with the official figure.

Healing Waters

San Bartolo lends his name to San Bartolo Agua Caliente, among Mexico's earliest colonial spa complexes. The site once included a thermal bath hospital dedicated to Bartholomew in his official capacity as patron of neurological conditions, though the waters likely also soothed those recovering from excessive drink.

Iconography

A plump, barefoot, slightly tipsy-looking monk.

Attributes

Wine jug and a ring of keys (to the wine cellar, to mystical ecstasy, to cosmic secrets, and perhaps to the medicine cabinet for morning-after remedies).

24 August
