Magickal Properties

Dirt Woman is the primordial woman, the spirit of Earth among the earliest goddesses. She is not Earth herself, but something that emerged from Earth, an avatar of Earth. She is so old she has no myth, she just is. Dirt Woman stalks people's dreams and is probably primeval, meaning no harm but so primordial that her presence can be frightening and overwhelming. She probably does not speak although she may make sounds.

Ritual Uses

The appearance of Dirt Woman signals the beginning of a psychic or spiritual journey you must take. She may appear if you are consciously or subconsciously resisting a spiritual calling. She can be invoked via visualization and divination, especially scrying, to help guard children or banish demons.

Offerings

Modern niceties mean nothing to Dirt Woman. She is too primordial for conventional offerings and only comprehends primeval emotion, action, and human skills.

Favored People

Those on spiritual journeys, people with children to protect, those being called to spiritual work who may be resisting the call.

Petition For

Guardian protection for children, help in accepting spiritual calling, assistance in banishing demons, protection during spiritual journeys.

Warnings

Her presence can be frightening and overwhelming due to her primordial nature. She appears in dreams and visions, sometimes from the corner of the eye, vanishing when you turn to face her directly.

Additional Notes

Dirt Woman's skin is coated with dirt or mud and may be any color including brown, dull green, or ghostly chalk white. She may be covered with thick layers of multicolored dried mud. Twigs and leaves may stick to her hair and body. She sometimes appears with feline spots on her body, possibly painted on, connecting her to the leopard, humanity's primeval predator. She is afraid of nothing and can banish demons.
