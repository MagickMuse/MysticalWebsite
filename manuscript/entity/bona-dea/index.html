Magickal Properties

  • Bona Dea literally means "good goddess" and refers to female spirits exclusively venerated by women
  • Known also as Mother Earth, the great nurturer who sustains and provides for all living things
  • Represents collective power of feminine mystery traditions from which men were completely excluded
  • Several different goddesses were classified among the Bona Dea, generally believed to refer to Fauna
  • Belonged to group of spirits who interact exclusively with women, men not privy to their mysteries

Ritual Uses

  • Participate in women-only mystery rituals and ceremonies with specific traditional requirements
  • Maintain absolute secrecy about specific practices and traditions
  • Honor feminine sacred space and women's spiritual autonomy
  • Preserve ancient traditions of female-centered spirituality

Offerings

Offerings determined by women-only mystery traditions, specific requirements kept secret

Favored People

Women exclusively; men were completely forbidden from these rites

Petition For

Female mysteries, women's spiritual needs, protection of feminine sacred space, women-only spiritual guidance

Warnings

Observe female-only rites and keep mysteries private; men were excluded from these mystery traditions. Little documented information survives due to secrecy.

Additional Notes

  • Because these were Mystery traditions excluding men, little documented information survives
  • Whatever information exists about these spirits derives from male sources, creating incomplete picture
  • Examples of other Bona Dea spirits include Baubo, who belonged to group interacting exclusively with women
  • Represents ancient tradition of women's spiritual autonomy and sacred feminine mysteries
  • Exclusion of men was absolute, violation of this boundary considered serious transgression
