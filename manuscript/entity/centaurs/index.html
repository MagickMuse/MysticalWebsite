Overview

A hybrid of horse and man, the centaur appears as the archer of the astrological sign Sagittarius. Wherever he is found he carries the meaning of lust, his arrows standing for both ejaculation and hunting prowess, so that he represents the animal nature of the human male.

Greek Myth

Greek myth describes centaurs living wild on the mountaintops, eating raw meat and unable to take any wine at all without becoming extremely drunk.

Symbolism

Centaurs are often shown with sorrowful faces, for they know that the brute force and lust they embody must be balanced against spiritual power. In this the centaur represents the constant struggle within human beings between spirit and matter, intuition and knowledge, the conscious and the unconscious.
