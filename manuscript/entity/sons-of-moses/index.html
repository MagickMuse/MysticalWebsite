Moroccan Jewish guardian spirits, the souls of Moses' contemporaries who periodically emerge to help Jews in peril.

Nature and Realm

Despite their name, these are not Moses' children but rather the souls of those who lived during his time. They resemble Theosophy's Ascended Masters, though their realm lies not in the Himalayas but beyond the Sambatyon River.

The Sambatyon may be a mythic river, though various attempts have been made to locate it geographically. Water does not flow in the Sambatyon. Instead, it is variously described as a river of fire, sand, stones, or some combination of these elements. It is a dangerous river, not easily crossed. From beyond it, the Sons of Moses periodically emerge on earth to assist Jews facing danger.

Lalla Rivka

The best known of the group is Lalla Rivka (Lady Rebecca), paradoxically also called Sidi Bel Abbes, a masculine name. Perhaps her behavior defies stereotypically ladylike expectations, creating confusion about her identity.

Lalla Rivka is a sword-wielding warrior spirit who rides her horse into battle. She is credited with killing soldiers who menaced a Moroccan Jewish community. She bears striking resemblance to the ancient Semitic deity Anat, once worshipped by Jews in Egypt.
