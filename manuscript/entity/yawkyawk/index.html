Mermaids

Classification

Aboriginal Australian mermaids who live in waterholes, freshwater springs, and rock pools, especially in the Stone Country of Australia's Top End. Although it is often suggested that Europeans spread mermaid tales around the world, these spirits were envisioned as mermaids long before Europeans arrived.

Magickal Properties

Yawkyawk literally means "young woman spirit being." They radiate incredible fertility power; allegedly just approaching their waterhole can cause a woman to conceive. They provide rain for plants and sweet water for people, but if angered, they raise destructive storms. They sometimes engage in marriages with people.

Manifestation

Yawkyawk manifest as classical mermaids: a young woman above the waist, fish below. Their long hair resembles green algae or fronds of seaweed. This is believed to be their true form, but if they wish, they can sprout legs and walk, or morph into dragonfly form and fly. They are a favorite subject of Aboriginal art, both traditional and contemporary.

Correspondences

Spirit ally: Ngalyod, the Rainbow Serpent, who may be a husband or father (or may be a different type of spirit or form). They manifest in white lilies and white clay, and live in waterholes. They tend to be most active and visible at night.
