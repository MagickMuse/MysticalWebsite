Magickal Properties

Greek spirits are not only ancient or Classical; the Exotika are a pantheon of modern Greek spirits. Exotika means things outside or things beyond. They are non-Christian spirits existing in Christian context. From a Christian perspective, spirits are either allied with God or the devil. The Exotika are spirits who are non- or pre-Christian, thus they are generally considered malevolent or, at least, outside the pale of safety.

The Exotika include spirits such as Charos, Gello, Gorgon, Kallikantzari, Lamia of the Sea, Lamiae, Neraida, and Zeus. These spirits represent the continuation of pre-Christian Greek spiritual traditions that persist into the modern era despite the dominant Christian framework.

Ritual Uses

Work with the Exotika requires understanding that these are spirits who exist outside Christian classifications. They maintain their own identities and powers despite attempts to categorize them as either good or evil from a Christian perspective.

Offerings

Non-Christian offerings appropriate to each individual spirit within the Exotika category.

Favored People

Those working with Greek folk magick, practitioners of non-Christian Greek spiritual traditions.

Petition For

Connection with pre-Christian spirits, work with Greek folk magick traditions.

Warnings

From a Christian perspective, the Exotika are generally considered malevolent or outside the pale of safety. They are not aligned with Christian spiritual categories and maintain their own pre-Christian natures and powers.

Additional Notes

The Exotika represent the living continuation of ancient Greek spiritual traditions in the modern world. They are evidence that pre-Christian spirits have not disappeared but continue to exist alongside and sometimes in tension with Christian spiritual frameworks.
