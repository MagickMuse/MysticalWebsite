Egyptian Deities

The ancient Egyptians worshipped many Gods, a great number of them conceived as hybrids of human and animal forms. Animals themselves were deified and accorded reverence in their own right; the more dangerous the creature, the greater its worship. The Crocodile God Sobek, for example, had an entire city dedicated to his veneration: Crocodilopolis. The pharaoh, too, was considered as one with the Gods and was deified after death.

The Ennead of Heliopolis

The nine Gods of Heliopolis, the City of the Sun, were known collectively as the Ennead: Atum, Geb, Isis, Nut, Osiris, Nephthys, Set, Shu, and Tefnut.
