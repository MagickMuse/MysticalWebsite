Magickal Properties

Fauns are Italian spirits who manifest as men with goats' legs and horns, similar to Pan and the satyrs. They are among the spirits in the retinue of Faunus, the ancient Italian horned god. Like the satyrs, they have a reputation for being lascivious, wild, and lustful.

Fauns represent the wild, untamed forces of nature and fertility. They embody the same anarchic, joyful energy as their leader Faunus and the Greek satyrs. They are part of the sacred company of wild spirits that celebrate life's physical pleasures.

Ritual Uses

Fauns are not typically petitioned individually but are part of the wild retinue when working with Faunus. They represent the uncontrolled fertility and wildness of nature.

Offerings

No specific offerings are recorded for Fauns as individual spirits. When working with Faunus, one works with his entire retinue.

Favored People

None specified—Fauns are wild spirits of nature rather than spirits who favor particular devotees.

Petition For

Not typically petitioned individually. Work with Faunus if seeking the energies these spirits represent.

Warnings

Fauns share the wild, lustful nature of satyrs and other spirits in Faunus' company. They are uncontrollable forces of nature and fertility.

Additional Notes

The word "faun" comes from Faunus, their leader. They are the Italian equivalent of Greek satyrs and share many characteristics with Pan's followers. Like other horned spirits and their companions, Fauns were eventually identified with demons and the devil by Christianity, though they are pre-Christian nature spirits.
