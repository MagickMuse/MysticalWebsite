Bhadra, whose name means "auspicious," is a minor Hindu goddess of the Epic and Puranic traditions. She is an attendant of Shiva and belongs to the household of divine companions who accompany him in the mountains and in the sacred precincts of his cult.

Domains

Auspiciousness, the good fortune that surrounds sacred company, and quiet devotional attendance upon the great gods.

Iconography

Bhadra is generally depicted seated. Her attributes are the blue lotus, fruit, a rosary (aksamala), and the trident, the last of which marks her as belonging to the sphere of Shiva.

Petition For

Auspicious beginnings, the blessing of good company, and steady devotional practice.
