Kanaloa is the Hawaiian god of the ocean, the underworld, and healing. He rules the vast depths of the sea and all its creatures, particularly the octopus and squid. Kanaloa is inseparable from Kane, the creator god; they are eternal companions who work together to maintain cosmic balance. Where Kane brings life and light, Kanaloa governs the dark depths and the mysteries of the underworld.

Kanaloa also presides over healing springs and fresh water sources, linking the ocean depths with healing waters on land.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kanaloa for ocean safety, fishing success, accessing deep wisdom, healing work (especially with water), and navigating the mysteries of death and the underworld. His energy supports diving deep into the subconscious and emotional healing through water.

Warnings

The ocean is powerful and dangerous. Kanaloa demands respect for the sea's might. Never take the ocean's power lightly when working with his energy. Respect oceanic rites and do not pollute or desecrate the sea when invoking Kanaloa. Offer proper maritime libations and avoid commanding storms for selfish ends.
