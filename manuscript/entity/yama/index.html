Classification

Lord of Death who rules the Hindu and Buddhist Hells.

Magickal Properties

Yama lives with his minions in the sixteen sealed-up iron cities of Hell. He maintains the Book of Destiny, in which the life span allotted to each individual is recorded. He himself sometimes comes to collect souls, carrying a rope with which to lasso and herd them to his realm.

According to Thai myth, Yama contracts with individuals at birth, establishing the exact moment of their death. Should an individual die before their appointed time (through suicide, violent death, or magickal causes), their soul must linger on Earth as a ghost until the scheduled appointment arrives. Yama will not see anyone early, nor will he adjust appointments; there are no temples dedicated to him, nor rituals officially intended to beckon or ward him off. He arrives at the appointed time, not one second before or after.

Yama maintains a record of all human deeds and calculates each individual's karmic debts, determining how long they must remain in Hell and the nature of their next incarnation. In Hindu cosmology, no event on Earth, regardless of how seemingly insignificant, goes unrecorded; the Akashic Records document every single action, thought, and intent that has ever occurred. Yama is among the sacred guardians of these records, one of the few with ready access to them.

Origin

Yama and his sister Yami are children of Surya, the Sun, and Saranya, daughter of Tvastr, the Vedic celestial artisan. According to the Rig Veda, Yama achieved his position by refusing to unite with Yami, preferring to die childless rather than commit incest. With no descendants to facilitate his rebirth, he became trapped in the realm of death and became its overlord.

Manifestation

Yama may manifest as a man or as a man with the head of a water buffalo.

Attributes

A cup, a sword, and a looped rope (lasso or noose). Mount: a black water buffalo. Direction: east. Bird: crow. Colors: red and black.
