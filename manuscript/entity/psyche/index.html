Magickal Properties

Psyche evolved from mortal princess to working goddess of love. Her myth encodes initiation through trials, death, and rebirth, making her approachable for compassionate, romantic, and erotic blessings.

Ritual Uses

Petition Psyche for compassionate love, reconciliation, soul growth, and initiatory guidance through difficult passages.

Offerings

Luxurious food and drink. Images of hearts and butterflies.

Favored People

Lovers, those seeking true love, and practitioners working romantic magick.

Petition For

Love, romance, erotic blessings, compassionate love, assistance, soul growth, initiation.

Warnings

None specified in traditional devotion. Approach with sincerity and respect for vows and trust.

Additional Notes

Traditionally portrayed as a beautiful young woman with butterfly wings. Consort: Cupid. Creature: Butterfly.

History

Tale Summary

Psyche’s story appears in Apuleius’ The Golden Ass. Venus sent Cupid to humble Psyche but Cupid fell in love. After secret union and separation through mistrust, Psyche completed impossible tasks (including a descent to Hades), died, and was revived by ambrosia on Olympus.

Interpretation

The tasks function as spiritual initiation. The descent represents death and rebirth. The myth is one of the world’s great love stories and frames Psyche as an active, compassionate goddess of love.
