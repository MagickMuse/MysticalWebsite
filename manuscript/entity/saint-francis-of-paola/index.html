Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Francis was born in Paola, Calabria on March 27, 1416 to aged parents who credited Francis of Assisi with interceding on their behalf for a child. At age twelve, Francis was sent to live with friars, where he first became associated with miracles, including bilocation.

The Fire Miracles

  • Absorbed in prayer when he should have been cooking, the fire went out, yet the beans were perfectly cooked
  • Sent to fetch burning coals for the thurible, young Francis had no container and carried the coals in the folds of his cassock. Neither the cassock nor Francis caught fire
  • He entered a blazing furnace and emerged unscathed

The Hermit Order

After pilgrimage with his parents to Assisi, Monte Cassino, Loreto, and Rome, Francis lived as a hermit from 1430-1435. Twelve disciples joined him, becoming the Hermits of Saint Francis (later renamed Minim Friars). In 1436, they built a church and cloister, founding a new monastic order.

Other Miracles

  • Caused a spring to gush forth by striking a rock, reproducing Moses's miracle
  • When hunters cooked his pet lamb Martinello, Francis resurrected it by calling its name
  • Returned his sister's son to life
  • Healed lepers, the blind, and the paralyzed
  • Exorcised demons from the possessed

Walking on Water

Summoned to Sicily, Francis was refused ferry passage. He spread his cloak on the water and walked across the Strait of Messina. Upon arrival, he resurrected a hanged man who then joined his order.

The Bleeding Coin

When King Ferrante offered Francis a gold coin, he refused. The king insisted, so Francis took and broke it. Blood flowed from the coin. Francis told the king: "This is the blood of your subjects whom you oppress and who cry out to God for vengeance."

Death

Francis spent his last three months in solitude, preparing for death. The day before he died, shortly after his ninety-first birthday, he summoned his community, exhorting them to maintain high standards and be charitable. On Good Friday, April 2, 1507, he received last rites, appointed a successor, and requested the Gospel of John be read. He died while listening.
