Phii Tai Hong

Favored People
Murder victims seeking justice (temporarily)
Culture
Thai
Petition for
Justice for murder victims, identifying killers, locating bodies for burial
Gender
Varied
Type
Spirit

Souls of Violent Death

Phii Tai Hong are souls of those who died violent deaths. In turn, they become violent ghosts. The Phii Tai Hong aren't just wailers or chain-rattlers. These ghosts actually attack, harm, and kill the living.

Possession for Justice

Phii Tai Hong can possess a living person in similar manner to a Dybbuk. However, possession by Phii Tai Hong is not always negative nor suffered long-term. Murder victims may simply wish to identify their killers or the location of their body so that they can receive burial or be returned to loved ones. Once justice is done, the Phii Tai Hong leaves, never to return. Alternatively, they maintain relations with family members who request that spirit mediums summon and channel the ghost.

Burial vs Cremation

Those perceived as likely to become Phii Tai Hong are usually buried as opposed to being given a traditional Thai Buddhist cremation, which is perceived as liberating the soul. Interment in Earth attempts to contain the soul or at least delay ghost transformation.

Vulnerable to Sorcerers

Phii Tai Hong are dangerous but vulnerable, too. They are susceptible to capture by skilled sorcerers who know how to control and direct the Phii Tai Hong's attacks: they become the sorcerer's supernatural slaves. Sorcerers dig up corpses of murder victims, especially anonymous young women.

Power Hierarchy

Not all Phii Tai Hong possess equal amounts of power. Manner of death determines power:

  • Those who died suddenly accompanied by tremendous pain and suffering are most feared
  • Generally those who died via scheduled execution, suicide, or lingering illness are less powerful
  • Most powerful: A woman who died in childbirth with her dead baby still in the womb
  • Exceptions: Souls of notorious criminals, thugs, and gangsters, whose destructive behavior continues after death

Attack Patterns

In general, Phii Tai Hong attack victims in the same way they were killed. They tend to linger where they died, making war zones and bad traffic intersections even worse. They also like places where people die or where corpses are stored, such as hospitals or morgues.
