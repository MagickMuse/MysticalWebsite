Eight-Legged Stallion

Sleipnir is Odin's eight-legged stallion, the swiftest horse in all the nine worlds. His eight legs allow him to traverse the boundaries between worlds, carrying Odin across the rainbow bridge Bifrost and through the realms of the living and the dead.

Loki's Offspring

Sleipnir was born when Loki transformed into a mare to distract a giant's stallion (Svadilfari) during the construction of Asgard's walls. The result of their union was Sleipnir, whom Loki gave to Odin as a mount.

Walker Between Worlds

Sleipnir can travel through air, over land, across water, and even into the underworld. His ability to cross boundaries makes him the perfect mount for Odin in his role as psychopomp and wanderer between worlds.

Symbols of Shamanic Journey

The eight legs may represent the eight directions or the World Tree's eight branches, making Sleipnir a symbol of shamanic journey and the ability to travel the cosmic axis.
