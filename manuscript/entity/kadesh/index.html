Kadesh is a Syrian goddess of sacred sexuality, ecstasy, pleasure, and fertility who was widely venerated in ancient Canaan and Egypt. Her name means "Holy One." She embodies the sacred nature of sexuality and sensual pleasure. Kadesh is typically depicted naked, standing on a lion, holding serpents and lotus flowers.

In Egypt, she was syncretized with Hathor and Astarte, forming a trinity of love goddesses. Kadesh represents the divine feminine principle of pleasure and the sacredness of erotic love.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kadesh for love magick, attraction, celebrating sacred sexuality, and honoring the body as a temple of pleasure. Her energy supports sex-positive practices, tantra, and the integration of sexuality with spirituality. She can be petitioned for fertility and for blessing romantic relationships with passion.

Offerings

Flowers, perfume, wine, incense, sensual offerings that honor pleasure and beauty.

Warnings

Approach with respect for the sacred nature of sexuality. Kadesh teaches that pleasure is holy, but requires consent, respect, and awareness of the divine within all partners.
