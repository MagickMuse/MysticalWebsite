The Wind Mother

Szélanya literally means "Mother of the Winds." She is the wind, but also the goddess who controls the winds. Szélanya stores and guards the winds in a cave at the top of a tall mountain at the end of the world. It's very important not to offend her because if she feels slighted, she releases violent storm winds. The winds mirror her moods.

Manifestation

Szélanya usually manifests as an elderly lady, but she can also literally appear as wind.

Folklore

Post-Christianity, Szélanya was demoted to a witch goddess, but traditions about her survived as superstitions. It is considered dangerous to curse the wind or to refer to it directly using derogatory words for witch. According to common belief in Central and Eastern Europe, whirlwinds contain witches, and it became traditional to slash or cut a whirlwind with an ax or knife to weaken, frighten, or harm the witch within. Hungarian folk wisdom strongly advises against this, however, as it only enrages her, raising her hackles and, with them, the winds.
