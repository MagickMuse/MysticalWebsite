Artemis

Magickal Properties

Ancient Cretan goddess, presiding spirit of Mount Dikte with dominion over protection, fertility, and abundance. Known as "Sweet Maiden" which may be euphemism to appease her fierce, volatile nature. Spirit of female mysteries and protectress of wild animals who has power to transform and protect those fleeing sexual violence.

Ritual Uses

Invoke for protection from sexual advances or violence, petition for fertility and abundance, seek protection for wild animals. Use in female mystery traditions and rituals for those needing to escape dangerous situations through transformation.

Offerings

Honey, spring water, images of wild animals, double axes (labrys).

Favored People

Those seeking protection from sexual violence, women in dangerous situations, protectors of wild animals, practitioners of female mysteries, those needing fertility and abundance.

Petition For

Protection from sexual violence, fertility, abundance, protection of wild animals, aid in escaping dangerous situations, female empowerment.

Warnings

Fierce, volatile spirit, approach with respect. Her name may be euphemism to appease her volatile nature. Temples were guarded by dogs fiercer than bears.

Additional Notes

According to myth, fled from sexual advances of King Minos by flinging herself into sea, was rescued by fishermen with nets and emerged as goddess. Also known as Aphaia ("the invisible") and Diktynna ("Lady of the Nets"). Cretan artifacts and coins indicate she was major goddess, not minor spirit. Depicted with fierce face sometimes reminiscent of Gorgon, holds snakes and may suckle griffins.
