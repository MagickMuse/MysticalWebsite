Source Notes

Arachne is a minor goddess of the Roman tradition, concerned with the craft of weaving. According to myth, she was transformed by Athena after losing a weaving contest, and in some traditions is said to weave the threads of human destiny. She is also connected to the Italian Tarantism dance cult.
