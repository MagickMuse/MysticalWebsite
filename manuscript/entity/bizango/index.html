⚠️ The Bizango are venerated within Haitian Vodou by closed secret societies. This is a strictly initiatory practice and should never be approached without lineage, training, and explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Haitian spirits served by secret mystical societies with sometimes sinister reputation for social enforcement
  • Subset of lwa understood as maximum extreme Petro spirits, fierce, powerful, fast, effective but dangerous
  • Associated with creation of zombis and enforcement of social order through mystical means
  • Include spirits like Baron Kriminel, Ezili Zandor, Marinette

Ritual Uses

  • Served only within proper secret society context and initiation
  • Used for extreme social enforcement and mystical punishment of wrongdoers
  • Creation and control of zombis for enforcement purposes
  • Not for casual practitioners, requires proper lineage and training

Offerings

Attributes and symbols: skulls, coffins, crosses, crucifixes

Favored People

Petition For

Warnings

These are maximum extreme Petro spirits, potentially very dangerous if approached improperly. Associated with secret societies that enforce social order through fear and mystical means.

Additional Notes

  • Two meanings: 1) Secret mystical societies 2) The spirits served by these societies
  • Related spirits: Baron Kriminel, Ezili Zandor, Marinette, other extreme Petro lwa
  • Symbols: Skulls, coffins, crosses, crucifixes characteristic of Bizango spiritual work
  • Not for casual practice, requires proper initiation and lineage training
