Magickal Properties

  • Scottish/Irish spirits witnessed in lonely places beside streams washing linens of those fated to die soon
  • Category of spirits, not single individual - women who died in childbirth before their allotted time
  • Fated to keep laundering clothes until their originally allotted lifespan arrives and they are released
  • Some versions are wish-giving spirits if proper rituals are performed

Ritual Uses

  • Various rituals exist for gaining wishes, but extremely dangerous if performed incorrectly
  • Must catch hold of her with left hand before she sees you for one wish
  • Getting between her and water while holding her with left hand grants three wishes
  • Bean Nighe of Isle of Skye reveals fate and destiny if caught and held

Offerings

No traditional offerings - these are spirits bound by fate, not venerated deities

Favored People

Those brave enough to attempt dangerous wish rituals (though this is not recommended)

Petition For

Wish granting, revelation of fate and destiny, though extremely risky

Warnings

EXTREME DANGER: If she sees you before you see her, she may inflict serious bodily harm. These are grouchy spirits who would prefer to be left alone. Some versions require nursing at her breast to become foster child. All wish-granting requires absolute honesty or wishes are forfeit.

Additional Notes

  • Manifestation varies: petite women in green, may have red webbed feet, long pendulous breasts, one nostril, or one large protruding tooth
  • Seeing Bean Nighe is harbinger of death (possibly for viewer or someone known to them)
  • Classification: Washer at the Ford
