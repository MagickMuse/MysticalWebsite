Magickal Properties

Atete is an Oromo goddess of reproduction and women’s wellbeing. She bestows fertility, safeguards pregnancy and childbirth, and protects households through stormy seasons and at the New Year.

Ritual Uses

Petition for conception, healthy pregnancy, safe delivery, and protection through volatile weather and yearly transitions.

Offerings

Libations and prayers at her seasonal three‑day ceremonies. Water rites are emphasized.

Favored People

Women, mothers‑to‑be, and families seeking blessing and protection.

Petition For

Fertility, pregnancy support, safe childbirth, and household protection.

Warnings

Observe her sacred periods with reverence; rites are conducted with water‑purity emphasis.

Additional Notes

Major observances: a three‑day rite in July for protection during dangerous weather, and a three‑day rite at the Ethiopian New Year.

Encyclopedia Source Notes (Kafa Tradition)

In the Kafa tradition of northeastern Africa in Ethiopia, Atete is a fertility goddess who became assimilated into the Christian cult of the Virgin Mary. She is probably the subject of an ancient fertility rite performed by women who collect various sacred plants and throw them into the river. The festival is known as Astar yo Mariam, the Epiphany of Mary.
