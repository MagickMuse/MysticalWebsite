Magickal Properties

The Akkan are a family of four Saami fertility spirits known as "grandmothers", Maderakka (the mother) and her three daughters: Sarakka, Juksakka, and Ugsakka. These powerful spirits control all aspects of human reproduction and fertility, working cooperatively to ensure successful conception, pregnancy, and childbirth.

Ritual Uses

The Akkan are venerated exclusively by women (except for Sarakka) and serve specific functions throughout the reproductive cycle. Petition Sarakka for female children and Juksakka for male children. Ugsakka and Juksakka have the power to change a child's sex before birth if properly petitioned. They make both women and animals fertile in exchange for proper offerings.

Offerings

Daily offerings of food and drink at the hearth or stove. They expect regular nourishment and appreciate consistent devotion from their petitioners.

Favored People

Women seeking fertility, pregnancy, and childbirth assistance. Those desiring abundance and protection for their families and livestock.

Petition For

Fertility and conception, safe pregnancy and childbirth, protection of children, abundance in family life, successful animal breeding.

Additional Notes

  • Maderakka creates and guards souls, representing general fertility and abundance
  • Sarakka opens the womb to permit conception, petitioned for female children
  • Juksakka is a guardian spirit dwelling beneath the earth's surface who protects living and unborn children, petitioned for male children
  • Ugsakka lives beneath the threshold and guards mothers and children within the home

All four appear on traditional Saami drum paintings and work as a unified family system.
