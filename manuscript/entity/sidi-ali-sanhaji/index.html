Venerated as a Sufi saint, Sidi Ali Sanhaji was a criminal and murderer who found redemption through a miraculous event.

Life and Legend

Sidi Ali Sanhaji, not yet a saint, was secretly lurking in a cemetery one night, out on nefarious business. He had already been responsible for ninety-nine deaths. That night he witnessed a miracle.

A young girl had refused the advances of a man who vowed to have her dead or alive. She died and was buried. The man approached her tomb late at night, opened it, removed her body, tore off her shroud, and attempted to possess her corpse.

The dead girl raised an arm to block her attacker. He cut it off. When she resisted with the other arm, he cut that off too. When she squeezed her legs together, he cut them off as well.

Sidi Ali decided there was no better candidate for his hundredth victim than this monstrous man. He vowed that with this last murder, he would redeem himself for the previous ninety-nine and never kill again.

A voice cried out three times: "Acquit yourself, Father of Acquittals!" And he did. Sidi Ali killed the man, then put the girl back together, got her a fresh shroud, and buried her again. He placed an olive branch at the head of the tomb, saying that if it was green the next morning, he would know God had accepted his vow and pardoned him.

When Sidi Ali returned the next day, the small cut branch had transformed into a magnificent olive tree covered with leaves.

Later Life

Sidi Ali became a wandering holy man, preaching the divine word and performing miracles. He was a disciple of Sidi Ali ben Hamdush. When he died, his disciples built him a mausoleum, but it collapsed. Its replacement also collapsed. Sidi Ali appeared to the caretaker of his shrine in a dream, advising that he did not wish a traditional koubba built over his grave.

Sacred Sites

Sidi Ali Sanhaji is buried in Lekhous, near Larache, Morocco. The sanctuary dedicated to him is the annual meeting place for the Hamadsha Brotherhood en route to Sidi Ali ben Hamdush's grave.
