Magickal Properties

Agathodaemon is a benevolent spirit of healing, protection, luck, and good fortune who manifests consistently as a snake, either ordinary snake or hovering, winged serpent. "Agathos" means good. It is a title, not a personal name. He is consistently benevolent and companionable.

Ritual Uses

Work with Agathodaemon for healing, protection, and good fortune rituals. He evolved into a Gnostic guardian angel in Egypt and survives in modern Egypt as snake-shaped guardian spirits protecting each of Cairo's quarters. He happily shares altar space with other benevolent spirits.

Offerings

Wine is the traditional offering, presented with respect for his consistently benevolent nature.

Favored People

Those seeking healing, protection, luck, and good fortune. He is companionable with other benevolent spirits and welcomes those who approach with positive intentions.

Petition For

Healing from illness, protection from harm, luck in endeavors, good fortune in general circumstances, and guardianship from benevolent spiritual forces.

Warnings

Consistently benevolent spirit with no known negative aspects. He is safe to work with and approach.

Additional Notes

Veneration traveled from Egypt to Greece and Italy, showing his widespread appeal. He is companionable with Serapis, Tyche, and Hermes. He may be fond of Sicily's Saint Agatha, who may be an ancient serpent-goddess in disguise. Each of Cairo's quarters allegedly possesses its own snake-shaped guardian spirit, its own Agathodaemon.

Origin in Alexandria

Agathodaemon is known locally from Alexandria. The Egyptian city is the center where his cult is most clearly attested in the historical record.

Possible Androgynous Origin

He may have originated as an androgynous fertility spirit. In that earlier form his nature does not split into male and female but holds both together, with his work bound to the generative powers of the land.

Consort of Agathe Tyche

In his later development Agathodaemon becomes identified as the consort of Agathe Tyche, an aspect of Tyche. The good spirit and the good fortune are paired as a couple, his benevolence joined to her gift of favorable circumstance.

Libations and Household Guardianship

Libations were made regularly to Agathodaemon after meals, and he was regarded as a friendly household guardian. His place in the home is the daily one of the table and the doorway, honored at the close of each meal.
