Magickal Properties

Chelone was a mountain Nymph who objected to the nuptials of Hera and Zeus. As punishment, Zeus transformed Chelone into a turtle, shutting her up by making her carry her house wherever she goes. She is the Turtle Goddess and the spirit of silence, operating as both one who enforces silence and protects those who have been silenced.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Chelone when you need silence or when you have been silenced. She serves as matron of political prisoners and those persecuted for voicing objections. She also protects those afraid to speak their minds and provides guidance for the homeless and those who carry all their possessions.

Offerings

Decorate altar space with turtle imagery or maintain a terrarium for turtles. Offer flowers of Chelone species, known as turtlehead flowers. Show respect for those who have been silenced or carry heavy burdens.

Favored People

Political prisoners and those persecuted for speaking truth, people who have been silenced or are afraid to voice their opinions, homeless individuals and those able to carry all possessions on their backs.

Petition For

The gift of silence when discretion is needed, protection for those who have been silenced, assistance for homeless individuals, aid for those persecuted for telling the truth, strength to speak when necessary.

Warnings

Remember that her transformation was a punishment for speaking out against divine authority. Approach with respect for both the power of silence and the courage required to speak truth.

Additional Notes

Her name means she was forced to carry her house wherever she goes after Zeus caused it to collapse on her. In some versions, Hermes cast the spell as Zeus' messenger. She represents both the burden of carrying one's shelter and the protective power of having a safe space always available.
