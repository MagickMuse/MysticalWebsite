Magical Properties

Au Co is a mountain Fairy who, with Lac Long Quan, bore an egg‑sac that hatched one hundred children. Fifty remained with Au Co and became the Vietnamese people; one son became the first Vietnamese emperor. She protects Vietnam and its people.

Ritual Uses

Petition for national and family protection, fruitful lineage, and safe journeys between mountain and sea.

Offerings

Not specified in the text.

Favored People

Warnings

None stated.

Additional Notes

Lovers divided by elemental affinity, Au Co for the mountains, Lac Long Quan for the sea; their parting established dual guardianship.
