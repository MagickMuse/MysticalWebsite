🦉Shrine to Lilith

🦉
Lilith · Lilitu · La Broosha

First woman, dark maid, spirit of night and feminine independence. She does not bow. She does not soften. She is the wilderness reclaimed.

🌑
Spirit of darkness, night, and feminine independence. The first woman who would not lie beneath. She fled to the wilderness and became sovereign of her own becoming.

Voices of the Wilderness

Video

Offerings

Red wine. Dark breads. Pomegranates. Incense, especially myrrh. Black candles. Owl feathers. Mirrors. Menstrual blood. Written poems and invocations spoken aloud.

Correspondences

Element: All four

Planet: Moon, waning

Sacred Bird: Owl

Sacred Creatures: Jackals, hyenas, wild cats, ostriches, snakes, unicorns

Domains: Sexual autonomy, magickal instruction, dream walking, feminine sovereignty

Petition For

Sexual autonomy. Liberation from abusive dynamics. Magickal instruction. Independence. Revenge and justice for women. Empowerment. Protection from malevolent forces. Dream walking.

Favored People

Feminists. Sex workers. Witches. Outcasts. Night workers. Independent women. Those reclaiming personal power. Survivors of patriarchal oppression.

Sacred Timing

Solstices and equinoxes. The astrological sign of Scorpio. The waning moon. Any night the wilderness calls.

Words of Power

Dark Maid Lilith. Spirit of darkness, night, and feminine independence. First woman of the wilderness. She who would not lie beneath.

Epithets drawn from Lilith

⚠️
Notes and Cautions

First woman and goddess of independence. She does not tolerate disrespect, dishonesty, or attempts at control. Approach with reverence and authenticity. Never present her as demon rather than goddess. She is sovereign, not subordinate.
