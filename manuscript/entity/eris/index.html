Magickal Properties

Eris (Discordia) is the catalytic edge of truth that breaks stagnation. Far from petty drama, Eris reveals what polite fictions hide, exposes corruption, and re‑energizes systems ossified by fear of conflict. Her current moves through dissent, creative rivalry, and the sharp clarity that follows disruption.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Eris to surface the real issue, to end corrosive stalemates, to puncture lies, and to reconfigure unjust arrangements. Frame petitions around transparent process and restorative outcomes. Pair with protections and clear ground rules.

Offerings

A golden apple marked “for the fairest truth,” simple flowers, spoken vows to act with integrity, and written confessions burned to ash to seal new commitments.

Favored People

Truth‑tellers, auditors, reformers, artists of provocation, and those tasked with breaking stuck patterns without dehumanizing anyone involved.

Petition For

Catalyzing necessary change, exposing deceit, cutting through performative harmony, energizing honest debate, and restoring right relation through clarified roles and responsibilities.

Warnings

Do not weaponize Eris for revenge or spectacle. Poorly framed petitions amplify chaos. Set boundaries, name desired outcomes, and include safeguards for consent, accountability, and repair.

Additional Notes

Roman: Discordia. Related to Strife lines that echo through Até and to the oath‑enforcers (Erinyes) when truth and perjury collide; honor distinctions between catalyst and enforcer.
