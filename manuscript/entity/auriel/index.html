Magickal Properties

  • Archangel of Earth’s light, healing, nature, and quiet clarity
  • Brings steadiness, discernment, and grounded illumination

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for healing, study, and clear judgment
  • Blessing of land, gardens, and natural sanctuaries

Offerings

  • Quiet reverence, frankincense, clean water, green boughs or herbs

Favored People

Those seeking clarity, healers, guardians of nature, and sincere students

Petition For

Healing, lucid understanding, protection of places, and steadying one’s path

Warnings

Invoke with purity. Do not try to command or bind angelic forces. Keep rites calm and clean; avoid mixing angelic invocations with darker conjurations.

Additional Notes

  • Also known as Uriel in many traditions
  • Often associated with earthy light and the West in ceremonial systems
