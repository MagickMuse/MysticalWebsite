Classification

Unofficial saint.

Life and History

Ernesto Guevara de la Serna Lynch (1928-1967), better known as Che Guevara, was born in Argentina of Basque and Irish ancestry. A Marxist revolutionary and leader of Cuban guerrillas, his iconic image has become symbolic of revolution and counter-culture.

In the years since his death, he has emerged as a folk saint known as San Ernesto. Although one would think a fervent Marxist would be dismayed by religious shrines devoted to him, San Ernesto is credited with miracles.

Controversy

Che Guevara remains deeply controversial. While some perceive him as a revolutionary ideal, many Cubans consider him a butcher.

Even his birth date is disputed: documents indicate June 14, 1928, but a persistent story claims the date was falsified to conceal that his mother was already pregnant at her wedding. An astrologer allegedly revealed this, claiming to have been given the true date (May 14) for an accurate horoscope.

Death and Veneration

In 1967, Guevara was handed over to the Bolivian army by peasants he had been preaching to in La Higuera. He was executed, and his body brought to Vallegrande for display. The corpse allegedly would not close its eyes. His hands were cut off for identification.

What happened to his body is debated:

  • He may have been cremated and ashes scattered
  • He may have been secretly buried at Vallegrande's airstrip (remains recovered 1997 and brought to Cuba)
  • Some devotees insist he remains in Vallegrande

The display was meant as humiliation and warning to revolutionaries. Instead, local people saw his broken body and postmortem photos as resembling Christ taken from the cross. They perceived him as a spiritual martyr and began veneration immediately.

Powers and Domains

In life, Che was a military physician, so he may be invoked for healing, especially for soldiers. He apparently responds consistently to prayers for rain.

His ghost has been witnessed near La Higuera.

Maria Lionza Connection

He is a member of the Political Court in the religion of María Lionza, under the name Che Guevara (not San Ernesto).

Iconography

The famous iconic photograph taken by Alberto Díaz Gutiérrez (Korda).

Sacred Sites

  • His mausoleum in Santa Clara, Cuba
  • The monument and memorial in La Higuera, Bolivia

Feast Days

  • 9 October (death)
  • 14 May or 14 June (disputed birth)
