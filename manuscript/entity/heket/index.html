Heket may be the oldest Egyptian spirit of all. She is a spirit of childbirth and the protector of the dead and the newly born, associated with the tomb, birth, and resurrection, all the transitions between them.

Ritual Uses

Heket helps place the child in the womb and prevents miscarriage and stillbirth. She has dominion over contraceptives and presides over the sacred transitions of birth and death. Midwives in ancient Egypt were affiliated with shrines and temples, serving as her priestesses.

Warnings

Invoke Heket as a childbirth ally with clean water, small votives, and midwife-led rites. Do not substitute ritual for professional medical care and always involve trained birth attendants when needed.

Heket, a frog goddess, may or may not derive from the same roots as Hecate, who considers frogs and toads among her holy animals. Both goddesses guard the threshold between life and death, and both are associated with midwifery and sacred transitions. Birth was considered sacred and magickal but also a dangerous experience requiring divine protection.
