Age

◇Culture
DahomeyWest AfricanBenin
◇Gender
Male
◇Type
Deity

Age is a god of animals in the Fon tradition of Benin, West Africa. His domain is the wild creatures of the open country and the relationship between hunter and game.

Hunters of the Savannah

Age is revered by hunters in the savannah regions. The grasslands where game gathers are his territory, and those who go out to take animals from that landscape carry their work into his presence.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Age belongs to the Fon religious tradition of Benin. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform Fon rites outside of the cultural and initiatory context in which they belong.
