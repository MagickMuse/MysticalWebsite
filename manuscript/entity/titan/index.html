Pre-Olympian Pantheon

The Titans are a pantheon of Greek spirits who preceded the Olympians. They are the good-looking children of Gaia and Uranus, who refused to be ruled by the Olympians. After the ten-year Titanomachie war, most were locked in Tartarus.

The Titanomachy

The war, known as the Titanomachy, was waged in Thessaly and lasted ten years. The Olympians won when Gaia promised Zeus victory if he freed the Cyclopes and Hecatoncheires from their captivity in Tartarus. After their defeat, most Titans were locked in Tartarus or sent into exile, guarded by the Hecatoncheires. The Titanesses were generally spared. Mount Othrys was to the Titans what Mount Olympus is to the Olympians.
