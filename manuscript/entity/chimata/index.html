Dosojin

Magickal Properties

  • Lord of the crossroads, highways, byways, and footpaths
  • Ancient phallic spirit represented by phallic images placed at crossroads
  • Phallic symbols serve to ward off and police malicious spirits who congregate at crossroads
  • Invoked to heal erectile dysfunction and enhance fertility
  • May or may not be the same spirit as Dosojin

Ritual Uses

  • Protection at crossroads and road junctions
  • Warding off malicious spirits from crossroads
  • Healing sexual dysfunction
  • Enhancing fertility for both men and women
  • Blessing travels and journeys

Offerings

Place a smooth pebble at a crossroads' very point of intersection and say a prayer to him

Favored People

Travelers using crossroads and pathways, those seeking sexual healing or fertility assistance, people needing protection from malicious spirits

Petition For

Crossroads guidance and protection, removal of malicious spirits from road junctions, healing of erectile dysfunction, enhanced fertility for men and women, safe travels

Additional Notes

Chimata is represented by stone road markers and phallic symbols. His image is also sometimes found at onsen (traditional Japanese hot springs). Allegedly stroking these images will remedy sexual dysfunction and infertility for both men and women.

In addition to representing male fertility power, his phallic symbols serve to ward off and police the malicious spirits who sometimes congregate at crossroads.
