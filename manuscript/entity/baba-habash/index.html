Baba Habash is renowned for healing male and female infertility and restoring virility.

Overview

Baba Habash is a Siddi saint and the brother of fellow saints Baba Ghor and Mai Mishra. According to Siddi legend, the siblings traveled together from what is now Ethiopia to the kingdom of Gujarat in India.

History

Settling in Gujarat, Baba Habash and his siblings expanded the local agate industry, becoming wealthy traders. Following their deaths, the three siblings were recognized as saints. They are each venerated independently, as well as together.

Powers and Domains

Baba Habash is specifically invoked to restore, renew, or supply virility and to remedy male infertility. He may also be invoked to heal female infertility, especially in conjunction with Mai Mishra.

Sacred Sites

His tomb-shrine in Ratanpur, Gujarat, shared with Baba Ghor and Mai Mishra.
