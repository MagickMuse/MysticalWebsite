Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and Legend

According to Welsh legend, Saint Ffraid was an Irish princess who crossed the Irish Channel to Wales riding on a clump of sod, landing on the banks of the Conwy River. It is possible that Ffraid is a Welsh name for Saint Brigid.

Travel Blessings

It is traditional to request her blessings before embarking on a journey.

Feast Traditions

Horned cattle and sheep are eaten at feasts honoring her.

Attributes

Acorns, oak leaves.

Sacred Sites

  • The remains of her chapel, Capel San Ffraid at Treaddur Bay, reputedly stand on the sod that brought Ffraid to Wales
  • Many churches in Wales are named Llansanffraid in her honor
