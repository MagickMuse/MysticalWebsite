Magickal Properties

Delphin is a dolphin spirit in service to Poseidon, honored as a skilled mediator who secured Amphitrite’s marriage and was placed in the sky as Delphinus.

Ritual Uses

Invoke for safe sea travel, diplomatic negotiations, and romantic mediation when a reluctant party needs gentle persuasion.

Offerings

Libations poured into the sea, offerings of fish, and nautical gifts.

Favored People

Sailors and sea‑workers. Messengers and diplomats. Matchmakers and counselors.

Petition For

Safe passage on water, successful negotiations, divine favor, help with romantic matters.

Warnings

Operate with respect for Poseidon and oceanic powers; assistance flows through Poseidon’s favor.

Additional Notes

Sometimes said to be Amphitrite’s brother; honored in the constellation Delphinus.
