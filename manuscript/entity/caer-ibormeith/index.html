Magickal Properties

Caer Ibormeith is an Irish goddess of dreams and prophecy who transforms into a swan every other Samhain. Her name may mean Yew Berry. She appeared to Angus in his dreams for a full year, causing him to waste away with longing. When he finally found her among 150 pairs of swans at Loch Bel Dracon, he recognized her by the golden chain and crown she wore even in swan form. Angus transformed into a swan himself to be with her, and they flew together singing so beautifully that all who heard them slept for three days and nights.

Ritual Uses

Petition Caer Ibormeith for prophetic dreams by calling to her before sleep, especially around Samhain when her power peaks. Work with her for transformation magick and swan symbolism. Invoke her when seeking a romantic partner through dreams or visions. She can reveal future lovers through dream visitations.

Offerings

Songs, poetry, items honoring swans and dreams, offerings made near lakes or bodies of water where swans gather.

Favored People

Dreamers, those seeking prophetic visions, lovers longing for connection, people working with swan medicine, those undergoing personal transformation.

Petition For

Prophetic dreams, guidance to find true love, transformation and shapeshifting work, swan magick, dream visitations from distant beloveds.

Warnings

Her transformations are tied to specific times of year. Respect the seasonal nature of her power. Her father initially resisted her union with Angus, showing she has strong family bonds.

Additional Notes

Linked strongly to Samhain and the turning of seasons. Connected to the sacred number 150 (pairs of swans). Her union with Angus demonstrates the power of persistent dreaming and recognition across forms. Golden chains and crowns mark her even in animal form.
