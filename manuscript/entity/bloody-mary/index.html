Magickal Properties

  • Urban myth spirit associated with slumber parties but some claim experiences are genuine and she is real
  • Summoned by repeating "Bloody Mary" three times in darkened bathroom while looking in mirror
  • Variations include midnight timing, twirling while calling, sprinkling/coating mirror with water, lighting red candles
  • May have emerged from romantic magick spells, medium mirror-gazing practices, or menstrual ritual gone wrong

Ritual Uses

  • AVOID: Do not perform casually; if studied at all, treat as dangerous boundary work with strong protections, never alone
  • Theories connect to conjuring future spouse image in mirror, medium spirit communication, or shamanic rituals
  • Modern reports describe spirit possessing homeless girls in multiple cities, forcing them into prostitution/addiction

Offerings

None — focus on protection and safe divination practices instead

Favored People

Do not petition; focus on protection, banishing, and safe scrying methods

Warnings

EXTREMELY DANGEROUS. Do not perform mirror summoning rituals lightly or alone; reports include physical harm, scratches, psychological effects, possession of homeless girls.

Additional Notes

  • Possible identities: Mary Tudor (Bloody Mary, Queen of England 1516-1558) who burned 300+ at stake and suffered miscarriages
  • Ritual variations include taunting "Bloody Mary, where is your baby?" or "I killed your baby"
  • Reported effects: seeing Mary's face in mirror, Mary weeping tears of blood, being pulled into mirror, blinding/insanity
  • Modern merger with La Llorona in homeless shelter reports, spirit with flaming black tears feeding on fear/misery
  • Pseudonym "Mary Whales" sounds like "Mary Wails" (Wailing Woman) connecting to La Llorona tradition
