⚠️ Bossu is a lwa venerated within Haitian Vodou. This is a closed initiatory practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Powerful Haitian Vodou lwa associated with bulls, strength, and masculine virility
  • Name "Twa Cornes" means "Three Horns" referring to his bull-like attributes
  • Represents raw masculine power, agricultural fertility, and physical endurance
  • Connected to cattle, farming, and the productive power of domesticated animals

Ritual Uses

  • Invoke for physical strength, endurance, and masculine vitality
  • Petition for agricultural success, especially cattle breeding and farming
  • Seek his protection for livestock and farm animals
  • Call upon for courage and determination in physical challenges

Offerings

Rum, cigars, strong drinks, offerings to bulls and horned animals

Favored People

Farmers, cattle herders, those seeking strength and virility, workers needing endurance

Petition For

Physical strength, virility, agricultural success, protection of livestock, masculine power

Warnings

Powerful, potentially aggressive spirit. Approach with respect and proper Vodou protocols. Associated with bull energy and masculine force.

Additional Notes

  • Represents the domesticated power of the bull channeled for human benefit
  • Connected to both agricultural productivity and masculine sexual energy
  • His three horns may represent his dominion over multiple realms of power
  • Important lwa for rural communities dependent on cattle and farming
  • Embodies the balance between wild masculine force and civilized agricultural productivity
  • Similar to the biblical Ba'al in representing primal male vigor
  • Unpredictable and dangerous, manifesting the potential outcome of excess testosterone
  • Considered among the more volatile lwa and is identified as patron of less ethical sorcerers
  • Despite his volatile nature, women petition him for enhanced personal fertility
  • His altars are decorated with horns reflecting his bull nature
  • Lwa of aggressive action, sometimes necessary if dangerous
