Leshii

Magickal Properties

Female Russian forest spirits, counterparts to the horned male Leshii. Master shape-shifters who appear as an old hag with long pendulous breasts, a beautiful naked woman, or a spectral woman in a white sarafan.

The Birth Test

Wanderers sometimes witness Leshovikha giving birth. The proper response is to cover the baby with your clothing if needed, but never offer Christian prayers or the sign of the cross, which enrages the mother.

Those who say the opportunity to serve is its own reward receive a future filled with good fortune. Those who request payment receive a reward, but once they leave the forest it turns to dust or ashes. The test reveals an ancient truth: genuine service requires no reward. Those who understand this receive lasting blessings; those who seek immediate gain receive only ephemeral rewards.
