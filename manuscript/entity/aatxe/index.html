Magickal Properties

Aatxe is a guardian spirit of Earth's riches, especially buried treasure, with dominion over justice and truth. He emerges during storms which he creates, signaling his presence when he wishes to appear. As a master shape-shifter, he can appear in any form but favors the red bull manifestation.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Aatxe for protection of Earth's treasures and sacred spaces. He can be called upon to enforce justice against those who disrespect the Earth, spirits, or sacred rituals. His storms can be petitioned to cleanse an area of negative influences.

Offerings

Aatxe requires no material offerings. Instead, approach him with complete honesty, bravery, and deep respect. Show reverence for Earth and all her creatures. Maintain sacred spaces and protect natural environments.

Favored People

Those who are genuinely brave, completely honest, and deeply respectful toward Earth, spirits, and sacred rituals. He favors environmental protectors and those who honor ancient traditions.

Petition For

Justice against environmental destruction, protection of buried treasures and sacred sites, assistance in matters requiring absolute honesty, help in exposing lies and cheating, guidance in showing proper respect to spirits and Earth.

Warnings

Never approach Aatxe with lies or deception. He will see through any falsehood and react with rage. His temper is fierce when witnessing disrespect toward Earth or spirits. Do not attempt to command or bargain with him; show only humble respect and honest petition.

Additional Notes

Aatxe lives deep within mountain caverns in the Pyrenees but emerges on stormy nights of his own choosing. The storms themselves signal his presence and power. He may be Mari's lover, enforcer, or even one of her many manifestations. His sacred color is red, appearing as a red bull or bull of red flames.

Etsai: The Evolved Name

Another Basque spirit named Etsai may or may not be identical with Aatxe. It remains unclear whether these were originally two distinct spirits or whether Aatxe evolved into Etsai, a spelling more amenable to French and Spanish clerics during the Inquisition's persecution of Basque witches. The name "Etsai" has become synonymous with "devil" in Christian contexts.

The Mountain Teacher

Etsai is frequently described as an "evil spirit," though whether this reputation is ancient or stems solely from Christian perceptions is now unknown. Etsai allegedly teaches his devotees arts described by Christian sources as "diabolical." His classroom is a mountain cave. According to legend, even his students fear him because he always forces one student to remain permanently with him following graduation. What happens to those who stay is unclear, though the intended Christian implication is that staying with Etsai is similar to being trapped in Hell.
