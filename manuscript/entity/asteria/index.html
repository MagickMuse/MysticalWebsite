Magickal Properties

Titan goddess of the starry night and the magickal arts of night: astrology, divination, dreaming, necromancy, and oracles.

Ritual Uses

Petition for prophetic dreams, star-guided divination, and night magick. Seek her aid to read omens in the heavens.

Offerings

Fragrant incense, divination tools, and night‑time vigils; pilgrimage in spirit to Delos.

Favored People

Astrologers, diviners, dream-workers, and necromancers who work ethically.

Petition For

Clear oracular messages, guidance through dreams, and mastery of celestial arts.

Warnings

Approach with purity of intent; do not profane the dead.

Additional Notes

Mother or close kin to Hekate in some strands; sacred bird is the quail; Delos is her site.
