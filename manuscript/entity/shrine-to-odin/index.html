Odin

Odhinn · Wotan · Woden · The All-Father · Glory Bearer · The Raven Lord · Master of the Gallows

One-eyed wanderer of wisdom, war, and ecstatic shamanic vision.

He hung nine days from the World Tree, sacrificed himself to himself, and rose with the runes.

Offerings

Poetry, stories, mead, unique occult tools, handcrafted items, and knowledge he doesn't already know. He craves what is rare and well-wrought.

Correspondences

Numbers 3 and 9. Sacred animals: ravens Hugin and Munin, wolves Geri and Freki, the eight-legged Sleipnir, snakes, and bears. Day Wednesday. Runes Ansuz and Gar.

Petition For

Wisdom, occult knowledge, hospitality rewards, wealth, success, finding missing treasure, healing if he chooses, and protection from the Wild Hunt.

Favored People

Travelers, traders, warriors, occultists, shamans, poets, writers, and those who capture his fancy.

Sacred Days

Wednesday is Woden's Day. In Iceland the twelve days of Christmas are called Odin's Yule Host. Honor him on stormy nights when the Wild Hunt rides.

Words of Power

Allfather. Wanderer. One-Eye.
By the Tree, by the Spear, by the ravens at thy shoulder.
By Hugin and Munin, by the runes you bled to know.

Manifestation

Master of disguise, Odin walks Earth as a shabby, dusty traveler. Look for the white beard, the wide-brimmed hat hiding one eye, the black hooded cloak. He answers to over a hundred and seventy names. Be gracious to wandering strangers; you may be hosting the Allfather himself.

Notes and Cautions

Odin is generous to his friends and merciless to his enemies. He bestows wealth, success, and healing only when he chooses. Master of the Gallows once received human sacrifice; honor him with depth, not lightly. Fulfill every vow.
