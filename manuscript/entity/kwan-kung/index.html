Kwan Kung is a Chinese deity of loyalty and justice.

Lord of War

As Kuan Ti, the Chinese Lord of War, he is a deified general said to have been born in 162 CE. His own martial victories taught him that war is hell, so his spiritual powers are invoked to prevent wars and to protect people, civilians as well as soldiers, from its horrors. His birthday falls on the 13th day of the 5th month of the Chinese calendar.
