🕯️Thunder Gods Invocation

Perun is the Supreme Being of the Slavic pantheon and its most important God. He is the God of Thunder and Lightning, the Lord of all other Gods, and his name appears more often in the historic records of the religion than any other deity. So fearsome was his power over storms that people painted thundermarks on the roofs of their houses to protect themselves from his devastating lightning.

Realm

Perun dwells atop hills and high places, occupying the Heavenly realms. He is hot and dry in nature, the very embodiment of the sky's force.

Rival: Veles

His great counterpart and rival is Veles, who occupies the lower depths near rivers and rules the damp and cold among the roots of the World Tree. The enmity between them extends even to the Sun herself, who was considered Perun's wife. When the Sun vanished over the western horizon each evening, some traditions held that she had absconded to spend the night with Perun's enemy; others held that her reappearance in the east each morning meant she had been kidnapped by Perun.
