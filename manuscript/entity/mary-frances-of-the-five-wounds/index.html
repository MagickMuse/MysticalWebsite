Anna Maria Rosa Nicoletta Gallo (1715-1791), now known as Saint Mary Frances of the Five Wounds, is a superstar among the saints of Naples. She is also known by the epithet Saint of the Family, and her full dates of birth and death are 25 March 1715 and 6 October 1791. The daughter of an angry, raging man, she endured abuse and was worked almost to death in her youth. At sixteen, her father tried forcing her into a financially advantageous marriage, but she refused, becoming a Franciscan tertiary on 8 September 1731. Though joining the religious order, she continued living at home. Her father never forgave her for costing him a wealthy son-in-law.

Domains

Mary Frances is invoked for all blessings, especially peace in the family and fertility. A chair at her birthplace shrine has developed a reputation for providing fertility miracles.

Lore

Mary Frances was a religious ecstatic whose siblings and even her confessor mocked her visions. She received the stigmata but, not wishing to attract attention, prayed that the visible signs be hidden. Her prayers were answered: she felt the five bleeding wounds of Christ without visibly demonstrating them, experiencing the pain of the Crucifixion every Friday, especially during Lent.

A visionary and clairvoyant, Mary Frances spent her final thirty-eight years as a recluse.

Sacred Sites

Her birthplace near Speranzella Street in the Spanish Quarter of Naples has been converted to a shrine and museum. Her tomb rests in the Church of the Alcantarines, Saint Lucia de Monte, Naples.
