Classification

Roman Catholic saints; the twelve disciples of Jesus Christ.

The Twelve Apostles

The Apostles are the original twelve disciples chosen by Jesus: Peter, Andrew, James (the Greater), John, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James (the Lesser), Thaddaeus (Jude), Simon the Zealot, and Matthias (who replaced Judas Iscariot).

Powers and Domains

The Apostles are invoked for justice and legal matters. They are particularly associated with Pow-Wow magic, a German-American folk magic tradition also known as Braucherei.

The Sage Leaf Spell

In Pow-Wow tradition, write the names of the twelve Apostles on sage leaves and place them in your shoes before entering a courtroom or legal proceeding. This practice is believed to bring favorable judgment and success in legal matters.

Hung Jury Magic

The Apostles may be invoked in spells intended to produce a hung jury. This reflects their number, twelve, which corresponds to the traditional size of a jury.

Feast Day

15 July (Feast of All Apostles)
