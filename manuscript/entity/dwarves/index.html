Magickal Properties

Dwarves (Dvergar in Old Norse) are master craftsmen and ancient earth spirits of Norse and Germanic tradition. They dwell in mountains, caves, and beneath the earth, forging magickal weapons, jewelry, and treasures of unparalleled quality. Dwarves created Thor's hammer Mjolnir, Odin's spear Gungnir, Freya's golden necklace Brisingamen, and countless other legendary artifacts. They are skilled in metalwork, stonecraft, mining, and magickal arts. Originally formed from maggots in the flesh of the primordial giant Ymir, they were given intelligence and form by the gods. Dwarves possess deep knowledge of runes, herbs, and earth magick.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Dwarves for craftsmanship skills, metalworking, mining, and creation of magickal tools. They can teach rune magick, earth mysteries, and the secrets of forging enchanted objects. Petition them for assistance with any skilled craft or when seeking objects of power. They are knowledgeable about herbs and healing, particularly fertility and virility magick. Work with them for protection of treasures and prosperity magick.

Offerings

Hard liquor (especially mead or strong spirits), hearty food, well-crafted goods, gold and precious metals, respect for their craftsmanship. They will often state what they wish and are open to bargaining. They appreciate quality over quantity in offerings.

Favored People

Smiths, masons, miners, shamans, herbalists, warriors seeking magickal weapons, healers, craftspeople of all kinds, those who appreciate fine workmanship and skill.

Petition For

Skill in smithcraft and metalworking, creation of powerful magickal objects, knowledge of runes and earth magick, protection of treasures, healing (including fertility and virility remedies), abundance and prosperity, mastery of crafts.

Warnings

Dwarves are stubborn, proud, touchy and volatile. They do not respond well to surreptitious or acquisitive behavior and must be addressed respectfully. Never try to cheat or steal from Dwarves. They hold grudges and exact terrible revenge for insults. Post-Christian reinterpretations sometimes cast them negatively as demons or bringers of disease, but this reflects later bias. Always bargain fairly and honor your agreements with them.

Additional Notes

Famous Dwarves include the brothers Brokk and Eitri (forgers of Mjolnir), the sons of Ivaldi (creators of Gungnir and Freya's boar), and Alviss (who sought to marry Thor's daughter). Four Dwarves, Norðri, Suðri, Austri, and Vestri, hold up the sky at the four cardinal directions. Dwarves cannot tolerate sunlight, which turns them to stone. They are typically portrayed as short and stocky but immensely strong. Their halls beneath mountains contain vast treasure hoards and workshops where they forge wonders. They are master illusionists and can make themselves invisible.
