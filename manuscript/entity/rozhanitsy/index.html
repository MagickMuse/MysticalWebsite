Magickal Properties

The Rozhanitsy are a pair of deer spirits, mother and daughter, believed to be part of the most ancient known pantheon of what is now Russia, dating back to Stone Age pre-agricultural hunter-gatherer times. They are deities of abundance and fertility.

The name Rozhanitsy refers to two distinct types of Russian female spirits. The horned Rozhanitsy described here are NOT the same as the Rozhanitsy Fate fairies who dwell in the Russian bathhouse.

Manifestations

The Rozhanitsy manifest in various ways:

  • As horned women
  • As women riding horned deer
  • As women riding horses but holding stag's antlers in their hands

The mother Rozhanitsy was the daughter of a deer. Her consort, Rod (Lord of Growth and Prosperity and father of her daughter), usually took the form of a stag.

Ritual Uses

Invoke the Rozhanitsy for fertility and abundance magick rooted in the most ancient traditions. Work with them to connect to Stone Age hunting and gathering practices, to honor the deer as sacred animal, and to understand the mother-daughter lineage of divine feminine power.

Offerings

Traditional offerings to deer goddesses and fertility spirits: seasonal foods, mead, milk.

Favored People

Hunters and gatherers, those seeking fertility and abundance, women honoring mother-daughter bonds.

Petition For

Fertility, abundance, connection to ancient hunting traditions, deer medicine, mother-daughter wisdom.

Warnings

Do not confuse the horned Rozhanitsy with the Rozhanitsy Fate fairies of the bathhouse; they are distinct spirits with different roles and domains.

Additional Notes

Era: Stone Age pre-agricultural hunter-gatherer times (Neolithic)

Pantheon: Earliest known Russian pantheon with Rod

Manifestations: Horned women, women riding horned deer, women on horses holding stag antlers

Parentage: Mother was daughter of a deer; father of daughter was Rod (as stag)

Sacred Animals: Deer, horses

Related Deities: Rod (consort/father)
