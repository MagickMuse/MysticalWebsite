Achilles is renowned for healing blood disorders including hemophilia, AIDS, and toxemia. He appears in dreams to offer advice and personally perform healings.

Magickal Properties

Achilles is the legendary Greek hero of the Trojan War, a liminal figure between human and divine as son of the sea goddess Thetis. His prowess, rage, pride, and healing skill made him a godlike warrior and master healer associated with the Black Sea and sea travel.

Ritual Uses

Work with Achilles for protection and guidance on the sea, aid in navigation, and healing concerning blood and wounding. He appears in dreams with advice and may personally perform healings. Honor him together with those he loves and is bound to.

Offerings

Lavish offerings suited to a prince and hero: precious metals and jewels, a vessel of saltwater, miniature boats, nautical items, and feasts. Always honor Patroclus alongside Achilles.

Favored People

Seafarers and warriors. Those he takes under his protection, especially companions and those who keep proper honors on his altar.

Petition For

Healing of blood disorders and difficult bleeding, protection and guidance at sea, navigation and sea safety, fortitude and prowess in just conflict.

Warnings

Achilles is a bargainer who expects generous tribute and clear respect, including honor for Patroclus. Inadequate offerings may be met with silence. Approach with sincerity, not for vanity or cruelty.

Additional Notes

  • Sacred island: Leuce (Snake Island) in the Black Sea, ancient center of his veneration
  • Bonds: deeply tied to Patroclus, Thetis, and marine powers; wears armor from Hephaestus
  • Imagery: nautical altar themes; sea birds tend his shrine in myth
