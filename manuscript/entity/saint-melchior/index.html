Classification

Venerated as a saint among most branches of Christianity.

Epithet

King of Light.

Overview

Because Saint Melchior, sultan of Arabia, was the eldest of the Three Kings, his image is traditionally placed before the others in manger scenes. Melchior is usually credited with bringing the gift of gold and is invoked for blessings of wealth. Alternative legends suggest that he brought the myrrh.

Appearance

Melchior is a small, elderly man with a long white beard wearing crimson robes.
