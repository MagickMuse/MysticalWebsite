⚠️ Aycayia is a sacred figure of Taino tradition. Taino spirituality is a closed practice belonging to the Indigenous people of the Caribbean. Do not invoke or work with her outside that lineage.

Classification

Taino mermaid spirit. The Taino are the Indigenous people of the Caribbean, including Puerto Rico, Hispaniola (Haiti and the Dominican Republic), Cuba, Jamaica, and the Bahamas.

The Voice That Sinks Ships

Aycayia is a mythical mermaid whose voice carries the power to sink ships. She belongs to the long lineage of singing sea spirits whose songs are at once unbearable beauty and certain death for any sailor who hears them and turns toward the sound.

Working With Her

Aycayia is not a spirit for outsiders to approach. She lives in the wild voice of the Caribbean sea and is held in stewardship by the Taino people. Listen for her in the surf, honor her presence by keeping the ocean clean and unpolluted, and leave the rest to those who carry her tradition by blood and lineage.
