Magickal Properties

Alala is the Greek goddess of the war cry, embodying the fierce battle shout that strikes terror into enemies and rallies warriors to victory. She personifies the primal scream of combat, that raw, powerful vocalization that can turn the tide of battle through pure psychological force. As daughter of Polemos (War personified), she embodies the inherited nature of conflict.

Ritual Uses

Work with Alala through battle chants and war cries performed before combat. She teaches warriors the proper war songs and chants, protects those who fight with honor and courage, and inspires battle fury in desperate moments. Use her energy for situations requiring courage and unified action.

Offerings

Battle chants, shouting her name before combat, victory songs sung after successful battles, and dedications of weapons and armor to her shrines. Group ceremonies that build unit cohesion.

Favored People

Warriors, soldiers, military personnel, athletes in competitive sports, anyone needing courage in confrontational situations, and people working to build team unity through shared voice.

Petition For

Courage, victory, protection in battle, strength in confrontational situations, unity of purpose in group efforts, and the power to inspire others through voice and presence.

Warnings

A war-cry spirit invoked for battle and victory, so only call her when you accept aggressive fallout and provide martial offerings and noise. Do not mix her rites with household fertility work because martial spirits can unsettle domestic sanctity.

Additional Notes

Her war cry is not merely noise, but a supernatural force that can shatter enemy morale, inspire warriors to superhuman acts of bravery, and communicate across battlefields despite chaos. She represents war as both destroyer and creator, destroying the old order while giving birth to new realities through the crucible of battle. Her essence survives in military traditions, sports team chants, and any situation where collective voice creates power.
