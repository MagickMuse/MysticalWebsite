Magickal Properties

Damballah Wedo is the primordial snake lwa of life, wealth, and wisdom, the great cosmic serpent who once was the only being who lay beneath Earth as a great snake, cushioning and protecting it from the watery abyss. His movements created mountains and valleys, shook stars into the sky, and released sacred waters. He is the sky father serpent who, together with his wife Ayida Wedo (the rainbow serpent/Aïdo-Hwedo), holds up the cosmos and maintains the balance of creation. He remains in love with Aïdo-Hwedo, and their love power manifests as white liquids: milk and semen.

He represents purity, wisdom, fertility, and the fundamental life force that sustains all existence. As an ancient primordial being, he is pre-articulate, he does not speak in human language but communicates through hisses, whistles, and movements. Despite his venerable age, he engages in sacred marriages with women and occasionally men.

Ritual Uses

Damballah Wedo is invoked for fundamental blessings of life, prosperity, fertility, and wisdom. He provides stability and protection, maintaining cosmic balance. He bestows wealth, prosperity, good health, and fertility to devotees and can expose the location of missing treasure. He regulates moisture and rain, maintaining the balance of forces that sustains all life on Earth. Work with him during major life transitions, when seeking foundational blessings, or when needing connection to primordial wisdom. Those possessed by him move like serpents, often climbing trees or lying on the ground in serpentine fashion, hissing and unable to speak.

Offerings

Exclusively white offerings: white candles, white flowers, white foods (rice, white eggs, milk, white cake), orgeat syrup, white fabrics and cloths. For lavish offerings: luxurious white fabrics, crystal or porcelain eggs, white snake items. Offerings should be pure and clean, left at the foot of sacred spaces rather than consumed by humans. Fresh clean water in white bowls. Keep shallow vessels of clean fresh water for him to curl up inside. Avoid any strong odors, tobacco, strong cleaning products, air fresheners, or colored items, he is a stickler for cleanliness.

Favored People

Devotees seeking fundamental life blessings, those needing fertility or prosperity at root level, people requiring cosmic wisdom and spiritual stability, families seeking protection and generational blessings, those who maintain ritual purity and cleanliness.

Petition For

Fundamental prosperity and wealth, fertility and life force, cosmic wisdom and spiritual knowledge, stability and protection, purification and blessing of new ventures, ancestral wisdom and connection, good health and healing, location of missing treasure, regulation of rain and moisture.

Wheel of the Year Connection

Damballah's feast day is March 17, which falls near Beltane and the spring celebrations. As the sacred sky serpent and creator of life force, honoring him during Beltane, a time of fertility, passion, and vital energy, creates a powerful resonance. His coils encase the earth in protective embrace, and the shedding of his skin falls as life-giving rain.

To honor the sacred serpent during Beltane, set up an altar with a white cloth, white flowers, a bowl of fresh water, and offerings of egg or chicken along with a white candle. As he is associated with intellect and intelligence, ask for his wisdom to guide you along your path.

Warnings

Offerings must be white and pure, he is extremely sensitive to cleanliness and strong odors. Do not attempt to handle live serpents during rituals unless you are an initiated practitioner with proper training. Always respect the paired nature of Damballah and Ayida Wedo, they are cosmically united. His rites require proper Vodou protocols and community context.

Additional Notes

Manifests as a huge snake so big his body forms seven thousand coils. Often syncretized with Saint Patrick (who drove snakes from Ireland) and Moses (staff that became serpent). Thursday is his sacred day. His veve (ritual symbol) features two intertwined serpents. Associated with white, silver, and all things pure. Sacred plants include bougainvillea and basil. Trees, especially silk cotton and Royal palms, are sacred to him. When he possesses devotees, they cannot speak but hiss and move as serpents, often climbing trees or lying on ground, requiring white cloths and care from attendants. He appears in dreams and is among the most beloved and important lwa. Usually not bothered for trivial matters, approach only when genuinely desperate or in serious trouble.
