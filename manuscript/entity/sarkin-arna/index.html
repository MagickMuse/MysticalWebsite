Chief of the Ninth House

Sarkin Arna is the chief of the ninth house of the Bori spirits, which comprises Pagan Bori spirits. (Some Bori spirits are Muslim; others still practice the traditional Hausa religion from which Bori derives.) He has at least three wives, and the affliction that he bestows and heals is alcoholism. He raised Mai Gizo, Owner of Matted Hair, after the boy's father, Sarkin Fulani, disowned him.
