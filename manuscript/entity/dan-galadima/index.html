⚠️ Dan Galadima is venerated within Hausa Bori traditions. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or under proper cultural guidance.

Magickal Properties

Dan Galadima ('The Prince') is the handsome prince, playboy, and gambler of the Bori pantheon. Son of Sarkin Aljan Biddarene but raised by Sarkin Aljan Suleimanu. He's exceptionally generous but his gifts come with strings attached, he expects repayment with interest. Once you accept his gifts, you're obligated to him like being in debt to the mafia.

Ritual Uses

Approach Dan Galadima when you need significant material assistance, but be very careful about accepting trivial gifts. Ask for what you really need because once you accept, this becomes a permanent relationship. He can break gambling addictions and heal compulsive spending disorders.

Offerings

Fragrant colognes and eaux de toilettes (Florida Water, Cananga Water, 4711), luxurious grooming items, hand mirrors for his vanity, large white kola nuts, handkerchiefs with cowrie shells, silk scarves, fans, gambling-related items (dice, cards).

Favored People

Beautiful women (he's very susceptible to their petitions), gamblers and those in financial trouble, people seeking luxury and refinement, those dealing with spending addictions.

Petition For

Wealth and valuable gifts, breaking gambling addictions, healing compulsive spending, luxury items and refined pleasures, assistance with financial obligations.

Warnings

He's extremely well-connected (four wives from different Bori houses) so crossing him is dangerous. His gifts always come with obligations. Don't accept trivial favors. Only petition for serious needs. Those possessed by him may feel urgent need to give everything away.

Additional Notes

A compulsive spender and gambler himself who gave all his children to his cousin Sarkin Ra to raise. He's associated with sweet, aromatic herbs. His mob boss nature means relationships with him are permanent once established.
