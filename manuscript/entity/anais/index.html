⚠️ Anaïs is venerated within Haitian Vodou. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or under proper cultural guidance.

Magickal Properties

Among Vodouistes, the image of the Black Madonna of Czestochowa is understood to portray the lwa Ezili Dantor. The baby in her arms is Anaïs, Ezili Dantor's beloved daughter. Ezili lives, breathes, and, crucially, works for Anaïs. Just as they appear together in the image, so the two spirits may be venerated together and will happily share altar space.

Ritual Uses

Anaïs serves as her mother's mediator. Those who seek Ezili may address themselves to Anaïs requesting that she please deliver their messages to her mother. Anaïs is her mother's translator and interpreter. According to some myths, Ezili can no longer speak, her tongue having been sliced out after the Haitian Revolution, and she is only able to articulate sounds. Anaïs understands her mother perfectly and can provide communication services.

Offerings

Offerings shared with Ezili Dantor, and childlike gifts appropriate for a young spirit.

Favored People

Those seeking to communicate with Ezili Dantor and children in general.

Petition For

Mediation with Ezili Dantor, translation services, and communication assistance when having difficulty reaching Ezili.

Additional Notes

Ezili Dantor may have other children, possibly as many as seven; however, Anaïs is the only consistent child. She, not any other of Ezili's children, is identified as the child accompanying the Madonna in the icon of Czestochowa. Anaïs appears as a child or a young woman. Propitiate Anaïs in order to get into Ezili's good graces.
