Aondo

Precautions
⚠️ Closed practice. Aondo belongs to the Tiv people of central Nigeria, West Africa. Engage only with explicit invitation and guidance from culture-keepers within that tradition.
Culture
NigerianWest African
Petition for
creation, sun, rain, storms
Gender
Male
Type
Deity

Aondo is the creator god of the Tiv people of central Nigeria in West Africa. He is an abstract principle who lives in the sky.

Domain

Aondo sends the sun out each morning. He roars with the thunder that heralds his storms, and he is the creator of the earth itself, the maker of the ground that lies beneath the sky he inhabits.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Aondo belongs to the religious tradition of the Tiv people of central Nigeria. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform Tiv rites outside of the cultural and initiatory context in which they belong.
