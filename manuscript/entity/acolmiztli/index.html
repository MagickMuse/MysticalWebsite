Mictlantecuhtli

Acolmiztli is a minor chthonic underworld god of the classical Mesoamerican Aztec tradition of Mexico. His name carries the meaning shoulderlion, an image that hints at the lion-shouldered or lion-bearing form by which he is identified among the lords of the dead.

The Mictlantecuhtli Complex

Acolmiztli is one of the deities collectively classed as the Mictlantecuhtli complex, a grouping of underworld gods who attend on Mictlantecuhtli, Lord of Mictlan. He is not the supreme ruler of the death realm but a specific presence within the larger company of chthonic gods whose collective work is the keeping of souls in the lower levels.

Domains

As a member of the Mictlantecuhtli complex, Acolmiztli governs a particular face of the underworld and its passages. His shoulderlion name suggests a guardian aspect, an underworld presence that carries strength on its shoulders even within the realm of the dead.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Acolmiztli belongs to the Aztec religious tradition of Mexico, a closed practice rooted in specific cultural and initiatory context. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform Aztec underworld rites outside of the lineages and communities to which they belong.
