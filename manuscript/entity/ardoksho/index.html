Magickal Properties

Ardoksho embodies the power of material abundance and prosperity that flows through trade routes and commerce. As a goddess who once blessed the extensive Kushan Empire's trade networks stretching from Afghanistan to Northern India, she carries the magickal essence of wealth generation, commercial success, and the transformation of resources into abundance.

Ritual Uses

Prosperity Workings: Create rituals focused on attracting material abundance, especially through trade, commerce, or business ventures.

Coin Consecration: Consecrate coins or currency in her name before important financial endeavors.

Abundance Altars: Establish ongoing altar work with symbols of material prosperity.

Offerings

Coins and currency from various nations, golden objects, amber, honey, fine fabrics, exotic spices, and items representing the fruits of successful trade.

Favored People

Merchants, traders, those seeking abundance and good fortune, business owners, and anyone seeking to improve their financial situation through legitimate commerce.

Petition For

Abundance, prosperity, money, good fortune, success in trade, financial stability, and protection of business relationships.

Warnings

Goddess of abundance sometimes identified with Hindu Lakshmi. Carrying coins with her image allegedly attracts wealth. She does not favor those who seek wealth through deception or exploitation.

Additional Notes

Frequently appeared on Kushan coins, suggesting she was invoked for protection and success in commercial ventures. Her seated throne posture suggests stability and sustained prosperity.
