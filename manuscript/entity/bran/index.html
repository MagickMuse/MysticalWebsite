Magickal Properties

Bran the Blessed is a Celtic god‑king of sovereignty, prophecy, and protection. A bridge between worlds, his oracular head protected Britain after death.

Ritual Uses

Invoke for protection of kin and land, oracular insight, safe passage across thresholds, and ancestral connection.

Offerings

Raven or crow feathers, mead, ale, offerings at riversides or bridges.

Favored People

Kings and leaders. Protectors of kinship bonds. Seekers of oracular guidance and threshold work.

Petition For

Protection, oracular visions, safe passage, sovereignty, healing of kinship rifts, ancestral wisdom.

Warnings

His myth involves sacrifice and painful kinship lessons. Approach with respect for duty and consequence.

Additional Notes

Name means raven or crow. Associated with the cauldron of regeneration and the White Hill in London.

History

Legendary Acts

Laid his body as a bridge for his army. Requested his head be buried to protect Britain.

Later Retellings

Later traditions claim Arthur unearthed the head, preferring reliance on present kingship.
