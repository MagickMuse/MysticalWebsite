Magickal Properties

Black Tara may be a wrathful form of Green Tara. She subdues and subjugates evil and vicious spirits, conquering all those who oppose or obstruct spiritual progress. Black Tara heals all illnesses stemming from spiritual or magickal causes. She bestows invincible will on her devotees so they are able to achieve all goals, aspirations, and resolutions. This is a comparatively rare form of Tara. Black Tara and Dark Blue Tara are sometimes considered to be the same; Black Tara may be Ekajata, the Blue Sky Goddess, at night.
