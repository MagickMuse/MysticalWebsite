Saint Isabel of Portugal (1271 to 1336), a Roman Catholic saint and Queen of Portugal, is known for her charity and peacemaking and is renowned for increasing milk flow in nursing mothers with breastfeeding difficulties. She is associated with Queen Esther in devotional contexts.
