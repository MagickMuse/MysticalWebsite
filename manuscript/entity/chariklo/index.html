Magickal Properties

Chariklo is the wife of Chiron the centaur and shared in raising and tutoring many of ancient Greece's greatest heroes. She possesses deep knowledge of healing, wisdom, and the training of exceptional individuals. Her cave was home to numerous heroes throughout their education, making her an expert in developing human potential.

Ritual Uses

Petition Chariklo for guidance in teaching, mentoring, or developing the potential of others. She can provide wisdom for those involved in healing work or educational endeavors. Invoke her assistance when training or supporting individuals destined for greatness.

Offerings

Share offerings with Chiron, including items that honor heroic traditions and the development of excellence in others. Present gifts that reflect teaching, healing, and the nurturing of potential.

Favored People

Heroes and those training to become heroes, teachers and mentors, healers and wisdom-keepers, those involved in developing human potential and excellence.

Petition For

Healing knowledge and wisdom, guidance in teaching and mentoring others, assistance in raising children or students to their full potential, support in developing heroic qualities in oneself or others.

Warnings

Respect her relationship with Athena and her role as mother to Tiresias. Her son accidentally saw Athena naked and was blinded, though he received the gift of prophecy as compensation through Chariklo's intervention with the goddess.

Additional Notes

Chariklo may be a centaur like her husband or she may be a nymph. She is Athena's best friend or possibly lover. The planetoid Chariklo, discovered in 1997, is the largest of the icy bodies known as centaurs, with an orbit of 62.4 years. She frequently appears in sixth-century BCE vase paintings depicting the wedding of Thetis, forming a triad of goddesses with Hestia and Demeter.
