Magickal Properties

  • Chief of the Eight Immortals, allegedly lived during Chou Dynasty (1122-249 BCE)
  • Great alchemist who obtained secrets of the Elixir of Life and the Powder of Transformation (Philosopher's Stone)
  • Invoked for his magickal and alchemical knowledge
  • Possesses powers of resurrection and ability to bestow longevity

Ritual Uses

  • Petition when alchemical or magickal knowledge is needed
  • Invoke for powers of resurrection
  • Seek his assistance for longevity work
  • Call upon for advanced occult knowledge

Offerings

Incense

Favored People

Those seeking alchemical and occult knowledge, people needing longevity or resurrection powers

Petition For

Alchemical secrets including the Elixir of Life, longevity and extended life, resurrection powers, advanced magickal knowledge and occult wisdom

Warnings

Respect alchemical and immortal lore. Avoid stealing elixirs or tricks of transmutation. Do not present yourself as a master without proper apprenticeship.

Additional Notes

Usually depicted as a fat man with a big naked belly. He always grasps his primary attribute, the magickal fan with which he resurrects the dead. Also carries peach of immortality.

In his famous story, he used magick to make his body appear dead while his soul took the form of a handsome young man to test his wife's fidelity. When she opened his coffin, he came alive and she hung herself. He set his house on fire, keeping only his fan and a copy of the I-Ching.
