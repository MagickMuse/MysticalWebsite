Magickal Properties

  • Magickal woman created by Gwydion from broom, oak, and meadowsweet flowers, each blooms at different time of year in different location, indicating her magickal nature
  • Extremely beautiful but magician unable to program her behavior or feelings despite creating her specifically as wife
  • Represents power to choose own path despite being created for another's purpose
  • Transformed into owl for eternity, nocturnal bird banished from solar spirit but can keep company with Moon Lady Arianrhod

Ritual Uses

  • Petition and perform rituals exclusively at night; she may be inaccessible during the day
  • Invoke her to enable you to choose your own lovers, not to be forced into marriage or relationships
  • Spirit of love and beauty who represents earlier spiritual traditions threatened by new patriarchal deities

Offerings

Oak, meadowsweet, and broom flowers (the flowers from which she was created)

Favored People

Those seeking to choose their own lovers, people forced into unwanted relationships or marriages

Petition For

Freedom to choose one's own romantic partners, independence from forced marriages, enable you to choose your own lovers

Warnings

Petition and perform rituals exclusively at night; she may be inaccessible during the day. Banished from solar spirit presence but can keep company with Moon Lady Arianrhod.

Additional Notes

  • Created to circumvent Arianrhod's taboos preventing her son Lleu's marriage
  • Took lover and plotted Lleu's death, inveigling secret of how he could be killed (he was almost immortal)
  • May represent following in Arianrhod's footsteps - earlier spiritual traditions seeking to halt progress of new solar, patriarchal deity
  • Resembles Irish spirit Blathnat, adding to argument she's not merely magician's invention but represents goddess tradition
  • By transforming into nocturnal owl, banished from presence of solar Lleu but can accompany lunar Arianrhod
