Earth Elementals

Gnomes are the earth elementals, known by various names across cultures including sprites or dryads. These nature spirits aid the growth of flowers, trees, and other plants. Look closely and you might spot them sitting in a tree or resting beneath a blackberry bush. When autumn comes, they change the leaves from green to red, orange, and gold.

Earth elementals play an important role in helping the earth heal from the effects of pollution, deforestation, mining, and other forms of destruction.

Temperament and Domains

Practical, no-nonsense beings, gnomes can seem a little gruff at times. However, they have a wonderful appreciation for material things and wealth. Remember the leprechaun's pot of gold? Ask the earth elementals to assist with prosperity spells and matters involving material abundance.

Working with Gnomes

Gnomes adore jewelry and crystals. Bury a token in the ground as a way of expressing thanks for their assistance. Always remember to thank the elementals that help you in your spellwork. If you behave disrespectfully toward them, they may retaliate by playing nasty tricks on you. Be generous, however, and your elemental friends will continue to serve you faithfully.
