Magickal Properties

Aegina is a Nymph or Naiad presiding over the island that bears her name in the Saronic Gulf. Deeply networked with sea and river spirits and afterlife powers, she serves as a capable mediator between realms and within families.

Ritual Uses

Work with Aegina for mediation between deities of many realms and to end family feuds. Visualize her island and call on her connections to sea and river spirits when seeking harmony and protection.

Offerings

Traditional offerings that honor water and family bonds. Items linked to goats and to river and sea spirits are appropriate.

Favored People

Those seeking mediation between realms, families needing harmony, and people with ties to islands and waterways.

Petition For

Mediation across spiritual factions, peace within families, prosperity and protection.

Warnings

Her myth includes an abduction by Zeus that was resisted; approach themes of mediation and protection with care for consent and sovereignty.

Additional Notes

  • Lineage and ties: daughter of Asopus and Metope; mother of Aeacus; ancestress of Achilles and Patroclus
  • Iconography: often shown in classical art tied to water and island sovereignty
