⚠️ Banamu Hehe is venerated within Manchu shamanic traditions. This is a closed Indigenous practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Manchu Earth Mother who grounds, nourishes, and harmonizes people with land and sky
  • One of a sacred triad from Abka Hehe; restores spiritual balance and ancestral connection

Ritual Uses

  • Petition for fertility, harmony with Earth, protection of land and home, and support in shamanic rites
  • Ancestral reconnection and steadying of community life

Offerings

  • Wine, beans, grains, cooked fish and meats (especially pork), presented with cultural respect

Favored People

Shamans, spirit‑workers, women, and devotees of traditional Manchu religion

Petition For

Grounding, fertility, harmony with Earth, spiritual balance, land protection, and ancestral reconnection

Warnings

Observe Manchu cultural protocols; traditions were historically suppressed, approach with awareness and respect.

Additional Notes

  • Names/titles: Earth Mother; venerated within a triad born from Abka Hehe
