Magickal Properties

Lady Chan is a divine midwife who rules the birth process and protects pregnant women and babies during labor. She possesses the supernatural ability to appear exactly when needed, even without being summoned, demonstrating her omniscient awareness of birthing emergencies. Her divine nature allows her to handle the most challenging pregnancies that would confound mortal midwives, including pregnancies involving supernatural elements or prolonged gestations.

As a celestial being, she has the power to materialize birthing rooms and specialized equipment as needed, and can command supernatural solutions to obstetric challenges. Her blessing transforms dangerous deliveries into safe ones, and her presence ensures both mother and child survive even the most perilous circumstances.

Ritual Uses

Lady Chan is invoked during difficult pregnancies and challenging labor situations where mortal medical intervention may not be sufficient. She is petitioned when pregnancies extend beyond normal term, when there are complications threatening mother or child, or when divine intervention is needed for safe delivery.

Her worship involves creating sacred birthing spaces and maintaining ritual cleanliness during labor. Practitioners seek her guidance for proper birthing protocols and her blessing for successful deliveries.

Lore

Lady Chan is venerated almost exclusively by women and serves as the matron saint of midwives and pregnant women. Her most famous legend involves delivering a white snake after seventeen months of pregnancy, demonstrating her ability to handle supernatural birthing situations. Her shrine and miraculous statue can be visited in Fuzhou, Fujian Province, China. She refuses payment for her interventions, accepting only simple gratitude tokens.
