King of Tengu

Sojobo is King of the Tengu. His abode is Mount Kurama, the birthplace of reiki healing, north of Kyoto.

Tutelage of Yoshitsune

Sojobo is especially famous for his tutelage of the hero Yoshitsune of the Minamoto clan. Yoshitsune's life was spared by rival clan leader Kiyomori of the Taira on the condition that he become a Buddhist priest at Kuramadera Temple in the mountains north of Kyoto. He met this condition, which resulted in his encounter with Sojobo, who resided nearby.

Sojobo took Yoshitsune under his wing, teaching him martial arts, including swordsmanship, tactical and military strategy, and magical skills. Yoshitsune fulfilled his priestly duties during the day and spent his nights engaged in practice bouts with Sojobo and his tengu friends. The exile was intended to weaken Yoshitsune and the Minamoto clan, but instead he emerged as one of Japan's greatest warriors, attributing his success and skills to Sojobo.

Manifestation

Sojobo has the long nose characteristic of tengu. He appears as an elderly, white haired yamabushi-tengu ("mountain priest tengu") with a long white beard and dark wings. He is a favored subject of Japanese woodblock prints.

Attribute

A seven-feather fan.
