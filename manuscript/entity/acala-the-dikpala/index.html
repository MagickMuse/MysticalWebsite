Acala, whose name means "immovable," is a Mahayana Buddhist tutelary god. He is also a dikpala, the guardian of the northeastern quarter.

Additional Notes

  • Color: blue
  • Attributes: jewel, lotus, staff, and sword
  • Distinct from the Vajrayana bhumi form, Acala the Bhumi
