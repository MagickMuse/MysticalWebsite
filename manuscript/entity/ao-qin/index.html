Dragon King of the Sea

Magickal Properties

Dragon King of the Eastern Sea; sovereign of waters and maritime order.

Ritual Uses

Petition for safe passage by sea, rain in due season, and balance of river and coastal waters.

Offerings

Clean water libations and respect at river or sea shrines.

Favored People

Sailors, fishers, coastal communities.

Petition For

Favorable weather at sea, rainfall, and protection of waters.

Warnings

Approach with formality befitting a sea sovereign; do not pollute ritual sites.

Additional Notes

One of the Dragon Kings; associated with sea palaces and marine courts.
