Spirit of Wealth and Household Prosperity

Daikoku is one of the Seven Lucky Gods of Japan, a Kami of wealth, prosperity, and household abundance. Originally a fierce deity from India (Mahakala), he transformed in Japan into a jovial, generous patron of prosperity.

Domains

Daikoku is particularly associated with kitchens, storehouses, and the hearth: the heart of household abundance. His magick sack never empties, and his mallet (uchide no kozuchi) grants wishes.

Manifestation

Often depicted as a jovial figure with a large belly, seated on bales of rice, holding the wish-granting mallet and his inexhaustible bag.

Sacred Creatures

Rats are his messengers and assistants, not pests; they represent prosperity and abundance in his context.

Spirit Allies

Sometimes paired with Ebisu (another of the Seven Lucky Gods) in business settings.

Festival

Day of the Rat in the New Year period.

Petition

Invoke Daikoku for household prosperity, business success, and material abundance. He excels at blessing homes, especially kitchens and storehouses, and brings good fortune to family enterprises while protecting household wealth. Work with him during New Year celebrations and when establishing new homes or businesses.
