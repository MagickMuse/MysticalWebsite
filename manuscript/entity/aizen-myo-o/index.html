Magickal Properties

Aizen Myo'o, Buddhist Lord of Passion who traveled from India to Japan, is venerated by Japan's esoteric Buddhist sects as King of Love, Lust, and Desire. He serves as patron of erotic love and sacred sexuality, helping to convert earthly, physical desires into transcendent love and spiritual awakening. He appears with three eyes, six arms, and either one or two heads, wearing a crown with a Shishi dog over his wild hair.

Ritual Uses

Work with Aizen Myo'o for assistance with the physical and emotional frustrations of suppressed sexuality. He helps control, explore, or transform lust into enlightenment and is revered as patron of gay love, though he may be invoked for assistance with any kind of love or romance, including self-love.

Offerings

Incense, prayers, and meditation practices focused on transformation of desire. Present offerings that honor his role in converting earthly desires into spiritual awakening.

Favored People

Prostitutes, sex workers, singers, musicians, landlords, those for whom sex is a business including sex shop owners and those working with pornography or erotica.

Petition For

Control of lust, transformation of desires into enlightenment, assistance with sexual frustrations, guidance in sacred sexuality, and help with any form of love or romance.

Warnings

Associated with erotic love and sacred sexuality. Approach with understanding that he helps convert earthly desires into transcendent love rather than simply amplifying physical desires.

Additional Notes

His attributes include bow and arrows, bell, stick, thunderbolt, and lotus. His sacred color is red. His main shrine is at Kakuonji Temple in Kamakura, Japan. He demonstrates how physical passion can be transformed into spiritual enlightenment through proper understanding and practice.
