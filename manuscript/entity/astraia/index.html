Magickal Properties

Astraia, the Starry One, is the Goddess of Righteous Justice. She departed Earth in the Bronze Age’s lawlessness and shines in the sky, bearing the hope of just order.

Ritual Uses

Petition for justice, integrity, and fair outcome. Invoke when restoring right relationship in communities.

Offerings

Clean water, quiet vows to uphold justice, and acts of fairness.

Favored People

Judges, advocates, reformers, and those seeking rightful redress.

Petition For

Justice, moral clarity, and restoration of right order.

Warnings

Keep petitions free of petty vengeance; align requests with the common good.

Additional Notes

Constellation: Virgo; closely associated with Dike and Nemesis.
