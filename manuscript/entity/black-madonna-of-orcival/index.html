The Black Madonna of Orcival is renowned for healing blindness and providing fertility.

Magickal Properties

  • Carved wood statue covered in silver except for face and disproportionately large hands
  • Large hands intended to demonstrate her generosity and power, or may resemble bear paws if originally bear goddess
  • Found when hammer thrown to determine church site, returned three times when moved
  • Deeply venerated by Romani (Gypsies) and considered their protectress

Ritual Uses

  • Heals the blind, provides fertility, frees captives, casts out demons
  • Protects sailors and travelers on sea, grants victory and success
  • Wards off epidemics through her protective power
  • Pilgrimage site especially for Romani people

Offerings

Votive offerings, images of her sacred animals

Favored People

Blind, those seeking fertility, captives, sailors/travelers, those facing epidemics, Romani (Gypsies)

Petition For

Healing blindness, fertility, freeing captives, casting out demons, protection at sea, victory, epidemic protection

Warnings

Orcival means "Valley of the Bears", may be ancient bear goddess. Her big hands resemble bear paws. Once bearskins were affixed to church door.

Additional Notes

  • Located in Puy-de-Dôme, France, "Orcival" means "Valley of the Bears"
  • Shrine founded by Saint Ursin in 3rd century (Ursin etymologically related to Ursus, Latin for "bear")
  • Theory she is originally ancient bear goddess based on location name, founder's name, large hands, bearskin tradition
  • Strong connection to Romani spiritual traditions, considered their special protectress
  • Demonstrates connection between Black Madonna sites and ancient bear worship locations
