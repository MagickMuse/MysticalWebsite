🐈Shrine to Freyja

✨
Freyja · Lady · Valfreya · Queen of Witches

Goddess of love, sex, fertility, magick, witchcraft, death, pleasure, and glory. Daughter of Njord and Herta. Twin to Freyr.

🟡
Gold and amber. Her tears for the lost Od fall as gold and harden into amber. Her cats are named Bee-gold and Tree-gold.
Most beautiful of the Norse spirits, who taught the Aesir charms and potions and introduced Odin to the runes. A witch in her own right and the role model for women's autonomy and magick.

Offerings

Honey, mead, flowers, jewelry, perfume, poetry, ale, apples, barley, pork. Feed or adopt a stray cat in her honor.

Correspondences

Animals: Two large gray cats, Bee-gold (Honey) and Tree-gold (Amber), pull her chariot. She rides a boar into battle.

Plants: Flax, hemp, nettles, her spinning-goddess fibers.

Mineral: Amber, her hardened tears.

Cloak: A falcon-feather cloak that lets her fly and shape-shift.

Petition For

Love, sex, fertility, beauty, magick, witchcraft, glory, female autonomy. Mediumship, shamanism, prophecy. Battle and the slain.

Favored People

Soldiers, veterans, jewelers, seers, shamans, practitioners of magick. Völvas and Valkyries serve as her priestesses.

Sacred Days

Friday, her sacred day. The number 13, the months of the lunar calendar, is her sacred number, making Friday the 13th especially sacred. Walpurgis and Midsummer festivities at The Brocken in the Harz Mountains.

Words of Power

Freya · Valfreya · Lady · Queen of Witches · Bee-gold · Tree-gold

Notes and Cautions

Rewards reciprocity and punishes manipulative desire. Never coerce another's love. Expects priority from close devotees. Be clear about primary patronage.
