Ah Cancum is a hunting god of the classical Mesoamerican Mayan tradition of Mexico. He stands among the deities whose work belongs to the bow, the trail, and the relationship between the hunter and the animals he tracks.

The Mayan Hunting Gods

Ah Cancum is one of a number of deities in Mayan religion identified with the hunt and the protection of animals. The Mayan tradition holds these two functions together: the same gods who oversee the taking of game also watch over the well-being of the animals and the limits of what the hunter is permitted to take. Ah Cancum's place is within that paired domain.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Ah Cancum belongs to the Mayan religious tradition of Mexico, a closed practice rooted in specific cultural and initiatory context. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform Mayan hunting rites outside of the lineages and communities to which they belong.
