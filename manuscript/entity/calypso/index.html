Magickal Properties

Calypso is a nymph who lives on the hidden island of Ogygia. Her name derives from the Greek word meaning to cover, conceal, or hide. She rescued Odysseus when he was shipwrecked and kept him on her island for seven years, offering him immortality and eternal youth if he would stay with her forever. Though she loved him deeply and treated him with great kindness and hospitality, he longed for his mortal wife and home. Eventually Zeus ordered her to release him. Calypso is a spirit of sanctuary, healing, and concealment.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Calypso when you need sanctuary or a hidden refuge from the world. She excels at providing healing space for those wounded in body or spirit. Work with her for concealment magick and creating safe havens. She can teach about offering hospitality and care even when not reciprocated.

Offerings

Items representing hospitality and comfort, wine, beautiful woven cloth, songs of longing and love, gifts that honor caregivers.

Favored People

Those seeking refuge from danger or hardship, healers and caregivers, people nursing broken hearts, anyone needing temporary sanctuary, women who love unavailable partners.

Petition For

Safe sanctuary, healing from trauma or injury, comfort during loneliness, strength to let go when necessary, hospitality magick, concealment and protection.

Warnings

Calypso demonstrates that you cannot force someone to love you or stay with you, no matter how much you offer. Her story teaches about the pain of unrequited love and the necessity of release. Even immortality cannot substitute for true belonging.

Additional Notes

Her island Ogygia was hidden from the world, accessible only by divine intervention or shipwreck. She is associated with caves and the number seven (years of Odysseus's stay). Some myths make her a daughter of Atlas or Oceanus. Her name connects her to concealment and the hidden realms.
