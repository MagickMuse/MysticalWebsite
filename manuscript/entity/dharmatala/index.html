Magickal Properties

Dharmatala is the servant, supporter, and messenger of the sixteen Arhats. Together with Hvashang, he is a Tibetan addition to the original sixteen Arhats. In Tibet, Dharmatala is counted as the seventeenth although he is a layperson, not a monk like other Arhats. He is a devotee of the Amitabha Buddha. His magickal tiger companion was created to banish malicious spirits and also carries his books.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Dharmatala for educational pursuits and scholarly work. He can be called upon to protect against malicious spirits that interfere with learning. His tiger companion serves as a guardian during study and meditation. Petition him when carrying heavy burdens, both physical and metaphysical.

Offerings

Books and scholarly materials are most appropriate. Written works, scrolls, and academic texts honor his role as bearer of knowledge.

Favored People

Students, scholars, teachers, librarians, and all those dedicated to learning and the preservation of knowledge.

Petition For

Educational success, wisdom, protection from malicious spirits during study, assistance in carrying life's burdens, support in scholarly endeavors.

Warnings

As a layperson among the Arhats, approach him with the same respect shown to ordained monks, despite his different status.

Additional Notes

Dharmatala carries a backpack filled with books and is accompanied by a magickal tiger created specifically to banish malicious spirits. The tiger also serves as his assistant, carrying his books. He represents the integration of lay practitioners into the Buddhist spiritual hierarchy.
