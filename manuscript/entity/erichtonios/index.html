Magickal Properties

Erichtonios, serpent‑born of Athena’s mysteries, embodies the hidden foundations of the polis, ancestral autochthony, and the guarded wisdom beneath Athens.

Ritual Uses

Honor in civic rites, protection of city foundations, household thresholds, and the safekeeping of sacred places and chthonic wisdom.

Offerings

Respectful observances, olive boughs, and care not to disturb sacred snakes or foundations.

Favored People

Civic guardians, keepers of ancestral rites, those seeking rightful place within the polis.

Petition For

Protection of foundations, legitimacy, guardianship of cities and households, wise governance.

Warnings

Do not pry into sealed mysteries. Disturbing sacred places or serpents brings consequence.

Additional Notes

Also known as Erichthonius; linked to Athena’s chest and the revelation of her serpent nature.
