About the "Demon" Label: Beelzebub derives from Baal Zebub, a Canaanite deity whose name means "Lord of the Flies" (or possibly "Lord of the High Place", the "flies" reading may be a Hebrew scribal mockery of the original name). A deity revered across the ancient Levant was reclassified as a major demon when the traditions that honored him were superseded. The danger assigned to him in later tradition is a theological construct, not an original attribute.

Beelzebub is a major demon whose name means "Lord of the Flies." Originally a Canaanite deity, he was demonized in Jewish and Christian tradition. Saint Francis of Assisi is his specific opposing force; invoke Francis to exorcise and banish Beelzebub.
