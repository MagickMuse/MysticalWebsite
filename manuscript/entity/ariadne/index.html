Magickal Properties

Pre‑Greek goddess and later deified queen associated with love, marriage, childbirth, and guidance through labyrinthine ordeals. Patron of thread, crowns, and joyful union with Dionysus.

Ritual Uses

Petition for true love that endures, for marriage blessings, and safe childbirth. Seek aid when navigating complex trials or betrayals.

Offerings

Wine, flowers, simple garlands or a circlet/crown, and songs shared with Dionysus.

Favored People

Lovers, spouses, and those seeking relief from heartbreak or addiction entanglements.

Petition For

True love, marriage, childbirth support, relief from alcoholism, and rescue from confusing paths.

Warnings

Do not weaponize love magick in her name.

Additional Notes

Pre‑Greek Minoan origins; associated with snakes, thread, and the Corona Borealis (her crown).

Encyclopedia Source Notes

Goddess of vegetation. Greek. Possibly derived from an unnamed Minoan goddess identified on Crete. According to Homer and Hesiod she is a daughter of Minos and a consort of Dionysos. Her crown, given by Zeus, is the Corona Borealis. Tradition has it that she was wooed and then deserted by the hero Theseus.
