Saint Barbara

Classification

Folk saint, Latin American manifestation distinct from the traditional Christian martyr.

Overview

Traditionally, "Saint Barbara" refers either to the Christian martyr or to Shango, the orisha syncretized to her. However, a new manifestation has emerged in recent years, primarily in Mexico and Latin America. Whether this represents continued evolution of the original saint or an entirely different spirit operating under her name remains unclear.

Powers and Domains

This Barbara is a warrior saint who rushes to rescue people from disasters, though not the fire, lightning, and explosions traditionally associated with her namesake. Instead, this manifestation specializes in supernatural and spiritual protection:

  • Routing demons
  • Stopping nightmares
  • Rescuing drowning victims (going underwater, unlike the fire-associated original)
  • Protection from spiritual dangers

Retablo paintings credit her with rescuing people from alluring mermaids and even alien abduction. Her function resembles that of a female Michael Archangel.

Manifestation

She appears as a Latin American woman ranging from youthful to mature in age. Her clothing is modest, typically a skirt and sweater or more formal gowns and robes, but never ostentatious.

Attributes

She consistently carries a sword but lacks the traditional attributes of Saint Barbara or Shango. No tower, lightning bolt, or white horse appears with her.

Color

Green or blue.

Feast Day

4 December
