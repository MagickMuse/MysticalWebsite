The Silent One

Vidar, son of Odin and frost giant Grid, is destined to avenge his father by slaying the Fenris Wolf at Ragnarok. He and his half-brother Vali will survive and rule the new world. Grid fashioned special shoes for him from leather scraps that cobblers had saved; wearing them at Ragnarok allegedly enabled him to slay the Fenris Wolf and survive.

Technically a Jotun, Vidar is closely allied with the Aesir and lives in Asgard.

Manifestation

Vidar is a huge, silent, powerful man, described as second in strength only to Thor.

Attributes

Thick-soled shoes.

Additional Notes

Vidar's hall in Asgard is called Vidi. Despite his silence, he likes to drink and eat, like a giant. When his shoes wear out, rip off the soles and leather bits, offer them to him, then discard them.
