Venerated as a Sufi saint, Sidi Belyout is the patron saint and guardian of the city of Casablanca.

Life and Legend

Sidi Belyout was so disgusted with the ways of mankind that he blinded himself and spent the rest of his life as a hermit in the forest. Wild animals cared for him. When he died, animals guarded his body until it was found and buried.

Magical Properties

If you drink water from the miraculous fountain near his grave, you are guaranteed to return to Casablanca.

Veneration

Both Jews and Muslims venerate Sidi Belyout as a saint, and both religious groups claim him as one of their own.

Sacred Sites

His koubba (traditional Moroccan mausoleum) was built at the end of the 19th century. A district southeast of the seaport is named after him.
