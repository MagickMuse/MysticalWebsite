Source-Text Additions

Lineage

According to the Theogony of Hesiod, she is one of the fifty daughters of Nereus and Doris.

Domain and Travel

She is considered to calm stormy seas, traveling in a boat made of mussels.

Mythological Presence

She was among those present at the birth of Apollo.

Relationship with Poseidon

Amphitrite is mistress and owner of the Mediterranean. Poseidon raped her before she agreed to marry him, and she takes his many infidelities in stride.
