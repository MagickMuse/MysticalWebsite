Saint Francis of Assisi is the patron saint of animal rescue, environmentalists, and all who work on behalf of animals.

Saint Francis of Assisi is renowned for healing and protecting animals of all kinds, and for providing protection against bites, stings, and venomous attacks.

Classification

Anglican and Roman Catholic saint.

Epithet

Patriarch of the Poor.

Life and History

Francis of Assisi (1181-1228), founder of the Franciscan order, may be the most famous of all saints, beloved even by those who don't normally love saints. He was an unlikely candidate for sainthood.

His father, a wealthy Italian merchant, named his son Francisco after his love of all things French. Francis's mother was French. The young Francis developed a reputation as a dissolute youth devoted to pleasure. He became a troubadour, a street brawler, and aspired to be a soldier.

Then came a profound spiritual conversion. Francis heard Christ summon him to abandon his worldly life. He heeded the call, took a vow of poverty, abandoned his finery, dressed in rough clothes, walked barefoot, begged for sustenance, and preached in the streets. His father disowned him.

The Stigmata

In 1224, Saint Francis received the stigmata, wounds corresponding to those Jesus received during the Crucifixion. He was the first person whose stigmata were documented and officially acknowledged by the Vatican.

The Crèche

In 1223, Saint Francis invented the crèche, now a Christmas standard. This three-dimensional nativity scene depicts the Holy Child in the manger surrounded by Mary, Joseph, shepherds, the Magi, and animals.

Francis and Animals

Francis is famous for his love of all creatures. He called all living beings his sisters and brothers. Among his last words were thanks to his donkey for serving him faithfully; the donkey reputedly wept.

  • He preached to the birds
  • He negotiated a truce between the townsfolk of Gubbio and a wolf preying upon them. Francis sought out the wolf, who lay at his feet. After lecturing it about good behavior, he led it into town and introduced it to the startled people. The townsfolk agreed to feed the wolf regularly; the wolf agreed to cease hunting them.

Many churches hold animal-blessing ceremonies on his feast day.

Powers and Domains

  • Animal control: If wild animals, vermin, or neighbors' dogs encroach on your territory, request a happy solution for all
  • Animal protection: Small medallions bearing his image are attached to dog collars for blessing and protection
  • Healing: Protection against bites, stings, and venomous attacks
  • Household harmony: Restores peace in the family
  • Exorcism: Specifically invoked to defeat and banish the demon Beelzebub-Francis is his opposing force
  • Dying: Invoked against dying alone

In 1979, Pope John Paul II proclaimed Saint Francis patron saint of the ecology movement.

Iconography

A monk preaching to birds; a monk surrounded by animals; Francis receiving the stigmata.

Altar

Decorate with images of animals, but keep it simple. Francis disliked the ostentatious.

Color

Sacred Animals

All of them!

Sacred Site

Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi in Assisi, Italy. His body was hidden here to prevent relic-scattering. His stone coffin may be viewed in the crypt.

Ritual

Burn one brown candle consecutively for nine days to request Francis's aid. Alternatively, use a single nine-day candle.

Feast Day

4 October
