🧹Tlazolteotl's Cleansing Spell

Magickal Properties

Tlazolteotl's most famous manifestation is as a statue of a naked, grimacing, squatting, laboring woman. She is also depicted naked but for a peaked barked hat, riding on a broomstick in the company of owls, ravens, and bats.

The Spanish Conquistadors were shocked and perplexed when they first saw these images of the Aztec goddess. They thought they left images like these back in Europe with the witch trials. Witchcraft is international.

Tlazolteotl is the spirit of magick, healing, love, sex, desire, cleansing, and garbage. Her name means "Eater of Filth." In her capacity as the Spirit of Filth, Tlazolteotl cleanses individuals and Earth of spiritual debris, sin, and shame. She is the Matron of Female Healers, Midwives, and Weavers.

Ritual Uses

Not surprisingly, Tlazolteotl is credited with invention of the Aztec sweat bathhouse, the temescal. Work with her for spiritual purification and cleansing rituals, particularly those involving removal of shame, guilt, or spiritual debris. Approach her with honesty about what needs to be cleansed.

Offerings

Offerings at temescal (sweat bath), ritual cleansing items.

Favored People

Female healers, midwives, weavers, those seeking spiritual cleansing and purification.

Petition For

Magick, healing, love, sex, desire, spiritual cleansing, removal of sin and shame, midwifery assistance, weaving skills, purification from spiritual debris.

Warnings

Approach her with honesty about what needs to be cleansed. She eats filth to purify; do not hide your spiritual debris from her.

Additional Notes

Tlazolteotl is an Aztec witch-goddess who has dominion over life, death, magick, and spiritual purification. Her association with owls is particularly strong. Her attribute is the broom. Her sacred birds include owls, ravens, and vultures. Her color is turquoise. Her creatures include bats and snakes.
