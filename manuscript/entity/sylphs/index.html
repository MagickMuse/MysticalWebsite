Air Elementals

Sylphs are the air elementals, though most people think of fairies as small flying creatures like Tinker Bell. These beings aren't just cute and delicate winged creatures as contemporary films portray them—they handle many things related to the air and sky. Sylphs have the power to manipulate the winds, influence air quality, and help earthlings breathe. They also assist birds and flying insects.

Temperament and Domains

In magickal work, sylphs can help with verbal spells such as incantations. If you want to ace a test, learn a new subject or skill, or communicate clearly with someone, ask a sylph for assistance. Air spirits also like to get involved with legal matters and contracts.

Offerings

Sylphs enjoy flowers. Place fresh blossoms on your altar or lay them in a sacred spot outdoors as an offering. Always remember to thank the elementals that assist you in your spellwork. If you behave disrespectfully toward them, they may retaliate by playing nasty tricks on you. Be generous, however, and your elemental friends will continue to serve you faithfully.

One of the Four Elementals

The sylph is the air elemental among the four described by the alchemist Paracelsus, the others being the gnome of earth, the salamander of fire, and the undine of water. Bound to the air, sylphs are also linked to breath, and one is said to be born on the last breath of a dying person, so that a sylph may likewise appear as a ghost.

Larger Than Life

Though we call a slight figure sylph-like, meaning wispy and insubstantial, Paracelsus held the opposite: he described the sylph as bigger, stronger, and coarser than a human being. Belief in such elemental beings was once so widespread that in the eighth century the Emperor Charlemagne issued an edict forbidding sylphs to appear, a decree his son Louis the Pious later renewed.
