Magickal Properties

Dike is the Greek goddess of justice, moral order, and fair judgment. Her name literally means "justice" in Greek. She is one of the Horae (Hours or Seasons), daughter of Zeus and Themis, and sister to Eunomia (Good Order) and Eirene (Peace). Dike represents natural law and the proper ordering of human society according to divine principles. She reports wrongdoing to Zeus and punishes those who pervert justice. During the Golden Age she lived among mortals, but as humanity became corrupt, she withdrew to the heavens where she became the constellation Virgo.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Dike for court cases, legal matters, and situations requiring fair judgment. She is petitioned when justice has been denied or when the legal system fails. Work with her to expose corruption and establish fair dealing in contracts and agreements. She is particularly powerful for oath-taking and ensuring people honor their commitments.

Offerings

Offerings of balance and fairness: white flowers, scales or symbols of justice, written petitions for fair judgment, acts of justice and ethical behavior in her name.

Favored People

Judges, lawyers, those seeking justice through legal means, whistleblowers exposing corruption, anyone wronged by unjust systems, people making important oaths.

Petition For

Fair judgment in legal matters, exposure of corruption and wrongdoing, success in court cases, ethical behavior in contracts, punishment of those who pervert justice, restoration of natural order.

Warnings

Invoke Dike for justice and fairness, not to pervert law for selfish ends. She punishes those who abuse legal systems or commit perjury. Do not petition her for revenge disguised as justice. Maintain ethical clarity and avoid tricks or corruption in petitions.

Additional Notes

Dike is often depicted holding scales (representing balanced judgment) and sometimes a sword. The Romans identified her with Justitia (Lady Justice), and she became the model for the blindfolded figure of Justice in Western legal tradition. Her constellation Virgo represents her withdrawal from corrupt humanity. Hesiod wrote that she sits beside Zeus and reports human wrongdoing. She is invoked in oaths and legal proceedings throughout the ancient Mediterranean world.
