Classification

Venerated by most branches of Christianity.

Overview

Gaspar, one of the Three Kings, the emperor of the Orient, traveled the farthest to visit the Christ Child, bearing the gift of frankincense. Despite his provenance, Gaspar is usually envisioned as a beardless Caucasian man wearing gilded clothing.

According to French author Michel Tournier (born 1924), however, Gaspar is the Black King, identified as the king of Meroe, now within the borders of modern Sudan, and he brought the gift of gold.
