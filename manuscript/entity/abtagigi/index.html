Magickal Properties

Abtagigi is the Sumerian spirit of sacred sexuality who awakens and stimulates erotic impulses. She possesses the secrets of the Great Rite and is among the spirits in Inanna-Ishtar's entourage. Ancient Sumerian rulers maintained their power by annually enacting the Great Rite with Inanna-Ishtar through her high priestess.

Ritual Uses

Create rituals for awakening or reawakening sexual desires, especially for those whose sexuality has been profaned by trauma. Work within temple contexts for sacred sexuality practices. Invoke for protection of sex workers and punishment of those who profane the sexual act.

Offerings

Aphrodisiacs, incense and fragrance that inspire desire. Present offerings that honor the sacred nature of sexuality and erotic energy.

Favored People

Sex workers, those seeking to heal from sexual trauma, practitioners of sacred sexuality, people working to reclaim their sexual power and autonomy.

Petition For

Awaken or reawaken sexual desire, protection for sex workers, healing from sexual trauma, understanding of sacred sexuality, punishment for those who profane sexual acts.

Warnings

Place her altar in a boudoir or love-shrine with aphrodisiacs and fragrant incense. Never petition her to coerce or harm someone because she is a powerful temple spirit of sacred sexuality who protects sex workers and punishes profaning sexual acts.

Additional Notes

The resemblance of the name Kalili to Lilith cannot be ignored. Both are concerned with the power inherent in sexual desire and esoteric aspects of sexuality, but Abtagigi-Kalili works within the temple while Lilith remains outside in wild nature. She is sometimes described as an evil spirit of harlotry, but that perspective derives from those who associate sex with sin.
