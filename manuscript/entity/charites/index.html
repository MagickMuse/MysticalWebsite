Magickal Properties

The Charites ('The Graces') are three sisters, Aglaia ('The Wonderful'), Euphrosyne ('Joy'), and Thalia ('Plenty'), who serve as attendants to Aphrodite and Hermes. They are spirits of creative inspiration, botanical abundance, moisture, and human fertility. They are joyful, happy, sensuous spirits whose festivals had reputations for being raucous celebrations.

Ritual Uses

Invoke the Charites for creative inspiration and artistic work. They excel at bringing joy, beauty, and abundance into life. Work with them through dance, music, and celebration. They can help with fertility magick and botanical work, bringing moisture and growth to plants and projects.

Offerings

Dance and music (they love celebration), fresh flowers and botanical gifts, festive foods and celebratory feasts, creative works and artistic expressions, anything that brings joy and beauty.

Favored People

Dancers and performers, young women seeking joy and fulfillment, artists and creative people, those who need more beauty and abundance in their lives, anyone working with plants and gardens.

Petition For

Creative inspiration and artistic ability, botanical abundance and plant growth, human fertility and pregnancy, joy and happiness, beauty and grace, moisture and life-giving energy.

Warnings

Their festivals were known for being wild and raucous, they're not sedate spirits. They value genuine joy over forced politeness. Their energy is sensuous and celebratory.

Additional Notes

Manifest as beautiful, graceful, dancing young women, usually appearing as pair or triad. Most familiar image shows three Charites standing together, two face viewer while center one turns her back. At their shrine in Orchomenos, venerated as three uncut stones (meteorites) that fell from heaven.

The Gentle Aspect of the Triple Goddess

Alongside the Fates and the Furies, the Graces represent the gentler face of the Triple Goddess. As companions to the Muses they share in the work of inspiration, moving artists and artisans to create works of great beauty.
