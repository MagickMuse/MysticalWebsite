Magickal Properties

Austeja, the Baltic Bee Goddess, presides over bees, hives, honey, and flourishing gardens. She protects brides, pregnant people, households, and beekeepers, promoting abundance, fertility, and growth.

Ritual Uses

Petition before gathering honey, for thriving hives, fertility, and household prosperity. Honor her when starting or tending a hive or planting bee‑friendly gardens.

Offerings

Honey, mead tossed into the air, beeswax, spring water, and images of bees.

Favored People

Beekeepers and those seeking fertility, protection of home and family, and abundant gardens.

Petition For

Fertility, healthy hives, household protection, and prosperity.

Warnings

Treat bees and sacred trees with care; honey was sacred and not to be profaned.

Additional Notes

Her festival, Žolinė, is mid‑August when bees are most active. Lithuanian hives were traditionally kept high in trees to keep them near their mother.
