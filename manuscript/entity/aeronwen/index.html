Magickal Properties

Aeronwen is a goddess of fate from Wales who determines battle outcomes and decides which individuals survive. Her name may derive from a root word for "carnage," and she may be the same spirit as Welsh war goddess Agrona or Irish Morrigan. She is associated with carnage and death on the battlefield.

Ritual Uses

Aeronwen had a shrine on the banks of the River Dee at Glyndyfrdwy. The waters of Glyndyfrdwy were considered sacred long after Christianity's arrival. Work with her for battle protection and favorable outcomes in conflicts.

Offerings

Traditional Welsh offerings and items related to battle and warfare. Historically, human sacrifices were offered to her by drowning victims in sacred waters.

Favored People

Warriors, soldiers, those going into battle, and those seeking protection in dangerous conflicts.

Petition For

Safety on the battlefield, favorable battle outcomes, protection in combat, and determination of survival in dangerous situations.

Warnings

Aeronwen determines battle outcomes and decides which individuals survive. She may be the same spirit as Welsh war goddess Agrona or Irish Morrigan. Associated with carnage and death. Little concrete information survives regarding Aeronwen, making her a mysterious and potentially dangerous spirit to approach.

Additional Notes

Glyndyfrdwy means "Black Water" or "Water of the Goddess." In 1400, Welsh patriot Owain Glyndwr chose this location to declare himself Prince of Wales, beginning a fourteen-year rebellion against English rule. She may also be associated with the Aeron River. Her connection to ravens emphasizes her role as a battle goddess who determines the fate of warriors.
