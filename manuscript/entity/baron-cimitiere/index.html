⚠️ Baron Cimitière is venerated within Haitian Vodou. This is a closed initiatory practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

  • Cemetery Baron of the Gede who governs graves, crossings, and the decorum of the dead
  • Opens and closes paths between worlds under strict protocol

Ritual Uses

  • Work for burial protections, safe crossings for the newly dead, graveyard guard, and necromantic boundaries, always via proper house rites

Offerings

  • Per house protocol (never assume); black and white plain offerings are common within Gede contexts, given with rum and smoke under supervision

Favored People

Houses and initiates who keep cemetery decorum and serve the dead with respect

Petition For

Protection of graves, safe passage, firm boundaries with the dead, and clear cemetery work

Warnings

Always propitiate Maître Carrefour before calling Baron Cimitière. Do not attempt shortcuts or solitary bargains; follow Vodou protocols and protections.

Additional Notes

  • A Baron road; symbols include the cross and the cemetery gate
