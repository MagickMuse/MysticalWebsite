Magickal Properties

  • Shape-shifting lunar deity, witch, herbalist, and keeper of the Cauldron of Knowledge, Inspiration, and Transformation
  • Master of transformation with access to all magickal, oracular, and shamanic powers
  • Good mother who brewed potion to bestow all knowledge and powers
  • No magickal skill or botanical secret she does not know
  • Occult master whose every encounter may be considered a test of knowledge and psychic ability

Ritual Uses

  • Transformation and shape-shifting work
  • Herbalism and botanical magick
  • Cauldron mysteries and brewing
  • Knowledge and inspiration seeking
  • Shamanic initiation

Offerings

Grain, milk, and black cakes

Favored People

Those she considers her children or initiates, seekers of profound knowledge and transformation who approach with respect

Petition For

Knowledge and inspiration, magickal skill and power, botanical secrets, transformation, shamanic abilities, oracular vision

Warnings

Approach cauldron mysteries through apprenticeship and proper vows. Do not steal her inspiration or attempt advanced sorcery uninitiated. Keep offerings of grain, milk, and black cakes. Avoid reckless experimentation with life and death. Secrets may be reserved for those she considers her children or initiates.

Additional Notes

Her most famous myth is preserved in the Book of Taliesin. She was married to giant Tegidfoel, by whom she had two children: daughter Crearwy ("Beautiful" or "Light") and son Afagddu ("Ugly" or "Dark"). These children are theorized to be Celtic equivalent of oppositional yin-yang forces. Cerridwen contains and has access to all these powers.

She brewed a potion requiring tremendous variety of botanicals gathered ritually and added at just the right moment, kept boiling for a year and a day. Once tasted, it bestows all knowledge and all magickal, oracular, and shamanic powers.

Her name is interpreted as "white sow." She is a master of transformation who can take any form. The big question is not what form she will take but whether you will be able to recognize her. Her color is white and her animal is the pig.

The Old One

Described as the "Old One," she can take any form but favors that of a woman or a great white sow.

The Book of Taliesin

Cerridwen's most famous myth is preserved in the Book of Taliesin, a thirteenth-century manuscript named for the sixth-century Welsh poet. Her most famous myth tells of her marriage to giant Tegidfoel and their two children who may represent complementary forces that fuel Creation: yin and yang, female and male, night and day, summer and winter.

The Cauldron Brew

Cerridwen wished the best for her children. She didn't worry about her daughter Crearwy but feared that her son Afagddu lacked sufficient gifts for success. She decided to brew a potion to compensate. Only Cerridwen knows the formula for this potion that bestows all knowledge, magick power, and oracular and shamanic powers. It requires a tremendous variety of botanicals carefully gathered and added at just the right moment. Someone must continually stir the brew, which must be kept steadily boiling for a year and a day.

Gwion's Transformation

Cerridwen found a poor, ignorant orphan child named Gwion to watch the pot. Completely unloved with no one to miss him, Gwion didn't understand the contents or purpose of the cauldron. The year and a day nearly complete, Gwion got careless (or was blessed by his constant proximity to Cerridwen's cauldron). A few drops of scalding liquid fell on his hand. In pain he sucked his finger, and at that very moment, from those few drops, Gwion knew all: he was instantly transformed into a master shaman, shape-shifter, and seer.

The Chase

Understanding everything, Gwion knew Cerridwen was coming to kill him. He escaped by shape-shifting and she pursued him similarly. If he became a fish, she became a bigger one; if he became a bird, she became a raptor. Finally Gwion transformed into a grain of wheat, hiding in a bushel. Cerridwen transformed into a hen and ate him. He gestated in her belly (Cerridwen is the Cauldron of Generation) and was reborn as a beautiful, shining child, still in possession of the magickal powers attained.
