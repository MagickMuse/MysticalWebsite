Magickal Properties

Alberich's history and identity are mysterious as befits someone with his possible family connections. His name means "Elf Ruler" or "Elf King," and he may be an Elf or a human master of Elves similar to how King Solomon masters Djinn. Some legends describe him as a spirit, others as an incredibly powerful Frankish sorcerer possibly able to attain immortality.

Ritual Uses

Alberich is described as the brother of Merovech, founder of France's Merovingian royal dynasty. Work with him for understanding hidden royal lineages, accessing otherworldly wisdom, and connecting to the mysterious powers associated with the Merovingian line.

Offerings

Occult tools, botanicals, cigars, and alcoholic beverages, offerings befitting a powerful magickal practitioner who lives in luxury and appreciates refined things.

Favored People

Sorcerers and those seeking advanced magickal knowledge and otherworldly wisdom, people interested in hidden royal bloodlines, and practitioners working with Elven or Dwarvish spirits.

Petition For

Magickal knowledge, access to otherworldly wisdom, understanding of hidden royal lineages, possibly assistance with attaining immortality or extended life, and connection to Elven magickal traditions.

Warnings

History and identity are mysterious. May be an Elf or incredibly powerful sorcerer. Lives in beautiful subterranean palace. Connected to Merovingian royal dynasty. His true nature and loyalties remain unclear.

Additional Notes

Alberich lives in a beautiful, luxurious subterranean palace. The identity of Merovech's father has always been mysterious, with candidates including Neptune, Wotan (Odin), or a sea monster. Some connect this lineage to Jesus through the Davidic line shared with King Solomon. Wagner used Alberich as a composite character in his operas.
