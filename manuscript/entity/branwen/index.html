💖A Love Spell

Magickal Properties

Branwen is a Celtic goddess of love, beauty, and sovereignty from Welsh mythology, known as one of the most beautiful women in the world. She serves as a bridge between realms and nations, possessing the power to unite or divide through her relationships and alliances. As a sovereignty goddess, she embodies the sacred feminine power that legitimizes rulership and brings prosperity to the land. Branwen has dominion over diplomatic relations, marriage alliances, and the delicate balance between peace and warfare.

Ritual Uses

Branwen is invoked for matters involving diplomacy, marriage negotiations, and peaceful resolution of conflicts. She can be petitioned when seeking to heal family rifts, restore damaged relationships, or negotiate peace between warring parties. Her energy is called upon for marriage ceremonies, particularly those involving political or family alliances, and for blessing unions that serve the greater good.

Offerings

White flowers, silver jewelry, beautiful fabrics, honey, milk, items representing peace and beauty, gifts that demonstrate craftsmanship and artistry, wedding tokens, and offerings placed near water or bridges.

Favored People

Diplomats, marriage counselors, mediators, brides, those seeking to heal family conflicts, peace negotiators, sovereignty holders, women in positions of leadership or influence.

Petition For

Successful marriage alliances, diplomatic success, healing of family feuds, beauty and grace, protection of women, successful negotiations, peaceful resolution of conflicts, restoration of broken relationships.

Warnings

Offer reconciliation rites and respect for kinship ties. Do not invoke her to inflame family feuds. Her story involves great suffering caused by family betrayal, so approach with respect for the pain that can come from broken trust and divided loyalties.

Additional Notes

In the Mabinogion, Branwen's marriage to Irish King Matholwch was meant to create peace between Wales and Ireland, but mistreatment led to devastating war between the two lands. She was able to send a message via a starling she trained, showing her connection to bird messengers. Her brother Bran the Blessed fought a terrible war to rescue her, resulting in massive casualties on both sides. She died of a broken heart upon realizing the destruction caused by her situation.
