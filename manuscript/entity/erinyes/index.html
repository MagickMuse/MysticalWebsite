Magickal Properties

When Kronos castrated Uranus, drops of blood fell onto Gaia and she conceived the Erinyes, infernal spirits of justice, vengeance, and righteousness. There are three Erinyes: Alecto meaning Never Ending, Megaera meaning Envious Anger, and Tisiphone meaning Face of Retaliation.

The Erinyes are not interested in legal codes; they are spirits of primeval natural law and severely punish those who flout these laws. Among the crimes they punish are homicides, perjury, crimes against deities, and murder against one's own blood kin. When the Erinyes are not busy on Earth, they serve Hades and Persephone, supervising and inflicting punishment in Hades' dungeons.

The Erinyes are particularly defenders of motherhood and Mother Right, the matriarchal concept that emphasizes the sacredness of mothers as opposed to the authority of fathers. They appear when mothers are insulted, abused, or murdered. They will defend the rights of other family members too, but Mom is their priority. They pursue those who flout blood kinship.

Ritual Uses

The Erinyes may appear on their own volition in response to crime, but they may also be invoked to provide justice. Victims may call down the curse of the Erinyes against those who have harmed them. The Erinyes are particularly involved in family feuds, especially when one family member kills another. It may have been considered safer to allow the Erinyes to prosecute these crimes rather than extend blood feuds.

The Erinyes are invoked when you seek justice and revenge. They afflict their victims with insanity and madness, their wrath placated only with correct ritual cleansing. They may also set acts of penance, which must be accomplished.

Warnings

The Erinyes are scary spirits. They bark before approaching. Their bodies and breath smell bad. They have venomous serpents for hair. Blood and some kind of venomous moisture exudes from their rheumy eyes. They may have wings like a bat or large bird. They afflict their victims with insanity and madness. Their wrath can only be placated with correct ritual cleansing. Do not invoke them lightly.

Additional Notes

The Erinyes may wear black mourner's robes or dress in the short skirt of a hunting maiden like Artemis. They may appear dancing a circle-dance. Tisiphone is described as wearing a bloodstained sheet. She is the guardian of the gates of Tartarus. They carry whips with brass-studded thongs and torches. Their sacred animals are dogs and snakes. Athena renamed the Erinyes, calling them the Eumenides, meaning the Kindly Ones, though this is likely a euphemism rather than marking a true transformation.

Narcissus Wreaths

The Greeks twined the white stars of Narcissus blossoms among the tangled locks of the Eumenides. A crown of narcissus flowers was woven in honor of these vengeful gods, and the same crowns were placed upon the heads of the dead.

The Triple Goddess and the Serpent

The three Furies embody one aspect of the Triple Goddess, the lawmaker who metes out punishment. Their heads are crowned with serpents like the Gorgon Medusa, the serpent standing here for the destructive side of female power. So great was their authority that they were held to be older than the gods themselves, who were no less subject to their rule than to the working of the Fates.
