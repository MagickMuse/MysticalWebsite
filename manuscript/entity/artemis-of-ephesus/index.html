Artemis

Magickal Properties

Many‑Breasted Great Mother of Ephesus, provider and protectress whose shrine was among the Seven Wonders; meteor‑chosen site, date‑palm origins, and nurture for all.

Ritual Uses

Petition for provision, protection, fertility, and guardianship of cities and travelers; honor her with dignified rites at groves and by pillars.

Offerings

Honey cakes, dates, oils, and care for date palms; respectful reproductions of her image.

Favored People

Mothers, providers, travelers, and city‑guardians.

Petition For

Abundance, safety, communal welfare, and protection of sacred sites.

Warnings

Keep rites dignified; do not despoil groves or pillars; refrain from eating fish or doves offered at her precincts.

Additional Notes

Temple repeatedly rebuilt; vendors sold replicas; meteor stone in her crown; imagery links to Near Eastern Mothers.
