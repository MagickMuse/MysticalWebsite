Yewá

Favored People
Those seeking death rites cemetery work
Precautions
Yewá is a severe orisha. In Africa, devotees are obliged to be celibate, and she is attended by eunuchs under the supervision of Logunedé. Although restrictions are somewhat looser in the Western Hemisphere, she still loathes promiscuity, sexual banter, or humor; she dislikes explicit discussion of sex or innuendo, and strongly disapproves of vulgarity, foul language, and cursing. Do not indulge in any of this near her altar; one must always be fully dressed in her presence.

Shango

Queen of Cadavers

Yewá is a shadowy, reclusive orisha who resides in the graveyard with her sister Oya. She guards cadavers from death through burial, then delivers them to Oya. She was originally Olokun's beautiful daughter but exiled herself to the cemetery after being seduced by Shango.

Origin

Yewá did not always live in the cemetery. She was originally the marine orisha Olokun's most beautiful daughter, ruler of the lagoons together with Olosa, venerated within a cave that could only be reached by swimming across the lagoon.

According to legend, while still a very young virgin girl, she was secretly seduced by Shango, the womanizer, and conceived. Boromu (or another orisha) persuaded her to drink an herbal potion causing an abortion. Devastated and overwhelmed by these experiences, she bore the loss alone, buried the fetus beneath a tree, and exiled herself to the cemetery, the realm of death.

Either before or after she ran away, Boromu went to Olokun to report what had happened and was brought to the tree where the remains were unearthed. The child, named Borosia, was resurrected, at least in spirit, and now serves as guard of Olokun's court. Whether Boromu is now her husband because he always loved her, or through an arranged marriage to salvage her reputation, remains subject to debate.

Magickal Properties

As Queen of Cadavers, Yewá guards the dead from the time of death through funeral rites and burial, at which point she delivers them to Oya. Oya may allow time to consume the corpses, or she may preside over their reduction to skeletons, at which point Yewá takes over once more.

Manifestation

She has a regal, queen-like bearing. She is syncretized to the Black Madonna of Montserrat.

Correspondences

Elements: water, earth. Colors: pink, scarlet. Numbers: 7, 9, 11. Creatures: doves, owls, goats. Day: Friday. Plants: Turnera ulmifolia (yellow alder, marilope) and Phyllanthus augustifolia (Panatela).
