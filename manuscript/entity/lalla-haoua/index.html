Overview

Lalla Haoua, also known as Lady Eve, is a Moroccan Muslim saint or spirit credited with healing madness and mental illness at her shrine in Marrakech, albeit via harsh methods.

Sacred Site

Lalla Haoua is allegedly buried beneath a pillar called the Sariat L'Aaquel (Pillar of Wisdom) located near a fountain in the courtyard of Marrakech's old moristâne (asylum). She may initially have been the spirit of the spring, which is believed to have healing properties. It's not entirely clear whether the saint, the spring, or both provide the cure.

Traditional Healing Methods

The mentally ill were chained to the pillar in hopes that their condition would improve.

For misbehaving children, a different ritual was employed: the heads of misbehaving children were knocked against the pillar three times while Lalla Haoua was invoked to make them behave and "know better." The child's shirt was then washed in the fountain, followed by its face and hands.

Sacred Plant

Kelkh, a kind of wild fennel, is ritually used to treat mental illness in connection with Lalla Haoua's veneration.
