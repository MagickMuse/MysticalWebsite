⚒️Shrine to Thor

⚡
Thor · Donar · Thunar · Drum Beater · Mr. Barbaro

Lord of Thunder. Defender of the Aesir. Protector of homes, communities, land, laws, and civilization. Giant killer.

🔴
Red-haired and red-bearded with fiery eyes. The lightning, the hammer Mjollnir, the oak forests that once spread across Europe.
Consistent, predictable and dependable, the lord of the masses. He hallows sacred places, events, and crucial magickal workings by striking with his hammer.

🕯️
Leave Your Devotion

Make the sign of Thor's hammer over your altar to hallow it. Pour a measure of vodka or ale. Speak plainly, he favors directness.

Offerings

Thor has a massive appetite. He loves to drink and wins all drinking competitions. Offer him Thor's Hammer brand vodka but he will probably accept most offerings if given with respect and a sincere heart.

Correspondences

Animals: Goats pull his wagon.

Attributes: Mjollnir, his iron hammer. Iron-clad gloves.

Sacred Tree: Oak. He is the spirit of the oak forests once spread across Europe.

Hall: Bilskirnir, with over five hundred rooms, shared with Sif and their children.

Petition For

Protection of homes, communities, land, laws, civilization. Protection against disaster, chaos, fire, and lightning. Earth's fertility and abundance. The right amount of rain. Aid against anyone bigger than you.

Favored People

Everyone, the masses, travelers, the dead. Thralls were feted in his hall after death. He is not the patron of the elite but of the people.

Sacred Days

Thursday, Thor's Day. The oak groves once spread across Europe were his temples, including one near Dublin. His sacred oak near Fritzler, Germany was felled by Saint Boniface in 755.

Words of Power

Thor · Donar · Thunar · Drum Beater · Mr. Barbaro · Lord of Thunder · Mjollnir

Notes and Cautions

Does he come into conflict with Loki, spirit of chaos? You bet. Thor is a protector first. He is a giant killer, invoke his aid against any oppressor: spirit, human, corporate, commercial, or bureaucracy.
