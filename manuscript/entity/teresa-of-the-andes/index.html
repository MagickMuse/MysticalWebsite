Roman Catholic saint, the first Chilean to be canonized.

Life

Teresa of the Andes was born Juanita Fernández Solar on 13 July 1900 to a wealthy family in Santiago, Chile. As a young girl, she read the autobiography of Thérèse of Lisieux and was profoundly moved. At eighteen, she entered the Carmelite convent in the city of Los Andes, eighty kilometers from Santiago, adopting the name Teresa in honor of Teresa of Ávila.

Teresa contracted typhus and died on 12 April 1920, just three months before her twentieth birthday. Following her death, miracles were attributed to her. She was canonized in 1993.

Attributes

Flowers. Cross.

Spiritual Allies

Teresa may be invoked together with the other saintly Teresas: Thérèse of Lisieux and Teresa of Ávila, whom she loved.

Feast Days

12 April. 13 July.

Sacred Sites

  • The Sanctuary of Teresa of the Andes near the town of Los Andes, built in 1987 to house her relics, receives 100,000 visitors annually.
  • The Monasterio del Espíritu Santo on Santa Teresa Avenue in Los Andes, her former home, now contains a museum dedicated to Teresa featuring her clothing and photographs.
