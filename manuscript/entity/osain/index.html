The Divine Herbalist

Osain has one eye and one arm. He hops around like a bird on one leg. He does have two ears: one is huge, but the other is tiny and shriveled up. The huge ear is deaf, but the little one is so acute it can hear the sound of a single flower crying or the flap of a butterfly's wings. He has a tiny squeaky voice, possibly similar to the voice of plants.

Born from Mother Earth

Osain wasn't born of orisha parents. He sprang from Mother Earth just like a plant with beads shining all over his body.

Guardian of Botanical Secrets

Osain, divine herbalist and powerful sorcerer, knows all Earth's botanical secrets. (He lost those missing limbs and organs when punished by other orishas for attempting magickal world domination. Ogun saved him from being utterly destroyed; they are now close allies.) Osain is the guardian of all medicinal plants.

Invocations

Osain may be invoked to help identify herbal cures and to enhance the magickal and healing powers of plants.

Syncretization

Osain is syncretized to John the Baptist and Saints Sylvester and Benedict.
