Magickal Properties

Vedic Divine Twins, Dasra and Nasatya, solar horse brothers who disperse gloom at dawn; physicians of the Devas and saviors from peril.

Ritual Uses

Petition at sunrise for healing, relief from suffering, safe journeys, and oracular guidance. Invite their presence for medical insight and recovery.

Offerings

Honey and ghee (burn a cotton wick in ghee), clean water, and dawn prayers.

Favored People

The ailing, healers, travelers, newborns, and all who seek bright, helpful beginnings.

Petition For

Healing, protection in travel, clear omens, and renewed vitality.

Warnings

Petitions should be benevolent; the Twins aid health and rescue.

Additional Notes

Sons of the Sun; preside over Earth in Rig Veda; first lunar mansion Ashvini marks their star. Presence itself is healing.

Encyclopedia Source Notes (Asvins)

Physician gods of the Hindu Vedic tradition. The Asvins are twin gods who own horses, the sons of Vivasvan and Saranyu. They are depicted in a chariot drawn by horses or birds. Attributes include a book, a vessel with herbs, and a water jar.
