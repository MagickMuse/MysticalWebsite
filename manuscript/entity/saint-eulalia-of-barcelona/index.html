Classification

Venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity.

Life and Martyrdom

Saint Eulalia was born around 290 CE and died on February 12, 303 during Diocletian's persecutions in Barcelona. She was forced to undergo thirteen tortures, including:

  • Being placed in a barrel studded inside with knives or glass shards and rolled down the hill now known as Saint Eulalia's Descent
  • Having her breasts sliced off
  • Being crucified on an X-shaped cross
  • Finally being decapitated

A dove flew from her neck after her head was severed, indicating her sacred status.

Hidden and Recovered

Eulalia's body was hidden in 713 during the Moorish invasion and recovered in 878.

Powers and Domains

She is invoked against drought and to protect those who travel over water.

Attribute

An X-shaped cross.

Sacred Animal

Dove.

Sacred Site

Eulalia is the patron saint of Barcelona. She is interred in the crypt of the Cathedral of Saint Eulalia. An annual festival in Barcelona honors her on her Roman Catholic feast day.

Feast Days

  • 12 February (Roman Catholic)
  • 22 August (Orthodox Churches)
