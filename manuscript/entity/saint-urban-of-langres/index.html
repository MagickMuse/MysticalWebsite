Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Saint Urban (327-c. 390) served as the sixth bishop of Langres in the Champagne region of France from 374 until his death. During a time of persecution against Christians, Urban hid in a vineyard. By the time it was safe to emerge, he had converted the laborers who hid him to Christianity. Ever since, he has been the patron of the wine industry, especially French wines.

Domains

Urban is the patron of anyone who works with wine in any capacity, from those who tend the grapes to those who sell the bottles. He is also invoked to protect against any harm that may be caused by wine, such as alcoholism or drunk driving, as well as any harm that may affect wine, vineyards, and the harvest, like storms, frost, and blight.

Hair Growth

Saint Urban is also invoked for hair growth. Place a lock of your hair before an image of Saint Urban and request that he stimulate its growth.

Weather Lore

Allegedly, the weather on Saint Urban's Day reveals the upcoming autumn's weather. If the sun shines on Saint Urban's Day, a successful grape harvest is expected.

Iconography

Urban is portrayed as a bishop holding a bunch of grapes or with a grape vine at his side.

Attributes

Grapes, grape vine, book with grapes or a wine vessel on its cover.
