Magickal Properties

Celtic bear goddess and guardian of bears; patron of forests and right relationship with wild creatures.

Ritual Uses

Petition for protection in forests, balanced hunting, and courage. Approach in silence and with offerings suitable to bears.

Offerings

Fruit, honey, nuts; respectful presence in her groves.

Favored People

Forest guardians, ethical hunters, and those who protect bears.

Petition For

Safe passage, balance with wildlife, and steadfast courage.

Warnings

Never take cubs or hunt recklessly; remove perishable offerings the same day.

Additional Notes

Name means "Bear"; inscriptions found in Switzerland and near Trier; linked with Bern.

Encyclopedia Source Notes

Fertility goddess and guardian spirit of bears in the Romano-Celtic Continental European tradition. Known only from inscriptions and sculptures in the Berne region of Switzerland. A bronze depicts her offering fruit to a bear. She seems also to be a goddess of prosperity and harvest. She became syncretized with the Roman god Mercury as Mercury Artaios. Compare also Artemis Brauronia.
