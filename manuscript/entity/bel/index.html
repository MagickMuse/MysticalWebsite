Bel is a generic title meaning "lord" in the Mesopotamian tradition of Babylonian-Akkadian religion. The Babylonian god Marduk was often addressed as Bel. The name also appears at Palmyra, where Bel was worshipped as the tutelary creator god.

Domains

Lordship, creation (at Palmyra), and sovereignty over the divine assembly.

Ritual

The New Year festival of akitu in Babylon included a ceremony of "leading Bel by the hand," symbolizing the god's procession and the renewal of cosmic order.

Attributes

At Palmyra, his attributes include lightning and an eagle.

Sacred Sites

Babylon, where he was invoked as an epithet of Marduk during akitu, and Palmyra in Syria, where he presided as tutelary creator.
