Venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity. He is called the 'apostle of non-violence' and is distinct from Moses the Prophet.

Life and History

Moses (c. 330 to 28 August 405) was an Ethiopian slave forced to serve in the home of an Egyptian government official. After being driven into the desert, possibly for theft, he became the leader of a band of seventy-five outlaws who terrorized travelers, robbing and sometimes killing them.

Moses and his cohorts repeatedly defeated troops sent by the governor to capture them. When an overwhelming force was finally dispatched, Moses and his band scattered into the desert they knew so well. Moses sought refuge at the Petra monastery in Skete, whose seventy-five monks were hermits and anchorites.

Presenting himself as a pilgrim, Moses was sheltered by the monks despite their suspicions. While residing there, he experienced a spiritual epiphany and began a new life as a monk, spending the rest of his days struggling with his violent temper and passions.

Transformation

Moses vowed never to harm another human being. When four brigands once attacked him in the desert, the man who would have killed them in his former life instead overcame and tied them up, slinging all four over his shoulder and dumping them on the church floor for the other monks to decide their fate.

Eventually becoming a priest, Moses founded a new monastery consisting of seventy-five monks.

The Leaky Bag of Sand

Ordered to attend a meeting intended to pronounce judgment on a monk accused of infractions, Moses arrived carrying a leaky bag filled with sand on his back. When asked what he was doing, Moses explained that he carried his own sins, which spilled out behind him so they were not visible to him, though they were to others. The metaphor was understood; no judgment was passed on the erring monk, who was forgiven.

Death

Moses foresaw his own death when brigands attacked his monastery. Refusing to take up arms, he sent many monks into hiding and greeted his attackers at the door. He perceived that he was fulfilling the prophecy of Matthew 26:52: "All who live by the sword will perish by the sword." He was killed with a sword on 28 August 405, at seventy-five years old.

Iconography

Moses is depicted as an Ethiopian monk, sometimes holding or with a leaky bag or basket of sand.
