Venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity, Marina spent her life disguised as a male monk.

Life and Legend

Marina was born in what is now Lebanon during the fifth or sixth century. When her mother died while Marina was young, her father Eugenius of Bithynia joined a monastery. To stay together, he disguised Marina as a boy, renaming her Marinus. Her female identity remained secret throughout her life, and she eventually took monastic vows.

She spent virtually her entire life at the monastery in Qannoubin, Lebanon. During travels, she once stayed at an inn where another young woman observed her. When this woman later conceived out of wedlock, she accused the young monk of fathering her child to protect the true father. Marina refused to defend herself and was expelled from the monastery, forced to beg outside its gates while living in a nearby grotto.

The Miracle of Nursing

When the child was born, Marina was given custody. According to legend, she miraculously nursed the baby despite her circumstances. Eventually, mother and child were readmitted to the monastery, though Marina was assigned the most menial tasks as penance. The boy later became a monk himself.

Discovery and Veneration

Marina's true identity was discovered only after her death when her body was prepared for burial. She had left a note explaining her history. She was immediately hailed as a saint, and healing miracles were attributed to her right away.

Iconography

Though she passed perfectly as a man in life, her images clearly depict her as a woman in monk's clothing, usually accompanied by a child.

Ritual

In the Balkans, perpetual fires are extinguished on her feast day, then relit.

Sacred Sites

Saint Marina's Grotto in Qannoubin, Lebanon, where eighteen Maronite patriarchs are entombed. Marina was reputedly once entombed there, but her relics were stolen and brought to Constantinople, then stolen again and taken to Venice. Their whereabouts are now unknown.

The Church of Santa Maria Formosa houses a veil said to have belonged to Marina. Her right hand is allegedly kept at her shrine in the Church of the Holy Virgin in Harat-al-Rum, Cairo. Her left hand may be in Saint Catherine's Sinai Monastery.

Feast Days

12 February (Roman Catholic), 17 June (Orthodox Church), 17 July (Maronite Church and Venice), 21 August (Coptic Church).
