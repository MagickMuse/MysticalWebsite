✈️Talisman for Protection During Travel

Magickal Properties

Adeona is the Spirit of Safe and Speedy Returns, indigenous to the Roman region but predating the Romans. She often works in conjunction with her sister Abeona, Spirit of Departures. Together they provide comprehensive protection for travelers, especially children away from home for the first time.

Ritual Uses

Work with Adeona for safe return rituals, especially for children. She can be petitioned together with Abeona to guard children who journey back and forth from home daily. Create rituals ensuring safe passage and return from journeys.

Offerings

Traveler's talismans, protective amulets, and offerings of gratitude for safe journeys. Present items that symbolize safe return and homecoming.

Favored People

Children making regular journeys, travelers seeking safe return, parents of children away from home, and those working to recover missing or displaced persons.

Petition For

Safe returns, protection of children and travelers, recovery of kidnapped children, ensuring safe passage home, and protection during regular journeys between home and other locations.

Warnings

Works in conjunction with sister spirit Abeona. Honor both for complete protection during departures and returns.

Additional Notes

If a child is forcibly removed from home whether by kidnap or other means, Adeona is an appropriate spirit to petition for protection and speedy, safe return. Her partnership with Abeona demonstrates the importance of protection during both departure and return phases of travel.
