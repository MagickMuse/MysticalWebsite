Ka'ahu Pahau is a benevolent Hawaiian shark goddess who protects the people of Pearl Harbor. She and her brother Kahi'uka were born as shark spirits who could take human or shark form at will. When their father suggested they move from Maui to O'ahu, they journeyed together and Ka'ahu Pahau chose Pearl Harbor as her home while her brother settled in Kalihi Channel.

As the sacred guardian of Pearl Harbor, she protects swimmers, surfers, and ocean travelers in her domain. She maintains order among sharks in her waters, ensuring they do not harm humans under her protection. Her presence represents the Hawaiian understanding of shark spirits as protective rather than threatening forces.

⚠️ Ka'ahu Pahau is venerated within Native Hawaiian (Kanaka Maoli) traditions. These are closed practices and should be approached with explicit cultural permission and respect for Hawaiian protocols.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Ka'ahu Pahau before entering Pearl Harbor waters for protection and safe passage. She can be petitioned for protection from shark attacks and for safe ocean travel in Hawaiian waters. Her presence ensures harmony between humans and sharks in her domain.

Warnings

Ka'ahu Pahau's protection is strongest in Pearl Harbor, her chosen home. Respect Hawaiian protocols when working with shark deities and honor the sacred nature of these guardian spirits.
