Magickal Properties

Cymbee Spirits are Simbi spirits who survived the Middle Passage and made their home in South Carolina's Low Country waters. Each spring or small body of fresh water has its own Cymbee with unique appearance, size, and habits. They are guardians of water who bring prosperity, good fortune, and abundance, including fertility.

Ritual Uses

Sit quietly near water to see if a Cymbee will reveal itself. They may serve as personal or family guardians in addition to guarding their water source. Plank bridges placed over water serve as seats for the Cymbees. They demonstrate their power through water phenomena like roiling or churning waters.

Offerings

Always treat their water as precious and sacred. Small gifts appreciated: shiny trinkets, libations, traditional offerings for mermaids. Show respect through careful treatment of their water source.

Favored People

Communities living near springs and freshwater sources, families seeking a water guardian spirit, those needing prosperity and abundance, people respecting traditional African water spirit practices.

Petition For

Protection and blessing of water sources, prosperity and good fortune for families, fertility and abundance, guardianship over community water supplies, removal of drought or water problems.

Warnings

Fairly volatile spirits, any disturbance of the spring or disrespect toward it angers them. They show displeasure by making waters roil, raising storms, or removing water entirely (springs drying up). Sensitive to disrespect and require proper acknowledgment.

Additional Notes

Cymbees are proportionate in size to their water source and described as vaguely human in appearance. Some have webbed feet or full mermaid features with fish tails. Individual Cymbees have names, some wonderfully evocative like 'The Great Desire of the Unrotting Waters.' The Middle Passage and conditions in South Carolina did not improve their tempers.
