Vassago is a demon from the Medieval grimoire tradition, classified among the Names of Dark Power.

Specialties

  • Find what is lost or hidden

Origins

Vassago is numbered among the seventy two spirits catalogued in the Lemegeton, or Lesser Key of Solomon, and is also named by Wierus, pupil of Cornelius Agrippa, in his Pseudomonarchia Daemonorum. Grimoire tradition describes him as a mighty prince of Agares' nature who reveals things past, present, and to come, uncovers what has been lost or hidden, and governs twenty six legions of spirits. Knowledge of him reaches back further still, to before the earliest Babylonian records; he is counted among the Nephelim, and Eastern folklore numbers him among the seventy two Lords of the Djinn.

Nature and Approach

Though described as good by nature, Vassago is a being of primordial fire far older than humankind, and considerably more intelligent than the average practitioner attempting to summon him. He should be approached with real respect rather than casual curiosity, and is traditionally reserved for matters of extreme perplexity, called upon only once lesser forms of divination have been exhausted.

Timing

Traditional workings favor clear weather with the moon two, four, six, eight, ten, twelve, or fourteen days old and always on the increase. As a prince among the seventy two, however, Vassago is said to stand outside these sidereal restrictions and may be summoned at any hour.
