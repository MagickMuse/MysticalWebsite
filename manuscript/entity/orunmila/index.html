The Sacred Diviner

Orunmila is the sacred diviner. He is the master of Ifa, a complex, sacred system of divination. He also taught people cowrie shell divination. Orunmila is a spirit of righteousness and a miracle healer, considered among the most powerful orishas.

Witness to Creation

He was the only orisha permitted to actually witness Creation.

Among the Seven African Powers

Orunmila is traditionally classified among the Seven African Powers. However, he is a remote orisha with specialized interests who is not involved with the rank-and-file populace in the manner of other orishas. In recent years, Orunmila's place among the Seven African Powers has been popularly bestowed upon either Oya or Ochossi.

Syncretization

Orunmila is syncretized to Saint Francis of Assisi.
