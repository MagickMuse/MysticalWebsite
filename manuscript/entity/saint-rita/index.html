Classification

Roman Catholic saint.

Life and History

Saint Rita of Cascia (1381-1457) was an Italian Augustinian nun renowned as the patron saint of impossible causes and desperate situations.

Powers and Domains

Saint Rita specializes in impossible causes—situations where solutions seemingly do not exist. She is invoked by those in desperate circumstances, abused wives, and those suffering from tumors and women's reproductive disorders. In some North African devotional traditions, she is associated with Sidi Ali Sanhaji in intercession for the protection of graves.
