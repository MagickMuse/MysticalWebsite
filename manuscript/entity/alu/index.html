Magickal Properties

Alu are a type of Babylonian spirit whose name may be translated as "Storm" or "Tempest" but is also sometimes considered a synonym for demon. They cause destructive storms, illness and nightmares, including night paralysis. The Alu's presence may be sufficient to cause this effect, or it may physically press down heavily or suck out the sleeper's breath.

Ritual Uses

These are dangerous spirits to be avoided and banished rather than worked with. They are used primarily as examples of what to protect against in Babylonian magickal practice.

Offerings

None, these are dangerous spirits that should not be propitiated or encouraged.

Favored People

None, Alu cause harm rather than provide assistance.

Petition For

Banishment and protection from their influence. Seek protective magick and amulets to ward them off.

Warnings

DANGEROUS: Alu roam the streets at night looking for trouble. They lurk in dark corners, desolate places, and ruins. They manifest commonly as black dogs but may appear in half-human/half-animal form. Their presence causes night paralysis, destructive storms, and various illnesses.

Additional Notes

In Babylonian tradition, these spirits represent chaotic forces that must be contained through proper protective rituals and avoidance of their favored haunts during vulnerable times.
