Magickal Properties

  • Germanic Mountain Spirit who presides over iron mines, each mine may have its own Berggeist
  • Once anticipated gifts, offerings, and requests for permission to dig
  • Post-Christianity was blamed for mine disasters and accused of being in league with devil
  • Occasionally favors someone by directing them to untapped veins in the mine

Ritual Uses

  • Request permission before digging or mining operations
  • Leave offerings at mine entrances and seek guidance for safe extraction
  • Petition for discovery of ore veins and protection underground

Offerings

Gifts, offerings, and formal requests for permission to dig, traditional miner's courtesy

Favored People

Miners who earn his favor through respect and proper protocols

Petition For

Permission to dig, discovery of ore veins, protection within mines, guidance to untapped resources

Warnings

Post-Christianity demonization made him seem dangerous, but true danger comes from ignoring proper mining protocols and permissions. Disasters occur when he is not properly propitiated.

Additional Notes

  • Manifestation: Elderly white-bearded man in red cloak and wide-brimmed hat, accompanied by young deer
  • Famous as inspiration for Gandalf, J.R.R. Tolkien bought postcard of painting "Der Berggeist" by Josef Madlener
  • Literally means "Mountain Spirit" in German
  • Part of traditional Germanic mining spiritual practices
