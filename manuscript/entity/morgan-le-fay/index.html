In Witchcraft

Morgan le Fay belongs to the tradition of the enchantresses of medieval legend whose symbolism underlies the witchcraft chalice current. She stands alongside Viviane, Brisen, Nimue, Melusine, and the Lady of the Lake as one of the fays and enchantresses who partake of the power of the Lady of Delight: the governing feminine force of love and romantic enchantment, standing in polarity to the more aggressive energies of wand and athame. This current is invoked through the chalice in traditional love workings.
