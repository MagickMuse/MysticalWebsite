Saint Lawrence is the patron saint of archivists, librarians, cooks, chefs, brewers, comedians, and confectioners.

Saint Lawrence is renowned for healing burns, fever, and any condition associated with burning sensations.

Saint Lawrence (died 258 CE) was an archdeacon in Rome during the early Church, responsible for Church finances. When he learned of impending persecution and his own arrest, he sold all Church possessions and distributed the proceeds to the poor. When Roman authorities demanded he surrender the Church's treasures, he assembled the poor and declared them to be the Church's true treasures. He was arrested, tortured, and ultimately martyred on a gridiron.

Lawrence maintained his composure and sharp wit throughout his ordeal. According to legend, he told his executioners to turn him over because he was already "done" on one side. His defiant bravado inspired many onlookers to convert to Christianity.

Domains

Saint Lawrence is invoked against burns of any kind, including injuries caused by fire. He is petitioned to prevent and extinguish fires, and is called upon for relief from fever and any condition associated with burning sensations. He also protects crops from burning up, particularly during the harsh August sun, and is invoked against poverty.

Iconography

Saint Lawrence is typically depicted dressed in red, holding a book, gridiron, or money bag.

Lore

The Perseid Meteor Showers, which occur annually in August and often coincide with his feast, are also known as the Tears of Saint Lawrence.

Ritual

It is traditional to refrain from cooking on his feast day and serve only cold foods.

Sacred Sites

Saint Lawrence is the patron saint of Rome, and several churches there are dedicated to him. There is debate over the precise location of his martyrdom:

  • The Church of San Lorenzo in Panisperna is reputedly built over the site of his death
  • The Papal Basilica of San Lorenzo Outside the Walls claims to contain the stone on which his body was laid after death and is also said to stand over the execution site
  • The gridiron of his martyrdom is allegedly preserved in a closed recess behind the main altar of the Basilica of San Lorenzo in Lucina
