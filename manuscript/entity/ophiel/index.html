Ophiel

Olympic Spirit of Mercury

Ophiel is the Olympic Spirit of Mercury, one of seven planetary spirits from the Medieval grimoire tradition. These beings represent the pagan energies of celestial bodies and predate the angelic and demonic classifications of later Ceremonial Magick.

Attributes

Planet: Mercury ☿

Metal: Mercury (Quicksilver)

Day: Wednesday

Zodiac: Gemini, Virgo

Powers: Arts, speed, communication, knowledge acquisition, transmutation to quicksilver

Legions: Commands 100,000 legions

Working with Ophiel

Ophiel may be invoked to lend Mercurial power to spellwork. The spirit's sigil can be inscribed on candles, drawn on paper pentacles with spell materials placed atop, or written onto paper with charm words and burned or added to pouches.

Associated Names

Names of Planetary Spirits (Mercury): Azarel, Ichadiel, Yashiel

Names of Power: Tiriel, Taphthartharath

Source

Arbatel de Magia Veterum (1575)
