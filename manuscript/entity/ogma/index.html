Lord of Eloquence

Ogma, Lord of Eloquence and Literature and a member of the Tuatha Dé Danaan, is credited with inventing Ogham script, a system of writing incorporating notches and strokes traditionally cut into wood or stone. It may also be used for divination and to communicate with Ogma. Ogma is a poet and a warrior. He is skilled in warcraft. His name appears on curse tablets intended to provide justice or to enforce curses. The Romans identified Ogma with Hercules.

Manifestation

Ogma is described as an old but powerful bald, sunburned man yet he is simultaneously a strong-man akin to Heracles.

Iconography

Ogma leads a band of happy men attached to him by golden chains linking their ears to his tongue.

Spirit Allies

His father, the Dagda. Ogma forms a triad with the Dagda and Lugh.
