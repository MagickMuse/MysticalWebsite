Olympic Spirit of Saturn

Aratron is the Olympic Spirit of Saturn, one of seven planetary spirits from medieval grimoire tradition. These beings represent the pagan energies of celestial bodies and predate the angelic and demonic classifications of later Ceremonial Magick. Aratron governs Saturnian matters and is said to command 17,640 legions.

Attributes

Planet: Saturn ♄

Metal: Lead

Day: Saturday

Zodiac: Capricorn, Aquarius

Powers: Magick, spirit communication, esoteric knowledge, transmutation to lead, longevity, invisibility, alchemy

Working with Aratron

Aratron may be invoked to lend Saturnian power to spellwork. The spirit's sigil can be inscribed on candles, drawn on paper pentacles with spell materials placed atop, or written onto paper with charm words and burned or added to pouches.

Associated Names

Names of Planetary Spirits (Saturn): Vehiel, Vaol, Cassiel

Names of Power: Agiel, Zazel
