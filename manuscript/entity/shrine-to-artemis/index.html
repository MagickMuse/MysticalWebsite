Shrine to Artemis

🌙
Artemis · Diana · Cynthia · Jana · Tana

Moon goddess and twin to Apollo, born first. Mistress of wild nature, witchcraft, women's mysteries. Guardian of thresholds, hunts, and sacred groves.

⚪
White and silver. Her colors are explicit in the manuscript. The crescent moon, the lunar headdress, the silver bow.
Working in tune with nature, within and without, and dedicating oneself to mastery, can lead like her arrows to hitting any target.

🕯️

Devotional audio for Artemis

Video

Offerings

Honey cakes, pure water, laurel or bay. Vows to protect wild animals and wild places.

Correspondences

Animals: Stag, pack of hunting dogs, bear. The Lady of Beasts.

Attributes: Crescent-shaped bow and arrows, horned or lunar headdress.

Twin: Apollo, whom she midwifed at her own birth.

Consort (as Diana): Virbius, horned stag spirit subordinate to her.

Petition For

Midwifery protections, menstrual regulation, wildlife protection, success and restraint in the hunt, preservation of wild places. Women's autonomy and sovereignty.

Favored People

Girls and women, lesbians, sailors and mariners, Amazons, runners and archers. Midwives. Wolf-shamans and lunar priests served her in Celtic Europe.

Sacred Days

The moon and her phases. The crescent and the full. The Forest of Nemi where Diana lived with Virbius. Approach her at dusk and at the edges of wild places.

Words of Power

Artemis · Diana · Cynthia · Jana · Tana · Lady of Beasts · Mistress of Wild Nature
