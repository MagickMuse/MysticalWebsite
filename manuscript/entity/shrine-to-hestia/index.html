Hestia · Vesta

The first and last deity invoked in every Roman sacrifice. Keeper of the eternal flame. Guardian of the sacred hearth. The veiled goddess of the round temple, present in all flames.

As we approach the year's longest night,
I ask you, Hestia,
to be with me and my loved ones
and safeguard us through the hours.
May we be blessed in community,
bountiful in feast,
and rich in the earth's gifts
as we welcome the return of the light.

From Appeal for Protection Through Winter

Sacred Dates

Vestalia · 9-15 June

Her sanctuary opens exclusively to women who enter barefoot. The 15th is a cleaning day; dirt is disposed of in the Tiber.

1 March

The first day of the Roman year, when her flame is ritually relit.

Vernal Equinox

Bay laurel branches are discarded and replaced.

Words of Power

Inscribed on walls during chamber spells to invoke her presence in the home:

HESTIA, for a peaceful home life.
HESTIA, for a peaceful home.
VESTA, for a happy home.
VESTA, for stability.

Notes and Cautions

Vesta rewards steady household care. Maintain a clean hearth and offer regularly at the home altar. Never commercialize hearth rites or neglect the domestic flame.
