Overview

Luna is the Roman embodiment or spirit of the moon. Over time she became deeply associated with the Greek lunar goddess Selene, and today the two names are largely used interchangeably. Very little independent mythology survives about Luna on her own; much of her mythological content merged with Selene's tradition as Roman and Greek religious cultures blended. She was balanced by Sol, the spirit of the sun.

She had temples in Rome, including one on the Aventine Hill and another on the Palatine, the latter dedicated to her as Luna Noctiluca, "the Moon that Shines by Night."

Magickal Properties

In moon magick, Luna can be petitioned directly as the spirit of the moon herself. She is a pure embodiment of lunar energy and is appropriate to call upon for any work tied to the moon's phases, tides, and cycles. For fuller mythological context and ritual use, see Selene.
