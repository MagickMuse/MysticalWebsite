Magickal Properties

  • Among Britain's most famous spectral hounds, may be dangerous/malevolent OR helpful/benevolent
  • Usually serves as guardian of treasure or sacred place
  • Demonstrates supernatural powers including causing terrible wounds, paralysis, or death before vanishing
  • If left alone, usually leaves people alone, but responds to aggression with supernatural force

Ritual Uses

  • Do not invoke or attempt to command, acknowledge respectfully if encountered
  • Serves as natural guardian of sacred sites and treasures
  • Respect its presence as boundary marker and protector

Offerings

None required, respect its territory and purpose

Favored People

Respectful travelers, guardians of sacred places, those who honor boundaries

Petition For

Not petitioned directly, serves as protector rather than petitioned spirit

Warnings

Never show aggression toward Black Dog. It isn't afraid to demonstrate supernatural powers if threatened. Approach with respect and maintain appropriate distance.

Additional Notes

  • Manifestation: Shaggy black dog the size of a calf with glowing red eyes
  • Behavior: If left alone, usually leaves you alone; responds to aggression with supernatural retaliation
  • Function: Guardian of treasure, sacred places, natural boundary enforcement
  • Vanishing: Disappears into thin air after demonstrating its power or message
