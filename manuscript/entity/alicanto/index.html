Magickal Properties

The Alicanto is a magickal, nocturnal bird that appears nondescript in daylight but at night gleams with golden light from its wings. Its luminous eyes are like lasers, serving as a beacon in darkness. It subsists on gold and silver, making it too heavy to fly, so it runs with arms extended. It possesses the intelligence to judge the worthiness of those who seek it.

Ritual Uses

Work with the Alicanto through visualization and dream work for guidance in treasure seeking. Miners and gold prospectors long for glimpses of the Alicanto as it allegedly leads to treasure. It may be invoked in visualizations or appear in dreams to guide worthy seekers.

Offerings

Respect for its judgment and proper intentions when seeking treasure. The Alicanto responds to genuine need rather than greed. Present offerings that demonstrate worthiness and ethical intention.

Favored People

Miners, gold prospectors, and those seeking hidden treasures, particularly those with worthy intentions and genuine need rather than pure greed.

Petition For

Guidance to treasure, wisdom in material pursuits, discernment of true value, protection from dangers associated with treasure seeking, and judgment of worthiness in material endeavors.

Warnings

Magickal nocturnal bird. If it favors you, leads to treasure. If displeased, leads to danger. No one is required to follow it. The choice remains with the seeker. It can lead to jagged cliffs and danger if serving as punishment.

Additional Notes

The Alicanto allegedly lives in small caves or crevices within mines. The crucial thing to remember is that it's a magick bird capable of judging character and intent, not just a simple treasure-finding creature. Its ability to distinguish between worthy seekers and those driven by pure greed makes it both a guide and a test of character.
