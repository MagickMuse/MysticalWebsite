Basa-JaunMermaids

Magickal Properties

  • Basque mountain mermaid (Land/Mountain Mermaid) who embodies the allure and peril of high places
  • Keeper of Pyrenean secrets, liminal beauty, and sudden precipices

Ritual Uses

  • Approach politely at springs or ledges with small, beautiful gifts; request teachings about the mountains and hidden paths
  • Use for place‑based attunement and respect for dangerous terrain

Offerings

  • Ornaments for her hair, cosmetics, perfume, a beautiful shiny smooth stone

Favored People

Those who honor the mountains with restraint and courtesy

Petition For

Mountain knowledge and secrets of the Pyrenees

Warnings

Approach politely and cautiously. She lures men who pursue her to dangerous precipices so avoid pursuit and leave respectful offerings. Do not pursue her, she leads the unwary to dangerous precipices and unstable ground.

Additional Notes

  • Also called Land Mermaid or Mountain Mermaid; appears in Basque lore alongside Basa‑Jaun
