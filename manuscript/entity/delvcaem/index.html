Magickal Properties

Delvcaem ('Fair Shape') is described as an amazingly powerful sorceress, possibly an alter ego of Becuma or representing the life-giving aspect versus blight. She presides over taking one's rightful place and is associated with restoration of fertility and abundance to Ireland. Her arrival forces Becuma to immediately leave, suggesting they cannot coexist.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Delvcaem when you need to claim your rightful place or position. She is a great magician who can be petitioned for magical instruction and lessons. Her presence restores life, abundance, and fertility where there was barren waste. Work with her for powerful transformation magick.

Offerings

Wine served in a chalice (her attribute), beautiful and fair offerings that honor her name 'Fair Shape,' things that represent abundance and fertility, recognition of true love and rightful partnerships.

Favored People

Those seeking to find and claim their rightful place in life, magical practitioners needing powerful instruction and lessons, people pursuing true love and authentic relationships, communities needing restoration of abundance and fertility.

Petition For

Finding and claiming one's rightful place and position, powerful sorcery and magical abilities, true love and authentic relationships, fertility and abundance for land and people, restoration after blight or barren periods.

Warnings

Cannot be in same place as Becuma at same time, they are opposing forces. Her power is immense but may come with the responsibility of displacing what came before. Approach with understanding of the transformative nature of claiming one's rightful place.

Additional Notes

Lives on island ruled by her father King Morgan and mother Dog Head. Her arrival in Ireland immediately restored life and fertility. Art fell in love with her during his quest. She has a sister who bears a chalice of poison, while Delvcaem bears wine. Represents the spirit of rightful sovereignty and restoration.
