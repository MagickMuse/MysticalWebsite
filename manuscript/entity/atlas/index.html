Magickal Properties

Atlas, Lord of Dangerous Wisdom, knows the mysteries of sea and sky. As punishment for leading the Titans, he bears the weight that keeps Earth and Sky asunder.

Ritual Uses

Contemplate endurance and the separation of realms; seek steadfastness in impossible burdens.

Offerings

Not specified in the text.

Favored People

Those seeking resilience and profound, perilous knowledge.

Petition For

Endurance and strength under heavy responsibility.

Warnings

His wisdom is dangerous; approach with respect.

Additional Notes

Brother to Prometheus; father and ancestor to star and nymph lineages including the Pleiades, Hyades, and Hesperides. Modern image stands at Rockefeller Center.
