Deity of Wisdom and Obstacles

Ganesha is the deity of intelligence and wisdom as well as the remover of obstacles. It is worth noting that he can also place obstacles in someone's path as a form of education and correction, making him a more complex and active force than his popular reputation suggests.

The Elephant Head

There are several versions of the story behind Ganesha's hybrid appearance. In the most common telling, Ganesha was stationed as guardian of his mother Parvati's gate when he tried, with great diligence, to prevent his own father Shiva from entering. Shiva, displeased, cut off the young man's head. Regretting the act, he decreed that the head of the first passing animal would replace his son's. That animal was an elephant. Ganesha was restored to life with this unwieldy head, and his name means "elephant head."

The Mouse Vehicle

Ganesha is sometimes depicted standing upon a rat or mouse. This unlikely vehicle was assigned to him by a contemptuous demon. The mouse itself carries symbolic weight: the word derives from the Sanskrit musaka, meaning "thief," because mice steal grain. The mouse is therefore an obstacle in its own right, and Ganesha's mastery of it is a sign of his dominion over all such impediments.

On the Pink Lotus

The elephant-headed remover of obstacles is also seen seated on a pink Lotus, the bloom shared with Lakshmi.
