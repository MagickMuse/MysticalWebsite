🔥Salamander Courage Spell

Fire Elementals

Salamanders, in the magickal sense, are the fire elementals—not the lizard species. You may see these shining beings in a candle flame or the sun's rays, and they abide in all types of fire. They stimulate inspiration—when you have an "Aha!" moment and suddenly "see the light," you may have connected with a fire elemental.

Temperament and Domains

Salamanders like to work with people who have a spiritual bent and with those who exhibit initiative or daring. Invite them to join you when you do spells that involve passion, vitality, courage, or action.

Offerings

Salamanders like candles and incense. Burn these to honor your fiery helpers. Always remember to thank the elementals that assist you in your spellwork—if you behave disrespectfully toward them, they may retaliate by playing nasty tricks on you. Be generous, however, and your elemental friends will continue to serve you faithfully.

One of the Four Elementals

The salamander was first described as one of the four elemental spirits by the alchemist Paracelsus, the living manifestation of fire, for whom flames hold no threat. By a strange contradiction it was also thought so icy cold that it could quench a fire, and the ancient Egyptians used the image of the salamander as a hieroglyph for a person who had died of cold.

Composure and the Alchemist's Sulfur

As a religious symbol the salamander stands for one who keeps composure, moral certitude, and peace of mind while enduring constant attack. In alchemy it is one of the symbols for sulfur, which, like the elemental spirit itself, feeds upon fire.
