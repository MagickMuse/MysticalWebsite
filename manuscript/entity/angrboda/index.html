Witch goddess of death, birth, storms, foreboding. Shape-shifter: crow (fertility messenger), golden goddess Gulveig ("shining one"), fierce iron hag Angerboda (preferred form, "Hag of Iron Wood," "Hag of East Winds"). May be giantess, troll-queen, witch, or Vanir. Grandmother of trolls.

The Gulveig Story

As Gulveig/Heid, Vanir sent her to Aesir requesting gold. Aesir attacked, stabbed, burned three times, yet survived. Unforgiving, raised children in Iron Wood to resent Aesir.

Children of Doom

Three primary children by Loki destined for Ragnarok: Hel (Queen of Death leading ghost uprising), Jormungard (Midgard Serpent slaying Thor), Fenris Wolf (Odin's Bane). Also mother to eclipse-causing wolves and werewolf sons. As Hag of East Winds, songs drive ships into storms.

Giant Heritage

Nature of Giants: Primordial, permanent, eternal. First being was giant Ymir, universe created from his corpse. Sons of giantesses survive Ragnarok while Aesir perish. Giants predate/outlast gods.

Giantesses: Manifest as fierce hags, warriors, mothers. Master shape-shifters called troll-hags and ogresses, synonyms for "witch." Angrboða's multiple forms reflect giantesses' liminal transformative nature.

Complex Relations: Though portrayed as enemies, giants are gods' parents, teachers, lovers, spouses. Christian scholars recording mythology identified with Aesir and portrayed giants negatively, but deeper truth reveals giants as essential eternal forces.

Multiple names indicate one being's aspects, hence Angrboða/Aurboda/Gulveig/Heid may be manifestations of same spirit.

Magickal Properties

Angrboða embodies the primordial power of witch wisdom passed through the giantess line and the transformative force of unresolved conflict that shapes destiny. As a spirit burned three times yet surviving, she carries the archetypal energy of what cannot be destroyed by force and will inevitably return with consequences.

Ritual Uses

Work with Angrboða for understanding deep witch wisdom passed through feminine lines, accessing transformative power through shapeshifting and multiple forms, connecting with primordial forces that predate and outlast current power structures, and working storm magick and weather control.

Offerings

Wolf and snake motifs, iron objects, petrified wood, gold (acknowledging the original conflict), and offerings that honor both destruction and survival.

Favored People

Witches seeking deep transformative wisdom, those working with shapeshifting and multiple identities, protectors of wolves and wild creatures, and people whose power has been attacked but survives.

Petition For

Magickal instruction from ancient sources, prophecy and foresight, storm work and weather magick, protection during liminal rites, strength to survive attacks and return stronger, and understanding of deep time and eternal cycles.

Warnings

Keep petitions precise; avoid reckless rites and Loki-adjacent entanglements; do not mix with hostile Aesir rites. Her power involves unresolved conflicts and forces that cannot be controlled, only worked with carefully.
