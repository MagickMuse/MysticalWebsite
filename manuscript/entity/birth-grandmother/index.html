Magickal Properties

Samshin Halmoni, the Birth Grandmother, is among Korea's most powerful goddesses. She was a shaman whose parents are described as "heavenly beings." At age fifteen, she was seduced by a monk who was an avatar of the historic Buddha, giving birth to triplets despite terrible obstacles.

Ritual Uses

Offer for three consecutive days following birth to bless the newborn and household. She may be venerated anytime, but offerings are expected for the three consecutive days following birth.

Offerings

Incense, candles, spring water, alcoholic beverages, cooked meals including seaweed soup and rice.

Favored People

New mothers and their newborn infants, families welcoming children.

Petition For

Safe delivery, protection of babies during first hundred days, blessings for home births.

Warnings

Honor her immediately after birth with offerings for three consecutive days; neglect may weaken protective guardianship.

Additional Notes

Her actual name Tang Kum Agassi means "Tang Dynasty Silk-Like Baby." Because she delivered three sons at once despite terrible obstacles, she was honored as the Birth Grandmother. She is responsible for all births on Earth and protects infants for the first hundred days after birth. Although she supervises all births, she takes special care of home births. Her attribute is a gourd dipper (ladle).
