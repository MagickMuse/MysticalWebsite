Mictlantecuhtli

Acolnahuacatl is a minor chthonic underworld god of the classical Mesoamerican Aztec tradition of Mexico. He stands as one of the lesser presences in the company of the lords of the dead, attending on the broader work of the underworld rather than ruling over it.

The Mictlantecuhtli Complex

Acolnahuacatl is one of the deities collectively classed as the Mictlantecuhtli complex, a grouping of underworld gods gathered around Mictlantecuhtli, Lord of Mictlan. The complex holds together the many faces of Aztec death and the many tasks that fall to those who keep the realm of the dead.

Domains

As a member of the Mictlantecuhtli complex, Acolnahuacatl governs a specific corner of the underworld's labor. His domain is bound to the larger work of receiving souls into Mictlan and to the protocol that surrounds proper passage through its nine descending levels.

⚠️ Closed Practice

Acolnahuacatl belongs to the Aztec religious tradition of Mexico, a closed practice rooted in specific cultural and initiatory context. Approach this entry as reference only. Do not adapt or perform Aztec underworld rites outside of the lineages and communities to which they belong.
