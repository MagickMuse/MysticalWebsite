⚠️ Acat is venerated within Mayan and Indigenous Mesoamerican traditions. These are closed practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

Acat is the Mayan deity who sponsors tattoo artists and presides over the tattooing process, especially for those who perceive tattooing as a spiritual rite. When the Conquistadores arrived in Central America, they were appalled to discover indigenous people creating indelible images of spirits on their skin.

Ritual Uses

Request that Acat bless your instruments, guide your hands, and send you the right customers. If seeking the right tattoo design, petition Acat for guidance. Work with him for spiritual protection during the tattooing process.

Offerings

A personal tattoo, tamales, tropical fruit, and traditional Mayan food. The most meaningful offering is receiving sacred tattoos with proper spiritual intention.

Favored People

Tattoo artists, those who study sacred tattoos, people seeking spiritually meaningful body art, and practitioners of body modification as spiritual practice.

Petition For

Blessing tattoo instruments, guiding hands during tattooing, sending the right customers, protection from infection, mistakes, or harm during the tattooing process, and guidance in sacred tattoo design.

Warnings

Do not request sacred or permanent tattoos without clear informed consent, offer thanks with food or a voluntary dedication, and be meticulous about sterility and aftercare when working under his guidance.

Additional Notes

Acat represents the spiritual significance of permanent body art and the responsibility that comes with marking the skin. He oversees both the technical skill and spiritual intention required for meaningful tattoo work.
