Ve is one of the three sons of Bor in Nordic cosmogony, brother of Odin and Vili. Together with his brothers he stands at the head of the Aesir order, the trio that slew the primordial giant Ymir and shaped the ordered cosmos from the substance of his body.

The Shaping of Humanity

In Snorri Sturluson's account of the making of the first humans, Odin, Vili, and Ve came upon two lifeless forms, either logs of ash and elm or the raw material of Ask and Embla, the first man and woman. Odin gave them breath and life-spirit, Vili gave them consciousness and movement, and Ve gave them speech, hearing, sight, and the outward appearances of a living being. Ve's gift is the sensory and communicative surface of the human person, the presence through which one being reaches another.

The Meaning of the Name

The name Ve is generally interpreted to mean sacred or holy place, the same root that appears in Old Norse words for a shrine or sanctuary. He is the third brother in the triad of consciousness, breath, and outward form, standing at the outward edge where the inner life of a being meets the world of others.

Domains

The sacred, the shrine and sanctuary, the sensory surface of the living being, and the shaping power that gives the first humans their speech and sight.
