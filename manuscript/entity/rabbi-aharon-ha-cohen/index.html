Life and History

Rabbi Aharon ha-Cohen (literally, Rabbi Aaron the Cohen or Rabbi Aaron the Priest) was a renowned miracle-working rabbi. He is buried in Demnate, Morocco, where he is venerated by both Jews and Muslims. His birth and death dates are unavailable.

Powers and Domains

Rabbi Aharon performs miraculous cures from beyond the grave. His specialties include healing snake bite, blindness, and other visual disorders. He is also renowned for helping women find husbands.

Should he ever be displeased with someone, Rabbi Aharon's traditional mode of punishment is to strike him or her insane.

Sacred Site

His grave in Demnate, Morocco, is traditionally visited on Fridays. Jewish and Muslim women wishing to marry spend overnight vigils at his grave.

The Mwalin El-Gomra

A group of saintly yeshiva students, known as the Mwalin El-Gomra (literally, Masters of the Gemara, which are Torah commentaries), are buried near Rabbi Aharon. They were his students in life and they continue to study together. They may be petitioned in conjunction with Rabbi Aharon.
