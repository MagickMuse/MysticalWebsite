Guardian of the Cauldron of Creation

Third sister in trinity with Cailleach Bhéara and Cailleach Bolus. Known as Black Veiled One (long black hair) and Hag of the Black Cauldron. Tended primordial Cauldron of Creation containing Water of Life.

The Seven Snakes and Rivers

Seven hair strands fell into cauldron, became seven snakes that overturned pot and gnawed Earth. Gaps filled with spilled Water of Life, creating Earth's great rivers. Snakes banished to Earth's center.

Compensation

Granted seven periods of youth/beauty. Married seven husbands (outlived all), fostered fifty children who founded Earth's nations.

Territory: Southwestern Ireland.
