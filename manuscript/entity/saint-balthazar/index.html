Saint Balthazar is renowned for healing epilepsy and seizure disorders.

Classification

Venerated across most Christian traditions.

Overview

One of the legendary Three Kings who journeyed to honor the newborn Christ, Balthazar is traditionally identified as the Nubian monarch who ruled Ethiopia. While myrrh was his signature offering, stories credit him with presenting numerous precious gifts.

Powers and Domains

Those suffering from epilepsy and other seizure disorders call upon Balthazar for relief. He holds special significance for people of African heritage, particularly in South American nations like Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay, where his feast day brings vibrant celebrations featuring music, processions, and community gatherings.

Iconography

Artists typically depict him as an African king wearing a crown (sometimes over a turban), either kneeling before the infant Jesus or mounted on a camel. While most commonly shown as a Black man, interpretations vary.

Heraldry

His traditional banner displays a dark-skinned figure in red garments bearing a lance with pennant against a golden background.

Feast Day

8 January
