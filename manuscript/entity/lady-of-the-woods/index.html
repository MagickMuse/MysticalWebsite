Lady of the Woods

Favored People
Forest dwellers, herbalists, those who honor wild places
Precautions
Respect her domain. The Lady of the Woods is fiercely protective and does not suffer those who exploit or desecrate wild places. Always ask before taking from the forest and leave an offering in return.
Culture
Northern European
Petition for
Connection to the forest, plant knowledge, woodland protection, wildcraft, nature magick
Gender
Female

Flowers, nuts, seeds, water poured at the roots of old trees, handmade offerings from natural materials
Type
Spirit

Waldmichen, Wood Nymph, Forest Mother

Magickal Properties

The Lady of the Woods is the feminine spirit of the wild forest, the green consciousness that breathes through every living thing beneath the canopy. She is the counterpart to the The Green Man: where he is the vigorous, surging pulse of vegetative growth, she is the deep intelligence of the woodland itself, the ancient knowing that lives in root systems, moss, and the whisper of leaves.

In Lower Saxony, Holle is known as the Waldmichen, the wood nymph who dwells in a grotto where unborn souls play. The Lady of the Woods carries this same energy: she is the forest as mother, protector, and keeper of secrets.

Ritual Uses

Work with the Lady of the Woods when seeking to deepen your relationship with wild places, for plant communication, herbal knowledge, and forest-based magick. She is especially present among old-growth trees and in places where the forest has been left undisturbed.

She responds to those who tend the wild places, who plant trees and protect woodland habitats, and who approach the forest with quiet reverence rather than noise and demand.

Lore

Related figures include The Green Man (spirit of wildlife and Spring), Herne (Earth Father and Lord of Animals), and Holle as Waldmichen. The Lady of the Woods represents the feminine face of wild nature: nurturing yet untamed, generous yet demanding of respect.
