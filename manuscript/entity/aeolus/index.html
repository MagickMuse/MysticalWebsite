Magickal Properties

Aeolus is Lord and Warden of the Winds, commanding and controlling them. His name means "variable" or "changeable" like the winds. He rules the Aeolian Islands near Sicily and has residences including a cavern on Vulcano where winds are kept until released, and a palace on Stromboli where he hosts eternal parties.

Ritual Uses

Aeolus' musical instrument is the Aeolian harp (Wind Harp), played by wind rather than human hands. Installing one enables communication with Aeolus through spontaneous sounds and responses to speech or events. Visit his palace in dreams or visualization and request assistance.

Offerings

Write desires on paper or tree leaves and let them blow away. He is wealthy and enjoys entertaining others, so genuine gifts of the heart may be sufficient. Once lavish offerings were given by dropping them down a sacred well.

Favored People

Sailors, those seeking change, musicians (especially Aeolian harp players), and anyone needing winds of change in their lives.

Petition For

Perfect sailing weather, winds of change, sweeping clean, assistance with change, resurrection and renewal, and nature restoration to life.

Warnings

Aeolus wants nothing to do with anyone not in good graces with the general Spirit Realm. He is generous and benevolent but requires good standing with other spirits, as Odysseus discovered.

Additional Notes

He was venerated as a deity whose breath (winds) restores nature to life. He has a wife and twelve children (six sons married to six daughters) who live with him in his palace. His control over winds makes him essential for both sailors and anyone seeking transformation or change.

Encyclopedia Source Notes (Roman tradition)

  • Roman god of storms and winds, derived from the Greek storm god Aeolos
  • Consort of Aurora
  • Father of six sons, the directional winds: Boreas (north), Corus (northwest), Aquilo (west), Notus (southwest), Eurus (east), and Zephyrus (south)
