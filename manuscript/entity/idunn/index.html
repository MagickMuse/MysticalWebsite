Apple

Idunn is the goddess of eternal youth, fertility, and abundance. She is the custodian of a grove of magickal golden apples. A diet of these apples preserves ideal youthful appearance and vitality. The Norse deities possess the ability to mature without aging because of Idunn's golden apples. Should they ever feel too ancient or creaky or if signs of age begin to creep up, Idunn provides them with apples which eliminate all negative aspects of aging.

Ritual Uses

Her role is also to guard the apples so that those who are not authorized to eat them never get a taste. Dwarves and Frost Giants notoriously lust for her fruit. Request her assistance to stay ever-youthful via petition and visualization. Work with apple magick and preservation of youth and vitality.

Warnings

Idunn's home is the Grove of Brunnaker. Her apples are highly coveted and she must remain vigilant in protecting them from unauthorized beings.

Idunn manifests as a beautiful, ever-youthful, cheerful woman. Her attribute is a wooden box in which she carries ripe apples so that she always has some on hand in case of an emergency. No matter how many apples Idunn removes, the box magically always contains the same number of apples. Her consort is Bragi, Lord of Poetry.
