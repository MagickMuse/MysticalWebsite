Magickal Properties

Papa Bois, French for "Father Forest," is the Caribbean guardian of the woods, also known as Maitre Bois (Master of the Forest) and Gran Bois. He is a renowned shape-shifter who lives in the heart of the forest with his lover, Trinidad's anaconda spirit Mama D'Lo. He is the guardian of forest animals and the custodian of trees.

Papa Bois roams the forests of Trinidad and Tobago. He carries a cow horn, which he sounds to warn animals of approaching hunters. He despises and does not tolerate wanton destruction of the forest. Nor does he tolerate wastefulness or cruelty, or killing merely for the sake of killing.

Shape-Shifting Forms

Papa Bois is a master shape-shifter who manifests in several forms:

  • As a stag: His animal form
  • As half-man/half-animal: Usually a stag, similar to European horned gods
  • As a tree: With a human face and voice
  • As an old man: Usually dressed in old, raggedy clothes, very hairy with leaves growing out of his beard. Although he may look like a man at first glance, a quick glance downward usually reveals at least one cloven hoof. (He allegedly doesn't like people staring at his feet and considers it rude.)

Although usually manifesting as an aged man, Papa Bois is quite muscular and exceptionally strong.

Ritual Uses

Work with Papa Bois for protection of forests and animals, for understanding ethical hunting practices, for guardianship of trees. Honor him by respecting the forest, protecting animals, and avoiding wanton destruction or wastefulness. Sometimes Papa Bois lures hunters deeper into the woods by assuming the shape of a deer; he then transforms back to human shape to scold them or issue stern warnings.

Offerings

Respect for the forest and all its inhabitants, protection of animals and trees, ethical hunting practices, offerings honoring wild nature and the balance between humans and the natural world.

Favored People

Those who respect the forest and its creatures, ethical hunters who take only what they need, protectors of animals and trees, witches (who ride in his Wild Hunt troop), those who understand the balance between human needs and nature's preservation.

Petition For

Forest protection, guardianship of animals, ethical hunting guidance, protection of trees, understanding the balance between humans and nature, warnings about environmental destruction.

Warnings

Papa Bois despises wanton destruction of the forest. He does not tolerate wastefulness, cruelty, or killing merely for the sake of killing. He has been known to evict, trick, harm, and even kill hunters and woodcutters who incur his wrath. He doesn't like people staring at his feet (where his cloven hooves may be visible). Approach with respect and humility.

Additional Notes

The Wild Hunt:

The Wild Hunt rides in the Caribbean too, and Papa Bois is its leader. Among those in his nocturnal troop are a band of reveling witches.

African Roots:

Although Papa Bois closely resembles European horned spirits, down to his associations with the Wild Hunt, scholars believe his roots lie in Africa. He is also among those spirits categorized as "Wild Men."

Modern Softening:

Like Santa Claus, Papa Bois has been softened in recent years. Papa Bois, impersonated by a man, serves as ringmaster of Trinidad's famous annual Carnival. He is also more polite than he used to be: older versions were wilder, more ragged and anarchistic, close in spirit to Faunus than is generally now portrayed.

Relationship:

He lives in the heart of the forest with his lover, Mama D'Lo, Trinidad's anaconda spirit.

Resemblance to Faunus:

Papa Bois shares many characteristics with the Italian horned god Faunus, including forest guardianship, protection of animals, shape-shifting abilities, and leadership of the Wild Hunt.
