Hutash is a powerful Chumash deity who lived alone on Limuw Island (now Santa Cruz Island) off the coast of California. Lonely because her husband the Sky Snake lived in the sky, she decided to create people in her own image, forming them from magick seeds. She took good care of her people, the Chumash, teaching them skills and providing for them. They flourished and multiplied until Limuw became crowded and noisy, so noisy that Hutash couldn't sleep.

Ritual Uses

Work with Hutash to understand creation myths and the sacred relationship between creator and creation. She demonstrates compassion in leadership and problem-solving. When her people's noise disturbed her, she found a solution that honored both her needs and theirs. Her transformation of falling people into dolphins shows her compassion and unwillingness to let those she created come to harm.

Warnings

Hutash decided some people must move to the California mainland when overcrowding became a problem. She created a very high, very long rainbow bridge from the tallest point on Limuw to a tall mountain near what is now Carpinteria. Some became disoriented and dizzy looking down and fell. She felt responsible and transformed those who fell into dolphins so they wouldn't drown.

An annual festival honoring Hutash is scheduled after the autumn harvest, lasting almost a week.
