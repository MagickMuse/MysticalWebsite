Archons

◇Culture
GnosticChristian
◇Gender
Varied
◇Type
Deity
≡Also called
Eksousiai (Authorities), Rulers

Primordial creator gods of Gnostic Christian tradition from the eastern Mediterranean, celestial rulers of the cosmos whose worship dates from roughly 100 AD to 400 AD and likely persisted later.

Cosmogony

Gnostic cosmogony argues that the God of Israel was not the original or sole creator but a product of older tyrannical forces eventually defeated in the conflict between light and dark. The Archons are the original creators of mortal humanity, but in the form they contrived, the human did not possess a soul.

Worship and Cult

Known period of worship runs from circa 100 AD through 400 AD, probably persisting later. Centers of cult were undefined cells within the area of early Christian influence. No surviving art references are attested.

Literary Sources

The principal texts are the Hypostasis of the Archons and the treatise on The Origin of the World, both preserved in the Nag Hammadi codices, written down during the third or fourth century AD. The material owes much to Greek philosophy and was banned under the censorship of the early Christian fathers.
