⚠️ Danko Dan Muso is venerated within Hausa Bori traditions. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or under proper cultural guidance.

Magickal Properties

Danko Dan Muso ('honored little son of Moses') is head of the Bori snake spirits and a freshwater snake who lives in rivers, streams, and wells. He is a shape-shifter who may appear in forms other than serpentine, and visitors to freshwater places are cautioned not to disturb him or intrude on his privacy.

Ritual Uses

Danko possesses mediums easily, rapidly, and abruptly, hence his epithet 'Sudden Visitor.' He has access to scads of wealth which he can distribute to human devotees as he pleases. Always be very polite when near freshwater, especially to strangers, as he may be testing you.

Offerings

Danko Dan Muso is always insatiably hungry for raw eggs and raw liver. These should be offered with appropriate respect and understanding of his serpentine nature.

Favored People

Those seeking wealth and prosperity, people needing healing from stomach and digestive ailments, practitioners working with snake spirits and freshwater magick.

Petition For

Access to wealth and prosperity, healing from stomach ailments and digestive disorders, treatment of female infertility, healing from blindness (though he can also cause it), understanding of freshwater snake wisdom.

Warnings

He is a healer but also possesses a large repertoire of ailments with which to afflict people. He causes stomach ailments, digestive disorders, female infertility, and blindness by spitting into victims' eyes (learned from his wife Kwakiya, the Black Headed Cobra). Always maintain politeness near freshwater.

Additional Notes

Associated with black and indigo colors. His sacred tree is African ebony (jackal berry tree). His wife is Kwakiya, the Black Headed Cobra, and they have a son, Damatsiri (a harmless green snake). He is identified with biblical Moses and Congolese snake spirit Simbi.
