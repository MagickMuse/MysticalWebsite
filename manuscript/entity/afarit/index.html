Magickal Properties

Afarit are primordial fire spirits who existed on Earth thousands of years before humans. They are exceptionally dangerous, powerful, smart, and malevolent with no such thing as a "friendly Afarit." They possess powers of bilocation (being in multiple places simultaneously) and can render themselves visible or invisible at will.

Ritual Uses

Afarit are not worked with but can be commanded by powerful sorcerers through specific Moroccan Jewish traditions involving oil lamps and couscousières after the Sabbath ends on Saturday night, though this is extremely dangerous. They are primarily banished rather than summoned.

Offerings

Not traditionally offered to, as they are always dangerous and malicious. Any interaction should be for commanding or banishing purposes only, not appeasement.

Favored People

None - they don't like people and are considered universally malevolent. They are dangerous to all humans without exception.

Petition For

Afarit are not petitioned for favors but may be commanded by powerful sorcerers for specific purposes, though this is extremely dangerous and not recommended.

Warnings

Extremely dangerous primordial fire spirits. They can possess people, causing psychosis and extraordinary physical strength. The spilled blood of murder victims creates portals for Afarit emergence. Hammer a virgin nail into the spot to prevent this. They are responsible for solar eclipses.

Additional Notes

In modern Egypt, "Afarit" has become a catchall term for any evil spirit, ghost, or apparition. They are most associated with Egypt, North Africa, and the Middle East but can travel where they will. They especially favor abandoned cemeteries, ancient Egyptian tombs, and the Great Pyramid.
