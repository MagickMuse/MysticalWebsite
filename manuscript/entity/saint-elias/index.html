Classification

Venerated by most branches of Christianity.

Overview

The prophet Elijah was elevated to Christian sainthood in the fourth century. He is considered the second forerunner of Christ. Icons of Elijah generally depict him ascending to Heaven in his fiery chariot. He resembles a Pagan sun god, and several Pagan deities are believed to hide beneath his mask.

Syncretism

  • In Greece, Helios lurks behind the mask of Saint Elias. Elias even sounds like Helios. Mountaintop shrines once associated with Helios were rededicated to Saint Elias.
  • In Russia, thunder spirit Perun lurks beneath the mask. His feast days July 20 and 30 are called the Thunder Days. Rain falling on those days has magical and therapeutic powers. Saint Elijah the Prophet Water protects against the Evil Eye, malevolent magic, and disease.

Powers and Domains

Saint Elijah has power over drought and rain. Because of his ascension to Heaven, he is patron saint of airplane pilots. It is traditional to bless cars and other vehicles on his feast day.

Iconography

An older, bearded man in prophet's robes, ascending in a fiery chariot drawn by fiery horses.
