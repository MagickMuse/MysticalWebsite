⚠️ Djab are spirits within Haitian Vodou tradition. Vodou is a closed initiatory practice and should only be approached by those within the originating culture or under proper cultural guidance from a houngan or mambo.

Magickal Properties

Djab are mercenary spirits, spirits for hire. Each Djab is an independent contractor who can be paid or bribed to do magical work. They are invoked in rituals and spells to perform magickal work on behalf of the spell-caster, typically in aggressive, less savory spells that other spirits will refuse. They are not necessarily diabolical but are exceptionally independent and make up their own rules.

Ritual Uses

Djab are invoked for aggressive, less savory spell-work that other spirits will refuse. If safer spirits would participate in such spells, then no one would invoke Djab. They serve as guardians if so requested, acting as aggressive bodyguards willing to search out and destroy enemies. They are sometimes invoked to serve justice when it will otherwise not be found.

Offerings

Exactly like an independent contractor, they must be paid per job and can be very aggressive about collections, to the point where they can be described as loan sharks. Payment terms must be negotiated and honored.

Favored People

Those needing aggressive magical work done, people seeking justice when it will otherwise not be found, those requiring bodyguard services against enemies.

Petition For

Aggressive spells, malevolent work other spirits refuse, serving justice, guardian protection, searching out and destroying enemies.

Warnings

They are potentially dangerous spirits willing to do malevolent work. Eventually, inevitably, the Djab will turn the tables if they are compelled rather than paid fairly. They can be very aggressive about collections.

Additional Notes

Some Djab are affiliated with a person or family who may or may not be able to exert control over them. Others are wild, autonomous spirits who play by no rules other than their own. Preventive and safety measures exist for dealing with troublesome Djab, but they must be performed by experienced practitioners.
