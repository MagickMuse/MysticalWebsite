Magickal Properties

There are two different versions of Amymone's story, or possibly two different myths about someone named Amymone who may or may not be the same. In both cases she is credited with receiving the sacred springs of Lerna as a gift from Poseidon. She may be the same Amymone who was the only one of Danaus' fifty daughters not to kill her husband.

Ritual Uses

According to a pre-Olympian myth, when Poseidon ruled the Argolis region with Demeter and Persephone, and rulership was given to Hera, Poseidon stormed off taking all the freshwater with him. When a satyr attempted to assault the beautiful Nymph Amymone, Poseidon intervened and rescued her. He wooed her with the promise of springs, and she succumbed to his charms. He revealed the hidden springs of Lerna in which she now resides.

Offerings

Fresh water libations directly at springs. Keep offerings minimal and remove anything that decays; avoid loud public feasting at sacred springs.

Favored People

Those seeking spring water blessings and people needing access to pure water sources. She particularly aids those who respect natural water sources.

Petition For

Access to pure water, spring blessings, and connection to sacred springs. She provides the gift of life-giving water.

Warnings

Naiad nymph credited with receiving sacred springs of Lerna as gift from Poseidon. May be same as Danaus' daughter who didn't kill her husband.

Additional Notes

An entrance to Hades may be found at Lerna, once the site of Demeter's Lernaean Mysteries. Romantic images of Poseidon and Amymone are a popular motif on ancient Greek vase paintings. She carries a pitcher as her attribute.
