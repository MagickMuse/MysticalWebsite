Saint Peregrine is renowned for healing cancer, especially cancers of the legs and feet.

Classification

Venerated as a saint in the Roman Catholic Church. Canonized in 1726 by Pope Benedict XIII.

Overview

Peregrine Laziosi (1260–1345) serves as the patron saint of cancer patients. Born in Forlì, Italy, he initially opposed the Catholic Church before a transformative encounter led him to join the Servite order. In his later years, he developed cancer in his leg and was scheduled for amputation. The night before surgery, he prayed before a crucifix and experienced a vision of Christ reaching down to touch his diseased limb. When doctors examined him the next morning, the cancer had vanished completely.

Life and Legend

As a young man, Peregrine actively participated in anti-papal politics in Forlì. When Philip Benizi, the prior general of the Servites, came to preach reconciliation, Peregrine struck him across the face. Philip responded by turning the other cheek and offering forgiveness. This act of grace so moved Peregrine that he converted and eventually joined the Servite order himself.

He spent years as a priest known for standing in prayer for extended periods, which may have contributed to varicose veins and later cancer in his leg. His miraculous healing after decades of devoted service became the foundation for his patronage of cancer patients.

Magical Properties

Saint Peregrine is invoked specifically for healing from cancer and other serious diseases, particularly those affecting the legs and feet. His intercession is sought when facing medical procedures or when conventional treatments have been exhausted. Devotees report miraculous remissions and unexpected recoveries attributed to his aid.

Ritual Uses

Pray the Saint Peregrine novena for nine consecutive days when facing cancer diagnosis or treatment. Offer San Pellegrino mineral water (sharing his name) at his shrine or altar. Light candles on Sundays, his sacred day, while petitioning for healing.

Favored People

Cancer patients and survivors, those with leg or foot ailments, people facing amputation, those in need of miraculous healing, medical professionals working in oncology.

Petition For

Healing from cancer, recovery from serious illness, relief from leg and foot pain, strength during medical treatment, miraculous intervention when hope seems lost.

Related Entities

Philip Benizi, Virgin Mary (as Our Lady of Sorrows)
