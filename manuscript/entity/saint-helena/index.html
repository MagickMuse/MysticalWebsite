Saint Helena is renowned for healing and removing crossed conditions and curses.

Classification

Venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity.

Life

Flavia Julia Helena Augusta (c. 250-18 August 330) was a bar girl and courtesan, possibly of British origin. She captivated Roman general Constantius, who fathered her son, Constantine. When a more advantageous marriage became available, Constantius repudiated Helena and exiled her from Rome.

  • Folk tales suggest Helena's father was Britain's legendary Old King Cole
  • Another legend says she was an innkeeper's daughter from Bithynia
  • Innuendo suggested she was a prostitute or sacred temple priestess
  • Before converting to Christianity, she may have been a devotee and priestess of Aphrodite

Constantine remained close to his mother, bringing her back to Rome when declared Caesar in 306. He minted coins bearing her image. She influenced her son, who would become the first Christian emperor of Rome.

Discovery of the True Cross

In 328, Helena claimed to have discovered the True Cross in a crypt below the recently destroyed Temple of Aphrodite in Jerusalem. She also found the nails from the Crucifixion, the Spear of Destiny, and the Black Madonna of Czestochowa (allegedly painted by Saint Luke on wood from a table made by Jesus). She founded basilicas in Bethlehem and on the Mount of Olives.

The Three Nails

Helena allegedly preserved one nail as a relic, threw one into the sea for the salvation of drowned sailors, and dedicated the third to the lovers of the world to preserve them from heartache. This is why Helena is among the saints most identified with romantic miracles, especially in Iberian and Latin American folk magic.

Removing Crossed Conditions

Because Saint Helena discovered the True Cross, she is associated with the magical removal of "crossed conditions," the American folk name for certain curses and blocked, stymied situations. Dedicate cross-shaped candles to Saint Helena, requesting she remove the heavy cross you bear, making it disappear as burning candles vanish into thin air.

Love Spells

Santa Elena is the subject of countless romantic spells, most intended to force someone to love the spell caster:

Invocation of Santa Elena

Most loving Mother of the Roman Emperor Constantine,

To the Mount of Olives you did go

With three nails of Our Lord Jesus Christ;

One you threw into the sea for the salvation of sailors

Another you nailed into a dedicated object

The third I drive into (target's) heart

So that he (or she) is unable to eat, sleep, rest or converse

Nor have one moment of rest until surrendering at my feet

Please Santa Elena, if you grant my desire,

I will be your most sincere loving devotee.

Nail Love Spell

  1. Dedicate a large white candle to Saint Helena and burn it
  2. While burning, personalize another candle shaped like a man or woman to resemble your target. Carve their name and birthday into the wax.
  3. Talk directly to the candle, telling it your desires
  4. Stick a nail through the candle where the heart should be
  5. Burn this candle too

Hidden Goddesses

Saint Helena's official hagiography emphasizes her pious nature. However, the roles she plays in folk religion indicate other spirits may lurk under the saint's guise:

  • Isis: Some love spells associated with Helena recall spells once dedicated to Isis, dating back to the first century CE
  • Aphrodite: Helena's discovery of the True Cross was made in the ruins of Aphrodite's temple. Long before crosses were associated with Christianity, they were emblems of this love goddess.
  • Helen of Troy: Once an important goddess of love and fertility venerated throughout Greece. Like Helena, she has associations with love and crosses. Helen was crucified on Rhodes according to one legend.
