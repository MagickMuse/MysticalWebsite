Arnemetia is renowned for healing chronic conditions and restoring vitality through her sacred therapeutic waters at Buxton.

Magickal Properties

Celtic-Roman spring goddess of Buxton (Aquae Arnemetiae), Lady of the Sacred Grove and healing waters.

Ritual Uses

Petition for relief of wasting illness and renewal through baths and drinking cures; honor the grove and spring.

Offerings

Clean water libations, simple flowers, coins cast to the spring, and vows to protect sacred groves.

Favored People

Those seeking convalescence, water-keepers, and pilgrims to thermal springs.

Petition For

Healing of chronic conditions, purification, and restored vitality.

Warnings

Keep the waters clean; remove perishables promptly; respect the grove as sanctuary.

Additional Notes

Her name contains nemeton (sacred grove). Romans named the spa Aquae Arnemetiae.

Encyclopedia Source Notes

Water goddess. Romano-Celtic (British). A deity known only from inscriptions.
