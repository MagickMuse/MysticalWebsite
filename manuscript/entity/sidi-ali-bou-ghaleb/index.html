Venerated as a Sufi saint, Sidi Ali Bou Ghaleb is often considered the saint of last resort, sought by those for whom conventional medical solutions have failed.

Life and Legend

Sidi Ali Bou Ghaleb, a native of Andalusia, taught in Fez in the early 12th century. He now has a shrine near Fez where he performs healings.

Magical Properties

The terminally ill and those with seemingly incurable conditions travel to his grave shrine. It is traditional to spend Tuesday and Wednesday nights in his mausoleum, though some stay until their cure is complete. You know the cure is accomplished when you dream of the saint.

Sacred Sites

His shrine near Fez has separate areas reserved for men and women.
