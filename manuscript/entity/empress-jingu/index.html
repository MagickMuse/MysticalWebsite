Empress Jingu

◇Favored People
She is associated with protection of pregnancy and children and is widely venerated by women; devotees also petition her for victory and good fortune.
◇Precautions
Treat historical identifications with the shaman-queen Himiko with cultural sensitivity.
◇Culture
Japanese
◇Petition for
Petition Empress Jingu for fertility, safe childbirth, protection of children, victory in love, business, or conflict, and for abundance and good luck; she also sponsors and communicates with spirit mediums.
◇Gender
Female
◇Offerings
Mirrors, dolls, and other shiny or finely made objects; replicas of female breasts are traditional when petitioning for assistance with pregnancy or childbirth.
◇Type
DeityKami

Jingū, Okinaga-tarashi-hime
