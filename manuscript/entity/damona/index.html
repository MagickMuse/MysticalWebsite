Magickal Properties

Damona ('The Divine Cow') is a Celtic goddess of healing venerated in Burgundy and other regions. She presided over sacred springs and healing shrines that featured incubation of healing dreams. Her veneration wasn't dependent on relationships with other spirits, she was honored both independently and alongside consorts like Borvo and Apollo Moritasgus.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Damona for healing work, especially at natural springs or water sources. She specializes in healing dreams and visions received through sacred sleep or meditation. Work with her for fertility magick and purification rituals involving sacred waters.

Offerings

Burgundy wine (from her region), fresh milk (honoring her cow aspect), grain offerings especially wheat, healing herbs gathered from sacred sites, fresh spring water.

Favored People

Those seeking healing from illness or injury, people struggling with fertility issues, pilgrims visiting sacred springs and healing sites, dream workers and those seeking sacred visions.

Petition For

Healing from physical or spiritual ailments, fertility for people, animals, and land, sacred dreams and healing visions, purification and cleansing, guidance received through divine dreams.

Warnings

Approach her sacred springs with reverence and proper ritual preparation. Healing dreams may reveal difficult truths that require courage to face and act upon.

Additional Notes

Venerated at multiple healing shrines including Bourbonne-les-Bains and alongside Apollo Moritasgus at Alesia. Her carved image shows grain stalks and a snake curling around her hand, emphasizing her connection to both agricultural abundance and healing wisdom.
