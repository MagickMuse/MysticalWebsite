Overview

Triton is the son of Amphitrite and Poseidon. He lives at the bottom of the sea in his mother's golden palace.

Magickal Properties

Triton is a wise, oracular spirit who commands violent storms. He has a reputation as a violent lover toward women and young men, and is considered a very erotic spirit; Hekate is counted among his many lovers. He often accompanies his father, Poseidon.

Manifestation

Triton is a merman with long hair, and also a shape-shifter.

Attributes

A conch shell, used as a horn.
