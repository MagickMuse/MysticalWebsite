World Surveyor Man

Culture
Siberian
Petition for
Protection healing shamanic power knowledge
Gender
Male
Type
Deity
Also called
The Gander Prince, Gander Chief, Golden Prince
Other Entities2
KaltesSwan Goddesses

Classification

Spirit hero of Siberia's Finno-Ugric tribes.

Origin

World Surveyor Man is the seventh and youngest son of Sky Father Numi-Tarem and the goddess Kaltes, born after his mother fell to Earth from Heaven. His hands and feet are made from gold; he is the original golden goose.

Magickal Properties

His primary function is to see all, know all, and protect people from whatever endangers them. When the sun goes down, he traverses the sky on a gleaming white horse (some versions say the horse itself is gold, or that he rides a sleigh drawn by swans, geese, ducks, or horses). The horse is as magickal as he is: if he turns its nose toward someone, that person is healed.

World Surveyor Man gives shamans their power. In some cultures this is understood abstractly; in others, he gives power directly to the shaman while they are in trance.

Manifestation

He wears a golden hat and a garment adorned with seven goose wings, and he can adopt the form of any sacred creature.

Attributes

Amanita muscaria (fly agaric mushroom), an iron falcon, a double-edged knife, and a saddle with seven bells or tassels.

Correspondences

Consort: Swan Goddess. Creatures: geese, swans, falcons, waterfowl, hares, mice, pike. Time: appeals are made at night. Planet: the sun. Tree: larch. Number: seven.

Ritual Uses

He lives in a seven-sided house with seven doors inside a sacred forest. Once, people expected a visit from him three times a year at home, or else sent him many gifts. Traditionally, to summon him, four ritual metal plates, each bearing an image of the sun, are placed on the ground where his horse's four hooves would stand; alternatively, four silver cups placed the same way will also call him. Archaeological evidence of silver trays associated with such rituals, dating to the first millennium, has been found in areas associated with Ugric tribes.

Additional Notes

He is now sometimes identified with Saint George, Saint Nicholas, or occasionally Jesus Christ.
