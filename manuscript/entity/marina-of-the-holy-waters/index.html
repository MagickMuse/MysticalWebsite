Roman Catholic saint associated with healing wells and sacred springs.

Life and Legend

Saint Marina of the Holy Waters is the sister of Saint Liberata and one of the Nine Holy Twins. She hails from Galicia, a region of Spain with deep Celtic roots where healing wells have ancient significance.

According to legend, when Marina was beheaded, a spring of water gushed forth from where her severed head touched the earth. Whether her name is coincidental or reflects this miracle is debated. Celtic healing wells are frequently associated with severed heads, suggesting a possible pre-Christian origin for her veneration.

The name Marina of the Holy Waters may also refer to Margaret of Antioch, who is closely identified with healing springs elsewhere in Europe.
