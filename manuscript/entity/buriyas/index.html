Buriyas is a tutelary war god of the Kassite peoples, a highland population whose homeland lay in the Zagros region east of the Mesopotamian plain, in the country that later corresponded to western Iran. He was the patron of Kassite arms and of the ruling house through which Kassite power was projected onto the wider ancient Near East during the second millennium BC.

Domains

Warfare, tutelary protection of the army in the field, the fortunes of the Kassite ruling house, and the sovereignty won and defended by force of arms.

Iconography and Attributes

Buriyas belongs to the small circle of specifically Kassite gods whose names survive in the record of the second millennium BC. He is a male martial power of the highland-and-plain frontier, invoked in the context of the campaign and of dynastic legitimacy rather than of civic or agricultural life. His name is preserved in Kassite personal onomastics, above all in the theophoric royal names of the Kassite dynasty, marking those kings as claimed by, and claiming, his patronage.

Ritual

Buriyas was invoked as the guarantor of Kassite victory in war, and the armies that carried the god's name into battle did so as a matter of dynastic and religious identification with him. Offerings and dedications to Buriyas were bound to the successful campaign and to the stability of Kassite rule over the lands they had won.

Lore

In the sixteenth century BC the Kassite peoples descended from the Zagros highlands into the plain of Mesopotamia and, in the wake of the collapse of the Old Babylonian order, established themselves as the ruling power of Babylonia. The Kassite dynasty they founded held Babylonia for several centuries, one of the longest single dynastic tenures known in Mesopotamian history, and Buriyas travelled with them as the god of the conquering army and of the throne. Through Kassite Babylonia, Buriyas took his place beside the older Mesopotamian pantheon of Marduk, Enlil, Ishtar, and Shamash, an outsider god established at the centre of the ancient world's oldest urban civilisation.
