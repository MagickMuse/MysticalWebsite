The Kallikantzari are malevolent Greek goblins or demons who emerge from the underworld during the twelve days between Christmas and Epiphany (December 25 to January 6). During the rest of the year, they labor underground sawing at the World Tree's roots, attempting to topple it and collapse the world. Just before success, Christmas arrives and they emerge to wreak havoc on humans.

They are blind or nearly blind, grotesque creatures who cause mischief and harm. Their annual emergence means the World Tree heals during their absence, and they must begin sawing anew each year.

Ritual Uses

Do not invoke Kallikantzari. Instead, use protective rituals during the twelve days of Christmas. Keep fires burning to prevent them from entering through chimneys. Hang iron or steel items near doors. Place a colander on the doorstep, as they become obsessed with counting the holes until sunrise banishes them.

Warnings

These are dangerous spirits who harm humans and attempt to destroy the world. Focus entirely on protection during their season. Leave pancakes or sausages as distractions. Keep holy water, salt, and protective herbs on hand.
