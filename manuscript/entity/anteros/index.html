Magickal Properties

Anteros embodies the transformative power of requited love and the divine balance that comes when affection is mutually shared. As the spirit of "love returned," he carries the archetypal energy of mature, responsible love that transcends the ephemeral attractions governed by his brother Eros.

Ritual Uses

Relationship Balancing: Invoke his energy to create mutual attraction where interest flows in only one direction.

Love Justice Workings: Call upon him to address situations where genuine love is being exploited or ignored.

Emotional Maturity Magick: Use his energy to move beyond obsessive or one-sided attractions toward relationships based on genuine compatibility.

Vengeance Against False Lovers: Petition him to bring appropriate consequences to those who deliberately mislead others about romantic intentions.

Offerings

Paired items symbolizing mutual exchange, gifts that represent mature love rather than passionate infatuation, and butterflies (his distinguishing feature).

Favored People

Those seeking genuine reciprocal love rather than mere conquest, people recovering from unrequited love, and couples working to deepen mutual affection.

Petition For

Mutual love and affection, healing from unrequited love, recognition of true compatibility, and justice against those who exploit romantic feelings.

Warnings

Avoid revenge petitions; they rebound and complicate matters.

Additional Notes

Victorian sculptors particularly favored Anteros because they perceived him as representing mature, responsible love in contrast to Eros' frivolous emotions. His butterfly wings distinguish him from Eros, symbolizing transformation.
