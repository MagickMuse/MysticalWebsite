Magickal Properties

The Seven Hathors are Egyptian birth-spirit fairies, the earliest clear manifestation of the birth-fate tradition that would spread throughout Europe and beyond. They are aspects, avatars, or daughters of the goddess Hathor, appearing at births to pronounce a child's destiny.

Sacred Number: Seven

There are seven Hathors, each contributing to the complete pronouncement of destiny. The number seven carries sacred significance in Egyptian tradition (seven sacred oils, seven spirits, seven gates of the underworld).

Relationship to Hathor

The Seven Hathors are manifestations of Hathor, goddess of:

  • Love and beauty
  • Music and dance
  • Joy and celebration
  • Motherhood and fertility
  • Fate and destiny

They carry Hathor's graceful, joyful energy while specializing in fate-determination at birth.

Ritual Uses

Invoke The Seven Hathors for newborn blessings, connecting to the most ancient tradition of birth-spirit fairies. Prepare offerings that honor both their collective nature (seven of everything) and their connection to Hathor's domains.

Traditional Offering: Seven Red Ribbons

The classic offering is seven red ribbons, one for each Hathor. Red symbolizes:

  • Life force and blood
  • Protection and power
  • Hathor's vitality
  • The sacred feminine

Tie or drape these ribbons in the birthing space or present them on an altar.

Additional Offerings

  • Milk (sacred to Hathor)
  • Wine (particularly red)
  • Music and dance (Hathor's arts)
  • Beautiful objects (mirrors, jewelry, flowers)
  • Honey and sweet things
  • Incense (especially myrrh)

Historical Significance

The Seven Hathors represent the earliest clear documentation of birth-spirit fairies determining destiny. Egyptian evidence predates European fairy traditions by millennia, suggesting Egypt as the source or early expression of this widespread practice.

Etymology and Connections

Fairy → Old French fée → Latin fatua (female seer) & fatum (fate/destiny)

While the word "fairy" comes through European languages, The Seven Hathors demonstrate that the concept existed in ancient Egypt long before, influencing:

  • Greek Moirae
  • Roman Parcae
  • European birth fairies (Fatit, Oosood, etc.)
  • Sleeping Beauty fairy godmothers

Sleeping Beauty Connection

The fairy tale of Sleeping Beauty describes actual spiritual rituals: offering tables laid for fairies who bestow a baby's fate at birth celebrations. The Seven Hathors are the ancient Egyptian origin of this tradition, with European variations following millennia later.

Manifestation

The Seven Hathors often appear as beautiful women, sometimes wearing the horned sun-disk crown associated with Hathor. They may carry sistrums (sacred rattles) or mirrors. Some traditions show them as seven cows (Hathor's sacred animal form).

Warnings

The birth period is sacred across all cultures. The Seven Hathors must be properly honored with their traditional seven red ribbons and offerings befitting Hathor's domains. Their pronouncements shape the entire life path.
