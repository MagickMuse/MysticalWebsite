The Fairy Queen

Oonagh the Fairy Queen has golden hair so long it reaches the ground. Oonagh is a goddess of love and protectress of young animals. Oonagh may also have influence over the realm of death.

Mistress of Illusion and Glamour

She is Mistress of Illusion and Glamour: her silver gossamer dress appears to shimmer with diamonds, but it's really sparkling dew. Oonagh's blessings are invoked to find true love and to experience romantic happiness.

Manifestation

Oonagh is described as so beautiful that no one (at least no mortal) can look at her without being awed and amazed.
