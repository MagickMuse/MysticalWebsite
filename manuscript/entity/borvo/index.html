Magickal Properties

  • Celtic god of healing thermal springs and medicinal waters
  • Name derives from Celtic root meaning "to boil" or "bubble," referring to hot springs
  • Presides over therapeutic waters throughout Celtic lands, particularly Gaul (France)
  • Associated with purification, healing, and restoration through sacred waters

Ritual Uses

  • Visit thermal springs and healing waters for therapeutic treatments
  • Make offerings at sacred spring sites before seeking healing
  • Use spring water for purification rituals and cleansing ceremonies
  • Honor him at sites where hot springs emerge from earth

Offerings

Spring water, healing herbs, votive offerings at thermal springs

Favored People

Those seeking healing, visitors to thermal springs, people with chronic ailments

Petition For

Physical healing, purification, therapeutic treatments, recovery from illness

Warnings

Respect sacred springs and thermal waters; do not pollute healing sites. Approach with reverence for ancient healing traditions.

Additional Notes

  • Name appears in various forms: Borvo, Bormo, Bormanus across different Celtic regions
  • Many thermal spring sites throughout France and Celtic territories were sacred to him
  • Romans identified him with their healing god Apollo, showing cross-cultural recognition
  • Springs dedicated to Borvo often became Christian healing sites in later periods
  • Represents ancient understanding of water's therapeutic and purifying properties
