Magickal Properties

Adrano is Sicily's sacred guardian who, together with his sons the Palici, protects Sicily and Sicilians. He is the presiding spirit of Mount Etna with ultimate control over volcanic forces. The Cirneco dogs have lived in Sicily for over three thousand years and are closely identified with Adrano.

Ritual Uses

Adrano's temple on Mount Etna was guarded by a pack of one thousand Cirnechi dogs. These beautiful, intelligent hunting hounds greeted pilgrims and escorted them to his shrine, but attacked those who arrived with bad intent, especially thieves. Use visualization rituals involving traveling to Mount Etna and being greeted by the dogs.

Offerings

Anything on behalf of the Cirnechi dogs and Sicily in general. The breed almost disappeared until restoration efforts began in the early twentieth century, making conservation efforts appropriate offerings.

Favored People

Sicilians, those with connections to Sicily or Mount Etna, pilgrims visiting volcanic sites, and people working to preserve ancient dog breeds.

Petition For

Protection of Sicily and Sicilians, guardianship, volcanic safety, safe passage on Mount Etna, protection of property, and restoration of peace in Sicily.

Warnings

Do not invade his Mount Etna shrine or come with ill intent because his Cirnechi dogs attack thieves. Offer on behalf of the dogs and remove perishables promptly.

Additional Notes

Coins from the third century BCE show Adrano on one side and a Cirneco on the other. The cult image in his shrine depicted him as a warrior holding a lance. The modern town of Adrano at the foot of Etna is named in his honor. Whether he is indigenous Sicilian or arrived with the Phoenicians is debated.
