Owner of the Bush

Bori Spirit Hierarchy

Mai Dawa is the head of the eighth house of Bori spirits, the House of Archers and Hunters. Mai Dawa, courageous master hunter, is afraid of nothing. He's a spirit hunter, not a regular hunter: his prey includes demons.

Mai Dawa's primary wife is Barhaza, Inna's younger sister. Details of his parentage are hazy.

Afflictions and Healing

The afflictions that Mai Dawa causes and controls include unexplained bleeding. His illness begins as a slight cold but accelerates. The victim develops a head or bronchial cough but eventually coughs up blood. Because it's a progressive illness, Mai Dawa leaves time for the victim to consult a shamanic healer who can prescribe treatment.

Mai Dawa also possesses poison arrows for which no antidote exists.

Magickal Properties

As a spirit hunter whose prey includes demons, Mai Dawa represents courage, fearlessness, and the ability to confront dangerous spiritual entities. His progressive illness pattern demonstrates the Bori understanding that spirits leave time for healing and negotiation, unlike his poison arrows, which represent swift, irreversible spiritual justice.

Manifestation

Mai Dawa crouches as he walks, like a hunter stalking prey, hence his epithet, the Short Man. He's not that short when he stands up straight.
