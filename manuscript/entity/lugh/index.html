Origin and Identity

Lugh, Master of All Skills and The Shining One, Lord of Craftsmanship, Light, Victory and War, is a master builder, harper, poet, warrior, sorcerer, metalworker, cupbearer and physician. It's hard to envision anything at which Lugh does not excel. His name means "raven" (as Lugos in continental Europe) and "Bright One of the Skillful Hand" (as Lleu Llaw Gyffes in Wales).

Pan-Celtic Significance

Lugh was venerated throughout the ancient Celtic world. Modern scholars perceive him as especially significant because his veneration indicates the existence of pan-Celtic spiritual traditions. At least fourteen European cities are named for Lugh including Laon, Leyden, Loudon and Lyon. Lyon's old name was Lugdunum, meaning "Lugh's Fort." That city is believed to have been his cult center. Its coins bore the images of ravens, which may be a reference to Lugh. Carlisle in England, the former Lugubalium, is also named in Lugh's honor. Some theorize that Lugh's name is reflected in an older name for Paris: Lutetia.

The Romans identified Lugh with Hermes. Many European churches dedicated to Michael the Archangel are believed to have been built over sites once dedicated to Lugh. Post-Christianity many of Lugh's sacred functions were reassigned to saints like Patrick and Luke.

Mythological Arrival

Lugh apparently traveled westward through Europe. Irish and Welsh myths describe his first appearance in their pantheon. He is greeted with resistance from women in Wales.

In Ireland, his entry to the Tuatha de Danaan is recounted in a memorable exchange. He presented himself at the door of their hall and was asked what skill he had to offer. When he named himself a carpenter the Tuatha replied they already had one; when he offered his services as a smith, the answer was the same. One by one he listed every talent he possessed, from warrior and harpist to historian, poet, and sorcerer, and each time was told there was no vacancy. Only when he asked whether any single member of the Tuatha held all of those skills simultaneously was there silence, and he was admitted.

His first public act in Ireland is to join battle with the Tuatha Dé Danaan (his father's people) against the Fomorian, his mother's people. Lugh chooses allegiance with the paternal line; the myth may be interpreted as indicating the beginnings of patriarchy in Ireland.

Manifestation

Lugh is described as shining, handsome, charming and witty. He has a silver tongue to match his skillful hands. He carries a magical spear and harp.

Sacred Attributes

Plant: Red corn cockles

Consorts: Lugh has different consorts in different locations but he was frequently linked to Arianrhode.

Magickal Properties

Lugh embodies mastery of all skills and crafts. He represents the perfection of human achievement across multiple domains—from artistry to warfare, from healing to metalwork. His silver tongue and skillful hands make him the ultimate patron of excellence.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Lugh for skill, craft, and fair leadership. He is particularly powerful for workings related to mastery of multiple skills, victory in battle or competition, inspiration for creative work, and protection of artisans and craftspeople.

Lughnasa: The Festival of Lugh

Date: 1 August (the festival of August 1)

Lughnasa means "the marriage of Lugh." Lugh the sun and the Earth Mother renew their wedding vows annually during the full moon in August and invite all to gather and revel with them. Lughnasa celebrates the consummation of their sacred relationship.

Once upon a time, Lughnasa was a four week festival: the last two weeks of July and the first two weeks of August, roughly corresponding to when the sun is in Leo, the astrological sign that belongs to the sun and epitomizes its power. In modern Irish Gaelic, the month of August is Lunasa. However, the modern Wiccan sabbat of Lughnasa is almost always devoted solely to the eve of July 31st leading into Lughnasa Day on August 1st.

Spirit Allies

Lugh shared the city of Lyon with Kybele and Paris with Isis. In battle, Lugh used his own weapons but also those belonging to Manannan.
