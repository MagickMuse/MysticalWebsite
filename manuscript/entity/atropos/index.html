Atropos is a goddess of fate in the pre-Homeric Greek tradition. According to Hesiod she is one of the daughters of Zeus and Themis, and she belongs to an ancient trio of Moirai alongside Lachesis and Klotho.

Domain

She is responsible for the final part of a mortal life, the unturning inevitability of death. She is depicted holding a pair of scales.

Legacy

The plant Atropa belladonna, known as deadly nightshade, derives its name from her.

Atropa Mandragora

Mandrake's older botanical classification, Atropa Mandragora, carries Atropos's name into the plant kingdom alongside deadly nightshade. Both plants are deadly when consumed, a fitting tribute to the Fate who decides which mortals live and which die. The Greeks also called mandrake Circeium, after the witch goddess Circe whose poison path it shares.
