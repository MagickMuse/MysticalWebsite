Hydra is the daughter of Echidna and Typhon and Hera's beloved foster child. She lived at the sevenfold source of the River Amymone and haunts the neighboring swamps of Lerna. It was Hydra's job to guard the gates to Hades accessed through the waters of Lerna. Hydra has the torso of a canine with nine or more heads resembling snakes. One head is immortal. Her blood is poison.

Ritual Uses

Hydra's guardian powers can still be accessed for protection and gate-keeping work. With her body in the sky as a constellation and her immortal head secretly hidden in Earth, she survives and can be petitioned for boundary protection and threshold guardianship.

Lamb Offerings

Images of lambs offered on an altar may suffice to contact, propitiate, and reward her. Once upon a time, lambs intended for Hydra were cast into the waters of Lerna by those seeking to pass through the gates to Hades.

Warnings

Do not attempt to hunt, bind, or summon a Hydra because its regenerative power is dangerous. Use extreme protective and banishing rites if working near its mythology. The gates at Lerna were not considered metaphoric but an actual literal opening to the Underworld.

Heracles' second labor was to kill Hydra. When Heracles knocked off one of Hydra's heads with his club, two more appeared in its place. Hera placed Hydra in the sky as a constellation. Her blood was so poisonous that Heracles dipped his arrows in it so that any wounds they caused would be fatal.
