Buddhalocana, whose name means the eye of the Buddha, is a female buddha of the Buddhist tradition and particularly of the Shingon school of Japanese esoteric Buddhism. She is the personification of the buddhas' seeing, the visionary faculty by which enlightened awareness perceives all things as they are, and she is worshipped as the presence of that awakened sight in feminine form.

Under her shorter name Locana she is also known as the sakti of Aksobhya, the imperturbable dhyanibuddha of the east, and in that role she stands as the feminine counterpart through whom his awakened perception is embodied within the eastern quadrant of the Vajrayana mandala.

Domains

The awakened eye and the visionary faculty of the buddhas, the feminine embodiment of Aksobhya's perception in the eastern mandala, and the transmission of enlightened sight through Shingon and wider Vajrayana practice.

Alternative Name

Buddhalocana is also called Locana, and the two names denote the same female buddha.

Relation to Aksobhya

As the sakti of Aksobhya, Buddhalocana completes the eastern pole of the mandala of the five dhyanibuddhas. Where Aksobhya is the imperturbable presence of awakened consciousness, Buddhalocana is the seeing through which that consciousness beholds the phenomena of the world.
