Classification

Eastern Orthodox saint.

Life and Legend

The parents of Theophano Martiniake (died 10 November 897) were childless for many years. They attributed the eventual conception of their daughter to the intercession of Mary Theotokos, an aspect of the Blessed Mother whose name means "Mary who brings forth God." Theophano was named in honor of Theotokos.

A well-educated and beautiful young woman from the aristocracy, she was wed to Leo, heir to the Byzantine throne. Emperor Basil arranged the marriage, though the circumstances were complicated by rumors that Leo was actually the son of the previous emperor Michael rather than Basil. Leo's mother had been Michael's mistress while married to Basil, who later assassinated Michael before taking the throne.

Relations between Basil and Leo were deeply strained. When Leo was young, Basil once threatened to blind him. After the marriage, responding to rumors that Leo intended to assassinate him, Basil had Leo and Theophano imprisoned for three years.

When Basil died on 29 August 886 in what was officially described as a hunting accident (though Basil himself claimed on his deathbed it was an assassination plot), Leo succeeded to the throne as Emperor Leo VI and Theophano was crowned empress.

Spiritual Life

Theophano dedicated herself completely to spirituality and the Church, spending her time fasting, praying, and reciting psalms. She gave alms generously to the poor and built churches and monasteries, including the Monastery of Saint Anastasia. She also gave birth to a daughter named Eudokia.

Meanwhile, Leo fell in love with Zoë Zaoutzaina, who became his mistress. In 893, Theophano retired to a convent. Whether this retirement was voluntary remains unclear, though she was genuinely a deeply committed Christian. Zoë immediately assumed the functions of empress, although Leo did not marry her until after Theophano's death.

Theophano died in the convent on 10 November 897 at approximately twenty-seven years of age. Leo hailed her as a saint immediately and built a church over her grave. The Eastern Orthodox Church eventually concurred and she was glorified (canonized).

Domains

She is invoked especially for healing, with many miraculous healings attributed to her relics.

Iconography

A favorite subject of icons, Theophano is usually portrayed as a richly dressed young woman. She may wear a crown.

Attributes

Cross.

Sacred Sites

Her relics in the Church of Saint George in Istanbul are associated with many miraculous healings.

Related Entities

Saint Anastasia, Saint George.
