In Witchcraft

Nimue (also known as Viviane, Vivian, or the Lady of the Lake, and sometimes identified with Brisen) belongs among the enchantresses and fays of medieval legend whose symbolism underlies the witchcraft chalice current. She and her Arthurian companions Morgan le Fay and Melusine partake of the power of the Lady of Delight: the governing feminine force of love, enchantment, and the subtler unseen powers. This current is invoked through the chalice in traditional love workings. The symbolism is rooted more in the elven-folk and fay enchantresses of medieval legend than in purely classical sources, though Persephone and Euridice share the same underlying pattern.
