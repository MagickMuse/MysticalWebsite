Aitvaras

Magickal Properties

Aiatar is an ancient Finno-Ugric disease spirit banished by Christianity, hence she lingers in desolate forest and tundra areas. She's sometimes called "the devil in the forest" or "the devil's mother." After Christianity, Ukko (the Old Man, leader of the Finnish Pagan pantheon) was identified with the devil, and it's possible Aiatar is his mother.

Ritual Uses

Aiatar is traditionally avoided rather than approached. She may be petitioned by those seeking to understand forest spirits or disease magick, though this is extremely dangerous and not recommended.

Offerings

None - avoid this spirit entirely. Those seeking protection from disease should make offerings to benevolent healing spirits instead of attempting to appease Aiatar.

Favored People

None - malevolent disease spirit who is antagonistic to humans and causes illness through proximity alone.

Petition For

None - causes illness and should be avoided. For protection from disease, petition benevolent healing spirits rather than attempting to work with Aiatar.

Warnings

Malevolent disease spirit in Estonian/Finnish forests. Proximity causes illness - no direct contact is necessary. Ancient Finno-Ugric spirit banished by Christianity. Called 'devil in forest' or 'devil's mother'. Her method of disease transmission resembles a skunk's defensive emissions - a warning to stay away.

Additional Notes

She may appear as a dragon, snake, or woman suckling snakes. She lingers in desolate areas where people are unlikely to bother her. Her transmission of disease through proximity serves as her way of saying "keep away!" She represents the dangerous aspects of wild forest spirits that were demonized during Christianization.
