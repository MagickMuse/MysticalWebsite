Aparajita, whose name means unconquered, is a goddess of the Hindu Puranic tradition. She is a form of Durga.

Attendant Animal

Her attendant animal is a lion.

Attributes

Her attributes are the arrow, shield, snake, and sword.

Cross-Tradition Note

In the Buddhist Mahayana tradition, the god Bhutadamara may be depicted reclining upon Aparajita in this Hindu form, a ritual gesture through which the Buddhist deity establishes his dominion over the earlier Hindu power. The identification travels one direction only: the Hindu Aparajita retains her own independent standing as a form of Durga.
