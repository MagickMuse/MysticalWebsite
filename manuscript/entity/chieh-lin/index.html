Magickal Properties

Chieh Lin is a Chinese spirit of romance and marriage who determines marital unions by deciding who will marry whom. He records these decisions in his very large book, then connects the feet of predestined couples with invisible red silk thread. This thread ideally leads couples toward each other and happy matrimony.

Ritual Uses

Appeal to Chieh Lin when the thread of destiny seems tangled or nonexistent in your romantic life. He can help untangle relationship difficulties and guide people toward their destined partners. His intervention is sought when marriages seem blocked or relationships face unusual obstacles.

Offerings

Red ribbons or cord representing the silk thread of connection, honeyed wine tied with red ribbons, items that honor marriage ceremonies and romantic unions.

Favored People

Those seeking their destined marital partner, couples experiencing relationship difficulties that seem fated, people whose romantic connections appear blocked by unseen forces.

Petition For

Finding one's destined marriage partner, untangling relationship problems caused by crossed threads of fate, guidance in marital decisions, resolution of romantic obstacles that seem beyond ordinary causes.

Warnings

Respect the cosmic nature of his matchmaking, he works with destiny rather than mere desire. Some connections may need time to properly align according to celestial timing.

Additional Notes

Chinese folklore places a woman (Chang'O) in the moon, but Chieh Lin represents the old man also residing there. During marriage ceremonies, bride and bridegroom pledge their troth from two goblets of honeyed wine tied together with red ribbons or cord, honoring his red thread connections.
