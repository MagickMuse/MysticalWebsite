In Witchcraft

Eros and his Roman form Amor are invoked in traditional witchcraft as representatives of the classical symbolism of love that underlies chalice workings. The chalice in this tradition is dedicated to the subtler, feminine powers of love and enchantment, invoked through a symbolism akin to classical divinities such as Venus and Amor. They stand at the lighter, receptive end of the love-working spectrum, distinct from the more aggressive martial energies.
