⚕️A Ceremony to Assist with Health

Panacea is renowned for healing all ailments. As the goddess of universal remedy, she represents the hope that any illness can be cured.

Magickal Properties

Panacea is the Greek goddess of universal remedy, daughter of Asklepios and sister of Hygieia. Her name literally means "all-healing" and is the source of the word panacea for a cure-all remedy. She represents the ideal of complete healing and the hope that medicine can cure any ailment.

Ritual Uses

Work with Panacea for general healing support, especially when seeking a comprehensive remedy for complex health issues. Her energy complements practical medical treatment. Invoke her alongside her father Asklepios and sister Hygieia for the full spectrum of healing, from prevention to cure.

Offerings

Healing offerings made with responsible intent, herbal remedies, and symbols of wholeness and restoration.

Favored People

Healers, those seeking healing support alongside medical treatment, herbalists, and pharmacists.

Petition For

Healing, cure-all remedies, health restoration (alongside proper medical care), and comprehensive wellness.

Warnings

Invoke Panacea for healing support but do not substitute ritual for medical treatment. Always seek professional care when needed.

Related Entities

Asklepios (father), Hygieia (sister), Apollo (grandfather).
