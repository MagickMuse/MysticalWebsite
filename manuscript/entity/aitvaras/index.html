Magickal Properties

The Aitvaras traditionally serves as a family guardian, appearing as a golden rooster, large fire-breathing dragon, small grass snake with a long tail that emits light as it flies through the air, huge snake with a flaming head, or snake formed entirely of fire. These powerful spirits have dominion over wealth and relationships, bringing happiness, good fortune, and abundance to those they love.

Ritual Uses

Aitvaras live in forests or celestial realms but may be persuaded to join a family at home. An Aitvaras attached to a family can be sent to steal from other people, traditionally raiding barns or granaries but theoretically capable of retrieving anything. Work with them for family protection and abundance.

Offerings

Serve cooked meals that haven't been tasted by anyone else, even during the cooking process. Present offerings with respect for their role as family guardians.

Favored People

Families seeking guardianship and abundance, those desiring wealth and good fortune, people working with Baltic spiritual traditions, and practitioners seeking household guardian spirits.

Petition For

Protection, wealth, good fortune, abundance, happiness, family prosperity, and assistance in acquiring needed resources.

Warnings

Can cause fires and disasters if angry or offended. Does not get along with Perkunas. Was demonized post-Christianity. Handle with respect and avoid offending them to prevent destructive consequences.

Additional Notes

Aitvaras are associated with the wild pear tree. As spirits that usually manifest as flaming snakes, they were predictably demonized following Christianization of the Baltic region. They often work quietly and anonymously to bring prosperity to families they favor, but their methods may involve taking from others.
