Saint Gemma Galgani is renowned for healing spinal disorders, tuberculosis, and back ailments.

Classification

Roman Catholic saint, mystic, and stigmatic.

Life and History

Gemma Galgani (1878–1903) was born in Camigliano, near Lucca, Italy. Her mother died of tuberculosis when Gemma was seven, and her father died when she was eighteen, leaving her and her siblings in poverty. Despite aspirations to become a Passionist nun, she was rejected due to her poor health.

Illness and Suffering

Gemma suffered from spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease), which caused severe curvature of the spine and paralysis. Doctors declared her condition incurable. She also experienced meningitis, deafness, and hair loss. In 1899, she was miraculously healed—attributed to the intercession of Saint Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother—though she would continue to suffer other ailments.

The Stigmata

On June 8, 1899, Gemma received the stigmata—wounds corresponding to those of Christ's crucifixion. These wounds would appear every Thursday evening and heal by Saturday, leaving no trace. Her stigmata were examined by doctors and clergy who could find no natural explanation. She also experienced ecstatic visions of Jesus, Mary, and her guardian angel, with whom she claimed to converse regularly.

Mystical Experiences

Beyond the stigmata, Gemma experienced:

  • Crown of thorns marks appearing on her forehead
  • Marks from Christ's scourging on her back
  • Demonic attacks and temptations
  • Bilocation
  • The gift of reading souls

Death

Gemma died of tuberculosis on Holy Saturday, April 11, 1903, at age twenty-five. Her final words were "Jesus, I commend my spirit into your hands." She was canonized in 1940.

Powers and Domains

Gemma heals spinal disorders, back ailments, and tuberculosis. She is invoked by those suffering chronic illness and those seeking mystical union with Christ's suffering.

Iconography

Depicted as a young woman in simple dress, often shown with the stigmata visible on her hands. May be portrayed in ecstasy, gazing heavenward.

Attributes

Stigmata, crucifix, lilies.

Sacred Sites

  • The Sanctuary of Saint Gemma Galgani in Lucca, Italy
  • Her tomb in the Passionist monastery church in Lucca

Feast Day

April 11 (formerly May 16)

Related Entities

Gabriel of the Sorrowful Mother, Saint Pio, Therese Neumann, Saint Catherine of Siena
