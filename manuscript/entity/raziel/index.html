📿Book of Raziel and Psalm 126 Household Ward📖Book of Raziel Fire Warding

Raziel is an angel of Jewish mystical tradition, whose name is often translated as "Secret of God" or "Angel of Mysteries." Where Michael stood at the gates of Eden with a flaming sword to bar re-entry, Raziel proved more compassionate: as Adam passed through those gates, Raziel gave him the first book ever written, a compendium of magical knowledge. Raziel is said to hear everything that happens in the world, functioning as a kind of omniscient ear rather than the more usual all-seeing eye.

That original Book of Raziel was said to be engraved on sapphire and now rests on the ocean floor. Copies were made over the centuries, though no two versions were necessarily identical. The edition in circulation today was first published in Amsterdam in 1701. Dense with astrology, amulets, and arcana, it was sold as an amulet in itself, with the expectation that most buyers would not be able to read it.

The mere presence of the Book of Raziel in a home is said to grant special protection, particularly against fire. The Fire Angels, aware of the book's presence, are said to keep watch over the household.

Beyond fire protection, the book's presence is credited with warding off misfortune, robbery, and evil spirits, and with serving as a charm to raise wise and intelligent sons. Perhaps its most significant role, however, was providing the diagram for the most famous anti-Lilith protective childbirth amulets, cementing its long association with safeguarding mothers and infants during labor.
