Gabriel is venerated as a saint by most branches of Christianity. He is one of three archangels, along with Michael and Raphael, who are simultaneously saints and archangels, despite saints being born human by definition. Gabriel and Michael are the only angels mentioned in the Old Testament.

History of Veneration

Veneration of Gabriel predates Christianity. Gabriel was considered a deity in ancient Mesopotamia and is recognized as an archangel by Judaism and Islam as well as Christianity.

Résumé

  • One of only two angels identified by name in the Bible (Michael is the other)
  • Explains Daniel's visions in Book of Daniel 8:15-26
  • Possibly the angel sent to destroy Sodom and Gomorrah
  • Angel of the Annunciation, advising of births of Jesus and John the Baptist
  • May have been the angel in the Garden of Gethsemane
  • According to Islam, dictated the Koran to Muhammad
  • Kabbalah associates him with the sefirah of Gevurah (Power)
  • Credited with introducing coffee to people
  • Will blow the horn that signals Judgment Day

Role as Intercessor

According to traditional Jewish angelology, Gabriel stands to the left of the Divine Chariot and serves the Creator directly. In the Gospel of Luke 1:19, he states: "I am Gabriel, who stands before God." He is perfectly placed to whisper your needs into God's ear.

Powers and Domains

Gabriel has a reputation for generosity. In Ethiopian Christian tradition, he is the saint most likely to perform miracles. He presides over life and death. Invoked to heal infertility and aid conception, he also ranks among the Angels of Death. To have gentle Gabriel serve as escort to the after-life is considered an honor. He presides over a palace in the Sixth Heaven. He also has dominion over dreams.

Manifestation

Described as androgynous. There is debate as to whether Gabriel is male or female.

Feast Days

  • Western Church: 29 September (shared with Michael and Raphael); original feast 24 March
  • Eastern Church: 26 March, 24 July, 8 November
  • Ethiopian Church: 28 December (also 19th of each month)

Sacred Sites

  • Church of Saint Gabriel in Kulubi, Ethiopia (two annual pilgrimages)
  • Basilica of the Archangels Michael and Gabriel in Satu Mare, Romania
  • Archangel Gabriel Cathedral in Goritsky, Russia
