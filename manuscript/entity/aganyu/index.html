⚠️ Aganyu is venerated within Yoruba and Santería (Lukumi) traditions. These are closed initiatory practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

Aganyu is the primordial spirit of the forces of Earth, especially violent, powerful, cataclysmic upheavals. When tectonic plates move, that's Aganyu. He is the orisha of volcanoes and lava is his breath. He is violently passionate like a volcano. His emotions build, bottle up, then explode.

Ritual Uses

Aganyu is generally a solitary spirit who dwells in the wilderness. He often communicates via the orisha Shango due to his overwhelming power being too much for most people to handle directly. Shango serves as a mediator; an altar may be built for both orishas.

Offerings

Nine fruits, nine crackers with red palm oil, nine plantains drizzled with red palm oil, nine handkerchiefs or silk pocket squares each in a different solid color.

Favored People

Children (he has a soft spot for them and may be very kind and protective), porters, stevedores, those working with earth forces, and anyone involved in travel and transportation.

Petition For

Protection from aneurisms, high blood pressure, strokes, traffic accidents, safe travel and transportation, and protection during earth-moving or construction work.

Warnings

Violently passionate orisha like a volcano. His emotions build, bottle up, then explode. His power is too overwhelming for most people to deal with directly. He demonstrates displeasure through aneurisms, high blood pressure, sudden strokes, and traffic accidents.

Additional Notes

He is syncretized to Saint Christopher, whose feast day he shares (July 25th). He is the patron of travel and transportation. His sacred colors are red, his sacred numbers are 9 and 16, and his sacred day is Wednesday. He may be Shango's father, son, or brother depending on the tradition.
