Shrine to Loki

🦊
Loki · Norse Trickster · Lord of Chaos · Father of Monsters

The shape-shifter at the heart of Asgard. Bound beneath the earth with a serpent dripping venom above him, Sigyn at his side holding a bowl. He returns at Ragnarök.

🟢
The trickster of the crossroads of fate. Father of Fenrir the wolf, Jörmungandr the world-serpent, and Hel queen of the dead. Companion to Thor, plotter against Balder, ally and adversary of the Aesir by turns.
Be precise and explicit in bargains. Never leave terms vague. Trickster bargains rebound on the careless and reward the clever.

Drawn from Loki

🕯️
Leave Your Devotion

Speak your bargain plainly, with every term defined. Light a candle and write the bargain on paper before you petition. Loki rewards the clever and the precise.

Petition For

Trickery, cunning, inspiration, chaos, unexpected solutions. Disruption when systems have grown rigid. Wit when force fails.

Favored People

Spies, moles, trouble-makers, secret agents, double agents, plotters. Those who live by quick thinking and shifting masks.

Sacred Animals

Wolves: Fenrir, his son. The vargr, the outlaw, the wild teacher.

Snakes: Jörmungandr, his son who circles the world. Shape-shifting and the shedding of skins.

Spiders: Weavers of fate, holders of paradox, readers of every thread in the web.

Kin and Companions

Children: Fenrir, Jörmungandr, Hel, Sleipnir.

Wife: Sigyn, who holds the bowl that catches the serpent's venom.

Counterpart: Thor, his frequent traveling companion and adversary.

Touched by his hand: Balder, Thokk, Ran.

Sacred Days

Lughnasadh, Norse Hlafmaest, the first harvest. The season when the bright god dies and the cunning god still walks free.

Sacred Star

Sirius, the brightest star in Canis Major. The wolf-star for the father of wolves.

Sacred Rune

Ansuz, the messenger rune. It signals unexpected messages and intuitive hits. Ansuz advises alertness and careful attention to all forms of communication.

Words of Power

Loki · Trickster · Sky-Walker · Father of Monsters · The Bound One · Shape-shifter

Notes and Cautions

Norse trickster deity. Be precise and explicit in bargains, never leave terms vague. Do not invoke to harm innocents, trickster bargains rebound. He punishes laziness in language and rewards wit. He is not safe, he is not tame, but he is honest in his way.
