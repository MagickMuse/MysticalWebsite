Magickal Properties

Pastoral culture bearer who teaches beekeeping, cheesemaking, olive oil pressing, and healing arts learned from Nymphs. Protective patron of herds, hives, and artisans.

Ritual Uses

Petition for healthy hives, abundant honey, sturdy herds, skill in dairy and oil crafts, and nature‑aligned prosperity.

Offerings

Honey and comb, fine olive oil, Greek cheese, and spring water. Sponsor beekeepers or plant for pollinators.

Favored People

Beekeepers, cheesemakers, olive growers, pastoralists, and those who steward land with care.

Petition For

Abundant honey flow, hive health, protection of flocks, artisanal mastery, and guidance in healing and divination.

Warnings

Honor craft lineages and apprenticeships; do not steal secrets.

Additional Notes

Son of Apollo and Cyrene; raised by Nymphs, Chiron, and the Muses. Venerated in Euboia and Sicily.

Encyclopedia Source Notes

God of herdsmen. Greek. The consort of Autonoe. Of ancient origin, worshipped by peasants as a guardian of herds and beekeepers. The cult continued for many centuries at Kyrene in Libya.
