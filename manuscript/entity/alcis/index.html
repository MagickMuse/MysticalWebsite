The Alcis are twin deities (brothers) of Germanic and possibly Icelandic Nordic tradition. Status unknown per source. They are known only as sons of the sky gods.

Iconography

From Germanic times we have a La Tene urn with pictures of paired men on horseback and linked by a wooden beam.

Worship

Tacitus describes the worship of twin gods by the Naharvali tribe, their priests dressed in effeminate costume, compared by some sources to the Phrygian deity Attis. They may have been worshipped in forest sanctuaries along the northern coast of Europe.
