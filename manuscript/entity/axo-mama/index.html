⚠️ Axo Mama is venerated within Quechua, Aymara, and Andean Indigenous traditions. These are closed practices and should only be approached by those within the originating cultures or with explicit cultural permission.

Magickal Properties

Axo Mama is the Peruvian goddess of potatoes, one of the most important deities in the Andean agricultural pantheon. Potatoes were the sacred staple crop of the Andes, and Axo Mama presided over their fertility and successful cultivation.

Ritual Uses

Petition Axo Mama for agricultural abundance, especially for root vegetable crops. She is called upon during planting and harvest seasons to ensure a successful potato yield. Farmers working with any root vegetables may invoke her blessings.

Offerings

Potatoes (especially unusual or beautiful specimens), coca leaves, chicha, and food offerings placed directly into the earth. Traditional offerings honor the potato as sacred.

Favored People

Farmers, potato growers, those working with root vegetables, and Andean peoples who honor traditional agricultural practices.

Petition For

Fertility, agricultural abundance, potato harvest success, root vegetable cultivation, connection to earth's nourishment.

Warnings

Approach with respect for traditional Andean agricultural practices. Honor the potato as a sacred plant.

Additional Notes

As a manifestation of the Divine Feminine, Axo Mama embodies fertility, creativity, and the earth's nourishing abundance. She represents the goddess energy that sustains life through the cultivation of sacred crops.

Encyclopedia Source Notes

Axo-Mama is classified as the goddess of potato crops in the South American Indian tradition of Peru. A model of this minor deity was made out of parts of the potato plant as a harvest fetish and kept for a year before being burned in a ritual to ensure a good potato harvest.
