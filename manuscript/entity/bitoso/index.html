⚠️ Bitoso belongs to Romani spiritual lore. Romani traditions are largely closed; protective and expulsion practices should be approached with cultural humility and proper guidance.

Magickal Properties

  • Romani disease demon, little multiheaded worm who causes headaches, stomachaches, eliminates appetite
  • His children cause colic, cramps, toothache, tinnitus, unexplained ringing in ears
  • Created from union of demonic Loçolico spirits when Schilalyi needed spouse
  • Born from father urinating on garlic, eating it, then having sex with wife Ana

Ritual Uses

  • Not invoked but expelled through protective rituals and fumigation
  • Burn turmeric in copper bowls or throw pinch into fire to repel Bitoso and offspring
  • Use traditional Romani protective methods and healing practices

Offerings

None, treat as target for expulsion, not veneration

None, this is a disease demon to be avoided and expelled

Petition For

Protection from disease, healing from afflictions caused by Bitoso's brood, expulsion of parasitic spirits

Warnings

Disease demon created from demonic union. His children target vulnerable populations especially infants and young children. Use protective measures consistently.

Additional Notes

  • Spouse: Schilalyi (disease demon sister)
  • Father: King of Loçolico spirits
  • Mother: Ana
  • Brother: Melalo (who arranged the marriage)
  • Repellent: Scent of burning turmeric
  • Targets: Causes loss of appetite, digestive issues, head pain, hearing problems
