Magickal Properties

  • Black Lady of the Sacred Mountain who grants protection, justice, and provision to devotees
  • Patron of pilgrims and the poor; fierce enforcer of vows

Ritual Uses

  • Petition on the mountain and at her shrines for protection, redress of wrongs, and household provision
  • Make clear vows and return in thanks

Offerings

  • Bars of gold or silver, fruits, and vegetables presented with dignity

Favored People

Pilgrims, poor villagers, and those facing injustice

Petition For

Protection, justice, mountain‑road safety, and material help in hardship

Warnings

Fulfill all vows. She may punish theft of offerings or broken promises.

Additional Notes

  • Regional devotion spans Vietnamese and Cambodian communities; local rites and calendars apply
