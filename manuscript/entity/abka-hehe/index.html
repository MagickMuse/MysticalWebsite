Magickal Properties

Abka Hehe is the primordial Manchurian goddess who forms a creation triad with Banamu Hehe (Earth Mother) and Ulden Hehe (Light Mother). She is the source of all existence. Everything comes from her, including the other goddesses of the triad. She epitomizes all that is good and serves as guardian of the entire universe, an immortal, invincible spirit who defeats and banishes evil.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Abka Hehe during times of global crisis or personal disasters when the world seems to be falling apart. Create rituals that honor the triad of goddesses she leads. Use her battle-ready nature in protective workings against evil forces.

Offerings

Traditional Manchu offerings including wine, beans, grains, cooked fish and meats (especially pork). Present offerings that honor her role as creator and protector.

Favored People

Shamans and shamanesses (she sent the Eagle Goddess to raise the first shamaness), those seeking relief when the world is falling apart, people striving for harmony and protection from evil.

Petition For

Disaster relief, banishing evil, raising and training shamans, universal protection, help during global crises, defeating malevolent forces, and guidance for those called to shamanic work.

Warnings

Remember that she was officially replaced by Abka Enduri (the Man of Heaven) during the Qing Dynasty to align with Confucian male dominance, but she remains immortal and battle-ready in the shadows.

Additional Notes

She wears a battle skirt made of willow leaves. In one creation story variant, when she battled a demon, leaves from her skirt fell to Earth and transformed into the first people. Her sacred bird is the magpie. Traditional Manchu culture distinguished between female and male shamans with distinct powers.
