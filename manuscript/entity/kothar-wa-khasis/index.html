Kothar wa Khasis is the Canaanite and Phoenician god of craftsmanship, metalworking, magick, and invention. His name means "Skillful and Wise" or "Deft and Clever." He is the divine craftsman who forges magical weapons for the gods and builds palaces for them. Kothar wa Khasis represents the highest level of skill in all crafts and the magickal knowledge embedded in the act of creation.

He forged the magical clubs used by Baal to defeat Yam and built Baal's palace. Kothar wa Khasis embodies the union of practical skill with magickal knowledge; understanding that true craftsmanship is a spiritual practice.

Ritual Uses

Invoke Kothar wa Khasis when beginning new craft projects, when seeking to master a skill, or when you need divine inspiration for invention and building. He supports all who work with their hands and create with skill and intention. Petition him before major construction or building projects.

Warnings

Respect artisan protocol. Do not steal secret crafts or claim inventions without proper apprenticeship and learning. Kothar is the master craftsman who values the integrity of the craft tradition. Offer thanks to smiths and artisans. Do not claim mastery without putting in the work to earn it.
