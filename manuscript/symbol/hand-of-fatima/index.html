Overview

The Hand of Fatima, also known as the Khamsa, is named for Fatima Zahra, daughter of the Prophet Mohammed. It is a very old symbol, used widely as a talisman, and across the Middle East it appears everywhere, in homes, shops, taxis, and hotels.

Form and Protection

The hand is not shaped like an ordinary human hand. It has two balanced thumbs and no little finger. The eye set in its palm wards off the evil eye, making the Hand of Fatima a double emblem of protection, since the raised open palm is itself a forbidding gesture.

Shared Meaning

Khamsa means five, and the symbol carries relevance for both Muslims and Jews. In recent years peace activists have adopted it as a reminder of the many beliefs the two faiths hold in common.
