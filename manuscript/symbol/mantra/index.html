Overview

A mantra is a sacred phrase or series of sounds that, repeated over and over, is believed to set up a corresponding spiritual vibration. It is akin to a prayer, though it tends to belong to the Dharmic faiths, and whoever chants it becomes unified with the greater cosmos. "Aum," "Om Mani Padme Hum," and "Nam Myoho Renge Kyo" are all mantras. The visual counterpart of the mantra is the Yantra.

A mantra does not work in translation; the syllables of the original phrase must be pronounced correctly. Like a spell, the right mantra is believed to accomplish anything, provided the one chanting it is in the proper frame of mind, mentally and spiritually prepared. Because some mantras are so powerful, they are taught only with great care, and only to those judged worthy of using them.
