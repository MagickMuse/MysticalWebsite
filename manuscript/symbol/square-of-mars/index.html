What Is the Square of Mars?

The Magick Square of Mars is a 5x5 kamea holding the numbers 1 through 25, arranged so that every row, column, and diagonal sums to 65, and the grand total of all numbers in the square sums to 325. Like all planetary squares, it is a Cabalistic instrument adopted into Western magick as a focus for planetary power.

The five-by-five grid reflects the number of Mars (5), and its use in working concentrates and channels martial force: wrath, aggression, conflict, defense, courage, and the power to strike an enemy.

Making the Square

Exorcise a sheet of paper or a piece of iron with salt water and Martial Incense smoke before inscribing the numbers. Iron is the traditional material for Mars; paper is an acceptable substitute. Inscribe the numbers with deliberate intent, concentrating on the martial purpose for which the square is being made.

Once the square is inscribed, charge it by passing it through Martial Incense smoke while repeating a personal invocation or chain of Mars names.

Using the Square

Lay the square before you on your working surface. Place both hands upon it and fix the purpose firmly in mind. Trace the numbers 1 through 25 in sequence with a fingertip, repeating a chosen charm or invocation at each number. This tracing activates the current of martial force resident in the square.

Wrap the finished, charged square in red cloth or cord. It may be carried as a talisman, buried near the target's home or workplace, concealed in a location connected to the working, or kept on an altar dedicated to Mars.

Storage

Wrap the finished square in red cloth or keep it in a dedicated box with other martial tools.
