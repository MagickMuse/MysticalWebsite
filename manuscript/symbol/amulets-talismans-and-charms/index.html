Amulets, Talismans, and Charms

Although worn on the body as jewelry, the amulet is different from ordinary jewelry in that it holds a magickal significance peculiar to its owner or wearer. Generally, the powers of the amulet fall into two categories: to bring luck or to avert evil.

Amulet vs. Talisman

The talisman is effectively the same thing as an amulet, although its name derives from an Arabic word meaning "magick picture." A charm made specifically and inscribed with the names of the spirits, the Seal of Solomon, and other mystical symbols is more likely to be referred to as a talisman.

The Word "Charm"

Both amulets and talismans are referred to as charms. The origin of this word has the same root as the Latin word for "song," indicating the link between a magickal sound and a magickal intention.

Common Amulet Symbols

Significant symbols for use as amulets include:

  • Birthstones or other gems according to their magickal properties
  • Astrological signs
  • Specific symbols such as the Hand of Fatima or the cornus
  • Religious symbols specific to the beliefs of the wearer, such as the cross, the star, words, names, and numbers
