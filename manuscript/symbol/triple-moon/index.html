The High Priestess

Symbolic Meaning

The triple moon represents the three phases of the Goddess: Maiden, Mother, and Crone. This sacred symbol embodies the waxing, full, and waning aspects of lunar energy and divine feminine power.

Magickal Properties

  • Goddess trinity - honors the three faces of the divine feminine
  • Lunar cycles - represents the eternal dance of moon phases
  • Life stages - symbolizes birth, life, and death, or beginning, fulfillment, and completion
  • Comprehensive protection - builds, maintains, and banishes as needed

Associated Concepts

  • The triple goddess (Maiden, Mother, Crone)
  • Waxing, full, and waning moon phases
  • Divine feminine in all her aspects
  • The cycle of becoming, being, and release

Garden Magick

Hemispheric Considerations

From the Northern hemisphere, the waxing, full, and waning moon appear as )O(. However, in the Southern Hemisphere, the reverse is true (O). Even the constellations we are so familiar with could be reversed from our previous vantage point.

Utilize this pattern to create waxing, full, and waning moon motifs as one larger garden or three smaller gardens.

The Waxing Garden

Fill with herbs and plants to increase your personal, familial, or home's protection. This section is about growth, building energy, and drawing things toward you.

The Full Moon Garden

The full moon is the union of the sun and the moon, as the sun shines on the moon, the moon reflects its light onto the earth. That's why lunar eclipses happen only during full moons: the earth is passing between the moon and sun, casting a shadow on the moon.

The full moon is a powerful time for magick because of the alignment of the moon and sun, so it's a great time to make your wishes known to the universe.

Fill your full moon section with multipurpose plants:

  • Angelica
  • Rosemary
  • Clover
  • Anise

They're great multipurpose plants that can help the multitude of your magickal dreams come to fruition.

The Waning Garden

The waning part of your triple moon is to banish anything that can negatively impact your safety. You can banish:

  • Creepy people
  • Unwanted attention from romantic sources
  • Professional attention you don't want
  • Even creditors

Suggested plants for banishing:

  • Silver dollar Eucalyptus (won't get as large as a eucalyptus tree if you have limited space)
  • Sunflower
  • Lilac
  • Peony
  • Rue
  • Any of the mints (seed catalogs have an abundance of varieties: peppermint, apple mint, chocolate mint)
