Overview

Freemasonry draws heavily from the Temple of Solomon as a symbolic model. Lodges commonly place two columns at either side of the main door, reflecting the temple's pillars.

Jachin and Boaz

The right column is Jachin, associated with stability. The left column is Boaz, associated with strength. The two columns also carry a male and female polarity. Jachin is often painted red, tied to solar and active qualities, while Boaz is often painted white, tied to lunar and receptive qualities.

Indicating Grade

In some rites, the columns indicate grade. Apprentices stand before the red column, Masons before the white column, and Master Masons in the central space between them.
