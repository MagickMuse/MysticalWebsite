Overview

The Kundalini Serpent is the symbol of the vast reserve of physical and spiritual energy that lies half-dormant at the base of the spine. Its name comes from a Sanskrit word meaning spiral or coil, and the serpent is shown wound into three circles with its tail in its mouth, much like the ouroboros.

The Rising of the Serpent

Kundalini energy is female. When it is awakened through disciplined practice such as yoga and meditation, she uncoils and spirals up the spine through the chakras toward the crown of the head. There she meets the male energy descending from above, a union that symbolizes the consciousness of the unity of the Universe.
