One of the four cardinal points. Associated with the element Air, the season of Spring, and the archangel Raphael. Within the cycle of the day, the east corresponds to dawn.

Druidic Tradition

Druidic lore personifies the east as the "hawk of the dawn." In many Druid ceremonies the circle is opened to the elements and the directions, and the spirits of each quarter are honored in the hope that they will bless the proceedings.

Feng Shui Correspondences

In Feng Shui the east is masculine and stands for the first son. Its planet is Jupiter and its element is wood. Its further associations include thunder, spring, morning, the galloping horse, the flying dragon, young men, beans, bamboo shoots, excitement and stimulation, and movement and roads.
