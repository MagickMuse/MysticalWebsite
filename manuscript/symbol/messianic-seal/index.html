Messianic Seal

Notes/Warnings
⚠️ The insignia of the Messianic Christian movement, which some Jewish communities view as appropriative or aimed at conversion. Treat with cultural sensitivity. Composed of three stacked symbols: the Vesica Piscis (base), the Star of David (middle), and the Menorah (top).

Overview

The Messianic Seal is a composite emblem that stacks three symbols into a single vertical device: the Vesica Piscis at the base, the Star of David at the center, and the Menorah rising from the top. It serves as the insignia of the Messianic Christian movement, a group that has folded certain Jewish practices into its rituals.

History

The symbol is believed to have been used by very early followers of Christ and is found carved onto stone dated to the first century AD. Its modern revival signals a deliberate blending of Jewish and Christian imagery.

⚠️ Cultural Note

The movement that uses this seal has at times adopted Jewish practice in an effort to persuade Jewish people to convert to Christianity. The emblem carries that history, so handle it with care and context.
