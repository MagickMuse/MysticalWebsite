Horoscope Wheel Spread

Also known as widdershins in traditional Craft terminology.

In the Northern Hemisphere, counterclockwise means to the left, moving opposite to the apparent motion of the sun. Counterclockwise movement is used for workings of decrease, banishing, unbinding, and releasing energy.

Note: This meaning is reversed in the Southern Hemisphere, where the sun appears to move counterclockwise across the sky.
