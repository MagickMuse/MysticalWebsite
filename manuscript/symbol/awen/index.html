The Awen is related to many new Druid movements. The actual word, which is Welsh, means "inspiration" or "essence." Related to the Breton symbol called the Triban, and with a nod to the Trishul (the trident held by the Hindu deity Shiva), the Awen is composed of three convergent rays, like paths, leading to a high point, with a dot or three dots similar to the bindhu.

Threefold Meanings

Each ray carries various meanings, which are equally significant and come in sets of three:

  • Past, present, and future
  • Love, knowledge, and truth
  • Male and female energy and the balance between the two
  • The three pillars of wisdom

Another interpretation holds that the three rays represent the three fundamental letters of the name of God: I, O, and U, which when pronounced contribute to the actual word "Awen." It can be intoned in much the same way as the Aum of Eastern tradition, used as a meditative focus.

The Order of Bards, Ovates, and Druids

The Order of Bards, Ovates, and Druids place the three lines and three dots within three concentric rings, further amplifying the meaning of the symbol as well as placing it within protective magickal circles.
