Symbolic Meaning

The octagon represents action, perpetuity, and longevity. This eight-sided shape embodies continuous motion, activation, and the infinite cycle of energy.

Magickal Properties

  • Energy activation - stirs stopped or stagnant energy
  • Perpetual motion - keeps protective measures active indefinitely
  • Longevity - extends the duration of magickal workings
  • Infinity connection - an eight on its side (∞) symbolizes endless cycles

Associated Concepts

  • The eights in tarot (action, movement, mastery)
  • The infinity symbol (lemniscate)
  • Crossroads and transitions
  • Perpetual energy and momentum

Garden Magick

While some things with eight sides are usually painted red and meant to stop you at important crossroads, in magick, the tarot, and numerology, the eight is about action, perpetuity, and longevity.

How It Works:

  • In your protection magick, an octagonal garden can activate stopped or stagnant energy
  • If you feel like you've been stuck in a rut, an eight-sided garden bed can stir up the energies and keep them going
  • Your protective measures will get an extra boost with the right plants

Recommended: Climbing and Creeping Plants

Use vine plants and creeping flowers to enhance the perpetual motion energy:

  • Don Juan climbing roses
  • Clematis
  • Morning Glory on a trellis

The climbing, creeping nature of these plants mirrors the continuous energy flow that the octagon represents.

Adding the Element of Air

To let the element of air keep your protection energies going, consider adding a wind feature:

  • A small windmill
  • Colorful flags or prayer flags
  • Wind chimes
  • Pinwheels

These moving elements ensure the energy never becomes stagnant and your protections remain active.
