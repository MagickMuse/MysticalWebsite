Orange stones have some of the fire of red but are gentler in their effects. Projective, they have often been seen as symbols of the Sun. They are ideal for use in protective rituals and those designed to promote illumination.

These stones are related to personal power. Wearing one will enhance your ability to tap into and direct this energy during magickal rites.

These are excellent stones to wear for those people with low self-esteem, for they expand your awareness of self-worth.

Orange stones are also thought to be luck-attracting, and as symbols of success, they are worn during spells to assure a positive outcome.

Symbolic Overview

Orange has two aspects that we see time and time again, pivoting between the material and spiritual worlds, which is not surprising given that the color itself is a balance between red and yellow. As such, it represents the second chakra, the first being red, and the third, yellow.

Orange is a vibrant, cheerful color that definitely lifts the spirits. Hindu places of worship are indicated by an orange flag or banner, which is replaced once a year in a colorful and effusive ceremony.

A recent Unicef survey showed Dutch children to be the happiest in Europe; given that happiness is one of the symbolic associations with the color orange, could there be a link?

Balance of Energy and Thought

Orange is exciting, assertive, joyful, and persistent. It balances the life force of red with the lightness and goal-setting qualities of yellow. This fusion of physical energy and mental power creates potential for both wisdom and achievement. Orange encourages new ideas and fresh perspectives. It is thoughtful and considerate while remaining energetic. Orange symbolizes warmth, expansion, prosperity, harvest, tolerance, and love for all life.

Orange is virile and useful for those who need more force and drive.

Positive and Negative Qualities

Orange is usually positive in expression. However, when manifesting negatively, it can become sensual, self-indulgent, lazy, and lacking in energy.

Cultural and Historical Significance

Oranges were not imported into Europe until medieval times. Until the seventeenth century, the word orange referred only to the fruit. Items that were orange in color were simply called red, yellow, or gold. Around the seventeenth century, orange gained an erotic connotation. People of the era believed that Nell Gwynne (c.1650–1687) seduced King Charles II (1630–1685) with the oranges she sold.

In ancient Rome, bleach and henna were used to create red and orange hair, which was considered highly fashionable.

Orange was the color of the early Christian church, as oranges symbolized the fruits of the earth.

In Northern Ireland, Protestants who wish to remain part of the United Kingdom are called the Orangemen, named after the orange-colored flowers they have worn in their parades since 1795.

Orange stimulates the metabolism, digestion, sexual energy, and athletic performance. It creates feelings of well-being and contentment.

Personality of Orange

People who favor orange excel at building connections and getting along with diverse personalities. They radiate warmth and friendliness. Organization and efficiency come naturally to them, extending both to their own lives and to coordinating others. They are motivated and action-oriented. Without clear goals to direct their energy, they become restless and impatient.

Aversion to Orange

Disliking orange reveals deep-seated insecurity. When plans go awry or obstacles appear, feelings of frustration and defeat overwhelm you. Building confidence and resilience against setbacks becomes important.

Missing Orange

An absence of orange indicates low confidence and self-esteem. Eat orange fruits and vegetables, breathe in orange energy, and undertake movement involving your lower abdomen. Dancing works perfectly, but any movement helps stimulate the sacral chakra area.

Supportive Uses of Orange

Orange proves useful when indecision or lack of motivation strikes. It delivers confidence and security. Orange increases vitality, eliminates procrastination, and sharpens focus on desired outcomes. It strengthens close relationships while promoting sociability and outgoing behavior. Wear orange when feeling fearful, anxious, or uncomfortable being alone. Orange provides particular support after receiving shocks or bad news.

Candle Magick Applications

Use orange when your goal involves creativity, innovation, or seeking a more positive future. Women should work with orange to overcome sexual problems or dysfunctions. Orange supports adaptability and encourages optimistic perspectives on what lies ahead.

Dark Magick Applications

Beyond its creative and positive uses, orange carries a dual nature in darker workings. It supports sexual manipulation, sex magick, sudden upheaval, and forced change. Orange can be employed to seize control, reverse someone's luck, or compel unwanted shifts in a target's circumstances. The Sun rules orange, and these workings are most potent on a Sunday when the Sun is well positioned.

Lunar Magick

Orange brings a warm, action-oriented energy into lunar workings, making it ideal for road-opening and success rituals. It supports creativity, motivation, and confident movement under the moon's light. Use it to invite joy, playful energy, and courageous momentum into your practice.

Color in Your Home

Orange is warm, cheerful, exciting, and full of the joys of life. It is a good choice for any rooms that enjoy plenty of activity, such as hallways, kitchens, dining rooms, living rooms, and family rooms. Orange should not be used in studies and bedrooms.
