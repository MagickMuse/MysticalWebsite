Lozenge

Notes/Warnings
A diamond shape often with rounded ends. Representation of female genitalia and the Vesica Piscis.

Heraldry

A diamond shape often with rounded rather than pointed ends, the lozenge is frequently overlooked but is actually a representation of the female genitalia.

The Vesica Piscis

Its most prominent appearance is as the Vesica Piscis, the sacred doorway through which spirit enters the world of matter.

Heraldry

In heraldry, the lozenge is used in place of the masculine shield to denote a coat of arms belonging to a woman or a non-combative male, such as a member of the clergy.
