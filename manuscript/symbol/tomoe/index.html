Tomoe

Type
Wheel & Disc
Other Symbols2
🌀Spiral☸️Wheel

Overview

The word Tomoe means "turning" or "circle," and the symbol shows two, three, or four comma-like shapes arranged in a spiral. The shapes suggest flames, which is why it is sometimes called the fire wheel. It belongs to both the Shinto and Buddhist faiths and appears in temples of both, and it also featured in Japanese Samurai heraldry.

Two and Three Flames

Drawn with two flames, the Tomoe closely resembles the yin yang emblem and carries the same meaning. Drawn with three flames, it represents the Earth, the Heavens, and humankind, the three foundations of Shinto philosophy.
