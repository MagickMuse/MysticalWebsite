Magickal Associations

Right is associated with yang, the masculine, and active forces. The right side of every person's body, regardless of gender, is affiliated with male energy. This association exists across unrelated cultures worldwide, including Hawaiian, Chinese, Asian, African, and European magickal traditions, some of which were isolated from one another for centuries.

The word "dexterous" derives from the Latin for right, and the historical concept of Dualism linked the right direction with God, authority, and correctness. Phrases like "in the right" and "Mr. Right" reflect this cultural bias.

Ritual Uses

Offerings to male deities are traditionally made with the right hand by practitioners of any gender. Feng shui recommends that men sleep on the right side of a shared bed, though spells designed to shift a relationship's power dynamic may call for the positions to be reversed.

Right Hand in Spell-casting

When a spell specifies using the right hand, one of two things is typically being requested:

  • Accessing masculine/yang energy: The spell's success depends on channeling active, projective forces rather than receptive, feminine ones
  • Using the non-dominant hand: For naturally left-handed practitioners, using the right hand engages the weaker, less controlled side, which certain workings require. Modern spell directions will specify when non-dominance matters rather than the direction itself.
