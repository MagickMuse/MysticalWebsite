📅Numerological Day 4

Numerical Correlations

Letters: D, M, V

Planet: Uranus

Element: Air

Influences: Divination and Knowledge, Foundations, Strength, the Elementals

Divination Meaning

Solid, practical, organized, administrative, calm, stern, respectable, steady, industrious, hard-earned success, detail oriented, bursts of rage, suspicious, resentful, poor, defeat, melancholic.

Numerology

Within numerology, four carries the Earth, solidity, understanding, and order. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, four marks the stage of testing and trials.

Symbolism of Four

With a fourth point now in existence, a new shape becomes possible. Where the third point of three gave the triangle, the fourth point opens the potential not only for the square but also for the circle of the Earth, and where the lines of latitude and longitude meet a cross is formed. Four is a masculine number, and it gives the cardinal directions of North, South, East, and West, the four evangelists Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, and the four elements of earth, air, fire, and water. In the Sufi tradition there are four gates through which a person must pass on the way to enlightenment.

There is a wholeness to four, an organized and orderly quality that likes to make sense of things. The square is protective and box-like, stable and steady, holding what lies within the embrace of its confines, and that sober square often provides the framework for the exuberant, circular mandala. In alchemy four key ingredients go toward the making of the Philosopher's Stone: sulfur, mercury, salt, and azoth. In the Tarot, four is the Emperor, the archetypal male figure, the father, the King, the patriarch who creates order and harmony.

The number holds a hidden secret. When the numbers within it are added together, one plus two plus three plus four, the whole comes to ten, which implies the start of a new cycle, a rebirth or reincarnation. These ten numbers form the Holy Name, or Tetragrammaton. In Pythagorean philosophy the four, or Tetrad, was held to be the root of all things, the perfect and intellectual number, and the Pythagoreans believed that four symbolized God, a teaching explained in a secret and sacred discourse that described God as the Number of Numbers. They also held that there were four parts to the soul, mind, opinion, science, and sense, which align with the four qualities of sensation, intuition, thinking, and feeling.

In the Yantric system of Hindu numerology, the four, or Chatvarah, represents the four paths to God and the Self, which are wisdom, devotion, meditation, and action.
