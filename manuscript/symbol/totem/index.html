Symbol Grimoire

Totem

Type
Emblem & Device

Overview

The idea of a totem as a kind of spiritual mascot that protects and guides a single person or an entire clan has long outgrown its origins as an ancient shamanistic concept. Originally an Algonquin term, a totem is an animal or plant whose attributes are shared by the one to whom it belongs. A totem belonging to a tribe provides a vital part of its identity and a way of understanding the workings of the physical world.

Initiation and Kinship

Although a totem is generally associated with Native American spirituality, totems are found in cultures all over the world. The revelation of a totem is usually part of an initiatory ritual, which for some takes the form of a shamanic ceremony during the rite of passage at puberty. A shared totem forms a strong bond of kinship or brotherhood, a meaning carried in the word itself.

Totem Poles

Totem poles are the physical expression of this spiritual idea, tall trees carved with birds, animals, fantastical creatures, and other symbols that tell the story of the people they belong to. Because the poles are made of wood, they rot easily, which has made it difficult to pinpoint their origins precisely.
