Overview

A Square of Mercury is an eight-by-eight kamea holding the numbers 1 through 64, arranged so every row and column sums to 260. Though Cabalistic in origin, witches favor it as a personal attunement talisman, a kind of signaling device or psychic telephone number used to bring the diviner's deep mind into contact with Mercury's power before any reading.

Making Your Square

  1. Take a blank sheet of white paper and exorcise it with salt water and a Mercurial incense.
  2. Draw a sixty-four cell grid matching the layout above.
  3. Inscribe the numbers 1 through 64 in exact numerical order. At each number, repeat a short invocation of your own choosing, such as "Mercury, be propitious to me," or a chain of the power's names, such as "Mercury, Hermes, Odin, Thoth." The words matter less than the image they call to mind: wisdom, speed, starlight, the open air, a crossroads at night.
  4. On reaching 64, seal the talisman aloud with "So mote it be," tracing three crosses in the air above the paper with your right forefinger.
  5. Wrap the finished square in clean linen or silk, or keep it in a dedicated box, alongside your other magickal tools.

Using the Square

Before any divination, hold the completed square in your hands and let your gaze travel across the numbers in their proper order, repeating your chosen invocation at each one. No further sealing is needed; the divination can begin right after. The same square can be reused indefinitely.
