Symbolism

There are 22 letters in the Hebrew alphabet and 22 cards in the Major Arcana of the tarot, and this is no coincidence. In the Kabbalah the 22 letters are believed to give expression to the whole Universe, and the tarot cards likewise reflect its qualities.

The same idea, that everything in the world is somehow held within the number 22, appears among the Bambara and Dogon peoples, who see 22 as a symbol for the span of time from creation to ultimate perfection and completion, and hold that all mystical knowledge is embraced by the symbolism of the first 22 numbers. Twenty-two is also the number of books in the Avesta, the sacred scriptures of the Zoroastrians.
