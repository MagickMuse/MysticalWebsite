Overview

Also known as the Witch's Knot or the Witch's Charm, this symbol is made of four interlocking vesica piscis shapes, sometimes with a circle added at the center. Because it can be drawn in a single unbroken line without lifting the pen, it serves as a symbol of protection.

Both Charm and Counter-Charm

Despite its name, in the Middle Ages it was often used as an anti-witchcraft sign, yet witches also used it to control the weather or as a love charm. This double use shows that the symbol worked homeopathically, turned to whatever end the maker intended. There are many different magick knots.
