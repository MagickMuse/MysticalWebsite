Umbraculum

◇Notes/Warnings
Also called the Umbrella or ombrellino. A canopy on papal insignia signifying shade and protection. Parallels the parasol among the eight sacred symbols of Tibetan Buddhism.

Overview

The Umbraculum, or Umbrella, appears on papal insignia and signifies both the long tradition of shading the Pope and the wider idea of protection.

Background

An attendant once walked beside the Pope holding the actual canopy so that he was shaded at all times. The practice has ended, but the emblem remains. It has a parallel in the parasol, one of the eight sacred symbols of Tibetan Buddhism.
