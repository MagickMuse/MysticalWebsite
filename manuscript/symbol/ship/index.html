Interpretation

Passage between life and death, transmutation, fertility, and growth.

Runic Context

The ship is one of the most potent pictographs in the runic tradition. It represents the vessel of transition between states of being, carrying the soul across the threshold of life and death. In Norse funerary custom, the ship burial was among the most honored rites. Beyond its mortuary significance, the ship also symbolizes fertility and growth, evoking the life-sustaining voyages of trade, exploration, and seasonal renewal. It often appears paired with the Man and Horse pictograph above it, suggesting the Odhinic force guiding the vessel of transformation.
