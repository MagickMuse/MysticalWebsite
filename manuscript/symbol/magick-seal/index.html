Overview

Magickal seals are used in invocation, to bind or otherwise harness the power of an entity or spirit. A seal is a kind of signature for the spirit, and to understand it is akin to knowing the spirit's name. There are dozens upon dozens of such symbols, many of them recorded in old grimoires, some compiled in the seventeenth century from much earlier sources. Curious and decorative, seals are used in ritual magick, often alongside amulets.

Planetary Seals

A magick seal uses numbers and shapes to build a symbol said to help the magician draw on the powers and qualities of a planetary deity. Each planet has its own magic square, or Kamea, and both the size of the square and the order of the numbers within it carry great significance. A shape is then laid over the numbers, joining them together to form the sigil of that planet.
