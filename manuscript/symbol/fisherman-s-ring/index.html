Fisherman's Ring

◇Notes/Warnings
Also called the Pescatorio. A unique gold ring cast for each new pope and destroyed at the end of his reign. Once used to seal papal correspondence.

Emblem & Device

Overview

The Fisherman's Ring, or Pescatorio, is a gold ring showing St. Peter casting his nets from a boat. A new one is made for each pope with his name set around the edge, and the previous pope's ring is smashed, so only one exists at any time.

Purpose

Destroying the ring marked the end of the old reign and, in practical terms, prevented anyone from using it to forge seals during the interregnum, the period with no pope. The Pope once used it to seal private correspondence. Today its image is reproduced as a stamp, though kissing the ring remains a mark of respect.
