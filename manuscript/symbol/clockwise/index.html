Summer Solstice Sun Spread

Also known as deosil in traditional Craft terminology.

In the Northern Hemisphere, clockwise means to the right, following the apparent motion of the sun across the sky. Clockwise movement is used for workings of increase, attraction, blessing, and building energy.

Note: This meaning is reversed in the Southern Hemisphere, where the sun appears to move counterclockwise across the sky.
