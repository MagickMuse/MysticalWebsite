Seal of Shamash

◇Notes/Warnings
A Mesopotamian emblem of Shamash, the Sun god, used in the Euphrates and Tigris region for a few centuries around 1000 BC. It is built upon the Sun symbol, the circle with a central dot.
◇Type
Seal & Sigil
◇Culture
Mesopotamian
↗Other Symbols2
CirclePoint Within the Circle

Shamash was the Sun god of Mesopotamia, and this seal was used for a few centuries on either side of 1000 BC in the region of the Euphrates and Tigris. Its foundation is the Sun symbol, the simple circle with a dot at the center, but here that base is given four sets of three rays whose wavy lines closely resemble those a child still draws today when depicting the Sun. The vertical and horizontal beams were most likely meant to mark the jurisdiction of Shamash over the four directions.
