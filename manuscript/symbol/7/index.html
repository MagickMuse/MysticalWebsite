📅Numerological Day 7

Numerical Correlations

Letters: G, P, Y

Planet: Neptune

Element: Water

Influences: Subconscious, Intuition, Psychical, Mysticism, Union of Dual Triads, Change

Divination Meaning

Reclusive, mystic, scholarly, philosopher, occultist, meditative, aloof, penetrative, imaginative, fey, strange.

Numerology

Within numerology, seven carries spirituality, the occult, and intelligence. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, seven marks blossoming.

Symbolism

The number 7 is everywhere. The Sumerians and Babylonians counted seven days in a week, and the traditional planets that shape so much of our mythology number seven. There are seven deadly sins balanced by seven cardinal virtues, seven Orders of Angels, seven colors in the rainbow, and seven pure notes in the diatonic scale. Seven gives a pivotal, settling point to the indecision of six, rendering it satisfyingly complete and whole.

A Number of Completeness

Very early on this number was given special status as a sign of completeness and perfection. For the Ancient Egyptians, 7 was the number of eternal life. Legend holds that the prophet Mohammed, while in Jerusalem, ascended through the seven Heavens and came into contact with the Divine, so the Dome of the Rock was divided into seven sections to honor the experience. Pilgrims make seven circumambulations of Mecca.

The Pythagoreans called 7 the Septad and held it "worthy of veneration." It was both the number of religion, because of the seven celestial spirits tied to the seven planets, and the number of life. In the Septad, the 3 (mind, spirit, and soul) meets the Tetrad, or 4 (the world). The resulting 7 is the mystic number of Man, symbolized by the cube: six sides plus the seventh element, the space within. The sides stand for the directions, the cardinal points plus above and below, with man at the center.

The Moon's phase lasts 28 days, which is 4 x 7, and 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 = 28. Because God created the world in six days and reserved the seventh for rest, the seventh day became holy, a holiday. That rest also connects God with man, proving even a deity needs a break now and then.

World Traditions

In Tibetan Buddhism the soul rests in an intermediate stage for 49 days after death, that is 7 x 7; in Japan, souls are believed to rest on the roofs of their homes for the same span. The Sufis, too, revere the number, expressed in a famous Sufi poem in which the seven stages toward enlightenment appear as seven valleys the birds must cross before they can find their leader.

In the Hindu Yantric system, Sapta represents the seven chakras or energy vortices that correspond to the seven notes of Indian classical music. There are seven higher planes of consciousness, the seven Heavens, opposed by seven lower planes, the hells.

There are seven alchemical operations: calcination, dissolution, separation, conjunction, fermentation, distillation, and coagulation. In the tarot, the number 7 card is the Chariot, depicting a young king in a chariot drawn by two horses, a man who knows exactly where he is going and is full of determination.
