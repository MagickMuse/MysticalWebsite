Origin and Nature

Said to be the first shape invented by humanity, the square represents the created Universe as opposed to the spiritual dimensions depicted by the circle. Plato described the square, like the circle, as being "absolutely beautiful in itself."

Like the cross, the square is associated with the number 4. A square has four corners; to speak of the "four corners of the Earth" is something of an anomaly since the Earth is round and without corners. All the symbolism of the number 4 is encompassed within the square. Just as the square represents the created Universe, in the Hebrew faith the Holy Name of the Creator is comprised of four letters.

Stability and Boundaries

The square gives a safe, static reference point, a stable and unmoving shape as opposed to the continual motion of the circle.

Temples and holy buildings are often built in the form of a square, solidly designed to align with the four points of the compass. The Ka'aba at Mecca is a fine example, as is the base of the Buddhist Stupa. Altars, too, are square. Square shapes define limits and create boundaries. To speak of someone as being "square" means they are fixed and unchangeable.

Symbolic Meaning

The square represents stability, structure, and the four elements. This foundational shape embodies grounding energy, manifestation power, and connection to material reality.

Magickal Properties

  • Balance through equal sides - perfect symmetry creates equilibrium
  • Order and structure - represents organization and foundation
  • Grounding energy - anchors spiritual work to the physical plane
  • Manifestation - the square is the foundation upon which we build tangible results

Associated Concepts

  • The four elements (Earth, Air, Fire, Water)
  • Material reality and the physical world
  • Foundation and stability
  • Equal balance and symmetry

Garden Magick

Square gardens are incredibly easy to create, most home stores sell wooden or stone borders of equal length, perfect for this shape. Square gardens are a perfect choice for protecting the home as they add balance and stability to the energy of the home (all four sides are the same length, and four is an incredibly stable number). They lend their energies to a stable home security plan.

Practical Applications:

  • Create a border of brightly colored flowers to attract happy energies into the home
  • Squares are easily divisible, separate into smaller squares or triangles for plants relating to:
    • The four elements
    • The four greater or lesser sabbats
    • And much more
