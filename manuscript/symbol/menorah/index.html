Overview

The Menorah is the seven-branched candelabrum of Jewish tradition. Scripture gives precise instructions for the golden Menorah of the Holy Temple in Jerusalem, with three branches reaching from each side of a central stem.

The Seven Lights

Its seven flames recall the seven days of creation and were first kindled one a day until all burned together at the week's end. They also stand for the seven planets and so for the seven archangels of the celestial spheres that govern them.

An Emblem of a Faith

The Menorah is among the most recognizable emblems of Judaism and appears, among many other places, on the coat of arms of Israel.
