One of the four cardinal points. Associated with the element Water, the season of Autumn, and the archangel Gabriel. Within the cycle of the day, the west corresponds to twilight.

Druidic Tradition

Druidic lore personifies the west as the "salmon of wisdom." In many Druid ceremonies the circle is opened to the elements and the directions, and the spirits of each quarter are honored in the hope that they will bless the proceedings.

Feng Shui Correspondences

In Feng Shui the west is feminine and stands for the youngest daughter. Its planet is Venus and its element is metal. Its further associations include the sea, evening, the enchantress, the mouth and tongue, serenity and joy, reflections, death, and mid Fall.
