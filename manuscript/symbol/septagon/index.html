Symbolic Meaning

The septagon (heptagon) represents disruption, protection through chaos, and fae magick. This seven-sided shape embodies the unpredictable, wild energy that deflects harmful intentions.

Magickal Properties

  • Spell disruption - deflects and scatters malignant magick
  • Fae connection - the seven-pointed star is sacred to the fae
  • Chaotic protection - uses wildness itself as a shield
  • Boundary-breaking - topsy-turvy energy that destabilizes attacks

Associated Concepts

  • The sevens in tarot (challenges, illusions, choices)
  • The seven-pointed star (Elven star, Faery star)
  • Wild, untamed nature
  • Protective chaos and unpredictability

Garden Magick

While an odd number of sides can make for a more difficult time plotting out the boundaries of the garden, it makes for an incredible protection garden.

How It Works:

  • The sevens in the tarot can be topsy-turvy
  • They can disrupt spellwork aimed at you, your home, or your family
  • The seven-pointed star is associated with the Fairies

A Wild Garden for the Fae

Once you've laid the groundwork for this seven-sided beauty, grab a premixed flower assortment that you can sprinkle over your new soil.

Important Guidelines:

  • A garden laid for the fae is a wild place, no need to weed
  • Place this wild garden on the edge of your property
  • Let this wildness disrupt any malignant magick headed your way
  • You can mow around this garden if you need to
⚠️
Keep those nasty chemical sprays far away from this garden. The fae do not appreciate pesticides, herbicides, or other unnatural chemicals in their space.
