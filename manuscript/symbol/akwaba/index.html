Akwaba

◇Notes/Warnings
⚠️ The Akwaba is sacred to the Akan (Ashanti) people of Ghana and should be approached with respect by those outside the tradition. Ashanti fertility doll. An example of sympathetic magick to ensure the arrival of a child.
◇Type
Amulet & Charm
◇Culture
African
↗Correspondances1
🌱Fertility

The Akwaba is an African fertility symbol belonging to the Ashanti tribe. It is a doll, usually carved of wood, which commands the same attention as a real infant.

Sympathetic Magick

The Akwaba is dressed, washed, and even "fed" until the human child is actually born. This is an example of sympathetic magick believed to ensure the arrival of the true baby.
