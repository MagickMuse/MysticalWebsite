Origin and Nature

The triangle shares all the symbolic significance of the number 3. It represents the many things that come in groups of three, from the Holy Trinity to the triple aspect of the Goddess. In ancient times, the triangle was considered synonymous with light.

Orientation and Meaning

The meanings of the triangle vary according to which way up it is. When it sits firmly on its base, it is a masculine, virile symbol representing fire. Inverted, it becomes the water element, a chalice shape emblematic of the feminine powers. Balanced on its point, the triangle also represents the yoni, further underpinning the Goddess aspect.

The equilateral triangle is a harmonious form, used to indicate the Higher Powers, providing a framework, for example, for the All Seeing Eye of God.

Strength and Form

As a symbol of strength, the triangle reinforces the corners of the square, both physically and metaphysically. The solid shape also makes its appearance in yogic positions, as in the Trikona Asana or Triangle Posture.

Symbolic Meaning

The triangle represents ascension, manifestation, and balance. This dynamic shape embodies the sacred trinity, the union of opposites, and the bridge between spiritual and material realms.

Magickal Properties

  • Three-point power - represents mind, body, and spirit working in harmony
  • Ascension - points upward toward divine connection and spiritual growth
  • Manifestation - brings spiritual energy into physical form
  • Trinity - symbolizes the sacred number three in all its forms

Associated Concepts

  • The triple goddess (Maiden, Mother, Crone)
  • Mind, body, and spirit
  • Past, present, and future
  • Birth, life, and death
  • The elements combined with spirit

The Triangle of Manifestation in Ritual

When used beneath a show stone or magic mirror, the triangle takes on a more specific ritual form: a triangle of manifestation, roughly twelve inches to a side, painted or taped in white onto a black cloth or board that covers the table of practice. This shape gives spirit and vision a focus and boundary in which to take form, echoing the triangle's older meaning as the crystallization of form out of chaos. Some ceremonially inclined witches enclose it within a double circle bearing the names of planetary angels and demon kings, or fortify its sides with the divine names Primeumaton, Anaphaxeton, and Tetragrammaton, though none of this is strictly necessary for the triangle to do its work.

Garden Magick

This three-sided polygon is reminiscent of deities with three forms, three faces, or even three deities as a trinity. Planting a garden in this shape allows for further division into three smaller triangles, one for each:

  • Face of a deity in a trinity
  • Phase of the moon (waxing, full, and waning)
💡 The new moon deals more with the unseen and relates to crops grown beneath the soil, like potatoes, tubers, carrots, and peanuts.

Practical Applications:

  • Place three window boxes end to end on a patio for a triangular garden
  • Dedicate each section to a different aspect of triple deity work

Vulva Symbolism and Protection

The downward pointing triangle is one of the traditional symbolic stand ins for the female genitals, the most protective of all images. Display it in the home or wear it as jewelry to borrow that protective power. See Vulva.
