⭐The Pentacle of Protection

The Secret Seal of Solomon is the symbol by which Solomon bound the seventy-two lords of the demons and Djinn, imprisoning them in a brazen vessel that was sunk beneath the ocean, later released by Babylonian wizards. The legend is connected to the drowned Nephilim.

In the Pentacle of Protection working, the Secret Seal is drawn on the third paper square using blood ink, either the practitioner's own blood or blood from raw meat. After drying, it is retraced with the athame while chanting a binding conjuration that seals all demons, powers of adversity, and ill-wishers from the bearer of the talisman.

The Secret Seal forms the innermost layer of the three-part Pentacle of Protection packet, sandwiched between the Pentagram and Hexagram of Solomon with the outer stars facing outward.
