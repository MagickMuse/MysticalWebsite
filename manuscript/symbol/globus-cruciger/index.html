The Globus Cruciger is a globe surmounted by a cross. It is a Christian symbol of authority.

The orb represents the Earth, and the cross represents Christ’s supremacy over it.

It was used as a symbol on Roman coins from the period when Christianity became prominent, around the fifth century AD. Before the cross was added, the lone orb had been used in the same way to imply authority, and adding the cross brought the emblem firmly into the Christian domain. In Britain, it appears as a physical object used during the coronation of the monarch, called the Orb, and it is part of the Crown Jewels.
