One of the four cardinal points. Associated with the element Earth, the season of Winter, and the archangel Uriel. Within the cycle of the day, the north corresponds to night.

Druidic Tradition

Druidic lore personifies the north as the "great starry bear of the Heavens." In many Druid ceremonies the circle is opened to the elements and the directions, and the spirits of each quarter are honored in the hope that they will bless the proceedings.

Feng Shui Correspondences

In Feng Shui the north is masculine and stands for the second son. Its planet is Mercury and its element is water. Its further associations include midwinter, midnight, danger, madness, flowing motion, the color red, the ear, the Moon, lakes, and the pig.
