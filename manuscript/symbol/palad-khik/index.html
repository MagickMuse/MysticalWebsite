Palad Khik

◇Notes/Warnings
Thai phallic protective amulet whose name means roughly "Honorable Surrogate Penis." Worn offset from the body to absorb curses aimed at the genitals. Originated in India and reached Thailand about 1200 years ago.

Overview

The Palad Khik is a Thai amulet whose name translates roughly as "Honorable Surrogate Penis," which describes its form exactly. It is worn discreetly beneath the clothing of Thai men, deliberately offset from the real organ, so that it will absorb any curses or evil spells aimed at the genitals.

Origins

The charm came from India and reached Thailand around twelve hundred years ago. Early examples were inscribed with dedications to Shiva, and later to Buddha, since the impulse to protect the sexual parts crosses religious boundaries. Modern versions often bear an archaic script that few Thais can read today, which lends them an antique appeal in the tourist market.
