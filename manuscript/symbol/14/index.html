Symbolism

The Moon's phases split into two sets of 14, one as the Moon waxes and grows larger, the other as it wanes.

In Freemasonry the number 14 has not gone unnoticed, with attention paid to the tradition that the body of Osiris was cut into 14 pieces by his murderer, his jealous brother Typhon, and the pieces scattered to the four winds. Isis, the wife of Osiris, gathered the pieces and gave them a fitting burial, so 14 came to be associated with death and resurrection.

Forgetfulness & Reincarnation

Part of any resurrection or reincarnation must be a loss of memory, since it would hardly help a returning soul to recall all that came before. Fourteen is therefore the number of forgetfulness. The Ancient Greeks believed that a soul about to be reborn drank the waters of Lethe, a river of Hades, which made it forget everything in preparation for its return to an earthly body.

The fourteenth letter of the Hebrew alphabet is Nun, and in the Kabbalah it represents the spirit or soul clothed in the material body. In the tarot this corresponds to Temperance, whose figure blends liquid from a blue jug with liquid from a red one. The mixture yields violet, the color of temperance and of spirit and matter combined. Fittingly, Temperance is also the card of reincarnation: the figure pours the violet liquid, the spirit, carefully from one vessel to the other.

An Unlucky Number in China

In China, 14 is one of the unluckiest numbers. Pronounced "one four," the words sound like "want to die," and this unfortunate echo reflects back onto the number itself.
