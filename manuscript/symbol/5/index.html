Numerical Correlations

Letters: E, N, W

Planet: Mercury

Element: Air

Influences: Communication, the Pentagram (4 Elementals + Spirit), Fulfillment

Divination Meaning

Restlessness, impatience, clever, takes risks, speculator, adventurer, resourceful, quick-tempered, conceited, sarcastic, self-indulgent, excesses.

Numerology

Within numerology, five carries harmony, justice, protection, and love. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, five marks growth.

Symbolism of Five

The dots that add up to five, placed symmetrically, give the potential for a five-pointed star, the pentagram, or a five-sided shape, the pentagon. Like the three, the five is built by adding an odd number, three, to an even number, two, uniting male with female. The pentagram is a mystical symbol that holds within it all the qualities of the five.

Five is a number of balance, its central dot acting as a pivot for the two on either side. It is also the numerical symbol of a human being, who forms the five-pointed star shape when the legs and arms are outstretched, as in Leonardo's famous drawing of Vitruvian Man.

In the Western world there are four elements, but in the East there is a fifth, a quintessence that binds them all together, called ether. Alchemists indicate this with a five-petaled rose at the center of a cross, echoing the Rose Cross Lamen. The Pythagoreans held that the pentagram, or Pentad, was a sacred symbol of health, vitality, and light. The holy number ten is divided equally into two parts by the Pentad, which also symbolizes the triumph of spirit over matter.

In Japanese Buddhism there are five directions, the cardinal points plus the center. In the Mayan civilization five was the symbol of perfection, possibly related to the five digits on a hand. In Islam five is a lucky number that recurs again and again, in the five hours of prayer, the five senses, and the five keys to the mysteries of the Qu'ran. In the Yantric system five is called Pancha, standing for the five functions of the Divine: creation, preservation, destruction, obscuration, and liberation. There are also five aspects of a person, the physical, vital, astral, wisdom, and bliss. In the Tarot, five is the Hierophant, or the Pope, who represents the face of God on Earth.
