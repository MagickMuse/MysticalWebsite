🌟Greatest Name

◇Type
Sacred Word & Glyph
◇Culture
Arabic/Islamic
↗Other Symbols2
🏷️Name📜Tetragrammaton

Symbolism

The Greatest Name refers to the most holy and secret name of God, the name said to hold the entire essence of divine power. The idea runs through many faiths, and the name usually carries the sense of "light" or "glory." In the Baha'i faith, the symbol of the Greatest Name is a calligraphic rendering of the phrase "Glory of Glories," or "Ya Baha'ul-Abha."
