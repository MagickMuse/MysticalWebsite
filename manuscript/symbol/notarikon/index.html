Overview

Notarikon is a kind of acronym, a Hebrew method of treating the letters of a word as the initials of a whole phrase. The hidden phrase usually opens a different way of reading the original word. Sacred words and the secret names of God were given this treatment as a way of concealing their true meaning, and because such codes are often woven into sacred texts, the original written form must be preserved exactly. A simple example is the word Amen, built from the initial letters of the phrase "the Lord and Faithful King."
