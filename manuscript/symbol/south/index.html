One of the four cardinal points. Associated with the element Fire, the season of Summer, and the archangel Michael. Within the cycle of the day, the south corresponds to day.

Druidic Tradition

Druidic lore personifies the south as the "great stag in the heat of the chase." In many Druid ceremonies the circle is opened to the elements and the directions, and the spirits of each quarter are honored in the hope that they will bless the proceedings.

Feng Shui Correspondences

In Feng Shui the south is feminine and stands for the second daughter. Its planet is Mars and its element is fire. Its further associations include lightning, female soldiers, the eye, the Sun, midday, drought, bitterness, the pheasant, the snail, and the tortoise.
