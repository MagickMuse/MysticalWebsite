Overview

Metatron's Cube is a complex sacred-geometry figure built from the Fruit of Life. By joining the centers of its circles with straight lines, the figure resolves into a geometrically exact pattern that contains the five Platonic solids hidden within it. It is closely tied to the Flower of Life.

The Angel Metatron

In Christianity, Judaism, and Islam, Metatron ranks among the most powerful angels of Heaven. He was granted a seat there, a privilege otherwise reserved for God, in his role as the scribe who records the history and deeds of humankind. Early kabbalistic documents say Metatron formed the cube from his own soul.

Protective Power

Apart from its geometric significance, the cube is a powerful symbol of protection, able to keep demons and other unwanted influences at bay.
