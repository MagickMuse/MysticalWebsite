🌑Night of HekateSamhain⏳Saturday

Black stones are receptive. They represent the Earth and stability and are ruled by Capricorn, the planet of restriction. Black stones are symbolic of self-control, of resilience and quiet power.

They are sometimes viewed as protective, but black stones are more often used to earth a person. If you're light-headed, dizzy, or so focused on the spiritual that your physical life suffers, wear black stones.

Mystically, black is the color of the outer spaces, of the absence of light. If you wish to perform a spell of magickal invisibility, to ensure that your actions will not be noticed by others, use a black stone for this purpose. For example, make a small image of yourself out of black clay and adorn this with black stones. Place it in a black box or one made of mirrors, and then put the box in a dark place. This simply hides you from others if they represent a threat to your life.

Qualities of Black

Black is sophisticated, powerful, and mysterious. Although we call it a color, black is created by the absence of light, and therefore color. Black absorbs light but reflects none back.

Cultural Symbolism

Although it is the opposite of white, both shades are in fact due to an absence of color, and technically speaking black is not a "color" at all. This does not stop it having a wealth of symbolic meaning.

Despite the mirthless sobriety of black, it depends how you wear it. The "New Black" is a term applied to anything that is in vogue, since black is also somehow dangerous and sexy as well as practical. The "black dog" means depression. Conversely, a black cat is a very lucky symbol in the UK and other parts of the world.

A person who holds a black belt in any of the martial arts is considered to be at the pinnacle of their abilities, and in Japan, black is the color of wisdom, experience, and maturity. In this instance, black is a color of perfection, an idea shared by the Cathars who also saw black as a symbol of completion and purity.

Black is a secretive, mysterious color used as such in rite and ritual. A polished black mirror provides a perfect, glossy surface for scrying or seeing into the future.

Religious Art

Black symbolizes death, mourning, evil, sin, and darkness. Satan, the Prince of Darkness, wears black.

Jesus and Temptation: Jesus is sometimes shown wearing black in paintings of the Temptation. This symbolizes the entangling, enveloping measures used by Satan.

Symbolic Pairing: Interestingly, white and black together symbolizes purity. This is why the Carmelites and Dominicans wear these colors.

Negative Connotations and Expressions

Black traditionally has negative connotations. This is why we have terms such as blackball, blacklist, blackmail, black magic, and black market. Despite this, if you're "in the black," you're making a profit.

A "black sheep" is someone who causes embarrassment because he doesn't fit in with the rest of the group he belongs to. "Black magic" is magic performed for an evil purpose.

Funeral Traditions

Priests wear black as a sign of respectability. However, the tradition of wearing black at a funeral was not originally done as a sign of respect for the deceased. It is a continuation of an old Roman custom that says that, in the presence of death, we are totally insignificant. However, it may also have been intended to make the survivors invisible to the spirit who had taken away the soul of the deceased.

Black Cats: Good Luck or Bad?

A black cat can indicate either good or bad luck, depending on your beliefs. At one time, people believed that witches could transform themselves into cats. Naturally, these cats would be black, as this color was believed to be evil. Consequently, if a black cat walked across their path, these people would think that it could easily be a witch.

In the thirteenth century, Pope Gregory IX associated black cats with witchcraft. People believed it could be the devil in disguise. When the Order of the Knights Templar was suppressed in the early fourteenth century, its members, after being tortured, were forced to confess that they worshipped the devil in the form of a black cat.

Fortunately, black cats have not fared as badly in other parts of the world. The Japanese consider black cats to be a sign of good luck, and Buddhists believe that a dark-haired cat in the house indicates future prosperity. Today, although some people still believe that a black cat walking in front of them is a sign of bad luck, many also believe that a black cat walking toward them is a sign of good fortune. Needless to say, cat lovers love all cats, no matter what color they happen to be.

Norse Runic Symbolism

In the Norse runic tradition, black represents new beginning, just as night and winter herald the birth of day and summer. It embodies all-potential, the root force of all things, knowledge of hidden things, concealment, and the container of light.

Personality of Black

Those who favor black are independent thinkers with firm opinions on most matters. They speak their minds directly. Discipline, secrecy, and intensity mark their approach to life. They are goal-oriented with powerful ambitions that often remain hidden until achieved. If someone chooses black during a period of depression, reaching out to friends for support and making plans for new activities can help restore balance.

Aversion to Black

Disliking black suggests underestimating your own abilities. You fail to recognize your positive qualities, fixating instead on perceived failings. Learning to acknowledge your strengths and accomplishments becomes essential for growth.

Supportive Uses of Black

Black provides strength and the ability to assert yourself when others attempt to take advantage. It eliminates oversensitivity and heightened emotions. However, black also creates an emotional barrier that maintains distance from others.

Candle Magick Applications

Black absorbs negative energy and should be used only when you feel surrounded by negativity with no apparent way out. Burn a black candle for one hour each night until it has completely burned away. After this cleansing process, you can begin working with a colored candle that symbolizes your actual goal or desired outcome.

Dark Magick Applications

Black is the foundational color for destructive workings and should accompany other candle colors whenever possible in ritual. In baneful magick, black candles promote the slow, grinding destruction of enemies rather than sudden violence. They sow discord, confusion, and chaos in a target's life. Black absorbs and conceals, making it effective for masking your workings while destabilizing an adversary. As the container of all light, it ranks among the most formidable colors in offensive spellcraft. Saturn governs black, lending it endurance and patience. Perform destructive workings with black during a waning moon on a Saturday when Saturn is well positioned and gaining strength.

Lunar Magick

Black is strongly protective in lunar workings, especially during the dark moon and waning phases. Use it for banishing, setting firm boundaries, and releasing what no longer serves. It carries an energy of mystery, raw power, and conscious endings.

Color in Your Home

Black absorbs light, and this creates a negative, draining effect. Consequently, it should be used cautiously. It can be useful as an accent to other colors, but because it does not transmit the light we need, it fails to nourish us the way the other colors do.
