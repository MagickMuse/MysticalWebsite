Sun sign

Notes/Warnings
A general sign for the Sun, which appears in many forms including the circle with a central dot, the wheel, and the swastika. As the most prominent feature of the natural world, the Sun has long been venerated as a deity.

Nothing in the natural world looms larger than the Sun, the source without which life could not be, so it comes as no surprise that this blazing star has been worshipped as a god and that its reach as a symbol runs so deep. It is rendered in countless ways. Among the plainest is a single circle with one dot at its heart, an image so spare it asks for no gloss at all.

The Sun is pictured, too, as a wheel rolling across the sky from the eastern edge to the western. The oldest and best known form of that idea is the swastika, the solar wheel caught in motion.
