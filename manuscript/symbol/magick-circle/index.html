Overview

The Circle is a powerful symbol of strength, union, protection, and eternity, and the magick circle holds all of these meanings with the added power of being "charged" by ritual. It is usually inscribed on the floor or the ground to mark out the space in which magickal work will take place.

Casting the Circle

Before any working the circle is "cast," and practitioners differ in how they do it. Salt is a popular choice for its protective qualities, and candles and incense are used as well. The circle is treated with great respect as a sacred space and a symbol of the microcosm. Those who work the occult arts set great store by its correct construction; Aleister Crowley, who liked to summon otherworldly spirits, once found that the only thing standing between him and ruin was a circle of sand held with powerful intent.
