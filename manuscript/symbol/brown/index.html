Brown is the color primarily associated with the Earth, soil, the raw element before it is covered with greenery. The word for earth, in Latin, is humus, which carries the same root as humility. Religious ascetics wear brown as a reminder of this quality and also of their voluntary material poverty.

Etymology and Qualities

The word brown comes from the Anglo-Saxon brun.

Brown, the color of earth and of fall, is a grounding color. It symbolizes good health, hard work, stability, and reward through effort. Brown enhances all of the other colors.

Religious and Cultural Symbolism

Christian monks often wear brown garments, as they associate it with poverty, humility, and renunciation. This symbolism is derived from humble clay. In China, the emperors of the Sung dynasty had a completely different view of the color. The color brown was used to symbolize the dynasty.

Medieval Mourning

In the Middle Ages, brown was a color customarily used in mourning.

Great Depression and Other Views

Brown was an extremely popular color for men and women's clothing during the Great Depression. This was because it naturally concealed any dirty marks. Sigmund Freud, not the most cheerful of people at the best of times, thought that the main symbolism for brown was excrement.

Personality of Brown

People drawn to brown are grounded, practical, and hardworking. Honesty, stability, supportiveness, and security define their character. They perceive situations in clear terms and possess the ability to grasp problems' essential nature immediately. This skill enables them to solve issues that stump others. It also guides them toward quality purchases over fleeting trends. They avoid the spotlight and guard their inner thoughts carefully.

Aversion to Brown

Disliking brown indicates fearfulness, timidity, and withdrawal. Incessant worry about every aspect of your life paralyzes you. Developing courage and learning to manage anxiety becomes necessary for moving forward.

Supportive Uses of Brown

Brown eliminates panic attacks, anxiety, and emotional insecurity. It grounds you to earth and helps maintain proper perspective. As one of nature's primary colors, brown warms and affirms life. Wear brown when feeling frightened or uncertain about the future, or needing safety and security.

Healing for animals.

Dark Magick Applications

In baneful workings, brown candles cultivate indecision, hesitancy, and dullness in a target. They sow uncertainty, sadness, and a persistent lack of clarity that undermines confidence and forward momentum.

Color in Your Home

Brown is a grounding color. It creates feelings of support and strength. Natural wood is the ideal way to introduce brown into your color scheme. Pottery, ceramics, stones, bricks, and bare plaster are other ways to bring natural colors into the home. They all provide support and stability.
