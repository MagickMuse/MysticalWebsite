Trefot

◇Notes/Warnings
Runic ideograph. Made from three L-runes (21 + 21 + 21 = 63, or 7 x 9).
◇Type
Knot
↗Correspondances1
✨Inspiration

Interpretation

Dynamic power from the three realms of being and the threefold evolutionary force. Magickal inspiration throughout the cosmos.

Runic Context

The Trefot is constructed from three Laguz-runes (L-runes), each bearing the numerical value of 21. Their sum (21 + 21 + 21 = 63, or 7 x 9) yields a number of deep magickal significance, combining the sacred seven with the holy nine. The threefold structure channels dynamic power from all three realms of being, expressing the evolutionary force that permeates the cosmos. As a symbol of magickal inspiration, it represents the ceaseless creative current flowing through the multiverse.
