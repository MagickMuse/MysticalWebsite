Overview

In the Hindu faith the lingam is a ubiquitous symbol whose very name means "sign." It represents the phallus and procreation, but with the emphasis on creative energy and the spirit of life rather than on eroticism. The lingam is the symbol of the great deity Shiva.

The Tower of Light

In yoga the lingam is pictured as a tower of light rising from the base of the spine. The Kundalini Serpent of energy climbs this tower, which represents the power of knowledge.

Stone and Temple

Lingam shapes occur naturally in certain standing stones and rocks, while the linga that stand at the heart of temples are carved from stone. The base is square, a sign of the Earth and of stability and security, and the upper part is cylindrical.

Lingam and Yoni

On its own the lingam belongs to the realm of the untried and untested. Joined with the yoni it becomes empowered. The yoni is the symbol of the womb, of female energy and creativity, and is shown as the shallow basin that surrounds the lingam; the two are inextricably linked. The temple lingam is anointed with oil and water and dressed with flowers, and it is one of the many objects that represent the Axis Mundi.
