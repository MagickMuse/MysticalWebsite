📜Tetragrammaton

Type
Sacred Word & Glyph
Other Symbols3
🌟Greatest Name🏷️NameTetraktys

Overview

Words carry great power, and to speak a word aloud "enables" it. Names carry the most power of all, and there is no name more potent than the Tetragrammaton. This holy name is sometimes drawn in the form of the older Pythagorean Tetraktys, a sign that represents the Universe.

Literally, Tetragrammaton means "four-letter word" in Greek, and it refers to the secret Name of the God of the Israelites, written in Hebrew. The name is so holy and powerful that it may never be spoken, save once a year at Yom Kippur, by the High Priest within the Holy of Holies. To avoid uttering it, people refer instead to "The Name," "Elohim," or "Adonai."

Its letters are yodh, heh, vav, heh, and God is said to have told Moses that the name means "I Shall Be" or "I Am." Pronounced roughly "ee ah ou eh," hence Yahweh, the letters often appear as part of magickal amulets and talismans.

The true Name of God is said to be seventy-two letters long. It was written on a long slip of paper concealed inside the High Priest's jeweled breastplate, and when the Name was invoked the jewels would light up in a certain sequence, allowing the priest to communicate directly with God.
