Symbolic Meaning

The wheel represents fortune, cycles, and seasonal change. Closely related to the circle, this shape takes a larger footprint and emphasizes division into meaningful sections.

The Turning Wheel

What sets the wheel apart from the circle is its motion. Its turning mirrors the cycles of the Sun and Moon, the round of the seasons, the course of a human life, and the passage of time itself, so that it comes to stand for death and rebirth. Ancient peoples pictured the cosmos as a wheel revolving without rest, and the Zodiac is read as a wheel in the same way. The name recalls the Etruscan goddess Vortumna, 'she who turns the year,' whom the Romans knew as Fortuna; her Wheel of Fortune raises and lowers human lives, its eight segments holding a balance of opposing conditions such as passion and indifference, wealth and poverty, war and peace. As a solar and lunar emblem the wheel was honored at the winter solstice, when blazing wheels were rolled down hillsides to enact the turning of the seasons and the Sun's descent into winter darkness. The wheeled chariot carries the same meaning, and the rose windows of medieval cathedrals such as Chartres are wheels in the form of mandalas, figures that Jung described as representations of the Self.

  • Fortune reversal - especially powerful if you've been hexed due to bad luck

Garden Magick

Closely related to the circle, the wheel takes a larger footprint and can be divided into sections planted with flora you associate with holy days, seasons, or individual spokes to represent The Wheel of Fortune in the tarot, especially if you think you've been hexed due to a run of bad luck.

Use this wheel shape to change your luck for the better and keep it there.

Practical Applications:

  • Plant a sweet annual border with something useful like Marigold to divide the wheel into four parts
  • Place herbs associated with an element in each of the four quarters
💡 Marigold tip: Pinch the dead flower heads and dry them in a paper bag for a sweet steam facial that will unclog pores and kill pimple-causing bacteria!
☠️
Safety Warning: While poisonous plants are intimately associated with death, caution against planting protection vegetables with poisons in different sections of the same small area.

Popular vegetables like tomatoes, potatoes, eggplant, bell peppers, tomatillos, and cayenne are all closely related to datura and belladonna. These plants are found in the Solanum family (over 2,000 plants), meaning they can cross-pollinate and breed. There are stories of people being poisoned by the resulting fruit. Let's not tempt fate by planting them too close together.

This isn't to say "don't grow poisons"; lots of incredible flowers are potentially toxic, even some states' signature flowers.
