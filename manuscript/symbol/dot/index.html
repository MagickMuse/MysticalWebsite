Awen

The dot might seem unassuming, the first mark on a pristine sheet of paper. In this case, the dot is a beginning. But the dot also closes a sentence, making it a symbol of ending. The dot is both origination and conclusion, encompassing all the possibilities of the Universe within it, a seed full of potential and a symbol of the Supreme Being. The dot is the point of creation, the place where the arms of the cross intersect.

The Bindhu

The dot is also called the bindhu, meaning "drop." The bindhu is a symbol of the Absolute, marked on the forehead at the position of the third eye in the place believed to be the seat of the soul.

Dots Within Symbols

The presence of dots within a symbol can signify the presence of something else. A dot in the center of the Star of David marks the quintessence, or Fifth Element. It also acts as a reminder of the concept of space.

The decorated dots surrounding the doorways of Eastern temples are not merely ornamental devices but hold significance relevant to the worshippers. Dots frequently appear in this way, acting as shorthand for the tenets of a faith. In the Jain Symbol, the dots stand for the Three Jewels of Jainism. The dots in each half of the yin yang symbol unify the two halves: one is "yin," the other "yang." Together they demonstrate the interdependence of opposing forces.
