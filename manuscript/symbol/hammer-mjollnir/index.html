Hammer (Mjöllnir)

Interpretation

Mjöllnir, the Hammer of Thor: protection, increase, raw power, and will.

Runic Context

The hammer appears in both pictographic and ideographic form across runic inscriptions and talismans. As Mjöllnir, it is the weapon of Thor, the defender of gods and humankind. The symbol invokes protective force, the capacity for increase and expansion, and the concentrated expression of will. The hamarsmark (sign of the hammer) was traced over sacred objects and ritual implements to hallow and consecrate them, a practice predating Christianity.

Myth and Modern Use

Legend places the hammer's origin in a meteor that fell to Earth, while other sources credit the trickster god Loki with forging it. Its power was said to level mountains and topple giants in a single blow, and like a boomerang it returned to whoever threw it. As an amulet Mjöllnir grants protection, and despite roots squarely in the pagan world it slipped the net cast by Christianity to remain a major symbol of the Asatru faith today.
