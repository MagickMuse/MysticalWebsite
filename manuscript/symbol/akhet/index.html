Notes/Warnings

The High Priestess

In Egyptian, Akhet means "dawn." This symbol, often made into an amulet by the Ancient Egyptians, symbolized the new Sun rising over the sacred mountain.

The Guardian Aker

The symbol sometimes features the double-headed lion, or Aker, that guards it.

Astrological Connection

The Akhet is also related to the glyph used to denote the astrological sign of Libra.
