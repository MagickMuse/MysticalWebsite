Overview

The Trident, or Trisula, is a long staff or pole topped by three prongs. Whoever wields a trident wields great power, though that power can take many forms.

Shiva and the Three

The trident is the emblem of the Hindu god Shiva, whose followers wear a trident-like Tilaka on their faces. In Shiva's hands the trident represents the three phases of time, past, present, and future, or possibly Heaven, Earth, and Hell. It even has its own hand gesture or mudra, called the trishulahastra.

Sea, Sky, and Underworld

In the hands of Poseidon or Neptune, the trident takes on a practical role, echoing the shape of one of the earliest fishing implements. Poseidon uses it to command the seas, making it a symbol of authority, and through this link to water it became the alchemical symbol for that element. Where its three prongs are of even length, the trident serves as a secret symbol of the Christian Cross. Satan, however, is often shown harrying the souls of hell with a trident, likely to tie him to the pre-Christian gods who also carried it. Because its prongs resemble flames, the trident is also a fire symbol and a sign of thunderbolts and lightning, which is why many sky gods carry one, including Thor of Norse mythology.
