Magickal Associations

Left is associated with yin, the feminine, and receptive forces. The left side of every person's body, regardless of gender, is affiliated with female energy. This association predates Dualism and Christianity, appearing independently across Hawaiian, Chinese, Asian, African, and European magickal traditions.

The word "sinister" derives from the Latin for left, reflecting the historical bias against left-handedness. Children who were naturally left-handed were once forced to use their right hands, sometimes through physical punishment.

Ritual Uses

Offerings to female deities are traditionally made with the left hand by practitioners of any gender. Feng shui recommends that women sleep on the left side of a shared bed, though spells intended to reverse a relationship's power dynamic may call for the positions to be switched.

Left Hand in Spell-casting

When a spell specifies using the left hand, one of two things is typically being requested:

  • Accessing feminine/yin energy: The spell's success depends on channeling receptive, feminine forces rather than active, masculine ones
  • Using the non-dominant hand: Many older spell books assume all practitioners are right-handed, so "use your left hand" originally meant "use your weaker hand." Where the importance lies in the non-dominant hand specifically, modern spell directions will note this, instructing left-handed people to use their right hand instead. Where no such clarification is given and the only instruction is to use the left hand, that direction applies universally regardless of hand dominance.
