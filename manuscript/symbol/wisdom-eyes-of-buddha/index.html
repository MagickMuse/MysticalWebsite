Symbol Grimoire

Wisdom Eyes of Buddha

Notes/Warnings
Also known as the Buddha Eyes.
Type
Eye
Entities1
Buddha

Overview

This symbol shows the watchful eyes of the Buddha, found on stupas in the place where the actual eyes would sit if the building were a statue of the Buddha himself. The eyes are painted on every side, a reminder that the Buddha watches over all four corners of the Earth in his omnipotence.

The Third Eye

Between the eyes, at the point of the third eye, sits a mark signifying enlightenment. Beneath the eyes is a squiggle resembling a question mark; this is the Sanskrit character for the number one, symbolizing the unity of all things.

Symbol of Nepal

The Wisdom Eyes of Buddha are so common in Nepal that they have become, by default, a symbol of the country itself.
