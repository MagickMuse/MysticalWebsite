Aaskouandy

Notes/Warnings

The Aaskouandy is a magickal charm of considerable power originating from Iroquois tradition. It is any object that is unusual in some way, appearing unexpectedly or somehow out of place in its surroundings. For example, if a stone is found in the entrails of an animal that was particularly hard to hunt, then this stone is perceived to be the object that gave the animal its power, making it eligible for Aaskouandy status.

⚠️ The Aaskouandy is sacred to the Iroquois people and should only be practiced by those within Indigenous traditions.

A Living Charm

The Iroquois believe the Aaskouandy has a mind of its own. If neglected, it can turn against its owner. It also possesses trickster tendencies and can change shape at will, metamorphosing into another object altogether. Because the Aaskouandy is an independent being, the owner is careful to stay on the right side of it, keeping it happy with gifts and feasts.

The Onniont

If an Aaskouandy appears in the shape of a fish or serpent, it is considered particularly potent because of the inherent power of these creatures. In this case, the Aaskouandy changes its name and becomes an Onniont.
