Overview

Gematria is a system of numerology that links letters with numbers. Although similar methods appear elsewhere, Gematria applies specifically to the Hebrew language and is expounded throughout the mystical writings of Judaism. It is from this system that the Chai takes its fortunate value of 18.

The Hebrew alphabet has 22 letters, yet 27 numerals are needed to express every number up to 999 (one through nine, ten through ninety, and one hundred through nine hundred). The mystical Hebrew system notes that the five missing numerals correspond exactly to the alphabet's five word-final alternate forms.

Hidden Correspondences

Striking correspondences can emerge from this system. The numerical values of the words for lion, cheetah, and tiger, for instance, all reduce to 5. Words that share the same value are held to share the same qualities, and so can be used to reveal still further aspects of the Divine.
