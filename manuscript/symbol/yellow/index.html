Yellow stones and minerals are projective. Ruled by Leo, they are used in rituals involving communication. If you're having trouble expressing yourself in an intelligible way, try wearing a yellow stone. Writers may receive help with their work by utilizing yellow stones, while public speakers wear them for eloquence.

Also ruled by the Sun, yellow stones are protective, while the element of Air, also a ruler, tells us that they can be used to strengthen the conscious mind. They are worn during magick to heighten visualization abilities.

Spells involving travel can be performed with yellow stones, perhaps by holding one in your projective hand and visualizing yourself traveling to your desired location.

Healing

Healthwise, yellow stones are used to promote digestion, to regulate the nervous system, and for skin problems.

These are stones of movement, of exchange, of energy and mental awareness.

Qualities of Yellow

Yellow is lighthearted, carefree, and full of the joys of life. It is warm, bright, and cheerful. Yellow is intellectual, provides hope, and helps people find a sense of direction in their lives. It is always learning and striving for knowledge and wisdom.

The only disadvantage of yellow's approach is that it relies much more on logic and thought than on feelings and emotions.

Negative Associations

Yellow is sometimes considered a negative color. In medieval morality plays, the actor playing the devil always wore yellow. Cowards are called "yellow." When this color manifests negatively, it can lead to cowardice, jealousy, treason, treachery, dishonor, and perversion.

These negative associations arose because medieval artists believed that Judas Iscariot wore yellow robes. In Nazi Germany, Jewish people were forced to wear yellow armbands. Victims of the Inquisition were also made to wear yellow armbands.

Cultural Significance

In China, yellow was the imperial color and was considered highly auspicious. Lucky charms were often printed on yellow paper.

Religious Art

Yellow (and gold) symbolizes the sun, fruitfulness, and the goodness and perfection of God. Joseph, husband of the Virgin Mary, wears yellow.

However, yellow also symbolizes the negative qualities of jealousy, deceit, and disloyalty. Not surprisingly, Judas Iscariot is usually shown wearing yellow.

Halos of Saints: The halos of saints are yellow or gold to show the beauty and promise of eternal life.

Color Healing Properties

Yellow provides energy for the muscular system, the nervous system and the brain. It stimulates and purifies the blood and lymph systems. It cleanses the liver, intestines, and the skin. It has a psychological effect on the body, creating feelings of cheerfulness and positivity.

Constipation, diabetes, dyspepsia, eczema, flatulence, indigestion, kidney problems, paralysis, piles, rheumatism, spleen issues

Yellow is a sensory stimulant associated with wisdom and clarity. It is thought to carry decongestant and anti-bacterial properties, and to stimulate both the digestive and the lymphatic systems.

Norse Runic Symbolism

In the Norse runic tradition, yellow represents earthly power and signifies desire and lust directed toward manifestation. It is related to both green and gold within the runic color system.

Personality of Yellow

Those drawn to yellow express themselves with clarity and thrive on novelty and new experiences. They are natural entertainers who also enjoy being entertained. Communication flows easily for them, and their minds overflow with creative ideas. Their excellent sense of humor makes them the life of any gathering when they choose to shine.

Aversion to Yellow

Disliking yellow signals vulnerability to intimidation. Fear of criticism paralyzes action. Rather than risk judgment, you hold yourself back from opportunities. Developing courage to face potential criticism despite the discomfort becomes crucial for growth.

Missing Yellow

An absence of yellow suggests nervousness or stress. Breathe in yellow energy and massage the area just above your navel.

Supportive Uses of Yellow

Yellow helps overcome frustration, loneliness, and depression. It stimulates mental activity while promoting concentration, fresh ideas, and clear thinking. Yellow encourages communication and cultivates feelings of joy, happiness, and excitement. Wear yellow when taking things too seriously or underestimating yourself. Yellow provides particular benefit during depressive episodes. Yellow clothing enhances self-perception.

Candle Magick Applications

Use yellow when your goal involves study, learning, or intellectual pursuits. Yellow strengthens communication abilities, making it ideal for writers and public speakers. It also attracts like-minded friends and creates enjoyable, harmonious environments. Yellow supports all forms of mental work and expression.

Dark Magick Applications

In baneful workings, yellow candles are used to provoke infidelity, cowardice, and inconsistency. They can incite decay, disease, dying, and insanity in a target. Yellow also governs gossip, slander, rumor, and theft, making it effective for workings that attack a target's reputation or mental stability. Mercury governs yellow, and destructive workings are most effective on Wednesdays. When the intent is to overpower a target's will, perform the working on a Sunday instead.

Lunar Magick

Yellow brings brightness and mental clarity to moon rituals, supporting intentions around happiness, luck, and inspiration. It strengthens communication and sharpens the mind during lunar workings. Use it during waxing moons to invite fortunate energy and fresh ideas.

Color in Your Home

Yellow is warm, bright, cheerful, and invigorating. It can lift up a cold room or any area that receives little or no sun. However, it can appear overpowering when used in a room that already receives a great deal of sunlight. It is a good choice for kitchens, living rooms, and family rooms.
