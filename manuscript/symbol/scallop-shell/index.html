Scallop Shell

Notes/Warnings

The scallop, or mussel, shell was once worn on the caps of pilgrims, above all those who had completed the pilgrimage to Santiago de Compostela.

Moon and the Feminine

The shell is the home of the mussel, a marine creature that, like all dwellers in the watery world, is bound to the Moon and to the eternal feminine. That idea takes form in the image of the Goddess Venus rising from the ocean upon a shell. The shell is shaped like the female sexual organ, and in France the common word for it carries the same double meaning.

The Pearl

Pearls grow within these shells, and Mary, the Mother of Jesus, was said to carry a precious pearl within her womb. From these threads comes the shell worn on the pilgrim's cap.
