Symbolism

Known as the Royal Star of the Lion, 23 is in purely numerological terms an auspicious number, promising help from higher places or people, along with success and fame. Yet with 23 things are not quite so simple, for much of its mystique comes from a relatively recent source: Discordianism.

If 22 can encompass the whole of the Universe, what of the number that follows it? As its immediate successor, 23 logically heralds the start of a wholly new world, one no longer governed by the harmonious laws of the preceding 22. It also breaks down into 2 (female) plus 3 (spiritual energy), which combine to make 5, the number of harmony and balance, neatly echoed by the 23 chromosomes contributed by each parent at conception.

The 23 Enigma

Discordianism is a modern "prankster" spiritual movement that began in 1958. One of its central tenets is the "23 Enigma," the belief that everything in the Universe and every event is somehow connected to the number 23. The goddess who presides over the Discordians is Eris, the Greek goddess of strife and discord, also known as the Queen of the Night. The idea has had a long reach, inspiring a speculative-fiction trilogy that popularized the 23 Enigma and the related Rule of Fives (2 + 3 = 5), along with more than one film in which an obsession with the number drives the plot.
