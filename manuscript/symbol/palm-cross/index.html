Palm Cross

Notes/Warnings
Given to churchgoers on Palm Sunday, the week before Easter. Woven from palm leaves, an ancient emblem of victory.
Type
Cross
Culture
Gnostic/Early Christian
Other Symbols2
CrossLatin Cross

The Palm Cross is woven from palm leaves and handed to Christian congregations on Palm Sunday, the Sunday before Easter.

It commemorates the triumphant entry of Christ into Jerusalem shortly before the crucifixion, when palm leaves were strewn along his path. The palm leaf was a long-standing emblem of victory, which is why it became the material for these crosses.
