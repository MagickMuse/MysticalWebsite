Symbolic Meaning

The hexagon represents harmony, peace, and stillness. This six-sided shape embodies the ability to arise from strife and conflict, embracing calm and equilibrium.

Magickal Properties

  • Conflict resolution - helps overcome strife and discord
  • Energy calming - slows rapidly moving energies
  • Thought stilling - brings peace to racing minds
  • Emotional balance - soothes turbulent feelings

Associated Concepts

  • The number six in tarot (arising from conflict, embracing harmony)
  • Hexagonal patterns in nature (honeycomb, snowflakes)
  • Controlled flow of energy
  • Peace and contentment

Garden Magick

The sixes in the tarot explore our ability to arise from strife and conflict and to embrace harmony and peace. To bring these calming feelings to your home, consider a six-sided garden spot.

How It Works:

  • While a Circle has no edges and allows energy to flow continuously
  • The hexagonal shape slows rapidly moving energies
  • This stills thoughts, feelings, and emotions

Practical Applications:

  • Create a hexagonal bed for meditation gardens
  • Plant calming herbs within the six sides
  • Use for recovery gardens after periods of turmoil
  • Dedicate to peace work and emotional healing
