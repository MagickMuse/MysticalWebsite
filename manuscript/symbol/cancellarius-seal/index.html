Astrum Argentum Seal

One of the symbolic seals of Aleister Crowley's Astrum Argentum, or Silver Star order. As the name suggests, it indicates the position of Chancellor.

The symbol shows an Eye of Horus at the center of rays that are set in 12 groups of 3.

The Astrum Argentum

The Astrum Argentum was started by Crowley in 1907 as an alternative to MacGregor Mathers' Golden Dawn. Although he had initially been enamored of the Golden Dawn and its charismatic leader, Crowley liked to do things his own way, resulting in his expulsion from the Golden Dawn. He believed that his own personal angel, Aiwaz, approved of his decision to supplant Mathers' brotherhood.

The unusual structure of the Astrum Argentum was typical of Crowley's desire to be different. Each member was supposed to know only their immediate superior and anyone they introduced into the Order. For Crowley, the sole purpose of the Astrum Argentum was to disseminate his own teachings and mystical beliefs. It was assumed that anyone introduced into the Order would already have had a great degree of magickal training, in contrast to the Golden Dawn, which was dedicated to teaching.
