9️⃣Numerological Day 9

Numerical Correlations

Letters: I, R

Element: Fire

Influences: Aggression, Energy, New Path, Immortality, Indestructibility, Binding

Divination Meaning

Mental/spiritual achievement, impulsive, sympathetic, visionary, humanitarian, determined, science, teaching, artistic, unorthodox, intolerant of opposition, self-centered.

Numerology

Within numerology, nine carries the limited and the limitless, experience and virtue, and completion. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, nine marks the harvest.

Symbolism

As 3 x 3, the number 9 carries magical status and is held sacred. For Hindus it is the number of Lord Brahma, the Creator. It is also the numerological sum of Ba'hai, seen there as a symbol of perfection and unity, which explains the nine-pointed star of that faith.

The Pythagoreans knew 3 x 3 as the Ennead, the first square of an odd number. Because it falls one short of the perfect 10, it was classed as an unfortunate number, sometimes even thought evil as the inversion of 6. Yet it was also considered limitless, since beyond it lay nothing but infinity, represented by the perfect Decad, 10.

World Traditions

The Eleusinian mysteries described nine spheres through which human consciousness must pass on its journey toward rebirth in a new body. Apollonius of Tyana recognized the sacred status of 9 and set aside the ninth hour for silent meditation and hymns. The Ancient Romans held feasts of purification for newborn boys when they were nine days old, and buried their dead nine days after death.

In the Tantric system, 9 is called Nava. There are nine virtues of Dharma, the rules of living by divine law. The great festival of Durga Puja, in honor of the Goddess, unfolds over nine nights, with the tenth night as its culmination. The Nava Ratnas are the nine especially precious gemstones: pearl, ruby, cat's eye, beryl, diamond, red coral, yellow sapphire, emerald, and blue sapphire. Together, all nine make a very powerful talisman or amulet.

Completion & Man

As the last single-digit number, nine carries a sense of completion and wholeness, which is why it represents achievement and the culmination of a task, the zenith for a single figure. Because a human embryo needs nine months of gestation, nine is the number of Man, who also has nine physical apertures, symbolizing nine channels of communication with the world. There are nine known planets in our Solar System. In the tarot, 9 is the card of the Hermit. It is worth noting that 666, the number of the Beast, adds up to 18 in numerology, and 1 + 8 is 9: no great evil there, simply a matter of fact.
