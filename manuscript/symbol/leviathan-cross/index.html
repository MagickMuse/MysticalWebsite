Leviathan Cross

◇Notes/Warnings
Also called the Crux Satana. In alchemy it is the symbol for sulfur.
◇Type

◇Culture

The Leviathan Cross is sometimes called the Crux Satana, since Anton LaVey adopted it as a symbol for his Church of Satan. There is no record, though, of any satanic meaning before then; earlier it was used by both the Knights Templar and the Cathars. In alchemy the Leviathan Cross stands for sulfur, one of the three essential elements of Nature alongside salt and mercury, or quicksilver.

The symbol is elaborate. Two bars cross the upright, signifying double protection and a balance of male and female. At its base sits the Infinity sign, or lemniscate, which also reads as a double ouroboros. The cross carries phallic connotations as well.
