Agnus Dei

Notes/Warnings
Also known as the Paschal Lamb. Symbol of Christian sacrifice and redemption.
Type
Emblem & Device
Culture
Gnostic/Early Christian
Correspondances2
🛡️Protection🚫Sacrifice

Agnus Dei translates as the Lamb of God, and is also known as the Paschal Lamb. It is symbolized pictorially as a lamb with a halo, proudly trotting along, carrying a banner and a cross.

Passover and Sacrifice

Lambs were commonly sacrificed during the time of the Passover, the blood sprinkled in the doorway or rubbed onto the lintel. The connection to the sacrifice of Christ established the lamb as one of Christianity's central symbols.

The Catholic Mass

Part of the Catholic mass includes the plea, repeated three times:

Lamb of God, who takes away the sins of the world, have mercy on us.
