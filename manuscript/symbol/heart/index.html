Runic Interpretation

Sensuality, eroticism, and love. Actually an ancient representation of female genitalia and buttocks.

Runic Context

Though commonly understood as a symbol of romantic love, the heart shape derives from a far older and more explicit origin: the stylized depiction of female genitalia and buttocks. In Old Norse books of magick, the sign appears frequently in spells of love magick as a symbol of sexual intercourse. Its use in runic talismanic work draws upon the raw, primal force of erotic attraction and physical union.
