2️⃣Numerological Day 2

Numerical Correlations

Letters: B, K, T

Planet: Moon

Element: Water

Influences: Sensitivity and Personality, Truth, Blessing, Duality, Balance

Divination Meaning

Tactful, conciliatory, even-tempered, diplomatic, persuasive, non-assertive, changeable, deceitful, cruel, malicious.

Numerology

Within numerology, two carries duality, balance, and separation. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, two marks germination.

Symbolism

The single vertical line of the 1 is joined by a second, forming the Roman numeral II. Held up to a mirror, the Arabic symbol for 2 takes on the shape of a heart, the universal emblem of love and, more specifically, of two people in love. The resemblance is pure coincidence, but a pleasing little trick all the same.

For all the sense that two is somehow better than one, it is also the number of division, expressed perfectly in the Bible:

And God made two great lights; the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night: he made the stars also. And God set them in the firmament of heaven to give light upon the Earth, and to rule over day and night, and to divide the light from the darkness. (Genesis 1:16-18)

Philosophical Traditions

The Pythagoreans saw 2, the Duad, as a symbol of opposition and of audacity, because it had the cheek to separate itself from the first number. Revering the Monad, they despised the 2 as a symbol of polarity. If 1 represents the Heavens, then 2 is the depths of the seas that reflect them, and so the 2 was capable of illusion and became associated with Maya, the Great Void. The magi carried mirrors as a reminder of this illusion, since reflection is another quality of the 2.

The number 2 gives us the positive and negative aspects of all things; indeed, without it, positive and negative themselves would not exist. Day and night, light and dark, good and bad, male and female, attraction and repulsion, life and death are all encompassed by it. Two is the number of balance, but also of conflict and division. It belongs to Eve, the first woman.

In the Yantric system, the number 2, Dwau, likewise symbolizes the two paths of opposites: the Vama Marga (left hand), which is materialistic, literal, and straightforward, and the Dakshina Marga (right hand), which is subtle, spiritual, and oblique.

Tarot

In the tarot, 2 is the number of the High Priestess, underlining the female, "yin" nature of a number that is sacred to all female deities.
