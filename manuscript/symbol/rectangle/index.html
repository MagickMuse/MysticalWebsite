Symbol Grimoire

Rectangle

Type
Shape
Culture
Universal

Symbolic Meaning

The rectangle represents binding, warding, and fortification. This shape harnesses the stability of four sides while the longer sides amplify warding potential.

Magickal Properties

  • Binding power - ideal for protection work involving restraint
  • Fortification - creates strong magickal barriers and boundaries
  • Temple protection - echoes ancient sacred architecture
  • Directional warding - longer sides provide extended protective reach

Associated Concepts

  • Binding and restraint magick
  • Temple and sacred space boundaries
  • Home fortification
  • Directional protection

Garden Magick

This is the best shape for protection gardens that deal with binding someone from harming you, especially great if your gardens are prone to field bindweed (Convolvulus arvensis). This shape allows you to harness the stability of four sides and the warding potential of the longer sides.

Think back to the Rue fences around the temples of Hestia. They were around the exterior of the temples, which often used straight lines rather than curved for their fortification.

Practical Applications:

  • If you can border a home with rectangular gardens, you will have a great magickal fence to keep your home in check and block out unwanted energies
  • Use binding herbs and protective plants along the longer sides for maximum warding effect
⚠️
About Bindweed: Though bindweed is a member of the Morning Glory family, this invasive weed will happily move into garden beds and choke the life out of anything growing there; it is the toughest weed to get rid of. It will eventually flower, but the flowers are white and aren't as showy or plentiful as colorful, domesticated morning glory.

To be clear: this is an invasive weed, not moon flower or morning glory; they just happen to have a family resemblance. True morning glory is part of the Ipomoea family, along with High John the Conquerer root and ornamental sweet potatoes.
