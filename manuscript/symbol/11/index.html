Symbolism of Eleven

Eleven is considered the number of woman, since the female body has eleven apertures, and the unborn child receives eleven divine powers through them. Numerologists regard eleven as a master number that should not be reduced down into a single digit.

Some traditions are suspicious of eleven. If ten is the number of completion and wholeness, then eleven carries the idea of excess and extravagance, an idea echoed in popular culture where a famous amplifier is said to go up to eleven. By coincidence, Apollo 11 was the first manned spacecraft to land on the Moon. The eleventh hour holds a sense of urgency, for since the clock runs to twelve, this is the last hour in which something can be done, and the First World War ended on the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month.

The magician Aleister Crowley was especially fond of eleven for its occult significance. The K that he added to the word magick enjoys special status because it is also the eleventh letter of the alphabet. The eleventh card of the Tarot is either Justice or Strength, and it stands at the midpoint of the numbered series of cards.
