Four Faces, One Structure

The witches' pyramid is a teaching structure rather than a physical object: a four-sided model for how personal magical power is built and sustained. Each face names one essential quality a practitioner needs, and traditional teaching holds that none of the four works well in isolation.

Imagination supplies the raw inner vision a working is built from. Will focuses and drives that vision toward a single outcome. Faith holds the whole structure steady against doubt. Secrecy protects the working, and the practitioner, once it's underway.

The four faces are explored individually as their own correspondences: Imagination, Willpower, Faith, and Secrecy.
