Symbolism

Are you a triskaidekaphobe? If so, you are one of the many thousands, perhaps millions, who hold the number 13 to be unlucky. Some will not leave the house when the thirteenth of the month falls on a Friday. The number is sometimes left off door numbers, and many buildings skip the thirteenth floor altogether, labeling it 14, so deep-rooted is the superstition.

Thirteen has long been seen as unlucky or inauspicious. The Kabbalah, for instance, speaks of 13 spirits of evil. It is still thought unlucky to have 13 people sit down to dine, a reminder of the Last Supper, where Christ was betrayed by one of the 12 disciples eating with him, Judas Iscariot.

A Lucky Side

It is not all darkness for 13. Although the year is divided into 12 calendar months, there are in fact 13 lunar months, 13 sets of 28 days in a year, which is why the Mayans regarded 13 as auspicious. A secret, hidden thirteenth sign of the Zodiac is also said to exist: Arachne, the spider whose web binds the network of the Heavens.

In Classical Antiquity the thirteenth member of a group was considered the leader, the exalted one. Zeus sits at the head of the 12 Olympian deities, just as Christ sat among his disciples and King Arthur among his twelve knights at the Round Table. A coven consists of 12 members plus a leader. A baker's dozen, too, is one more than 12, so 13 stands for good value: the custom comes from medieval England, where bread was scarce and bakers who short-changed customers faced harsh punishment, so they gave away a free loaf with every twelve sold to be safe.

Modern Echoes

The Apollo 13 Moon mission was beset by misfortune, and an explosion could have ended in disaster, yet no one died and the crew returned to Earth safely, so the number could be called lucky for them. The number 13 appears in several places on United States currency, with no sinister meaning at all: there were simply 13 states in the original Union. In the tarot, 13 is the number of Death, which represents initiation and the facing of fears, perhaps even the fear of the number 13 itself.
