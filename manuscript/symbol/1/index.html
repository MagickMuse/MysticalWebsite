👑Festival of Juno Sospita📅Numerological Day 1

Numerical Correlations

Letters: A, J, S

Element: Fire

Influences: Developing the Self, the All, Beginning and Ending, Wholeness and Unity

Divination Meaning

Fixed in purpose, single-minded, powerful, obstinate, ambitious, independent, self-sufficient, assertive, self-reliant, inventive, creative.

Numerology

Within numerology, one carries the oneness of all things and the individual. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, one marks the seed, the opening stage in the turning of the seasons and humanity's progress across the Earth.

Symbolism

The straight line is among the simplest of all basic symbols. A person standing upright is the perfect picture of the number 1, since humans are the only creatures that naturally stand erect on two feet. The figure is a graphic reminder that we are one of a kind, unique in the animal kingdom. One is the number of confidence and of the One God, yet it also expresses the "one-ness" of the many. It belongs to Adam, the mythological first man, and it is the World Tree and the Axis Mundi. Turned on its side, the line becomes the horizon.

Generally One is the number of the individual and the leader. It is usually read as a male, "yang," solar number, which makes it the number of the autocrat and the dictator as much as that of the divine. Standing alone, at ease with its own individuality, it symbolizes the ego and the self.

Philosophical Traditions

Followers of Plato described 1 as "the summit of the many." Strictly speaking, the Pythagoreans did not regard 1 or 2 as numbers at all. One school of thought associated 1 with the Monad, the "noble number, Sire of Gods and Men," the whole that is made of many parts. To these philosophers it represented the mind, the intellect, and hermaphrodism, since the 1 was both male and female. Full of potential, it effectively gives birth to all the other numbers.

In the Yantric system of the Hindus, One, or ekah, stands for the universality of spirit called the Para Brahman, an energy beyond the laws of time and space. Despite the multitude of deities in the Hindu world, the One is the unifying spirit that binds them all into a single energy.

Character & Tarot

The phrase "odd one out" captures the eccentric and the maverick, the person who lives on the fringes of society as a matter of course. One is the number of the only child and of the questioner who challenges rules built around the needs of the many rather than the individual. It favors "out of the box" thinking and lateral solutions. In the tarot, 1 is the number of the Magician, the secretive loner with the power to amaze and confound us.
