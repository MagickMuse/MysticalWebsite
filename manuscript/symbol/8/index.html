📅Numerological Day 8

Numerical Correlations

Letters: H, Q, Z

Planet: Saturn

Element: Earth

Influences: Freedom, Dual Foundations, Material and Spiritual Worlds, Law, Self-discipline, Travel/News

Divination Meaning

Power, money, worldly involvement, practical, tough, cautious, tenacious, materialist, hard worker, curbed eccentricity, rebellious.

Numerology

Within numerology, eight carries eternity and infinity, along with justice. In the Yantra system of Hindu numerology, eight marks fruit.

Symbolism

In general, eight is read as a fortunate number, a symbol of cosmic harmony. Its eight directions, the four inter-cardinal points balancing the four cardinal ones, suggest a complete and ordered world. To the Pythagoreans the 8, or Ogdoad, was held sacred for several reasons: the cube has eight corners, and in their system eight was an "evenly even" number, the only one under 10, since it divides into 2 x 4 and again into 4 x 2. The serpents twisting up the staff of the caduceus trace figure-of-eight shapes.

World Traditions

There are eight trigrams in the I Ching that combine to make up its 64 hexagrams, itself 8 x 8. An important part of Buddhist doctrine is the eight Auspicious Items, the Ashtamangala. In the Yantric system, 8, or Ashta, is likewise auspicious, and Ashtanga yoga comprises eight limbs said to lead the practitioner toward the Divine.

In Hindu belief, each of the eight directions of the Earth is ruled by a God, and these eight divinities correspond to the eight petals of the Lotus, a reminder of the microcosm within the macrocosm. Eight qualities are said to belong to God: innocence, purity, self-knowledge, omniscience, freedom from impurities, benevolence, omnipotence, and bliss.

Infinity & Renewal

The figure 8 forms the lemniscate, the scientific sign for infinity, and it carries the same meaning on a more philosophical level, standing for the cycle of life, death, and rebirth. In the same way, the eighth day, following the six days of work and the seventh day of rest, marks a day of renewal and reinvigoration. In the tarot, 8 is the number of Justice, the first card of the second set of the major arcana, reinforcing the sense of 8 as a symbol of renewal.
