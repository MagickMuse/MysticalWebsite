Horns of Odin

◇Notes/Warnings
Used today as a sign of identity by followers of Asatru, a modern faith that honors the ancient pre-Christian Norse beliefs.

Odin

Overview

Norse legend tells of a magickal mead brewed from the blood of the wise god Kvasir; to drink it was to gain the god's wisdom. Odin won this drink, and the triple horns represent the three draughts he swallowed.

Symbolism

The horn is a masculine and phallic symbol, yet because it also serves as a container it takes in the feminine aspect as well. The triple horn appears in stone carvings above the heads of warriors, implying their reward in Valhalla, the hall of slain warriors that is Odin's home.

Modern Use

The symbol is now used as a mark of identity by followers of Asatru, a relatively modern religion that acknowledges the far older pre-Christian Norse beliefs.
