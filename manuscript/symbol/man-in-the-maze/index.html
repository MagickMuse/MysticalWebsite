Man in the Maze

Overview

The Man in the Maze is a Native American symbol, found in basketry and in Hopi silverwork. It shows a maze with the figure of a person poised either to enter or to leave it, representing the choices and decisions one faces along the journey of life. The center of the Labyrinth is dark, standing for the unknown world and the mysteries of initiation, while the outer area is light, the known world of familiar things.

A Living Tradition

This symbol belongs to the living traditions of the Hopi and other Native American peoples. These are closed Indigenous practices, best approached with respect rather than borrowed.
