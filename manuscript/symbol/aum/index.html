Symbolism

The Aum, or Om, is a living symbol that exists in varying forms and names across Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism, and Sikhism. It is sometimes translated as "So be it," which makes it close kin to the Hebrew "Amen" and the Awen of modern Druidry. The Aum is the symbol of The Word, the sound present at the Creation of the Universe; the yoga sutras of Patanjali declare that "God's voice is Aum." Spoken at the beginning and end of prayer, it is a chant that, used consciously, helps the chanter become part of the All. The idea of such a primal sound is found the world over.

Just as the symbol has three parts that join to form the whole, the Aum stands for the three gods Vishnu, Shiva, and Brahma. In yoga the sound becomes a meditation on the breath and on the nature of the Universe, drawing on every part of the lungs as it is intoned.

The Upkar

The Upkar shows how the Sikh "Ek Onkar" symbol can be gently reshaped into a sign of universal healing and protection. It serves as the emblem of a northern Indian hospital of the same name, and its meaning, "the welfare of all," joins the Red Cross of physical and medical care with the spiritual dimension of the Aum.
