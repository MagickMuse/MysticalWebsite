🕌Da'wah

↗Other Symbols2
☪️Bismillah🔢Gematria

Symbolism

Literally meaning "summons" or "call," the Da'wah is a secret method of incantation, a call to an entire mystical tradition, much as the Hebrew alphabet is far more than a simple series of letters. Working through the Arabic alphabet, each letter carries finely tuned associations with the names of God, with numbers, the elements, perfumes, planets, spirits, and angels, and each letter has its own nature. To master the Da'wah the adept must keep to a strict moral code and diet. Once its esoteric mysteries are mastered, they are said to confer a god-like power over the Universe.
