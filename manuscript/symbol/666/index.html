Symbolism

Do you suffer from hexakosioihexekontahexaphobia? If so, you fear the number 666, the so-called Number of the Beast, and you are not alone. This run of three sixes strikes fear into many who meet it, even prompting some to protect themselves with the sign of the cross, though they may not be sure why.

Other than as a natural occurrence, the number first appears in the First Book of Kings in the Old Testament, where it refers to the wealth that came to King Solomon, perhaps through his partnership with the Queen of Sheba. Much of the superstition, however, comes from the New Testament Book of Revelation:

Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast; for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six Hundred Threescore and Six. (Revelation 13:18)

The Antichrist & Modern Fears

Ever since, there have been countless theories about whom or what the description means. For Christians it became a catch-all idea of an Antichrist, a label applied at various times to enemies of the faith, among them the Emperors Nero and Domitian, Genghis Khan, Napoleon, and Hitler. Even the Roman Catholic Church and various popes have been named as the Beast.

More recent ideas speculate that the number is a code by which the Devil will brand people so they may buy or sell. Credit cards, barcodes, social security numbers, and microchip technology have all drawn the scrutiny of conspiracy theorists, with varying degrees of disbelief and paranoia. It is also worth noting that the atom of carbon, the basis of all life on Earth, is made up of 6 neutrons, 6 protons, and 6 electrons.

Occult & Esoteric Meanings

Those who enjoy playing at being dangerous or satanic have embraced the number, among them the self-styled Great Beast, Aleister Crowley, who wove 666 into his personal sigil and experimented with interpretations of the nickname, eventually rendering it in Greek as "Therion," whose Gematria, or numerical value, for "Great Beast" adds up to 666. Some heavy and death metal musicians share the fascination.

In the Kabbalah the satanic associations are ambivalent: 666 is the number of the solar demon Sorath, the opposite of the Archangel Michael, yet it is also held sacred as a depiction of the entire Universe. The darker associations do not give the full picture. The number belongs to Hakathriel, the Angel of the Diadem, and in Sacred Geometry it is called the magic square of the Sun, built so that the first 36 numbers total 111 along every horizontal, vertical, and diagonal, while the whole square sums to 666. Its significance predates the Bible.

A Number of Man

The architects of Chartres Cathedral may have known this older meaning, since the Labyrinth there measures exactly 666 feet long. Numerologically, 666 reduces to 18, and then to 9, the number of man, so perhaps the simplest reading lies there: 666 stands for the material, "beast" side of man rather than the spiritual. Finally, in China the number spoken aloud sounds like "things going smoothly," which has made it one of the luckiest numbers in Chinese culture, used on banners and good-luck cards.
