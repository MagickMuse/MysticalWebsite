Numerology & Colors

Life Path Numbers & Personal Colors

In numerology, your Life Path number reveals your soul's purpose in this incarnation. This number, derived from your birth date, corresponds to a specific color that carries significance throughout your lifetime. Unlike colors you choose based on circumstance or need, your Life Path color is an unchangeable signature of your spiritual journey.

Prosperity Stones by Life Path Number

Beyond the general prosperity stones that anyone can carry, each Life Path number has a specific crystal that attracts wealth and abundance aligned with that path's unique energy signature. These stones have been traditionally associated with financial success for their corresponding numbers.

  • Life Path 1: Ruby
  • Life Path 2: Carnelian
  • Life Path 3: Yellow Citrine
  • Life Path 4: Emerald
  • Life Path 5: Sodalite
  • Life Path 6: Azurite
  • Life Path 7: Amethyst
  • Life Path 8: Rose Quartz
  • Life Path 9: Tiger's Eye
  • Life Path 11: Hematite
  • Life Path 22: Gold Topaz

Working With Your Prosperity Stone

Carry your prosperity stone close to your skin, in your pocket, or wear it as jewelry. Allow it to receive plenty of sunlight to charge its energy. Place it on your altar during wealth workings to amplify your intentions.

Daily Affirmation Practice:

Hold your prosperity stone in your hand at least once a day while speaking positive affirmations aloud. Affirmations are phrases repeated in the present tense, as if you already possess the quality you seek. This instills the thought pattern in your mind and programs the stone with your intention.

Create affirmations that resonate with your specific goals and circumstances. Here are a few examples to get you started:

  • I create wealth and abundance.
  • I gratefully accept my share from an abundant universe.
  • Prosperity flows to me all the time.
  • All the money I spend returns to me multiplied.

Your prosperity stone works in harmony with your soul's purpose to draw prosperity that supports your life's mission. Through regular contact, sunlight charging, and affirmation work, you attune the crystal to your unique energetic signature and financial goals.

Calculating Your Life Path Number

Add together your birth month, day, and year, then reduce to a single digit. Exception: Stop reducing when you reach 11 or 22, these are Master Numbers with special significance.

Calculation Method

To preserve Master Numbers, calculate in stages rather than adding all digits in a straight line:

  1. Month (as single or double digit)
  2. Day (as single or double digit)
  3. Year (add all four digits together, reduce if needed)
  4. Add these three sums together
  5. Reduce to single digit (unless 11 or 22)

Example 1: July 12, 1973

  • Month: 7
  • Day: 12
  • Year: 1 + 9 + 7 + 3 = 20, then 2 + 0 = 2
  • Total: 7 + 12 + 2 = 21, then 2 + 1 = 3

Life Path: 3 (Yellow)

Example 2: February 29, 1944

  • Month: 2
  • Day: 29
  • Year: 1 + 9 + 4 + 4 = 18, then 1 + 8 = 9
  • Total: 2 + 29 + 9 = 40, then 4 + 0 = 4

Wait, let's recalculate using the sum method to check for Master Numbers:

  • 2 + 29 + 1944 = 1975
  • 1 + 9 + 7 + 5 = 22

Life Path: 22 (Gold), Master Number preserved

Why this method matters: If you add straight across (2 + 2 + 9 + 1 + 9 + 4 + 4 = 31, then 3 + 1 = 4), you lose the Master Number 22 hidden in the calculation. The staged method reveals Master Numbers that would otherwise disappear.

Your Unchangeable Personal Colors

Life Path Color: Fixed by your birth date, this color reflects your soul's purpose. You may love it or resist it, but it remains your spiritual signature throughout this incarnation.

Birth Day Color: The day of the month you were born (reduced to a single digit, unless you were born on the 11th, 22nd, or 29th) also corresponds to a personal color. This secondary color adds another layer to your numerological profile.

These colors can be incorporated into your wardrobe, altar decorations, candle magick, and visualization practices to align more deeply with your soul path.

Expression Number, Your Natural Talents

While your Life Path reveals your soul's purpose, your Expression Number illuminates your innate abilities and natural talents. This number is calculated from your full birth name and shows the gifts you brought into this incarnation.

Letter-to-Number Conversion Chart

Each letter corresponds to a number from 1-9:

123456789
ABCDEFGHI
JKLMNOPQR
STUVWXYZ

Calculation Method

  1. Convert each letter in your full birth name to its corresponding number
  2. Add the values for each name separately (first, middle, last)
  3. Reduce each name total to a single digit (or Master Number 11/22)
  4. Add those reduced values together
  5. Reduce final sum to single digit (or Master Number)

Example: Bradley Sebastian Smith

First Name, Bradley:

  • B(2) + R(9) + A(1) + D(4) + L(3) + E(5) + Y(7) = 31
  • 3 + 1 = 4

Middle Name, Sebastian:

  • S(1) + E(5) + B(2) + A(1) + S(1) + T(2) + I(9) + A(1) + N(5) = 27
  • 2 + 7 = 9

Last Name, Smith:

  • S(1) + M(4) + I(9) + T(2) + H(8) = 24
  • 2 + 4 = 6

Final Calculation:

  • 4 + 9 + 6 = 19
  • 1 + 9 = 10
  • 1 + 0 = 1

Expression Number: 1 (Red)

Bradley's Expression Number of 1 indicates natural independence, enthusiasm, and ambition. Red, the color of Life Path 1, reinforces these pioneering qualities throughout his life.

Soul Urge Number, Your Inner Desires

While Life Path reveals your purpose and Expression shows your talents, your Soul Urge Number exposes your deepest motivations and heart's true desires. This number is calculated from the vowels in your full birth name, revealing what drives you at the soul level.

Identifying Vowels: The Y Rule

Standard vowels are A, E, I, O, U. The letter Y requires special consideration:

  • Count Y as a vowel when: It's silent or creates a vowel sound (Bradley, Carolyn, Yvette)
  • Do NOT count Y as a vowel when: It's pronounced as a consonant at the beginning of a name (Yolande, Yusuf, Yasmin)
  • Names with multiple Y's: Some surnames contain two Y's, one vowel, one consonant (Yancey, Yockney). Evaluate each Y individually.

Calculation Method

  1. Identify all vowels in your full birth name (including Y when appropriate)
  2. Convert each vowel to its number using the chart above
  3. Add vowel values for each name separately (first, middle, last)
  4. Reduce each name total to a single digit (or Master Number 11/22)
  5. Add those reduced values together
  6. Reduce final sum to single digit (or Master Number)

Example: Bradley Sebastian Smith

First Name, Bradley:

  • A(1) + E(5) + Y(7) = 13
  • 1 + 3 = 4

(The Y is silent/forms vowel sound, so it counts)

Middle Name, Sebastian:

  • E(5) + A(1) + I(9) + A(1) = 16
  • 1 + 6 = 7

Last Name, Smith:

  • I(9) = 9

Final Calculation:

  • 4 + 7 + 9 = 20
  • 2 + 0 = 2

Soul Urge Number: 2 (Orange)

Bradley's Soul Urge of 2 reveals a deep desire for connection, harmony, and diplomatic relationships. He finds fulfillment through cooperation and bringing people together. Orange, the color of peaceful interaction, reflects his soul's true yearning.

Your Soul Urge Number shares the same color correspondences as Life Path and Expression:

Your Personal Color Signature

Between Life Path, Expression, and Soul Urge, you now have up to three personal colors that form your numerological signature:

Life Path = Your soul's purpose and journey

Expression = Your natural talents and outer gifts

Soul Urge = Your inner desires and motivations

These colors may be identical (creating a unified, focused energy) or different (creating a multi-layered color palette for your magickal work). When all three numbers share the same color, that hue becomes exceptionally significant in your life. When they differ, you're working with complementary energies that add depth and nuance to your spiritual practice.

Incorporate these colors into candle work, altar cloths, visualization practices, wardrobe choices, and crystal selections to align with your numerological blueprint.

Day of Birth Number

Your Day of Birth number adds a fourth personal color to your numerological profile. Simply reduce the day you were born to a single digit (unless it's 11, 22, or 29).

Complete Example: Bradley Sebastian Smith

Born August 4, 1975:

  • Life Path: 7 (Violet), soul purpose
  • Expression: 1 (Red), natural talents
  • Soul Urge: 2 (Orange), inner desires
  • Day of Birth: 4 (Green), additional personal influence

Working With Your Four Colors

Each of your personal colors serves a specific function:

  • Life Path color (Violet): Use when working on something that plays a major role in your life purpose
  • Expression color (Red): Use when you want to excel at something or develop your talents
  • Soul Urge color (Orange): Use when you want to make something more enjoyable or fulfill your desires
  • Day of Birth color (Green): Use anytime, this is a supportive, always-available energy

All four colors are part of your energetic makeup. Each will prove stimulating and invigorating when you work with them consciously.

Name Variations & Shifting Colors

Few people are known by their full birth name in every context. Nicknames, shortened forms, and signature variations create different Expression and Soul Urge numbers, which changes your active color palette.

How Name Changes Affect Colors

Bradley Sebastian Smith (full birth name):

  • Expression: 1 (Red)
  • Soul Urge: 2 (Orange)

Brad Smith (nickname):

  • Expression: 4 (Green)
  • Soul Urge: 1 (Red)
  • Effect: More green reflected, orange eliminated from active palette

Bradley S. Smith (signature form):

  • Expression: 2 (Orange)
  • Soul Urge: 1 (Red)
  • Effect: All four original colors maintained, an excellent everyday name

The Permanence of Birth Name Colors

In numerology, it's believed that each soul chooses their birth name to receive specific experiences needed for growth in this incarnation. The colors from your birth name never entirely disappear, regardless of name changes. Your birth name colors form your foundational palette, while name variations temporarily emphasize different aspects of that foundation.

Missing Numbers & Colors

The individual letters in your full birth name reveal an even more detailed color portrait. By analyzing which numbers (1-9) appear in your name, you discover which colors are present and which are missing.

Analysis Method

  1. Convert every letter in your full birth name to its number
  2. Note which numbers from 1-9 appear in your name
  3. Identify which numbers are absent
  4. Those missing numbers indicate colors you need to consciously incorporate

Example: Bradley Sebastian Smith

Letter breakdown:

  • B(2), R(9), A(1), D(4), L(3), E(5), Y(7)
  • S(1), E(5), B(2), A(1), S(1), T(2), I(9), A(1), N(5)
  • S(1), M(4), I(9), T(2), H(8)

Numbers present: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9

Missing: 6 (Indigo)

Bradley would benefit from consciously adding indigo to his life, wearing indigo clothing, visualizing indigo, or working with indigo crystals. This would help him feel more whole and complete at a subconscious level.

Example: Wendy Fox

Letter breakdown:

  • W(5), E(5), N(5), D(4), Y(7)
  • F(6), O(6), X(6)

Numbers present: 4, 5, 6, 7

Missing: 1 (Red), 2 (Orange), 3 (Yellow), 8 (Pink), 9 (Bronze)

However, Wendy's Expression number is 8 and her Soul Urge is 9, which means pink and bronze aren't entirely missing. She would benefit most from consciously absorbing red, orange, and yellow, the three colors completely absent from both her name letters and core numbers.

Color Balance Analysis

Beyond simply identifying missing colors, you can assess whether each color is balanced, deficient, or excessive in your birth name. This reveals patterns in how you express (or struggle with) each color's qualities.

Ideal Balance Guidelines

For an 18-letter name, the ideal distribution is:

  • Red (1): 3 occurrences
  • Orange (2): 2 occurrences
  • Yellow (3): 2 occurrences
  • Green (4): 2 occurrences
  • Blue (5): 2 occurrences
  • Indigo (6): 2 occurrences
  • Violet (7): 1 occurrence
  • Pink (8): 2 occurrences
  • Bronze (9): 2 occurrences

For names with different lengths, scale these proportions accordingly. A color is considered low if it appears less than expected, balanced if near the ideal, and high if it appears significantly more than expected.

Balanced: Work well with others; cooperative, diplomatic, open, honest, and self-assured

Low/Absent: Oversensitive; struggle to cooperate easily with others

High: Highly emotional and overly reliant on others for validation

Yellow (3), Expression & Communication

Balanced: Outgoing, sociable, expressive; desire to communicate creatively

Low/Absent: Struggle to express yourself; retiring or shy nature; anxious and lacking enthusiasm

Life Lessons Through Color

Color imbalances in your name represent lessons your soul chose to learn in this incarnation. People often repeat the same patterns until they finally integrate the lesson a color is teaching them.

By consciously working with your missing or imbalanced colors, wearing them, visualizing them, surrounding yourself with them, you accelerate your growth and feel more whole. This is not about fixing defects but about honoring the full spectrum of your being.

Color of the Day

You can apply the Life Path calculation method to any specific date to discover that day's color. This practice offers a dynamic way to experiment with color energy and observe how different hues influence your mood, energy, and behavior.

How to Calculate

Use the same method as Life Path calculation:

  1. Add the month + day + year of any date
  2. Reduce to a single digit (or Master Number 11/22)
  3. Note the corresponding color

Monthly Experiment

Try wearing or carrying the color of each day for a month. Observe how different colors affect you:

  • Extroverts might notice themselves becoming quieter and more contemplative on violet (7) days
  • Introverts might find themselves more outgoing and bold on red (1) days
  • Everyone will notice subtle shifts in energy, mood, and self-expression

This experiment teaches you firsthand how each color feels in your system, which colors energize you, which calm you, and which challenge you.

Intentional Color Use

After experimenting with daily colors, you can make conscious wardrobe and decor choices to support who you're becoming:

If you're shy or retiring: Add red or orange to boost confidence and assertiveness

If your work demands mental focus: Incorporate yellow to enhance intellect and clarity

If you need to collaborate: Wear orange to improve diplomacy and cooperation

If you're seeking wisdom: Choose violet for introspection and spiritual connection

This isn't artifice, it's alchemy. You're not pretending to be someone you're not. You're using color as a tool to amplify qualities you want to develop and embody qualities that serve your highest expression.

Beyond Personal Colors

While your Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, and Day of Birth colors reveal core aspects of your being, they don't tell the complete story. All nine colors (plus Master Number colors) exist within your energetic field. Your personal colors are simply the most prominent frequencies in your natal blueprint.

Working with color of the day allows you to consciously explore the full spectrum, developing relationship with each hue and discovering how they support different facets of your life and work.
