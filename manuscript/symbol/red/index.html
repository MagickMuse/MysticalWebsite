Red is the color of blood, of birth and death. In many cultures it has been sacred, or dedicated to the deities. Red stones are projective and active. They are related to the planet Mars and the element of Fire, both aggressive energies.

These are protective stones and work to strengthen the body and will power. Red stones are used to promote courage, to lend energy to the body, and to provide additional power to rituals by their presence on the altar.

In ancient times, red stones were worn as antidotes to poison, to keep one's thoughts pure, and, by drawing out the causes, to banish anger and all violent emotions. They were also used as a guard against fire and lightning.

Healing

In healing, red stones are intimately linked with blood. They are often worn to relieve anemia, to halt bleeding and to heal wounds. They also seem to work on rashes and inflammation. Perhaps in association with its blood links, red stones were once carried to prevent miscarriage.

Red stones can be empowered and used to overcome sexual dysfunctions, usually by placing the stone near or on the genitals while visualizing.

Color of Life and Blood

Red is the color of life itself, the hue of blood flowing through living bodies. It carries the essence of vitality, stimulation, enthusiasm, energy, and passion. To be "red-blooded" is to embody these very qualities. Red pulses with intensity and a fierce desire to achieve. It is the color of love and romance, which is why red flowers dominate Valentine's Day and why poets write of love as "a red, red rose."

Positive Qualities

When used with positive intent, red expresses creativity, ambition, persistence, and motivation. It is fun loving and full of life. Red symbolizes strength, power, and self-confidence. It is the color of conquest and success, which is why we roll out red carpets for dignitaries and mark important occasions as "red-letter days." A red sports car embodies all of red's dynamic qualities: achievement, desire, boldness, and the thrill of success.

Religious Art

Red symbolizes divine energy, love, and the Holy Spirit. When used negatively, red also signifies blood, enmity, and punishment.

Jesus and Mary: Jesus and the Virgin Mary usually wear a red tunic and a blue mantle. This signifies love (red) and truth (blue).

White and Red Roses: In religious art, white and red roses are a sign of innocence and spiritual love.

Negative Associations

When red manifests negatively, it becomes destructive. An enraged person "sees red." To be caught "red-handed" is to be discovered in the act of wrongdoing, the phrase itself conjuring images of blood on guilty hands. In seventeenth-century New England, adulteresses wore a scarlet letter A as a mark of shame and disgrace. The Bible speaks of sin as scarlet and crimson, colors that must be washed white as snow. Because red commands immediate attention, it appears on stop signs and danger warnings, signaling threat and caution.

Red hair and red beards once carried suspicion, as Judas Iscariot was believed to have had red hair.

Cultural and Historical Significance

The word red derives from the Sanskrit rudhira and the Anglo-Saxon read. Red is associated with Mars, the planet named for the Roman god of war who drove a red chariot across the sky. Red became the color of the Russian Revolution, giving communists the name "Reds."

Cardinals wear red vestments to honor the blood of Christian martyrs. Ancient Greeks wore red robes when performing the Iliad to remember the blood spilled in battle. Medieval alchemists believed the legendary philosopher's stone, which could cure disease and transform base metals into gold, was red in color.

For the Hebrew people, red represents sacrifice and sin. For Christians, it symbolizes the blood of Christ and was sometimes worn by martyrs.

Practical Applications

Red stimulates the pituitary gland and speeds up the body's metabolism, making it useful for those seeking to lose weight. Insurance records show that red cars are statistically more likely to be involved in accidents than cars of other colors. However, tradition also holds that tying a red ribbon inside a new car promotes luck and safe driving.

Color Healing Properties

Red is stimulating and exciting. It revitalizes the entire body. It activates the blood and the senses. Consequently, it can be used with problems concerning sight, sound, smell, taste, and touch. It is also good for muscular problems and the liver.

Anemia, asthma, bronchitis, colds (as long as there is no fever), circulation problems, constipation, depression, endocrine gland problems, paralysis, pneumonia, reproductive problems, tuberculosis, varicose veins

Red promotes energy, empowerment, and stimulation. Physically, it is thought to improve circulation and stimulate the production of red blood cells.

Norse Runic Symbolism

In the Norse runic tradition, red signifies magickal might and main, protective power, spiritual life and vigor, and aggressive force. It is the principal color of the runes and also carries an association with death. Red is often linked to gold in the runic color system.

Personality of Red

Red attracts extroverts and those seeking to cultivate extroverted qualities. People drawn to red are outgoing, ambitious, and hardworking. They thrive on competition and physical challenges, driven to achieve their goals. Those who favor red possess dramatic flair and know how to command attention. They are optimistic and forward-thinking, generous in spirit, warm in affection, and quick to forgive. Impulsiveness marks their actions. They possess natural leadership qualities, combining drive with charisma.

Aversion to Red

Disliking red indicates a need to develop emotional regulation skills. While emotional outbursts may provide temporary relief from anger, they disturb and upset those around you. Learning to control and channel these emotions constructively becomes essential.

Missing Red

An absence of red signals exhaustion. You may be ill or overtired. Improve your diet, ensure adequate sleep, and gradually raise energy levels through exercise.

Supportive Uses of Red

Red eliminates negative thoughts and counteracts low energy. It conquers fatigue. Wear red when experiencing frustration, apathy, or hopelessness. Red stimulates and delivers confidence, strength, stamina, enthusiasm, and persistence. It dismantles inhibitions and restores joy and happiness. Choose red for any occasion demanding success. Red proves valuable during wartime for its courage-giving properties. After illness, red generates vitality and energy for recovery.

Candle Magick Applications

Use red when your goal involves courage, strength, sexual vitality, or significant life changes. Men should work with red to address sexual difficulties or dysfunctions. Red enhances vitality and activates the will to manifest transformation.

Dark Magick Applications

In baneful workings, red candles bring sudden, violent results in contrast to the slow destruction associated with black. Red channels accidents, fires, injuries, bloodshed, and hatred. It fuels wars, anarchy, and cruelty. Red is also used to invoke raw power and intensity as a prelude to other destructive workings. Where black erodes over time, red strikes fast and hard. Mars rules red, and baneful workings with this color are most potent on a Tuesday when Mars is well positioned and waxing.

Lunar Magick

Red channels the moon's primal current into rituals of passion, desire, and physical vitality. It is ideal for workings involving courage, action, and bodily strength. Use it also to repel unwanted energies or to reclaim personal power under the waning or dark moon.

Color in Your Home

Red is a stimulating, opulent color that creates movement, activity, and excitement. As it stimulates the appetite and conversation, it can be a good choice for dining rooms. It also works well in hallways and playrooms. It is rich, grand, and powerful, creating an impression of wealth. Red always demands attention. It should not be used in children's bedrooms, as it can make it hard to fall asleep.
