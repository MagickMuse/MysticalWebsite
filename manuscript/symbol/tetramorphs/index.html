Overview

The prophet Ezekiel saw a vision of four figures that the Book of Revelation later calls the Four Beasts: the bull, the lion, the eagle, and the man. Collectively these creatures are known as the Tetramorphs, and they carry several layers of meaning.

The Four Evangelists

The most common reading links them to the four evangelists. The man is St. Matthew, the lion is St. Mark, the bull is St. Luke, and the eagle is St. John. The animals also stand for the four pillars of the Christian faith and the omnipotence of God.

Elements and Directions

The Tetramorphs reach far beyond Christian tradition. The creatures that appeared to Ezekiel carry a universal resonance, shown by their presence in prehistoric cave paintings. The man, the bull, the lion, and the eagle map to the four points of the compass and the four elements. The eagle is air, holding all aspects of the mind and intellectual activity. The lion is fire, signifying action and strength. The bull is earth, representing labor and tenacity. The man is water and the idea of spiritual intuition.
