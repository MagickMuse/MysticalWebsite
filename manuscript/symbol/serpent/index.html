Symbol Grimoire

Serpent

Interpretation

Enclosure, containment, chthonic force, and the magickal unconscious.

Runic Context

The serpent or lingworm is one of the fundamental pictographs found in runic art and talismanic inscriptions. It represents the encircling force that binds and contains magickal energy, drawing upon the chthonic (subterranean) currents of power. As a symbol of the magickal unconscious, it points to the deep, hidden reservoirs of wisdom and primal force that underlie conscious awareness.
