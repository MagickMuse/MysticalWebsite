Compasses and Square

Overview

Compasses combined with the Mason's Square form one of the foremost emblems in Freemasonry. Some depictions also incorporate the letter G and may add solar and lunar elements.

Measurement and Meaning

Both tools are instruments of precise measurement used in disciplines that allow little room for error, including building, construction, and architecture. Because measuring tools define and delimit form, they can symbolize judgement and definition.

Union of Opposites

In this pairing, the circle created by the compasses is framed as feminine, while the square is framed as masculine. The union can be read as spirit combined with matter, and Earth combined with Heaven. Another reading frames the circle as space and the square as time, with the mason as the architect who works across these dimensions.

The Compasses

Because the compasses draw the perfect circle, they can signify a divine architect of the Universe. Since they turn on a central pivot, they can also symbolize the Axis Mundi, the circular nature of time, and the ouroboros.

The Square

The phrases on the square and on the level refer to being open, honest, proper, and above board. The square carries the symbolism of the number 4 and can represent solidity, respect, and security.

The Letter G

When the letter G appears within the emblem, its meaning varies. Readings include God, Geometry, Generation, Government, and the Great Bear as a marker of the celestial pole. There is no single definitive meaning.

The Six Pointed Star

Drawing a line across the open point of the square and compasses produces a six pointed star, also called the Seal of Solomon. The seal consists of two interlocking triangles and is one of the most ancient and widely used magickal symbols, including in alchemy, where one reading is as above, so below.
