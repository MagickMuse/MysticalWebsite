Troll Cross

◇Notes/Warnings
A protective amulet from Scandinavian folk tradition, worn to guard against trolls and other troublesome spirits. Despite the name it is not a conventional cross but a ring left open at the base in a C shape, traditionally forged from iron.

The troll cross is a folk charm worn for protection against trolls and similar troublesome beings. In spite of its name it looks little like an ordinary cross, resembling instead a ring or circle left open at the bottom in the shape of the letter C.

The amulet was made from iron, a metal long believed to repel mischievous and malevolent spirits. Forging the charm from iron therefore strengthened its guarding power twice over, since the material itself was thought to turn away the very creatures the charm was meant to ward off.
