Overview

The Kalachakra is an elaborate and deeply meaningful symbol, a major device of Tibetan Buddhism seen in monasteries and other holy places. It stands for the arcane knowledge into which relatively few lamas are initiated, and its meaning unfolds on many levels.

The seal is a calligraphic arrangement of letters in a script called Lantsa, or Ranjana, a sacred script developed from Sanskrit and used only for religious texts, mandalas, and mantras. Many holy documents in the Ranjana script were destroyed when China invaded Tibet.

Also called The Tenfold Powerful One, the seal hides within its curlicues the seven syllables of the holy Mantra known as the Kalachakra: "Ham Ksa Ma La Va Ra Ya." Three further elements give the symbol its tenfold nature: the crescent moon, the disc of the full Moon or Sun, and the flame of fire.

Composition

Symbol and sound are inseparable here, and the seal is often drawn in color, since the colors are themselves meaningful. The syllables are written one above another and interlock. The whole rests on a stylized lotus, emblem of the heart, with the characters for "emptiness" and "bliss" set to its left and right. The surrounding framework represents a mandala of flames known as the Circle of Wisdom.

The Syllables

An eighth element is never drawn but only implied: the letter A, emblematic of space. The seven written syllables, with the colors they may take, are:

  • Ham (blue): the formless realms, the vacuum, the spiritual world, bliss, enlightened wisdom, the gods.
  • Ksha (green): the world of form and desire, the material world; it stands for the body, the mind, and the power of speech.
  • Ma (multicolored): a reminder of the holy Mount Meru, linked to the spinal column, which ascends toward the Heavens like the mountain.
  • La (yellow): the grounding element of earth.
  • Va (white): the element of water.
  • Ra (red): the element of fire.
  • Ya (black): the element of air.

Together these elements represent the spiritual and material life of humanity, the elements, the Wheel of Time, the emptiness of the void, and the pregnant seed of creation. The symbol has even inspired the calculations of calendars and astrologers.
