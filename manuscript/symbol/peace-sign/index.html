Peace Sign

◇Notes/Warnings
The CND symbol. Designed by Gerald Holtom in 1958 from the figure of a person in despair with arms lowered. Often misread as an inverted cross or a runic sign for death.

Overview

This circular sign with a downward forked cross is recognized worldwide as a peace symbol and as the emblem of the Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament.

Origin

Gerald Holtom designed it for the CND. He first tried a Christian cross inside the circle, but objections led him to the shape of a human figure standing in despair, arms outstretched downward in a pleading gesture.

Misinterpretations

Because it loosely resembles an inverted cross, some have wrongly tied it to the Cross of Nero and to Satanism. Others mistook it for a neo-Nazi runic sign. In truth the rune they had in mind simply means "elk," not "death," so the supposed sinister meaning was never there.
