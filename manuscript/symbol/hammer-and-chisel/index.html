Hammer and Chisel

◇Notes/Warnings
Usually shown working the rough ashlar, the stone that the tools shape and perfect.
◇Tradition / Origin
Freemason
◇Type
Emblem & Device
◇Culture
Freemasonry
↗Other Symbols1
Ashlar

Overview

In lodge meetings, the hammer represents the powers of intellect that drive thought and will. It also serves as a symbol of authority for the Worshipful Master who presides over the lodge.

The Chisel

The chisel is useful only when directed by the hammer. It can represent intellect and the finer discernment that shapes rough material into refined form.
