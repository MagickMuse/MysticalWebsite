18🔢Gematria

Symbolism

Often seen today on souvenirs and artwork from Israel, the Chai is a secret symbol made up of the Hebrew letters Het and Yod. Together they spell "chai," meaning "living," a word that refers not only to life itself but also to the Jewish people. In Gematria, the Jewish system of numerology, these letters add up to 18, a number held to be favorable, and so the Chai also serves as a lucky charm.
