Born on March 2nd

The birthday of personal vision

Profile

People born on March 2 have strong convictions and a personal vision they will pursue with unwavering loyalty, regardless of the opinions of others or the shifting climate around them. They are truly independent thinkers with the ability to inspire — and occasionally alarm — others with their intensity.

Once those born on this day have committed to an ideal or decided on a course of action, they will hold fast to it. They can occasionally take this to an extreme, blocking out everything and everyone else. Although others have much to learn from their dedication, they can develop a one-track mind and a tendency to deny themselves opportunities that could enrich their work. It is important for them to make sure their personal convictions neither exclude the possibility of change nor alienate them from the closeness and security of personal relationships. They need to be especially careful of this between the ages of eighteen and forty-eight, when their personal vision is most likely to dominate their lives.

The vision March 2 people dedicate themselves to so passionately is often one that tries to make positive changes to their world or environment. This is challenge enough, but by far the greatest test for these devoted souls is balancing their personal needs with the needs of the world. If they are unable to find that balance, the people most likely to suffer are those closest to them. They can become the politicians or the campaigners devoted to their cause who are never there for their loved ones, or the artists and writers so absorbed in their work that they neglect their family. If, however, they find a way to bring harmony to both their personal lives and the wider world, their unwavering dedication gives them the potential to be powerful forces for positive change and progress.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with confrontation.

The Way Forward

Be more relaxed and pragmatic in your approach and do not shy away from conflict; conflict is inevitable, but it can encourage creativity, change, and progress.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You both like to put your partner on a pedestal. Take turns doing this to create a loyal and satisfying union.

Luck Maker: Keep Making New Friends

Not only will this promote your self-esteem, but having lots of friends and acquaintances, all with their own ideas, contacts, and talents, is a great way to increase your chances of luck.

At Your Best

Loyal, trusting, purposeful.

On the Dark Side

Inflexible, escapist, needy.

Love: Only for You

When March 2 natives fall in love it is an undying and devoted love, but their unstinting adoration of their partner, children, or anyone who inspires them can run the risk of stifling them. It is important for them to develop a more detached and independent attitude toward both their work and their personal lives.

Health: Get Out There

People born on this day have a tendency to isolate themselves, and this can have a negative effect on their health and well-being. They need to make sure they get out into the world, as they have a lot to offer it. All kinds of exercise involving other people, such as team sports or aerobics classes, will benefit them. As far as diet goes, they need to steer clear of too much alcohol and make sure they eat plenty of fresh, natural whole grains and vegetables. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to seek out warmth and physical contact with others.

Career: Born Charity Workers

These people need to make career plans that include their natural sense of vision. The medical and healing professions may be of interest, as might teaching, politics, writing, social reform, or charity work. They may also choose to express their creative vision through music, theater, or art.

Destiny: To Use Their Vision to Make the World a Better Place

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to give more of themselves to others. Once they reach outside themselves, their destiny is to translate their personal vision into a reality and make the world a better, more enlightened place.

Power Thought

I will always ask for the help that I need.
