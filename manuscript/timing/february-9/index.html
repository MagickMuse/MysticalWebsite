Born on February 9th

The birthday of the winning attitude

Profile

People born on February 9 are independent and generous individuals with their own unique and at times nonconformist perspective on life. They are shrewd observers of human nature, capable of enormous understanding of others' problems. Above all, they are fighters; life may have dealt them a few hard knocks, but they have always managed to bounce back with resilience, and this winning attitude can lead them to great achievements.

The remarkable ability of February 9 natives to understand people and situations, even those they have not personally experienced, makes them much sought after for advice and support. They make great teachers and leaders, and they influence and inspire not so much by technique but by example, showing others through their own actions how to rise above challenges.

Yet when it comes to applying that same penetrating insight to their own lives and relationships, they tend to be overly critical, measuring themselves against an impossibly high standard. It is important for them to learn to be as tolerant and supportive of themselves as they are of others. Before the age of forty, the needs and approval of others tend to dominate, but at forty a turning point arrives that places greater emphasis on self-awareness and acceptance, as well as a need to take the initiative in all areas of life, possibly with a new relationship or venture.

The strong presence of those born on this day can sometimes make others perceive them as aggressive, but behind that exterior lies a soft side that takes rejection and criticism deeply to heart. They also have a tendency to act rashly and need to remain calm under pressure while guarding against having their goodwill exploited. Once they are able to regard themselves more positively and ease up on their self-criticism, they can achieve the high goals they set, becoming an inspirational model for everyone lucky enough to cross their path.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning not to beat yourself up for some perceived weakness.

The Way Forward

When you do stumble, talk to yourself as though you were a small child: be positive, supportive, and gentle.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You share a passion for intensity and the unconventional, and this creates an exciting match.

Luck Maker: Keep Your Cool

Keeping your cool creates luck because others will respect you, meaning you are less likely to accumulate enemies. Lucky people, by definition, have few.

At Your Best

Powerful, generous, resilient.

On the Dark Side

Inconsistent, long-suffering, anxious.

Love: In for the Long Haul

February 9 natives have the charisma to attract people from all walks of life, but they feel happiest with someone whose intelligence matches their own. In a relationship they are loyal, passionate partners with a clear vision of where the partnership should be headed. They do not give up easily if a relationship starts to falter, because they believe that with the right attitude almost any problem can be worked through.

Health: Time for You

People born on this day are often blessed with boundless energy, so unless they give in to cravings for comfort food or neglect self-care, they are unlikely to struggle with their weight. If weight issues do arise, it is usually because they have been neglecting themselves or have dipped into depression or anxiety. Eating little and often is important, as is steering clear of excessive alcohol and mood-altering substances. A regular exercise routine is recommended to keep their metabolism high. Breathing exercises and meditation are also beneficial, and if they are feeling low, a few drops of jasmine essential oil on a handkerchief to breathe in will help them see the sunnier side.

Career: Born Mentors

These people are natural mentors and messengers who make great teachers, advisors, counselors, consultants, therapists, and psychologists. Their multi-talented personality may lead them into many different careers and in many cases more than one over a lifetime. Science and social reform offer freedom and growth, but other possible paths include the arts, theater, design, property development, technology, and careers involving travel and change such as aviation, navigation, and international business.

Destiny: To Inspire Others Toward Worthwhile Causes

The life path of those born on this day is to be less self-critical. When they learn to be more positive about themselves, their destiny is to lead and inspire others with their winning attitude, devoting their energies to a worthy cause.

Power Thought

I will always seek the possible, not the obligatory.
