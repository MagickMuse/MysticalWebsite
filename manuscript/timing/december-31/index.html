Born on December 31st

The birthday of the connoisseur

Profile

Those born on December 31 take genuine pride in their impeccable taste, and their confidence and natural charisma attract admirers wherever they go. Esthetes and idealists at heart, they aim for nothing less than perfection, yet their practical side gives them enough common sense to acknowledge the flaws in the world around them.

These individuals are driven to bring beauty and refinement into every environment they inhabit, whether at home or at work. Appearance matters deeply to them, and they cultivate an attractive, polished presence. They set high standards for everyone, but what makes them such fair and gifted leaders is their refusal to demand more from others than they are willing to give themselves.

Their greatest pitfall is the tendency to impose their own definitions of beauty and correctness onto situations, dismissing the perspectives of others without consideration. Left unchecked, this can make them narrow-minded and intolerant. They benefit from reminding themselves again and again that beauty truly is in the eye of the beholder.

Before age twenty, they often come across as artistic yet disciplined young adults. After twenty-one, a dramatic turning point brings greater independence and less reliance on tradition. New opportunities to express their unique perspective emerge, and they begin playing a key role in making the world more beautiful. Around fifty-one, another shift puts the emphasis on sensitivity and a richer inner life. At any age, cultivating intuition will reveal that beauty is not only something created in the outer world; it must first be found within.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting that you are not always right.

The Way Forward

Recognize that what suits you may not suit someone else. Each person is unique, and that diversity is what makes life wonderful.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You share excellent taste and refined manners, and this can create a loving and beautiful union.

Luck Maker: Say, I Didn't Know That!

Showing a genuine willingness to learn draws others toward you, because people naturally want to help those who are trying to help themselves.

At Your Best

Tasteful, well groomed, charismatic.

On the Dark Side

Opinionated, materialistic, superficial.

Love: Free Spirit

Those born on this day easily attract admirers with their flair and dramatic sense. They may give the impression of being a free spirit, but those who know them well soon discover a deep need for security and affection. Low moods may surface from time to time, yet they can also be loyal, passionate and supportive partners.

Health: Moonstone

Worry, pessimism and overwork tend to be the downfall of these individuals, so looking inward for confidence and fulfillment is essential. Until they find these qualities within, they will struggle to locate them in the outer world. Social occasions can be draining because of a distaste for false facades, but spending time with close friends and loved ones is highly recommended. Food allergies may be a concern, and checking with a doctor or nutritionist to identify triggers is worthwhile. Because appearance matters so deeply, they are likely to eat well and exercise regularly, which are the true foundations of beauty. Holding a Moonstone and meditating on it strengthens intuition, clarifies life goals and helps recognize the beauty that already lies within.

Career: Born Team Builders

These individuals thrive in roles where they can create harmony. Business management, event organization, education, lecturing and interior design all appeal. They may also develop their creativity in theater, opera or the art studio.

Destiny: To Bring Harmony and Beauty to the World

The life path of those born on this day is to remember that everyone carries their own idea of taste and beauty. Once they are willing to compromise, their destiny is to build organized methods for advancement and to make the world a more harmonious, beautiful place.

Power Thought

Every day there are beautiful things happening in my life.
