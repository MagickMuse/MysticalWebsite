Born on July 26th

The birthday of self-assurance

Profile

Those born on July 26 tend to be strong-willed individuals who are at their most content when they are giving and teaching. They draw on their extensive knowledge and experience and become confident and capable enough to take on the role of guide or mentor. Their strength of conviction and purpose is so powerful that others tend to look to them for advice and direction.

Although they can be incredibly generous, supportive and nurturing toward others, there is also a part of them that likes to be in control. Between the ages of twenty-seven and fifty-seven there is an emphasis on being more discriminating and efficient, and they need to ensure that they channel their considerable energy toward the welfare of others. After the age of fifty-seven there is a turning point that highlights their growing need for relationships, creativity and harmony.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding complacency.

The Way Forward

Understand that self-assurance without growth leads to stagnation. Keep challenging yourself so that your confidence is rooted in progress, not pride.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 20

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You both value loyalty, hard work and commitment, and this can be a very stable and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Stay Open to New Ideas

Lucky people understand that new ideas and perspectives are essential for growth. They remain open and curious, even when they feel they already have all the answers.

At Your Best

Confident, generous, knowledgeable.

On the Dark Side

Controlling, complacent, judgmental.

Love: Generous and Devoted

Those born on this day are generous and devoted partners who enjoy nurturing the people they love. They need to be careful, however, that their desire to guide and support does not become overbearing. They are attracted to intelligent, independent individuals who can challenge them intellectually.

Health: Avoiding Excess

Those born on this day tend to be robust and energetic but they need to be careful not to overindulge in rich food, drink or the pleasures of life. Moderation is key to their health and well-being. Regular exercise is important, especially activities that help them stay grounded such as hiking, gardening or yoga. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to think more objectively and stay calm.

Career: Born Teachers

These individuals are natural teachers and mentors. They may excel in education, counseling, coaching, management, politics or social work. Their generosity and desire to share knowledge may also lead them to careers in writing, publishing, medicine or the healing arts.

Destiny: To Use Their Knowledge to Serve Others

The life path of those born on this day is to keep growing and learning so that their confidence is rooted in wisdom, not habit. Once they have done that, their destiny is to use their considerable knowledge and experience to guide and inspire others.

Power Thought

Every day I learn something new about myself and the world.

  • Lucky colors: Gold, maroon, Brown
