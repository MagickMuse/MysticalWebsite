Born on August 11th

The birthday of the commentator

Profile

Those born on August 11 are keen observers of the world around them and have a sharp, incisive ability to comment on and analyze the people and situations they encounter. They are gifted communicators with a talent for expressing complex ideas in a way that is both accessible and thought-provoking. Others often turn to them for their opinions because they have a way of cutting through the noise and getting straight to the point.

Although they are articulate and persuasive, they are also deeply intuitive and sensitive, and their observations are often fueled by genuine empathy and concern for others. They care about truth and justice, and they are not afraid to speak out against injustice or to challenge the status quo. The danger, however, is that their desire to comment on everything can sometimes make them seem judgmental or preachy, and they need to learn when to speak and when to simply listen.

Until age forty-one there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to develop their analytical and communicative skills. After age forty-two there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. These are the years when they learn to soften their approach and become more diplomatic.

After age seventy-one there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to balance their desire to comment with the ability to listen and empathize, they have the potential to become truly influential and inspiring communicators.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing when to stay silent.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes the most powerful commentary is silence; listening without judgment is one of the greatest gifts you can give another person.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both intensely perceptive and emotionally deep, and this can create a passionate and transformative partnership.

Luck Maker: Listen More

Lucky people understand that listening is just as important as speaking. The more you listen, the more you learn, and the more you learn, the luckier you become.

At Your Best

Articulate, insightful, empathetic.

On the Dark Side

Judgmental, preachy, opinionated.

Love: Heart to Heart

Those born on this day are charming, witty and engaging companions, and they rarely lack admirers. In love they need intellectual stimulation as much as emotional connection, and they thrive with a partner who can challenge their thinking and match their verbal dexterity. Once committed, they are loyal, thoughtful and deeply caring partners.

Health: Rest Your Voice

These individuals tend to put a lot of energy into their communication and may suffer from throat problems, voice strain and tension headaches. They need to learn how to rest and recharge, and taking regular breaks from talking and thinking is essential. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular meals and not rush through them. Moderate exercise is recommended, and they should balance vigorous activities with restorative ones such as yoga and meditation. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their self-confidence and encourage them to focus on the positive.

Career: Born Journalists

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in journalism, broadcasting, writing, teaching, lecturing, law, politics, social commentary or any field that allows them to express their views and influence opinion. They may also excel in counseling, psychology, social work, public relations, management consulting or the performing arts.

Destiny: To Enlighten Others with Their Insightful Commentary

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance speaking with listening. Once they have developed the ability to listen with empathy, their destiny is to enlighten and inspire others with their insightful, honest and thought-provoking commentary.

Power Thought

I listen with my heart as well as my mind.
