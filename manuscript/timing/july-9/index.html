Born on July 9th

The birthday of the lovable opportunist

Profile

People born on July 9 are often a powerhouse of energy and enthusiasm. They love to taste all that life has to offer and put their heart and soul into everything they do, whether it is work or a relationship. Endearingly curious and eager to learn, their wide-eyed wonder has an energizing and motivating effect on everyone they deal with.

The opportunist streak they possess, when combined with their questing imagination and boundless energy, gives them great creative and innovative potential. Indeed, their conviction that there is so much yet to be discovered may lead them to explore concepts that others dismiss as totally unfeasible. Despite their fierce originality, however, they are not unrealistic about the possibility of success and are able to supplement their intellect and intuition with persistence and effective practical skills. When their optimism and charisma are added to this combination, it's no surprise that they are often attractive and popular individuals.

Although they tend to have healthy self-esteem, they are not always good at dealing with rejection or setbacks and can suffer from exhaustion. When feeling low, they may withdraw into bitterness, frustration, or disillusionment; it is vital they find more constructive ways to deal with disappointment, using it as an incentive or learning experience. During their teenage years they will be presented with opportunities to develop more confidence; it is important they take advantage of these, ensuring they believe in their potential regardless of setbacks. After forty-three they are likely to become more discriminating, practical, and perfectionist.

These people really do believe that anything is possible; if they can just learn to try another approach if the first fails, their enduring interest in investigating, exploring, and pushing boundaries gives them outstanding potential to blaze truly innovative trails.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting the word "no."

The Way Forward

"No" can be an awful word to hear but the only way to move onward is to find ways to turn rejection into direction.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both expressive and energetic, and this can be a passionate and stimulating relationship.

Luck Maker: Ask Why

When they hear "no," lucky people do everything they can to find out why. They focus on the reasons they didn't get what they want and learn to move on from their mistakes.

At Your Best

Vital, imaginative, persistent.

On the Dark Side

Disillusioned, unrealistic, withdrawn.

Love: High Expectations

July 9 natives are blessed with natural charm and this makes them extremely popular. They have high expectations of relationships and give a lot to those they love, but need to be loved and appreciated in return. Their choice of romantic partners tends to be those who share their curiosity and come from different backgrounds.

Health: Energy Dips

For the benefit of their emotional and physical health, those born on this day need to learn to pace themselves because they tend to throw themselves wholeheartedly into whatever they do, leading to energy dips and exhaustion. Making sure they have plenty of time for rest, relaxation, and periods of solitary meditation is recommended. As far as diet, they are likely to go for variety so their nutritional intake is probably sound, but they might benefit from daily multivitamins as an insurance policy. Extreme exercise should be avoided although moderate-intensity exercise such as jogging, dancing, or cycling should not. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the colors Orange and Yellow will give them a confidence and energy boost when feeling low.
