🔱Sacrifice to Zeus the Saviour and Athena the Saviour

◇Notes
Year-end sacrifice offered on the last day of Skirophorion to Zeus Soter and Athena Soteira, to secure good health and well-being for the coming year.

The Sacrifice to Zeus the Saviour and Athena the Saviour is offered on the last day of Skirophorion, the closing day of the Athenian year. The sacrifice, possibly of a bull, was made to secure good health and well-being for the coming year.

This rite falls on the last day of the twelfth lunar month, one of the last three days given to the chthonic deities and the final day sacred to Hekate in every month. The new year opens with the Noumenia of Hekatombaion on the new moon following the summer solstice.

On a normal calendar. The Sacrifice to Zeus the Saviour and Athena the Saviour falls on the last day of Skirophorion, generally landing in late June of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
