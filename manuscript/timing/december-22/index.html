Born on December 22nd

The birthday of enduring poise

Profile

Those born on December 22 may have formulated a long-term plan for their lives as early as childhood. At various stages they will review their goals, setting themselves new ones to achieve in the next five or ten years. These individuals know what they want out of life and how they are going to go about achieving it, and this augurs well for both professional and personal success.

Understanding the importance of preparation, they are willing to bide their time and work steadily toward their goals in a self-assured and dignified way. Their remarkable poise is the result of an absolute belief that winning in the end is their birthright, and because they are so comfortable in this self-belief they can be a great role model for others. The danger, however, is that they can sometimes become a little too complacent, remaining for years in a position that does not challenge them. When a difficult challenge finally does emerge, they may fail to live up to their own and everybody else's high expectations. The secret of their success is to keep learning, fine-tuning their skills and testing themselves.

Until age twenty-nine, they are likely to be goal-oriented with a practical approach to achievement. At thirty, a turning point highlights a growing need for freedom, new ideas and expressing creativity. After sixty, another turning point brings more emphasis on emotional receptivity, imagination and intuitive awareness.

At any age, they need to find a balance between self-belief and humility, between long-term planning and spontaneity, and between seriousness and having more fun. As soon as they strike this balance, they will maintain it with characteristic poise, finding themselves at last in exactly the right position to develop the unusual potential for happiness, success and fulfillment associated with this birthday.

Your Greatest Challenge

Broadening your outlook.

The Way Forward

Understand that there is a difference between being focused and being one-track in your approach. The former is called determination; the latter is called stubbornness.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 21 and July 22

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share an appreciation of family and tradition, and can give each other the unconditional love you crave.

Luck Maker: Have More Fun

The more relaxed you are and the more fun you have with your life, the more likely you are to feel good about yourself and attract good luck your way.

At Your Best

Prepared, self-confident, dignified.

On the Dark Side

Narrow-minded, controlling, workaholic.

Love: Accomplished

Those born on this day tend to be attracted to people as accomplished and intelligent as themselves, but they may actually feel happier with those who are more rough and ready in their approach to life. It may take a while for them to let down their guard and show a partner that they are human, but when they do they can be passionate and loyal lovers.

Health: Flexibility and Variety

The sooner these individuals learn to play as hard as they work, the better. Unwinding, having fun and spending more time with loved ones will give them the sense of balance and perspective they need to be more productive and fulfilled. They also tend to repress difficult feelings, and the more they open up and trust, the better. Their diet should aim for as much variety as possible, and exercise should include activities that encourage them to reach, jump, bend and stretch, promoting flexibility in life as well as in body. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages them to be more open and fun-loving.

Career: Born Architects

These individuals flourish in careers that offer clear signs of progression. Options include finance, stockbroking, accountancy, administration, politics, management, research, science and community work. Alternatively, they may explore their latent creativity in acting, design, architecture or music.

Destiny: To Put Their Innovative Plans into Action

The life path of those born on this day is to find a balance between professional and personal needs. Once they understand that life is a series of challenges and that learning never stops, their destiny is to put their plans or innovative concepts into action so that they benefit society.

Power Thought

Problems are unique opportunities for me to learn and to grow.
