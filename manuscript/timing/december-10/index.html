Born on December 10th

The birthday of calm intensity

Profile

A defining characteristic of those born on December 10 is their remarkable strength of spirit and steady determination to achieve their goals. Deep and intense thinkers, they are often driven by an urge to further human knowledge or instigate reform. Blessed with an inner calm that helps them stand back and make decisions objectively, their organizational skills are outstanding.

These individuals have leadership potential, and when they find a cause they believe in, they will wholly devote themselves to it. The key word here is "belief," because if they do not believe in what they are doing they simply cannot go through the motions. As a result their choice of career often has a vocational element; they need to feel they are serving some kind of higher cause, whether in education or in spiritual matters. They devote a great deal of time to examining the meaning of not just their own life but the lives of everyone and even the universe. For this reason they can sometimes come across as slightly removed from the world they live in. This is not to say they are not sociable; they are. It is just that the sensitive part of them always seems to inhabit a world where injustice and suffering do not exist.

Until age forty-one, they will be presented with opportunities to be more practical and to put more order and structure into their lives. They should take advantage of these to develop a thicker skin and be more resilient, as they do not tend to cope well during times of stress and conflict. After forty-two, a turning point highlights a growing need for independence.

At any age, when they finally find an ideal or cause about which they are passionate, they will discover within themselves all the self-discipline, responsibility and passion they need to live up to their exceptional potential as gifted, inspired and progressive leaders.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with rejection.

The Way Forward

Remind yourself that everyone gets knocked back at some point. The difference between success and failure is whether or not you get back up and try again.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both intense and passionate, and this can be a happy and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Don't Face Your Problems Alone

When you are alone, problems seem huge; but when you share them, you gain perspectives that can help you find solutions.

At Your Best

Determined, spiritual, devoted.

On the Dark Side

Elusive, isolated, overly sensitive.

Love: Love Freely

Those born on this day are charming and attractive individuals who rarely have problems attracting friends and admirers. They may, however, make things harder for themselves by falling in love with someone who lives far away or comes from a completely different background. They should always follow their heart but should not let romantic idealism destroy their chances of finding real happiness in love.

Health: Don't Retreat

These individuals have a tendency to isolate themselves during times of stress, so it is important to keep in touch with friends and loved ones. Reaching out to others boosts both emotional and physical health. They also need to ensure they do not put all their emotional eggs in one basket, relying solely on one person for support and uplift. They could be prone to eating disorders, so they need to make sure food, or denying themselves food, does not become a way of avoiding uncomfortable feelings. Regular moderate exercise is highly recommended, especially outdoors to get the mood-boosting properties of sunshine. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange increases feelings of warmth, physical enjoyment and security.

Career: Born Event Managers

These individuals may find fulfillment in people or event management, in politics or other forms of community service. They may also be drawn to the academic world. Other options include writing, promotion, sales, therapy, and exploring creativity in art, film, drama or architecture.

Destiny: To Formulate Effective Strategies

The life path of those born on this day is to get in touch with their own feelings and those of others. Once they keep alive strong bonds with others and become more resilient in the face of conflict, their destiny is to formulate effective plans or strategies for the benefit of themselves and others.

Power Thought

My life is a celebration to be shared with everyone I know.
