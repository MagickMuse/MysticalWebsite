Born on July 25th

The birthday of pure intention

Profile

Those born on July 25 are motivated by a sincere desire to find meaning and truth in their lives. They set themselves very high standards and are driven by a deep need to live and act with integrity and purity of intention. They tend to be idealistic, romantic and sensitive individuals who are attracted to humanitarian causes.

Although they are full of compassion and feeling for others, they sometimes find it hard to express their emotions in intimate relationships. Between the ages of twenty-eight and fifty-eight there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and analysis. During these years they need to make sure that they do not withdraw from emotional situations. After the age of fifty-eight they may become more relationship-oriented and creative.

Your Greatest Challenge

Expressing your feelings.

The Way Forward

Understand that sharing your feelings with others does not make you weak; it makes you more connected and more human.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 23

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both passionate and idealistic, and this can create a deeply fulfilling and intense relationship.

Luck Maker: Trust Your Heart

Lucky people understand that following their heart, even when it leads them in unexpected directions, is one of the most powerful ways to create good fortune in their lives.

At Your Best

Idealistic, sincere, visionary.

On the Dark Side

Withdrawn, perfectionist, unrealistic.

Love: Deep and Meaningful

Those born on this day are romantic idealists who long for deep, meaningful connections. They can be shy when it comes to expressing their feelings, but once they open up they are passionate and devoted lovers. They are attracted to people who share their idealism and sense of purpose.

Health: Nourish Your Emotions

Those born on this day tend to be emotionally sensitive and may bottle up their feelings, which can lead to stress and tension. It is important for them to find healthy outlets for their emotions, such as journaling, counseling or creative expression. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat plenty of fresh, wholesome foods and avoid processed or artificial foods. Gentle exercise such as swimming, walking or tai chi is recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more open and expressive.

Career: Born Humanitarians

These individuals are drawn to careers that allow them to make a positive difference in the world. They may excel in medicine, charity work, social reform, teaching, counseling, the arts or spiritual leadership. Their idealism and creativity may also draw them to writing, music or film.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Vision

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their idealism with emotional openness. Once they have done that, their destiny is to inspire others with their pure vision and unwavering commitment to truth.

Power Thought

I open my heart and let my feelings guide me toward my highest purpose.
