Born on October 23rd

The birthday of the maelstrom

Profile

Those born on October 23 are intense individuals with strong convictions and the willpower to see their goals accomplished. Like a tornado, they arrive on the scene without announcement, sweeping up everyone and everything around them. Others may be alarmed by the power of their presence, but they simply cannot help themselves; stirring things up is what they are born to do.

Although they have a reputation for being impulsive and unpredictable, there is always a method to their madness. Their observational skills are second to none and they are quick to pick up on the moods and feelings of others, making them excellent judges of character. This sensitivity, combined with their razor-sharp mind and gift for communication, gives them the ability to present compelling arguments in their favor and win others over. Unfortunately this also gives them the ability to manipulate others with flattery or emotional blackmail.

People tend to have very strong views about these individuals; they either love them or dislike them. Their behavior is indeed provocative and their lives are characterized by conflict. In their minds, however, the confrontation they provoke is never gratuitous because they firmly believe that it is only through conflict that progress can be made. This approach clearly has its drawbacks but one of the most positive things about these people is that they do not bear grudges; once a disagreement is over, it really is over.

Before age twenty-nine they may seem to take an excessive number of risks and they should ensure they do not become self-destructive during this period. After thirty, there is a turning point that highlights a growing need for freedom and the desire to expand their horizons through philosophy, study or travel. Whatever age they are, to truly fulfill their destiny and become the inspirational force of nature they are destined to be, they must learn when to hold on and when to let go, when to attack and when to retreat, and when to speak and when to remain silent.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing when to stop.

The Way Forward

Understand that there are times when holding back and having patience is not a sign of weakness but a sign of strength and maturity.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You bring excitement and they bring stability; this can be a creative and mutually beneficial union.

Luck Maker: Think Before You Act

Those who rush into things often end up rushing out. Taking time to make decisions and acting with quiet confidence is a sure way to attract luck.

At Your Best

Charismatic, influential, progressive.

On the Dark Side

Disruptive, manipulative, reckless.

Love: Not for the Faint-Hearted

Loving those born on this day is not for the faint-hearted, as their intensity can be overwhelming. They are exciting and passionate partners, but they need to learn to give their partner room to breathe. When they find someone who can challenge and engage them intellectually, as well as emotionally and physically, they can become devoted and loyal lovers.

Health: Energy Burns Bright

These individuals burn a tremendous amount of emotional and physical energy. It is vital that they refuel regularly with a balanced diet rich in whole grains, legumes, fruits, vegetables, lean protein and healthy fats. They should also make sure they get plenty of quality sleep, since their frenetic pace of life can lead to burnout. Regular moderate exercise, preferably outdoors, will help them manage their stress levels, and activities such as yoga and meditation are highly recommended for calming their restless minds. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to approach situations with greater calm and composure.

Career: Born Debaters

These individuals may be drawn to careers in law, politics, business, media, sales, promotion and campaigning. Their creativity may also draw them to the arts, music and drama. Other possible career choices include teaching, counseling, medicine, social reform and charity work.

Destiny: To Push the Boundaries of Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the art of patience and diplomacy. Once they understand that timing and tact are just as important as passion and conviction, their destiny is to push through boundaries and inspire progressive change.

Power Thought

Before I express an opinion I will stop, breathe and listen first.
