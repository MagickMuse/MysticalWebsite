Born on August 7th

The birthday of the undercover visionary

Profile

Those born on August 7 are imaginative, intuitive and often quietly brilliant individuals who tend to operate behind the scenes rather than in the spotlight. They have a rich inner life and a vivid imagination that gives them the ability to see possibilities and solutions that others miss entirely. Although they may not always seek recognition, their visionary ideas and insights often have a profound impact on the people and projects they are involved with.

They are gentle, compassionate souls with a deep concern for the well-being of others, and their sensitivity gives them a rare ability to understand and empathize with people from all walks of life. The danger, however, is that their sensitivity can make them vulnerable to the negative energies and emotions of others, and they may retreat into their own world rather than face the harsh realities of life.

Until age forty-five there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to develop their skills quietly and build a solid foundation for their future success. After age forty-six there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a positive period when their visionary ideas are more likely to find an audience.

After age seventy-five there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional depth and personal transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to step out of the shadows and share their visionary ideas with the world, they have the potential to make a truly significant and lasting contribution.

Your Greatest Challenge

Stepping into the spotlight.

The Way Forward

Understand that your visionary ideas deserve to be heard; hiding your light under a bushel benefits no one, least of all you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 20 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. You are both imaginative, intuitive and compassionate, and this can create a deeply spiritual and emotionally fulfilling partnership.

Luck Maker: Share Your Vision

Lucky people know that ideas hidden away cannot attract luck. When you share your vision, you invite the support and opportunities you need to bring it to life.

At Your Best

Visionary, compassionate, imaginative.

On the Dark Side

Withdrawn, oversensitive, escapist.

Love: Soul Connection

Those born on this day seek a deep soul connection in their relationships rather than surface-level attraction. They are romantic, imaginative and deeply devoted partners who need a lover who can appreciate their sensitivity and share their rich inner world. They may be prone to idealizing their partners and need to see people as they really are.

Health: Come Out of Your Shell

These individuals may be prone to stress-related issues, anxiety and depression, especially if they spend too much time alone or retreat into fantasy. It is important for them to stay connected to the real world and to maintain a strong social network. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, grounding meals and avoid alcohol and recreational substances that could intensify their escapist tendencies. Gentle exercise such as walking, swimming or yoga is ideal. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red will help boost their energy and confidence.

Career: Born Visionaries

These individuals are ideally suited to careers that allow them to use their imagination and creativity, such as writing, art, music, film, photography, design, architecture or invention. They may also excel in counseling, psychology, healing, social work, spirituality, research or any field that requires intuition and empathy. Their organizational skills can also lead them to success in business and management.

Destiny: To Share Their Visionary Ideas with the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to step out of the shadows and share their unique vision with the world. Once they have found the courage to be seen, their destiny is to inspire and enlighten others with their visionary ideas and compassionate spirit.

Power Thought

I step into the light and share my gifts with the world.
