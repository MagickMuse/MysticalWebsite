Born on July 5th

The birthday of fireworks

Profile

Those born on July 5 have a flair for injecting sparkle, energy and excitement into every situation. Everything about them is vivid and colorful, and their charming manners and interesting conversation light up the workplace and social gatherings alike.

Thriving on constant variety and stimulation, their life never seems to stand still. They can, however, alienate others with their boundless energy and enthusiasm, and their wild shifts in mood may frustrate those who admire them but doubt their reliability. Between the ages of forty-seven and seventy-seven there is an emphasis on discrimination and efficiency. During these years they need to learn to maintain stability rather than frittering their energy away in too many directions. After the age of seventy-seven there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Concentrating for long periods of time.

The Way Forward

Understand that concentration is not something people are born with. It is a skill they learn and master.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 24

Those dates fall under Virgo. This is a classic case of opposites attract. You both have so much to learn and give to each other.

Luck Maker: Learn to Meditate

Meditation is a powerful way to attract more luck and happiness into your life. It teaches you to focus your thoughts on the here and now so your intuition can get your attention.

At Your Best

Exciting, entertaining, imaginative.

On the Dark Side

Wild, erratic, unreliable.

Love: Someone Very Special

Those born on this day may often resist attempts to make them settle down, and it will take someone truly special to hold their attention for long and add much-needed stability to their life. They prefer to be associated with people who are charismatic and exciting like themselves, but they would benefit more from those who have a calmer, gentler and more centered approach.

Health: Experimental

Those born on this day love to experiment in all aspects of their life. Their diet is likely to be varied and interesting and, as long as they make sure they do not go for long periods without food, they are likely eating healthily. As their life is often hectic and they are prone to coughs and colds, they might benefit from a daily multivitamin. Regular exercise is recommended, especially activities and sports that involve other people in a competitive or social way. Mind-training therapies that encourage them to slow down and focus, such as meditation or cognitive therapy, are highly recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help them feel calmer and more centered.

Career: Born Inventors

These individuals have creative flair that may express itself in careers in design, invention, technology, art, music, film or dance. Multi-talented, they may also excel in sales, promotion, banking, counseling or entertainment. A dislike for taking orders often leads them into positions of authority.

Destiny: To Realize Their Visionary Sense of Purpose

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to maintain stability in their relationships and their lives. Once they are more grounded, their destiny is to innovate and reform, realizing their visionary sense of purpose.

Power Thought

I am here, in the present, in the moment.
