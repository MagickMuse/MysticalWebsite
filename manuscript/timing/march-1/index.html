Born on March 1st

The birthday of the practical visionary

Profile

People born on March 1 possess a talent for lifting the spirits of those around them and for transforming concepts into solid achievements. They tend to see the world with the eyes of an artist, appreciating beauty in unexpected places. Yet they can also be extremely practical and level-headed; anyone who dismisses them as lightweights will be making a big mistake.

When the strong willpower of those born on this day is focused, they can accomplish remarkable things through their determination and conviction. Unfortunately, despite this incredible potential, they are also prone to panic, negative thinking, and a lack of confidence. When anxious, they become easily influenced and likely to attract people who will take advantage of their talents. Building up self-esteem is essential so they can steer their life in the right direction rather than letting others take the wheel.

Until the age of nineteen, their plans for the future tend to be vague or constantly shifting; this is when they are most vulnerable to negative influences. Fortunately, between the ages of twenty and forty-nine they enter a phase of increasing assertiveness and self-assurance, though they may occasionally become controlling or impatient when things do not go their way. After the age of fifty they often feel a deep need to devote themselves to loved ones or humanitarian causes.

Despite their tendency toward self-doubt, these people have great intelligence, charisma, and originality. Once they learn to take responsibility for their decisions, they often find themselves in a leadership position, making a difference and paving the way for those less fortunate through the power of their personality.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to cope with feelings of worry and anxiety.

The Way Forward

Imagine yourself being calm and bring that image to mind every time you feel anxious or fearful.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both authoritative yet refined figures with huge hearts, creating an honest, supportive, and loving relationship.

Luck Maker: Stop Thinking "What If"

Worry is the enemy of good luck because it causes inaction and helplessness. Stop wasting valuable energy on disaster scenarios that will never transpire; use that energy positively and proactively instead.

At Your Best

Refined, artistic, ambitious.

On the Dark Side

Controlling, selfish, impatient.

Love: Room to Breathe

March 1 natives tend to be drawn to strong, commanding individuals, but to feel fulfilled in a relationship they need plenty of freedom and space. Until they find a relationship that offers security while also giving them room to breathe, their love life may be somewhat chaotic.

Health: Time to Daydream

People born on this day can be a bit careless when it comes to their health, and it is important for them to keep in regular touch with their doctor, eat well, and exercise every day. They may find it hard to stick to a strict exercise routine as it contradicts their dreamy nature, but brisk walks in the park or countryside allow them both to get fit and to let their mind wander. As sensuous individuals, they will almost certainly enjoy regular massages, particularly aromatherapy. If they have a load of work or study ahead, a ginger-scented aromatic candle is a good choice, as ginger scent also helps when feeling confused or lacking in purpose.

Career: Born Creative Directors

These people are natural creative directors, channeling their imaginative thoughts and original ideas into constructive projects. Although they have the skills to succeed in business, they tend to be happier when using their imagination and may be drawn toward writing, drama, art, design, or music. Later in life they may also gravitate toward social reform, charity work, or humanitarian causes.

Destiny: To Transform Ideas into Achievements

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance the emotional extremes of negativity to which they are prone. Once they accomplish that, their destiny is to transform their own ideas and the ideas of others into solid achievements.

Power Thought

It is myself rather than my worry which will change things.
