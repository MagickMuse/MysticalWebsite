Born on January 14th

The birthday of conviction

Profile

One of the greatest strengths of those born on January 14 is their ability to absorb vast amounts of information and still see the bigger picture. They are shrewd judges of people and situations, and their inquisitive mind is forever hungry for new ideas, new information, and new challenges. This panoramic view makes them particularly strong decision makers. Their firm sense of right and wrong, combined with a highly developed sense of conviction, makes them excellent and much-sought-after peacemakers at home and at work.

The conviction and certainty that define those born on this day also carry risks. Once they have settled on a course of action, it is nearly impossible for them to change direction, and they may go to extreme lengths to see things through to the bitter end. Ironically, for people so uncompromising in their professional lives, they are not always able to make that same commitment in their personal lives. Their personal world often takes second place, perhaps because they view emotions and close connections as distractions, or perhaps because they fear disappointment. The way forward is to bring the same level of dedication to friendships and partnerships, learning to respect and appreciate the emotional support of others.

Although they project extreme confidence and have the ability to implement radical change, they are inwardly far more complex. Behind the solid image and the apparent love of risk lies a person who often feels misunderstood, especially if they have not yet found a direction worthy of their considerable energies. Once they understand that what they truly crave is personal freedom and the ability to bring positive change to the world, they can leave their insecurities behind and achieve extraordinary things.

Your Greatest Challenge

Finding direction and purpose for your life.

The Way Forward

Understand that you succeed best when you find an activity you truly love and believe in.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. You share a passion for staying busy and taking on new challenges, creating an adventurous union based on respect and mutual understanding.

Luck Maker: Bend but Don't Break

You need consistency, but you also need to be open-minded and willing to reconsider your options if life gives you reason to.

At Your Best

Peacemaker, inquisitive, risk-taker.

On the Dark Side

Stubborn, obsessed, insecure.

Love: Passions Run Deep

The passions of those born on January 14 run deep and are typically uncomplicated by jealousy. Family, friends, and loved ones need to be faithful, patient, and understanding, but the effort is well worth it, as they make adventurous and exciting partners. Their ideal match is someone with a like mind who avoids jealousy or possessiveness and supports them in their convictions.

Health: Accident Prone

Fortunately, those born on this day are unlikely to be tempted by drugs, alcohol, or smoking, which has a protective effect on their physical and emotional health. Despite their common-sense approach, they are often guilty of driving themselves too hard or living on the edge, and their body inevitably pays the price with accidents, stress-related headaches, and insomnia. They are drawn to extreme sports, but their health would be better served by gentle to moderate exercise such as cycling or swimming. A cup of lemon balm or passionflower herbal tea before bedtime will help them sleep well and relieve aches and pains.

Career: Born People Watchers

It is important for these people to find an outlet for their powers of observation, their ability to see the whole picture, and their untapped well of creativity. Writing and photography may be of particular interest, as might property development and design. Because of their unusual ambition and determination, they can master almost any skill or career. Media, public relations, advertising, business, banking, and the stock exchange may also appeal.

Destiny: To Introduce Positive Reforms

The life path of those born on this day is to find a cause to which they can devote their considerable energy. Once they learn the importance of connections with others, they will discover their destiny: to introduce reforms or groundbreaking ways of doing and seeing things.

Power Thought

I can relax and think peacefully about my goals.
