Born on October 20th

The birthday of the double life

Profile

Those born on October 20 are among the most sociable of the year. They have an easy-going, attractive manner and their company is much sought after by others. For them, networking is not just a career tool, it is a way of life. They genuinely enjoy meeting people, asking them questions and fitting into whatever group they find themselves in.

Although they present a gregarious and outgoing face to the world, beneath the surface they are far deeper and more complex individuals than most people realize. In fact, they often feel as though they are living a double life. One part of them is the fun-loving social butterfly that everyone sees. The other part is the serious, intense and driven person who craves meaning, depth and privacy. Integrating these two sides of their personality is their biggest challenge, and also the key to their happiness and success.

Their social skills give them the ability to build an extensive network of contacts and supporters, and this can help them get ahead in virtually any career. Their more private, deeper side gives them the psychological insight and the capacity for sustained effort that can take them to the very top. Problems arise, however, when they allow their social side to dominate at the expense of their more reflective side, or vice versa. If they are all charm and no substance, they will never fulfill their potential. If they are all work and no play, they will deny themselves the connections and joy that life has to offer.

Before age thirty-one they may be most concerned with issues of emotional change, power and transformation. After thirty-two, a turning point arrives when they are likely to become more adventurous and open-minded. Whatever age they are, they need to find ways to successfully integrate both parts of their personality, because when they do they are capable of extraordinary achievements.

Your Greatest Challenge

Integrating the different sides of your personality.

The Way Forward

Understand that depth and sociability are not opposites. You can be both fun-loving and serious. Embrace both sides and you will find balance and fulfillment.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 21 and July 22

Those dates fall under Cancer. You are both nurturing and intuitive, and this can be a supportive and understanding union.

Luck Maker: Be Authentic

Lucky people are authentic. They do not pretend to be someone they are not. When you are genuine, people trust you and good opportunities flow your way.

At Your Best

Sociable, insightful, versatile.

On the Dark Side

Superficial, conflicted, secretive.

Love: Deeper Than They Appear

Those born on this day are attractive and flirtatious, and they rarely lack admirers. However, they may struggle to find a partner who appreciates both sides of their personality. They need someone who enjoys their social sparkle but also understands and respects their need for depth and solitude. When they find this person, they can be devoted, passionate and deeply loving partners.

Health: Find Your Balance

Finding balance between their public and private selves is essential for the emotional health of these individuals. They may be prone to stress-related conditions, particularly when they feel pulled in different directions. Regular exercise that combines social interaction with moments of solitude, such as swimming or cycling, can be particularly beneficial. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein is essential, and they should take care to limit their alcohol intake, especially during social events. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage balance and harmony.

Career: Born Diplomats

The networking skills of those born on this day make them natural diplomats, negotiators, public relations professionals, event organizers and politicians. Their deeper, more reflective side may draw them toward careers in psychology, writing, research, counseling, philosophy and the healing professions. Their versatility also makes them well suited to careers in business, entertainment and the arts.

Destiny: To Build Bridges Between People and Ideas

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to integrate the different aspects of their personality. Once they have found their inner balance, their destiny is to use their unique combination of social skills and psychological depth to build bridges between people and ideas.

Power Thought

I am whole, and every part of me has something valuable to contribute.
