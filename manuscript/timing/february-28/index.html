Born on February 28th

The birthday of the original charmer

Profile

People born on February 28 have a warm glow about them and can light up the lives of others with their energy and originality. They love to be center stage and are often in just that spot at social gatherings. Natural performers, they are never short of admirers and are blessed with the ability to charm just about anyone they meet.

Articulate and entertaining, those born on this day will go out of their way to get a laugh or reaction from others, even if it means exaggerating the truth. They thrive on being noticed, but attention seeking is not what truly drives them. Their real motivation is a thirst for adventure, and they will enthusiastically follow wherever their impulses direct them. Underlying their restlessness, however, is a deep-seated fear of standing still; this fear can drive them toward sensation-seeking and sometimes self-destructive behavior.

They will never lose the glint in their eye, but beneath the bravado they do secretly long to find a real purpose and achieve lasting success in the eyes of the world. This will not be possible until they discover that self-esteem is not created by thrill-seeking but by being the person they truly are. It is important for them to learn to be more comfortable with being rather than doing, because until they reach this level of awareness their life may hurtle chaotically from one situation to another. They should learn to cultivate inner calmness between the ages of twenty-two and fifty-one, when there is an emphasis on new directions and ventures.

Because February 28 natives live in such a vibrant and wholehearted way, they experience life more intensely than most. They do, however, need to rein in their compulsion to indulge every whim and learn greater self-control. They should never suppress their optimism and curiosity, but if they can just learn to look before they leap, they can become far more than original charmers — they can become life's pioneers, boldly going where none have gone before.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to rein in your impulses.

The Way Forward

Understand that you can replace addictions with preferences.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a love of adventure and glamour, and this can create a loving, energetic bond.

Luck Maker: Want What You Already Have

Luck follows those who appreciate and are grateful for what they already possess. If you acknowledge how far you have already come, you will be far more positive in your outlook.

At Your Best

Charming, original, vibrant.

On the Dark Side

Thoughtless, melodramatic, reckless.

Love: Step Back

February 28 natives can find it hard to stay committed to one relationship. They can be big flirts, which can upset a partner. To enjoy all the fulfilling rewards of a loving relationship, they need to learn greater self-control and to step back every now and again to let their partner take center stage. They tend to stick with partners who are loyal, energetic, and glamorous.

Health: Life in the Fast Lane

Life in the fast lane can take its toll on the health of those born on this day, and it is important for them to set aside days of peace and rest to prevent burnout. They have a tendency to neglect their health as they jump from one adventure to another, so regular health check-ups are recommended. They often enjoy cooking, which encourages healthier eating. As for exercise, they benefit most from activities with a mind-body approach, such as yoga or tai chi, although swimming and running also suit them well. Wearing, surrounding themselves with, or meditating on shades of Purple will encourage them to look within rather than without for a sense of self-esteem.

Career: Born Travelers

These people need careers that channel their energy and curiosity. They are ideally suited to tourism and travel, as well as arts and sports. Their sharp mind and desire for mental exploration may also draw them to science, teaching, research, and philosophy. They can thrive in leadership roles such as management or business, or in working for themselves. Later in life they may be drawn toward helping others, and their love of the dramatic may also steer them toward the arts, writing, and the entertainment world.

Destiny: To Be Life's Pioneer and Adventurer

The life path of those born on this day is to look within as well as without for a sense of fulfillment and adventure. Once they find inner peace, their destiny is to be the pioneer and adventurer of this world.

Power Thought

I celebrate what I have, and I meet new challenges with open arms.
