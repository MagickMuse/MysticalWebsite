Born on August 12th

The birthday of the historian

Profile

Those born on August 12 have a deep fascination with history, tradition and the lessons of the past. They are keen observers of human behavior and tend to study the patterns of the past to understand the present and predict the future. Their insight and wisdom give them a maturity beyond their years, and others often seek them out for their sage advice.

Although they have a deep respect for tradition, they are not stuck in the past. They use their knowledge of history to inform their approach to the present, and they are often at the forefront of innovation and progress. They are also highly articulate and persuasive communicators, and their ability to draw parallels between the past and the present makes them compelling storytellers and teachers.

Until age forty there is an emphasis on practicality, order and efficiency. During these years they are likely to develop their knowledge and establish themselves in their chosen field. After age forty-one there is a turning point that highlights the growing importance of relationships, creativity and harmony. These are the years when they are most likely to share their wisdom and experience with a wider audience.

After age seventy there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional depth and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to balance their fascination with the past with an equal enthusiasm for the possibilities of the future, they have the potential to become truly wise and influential individuals.

Your Greatest Challenge

Living in the present.

The Way Forward

Understand that while the past is a wonderful teacher, the present moment is where life is lived and where your power to create change resides.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both seekers of knowledge and truth, and this can create an intellectually stimulating and adventurous partnership.

Luck Maker: Learn from the Past, Live in the Present

Lucky people study the past for wisdom but do not dwell there. They apply what they learn to the present and use it to create a brighter future.

At Your Best

Wise, articulate, insightful.

On the Dark Side

Backward-looking, rigid, preachy.

Love: Old-Fashioned Romance

Those born on this day have an old-fashioned approach to romance and courtship that many find charming and refreshing. They are loyal, devoted and traditional in their approach to love, and they need a partner who shares their respect for commitment and values. Once settled, they are generous, supportive and endlessly fascinating companions.

Health: Keep Moving Forward

These individuals need to be careful that their love of the past does not prevent them from embracing new approaches to their health. They should be open to trying new forms of exercise and new dietary approaches. Regular moderate exercise, such as walking, swimming or cycling, is recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a varied and balanced diet and try new foods regularly. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to embrace new experiences and think more progressively.

Career: Born Historians

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in history, archaeology, teaching, research, writing, journalism, museum curating, librarianship, law, politics or any profession that allows them to study patterns and draw conclusions. They may also excel in business, management, publishing, editing, architecture or art restoration.

Destiny: To Use the Wisdom of the Past to Inspire the Future

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their reverence for the past with an excitement for the future. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to use their remarkable wisdom and insight to inspire and guide others.

Power Thought

I honor the past and embrace the present with open arms.
