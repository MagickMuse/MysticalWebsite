Born on July 18th

The birthday of the better way

Profile

Those born on July 18 are progressive individuals who are always looking for a better, more effective way of doing things. They are convinced that there is always room for improvement, and their innovative approach to life often leads to remarkable breakthroughs and achievements.

Although their constant quest for improvement can sometimes exhaust those around them, their intentions are always positive and their desire to make things better extends to helping others reach their full potential. Between the ages of thirty-four and sixty-four there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to ensure that their perfectionism does not become counterproductive. After the age of sixty-four there is a turning point highlighting creativity and partnerships.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting imperfection.

The Way Forward

Understand that perfection is an illusion. Progress, not perfection, is the real measure of success.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both energetic and driven, and this can create a powerful and stimulating partnership.

Luck Maker: Celebrate Small Wins

Lucky people understand that celebrating small victories creates positive momentum that attracts bigger wins. Do not wait for perfection before you celebrate.

At Your Best

Innovative, progressive, determined.

On the Dark Side

Perfectionist, restless, critical.

Love: High Standards

Those born on this day have high standards in love as in everything else, and they need a partner who can live up to their expectations while also helping them relax and enjoy life. They are attracted to intelligent, ambitious individuals and can be incredibly loving and supportive partners when they learn to accept imperfection.

Health: Embrace Imperfection

Those born on this day may be prone to stress-related conditions because of their constant quest for improvement. It is important for them to learn to relax and accept that good enough is sometimes perfectly fine. Regular exercise is essential, especially activities that encourage mindfulness such as yoga, tai chi or walking in nature. As far as diet is concerned, they should aim for a healthy, balanced diet without obsessing over every detail. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will encourage them to relax and enjoy life more.

Career: Born Innovators

These individuals are natural innovators and may excel in science, technology, engineering, medicine or any field that rewards creative problem-solving. They may also be drawn to teaching, writing, consulting or management. Their desire to improve things may lead them to careers in social reform, environmental work or public service.

Destiny: To Find a Better Way

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their quest for improvement with acceptance and gratitude for what is. Once they have done that, their destiny is to use their innovative thinking and determination to find better ways of doing things that benefit everyone.

Power Thought

Progress, not perfection, is the true measure of my success.

  • Lucky days: Monday and Tuesday, especially when these days fall on the 7th or 9th of the month
  • Lucky colors: White, Crimson, cream
