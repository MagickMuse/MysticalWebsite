🎭City Dionysia

◇Notes
The largest of the Dionysian festivals, held in Athens on Elaphebolion 9 through 13. Setting for the famous dramatic contests, dithyrambs, and plays.

↗Entities1
Dionysus

The City Dionysia, also called the Greater Dionysia, is the largest of the Dionysian festivals. It is held in Athens on the ninth through the thirteenth of Elaphebolion, in the theatre where Dionysus had His sanctuary, and where dithyrambs and plays were performed.

Ritual Procedure

  • Before the festival began, a statue representing the God was placed on the road leading to the city.
  • A sacrifice was offered before the statue.
  • The statue was then escorted back to the temple in procession, thereby bringing the God into the city for the festival.
  • On the first day, a great procession was held with various offerings, leading into the komos, or revel, a night-long feast and celebration.
  • The days following the procession were given to the famous dramatic contests of Athens.

On a normal calendar. The City Dionysia falls on the ninth through the thirteenth of Elaphebolion, generally landing in March of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
