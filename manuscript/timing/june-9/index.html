Born on June 9th

The birthday of unexpected opportunity

Profile

People born on June 9 have a wonderful zest for life. They have limitless energy, and others may find it hard to keep up with their ever-changing thoughts, feelings, and emotions. Although they have quick minds and a logical orientation, everything about them is unexpected.

They have a quick temper but are quick to forgive. They are forthright and strong-willed in their worldview, but surprisingly passive in personal relationships. They are unconventional yet respectful toward authority. Sympathetic and generous to those less fortunate, but sometimes insensitive to loved ones. Because they have so many different faces, they are endlessly fascinating.

Action is a key word for them and they thrive on challenges and variety. Life can be a mad rush between one activity and another. It is important for them to occasionally slow down and catch their breath, and to bring all aspects of their personality into an integrated whole. Until age forty-two they focus on home, security, and family. Around forty-two there is a turning point emphasizing energy, power, and self-confidence.

Above all, they are spectacularly colorful and versatile. If they can learn to channel their energies into mature goals, life will offer them a series of unexpected but well-deserved opportunities for happiness and fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being consistent.

The Way Forward

Consistency and responsibility are not limiting energies but extremely creative ones; without them, no project stands a chance.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both fiery, passionate, and gentle, and this can create an intense and exciting union.

Luck Maker: Don't Give Up So Soon

Lots of people miss out on good fortune because they aren't patient enough to wait for it. Lucky people are patient people.

At Your Best

Youthful, direct, logical.

On the Dark Side

Immature, changeable, tactless.

Love: Don't Rush into Love

June 9 natives fall in love easily but can rush in and make wrong decisions in the heat of passion. They can also jeopardize relationships by not investing enough time and energy. They need to understand that relationships, like flowers, need work and tender care to flourish.

Health: Love to Move

Those born on this day love to move and are often naturally good at sports of all kinds, especially competitive sports and athletics. They need to keep diet and lifestyle simple, fresh, and natural, steering clear of alcohol and recreational drugs. Mind-body therapies such as massage, yoga, and tai chi are extremely beneficial. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will encourage self-awareness and confidence.

Career: Born Rock Climbers

These people generally prefer active, hands-on careers over administrative ones. They would excel in a multitude of professions from writing, music, and drama to medical research, politics, or computing. They may be drawn to careers involving fresh air and activity such as sport, rock climbing, tourism, and farming. Journalism may also appeal, as would careers working with children or being self-employed.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Reach for Their Dreams

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to get to know themselves better. Once they bring all the different parts of their personality together, their destiny is to stay young at heart and inspire others to reach for their dreams.

Power Thought

I draw strength from the still center within me.
