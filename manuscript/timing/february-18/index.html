Born on February 18th

The birthday of the eternally young

Profile

Whatever their age, people born on February 18 never grow old in their minds and hearts. With a charismatic energy about them, they never fail to brighten up their surroundings with infectious optimism and enthusiasm for new ideas and projects, however far-fetched or impossible they may seem.

Those born on this day are also courageous risk-takers. They will always be the first to volunteer or put themselves forward, loving nothing better than to live life on the edge. There is, of course, a danger with this reckless approach: it can lead them into serious trouble, and on more than one occasion it will do just that. They are never happier than when pushing things one degree harder, higher, or faster, but they do need to ask themselves why they feel compelled to live this way. More often than not, deep-seated fears and insecurities hide behind their mask of indestructibility.

With their youthful spirit and the endearing vulnerabilities that come with it, February 18 natives do not always realize that others are naturally drawn to their sunny optimism. They may even find that people look to them for leadership, but they are not always comfortable in that role, as it limits their freedom to experience and explore the wonders of the world. Although their life sparkles with variety and adventure, there is a price to pay. One day they may look at their life and wonder why they lack a genuine sense of achievement. Learning to focus their energies rather than scattering them is essential; fortunately, after the age of thirty-two they become more assertive and self-disciplined in their daily affairs.

As long as they avoid seeking risk and adventure in the wrong places and understand that showing off is not the most fulfilling way to gain supporters, they have the potential to win admiration and respect from others and, most importantly, a sense of pride in themselves and their accomplishments.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing when to stop.

The Way Forward

Understand that with all things there is a tipping point, and more often than not, less is more.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You both have a youthful approach to life and love, and this can create a tender and refreshingly honest union.

Luck Maker: Find the Stillness Within

Intuitive, luck-making guidance can be yours when you learn to still your mind. Try meditating at least once a day.

At Your Best

Youthful, dynamic, charismatic.

On the Dark Side

Reckless, immature, impatient.

Love: Superstar

February 18 natives tend to have an immature approach to love, believing that showing off or being the center of attention is the way to win hearts. They need to learn that being calm and in control is a far more effective approach, as it puts others naturally at ease. Once they find a partner, one thing is certain: their partner will never be bored.

Health: Something's Cooking

The relentlessly upbeat and youthful approach to life that February 18 people enjoy helps keep illness and aging at bay. They do not grow old; they just get better — or, some might say, crazier. The biggest risk to their health is their addiction to thrill-seeking, and they should avoid overindulgence in extreme sports as well as addictive pastimes such as gambling, drinking, and drug use. Knowing when to stop is an important lesson in all aspects of life, diet and exercise included. Learning to cook properly would not only improve their diet, which tends to be irregular, but would also be therapeutic, encouraging them to slow down and think about what they are putting into their bodies. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help bring calmness and control into their lives.

Career: Born DJs

These people need careers that give them plenty of freedom to express their original thoughts. Multi-talented, they can flourish in any field provided they are allowed to follow their own path. They may be particularly drawn to business, public relations, publishing, writing, journalism, the leisure industry, music, acting, and the world of entertainment.

Destiny: To Motivate Others with Their Enthusiasm

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the importance of self-discipline. When they have found a degree of calm, their destiny is to motivate others with their enthusiasm and vibrant energy.

Power Thought

Today I will find the stillness within me.
