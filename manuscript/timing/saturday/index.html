Ruling Planet

Saturn ♄

Areas of Influence

Limitations, authority, endurance, stability, protection

Best Day for Magick

Saturday is the best day of the week to perform magick for the law, loss, endings, transforming, banishings, and interrupting.

Lunar Magick

Saturday, ruled by Saturn, favors workings around time, structure, organization, systems, and boundaries. Pair it with a waning or dark moon for powerful banishing, binding, or endings work. Use Saturday when favorable lunar timings are still days away but your working involves restriction, discipline, or long-term change.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.

Dark Magick Timing

Saturday ranks alongside Tuesday as the most effective day for harmful and destructive magick. Saturn's cold, binding energy supports curses, hexes, and workings of slow ruin or restriction. The first planetary hour at dawn on Saturday belongs to Saturn, making sunrise the optimal moment for baneful spellcraft. On any other day, working within a planetary hour of Saturn still draws on this same heavy, patient force. For the strongest results, perform dark workings on Saturday during a waning moon when Saturn is well placed.
