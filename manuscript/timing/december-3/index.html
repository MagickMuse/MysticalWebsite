Born on December 3rd

The birthday of progressive expertise

Profile

Those born on December 3 are blessed with inquisitive and progressive minds and are at their happiest and best when formulating original strategies to bring about improvements. Although their ideas are highly original, unorthodox even, they are also thorough and rational types. When these qualities are added to their formidable organizational and technical skills, the result is a person with stunning expertise in their chosen field.

Not surprisingly, given their perfectionist nature, work plays a huge part in their lives and they will often dedicate themselves wholeheartedly to their career. They tend to seek out others with similar mindsets, and while others respect their energy, ambition and focus and admire their well-deserved professional success, they can feel as if these individuals are hard to get to know. This is to some extent true, as they do not really have much time for socializing and often feel a need simply to be alone. This is not for religious or spiritual reasons but to help renew their focus and concentration and fine-tune their skills. When they are ready, they emerge from their silence to astonish everyone with their accomplishments.

The dedicated and ambitious aspects of their personality do not tend to emerge until at least their twenties, but when they do they provide a focus and determination that is second to none. After age fifty, however, a significant turning point brings opportunities to focus on friendship and group awareness.

At any age, they need to take advantage of every opportunity to participate more fully with others, because this will help them understand that their ambition is not only driven by a desire to achieve professional excellence but also by a desire to assist others and play an inspirational role in their lives. As long as they make sure emotional needs do not take second place to work, they have the innovative potential to become dynamic instruments of progress.

Your Greatest Challenge

Finding an interest outside work.

The Way Forward

Understand that if you do not see to your own needs, not just your work but all aspects of your life will suffer as a result.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are inquisitive, original and driven individuals, and this can be an exciting and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Stay in Touch

Keep your connections with other people alive and you maximize your chances of luck, because luck always comes through other people.

At Your Best

Innovative, meticulous, ambitious.

On the Dark Side

Withdrawn, workaholic, difficult.

Love: When You Are Ready

Those born on this day are intense and independent individuals. They may spend long periods of time alone, unaware that they actually have an army of admirers silently waiting in the wings. When they finally feel ready to open up emotionally, they will not be short of admirers. They need a partner who respects their independence and need for freedom but also gives them plenty of love and support.

Health: Pleasure in Simple Things

These individuals run the risk of losing themselves in work, so they need to constantly remind themselves of the importance of taking pleasure in simple things. Activities like gardening, cooking, flower arranging, walks in the countryside, talking to friends and holding hands with a loved one should never be dismissed as a waste of time; for emotional and physical well-being they need to make a real effort to stay connected with those they love. Their diet should include a wide variety of foods, and although their interest in nutrition is admirable, they should never forget that food is also meant to be enjoyed. Regular moderate exercise, in particular social forms such as dancing, is recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages spontaneity and expressiveness.

Career: Born Engineers

These individuals can combine innovative potential with technical skills to excel in science, psychology and engineering, as well as in sport. Other options include sales, advertising, public relations, promotion, education and charity work, as well as art, music, writing and drama.

Destiny: To Influence and Inspire Others with Their Expertise

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance professional and personal needs. Once they feel ready to participate more fully in society, their destiny is to influence and inspire others with their expertise and progressive ideas.

Power Thought

I work to live; I don't live to work.
