Born on June 17th

The birthday of the influential example

Profile

People born on June 17 are often strong and inspirational individuals who believe everyone has it within them to follow their dreams. A fascinating combination of creativity and structure, they know how to conform but also have a vivid imagination they want to explore and, if possible, profit from.

Demanding a lot of themselves and those they live and work with, whatever situation they are in they are likely to be excellent performers and a forceful presence. Although they set a terrific example and people are drawn to their vitality and masterful demeanor, if they find themselves in a mentor role they may struggle because they are often too wrapped up in their own goals, lacking the patience to listen to others.

There is a danger they might stretch the truth if they feel it serves their purposes. This isn't motivated by a desire to deceive but simply to make their arguments sound more plausible. If they don't control this tendency, they can become known as untrustworthy. Until age thirty-four they focus on emotional relations, security, and family; for their own happiness they need to discover their feelings and learn how to express them. After thirty-five they enter a period of strength, power, and confidence, and these are the years when they come into their own as inspirational leaders or pioneers.

Above all, they are fired by progressive ideals. Once they develop an understanding of the effect their intensity has on others, it will bring them greater emotional fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Explaining how you feel.

The Way Forward

Opening up to others does not weaken your position; it strengthens it, because other people will find you easier to relate to.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are both successful, positive, and intelligent, and this can be a stimulating and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Forgive Others as Yourself

There is something irritating about everyone, but don't let your own anger or impatience upset others and destroy your happiness and your luck.

At Your Best

Influential, physical, creative.

On the Dark Side

Impatient, detached, untrustworthy.

Love: Aloof

Although often surrounded by potential partners, establishing a close relationship may prove difficult for June 17 natives as they tend to be physically undemonstrative and emotionally aloof. They are attracted to people who are successful or living their dreams, and once they find that special someone they make faithful, protective, and loving partners.

Health: Willpower Needed

Those born on this day often have a strong physical presence and need to make sure they don't succumb to addiction of any kind, be it drink, recreational drugs, or food. Although they have terrific willpower professionally, in their personal life they don't show the same resolve. It is important that they take charge of their well-being by eating healthily and exercising regularly. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to look inward and think of higher, purer things.

Career: Born Conductors

These people have a quick mind and the ability to lead, offering many career opportunities where they can rise to prominent positions. They may prefer to work for themselves. They are well suited to intellectual professions such as law, research, or writing, and to careers involving humanitarian or social reform. A desire for creativity may lead them to the arts, theater, and particularly music or conducting orchestras.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Fight for Their Values

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to get in touch with their feelings so they can express them outwardly. Once they are less detached in their dealings with others, their destiny is to inspire others with their courageous ability to fight for their values.

Power Thought

I don't need external force or validation to feel at harmony with myself.
