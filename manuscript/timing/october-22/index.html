Born on October 22nd

The birthday of the golden aura

Profile

Those born on October 22 have an undeniable golden quality about them. They can light up a room with their presence and are often the center of attention. Whatever they say and do seems to be touched with a special kind of magick that makes them irresistible to others.

There is a magnificent quality about these people; they tend to think big and have no time for small talk or inconsequential matters. Others are drawn to them because of their charisma and their aura of calm authority and competence. Their ability to come up with solutions to problems, to improvise and to get things done fuels the confidence they inspire in others.

Although they possess outstanding qualities, they are not without their weaknesses. Their desire to live life on a grand scale can turn into grandiosity, and they may not notice when they are being arrogant or dismissive toward others. They also have a tendency to gloss over feelings and details in their desire to think and act big, and their conviction that they are uniquely blessed can lead them to take unreasonable risks with money, their career and their relationships.

Until age twenty-nine their key challenge is to learn to manage the power they have to attract and influence others responsibly, ensuring they do not become intoxicated by it. After thirty, there is a turning point that highlights a growing need for freedom, and they may wish to expand their horizons through philosophy, study or travel. Whatever age they are, these extraordinarily gifted individuals need to stay grounded and humble. When they can do that while remaining true to their ideals, they do not just have a golden aura, they live a golden, purposeful and fulfilling life.

Your Greatest Challenge

Staying grounded.

The Way Forward

Understand that no matter how talented or blessed you are, no one is above the rules that govern relationships and success: hard work, discipline and sensitivity toward others.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 20 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You are both independent, creative and visionary, and this can be a stimulating and exciting union.

Luck Maker: Stay Humble

Lucky people never feel that they are above others. They understand that arrogance repels luck and that humility attracts it.

At Your Best

Magnificent, influential, dynamic.

On the Dark Side

Grandiose, arrogant, reckless.

Love: Larger Than Life

Those born on this day tend to have a larger-than-life approach to everything, including love. They are extremely passionate and seductive, and once in a committed relationship they can be devoted and loyal partners. However, they need to be careful that their love of grand gestures does not make them neglect the small, everyday acts of kindness that are the real foundation of lasting love.

Health: Watch the Excesses

These individuals love to live large, and this can extend to their eating and drinking habits. They need to be very careful that their appetites do not endanger their health. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein is essential, and they should limit their intake of alcohol, rich food and sugary treats. Regular exercise is strongly recommended, in particular activities that keep them grounded such as hiking, gardening and yoga. Meditation will also help them develop the inner calm and humility they need for success. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to think of higher things and keep their ego in check.

Career: Born Entertainers

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers that put them in the spotlight, such as entertainment, politics, media, public relations and sales. Their leadership skills may also draw them to business, management and entrepreneurship. Other possible career choices include law, teaching, writing, counseling and the healing and caring professions.

Destiny: To Be a Shining Example of Visionary Leadership

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that true greatness lies in humility and service to others. Once they understand this, their destiny is to be a shining example of what can be achieved when outstanding talent and vision are combined with discipline and compassion.

Power Thought

The brightest light shines from the most grounded place.
