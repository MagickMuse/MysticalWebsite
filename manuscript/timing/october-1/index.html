Born on October 1st

The birthday of unusual capability

Profile

Those born on October 1 are hardworking, multi-talented individuals with an unusual breadth of capability. They seem to be good at everything they turn their hand to, and their versatility and drive make them formidable in any field they choose. What sets them apart from other multi-talented people is their ability to focus their diverse abilities on a single goal and pursue it with extraordinary determination.

They are natural leaders who inspire confidence and respect in others. They have a calm, authoritative manner that makes people want to follow them, and their ability to see the big picture while also attending to the details makes them exceptionally effective managers and organizers. They take their responsibilities seriously and others know they can be relied upon to deliver results.

Despite their impressive capabilities, they can be their own worst enemy. They set themselves punishingly high standards and when they inevitably fall short, they can become harsh and self-critical. They may also find it difficult to relax and enjoy their achievements because they are always focused on the next goal. Learning to celebrate their successes and to be kinder to themselves when they fall short is essential for their happiness and well-being.

Before age fifty-one they are likely to be most focused on issues of personal power, emotional depth and transformation. After fifty-two, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for freedom, adventure and expansion. Whatever age they are, they need to remember that capability is a gift, not a burden. When they can use their remarkable talents with joy, lightness and self-compassion, there truly is nothing they cannot achieve.

Your Greatest Challenge

Celebrating your achievements.

The Way Forward

Understand that taking time to appreciate and celebrate what you have achieved is not laziness or complacency. It is essential fuel for your continued growth and motivation.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both ambitious and driven, and this can be a powerful, passionate and mutually inspiring partnership.

Luck Maker: Enjoy the Journey

Lucky people do not just focus on the destination. They enjoy the journey, and this positive energy attracts more good fortune along the way.

At Your Best

Capable, versatile, authoritative.

On the Dark Side

Self-critical, driven, joyless.

Love: A Capable Partner

Those born on this day are loyal, dependable and deeply caring partners. They bring the same level of dedication and commitment to their relationships as they do to their work. However, they can sometimes be so focused on achieving and providing that they forget to relax, have fun and simply enjoy being with their partner. They need someone who can help them lighten up and live in the moment.

Health: Make Time for Fun

These individuals can be so driven and focused that they forget to have fun, and this can take a toll on their mental and emotional health. It is essential for them to schedule regular periods of leisure, play and relaxation into their busy lives. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will keep them energized, but they should also allow themselves to enjoy treats without guilt. Regular exercise is important, but it should be fun rather than punishing, and activities like dancing, team sports or recreational hiking are ideal. They should also make sure they get enough quality sleep. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage warmth, joy and spontaneity.

Career: Born Achievers

These individuals can succeed in virtually any career because of their exceptional versatility and drive. They may be drawn to careers in management, business, finance, law, politics, science, medicine, engineering or education. Their creative talents may also lead them toward careers in art, music, writing, architecture and design. Their leadership skills make them effective in any role that requires coordination, strategy and vision.

Destiny: To Use Their Remarkable Talents to Inspire and Lead

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to enjoy and celebrate their achievements rather than constantly pushing for more. Once they can combine their exceptional capability with joy and self-compassion, their destiny is to inspire and lead others by their remarkable example.

Power Thought

I celebrate what I have achieved and I am grateful for my many gifts.
