Born on July 1st

The birthday of delicate equilibrium

Profile

Charismatic, enterprising and determined to get ahead in life, those born on July 1 possess an adventurous spirit, a shrewd mind and a remarkable memory. Insightful, intuitive and imaginative, many of them are also great humanitarians capable of making tremendous sacrifices for others and for society as a whole.

Although they have the ability to be extroverts and are often genial and outgoing in public, in private they can be more moody and changeable than most realize. Balancing the introvert and extrovert energies within them, they are equally solitary and gregarious. Between the ages of fifty-one and eighty-one there is an emphasis on pragmatism and service. During these years they need to learn to reconcile their inner and outer selves so that they do not get trapped in only one half of their lives. After the age of eighty-one there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being consistent.

The Way Forward

Understand that when you are ruled by your impulses, you are not in control of your life.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a love of excitement and a need for security, and this can create a passionate but secure union.

Luck Maker: Stop Self-Destructive Behavior

Lucky people do not destroy their own chances of success and happiness with self-sabotaging behavior. Begin to notice when you are doing things that do not promote positive results.

At Your Best

Warm, ambitious, charismatic.

On the Dark Side

Self-doubting, negative, impulsive.

Love: The Big Issue

Love for those born on this day is the big issue of their lives, and they need a partner who is solid and secure and of whom they can feel proud. Loyalty and trust play an important role in their relationships, but they need to make sure that passion and love are not the price they pay for safety and security.

Health: Happy Inside

Those born on this day do not always have a positive relationship with their body, and their health and wellbeing may suffer as a result. They are prone to eating disorders, or to exercising and dieting obsessively. It is extremely important for them to learn to get a sense of balance and perspective, remembering that until they feel happy inside, no amount of exercising or dieting is going to make any difference. Depression is another major health risk for them and they would benefit from counseling or therapy. Listening to music may also be extremely therapeutic, as would plenty of mild mood-boosting aerobic exercise such as walking, dancing and swimming. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will help boost their self-confidence.

Career: Born Actors

These individuals like to be in control, so they may succeed in management or executive positions. They may also prefer to work for themselves. Other careers that might suit them include politics, theater, film, fashion, acting, directing, art, music, dance, the caring professions, social work, teaching or working with children.

Destiny: To Help Alleviate Suffering in the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to give and receive. When they reach this balance, their destiny is to turn the spotlight on injustices and help alleviate suffering.

Power Thought

The only constraints I have are the constraints I decide to believe in.
