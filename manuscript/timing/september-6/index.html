Born on September 6th

The birthday of destiny

Profile

Those born on September 6 tend to identify strongly with their professional role, be it that of mother, father, manager, employee, sportsperson, artist or friend. Their sense of duty and responsibility is so strong that sometimes they may be unable to separate who they are from what they do. Although their dedication and sense of duty are worthy of the greatest respect, they need to guard against investing so much of their identity in one role that they lose sight of the bigger picture and of who they really are.

There is something about these people that suggests that they were always destined for their role. They believe that they were born to do what they are doing and, in the process, they are willing to make great personal sacrifices. In some respects this is a great strength, giving them incredible determination, persistence and focus. But it also has its dangers, as it can make them neglect other areas of their lives, such as their personal lives, their health and their emotional needs.

Until age forty-six there is an emphasis on relationships, social skills and financial success. They need to take advantage of these opportunities to broaden their horizons and not become too narrowly focused on one role. After age forty-seven there is a significant turning point when their need for personal transformation is highlighted, along with a stronger desire for power and control.

Throughout their lives, if they can understand that success and fulfillment in the outside world will always feel incomplete without inner fulfillment, they have the creative potential and the determination to make their mark on the world and, in the process, fulfill their destiny.

Your Greatest Challenge

Separating who you are from what you do.

The Way Forward

Understand that you are not what you do. You are a complex human being with many different roles to fill in this life.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You share a passion for romance and adventure, and this can be a creative and passionate union.

Luck Maker: Do Not Neglect Your Personal Happiness

Whatever your calling in life, do not neglect your personal happiness because, without it, all your achievements will feel hollow.

At Your Best

Dedicated, focused, selfless.

On the Dark Side

Narrow, obsessive, isolated.

Love: Generous and Supportive

Those born on this day are generous and supportive partners, and they do best in relationships with people who can encourage them to be more relaxed and to see other sides of life. They are extremely loyal to those closest to them, and being in a long-term committed relationship will help provide the stability they need to thrive.

Health: Other Outlets

These individuals tend to be health-conscious but only when their health relates to their ability to perform their role. They need to understand that health is more than simply having the physical and mental stamina to perform. It also involves nurturing relationships, having fun, developing interests outside work, and taking time out just for themselves. As far as diet is concerned, they should make sure they eat regular, well-balanced meals, and do not skip meals when distracted by work. Exercise is essential for them, but team sports or sports with a social element would be better suited to their needs than solo training. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more spontaneous and to enjoy life more.

Career: Born Performers

These individuals often feel passionately about their work and may be drawn toward public life or the performing arts. They may also excel in education, research, medicine, social work, the military, administration, politics and business. Whatever career they choose, their dedication and persistence ensure that they often rise to the top.

Destiny: To Fulfill Their Unique Role

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that personal fulfillment and happiness are just as important as professional accomplishment. Once they are able to balance their inner and outer lives, their destiny is to fulfill their unique and important role.

Power Thought

Enjoying life and having fun are important to me.
