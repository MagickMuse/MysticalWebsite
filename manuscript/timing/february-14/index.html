Born on February 14th

The birthday of the vulnerable wit

Profile

Charming, intelligent, and warm-hearted, people born on February 14 are shrewd observers of human foibles. They think quickly and analytically, tending to express themselves succinctly in great one-liners.

The incisive wit of these people can work both for and against them. Their biting sense of humor makes them wonderful, entertaining company and formidable allies in the workplace, yet they can also be blunt to the point of sarcasm, driving others away with deeply wounding comments. Their sharp tongue tends to surface most when they feel impatient or frustrated because others are not attending to their demands. And because people tend to confide in them, they need to be careful not to become gossips.

Jovial banter can be a way for February 14 natives to camouflage their true emotions. They are typically the first to cry when a sad song plays or to feel heartbreaking empathy for those suffering misfortune. This vulnerability can surprise those around them, who often expect someone with such a cool, incisive wit to be emotionally tougher.

Those born on this day should apply some of their penetrating insight to themselves and understand that their emotions bubble so quickly to the surface because they carry an important message. The strong reactions they have to others' misfortunes are often a sign of their own repressed emotions searching for an outlet. Fortunately, around the age of thirty-six a turning point emphasizes becoming more self-aware and assertive about their own emotional needs.

A lamb in wolf's clothing, February 14 people may appear tough but they do not take themselves too seriously. This is not to say they are superficial; there is great emotional depth behind their banter. It simply means they are great company to keep, because whenever they are around, life always seems easier, lighter, and much, much happier.

Your Greatest Challenge

Keeping confidential information to yourself.

The Way Forward

Understand that the trust and respect of others are far more rewarding than the short-lived thrill of being the center of attention.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 22 and June 21

Those dates fall under Gemini. You both have piercing insight, and if you remain honest with each other this can create an intense and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Do Not Have Enemies

Lucky people regard everyone they meet as a potential luck maker. You never know who could eventually be of help. There should always be time for politeness, sensitivity, decency, tact, and trust.

At Your Best

Witty, astute, engaging.

On the Dark Side

Insensitive, cutting, demanding.

Love: Mischievous Flirt

Not surprisingly, February 14 natives have the ability to seduce others with humor and their live-for-the-moment approach to life. They will probably have quite a few relationships before finally settling down with someone who can rise to their verbal challenges while also offering loyalty, honesty, and loving security.

Health: See Green

People born on this day may project the image of being cool and in control, but they can often become stressed from repressing their insecurities behind a tough exterior. It is important for them to find healthy ways to work through those insecurities, such as exercise and honest conversations with friends and loved ones. Breathing exercises and meditation are recommended. A healthy diet and plenty of exercise are essential. They should also steer clear of too much alcohol and nicotine, and avoid mind-altering substances. Meditating on, wearing, or surrounding themselves with the color pale Green will help them manage their emotions.

Career: Born Talk-Show Hosts

These people make ideal interviewers, magazine editors, radio and TV hosts, or networkers in large organizations. Alternatively, banking, insurance, or the stock market may appeal, as might writing, publishing, or the entertainment world. Their empathy for the suffering of others may also draw them toward social reform, humanitarian work, or education. In business, they will use their verbal skills and personality to promote themselves, sometimes all the way to the very top.

Destiny: To Use Words to Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to use their excellent communication skills positively rather than negatively. Once they understand this, their destiny is to influence and inspire those around them with the power of their words.

Power Thought

Giving is its own reward.
