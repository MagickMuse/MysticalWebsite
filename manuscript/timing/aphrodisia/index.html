🕊️Aphrodisia

The Aphrodisia is the bathing festival of Aphrodite and Peitho (Persuasion), held on the fourth day of Hekatombaion in the Athenian calendar.

Ritual Procedure

The rite was preserved in three linked movements.

  • The temple was purified by the sprinkling of dove's blood.
  • The altars were anointed.
  • The two cult statues, one of Aphrodite and one of Peitho, were carried in procession to the washing place, where they received the bathing proper to the festival.

On a normal calendar. The Aphrodisia falls on the fourth day of Hekatombaion, counted from the Noumenia after the summer solstice. It usually lands in July of the Gregorian calendar and shifts each cycle.
