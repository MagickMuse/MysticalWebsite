Born on December 11th

The birthday of intense purpose

Profile

Those born on December 11 may have felt from an early age that there was a serious purpose to their lives. Whatever profession they choose, they are notable for the driving energy and determination they bring to their causes and visions.

As perfectionists, they demand as high a level of commitment and dedication from others as they demand from themselves. This means they often excel professionally, but it can also work against them and exhaust everyone involved, including themselves. In their personal and social life there is no let-up in their intensity; they are influential and persuasive individuals with the ability to win over, or in some cases wear down, others with their charming persistence. When it comes to advancing their cause, one of their favorite approaches is cultivating influential contacts, because they know that with powerful backing almost anything is possible.

Until age forty, a running theme is the need for a more practical and realistic approach to achieving goals. During these years they are likely to assume positions of responsibility or authority, but they should ensure that the single-minded pursuit of goals does not make them manipulative or overly materialistic. It is particularly important that their networking strategy does not slip into ambitious social climbing. After forty-one, a turning point highlights a desire to express individuality and independence. They may become more involved with social issues and establishing a life outside work.

It would benefit them always to consider how others perceive them. Once they discover the lighter side of life, together with the spiritual ideals to balance their materialistic inclinations, they will find that their serious purpose is to become an outstanding human being capable of improving the lives of all around them, and in some cases humanity as a whole.

Your Greatest Challenge

Having fun.

The Way Forward

Understand that the ability to take things less seriously is one of the most powerful and influential ways to get your point across.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You have so much to learn from and love about each other, making this a natural and easygoing combination.

Luck Maker: Have Faith

Studies show that people who believe there is plenty of luck to go around tend to have a higher level of life satisfaction than those who do not. Believe in luck and it will change your life.

At Your Best

Energetic, purposeful, charming.

On the Dark Side

Materialistic, manipulative, selfish.

Love: Inveterate Charmer

Those born on this day are charming and seductive, and few can resist their spell. However, they need to ensure they do not abuse their magnetic power to get whoever they want. They are drawn to ambitious, hardworking individuals like themselves but may feel happier with someone who has a more spontaneous, relaxed approach to life.

Health: Take Care of Your Soul

These individuals pay great attention to appearance and to mental stimulation, but unless they learn to nurture their soul as well they are likely to feel unfulfilled and unhappy. They would benefit greatly from a more spiritual outlook and more quiet time spent reflecting on their real priorities. Their diet should include less red meat and dairy, and more fresh fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, oily fish, nuts and seeds. Ready meals and foods high in additives and preservatives should go. Regular exercise is essential; at least thirty minutes of aerobic activity a day with three to four body-toning sessions a week. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple encourages them to think of higher things.

Career: Born Executives

These individuals may be drawn to engineering, technology or mechanics, but they may also excel in business, debating, law and research. With their fine mind they could be gifted teachers, artists and writers, and their natural executive skills might place them in senior management positions.

Destiny: To Achieve Their Progressive Aims

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that their need for a serious sense of purpose is in fact a need to find a higher purpose. Once they rediscover their spiritual dimension and their sense of humor, their destiny is to work with energy and determination toward the achievement of their progressive aims.

Power Thought

Today I will invite happiness, laughter and love into my life.
