Born on December 17th

The birthday of the practical realist

Profile

Those born on December 17 tend to say exactly what they mean and expect others to do the same. Success to them is something that can be measured in concrete terms, and as practical realists they garner a good deal of responsibility and a reputation for honesty and hard work.

With the courage and vitality to achieve almost any goal they set for themselves, these individuals are doers rather than thinkers. What interests them is facts, results and actions, not dreams, debates or theories. Everything is focused on what can be achieved or produced right now, and this ability to concentrate only on what is before their eyes means they can achieve spectacular results.

Although their friends and family value their sincerity and steady temperament, social involvement can be an area of confusion and difficulty. Their organizational skills mean they are excellent at keeping in touch with old friends, but true intimacy may prove elusive. This is largely because they simply do not understand how important small talk and a sense of humor are for breaking down barriers. It is important for them to learn to be a little less serious and to recognize that emotions sometimes cannot be explained or categorized.

Until age thirty-four, there is an emphasis on practical issues and a need for order and structure. Since they already tend toward pragmatism, it is important during these years that they do not become too materialistic. After thirty-five, a turning point may bring a desire for freedom or a more experimental approach to life. Although disorienting at first, they will ultimately find this change of emphasis liberating. Above all, the key to their success and happiness is their ability to introduce a spiritual dimension into their lives, because this will give them the sense of certainty, truth, order and wonder they have always been seeking.

Your Greatest Challenge

Seeing the funny side.

The Way Forward

Understand that one of the quickest ways to improve life satisfaction is to take everything and everyone, including yourself, a little less seriously.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are both sensual and practical people, and this can be a passionate and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Carry a Good-Luck Charm

What matters is not whether the charm works but the luck-attracting sense of wonder and positive expectation of good fortune it can inspire in you.

At Your Best

Honest, structured, steady.

On the Dark Side

Prosaic, tactless, uninvolved.

Love: For Keeps

Those born on this day are sensual individuals who thrive in the company of intelligent, enterprising people like themselves. They believe in long-term relationships and seek someone they can trust and settle down with. To ensure success, they need to inject some spontaneity and romance.

Health: Stand Tall

These individuals tend to have a sedentary lifestyle, which can negatively affect physical and emotional health, triggering weight problems, fatigue and despondency. Bloating could also be an issue, and to avoid it they should cut down on salt, alcohol and caffeine, drink lots of water and get plenty of fresh air and vigorous exercise. Cutting down on meat, saturated fats and processed foods, and eating fresh, wholesome food such as fruits, vegetables and whole grains will also help. Paying attention to posture, gently pulling in the stomach with the back straight and head held high, will not only make them feel slimmer but more upbeat. They need plenty of quality sleep but should aim for no more than eight hours a night. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages spontaneity.

Career: Born Managers

These individuals may be drawn to careers offering management opportunities, such as business, retail, commerce, administration and sales. They may also excel in education, writing, science or research. Their artistic side may eventually pull them toward music or other creative pursuits.

Destiny: To Pioneer Creative Plans of Action

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more creative in their approach to life. Once they are more in touch with their emotions and those of others, their destiny is to devise pioneering and creative plans of action.

Power Thought

Life for me is a joyful dance.
