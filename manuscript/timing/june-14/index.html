Born on June 14th

The birthday of the supervisor

Profile

The bold individuals born on June 14 often have a powerful desire to take charge of people or situations, whether these are co-workers, friends, family, or projects that need supervising. Their instinctive urge to take control stems from their ability to observe what is going on and quickly sum up what needs to be done and who should do it.

The supreme confidence they have in their convictions is the result of strong self-belief and an inability to stand on the sidelines when work needs to be done. It's no surprise they often make inspirational and dynamic leaders, but their actions can sometimes be perceived as bossy and abrupt by those who prefer to make up their own minds. When their methods are challenged they can become impatient and confrontational, which works against them, especially in personal relationships. It is important for them to anticipate the reactions their forcefulness can arouse.

Until age thirty-seven they may focus on emotional security, and during these years they should develop greater awareness and consideration of others' feelings. After thirty-eight they enter a period of increased strength and confidence; they need to make sure their direct approach does not tip into arrogant certainty.

Above all, they have clear-cut, strong opinions together with a compulsion to take uncompromising action to achieve their far-sighted visions. They are also willing to work very hard, making considerable sacrifices for people or causes close to their heart. Once they learn to respect the sensitivities of others, they have the potential to make outstanding leaders and contributors to whatever field they choose.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting the urge to take charge.

The Way Forward

Sometimes the only way for people to learn and grow is for them to make their own mistakes.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. This is a classic case of opposites attract. You both have so much to learn and gain from each other.

Luck Maker: Treat Everyone with Respect

Treat important people as if they are important and less important people as if they were important too. You never know who might hold the key to your good fortune.

At Your Best

Persistent, authoritative, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Bossy, confrontational, abrupt.

Love: Loners

June 14 natives can be loners, not because they lack admirers but because their lives are so full of activities and interests that there is no time for a special relationship. Although personal space is important, there is a part of them that needs emotional security. Should they fall in love, they will remain hopelessly devoted to their chosen partner.

Health: Pace Yourself

Those born on this day can be impatient not just with others but with themselves. If ill, they often go back to work before they are better. Although their positive attitude helps them shake off illnesses more easily, they need to realize their bodies are telling them to rest. A healthy diet instead of eating on the go is recommended, as is plenty of moderate outdoor exercise. The zesty aroma of lemon will help keep energy levels high. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to be more sensitive to others' feelings.

Career: Born CEOs

These people love the written word and all things media, and would excel in publishing, film, journalism, or television. They also make gifted CEOs and business leaders, and their strongly competitive nature might draw them to sport, sales, commerce, and business.

Destiny: To Lead, Motivate, and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that their opinion is just an opinion and that others have valuable contributions to make as well. Once they consider the views and feelings of others, their destiny is to do what they do best: lead, motivate, and inspire.

Power Thought

Today I will be kind to everyone I meet.
