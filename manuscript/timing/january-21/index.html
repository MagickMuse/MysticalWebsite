Born on January 21st

The birthday of the trendsetter

Profile

Those born on January 21 are life's natural trendsetters. No matter what they are doing or saying, people want to follow them and hear their opinions. They also possess tremendous charm and an ability to get along with virtually anyone. When all of this is paired with their soaring ambition, they have everything they need to reach the top.

Freedom of expression is especially important for people born on this day. They will never find happiness if forced to follow someone else's rules or expectations, needing the space to follow their own instincts. If they stumble along the way, the experience still serves them well, because they have a remarkable gift for learning from their mistakes.

Leadership is something January 21 natives seem ideally suited for, and it is often thrust upon them, but in the long run they are not natural commanders. They simply are not ruthless enough to enforce discipline and routine. They are the ones with the ideas and the spark to launch something new, but it falls to others to carry it through to completion.

Alongside their undeniable star quality, people born on this day have a tendency to speak quickly, sometimes spilling out ideas in a jumbled rush. They also carry a deep need to be liked, which can lead to crippling nervousness and indecision. Learning to think before they speak and to be less rattled by criticism is essential. Fortunately, a turning point often occurs around their thirtieth birthday, sometimes sooner, when their sense of self matures and they begin trusting their own instincts more fully.

Their unusual charm and personality allow them to forge ahead in life and venture into territory few others ever reach. They dislike being tied down, but if they can learn that a little staying power sometimes goes a long way, these boldly original individuals really can shatter boundaries and set new standards for others to aspire to.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to distinguish between your fear and your intuition.

The Way Forward

Understand that intuition is far quieter than fear. You simply know something without needing many words to explain it.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous spirits, and this creates an intensely rewarding union.

Luck Maker: Connect with Your Feelings

The next time you face a decision, check in with your feelings and your intuition before you proceed.

At Your Best

Inventive, optimistic, likable.

On the Dark Side

Needy, chaotic, nervous.

Love: Adventurous

The warmth and charm of January 21 natives can be deeply appealing. They do not enjoy being tied down and prefer to experiment and explore within relationships. This does not mean they are incapable of commitment; it simply means they need a partner who reassures them and respects their need for adventure and variety.

Health: Play a Round of Golf

People born on this day love experimenting with diets and exercise routines, and may even design their own lifestyle plan to share with others. Their open-minded, well-informed approach to health means they generally understand the connection between diet, lifestyle, and well-being and tend to take good care of themselves. That said, they need to watch for extremes. Activities that are both healthy and social, like golf or hiking, are especially positive for them. If stress threatens their balance, burning a chamomile, Lavender, or sandalwood-scented candle can produce a wonderfully calming effect.

Career: Born Artists

The blend of innovation and sensitivity that marks these people gives them great potential in the arts, particularly novel writing. Their engaging personality also makes them gifted idea generators, and they excel in any career that values that skill, from academia and technology to sales and business. Their natural empathy for the underdog may also draw them into charity work, politics, law, and social reform.

Destiny: To Inspire and Improve the Lives of Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to trust and act upon their instincts. Once they have absorbed that lesson, their destiny is to inspire and improve the lives of everyone around them.

Power Thought

My intuition works with me and for me.
