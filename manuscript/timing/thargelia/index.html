🌿Thargelia

The Thargelia is the festival marking the birthdays of Apollo and Artemis, held on the sixth and seventh of Thargelion. The month itself takes its name from this festival.

The First Day: Purification

The first day was given wholly to purification. Two poor people were chosen as pharmakoi, scapegoats, one representing the women of the city and the other the men. The pharmakoi were fed well and then beaten in order to carry away the impurities of the city.

The Second Day: First Fruits

The second day was devoted to offerings of first fruits, called the thargelos, a vegetable and grain stew, or possibly bread. Hymn-singing contests were held for the choirs of men and of boys.

On a normal calendar. The Thargelia falls on the sixth and seventh of Thargelion, generally landing in May of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
