Born on October 21st

The birthday of eloquent charm

Profile

Those born on October 21 have the ability to communicate their ideas with irresistible charm. They are excellent conversationalists who can hold court and are also compelling writers. Whatever field they choose to work in, they have the enviable ability to make people sit up and pay attention.

Word power is the key to their success, and they tend to use it both wisely and effectively. They know when to push their case and when to back down, and their instinct for timing is usually superb. They are also extremely perceptive and intuitive, and this helps them gauge the mood of others so they can adjust their message and delivery accordingly. Other people often find them extremely attractive and are magnetically drawn to them.

Despite their outward show of confidence, however, they can be plagued by self-doubt, and this can hold them back from reaching their full potential. They set themselves very high standards and when they do not live up to them they can become anxious and discouraged. It is important for them to learn to believe in themselves and trust that they have what it takes, because they most certainly do.

Before age thirty they are likely to be most concerned with issues relating to personal power, intensity and transformation. After thirty-one, however, a turning point arrives where they may wish to expand their horizons through travel, education or philosophy. Whatever age they are, the key to their success is to stop doubting themselves and embrace the considerable intellectual, creative and communicative talents they have been blessed with. When they do this, they will discover that their destiny is not just to win friends and influence people but to inspire and educate them as well.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming self-doubt.

The Way Forward

Understand that self-doubt is just a feeling, not a fact. Challenge it by remembering all the times you have succeeded and the talents others admire in you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both articulate and adventurous, and this can be a lively and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Believe You Deserve It

Lucky people believe they are worthy of good fortune. If you do not believe you deserve success, you will sabotage your chances of achieving it.

At Your Best

Eloquent, charming, perceptive.

On the Dark Side

Self-doubting, anxious, over-accommodating.

Love: A Way with Words

Those born on this day have no trouble attracting admirers; their eloquence and charm make them extremely appealing partners. However, they can sometimes use their verbal skills to avoid getting emotionally close to others, hiding behind wit and conversation rather than revealing their true feelings. When they find someone who sees through the words to the vulnerable person within, they can form deep, committed and rewarding bonds.

Health: Trust Yourself

Because self-doubt is a recurring theme for these individuals, stress-related conditions may be an issue for them. Learning techniques to build their self-confidence, such as positive affirmations, visualization and cognitive behavioral therapy, can be extremely beneficial. A regular exercise routine will also help boost their mood and confidence, with team sports or dance classes offering the added bonus of social interaction. A diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes and omega-3 fatty acids will keep their mind sharp and their energy levels stable. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their confidence and self-esteem.

Career: Born Writers

Given their way with words, these individuals may find careers in writing, journalism, public speaking, media, advertising, law, education and politics particularly rewarding. Their creative flair may also draw them toward the arts, music, design and entertainment. Their people skills make them effective in management, counseling and the healing professions.

Destiny: To Inspire and Educate Others with Their Words

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to trust in their considerable talents and abilities. Once they believe in themselves, their destiny is to use their gift of communication to inspire, educate and influence others for the better.

Power Thought

I trust in my abilities and I am worthy of success.
