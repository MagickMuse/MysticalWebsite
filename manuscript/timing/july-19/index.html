Born on July 19th

The birthday of energetic self-awareness

Profile

Those born on July 19 are dynamic, confident individuals who possess a remarkable degree of self-awareness. They understand their own strengths and weaknesses with unusual clarity, and this self-knowledge gives them the ability to present themselves to the world with great authority and impact.

Although they are energetic and outgoing, they also have a deeply reflective side. They are driven by a desire to understand not just themselves but the world around them, and their insights are often remarkably perceptive. Between the ages of thirty-three and sixty-three there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to make sure they use their self-awareness constructively rather than becoming self-critical. After the age of sixty-three there is a turning point highlighting creativity and relationships.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not being too hard on yourself.

The Way Forward

Understand that self-awareness is a gift, but only when it is balanced with self-compassion. Use your insights to grow, not to punish yourself.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 23

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both intense and perceptive, and this can create a deeply passionate and transformative relationship.

Luck Maker: Be Kind to Yourself

Lucky people understand that self-compassion is not self-indulgence. Being kind to yourself creates the positive energy and confidence you need to attract good fortune.

At Your Best

Self-aware, dynamic, insightful.

On the Dark Side

Self-critical, intense, controlling.

Love: Passionate and Devoted

Those born on this day are passionate and devoted partners who bring the same intensity to their relationships as they do to everything else in their lives. They need a partner who can appreciate their depth and who is not intimidated by their self-awareness. They should be careful not to be too analytical about their relationships.

Health: Listen to Your Body

Those born on this day are so self-aware that they may become overly focused on their physical health, leading to hypochondria or excessive worry. It is important for them to find a balance between health consciousness and health anxiety. Regular exercise is essential, especially activities that help them get out of their head and into their body, such as dancing, martial arts or swimming. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat wholesome, natural foods and avoid fad diets. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more spontaneous and joyful.

Career: Born Psychologists

These individuals are drawn to careers that require deep understanding of human nature. They may excel in psychology, counseling, teaching, writing, journalism or research. Their dynamic energy may also draw them to leadership positions in business, politics or the arts.

Destiny: To Use Their Self-Knowledge to Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance self-awareness with self-acceptance. Once they have done that, their destiny is to use their remarkable self-knowledge and energy to inspire and empower others.

Power Thought

I accept myself completely and celebrate who I am.
