Best Day for Magick

Monday is the best day of the week to perform magick for spirituality, virtue, emotional security, and well-being.

Lunar Magick

Monday carries the Moon's energy directly, making it one of the most potent days for lunar workings of any kind. Pair Monday with a favorable moon phase for amplified results — healing, home blessings, intuitive development, psychic practice, and emotional workings all flourish on this day. A waxing moon on Monday is especially powerful for drawing in what you need; a waning moon on Monday supports release and cleansing.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.
