Born on January 26th

The birthday of the last word

Profile

Strong-willed and enterprising, those born on January 26 possess a commanding presence that demands attention. They love to spearhead new trends and ideas, and their determination and success-oriented approach give them the potential to turn their biggest dreams into reality.

The natural air of authority and insistence on having the final say that characterize January 26 natives make them outstanding leaders. They excel at motivating and organizing others, firmly believing that the only way to move things forward is for somebody, preferably themselves, to take charge. They are pioneers of new ventures who generally earn the deep respect of those around them, especially those who work under their guidance.

While they are honest and authoritative, patience is not their strongest suit. They are prone to snap judgments about people and to making decisions without consulting anyone else, which can breed antagonism. The one thing they cannot tolerate is having their authority questioned. It is vital that they keep an open mind and carefully weigh the pros and cons before acting. Once they appreciate the power of compromise, their grounded approach and dynamic energy will guarantee both success and the loyalty of others.

Those born on this day are always found where the action is. They are intensely success-oriented, but to lead a truly balanced life and experience deeper happiness they need to pay more attention to their inner world and their relationships. Fortunately, after the age of approximately twenty-five, and sometimes later, they begin to grow more sensitive, inner-focused, and less fixated on external achievement.

Part of the secret behind the success January 26 people attract is their ability to bounce back from setbacks. Difficult experiences in childhood and adolescence taught them they have the power to surprise everyone who doubts them, and once they know what they want, nothing can stand in their way.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping when your authority or your ideas are questioned.

The Way Forward

Always listen to viewpoints that differ from your own, because sometimes other people can see what you may have missed.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. You both love to be where the action is but are also capable of a deep sense of connection.

Luck Maker: Learn to Listen

Those who attract good fortune know how to listen, because they understand that someone else may have the next great idea.

At Your Best

Dynamic, enterprising, determined.

On the Dark Side

Inflexible, opinionated, dictatorial.

Love: Born Flirts

January 26 natives are natural flirts and run the risk of becoming involved with someone for the wrong reasons, perhaps because that person might help them climb the social or professional ladder. This is a dangerous approach to love and one they should avoid at all costs, because they are more than capable of giving a relationship everything they have.

Health: Early to Rise

People born on this day need to avoid extremes, and an early-to-bed, early-to-rise routine does wonders for their health. They should watch out for sudden bursts of anger and frustration, which can make them accident-prone, particularly around the legs, knees, and ankles. Competitive sports are not advisable, but moderate exercise such as walking, swimming, and cycling suits them well. Their diet benefits from less meat and dairy and more whole grains, vegetables, legumes, nuts, and seeds, with omega-3 fish oils if their skin tends toward dryness or sensitivity. Placing an Amethyst crystal under their pillow or wearing amethyst jewelry will help foster feelings of calm and contentment.

Career: Natural Leader

These people undoubtedly possess natural leadership qualities, and this will carry them far if they can avoid power struggles. Their ability to spot opportunities makes them excellent sales leaders, agents, negotiators, CEOs, managers, and advisors. Their individual streak may also express itself in media and entertainment, or they may find fulfillment as consultants or natural therapists.

Destiny: Trendsetter

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to motivate people through the spirit of cooperation rather than the spirit of dictatorship. Once they grasp the importance of compromise, their destiny is to stand at the forefront of new trends and ideas.

Power Thought

Today I will be prepared to see life differently.
