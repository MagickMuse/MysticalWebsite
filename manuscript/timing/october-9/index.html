Born on October 9th

The birthday of the psychologist

Profile

Those born on October 9 have an extraordinary ability to read people. They are natural psychologists with an intuitive understanding of what makes others tick. Their perceptive powers are so highly developed that they can often sense what someone is thinking or feeling before that person has even articulated it themselves.

This gift for reading people makes them invaluable friends, colleagues and advisors. Others trust them with their deepest secrets and most sensitive problems because they know they will be understood, not judged. They have a calm, reassuring presence that puts people at ease, and their advice is always thoughtful, considered and deeply insightful.

Despite their ability to understand others, they can find it much harder to understand themselves. They may be so focused on the emotional lives of other people that they neglect their own inner world. They can also use their psychological insight as a defense mechanism, analyzing their own feelings rather than actually experiencing them. Learning to turn their perceptive gaze inward and to feel as well as think is one of their most important life challenges.

Before age forty-three they are likely to be most focused on issues of emotional intensity and personal transformation. After forty-four, a turning point arrives that highlights a need for more freedom, adventure and philosophical exploration. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that they use their remarkable gift for understanding others to build genuine connections rather than to keep people at a safe emotional distance. When they allow themselves to be as vulnerable as the people who confide in them, they unlock their full potential for deep, meaningful and transformative relationships.

Your Greatest Challenge

Understanding yourself as well as you understand others.

The Way Forward

Understand that the same compassion and insight you offer others is something you deserve to receive, including from yourself. Turn your perceptive gaze inward.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You bring depth and insight, and they bring energy and directness. This can be a stimulating and mutually enriching partnership.

Luck Maker: Feel Your Feelings

Lucky people do not just think about their emotions, they feel them. When you allow yourself to fully experience your feelings, you make better decisions and attract more authentic connections.

At Your Best

Perceptive, compassionate, insightful.

On the Dark Side

Detached, self-neglecting, over-analytical.

Love: The Confidant

Those born on this day are deeply caring partners who will always be there to listen, support and advise. They are attracted to individuals who are emotionally expressive and who can help draw them out of their analytical shell. They need a partner who will not just lean on them but who will also encourage them to share their own feelings and vulnerabilities.

Health: Nourish Your Inner World

These individuals spend so much energy supporting others that they can deplete their own reserves. It is essential for them to schedule regular periods of solitude and self-care. Practices like meditation, journaling and creative expression can help them connect with their own emotions. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein and omega-3 fatty acids will support their mental and emotional health. Regular exercise, particularly walking, swimming or cycling, will help them clear their mind and process their feelings. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their self-confidence and encourage them to prioritize their own needs.

Career: Born Therapists

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in psychology, counseling, therapy, social work, psychiatry and coaching. Their perceptive nature also suits them to careers in writing, journalism, research, detective work and law. Their desire to help others may lead them toward medicine, nursing, charity work and humanitarian causes.

Destiny: To Use Their Insight to Foster Genuine Human Connection

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be as open and vulnerable with themselves as others are with them. Once they can do this, their destiny is to use their extraordinary insight into human nature to foster genuine, deep and transformative connections.

Power Thought

I honor my own feelings with the same compassion I show to others.
