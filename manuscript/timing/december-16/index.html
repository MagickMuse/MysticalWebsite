Born on December 16th

The birthday of the creative anthropologist

Profile

Those born on December 16 are blessed with a soaring imagination, but their logical and objective approach to situations and people ensures that they never succumb to flights of fancy. In fact, they have the ability to view everyone and everything in their life with the inquisitive detachment of an anthropologist.

Nothing escapes the scrutiny of these intelligent and sharp-witted individuals, and when their analytical, logical and determined mind is combined with their imaginative intellectual qualities, they have the potential to engineer far-reaching innovations. Once they have settled on their objectives, they pursue them with remarkable determination. Although this dramatically enhances their potential for professional success, as they often rise to management level or the very top of their careers, their single-minded focus on career goals can isolate them emotionally from others.

Until age thirty-five, they lean toward a more practical and realistic approach to their goals, and these are the years when they are most likely to lose themselves in their career. During this time, establishing a work-life balance and becoming more sensitive to the feelings of others are important. After thirty-six, a significant turning point highlights a need to be free from responsibilities, to be more independent and to express their individuality. They may become involved with humanitarian ideals or questions of universal spirituality. At first this shift may feel confusing and disorienting, but as the years go by they start to understand themselves better and experience life less as a research lab and more on a deeper, intuitive level where certain things are simply to be experienced rather than examined.

They also begin to realize that the key to unlocking their potential for outstanding achievement is to keep their lives as real and grounded as possible, so that they are relaxed and receptive enough to tune into their powerful intuition and make the right choices for themselves and others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting the impulse to criticize.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes the best approach to a situation is to focus on the solution, not the problem.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both imaginative and adventurous, and this can be an exciting and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Don't Isolate Yourself

If you isolate yourself to satisfy career aspirations or personal interests, you isolate yourself from the biggest source of luck in life: other people.

At Your Best

Innovative, thorough, visionary.

On the Dark Side

Impractical, isolated, unaware.

Love: Poetic Spirit

Those born on this day are loving and supportive partners who pride themselves on being good judges of character. They understand the importance of give and take, but sometimes their pragmatic approach can be interpreted as passionless, so it is important for them to be more spontaneous to keep the romantic sparkle alive. They tend to be attracted to artistic or creative individuals, because deep inside is a poet's spirit longing to express itself.

Health: The Color Violet

These individuals can be prone to fatigue and digestive disorders with unexplained origins, and they may find there is more stress in their lives than they realize. Their diet may not be as healthy or nutritious as it could be, and their exercise routine may need updating. Increasing fruits, vegetables, whole grains and legumes will boost digestive health; moderate exercise, preferably outdoors to get mood-boosting benefits of sunshine, will improve mood and sleep. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color violet helps them get in touch with their intuition.

Career: Born Psychologists

These individuals may feel stifled within corporate structures and prefer to work for themselves. They are well suited to research, science, production management and software development. Other options include education, writing, politics, strategic planning, music, art, acting and psychology.

Destiny: To Pioneer Far-Reaching Innovations and Ideals

The life path of those born on this day is to get in touch with their own feelings and those of others. Once they develop an intuitive sense of what is right and wrong for them, their destiny is to pioneer far-reaching innovations and ideals.

Power Thought

The universe flows through me and my intuition continually guides me.
