Born on March 29th

The birthday of the discerning presence

Profile

Undoubtedly intuitive, people born on March 29 like to observe everything going on around them, carefully considering all aspects of a situation before making a decision. This slow and steady approach to life often proves highly successful. Others may criticize them for being overly cautious or lacking focus, passion, and commitment, but they are the ones with the knack of winning in the end.

These people are often polite and genuine in every aspect of their life. Not to be so would imply rudeness or insincerity, and their intellect, sensitivity, and honesty would not stand for this. They are not driven by personal ambition but by a desire to make a positive difference in the world through their perception and understanding. In fact, they can be perhaps a little too wise at times, which can lead them to becoming disillusioned if they are not careful, especially when it comes to close personal relationships.

The danger for those born on this day is that their caution can lead to negativity or pessimism. It is important for them not to spiral into cynicism if people let them down. They need to understand that human beings are complex creatures with both strengths and weaknesses, and that it is far better to believe the best of people than the worst. People have a tendency to live up to the expectations placed upon them. Between the ages of twenty-one and fifty-one they need to be especially careful not to sink into inflexibility, as there is an emphasis in their life on security, stability, and establishing themselves.

Although they do like to keep to themselves, when in public their discerning presence has a calming and soothing influence on those around them. Their self-containment can also — much to their surprise — thrust them into the limelight. These authentic, loyal, and coolly intelligent individuals, with their own enigmatic purity and beauty, are more than qualified to assume the reins of power when put into positions of authority and leadership.

Your Greatest Challenge

Asserting yourself.

The Way Forward

Understand that being assertive is not the same as being aggressive or rude. You are simply making sure your valuable contribution is recognized.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You are both genuine and open individuals, and this can create a lasting union of rare honesty.

Luck Maker: Expect Good Luck

Do not merely hope for good fortune — expect it. People who are lucky believe with absolute certainty that they will succeed, even when life does not go according to plan. We tend to get what we expect.

At Your Best

Creative, genuine, perceptive.

On the Dark Side

Lacking in focus, aloof, cautious.

Love: At First Sight

When it comes to relationships, March 29 natives are extremely romantic and intense. They believe in love at first sight but are unlikely to throw themselves into a love affair without first weighing up the pros and cons. Once they have made up their mind that they have found "the one," however, they rarely meet any resistance, remaining loyal and deeply affectionate partners for life.

Health: Highly Sensitive

People born on this day are prone to hormonal imbalances, skin problems, and food allergies. Being so sensitive, it is important for them to make sure they eat a healthy diet and avoid foods high in sugar, saturated fat, salt, additives, and preservatives. They do not generally have weight problems if they stay active, but there may be issues with bloating and weight gain around the middle, especially in midlife; again, healthy eating and a daily exercise program can help prevent this. Making sure they get plenty of time to relax, unwind, and recharge their energy is important, as is spending time with a close circle of friends. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the colors Orange and Yellow will encourage them to be more optimistic.

Career: Born Producers

Whatever career these positive and multi-talented individuals choose, they are likely to be successful. The fields of education, publishing, law, business, computing, engineering, medicine, and social reform may particularly appeal. If they choose to express their creative side, they may be drawn to music, art, dance, photography, and film production.

Destiny: To Inspire, Comfort, and Influence Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more assertive so that others do not take them for granted or assume credit for their work. Once they have ensured they do not go unrecognized, their destiny is to inspire, comfort, and influence others with their loyalty, steadfastness, calmness, and courage.

Power Thought

I deserve and expect only the very best.
