Born on July 21st

The birthday of daring

Profile

Those born on July 21 are exciting and dramatic individuals who love to push the boundaries of what is possible. They have a daring, adventurous spirit that refuses to be confined by convention, and their wild energy and enthusiasm can be incredibly infectious.

Although their boldness can sometimes border on recklessness, they are also surprisingly resilient and possess an ability to learn from their mistakes. They are driven by a desire to experience everything life has to offer. Between the ages of thirty-one and sixty-one there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to find constructive outlets for their tremendous energy. After the age of sixty-one there is a turning point highlighting their growing need for partnerships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding excess.

The Way Forward

Understand that moderation is not the enemy of excitement. Some of the most thrilling experiences come from mastering discipline and focus.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous and passionate, and this can create an exciting and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Know Your Limits

Lucky people understand that knowing when to stop is just as important as knowing when to start. Setting healthy boundaries protects your energy and your luck.

At Your Best

Exciting, dramatic, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Reckless, excessive, attention-seeking.

Love: Thrilling and Intense

Those born on this day are thrilling and intense partners who love the excitement of new romance. They need a partner who can keep up with their adventurous spirit and who is not afraid of their dramatic energy. They should be careful not to let their love of excitement lead to instability in their relationships.

Health: Moderation Is Key

Those born on this day are prone to excess in all areas of life, including diet, exercise and social activities. It is important for them to learn the value of moderation. They should be careful not to overindulge in rich food and drink, and should aim for a balanced diet with plenty of fresh fruits and vegetables. Regular exercise is essential but they should avoid overdoing it. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to be more calm and reflective.

Career: Born Performers

These individuals are natural performers and may excel in acting, music, dance, comedy or any career that allows them to express their dramatic energy. They may also be drawn to extreme sports, exploration, journalism or entrepreneurship. Their daring nature may lead them to careers in the military, aviation or emergency services.

Destiny: To Push the Boundaries of What Is Possible

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their daring nature with discipline and moderation. Once they have done that, their destiny is to push the boundaries of what is possible and inspire others with their courageous example.

Power Thought

I find adventure in stillness and excitement in peace.
