Born on February 12th

The birthday of the bold integrator

Profile

Those born on February 12 have a talent for integration: the ability to marshal all available information, evaluate opposing viewpoints, and then bring everything and everyone together in a united front. Nothing matters more to them than the status quo, and if need be they will boldly and courageously defend it. This makes them highly valued in both their professional and personal lives.

People born on this day like to see themselves in the role of peacekeeper, pointing others in the right direction — which is, of course, the direction they believe to be correct. This is not to say they are stubborn and inflexible, but they do tend to dismiss what others think and to believe that the best course of action is always their own. It is important for them to recognize the value of consensus and to understand that while their ability to see and evaluate the bigger picture more than qualifies them to lead, great leadership is not about dictatorship but about motivating others.

As well as uniting others and seeing the way forward with single-minded tenacity, February 12 natives possess confidence, originality, and creativity. They do need to be careful, however, that their many talents do not cause them to scatter their energies in too many directions. Until their late thirties there are opportunities to develop greater self-awareness, but in their forties and beyond a focus on personal integration and purpose takes center stage; in many ways, this is when these people truly come into their own.

With strong convictions and a progressive instinct developed through powers of original thought and high moral and ethical standards, they have the courage and charisma to lead and inspire others. More often than not, they can be found living out their mission to make the world a better and more peaceful place.

Your Greatest Challenge

Focusing your energy on one project alone.

The Way Forward

Understand that developing concentration is essential for anyone who aspires to take charge of their life. Without it, efforts become scattered.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. Both fun-loving and focused on achievement, the two of you love to communicate, creating an intense and loving bond.

Luck Maker: Learn to Meditate

Meditation is a luck maker because it not only helps ease stress but also teaches you to direct and focus your thoughts so they can attract success.

At Your Best

Patient, purposeful, original.

On the Dark Side

Inflexible, moody, intolerant.

Love: Fun-Loving

Although February 12 natives have no trouble falling in love, they need to be careful that their work and goals do not overshadow their relationships or that they take a partner for granted. They prefer to get involved with people who are mentally stimulating and interested in self-improvement as well as having a good time. Their detached exterior can make it hard to open up, but once they do they become sensitive, loyal, and fun-loving partners.

Health: Health Before Habit

People born on this day are often creatures of routine when it comes to health, diet, and exercise, and although this generally keeps them in good shape, experimenting a little can give them more energy and more fun. They should try a wide variety of nutritious foods rather than sticking with old favorites and explore different types of exercise. When feeling stressed or anxious, a warm bath with a few drops of their favorite aromatherapy essential oil will soothe frayed nerves far better than alcohol or chocolate. Meditation and breathing techniques can also help focus their energy and thoughts.

Career: Born Politicians

These people may well consider politics or social reform as their vocation. Whatever they choose, they are likely to rise to the top, perhaps as the principal of a school or a group leader. Their people skills and business sense may draw them toward management. Other paths include counseling, publishing, advertising, accountancy, science, invention, anthropology, archaeology, writing, and the arts.

Destiny: To Create Harmony in All Environments

The life path of those born on this day is to beware of their tendency to dismiss viewpoints that differ from their own. Once they learn to be more open-minded, their destiny is to create harmony in any environment in which they find themselves.

Power Thought

The equilibrium of my mind is mirrored in my life.
