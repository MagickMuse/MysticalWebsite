Born on December 14th

The birthday of the flamboyant philosopher

Profile

Those born on December 14 rarely merge into the crowd. This does not mean they are attention seekers. Quite the opposite: they can be deeply private individuals. It is just that their flamboyant tastes and unique ideas set them apart, as do their controlled energy, capability and efficiency. As natural philosophers, finding their own truth rather than conforming to the rules of others will always be important to them.

Although their provocative nature can outrage, it can also surprise and stimulate others, encouraging people to think outside the box. Nothing gives them greater satisfaction than knowing they have fired someone's creativity or imagination. Activity and progress are their driving forces, and they cannot bear complacency when there are issues to explore and advances to be made. Blessed with strong organizational skills and considerable energy, they approach everything with enthusiasm, determination and insatiable curiosity.

Sociable and generous to others, they are also extremely self-contained and self-reliant. Their potential for fulfillment lies in the quest for discovery and progress. Although they will never turn away those who come to them for guidance, part of them longs to shut out all outside distraction so they can pursue personal goals undisturbed. They prefer to step into the limelight only when the time is right to reveal the fruits of their labor.

Until age thirty-eight, there is an emphasis on practical order and structure, making them more goal-oriented and responsible. After thirty-nine, a turning point brings more concern with expressing individuality and contributing to the world. The keys to success are their ability to find a work-life balance and their willingness to develop diplomatic skills, motivating others to follow their example and become potent, productive and truth-seeking forces for positive change.

Your Greatest Challenge

Getting your priorities right.

The Way Forward

Take time for introspection, because until you get your priorities right, life will feel confusing and overwhelming.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 21 and June 20

Those dates fall under Gemini. You both love freedom, and if you can stay in one place, this relationship can be creative and passionate.

Luck Maker: Strengthen Your Self-Esteem

Attracting luck into your life begins with the way you think and feel about yourself. If you do not feel good about who you are, how can you expect others to improve your fortune?

At Your Best

Dramatic, original, bold.

On the Dark Side

Withdrawn, workaholic, lonely.

Love: Unique and Intelligent

Those born on this day like to mix with people who are as intellectually quick and inquisitive as themselves. Although idealistic, they may not always be good at opening up emotionally, and if they have been hurt deeply, work may take center stage. When they find someone who loves them for their uniqueness, they truly come into their own.

Health: Get Your Life in Balance

Low self-esteem can have a negative effect on emotional and physical health. Burying themselves in work or withdrawing from others is not the answer; taking care of mind, body, heart and soul is. Only when all four aspects of life are nurtured daily will everything feel balanced and self-esteem soar. Recreational drugs should be avoided entirely and alcohol consumption kept in check, as addictive tendencies may be present. The secret to diet is keeping everything as balanced and structured as possible, with moderation in all things. Mild to moderate exercise is recommended for its calming effect, and wearing or meditating on the color Blue encourages a more objective and diplomatic approach to life.

Career: Born Philosophers

Decisiveness and perceptiveness make these individuals natural leaders, but they tend to thrive in careers where they can operate independently and where research or development plays a key role. Possible paths include sales, business, education, acting, philosophy, writing, advertising, publishing and sport.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Discover and Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance emotional needs with professional ambitions. Once they consider the impact of their words and actions, their destiny is to inspire others to look in the direction of discovery and progress.

Power Thought

My positive energy attracts only good things into my life.
