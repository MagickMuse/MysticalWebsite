Born on December 13th

The birthday of erratic precision

Profile

Those born on December 13 bring confidence, resourcefulness and tenacity to everything they do, plus a precise and occasionally painstaking attention to the smallest detail. Although their potential for professional and personal success in the long term is great, their careful and time-consuming approach can become overly cautious and hesitant, which can unfortunately lead to disillusionment.

These individuals with an eye for detail can be incredibly observant and perceptive when it comes to other people. Regrettably, when it comes to themselves they can lack self-awareness and may not realize that they have erratic habits that irritate others and stop them working as efficiently as they might like. For example, they do not always seem to know when to back off when an argument is going nowhere or when their point has been made, and will unnecessarily restate their case over and over again. They may also procrastinate when something important needs to be done, making life much harder for themselves than it needs to be.

Until age thirty-eight, they may feel a need for a practical and realistic approach to achieving their goals. These are the years when they need to be especially careful not to focus so much on the details that they lose sight of the bigger picture. After thirty-nine, a turning point brings a desire to express their individuality more, and this can be an incredibly liberating time when they start to put their personal mark on the success they have already built.

Regardless of age, they need to guard against becoming too exacting and finicky. When they are able to step back and look at the impressive picture they are painting with their lives, they will realize they have both a lot to be grateful for and a lot to look forward to.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting go.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes things run their course and no amount of craftsmanship can make them run properly again.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 20 and February 18

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You are both broadminded and inquisitive individuals, and this relationship has great potential for happiness.

Luck Maker: Stop Waiting

Be ready to grab luck, even if it happens before you are fully prepared. Unlucky people are afraid of making mistakes and looking silly, but lucky people seize the moment whether they are ready for it or not.

At Your Best

Thorough, insightful, inquisitive.

On the Dark Side

Fussy, exacting, procrastinating.

Love: Don't Put Your Lover on a Pedestal

Those born on this day have a passionate side, and when it is first unleashed in a relationship they feel liberated and their partner feels cherished. However, they need to resist the tendency to put their partner on a pedestal and then wear them down with constant criticism and nitpicking. Once they understand that no one is perfect, they get their priorities right and place love at the very top.

Health: Balance Your Blood Sugar

Work is extremely important for these individuals, but for their health and happiness they need to cultivate things that bring them joy. They may also tend to socialize only with colleagues from work; looking to the outside world for companionship might bring far greater happiness. They should cut down on caffeine and sugar, which can cause anxiety and extreme blood sugar fluctuations, and reach instead for fruit, vegetables or a wholegrain cracker. Regular moderate-intensity exercise will ease anxiety and boost well-being. Regular check-ups should be scheduled, but they should also make sure they do not become obsessive about health, as this is counterproductive. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages creativity and spontaneity.

Career: Born Computer Programmers

These individuals thrive in careers where patience and attention to detail are essential. They may be drawn to editing, restorative or museum work, art, writing, decorating and archaeology, as well as computer programming. Occupations involving travel and variety will be beneficial, as will any work that keeps them mentally challenged.

Destiny: To Suggest Ingenious and Effective Technical Advances

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to move on when situations cannot be mended or their point has been made. Once they learn to look at themselves as objectively as they look at others, their destiny is to suggest ingenious and effective technical advances.

Power Thought

Every day I am more and more at ease with myself and with life.
