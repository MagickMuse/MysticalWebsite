Born on October 8th

The birthday of the free spirit

Profile

Those born on October 8 are independent, adventurous individuals with a powerful need for personal freedom. They cannot bear to have their wings clipped in any way and will resist any attempt to confine, restrict or control them. This love of freedom extends to every area of their lives, from their career to their relationships, and it gives them a restless, questing energy that makes them fascinating and unpredictable companions.

They are natural rebels who question authority and challenge convention. They have their own way of doing things and refuse to follow the crowd. This independent streak can sometimes alienate others, but more often it inspires admiration and respect. Others are drawn to their authenticity and their courage to live life on their own terms.

Despite their love of independence, they are not loners. They enjoy the company of others and can be warm, generous and supportive friends and partners. They are, however, selective about the company they keep. They are attracted to people who share their love of freedom and who will not try to cage them. They also have a strong sense of justice and will fight passionately for the rights and freedoms of others.

Before age forty-four they are likely to be most focused on issues of emotional intensity, personal power and transformation. After forty-five, a turning point arrives that emphasizes a growing need for freedom, optimism and expansion, perhaps through travel, education or philosophy. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that their love of freedom does not become an excuse to avoid commitment or responsibility. When they can balance their need for independence with the willingness to commit to people, projects and causes they believe in, they become truly inspirational free spirits who show others what it means to live authentically.

Your Greatest Challenge

Committing without feeling trapped.

The Way Forward

Understand that commitment is not a cage. When you choose to commit freely, it becomes an expression of your freedom, not a limitation of it.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 20 and May 20

Those dates fall under Taurus. You bring adventure and excitement, and they bring stability and sensuality. This can be a grounding and exhilarating partnership.

Luck Maker: Know When to Land

Lucky people know that while flying high is exhilarating, you also need to land from time to time. Grounding yourself in routines, relationships and commitments creates a stable platform from which to launch your next adventure.

At Your Best

Independent, authentic, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Restless, noncommittal, rebellious.

Love: Freedom and Togetherness

Those born on this day need a partner who understands and respects their need for personal space and independence. They are passionate and exciting lovers, but they can become restless or distant if they feel smothered. The ideal partner for them is someone who is equally independent and who can enjoy the thrill of togetherness without losing their own identity.

Health: Ground Yourself

These individuals can be so restless and on-the-go that they forget to take care of their basic health needs. It is important for them to establish regular routines for eating, sleeping and exercising. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will keep their energy levels stable. Regular exercise is essential, and activities that combine physical exertion with a sense of freedom, such as running, hiking, cycling or horseback riding, are ideal. They should also make sure they get enough quality sleep, as their restless minds can keep them awake at night. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage balance and stability.

Career: Born Explorers

These individuals thrive in careers that offer freedom, variety and the opportunity to break new ground, such as travel, journalism, photography, filmmaking, entrepreneurship and the arts. Their rebellious spirit may also draw them toward careers in social reform, activism, law and politics. Their independence makes them well suited to freelance work and self-employment.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Live Authentically

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance freedom with commitment. Once they understand that true freedom includes the freedom to commit, their destiny is to inspire others to live more authentically and courageously.

Power Thought

I am free to commit, and my commitments set me free.
