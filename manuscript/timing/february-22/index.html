Born on February 22nd

The birthday of the investigator

Profile

People born on February 22 love nothing better than a good mystery. They are born problem solvers with an inquisitive, intuitive mind and a natural talent for unearthing the truth.

The chances are those born on this day do not have a conventional career, or if they do, their hobbies and interests reflect their eclectic tastes. They may have many different ideas about their life path, trying to pursue opposing things simultaneously; this can confuse and frustrate others while also fascinating them. There is always method in their apparent madness, however. They believe that people are defined not by what they do but by how they do it. Whatever activity they pursue, one thing remains central: they can indulge their passion for investigation and problem solving.

Although these people are particularly good at uncovering information and proposing solutions, one area of their life is often neglected: their own inner world. Because they tend to be self-reliant, they often expect others to be the same. They deny themselves emotional security by not sharing their anxieties with those closest to them. They also hold rather high standards, and when others do not live up to them they can become overly critical and pessimistic. Learning to open up to others and to be more understanding of those who cannot meet their expectations, including themselves, is essential. They can be extremely self-critical when they do not find an answer immediately and would benefit from a more compassionate approach.

The tendency to experiment with many directions is highlighted between the ages of twenty-eight and fifty-seven. After fifty-seven they may focus their energies in a single direction. But whatever age they are and whatever goal they are currently pursuing, one thing is certain: it will always be a fascinating one that may take them a step closer to their ultimate goal of making their own life — and the lives of others — a little less complicated and a whole lot more truthful.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being less self-critical.

The Way Forward

Understand that perfection is an impossible ideal; our failures sometimes propel us toward success, and our vulnerabilities bring others closer to us.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You share a passion for the mystery of love, and this creates a stimulating and intense union.

Luck Maker: Get a Kick Out of Being You

Only when you are comfortable with yourself can you be confident in your skills and talents, and deal positively with negative criticism. Recognize your own value and others will soon see it too.

At Your Best

Dynamic, problem-solving, articulate.

On the Dark Side

Detached, over-critical, pessimistic.

Love: Falling In and Out of Love

February 22 natives tend to be a bit fickle in close personal relationships, blowing hot one day and cold the next. This can be terribly confusing for those near them, and they need to learn the importance of commitment and honesty in a fulfilling relationship. Once they place real value on emotional honesty, they make endlessly fascinating and exciting lovers.

Health: Keep It Steady

It is likely that those born on this day will neglect their health, especially when passionately engaged in their current hobby or interest. It is vital for them to eat regular meals and snacks, leaving no more than three hours between eating to keep energy levels high and give a regular supply of glucose to their constantly hungry brain. A vitamin and mineral supplement may also help. Vigorous or competitive, preferably social, sports are recommended for exercise. Reading, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the colors Pink or Green will encourage them to be more nurturing and less detached.

Career: Born Investigators

People born on this day thrive in careers that offer plenty of variety and utilize their problem-solving skills. They make great accountants, secret agents, detectives, scientists, and politicians. Their communication skills also suggest success as writers, journalists, musicians, or actors. The part of them interested in the bigger picture may draw them to the clergy, the caring professions, alternative healing, social reform, or the world of medicine.

Destiny: To Help and Heal Others

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that diversity and problem solving are their gifts. Once they accept themselves as they are, their destiny is to help and heal others.

Power Thought

I focus on people's good points and help them bring out their best qualities.
