Born on June 1st

The birthday of the enigmatic student

Profile

People born on June 1 are adored for their wit, humor, and entertaining personalities. Chatty, fun, and mischievous, they possess a mercurial inquisitiveness that rarely allows them to focus on one subject for long because details bore them. There is, however, one topic that never fails to fascinate them: human behavior.

In all areas of their lives, they tend to keep the focus on other people, studying and imitating the styles of those at the top in hopes of achieving similar success. Since feelings often follow behavior, this approach frequently works. The downside is that they never truly get to know themselves or discover their own talents, hopes, and dreams.

Their positive energy attracts many admirers, though they can be vain and tempted to flit from one admirer to another depending on who flatters them most. This need for flattery often stems from deep feelings of uncertainty and confusion. Despite their compulsive interest in others, they are private individuals who rarely reveal their deeper thoughts. Between the ages of twenty and fifty there are opportunities to establish their own individuality; it is crucial that they don't scatter their energies on unworthy causes. After fifty they become more confident and self-assured.

If they can find the courage to trust their instincts, they will unite the enigmatic and melancholic aspects of their personality with their impulsive and charismatic sides, giving them the focus they need to realize their own unique potential.

Your Greatest Challenge

Understanding yourself.

The Way Forward

Acquiring self-knowledge is a lifetime's task, and there will be good days and bad days. Embrace the journey.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a passion for conversation and adventure, and this can create a stimulating and intense relationship.

Luck Maker: Follow Your Own Star

Believe in your own uniqueness and you will attract all you need to fulfill your life's purpose. You are the one and only you.

At Your Best

Discerning, popular, sociable.

On the Dark Side

Scattered, impatient, vain.

Love: Fickle

June 1 natives will often have many admirers but few who really know them. They will only open up in a secure relationship with an accepting partner. Love comes easily to them, but they can get bored and there is a tendency toward fickleness. They are attracted to complicated people but would thrive best with those who are more self-assured.

Health: Preventative Medicine

Those born on this day simply don't have time to get ill because they are always on the go. They often distrust doctors, but if they push too hard they could suffer from circulation problems, nervous exhaustion, and lowered immunity. The best advice is to practice preventative medicine with a healthy, varied diet and plenty of fresh-air exercise. Inhaling a drop of lemon on a tissue can help clear their active minds. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will help them focus on higher things.

Career: Born Detectives

These people have the potential to achieve success in marketing, advertising, media, politics, and perhaps psychology or detective work. Their natural communication skills also make them great salespeople, and they may be drawn to writing, music, or theater. Whatever they choose, there must always be an emphasis on change and variety.

Destiny: To Bring a Touch of Glamour, Magick, or Flair

The life path of those born on this day is to find out what they truly want. Once they learn to look within, their destiny is to bring an inspiring touch of glamour, magick, or flair to every enterprise they are involved in.

Power Thought

I relax and recognize my potential for greatness.
