Born on February 10th

The birthday of achievement

Profile

People born on February 10 have a clear picture of what they want to achieve and exactly how they intend to get there. The pursuit of their goals tends to take precedence over everything else. Achievement matters greatly to them, as do the approval and recognition of others. Their clear-sighted understanding of their aspirations, combined with an ability to concentrate, means they are more than likely to surpass their professional ambitions.

Once they settle on a chosen course, the explosive drive these people possess can carry them straight to the top. They do need to be careful, however, that they do not become obsessive in the process. It is important for them to look deep within and identify the real motivation behind their determination. They may well discover that it is not material success they crave but rather making their mark on the world and earning the respect of others.

Although their ambition and drive are formidable, it never leads them to betray the trust of others. Intuitive, honest, and decent, their ideal is to achieve success the right way, without causing harm. Unfortunately, there are people they are capable of hurting without realizing it: those closest to them, who get neglected when goals eclipse everything and everyone else. If they are not careful, this can leave them emotionally isolated and, in extreme cases, alone. Fortunately, between the ages of twenty and thirty-nine there are many opportunities to open their hearts and shift the spotlight from themselves onto others. At forty a turning point arrives when they feel the need to be more assertive yet also more passionate in their personal relationships.

As long as February 10 natives remember that admiration is no substitute for affection, and can learn from any setbacks, their considerable achievements will earn them both personal fulfillment and a large following of admirers.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to give others a chance.

The Way Forward

Understand that giving others the opportunity to prove themselves is an important part of their psychological development.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. If you can learn to fulfill your mutual need for admiration and closeness, this can be an extraordinarily intense union.

Luck Maker: Quiet Down and Slow Down

Just ten or fifteen minutes of quiet time a day can make quite a difference in connecting with your intuitive, luck-making power.

At Your Best

Positive, creative, bold.

On the Dark Side

Aloof, self-obsessed, anxious.

Love: Heartbreakers

February 10 natives have a charismatic charm that draws others to them. When it comes to affairs of the heart, they know how to win someone over but can have trouble keeping them. It is important for them not to neglect those closest to them, as the loving support of a partner gives them the security and perspective they need to be truly successful.

Health: Honor Life's Reflective Moments

Because people born on this day tend to be so driven, they are often prone to stress- and anxiety-related disorders, especially if there are problems at work or their career path has been thwarted. Insomnia may be a particular concern, and it is important for them to maintain a clear boundary between home and work. Their home should be a calm, restful space. Plenty of exercise in the fresh air, hobbies, and a wide circle of friendships outside the workplace are also beneficial. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will help them calm down and honor the simple pleasures and reflective moments in life.

Career: Born Workers

These people have the drive, ambition, and determination to succeed in almost any career. They could be a lawyer, actor, agent, politician, civil servant, charity worker, entrepreneur, sportsperson, artist, researcher, photographer, pilot, or director. In fact, given their tendency toward tunnel vision, it might be better for them to pursue not one but several careers in a lifetime.

Destiny: To Be Recognized for Dedication and Integrity

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to look outside themselves to reach out to others, as well as inside to understand their true motivation. Once they unite both the external and the internal, their destiny is to attract recognition for their hard work, dedication, and integrity.

Power Thought

Today I will notice what is going on around me as well as within me.
