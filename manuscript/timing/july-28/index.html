Born on July 28th

The birthday of the independent spirit

Profile

Those born on July 28 are fiercely independent and competitive. They love to work independently and although they have superb communication skills they prefer to lead by example rather than by hollow words. They relish testing themselves against challenges and their urge to win is so powerful that they find it almost impossible to admit defeat.

The dominating characteristic of these individuals at first appears to be an urge to win at all costs, but this competitive streak is fueled by their intense desire to appear self-reliant. From an early age this independent spirit will probably have manifested itself in a refusal to conform or acknowledge authority they do not respect.

Other people tend to be awed by this self-assurance and although it can lead to considerable achievements it can also lead to loneliness. The admiration that they earn for their courage and willingness to break new ground is merely admiration; what they crave is the affection of others. Fortunately, from the age of twenty-five there are opportunities for them to become more discriminating, practical and thoughtful with their energy and the image they present to others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Asking others for help.

The Way Forward

Understand that working cooperatively toward a goal with people you trust does not weaken your position, it strengthens it.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous and determined, and this can be an exciting and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Seek the Right Advice

Lucky people are good at pinpointing smart, hardworking people who can improve their luck. Not only do they seek their advice, they give everyone a stake in their achievement.

At Your Best

Resolute, independent, dynamic.

On the Dark Side

Insensitive, detached, selfish.

Love: Keeping the Romance Alive

People born on this day love the thrill of the chase and need a partner who can keep the excitement alive. They can be demanding in relationships but when they find someone who can match their energy and independence, they are incredibly loyal and passionate lovers.

Health: Channeling Competitive Energy

Those born on this day have incredible stamina and drive but they need to make sure they channel their competitive energy wisely. They can be accident-prone because of their reckless approach, so they need to avoid taking unnecessary physical risks. Competitive sports will be a good outlet for their energy. As far as diet is concerned, they should make sure they eat regular meals with plenty of fresh fruits and vegetables. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be warmer toward others.

Career: Born Trailblazers

These individuals thrive in careers where they can work independently and be their own boss. They may excel as entrepreneurs, athletes, explorers, pilots, journalists or in the military. Their love of challenge may also draw them to the law, medicine, scientific research or the world of entertainment.

Destiny: To Blaze an Inspirational Trail for Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their need for independence with the need for cooperation and intimacy. Once they are able to do that, their destiny is to blaze an inspirational trail for others to follow.

Power Thought

Love is finding strength in togetherness, not just in standing alone.
