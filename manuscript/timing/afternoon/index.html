Magickal Properties

During the afternoon, the sun's energy begins to wane (ebb) as it moves toward sunset. During the summer months, the direct, intense sunlight warms the earth and creates hot afternoons and warm evenings; during winter, with a lower and less intense sun, the earth begins to cool almost immediately as the sun drops to the horizon.

Best Used For

  • Evaluation and introspection
  • Journaling or planning
  • Taking walks (especially winter afternoons)
  • Natural augury — identifying and interpreting naturally occurring signs and omens
  • Reflective work
