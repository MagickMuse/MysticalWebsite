Skirophorion

◇Notes

Demeter

Skirophorion is the twelfth and final month of the Athenian year, named for the Skiraphoria festival of Demeter which falls within it. The year closes with a sacrifice to Zeus and Athena as Saviours on the last day, and the new year opens with the Noumenia of Hekatombaion on the new moon following the summer solstice.

Major Festivals

  • Beginning, likely 3: Arrhephoria, the hidden rite of the Arrhephoroi
  • 12: Skiraphoria, the festival of the cutting and threshing of the grain
  • 14: Dipolieia, the festival of Zeus as God of the city
  • Last day: Sacrifice to Zeus the Saviour and Athena the Saviour

On a normal calendar. Skirophorion is the twelfth lunar month of the Athenian year, counted from the Noumenia following the summer solstice. It generally corresponds to June of the Gregorian calendar and shifts each cycle.
