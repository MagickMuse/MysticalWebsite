Best Day for Magick

Wednesday is the best day of the week to perform magick for knowledge, change, charm, and communication.

Lunar Magick

Wednesday is Mercury's day, making it ideal for magick around communication, information, research, technology, and all forms of speaking and writing. Layer Wednesday with a waxing moon to draw in clarity or new connections, or with a full moon to illuminate truth and amplify your words.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.
