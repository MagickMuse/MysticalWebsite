Born on June 20th

The birthday of excitement

Profile

People born on June 20 are affectionate and spontaneous with everyone they meet, because expressing their emotions comes naturally to them. Indifference is something they simply don't understand since, above all else, these people love and thrive on excitement.

Life is never dull when these popular people are around since everything elicits a passionate reaction from them. They are dramatic, charismatic, outgoing charmers who love to talk and adore being the center of attention. Excellent communication skills and an innovative mind crammed with insights make them vibrant, witty conversationalists. However, because they love excitement they can sometimes provoke an argument for the sake of it. They can also be a little too needy of praise, and if they don't get the reinforcement they crave they may respond with attention-seeking or irrational behavior.

Until age thirty there is an emphasis on home, family, and emotional security, and they should take advantage of opportunities to find inner balance. After thirty-one they become more creative and confident, developing the assertiveness to be more adventurous. If they can get a grip on their passionate responses and their ability to churn up strong emotions in others, these are the years when they can really come into their own.

Although they can sometimes create fireworks unnecessarily, more often than not they are a positive force. The atmosphere seems lighter when they are around because they help bring out the repressed emotions of others. If they can find a worthy cause and ensure they occasionally check in with reason as well as emotions, they can transform their creative and exciting dreams into reality.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding extremes of emotion.

The Way Forward

The only way to find real fulfillment is to temper your reactions with a heavy dose of self-discipline.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. Opposites attract here, as you can learn much from each other, creating a fulfilling and happy union.

Luck Maker: Stop Exaggerating!

Exaggerating only makes you feel more out of your depth and sends others the wrong signals. Describe situations as they are instead of dramatizing them.

At Your Best

Dynamic, exciting, rousing.

On the Dark Side

Insecure, irrational, hypersensitive.

Love: Make Time for Love

June 20 natives are often so popular they haven't got time for that special someone. However, once they fall in love the experience is incredibly fulfilling so they should always remain open to it. They are drawn toward intelligent, level-headed individuals who can help calm and balance them.

Health: Cool, Calm, and Collected

Those born on this day tend to attract stressful people and situations into their lives and need to find out why. Getting in touch with their own feelings and increasing their self-understanding will help them attract more positive energy. They would benefit enormously from meditation, yoga, and counseling, as well as more quiet time alone. They need to structure fixed meal and snack times into their day, and the same applies for exercise. Sticking to regular sleep times is also recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to be more cool, calm, and collected.

Career: Born Investigative Journalists

Those born on this day have the ability to think on their feet and sense the potential drama or opportunity in a situation, making them excellent problem solvers, investigative journalists, politicians, teachers, and researchers. Their easy charm and organizational skills will also help them succeed in people-oriented careers, whether in business or the public sector. Creative outlets are likely through media, theater, or music.

Destiny: To Encourage Others to Be More Open and Receptive

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to temper their restlessness and thirst for stimulation with a streak of realism and self-discipline. Once this is achieved, their destiny is to draw people to them with their sympathetic nature, encouraging others to be more open and receptive.

Power Thought

I don't need a crisis to feel alive.
