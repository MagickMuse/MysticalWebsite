Born on August 14th

The birthday of reflection

Profile

Those born on August 14 tend to be perceptive, thoughtful individuals with a talent for assessing the situations and people they encounter. They are keen observers of the human condition and their insight and sharp intellect give them the ability to shine a revealing light on people and situations, often reflecting back truths that others may not want to see.

Although their penetrating insight and frank opinions are valuable, they may find that others do not always appreciate their honesty. They are also deeply intuitive and sensitive, and when they learn to balance their analytical gifts with their emotional intelligence, they become a powerful force for positive change. The danger is that they can become too caught up in analyzing and observing life rather than participating in it, or that they use their insight to criticize rather than to help.

Until age thirty-eight there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to become very skillful at whatever career they choose, and their ability to see through pretense and get to the heart of things gives them a real edge. After age thirty-nine there is a turning point that places an increased emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony.

After age sixty-eight there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to temper their insight with compassion and use their powers of reflection to enlighten rather than criticize, they have the potential to become remarkably influential and inspiring individuals.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being less critical of others.

The Way Forward

Understand that criticism without compassion is destructive; when you temper your insight with kindness, your words become a force for healing.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both thoughtful and perceptive, and this can create a balanced and intellectually stimulating partnership.

Luck Maker: Mirror Kindness

Lucky people understand that what you reflect back to the world comes back to you. Mirror kindness and compassion, and that is what you will attract.

At Your Best

Perceptive, insightful, honest.

On the Dark Side

Critical, detached, judgmental.

Love: Looking for Depth

Those born on this day are attracted to intelligent, witty people who can match their mental agility. They tend to analyze their relationships rather than simply enjoy them, and they need to learn to let go and be more spontaneous in love. Once they find a partner who appreciates their depth and honesty, they make thoughtful, loyal and devoted lovers.

Health: Think Less, Feel More

These individuals tend to overthink things, and this can lead to anxiety, stress and insomnia. They would benefit enormously from relaxation techniques such as meditation, yoga or deep breathing. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a varied, balanced diet and enjoy the sensual pleasures of cooking and sharing meals with friends. Moderate exercise is recommended, and they should try to spend more time outdoors in nature. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be warmer, more spontaneous and less analytical.

Career: Born Commentators

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in journalism, writing, criticism, psychology, counseling, teaching, research or any field that allows them to use their powers of observation and analysis. They may also excel in law, medicine, science, social work, management consulting, editing or the media.

Destiny: To Enlighten Others with Their Perceptive Insights

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their analytical gifts with warmth and compassion. Once they have tempered their insight with kindness, their destiny is to enlighten and inspire others with their perceptive and thought-provoking reflections.

Power Thought

I reflect the beauty and potential I see in others.
