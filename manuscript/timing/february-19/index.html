Born on February 19th

The birthday of wonderlust

Profile

There is only one way for people born on February 19 to do things, and that is their way. They value their independence above all else and do not like being told what to do, particularly by their parents. They want to find their own path in life, even if that means making mistakes along the way. As a result, they often have a deep lust for travel, thriving on new situations and new people.

With their independent spirit and need to stamp their individuality on everything they undertake, February 19 natives can often be found at the cutting edge of their chosen field. Although they perform best alone, they can also make inspirational leaders or enthusiastic team players; those who work alongside them will find themselves surprised by the strength of their wholehearted commitment to success.

People born on this day quickly make a name for themselves wherever they go — and they tend to go to a lot of places. Their appetite for life and new situations is enormous, and even when they appear to be settling down in a career or a relationship, their eye is always on the horizon, wondering what else might be out there. The danger is that this inquisitive approach can make them seem reckless or, at worst, selfish. They should learn that their go-it-alone attitude can prevent them from connecting with the needs of others. Around the age of thirty-one their ambition becomes more apparent and they may start new projects or pioneer new ideas. It is especially important at this stage not to scatter their energies selfishly.

The life path of February 19 people will always be rich with opportunities, because they have a knack for knowing where to find them. Along the way their fiercely individual approach may encounter setbacks and rejections, but they never let these discourage them. In their mind, the only way is their way — and that way is always up.

Your Greatest Challenge

Finishing what you started.

The Way Forward

Understand that your ability to share the load and complete what you started determines whether you will be seen as a leader or left behind.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a passion for adventure and experimentation, and this can create a bond with explosive potential.

Luck Maker: Be a Role Model

Be a role model for your loved ones, children, and friends. Ask yourself: do you want them to learn selfishness or compassion from you?

At Your Best

Inspiring, wholehearted, independent.

On the Dark Side

Indecisive, rash, selfish.

Love: Born to Be Worshiped

Once February 19 natives are able to balance their desire for independence and adventure with empathy for others, they have the potential to mesmerize everyone they meet with a wonderfully alluring mixture of vulnerability and excitement. Although it may take a while for them to find that special someone, they do not find it difficult to fall in love, and people do not find it hard to fall for them. Everyone adores them — children in particular — and they make wonderful parents.

Health: Not All Rules Are Made to Be Broken

People born on this day will generally resist any attempt to have a diet or exercise regime imposed on them, preferring to go their own way. But they need to understand that for their body to keep up with their free-spirited lifestyle, it must be properly cared for. A healthy diet rich in energy-boosting nutrients is particularly important, along with staying hydrated with six to eight glasses of water a day and avoiding too much coffee to stay alert. Regular exercise to maintain fitness for their high-octane lifestyle is essential, though they should watch out for injuries to their legs, feet, and ankles. Unwinding with a glass of chamomile herbal tea before bedtime can help if they have trouble sleeping.

Career: Born Explorers

These people may be drawn to social work or the caring professions, but they also make outstanding environmental campaigners and gifted artists and performers. Other careers that might appeal include sales, promotion, design, acting, dancing, singing, and comedy, as well as science, astronomy, philosophy, and exploration.

Destiny: To Move Themselves and Others Forward

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to take into account the needs and feelings of others in their quest for fulfillment. Once they develop greater empathy, their destiny is to test the limits with their original thoughts and boundless energy and, in doing so, move themselves and everyone else one step forward.

Power Thought

Today I will let my happiness inspire me to listen and to give.
