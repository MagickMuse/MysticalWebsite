Born on December 2nd

The birthday of the kaleidoscope

Profile

Those born on December 2 are dynamic and colorful characters who tend to command attention wherever they go. They wear their heart on their sleeve and are so spontaneous that the whole kaleidoscope of their constantly changing emotions will be on display for all to see.

Their emotional honesty and energy can be refreshing, and their drive and determination make them excellent leaders and an inspiration to friends and colleagues. The effect they can have on others is dramatic, but it could be life-changing for both themselves and others if they learned to balance their directness with a little tact, occasionally stepping back to examine what their emotions are telling them. With a little more self-understanding they might see that some kind of pattern is emerging.

Until age nineteen, they may be something of a wild child, exploring and expanding horizons in as many different directions as possible. After twenty, a turning point brings opportunities to become more practical, goal-oriented and realistic. They should take advantage of these to establish order and structure, because too many changes of direction can lead to confusion and uncertainty. After fifty, another turning point highlights a growing need for independence and a need to become more humanitarian in their approach.

Above all, these individuals are charismatic, but they are also problem solvers looking for a deeper meaning or pattern to their life. Their inner sensitivity may not always be visible from their colorful, confident front. Once they find a way to connect with it and work with it, they will not only intuitively know the answer to other people's dilemmas; they will be able to take charge of their constantly changing emotions, find a cause worthy of their creative talents, and create the magickal pattern of peace and happiness they so richly deserve.

Your Greatest Challenge

Outgrowing your constant need for recognition.

The Way Forward

Realize that no matter how much recognition and applause others give you, it will never be enough until you can grow in self-esteem.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You have so much to love and learn from each other; anything could happen in this exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: Sometimes Put Yourself Second

There is nothing more powerful than asking people what they think and quietly listening to their responses. This makes them feel important, special and far more likely to want to help you.

At Your Best

Inspirational, creative, demonstrative.

On the Dark Side

Unaware, volatile, intimidating.

Love: Incorrigible Flirts

Those born on this day do not mean to but simply cannot help being terrible flirts. They will often be surrounded by admirers even when in a committed relationship. Potential partners will need to adjust to their shifting moods, but once they find someone they connect with on a deep level, they make loyal and loving partners.

Health: Let the Music Play

Music is a great therapy for these individuals. It can help clear the mind and encourage them to see patterns and connections instead of confusion or fantasy. They should listen to music as much as possible to enhance creativity and language skills, as well as the logical, analytical side of the brain. Their diet would benefit from more careful planning of menus and meal plans; a cookery course might also be worthwhile. Regular moderate exercise is absolutely essential, preferably daily, to keep weight under control and boost circulation, as they are prone to circulatory problems and varicose veins. Time spent reading and studying spiritual disciplines or mastering meditation will not be wasted. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue helps bring order and clarity when there is confusion.

Career: Born Opera Singers

These individuals have the potential not only to achieve personal happiness but to make significant contributions to society, perhaps as pioneering scientists or as inspirational artists and performers. There may be a number of career changes, but work opportunities that might also appeal include education, sales, publishing, media, writing, charity work and the tourist and leisure industries.

Destiny: To Make an Inspirational Mark on the World

The life path of those born on this day is to take charge of their emotions so they feel more in control of their lives. Once they are more self-aware, their destiny is to make an inspirational or pioneering mark on the world.

Power Thought

Peace and understanding begin within me.
