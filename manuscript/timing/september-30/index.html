Born on September 30th

The birthday of revelation

Profile

Those born on September 30 are focused, knowledgeable individuals driven by a powerful desire to champion and reveal the truth. They possess an uncanny ability for spotting intellectual or social injustices and proposing progressive alternatives to bring about meaningful change.

They are motivated by a deep urge to expose unfairness in any form. Because they understand that standing up for the truth can put them in the line of fire, they tend to develop a tough and courageous exterior that inspires both respect and apprehension. Respect, because others recognize that these highly attractive and persuasive people have the insight and star quality to draw support and success. Apprehension, because their uncompromising sense of fairness and strong need to hold others to high moral standards can easily shift into judgmental or aggressive behavior.

It is important for them to understand that while pointing out the failings of those around them can clear the air, it can also create discomfort for everyone involved. After age twenty-three there is a turning point that highlights issues of emotional intensity, change and transformation. Whatever age they are, their challenge is not only to be more open and accepting in their beliefs, but to show as much interest in discovering the truth within their own lives as they do in uncovering it in the world around them.

Once they are able to acknowledge their own vulnerabilities, they can move beyond self-righteousness toward greater tolerance of human weaknesses. When that tolerance is combined with their remarkable courage and impressive resourcefulness, they can ensure that justice is served and falsehood is exposed, while also discovering within themselves the ability to motivate and inspire others to work alongside them in creating a fairer and better world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting that you might be wrong.

The Way Forward

Understand that without awareness of your own fallibility, you will never be able to uncover the truth in yourself or in any situation.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both charming and insightful, with enough differences and similarities to endlessly fascinate each other.

Luck Maker: Believe the Impossible

When you open your mind to believe that what appears impossible is truly possible, you open the door to luck.

At Your Best

Knowledgeable, fair, influential.

On the Dark Side

Self-righteous, critical, smug.

Love: Fairness and Openness

Friends and loved ones of those born on this day will often find their words and actions placed under the spotlight. Although September 30 people have the ability to make others laugh at their weaknesses, they must be careful not to become overly critical. They demand absolute fairness and openness from their partners and need to ensure they offer the same in return.

Health: Love of Food and Drink

These individuals often excel in sporting activities when young, but after leaving school or college they may ease up on physical activity. Their love of food and drink can lead to a sedentary lifestyle and weight gain, especially around the middle. It is vital for them to increase their activity levels and eat plenty of fresh, nutritious food to boost their metabolism. Physical appearance matters greatly to them and the mirror is usually all the incentive they need to take control of their diet and exercise routine. Rose or jasmine essential oils are excellent if they feel lethargic and need a boost. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more open, warm and accepting in their relationships.

Career: Born Judges

These individuals are clearly suited to careers in law, law enforcement, politics, social campaigning and medicine. They may also have a natural affinity for the arts, seeking to help others by providing inspiration through writing, music, art or song. Other careers that might appeal include publishing, journalism, education and the catering industry.

Destiny: To Be a Dynamic Force for Progress, Justice and Reform

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more tolerant of their own weaknesses and those of others. Once they understand that everyone has their own interpretation of the truth, their destiny is to be a dynamic force for progress, justice and reform.

Power Thought

I feel tolerance and consideration for everyone, myself included.
