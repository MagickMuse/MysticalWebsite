Born on July 16th

The birthday of passionate logic

Profile

Those born on July 16 are a fascinating blend of passion and logic. They have the ability to combine emotional depth with rational thinking, and this unique combination makes them highly effective problem-solvers and communicators.

Although they are deeply passionate about their beliefs and their work, they also have a practical, analytical side that prevents them from being swept away by their emotions. Between the ages of thirty-six and sixty-six there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to make sure they do not suppress their emotional side in favor of pure logic. After the age of sixty-six there is a turning point highlighting creativity and relationships.

Your Greatest Challenge

Balancing head and heart.

The Way Forward

Understand that logic without passion is cold, and passion without logic is chaotic. The magick happens when you bring both together.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both passionate and energetic, and this can create an exciting and dynamic relationship.

Luck Maker: Trust Your Instincts

Lucky people understand that their instincts are a powerful source of guidance. When logic and instinct agree, you can be confident you are on the right path.

At Your Best

Passionate, logical, effective.

On the Dark Side

Overthinking, detached, stubborn.

Love: Head and Heart

Those born on this day are complex and fascinating partners who bring both passion and thoughtfulness to their relationships. They need a partner who can appreciate both their emotional depth and their analytical mind. They should be careful not to overthink their relationships and should allow themselves to simply feel.

Health: Trust Your Feelings

Those born on this day may suppress their emotions in favor of logic, which can lead to stress and emotional problems. It is important for them to find healthy outlets for their feelings, such as creative expression, journaling or counseling. Regular exercise is essential, especially activities that engage both body and mind such as yoga, martial arts or dance. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to trust their passionate side.

Career: Born Strategists

These individuals are natural strategists and may excel in business, law, politics, science or any field that requires both analytical thinking and emotional intelligence. Their creative side may also draw them to writing, journalism, teaching or the arts.

Destiny: To Blend Passion and Logic for the Greater Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to honor both their emotional and rational sides. Once they have found that balance, their destiny is to use their unique blend of passion and logic to solve problems and inspire others.

Power Thought

I honor my feelings and trust the wisdom of my heart.
