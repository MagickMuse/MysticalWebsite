Born on September 2nd

The birthday of the egalitarian

Profile

Those born on September 2 have a strong sense of justice and a deep commitment to fairness. They believe passionately that all people are created equal and will work tirelessly to defend the rights of the underprivileged and the underrepresented. Perceptive and intuitive, they have an uncanny ability to sense what others are feeling and thinking, and this makes them extremely effective communicators and negotiators.

Although their idealism is admirable, it can sometimes lead them to be overly critical of those who do not share their values or who fail to live up to their high standards. They need to learn that not everyone sees the world as they do, and that tolerance and patience are essential if they want to bring about the changes they envision. They also need to be careful that their desire to help others does not lead them to neglect their own needs.

Until age fifty there is an emphasis on relationships, partnerships and the desire for harmony. These are excellent years for them to develop their diplomatic skills and to learn the art of compromise without compromising their principles. After age fifty-one there is a turning point that highlights emotional depth and the need for personal transformation.

Throughout their lives, if they can learn to balance their idealism with pragmatism and their desire to serve others with self-care, they have the potential to be powerful agents of positive change. Their combination of sensitivity, determination and a deep commitment to fairness can make them truly inspiring individuals.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding burnout.

The Way Forward

Understand that you cannot help others effectively if you are running on empty. Taking care of yourself is not selfish; it is essential.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 21 and July 22

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a deep sensitivity and compassion, and this can be a nurturing and deeply supportive union.

Luck Maker: Set Healthy Boundaries

Lucky people know that saying no to others sometimes means saying yes to themselves. Setting healthy boundaries protects your energy and attracts respect.

At Your Best

Fair, perceptive, compassionate.

On the Dark Side

Critical, self-neglecting, rigid.

Love: Deeply Caring

Those born on this day are deeply caring and attentive partners who will go to extraordinary lengths for those they love. They need a partner who appreciates their sensitivity and who can encourage them to express their own needs. They are attracted to individuals who share their commitment to making the world a better place.

Health: Listen to Your Body

These individuals are so focused on the needs of others that they often ignore the warning signs their own body sends them. They need to pay more attention to their physical health, particularly their digestive system and their immune system, both of which can suffer when they are under stress. A diet rich in whole grains, fresh fruits, vegetables and probiotic foods will support their digestion. Regular exercise, particularly activities with a social element such as group fitness classes, walking groups or team sports, will boost both their physical and emotional health. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their confidence and sense of personal power.

Career: Born Advocates

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers where they can fight for justice and equality, such as law, politics, social work, human rights and charity work. They may also excel in counseling, psychology, education and the healing professions. Their communication skills make them effective writers, journalists and public speakers.

Destiny: To Champion Equality and Fairness

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance caring for others with caring for themselves. Once they have learned to set healthy boundaries, their destiny is to champion equality and fairness and to inspire others with their deep commitment to a better world.

Power Thought

I honor my own needs as readily as I honor the needs of others.
