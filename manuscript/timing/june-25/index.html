Born on June 25th

The birthday of original sensitivity

Profile

People born on June 25 tend to be highly creative and sensitive. They react strongly to external influences, using their powerful intuition to draw clear-sighted conclusions or solutions. Others, however, don't just value them for their problem-solving abilities, but also for their fine minds and stunning originality of thought.

The sensitivity of these people makes them valuable team players because they sense what others need and enjoy helping them. Their heart definitely rules their head, and most of their actions are closely allied to their feelings and emotions, which can make them appear vacillating or contradictory. It is important for them to reconcile their emotional and mental responses because, if they do, they have the originality and ability to be effective instruments of progress. When their minds and hearts are unbalanced, it can result in insecurity, confusion, hypersensitivity, or inconsistent motivations.

This sensitivity becomes less of a problem after age twenty-seven, when they become bolder and more self-assured. During this period, opportunities to develop greater self-awareness arise and, if they can build their self-esteem and rely less on the approval of others, these are the years when they are capable of making outstanding contributions. After fifty-seven they are likely to apply greater patience and precision to their skills, taking a more practical approach.

To achieve success, it is crucial for them to find a subject or cause that really captures their imagination, one that will keep them focused. If they fail to keep themselves mentally challenged, they run the risk of scattering their energies. However, if they learn to develop patience and discipline, they will discover they also possess depth of thought and a mind capable of producing truly inspirational work.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not feeling overwhelmed by confusion.

The Way Forward

You have absorbed and internalized a variety of contradictory messages. You need to be more logical and objective in your approach.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 20 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. You share an almost telepathic bond as well as much else, so this can be a close and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Set Clear Boundaries

If you are exhausted because you are burdened with the problems of others, you block luck from your life. Set clear boundaries, letting others know there is a line they cannot cross.

At Your Best

Creative, empathetic, sensitive.

On the Dark Side

Over-sensitive, insecure, inconsistent.

Love: Aim for Equality

June 25 natives usually have many admirers and friends, but there is a tendency to please others before themselves. This is a disastrous strategy because it leads to unhappiness and frustration. It is important to aim for greater equality in all relationships.

Health: Protective Bubble

It is vital for the physical and emotional well-being of those born on this day that they pour less of themselves into the lives of others. Highly sensitive to the moods of others, they need to protect themselves by imagining a protective bubble that screens them from negative emotions. They also need to cultivate greater objectivity. If they are unable to do this, they may suffer from mood swings, stress, anxiety, and in extreme cases depression. Regular periods of quiet and solitude will help them regain a sense of self. They should eat a nutritious diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, oily fish, nuts, and seeds. Moderate exercise such as walking or swimming is recommended daily. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to be cooler and more objective.

Career: Born Commercial Artists

The sensitivity these people possess, as well as their natural affinity for art, suggests great success as designers and commercial artists. A need to express their individuality may also draw them to journalism, architecture, art, and music. In their choice of career they need to guard against boredom, as it is important to have plenty of variety and opportunities to learn.

Destiny: To Act as a Sensitive and Compassionate Force for Good

The life path of those born on this day is to develop their capacity for objectivity and setting priorities. Once they learn to be realistic about what really matters, their destiny is to act as a sensitive and compassionate force for good.

Power Thought

Today I will look after body, mind, heart, and soul. I feel great!
