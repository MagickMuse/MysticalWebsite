Born on October 7th

The birthday of acquired taste

Profile

Those born on October 7 are complex, enigmatic individuals who may not make a strong first impression but who grow on people over time. Like an acquired taste, the more time others spend with them, the more they appreciate their unique qualities and the depth of their character. They are original, thoughtful and quietly compelling.

They have a rich inner world and a deep need for privacy and solitude. They are not antisocial, but they need regular time alone to recharge and reflect. They are deep thinkers who enjoy exploring ideas, theories and philosophies, and they have a natural affinity for the arts, music and literature. Their creativity is one of their greatest strengths, and when they channel it effectively they can produce work that is truly original and thought-provoking.

Despite their quiet exterior, they have strong opinions and are not afraid to express them, often with a dry wit and a sharp intelligence that can catch others off guard. They can, however, be overly critical and judgmental, both of themselves and of others, and this tendency can make them seem aloof or unapproachable. Learning to be more accepting and less exacting in their standards is important for their personal happiness.

Before age forty-five they are likely to be most focused on issues of emotional depth, personal power and transformation. After forty-six, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for freedom, optimism and exploration. Whatever age they are, they need to resist the temptation to retreat too far into their own world and make an effort to connect with others. When they open up and share their unique perspective and creative gifts with the world, they discover that they are not just an acquired taste but an essential one.

Your Greatest Challenge

Opening up to others.

The Way Forward

Understand that sharing your thoughts, feelings and creative gifts with others does not diminish them. It amplifies them and creates connections that enrich your life immeasurably.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You both value harmony and beauty, and this can be an aesthetically pleasing and intellectually stimulating partnership.

Luck Maker: Let People In

Lucky people understand that good fortune flows through relationships. The more genuine connections you make, the more opportunities come your way.

At Your Best

Original, thoughtful, compelling.

On the Dark Side

Aloof, critical, reclusive.

Love: Slow Burn

Those born on this day are not the type to fall head over heels at first sight. Their relationships tend to develop slowly, building in depth and intensity over time. They need a partner who is patient enough to wait for them to open up and who appreciates their subtle, understated way of expressing love. Once they commit, they are deeply loyal and devoted.

Health: Come Out of Your Shell

These individuals can be prone to isolation and the health problems that come with it, including depression, low energy and a weakened immune system. It is important for them to make a conscious effort to socialize and stay connected with friends, family and colleagues. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein is essential, and they should make sure they eat regular meals rather than skipping them because they are absorbed in their thoughts or creative projects. Regular exercise, particularly activities that get them out of the house and into nature, such as walking, hiking or gardening, will boost their mood and energy. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage warmth, sociability and openness.

Career: Born Artists

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in the arts, music, writing, design, photography, filmmaking and craftsmanship. Their analytical minds also suit them to careers in science, research, technology, philosophy and academia. Their need for autonomy may lead them toward freelance work, consulting or entrepreneurship.

Destiny: To Share Their Unique Creative Vision with the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their need for solitude with the need for meaningful connection. Once they can open up and share themselves with the world, their destiny is to enrich the lives of others with their unique creative vision and original perspective.

Power Thought

I share my gifts freely and the world is richer for it.
