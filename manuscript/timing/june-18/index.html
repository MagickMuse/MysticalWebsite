Born on June 18th

The birthday of perceptive congeniality

Profile

People born on June 18 often have a charming manner and a likable vitality that uplifts everyone they meet. Their instant likability, however, conceals an insightful mind and a serious head for responsibility and business. They place as high a priority on meeting their personal, financial, and professional goals as they do on being popular.

Whether they realize it or not, their personality has a lasting effect on others, and even if not physically present, their influence will be felt. They possess a perceptive and highly intuitive mentality, and when combined with their sharp wit and quirky sense of fun they manage to get their point across without causing offense. Their ability to leave a lasting impact gives them potential for leadership, although they need to be careful not to abuse this power and become manipulative.

Although they enjoy attention and popularity comes naturally, they are also good at reciprocating it, especially when it comes to helping others. They will often be indefatigable fighters for the rights of others and make witty and dramatic speakers who refuse to tolerate injustice. Despite their sincerity, they need to be entertained and can get easily bored. Their need for constant change and excitement can make them behave erratically and occasionally selfishly.

Until age thirty-two they focus on emotional security, but after thirty-three they become more self-expressive and assertive. They should ensure they don't fritter away their talents with a "grass is always greener" mentality. Once they learn to be grateful for what they already have, they will discover a capacity for spiritual achievement.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with boredom.

The Way Forward

The answer to boredom is not in external stimulation but in the sense of excitement and change generated within.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both passionate and adventurous, and this can be a powerful and intense union.

Luck Maker: Focus on What Is Good

Gratitude is the foundation of a lucky life, so instead of looking over the horizon for excitement, try thinking about all that is good in your life right now.

At Your Best

Charming, powerful, intelligent.

On the Dark Side

Egotistical, easily bored, erratic.

Love: Confusing

June 18 natives tend to be slightly erratic in affairs of the heart; sometimes very devoted and passionate, other times detached. They also tend to drop partners at a moment's notice, thinking they will easily find someone better. It would be good for everyone if they learned to appreciate what they already have.

Health: Spiritual Orientation

Those born on this day usually understand the importance of investing in their health, and because they are spiritually oriented they may be attracted to mind-body therapies such as meditation, hypnotherapy, yoga, and tai chi. They should avoid saturated fat and refined foods, which could lead to mood swings and weight problems. Regular exercise, particularly walking, swimming, cycling, and body toning, is recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to be more consistent and balanced.

Career: Born Lyricists

These people have the potential to make their mark in the performing arts, in particular music, poetry, and writing, but they may also blaze a trail as scientific researchers or entrepreneurs. They may be attracted to law, education, media, politics, labor union leadership, counseling, charity work, and social and humanitarian reform.

Destiny: To Influence, Inspire, and Encourage Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that much of what they seek already lies within them. Once they become more self-contained, their destiny is to influence, inspire, and encourage progress with their ideas, visions, and humanitarian concerns.

Power Thought

Today I will reflect on all that I have to feel grateful for.
