Born on February 7th

The birthday of the prophet

Profile

People born on February 7 are progressive individuals with a keen intellect and an inherent sense of justice. It is impossible for them to witness injustice or cruelty without speaking out. Above all, they are prophets with a vision and a burning desire to change social attitudes and right wrongs.

They have a wonderful imagination and a youthful approach to life, but also a tendency to exaggerate or embellish things. This openness and spontaneity can sometimes make them appear naive or childlike. Others may dismiss them or take them for granted, but that is a mistake. Although they love to share their dreams, the difference between them and other dreamers lies in their ability to turn those dreams into reality.

Quick to identify solutions, these people often pursue their goals with zest and enthusiasm, sometimes with fanatical zeal. This makes others sit up and take notice, but occasionally they have not thought their plans all the way through. Their excellent communication skills easily win them supporters, yet when things do not run smoothly and criticism arrives, they can hide disillusionment behind a wall of cynicism. They must understand that life is too complex for simple right-or-wrong thinking or quick-fix solutions. Around the age of forty-three a significant turning point helps them fine-tune their relationship skills and become more open-minded.

February 7 people love nothing more than helping others, but there is one thing they will not tolerate: authority imposed upon them. They have little patience for enforced conformity, and if forced into a corner they may resort to subversive or disruptive behavior. Along with their rebellious streak they also possess great determination; once they find a true outlet for their self-expression and commit to it fully, these modern-day prophets really can make the world a better and fairer place.

Your Greatest Challenge

Getting others to take you seriously.

The Way Forward

Prove you mean business by acting out your ideals rather than merely talking about them.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 20 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. Your hearts are free of greed and full of idealism, and this can create a loving, tender relationship.

Luck Maker: Do Not Take It All Personally

Just because people reject your ideas does not mean they are rejecting you. Lucky people have the resilience to absorb criticism without letting it damage their self-esteem.

At Your Best

Visionary, fair, spontaneous.

On the Dark Side

Critical, unrealistic, dissatisfied.

Love: High Expectations

The high expectations that February 7 natives hold can be quite daunting for potential partners. They are more than likely to live up to those standards themselves, but if others cannot do the same they can become bitter. It is important for them to allow their partner to have their own ideals rather than imposing theirs, and to be clear about the line between what is real and what is fantasy.

Health: Stress-Proofing Techniques

People born on this day generally take care of their health, eating well and exercising regularly, but they need to watch for unhealthy eating patterns when under stress. Mind-body therapies such as yoga, tai chi, and physical therapies like massage — or even thumping a punch bag — can help them unwind when the going gets tough. If stress is a constant companion, burning a chamomile-, Lavender-, or sandalwood-scented candle may help. Wearing, meditating on, and surrounding themselves with the colors Blue or Green produces a calming effect.

Career: Born Storytellers

These people have the ability to present their thoughts compellingly and can make excellent writers, storytellers, actors, journalists, politicians, teachers, and lecturers. Routine office work is best avoided; careers offering variety, travel, and challenge appeal far more. As self-starters, they may also find fulfillment working for themselves, or they could apply their analytical ability to the world of science or research. Humanitarian and social causes, as well as conventional or alternative health care, will almost certainly appeal, if not as a career then as a passionate outside interest.

Destiny: To Lead the Way to Social Reform

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to accept that not everyone will agree with them all of the time. When they can be tolerant of divergent viewpoints, their destiny is to persuade rather than dictate the need for social reform.

Power Thought

I can instruct, but I cannot enforce.
