Born on February 8th

The birthday of the hypnotist

Profile

People born on February 8 may not be able to see into the future, but they have the ability to shape it with their thoughts and words. They possess an intuitive understanding of people and situations, and are often able to spot emerging trends and then steer everyone in that direction.

The hypnotic power they carry gives them tremendous influence over the people and situations around them. This influence often astounds those who may have made more obvious efforts to move things forward and who may have dismissed them as mere dreamers.

Perhaps because they are aware of their influence over and responsibility toward others, February 8 natives can come across as grave or serious. They can tolerate small talk but do not particularly enjoy it, much preferring to sink their teeth into life's deeper problems. As a result, they often become the treasured confidant of friends and loved ones. On the personal side, however, there is both a passive quality and a shadow side that draws them toward people who are not good for them. Fortunately, in their early forties a significant turning point can help them become more self-aware and assertive.

The profound sensitivity and intuitive powers of these people mean they can effortlessly tune into the moods of those around them. They also run the risk of over-identifying with another person's problems and falling into the worry trap, although few people will notice, as they tend to conceal their true feelings to avoid being hurt. It is important for them to learn where other people end and they begin.

When February 8 people find a project that captures their interest, they have all the drive and determination they need to succeed. They can think on a grand scale, and once they learn to project success they may well find that their bright ideas lead to great progress and reform.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding the seedier, self-destructive side of life.

The Way Forward

Come to terms with your shadow side first, so you will have less need to explore it in the outside world.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 22 and June 21

Those dates fall under Gemini. You are both passionate, but it is more than passion that holds you together — you share so many interests.

Luck Maker: Remember Your Dreams

Learn to interpret the images of your dreams, because they are an important voice of your intuition, and following your intuition will bring you luck and happiness.

At Your Best

Driven, charming, sensitive.

On the Dark Side

Unassertive, overly serious, uncertain.

Love: Learn to Let One Person In

February 8 natives may find it hard to give their all in a relationship, as they can be preoccupied with their thoughts and with the problems of others. Their partner may feel excluded, since these people tend to live in a world of their own ideas. Once they find a partner who nurtures them as much as they nurture others, they can become extremely loyal, loving, and happy.

Health: Avoid Self-Destructive Behavior

People born on this day can sometimes take an irresponsible approach to their health, indulging in too much rich food, partying, and sensual pleasures. They may also be a little lazy and should learn that too much sleep can be just as harmful as too little. A healthy, balanced diet and plenty of exercise, along with meditation to help them recognize self-destructive tendencies, are highly recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green and listening to upbeat music will help bring calm while chasing worries and anxieties away.

Career: Born Psychics

These people may not become psychics in the traditional sense, but they will have great intuitive powers to put to use in their chosen career, thriving best where they are allowed plenty of freedom. Their ability to see the bigger picture and project ahead makes them excellent designers, property developers, stock market traders, and architects. They may also be drawn toward intellectual, planning, or forecasting roles. Their empathy and communication skills could lead them toward education, advertising, media, or the arts, where they can excel in writing, acting, and music.

Destiny: To Bring Greater Harmony and Understanding

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to set clear boundaries between themselves and others. Once they understand the impact they have on others and the impact others have on them, their destiny is to bring greater harmony and understanding to the world.

Power Thought

The universe runs through me, giving me direction.
