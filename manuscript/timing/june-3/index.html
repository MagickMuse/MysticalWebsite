Born on June 3rd

The birthday of original wit

Profile

People born on June 3 have a wonderful way with words, and their superb communication skills are the keys to their success. At work they use their persuasive powers to influence negotiations, and socially they use their sparkling wit to entertain others and win admirers.

Their ideas are always innovative and progressive, sometimes so far ahead that others struggle to understand them. Not feeling understood can be incredibly frustrating because they have plenty of important things to say. Free spirits who need to express their individuality, if they feel their position is misrepresented they will defend it passionately.

With sharp wit and a fantastic sense of humor, their feelings run deep and they have a strong belief in equality. But when disputes arise they are not afraid of using biting sarcasm to get their point across. It is important for them to become more sensitive to the effect their words have on others. Between ages eighteen and forty-eight there are opportunities to become more empathetic. After forty-nine they have a strong need for self-expression and assertiveness.

Once they learn to be more aware of their impact on others, there is very little to stop them reaching the top. They will always be slightly eccentric, but this originality is their driving force.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding sarcasm and negativity when things don't go your way.

The Way Forward

Cultivate a genuine respect for the rights of others.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You share a passion for communication, intellectual discovery, and solitude, and this can create a stimulating union.

Luck Maker: Simple Acts of Kindness

Add small words and acts of kindness into your day and see how your luck improves.

At Your Best

Expressive, articulate, witty.

On the Dark Side

Argumentative, unclear, cutting.

Love: Unique Spirit

June 3 natives are attracted to those with high ideals and aspirations. They need to be careful not to draw in those who want to dampen their unique spirit. They need deep intimacy and can be extremely warm and loving, but there may be conflicts between love and work.

Health: Regular Check-Ups

Those born on this day are often reluctant to visit the doctor, preferring natural approaches. Generally their health is sound, but regular check-ups are still advised. Regular exercise is highly recommended, as they tend to prefer mental to physical activity. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages warmth, physical enjoyment, and security.

Career: Born Lecturers

These people have the potential to excel in teaching, lecturing, research, and the performing arts, particularly music. A mentally challenging career is essential, and they may also find themselves drawn to sales, writing, publishing, commerce, and industry.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Original Ideas

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more lucid and sensitive in presenting their arguments. Once they find this balance, their destiny is to express their original ideas, inspire others, and make their unique mark on the world.

Power Thought

I now choose to release every negative thought from my mind and my life.
