Born on January 30th

The birthday of assurance

Profile

Confident in their convictions and beliefs, those born on January 30 love to put their personal stamp on everything they do. They live according to their own moral code and are always found on the side of the underdog or those less privileged. Their strong social conscience, paired with determination, charm, and intelligence, means they were born to take the lead.

Everyone who knows January 30 natives is in no doubt about where they stand on the issues that matter. They have a talent for bringing like-minded people together, and they would willingly live on the margins of society if it meant staying true to their ideals. Although they arrive at their positions through careful reasoning, they also rely heavily on instinct. This ability to blend intuition with logic marks them out as exceptionally gifted leaders.

Despite their self-assurance and firm convictions, people born on this day are just as prone to worry and impulsive behavior as anyone else. A desire to win approval can tempt them to bend the truth if they believe it will bring others on board. It is important for them to find balance between their convictions and reality, and to know when to hold their course despite opposition or setbacks. Once they understand that their determination, properly channeled, will always carry them through, they become far less susceptible to anxiety and mood swings. Fortunately, around the age of twenty-one and again at fifty-one, significant turning points make them more confident and assertive.

Highly ambitious and strong-willed, January 30 natives prefer to give orders rather than take them, and while they give orders superbly, they should learn to value the perspectives of others. Once they discover their own humility and learn to listen for and trust their inner guidance, they can move mountains with both their convictions and their generosity of spirit.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with being left alone while others enjoy the companionship you long for.

The Way Forward

Learn to accept people as they are, without finding too much fault or expecting them to always agree with you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. The principled and idealistic yet lighthearted and adventurous people born on these days are good for you, and you are good for them.

Luck Maker: Don't Have the Last Word

Every time you catch yourself topping someone's story with your own or holding forth, ask a question instead and truly listen to the answer. Know-it-alls are rarely lucky because they isolate themselves from the people around them.

At Your Best

Generous, outspoken, creative.

On the Dark Side

Driven, temperamental, untruthful.

Love: Comrade

January 30 natives do not simply want a partner; they want a comrade, someone who shares their convictions, ideas, and plans. They are most likely to meet their match through community activities, which provide the ideal outlet for their social instincts and leadership skills. They need to be careful, however, that their relationship does not take second place to a cause. They do best with partners who can make them laugh and draw them out of themselves from time to time.

Health: Take It Easy

People born on this day are extremely energetic and need to schedule regular time for rest, relaxation, and even the occasional afternoon nap. Their diet benefits from less meat and alcohol, while jogging or brisk walking is particularly recommended, as it tones the body and calms the mind simultaneously. Waking each morning to a glass of hot water with lemon rather than coffee will help revitalize and re-energize their system.

Career: Born Campaigners

These people have the potential to excel in careers that are people-focused and require them to lead, inspire, educate, or inform. They may also find themselves in executive or management positions, or thrive in negotiation. Routine does not suit them unless it serves a higher purpose. They could do well as airline personnel, pilots, navigators, or consultants of any kind, whether as department heads, doctors, social workers, psychologists, or even producers and directors.

Destiny: To Transform Empathy into Direct Action

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to respect and value the input of others without seeing it as a threat to their own convictions. Their destiny is to transform feelings of empathy into direct action and, in doing so, make a real and positive difference.

Power Thought

Today I will let go of expectations and let happiness in.
