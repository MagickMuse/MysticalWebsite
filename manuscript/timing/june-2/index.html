Born on June 2nd

The birthday of ingenuity

Profile

Analytical and intense, people born on June 2 have a talent for unraveling complicated situations. Their lives are rarely problem-free, but they would have it no other way. They are at their happiest when testing their ingenuity, and if life won't present them with problems, they will seek them out.

Quick-witted individuals who can readily analyze and adapt to any situation, they often devise effective solutions or courses of action. They can be the lifesavers who restore order, but their addiction to new stimuli can also work against them by unnecessarily complicating their lives when things are running smoothly.

The enthusiasm they have for problems can attract them to people and causes that are troubled or unworthy. The greatest challenge for them lies not in the outside world but in getting to know themselves better. Between the ages of nineteen and forty-nine there are numerous opportunities for emotional awareness. After fifty they enter a period of growing vitality and confidence.

If they can learn to focus less on external stimuli for fulfillment and more on their own imaginative powers, their potential for success is limitless because they have highly developed intuitive powers that, when directed toward a worthy cause, will lead them toward spell-binding creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Enjoying the ordinary.

The Way Forward

A fulfilling life is not one of extremes but of steady, positive feelings about every aspect, including routine and chores.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You share a passion for adventure, and this can create an exciting and intense union.

Luck Maker: Make Every Day Special

Every day, including those without highlights, is unique and special. Never waste an opportunity to feel positive about your life.

At Your Best

Intuitive, inventive, adaptable.

On the Dark Side

Self-defeating, restless, complicated.

Love: Don't Try to Change Others

June 2 natives may find themselves involved in difficult relationships repeatedly, but the only way to break this cycle is to understand they cannot change others, only themselves. When they find a loving partner, they must resist the temptation to cultivate excitement by creating tension.

Health: Bon Appétit!

Those born on this day may tend to gain weight, especially since they love to dine out. Rather than fad diets, the best approach is to cut down on saturated fats, sugar, and processed foods while increasing fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Combined with regular exercise, they can enjoy dining out without the pounds piling on. Like many born in early June, they are prone to nervous exhaustion, so regular rest is essential. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage harmony and balance.

Career: Born Troubleshooters

These people thrive in careers that allow autonomy in thought and action. They may be drawn to the artistic sphere, especially as performers, and also to scientific research or corporate troubleshooting and consulting. They also make excellent buyers, agents, or negotiators, and their humanitarian streak may draw them to counseling.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Resilience, Ingenuity, and Flair

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that change begins from the inside out. Once they understand this, their destiny is to influence, motivate, and inspire others with their resilience, ingenuity, and flair.

Power Thought

Every day offers me an opportunity to learn something new about myself.
