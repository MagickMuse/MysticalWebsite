Born on July 15th

The birthday of stimulus

Profile

Those born on July 15 are driven by a deep desire to stimulate and provoke a response in others. They are natural provocateurs who love to challenge the status quo and shake others out of their complacency. Their energy and enthusiasm are infectious, and they have a gift for making others see things in a completely new light.

Although their provocative nature can sometimes get them into trouble, their intentions are usually positive. They genuinely believe that challenging people to think differently is one of the greatest gifts they can offer. Between the ages of thirty-seven and sixty-seven there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to ensure that their desire to provoke does not alienate those they care about. After the age of sixty-seven there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing when to stop pushing.

The Way Forward

Understand that not everyone appreciates being challenged all the time. Sometimes people need support and encouragement more than stimulation.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both lovers of beauty and intellectual stimulation, and this can create a harmonious and exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: Read the Room

Lucky people understand that timing is everything. Knowing when to push and when to hold back is one of the most important skills for attracting good fortune.

At Your Best

Stimulating, provocative, energetic.

On the Dark Side

Pushy, insensitive, confrontational.

Love: Exciting and Challenging

Those born on this day are exciting and challenging partners who keep relationships vibrant and alive. They need a partner who enjoys intellectual sparring and who is not afraid of a little healthy conflict. They should be careful not to push their partner too hard or too often.

Health: Know Your Limits

Those born on this day have so much energy that they may push themselves too hard physically and emotionally. It is important for them to learn to recognize the signs of burnout and to take time to rest and recharge. Regular exercise is essential, especially activities that challenge them physically such as martial arts, rock climbing or competitive sports. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and avoid stimulants. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to be more calm and patient.

Career: Born Motivators

These individuals are natural motivators and may excel in coaching, teaching, management, sales, marketing or public speaking. Their provocative nature may also draw them to journalism, law, politics or social activism. Their creativity may lead them to careers in the arts, entertainment or design.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Think Differently

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their desire to stimulate with sensitivity to others' needs. Once they have done that, their destiny is to inspire others to think differently and to see the world in a new light.

Power Thought

I inspire others by listening as much as I challenge.
