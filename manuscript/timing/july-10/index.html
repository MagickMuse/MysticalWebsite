Born on July 10th

The birthday of the dark horse

Profile

Those born on July 10 are quietly determined individuals who often surprise others with their hidden depths and unexpected talents. They tend to be underestimated by others, but their quiet strength, resilience and determination ensure that they consistently exceed expectations.

Although they may appear unassuming and modest, they have a powerful inner drive and a deep well of creativity and ambition. Between the ages of forty-two and seventy-two there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and analysis. During these years they need to make sure they do not hide their talents under a bushel but find the confidence to share them with the world. After the age of seventy-two there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Putting yourself forward.

The Way Forward

Understand that hiding your talents does not serve you or the world. Sharing your gifts is not showing off; it is fulfilling your potential.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both creative and thoughtful, and this can create a harmonious and deeply satisfying relationship.

Luck Maker: Let People Know What You Can Do

Lucky people understand that nobody can help you if they do not know what you are capable of. Letting people know about your talents and abilities increases your chances of being in the right place at the right time.

At Your Best

Surprising, determined, creative.

On the Dark Side

Self-effacing, withdrawn, underestimated.

Love: Surprising Depth

Those born on this day are often more passionate and romantic than they appear on the surface. They need a partner who can appreciate their quiet strength and who is willing to look beneath the surface to discover the remarkable person within. Once they open up, they are loyal and deeply loving partners.

Health: Believe in Yourself

Those born on this day may suffer from low self-esteem because they tend to undervalue themselves. It is important for them to build their confidence through positive self-talk and by celebrating their achievements, no matter how small. Regular exercise is important, especially activities that build confidence such as martial arts, weight training or competitive sports. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, nutritious meals. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to be more assertive and confident.

Career: Born Underdogs

These individuals often thrive in careers where they can quietly prove their worth. They may excel in research, science, technology, writing, art, music or any field that rewards patience and perseverance. Their strategic mind may also draw them to business, law, finance or politics.

Destiny: To Surprise the World with Their Hidden Talents

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to believe in themselves and share their remarkable talents with the world. Once they have found the confidence to do that, their destiny is to surprise and inspire others with their hidden depths and unexpected abilities.

Power Thought

I step into the light and share my gifts with the world.
