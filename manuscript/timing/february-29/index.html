Born on February 29th

The birthday of wistful vivacity

Profile

In their early years, people born on this day may find it hard to be taken seriously. They may also sense something different about themselves, since they only celebrate a real birthday every four years.

Those born on this unusual day are likable and diplomatic — the latter skill perhaps learned early in life when they had to compromise over the date of their birthday. They have excellent social skills and the ability to relate to just about anyone. In addition, they carry an ethereal quality that makes them appear less resilient than they really are. Although others may dismiss them as dreamers or social butterflies, they are in fact surprisingly tough and ambitious.

They may feel driven to aggressively validate their self-perceived differences by working harder to achieve their goals, a strategy they can take to self-destructive extremes, especially between the ages of twenty-one and fifty-one, during which there is an emphasis on being assertive and ambitious. They should understand that over-promoting themselves is more likely to alienate than impress.

February 29 natives often have great insight into what makes people tick, but others are unlikely to understand what motivates them — though their motivation is simple. They simply want to fit in, to feel needed, and above all, to feel no different from everyone else. This is why they tend to be very caring and nurturing toward the problems of others and sincere in their desire to help in times of crisis. However, being rather sensitive, if their efforts do not receive the response or gratitude they expect, they can withdraw into immature or indulgent behavior.

They do have a tendency to overcompensate for their feelings of difference, and they should try to avoid extremes of behavior. Once they realize that their powerful intuition and youthful vivacity are strengths, not weaknesses, these special individuals will find that others not only accept but highly value their unique qualities.

Your Greatest Challenge

Believing in yourself.

The Way Forward

Stop comparing yourself with other people and remind yourself that you are a special and unique person.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. Home and security are extremely important in this partnership, creating a nurturing and sensitive bond.

Luck Maker: Adjust Your Self-Image

Your self-image guides your life, so notice how you are thinking of yourself and replace any negative thoughts with positive ones.

At Your Best

Youthful, intuitive, offbeat.

On the Dark Side

Immature, unfocused, moody.

Love: Put the Focus on Want, Not Need

February 29 natives want a loyal and affectionate partner who is always there for them, but they need to beware of becoming too dependent. Being insecure or needy in a relationship can destroy the very security they crave. They should cultivate an independent perspective and keep some part of their life separate from the partnership.

Health: Forever Young

The natural tendency of those born on this day is to indulge their hedonistic impulses, so it is important they stay level-headed about their health and avoid unnecessary risks. They have great physical and mental energy, and in their mature years others will admire their vigor. They may even take up youthful pursuits like skateboarding, parachuting, disco dancing, or rock climbing in middle age, though they should be careful of injuries to the lower body. Because they often love cooking, they tend to eat well, though they need to watch for overindulgence. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with shades of Blue will be good for them when they want to appear cool or do some logical thinking.

Career: Born Competitors

People born on this curious day thrive in careers that allow them to indulge their competitive spirit and prove their worth. They may be drawn to sport, business, and the stock exchange, or to the world of art, design, poetry, writing, and music. Their natural sympathy for others may also draw them toward humanitarian work or politics.

Destiny: To Talk Directly to the Souls of Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to value rather than regret their uniqueness. Once they develop enough self-confidence, their destiny is to find outlets for their optimism and creativity and, in doing so, talk directly to the souls of others.

Power Thought

I am perfect just as I am.
