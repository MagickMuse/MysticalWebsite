Born on June 11th

The birthday of expansion

Profile

Disciplined, energetic, perceptive, and optimistic, the ambitious people born on June 11 will often push their way toward their goals with surprising force, tearing down any obstacles in their path. Their driving force is always to push ahead, test their limits, and expand their knowledge and experience.

With the enviable ability to lose themselves wholly in their work, their aim is to learn as much as possible in one specialist area and then move the goalposts by pioneering a breakthrough. Their sense of fair play and willingness to get their hands dirty makes them a much-sought-after team player. Less evolved individuals who lack the support of others may find their self-absorption has slipped into arrogance; if they shift toward sensitivity and humility, others will be more willing to seek them out.

Their remarkable zest for life means they have little time for those with less energy or enthusiasm. They instinctively understand that a positive attitude increases their chances of success. But happiness and success are not always enough because their greatest desire is excellence, which is why they are often found pushing their work or lifestyle to new heights. Although this winning attitude can take them to the very top, it can lead to emotional isolation when others find their constant pushing exhausting.

Before age forty-one there are opportunities to develop a sense of emotional security. After forty-two they become more confident and aggressive in their approach; if they have learned to recognize the damaging effect their obsessive tendencies can have, this is the period when they can achieve truly great breakthroughs.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with domestic responsibilities.

The Way Forward

Great fulfillment can only be found when all areas of life, including the domestic, are harmonized.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You share a passion for progressive ideas but also a need to feel nurtured and secure within a relationship.

Luck Maker: Diversify Your Goals

Studies show that happiness is greater among those whose lives are generally positive in many areas rather than just one.

At Your Best

Progressive, positive, disciplined.

On the Dark Side

Stubborn, forceful, arrogant.

Love: Personal Charm

June 11 natives have great personal charm and a desire to protect and nurture those they love. However, they can find it hard to sustain family life because the lure of outside interests draws them away from domestic responsibilities. They need an intelligent partner who can give them security but also plenty of personal space.

Health: Work to Live

Those born on this day tend to live to work rather than work to live, which can lead to obsession if taken to extremes. They need to establish a healthier work-life balance or risk emotional isolation. More time and care should be taken with diet, as they tend to eat on the go. Regular exercise will encourage a more balanced perspective. Too busy to be ill, they may neglect regular check-ups. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will increase feelings of warmth, physical enjoyment, and emotional security.

Career: Born Marathon Runners

These people are attracted to the realms of scientific research, artistic innovation, and sport. Bright and versatile with excellent communication skills, they could thrive in any career but may find themselves drawn toward social work, politics, business, law, and the civil service. If they develop their innate creativity, they may also gravitate toward film, painting, or music.

Destiny: To Pioneer Significant Breakthroughs

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to sustain a settled and happy family or social life. Once they stop sacrificing the personal for the professional, their destiny is to push the boundaries and pioneer significant breakthroughs.

Power Thought

Every step forward in my life fills me with gratitude and respect.
