Born on April 24th

The birthday of devotion

Profile

People born on April 24 find their greatest fulfillment in knowing they have inspired or guided the lives of others. They possess enormous hearts and are devoted, protective friends who believe the world should be a place of universal love and equality.

They often take on a strong parental or protective role toward those they love, which can be both endearing and exasperating. Others are grateful for their attentions but may also find them draining or restrictive. They can be the nurturing figure who struggles to let go when a loved one wants to spread their wings, or the partner who cannot imagine a world outside their relationship. They may also feel let down when those they care about fall from the lofty pedestal they have placed them on. Learning to allow others the freedom to follow their own hearts and make their own mistakes is essential for their growth.

As well as being devoted to close relationships, they can become completely dedicated to their career, often fully identifying with it. When conflict arises between commitment to work and family, they may agonize over maintaining a balance. This is because they find it hard to separate their heart from their lives; but if they learn to give a little less and sometimes put themselves first, they may discover that it is possible to be more accommodating.

Until the age of twenty-six their lives are often centered on a need for affection and material security; after twenty-seven there are opportunities to develop more interests. There is another turning point after fifty-seven, placing more emphasis on fulfilling their emotional needs. Throughout their lives, learning to confidently use the word "no" will help them feel less torn between career and family and allow them to make their unique mark on the world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting the need to respond to every request.

The Way Forward

Understand that there is a difference between generosity and overextending yourself. Do not give endlessly to people who are perfectly capable of helping themselves.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You share a passion for romance and a desire for care, and this can create an intense and loving union.

Luck Maker: Stop Saying Yes to Everyone

Saying "no" more often to others and "yes" to yourself allows you to put your energies where they belong. If you spread yourself too thinly, you may miss an opportunity to contribute to the world in a meaningful way.

At Your Best

Devoted, nurturing, creative.

On the Dark Side

Indecisive, moody, smothering.

Love: Love-Struck

April 24 natives have great magnetism when it comes to affairs of the heart, but they need to be careful not to become so love-struck that they are blind to the faults of their partner. They should also avoid becoming too smothering in a relationship, as it will drain the romance right out of it.

Health: A Good Clear Out

Stability and domestic harmony are extremely important for those born on this day, and they may end up sacrificing a great deal of their individuality to achieve it; this can manifest in stress, depression, and comfort eating. It is crucial for them to learn to say "no" to the constant demands placed upon them and to put themselves first more regularly. They may also be prone to hormonal problems or fertility issues. Moderate to light exercise, in particular brisk walking, will increase their fitness levels and also give them much-needed time and space to think and be alone. As far as diet is concerned, they need to avoid routine and choose a varied but light diet, rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Their homes are likely to be cluttered with things from the past, and having a good clear out now and again will help them feel freer and lighter. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Red will boost their energy and encourage them to be more assertive.

Career: Born Educators

These people have excellent communication skills and a desire to protect and guide others. They can make excellent teachers, nurses, coaches, doctors, and counselors. Writing is also a skill that comes naturally to them, and because they are attracted to public life they can become involved in politics, acting, music, or entertainment. They may also be drawn to environmental concerns, philosophy, or mysticism.

Destiny: To Guide, Motivate, and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to draw a clear dividing line between their personal and professional lives. Once they are able to be more diverse in their approach to life, their destiny is, by example, to guide, motivate, and inspire others.

Power Thought

I am in charge of my life. I reclaim my power.
