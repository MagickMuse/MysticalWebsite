Born on November 5th

The birthday of the representative

Profile

Those born on November 5 often feel that they have a message to deliver to the world. They are natural spokespersons who can present their views with clarity and conviction, and are particularly skilled at summarizing and expressing the feelings and thoughts of others.

Possessing a strong sense of social justice, they often find themselves speaking up for or representing the interests of those who cannot or do not speak for themselves. This makes them valued members of any team or community, and their insights are highly sought after. They have a natural ability to tap into the mood of a group and articulate what everyone is thinking but nobody has yet been able to say.

Despite their orientation toward others, they are also independent, original thinkers who are not afraid to challenge conventional wisdom when they believe it is wrong. This can sometimes put them at odds with those they are trying to represent, but more often than not their boldness earns them respect. They do, however, need to be careful that their desire to represent or speak for others does not tip over into manipulation or self-righteous preaching.

Until age forty-six there is an emphasis on adventure and freedom, and they may take risks or explore new ideas through study or travel. After forty-seven, a turning point arrives when they start to become more practical, determined and focused. Whatever age they are, learning to listen as well as they speak, and to accept that they do not always have all the answers, will help them earn the trust and loyalty they need to fulfill their considerable potential as a voice that genuinely makes a difference.

Your Greatest Challenge

Listening as much as you talk.

The Way Forward

Understand that the most effective communicators are those who listen deeply before they speak.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are both analytical and service-oriented, and this can be a stimulating and deeply supportive union.

Luck Maker: Ask More Questions

Lucky people know that asking questions is one of the most powerful tools available. The more you ask, the more you learn, and the more opportunities reveal themselves.

At Your Best

Articulate, fair-minded, inspiring.

On the Dark Side

Preachy, opinionated, self-righteous.

Love: Stimulating Company

Those born on this day are lively, witty and stimulating partners who bring energy and intelligence to their relationships. They can be extremely loyal and supportive but need to be careful not to dominate conversations or always insist on being right. The right partner for them will be someone who is equally articulate and confident, and who can challenge them intellectually while also offering warmth and emotional depth.

Health: Watch Your Throat

Given that communication is central to their lives, these individuals need to take special care of their throat and vocal cords. They should avoid shouting, and warm drinks with honey and lemon are advised when their throat feels strained. They may also be prone to stress and tension in the neck and shoulders, and regular massage or stretching exercises are recommended. A balanced diet rich in antioxidants and anti-inflammatory foods will support their overall health. Moderate exercise, in particular walking or swimming, is ideal for them. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage balance and harmony, and carrying a Topaz crystal will enhance their communication skills and confidence.

Career: Born Advocates

These individuals are natural advocates and may be drawn to careers in law, politics, journalism, public relations, social work, teaching, counseling or the charity sector. Their communication skills also make them well suited to writing, broadcasting, sales and motivational speaking. In business, they may excel in roles that involve negotiation, mediation or team leadership.

Destiny: To Be a Voice for Those Who Cannot Speak for Themselves

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the power of listening as well as speaking. Once they have found that balance, their destiny is to use their remarkable communication skills to be a genuine voice for progress and positive change.

Power Thought

I listen with my heart as well as my ears.
