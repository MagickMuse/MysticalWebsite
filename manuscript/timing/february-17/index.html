Born on February 17th

The birthday of self-discipline

Profile

People born on February 17 often figure out early in life that the key to success is discipline. They are determined, ambitious people with a clear idea of where they want to go and what they need to do to get there. These qualities, combined with their remarkable self-discipline, can give them an air of invincibility.

Although they may appear superhuman and extraordinary, others generally warm to them instantly, respecting their honesty and ability to be true to themselves and their beliefs. Underneath their tough exterior, however, they are sensitive souls who can be profoundly hurt by careless words or actions. Neglect or criticism may have been a feature of their early life, leading them to realize that if they are to survive in the world they need to build a strong shell. Occasionally they make their defenses so impenetrable that others find it impossible to break through. When this happens, they risk becoming emotionally detached and inflexible.

February 17 natives have a tunnel-vision approach to their goals and objectives, giving them the potential to rise to the very top. They are the athletes who train relentlessly, the entrepreneurs who sacrifice everything for their shot at success, the artists or scientists who devote their lives to their craft. The downside is that anything that hinders their quest for fulfillment will be ignored — all too often this means personal relationships. They should make sure emotional happiness does not take a back seat to professional ambition, especially after the age of thirty-three, when they often become even more determined and aggressive in their approach.

The incredible endurance, intelligence, and stamina of those born on this day mean they are able to achieve a level of self-mastery and resulting satisfaction that others can only aspire to. Once they figure out what they are best at, there is nothing that can stop them from achieving remarkable things.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to let others in.

The Way Forward

Understand that success may earn the admiration of others, but it will not necessarily win their love.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You both appreciate those who are disciplined and hardworking, and this can create a vibrant and rewarding union.

Luck Maker: Move Out of the Corner

When you have the flexibility to learn other ways of doing things, you can make luck from a vast playing field of opportunities rather than a narrow corner of one room.

At Your Best

Disciplined, determined, appealing.

On the Dark Side

Isolated, inflexible, suspicious.

Love: Cool and Controlled

February 17 natives can be distant and inflexible in close personal relationships. If they are to find success and happiness in love, they need to address this. Although they have no problem attracting admirers, they do find it hard to open up. Once they find a partner who encourages them to give as well as take, they become loyal, caring, and endlessly fascinating.

Health: Athletes

People born on this day push themselves hard in all areas of life, and the physical realm is no exception. Whether or not they are professional athletes, they tend to regard themselves as athletes in training and take great care of their physical health through attention to diet and exercise. Some may push too hard, however, and others may neglect their health entirely when deadlines loom, so moderation is just as important as self-discipline. A healthy diet low in saturated fats and sugar is recommended to reduce the risk of circulatory problems, along with an exercise routine where they can clearly monitor improvement, such as weight training. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with shades of Green will remind them of the importance of balance in their personal and professional lives.

Career: Born Detectives

These people love to unravel a mystery and may be drawn to careers in law or law enforcement. They also have a talent for writing and self-expression, which could lead to journalism, writing, or education. They make great athletes, artists, and scientists, and because they thrive in careers demanding self-discipline and self-motivation, they may also be attracted to management, charity work, social reform, or self-employment.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Vitality

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to place as much importance on personal happiness as on the pursuit of their goals. Once they find a sense of balance, their destiny is to influence and inspire others with their considerable vitality and self-discipline.

Power Thought

Today I will look at life in a different way.
