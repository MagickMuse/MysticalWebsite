Born on June 28th

The birthday of the sprite

Profile

People born on June 28 are often focused and driven individuals, but they also have a sense of fun and lightness that permeates their lives. They can laugh at themselves if the joke is on them and don't take themselves too seriously. Motivated and enterprising, they eagerly latch onto every opportunity to further their plans and endeavors.

They can typically be counted on to break the tension at any social gathering, their sharp wit turning the spotlight on them and winning them many admirers. Although they have a lighthearted quality, it's a mistake to underestimate their ability in the competitive world, as underneath their fun-loving exterior they have an iron will and a facility to turn their visions into reality.

Sometimes they may be accused of disorganization because they like to be on the go; if they aren't moving, dancing, or running they will probably be fidgeting. But the quality of work they produce is anything but chaotic. Others will wonder how someone so lighthearted makes difficult tasks look so easy. What others don't realize is that they have worked as hard as everyone else, sometimes harder, but instead of complaining they simply get on with it and "effortlessly" generate quality results.

They do need to be aware that their longing to be noticed may be the result of hidden fears and insecurities. In their early life they may have been painfully shy, but after age twenty-three they gain a much-needed boost of strength and confidence. If they take advantage of opportunities to build self-esteem, they have the determination to become an advisor or authority figure that others admire, respect, and hope to emulate.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not always feeling you need to perform or deliver.

The Way Forward

You alone teach other people what to expect from you, so teach them to treat you with respect.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both fiercely individualistic and inquisitive, and this can be a warm and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Trust in Yourself

Unwavering belief in your own potential is the one essential element. It generates the ability and enthusiasm necessary to attract luck and become a winner in life.

At Your Best

Warm, lively, delightful.

On the Dark Side

Chaotic, tactless, self-conscious.

Love: Share the Spotlight

Being witty, friendly, and charming, June 28 natives don't often have problems attracting partners, but anyone who wants to share their life may have to be content with a supporting role. Finding someone who can share their stimulating intellectual interests and sense of fun will create the ideal relationship.

Health: Serious Thinking

Those born on this day understand the importance of fun, laughter, and love in their lives, and their health benefits as a result. They need to be careful not to get over-involved in the problems of friends and family, as this will drag some lightness out of them. They may have problems with weight, so they need to eat healthy, nutritious food and avoid fad diets. Regular exercise is highly recommended, but even without it they will burn off plenty of energy with their constant fidgeting. Meditation techniques can help them travel internally rather than externally. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage others to take them more seriously.

Career: Born Celebrities

Those born on this day have a yearning to be instruments of progress, which may draw them toward the caring professions or humanitarian projects. Being natural psychologists, they may also excel in careers involving personal contact such as counseling, personnel, promotions, or public relations. Teaching and lecturing may also appeal, and a desire to be creative may draw them toward catering, design, acting, music, and the world of entertainment, or setting up their own business as an entrepreneur.

Destiny: To Share Their Happiness and Inspiration with Others

The life path of those born on this day is to discover that people can and do love them for who they are, not for their ability to entertain. Once they have worked on their self-esteem, their destiny is to share their happiness and inspiration with others.

Power Thought

I am perfect, just as I am.
