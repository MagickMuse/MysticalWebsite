Born on June 5th

The birthday of the intellectual juggler

Profile

People born on June 5 can often be found successfully juggling ideas and projects simultaneously. They also have the ability to generate such innovative ideas that others either admire or fail to understand them. Their vision and soaring imagination seem unlimited in scope.

With unlimited energy, an expansive mind, excellent conversation skills, and the ability to get things done, they are always in demand. Born to stimulate others to think, they are always seeking to unearth some great discovery or mystery. The key to their success lies in their ability to communicate effectively. If misunderstood, it wounds them deeply and may express itself in flashes of anger or nervous tension.

Between ages sixteen and forty-six they focus on emotional security, and home issues come to the fore. It is important during this time that they guard against sudden anger and criticism. Born worriers, they also need to manage their negativity and hold on to the dreams that inspire them. After forty-seven there is a turning point signaling growing self-confidence.

They have boundless energy and creativity. Although they should never lose their vitality, they need to learn how to channel it effectively and become more focused.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to pace yourself.

The Way Forward

Just because you are capable of doing lots of things at once doesn't mean you have to.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. You share a passion for intellectual stimulation and thoughtfulness, and this can create a fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Get Rid of "What Ifs"

"What ifs" rob your present and distort your future. If you are always exhausted by worrying about things that won't happen, you won't have energy to go after what you want.

At Your Best

Articulate, versatile, intelligent.

On the Dark Side

Anxious, chaotic, highly strung.

Love: What Lies Beneath

June 5 natives often have plenty of friends and admirers because they are genuinely interested in people. However, few will really know them well, and only after finding the perfect partner will they start to open up and reveal their true, passionate nature within.

Health: Put It in the Diary

Always on the go with seemingly limitless energy, their health is generally good, but they need to be careful not to rely on caffeine and other stimulants. They also need to recharge with plenty of rest. A routine approach to diet and exercise is recommended to balance their hectic lifestyle. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to stay cool and in control.

Career: Born Comics

These people have a talent for using language and possess the originality to become great comics, writers, poets, and lyricists. The realm of science and technology will also allow their talents to flourish, and their verbal skills may serve them well in law, diplomacy, business, advertising, and healthcare.

Destiny: To Inspire and Even Shock Others with Their Ideas

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to worry less and live more. Once they can manage their negativity, their destiny is to refresh, inspire, and occasionally shock others with their innovative ideas or approach.

Power Thought

Today I let go of my worry and uncertainty.
