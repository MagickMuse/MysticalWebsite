Born on October 19th

The birthday of the peaceful activist

Profile

Those born on October 19 are idealistic individuals with a powerful vision for a better, more peaceful world. Their gentle, diplomatic manner disguises a steely inner resolve and a passionate desire to make a positive difference. They are natural campaigners who lead by example rather than by force, and their quiet determination can achieve remarkable results.

Although they are peace-loving by nature, they are not afraid to stand up for what they believe in. When they see injustice, they will speak out against it with courage and conviction. They are articulate and persuasive, and have a gift for winning others over to their cause without alienating them. Their ability to see both sides of an argument makes them excellent mediators and diplomats, and others often turn to them for advice and guidance.

Despite their outward composure, they can be prone to inner conflict. Their desire for peace and harmony can sometimes clash with their equally strong desire to challenge the status quo and fight for change. Learning to balance these two impulses is one of their biggest challenges. They also have a tendency to take on the problems of others as their own, and this can lead to stress and emotional exhaustion if they are not careful to set boundaries.

Before age thirty-two they are likely to be most concerned with issues of emotional intensity and personal power. After thirty-three, a turning point arrives when they are likely to become more optimistic and adventurous. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that their desire to help others does not come at the expense of their own well-being. When they find the right balance between activism and self-care, they have the potential to be truly inspirational figures who lead by positive example.

Your Greatest Challenge

Setting boundaries.

The Way Forward

Understand that you cannot pour from an empty cup. Taking care of yourself is not selfish; it is essential for sustaining the energy you need to help others.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both passionate idealists, and this can be a warm, supportive and inspiring relationship.

Luck Maker: Recharge Your Batteries

Lucky people know that rest and relaxation are not luxuries but necessities. When you recharge, you attract more energy, creativity and good fortune into your life.

At Your Best

Idealistic, diplomatic, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Self-sacrificing, conflicted, exhausted.

Love: A Gentle Touch

Those born on this day are romantic and devoted partners who bring warmth, kindness and understanding to their relationships. They are attracted to people who share their idealism and passion for justice. However, they need to be careful not to lose themselves in their partner or their relationship, and to maintain their own identity and interests.

Health: Nurture Yourself

These individuals give so much of themselves to others that they can neglect their own health and well-being. It is essential for them to schedule regular time for relaxation and self-care. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will help keep their energy levels stable. Gentle exercise such as walking, swimming or yoga is ideal for managing stress without depleting their reserves. They should also pay attention to their sleep, as they can sometimes lie awake worrying about the problems of the world. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will help boost their energy and enthusiasm.

Career: Born Counselors

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in counseling, social work, charity, diplomacy, human rights, politics and community leadership. Their communication skills may also draw them toward journalism, writing, teaching and law. Their artistic sensibility may express itself through careers in music, art and design.

Destiny: To Lead by Positive Example

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their desire to help others with the need to nurture themselves. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to lead by positive example and inspire others to work toward a more peaceful and just world.

Power Thought

I nurture myself so that I can nurture the world.
