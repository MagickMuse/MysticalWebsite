Born on February 23rd

The birthday of the capable front runner

Profile

People born on February 23 have an optimistic, positive, can-do approach to life, and this is the key to their success. They are quietly rather than openly confident, believing that their accomplishments will speak for themselves. Because they are neither flashy, pretentious, nor showy, other people tend to be drawn to them.

February 23 natives take great care in every aspect of their lives and bring an analytical approach to problems, yet they are far from ponderous. They can be incredibly efficient, capable of delivering quality results for any task they undertake. They often find great joy in the work itself rather than the reward. Having weighed up the alternatives, they believe they are the best person for the job with the best approach to it. The confident certainty they project can appear uncompromising, but it is also contagious. More often than not, others believe exactly what they say and place enormous trust in them.

Another strength of those born on this day is their power of self-expression. Not only are they extremely articulate, they are also excellent listeners — an unusual combination that distinguishes them from other great speakers. They often find themselves in the position of confidant, which can sometimes give them an unfair advantage; they should be mindful not to become manipulative when things are not going their way. They should channel their verbal skills and empathy positively, especially between the ages of twenty-seven and fifty-six, when they become more confident and ambitious and are likely to launch many new ventures.

Above all, February 23 people pride themselves on being the best person for the job. They will devote enormous effort to making everything in their lives — including themselves — the very best it can be, and as a result it often is. As long as they accept that life is not, and was never meant to be, perfect, these capable front runners have the potential to earn great respect and affection from everyone who crosses their path.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming any shyness.

The Way Forward

Simply pretend you are confident. The more you pretend, the easier it becomes to slip into that role naturally.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 22 and June 21

Those dates fall under Gemini. You share a passion for talking and for listening, and this creates an unbreakable bond.

Luck Maker: Reveal Your Passion

When you show excitement, you give off energy, and this attracts others because it shows that you are committed and that you care.

At Your Best

Capable, self-starting, articulate.

On the Dark Side

Manipulative, cautious, uncompromising.

Love: Take Your Time

February 23 natives have the ability to seduce others with a few carefully chosen glances and words. They are looking for someone to share their life with and build a future; one-night stands do not really appeal. They tend to be dazzled by looks first and hearts and minds second, so it might help if they applied the same pragmatic approach to relationships as they do to their professional life, taking their time before rushing headlong in.

Health: Hands On

Wanting to stay young and fit, those born on this day should eat carefully and exercise plenty to keep themselves toned and healthy. They treat their body the same way they treat everything else: as an opportunity to fine-tune and improve. There is a danger, however, that they push themselves too hard, so they need to find ways to relax, unwind, and above all have fun. Plenty of sleep is important, and their bedroom should be a comforting, peaceful space, perhaps painted in the color Green to promote harmony and balance. They may also benefit from regular massage or reflexology, as they can be prone to aches and pains, especially in their feet, from their hard-working approach to life.

Career: Born Analysts

These people make great advisors, agents, negotiators, analysts, planners, and expert consultants in any field. Their communication skills ensure success in any people-related career, and they may be drawn to artistic, musical, or dramatic forms of expression. Whatever career they choose, the capable and determined approach that defines February 23 natives will push them to the forefront of their area of expertise.

Destiny: To Encourage Others to Do Things the Right Way

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be the vibrant person they truly are. Once they learn to take themselves a little less seriously, their destiny is to encourage others to do things the right way.

Power Thought

My life is wonderful in every way.
