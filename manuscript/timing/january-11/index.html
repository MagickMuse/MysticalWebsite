Born on January 11

The birthday of the expert assessor

Profile

People born on January 11 possess a natural gift for sizing up every situation and measuring everyone they encounter. They discard what has no use with little hesitation and cut straight to the core of people and circumstances, judging them against their own demanding standards. When those formidable powers of perception combine with high intelligence, the result is an outstanding decision maker.

Underpinning this talent for assessment is a powerful sense of fairness. They feel bound to do the right thing and to pass judgment, but they can sometimes lose the boundary between what is right for them and what is right for someone else. This leads them to convince themselves that they must shoulder responsibility for everyone and everything, and that is where trouble can begin, breeding domineering or controlling behavior and the conviction that their word is law.

It is important for them to learn to voice their opinions with less force so they do not alienate people who see things differently. This will not always come easily, but alongside their rigidity lives a deeply caring and compassionate side. The more they draw on it, the more they understand that holding a different opinion is not just their right but everyone's. Around age forty, often earlier, their emotional sensitivity strengthens and their inner life grows richer.

They hold others to high standards, but they hold themselves to even higher ones. Because they have the courage and determination to meet those standards, they frequently end up in the very position they desire: the seat of judgment. Others come to them for advice, for verdicts, and, once they soften their inflexibility, for genuine inspiration.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with feeling powerless to change things.

The Way Forward

Understand that you can only change the things that you can. When a situation cannot be changed, you need to have faith and learn to let go.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a passion for helping others and for social justice, and this creates an enduring bond.

Luck Maker: Give Others the Benefit of the Doubt

If you think the best of others they are more likely to fulfill that expectation.

At Your Best

Balanced, objective, fair.

On the Dark Side

Dominating, stubborn, superior.

Love: Loyal and Devoted

Once January 11 natives find a partner who stimulates their mind or sparks their creativity, they become fiercely loyal and devoted. Relating comes naturally to them, yet they can sometimes hesitate to open up emotionally. There is also a risk of taking on too much responsibility for the relationship; they need to learn to step back, open their heart, and let their partner take the lead.

Health: Take Care of Their Own Needs

These individuals must guard against compassion overload, exhausting themselves by caring for others or battling for their rights. They need to practice letting go and tending to their own physical and emotional needs. Their diet should steer clear of anything restrictive or extreme, favoring balance and variety instead, perhaps with a multivitamin supplement to cover nutritional gaps. Regular, gentle exercise is important. Burning candles scented with cypress, clary sage, jasmine, coriander, cloves, or sandalwood, and wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color silver, helps them feel more secure and at peace.

Career: Born Helpers

January 11 people gravitate toward work where they can help others. Their compassion makes them excel in education, teaching, careers with children or students, counseling, and psychology. They may also be drawn to research that uses their sharp, incisive mind, or to fields where they can fight injustice, such as politics, social work, law, or the clergy. They make fine alternative therapists and astrologers, and their communication skills point to a talent for writing, music, and the arts.

Destiny: To Help Others Turn Dreams into Reality

Once they develop compassion and sensitivity toward the shortcomings of others, the life path of January 11 natives is to confront injustice and right wrongs. In doing so they uncover their destiny, which is to help people transform hopes and dreams into practical reality.

Power Thought

Today I will take it easy on myself and everyone else.
