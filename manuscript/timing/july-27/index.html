Born on July 27th

The birthday of the director

Profile

Those born on July 27 tend to be hardworking, confident and authoritative individuals with the drive and determination to manage or direct others. They are at their most fulfilled when they are organizing and managing others, and their strength of purpose is so strong that others tend to stand aside in their wake.

Their natural authority, self-discipline and pragmatism make them excellent leaders who prefer to communicate through actions rather than words. They tend to put their career first, often at the expense of their personal relationships. Between the ages of twenty-six and fifty-six there is an emphasis on order and practicality; these are the years when they are most likely to make important career choices. After the age of fifty-six they may become more involved in relationships, creativity and harmony.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning when to back off.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes the best way to manage a situation is to take a step back and let things unfold naturally.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both driven, energetic and self-assured, and this can create a dynamic and passionate union.

Luck Maker: Choose Your Battles

Lucky people understand that not every battle is worth fighting. They save their energy for the ones that matter and let the small things go.

At Your Best

Purposeful, organized, authoritative.

On the Dark Side

Domineering, workaholic, inflexible.

Love: Lead with Your Heart

Those born on this day are passionate and determined partners but they need to make sure they do not try to control their relationships. They are attracted to strong, independent individuals and need a partner who can stand up to them while also appreciating their loyalty and commitment.

Health: Balance Work and Rest

Those born on this day are so driven that they may neglect their health in pursuit of their goals. It is important for them to schedule regular downtime and relaxation. They should watch their stress levels and make sure they get enough sleep. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and avoid comfort eating or skipping meals when busy. Vigorous exercise is recommended to help them release tension and excess energy. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more spontaneous and warm.

Career: Born Managers

These individuals are natural managers and may excel in business, politics, law, the military or any career that allows them to take charge. They may also be drawn to directing in theater or film, coaching, teaching or sports management.

Destiny: To Lead Others Toward Achievement

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their need for control with the need for flexibility and emotional openness. Once they have done that, their destiny is to use their remarkable organizational skills to lead others toward achievement.

Power Thought

I lead with strength and listen with compassion.
