Born on October 27th

The birthday of the galvanizer

Profile

Those born on October 27 are emotional individuals who tend to react instantly and spontaneously to anyone or anything they encounter. Acting on their impulses comes as second nature to them, and their ability to excite and galvanize others with the intensity of their emotions gives them outstanding potential to lead and motivate.

Impossible to ignore, they are at their happiest and their best when expressing their thoughts and feelings, and influencing or directing those around them. Their decisions and opinions are undoubtedly driven by emotion, but they also have the intellect, communication and practical skills they need to see their goals realized. Once their imagination or heart has been touched they are, quite literally, unstoppable.

Given their impulsive nature, it is not surprising that they are also prone to mood swings, and a recurring theme in their life will be intense highs followed by intense lows. One of the reasons for this unpredictability is that beneath their outspoken, expressive exterior there is often a fragile and vulnerable side that simply is not getting the nurturing or respect it needs. This is especially the case if getting the approval of others matters more to them than anything else. It is therefore extremely important that they work on building their self-esteem, learning to listen more to their intuition, as their inner wisdom will be able to gently warn them when their emotions are blocking their potential for luck and happiness.

Before age twenty-five their emotional impulsiveness will be heightened, but after twenty-six they are likely to become less sensitive, and more independent and adventurous. Whatever age they are, they will always follow their heart rather than their head, and they will feel things more deeply than other people. The key to their success will be their ability to direct their emotional intensity and energy positively so that they do not just galvanize but light up both their own lives and the lives of others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming your impatience.

The Way Forward

Understand that lots of people miss out on good fortune because they are not patient enough to wait for serendipity to visit.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both energetic and impulsive, and this can be a very satisfying and intense relationship.

Luck Maker: Let Your Intuition Lead You

Your intuition will tell you when to be patient and when to act. Slow down, cool down and listen to it.

At Your Best

Exciting, energetic, intelligent.

On the Dark Side

Reckless, insecure, scattered.

Love: Don't Hide

Although they are intensely emotional individuals, when it comes to affairs of the heart those born on this day can be surprisingly hesitant. They may try to hide their feelings behind a mask of small talk, bravado or in some cases jealousy and controlling behavior. Love can be scary for them, but throwing themselves in its path will be easier than the anxiety that avoiding it causes.

Health: Don't Put All Your Eggs in One Basket

These individuals can take longer to recover from disappointments and setbacks than others. They need to make sure they take every opportunity to rest and recharge. It is also wise for them not to put all their eggs in one basket, so that if one area of their life is not going well they can draw strength from another area that is. Overwork is a big threat to their health and, because they are so impulsive, accidents and injuries can also be a common problem. They need to pay attention to their digestive health and eat a diet rich in fruits, vegetables and whole grains. Exercise such as walking is also recommended for its mood-boosting benefits, as well as its ability to calm and quiet the mind. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will have a cooling, calming effect.

Career: Born Musicians

These individuals will flourish in careers in which they can impart their knowledge or ideals, such as teaching or journalism, but they may also thrive as musicians, actors and writers. Other career options include promotion, sales, media, advertising and the caring and healing professions.

Destiny: To Play a Directional Role in Life

The life path of those born on this day is to find balance and harmony within themselves. Once they are able to think before they speak or act, their destiny is to play an educational, inspirational or directional role in life.

Power Thought

No matter what anyone thinks, I will listen to my gut feelings.
