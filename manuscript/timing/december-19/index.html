Born on December 19th

The birthday of revealing honesty

Profile

Those born on December 19 come across as sensitive individuals, but this conceals remarkable hidden strength and courage. They are not afraid to express themselves freely, and when people cross or upset them they are honest enough to reveal their true feelings. Others may mistake their sensitivity and outspokenness as weakness, but releasing emotions in this way actually makes them feel stronger.

Uncompromisingly individualistic in their approach, they are unlikely to thrive in environments where they need to conform. They have a strong need to challenge convention, question norms and generate original alternatives. Although their revealing honesty can work against them, others tend to respect them for their integrity and individuality. Their greatest desires are to educate or enlighten others and to further human progress.

When faced with challenges or setbacks, they reveal a fighting spirit and steady determination to succeed, giving them endless potential for professional success. Unfortunately, they sometimes seem unable to show similar resilience in personal life. They can be prone to bouts of negativity and self-pity that manifest in flashes of anger or outrageous behavior, perplexing and confusing both themselves and others. If they can learn to turn inward the same spirit of daring and courage they display in the outer world, there are no mountains too high for them to climb.

Until age thirty-two, they are likely to focus on practical issues with a need for order in their lives. A turning point around thirty-three brings a growing desire for more personal freedom and experimentation. If they can learn to think before they act and positively redirect their thoughts when they start to spiral, these are the years when they truly come into their own, revealing to the world just how committed they are to making a valuable contribution to the greater good.

Your Greatest Challenge

Turning your back on self-pity.

The Way Forward

Understand that feeling sorry for yourself brings absolutely nothing positive into your life, only the pity of those whose admiration you seek.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are exuberant and daring characters, and if you share the limelight this can be a fulfilling and exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: Power Is in Your Thoughts

Let go of negative thoughts and difficulties from the past. Your luck-making power is in your thoughts, which means you are the only one who can create the life you deserve.

At Your Best

Courageous, honest, expressive.

On the Dark Side

Negative, depressed, perplexing.

Love: Expressive and Open

Those born on this day love to flirt, and others are instantly drawn to their expressive and open personality. They attract lovers easily but need to be careful about their choice of partner, as they are prone to bouts of anxiety and negativity. They need someone who is both sensitive and supportive to help them work through difficult patches.

Health: Calm and in Control

The biggest threat to the health of those born on this day is the tendency to indulge in negative feelings. Depression is a real possibility if they do not learn to accept and manage their emotional intensity. They should remind themselves that they are in charge of their feelings, not the other way around. If they cannot achieve this level of objectivity, cognitive behavioral therapy techniques will certainly help reprogram their thoughts. They should steer clear of drugs and limit alcohol because under the influence their lack of control can have potentially dangerous consequences. The best way to keep thoughts and mood positive is to eat a healthy, balanced diet, get between six and eight hours of quality sleep every night and take plenty of vigorous exercise. Staying in touch with friends and loved ones, keeping a diary and developing interests outside work are also recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue encourages calm and control.

Career: Born Performers

These individuals often choose to share their knowledge through writing and teaching, but they may also be drawn to music, acting, the media, publishing, advertising, the healing professions and art.

Destiny: To Enlighten and Enchant Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to accept and manage their emotions, even the negative ones. Once they loosen up and enjoy life a little more, their destiny is to enlighten, influence, educate and enchant others with their emotional honesty.

Power Thought

When I focus on positive things, it helps me and also the world.
