Born on June 4th

The birthday of the apprentice

Profile

The intellectual powers of those born on June 4 are spectacular. The key to their success is education or apprenticeship that can help nurture their quick minds. They are at their best in the role of student acquiring new knowledge.

With an intense desire to learn and perfect themselves, they are always hungry for novel information or new challenges. Their natural curiosity is one of their greatest strengths, but it can become a weakness if they rely on it too much and neglect their emotional side. If trapped in the learning stage, they risk losing flexibility by becoming totally absorbed in the project at hand, and may explode with anger if their work is interrupted.

It is important for them to place more emphasis on the feeling part of their nature, especially in close relationships. Between ages seventeen and forty-seven there are opportunities to focus on emotional well-being. After forty-eight they become more confident and there will be opportunities to realize their visions.

They have great leadership potential, but until they grow in self-confidence they are often happiest working as part of a team. Once they tune into their own and others' feelings, they will discover a creative potential that may have been lying dormant.

Your Greatest Challenge

Acknowledging your emotions.

The Way Forward

Every emotion, even so-called negative ones such as anger, fear, and envy, has something important to tell you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You both have intelligence and an inquisitive approach to life, and this can be a fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Put First Things First

Although work is important, the physical and emotional well-being of yourself and the people you care about is more so.

At Your Best

Intelligent, intuitive, empathetic.

On the Dark Side

Critical, stressed, unfulfilled.

Love: Develop Your Nurturing Side

June 4 natives tend to let work take precedence over their personal life. It is important that they learn to accentuate the feeling and nurturing part of their nature. A relationship is about warmth, reassurance, and small acts of kindness as well as intellectual stimulation.

Health: Laughter Is the Best Medicine

Those born on this day may be prone to weight gain, especially in midlife. Long hours of work take their toll, so they need plenty of opportunities to relax and unwind. A little on the serious side, they could benefit from more fun. Mind-body techniques such as meditation and yoga will lift their spirits. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will encourage them to be more optimistic and creatively confident.

Career: Born Researchers

These people have an aptitude for study and would make excellent teachers, researchers, and lecturers. Once they get in touch with their creative side they may be drawn to art, music, dance, and drama. They enjoy sharing knowledge and working in a team, so they may also excel in sales, publishing, advertising, and journalism.

Destiny: To Share Their Knowledge with Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to have more confidence in their own creativity. Once they connect with their intuition, their destiny is to achieve progress by sharing their knowledge with others.

Power Thought

Today I am an open channel for original ideas.
