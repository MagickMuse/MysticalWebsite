Born on December 23rd

The birthday of the cautious revolutionary

Profile

Those born on December 23 are hardworking, quietly ambitious individuals who are at their happiest and best when they can identify areas of improvement and then formulate original, sometimes radical but always practical solutions. Gifted organizers, they prefer to plan carefully and work steadily toward progressive change.

They distrust sudden change and feel uncomfortable when it is thrust upon them, as it upsets their determined plans for steady progress. When they assume positions of power, which they often do given their authoritative presence and excellent communication skills, they can become resistant to change. They can also grow controlling and defensive when challenged, reacting with hostility when alternative viewpoints are offered. It is therefore extremely important for their psychological growth and professional success that they learn to be more flexible and open-minded in their approach.

Before age twenty-eight, they may have shown a responsibility beyond their years, perhaps getting their foot on the property ladder long before their peers, taking on partner and family responsibilities, or firmly establishing themselves in a career. After twenty-nine, a gradual shift highlights a growing need to be more carefree and independent and to express their individuality. Another turning point occurs around sixty, when they are likely to become more sensitive and responsive to their creative urges.

At any age, they need to resist the urge to withdraw into stubbornness, inflexibility and complacency. When they start to be more spontaneous and share their compassion, generosity, creativity and curiosity with others, they discover that they have the ability to lead and inspire people to follow them along the optimum path to progress, in whatever direction it takes.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with sudden change.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes it is impossible to control the outcome of events; you simply have to bend in the direction life is taking you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are supportive and hardworking individuals with a natural, easygoing rapport.

Luck Maker: Live in the Now

Lucky people have a habit of squeezing happiness out of their present circumstances. Do not waste the present focusing on the future; make sure every day is your lucky day.

At Your Best

Responsible, innovative, steady.

On the Dark Side

Complacent, authoritarian, inflexible.

Love: Dynamic but Cold

Those born on this day are dynamic, charming and rarely short of admirers, but they can be quite cold and withdrawn in matters of the heart. It is crucial that they get in touch with their own feelings and those of others, because their strong emotions need to express themselves positively in a loving, supportive relationship.

Health: Cautious

These individuals tend to have a conservative, cautious but stable approach to health. While this can sometimes block progress, their chances of living to a ripe and healthy old age are high. On the downside, tendencies to worry and overwork can lower immunity and trigger stress and mood swings. Rheumatism could become a concern with advancing age, making it important to stay as active and flexible as possible. A regular program of moderate activity is highly recommended, along with daily stretching. They also benefit from keeping mentally flexible; whatever their age, learning a new skill or language is encouraged. Their diet should be lower in salt and sugar and richer in whole grains, fruits, vegetables, oily fish, nuts and seeds. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red encourages them to be more passionate and impulsive.

Career: Born Judges

These individuals are well suited to careers in politics, law enforcement or commerce, though those who wish to use their creativity may be drawn to science, art or spirituality. Other options include management, administration, promotion, photography, writing, music and drama.

Destiny: To Advance the Greater Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more tolerant, accepting and flexible. Once they are able to go more with the flow of life, their destiny is to direct others along lines that can advance the greater good.

Power Thought

There is no greater power for me than the power of the present moment.
