Born on June 22nd

The birthday of breathless anticipation

Profile

The romantic, affectionate, and sensitive individuals born on June 22 tend to greet each new day, however ordinary, with breathless anticipation. For them every day is an optimistic new beginning where anything could happen; their imagination infuses everyday events with excitement and potential.

Whether or not they realize it, they are constantly seeking their personal nirvana, be it the perfect love affair, lifestyle, or a combination of both. Although they can be practical and will often excel in their careers, personal happiness typically takes precedence over professional success because they feel that with the love of a soul mate they can achieve virtually anything. Finding true love is therefore of paramount importance to their emotional and professional well-being.

Until age thirty they focus on security, and if they don't marry young they may prefer to stay at home or build a network of supportive friends. After thirty they usually gain confidence and perform with skill in their chosen field. In their middle years, if they have not already used the powerful energies of their birthday, they will get another opportunity to achieve success.

Although they can keep romance alive long after routine has stripped away much of its mystery, they need to look at the surface as well as the depths of the people they are attracted to. If they don't keep their feet on the ground, they may find themselves isolated in their fantasy world. However, such is their zest for life and enthusiasm for intellectual and sensory stimulation that they will not stay down for long.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with disillusionment.

The Way Forward

Disappointment is likely if you credit others with qualities they do not actually possess, so don't place unrealistic demands on yourself or others.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You are energetic and exciting individuals with a love of beauty, and this can create an intense and fabulous union.

Luck Maker: The Power Is Within You

Ultimate fulfillment can never be found in the perfect relationship or lifestyle. True fulfillment can only come from within.

At Your Best

Imaginative, sensitive, romantic.

On the Dark Side

Naive, dreamy, confused.

Love: Affectionate and Attractive

June 22 natives are often affectionate and attractive, rarely having trouble attracting potential partners. There is a danger, however, that some will remain unsatisfied because their romantic visions are too unrealistic or because the intensity of their emotions frightens off potential partners who feel they cannot live up to the ideal projected on them.

Health: Serious Thinking

Serious illnesses tend to creep up on those born on this day without anyone noticing until they have slumped into profound depression or become so stressed they simply can't function. It is important they make plenty of opportunities for rest and relaxation. They may be prone to digestive disorders, anemia, and lowered vitality. Boosting iron intake, switching to low-fat options, and getting plenty of bone-strengthening exercise such as walking and swimming will help. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help when they need time for serious thinking.

Career: Born Designers

The sensitivity of those born on this day suits them well for careers in the arts where they can live their fantasies, and they may be drawn to writing, music, or theater. Other careers that appeal include interior design, photography, filmmaking, counseling, medical or alternative health, education, social work, and law.

Destiny: To Bring Happiness to Those Around Them

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to see the world not just from their point of view but from the point of view of others. Once they are more realistic in their assessment of people and situations, their destiny is to use their imagination, sensitivity, and boundless enthusiasm for life to bring happiness to those around them and to the world at large.

Power Thought

I already have all the wisdom and power I will ever need.
