Born on September 28th

The birthday of the seducer

Profile

Other people are often drawn to the magnetic and highly seductive individuals born on September 28. Whether conventionally attractive or not, they have the ability to wrap almost anyone they want around their little finger. Many of them seek and find personal fulfillment through affairs of the heart, indulgence of the senses and the pursuit of beauty in all its forms.

They are highly imaginative and sensitive, with a strong desire to bring harmony and beauty to the world. However, they run the risk of believing that their ability to seduce others with their interesting and enlivening aura is enough to bring good fortune their way. They should understand that although charm will take them far, if they want to go all the way they also need discipline, insight and hard work.

Until age twenty-four they are likely to be concerned with relationship issues, but after age twenty-five there is an important turning point that emphasizes a need for emotional change, personal power and transformation. How they respond to the opportunities life presents them to back up their charm with substance will determine their success both personally and professionally. If they can move away from complacency, making practical decisions about how to realize their goals and supporting those decisions with hard work, they have enormous potential. If, however, the thrill of the chase becomes a dominant force, their creativity will be blocked by game playing, power struggles and procrastination.

These people will always have the ability to charm the birds off the trees, but the key to their success and happiness will never be their seductive warmth but their willpower. When they can take control of their passions and steer their energies in a clear direction, they will not only continue to captivate everyone who crosses their path but also realize the ideals of beauty and harmony that are powerfully linked to their emotional fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Tolerating boredom.

The Way Forward

Understand that boredom is not necessarily something you should avoid. Your need for constant stimulation may be holding back your personal development.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both charismatic and flirtatious, and as long as you share the spotlight this can be a very passionate combination.

Luck Maker: Stop Making Excuses

Inaction and procrastination are the enemies of luck. Start doing the things you have always wanted to, now.

At Your Best

Charming, magnetic, exciting.

On the Dark Side

Manipulative, impulsive, destructive.

Love: Master Flirts

Those born on this day are masters at the art of flirtation and seduction and will often be surrounded by admirers. In a relationship they can also be masters of manipulation, as capable of inflicting pain as they are of giving pleasure. That said, when they meet someone with whom they do not feel the need to play power games, they can be loyal and dependable partners.

Health: Healthy Libido

These individuals are often blessed with a sensual, passionate nature, but when life threatens to overwhelm them they may suffer from loss of libido. They should take this as a warning sign that something in their life is not working. Talking to a doctor to rule out medical causes and possibly a therapist to highlight emotional burdens will prove helpful, as will eating a healthy, balanced diet rich in libido-boosting fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds and oily fish. Regular mild to moderate exercise will help balance their hormones. Looking good is important for them, and if they drink or smoke they need to question why they indulge in activities that damage both their looks and their health. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with cooling shades of Blue will help them feel more in control of their emotions and their lives.

Career: Born Opera Singers

These individuals generally flourish in careers where they can indulge their passionate inclinations and inspire others at the same time, such as writing, art, acting, music or even sport. Other choices might include advertising, the media, publishing, the leisure and beauty industries, and public relations.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Passionate Inclinations

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to manage their emotions instead of having their emotions control them. Once they are able to get into the driving seat of their lives, their destiny is to entertain and inspire others with their passionate and individualistic inclinations.

Power Thought

I am powerful and inspired and enjoy getting my life in order.
