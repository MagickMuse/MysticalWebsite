Born on December 27th

The birthday of the golden heart

Profile

Those born on December 27 may give the impression of being sturdy and strong on the outside, but inside they have a heart of pure gold. Although they can be stubborn at times, they are life's givers, not takers. They also have a heroic side and will be the first to rush in with support or help when someone is in trouble.

These individuals set incredibly noble standards for themselves and are giving to the point of self-sacrifice. They pride themselves on being kind, thoughtful and compassionate, always willing to do the right thing or offer support when it is needed. Because their goodwill makes it hard for them to refuse any request, however, they may become overburdened with problems that are not their own. Their generosity and good-natured charm win them many admirers, but underneath they can be plagued by self-doubt and silent frustration. Part of this insecurity stems from feeling torn between a strong sense of personal responsibility and the need for time and space to pursue their own interests.

Until age twenty-four, they tend to have a very goal-oriented, practical approach to life. After twenty-five, a shift in emphasis presents opportunities to develop their individuality. It is important that they seize these, because only when they reconcile the desire to help others with the desire for personal fulfillment can they unlock their remarkable potential.

At first, those who live and work with them may find it unsettling to see them becoming more independent, but it is absolutely crucial that they are not deterred. They only have to make the effort to strike out on their own and pay attention to what they want to achieve to perform miracles, rise to the top of their career and secure lasting success while maintaining the respect and affection of everyone around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Saying "no."

The Way Forward

Understand that saying "no" when you are not in a position to give is saying "yes" to yourself.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You have so much to learn from and love about each other, making this a passionate and powerful combination.

Luck Maker: Receive Without Guilt

To receive makes you vulnerable, but to create luck you must be fluid, spontaneous and, above all, willing to gratefully accept help when it is offered.

At Your Best

Generous, charming, noble.

On the Dark Side

Self-sacrificing, insecure, frustrated.

Love: Give and Receive in Equal Measure

When it comes to affairs of the heart, those born on this day may be drawn to wild, unconventional and sometimes selfish characters, but they are happiest with a partner who can offer security, affection and support. They need to be careful not to become too dependent in romantic affairs, and to take as much as they give.

Health: Learn to Accept Praise Graciously

These individuals may be prone to anxiety, worry and depression, partly because others may take advantage of their giving nature and partly because of low self-esteem. They need to learn to accept praise and to place their own happiness at the top of their priorities. Their diet should be low in salt and sugar and rich in whole grains, fruits and vegetables. As for exercise, the more the better; it will keep weight down, joints flexible and self-esteem high. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red boosts confidence.

Career: Born Counselors

These multi-talented individuals are likely to make valuable contributions in whatever career they choose. They may be drawn to teaching, nursing, medicine, the caring professions, public relations, human resources, counseling, charity work, emergency services, the leisure and beauty industries, sport and social reform. Their creative side may also lead them to writing or the performing arts.

Destiny: To Be a Role Model for Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their own needs with those of others. Once they are able to take as well as give, their destiny is to show others, by example, that there is always a place in this world for compassion, kindness and understanding.

Power Thought

If I put my mind and heart into it, there is nothing I cannot do.
