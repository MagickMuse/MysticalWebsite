Born on December 28th

The birthday of the shining example

Profile

Those born on December 28 tend to impress others with their energy and clear sense of direction. They are a shining example of calmness, self-assurance and dependability, and people often turn to them for support or advice during a crisis. That support is typically given without hesitation.

The image they present to the world is one of capability and sophistication, but their competent exterior can mask an intense search for deeper and more fulfilling meaning in their lives. Their charismatic presence has both a positive and a negative effect: positive, in that they gain enormous satisfaction from helping or motivating others; negative, in that their concern for the well-being of others may cause them to neglect their own needs. Their careers or family life will often give them a way of uniting these external and internal orientations with outstanding results.

As self-assured individuals, they inspire confidence and even awe through their incredible perception, compassion and genuine desire to help others progress. They would be nearly perfect if it were not for the fact that they find it incredibly hard to deal with rejection. Instead of fighting back, they can slump into depression, uncertainty or confusion. They can also give the impression that they have all the answers, which of course is not the case, and this overconfidence can alienate even their strongest supporters.

After age twenty-four, opportunities emerge to become less concerned about image and more focused on expressing individuality. They should seize these, because once they recognize that personal fulfillment and service to others are not incompatible but highly compatible human needs, they can unlock their potential to be shining, inspirational examples to everyone around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with disappointment.

The Way Forward

Remember that everyone, however successful, makes mistakes. Start to view setbacks not as failures but as stepping stones on the road to success.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both self-assured and sophisticated individuals, and this can be a passionate and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Focus on the Positive

You tend to get what you focus on, so focus on the positive and leave negativity behind. You can put a positive spin on everything, even rejection, by acknowledging that it will help you learn and grow.

At Your Best

Inspiring, sophisticated, confident.

On the Dark Side

Overconfident, fragile, serious.

Love: Cool Head but a Warm Heart

Those born on this day are too serious to be flirts, but they will often find themselves surrounded by admirers. They tend to be attracted to sophisticated and intelligent individuals like themselves, but might fare better with someone who introduces excitement and adventure into their lives.

Health: Don't Get Stuck in the Mud

These individuals are often passionate about music, and listening to music can be a great morale booster when life knocks them back. Owning a pet, especially a dog, may help them express their playful, spontaneous side. Their diet should include as much variety as possible rather than the same meal plans over and over. Variety is also key for exercise; if they have been doing the same routine for years, cross-training with a new physical challenge is strongly advised. This will boost weight-loss efforts and encourage them to take on new challenges in other areas of life. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages spontaneity.

Career: Born Models

These individuals are often attracted to fields where they can help, guide, enlighten or delight others, and they may choose careers in the arts, media, fashion, communications or spiritual studies. Other options include teaching, writing, acting, singing, journalism, charity work, social reform and the caring professions.

Destiny: To Inform, Guide and Enlighten Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn from their mistakes instead of repeating them. Once they find a balance between their own needs and those of others, their destiny is to guide, enlighten and delight others.

Power Thought

Keeping my thinking positive means I can overcome any problems.
