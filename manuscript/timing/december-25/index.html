Born on December 25th

The birthday of the peak experience

Profile

Those born on December 25 can struggle with the more mundane aspects of life, and the main theme in their journey is the search for a state of heightened awareness where they can transcend the everyday. Others may dismiss them as unrealistic dreamers, but many secretly admire the sense of awe and wonder they bring to everything they say and do.

In both professional and personal life, they bring energy, strength of purpose and a gift for organization. Above all, they bring a willingness to push things just that little bit further than others would dare in pursuit of the peak experience they long to reach. One reason they feel the need to make their experience of life extraordinary is that they generally receive less attention on their birthday than anyone else in the year. They may therefore feel they are missing out in some way. These feelings persist throughout their lives, giving them the drive and determination to stand out and achieve ambitious aspirations.

Before age twenty-six, they are likely to have a goal-oriented, straightforward approach to achievement, but after twenty-seven and for the next thirty years, they feel a growing need to experiment with different concepts and express their individuality. Another turning point comes around fifty-seven, when greater emphasis falls on their already enhanced sensitivity and feelings.

At any age, they will always place spiritual aspirations above material ones; this does not just set them apart, it puts them way ahead of the rest. As long as these goals are not used to escape life's complications, and as long as they inject realism into their idealistic visions, they are capable not only of great happiness and fulfillment but of making lasting contributions to the greater good.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being realistic.

The Way Forward

Understand that setting goals or ideals that are unattainable is not uplifting; it is the path to disillusionment and unhappiness.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 19 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. You are both mystical, mysterious creatures, and if you keep your feet on the ground, this will be a fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Be Willing to Compromise

Lucky people are self-aware enough to know who they are and what they believe in, but they are also open-minded, constantly increasing their luck-making potential with experience, insight, feedback and information.

At Your Best

Visionary, courageous, spiritual.

On the Dark Side

Escapist, restless, sensation seeking.

Love: Higher Love

Those born on this day have a strong need for love and affection, and this may cause them to search for an idealized romance. They are happiest with someone who shares their spiritual aspirations. Although their gentle, uplifting charm can attract admirers, they should avoid flitting from one relationship to another; once committed, they need to make sure they do not put their partner on a pedestal or become overly dependent.

Health: Keep Your Feet on the Ground

These individuals can be extremely sensitive to allergies of all kinds and should steer clear of recreational drugs entirely because there is an addictive side to their personality. Cutting down on caffeine, sugar, salt, saturated fat and food additives, drinking plenty of water and increasing fresh, natural food intake are all highly recommended. Regular rather than sporadic exercise will boost self-esteem and help them feel more connected to their bodies, as they have a tendency to detach and live in their dreams. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green helps keep them grounded.

Career: Born Philanthropists

These individuals have the ability to combine practical skills with intellectual insight and may be drawn to science, business, politics or the arts, where they will demonstrate humanitarian or philanthropic inclinations. Other options include social reform, charity work, the healing professions, teaching, writing, music, astronomy, chemistry and biology. Their love of metaphysics may also inspire them to study or teach philosophy, astrology, religion and spirituality.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Idealism

The life path of those born on this day is to retain their sense of awe and wonder while keeping their feet firmly on the ground. Once they discover the intensity and joy of living in the here and now, their destiny is to inspire others with their progressive and idealistic outlook.

Power Thought

I already have all of the joy I need to feel fulfilled and truly alive.
