Born on August 23rd

The birthday of sparkling accuracy

Profile

Those born on August 23 have a great eye for detail and their approach to life is meticulous, painstaking and extremely thorough. Whatever they do, they like things to be well ordered and well presented, and they have a sparkling and creative approach that is the hallmark of everything they undertake.

A recurring theme for these individuals is attention to detail and accuracy. They like to analyze everything with scientific precision and bring structure and organization to their world. This can mean that they are sometimes overly focused on the details and can miss the bigger picture. When it comes to work, they have a flair for accuracy but they are also blessed with tremendous creative and artistic gifts. Once they learn to marry their technical skill with their creative skills, the result is true originality and sparkle.

Until age twenty-nine they are likely to be concerned with practical order and efficiency in their lives, and this is a time for them to develop their analytical skills. After age thirty, however, there is a turning point which emphasizes growing importance on relationships, creativity and harmony. It is important for them during this period not to become too perfectionist or critical, as this will dampen the creative spark that is so vital to them.

After age fifty-nine there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper awareness and personal power. These are the years when they really come into their own and their potential for success and happiness increases significantly.

Whatever age they are, once they stop feeling that everything has to be in its right place and learn to take more risks, they will discover within themselves the potential for truly inspired creativity that can touch the lives of everyone around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding being too critical of yourself and others.

The Way Forward

Understand that perfectionism is self-defeating because it leaves no room for growth or adventure.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You both have inquiring, probing minds, and this can be a stimulating and intense union.

Luck Maker: Take More Risks

Lucky people understand that in order to win you sometimes have to take a gamble. Taking calculated risks keeps things interesting and opens the door to luck.

At Your Best

Accurate, creative, meticulous.

On the Dark Side

Perfectionist, critical, obsessive.

Love: More to Life Than Perfection

Those born on this day need to be especially careful that their perfectionist tendencies do not spill over into their personal relationships, as this can be disastrous. Learning that there is more to life than perfect order will help them find a partner who brings out the best in them. Affectionate and passionate, once they find a partner who can accept them as they are, they make loyal, devoted and supportive lovers.

Health: Stress Busters

These individuals need to be careful that their tendency toward perfectionism does not lead to frustration, stress and low self-esteem. It is important for them to relax and have fun, and to stop analyzing everything. As far as diet is concerned, they should try not to be too rigid about what they eat and should allow themselves to eat for pleasure. Vigorous exercise is recommended because it will help them release any pent-up tension. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more spontaneous, and carrying a turquoise crystal will help boost their creativity and confidence.

Career: Born Editors

These individuals have a natural aptitude for science, research, editing, accountancy, law, engineering and the media. Their love of precision may also draw them to medicine, architecture or design, and their creative skills may express themselves in art, music, writing, photography or film-making.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Sparkle and Creativity

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their need for order with their need for creativity. Once they have learned to take more risks, their destiny is to inspire others with their sparkle, originality and creativity.

Power Thought

I step outside my comfort zone and welcome the new.
