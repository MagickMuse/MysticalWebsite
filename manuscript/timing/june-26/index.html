Born on June 26th

The birthday of energetic fortitude

Profile

People born on June 26 tend to have an energetic, sturdy, resilient approach to life. They stand up well to attack and want those they love to lean on them; as a result others often wholly rely on them. Warm and sensual, they have great compassion and are good at taking control of people in need of guidance. They love life's comforts and are willing to work hard to provide these for themselves and those they love.

Another trait is their prodigious energy, physical strength, and stamina. Enjoying all kinds of physical, preferably sporting activity, their energetic approach manifests in other areas of their lives as well. The area to which they devote most energy, however, will always be the people around them. They are truly empathetic individuals whose intuitive response to others' feelings arouses their urge to protect, guide, and nurture.

Whatever path they choose, they are at their best when part of a team or community. Their strong social orientation is perhaps their defining feature, but it has the potential to bring both enormous fulfillment and enormous pain, because others may perceive their advice as an attempt to control their independence. It is extremely important for them to control their well-intentioned directional tendencies before it alienates others and smothers their own emotional needs.

Curiously, when it comes to their personal life, hidden insecurities may manifest in unusual or compulsive behavior. After age twenty-six there are opportunities for them to connect with their own emotions and discover courage and confidence within. Once they achieve this, they can display their strong ideological beliefs and inspirational vision in a positive and self-assured way.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting others fend for themselves.

The Way Forward

Sometimes the best way for people to learn and grow is for them to make their own mistakes.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 19

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You share a respect for family and tradition, and you have much you can learn to love about each other.

Luck Maker: Don't Feel Guilty About Receiving

If you have problems receiving help from others, ask yourself why and how you are blocking good things from happening. Why do you always prefer to be the giver?

At Your Best

Energetic, resilient, sensual.

On the Dark Side

Over-protective, aggressive, compulsive.

Love: Warm and Caring

Committed, loyal, and steadfast, June 26 natives take their relationships very seriously. Since they are affectionate and sociable they are likely to have many friends, staying in touch with them all. They make wonderfully warm and caring lovers, but need to make sure they don't become over-protective or stifling in their compassion.

Health: Physical Stamina

Those born on this day often enjoy exercise and sports because they love to test their physical stamina. They do have a tendency to push too hard and need to guard against accidents and injuries, particularly to their chest, stomach, and rib cage. They function much better as part of a team or group. They should eat little and often to keep energy levels consistent. Any form of exercise is recommended, as long as it is not taken to extremes. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will increase their self-confidence and creative optimism, turning the spotlight away from others onto them.

Career: Born Governors

Those born on this day will often be drawn toward careers in which they can make a practical contribution to the common good, for example social work, charity work, education, economics, politics, and lecturing. They may also flourish in research, technology, the performing arts, sales, promotion, catering, psychology, and counseling.

Destiny: To Promote the Common Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be as protective and nurturing of their own emotional needs as they are of others. Once they place self-care at the top of their priorities, their destiny is to make their valuable contribution toward promoting the common good.

Power Thought

Everything in my life works better when I love and take care of myself.
