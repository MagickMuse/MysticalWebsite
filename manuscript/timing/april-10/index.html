Born on April 10th

The birthday of the shrewd hero

Profile

People born on April 10 are daring and courageous, with a bold, go-ahead spirit. In the eyes of others they have an almost heroic quality about them, being willing to take risks others would not dream of, but their daring approach is not as dangerous as it might appear. They are shrewd people who rarely make a move without first weighing up the pros and cons.

Although they are competitive, they also have a powerful sense of justice and will be the first to speak out or take action when they see unfairness. They are strongly influenced by personal contact with others, and the force of their personality can make them sought-after companions, even leaders; they should, however, watch out for an uncharacteristic need for outside approval, which can undermine all the good they have achieved.

Until the age of forty there is an emphasis on material stability and security; during this time they are likely to develop their practical skills and to lay the foundations for future success. After the age of forty-one there is a shift of emphasis toward new interests, learning, and communication.

Regardless of their age, they may find that their seemingly easy-going, can-do approach is marred by episodes of self-doubt. These episodes are caused by their inclination to put the needs of others above their own, and by the conflict between their natural modesty and their bold, competitive ambition. Part of them wants success while another part fears it, and this inner struggle can create problems. If they learn to manage the conflict between boldness and sensitivity, they have the potential to make a significant contribution to the world. They should never forget that they are at heart shrewd heroes, and heroes need to devote themselves to a worthy quest.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming your need for the approval of others.

The Way Forward

Understand that the greatest success in life comes not when you please others, but when you please yourself.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a love of adventure and risk-taking, and this creates a dynamic and exciting union.

Luck Maker: Take Your Dreams Seriously

Lucky people take their dreams seriously and try to find meaning and insight from them. They carry a notebook to jot down their feelings about their dreams as soon as they wake up.

At Your Best

Daring, courageous, capable.

On the Dark Side

Needy, reckless, conflicted.

Love: Deeply Romantic

April 10 natives can be deeply romantic and passionate, and they will often throw everything into the pursuit of love. They love socializing and have no problems attracting admirers, but once in a relationship they can become rather serious and intense. Their ideal partner is someone who shares their sense of adventure and who can also offer them the stability and encouragement they need.

Health: Your Sense of Duty Can Be Draining

People born on this day tend to put others first when it comes to matters of health; so they need to make sure they take better care of their own physical and emotional well-being. Getting enough sleep and having regular mealtimes are essential, as they tend to skip meals and push through tiredness. As far as diet is concerned, they should avoid excessive amounts of meat and dairy and eat more whole grains, fruits, and vegetables. They should not overdo it with exercise but should make sure they keep themselves in trim with activities such as swimming, cycling, and brisk walking. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will increase feelings of warmth, security, and enjoyment.

Career: Born Crusaders

These people have natural leadership abilities and may be drawn toward careers in management, politics, law, the emergency services, and the military. Their courage and conviction also suit them to careers in journalism, education, social reform, and charity work. Whatever career they choose, their originality and creativity will shine through, so the worlds of art, music, design, and theater may also appeal.

Destiny: To Lead Others into Unexplored Territory

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to manage the conflict between their boldness and sensitivity. Once they have achieved that balance, their destiny is to lead others into unexplored territory.

Power Thought

I have everything I need to be happy right now.
