Born on December 7th

The birthday of the dreamer

Profile

Those born on December 7 often feel as if there is a special purpose to their life. They dare to be different, and as highly original and creative thinkers they do indeed stand apart from the crowd. Although they have a thirst for adventure, they are first and foremost dreamers, and their minds are capable of taking them to places others simply have not the imagination to go.

They are often trendsetters who make up their own rules as they go along, and their free-thinking, pioneering style frequently startles others. Tending to be loners, they also know how to blend in when they want to, and their conversation is always colorful and interesting. The big problem, however, is that they tend to talk more than they act; if there is no follow-through, people may dismiss them as dreamers, not doers. To a certain extent this is true, as they are so creative and original they tend to get lost in their ideas.

Until age forty-four, they will often feel a need to be more practical and realistic in their approach to goals. They should pay attention to this because their tendency to flit from job to job or stick with a job to which they are clearly unsuited can cause great unhappiness. After forty-five, a turning point makes the desire to express individuality stronger than ever. If they have not found their vocation by then, they are strongly advised to seek advice about retraining. Alternatively, they may seek satisfaction outside of work; if so, they need to make sure they do not compromise too much of their spirit for financial security.

Conformity will never be for them, but they need to find what makes their heart sing. It would truly be a tragedy if the world did not benefit from their unique but truly magickal creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Finding the right career.

The Way Forward

Keep experimenting and gather as much information and advice as possible. Try also to develop outside interests; one of those may develop into a career.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both dreamers, but one is more practical than the other, keeping your intense relationship on track.

Luck Maker: Stay Focused but Flexible

Successful people know that success often results from something they least expect, so stay focused but flexible in your goal setting.

At Your Best

Creative, individual, original.

On the Dark Side

Detached, stressed, muddled.

Love: Meaningful Relationships

Those born on this day have highly romantic expectations, and the quest for a soul mate begins from an early age. Relationships have to be meaningful, and as long as they find an intelligent, idealistic partner who can keep up with their quick mind and ever-expanding search for knowledge, they can be spontaneous, passionate and loyal partners.

Health: Don't Skip Breakfast

The more these individuals can escape into the countryside the better, because nature has a calming and uplifting effect on them. If they live in the city, they should seek out local parks and make sure there are plenty of plants in their living and working spaces. Classical music is also highly therapeutic during bouts of stress and anxiety. Mixing with upbeat, positive and focused people encourages them to find a sense of purpose and direction for themselves. They should always eat breakfast, because missing it unsettles the metabolism and leads to weight gain, especially around the waist and thighs. Regular moderate to mild exercise, especially walking, is recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages warmth and spontaneity.

Career: Born Teachers

These individuals are well suited to careers in which they can act independently. Options include teaching, writing, law, politics, commerce, sales, promotion and negotiation. Their need for creative self-expression may also lead them to music, art or drama.

Destiny: To Educate, Inform and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to balance their imaginative, creative side with their need to find meaning and purpose. Once they find ways to put everything into place, their destiny is to use their imagination and creativity to educate, inform and inspire others.

Power Thought

Today I will be part of the solution, not the problem.
