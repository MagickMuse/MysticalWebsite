Born on December 1st

The birthday of outrageous charm

Profile

Those born on December 1 are extremely energetic. Unhindered by convention, they express themselves freely and delight in surprising others with their outrageous wit and seducing them with their charm. Any attempts to restrict their freedom will simply spur them along their idiosyncratic path with renewed enthusiasm and determination.

Although they will defend or promote their ideas fiercely, they are not necessarily combative or confrontational; they prefer to influence or win others over with the sheer force of their personalities. They may at times lack tact or diplomacy, but they also radiate self-confidence, and this draws others to them. Underneath their entertaining, occasionally superficial but always eyebrow-raising mask, however, they are far more deep and complex than others realize. In fact, sometimes they feel so complex that even they are not sure of their own motivations; the vibrant, funny personality they present to the outside world is simply their way of coping with the confusion.

After a rebellious childhood, around age twenty there is an important turning point lasting about thirty years. During this period there are opportunities to become more pragmatic, orderly and structured. As long as they do not neglect friends and loved ones, they should take advantage of these to focus on their career; the sooner they find professional direction and purpose the better, as work can be an important outlet for their energy and talents. After fifty-one, another important turning point highlights a growing need for independence and for sharing some of their progressive ideas with others.

Throughout their lives they should never forget that they are free spirits, but if they can learn to be a little more diplomatic, cautious and self-aware, they will not only bring pleasure, love and enlightenment to the lives of others; they will also blaze their own, highly original trail through life.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing what you want.

The Way Forward

Appreciate that acquiring self-knowledge is not only the beginning of wisdom; it is also a lifelong task.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both courageous and outspoken individuals, and there will be plenty of love and laughter in this relationship.

Luck Maker: Give to the Appreciative

Generosity guarantees you a place at the top of people's list, which is often all you need to improve your luck.

At Your Best

Energetic, generous, entertaining.

On the Dark Side

Confused, tactless, reckless.

Love: Uninhibited and Fun Loving

In their teens and twenties, those born on this day may feel a bit shy in affairs of the heart. But when they hit their thirties and beyond, they come into their physical prime as charismatic, fun-loving and uninhibited lovers. When in a close relationship they can be generous and supportive, but they need to be careful that they are not too busy to give those they love the time they deserve.

Health: Regular Check-Ups

These individuals are often vigorous and athletic children, but as they get older they can be prone to weight gain, especially around the middle. Overindulgence in food or drink can be their downfall, as can accidents and injuries caused by sport or risk-taking. Exercising caution with physical health is advised, as is scheduling regular check-ups, since they tend to ignore niggling complaints until they get serious. Fad diets should be avoided because they make it harder to lose weight long-term; a healthy, balanced diet rich in nutrients from all food groups and plenty of moderate to vigorous exercise at least five times a week is advised instead. Cognitive behavioral therapy can help them become more aware of how their thoughts influence their moods.

Career: Born Comedians

These individuals work well in any career where they can act independently and make their individual contribution. Although they can function well in a team, they may prefer to be self-employed. They are well suited to the sporting or artistic fields, as well as acting, music, film, media, advertising, comedy and entertainment.

Destiny: To Entertain and Enlighten Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be a bit more diplomatic. Once they find a cause worthy of them and improve their self-knowledge, their destiny is to bring pleasure and enlightenment to others.

Power Thought

Balancing my priorities helps me rediscover my happiness.
