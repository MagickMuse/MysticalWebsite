Born on March 16th

The birthday of equilibrium

Profile

People born on March 16 are blessed with vivid imaginations, but they also have the ability to channel their fantasies into reality. This unusual combination of dreamer and doer gives them a rare equilibrium that can take them far.

Those born on this day tend to have a calming, steady presence. They are independent and imaginative thinkers, but they also have a practical, grounded side that prevents them from drifting too far into the realm of fantasy. They combine a rich inner life with a strong desire to make their dreams happen, and this productive blend of qualities endows them with truly creative power. They make great lateral thinkers.

Around the age of thirty-four, sometimes earlier, they tend to discover or develop their unique talents, and their lives may undergo an important transformation as a result. Up until this time they may have experimented with many different fields without finding their vocation. Once they do find it, however, their strong sense of purpose, combined with their practical skills, usually assures them of success.

Although they are often gifted and multi-talented, these people tend to undervalue themselves and put others first. While their selflessness is admirable, it can prevent them from reaching their full potential. They need to learn to stand up for themselves more. Another danger for people born on this day is their tendency to become too absorbed in their own inner world and lose touch with reality or with the needs of those around them. They can spend too much time alone and neglect their need for social interaction.

Despite these challenges, when people born on this day find their focus and learn to value themselves, they possess all the creativity, talent, and energy they need to achieve remarkable things and make an outstanding contribution to the world around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming feelings of inferiority.

The Way Forward

Understand that the meek rarely inherit the earth. Self-belief is essential for happiness and success in life.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both intuitive and empathetic individuals, and this can create a deep and transformative bond.

Luck Maker: Stop Comparing Yourself to Others

Comparing yourself to others is a lose-lose situation. When you see yourself as unique, you will stop trying to match others and start focusing on fulfilling your own potential.

At Your Best

Imaginative, practical, empathetic.

On the Dark Side

Passive, self-effacing, isolated.

Love: Devoted and Thoughtful

March 16 natives make devoted and thoughtful partners. They are sensitive to the needs of their loved ones and will go out of their way to make them feel happy and cherished. They can, however, be prone to putting the needs of others before their own, and should ensure that they do not sacrifice their own happiness in the process.

Health: Come Out of Your Shell

People born on this day need to make sure they are getting enough social interaction. Their tendency to spend too much time alone or in their own heads can lead to feelings of isolation and depression. Joining a club or group is an excellent way for them to feel more connected. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat plenty of energy-boosting foods, including fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts, seeds, and whole grains, and limit their intake of sugar, caffeine, and processed foods. Regular exercise is important, and dance, team sports, or exercise classes are particularly beneficial. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will boost their confidence and encourage them to be more outgoing.

Career: Born Designers

These people have the creativity and the practical skills to excel in design, architecture, art, photography, filmmaking, and writing. Their empathetic nature may also draw them to careers in counseling, teaching, psychology, social work, and the healing professions. They may also be attracted to careers in science, technology, computing, and research.

Destiny: To Transform Their Visions into Reality

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to value themselves as much as they value others. Once they have found a healthy balance between giving and receiving, their destiny is to use their unique blend of vision and practicality to transform their dreams into reality.

Power Thought

I value myself as much as I value others.
