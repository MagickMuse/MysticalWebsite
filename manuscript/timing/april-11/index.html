Born on April 11th

The birthday of the mediator

Profile

People born on April 11 have a wonderful ability to read people and situations and to get others on their side. Their diplomatic skills are so finely tuned that they can mediate between opposing factions, bringing peace where there is conflict. These people possess great energy, natural authority, and a gift for persuasion.

Although they are highly perceptive, they can sometimes be unaware of their own feelings and react with surprise when others tell them they appear angry, upset, or frustrated. This lack of self-awareness can have a negative impact on their personal life, and they should learn to be more in tune with their emotions and to express them openly and honestly.

They have a strong social conscience and are often found fighting for the rights of others. Their natural communication and leadership skills make them highly effective in this role. They can, however, become so concerned with the problems of others that they neglect their own personal development. Between the ages of nine and thirty-nine they tend to focus on issues of stability and security; this is when they are most likely to become the spokesperson for others. After the age of forty there is a turning point that emphasizes the importance of communication, new ideas, and learning new skills.

They are natural leaders with the ability to draw others toward them. When their enormous social skills are combined with their intelligence and insight, they have a recipe for outstanding success. They have all the qualities needed to rise to the top in their chosen field, but they need to learn to balance their concern for others with their own need for personal fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not losing yourself in the lives and problems of others.

The Way Forward

Understand that you cannot save the world; looking after your own well-being is not selfish, it is essential.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a love of home and family, and this creates a nurturing and supportive union.

Luck Maker: Get to Know Yourself

The more you understand yourself, the more focused and effective you become in everything you do. Self-awareness is a powerful magnet for good fortune.

At Your Best

Perceptive, articulate, diplomatic.

On the Dark Side

Self-unaware, overinvolved, neglectful of self.

Love: Selfless

April 11 natives have a selfless quality when it comes to relationships, often putting the needs of their partner first. This makes them extremely generous and loving partners, but they need to make sure they do not lose themselves in the process. It is important for them to find a partner who appreciates their generosity but who also encourages them to develop their own interests.

Health: Do Not Neglect Your Own Needs

People born on this day are so focused on the well-being of others that they often neglect their own health. They may ignore symptoms of illness and push themselves too hard. It is essential that they schedule regular medical checkups and that they take time out for relaxation and self-care. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, well-balanced meals and avoid skipping meals when they are busy. Moderate exercise, such as walking, yoga, or swimming, is recommended to help them unwind. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color silver will encourage them to look inward and connect with their intuition.

Career: Born Diplomats

These people have exceptional communication and negotiation skills that make them natural mediators, diplomats, counselors, and politicians. They may also be drawn to careers in law, teaching, social work, and human resources. Their creativity and insight can also lead them toward careers in writing, journalism, and the arts.

Destiny: To Build Bridges Between People

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their concern for others with their own personal growth. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to build bridges between people and to promote understanding and cooperation.

Power Thought

I choose to prioritize myself and my own happiness today.
