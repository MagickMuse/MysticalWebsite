Born on November 10th

The birthday of awareness

Profile

Those born on November 10 tend to be acutely aware of their environment and the people around them. They are sharp observers of human interaction and masters at reading between the lines. What truly sets them apart is the way they combine this awareness of others with an extraordinary ability to transform, change and improve the lives of those around them.

They are keen students of human nature and this gives them the ability to detect the hidden strengths of others. They can often see things about people that others miss, and they will not hesitate to point this out in the hope of encouraging others to become the best that they can be. This ability to see the best in others is a rare and wonderful thing, but it can sometimes tip over into meddling, causing resentment and hostility.

Until age forty-one there is an emphasis on expansion and adventure, and they may be taking risks, feeling optimistic, or broadening their horizons through travel, education or philosophy. After forty-two, a turning point arrives when they become more practical, determined and focused. Whatever age they are, they will always be passionate about change and reform. When they channel their energy and ability to raise awareness positively, they can be a powerful force for progress.

The key to their success is their ability to step back and observe themselves with the same level of awareness they apply to others. Once they can see themselves more clearly, identifying and confronting their own weaknesses rather than those of others, they will discover an extraordinary ability to transform their own lives and the lives of all they meet.

Your Greatest Challenge

Seeing your own faults.

The Way Forward

Understand that those who cannot see their own faults are unlikely to grow. Self-awareness is the foundation for all personal progress.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both passionate and dramatic, and this can be an exciting and mutually inspiring relationship.

Luck Maker: Reflect Before You Advise

Before offering others your insights, take a moment to consider whether your advice would benefit from being applied to your own life first.

At Your Best

Perceptive, transforming, progressive.

On the Dark Side

Meddling, unaware, controlling.

Love: Passionate and Intense

Those born on this day are passionate and intense in relationships, seeking partners who can match their depth and energy. They are extremely loyal and devoted once committed, but they do need to guard against possessiveness and a tendency to try to change or improve their partner. The right relationship for them is one where both partners encourage each other to grow while accepting each other as they are.

Health: Practice What You Preach

These individuals tend to be good at advising others about how to improve their health, but they are not always as disciplined when it comes to their own well-being. They need to make sure they follow their own advice when it comes to eating a balanced diet, getting plenty of exercise and taking time to relax. Stress-related conditions could be a problem, and they would benefit from regular time out in nature, as well as meditation and yoga. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will boost their creativity, warmth and enjoyment of life, and carrying a Topaz crystal will encourage self-awareness and inner reflection.

Career: Born Reformers

These individuals may be drawn to careers in social work, counseling, teaching, psychology and the healing professions, where they can use their observational skills to help others. They may also excel in journalism, writing, research, law enforcement and detective work, or they may use their transformative abilities in business, the arts, music or the theater.

Destiny: To Transform and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to apply their powers of awareness to themselves. Once they understand their own motivations as clearly as they understand those of others, their destiny is to transform and inspire others with their vision of a better world.

Power Thought

I see myself as clearly as I see others.
