Born on August 13th

The birthday of the sharpshooter

Profile

Those born on August 13 are sharp, focused individuals who take aim at their goals with the precision of a sharpshooter. They have a remarkable ability to see through confusion and cut straight to the heart of any issue. Their directness and clarity of vision make them natural leaders and problem-solvers, and others rely on them for their clear-headed advice and decisive action.

Although they are often admired for their determination and efficiency, they can sometimes come across as blunt or tactless. Their single-mindedness can also make them inflexible, and they need to learn that sometimes the most effective approach involves compromise and diplomacy. Despite their tough exterior, they are often deeply sensitive and care passionately about the causes and people they believe in.

Until age thirty-nine there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to become highly skilled in their chosen field and build a solid reputation. After age forty there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a positive period when they can soften their approach and develop their diplomatic skills.

After age sixty-nine there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional and spiritual transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to combine their sharpshooter accuracy with sensitivity and diplomacy, they have the potential to hit their targets in life with extraordinary precision and to inspire others to do the same.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being more diplomatic.

The Way Forward

Understand that bluntness may get results in the short term, but diplomacy and tact build the lasting relationships that lead to long-term success.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 20 and February 28

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You are both independent and original thinkers, and this can create a progressive and stimulating partnership.

Luck Maker: Aim with Heart

Lucky people know that accuracy without heart is cold and uninspiring. When you aim your talents at goals that also serve others, you create luck for everyone.

At Your Best

Focused, direct, decisive.

On the Dark Side

Blunt, inflexible, tactless.

Love: Heart of Gold

Those born on this day may appear tough on the outside, but they have a heart of gold and are extremely loyal and protective of those they love. They need a partner who can see past their directness to the warmth and vulnerability beneath. Once committed, they are devoted, generous and fiercely protective.

Health: Aim for Balance

These individuals tend to push themselves hard and may be prone to stress, headaches and tension. It is important for them to build relaxation into their daily routine. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and avoid skipping meals when they are focused on a project. Exercise is highly recommended, especially activities that require focus and precision such as archery, golf or martial arts. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to think of the bigger picture and develop their spiritual awareness.

Career: Born Strategists

These individuals excel in careers that require precision, focus and strategic thinking. They may be drawn to the military, law enforcement, science, surgery, engineering, management, business strategy, journalism, politics or investigative work. Their creative side may also find expression in photography, film, writing or architecture.

Destiny: To Hit Their Targets and Inspire Others to Do the Same

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to temper their directness with diplomacy. Once they have developed their ability to inspire rather than intimidate, their destiny is to achieve remarkable things and motivate others to aim high.

Power Thought

I aim with precision and lead with compassion.
