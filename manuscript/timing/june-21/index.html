Born on June 21st

The birthday of rhapsody

Profile

People born on June 21 tend to be intense, exciting, and sensual individuals. Born on the longest and perhaps most magickal day of the year, they are gregarious, sociable, and relentlessly busy. They love every aspect of their lives with rarely the time to meet all their goals.

Fiercely individualistic, they detest pigeonholing of any kind and believe they can be a style icon, researcher, athlete, devoted parent, and talented artist all at the same time. Since it is almost impossible to achieve so much in one lifetime, they run the risk of driving themselves and others to exhaustion. They have terrific enthusiasm and determination to exceed, giving them the power and momentum not just to overcome obstacles but to grow stronger after surmounting them.

These highly sensual people take great delight in all the world has to offer, but their thoughts and feelings are intense and passionate too. The big danger is that they can go to extremes, losing themselves in a world of sensation or obsession; they need to learn greater self-control. Until age thirty they focus on emotional security, home, and family, and need to make sure they don't become too dictatorial and impatient. After thirty they become more creative and confident. If they can keep a sense of balance and focus, these are the years when they will realize they can have it all, just not all at once.

Blessed with natural charisma, if they can develop their introspective gifts of empathy and understanding and avoid becoming obsessive about what excites them, their capacity for original and creative thought gives them genius potential.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not becoming obsessive about your interests.

The Way Forward

Sometimes when you throw yourself into things too deeply you lose a sense of perspective, excitement, and enjoyment.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both adventurous, intelligent, and fascinating, and this can be an intense and magickal union.

Luck Maker: Minimize Addiction

An addiction is a demand that makes you feel angry or fearful when it is not met. Creating luck is about having preferences and doing what it takes to get them satisfied without getting totally attached.

At Your Best

Sensual, exciting, intense.

On the Dark Side

Excessive, dictatorial, extreme.

Love: Don't Set the Bar Too High

June 21 natives are often highly sensual and will attract many admirers. They do, however, set extremely high standards for potential partners, which can make them dictatorial and controlling. They need to stop looking for perfection because it does not exist and appreciate the qualities that make others special.

Health: Look Within

Those born on this day often take things to extremes and push themselves rather hard, so they need a more balanced and moderate approach. Addictive behavior is a concern. They would benefit enormously from meditation, cognitive therapy, or counseling, as well as more time with family and friends for healthy perspective. They should steer clear of alcohol and limit sugar, salt, and processed foods, while avoiding extreme dietary measures as eating disorders are a potential risk. Moderate to mild outdoor exercise is recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to look within for comfort and fulfillment.

Career: Born Reformers

Whatever career these people choose, they will have a need to express their creativity. They also need jobs that offer variety, travel, and human contact. Often humanitarian by nature, they may be drawn to education, counseling, healing, or social reform. Their love of knowledge may attract them to law, religion, and philosophy. Usually good with their hands, they may excel in design, and with their communication skills they may make excellent writers, journalists, advertisers, presenters, and promoters.

Destiny: To Share Their Vision and Intensity with Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to avoid excessive extremes and to cooperate with others. Once they have learned this, their destiny is to make their mark on the world by sharing their vision and their intensity with others.

Power Thought

Every moment contains an opportunity for me to feel inspired.
