Born on March 4th

The birthday of inspired aloneness

Profile

People born on March 4 tend to come across as self-contained individuals with an ingenuity that does not require outside stimulation and a creativity that is entirely self-generated. They are able to work and, if need be, live alone. It is not that they are antisocial or seek to cut themselves off from the world; it is simply that striking out alone is a dominant trend in their lives, often leading them to success.

For those born on this day, being alone is not scary — it is a liberating experience and an opportunity to concentrate and be productive. Extremely comfortable with themselves, they often feel confined or trapped by social pressure and conformity. When alone they feel free rather than isolated, and even when given the option to interact more, they will often choose to go it alone. This can come across as shyness or fear of involvement, but that misses the point about these gentle people.

Although they hate confrontation and will withdraw at signs of conflict, they are not shy or passive but rather aware that they are most productive when living and working within guidelines set by themselves. They have an innovative and ingenious mind that works best when left undisturbed and an ability to explore concepts with incredible tenacity. Between the ages of seventeen and forty-six there is an emphasis on daring new ventures and many opportunities for them to develop their assertiveness; they need to ensure others do not take all the credit.

The big danger for these people is that they indulge their natural tendency for self-involvement so much that they cut themselves off from reality and from the joys and rewards of close personal relationships. It is unfortunate when this happens, as they do love to share the results of their work with others despite their natural reserve. They also have great empathy for others, and when they choose to reveal or share their talents, they have the potential to influence, intrigue, and inspire others with their vision.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to express your feelings.

The Way Forward

Understand that if you do not say what you mean or ask for what you want, people will not understand you or be able to help you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. A relationship of opposites, you can learn much from each other about balancing inner and outer worlds.

Luck Maker: Overcome Shyness

Lucky people are not necessarily the life and soul of the party, but they know how to overcome shyness to get others on their side. Instead of thinking about yourself when you walk into a room, think about the people in the room.

At Your Best

Tenacious, independent, ingenious.

On the Dark Side

Impersonal, self-involved, shy.

Love: Give Me Space

March 4 natives really need space in a relationship and will suffer if a partner tries to control or crowd them in any way. They tend to be attracted to people who are free-spirited and self-contained like themselves. Although they may have long gaps between relationships, when they find the right person they are capable of deep and lasting love and commitment.

Health: Having a Good Time

Those born on this day need to make sure they do not cut themselves off too much from the benefits of socializing. All activities involving working or dealing with others are therefore recommended. Social forms of exercise and team sports are particularly beneficial as they can experience the joys of synergy. Having said that, it is equally important for them to have plenty of space for their own thoughts; having regular time out, rest, and relaxation, especially outside in the fresh air, is important. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to come out of themselves more.

Career: Born Magicians

These people are naturally suited to setting up and running their own business, preferably from their own home or office. They may also be attracted to writing, teaching, education, psychology, academia, research, the clergy, or computer programming. They may also be drawn to music, magick, and the arts, and can make exceptional illusionists, conductors, or DJs.

Destiny: To Educate and Inspire with Their Ingenuity

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that they have just as much right to be heard as anyone else. Once they have worked on their assertiveness, their destiny is to educate, motivate, and inspire others with their ingenuity.

Power Thought

I can always learn something from others.
