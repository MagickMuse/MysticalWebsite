Born on August 18th

The birthday of the deep heart

Profile

Those born on August 18 are among the most sensitive and emotionally deep individuals of the year. Although others may not realize it, beneath their composed and capable exterior lies a rich and complex inner world of emotions, dreams and desires. They feel things very intensely and their capacity for love and compassion runs deeper than most.

Despite their emotional depth, they are not fragile or weak. On the contrary, they are often remarkably strong and resilient, with a determination and willpower that others find inspiring. They tend to be fiercely protective of those they love and are willing to make great sacrifices for their happiness. The danger is that they may give so much of themselves that they neglect their own needs, or that they may become so emotionally intense that they push others away.

Until age thirty-four there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order, and during these years they may find it hard to express their emotional side. After age thirty-five, however, there is a turning point that stimulates them to focus more on their relationships, creativity and emotional fulfillment. This is a period when they are more likely to allow their deep feelings to surface.

After age sixty-four there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on transformation and personal power. Whatever age they are, the sooner they learn to acknowledge and express their deeper emotions rather than hiding them, the more fulfilling and rewarding their lives will become.

Your Greatest Challenge

Expressing your deepest feelings.

The Way Forward

Understand that keeping your emotions locked away does not make you stronger; true strength lies in vulnerability and emotional honesty.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both emotionally deep and intense, and this can create a passionate and transformative relationship.

Luck Maker: Open Your Heart

Lucky people understand that the more open their hearts are, the more luck and love they attract into their lives.

At Your Best

Compassionate, strong, deep.

On the Dark Side

Secretive, intense, self-sacrificing.

Love: Heart and Soul

Those born on this day love with their whole heart and soul, and when they commit to a partner it is usually for life. They can be fiercely possessive, however, and need to learn the difference between protection and control. They are at their best with a partner who values emotional honesty and is not afraid of intensity.

Health: Emotional Release

These individuals need to find healthy ways to express their emotions as suppressing them can lead to depression, anxiety and stress-related illness. Journaling, creative expression, counseling and talking to close friends are all highly recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet rich in fresh fruits, vegetables and whole grains. Regular vigorous exercise is essential for releasing pent-up emotional energy, and martial arts or boxing may appeal. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their confidence and encourage them to express their emotions more openly.

Career: Born Counselors

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in counseling, psychology, social work, medicine, healing or any profession that allows them to use their emotional depth and compassion. They may also be drawn to creative careers in writing, music, art or acting, and their determination and work ethic can bring them success in business, law or management.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Emotional Courage

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to express their deepest emotions openly. Once they have found the courage to be vulnerable, their destiny is to inspire others with their emotional depth, compassion and courage.

Power Thought

I express my feelings with courage and grace.
