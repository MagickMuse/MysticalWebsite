Born on July 13th

The birthday of action

Profile

Those born on July 13 are action-oriented individuals who believe that actions speak louder than words. They are dynamic, energetic and courageous, and have a powerful desire to make things happen. Their can-do attitude and willingness to take risks make them natural leaders.

Although their impulsive nature can sometimes lead them into difficulties, their resilience and determination usually ensure that they bounce back quickly. Between the ages of thirty-nine and sixty-nine there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to make sure their actions are guided by thought and reflection, not just impulse. After the age of sixty-nine there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Thinking before acting.

The Way Forward

Understand that a moment of reflection before action can save you hours of regret afterward. Wisdom is knowing not just what to do, but when to do it.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both energetic and action-oriented, and this can create a thrilling and dynamic partnership.

Luck Maker: Plan Your Next Move

Lucky people understand that preparation increases your chances of success. Taking time to plan your next move makes you more likely to be in the right place at the right time.

At Your Best

Dynamic, courageous, action-oriented.

On the Dark Side

Impulsive, reckless, impatient.

Love: Passionate and Direct

Those born on this day are passionate and direct in their approach to love. They are attracted to strong, independent individuals who share their zest for life. They need to be careful not to rush into relationships without considering the long-term implications.

Health: Channel Your Energy

Those born on this day have enormous energy that needs to be channeled constructively. If they do not find healthy outlets, they may become restless and irritable. Vigorous exercise is essential, especially competitive sports or martial arts. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and avoid fast food and sugary snacks. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to pause and reflect before acting.

Career: Born Entrepreneurs

These individuals are natural entrepreneurs and may excel in business, sales, marketing or any career that rewards initiative and boldness. Their courage may also draw them to careers in the military, emergency services, law enforcement or extreme sports. Their creativity may lead them to careers in the arts or entertainment.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Courage

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their impulse to act with thoughtful reflection. Once they have done that, their destiny is to inspire others with their courage, determination and remarkable ability to make things happen.

Power Thought

I act with purpose and pause with wisdom.
