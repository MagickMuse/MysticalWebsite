Born on August 20th

The birthday of thoughtful mystery

Profile

Those born on August 20 have an enigmatic quality about them that intrigues and fascinates others. They are complex characters whose looks, ideas and behavior can be at odds with what others might expect of them. They are also extremely empathetic and observant, with a gift for understanding the subtle nuances of human behavior.

Despite their perceptive and compassionate exterior, they can be surprisingly driven, secretive and intense. Their motivation is rarely materialistic; it is more often a desire to uncover the truth or to protect others from harm that spurs them on. They feel things deeply and are strongly influenced by their emotions, which they can find hard to express. Although they are passionate about the causes they believe in, their mysterious air often means that others have trouble understanding them and their motives.

Until age thirty-two there is an emphasis on order, practicality and efficiency, and during these years they may find themselves getting drawn into the details of life. After age thirty-three, however, there is a significant turning point which emphasizes an increased need for close personal relationships, creativity and harmony. After age sixty-three there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional and spiritual regeneration.

Whatever age they are, once they learn to be more open about their feelings and their motivations and use their sharp mind and creative potential to explore the deeper mysteries of life, they have the potential to discover extraordinary things about themselves and the world around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Opening up to others.

The Way Forward

Understand that by keeping others at a distance, you deny yourself the support, love and understanding you need to thrive.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both drawn to mystery and emotional depth, and this can be a transformative and passionate relationship.

Luck Maker: Let People In

Lucky people understand that true connection requires vulnerability. The more you share your authentic self with others, the more luck and opportunity you attract.

At Your Best

Enigmatic, empathetic, determined.

On the Dark Side

Secretive, intense, misunderstood.

Love: Magnetic Mystery

Those born on this day are naturally alluring and rarely lack admirers, but they can find it hard to open up fully to a partner. When they do find someone who can see past their enigmatic exterior and appreciate their depth, they are intensely loyal and devoted. They need a partner who can offer them both emotional security and intellectual stimulation.

Health: Emotional Wellness

These individuals need to pay attention to their emotional well-being as they tend to suppress their feelings, which can lead to stress, anxiety and even depression. Journaling, therapy or talking to trusted friends is highly recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and avoid using food as a substitute for emotional expression. Gentle exercise, particularly yoga, swimming and walking in nature, is highly recommended as it helps them process their feelings. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more open and warm with others.

Career: Born Investigators

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in research, investigation, psychology, counseling or detective work. Their analytical minds and creative flair also make them excellent writers, filmmakers, photographers and artists. They may also thrive in medicine, science, social work or any profession where they can use their empathy and insight to help others.

Destiny: To Use Their Insight to Illuminate the Lives of Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more open and expressive with their feelings. Once they have found the courage to share their authentic selves, their destiny is to use their remarkable insight and creativity to illuminate and enrich the lives of others.

Power Thought

I share my true self with the world and welcome connection.
