Born on August 3rd

The birthday of the heroic rescuer

Profile

Those born on August 3 have a powerful desire to protect, rescue and champion the underdog. They are brave, generous and compassionate individuals who feel compelled to step in whenever they see injustice or suffering. Their courage and willingness to fight for others make them natural heroes, and they are often the first to volunteer in a crisis.

Although their heroic instincts are admirable, they can sometimes take on too much responsibility for others, neglecting their own needs in the process. They may also have a tendency to see the world in black and white terms, dividing people into heroes and villains, which can make them judgmental and self-righteous. Despite these tendencies, their heart is always in the right place, and they are driven by a genuine desire to make the world a better, safer place.

Until age forty-nine there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to develop their leadership skills and establish a reputation as someone who can be relied upon in a crisis. After age fifty there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a positive period when they can learn to balance their desire to rescue others with their own need for personal fulfillment.

After age seventy-nine there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn that true heroism also involves taking care of themselves and allowing others to fight their own battles, they have the potential to become truly inspiring and influential leaders.

Your Greatest Challenge

Allowing others to rescue themselves.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes the most heroic thing you can do is to step back and allow others to find their own strength.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous, generous and idealistic, and this can create an exciting and mutually inspiring partnership.

Luck Maker: Save Some Energy for Yourself

Lucky people understand that you cannot pour from an empty cup. Taking care of your own needs is not selfish; it is essential for your ability to help others.

At Your Best

Brave, generous, protective.

On the Dark Side

Self-sacrificing, judgmental, overburdened.

Love: Knight in Shining Armor

Those born on this day tend to play the role of knight in shining armor in their relationships, always rushing to the rescue. While this is endearing, they need to be careful not to attract partners who are overly dependent on them. They need a partner who is strong, independent and capable of reciprocating their generosity and devotion.

Health: Rescue Yourself First

These individuals tend to put the needs of others before their own, which can lead to exhaustion, stress and burnout. It is crucial for them to make their own health a priority. Regular exercise, preferably activities that are fun and social such as team sports or dance, is recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular, balanced meals and not skip meals when they are busy helping others. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their self-esteem and remind them of their own worth.

Career: Born Protectors

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in the emergency services, the military, law enforcement, firefighting, medicine, nursing, social work, counseling, charity work, humanitarian aid or any profession that allows them to protect and help others. They may also excel in law, politics, teaching, coaching or the performing arts.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Courage and Compassion

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that taking care of themselves is just as important as taking care of others. Once they have found this balance, their destiny is to inspire others with their remarkable courage, compassion and selfless devotion.

Power Thought

I am my own hero, and I nurture myself with love.
