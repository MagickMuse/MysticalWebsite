Born on October 15th

The birthday of the provocative role model

Profile

Those born on October 15 are charismatic individuals with a gift for commanding attention and inspiring others. They are provocative in the best sense of the word, challenging others to think more deeply, act more boldly and live more authentically. Whether they realize it or not, they are role models whose words and actions have a powerful influence on those around them.

They have a strong sense of right and wrong, and are not afraid to voice their opinions, even when those opinions are unpopular. They are articulate and persuasive, and their ability to present their ideas with passion and conviction makes them natural leaders and campaigners. Others are drawn to their confidence and their willingness to stand up for what they believe in.

Despite their apparent self-assurance, they can be surprisingly sensitive to criticism. They set themselves very high standards and when they fall short they can become despondent and self-critical. They need to learn to be kinder to themselves and to accept that imperfection is not the same as failure. They also need to be aware that their provocative nature can sometimes alienate the very people they are trying to inspire.

Before age thirty-seven they are likely to be most focused on issues of personal power, intensity and emotional depth. After thirty-eight, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for optimism, adventure and freedom. Whatever age they are, they need to remember that the most effective role models are those who lead with compassion and humility as well as conviction and courage. When they can do this, their influence becomes truly transformative.

Your Greatest Challenge

Handling criticism gracefully.

The Way Forward

Understand that criticism, when constructive, is a gift. It can help you grow and improve. Learn to separate constructive feedback from personal attacks, and respond to both with grace.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 21 and June 20

Those dates fall under Gemini. You are both intellectually curious and communicative, and this can be a stimulating and lively partnership.

Luck Maker: Lead with Empathy

Lucky people understand that empathy is the foundation of influence. When people feel understood, they are far more receptive to your ideas and vision.

At Your Best

Charismatic, principled, inspiring.

On the Dark Side

Provocative, self-critical, alienating.

Love: Deep Connection

Those born on this day crave deep, meaningful connections in their romantic relationships. They are passionate and devoted partners, but they need someone who can match their intensity and who is not intimidated by their strong opinions. They also need a partner who can help them relax and not take life so seriously all the time.

Health: Balance Intensity with Lightness

These individuals can burn themselves out with their intensity. It is important for them to schedule regular periods of fun and relaxation into their busy lives. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will keep their energy levels stable. Regular exercise is essential, and activities that combine physical exertion with social interaction, such as team sports or group fitness classes, are particularly beneficial. They should also consider practices like meditation or mindfulness to help them manage their tendency toward self-criticism. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage warmth, optimism and spontaneity.

Career: Born Advocates

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in advocacy, law, politics, journalism, public speaking, teaching and social reform. Their creative talents may lead them toward careers in writing, music, art, design and entertainment. Their desire to help others may draw them to the healing and caring professions, counseling and charity work.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Live More Boldly and Authentically

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance conviction with compassion and intensity with lightness. Once they achieve this balance, their destiny is to be an inspiring role model who challenges others to think more deeply and live more authentically.

Power Thought

I inspire others best when I lead with both courage and compassion.
