Born on December 26th

The birthday of the vantage point

Profile

Those born on December 26 are never afraid to push themselves and their ideas forward, and with their nonstop energy and determination it is no surprise that they often achieve what they strive for in life. Once they reach the top, however, they frequently refuse to move anywhere else, and their energy shifts from moving forward to sustaining their vantage point.

They are a curious mixture of ambition, single-minded perseverance and a desire for security and stability. While this combination does attract considerable professional success, it also carries the risk of becoming too mechanical or unfeeling, both toward themselves and toward others. It is vital for their psychological growth that they get in touch with their emotions and those of the people around them, because they can come across at times as intense, serious and hard.

Until age twenty-five, they will often feel a need for order and structure, and practical considerations take priority. During these years the key to success is practicing the art of compromise and remembering that the feelings of other people should always be taken into account. After twenty-six, a significant turning point offers opportunities to express their individuality. After fifty-six, there is likely to be increased emphasis on emotional receptivity, imagination and spiritual awareness, and these are the years when they feel most content and fulfilled.

At any stage, they must avoid the tendency to cling to what they know or to become complacent. Once they understand that the greatest forward progress often requires taking risks, giving up some ground and exploring unfamiliar territory, they have the potential not only to make things happen on a grand scale but to inspire others as well.

Your Greatest Challenge

Admitting you made mistakes.

The Way Forward

Understand that until you acknowledge where you may have been wrong, you cannot learn or move away from your mistakes.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are both dependable partners, and as long as you stay spontaneous, this can be a loving and supportive union.

Luck Maker: Sharpen Your Awareness

Sharpen your awareness by continuing to seek out new experiences and information. The more inquisitive and alert you are, the more lucky breaks will present themselves.

At Your Best

Energetic, methodical, inspirational.

On the Dark Side

Defensive, rigid, unfeeling.

Love: Dynamic

Those born on this day are dynamic, attractive individuals, and once they set their sights on someone they tend to get them. They have a tendency to be controlling in close relationships and should learn to give others the freedom and autonomy they expect for themselves. Loyalty is extremely important to them, and any indiscretion on their partner's part is particularly hard for them to handle.

Health: A Big Shrug

These individuals can suffer from tension in their bodies, triggering aches, pains, headaches and fatigue. They should perform exercises, particularly shrugging around the shoulder area, to release pent-up energy. Their diet should include more fiber, fruits and vegetables, with less sugar, salt, caffeine, saturated fat and processed food. Moderate exercise is essential, especially activities such as dance, swimming or aerobics that encourage flexibility; yoga is also highly recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages them to be more spontaneous and warm.

Career: Born Publishers

These individuals may be drawn to technology, politics, social services or the media. Other options include big business, publishing, advertising, promotion, writing, acting and the film world. Whatever career they choose, it should offer plenty of diversity and challenge.

Destiny: To Make Radical Changes

The life path of those born on this day is to get in touch with their emotions and the feelings of others. With an open mind and an open heart, their destiny is to promote ideals that can make wide-ranging, concrete improvements in the lives of both themselves and others.

Power Thought

My loving heart knows no bounds and my flexible mind knows no boundaries.
