Born on March 7th

The birthday of extraordinary vision

Profile

Despite their ability to instantly establish rapport with anyone they meet, people born on March 7 often have an otherworldly quality about them. This is because a part of them tends to live in an abstract world of thoughts and ideals produced by their own vivid imagination.

Those born on this day possess the gift of extraordinary vision and their minds love to roam far and wide. Typically, they can manifest these ideas in practical form by analyzing situations, experiences, and people. Along the way, they will do their best to ensure they have the support of colleagues, friends, and loved ones, taking great care to make sure everyone feels involved. At work they never forget a name or personal detail about their colleagues; at home they ensure everyone can voice their opinions.

Even though they are often surrounded by friends and admirers, there is a haunting loneliness about these people. Because they dislike conflict, they tend to withdraw when there is heated discussion or criticism of their methods. In their self-imposed isolation, they may become insecure about themselves and their abilities, and secretive and suspicious of others. They should find other ways to cope when under pressure; although they are unlikely to become close to large numbers of people, they should make sure they have the love and support of family or a few good friends. Up to the age of forty-three they tend to be active and assertive, a positive sign given their natural tendency to withdraw. After forty-four there is an emphasis on greater emotional and financial stability.

Because they are so receptive to all kinds of intellectual pursuits, these people may take a while to settle on their chosen aim. However, once they are able to focus their vision and energy in a worthwhile direction, their intelligent and sensitive approach assures success. A part of them will always remain untouchable, but this does not make them appear or feel lonely — it just adds to their magick.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to assert yourself.

The Way Forward

Be as encouraging and positive as you can when trying to get a point across. Be critical of ideas, not people themselves.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You share charm, creativity, and sensitivity, and this can create an intense and loving bond.

Luck Maker: Learn to Deal with Criticism

If criticism is just, see it as an opportunity to learn and grow. If it is unjust, take it as a compliment — it often means the critic feels jealous or threatened by you.

At Your Best

Thoughtful, generous, intelligent.

On the Dark Side

Detached, isolated, secretive.

Love: Easily Hurt

Charming and generous, March 7 natives have no problem finding friends or lovers. They do, however, hate conflict, and an argument with a partner can make them feel low for days. Instead of expressing what they feel, they will simply withdraw into resentment, leaving their partner not knowing why. It is very important for them to learn to be more honest in their relationships and try to reach agreement through compromise.

Health: See More Red

People born on this day can be prone to food allergies and sensitivities, so it is important for them to pay attention to their diet and identify any trigger foods so they can limit consumption. In general their diet should include a wide variety of foods, and a multivitamin and mineral supplement is advised as they can be prone to lowered immunity during times of stress. Moderate exercise, in particular swimming, diving, and all forms of dance, is recommended, as well as enjoying plenty of fresh air through walks in the park or countryside. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to assert themselves more.

Career: Born Photographers

Whatever career these people choose — be it business, politics, or sport — they will mount a determined campaign to ensure their success. They have a strong need to express themselves and may be especially drawn to careers in film, art, music, photography, or dance, as well as teaching, social work, and the healing professions.

Destiny: To Realize Their Visionary Goals and Ideals

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more honest with others. Once they are able to assert themselves, their destiny is to realize their visionary goals and ideals.

Power Thought

Today I will ask for what I desire.
