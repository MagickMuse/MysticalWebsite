Born on August 9th

The birthday of the mentor

Profile

Those born on August 9 have a natural authority and a genuine desire to guide, protect and inspire others. They are born mentors who lead by example and have the ability to bring out the best in the people around them. Their combination of strength, wisdom and warmth makes them highly effective leaders and teachers, and others are naturally drawn to them for guidance and support.

Although they are strong and capable, they are not authoritarian. They believe in empowering others rather than controlling them, and they derive enormous satisfaction from watching others grow and flourish. They set incredibly high standards for themselves and for others, and the danger is that their expectations can sometimes be unrealistic, leading to frustration and disappointment.

Until age forty-three there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to establish themselves in a position of authority and influence. After age forty-four there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. These are the years when they are most likely to step into their role as mentor and guide.

After age seventy-three there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to temper their high expectations with patience and acceptance, they have the potential to become truly inspirational figures who leave a lasting positive impact on the lives of others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting that not everyone can meet your high standards.

The Way Forward

Understand that everyone is on their own journey and develops at their own pace; patience and acceptance are the hallmarks of a great mentor.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 20 and April 21

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both energetic, passionate and determined, and this can create a dynamic and empowering partnership.

Luck Maker: Be Patient with Growth

Lucky people understand that growth takes time. The more patient you are with yourself and others, the more extraordinary the results.

At Your Best

Inspiring, wise, protective.

On the Dark Side

Demanding, perfectionist, overbearing.

Love: The Guardian

Those born on this day are devoted, protective and incredibly loyal in love. They tend to take on the role of guardian in their relationships and need to be careful not to become too controlling. They need a partner who appreciates their strength and wisdom but who is also independent enough to maintain their own identity.

Health: Lead by Example

These individuals tend to be conscious of the importance of leading a healthy lifestyle and can be excellent role models. They need to make sure, however, that they do not become too rigid in their health routines. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet and try new foods and cuisines regularly. Regular exercise is important, and they may enjoy activities that combine physical fitness with mental discipline, such as martial arts, rock climbing or tennis. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more flexible, spontaneous and fun.

Career: Born Mentors

These individuals excel in careers that allow them to guide, teach and inspire others. They may be drawn to teaching, coaching, training, counseling, management, military leadership, sports coaching, medicine, social work or any profession that involves mentoring. They may also succeed in law, politics, writing, business or the performing arts.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Reach Their Full Potential

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their high expectations with patience and acceptance. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to mentor, guide and inspire others to reach their full potential.

Power Thought

I empower others by believing in their potential.
