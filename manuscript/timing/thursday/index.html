Lunar Magick

In Sex Magick

In the witchcraft tradition of this grimoire source, Thursday is the preferred day for sex magick and lust spells under the rule of Cernunnos. The favored working hours on Thursday are 8AM, 3PM, and 10PM. The altar is decked with animal horns, pinecones, and evergreens, and the instruments of power are the athame and chalice within a triangle of manifestation.

Thursday belongs to Jupiter, the planet of expansion, making it a powerful day for abundance, new opportunities, luck, increase, and favorable legal outcomes. Combining Thursday with a waxing or full moon layers Jupiter's expansive energy with lunar amplification for especially potent prosperity and growth workings.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.
