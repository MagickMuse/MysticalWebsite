Born on February 20th

The birthday of the perceptive charmer

Profile

People born on February 20 are often thoughtful and receptive, with the ability to immediately tune into the moods of those around them and adjust their reactions in an instant. Highly ambitious, they are sure to stand out in their career, at home, or on the social scene. They have an appealing personality and an easy charm, but it is impossible to dismiss them as superficial, because behind their looks and charm there is always great intelligence.

February 20 natives have a great deal of compassion, instinctively treating anyone — regardless of background or social status — with warmth and understanding. In some cases they can become over-sensitive and impressionable, unable to separate their own emotions from those of others. This is because they identify so deeply with other people's viewpoints that they risk losing their own perspective in the process. It is vitally important for them to learn to protect themselves from over-identification. Before the age of thirty this tendency to merge with others is especially pronounced, but after thirty they become more assertive, confident, and self-protective.

There is a danger, once they become more aware of their ability to instinctively relate to others, that they could misuse it. It is important for them to learn not to compromise themselves or take advantage of others in single-minded pursuit of their goals.

Those born on this day who remain true to their principles and who learn to make their receptivity work for rather than against them have remarkable potential to make a real difference. They are rarely content to sit on the sidelines and desperately want to make their mark. Although they have all the drive, intelligence, and charisma needed to climb to the top, what they do not often realize is that simply being themselves already makes a huge difference — because when they are around, people just feel better about themselves.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to say no.

The Way Forward

Understand that only after you give to yourself can you truly give to others. If you neglect yourself, you cannot be a real help to anyone.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You are both highly sensitive and intuitive, and this can create an exceptionally close and loving bond.

Luck Maker: Treat Yourself

Try to devote at least one day a week to something you truly want: a book, a film, a haircut. Make sure it lifts your spirits, because the better you feel, the greater your chances of attracting luck.

At Your Best

Intelligent, appealing, intuitive.

On the Dark Side

Indecisive, hypersensitive, impressionable.

Love: Little Flower

February 20 natives can be extremely sensitive in affairs of the heart and need a partner who understands this. Every detail matters to them, and a missed phone call or a few misplaced words can send them into a downward spiral. When they do fall in love, they make understanding and passionate lovers. They can tend to put their partner on a pedestal, and it is important for them to remember that everyone has faults.

Health: Learn to Say No

It is important for those born on this day to avoid becoming drained, anxious, stressed, depressed, or burned out from giving too much to others or absorbing their emotions. They should find ways to avoid turning to drink, drugs, and comfort eating as an escape, and would benefit enormously from substituting relaxation techniques such as meditation, natural remedies, and herbal teas for those habits. Moderate to vigorous exercise several times a week and plenty of sleep are recommended for these sensitive souls. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will build their self-confidence and encourage optimism.

Career: Born Healers and Givers

The giving personality of February 20 natives may draw them toward careers in medicine, entertainment, music, or the arts, where they can give to an audience. Being receptive and versatile, they tend to thrive in whatever career they choose, but music, dancing, health and healing work, and all kinds of public relations roles have particular appeal.

Destiny: To Inspire Others by Giving

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to set boundaries. Once they become more self-aware and assertive, their destiny is to inspire and influence others with their giving presence.

Power Thought

I am in charge of all aspects of my life.
