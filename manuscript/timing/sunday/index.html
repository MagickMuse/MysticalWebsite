Best Day for Magick

Sunday is the best day of the week to perform magick for personal empowerment, success, generosity, and luck.

Lunar Magick

Sunday is aligned with the sun and supports workings around success, creativity, positivity, health, and general blessings. Combining Sunday with a waxing or full moon creates a powerful dual radiance — solar confidence meets lunar amplification. Any intention you want to bless benefits from Sunday timing.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.
