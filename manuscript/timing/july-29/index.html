Born on July 29th

The birthday of the partisan leader

Profile

Those born on July 29 tend to be energetic and positive individuals dedicated to fostering community awareness. Their ambitions are directed less toward achieving their own success and more toward benefiting the social group to which they belong, be it their family, local community, workplace, country or the world as a whole.

Within their own social group, they tend to gravitate toward leadership positions and, because they are strong-willed with clear-cut goals and the organizational talents to motivate others, they can be inspirational. Their willingness to nurture and assume responsibility for those around them generally earns them affection, respect and gratitude.

It is important for them to make time for themselves and for their own psychological development, especially between the ages of twenty-four and fifty-four, when their mental focus becomes more analytical and practical. These are the years when they are likely to make outstanding contributions to their community, but they need to make sure they do not regard their own personal needs as less important.

Your Greatest Challenge

Using your own judgment.

The Way Forward

Understand that subordinating your individuality to the needs of the group often leads to emotional problems and resentment.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You have much to learn from each other, and this can create a fulfilling and intense union.

Luck Maker: Let Go of Your Past

Hereditary patterns, cultural codes and social beliefs can be limiting. Lucky people understand they are the only ones whose power can create the life they deserve.

At Your Best

Generous, loyal, cooperative.

On the Dark Side

Conformist, restricted, generalizing.

Love: Selfless Devotion

Those born on this day are often selflessly devoted to those they love, but they need to be careful that they do not sacrifice their own identity in the process. They are attracted to strong-willed, independent individuals but should avoid becoming overly dependent or overly dominant in relationships.

Health: Learning to Say No

Those born on this day are so dedicated to their community that they may neglect their own health. It is important for them to make sure they take time out for rest and relaxation and learn to say no sometimes. As far as diet is concerned, they need to make sure they do not overindulge; moderation and balance are key. Regular exercise is recommended to help manage stress, and activities such as walking, swimming and yoga are particularly beneficial. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to focus on their own needs.

Career: Born Community Leaders

These individuals are natural leaders and may be drawn to politics, social work, community service, charity or management positions. They may also excel in teaching, counseling, law, journalism and the media. Their creativity may lead them to careers in art, music, writing or design.

Destiny: To Inspire Others Through Leadership

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance the needs of the group with their own personal development. Once they have done that, their destiny is to use their considerable leadership skills to inspire and benefit others.

Power Thought

I listen to myself first and then I can truly listen to others.
