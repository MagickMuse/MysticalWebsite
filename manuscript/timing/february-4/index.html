Born on February 4th

The birthday of the bedazzler

Profile

People born on February 4 often try to fit in, but no matter how hard they try they will always stand out. They have a gift for bedazzling others with original thoughts and flashes of brilliance. Their methods may not always be orthodox, yet their thought processes are consistently original and their problem-solving techniques always effective.

Although February 4 natives are frequently admired for their sincerity, discipline, and tireless work ethic, the logic behind their thoughts and actions is often incomprehensible, not just to others but sometimes to themselves. Their speed of thought and energy can exhaust those around them, and people may respond with bewilderment rather than admiration. Feeling like a square peg in a round hole can make them insecure and confused, and they may try to fit in by toning themselves down. This is a mistake, because they should never limit their greatest strength: their originality.

February 4 people are happiest and most bedazzling when they allow themselves to simply be who they are. Because so much is constantly going on inside their heads, they may pay little attention to their emotions; analyzing their feelings does not come naturally. As a result they can be incredibly harsh on themselves, expecting more from themselves than anyone else ever would. They can also be impatient and impulsive without considering the impact of their actions on others. Fortunately, between the ages of sixteen and forty-five there are opportunities to become more emotionally self-aware, and after forty-five a significant turning point arrives when they grow bolder, more self-accepting, and assertive.

Once those born on this day understand that others will respect and admire them far more when they are authentic, they have the potential to bring about real innovation in both their personal and professional lives.

Your Greatest Challenge

Understanding the feeling that you are different.

The Way Forward

Understand that everyone is unique and gifted in their own way; you just happen to be a little more unique than most.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You share a love of adventure and boundary-breaking, and this creates an inspiring, exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: Be True to Yourself

Lucky people never try to be something they are not. They understand that the only path to true fulfillment and the respect of others is to open up and let people know the real you.

At Your Best

Imaginative, unconventional, sincere.

On the Dark Side

Uncommunicative, confused, unstable.

Love: Explorers

February 4 natives may be difficult to understand, but in matters of the heart they are original, adventurous, and exciting. They love exploring every dimension of a relationship, and their partner will experience deep intimacy with them. They are attracted to courageous, strong-willed people; timid and unadventurous types do not usually catch their eye, unless smoldering passions lurk beneath the surface.

Health: New Does Not Always Mean Better

People born on this day are drawn to innovation, and most alternative medicine and holistic health practices will probably appeal to them. They need, however, to distinguish credible approaches from quackery. Their love of novelty also applies to diet and exercise; they are typically the first to jump on a trend, only to drop it weeks later for something new. Although they have incredible energy, they are prone to neglecting food and sleep when caught up in a mental adventure. It is important for them to remember that sometimes traditional health advice — eat little and often, get enough sleep — is the wisest course. Meditating on, wearing, or surrounding themselves with the colors rose or Green will help create balance and harmony.

Career: Born Innovators

The originality of these people enables them to succeed in a wide variety of careers. They may be drawn toward cutting-edge science and technology, or toward the stage or politics. They may also specialize as therapists, psychologists, counselors, or natural-health practitioners. Their humanitarian focus could draw them toward charity work or social reform, and they also make fantastic artists, photographers, designers, architects, sculptors, and painters — all excellent outlets for their unusual perspective on life.

Destiny: To Amaze Others with Their Original Approach

The life path of those born on this day is to celebrate rather than curb their originality. Once they are able to do that, their destiny is to amaze everyone around them with their honest, direct, and brilliantly original approach to life.

Power Thought

I am pleased to be myself.
