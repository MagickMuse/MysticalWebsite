Born on June 6th

The birthday of anticipation

Profile

Whenever people born on June 6 enter the room there is a sense of excitement and anticipation. They know how to make things happen, and others instinctively sense this. They have no problem communicating their progressive ideals and will work steadfastly to make them a reality, often gaining a following with their commitment to improving lives.

Although they are movers and shakers, they can take their ideals to an extreme, which can manifest in unusual or dangerous behavior. Sometimes their dreams are so far-reaching that others may find them opaque. Learning to express themselves with greater simplicity will help get their point across.

Between ages fifteen and forty-five they are likely to be more restrained and security conscious, with a strong focus on family and home. After forty-six there is an increased need for self-expression and leadership. It is important that they understand how their actions serve as a role model and that a more balanced approach will help others relate to them.

Once they find an audience they can connect with, they more than live up to the anticipation their progressive visions have created. The relief of finally being understood encourages them to express their caring nature and their revolutionary determination to change the world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding feeling misunderstood by others.

The Way Forward

Put yourself in your audience's shoes and tailor the presentation of your vision to their interests.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You share a passion for communication, adventure, and commitment, and this can create a close and happy union.

Luck Maker: Be Flexible

If you are flexible, life can be more satisfying and enjoyable.

At Your Best

Idealistic, visionary, artistic.

On the Dark Side

Misunderstood, extreme, detached.

Love: Born to Love

Love and friendship come easily to June 6 natives. Friends may sometimes find them stubborn, but they more than make up for it with their compassion, vivaciousness, and madcap humor. Their chosen partner will share many adventures with them but also be hardworking and reliable.

Health: Going to Extremes

The greatest threat to their health is themselves. They often take ideas to extreme lengths, putting themselves in danger through excessive overwork or extreme behavior. A healthy, varied diet will keep their moods balanced and active minds well nourished. Moderate exercise such as brisk walking or swimming is recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage them to feel more rational and in control.

Career: Born Event Organizers

These people are likely to excel in careers where their unusual ideas can flourish: design, art, writing, music, drama, dance, advertising, sales, journalism, education, and entertainment. They may also make great wedding or party planners and, if they develop rational inquiry skills, scientists and technologists.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Independence of Thought

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the importance of compromise. Once they strike a balance between their minds and hearts, their destiny is to influence and inspire others with their independence of thought.

Power Thought

Every time I sit in silence my life energy is recharged.
