Born on July 30th

The birthday of the robust explorer

Profile

Those born on July 30 tend to be practical and down-to-earth individuals. They set ambitious material goals for themselves and love exploring all aspects of the physical world. Sensual and robust, they are often comfortable in their bodies, and their forceful, self-confident manner usually takes them to the top in their careers.

Although friends and family will often take second place to material goals, their relationships are conducted with steady equanimity and a concern for the well-being of loved ones. They are fair, ethical and almost always true to their word. One of the reasons they devote so much energy to increasing their earning power is that they enjoy being able to offer material support to those close to them.

Despite their reliability and generosity, their ambitions tend to exclude recognition of the importance of emotional and spiritual fulfillment. Between the ages of twenty-three and fifty-three there is an emphasis on practicality, and it is crucial that they try to see beyond the material world. After age fifty-four there is a turning point highlighting their growing need for intimate relationships, creativity and harmony.

Your Greatest Challenge

Seeing beyond the material.

The Way Forward

Understand that focusing on material satisfaction without a spiritual dimension is a recipe for frustration, however rich or successful you are.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. As long as you give each other plenty of space and loyalty this relationship can be dynamic and exciting.

Luck Maker: Take Care of Your Soul

Lucky people understand that when they forget to pay attention to their soul, they become nervous, stressed and afraid, blocking the possibility of luck.

At Your Best

Generous, sensual, ambitious.

On the Dark Side

Materialistic, mundane, repressed.

Love: First Impressions

Those born on this day tend to be attracted to people based on physical appearance and status, but they will find that lasting relationships are only possible when they seek partners who share their values and visions. They can be incredibly loving and giving when they are in a committed relationship, but need to avoid jealousy and possessiveness.

Health: Learning to Relax

Those born on this day have enormous energy and stamina but they need to learn the importance of relaxation if they are to avoid stress-related health issues. A regular exercise routine will help them work off excess energy, and more gentle pursuits such as yoga and meditation will also help balance their tendency to work too hard. As far as diet is concerned, they need to make sure they eat a healthy, balanced diet and do not skip meals to fit in more work. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to think about higher things.

Career: Born Entrepreneurs

These individuals are well suited to commercial ventures and business. They may also excel in banking, real estate, the stock market, politics and public service. Their love of the physical world and beautiful objects may lead them to careers in art dealing, interior design, architecture or the beauty and leisure industries.

Destiny: To Benefit Others with Their Practical Contributions

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their material ambitions with their emotional and spiritual needs. Once they are able to do that, their destiny is to use their considerable practical and leadership abilities to benefit others.

Power Thought

Peace is just a thought away.
