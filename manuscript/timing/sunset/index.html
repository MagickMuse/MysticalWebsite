Magickal Properties

Sunset takes place when the sun drops below the horizon. Darkness and chill come quickly after a winter sunset and are followed by a short dusk (also called twilight). The sun's energies fade rapidly, giving way to a clear, crisp night sky.

This liminal time—standing between day and night—is deeply magickal.

Best Used For

  • Working with charms and spells
  • Appealing to or petitioning deities
  • Liminal workings
  • Transition rituals

Notes

Work quickly: at Midwinter, this in-between time vanishes in minutes.
