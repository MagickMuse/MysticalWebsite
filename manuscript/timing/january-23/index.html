Born on January 23rd

The birthday of the dissenter

Profile

Those born on January 23 are natural dissenters. They dislike, and often outright refuse, to take orders or even advice from others, preferring to live by their own rules and devote themselves to their own ideals. Although this approach carries risks, their courageous, buoyant character more often than not ensures they end up making the rules rather than following them.

Rarely motivated by financial reward alone, January 23 natives are idealists who desire a richly rewarding life on their own terms. This quality, combined with original thinking and a natural sense of style, makes them stand out from the crowd in the best possible way. They are truly inspirational figures.

Despite their can-do attitude and magnetic charisma, people born on this day never feel entirely worthy of the admiration they attract. While this adds to their charm, it can also hold them back. Once they learn to believe in themselves, however, nothing can stop them from achieving their dreams.

With their splendid disregard for convention and their highly intellectual, original approach to life, January 23 people get along with almost everyone they meet, though people driven by purely materialistic motives present a challenge. Those who flaunt their wealth or try to impress others through social climbing particularly repulse them, because integrity and moral strength are the ideals by which they live.

Aware of the limits of the physical body, those born on this day prefer to live an intellectual life. This can occasionally make those closest to them feel excluded, and it is important that they cultivate a fully integrated personality capable of offering others genuine warmth and emotional understanding. Typically around the age of twenty-eight, they become more emotionally receptive and attuned to the needs of those around them.

If those born on January 23 can ensure that their fascination with the abstract does not overshadow their personal relationships, they have every chance of becoming rebels not just with a cause but with ideals that truly influence the world around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting go of insecurity about yourself.

The Way Forward

Make every thought and action of the day one that supports and encourages you. Nurture yourself for a single day and the habit will grow.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. You share an inquisitive approach to life and keen mental alertness, and this can take you both on a voyage of discovery for body, mind, and soul.

Luck Maker: Allow Others to Give to You

Good fortune is always knocking, but it will not come in unless you open the door and are willing to receive it.

At Your Best

Principled, independent, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Isolated, rebellious, troubled.

Love: Marriage of Minds

Those born on January 23 absolutely need a partner who can challenge them intellectually, as they love to talk about almost anything. They crave the freedom to be themselves but also the stability of a loyal, secure relationship. They should be careful that their independent, self-reliant attitude does not lead partners to believe they do not need anyone. In truth, they need a close relationship far more than they would ever admit.

Health: Headstrong

January 23 natives tend to distrust doctors and seek medical advice only as a last resort. They like to feel they are the experts on their own health and hold strong opinions about diet and exercise. Because of this, they can swing between extreme health fanaticism and total indifference. It is important that they learn to seek professional guidance when appropriate, because while they are usually the best judge of what works for them, sometimes they are not. Reading, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage open-mindedness and the ability to embrace change with trust and optimism.

Career: Born Academics

These people are naturally drawn to intellectual pursuits and thrive in academic or research environments. Their analytical mind also marks them as potential scientists, though any career that keeps them mentally stimulated will appeal. Their practical side may lean toward business or market research, their idealistic side toward charity work, and their rebellious side toward entrepreneurship. Whatever path they choose, their originality will find a way to express itself in a unique and creative fashion.

Destiny: To Bring Fresh New Insights into the World

The life path of those born on this day is to keep their feet firmly on the ground without sacrificing their rebellious streak or individuality. Their destiny is to bring fresh insights into the world and encourage others to see the things they normally take for granted in an entirely new light.

Power Thought

Today I will share my dreams with others.
