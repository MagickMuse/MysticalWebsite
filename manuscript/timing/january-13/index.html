Born on January 13th

The birthday of progression

Profile

Progression is the defining word for those born on January 13. They never stand still, always pushing forward regardless of their circumstances or starting point. Their ability to overcome obstacles and make even the toughest transitions look effortless gives them a natural charisma.

People who make life look easy are universally admired, and those born on this day possess not only the ability to succeed but also the composure to stay calm when everyone around them is losing their nerve. When setbacks strike, they pick themselves up, learn from their errors, and do whatever it takes to reach their goals.

January 13 natives have no trouble leaving the past behind. They understand the importance of letting go in order to grow and move beyond limitations. They take particular pleasure in launching new projects and ideas, working steadily and with discipline until they achieve what they set out to do. Although their imagination and intelligence open doors in many fields, the arena of social and humanitarian reform holds special appeal. They feel disappointment and disillusion at times, but typically after the age of thirty-eight, and often sooner, they discover the importance of making their mind work for them rather than against them.

It is impossible for those born on January 13 to hold back. They see no point in doing anything unless they can give it one hundred per cent of their attention and energy. If others lack commitment or focus, they will say so. They progress faster when they accept that not everyone shares their drive, and that the price of high ideals sometimes means standing alone. Taking time to relax helps them discover whether they have been pushing themselves too hard or drifting too far from the world they are so determined to improve.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing how to cope with anger or disappointment.

The Way Forward

Understand that painful feelings are only released when they are faced. The only way out is through.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You share a broad outlook and a passion for accomplishment, and this can create an imaginative, exciting union.

Luck Maker: Have Faith

Unwavering self-belief when the going gets tough generates the ability and enthusiasm you need to succeed.

At Your Best

High achiever, expert, revolutionary.

On the Dark Side

Stubborn, rebellious, bossy.

Love: Charismatic Flirts

January 13 natives tend to be drawn to partners from whom they can learn or who sit a step above them socially. They need to respect and admire their partner and take deep pride in that person's accomplishments. Until they find someone to whom they feel ready to commit, their charismatic personality naturally attracts admirers and can lead to an erratic love life with numerous partners in succession.

Health: Healthy Is Better Than Perfect

Those born on this day tend to look after their health early, recognizing that a healthy body leads to a healthy mind. They need to guard against becoming too obsessive, however, worrying about every ache and pain. Exercise is important for flexibility and tone, but they should avoid overworking themselves in pursuit of physical perfection. Spending more time in nature, reading, meditating, or surrounding themselves with shades of Green will keep them grounded yet flexible enough to change course when necessary.

Career: Born Advisors

Their ability to stay calm in a crisis suits them perfectly for careers in medicine, the armed forces, personal relations, and emergency services. Education may also attract them, particularly the fields of philosophy and psychology. Their commitment to making the world a better place may draw them toward humanitarian causes. Imaginative and creative enough to work alone, they are also comfortable in advisory or specialist roles.

Destiny: To Make the World a More Harmonious Place

The life path of those born on this day is to rise above adversity and help others do the same. Once they have found the courage to stand alone and learned to respect differences as well as similarities in others, their destiny is to bring people together and settle disputes, making the world a more harmonious place.

Power Thought

I can and will reach my full potential.
