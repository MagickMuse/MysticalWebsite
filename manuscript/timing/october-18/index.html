Born on October 18th

The birthday of vulnerable expectation

Profile

Those born on October 18 tend to have high expectations of both themselves and others, and it is precisely this combination of high standards and vulnerability that defines them. They are driven, ambitious and determined, but there is a fragility about them that makes others want to protect them. This is because, despite their many talents and accomplishments, they always seem to be seeking some elusive goal that remains just beyond their reach.

They expect the best from life and they often get it, but there is always a nagging feeling that something is missing or that what they have achieved is not quite enough. This dissatisfaction can drive them to accomplish great things, but it can also lead to feelings of frustration and disappointment. Learning to appreciate what they have rather than constantly striving for more is one of their biggest challenges.

Other people are drawn to their intensity and their vulnerability, and they have a magnetic quality that makes them extremely attractive. They are passionate and giving in their relationships, but their high expectations can sometimes cause friction with partners who feel they can never quite measure up. They need to learn to be more accepting and forgiving, both of themselves and of others.

Before age thirty-three they are likely to be most concerned with issues of personal power, emotional intensity and transformation. After thirty-four, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for freedom and expansion. Whatever age they are, they need to learn that perfection is an illusion and that the beauty of life lies in its imperfections. When they can embrace this truth, they will finally find the contentment and peace they have been searching for.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting imperfection.

The Way Forward

Understand that perfection is not a destination but a mirage. The real beauty and meaning of life is found in its messy, unpredictable, imperfect reality.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both passionate and intense, and this can be an exciting and dynamic relationship.

Luck Maker: Lower Your Expectations

When you expect less, you appreciate more. And when you appreciate what you have, you create the positive energy that attracts even more good things into your life.

At Your Best

Driven, magnetic, passionate.

On the Dark Side

Dissatisfied, demanding, fragile.

Love: The Romantic Ideal

Those born on this day are passionate and romantic, and they have very high expectations of their partners. They are looking for the ideal relationship and can become disillusioned when reality does not match their fantasy. They need to learn that love is not about finding the perfect partner but about building a real, honest and imperfect partnership with someone they respect and care for.

Health: The Power of Gratitude

These individuals are prone to stress and anxiety, particularly when their expectations are not met. Practicing gratitude on a daily basis, whether through journaling or meditation, can have a profoundly positive effect on their mental and emotional health. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and omega-3 fatty acids will support their emotional well-being, and regular exercise, particularly in the fresh air, will help them manage stress and boost their mood. They should also make sure they get plenty of quality sleep. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage balance and acceptance.

Career: Born Performers

These individuals are drawn to careers that allow them to express their passion and creativity, such as acting, music, art, writing and design. Their ambition and determination may also lead them toward careers in business, law, politics and management. Their desire to help others may draw them toward the healing and caring professions, counseling and social work.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Passion and Vulnerability

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to appreciate the beauty of imperfection. Once they can accept themselves and others as they are, their destiny is to inspire others with their unique combination of passion, drive and vulnerability.

Power Thought

I am grateful for what I have and excited about what is to come.
