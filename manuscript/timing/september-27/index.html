Born on September 27th

The birthday of paradox

Profile

Although those born on September 27 often fail to recognize it, they have the ability to make a lasting and positive impression on everyone they meet. This is because they are extremely versatile and sensitive to the feelings of others, and love nothing more than to bring harmony to any situation. Paradoxically, however, underneath their sociable and relatively normal persona they may be riddled with doubts, insecurities, contradictions and hidden fears. Learning to develop faith in their abilities, which are considerable, is crucial to their success and happiness.

These people are far deeper and more complex than anyone, themselves included, realizes. This may be due to the fact that they set very high standards for themselves and that professional success tends to take priority over personal fulfillment. As a result they may find themselves mixing with groups or working in careers that are wholly unsuitable for them, although few would notice their discomfort. Until they get in touch with their feelings and find a path that is truly fulfilling, nagging feelings of depression and despondency will haunt them.

Before age twenty-six they are likely preoccupied with fine-tuning their people skills and carving out career success. After age twenty-seven, emphasis shifts to emotional change and transformation, and these are the years when they need to be alert to the way their career choices shape their character. As long as they believe that professional or material success is the only option, happiness will be elusive. However, once they understand that their hidden feelings and insecurities are not enemies but teachers leading them toward a fuller awareness of their potential, there is nothing these determined, multi-talented and vigorous individuals cannot achieve.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being true to yourself.

The Way Forward

Understand that until you are true to who you are, life will forever feel confusing and overwhelming.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are very different characters but at the same time beautifully complementary.

Luck Maker: Visualize What You Desire

Visualize with positive expectation the way you want to be, and your behavior will catch up with the mental image of yourself, attracting good luck in the process.

At Your Best

Charismatic, ambitious, successful.

On the Dark Side

Conflicted, insecure, frustrated.

Love: Injection of Self-Confidence

Although they are charismatic and attractive, when it comes to affairs of the heart those born on this day may lack confidence and self-belief. Until they gain self-confidence, their chances of success in relationships will be limited. They are attracted to powerful and determined individuals who know what they want, and although they can learn much from them, they need to be careful not to become dependent.

Health: Pump Iron

It has been proven many times that exercise boosts not only immunity and weight management but also confidence. More recent research has shown that lifting weights or muscle-toning exercises also have a positive effect on mood. Regular toning or weight training, two or three sessions a week maximum, is highly recommended for those born on this day, so long as they do not take it to extremes. They also need to complement this with plenty of aerobic exercise such as walking or running. They should take care of their liver, kidneys and reproductive organs, and a healthy, balanced diet rich in nutrients will support that. Learning techniques from cognitive therapy and hypnotherapy will help them challenge negative thinking. Yellow is the color of optimism and self-confidence, and meditating on it will encourage them to have more faith in themselves.

Career: Born Freelancers

These individuals may be drawn toward politics, social and legal campaigning, or freelance artistic, technical or design projects. Other career choices include management, self-employment, entertainment, education, science, writing, music and the caring professions.

Destiny: To Point the Way Toward Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to believe in themselves and their talents. Once they have accepted that to be human is to be complex, their destiny is to point the way toward progress through the most unlikely of approaches.

Power Thought

It is safe for me to look within.
