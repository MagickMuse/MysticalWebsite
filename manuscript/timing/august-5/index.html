Born on August 5th

The birthday of steely determination

Profile

Those born on August 5 are blessed with remarkable determination and willpower. Once they set their mind to something, they pursue it with an intensity and focus that is hard to match. They are natural fighters who never give up, even when the odds are stacked against them, and their steely resolve often carries them through difficulties that would defeat others.

Although they are tough and tenacious, they are also highly intelligent and articulate, with a gift for communication that allows them to express their ideas clearly and persuasively. They are often passionate advocates for causes they believe in, and their combination of determination and eloquence makes them powerful forces for change. The danger, however, is that their single-mindedness can sometimes make them inflexible or confrontational.

Until age forty-seven there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to develop their communication skills and establish a solid professional reputation. After age forty-eight there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a positive period when they can learn to balance their determination with diplomacy.

After age seventy-seven there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to combine their steely determination with flexibility, sensitivity and an openness to new ideas, they have the potential to achieve extraordinary things and to inspire others with their courage and conviction.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being more flexible in your approach.

The Way Forward

Understand that flexibility is not weakness; the strongest trees are those that can bend in the wind without breaking.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 22 and June 21

Those dates fall under Gemini. You are both intelligent and articulate, and this can create a stimulating and communicative partnership.

Luck Maker: Stay Open to New Ideas

Lucky people understand that clinging to one way of doing things limits their potential. Staying open to new ideas and approaches keeps the door to luck wide open.

At Your Best

Determined, articulate, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Inflexible, confrontational, stubborn.

Love: Passionate and Intense

Those born on this day bring their characteristic intensity to their relationships. They are passionate, devoted and fiercely loyal partners who will fight for the people they love. They need a partner who is strong enough to match their intensity but gentle enough to soften their edges. Open communication is essential in their relationships.

Health: Channel Your Energy

These individuals have enormous physical and mental energy, and they need to find constructive ways to channel it. If they do not, they may become agitated, anxious or aggressive. Regular vigorous exercise is essential, and competitive sports, martial arts or running are ideal outlets. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet and avoid stimulants such as caffeine and alcohol that could intensify their already considerable energy. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help them stay calm and focused.

Career: Born Advocates

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in law, politics, journalism, social activism, business, management, the military, sports or any field that rewards determination and fighting spirit. Their communication skills may also draw them to writing, teaching, broadcasting, public relations or sales.

Destiny: To Champion Causes and Inspire Others with Their Courage

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their determination with flexibility and openness. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to champion worthy causes and inspire others with their courage and conviction.

Power Thought

I am strong and flexible, open to all possibilities.
