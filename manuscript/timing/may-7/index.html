Born on May 7th

The birthday of absolute perfection

Profile

People born on May 7 are a curious mixture of inward-looking spirituality and outward concern with personal image. Although they recognize the most important values are non-materialistic, they also have a great desire to make an impression. This combination is in keeping with their quest for absolute perfection in all areas of life.

Deeply sensitive and compassionate, they are also elegant and gifted communicators who can inspire others with their ideals. They are willing to give every ounce of their energy to these ideals, though this can develop into a tendency to give too much of themselves.

They should retain a sense of perspective in their twenties, thirties, and early forties, when there is an emphasis on change, communication, and learning new skills. After forty it is vital not to lose a sense of self as they focus more on emotional relationships and family.

Their search for perfection best defines them. There is always a danger this manifests in unrealistic fantasies, and they need to focus on making their high ideals a workable reality. They should use their natural intuition to get in touch with their wisdom and creativity, which if allowed to express freely will satisfy their deepest yearnings for fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Living up to your high standards.

The Way Forward

Understand that there is nothing wrong with high standards, but we all have room for improvement.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 20 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. You share a passion for commitment and mental stimulation, and this can create a passionate and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Get to Know Yourself Better

If you want to attract luck into your life, start with self-awareness. Only when you know what makes you happy and unhappy can you start making positive changes.

At Your Best

Refined, discerning, dedicated.

On the Dark Side

Unrealistic, frustrated, superficial.

Love: Higher Love

May 7 natives have a deep need to experience a spiritual union but a part of them may doubt if this is possible. They need to remain open to the fact that it can be. Their ideal mate is likely to be intelligent and creative, with a deep hunger for a spiritual dimension in their lives.

Health: Strike a Balance

Those born on this day must strive for balance as they are prone to neglect themselves for the sake of others or work demands. They do have a tendency to gain weight, especially around the waist during midlife, but this can be remedied by healthy eating and daily exercise. Meditation and spiritual pursuits are important for their mental and emotional well-being. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to keep a sense of balance.

Career: Born Composers

They have excellent communication skills and potential to excel in the artistic sphere as poets, composers, and writers, but also as spiritual or political evangelists. Other careers include advertising, business, art dealership, education, and social reform.

Destiny: To Make the World a More Beautiful Place

The life path of those born on this day is to understand their inner conflicts better. Once they are more self-aware, their destiny is to put their high ideals to good use by making the world a more beautiful place.

Power Thought

Self-understanding is the beginning of my wisdom.
