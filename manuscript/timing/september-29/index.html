Born on September 29th

The birthday of the maverick

Profile

Those born on September 29 are mavericks at heart. At every opportunity they will question authority and convention, and if they disagree with rules and regulations they are not afraid to spark a rebellion. Real livewires, life is never dull when these people are around.

They are rebellious by nature, but this is not because they lack discipline or self-control. Quite the opposite: they are highly capable individuals with the ability to startle others with their talents. Despite their intelligence, they can also be rather unpredictable at times, suffering from bouts of low confidence because, however many followers they attract, a part of them never truly feels accepted. This sense of not belonging can cause them to swing between extroversion and introversion with confusing speed.

Until age twenty-three their emphasis is on relationships, and during these years their incessant need to be on the cutting edge may earn them more enemies than friends. The friends they do make, however, will remain loyal for life. After age twenty-four a turning point places the emphasis on emotional change. In the years that follow they will gradually realize that, although they can compromise and work productively with others, they are not and never will be team players. The sooner they learn to listen to their intuition, go their own way and use their own methods, the sooner they will realize their outstanding potential for success and fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with feeling like you do not fit in.

The Way Forward

Celebrate rather than feel ashamed of your uniqueness. Going your own way is your destiny.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both passionate and fiercely individualistic, and this can be a match made in heaven.

Luck Maker: You Are Luckier Than You Think

If you start focusing more on the good than on the bad in your life, you will automatically feel happier. And whenever you feel happy, luck is never far behind.

At Your Best

Exciting, capable, bold.

On the Dark Side

Rebellious, insecure, disruptive.

Love: Passionate and Romantic

The exciting and interesting people born on this day often attract many admirers, but their need to constantly challenge, question and shine can cause tension in personal relationships. However, because they are also passionate and romantic, loving heartfelt gestures such as flowers and poems, partners tend to forgive them. Because their lives tend to be unpredictable, they thrive best with a partner who can offer stability and security.

Health: Just Say Om

These individuals can be impulsive and prone to accidents and injuries, so it is important for them to exercise more caution with their physical well-being. Meditation or quiet time for thinking will help them get in touch with their feelings and figure out where they want their life to be heading. They tend to be very opinionated and may have certain theories about their diet and health that are worth checking with a doctor. They typically love exercise, especially running and swimming, and if they are not already exercising regularly they are strongly advised to start. Learning not to take things so personally will help them deal with rejection, and cultivating an attitude of positive expectancy will help them cope with bouts of negativity. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to celebrate their brilliant uniqueness.

Career: Born Dramatists

These individuals are naturally attracted to all careers that are creative, dramatic or artistic, such as acting, writing, music, dance and painting. Other choices include politics, social reform, the media, entertainment, business, self-employment, education, teaching and lecturing.

Destiny: To Inject Excitement into the Lives of Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn not just to accept but to be proud of their uniqueness. Once they have gained self-confidence, their destiny is to inject a sense of excitement and possibility into the lives of others.

Power Thought

I appreciate and place the highest value on all that I am.
