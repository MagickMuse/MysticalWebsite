Born on December 15th

The birthday of the optimist

Profile

Those born on December 15 are among the most upbeat individuals of the year. Once they set their sights on something, however unattainable, they truly believe they can achieve it. This positive, can-do and can-have attitude tends to attract most, if not all, of the professional and personal success their many talents deserve.

As well as being expansive and optimistic in their outlook, they are also blessed with an insatiable curiosity. They delight in discovering new information and in sharing what they have learned with colleagues and friends. Although their curiosity is infectious and their optimism energizing, it is important for them to consider whether the information they are sharing and the sense of possibility they are encouraging are in the best interests of themselves and others. Because they see the world through the eyes of an optimist, they do not always take into account or protect themselves against the negative potential of a situation. In other words, their plans can sometimes be unrealistic or even foolish, and their influence on others can be condescending or irresponsible.

Until age thirty-five, they have many opportunities to develop a more practical and realistic approach, and they need to take advantage of these because optimism can only carry them so far. After thirty-six, a turning point highlights a growing need for independence, progressive ideas and expressing individuality. During these years it is vital for them to listen to the advice of others and weigh the pros and cons before jumping in over their heads.

Throughout their lives, the key to success is the ability to recognize and leave behind unproductive situations so they can invest their outstanding potential in doing what they do best: moving in the direction of progress while motivating and inspiring others with their invigorating enthusiasm, stunning creativity and can-do attitude.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting your limitations.

The Way Forward

Understand that an overly optimistic evaluation of a situation is just as unhelpful as one that is overly pessimistic. Strike a balance between the two.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both charming and easygoing, and this relationship has great potential for happiness.

Luck Maker: Be Bold, Yet Realistic

Unlucky people set unattainable goals, preparing themselves to fail. The more realistic and obtainable your goals, the more likely you are to achieve them.

At Your Best

Energizing, inspirational, popular.

On the Dark Side

Uncontrolled, manipulative, foolish.

Love: Spoiled for Choice

Those born on this day are passionate and sensual individuals who rarely lack admirers. They have an insatiable appetite for new experiences, and although lovers will be plentiful, a quality relationship will prove elusive until they learn greater self-control. Once in a relationship with someone special, they are capable of great devotion, but fluctuating moods may require their partner's sensitivity.

Health: Breakfast Like a King

The optimism characteristic of these individuals tends to carry through into their attitude toward health. They may take health for granted and overlook problems that should be brought to a doctor's attention. They are advised to breakfast like a king, lunch like a prince and sup like a pauper, because stacking food intake this way gives the digestive system a rest during sleep and helps with insomnia and night waking. When it comes to exercise, the best advice is to take up an activity they enjoy, whether dancing, tennis, swimming or any other sport, because studies show that when people enjoy exercise they stick to it. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue or Indigo encourages a more realistic approach to life.

Career: Born Advertisers

These individuals have the ability to excel in business, new technology and management, but they may also be drawn to sales, writing, teaching, music, the media, advertising and performing. Whatever career they choose, variety and plenty of travel will keep them motivated.

Destiny: To Enlighten, Inspire and Educate Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to see all aspects of a situation, not just the ones they want to see. Once they improve their judgment and learn to be more realistic, their destiny is to enlighten, educate and inspire others and, by doing so, make their mark on society.

Power Thought

I appreciate my life in this moment, just as it is.
