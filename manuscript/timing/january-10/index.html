Born on January 10

The birthday of the realist

Profile

People born on January 10 are a force to be reckoned with. They feel an irresistible urge to speak their mind at all times, and as a result others prize them for their honesty and their clear-eyed reading of any situation. They never hesitate to back an unconventional stance or to champion the underdog.

They tell it exactly as it is, and this quality can carry them to success, admiration, and the very top. The flip side is that their refusal to sugarcoat the truth occasionally stings the people around them, slowing their climb compared to more diplomatic peers. Their straight-shooting style also leaves little room for hope, which can make them seem grumpy. That impression misrepresents them. They are neither unhappy nor negative; they simply see things as they are, blemishes included. Around age forty-one, and often earlier, their emotional attunement to others deepens.

At first contact their blunt manner can shock, but over time people find their clarity genuinely refreshing. During crises or moments of doubt, others seek them out for advice. They are glad to fill that role, since the respect of others matters to them. Yet the same traits that earn that respect can put distance between them and those who struggle more with change and life's swings. Once they grasp that not everyone shares their clear sight, and that gentleness can advance their aims more effectively, they discover they can win lasting loyalty and make a powerful mark on the world around them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Showing your true feelings.

The Way Forward

Tell yourself that vulnerability is not a sign of weakness but a sign of strength.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a passion for directness and a love of the unconventional; this can create a stimulating and intense bond.

Luck Maker: Pay Others Compliments More Often

The more you can make others feel good about themselves, the more impressed they are likely to be by you.

At Your Best

Honest, direct, accepting.

On the Dark Side

Tactless, jealous, distant.

Love: Not a Closed Book

January 10 natives can come across as a locked door. A lack of trust in others may drive controlling, jealous, and occasionally unfaithful behavior. Material concerns can also weigh too heavily in their closest bonds. Yet when they find a lover who sees the generosity and vulnerability hidden beneath their pride and rough exterior, they become sensual and passionate partners.

Health: Stay Flexible

Their tough, hardy shell can surface in health problems tied to rigidity, such as back pain, arthritis, and poor circulation. They need an energetic physical outlet like squash, aerobics, or rock climbing, and yoga or stretching to temper their strength with calm. A nutritious diet, plenty of fresh air, and sunshine all help keep them light in body and mood. When sadness arrives without an obvious reason, a bergamot-scented candle lifts their spirits. Wearing, meditating on, and surrounding themselves with the color Orange invites more spontaneity into their lives.

Career: Born Troubleshooters

They are drawn to work where they can solve problems for others: construction, building, computer programming. If the arts call to them, financial rewards will matter; they gravitate toward big-business ideas over niche specialties, and they thrive in high-return fields like management, advertising, and promotion. Their unconventional streak may also pull them toward the unknown; space tourism holds particular appeal.

Destiny: To Bring Others Closer to the Truth

The life path of January 10 natives is to draw people nearer to the truth through honest, realistic assessments. Their destiny is to leave a mark on others and on the world with their spirited determination and their unfussy approach to solving problems.

Power Thought

Today, I will see something positive in all situations.
