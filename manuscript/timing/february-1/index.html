Born on February 1st

The birthday of the spectacular turnaround

Profile

Those born on February 1 tend to be multi-talented individuals who refuse to think or act in conventional ways. Despite the fact that others are often swayed by their conviction, it is not uncommon for them to make a dramatic change of direction shortly after committing to a course of action.

This unique blend of originality, intuition, and flexibility gives February 1 natives a remarkable ability to attract and sustain success. They can assess a situation in an instant and formulate the right response, yet they are just as capable of reversing course entirely, so long as it moves them closer to where they want to be. They manage this with ease because they have the maturity to acknowledge that multiple valid viewpoints always exist. Values and beliefs guide them, but they never shut out options that might bring good fortune their way.

The willingness to learn and adapt that characterizes people born on this day makes them an intriguing mix of seriousness and fun, able to relate to and influence virtually everyone they meet. The only danger is that by constantly accommodating others they can lose touch with who they truly are. It is especially important for them to shift their focus toward deeper self-understanding and to become more aware of the effect others have on them. Fortunately, from around the age of nineteen a turning point places the emphasis firmly on discovering their goals in life, and at around forty-nine another shift nudges them toward even greater self-awareness.

With their ability to adapt and strike out in bold new directions, February 1 people are often trailblazers. Although deciding what to do can sometimes be agonizing, once they know where they are headed and, more importantly, who they want to be, their communication skills, versatility, and charisma have the potential to attract more success than they ever dreamed possible.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with feeling unsure about what to do with your life.

The Way Forward

Analyze your emotions like a scientist. Once you can identify what brings you joy, you are closer to finding your answer.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a passion for honesty and fun, and this can create an intense, magickal bond.

Luck Maker: Just Do Something

Sometimes when you cannot decide what to do, you simply have to act and stop overthinking. If it does not work out, at least you will have learned something about yourself from the experience.

At Your Best

Inspiring, original, mentally quick.

On the Dark Side

Stubborn, fickle, self-absorbed.

Love: Heart on Sleeve

February 1 natives tend to wear their heart on their sleeve and prefer relationships built on honesty and openness. They are never short of admirers, but they also long for the security of a partnership that allows them to be truly themselves. As long as their lover approaches them with sincerity, they make loyal, entertaining, and deeply loving partners.

Health: Breathe In and Out

Those born on this day often have complicated emotional lives, which can predispose them to nervousness, sensitivity, and unhealthy lifestyle choices. They may also be prone to hypertension and heart concerns. It is extremely important for them to avoid stimulants, alcohol, smoking, drugs, and the temptation of reckless behavior, and to find healthier outlets for their emotions, such as vigorous exercise. A sensible diet low in refined sugar and rich in whole grains, nuts, seeds, fruits, and vegetables is highly recommended. Regular breathing exercises, including yoga and chi kung, can also help reduce stress and prevent oversensitivity.

Career: Born Firefighters

Careers requiring quick action or split-second thinking, such as paramedic, pilot, firefighter, doctor, or nurse, appeal to these people. They may also be drawn to management or executive roles in large organizations, or to sales and promotion. With their love of change they should steer clear of routine positions. Their inventive, original ideas may also draw them toward humanitarian work, entrepreneurship, or excelling in media, writing, and the arts.

Destiny: To Break New Ground

The life path of those born on this day is to discover who they truly are and what they want out of life. Once they accomplish that, their destiny is to break new ground and reach further than anyone has reached before, powered by their determination and drive.

Power Thought

The secret of my success is self-understanding.
