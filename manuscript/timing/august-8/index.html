Born on August 8th

The birthday of versatility

Profile

Those born on August 8 are remarkably versatile and multi-talented individuals who have the ability to master a wide range of skills and disciplines. They are driven, ambitious and determined, with a powerful desire to succeed in everything they attempt. Their versatility is their greatest asset, but it can also be their greatest challenge, as they may struggle to focus their energies on one thing long enough to see it through to completion.

Although they are often admired for their many talents, they are also deeply practical and grounded. They have a strong work ethic and are willing to put in the hard work necessary to achieve their goals. They are also highly organized and efficient, with an ability to juggle multiple projects and responsibilities simultaneously. The danger is that they can spread themselves too thin and become overwhelmed by the sheer number of commitments they take on.

Until age forty-four there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to develop their skills in a wide variety of areas and build a diverse portfolio of experience and achievement. After age forty-five there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony.

After age seventy-four there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional depth and personal transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to focus their extraordinary versatility on a few key goals rather than scattering their energy in too many directions, they have the potential to achieve truly remarkable success.

Your Greatest Challenge

Focusing your energy.

The Way Forward

Understand that true mastery requires focus and commitment; being good at many things is impressive, but being exceptional at one thing is extraordinary.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 20

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You are both ambitious, disciplined and practical, and this can create a stable and deeply supportive partnership.

Luck Maker: Commit to Mastery

Lucky people understand that luck favors those who commit deeply. When you focus your energy on mastering one thing, you create the conditions for extraordinary luck.

At Your Best

Versatile, dynamic, accomplished.

On the Dark Side

Scattered, overwhelmed, unfocused.

Love: A Multi-faceted Partner

Those born on this day are exciting, fun and fascinating partners with many interests and talents to share. They need a partner who can keep up with their energy and who appreciates their versatility. However, they need to be careful not to let their many commitments crowd out quality time with their loved ones.

Health: Quality Over Quantity

These individuals tend to pack their schedules to bursting and may be prone to burnout, exhaustion and stress. It is important for them to learn the art of prioritizing and to build rest and relaxation into their daily routines. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regular meals and not skip them when they are busy. Exercise is important, but they should not take on too many different fitness activities; choosing one or two that they enjoy and sticking with them is ideal. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to slow down, reflect and focus on what truly matters.

Career: Born Entrepreneurs

These individuals are ideally suited to careers that reward versatility and multi-tasking, such as entrepreneurship, management, consulting, media, journalism, education, medicine, law or any field that requires a diverse skill set. They may also be drawn to the performing arts, writing, science, engineering or technology.

Destiny: To Achieve Mastery Through Focus and Commitment

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to focus their extraordinary versatility on a few key goals. Once they have committed to mastering their chosen field, their destiny is to achieve remarkable success and inspire others with their exceptional range of talents.

Power Thought

I focus my energy with intention and create greatness.
