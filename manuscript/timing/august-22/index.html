Born on August 22nd

The birthday of the commander

Profile

Those born on August 22 tend to be dignified, hard-working and serious in outlook. They are natural commanders, and their air of authority and self-assurance is so strong that few would dare to challenge them. Blessed with vitality and determination, they believe that everyone, themselves included, should always give one hundred percent.

Few will doubt their ability to lead or manage a group, as they are extremely good at delegating and organizing others into productive teams. When they set their minds to something, their determination, tenacity and willingness to sacrifice their personal desires for the greater good give them incredible power and potential for success. The danger for these individuals, however, is that they may become obsessed with their work to the detriment of their personal lives. They may also have a tendency to become dictatorial, and it is important for their psychological growth that they listen to and take note of the opinions and desires of others.

Until age thirty there is an emphasis on practicality and perfectionism; these are the years when they are most likely to immerse themselves completely in their work. After age thirty, however, there is a turning point that emphasizes growing importance on relationships, creativity and harmony. If they can use this influence to develop their diplomatic skills and allow others to help, they have enormous potential for success and happiness.

After age sixty there is another turning point which heightens their awareness of power and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they understand that commanding others is not the only way to gain their respect, they can be extraordinarily creative, productive and effective leaders who inspire the very best in others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning that you do not always know best.

The Way Forward

Understand that greatness is only achieved when you know your own limitations and learn to ask for help.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 20 and February 18

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You are both independent and strong, and this can be a dynamic and progressive relationship.

Luck Maker: Be Open-minded

Lucky people understand that their way is not the only way, and they are always willing to try different approaches; this creates luck because it opens up a world of possibility.

At Your Best

Dignified, commanding, hard-working.

On the Dark Side

Dictatorial, detached, self-sacrificing.

Love: A Softening Influence

Those born on this day can be very intense in relationships, sometimes to the point of being controlling or jealous. They need a partner who has a softening influence on them and who can help them relax. Once they find someone who understands them, they are loyal, passionate and incredibly devoted lovers who will stop at nothing to make their partners happy.

Health: A Little More Fun

These individuals may find that their all-or-nothing approach to work and the demands they place on themselves can take their toll. It is vital for them to learn how to relax and how to have fun. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a healthy, balanced diet and make sure they do not skip meals when they are busy or stressed. Regular exercise is important because it helps them wind down and sleep better, and walks in natural surroundings are also highly recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more spontaneous and fun-loving.

Career: Born Managers

These individuals are ideally suited to management, business or politics, and they can be effective leaders in whatever career they choose, including science, research, engineering, law, medicine, the military and law enforcement. They may also be drawn to the entertainment world, music, art and design, as well as teaching, training and social reform.

Destiny: To Lead Others Toward Their Full Potential

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the art of diplomacy. Once they have developed their ability to listen to and inspire others, their destiny is to lead and empower others toward achieving their full potential.

Power Thought

I allow others to help and inspire me.
