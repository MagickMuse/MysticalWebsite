Born on December 20th

The birthday of the producer

Profile

Those born on December 20 are energetic and gifted problem solvers and decision makers with a marked talent for motivating and organizing others. Born leaders, they are driven by a desire to help society progress and are at their happiest and best when generating ideas and initiating projects. Once a project has gotten off the ground, however, they prefer to move on to the next one and put others at the helm, content in their self-appointed role of producer and creator.

There simply are not enough hours in the day for these individuals. Their impulse to keep moving forward is strong, and because they are also efficient their output and achievements are often remarkable. Their instinct is always to understand the bigger picture, and when heading toward their goals criticism will not deter them. Hardworking and committed, they can make the mistake of assuming others are as tireless and determined as they are, becoming frustrated and impatient with those who simply cannot keep up. Despite their vital concern for the welfare of others and their intense desire to make the world a better place, their interpersonal skills often need attention.

Until age thirty-one, they are likely to be more practical and realistic in their approach to achieving goals, and their results-oriented style will draw both praise and criticism from those who feel they are too superficial or inattentive to details. After thirty-two, a turning point sparks a need to be more independent and put their individual stamp on things. These are the years when both professional and personal success is most likely.

At any age, developing their latent imaginative and creative powers and rediscovering a joyful, childlike spirit will give them the ability not only to generate innovative ideas and motivate others but to lift people's spirits and truly enjoy life.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning from mistakes.

The Way Forward

Look at mistakes as opportunities to learn about what does or does not work in your life, so you can constantly fine-tune and improve your performance.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. You are both hardworking and original individuals with qualities that can bring out the best in each other.

Luck Maker: Your Only Competitor Is Yourself

If competing against others is a high priority, you will not embrace opportunities that attract luck because your energy is focused on competitors rather than on your own luck-making potential.

At Your Best

Productive, energetic, fast.

On the Dark Side

Superficial, hasty, stubborn.

Love: Short Fuse

Those born on this day are energetic and vital, and potential partners are drawn to their directness and can-do attitude. Their short fuse and impatience when things are not going their way can, however, threaten long-term relationships. Restless and sensitive, they may try many different relationships before settling on a partner who brings out the youthful and fun-loving aspects of their personality.

Health: Boosting Immunity

These individuals may suffer from endless coughs and colds, especially during the summer months, so building up the immune system and strengthening their delicate constitution should be a priority. A healthy, nutritious diet rich in fruits and vegetables and plenty of moderate, non-competitive exercise on a daily basis form the foundation. They should also make sure they do not run themselves down with too many activities and too much pressure. Regular check-ups are recommended. Burning essential oil of frankincense while working or relaxing is particularly beneficial for respiratory infections and mild depression caused by lack of natural sunlight. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green helps them seek balance in their lives.

Career: Born Agents

These individuals are well suited to careers where they can play the role of agent, leader or guide, excelling in fields as diverse as politics, teaching, the arts or science. Other options include business, public relations, promotions, sales, writing, music, the entertainment world, alternative healing and sport.

Destiny: To Play a Guiding Role

The life path of those born on this day is to learn from their mistakes and to slow down every now and again to assess progress. Once they are able to take on board the advice of others, their destiny is to play a guiding role in life.

Power Thought

I am grateful for the precious breath of life.
