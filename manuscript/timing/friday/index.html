In Love Workings

Friday is the preferred day for all traditional love workings, philter-craft, and chalice operations. The favored working hours are 8 in the morning, 3 in the afternoon, or 10 at night, ideally when the moon is waxing. More precisely minded practitioners also time the moon to the first ten degrees of Taurus or Virgo, well aspected to Venus and Saturn, though this level of precision is optional. Observing the correct moon phase, day of the week, and working hour, combined with firmly resolved intention, is held to be sufficient for success.
