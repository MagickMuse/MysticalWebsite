Born on October 14th

The birthday of the middle path

Profile

Those born on October 14 are moderate, balanced individuals who instinctively seek the middle ground in all areas of their life. They have a natural ability to see both sides of any argument and to find compromise and common ground where others see only conflict. This diplomatic skill makes them invaluable in both their personal and professional lives.

Their love of balance extends beyond interpersonal relationships to every aspect of their existence. They strive for moderation in their diet, exercise, work habits and leisure activities, and they are uncomfortable with extremes of any kind. This balanced approach gives them a calm, steady and reassuring presence that others find extremely appealing.

However, their desire for balance can sometimes become a weakness. They can be so intent on keeping the peace that they avoid confrontation altogether, even when standing up for themselves or others is necessary. They can also become indecisive, endlessly weighing the pros and cons of a situation without ever committing to a course of action. Learning when to be decisive and when to fight for what is right is one of their most important life lessons.

Before age thirty-eight they are likely to be most focused on issues of emotional depth and personal transformation. After thirty-nine, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for optimism, adventure and expansion. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that their love of balance does not prevent them from taking risks and making bold decisions. When they can combine their natural diplomacy with greater decisiveness and courage, they become truly effective leaders who can guide others toward harmony and progress.

Your Greatest Challenge

Making decisive choices.

The Way Forward

Understand that indecision is itself a decision. Sometimes the best way to find balance is to commit to a path and adjust as you go.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 21 and June 20

Those dates fall under Gemini. You are both communicative and adaptable, and this can be a harmonious and intellectually stimulating partnership.

Luck Maker: Trust Your First Instinct

Lucky people trust their gut feelings. Over-analyzing every option can lead to paralysis. Sometimes the best decision is the one you make quickly and confidently.

At Your Best

Diplomatic, balanced, perceptive.

On the Dark Side

Indecisive, passive, avoidant.

Love: Harmonious Partnership

Those born on this day are loving, considerate and devoted partners who place a high value on harmony in their relationships. They are attracted to people who are calm, cultured and emotionally mature. They need to be careful, however, that their desire for harmony does not lead them to suppress their own needs and feelings, as this can create resentment over time.

Health: Everything in Moderation

The moderate approach to life that these individuals favor can serve them very well when it comes to health. They tend to eat balanced diets, exercise regularly and avoid excesses. However, they need to make sure that their moderation does not become monotony. Varying their exercise routine and trying new foods and activities will keep them physically and mentally stimulated. They also need to ensure that they express their emotions rather than suppressing them in the name of keeping the peace, as this can lead to stress and its related health problems. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage boldness and decisiveness.

Career: Born Mediators

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers in mediation, diplomacy, law, counseling, human resources and social work. Their love of balance and beauty may also draw them toward careers in art, design, architecture, music and fashion. Their ability to see the bigger picture makes them effective managers, administrators and consultants.

Destiny: To Guide Others Toward Harmony and Balance

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to combine diplomacy with decisiveness. Once they can take bold action when it is needed while maintaining their natural sense of balance, their destiny is to guide others toward harmony, compromise and peaceful resolution.

Power Thought

I trust my instincts and I act with calm confidence.
