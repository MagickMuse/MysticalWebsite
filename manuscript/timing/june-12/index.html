Born on June 12th

The birthday of realistic positivity

Profile

People born on June 12 tend to have a cheerful disposition, and their optimistic, positive approach to life serves them well. Their strong belief in the power of good also has an uplifting effect on those around them, helping others change for the better.

Extremely generous and supportive, their positivity is always tempered by realism. They support what they know they can achieve or what they believe others can achieve. Their aim is not perfection but improvement, believing the best way to help someone is to encourage them to help themselves. Occasionally this manifests in judgmental words, but their tough-love approach generally works.

The sunny disposition they present to the world gives them the capacity not only to achieve great things but to be pioneers. They can't bear inertia and will push themselves to their limits, even devising new activities for friends and family or learning a new skill. The downside is that their cheerfulness can sometimes seem irritating to others who see them as lacking depth. Although they may appear superficial, inner conflicts often lie beneath their cheerful exterior. It is important they don't try to bury these conflicts with external activities.

Until age thirty-nine they focus on emotional security and should take advantage of opportunities to learn about love and understanding. After forty they become more self-confident. During this time they need people who challenge them intellectually and encourage them to focus inward. Once they connect with their intuition, their infectious dynamism will be validated by remarkable achievements.

Your Greatest Challenge

Facing your fears and insecurities.

The Way Forward

Acknowledging that you have fears and insecurities actually reduces their power over you. Once the enemy is understood, it is far easier to manage.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You both share a cheerful disposition, and this can be an exciting and fulfilling union.

Luck Maker: Ask Your Intuition Questions

Interact with your intuition as if it were a palpable force. Ask questions and expect to receive answers that improve your good fortune.

At Your Best

Optimistic, decisive, generous.

On the Dark Side

Judgmental, unaware, superficial.

Love: Self-Knowledge

Pursuing self-knowledge and deepening emotional awareness will help June 12 natives become lucky in love. They need to understand that loving someone else is impossible if they don't love themselves first. They should avoid cynical, manipulative people and find a partner who is as intelligent, positive, loving, and giving as themselves.

Health: You Are Not Invincible

Those born on this day have an extremely positive attitude toward health, but they need to remember they are not invincible. Regular check-ups are advised. They should aim for variety and food that is as fresh and natural as possible. Solitary activities such as running, swimming, and cycling are recommended for both immunity and quiet reflection time. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to look within and find inner peace.

Career: Born Motivational Speakers

These people would make excellent motivational speakers or personal coaches. Their strong organizational skills suit them to a variety of professions, from outdoor physical work to administrative jobs. Travel and tourism may suit their adventurous spirit, their love of action may attract them to sport or leisure, and their sensitivity may draw them to medicine, drama, or music.

Destiny: To Encourage, Motivate, and Inspire Others by Their Example

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to understand themselves better. Once they become more self-aware, their destiny is to lead, encourage, motivate, and inspire others by their example or their words.

Power Thought

Any time I wish, the wisdom of my intuition is there for me to use.
