Born on December 21st

The birthday of the secret

Profile

Although they are energetic and strong-willed, it is very hard to know what those born on December 21 are really thinking and feeling, as they are secretive by nature. They prefer to express themselves with actions rather than words, and their powerful, silent presence can be inscrutable even to those closest to them.

Even though they may be a mystery to others, this does not mean they are reserved or passive. Quite the opposite: they are determined to achieve their goals and get their point across. It is just that instead of exchanging opinions they usually prefer to push ahead regardless of what others are saying or thinking. If this means battles along the way, so be it. Their presence can be intimidating, not just because others never know what to expect but because when they do lash out their few carefully chosen words can be harsh and cutting. Others may feel they can never relax around them, as though near a dormant volcano: quiet on the outside but with a burning intensity within.

Despite sometimes appearing menacing or brooding, they are surprisingly insecure, although they would never allow others to know it. This insecurity forces them to be defensive, to nurture hidden resentments and, above all, to crave the admiration and respect of others. They should understand that they already have that admiration, but what they really need is affection, and that can only be earned when they learn to trust and share their feelings.

Around age thirty-two, a turning point offers opportunities to focus less on proving themselves and more on finding their place in society. If they seize these chances and open their minds to alternative viewpoints and their hearts to the magickal potential within, they will discover not only the secret of their own happiness but the secret of everyone else's too.

Your Greatest Challenge

Trusting and sharing with others.

The Way Forward

Understand that everything in life, including relationships, involves a certain amount of risk-taking. Sometimes you just have to take a leap of faith.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. If you can both learn to be less secretive, open up and share, this relationship has great potential for happiness.

Luck Maker: Let Go of Hidden Resentments

Hidden resentments poison your thoughts, keep you stuck in the past and attract bad luck your way. Letting go or forgiving has the opposite effect, channeling your energy into attracting luck.

At Your Best

Strong-willed, authoritative, intriguing.

On the Dark Side

Domineering, inflexible, self-involved.

Love: A Feisty Partner

Those born on this day are attractive but slightly unsettling for potential partners. They like to do their own thing but will not allow others to do theirs. If things are not perfect, they will withdraw or move on without explanation or discussion. A feisty partner who is not intimidated by them would help them find happiness.

Health: Open Up and Share

These individuals can suffer from tension and mental overload, so solitude is vital for quieting and calming the mind. They need to be careful, though, not to go overboard with alone time, because too much isolation is not good for them. Spending time with friends and loved ones is often the best way to deal with tension. Counseling or therapy is advised if they find it very hard to open up. They should watch their intake of sweet and fatty foods, as weight gain around the middle can be a problem. Caffeine and alcohol should be limited, and if they smoke, quitting immediately is urged. Vigorous exercise, preferably of the social kind such as dancing, aerobics or team sports, is recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages more spontaneity and expressiveness.

Career: Born Entrepreneurs

These individuals may often be found leading in a variety of careers in science, business, sports, arts and entertainment. They typically rise to management level but may also work for themselves and become high-flying entrepreneurs.

Destiny: To Become Pioneers and Leaders in Their Chosen Field

The life path of those born on this day is to recognize the power of positive thinking. Once they understand that they are in charge of who or what they attract into their lives, their destiny is to combine authority and decisiveness with compassion and flexibility, becoming outstanding leaders in their chosen field.

Power Thought

Whatever the question, I know that love is the answer.
