Born on February 26th

The birthday of the wise soul

Profile

People born on February 26 may often be described by others as old souls because they seem so comfortable in their own skin. They frequently have great insight into how the world works and what motivates the people around them.

When the deep insight of these people is combined with their somewhat impersonal and detached persona, it can make others stand back in awe. In fact, they have quite a hypnotic power over others; people tend to do what they say or follow their example. It is important that they do not abuse this power — fortunately they rarely do, as they also possess a powerful sense of integrity and social justice. They like to find something to appreciate in everybody and in every situation, and their unfailing optimism is truly enlightening.

One danger for February 26 natives is a tendency to preach and lecture, or to be rigid or harsh in their opinions. They will often be unaware when they are showing this side of their personality, which is particularly emphasized between the ages of twenty-four and fifty-four. During these years they should surround themselves with close friends or loved ones who can warn them when they are veering off track. Fortunately, they respond extremely well to constructive criticism and have the ability to change their ways. If someone reaches out to them emotionally and opens their heart, they will more often than not transform into a more fully rounded human being.

Often blessed with great wisdom, their ability to arouse and inspire others will help them achieve worldly success. Although they enjoy social recognition, part of them feels more comfortable as an outsider looking in. They will sometimes feel an urge to be alone with their thoughts or to sacrifice themselves to a higher cause. Being wise souls, however, they will also have learned the importance of emotional connection, so when they do feel the need to withdraw it is not to isolate themselves but simply to recharge before taking the next step forward.

Your Greatest Challenge

Taking yourself a little less seriously.

The Way Forward

Understand that humor, when used positively, can be a powerful force for good.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both tough on the outside but warm on the inside, and this can create a close, tender bond.

Luck Maker: Show the World Your Teeth

Open your mouth wide when you smile. Being a wise soul, you should know better than anyone that the old saying "Smile and the world smiles with you" really does make sense.

At Your Best

Insightful, hypnotic, honest.

On the Dark Side

Dogmatic, moody, harsh.

Love: Throw Caution to the Wind

February 26 natives tend to see love as a threat either to their enormous self-discipline or to their work, and as a result they may not allow others to get too close. This is a mistake, as they have a huge heart with so much love to give and they need to let that love express itself fully. Once they are able to open up, they make loyal, passionate, and supportive partners.

Health: Food Is Not the Answer

The optimism of these people tends to keep them in good health, but if their need for love and closeness is denied they may seek it in food, drugs, or other addictions. Diet can be a real concern, and they need to watch the amount of fat, sugar, cream, and refined foods they consume; otherwise their blood sugar levels could spike, setting them up for future problems with weight, heart health, and diabetes. Gentle to moderate exercise is also important, especially later in life when there is a tendency to slow down. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will help them feel warmer and more secure. It might also give their love life a boost.

Career: Born Judges

These people have the wisdom and determination to be great judges or lawyers. They may also be drawn to teaching, lecturing, politics, counseling, consultancy, or social reform, where they can speak on behalf of others. Their need for artistic expression may be satisfied through art, design, music, writing, poetry, or drama.

Destiny: To Follow Their Conscience and Right Social Wrongs

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that their need for love is not a weakness but a great strength. Once they are able to both give and receive love, their destiny is to follow their conscience and right social wrongs.

Power Thought

Today I will try to laugh at everything, myself included.
