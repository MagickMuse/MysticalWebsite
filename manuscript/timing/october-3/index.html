Born on October 3rd

The birthday of the cutting edge

Profile

Those born on October 3 are progressive, innovative individuals who are always one step ahead of the crowd. They have an instinctive feel for emerging trends and future possibilities, and their ability to anticipate what is coming next gives them a significant advantage in both their personal and professional lives. They are the people others look to when they want to know what the next big thing will be.

They are intellectually curious and adventurous, with a restless energy that drives them to explore new ideas, new places and new ways of doing things. They are not content with the status quo and are always looking for ways to improve, innovate and push forward. Their enthusiasm for the new and the novel is infectious, and others are drawn to their energy and vision.

Despite their forward-thinking nature, they can sometimes neglect the present in their eagerness to get to the future. They may start many projects but finish few, as their attention is always being pulled toward the next exciting possibility. They can also be so focused on what is coming that they fail to appreciate what they already have. Learning to be present and to follow through on their commitments is one of their most important life lessons.

Before age forty-nine they are likely to be most focused on issues of emotional intensity and personal transformation. After fifty, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for freedom, adventure and philosophical exploration. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that their love of the cutting edge does not leave them disconnected from the people and things that matter most. When they can combine their visionary nature with groundedness and follow-through, they become truly pioneering individuals who do not just predict the future but help create it.

Your Greatest Challenge

Following through on commitments.

The Way Forward

Understand that starting something is easy; finishing it is where the real achievement lies. Discipline and follow-through are the bridges between vision and reality.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous and forward-thinking, and this can be a stimulating and expansive partnership.

Luck Maker: Finish What You Start

Lucky people understand that unfinished projects and broken commitments drain energy and credibility. Finishing what you start builds the momentum and reputation that attract luck.

At Your Best

Innovative, visionary, dynamic.

On the Dark Side

Scattered, restless, uncommitted.

Love: The Thrill of the New

Those born on this day love the excitement of new relationships and can be charming, attentive and passionate in the early stages of romance. However, they need to work hard to maintain their interest and commitment once the initial thrill fades. They need a partner who can keep things fresh and exciting while also providing the stability and grounding they need to build a lasting relationship.

Health: Stay Grounded

These individuals can burn through their energy quickly because they are always on the go, mentally and physically. It is important for them to establish regular routines for eating, sleeping and exercising. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will provide the sustained energy they need. Regular exercise is essential, and activities that require focus and discipline, such as weight training, swimming or martial arts, will help ground their restless energy. They should also make time for practices like meditation or mindfulness that encourage them to slow down and be present. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage balance, stability and a connection to the present moment.

Career: Born Innovators

These individuals are naturally drawn to careers on the cutting edge of their field, such as technology, science, research, design, fashion, media, marketing and entrepreneurship. Their communication skills may also lead them toward careers in journalism, writing, broadcasting and public speaking. Their restless energy and love of variety may draw them toward travel, consulting and project-based work.

Destiny: To Pioneer New Ideas and Possibilities

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their love of the new with the discipline to follow through. Once they can do this, their destiny is to be true pioneers who anticipate, create and shape the future.

Power Thought

I am fully present in this moment, and this moment is where my power lives.
