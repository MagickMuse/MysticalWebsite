Born on August 15th

The birthday of regal presence

Profile

Those born on August 15 have a naturally regal bearing and commanding presence that draws others to them. They are proud, confident individuals with high standards and a strong sense of personal dignity. Whether they are aware of it or not, they tend to dominate any social or professional group, and their opinions carry considerable weight.

They have a natural authority that inspires respect, and their desire is to be recognized and admired for their achievements. Although they can appear aloof, they are actually warm-hearted and generous, and they use their considerable talents to help those less fortunate. The danger, however, is that they can become too concerned with appearances and status, losing sight of what truly matters. They may also be overly proud and unwilling to ask for help when they need it.

Until age thirty-seven there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they need to make sure that their desire for recognition does not overshadow their emotional needs. After age thirty-eight there is a turning point that emphasizes the growing importance of relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a very positive time for them, especially if they can learn to be more open and vulnerable with those close to them.

After age sixty-seven there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional depth and personal transformation. Whatever age they are, once they understand that true nobility comes from the heart and not from status or achievement, they have the potential to become truly inspiring and influential figures.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting go of the need to impress.

The Way Forward

Understand that the people who matter most are not impressed by status or achievement but by authenticity and kindness.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both refined and elegant, and this can be a harmonious and beautiful partnership.

Luck Maker: Drop the Mask

Lucky people do not hide behind a facade of perfection. They show their true selves, flaws and all, and this honesty draws people and luck toward them.

At Your Best

Dignified, generous, commanding.

On the Dark Side

Proud, status-conscious, aloof.

Love: Royal Treatment

Those born on this day expect and deserve the royal treatment in love, and they give as good as they get. They are generous, devoted and passionate partners, but they can be overly proud and may struggle to apologize when they are wrong. They need a partner who respects their dignity but who is not afraid to challenge them when necessary.

Health: Inner Beauty

These individuals tend to place a great deal of importance on their appearance, and while this means they generally take good care of themselves, they may neglect their inner emotional health. It is important for them to develop their spiritual and emotional side through meditation, journaling or counseling. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet rich in fresh fruits and vegetables. Regular exercise is important, and they may enjoy activities that have a social element, such as tennis, dance or group fitness classes. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Pink will encourage them to open their hearts and be more loving and approachable.

Career: Born Directors

These individuals are ideally suited to leadership roles in any field, including business, politics, entertainment, fashion, the arts, education and management. Their natural authority and organizational skills can also be applied to law, medicine, the military and public service. They may also find fulfillment in philanthropy and social reform.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Generosity and Grace

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that true greatness is measured not by achievements or status but by the positive impact they have on others. Once they understand this, their destiny is to inspire others with their generosity, grace and compassionate leadership.

Power Thought

I let go of the need to impress and simply let my light shine.
