Born on March 15th

The birthday of the mountain climber

Profile

People born on March 15 are adventurous and determined individuals with a can-do, will-do approach to life. Like intrepid mountain climbers, once they have set their sights on a goal they steadily and persistently make their way to the summit.

With a clear-sighted and progressive outlook, these people tend to be hardworking, ambitious, and goal-oriented. They are often leaders or trendsetters in their chosen field. When they commit to a project or a course of action, their organizational talents, determination, and tenacity ensure they see it through. Although they are sometimes criticised for being too pragmatic, they are also blessed with imagination and sensitivity. These qualities, if allowed to express themselves, can help soften their more driven, ambitious side and provide balance.

Up until around the age of thirty-five there is an emphasis on developing confidence, assertiveness, and ambition. During this period they will learn many valuable lessons through their efforts and mistakes, and these lessons should not be ignored. After thirty-six, there is a turning point suggesting they will slow down a little, placing greater emphasis on stability and emotional security.

For people born on this day there is always a danger of becoming consumed by their ambitions and their desire to climb their way to the top. If they do not keep their ego in check, they can become arrogant, selfish, and unscrupulous in the pursuit of their goals. It is important for them to understand that the key to their success is not how far they have risen but how many people they have helped along the way. Once these creative and driven individuals discover a sense of perspective, tempering their ambitions with awareness of the world around them, they are likely to discover that the summit was worth climbing to after all, and the view from the top is simply spectacular.

Your Greatest Challenge

Showing concern for other people.

The Way Forward

Understand that the higher you climb the more people you should bring with you, because you cannot stand on the top of the mountain alone.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 23

Those dates fall under Virgo. You share a passion for excellence and a desire for order, creating a fulfilling and stable partnership.

Luck Maker: Stop Blowing Your Own Trumpet

If you are always talking about yourself and how great things are for you, not only will you make others feel insecure or envious, you will give the impression that you do not care about anyone else.

At Your Best

Determined, progressive, ambitious.

On the Dark Side

Obsessive, arrogant, selfish.

Love: Intense and Loving

March 15 natives tend to be attracted to those who are powerful, successful, or influential. They are intense, loving partners, but they do have a tendency to place greater importance on work than on their relationships. They need to prioritize quality time with friends, family, and loved ones, making sure that their partner does not feel overlooked or taken for granted.

Health: Watch Your Back

People born on this day tend to push themselves hard and may suffer from stress-related health problems. They may also be prone to back, shoulder, and knee problems as a result of their relentless climb. They need to ensure they take regular breaks, especially from desk-bound work, and a regular program of stretching, yoga, or tai chi is highly recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should limit their intake of alcohol and rich food, and eat a diet rich in fiber, whole grains, fruits, and vegetables. Regular moderate exercise is recommended, such as walking, cycling, or swimming. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to think of higher, more spiritual things rather than just material success.

Career: Born Executives

These people are born executives with the potential to reach the top in whatever career they choose. They may be drawn to business, finance, management, law, or politics, and they also have creative talents that could lead to careers in art, music, architecture, writing, or design. Education, science, and medicine may also appeal.

Destiny: To Rise to the Top and Inspire Others to Do the Same

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that success is not defined by material gain but by the quality of the relationships they build along the way. Once they have learned to temper their ambition with concern for others, their destiny is to rise to the top and inspire others to do the same.

Power Thought

The measure of my success is the number of people I help along the way.
