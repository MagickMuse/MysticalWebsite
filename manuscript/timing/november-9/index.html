Born on November 9th

The birthday of temptation

Profile

Those born on November 9 are dynamic and seductive individuals who are not afraid to take risks or push things to the limit. Their greatest challenge, however, is themselves, as their impulsive nature and addictive tendencies have the potential to ruin everything they have worked to achieve.

They are passionate, energetic and exciting people to be around. When they put their mind to something they can accomplish fantastic things, but their problem is that they tend to act on impulse rather than reason. Living life on the edge may be fun and exhilarating for a while, but sooner or later it is likely to have damaging consequences. They tend to be drawn to the forbidden and their curiosity is insatiable, which means they will rarely say no to temptation. If they can learn self-discipline and self-control, their enormous energy and passion can be channeled positively; if they cannot, their lives can become a roller coaster of highs and lows.

Until age forty-two there is an emphasis on optimism, adventure and risk taking. During these years they need to be especially careful not to let their impulsive nature get the better of them. After forty-three, a turning point arrives when they start to take a more disciplined, pragmatic and realistic approach to life. This is a positive turning point, as it can help them channel their wild energy more constructively.

Whatever age they are, their greatest asset is their remarkable willpower. Once they learn to exercise it, using it to resist temptation rather than seek it out, they become a force to be reckoned with. When that willpower is combined with their courage, passion and determination, the extraordinary potential for success and fulfillment that is associated with this birthday can be theirs.

Your Greatest Challenge

Saying no.

The Way Forward

Understand that true strength lies not in always saying yes, but in knowing when to walk away.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both passionate and daring, and this can be a highly energized and exciting union.

Luck Maker: Delay Gratification

The ability to delay immediate gratification in favor of long-term rewards is one of the most important qualities lucky people possess.

At Your Best

Passionate, exciting, magnetic.

On the Dark Side

Impulsive, addictive, reckless.

Love: Irresistibly Attractive

Those born on this day are irresistibly attractive and seductive, and they will rarely be without admirers. They are exciting and generous partners, but their love of risk and excitement can make it difficult for them to settle down. Once they find a partner who can match their passion and keep them guessing, however, they can be extremely loyal and devoted.

Health: Channel Your Energy

These individuals have boundless energy, but if they do not channel it constructively they may suffer from burnout, stress-related illnesses or the consequences of addictive behavior. They need to be especially careful with alcohol, recreational substances, caffeine and rich foods, as they have a tendency to overindulge. Regular vigorous exercise such as running, competitive sports or martial arts is highly recommended, as it will help them burn off their excess energy in a healthy way. They would also benefit from meditation, counseling or any discipline that helps them develop self-awareness and impulse control. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help calm their impulsive nature and encourage thoughtful reflection.

Career: Born Adventurers

These individuals thrive in careers that offer them excitement, challenge and variety. They may be drawn to careers in business, the stock market, sales, sport, the military, the emergency services, journalism or the entertainment world. Their creativity and passion may also find expression in writing, acting, music or the arts.

Destiny: To Channel Their Extraordinary Energy Positively

The life path of those born on this day is to learn the art of self-discipline. Once they have learned to channel their passion and energy constructively, their destiny is to achieve remarkable things and inspire others with their courage and determination.

Power Thought

I am in control. I choose my path with wisdom and strength.
