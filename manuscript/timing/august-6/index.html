Born on August 6th

The birthday of high expectations

Profile

Those born on August 6 have incredibly high expectations of themselves and others, and they dedicate their lives to achieving the very best in everything they do. They are perfectionists with a keen eye for quality and an unwillingness to compromise their standards. Although their expectations can sometimes seem unrealistically high, their determination and focus often help them achieve the extraordinary results they demand.

They are natural leaders who inspire others with their vision and drive, and they have a genuine desire to make a difference in the world. They are warm-hearted, generous and loyal, and they care deeply about the people in their lives. The danger, however, is that their impossibly high standards can lead to disappointment, frustration and criticism, both of themselves and others.

Until age forty-six there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they are likely to set and pursue ambitious goals, often achieving impressive results. After age forty-seven there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a period when they can soften their approach and become more accepting of imperfection.

After age seventy-six there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and transformation. Whatever age they are, once they learn to temper their high expectations with acceptance and compassion, they have the potential to achieve remarkable things and to inspire others to raise their own standards.

Your Greatest Challenge

Accepting imperfection.

The Way Forward

Understand that perfection is an illusion; striving for excellence while accepting imperfection is the key to happiness and true success.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both refined, idealistic and relationship-oriented, and this can create a harmonious and beautiful partnership.

Luck Maker: Lower the Bar Sometimes

Lucky people understand that setting impossibly high standards can be counterproductive. Sometimes lowering the bar and allowing room for surprises invites more luck into your life.

At Your Best

Driven, inspiring, generous.

On the Dark Side

Critical, disappointed, demanding.

Love: High Standards in Love

Those born on this day bring the same high expectations to their relationships that they bring to everything else. They are passionate, devoted and generous lovers, but they can be overly critical of their partners. They need someone who meets their high standards but who also teaches them the beauty of acceptance and forgiveness.

Health: Ease Up on Yourself

These individuals tend to place enormous pressure on themselves and may suffer from stress-related ailments such as headaches, digestive problems and insomnia. It is crucial for them to learn how to relax and let go. Regular exercise is important, especially activities that are fun and non-competitive such as dancing, swimming or hiking. As far as diet is concerned, they should try not to be too rigid about what they eat and should allow themselves to enjoy the pleasure of food. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to be more accepting and balanced.

Career: Born Quality Controllers

These individuals excel in any career that demands high standards and attention to detail. They may be drawn to management, business, quality control, architecture, design, fashion, medicine, law, education, science, engineering or the arts. Their natural leadership abilities and drive to excel can also take them to the top of whatever profession they choose.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Reach for Excellence

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their high expectations with acceptance and compassion. Once they have achieved this balance, their destiny is to set an inspiring example and motivate others to reach for excellence.

Power Thought

I embrace imperfection as a doorway to growth and joy.
