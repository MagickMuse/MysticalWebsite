💘Feast of Eros

◇Notes
Festival of Eros, held on Mounikhion 4. The date may reflect the God's connection with Aphrodite.
◇Type
Holidays
◇Culture
Greek
↗Entities2
AphroditeEros

The Feast of Eros is held on the fourth day of Mounikhion. The choice of day may reflect the God's close connection with Aphrodite, since the fourth of every month is sacred to Aphrodite, Hermes, Herakles, and Eros. Little further detail of the rite is preserved in the surviving sources.

On a normal calendar. The Feast of Eros falls on the fourth day of Mounikhion, generally landing in April of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
