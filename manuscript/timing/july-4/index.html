Born on July 4th

The birthday of dedication

Profile

Those born on July 4 tend to identify strongly with groups and organizations, whether their family, co-workers, local community, country or even humanity as a whole. The creation of shared human bonds and goals is extremely important to them, and they will often dedicate themselves to defending people's wider interests.

Ironically, for those so focused on communal identification, they are also quite private individuals who prefer to keep their own feelings to themselves. Their dedication and loyalty are matched only by their courageous spirit and desire to defend and protect those less fortunate. Between the ages of forty-eight and seventy-eight there is an emphasis on practical considerations and analysis. During these years they need to learn to make the right choices by investing time and energy in listening to their inner voice. After the age of seventy-eight there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Staying objective.

The Way Forward

Understand that when your dedication to a cause makes it impossible for you to listen to alternative viewpoints, you run the danger of fanaticism.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You share a passion for justice, harmony and progress, and this can create a respectful and supportive union.

Luck Maker: Trust Your Hunches

Your intuition is a gift that can bring you good luck, success and happiness. You owe it to yourself to listen to it and trust it.

At Your Best

Principled, courageous, generous.

On the Dark Side

Biased, demanding, stubborn.

Love: Always Knows Best

Those born on this day have a mild-mannered charm that will often attract admirers, but when in a relationship they need to watch their tendency to always know what is best for everyone. They tend to be attracted to enterprising, successful people who are determined to succeed. Once in a loving relationship, however, they can be extremely affectionate, generous, loyal and loving.

Health: Do Not Neglect the Gift of Health

Those born on this day have a tendency to neglect their own health and wellbeing for the sake of the family or the group they are working with. It is important for them to make sure that their own personal wellbeing takes center stage. They would benefit greatly from scheduling regular exercise into their diary. Team sports and regular fitness classes are ideal ways for them to keep in shape. As far as diet is concerned, they need to increase their intake of fresh produce, in particular fruits and vegetables. Mind-body therapies that encourage them to get in touch with their intuition, such as meditation, hypnotherapy and yoga, would be extremely beneficial. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to focus more on their inner life.

Career: Born Firefighters

These individuals will thrive in any job that involves people and an element of risk. They might make great firefighters, paramedics, police officers or emergency care workers. They may also be drawn to sales, negotiation, promotion, law, publishing, banking or politics. Any occupation that deals with items for the home, food or caring would also be a possibility.

Destiny: To Devote Their Talents to the Common Good

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that some of the answers they are looking for can only be found by searching within. Once they are able to connect with their intuition, their destiny is to devote their considerable talents to the common good.

Power Thought

I bring good to others by discovering the good in me.
