Born on February 13th

The birthday of the enigmatic extrovert

Profile

Those born on February 13 are difficult to ignore. They are open and uninhibited in almost everything they undertake, bursting with energy, originality, and fun. Often regarded as trendsetters, they are at their best when performing to an audience.

February 13 natives have a unique approach to life, and their ideas and plans have the potential to make them a fortune. Because they are rebellious and more than a little wild, they will almost certainly encounter criticism and rejection, particularly during their teens and early twenties. It is important for them to hold fast to their individuality and resist the urge to fit in. They simply need to find the right path, the right goals to devote their considerable energy to, and the right people to encourage them to be themselves — and their success is assured.

Exuberant, original, and daring, these people see a world of possibilities and potential around them. Sometimes in their enthusiasm to push forward they can come across as bossy or eccentric; they do need to learn to slow down and look before they leap. They are often guilty of following their hearts before their heads, which can land them in trouble and sometimes hurt other people in the process. After the age of thirty-seven a turning point suggests they will become more aggressive and focused in pursuing their goals, making it more important than ever to center themselves and find inner security.

Although their uninhibited nature relishes being the center of attention, there are certain aspects of themselves they keep strictly private. This gives them a fascinating enigmatic quality and a complexity that only serves to intrigue and delight their audience even more. Once they learn to control rather than repress their exuberant spontaneity, these elusive yet extroverted individuals can often be found entertaining, educating, and intriguing their adoring fans.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding rushing into things.

The Way Forward

Learn to take a deep breath, ask others for their opinion, and list the pros and cons before making a decision.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. Your mutual fascination will keep you both intrigued and involved, creating a passionate and slightly mysterious union.

Luck Maker: Get in Touch with Nature

To increase your chances of good fortune you need to learn to listen to your intuition. One of the most effective ways to calm your mind is to spend time in nature.

At Your Best

Uninhibited, unique, fun.

On the Dark Side

Wacky, reckless, bossy.

Love: In and Out of Love

The address book of February 13 natives is often packed with friends and admirers, yet they may find it difficult to let one person get truly close. Falling in and out of love frequently, they may struggle to stick with relationships because they value their freedom so highly. But when they do find the right person — often someone as elusive and dramatic as themselves — their love is for keeps.

Health: Not So Fast

People born on this day have a tendency to take risks with their body and health, making them prone to accidents and injuries, particularly in their legs and ankles. They need to learn to take things a little more steadily and remain calm in difficult situations. Mind-body exercises such as yoga, tai chi, and meditation techniques would benefit them enormously. They should also watch their intake of rich food and excessive amounts of alcohol, red meat, and dairy. Moderate to mild physical exercise is preferable to vigorous routines. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with shades of Blue and Green will help them handle challenges with calmness, objectivity, and optimism.

Career: Born Entertainers

These people have a love of variety, adventure, and acclaim, along with the versatility to handle sudden changes, making them ideally suited to the world of entertainment. Other careers that might appeal include astronomy, meteorology, aviation, scientific research, and education. Their charismatic ability to lead campaigns could also draw them toward politics, social reform, or launching their own business.

Destiny: To Encourage Others to Express Themselves

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to follow both their heart and their head. Once they have learned a measure of self-control, their destiny is to express themselves and, by doing so, influence and inspire others.

Power Thought

Today is my bridge to greater self-knowledge and joy.
