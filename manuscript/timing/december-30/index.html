Born on December 30th

The birthday of the choreographer

Profile

Those born on December 30 are at their happiest and best when they are bringing order to chaotic situations. They have a gift for spotting what is not working or what needs improvement, and they also possess the creativity and vision to implement effective changes.

In many ways, these individuals are like choreographers, coordinating details while keeping the bigger picture in mind. At times their intentions may seem unclear, but in the end everything always comes together. Their ability to motivate and inspire the people around them to perform at their best makes them highly successful leaders. They can lean toward a glass-half-empty outlook, but this does not mean they lack a sense of fun. They appreciate humor and the lighter side of life, and they love relaxing with friends. They are simply practical realists who will always account for worst-case scenarios in their plans.

Their decisive tendency to take charge of collective efforts can produce excellent results, and they are often at the cutting edge of their professional field. One trait that can hold them back, however, is their tendency to come across as disinterested because they are people of few words. When they do speak, others are often stunned by their perceptiveness, but working on communication skills will benefit them enormously in both personal and professional life.

Before age twenty-one, they are likely to be cautious in their approach to life, but after twenty-two, opportunities arise for them to become more adventurous, more independent and less influenced by the opinions of others. They should seize these chances to express their individuality, because once they realize how creative and capable they truly are, their self-confidence will bloom and they will attract the success and happiness they deserve.

Your Greatest Challenge

Expressing yourself clearly.

The Way Forward

Understand that explaining yourself and your methods to others builds bridges of understanding.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You both have personality traits that can balance each other's, giving this relationship great potential for lasting happiness.

Luck Maker: Change Your Talk About Luck

If you believe luck always slips through your fingers, that is exactly what will happen. Tell yourself instead that luck does come your way, and with that attitude of positive expectancy, it likely will.

At Your Best

Perceptive, capable, authoritative.

On the Dark Side

Misunderstood, negative, stressed.

Love: Don't Get Complacent

Those born on this day can attract attention, yet they are not particularly demonstrative when it comes to affairs of the heart. They need to be careful that complacency or fear of putting themselves on the line does not cause them to miss opportunities for love. They may find themselves drawn to gregarious and dramatic individuals who encourage them to be more outgoing.

Health: Quartz Crystal

Unless these individuals see instant results, they may struggle to stick with a regular exercise and healthy eating program. If they can commit for at least three to four months, however, they will notice significant improvements. Regular check-ups should also be scheduled, as they have a tendency to ignore warning signs. Bouts of negativity and depression can arise, and they are sensitive to stress, but healthy eating, regular exercise and plenty of sleep help them conquer these. Cognitive behavioral therapy techniques can reprogram thoughts in a more positive direction. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange encourages a more positive outlook.

Career: Born Commanding Officers

These individuals work well in business or commercial ventures, but they may also be drawn to politics, teaching, coaching, training, the military, diplomacy and other positions that require coordinating large numbers of people. Other options include management, art, music and writing.

Destiny: To Bring Harmony, Cohesion and Cooperation

The life path of those born on this day is to reprogram their thoughts so that both negative and positive outcomes are taken into account. When they learn to be more flexible and forgiving, their destiny is to bring harmony, cohesion and cooperation.

Power Thought

I am rich in inner wisdom and can express my inspirations clearly and eloquently.
