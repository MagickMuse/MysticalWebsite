Born on May 6th

The birthday of the sensitive star

Profile

The highly imaginative and intuitive people born on May 6 are acutely sensitive to the feelings, dreams, and hopes of others, especially those less fortunate. Fascinated by the human psyche, they often feel compelled to pass their wisdom on. If they are not guiding others, they will be inspiring them by living out dreams that most of us repress.

As well as being finely tuned to others, they are highly sensitive themselves, which can lead to unnecessary misunderstandings and hurts. They should learn to be more objective; otherwise they will experience insecurity and disappointment. Between ages fifteen and forty-five there is an emphasis on communication and the exchange of ideas. After forty-five they focus more on emotional closeness, family, and security.

Their interest in human nature can lead to a deep desire to do something meaningful. The most common block to their progress is a lack of faith in their own abilities, which can trigger mood swings and indecision. They need to turn the understanding they have of others upon themselves. Their self-doubt may result in taking a secondary role rather than making full use of their creative potential. They respond extremely well to encouragement, and should remember that every step toward their ambitions inspires others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with your extreme sensitivity.

The Way Forward

Understand that there are ways to thrive when the world threatens to overwhelm you. Take regular time out and pace yourself.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. You are both highly sensual and loving, and this can create a deeply fulfilling and passionate union.

Luck Maker: Distinguish Between Intuition and Fear

Intuition tends to be calmer and less wordy than fear. If your inner voice is indecisive, harsh, and judgmental, it is fear speaking.

At Your Best

Sensitive, artistic, astute.

On the Dark Side

Insecure, indecisive, moody.

Love: Waiting

May 6 natives are unwilling to commit unless they feel a relationship has the potential to be a perfect union. They may spend many years searching, but when they find their soul mate they are at their happiest, because love and giving to others is a strong motivating force in their lives.

Health: Boosting Your Self-Esteem

Those born on this day tend to suffer from a lack of self-confidence. Food is a passion, so they should watch for comfort eating. Regular exercise boosts their immune system and helps them cope with excessive worry. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will boost their self-esteem and positivity.

Career: Born Doctors

They often find fulfillment in the medical, psychiatric, or caring professions, but may also be well suited to politics and the arts. Whatever career they choose, their natural psychological skills will be of great assistance.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Energy

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to believe in themselves. Once they develop greater self-confidence, their destiny is to direct their prodigious energy toward the progression of others.

Power Thought

I trust that every decision I make will enhance my life or the lives of others.
