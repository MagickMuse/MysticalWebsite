Born on February 6th

The birthday of the charmer

Profile

People born on February 6 are generous, accommodating individuals who are generally liked by everyone they meet. It is almost impossible not to warm to them, thanks to their winning manner and enthusiastic personality. As a result, they are often extremely popular and widely respected.

Positive feedback and approval from others truly matter to those born on this day, but this need for affection is far from one-sided; in many ways, life itself is one big love affair for them. The only downside is that when things do not go their way, or when their love and generosity are met with contempt and selfishness, they can become hurt and emotional. Disappointment and disillusionment may occasionally push them toward dramatic or insecure behavior, which can irritate rather than endear them to others.

Although they can be insecure at times, the generous and positive nature of February 6 natives attracts plenty of love, admiration, and success. They do need to be careful, however, that in their eagerness to please they do not become overly accommodating and lose touch with their own emotional needs. They need to understand that friendship is built not just on accommodation but on trust, respect, generosity, and healthy boundaries. Around the age of forty-four a turning point arrives when they become more assertive and aware of their own character.

The flexible nature of these people also extends to their thought processes. They are never caught off guard by the unexpected, always willing to explore new territory. They can sometimes court popularity for its own sake, but more often than not their unpretentious nature earns them respect and praise in both good times and bad. As long as they do not take others' regard for granted and remember that self-worth must rest on more than popularity, life tends to be very good to these charming, thoughtful people.

Your Greatest Challenge

Moderating your desire to be needed by everyone.

The Way Forward

Understand that people will like you for who you are, not because you are accommodating.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. Sensuality and the thrill of seduction and togetherness excite you both, and when the two of you are together, sparks can fly.

Luck Maker: Be Loyal and True

Do not talk about others behind their back to gain popularity. It will backfire and repel rather than attract good fortune.

At Your Best

Loving, generous, likable.

On the Dark Side

Needy, uncertain, insecure.

Love: Swept Off Your Feet

February 6 natives are in love with the idea of love itself, and as a result they have a tendency to leap from one lover to the next in search of greater passion, intimacy, and excitement. They are forever chasing an elusive high. When they do find someone as clever and informed as they are, incredible loyalty and commitment can follow.

Health: Look Inside

Those born on this day tend to be extremely concerned about their appearance. Often there is little need to worry, as they tend to be blessed with good looks, but this does not stop them from obsessing. They should avoid fad diets and stick to regular, healthy meals and snacks. They probably enjoy competitive, vigorous forms of exercise but would benefit equally from gentler pursuits such as long walks in the countryside. Mind-body therapies like meditation can shift their focus from the exterior to the interior. If they find themselves feeling negative, spending more time with family and friends is recommended. Wearing or carrying a crystal Amethyst can also help lift their mood.

Career: Born Charmers

Any career that allows these people to use their fantastic people skills will bring great success: public relations, entertainment, media, public speaking, politics, sales, marketing, and promotion. They may also be drawn toward teaching, research, science, or sport. Their ability to captivate an audience could also be channeled into humanitarian causes, and their strong sense of justice may steer them toward human rights work.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Personality

The life path of those born on this day is to make sure their own needs are not ignored while they cater to others. Once they accomplish that, their destiny is to lead others to an exciting new world of opportunity.

Power Thought

I also flourish with love and care.
