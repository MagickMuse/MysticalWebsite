Born on July 31st

The birthday of the descriptive artist

Profile

Those born on July 31 are eloquent observers of the human condition. They always seem to be researching or digging around for information, possessing the ability to describe people and situations with remarkable accuracy and insight.

Nothing seems to escape their attention, even their own faults, which they are quick to correct. Their communication skills are superb and their insightful observations are often laced with a keen sense of humor. Whether they become artists or not, they often have a well-developed esthetic sense, loving to surround themselves with beautiful objects and attractive people.

Work matters greatly to them and they may throw themselves so wholeheartedly into it that they have little time to spare for friends and family. They also need to watch their tendency to think negatively, especially between the ages of twenty-two and fifty-two when there is an added emphasis on practicality and realism. If they can keep their generous spirit alive with uplifting thoughts and compassion, they may transform their high ideals and dreams of beauty into reality.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not being cynical.

The Way Forward

Understand that the pessimistic approach to life is just as unrealistic as the optimistic because it means you focus too much on one perspective.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. Although different in many ways, you share a love of communication and this can create a union of surprising compatibility.

Luck Maker: Keep Believing in Yourself

Keep believing in yourself, especially in times of misfortune, as this will inspire others to believe in you also, attracting the help that can turn misfortune around.

At Your Best

Articulate, artistic, hardworking.

On the Dark Side

Work-obsessed, anxious, detached.

Love: Shared Goals

Physical beauty is a high priority for those born on this day, but for long-term satisfaction they should seek someone who shares their strong work ethic and artistic sensitivity. With their charm and ability to radiate warmth they can be attractive to others but need to be careful that their restless nature does not involve them in power plays with partners.

Health: Do Not Sit on the Sidelines

These individuals love to watch and learn but they need to be careful that this does not leave them sitting on the sidelines. Social interaction and participation are important for their psychological growth, helping them manage their thoughts in a more positive direction. They also have a tendency to worry endlessly, and learning to make decisions about things they can change and letting go of things they cannot will benefit them enormously. They need to make sure they eat a healthy, nutritious diet because it will boost their mood and their powers of concentration. Regular moderate exercise is highly recommended, especially those which involve social interaction such as dancing, fitness classes or team sports. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage them to be more upbeat.

Career: Born Investigative Researchers

The love of observation and description that defines these individuals may draw them toward investigative careers such as forensic science, detective work, journalism, law or science. They may also gravitate toward teaching, management, administration, politics, charity work, medicine and art.

Destiny: To Utilize Their Findings to Help Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to make their thoughts work for them, not against them. Once they have managed to control their tendency toward negativity, their destiny is to make great discoveries and utilize their findings to help others.

Power Thought

My beautiful, loving thoughts create my beautiful, loving world.
