Born on August 16th

The birthday of the powerhouse

Profile

Those born on August 16 are among the most powerful and dynamic individuals of the year. They have a commanding presence and an incredible amount of energy and drive that they channel into achieving their goals with single-minded determination. Others are naturally drawn to their confidence and vitality, and they often find themselves in positions of leadership and authority.

Their strength of will and determination are formidable, but they also have a softer, more compassionate side that they may not always show. They care deeply about the well-being of others and are often motivated by a desire to protect and provide for those they love. The danger is that their intensity and drive can sometimes make them overpowering, and they need to learn that gentleness and sensitivity are not signs of weakness but of true strength.

Until age thirty-six there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they will steadily build their reputation and develop their skills. After age thirty-seven there is a turning point that emphasizes growing importance on relationships, creativity and harmony. This is a period when they are likely to become more tactful and less confrontational.

After age sixty-six there is another turning point that brings an emphasis on emotional and spiritual regeneration. Whatever age they are, once they learn to temper their power with tenderness and use their incredible energy to uplift rather than overpower, they have the potential to achieve truly remarkable things.

Your Greatest Challenge

Templing your strength with sensitivity.

The Way Forward

Understand that true power is not about dominating others but about empowering them; the strongest leaders are those who lead with compassion.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 19 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. You can teach each other much about the balance between strength and sensitivity, and this can create a deeply complementary union.

Luck Maker: Lead with Kindness

Lucky people understand that kindness is not weakness; it is the most powerful force in the universe. The more kindness you show, the more luck you attract.

At Your Best

Dynamic, commanding, protective.

On the Dark Side

Domineering, intense, inflexible.

Love: All or Nothing

Those born on this day tend to approach love with the same intensity they bring to everything else. They are passionate, devoted and fiercely loyal partners, but they can also be possessive and jealous. They need a partner who is strong enough to stand their ground but gentle enough to bring out their softer side.

Health: Power Down

These individuals need to learn how to relax and power down, as their relentless drive can lead to burnout, stress and exhaustion. Regular exercise is essential, but they should balance vigorous activities with more gentle, restorative practices such as yoga, meditation or tai chi. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet and not skip meals when they are busy. Adequate sleep is vital. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will promote emotional balance and healing.

Career: Born Leaders

These individuals are natural leaders and will thrive in any career that offers them authority and responsibility. They may be drawn to business, politics, the military, law enforcement, management, medicine, sport or engineering. Their creative side may also find expression in writing, music, art or the performing arts.

Destiny: To Use Their Power to Protect and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that true power comes from the heart, not the will. Once they have tempered their strength with sensitivity, their destiny is to use their extraordinary energy and drive to protect, uplift and inspire others.

Power Thought

My greatest strength is my ability to be gentle.
