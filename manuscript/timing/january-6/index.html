Born on January 6

The birthday of the philosopher

Profile

People born on January 6 are always searching beneath the surface for deeper meaning in events. They instinctively look for the glimmer of goodness in everyone, yet this philosophical, soulful orientation can lead others to dismiss them as naive or childlike, gravely underestimating the force of their energy and the sharpness of their mind.

For all their reflective bent, they are intensely ambitious and goal-driven, and they usually end up getting what they set out to gain. Beneath a quiet, inward-leaning exterior they can summon surprising resolve when their principles are on the line. The hazard is that they trust their instincts so completely, and assign such weight to everything that happens to them, that they may reflexively reject outside perspectives. This can earn them a reputation for being unrealistic, rigid, or unreasonable, and their habit of expecting everyone else to think like them can strain those who lack the same philosophical cast of mind.

Underneath the stubbornness and forthright speech, they are deeply tender and easily wounded when their contributions go unappreciated or people fail to take them seriously. The hurt may drive them to rebel against authority or slip into reckless behavior. Around the age of forty-five they come to understand that rebellion, however cathartic, is never the whole answer. They need somewhere to channel their untamed side, and sport, work, or study often saves them by offering the guardrails and discipline required to steady their emotions and funnel their energy productively.

In the end, even when they face criticism or rejection, the idealism and sincerity of January 6 natives always breaks through. Once they discover what truly deserves the commitment of their life, their determination and gift for communicating their vision in an electrifying way draw admirers and notable success to them.

Your Greatest Challenge

To avoid feeling burdened down with responsibilities at work.

The Way Forward

Devote as much time to your personal life as to achieving your ideological objectives.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. You share a passion for harmony, beauty, and love; this will keep the relationship or friendship balanced and nourishing.

Luck Maker: Listen First, Talk Second

Everyone wants to be understood and the key to understanding is to listen. To get people on your side, listen to other points of view before giving your own.

At Your Best

Idealistic, philosophical, understanding.

On the Dark Side

Naïve, unrealistic, unreasonable.

Love: In Love with Love

Relationships carry an overwhelming charge for January 6 people, and they risk vanishing into them. They can sometimes seem more captivated by the concept of love than by the actual person beside them. It is essential for them to learn how to receive as well as give. A broad circle of thoughtful friends helps prevent them from clinging too tightly to any one partner.

Health: Ease Up on Yourself

Their devotion to ideals and to others must not come at the expense of their own health. Nutritious eating and ample sleep keep their irresistible energy primed for the challenges ahead. A skin complaint may surface at some point, though it typically fades once diet and lifestyle are addressed. They should be wary of extreme dietary regimens pushed by persuasive gurus, such as plans that eliminate entire food groups or overload on protein. The secret to a healthy diet, like a healthy life, is balance. Social forms of exercise like dancing and aerobics suit them well, and a few drops of Lemon Essential Oil essential oil on their pillow or bedclothes at night will help them drift off and wake refreshed.

Career: Born Visionaries

These individuals carry a vision into their work as they do everywhere else. If they cannot communicate it effectively within an organization, they may strike out alone, launching their own business or becoming self-employed. They make outstanding therapists, doctors, counselors, engineers, architects, property developers, computer programmers, psychologists, and alternative health practitioners. Religion and spirituality may also call to them.

Destiny: Help Others Learn About Themselves

The life task of January 6 natives is to spread the truth that opposites can be reconciled: the bright and the shadowed, the visionary and the concrete, the sacred and the everyday. Their destiny is to guide others through fear and uncertainty toward discovering their own truth.

Power Thought

I help others by listening to them.
