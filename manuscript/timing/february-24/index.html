Born on February 24th

The birthday of the romantic bard

Profile

People born on February 24 have an intuitive, generous, and loving spirit that makes them a much-sought-after friend, acquaintance, or partner. Highly romantic, they sometimes see the world through the eyes of a poet blinded by the power of love. Intimacy is extremely important to them — without it they can wither and pine away like the hero or heroine of a romantic novel.

Those born on this day are highly sensitive to their environment, constantly tuning into the moods of others and the dynamics of every situation around them. This makes them excellent peacemakers and valuable members of any team or family, as they can often spot trouble or find ways to resolve a situation without offending anyone. The danger lies when they concentrate on the emotional needs of others rather than their own. Setting boundaries in interpersonal relationships is vital for them. This will not be easy, and they may even bend the truth if they think it will prevent an argument. In the long run, however, this kind of evasive and dishonest behavior only breeds anxiety and uncertainty. Fortunately, between the ages of twenty-six and fifty-six there are opportunities for them to develop their assertiveness and be more straightforward in relationships. After fifty-six, they become even steadier and more mature.

The longing for intimacy and emotional connection always rules the life of February 24 natives in some way. They may have tried to suppress emotional traumas in the past, but whatever their background or experience, those buried feelings will somehow manifest — perhaps in a quest for the perfect partner or in passionate devotion to a cause.

Those born on this day have a deep desire to use their energies selflessly on behalf of others. Once they learn that love is not just about pain but also about joy, and that intimacy is not only about sacrificing and giving but also about receiving and gaining, they will discover within themselves the tenacity and vision to achieve just about anything.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to assert yourself.

The Way Forward

Take charge of your life, say no when you need to, express your true feelings, and do not let others treat you like a doormat.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. Your mutual passion for art, romance, and poetry can create an intense and fulfilling bond.

Luck Maker: Believe That You Deserve the Best

If you do not believe that you deserve the best, you will not allow good things into your life. Believe you deserve the best right now and you will get it.

At Your Best

Loving, generous, altruistic.

On the Dark Side

Needy, moody, passive.

Love: Born to Be in Love

February 24 natives throw themselves wholeheartedly into relationships and may end up giving too much with very little in return. For their relationships to be truly satisfying, they need to learn to love themselves first. Once they accomplish that, they will find the soul mate they have been pining for.

Health: Weak at the Knees

People born on this day need to be careful not to relax into a life of sensual pleasure, rich food, addiction, and little exercise. They may experience disorders involving the legs, feet, and circulatory system; most of these could be avoided with an active lifestyle. Finding ways to incorporate more movement into their days is vital: taking the stairs instead of the lift, walking around the block at lunch, carrying rather than pushing their shopping. They should also watch their intake of red meat, alcohol, saturated fats, and sugar, especially chocolate. To curb cravings for sweet foods, smelling vanilla essential oil can help. Wearing or meditating on the color Blue will help calm and soothe them when they feel emotional.

Career: Born Poets

These people are often drawn to art, music, writing, dance, poetry, and the entertainment world in general. Sensitive to color and pattern, they may also express themselves through design, especially costume design, styling, or the beauty industry. Because they are so talented in dealing with people, any career that casts them in the role of mediator, adviser, counselor, agent, manager, or negotiator will suit, as will social reform, humanitarian or charity work, teaching, and health careers.

Destiny: To Help and Heal Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to love and give to themselves as much as they are willing to give to others. Once they can do that, their destiny is to help and heal others and, in doing so, make the world a more loving and generous place.

Power Thought

I am worthy of all life has to offer.
