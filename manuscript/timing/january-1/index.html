Born on January 1st

The birthday of self-improvement

Profile

Those born on January 1 are full of energy and enthusiasm, drawn to lead and show others the way forward. Once they settle on a goal, their drive, integrity, and originality attract good fortune and almost guarantee success. The very qualities that pull success toward them, however, can also hold them back.

It is essential for January 1 natives to accept that mistakes are part of life. When they go through life expecting things to always unfold the way they wanted, and people to always do what they said they would, they end up perpetually frustrated. They need to deviate from the familiar, learn from mistakes, and accept the unexpected. Once they turn rejection into resolve, they discover an emotional resilience that carries them through fear.

Above all else, January 1 natives value dedication, discipline, and anything to do with education, psychology, and study. They are born to lead and to inspire, at home and at work. A voice inside them is always urging them to work harder, faster, and longer. This can make them charismatic achievers who set an example to others, the bosses who burn the midnight oil, the teachers who give up their spare time, the politicians who take a pay cut. The danger is that they get so caught up in the process of self-improvement that they lose sight of the goal, their sense of humor, and the bigger picture.

Those under thirty in particular run the risk of becoming too focused on work and responsibility, pushing themselves and others hard in the process. Once they realize that optimism, resilience, and listening to others matter just as much as hard work and dedication, they unlock tremendous potential for creativity, insight, and inspired leadership.

Your Greatest Challenge

To stop being so hard on yourself when you make mistakes.

The Way Forward

Learn from your mistakes and turn regret into positive resolve. Let the powerful current of positivity sweep into your life and raise your game.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share the same wild energy and drive, and this mutual understanding forges an intense, passionate bond.

Luck Maker: Believe in a Better Plan

When plans turn out differently than expected, do not drown in remorse or anxiety. Open your mind to the belief that there must be a better plan, or a better way.

At Your Best

Drive, dedication, honesty.

On the Dark Side

Oversensitive, impatient, manipulative.

Love: Overpowering and Seductive

The seductive power and drive of those born on January 1 can overpower others when unchallenged. They thrive on variety and constant challenge. If a relationship cannot hold their interest, they grow bored and controlling. Once involved with someone creative who keeps them on their toes and offers peace and security when plans unravel, they tend to stay loyal.

Health: Danger of Burnout

Emotional and physical burnout is the greatest health risk. Because they can be intensely self-critical, January 1 natives are prone to bouts of depression. Trusted people to talk to about insecurities are essential, whether family, friends, or counselors. Stress-related illnesses such as headaches, high blood pressure, comfort eating, and digestive trouble are common concerns. Alcohol, smoking, caffeine, and sugar should be moderated, with plenty of fresh air, exercise, and relaxation in their place. Three drops of Lavender essential oil on a handkerchief, breathed in when life moves too fast, gives them a much-needed pick-me-up.

Career: Born Specialist

January 1 natives like to be in charge, so they are drawn to careers that allow it. In business they work well as planners, producers, CEOs, and account managers. When those routes are closed, self-employment suits. Politics, education, engineering, astronomy, geology, and medicine also attract them. Any path that lets them specialize in and rise to the top of a particular field will appeal.

Destiny: A Voice of the People

The life task of January 1 natives is to recognize that weaknesses in themselves and others are not necessarily insurmountable. With a change of perspective, weaknesses can become strengths. That insight, paired with the knowledge that everyone has something to offer, gives them the emotional strength to fulfill their destiny as a voice of the people.

Power Thought

When one door closes, another always opens for me.
