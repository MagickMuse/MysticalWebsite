Born on June 19th

The birthday of the activator

Profile

People born on June 19 are blessed with the ability to stimulate and uplift others. Their resolve and courage, along with their patience, tolerance, and generally good intentions, serve as an inspiring example. Whether they realize it or not, they are catalysts spurring others into action and moving situations forward.

They may choose to fight vociferously or to quietly hold firm, but whatever strategy they choose they rarely crumble under pressure. Their resolve comes from their most outstanding characteristic: their self-belief. They are rarely beset by doubt, which can arouse both admiration and irritation. Their single-minded determination is not the result of blinkered obstinacy but rather of supreme confidence born from knowing they have evaluated every viewpoint and arrived at the best conclusion. They are blessed with incisive analytical skills, intellectual inquisitiveness, and profound intuition.

Less evolved types may express themselves in controlling or dictatorial behavior, but life usually teaches them the benefits of a less confrontational approach. Until age thirty-one they focus on emotional security, home, and family. After thirty-two they enter a period of greater self-expression and creativity with added assertiveness. It is important during this period that they learn the art of diplomacy. At sixty-two they may feel a desire to be more methodical and to offer practical service.

Above all, they have the ability to empower others with their compassion, sparkling wit, and youthful vitality. As long as they maintain balance and perspective, they have the potential to make their mark by guiding, improving, and energizing others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not being too confrontational.

The Way Forward

The direct approach simply won't work in every situation. The best way to get people on your side is often to approach them gently.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both courageous, outgoing, and sensitive, and this can be a passionate and exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: Make Others Feel Special

The more special you make people feel by listening and complimenting them, the more likely they are to listen to and support you.

At Your Best

Energizing, courageous, persistent.

On the Dark Side

Over-ambitious, unaware, inflexible.

Love: Young at Heart

Young at heart and outgoing, June 19 natives are often sociable and popular. They are passionate and committed lovers but can also be stubborn, vain, and argumentative. However, should their partner face problems, there is no one more loyal or understanding.

Health: Burnout

Those born on this day tend to drive themselves too hard and, because they perform well under stress, may not notice how run down they are until poor health forces them to take time out. It is important for them to include plenty of rest and relaxation and practice stress-management techniques such as deep breathing, meditation, or simply having a long soak in an aromatherapy bath. They should avoid eating on the run and opt for wholesome, home-cooked food. Exercise should be moderate and low impact. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will help them feel more in balance, and carrying a Rose Quartz crystal can help release pent-up stress.

Career: Born Teachers

Those born on this day have the ability to inspire and motivate others and may be drawn to education, counseling, consultancy, or lecturing. Other appealing careers include sales, commerce, promotion, negotiation, business training, writing, research, social reform, and politics. Their need for self-expression may also draw them to art, design, advertising, and the media.

Destiny: To Share Their Sense of Purpose and Vision of Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to become aware of the influential effect they have on others and not abuse it. Once they learn to be more tolerant and diplomatic, their destiny is to encourage others to share their sense of purpose and their vision of progress.

Power Thought

With practice my compassion deepens and my life becomes richer.
