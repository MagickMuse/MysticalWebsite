Born on March 11th

The birthday of the magician

Profile

Individuals born on March 11 are progressive people with one foot lightly placed in the present and another firmly placed in the future. The key to their success is their intuition. They use it not in a dreamy but in a highly productive way to increase their chances of success. Magician-like, they have learned to harness its power to achieve their goals.

The keen mind and visionary ability of these people gives them an uncanny knack of seeking out opportunities and people that will help them progress. They always seem to be one step ahead, and if they are not the source of a trend, they will use their imagination and energy to work with it or, better still, move beyond it. The upside of all this is that they are often right at the cutting edge; the downside is that they can lapse into selfish or manipulative behavior if it helps them get what they want.

Although they do possess strong ambition and a powerful influence over others, their goals are generally personal rather than global. Once they set out to achieve a goal, they will work tirelessly until it is theirs. Their emphasis on looking ahead is highlighted from childhood until around the age of thirty-nine; these are the years in which they develop their self-confidence. After forty, however, they become more relaxed about their goals, less focused on change and more on recognition and stability.

The key to their success lies in an ability to get their powerful intuition to work for rather than against them. It is their intuition that values objects, situations, or people, and their intuition which eventually teaches them to value their own judgment over everything else. Once they have settled on a course that is worthy of them — understanding that there are some things they can never control — they often use their intuition and strong willpower not only to successfully predict the future but also to play a part in creating it.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to relax the need to control everyone and everything.

The Way Forward

Understand that however important you are, no one, including yourself, is indispensable.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a passion for imaginative debate and speculation, and this can create an intense and exciting bond.

Luck Maker: Be Here Now

If you are so organized that you are busy planning and living in the future, you are missing the true pleasure of the moment. The power to attract luck your way is always in the moment.

At Your Best

Progressive, intuitive, powerful.

On the Dark Side

Domineering, gossipy, selfish.

Love: Lively and Fun

Fortunately, March 11 natives tend to be more relaxed in their relationships than in their working lives. They understand the importance of downtime and spending time with loved ones, enjoying nothing better than conversation with family and friends. Life is always fun and lively when they are around, and their partner needs to understand that discipline and routine in a relationship does not suit them well.

Health: Look at Me

People born on this day tend to be quite concerned about their appearance and may spend a lot of time shopping for new clothes, at the salon, or having treatments of some kind. Although they often look stylish and well presented, they need to remember that the foundation for good looks lies not in the mirror but in a healthy diet and exercise program. They should eat a diet rich in anti-aging nutrients and antioxidants — in other words, a diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and other unrefined and unprocessed foods — and take regular exercise such as walking, swimming, jogging, cycling, or dancing. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will help them focus less on what the future holds and more on spiritual or higher things.

Career: Born Investors

These people often make good investors or stock market traders, as they have an instinct for what will work and what will not. They also make great antique collectors. Alternatively, their love of good food may lead them toward a career in catering or nutrition, and their ability to predict the need for reform may push them toward politics, public service, labor unions, or education, as well as music, arts, entertainment, healing, or alternative therapies.

Destiny: To Make Their Work the Best It Can Be

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to value both the present and the future. Once they have learned to make their intuition work for them and harnessed their creativity, their destiny is to make whatever they are working on or involved in the best and most effective it can possibly be.

Power Thought

I can see the magick and beauty of the present moment.
