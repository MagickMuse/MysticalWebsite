Born on June 15th

The birthday of irresistible charm

Profile

June 15 people are blessed with natural charm. They can easily bring others around to their point of view, often using their seductive power to win the support of co-workers, friends, family, and indeed anyone they meet. Their charm and appeal are so strong that even the most cynical and suspicious of people find it hard to resist them.

What makes them so charming is their genuine interest in others and their remarkable ability to guess what people are thinking or feeling. This makes others feel they are on the same wavelength as these attractive individuals. Their outstanding people skills will often fast-track them into positions of influence; if they find themselves in such positions, they should devote their persuasive powers to worthy causes. They also need to ensure they don't give others the wrong impression or charm them into believing a friendship exists when it doesn't.

Until age thirty-six they focus on emotional security; during this time they should ensure they are honest with themselves and others and don't place being popular or attractive above personal achievement. After thirty-seven they become more confident, and this may encourage them to use their people skills more assertively. It is important during these years that they stay on the straight and narrow.

They have the potential to make great contributions to partnerships, groups, and society as a whole, as long as they are aware of the power they have over others. If they truly believe in the cause they are promoting, their ambition and personal charm virtually guarantee their popularity, happiness, and success.

Your Greatest Challenge

Believing in your own desirability.

The Way Forward

However many people you charm into loving or flattering you, the only way to feel truly desirable is to believe in yourself first.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. You share a love of beautiful things and intelligent debate, and this can create a refined but exciting relationship.

Luck Maker: All Change Starts with You

If you want to make a change in your life, the way to start isn't with your wardrobe, partner, or friends but in the way you think and feel about yourself.

At Your Best

Seductive, attractive, intelligent.

On the Dark Side

Calculating, misleading, unstable.

Love: Irresistible

June 15 natives are powerfully attractive as romantic partners and enjoy winning hearts with their charming words and smiles. They are attracted to beautiful, intelligent people and enjoy a good debate, but need to be careful they don't value outer over inner beauty. Once they find the perfect partner, they can be loyal, gentle, and loving.

Health: Moderation Is Key

Physical appearance matters greatly to those born on this day, which can either manifest in careful attention to diet and exercise or in extreme, obsessive behavior. They should learn that true beauty comes from within. Some may neglect their appearance in pursuit of pleasure, leading to weight problems. Moderation is key. They should eat little and often to keep energy levels boosted. Regular exercise will boost self-esteem because, despite appearances, they can suffer from nerves and insecurity. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Purple will encourage them to focus less on the material and more on higher things.

Career: Born Advertisers

These people will often find fulfillment in artistic careers, in particular drama, art, or music, where they can gather a following and bring enjoyment to others. They may also be drawn to advertising, marketing, retail, law, public relations, promotion, consultancy, agency work, and the leisure and beauty industries.

Destiny: To Uplift, Inspire, and Motivate Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to use their people skills wisely and productively. Once they find a cause worthy of them, their destiny is to use their considerable powers of intuition and perspicacity to uplift, inspire, and motivate others.

Power Thought

I now choose to recognize the brilliance of my being.
