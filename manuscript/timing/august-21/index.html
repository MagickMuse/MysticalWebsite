Born on August 21st

The birthday of the attention magnet

Profile

Those born on August 21 are among the most expressive and magnetic individuals of the year. They have a creative, dramatic approach to life and an ability to make others sit up and take notice. Others are always drawn to them and want to learn more about them because they have a charm and an energy that is virtually impossible to ignore.

Their magnetic personality means that they tend to attract a lot of attention wherever they go, and this attention fuels them further to seek ways to dazzle others. Although they have a real need for an audience, they are not merely superficial attention seekers. There is usually real substance beneath the appearance, and their aim is to communicate and share what they know. They may come across as dreamers but they are also practical, organized and methodical in their approach, and they should not be underestimated as opponents.

Until age thirty-one there is an increasing emphasis on practical order and efficiency. During these years they have an incredible ability to learn from experience and to develop professionally. After age thirty-two, however, there is a significant turning point which stimulates them to become more relationship-oriented, and their creative skills are enhanced. This is a positive time when they become more diplomatic and less confrontational.

After age sixty-one there is another turning point when they may seek deeper meaning and significance. Throughout their lives, however, they need to make sure they don't suppress or neglect their need for personal expression in favor of family or professional responsibilities. Once they have found an outlet for their considerable creative potential and a positive way to engage, educate and inspire others, they are at their happiest and have the potential to achieve remarkable things.

Your Greatest Challenge

Not losing yourself in others' expectations.

The Way Forward

Understand that your need for attention can lead you away from your authentic self; staying true to your values is the key to lasting admiration.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both adventurous and expressive, and this can create an exciting and passionate relationship.

Luck Maker: Be Yourself

Lucky people do not try to be somebody they are not. They understand that authenticity draws more luck than any amount of showmanship.

At Your Best

Charismatic, expressive, creative.

On the Dark Side

Attention-seeking, dramatic, superficial.

Love: Center of Attention

Those born on this day crave attention and admiration in their relationships. They need a partner who can offer them devotion without losing their own identity. They are passionate, generous and loyal lovers who can sometimes be overly dramatic in their relationships. Intellectual affinity with a partner who challenges them is essential.

Health: Express Yourself

These individuals need to make sure that they do not neglect their health for the sake of their career or social life. They tend to push themselves hard and may be prone to stress-related ailments. Regular exercise, especially dancing or swimming, is recommended as it will help them release tension and express themselves physically. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat regularly and not skip meals. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help them stay calm and focused.

Career: Born Entertainers

These individuals are ideally suited to the performing arts, media, entertainment, writing, journalism or public speaking. Their natural charisma and leadership qualities also make them excellent managers, politicians and educators. They may also be drawn to fashion, design, advertising and the beauty industry.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Creativity and Vision

The life path of those born on this day is to find the courage to be authentic. Once they have learned that true magnetism comes from within, their destiny is to inspire, educate and enlighten others with their creative vision.

Power Thought

I express my true self with confidence and joy.
