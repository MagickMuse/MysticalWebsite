Born on February 15th

The birthday of the enterprising adventurer

Profile

People born on February 15 are full of vitality, adventure, and fun. They love to walk on the wild side of life and bring great enthusiasm and ingenuity to everything, especially when it involves exploring something novel. When faced with a challenge, it is impossible for them to sit on the sidelines; they have to jump in and offer a solution — their solution.

The charming, energetic people born on this day have the ability to master skills quickly, using their enterprise and ingenuity to elevate a skill, craft, or project to undreamed-of heights. They like to live life in the fast lane and have no intention of slowing down. Although this approach means they are often a powerful force to be reckoned with, it can also mean they sometimes find it hard to know when to stop.

February 15 natives value their intellectual freedom above all else and like to experience or investigate just about anything. This can lead, however, to burnout or information overload; when this happens, they may come across as chaotic or like rebels without a cause. Their moods may also swing from high to low for no clear reason — a result of living in too heightened a state. It is important for them to understand that sometimes discipline and limits have great value. Fortunately, they tend to grow more self-disciplined with age; around thirty-five they can become extremely assertive, so they should channel that dynamic energy positively.

As long as they learn the importance of self-discipline and goal setting, and others allow them to explore the wonders of the world, these multi-talented individuals have the potential to realize their original and intelligent dreams. They may have an alarming wild streak that often lands them in trouble, but they are genuinely motivated by a desire to make the world a happier, more exciting place. With them around, life can be many things, but it is never dull.

Your Greatest Challenge

Paying attention to the details.

The Way Forward

Understand that details and the routine that often accompanies them are unavoidable in life; to ignore them is often to ignore something truly special.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 24 and October 23

Those dates fall under Libra. You both walk on the wild side in relationships, but you can fall deeply in love when you meet your match.

Luck Maker: Avoid Self-Destructive Behavior

To create luck you must avoid self-destructive habits and devote your energy to whatever fills your life with meaning and purpose.

At Your Best

Self-starting, curious, bold.

On the Dark Side

Critical, wild, moody.

Love: Sticky Situations

February 15 natives love adventure and variety in their relationships and are more than likely to have numerous partners, sometimes simultaneously. This can land them in all sorts of sticky situations. Those who are more self-disciplined have it within them to commit to one person, as long as that person is as willing to experiment and have fun as they are.

Health: Spontaneity

People born on this day are prone to mood swings, so learning coping techniques for their emotions is important. Mind-body therapies such as meditation, yoga, and tai chi would help them slow down and find calm. They should watch out for food allergies and sensitivities in their diet, and vigorous exercise, preferably outdoors, will help burn off some of that manic energy. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help stabilize their mood, bringing calmness and a sense of promise.

Career: Born Mountain Climbers

These people have many talents and are capable of success across a wide range of careers, but their wild physicality draws them to extreme sports, mountaineering, dancing, skating, aviation, space travel, nature trekking, and stunt work. On the safer side, they may gravitate toward teaching, writing (especially science fiction and fantasy), art, and psychology. As nature lovers, they may also be drawn to conservation, farming, or working with animals.

Destiny: To Encourage Others to a Life of Adventure

The life path of those born on this day is to learn self-discipline without crushing their wonderful spontaneity. Once they accomplish that, their destiny is to encourage others by their own example to embrace a more adventurous and open-minded spirit.

Power Thought

The harmony and balance in my mind is reflected in my life.
