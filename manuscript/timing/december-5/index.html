Born on December 5th

The birthday of the self-assured adventurer

Profile

Those born on December 5 reach for the skies both personally and professionally, and although others may think they are overreaching, somehow or other they manage to get to where they want, or at the very least close to it.

From an early age they may have displayed unusual self-confidence and willingness to go it alone. If this was not the case, life may have given them a series of knocks to dent their confidence, but sooner or later their characteristic optimism will emerge. They truly believe that anything is possible, and throughout their lives they are a shining example of what confidence and self-belief in the face of adversity can achieve. Sometimes they can be overconfident and unwilling to listen to cautionary advice; although this may result in remarkable innovations, it can also lead to serious errors of judgment.

Around age seventeen, they start to think seriously about their life goals and the mark they want to leave on the world. Although they should never lose their idealism and optimism, it is important for them to make sure goals are realistic and achievable; otherwise they are setting themselves up for disappointment. Listening to the advice of others could be the key to unlocking their potential. After forty-seven, they may become even more progressive and original in their ideas, and if they have managed to learn from past experience and improve their judgment, these are the years when they can really come into their own.

These individuals are hard to ignore or dislike, and even though their ambitions may at times be over the top, colleagues and friends will often regard them with affection and tolerance. They have a real desire to make a positive contribution to society, and once they direct their determination, focus and willpower to a worthy cause, they can and do find ways to benefit the greater good.

Your Greatest Challenge

Taking the advice of others.

The Way Forward

Understand that other people always bring different perspectives; these are always worth listening to, as you may have overlooked them.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 21 and June 20

Those dates fall under Gemini. You are both inquisitive and adventurous spirits, and you can make a great couple.

Luck Maker: Don't Get Overconfident

Although positive thinking will get you far, overconfidence will not; humility is an important part of the luck equation.

At Your Best

Self-assured, daring, energetic.

On the Dark Side

Overconfident, conceited, unaware.

Love: Love-Hate

Those born on this day long for a settled and secure relationship, but as soon as they are in one they may start to pick arguments or stir up trouble simply to keep the intensity alive. Partners may find this particularly difficult, and for long-term happiness they need to find someone who is emotionally confident and direct.

Health: Conserve Your Energy

These individuals need to conserve their energy and watch out for problems with bones and joints. Making sure they have enough calcium and magnesium in their diet is important, along with eating a healthy balanced diet rich in whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts, seeds and oily fish. They should cut down on coffee, as it can lead to insomnia, and be equally careful about excess sugar, salt, additives and preservatives. Regular exercise keeps bones and joints flexible and boosts all aspects of health, and lots of different activities are recommended to keep them interested and motivated. Spending more time with loved ones is advised, and for those times when they feel alone, a few drops of rose, clary sage or frankincense essential oil on a handkerchief will produce feelings of inner security and self-acceptance; for relaxation and a good night's sleep, they should try Lavender.

Career: Born Fashion Designers

These individuals thrive in any career where they can be constantly stimulated. They may be drawn to art, writing, music, fashion, filmmaking and the world of entertainment. They may also excel in education, research, social reform, politics and charity work.

Destiny: To Introduce Innovative Concepts to the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more realistic in their assessment of people and situations. Once they examine themselves honestly, their destiny is to introduce and promote innovative concepts.

Power Thought

Every day my capacity for understanding myself and others grows.
