The Arrhephoria is a hidden rite of two young priestesses of Athena, called the Arrhephoroi, Carriers of Unspoken Things. It is held at the beginning of Skirophorion, most likely on the third.

The Arrhephoroi

The Arrhephoroi had lived in the temple of Athena for two years and had prepared for the rite in that time. In the observance itself, they performed a number of secret rituals. Chief among these was the carrying of a package along a secret path to the sanctuary of Aphrodite in the Gardens, and the bringing back of another sealed package by the same hidden way.

After the completion of the rite, the two Arrhephoroi were replaced by two new girls, who then began their own two years of temple service in preparation for the following Arrhephoria.

On a normal calendar. The Arrhephoria falls at the beginning of Skirophorion, most likely on the third, generally landing in June of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
