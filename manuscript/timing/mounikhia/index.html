🌕Mounikhia

Notes
Festival of Artemis as moon Goddess and mistress of the animals, held on Mounikhion 6 or 16. The month itself takes its name from this festival.

The Mounikhia is the festival of Artemis as the moon Goddess and mistress of the animals, held on the sixth or the sixteenth day of Mounikhion. The month itself takes its name from this festival.

Ritual Procedure

  • A procession of girls carrying boughs came to the shrine of Apollo and Artemis.
  • A she-goat was sacrificed to the Goddess, along with other offerings.
  • A further procession consisted of people carrying amphiphontes, shining-all-around, round cakes bearing lit candles arranged in a circle.

On a normal calendar. The Mounikhia falls on the sixth or sixteenth day of Mounikhion, generally landing in April of the Gregorian calendar, and shifts each cycle.
