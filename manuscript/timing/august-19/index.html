Born on August 19th

The birthday of the editor

Profile

Those born on August 19 have the ability to make the best of any situation and improve it or refine it in some way. Whatever life presents them with, they have the ability to edit and reshape it, finding and fixing problems, and presenting an improved version.

They are sharp-witted, observant individuals with a keen eye for detail. They are often highly articulate, with the ability to express themselves clearly and persuasively. Although they are typically honest and direct in their speech, they can also be diplomatic when the need arises. Their aim is always to improve the lives of others and the world around them, and they are at their happiest when they are able to put their remarkable talents to work in this way.

There is a danger, however, that their desire to improve things can become obsessive or that they become overly critical of themselves and others. Until age thirty-three there is an emphasis on practicality and perfectionism, and during these years they need to be especially careful that their desire to improve does not become a desire to control. After age thirty-four there is a turning point when they become more aware of the importance of relationships, creativity and harmony.

After age sixty-three there is another turning point which brings an emphasis on emotional and spiritual regeneration. Whatever age they are, once they learn to balance their desire to improve things with the ability to accept things as they are, they have extraordinary potential to make a positive, lasting contribution to the world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding the urge to micromanage everything.

The Way Forward

Understand that some things do not need to be fixed; sometimes the most powerful thing you can do is simply accept and appreciate what is.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both creative, expressive and passionate, and this can create a dynamic and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Accept Imperfection

Lucky people know that imperfection is not a flaw but an opportunity for growth. Accepting imperfection in yourself and others opens the door to gratitude and luck.

At Your Best

Insightful, articulate, constructive.

On the Dark Side

Critical, controlling, obsessive.

Love: Seeking Depth

Those born on this day tend to be attracted to people who share their intellectual curiosity and passion for self-improvement. They can be very devoted partners, but they need to be careful not to try to edit or improve their loved ones. Once they find a partner who can appreciate their depth and directness, they make loyal, devoted and supportive lovers.

Health: Balance Work and Play

These individuals tend to put enormous pressure on themselves and may suffer from stress-related problems as a result. It is important for them to learn how to switch off and relax. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a varied and balanced diet and not get too obsessive about health regimes. Regular exercise is recommended, especially activities that combine mind and body such as yoga or tai chi. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will boost their optimism and sense of fun.

Career: Born Editors

These individuals excel in any career that requires attention to detail, analytical skills and the ability to improve systems. They may be drawn to editing, journalism, writing, publishing, teaching, research, science, medicine, architecture, design, engineering, management consulting or quality control.

Destiny: To Improve and Inspire the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to accept imperfection. Once they have balanced their desire to improve with their ability to appreciate what is, their destiny is to use their remarkable editorial skills to improve and inspire the world around them.

Power Thought

I appreciate the beauty and wisdom in things as they are.
