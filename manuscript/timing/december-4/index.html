Born on December 4th

The birthday of the captain

Profile

Those born on December 4 are ambitious, hardworking and resilient individuals who can display remarkable self-control in both professional and personal life. They have the rare ability to take charge of their emotions without losing their creativity, and this gives them tremendous self-confidence, power over themselves and authority over others. They are like flamboyant and daring but highly skilled and well-prepared captains with a thirst for adventure and all the courage and ingenuity they need to steer through uncharted waters to undiscovered lands.

Although they cherish their own individuality and are unwilling to submit to the ideas or authority of others, they may feel compelled to impose their ideas on those around them, sometimes forcefully. Unaware of the contradiction between their directional urges and their own right to autonomy, they can come across as dictatorial or egotistical, but this is rarely the case. For the great majority of the time they are genuinely concerned for the greater good rather than any selfish ambition. Like a brave captain who will not leave the ship until everyone is safe, their natural sense of justice and honor propels them toward activities intended to bring about a more enlightened or better-regulated society.

At age eighteen, they may start to manifest their natural leadership skills, and over the next thirty years they will gradually become more practical, goal-oriented and realistic. They may also have a strong desire for order and structure. After forty-eight, another significant turning point highlights a growing need for freedom, new ideas and expressing individuality within a group setting.

At any age, if they can find a middle way between nobility and ambition, love and success, compassion and power, independence and compromise, they will not only be capable of inspired leadership but of becoming the visionaries of their generation.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with not being listened to.

The Way Forward

Understand that authority is something that has to be earned; balance your leadership skills with a concern for the welfare of others.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 20 and February 18

Those dates fall under Aquarius. Although you are different in many ways, there is much you can learn to love about each other.

Luck Maker: Be an Energy Source

When you invite other people into your limelight or offer them recognition, you become an energy source and others will want to keep you center stage. The outcome of your generosity will be new opportunities.

At Your Best

Powerful, ambitious, inspirational.

On the Dark Side

Authoritarian, hypocritical, inflexible.

Love: Give and Take

Those born on this day rarely have problems attracting potential partners, but long-term relationships may prove elusive. It is important for them to learn the importance of give and take, and to find a balance between idyllic romantic optimism and practical reality. Once they commit, they need a partner who gives them the freedom they need to feel alive.

Health: Healthy Balance

These individuals tend to have an optimistic approach to life and are not prone to depression. There will be times, however, when they feel tired or run down, and they should learn to pace themselves and take regular vacations. They also need to master the art of delegation, because allowing others to help eases their workload and gives them time for a healthy balance of interests outside of work. Meditation techniques are highly recommended for the sense of calm, peace and balance they bring. Their diet should include less sugar and processed food and more whole grains, fruits and vegetables. Moderate to vigorous exercise is recommended, particularly team sports and outdoor activities. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple encourages them to think about higher things and brings a true sense of harmony, peace and balance.

Career: Born Conservationists

These individuals may be drawn to politics or may choose to promote their ideological beliefs through the arts. Other options include business, commerce, advertising, sport, agriculture, conservation, management and the world of entertainment.

Destiny: To Advance the Greater Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to listen to others' views and find a balance between idealism and ambition. Once they achieve their goals while retaining the affection and respect of those around them, their destiny is to advance the greater good.

Power Thought

In my world everyone is a winner.
