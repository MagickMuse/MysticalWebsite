Born on December 24th

The birthday of complicated far sight

Profile

Those born on December 24 seem destined to live complicated, uncertain but exciting and fast-moving lives. Life is never straightforward or stress-free for them, but they have a knack for rising above challenges and achieving great success.

There are many reasons why life can feel unnecessarily stressful for these individuals. They can find it hard to react with tact and diplomacy, and they are not always good at learning from their mistakes. They also have a gift for seeing into the future, for knowing which methods will or will not work. In this respect they could be called visionary, but unfortunately it takes a while for others, and for themselves, to recognize and appreciate that gift of far-sightedness. Until that recognition arrives, others will wonder why they insist on making life so difficult, and they themselves will wonder why everything always feels so complicated.

Until age twenty-seven, they tend to place practical considerations and a desire for order and security first, but after twenty-eight, things begin to change and they feel a growing need for independence and self-expression. After fifty-eight, more emphasis falls on emotional receptivity, and these are the years when their intuitive potential may develop into psychic ability.

At any age, the key to success is their ability to learn from their mistakes and be more sensitive and tactful with others, especially those who recognize their potential and want to help. If a dose of self-confidence is added to the mix, life starts to feel much easier and more rewarding. When all this comes together, they finally see their potential clearly and attract considerable success and happiness.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning from your mistakes.

The Way Forward

Understand that if an approach does not work the first time around, it will not work the second time either unless changes are made.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 20 and May 20

Those dates fall under Taurus. You are both sensual and dramatic individuals, and if you remain loyal, this can be a fulfilling and intense union.

Luck Maker: Show People How Help Helps

When you ask people for advice or input, keep them updated on your progress. Something as simple as an update can keep them motivated to continue sending opportunities your way.

At Your Best

Innovative, visionary, exciting.

On the Dark Side

Confusing, tactless, stubborn.

Love: Magnetic

Those born on this day can be extremely attractive in a sensual way, and potential partners find them honest, romantic and exciting. Because they are so sensitive, demonstrations of love and affection are especially important and can help them feel calmer and less confused. Although they may have many partners, their need for security helps them settle down and commit when they find the right person.

Health: Building Self-Confidence

The lives of these individuals can be very complicated emotionally, and as a result they may suffer from stress, anxiety and occasional depression. Learning to accept and manage their emotions is crucial; once they understand that they are in control of how they feel and that their emotions do not control them, life improves immeasurably. They should steer clear of recreational drugs and focus on building self-confidence and self-worth. Their diet should aim for variety, and vigorous exercise at least four or five times a week helps them deal with pent-up emotions. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue gives them the objectivity they need to make better decisions.

Career: Born Innovators

They may work as technical, commercial, political or educational innovators, or become pioneers in the arts. Other options include writing, teaching, acting, politics and the world of entertainment. They may also be attracted to the study of philosophy, metaphysics or mysticism.

Destiny: To Point the Way Toward Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn from the past, not repeat it. Once they have started to build their self-esteem, their destiny is to better themselves and, by doing so, to better society and point the way toward progress.

Power Thought

I will grow wiser and stronger emotionally every day.
