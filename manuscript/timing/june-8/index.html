Born on June 8th

The birthday of expectation

Profile

People born on June 8 are honest and direct in their approach to life. They speak their mind and, because they have such high expectations of themselves and others, despise laziness or injustice in any form.

Although highly independent and quite happy to work alone, they will often find themselves in leadership positions. They have a strong sense of fair play and understand the importance of rules — as long as those rules are their own. They set an inspiring example through wholehearted dedication to their life's work. There is a danger, however, that their dedication can tip into workaholism.

Until age forty-three they focus on home and emotional life. Their basic nature is personable and fun, so it is important they don't let spontaneity disappear. After forty-four they become more self-expressive and assertive, but need to ensure they don't become preachy or overly zealous about work. The key to their success is balancing emotional needs with their strong sense of responsibility.

The integrity, industriousness, and devotion they are capable of enable them to blaze a pioneering trail. Once they develop empathy and greater tolerance for vulnerabilities, the high expectations of fulfillment they have always held will be realized.

Your Greatest Challenge

Knowing how to relax.

The Way Forward

Time out is not time wasted but time gained. You will return to your work with greater enthusiasm, energy, and clarity.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between April 21 and May 21

Those dates fall under Taurus. You are both loyal, hardworking, and romantic, and this can be a rewarding and fulfilling relationship.

Luck Maker: Anchor Your Goals in Reality

Be realistically optimistic in your goal setting. Clear goals within reason attract luck.

At Your Best

Independent, honest, dedicated.

On the Dark Side

Workaholic, stressed, judgmental.

Love: Fun to Be Around

June 8 natives are great fun, but their long absences and commitment to work may put relationships at risk. They need to set aside time for relationships, cultivate a sense of play, and find ways to express their feelings without letting hidden insecurities manifest as judgmental behavior.

Health: An Active Life

Exercise is vital for those born on this day. It will boost their immunity and self-esteem and force them to take time for their well-being. There is a tendency toward workaholism that, if unchecked, could lead to stress and depression. A healthy diet rich in fruits, vegetables, oily fish, nuts, and seeds, along with time relaxing with loved ones, will help them feel balanced. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Orange will increase feelings of warmth, physical enjoyment, and security.

Career: Born Architects

These people are well equipped for science-related careers such as research and computer programming, but design-related careers such as architecture, landscaping, and planning are also favored. Other careers include law, education, banking, or accountancy, and there is potential for success in show business, art, and music.

Destiny: To Influence and Guide Others with Their Perception

The life path of those born on this day is to balance their sense of integrity and fair play with a more liberal approach to life. Once they find this balance, their destiny is to influence and guide others with their incisive powers of perception.

Power Thought

Every day I will keep a clear focus on what is truly important.
