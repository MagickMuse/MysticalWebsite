Born on April 1st

The birthday of quiet confidence

Profile

Despite the reputation of their birthday, people born on April 1 are far from being April Fools. They often display wisdom and a quiet confidence well in advance of their years. They were the children on whom parents and teachers knew they could rely, and in their adult life they continue to be dependable, always showing up punctually and giving one hundred percent.

Although they have a reputation for reliability and responsibility, these people are rarely plodding or dull, possessing a youthful and wholesome appeal that draws others to them. Incapable of hiding their emotions, their emotional spontaneity earns them many admirers; their natural shyness and reserve may, however, make it hard for them to respond to and appreciate this attention. They also have a great need for privacy and personal space, loving to sit thinking for long hours and coming up with highly original plans and projects.

The aura of quiet confidence and honesty with which these people are blessed is capable of inspiring enormous faith and trust in others. They make excellent leaders, but their motive is never a desire for self-aggrandizement, as it is the work itself rather than being the center of attention that inspires them. All they really want is to be allowed to get on with their work, since a job well done gives them enormous satisfaction.

Although their outstanding ability to focus on their work assures success in virtually any field, they do need to be careful that in the process they do not become isolated workaholics, especially between the ages of nineteen and forty-nine, during which they seek stability, security, and routine. After the age of fifty, however, they move toward new interests, learning, and communication.

The one similarity between the archetypal jester and these mature souls may be the sense of affection they inspire in others. Their responsible and quietly confident approach to life and lack of desire to promote themselves — unless thought necessary for the task at hand — is a true joy to behold.

Your Greatest Challenge

Coping with work and the requests of others.

The Way Forward

Learn to delegate your responsibilities and stop expecting so much from yourself.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You both have qualities the other needs to learn and develop to feel truly fulfilled.

Luck Maker: Take Care Whom You Help

If you are exhausted because you have taken on too much, you will block luck from your life. Anger and negative thoughts may take over, or you may be too tired to seize opportunities.

At Your Best

Focused, responsible, genuine.

On the Dark Side

Shy, workaholic, isolated.

Love: An Open Heart

April 1 natives have an extremely warm and open heart that is incapable of deception, but they need to be careful not to put their relationships at risk because of their obsession with work. They tend to be attracted to intelligent people who can give them the mental stimulation they need, but they thrive best with someone who can offer affection and warmth.

Health: Keep Active

Regular exercise is extremely important for those born on this day, as they have a tendency to choose professions that keep them mentally active but physically sedentary. They should try to schedule at least half an hour of activity a day; walking, jogging, swimming, and cycling are all activities that suit, as they can also use this time to be alone and gather their thoughts. As far as diet is concerned, they need to remember to eat regularly and healthily, and not get so carried away with their current project that they eat on the go or forget to eat altogether. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Red will encourage them to open up and respond more freely to others.

Career: Born Teachers

These people command respect with their quiet confidence, insight, and brilliant organizational skills; for this reason they would excel in politics, education, management, administration, merchandising, or the military. They have a sharp business sense but may also use their great imagination to excel in art, music, or drama. So varied are their talents, however, that they usually excel in any career they choose.

Destiny: To Find Practical Solutions to Problems

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to trust and to speak their mind. Once they have learned to open up, their destiny is to find practical solutions to problems and to see others benefit from these solutions.

Power Thought

I choose to be self-sufficient in loving, joyous ways.
