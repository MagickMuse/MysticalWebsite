Born on December 12th

The birthday of the outspoken teacher

Profile

Those born on December 12 often feel that they have an important message to deliver to the world, one that they believe will help others progress and learn. They also long to broaden their mind through study and travel, and as well as being mentally agile they tend to be physically agile, enjoying moving from place to place or experience to experience.

Their insatiable appetite for knowledge and experience is heavily influenced by a powerful desire to make a tangible, beneficial and enlightening contribution to the lives of others. Colleagues and friends will admire their mental dexterity and their ability to identify areas in need of improvement and communicate their remedial visions in truly memorable ways.

Until age thirty-nine, there is an emphasis on the need for order and structure. These are the years when they are most likely to feel boxed in or tied down, and the struggle between the desire to establish themselves and their thirst for adventure can be complicating and confusing. After forty, an important turning point makes them even more experimental, and the drive toward freedom is particularly strong. They may suddenly feel the need to make dramatic changes in personal and professional life.

These individuals must never forget that they possess a tremendous amount of willpower, and when they finally devote themselves to a worthy career with clear goals, they have all the ambition and talent they need to succeed. If they can channel some energy inward to develop their intuition and spirituality, they will draw on their extensive knowledge and life experience to realize their ambition of delivering an empowering message of hope, love and positive expectation to the world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Overcoming the feeling of being boxed in.

The Way Forward

Understand that until you can generate a sense of freedom and excitement from within, no matter how many times you change your circumstances you will sooner or later feel trapped.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 22 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both free-spirited, easygoing and spontaneous, and this can be a passionate and exciting partnership.

Luck Maker: Stop Hoping and Start Doing

If you are waiting for good luck to change your life, you may wait a long time. Stop hoping and start doing. Make good things happen by putting practical plans in place.

At Your Best

Informative, interesting, dramatic.

On the Dark Side

Scattered, unaware, materialistic.

Love: Let's Get Physical

Whether conventionally attractive or not, those born on this day often have a seductive voice and a powerful, dramatic physical presence. They can use these to their advantage when attracting potential partners. They will probably have many relationships, but once they finally find a partner they want to commit to, they will fully dedicate themselves to making it work.

Health: Preventative Health

These individuals can sometimes think they have inherited poor health and are likely to succumb to the same conditions as their parents. In the great majority of cases their fears are unfounded, and practicing preventative medicine can reduce or eliminate their risk. Once they understand the connection between current lifestyle habits and long-term health risks, it is often enough to motivate positive changes: a fresh, nutritious diet and a consistent exercise routine. They benefit greatly from grounding and calming activities such as tai chi and yoga, and mental disciplines such as meditation. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Purple encourages them to look within for a sense of excitement.

Career: Born Advisors

These individuals have a message for the world, and careers that allow them to impart knowledge, such as lecturing, teaching, writing, counseling, advising, politics, coaching and training, will appeal. They may also be drawn to advertising, sales, media, publishing, theater or the arts.

Destiny: To Educate, Advise and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to balance the need for freedom with the need to establish themselves. Once they add a spiritual dimension to their lives, their destiny is to educate, advise and inspire others.

Power Thought

My intuition is the source of my knowledge and power.
