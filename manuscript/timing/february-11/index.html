Born on February 11th

The birthday of the upgrader

Profile

Those born on February 11 feel they were sent into this world for one purpose: to improve the lives of everyone around them. In their eyes, both people and things are always in need of improvement or an upgrade. They often carry an earnest quality and a drive that makes others want to learn from them. They also have a talent for invention and enjoy finding new ways to make life easier for those in their circle.

They do this not for material reward or even recognition, but because they believe that the less stress and discomfort people endure, the more they can devote themselves to more meaningful and spiritual pursuits.

Although they prefer to motivate by example rather than words, February 11 natives have a knack for making others feel good about themselves. Their curious, inventive minds need constant intellectual stimulation, yet their current absorption in a project or social group should not lead them to neglect close personal relationships. Thinking of themselves as improvers and educators, they should understand that not everyone appreciates or wants their help. Some people prefer to work things out on their own and may become resentful if someone else tries to point things out, especially in a blunt manner. It is important for those born on this day to develop their intuition and sensitivity. Fortunately, between the ages of nineteen and thirty-eight there is an emphasis on emotional sensitivity, but after thirty-nine directness takes center stage, making it more important than ever to channel that energy positively.

With their inventive mind and sharp insight into what a situation or person needs, there is little doubt that February 11 people, as long as they master the art of diplomacy, will make a mark on the world by helping and educating others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Allowing others to do things their own way.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes the only way for other people to learn is through making mistakes and finding their own solutions.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a passion for self-improvement and a need for security, and this can create a tender, considerate bond.

Luck Maker: Sometimes Say "I Didn't Know That"

Nobody likes a know-it-all. Demonstrating a sincere openness and willingness to understand and learn from others will naturally draw people to you.

At Your Best

Progressive, inventive, knowing.

On the Dark Side

Tactless, self-indulgent, excessive.

Love: Loyalty and Trust

February 11 natives value their personal freedom and need room to breathe in a relationship, but they are also firm believers in loyalty and trust. They are easygoing and entertaining, and their need for intellectual stimulation will often lead them toward unorthodox or unusual individuals. They love tenderly and passionately, and will use their strong will to help and support those they care about.

Health: The High Life

People born on this day often find it tough to understand why others do not share their point of view, which can cause considerable psychological stress. The support of a partner, family, and friends will help them work through this, though they may also benefit from counseling. There is also a danger that their love for the high life can lead to weight issues and risky adventures that harm their health. Although a healthy diet and regular exercise routine may be hard to maintain, they need to understand the strong links between mental, emotional, and physical well-being. Meditating on an Amethyst crystal or wearing and surrounding themselves with the color violet will help keep their mood balanced and their step lively.

Career: Born Inventors

These people are attracted to any career involving education or personal and social development. They are inventive and progressive, making great inventors, computer programmers, and architects. In business, employers will appreciate their willingness to embrace new ideas, and they also work well as advisors, specialists, or freelancers. Alternatively, they may devote themselves to charity work, psychology, philosophy, or spirituality, where they can make a real difference in people's lives.

Destiny: To Break New Ground and Experiment

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that everyone, including themselves, needs to stumble now and again as it is an essential part of the human experience. Once they learn to be less critical of themselves and others, their destiny is to break new ground and experiment with original concepts.

Power Thought

I am aware that what I do not want to change is what I need to change.
