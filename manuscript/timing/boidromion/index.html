Boidromion

Boidromion is the third month of the ancient Athenian calendar. The name is drawn from an epithet of Apollo, meaning to help in response to a shout, honouring the God as a rescuer in war.

Major Festivals

  • Genesia on the 5th, the state festival in honour of the dead.
  • Kharisteria on the 6th, the feast of Artemis Agrotera.
  • Boidromia on the 7th, thanksgiving to Apollo as saviour in battle.
  • Eleusinian Mysteries on the 15th through the 21st, the mystery rites of Demeter and Persephone at Eleusis.

On a normal calendar. Boidromion generally falls in September, and shifts each cycle relative to the Gregorian calendar.
