Born on May 9th

The birthday of the activist

Profile

People born on May 9 fight passionately for what they believe in. Natural humanitarians, they strive to improve the lives of those around them and, when they witness injustice or corruption, they will not hesitate to take direct action. They are gifted with the ability to identify the core of a problem and form a strategy to overcome it.

Although they can sometimes appear confrontational, their intentions are always good. They want to bring about positive change and will use whatever methods they can. Between ages twelve and forty-two there is an emphasis on new ideas and learning, and they should use these years to develop their communication skills so they can express their ideas without alienating people. After forty-two there is a turning point where emotional security and home life become more important.

Their greatest strength is their ability to fight courageously for the rights of others. Their greatest weakness is their tendency to become so focused on their cause that they lose touch with their own feelings and the feelings of those closest to them. They need to remember that charity begins at home.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding tunnel vision.

The Way Forward

Understand that if you neglect your personal life, you risk the very relationships that give you strength.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You are both passionate and committed individuals, and this can create a deeply supportive and loving union.

Luck Maker: Be Open to New Approaches

Lucky people know that there is always more than one way to solve a problem. Be open to new ideas and approaches, even if they challenge your existing beliefs.

At Your Best

Courageous, dedicated, compassionate.

On the Dark Side

Confrontational, obsessive, neglectful.

Love: Passionate and Loyal

May 9 natives are passionate and loyal partners who need a mate who can match their intensity. They are drawn to strong, independent people who have their own passions and causes. Once committed, they are devoted and protective, though they need to ensure they make time for romance alongside their crusades.

Health: Channel Your Energy

Those born on this day have abundant energy that needs constructive outlets. Vigorous exercise such as martial arts, running, or competitive sports is ideal. They should be careful not to skip meals when absorbed in their work, and should maintain a balanced diet rich in whole grains, fruits, and vegetables. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Red will boost their vitality and passion.

Career: Born Activists

Their strong sense of justice and desire to make a difference draws them to careers in law, politics, social work, charity, and campaigning. They may also thrive in journalism, education, or any field where they can advocate for change. Their strategic thinking also suits them to business leadership and management.

Destiny: To Champion the Rights of Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their public mission with their private needs. Once they achieve this equilibrium, their destiny is to champion the rights and welfare of those who cannot fight for themselves.

Power Thought

I nurture my relationships as passionately as I fight for my beliefs.
