Best Day for Magick

Tuesday is the best day of the week to perform magick for drive, confidence, ambition, victory, and vitality.

Lunar Magick

Tuesday is ruled by Mars and supports workings around vitality, physical energy, action, and passion. When favorable lunar timing is days away, Tuesday can provide a boost for workings that need drive or momentum. Combining Tuesday with a waxing moon amplifies energy and initiative; pair it with a full moon for bold, decisive magick.

Historical Context

Before powerful telescopes, only seven heavenly bodies could be observed from earth. Early people believed gods and goddesses inhabited these bodies, and each deity's power reigned supreme on one of the seven days. By scheduling activities on a day when the ruling deity matched your intention, you increase your chances of success.

Dark Magick Timing

Tuesday stands as one of the two most potent days for baneful and destructive workings. The aggressive, combative force of Mars fuels hexes, curses, and offensive spellcraft. The very first planetary hour at sunrise on Tuesday belongs to Mars, making dawn the single most concentrated window for this kind of work. On other days of the week, performing dark workings during a planetary hour of Mars still channels the same fiery, martial current. Pairing Tuesday with a waxing Mars amplifies the intensity of any harmful working.
