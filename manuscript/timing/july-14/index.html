Born on July 14th

The birthday of the illusionist

Profile

Those born on July 14 are fascinating and complex individuals who have the ability to create powerful illusions and impressions. They are masters of perception, understanding intuitively how to present themselves and their ideas in the most compelling and persuasive way.

Although their ability to influence and persuade can sometimes be used manipulatively, at their best they use their gifts to inspire and enlighten others. They are deeply creative and have a vivid imagination. Between the ages of thirty-eight and sixty-eight there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to make sure they use their persuasive abilities ethically and honestly. After the age of sixty-eight there is a turning point highlighting relationships and creativity.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being authentic.

The Way Forward

Understand that the most powerful impression you can make is one that is genuine. Authenticity is more compelling than any illusion.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 24 and September 24

Those dates fall under Virgo. You are both detail-oriented and perceptive, and this can create a grounded and deeply satisfying relationship.

Luck Maker: Be Honest with Yourself

Lucky people understand that self-deception is the greatest obstacle to good fortune. Being honest with yourself about your strengths and weaknesses is the foundation of lasting luck.

At Your Best

Persuasive, creative, imaginative.

On the Dark Side

Manipulative, deceptive, elusive.

Love: Enchanting and Mysterious

Those born on this day are enchanting and mysterious partners who can make others feel as though they are under a spell. They need a partner who can see beyond the illusion to the real person underneath and who values honesty and authenticity above all else.

Health: Stay Grounded

Those born on this day can sometimes lose touch with reality because of their vivid imagination. It is important for them to stay grounded and connected to the physical world. Regular exercise, especially outdoor activities such as hiking, gardening or walking, is highly recommended. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat wholesome, natural foods and avoid processed or artificial foods. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will help them stay grounded and connected to reality.

Career: Born Storytellers

These individuals are natural storytellers and may excel in writing, acting, film, advertising, marketing or public relations. Their understanding of perception and persuasion may also draw them to psychology, counseling, law or politics. Their creativity may lead them to careers in art, music or design.

Destiny: To Use Their Gifts to Inspire and Enlighten

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to use their remarkable gifts of persuasion and perception honestly and ethically. Once they have done that, their destiny is to inspire and enlighten others with their creativity and insight.

Power Thought

My greatest power lies in being genuinely and unapologetically myself.
