Born on December 9th

The birthday of the dashing hero

Profile

Those born on December 9 are blessed with a fertile imagination. From childhood they will have cast themselves in the role of the dashing hero swooping in to save the day and astonishing everyone with acts of daring and courage. In adulthood they often become highly energetic individuals who love to be the center of attention.

They have a powerful urge to make important and inspirational contributions to the world, and with their imaginative mind, fixity of purpose and adventurous spirit they possess outstanding pioneering potential. Born leaders, their biggest challenge is patience. Their patience can easily snap when things are not going exactly the way they want or when people are not listening to their suggestions. They need to learn when to promote their progressive ideals and when to step back and let things unfold. Calming their emotional intensity in response to any challenge is crucial for their psychological growth and their chances of success.

Although they may have been shy as children, by the time they reach their twenties they will start to come out of their shells; if they cannot manage this, developing self-confidence is crucial, because they were born to be in the limelight. Until age forty-two, there are opportunities to find realistic ways to achieve their goals. They need to take advantage of these; otherwise their dreams of progress and adventure will never move beyond the planning stage. After forty-three, a turning point may bring a desire to be more independent and to express their individuality.

At whatever age or stage, they have an all-encompassing, albeit occasionally self-centered, urge to play a leading role in the lives of those around them. If they can implement their original and progressive visions, they have the potential to make not just an imaginative but a real and significant contribution to the world.

Your Greatest Challenge

Patience.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes you cannot force progress; the best approach is simply to bide your time.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. This is a tremendously energetic and passionate relationship with the potential to last.

Luck Maker: Give Others Credit

Lucky people give credit and share their success, because when you recognize someone for the help they have given you, they are more likely to think well of you and steer opportunities your way.

At Your Best

Progressive, romantic, dynamic.

On the Dark Side

Selfish, controlling, unrealistic.

Love: Emotional Intensity

Those born on this day are romantic, ardent and passionate lovers, but for long-term success they need to find someone who can show them the joys of the simpler, quieter things in life. Although routine and responsibilities are not for them and they need freedom to travel and explore, when they find a partner who is romantic and adventurous but also solid and dependable, they can be surprisingly loyal and supportive.

Health: Pent-Up Energy

These individuals tend to be high-energy people who live fast and eat fast. This can cause digestion problems, so it is important to slow down, sit down and chew food carefully. Eating on the go leads to higher-calorie foods loaded with fat, additives and preservatives. For psychological and physical health they also need to control their temper; when blinded by anger they can become accident-prone. If they cannot find ways to deal with anger, it can lead to stress and frustration. Anger-management techniques are recommended for severe cases; for milder cases, meditation, breathing techniques or cognitive behavioral therapy will help. Regular vigorous exercise, running in particular, is recommended to release pent-up tension. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue helps them stay more in control of their emotions.

Career: Born Lawyers

These individuals may be drawn to the political or scientific spheres, or to artistic media such as music, writing, literature or drama. They love to express ideas, so they may also gravitate toward education, law, business, journalism, social reform and the world of entertainment.

Destiny: To Lead Others Toward Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be calmer and more realistic in their approach. Once they have taken charge of their emotions, their destiny is to lead others to implement original and progressive changes.

Power Thought

My positive and patient thoughts keep me in harmony with the universe.
