Born on January 31st

The birthday of the bright spirit

Profile

Those born on January 31 carry an overwhelming need to be noticed, heard, and taken seriously. And because more often than not these bright, appealing individuals achieve that goal with ease, they are admired by others for their creativity, vision, and originality.

Strong willpower, steadfastness, and an emphasis on self-expression define people born on this day. They may also be quite progressive, with a touch of genius about them. Although they can appear absent-minded and chaotic at times, this is only because their thoughts are always racing ahead, their minds brimming with inventive ideas and concepts.

When they feel they have made some kind of breakthrough, they run the risk of getting overexcited, but others tend to find this endearing rather than annoying. People born on this day are generally well liked for being so inventive and entertaining in their relentless quest for knowledge. They are magnetic personalities, but they harbor a tendency to be occasionally oversensitive, reading hidden meanings into the actions and words of others. When they feel upstaged, taken advantage of, or let down, they may overreact and either withdraw completely into depression or startle everyone around them with a razor-sharp tongue. They need to learn to bring less intensity to their relationships and to accept that sometimes others want to share the spotlight.

Occasionally those born on this day may feel pressured to conform to others' expectations in order to be liked, and by doing so they risk losing their unique charm. Fortunately, around the age of twenty a turning point suggests they are developing greater self-reliance, and at fifty another turning point highlights their fighting spirit and emotional resilience.

Above all, January 31 natives are bright spirits with the ability to light up the world with their bubbly personalities and brilliance. Once they learn to truly value themselves, they have the potential not only to bring great happiness to the world but also to influence and inspire.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting the urge to lose interest when others do not give you their heartfelt support.

The Way Forward

Do not jump to something new. Trust your instincts and make your own decision about what is or is not right for you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. The two of you enjoy defying and surprising public opinion, and this creates a magnetic, mutually supportive bond.

Luck Maker: Take a Stand

Those who attract good fortune are certain about what they want. It is this certainty, not the approval of others, that gives them the strength and determination to achieve their dreams.

At Your Best

Appealing, original, strong.

On the Dark Side

Uncertain, suspicious, groveling.

Love: Bubbling Over

January 31 natives throw themselves into relationships with bubbling enthusiasm. They make endlessly fascinating and entertaining partners, but also incredibly supportive and loyal ones. Their partner may find it hard to get a word in, and it is important for them to learn to calm down and listen from time to time. If they find a lover who encourages them to show their serious side as well as their playful one, a passionate and intense connection is possible.

Health: The Color Blue

Difficulties in their emotional life, particularly with friends and loved ones, can affect the health of January 31 natives and manifest as depression, low self-esteem, or insecurity. It is important for them to learn to feel comfortable with and express their feelings to family and friends. Counseling or alternative therapies such as meditation, which highlight the mind's power to reshape how they feel both emotionally and physically, can be highly beneficial. They need to ensure they eat a healthy, balanced diet, as they may be prone to disordered eating. Fairly vigorous exercise such as aerobics or jogging will help calm the mind. Meditating on, wearing, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help stabilize their mood and bring a sense of calm.

Career: Born Consultants

These people work well as philosophers, teachers, consultants, healers, writers, and academics, as they have a love of gathering knowledge and are expert at sharing it with others. Once they master their own insecurities, they also make outstanding counselors and champions of social or humanitarian reform. They may choose to channel their creativity into the entertainment world or the arts, particularly poetry or songwriting.

Destiny: To Bring Great Joy to the World

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to rely less on the reactions of others and more on their own instincts. Once they learn to trust themselves, their destiny is to bring great joy to the world with their charm, wit, and insight.

Power Thought

I have a mission and I choose to accept it.
