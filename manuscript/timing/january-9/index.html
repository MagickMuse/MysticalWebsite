Born on January 9

The birthday of the striver

Profile

People born on January 9 move fast in thought, feeling, and action. They want the top and will do whatever it takes to reach it. In both career and home life, they accept nothing less than the best. They expect high standards from themselves and from everyone around them, and they have a visceral contempt for mediocrity. At the same time, their fierce attachment to personal initiative and freedom often leads them to work or push ahead alone rather than in a group.

They are so locked onto the next goal that they rarely pause to enjoy what they have already achieved, or even the moment they are in. Unwinding feels almost impossible. A partner, a close friend, or even a pet becomes essential to help them lighten up and stop taking themselves and their ambitions quite so seriously. Around age forty-two, and sometimes earlier, their sensitivity to others and their interior life begins to take on greater weight.

January 9 natives excel at surmounting obstacles and can rebound from practically anything. But the recovery process can also reveal a ruthless edge. When they are knocked down, they will claw back up by any route available, even if it means hurting trusted friends and collecting enemies along the way. One of the real secrets of success is not making enemies in the first place, and they need to learn that. Anger, occasionally explosive, is often their first reflex. If they can step back and cultivate some objectivity, they will see that frustrating circumstances almost always have other exits.

Utterly fearless and loaded with admirable qualities, these people need only to listen to their conscience and work as hard at staying balanced as they do at winning. When they manage that, nothing stops them from enjoying the liberating rewards of a purposeful life sparkling with happiness and excitement.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning not to lose your temper.

The Way Forward

Take a break. Go for a walk. A nap can clear things out; so can chatting with friends or putting yourself in someone else's shoes. If you are going to lose it, keep your cool until you can blow off steam somewhere safe.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You share a mutual energy and an adventurous approach to life and to love, making this a hot and passionate union.

Luck Maker: Do Not Burn Your Bridges

One of the most effective ways to avoid bad luck is to have as few enemies as possible.

At Your Best

Ambitious, forceful, resilient.

On the Dark Side

Ruthless, mistrustful, reckless.

Love: Ardent Lovers

January 9 natives are passionate and ardent in love. Forceful and energetic, they need a partner who understands and can match their physical intensity. They hold an idealistic vision of relationships and believe in finding a soul mate. Family is deeply important to them, and they are capable of making enormous sacrifices for those they love.

Health: Take More Time Out

Their habit of pushing themselves relentlessly makes them vulnerable to stress, exhaustion, and a weakened immune system. If they neglect self-care, they can lose significant time to illness and infection. Regular breaks, a balanced diet, and all the nutrients needed to stay robust are non-negotiable. For exercise, gentler routines are recommended; they possess a dangerous attraction to extreme, high-risk sports. They may resist a daily walk or even a proper holiday, but both are essential for restoring equilibrium. A cup of lemon balm herbal tea supports digestion and encourages calm.

Career: Born Experimenters

The drive to advance means they need work that offers growth, variety, or excitement. In business, marketing and advertising may pull them in. Politics, the media, the arts, and any career heavy on travel and challenge also appeal. Astronomy and computer programming hold particular fascination. They are likely to experiment across several fields before settling into and excelling at their chosen vocation.

Destiny: To Break Through Barriers

Ambitious and driven, January 9 natives lead by example, demonstrating that going the extra mile really does make a difference. Once they learn to fix their attention on the present moment rather than the future prize, their destiny is to demolish outmoded patterns of behavior and, in doing so, strive to make the world a better place.

Power Thought

I have a peaceful and calming heart and soul.
