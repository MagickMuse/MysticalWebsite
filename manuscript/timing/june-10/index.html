Born on June 10th

The birthday of dichotomy

Profile

People born on June 10 are gifted but extreme individuals with strongly held views they are not afraid to express. They have an abundance of talent and ideas, and boundless energy to put them to good use. Everything about them exudes vitality, confidence, and charisma, but despite this, they can suffer from bouts of crippling insecurity and self-doubt.

They are experts at keeping up a happy front in both their professional and personal lives, but underneath they have a serious and intense mind prone to negativity and worry. This dichotomy between public persona and personal fears splits their personality, making it hard for them to feel truly happy. They are unwilling to acknowledge their insecurities because it would mean admitting them to themselves.

Until age forty-one they may focus on emotional security, home, and family. They should take advantage of these opportunities to get in touch with their feelings and build a network of close friends. After forty-two they enter a period of increasing self-confidence, authority, and self-expression.

Above all, they should not underestimate their ability to cope. They are capable of bold and daring action once they confront their obstacles directly. Once they find the courage to face their inner fears, they will discover a powerhouse of creativity and star potential to achieve not just one, but all of their amazing dreams.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being honest with yourself.

The Way Forward

Overcoming the worries that plague your peace of mind is the only way to move forward.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 24 and August 23

Those dates fall under Leo. You share a passion for glamour, art, and closeness, and this can create a fulfilling and intense union.

Luck Maker: Buy a Lucky Charm

Lucky charms don't carry special magick, but they can bring you luck because you expect that they will. A positive attitude to luck attracts luck.

At Your Best

Gifted, warm, daring.

On the Dark Side

Erratic, confused, self-destructive.

Love: Make Up Your Mind

June 10 natives do have a tendency to have more than one love interest, but for their own happiness they need to commit to one special person. They are often attracted to intelligent personalities who can help them develop a more serious attitude to love.

Health: Self-Destructive Tendencies

Those born on this day have self-destructive tendencies and need to stay away from recreational drugs and excessive alcohol. Depression is a danger, and the way to avoid it is to surround themselves with optimistic and positive people. They should aim for variety in their diet with as many cooked meals as possible. Exercise is essential for releasing pent-up anger and boosting mood, with brisk walking particularly recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Green will help them feel less split and more balanced.

Career: Born Performers

These people have a natural flair for the dramatic and may be drawn to careers in entertainment, acting, music, art, or writing. They may also be attracted to politics, social reform, education, or sales and marketing. Whatever career they choose, their magnetic personality will guarantee them success.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Bold Originality

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to believe in themselves. Once they have developed a core of inner strength, their destiny is to inspire others with their bold originality and daring.

Power Thought

I choose to see myself in a positive light.
