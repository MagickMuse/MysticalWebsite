Born on June 27th

The birthday of protective conviction

Profile

People born on June 27 tend to be watchful, diligent, and very capable of defending themselves and their interests from attack. They are competitive, driven, and persuasive, and those who dare to criticize or argue with their convictions may find themselves out of their depth.

They feel they have a duty to guide and, if necessary, compel others to follow the same uncompromising moral convictions they themselves hold. The profound empathy they feel for those less fortunate arouses their fiercely protective instincts and a burning desire to effect social improvement. Such steadfastness does, however, have its drawbacks: they tend to become inflexible and overly defensive when others try to offer opinions or criticize them. If this happens, their favored response is to withdraw, shutting out everyone in the process.

Emotions and family matters may occupy them into their mid-twenties, and they should take advantage of opportunities to become more sensitive to the feelings of others. Although they may appear self-assured, the solid confidence they seek may not come until after age twenty-five. It is important during these years that they keep their minds and hearts open, avoiding becoming too defensive or inflexible, which could provoke unnecessary rifts. After fifty-five they become more practical, analytical, and discerning.

The single-mindedness of these people can also mean they miss out on opportunities to develop new assets or relationships. It is crucial for their psychological development that they remain open to debates, because learning to become more accommodating is the key to their personal happiness and fulfillment.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with criticism.

The Way Forward

Constructive criticism can be enormously helpful because it can help you learn, improve, and fine-tune your strategies.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 20

Those dates fall under Aries. You both have a strong will and bags of energy, and this can create a hot, fiery, but intensely rewarding relationship.

Luck Maker: Open Your Eyes and Ears

You must explore new possibilities if you want to strike lucky. Lucky people are always hungry for experiences, knowledge, and new insights because they have learned that they will eventually find something wonderful.

At Your Best

Persuasive, protective, driven.

On the Dark Side

Inflexible, defensive, isolated.

Love: Fluctuating Moods

Charming and friendly with a warm and caring personality, June 27 natives are often loyal and devoted partners as well as loving parents. Although they find it easy to make friends and attract admirers, they can have fluctuating moods and be quick to take offense at criticism, which can affect their relationships negatively.

Health: Limber Up

Those born on this day tend to be inflexible in both mind and body, which can manifest in physical discomforts such as joint pain, backache, sciatica, and headaches. They would benefit enormously from all forms of stretching such as yoga and dance, or any exercise that encourages flexibility. They need to optimize their intake of health-boosting nutrients and include a wide variety of foods rather than repeating the same menu week after week. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will encourage them to be more open, optimistic, and confident.

Career: Born Charity Workers

Those born on this day may express their humanitarian concerns in a range of careers including nursing, teaching, therapy, social work, or charity work. Alternatively, they may choose to spread their message more overtly via the arts in music, acting, or writing, although their theatrical side conjoined with their idealism may also lead them into politics.

Destiny: To Devote Their Energy to Helping and Inspiring Others

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to be more open-minded in their approach to people and situations. Once they become more flexible, their destiny is to devote their considerable energy to helping and inspiring others.

Power Thought

My understanding is clear, but my opinions are flexible.
