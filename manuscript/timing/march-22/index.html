Born on March 22nd

The birthday of frankness

Profile

People born on March 22 are forthright, honest individuals who say exactly what they think and feel. Their frankness can be both their greatest strength and their greatest weakness. On the positive side, people always know where they stand with them; on the negative side, their bluntness can sometimes cause offense.

Those born on this day occupy the Aries/Pisces cusp, giving them both fiery directness and deep emotional sensitivity. They are natural communicators with a gift for cutting through pretense and getting straight to the point. They value authenticity above all else and have little patience for those who are evasive or dishonest.

Despite their tough exterior, these people are deeply sensitive and can be easily hurt by the thoughtlessness or ingratitude of others. When wounded, they tend to retreat into themselves rather than confront the source of their pain. It is important for them to learn to express their hurt rather than bottling it up, as unexpressed emotions can lead to bitterness and resentment.

Between the ages of nine and twenty-eight there is an emphasis on daring, directness, and new ventures. These are action-oriented years when they learn to trust their instincts and assert themselves. After twenty-nine there is a shift toward greater stability, financial security, and emotional grounding.

The key to success for those born on this day lies in learning to temper their frankness with diplomacy. When they can combine their refreshing honesty with sensitivity and tact, they become powerful communicators who can inspire trust and respect. Their ability to see through deception and speak the truth, delivered with warmth rather than harshness, makes them invaluable in both their personal and professional relationships.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning when to hold your tongue.

The Way Forward

Understand that silence is sometimes more eloquent than words, and that not every truth needs to be spoken aloud.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You share a love of independence and honesty, creating a stimulating and understanding partnership.

Luck Maker: Think Before You Speak

Pause for a moment before saying what is on your mind. A brief pause can make the difference between a comment that inspires and one that wounds.

At Your Best

Honest, direct, authentic.

On the Dark Side

Blunt, tactless, withdrawn.

Love: What You See Is What You Get

March 22 natives are refreshingly honest partners who bring a no-nonsense approach to their relationships. They cannot stand pretense or game-playing and need a partner who is equally straightforward. Once committed, they are loyal and devoted, though they need to guard against becoming too critical of their partner.

Health: Channel That Energy

People born on this day have abundant physical energy that needs to be channeled constructively. If they do not get enough exercise, they can become irritable and restless. Vigorous physical activity, particularly sports, running, or martial arts, is essential. As far as diet is concerned, they should aim for simple, wholesome food and avoid overcomplicating their meals. Getting enough sleep is important, as they tend to push themselves hard during the day. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with the color Blue will encourage calm, clear communication and help them think before they speak.

Career: Born Commentators

With their gift for honest communication, these people are naturally drawn to careers in journalism, broadcasting, writing, and public speaking. They may also excel in law, politics, diplomacy, and business. Their creative side may lead them to careers in art, music, design, or acting, while their sensitivity may draw them to counseling, social work, or the healing professions.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Authenticity

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to deliver their truths with compassion and sensitivity. Once they have developed this skill, their destiny is to inspire others with their refreshing authenticity and unwavering honesty.

Power Thought

I choose my words with care and kindness.
