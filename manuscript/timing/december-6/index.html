Born on December 6th

The birthday of the developer

Profile

Practical and clear-sighted, those born on December 6 have a real talent for management. They will often be found organizing teams and trying to improve or develop situations or ideas so they produce better results. They are the people to whom everyone turns first when things are not working, and others value them for their consistently rational and perceptive way of looking at the world, as well as the tactful way they present their conclusions so that others feel motivated to make positive changes rather than feeling vulnerable and disappointed.

Lacking any hidden agenda, these individuals are direct, honest and to the point in both professional and personal life. They can see immediately the weaknesses or flaws in a situation and how these can be replaced, removed or improved for the best possible result. Although friends and colleagues are often extremely grateful for their sage advice, occasionally their desire to interfere and control can come across as meddling. As illogical as it may seem to them, they need to respect the fact that some people are stuck in their ways and do not actually want someone wading in with advice.

Until age forty-five, they feel a growing need for order and structure, and practical issues dominate. During these years, evaluating concepts and systems and devising strategies to improve them are likely to be top of their agenda. After forty-six, a turning point highlights a growing need for more independence but also for group awareness. They feel more experimental, but these are also the years when they are likely to enlist the support of others and become the spearhead for highly motivated, smoothly operating teams.

Although creativity may not be their strongest suit, their highly developed qualities of clear thinking, objectivity and progressiveness make them natural leaders with the potential to achieve results that enhance their own lives and everyone else's.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting the urge to interfere.

The Way Forward

Understand that sometimes people need to learn from their own mistakes.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. This is a very easygoing and natural pairing, and the potential for long-term happiness is great.

Luck Maker: Give Without Expecting a Return

The more you give selflessly and without condition, the luckier you become, because sooner or later people will want to repay you.

At Your Best

Perceptive, supportive, realistic.

On the Dark Side

Interfering, controlling, unimaginative.

Love: Interesting Conversation

Bright and articulate, those born on this day are attracted to intelligent, hardworking people, and nothing is more enjoyable to them, or more sensual if it is the right person, than an interesting conversation. Others will be drawn to them for guidance and support, so it is important for them to pick and choose who they help, ensuring their energy is not dimmed by clingy or needy people.

Health: The Scent of Ginger

These individuals have workaholic tendencies and may rely on caffeine and smoking to stay alert. This is bad for their health and they should find healthier ways to stay sharp, such as eating little and often to keep blood sugar balanced and choosing foods rich in concentrated nutrients like oily fish, nuts and seeds. Regular exercise, ideally around thirty minutes daily, will also boost energy levels, and they need plenty of good-quality sleep. Burning candles with a ginger scent may help clear the head and improve memory; to combat stress, chamomile, Lavender or sandalwood candles are recommended. Wearing, meditating on or surrounding themselves with the color Purple, and taking up drawing or writing as a hobby, will encourage them to connect with their intuition and latent creativity.

Career: Born Managers

These individuals thrive in any career offering the freedom to organize and implement improvements. Options include management, publishing, advertising, sales, business, administration, law, social reform and education. A deeper need for harmony may also draw them to music and the arts.

Destiny: To Be at the Forefront of Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to understand that not everything in life needs to be organized and managed. Once they learn to leave others alone when their advice is not required, their destiny is to be at the forefront of progress.

Power Thought

Today I change my beliefs about what is impossible.
