Born on December 29th

The birthday of the laid-back commander

Profile

Those born on December 29 are often in great demand because they are extremely confident, responsible and adaptable, and they genuinely relish opportunities to assist or enlighten others. They are not overtly ambitious, but because they have a superb sense of timing, they frequently find themselves in positions of responsibility.

Although they can be quite laid back, they are willing to work hard for what they want and equally willing to help others achieve their goals. While this endears them to many, they must be careful not to take on more than they can handle. Their greatest strength is the ability to command or guide people and situations without appearing domineering, thanks in large part to their excellent communication skills.

They may come across as serious or deadpan at first, but the more time people spend with them, the more they appreciate their wonderful sense of irony and dry humor. Given the chance, they can captivate audiences with their intelligent word choice and wickedly perceptive observations, understanding that humor delivers their message strongly and effectively without causing offense.

After age twenty-three, they become less influenced by rules and traditions and more willing to develop their own unique perspective. Another turning point arrives around fifty-three, bringing an emphasis on emotional life reflected in dreams and intuitive understanding of others. At any age, the key to success is to stop living in the past and stop doubting themselves, because they are bursting with creativity and have all the talent they need to make pioneering innovations. They have the potential; all they need to do is step into their own shoes, shake off pessimism and become the great leader they are destined to be.

Your Greatest Challenge

Living up to your potential.

The Way Forward

Direct your energy inward and discover the courage you need to step out of your comfort zone and put yourself on the line.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 21 and July 22

Those dates fall under Cancer. You are both expressive and supportive individuals who can give each other understanding and unconditional love.

Luck Maker: Cultivate Tact and Diplomacy

No matter how right you are or how just your cause, presenting your argument with tact and diplomacy is what attracts the luck you deserve.

At Your Best

Supportive, entertaining, commanding.

On the Dark Side

Unfulfilled, negative, unrealistic.

Love: Strong Need for Companionship

Those born on this day have a strong need for companionship, and their ideal partner will be someone who is successful and creative, like themselves. Once committed to a relationship, they understand the importance of keeping a work-life balance, but they need to make sure the spirit of passion and romance stays alive by remaining as spontaneous as possible.

Health: Bring More Color into Your Life

These individuals may go through phases when they are more sedentary than they should be. It is important for them to treat exercise not as something occasional but as part of their daily routine. They should stay away from recreational drugs of all kinds and schedule regular check-ups, as they may be prone to rheumatism, bone conditions and circulatory disorders. When it comes to diet, food should be enjoyed, and making sure there is always plenty of color on their plate in the form of fruits and vegetables will boost nutrient intake and mood. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow encourages greater self-belief.

Career: Born Interpreters

These individuals are especially suited to realms where they can offer guidance and support, such as teaching or politics, or to fields where they can advance knowledge, such as science or engineering. Other options include lecturing, research, communication technology, interpreting, translation, music, acting and poetry.

Destiny: To Advance Human Knowledge

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to trust themselves more. Once they begin to think positively about their potential for success, their destiny is to lead others toward advancement in human knowledge.

Power Thought

My possibilities are endless.
