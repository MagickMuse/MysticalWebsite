Born on August 17th

The birthday of the dormant volcano

Profile

Those born on August 17 present a calm and controlled front to the world, but underneath the surface there is a smoldering intensity that can erupt at any time. Like a dormant volcano, they are a powerhouse of energy and drive, and although they may appear serene on the outside, inside they are bursting with ambition, determination and passion.

They are typically hard-working, disciplined individuals who set themselves extremely high standards. They are also highly intelligent and observant, with a knack for understanding the motivations of others. Although they can be charming and diplomatic when the situation demands it, they can also be blunt and direct when they feel their principles are being compromised. Their intensity and sense of purpose can be inspiring, but it can also be intimidating to those who do not understand them.

Until age thirty-five there is an emphasis on practicality, efficiency and order. During these years they may feel restless and impatient for success, and it is important for them to channel their incredible energy constructively rather than allowing it to build up inside. After age thirty-six there is a turning point that places greater emphasis on creativity, relationships and harmony.

After age sixty-five there is another turning point which stimulates a desire for deeper emotional awareness and personal transformation. Whatever age they are, the sooner they find positive outlets for their volcanic energy and learn to manage their intensity, the greater their chances of achieving the extraordinary success and fulfillment they are destined for.

Your Greatest Challenge

Managing your intensity.

The Way Forward

Understand that suppressing your passions will only make them more explosive; finding constructive outlets for your energy is the key to your success.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 19

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You are both ambitious, disciplined and driven, and this can create a powerful and enduring partnership.

Luck Maker: Channel Your Fire

Lucky people know that passion without direction is destructive, but passion with purpose is unstoppable. Channel your fire and you will attract extraordinary luck.

At Your Best

Dynamic, powerful, disciplined.

On the Dark Side

Explosive, intimidating, repressed.

Love: Beneath the Surface

Those born on this day can appear reserved and cool in love, but beneath the surface they are intensely passionate. They need a partner who is not intimidated by their intensity and who can match their depth of feeling. Once committed, they are loyal, devoted and incredibly protective of their loved ones.

Health: Release Valve

These individuals need to find healthy ways to release their pent-up energy, as suppressing it can lead to headaches, digestive problems, high blood pressure and stress-related illness. Regular vigorous exercise is essential, and activities like running, swimming, martial arts or competitive sports are ideal. As far as diet is concerned, they should eat a balanced diet and limit their intake of stimulants such as caffeine and alcohol. They would also benefit from relaxation techniques such as meditation or deep breathing. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue will help them stay cool and calm.

Career: Born Strategists

These individuals are ideally suited to careers in business, management, politics, the military or any profession that requires strategic thinking and leadership. They may also be drawn to science, medicine, research, engineering, law enforcement or investigative journalism. Their creative side may find expression in writing, music, art or film.

Destiny: To Channel Their Extraordinary Energy for the Greater Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to manage their intensity without suppressing it. Once they have found constructive outlets for their volcanic energy, their destiny is to channel their extraordinary power and drive for the greater good.

Power Thought

I channel my energy wisely and create something extraordinary.
