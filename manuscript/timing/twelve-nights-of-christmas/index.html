The famous Christmas carol celebrates the gifts given on each of the twelve days of Christmas. Witches celebrate during the twelve nights. Ever wonder why the Wild Hunt rides at Christmas time?

A Gap in Time

According to ancient Celtic, German, Greek, Roman, and Slavic calendars, a gap in time occurs in midwinter for twelve nights, which now correspond to the Twelve Nights of Christmas. This period begins with what is now Christmas Eve and continues until what is now the Feast of the Epiphany.

During this period, there's a gap, a void where the veil between realms is particularly thin or even non-existent. During these twelve days and nights, the dead roam the Earth and the Spirits join together with their devotees.

Germanic Names and Traditions

Known in Germanic lands as the "Zwölften" or "Twolven," this was the time when Odin and Frigg held forth.

A Wild, Raucous Time

The Twelve Nights are a wild, raucous time devoted to:

  • Merry-making
  • Gift-giving
  • Masquerading
  • Divination
  • Spell-casting
  • Magic ritual

Romantic Origins

Festivities during this period once had more in common with Valentine's Day or romantic Halloween celebrations than with modern conventional, staid Christmas devotions.

Spiritual Significance

This twelve-night gap represents a time outside of normal time—a liminal period when the usual rules don't apply. The boundaries between worlds dissolve, making it:

  • A powerful time for spirit communication
  • Ideal for divination and seeing the future
  • Perfect for wild celebration and breaking social conventions
  • A time when the Wild Hunt rides through the skies
  • A period for magic that bridges worlds

Twelfth Night: Naming Day

January 6 marks Twelfth Night, the final night of the twelve-day celebration—twelve days after the departure of the Holly King on December 25. In some traditions, this is a ritual time for Naming Day ceremonies, honoring the transition from the old year's energies to the new.

Modern Practice

Many modern Pagans still honor these twelve nights as a time apart from ordinary time. Some stay up through the nights, some perform divination each evening, and some simply acknowledge that this period carries special power for magical work and spiritual connection.
