Born on November 8th

The birthday of dark fascination

Profile

Those born on November 8 are irresistibly drawn to the hidden, the forbidden and the darkly mysterious. Although they would never break with their own personal moral code, they are fascinated by the unconventional, the extreme and the shadowy side of life.

There is a deep intensity about these individuals that manifests itself in their determination to uncover the truth or get to the bottom of a mystery. They are perceptive and resourceful, with a powerful desire to understand what makes people tick. They also possess an astounding ability for transformation and reinvention. They are the type of person who will experience many setbacks in life but will always bounce back, reinventing themselves and their lives with skill and courage.

Despite their obvious strength, they can be highly secretive and mistrustful. Learning to share and open up is essential, as is their willingness to accept help from others. Until age forty-three there is an emphasis on expansion and adventure, and they may wish to broaden their outlook through travel, education or personal development. After forty-four, a turning point arrives when they become more progressive, disciplined and realistic in their approach.

Whatever their age, the key to their fulfillment is to resist the urge to completely control every aspect of their lives and the lives of those around them. Their fascination with the darker side of life can also sometimes tip into obsession; they need to make sure they channel this curiosity in a positive, productive direction. When they can combine their probing intellect with their remarkable resilience and capacity for transformation, they have all they need to not just survive the dark times, but to emerge from them stronger, wiser and more brilliant than ever.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting go of the need to control.

The Way Forward

Understand that some of the most extraordinary things in life happen when you loosen your grip and trust in the unexpected.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 19

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You are both determined, resilient and deeply loyal, and this can be a powerful and enduring partnership.

Luck Maker: Let Light In

Spending too much time in the shadows can block the flow of luck. Allow some lightness, humor and openness into your life, and watch how opportunities begin to appear.

At Your Best

Resilient, perceptive, magnetic.

On the Dark Side

Secretive, controlling, obsessive.

Love: All or Nothing

Those born on this day approach love with the same intensity they bring to everything else. They are deeply passionate and loyal, but they can be possessive and jealous. They need a partner who can handle their intensity and who understands that beneath their strong exterior lies a deeply sensitive and vulnerable soul. Trust is essential in their relationships, and once it is given and received, their capacity for devotion is remarkable.

Health: Release and Renewal

These individuals can be prone to holding tension in their bodies, which may manifest as headaches, back pain or digestive issues. Learning to release stress through regular exercise, in particular swimming or martial arts, is essential. They may also benefit enormously from therapeutic massage, acupuncture or other bodywork. They should be mindful of their diet, avoiding excessive caffeine, alcohol and rich foods, and increasing their intake of fresh fruits, vegetables and water. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color White will help them feel lighter and more open, and carrying a Topaz crystal will encourage warmth, generosity and openness.

Career: Born Investigators

These individuals excel in careers that require probing, research and investigation. They may be drawn to detective work, forensic science, psychology, psychiatry, journalism, surgery, research and archaeology. They may also find fulfillment in finance, business strategy, the military or the emergency services, or they may express their darker creative sensibilities through writing, filmmaking or the arts.

Destiny: To Transform Darkness into Light

The life path of those born on this day is to learn that vulnerability is not weakness. Once they can open themselves up to trust and lightness, their destiny is to transform darkness into light, inspiring others with their remarkable resilience and depth.

Power Thought

I welcome the light as warmly as I understand the dark.
