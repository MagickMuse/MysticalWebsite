Born on January 29th

The birthday of the mystic warrior

Profile

Highly intuitive yet deeply persuasive, those born on January 29 always say what they think, managing to be direct and forceful without ever becoming offensive. Their generous nature and unwavering belief that there is good in everyone earn them not just respect but genuine love from all who cross their path.

People born on this day often channel their creative, quietly rebellious streak into championing the rights of others. Excellent communication skills serve them well in both their professional and social lives. The keys to their success as negotiators and as human beings are their powerful intuition and their preference for cooperation over independence. They have a gift for sensing what others are thinking and feeling, and for knowing exactly when the moment is right to act. They also understand the power of synergy, recognizing that a group united behind a common goal is the most formidable force of all.

Although they are open-minded and generally tolerant of differing viewpoints, they may occasionally retreat into unassertive behavior, and in extreme cases, into complete inertia. Once they learn to trust their ability to make the right call, however, they can truly reach for the stars. Fortunately, around the ages of twenty-two and fifty-two, powerful turning points encourage them to develop greater self-awareness. Life generally becomes much easier in their early thirties and beyond, as they begin to realize that within them lies the power to achieve almost anything.

Once January 29 natives have built their self-belief, their formidable determination to right wrongs can provoke strong reactions. When the response is positive, their courage grows. When it is negative, the hurt can cut deep. It is important for them to understand that it is impossible to please everyone, and that sometimes a dose of tough love is the kindest thing they can offer. When they learn this, they step fully into their role as the mystic warrior: strong-willed, inspiring, and able to rally others behind their chosen cause.

Your Greatest Challenge

Dealing with confrontation.

The Way Forward

Do not withdraw. Instead, calmly state your opinion and stay true to yourself. Whenever you compromise your identity and beliefs, you diminish your chances of both success and happiness.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between June 22 and July 23

Those dates fall under Cancer. You share a sensitive approach to life and to love, and this can create a match made in heaven.

Luck Maker: Be Proactive

Reactive people make choices on impulse; they are like reeds blowing in the wind. Proactive people attract good fortune because they recognize that while they cannot always control what happens, they can always control how they respond.

At Your Best

Intuitive, generous, tactful.

On the Dark Side

Withdrawn, unassertive, indecisive.

Love: Late Bloomers

January 29 natives tend to hold back or withdraw from confrontation in relationships, and this can have a negative impact on both them and their partner. Once in a secure relationship, however, they are endlessly generous and giving. They need to make sure they are with someone who allows them to receive as well as give. Although romantic connections can be intense when they are young, they often find their truest love later in life, once their sense of self-worth has fully blossomed.

Health: Sensitive to the Environment

People born on this day are sensitive not only to others but to their physical surroundings. As a result, they may experience unexplained mood swings, fatigue, headaches, allergies, or food sensitivities. They need to be extremely thoughtful about diet, as digestive issues can arise. They thrive on a wholesome, down-to-earth diet rich in natural produce and low in meat, dairy, and refined or sugary foods. Regular exercise benefits their circulation, as they may be prone to stiff muscles and joints, particularly in the lower body. Time spent catching up with friends or absorbed in a good book brings great pleasure. Regular cups of cinnamon herbal tea may support both circulation and digestion, and reading, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with shades of Blue can help steady their mood.

Career: Born Campaigners

The compassionate, sensitive nature that marks these people draws many toward politics, law, and humanitarian or social reform. Because they are intellectual and artistic as well, careers in media, entertainment, lecturing, teaching, or writing are also strong fits. When inspired, they make exceptional campaigners and negotiators, and their outstanding people skills may also attract them to sales, marketing, personal relations, or advertising.

Destiny: To Believe in Themselves

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to trust their gut instincts and believe in themselves. Once they accomplish this, their inner voice will guide them toward the important things they are destined to do with their lives.

Power Thought

My best relationship is the one I have with me.
