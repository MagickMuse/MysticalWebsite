Born on November 25th

The birthday of social responsibility

Profile

Those born on November 25 are rational, capable and quietly progressive individuals who are willing to take all the time needed to complete a project perfectly. Their motivation is a benevolent urge to achieve excellence and make a difference, rather than gain power and money, and this gives them the staying power to make things happen and the humility to be the essential means of support for a powerful individual or social group.

Social responsibility is an important theme in their lives. This is not to say they are not independently minded; indeed their desire for personal fulfillment is strong. It is just that they are ultimately concerned with inspiring, enlightening or otherwise acting on behalf of others or society as a whole. Their fixity of purpose, self-discipline and high expectations of themselves and others can, however, make them become overly fixated on their goals and too judgmental. Learning to establish an identity for themselves outside of their work or the social group with which they identify, and learning to accept and work with those who have alternative views, are essential requirements for their psychological growth.

Until age twenty-six they may find that they are concerned with issues of freedom, and they should take advantage of opportunities to expand their horizons through study or travel. After twenty-seven, a turning point focuses their minds on a more pragmatic, ordered and structured approach. There is another turning point at fifty-seven when a growing need for original and progressive ideas and, at long last, independence arises. Whatever age or stage they are at, they should try to find the courage to strike out on their own, directing their creative intellect toward finding a path that combines their need for personal fulfillment with their socially oriented concerns.

Your Greatest Challenge

Establishing your independence.

The Way Forward

Understand that until you are able to strike out on your own, the contribution you make to a team will not be as effective or valuable.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 23 and November 21

Those dates fall under Scorpio. This relationship is so exciting because when directness and optimism meet subtlety and intensity, anything can happen.

Luck Maker: Let Yourself Off the Hook

Rigid expectations of success and relentlessly beating yourself up for some perceived or real weakness will do nothing to improve your self-esteem, drive, motivation or luck-making potential.

At Your Best

Steady, accomplished, supportive.

On the Dark Side

Workaholic, judgmental, inflexible.

Love: As Good as It Gets

Those born on this day have very high expectations and ideals when it comes to affairs of the heart. Sometimes these can be so high that they are impossible to attain. They need to learn that simply feeling and being happy with someone really is good enough and quite sufficient reason to build a life with them.

Health: The Joy of Healthy Living

These individuals have workaholic, perfectionist tendencies, and when it comes to their health they will often be just as thorough and demanding of themselves. They need to make sure that their concern to eat only the very best does not rob them of the joy of eating, and that their desire for fitness does not take away the joy of movement. Moderation, as far as exercise and diet are concerned, is therefore strongly recommended. A routine of regular meals and snacks is highly advised, as is regular exercise to boost circulation, reduce the risk of heart disease and enhance mood. Their natural optimism tends to make them underrate illness until it becomes serious, so regular check-ups are advised. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to be more tolerant and accepting of others, and the colors Yellow or Red will encourage them to stand up for themselves.

Career: Born Teachers

These individuals may work as teachers, scientists, politicians, or in any career that allows them to combine personal ambitions with the urge to be socially responsible. They may also be drawn to social and charity work, the caring and healing professions, law and law enforcement. Their need for self-expression may manifest in writing, music, art or the entertainment world.

Destiny: To Assist, Educate and Inspire Others

The life path of those born on this day is to balance their own needs with those of others. Once they have learned to stand up for what they want and say no if they have to, their destiny is to assist, educate and inspire others.

Power Thought

The happier I am, the more I can inspire others to find joy.
