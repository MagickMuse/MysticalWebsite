Born on March 12th

The birthday of the untamed spirit

Profile

People born on March 12 have tremendous spirit and a desire to explore as many aspects of life as they can in order to gain knowledge and test themselves in increasingly demanding challenges and adventures. Others may fear for their safety or warn them to be more responsible, but it is impossible for these untamed spirits to listen, let alone heed that advice.

Although they thrive on competition, these people are not generally motivated by a need to score points over others. The motivation is to challenge themselves and see how far they can go with their own natural abilities. In their work life they think nothing of leaving a secure job to start a new venture; in their personal lives they are attracted to people with an air of danger about them. Sometimes their exploits will land them in big trouble, but they will usually have considered the risks beforehand so that they know what they are letting themselves in for. Because they are so courageous, they tend to be resilient enough to withstand any setbacks, learn from their mistakes, and bounce back even stronger.

The danger for these people is a lack of direction; they need to settle on a course worthy of their courage and resilience. Since they are often multi-talented, it can be hard for them to find focus, but it is important for them to specialize in a particular field if they are to come into their own. Up until the age of thirty-eight there is an emphasis on change and new ventures. Then, after thirty-nine, there is a turning point when they tend to slow down, feeling a greater need for stability and financial security.

Strong believers in an afterlife and fate, these people have a profoundly intuitive and reflective side which they would do well to cultivate. Whatever they end up doing, one thing is sure: wherever these creative and courageous spirits are to be found, there will always be an element of danger, controversy, and excitement swirling about them.

Your Greatest Challenge

Avoiding extreme attention-seeking behavior.

The Way Forward

Understand that you are unique and special; you do not have to do something wild to prove it.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between November 23 and December 21

Those dates fall under Sagittarius. You are both equally adventurous; if you rein in your wilder tendencies, this is an exciting and spontaneous relationship.

Luck Maker: Write an Action Plan

Whatever your goals, an action plan can help you concentrate your energies productively. When you commit yourself in writing, you tend to take yourself and your goals more seriously.

At Your Best

Spontaneous, creative, courageous.

On the Dark Side

Impulsive, irresponsible, insecure.

Love: Dangerous

March 12 natives have an air of danger about them and are attracted to people who are equally edgy. They love to play games and set traps in relationships and will often attract steady, genuinely loving people who are drawn to their wild energy. They need to steer clear of exploiting those more vulnerable than them and seek out people who match their energy, daring, and eccentricity.

Health: Inner Conflicts

Despite their outward show of courage and daring, people born on this day may be prone to great emotional instability, perhaps from hardships growing up. They need to learn to deal with their inner conflicts and face up to their insecurities, perhaps with the help of a counselor or therapist. As far as diet is concerned, they tend to have a fast metabolism and can eat virtually what they want, but if weight problems occur they should cut down on saturated fat and refined, processed comfort foods. Vigorous exercise and competitive sport to work off some of that manic energy is highly recommended. Wearing, meditating on, or surrounding themselves with shades of Blue and Green will induce calm and encourage greater harmony and balance.

Career: Born Stockbrokers

These people have the potential to succeed in any career requiring them to take risks. If the world of business appeals, they may be drawn to stockbroking, investment, or starting their own business. They may also be interested in detective work, as well as careers in art, music, comedy, writing, journalism, and dance. Promotion, publishing, the media, education, and lecturing may also suit.

Destiny: To Lead Others in Radically New Directions

The life path of those born on this day is to find a sense of balance and stability in their lives. Once they are able to focus their energies, their destiny is to pursue radical quests and by doing so lead others in radically new directions.

Power Thought

I want my life to be filled with direction and purpose.
