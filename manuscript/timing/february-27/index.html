Born on February 27th

The birthday of hypnotic allure

Profile

People born on February 27 have the ability to turn heads and win hearts wherever they go. There is a magnetic, striking intensity about them that can hold others spellbound.

These people like to be in the limelight, and sooner or later they will be. Others admire them not just for their hypnotic personality but because they stride confidently and with purpose through life, achieving their goals with ease and grace. They have a mind forever open to the new, but they are also able to analyze in depth. By devoting a great deal of energy to understanding how things work, their knowledge base grows steadily until it becomes so solid that it can carry them from one ambitious project to the next, straight to the very top. There is one area of their life, however, that is unlikely to be envied: their personal relationships.

Although this may seem contradictory given their cool, charismatic image, February 27 natives often have a chaotic emotional life, with broken relationships scattered around them. The reason is that they possess a deeply emotional nature; although they have learned to control it in impersonal settings, their own personal relationships are constantly threatened by their impulse to follow their heart. It is extremely important for them to apply discipline to their personal life and stop making unreasonable, childish demands on others, especially between the ages of twenty-three and fifty-two, when they become even more active and adventurous. If self-discipline is not learned during this period, the result could be emotional and personal chaos.

At their worst, February 27 people can be social-climbing attention seekers. At their best, however, they are lovable, spontaneous individuals who can both intoxicate and inspire others with their presence. They may often be described as a little crazy by those who know them, but once they invest their energy in a worthwhile cause they can achieve great things and enjoy the limelight they seem destined for.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to take charge of your emotions in your personal life.

The Way Forward

Understand that you are in charge of your emotions; they will not take you over unless you allow them to.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between October 24 and November 22

Those dates fall under Scorpio. If you can learn to nurture each other's vulnerabilities, this creates an intense and passionate bond.

Luck Maker: Take a Deep Breath

The more you can control yourself and find healthy ways to let off steam, the more respected you will be and the fewer enemies you will have. Lucky people do not have enemies.

At Your Best

Magnetic, creative, versatile.

On the Dark Side

Confused, highly strung, social climber.

Love: High Maintenance

February 27 natives expect total commitment and support from others but need to learn to give it in return. They can be incredibly inconsistent lovers, and those in a relationship with them will need to master the art of avoiding head-on confrontations. When they do finally feel able to commit, however, they can be spontaneous, passionate, and vulnerable lovers — a little high maintenance, perhaps, but well worth the effort.

Health: Stay Cool and In Control

People born on this day are full of energy, so a vigorous exercise program is recommended to prevent them from channeling that energy in unhealthy directions, such as chasing numerous partners, drinking, or gambling. A simple, natural diet is recommended, with reduced amounts of red meat, dairy, and saturated fat. Breathing exercises, meditation, and yoga can also help them develop the self-discipline to keep their passions under control. Meditating on, wearing, or surrounding themselves with the colors Blue and Lavender will help them stay cool and logical when passions flare.

Career: Born Actors

These people seem destined for the limelight, and careers on the stage, in the movies, or anywhere people will be watching them perform hold great appeal. If they work in business, they will gravitate toward sales, advertising, and marketing. They can also make inspirational teachers and lecturers, and are ideally suited to life as an entrepreneur, finding deep satisfaction in building their own business.

Destiny: To Enchant Others with Their Magick

The life path of those born on this day is to learn self-discipline in their personal relationships. Once they learn to keep their cool, their destiny is to enchant others in some way with their unique brand of magick.

Power Thought

Today I will put love before all else.
