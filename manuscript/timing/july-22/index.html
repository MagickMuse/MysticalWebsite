Born on July 22nd

The birthday of brave compulsion

Profile

Those born on July 22 are driven by a powerful compulsion to experience life to the fullest. They are courageous individuals who are not afraid to take risks and push boundaries, and their bravery inspires others to do the same.

Although their daring nature can sometimes get them into trouble, they are also remarkably resilient and resourceful. They have an ability to bounce back from setbacks that others find impressive. Between the ages of thirty-one and sixty-one there is an emphasis on practicality and analysis. During these years they need to ensure that their courage does not become recklessness. After the age of sixty-one there is a turning point that highlights their growing need for balance and harmony.

Your Greatest Challenge

Thinking before you act.

The Way Forward

Understand that courage without wisdom is recklessness. Taking a moment to consider the consequences of your actions does not make you less brave.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between January 21 and February 19

Those dates fall under Aquarius. You are both unconventional and freedom-loving, and this can create an exciting and liberating relationship.

Luck Maker: Pause and Reflect

Lucky people understand that sometimes the bravest thing you can do is pause and reflect before taking action. This moment of clarity can save you from unnecessary mistakes.

At Your Best

Courageous, resilient, inspiring.

On the Dark Side

Reckless, impulsive, compulsive.

Love: All or Nothing

Those born on this day are passionate and intense lovers who tend to throw themselves wholeheartedly into relationships. They need a partner who can match their intensity and who is not afraid of their emotional depth. They should be careful not to let their compulsive nature lead them into unhealthy relationship patterns.

Health: Slow Down

Those born on this day live life at such a fast pace that they may neglect their health. It is important for them to slow down and listen to their body. They are prone to stress-related conditions and may benefit from relaxation techniques such as meditation, deep breathing or yoga. As far as diet is concerned, they need to eat regular, balanced meals and avoid using food or alcohol as a way to manage stress. Moderate exercise such as swimming, cycling or walking is recommended. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to find balance and calm.

Career: Born Adventurers

These individuals are drawn to careers that offer excitement and challenge. They may excel as explorers, athletes, journalists, entrepreneurs or in the military. Their creativity and emotional depth may also draw them to the arts, music, writing or acting. Their resilience and resourcefulness make them excellent crisis managers.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Courage

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their daring nature with wisdom and reflection. Once they have done that, their destiny is to inspire others with their courage and their remarkable ability to bounce back from adversity.

Power Thought

I am brave enough to pause and wise enough to reflect.

Signs & Symbols

  • Sun sign: Cancer (cusp with Leo)
  • Ruling planet: Moon / Sun
  • Symbol: The Crab
  • Birth date ruler: Uranus, the visionary
  • Tarot card: The Fool (freedom)
  • Favorable numbers: 2, 4
  • Lucky days: Monday and Sunday, especially when these days fall on the 2nd or 4th of the month
  • Lucky colors: Gold, Purple, cream
  • Birthstone: Pearl
