Born on February 16th

The birthday of the alchemist

Profile

People born on February 16 have the ability to transform even the most difficult experiences into something positive. There is a touch of the alchemist about them — an ability to see the bigger picture, bring together all the relevant information, and cut right to the essence or truth of a situation.

It is likely that those born on this day have at some point experienced some form of upheaval, trauma, or setback. Whatever the nature of that experience, they have taken it in stride, learned from it, and grown stronger. The positive side is that it has given them great confidence; even if they sometimes appear quiet on the outside, underneath one can sense an inner strength and brightness. They also possess an astute understanding of others and how the world works; rarely, if ever, do they miss a trick. The downside is that they can sometimes appear cold and detached, and their bluntness or impatience with others' weaknesses may come across as arrogance. Others may at times resent their imperious manner, but more often than not people end up admiring their cleverness and ability to know exactly what to say, what to do, and how to do it.

If they can learn to control their bossiness, their tremendous capacity for realism and intuition makes them potentially great leaders. Around the age of thirty-four a turning point arrives when ambition and focus take center stage. It is particularly important at that point that they become more conscious of their emotions, rather than masking them with a bossy manner or emotional reserve.

When February 16 natives are able to strike a balance between their inner and outer life, they truly are capable of striking gold both personally and professionally — which is, after all, every true alchemist's dream.

Your Greatest Challenge

Resisting being a know-it-all.

The Way Forward

Understand that just as you have learned from your own mistakes, sometimes other people need to learn from theirs.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between February 20 and March 20

Those dates fall under Pisces. This relationship is powerful and passionate because you both have an intuitive understanding of what the other needs.

Luck Maker: Send Your Inner Critic on Holiday

Every time you hear your inner critic, visualize it resting in a deck chair on a sunny beach. The more you silence that voice, the more you start to love and value yourself.

At Your Best

Realistic, intuitive, confident.

On the Dark Side

Arrogant, blunt, detached.

Love: Let Past Hurts Go

February 16 natives need to move on from past hurts, disappointments, or experiences that may have made them wary of falling in love. They need to learn to trust their hearts more, because deep down they still believe in the possibility of love even if their heads do not. They also need to start believing that they deserve to be loved — because they certainly do.

Health: One Tough Cookie

People born on this day are mentally and physically tough, but they can sometimes push themselves too hard, leading to stress-related conditions such as headaches and insomnia. Regular health reviews are important, as is an energy-boosting diet with plenty of nutritious meals and snacks. They thrive best when they stay active throughout the day; lounging about does not suit them, although they do need adequate rest and relaxation. Meditation and breathing exercises can help them strike that important balance between their inner and outer life.

Career: Born Reviewers

These people have a fantastic eye for detail and an ability to see the bigger picture, making them superb editors, journalists, reviewers, film critics, lecturers, and teachers. Alternatively, they may be drawn to business, banking, the stock market, or careers where they can express their intuitive creativity such as art, entertainment, charity work, and social reform.

Destiny: To Help Others See the Positive in Life

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to accept that they deserve all the love and goodness the world can give them. Once they have strengthened their self-esteem, their destiny is to help others focus on the positives in any situation or experience.

Power Thought

Today I will seek to praise rather than criticize.
