Born on September 5th

The birthday of the extraordinary mind

Profile

Those born on September 5 are blessed with an extraordinary imagination. In their minds, anything is possible. They are positive and progressive individuals who see opportunities everywhere and potential in everyone. Their minds are so sharp and active that they sometimes have trouble slowing down and coming back to the real world; this can make them seem absent-minded or scatter-brained, but in fact they are anything but.

Others tend to admire them for the sharpness of their wit and their original ideas, and they can be excellent communicators. However, there is a tendency for them to daydream, lose focus or become distracted. They also tend to spread themselves too thin in an attempt to explore all the possibilities that life has to offer. They need to learn to prioritize and to channel their considerable intellectual gifts. When they do manage to focus their attention and direct their energy, they can achieve remarkable things.

Until age forty-seven there is an emphasis on socializing, partnerships and the desire to create more harmony and beauty in their lives. After age forty-eight there is a turning point that highlights a growing need for emotional change, personal power and transformation. Throughout their lives, the key to their success lies in their ability to find a purpose, a project or a calling that is worthy of their extraordinary mental gifts. Once they learn to focus and commit, their quick minds, sharp observations and extraordinary creativity will be recognized and rewarded.

Your Greatest Challenge

Focusing your energy.

The Way Forward

Understand that when your energy is scattered you will make slower progress. Focusing your attention on one thing at a time leads to greater achievement.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. You both have a love of knowledge and mental stimulation, and this can be a highly creative and rewarding union.

Luck Maker: Stop Multi-tasking

Lucky people understand that, contrary to popular belief, multi-tasking is not productive. Focusing on one task at a time ensures that it gets done properly, boosting both your confidence and your luck.

At Your Best

Imaginative, witty, original.

On the Dark Side

Scattered, unrealistic, absent-minded.

Love: Magnetic

Those born on this day have a magnetic personality that draws others to them. They are exciting and fun to be around, although their tendency to get distracted can be frustrating for partners who crave stability. They are attracted to highly intelligent, creative individuals who can keep up with their quick minds.

Health: Mind Over Matter

These individuals tend to live very much in their minds and may neglect their physical health as a result. They need to ground themselves with regular physical exercise; walking and gardening are particularly beneficial. They should also make sure they eat a balanced diet of nutrient-rich whole foods to fuel their active brains. They may be prone to nervous tension, headaches and insomnia, and would benefit from relaxation techniques such as meditation, yoga and deep breathing. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to find balance and feel more grounded.

Career: Born Inventors

These individuals are well suited to careers that allow their minds to roam free, such as science, research, inventing, writing, journalism, the media and technology. Their communication skills also suit them to teaching, lecturing, public relations and politics, and their creativity may draw them toward art, design, music and drama.

Destiny: To Inspire Others with Their Original Vision

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to focus their energy and attention. Once they are able to channel their extraordinary mental gifts, their destiny is to inspire others with their original and progressive vision.

Power Thought

Today I will focus all my energy on one task at a time.
