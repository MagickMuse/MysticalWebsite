Born on September 8th

The birthday of complicated superiority

Profile

Most people born on September 8 have a very black-and-white view of the world. This makes it all the more surprising that, although others are quick to acknowledge their intellectual superiority, they often come across as complicated or enigmatic individuals. This is because instead of showing others their true selves, they will often take on the identity of the cause or group they are representing.

Their fierce determination and conviction to set others on the right path, combined with excellent communication skills, earns them great respect, although this may not extend to affection. Problems and occasionally bitter confrontation can occur when others do not agree with them. They are often so convinced of the superior merits of their position that they will dismiss any conflicting viewpoint; this can not only gain them enemies but earn them a reputation for being narrow-minded. It is crucial for them to appreciate the negative effect their superior attitude can have on others.

Between ages fourteen and forty-five they will gradually become more aware of the importance of social relationships and their creative abilities will be enhanced. These years can be dynamic if they learn to be a little less bossy and a little more sensitive. After age forty-five there is a turning point when they are likely to become more self-aware; now the emphasis is on power, intensity and personal transformation.

Nothing is more important to their psychological growth than their ability to show tolerance toward others, because although these advanced souls do often know what is best for others and for the world, other people and the world may not yet be ready to listen.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being yourself.

The Way Forward

Understand that you, like everyone else, are human: a mass of contradictions.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 19

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You share an appreciation of the good things in life, and this can be a passionate and satisfying union.

Luck Maker: Listen and Learn

Nobody likes a know-it-all. Demonstrating a sincere openness and willingness to listen and learn, even if you think you already have the right answer, will draw other people to you.

At Your Best

Influential, progressive, committed.

On the Dark Side

Difficult, unyielding, proud.

Love: Not Black and White

Those born on this day may not always be easy to get close to and, because they do not tend to rely on the approval or opinions of other people, others may feel they do not need anyone. This is of course untrue. They are at their happiest and best when in a loving and supportive relationship; they just need to relax a little and understand that when it comes to affairs of the heart, there are no rights or wrongs.

Health: Fixed Ideas

These individuals can be very stubborn when it comes to their physical health and it is important that they do not neglect important advice from doctors and loved ones. They need to steer clear of excess, especially when it comes to foods rich in sugar, salt, additives, preservatives and added fat. The emphasis should always be on fresh and natural produce. Regular moderate exercise is highly recommended as it will help them deal with stress, preventing the risk of cardiovascular disease or high blood pressure. They would also benefit from daily stretching exercises, such as those performed in yoga, as these encourage flexibility in both body and mind. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Yellow will encourage them to be more creative and optimistic.

Career: Born Politicians

These individuals tend to identify strongly with their careers and are well suited to careers in politics, the military, law and education. Other careers that might appeal include commerce, research, science, writing, journalism and the worlds of art or entertainment.

Destiny: To Point Others in the Direction of Progress

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to let others make their own mistakes. Once they have found the courage to be themselves, their destiny is to point others in the direction of progress.

Power Thought

One of the top priorities in my life is to be a source of love.
