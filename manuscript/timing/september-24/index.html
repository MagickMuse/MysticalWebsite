Born on September 24th

The birthday of the restless humanitarian

Profile

Those born on September 24 tend to be hard to catch or pin down because they are nomads at heart. If their restlessness does not manifest externally in a love of travel or movement, they will be travelers in their minds, constantly reading, thinking and jumping to original conclusions. Their desire to seek out the new will be a dominant theme in their lives, alongside their powerful desire to love and to be loved.

Although they naturally express themselves in different ways, they often tend to be driven by an urge to discover and an urge to help others with their resourceful and creative efforts. Considerate and empathetic, they have the almost psychic ability to detect unhappiness in others, even if that unhappiness is unspoken, and this is followed by a desire to relieve feelings of distress.

Despite their concern for the well-being of others, they also find it hard to commit to a settled existence. A part of them longs to feel secure but another part is always wondering whether the grass really is greener on the other side. As a result they vacillate a great deal. The key to their psychological growth is being able not just to choose goals that inspire them, but to stick to them.

Before age twenty-eight they are likely to experiment in their social life, forming friendships and relationships with people from a wide variety of different backgrounds. There may also be numerous career changes or professional uncertainty. After age twenty-nine there is a turning point that highlights issues of emotional change, creating a desire to find deeper meaning. This is an extremely powerful and positive influence for them, because once they learn that discipline, perseverance and commitment can be liberating rather than restrictive, these versatile, progressive, humanitarian and multi-talented people will discover within themselves the potential for tremendous power, which can both move and inspire others.

Your Greatest Challenge

Staying in one place for long enough.

The Way Forward

Understand that however many times you move or change direction, you take the same personality with you.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between September 23 and October 22

Those dates fall under Libra. You are both charming and attractive, and this can be a passionate and intense relationship.

Luck Maker: Stop Trying and Start Doing

When you have chosen meaningful goals for yourself, go after them with all your heart. Lucky people do not just dream about their goals; they take active steps toward reaching them.

At Your Best

Concerned, giving, creative.

On the Dark Side

Unsettled, detached, unfocused.

Love: Intimacy Is a Challenge

Although those born on this day love to be admired and their company is much sought after, few people get close enough to become really intimate. This is because they often have a fear of commitment and may find it hard to feel comfortable with intimacy. However, once they find an intelligent partner who can teach them about intimacy, they blossom into passionate, supportive, generous and loyal lovers.

Health: Comfort Eating

Those born on this day need to be careful that their tendency to comfort eat when feeling low or bored does not lead to weight problems, especially as being overweight is linked to high blood pressure and other disorders. When feeling restless they need to find healthier ways to satisfy their hunger, such as phoning a friend, writing in a journal or exercising. If emotional intimacy is a problem, they may benefit from counseling or therapy to get to the root cause. They also need to avoid dependency on alcohol or recreational drugs, and should aim for a healthy, balanced diet with regular snacks and mealtimes, regular mild to moderate exercise and plenty of quality sleep. Finding a creative outlet for their wonderful imagination, such as art, writing or design, will also be extremely therapeutic. Wearing, surrounding themselves with and meditating on the color gold will encourage them to commit to and persevere with their dreams.

Career: Born Networkers

These individuals will often be drawn to social, political or humanitarian causes but may also excel in artistic endeavors. Career choices might include events organization, diplomacy, networking or fundraising, law, social reform, writing, drama, music, photography or the arts.

Destiny: To Take Action to Realize Their Progressive Ideals

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to focus their energies in one direction. Once they have learned to commit, their destiny is to realize their progressive ideals by means of direct action.

Power Thought

There is nothing I cannot do when inspired by self-discipline and integrity.
