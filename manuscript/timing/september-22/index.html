Born on September 22nd

The birthday of the master builder

Profile

Those born on September 22 tend to be multi-talented, hardworking and intelligent individuals with excellent communication skills and their own slightly eccentric but wonderfully endearing personal style. They are at their happiest and their best when they are creating or building, and such is their love of challenge that no sooner have they completed one project than they jump to the next without a hint of a pause in between.

From an early age these people may have felt that they were here for a reason, and this explains their restless urge to constantly challenge themselves and make their mark on the world. Whatever it is that they feel called to do, they will follow their vision no matter how disruptive or difficult. They may sometimes find that their ideals of fairness and equality and their superior intelligence clash with those in authority over them; this is potentially dangerous as enemies are not conducive to professional or personal success. Learning to compromise and play the game when necessary will be difficult but will make their transition to success smoother.

Until age thirty, relationships are likely to be important, as will the need for harmonious surroundings in both private and professional spheres. Establishing a work-life balance so that they have enough time for themselves and their friendships will be important during these years. After age thirty there is a turning point when they start to become more emotionally self-reliant and in control. If they can learn to be more flexible in their thinking, these are the years when they are likely to come into their own.

Once they have learned to balance their restlessness with plenty of quality time out to recharge and connect with their intuition, amazing opportunities to build a career and a life of great and unique creativity will come their way.

Your Greatest Challenge

Learning to relax.

The Way Forward

Understand that downtime is not time wasted but time gained, because you give yourself an opportunity not just to rest but also to recharge.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between August 23 and September 22

Those dates fall under Virgo. As long as you both learn to balance logic with intuition, this relationship has the potential to be intense and rewarding.

Luck Maker: Do Not Have Enemies

Lucky people try not to make enemies for one simple reason: enemies attract bad luck your way.

At Your Best

Progressive, hardworking, individual.

On the Dark Side

Workaholic, stubborn, isolated.

Love: Lasting Unions

Those born on this day are outgoing and attractive, with plenty of acquaintances, but they can be slow to make close friends. Because they tend to avoid social situations, they may find it hard to meet people they are attracted to outside of work. They believe in lasting unions and can be very affectionate and loyal in a relationship, but if they do not get the attention they feel they deserve, they can become insecure or anxious.

Health: Too Busy

Social isolation is the biggest health risk for those born on this day because it makes them prone to stress, anxiety and depression. Making an effort to stay in touch with friends is therefore recommended. It is important for them to make sure they do not carry their work around at all times, having clear cut-off points when they leave work behind and get on with living. Drinking a cup of chamomile tea when they get home, chatting with friends, going to the movies, reading a book or relaxing in an aromatherapy bath are all great ways to switch off. They need to avoid food that is too spicy or rich as digestive problems may be an issue. A regular exercise program will encourage them to have a more balanced and focused approach to their lives. Carrying a citrine crystal will help them deal with anger and stress. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green will encourage them to find a balance between work, stress and play.

Career: Born Architects

These individuals are well suited to scientific, public service or humanitarian work, as well as the healing or caring professions, although they may also be drawn to writing or research. Other possible career choices include editing, teaching, sales, promotion, public relations, commerce, social reform, design or architecture.

Destiny: To Build Progressive Structures

The life path of those born on this day is to find a balance between their need to push the boundaries and their need to be happy, fulfilled human beings. Once they have learned to be less self- or work-absorbed, their destiny is to build progressive structures from which others might learn.

Power Thought

I act appropriately on my intuition.
