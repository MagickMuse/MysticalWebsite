Born on December 8th

The birthday of passion

Profile

Those born on December 8 tend to sparkle with energy and enthusiasm, and their lively personality attracts attention wherever they go. Their passionate approach to life is their defining feature, and they display intense emotional and sensual responses. When an opportunity presents itself in either their personal or professional life, they rarely hesitate and jump in head first.

Whether committing to an individual, a team, a project or an idea, they cannot see the point of being half-hearted; their nature is to give one hundred percent. True idealists, they are constantly engaged in a quest to find fulfillment, whether emotional, intellectual or spiritual, and their infectious optimism may inspire others to seek their own personal nirvana. The trouble is that the perfect fulfillment they seek is unobtainable, and this can cause them to become manic or confused in their search for stimulation. Relaxing their expectations of perfection and understanding that imperfection is the natural human state is essential for their psychological growth.

Until age forty-three, there is an emphasis on the need for order or structure, and they should take advantage of opportunities to be more practical. This is because they do not always direct their energy well, and poor judgment can lead them into relationships or situations that are destructive or obsessive. After forty-four, they may feel a growing need to develop their individuality.

At any age, the key to their potential for happiness and success is being more cautious and aware in their approach to people and situations, so that passionate intensity does not overpower common sense. With a little more realism added to their stunning repertoire of creativity and idealism, they will find the fulfilling passion they have been searching for, bringing great happiness to others in the process.

Your Greatest Challenge

Being responsible.

The Way Forward

Understand that being responsible and passionate are not mutually exclusive; working together they can put you on the winning team.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between December 22 and January 19

Those dates fall under Capricorn. You may have much to argue about, but the attraction between you is such that making up afterwards will be wonderful.

Luck Maker: Think Before You Act

Lucky people actively take steps to reduce mistakes by learning about the issues involved and listening to knowledgeable people before acting.

At Your Best

Energetic, charismatic, passionate.

On the Dark Side

Obsessive, addictive, irresponsible.

Love: Tempestuous

Those born on this day tend to be attracted to people with opposing views, leading to tempestuous relationships. It is important not to confuse tension with passion. Once in a long-term relationship they can be incredibly romantic and spontaneous, but their partner should be aware that their resolve may not always hold firm when tempted to stray.

Health: Don't Burn the Midnight Oil

These individuals tend to go to bed in the small hours and get up as late as possible. Although everyone has natural sleep preferences, they should try to be in bed by midnight, as studies show this leads to better-quality sleep, which is essential for physical and emotional well-being. They should beware of recreational drugs and alcohol because of addictive tendencies. Moderation is key for diet, and the same goes for exercise, which is extremely important for its mood-boosting powers. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Blue encourages objectivity and responsibility.

Career: Born Dancers

These individuals tend to go where their heart leads them, and as long as they sustain passionate intensity their potential for success is strong in any career. They have a great desire to inspire others, so they may excel as writers, dancers, actors, singers, artists and performers. Self-employment appeals, as do large enterprises with plenty of opportunities for career advancement, change and travel.

Destiny: To Bring Happiness to Others

The life path of those born on this day is to balance passion with a need to be more responsible. Once they can direct their instincts, their destiny is to bring happiness to others.

Power Thought

I am the creative and responsible power in my world.
