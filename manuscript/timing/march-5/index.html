Born on March 5th

The birthday of agony and ecstasy

Profile

On the surface, people born on March 5 are smooth and charming individuals with the gift of gab, but troubled waters lie below. Their easy style conceals a complex personality that is as fascinating as it is frustrating.

The emotional current beneath the light-hearted exterior of those born on this day is extraordinarily powerful. One moment they are entertaining, empathetic companions who can put everyone at ease with their sharp wit and incisive mind. The next moment, however, when their emotional equilibrium is unbalanced, they can dissolve into insecurity, negativity, and fits of temper. Given the instability of their emotional life, it is very important for them to learn to manage their emotions, especially between the ages of sixteen and forty-five when they become more aggressive, assertive, and determined to make their mark. After forty-seven there is another turning point which sees them slow down a little, searching for stability in all areas of their lives.

Underneath the sociable and confident exterior is a highly sensitive soul that desperately needs regular time in quiet, solitary reflection. Inside they may have many hidden fears and insecurities to deal with, and if they do not allow themselves time to acknowledge these shadows, they become the mercy of their impulses with no telling in what direction they will go. They may worry that becoming more balanced will result in a loss of intensity or edge, but they need to understand that cultivating personal willpower and self-control will not lessen but strengthen their creativity and impact.

With their emotional honesty, March 5 people have the ability to bring out both the best and the worst in themselves and others. If they can learn to find balance and use their power responsibly, the world will always be a brighter place with these impulsive individuals around.

Your Greatest Challenge

Staying calm and in control when things do not go your way.

The Way Forward

Put yourself, and not your emotions, in the driving seat of your life.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between May 22 and June 21

Those dates fall under Gemini. You both love adventure and variety; if you can handle each other's mood swings, you will create a supportive bond.

Luck Maker: Do Small Things with Great Love

Sometimes the smallest things can make a huge difference. Add small acts of kindness into your life every day and see how much more positively people regard you and how your luck improves.

At Your Best

Entertaining, intelligent, witty.

On the Dark Side

Insecure, unreliable, negative.

Love: Blowing Hot and Cold

March 5 natives can fall passionately in love one day, only to change their mind and blow cold the next. Given the extremes to which they are prone, they need unconditional love and support from those close to them, although they also need firmness. Once they know what they can and cannot get away with, they are much less likely to test the waters and will remain stable in a relationship.

Health: Highs and Lows

When people born on this day have a good time, they may have trouble knowing when to stop. This can have a negative effect on their health, especially when that good time includes alcohol, rich food, nicotine, sugar, and multiple partners. They may also find it difficult to sleep; if insomnia is a problem, making sure their bedroom is light and airy, taking an aromatherapy bath, or drinking a cup of chamomile tea may help, as will avoiding stimulation from screens, conversation, or exercise before bed. Regular exercise is highly recommended, as are vitamin and mineral supplements. Because they are prone to mood swings, it is especially important that they learn to connect with their inner peace and calm, perhaps through regular meditation, spending time in nature, or listening to beautiful music.

Career: Born Movie Directors

The impulsive nature of these people draws them toward the world of film — where they can make great directors — as well as acting, music, drama, and entertainment. Their love of adventure and excitement may also lead them toward careers involving travel, politics, design, and fashion. They may also be drawn toward social reform and charity work, but they are likely to excel in whatever career they choose because of their intelligence and superior communication skills.

Destiny: To Make Lofty Dreams a Reality

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to work with both their head and their heart. Once they have learned to rein in their impulses, their destiny is to make their lofty vision and dreams for the future a reality.

Power Thought

I acknowledge my spirit at the start of each day.
