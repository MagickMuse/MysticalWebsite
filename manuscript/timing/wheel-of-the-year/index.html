The Wheel of the Year

Reference is often made to the Wheel of the Year, especially among Druid, Wiccan, and other neopagan groups. It is the name given to the unbroken cycle of festivals that turns through the course of the year. The symbolism of the wheel mirrors the cyclical movement of the seasons and the orbits of the stars and planets, and the seasons of a human life are held to echo the same turning, their key moments answering to the changing year.

The names of the eight festivals vary from place to place, yet all mark turning points in the year, and many have been adapted to carry Christian meaning while keeping their older spirit and substance largely intact. It is striking how the same impulse to mark time, the planting and the harvest, the solstices and the equinoxes, takes different shapes wherever people live.
