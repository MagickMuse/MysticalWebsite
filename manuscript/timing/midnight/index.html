Magickal Properties

Midnight has always been known as "the witching hour", and this is high time for magick of all sorts, particularly as a reference to the darkness and danger of winter.

Best Used For

  • Magick of all kinds
  • Working with darkness and danger themes
  • Powerful spellwork
  • Deep transformation
  • Shadow work

The Hours Around Midnight

There are those who believe that the hour immediately preceding midnight, culminating at the stroke of 12, is ideal for benevolent magic. This hour builds toward the threshold with increasing power, perfect for workings of healing, blessing, and positive transformation.

The hour immediately following midnight is considered the most powerful for magical spells stemming from anger and a fierce desire for justice. This is the time for hexes, curses, banishments, and righteous retribution when other methods have failed.
