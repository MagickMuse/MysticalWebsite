Feast of Hathor

Type
Holidays
Related Timing1
📅November 29

A feast honoring the Goddess as Horned Mother through Hathor, and also as Sekhmet, the Lion Goddess of the Sun, and as Bastet, the Cat Goddess of Fertility and Life. This celebration honors the Triple Goddess in her Egyptian form as Bast-Hathor-Sekhmet.
