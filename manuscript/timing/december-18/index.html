Born on December 18th

The birthday of possibility

Profile

Those born on December 18 are blessed with a soaring imagination and a sense of possibility that less imaginative people would dismiss or even ridicule. Their determination to translate their dreams into reality is, however, so powerful that they can withstand almost any criticism.

From an early age, they are likely to have been quick learners, soaking up information and mastering skills long before others. When all this knowledge is added to their incredible creativity, anything is quite literally possible. They may develop a game plan for life in their teens or twenties that includes far-reaching ambitions, and then, as the years pass, they will devote themselves fully to achieving their goals and realizing their dreams. These individuals think in the long term, not the short term, and although progress may appear slow to others, they are slowly, carefully and steadily climbing their way to the very top.

Until age thirty-three, life may offer them opportunities to develop a more practical and realistic approach to achieving their goals. They should take advantage of these by accepting assistance when offered, involving others in their plans and simplifying their workload. They run a real danger otherwise of becoming exhausted, disappointed and alienated. After thirty-four, a turning point may bring a wish to become more independent and to express their individuality.

These years are filled with potential, but whatever their age, the key to success is the ability to set realistic goals and the willingness to slow down so they can get in touch with their intuition and the silence within. Connecting with their feelings will help them see that the sense of wonder, discovery and possibility they long to create in the world around them already exists within; all they need to do is find it.

Your Greatest Challenge

Silence.

The Way Forward

Understand that stillness and aloneness are powerful forces for enlightenment, change and progress.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between March 21 and April 19

Those dates fall under Aries. You are both tremendously energetic and sensual, and there is great potential for long-term fulfillment and happiness.

Luck Maker: Trust Yourself

The next time you make a decision, analyze your feelings and intuition before you proceed. Ask yourself how you feel about something or someone, and why. Trust that you will find the right answer, and you will.

At Your Best

Imaginative, energetic, determined.

On the Dark Side

Unrealistic, preoccupied, unfocused.

Love: No Time for Us

The sense of possibility possessed by those born on this day inspires respect and admiration, but it can also cause frustration because they appear to be taking on too much. Although they are sensual, charismatic and like to take the lead in relationships, they will often bury themselves in work, only emerging when they want support or attention. Others may wish they would simplify their lives so they have more time for friends, loved ones and themselves.

Health: Learn to Pace Yourself

These individuals tend to fill their lives with constant activity. While this makes them extremely productive, it can mean they rarely take time to relax. It is important for them to know their limits and not bite off more than they can chew, as they are prone to stress and burnout. They should also resist the tendency to eat as quickly as possible, and cut down on sugar, caffeine and other stimulants, replacing these with fruit, nuts and seeds. Mild exercise is highly recommended, as are meditation and breathing activities to help them find space and stillness within. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Green encourages natural healing and balance.

Career: Born Directors

These individuals thrive in careers that allow them to think and act independently in pursuit of their visions. They may be drawn to science, technology, art or sport. Other options include business, writing, sales, publishing, teaching, charity work, fundraising, politics, social reform and the worlds of film-making, entertainment and media.

Destiny: To Inspire Others to Follow Their Dreams

The life path of those born on this day is to get in touch with their own feelings and those of others. Once they have made contact with the power of their intuition and set attainable goals, their destiny is, by example, to inspire others to follow their dreams.

Power Thought

Using and trusting my intuition leads to clarity.
