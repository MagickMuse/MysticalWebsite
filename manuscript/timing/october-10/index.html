Born on October 10th

The birthday of the overseer

Profile

Those born on October 10 are natural managers and organizers with an exceptional ability to see the big picture. They have a commanding presence and an instinctive understanding of how all the different parts of a project, team or organization fit together. Like an overseer surveying the landscape from a vantage point, they can identify problems, spot opportunities and coordinate the efforts of others with impressive skill.

They are ambitious and driven, but their ambition is not purely self-serving. They genuinely want to create systems, structures and environments that work well for everyone. They take their responsibilities seriously and others trust them to get things done efficiently and fairly. Their organizational skills are second to none, and they are often the person that colleagues, friends and family turn to when things need sorting out.

Despite their capable exterior, they can be surprisingly sensitive and may struggle with feelings of self-doubt, particularly when their efforts are not recognized or appreciated. They also have a tendency to take on too much responsibility, which can lead to stress and burnout. Learning to delegate and to accept that they cannot control everything is one of their most important life lessons.

Before age forty-two they are likely to be most focused on issues of personal power and emotional transformation. After forty-three, a turning point arrives that highlights a growing need for freedom and expansion. Whatever age they are, they need to ensure that their desire to oversee and organize does not prevent them from enjoying the spontaneous, unplanned moments that give life its sparkle. When they can balance their love of order with an openness to the unexpected, they become truly effective and inspiring leaders.

Your Greatest Challenge

Letting go of control.

The Way Forward

Understand that you cannot control everything. Sometimes the most productive thing you can do is step back and trust others to handle things their way.

You Are Drawn to People Born Between July 23 and August 22

Those dates fall under Leo. You are both natural leaders with big hearts, and this can be a powerful, passionate and mutually supportive partnership.

Luck Maker: Make Room for Serendipity

Lucky people leave room in their schedules and their plans for the unexpected. Some of the best opportunities come from unplanned encounters and spontaneous decisions.

At Your Best

Organized, commanding, fair.

On the Dark Side

Controlling, overworked, unappreciated.

Love: A Steady Hand

Those born on this day are loyal, reliable and devoted partners who provide stability and security in their relationships. They are attracted to people who are equally grounded and responsible. However, they need to make sure they do not try to manage or control their partner, and that they make time for romance, fun and spontaneity in their relationship.

Health: Schedule Your Downtime

These individuals are so busy organizing everyone and everything else that they often forget to take care of themselves. It is essential for them to schedule regular periods of rest and relaxation, and to stick to them as rigorously as they stick to their work schedule. A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and lean protein will fuel their busy lifestyle. Regular exercise is important, and they should choose activities that help them unwind and switch off, such as yoga, swimming or walking in nature. They should also limit their caffeine intake, especially in the afternoon and evening, to ensure quality sleep. Wearing, meditating on and surrounding themselves with the color Orange will encourage warmth, creativity and spontaneity.

Career: Born Managers

These individuals are naturally suited to careers in management, administration, business, finance, accounting and project coordination. Their organizational skills also make them effective in event planning, logistics, engineering and architecture. Their desire to serve others may lead them toward public service, law, education and the healing professions.

Destiny: To Create Systems and Structures That Serve the Greater Good

The life path of those born on this day is to learn to balance their love of order and control with openness to the unexpected. Once they can do this, their destiny is to create efficient, fair and well-organized systems and structures that benefit everyone.

Power Thought

I release the need to control and trust in the natural flow of life.
