Acacia

Acacia Absolute is the genus-level catch-all label for solvent-extracted absolutes drawn from one of the four hundred-plus Acacia species. In commerce the name most often refers to Cassie Absolute, drawn from Acacia farnesiana. See Cassie Absolute for the canonical perfumery oilCassie for the parent plant.

Disambiguation

  • Cassie Absolute (Acacia farnesiana): the sweet acacia profiled in detail at Cassie Absolute. The deep, warm, floral-spicy absolute that dominates oriental fine perfumery.
  • Mimosa Absolute (Acacia dealbata): a separate absolute from the closely related mimosa cousin. See Mimosa.
  • Roman Cassie Absolute (Acacia cavenia): another perfumery absolute traded under similar names.
  • Gum Arabic (Acacia senegal): yields a gum exudation, not an absolute. See Acacia for the gum-producing species and for the resin itself.

Do not confuse acacia absolute with opopanax or bisabol myrrh (Commiphora erythrea), which share a common perfumery name but come from a different family.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Possessing high spiritual vibrations, acacia oil is worn to aid meditation and to develop psychic powers. It aids in prayer, atonement, and divination.
