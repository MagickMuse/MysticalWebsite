Botanical Information

Litsea cubeba essential oil extracted from the fruit of the May Chang tree. Also known as mountain pepper or Chinese pepper.

Extraction & Characteristics

May Chang Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the small pepper-shaped fruits. The finished oil is a pale yellow mobile liquid with an intense, lemony, fresh-fruity odour, sweeter than lemongrass but less tenacious. Its principal constituent is citral at up to 85 percent.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Acne, dermatitis, excessive perspiration, greasy skin, insect repellent, and spots.

Digestive System: Flatulence and indigestion.

Immune System: Epidemics and sanitation.

Recorded actions include antiseptic, deodorant, digestive, disinfectant, insecticidal, stimulant, and stomachic.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant. Possible sensitization in some individuals.

Where You Will Encounter It

May Chang Essential Oil is used extensively as a fragrance component in air fresheners, soaps, deodorants, colognes, toiletries, and perfumes. It is employed in flavouring work, especially for fruit products, and serves as a global source of natural citral.

Scent Profile

Fresh, lemony citrus scent.

Magickal Uses

Therapeutic Applications

  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Excessive Perspiration: Apply via skin oil/lotion or bath.
  • Insect Repellent: Apply via skin oil/lotion or vaporization.
  • Spots: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
