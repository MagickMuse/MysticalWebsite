Cedarwood Essential Oil is distilled from the bark of various cedar species, creating a dry, musky, smoky oil that has been used for protection and purification since ancient times. Available in several varieties (Atlas cedar (Cedrus atlantica), red cedar (Juniperus virginiana), Texas cedarwood (Juniperus mexicana), and western red cedar (Thuja plicata)), all of which share similar magickal properties.

Scent Note

Cedarwood has a woodsy scent. Its energies are particularly useful in enhancing spirituality.

Physical Uses

Cedarwood stimulates the emotional center of the brain and has a calming effect. In emotional aromatherapy it can relieve stress, help ease depression, banish stress-induced insomnia, and promote a spiritual mind-set. Dilute heavily before any topical use; cedarwood is a potential sensitizer, and a dilution of 0.5 percent is suggested. Avoid during pregnancy.

Herbal Lore

In antiquity, the written word was stored in cedar trunks because the strong scent kept pests away from precious parchment and vellum scrolls. If something was a great work of literature, it was said to be "worthy of being encased in cedar." Cedar chests discovered at the Temple of Apollo at Utica were carbon-dated at over two thousand years old.

The protective qualities of cedar extend to humans as well. The skins of animals (calves, goats, and sheep) used for parchments and vellum have a similar composition to human skin. Cedar coffins and caskets gained popularity when their preservative nature was discovered.

Magickal Uses

For psychic protection, place a drop of diluted cedarwood on your third eye for protection that doesn't block beneficial information.

For anointing, use diluted cedarwood essential oil as an all-purpose anointing oil for candles, talismans, mojo bags, and divination tools.

For cleansing ritual space, diffuse one drop of cedarwood essential oil in 100 ml water before prayer, ritual, and magickal workings.

For blocked chakras, anoint energy centers with diluted cedarwood oil (patch test first on sensitive skin).

To banish nightmares, place a drop of cedarwood essential oil on a tissue inside your pillowcase before bed.

For emotional purification, diffuse 1 drop cedarwood in 100 ml water or anoint your heart with diluted cedarwood oil to purify caustic emotions.

For emotional balance, anoint a pocket-size tiger's eye with diluted cedarwood essential oil and carry during emotionally turbulent times.

For prosperity and luck, place a drop of cedarwood on the bottom of each shoe before leaving the house.

For increased satisfaction, carry a sprig of cedar in a cotton bag on a cord around the neck beneath clothing, or place a drop of cedarwood essential oil on a tissue inside a cotton bag.

For purification during networking, anoint wrists with diluted cedarwood essential oil before events with frequent handshakes. Negative people will avoid the uplifting scent while it helps with nervousness.

For clairvoyant work, diffuse a drop of cedarwood essential oil in 100 ml of water before beginning. Gallery readings proceed more smoothly with the smell of cedar.

To consecrate altars, use diluted cedarwood on holy spaces. Patch test on finished surfaces first to ensure no discoloration.

For wand consecration, cedarwood wands are well suited to various magick. If cedar is hard to obtain, anoint a pinewood wand with diluted cedarwood oil. The carrier oil seals the pine while the cedarwood permeates the porous wood.

Spellwork Correspondences

Rub oil from center to either end to dress the candle, from top to bottom to draw energy toward you, or bottom to top to banish. Oils are flammable; use with care. Spellwork uses include cleansing, strength, and meditation.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

An oil of Jupiter, promoting peace, contentment, wealth, and good fortune, in addition to driving away all evil and negativity. Believed to attract good spirits and repel bad ones.
