Cananga Essential Oil is produced by water distillation from the freshly picked flowers of Cananga odorata var. macrophylla.

Characteristics

A greenish-yellow to orange viscous liquid with a sweet, floral-balsamic, tenacious odour. Blends well with calamus, birch tar, copaiba balsam, neroli, oakmoss, jasmine, labdanum, guaiacwood, and oriental-type bases.

Principal Constituents

Caryophyllene, benzyl acetate, benzyl alcohol, farnesol, terpineol, borneol, geranyl acetate, safrol, linalol, limonene, methyl salicylate, plus over a hundred minor compounds.

Actions

Antidepressant, antiseborrhoeic, antiseptic, aphrodisiac, hypotensive, nervine, sedative, tonic.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Skin Care: Insect bites.

Nervous System: Depression, nervous tension, and stress-related complaints.

Safety Data

Non-toxic, non-irritant, possible sensitization.

Other Uses

Fragrance component in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially men's fragrances. Limited use as a flavour ingredient.

Cananga Plant

See Cananga for the parent plant.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Anxiety: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • High Blood Pressure: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Insect Bites: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
