Skin Benefits

Very penetrating and much heavier than apricot kernel oil. Cold-pressed avocado oil has incredible fats that make for a rich moisturizer for all skin types. Can help clear problem skin and has anti-inflammatory properties. Contains vitamin E and other antioxidants.

Magickal Properties

Avocado oil is magickal by itself. That magick involves creating new life and new beginnings as well as prosperity, the energy of new life waiting to burst out of the seed.

Because avocado has that round, full seed, it is useful in magick for fertility, including ritual oils for procreation, increasing sex drive in a partner, and lust. Not just for physical fertility. If you need to make your business fertile, using an avocado-based oil is the way to go.

Shelf Life

Relatively short shelf life. Make small batches or only what will be used immediately. Use within three months.
