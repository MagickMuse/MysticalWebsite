A dark greenish-brown viscous liquid produced by solvent extraction from the flowers of Calendula (Calendula officinalis). The absolute carries an intensely sharp, herbaceous odour. The real calendula absolute is only produced in France and only in small quantities, making it difficult to find on the open market.

Extraction & Characteristics

Produced by solvent extraction from the flowers. The absolute contains calendulin (a yellow resin), waxes, and volatile oil.

Therapeutic Uses

For skin care, the absolute is applied to burns, cuts, eczema, greasy skin, inflammations, insect bites, rashes, and wounds. The source notes that the infused oil is very valuable in aromatherapy for its powerful skin-healing properties; see Calendula Infused Oil for the more accessible form.

Blending

Blends well with Oakmoss Absolute, Hyacinth Absolute, and floral and citrus oils.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used in high-class perfumery.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Cuts & Sores: Apply via skin oil/lotion or compress.
