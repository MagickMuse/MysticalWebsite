Camphor Essential Oil is distilled from the wood and bark of the camphor laurel tree (Cinnamomum camphora), producing a sharp, penetrating oil with powerful cooling properties.

Botanical Information

Scientific name: Cinnamomum camphora

Botanical family: Lauraceae

Origin: China, Japan, Taiwan

Source: Wood and bark

Evaporation: Top to middle note

Scent Profile

Description: Sharp, penetrating, medicinal, cooling

Impact: Clarifying, purifying, mentally stimulating

Magickal Correspondences

Element: Water

Planet: Moon

Magickal uses: Chastity, health, divination

Tincture

ColorClear
ScentPenetrating, cool
UsesLessening desire, healing amulets, Full Moon baths

Use only real camphor for tincturing. Synthetic camphor will not produce the same magickal results.

Magickal Uses

Camphor is traditionally used to lessen sexual drive. Simply smelling camphor cools ardor immediately. Place near the bed for this same purpose.

Use in health and healing workings. A bag of camphor hung around the neck traditionally wards off colds and flu.

A small amount of camphor added to divinatory incenses strengthens psychic powers. Burn in the bedroom prior to sleep to induce prophetic dreams.

Safety

⚠️ Caution: Not recommended for internal use. External use only. Do not use near the faces of small children as it can cause respiratory distress.

Extraction

The crude camphor is collected from mature trees (over fifty years old) by steam distillation from the wood. It is then purified by vacuum distillation and filter pressed, which separates the oil into three fractions known as white, brown, and yellow camphor.

Characteristics (White Camphor)

A colourless to pale yellow liquid with a sharp, piercing, camphoraceous odour. Top to middle note.

Principal Constituents

White camphor contains primarily cineol, pinene, terpineol, menthol, and thymol, among others. It contains no safrol. The brown camphor fraction contains up to 80 percent safrol; the yellow fraction is mainly safrol with sesquiterpenes and sesquiterpene alcohols.

Actions (White Camphor)

Anaesthetic, analgesic, antidepressant, antiseptic, antispasmodic, antiviral, cardiac, carminative, counter-irritant, diuretic, expectorant, febrifuge, hypertensive, insecticide, laxative, rubefacient, stimulant (nerves, adrenal cortex, respiration, circulation), sudorific, vermifuge, vulnerary.

Aromatherapy / Home Use (White Camphor Only)

Skin Care: Acne, inflammation, oily conditions, spots; insect prevention (flies, moths, etc.).

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Arthritis, muscular aches and pains, rheumatism, sprains.

Respiratory System: Bronchitis, coughs, colds, fever.

Immune System: Colds, flu, infectious diseases.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Fever: Apply via compress or bath.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Aches and Pains: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Sprains & Strains: Apply via compress.
  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Insect Repellent: Apply via skin oil/lotion or vaporization.
  • Irritated & Inflamed Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Spots: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Chills: Apply via massage or bath.

Safety Data (White Camphor)

Non-toxic (in white form), non-irritant, non-sensitizing. Brown and yellow camphor are toxic and carcinogenic and should not be used in therapy at all. Avoid during pregnancy.

Other Uses

The oil is used in pharmaceutical preparations, in detergents, soaps, disinfectants, and household sprays, and in some perfumes (especially of the lavender type). The brown and yellow fractions are used as a source of safrol and natural cineol.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Wear a drop to strengthen psychic powers. Also anoint yourself when you have decided to break off with a lover, or when they have done so with you and you find it hard to let go.
