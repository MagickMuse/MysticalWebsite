Cubeb Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the unripe but fully grown berries of Piper cubeba. The result is a viscous oil with a pale greenish-yellow to bluish cast and a warm, spicy-woody aroma touched with a soft camphor undertone. A small quantity of oleoresin is also produced from the same berries. Cubeb shares its botanical family (Piperaceae) with black pepper but distills out a cooler, more resinous character.

Botanical Information

Scientific name: Piper cubeba

Botanical family: Piperaceae

Origin: Native to Indonesia; cultivated across southeast Africa, often interplanted with coffee crops. The essential oil is produced almost entirely at source in Indonesia.

Source of plant's oil: Unripe but fully grown fruits (berries)

Evaporation: Middle note

Extraction & Characteristics

Method: Steam distillation from the crushed unripe berries

Scent: A viscous, pale greenish-to-bluish-yellow liquid carrying warm spicy-woody character with a faint camphor undertone

Constituents: Predominantly sesquiterpenes and monoterpenes, including caryophyllene, cadinene, cubebene, and sabinene, among others.

Safety

Cubeb oil is generally classed as non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing in topical aromatic use. Adulteration is common in the cubeb trade: false cubebs (Piper crassipes) and other related Indonesian species are routinely substituted for the true berry, so reputable sourcing is essential. Internal use is also traditionally avoided in cases of nephritis.

Therapeutic Actions

Antiseptic (pulmonary and genito-urinary), antispasmodic, antiviral, bactericidal, carminative, diuretic, expectorant, and a general stimulant.

Aromatherapy Uses

  • Respiratory system: bronchitis, catarrh, congestion, chronic coughs, sinusitis, throat infections
  • Digestive system: flatulence, indigestion, piles, sluggish digestion
  • Genito-urinary system: cystitis, leucorrhoea, urethritis

Blending Partners

Cubeb pairs especially well with Cananga Essential Oil, Galbanum Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Rosemary Essential Oil, Black Pepper Essential Oil, Allspice Essential Oil, and other spice oils.

Other Uses

Cubeb oil features in diuretic and urinary-antiseptic pharmaceutical preparations and supplies a fragrance accent to soaps, detergents, toiletries, cosmetics, and perfumes. It also serves as a flavouring agent across most major food categories and is used in tobacco flavouring.
