Pine Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the needles of various pine species, producing a fresh, resinous oil with strong cleansing properties.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.

Scent Profile

Fresh, clean, and resinous with woody undertones. The scent evokes forest clearings and crisp mountain air.

Magickal Uses

Pine's fresh, clean scent makes it a natural choice for purification work. Add to cleansing blends or diffuse to clear stagnant energy from spaces.

The protective nature of evergreen trees extends to their essential oil. Include pine in protective formulas or anoint thresholds with diluted oil.

Pine supports prosperity workings. Add to money-drawing blends.

The cleansing properties of pine support healing work. Include in healing formulas, particularly those focused on respiratory health or clearing illness from a space.

Spellwork Correspondences

Candle Dressing: Rub oil from center to either end to "dress" the candle, from top to bottom to draw energy toward you, or bottom to top to banish. Oils are flammable, use with care.

Spellwork Uses: Energy, Cleansing, Strength, Clarity, Action, Protection

Traditional Grimoire Notes

An oil of Mars — purifying and deodorizing in nature. Particularly well-suited to exorcism work and defense against negative forces and influences.
