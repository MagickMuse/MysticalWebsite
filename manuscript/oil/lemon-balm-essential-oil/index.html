◇Notes/Warnings
Use in low dilution due to possible skin sensitization and dermal irritation. Botanical Information

Melissa officinalis from the Lamiaceae family, extracted from small white flowers. Originates from France and Utah. Middle note with fast absorption. Latin binomial melissa means honeybee, reflecting bees' preference for this plant. Due to high price of this highly prized sedative oil, often diluted with carrier oil for ease of use.

Scent Profile

Citrusy, sweet, clean, and fresh. Encourages gentle nature and soothes what ails you. The oil blends well with lavender, geranium, floral, and citrus oils. Principal constituents include citral, citronellol, eugenol, geraniol, and linalyl acetate.

Aromatherapy Use

Lemon balm oil is reached for across several systems of the body. In skin care it addresses allergies, insect bites, and acts as an insect repellent, and at very low concentrations it is a valuable oil for treating eczema and other skin problems. For the respiratory system it supports asthma, bronchitis, and chronic coughs. In the digestive system it eases colic, indigestion, and nausea. For the genito-urinary system it is used for menstrual problems. On the nervous system it addresses anxiety, depression, hypertension, insomnia, migraine, nervous tension, shock, and vertigo. Reported actions are antidepressant, antihistaminic, antispasmodic, bactericidal, carminative, cordial, diaphoretic, emmenagogue, febrifuge, insect-repellent, nervine, sedative, stomachic, sudorific, tonic, uterine, and vermifuge.

Other Uses

Lemon balm oil is occasionally used in pharmaceutical preparations and is used extensively as a fragrance component in toiletries, cosmetics, and perfumes. It is employed in most major food categories, including alcoholic and soft drinks.

Herbal Lore

So beloved by bees that its Latin name means honeybee. As bees constantly work, lemon balm never stops working for the practitioner. Purifying qualities so renowned it once scented wooden furniture for purification, and modern lemon-scented wood polishes derive from this practice and the aromatic idea that lemon equals clean.

Though melissa is prolific mint family herb, only small white blossoms create this delicately scented oil. Ethical companies diluting this expensive sedative clearly label dilution for consumer protection.
