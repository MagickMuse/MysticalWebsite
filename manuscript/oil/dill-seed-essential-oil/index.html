Dill Seed Essential Oil is steam-distilled (sometimes water-distilled) from the fruit or seed of Dill (Anethum graveolens), an annual or biennial herb in the Apiaceae family. The seed oil is produced mainly in Europe (France, Hungary, Germany, England, and Spain).

Extraction & Characteristics

The oil is a colourless to pale yellow mobile liquid with a light, fresh, warm-spicy scent. Principal constituents are carvone (30–60 per cent), limonene, phellandrene, eugenol, and pinene, among others. Several chemotypes exist; phellandrene is present in the English and Spanish oils but not in the German.

Related Species

Indian or East Indian dill (A. sowa) is widely cultivated in India and Japan. A commercial oil is produced from its seed with a different chemical composition that contains 'dill apiol'.

Folk Tradition

Dill has been used since the earliest times as both a medicinal and culinary herb. In Germany and Scandinavia it is used with fish and cucumber, and the seeds baked in bread. In the west and east it serves as a soothing digestive aid for indigestion, wind, colic, and similar complaints, especially in children, a use still current in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia.

Therapeutic Uses

In the digestive system, dill seed oil addresses colic, dyspepsia, flatulence, and indigestion. In the genito-urinary and endocrine systems, it is used for lack of periods and to promote milk flow in nursing mothers.

Blending

Dill seed blends well with elemi, Mint Essential Oil, Caraway Essential Oil, Nutmeg Essential Oil, and other spice and citrus oils.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Dill seed oil is used in some pharmaceutical digestive preparations such as 'dill water', and extensively as a flavouring in alcoholic and soft drinks and foodstuffs, especially pickles and condiments.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Colic: Apply via massage.
  • Griping Pains: Apply via massage.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
  • Amenorrhoea: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Lack of Nursing Milk: Apply via massage.
