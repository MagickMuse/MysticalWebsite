Caraway Essential Oil is the steam-distilled oil of Carum carvi seeds.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Dried ripe seeds (fruit) of Carum carvi

Method: Steam distillation, with a typical yield of 2 to 8 percent

Note: Top to Middle

Scent: Crude caraway oil is a pale yellowish-brown liquid with a harsh, spicy aroma. The redistilled oil is colourless to pale yellow with a strong, warm, sweet-spicy fragrance reminiscent of fresh rye bread. Blends well with jasmine, cinnamon, cassia, and other spices, but is very overpowering on its own.

Constituents: Mainly carvone (50 to 60 percent) and limonene (around 40 percent), with carveol, dihydrocarveol, dihydrocarvone, pinene, and phellandrene among the supporting compounds.

Therapeutic Uses

Respiratory Support: Bronchitis, coughs, and laryngitis.

Digestive Support: Dyspepsia, colic, flatulence, gastric spasm, nervous indigestion, and poor appetite.

Immune Support: Colds.

Blending

Blends well with jasmine, cinnamon, cassia, and other spice oils. Caraway is very overpowering on its own.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-sensitizing in normal use, but may cause dermal irritation at high concentrations. Always dilute well and patch-test before broader use. Avoid during pregnancy because of its emmenagogue action.

Other Uses

  • Used in carminative, stomachic, and laxative pharmaceutical preparations, and as a flavour ingredient to mask unpleasant tastes.
  • A fragrance and flavour component in toothpaste, mouthwash, cosmetics, and perfumes.
  • Used extensively as a flavour in food, especially condiments. The German brandy Kummel is made from the seeds.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Colic: Apply via massage.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Laryngitis & Hoarseness: Apply via steam inhalation.
  • Cramp & Gastric Spasm: Apply via massage or compress.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
