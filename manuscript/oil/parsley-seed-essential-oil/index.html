Notes/Warnings
Moderately toxic and irritant. Apiol (the dominant constituent of the seed oil) has been shown to have irritant properties, and myristicin has been shown to have toxic properties. Otherwise non-sensitizing. Use in moderation and well diluted. Avoid during pregnancy.

A yellow, amber, or brownish liquid steam distilled from the seeds of Parsley (Petroselinum sativum). The oil carries a warm, woody-spicy, herbaceous odour. The principal oil-producing countries are France, Germany, Holland, and Hungary.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the seeds. Principal constituents are mainly apiol, with myristicin, tetramethoxyallyl-benzene, pinene, and volatile fatty acids. An oleoresin is also produced from the seeds by solvent extraction for flavouring use.

Therapeutic Uses

For circulation, muscles, and joints, the oil is applied to accumulation of toxins, arthritis, broken blood vessels, cellulitis, rheumatism, and sciatica. For the digestive system, it addresses colic, flatulence, indigestion, and haemorrhoids. Genito-urinary applications include amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, aid in labour, cystitis, and urinary infection. Folk tradition also records the plant's use for stimulating hair growth and helping to eliminate head lice.

Blending

Blends well with Rose Oil, Neroli Essential Oil (orange blossom), Cananga Essential Oil, Tea Tree Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Clary Sage Essential Oil, and spice oils.

Safety

Moderately toxic and irritant. Apiol is irritant; myristicin is toxic in concentrated form. Non-sensitizing. Use in moderation and well diluted. Avoid during pregnancy.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used in soaps, detergents, colognes, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially men's fragrances. Also used in some carminative and digestive remedies such as gripe waters, and in food flavourings (meats, pickles, sauces, alcoholic and soft drinks).

Therapeutic Applications

  • Colic: Apply via massage.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Accumulation of Toxins: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.

  • Lice: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
