Skin Benefits

Sweet almond oil is commonly used in cosmetics and as a carrier oil in many different applications. It is very gentle on the skin and has a light texture. This oil is a good choice for massage oils, as it is not quickly absorbed, and anointing oils. Sweet almond is a nice alternative to the heavy feeling of olive oil.

Magickal Properties

In magick, the oil is useful for prosperity work. It is beneficial for asking the blessings of nature spirits such as the fae or daevas. The energy of sweet almond is that unconditional positive regard. Magickally, it is a gentle oil and will work forgivingly. It is an all-purpose oil that is well suited for protection spells, especially spells involving children and their innocence. It has been used in love spells for that gentle, innocent first love.

Shelf Life

The shelf stability of sweet almond oil is nine to twelve months.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Sweet almond oil, the symbol of wakefulness to the ancient Egyptians, is used in rituals (anointing candles, money, etc.) and also added to money incenses. A good carrier oil.
