West Indian Bay Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the leaves of Pimenta racemosa.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Leaves of Pimenta racemosa (an oleoresin is also produced in small quantities)

Method: Water or steam distillation

Note: Top

Scent: A dark yellow mobile liquid with a fresh, spicy top note and a sweet, balsamic undertone.

Constituents: Eugenol (up to 56 percent), myrcene, chavicol, with smaller amounts of methyl eugenol, linalool, and limonene.

Blending

Blends with lavender, lavandin, rosemary, geranium, ylang ylang, and citrus or spice oils.

Therapeutic Uses

Hair & Scalp Care: Dandruff, greasy or lifeless hair, hair growth, scalp stimulation.

Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Muscular and articular aches and pains, neuralgia, poor circulation, rheumatism, sprains, and strains.

Immune Support: Colds, flu, and infectious illness.

Safety

Moderately toxic due to its high eugenol content and a known mucous membrane irritant. Use in moderation only and always dilute well in a carrier. Avoid during pregnancy. Unlike bay laurel, it does not appear to cause dermal irritation or sensitization, but a patch test is still wise.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Neuralgia: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Sciatica: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Aches and Pains: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Baldness & Hair Care: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Dandruff: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Low Blood Pressure: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Poor Circulation: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Sprains & Strains: Apply via compress.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

A very powerful scent, useful for protection, meditation, visions, divination, exorcism, purification, and power in general.
