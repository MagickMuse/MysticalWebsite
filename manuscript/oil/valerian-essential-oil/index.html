Valerian Essential Oil is steam distilled from the rhizomes of Valerian. An absolute and concrete are also produced by solvent extraction of the rhizomes.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Rhizomes of Valeriana fauriei and related species.

Method: Steam distillation produces the essential oil. Solvent extraction produces a concrete and absolute.

Appearance: The essential oil is an olive to brown liquid that darkens with age. The absolute is an olive-brown viscous liquid.

Scent: The essential oil has a warm-woody, balsamic, musky odour with a green top note in fresh oils. The absolute is balsamic-green, woody, and bitter-sweet, with a strong odour.

Principal constituents: Mainly bornyl acetate and isovalerate, with caryophyllene, pinenes, valeranone, ionone, eugenyl isovalerate, borneol, patchouli alcohol, and valerianol, among others.

Therapeutic Actions

Mild anodyne, antidandruff, diuretic, antispasmodic, bactericidal, carminative, depressant of the central nervous system, hypnotic, hypotensive, regulator, sedative, and stomachic.

Therapeutic Uses

Nervous system: Insomnia, nervous indigestion, migraine, restlessness, and tension states.

Blending

Blends well with Patchouli Essential Oil, Costus Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Pine Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Atlas Cedarwood Essential Oil, mandarin, Petitgrain Essential Oil, and Rosemary Essential Oil.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant, but may cause sensitization in some individuals. Use in moderation.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used in pharmaceutical preparations as a relaxant and in herbal teas. The oil and absolute are used as fragrance components in soaps and in moss and forest fragrances. Used to flavour tobacco, root beer, liqueurs, and apple flavourings.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Migraine: Apply via compress.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
  • Insomnia: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
