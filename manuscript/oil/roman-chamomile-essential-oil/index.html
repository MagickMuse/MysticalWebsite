Botanical Information

Scientific name: Chamaemelum nobile

Botanical family: Asteraceae

Origin: Southern Europe, Western Europe, Mediterranean, North America, England, Belgium, Hungary, Italy, France, United States, Egypt

Source of plant's oil: Flowers

Evaporation: Middle note

Scent Profile

Description: Herbal, apple-like, green, sunny

Impact: Releases depression, anxiety, and insomnia. Relieves stress responses in the body.

Application

Topical, diffuse, direct inhalation

Therapeutic Uses

Roman chamomile oil acts on a wide range of body systems. It is analgesic, anti-anaemic, antineuralgic, antiphlogistic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, bactericidal, carminative, cholagogue, cicatrisant, digestive, emmenagogue, febrifuge, hepatic, hypnotic, nerve-sedative, stomachic, sudorific, tonic, vermifuge, and vulnerary.

Constituents

Mainly esters of angelic and tiglic acids at approximately 85 percent, with pinene, farnesol, nerolidol, chamazulene, pinocarvone, and cineol among the remainder. Steam-distilled from the flower heads, the oil is a pale blue liquid that turns yellow on keeping, with a warm, sweet, fruity-herbaceous scent.

Warnings

Contact a certified aromatherapist before using chamomile essential oil while pregnant. Avoid this oil if you are taking blood thinners.

Blending

Chamomile blends well with Bergamot Essential Oil, Cedar Essential Oil, Lavender, Rosemary, Myrrh, and Angelica Root Essential Oil. These combinations create nurturing, empowering, and soothing blends.

Roman chamomile also blends well with Clary Sage Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Jasmine Absolute, labdanum, Neroli Essential Oil, Rose Oil, and Geranium Essential Oil.

Uses

Protection from lightning: Place 9 drops of chamomile oil on a cotton handkerchief and hang in the attic to protect the home from lightning strikes.

Nightmare prevention: Place 3 drops of chamomile on a tissue inside your pillowcase to banish nightmares.

Menstruation: Place 1 drop of chamomile essential oil in an aroma locket each day until menses resumes.

Calming nerves: Diffuse 1 drop of chamomile essential oil in 100 ml water to calm frazzled nerves.

Sore muscles: Blend 1 drop Roman chamomile in 1 tablespoon carrier oil and gently massage into sore muscles.

Patience: Place a drop of chamomile in an aroma locket and wear, or place 3 drops on a tissue and carry near your heart to increase patience.
