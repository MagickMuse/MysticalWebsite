Scent Profile

The distilled oil is a pale yellow or greenish mobile liquid with a mild, sweet, balsamic-peppery odour. The crude balsam itself is a viscous yellowy-brown to greenish-grey liquid that hardens on exposure to air, carrying a milder woody and slightly spicy note.

The oil blends well with: cananga, ylang ylang, vanilla, jasmine, violet, and other florals.

The crude balsam blends well with: styrax, amyris, lavandin, cedarwood, lavender, oakmoss, woods, and spices.

Principal Constituents

Mainly caryophyllene.

Extraction

  1. The crude balsam is collected by drilling holes into the tree trunks. It is one of the most plentiful naturally occurring perfume materials.
  2. The essential oil is obtained by dry distillation from the crude balsam. The para balsam type, with its high oil content of 60 to 80%, is the form most often used for distillation.

Aromatherapy Use

  • Digestive System: Intestinal infections, piles.
  • Respiratory System: Bronchitis, chills, colds, coughs.
  • Genito-Urinary System: Cystitis.
  • Nervous System: Stress-related conditions.

Actions

Bactericidal, balsamic, disinfectant, diuretic, expectorant, stimulant.

Other Uses

The oleoresin is used in pharmaceutical products, especially cough medicines and diuretics. The oil and crude balsam are extensively used as a fixative and fragrance component in all types of perfumes, soaps, cosmetics, and detergents. The crude balsam is also used in porcelain painting.

Safety

⚠️
Relatively non-toxic and non-irritant, with possible sensitization. Large doses can cause vomiting and diarrhoea.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Hemorrhoids: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Chills: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Cystitis: Apply via compress, bath, or douche.
