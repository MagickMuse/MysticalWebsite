◇Drug Interactions
None known. Do not use on or around infants and young children (1,8-cineole content). Note: 'Ravensara aromatica' (true ravensara) and 'Ravintsara' (Cinnamomum camphora ct. cineole) are often confused — verify your source. True ravensara contains methyl chavicol and limonene; ravintsara is rich in 1,8-cineole.
