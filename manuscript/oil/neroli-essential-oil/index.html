Botanical Information

Citrus aurantium from the Rutaceae family, extracted from flowers through solvent extraction. Originates from Morocco. Top note with fast absorption. Neroli oil derived from bitter orange tree flowers. Introduced to Mediterranean by Arab traders from China, and extremely costly for centuries, available only to royalty and ultrarich.

Three oils from one tree: Bitter orange oil (steam-distilled from peel), neroli oil (solvent-extracted from flowers), and petitgrain oil (middle note distilled from stems and leaves). All three share magickal uses. Important: Cold-pressed bitter orange is phototoxic; petitgrain and steam-distilled bitter orange are not phototoxic.

Scent Profile

Citrusy, sweet, and lightly floral. Fights depression and brings peace.

Magickal Correspondences

Deity: Guan Yu (Taoist god of brotherhood and righteousness in context of war)

Crystal: Sunstone for banishing fear

Herbal Lore

Orange blossoms traditionally associated with Hera and Juno, placed in bridal bouquets to calm pre-wedding jitters. Brides anointed with orange blossom waters asking Hera or Juno to bless union with fertility and happiness.

Commonly diluted and chemically adulterated. Beware of "neroli oil" in clear glass bottles, unreasonably cheap, or clear in color. Practitioners often substitute bitter orange or petitgrain for cost-prohibitive neroli in magickal work.

Magickal Uses

Solar associations make well suited to protection work. Stands up under heavy fire, works on psychic attacks. Diffuse in home if fearing attack on home or residents. Anoint intended target with diluted oil (3 drops in 10 ml roller bottle with carrier). Prayers acceptable but not required.

Calming neroli and soothing petitgrain work as sedatives, perfect for sleep and stress relief magick. Place tissue with drop inside pillowcase to induce sleep. Diffuse 1 drop in 100 ml water diffuser for stress relief.

One of most beneficial antianxiety essential oils. Encourages release of negative emotions. Well suited to help reduce impact of sexual trauma, especially with talk therapy from licensed therapist. Antianxiety effect markedly noted in petitgrain as well.

Matrimonial associations sometimes overshadow fertility aspect. For new family addition or fertile environment for business or project, anoint green candle with diluted oil. Write intention on paper, place underneath candleholder, light candle (in fireproof container), state intent aloud. Burn during waxing portion of month or on Friday.

Increases both psychic senses and physical awareness. Makes incredible addition to protection and hex-breaking blends. Alerts person monitoring wards that someone attempting to cause trouble. Write intention on paper, place under candleholder, light candle, state intent aloud. Burn during waning portion of month or on Sunday. Black candle suggested but substitute as needed.

Change is large part of human condition. If change occurs suddenly or too close to home, grief natural. Neroli helps process grief beneficially. If grief impacts day-to-day life after significant period, remember help is available.

Excellent for establishing and nurturing self-confidence. Uplifting oils act as emotional scaffolding while structure strengthened. Anoint wrists and behind ears with diluted oil or add drop to aroma locket until strength restored.

Entrusted with magick for banishing depression. With depression can come physical sensitivity to pressures. Helps both conditions. Anoint figural candle in healing color (pink, green, pale blue), name for person experiencing pain and depression. Burn on Sunday.

Citrus scents and florals associated with joy and happiness. Diffuse in home or office or add to aroma locket for extra spring in step.

Not just orange's sleepy cousin. Excellent collaborator for guided journeys and meditation of all kinds. Diffuse in meditative spaces, yoga studios, and more for sense of relaxation and peace rarely found.

Tension no match for neroli or petitgrain. Petitgrain is divine middle note tension tamer. Neroli-based bath oil particularly suited to tension-relieving baths. Traditional Grimoire Notes

Also known as Orange Blossom, this magnetic oil resonates strongly with love and attraction. Traditionally worn to draw romantic interest and reputed to encourage proposals of marriage. Sometimes applied between the breasts as a drawing oil.
