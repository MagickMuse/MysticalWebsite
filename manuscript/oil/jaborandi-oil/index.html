Jaborandi

Jaborandi Oil is the essential oil distilled from the dried leaflets of Jaborandi (Pilocarpus jaborandi). It is toxic and is not used in aromatherapy or home practice.

Safety

Do not use Jaborandi Oil in home aromatherapy, externally or internally. It is an oral toxin, a skin irritant, and an abortifacient. Avoid during pregnancy.

Extraction & Characteristics

The oil is produced by steam distillation from the dried leaflets. It is an orange or yellow liquid with a sweet-herbaceous, fruity odour. Its principal constituent is pilocarpine, with isopilocarpine, pilocarpidine, methyl nonyl ketone, dipentene, and other hydrocarbons also present.

Where You Will Encounter It

The crude oil itself is rarely used. Pilocarpine is extracted for various hypodermic medical solutions. The oil sees little use in perfumery or flavour work because of its toxicity.
