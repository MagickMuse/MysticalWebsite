A pale yellow to brownish yellow mobile liquid steam distilled from the flowering tops of Moroccan Chamomile, also known as Chamomile Maroc or Ormenis oil.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the flowering tops of Ormenis multicaulis. The oil is a mobile liquid ranging from pale yellow to brownish yellow, with a fresh-herbaceous top note and a sweet, rich-balsamic undertone. Principal constituents are not currently documented.

Therapeutic Uses

In aromatherapy, the oil supports sensitive skin and is used for headache, migraine, and insomnia. It eases irritability and is applied for menstrual conditions including amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, and menopausal complaints. It is also used for colic, colitis, and liver and spleen congestion. The oil exhibits antispasmodic, cholagogue, emmenagogue, hepatic, and sedative actions. Therapeutic history and usage remain limited.

Blending

Blends well with Cypress Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Vetivert Essential Oil, cedarwood (Atlas Cedarwood Essential Oil, Texas Cedarwood Essential Oil, and Virginian Cedarwood Essential Oil), Oakmoss Absolute, Rock Rose Essential Oil (labdanum), Frankincense Essential Oil (olibanum), and artemisia oils.

Safety

Generally non-toxic and non-irritant. More specific safety data is not yet established.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used extensively in perfumery work, especially in colognes, chyprès, and fougère fragrances.
