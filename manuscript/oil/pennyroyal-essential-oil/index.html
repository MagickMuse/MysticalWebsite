DO NOT USE PENNYROYAL IN AROMATHERAPY, INTERNALLY OR EXTERNALLY. Pennyroyal Essential Oil is an oral toxin and an abortifacient. Ingestion of large doses has resulted in death. Avoid during pregnancy.

Pennyroyal Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the herb of Pennyroyal (Mentha pulegium), the small mint-family plant whose oil is rich in the toxic ketone pulegone.

Safety

Pennyroyal oil should not be used in aromatherapy, whether internally or externally. It is an oral toxin and an abortifacient because of its pulegone content, and ingestion of large doses has resulted in death.

Extraction & Characteristics

The essential oil is produced by steam distillation from the fresh or slightly dried herb. It is a colourless or pale yellow liquid with a very fresh, minty-herbaceous odour. Its principal constituents are mainly pulegone, with menthone, iso-menthone, octanol, piperitenone, and trans-iso-pulegone. Constituents vary by source: Moroccan pennyroyal oil contains up to 96 percent pulegone. The oil is recorded as blending with Geranium Essential Oil, Rosemary Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Sage Essential Oil, and Untitled, but these blends are restricted to industrial fragrance work.

Where You Will Encounter It

Pennyroyal oil is used as a fragrance material mainly in detergents and low-cost industrial perfumes, and is principally employed as a source of natural pulegone.
