Meadowfoam

What It Is

Meadowfoam seed oil comes from the seeds of the meadowfoam flower (Limnanthes alba), a delicate bloom native to California, Oregon, and Washington. These flowers are beloved by beekeepers for the distinctive marshmallow-flavored honey their nectar produces. The seed oil, however, remains lesser known despite its exceptional qualities.

Skin Benefits

This gloriously stable oil absorbs well into the skin without leaving a heavy residue. Its lightweight texture makes it suitable for facial applications and blends where you want moisture without greasiness.

Chemical Composition

What sets meadowfoam apart is its remarkable molecular structure: over 98 percent of the oil consists of long chain polymers. This composition contributes directly to its outstanding stability and resistance to oxidation.

Shelf Life

When stored properly, meadowfoam seed oil can remain stable for up to three years. This makes it an excellent investment for practitioners who create blends in larger batches or want carrier oils that will last. The extended shelf life provides solid return on investment compared to carriers that spoil within months.

Magickal Properties

Meadowfoam carries the magick of sweetening dispositions. Use this oil in workings designed to soften someone's attitude toward you, encourage kindness, or improve how others perceive and receive you. Its gentle, honeyed nature makes it ideal for social harmony, winning favor, and smoothing over difficult relationships.
