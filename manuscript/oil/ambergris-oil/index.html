Ambergris is a waxy substance produced in the digestive system of sperm whales. It washes ashore after being expelled by whales and has been prized in perfumery and magick for centuries due to its unique, complex scent.

Scent Profile

The scent of ambergris is difficult to describe. It combines woody and animalic notes with a distinctive oceanic quality that develops and mellows over time.

Ethical Considerations

Ambergris is a product of the sperm whale, a species that has faced severe population decline due to historical whaling practices. While ambergris itself is not harvested directly from whales (it washes ashore naturally), the use of true ambergris perpetuates demand for whale-derived products.

For ethical practitioners, synthetic ambergris alternatives or the Ambergris Bouquet substitute recipe (Cypress Oil combined with Patchouli Oil) provide similar magickal properties without ecological impact.

Magickal Uses

Ambergris has a long history in love magick. Its warm, sensual scent makes it a powerful addition to love mixtures and attraction workings. The oceanic origin connects it to the element of water and emotional matters of the heart.

Like other animal-derived oils, ambergris acts as a powerful fixative and amplifier when added to other magickal blends. A small amount can extend the longevity of a blend while intensifying its magickal properties.

Substitution

For those who prefer to avoid animal-derived ingredients, the Ambergris Bouquet recipe combines Cypress Oil with Patchouli Oil to create a similar scent profile and magickal effect. This substitute works well in any formula calling for ambergris.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

This comes from whales, so always buy the synthetic type. Resonates with the highest spiritual energy and promotes the purest love. Clary sage is a close substitute for ambergris.
