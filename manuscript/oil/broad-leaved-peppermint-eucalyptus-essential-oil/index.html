Broad-Leaved Peppermint Eucalyptus

A colourless or pale yellow mobile liquid steam distilled from the leaves and twigs of Broad-Leaved Peppermint Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus dives var. Type). The oil has a fresh, camphoraceous, spicy-minty character.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from leaves and twigs. Principal constituents are piperitone (40 to 50 percent) and phellandrene (20 to 30 percent), with camphene, cymene, terpinene, and thujene among others. The oil is sold as Grades A, B, or C according to the exact balance of constituents. A separate cineol-rich variety (E. dives var. C) is harvested from trees that look identical but yield a different chemotype.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin care applications include cuts, sores, and ulcers. For circulation, muscles, and joints, the oil is applied to arthritis, muscular aches and pains, rheumatism, sports injuries, and sprains. Respiratory uses cover asthma, bronchitis, catarrh, coughs, and throat and mouth infections. Immune-system applications include colds, fevers, flu, and infectious illness such as measles. For the nervous system, it is used for headaches, nervous exhaustion, neuralgia, and sciatica.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant in dilution, and non-sensitizing. Eucalyptus oil is toxic if taken internally.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used in deodorants, disinfectants, mouthwashes, gargles, and veterinary practice. Piperitone-rich oils are also used in solvents and as a starting material for the manufacture of thymol and menthol from piperitone.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Fever: Apply via compress or bath.
  • Headache: Apply via massage, compress, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Exhaustion: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Neuralgia: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Sciatica: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Asthma: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Aches and Pains: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.

  • Sprains & Strains: Apply via compress.
