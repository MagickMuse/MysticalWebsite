Vetivert Essential Oil is steam distilled from the roots and rootlets of Vetivert, which are washed, chopped, dried, and soaked before distillation. A resinoid is also produced by solvent extraction for perfumery work. In India and Sri Lanka the essence is known as the oil of tranquillity.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Roots and rootlets of Vetiveria zizanoides.

Method: Steam distillation, with a resinoid produced by solvent extraction for perfumery.

Appearance: Dark brown, olive, or amber viscous oil. Colour and scent vary by source, with Angola producing a very pale oil with a dry-woody odour.

Scent: Deep smoky, earthy-woody, with a sweet persistent undertone.

Principal constituents: Vetiverol, vitivone, and terpenes such as vetivenes, among others.

Therapeutic Actions

Antiseptic, antispasmodic, depurative, rubefacient, sedative for the nervous system, stimulant of circulation and red corpuscle production, tonic, and vermifuge.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin care: Acne, cuts, oily skin, wounds.

Circulation, muscles, and joints: Arthritis, muscular aches and pains, rheumatism, sprains, stiffness.

Nervous system: Debility, depression, insomnia, and nervous tension. Vetiver is deeply relaxing and valuable in massage and baths for anyone experiencing stress.

Blending

Blends well with Sandalwood Essential Oil, Rose Oil, Violet Leaf Absolute, Jasmine Absolute, Opopanax Essential Oil, Patchouli Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Lavender Essential Oil, Clary Sage Essential Oil, Mimosa Absolute, Cassie Absolute, and Ylang Ylang Essential Oil.

Safety

Possible skin sensitizer; use in dilutions below 2.5%. Otherwise considered non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Employed as a fixative and fragrance ingredient in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially oriental types. The oil is used in food preservatives, especially for asparagus.

Botanical Information

Vetiveria zizanioides from the Poaceae (grass) family, extracted from roots. Originates from India and Haiti. Base note with average absorption. Also known as Vetivert.

Scent Profile

Dark, resinous, grassy, earthy, and sweet. Finds grounding and beneficial coping mechanisms.
