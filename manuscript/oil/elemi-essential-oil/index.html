A colourless to pale yellow liquid steam distilled from the resinous gum of Elemi, a tropical Burseraceae tree native to the Philippine Islands and the Moluccas. Although the exudation is called a gum, it is almost entirely composed of resin and essential oil.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the gum, which contains roughly 10 to 25 percent essential oil. The product is light and fresh with a balsamic-spicy, lemon-like character. Principal constituents include phellandrene, dipentene, elemol, elemicin, terpineol, carvone, and terpinolene. A resinoid and a resin absolute are also produced in smaller quantities.

Therapeutic Uses

For skin care, the oil is applied to aged skin, infected cuts and wounds, inflammations, rejuvenation, and wrinkles, and is said to signify drying and preservation. Respiratory uses include bronchitis, catarrhal conditions, and unproductive coughs. For the nervous system, it is applied to nervous exhaustion and stress-related conditions.

Blending

Blends well with Myrrh Oil, Frankincense Essential Oil, Rock Rose Essential Oil (labdanum), Rosemary Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Sage Essential Oil, Cinnamon Bark Essential Oil, and other spices.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing in standard use.

Where You Will Encounter It

The resinoid and oil are used primarily as fixatives but also as fragrance components in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes. Occasionally used as a flavouring ingredient in food products and in alcoholic and soft drinks. Elemi was one of the aromatics used by the ancient Egyptians in the embalming process.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Nervous Exhaustion: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Chronic Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or steam inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Cuts & Sores: Apply via skin oil/lotion or compress.
  • Irritated & Inflamed Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Scars & Stretch Marks: Apply via massage or skin oil/lotion.
  • Wounds: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Wrinkles & Mature Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
