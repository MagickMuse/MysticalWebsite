DO NOT USE SUMMER SAVORY OIL ON THE SKIN AT ALL. Summer Savory Essential Oil is a dermal toxin, dermal irritant, and mucous membrane irritant due to its carvacrol content. Avoid during pregnancy. Not for home aromatherapy use.

Summer Savory Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the dried herb of Savory (Satureja hortensis), the peppery annual of the Lamiaceae family closely related to the thyme tribe. The oil is dominated by the irritant phenol carvacrol and is not used in home aromatherapy.

Safety

Summer Savory oil should not be used on the skin at all. It is a dermal toxin, a dermal irritant, and a mucous membrane irritant. The oil is also an abortifacient and must be avoided during pregnancy.

Extraction & Characteristics

The essential oil is produced by steam distillation from the whole dried herb; an oleoresin is also produced by solvent extraction. The finished oil is a colourless or pale yellow liquid with a fresh, herbaceous, spicy odour. The oil is recorded as blending with Lavender Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Pine Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Rosemary Essential Oil, and citrus oils, but these blends are restricted to controlled perfumery work. Principal constituents are carvacrol, pinene, cymene, camphene, limonene, phellandrene, and borneol, among others.

Where You Will Encounter It

Summer Savory oil is occasionally used in perfumery work for its fresh herbaceous notes. The oil and its oleoresin are used in most major food categories, especially meat products and canned food.
