♊Gemini Oil

Sweetpea Oil (Lathyrus odoratus) captures the delicate, romantic fragrance of sweetpea flowers. No genuine sweetpea essential oil is commercially available due to the difficulty of extraction, but the scent can be approximated through the Sweet Pea Bouquet recipe.

Scent Profile

Sweet, light, and intensely floral with honey undertones. The scent is delicate and romantic, evoking garden parties and gentle spring days.

Magickal Uses

Sweetpea's gentle, romantic fragrance makes it ideal for love workings, particularly those focused on tender, committed relationships rather than passionate encounters.

Excellent for strengthening friendships and attracting new friends. The welcoming, approachable nature of this scent draws people together in platonic affection.

Use in workings to smooth social situations, encourage pleasant gatherings, and create welcoming atmospheres.

Application

Dilute with a base oil and wear to attract new friends and draw love.

Substitution

Since no genuine oil exists, use the Sweet Pea Bouquet recipe combining Neroli, Ylang-Ylang Oil, Jasmine Oil, and Benzoin Oil.
