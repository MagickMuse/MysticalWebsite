Camphor

Brown Camphor Essential Oil is the middle fraction obtained when the rectified steam-distilled oil of Cinnamomum camphora is separated by vacuum distillation and filter pressing.

Principal Constituents

Up to 80 percent safrol, with terpineol, among others.

Safety Data

Toxic and carcinogenic. Should not be used in therapy at all.

Other Uses

Used as a source of safrol and other perfumery chemicals.
