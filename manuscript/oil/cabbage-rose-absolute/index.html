Cabbage Rose Absolute is the deep, honeyed solvent-extracted aromatic of Cabbage Rose (Rosa centifolia), the rose maroc and rose de mai of perfumery. The same plant also yields rose otto by water or steam distillation; both forms are covered here.

Extraction & Characteristics

Two aromatic products are produced from cabbage rose. The essential oil or otto is produced by water or steam distillation from the fresh petals, with rose water as a byproduct. The concrete and absolute are produced by solvent extraction from the fresh petals; a rose leaf absolute is also produced in small quantities in France. The otto is a pale yellow liquid with a deep, sweet, rosy-floral, tenacious odour. The absolute is a reddish-orange viscous liquid with a deep, rich, sweet, rosy-spicy, honeylike fragrance. The plant has over 300 constituents, some in minute traces, with the principal components being citronellol (18 to 22 percent), phenyl ethanol (63 percent), geraniol and nerol (10 to 15 percent), stearopten (8 percent), and farnesol (0.2 to 2 percent), among others.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Broken capillaries, conjunctivitis (rose water), dry skin, eczema, herpes, mature and sensitive complexions, and wrinkles.

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Palpitations and poor circulation.

Respiratory System: Asthma, coughs, and hay fever.

Digestive System: Cholecystitis, liver congestion, and nausea.

Genito-Urinary System: Irregular menstruation, leucorrhoea, menorrhagia, and uterine disorders.

Nervous System: Depression, impotence, insomnia, frigidity, headache, nervous tension, and stress-related complaints. Above all, rose procures a feeling of well-being, even of happiness, and the individual under its influence develops an amiable tolerance.

Recorded actions include antidepressant, antiphlogistic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, anti-tubercular, antiviral, aphrodisiac, astringent, bactericidal, choleretic, cicatrisant, depurative, emmenagogue, haemostatic, hepatic, laxative, regulator of appetite, sedative (nervous), stomachic, and tonic of the heart, liver, stomach, and uterus.

Blending

Cabbage Rose blends well with Jasmine Absolute, cassie, mimosa, Neroli Essential Oil (orange blossom), Geranium Essential Oil, Bergamot Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Untitled, Sandalwood Essential Oil, Guaiacwood Essential Oil, Untitled, Benzoin Absolute, Roman Chamomile Essential Oil, Peru Balsam Essential Oil, Untitled, and Palmarosa Essential Oil.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Rose water is used as a household cosmetic and culinary article, especially in Persian cookery. The concrete, absolute, and oil are employed extensively in soaps, cosmetics, toiletries, and perfumes of all types, including floral, oriental, and chypre compositions. Rose has some flavouring uses, especially in fruit products and tobacco. Synthetic rose oil is widespread: rose is found as a component in 46 percent of men's perfumes and 98 percent of women's fragrances.
