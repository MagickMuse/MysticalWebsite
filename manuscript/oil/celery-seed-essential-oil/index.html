Celery Seed Essential Oil is produced by steam distillation from the whole or crushed seeds of Apium graveolens. An oil from the whole herb, an oleoresin, and an extract are also produced in small quantities. The oil is principally produced in India, and also in Holland, China, Hungary, and the USA.

Characteristics

A pale yellow or orange oil with a spicy-warm, sweet, long-lasting odour. Blends well with lavender, pine, opopanax, lovage, tea tree, oakmoss, coriander, and other spices.

Principal Constituents

Limonene (60 percent), apiol, selinene, santalol, sedanolide, and sedanolic acid anhydride, among others.

Actions

Anti-oxidative, antirheumatic, antiseptic (urinary), antispasmodic, aperitif, depurative, digestive, diuretic, carminative, cholagogue, emmenagogue, galactagogue, hepatic, nervine, sedative (nervous), stimulant (uterine), stomachic, tonic (digestive).

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Arthritis, build-up of toxins in the blood, gout, rheumatism.

Digestive System: Dyspepsia, flatulence, indigestion, liver congestion, jaundice.

Genito-Urinary and Endocrine Systems: Amenorrhoea, glandular problems, increases milk flow, cystitis.

Nervous System: Neuralgia, sciatica.

Safety Data

Non-toxic, non-irritant, possible sensitization. Avoid during pregnancy.

Other Uses

Used in tonic, sedative, and carminative preparations, and as a fragrance component in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes. Extensively used as a flavouring agent in foods, especially by the spice industry, and in alcoholic and soft drinks.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Neuralgia: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Sciatica: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Accumulation of Toxins: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Gout: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
  • Liver Congestion: Apply via massage.
  • Amenorrhoea: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Lack of Nursing Milk: Apply via massage.
  • Cystitis: Apply via compress, bath, or douche.
