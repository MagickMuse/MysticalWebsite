Birch

Birch Bud Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the fresh leaf-buds of Betula alba.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Fresh leaf-buds of Betula alba (also produced from B. papyrifera and B. verrucosa)

Method: Steam distillation

Note: Middle

Scent: A pale yellow, viscous oil with a woody-green, lightly balsamic scent. The oil crystallizes at low temperatures.

Constituents: Mainly betulenol and other sesquiterpenes.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin & Hair Care: A traditional ingredient in hair tonics and shampoos. Useful for dermatitis, dull or congested skin, eczema, hair care, poor skin tone, and psoriasis.

Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Accumulation of toxins, arthritis, cellulite, muscular pain, oedema, poor circulation, and rheumatism.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing in normal use. Patch-test before applying broadly, and dilute well in a carrier oil. Not interchangeable with Sweet Birch oil, which contains methyl salicylate and is unsafe for home use.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Accumulation of Toxins: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Baldness & Hair Care: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Cellulite: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
  • Congested & Dull Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, or inhalation (steam).
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Low Blood Pressure: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Obesity: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Oedema & Water Retention: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Poor Circulation: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Psoriasis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Associated with new beginnings. Birch leaf oil is used to anoint special love candles that are burned to protect one's lover and to smooth over romantic difficulties.
