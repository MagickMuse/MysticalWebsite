Scent Profile

A pale amber or brown viscous liquid with a rich, sweet, balsamic, vanilla-like scent.

Blends well with: ylang ylang, patchouli, petitgrain, sandalwood, rose, spices, and floral and oriental bases.

Principal Constituents

Benzoic and cinnamic acid esters (including benzyl benzoate, benzyl cinnamate, and cinnamyl cinnamate), along with other trace compounds. The crude balsam contains roughly 50 to 64% oil (referred to as cinnamein) and 20 to 28% resin.

Extraction

A resin-free essential oil is produced from the crude balsam by high vacuum dry distillation.

💡
A few related preparations are also produced from the same tree: a steam-distilled wood oil from wood chippings (considered inferior in quality), a white balsam called myroxocarpin made from the fruit, and an extract called balsamito from the young fruit.

Aromatherapy Use

  • Skin Care: Dry and chapped skin, eczema, rashes, sores, wounds.
  • Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Low blood pressure, rheumatism.
  • Respiratory System: Asthma, bronchitis, coughs.
  • Immune System: Colds.
  • Nervous System: Nervous tension, stress. Like other balsams, it has a warming, opening, comforting quality.

Actions

Anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, balsamic, expectorant, parasiticide, stimulant. Promotes the growth of epithelial cells.

Other Uses

The balsam is extensively used in tropical medicinal preparations, and appears in pharmaceutical products such as cough syrup. Both the oil and the crude balsam serve as a fixative and fragrance component in soaps, detergents, creams, lotions, and perfumes; the oil is often preferred in perfumery to avoid resin deposits and discolouration. It is also used in most food categories, including alcoholic and soft drinks.

Safety

⚠️
Non-toxic and non-irritant. The crude balsam (not the oil) is a common contact allergen and can cause dermatitis. Those who react to it may also react to benzoin resinoid, known as cross-sensitization. Commercial oil is often a water-white liquid, having been diluted with a solvent such as benzyl alcohol.
