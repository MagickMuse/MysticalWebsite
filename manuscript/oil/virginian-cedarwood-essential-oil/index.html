Virginian Cedarwood Essential Oil is the steam-distilled oil of Juniperus virginiana, the eastern red cedar of North America. Mild, sweet, and softly woody, with the unmistakable "pencil-wood" scent of a sharpened cedar pencil, it is a workhorse of perfumery, household products, and skincare.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Timber waste, sawdust, and shavings of Juniperus virginiana. A superior oil was once distilled from the red heartwood of trees over twenty-five years old.

Method: Steam distillation

Note: Base. Acts as a fixative in blends.

Scent: A pale yellow or orange oily liquid with a mild, sweet-balsamic, pencil-wood aroma.

Constituents: Mainly cedrene (up to 80 percent), cedrol (3 to 14 percent), and cedrenol, among others. Industrially, the oil is the starting material for the isolation of cedrene.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Acne, dandruff, eczema, greasy hair, oily skin, psoriasis, and use as an insect repellent.

Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Arthritis and rheumatism.

Respiratory Support: Bronchitis, catarrh, congestion, coughs, and sinusitis.

Genito-Urinary Support: Cystitis and leucorrhoea.

Nervous System: A sedative for nervous tension and stress-related disorders.

Blending

Pairs beautifully with sandalwood, rose, juniper, cypress, vetiver, patchouli, and benzoin.

Safety

Externally relatively non-toxic, but the oil can cause local skin irritation and possible sensitization in some individuals. Use in dilution only, with care and moderation.

Avoid entirely during pregnancy. Virginian cedarwood oil is a powerful abortifacient and fatal poisoning has been documented. Practitioners who want a true cedarwood scent during pregnancy should switch to Atlas Cedarwood Essential Oil, which carries a much gentler safety profile.

Where You Will Encounter It

  • A standard ingredient in room sprays and household insect repellents, especially in cedar-based moth and pest products.
  • A fragrance component in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumery, often standing in for true cedarwood at lower cost.
  • The industrial starting material for the isolation of cedrene, a building block for downstream synthesis.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Baldness & Hair Care: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Dandruff: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Insect Repellent: Apply via skin oil/lotion or vaporization.
  • Leucorrhoea: Apply via bath or douche.
  • Cystitis: Apply via compress, bath, or douche.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

An oil of Jupiter, promoting peace, contentment, wealth, and good fortune, in addition to driving away all evil and negativity. Believed to attract good spirits and repel bad ones.
