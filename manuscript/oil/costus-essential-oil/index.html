Costus essential oil is a tenacious, woody-musty oil distilled from the dried roots of Saussurea costus, a tall perennial of the Himalayas. The bulk of commercial production comes from India.

Extraction & Characteristics

The dried roots are macerated in warm water, then steam distilled. The distillate water undergoes solvent extraction to recover further aromatic material. Concrete and absolute forms are also produced in small quantities. The oil is a pale yellow to brownish viscous liquid with a soft, woody-musty, extremely tenacious odour. Principal constituents are sesquiterpene lactones, including dihydrocostus lactone and costunolide, which together make up to 50 percent of the oil, alongside other sesquiterpenes such as costols, caryophyllene, and selinene, with costic and oleic acids among additional components.

Related Species

Costus is closely related to Elecampane (Inula helenium), whose roots are also used to produce an essential oil.

Therapeutic Uses

For the respiratory system, costus addresses asthma, bronchitis, and spasmodic cough. In the digestive system, it eases flatulence, indigestion, and spasm. For the nervous system, it supports debility, nervous exhaustion, and stress-related conditions. In skin care, its main aromatherapy application is as a perfume.

Blending

Blends well with Patchouli Essential Oil, opopanax, Ylang Ylang Essential Oil, and oriental and floral fragrances.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant. Sensitization is possible in some individuals. The oil is subject to frequent adulteration, so source quality matters.

Where You Will Encounter It

Costus serves as a fixative and fragrance component in cosmetics and perfumes. The food industry uses it as a flavour ingredient, particularly in confectionery, alcoholic drinks, and soft drinks.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Nervous Exhaustion: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Chronic Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or steam inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Asthma: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Cramp & Gastric Spasm: Apply via massage or compress.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
