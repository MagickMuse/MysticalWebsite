Atlas Cedarwood Essential Oil is the steam-distilled oil of Cedrus atlantica, the pyramid-shaped evergreen of the Algerian Atlas mountains.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Wood, stumps, and sawdust of Cedrus atlantica

Method: Steam distillation. A resinoid and absolute are also produced in small quantities.

Note: Base. Acts as a fixative in blends.

Scent: A yellow, orange, or deep amber viscous oil with a warm, camphoraceous top note over a sweet, tenacious, woody-balsamic body.

Constituents: Atlantone, caryophyllene, cedrol, and cadinene, among others.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Acne, dandruff, dermatitis, eczema, fungal infections, greasy skin, hair loss, skin eruptions, and ulcers.

Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Arthritis and rheumatism.

Respiratory Support: Bronchitis, catarrh, congestion, and coughs.

Genito-Urinary Support: Cystitis, leucorrhoea, and pruritis.

Nervous System: Nervous tension and stress-related conditions.

Blending

Blends well with rosewood, bergamot, boronia, cypress, calamus, cassie, costus, jasmine, juniper, neroli, mimosa, labdanum, olibanum, clary sage, vetiver, rosemary, ylang ylang, and oriental and floral bases.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing in normal use. Best avoided during pregnancy as a precaution. Standard dilution practices apply for skin work.

Other Uses

Fragrance component and fixative in cosmetics and household products, soaps, detergents, perfumes, and especially men's fragrances.

See the Atlas Cedar plant entry for the full botanical and folk-tradition context.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Baldness & Hair Care: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Dandruff: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Irritated & Inflamed Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Pruritis: Apply via douche.
  • Leucorrhoea: Apply via bath or douche.
  • Cystitis: Apply via compress, bath, or douche.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

An oil of Jupiter, promoting peace, contentment, wealth, and good fortune, in addition to driving away all evil and negativity. Believed to attract good spirits and repel bad ones.
