Santolina

Santolina Essential Oil is steam distilled from the seeds of Santolina. It is not for home aromatherapy use.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Seeds of Santolina chamaecyparissus.

Method: Steam distillation.

Appearance: Pale yellow liquid.

Scent: Strong, acrid, herbaceous.

Principal constituent: Santolinenone is the only principal constituent.

Therapeutic Actions

Antispasmodic, antitoxic, anthelmintic, insecticidal, stimulant, vermifuge.

Safety

Do not use Santolina essential oil in home aromatherapy. It is an oral toxin. There is no safety data available, and the oil is likely to be dangerously toxic. Avoid during pregnancy. Avoid all internal and topical home use.

Where You Will Encounter It

Little used in flavour or perfumery work due to its toxicity.
