Wormseed

Wormseed Essential Oil is steam distilled from the whole herb of Wormseed, especially the fruit or seeds. It is one of the most toxic essential oils and must not be used in home aromatherapy.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Whole herb of Chenopodium ambrosioides var. anthelminticum, especially the fruit or seeds.

Method: Steam distillation.

Appearance: Colourless or pale yellow oil.

Scent: Sweet-woody, camphoraceous, heavy, and nauseating.

Principal constituents: Ascaridole (60 to 80 percent), cymene, limonene, terpinene, and myrcene.

Therapeutic Actions

Anthelmintic, antirheumatic, antispasmodic, expectorant, and hypotensive.

Safety

Do not use Wormseed essential oil in home aromatherapy. It is one of the most toxic essential oils available. Cases of fatal poisoning have been reported even in low doses, and effects can be cumulative. The oil should not be used in therapy either internally or externally. Avoid during pregnancy. Due to high ascaridole content, the oil may explode when heated or treated with acids.

Where You Will Encounter It

In pharmaceuticals, its anthelmintic applications have been replaced by synthetics. Used as a fragrance component in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes. Its use is not permitted in foods.
