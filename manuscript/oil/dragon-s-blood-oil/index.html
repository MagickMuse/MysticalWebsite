Traditional Grimoire Notes

Dragon's blood resin (from Calamus draco) has a sweetly pungent aroma unlike any other resin. A powerful magickal amplifier — it intensifies the potency of any incense or formula it is added to. As an oleo-resin, it burns with a smoky flame and is best fumed on incense charcoal rather than burned directly.
