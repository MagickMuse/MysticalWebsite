Traditional Grimoire Notes

The universally recognized oil of sexuality and attraction — worn with equal effectiveness by any gender. As this is an animal-derived scent, synthetic substitutes are strongly recommended. Beyond its erotic reputation, musk also lends itself to workings of courage, determination, and energy.
