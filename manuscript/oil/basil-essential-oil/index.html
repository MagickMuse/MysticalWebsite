Magickal Applications

The scent of basil causes sympathy between two people, making it useful for avoiding major clashes. Wear diluted basil oil when you need to maintain harmony with others.

Basil essential oil is useful in blends designed for encouraging happiness and peace. It also stimulates the conscious mind.

Basil works well in money-attracting magickal oils. Historically, prostitutes in Spain wore basil to attract customers.

Add 1 drop to a room diffuser after arguments to encourage loving communication and diffuse tension between partners. For passionate connection, combine 1 drop basil oil with 1 drop cinnamon oil in a diffuser to stoke lust between committed partners.

Add 3 drops to a dark-colored bowl of water to enhance psychic abilities during water scrying and divination work.

Place 1 drop on the bottom of each shoe before a difficult day. This application clears mental fog, improves focus, and decreases anxiety throughout the day. For deeper emotional work, blend 1 drop in 1 tablespoon carrier oil and apply to pulse points to lift spirits, calm anxiety, and banish melancholy.

To turn aside evil, dilute 1 drop sweet basil or holy basil essential oil in 1 tablespoon olive oil. Anoint the back of the neck and massage into the bottoms of the feet. For household protection, blend 3 drops into jojoba or coconut carrier oil and anoint all doors and windows to prevent evil from crossing the threshold. First cleanse your home's interior to drive out existing negative spirits.

Basil exorcises misfortune, thereby attracting wealth. Place 1 drop on a photocopied dollar bill and keep it in your wallet or cash register to bring prosperity to businesses and personal finances.

Therapeutic Uses

For skin care, basil soothes insect bites and works as a natural insect repellent. For circulation, muscles, and joints it eases gout, muscular aches and pains, and rheumatic stiffness. For the respiratory system it is helpful for bronchitis, lingering coughs, earache, and sinus congestion. For the digestive system it calms dyspepsia, flatulence, and nausea. For cycle support it is useful for cramping and scanty or sluggish menstruation. For the immune system it supports the body through colds, fever, and flu. For the nervous system it lifts the spirit through anxiety, depression, fatigue, insomnia, migraine, and nervous tension; long regarded as one of the finest aromatic nerve tonics, basil clears the head, relieves intellectual fatigue, and gives the mind strength and clarity.

Spellwork Correspondences

Rub oil from center to either end to dress the candle, from top to bottom to draw energy toward you, or bottom to top to banish. Oils are flammable; use with care. Spellwork uses include intuition, optimism, and psychic awareness.
