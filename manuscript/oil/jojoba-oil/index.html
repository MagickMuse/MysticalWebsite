What It Actually Is

Despite being called an oil, jojoba is technically a vegetable wax that remains liquid at room temperature. This unique composition makes it exceptional at blocking oxygen, which is the primary culprit behind oil rancidity. The result is remarkable longevity for your blends.

Skin Benefits

Highly moisturizing and good for all skin types, jojoba is penetrating and filled with antioxidants. It also has naturally occurring fungicidal properties, and so is beneficial for nail beds and feet.

Magickal Properties

Jojoba thrives in harsh desert environments where other plants cannot survive. This tenacity translates directly into its magickal properties: courage and the unwavering ability to overcome obstacles. When something stands between you and your goal, jojoba refuses to let it remain there. Use this carrier for workings involving perseverance, tackling the seemingly insurmountable, and pushing through when others would give up. It also strengthens self-confidence, banishes doubt and melancholy, and supports any magick requiring steadfast determination.

Shelf Life

Jojoba boasts an extraordinary shelf life. While most carrier oils last nine to twelve months, jojoba-based blends can remain stable for far longer. Some practitioners report jojoba blends remaining fresh for ten years or more without turning rancid. While all organic matter eventually decomposes, jojoba's wax structure buys you significant time. The higher price point compared to other carriers is justified by this exceptional longevity.
