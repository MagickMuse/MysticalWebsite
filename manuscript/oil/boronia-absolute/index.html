Boronia Absolute is a solvent-extracted absolute drawn from the flowers of Boronia megastigma.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Flowers of Boronia megastigma

Method: Concrete and absolute by enfleurage or petroleum-ether (solvent) extraction. A small quantity of true essential oil is also produced by steam distillation.

Note: Middle

Scent: The concrete is a dark green, butter-like mass with a warm, woody-sweet aroma. The absolute is a green viscous liquid with a fresh, fruity-spicy top, a rich tenacious floral heart, and a soft woody base.

Constituents: Notably ionone (the violet-and-iris molecule), with eugenol, triacontane, phenols, ethyl alcohol, and ethyl formate among the supporting cast.

Blending

Blends with clary sage, sandalwood, bergamot, violet, helichrysum, costus, mimosa, and other delicate florals.

Therapeutic Uses

Classed as aromatic. Used almost exclusively for perfume rather than aromatherapy.

Other Uses

  • A signature note in high-class perfumery, especially in floral compositions.
  • A flavour ingredient in some fruit products, alcoholic and soft drinks.

Safety

No significant safety concerns at perfumery dilutions. Because the genuine absolute is so costly, the main risk is paying for an adulterated or synthetic substitute. Patch-test before broader skin use, as with any rich floral absolute.
