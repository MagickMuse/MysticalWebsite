Traditional Grimoire Notes

Considered the most sacred oil of the ancient Egyptians — a powerful oil sacred to the Moon, the goddess Isis, and magick itself. May be worn on the body or used to anoint sacred objects. Excellent for psychic protection, happiness, good health, fertility, good fortune, peace, harmony, and blessings. Especially prized for astral projection and visionary work. Carries a very high spiritual vibration.
