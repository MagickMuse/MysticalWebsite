This is a perfumer's fantasy. No genuine new-mown hay oil exists. Use the New-Mown Hay Bouquet substitute recipe to capture this scent.
Drug Interactions
High coumarin content (typically derived from melilotus, sweet woodruff, or tonka). Anticoagulant activity — avoid with anticoagulant and antiplatelet drugs. Hepatotoxic at high doses. Use restricted to perfumery.

New-Mown Hay Oil captures the honey-fresh scent of a just-mowed field of hay. This evocative fragrance does not exist as a true essential oil but can be recreated through the New-Mown Hay Bouquet recipe.

Scent Profile

The scent evokes freshly cut grass, warm summer days, and the sweet smell of drying hay. It combines green, herbaceous notes with a subtle honey-like sweetness.

Magickal Uses

New-mown hay oil helps practitioners "turn over a new leaf" and attain fresh perspectives on difficult problems. The scent of renewal and new growth supports mental clarity and innovative thinking.

Particularly powerful for breaking negative habits and thought patterns. Use when working to overcome addictions or ingrained behaviors that no longer serve you. The fresh, clean scent symbolizes clearing away the old to make room for new growth.

The association with harvest and agricultural cycles makes this oil ideal for workings involving new chapters, fresh starts, and transformative change.

Substitution

Since no genuine oil exists, use the New-Mown Hay Bouquet recipe combining Woodruff Oil, Tonka Oil, Lavender Oil, Bergamot Oil, and Oakmoss Oil.
