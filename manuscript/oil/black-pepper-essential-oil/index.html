Black Pepper Essential Oil (Piper nigrum) is a fiery, warming oil with a sharp, spicy aroma. Steam distilled from unripe peppercorns, this energizing oil fuels courage, alertness, and strength. While it was the preferred warming agent in European essential oil magick, its tendency to irritate skin at higher concentrations means it should always be used with care at no more than 0.5% dilution.

Scent Note

Black pepper has a sharp, sweet scent. It is best added to blends rather than worn alone, even when diluted.

Magickal Properties

Magickal uses: Courage, strength, alertness, perception, protection, banishing, justice, exorcism, breaking bad habits, energy

Physical Uses

When diluted to 0.5%, Black Pepper Essential Oil can:

  • Increase circulation to cold, stiff muscles
  • Provide warmth for sore joints
  • Support digestion when applied to the abdomen
  • Stimulate lymphatic drainage

Cautions: Sensitizing oil. Maximum 0.5% dilution. Can irritate skin at higher concentrations. Phototoxic, so avoid sun exposure for 12 hours after topical use.
