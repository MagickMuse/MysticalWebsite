Boldo

Boldo Leaf Essential Oil is the steam-distilled oil of Peumus boldus.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Leaves of Peumus boldus

Method: Steam distillation

Note: Top to Middle

Scent: A yellow liquid with a powerful, spicy-camphoraceous odour, often described as disagreeable.

Constituents: Cymene, ascaridole, cineol (cineole), and linalool. The ascaridole content drives the toxicity.

Safety

Do not use boldo essential oil in any form. It is considered extremely toxic and harmful to the human organism even in very small doses, whether internal or external. The oil is not safe for therapy, aromatherapy, or skincare. It appears in pharmaceutical preparations only in minute, professionally controlled amounts.

For boldo's renowned liver and gall bladder benefits, work with the dried leaves as a tea or tincture, prepared by a qualified herbalist, not the essential oil.

Where You Will Encounter It

Pharmaceutical preparations may include boldo leaf extract or oil in minute quantities for liver and gall bladder support. These are professionally formulated and are not equivalent to using the oil at home.
