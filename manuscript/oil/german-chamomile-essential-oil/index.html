Botanical Information

Scientific name: Matricaria chamomilla (or M. recutita)

Botanical family: Asteraceae

Origin: United States, Egypt

Source of plant's oil: Flowers

Evaporation: Middle note

Scent Profile

Description: Fruity, incredibly full, herbal, apple-like, green, sunny

Impact: Achieves emotional stability, releases anger, heals skin ailments.

Extraction

Essential oil by steam distillation from the flower heads, with up to 1.9 percent yield. An absolute is also produced in small quantities, which is a deeper blue colour and has greater tenacity and fixative properties.

Principal Constituents

Chamazulene, farnesene, bisabolol oxide, and en-yne dicycloether, among others. The chamazulene is not present in the fresh flower but is only produced during the process of distillation.

Actions

Analgesic, anti-allergenic, anti-inflammatory, antiphlogistic, antispasmodic, bactericidal, carminative, cicatrisant, cholagogue, digestive, emmenagogue, febrifuge, fungicidal, hepatic, nerve sedative, stimulant of leucocyte production, stomachic, sudorific, vermifuge, vulnerary.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Skin Care: Acne, allergies, boils, burns, cuts, chilblains, dermatitis, earache, eczema, hair care, inflammations, insect bites, rashes, sensitive skin, teething pain, toothache, wounds.

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Arthritis, inflamed joints, muscular pain, neuralgia, rheumatism, sprains.

Digestive System: Dyspepsia, colic, indigestion, nausea.

Genito-Urinary System: Dysmenorrhoea, menopausal problems, menorrhagia.

Nervous System: Headache, insomnia, nervous tension, migraine, and stress-related complaints.

Safety Data

Non-toxic, non-irritant. Causes dermatitis in some individuals.

Other Uses

Used in pharmaceutical antiseptic ointments and in carminative, antispasmodic, and tonic preparations. Extensively used in cosmetics, soaps, detergents, high-class perfumes, and hair and bath products. Used as a flavour ingredient in most major food categories, including alcoholic and soft drinks.

Blending

Chamomile blends well with Bergamot Essential Oil, Cedar Essential Oil, Lavender, Rosemary, Myrrh, and Angelica Root Essential Oil. These combinations create nurturing, empowering, and soothing blends.

Uses

Add 1 drop German chamomile to 1 tablespoon natural aloe vera and apply to affected area. For PMS or PMDD, rub lower abdomen in gentle clockwise circles (counterclockwise motion can cause bowel discomfort).

German chamomile is particularly effective for healing skin ailments when applied topically (properly diluted).

Diffuse or use in direct inhalation to achieve emotional stability and release anger.

Place 3 drops of chamomile on a tissue inside your pillowcase to banish nightmares.

Diffuse 1 drop of chamomile essential oil in 100 ml water to calm frazzled nerves.

Place a drop of chamomile in an aroma locket and wear, or place 3 drops on a tissue and carry near your heart to increase patience.
