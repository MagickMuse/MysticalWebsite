A yellowy-brown mobile liquid steam distilled from the grass of Citronella. The Sri Lanka oil has a fresh, powerful, lemony scent. The Java variety (Java Citronella, Cymbopogon winterianus) yields a colourless to pale yellow oil with a fresh, woody-sweet character that is regarded as superior in perfumery work and produces roughly twice as much oil as the Sri Lanka type.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the fresh, part-dried, or dried grass. Principal constituents include geraniol (up to 45 percent in the Java oil) and citronellal (up to 50 percent in the Java oil), alongside geranyl acetate, limonene, and camphene. The Sri Lanka variety contains a higher proportion of monoterpene hydrocarbons.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin care applications include excessive perspiration, oily skin, and use as an insect repellent. Mixed with Virginian Cedarwood Essential Oil, citronella was a popular remedy against mosquito attacks for many years before the appearance of DDT and other modern insecticides. Immune-system uses include colds, flu, and minor infections. For the nervous system, the oil is applied to fatigue, headaches, migraine, and neuralgia.

Blending

Blends well with Geranium Essential Oil, Lemon Essential Oil, Bergamot Essential Oil, Orange Essential Oil, cedarwood (Atlas Cedarwood Essential Oil, Texas Cedarwood Essential Oil, and Virginian Cedarwood Essential Oil), and Pine Essential Oil.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant in standard use, though it may cause dermatitis in sensitive individuals. Avoid during pregnancy.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used extensively in soaps, detergents, household goods, and industrial perfumes. Employed in insect repellent formulations against moths, ants, fleas, and other pests for use in the home and garden. The Sri Lanka oil is used in major food categories, including alcoholic and soft drinks. The Java oil is the starting material for the isolation of natural geraniol and citronellal.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Headache: Apply via massage, compress, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Exhaustion: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Neuralgia: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Sciatica: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Excessive Perspiration: Apply via skin oil/lotion or bath.
  • Insect Repellent: Apply via skin oil/lotion or vaporization.
  • Migraine: Apply via compress.
