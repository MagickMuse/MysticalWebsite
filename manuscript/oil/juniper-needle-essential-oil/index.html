Scotch Pine Essential Oil

A water-white or pale yellow mobile liquid steam distilled from the needles and wood of Juniper (Juniperus communis). The oil carries a sweet-balsamic, fresh, turpentine-like odour.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the needles and wood of the juniper tree. The wood oil is usually adulterated with turpentine oil and is considered the inferior of the two juniper essential oils.

Safety

Non-sensitizing and may be slightly irritating; generally non-toxic. The oil stimulates uterine muscle and must not be used during pregnancy. It must also be avoided by those with kidney disease due to its nephrotoxic effect. Source recommends using Juniper Berry Essential Oil in moderation rather than the wood or needle oil.
