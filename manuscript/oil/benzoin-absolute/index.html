Benzoin Absolute is a thick, amber-colored resin extracted from Styrax benzoin trees native to Southeast Asia. Its warm, vanilla-like scent with balsamic undertones makes it valuable as a fixative in perfumery and incense.

Botanical Information

Scientific name: Styrax benzoin

Botanical family: Styracaceae

Origin: Sumatra, Java, Thailand

Source: Resin

Evaporation: Base note

Scent Profile

Description: Sweet, warm, vanilla-like with balsamic undertones

Impact: Grounding, comforting, spiritually elevating

Two varieties are commonly available: Benzoin Siam and Benzoin Sumatra. Both share the same magickal properties, though Benzoin Sumatra is better suited for cleansing work while Benzoin Siam excels in attraction magick.

Tincture

ColorDark brown, translucent
ScentClean, antiseptic
UsesBusiness success, mental powers, purification

Benzoin tincture can be added to oils and ointments as a preservative.

Magickal Uses

Benzoin is an excellent clearing agent for removing negative energies. Add to purification blends or diffuse to cleanse spaces before ritual work.

For prosperity magick, blend benzoin with cinnamon and basil. Burn this combination to attract customers to a place of business or draw abundance.

Benzoin's pleasant fragrance and binding properties make it an ideal base for incense formulations. It helps other ingredients burn evenly and adds depth to blends.

Substitution

Benzoin Siam can substitute for storax, to which it is botanically related.
