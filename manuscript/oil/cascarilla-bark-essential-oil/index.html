Cascarilla Bark Essential Oil is produced by steam distillation from the dried bark of Croton eluteria, with a yield of 1.5 to 3 percent.

Characteristics

A pale yellow, greenish, or dark amber liquid with a spicy, aromatic, warm-woody odour. Blends well with nutmeg, pepper, pimento, sage, oakmoss, oriental and spicy bases.

Principal Constituents

Cymene, diterpene, limonene, caryophyllene, terpineol, and eugenol, among others.

Actions

Astringent, antimicrobial, antiseptic, carminative, digestive, expectorant, stomachic, tonic.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Respiratory System: Bronchitis, coughs.

Digestive System: Dyspepsia, flatulence, nausea.

Immune System: Flu.

Safety Data

Non-irritant, non-sensitizing, relatively non-toxic. Possibly narcotic in large doses.

Other Uses

Fragrance component in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially men's fragrances. Flavour ingredient in most major food categories, soft drinks, and alcoholic beverages, especially vermouths and bitters.

Cascarilla Plant

See Cascarilla for the parent plant.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Nausea & Vomiting: Apply via massage or vaporization.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
