Calamintha Essential Oil is produced by steam distillation from the flowering tops of Calamintha clinopodium.

Characteristics

A pale yellow liquid with a herbaceous-woody, pungent, pennyroyal-like odour.

Principal Constituents

Citral, nerol, citronellol, limonene, geraniol, and metatabilacetone (3 to 5 percent), among others.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Chills, joints, muscular aches, rheumatism.

Digestive System: Colic, flatulence, nervous dyspepsia.

Nervous System: Insomnia, nervous tension, stress-related conditions.

Safety Data

Non-irritant, non-sensitizing, possibly toxic in concentration. Avoid during pregnancy.

Other Uses

Used in the USA as a wild cat lure. Used in perfumery.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Colic: Apply via massage.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Aches and Pains: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Chills: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
  • Insomnia: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
