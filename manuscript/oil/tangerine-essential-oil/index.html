Botanical Information

Citrus reticulata from the Rutaceae family, cold-pressed from peels. Originates from China, United States, and South America. Top note with fast absorption. Also known as Mandarin.

Extraction & Characteristics

The primary oil is produced by cold expression from the outer peel of Tangerine. A mandarin petitgrain oil is also produced in small quantities by steam distillation from the leaves and twigs.

Mandarin oil is a yellowy-orange mobile liquid with a blue-violet hint, carrying an intensely sweet, almost floral citrus scent. Tangerine oil is an orange mobile liquid with a fresh, sweet, orange-like aroma; it has less body than mandarin and is little used in perfumery work. Both share a constituent profile dominated by limonene, with methyl methylanthranilate, geraniol, citral, and citronellal among others.

The terms mandarin, tangerine, and satsuma are used somewhat interchangeably, but the oils represent different chemotypes and are quite distinct in scent.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Acne, congested and oily skin, scars, spots, stretch marks, and toner.

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Fluid retention and obesity.

Digestive System: Digestive problems, dyspepsia, hiccoughs, and intestinal problems.

Nervous System: Insomnia, nervous tension, and restlessness.

Mandarin is often used for children and pregnant women and is recommended in synergistic combinations with other citrus oils. Recorded actions include antiseptic, antispasmodic, carminative, digestive, mild diuretic, mild laxative, sedative, digestive and lymphatic stimulant, and tonic.

Blending

The oil blends well with other citrus oils, especially Neroli Essential Oil, and with spice oils such as Nutmeg Essential Oil, Cinnamon Bark Essential Oil, and Clove Essential Oil.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing. Possibly phototoxic, although this has not been decisively demonstrated.

Where You Will Encounter It

Mandarin oil is used in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially colognes. It is employed as a flavouring agent, especially in sweets, soft drinks, and liqueurs.

Scent Profile

Sweet, citrus, light, tangy, and fruity. Attains joy.
