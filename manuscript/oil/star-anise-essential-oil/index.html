Star Anise

Star Anise Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the fresh or partially dried fruit of the Illicium verum tree, with a smaller quantity of oil also drawn from the leaves. The finished oil is a pale yellow liquid carrying a warm, spicy, intensely sweet liquorice-like scent. It blends well with rose, lavender, orange, pine, and other spice oils, and has excellent masking properties. The principal constituent is trans-anethole at eighty to ninety percent.

Aromatherapy Use

Star anise oil is reached for across several systems of the body. In circulation, muscles, and joints it eases muscular aches and pains and rheumatic complaints. For the respiratory system it supports bronchitis and persistent coughs. In the digestive system it is used for colic, cramp, flatulence, and indigestion. For the immune system it is reached for during colds. Reported actions are antiseptic, carminative, expectorant, insect repellent, and stimulant.

Other Uses

The pharmaceutical industry uses star anise oil in cough mixtures and lozenges and to mask unwelcome odours and flavours in medicines. It also appears as a fragrance component in soaps, toothpastes, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes, and is widely used for flavouring food, especially confectionery, alcoholic drinks, and soft drinks.
