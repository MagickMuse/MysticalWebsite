Tolu Balsam Essential Oil is distilled from the crude balsam of Myroxylon balsamum var. balsamum.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Crude balsam tapped from Myroxylon balsamum var. balsamum

Method: Two distillation styles produce two distinct oils. Steam distillation yields a pale yellow-brown liquid with a sweet, floral scent and peppery undertone. Dry distillation yields an amber liquid with a richer balsamic-floral scent that slowly solidifies into a crystalline mass on cooling. Resinoids and absolutes are also produced, mainly for use as fixatives.

Note: Base

Scent: Sweet, balsamic, and floral, with a soft peppery warmth

Constituents: Roughly 80% resin and 20% oil, carrying cinnamic and benzoic acid esters, traces of terpenes, eugenol, and vanillin (the source of its honeyed undertone).

Blending

Blends with mimosa, ylang ylang, sandalwood, labdanum, neroli, patchouli, and cedarwood. Used in oriental, spicy, and floral perfume bases as a slow-evaporating base note and natural fixative.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Dry, chapped, and cracked skin; eczema, rashes, scabies, sores, and wounds.

Respiratory Support: Bronchitis, congestion, coughs, croup, and laryngitis. Traditional inhalation uses about a teaspoon added to a steam bath.

Safety

Generally non-toxic and non-irritant, with possible sensitization in those already reactive to benzoin or Peru Balsam. Patch test before use, and always dilute in a carrier.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Chapped & Cracked Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Dry & Sensitive Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Rashes: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Scabies: Apply via skin oil/lotion.
  • Wounds: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Laryngitis & Hoarseness: Apply via steam inhalation.
