Angelica Root Essential Oil is a strong, musky oil extracted from the roots and seeds of angelica. Its resinous strength makes it a natural fixative for magickal oils.

Oil Characteristics

Evaporation: Middle note

Scent: Earthy, sweet, musky

Scent impact: Releases negative emotions and thought patterns

Application: Bath and massage oil, compress, direct inhalation

Magickal Properties

Suggested crystals:

  • Black tourmaline (ruled by Capricorn): grounding and protection
  • Fire agate (ruled by Aries): spiritual strength

As a Fixative

Angelica is a strong, musky oil. Its strength, which comes from resin-like root material, makes angelica a natural fixative for any magickal oil. The purpose of a fixative is to lengthen the scent's staying power and to act as a counterbalance to the top notes in a perfume. In most cases the top note is the scent you smell first. In the case of blends containing angelica, it is powerful enough to overpower the top notes. Angelica can also outlast some base notes.

Blending and Aromatherapy

Aromatherapists suggest using angelica in respiratory blends. The calming action and bronchial relationship make it a fantastic addition to meditation blends as well as protective oils and charms. Due to its affinity with the lungs and the characteristics of the notes, it will blend well with chamomile (sleep), lavender (peace), and lemon (healing).

Aromatherapy Use

The oil is reached for across several systems of the body. For skin care it supports dull or congested complexions, irritated conditions, and psoriasis. In circulation, muscles, and joints it works on the accumulation of toxins, arthritis, gout, rheumatism, and water retention. For the respiratory system it supports bronchitis and persistent coughs, and for the digestive system it is used in cases of anaemia, anorexia, flatulence, and indigestion. On the nervous system it eases fatigue, migraine, nervous tension, and stress-related disorders. For the immune system it is reached for during colds. Principal constituents include phellandrene, pinene, limonene, linalol, and borneol, alongside coumarins such as osthol, angelicin, bergapten, and imperatorin, plus plant acids. Reported actions are antispasmodic, carminative, depurative, diaphoretic, diuretic, emmenagogue, expectorant, febrifuge, nervine, stimulant, stomachic, and tonic, with bactericidal and fungicidal properties.

Other Uses

Angelica root oil is highly valued as a fragrance component in soaps, lotions, and perfumes, particularly colognes, oriental compositions, and heavy chypre fragrances. Some cosmetics use it for its soothing effect on skin complaints. As a flavouring agent it appears in most food categories and in alcoholic and soft drinks, especially liqueurs.

Overcoming Doubt

Angelica is indicated for use in magickal oils for those times when it is hard to believe in yourself. Remember, doubt is the killer of magickal energy. If you doubt your spell or working will be effective, it is better not to do it, and come back to it later. You can take a walk to clear your head, talk to a magickal friend about the working you are considering, or consult a divination tool. The magickal friend is an important part, because it is hard to continue to understand where your doubts lie if all of your soul-searching reaps the return of cynicism or hostility toward magick.

Angelica can stimulate the mind, help release negative patterns in thinking, and clear feelings of emotional hollowness. This plant can also help you regain a connection to divinity, and is therefore good for use in dedication or initiation rituals or workings involving abundance.

Magickal Uses

For new situations: Place a drop of angelica essential oil on a square of tissue and tuck it inside your shoe when visiting a new open circle, or when petitioning to join a new coven or magickal working group. It will help fight the "new kid" nerves that can crop up.

To attract good luck: Place a drop of angelica oil at each outside corner of your home, or each interior corner if you live in an apartment, dorm, or shared space. Alternatively, fill your home with the scent by using an aromatherapy diffuser to gently waft a drop of this strong-smelling herb. This method will also help with purification of space in preparation for ritual and divination.

For ritual preparation: To prepare the body and mind for ritual, add a drop of angelica to your favorite shampoo or body wash. A few drops can be added to a salt bath for an adult to help ease grief, enter a meditative state, or reconnect with the gods.

Protection spray: Fill a custard cup with 3⁄4 cup salt and 3 drops angelica oil. Stir with a popsicle stick or coffee stick to blend. Pour the mixture into a spray bottle and fill it with warm water. Shake to dissolve the salt. Spray around to protect the home, break jinxes and curses, or banish unquiet spirits.

Warnings

⚠️ Use caution if you are pregnant. Less is more with this oil; the scent can quickly become overpowering.

⚠️ Angelica is phototoxic, so avoid the sun for twelve to seventy-two hours after skin exposure. Try a patch test with diluted oil before applying the oil to a large surface area.

⚠️ Should be avoided by diabetics.
