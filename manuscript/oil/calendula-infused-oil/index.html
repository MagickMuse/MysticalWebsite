🌼Calendula Oil Salve

An infused oil produced by macerating the flowers of Calendula (Calendula officinalis) in a fixed carrier. The infused oil is the most accessible form of calendula for skincare and aromatherapy use, distinct from the rare Calendula Absolute produced only in France.

Therapeutic Uses

The infused oil is very valuable in aromatherapy for its powerful skin-healing properties. It is useful for a wide range of skin problems including cracked and rough skin, nappy rash, grazes, cracked nipples, varicose veins, and inflammations. It is also used for burns, cuts, eczema, greasy skin, insect bites, rashes, and wounds.

Folk Tradition

The flowers are current in the British Herbal Pharmacopoeia, specific for enlarged or inflamed lymph nodes, sebaceous cysts, duodenal ulcers, and inflammatory skin lesions. Traditional use also covers skin complaints, menstrual irregularities, varicose veins, haemorrhoids, conjunctivitis, and poor eyesight; the herb was said to comfort the heart and spirits.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing. Suitable for sensitive skin, including infant skin (nappy rash) and cracked nipples in nursing care.

Where You Will Encounter It

Widely available in salves, balms, baby-care preparations, and skincare products. The infused oil is far more common than the absolute, which is reserved for high-class perfumery.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Burns: Apply the infused oil directly to the cooled, intact burn.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Insect Bites: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Irritated & Inflamed Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Rashes: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Wounds: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
