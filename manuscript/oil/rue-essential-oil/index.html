DO NOT USE RUE OIL IN AROMATHERAPY. Rue Essential Oil is an oral toxin, a skin and mucous membrane irritant, and an abortifacient. The source explicitly warns it should not be used at all in aromatherapy and should never be used in perfumery or flavour work. Avoid during pregnancy.

Rue Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the fresh herb of Rue (Ruta graveolens), the bitter Mediterranean shrub of the Rutaceae family whose oil is dominated by the toxic ketone methyl nonyl ketone.

Safety

Rue oil should not be used at all in aromatherapy and should never be used in perfumery or flavour work. It is an oral toxin due to its main constituent, methyl nonyl ketone, which makes up around 90 percent of summer rue oil. The oil is also a skin and mucous membrane irritant and an abortifacient.

Extraction & Characteristics

The essential oil is produced by steam distillation from the fresh herb. It is a yellow or orange viscous mass that generally solidifies at room temperature, with a sharp, herbaceous-fruity, acrid odour. Winter rue oil does not solidify at room temperature. The principal constituent is methyl nonyl ketone (around 90 percent in summer rue oil).

Where You Will Encounter It

Rue oil is employed industrially as a source of methyl nonyl ketone.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Traditionally used to break negativity and dissolve curses. Adding nine drops to the bath each night for nine consecutive nights during the waning Moon is a classic method for lifting a spell cast against you. Soaking a found magical image or poppet in this oil, then burning it, is said to break the associated working.
