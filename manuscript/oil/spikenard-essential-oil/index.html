Spikenard Essential Oil is steam distilled from the dried and crushed rhizome and roots of Spikenard.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Dried and crushed rhizome and roots of Nardostachys jatamansi.

Method: Steam distillation.

Appearance: Pale yellow or amber-coloured liquid.

Scent: Heavy, sweet-woody, spicy-animal, somewhat similar to valerian oil.

Principal constituents: Bornyl acetate, isobornyl valerianate, borneol, patchouli alcohol, terpinyl valerianate, terpineol, eugenol, and pinenes, among others.

Therapeutic Actions

Anti-inflammatory, antipyretic, bactericidal, deodorant, fungicidal, laxative, sedative, and tonic.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin care: Allergies, inflammation, mature skin (rejuvenating), rashes.

Nervous system: Insomnia, nervous indigestion, migraine, stress, and tension.

Blending

Blends well with labdanum, Lavender Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Patchouli Essential Oil, pine needle, Vetivert Essential Oil, and spice oils.

Safety

Probably non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing, with a safety profile similar to valerian oil.

Where You Will Encounter It

Little used these days, usually as a substitute for valerian oil.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Allergies: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, or inhalation (steam).
  • Irritated & Inflamed Skin: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Rashes: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Scars & Stretch Marks: Apply via massage or skin oil/lotion.
  • Wrinkles & Mature Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Traditionally worn during rituals honoring the ancient deities of Egypt.
