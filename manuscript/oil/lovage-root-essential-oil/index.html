An amber or olive-brown liquid steam distilled from the fresh roots of Lovage (Levisticum officinale). The oil carries a rich, spicy-warm, root-like odour and is the more commercially significant of the two lovage essential oils.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the fresh roots. Principal constituents are mainly phthalides (up to 70 percent), including butylidene, dihydrobutylidene, butylphthalides, and ligostilides, with lesser amounts of terpenoids, volatile acids, coumarins, and furocoumarins.

Therapeutic Uses

For circulation, muscles, and joints, the oil is applied to accumulation of toxins, congestion, gout, oedema, poor circulation, rheumatism, and water retention. For the digestive system, it addresses anaemia, flatulence, indigestion, and spasm. Genito-urinary applications include amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, and cystitis.

Blending

Blends well with Rose Oil, Galbanum Essential Oil, Costus Essential Oil, opopanax, Oakmoss Absolute, Bay Laurel Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, and spice oils.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant. Possible sensitization and phototoxic effects due to furocoumarin content. Use with care. Avoid during pregnancy.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used as a fragrance component in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes. The oils and extracts are used as savoury flavouring agents and in liqueurs and tobacco.
