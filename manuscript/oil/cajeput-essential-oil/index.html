Niaouli Essential Oil

Cajeput Essential Oil is the steam-distilled oil of Melaleuca cajuputi.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Fresh leaves and twigs of Melaleuca cajuputi

Method: Steam distillation

Note: Top

Scent: A pale yellowy-green, mobile liquid (the green tinge comes from traces of copper found in the tree) with a penetrating, camphoraceous-medicinal aroma over a softer, slightly fruity body note. Gentler than eucalyptus.

Constituents: Cineol (14 to 65 percent depending on source), terpineol, terpinyl acetate, pinene, nerolidol, and other traces.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Insect bites, oily complexion, and spots.

Circulation, Muscles & Joints: Arthritis, muscular aches and pains, and rheumatism. Cajeput produces a sensation of warmth and quickens the pulse.

Respiratory Support: Asthma, bronchitis, catarrh, coughs, sinusitis, and sore throat.

Genito-Urinary: Cystitis, urethritis, and urinary infection.

Immune Support: Colds, flu, and viral infections.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-sensitizing in normal use, but it may irritate the skin in high concentration. Always dilute well in a carrier oil and patch-test before broader use. Avoid during the first trimester of pregnancy and use cautiously thereafter.

Other Uses

  • Used in dentistry and pharmaceutical work as an antiseptic.
  • A standard ingredient in expectorant and tonic formulations, throat lozenges, and gargles.
  • A fragrance and freshening agent in soaps, cosmetics, detergents, and perfumes.
  • Occasionally a flavour component in food products and soft drinks.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Asthma: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Rheumatism: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Aches and Pains: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Arthritis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Insect Bites: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Spots: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Sinusitis: Apply via steam inhalation.
  • Sore Throat: Apply via vaporization or steam inhalation.
