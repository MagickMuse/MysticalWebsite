Galangal Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the rhizome of the Alpinia galanga plant, producing a warm, spicy oil with ginger-like notes used in prosperity and protection magick.

Tincture

ColorLight yellow
ScentGinger and camphor
UsesLuck, money, protection, exorcism, psychic development

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the rhizomes of Galangal. The oil is a greenish-yellow liquid with a fresh, spicy-camphoraceous odour. Principal constituents include pinene, cineol, eugenol, and sesquiterpenes. An oleoresin is also produced by solvent extraction.

Aromatherapy Uses

Aromatherapy use is limited; the oil has been suggested as possibly suitable for digestive upsets.

Blending

Blends well with Moroccan Chamomile Essential Oil, Sage Essential Oil, Cinnamon Bark Essential Oil, Allspice Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Pine Essential Oil (pine needle), Rosemary Essential Oil, Patchouli Essential Oil, Myrtle Essential Oil, opopanax, and citrus oils.

Safety

Safety data is not currently established.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used as a flavour ingredient, especially in spice and meat products. Occasionally used in perfumery work.
