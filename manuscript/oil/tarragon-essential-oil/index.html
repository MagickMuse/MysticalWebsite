Tarragon Essential Oil is steam distilled from the leaves of Tarragon.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Leaves of Artemisia dracunculus.

Method: Steam distillation.

Appearance: Colourless or pale yellow mobile liquid that turns yellow with age.

Scent: Sweet-anisic, spicy-green.

Principal constituents: Estragole (up to 70 percent), capillene, ocimene, nerol, phellandrene, thujone, and cineol, among others.

Therapeutic Actions

Anthelmintic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, aperitif, carminative, digestive, diuretic, emmenagogue, hypnotic, stimulant, stomachic, and vermifuge.

Therapeutic Uses

Digestive system: Anorexia, dyspepsia, flatulence, hiccoughs, intestinal spasm, nervous indigestion, sluggish digestion.

Genito-urinary system: Amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, PMT.

Blending

Blends well with labdanum, Galbanum Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Oakmoss Absolute, Vanilla Absolute, Pine Essential Oil, and Basil Essential Oil.

Safety

Moderately toxic due to estragole (methyl chavicol) and possibly carcinogenic. Use in moderation only and avoid during pregnancy. Otherwise non-irritant and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used as a fragrance component in soaps, detergents, cosmetics, and perfumes. Employed as a flavour ingredient in most major food categories, especially condiments and relishes, as well as alcoholic and soft drinks.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Dysmenorrhoea: Apply via massage, compress, or bath.
  • Constipation & Sluggish Digestion: Apply via massage or bath.
  • Cramp & Gastric Spasm: Apply via massage or compress.
  • Indigestion & Flatulence: Apply via massage.
  • Amenorrhoea: Apply via massage or bath.
  • PMS: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
