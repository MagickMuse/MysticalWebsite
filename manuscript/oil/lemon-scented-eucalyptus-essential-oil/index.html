Lemon-Scented Eucalyptus

Lemon-Scented Eucalyptus Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the leaves and twigs of Lemon-Scented Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus citriodora), a tall evergreen tree in the Myrtaceae family native to Australia and cultivated mainly in Brazil and China.

Extraction & Characteristics

The oil is a colourless or pale yellow mobile liquid with a strong, fresh, citronella-like odour and a sweet balsamic undertone. Principal constituents are citronellal (80–95 per cent), citronellol, geraniol, and pinene, among others. The gum or 'kino' contains the antibiotic substance 'citriodorol'.

Related Species

There are numerous other species of eucalyptus, including Eucalyptus Essential Oil from E. globulus (blue gum), for which lemon-scented eucalyptus is sometimes substituted in fragrance applications.

Folk Tradition

The dried leaves were traditionally placed in small cloth sachets to perfume the linen cupboard. During the last century, the plant was regarded as a good insect repellent, especially against cockroaches and silverfish.

Therapeutic Uses

In skin care, lemon-scented eucalyptus addresses athlete's foot and other fungal infections such as candida, cuts, dandruff, herpes, scabs, sores, and wounds, and serves as an insect repellent. In the respiratory system, it is used for asthma, laryngitis, and sore throat. In the immune system, it supports the body through colds, fevers, infectious skin conditions such as chickenpox, and other infectious disease. The essential oil contained in the leaves appears to have bacteriostatic activity toward Staphylococcus aureus, attributed to synergism between the citronellol and citronellal present in the oil.

Safety

Non-toxic and non-irritant, with possible sensitization in some individuals. Eucalyptus oil is toxic when taken internally.

Where You Will Encounter It

Lemon-scented eucalyptus is used as a fragrance component (in place of E. globulus) in soaps, detergents, and perfumes, and is also used in room sprays and insect repellents. It is also employed for the isolation of natural citronellal.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Fever: Apply via compress or bath.
  • Chickenpox: Apply via compress, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
  • Asthma: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Cuts & Sores: Apply via skin oil/lotion or compress.
  • Insect Repellent: Apply via skin oil/lotion or vaporization.
  • Spots: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
  • Wounds: Apply via skin oil/lotion, compress, or bath.
  • Laryngitis & Hoarseness: Apply via steam inhalation.
  • Sore Throat: Apply via vaporization or steam inhalation.
