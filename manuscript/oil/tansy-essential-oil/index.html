Tansy
⚠️
DO NOT USE TANSY ESSENTIAL OIL IN AROMATHERAPY, INTERNALLY OR EXTERNALLY. Tansy oil is an oral toxin and abortifacient due to its very high thujone content (66 to 81 per cent). Avoid during pregnancy. Not for home aromatherapy use.

Tansy Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the aerial parts of Tansy (Tanacetum vulgare), the hardy perennial wayside herb of the Asteraceae family also called Bitter Buttons. The oil is dominated by the toxic ketone thujone and is not used in home aromatherapy.

Safety

Tansy oil should not be used in aromatherapy whether internally or externally. It is an oral toxin, poisonous due to its very high thujone content of 66 to 81 per cent, and an abortifacient. Avoid during pregnancy.

Extraction & Characteristics

The essential oil is produced by steam distillation from the whole herb, the aerial parts. The finished oil is a yellow, olive, or orange liquid that darkens with age, with a warm, sharp-spicy herbaceous odour. Principal constituents are thujone at 66 to 81 per cent, camphor, and borneol, among others.

Where You Will Encounter It

Tansy oil is occasionally used in herbaceous-type perfumes. The oil used to be used in alcoholic drinks but is no longer used for flavouring.
