Solar Affiliation

Sunflower is useful because of its affiliation with the sun. This is not just because it looks like the sun. This flower's big, fiery, orange-yellow brightness attracts all eyes in the meadow. The sunflower is the quickest producer of any oils.

Magickal Properties

What does that mean for magick? Prosperity. If you need quick cash, reach for the sunflower oil. If you need oil for the absolute best results, where failure is not an option, grab that sunflower oil. This oil is protected growth. For long-term goals (a year, five years, or ten years), you want to nurture that growth very slowly, and sunflower oil will shine its light upon you. Sunflower turns light into tangible food sources.

Shelf Life

The shelf life for sunflower oil is twelve months.
