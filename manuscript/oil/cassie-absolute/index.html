Cassie Absolute is produced by solvent extraction from the flowers of Acacia farnesiana.

Characteristics

A dark yellow to brown viscous liquid with a warm, floral-spicy scent and rich balsamic undertone. Blends well with bergamot, costus, mimosa, frankincense, ylang ylang, orris, and violet.

Principal Constituents

The absolute contains about 25 percent volatile constituents, mainly benzyl alcohol, methyl salicylate, farnesol, geraniol, and linalol, among others.

Actions

Antirheumatic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, aphrodisiac, balsamic, insecticide.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Use with care for:

Skin Care: Dry, sensitive skin, perfume.

Nervous System: Depression, frigidity, nervous exhaustion, and stress-related conditions.

Safety Data

No available data on toxicity.

Other Uses

Used in high-class perfumes, especially oriental types. Used as a flavour ingredient in most food categories, especially fruit products, alcoholic and soft drinks.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Depression: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Exhaustion: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Nervous Tension and Stress: Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
  • Dry & Sensitive Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Frigidity: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, bath, or vaporization.
