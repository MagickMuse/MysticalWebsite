Carrot Seed Essential Oil is produced by steam distillation from the dried fruits (seeds) of Daucus carota. The oil is mainly produced in France.

Characteristics

A yellow to amber liquid with a warm, dry, woody-earthy odour.

Principal Constituents

Pinene, carotol, daucol, limonene, and bisabolene, among others.

Actions

Anti-oxidant, antiseptic, antispasmodic, carminative, cytophylactic, depurative, diuretic, emmenagogue, hepatic, stimulant, tonic, vermifuge.

Aromatherapy / Home Use

Skin Care: Dermatitis, eczema, psoriasis, rashes, mature complexions, wrinkles.

Circulation, Muscles, and Joints: Accumulation of toxins, arthritis, gout, oedema, rheumatism.

Digestive System: Anaemia, anorexia, colic, indigestion, liver congestion.

Genito-Urinary System: Amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, PMT, glandular problems.

Safety Data

Non-toxic, non-irritant, non-sensitizing.

Other Uses

Used for flavouring, especially seasonings, also liqueurs, soft drinks, and as a fragrance ingredient in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes. Note: a separate solvent extract is produced from the red fleshy carrot root and is used as a food colouring agent.
