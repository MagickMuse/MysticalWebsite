Types and Characteristics

Coconut oil comes in two primary forms, both equally nourishing for skin but with different physical properties:

  • Expressed coconut oil: Solid at room temperature with a low melting point
  • Fractionated coconut oil: A liquid wax that remains fluid at room temperature, offering extended shelf life compared to most carrier oils

Both types are neutral-smelling and readily available at grocery stores. Many essential oil companies use jojoba or fractionated coconut to dilute their more expensive offerings, making quality aromatherapy more accessible. Organic options are easy to find at reasonable prices.

Magickal Properties

Coconut oil aligns beautifully with love, lunar magick, abundance, balance, fertility, conception, good luck, and elemental workings.

Fractionated coconut carries a distinct magickal signature: singular focus and the drive to accomplish a specific goal. When your intention requires laser-sharp concentration on one outcome, fractionated coconut helps channel all energy toward that single purpose without distraction.
