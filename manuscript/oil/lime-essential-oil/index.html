◇Drug Interactions
Cold-pressed (expressed) lime oil is strongly phototoxic due to furanocoumarins (bergapten and others) — avoid intense UV exposure for 12–18 hours after dermal application. Steam-distilled lime is not phototoxic. Furanocoumarins inhibit CYP3A4 — caution at high oral doses with statins, cyclosporine, and other CYP3A4 substrates (the 'grapefruit juice' interaction class). Tisserand & Young dermal max for cold-pressed lime: 0.7%.

Lime Essential Oil is cold-pressed or steam-distilled from the peel of limes (Citrus aurantifolia), producing a bright, zesty citrus oil with uplifting and energizing properties.

Scent Profile

Fresh, bright, citrus, and slightly sweet. Energizing and mood-lifting.

Magickal Correspondences

Element: Fire, Water

Planet: Sun, Moon

Sign: Gemini

Day: Sunday, Wednesday

Magickal uses: Energy, purification, protection, love, happiness, cleansing

Magickal Uses

Lime's bright scent cuts through mental fog and lifts spirits. Diffuse when energy is low or mood needs a boost.

Like all citrus oils, lime excels at purification work. Diffuse to clear stagnant energy from spaces.

The acidic nature of citrus oils creates energetic barriers. Add to protective blends or diffuse near entryways.

Spellwork Correspondences

Candle Dressing: Rub oil from center to either end to "dress" the candle, from top to bottom to draw energy toward you, or bottom to top to banish. Oils are flammable, so use with care.

Spellwork Uses: Invigorate · Joy · Energy
