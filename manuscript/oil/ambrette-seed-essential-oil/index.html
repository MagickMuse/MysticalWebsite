Cypress Essential Oil

Ambrette seed essential oil is steam-distilled from the kidney-shaped seeds of Musk, a plant whose seeds carry a deep musky odour and have given the oil its long-standing place as a botanical musk substitute. Liquid ambrette seed oil benefits from being aged for several months before use, as the scent matures and softens with time. A concrete and absolute are also produced by solvent extraction of the seeds.

The finished oil is pale yellowy-red and pours a rich, sweet, floral-musky scent that is very tenacious on the skin and in blends. It pairs well with rose, neroli, sandalwood, clary sage, cypress, patchouli, and oriental or sophisticated bases. Its principal constituents are ambrettolide, ambrettolic acid, palmitic acid, and farnesol. Available information indicates the oil to be non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Aromatherapy Use

For circulation, muscles, and joints, ambrette seed oil supports cramp, fatigue, muscular aches and pains, and sluggish circulation. On the nervous system it is reached for in cases of anxiety, depression, nervous tension, and stress-related conditions.

Other Uses

The cosmetic and perfumery industries use ambrette seed in oriental-type compositions and as a botanical musk substitute, including in the adulteration of true musk. It also flavours alcoholic and soft drinks and a range of foodstuffs, particularly confectionery.
