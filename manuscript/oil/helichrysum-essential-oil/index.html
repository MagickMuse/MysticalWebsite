A pale yellow to red oily liquid steam distilled from the fresh flowers and flowering tops of Helichrysum. The oil carries a powerful, rich, honey-like scent with a delicate tea-like undertone. An absolute and concrete are also produced by solvent extraction; the absolute is a yellowy-brown viscous liquid with a rich, floral, tea-like scent.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the fresh flowers and flowering tops; an absolute and concrete are also produced by solvent extraction. Principal constituents include nerol and neryl acetate (30 to 50 percent), geraniol, pinene, linalol, isovaleric aldehyde, sesquiterpenes, furfurol, and eugenol.

Therapeutic Uses

For skin care, the oil is applied to abscesses, acne, allergic conditions, boils, burns, cuts, dermatitis, eczema, inflammation, spots, and wounds. For circulation, muscles, and joints, it is used for muscular aches and pains, rheumatism, sprains, and strained muscles. Respiratory uses include asthma, bronchitis, chronic coughs, and whooping cough. For the digestive system, it addresses liver and spleen congestion. Immune-system applications include bacterial infections, colds, flu, and fever. For the nervous system, the oil is used for depression, debility, lethargy, nervous exhaustion, neuralgia, and stress-related conditions.

Blending

Blends well with Roman Chamomile Essential Oil, German Chamomile Essential Oil, Moroccan Chamomile Essential Oil, Boronia Absolute, Rock Rose Essential Oil (labdanum), Lavender Essential Oil, mimosa, Oakmoss Absolute, Geranium Essential Oil, Clary Sage Essential Oil, Rose Oil, Peru Balsam Essential Oil, Clove Essential Oil, and citrus oils.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used as a fixative and fragrance component in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes. The absolute is used to flavour certain tobaccos and is employed for the isolation of natural anethole.
