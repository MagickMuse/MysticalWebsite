Scent Note

Apricot kernel oil does not have an apricot-like scent despite its name.

Aphrodisiac Properties

This oil is aphrodisiac in nature and serves as an excellent base for mixing true essential oils.

Skin Benefits

Apricot kernel oil has a nourishing effect on the skin with easily absorbed nutrients. The penetrating action delivers whatever has been added to it down into the deeper layers of skin and subcutaneous tissue. Well suited for sensitive or aging skin.

Magickal Properties

This oil becomes a part of you rather than sitting on the surface of the skin with a greasy feel. The oil becomes part of the practitioner, a fitting visual for oils dedicated to certain deities and their dedicants.

Useful for anything related to Venus: love, the arts, beauty, luxury. Works well in blends designed to facilitate a good night's sleep.
