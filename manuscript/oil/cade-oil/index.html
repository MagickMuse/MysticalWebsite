Cade

Cade Oil is produced by destructive distillation of the wood of Juniperus oxycedrus, then steam-distilled to yield the rectified oil.

Extraction & Characteristics

Source: Branches and heartwood of Juniperus oxycedrus, typically as shavings or chips. An oil is also occasionally produced from the berries by steam distillation.

Method: Slow destructive distillation produces the crude tar. The crude is then steam-distilled or vacuum-distilled to yield the rectified oil that is used in skincare, perfumery, and pharmacy.

Note: Base

Scent: A clear, orange-brown, oily liquid with a woody, smoky, leather-like aroma. It blends beautifully with thyme, origanum, clove, cassia, tea tree, pine, and other medicinal-type bases.

Constituents: Cadinene, cadinol, p-cresol, guaiacol, and other phenolic compounds that drive both the smoke and the antiseptic action.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Cuts, dandruff, dermatitis, eczema, spots, and other chronic skin troubles. Used for scalp conditions and slow-healing wounds.

Blending

Blends with thyme, origanum, clove, cassia, tea tree, and pine.

Safety

Generally non-toxic and non-irritant, but sensitization can occur, especially on already inflamed or allergic skin. Always patch-test before broad application and dilute well in a carrier. The crude tar is reserved for industrial, pharmaceutical, and veterinary use; only the rectified oil belongs in skincare blends. If your skin is reactive, turpentine (terebinth) oil can sometimes serve as a gentler alternative.

Other Uses

  • A solvent for chemical drugs in pharmaceutical formulation.
  • An ingredient in dermatological creams and ointments.
  • A fixture in veterinary medicine for similar skin and wound applications.
  • A signature note in leather and spice perfumery, soaps, lotions, and creams.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Dandruff: Apply via skin oil/lotion or hair care.
  • Cuts & Sores: Apply via skin oil/lotion or compress.
  • Dermatitis: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, compress, flower water, or bath.
  • Eczema: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, or bath.
  • Spots: Apply via skin oil/lotion or neat application.
