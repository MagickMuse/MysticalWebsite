Myrtle Essential Oil is steam-distilled from the leaves and twigs (sometimes the flowers) of Myrtle (Myrtus communis), the fragrant Mediterranean shrub of the Myrtaceae family. It is a notably mild oil traditionally favoured for children's coughs and chest complaints.

Extraction & Characteristics

A pale yellow or orange liquid with a clear, fresh, camphoraceous, sweet-herbaceous scent somewhat similar to eucalyptus. Its principal constituents are cineol, myrtenol, pinene, geraniol, linalol, and camphene, among others.

Therapeutic Uses

Skin Care: Acne, haemorrhoids, oily skin, and open pores.

Respiratory System: Asthma, bronchitis, catarrhal conditions, chronic coughs, and tuberculosis. Because of its relative mildness, myrtle is a very suitable oil for children's coughs and chest complaints.

Immune System: Colds, flu, and infectious disease.

Recorded actions include anticatarrhal, antiseptic (urinary and pulmonary), astringent, balsamic, bactericidal, expectorant, regulator, and slightly sedative.

Blending

Myrtle blends well with Bergamot Essential Oil, Lavandin Essential Oil, Lavender Essential Oil, Rosemary Essential Oil, Untitled, Hyssop Essential Oil, Bay Laurel Essential Oil, Lime Essential Oil, Ginger Essential Oil, Untitled, and other spice oils.

Safety

Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-sensitizing.

Where You Will Encounter It

Myrtle Essential Oil is used mainly in eau-de-cologne and toilet waters. It is also employed as a flavouring ingredient in meat sauces and seasonings, generally in combination with other herbs.

Therapeutic Applications

  • Common Cold: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Flu: Apply via massage, bath, vaporization, or steam inhalation.
  • Fever: Apply via compress or bath.
  • Chronic Coughs: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or steam inhalation.
  • Catarrh: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Bronchitis: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Asthma: Apply via massage, vaporizer/diffuser, or gentle inhalation.
  • Acne: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, inhalation (steam), or neat application.
  • Congested & Dull Skin: Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, flower water, bath, or inhalation (steam).
  • Sore Throat: Apply via vaporization or steam inhalation.
  • Tonsillitis: Apply via steam inhalation.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

Sacred to Venus, this oil resonates with vibrations of love when used in small, measured amounts.
