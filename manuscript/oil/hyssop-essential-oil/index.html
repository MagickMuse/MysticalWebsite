A colourless to pale yellowy-green liquid steam distilled from the leaves and flowering tops of Hyssop. The oil carries a sweet, camphoraceous top note with a warm spicy-herbaceous undertone.

Extraction & Characteristics

Steam distilled from the leaves and flowering tops. Principal constituents include pinocamphone, isopinocamphone, estragole, borneol, geraniol, limonene, thujone, myrcene, and caryophyllene.

Therapeutic Uses

For skin care, the oil is applied to bruises, cuts, dermatitis, eczema, inflammation, and wounds. For circulation, muscles, and joints, it is used to support low or high blood pressure and rheumatism. Respiratory uses include asthma, bronchitis, catarrh, cough, sore throat, tonsillitis, and whooping cough. For the digestive system, it addresses colic and indigestion. Genito-urinary applications include amenorrhoea and leucorrhoea. Immune-system uses cover colds and flu. For the nervous system, the oil is applied to anxiety, fatigue, nervous tension, and stress-related conditions.

Blending

Blends well with Lavender Essential Oil, Rosemary Essential Oil, Myrtle Essential Oil, Bay Laurel Essential Oil (bay leaf), Sage Essential Oil, Clary Sage Essential Oil, Geranium Essential Oil, and citrus oils.

Safety

Non-irritant and non-sensitizing in standard use. The oil is moderately toxic due to its pinocamphone content. Use only in moderation. Avoid in pregnancy and by those with epilepsy.

Where You Will Encounter It

Used as a fragrance component in soaps, cosmetics, and perfumes, especially eau-de-cologne and oriental bases. Employed as a flavour ingredient in many food products, mainly sauces and seasonings, and in alcoholic drinks, especially liqueurs such as chartreuse.
