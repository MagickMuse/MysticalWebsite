A dark orange, olive, or green viscous liquid produced by solvent extraction from the flowers of Narcissus (Narcissus poeticus). Both a concrete and an absolute are produced; only Holland and the Grasse region of France produce them commercially. The absolute carries a sweet, green-herbaceous odour with a heavy floral undertone, prized in narcotic-floral perfumery.

Extraction & Characteristics

A concrete and absolute by solvent extraction from the flowers. Principal constituents are quercetin and possibly narcissine (the alkaloid that causes nausea). Two main commercial types are produced in France: the cultivated des plaines variety and the wild des montagnes type. The closely related jonquil (N. jonquilla), campernella (N. odorus), and Daffodil (N. pseudo-narcissus) are also occasionally used to produce an absolute.

Therapeutic Uses

The source indicates only perfume use. Folk tradition records antispasmodic use in France for hysteria and epilepsy, devotional anointing in India before temple prayer (alongside rose, sandalwood, and jasmine), and Arabian use as a cure for baldness and as an aphrodisiac. These traditions are historical and cultural, not modern aromatherapy indications.

Blending

Blends well with Clove Essential Oil (clove bud), Jasmine Absolute, Neroli Essential Oil, Ylang Ylang Essential Oil, Rose Oil, Mimosa Absolute, Sandalwood Essential Oil, and oriental and floral fragrance bases.

Safety

All members of the Amaryllidaceae family, especially the bulbs, have a profound effect on the nervous system, causing paralysis and even in some cases death. The bulbs of N. poeticus are more dangerous than those of the daffodil, being powerfully emetic and irritant. The scent of the flowers in a closed room can produce headache and vomiting in some people. Restricted to perfumery; avoid all therapeutic and internal use. Avoid completely during pregnancy.

Where You Will Encounter It

The absolute and concrete are used almost exclusively in high-class perfumes of the narcotic-floral type.

Traditional Grimoire Notes

A narcotic-type oil sometimes called the perfume of Persephone. Said to support good health and a harmonious, relaxed outlook. Promotes sleep and trance states, and can be used for protection.
