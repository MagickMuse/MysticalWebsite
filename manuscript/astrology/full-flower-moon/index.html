The Flower Moon takes its name from the explosion of blossoms that defines this lunation. Spring is in full swing, and the land is alive with color, fragrance, and growth. Other names for this cycle reflect the same vitality: Faery Moon and Goddess Moon speak to the liveliness of nature spirits and divine feminine energy, while Hare Moon nods to the fertile, quick-footed creatures thriving in the warming fields. Dyad Moon hints at partnership and pairing, themes that run through the entire month.

This lunation overlaps with Beltane, and its energy carries the same zest and sensuality. The fae are thought to be especially active now, and creative energy flows freely. Fertility is everywhere, not just in the soil but in ideas, relationships, and artistic pursuits. The air carries enchantment on the breeze, and psychic insight drawn from the natural world, such as reading tea leaves or working with plant spirits, tends to sharpen during this cycle.

Magickal Workings

Flower magick is the obvious starting point: weave blossoms into spells for love, beauty, and attraction. Connect with nature spirits by leaving offerings beneath the full moon, when they are most likely to be present and receptive. In the spirit of the Milk Moon, try a ritual bath of milk and flowers for beauty, renewal, and self-blessing. This is a potent cycle for creative endeavors, prosperity work, and anything that benefits from alluring or magnetic energy. Partnership spells thrive now too, whether for deepening a romantic bond or strengthening a personal friendship. Let the Flower Moon remind you that what you nurture in this season of abundance will bloom far beyond it.
