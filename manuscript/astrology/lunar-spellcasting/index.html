Astrology Grimoire

Lunar Spellcasting

The moon moves through a constant rhythm of waxing and waning, and within that rhythm lies tremendous potential for your magick. Once you understand the meaning of each phase, the intensity of eclipses and supermoons, and the qualities the moon takes on as she moves through each zodiac sign, you may feel ready to dive straight into spellwork. Before you do, there are a few foundational principles worth knowing.

This section covers the essential building blocks for effective moon magick: from keeping a lunar grimoire and tending a lunar altar, to using affirmations and visualization to direct your intention and bring your spells to life.

Keeping a Lunar Grimoire

Many witches maintain a dedicated journal or notebook (sometimes called a grimoire or book of shadows) as a record of their magickal practice. This is the place to note spells that have worked well, information you want to be able to reference, and observations that help you track and deepen your spellcasting over time. Some witches prefer a casual, personal journal, while others opt for a binder that can be reorganized as their practice evolves. Whatever format suits you best is the right choice.

Tracking Your Personal Experiences with the Moon

A lunar grimoire becomes a resource you can return to again and again. The way the moon affects you is deeply individual. One sign might heighten stress for you, while another brings calm or a surge of confidence. Another witch might experience those same signs in the opposite way. Just as the moon reflects the sun's light, she also acts as a mirror for your inner world and outer circumstances.

Beginning to notice how particular phases and signs shape your mood, energy, and daily life can both strengthen your magick and deepen your personal relationship with the moon.

What to Record

To start noticing patterns, you'll need to know the moon's basic position at any given moment. Each time you sit down to journal, note:

  • What phase is the moon currently in?
  • What sign is the moon in, or is she void of course?
  • Are there any special lunar events occurring, such as an eclipse or a supermoon?

Many apps and websites track this information in real time. You can also find printed datebooks that list the moon's phase and sign for every day of the year. Once you're capturing this information consistently, you'll be able to look back and see what was happening in your life during particular placements, which spells you cast, and how they unfolded. Over time, the threads and themes of the moon's influence on your practice will begin to emerge naturally.

Recording Other Details

Beyond the moon's placement, there is plenty more you might choose to capture in your lunar grimoire, moon journal, or book of shadows. If tracking everything at once feels like too much, start with a single element, or focus on whatever feels most meaningful to you:

  • How are you feeling right now, emotionally and physically?
  • Are any significant life events unfolding at this time?
  • What spells are you casting, or what intentions are you actively working with?
  • What aspects of your lunar magick are going well? Is anything calling for adjustment?
  • Which herbal or crystal allies have felt most resonant? Which would you like to explore next?
  • What impressions arise during moon meditations, or simply when you pause to look up at the sky?
  • Are any recurring themes or patterns becoming visible in your life?

Write as much or as little as suits you. The moon is intimately connected to your emotional nature, and your grimoire should feel like an extension of that: organic, personal, and entirely your own. The more attention you give to the moon's influence, the more clearly those broader themes will emerge. Over time, your grimoire will also become a home for the lunar recipes, rituals, and spells that have proven their worth and deserve to be revisited.

Creating Your Lunar Altar

An altar is a sacred space for spellcasting, ritual, and meditation. Returning to the same space over time helps build up a charged, consecrated atmosphere that can deepen and amplify your lunar practice. That said, an altar is not a requirement. The earth itself is sacred ground, and witchcraft asks only that you work with what you have. When it comes to moon magick specifically, a dedicated space for casting lunar spells or charging crystals and ritual tools under moonlight can be a meaningful addition to your practice. It can be as modest as a repurposed tin or as expansive as a full table. Start with whatever is available to you.

Where to Set Up Your Altar

The ideal spot for a lunar altar is somewhere near a window. A windowsill where you can leave items to charge under moonlight is especially lovely, and if you can see the moon directly from that spot, it doubles as a natural meditation and reflection point. But no view is required. Any location will serve, and a moon symbol placed on your altar can always call that energy into the space regardless of visibility.

For those who need flexibility, a moveable altar is a practical and beautiful option. Many witches assemble travel altars inside small tins to carry on trips. A moveable setup might include a lunar symbol, incense, salt, and a crystal or two. A thrifted silver tray works particularly well: it provides a fire-safe surface for candles, and you can carry it outdoors to work under the moonlight, then bring it safely inside when you're done. Even a special cloth you unfold for spellwork counts as a moveable altar.

What to Place on Your Altar

These items make natural additions to a lunar altar and can support both meditation and spellcasting:

  • A lunar symbol: Artwork, a moon-shaped object, a tapestry, or even the tarot card The Moon can serve as a focal point that draws your attention to the moon's energy and invites reflection.
  • Incense: Scent is a powerful tool for shifting the mind into a meditative state and setting the atmosphere for ritual. Choose something calming, or select an incense with a specific lunar association.
  • A devotional candle: A candle lit only during moon magick, meditation, or lunar connection work (and snuffed out promptly when finished) builds a powerful energetic association over time. White and silver are traditional lunar colors, but follow your intuition. You might dress it with Moon Oil, cleanse it with Moon Water, or dust the top with lunar herbs.
  • A crystal: Stones like moonstone and selenite carry a natural resonance with lunar energy. Holding one while meditating can help attune your energy before working.
  • A tarot or oracle deck: The moon communicates readily through symbols. Pulling a daily card while working with lunar energy can offer meaningful insight into what the moon is reflecting back to you at any given moment.
  • A pendulum: A pendulum is a useful divination tool for receiving guidance, reading energy, and selecting lunar herbs or ingredients when you're uncertain which to use.

How to Use a Pendulum

Begin by establishing what "yes" and "no" look like for you. Hold the string or chain with your dominant hand, letting the charm hover over the palm of your nondominant hand. Ask the pendulum to show you "yes" and wait patiently for movement. Once you have a clear sense of that response, ask it to show you "no." You can repeat this calibration each time you work with it.

Common movement patterns include: clockwise for yes and counterclockwise for no, a vertical line for yes and horizontal for no, or a circle for yes and a diagonal line for no. Some people choose their own meanings in advance, or create a simple pendulum board by drawing "yes" and "no" on paper and letting the pendulum swing toward the written answer. Every practitioner's responses are a little different. Discovering your own is part of building a genuine connection with the tool.

As you settle into a lunar practice, you may feel drawn to set certain tools apart exclusively for moon magick. This is never required, but blessing and consecrating your tools to the moon can deepen their resonance and add another layer of intention to your ritual work. Get Ready to Perform Magick

Knowing the moon's phases, eclipses, seasonal cycles, and correspondences gives you a strong foundation, but before you begin spellwork, there are a few key techniques worth practicing. This section walks through how to prepare and plan for lunar spells, cleanse and ground your energy, and work with visualization. These principles apply to any moon spell or meditation, and as always, follow your intuition to find the approach that feels most natural to you.

Prepare Your Tools and Environment

Before reaching for your tools, take a moment to consider both your ingredients and your mindset. The environment you create shapes everything that follows. Think through these elements to build a calm, focused, and magickally supportive space:

  • Scent: What helps you feel centered? Incense, a mist, or an essential oil can shift your mental state quickly, easing you into a more receptive space for meditation or ritual.
  • Sound and music: Sound carries vibration, and vibration is the language of energy. A drum, rattle, or chant can anchor your focus, while a curated playlist or ambient music found online can provide a steady, immersive backdrop.
  • Comfort: It's hard to maintain focus when you're cold or uncomfortable. Lay out a blanket, cushion, or pillow before you begin so you won't need to break the flow of your ritual to tend to something basic.
  • Drink: A warm cup of tea or a glass of water is a simple but grounding ritual in itself. Breathing in the steam, feeling the warmth, and consciously hydrating before spellwork can help settle you into the work.
  • Light: Bright overhead light tends to be harsh and distracting. A small lamp, a cluster of candles, or even near-darkness can help soften the space and draw your attention inward toward the magick.
  • Visuals: Meditation doesn't require closed eyes. If you're visually oriented, consider an image of the moon, a meaningful painting, or even the tarot card The Moon as a focal point for your practice.
  • Touch and movement: If you're someone who connects through the body, hold a crystal whose texture and energy you love, or incorporate gentle movement before settling in. A slow neck roll, an abdominal rotation, or a quiet walk by moonlight can be a ritual in its own right.
  • Journaling: Keep your grimoire within reach. Writing down what's on your mind before you begin can help clear mental clutter, and having it nearby means you can capture any insights that arise during the working.

Once your altar and tools are assembled, you may wish to ritually prepare them before beginning, dedicating them to the moon's energy and the work ahead.

Prepare Your Mind and Energy

With your tools assembled and your environment ready, the next step is to prepare your inner landscape. Spellwork loses its potency when the mind is still tangled up in the events of the day. The following techniques help you quiet mental noise, clear energetic residue, and align yourself with the moon's rhythm before you begin.

Center Yourself

Centering calls your awareness back into your body and the present moment, quieting the mental chatter that spills over from daily life. Here's one simple approach:

  1. Take three slow, deep belly breaths. Feel your stomach expand as you inhale and contract as you exhale. Let the sound of your own breathing become your anchor.
  2. Hold each breath for a few seconds before releasing, resting in the stillness between inhale and exhale.
  3. As you breathe out, let your shoulders soften. Notice the brief silence after each exhale before the next breath begins. With each cycle, your awareness settles more fully into your body.
  4. Bring your focus to the solar plexus, the energy center just below the breastbone, slightly above the navel. Breathe in for four counts, hold for four, exhale for four. Adjust the counts to whatever rhythm feels most natural. With each breath, sense that center glowing with warmth and energy.

Cleanse Your Energy

Throughout the day, you naturally absorb energies from the people and places around you, sometimes forming unconscious energetic cords in the process. Before meditation or spellwork, it's worth clearing this accumulated residue. Think of it as an energetic bath. Some effective methods:

  • Selenite wand: Selenite is named after the moon goddess Selene, making it a particularly fitting tool for lunar work. Slowly trace the wand around your body, giving extra attention to the back of your neck, crown of your head, and heart center. Move with intention and notice any subtle shifts as you work.
  • Cleansing spray: Mists containing cleansing essences like lemon can clear both your aura and your space quickly. You can also craft your own by combining purified or spring water, essential oils, and a splash of alcohol.
  • Herb smoke: Burn the tip of a dried herb sprig or a purpose-made bundle and let the smoke drift through your space. Rosemary is a reliable choice. Open a window for ventilation and be mindful of smoke alarms.
  • Breath: Use your breath as an energetic broom. Inhale cleansing, clear energy and exhale anything you're ready to release, including unwanted thoughts, emotions, or psychic debris.
  • Water: Water is deeply connected to the moon and carries natural cleansing properties. Spritz salt water around yourself, touch it to your skin, or visualize a stream of cool, clear water washing away whatever you no longer need.

Ground Yourself

Grounding anchors your awareness in the physical world and keeps you from being swept away by the intensity of lunar energy, which can stir up deep, vulnerable emotions. It also ensures that the energy you work with during ritual lands in a tangible, real way rather than dissipating into abstraction. Some approaches:

  • Go barefoot outside: The simplest and most direct grounding technique. Stand or walk on grass, soil, sand, or stone. Feel the texture and temperature beneath your feet and let your awareness drop down into the earth.
  • Hold a grounding stone: Hematite or black tourmaline are classic choices. Hold the stone, breathe deeply, and bring your attention to wherever your body meets a solid surface. You may begin to feel the subtle pulse of the earth beneath you.
  • Visualize a grounding cord: Imagine a root or beam of energy extending from the base of your spine deep into the earth's core. With each breath, draw nourishing earth energy up through this cord and let any excess or unwanted energy flow back down. This technique not only connects you to the earth but can actively replenish your energy so your spellwork draws from that source rather than depleting you.

Connect to the Moon

Once you are centered, cleansed, and grounded, you are ready to open to the moon. Go outside or find a window and simply look up. Take in the stars and the moon, and let yourself feel small in the best possible way, part of something vast and ancient. Hold a lunar crystal like moonstone or selenite if you have one nearby.

If the moon isn't visible, you can still make this connection inwardly. Picture yourself as a tree: your roots reaching deep into the earth through your grounding cord, and your branches stretching upward toward the moon. Or visualize a column of silver light descending from the moon and flowing through the crown of your head, filling your body with her energy. Feel the moon's gravitational pull on you, because it is real, and you are part of this same system of tides.

Working with Energy

Once you have a grounding and centering practice in place, learning to sense and send energy becomes the next meaningful step. Being able to feel energy allows you to communicate your intentions to plant spirits, crystal allies, and the forces you call upon in your lunar workings.

Sensing Crystal Energy

Hold a stone in your hand and slow your breathing. Gently bring your awareness to where the stone rests against your palm. You may notice a subtle hum or vibration, or a quiet shift somewhere in your body. Try this with several stones, one at a time, taking deep breaths and tuning in slowly. Some crystals may feel like nothing at first; others may connect with you immediately. Both responses are valid and informative.

Feeling Auras and Energetic Boundaries

Everything has an energy that surrounds it, often called an aura. As you draw closer to that field, the energy tends to grow denser and more defined. One way to begin recognizing what an aura feels like is to work with plants and crystals as practice partners. Go outside or gather a few crystals of different sizes. Hold your hand several feet away from a crystal or plant, then bring it in slowly, paying close attention to any point where you feel a shift. A soft buzzing or increased density of energy is common as your palm draws near.

Sending Energy

Sending energy is how you communicate your intentions to plant spirits, crystal allies, or other forces in your magickal work. Before attempting this, complete one of the centering and grounding practices described above. This ensures you are drawing from an external source rather than depleting your own reserves.

Visualize branches growing from your head and shoulders, reaching all the way up toward the moon and beyond into the cosmos. Just as leaves photosynthesize sunlight, the leaves of your branches gather ethereal moonlight and starlight, drawing that energy back down through you. If this image feels complex, simply visualize a beam of silver lunar light descending from the sky and flowing down through your head and arms.

Once you feel that energy moving through you, channel it outward with a clear intention. You might assign the energy a color that corresponds to your desired outcome, or simply hold your goal clearly in mind as you direct it toward a crystal or plant ally. You may notice a subtle response from them as you do so, or you can visualize your ally attuning to the energy the way a night-blooming flower opens toward the moon.

These techniques sharpen your ability to discern different qualities of energy, recognize when your tools are aligned with your intentions, and assess where your spells may need additional support.

Affirmations and Visualization

Even with solid preparation, we sometimes get in our own way. A spell cast with doubt, or without mental and physical space for the outcome to take root, often falls flat. Two practices that directly address this are affirmations and visualization. Used consistently, they become spells in their own right.

Write a Moon Affirmation

An affirmation is a statement repeated aloud or internally, phrased to affirm your desired mood, feeling, or result. Writing and repeating one throughout the lunar cycle helps align your mind and energy with your intentions and creates room for them to manifest.

Begin with what you want to bring into your life. Jot down a word or phrase, or simply write the desire plainly. Then get more specific. Is there a purpose behind it? A particular form you want it to take? Think about the quality of energy you want it to carry, something that flows freely, or arrives with ease.

Next, write the intention as though it is already happening, from a positive and confident perspective. "Money flows effortlessly and freely to me. I have plenty to cover my needs, save extra, and afford what I want" is more anchored than a vague wish. When an affirmation resonates, you will feel it land in your body.

Decide how often to repeat it. Twice daily is a reliable baseline, but at the start of a new cycle, more frequent repetition helps create the mental and energetic space your spell needs to take hold. Write the final version on its own piece of paper. If you like, anoint it with Moon Oil and place it somewhere you will see it daily, such as a bathroom mirror, so you can say it while preparing for the day and winding down at night.

Repeat the affirmation throughout the waxing phase, adjusting the wording as needed. If desired, burn the paper at the full moon as a release.

To deepen the practice, pair your affirmation with a magickal anchor: hold a crystal aligned with your intention, spray a mist or perfume as you speak the words, or connect the affirmation to a specific symbol, sigil, or charm. Keeping that object visible reinforces the working between formal practice sessions.

Visualization

Visualization is exactly what it sounds like: imagining how something might look or feel if it were already real. In magickal practice, conscious visualization is a powerful way to attune yourself to the energy of your desired outcome and call it toward you. It works especially well for visual and kinesthetic learners, and the key is to stay open to alternate possibilities as circumstances evolve.

When you visualize a goal, manifestation, or spell outcome as though it has already come true, you align your own energy with that result, supporting the intentions you cast at the new moon. Here is one approach:

  1. Begin by cleansing your energy, or clear mental clutter by writing your thoughts out on paper. When ready, close your eyes and breathe deeply.
  2. Bring your desired outcome to mind. Visualize it unfolding. How does it feel in your body? Connect fully with that emotion.
  3. Focus on the end result rather than the mechanics of how it arrives. Feel it with your whole body. Engage your senses: what will you see, hear, smell, taste, or feel when this is real?
  4. Continue until joy or excitement rises naturally in your body. When you feel ready, breathe that energy and that vision into every part of yourself.

Visualization is especially useful during the waxing phase or immediately before a spell, though it supports any moon effort. Returning to it daily allows the details of your manifestation to become clearer and more tangible over time.
