A fixed fire transit governed by the Sun, the Moon in Leo channels emotional energy toward self-expression, creativity, and radiant warmth. This placement ignites the lunar body with solar confidence, heightening the desire to shine, connect, and celebrate.

Natal Moon in Leo

The Moon represents your emotional body, how you feel your experiences, where you find comfort, and what you know instinctually. With the Moon in Leo natally, emotional nature is warm, generous, and expressive.

Emotional Nature: Bold, generous, dramatic, deeply loyal

Self-Care: Emotional fulfillment comes through creative expression, recognition, and the opportunity to shine. You give love abundantly and openly, and you need that same warmth reflected back. Pride is tied to your emotional life: criticism can wound deeply, while praise lifts you tremendously. You need to feel proud of yourself and those you love.

  • Engage in creative projects that let you shine
  • Celebrate yourself and your accomplishments
  • Surround yourself with warmth, gold, and sunlight
  • Perform or share your talents with appreciative audiences
  • Practice generous self-love without guilt
  • Spend time with people who appreciate you
  • Create art, music, or performance as emotional expression
  • Remember that needing recognition doesn't make you shallow

Transiting Moon in Leo

When the Moon transits through the Sun-ruled fire sign of Leo (approximately every 2.5 days), creativity, expression, romance, and playfulness fill the air and the heart. Leo is about self-expression, talents, and sharing your warmth with others: the inner light that everyone carries, radiating from the inside out. Connect with whatever makes you glow, whether that is a person or a passion.

Creative energy and romantic ventures are amplified under this transit. You may feel inspired to throw a party, celebrate yourself, host a gathering to honor your friendships, or simply relax and have fun. Leo rules the heart, inviting heart-to-heart connection and inspiring inner strength. Reflect on how you express yourself in the world and step into the starring role of your own life.

New Moon in Leo (Leo season, roughly July 23 to August 22): Strengthens new beginnings in all Leo-favored endeavors. Embrace a fresh sense of confidence, explore talents and creative projects, or practice expressing yourself and sharing your warmth with the world.

Full Moon in Leo (Aquarius season, roughly January 20 to February 18): Themes of romance, self-expression, and celebration take center stage. The energy bridges individualized Aquarius with connective, expressive Leo. Find your unique self in the balance and do not be afraid to embrace it.

Leo Moon Magick

Connect with your inner lunar radiance. Embrace your uniqueness and cast confidence spells, since Leo ignites your inner light. This extends to glamour magick: let your inner light shine outward, giving your aura a radiant glow that attracts blessings, friendships, and gifts. Cast spells for friendship, inner strength, and creative ventures. The Leo full moon is an especially advantageous time to channel divine energy into creative works.

Do: Confidence and charisma spells, creative projects and artistic expression, solar magick and fire element work, celebration rituals, self-love and glamour magick, leadership spells, heart-opening and generosity work, talent development, bold manifestation

Don't: Excessive self-criticism or diminishing yourself, hiding your talents or playing small, work requiring you to suppress your expression, attempt emotional restraint, shadow work focused on shame
