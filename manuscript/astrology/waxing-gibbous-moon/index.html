In Love Workings

The waxing moon is the preferred lunar phase for all traditional love workings, philter-craft, and chalice operations. For the most traditionally precise timing, the moon should be in the first ten degrees of Taurus or Virgo, well aspected to Venus and Saturn. Workings are performed on a Friday at 8am, 3pm, or 10pm while the moon is waxing.
