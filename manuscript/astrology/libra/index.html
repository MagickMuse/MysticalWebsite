True to Your Sign

Libra holds the seventh house of the zodiac and falls between September 23 and October 22. As an air sign ruled by Venus and symbolized by the scales, this sign carries a peace-loving, agreeable, harmonious, and balanced current with strong empathetic instincts. The shadow side leans toward insecurity and indecisiveness.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Libra color: Green

Green stones align with Libra's harmonious, balanced, relationship-oriented energy. Choose from Jade for harmony and justice, or Peridot for balancing relationships and releasing jealousy.

The Scales

The striking emblem of Libra, seventh sign of the zodiac, is the set of scales, the only lifeless object to stand for a sign of the wheel, though they are often shown held in the hands of a human figure. Some hold that Libra was the last sign to win formal recognition, having once belonged to the sign before it, Scorpio, while others dispute that account. The theme of balance and equilibrium runs through many of the old astrological traditions, with the Indian, Persian, Greek, and Babylonian systems all in agreement. The Greeks named the sign Zugos, meaning yoke. Fittingly, Libra was tied to Maat, the Egyptian goddess of justice, and the image of a woman bearing balanced scales and the sword of justice still carries great force as the emblem of the law.

The Glyph

The shorthand for Libra does indeed resemble the yoke the Greeks saw in the constellation, yet it also bears a strong likeness to an Egyptian symbol called the Akhet, which shows the Sun rising between two hills. The Egyptians named the constellation Ta Akhet, the place of the sunrise. The Akhet too speaks of balance, between male and female or between the Sun and the Earth, with the space between them serving as mediator and the point of equilibrium.

Qualities

Libra mattered greatly to the Romans, who held that Italy lay under its sway, partly because Rome was founded on October 4, when the Sun stands in this stretch of the zodiac; in later thinking the country was given over to Leo instead. As a sign of the air element, Libra has a gentle nature, peaceful, affectionate, orderly, and elegant, yet also changeable and given to mood swings when its balance is upset. Arriving in a mellow season, when activity slows after the harvest is gathered, Libra may be more relaxed and even-tempered than other signs. It is fittingly the sign of the diplomat, one who can weigh both sides of a matter and reach an impartial judgment, a quality that suits its standing as the zodiac's only inanimate symbol.
