The Hunter's Moon takes its name from the practice of tracking prey by the light of this final harvest-season full moon. With winter approaching, hunters used the extended moonlight to secure meat that would be preserved for the cold months ahead. Since this lunation falls near Samhain, it marks the last of the three harvests, and the themes of sacrifice run deep: the sacrifice of the land that fed the harvest, the sacrifices necessary to sustain the cycle of life, the sacrifices of the ancestors and witches who came before, and the personal endings that make room for what comes next.

Whether called the Hunter's Moon, Blood Moon, or Harvest Moon, this cycle centers on the give and take woven through all living things. As the darker months settle in, many witches find this to be a time of heightened power. Shadow work and personal alchemy feel more accessible, divination sharpens, and psychic communication with the realm of spirit comes more naturally. The thinning of the veils around Samhain amplifies everything this lunation already carries.

Magickal Workings

Focus on the cycle of life and offer gratitude for the exchange of energy that sustains it. Divination, psychic rituals, and practices that connect you to the dead thrive under this moon. Reflect on the lessons of the past, work with bones for charm casting or bone reading, or craft your own bloodred ink from pomegranate for spellwork. Since Samhain is often called the witch's new year, use this full moon for deep cleansing and banishing: extinguish bad habits, release stagnant patterns, and clear old energy so you can step into the new cycle unburdened. Ask yourself honestly what you wish to lessen in your life as winter takes hold, and let the Hunter's Moon light the way forward.
