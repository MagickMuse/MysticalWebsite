Correspondence Chain: Venus is the planetary hub of the love current in Western magickal tradition.

Love → Venus → Friday → Rose → Pink

Venus governs this entire chain. When a spell calls for rose petals, a pink candle, or a Friday working, it is reaching toward Venus's domain whether or not the planet is named explicitly. Working directly with Venus, through her seal, her hour, or her day, concentrates the same energy at its source.

The Planetary Seal of Venus

Seal Symbolism

The Venus seal features graceful curves reminiscent of the feminine form. An element of encircling is woven throughout the design, the line extending from the center circle wraps and writhes around the rest of the seal, like words that literally spellbind. This reflects Venus's power to enchant, attract, and bind through beauty and influence.

Powers of Venus

Venus rules emotions, love, friendships, romance, enchantment, beauty, art, parties, and all types of attraction and influence. Her domain encompasses the magnetic forces that draw people together and the aesthetic pleasures that make life beautiful.

Applications of the Seal

The Venus seal can be used in five primary ways:

Illumination: Develop understanding of emotions and cultivate empathy. Work with sex magick to explore sacred sensuality and spiritual connection through physical union.

Financial: Leverage influence and relationships. Success often depends on who you know rather than what you know. Venus opens doors through connection and charm.

Love: Venus encompasses all aspects of love: romantic, platonic, familial, and self-love. The seal supports attraction, partnership, and deepening bonds.

Wrath: Invoke Venus to withdraw from a person, causing lovers and friends to turn against them. Venus can also be the cause of strife, remember how many conflicts arose from Aphrodite's actions.

Healing: Address all forms of emotional healing and sexual maladies. Venus soothes heartbreak, restores confidence, and heals wounds of intimacy.

Geomantic Characters of Venus

Venus governs two geomantic figures, each carrying a different expression of her enchanting power. These characters can be drawn on talismans, etched into candles, or inscribed on spell materials to attract love, deepen beauty, and weave the magnetic pull of desire.

Ceremonial Planetary Seals of Venus

In the ceremonial tradition, these sigils are arranged within a double-ringed circle. The Seal is positioned at the top center, the Intelligence beneath it, and the planetary symbol to one side or below for visual balance. Copper is the traditional material for inscribing Venus's ceremonial seals. The Intelligence is regarded as benevolent in nature, while the Spirit is regarded as malevolent. For this reason, only the Seal and Intelligence are incorporated into ceremonial magick circles.

In Love Workings

Venus governs the symbolic domain of the chalice in traditional love workings. The chalice is dedicated to the subtler powers of love and enchantment, invoked through a symbolism akin to classical divinities of love such as Venus and Amor. Love spells and philter-craft are ideally timed to a Friday (Venus's day) during a waxing moon, with advanced practitioners placing the moon in the first ten degrees of Taurus or Virgo, well aspected to Venus and Saturn.
