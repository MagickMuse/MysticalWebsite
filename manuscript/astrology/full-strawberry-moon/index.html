The Strawberry Moon arrives with the ripening of early summer's first sweet fruits, and its energy carries that same warmth and sweetness. This cycle bridges the transition from late spring into full summer, and it brims with positivity, vitality, and the harmonious hum of a world in full production. Bees work the flowers, grain pushes upward in the fields, and the longest day of the year may fall within this lunation if it arrives close to the summer solstice.

The many nicknames for this moon reflect its character. Rose Moon and Honey Moon evoke love and sweetening. Partner Moon speaks to commitment and devotion. Mead Moon connects to celebration, feasting, and the tradition of honeymoon, the sweetest stretch of a new union. Strong Sun Moon and Hot Moon acknowledge the blazing light that defines this time of year, while Green Corn Moon points to the agricultural abundance already building toward the harvest season ahead.

Magickal Workings

Love and relationship magick are the heart of this lunation. Focus your efforts on happiness, harmony, and deepening the bonds that matter to you. Honey jar spells are especially fitting now: create and bless one to draw your desires closer with steady, sweetening energy. Work with strawberries to enchant positive outcomes and invite harmony into your space. This is also a strong cycle for prosperity and abundance, as the productive energy of early summer supports growth that will pay off during the harvest moons to come. Spells for commitment, whether to a relationship, a goal, or a personal vision, carry extra weight under the Strawberry Moon's warm light.
