Waxing Moon For Growth and New Projects

Moon Phase Magick

The waxing moon is growing toward full. This is the time to do magick for increase, prosperity, health, wellness, and love.

Energy of This Phase

The First Quarter Moon appears when exactly half of the Moon's face is illuminated, marking the midpoint between new and full. This brief, transitional phase lasts roughly one day before the Moon slides into the waxing gibbous. It sits at the crossroads of your manifestation: the dark half of the cycle, representing the unknown and unmanifest, meets the light half, where material reality and its physical boundaries begin to take shape.

Astrologically, the Moon and Sun form a square at this point, positioned 90 degrees apart with Earth at the center. Squares carry friction between the two celestial bodies involved. The initial spark of the new moon may have faded, and challenges, blockages, or stalled energy can surface. While this tension can feel uncomfortable, it holds tremendous potential for growth when you lean into it rather than pull away. This is where compromise and perseverance become your greatest allies.

The first quarter is a time of crossroads. You may need to rethink your intentions, pivot your approach, or push forward with renewed determination. Adaptation, creativity, and strengthening are all favored now. Pull tarot cards for insight, cast a spell to carry you beyond the crossroads, or work magick focused on restoring balance.

There is also an element of the unknown woven into this phase. Like the dark half of the Moon itself, you may not be able to see what lies ahead. Sometimes the half moon calls for risk, for rolling the dice when certainty is not an option. Fortunately, there is magick for that as well. Work spells to shift luck and chance in your favor, and trust that the momentum of the waxing cycle is still carrying you forward.

Work with this energy for:

  • Pushing through obstacles and building resilience
  • Reassessing and refining your intentions
  • Drawing on divination for clarity at the crossroads
  • Spells for balance, luck, and turning chance in your favor
  • Taking decisive action or pivoting with purpose
  • Creative problem solving and adaptation
  • Strengthening resolve and courage
  • Checking in on progress and recalibrating
