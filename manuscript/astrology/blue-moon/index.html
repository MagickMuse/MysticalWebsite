A blue moon is the name given to an extra full moon that appears within a given time frame. Because the average lunar cycle runs about 29.5 days, slightly shorter than most calendar months, a second full moon occasionally fits into one month or season. The term carries a sense of rarity and good fortune, and many practitioners treat it as an amplified full moon window.

Types of Blue Moons

The phrase "blue moon" can refer to three different occurrences:

  • Astronomical: Each season typically contains three full moons. When a season holds four, the third full moon is called the astronomical blue moon. This happens roughly once every two to three years and has historical roots in agricultural timing, since the interplay of seasonal and lunar rhythms once guided planting and harvest.
  • Calendrical: The second full moon falling within a single calendar month. This definition became popular in modern times after being introduced through an error in a 1946 astronomy magazine. Because most months are 30 or 31 days long, a calendrical blue moon also occurs about every two to three years.
  • Astrological: The second full moon occurring within a single zodiacal season. In a typical zodiac season there is one full moon in the opposing sign. If the timing overlaps so that two full moons fall during the same zodiacal window, the second one carries the blue moon label. This type is not formally standardized but follows the same principle of repeated lunar energy.

Magickal Significance

Some practitioners treat a blue moon as no different from any other full moon, since the underlying cycle does not change. Others see the extra occurrence as a meaningful amplification, a sense of second chances, repeated energy, wonder, or good luck.

When approached as a time of repeated energy, a blue moon can be used to revisit and amplify intentions set during a previous lunation. If a working did not land the way you hoped, the blue moon offers an energetic redo.

Each type of blue moon lends itself to a slightly different angle:

  • A calendrical blue moon weaves the lunar cycle into our constructed calendar, making it well suited for self-made success, business goals, and monthly planning.
  • An astrological blue moon (a second full moon in the same sign) invites you to revisit and deepen that sign's themes.
  • An astronomical blue moon adds an extra lunation to a season, giving you more time to work with the combined seasonal and lunar energy.

Many consider the blue moon especially potent for luck, success, power, and prophecy. With so much of modern life planned around monthly cycles, a calendrical blue moon can mark an illuminated and energetically supercharged month.

Esbat Tradition

Within the Wiccan Esbat lunar cycle, the Blue Moon functions as a special descriptive name layered onto the regular monthly Full Moon name. When a second Full Moon appears in any given month, it carries the additional designation of Blue Moon and is believed to infuse that lunation with heightened Spiritual Energy. This makes it particularly powerful for workings involving spiritual development, connection to the divine, and deepening one's practice.
