True to Your Sign

Virgo holds the sixth house of the zodiac and falls between August 23 and September 22. As an earth sign ruled by Mercury and symbolized by the virgin, this sign carries a logical, analytical, practical, and focused current that approaches life with methodical precision. The shadow side leans toward perfectionism and self-criticism.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Virgo color: Apple-Green

Apple-green stones align with Virgo's analytical, healing, perfectionist energy. Choose from Green Feldspar for emotional balance and heart opening, or Green Chrysoprase for renewal and compassionate service.

The Virgin

For all that her name suggests, Virgo has worn many guises across the long ages since the zodiac was first mapped, from the chaste maiden to the wanton temptress and every shade of the feminine that lies between. She has been named for many goddesses. To the Romans she was Ceres, cradling a sheaf of corn as a token of fertility and the harvest, and indeed Virgo rises just as the crops are gathered in. The Egyptians tied her to Isis and to Maat, goddess of truth. Every name given to her speaks of the Virgin or the Maiden.

A Greek tale connects her with Astraea, goddess of truth and virtue, who once dwelt among mortals alongside the other immortals. When Pandora loosed every kind of misery upon the world, life on Earth grew unbearable, and though Astraea lingered as long as she could endure it, she was the last of the deities to depart, her finer nature wounded by human conduct. It is said she will come again when humankind is ready to receive her. For Christians she becomes Mary, virgin mother of Christ, at times shown with a five-petaled Marian flower that hides the five-pointed star or pentagram within it. The earliest images of Virgo render her as a winged angel.

The Glyph

The glyph of Virgo ties her to the signs for Scorpio and Libra, and some have read it as the letters MV, for Maria Virgo, a nod once more to the Virgin Mary. At times the closing loop of its triple arches is drawn as a fish or vesica piscis, a shape that pictures the realm of spirit passing into the realm of matter and so deepens the link to the Christian virgin.

Qualities

Under the earth element, Virgo carries qualities of calm, discernment, efficiency, grace, and a keen mind. To these are joined self-command and discipline, along with patient, methodical diligence and a love of order. Virgo is a spiritual sign, a truth shown in the wings she is sometimes given to mark the soul's ascent. Rising in the season of harvest, she is maternal, fertile, and nurturing, ready for whatever may come her way.
