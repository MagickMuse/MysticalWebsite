Moon Void-of-Course - A Pause Between Signs

What is Void-of-Course?

The Moon is considered Void-of-Course when it is passing from one zodiac Sign into the next. During this transitional window, which typically lasts between one and four hours, the Moon holds the influence of both the departing and arriving Signs in a temporary balance. This creates a liminal space where lunar energy is neither fully settled in one Sign nor the other.

Magickal Significance

The Void-of-Course period is generally considered unfavorable for magickal workings. Spells cast, projects launched, or important decisions made during this window tend to fizzle out, stall, or resolve in ways you did not anticipate. The energy is too unsettled to carry focused intention forward reliably.

Best Practices

  • Avoid beginning new spells, rituals, or important ventures during the Void-of-Course window
  • Use this time for rest, reflection, meditation, journaling, or reviewing past workings
  • Consult a Magickal Almanac, Lunar Almanac, or Farmer's Almanac to find current Void-of-Course times for your area
  • Routine tasks that do not require a specific outcome are generally safe during this period

Connection to Other Phases

The Void-of-Course can occur during any moon phase. Its influence overlays the energy of whatever phase the Moon is currently in, temporarily muting or destabilizing that phase's qualities until the Moon fully enters the next Sign.
