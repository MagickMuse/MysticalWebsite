True to Your Sign?

Gemini (May 21 to June 20)

The third sign of the zodiac, Gemini is an air sign ruled by Mercury. Its symbol is the twins. Geminis are often intellectual and natural communicators driven by forward thinking and analysis, with minds capable of going anywhere. Inquisitive captures the core of the Gemini temperament. The shadow side shows in inconsistency and changeability. Their endearing zest for new ideas and experiences can make committing to anyone or anything difficult.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Gemini color: Variegated or Striped

Gemini's dual nature calls for stones with multiple colors or patterns. Choose from Agate for balance and grounding, Banded Onyx for protection and integration of opposites, or Striped Chalcedony for communication and adaptability.

The Third Sign

Gemini arrives just before the summer solstice, the halfway turn of the year, and its twins speak to the human need for contact with another. The sign carries the importance of forming and keeping a relationship, and the idea that two can join into a perfect whole even as each struggles to keep an identity of its own. At heart it is the sign of duality, in both outward appearance and inner identity, and of the soul mate.

The Twins

Astrologers agree that the constellation shows two figures, usually embracing or holding hands, though their sex and their relationship are open to debate. The Egyptians saw a man and a woman; the Romans read them as Apollo and his twin sister Diana; the Persians and Greeks named them Dopatkar and Didumoi; the Babylonians took them for twins but left the gender undefined; and in India they were called the Lovers, or Mithuna. Whether physical twins, lovers, or androgynous beings, it scarcely matters, for the sign stands for duality and the meeting of soul mates. Early depictions show a couple making love, an image of opposites united in harmony that is echoed by the yin yang and the yab yum.

The Glyph

Fittingly, the glyph of Gemini resembles the Roman numeral for two. The two upright figures are joined at the head, the realm of spirit, communication, and ideas, and again at the feet, the realm of matter, quite literally their common ground.

The Air of the Twins

Gemini is a sign of opposing forces, material and spiritual, and at times one twin is shown as mortal, marked by a scythe, and the other immortal, holding a lyre. Belonging to the air element and ruled by Mercury, the sign is communicative, idealistic, inquisitive, versatile, and inventive. It is often linked with mythological twins such as Romulus and Remus, the founders of Rome, or Castor and Pollux, the heavenly twins, and it appears in the Tarot as the Lovers. Those born under Gemini are said to be dualistic in their interests and ways of living, restless, impatient, and easily bored, and their lifelong quest is to reconcile the opposing forces within and find an inner harmony.
