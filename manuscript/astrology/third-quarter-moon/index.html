Waning Moon For Releasings, Cleansings, and Banishings

Triple Goddess Association

The Last Quarter Moon marks the transition from the Crone's waning wisdom into the deeper mystery of the Dark Moon. In the Triple Goddess framework, this represents the moment of active surrender, when the Crone teaches us the necessity of letting go of what has served its purpose.

Moon Phase Magick

When the moon has waned to a half circle, it reaches the last quarter moon. Similar to the first quarter, the moon's light is in balance, being half bright and half dark, for about a day. However, during this phase, the moon is receding into the darker part of its cycle.

At this halfway point, a transition from one energy to the next occurs, accompanied by a sense of approaching finality. This phase bridges the gap between light and dark, signaling that the moon's light will soon be overcome by darkness.

Energy of This Phase

The waning gibbous phase offered a time to reflect on the forthcoming decline of lunar light, allowing you to glean insight and seek final manifestations. At the last quarter, another point of balance between dark and light, you stand at a tipping point. You are now called to embrace surrender and fully let go.

As the themes of release begin to take hold, you must decide what to release and what to keep. Much like a caterpillar casting a cocoon, this phase invites you to turn inward toward matters of the spirit, metamorphosing toward new energy.

Overcoming Tension

Like the first quarter moon, the last quarter forms a square, creating a right angle between the sun and the moon. This alignment can introduce tension, particularly within the internal and spiritual realms.

Analyze any internal conflicts or revelations that arise, as they provide insight into what it is truly time to release. The last quarter moon assists in letting go of anything you have been holding onto. Use this tipping point to identify stuck energy that has blocked your progress. With this insight, you can cast spells to eliminate bad habits, break repeating patterns, and vanquish critical self-talk.

Work with this energy for:

  • Surrendering and fully letting go of attachments
  • Forgiveness and resolving internal conflicts
  • Making decisions about what to release and what to keep
  • Breaking bad habits and repeating patterns
  • Banishing critical self-talk and stuck energy
  • Turning inward for spiritual metamorphosis
