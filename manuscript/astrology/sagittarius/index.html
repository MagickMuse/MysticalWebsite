True to Your Sign

Sagittarius holds the ninth house of the zodiac and falls between November 22 and December 21. As a fire sign ruled by Jupiter and symbolized by the archer, this sign carries an unconventional, idealistic, open-minded, and visionary current with a spiritually minded orientation. The shadow side leans toward recklessness and ruthlessness.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Sagittarius color: Blue

Blue stones align with Sagittarius' philosophical, expansive, truth-seeking energy. Choose Sapphire for wisdom and spiritual insight, helping you aim your arrow toward higher truth.

The Centaur and the Bowman

The old astrologers all saw the constellation of Sagittarius as an image of the hunt. In India the bow alone stood for the sign, while in the Persian zodiac the centaur himself appears, drawing back his bow, and in Rome it was Diana, goddess of the hunt, who held the bow and arrow. The centaur is half human, and his other half points to a leaning toward the bestial. That same duality can be read another way, as the spiritual nature of a person, shown in the arrow poised to fly up into the heavens, held back or burdened by the weight of the physical world.

The Glyph

The sign of Sagittarius is among the clearest in the whole zodiac. It pictures the arrow, an emblem of spiritual transcendence, and at the arrow's base sits a bar that carries all the meaning of the cross: the four elements and the material world. Here again is the duality of the sign, the effort to hold the world of matter in balance with the world of spirit. Sagittarius hunts for far more than game, and so the figure also speaks of a spiritual quest.

Qualities

Sagittarius rises in winter, a season when the wheel of the year falls into a natural pause and the time for hunting comes. From this come its nomadic streak and its love of wide-open country. The fire that rules the sign has moved on from the perilous blast of Aries and the steady burn of Leo to become, in Sagittarius, the flame of spiritual enlightenment, the very flame pictured hovering above the heads of certain saints and other illumined figures. Those born under it may spend their lives seeking to join the material and the spiritual. For all that, it is a deeply practical sign: raised up on the horse's body, Sagittarius can see the wider horizon beyond the trees, while the four hooves planted on the ground echo the four directions of the cross at the foot of the arrow. Above all, the Sagittarian aims high yet keeps both feet firmly on the earth.
