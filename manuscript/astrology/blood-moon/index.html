Moon Phase Magick

The Blood Moon is an uncommon celestial event in which the Full Moon takes on a deep russet to wine-red coloration. This striking appearance most frequently occurs between August and October, though it can manifest at other times depending on atmospheric conditions or during lunar eclipses.

Esbat Tradition

Within the Wiccan Esbat cycle, the Blood Moon serves as a special descriptive name that overlays the regular monthly Full Moon name when this rare coloration appears. Rather than replacing the month's moon name, it adds a layer of additional meaning and power.

Magickal Properties

When a Blood Moon rises, it amplifies the month's Full Moon with intensified Power, raw Energy, and Aggressive force. This makes it ideal for:

  • Workings requiring exceptional strength or force
  • Competitive situations and victory magick
  • Protection spells with teeth
  • Reclaiming personal power
  • Breaking through stubborn obstacles
  • Assertive manifestation

The primal, visceral quality of the Blood Moon's appearance reminds practitioners of the raw, untamed aspects of lunar power. Work with this energy when you need to push through barriers or claim what is rightfully yours.
