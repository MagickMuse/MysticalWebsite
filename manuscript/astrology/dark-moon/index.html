New/Dark Moon: For divination and dark power magick

Triple Goddess Association

The Dark Moon is the Crone as Mystery Moon, totally non-illuminated. In the Triple Goddess framework, the Dark Moon reveals the Hidden Face of the Goddess as the One Who Transforms. She is the Womb as the Tomb, the passage to Rebirth. This phase is reserved for divinations, meditations, and Dark Power Magicks. As a general rule, the influence of any moon phase lasts approximately three days.

Moon Phase Magick

The phrase “dark moon” is used in a few different ways in modern witchcraft. Some practitioners use it interchangeably with “new moon.” Others use it for the full stretch of nights when the Moon is not visible, and reserve “new moon” for the first visible sliver of the waxing crescent. Here, “dark moon” refers to the final window of the waning cycle, after the last visible crescent fades, but before the exact Sun-Moon alignment of the new moon.

This in-between space can feel like limbo, tucked beneath the waning crescent as a hidden ninth beat in the lunar rhythm. It carries an atmosphere of mystery and transformation, where banishing work tends to feel sharp and decisive. This is also a strong time for shadow work and inner alchemy: bringing awareness to fears, insecurities, ego patterns, and the parts of the self that stay out of sight, yet still influence choices and behavior.

If something needs to be cut off at the root, released without negotiation, or dissolved so it cannot regrow, this phase supports that kind of finality. In skilled hands, the dark moon becomes a threshold where old stories can be transmuted into new ones.
