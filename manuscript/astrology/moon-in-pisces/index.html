A mutable water transit governed by Neptune, the Moon in Pisces channels emotional energy toward mysticism, healing, and boundless compassion. This placement dissolves the lunar body into the currents of collective feeling, heightening psychic sensitivity, dreamwork, and the power of going with the flow.

Natal Moon in Pisces

The Moon represents your emotional body, how you feel your experiences, where you find comfort, and what you know instinctually. With the Moon in Pisces natally, emotional nature is empathic, mystical, and boundless.

Emotional Nature: Deeply psychic, compassionate, imaginative, permeable

Self-Care: You feel not only your own emotions but also absorb the feelings of everyone around you. You're deeply psychic and connected to the collective unconscious. Emotional boundaries are challenging, you may struggle to know where you end and others begin. You need time alone to discharge absorbed emotions and reconnect with yourself. You show love through compassion, sacrifice, and spiritual connection.

  • Develop strong energetic boundaries and shielding
  • Spend time near oceans, lakes, or water
  • Engage in creative and artistic expression
  • Practice discernment about whose emotions you're feeling
  • Maintain healthy escapism through art, music, or meditation
  • Work with water magick and emotional healing
  • Allow yourself to cry and release
  • Remember that you don't have to save everyone

Transiting Moon in Pisces

The Moon in watery Pisces (approximately every 2.5 days) highlights themes of psychic ability, healing, and going with the ebbs and flows of life. Represented by the fish, Pisces has the ability to dive deep: into spirituality, into the realm of the collective unconscious, or into old wounds. Pisces is in tune with the currents of life and seamlessly swims through them, teaching us to go with the flow. The Piscean association with music and channeling great works of art, coupled with the Moon's inspiring tendencies, makes this a wonderful time to tap into inspiration from the universe. Since Pisces is ruled by Neptune, dreams and fantasy are heightened during this transit.

New Moon in Pisces (Pisces season, roughly February 19 to March 20): An occasion for beginnings in all Pisces themes. Start a new meditation practice, embrace the idea of going with the flow, or try a new way to unwind and connect to the currents of life.

Full Moon in Pisces (Virgo season, roughly August 23 to September 22): Creates a seeming conflict between planning and overthinking versus going with the flow. Harness the balance of these two forces for profound moon magick. With the full Moon in psychic Pisces, you may experience amazing psychic insights, discern carefully which are premonitions and which are fantasies.

Pisces Moon Magick

Combining the Moon's impact on sleep with Pisces being ruled by the planet of dreams, this is a time to explore dream magick. Uncover your dreams' symbolism for psychic information or to reveal wounds that need healing. Cast spells for sleep and dreamwork. Psychic work of all kinds is favorable now. In Pisces, the Moon invites you to tap into your sense of feeling: not just emotions, but the flow of energy around you. If you are a creative, try channeling psychic information into works of art. Casting moon magick while listening to music is especially fitting for the Pisces Moon. Healing is also available now: soothe and invoke peace and harmony with the healing power of water. Cleanse away old wounds. Take a moon bath or drink a lunar tea and read the tea leaves. Investigate past lives. Meditate with the Moon and tune into the collective unconscious.

Do: Psychic development and intuition work, dream magick and oneiromancy, spiritual connection and mystical union, compassion and healing magick, water magick, creative and artistic expression, meditation and trance work, ancestral and spirit communication, moon baths and lunar teas, past life investigation, music-infused spellwork

Don't: Expect clear boundaries or practical focus, make important decisions requiring clarity, detailed analytical work, harsh banishing or aggressive magick, ignore your need for rest and solitude, push through when you need to retreat
