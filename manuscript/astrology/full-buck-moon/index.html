The Buck Moon marks a time of coming into personal power at the height of summer. Its name comes from the deer whose antlers regrow during this season, a symbol of renewal, strength, and the crowning of spiritual and physical insight. Though the summer solstice has passed, the blazing energy of the longest days still lingers, and the focus begins to shift from outward celebration toward deeper spiritual matters.

This lunation is also widely known as the Wort Moon or Herb Moon, a nod to the herbal allies that reach full maturity now and stand ready for harvest. Gathering, drying, and preparing herbs for healing and magickal use is one of the most natural ways to work with this cycle. Hay is cut in the fields, sustaining the animals that sustain us, and summer storms roll through with revitalizing rain and electrifying lightning, mirroring the spark of personal power and insight that characterizes this time. The names Thunder Moon, Hay Moon, and Horn Moon all reflect these seasonal rhythms, while Blessing Moon invites you to consecrate your endeavors under the full summer light.

Magickal Workings

Strength, vitality, and personal power are the threads running through this lunation. Reflect on your goals and prepare for the harvest season ahead by clarifying what you want to reap. Work closely with your herbal allies: harvest and dry plants for healing, craft blends and teas for dreamwork, astral travel, or prophetic vision. In the spirit of the Blessing Moon, use this cycle to formally bless projects, tools, relationships, or any endeavor that matters to you. Seek insight that helps you leap toward the achievements that make your spirit sing. The Buck Moon reminds you that you are crowned with power; use it wisely and deliberately.
