Full Moon For Completions, Healing, and Empowerment

Triple Goddess Association

The Full Moon is the Mother Moon, fully illuminated and at the peak of her power. In the Triple Goddess framework, the Mother represents completion, nurturing, and abundance. Her influence is felt three days before and three days after the exact Full Moon, making this the widest window of any phase. This is the time for magicks of completion, success, gains, and accomplishment.

Moon Phase Magick

The full moon represents the alignment of moon, earth, and sun (sometimes resulting in an eclipse). This is an all-purpose time. Use the extra boost for metaphysical heavy lifting, court cases, and protection.

Energy of This Phase

The Full Moon is the peak of the lunar cycle, when lunar energy reaches maximum power. The Moon sits directly opposite the Sun, fully illuminated. This phase supports culminating, presenting, and clarifying your intentions. What you began at the New Moon now comes to harvest.

Work with this energy for:

  • Bringing projects to completion and celebration
  • Presenting your work to others
  • Gaining clarity on confusing situations
  • Manifesting at peak power
  • Charging crystals and tools
  • Drawing down lunar energy
  • Divination and psychic work
  • Releasing what no longer serves
  • Recognizing achievements and progress
  • Illuminating hidden truths

The Astrological Opposition

At the time of the full moon, the moon sits directly opposite the sun with earth positioned between them. This alignment is known as an opposition aspect. While the opposition can create challenges and heightened emotions (as full moons are famously known for), the energy can also become harmonious through awareness and balance within the polarity.

Celebration and Harvest

Like farmers harvesting wheat beneath the full moon, this is the time to reap the rewards of efforts sowed during the waxing phase. You may receive guidance, signs, and insights that the fruits of your labor are on the way. Even if things have not yet fully culminated, abundance can still arrive in the future. The moon in her radiance inspires a sense of power that many practitioners channel to supercharge their magick.

Working with Full Moon Energy

Psychic powers, creativity, and emotions may feel intensely heightened now. Because the moon is visible throughout the night, you might work or bathe in her light, or simply gaze at her beauty in wonder. Charge any special items such as favorite stones or magickal tools under the moonlight to infuse them with lunar energy.

Release, Healing, and Self-Care

As the full moon straddles the boundary between waxing and waning phases, some practitioners feel called to release, forgive, and focus on self-care. With waxing-phase results at hand, release expectations and preemptively celebrate, offering gratitude to Mother Moon. Just as the moon fully reflects the sun, insight on your waking life may be illuminated now. Whatever patterns or wounds are exposed can begin to be healed. Strengthen your intuition, tune into yourself, and depending on how this lunar cycle has impacted you, decide whether it is time for self-care or manifestation magick.

Adapting Your Magick to Any Phase

💡
When you need to work magick that cannot wait, adapt your intention to suit the themes of the current phase. For focus spells in the waxing phase, cast to increase focus, peace, and willpower. In the waning phase, cast to decrease distractions, reduce the number of tasks, or quiet the wandering mind. Just as the moon shows many sides, your intentions can have many facets to support your aims.
