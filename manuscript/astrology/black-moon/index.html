A black moon is the name given to an extra new moon that appears within a given time frame. Just as a blue moon marks a repeated full moon, the black moon marks a repeated new moon. Because the lunar cycle is slightly shorter than most calendar months, this doubling happens occasionally and carries a sense of rare, concentrated new moon energy.

Types of Black Moons

The phrase "black moon" can refer to three different occurrences:

  • Astronomical: Each season typically contains three new moons. When a season holds four, the third new moon is called the astronomical black moon. This happens roughly once every two to three years and can extend the window for working with the combined seasonal and lunar themes.
  • Calendrical: The second new moon falling within a single calendar month. Like its blue moon counterpart, this definition follows the modern calendar and occurs about every two to three years.
  • Astrological: The second new moon occurring within a single zodiacal season. If the timing overlaps so that two new moons fall during the same zodiacal window, the second one carries the black moon label. This type is not formally standardized but follows the same principle of repeated lunar energy.

Magickal Significance

Some practitioners treat a black moon as no different from any other new moon, since the underlying cycle does not change. Others view the extra occurrence as an amplified portal for intention-setting and inner work.

When approached as a time of repeated energy, a black moon can be used to revisit and strengthen intentions set during a previous new moon. If a manifestation did not take hold, the black moon offers an energetic redo, a second chance to plant that seed.

Each type of black moon lends itself to a slightly different angle:

  • A calendrical black moon weaves the lunar cycle into our constructed calendar, making it well suited for self-made success and manufactured momentum.
  • An astrological black moon (a second new moon in the same sign) invites you to revisit and deepen that sign's themes from a fresh starting point.
  • An astronomical black moon adds an extra lunation to a season, giving you more time to work with the combined seasonal and new moon energy.

For new moon manifesters, the black moon can be an especially powerful opportunity for long-range planning. Since a new moon is already a time to align with fresh energy, the black moon can help you commit to powerful and enduring dreams.

Many practitioners also associate the black moon with truth: realizing personal truth, confronting hidden themes, or gaining insight into mysteries. If you feel drawn to that quality, use the black moon to journey inward.

Working with a Black Moon

Approaches that suit a black moon include:

  • Deep intention-setting and long-term manifestation
  • Serious binding, banishing, and heavy protective work
  • Shadow work and confronting hidden patterns
  • Divination for truth and clarity
  • Spirit communication and accessing hidden knowledge

Sidhe Moon (Otherworld Moon)

In the Wiccan Esbat tradition, a second Dark Moon occurring within a single month is also called the Sidhe Moon or Otherworld Moon. This rare occurrence amplifies the Dark Moon's natural energies with heightened Intuitive Power, Psychic Energy, and Occult Wisdom. The veil between worlds grows especially thin during a Sidhe Moon, making it ideal for divination, spirit communication, shadow work, and accessing hidden knowledge. The Fae and Otherworldly beings are said to be more accessible during this time.
