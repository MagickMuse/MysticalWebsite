A supermoon occurs when a full moon coincides with the Moon's perigee, the closest point in its elliptical orbit around Earth. Because the Moon's path is oval-shaped and Earth is not perfectly centered within it, its distance from us shifts throughout each cycle. The nearest point is called the perigee, and the farthest is the apogee.

When a full moon lines up at or near perigee, it is called a supermoon or perigee moon. This happens roughly three to four times per year.

Appearance and Physical Effects

At perigee, the Moon can appear up to fourteen percent larger than it does at apogee, though the size difference can be hard to notice with the naked eye. What stands out more is brightness: a supermoon shines roughly thirty percent brighter than a micromoon (a full moon at apogee). The closer proximity also strengthens the Moon's gravitational pull, which can produce higher-than-usual tides.

When the perigee aligns with a new moon instead of a full moon, some astrologers refer to it as a super new moon. In that case, new moon themes like fresh starts, intention-setting, and quiet inner work may feel more pronounced.

Magickal Significance

Opinions vary on how much weight to give a supermoon in practice. Some astrologers treat it as just another full moon, while others consider it a meaningfully amplified event. With the Moon at its closest and brightest, many practitioners describe the energy as electric, more emotional and more potent than a typical full moon. Manifestation work, lunar devotion, and any magick tied to full moon energy may feel especially empowered.

The gravitational and luminous intensity can heighten emotional sensitivity and sharpen psychic impressions. If you notice a stronger pull during a supermoon, consider tracking perigee timing during other phases as well. You may find that perigee adds a subtle charge to whatever phase it overlaps with, offering insight into how distance shapes your felt connection to the Moon.

Working with a Supermoon

Approaches that suit a supermoon include:

  • Full moon rituals with an extra layer of intention
  • Manifestation and empowerment workings
  • Charging crystals and tools under the amplified light
  • Divination and psychic work, which may feel especially vivid
  • Journaling or reflection on emotional patterns

Trust your own senses. Tune in and let the experience tell you how the supermoon lands for you.
