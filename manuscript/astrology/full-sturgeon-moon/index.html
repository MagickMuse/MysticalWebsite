The Sturgeon Moon opens the harvest season. Its primary name honors the large freshwater fish that were once abundant in the Great Lakes at this time of year, but its many alternate names, Barley Moon, Corn Moon, and Grain Moon, point to the real theme of this lunation: abundance. The full moon's light is especially valued now, extending the hours available to gather the earth's bounty before the waning of summer.

This cycle begins alongside Lammas, the first of three harvest celebrations. It is a time of gathering, sharing, and celebrating with chosen family, and of giving thanks to land spirits, ancestors, and spirit allies who helped the harvest come to fruition. Yet abundance is not only material. Each kernel of grain invites reflection on the sustenance of mind, spirit, and emotion alongside the body. When you harvest today's provisions, you are also collecting the seeds of tomorrow. The principle of reaping what you sow runs through every aspect of this lunation, encouraging conscious awareness of how your efforts, magickal or otherwise, shape what is still to come.

Magickal Workings

Since this is only the first harvest, there is still time to use the seasonal momentum to manifest results. Cast spells for plenty and prosperity, and name what you wish to bring in with the remaining harvest lunations. Give thanks for what has already arrived. Reflect on patterns that need cleansing before the darker months ahead. Kitchen witchery is especially potent now: bake bread and share it as an act of offering, reconciliation, gratitude, and abundance. Work with grains, corn dolls, seasonal foods, and even beer offerings or beer baths. This is the moon that reminds you abundance is both a gift and a responsibility, so tend it with intention.
