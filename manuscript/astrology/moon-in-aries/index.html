A cardinal fire transit governed by Mars, the Moon in Aries directs emotional energy toward action, initiative, and courageous new starts. This placement channels the ram's drive into the lunar body, heightening impulse, passion, and the desire to lead.

Natal Moon in Aries

The Moon represents your emotional body, how you feel your experiences, where you find comfort, and what you know instinctually. With the Moon in Aries natally, emotions arrive fast, burn hot, and move quickly.

Emotional Nature: Initiating, independent, impassioned, bold

Self-Care: When experiencing emotional extremes (anxiety, stress, depression, overwhelm, exhaustion), align with your Aries Moon energy to restore balance. Physical exercise, competitive games, starting new projects, acting on new ideas, and initiating plans all help return you to equilibrium.

Transiting Moon in Aries

When the Moon moves through Aries (approximately every 2.5 days), lunar energy becomes fiery, enthusiastic, and impulsive. The ram's courage and swift decisiveness rise to the surface. As the first sign of the zodiac, this transit favors new beginnings of any kind, offering a surge of zest for fast, powerful starts.

Use this window to take first steps on stalled projects, push past hesitation, or simply get things moving. Mars's influence makes this a favorable time for matters of health, physicality, and the body.

New Moon in Aries (Aries season, roughly March 21 to April 19): Favors new ventures, restarts, and bold planning. Ideal for developing a plan of attack for your magickal goals.

Full Moon in Aries (Libra season, roughly September 23 to October 22): A powerful time for passion and empowerment. The tension between headstrong Aries and reflective Libra invites growth through balancing spontaneity with consideration.

Aries Moon Magick

Cast spells for victory, success, and overcoming obstacles. Work magick that boosts vitality, vigor, and physical energy, or incorporate movement into your spellwork. Motivation, inspiration, courage, and passion all thrive under this transit.

Do: Physical exercise, competitive games, start new projects, initiate plans, courage and confidence workings

Don't: Start arguments or lose your temper, act hastily, drive too fast
