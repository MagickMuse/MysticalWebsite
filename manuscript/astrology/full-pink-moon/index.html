The Pink Moon is named for the wild ground phlox whose eager pink blossoms are among the first wildflowers to push through the spring soil. By this lunation, the seasonal shift is complete: spring is fully awake, and all of nature is alive with it. The names Egg Moon and Hare Moon underscore the fertility of this time, promising multipliable abundance for whatever you sow. Planter's Moon and Seed Moon speak to the practical work of putting things in the ground, while Waking Moon, Meadow Moon, and Fish Moon reflect a world that has shaken off winter entirely.

The energy of the Pink Moon is bright and excitable. It bridges spirituality and physical growth, making it equally potent for inner attunement and outward action. Health, happiness, love, youth, and abundance all thrive under this lunation, and new beginnings of every kind are favored.

Magickal Workings

Cast spells for excitement, fertility, health, renewal, and fresh starts. This is a natural cycle for love and beauty magick: work on glamour, attraction, vitality, and anything that makes you feel radiant. If April showers are part of your landscape, collect rainwater for water blessings, washes, and beauty baths charged under the moon. Moon water crafted during this lunation carries especially vibrant spring energy. Celebrate the growth happening all around you by incorporating seasonal abundance into your meals and magickal workings, whether that means edible violets, spring flowers, eggs, or whatever the land near you is offering. The Pink Moon invites you to bloom alongside everything else.
