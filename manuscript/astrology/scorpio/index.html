True to Your Sign

Scorpio holds the eighth house of the zodiac and falls between October 23 and November 21. As a water sign ruled by Mars and Pluto and symbolized by the scorpion, this sign carries a passionate, focused, sensual, and intense current paired with deep sensitivity and intuition. The shadow side leans toward secrecy and destruction.

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. The Eighth Sign

Scorpio falls midway through autumn, a season of decay that is also a season of fermentation, with Samhain, or Halloween, drawing near. The sign rules this festival dedicated to the dead, and the ancient Egyptians held that their god Osiris was carried into the Otherworld while Scorpio stood in the heavens. Yet because the sign governs the reproductive organs, the promise of rebirth is never far behind, and a child conceived under the reign of the Scorpion is generally born under Leo.

The Scorpion

Of all the figures laid over their constellations, the stars gathered for Scorpio truly do resemble a scorpion, so it is no surprise that the ancient astrologers agreed completely on what they saw. Antares, the red star set within the shape, was known as the Heart of the Scorpion and was dedicated to the god Mars on account of its fiery color.

The Glyph

The modern shorthand for Scorpio looks quite unlike the versions found in medieval manuscripts, which resembled an actual scorpion far more closely. The Egyptians used an upright serpent for the sign, since both creatures are poisonous. The modern sigil, an M whose final stroke lifts into a curved, arrow-headed tail, may be read as the severed tail of a scorpion or as the male sexual organs, while the arrow at its end also recalls the tail of a dragon.

The Water of the Scorpion

Astrologers often say that Scorpio people may meet more hardship in life than other signs, the assumption being that they are karmically prepared for it and more than able to cope. The sting in the scorpion's tail speaks of quick wit and sharp intelligence, and of a sharp tongue as well. A deep and passionate nature rounds out the picture, along with a magnetic, shrewd, creative, and intense personality, with a strong sex drive counted among the sign's traits. Much of this intensity flows from Pluto, though before that planet was discovered the Scorpion answered to Mars.
