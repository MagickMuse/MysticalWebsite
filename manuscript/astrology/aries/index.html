True to Your Sign?

Aries (March 21 to April 19)

The first sign of the zodiac, Aries is a fire sign ruled by Mars. Its symbol is the ram. Those born under Aries tend to be dynamic, energetic, and driven toward action. Assertive captures the core of the Aries temperament. They are often enterprising, impulsive, warm-hearted, and confident free spirits who say what they mean and mean what they say. The shadow side shows in impatience, rashness, tactlessness, excitability, and a tendency toward bossiness.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Aries color: Red

Red stones align with Aries' fiery, passionate, pioneering energy. Choose from Ruby for leadership and vitality, or Red Jasper for grounding that fire energy into action.

The First Sign

Though the zodiac turns as an endless wheel with no true head or tail, Aries is usually counted first in the procession of signs. The reason lies in its tie to the vernal equinox, the moment when the year stirs back to life and everything begins to grow again. In this sense Aries stands for the seed of life itself, brimming with potential and possibility.

The Ram

Different peoples gave the ram their own names. To the Greeks it was Krios; in India it was Mesha, the ram, or Aja, the goat; the Persians knew it as Varak, a lamb. The Babylonians called it either Zappu, meaning hair, or Hunga, the hired worker.

The Glyph

Each sign carries a piece of shorthand, the kind of mark astrologers set down when drawing up a chart, and these little squiggles invite more than one reading. The glyph of Aries clearly echoes a ram's curving horns, and horns on their own have long stood for lust and sexuality. The same shape can be seen as a fountain of energy shooting upward, or as a flame. Because Aries rules the head and face, the glyph is sometimes laid over the face of a human figure to show the sign's influence; and because it governs the head and mind, those upward horns are also read as reaching toward the spiritual realm. It is striking how a single simple sign can gather so many of a sign's qualities into itself.

The Fire of the Ram

The wheel of star creatures has shifted over time, and the place now held by Aries once belonged to Taurus, leaving a faint trace of the bull-in-a-china-shop about it. The spring equinox is a season of furious growth, when everything strains to break through the soil after the bare winter, and the untamed vigor of ram and bull alike suits the moment. Ruled by Mars, the planet of the war god, Aries brims with energy and drive, determination and stubbornness, and a strong streak of impulsiveness; it can turn quick-tempered and fiercely ambitious. Its fire is the fire of creation, flaring out in every direction, an outburst of flame that may build or destroy depending on how it is steered. The blunt force of the ram captures the sign perfectly: as the first of the zodiac it has a childlike directness and an honest, head-on approach.
