A lunar eclipse occurs when Earth moves between the Sun and the full Moon, casting its shadow across the lunar surface. From the night side of Earth, the Moon may darken or shift in color, sometimes taking on a deep copper or wine-red tone. Lunar eclipses happen only during full moons, but not at every full moon, because the Moon's orbit is slightly tilted relative to Earth's. On average, they occur about three times per year. Visible only from the night side of the Earth, the shadow often makes the Moon appear a striking, foreboding red.

Earth casts two layers of shadow during a lunar eclipse:

  • Penumbra: The lighter, outer shadow
  • Umbra: The darker, central shadow

Types of Lunar Eclipses

  • Penumbral: The Moon passes through only the penumbra, producing a subtle dimming or faint shift in tone. This type is easy to miss with the naked eye.
  • Partial: Part of the Moon enters the umbra, and the shaded portion can appear red, orange, brown, or yellow.
  • Total: The entire Moon moves through the umbra. Because it also crosses the penumbra on the way in and out, a total lunar eclipse is the longest form and can last as long as two and a half hours. The deep red coloring during totality is the source of the term Blood Moon.

Magickal Significance

If a solar eclipse is a supercharged new moon, a lunar eclipse is a supercharged full moon. Emotions can feel amplified, and situations may come to a head quickly. Old wounds, repeating patterns, and unspoken truths tend to rise to the surface, creating vulnerability alongside a strong opportunity for healing and transformation. The heightened emotional charge can also stir conflicts and disproportionate outbursts, so stay aware of that pull.

This is a potent time to check in with your path: what is reaching completion, what needs to be released, and what kind of change is ready to begin. Because the full moon is already a time of culmination, a lunar eclipse can accelerate endings and reveal what has been hidden.

Working with a Lunar Eclipse

Some practitioners avoid outcome-driven spellwork during lunar eclipses because the energy can feel erratic and hard to direct. Others use the amplified potency for major manifestation or for work that demands a decisive turning point.

Approaches that tend to work well include:

  • Divination and card readings for deep insight
  • Reflective ritual and journaling
  • Energetic cleansing and release work
  • Healing baths and restorative practices
  • Spirit communication and meditation

Psychic sensitivity is often heightened during a lunar eclipse, so divination and spirit work can feel especially vivid. Stay grounded, and let the eclipse reveal what it needs to.

A Note on Eclipse Magick

Eclipse energy can be unpredictable. If the atmosphere feels chaotic, lean into release and reflection rather than trying to steer outcomes. If the themes feel clear and you feel steady, you may choose to engage the power directly. In all cases, let your intuition guide the choice, and keep your work centered on alignment and healing.
