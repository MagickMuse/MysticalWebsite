Waning Moon For Releasings, Cleansings, and Banishings

Triple Goddess Association

The Waning Crescent is the Crone Moon at her last sliver, the final visible crescent before the Dark. In some traditions this phase is called the "New Moon" and is specifically associated with magicks of banishing, exorcism, and cleansings. The Crone here stands at the threshold between the visible and the invisible, wielding the power to strip away what must go before the cycle renews.

Moon Phase Magick

The waning moon is a period of shrinking light, moving steadily toward the darkness of the new moon. This is the time to practice magick of decrease, banishment, and bringing things to a close. As the full moon illuminated the surface, the waning phase serves as a time to digest that information. While the waxing moon demanded active effort, this phase invites reflection, release, and the integration of lessons learned during the lunar cycle. It is a period for rest and relaxation following a time of active growth.

Like energetic gardeners, this is the time to prune, cutting back what did not work or is no longer needed. Alternatively, efforts can be turned inward. Focus on spiritual matters and tend to your own roots, much like the subterranean plants favored by the waning phase.

This is the ideal time to break bonds, let go, cleanse, and release. By doing so, you make room in your energy and your life for the fresh blessings and magick of the upcoming lunar cycle. Workings to extinguish bad habits, shrink debt or insecurity, and remove unwanted influences are highly favorable now. It is time to end whatever you are ready to forgive, move on from, or transform.

Energy of This Phase

The Waning Crescent Moon, sometimes referred to as the balsamic moon, is the final official phase before the lunar cycle begins anew. Having slipped past half illumination, the moon now resides more in darkness than in light, appearing as a thin, vanishing crescent. This period heralds a return to the world of mystery and shadow.

The term "balsamic" derives from balsam, a restorative substance and sacred resin burned as incense during magickal ceremonies. In alignment with this namesake, the phase is deeply restorative, packed with powerful healing that allows practitioners to reconnect with spiritual matters. It is a time for soothing, repair, and making peace.

As the cycle concludes, themes of endings and death are prominent, yet they are accompanied by a profound sense of peace. The veil between worlds feels thinner during this mystical time. It is highly advantageous for developing mediumship skills, honing psychic senses, and connecting with spirits, ghosts, and ancestors. Practitioners can use this time to analyze their egos and examine repeating patterns.

This phase supports releasing, surrendering, clearing out, and purifying to make space for the next cycle. Focus on rest, especially if your energy is low, and clean your physical and spiritual house to prepare for the alignment of the approaching new moon.

Work with this energy for:

  • Releasing attachments, habits, and repeating patterns
  • Surrendering control and trusting the inevitable cycles of life
  • Clearing out physical and energetic clutter
  • Purification, banishing, and cord-cutting rituals
  • Forgiveness, making peace, and letting go
  • Deep healing, rest, and restoration before the new cycle
  • Shadow work, analyzing the ego, and facing endings
  • Connecting with ancestors, spirits, and the psychic realm
  • Honing mediumship skills and related psychic senses
