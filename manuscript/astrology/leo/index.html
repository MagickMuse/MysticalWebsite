True to Your Sign?

Leo (July 23 to August 22)

The fifth sign of the zodiac, Leo is a fire sign ruled by the Sun. Its symbol is the lion. Like the Sun, Leos are magnetic and bright, drawing the attention of everything they touch. Charismatic captures the core of the Leo temperament. Their lives are typically full of activity, and they are often courageous, natural leaders. The shadow side shows in arrogance and attention seeking.

Attributes

Power, optimism, vitality, attention oriented.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Leo color: Yellow

Yellow stones align with Leo's sunny, radiant, confident energy. Choose from Topaz for success and manifestation, or Zircon for grounding that solar power into practical achievement.

The Fifth Sign

Leo rises at the height of summer, and it holds a place unlike any other in the zodiac, for it is the only sign ruled by the Sun, a star, while every other sign answers to a planet. One of its oldest names, recorded by the Babylonians, meant the Great Light, a fitting nod to its solar ruler.

The Lion

The constellation has always been pictured as the lion. The Babylonians cast the beast as female and called her Urgula, the lioness, while the Romans gave the sign's brightest star the name Regulus, meaning Heart of the Lion, a word that also carries the senses of little king and paw of the lion.

The Glyph

The sigil of Leo may be read as the tail of the lion. The circle can also be seen as the heart, which the lion rules, with the scooped curve standing for the excitement and joy a Leo feels in the presence of something beautiful or inspiring, while the downward slope hints at the unfinished projects that so often pile up in a Leo's life. The mark may equally suggest the lion's mane.

The Fire of the Lion

Like the Sun it rises with, Leo is passionate and extroverted, marked by generosity, nobility, and a commanding gift for leadership. Its fire makes it theatrical, flamboyant, creative, and hospitable, with a regal streak that can tip into condescension; yet this is the controlled heat of the Sun rather than the untamed flame of Aries. The lion stands for power, royalty, and pride, the emblem of emperors, a force that may be turned to good or ill, and its mane recalls the rays of the Sun, so that a mane of hair is often counted a Leo trait. The Egyptian Dendera zodiac shows a scene much like the Tarot card called Strength, a woman parting the jaws of a lion with apparent ease. Leo rules the spine and the heart, and the winged lion of St. Mark, carved in stone, strides in majesty around St. Mark's Square in Venice.
