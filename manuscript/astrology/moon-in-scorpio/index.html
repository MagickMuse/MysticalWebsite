A fixed water transit governed by Pluto and Mars, the Moon in Scorpio channels emotional energy toward depth, transformation, and truth. This placement intensifies the lunar body, heightening psychic sensitivity, desire for intimacy, and the drive to uncover what lies beneath the surface.

Natal Moon in Scorpio

The Moon represents your emotional body, how you feel your experiences, where you find comfort, and what you know instinctually. With the Moon in Scorpio natally, emotional nature is intense, transformative, and deeply private.

Emotional Nature: Profound, perceptive, psychic, fiercely loyal

Self-Care: You feel everything at profound depths. Superficial connections leave you empty, you crave soul-deep intimacy and emotional truth. You're naturally psychic and can sense hidden motives and unspoken feelings in others. You guard your emotions carefully, revealing vulnerability only to those who've earned absolute trust. Betrayal wounds you deeply. Transformation is your natural state.

  • Honor your need for privacy and emotional boundaries
  • Engage in regular transformation and shadow work
  • Develop trusted intimate connections
  • Practice healthy release of resentment and grudges
  • Work with water magick and deep emotional healing
  • Allow yourself to feel intensely without judgment
  • Spend time near deep water for emotional processing
  • Remember that not everyone can handle your depths

Transiting Moon in Scorpio

With a stinger that pierces right to the heart, Scorpio gets to the truth of things. The Moon in Scorpio (approximately every 2.5 days) brings a time of revealing truths in a sometimes painful way that often instigates transformation and personal growth. This transit prompts investigation of the ego, and deep inner shadow work comes with the territory.

Connected to the realm of the occult, Scorpio carries an energetic favorability toward psychic abilities, the dead, mediumship, and divination. With the Moon in Scorpio, this energy is magnified, making it an effective time to investigate the realm of mystery, explore occult themes, and strengthen psychic abilities. Scorpio is also known for being sensual, making this a time to embrace passion, intimacy, and the rawness of life.

New Moon in Scorpio (Scorpio season, roughly October 23 to November 21): All Scorpio Moon-favored endeavors move forward with the blessings of both Moon and Sun.

Full Moon in Scorpio (Taurus season, roughly April 20 to May 20): A sharp contrast between immovability and comfort on one hand and the revelation of hidden truths and necessary change on the other. Also a favorable lunation for financial focus, as Taurus provides the structure of wealth while Scorpio is particularly auspicious for business partnerships.

Scorpio Moon Magick

Spells for uncovering truths and getting to the heart of things do especially well now. Repeating patterns and karmic issues may surface, but this recognition allows them to be powerfully changed, transformed, and healed. The Scorpio Moon also favors banishing and ending, important parts of transformation. Its sharp stinger is useful for cleansing and protection spells. For the psychically attuned, cast spells for divination and meditate to connect with the realm beyond the veil.

Do: Shadow work and deep emotional healing, psychic development and occult study, transformation and death/rebirth magick, sensuality and intimacy, banishing and binding (with care), ancestral communication and mediumship, power-building, divination, truth-uncovering spells, cleansing and protection, financial partnership magick

Don't: Superficial social magick, work requiring emotional lightness, avoid processing intense emotions that arise, trust too easily or reveal secrets carelessly, force others deeper than they are ready for, manipulation magick (karmic consequences intensified)
