Waning Moon For Releasings, Cleansings, and Banishings

Triple Goddess Association

The Waning Moon is the Crone Moon, moving from the Full phase toward the Dark. In the Triple Goddess framework, the Crone represents wisdom, release, and the power of decrease. All waning phases carry the energy of diminishment, used for magicks that reduce, remove, and dissolve.

Moon Phase Magick

The waning moon is a period of shrinking light, moving steadily toward the darkness of the new moon. This is the time to practice magick of decrease, banishment, and bringing things to a close. As the full moon illuminated the surface, the waning phase serves as a time to digest that information. While the waxing moon demanded active effort, this phase invites reflection, release, and the integration of lessons learned during the lunar cycle. It is a period for rest and relaxation following a time of active growth.

Like energetic gardeners, this is the time to prune, cutting back what did not work or is no longer needed. Alternatively, efforts can be turned inward. Focus on spiritual matters and tend to your own roots, much like the subterranean plants favored by the waning phase.

This is the ideal time to break bonds, let go, cleanse, and release. By doing so, you make room in your energy and your life for the fresh blessings and magick of the upcoming lunar cycle. Workings to extinguish bad habits, shrink debt or insecurity, and remove unwanted influences are highly favorable now. It is time to end whatever you are ready to forgive, move on from, or transform.

Energy of This Phase

Following the radiance of the full moon, the waning gibbous phase marks the beginning of decreasing light until it reaches the halfway point of the last quarter. While the moon remains in the brighter portion of its cycle and the culmination energy of the full moon lingers, the visible decline inspires reflection.

This is a time to process experiences and psychic insights brought forward by the full moon. As you reflect, release, and harvest the longer-term results of your lunar labor, this phase invites you to give thanks for harvests yet to come. When harvesting, seeds for the future are also collected. The knowledge gleaned now sets the tone for the next lunar cycle. Consider what you have learned, what you should carry forward, what is blocking you, and what you must begin to let go of.

Because you are still in the brighter half of the cycle, you may feel empowered to cast additional lunar magick. Also known as the disseminating moon, this is an ideal time to spread blessings and knowledge widely. Echoing the partnership themes of the waxing gibbous phase, focus your magick on sharing and helping others in the spirit of gratitude.

As the moon pulls back from her peak power, you may feel called to do the same. Work in tandem with this energy: relax and regroup after the excitement of the full moon and the push of the waxing phase. Cleanse your space and begin the process of releasing.

Work with this energy for:

  • Expressing gratitude for lessons, blessings, and future harvests
  • Processing and integrating psychic insights and experiences
  • Spreading knowledge and sharing abundance with others
  • Reflecting on what to carry forward and what to release
  • Cleansing and resting after intense manifestation work
  • Beginning to let go of blockages and attachments
