Overarching Themes

Alignment, Intention Setting, Insight, Manifestation, New Beginnings

Moon Phase Magick

The lunar cycle begins with the new moon. At this time, the moon is absent from the night sky and the stars shine ever brighter in her absence. The precise moment it occurs is when the moon reaches a 0-degree alignment with the sun, positioned between the sun and the Earth. This means the moon rises and sets with the sun. Because her absence often lasts a day before and after the exact alignment, and because the new moon is such a powerful energetic marker, many practitioners find the energy of this phase accessible for a total of three days.

This moment of cosmic alignment brings together the two most significant celestial bodies in our sky: the sun (consciousness, identity, ego) and the moon (subconscious, emotion, the soul). It is a time to connect to both the celestial energies above and the energies within, and to reflect on what your soul is calling for.

Energy of This Phase

Straddling the waning and waxing phases, the new moon is an occasion of celestial alignment that witches use in deeply personal ways. Some use it for rest; others for intention setting; many for both.

Rest and Reflection:

To mirror the absence of the moon from the sky, you might use this time to take an energetic break and restore the vitality you will need to take action with the waxing moon. This is an ideal time to take a cleansing salt bath, purify your energy, meditate, or read tarot cards for insight. With the dark sky above, occult themes may feel more accessible, making this a favorable window for beginning new magickal studies, strengthening your psychic abilities, investigating past lives, and exploring your inner truth.

Manifestation and Intention:

The lunar cycle is beginning anew, and boundless cosmic energy propels the moon to grow in light once more. You might call forth a new desire or goal that can build alongside the waxing phase, set intentions for the entire lunation, or cast spells for rebirth and new beginnings. The meeting of sun and moon also makes this an excellent time for alignment magick, such as balancing the solar and lunar energies within yourself or aligning to a new life vision. The astrological sign of the new moon can offer particular potential around certain themes.

Work with this energy for:

  • Setting clear intentions for the month ahead
  • Brainstorming new projects or creative endeavors
  • Visualization and vision boarding
  • Journaling about goals and dreams
  • Planning your next steps
  • Meditation on what you want to call into your life
  • Shadow work and introspection
  • Cleansing salt baths and energy purification
  • Tarot and divination for insight
  • Psychic development and past life exploration
  • Alignment and rebirth spells

Dark Moon vs. New Moon

In the modern era, the day marked on calendars for the new moon refers to the moment of the moon's 0-degree alignment with the sun. However, the term "dark moon" carries a different meaning. While the two are sometimes used interchangeably, the dark moon more specifically refers to a distinct period at the very end of the waning phase, carrying its own unique energy separate from the new moon.

Void-of-Course Moon

As the Moon progresses through the zodiac, it forms angles (called aspects) to other planets. The last aspect the Moon makes before leaving a sign marks the beginning of a void-of-course period, which lasts until the Moon enters the next sign. This can be anywhere from a few minutes to several hours.

Think of the Moon like a person finishing their workday. When the last task is complete, it's time to rest. During a void-of-course Moon, the lunar energy shifts toward restoration, rest, and non-productivity.

During void-of-course periods:

DO:

  • Hang out with friends
  • Go for walks or enjoy nature
  • Daydream and let your mind wander
  • Meditate or practice gentle yoga
  • Relax and sleep
  • Engage in low-stakes creative play

DON'T:

  • Push for productivity or start major projects
  • Have important conversations or negotiations
  • Start new ventures or initiatives
  • Sign contracts or make purchases
  • Launch businesses or products
  • Make critical decisions

If you try to do productive work during a void-of-course Moon, you'll often feel resistance or find that things don't stick. Modern life doesn't always allow us to pause, but simply being aware of these periods helps you set appropriate expectations.
