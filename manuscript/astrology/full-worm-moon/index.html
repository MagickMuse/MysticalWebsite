🌕Full Worm Moon

The Worm Moon takes its name from the trails left by earthworms and larvae as the frozen ground begins to thaw, a small but unmistakable sign that life is returning to the surface. Other names capture the season's many moods: Storm Moon and Windy Moon speak to the last bursts of winter weather, Death Moon marks the final dying breath of the cold season, and Sugar Moon, Sap Moon, and Seed Moon point to the sweetness and fertility already stirring beneath the soil.

This lunation sits right on the threshold. Depending on when it arrives, it may be the last full moon of winter or the first of spring. Either way, the energy is one of emergence: thawing ground, sprouting bulbs, and the slow but unstoppable return of life. Rejuvenation, fertility, regrowth, and the first whispers of coming prosperity ride the spring breezes. It is a time to reconnect with imagination and prepare for a fresh, boundless start as the season turns.

Magickal Workings

Refresh your mindset and reconnect to your inner child. Whether you are seeking vitality, a limitless perspective, or a renewed sense of joy and possibility, this lunation supports it. Creativity runs high, but give it direction so it can build toward lasting success. Water cleansings are especially potent now: collect dew after the full moon and add it to magickal baths or use it to craft enchanting lunar mists. Health-focused workings are favored as the energy of rebirth saturates the air. In the spirit of the Seed Moon, plant the seeds of your magickal goals with intention, preparing the ground for the boundless growth that spring will bring.
