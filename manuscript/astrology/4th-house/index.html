The 4th House represents home, parents, and domestic matters. It governs your roots, family foundation, private life, and emotional security.

Attributes

Home, family, childhood, property.

Practical Use

Inscribe the 4th House symbol on spell items to influence domestic harmony, family relationships, property matters, or healing childhood wounds.
