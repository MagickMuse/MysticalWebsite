Chinese Astrology Associations

Element: Metal
Color: White

In Chinese astrology, the Monkey carries associations with the Metal element and the color white. These correspondences can be incorporated into your home décor and personal environment alongside your personal element color determined by your birth year.

When your personal element conflicts with your zodiac animal's color through the Destructive Cycle, prioritize your personal element. Your birth year element takes precedence over your zodiac animal's color in cases of elemental conflict.

Japanese Kigaku Colors

Kigaku, meaning "science of the spirit," is the Japanese system of astrology. It uses the same twelve animal symbols as Chinese astrology but provides additional color refinements by combining your birth year element with your zodiac animal. These colors work particularly well in frequently used rooms and when worn during occasions requiring confidence and control.
