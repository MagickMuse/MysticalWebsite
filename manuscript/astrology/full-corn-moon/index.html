🌕Full Corn Moon

🍂September

The Corn Moon arrives at the heart of harvest season, when the earth's abundance is being gathered in earnest. Nuts, barley, corn, and grapes all reach their peak now, lending their names to this lunation. The nickname Wine Moon reflects the grape harvest and the ancient practice of pressing wine at this time, while Nut Moon and Barley Moon speak to the staple crops filling storerooms for the months ahead. If this full moon happens to fall closest to the autumn equinox, it also earns the title Harvest Moon.

The gratitude that defined the previous lunation carries forward, but a new quality enters the energy as summer gives way to autumn. Attention turns toward the home, toward protection and warmth, and toward creating a space that will sustain you through the darker months. The last rays of summer mingle with the first hints of introspection, and the balance between outward effort and inward reflection begins to shift. Wisdom and truth feel more accessible at this threshold, bridging light and dark.

Magickal Workings

Call in any remaining manifestation efforts while the harvest energy still supports growth. Cast spells for confidence, prosperity, and abundance using the ingredients this moon is named after: nuts, barley, cornmeal, corn bread, corn husks, or grapes. Grapes carry strong lunar associations and connect to abundance, prosperity, garden magick, and trance work. Wine, long used to alter consciousness and commune with deities, makes an especially fitting offering of gratitude during this cycle. Begin preparing your home magickally for the colder season with protective wards and cozy blessings. Turn your focus inward toward spiritual development and inner awareness, reflecting on what you have harvested and what seeds you are still planting for the future.
