The Planetary Seal of Mars

Seal Symbolism

The Mars seal is an engine of martial power. The center functions as a wheel of an immense war machine or a reactor generating current. The force generated by the circle explodes outward in a sharp arrow-spike, as in the traditional sigil. This seal also features a looping lasso that draws whatever it snares inward toward the arrowhead, combining offensive and capturing forces.

Powers of Mars

Mars governs war, conflict, defense, aggression, strength, competition, lust, courage, and confidence. Beyond raw martial force, Mars also possesses the power to establish calm, the warrior's calm that soldiers and police must maintain in tense situations. When crisis strikes and you need to gather yourself and wait for the proper time to strike, call upon Mars.

Applications of the Seal

The Mars seal can be used in five primary ways:

Illumination: Subjugate demons and other entities and forces that cause obstruction and stagnation. Develop confidence and the warrior's calm, the steady composure needed for spiritual battle.

Financial: Handle competition, launch aggressive marketing strategies, and establish the ability to lead and manage. Mars is essential to business success, providing the drive and decisiveness required to win.

Love: Cultivate confidence that attracts when balanced appropriately. Conquer your own fears of rejection and weakness. Confidence can be a powerful attractor when not overdone.

Wrath: Mars is the planet of war and wrath in all its forms. Direct martial force against your enemies with full intensity.

Healing: Attack disease and fight infection. The terminology of medical combat is no accident, Mars helps you fight for your life, making it perhaps the most critical planetary force in healing work.

Geomantic Characters of Mars

Mars commands two geomantic figures, each forged with a different edge of martial force. These characters can be carved into talismans, scored into candles, or drawn on spell materials to summon courage, sharpen aggression, and fortify defense.

Ceremonial Planetary Seals of Mars

In the ceremonial tradition, these sigils are arranged within a double-ringed circle. The Seal is positioned at the top center, the Intelligence beneath it, and the planetary symbol to one side or below for visual balance. Iron is the traditional material for inscribing Mars's ceremonial seals. The Intelligence is regarded as benevolent in nature, while the Spirit is regarded as malevolent. For this reason, only the Seal and Intelligence are incorporated into ceremonial magick circles.
