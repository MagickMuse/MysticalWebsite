The Planetary Seal of Jupiter

Seal Symbolism

The Jupiter seal embodies gathering and accretion. The lines of force draw and gather all they come into contact with, creating an uplifting energy reminiscent of a growing plant. This seal represents the accumulation of power, wealth, and influence.

Powers of Jupiter

Jupiter governs grace, accretion, wealth, abundance, success, health, honors, and materialism. More than anything else, Jupiter is about sovereignty, leading a life where you are the sovereign of yourself and your domain. Jupiter is most often associated with the ruling deity of a pantheon: Zeus, Enlil, Dagda, Perun, Marduk, and Jehovah.

Applications of the Seal

The Jupiter seal can be used in five primary ways:

Illumination: Become master of your fate, the captain of your soul. Achieve true sovereignty over your own life and spiritual path, embodying complete self-mastery.

Financial: Jupiter is the planet for financial matters in almost every aspect. Use it to accumulate wealth, create abundance, and build lasting prosperity.

Love: Jupiterian confidence and command are strong attractors. Money itself is also attractive. Cultivate the sovereign presence that naturally draws others to you.

Wrath: Jupiter is a powerful force to invoke in battle. It provides the command necessary to control martial forces and bolsters your luck and presence in the face of forces that would tear you down.

Healing: One who does not have control over their body or appearance cannot be said to be sovereign. The greater benefic has a positive influence on all health, supporting complete well-being and vitality.

Geomantic Characters of Jupiter

Jupiter governs two geomantic figures, each channeling a different current of abundance and sovereignty. These characters can be inscribed on talismans, pressed into candles, or drawn on spell materials to expand wealth, attract success, and claim dominion over one's fate.

Ceremonial Planetary Seals of Jupiter

In the ceremonial tradition, these sigils are arranged within a double-ringed circle. The Seal is positioned at the top center, the Intelligence beneath it, and the planetary symbol to one side or below for visual balance. Silver is the traditional material for inscribing Jupiter's ceremonial seals. The Intelligence is regarded as benevolent in nature, while the Spirit is regarded as malevolent. For this reason, only the Seal and Intelligence are incorporated into ceremonial magick circles.
