True to Your Sign

Capricorn holds the tenth house of the zodiac and falls between December 22 and January 19. As an earth sign ruled by Saturn and symbolized by the goat, this sign carries a persistent, cautious, steady, and self-disciplined current grounded in stability and warm-heartedness. The shadow side leans toward inflexibility and meanness.

Birthstones

Traditionally, birthstones are crystals associated with zodiac signs based on ancient correspondences. However, modern jewelry store lists vary wildly and often bear little resemblance to historical traditions. Rather than following inconsistent commercial lists, you'll achieve more powerful results by choosing stones that match your sign's elemental color.

Capricorn color: Dark Blue or Black

Dark stones align with Capricorn's disciplined, ambitious, Saturn-ruled energy. Choose from Black Tourmaline for grounding and protection, or Black Opal for manifesting structure and achieving long-term goals.

The Goat

The ancients agreed that the Capricorn constellation was a goat, though it usually took the form of a goat-and-fish hybrid. The Persians knew it as the Sea Goat, Vahik; the Babylonians as the Goat-Fish; and the Greeks as the Goat-Horned One. In Sanskrit it was Mahara, meaning sea monster. Only the Romans broke from this pattern, reading the figure of the goddess Vesta into the stars. Modern images of Capricorn often shed the sea-monster element, but older renderings give it four legs and the tail of a fish, or an entire hindquarter formed of a serpent-like tail.

The Glyph

The sign of Capricorn is an unusual squiggle that may be a sketch of the goat's horns, though it turns up in several forms. Built from both straight lines and curves, it speaks to the dual nature of the sign: the material side rooted on the earth and the spiritual side at home in the water. An old saying held that time itself ended with Capricorn, a notion drawn both from the season in which the constellation rises and from its close tie to Saturn, also called Chronos, god of time.

Qualities

Capricorn rises at the winter solstice, the gateway of the gods, a moment that in the Far East marks the New Year. Saturn, its ruler, lends patience, perseverance, and industry, while the hybrid form of the symbol points to an inborn duality. On one side stands the sure-footed mountain goat, in close touch with its earth element, worldly and intellectual and forever aspiring to the heights; on the other lies the watery aspect of unconscious and psychic power, of depth and intuition. Capricorn is hardworking and ambitious yet carries a mystical streak, and the Saturnine cast of the sign shows in the dignity and self-discipline of those born under it, who can be withdrawn and contemplative. Their lifelong task is to reconcile these two distinct natures, the worldly with the spiritual and the ambitious with the contemplative.
