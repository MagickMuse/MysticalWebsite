A fixed earth transit governed by Venus, the Moon in Taurus channels emotional energy toward comfort, stability, and the pleasures of the senses. This placement grounds the lunar body in the physical world, heightening appreciation for beauty, lasting value, and material security.

Natal Moon in Taurus

The Moon represents your emotional body, how you feel your experiences, where you find comfort, and what you know instinctually. With the Moon in Taurus natally, emotional nature craves security, comfort, and sensory pleasure.

Emotional Nature: Grounded, steady, loyal, sensually attuned

Self-Care: Peace comes through physical comforts: good food, soft textures, beautiful surroundings, and the reassuring presence of loved ones. Feelings develop slowly but run deep. Once committed emotionally, loyalty and steadfastness are strong suits. Sudden changes can leave you feeling deeply unsettled, so building predictable routines that provide emotional security is key.

  • Create a beautiful, comfortable sanctuary space
  • Engage your senses: cook nourishing meals, use luxurious textures, light candles
  • Spend time in nature, especially gardens or green spaces
  • Build predictable routines that provide emotional security
  • Allow yourself time to process changes slowly
  • Work with your hands: pottery, gardening, baking
  • Surround yourself with beauty and quality over quantity

Transiting Moon in Taurus

When the Moon moves through Taurus (approximately every 2.5 days), themes of value rise to the surface: finances, self-worth, and what you truly treasure. The bull has long symbolized wealth, plenty, and fertility, and under Venus's rulership this transit calls you to celebrate your own value and engage the pleasures of the senses. As a fixed earth sign, Taurus provides fertile ground for growing almost any intention and a sturdy foundation for building structures meant to last, whether financial goals, long-term relationships, intimacy, or material security. The fixed quality blesses endurance but also asks for patience: slower and steadier efforts often win.

New Moon in Taurus (Taurus season, roughly April 20 to May 20): A blessed window for planting seeds of any kind, literal or energetic, that will take time to grow. Cast spells for anything you want to last long-term: financial security, romance, or stability.

Full Moon in Taurus (Scorpio season, roughly October 23 to November 21): A sharp contrast between two fixed energies, comfortable Taurus and intense, transformative Scorpio. Powerful for wealth themes, and because Scorpio touches joint finances and debts, it is especially fruitful for magick aimed at changing your financial situation. The pairing of Taurus's loyalty with Scorpio's passion also makes this a deeply romantic full moon.

Taurus Moon Magick

Work magick for money, assets, and long-lasting results. The fixed earth energy stabilizes intentions and secures wealth over time. Lean into greenery, garden, and earth-related practices. With Venus shining through this sign, engage your senses: use aromatic flowers, rich textures, and sensual ingredients for workings related to self-value, beauty, and intimacy.

Do: Financial planning and wealth workings, abundance and prosperity rituals, grounding and earthing practices, kitchen witchery, planting seeds (literal or metaphorical), building lasting foundations, sensory self-care, earth element magick, creating beauty in your environment

Don't: Rush major decisions or force quick changes, start restrictive practices, ignore your body's need for rest, make impulsive financial decisions, begin projects requiring rapid adaptation
