# Symbol pictures

89 pictures for the Symbols grimoire (512 × 512, transparent), named by their Manuscript page and shown at the top of
it and on its card: the 28 colours (a disc of the colour in a gold ring; Clear, Iridescent, Rainbow and Motley drawn
as glass, mother-of-pearl, bands and patchwork) and the 61 numbers (in Cormorant, the site's display font, in gold).
