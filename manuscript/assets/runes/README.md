# Rune images

33 runes as 512 × 512 PNGs with transparent backgrounds, in the site's gold (#b8913f), named by their Manuscript
page (`fehu.png` is manuscript/rune/fehu).

- **Elder Futhark (24):** fehu, uruz, thurisaz, ansuz, raidho, kenaz, gebo, wunjo, hagaiaz, nauthiz, isaz, jera, eiwaz,
  pertho, algiz, sowilo, tiwaz, berkanan, ehwaz, mannaz, laguz, ingwaz, othala, dagaz.
- **Anglo-Saxon Futhorc (9):** ac, osa, yr, ior, ear, cweorth, calc, stan, gar.

Drawn from Noto Sans Runic (SIL Open Font License, © The Noto Project Authors). Kenaz and Ingwaz are drawn by hand
in the same stroke, since the font's forms of those two differ from the usual Elder Futhark shapes. Wyrd (blank) has
no image; Erda, Ul, Sol and Ziu have none yet (they have no standard Unicode form).
