# Rune images

62 runes, alphabets and symbols as 512 × 512 PNGs with transparent backgrounds, in the site's gold (#b8913f), named by their Manuscript
page (`fehu.png` is manuscript/rune/fehu).

- **Elder Futhark (24):** fehu, uruz, thurisaz, ansuz, raidho, kenaz, gebo, wunjo, hagaiaz, nauthiz, isaz, jera, eiwaz,
  pertho, algiz, sowilo, tiwaz, berkanan, ehwaz, mannaz, laguz, ingwaz, othala, dagaz.
- **Anglo-Saxon Futhorc (9):** ac, osa, yr, ior, ear, cweorth, calc, stan, gar.
- **Witch's Runes (12):** crossroads, eye, flight, harvest, man, moon, rings, romance, scythe, star, sun, wave. Redrawn
  as clean line art following the pictures on their Notion pages.
- **Alphabets, ciphers and symbols (17):** Hebrew, Greek, Atbash, hieroglyphs, Pyramid Texts, Coffin Texts, the
  alchemical alphabet and the Ogham tree alphabet from free Noto fonts (SIL OFL); Pigpen, Rosicrucian, Royal Arch,
  Aiq Bekar, the Templar cross, the Helm of Awe and the Web of Wyrd drawn as their fixed geometric forms; the runic
  alphabet and the witches' (Theban) alphabet put together from their own letters' pictures.

Drawn from Noto Sans Runic (SIL Open Font License, © The Noto Project Authors). Kenaz and Ingwaz are drawn by hand
in the same stroke, since the font's forms of those two differ from the usual Elder Futhark shapes. Wyrd (blank) has
no image; Erda, Ul, Sol and Ziu have none yet (no picture of them in Notion).
