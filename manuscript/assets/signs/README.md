# Astrology pictures

132 gold pictures (512 × 512, transparent), named by their Manuscript page in the Astrology grimoire and shown at the
top of it: the 12 Western zodiac signs, the four element groups (the alchemical triangles), The Zodiac (all twelve
around a ring), the Sun, Moon and eight planets (Pluto as the circle in the crescent above a cross), the lunar nodes, Chiron, Lilith, and the twelve Moon-in-a-sign transits
(the crescent beside the sign). Drawn from Noto Sans Symbols (SIL Open Font License); the Sun is a circle and point.

Also: the 12 houses (the chart wheel with that house filled in, house 1 just below the horizon on the left), the moon
phases (the lit shape of each phase; full moons by name as the full disc; super- and micromoon, dark and black moon,
both eclipses, the void-of-course moon), stars and constellations (a four-point star for single stars; the Big Dipper,
the Pleiades, Canis Minor, the Milky Way, the aurora and a comet drawn as themselves), the Chinese zodiac (each
animal's character, Noto Serif TC, OFL) and the Celtic tree signs (each tree's Ogham letter, drawn on one tall stem).
The same picture is the small icon beside the name in lists and page titles, unless the page has its own icon.
