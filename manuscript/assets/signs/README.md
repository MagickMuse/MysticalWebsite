# Astrology sign pictures

44 gold pictures (512 × 512, transparent), named by their Manuscript page in the Astrology grimoire and shown at the
top of it: the 12 Western zodiac signs, the four element groups (the alchemical triangles), The Zodiac (all twelve
around a ring), the Sun, Moon and eight planets, the lunar nodes, Chiron, Lilith, and the twelve Moon-in-a-sign transits
(the crescent beside the sign). Drawn from Noto Sans Symbols (SIL Open Font License); the Sun is a circle and point.
