😮‍💨Halitosis

Halitosis, or persistent offensive breath, refers to bad-smelling exhalations originating from the mouth, throat, gums, sinuses, stomach, or systemic conditions. Common causes include poor oral hygiene, periodontal disease, dry mouth, sinus or throat infection, smoking, dietary factors, and digestive issues.

Aromatherapy Approach

Aromatherapy supports halitosis through antiseptic, antimicrobial, deodorizing, and digestive oils used as a diluted mouthwash or gargle. The oral mucosa is highly absorbent, so essential oils must always be carried in a suitable solubilizer (vodka, witch hazel, or glycerin) before being added to water. Never apply essential oils undiluted to oral tissue, and never swallow.

Application Methods

  • Mouthwash: Add 1 to 2 drops of essential oil to 1 teaspoon of vodka or witch hazel as a solubilizer, then dilute in 1 cup (8 oz) of warm water. Swish gently for 30 seconds, then spit. Do not swallow. Use up to twice daily.
  • Gargle: Prepare as above and gargle for 15 to 30 seconds, then spit. Useful when halitosis stems from throat, tonsillar, or sinus infection.

Recommended Oils

  • Bergamot Essential Oil
  • Cardamom Essential Oil
  • Sweet Fennel Essential Oil
  • Lavandin Essential Oil
  • Lavender Essential Oil
  • Spike Lavender Essential Oil
  • Peppermint Essential Oil
  • Spearmint Essential Oil
  • Myrrh Oil

Safety & When to Seek Care

⚠️ Never swallow essential oils. Persistent halitosis despite good oral hygiene may signal periodontal disease, sinus or tonsil infection, gastric reflux, or systemic illness; consult a dentist or physician. Avoid mouthwash applications in young children. Pregnant individuals should avoid sweet fennel, peppermint, and myrrh.
