🌬️The Great Spirit

◇Type
Concept
⚠️
⚠️ The Great Spirit is a sacred concept belonging to many living Native American traditions. Approach with deep respect and avoid appropriation of specific tribal beliefs and practices.

The Great Spirit is perhaps the closest concept in many Native American traditions to the all-pervading universal deity found in other world religions. It is a sexless, wholly encompassing presence, compared by some observers to the concept of Brahman in Hindu thought. Largely benevolent, the Great Spirit presides over the Happy Hunting Grounds, a realm of the afterlife that carries some resemblance to the Christian concept of Heaven.

A Bridge Between Worldviews

The inherently monotheistic quality of the Great Spirit concept meant that when Christian missionaries arrived, the idea of a single, fatherly God sat relatively comfortably with many tribal peoples. There is even a legend in which the Great Spirit presented a set of inscribed stone tablets to the people, drawing a clear parallel to the tablets given by God to Moses.

Across the Nations

While specific beliefs and names vary significantly between tribes and nations, the notion of an overarching benevolent universal presence is a thread that runs broadly through many Native American spiritual traditions. It functions as the animating intelligence behind the web of spirits and forces that govern the natural world.
