Magical Ethics

Witchcraft lacks a central authority or universal rulebook, leaving ethical decisions to the individual practitioner. Most traditions share a common thread: the understanding that magical actions carry consequences. This personal accountability encourages thoughtful consideration before working any spell or ritual.

The Boomerang Effect

Many practitioners believe that the energy sent out into the world returns to its source. Some traditions teach this return is amplified, with positive or negative energy multiplied when it comes back. This principle encourages practitioners to consider carefully the nature of the energy they release through their magical workings.

Personal Ethical Codes

Without external doctrine, each witch develops their own moral framework. Some follow the Wiccan Rede's guidance to "harm none." Others operate with different parameters, perhaps focusing on justice, balance, or personal boundaries. The responsibility lies with the practitioner to establish and maintain their own ethical standards.

Good and Harmful Practitioners

Throughout history, people have distinguished between those who use their skills to help and those who cause harm. Terms like "white witch" or "good witch" arose to differentiate helpful practitioners from those with malicious intent. The community has always recognized that knowledge and power can be directed toward beneficial or destructive ends.

Magick Is Ethically Neutral

Magick itself is neutral, like electricity. Both can help or harm. Casting a spell is simply a means to an end, using willpower to direct energy toward a goal. Intention is what colors the magick.

White, Black, and Gray

White magick furthers spiritual growth, strengthening connection with the divine or gaining wisdom from higher sources. Black magick intends to harm, manipulate, or interfere with another person's free will. Everything else falls into gray territory. Most practical magick, including spells for jobs, love, or prosperity, operates in the gray zone.

The Threefold Return

Whatever you do magically returns to you like a boomerang. Many traditions teach it comes back magnified threefold. This principle encourages keeping thoughts focused on positive outcomes. When everything seems to be going wrong, blessing instead of cursing usually produces better results.

Everyday Black Magick

Most black magick is not performed by evil sorcerers. It is done by ordinary people who do not realize what they are doing. Cursing someone who stole your parking space or cut in line counts as black magick. Doing a revenge spell against a difficult coworker, however justified it feels, still falls into black magick territory.
