😣Griping Pains

◇Type
Medical
↗Oils4
Cardamom Essential OilDill Seed Essential OilParsley Seed Essential OilSweet Fennel Essential Oil

Griping pains are sharp, cramping abdominal pains caused by trapped gas, intestinal spasm, or sluggish digestion. Aromatherapy supports relief through carminative and antispasmodic oils that ease the muscle wall of the gut, encourage the release of trapped gas, and quiet the cramping sensation.

Apply only to intact skin. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before massage, typically at 1 to 2 percent. Avoid during pregnancy when an oil's safety profile cautions against it. Patch test before broader use, and discontinue if irritation develops.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute 4 to 6 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier oil and massage gently into the abdomen in clockwise circles to follow the natural flow of the digestive tract.

Oils to Use to Address Griping Pains

  • Cardamom Essential Oil
  • Dill Seed Essential Oil
  • Sweet Fennel Essential Oil
  • Parsley Seed Essential Oil
