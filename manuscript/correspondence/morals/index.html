Magical Ethics

Every magical tradition maintains its own code: principles that guide practice, boundaries that remain uncrossed, core beliefs that permeate all work. In Wicca, the primary principle is to harm nothing and no one. Other traditions hold different standards, and individual practitioners develop personal codes as they mature in their craft.

The Boomerang Effect

Many practitioners believe that magical actions return to the sender. Casting a spell that harms another may attract harm in return or draw harmful people into your life. This principle encourages careful consideration before any working. Some phrase their spells to end with variations of "for the greatest good of all, harming none," delegating final outcomes to wiser forces who better understand potential consequences.

Personal Codes

Cultural background, life experience, and individual temperament all shape a practitioner's ethical framework. What feels right to one person may not suit another. As magical ability develops, reliance on inner guidance becomes increasingly important. Each witch ultimately determines how to wield their power based on conscience and personal truth.

Good and Bad Practitioners

Harmful practitioners exist, just as harmful people exist in every group and tradition. These cases remain exceptions rather than rules. Fear and misunderstanding have historically fueled negative stereotypes, but witches are fundamentally people who simply view the world through a different lens.
