IX of Swords

For those afflicted with chronic nightmares, sleep is not a refuge but a place of persecution. Rather than rest, it brings horror. Temperament, work, health, and magickal power all suffer as a result.

An occasional disturbing dream may carry genuine meaning, worthy of careful interpretation. For the chronic nightmare sufferer, however, every dream is a horror, and the only tolerable night is a dreamless one.

Origins and Causes

The word "nightmare" derives from the German mara, a type of evil spirit believed to squat on a sleeper's chest, causing terrifying dreams and a sensation of suffocation. Magickal traditions offer several explanations for chronic nightmares: they may reflect a haunting or curse, the accidental opening of a spiritual portal, or an act of deliberate malice by another person. Spirits of all kinds move through Dreamland, not always with benevolent intent. Vampires, incubi, and succubi are among the creatures credited with generating nocturnal disturbance. Spells to banish dream visitations from incubi and succubi can be found in the Vampire Spells section of this grimoire.

Spiritual and Emotional Imbalance

More commonly, chronic nightmares signal spiritual and emotional imbalance, or an extended spiritual test. Avoidance of sleep and suppression of dreams is not the solution. Dreams must be examined, untangled, and confronted so they can disperse and give way to more beneficial ones. Professional dream diviners may assist when self-analysis proves insufficient.

Dream Messages

Dreams carry messages from the earth, from spirits, from ancestors and allies, and from the dreamer's own subconscious. An unheard message repeats itself, in the same form or recognizable variations, until it is received and understood. Pursuing or frightening figures in dreams often represent animal allies attempting to make contact. Deep fear can signal an alliance just as strongly as deep love or attraction. These figures may not intend harm; they may be attempting to assist. Taking conscious control of dreams is possible, though it requires time and practice. Magickal tools exist to provide protection, place difficult dreams in perspective, and open the path toward resolution.
