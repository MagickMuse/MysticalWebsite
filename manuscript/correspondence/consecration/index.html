Consecration is the process of dedicating an object to a specific spirit or deity, enabling it to serve as a conduit for that being's energy and blessings. Where charging infuses an object with the practitioner's own intention, consecration links it to a spiritual source beyond the self.

When to Consecrate

Some spells specifically recommend consecration, generally those that invoke spiritual assistance of one kind or another. The consecrated object becomes a permanent or semi-permanent channel between the practitioner and the spirit.

Consecration Process

  1. Build an altar dedicated to the spirit or deity
  2. Charge the object with your desire and intention
  3. Consciously request that the object receive the needed blessing and energy of the spirit
  4. Allow the object to remain on the altar in close proximity to the spirit's image (if one is used) or to various power items associated with that spirit

The time required varies by tradition and spirit, but the object should remain on the altar for at least one full night.
