II of Cups

Definition

Connection is the correspondence of bonds, links, and the energetic threads that tie one being to another. In magickal practice, connection work focuses on establishing, strengthening, or repairing the sympathetic links between people, between a practitioner and their tools, or between the practitioner and the natural world.

The Cosmic Web

From the perspective of magickal philosophy, everything is energy, and all energy is connected through an invisible matrix that practitioners call the cosmic web. This web envelops the earth and permeates all things, extending throughout the solar system and beyond. It pulses with subtle vibrations that magicians, psychics, and other sensitive individuals can feel.

These energetic connections are what enable witches to work magick, even over long distances. When you send a thought or emotion along one of these energy lines to wherever or whomever you wish to reach, you are working with the correspondence of Connection at its most fundamental level.

Sympathetic Links

The Law of Similars teaches that objects sharing a resemblance or energetic signature create a sympathetic link. Connection magick builds on this principle deliberately. When you hold someone's photograph, wear a piece of jewelry they gave you, or work with a material that both of you associate with your relationship, you are tugging on the thread of the cosmic web that links you together.

Working with Connection Energy

Connection draws heavily on the Air element (communication, thought, movement) and Water element (emotion, intuition, bonds). Materials that appear in both elemental lists will be especially effective for connection work.

Common Connection Intentions

  • Deepening a bond with a partner, friend, or family member
  • Building rapport with a new person or group
  • Strengthening the link between a practitioner and their tools or familiar
  • Establishing communication with spirits, ancestors, or guides
  • Repairing a broken or damaged relationship
  • Enhancing empathy and mutual understanding

Sensing Connection

Developing awareness of connection energy can be practiced through simple exercises. Hold your palms facing each other about a foot apart and slowly move them closer together without touching. You may feel warmth, coolness, tingling, or other sensations. This is your energy field interacting with itself. The same awareness can be extended to sense the energy fields of plants, stones, animals, and other people.

Communication Before Taking

Connection magick requires respect and reciprocity. When gathering materials from nature for spellwork, communicate with the plant, stone, or other natural being first. State what is needed, ask permission, and express gratitude. Always give a gift to receive a gift: milk, grain, a beverage, crushed eggshells, or a coin are all suitable offerings.
