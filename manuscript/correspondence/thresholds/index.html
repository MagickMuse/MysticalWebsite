Thresholds are boundary zones where one force, power, or element meets another. These transitional spaces are among the most magickally charged locations in existence, holding both immense potential and extreme vulnerability.

Types of Thresholds

Natural Thresholds

Magickal energy radiates from everything that occurs naturally on Earth, though some sources carry greater power than others. Frankincense, roses, and wild Syrian rue, for example, grant deeper access to magickal power than many other botanicals. Certain areas are also stronger sources of power, with "area" understood in both a literal and metaphorical sense.

The most potent natural thresholds include:

  • Seashores: the transitional zone where ocean meets land
  • Mountain bases: where flat land begins to rise
  • Caves: subterranean boundaries between Earth's outer and inner forces

Architectural Thresholds

Doors and windows function as thresholds within built structures, marking the division between interior and exterior space.

Temporal Thresholds

Thresholds also exist in time. Twilight and dawn are transitional moments where an incoming power approaches before the outgoing power has fully dispersed. These in-between times carry heightened magickal charge.

Life Cycle Thresholds

Birth, death, and transformation all constitute thresholds. The arrival of a first child, which transforms a person into a parent, is a particularly significant crossing. Death marks the threshold between one existence and the next. Anyone lingering in a half-life is said to have a foot in both worlds, straddling the boundary. Every transformative ritual qualifies as a threshold by virtue of its capacity to change what exists into something new.

Thresholds of the Body

The mouth is the threshold between thought and speech, marking the point where internal experience crosses into external expression.

Doors & Windows as House Spirits

Doors and windows are the architectural thresholds of the home. Like the Faucet and Stove, they require special spiritual attention, they are not just passive structures but active participants in the spiritual ecosystem of your home.

If treated properly, the doors and windows of your home will:

  • Bring in good things
  • Prevent bad things from entering
  • Help release what no longer serves you

We can never fully master liminal spaces, but we can enter into a dance with them.

Protective Charms for Doors & Windows

Place a protective charm at your doors and windows. Options include:

  • An image of a liminal deity: Hermes, Apollo Agyieus ("Apollo of the Streets"), and Hekate were all commonly given shrines just outside ancient Greek homes to guard the threshold.
  • A protective figure: Saint Michael and other guardian spirits are traditional choices.
  • Image formats: A picture, holy card, or statue. If using a flat image, laminate it or put it in a frame so it can be anointed with oil.

Anointing Oils for Threshold Charms

Choose one of the following:

  • An oil specifically dedicated to the deity depicted
  • A spiritual condition oil prepared for protection magic
  • Regular olive oil you have prayed over yourself

Door Guardians

The front entrance is the most vulnerable threshold of a home, since both good news and bad news usually arrive straight through the front door. Door guardians are objects chosen for the protective magick they give off. They are usually placed beside the entrance facing outward, ready to confront and drive back any threat or evil that approaches.

Most door guardians are fierce, threatening, even frightening images, and that is the point. A club hires a big bruiser as a bouncer, not a sweet little kid, and a rottweiler stands guard more often than a miniature poodle. The fiercer and more frightening the image, the deeper the protection it offers. The one exception is the Holy Child of Atocha, whose sweet appearance hides a primordial spirit.

Doorsteps

The doorstep is the threshold in its most literal form, and an especially vulnerable spot. Many hexes are cast by "dusting the doorstep": the spell is set off by leaving its remains or other magickal materials at the target's front door.

Many rituals aim to turn the doorstep into a zone of safety instead. These spells are usually repeated weekly, and many involve cleansing. Because some of them simply look like careful, house proud cleaning, doorstep magick can be a very subtle and discreet style of protection. If your doorstep is ever dusted, safely remove what was left and repel the spell, then follow up with protective measures.

Magickal Significance

Thresholds are simultaneously sites of extraordinary magickal potential and acute vulnerability. A vast number of protective amulets, rituals, and spells are designed specifically to safeguard thresholds and the transformative processes they represent. Every magick spell can itself be understood as a transformative threshold, bridging a past that left something to be desired and a future the spell aims to produce.

Simple Boundaries and Expanded Thresholds

Most thresholds consist of a straightforward boundary: one foot inside the house, the other outside. With careful balance, a person can stand poised at the dividing line, belonging to neither side.

The ancient Egyptians recognized this principle in their own landscape. They called their country "the land of the red and the black" because a visible dividing line separated the black fertile soil of the Nile silt from the stark red desert sand. A person could literally stand with one foot in each color. Each color also represented a different kind of magick and a different spiritual ruler: the black belonged to Osiris, with his arts of orderly civilization, while the red belonged to his brother Seth, the anarchic and chaotic Lord of Magick.

These are simple, hoppable boundaries. Beyond them lie expanded, exponentially supercharged thresholds such as the crossroads, where multiple forces converge and magickal potential compounds.
