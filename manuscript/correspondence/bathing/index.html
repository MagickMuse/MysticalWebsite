Overview

Although it may seem like the magical equivalent of instructions for boiling water, many spells are accomplished through baths. Various magical techniques increase the odds of a spell's success.

General Technique

In general, it is beneficial to submerge completely in the water at least once, although some spells may specify the number of submersions.

Direction of Bathing

The direction in which you bathe yourself matters:

Banishing (Down and Out)

When you want to rid yourself of something, a problem, bad debts, an unwanted situation, bathe down and out: start at the top of your head, work your way down your body, and out the arms and legs.

Attracting (Up and In)

When you want to draw something towards you — love, cash, a job — start at the feet and move in towards your heart. Start at the ends of your hands and move inwards.

Air Drying

Allow yourself to air dry. Drying yourself with a towel wipes much of the residue, the aura of the bath, off your skin. Nothing catastrophic will happen if you use a towel, but you will receive consistently stronger, better results if you take the time to air dry.
