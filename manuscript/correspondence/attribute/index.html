🎨Iconography🕯️Saints

An attribute is something that represents a saint or other holy being. An attribute may be understood as the equivalent of a calling card intended to identify a saint.

Saints are virtually always portrayed with at least one of their attributes. Some saints possess many, while others may have only a single attribute. Saints deriving from spiritual traditions that discourage visual imagery typically lack attributes.

In the days before universal literacy, someone could look at an image and identify a saint by the attributes present.

How Attributes Work

Saint Barbara's attribute is a tower. She is virtually always accompanied by a tower. It is a safe bet to assume that any religious image of a woman with a tower portrays Barbara. The tower may be full-size, or she may hold a miniature in her hands.

If you wanted to create an image of Barbara but lacked artistic skill, simply drawing a figure accompanied by a tower should be sufficient for the faithful to recognize her. It is the attribute that differentiates this particular figure from any other. A figure bearing a plate of severed breasts, on the other hand, would immediately be recognized as Saint Agatha.

Symbolic Significance

Attributes are never random symbols. Instead, they convey significant information about a holy being. Construction of a tower is central to Barbara's mythos and the saga of her martyrdom. This attribute also serves to remind us that Barbara is the matron saint of architects and that she provides fire safety, protecting devotees from burning buildings.

Attributes exist in all spiritual traditions that use visual imagery to identify holy beings. The bodhisattva Kwan Yin is frequently portrayed holding a pomegranate, indicating that she performs miracles of fertility.

Practical Uses

If you are unsure what sort of offering to give a saint, attributes can lend hints. Place small towers or pictures of them on Barbara's altar. Offer Kwan Yin pomegranates.

Attributes can substitute for an altar image. If you have not yet found the perfect statue of Saint Sebastian, use an arrow (his primary attribute) to represent him until you do. Alternatively, for those who object to the use of graven images, an attribute can serve as a permanent symbolic altar image.

Vehicles of Communication

Attributes are also vehicles of communication. If you have requested a favor from a saint, sudden and unexpected appearances of that saint's attributes may be a response or a message. Pay attention and analyze the context.

For example, if you have requested healing from Saint Roch and a strange dog unexpectedly breaks free from its leash to lick you with enthusiasm, that is a good omen. Dogs are this saint's constant companions and messengers, and thus are one of his attributes.
