Drawing as a Magickal Goal

Drawing is magick performed with the purpose of bringing something toward you. This includes attracting money, love, health, a new job, or any other desired outcome.

The Power Behind Magick

The source of magickal power is the practitioner, not the tools. Candles, herbs, crystals, and ritual objects help focus the mind, but they are not where the power originates. Magick is fundamentally a practice of consciousness. The most significant tools are your thoughts and feelings.

Thinking Makes It So

What you think determines what you get. When belief and intent are strong enough, when passion fuels the spell, and when energy focuses clearly toward a specific goal, results follow. Knowing exactly what you want and stating your intention with absolute clarity is essential. Vague or scattered intentions produce vague or scattered results.

The Law of Attraction

Your thoughts, emotions, and actions affect the energetic patterns around you. For every action there is a reaction. The energy you put out returns to you. Positive thoughts and feelings attract positive outcomes. Negative energy draws more of the same. This principle underlies all magickal practice: be careful what you ask for, because your focused intention sets forces in motion.

The Power of Belief

Belief forms the core of magick. Without it, rituals become empty gestures. Most people carry self-limiting beliefs adopted in childhood from parents, teachers, and culture. These unconscious assumptions shape reality: believing you are unworthy creates unworthiness, believing life is a struggle creates struggle. The practitioner must believe in their own ability to produce results. Doubt extinguishes creative fire.

Shifting Your Beliefs

You are not trapped by your conditioning. You can choose what you believe, what you desire, and what parameters define your life. When beliefs change, circumstances change. Once you recognize this, you can begin consciously creating your destiny according to your own vision and keying into your magickal nature to make it happen.

Thought Forms

Passionate ideas, held over time, produce thought forms that serve as energetic patterns eventually becoming physical forms. Just as a designer must envision a dress before creating it, magickal work begins with a mental image. imbued with energy and emotion. What you conjure mentally will eventually emerge into the material world.

Training the Mind

Becoming a powerful practitioner means training your mind: focusing thoughts, raising energy, and using will to bring intentions to fruition. Multitasking diminishes magickal returns. When attention scatters across many subjects, power diffuses and nothing moves forward strongly.

Redirecting Thought

Pay attention to your thoughts. Focusing on what you lack perpetuates lack. Lamenting problems creates more problems. When you catch yourself thinking about what you do not want, do a mental reversal and focus instead on what you do want.

A More Nuanced View of the Law of Attraction

The basic premise of the Law of Attraction is that energy comes before manifestation. Where you put your energy is what you will manifest. What you focus your thoughts on will become your reality. This gets boiled down to the belief that positive thoughts create a positive reality, while negative thoughts create a negative reality.

There is some truth to this idea. If you are a negative person, it is easy to look around the world and see the negative things that will reinforce your negativity; vice versa if you are a positive person. But being positive or negative does not call positive or negative events into your world. Good and bad things happening in your life is simply called living. It is how we react to those things that determines what will manifest.

The popular "Law of Attraction" messaging took the complex concept of karma and watered it down as a way of wishing desires into existence, while also implying that anything negative in your life, you asked for and manifested. That stubbed toe? That random car accident? Getting fired? Your beloved pet dying? According to the LoA, you manifested all of that.

This is not helpful, and it is not how manifestation actually works.

A Practical Path to Manifestation

Step 1: Identify Your Big, Audacious Dream

In order to manifest your dreams, you have to know what your dreams are. If you keep a desire as a secret kernel in your heart that you do not even admit to yourself, of course you will not be able to manifest that dream.

Sometimes we water down our dreams because the smaller goal feels possible, but the big, audacious dream is just too much, too big, too scary. The universe has a way of putting amazing opportunities in our path, but so often we play small and do not see the opportunity for the dream that it is.

What is your big, audacious dream? Not more money or a new lover, but the big thing that lives behind that.

Step 2: Speak Your Dream Out Loud

Once you can see the dream, once you can admit it exists, the next step is to name it and say it out loud. This can be terrifying. Speaking the desire out loud for others to hear? Oof.

But by naming your desire out loud, the universe (or God, or your higher self, or destiny, or whatever you want to call it) starts to set up the pieces for you to follow that path. You do not know who has connections, resources, or information that could help you. Speaking your dream creates openings for synchronicity.

Step 3: Take Action

Naming the desire is just the first step. Then you have to take the additional steps to manifest your goal. These steps are likely both magickal and mundane.

You cannot just wish the keys to your dream into your hand. You have to fill out paperwork, make phone calls, do research. You also do ritual, burn candles, make offerings, work with deities or spirits who align with your goal.

The real-world steps are just as important as the magickal ones. They go hand in hand.

Consider:

  • Reaching out to people already doing what you want to do
  • Creating an altar for the manifestation process
  • Adding every tangible step (documents, emails, contracts) to that altar
  • Working with a deity or spirit whose energy aligns with your goal
  • Sending out requests for help, loans, partnerships, or support

Step 4: Say Yes to Your Dream

At some point in the process, fear will rear its ugly head. When the dream gets closer and closer to reality, those doubts and fears are likely to surface. All of the stories you have told yourself about your worthiness will show up.

A breakdown is a not-uncommon part of the manifestation process. The weight of something coming into manifestation can land like a ton of bricks. You may have to decide if you really want this thing, this dream, to become reality, or if you want to play small and continue with how things were.

This is when you have a choice: give up on your dream and walk away, or push forward and go for it. There is not a right answer. Saying no is a legitimate possibility. But in order to accept your abundance, to allow your dream to manifest, you have to be brave and you have to say yes.

Step 5: Bring Your Shadow into the Light

So much of what keeps us from manifesting our desires or having the abundance we want is rooted in a lack of belief that we deserve those things.

Questions to excavate your shadow:

  • What are the old stories you tell yourself about why you do not deserve what you desire?
  • What family-of-origin programming is keeping you in a scarcity mindset?
  • What lessons over your lifetime have you believing that you cannot have wealth, luck, or abundance?

These questions and the old stories you hold are the places to start mining for information. Uncover these hidden shadows and bring them into the light. Once these issues are revealed, you can either make friends with them or banish them.

Abundance starts with your deep-seated beliefs, and it is only through exploring these parts of yourself that you can start to make a real difference in your real world.

You deserve to manifest your dreams. You deserve to have the abundance you desire. It is there, just waiting for you to make the space to accept it.
