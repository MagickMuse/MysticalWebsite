🫙Containment Magick

◇Type
Concept

Definition

Containment Magick refers to practices designed to hold, trap, bind, or restrict energy, entities, influences, or intentions within a defined space or vessel. It is the art of creating boundaries that keep something in rather than out.

How It Works

Through circle casting, vessel work, binding, or warding, the practitioner creates an energetic barrier that prevents the contained force from escaping or affecting the outside world. The container may be physical (a jar, box, mirror, or bound object) or energetic (a visualized sphere, circle, or seal).

Common Applications

  • Spirit traps — vessels designed to hold or confine unwanted entities
  • Jar spells — containing intentions, influences, or even harmful energies within a sealed container
  • Binding work — restricting a person's harmful actions or an entity's movement
  • Mirror boxes — reflecting negative energy back on itself while containing it
  • Freezer spells — "freezing" someone's actions or influence by containing them in ice
  • Quarantine magick — isolating cursed objects or contaminated energy from spreading

Sub-Categories

Containment, deflection, and reflection all serve the purpose of placing limitations on energy or influence.

Shielding protects the practitioner from incoming negativity.

Confining restricts negativity to its source, preventing it from spreading outward.

Dispersing dissipates incoming negative energies, breaking them apart so they lose their effect.

Turning returns deliberately sent negative energy back to its sender.

Related Concepts

  • Protection magick (keeping things out)
  • Binding magick (restricting action)
  • Warding (boundary setting)
  • Banishing (removal rather than containment)
  • Circle (sacred boundary)

Caution

Contained energies do not disappear—they remain held. Practitioners should have a plan for eventual release, transformation, or permanent sealing. Neglected containment can weaken over time.
