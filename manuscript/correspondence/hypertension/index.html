Hypertension, or chronically elevated blood pressure, is a common cardiovascular condition where blood exerts excessive force against artery walls. It often presents without symptoms yet increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney damage over time. Aromatic support focuses on relaxation, vasodilation, and stress reduction; essential oils complement but do not replace lifestyle changes and medical care.

⚠️ Hypertension is a serious medical condition. Essential oils support but do not replace antihypertensive medication. Several oils on this list are contraindicated in pregnancy (clary sage, sweet marjoram, lemon balm, garlic, yarrow). Garlic oil is taken internally as capsules; it is not generally used in massage, bath, or vaporization due to its strong odour and irritant potential. Always consult a healthcare provider for chronic high blood pressure.

Suggested Application

  • Massage: Dilute 2 to 3 percent in carrier oil and use for full-body or back massage to support relaxation and circulation.
  • Bath: 6 to 8 drops total in a dispersant (carrier oil, milk, or unscented bath base), then add to a warm (not hot) bath. Soak to encourage parasympathetic relaxation.
  • Vaporizer/diffuser: Diffuse 3 to 5 drops in water for ambient calm; effective for daily stress reduction and evening wind-down.
