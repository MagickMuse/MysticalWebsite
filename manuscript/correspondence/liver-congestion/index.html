🟫Liver Congestion

Liver congestion is a sluggish, overburdened state of the liver in which the organ's capacity to process toxins, fats, hormones, and metabolic waste slows down. It can show up as bloating after meals, dull right-side discomfort, poor fat tolerance, sallow skin, low energy, irritability, headaches, and a general sense of heaviness. Aromatherapy supports the liver through warming, decongestant, depurative, and hepatic oils applied externally over the upper-right abdomen to encourage circulation, bile flow, and gentle detoxification.

⚠️ Liver congestion can also reflect more serious hepatic conditions. Persistent right-upper-quadrant pain, jaundice, dark urine, pale stools, or unexplained weight loss require medical evaluation rather than home aromatherapy. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before skin application, patch test first, and avoid liver-supportive oils during pregnancy unless cleared by a qualified practitioner. Several oils on this list (rosemary, turmeric, Spanish sage, celery seed, carrot seed, lemon verbena) are pregnancy-avoid. Lemon verbena is phototoxic.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Abdominal Massage: Dilute 4 to 6 drops of essential oil in 1 tablespoon of carrier oil and massage gently over the upper-right abdomen in slow clockwise circles for 5 to 10 minutes. Best done once or twice daily, away from meals.
  • Castor Oil Pack with Essential Oils: Add 2 to 3 drops of essential oil to a tablespoon of castor oil, soak a soft cloth, lay it across the right ribcage, cover with plastic and a warm compress, and rest for 30 to 45 minutes.
  • Blending Ratios: Adults use a 2 to 3 percent dilution for sustained liver-support massage. Reduce to 1 percent for sensitive skin or extended use.

Oils to Use to Address Liver Congestion

  • Helichrysum Essential Oil
  • Linden Absolute
