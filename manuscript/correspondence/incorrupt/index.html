✨Incorrupt

◇Type
Concept
↗Other Correspondences3
💀Death🦴Relics🕯️Saints

Ashes to ashes; dust to dust. It is usual for a corpse to deteriorate after death. Except that sometimes it does not.

An incorrupt body is not the same as a mummified corpse. No outside intervention is required to produce an incorrupt cadaver; it is perceived as a supernatural event. Thus no matter how lifelike her corpse, Santa Evita is not incorrupt, because she was embalmed. The body of Saint Agatha, however, was reputedly unearthed in the 11th century and discovered to be incorrupt without human intervention.

Characteristics

An incorrupt body remains lifelike in appearance.

Two Types of Incorruptibility

The quality of being incorrupt is associated with two vastly different types of dead souls:

  • Vampires: Traditionally believed to remain incorrupt because they feed on the living
  • Saints: Incorruptibility is perceived as a special mark of sanctity, the signal that someone may be a saint

The Odor of Sanctity

Incorrupt saints may also give off the odor of sanctity, described as a sweet, floral scent completely different from rotting flesh.

Display

The bodies of incorrupt saints may be placed on display as proof of their sanctity and as sacred inspiration.
