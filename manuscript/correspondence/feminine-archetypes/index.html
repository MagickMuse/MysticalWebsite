The Divine Feminine

When we talk about feminine and masculine, we mean energies rather than woman and man. Receptivity, emotion, passivity, and intuition are all expressions of feminine energy. You can see it operating in water, earth, the moon, darkness, night, silence, and cool colors. The Goddess is a depiction of the feminine force-the face we put on the energy to personify it.

Many cultures speak of a feminine and masculine principle existing in and around us. The Chinese refer to these two energies as yin (feminine) and yang (masculine). Native Americans respect Mother Earth and Father Sky. These two polarities function in tandem to balance one another and create wholeness.

The Divine Masculine

The feminine is not complete without the masculine; together, these energetic polarities form a whole. In the yin-yang symbol, the white part represents the masculine force, the black side the feminine. Notice how, when joined, they form a circle-the symbol of wholeness.

Masculine energy expresses itself outwardly as action and assertiveness. You can see this principle operating in fire, wind, the sun, light, daytime, noise, and warm colors. Gods such as Thor, god of thunder and lightning, and the war god Mars symbolize the qualities of the masculine force.

Tripartite Goddess

The Goddess is frequently depicted as a tripartite being represented by three images that signify the three stages of womanhood. Likewise, witches often see the God as having three faces representing youth, maturity, and old age.

Depending on the type of magick you are doing, you might call upon a certain aspect. If you need extra vitality to win a competition, invite the youthful side of the Divine Masculine. If you are trying to conceive, ask the mother aspect of the Divine Feminine to lend her fertility.

The Maiden

Youth, innocence, learning, wonder, curiosity

Call on the Maiden when learning a new skill, entering unfamiliar territory, or seeking a fresh perspective. Her energy encourages curiosity, play, and the courage to grow through imperfection.

The Mother

Fertility, creation, compassion, generosity, nurturing

The Mother supports creative projects and life transitions. She births new realities, whether it’s a piece of art, a home, or a more healed version of you. Embody her when you need to nourish, protect, or sustain.

The Crone

Wisdom, maturity, introspection, patience, inner peace

Invoke the Crone in moments of spiritual depth, reflection, and decision-making. She teaches you to trust your knowing and sit in silence long enough to hear your soul’s voice.

The Queen

Leadership, power, confidence, loyalty, partnership

The Queen offers sovereignty and strategy. Embody her when claiming your authority, making empowered choices, or forging strong partnerships. She leads with integrity and vision.

The Huntress

Fearlessness, independence, freedom, self-reliance, ambition

Turn to the Huntress when taking bold action or reclaiming your independence. She thrives in motion, and reminds you to pursue your goals without apology or hesitation.

The Lover

Sensuality, passion, pleasure, magnetism, creativity

The Lover awakens joy, artistry, and embodied presence. Embody her to reconnect with pleasure, play, and the beauty of each moment. She teaches that life is meant to be felt deeply.

The Mystic

Intuition, imagination, spirituality, healing, mystery

The Mystic lives in the in-between. Work with her in ritual, divination, or dreamwork. She guides you through unseen realms and into deep communion with your soul’s truth.
