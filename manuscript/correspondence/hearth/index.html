The Hearth as Cauldron

The burning hearth fire holds incredible protective power and strengthens any spell cast within it. The hearth symbolizes female primal power and the fire male, so whatever enters the hearth enters a cauldron of generation. This is why hearth spirits such as Vesta and the domovoi matter so much, and why the hearth fire must never be allowed to go out. Protective botanicals can be powdered and burned as incense, but burning them on the hearth is believed to be especially powerful.
