Zig-Zag

The shape of the lightning bolt, another emblem of the gods' command over the elements, is a sharp zigzag much like the sigrune that was later seized upon as the emblem of Hitler's SS. The form of that rune not only resembles the letter S but reaches back to the Norse deities whose potent imagery inspired so many symbols of the Nazi regime. It is no surprise that lightning is also a phallic symbol, often drawn as a jagged scepter. Mithras, the Roman god who came before Christ, was likewise born of a virgin: a supernatural flash of lightning is said to have made his mother conceive, the bolt here standing for the spark of life and the quickening of the soul. It is tempting to think his mother was struck by a kind of divine coup de foudre, a French phrase that means both love at first sight and a bolt of lightning.
