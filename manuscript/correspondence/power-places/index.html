🌀Liminality

Natural locations where the stream of magick pools, creating auspicious spots of concentrated energy. In Ozark folk belief, these are sites where the divine or magickal current collects, offering practitioners a wellspring of power for their work.

What Defines a Power Place

The traditional formula:

Oddity or rarity produced by nature itself + secluded location = auspiciousness and latent pool of power

Power places announce themselves through unusual features. The strangeness of the land signals the pooling effect beneath. Common folk historically avoided these spots due to their chaotic energies and reputation as rifts between worlds, but healers and practitioners have always sought them out.

Traditional Ozark Power Places

  • Natural springs and waterfalls
  • Ancient trees (especially solitary specimens)
  • Solitary boulders or rock piles away from bluffs or other rock sources
  • Natural holes in trees or rocks
  • Natural arches and animal-shaped formations
  • Rare plant groves (ginseng, witch trees)
  • Caves and cavern mouths
  • Foggy glens and shady hollows

These groves and formations are believed to be produced by latent springs of magick beneath the ground.

Urban Power Places

Towns and cities are not out of bounds. As one practitioner said, "Town is in nature too." Urban power places include:

  • City parks protecting rare land features or ancient trees
  • Abandoned lots where life persists (a maple growing through cracked concrete)
  • Any location where oddity and auspiciousness converge

Pools of magickal energy will always support life, even in unlikely places.

Finding Power Places

Observation

Keep your eyes open for strange or rare land features. Walk slowly and survey the land around you. What stands out? Caverns, springs, waterfalls, hidden groves with wild medicinal plants, spooky fog-filled glens. Use all your senses to create a mental map.

Feeling the Energy

Power places have an energetic feel. It may manifest as:

  • A sensation like static electricity building
  • Goosebumps
  • Hairs standing up on arms and neck
  • A "gut feeling" or pull in your center

Ozark folk magick relies heavily on feeling latent energies. This intuitive sense helps distinguish a merely unusual spot from a true power place.

Dowsing

Without inborn second sight, even the observant may glance past power places. Divination methods help:

  • Pendulum
  • Dowsing rods
  • Canes
  • Other dowsing tools

Oddly, locations found through dowsing often also feature strange or rare land formations.

Trust Your Intuition

Ozark folk magick is highly intuitive. There is no standard definition for what counts as a power place. It really is about how the place feels. "We all have the gift inside us, so we all have that little voice that will guide us."

Using Power Places

Energetic Recharge: Feeling the Flow

When your spiritual batteries are low, visit your power place to recharge.

  1. Approach slowly. Let yourself feel the energy radiating from the spot. Ask yourself why this feels like a power place. What features stand out as auspicious?
  2. Sense the energy pattern. Does it feel like a swirling vortex or a calm pool?
  3. If a vortex: Spiral slowly inward toward the center. Let the energy pull at your gut (the source of intuition). Notice whether you spiral clockwise, counterclockwise, or both.
  4. If a calm pool: Imagine starting at the bank, then moving deeper until you reach the center.
  5. Relax into the spot. Sit, lie down, or stand. Let all tension dissipate.
  6. Visualize the flow. Energy spirals or flows into your gut, radiates through your entire body, then returns to the current around you.
  7. Remain as long as needed.

Amplifying Ritual Work

Standing in an auspicious spot empowers any working with latent magickal energy. Traditional uses include:

  • Taking clients or patients to power places for healing work
  • Performing cleansings or curse-breakings at waterfalls or springs
  • Working difficult rituals when doubting your own energy level
  • Combining location power with celestial timing (full moon rituals at power places)

"One person can't possibly have the gift for everything." We all need help sometimes. Power places lend their energy to ours.

Considerations When Working with Power Places

1. Honor the Land

Honor the spirits of the land by honoring the land itself. Leave it better than you found it.

2. Mindful Offerings

Leave food that nourishes the land and its creatures, seen and unseen:

Good offerings:

  • Whole-grain bread slices
  • Seeds and nuts
  • Dry grains (oats, barley, millet)
  • Water, milk, or tea (poured directly on land)

Avoid:

  • Plastic packaging (take all trash with you)
  • High salt or sugar foods
  • Chocolate (poisonous to many animals)
  • Cups or containers (don't leave them behind)

3. Let the Land Be

Don't try to change or "fix" the power place. Let it be as it is. The exception: picking up litter as devotional practice.

4. You Cannot Own a Power Place

Nature does not work that way. The stream of life provides pools for all beings. Protecting a power place is honored, but give freely, as the magick was freely given to you. Don't be stingy with auspiciousness.

5. Power Places Can Change

Work as nature works. Power places come and go. Use the energy while present. Don't grieve when it passes. Find another power place. Nothing remains forever.

Safety Considerations

  • In the wilds: Know where you are in relation to trails. Don't explore caves or crevices alone.
  • In urban areas: Know if it's safe to explore an area before entering.
  • The stream of magick does not consider safety when creating pools, but we must.
