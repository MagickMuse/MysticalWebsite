A bruise, or contusion, is the discoloration that appears when blood vessels beneath intact skin are broken by impact and blood pools into the surrounding tissue. Aromatherapy supports the body's natural healing process by easing inflammation, encouraging circulation to clear pooled blood, and soothing tenderness while the area mends.

Apply only to intact skin. Never use essential oils on broken skin or open wounds. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before topical use, and patch-test first if the skin is sensitive. Arnica essential oil (steam-distilled) is highly toxic and is never used at home. Only arnica infused oil, tincture, cream, or ointment is safe for topical care.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Skin oil or lotion: Dilute the chosen essential oil in a carrier oil or unscented lotion at roughly 2 to 2.5 percent (12 to 15 drops per ounce of carrier) and gently smooth over the bruised area. Take care not to press hard on tender tissue.
  • Compress: Add 4 to 6 drops of essential oil to a bowl of cool water, swirl, soak a soft cloth, wring out, and lay over the bruise. Refresh as the cloth warms.
  • Arnica preparations: Arnica is used in cream, infused oil, tincture, or ointment form rather than as an essential oil. Apply directly to the affected area following the product's dilution guidance.

Oils to Use to Address Bruises

  • Arnica Oil (as cream, infused oil, tincture, or ointment)
  • Borneol Essential Oil
  • Clove Essential Oil
  • Sweet Fennel Essential Oil
