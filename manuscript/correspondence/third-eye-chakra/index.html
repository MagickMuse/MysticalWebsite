A chakra is an energy node in the human body. In Sanskrit this one is called Ajna, and it is pictured as a violet lotus of two petals. It is associated with the pineal gland, which is sensitive to light.

Colors

Purple

Attributes

Psychic Awareness, Insight

Governs

Intuition, inner knowing, and psychic vision

Chant sound: IH (like the i in "in")

Location: Situated at the level of your forehead, slightly above your eyebrows

Chakra Healing

The brow chakra is concerned with thinking, perception, spirituality and extrasensory perception. It is situated in the area known as the third eye. It controls the endocrine system and is also involved, with the chakras immediately above and below it, with the senses of sight and hearing.

Balanced State: When the brow chakra is well balanced you will be able to "see" clearly. This relates to your vision, but also includes your ability to perceive or understand, the whole picture. This perception includes your intuition so that you will feel able to act on your hunches.

Imbalance Issues: When the brow chakra is out of balance you are likely to experience headaches and eye problems. You will also find it hard to maintain a sense of perspective.

Stimulation: The brow chakra can be stimulated by deliberately spending time away from your problems.

Body Systems

The third eye chakra governs the following body systems and organs:

  • Eyes
  • Ears
  • Nose
  • Brain
  • Nervous system
  • Pineal gland
  • Pituitary gland
