🍄Ringworm

Ringworm is a contagious fungal infection of the skin and scalp (not a worm, despite the name) caused by dermatophytes such as Trichophyton and Microsporum species. It produces ring-shaped, scaly, itchy patches that can appear on the body (tinea corporis), scalp (tinea capitis), feet (athlete's foot), or groin (jock itch). The fungicidal and antimicrobial action of certain essential oils makes them excellent allies against ringworm, particularly when applied directly to affected areas or worked into the scalp.

Safety: Always dilute essential oils before applying to skin (typically 2 to 3 percent in a carrier oil). Patch test before broader use, especially on irritated or broken skin. Ringworm is highly contagious, so wash hands, towels, and bedding thoroughly during treatment. Persistent or worsening cases should be evaluated by a healthcare provider, as oral antifungals may be needed.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Skin oil/lotion: Dilute the chosen oil in a carrier (such as jojoba, coconut, or sweet almond) and apply directly to affected patches two to three times daily until clear.
  • Hair care: For scalp ringworm (tinea capitis), add a few drops of diluted oil to shampoo or massage into the scalp before washing.
