Spirit of Place

Pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral, spirit of place is the energy signature of a location. Also called genii loci, these are the accumulated awareness and presence of a place, a grove, a rock formation, a river, a building.

What is Sacred Space?

Sacred space is an area cleansed of distractions and unwanted energy that might interfere with magickal work. Within this purified zone you may meditate, make offerings, and conduct spells or rituals. If you have a temple or dedicated worship area in your home, that becomes your permanent sacred space.

Sacred Space vs. Magick Circles

A magick circle is a consciously constructed area that partially overlaps both the material world and the divine world, creating a region "between the worlds." Sacred space is different. It is a place of peace and calm, but not necessarily between the worlds. Sacred space is what goes into the circle, or it can exist on its own.

When you are in sacred space, you can still interact with the world beyond it. It does not set up a barrier the way a circle does. When you create sacred space, you make the existing environment holy rather than constructing a whole new surrounding. You remain open to the good energies in the area instead of sealing yourself away from them.

When to Use Sacred Space

Sacred space works wonderfully when you want to create a harmonious atmosphere for gatherings, particularly if attendees follow different spiritual paths or if the space is unfamiliar to you. You can create it without anyone else's knowledge by purifying and harmonizing the energy of the area, removing distracting, harmful, or stale energy and leaving a positive, comfortable feeling instead.

Creating sacred space for others does not manipulate them or disrespect their own beliefs. You are simply offering them a peaceful and balanced environment. Try creating sacred space before a dinner gathering when everyone is tired and stressed. Watch how everyone relaxes in the serene energy.

The Four Steps of Creating Sacred Space

The process takes place both inside you and outside, both in your mind and in physical space.

1. Create (Internal Preparation)

Eliminate thoughts, feelings, and energies that would distract you, so your psyche is calm and everyday annoyances have been left behind. Before any magick, calm your spirit and mind. Put aside daily concerns and take a few minutes to relax your body. Your goal is to shift from ordinary consciousness to a more serene and elevated state. Even five minutes of meditation helps you get in the right frame of mind.

2. Cleanse (Physical Cleaning)

Clean up the area so you have a tidy, pleasant place in which to work. Physical clutter and dust create chaotic energy. Cleansing signifies your intention to honor this space and make it special. Sweep the floor, clear your altar or workspace, and tidy the general area. Put away loose papers, books, clothes, and other clutter. Once physical clutter is removed, clean and polish surfaces.

Cleanse yourself too. Many witches take a relaxing bath or shower before ritual. Though this focuses on physical cleansing, it also prepares you mentally and emotionally for magick.

3. Purify (Energetic Cleansing)

Purification is the magickal counterpart to cleansing. It removes negative energy rather than physical dirt. Methods include:

  • Sweeping counterclockwise with a besom (witch's broom)
  • Spritzing lavender scent or burning calming incense
  • Burning a smudge stick and wafting smoke around the area
  • Sprinkling small amounts of salt
  • Visualizing a ball of white light materializing in the center, then growing outward and driving away negativity
  • Walking counterclockwise spirals with a charged crystal, pushing unwanted energy away

4. Consecrate (Blessing the Space)

To consecrate means to sanctify. This definitive step makes the space sacred. Methods include:

Elemental Consecration: Bring into your space the energies of the four elements. A candle symbolizes fire, a stone signifies earth, a bowl of water stands for water, and smoking incense brings in air.

Energy Radiation: Stand in the center and feel yourself drawing up energy from the earth into your body's center. Connect with the energy of the sky flowing down into you. Allow these two energies to mingle and blend in your core, then envision yourself radiating this dual energy outward until it fills the space.

After Your Working

Sacred space does not need to be dismantled as a circle does. You can leave it and return whenever you choose, knowing your sanctuary awaits. To signal your work is finished, simply say something like: "This ritual is ended. I go in peace."

How long does sacred space last? It depends on location. If your sacred space is in your home, its sanctity lingers longer than a temporary space in a public area. The more often you use a space, the more you reinforce its sanctity.
