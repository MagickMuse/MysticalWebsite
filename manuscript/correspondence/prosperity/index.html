Defining Your Prosperity

What does prosperity actually mean to you? Is it a specific number in your bank account, or more of a feeling of comfort and security? Does abundance mean accumulating more, or simply having enough?

Before casting any prosperity spells, take time to clarify what these concepts truly mean in your life.

Unpacking Old Beliefs

Most of us absorbed our beliefs about money and wealth early in life, whether from family, religious teachings, or cultural messaging. If these inherited attitudes are blocking your path to abundance, they need to be examined and replaced with beliefs that actually serve you.

Reflection Questions

Complete these sentences quickly, without overthinking. Your first responses reveal your authentic feelings:

  1. I am happiest when...
  2. My wildest dream is to...
  3. Given the choice, I would most like to spend my time...
  4. I thoroughly enjoy...
  5. I would love to...
  6. I now spend my free time...
  7. I feel a sense of accomplishment when...
  8. My greatest passion is...
  9. One of my favorite hobbies is...

This list reveals what genuinely brings you joy. True abundance centers on happiness. Wealth without fulfillment serves little purpose.

Your Prosperity Wish List

Define what prosperity and abundance mean to you right now, in this moment. Write down ten things that would make you feel prosperous if you had them.

Gathering Symbolic Objects

Find a physical object to represent each item on your list. Choose these objects thoughtfully, as you will use them in your magickal practice.

Example: If flying is your wildest dream, you might choose a model airplane, a photo of an aircraft you would love to pilot, or even a simple toy plane. The key is selecting something that instantly connects you to the feeling of that experience.

Once you have all ten items, display them somewhere visible: your desk, a windowsill, or your altar. Every time you see them, they will reinforce your intentions and remind you of your abundance goals.

Cultivating Prosperity Consciousness

Prosperity consciousness is the deep belief that you deserve whatever you desire. It means knowing that your success does not diminish anyone else's, because the universe offers infinite abundance. You welcome all forms of wealth and use your resources to enrich both your life and the lives of others.

Questions to Consider

  • Do you feel worthy of prosperity?
  • Do you believe in your inherent value?
  • Do you trust the universe to meet your needs?
  • Do you frequently worry about finances or fear scarcity?
  • Do you hold beliefs like "money is the root of evil"?
  • Do you think spiritual growth and material wealth are incompatible?

Developing a prosperity consciousness is essential groundwork for successful abundance spells.

Shifting Limiting Thoughts

When you catch yourself thinking something that undermines your prosperity (like "I can't afford that"), mentally cross out that thought. Then consciously reframe it in positive terms.

Affirmations for Abundance

Create affirmations around prosperity and repeat them frequently. Say them aloud at least twelve times daily until your subconscious absorbs the message:

My life overflows with abundance of all kinds.
I have everything I need and desire.
All my bills are paid, and I have plenty to spare and share.
I am happy, healthy, wealthy, and fulfilled in every way.

Release attachment to how the prosperity will arrive. Trust that opportunities will emerge as you maintain your affirmations and belief in yourself.

Optimal Timing for Prosperity Spells

Moon Phase

Prosperity spells work best during a waxing moon. The intentions you set will grow alongside the moon's light, and your wealth will increase as the moon moves toward fullness.

Zodiac Timing

  • Taurus: This sign governs money, material possessions, and physical resources. Cast prosperity spells when the moon or sun is in Taurus for best results.
  • Scorpio: For matters involving shared finances, insurance, taxes, inheritance, or legal settlements, work when the moon or sun is in Scorpio.

Days of the Week

The most favorable days for prosperity magick:

  • Thursday (Jupiter's day): supports growth, expansion, and good fortune
  • Friday (Venus's day): supports attraction and abundance

Specific Correspondences

  • Lily, tiger lily specifically
  • Oak, white oak specifically
  • Iris, blue iris specifically
  • Sapphire, star sapphire specifically (for attracting prosperity)
  • Oyster, specifically for attraction
