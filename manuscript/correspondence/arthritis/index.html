Arthritis is the inflammation of one or more joints, marked by pain, stiffness, swelling, and reduced range of motion. The condition encompasses many forms including osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and gout-related joint inflammation. Aromatherapy supports arthritis by easing pain, calming inflammation, warming stiff tissue, and improving local circulation, working alongside medical treatment rather than replacing it.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before applying to skin, and patch-test before broader use. Avoid massage directly over inflamed, hot, or actively flared joints; use gentle skin oil or compress instead. Many of the oils listed here, including camphor, juniper berry, parsley seed, sweet marjoram, rosemary, thyme, turpentine, turmeric, Spanish sage, myrrh, vetivert, and nutmeg, are contraindicated in pregnancy. People with epilepsy should avoid camphor, rosemary, and Spanish sage. Those with kidney disease should avoid juniper berry and turpentine. Black pepper and clove are sensitizing and must be used at very low dilution. Turpentine should only be used in moderation and well diluted, if at all.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute three to five drops of essential oil per teaspoon of carrier oil and massage gently into the area surrounding the affected joint, working in slow circles outward to support circulation. Avoid pressing directly on actively inflamed joints.
  • Skin oil or lotion: Blend essential oils into an unscented base lotion at a one to two percent dilution and apply daily to stiff or aching joints. Useful for ongoing maintenance between flares.
  • Compress: Add four to six drops of essential oil to a bowl of warm water, soak a clean cloth, wring out, and lay over the affected joint for fifteen to twenty minutes. Use a cool compress on hot, inflamed joints and a warm compress on stiff, cold joints.
  • Bath: Disperse six to eight drops of essential oil in a carrier or unscented bath base, then add to a warm bath. Soak for fifteen to twenty minutes to ease whole-body stiffness and pain.
