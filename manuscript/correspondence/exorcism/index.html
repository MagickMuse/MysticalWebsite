Exorcism addresses spiritual interference ranging from oppressive presence (obsession) to complete displacement of personality (possession). The framework below emphasizes that successful exorcism depends on the practitioner's spiritual authority and connection to higher powers, not on ritual complexity alone.

Types of Spiritual Interference

Obsession is far more common than possession. It manifests as hostile presence affecting a person or place through paranoia, disturbing visions, physical phenomena like pressure on the chest during sleep, or pervasive malevolent atmosphere. The victim remains themselves but experiences external spiritual harassment.

Possession is rare and involves a spirit displacing the host's personality entirely. The victim exhibits behaviors, knowledge, or abilities completely foreign to their normal character. This requires the most serious response and professional coordination.

Critical Warnings

Never attempt possession exorcism without first exhausting all psychological and medical treatment options. Coordinate with medical professionals throughout the process. Videotape all sessions, maintain witnesses, and have emergency plans in place. Legal risks exist, including potential assault or murder charges if harm occurs. Multiple cases have resulted in deaths during exorcism attempts.

The battle is one of wills. The most elaborate and time-honored rites will fail if those performing them lack knowledge of their true will and access to higher powers. Faith that divine will manifests through the exorcist is what wins the confrontation.

Exorcism Framework

This is an outline, not a script. Anyone ready to perform exorcism should be able to construct their own ritual from this framework based on their tradition and spiritual relationships.

1. Assemble Your Team

Gather your working group and confirm everyone's commitment to see the work through. Exorcism is not undertaken lightly or alone.

2. Invoke Your Powers

Call upon the deities, angels, ancestors, or spiritual forces with whom you have established relationships. Your authority stems from these connections.

3. General Banishing

Perform a broad banishing of all evil and unwanted forces. Clear the space before addressing the specific entity.

4. Demand the Name

Command the interfering spirit to reveal its name, or assign it one. Naming gives you power over the entity and a specific target for your commands.

5. Command Departure

Order the entity to depart by the authority of the divine powers you serve. Speak with absolute conviction and unwavering will.

6. Bind and Destroy the Name

Write the entity's name on paper and burn it in a triangle of manifestation. The Triangular Hekatean Spirit Trap works well for this purpose.

7. Adjure by the Elements

Command departure by Earth, Air, Fire, Water, and Spirit. Call upon the totality of creation to enforce your will.

8. Protective Aftermath

Immediately following exorcism, establish protective wards around the space and person. Perform purifying baths. The victim should undergo spiritual cleansing to prevent return.

9. Monitor and Repeat if Necessary

Watch for signs of return over the following weeks. Some entities require multiple exorcisms before they are permanently banished. Remain vigilant and prepared to repeat the work.

On Authority Over Ritual

The ritual structure matters less than the person wielding it. Someone with genuine spiritual authority and connection to higher powers can succeed with the simplest words. Someone lacking that foundation will fail even with the most elaborate ceremony. Build your relationship with your spiritual allies before you ever need to call upon them in extremis.
