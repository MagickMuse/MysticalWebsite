Definition

Negative Magick refers to workings that draw upon chaos, destruction, or dark energies, often associated with the Dark Aspects of the Goddess and the God. While frequently misunderstood as purely harmful, negative magick encompasses a broader spectrum of transformative forces.

Dark Power

Dark Power consists of generally negative or chaos energies drawn from the Dark Aspects of the Goddess and the God. Despite their unsettling nature, these energies are:

  • Transformative, catalyzing deep change that gentler forces cannot achieve
  • Highly creative, destruction clears space for new creation
  • Innovative, breaking old patterns to allow new ones to emerge

Working with Shadow

Practitioners who work with negative magick often do so for:

  • Banishing, forcefully removing harmful influences
  • Binding, restricting harmful actions
  • Curse-breaking, reversing malevolent workings
  • Shadow integration, confronting and integrating rejected aspects of self
  • Transformation, when gentle approaches have failed

Ethical Considerations

Working with dark or negative energies requires heightened awareness of intention and consequence. The transformative power of these forces can be directed toward healing and growth or toward harm, the practitioner's ethics and clarity of purpose determine the outcome.

Timing

The most potent days for harmful or destructive workings are Tuesdays and Saturdays. Tuesday channels the aggressive, combative force of Mars, while Saturday draws on the cold, patient power of Saturn. Both planets align naturally with curses, hexes, and offensive spellcraft.

Any day of the week can serve for baneful magick, however, as long as the practitioner works within the appropriate planetary hour. Planetary hours divide each day into segments: the first twelve run from sunrise to sunset, and the final twelve from sunset to the following dawn. The opening planetary hour always matches the planet that rules the day, so dawn on Tuesday belongs to Mars, and dawn on Saturday belongs to Saturn. Working within those specific hours on their corresponding days creates the most concentrated window of power for destructive operations.
