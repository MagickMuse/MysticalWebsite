Headache covers the broad range of cranial pain caused by muscular tension, vascular dilation, fatigue, dehydration, congestion, eyestrain, hormonal shifts, hunger, or stress. Aromatherapy supports headache relief by easing muscular tension across the scalp, neck, and shoulders, calming the nervous system, opening congested sinuses where pressure contributes to pain, and cooling or warming the affected area as the headache type demands.

Dilute essential oils in a vegetable carrier oil before applying to the temples, forehead, neck, or shoulders. Keep oils well clear of the eyes. Avoid peppermint and eucalyptus on infants and small children. Rosemary, sage, and hyssop should be avoided in epilepsy and pregnancy. If a headache is severe, sudden, persistent, accompanied by visual disturbance, stiff neck, fever, or follows a head injury, seek medical attention rather than self-treating.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute 2 to 3 drops of essential oil per teaspoon of carrier oil and massage gently into the temples, forehead, base of the skull, neck, and shoulders. Slow circular strokes release the muscular tension that drives most everyday headaches.
  • Cold or warm compress: Add 3 to 5 drops of essential oil to a bowl of cold water (for throbbing or sinus-pressure headaches) or warm water (for tension headaches). Soak a cloth, wring it out, and lay it across the forehead, temples, or back of the neck. Refresh as the compress reaches body temperature.
  • Vaporization: Add 4 to 6 drops to an electric diffuser or aroma vaporizer to fill the room with relieving vapor. A drop on a tissue held near the face works for acute relief when a diffuser is not available.
