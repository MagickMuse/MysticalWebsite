A chakra is an energy node in the human body. In Sanskrit this one is called Manipura, and it is pictured as a yellow lotus of ten petals, ruled by the element of fire.

Colors

Yellow

Attributes

Will Power, Balance

Governs

Confidence, courage, and self-esteem

Chant sound: AW (like the oo in door)

Location: Situated at the level of your solar plexus

Things to do when focusing on this chakra:

  • For Yoga, the cat-cow exercise is the best for this chakra.
  • Each day, take a few moments to repeat the following affirmations several times as they will help your Self-Esteem:
    • I treat myself with Respect, Love, and Compassion.
    • I deserve and am open to the very best in life: Joy, Happiness, Love, and Prosperity.
    • I choose, today and every day, how I will think and how I will behave.

Chakra Healing

When the solar plexus chakra is working efficiently, you feel good about yourself and who you are. It is your seat of personal power. If your personal power is threatened or taken away, you are likely to experience problems with the areas looked after by this chakra. Feelings of guilt and shame are common ways of depleting personal power.

Physical Areas: The solar plexus chakra looks after the muscular system, eyesight, the skin, and the organs close to it, such as the stomach and gall bladder.

Stimulation: Spending time in the sunlight stimulates the solar plexus chakra. Thinking positive and supportive thoughts about yourself helps raise your self-esteem and stimulates your solar plexus chakra.

Body Systems

The solar plexus chakra governs the following body systems and organs:

  • Digestive system (stomach, upper intestines)
  • Liver
  • Gallbladder
  • Pancreas
  • Kidneys
  • Adrenal glands
  • Middle vertebrae
