Alopecia is the medical term for baldness or the loss of hair. Aromatherapy approaches to alopecia and general hair care focus on stimulating the scalp, balancing oil production, and supporting healthy follicles. Several essential oils have a long folk and clinical reputation for encouraging hair growth, conditioning the scalp, and soothing common hair and scalp complaints.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil or shampoo base before applying to the scalp. Patch test before broader use, and discontinue if irritation develops.

Suggested Application of Oils

Use these oils for alopecia and hair care via the following methods:

  • Skin oil or lotion (massaged into the scalp in a carrier oil)
  • Hair care preparations (shampoos, rinses, conditioners, scalp tonics)
