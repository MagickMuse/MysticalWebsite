Newborn Cleansing Baths

Across many folk traditions, the newly delivered baby is welcomed to Earth with a ritual magickal bath, usually administered immediately after birth. These baths serve as both the baby's first spiritual cleansing and a special protection against demons or malevolent forces drawn to new life.

Specific Correspondences

  • Owls, burrowing owl specifically
  • Cattle, cow specifically
  • Frogs, tadpole specifically
