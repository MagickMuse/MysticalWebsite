Anxiety is a state of persistent worry, restlessness, and bodily tension. It often shows up as a racing mind, shallow breathing, muscle tightness, sleep disruption, or digestive unease, and it can layer onto every other condition in this index. Aromatherapy supports the nervous system through oils that quiet the sympathetic response, soften emotional spikes, and bring the body back to a parasympathetic, restful baseline. These oils are best used as part of a broader plan that includes professional care when anxiety is severe, chronic, or interferes with daily life.

Use on intact skin only. Dilute to 2 to 2.5 percent in a carrier oil (12 to 15 drops per ounce) for massage and bath blends. Patch test before broader use, especially with floral absolutes and resinous oils. Avoid hyssop, jasmine, and juniper during pregnancy. Hyssop is contraindicated in epilepsy. Use sparingly with ylang ylang, since the heady scent can trigger headaches or nausea at high concentrations.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Blend 12 to 15 drops in 30 ml carrier oil. Apply to the chest, neck, shoulders, and abdomen with slow, grounding strokes. Especially effective before bed or after a stressful event.
  • Bath: Disperse 4 to 6 drops in a tablespoon of carrier oil, milk, or unscented bath base, then add to a full tub. Soak for 15 to 20 minutes with warm (not hot) water.
  • Vaporization: Diffuse 3 to 5 drops in a water diffuser for 30 to 60 minutes in a well-ventilated room. Use during meditation, journaling, breathwork, or whenever the body needs to downshift.
