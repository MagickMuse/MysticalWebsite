Constipation is congestion of the bowels with incomplete or infrequent bowel action, and sluggish digestion often accompanies it. Aromatherapy supports the digestive tract through warming, carminative, and antispasmodic oils that stimulate peristalsis, ease abdominal tension, and encourage healthy elimination.

Massage and bath applications are the recommended routes. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before applying to skin. Use 1 to 3 percent dilution for adult abdominal massage, working in slow clockwise circles to follow the natural direction of peristalsis. Bath blends should disperse 5 to 10 drops in a tablespoon of carrier oil or full-fat milk before adding to warm water. Avoid phototoxic oils (bitter orange and lovage root) before sun exposure, use tarragon and nutmeg sparingly due to their toxicity considerations, and consult a qualified practitioner during pregnancy, while nursing, or with chronic bowel conditions.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute the chosen oil to 1 to 3 percent in a vegetable carrier oil and massage the abdomen in slow clockwise circles for several minutes, ideally morning and night. The motion follows the colon's natural path and stimulates peristaltic action.
  • Bath: Disperse 5 to 10 drops of essential oil in a tablespoon of carrier oil, full-fat milk, or unscented body wash, then add to a warm bath. Soak for fifteen to twenty minutes to relax the abdominal muscles and ease digestive tension.
