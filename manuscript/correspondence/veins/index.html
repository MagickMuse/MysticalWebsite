Lemon and lime essential oils are phototoxic. Avoid sun exposure on treated skin for 12 to 72 hours after application. Cypress should be properly diluted and is best avoided internally during pregnancy. Patch test yarrow before topical use, as it can cause sensitization in some individuals. When working over visible varicosities, use gentle upward strokes toward the heart and avoid direct downward pressure.

This correspondence covers veins generally and varicose veins specifically. The oils listed here support circulation, tone vessel walls, and ease the heaviness and discomfort associated with weakened or distended veins.

Suggested Application

  • Skin oil or lotion: Dilute the chosen oil in a carrier such as sweet almond, jojoba, or arnica-infused oil, and massage into the affected areas with gentle upward strokes toward the heart.
  • Compress: Add a few drops to a bowl of cool water, soak a cloth, wring it out, and apply to the affected area for relief from heaviness, swelling, or aching.

Oils for Varicose Veins

  • Cypress Essential Oil (S, C)
  • Lemon Essential Oil (S, C)
  • Lime Essential Oil (S, C)
  • Neroli Essential Oil (S, C)
  • Yarrow Essential Oil (S, C)
