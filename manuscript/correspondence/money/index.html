Money magick is one of the most commonly practiced forms of spellwork, and for good reason. Financial concerns touch nearly every aspect of daily life. Whether you are working to pay off debt, attract a new income stream, or simply ensure your bills are covered, money spells offer a way to align your energy with your financial goals.

Money as a correspondence overlaps heavily with Prosperity and Abundance, but carries a more specific and tangible focus. Where prosperity encompasses a broad sense of having enough, money magick zeroes in on currency, income, and material wealth.

Working with Money Magick

Before casting any money spells, clarify exactly what you need. Vague intentions like "I want more money" are less effective than specific ones like "I need $500 for car repairs by the end of the month." The more precise your intention, the more focused your energy becomes.

Consider what is blocking your flow of income. Old beliefs about money being evil or undeserved can act as energetic barriers. Examine your relationship with wealth honestly. Replace limiting thoughts with affirmations that support your financial goals.

Common Tools and Materials

Several items appear frequently in money spells across the Manuscript:

  • Green candles for attracting wealth and financial growth
  • Gold candles for success and solar energy related to abundance
  • Coins and currency as sympathetic links to the money you wish to attract
  • Lodestones for drawing money toward you
  • Cinnamon for fast results and fiery attraction energy
  • Basil for steady wealth building
  • Bay leaves for writing wishes and burning them to release intentions
  • Patchouli and bergamot oils for anointing candles and tools

Timing Your Money Spells

Moon Phase

Money spells work best during a waxing moon, when energies of increase and growth are strongest. Begin new money workings as close to the new moon as possible so your intention builds alongside the moon's light.

Zodiac Timing

  • Taurus: Governs money, possessions, and material security. Cast money spells when the moon or sun is in Taurus for the strongest results.
  • Capricorn: Supports long term financial planning, career advancement, and disciplined wealth building.

Days of the Week

  • Thursday (Jupiter's day): Supports expansion, generosity, and financial growth
  • Sunday (Sun's day): Supports success, vitality, and achievement

Sympathetic Magick and Money

The Law of Similars teaches that like attracts like. Surrounding yourself with symbols of wealth sends a clear message to the universe about what you wish to attract. Keep coins on your altar, place a bowl of rice near your front door, or carry a small sachet of prosperity herbs in your wallet.

Color plays a powerful role in money magick. Green represents growth, fertility, and financial gain. Gold connects to solar energy, success, and abundance. Incorporating these colors into your candles, altar cloths, and spell components strengthens your workings.

Maintaining the Flow

Money magick is most effective when paired with practical action. Cast your spell, then actively pursue opportunities. Apply for jobs, follow up on leads, and remain open to unexpected avenues of income. Magick opens doors, but you must walk through them.
