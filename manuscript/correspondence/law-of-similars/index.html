Correspondence Grimoire

Law of Similars

The Law of Similars (also called the Law of Sympathy or Doctrine of Signatures) is one of the foundational principles of magickal correspondence. It states that colors, shapes, and characteristics of a plant, stone, or other object give clues to its magickal function.

How It Works

According to this law, objects that look like or resemble something share an energetic connection with it. This resemblance creates a sympathetic link that can be used in magick.

Examples:

  • Red stones might be used in magickal cures for blood problems (color similarity)
  • Heart-shaped leaves might be part of a love spell (shape similarity)
  • Walnuts, which resemble brains, have been used for mental clarity and memory (form similarity)
  • Eyebright, a flower with markings resembling eyes, has been used for vision issues

Why It Works

Symbolic images affect the subconscious in specific ways. Advertisers understand this; it's no accident that certain products use specific shapes and colors to evoke particular feelings and associations. Magick works with these same symbolic connections intentionally.

Applications in Practice

Sympathetic Magick

The Law of Similars is the foundation of sympathetic magick, the principle that like affects like. When you work with an object that resembles your target or intention, you create a sympathetic link.

Poppets and Dolls

One of the most common applications is the poppet, a doll made to represent a person. What you do to the poppet symbolically affects the person it represents. This can be used for:

  • Healing: Bandaging the doll's injured area while sending healing energy
  • Protection: Wrapping the doll in protective materials
  • Binding: Restricting the doll's movement to restrict harmful actions

Correspondences

The entire system of magickal correspondences is built on the Law of Similars:

  • Colors correspond to intentions (green for money, pink for love)
  • Shapes correspond to purposes (circles for protection, triangles for manifestation)
  • Natural objects correspond to their resemblances (roots for grounding, feathers for air/communication)

Working with the Law

When choosing materials for spellwork, ask yourself:

  • What does this object look like?
  • What does its color suggest?
  • What does its shape resemble?
  • What qualities does it naturally possess?

These answers point toward the object's magickal correspondences and best uses.
