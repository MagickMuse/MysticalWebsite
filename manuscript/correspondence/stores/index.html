What Are "Stores"?

In the context of spiritual hygiene and household magic, stores refers to any place in the home where provisions are kept: pantries, refrigerators, cabinets, stash boxes, shelves, spice racks, and closets.

Spirits, both welcome and unwelcome, are drawn to these areas. Care should be taken to prevent unwelcome visitors (whether metaphysical or literal) from moving in.

Spiritual Significance

Storerooms represent survival and abundance. They hold the basic material of life: food, supplies, tools. In pre-industrial societies, an empty storeroom meant death; a full one meant security, celebration, and the ability to share with others.

For this reason, storerooms have attracted powerful protective spiritual attention across world cultures.

Zeus Khthesios: The Greek Storeroom God

The ancient Greeks installed Zeus Khthesios ("Zeus of the Property") in their storerooms. Housed in a small two-handled earthenware vessel called an akadiskos, with handles wreathed in white wool and saffron thread, he was fed water, olive oil, and fresh fruit. His job was to protect and actively increase household provisions.

Protective Strategies

Inside the Stores: Protect What You Have

Place charms inside your storage spaces focused on preservation and protection:

  • Fava bean charm (Italian/Sicilian): Place dried fava beans in your pantry as a charm against poverty. When all crops failed in Sicily and only fava beans survived the famine, the people lived. As a magical charm, the fava bean grants peace of mind that there will always be enough, if only just enough.
  • Protective figures: An image of Zeus Khthesios, a protective deity, or a home guardian.

Outside the Stores: Attract More

Place charms on the outside of your storage spaces focused on attraction and increase:

  • Lodestones and magnets: Natural magnets draw things to them, a new partner, more money, a hard-to-find item. Even the refrigerator magnets on your kitchen appliances can be used intentionally to draw in whatever makes life worth living.
  • Abundance symbols: Cornucopia imagery, prosperity plants, coins.

Related Spirits

Spirits of various traditions are associated with storerooms and provisions. In addition to Zeus Khthesios, consider working with spirits of abundance, harvest, and protection from your own ancestral tradition.
