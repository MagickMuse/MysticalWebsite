Finding Your Glamour in Your Shadow

Glamour magick is the art of stepping into your truest self, discovering what makes you intoxicating and exciting to yourself and others, and presenting that to the world. Unlike cultural glamour with its static expectations about appearance and behavior, magickal glamour works with traits you already possess, focusing and magnifying them through intentional practice.

The Nature of Glamour

Glamour is the magick of everyday life: thousands of tiny choices you make throughout the day to influence how you are seen by yourself, your deities, your spirits, your ancestors, the universe, and other people.

On a practical level, glamour encompasses:

  • How you speak to others
  • The scent you choose to wear (or not wear)
  • How you dress and present yourself
  • When and why you choose to be charming or assertive
  • Your approach to social interactions

Magickally, glamour is the smallest pieces of your witchcraft weaving together into a tapestry: from introspective shadow work, to candle magick with personal sigils, to the protection wards you develop for yourself.

Glamour and Shadow Work

Both shadow work and glamour magick require inner alchemy. Everyone has a tiny terrarium inside themselves where everything bright and shadowy resides. How can you control your will and your magick if you do not know what is inside yourself?

If you can figure out where the aspects of your shadow are, you can reframe them both practically and magickally in your life and make them more useful to your magick. Anger is part of passion. Fear can become fuel. The parts of yourself you find less desirable can be woven into your glamour when properly understood and transformed.

Fortuna's Wheel

The goddess Fortuna rules over matters of fate with her ever-turning wheel divided into four parts:

  • Regnabo (I shall reign)
  • Regno (I reign)
  • Regnavi (I have reigned)
  • Sum sine regno (I have no kingdom)

This is the human experience: moving from the awesome parts to the awful parts and back again. Your work as a witch is to lengthen the awesome parts and shorten the difficult ones. To shorten your time in difficulty, you must have enough time to learn the lesson and heal from the event. Shadow work during difficult times gives you focus and helps turn the wheel back toward the light.

Practical Applications

Strengthening Your Glamour

  • Work on your wit with a friend
  • Add honey to morning tea with the intention that your words will be honeyed
  • Take classes that develop skills aligned with your glamour
  • Work with deities, ancestors, and spirits to enhance your natural gifts

Transforming Shadow Energy

  • Use anger as passion for creating art
  • Channel difficult emotions into physical movement
  • Bake with cinnamon to bring change faster
  • Create crystal grids to express and release shadow feelings

The Bonfire Within

Remember the roots of your glamour. Remember the darkest parts of your shadow. You have seen them before and you will see them again. When you are at your darkest point, that is when you seek your true self reflected back. That is your glamour. That is your shadow. That is the ever-blazing bonfire within you, and it is always yours.
