🧵Copper Garter Arthritis Healing Spell

Aches and pains is a broad category covering muscular soreness, articular stiffness, and the dull, lingering discomfort that follows exertion, tension, or chronic strain. Aromatherapy supports the body through warming, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory oils applied externally to ease tension, encourage circulation, and quiet pain at the surface and in the muscle bed beneath.

Use only on intact skin. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before topical use, and patch test before broader application. Several oils in this list carry pregnancy, epilepsy, hypertension, or sensitive-skin contraindications, including camphor, sage, rosemary, hyssop-type oils, peppermint, spike lavender, nutmeg, and turpentine. Verify each oil's individual safety profile before use.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Blend at 2 to 3 percent dilution, roughly 12 to 18 drops per 30 mL of carrier oil, and work into the affected muscles or joints with firm, circular strokes.
  • Compress: Add 4 to 6 drops to a bowl of hot water, soak a cloth, wring out, and apply to the painful area. Cover with a dry towel and rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
  • Bath: Add 6 to 8 drops, dispersed in a teaspoon of carrier oil or unscented bath base, to a full tub of warm water and soak for 15 to 20 minutes.
