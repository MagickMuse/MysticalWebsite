Guardian Animals

Animal allies can be petitioned for safety. The most powerful allies are always your own personal ones. If you do not know who they are, ask them to make themselves known in your dreams and visualizations, or seek professional shamanic or spirit working advice. In the meantime, certain animals will provide magickal guardian services when asked. Magickal guardian animals tend to be fierce, for the same reason a Doberman is a more popular guard dog than a golden retriever.
