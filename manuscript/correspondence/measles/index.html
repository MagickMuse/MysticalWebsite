Measles is a highly contagious viral infection that produces fever, respiratory symptoms, and a characteristic rash. Essential oils can ease the discomfort of the rash, support the body through fever, and gently soothe the respiratory tract while the illness runs its course.

Measles is a serious viral illness. Essential oil applications are supportive, not curative, and should not replace medical care. Keep oils well diluted on irritated skin, avoid the eyes and mucous membranes, and consult a qualified practitioner before using on children, during pregnancy, or alongside other treatments.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Skin oil or lotion: Dilute 2 to 3 drops of essential oil in a tablespoon of carrier oil such as sweet almond or jojoba, and dab gently over the rash to calm irritation and protect skin integrity.
  • Bath: Add 6 to 8 drops of essential oil dispersed in a tablespoon of carrier oil or whole milk to a lukewarm bath to soothe the skin, reduce fever, and ease body discomfort.
  • Inhalation (steam): Add 2 to 3 drops of essential oil to a bowl of steaming water, cover the head with a towel, and breathe gently for 5 to 10 minutes to ease congestion and cough.
  • Vaporization: Diffuse 4 to 6 drops in the sick room to support the respiratory tract, freshen the air, and create a calmer recovery environment.

Oils to Use to Address Measles

  • Bergamot Essential Oil
  • Eucalyptus Essential Oil
  • Spike Lavender Essential Oil
  • Lavender Essential Oil
  • Tea Tree Essential Oil
