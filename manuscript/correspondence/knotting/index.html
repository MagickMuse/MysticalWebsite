Overview

Knots are so intrinsically and anciently connected with magic that tying knots was once synonymous with magic in general. The art goes back to the Babylonians and likely much further into unrecorded history.

The Hebrew word for amulet, kame'a, has a root meaning "to bind." Knotting is the original binding spell.

Principles

Any intention or force can be tied or controlled by the knot. What separates the magical knot from tying a shoelace is the focus and intention of the one making the knot. However, the act of tying a shoelace can be transformed into a magical act: tie a child's sneaker while focusing on blessing and protecting as you pull the knot tight.

In a knot charm, you are focusing and concentrating your energy with every knot.

Materials

Materials used in knot charms include:

  • Thread and cord
  • Plant stems
  • Metal wire
  • Animal or human hair

Uses

Knots can be used for positive or malevolent intent. Common uses include:

  • Love spells
  • Healing spells
  • Wealth spells
  • Weather spells (controlling wind and rain)

Knots are used to bind intent and also to remove spells. They are common Celtic, Chinese, Egyptian, and Scythian motifs.

Cultural Legacy

The concept of the "lover's knot" lies in ancient love knot magic. Some believe the inspiration for the ring arose from knot magic.

Animal Patron

The animal patron of knot magic is the spider.

Basic Knot Spell

  1. Focus on your goal and desire while holding a red cord in your hands.
  2. Tie a knot.
  3. Wrap it in fabric, place it in a magic box, and keep it in a safe, secure location.

Life Binding: Child Protection Knot Charms

A widespread practice across cultures uses a knotted cord or thread tied around a child's body to protect against illness, the Evil Eye, soul loss, and spirit interference. Focus blessings, hopes, and protective wishes into every knot as it is tied.

  • India: Wrap a black thread around the child's waist, concentrating blessings into the closing knot.
  • Jewish: Wrap red thread around the child's wrist, focusing wishes of good health, protection, and safety into every knot tied.
  • Romany: Wrap red thread or a used violin string around the baby or child's wrist, focusing hopes, blessings, and desires into every knot.

A Traditional Knot Incantation

This incantation accompanies the tying of nine knots:

By knot of one, the spell's begun!
By knot of two, my spell comes true
By knot of three, so mote it be
By knot of four, power I store
By knot of five, my magic is alive
By knot of six, this spell I fix
By knot of seven, this spell I leaven
By knot of eight, it is fate
By knot of nine, what's wished is mine!
