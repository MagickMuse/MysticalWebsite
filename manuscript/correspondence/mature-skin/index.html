Wrinkles and mature skin reflect the natural slowing of cell turnover, collagen loss, and reduced elasticity that come with age, sun exposure, and dehydration. The oils below are rejuvenating, regenerating, and toning, traditionally used to soften lines, support cell renewal, and restore suppleness to aging skin.

🧴
Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before topical use. Patch test before broader application. Avoid sun exposure for at least twelve hours after applying expressed citrus oils such as mandarin. Those who are pregnant, nursing, or sensitive to specific botanicals (jasmine, clary sage, rose, sandalwood) should consult a qualified practitioner.

Suggested Application of Oils

Massage: Blend 5 to 10 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier (jojoba, sweet almond, rosehip, or apricot kernel) and massage into the face, neck, and décolleté with gentle upward strokes.

Skin oil or lotion: Add 5 to 10 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier or unscented lotion and apply twice daily after cleansing.

Flower water: Mist or compress with a matching hydrosol over the face to tone and hydrate before sealing with a carrier oil.

Bath: Disperse 5 to 8 drops total in a tablespoon of carrier oil or unscented bath base, then add to a warm bath and soak.
