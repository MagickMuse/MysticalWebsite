💪Slack Tissue

Type
Medical

Slack tissue describes loose, lax skin and underlying connective tissue that has lost firmness, often from age, weight changes, hormonal shifts, or reduced circulation. Astringent and tonic essential oils can support skin tone and tissue firmness when used regularly in topical care.

⚠️
Safety: Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before topical use, and patch test before broader application. Several oils listed are phototoxic (grapefruit, lime, black pepper), avoid sun exposure for 12-72 hours after application. Avoid juniper, marjoram, and rosemary during pregnancy. Black pepper requires very low dilution (no more than 0.5%).

Suggested Application

Apply via massage, skin oil/lotion, or bath.
