Spirit-Summoning Dreams

Dreams are among the most accessible venues for spirit communication. Spirits of all kinds, including ancestors, allies, and familiars, frequently make their first approach through dreams. Keeping a dream journal is therefore a valuable practice for recognizing and developing these connections over time.

Remembering Dreams

Dreams are elusive by nature. Even a dream that feels permanently engraved in memory at the moment of waking will fade within hours, often within minutes. The single most effective practice for retaining dreams is to record them immediately upon waking, before doing anything else.

Keep a pen and paper, or a dedicated dream journal, within reach of the bed. The ideal state for recording dreams is a semi-waking state: just conscious enough to write without fully shaking off sleep. Some dreamers learn to write in the dark or with their eyes closed. Legibility matters less than speed. The goal is to capture the dream before it dissolves. Transcribe the rough notes into a proper journal once fully awake.

Waking naturally, without an alarm, significantly increases dream retention. If an alarm is necessary, a gentle transition, such as soft music gradually increasing in volume, preserves more of the dream than a sudden, jarring sound. Abrupt physical movement upon waking also causes dreams to vanish faster.

Botanicals and ritual baths can further extend the window in which dreams remain accessible after waking.

Financial Dreams

Financially prophetic dreams can take several forms: visions offering investment guidance, solutions to active financial problems, or lucky numbers. The most effective botanicals for stimulating this type of dream include basil, chamomile, cinnamon, coriander, High John the Conqueror, parsley, and vervain. These can be used in a dream pillow, charm bag, or covered herb box opened at bedtime.

Erotic Dreams

Certain botanicals stimulate sensual dreams and erotic pleasure during sleep in the same way aphrodisiacs enhance wakefulness. These plants may be used in any of the following ways: fill a dream pillow, place loose in a covered box by the bed and inhale before sleep, infuse and add to a bath (excluding tagetes), or simply place fresh cut flowers or living plants close enough to waft their fragrance over the bed.

Botanicals associated with erotic dreaming: agarwood, anise, cardamom, catnip, cloves, coriander seed, damiana, gardenia, henna, hibiscus, jasmine, myrrh, orchid, rose, marigold (tagetes), tuberose, vanilla, vervain.

Dream Protection

Sleep involves a genuine surrender of ordinary defenses. Magickal protections for the sleep state address spiritual vulnerabilities specifically, not nightmares or disturbing dream content. The spells in this category are designed to ward off spiritual threats during the night and are appropriate for both nervous and confident dreamers. General protection workings make a good complement.

The most effective botanical protectors for sleep include mugwort, wormwood, and yarrow, all of which provide spiritual shielding while also enhancing dream quality. Iron is considered the single most powerful material protection against nocturnal threats.

Fragrant botanicals that confer protective influence during sleep include coconut, frankincense, gardenia, hyacinth, mimosa, and myrrh. These work best as real plant material placed beside the bed, cut fruit or bouquets, or warming in an aroma burner. Synthetic versions of these scents lack the protective potency of the genuine botanical.

Dream Potions

Dream potions are beverages consumed just before bedtime to promote dreaming. Do not confuse them with medicinal sleep aids: their purpose is to stimulate and enhance the dream state, not to sedate. Most are ancient formulas. Drink them just before going to sleep.

Dream Pillows

Dream pillows are herb-filled bags or small pillows placed at the head during sleep. Their fragrance passively supports dreaming throughout the night. For a quick version without sewing, place dream herbs in a white handkerchief and tie the bundle closed with a red, blue, or silver ribbon. A drawstring bag filled with dream herbs works equally well. Muslin releases fragrance more intensely than flannel and is preferred when strong scent is the goal.

Dream Incubation

Dream incubation is the practice of requesting a specific dream. Rather than receiving whatever arises naturally during sleep, the dreamer actively petitions for a particular type of vision, answer, or encounter. The technique becomes easier with practice and should not be abandoned after a single failed attempt. Different methods work better for different people; experimentation is part of the process.

Dream incubation is an ancient practice with roots in the healing temples of the ancient world. Patients at these shrines would undergo preparatory rituals and then sleep within the sacred space, expecting to receive healing directly in the dream, a diagnosis from the resident deity, or a visitation by a spirit in the form of a snake. The technique has since expanded far beyond healing and can be used to seek information, guidance, or visions on any matter.

Dream incubation differs from prophetic dreaming in its active intent: the dreamer specifies what is needed rather than receiving what the dream state offers spontaneously.

Flying Ointments

Witches' flying ointments were used historically to facilitate astral travel and spirit flight. The most commonly cited ingredients include: aconite (wolfsbane), belladonna (deadly nightshade), betel (not yet in Plants database), cantharides or Spanish fly (not yet in Materials database), cinquefoil (five-finger grass), foxglove, hashish, hellebore root, hemlock, hemp, mandrake, opium, parsley, poplar, poppies, soot (not yet in Materials database), thorn apple (datura), tobacco, and water parsnip, believed to refer to water hemlock. Baby fat also appears in some historical recipes (not yet in Materials database). Most of these ingredients are poisonous or otherwise toxic and are listed here for historical reference only.

Insomnia

Without adequate rest, the dream state becomes inaccessible. Persistent insomnia also erodes broader wellbeing: physical resilience, mental clarity, and the quiet receptivity that supports both psychic sensitivity and magickal work. Pharmaceutical sleep aids often produce unconsciousness without genuine dreaming. Traditional botanical and magickal approaches work differently, easing the body and mind toward natural sleep without overriding the dream state. Most plants used for sleep carry real physiological effects alongside their magickal ones; consult a healthcare provider before using them regularly, especially where insomnia is tied to an underlying condition.

Dream Baths

Dream baths are designed to be relaxing, sensuous, and soothing, their purpose is to ease the body and mind toward sleep and receptive dreaming. Take them just before going to bed. Use warm water rather than hot or cold, as warmth is generally more conducive to sleep and the dream state. Do not toss botanicals over yourself; simply recline in the water and relax. Burning candles and dream incense in the bathroom during the bath can deepen the effect.

When adding essential oils to a bath, always do so after the water has been drawn and the temperature adjusted. With essential oils, less is always more: begin with the smallest amount and adjust only to what your nose finds pleasant.

Nightmare Crystals

Certain crystals repel and relieve nightmares. Blue crystals are particularly effective, especially angelite, lapis lazuli, and turquoise. Black crystals are generally useful, with the possible exception of onyx. Chrysolite (olivine or peridot), citrine, and topaz are also recommended.

Onyx is a stone of unpredictable personal reaction. It is used to halt nightmares, but occasionally causes them. Those who habitually wear onyx and also habitually suffer bad dreams should remove the stone and observe any change.

To access a crystal's nightmare-repelling power, wear it during sleep so it stays in contact with the skin, place it on or beneath the pillow, tuck one or more stones into a small flannel drawstring bag worn across the chest or placed beneath the cheek, or cast a circle of gemstones around or on the bed.

Dream Travel

Astral travel can be accomplished consciously through the dream state. Before sleep, fix clearly in your mind where you want to go and whom you want to visit. Visualize the destination in detail, and equally visualize yourself, your appearance, your clothing. Charge yourself with a specific mission rather than drifting without intent. Keep a dedicated diary of your journeys, noting details immediately upon waking. Practice is essential; the skill develops over time.

Waking Dreams

A waking dream is a consciously directed dream entered at the threshold of sleep. Before lying down, decide where you will go and what will happen. As you feel yourself falling asleep, walk deliberately into the dream you have already envisioned.

The technique can be developed through practice with images: step into a tarot card, a painting, or any safe and appealing image, then practice returning. Choose images that feel welcoming rather than unsettling. Facility with entering and exiting at will is as important as the initial entry.
