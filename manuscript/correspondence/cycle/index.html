Birth, Death, and Return

The cyclical nature of existence extends beyond seasonal or lunar patterns. Many practitioners hold that souls move through repeating phases of physical incarnation, death, rest in other realms, and eventual return to new bodies. This framework positions earthly life as purposeful, a place where specific growth occurs. When all intended development is complete, the soul graduates to a state of peace and restoration.
