Lughnasadh

A blessing empowers and charges an object, tool, or material with specific energy. Blessings may come from spiritual sources, elemental forces, or the practitioner's own focused intention.

Blessings of the Elements

Each element can provide ritual blessings for spell tools and materials. Whatever receives an elemental blessing is empowered and charged with that element's particular energy. Choose the appropriate element for the working, or combine multiple blessings as desired.

  • Air -- Pass the object through incense smoke or smudge it with a smudge stick
  • Earth -- Sprinkle with dirt, particularly specially chosen dirt such as crossroads dirt or soil gathered from a shrine or holy place
  • Fire -- Pass through a flame or hold within the flame briefly (only for materials that will not burn or sustain damage)
  • Metal -- Allow the object to rest overnight atop the specific metal whose properties are desired
  • Water -- Sprinkle with regular water or magickally charged formula water (only for materials that will not be damaged by moisture)
