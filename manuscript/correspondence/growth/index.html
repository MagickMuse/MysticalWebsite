Growth is the energy of expansion, development, and becoming. In magickal practice, growth encompasses not only the physical (plants reaching toward sunlight, bodies healing and strengthening) but also the spiritual, emotional, and intellectual unfolding of the practitioner.

Elements

Growth is rooted in Earth and Water. Earth provides the fertile ground and stable foundation from which all things rise. Water nourishes and sustains, carrying nutrients to where they are needed most. Together, these elements create the conditions for steady, organic expansion.

How It Works

The Law of Similars connects growth magick to anything that naturally expands, sprouts, or multiplies. Seeds, young plants, eggs, and spirals all carry the energy signature of growth. The cosmic web recognizes these patterns and amplifies them when a practitioner works with intention.

Growth magick is patient magick. Unlike workings that seek immediate results, growth spells unfold over time. The practitioner plants the seed of intention and then tends it with consistent energy, trusting the process even when progress is not yet visible.

Timing

The waxing moon is the most powerful phase for growth workings, as the moon itself is growing toward fullness. Spring is the ideal season, when the natural world models the energy of new life pushing through the soil. Workings begun on a Thursday (Jupiter, expansion) or Friday (Venus, flourishing) carry additional momentum.

In Practice

Growth magick often involves living things: planting a seed charged with intention, tending a garden as a form of ongoing spell work, or working with fresh herbs and green candles. Burying a written intention in soil and watering it regularly is a simple but effective technique.

The practitioner should also examine what might be blocking growth. Sometimes the most powerful growth magick involves removing obstacles: clearing stagnant energy, releasing old patterns, or cutting ties that hold one in place.

Related Intentions

Growth shares strong connections with Abundance, Healing, and Transformation. Growth and abundance feed each other naturally. Healing often requires growth of new tissue, new habits, or new perspectives. And transformation is simply growth taken to its fullest expression.
