🧊Sexual Overactivity

Type

Definition

Sexual overactivity describes a persistent state in which sexual urges or behaviours occupy a disproportionate share of attention, energy, or daily life, often accompanied by restlessness, distractibility, irritability, or the inability to settle into other activities. The pattern may arise from hormonal imbalance, nervous overstimulation, certain medications, or unresolved emotional tension. Aromatherapy supports this state with calming, mildly anaphrodisiac, and oestrogenic-leaning oils that quiet nervous drive, settle the endocrine background, and help the body return to a more even rhythm.

Persistent sexual overactivity, especially when accompanied by intrusive thoughts, distress, or behaviours that compromise relationships, work, or safety, warrants evaluation by a clinician or mental-health professional, since the pattern can signal hormonal, neurological, or psychiatric conditions that benefit from direct treatment.
Hops is narcotic in excessive amounts and must be avoided by those suffering from depression. Both Hops and sweet marjoram must be avoided during pregnancy. Dilute well, patch test before broad use, and discontinue if irritation or unwanted sedation develops.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Add 5 drops of essential oil to a tablespoon of carrier oil and work over the lower back, abdomen, chest, and shoulders with slow, settling strokes. Daily massage during the calmer evening hours soothes nervous excitation and supports the body's return to balanced drive.
  • Bath: Add 6 to 10 drops of essential oil to a full bath of warm water, agitate the surface to disperse, and soak for 15 to 20 minutes. Warm-water immersion eases muscular tension, calms the nervous system, and lets the sedative and anaphrodisiac qualities of the oils work through inhalation and skin absorption together.

Oils to Use to Address Sexual Overactivity

  • Marjoram Essential Oil
