Curses are the dark mirror of blessings, harmful wishes or words spoken with intent to cause misfortune, injury, or suffering. The power of the spoken word lies at the heart of curse magick.

A curse is a magickal, ritual action intended to cause harm to a person, persons, corporations, public figures, or governments, usually because of perceived (or actual) wrongdoing on the target's part. This may entail a plea to gods, spirits, saints, or other nonhuman entities, or it may involve any magickal tools, actions, or motions. Curses often seek help from external sources, distinguishing them from hexes which rely on the practitioner's own will.

Containment Principle

Curses contain malevolence to the sphere of the generator of the negative energy. In other words, the harm a curse invokes is tied to and directed back toward whoever initiated the negative intent. This principle underlies many curse-breaking and reversal workings, the energy returns to its source.

The Power of Spoken Words

Curses demonstrate the profound magical power inherent in human speech. Words shape reality, and when spoken with intensity, emotion, and intent, they become potent magical forces. A curse is essentially a blessing in reverse, instead of invoking good fortune, it calls down harm.

Traditionally Feared Cursers

Certain individuals were historically believed to possess particularly powerful cursing abilities:

Witches: Practitioners of magick were feared for their ability to curse as much as they were sought for their healing and blessing powers. The witch's curse became a central theme in witch-hunt propaganda.

Menstruating Women: In many cultures, menstruating women were believed to have dangerous magickal power. Their words and glances during this time were thought capable of causing tremendous harm, particularly to crops, livestock, and male fertility.

Dying People: Those at the threshold between life and death were believed to have access to supernatural power. A dying person's curse was considered especially potent and nearly impossible to reverse.

Death Curses and Execution Protocols

The fear of death curses led to specific execution protocols during the witch-hunt era. Authorities took elaborate precautions to prevent condemned witches from cursing their executioners, judges, or the community:

  • Witches were often gagged or had their tongues cut out before execution
  • Executioners worked quickly to prevent the condemned from speaking final words
  • Some were strangled or drowned to prevent them from uttering curses while burning
  • The condemned were sometimes forced to face away from the crowd to prevent the evil eye

These brutal measures reveal the genuine fear that even those who condemned witches to death held for the power of the curse.

Saturn vs. Mars: Two Theories of Cursing

In traditional witchcraft, two planetary powers preside over offensive magick, and they work in distinctly different ways.

Martial cursing (Mars) scorches, blasts, and burns. It is fast, ferocious, and direct. A Martial curse strikes like a weapon: it ignites bad luck, sickness, accident, violence, and sudden collapse. Mars governs wrath in its explosive, aggressive dimension. Martial curses are best worked on Tuesdays under waning or dark moon conditions with red candles, iron instruments, and incenses compounded from hot, aggressive plants.

Saturnian cursing (Saturn) binds, freezes, and incarcerates. It is slow, heavy, and inexorable. A Saturnian curse does not destroy outright but arrests: it stops the target's movement, dries up their luck, incarcerates their will, and creates an ever-tightening net of inertia around them. Saturn governs limitation, restriction, time, and endings. Saturnian curses are best worked on Saturdays under waning or dark moon conditions with black candles, lead imagery, and cold, heavy incenses.

The two approaches can be combined: a working might first bind with Saturn and then blast with Mars, or a practitioner might choose one approach depending on the desired effect.

Methods of Delivery

A curse must reach its target to take effect. Traditional delivery methods include:

Eye contact: The gaze is the most direct and potent channel. The eye projects witch power directly from the practitioner into the target's field.

Touch: Contact through the hands passes the current into the target's body or aura. Handing an object to the target is a classic carrier method.

Breath: Breathing on or near the target, particularly toward the face, delivers the practitioner's vital force and intent into the target's sphere.

The Pyramid Power of Cursing

Building a dark current before the curse is delivered increases its potency substantially. Rather than projecting a single burst of malevolent intent, the practitioner can spend days or weeks constructing a vortex of hostile energy through focused thought, fasting, ritual acts, and deliberate accumulation of the emotional charge associated with the wrong done. By the time the curse is finally launched, it carries the weight of all that accumulated energy and strikes with far greater force.

The Black Fast is one traditional method for this kind of pyramid building: a two-week period of strict fasting combined with daily focused ill-wishing directed at the target.

The Conveyed Doll Strategy

A voodoo doll or poppet curse gains power from physical contact with the target or with something that belongs to them. One method is to have the poppet pass through the target's hands without their awareness of what it is: hidden inside a gift, slipped into their belongings, or concealed in an object they will handle. This contact creates a direct sympathy link between the poppet and the target's physical body, greatly strengthening the sympathetic bond on which the doll's power depends.
