⚡Charging

◇Type
Action

Charging is the process of infusing an object with magickal energy, intention, or specific forces so that it carries that essence within it. Charged objects serve as reservoirs of directed power, ready to deploy in spell work or ongoing protection.

Expanded Ritual Charging

  1. Perform appropriate space and personal cleansings as needed
  2. Prepare an altar dedicated to charging the materials
  3. Hold a white candle between your palms while concentrating on your intention for the object being charged
  4. When ready, place the candle on the altar and light it
  5. Hold the article between your palms and charge it with your energy and goals
  6. When ready, place the object down next to the candle
  7. Allow the object to remain there until the candle burns out completely

Charging with Natural Forces

Objects can be charged with specific forces, most commonly sunlight or moonlight, so that they absorb and retain some of that essence. Leaving an object in direct moonlight during a full moon, for example, infuses it with lunar energy. Solar charging works the same way with sunlight.

Objects may also be charged with elemental forces through the Blessings of the Elements.

Notes on Divination Objects

When charging an object intended for divination, stray thoughts or impressions may arise during the process. Rather than dismissing them, follow the thought and see where it leads. This communication can be valuable. If the impressions turn out to be disturbing or undesirable, perform a stronger cleansing before attempting to charge the object again.
