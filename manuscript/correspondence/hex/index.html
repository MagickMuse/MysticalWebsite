A hex is a ritual action where the practitioner announces to the universe, deities, spirits, or other forces their own desire to manifest harm toward another person, group, or entity because of perceived wrongdoing. Unlike a Curses, which typically invokes external entities to carry out the harm, a hex is handled by the practitioner through their own will and intent.

Distinguishing Hexes from Curses and Jinxes

The Ritual Grimoire Guide includes baneful workings across a spectrum of intensity:

Curses invoke gods, spirits, saints, or other nonhuman entities to cause harm. The practitioner petitions external forces, seeking help from outside sources to deliver the effect.

Hexes are powered by the practitioner's own will. The spellcaster takes personal responsibility for manifesting the harm rather than delegating to spirits or deities. This makes hexes more direct but also more personally demanding.

Jinx are low-energy, often unconscious harmful energies. They can be directed spontaneously through emotional charge, sometimes without magical training or intent. A rude gesture in traffic might carry jinx energy, resulting in minor inconveniences like stubbed toes or spilled coffee.

Ethical Considerations

Hex work falls under baneful magick. Effects and ethics are the practitioner's responsibility. Some traditions forbid harmful magick entirely. Others permit it under specific circumstances: self-defense, protection of loved ones, or justice for genuine wrongdoing. Know your boundaries before working.

Working with Hexes

Before casting a hex, consider:

  • Is this truly warranted, or is emotion driving the decision?
  • Have mundane solutions been exhausted?
  • Are you prepared for potential blowback or karmic return (depending on your tradition)?
  • Will you be able to live with the consequences?

Divination before hex work is strongly recommended. Confirming your target and understanding potential outcomes prevents misdirected energy and unintended harm.
