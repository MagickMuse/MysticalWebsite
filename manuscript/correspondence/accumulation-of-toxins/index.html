🧪Accumulation of Toxins

Minerals2
BezoarFlint

Accumulation of toxins refers to a buildup of metabolic waste and environmental compounds in the body, often manifesting as sluggishness, dull skin, water retention, or general malaise. Essential oils with detoxifying, diuretic, lymphatic, and circulatory-stimulating properties can support the body's natural elimination pathways through the liver, kidneys, lymph, and skin.

⚠️ Safety: Some detoxifying oils (such as juniper berry, parsley seed, and lovage) should be avoided during pregnancy and in cases of kidney disease. Always dilute properly for skin application and discontinue if irritation occurs.

Suggested Application of Oils

Apply via massage (especially over the abdomen, lower back, and along lymphatic pathways), as a skin oil or lotion, or in a warm bath to support whole-body detoxification.

Oils to Use to Address Accumulation of Toxins

  • Angelica Root Essential Oil
  • Birch Bud Essential Oil
  • Carrot Seed Essential Oil
  • Celery Seed Essential Oil
  • Coriander Seed Essential Oil
  • Cumin Essential Oil
  • Sweet Fennel Essential Oil
  • Grapefruit Essential Oil
  • Juniper Berry Essential Oil
  • Lovage Root Essential Oil
  • Parsley Seed Essential Oil
