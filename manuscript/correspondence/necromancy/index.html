Spirit Communication Through Necromancy

Among the most demanding operations in witchcraft, necromancy involves the deliberate summoning of the dead. It can be more taxing than even the conjuration of demons, particularly when a physical manifestation is required. The nervous and physical depletion placed on participants can be severe and, in extremely rare cases, fatal. For this reason it is most often performed at Sabbat with a full coven present, allowing the group to function as collective energy donors.

The optimal season falls when solar power is at its lowest ebb, between the autumnal equinox and the winter solstice. Halloween, the Feast of the Dead and one of the four great Sabbats, is the traditional occasion for its practice. Two categories of motivation make the operation permissible: intelligence, pursued when all other divinatory methods have failed and the one person capable of answering a question is dead; and love, the desire to reunite with a deceased loved one, a working sometimes called the Dumb Supper, often incorporated into the grand Sabbat of Halloween. The Witches' New Year begins on November 1st, and it is the ties of love and magick that draw the illustrious magickal dead back into the company of the living to share in the joy of the Sabbat and bestow their blessing on those gathered.
