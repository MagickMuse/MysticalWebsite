Gout is a form of inflammatory arthritis caused by the accumulation of uric acid crystals in the joints, most often the big toe. Acute attacks bring sudden, severe pain, swelling, redness, and tenderness. Aromatic support focuses on detoxification, anti-inflammatory action, and gentle circulation around the affected joint between flares.

⚠️ Gout flares can mimic infection. If the joint is hot, red, and severely painful with fever, see a healthcare provider. Essential oils support but do not replace medical care for acute attacks. Several gout-relevant oils are contraindicated in pregnancy or kidney disease (juniper berry, lovage, turpentine, etc.), check the individual oil page before use.

Suggested Application

  • Massage: Dilute 2 to 3 percent in carrier oil and massage gently around (not directly on) the affected joint to ease pain and support circulation.
  • Skin oil/lotion: Add to a daily lotion or skin oil for ongoing inflammation and uric acid clearance.
  • Bath: 6 to 8 drops total in a dispersant (carrier oil, milk, or unscented bath base), then add to a warm bath. Soak the affected limb if a full bath is not practical.
