To center is to gather yourself, to connect completely with your inner self. To be centered means to be alert, attentive, focused, and very much present in the here and now.

Centering in Magickal Practice

Ground and center before all magickal work to avoid depleting personal energy levels by augmenting internal energies. Centering aligns your focus and stabilizes your personal power so that energy flows through you rather than from you. Without centering, spellwork draws directly on your own reserves, leading to fatigue, scattered results, or energetic imbalance.
