⚖️Obesity

Type
Medical
Oils7

Obesity is a chronic condition characterized by excess body fat that increases the risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, joint strain, and other health complications. Aromatic support targets fluid balance, lymphatic drainage, and metabolic stimulation; essential oils are an adjunct to balanced nutrition, movement, and medical guidance, not a standalone weight-loss intervention.

⚠️ Essential oils do not cause weight loss on their own. Sweet fennel and juniper berry are contraindicated in pregnancy; sweet fennel is also contraindicated in epilepsy and hormone-sensitive conditions due to its high anethole content. Juniper berry must be avoided in kidney disease. Cold-pressed citrus oils (lemon, bitter orange) are phototoxic; avoid UV exposure on treated skin for at least 12 hours. Always work with a qualified practitioner for chronic conditions.

Suggested Application

  • Massage: Dilute 2 to 3 percent in carrier oil and use for full-body or targeted abdominal, hip, and thigh massage to support lymphatic flow and circulation.
  • Bath: 6 to 8 drops total in a dispersant (carrier oil, milk, or unscented bath base), then add to a warm bath. Soak to encourage detoxification and metabolic stimulation.
