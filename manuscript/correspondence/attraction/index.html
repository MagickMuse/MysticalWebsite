Intention in Love Magick

Love spells raise the most ethical questions. Targeting a specific person who is already in a relationship or coercing someone into feelings they would not have otherwise violates free will. Good spells respect other people's right to make their own choices.

Ethical Alternatives

Rather than targeting a specific person, consider other approaches: magically enhance your own attractiveness, remove obstacles between you and another, or cast a spell to attract someone who is right for you without naming a particular individual. Turning the final decision over to a higher power lets the universe guide you toward an outcome that truly fits.

The Binding Problem

A well-executed love spell creates a strong bond. If you later change your mind, breaking that bond can be extremely difficult. Consider carefully before creating magical ties that may outlast your actual desire.
