A chakra is an energy node in the human body. In Sanskrit this one is called Vishuddha, and it is pictured as a sky-blue lotus of sixteen petals.

Colors

Blue

Attributes

Expression, Speech, Power of Words

Governs

Authentic and open communication

Chanting sound: EH (like the ea in feather)

Location: Situated at the level of the throat

Your throat chakra is responsible for the choked-up feeling you experience when you can't speak.

Chakra Healing

The throat chakra is concerned with communication and creativity. It relates to the arms, shoulders, neck, and thyroid gland. It is also involved with your sense of hearing.

Balanced State: When the throat chakra is well balanced you will feel contented and at peace. You will listen to what others have to say, and feel able to freely express your own views.

Stimulation: Singing, dancing, or playing a musical instrument are effective ways to stimulate the throat chakra. Any form of self-expression, such as repeating mantras or affirmations, stimulates the throat chakra.

Body Systems

The throat chakra governs the following body systems and organs:

  • Throat
  • Neck
  • Thyroid gland
  • Parathyroid gland
  • Trachea
  • Esophagus
  • Mouth
  • Teeth
  • Gums
  • Hypothalamus
