Dyspepsia: difficulty with digestion associated with pain, flatulence, heartburn, and nausea.

Heartburn

Use only diluted oils for gentle abdominal massage on intact skin. Patch test before broader use, and consult a qualified practitioner during pregnancy or when working with sensitizing oils such as black pepper.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: dilute the chosen oil in a carrier and rub gently over the upper abdomen and lower chest in slow, clockwise strokes after meals. Use 1 to 2 percent dilution for cardamom and no more than 0.5 percent for black pepper.

Oils to Use to Address Heartburn

  • Cardamom Essential Oil
  • Black Pepper Essential Oil
