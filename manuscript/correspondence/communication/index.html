Definition

Communication is the correspondence of expression, clarity, and the exchange of information between beings. In magickal practice, communication work enhances the ability to speak clearly, listen deeply, write persuasively, and transmit intention across both physical and nonphysical distances.

Communication and the Air Element

Communication is strongly aligned with the Air element, which governs thought, intellect, and the movement of ideas. Air energy moves swiftly and carries messages, making it the natural elemental ally for any working that involves words, signals, or transmission of meaning. The direction East, the season of Spring, and the suit of Swords in tarot all share this communicative energy.

The Power of Words in Magick

Every spell begins with intention, and intention is shaped by language. The words you choose when crafting an incantation, writing an affirmation, or stating your purpose carry their own energetic vibration. Clear, precise language creates clear, precise results. Vague or muddled wording produces vague or muddled outcomes.

This is why many practitioners write their goals down rather than keeping them in their heads. Writing takes ideas out of the realm of imagination and into the material world. The tactile nature of putting words on paper makes intentions more tangible and directs the subconscious to carry out your wishes.

Working with Communication Energy

Common Communication Intentions

  • Speaking truth with clarity and confidence
  • Improving understanding between two or more people
  • Enhancing persuasion or negotiation skills
  • Opening blocked communication channels in a relationship
  • Strengthening connection with spirits, guides, or ancestors
  • Supporting creative writing, public speaking, or teaching
  • Sending messages or intentions across distance

Symbolic Correspondences

The Law of Similars connects communication to objects that resemble its function. Feathers correspond to air and the carrying of messages. Keys correspond to unlocking understanding. Objects that produce sound (bells, chimes, singing bowls) naturally resonate with communication energy.

Timing Communication Work

Wednesday is ruled by Mercury, the planet of communication, intellect, and commerce. This makes it the ideal day for spells involving clear speech, successful negotiations, or written projects. The waxing moon supports building communication skills or opening new channels of dialogue. The waning moon is better for releasing communication blocks or silencing gossip.
