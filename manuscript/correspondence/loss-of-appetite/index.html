🕴️Loss of Appetite

Type

Loss of appetite is a reduced or absent desire to eat, ranging from mild disinterest in food to severe inappetence. It can follow illness, stress, grief, medication, hormonal shifts, or simply a sluggish digestive system. Persistent loss of appetite leads to weakness, weight loss, and nutritional deficiency. Aromatherapy supports appetite through warming, carminative, and digestive-stimulant oils used in abdominal massage and pre-meal inhalation to wake the stomach, prompt digestive secretions, and restore the body's desire to eat.

⚠️ Sudden, sustained, or unexplained loss of appetite, especially with weight loss, fatigue, or pain, can signal serious medical issues including depression, eating disorders, infection, thyroid disease, or malignancy. Seek medical evaluation rather than relying on home aromatherapy. Always dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before topical application, and patch test first. Many appetite-stimulant oils (bay laurel, caraway, myrrh, black pepper) are pregnancy-avoid. Bergamot is phototoxic when applied to skin exposed to sunlight.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Abdominal Massage Before Meals: Dilute 3 to 5 drops of essential oil in 1 tablespoon of carrier oil and massage gently over the stomach in slow clockwise circles for 5 minutes, about 15 to 30 minutes before eating. The warming, stimulating action wakes the digestive tract and prompts hunger signals.
  • Pre-Meal Inhalation: Place 1 to 2 drops of essential oil on a tissue and inhale gently for a minute or two before a meal. The aroma alone can stimulate salivation, gastric secretions, and the desire to eat.
  • Blending Ratios: Adults use a 2 to 3 percent dilution for appetite-stimulating massage. Reduce to 1 percent for sensitive skin, elderly individuals, or those recovering from illness.

Oils to Use to Address Loss of Appetite

  • Bergamot Essential Oil
  • Caraway Essential Oil
