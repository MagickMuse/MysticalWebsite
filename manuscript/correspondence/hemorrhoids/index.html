Hemorrhoids, also called piles, are dilated and inflamed veins of the rectum and anus that can cause itching, burning, swelling, and bleeding. Astringent woods, balsamic resins, and circulation-toning oils ease discomfort, reduce inflammation, and support venous tone in the affected tissue.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before topical use. Do not apply oils to actively bleeding or fissured tissue without consulting a practitioner. Avoid juniper, myrrh, parsley, and yarrow during pregnancy. Cubeb and parsley should be used sparingly and over short periods. Those with kidney conditions should avoid juniper and parsley.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Skin oil or lotion: Add 5 to 10 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier (calendula-infused, sweet almond, or jojoba) and apply gently to the external area after bathing.
  • Compress: Add 5 to 8 drops to a bowl of cool water, swirl, soak a soft cloth, wring lightly, and apply to the area for ten to fifteen minutes to soothe swelling and inflammation.
  • Bath: Disperse 5 to 8 drops total in a tablespoon of carrier oil or unscented bath base, then add to a warm sitz or full bath and soak for fifteen to twenty minutes.
