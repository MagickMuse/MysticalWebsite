The Magick Manuscript

Correspondence Grimoire

The central index of the Magick Manuscript. Correspondences are the qualities, intentions, and concepts that everything else maps to.

Within These Pages:

Intentions (Protection, Love, Prosperity), qualities (Courage, Wisdom, Balance), elements (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), body parts, emotions, and abstract concepts. Each one links to the crystals, plants, entities, timing, and materials that embody it.

How to Walk It:

Start with what you want to accomplish or understand. Search for that intention (like "Protection") and you'll see it connects to Black Tourmaline (crystal), Rosemary (plant), Saturday (timing), Mars (planet), and Archangel Michael (entity). Follow those links to explore deeper.

Why it's called "Correspondence":

In magickal practice, a correspondence is a symbolic relationship between different layers of reality. When multiple things share the same energetic signature or intent, they "correspond" to each other.
