👣Foot Track Magic

Overview

"Leave nothing behind but your footprints", or so go the instructions for today's eco-tourists. Even footprints, however, have their magical uses.

Foot track magic is most closely associated today with hoodoo traditions, but it is actually a particularly primal, international magical technique. There are oblique references to it in the Talmud. Instructions attributed to Pythagoras forbade people to pierce footprints with nail or knife.

Origins

This ancient practice derives from dry lands, where a footprint lingers in the earth. To some extent, heavier, deeper imprints lend themselves more readily to foot track magic.

Two Varieties

1. Acting on the Footprint

Usually used for banishing or hexing: something is done to the actual footprint in the ground, piercing, burning, salting, or otherwise manipulating it.

2. Gathering the Footprint

The dirt from the footprint is scooped up in its entirety. This gathered earth is usually later combined with other ingredients for magical purposes, both positive and malevolent.

Critical Rules

⚠️
When gathering up footprints, it is vital to the success of the spell that the entire footprint is gathered. It is also crucial, and not always simple to determine, that one obtains the footprint of the correct person.
