🌼Calendula Oil Salve

Rashes are red, irritated, and often itchy skin eruptions that may arise from allergic reactions, heat, friction, contact with irritants, or underlying medical conditions. The essential oils below are gentle, anti-inflammatory, and skin-soothing, traditionally used to calm and heal rashed skin. Always consult the safety data on each oil before use, and patch-test before broader application since rashed skin is reactive.

⚠️
Safety: Always dilute essential oils properly before applying to inflamed or broken skin. Avoid German chamomile, true lavender, and spikenard during pregnancy unless cleared by a qualified practitioner. Hops should be avoided by anyone prone to depression. Patch-test on a small area first, especially when the rash is from an allergic source.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute 5 to 10 drops per ounce of carrier oil and gently massage around (not directly on) the affected area to support circulation and ease inflammation.
  • Skin oil/lotion: Add 5 to 10 drops per ounce of carrier oil or unscented lotion and smooth over the rash.
  • Compress: Add 5 to 10 drops to a bowl of cool water, soak a clean cloth, wring out, and lay over the rash to soothe heat and itching.
  • Flower water: Use as a gentle skin spray or in place of toner. Hydrosols of chamomile, lavender, and rose are especially gentle.
  • Bath: Add 5 to 10 drops to a tablespoon of carrier oil and disperse in a warm (not hot) bath.
