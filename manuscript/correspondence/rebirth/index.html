Death

The Continuing Soul

For many practitioners, physical death marks a transition rather than an ending. The soul, spirit, or essential energy of a person moves beyond the material world into another realm, eventually returning to physical form in a new body and era.

Earth as a Place of Learning

This worldview frames earthly existence as educational. Souls incarnate here to grow, learn, and work through specific challenges. The cycle repeats until all intended lessons are complete, after which the soul moves on to a state of rest and renewal.

The Summerland

Wiccan tradition holds that souls travel to the Summerland after death. This realm serves as a place of peace and restoration between incarnations. Where Christianity offers heaven and Buddhism describes nirvana, Wicca presents the Summerland as part of an eternal cycle of return.
