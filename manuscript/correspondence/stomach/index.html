To speak of the "guts" of something, a machine for instance, is to mean its innermost workings, its most important part. In the symbolism of the body the stomach and the guts are one and the same. To say we "can't stomach" something means it is intolerable, and "butterflies in the stomach" describes a fluttery, nervous feeling. In Ghana, twin crocodiles joined at the stomach form an adinkra symbol for the belief in democracy. The gut, or stomach, is also linked with the solar plexus chakra.

The guts are strongly associated with instinct. To feel something "in the gut" is to sense it through intuition, when the logical mind gives way to the instinctive, animal side of human nature.
