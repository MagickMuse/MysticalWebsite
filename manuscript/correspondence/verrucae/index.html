🦶Verrucae

◇Type
Medical
↗Oils2
Tagetes Essential OilTea Tree Essential Oil
⚠️
Tagetes is potent and contains tagetone, which may be irritating; use only in low dilutions, patch test before applying, and avoid on broken or sensitive skin. Tagetes is phototoxic, so avoid sun exposure on treated areas. Tea tree is generally safe but may cause sensitization in some individuals; patch test before topical use.

Verrucae are small, rough skin growths caused by the human papillomavirus (HPV), most commonly appearing on the hands and feet (plantar warts). The oils listed here have antiviral, antifungal, and antiseptic actions that can help break down the wart tissue over time and support clearer skin.

Suggested Application

  • Skin oil or lotion: Dilute the chosen oil in a carrier such as jojoba, coconut, or sweet almond oil and apply directly to the verruca once or twice daily. Cover with a small bandage if desired.
  • Neat application: A single drop applied directly to the verruca with a cotton swab. Take care to keep the oil on the verruca itself and avoid surrounding healthy skin, especially with tagetes.

Oils

  • Tagetes Essential Oil (S, N)
  • Tea Tree Essential Oil (S, N)
