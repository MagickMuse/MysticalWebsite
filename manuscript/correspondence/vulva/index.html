The Most Protective Image

Of every protective symbol, the female genitals may be the strongest of all. They are the doorway every living being passes through on its way into the world, and they hold the power of the mothers and ancestral women who keep watch over their descendants. Because they stand for life itself, they work against death and destruction.

Exposure Against Spirits

Flashing, the deliberate baring of the genitals, is an old method for driving off ghosts and unwanted spirits.

The Sentinel Vulva

In parts of rural North Africa, the dried vulvas of cows are mounted on stakes, where they stand guard as protective sentinels.

Symbolic Substitutes

Most often the real thing is replaced by an image that echoes its shape. Common stand ins include:

  • Seashells
  • Figs, pomegranates, cocos de mer, and apricots or peaches with a deep cleft
  • Crescents, both the lunar crescent and the horseshoe
  • Hair combs, through their link to mermaids
  • Triangles pointing downward

Using the Imagery

Display any of these images in the home, or wear them as jewelry, to draw on their protective power.
