The Sacred Union

Sex magick harnesses one of the most potent forces available to practitioners-the creative energy generated through sexual expression. This practice channels the intense focus and raised energy of intimacy toward magickal goals, treating sexuality as a sacred conduit for manifestation.

Historical Roots

The foundations of Western sex magick draw from diverse traditions. Sufi mystics explored ecstatic states through sacred union, while the Knights Templar were rumored to practice ritualized sexuality. In the modern era, Aleister Crowley and the Ordo Templi Orientis (OTO) systematized these practices, blending Eastern tantric principles with Western ceremonial frameworks.

Tantra and Sacred Sexuality

Tantric traditions from India and Tibet approach sexuality as a pathway to spiritual enlightenment. Rather than suppressing desire, tantra transforms it into fuel for awakening. The union of masculine and feminine energies, whether between partners or balanced within oneself, mirrors the cosmic dance of creation.

The Great Rite

In Wiccan practice, the Great Rite represents the symbolic or actual union of the God and Goddess. When performed symbolically, the athame (representing masculine energy) is lowered into the chalice (representing feminine energy). When performed literally between consenting adult partners, it becomes one of the most powerful workings in the Craft. This sacred act celebrates the generative forces of nature and raises tremendous magickal energy.

Working with Sexual Energy

Practitioners may direct the energy raised during arousal toward spellwork, charging objects, or manifesting intentions. The moment of climax represents a peak of focused will and released energy-an ideal launching point for magickal intent. Solo practice is equally valid, as the energy generated belongs to the practitioner alone.

In Traditional Witchcraft: Cernunnos and the Art of Fascination

In the folk/ceremonial witchcraft tradition of this grimoire source, sex magick falls under the dominion of Cernunnos, the Horned One, and is governed by the art of fascination: the deliberate projection of witch power to bewitch a prospective partner. This is distinguished from gentle love magick under Habondia; where Habondia governs romantic tenderness, Cernunnos rules the Wildwood, the carnal and animal dimensions of desire.

Fascination is the direct use of the aura, understood as a projected nervous energy called od, odyle, magnetism, or telergy. It is projected through three main channels: the gaze (the basilisk stare, held steady on the point between the target's eyes), touch (especially via a shared object such as a witch jewel), and breath (exhaled near or into the target's face). Rhythmic breathing is used to build the power before projection.

Thursday, at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM, is the preferred day and time for Cernunnos workings. The altar triangle is decked with animal horns, pinecones, and evergreens sacred to him.

Ethics and Consent

Sex magick absolutely requires informed, enthusiastic consent from all participants. Any attempt to use these practices manipulatively or without consent crosses fundamental ethical boundaries and corrupts the working.
