Materia Magica

Materia magica refers to the full range of items and materials used in spell-casting. Tools, like spells, are creations of people. What is indispensable depends entirely on the practitioner's personal traditions and needs. For those drawn to Wicca, an athame is essential. In the old Saami tradition, it was the drum. Ceremonial high magick may require extensive equipment. For others, everything necessary fits into a medicine bag, or even less.

Because so many published works emphasize the ceremonial aspects of magick, there is often a heavy focus on tools. These may or may not be relevant to every practitioner. The magick of the poor, of enslaved people, of nomads who travel light, is no less powerful but may require less equipment, or at least different equipment.

The variety of magickal tools demonstrates human ingenuity and creativity at its finest. Some creations serve no purpose other than magick, while others masquerade as common household items. The key is to understand personal needs, tastes, and power, then gravitate toward the tools that call out and resonate. Many occur naturally on Earth, including rocks, metals, and flowers. Others are human creations crafted from those original materials, such as magick wands, candles, and magick mirrors.

Personal Resonance

The variety of available materials is vast, and no practitioner will need them all. Some materials will appeal on an instinctive level and hold attention effortlessly. Those are the practitioner's best tools. Materials that fail to spark interest or personal connection are unlikely to produce consistent results, no matter how highly recommended they may be.

Handmade vs. Purchased

Historically, all magick was crafted from scratch. If a spell required paper, the practitioner would make it, possibly even gathering the raw material. The ink would be homemade as well. This approach carries significant benefits: complete control over materials and the working itself. When everything is done by hand, the practitioner knows the process was correct, all powers were properly honored, and all ingredients are genuine.

That said, magick is intended to improve life, not complicate it. Practitioners pressed for time can purchase ready-made supplies without diminishing their craft. Botanicals grown with personal care and attention will always carry more power for the grower, but those without a garden can buy from someone who does cultivate with intention. Those who are not naturally crafty can still cast effective spells. Other artisans are happy to provide oils, candles, wands, and herbs.

Standards for Sourced Materials

Purchased materials should be held to the same high standard as anything made by hand. Practitioners should not hesitate to ask questions of their suppliers and to specify their needs clearly. The quality and integrity of spell components matters regardless of who produced them.
