Children know the power of imagination, trusting what they see and believing in things many may not understand. As we age, the world pressures us to give up our belief in things considered childish, illogical, or impractical. But when a child is in their creative space or zone, they are completely focused, tuned in, connected. As adults, and especially as magickal people, we need that type of connection in our lives.

Keeping the Portal Open

The imagination is a portal. Like any portal, it can close if neglected. Social media, endless scrolling, and the pressure to perform for others draws focus away from the inner world and into external validation. The antidote is simple: reclaim your right to play.

Daydreaming

Upon awakening, instead of getting up immediately and doing whatever you normally do, give yourself permission to stare at the ceiling and daydream.

Daydreaming is awesome. Just pause and stare.

You're not meditating or actively engaging in a spiritual process. You're just looking through the portal of your eyes. It's amazing what you might see. Stare at the sky, at trees, at a patch of light on the wall. The sky is different every day. Sometimes cloudy, sometimes huge billowy clouds pass by, sometimes foggy. If you tilt your head just right, you might see the moon shining in the daytime.

It's not a big to-do. You're not looking with intention. You're just looking through the portal of your eyes and allowing the moment to unfold.

The Craft Store Exercise

Walking through a craft store is a great exercise for your imagination. Items in a craft store can unlock the imagination and stir the mind with possibilities.

Grab a cart just in case something special catches your eye, but also because leaning on a cart gives way to strolling and taking your time as you peruse the aisles. By having no preconceived notion of what you are looking for, you allow room for inspiration.

This exercise may open the portal to your inner child, who is just waiting for a playday.

Muses, come! Look at all the paints! Wait, there are paint-by-numbers kits? What is that smell? Oh, it's the smell of wood from the wood aisle where you can buy premade wooden boxes, houses, letters, and numbers. What would you use them for? Unlimited possibilities exist.

Bubbles

Worlds upon worlds live inside bubbles. Those worlds are temporary and fleeting. You create them with the power of air. You may get some of the sticky solution on your hands, but the cleansing power of water will wash that away in an instant.

Keep a bottle of bubbles on your porch. You may not blow bubbles every day, but just looking at the bottle brings tiny bits of joy.

As you watch bubbles float through the air, allow your gaze to soften. You might be surprised by the flashes of imagination you experience.

The Power of No

The word no is a powerful portal. It's one of the first ways that children assert their autonomy.

We lose our ability to respect and give space to our autonomy as we age. Society sets forth its rules for civilization. We wake up at certain times so we can go to jobs or work on projects. The sun rises and tells us it's a new day. The sun sets and tells us night is coming.

But what if, for one day, you said no? What if, for one day, when the sun rose, you didn't get up and do your dreary routine that has become a habit? Even good habits can become ruts. What if you did one thing differently to change your routine?

What portal might open for you?

The Witches' Pyramid: Imagination as Cornerstone

Within the four-sided framework known as the witches' pyramid, imagination stands as the first cornerstone, the faculty of conjuring vivid inner scenes that engages both the senses and the emotions. It's imagination that gives a working its shape and voice.

The strength of a spell often tracks the intensity of feeling behind it: the more charged and personal the inner images, the more force they carry. Some practitioners borrow the actor's trick of sense memory, recalling a real emotional memory to reach the right intensity, while others lean on scent, sound, movement, or music to get the current flowing.

Setting matters too. A space dressed with a few meaningful objects, however humble, helps prime the imagination for the work ahead, which is why many witches slowly gather a small collection of evocative tools and curiosities over time.

Treat your inner visions as an asset to cultivate rather than something to outgrow, the richer your capacity for reverie, the more raw material you have to work with.
