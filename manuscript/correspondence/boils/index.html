Boils are inflamed, often painful skin eruptions caused by infection of a hair follicle or oil gland. Several essential oils can ease pain, draw out infection, and support healing of the surrounding tissue.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier or compress base before applying. Do not lance or squeeze a boil yourself. Seek medical care if a boil is large, persistent, on the face, or accompanied by fever.

Suggested Application of Oils

Use these oils for boils via the following methods:

  • Skin oil or lotion (diluted in a carrier oil and applied around the affected area)
  • Compress (a few drops in warm water on a clean cloth, applied to the boil)
  • Bath (added to bathwater for systemic skin support)
