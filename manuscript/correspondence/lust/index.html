In Witchcraft: Cernunnos and the Lustful Arts

In the traditional witchcraft of this grimoire source, lust magick falls under the dominion of Cernunnos, the Horned God, functioning as the active, carnal counterpart to Habondia's gentler love. Where love philters summon tender yearnings, lust workings engage the deeper will and the driving force of animal desire.

The key tool is the art of fascination: the projection of witch power through eye contact, touch, and breath to bewitch a prospective partner. The three channels of projection are the gaze, the hands, and the breath, with the gaze considered the most potent. Rhythmic breathing quickens the metabolism to amplify available power.

The altar for lust workings is decked with animal horns, pinecones, and evergreens sacred to Cernunnos. Thursday is the preferred day, at 8AM, 3PM, or 10PM. The Love Knot is the primary spell of this tradition for stirring up overwhelming lust.
