The Witches' Pyramid: Faith as Cornerstone

Faith is the third cornerstone, and it functions less as religious belief than as unwavering confidence in your own working and its outcome. Without it, will and imagination have nothing to hold them steady, faith is what lets you commit fully to a spell instead of hedging against your own doubt.

As the alchemist Paracelsus observed, faith is what completes and strengthens imagination; every flicker of doubt chips away at a working's potential. This cornerstone also asks for integrity in daily life: a witch's word, once given, should be treated as binding. Each broken promise, whatever the excuse, erodes the same confidence a working depends on, so it's worth being careful about what you commit to in the first place.
