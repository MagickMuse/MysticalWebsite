🛏️Bed

The Bed as Sacred Space

The bed is far more than a place to rest. It is a ship for journeying, through sleep, dream, and altered states. It is one of the most spiritually charged locations in the home, because it is where we are most vulnerable, most open, and most alive.

In the bed we:

  • Sleep and lie vulnerable, night after night
  • Dream and travel to other realms
  • Open ourselves to intimacy in ways shared with no one else
  • Experience la petite mort ("the little death"), the French term for orgasm, a threshold between ordinary and ecstatic consciousness
  • Potentially create new life from nothing

Spiritual Vulnerability

Because the bed is a place of vulnerability, where the rational mind relaxes its guard, it is a magnet for both helpful and harmful spiritual activity. Protective attention to the bed space is especially important for:

  • Those developing psychic or mediumistic abilities
  • Those experiencing disturbing dreams or night terrors
  • Newlyweds and those beginning new intimate partnerships
  • Those working on fertility

Protective Folk Practices

The Newspaper Under the Bed (Rural Italy)

In rural Italy, it was once common to place a newspaper under the bed where a marriage would be consummated. Any evil spirit hiding under the bed would be compelled to read the whole newspaper before meddling in the newlyweds' affairs, a task synonymous with forever in a majority-illiterate society, like asking a faerie to count every grain of sand on a beach.

This charm reflects a broader magical logic: trap or distract malevolent forces with an impossible task.

Charms Placed Under the Bed

Protective charms placed under the bed can assist with:

  • Fertility
  • Sexual performance and vitality

Mugwort Pillow Charm

A small pouch of mugwort placed beneath the pillow promotes dreaming and enhances the vividness of dream journeys.

Mugwort Bed Corner Charm

Mugwort tied to the four corners of the bed protects the dreamer throughout the night.
