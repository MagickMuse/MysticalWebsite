🌿Manitou

◇Type
Concept
⚠️
⚠️ Manitou is a sacred concept of Algonquian-speaking peoples and other Native American nations. This is a living tradition; approach with respect and caution against appropriation.

Manitou refers to the spirit or spiritual power believed to inhabit every aspect of the natural world. Stones, trees, animals, rivers, weather phenomena, all carry their own Manitou, their own innate spirit presence. The term comes most directly from Algonquian peoples and related nations, though the underlying animistic belief that every natural thing has its own life-force is shared broadly across many Indigenous North American traditions.

The Living World

This worldview understands the world as fundamentally alive and ensouled rather than as an inert backdrop to human activity. Every interaction with the natural world is therefore also an interaction with spirit. Totem poles are one physical expression of this idea, carving the Manitous of animals and ancestors into visible, honoured form.
