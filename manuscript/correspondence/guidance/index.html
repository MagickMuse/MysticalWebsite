Guidance is the practice of seeking direction through magickal means, opening channels between the practitioner and the unseen forces that move through the cosmic web. It is the art of listening as much as asking.

Elements

Guidance resonates most strongly with Water and Air. Water opens the intuitive channels, allowing the practitioner to feel the pull of the right path. Air sharpens the mind and carries messages from the spiritual realm into conscious awareness.

How It Works

The cosmic web holds the patterns of all things, past and present. Guidance magick taps into these patterns, helping the practitioner perceive connections and pathways that the mundane mind overlooks. Through divination tools, meditation, and communion with spiritual allies, the practitioner learns to read the signs woven into everyday experience.

The Law of Similars reminds us that objects associated with sight, clarity, and pathfinding carry the energy of guidance. Mirrors, clear crystals, and items connected to the moon all serve as focal points for this work.

Timing

Mondays, ruled by the Moon, are ideal for guidance workings. The Moon governs intuition, reflection, and the subconscious mind. The full moon in particular illuminates what has been hidden, making it a powerful time to seek answers.

In Practice

Guidance magick takes many forms: pulling tarot or oracle cards, scrying in water or mirrors, burning herbs that open the third eye, or simply sitting in stillness and asking for a sign. The key is approaching the work with genuine openness. Guidance cannot reach a mind that has already decided what it wants to hear.

Keeping a journal of signs, dreams, and synchronicities strengthens the practitioner's ability to receive guidance over time. The more attention given to these subtle messages, the louder they become.

Related Intentions

Guidance shares deep connections with Divination, Wisdom, and Psychic Abilities. Where guidance asks the question, divination provides the method, and wisdom helps interpret the answer.
