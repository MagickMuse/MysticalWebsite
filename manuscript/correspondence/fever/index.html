Fever is the body's natural response to infection, in which core temperature rises above its normal range to support immune function. Essential oils can ease accompanying discomfort, encourage perspiration, support the immune response, and create a calmer recovery environment.

A fever above 39.4°C (103°F), one lasting more than three days, or one accompanied by stiff neck, severe headache, confusion, difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting, or rash requires immediate medical attention. Aromatherapy is supportive only and does not replace medical care, particularly for infants, young children, the elderly, or those with chronic illness. Keep oils well diluted and avoid eyes, mucous membranes, and broken skin.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Compress: Soak a cloth in cool or tepid water mixed with 4 to 6 drops of essential oil dispersed in a tablespoon of carrier oil or whole milk, then wring out and apply to the forehead, neck, or wrists to ease discomfort and encourage cooling. Refresh every 15 to 20 minutes.
  • Bath: Add 6 to 8 drops of essential oil dispersed in a tablespoon of carrier oil or whole milk to a tepid (not cold) bath to help reduce temperature, soothe aching muscles, and encourage perspiration.
