Athlete's foot is a fungal infection of the skin, most often appearing between the toes, on the soles, or along the sides of the feet. It thrives in warm, damp environments and is highly contagious. The essential oils below offer anti-fungal and anti-microbial support. Always dilute oils properly in a carrier before applying to broken or irritated skin, and patch-test before broader use.

Suggested Application of Oils

Use these oils for athlete's foot via the following methods:

  • Skin oil/lotion

Oils to Use to Address Athlete's Foot

  • Spike Lavender Essential Oil
