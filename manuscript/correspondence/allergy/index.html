👃🏻Nose

A hypersensitivity to a foreign substance, in which small doses provoke a violent bodily reaction. The essential oils below can be used to ease allergic responses. Always consult the safety data on each oil before use, and patch-test before broader application since allergic skin can be especially reactive.

Suggested Application of Oils

Use these oils for allergies via the following methods:

  • Massage
  • Skin oil/lotion
  • Flower Water
  • Bath
  • Inhalation (Steam)

Oils to Use to Address Allergies

  • Lemon Balm Essential Oil
  • German Chamomile Essential Oil
