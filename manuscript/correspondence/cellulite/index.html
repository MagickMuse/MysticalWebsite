🧈Cellulite

Cellulite is the accumulation of toxic matter in the form of fat in the tissue, producing the dimpled, congested texture of the subcutaneous layer. Aromatherapy supports cellulite work by stimulating lymphatic drainage, encouraging detoxification, and toning the skin and circulation through massage, daily skin oils, and warm baths.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier (jojoba, sweet almond, or unscented lotion) before topical use, and patch test before broader application. Avoid juniper, sweet fennel, parsley seed, rosemary, and common thyme during pregnancy. Cold-pressed grapefruit and cold-pressed lemon are phototoxic; avoid sun exposure on treated areas for 12 to 72 hours, or use steam-distilled grapefruit and steam-distilled lemon when sun cannot be avoided. Parsley seed is moderately toxic and irritant, and red and thymol thyme oils are phenol-rich, so both must be used in low dilution and in moderation. Aromatherapy is supportive only and works best alongside diet, hydration, and movement.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Add 5 to 10 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier oil and massage firmly into the affected area in upward, circular strokes toward the heart to stimulate circulation and lymphatic flow.
  • Skin oil or lotion: Add 5 to 8 drops of essential oil per ounce of carrier oil or unscented lotion and apply daily to the affected area after bathing.
  • Bath: Disperse 5 to 8 drops total in a tablespoon of carrier oil or unscented bath base, then add to a warm bath and soak for at least 15 minutes.
