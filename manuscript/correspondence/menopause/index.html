🩸Menstruation

Menopause marks the natural cessation of menstruation and a profound transition in a woman's life. Hormone-balancing and emotionally supportive essential oils ease the hot flushes, mood shifts, fatigue, and sleep disruption that often accompany this passage, while encouraging a steady sense of renewal.

Dilute essential oils in a carrier before applying to skin. Keep baths warm rather than hot to avoid intensifying hot flushes. Patch test if skin is reactive, and consult a qualified practitioner alongside ongoing medical care.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage: Dilute 2 to 3 drops per teaspoon of carrier oil and apply over the lower abdomen, lower back, chest, or neck daily as symptoms call for it.
  • Bath: Add 6 to 8 drops to a warm bath, dispersed in a small amount of carrier oil or unscented bath base before adding to the water.
  • Vaporization: Diffuse 4 to 6 drops in a room diffuser or oil burner to steady mood and ease tension throughout the day.

Oils to Use to Address Menopausal Problems

  • Cypress Essential Oil
  • Sweet Fennel Essential Oil
  • Geranium Essential Oil
  • Jasmine Absolute
  • Cabbage Rose Absolute
  • Rose Otto Oil
