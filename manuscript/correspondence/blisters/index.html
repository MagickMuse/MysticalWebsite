🩹Blisters

Blisters are small, fluid-filled pockets that form between layers of skin in response to friction, burns, infection, or irritation. Several essential oils can soothe inflammation, support skin healing, and protect against secondary infection while a blister recovers.

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier before applying near a blister. Do not pop intact blisters; the unbroken skin is the best protection against infection. Seek medical care for blisters caused by burns, infection, or that show signs of spreading.

Suggested Application of Oils

Use these oils for blisters via the following methods:

  • Skin oil or lotion (diluted in a carrier oil and applied around the blister)
  • Compress (a few drops in cool water on a clean cloth, applied to the affected area)
  • Bath (added to bathwater for systemic skin support)
