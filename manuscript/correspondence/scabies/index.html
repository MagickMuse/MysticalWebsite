🪲Scabies

Scabies is a contagious skin infestation caused by the Sarcoptes scabiei mite, which burrows into the upper layer of skin to lay eggs. The hallmark symptoms are intense itching (especially at night), small red bumps, and burrow tracks, often appearing in skin folds, between fingers, on wrists, elbows, and around the waistline. Standard treatment requires prescription scabicide. Aromatherapy can support the medical protocol by soothing itching, calming inflammation, and adding antimicrobial action to skin oils and washes.

⚠️
Safety: Scabies requires medical diagnosis and prescription scabicide treatment to fully eradicate the mites and their eggs. Essential oils are supportive only. All household members and bedding/clothing must be treated simultaneously to prevent reinfestation. Many oils on this list (turpentine, common thyme, peppermint, cinnamon leaf, lemongrass) are skin irritants in concentration; always dilute heavily, patch test first, and avoid during pregnancy and on young children unless cleared by a qualified practitioner. Avoid use on broken or weeping skin.

Suggested Application

Apply diluted essential oil to affected areas as a skin oil or lotion, layered alongside prescribed scabicide treatment. A 2 percent dilution (12 drops per ounce of carrier) is appropriate for most oils on this list; drop to 1 percent or lower for the more aggressive constituents (peppermint, cinnamon leaf, common thyme, turpentine). Reapply two to three times daily for up to two weeks.
