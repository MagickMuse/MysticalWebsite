Spells for Health and Healing

Healing is a term used loosely in the magical community. It covers everything from physically curing to relieving pain, soothing anxiety, promoting rest, and offering comfort. Healing spells often focus on supporting the body's natural processes rather than replacing medical care, always consult a healthcare professional for serious conditions.

A Holistic Approach

Holistic health treats the whole person, body, mind, and spirit, as an interconnected system. In magical practice, healing is often a two-step process:

  1. Cleanse the negative: Remove stagnant energy, emotional blockages, or spiritual intrusions that may be contributing to illness or discomfort.
  2. Replace with positive: Fill the cleared space with healing light, protective energy, or strengthening intention.

This approach recognizes that physical symptoms often have energetic or emotional roots. A headache might stem from stress; chronic fatigue might reflect spiritual depletion. Effective healing addresses all layers.

Body Talk

The body speaks through symptoms. Many healers believe that the location and nature of an ailment reveals its deeper cause. Consider how language reflects this connection:

  • "I can't stomach this situation"
  • "They're a pain in the neck"
  • "My heart is broken"
  • "I can't stand on my own two feet"
  • "I've got the weight of the world on my shoulders"

When working healing magic, ask yourself: What might this symptom be trying to tell me? The answer can guide your spellwork toward addressing root causes rather than just surface symptoms.

Health Inventory

Before performing healing work, take an honest inventory. These questions can help identify where to focus your magical efforts:

  • How would you describe your current state of health?
  • Do you have any chronic conditions or recurring symptoms?
  • How well do you sleep? Do you feel rested upon waking?
  • How is your energy level throughout the day?
  • Do you experience frequent headaches, digestive issues, or muscle tension?
  • How do you handle stress? Where do you feel it in your body?
  • Are there emotions you tend to suppress or avoid?
  • Do you feel connected to your body, or disconnected from it?
  • What does your body need that you haven't been giving it?
  • If your illness could speak, what would it say?

The Human Energy Field

Every living being is surrounded and permeated by an energy field. This life force goes by many names across cultures:

  • Chi (Chinese)
  • Ki (Japanese)
  • Prana (Sanskrit)
  • Baraka (Sufi)
  • Mana (Polynesian)
  • Ruach (Hebrew)

This vital energy flows through the body along specific pathways. In Traditional Chinese Medicine, these are called meridians, channels that carry chi to every organ and tissue. Blockages or imbalances in these meridians manifest as illness, pain, or emotional disturbance.

In the yogic tradition, energy flows through nadis (subtle channels) and concentrates at chakras, spinning wheels of energy located along the spine. Each chakra governs specific physical systems, emotional states, and spiritual functions.

The Chakras and Body Systems

Understanding which chakra governs which body systems can help you target your healing work more precisely. When a particular area of the body is affected, consider working with its corresponding chakra:

  • Root Chakra: Immune system, rectum, feet, legs, bones
  • Sacral Chakra: Sexual organs, large intestines, appendix, hips, bladder
  • Solar Plexus Chakra: Abdomen, stomach, upper intestines, liver, kidneys, gallbladder, pancreas, middle vertebrae, adrenal glands
  • Heart Chakra: Heart, lungs, shoulders, arms, circulatory system, diaphragm, ribs, breasts, thymus
  • Throat Chakra: Throat, neck, thyroid, parathyroid, trachea, esophagus, mouth, teeth, gums, hypothalamus
  • Third Eye Chakra: Eyes, ears, nose, brain, nervous system, pineal and pituitary glands
  • Crown Chakra: Skeletal system, skin, muscular system

Before Casting Healing Spells for Others

When someone you care about is struggling, it's tempting to jump in with magickal assistance. But consider these factors first:

Pain often carries a message. Discomfort can be the body's way of drawing attention to something deeper. Sometimes the healing process requires sitting with that discomfort to access its lessons. Removing it prematurely might delay genuine resolution.

Free will matters. Magick works best when the recipient is open to change. Casting for someone without their knowledge or consent can backfire or simply not take hold. An illness might be serving an unexpected purpose in someone's life, like forcing a workaholic to finally rest. Unless you're asked for help, tread carefully.

Magick complements medicine; it doesn't replace it. Even the most potent spellwork shouldn't substitute for professional care. Miraculous healings happen, but so do infections, fractures, and conditions that genuinely require a doctor. Blend your craft with common sense.

Optimal Timing for Health Magick

Aligning your healing work with celestial rhythms amplifies its effectiveness.

Moon Phases

  • Waxing: Building, strengthening, repairing. Ideal for boosting vitality or mending tissue and bone.
  • Waning: Diminishing, releasing, reducing. Use for weight loss, shrinking tumors, or eliminating infection.
  • Full: Peak energy, but also heightened emotions. Great for vitality work, though psychological issues may surface.
  • New (and three days prior): Low energy period. Recovery slows during this time, so avoid pushing too hard.

Astrological Correspondences

  • Virgo: Rules health directly. Powerful for any healing work when the sun or moon transits here.
  • Leo: Solar vitality. Good for strength and life force restoration.
  • Aries: Mars-ruled physical power. Supports recovery requiring stamina and fight.

Days of the Week

  • Saturday: Limiting, binding, reducing. Best for containing disease spread or managing chronic conditions.
  • Tuesday: Mars energy for physical strength and fighting illness.
  • Thursday: Jupiter's expansion supports growth and recovery.
  • Sunday: Solar vitality and overall wellness.

Mercury Retrograde

Avoid elective surgeries and medical procedures during retrograde periods when possible. Communication errors and mix-ups are more common. If test results come back concerning during a retrograde, consider retesting afterward. Mercury retrogrades occur roughly every four months, lasting about three weeks each.
