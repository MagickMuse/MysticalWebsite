💋Kiss

◇Type
Action

A kiss can be a symbol of erotic love or of union. The Romans distinguished three kinds: the osculum, a kiss on the cheek; the basium, a kiss on the lips; and the suavium, the deep, involved kiss of lovers. To kiss someone is to mingle not only saliva but breath, which is itself akin to the spirit of life. It is no coincidence that, once the marriage vows are spoken, the couple are told they may kiss: the mingling of their breath signals that they now breathe the same air, a sign of their union in both a physical and a spiritual sense.

In the tale of Sleeping Beauty, sleep stands for the innocent state of the sleeping princess, who is awakened, literally and sexually, by the kiss of the one who has pressed through the symbolic thorny thickets to reach her.

Kissing the ground is a sign of affection for, and union with, a territory. Kissing the feet is a sign of respect and obeisance. A kiss may also serve as a greeting or a farewell, and kissing a sacred image shows loyalty and devotion. Kisses are used for luck as well, as when a gambler kisses the dice before a throw.

The kiss has its darker meanings too. The "Kiss of Death" is the final blow, the gesture that destroys something or brings it to an end; the kiss of the Mafia signifies death, and the kiss of the Devil was said to mean eternal damnation.

Religious Associations

In Christian services the Kiss of Peace is a sign of mutual recognition, given as a kiss or a handshake. In the Bible, the kiss Judas gives Christ is a mark of betrayal, remembered ever after as the Judas Kiss.
