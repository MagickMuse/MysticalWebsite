Insomnia is the inability to fall asleep or stay asleep, often linked to nervous tension, stress, anxiety, hormonal shifts, pain, or disturbed circadian rhythm. Aromatherapy supports rest through sedative oils that quiet the nervous system, lower elevated arousal, and prepare body and mind for deep sleep.

⚠️
Important safety notice: Several oils named here carry pregnancy cautions, sedative interactions, or sensitization risk. Hops must be avoided by those with depression. Valerian, melissa, and ylang ylang can intensify the effects of sedatives, alcohol, and other CNS depressants. Patch test any unfamiliar oil before broader topical use, and consult a qualified practitioner during pregnancy, while nursing, or alongside prescription sleep, anti-anxiety, or hormonal medications.

Suggested Application of Oils

Apply via massage, bath, or vaporization.
