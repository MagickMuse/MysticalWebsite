A chakra is an energy node in the human body. In Sanskrit this one is called Anahata, and it is pictured as a green lotus of twelve petals, ruled by the element of air.

Colors

Green

Attributes

Energies Meet, Love, Healing

Governs

Love, nurturing, and compassion

Chanting sound: ay (like the a in hay)

Location: Situated between the shoulder blades in line with your heart

Feeling Your Heart Chakra

The heart chakra is the easiest one to feel. You've likely felt it respond involuntarily when overcome with emotion, that physical sensation in your chest.

To consciously feel your heart chakra:

  1. Hold your hand about twelve inches away from your chest
  2. Slowly bring it closer and closer
  3. Pause when you feel a slight resistance
  4. Draw your hand away until the resistance goes, then bring it forward again to experience it once more
  5. Once you're certain you've felt your heart chakra, explore its size and shape
  6. You'll find it shaped like a cone or trumpet, and it will feel surprisingly solid

Once you've examined your heart chakra, you can start feeling for the others using the same technique.

Chakra Healing

Not surprisingly, the heart chakra is concerned with feelings of love and your relationships with the people who are closest to you.

Physical Areas: The heart chakra is concerned with the well-being of your heart, lungs, immune system and the circulation of blood throughout the body.

Balanced State: When the heart chakra is well balanced you will accept yourself and other people for who they are. You will be understanding, tolerant, and not likely to feel threatened by insecure people who try to control everything around them.

Imbalance Issues: When you feel insecure, unloved, or unlovable, you may experience breathing problems (as this chakra is associated with the element of Air) or have difficulties with your immune system.

Stimulation: Thinking about the special people in your life is an effective way to stimulate the heart chakra. A policy of being compassionate and gentle with everyone you meet also helps energize your heart chakra. Walking in a park with plenty of greenery also stimulates the heart chakra.

Body Systems

The heart chakra governs the following body systems and organs:

  • Heart
  • Lungs
  • Circulatory system
  • Shoulders
  • Arms
  • Diaphragm
  • Ribs
  • Breasts
  • Thymus gland
