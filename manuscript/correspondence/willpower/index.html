The Witches' Pyramid: Will as Cornerstone

The second cornerstone of the witches' pyramid is will, a single-pointed, almost unreasoning determination that, within the frame of a working, is meant to be indulged without apology. Outside of ritual this same intensity might look like stubbornness; inside it, it's simply the engine that drives a spell to completion, focusing whatever emotional charge the imagination has raised.

Building this capacity benefits from small, steady practice. Concentration exercises, holding attention on a single candle flame, a fixed point, or the sweep of a second hand for a full minute, are traditional ways to train focus. So is a daily habit of gentle self-assertion: choosing, on purpose, to hold a position or see through a small intention in ordinary situations that don't matter much, simply to feel the will switch on.

One caution worth keeping: don't direct your will at outcomes you privately believe are impossible. Aim it first at things you know can actually happen, and let confidence build from there rather than risking an early, discouraging failure.

The traditional charge that accompanies this cornerstone: as my will, so mote it be.
