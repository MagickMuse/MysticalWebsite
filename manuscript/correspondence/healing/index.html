A restorative intention that channels energy toward mending what is broken, soothing what is inflamed, and supporting the body, mind, or spirit in returning to wholeness. Healing is one of the oldest and most universal applications of magick, rooted in every culture's traditions of folk medicine, spiritual remedy, and energetic restoration.

The Nature of Healing Magick

Healing workings do not replace medical care. They complement it. Magickal healing operates on the energetic and spiritual levels, addressing the root causes of imbalance that physical treatments alone may not reach. A healing spell might ease anxiety that worsens a physical condition, clear energetic blockages that slow recovery, or simply provide comfort and strength during a difficult time.

Healing energy tends to be gentle and persistent rather than forceful. Where Protection builds walls and Fire transforms through intensity, Healing works like water seeping into parched earth: slowly, deeply, thoroughly.

Types of Healing Work

  • Physical Healing supports recovery from illness or injury, easing pain, and promoting overall vitality and wellness
  • Emotional Healing involves processing grief, releasing old wounds, and restoring emotional equilibrium after trauma or loss
  • Spiritual Healing focuses on repairing damage to the energetic body, clearing attachments, and restoring connection to one's higher self or spiritual path
  • Relational Healing centers on mending broken bonds, fostering forgiveness, and rebuilding trust between people
  • Environmental Healing involves cleansing a space of stagnant or harmful energy and restoring balance to a disrupted environment

Working with Healing Correspondences

Healing draws allies from every grimoire. The planetary influences of the Moon (emotional healing) and the Sun (vitality and strength) both support healing work, as do the nurturing energies of Water and Earth.

When designing a healing working, identify which type of healing is needed and select correspondences that match. A ritual for emotional healing after a breakup might emphasize Moon energy and gentle stones, while one for physical recovery might call upon Solar influence and invigorating herbs.

The Healer's Responsibility

Two important principles guide ethical healing magick:

  1. Consent matters. Performing healing work on someone without their knowledge or permission is considered intrusive in most traditions. If someone has not asked for healing, ask before working on their behalf.
  2. Know your limits. Magickal healing supports wellness. It does not cure disease. Never advise someone to forgo medical treatment in favor of spellwork. The best outcomes come from combining practical medicine with spiritual support.

Folk Practices

Votive candle offering: A European custom dating to the Middle Ages, still practiced today: offer any spirit, deity, or saint a candle as tall as the person for whom healing is requested. This gesture is particularly effective when petitioning fiery spirits such as Brigid or Diana.

Ancient Mesopotamian cleansing: Rub the body with oil, then rub the body with bread or dough. This provides spiritual cleansing and purification for the ill and may be used as a regular preventive practice to repel disease demons. Scholars note a remarkable similarity to modern healing rituals associated with Afro-Caribbean healer Babalu-Ayé.

Magical Healing Aromas: Add essential oils of frankincense, lavender, and mastic to an aroma burner and disperse the fragrance through the area. This magically strengthens the body and inner resolve while simultaneously summoning benevolent spirits and putting harmful ones to flight.

Self Healing as Practice

Before healing others, tend to yourself. Regular self healing practice, whether through meditation, energy work, herbal remedies, or ritual, keeps your own reserves strong and prevents burnout. A depleted healer has little to offer. Ground and center before all healing work to avoid depleting personal energy levels.
