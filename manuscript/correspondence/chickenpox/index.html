🫧Chickenpox

Chickenpox is a highly contagious viral infection caused by the varicella-zoster virus. It presents as an itchy, blistering rash accompanied by fever, fatigue, and general malaise, most often in children but increasingly serious when contracted in adulthood. Aromatherapy supports the body's response to the infection, eases the itch and inflammation of the lesions, and helps prevent secondary bacterial infection of the broken skin.

Use diluted oils on intact-skin areas only. Avoid open lesions and broken skin. Patch test before broader application, and seek medical care for high fevers, breathing difficulty, severe rash, or symptoms in pregnant individuals, infants, or immunocompromised people. Eucalyptus should not be used on children under six years old.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Compress: Soak a soft cloth in cool water with three to five drops of essential oil and apply gently over inflamed areas to soothe itching and reduce inflammation.
  • Skin oil or lotion: Dilute two to five drops of essential oil per tablespoon of carrier oil or unscented lotion and apply lightly to intact skin around lesions to calm irritation and support skin healing.
  • Bath: Add six to eight drops of essential oil to a tablespoon of carrier oil or unscented bath base, then disperse in a tepid bath to ease whole-body itch, soothe inflammation, and support immune response.
