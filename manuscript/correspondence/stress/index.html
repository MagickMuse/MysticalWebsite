Correspondences: Correspondence Grimoire

Nervous tension and stress arise when daily demands outpace the body and mind's capacity to recover. Aromatherapy works on the autonomic nervous system through the breath, the skin, and the limbic response to scent, easing the body out of fight-or-flight patterns and supporting calm, regulated states. The oils below address tension at multiple levels, from acute overwhelm to chronic, low-grade strain.

Use only on intact skin. Dilute essential oils in a carrier oil before massage, and disperse no more than six to eight drops total in a full bath. Avoid hyssop, sage, and rosemary during pregnancy or with epilepsy. Patch test before regular use, especially when working with citrus or absolute oils, which can be more sensitizing.

Suggested Application of Oils

  • Massage diluted oil into the shoulders, neck, lower back, and solar plexus to release held tension and steady the breath.
  • Add a small amount of essential oil to a warm bath and soak for fifteen to twenty minutes to settle the nervous system at the end of the day.
  • Vaporize selected oils in a diffuser to scent the room during work, study, or rest, supporting calm focus or letting go.
