Strapholos

A small two-holed disk strung with cord. When the cords are pulled taut and released in turn, the disk whirls and hums with a hissing tone. Originally a wryneck bird (Iynx torquilla) bound spread-eagle to a spinning wheel, the device was simplified into this disk form, which retained the bird's nickname. It is used to compel the attention of a bound spirit or god, and can be inscribed with sigils and names to consecrate it as the permanent home of a spirit-servant.
