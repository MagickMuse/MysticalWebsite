Magickal Uses

In several traditions, ancestral bones are periodically disinterred to participate in rituals and celebrations, then returned to the grave afterward. Other traditions retain bones permanently as a means of honoring and communicating with the dead. In areas where land scarcity limits burial duration, bones are moved to catacombs or ossuaries when decomposition is complete.

Eternal Life Working

Human bones may be painted red to provide the deceased with eternal life or to activate reincarnation. Menstrual blood was the oldest recorded activation material for this purpose; red ochre has been used since prehistoric times as an alternative.

This practice survives in isolated traditions today. In modern Western magickal practice, only Palo Mayombe formally acknowledges the ritual use of human bones.
