🧹Cleansing Objects and Ritual Tools

Before working with any ritual tool or object, it's worth taking a moment to cleanse it spiritually. Choose a method that won't cause physical damage to the item. Indigo Water is safe to sprinkle on crystals, for example, but will stain fabric permanently.

Common methods:

  • Pass the object through incense smoke
  • Hold it in the smoke of a smudge stick
  • Lightly sprinkle or asperge it with a cleansing water such as Holy Water, Marie Laveau Water, or Rose of Jericho Water
  • Submerge or rinse in salt water, only if the object can safely withstand it
  • Sound cleanse using a singing bowl
