🥃Amaretto

◇Notes/Warnings
Italian liqueur made from apricot pits or almonds. WARNING: Bitter apricot seeds contain amygdalin which converts to cyanide when metabolized. The liqueur is safe but avoid raw bitter apricot pits. Main Botanical: Apricot (Prunus armeniaca).

Magickal Uses

Creativity

Amaretto stimulates creative flow and artistic inspiration. Use before creative work or to break through blocks.

Turning Aside Evil

The bitter element in amaretto works to deflect negative energy and malevolent intent. Add to protection workings.

Knowledge of Goodness

Opens the mind to recognize what is truly beneficial and wholesome. Useful for discernment work.

Love

Associated strongly with Aphrodite, amaretto supports all forms of love magick: romantic attraction, deepening bonds, and self-love.

Passion

Ignites desire and enthusiasm. Use in workings for lust, motivation, and pursuing what you want wholeheartedly.

Opening the Mind

Encourages receptivity to new ideas, perspectives, and experiences. Good for learning and expanding awareness.

Relaxation

Promotes calm and ease. Use in workings to release tension and create peaceful states.

Remedies for Distrust

Helps rebuild trust and overcome suspicion. Use in reconciliation work or when you need to let your guard down.

Peace and Healing

Supports emotional equilibrium and recovery from hurts. Works especially well for healing love relationships.

Healing Love Relationships

Specifically aligned with mending romantic bonds, restoring affection, and healing wounds between lovers.

Safety Warning

⚠️ Bitter apricot seeds contain amygdalin, which converts to hydrogen cyanide when metabolized. The liqueur itself is safe. The distillation process removes dangerous compounds. Avoid consuming raw bitter apricot pits.
