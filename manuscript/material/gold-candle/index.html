Magical Properties

Gold candles channel the power of the sun, bringing glory, victory, and abundant wealth through solar magic.

Primary Uses

  • Wealth: Attracting significant financial abundance
  • Glory: Recognition, fame, and honor
  • Victory: Success in competitions and challenges
  • Solar Magic: Harnessing the sun's masculine, active power

Solar Power

Gold candles embody the sun's energy:

  • Masculine, active energy
  • Confidence and authority
  • Success and achievement
  • Illumination and clarity
  • Life force and vitality

Wealth and Abundance

Gold candles are among the most powerful for prosperity work, particularly:

  • Large-scale wealth manifestation
  • Business success
  • Investment returns
  • Luxury and abundance
  • Gold and precious metals

Ritual Applications

Burn gold candles for:

  • Major prosperity rituals
  • Success and victory spells
  • Solar deity worship
  • Confidence and personal power
  • Fame and recognition
  • Leadership and authority
  • Winning competitions
  • Midsummer and solar celebrations

Additional Correspondences

Gold candles also govern:

  • The God: Masculine divine energy and solar deities
  • Fast Luck: Quick fortune and rapid good fortune
  • Intelligence: Mental acuity and brilliance
  • Solar Influence: All aspects of sun magic and power

Traditional Color Correspondences

Gold (or very clear light yellow) helps with great fortune, intuition, understanding, divination, and fast luck when circumstances are out of your control. It attracts higher influences, financial benefits, money knowledge, healing, and happiness.

Planetary Associations

  • Sun — Gold is a primary Sun color, used on Sundays or when calling upon solar energy (vitality, success, authority, illumination).

Days of the Week

  • Sunday (The Sun) — Gold candles are burned on Sundays to invoke solar energy: vitality, success, great fortune, and illumination.
