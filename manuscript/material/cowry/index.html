Cowrie Shell Divination

Cowrie shells serve as the oracular medium in sacred divination systems throughout Africa, functioning as the voice of the orisha. They can also be used in simpler household divination practices. Because cowries were historically used as currency across Africa and Asia, they function as natural substitutes for coins in any coin-based divination working. They are particularly well-suited as a substitute when the practitioner wishes to invoke the elemental energy of water rather than metal.

To prepare a cowrie for divination, slice off the rounded, closed side of the shell so that it lies flat when tossed and lands cleanly on either face. Pre-sliced cowries are also available for purchase. Once prepared, assign one face as heads and the other as tails, formulate your question clearly, and toss.

Seashells carry the fertility power of the sea, the ancient source of all life. Among all shells, the cowrie holds the strongest associations with fertility — its shape closely resembles the vulva, making it a natural embodiment of generative power. Cowrie shell belts worn over the reproductive area are among the most ancient human artifacts known, unearthed on prehistoric remains in Northern Europe, Asia, and Africa. They were simultaneously sacred objects and valued trade goods, crossing continents long before recorded history.

Because of this deep resonance with the body's reproductive center, cowries are strung into belts and cords worn directly over the hips and ovaries, used in knot magick where desires are tied into each knot as the cord is assembled, and incorporated into fertility amulets of all kinds. They are also associated with wealth and prosperity, reflecting their historical use as currency, and with the sea's broader portfolio of power: depth, mystery, and the relentless force of generation.

Labor Pain Relief

Beginning earlier in pregnancy, wear cowrie shells against the skin (ideally on a belt, though they may be worn elsewhere if a belt causes irritation) to help protect against painful labor.
