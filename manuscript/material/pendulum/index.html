Divination Tool

A pendulum is a weighted object suspended from a cord, string, or chain. When held steady, the pendulum responds to questions through its movement, providing yes/no answers or indicating directions and choices.

How Pendulums Work

Pendulums function through subtle muscle movements guided by the unconscious mind. They serve as amplifiers of intuition, making visible what the conscious mind cannot perceive. Some practitioners believe pendulums also respond to external energies or spiritual guidance.

Materials and Construction

Pendulums can be crafted from:

  • Crystals: Clear quartz, amethyst, or other stones on chains
  • Metal: Brass, copper, silver, or gold weights
  • Found objects: Any weighted item on a string works
  • Specialized tools: Ornate pendulums designed for specific purposes

Calibrating Your Pendulum

Before use, establish your pendulum's personal language. Hold the string or chain with your dominant hand, letting the weight hover over the palm of your nondominant hand. Ask the pendulum to show you "yes" and wait patiently — it may take a little time, but stay with it. Once you have a clear sense of that response, ask it to show you "no." You can repeat this calibration at the start of each session to confirm the signals are consistent.

Every practitioner's yes and no looks a little different, and discovering your own is part of building a genuine bond with the tool. Some people prefer to decide their signals in advance, or create a simple pendulum board — a piece of paper with "yes" and "no" written out — and let the pendulum swing toward the written answer.

Common movement patterns include:

  • Clockwise circle for yes, counterclockwise for no
  • Vertical line for yes, horizontal line for no
  • Circle for yes, diagonal line for no
  • Any combination that feels clear and consistent for you

Uses in Magick

Pendulums are used for:

  • Answering yes/no questions
  • Finding lost objects
  • Detecting energy fields and blockages
  • Choosing between options
  • Dowsing for water, minerals, or ley lines
  • Communicating with spirits

The Traditional Witch's Pendulum

Beyond the crystal or metal pendulums described above, an older witch's method builds the weight from a lodestone or, if none is available, a small steel magnet. Clean the stone or magnet of dust or grease, then attach it to roughly nine inches of strong twine with a metal safe adhesive. Once dry, sprinkle it with salt water and pass it through smoke from a wormwood and mastic incense to finish the charging.

Hold the free end of the twine between the finger and thumb of your dominant hand, elbow held away from your body so the weight can swing unimpeded. Alternatively, tie the twine to the middle of a short stick or pencil and hold one end in each hand, resting your elbows on a table so the pendulum hangs between them.

In this traditional method, a swing toward and away from you signals yes, while a sideways swing signals no. You can pose spoken questions directly, or write a question on paper and let the pendulum swing freely above it; a photograph of the person in question works the same way when the question concerns someone specific.

More advanced practitioners also use the pendulum to dowse for hidden water, buried minerals, or lost items by holding it over a large scale map until it signals a location, then narrowing the search on the ground itself. This is considered advanced work; beginners should start with straightforward yes or no questions.

As with most divinations, working your Square of Mercury beforehand helps steady the mind, though many witches skip this step for the pendulum given how simple the technique already is.

Moon Magick

Pendulums are valuable divination tools for lunar workings, particularly when you need clear guidance on questions, choices, or which materials to use in a spell. Hold the suspended weight steady and allow its movement to answer yes or no questions, point you toward lost objects, or help you select between ritual items when your instincts feel uncertain. Many practitioners gravitate toward pendulums carved from stone or crystal that holds personal resonance. That said, no purchase is required: a pendant on a necklace works just as well, and you can even string a charm from a craft store to create your own.
