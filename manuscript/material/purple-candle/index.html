Magical Properties

Purple candles channel intense personal power, self-confidence, and sexual energy, making them potent tools for sovereignty and desire work.

Primary Uses

  • Personal Power: Commanding your own authority
  • Self-Confidence: Strong, assured confidence
  • Sex: Sexual power, desire, and magnetism

Personal Power and Sovereignty

Purple has long been the color of royalty and command:

  • Claiming personal authority
  • Leadership and dominance
  • Psychic power
  • Spiritual authority
  • Commanding respect
  • Power over situations

Confidence and Strength

Purple builds powerful, unshakeable confidence:

  • Bold self-assurance
  • Overcoming intimidation
  • Standing in your power
  • Refusing to back down
  • Courageous action

Sexual Magic

Purple candles are powerful for sex magic:

  • Sexual magnetism and attraction
  • Sexual confidence

  • Sexual healing
  • Sacred sexuality
  • Sensual empowerment

Candle Magic Applications

Purple candles support spiritual endeavors and service to humanity as a whole. Use purple for repairing friendships and releasing tension between people. Purple brings the elevated perspective needed to see beyond personal concerns and work for the greater good.

Traditional Color Correspondences

Purple helps with success, idealism, higher psychic ability, wisdom, progress, protection, honors, spirit contact, breaking bad luck, driving away evil, divination, greater magickal knowledge, spiritual protection and healing, removing jinxes and hexes, success in court cases, business success, and influencing people who have power over you. Use with caution — purple is very powerful and its energies can be difficult to handle.

Planetary Associations

  • Jupiter — Purple is a primary Jupiter color, used on Thursdays or for Jupiterian energy (expansion, wisdom, prosperity, spiritual growth).
  • Saturn — Very darkest purple; used on Saturdays or for Saturnian energy (binding, karmic work, neutralizing opposition).

Days of the Week

  • Wednesday (Mercury) — Purple candles are burned on Wednesdays to invoke Mercurian energy: intellect, communication, cunning, and swift action.
  • Saturday (Saturn) — Purple candles are also burned on Saturdays to invoke Saturnian energy: binding, endings, karmic justice, and discipline.
