Figure Candle

Also called
Image Candle, Human Candle
Category1
🕯️Candle
Rituals13
🌸Candle Dressing👤Eight-Night Image Candle Debt Spell💖Bride and Groom Candle Binding📌Zula-Zula Oil Hex🔮Basic Working🔮Do As I Say Oil Spell🕯️Black Devil Banishing Spell🕯️Boss Fix Candle Spell🕯️Divorce Figure Candle Timed Separation Spell🕯️Gamblers' Figure Candle Spell🕯️Has No Hanna Figure Candle Depression Spell🕯️Seven African Powers Figure Candle Rite😈Devil's Power Blast Protection Bag

Shaped like a man or woman. Figure candles are used in sympathetic magick targeting a specific person, including the practitioner. They are central to workings involving love, healing, domination, and binding. The candle is often dressed and named to represent the individual before the spell is cast.
