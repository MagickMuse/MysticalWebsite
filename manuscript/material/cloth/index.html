Magical Cloths

Cloths serve numerous purposes in spellcraft. Practitioners keep a variety of solid colors in cotton or other natural fabrics for wrapping tarot cards, crystals, and stones, holding herbs, constructing poppets, and crafting herbal or dream pillows.

Color Correspondences

The color of cloth used in magical work should align with the intention of the spell. White cloth serves as a universal option, while specific colors add their own energy to the working. Natural, undyed fabrics work well for neutral applications.

Storage and Wrapping

Many practitioners wrap their divination tools and crystals in cloth when not in use, both for protection and to prevent the items from absorbing unwanted energies. Silk is traditional for tarot cards, though cotton works equally well.
