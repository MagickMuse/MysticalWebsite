7-Day Candle

Also called
Novena Candle, 7-Day Glass Candle, Prayer Candle
Category1
🕯️Candle
Rituals3
🕯️Dr. José Gregorio Hernández Healing Petition🕯️Has No Hanna Seven-Day Candle Extra-Strength Depression Spell🕯️Jezebel Oil Seduction Seven-Day Candle Spell

A tall pillar candle set inside a glass cylinder, designed to burn continuously over the course of a week. Used for sustained, long-term workings such as healing, protection, petition spells, and devotional practice. The glass can be dressed with oils, labels, or sigils drawn directly on the surface. Rooted in Catholic folk traditions and widely adopted across hoodoo, rootwork, and eclectic practices.
