Storage Vessels

Glass bottles serve as essential storage containers for herbs, oils, spell components, and other magical supplies. Glass is preferred over plastic as it does not leach chemicals or absorb energies from its contents.

Cleansing New Bottles

Before use, rinse glass bottles with spring water and sea salt to cleanse them of any residual energies. Allow to dry completely before adding contents. Dark or amber glass protects light-sensitive materials like herbs and essential oils.

Spell Bottles

Beyond storage, glass bottles are used in spell work itself. Witch bottles, charm bottles, and spell jars all employ glass vessels to contain magical workings. The bottle becomes a sealed container holding the spell's energy until released or disposed of properly.

Moon Magick

Jar spells are among the most common workings in witchcraft, and jars earn their place in lunar practice as both spell vessels and storage containers. Use them to hold moon water, store herb blends and oils aligned to specific lunar intentions, or seal a completed jar spell charged under a particular moon phase. There is no need to purchase jars: grocery staples come in glass, and upcycling those containers is both practical and resourceful. Over time, a collection builds naturally. Jars provide an airtight environment that preserves the potency of dried herbs and blended oils between moon rituals.
