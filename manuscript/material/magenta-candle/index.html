Magical Properties

Magenta candles provide intense fuel for immediate action, enhancing the power of other candle colors and adding speed to any magical working.

Primary Uses

  • Immediate Action: Fuel for urgent magical work
  • Speed Enhancement: Accelerates the manifestation of spells
  • Power Amplification: Enhances the effectiveness of other candle colors

Urgent Magic

Magenta is ideal when time is of the essence:

  • Emergency situations requiring fast results
  • When delay could cause problems
  • Quickening slow-moving situations
  • Accelerating manifestation
  • Urgent protection

Color Amplification

  • Burn alongside any color to increase its power
  • Adds urgency and speed to the working
  • Intensifies energy
  • Pushes hesitant magic into action

Ritual Applications

Additional Correspondences

Traditional Color Correspondences

Magenta (or Burgundy) is a very dark but vibrant red with a deep purple tint, like a dark cranberry color. A very high vibrational frequency that tends to make things happen fast, it is usually burned alongside other candles. Burned alone, it is used for quick changes, spiritual healing, and exorcism.
