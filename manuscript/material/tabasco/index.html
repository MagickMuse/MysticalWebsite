🌶️Tabasco

Notes/Warnings
Tabasco is a fermented hot pepper sauce made from Tabasco peppers (Capsicum frutescens), vinegar, and salt. In spell-casting it is used for banishing and repulsion work. Contains no sugar or sweeteners, making it ideal for banishing spells where sweet ingredients would carry summoning energy contrary to the working's purpose. ⚠️ Do not touch eyes, mouth, or sensitive areas after handling. Wash hands thoroughly.

Magical Uses

Tabasco is used in banishing and repulsion workings, particularly those involving physical dressing of candles or objects. Its fiery, caustic nature drives away and punishes. The absence of sugar or sweeteners is magickally significant — sweet ingredients carry summoning energy, so Tabasco is ideal when the goal is pure expulsion.

Safety

⚠️ Always wash hands thoroughly after handling Tabasco in spell-casting contexts, especially when combined with other caustic ingredients like alum. Never touch eyes, mouth, or sensitive areas before washing.
