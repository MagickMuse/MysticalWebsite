Caring for Your Magick Tools

A guide to storing, cleansing, acquiring, and displaying your sacred implements.

What is an Altar?

If you do not have a dedicated altar, or if your altar is not suitable for casting spells, a flat surface such as a table, desk, or counter can be substituted. What matters is that you have a clean, stable workspace for your magickal practice.

Storage and Protection

How do you protect your expensive jewelry? Your heirloom silverware? Your favorite designer clothes? Devote the same care to storing your magick tools.

If you have an altar, you may wish to display your tools on it. Many witches, however, prefer to store their tools safely out of sight, partly to prevent other people from handling them and partly to avoid uncomfortable questions.

Traditional Storage Methods

  • Silk wrapping: Protects from dust, dirt, and ambient vibrations
  • Velvet pouches: Soft protection with elegant presentation
  • Wooden boxes: Natural material that won't interfere with energy
  • Dedicated drawers, trunks, closets, or cabinets: Secure and private

Like any precious possession, you want to safeguard your tools from damage and keep them from falling into the wrong hands.

Precautions and Protocols

When caring for and working with your magick tools, remember these guidelines:

Handling

  • Don't let others use or handle your tools, except perhaps a magickal partner with whom you work regularly
  • If someone touches a tool, smudge or wash it to remove their energy

Divination Decks

  • Use a different tarot deck for reading others than for reading yourself
  • Keep a third deck for spells
  • The same goes for rune sets

Cleansing

  • Clean and smudge all tools before you begin using them for magick
  • After initial cleansing, you needn't cleanse again unless someone else handles them
  • Always wash chalices and cauldrons used for food or drink before storing

Purpose

  • Use your tools for working magick only, not for mundane purposes
  • Treat your magick tools with care and respect, and they'll serve you for a lifetime

Acquiring Magick Tools

When you buy a car, you want to know something about its history. The same holds true for magick tools.

New Items

  • Check out the seller's reputation
  • Get information about the craftspeople who made the objects
  • Read users' reports online

Vintage and Antique Items

Tracking down pedigrees can get tricky. Antique chalices and swords may be exquisitely beautiful, reflecting a level of craftsmanship rarely seen today. However:

⚠️
Energy Warning: Magick tools used by someone else may hold that person's energy for a long time. This is especially true if the objects contain gemstones. Always check your tools' histories if possible before using them.

Cleansing, purifying, and consecrating practices can remove old energies, but knowing the history first is always wise.

Crafting Your Own

You can fabricate your own magick tools if you have skills in woodworking or metalsmithing.

Wands from Wood:

  • Choose a branch that has fallen naturally from the tree (perhaps in a storm or as part of aging)
  • Or request permission from the tree to cut a twig
  • After cutting, make an offering to the tree as thanks

Personalizing Purchased Items:

Even if you buy new items from a metaphysical store, you can add personal touches:

  • Use special paints from craft stores to decorate glass
  • Add meaningful images and symbols
  • Make your tools uniquely yours

Displaying Your Tools

Some witches like to display their primary tools on their altars, alongside:

  • Candles for illumination
  • Statues or deity representations
  • Items required for particular spells or rituals
  • Food and drink offerings
  • Book of shadows

Space Considerations

Things can get crowded on an altar. You're free to move items around as needed:

  • Set up a secondary altar
  • Create four shrines within your sacred space (one for each direction/element)
  • Do whatever you find comfortable and convenient
⚖️
Balance Tip: If you choose to leave your primary tools out on your altar, be sure to display all four for balance—wand, athame, chalice, and pentacle.
