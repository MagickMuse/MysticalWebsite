Figa Charm

≡Also called
Figa, Fica, Fig Hand Amulet, Mano Fica, Mano Figa
↗Rituals1
🛡️Figa Charm Child Protection Necklace

The figa (also fica or fig hand) is an amulet depicting a closed hand with the thumb tucked between the first and second fingers, forming the "fig" gesture. One of the oldest protective amulets in the Western world, it dates to at least ancient Roman times and is used across Mediterranean, Latin American, and Brazilian traditions.

Forms and Materials

Figa charms are made from a wide variety of materials including bone, coral, wood, stone, jet, gold, and silver. Charms carved from jet are considered most beneficial and protective for children specifically.

Magickal Uses

The figa is a powerful protective charm, particularly for babies and children. It is worn on a cord or chain as a necklace during the day and suspended above the child's sleeping area at night. It guards against the Evil Eye, malevolent spirits, and general harm.
