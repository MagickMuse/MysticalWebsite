Crystal balls are used for divination through scrying and for spirit summoning. The art of scrying is an ancient and well-respected technique that far transcends the stereotype of the fraudulent fortune teller. Crystal balls are perhaps the most recent evolution in this practice, particularly effective because the clear round form evokes the image of the moon.

The Art of Scrying

Scrying is divination through the use of reflective surfaces. It ranks among the more difficult divination techniques but is also likely one of the most ancient. The crystal ball is only one of many scrying tools. The first scryer presumably gazed into a still lake.

Roman images of the primal goddess Cybele depict her holding a flat pan of water for divination. A scrying tool may be as simple as that pan of water or as lavish as a star sapphire or star ruby, both famed divination devices. When using these precious gems, the practitioner gazes at their crossed lines and lets the mind wander.

Less expensive gemstones also serve well for scrying: aquamarine, clear quartz stones, and polished balls are all effective. Smoky quartz is considered particularly beneficial as a device for communicating with spirits or ghosts.

Alternative Scrying Methods

  • In India, the water in the pan may be replaced by a pool of ink
  • Egyptian techniques involved gazing into ink held in the left palm
  • The simplest method requires no tools at all: buff the left thumbnail to a high polish and gaze into it

Care and Charging

Unlike pans, crystal balls (like mirrors and gemstones) store visions and memories. They should be cleansed as needed, and a new crystal ball must be cleansed and charged before its first use.

  • Charge crystal balls by exposing them periodically to the light of the full moon
  • In some traditions, a crystal ball must be fully activated through exposure to the light of thirteen consecutive full moons before it is ready for use
  • If using the crystal ball for spiritual communication, keep it covered with a dark cloth between uses
