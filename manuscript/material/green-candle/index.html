Magical Properties

Green candles harness the power of growth, prosperity, and physical healing, making them among the most versatile candles for material and health concerns.

Primary Uses

  • Growth: Development, expansion, and increase
  • Prosperity: Financial success and wealth
  • Fertility: Conception and abundant harvests
  • Financial Success: Money, jobs, and material security
  • Healing: Especially for physical ailments including serious diseases

Physical Healing

Green candles are particularly powerful for physical health magic:

  • Healing physical ailments
  • Cancer and serious illnesses
  • Chronic conditions
  • Physical recovery
  • Body vitality
  • Immune system support

Prosperity and Growth

The fertile energy of green makes it ideal for:

  • Money spells
  • Job hunting and career growth
  • Business expansion
  • Investment growth
  • Agricultural success
  • Garden blessings

Ritual Applications

Use green candles for:

  • Money and prosperity rituals
  • Physical healing work
  • Fertility magic (crops, animals, humans)
  • Growth and expansion spells
  • Success in new ventures
  • Nature magic
  • Heart chakra work
  • Earth element rituals

Additional Correspondences

Green candles also govern:

  • Fast Money: Quick cash and immediate financial help
  • Healing Past Lives: Resolving karmic patterns and ancestral wounds
  • Stable Financial Resources: Use with brown candles for lasting wealth and material security
  • Stable Financial Resources: Use with brown candles for lasting wealth and material security

Candle Magic Applications

Green candles support both healing work and financial prosperity. Use green for physical health recovery and to manifest abundance in material form. Green brings persistent, steady growth energy that builds wealth and vitality over time.

Traditional Color Correspondences

Green is associated with abundance, fertility, good fortune, generosity, material gain, renewal, marriage, balance, healing, and communication with nature spirits. It can also give a fresh outlook on life or bring balance to an unstable situation.

Planetary Associations

  • Venus — Green is a primary Venus color, used on Fridays or when calling upon Venusian energy (love, beauty, growth, attraction, the arts).

Days of the Week

  • Friday (Venus) — Green candles are burned on Fridays to invoke Venusian energy: love, beauty, abundance, fertility, and attraction.
