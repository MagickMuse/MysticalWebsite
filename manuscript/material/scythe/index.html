One of the most familiar emblems of death, the scythe is the tool the Grim Reaper carries as he gathers his harvest of souls. The same tool was an attribute of the god Saturn, who also appears as Chronos, the god of time. In the hands of these ancient gods the scythe was not the doom laden instrument it becomes in the skeletal grip of the hooded Reaper; it stood instead as a reminder of the cycle of life.
