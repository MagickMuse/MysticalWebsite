The Offering Plate

The ritual plate holds bread, cakes, cookies, or other food offerings blessed during the Cakes and Wine ceremony. It represents the bounty of the Earth and the nourishment provided by the God and Goddess.

Purpose

Food placed upon the plate is consecrated and shared among ritual participants, with a portion often left as an offering to the Divine or to spirits of the land. The act of sharing food creates communion between practitioners and the sacred.

Materials

Plates may be ceramic, wood, metal, or glass. Many practitioners choose a plate with symbolic imagery or one that holds personal significance. The plate should be reserved for ritual use rather than everyday meals.

Moon Magick

Plates and trays are among the most practical and versatile tools you can keep for moon magick. Use them as portable bases for crystal grids, candle spells, and lunar offerings, moving them freely around your space as the working requires. A plate placed beneath a candle also provides an additional layer of fire safety, catching dripping wax and creating a barrier between flame and surface. Keeping a dedicated plate on your moon altar gives you a contained, intentional space to arrange your materials throughout the lunar cycle.
