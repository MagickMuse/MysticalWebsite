In Love Workings

In traditional witchcraft, the chalice is the primary instrument of romantic love workings and philter-craft. It is never replaced by the athame in this context: the athame is an instrument of defence or aggression and plays no part in the romantic branch of witchcraft. The chalice is dedicated to the subtler powers of the unseen considered feminine in nature, akin to the symbolism of Venus and Amor in classical tradition and more broadly to the elven-folk, fays, and enchantresses of medieval legend: Melusine, Viviane, Nimue, Brisen, the Lady of the Lake, Morgan le Fay. It is receptive and enchanting in character, as opposed to the thrusting aggressiveness of wand and athame.

In a love working setup, the chalice stands at the center of the altar triangle, flanked by the lamps of art, with the thurible present and the philter materials compounded in it using a pestle and mortar. The altar triangle is encircled with a ring of fresh flowers.
