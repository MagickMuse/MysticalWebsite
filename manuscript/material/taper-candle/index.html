Tall and slender, taper candles are the traditional choice for formal ritual use. Their elegant shape makes them ideal for altar work, invocations, and ceremonial settings. Tapers burn at a steady, moderate pace and are easy to dress with oil along their length. They are also well suited to meditation, divination, and any working that benefits from a focused, upright flame.
