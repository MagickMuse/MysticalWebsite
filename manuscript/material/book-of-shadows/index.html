Overview

Known under several names, including witches' Bible, workbook, Liber spirituum, and Book of Shadows, this is the personal spellbook every witch keeps, recording rituals, spells, and magical experience over time.

Among the Working Tools

Alongside the witches' knife, cord, censer, and cup, the spellbook completes the basic set of working tools, growing as a personal record of what has and hasn't worked in practice.

Construction and Consecration

A tradition minded witch may sew her own book from parchment and add a cover by hand. A simpler approach is to buy a thick book of good quality drawing paper, roughly the size of a large exercise book, then cover its spine and back during the waxing moon in a fabric of choice, velvet and moire silk are popular, though leather or skin (often reptile) are used as well. Covers range from black or white to red, green, or even silver and gold.

Once covered, exorcise the book with charged water and fire. Using a pen and ink reserved just for this purpose, draw a pentacle and its runes on the inside of the first page and the back of the last, chanting as each rune is drawn:

Book of words,

book of deeds,

blessed be

thou book of art!

Finally, write your witch name in the center of each pentacle, charging every letter as you go, and close with the words So mote it be! Together with the Athame, this book becomes a witch's most treasured possession, shown only to fellow witches and coven members.
