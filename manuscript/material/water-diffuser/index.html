💨Water Diffuser

Notes/Warnings
Simply and effectively disperses essential oils into the air. It takes only one molecule of volatile oil for cells in the olfactory epithelium to detect an odor. Since these specialized cells are so important for survival, the body replaces them every thirty to sixty days. A water diffuser atomizes the water contained within its reservoir and disperses it so much less essential oil is needed, thus saving money.
