🧂Salt Diffuser

◇Notes/Warnings
A no-energy way to diffuse synergies where a water diffuser is impractical (around electrical wiring or electronics). To make: Add 1⁄4 cup salt (table salt is cheapest, but large chunks of pink Himalayan salt can be beautifully decorative) to a large glass bowl. Add 10 to 15 drops of an essential oil or synergy. Place in a cubicle where a pronounced fragrance might bother coworkers, or on a bedside table. Because there is no water present, cleanup is easy and there is no danger of electrocution from electronics.
