Source Entry Notes

Salt is not an herb but is very important in many magickal workings. Coarse-grained sea salt or Dead Sea salt is best for rituals and spells as it has no additives (usually anti-caking agents). Salt is a creature of the Earth, and may be added with good effect to any incense of the Earth element, or a few grains to other types.

Salt carries a double elemental nature. Because the sea is full of salt, the mineral is bound up with the element of water, yet salt is also won by letting seawater evaporate on open ground, which ties it just as firmly to the Earth. The saying salt of the earth, used of someone good, grounded, and honest, reflects that earthy character.

Element and Protection

The cuboid structure of salt is a further reason it stands as a symbol of the earth element and as an emblem of protection. Its long practical use as a preservative of meat, fish, and other foods gave it a parallel symbolic role, that of sanctifying and protecting holy or magickal places. For this reason salt appears in rites from cathedral services to the Magick Circle. The practice may be a remnant of an older one in which the blood of sacrificial animals was sprinkled with salt so the salt would soak it up.

Value and Folklore

The power of salt to preserve food was invaluable to those making long sea voyages, when fresh meat was almost impossible to keep. Salt also draws out the flavor of any food it is added to, and it was once so prized that it served as currency to pay people for their work, which is the origin of the word salary, from the Latin salarium. Because salt is valuable in so many ways, spilling it is held to be a bad omen. The superstitious counter it by making a cross in the spilled salt and then throwing a pinch over the left shoulder, said to land in the eye of the Devil.

Child and Postpartum Protection

In some traditions, a new mother bathes daily in salt water for one lunar month following birth. This practice is intended to protect both mother and newborn from the Evil Eye and harmful spirits during the vulnerable postpartum period.

Alchemy

Alchemists held that salt represents the human body and counted it as one of the three vital natural ingredients, the three alchemical principles. In this scheme salt forms a trinity with sulphur and mercury.

Choosing Salt for Protection

Every form of salt offers strong spiritual and magickal protection, but sea salt is held to be the most potent. Hoodoo tradition favors kosher salt, regarding it as blessed. Any salt will serve in a pinch, though the closer it stays to its natural state, the less processed and tampered with, the more protective power it carries.
