🧡Oolong Tea

About Oolong Tea

Oolong is a partially withered tea from Camellia sinensis, mildly caffeinated with a soothing flavor somewhere between green and black tea. It represents the marriage of black and green tea, making its magical action that of love.

Brewing Instructions

  • Amount: 1 tsp tea
  • Temperature: 195°F (90°C)
  • Steep Time: 3-4 minutes

Magical Correspondences

Season: Fall

Associations:

  • Peace and inner examination
  • Divination and meditation
  • Centering emotions
  • Affection and love
  • Balance and harmony

Magical Applications

The ritual of brewing oolong leaves time for meditation and inner thought, making it appropriate for working magic for self-examination while brewing.

Balance Work: The balance of green and black tea leaves makes oolong perfect for finding balance in mind and heart.

Love Magic: Its nature as a "marriage" of tea types makes it ideal for relationship harmony and deepening affection.

Divination Use: Oolong's partial fermentation places it between green and black tea, making it the right choice for readings about crossroads, mixed states, and decisions between two clear paths. If recurring cards in a tarot spread are creating confusion rather than clarity, oolong can help bring a more nuanced perspective to the reading.
