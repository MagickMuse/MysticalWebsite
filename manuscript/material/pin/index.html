A pin is a small, pointed implement used in sympathetic and folk magick to pierce, fix, bind, or direct intention. Pins are driven into candles to mark intentions and release them as the wax melts, inserted into poppets to direct energy toward a target, and used to break patterns in water divination such as oil scrying. Their sharp point makes them instruments of focused will, cutting through influence or fixing something in place.
