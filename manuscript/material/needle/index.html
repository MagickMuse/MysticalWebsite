🪡Needle

Notes/Warnings
In the context of poppet magick, needles that have been ritually exorcised and consecrated for use in working are called Dagydes. A Dagyde is driven into a designated body part of the poppet with a conjuration to direct the working. See the Operation of Grand Bewitchment for full usage context.
