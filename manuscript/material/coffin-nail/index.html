Coffin Nails

Coffin nails are generally considered a component of baneful and negative magick with a rich history across Hoodoo and other magickal systems. However, they, or any nail, can also be imbued with the properties of "fixing down" energies or sealing a spell. Iron nails are preferable due to their energetic properties. Genuine coffin nails, those formerly used to affix a coffin lid or any part of a casket, are exceptionally rare.

The use of coffin nails in magickal spells and ritual is said to raise the level of power and sharpen its focus. They are primarily used for:

  • Protection: Negating or neutralizing malevolent influences and rendering them useless
  • Breaking spells and curses
  • Dark art and Luciferian initiation
  • Closure, purging, revenge, and destruction

The exact roles and rituals involving coffin nails depend on the particular craft practices of the magician.

Respect and Responsibility

Coffin nails are magickal instruments with intrinsic power that must be treated with respect. They are not toys or novelty items. If you come into possession of genuine coffin nails, you must honor the spirit of the deceased whose coffin they came from.

Iron and Steel

In ancient times, nails and spikes were made of iron. Modern nails are made of steel, a mixture of iron and carbon, which works just as effectively. The exception is railroad spikes, but for subtle ritual workings only a nail will do. Iron is a natural metal said to carry a very powerful protective and disruptive energy.

Connecting with the Dead

Folk magick practitioners use coffin nails as a way of connecting with the dead, believing that spirits possess many abilities to influence and control things beyond the earthly realm. Some practitioners use the nails to work with a specific spirit or ancestor they knew in life or work with frequently.

When petitioned for aid and compensated properly, spirits will lend their assistance and the power of the spellwork improves. Coffin nails are not limited to protection or curses; many practitioners across folk magick and Pagan traditions use them in nearly any spell to boost spiritual energy.

Dark Magick Applications

In baneful magick, the principle of contagious magick applies: whatever caused the death of the person whose coffin the nail came from will transfer itself through the nail and cause harm to the target of the spell. Any hex is said to gain potency when coffin nails are substituted for the generally prescribed pin.

In African traditions, an image of an enemy is crafted and kept ready for use. Whenever the practitioner feels threatened, a coffin nail is driven into the image, shielding the practitioner from any harm intended by their enemy.

In Voodoo, a spell to drive an enemy to madness is performed using a doll, a coffin nail, and black arts oil.

Coffin nails remain in active use today across Wicca, Witchcraft, Voodoo, Hoodoo, Obeah, Santeria, and other traditions.

Sourcing Coffin Nails

For obvious reasons, genuine coffin nails are difficult to come by. Deliberately digging up old coffins in a graveyard to harvest nails and wood is desecration, and it can bring down the wrath of the spirit of the deceased as well as serious legal consequences.

You can make your own coffin nails using steel nails, or purchase them from occult shops. They are also available online in small lots.

Making Your Own Coffin Nails

What You Need

  • A package of nails
  • Graveyard dirt (selected to match your intent)
  • A resealable plastic bag

Instructions

  1. Count out the number of nails your spell requires and charge them with your desire.
  2. Place the nails in the bag and sprinkle them with a few tablespoons of graveyard dirt appropriate to your working.
  3. If the spell calls for rusty nails, sprinkle the nails generously with water before sealing.
  4. Seal the bag and allow the nails to sit in the dirt overnight. They will be ready for use the next day.
Important: If your spell calls for rusty nails, do NOT wipe the nails clean after soaking. The rust and dirt residue are part of the working.

Nails from Other Locations

Nails sourced from specific locations carry the energy of that place and can be used to direct spellwork accordingly:

  • Courthouse: To bring justice, or to cause the target to lose a legal battle
  • Hospital: To cause illness
  • Jail: To bring jail or prison time
  • Mental Institution: To cause insanity
  • Police Station: To bring about an arrest
  • Workplace: To cause trouble for a coworker or employer
