🍸Cocktail Shaker

Magickal Applications

The cocktail shaker serves as a vessel of transformation, combining separate ingredients into a unified whole. The act of shaking builds energy through motion—similar to dancing, drumming, or other methods of raising power.

Reading the Frost

When frost appears on the metal of the shaker, the drink is properly chilled and the energy has been raised. This visible sign indicates the working is ready to be released into your glass.

Visualization During Shaking

The rhythmic shaking motion creates an ideal state for visualization. As you shake, hold your intention clearly in mind. The physical exertion and repetitive motion help bypass the conscious mind and direct energy into the drink.
