🔩Railroad Spikes

Category1
✨Foils & Metals
Correspondances1
🛡️Protection

Protective Home Magick

Railroad spikes are commonly used in protective magick, particularly for the home. Made of iron or steel, they carry the protective properties associated with these metals.

Rune Candle Magick

Railroad spikes make excellent scribes for carving runes into pillar and taper candles. Their iron composition adds protective energy to the working, and their sturdy construction allows for precise, deep carving.

Best uses:

  • Protection spells
  • Home blessing candles
  • Warding work
  • Any candle magick requiring the strength of iron
