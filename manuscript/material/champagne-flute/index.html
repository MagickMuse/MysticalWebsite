🥂Champagne Flute

Notes/Warnings
Tall, narrow glass designed to preserve bubbles. Ideal for broadcasting intentions and celebratory magick.
Category1
🍷Drinkware
Recipes2
💃Ginger Rogers Passion🍊Mimosa Marvel

Magickal Applications

The champagne flute's tall, narrow shape directs energy upward—making it the perfect vessel for spells that announce your intent to the universe. The rising bubbles carry your wishes skyward.

Why Bubbles Matter

Champagne's effervescence isn't just festive. Each bubble that rises and pops releases a tiny burst of energy into the air. When you charge a drink in a flute, you're creating hundreds of tiny messengers carrying your intention outward.

Best Uses

  • Broadcasting intentions to the universe
  • Celebratory magick
  • Manifestation work
  • New beginnings and announcements
  • Success spells
