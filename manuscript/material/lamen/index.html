Lamen

Notes/Warnings
Ritual pendant worn over the heart. From Latin meaning plate, symbolic of the High Priest's breastplate. Used in ceremonial magick to express authority and protect during spirit work.
Category1
💎Adornments

A lamen is a specialized ritual pendant worn on the chest over the heart, suspended by a cord, collar, or ribbon, or sometimes stitched directly onto the robe itself. The word comes from a Latin word meaning "plate," marking it as symbolic of the breastplate of the High Priest in the book of Exodus.

Purpose

The lamen is a type of phylactery worn by magicians to:

  • Express their authority
  • Protect them from spirits invoked, evoked, or commanded during ritual

Historical Forms

In medieval times, lamens often took the form of a double-sided piece of parchment with:

  • Front side: The name and sigil of the spirit being invoked
  • Back side: The name and sigil of the angel who controls the spirit, lying protectively against the magician's heart

Notable Historical Lamens

The Book of Abramelin (15th century)

A silver plate upon which the Holy Guardian Angel wrote letters in dew.

The Fourth Book of Occult Philosophy

A golden lamen inscribed with crosses and the name Tetragrammaton, affixed to the front of a white linen robe.

John Dee's Heptarchia (16th century)

Enochian letters inscribed on a four-inch square of paper, hung around the neck when dealing with spirits.

The Lamen of Michael (from The Magus)

Shows the name of Michael in Hebrew, along with the archangel's sigil placed within a hexagram, surrounded by six pentagrams. Used for drawing spirits into crystals.

The Rose Cross Lamen (Golden Dawn)

Combines elements, planets, zodiacal signs, Sephiroth, Hebrew alphabet, pentagrams, hexagrams, and alchemical principles. Worn by every adept of the Inner Order.

Key Distinction

The lamen is similar to a talisman in that both function as repositories of specific magickal energies. However, a lamen is specifically worn by magicians to express their authority and protect them during spirit work, and is only used in its intended ritual.
