Living Water

Spring water collected from a natural source carries the vital energy of the Earth. This living water is used in spell work, ritual, and for cleansing magical tools and spaces. Its purity and connection to the land make it valuable for magical applications.

Collection and Storage

Water collected at a natural spring should be stored in glass rather than plastic to preserve its energetic properties. If purchasing prepackaged spring water, transfer it to a prepared glass bottle that has been cleansed with sea salt.

Magical Uses

Spring water serves as a base for magical washes, floor washes, and blessing waters. It may be added to baths, used to cleanse crystals (those that are water-safe), and incorporated into any spell requiring pure water. Some practitioners collect spring water during specific moon phases or seasons to enhance particular properties.
