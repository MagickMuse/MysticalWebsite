Burning Resin Incense

Charcoal discs provide the heat source needed to burn loose resin incenses such as copal, dragon's blood, frankincense, and myrrh. Unlike stick or cone incense, resins require sustained heat to release their fragrant smoke.

How to Use

Place the charcoal disc in a heat-safe container partially filled with sand. Light the edge of the disc and wait for it to spark across the surface, turning gray as it heats. Once the disc is glowing, place small amounts of resin on top. The resin will melt and release smoke.

Safety Considerations

Charcoal discs become extremely hot and will scorch any surface beneath them. Always use a proper censer, cauldron, or heat-resistant dish with sand. Never handle a lit disc with bare hands. Allow discs to cool completely before disposal.
