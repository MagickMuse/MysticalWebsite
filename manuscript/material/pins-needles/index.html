What Are Pins & Needles?

Pins and needles in magical practice refer to standard straight pins or sewing needles used to pierce, seal, bind, or mark magical workings. They are not specialized ritual tools — ordinary pins from a fabric store are perfectly appropriate.

Magical Uses

Sealing and Binding

In Hoodoo beef tongue spells and poppet work, pins and needles are inserted into the working to seal intentions in place. Each pin seals a slit made to insert a name paper, physically and symbolically locking the person's influence or testimony in place.

Marking and Directing

Pins can mark specific areas of a poppet or material to direct magical intent toward a specific part of a person's life — their speech, their actions, their involvement in a situation.

Candle Work

Pins are sometimes pushed into candles at specific intervals to mark when a candle should stop burning (as a timer) or to fix intentions into the wax.

Lilith Protection

Place a needle close to the wick of a candle or oil lamp in the room where a woman who must be protected from Lilith sleeps.

Disposal

Pins and needles used in binding or sealing workings carry the energy of the spell and should be disposed of thoughtfully:

  • Do not reuse them.
  • Do not throw them out inside your home.
  • Carry them away from your home in a brown paper bag.
  • Tradition suggests going and returning by different, circuitous routes so the energy does not follow you back.
