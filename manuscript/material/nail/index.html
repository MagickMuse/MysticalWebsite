🔩Horseshoe Nail

Piercing and Pinning Work

A nail's point and iron content have long made it a tool for piercing, pinning, and restraining, whether driven into the ground to anchor a spirit in place or hammered into a poppet to fix an intention.

Virgin Nails

Some workings specify a virgin nail, meaning one that has never been used for any mundane or magical purpose. This freshness is thought to leave the nail free of outside influence, ready to take on the working's intent alone.
