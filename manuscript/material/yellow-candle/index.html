Magical Properties

Yellow candles radiate the bright, joyful energy of love and romance, bringing warmth and affection to relationship magic.

Primary Uses

  • Love: Affectionate, joyful love
  • Romance: Sweet, happy romantic relationships

Love and Affection

Yellow brings a lighter quality to love magic:

  • Joyful love
  • Friendship in relationships
  • Happy romance
  • Affection and warmth
  • Playful love
  • Companionship

Cheerful Romance

Yellow romance is bright and optimistic:

  • New love and attraction
  • Fun and laughter in relationships
  • Lighthearted courtship
  • Communication in love
  • Mental connection
  • Shared joy

Solar Love

Unlike red's passionate fire, yellow offers:

  • Warm, sunny affection
  • Intellectual attraction
  • Cheerful partnership
  • Optimistic love
  • Mental and emotional connection

Ritual Applications

Use yellow candles for:

  • Love and romance spells emphasizing joy
  • Attracting happy relationships
  • Bringing lightness to love
  • Communication in partnerships
  • Friendship within romance
  • Cheerful courtship magic
  • Solar plexus work in relationships
  • Mental connection with lovers

Additional Correspondences

Yellow candles also govern:

  • Success: Achievement and accomplishment
  • Thought: Mental clarity and focus
  • Will: Determination and intent
  • Intent: Clear purpose and direction
  • Intent: Clear purpose and direction

Candle Magic Applications

Yellow candles enhance study, learning, and communication. Use yellow to improve mental clarity, aid in acquiring new knowledge, and attract friends or strengthen social connections. Yellow brings the bright, clear energy needed for intellectual pursuits and joyful communication.

Traditional Color Correspondences

Yellow aids with intellect, imagination, power of the mind, creativity, confidence, gentle persuasion, attraction, concentration, inspiration, mental clarity, knowledge, commerce, medicine, counseling, and healing.

Planetary Associations

  • Mercury — Yellow is a primary Mercury color, used on Wednesdays or for Mercurian energy (communication, intellect, learning, business).
  • Sun — Deep yellow is a Sun color, used on Sundays or for solar energy (vitality, success, illumination, happiness).

Days of the Week

  • Sunday (The Sun) — Yellow candles are burned on Sundays to invoke solar energy: vitality, success, authority, and illumination.
  • Wednesday (Mercury) — Yellow candles are also burned on Wednesdays to invoke Mercurian energy: communication, intellect, learning, and commerce.
