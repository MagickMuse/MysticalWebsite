Candle Magic Applications

Orange candles support creativity, innovation, and adaptability. Use orange to address sexual issues for women and to bring flexibility when facing changes or transitions. Orange helps you adjust your approach and find creative solutions when original plans need modification.

Traditional Color Correspondences

Orange helps with adaptability, encouragement, stimulation, attraction, sudden changes, prosperity, creativity, enthusiasm, success, energy and stamina, and mental agility. It also discourages laziness, helps to gain control, draws good things, and changes luck. As this is a very fast-acting color, be careful and certain you are willing to face major changes.

Planetary Associations

  • Mercury — Orange is a Mercury color, used on Wednesdays or for Mercurian energy (communication, intellect, commerce, swift change).
  • Sun — Orange is also a Sun color, used on Sundays or for solar energy (vitality, creativity, confidence, success).
