Eggs as Fertility Symbol

Eggs rank among the most ancient and powerful of all fertility symbols, appearing across cultures as embodiments of the cosmic creative force, new life, and rebirth. The shell suggests the cosmos itself; the yolk, the sun; the white, the primordial void from which life emerges. Their use in spells is as old as the symbolism — the eggshell carried as an amulet represents creation in miniature.

Amulet Uses

An eggshell carried as an amulet symbolizes the cosmos, creation, and fertility.

Moon Magick

Key themes for lunar workings: fertility, new beginnings, rebirth, purification, divination, protection.
