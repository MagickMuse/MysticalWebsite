Magical Properties

Black candles are powerful tools for both malevolent and protective magic, embodying the full spectrum of shadow work.

Primary Uses

  • Fertility: Black absorbs and transforms energy, supporting fertility work
  • Healing: Particularly effective for deep, transformative healing
  • Prosperity: Draws wealth through shadow work and removing obstacles
  • Protection: One of the strongest protective candle colors

Defensive Magic

Black candles are particularly powerful in defensive magic to counteract another's negative intentions. They absorb harmful energy, reverse curses, and create barriers against malevolent forces.

Baneful Work

While black candles are burned in malevolent spells, practitioners should approach such work with extreme caution and clear ethical consideration. Black candle work always carries consequences and should never be undertaken lightly.

Ritual Applications

Use black candles for:

  • Banishing unwanted influences
  • Breaking hexes and curses
  • Absorbing negative energy
  • Shadow work and confronting darkness
  • Binding spells
  • Protection rituals
  • Reversing spells sent against you

Additional Correspondences

Black candles also govern:

  • Banishing: Removing unwanted energies and influences
  • Hex Breaking: Destroying curses and negative magic
  • Breaking Bad Habits: Ending destructive patterns

Traditional Color Correspondences

Black absorbs and removes anything — it reverses, uncrosses, binds negative forces, protects, releases, breaks up blockages, and unsticks stagnant situations. It also creates confusion and discord among enemies or repels negative thoughtforms. This color is one of the most powerful available. Take care not to use black for selfish reasons or evil purposes; the energy can backfire.

Planetary Associations

  • Saturn — Black is a primary Saturn color, used on Saturdays or when calling upon Saturnian energy (endings, binding, discipline, karmic work).

Days of the Week

  • Saturday (Saturn) — Black candles are burned on Saturdays to invoke Saturnian energy: endings, binding, banishing, discipline, and karmic work.
