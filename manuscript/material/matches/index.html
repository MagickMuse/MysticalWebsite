Bringing Fire to Ritual

Matches provide the spark that lights candles, incense, and ritual fires. Both regular and long stick varieties serve this purpose, with longer matches offering safety when lighting candles in deep holders or cauldrons.

Matches vs. Lighters

Some practitioners prefer matches for their elemental purity, as striking a match involves all four elements: the wood (Earth), the friction (Fire), the air feeding the flame (Air), and the chemical reaction (Water in the form of transformation). Others find lighters more practical and see no diminishment in magical effectiveness.

Practical Considerations

Keep matches stored in a dry location. Long fireplace matches prove especially useful for lighting items in deep vessels without burning fingers. Some practitioners keep dedicated ritual matches separate from household supplies.
