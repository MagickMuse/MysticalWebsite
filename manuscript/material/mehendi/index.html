🤚Mehendi

◇Notes/Warnings
Henna body art, drawn from the henna plant (Lawsonia inermis).
≡Also called
Mehndi; Henna
↗Category1
💎Adornments

Overview

Mehendi is the ancient art of adorning the hands and feet with henna paste, drawn from the henna plant, Lawsonia inermis. It is practiced wherever the plant grows, though India is most closely tied to the form. The paste was once prepared at home through an elaborate process and is now often bought ready-made in tubes for easy application.

Symbolism and Use

Its best-known use is decorating a bride for her wedding, and brides of lesser means wear mehendi in place of costly gold jewelry. The styles and meanings of the patterns vary by country and region, and many traditional meanings have been lost over time, but most designs carry wishes for fertility, prosperity, and a happy life. The names of the bride and groom are sometimes hidden among the curling lines, and by custom a bride does no housework until her wedding mehendi has faded away.
