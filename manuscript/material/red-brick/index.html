Red Brick in Magic

Red bricks are used in Hoodoo magical workings as grounding, sealing, and enclosing vessels. Their weight, solidity, and earth origin make them natural symbols of imprisonment, stasis, and immovable force.

🔒
Closed Practice: Brick magic of this type is rooted in Hoodoo tradition. Approach with respect.

Magical Properties

  • Element: Earth
  • Energy: Grounding, sealing, freezing in place, confinement
  • Color significance: Red = heat, intensity, domination

Court Case Use

In Hoodoo court case spellwork, two red bricks are used as a vegetarian alternative to the beef tongue spell. Name papers are placed between the bricks, effectively sandwiching and trapping the targets. A metal bucket of ice set atop the bricks during proceedings adds a freezing element, locking opponents in place.

The bricks may be:

  • Sprinkled or dressed with menstrual blood or urine to add personal power
  • Ringed with appropriately colored burning candles
  • Kept assembled until the legal matter is fully resolved

Additional Uses

Red brick dust (Rojo) is a separate and well-known Hoodoo material — the dust scraped from red bricks — used to mark thresholds for protection and to define sacred or protected space. The dust and the brick itself carry related but distinct uses.
