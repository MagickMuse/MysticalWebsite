Binding and Tying Magic

Thread and embroidery floss in various colors serve for tying up magical works, binding spell components together, and securing charm bags. The act of tying creates a bond, while cutting or untying releases the energy.

Color Selection

Choose thread colors that align with the intention of the working. Red thread is common in protection and love magic across many cultures. Black binds and banishes, white purifies, green attracts prosperity. Natural cotton or silk threads are preferred over synthetic materials.

Applications

Thread is used to bind poppets, secure herb bundles, tie charm bags closed, create witch's ladders, and wrap spell components together. The number of times thread is wrapped or knotted often holds magical significance, with three, seven, and nine being particularly powerful numbers.
