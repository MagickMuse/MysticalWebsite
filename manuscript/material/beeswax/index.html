Epidemic Prevention

According to the metaphysical wisdom of the Andaman Islanders, the scent of beeswax offends the demons responsible for epidemics. Burn beeswax candles, maintain blocks of beeswax in the home, or dip wooden stakes in melted beeswax and drive them into the ground around the perimeter of the home to serve as sentinels.
