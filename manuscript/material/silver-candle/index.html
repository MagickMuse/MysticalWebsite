Magical Properties

Silver candles channel the cool, reflective power of the moon, connecting to lunar deities, personal fertility, and the mysteries of success through intuition.

Primary Uses

  • Lunar Power: Harnessing moon energy
  • Personal Fertility: Conception and creative fertility
  • Success: Achievement through intuition and timing
  • Lunar Deities: Connecting to moon goddesses and gods

Moon Magic

Silver embodies lunar energy:

  • Moon phases and cycles
  • Intuition and psychic ability
  • Dreams and visions
  • The subconscious
  • Mystery and hidden knowledge
  • Feminine power (receptive, cyclical)

Fertility and Cycles

Silver candles work with natural rhythms:

  • Personal fertility and conception
  • Menstrual cycle magic
  • Creative fertility
  • Natural timing
  • Working with the body's wisdom

Success Through Intuition

Silver brings success through:

  • Psychic guidance
  • Right timing
  • Intuitive decisions
  • Hidden opportunities
  • Receptive manifestation

Ritual Applications

Use silver candles for:

  • Moon phase rituals
  • Lunar deity worship
  • Fertility magic
  • Psychic development
  • Dream work
  • Success spells requiring intuition
  • Goddess worship
  • Water element magic
  • Reflection and scrying
  • New and Full Moon celebrations

Additional Correspondences

Silver candles also govern:

  • The Goddess: Feminine divine energy and moon deities
  • Resolve Inner Conflict: Healing internal struggles and contradictions
  • Persistence: Endurance and continued effort
  • Remove Negativity: Cleansing harmful energies and influences

Traditional Color Correspondences

Silver (or very clear light gray) aids with victory and stability, helps with meditation, develops psychic abilities, removes negative powers, neutralizes any situation, and repels destructive forces.

Planetary Associations

  • Moon — Silver is the primary Moon color, used on Mondays or when calling upon lunar energy (intuition, dreams, psychic ability, cycles, the divine feminine).

Days of the Week

  • Monday (The Moon) — Silver candles are burned on Mondays to invoke lunar energy: intuition, dreams, psychic development, cycles, and the divine feminine.
