Overview

Along with the pendant and the necklace, the ring is one of the only witch jewels set with actual gemstones. Rings and pendants serve as the primary "fascination" jewels: the more distinctive the stone, the more effective it's believed to be. Sapphire and opal are considered the best choices, though most stones traditionally credited with warding off the evil eye work well, since the same qualities make them strong accumulators of witch power.

Fascination Gems

Stones traditionally set into rings and pendants include: Amber, Beryl, Bloodstone, Carnelian, Cat's-eye, Coral, Diamond, Emerald, Garnet, Jade, Jasper, Jet, Lapis Lazuli, Moonstone, Onyx, Peridot, Sardonyx, Staurolite (Cross-stone), Ruby, Turquoise, and Zircon.

Engravings

A witch name may be engraved on the back of the ring or around the stone itself, sometimes joined by a zodiac sign or a Cabalistic word of power such as Ararita, Tetragrammaton, Mehafelon, Ananizapta, or Shemhamphorash.

The Poison Ring

A hollow poison ring makes a clever choice, since its inner cavity can hide a written charm or a philter powder.

Metal Correspondences

  • Gold: energy and general success
  • Silver: intuition and magical ability
  • Copper: success in love
  • Brass or fixed mercury: mental agility
  • Tin: expansiveness and generosity
  • Iron: courage and aggressive instinct
  • Lead: stability

Blending several of these metals produces an alloy known as magical electrum, though gold, silver, or copper alone are the more common choices.

Consecration

Like other witch jewels, the ring is exorcised and consecrated with fire and water during the waxing moon, then named with the possessor's witch name.
