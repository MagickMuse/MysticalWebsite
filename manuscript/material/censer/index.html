The Portable Incense Vessel

A censer is a container designed to hold burning incense during ritual. Unlike stationary incense burners, censers are typically portable, allowing practitioners to carry sacred smoke around the Circle or throughout the ritual space.

Construction and Materials

Censers may be fashioned from brass, ceramic, shells, or other heat-resistant materials. Many feature chains or handles for safe carrying while the incense burns. The interior should be partially filled with clean sand to anchor the incense and protect the vessel from excessive heat.

Ritual Use

The censer is carried deosil around the Circle to purify and consecrate the space with sacred smoke. It may also rest upon the altar when not being carried. The rising smoke carries prayers and intentions upward while cleansing the area of unwanted energies.

Distinction from Incense Burner

While a stationary incense burner remains in place on the altar or designated spot, the censer is meant for procession. Both serve the purpose of safely containing burning incense, but the censer's portability makes it essential for rituals requiring the practitioner to move smoke throughout the space.
