Red String

◇Notes/Warnings

🧿Evil Eye

A length of red thread or string worn around the left wrist marks the wearer as a follower of Kabbalah, an esoteric strand of Jewish mysticism. The string is said to protect against the Evil Eye and the envy of others. It is an deliberately humble object, a reminder of our modest origins and of human fallibility. It is worn on the left because that side is believed to receive energy first.

Knots and Blessing

Red string is tied around the tomb of Rachel, the great Jewish matriarch, then cut into lengths and given as amulets. Each piece is bound with seven knots, and with every knot the wearer is reminded to hold back negative thoughts about others, so the charm carries only positive intention.

Easing Labor

Wind red silk thread around the body of a laboring woman to help ease her pains during childbirth.
