Stones can act as magical batteries. While specific stones are mentioned in the entry for each plant, there are a near infinite number of stones to choose from for the purpose of magic.

Reference Books

To learn more about crystal magic, see:

  • The Book of Stones by Robert Simmons and Naisha Ahsian
  • Melody's Love Is in the Earth: A Kaleidoscope of Crystals (Updated) — a comprehensive reference to the mineral kingdom. Note: the original volume does not include photos, so it is of more use to experienced practitioners who can identify the stones they are working with.

Using Stones in Charm Bags

When using stones in charm bags, smaller is usually better. The stone's size will not impact its effect.

Once the spell, working, or intention has been completed, the charm can be opened and the stone removed, cleansed, and set aside to be used again. Simply compost or discard the other materials as appropriate.
