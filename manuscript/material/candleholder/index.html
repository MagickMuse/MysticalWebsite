Moon Magick

If candles are part of your lunar practice, a firesafe candleholder is an essential companion. Safety comes first: always have a reliable holder on hand rather than improvising. In a pinch, a bowl filled with sand works as a makeshift holder and provides a stable base for candles of various sizes. If you frequently work with candles, invest in a dedicated holder suited to the type of candle you burn most often. You can also stabilize a candle by briefly holding a lighter flame to the bottom until a little wax melts, then pressing it firmly onto a fireproof surface such as a plate. This technique works well for taper and chime candles when no holder is available.
