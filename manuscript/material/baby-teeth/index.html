Baby Teeth

Baby teeth are the shed deciduous teeth of a child. As a personal curio, they carry a strong sympathetic link to the individual child they came from, making them potent ingredients in spells of protection, identity, and ancestral or reincarnation workings.

Magickal Uses

Baby teeth are used as personal taglock items in child protection charms and mojo hands. Because they are shed naturally, they represent a transition point in the child's physical development and are considered spiritually charged with the child's essence.
