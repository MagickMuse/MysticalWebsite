An amulet is an object worn on a person's body to attract good luck or, more often, to protect the wearer from harm, illness, or evil influences. The word derives from the Latin amuletum, which comes from the Arabic hamalet, meaning an object worn on the body or around the neck to defend against a host of ills.

Characteristics

Amulets are primarily defensive objects. They repel or shield rather than attract. In ancient times, magicians often mass-produced amulets for their clients.

Materials

Amulets can be made from a number of different materials, including:

  • Gemstones
  • Metals
  • Herbs
  • Parchment containing written text

They can also take the form of coins, rings, pendants, bracelets, and other types of jewelry.

Key Distinction

While a talisman is an active object created and consecrated with magickal power toward the achievement of a specific purpose, an amulet is a passive magickal object of general protection which cannot be controlled by its wearer.

In short: Amulets keep things away from you.

Two Forms

Amulets typically take one of two forms:

  • Written amulets -- texts, symbols, or sigils inscribed on paper, parchment, or metal
  • Independent objects -- physical items such as stones, jewelry, or crafted figures that carry protective power in their material nature

Magick spells are frequently required in order to create effective amulets.

Amulets for Infants and Children

A host of protective amulets evolved to guard infants and children against extreme psychic vulnerability. These amulets were devised in eras when fears for a baby's psychic safety outweighed fears for physical safety, before "infant-proofing" existed as a concept — yet babies were also rarely left unsupervised, kept in a cradle beside an adult or carried on someone's body. When adapting these amulets today, account for modern physical safety (choking, strangulation, small parts) alongside the original psychic intent; there is no point warding off the Evil Eye while leaving a baby vulnerable to a hazard the original design never anticipated.
