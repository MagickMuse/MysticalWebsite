🥩Beef Tongue

What Is Beef Tongue in Magic?

Raw beef tongue is the primary physical vehicle for one of the most notorious genres of court case spellwork in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo tradition. The tongue's obvious symbolic association — speech, testimony, persuasion — makes it the ideal vessel for workings intended to control what is said in a legal proceeding.

🔒
Closed Practice: Beef tongue court case spellwork originates in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo, which are closed practices with African American cultural and spiritual lineage. Approach with respect.

Historical Background

Beef tongue spells have ancient roots reaching back to Great Britain, featuring sheep and cow hearts as frequently as tongues in older workings. Transported to the Western hemisphere, these spells were developed and refined particularly in Hoodoo and New Orleans Voodoo traditions, and today the tongue version remains the most popular — likely because beef tongue is still available from butchers, whereas sheep hearts require explanation.

This is not animal sacrifice. The meat is obtained as a normal food product from a butcher. Raw beef tongue can still be purchased from specialty butchers, though it has become a more expensive item in many areas.

How It Works

The tongue is slit to receive name papers (the names of opponents, witnesses, judges, or attorneys), then sealed with pins and needles. It is then treated with cayenne and vinegar to "heat up" and "sour" the target's influence. Depending on the working, it is:

  • Frozen (to freeze out opposition)
  • Cooked and eaten (to internalize power over the targets)
  • Suspended over fire (for a hung jury effect)
  • Buried wrapped in silk (traditional no-refrigeration method)

Sourcing

Raw beef tongue can be purchased from:

  • Specialty or ethnic butchers
  • Latin American grocery stores (commonly stocked for lengua dishes)
  • Farmers markets with whole-animal butchers
