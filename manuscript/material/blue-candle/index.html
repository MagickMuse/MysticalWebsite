Magical Properties

Blue candles channel the healing, protective power of water and the Sacred Mother, offering soothing energy for emotional and psychic work.

Primary Uses

  • Healing: Especially effective for emotional and psychic healing
  • Protection: Creates gentle but powerful shields
  • Sacred Mother Energy: Connects to maternal divine energies
  • Banishing Malevolent Spirits: Blue light drives away harmful entities

Emotional and Psychic Healing

Blue candles excel at healing wounds of the heart and mind. Use them for:

  • Processing grief and trauma
  • Healing emotional pain
  • Psychic protection and cleansing
  • Calming anxiety and fear
  • Restoring peace to troubled minds

Spirit Work

The frequency of blue light is particularly effective at banishing malevolent spirits and creating sacred, protected space for spiritual work.

Ritual Applications

Burn blue candles for:

  • Emotional healing rituals
  • Psychic development and protection
  • Meditation and tranquility
  • Invoking the Sacred Mother or maternal deities
  • Cleansing spaces of negative entities
  • Dream work and peaceful sleep
  • Truth-seeking and communication

Additional Correspondences

Blue candles also govern:

  • Spiritual Healing: Deep soul healing and spiritual recovery
  • Physical Healing: Bodily health and wellness
  • Wisdom: Deep understanding and knowledge
  • Balance: Harmony and equilibrium
  • Trust: Faith and confidence in others
  • Tranquility: Peace and calm
  • Tranquility: Peace and calm

Candle Magic Applications

Blue candles enhance loyalty, trust, and honesty in all relationships. Use blue to encourage clear thinking and resolve conflicts through calm communication. Blue brings the soothing energy needed to restore peace and rebuild trust when relationships have been strained.

Traditional Color Correspondences

The uses of blue depend upon the depth of its hue. Light blue is for truth, inspiration, wisdom, protection, understanding, good health, happiness, inner peace, fidelity, patience, harmony in the home, and contacting the Higher Self. Royal blue is for happiness, loyalty, group success, occult power, and expansion. Use royal blue with caution.

Planetary Associations

  • Jupiter — Royal blue and bright blue; used on Thursdays or when calling upon Jupiterian energy (expansion, abundance, luck, sovereignty).
  • Moon — Very light blue; used on Mondays or for lunar energy (intuition, dreams, psychic work).
  • Saturn — Very darkest blue; used on Saturdays or for Saturnian energy (binding, endings, discipline).
  • Venus — Pale blue; used on Fridays or for Venusian energy (love, beauty, attraction).

Days of the Week

  • Thursday (Jupiter) — Blue candles are burned on Thursdays to invoke Jupiterian energy: expansion, abundance, luck, prosperity, and sovereignty.
