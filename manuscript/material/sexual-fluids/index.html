💧Dreaming of Semen

Male and female sexual fluids are intimate materials of extreme power and vulnerability. Possession of someone's sexual fluids leaves them susceptible to potent love spells, though these substances serve other magickal purposes as well.

Magickal Applications

Semen appears frequently in healing spells, with topical application traditionally prescribed for headaches. Women's sexual fluids provide access to the life-force and were a common ingredient in ancient Chinese alchemical preparations. Both vaginal secretions and menstrual blood were considered the preferred sustenance for vampires seeking increased sexual vitality and life-force.

⚠️
Warning: Spells involving intimate bodily substances rank among the oldest in the worldwide magickal repertoire. These workings originated in a world with different dangers. If you carry any transmissible condition (HIV, hepatitis, or otherwise), these spells are not appropriate for your practice. Magick is always about improving life and circumstances; behave responsibly.
