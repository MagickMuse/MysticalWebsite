🥄Muddler

◇Notes/Warnings
Wooden bar tool used for crushing ingredients. Functions as a wand equivalent in cocktail magick. Substitutes: rolling pin handle, back of a spoon. When muddling herbs for drinks, slap them on a hard surface first to release fragrance without imparting chlorophyll color.

The Bar Wand

Wooden muddlers serve as the wand of cocktail magick. Used for crushing ingredients against the bottom of a glass to release their flavors and mix them, the muddler directs intention into the drink through physical action and will.

Practical Uses

Famously used to crush mint for the perfect mojito (from the Spanish word mojado, meaning wet), the muddler excels at expressing citrus. Both the juice from the fruit and oil from the rind make it into the drink, giving it a more sustainable flavor. As the ice melts, the oil from the peel still stands out.

Substitutions

If you do not have a muddler on hand, you can use:

  • The handle end of a rolling pin
  • The back of a spoon

Herb Technique

If you want the flavor of a chosen greenery but do not want to impart the color of the chlorophyll, simply slap the herbs on a hard surface a few times and add them to the drink. They will be fragrant without turning your drink green.

Magickal Uses

Like the wand, the muddler directs will and intention. Being wooden, it shares the wand's advantage of working with elementals and spirits who are offended by iron. The physical act of muddling raises energy through repetitive motion while simultaneously infusing ingredients with intent.
