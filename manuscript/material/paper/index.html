Paper in Magick

Many spells contain a written component, making paper an essential magickal material. Something often needs to be written down, even if only to be burned afterward.

Parchment and Vellum

Classical grimoires frequently recommend parchment or vellum, particularly when creating talismans. These materials offer superior durability, though they can be difficult to acquire in modern times.

Brown Paper

In Hoodoo, brown paper is a preferred material. Cut-up paper bags or butcher's paper serve well, offering low cost and easy availability. Brown carries the correspondence of justice, making this paper particularly effective for any spell that demands justice be served.

Handmade Paper

Creating paper by hand requires minimal skill or artistic talent, and basic paper-making supplies are widely available. Since a spell rarely calls for more than a single sheet, this is an accessible option. The advantage of handmade paper is the ability to imbue it with chosen botanicals and fragrance during the crafting process.

Paper as Spell

Certain scripts are perceived as inherently powerful, including Arabic, Chinese, and Hebrew. In pagan Greek belief, the world was created and activated through the sound of vowels, while traditional Judaic teaching holds that life is activated through the Hebrew letters. Ancient Egyptians used different scripts for different purposes, both mystical and mundane. Northern European runes and Celtic Ogham script exist specifically for magickal and spiritual use, and many contemporary Wiccans and ceremonial magicians employ various magickal scripts.

Paper can form lasting amulets. The most familiar example is the Jewish mezuzah, attached to doorposts. Some Hoodoo practitioners have adopted the use of mezuzahs, and similar written amulets exist across Chinese, Japanese, Ethiopian, Muslim, and Tibetan traditions.

Chinese Paper Magick

Paper as we know it was invented in China in 105 CE, and China remains the primary home of paper magick. Paper charms are traditionally written in red cinnabar ink on yellow or red paper with a peach wood pen, using special magickal script known as "thunder writing" or "celestial calligraphy." These charms are used in various ways: pasted over doors or on walls, worn in the hair, or carried in a medicine bag.

Destroying Paper Spells

Some paper spells are specifically created to be destroyed, whether by fire or water. Destroying the paper releases its energy into the atmosphere, allowing the spell to work as intended. In some Chinese traditions, charms are burned first, then the ashes are mixed with tea or water and drunk. Rice paper works particularly well for this, as it dissolves easily in water.

Funerary Paper Amulets

In Mesoamerican tradition, a paper dog figure crafted and placed inside a coffin serves as a protective amulet and psychopomp, guiding and guarding the soul on its journey to the afterlife.

Clay Bowl Alternative

Written spells do not strictly require paper. An ancient custom involves inscribing a clay bowl or plate with spells and incantations, then shattering it to release the energy. For those who make their own pottery, the traditions can be combined: embed tiny pieces of inscribed paper directly into the clay, add further inscriptions to the surface, and then shatter the vessel to release the magick contained both inside and out.

Chromolithographs

Not all paper spells require words. Images can carry spells as well. Chromolithographs incorporate the power and blessing of a spirit and may substitute for a statue. Handmade collages of sacred and power images also function as amulets. A traditional alternative is to write the name of the desired spirit in gold ink on red paper and display it on a wall.

Magickal Ink

Many spells call for magickal ink formulas. While never strictly required, magickal ink can significantly empower a spell.

Moon Magick

In moon magick, paper is an indispensable companion for writing intentions, affirmations, and petitions. Craft a petition paper by writing your desire clearly and placing it beneath a dressed candle during a lunar working, or burn it in your cauldron or over a fireproof dish to release the intention into the atmosphere. The act of writing focuses and solidifies your goal, serving as a direct channel between your inner desire and the magick you are shaping. Any paper will serve; what carries the power is the intention behind the writing.
