Phylactery

Notes/Warnings
Protective object created by a magician for their own use during a specific ritual. Unlike talismans, phylacteries are only used for the duration of a particular rite.
Category1
💎Adornments

A phylactery is a protective object worn only by a magickian, created by the magician for their own use and protection, only for the duration of a particular magickal rite.

Characteristics

Unlike amulets or talismans which may be worn continuously or kept in a permanent location:

  • A phylactery is ritual-specific
  • Created by the magician for themselves
  • Used only during a particular rite

Relationship to Lamens

The lamen is a type of phylactery. Both are worn by magicians during specific rituals for protection. The distinction lies in the lamen's specific placement over the heart and its role in expressing authority during spirit work.

Key Distinction

While amulets provide general passive protection and talismans actively draw specific energies, phylacteries are temporary protective objects used only during the performance of a specific magickal operation.
