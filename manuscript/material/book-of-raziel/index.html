The Book of Raziel (Sefer Raziel HaMalakh in Hebrew) is a work of Jewish mystical literature traditionally attributed to the angel Raziel, the Angel of the Creator's Secrets. Its material was collected over long periods, with some sections said to date back to Talmudic times, though the book did not reach its present printed form until 1701 in Amsterdam.

The book was never intended to be read. It was assumed that most owners could not read it, would not understand it, and would not even attempt to. Instead, the publisher's intention was to provide an amulet: simple possession of the book was believed to grant psychic protection to its owner and to the place where it was kept.

Magickal Uses

  • Protection from Misfortune: Keeps its owner and the home safe from misfortune and danger, including fire and robbery
  • Driving Away Evil Spirits: The book's mere presence is said to repel evil spirits
  • Charm for Wise Sons: Owning the book reputedly works as a charm to raise wise and intelligent sons (angels were said to give the Daughters of Men private lessons instead)
  • Anti-Lilith Childbirth Amulets: Perhaps the book's most crucial magickal role was providing the diagram for the most famous anti-Lilith protective childbirth amulets
  • Portable Protection: Modern editions are reissued in numerous forms, including microfiche the size of a credit card. Though unreadable in that form, it still serves as an amulet for a pocket, charm bag, or glove compartment

Childbirth Protection

The most popular use of the Sefer Raziel, now as previously, is providing protection during childbirth. Maintain a copy of the Book of Raziel beneath a laboring woman's pillow for the duration of childbirth.
