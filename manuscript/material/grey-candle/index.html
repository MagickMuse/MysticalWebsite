Magical Properties

Grey candles embody neutrality and are powerful tools for self-defense, confusion work, and neutralizing harmful energies.

Primary Uses

  • Neutrality: Neither positive nor negative, balanced energy
  • Causing Confusion: Disorienting enemies when hexes are directed your way
  • Self-Defense: Protecting yourself from magical attack
  • Neutralizing Harmful Energies: Canceling out negative forces

Neutral Power

Grey occupies the space between light and dark:

  • Neither good nor evil
  • Balanced perspective
  • Objective viewpoint
  • Canceling extremes
  • Creating stalemate
  • Dampening intense emotions

Defensive Confusion

Grey candles can cause confusion in adversaries:

  • Clouding enemy judgment
  • Creating uncertainty in attackers
  • Deflecting hexes by making you hard to target
  • Scrambling negative intentions sent your way
  • Muddying the waters for those who wish you harm

Neutralization

Grey excels at canceling and neutralizing:

  • Removing harmful energies
  • Calming excessive energy
  • Creating balance from chaos
  • Dampening negative magic
  • Settling turbulent situations
  • Bringing things to neutral ground

Ritual Applications

Use grey candles for:

  • Self-defense against magical attack
  • Confusion spells against enemies
  • Neutralizing negative energies
  • Canceling out harmful magic
  • Creating protective ambiguity
  • Balancing extreme situations
  • Defensive deflection work
  • Making yourself hard to target psychically

Traditional Color Correspondences

Silver (or very clear light gray) aids with victory and stability, helps with meditation, develops psychic abilities, removes negative powers, neutralizes any situation, and repels destructive forces.

Planetary Associations

  • Moon — Light gray is a Moon color, used on Mondays or when calling upon lunar energy (psychic ability, dreams, intuition, the subconscious).

Days of the Week

  • Monday (The Moon) — Light gray candles are burned on Mondays to invoke lunar energy: psychic ability, dreams, intuition, and the subconscious.
