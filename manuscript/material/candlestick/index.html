The Witch's Lamps of Art

Candlesticks, or lamps of art, are among the working tools most often improvised at the last minute, yet they deserve the same care and consecration given to an Athame or wand. Beyond their symbolic role, they serve the very practical purpose of lighting your workbook during rituals that call for long invocations.

How Many You Need

A solitary witch needs only two candlesticks. Covens sometimes place one at each cardinal point of the circle plus one or more on the table of practice, for five in all. Rituals involving a show stone or speculum call for two or three lights on the table in addition to any cardinal point lights, bringing the total to seven or eight. Cardinal point lights are usually reserved for full ceremonial coven work and treated as shared property of the group.

Buying or Making

If you are not inclined toward woodcarving or pottery, simply buy as many candlesticks as you need without haggling over the price. Should you wish to make your own instead, they may be crafted from clay, wood, or metal, and consecrated using the method described in Consecration of the Candlesticks.

Use

Always burn exorcised white candles in your candlesticks during ritual, unless a particular working calls for another color.
