About Honeybush Tea

Honeybush (Cyclopia intermedia) is a South African plant—not a true tea from Camellia sinensis. Named for its honey-like fragrance, it was mostly unknown outside South Africa until 2001. Both honeybush and rooibos are drought tolerant plants.

Brewing Instructions

  • Amount: 1½ tsp tea
  • Temperature: 208°F (98°C)
  • Steep Time: 5-6 minutes
✨
Honeybush does not get bitter if over-steeped, making it forgiving for beginners.

Magical Applications

Honeybush has a dark red-brown color and mild fruity flavor with earthy energies suitable for:

  • Invoking motherly energy
  • Fertility workings
  • Prosperity spells
  • Grounding rituals
  • Nurturing self-care magic

Natural Sweetness: The honey-like quality makes honeybush especially suited for sweetening spells—drawing good things, attracting love, and nurturing growth.
