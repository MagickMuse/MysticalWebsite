Privacy Policy

  • Last updated: November 2026

Who We Are

The Magick Muse (magickmuse.com) is a digital grimoire and educational resource for modern practitioners, operated by The Magick Muse. You can reach us at TheMagickMuse.co@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

We collect limited data only:

  • Analytics data — if you consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, time on site, broad geographic region). This data is anonymized and aggregated. We do not use it to identify you personally.
  • Purchase and account data — when you buy something, join the Manuscript, or pick up a free download, we keep your email address, what you bought or downloaded, when, and the amount paid, so we can give you access, send your sign-in links, and keep business records. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
  • What you keep in the Manuscript — if you are a member, the notes you write, the workings you save from the Spell Builder, and the pages you save. These are private: only you can see them. We do not read them, share them, or use them for anything else, and you cannot upload pictures or files.
  • The beginner path — if you choose to start the 30-day beginner path, we email you one Manuscript page a day and keep track of which day you are on. Every email has a link to stop them, and they stop on their own after day 30 or if your access ends.

Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses:

  • Google Analytics cookies — only if you consent via the banner when you first visit. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by declining when prompted.
  • Sign-in cookie (essential) — when you sign in to the Manuscript or your library, a cookie keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. It is only used for that.
  • Local storage (functional) — we store your cookie consent choice and whether you have already seen our welcome offer popup. These are not tracking cookies; they exist only to avoid repeatedly showing you the same prompts.

We do not use advertising cookies, profiling cookies, or third-party tracking pixels.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4 — analytics, consent-gated. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing and receipts. Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Gumroad — purchases made before we moved to our own shop were processed by Gumroad. Gumroad Privacy Policy.
  • Discord — our community server. Discord's own privacy policy applies when you join.

Your Rights

Under GDPR (if you are in the EU or UK) and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask for a copy of your notes, saved workings and saved pages, email us at TheMagickMuse.co@gmail.com. We will send you a copy within 30 days.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained in Google Analytics for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

Purchase and account data is kept for as long as you have an account with us, and for as long as we are required to keep business and tax records.

Your notes, saved workings and saved pages are kept while you have access to the Manuscript. If your access ends (for example, you cancel a monthly membership), they are kept for 90 days in case you come back, and then permanently deleted. If you would like a copy before then, email us.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last changed. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
